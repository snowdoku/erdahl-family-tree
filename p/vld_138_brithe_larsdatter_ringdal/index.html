About the family

Mother of Giertrud Knudsdatter (see her page), Probable. Why this is our Giertrud: (1) Gertru Knudsdtr. of Rindalen (=Ringdal) married Joen Arnesen of Løvigen at Sunnylven on 7 Apr 1776 (index record read). (2) On 9 Nov 1777 Knud, son of Joen Arnesen and Gertru Knudsdtr. of Rindal, was baptised in Norddal parish. The godparents were Morten Persen Gierde, Lars Knudsen Rindal, Niels Arnesen Tryggestad, Marthe Knudsdtr. Rindal and Olave Knudsdtr. Rindal. Lars, Marthe and Olava are three of the siblings that the 1759 probate names alongside Giertrud. (3) Knud Jonsen of Melset, Ørskog, was confirmed in 1795 aged 17 and was 23 in the 1801 census, in the household of Jon Arnesen and Giertrud Knudsdatter of Melset, both in their 1st marriage. His age fits the 1777 Knud. Their next son Ole was baptised at Ørskog in 1782. (4) Giertrud Knutsdtr. of Ringdal was confirmed at Geiranger in 1770 aged 16. The ages vary: 8 in 1759, 16 in 1770, 48 in 1801 and 'b. 1750' at her 1815 burial, which points to about 1751-54. No single record names Giertrud of Melset as the daughter of Knud Olsen, so the parents are Probable: the case rests on the matching names and the godparent pattern. ¶2

Parents: daughter of Lars Gregoriussen and Maret Andersdatter of Store Ringdal (Probable), from his probate of 26 Sep 1727, which names a daughter Brit Larsdatter (3A 09 fol. 787, 24551/410, scan read). See the notes on Lars Gregoriussen Ringdal for why this is Probable, not Confirmed. ¶4

Life story

Burial: 'Brite Larsdtr.' of Ringdal, aged 37 (b. about 1722), buried 16 Apr 1759 at Sunnylven (Norddal ministerialbok, Sunnylven 1736-1760, p. 191, no. 367; index pg00000001731284). Name, farm and year fit her probate at Store Ringdal held 26 Jul 1759, and the age suits her 1742 marriage. Her birth is not entered; the burial implies about 1722. ¶5

Marriage (index record read): Knud Olsen of Fivelstad and 'Brite Larsdtr.' of Rindalen, engaged 23 Jun 1742, married 29 Dec 1742 at Sunnylven; trulovar Lars of [S]karbøen and Haagen of Lille Rindalen (Norddal parish, Sunnylven 1736-1760, 1524P, p. 138 no. 35; pv00000002059244). This fits her eldest children's ages in 1759 (Marthe 17, Ole 16). ¶3

Probate (scan read): 'Store Ringdahl udi Sunelvens Schibr[ede]. Anno 1759 d. 26 July' a skifte was held after the late 'Brithe Lars Datter' between her surviving husband [Knud] Olsen (the left edge of the name is in the gutter; the share list on fol. 232a names 'Enkemanden Knud Olsen', the widower) and their 6 children: Ole Knudsen 16 years (guardian Haagen Thorsen Lille Ringdahl), Lars Knudsen 12 (guardian Arne Arnesen Tibelstad), Marthe Knudsdatter 17 (guardian Anders Olsen Tryggestad), 'Giertrud Knuds Datter gl. 8 Aar' (guardian Ole Andersen [Sætre?]), Synneve Knudsdatter 6 (guardian Ole Jensen Lange Landskoug) and Olava Knudsdatter 3 (guardian Haagen Thorsen Lille Ringdahl). The share list on fol. 232a has 'Sønnen Ole Knudsen', 'Sønnen Lars Knudsen', 'Datteren Marthe Knudsdatter' and 'Datteren Giertrud Knudsdatter'. Image. ¶1

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
