Life story

Her full name was Wilma Almira Nelson, as given in the Minnesota marriage index for Freeborn County, Book F page 258, 1 October 1913. ¶7

Marriage: The Lake Mills Graphic of 1 October 1913 (p. 5) reports her marriage to Theodore Ramsey at Trinity Lutheran church, Albert Lea, Minnesota, by Rev. Edward Nervig, at 9:30 a.m. on Wednesday 1 October 1913; both bride and groom were 'from Joice', and they went on a honeymoon trip to Battle Lake, Minnesota. ¶12

Maiden name from her Find a Grave memorial and the 1918/1922 Beaver Creek baptisms of her daughters. ¶1

1934: Her mother Amelia (Hanson) Nelson died at Wilma's home on 5 July 1934 (Lake Mills Graphic, 18 Jul 1934, p. 5). ¶14

A 90th-birthday notice (Lake Mills Graphic, 3 Apr 1985) also places her birthday on 30 March. ¶5

CORRECTION (found 10 Oct 2026). Her parents were not Peter S. Nelson's family of Freeborn County, Minnesota. Her obituary in the Lake Mills Graphic of 9 September 1992 states: "Wilma A. Ramsey was born March 30, 1895, in Winnebago County, to William and Amelia (Hanson) Nelson." The censuses agree: the 1920 census gives her birthplace as Iowa with BOTH parents born in Iowa, and the 1950 census also gives Iowa. The Freeborn County, Minnesota connection appears to have been inferred from the place of her wedding; Albert Lea was simply the nearest town with a Norwegian Lutheran parsonage, about 18 miles north of Joice. This correction needs to be reconciled with the Nelson line. ¶6

Obituary (Lake Mills Graphic, 9 Sep 1992, p. 3): Wilma A. Ramsey, 97, of Joice, born 30 Mar 1895 in Winnebago County, Iowa, to William and Amelia (Hanson) Nelson. Died Wednesday 2 Sep 1992 at the Lake Mills Care Center. Funeral 5 Sep 1992 at Bethel Lutheran Brethren Church, rural Joice (Rev. Elwin Walvatne); buried Beaver Creek Cemetery, rural Joice. ¶2

Same obituary: she attended rural schools through eighth grade and later took a sewing course from a Mrs. Brones in Forest City. She married Theodore Ramsey on 1 Oct 1913 at a Lutheran parsonage in Albert Lea (Freeborn Co., Minnesota). This corrects the earlier 'ABT 1917' estimate. They farmed southwest of Joice and later moved into Joice. ¶3

She died on Wednesday 2 September 1992 at the Lake Mills Care Center, aged 97. The funeral was on Saturday 5 September at Bethel Lutheran Brethren Church, rural Joice, Rev. Elwin Walvatne officiating, with burial at Beaver Creek Cemetery. She shares a marker with her husband. ¶8

She had a rural-school education to the eighth grade and took a sewing course from a Mrs Brones in Forest City. ¶9

At her death she had four surviving children's families recorded as three — Beryl and Rev. Lloyd Sand, Wava and Palmer Valder, and Lynn and Norma Ramsey — plus nine grandchildren, 15 great-grandchildren and one great-great-granddaughter. Her estate was probated in Winnebago County from 21 September 1992, with her son Lynn as executor. ¶11
