About the family

Eldest child of Thomas Olsen and Eli Larsdatter, and Beret Martha's elder brother. Born 3 March 1848 and baptized at Værnes on 2 April 1848, entry no. 93. ¶1

Life story

His godparents, read from the register page, were Anne Johnsdatter of Beitland (a farmer's wife); Mali Larsdatter of Almlitrøen - his mother's own sister, born 1810; Inger Anna Olsdatter; Peder Christophersen of Beitland (a farmer); John Johnsen of Lillekleven (a crofter); and Anders Larsen of Bakmoen. Anders Larsen of Bakmoen stood again for Beret Martha the next year; Bakmo was where his father's first wife had come from. ¶2

Confirmed at Hegra church on 5 October 1862, entered as born 1848 at Beitlandsplads, son of Thomas Olsen, husmann, and his wife Eli Larsdatter. This is the last dated Norwegian record of the family apart from his sister Kirstine's baptism a week later. ¶3

He is not named on any of the family's Find a Grave memorials at Bricelyn and has not been traced in America. He may have died young, stayed behind, or settled elsewhere under an anglicised name. ¶4
