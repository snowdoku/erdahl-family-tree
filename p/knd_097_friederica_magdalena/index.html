About the family

Mother of Louisa Catharina Finck (see her page): the Illingen baptism of 13 Feb 1774 names the parents 'Johannes, Friederica Magdalina' (Ancestry 61023 record 8396225, image 1187139-00301). Probable: rests on identifying the 1774 child with the 1803 bride (same name, same father, same parish, only such baptism) and on the unlabelled index order. Surname not indexed. Also mother of Johann Gottfried (1772) and Georg Carl (1780) Finck. ¶1

Life story

Baptism (identification Probable): Friderica Magdalena 'Kuntzelman', baptised at Illingen on 7 May 1748, parents 'Matthaeus, Agnes Catharina' (Ancestry 61023 record 8395322, image 1187139-00243). The 1771 Illingen bride Friderica Magdalena Conzelmann names her father Mattheus (908396924); she is the only Friderica Magdalena of the surname indexed (Conzelmann/Kuntzelmann/Luntzelman spellings), and would be 22 at the marriage. Her grade (on her link to Louisa Catharina) is unchanged. ¶3

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed, graded on her link to her daughter Louisa Catharina Finck (see her page). FamilySearch, 'Deutschland, ausgewählte evangelische Kirchenbücher 1500-1971' (= 'Germany, Lutheran Baptisms, Marriages, and Burials, 1500-1971'), QPG5-KNXH: Louisa Catharina Finckh, bapt. Illingen 13 Feb 1774, Parents 'Friederica Magdalina, Johannes Finckh'. The 1803 bride Louisa Catharina is the same woman: her burial at Bietigheim on 19 Sep 1813 (QPGL-X2R5) gives Age 39 years, Father 'Johannes Finck', Mother 'Magdalena', Spouse '(Joh Mathias) Lindermajer'; her 1803 marriage (QPGR-8VHN) names Father-in-law 'Johannes Fincken'. The only rival Johannes Finck at Illingen (wife Maria Barbara) has the wrong wife's name. The earlier identification note (only such baptism; unlabelled order) is superseded. Also: her own baptism is labelled, QPGP-CH5H, Friderica Magdalena Kuntzelman, Illingen 7 May 1748, Parents 'Matthaeus Kuntzelman, Agnes Catharina'. Read signed in, 2 Nov 2026; labelled index fields, image not seen (partner-only). ¶4

The case for this identification 1

Surname and father: the bride's own row of her marriage to Johannes Finckh at Illingen on 30 Apr 1771 reads 'Friderica Magdalena Conzelmanns', relatives 'Mattheus, Johannes' (record 908396924, image 1187139-00345); 'Conzelmanns' is the genitive of Conzelmann. A Matthias Conzelmann x Agnes Catharina had a son Matthias baptised at Illingen on 26 Dec 1750 (record 8395428); possibly her parents, but her own baptism is not indexed. Her grade (on her link to Louisa Catharina) is unchanged. ¶2
