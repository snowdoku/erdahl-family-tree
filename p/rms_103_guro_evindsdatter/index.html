About the family

NEW ANCESTOR, graded on her link to her son Evind Jørgensen (see his page). Named as his mother, 'Guroe Evindsdtr. + sk. 18/3 1707', in the archivist's note on Askland card 79 (Skp. 8 fol. 67, 1729). Archivist's note only, so Probable. ¶1a

Parents from the bygdebok (Probable): Åmli ætt og heim II, Oland L.nr. 62 (canvases 264-265): 'Guri' daughter of Eivind Gunnarson Oland (1594-1650) and Birgit; she 'hadde arva [jord] etter foreldra'. Her children per the book: Eivind (m. 1. Ulvhild [Olavsdtr.] Askland, 2. Tone Svennsdtr. Askland), Salve (m. Ingeborg Eivindsdtr. Øvre Ramse), Olav (m. 1. Kari Knutsdtr. Øy, 2. 1723 Kari Gunnarsdtr. Simonstad, 3. Birgit Kjetilsdtr. Tveit), Sigrid (m. 1. Olav Eivindson Oland, 2. 1724 Salve Eivindson). Since her father died about 1650, she was born by about 1651. Her own grade is unchanged. ¶4

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: graded on her link to her son Evind Jørgensen. Her own skifte, 18 Mar 1707 at Oenland in Gjøvdal (Skifteprotokoll nr. 2 fol. 186b, DA 24350 view 187, read on the scan; card 39942/10017), is held 'efter... Guroe Evindsdatter Oenland' and lists her children Evind Jørgensøn Askeland, Salve Jørgensøn Oenland, Ole, Andgrim and Siri Jørgensdatter (wife of Ole Evindsøn Oenland), with their father Jørgen Olsen. Continuity to the Evind Jørgensen who died at Askland in 1729 is shown through the 1723 Oland skifte, where 'Morbroren Evind Jørgensøn Askland' is guardian to the children of his sister Sigri Jørgensdatter (cards 10033/10035). ¶2

Open questions and the next record to read 2

Next: her estate of 18 Mar 1707 (Østre Råbyggelag Skp. 3, DA 24351). ¶1b

Lead for her father: the 1664 bailiff's census lists an 'Effuin 70 aar' (Evind, b. about 1594) among the users of Ommeland/Oland, 8 hud, where her own skifte was held in 1707 (DA 35567 view 57, p. 78). He is gone from the 1666 list (DA 35510 view 32). A father aged about 56 or more at her birth is possible but not shown, and no record names her father, so he is not added. The 1664-65 bailiff's list of men under 50 (DA 35567 view 25, p. 13) lists at Ommeland 'Angrim Effuindsøn', age read as 20 but probably 70 (the 1664 and 1666 lists have an Angrim of 70 and 73 there and no young Angrim); her son was named Andgrim. She was not a daughter of the other Oland Evind, Evind Tellefsen (skifte 9 Jun 1706 - 18 Mar 1707, Skp. 2 fol. 188, card 10019): his heirs were Ole, Evind, Karry, Ingeborg and Groe (Groe married Ole Knudsen Miaaland), not Guro. ¶3
