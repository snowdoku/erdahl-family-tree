Confidence: the grade and how it changed 3

Speculative father of Elisabetha Margaretha Haubert (see her page), wife of Joh Caspar Stierlin. ¶1a

Graded on that link: the bride row of her first marriage, to Tobias Mayer at Bietigheim on 29 Oct 1715, reads 'Elizabetha Margarethe Hauberten', relatives 'Johann Christoph, Tobias' (record 907270306, image 1184899-00382). Joining that bride to Stierlin's wife of 1718 rests on the inference that 'Tobias' in the 1718 row (907270350) is her late husband (Tobias Mayer, buried 2 Jun 1718, 7276045), so Speculative; unlabelled index rows. Surname read from the genitive 'Hauberten' (Haubert or Hauber). No Haubert/Hauber rows otherwise indexed at Bietigheim. ¶1b

Upgrade: Speculative to Probable, graded on his link to Elisabetha Margaretha (see her page): FamilySearch, 'Germany, Marriages, 1558-1929' JH63-T9D, an extraction independent of Ancestry, gives Johann Caspar Stierlin's bride of 21 Aug 1718 as 'Elisabetha Margretha Herbort'. That ties the 1718 bride to the Elisabetha Margarethe 'Hauberten', daughter of Johann Christoph, who married Tobias Mayer at Bietigheim on 29 Oct 1715 (Ancestry 7270306/907270306). Still Probable: the 1715 father row is unlabelled and no labelled twin of the 1715 marriage was found on FamilySearch (search Tobias Mayer x Elisabetha Margaretha, Bietigheim 1715). ¶3a

Searches that found nothing 1

Searched without result: a baptism of Elisabetha Margaretha Hauber/Haubert 1688-98 (none in the index); Johann Christoph Hauber 1640-1700 near Bietigheim (only men at Stuttgart, m. 1702, and Schorndorf, b. 1680, m. 1700, both too young to be her father). A Hauber family lived at Hohenhaslach (Hanß Jacob x Anna Margretha, children 1688-91; Jacob, 1720s), but no Johann Christoph is indexed there. ¶2
