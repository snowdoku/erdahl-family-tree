Life story

1666 vicar's census, Tjugum, Torsnæs (35519 view 170, p. 314): 'Tollach 8 aar', son of Ola bruger, 60, with brothers Marcus 16 and Ola 2 1/2. His birth is therefore nearer 1658 than the 1651 implied by the 1701 age of 50. ¶3

Collateral, added to prove the parentage of his sister Brite Olsdatter. At her husband Hans Hansen's probate at Lunde on 29 May 1713 he acted for the widow as 'hendis broder Tollach Torsnæs' (Ytre Sogn 2b fol. 445a). 1701 manntall: Tollach Olsen, 50, bonde at Toesnes, sons Marchus 20 and Olle 15. ¶1

Probate 4 Oct 1729 at Torsnes in Tjugum skipreide after 'Dannemand Tollef Olsen', between his widow Anna Olsdatter and their children Ole, Marcus, Hermund, Guri and [Giesløf?]; the minors' paternal uncle (Farbroder) Ole Torsnes; lensmann Jon Tiugum; appraisers Siur Sveren and Ole Torsnes (Ytre Sogn 3b fol. 378a). Sons Marcus and Ole match the 1701 list, so Tollach Olsen of 1701 and of 1713 are one man. ¶2
