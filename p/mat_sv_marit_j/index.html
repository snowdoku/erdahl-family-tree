About the family

Her patronymic says her own father was a John. Her marriage to John Nielsen Langlie is not in the indexed Selbu marriages and must be about 1812-1818; that entry would name both their fathers and is the next record on this line. ¶2

She is Mayme Eidem's great-great-great-grandmother and was entirely unknown to the family before this search. ¶3

Parents identified at Probable as Jon Thomassen and Brynil Pedersdatter of Lien, Selbu (orchestrator, line 8). Parentage argument (orchestrator, line 8): (1) Marit, baptised 12 Sep 1784 to Jon Th[omassen] Lien and Brynild Pedersdatter, fits her burial age (69 in June 1854); (2) in the 1801 census that couple's household at Lien holds 'Marit Jonsdatter 16' among 'Deres børn', with siblings Peder (24), Brynil (14) and Ingebor (3); (3) at the baptism of Marit's first son Nils (25 Jun 1815) two godparents were 'Peder Johnsen Lien' and 'Ingebor Johnsdatter Lien', and at her daughter Brynhild's baptism (1818) a godparent was 'Brynhild Johnsdatter Langlie' (née Lien, wife of her husband's brother Ole Nielsen) - i.e. three of the Lien siblings stand sponsor to her children; (4) she named her first daughter Brynhild, the name of the Lien mother; (5) her second son is John (after a father Jon). ¶13a

Life story

Bygdebok lead: her dates 1784/1854 appear on the p. 726 band, and 'Øver-Lien' appears on the p. 719 band near the Jon/Marit entry - consistent with the Lien origin (Probable parents Jon Thomassen and Brynil Pedersdatter of Lien). The book spells the mother's name Brynhild (no 'Brynil' boxes); neither parent's name has a box on pp. 714-727. ¶15

Married John Nielsen Langlie on Trinity Sunday, 21 May 1815, in Selbu: 'Pigen Marithe Johnsd. Auglem' - so she was unmarried and of the farm Auglem (Uglem), Selbu, though the first banns (19 March 1815) place her at Langlie, probably in service there. The register does not name her father or give her age; Auglem/Uglem is the lead for her family. ¶4

At the baptism of her first son Nils (25 June 1815) the clerk wrote her as 'Marith Jens d.' (twice, once struck through), against 'Johnsd.' in the banns, the marriage and the 1818 baptism. Johnsdatter is the majority reading; the 'Jens d.' is noted as a conflict. The godparents there included Peder Johnsen Lien and Ingebor Johnsd. Lien, but the Lien Johnsen family were already kin to the Langlie family through Ole Nielsen's wife Brynhild Johnsdatter Lien, so they do not by themselves identify Marit's family. ¶5

Churched (introduced in church after childbirth) on the 10th Sunday after Trinity 1815: 'John Nilsens Hustru af Langlie'. ¶6

Named as the mother in her daughter Brynhild's baptism, Selbu parish register 1816-1824, page 63 serial 32, 29 November 1818: 'Foraeldrene Gaardmand Joh Nielsen Langlie og Hustrue MARIT JOHNSD'. This is the only record of her found so far, and it is read from the scanned page. ¶1

1825 census: residence 0047* Langlie, household 01, person 013 - 'Marit Johnsd.', 42, 'Hustru' (wife), after her husband John Nilsen, 42, Hmd., and before their children Nils (11), Brynhild (8) and John (3), in the household of her mother-in-law, the widow Guri Olsdatter. ¶7

Death not yet found: Selbu burials 1825-1880 for forename Marit born 1780-1786 give seven Johns- or Jensdatter entries, none with Langli as residence in the index. Ruled out: Marit Johnsdatter, widow, of Haarstad, buried 18 March 1860 (John was still alive until 1862); Marit Jensdatter, cottar's wife (Hsmdkone), of Haarstad, buried 1873 (husband living); and the earlier candidate Marit Johnsdatter, Husmandskone, of Eidem, buried 22 July 1866 aged 72 (a husband living in 1866, and a Marit Johnsdatter b. about 1795 was a wife at Eidem in 1825 while this Marit was at Langlie). Still open: Marith Johnsdr, 57, buried 26 May 1839 (1824-1842 book p. 395 no. 35); Marit Johnsdatt, 69, buried 11 June 1854 (1843-1859 book p. 392 no. 31); Marit Johnsdatter, 77, buried 5 April 1857 (same book p. 398 no. 13) - all three with no residence in the index, so the scans must be read; and Marit Johnsdr, 89, inderstenke (lodger's widow) at Langseth, buried 20 December 1874. ¶10

Death and burial (read on the scan): Selbu ministerialbok 1843-1859, burials 1854, p. 392 no. 31 - died 5 June, buried 11 June 1854, 'Hsmdkone Marit Johnsdatt.' (husmandskone, a cottar's wife), aged 69, of Langli. The entry does not name her husband and has no remark. It is taken as hers because she was a cottar's wife with a living husband at Langli in 1854, when John Nielsen was a living husmand at Langli (buried 1862 as 'Husmand John Nilsen, 77, Langli'); her age 69 (born about 1785) agrees with the 1825 census age of 42 within the 1-2 years by which that household's ages run high; the 1825 census has only one Marit Johnsdatter at Langlie, this one; and the Langli farm history in Selbu i fortid og naatid vol. 3, p. 726, prints '1854' beside 'Marit Jonsdt.' on the line for Jon (seen only as OCR word boxes, a lead). Confidence in this identification: Probable-to-strong; a probate (skifte) after her in 1854 or the bygdebok text read in full would confirm it. ¶11

Corrections 1

Correction: birth estimate changed from 'BEF 1800' to 'ABT 1783', calculated from her census age of 42 in 1825. The ages in this household run one to two years high (her husband is 42 against about 40 by his other records), so a birth between about 1783 and 1785 is the realistic range. ¶8

Alternatives considered and set aside 4

Origin, candidates only: in the 1801 census of Selbu there are six women indexed as Marit Jonsdatter born 1780-1786, and none as Marit Jensdatter. Svinaas b. 1780 (unmarried); Krogstad b. 1783 (daughter of Jon Reinalssen and Ane Olsdatter); Haarstad Oever b. 1781 (servant); Eidum b. 1784 (servant); Dyrdahl b. 1785 (daughter of Jon Halvorssen); Lien b. 1785 (daughter of Jon Thomassen and Brynil Pedersdatter). None was living at Uglan (Uglem, her residence at the 1815 marriage) in 1801, and no Uglan household had a daughter Marit of the right age. The Lien family is the best fit: that household also held a Brynil Jonsdatter b. 1787, who is very probably her husband's brother Ole's wife Brynhild Johnsdatter, and Peder Jonssen and Ingebor Jonsdatter of the same household match the godparents 'Peder Johns. Lien' and 'Ingebor Johnsd. Lien' at Marit's son Nils's baptism in 1815. But nothing yet ties that Marit to Auglem, and the 1815 baptism writes her 'Jens d.'. Her father is therefore not added. Her confirmation (about 1799-1801, Selbu, scan-only) or a probate after Jon Thomassen Lien would settle it. ¶9

Rejected burial candidates, all read on the scans: Marith Johnsd. Stokke, 'Pige' (unmarried), 57, of Stokke, buried 26 May 1839 (1824-1842 book p. 395 no. 35); Enke (widow) Marit Johnsdatter, 77, of Haarstad, buried 5 April 1857 (1843-1859 p. 398 no. 13) - a widow while John was still alive; Inderstenke (lodger's widow) Marit Johnsdr., 89, of Langseth, buried 20 December 1874 (1860-1877 fol. 53 no. 69) - no husband named and a different farm. Earlier rejected: Haarstad widow 1860, Haarstad husmandskone 1873, Eidem husmandskone 1866. ¶12

Rival: Marit (b. 1785) daughter of Jon Halvorsen Dyrdal and Karen Estensdatter, also aged 16 in 1801, but no Dyrdal kin appear at her children's baptisms. Unresolved: 'Auglem' as her farm in 1815 (probably service) and the clerk's slip 'Marith Jens d.' in June 1815. No record states her parents outright, so the link is Probable. ¶13b

Ruled out as her baptism: the Marit baptised at Cantate 1783 was a daughter of Jacob Hansen Draxen and Ingeborg Siursdatter, not of a Jon. The 12 Sep 1784 Lien baptism remains the best match. ¶14
