Life story

Origin: almost certainly the Rasmus Alfsen of Store Sætre in Skodje (Grytten skipreide), b. about 1676-81, servant at Holen in Borgund in 1701 and 1704, and named as son (aged 30) in his father Alf Rasmussen's probate of 10 Sep 1706. Birth date taken from those three records; see Alf Rasmussen for why that link is Probable. ¶6

The Sunnmøre probate index has entries for an 'Alf Alfsen' registered 14 April 1697 and an 'Alf Rasmussen' registered 10 September 1706, which may well belong to this family, but neither has been read and neither is linked here. ¶3

The 1 Apr 1701 skifte after Alf Rasmussen's wife cannot be read in the probate protocols: Sunnmøre Skifteprotokoll 01 (source 24544) ends with entries of Sep 1700 (views 317-319; view 320 is the end target), and Skifteprotokoll 02A (source 24545) and its typed register begin in 1703. The protocol for 1700-1703 has not survived. Test record (revised): the morsarv clause of the 1706 protocol entry itself (24545, fol. 300b, view about 311), which may name the dead mother; otherwise the Sunnmøre tingbok for 1701 or a 1701-02 guardianship record (overformynderi). ¶8

Marriage (index): Rasmus Alfss. of Blindem married Ingeborg Knudsd. at Borgund on 1 Jan 1710 (pv00000002827133); the index marks it as his first marriage. She is the Inger Knudsdatter whose probate of 1738 calls her 'Rasmus Alfsens hustru'. ¶5

Named as the father in the baptism of 'Alf' at Borgund on 3 April 1712 - the Alv Rasmussen who farmed at Blindem and was buried there in 1778 with his birth year given as 1712. The baptism entry itself records no residence, but a 'Rasmus Alfss.' had a child baptized at Blindem on 22 November 1726, and he is the only Rasmus Alfsen recorded on that farm, so the identification is Probable rather than Confirmed. ¶1

His own patronymic shows his father was named Alf. The names alternate in the usual Norwegian way down four generations at Blindem: Alf, then Rasmus Alfsen, then Alv Rasmussen (1712-1778), then Rasmus Alvsen (about 1746-1790). ¶2

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed, graded on his link to his son Alv Rasmussen (see his page). The probate after his wife Inger Knudsdatter at Blindem, 13 May 1738 (Sunnmøre skifteprotokoll 3A 12 fol. 481b, image 24553/216, scan read), was held between 'hendes efterladte Mand Rasmus Alfsen' and their children, and in the division the first heir is 'Sønnen Alf Rasmussen' (the first letters of 'Alf' are blotted; 'Rasmussen' and 'Sønnen' are clear), followed by daughters (Marit Rasmusdatter and others, two aged 16 and 14). Continuity: Alf son of Rasmus Alfss. was baptised at Borgund on 3 Apr 1712, two years after Rasmus Alfss. of Blindem married on 1 Jan 1710; Rasmus Alfss. of Blindem had further children baptised in 1714, 1717, 1720 and 1726; Alv Rasmussen farmed Blindem until his death in 1778. No other Rasmus Alfsen or Alf Rasmussen is recorded at Blindem. ¶4
