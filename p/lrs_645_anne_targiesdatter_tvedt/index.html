About the family

Her husband was alive on 6 Oct 1783, when 'Orm Egilsen Tvedt' sold Tvedt (matr. 26, Treungen) to 'min Søn Gunlog Ormsen' (pantebok I 9, 10875, No 521, view 135). She is not named in the deed, so whether she was still living is unknown. Gulloug was born about 1741; if Anne was his mother, she married Orm by about 1740. ¶5

Life story

Probate of her father: at the probate after 'Torgiej Torrofssøn' held at Sønderland in Treungen on 15 Sep 1756, one heir is 'Anne Torgiejsdatter gift med Orm Egilssøn Tvedt af Treungen' (Anne, daughter of Targiej, married to Orm Egilsen Tvedt of Treungen). Her mother was the widow Sigri Evensdatter. Vest-Telemark skifteprotokoll 1752-1759 (source 25673), view 331 right page. ¶1

So she was the wife of Orm Egilsen Tvedt (see his page) by 1756. He is the only Orm at Tvedt in Treungen in 1755-1775, and the 1769 and 1763 records give him the patronymic Egilsen, so the husband in the probate is the man in the tree. ¶2

Burial: 'Ditto Dag ved Triungens Kirke paa Orm Egildsen Tveds Hustrue Anne gl. 68 Aar' (the same day, at Treungen church, [earth cast] on Anne, wife of Orm Egildsen Tvedt, aged 68). The entry follows the burials of the 6th Sunday after Easter (Exaudi), which in 1786 fell on 28 May (Easter 16 Apr 1786). Kviteseid ministerialbok 1773-1786 (7982), p. 189, canvas 183 right page. So she died in May 1786, born about 1718, and her husband Orm was still living (she is 'Hustrue', not widow). Born about 1718, she was of an age to be the mother of Gulloug Ormsen (b. about 1741), Egild Ormsen (b. about 1751) and Rannei Ormsdatter (married 1775-76), but the entry names no child, so her grade (Probable, on her link to Egild and Rannei) is unchanged. ¶7a

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the grade · 1 alternative set aside · 4 searches with no result · 4 open questions

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Confidence: Probable, graded on her link to her children in the tree, Egild Ormsen (see his page, b. about 1751) and Rannei Ormsdatter (see her page). No record read names her as their mother: the Treungen baptism entries of this period name only the father, and the Kviteseid books begin in 1754, after Egild's birth. The grounds are that she was Orm's wife in 1756; that Orm's children buried in 1759-1763 were named Sigri and Targgi, after her parents Sigri Evensdatter and Targiej Torrofsen (so she was the mother of the children of the late 1750s); and that no other wife of Orm is known. ¶3a

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Rival: none named, but Orm's probable son Gulloug Ormsen was born about 1741 (60 in 1801), and an earlier marriage of Orm before about 1750 has not been ruled out. Egild's own link to Orm is also Probable (see his notes). ¶3b

Open questions and the next record to read 4

Next record: the probate after Orm Egilsen Tvedt or after Anne, which would name all their children. It is not in the 1776-1781 protocol (register 25676 views 771-779: the only Treungen Tvedt entries are No 250, after Gunder [..]sen, fol. 291, and No 246, Grøven under Tvedt, after Torgen Halvorsen, fol. 281). The 1781-84 (25677) and 1784-90 (25678) protocols have no register and are unread. The panteregister I 2 pages for Tvedt matr. 26 in Treungen (10916 views 143-144) show Gulloug Ormsen's deed to Tvedt Nordre (dated Oct 1783, tingl. 9 Mar 1784, No 521, sellers not named on the page), his own skifte (closed 31 Jan 1807), and a pantebrev of Egild Ormsen on Tvedt Søndre, but no skifte after Orm or Anne. Orm's death therefore probably falls after 1775 (a cautioner in Nov 1775) and his farm passed to Gulloug in 1783; pantebok No 521 (1784) may name Orm's heirs as sellers. ¶4

Lead: her sister Asloug Targiesdatter, wife of Halvor Asbjørnsen Tvedt in Nissedal, had a probate in 1777: register to skifteprotokoll 1776-1781 (25676 view 773), No 154, 'Tvedten i Nissedals Annex... efter Asloug Targjesdatter', fol. 108. Not read. ¶6

Test record: the probate after her (mid-1786 onwards) in Vest-Telemark skifteprotokoll 1784-1790 (source 25678, no register; view 100 = No 555, 8 Aug 1785, so a summer or autumn 1786 skifte should lie about views 150-250). It would name Orm as widower and all the children she left. checked the entry headings of 25678 views 181-243 (Nos 585-606; entries are copied in at their closing at Sundbø, dated Oct 1785 to Dec 1786, so a 1786 registration can be closed and entered later): no skifte after Anne or Orm Tvedt. Treungen entries there: No 592 (v201 left), closing 8 Dec 1785 of the skifte after Margith Targiesdatter of Hiemdahl in Treungen (registered 20 Aug 1785; Anne's sister), and No 605 (v232 right), after Anne, wife of Anders Evensen, of Tvedt in Treungen (died 25 Oct, sons Even 5 and Jørgen 2; not this Anne). ¶7b

Next: views 244 onward. Orm had handed the farm to his son Gulloug in Oct 1783, so no skifte may have been held at all. ¶7c
