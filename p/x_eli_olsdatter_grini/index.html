Life story

Baptism: Eli, daughter of Ole Larsen of Griner and Kirsti Olsdatter, was baptised at Gran church on 5 Mar 1741 (3rd Sunday in Lent). Godparents: Halvor Olsen Ballangrud, Erich Mortved, Ole Hansen Dal, a woman of Smedsrud whose name is uncertain, and Ingerie Andersdatter Ballangrud. Mother churched 30 Mar (Gran church book 1732-1744, fol. 124v; scan). ¶2

Was living at Griner (Grini) when she married Nils Pedersen of Fallang on 8 Mar 1764 at Gran. She was 58 at Øvre Fallang in the 1801 census. Her granddaughter Eli Pedersdatter (b. 1793) was named for her. Her death has not been found. ¶1

Probate (index card): she is the widow 'Elie Olsdtr.' at her husband Niels Pedersen's estate at Fallang, 22 Jan 1811 (f. 547b), so she was living in January 1811. ¶4

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: the probate protocol of her husband was read (Hadeland og Land skifteprotokoll 1802-1821, fol. 547b-548a, view 571). At the estate of Niels Pedersen Fallang, 22 Jan 1811, she is the widow Elie Olsdatter, and the seven children, first among them the son Peder Nielsen (Peder Nielsen Fallang in the shares), are stated to be born in the marriage ('i Ægteskab avlede'). That names her as Peder Nilsen's mother; confidence raised from Probable to Confirmed. Her own parentage (Ole Larsen and Kirsti Olsdatter of Griner) remains Probable, resting on the 1741 baptism. ¶5

The case for this identification 1

Identification: she is the only Eli baptised to a father Ole in the Gran index for 1738-1744, and her father lived at Griner, where she lived when she married in 1764. Her 1801 census age of 58 implies about 1743, two years later. Her son Ole (b. about 1769) would be named for her father. Probable: Gran baptisms 1745-1746 are not indexed and were not read, and no record yet states the link outright. ¶3
