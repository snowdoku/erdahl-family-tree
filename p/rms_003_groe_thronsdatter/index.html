About the family

Probable child of Tron Felle (see his page): her patronymic is Thronsdatter and she lived at Felle, where Tron's son Tore Thronsen and daughter Astri Thronsdatter lived with Tron's widow Gunild Andersdatter in 1801. Five Tronsen/Thronsdatter children of Felle married 1778-81 (Hæge, Ingebor, Groe, Andres, Thore), and no other Tron was seen at Felle in the marriage register 1773-86 (other Felle households: Knud, Christopher, Biørn). Her father is not named in the entry. Her mother is presumably Gunild Andersdatter, but not shown. Would be confirmed by her baptism (Kviteseid ministerialbok before 1773) or by Tron's probate (skifte) listing heirs. ¶2

Life story

Betrothed on the 1st Sunday after Easter 1779 at Tvedsund, Treungen, as 'Groe Thronsdatter Fælle' to the soldier Christoffer Kittilsen Dyraas of Nissedal; cautioners Biørn Onstad and Jørgen Sigurdsen Home; married in Treungen church 2 Jul 1779 (Kviteseid ministerialbok 1773-1786, image 122, p.116b). ¶1

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
