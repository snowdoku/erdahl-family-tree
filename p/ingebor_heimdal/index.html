About the family

Siblings baptized in Treungen: Osmund (b. 22 Apr 1851), Anne (b. 15 Dec 1855), Gunvor (b. 11 May 1858; probably buried Nov 1858) and Gundvor (b. 16 Nov 1859; probably buried Feb 1860). Born in America: Oline (1861) and Thea Gurine (1864) in Perry, Dane Co., Wisconsin, and Gunhild (b. 18 Nov 1868, Worth Co., Iowa). ¶3

Her mother came with her to America. The same 1900 household includes "Gundbyr Heimdal", mother-in-law of the head, born June 1827 in Norway and immigrated 1862 — so Ingebor's widowed mother was living with the Ramsey family at the age of 72. ¶7

Children: Eight, per her obituary: Torjus (died in infancy), Turi (died aged 37), Alvina (died aged 20), Annie (Mrs. Ole J. Tweed, died 10 March 1937 aged 57), and, living in 1937, Tomina (Mrs. J. J. Rusley), Ida (Mrs. Lars Amundson), Theodore and Oscar, all of the Joice community. She also left 27 grandchildren and 23 great-grandchildren. Her husband had died on 3 March 1933. ¶11

Life story

Recorded as 'Ingeborg/Inger Thorofsd.' in the Winnebago baptisms of her children. ¶1

She emigrated with her parents in 1861 to Perry, Dane Co., Wisconsin, and moved with them to Worth Co., Iowa, between 1864 and 1868. ¶4

Emigration: she left Treungen for America with her parents and siblings Osmund and Anne. The family is on the out-migration list dated 7 Apr 1861 (rows 9-13), where she appears as 'Ingeborg', aged 7½. ¶15

Winnebago church baptisms of her children: Torjus (1875; mother 'Inger Thorofsd.'), Thuri Oline (1877), Gunbjørg Thomine (1881; named after her mother) and Thonette (1884). ¶5

Marriage: She married Tellef T. Ramsey on 24 February 1875, by Rev. J. M. Dahl. Five years later they moved to the Worth County farm where they lived from then on, apart from six years in Joice (1937 obituary). ¶10

Last years and church: Her health had been failing for some years, and she was confined to bed for about the last ten weeks. She was a member of Beaver Creek Lutheran church and attended services and the Lord's Supper for as long as her health allowed (1937 obituary). ¶12

BIRTH YEAR CORROBORATED. The 1900 census of Fertile Township, Worth County, Iowa records her as born August 1853 in Norway, immigrated 1862. This supports the Treungen church book reading of 19 August 1853 against the 1854 given on Find a Grave. ¶6

Obituary: The Lake Mills Graphic of 25 August 1937 (p. 8, 'Mrs. Ingbor Ramsey') says she was born at Treungen, Telemark, Norway, on 19 August 1853 to 'Torof and Gunbjor Helmdal' (Heimdal), was baptised as an infant, and came as a child with her parents first to Dane County, Wisconsin, then to a farm near Northwood, Iowa, for a few years, and then to a farm in Fertile Township, Worth County. This matches her Treungen baptism. ¶8

Confirmation: She was confirmed by Rev. T. A. Torgerson (1937 obituary; no date given). Torgerson served the Winnebago and Silver Lake congregations from 1865 (Norlie), so the confirmation was probably about 1868-70 in one of them (inferred). ¶9

Funeral and burial: The funeral was on Thursday 19 August 1937, at 1 p.m. at the home and 2 p.m. at Beaver Creek church. Rev. L. F. Schele officiated, and Rev. H. F. Huseth spoke and sang in Norwegian. She was buried in the family lot in Beaver Creek cemetery (1937 obituary). The WPA's 1939 survey of Worth County stones reads 'Ramsey, Ingebor. 1853 to 8-16-1937; Beaver Creek cem.' The page image clearly shows 1853; an earlier OCR reading of '1855' was a misreading. ¶13

Records that disagree 1

Conflict over the death date: Her obituary says she 'died Aug. 15' aged 83 years, 11 months and 27 days, which counts exactly to 15 August 1937 from her birth on 19 August 1853. The WPA stone transcription (1939) and Find a Grave give 16 August 1937. The age was probably worked out from the date the family gave the paper, so it is not independent evidence. 16 August is kept for now; her Iowa death certificate (Worth County, 1937) would settle it. ¶14

Corrections 1

CORRECTION: born 19 Aug 1853, not 1854. Both Treungen church-book copies record her baptism on 20 Aug 1853 as Jngeborg/Ingeborg Thorufsdatter, daughter of Thoruf Osmundsen and Gundbjør Ormsdatter, residence Tvedt. The klokkerbok copy gives her birth date as 19 Aug. Find a Grave has the day right but the year wrong. ¶2
