Life story

Farmer of Holan (gnr. 24), Skatval. Bygdebok (Skatval vol. 1, canvas 132): 'Lars Håkonsen, [f.] 1683, [d.] 25.11.1765'; 1st marriage to Kari Toresdtr. (b. 1688, d. 20.07.1732; children Tore 1716, Håkon 1719, Mali 1720, Marit 1722, Tore 1724, Mali 1726, Lars 1727, Håkon 1731); 2nd marriage 31.05.1733 to Marit Pedersdtr. Kvål (children Kari 1734, Kari 1735, Mali 1736, Marit 1738, Kari 1739); '3. gang' to a wife whose name was not recovered (b. 1713, d. 26.03.1772), children [name not recovered] døpt 19.04.1744, 'Peter, døpt 15.01.1747, 77/3' and Mali døpt 10.08.1749. He sold the farm to Lars Danielsen in 1755. ¶1

Age check: the book makes him 63-64 at Peter's birth (by a third wife born 1713, who was 33). Unusual but possible; if the birth year 1683 is a rebuild error, it does not change the link. The Holan baptism of 1747 will settle it. ¶4

Primary records for his marriages, card index 77804: canvas 811, 'Holan, søndre, Skatval, 31/10-1732: Kari Tordsdtr.; Enkemann: Lars Haagensen Hollum; Barn: Tord Larsen 8 år, Lars Larsen 5 år, Haagen Larsen 1 1/2 år'; guardian 'Morbroren, Ole Tordsen, og faren, Lars Haagensen Hollum' (prot. 2, fol. 463b). Canvas 813, 'Holan søndre, dragongård, Skatval, 26/3-1738: Marit Pedersdtr.; Enkemann: Lars Haagensen Hollum'. These agree with the bygdebok's first wife Kari Toresdtr. (d. 1732) and second wife Marit Pedersdtr. (m. 1733), and show his patronymic Haagensen (Håkonsen) in a primary record. They do not touch his link to Peter Larsen (the third wife's son) or to his parents. Grade unchanged. ¶7

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the grade · 2 open questions

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Graded Probable on his link to his son Peter Larsen (see his page): bygdebok statement only (the cross-reference '77/3' ties this Peter to the Skjervold holder). Peter's mother is the unnamed third wife; she is not added. ¶2

Open questions and the next record to read 2

Test record: the Skatval baptism of 15 Jan 1747 (Stjørdal ministerialbok 1740-1756, source 16370, scan only), which should read 'Lars Holans barn Peder'; and a probate after Lars Håkonsen Holan, 1765-66 (no Holan entry for him found by grep in the typed registers of 24753/24755; check skifteprotokoll 5, 24755, by date). ¶3

Lead: the typed register of skifteprotokoll 5 (24755, c.1754, fol. 36) has a probate after 'Peder Hågensen, husm. under Sønder Hoelen' (Holan, Skatval). The bygdebok lists a son Peder of Håkon Pedersen as husmann on the farm, so this is probably Lars's brother; the heirs or guardians may name Lars Haagensen. ¶5
