About the family

Son of Gilbert Olsen Slind and Magna Walli. HIS MIDDLE NAME IS HIS MOTHER'S MAIDEN NAME, which is what identifies him: the VA Nationwide Gravesite Locator records "SLIND, GERALD WALLI, A1C US Air Force, Korea, b. 1 Nov 1936, d. 12 Apr 2021, Selby Lutheran, LaCrosse, WA" - "Selby" being the clerk's spelling of SELBU, and Lacrosse being the Whitman County village beside the Selbu churchyard. ¶1

Life story

He is the "Gerald"/"Yerald" enumerated with his parents in the 1950 census of Whitman County, ED 38-25. ¶2

Marriages, from the Washington State Digital Archives: Gerald W. Slind x Shirley A. Miller, 1962, Skagit County, and again 1967, Clark County (two licences, the pair evidently remarrying or the record duplicated across counties); "SLIND, GERALD W x TURNER, ADELE, 2004", statewide DOH index. A "Miller, Donovan x Slind, Shirley A, 1966, Snohomish" sits between the two, so the 1962 and 1967 entries are probably a marriage, a divorce and a remarriage. ¶3
