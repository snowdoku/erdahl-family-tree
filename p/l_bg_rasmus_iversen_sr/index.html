About the family

Parents: His father is Iver of Botnen and his mother Lisbeth (named in the 1750 baptism). Their patronymics are not proved. In 1774 an 'Iwer Mogensen Botnen' and a 'Lisbeth Rasmus D. Botnen' were godparents together (9 Jul 1774), and a Johannes Iwersen and a Lisbeth Iwersdatter of Botnen stood together on 1 May 1774; these are probably his parents and two of his siblings. His elder sister Lisbeth was baptised 5 Jan 1748 to the same parents. Trap: Iver Olsen of Botnen, betrothed in Jan 1748 to Kari Arnesdatter of Kjekevoll, is a different man. ¶11

Children's marriages (index level, cross-checked against the 1837 probate): (1) Lisbeth Rasmusdatter of Botnen married Ole Mikkelssøn, bachelor, of Harrebakke at Brekke church on 1 Jul 1804 (engaged 26 May); the probate's 'eldest daughter, the widow Lisbeth Rasmusdatter Hasbaane[?]' fits. (2) Borni Rasmusdatter, unmarried, of Botnen, engaged 9 Nov 1805 and married 2 Jan 1806 at Lavik church to Hans Jenssøn, bachelor, of Øvre Værholmen. (3) Randi Rasmusdatter, unmarried, of Botnen, engaged 5 Dec 1807 and married 1 Jan 1808 to Lasse Bendixsøn, bachelor, of Ytre Hiertholm; the probate's 'Randi with husband Lasse Bendixsen Holmaas' fits. (4) Niels Rasmussen of Botnen married Ragnilda Iversdatter of Tøfting on 24 Jun 1810 in Lindås parish; the probate's 'second son Niels Rasmussen Tofting[?] in Lindås' fits. (5) Brithe Rasmusdatter, unmarried, of Botnen, engaged 29 May 1814 and married 3 Jul 1814 to Hans Joensøn, bachelor, of Høye. ¶16

Parents (Confirmed): Probate no. 45, held at Botten on 22-23 Sep 1779 after Lisbet Rasmusdatter, divides her estate between her widower Iver Monsen and their children, among them the second son 'Rasmus Iversen boende paa … Botten', of age and present. This names his parents as Iver Monsen and Lisbet Rasmusdatter and supersedes the note that their patronymics were not proved. Probate no. 46 (25 Sep 1779) adds that Iver was to live with Rasmus at Botten and that Rasmus was guardian of his half-sister Eli. Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll A 9b, pp. 1041-1044. ¶18

Life story

Baptism: Baptised 24 Feb 1750 in Gulen parish as 'Iver Botnens B[arn] N[omine] Rasmus, Mat[er]: Lispet', that is, Iver Botnen's child named Rasmus, mother Lisbeth. Godparents (testes): Madame Sommer, Johannes in Taskle, Per in Taskle, Barbra [surname uncertain] of (Mid-)Taskle and Eli Johansdatter of the same farm. The mother was churched (introduced, her first church visit after the birth) on 30 Mar 1750. Gulen ministerialbok 1749-1776, fol. 8. ¶8

Betrothal (trolovelse): On Whit Sunday, 30 May 1773, at Brekke church, 'Rasmus Iwersen Botnen og Synneve Olsd: Hiertholm' were betrothed. Cautioners (forlovere, sureties that there was no impediment to the marriage): Colbein of Hiertholm and Johannes of Taskle, both signing with marks. Gulen ministerialbok 1749-1776, fol. 163. ¶12

Marriage: Married (Copul.) at Brekke church on 31 Dec 1773: 'Dito Rasmus og Synneve Botnen', the third of three couples married that day (the others: Jon and Flora of Midtaskle; Torsten and Siri of Engeset). The entry gives no patronymics or witnesses; the identification rests on the betrothal of 30 May 1773. Gulen ministerialbok 1749-1776, fol. 167. ¶13

Godparent: As 'Rasmus Botnen' he stood godfather to Brite, daughter of Colbein Hiertholm (spring 1775), and to Brite, daughter of Bendix Hiertholm (2 Feb 1776), which shows the Botnen-Hjartholm ties of his wife's family. No baptism of a child of his was found from Jan 1774 to Jun 1776 (Gulen ministerialbok 1749-1776, canvases 163-188 read in full). ¶15

Tax-paying farmer (skattebonde) at Botnen. In the 1801 census he is 52, in his first marriage, with wife Synneve Joensdatter. Children: Bornie (23), Randi (21), Johanne (19), Brite (17), Iver (16), Niels (13), Lasse (9). Children's baptisms 1779-1793 name him as Rasmus Iverssøn of Botnen. ¶1

Probate puzzle: the 'eldest daughter Lisbeth' is not in the 1801 Botnen household, where the daughters are Bornie 23, Randi 21, Johanne 19 and Brite 17. The deceased daughter, mother of the Hauge children, is probably Johanne or Brite. A Lisbeth Rasmusdatter, 26, was a servant at Brekke Yttre in 1801; she is not linked. ¶6

Buried 1837 as a retired farmer ('kaaremand') at Botnen, b. 1750. Probate 1837-1840 (Ytre Sogn). His father was probably named Iver; his own baptism (about 1749) was not found because the early Gulen registers are not indexed. ¶2

Probate: The probate court (skifteretten) sat on 18 Nov 1837 at the court-meeting place Va[d?]holmen to settle the estate of the late kaarmand (retired farmer on a farm pension) Rasmus Iversen Botten. A registration, valuation and auction had been held on 23 Oct 1837. The estate was settled by agreement (Accordat) and closed on 5 Jan 1838 by the district judge (sorenskriver) Cappelen. Ytre Sogn sorenskriveri, skifteprotokoll A 20a, fol. 111a-111b, entry no. 12/1838. ¶3

Probate: The estate held only movables (løsøre), sold at auction for 35 speciedaler 14 skilling. After costs of 5 Spd 14 ß, 30 Spd were divided. The estate holder, his son Iver Rasmussen Botten, took every lot and paid out the heirs. No land is listed. No widow takes a share, although his widow Synneve Joensdatter was alive (she died in Dec 1838), which fits her living on a farm pension (kår). ¶4

Probate heirs: eldest son Iver Rasmussen Botten 6 Spd; second son Niels Rasmussen Tofting[?] in Lindås 6 Spd; third son Lasse Rasmussen Holmaas in Lindås 6 Spd; eldest daughter, the widow Lisbeth Rasmusdatter Hasbaane[?], 3 Spd; second daughter Bornie Rasmusdatter, with husband Hans Iversen Raandahl[?], 3 Spd; third daughter Randi Rasmusdatter, with husband Lasse Bendixsen Holmaas, 3 Spd; and the children of a deceased daughter, who share her 3 Spd: John Hansen Hauge 1 Spd and Rasmus Hansen Hauge 1 Spd (curator and guardian Hans Johnsen Hauge), Synneve Hansdatter (guardian Iver Rasmussen Botten) and Anne Hansdatter (guardian Hans Johnsen Hauge). The readings marked [?] are uncertain on a binarised scan. Witnesses: B. Nielsen, O. J. Omnstad, H. Rasmussen. ¶5

Probate cross-check: The 1837 probate's deceased daughter is probably Brithe (married 1814 to Hans Joensøn of Høye): her heirs are John and Rasmus Hansen Hauge and Synneve and Anne Hansdatter, with Hans Johnsen Hauge as their curator and guardian. Hans Joensøn and Hans Johnsen are the same name, Høye and Hauge are plausibly the same farm, and the children Rasmus and Synneve carry the names of Brithe's parents. Unresolved: the probate gives Bornie's husband as 'Hans Iversen Raandahl[?]', while her 1806 husband was Hans Jenssøn of Øvre Værholmen; either the probate reading is wrong or Bornie remarried. Daughter Johanne (19 in 1801) has no marriage in the Gulen index 1795-1825 and is not in the probate. The marriages are read from the Digitalarkivet index only, not the scans, and the index gives no fathers; each daughter's parentage rests on the Botnen residence, the patronymic and the probate. ¶17

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Confidence: Upgraded from Probable to Confirmed. The 1837 probate, a primary record, names Iver Rasmussen Botten as the eldest son and heir of the kaarmand Rasmus Iversen Botten. This agrees with the 1801 census (Iver, joint son, in Rasmus's household at Botnen) and the 1786 baptism. His own parentage is still unknown; the probate gives no origin. ¶7

The case for this identification 1

Identity of the 1750 baptism: Confirmed. This is the only Rasmus son of an Iver baptised in Gulen between 28 Jun 1749 and Oct 1751 (11443 canvases 1-20 read in full; the other Rasmuses were sons of [Jeßling?] Ede and Hans Knudsen Vatne), and no Iver Botnen child named Rasmus appears in Jan 1748 - Jun 1749 (11442 canvases 62-73). The child's farm (Botnen) and father's name (Iver) match the groom 'Rasmus Iwersen Botnen' of 1773 and the farmer Rasmus Iverssen of Botnen in 1801. The date fits the burial index's birth year 1750 and roughly fits the 1801 census age 52 (strictly he was 50 or 51 in Feb 1801; 1801 ages are often a year or two out). Taskle people stand as godparents both in 1750 and as forlover (Johannes Taskle) at his betrothal in 1773. ¶10

Corrections 2

Correction: The birth moves from 'ABT 1749' to 'BEF 24 FEB 1750', from the baptism. The earlier note that his baptism 'was not found because the early Gulen registers are not indexed' is superseded: the register is unindexed, but the scan was read page by page. ¶9

Correction: The marriage date moves from 'ABT 1778' to 31 Dec 1773 (Brekke church), from the betrothal and marriage entries above, read from the scans. ¶14
