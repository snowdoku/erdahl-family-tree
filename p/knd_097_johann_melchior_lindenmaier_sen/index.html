About the family

Father of Johann Melchior Lindenmaier the younger (see his page). The groom row of the younger man's marriage at Illingen on 28 Apr 1803 gives relatives 'Johann Melchior, Louisa Catharina' (father, then bride; roles not labelled; Ancestry 61023 record 8261325, image 1187140-00632). Probable: roles unlabelled, and the 1803 groom is identified with Luise Catharina's father by name, wife and date across parishes. ¶1

Parents entered: the groom row of 8 Feb 1774 names his father Nicolaus (record 7053963). His mother is entered as Catharina Schopf, the only wife of Nicolaus Lindenmaier found (married 1737; children with Catharina 1738-1761). His own baptism (about 1745-50) is not indexed: the Bietigheim baptisms of the 1740s-50s are only partly indexed. ¶5

Life story

Probably the Johann Melchior Lindenmaier of Bietigheim who married Elisabetha Regina 'Renters' (daughter of Jacob) at Bietigheim on 8 Feb 1774 (record 907053963, image 1184901-00062); their children in the index: Johann Jacob (buried 7 Sep 1777), Beata Friederica (bapt. 28 May 1782, buried 18 Dec 1782) and Johann Friedrich (buried 18 Sep 1847, parents Johann Melchior x Elisabetha). Not entered as a link: the younger Johann Melchior's baptism (about 1775-80) is not in the index. Speculative identification. ¶2

If he is the 1774 groom: the groom row of 8 Feb 1774 reads 'Johann Melchior Lindenmajer', relatives 'Nicolaus, Elisabetha Regina' (father, then bride; record 7053963). Nicolaus Lindenmaier of Bietigheim and his wife Catharina had children baptised there 1738-1761 (Christina Catharina 1738, Maria Barbara 1739, Sophia Rosina 1759, Nicolaus 1761), and further children married there: Christoph Jacob (1764), Catharina (1777, to a Melchior) and Rosina Catharina (1783). Not entered as people: the chain 1803 groom's father = 1774 groom rests on name and place only. The baptisms of the younger Johann Melchior (about 1775-80) and of this man (about 1745-50) would settle it (S0230). ¶3

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed, graded on his link to his son Johann Melchior Lindenmaier the younger (see his page). (1) FamilySearch, 'Deutschland, ausgewählte evangelische Kirchenbücher 1500-1971' (= 'Germany, Lutheran Baptisms, Marriages, and Burials, 1500-1971'), QPGR-8VHN: marriage at Illingen 28 Apr 1803, groom Johann Melchior Lindenmaier, field Father = 'Johann Melchior Lindenmaiers', Wife 'Louisa Catharina Fincken'. (2) FamilySearch, 'Germany, Births and Baptisms, 1558-1898', V4L9-4ZJ: Gottlob Melchior Lindenmajer, christened Bietigheim 20 Aug 1809, Father 'Johann Melchior Lindenmajer', Mother 'Louisa Catharina Finck', Paternal Grandfather's Name 'Johann Melchior Lindenmajer'. Both labelled records name him as father of the tree's younger Johann Melchior, the husband of Louisa Catharina Finck. Identity with the Bietigheim groom of 8 Feb 1774 (x Elisabetha Regina Render, son of Nicolaus) is still Probable, not part of this grade: the younger man's baptism V4L9-W4H (Bietigheim 13 Sep 1778, Father 'Johann Melchior Lindenmajer', Mother 'Elisabetha Reuter') and his remarriage VCYQ-S6T (birth 1778 Bietigheim, previously married, parents 'Johann Melchior Lindenmaier, Elisabetha Reitter') fit, but the remarriage is dated 22 Feb 1813 in the Lutheran index (QPGG-ZGWB) and 1814 in 'Germany, Marriages', while his first wife was buried 19 Sep 1813 (QPGL-X2R5). ¶6a

The case for this identification 2

Identification strengthened (still Probable): Johann Melchior Lindenmaier, son of 'Johann Melchior, Elisabetha', married Rosina Catharina 'Frienzlin' (daughter of Johann Gustav and Johanna Maria) at Bietigheim on 22 Feb 1813 (groom row 7054597, bride row 907054597, image 1184901-00191); their son Friderich Ludwig Melchior was buried there 12 Sep 1815 (record 7062944, parents 'Joh Melchior, Rosina Cathar'; this burial belongs to the 1813 couple, not to Louisa Catharina Finck). The burial of Johann Friedrich Lindenmaier, 18 Sep 1847, also names 'Johann Melchior, Elisabetha' (record 7233096). So the Bietigheim couple Johann Melchior x Elisabetha Regina (m. 1774) had a son Johann Melchior. The 1803 Illingen groom (Luise Catharina's father, son of a Johann Melchior) was very probably that son, remarried in 1813 after Louisa Catharina's death (her burial not indexed), or else a brother of the same name (unlikely while both lived). In the whole Bietigheim index (about 60 Lindenmaier rows) no other Johann Melchior of fathering age about 1775-80 appears (others: Joh Melchior x Kunigunde 1693, the Joh Melchior of 1711-1725, and a child buried 1750). Elimination and naming, so Probable. ¶4

Identification settled, grade unchanged (already Confirmed on his son): Bietigheim marriage register 1814, entry 3 (image read): groom 'Johann Melchior Lindenmaier', Bürger und Schuster allhier, born 12 Sept 1778 zu Bietigheim, status 'Wit[wer] der Louise Catharina geb. Fink'; his parents 'Johann Melchior Lindenmaier, B[ürger] und Schuster allhier' and 'Elisabetha geb. Reitter(in)'; bride 'Rosina Catharina geb. Kienzlin', ledig, born 13 Nov 1778 zu Hofeneck, daughter of the late Johann Caspar Kienzlin and Johanna Maria geb. Luz; married 22 Feb 1814; dispensation of the banns dated 19 Febr. 1814. The entry stands under the year heading 1814 on the page headed 1813/1814. So the tree's younger Johann Melchior (widower of Louise Catharina Fink) is the boy born 12 Sep 1778 (baptism V4L9-W4H, parents Johann Melchior Lindenmajer x Elisabetha Reuter), and his father was a master shoemaker of Bietigheim, alive in 1814. The 1774 marriage image (entry 3, 8 Feb 1774) reads 'Johann Melchior Lindenmajer, Meister des Schuhmacher Handwerks, weil. Nicolaus Lindenmajers Bürger u. Kupferschmids allhier ehel. led. Sohn, und Elisabetha Regina, Jacob Reuters [or Renters] Bürger und Weingärtners allhier ehel. led. Tochter'. Same name, same trade (shoemaker), same wife Elisabetha (Regina), same town, 4 years before the 1778 baptism; no other Johann Melchior Lindenmaier of fathering age at Bietigheim. He is therefore the 1774 groom, son of the late Nicolaus. The earlier notes that call this identification Probable or cite the 1813/1814 conflict are superseded. Image read signed in on FamilySearch, 2 Nov 2026. ¶7
