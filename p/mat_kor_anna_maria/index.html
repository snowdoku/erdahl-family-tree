Life story

Baptism index: born 29 Jul 1875 (Old Style) at Friedenstal, child of Ludwig Knodel and Wilhelmine Tarnowsky/Farowsky (St Petersburg Consistory extract, FHL film 1884121/3 p.202 no.32). A probable elder sibling of Gottlieb Knodel (b. 1879); probable because Gottlieb's own identification with this family is probable. Later life unknown. ¶1

Baptism, the register image (read Oct 2026, S0154): born 29 July and baptised 3 August 1875 (Old Style) at Friedenstal, daughter of 'Ludwig Knodel, Ausländer' (a foreign national) and his wife Wilhelmine [T/F]arowsky, both Evangelical Lutheran. Godparents: Gottlieb Knodel, Anna Maria Gall and Friederike E?nzminger (reading uncertain, probably Enzminger), settlers (Ansiedler). St Petersburg Consistory duplicate, FHL 1884121/3 (DGS 4280537, image 196), p.202, entry 32. ¶4
