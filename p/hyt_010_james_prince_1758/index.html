Life story

Birth: 'James Son of Job Prince and Elizabeth his Wife', born 20 February 1758 (Boston Births, Record Commissioners vol. 24, p. 295, read from the page image). ¶1

Estate of his sister: by R. P. Murray's account of Suffolk probate docket 23931, James Prince paid his sister Elizabeth Creed her share of their father's estate before 1812 (cited, not read). His father Job Prince and mother Elizabeth are named in the birth record; that the mother is Elizabeth Allen is inferred from the 1748 marriage. His link to Elizabeth Creed is Probable, resting on that cited docket. ¶2

Probate: the Suffolk probate index lists a James Prince administration in 1818 (docket 25454) and a James Prince will in 1821 (docket 26344); whether either is this James is not established (Index to the probate records of the County of Suffolk, vol. 3, p. 59). ¶3

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
