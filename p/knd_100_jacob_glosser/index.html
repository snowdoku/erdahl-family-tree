About the family

Father of Maria Jacobina Glosser (see her page). ¶1a

Family at Enzweihingen: as Johann Jacob Glott/Gloss/Bloss x Anna Maria he baptised Maria Jacobina 4 Dec 1719 (4606317), Johann Jacob 18 Jan 1722 (4606374), Maria Elisabetha 28 Jan 1723 (4606405), Eva Maria 29 Jan 1724 (4606431) and Anna Maria 20 Apr 1731 (4606656). The surname is uncertain between Gloß/Glosser and Bloß (both readings occur for the same couple). Possible marriage, Speculative and not entered: 'Hanß Jacob Schlosß' (perhaps a misreading of Gloß), son of Martin, married Anna Maria Schmidgall, daughter of Hanß Adam, at Enzweihingen in 1719 (groom row 4607563, bride row 904607563, image 1184569-00135). ¶2a

Life story

Glosser vs Schloss, not settled: no Schloß/Schlosser rows are indexed at Enzweihingen apart from the 1719 groom 'Hanß Jacob Schlosß' (4607563), while Glosser/Gloß/Bloß rows are common there: Anna Catharina 'Glosser', daughter of Nicolas, married 1711 (904607515, image 1184569-00132); 'Hß. Jacob Bloß', relative 'Jacob', buried 1723 (5160223, image 1184570-00735), probably the son Johann Jacob 'Gloss' baptised 18 Jan 1722 to Johann Jacob x Anna Maria (4606374); 'Burens Glosser' buried 1731 (5160444) and 'Bergermeister Bloß' buried 1733 (5160507), perhaps one burgomaster Gloß. This makes 'Schlosß' a likely misreading of 'Gloß', but nothing in the index ties the 1719 groom to a father Martin Gloß (no Martin of any of these spellings is indexed at Enzweihingen), and Nicolaus Glosser (living 1711) is an equally possible father. Kept Speculative; no father entered. ¶4a
