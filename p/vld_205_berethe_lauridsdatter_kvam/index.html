About the family

Mother of Kari Endresdatter (see her page), Confirmed; graded on her link to Kari. Primary: the skifte after her held at Nordre Qvam in Lesjaskogen on 3 Sep 1708 (Nord-Gudbrandsdal skifteprotokoll 1690-1719, source 25155, s. 73b, view 78; probate card 39945 image 7527) is held 'efter Sl. Birete Lauridzd: og imellem hendes efterlatte Mand Ender Olufss: og deris 6 Børn Nemblig Oluf, Lauridz og Birete Endress: samt Elli, Karri og Magnild Endresd:' - 'deris' (their) names her as the mother of Kari by Endre. Continuity to the tree's Kari: the skifte after her widower 'Endre Qvam' at Kvam on 17 May 1726 (fol. 346b) lists the same six children, Ole, Lars, Berthe, Eli, Kari and Magnhild, with '5. Kari Endresdatter Knud Rolfsen Slettums Hustru'. The gap is 18 years, but the father and the whole sibling set appear together at both ends on the same farm (audit 8 rule 3, pair match; audit 9 rule 2), and no other Kari Endresdatter of Kvam is known (no rival). ¶1

Life story

Name: the 1708 protocol writes 'Birete Lauridzd.', the card 'Bethe Lauridzdtr.', the bygdebok 'Brit Larsd.' (Berethe/Birte/Brit are forms of one name). Her own origin: the bygdebok has 'kanskje frå' followed by a word not recovered from the rebuild. Test record for her parents: a Lauritz/Lars probate in the 1673-1708 protocols naming a daughter Berethe married to Endre Olsen Kvam (card index 39945). ¶2
