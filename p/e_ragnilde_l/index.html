About the family

Wife of Rasmus Jørgensen Erdal and mother of Lasse Rasmussen (confirmed by her probate, 29 Apr 1783). ¶1

Parentage Probable. In 1740 Rasmus Jørgensen was said to have married 'Lasse Floes Datter' about 1738, and Ragnilde is the only Lasse-daughter recorded as his wife. Her daughter Brithe later lived at Nedre Flo, and her first grandson there was named Lasse. ¶2

Life story

Probate of her father: in the probate after Lasse Rasmussen of Nedre Flo, held 10 October 173[1 or 2] (skifteprotokoll L0007, fol. 151), she is named last among the children as 'Ragnilde Lassesdatter, now 19 years old', which puts her birth about 1712/13. Her siblings were Aamund, Ole, Peder and Rasmus Lassesen, and Mallene and Helge Lassesdatter (both married by then). Her father's widow was [?]gne Aamundsdatter, probably her mother (see Malene Aamundsdatter). This raises her parentage from Probable: see Lasse Rasmussen Flo. ¶3

Aaland 1932: Aaland's Erdal history names Rasmus Jørgensen's wife 'Ragnh. Lassed. N.-Flo, † 70 a. g. 1783', Ragnhild Lassesdatter of Nedre Flo, died 1783 aged 70. This agrees with her burial and probate. Aaland's Nedre Flo history lists her father Lasse Rasmussen (holder from 1716, died 1733 aged 51) and his wife Malene Aamundsdatter. ¶4
