About the family

Wife of Egild Ormsen at Tvedt in the 1801 census, aged 49. Her granddaughter Gunbjør Ormsdatter was evidently named after her. ¶1

Father (Probable): Toruv Andersen Hiemdal, who was at Hiemdal in 1801 aged 81 (b. ~1720), a widower after his 2nd marriage, living with his son Anders Toruvsen (56). As a widower he remarried Liv Halvorsdatter Homme in 1775. Gundbjør's patronymic and farm match, but no baptism has been found. ¶3

Mother added: Gunild Olsdatter (see her page). 'Gundbiør Torofsdatter' is an heir with Torof Andersen in the skifte after Gunild Olsdatter on søndre Hiemdahl (between Oct 1773 and 1779; Panteregister I 1 fol. 318, DA 10915 view 361). The mother is graded on her link to Hæge; this link rests on the same abstract and is no stronger. ¶5

Life story

Of Hiemdal when betrothed to Egild Ormsen Tvedt (6 Jun 1775; married 10 Nov 1775, Treungen). ¶2

Named in the skifte after her mother Gunild Olsdatter, søndre Hiemdahl, 15 Feb 1775 (DA 25675 fol. 827a, No. 71, view 831): 'Gundbiør Torrofsdatter 23 aar' (so born about 1751-52), guardian Joen Sigurdsøn Hiemdahl; she was then still unmarried. Daughter of Gunild Olsdatter and Torrof Andersøn. ¶6

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: Confirmed as the mother of Orm Egildsen. The 1801 census of Tvedt in Treungen (household 05) lists Orm Egildsen, 12, among the children ('Deres børn', their children) of the householder Egild Ormsen, 50, farmer and innkeeper at Tvedsund, and his wife Gundbiør Toruvsdatter, 49, both in their first marriage. Orm married on 3 Nov 1817 as Orm Egilsen of Tvedt, about 28, which matches the census by patronymic, farm and age, and he named children Gundbjør and Torof after the couple. The census states the relationship, so the link to Orm (see his page) is Confirmed; the earlier 'Probable' for Orm's parentage rested on this same census. Her father Toruv Andersen Hiemdal stays Probable. ¶4
