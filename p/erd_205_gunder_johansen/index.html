About the family

Father of Gjertrud Gundersdatter (see her page). At the registration of 17 Nov 1744 at Frøyum, Stjørdal, after David Andersen, between 'hans efterladte Enke Gjertrud Gundersdatter' and their only child, a daughter 10 weeks old, the court appointed as the infant's guardian (formynder) 'Morfaderen Gunder Johans[en]' - the child's mother's father (Stjør- og Verdal skifteprotokoll 3, fol. 102b, last lines from the scan). 'Morfaderen' and 'Gunder' are clear; the patronymic is abbreviated ('Johansl.' or 'Johans.'), and the word after it, probably his farm, reads like 'Hamre', i.e. very likely Hemre in Hegra. Graded on his link to Gjertrud: Confirmed, because the same probate record names Gjertrud as the widow and mother and names him as her child's morfar, and her patronymic Gundersdatter agrees; there is no rival. (The infant's identity with Lisbet Davidsdatter of the tree belongs to Gjertrud's grade, which stays Probable.) ¶1

Life story

Bygdebok: Hegra gårds- og slektshistorie B. 1, Hembre Øvre, canvas 183 (rebuilt from word boxes): 'Gunnar Johansen Hembre, [f.] 1688, gravlagt 20/5 1760. Fekk skjøte på [garden]...; Gift 13/7 1718 m. Ingeborg...', son of Johan Olsen (b. about 1659, buried 4 Apr 1717, from Hembre Nedre) and Gjertrud. Children: Johan (b. 1719, the next brukar, buried 9 Dec 1763, who had a deed from his father 25 Jul 1746 and married 1745 [Berit] Andersdatter, b. 29 Oct 1724, buried 8 May 1764), Gjertrud (b. 16 Jan 1721, m. David Andersen, Stjørdal), Randi (b. 1723), Ingeborg (b. 1731, buried 1760, unmarried), a son at Skatval (b. 23 Nov 1733), and others. Hegra B. 2 (Frøyum, canvas 418) likewise calls Gjertrud 'Gjertrud Gunnarsdtr. Hembre' and names 'Johan Gunnarsen Hembre' with her. This turns the earlier inference into a bygdebok statement: Johan Gundersen of Øvre Hemre (probate 10 Jan 1764, 24755 fol. 385) was his son, and Johan's widow Berit Andersdatter (probate 1764, 24755 fol. 414b) matches the bygdebok's 'Andersdtr.' b. 1724, buried May 1764. The farbror Gunder Gundersen of 1764 is evidently the Skatval son b. 1733. Grade unchanged (Confirmed on his link to Gjertrud, via the 1744 probate). His new parents are graded on their own records (Probable). ¶9

Born before about 1700, since his daughter Gjertrud was born about 1721 (burial 1786, aged about 65). ¶2

His marriage is read - 16368 canvas 53, 13 Jul 1718 at Hegra, 'Copul. Gunder Johansen Hemra og Ingeborg Rollofsd. Bjørgaard'; his daughter Gjertrud's baptism, 16368 canvas 83, 2 Mar 1721, 'Gunder Hemræs b. Gjertrud, Mod. Ingborg' (godparents Lars Hemra, Rollof Bjørgaard, Kari Larsd. Hemra); and he stood godfather ('Gunder og Ranni Hemre') to his granddaughter Lisbet, daughter of the late David Frøyum, 20 Sep 1744 (16370 canvas 58). His sister Mali Johansdatter Hemra married Henrik Olsen on Trinity Sunday 1718 (16368 canvas 52). ¶12

Land records: the Stjør- og Verdal panteregister 1726-1765 (source 22315, images 61-62, under G, Stjørdalen) has a man of exactly this name at Hemre: 'Gunder Johansen Hemres Skiøde paa Øver Hemre udg. af Borgemester Hans Hagerup' (he bought Øvre Hemre from Burgomaster Hans Hagerup) and 'Gunder Johansen Øver Hemres udg. Pante Obligation paa Øvre Hemre for 200 rd' (both pantebok A-1 fol. 242). The deed itself (pantebok A-1 fol. 242b, source 19750 view 245): Hans Hagerup, burgomaster of Trondhjem, conveys to 'Dannemand Gunder Johansen og hans Arvinger' the farm (Øvre Hemre) of 1 spann 1 øre with bygsel in Stjørdal, which Gunder Johansen already occupied, for 200 rd, dated Trondhjem 19 Dec 1737; it is followed by Gunder Johansen's mortgage of 100 rd to the merchant Hendrich Minck on the same farm, Dec 1737. Neither names his wife or children, and an earlier 'Gunder Johansen Hemres med flere Revers', concerning Jørgen Peder Brunsman's guardianship (fol. 173-174, c.1735). So Gunder Johansen was owner-occupier of Øvre Hemre, Hegra (Stjørdal prestegjeld), by about 1740, which fits the farm written after his name in 1744. The dragoon Johan Gundersen of Øvre Hemre (d. by Jan 1764; children's guardian their farbror Gunder Gundersen) was evidently his successor there, and probably his son; inference only. ¶7

Re-check of the 1744 Frøyum probate at full resolution (24753 view 123, fol. 102b, last lines): the guardian is written 'Morfaderen Gunder Johansl. Hamre' (or 'Johans.'); 'Morfaderen' is clear (Mor-faderen), and the farm reads Hamre, the usual old spelling of Hemre. The earlier reading stands. Graded link unchanged (Confirmed, on his link to his daughter Gjertrud Gundersdatter). ¶6

Burial read from the register, 16371 canvas 55 left: 'd. 20de May [1760] Hegre Pastor... grafsat Gunder Johansen Hembre 7[?] aar' (age's second digit not legible at 1600 px). This confirms the bygdebok's 'gravlagt 20/5 1760' from the primary record. ¶14

Identity with Gunder Jonsen, owner of Frøyum 1762-1780, is OPEN. For: Jon and Johan were used interchangeably; Gjertrud lived and died (1786) as a lodger on Frøyum; a Gunder Joensen whose farm reads like Frøyum was a guardian at the 1764 Hemre probate of Johan Gundersen; and in the 1780s Joen Gundersen Frøyum was guardian of Karen Joensdatter Hembre (Hemre) (Stjørdal panteregister 22318 image 19, afkald C fol. 432). ¶4a

Name: farm Hemre added on the evidence below; patronymic unchanged. ¶5

Probably also father of the dragoon Johan Gundersen of Øvre Hemre, Hegra (d. by Jan 1764; his son, the eldest heir, was named Gunder Johansen, and his children's guardian was 'deres Farbroder Gunder Gundersen', so Johan was a son of a Gunder). Inference only: Johan's probate (skifteprotokoll 5, fol. 385a-386a, 10 Jan 1764) does not name his father, but the farm Hemre fits the reading 'Hamre' in 1744. One of Johan's children's guardians was a Gunder Joensen whose farm reads like Frøyum (the Gunder Jonsen who owned Frøyum 1762-1780). ¶3

The case for this identification 2

Identity with Gunder Jonsen of Frøyum (owner 1762-1780): now unlikely but not excluded. Every record of the Hemre man calls him Johansen and places him at (Øvre) Hemre in c.1735-1744, while the Frøyum owner is Jonsen/Joensen; the Frøyum man was still making a retirement contract with his son Jon in 1780, whereas the Hemre man was a grandfather in 1744 and so born before about 1700; and at the 1764 Hemre probate the Frøyum Gunder Joensen acted only as a guardian, not as farfar. The 1701 manntall for Stjørdal fogderi is not in Digitalarkivet's person index (searches for Gunder at Hem*/Fr* in 1701 return only other districts), so it was not checked; it needs the scan. ¶8a

Identity with Gunder Jonsen of Frøyum: now RESOLVED as two men. Hegra B. 2 (Frøyum, canvas 418) gives the Frøyum owner as 'Gunnar Johnsen Lien Skatval', who took over Frøyum after Anders Davidsen in 1745 and bought it in 1763, with a wife b. 1710 (buried 25 Jan 1784) and children b. 1746-1763 including John Gunnarsen (b. 20 Jun 1756, later brukar). Gunder Johansen Hemre (b. about 1688, buried 1760) is a different man. ¶10

Records that disagree 1

Against: that Gunder handed Frøyum to 'min kiære Søn Jon Gundersen' by deed of 26 Aug 1775 and made a retirement contract with him on 14 Jan 1780 that provides for his wife (pantebok B-2 fol. 434b; C-1 fol. 225b-226a), which suggests a man born about 1710-20 rather than the father of a woman born 1721; and he is not called Farfar at the 1764 Hemre probate. Neither document names a daughter Gjertrud in the parts I could read. ¶4b

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Distinct from Gunder Trøite. At the baptism of Gunder Trøite's daughter Berit, 29 Sep 1720 (16368 c78), 'Gunder Hemra' stands as godfather while 'Gunder Trøite' is the father, wife Mali. The Trøite man is 'Gunder Rollufsen Trøita' in the 1743 trolovede list (16370 c260). His daughter Gjertrud is 'Geertrud Gundersd. Hemre' at her betrothal to David Andersen Frøyum, 2 Oct 1743 (16370 c262). ¶13

Searches that found nothing 1

No probate after him (d. 1760) or his wife Ingeborg is in the typed Stjør- og Verdal registers 24753/24755 under Hemre; the 77804 card index (1689-1742) has a single Hemre card (743: skifte 1 Oct 1733 after Sivert Pedersen, widow Siri Haagensdatter; heir his sister Ingebor Pedersdatter, 60, married; her guardian Gunder Gundersen) - not this family. ¶11
