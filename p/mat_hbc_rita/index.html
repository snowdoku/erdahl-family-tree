Life story

First marriage, BC 39-09-479609: Edith Rita Hayter, aged 21, United Church, of Summerland, born Gaspereaux, Prince Edward Island; father Benjamin Hayter, born 'Mary River' (Murray River), PEI; mother Lydia A. Hicken, born Gaspereaux. She married Alavander Eric Elof Borgstrom, a truck driver of Summerland born at Ferintosh, Alberta, on 7 October 1939 at West Summerland, by licence, Rev. W. R. Ashford (United Church) officiating. The witnesses were her sister Winnifred Hayter of Penticton and Howard Shannon (reading uncertain) of Summerland. A note typed on the registration records that the marriage was dissolved at Penticton on 26 May 1950. ¶2

PEI records: born 19 September 1919 (PEI baptism index); baptised in PEI 3/5 December 1933; in the 1931 census of Gaspereaux as Rita, daughter, 11, student. ¶5

Summerland life 1946-1951 (Summerland Review): she was secretary of the women's bowling league in 1948-49, worked at the Summerland Co-operative packing house in 1950, and lived in Victoria Gardens, West Summerland. ¶7

Second marriage: after 1950 she married Donald Hermiston. That marriage is too recent for the public BC index. Her death record calls her divorced, with Donald Hermiston as her former spouse. ¶3

Second marriage: the Summerland Review of 3 January 1952 reported that on Saturday evening, 29 December 1951, 'Edith Rita Borgstrom, daughter of Mrs. D. Hayter, Penticton, and the late Mr. Hayter' married James Donald Hermiston, son of Mrs. H. Hermiston of West Summerland and the late Mr. Hermiston. Rev. H. R. Whitmore officiated, and 'the bride was given in marriage by her brother, Mr. Cecil Hayter of Kaleden.' At the reception in her home in Victoria Gardens her sister Mrs. Verner Thomsen of Naramata received the guests; the out-of-town guests included her sisters and brothers-in-law Mr. and Mrs. Thomsen of Naramata and Mr. and Mrs. William Borisenko of Penticton, and her brother and sister-in-law Mr. and Mrs. Hayter of Kaleden. The couple made their home in Summerland. ¶6

Death, BC 1997-09-017380: Edith Rita Hermiston, who had worked in office accounting, of Summerland, died at Summerland General Hospital on 26 August 1997, aged 77, born 19 September 1919 at Gaspereaux, PEI; father Benjamin Hayter, mother Della Hicken. Her son reported the death. She was cremated at Penticton. ¶4
