Life story

The compiled Island Register genealogy of the Prince Edward Island Hickens names an eldest daughter Elizabeth "Eliza", born 29 January 1802, who married John Wise "of Plymouth". This is very probably the same Eliza, the compiler's birth year being off: a sister Jane was baptised at Deal in December 1801, so an Eliza born in January 1802 is impossible. Her marriage to John Wise has not been found in the England marriage index (Ancestry 9852) - it may have taken place on Prince Edward Island. ¶2

Baptism: Eliza, daughter of George and Phebe Hicken, was baptised at St Leonard, Deal, Kent, on 19 August 1804 (FreeREG; also England Births and Christenings index). ¶1

Prince Edward Island: as Eliza Wise she had children baptised at Holy Trinity, Georgetown, to John and Eliza Wise - Mary Anne and Eliza (both 19 March 1844), Robert (born 19 September 1844, baptised 10 February 1845) and John Hecken [Hicken] Wise (25 May 1848) (PEI baptisms/marriages/burials index, FamilySearch film 005240343). Using Hicken as a middle name confirms her as a Hicken. A John Wise born 1804 died on the Island in 1862 (PEI death cards). The compiler's "John Wise of Plymouth" may record his English birthplace. ¶3

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
