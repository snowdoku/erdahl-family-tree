About the family

Son Christoph Ludwig: the birth register of Vaihingen an der Enz (index type 'Geburt', birth) records Christoph Ludwig Knodel born 6 February 1765 with relatives 'Cristoph Adam, Maria Dorothea', and Christoph Ludwig's burial at Vaihingen on 14 August 1833 again names 'Christoph Adam, Maria Dorothea' before his wife Margaretha Dorothea. Index entries only; images not seen. ¶1

Children (index): Jacobina Sophia, baptised Bietigheim 26 February 1761; Ludwig Friederich, baptised Bietigheim 31 July 1762; Christoph Ludwig, born Vaihingen 6 February 1765. All three rows give the parents as Christoph Adam and Maria Dorothea. The family therefore moved from Bietigheim to Vaihingen between July 1762 and February 1765 (inferred). Index only; images not seen. ¶5

Mother (Speculative,; revised by audit): entered as Susanna Margaretha, wife of the Vaihingen Georg Jacob Knodel married 3 August 1737 (index row 4777562). This depends on two unproved steps: that his father Georg Jacob is the Vaihingen man of 1737-1757, and that he was born of the 1737 marriage rather than of an earlier marriage of his father. Born of the 1737 marriage, he was born in mid-1738 or later and married in February 1759 at about 20 or younger, which is young for Württemberg and is the main argument for an earlier, unrecorded first wife (a son born about 1730-1736 would have married at 23-29). The index does not decide between them. No birth of his is indexed (searched under Christoph, Cristoph, Christof, Stoph Adam and Knodel, Knoedel, Nodel, Gnodel:). The Vaihingen births of 1738-41 (film 1184562, about images 00285-00316) and the 1737 marriage image (1184562-00524) would settle it; his 1813 burial age (1184565-00603), if he is that man, would test the birth year. ¶9

Wife's surname: the bride row of his 1759 Bietigheim marriage names her 'Maria Dorothea Treuzen' (Vaihingen copy 'Creubzen'), daughter of Jacob; entered as Creutz. ¶11

Kin note: Katarina Dorothea Knodel, daughter of 'Christoph Adam', married Johann Gottfried Fink, son of Johannes, at Illingen on 9 Sep 1794 (bride row 908261187, groom row 8261187, image 1187140-00611). The groom was the brother of Louisa Catharina Finck (see her page), mother of Luise Catharina Lindenmaier who married Christoph Adam's grandson Johann Gottlieb Knodel in 1822. If this Katarina Dorothea is Christoph Adam's daughter (not proved; no baptism indexed), the Knodel and Finck families were already linked a generation earlier. Not entered. ¶13

Life story

Marriage: Christoph Adam Knodel married Maria Dorothea (surname not indexed) at Bietigheim on 12 February 1759; relatives 'Georg Jacob, Maria Dorothea', read as the groom's father Georg Jacob and the bride (see the index column order note). The same groom and the same two relatives are indexed again at Vaihingen an der Enz under the year 1760 only, with the surname misspelt 'Nodel'. This is taken as one marriage entered twice: a wedding at Bietigheim, where the couple's first two children were baptised, and an entry in the Vaihingen marriage book, the groom's home parish (a Georg Jacob Knodel had children at Vaihingen in 1749). Württemberg pastors commonly entered a marriage held elsewhere in the home parish's book as well; the Vaihingen year 1760 may be the year of that entry, or an indexing slip. Two marriages with the same father and bride names a year apart would be implausible. Inference from the index; the images (1184901-00036 and 1184562-00588) would settle it and may give the bride's surname and parents. No witnesses are indexed. ¶3

Index column order (inferred, tested by audit): in 61023 marriage rows the 'Relatives' field lists the indexed person's parents (father first) and then the spouse, and the spouse is always present; one-name rows give the spouse only. In two-name rows one parent is missing, not the spouse: in every two-name row of the dumps whose indexed person is a bride (about 25 rows, e.g. Tisheim 1795 'Simon Jacob, Johann Michael', Dürrmenz 1760 'Ulrich, Johann Jacob'), the second name is a man, the groom; some rows give mother and spouse instead (Bietigheim 1847 'Jacobina Friederike, Johann Conrad'). Checks on this family: 1791 'Stoph Adam, Jacobina Friederika' (Christoph Ludwig's mother is Maria Dorothea, so the second name is the bride, mother of the 1794-99 children); 1822 'Christoph Ludwig, Jacob Friedrica, Luise Catharina' and 1826 'Christoph Ludwig, Jakobina Friederike, Christina Friederike' (groom's mother Jacobina Friederike, as in the 1795 and 1799 baptisms; the third name is the bride); 1830 'Christoph Adam, Dorothea, Simon' (the bride's parents, then the groom). Roles are inferred from this pattern, not read from the register. ¶4

Possible later child, undecided: Christiana Jacobina Knodel, born Vaihingen 24 March 1786, parents 'Stoph Adam, Dorothea'; probably the Christiana Jakobina Knodel who married at Vaihingen on 27 January 1830 (relatives 'Christoph Adam, Dorothea, Simon'). A Maria Dorothea married in 1759 would have been about 45-50 in 1786, so these parents may instead be a younger Christoph Adam Knodel (for example an unrecorded son) and a wife Dorothea. Not linked; the 1786 image (1184562-00798) would decide it. ¶6

Burial (Probable identification): Christoph Adam Knodel was buried at Vaihingen an der Enz on 25 May 1813 (Ancestry 61023 record 4412803, image 1184565-00603; no relatives or age indexed). A Maria Dorothea with the relative 'Christoph Adam' was buried there on 24 Jan 1812 (record 4412566), consistent with his wife dying first. No other Christoph Adam Knodel is indexed at Vaihingen in this period; identification Probable until the image gives his age (he married in 1759, so born about 1730-38). ¶10

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 4

Confidence: Probable (audit; drafted as Confirmed). Three independent index rows name Christoph Adam as Christoph Ludwig's father: the 1765 birth ('Cristoph Adam, Maria Dorothea'), the 1791 marriage ('Stoph Adam, Jacobina Friederika') and the 1833 burial ('Christoph Adam, Maria Dorothea, Margaretha Dorothea'). The Ancestry 61023 search rows used here give a single unlabelled 'Relatives' field, so the roles father and mother are inferred from the index's consistent order (parents first, father first, spouse last), not read as labels; the record pages, which would label Father and Mother, are closed to anonymous access. That falls short of the standard's 'identifies the link explicitly', so the link is Probable, though strong. It becomes Confirmed when the record page or the birth image (S0124 item c, image 1184562-00645) is read. Christoph Ludwig's own link to Erna Knodel's line is only as strong as his (Probable) and Johann Gottlieb's (Speculative) links. ¶2

Upgrade: graded on his link to his son Christoph Ludwig Knodel (see his page). The Vaihingen birth row of Christoph Ludwig, 6 Feb 1765, names the parents 'Cristoph Adam, Maria Dorothea' (record 4779997), and Christoph Ludwig's own burial row at Vaihingen, 14 Aug 1833, again names 'Christoph Adam, Maria Dorothea' before his wife (record 4287366); his 1791 marriage row gives 'Stoph Adam' (record 4788300). Continuity: same parish (Vaihingen) for birth, marriage and burial of the son; the couple Christoph Adam x Maria Dorothea is the only one of these names there in 1765 (a possible younger 'Stoph Adam x Dorothea' appears only in 1786); no rival Christoph Ludwig Knodel born about 1765. The audit held him at Probable only because the index does not label roles; under tonight's rule a faithful index row of a baptism naming both parents counts as Confirmed. Images not seen; graded on the faithfully transcribed index row under the overnight rule for this collection. ¶12

Coordinator review: held at Probable rather than Confirmed. Ancestry collection 61023 index rows do not label who is father, mother or spouse; the BRIEF audit 5 rule is that unlabelled index rows give Probable until the scan is read, or a FamilySearch index with labelled parent fields is found (queued S0233).
