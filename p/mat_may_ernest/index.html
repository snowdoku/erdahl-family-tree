About the family

Husband of Myrtle 'Sadie' Eidem, married 2 June 1939 at St. Paul Lutheran Church, rural Elk Point, Union County, South Dakota. A farmer; he and Myrtle served on a mission station in Liberia in 1975 and moved from Worthington to St. Cloud, Minnesota in 1991. He died in 2002. ¶1
