About the family

Mother of Peder Hansen of Nordre Einbu (see his page), Probable; graded on her link to Peder. The 1698 skifte after her husband Hans Jacobsen (source 25155 fol. 44a, read) is held between 'hans efterlatte Hustru Karri Sifuersd: og deris 6 umyndige Børn', among them Peder Hanss., so she is named as the mother (audit 8 rule 2 is met: 'deris Børn'). Her own skifte of 23 Jun 1710 (fol. 94b, card 39945/14059, read) is held among 'hendis 6 Børn' with Peder again. Probable only because the step from this Peder to Peder Hansen of Nordre Einbu rests on the bygdebok, age and naming (see Hans Jacobsen Tande); Peder named a daughter Kari. The bygdebok (vol. 3 canvas 99) gives her as 'Kari [gap] frå [gap] (1-4)'; her origin farm is in a gap, and the 1698 entry names a 'Morbroder' of the children (her brother) whose name and farm are faint. ¶1

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
