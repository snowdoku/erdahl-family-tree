Life story

Listed in the 1701 male census as 'Aamund Jørg.', 'Søner' (son), aged 3, the only other person in the household of Jørgen (39) at Alvestad in the Vatne district of Haram. He was named after his paternal grandfather. ¶1

He was still at Alvestad as an adult: 'Aamund Jørgensen' stood as a godparent there on 25 December 1723. ¶2
