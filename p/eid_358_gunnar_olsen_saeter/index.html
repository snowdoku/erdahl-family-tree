Life story

Probate after Gunder Olsen Sætter read: 19 Feb 1727, 24750 fol. 1 (view 8 right; card 77804 c1973). The skifte was held between the widow 'Gjøroe Peders datter' and 'deres tilsammen avlede 4 Børn': Ole Gundersen 31, married, on the farm; 'Sigre Gunders datter Hans Rennaas Hustru'; Ingeborg Gundersdatter, wife of the late Peder Pedersen Kolset; Kirsti Gundersdatter, wife of Thomas Grimsen Rønsberg. This is the test record named in these notes, and it proves Gunder and Goro (Pedersdatter) as the parents of Sigri, wife of Hans Renå, in 1727. It does NOT by itself prove that Sigri is Siri 'Setter' who married Torsten Kjøsnes in 1704 (Siri): that identification (the bygdebok's 'g. I m. Torsten Jonsen Kjøsnes, g. II m. Hans Jonsen Renå') spans 23 years and two farms, and no record of the widow Siri Kjøsnes marrying Hans has been read. Kept Probable on the link to Siri. ¶4a

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Graded Probable on his link to his daughter Siri (see her page), wife of Torsten Kjøsnes. Selbu i fortid og nåtid vol. 6, Sæter 'Brukere' (canvas 378): '1686-1718 Gunnar Olsen [...] ca. 1650 [d.] 1726, [g.m.] Goro [...] døde [i] 1727'; children '[1.] Siri, f. ca. 1680, [g.I m.] Torsten Jonsen Kjøsnes, [g.II m.] Hans Jonsen [Renå]', 'Per, f. ca. 1685, død ugift 1717', 'Kirsti, f. ca. 1687, [g.m.] Tomas [...]', '4. Ingeborg, f. ca. 1690, [tok] over' (holder 1719-1725, m. 1719 Per Persen, drowned 1724), '5. Ola, f. ca. 1697, [g.] 1726 [m.] Gjertrud'. Vol. 7, Kjøsnes (canvas 318) agrees: '1703-1719 Torsten Jonsen, f. ca. 1678, d. 1719 [...] 1704 [m.] Siri [...] Sæter, f. ca. 1680, d. 1744, [g.II m.] Hans Jonsen Renå', children 'Kari, f. 1705, [g.m.] Ola Gunnarsen Overvik', Goro 1707, Jon 1711, Gunnar 1713. The tree has the 1704 marriage of Torsten Kiøsnes to 'Siri Setter' and the 1705 baptism of Kari (16310 c19, c22), so the identification of Siri is good; the father link rests on the book. Naming: Siri's children Goro (1707) and Gunnar (1713) carry her parents' names. ¶1
