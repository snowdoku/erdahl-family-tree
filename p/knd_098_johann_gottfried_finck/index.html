Life story

Marriage: 'Johann Gottfried Finken', son of Jacob, married Maria Catharina 'Hausers' (genitive of Hauser), daughter of Jacob, at Illingen on 22 Apr 1738: groom row 8393752, relatives 'Jacob, Maria Catharina'; bride row 908393752, relatives 'Jacob, Johann Gottfried' (image 1187139-00168). Their first indexed child, Jacob Friderich, followed on 27 Jan 1739 (8393227). Continuity of the couple: the burial of their son Johann Gottfried Finck at Illingen, 24 Apr 1823, names 'Johann Gottfried, Maria Catharina' before his wife (7505918). His own baptism (about 1710-16) is not indexed; Illingen baptisms in the index begin about 1718. ¶4

Marriage image read: Illingen marriage register 1738 (image read), entry of 22 Apr: 'Johann Gottfried, weyl. Jacob Fincken gewesenen Burgers und Krämers allhier ehel. Sohn, mit Maria Catharina, Jacob Hausers Burgers und Gerichtsverwandten allhier ehel. Tochter, copulirt worden'. This replaces the unlabelled Ancestry rows 8393752/908393752 as the source for his marriage and his father. The 1771 marriage image of his son Johannes (Illingen image 346, entry 5) calls him 'H. Joh. Gottfried Finckh', Handelsmann (merchant) at Illingen. Image read signed in on FamilySearch, 2 Nov 2026. ¶6

Graded on that link: the groom row of Johannes's own marriage at Illingen on 30 Apr 1771 reads 'Johannes Finckh', relatives 'Joh. Gottfried, Friderica Magdalena' (father, then bride; record 8396924, image 1187139-00345). Continuity: Johannes and Friederica Magdalena then baptise children at Illingen from 1772 (Johann Gottfried 1772, Louisa Catharina 1774, Georg Carl 1780). Images not seen; graded on the faithfully transcribed index row under the overnight rule for this collection. ¶1b

Coordinator review: held at Probable rather than Confirmed. Ancestry collection 61023 index rows do not label who is father, mother or spouse; the BRIEF audit 5 rule is that unlabelled index rows give Probable until the scan is read, or a FamilySearch index with labelled parent fields is found (queued S0233). The naming evidence in the note above is otherwise strong, so this person is a priority for an image read. ¶3

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed, graded on his link to his son Johannes Finck (see his page). FamilySearch, 'Deutschland, ausgewählte evangelische Kirchenbücher 1500-1971' (= 'Germany, Lutheran Baptisms, Marriages, and Burials, 1500-1971'), record QPGR-XQL7: marriage at Illingen, 30 Apr 1771 (page 17), groom 'Johannes Finckh', field Father = 'Joh. Gottfried Finckhens', Wife = 'Friderica Magdalena Conzelmanns', Father-in-law = 'Mattheus Conzelmanns'. This labelled index names the relationship, so the audit-5 bar for unlabelled Ancestry rows no longer applies. Continuity: the 1771 groom and his wife Friderica Magdalena are the parents of Louisa Catharina, baptised at Illingen 13 Feb 1774 (QPG5-KNXH), the couple in the tree; same parish, 3 years. No rival named. Read signed in, 2 Nov 2026; labelled index fields, image not seen (partner-only). ¶5
