Life story

Born in Boston 21 Sept. 1668, daughter of Capt. Pilgrim and Katharine (Richardson) Simpkins; married Christopher Kilby 20 March 1691; living 1738, when her husband's will left her the personal estate (TAG 27 (1951): 202-203, compiled). Graded Probable on her link to Rebecca Kilby: TAG lists Rebecca (b. 1701) among the children baptised at the Old South Church to Christopher and Sarah; no record read names Sarah as Rebecca's mother. ¶1

Birth: 'Sarah of Pilgrim & Cathar. Symkins born Sept. 21', in the 1668 births of the Boston register; and 'Christopher Kilbey & Sarah Simkins were married Mar. 20' in the 1691 marriages (Record Commissioners vol. 9; OCR read against the year headings). She is the only Sarah Simpkins in the Boston register before 1691. Her parents are entered as Probable, because the birth (1668) and the marriage (1691) are 23 years apart and the identification rests on name and the absence of a rival, with TAG 27:202 as a compiled statement. ¶2

Her own parents (Pilgrim and Katharine Simpkins) are now tied to her by her father's will of 1714, which names 'Sarah Kilbe' among his children, and by her mother's 1721 petition naming 'her beloved son-in-law Christopher Kilby' (TAG 28:88; see Pilgrim Simpkins). Baptised at the Old South Church 15 Dec. 1676 with her siblings Miriam, Rebecca and Thomas, as children of Pilgrim and Katharine (TAG 28:88-89, from the church records). ¶4

1 on the grade · 1 alternative set aside

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: graded on her link to Rebecca Kilby. The Boston town register of births for 1701 (Record Commissioners vol. 24, printed transcript of the town record) enters 'Rebecca Daughter of Christopher Kelbey and Sarah his Wife'; the date column of the OCR aligns this entry with 27 December 1701, the date TAG 27:203 gives for Rebecca's birth (baptised Old South 28 Dec. 1701). The record names Sarah as Rebecca's mother. Continuity: 'Christopher Kilbey & Sarah Simkins were married Mar. 20' 1691 (vol. 9); the couple's children are entered in the town register as of Christopher and Sarah without a break from 1694 to 1712 (Nathaniel 1694, John 1699, Rebecca 1701, Samuel 1706, Sarah 1708, Mary 1712); Christopher's will of 1738 names his wife Sarah and 'my Daughter Rebecca Allen' (already Confirmed, Christopher Kilby). ¶3a

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

No rival: the only other Christopher Kilbys in Boston were his nephews Christopher (b. 1693, d. 1698) and Christopher (b. 1705), neither a father in 1701. ¶3b
