About the family

Wife of Ole Østensen of Tiellov (Tjellaug), Oppstryn, and mother of the children baptized there in 1755-1765. ¶1

Her mother's probate (Britte Rasmusdatter, Tjellaug, 17 May 1754; Nordfjord skifteprotokoll A 12 fol. 77b-78a) lists her as 'Aase Rasmusdatter 30 aar', one of six children: Rasmus, Synneve 41, Ingebor 35, Brite 32, Aase 30 and Else 24. Age 30 in May 1754 gives birth about 1724 (inferred from the stated age). ¶5

Life story

Guardianship bridge: the Nordfjord guardianship protocols follow her from child to wife. Protocol 2, p. 256 (scan read): under the skiftebrev of 6 Jun 1742, Jens Tiellov is guardian of 'Aase...datter Tiellov, som nu er 18 aar gaml.' and of 'Else Rasmusdatter, 12 aar'. Aase was born about 1724, which fits the 30 given for her in her mother Brite's 1754 probate. Protocol 3, p. 150 carries the same account forward after 'Sal. Jens Tiellov' (fol. 100) under the name Aase Rasmusdatter, and says the funds were held by 'deris Enkemoder Brite Tiellov' (their widowed mother). Protocol 3, p. 79 (transcribed remark): on 9 Jun 1755 'Ole Ostensen Tiellof som har ægtet Aase Tiellov' acknowledged receipt of her 15 rd 14 s. This is a primary record that she, as Brite's daughter, was the wife of Ole Østensen (see his page) by June 1755. ¶6

Probate, 1780: named as the widow Aase Rasmusdatter in the probate after her husband Ole Østensen of Tjellog (Nordfjord skifteprotokoll A 16b, fol. 834b-836b). Her legal guardian (laugverge) was Cornelius Nielsen U[tvik?]. She received half of the 220 rd estate. Five children were named: Østen, Rasmus, Ole, Anne and Brithe Olsen/Olsdatter. ¶2

Aaland 1932: Aaland's Kjellog (Tjellaug) history names her 'Aase Rasmusd. Kjell., † 73 a. g. 1797': she died in 1797 aged 73 (born about 1724). She was the daughter of the previous holder Rasmus Kristoffersen (deed 1714) and Brite Rasmusdatter of Brekke (died 1765 aged 77); her husband is listed as their son-in-law (verson). Her children's names support this: Rasmus (1760) and Berthe/Brithe (1759, 1765) repeat her parents' names. See Rasmus Kristoffersen Tjellaug and Brite Rasmusdatter. Her 1797 burial (Innvik) would confirm her age. ¶4

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Confidence moved Probable to Confirmed on that probate. No guardian is named Rasmussen, so it does not show who her father's family was. ¶3

Records that disagree 1

Discrepancy (minor, explained): in protocol 2, p. 256, the clerk writes her patronymic as 'Christophersdatter'; the transcription has 'Aase Christophersdatter', and the scan reads that way too. The same account (Jens Tiellov's wards, carried over to protocol 3 fol. 100 and p. 150) calls her Aase Rasmusdatter, and her age matches Aase Rasmusdatter of the 1754 probate. Her brother Christopher Rasmussen (named in Brite's 1754 probate) is a likely source of the slip. No second Aase exists at Tjellaug in these protocols. ¶7
