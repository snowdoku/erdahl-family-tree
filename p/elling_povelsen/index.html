About the family

Named as Paul's father ('Elling Paulsen Lillemark') in the 1838 marriage. Buried 27 Dec 1840. ¶2

He is named as father in Povel's (Paul's) 1805 baptism ('Elling Povels.', Guldset), and Paul lived with him at Lillemark in 1825. In the 1838 marriage he is 'Elling Paulsen Lillemark'. These records make the link Confirmed. ¶3

Children: Ane (~1796), Peder (~1797), Mali (baptized 20 Sep 1801; married 26 Jun 1847), Nicoline (~1802), Povel/Paul (1805) and Ingeborg Anna (baptized 5 Dec 1813). ¶5

Parentage (Speculative): in the chronological Kvernes parish book 1753-1780 (source 16076, p. 114), the wife of 'Poul Ellingsen Stokkes', Ane Sivertsdatter, was churched (introdusert) on Invocavit Sunday (about 9 Feb) 1766. That points to a birth about Dec 1765/Jan 1766, which matches Elling's age of 35 in the Feb 1801 census. Paul Ellingsen of Stokke was also a godfather in Oct 1765. Naming supports the link: Elling's eldest daughter was Ane (born about 1796) and a son was Povel/Paul (1805). The actual baptism was not found on the pages read, so this remains Speculative. ¶6

Parentage now Probable (scan read): Kvernes chronological book 1753-1780 (source 16076) p. 122 records the baptism on Laetare Sunday, 29 Mar 1767, of Elling, son of Paul Ellingsen Stokke and his wife Ane Sivertsdatter. This is the only eighteenth-century Kvernes Paul Ellingsen found, so it is very likely Elling Povelsen's baptism, though the 1801 age of 35 implies a birth a year earlier. ¶8a

Parentage record found: the probate after his mother Ane Sivertsdatter at Stokke, opened 21 Jan 1785 (Nordmøre skifteprotokoll 1783-87, source 24652, fol. 321b-322a), names him first among her heirs: 'Sønnen Elling Paavelsen 17½ aar med Formynder Størcher Olsen Stokke'. His siblings were Sivert (14), Ole (11) and Maria (9), and his mother's widower was his stepfather Erich Olsen Stokke. The age gives a birth about mid-1767, which matches the 29 Mar 1767 baptism. His parents' cards are raised to Confirmed on this record. ¶13

Father's probate: Nordmøre skifteprotokoll nr 15 (source 24650), fol. 365a, opened 25 Jan 1779 at Stokke after Povel Ellingsen Stoche, lists as first heir '1 Sønnen Elling Povelsen gammel 12 aar' with guardian Størcher Olsen Stoche (the same guardian as in his mother's 1785 probate, where he is 17½). His full siblings were Christie (17), Anne (10), Sivert (8), Ole (6), Povel (4) and Maria (3). ¶15

Life story

Bridge from Stokke to Guldset found: Kvernes betrothal register 16080, canvas 138, p. 275, 1796: 'Aug: 6te forlangte u[ng]k[arl] og Sold[at] Elling Povlsøn Stokke, som skal faae 1 Part i Gaarden Gulset til beboelse, at trolove i Kvernes d. 28 ejusd. s[om er] D[om]: 14 p. Tr[initatis] med p[ige] Ingbor Endresd[atte]r Grønset. For deres Egteskabs Lovlighed godsige Ole Ellings[en] Stokke [and] Anders Endres[en] Grønset.' This ties Elling Povelsen of Stokke (the heir aged 12 in 1779 and 17½ in 1785, son of Paul Ellingsen and Ane Sivertsdatter) to Guldset, where the 1801 census finds Elling Povelsen with his wife Ingeborg Endresdatter. His surety was his paternal uncle Ole Ellingsen Stokke (named 'Farbroderen' in the 1779 probate). The identification of the Stokke heir with the Guldset farmer, which earlier rested on name, parish and age, is now shown by a record. He was then an unmarried soldier ('u[ng]k[arl] og Sold[at]'). Betrothal 6 Aug 1796; wedding at Kvernes from 28 Aug 1796. The earlier marriage searches of 16080 (c116-c136) stopped one canvas short. ¶16

1801: farmer and militia soldier at Guldset, Kvernes. 1825: at Lillemark with wife Ingeborg and children Peder (~1797), Nicoline (~1802), Paul (~1804), Ingeborg (~1813). ¶1

1801 census at Guldset: Elling Povelsen 35, farmer and militia soldier, and Ingeborg Endresdatter 31, both in their first marriage, with daughter Ane (5). ¶4

On his own parentage, which is still Speculative: a search of every indexed record in Kvernes and Kornstad found no Poul, Povel or Paul Ellingsen before 1839 and no Ane Sivertsdatter at Stokke at all, so the two entries in the scanned chronological Kvernes book for 1765-1766 remain the only trace of the couple. The 1801 census does, however, show an Ellingsen family established at Stokke in the previous generation: Ole Ellingsen, born about 1745, living there as 'the householder's father, a widower, a lodger', and Elling Olsen, born about 1769, a farmer and head of his own household on the same farm. An Ole Ellingsen of Stokke, born 1735, was buried in 1807. A Poul Ellingsen at Stokke in 1765 would fit as Ole's brother. See the note on Paul (Poul) Ellingsen Stokke for the sources that would prove or break this. ¶7

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Confidence adjusted: Confirmed to Probable (13 Oct 2026). In this tree a person's own confidence carries the evidence for his link to his parents. His records as a man (1801 census at Guldset, 1825 census at Lillemark, his son Povel's 1805 baptism, his 1840 burial) are primary and secure, but his link to Paul Ellingsen and Ane Sivertsdatter of Stokke rests on the Kvernes chronological book 1753-1780 (source 16076), p. 122, baptism of Elling on Laetare Sunday (29 Mar 1767), identified with him as the only eighteenth-century Kvernes Paul Ellingsen, while his 1801 census age of 35 implies a birth about 1765-66. That is a Probable link, so his own level is Probable. ¶9a

Upgrade: regraded to Confirmed under the overnight grading rule (1 Nov 2026), which makes a person's grade cover his link to the child who puts him in the tree, not his link to his own parents. That link is proved by primary records already on this card: the 1805 Kvernes baptism of Povel names the father as 'Elling Povels.' of Guldset; the 1825 census has Paul in Elling's household at Lillemark; and Paul's 1838 marriage names his father as 'Elling Paulsen Lillemark'. His own link upward to Paul Ellingsen and Ane Sivertsdatter of Stokke stays Probable and is carried on their cards. ¶10

Corrections 2

Correction: the churching date in the parentage note is 16 Feb 1766 (Invocavit), not about 9 Feb. ¶8b

Correction: the marriage was not before 1796. The couple were betrothed on 6 Aug 1796 (16080 c138 p.275) with the wedding at Kvernes from 28 Aug 1796. Daughter Ane, aged 5 in the Feb 1801 census, was therefore born about the time of the wedding or a little before; her baptism should be looked for in Kvernes 16077 for 1795-97. ¶17

Searches that found nothing 3

Confirmation search, negative: no Elling Povelsen/Paulsen in the Kvernes confirmation lists of Feb 1782 (16077 c12, p.18), 23 Nov 1783 (c21, pp.36-37), 9 Jan 1785 (c29, pp.52-53), nor in the joint Kornstad/Bremsnes/Kvernes list of 5 Feb 1786 (65 names, written in the Kornstad book 16096 c66, pp.130-131). Kvernes lists give name, farm and age only, never parents, so even a hit would identify him by farm (Stokke) and age, not by father. Not yet read: confirmations 1787-1790 (16077 from c39; no confirmation on c41 Jul-Nov 1787 or c44 Apr-Aug 1788 overviews). ¶11

Marriage search, partial: the prestegjeld betrothal register 16080 (unindexed before 1801, gives forlovere) has no Elling Povelsen / Ingeborg Endresdatter betrothal between May 1794 and Jun 1795 (c128-c132). Daughter Ane was 5 in Feb 1801, so the marriage was probably 1793-95: read c120-c127 next. ¶12

Marriage search, negative: there is no Elling Povelsen / Ingeborg Endresdatter betrothal in the prestegjeld betrothal register 16080 from May 1792 to May 1796 (c116-c136). Together with (c128-c132), that range is closed. The marriage was therefore before May 1792 or after May 1796. ¶14a
