About the family

Brother of Anna Olene (Satter) Eidem, so Mayme (Eidem) Eidem's maternal uncle. Linked as a sibling on Anna's Find a Grave memorial. ¶1

Eldest child of Gunder Gundersen Satter and Guri Anna Walstad, and the only one born in Norway: he was an infant when the family emigrated in 1865. Called Gust and Gustave; the middle name Parelius is a Trøndelag name and is itself a clue to the home parish. ¶6

Married twice. First, before 1888, Christine Carlson (1867-1889), daughter of Bengt Carlson; she died in 1889, within a year of their only child. Second, about 1894 at the Dale church in Union County, Sophia Larson (1871-1942), daughter of Lars Larson Jr. and Ellen A. Helseth. ¶8

Ten children: Clara Matilda (1 Nov 1888) by Christine Carlson; then by Sophia Larson, Ellen Amanda (23 Sep 1894), Lewis Griffin (3 Dec 1896), William Oliver (7 Oct 1898), Ida Elizabeth (1 Oct 1900), Ann Gladys (27 Dec 1903), John G. (2 Nov 1905), Elizabeth F. 'Lise' (13 Dec 1907), Agnes M. (3 Aug 1910) and Grace Adeline (23 Jun 1913). Nine of the ten are independently confirmed by their South Dakota birth registrations, which name the mother as Christine Carlson for Clara and Sophia Larson for the rest. ¶12

His sister's obituary of 18 March 1937 places him at Vermillion, three days before his own death three years later. He was the eldest of the Satter children and the only one born in Norway: a compiled biography gives his birth as about 1864 in Norway and says he immigrated with his parents in 1865, which if right makes Anna Olene (b. 1872 in Union County) one of the first of the family born in America. ¶15

Life story

His Norwegian birth in July 1864, stated twice, is what brackets the family's crossing: the Satters were still in Norway in 1864 and had reached Spink, Union County, by March 1872. ¶4

Baptism: Bakklandet (Bakke) parish, Trondheim, 1864, serial no. 108 — born 22 July 1864, baptised 4 September 1864, legitimate; father Gunnar Gundersen, arbeidsmand (day-labourer); mother Guri Anna Petersdtr., his wife; the household living at Lademoen. He is the only Gustav Parelius born anywhere in Norway between 1863 and 1865 in the Digital Archives' searchable registers, and his parents' names, his own name and the year all match the Union County family, so this record identifies the family in Norway and is the reason his parentage and birth date are rated Confirmed. ¶7

Marriage: a newspaper report of the second wedding survives as a photograph on his memorial. 'The contracting parties were Gustave Setter and Miss Sophia Larson, Rev. Dale officiating. The bridesmaids were Miss Anna Setter and Bessie Helseth of Sioux City, with Albert Larson and Henry Lewis as groomsmen.' His father gave $25, by far the largest gift in the published list; his brothers Fred and Lewis and his brother-in-law Andrew Abraham gave $5 each; so did Ole Walstad, his mother's brother. ¶9

1870 census: 'Setter, Gusta', aged 6, born NORWAY, in his father's household at dwelling and family 122, Sioux Valley, Union County, Dakota Territory, enumerated 21 August 1870. His age is exact for a birth on 22 July 1864, and the Norway birthplace confirms that he was the child carried across in 1865. This is the entry his Find a Grave memorial's research summary refers to, and it had never been located. ¶18

1880 census, read on the image: 'Gustav', son, 16, farmer, born Norway, in Gunder Satter's household at Spink Township, Union County, Dakota Territory, 15-16 June 1880 (ED 5, sheet 407A, line 5; mis-indexed by FamilySearch as 'Burrton'). Born July 1864, he was 15. This confirms from the image the memorial's statement that he was in the 1880 Spink household next door to Ole Walstad. ¶19

Marriages, as a lead from the same compiled biography and not verified here: (1) Christine Carlson, daughter of Bengt Carlson, before 1888; (2) Sophia Larson, daughter of Lars Larson Jr. and Ellen A. Helseth, about 1894. He farmed in Sioux Valley Township, Union County, and bought land in section 19 there before 1910. ¶16

1900 federal census: Sioux Valley township, Union County, South Dakota, Ancestry image 4120605_00238 - 'Gustav Satter, head, born JULY 1864, NORWAY', with wife Sophia (b. Dec 1871, South Dakota) and children Ellen A. (Sep 1894), Louis G. (Dec 1896) and William O. (Oct 1898). ¶2

1910 federal census: Sioux Valley township, Union County - 'Gust Satter, head, b. 1864, Norway', with Sophia and SEVEN children: Ellen A. (1895), Louis G. (1897), William O. (1899), Ida E. (1901), Anna G. (1904), John G. (1906) and Lizzie S. (1908). Nine people. ¶3

Land: he bought land before 1910 in Sioux Valley Township, Union County — Section 19, Township 93 North, Range 49 West. ¶11

He appears constantly in the Elk Point papers from the 1890s to the 1930s as 'Gust Satter of Sioux Valley township' and 'of Spink', and in 1921-24 as the executor of a will - a probate file that would name heirs. ¶17

He died at Vermillion, Clay County on 21 March 1940 and was buried at Brule Creek on 23 February 1940 according to the memorial's note — the burial date is plainly a transcription error for 23 March. ¶13

Dates and burial from Find a Grave memorial 62813956: died 21 March 1940 in Clay County, South Dakota (he was living at Vermillion, the county seat); buried at North Brule Cemetery, Union County, grave 4, lot 25, block 2, the burial recorded 23 February 1940 in one index and after 21 March 1940 in another - the February date is plainly a transcription error. ¶14
