About the family

Sister of Guri Anna Pettersdatter Walstad, two and a half years her elder. Born 27 September 1833, baptised 3 November 1833, Church book from Stjørdal parish 1833-1844 (1714Q), page 15, serial no. 196. Father 'Peter Olsen Lillehvalstad', bonde; mother Doret Pedersdatter, kone. This is the first of the family's baptisms in which the father is a bonde rather than an inderst. ¶1

Life story

Probable death: Olava Pettersdatter, 'barn' (a child) of Lille Valstad, aged 7, died on 1 January 1841 and was buried on 9 January 1841 (Stjørdal 1833-1844, burials p. 502 no. 17). The indexer calculated her birth year as 1834. Olive, born on 27 September 1833, was 7 years and 3 months old on that date. Olava and Olive are forms of the same name, and the entry follows directly after that of her brother Christopher, who was buried the same day. The entry names no parent, so this is Probable. ¶2
