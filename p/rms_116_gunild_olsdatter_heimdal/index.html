Life story

NEW. Wife of Toruv Andersen Heimdal (see his page) and Probable mother of Hæge Toruvsdatter (see her page); graded on that link. Record: the Panteregister I 1 abstract of the skifte (probate) after 'Gunild Olsdatter' on søndre Hiemdahl, held 5 May (year not legible; after her burial on 22 Oct 1773 and before an entry registered 11 Mar 1779). Land was laid out to Torof Andersen (5 Bdtg, the widower's share), to 'Anders, Knud, Torof Torofsønner' and to 'Astri, Gundbiør og Hæge Torofsdøttre' (DA 10915 fol. 318, view 361). Only the deceased's own children inherit from her estate, so the six Torofsen children are hers and not stepchildren. The heirs Gundbiør and Hæge match the tree's Gundbiør (m. 1775) and Hæge (bapt. 1759 as 'Toruvs Hiemdals daatter Hæge', m. 1781 as Hæge Toruvsdatter Hiemdal): the same farm, gaps of 15 years or less, and no other Hæge or Gundbiør Toruvsdatter on Hiemdal. Why Probable and not Confirmed: the panteregister is an abstract that does not label the heirs as 'børn' or name a widower (audit rule 3: an unlabelled list stays Probable until the protocol is read). Would settle: the skifteprotokoll entry itself, in protocol 11 (DA 25675, 1768-76) or 12 (DA 25676, 1776-81). Neither volume is chronological, entries are numbered, and read 25675 images 700-800 by heading and missed it, so the entry is probably in 25675 before image 700 or early in 25676 (fol. 6a = No. 86, 1776; fol. 17b = No. 96, Mar 1777). ¶1

Burial: 'Samme dag samme sted jordfæstet Torofs Hiemdahls Hustrue Gunild[?], gl. 53 aar', Treungen, 22 Oct 1773 (7981 p.59, image 183; read by an earlier pass). The skifte name Gunild Olsdatter confirms the earlier tentative reading 'Gunild'. Age 53 gives birth about 1720. ¶2

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed, graded on her link to her daughter Hæge Toruvsdatter (see her page). Record: skifteprotokoll 11 (DA 25675) fol. 827a, No. 71, view 831: 'Anno 1775 den 15de Februarii' at 'Gaarden Sønder Hiemdahl i Treungens Annex og Hvideseid Præstegield', to register and divide the estate 'efter den under 13de Aug.(?) 1773 afdøde Qvinde Gunild Ols Datter og igienlevende Enkemand Torrof Andersøn', between 'hendes 7 Børn, 4 Sønner og 3 Døttre, nemlig': Anders Torrofsøn, of age; Knud Torrofsøn, of age; Torrof Torrofsøn, 19 (curator Jørgen Olsøn Hiemdahl); Joen Torrofsøn, 17 (guardian 'Faderen Torrof Andersøn'); the eldest daughter Astri Torrofsdatter, dead, who in marriage with Halvor Targeisøn Sønderland left a daughter Astri Halvorsdatter, 12 (guardian her father); Gundbiør Torrofsdatter, 23 (guardian Joen Sigurdsøn Hiemdahl); and Hæge Torrofsdatter, 16, for whom 'Broderen Anders Torrofsøn' was appointed guardian. Valuers Kittil Torrofsøn(?) Nordbøe, Jørgen Sigurdsøn Hiemdahl and Jørgen Olsøn ibid. The death day reads '13de Aug.' (month abbreviation slightly uncertain); her burial was 22 Oct 1773. This is the protocol entry behind the panteregister abstract (DA 10915 fol. 318), which dates the division 5 May [1775]. The record calls the heirs 'hendes 7 Børn', so Hæge is named as Gunild's own daughter, and Torrof Andersøn as the surviving husband and 'Faderen'. Continuity to the tree's Hæge (see her page): she was baptized 11 Mar 1759 as 'Toruvs Hiemdals daatter Hæge' (16 in Feb 1775 fits), was confirmed in Nov 1775 as 'Hæge Torufs daatter Hiemdal, gl. 16 aar', and was betrothed on 30 Mar 1781 as 'Hæge Toruvsd: Hiemdal' to Thore Tronsen Fælle: the same farm, gaps of 6 years or less, and no other Hæge Toruvsdatter on Hiemdal (rival: none named; Toruv Andersen is the only Toruv at Hiemdal in the 1773-86 registers). This settles the open point in her note (the panteregister abstract did not label the heirs). ¶4

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Caution: a different Gunild Olsdatter, widow of Ole Hansen Fiene (Nissedal), appears in DA 25677 No. 436 (23 Mar 1781, fol. 174b-176a) with daughters Gundbiør and Aasne Torofsdatter from an earlier marriage to a Toruf Olsen; she is not this Gunild (who died in 1773 as Toruv Andersen's wife). ¶3b

Searches that found nothing 1

Search narrowed. The original register bound at the end of skifteprotokoll 12 (DA 25676, views 771-780) lists Nos. 84-306 (10 Nov 1776 to 1780) by farm and folio. Its Treungen entries are Unnestad (No. 89), Haugsjø (121), Felle (122, 252), Skaalt (161), Wellestien under nordre Haugsjå (182), Lilledal (183), Groven under Tvedt (246) and Tvedt (250); there is NO Hiemdal entry. So the 5 May skifte after her was not registered in 1776-80, and it is most likely in protocol 11 (DA 25675) as a 1774 or 1775 entry among the images not yet read (768-769, 771-779, 781-789, 791-809, 811-829, 831-855). ¶3a
