About the family

Father of Pål (Pofuell) Bjertesen of Einbu (see his page), Probable; graded on his link to Pål. Pål's patronymic is in primary records (skifte 18 Dec 1676, 'Pofuell Biertesen', card 39945/2433, protocol 25154 fol. 48b), so his father was a Bjerte. That this Bjerte is the Bjerte of Einbu, husband of Magnhild who held Einbu until 1628 when Pål (b. about 1604) took it over, rests on the Lesja bygdebok (vol. 1 canvas 180: 'Magnhild og Bjerte hadde kanskje to søner, og kanskje ei dotter: 1. Pål, ca. 1604, tok over. 2. Ola, ca. 1608, sjå Nørdre Kvam. 3. Marit') and on the farm succession; the bygdebok itself hedges ('kanskje'). Pål named his eldest son Bjerte. Probable at most (compiled source plus succession). ¶1

Life story

Also, by the same bygdebok entry, the father of Oluf (Ola) Biertesen of Nordre Kvam (see his page), whose 1681 skifte gives his patronymic 'Biertesen'. That second link is weaker (Speculative to Probable: patronymic plus the bygdebok's 'kanskje'); this person's grade rests on the link to Pål (audit 5 rule 2). He is ancestor twice: through Pål (Einbu line) and through Oluf (Kvam line). ¶2

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

The 1647 skattematrikkel (vol. 4 Oppland, nb.no b14c750cb96ff0fdfa0a4328bb1cf8b1) was content-searched: it gives Lesja farms but no user names were hit. ¶3
