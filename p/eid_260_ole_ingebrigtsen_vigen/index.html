About the family

Father of Gunnar (Gunder) Olsen Vigen: the 1666 list names his son Gunder Olsen at Over-Vigen; Gunder Olsen is the farmer of Over-Vigen in the 1701 manntall, with 'Bersvend Olsen', 49, living with him - the brother Bersvend Olsen of 1666. Both brothers are about five years younger in 1666 (9 and 6) than their 1701 ages imply (born 1652 and 1655), a consistent offset, so they are the same pair. Rated Confirmed for this link (explicit census son + Gunder's own 1701 record at the same farm + no other Gunder at Vigen); Gunnar's own link to his son Ola is Probable. ¶2

Father added, Probable: Ingebrigt Overvik (see his page), user of Overvik in 1647 per the bygdebok (vol. 6, canvas 324: 'Ingebrigt [...] bruker [på] Overvik. Etter [...] Ola [og] Bersvend', 'tredje sønn av Ingebrigt') and listed as 'Ingebrigt Vigge' in the 1646-47 kongsjordebok (51306 v181 R). Ole's patronymic Ingebrichtsen and his brother Bersvend Ingebrichtsen on the same farm in 1666 fit; no record names the relationship. ¶9

Life story

1624 leidang roll for Selbu (39890 view 78 left): 'Bersuend Wiigen' holds a Vigen farm; no Ingebrigt is listed at any Vigen farm. Ole's brother was Bersvend Ingebrigtsen and his nephew Ingebrigt Bersvendsen (1666), so Bersvend Vigen of 1624 is a possible father of their father Ingebrigt (naming pattern only; not added to the tree). ¶6

Bygdebok (Selbu i fortid og nåtid vol. 6, Overvik canvases 324-325): '[...] 1647 [...] Ingebrigt [var] bruker [på] Overvik. Etter [ham...] Ola [og] Bersvend [...] født [ca.] 1580' - the father Ingebrigt (b. about 1580?) used Overvik in 1647, and a 'tredje sønn av Ingebrigt' is mentioned (y1457); 'Brukere': '-1665-1686- Ola Ingebriktsen, f. ca. 1610', sons [Bersvend] ca. 1654 (d. 1719?), Gunnar ca. 1658 (took over), Ingebrikt 1665 (m. Marit, at Stor-Evja). The book's 'født [ca.] 1580' may belong to the father Ingebrigt; it is not added as a person here (no record, bygdebok gap text). ¶8

Court book 28707 view 136 right (fol. 135), at the Selbu session just before 17 Feb 1693: a merchant's list of Selbu debtors includes 'Olle Ingbretsen Vigen 2 D.' next to 'Ingbret Kolset' and 'Enchen [widow] Vigen 1½ D.'. So an Ole Ingebrigtsen was still a debtor at Vigen in about 1692. That fits Ole of Over-Vigen (b. c.1616-30) if he was still living, though a namesake cannot be excluded. It is not used to grade any link. ¶7

How sure are we?

Records that disagree 1

1666 vicar's manntall, Selbu, no. 11 'Øffuer Wiggen' (Over-Vigen, Overvik), tax 2 spann: 'Olluf Ingebrichtsen bruger 1 spd., 50 aar; Bersvend Ingebrichtsen bruger 1 spd., 46 aar', with sons 'Bersvend Olsen 9 aar, Gunder Olsen 6 aar, Ingebricht Olsen 6 maaneder' and 'Ingebricht Bersvendsen 1 aar' (Digitalarkivet 35528, view 20, book p. 21, page checked by the orchestrator). Earlier lists give him at Over-Vigen as 'Oluf' 35 (1665, v13) and 'Olle Abrm.[?]' 40 (1664 fogd's list, 35578 v18) beside his brother Bersvend Ingebrigtsen - the ages disagree, so his birth is set to 1616-1630. His father was an Ingebrigt. ¶1

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Possible burial, not adopted: 'graffæstet Olaf Wigen[?] ætat 96', Septuagesima 1702 (about 29 Jan 1702), Selbu 16310 c11 right page. The farm name is unclear ('migen' or 'wigen'), and there were several Vigen farms, so this is only a lead. An age of 96 is too old for his 1664-66 ages (40, 35, 50), though burial ages of the very old are often inflated. He is not in the 1701 manntall for Over-Vigen, where Gunder Olsen (46) is the farmer. ¶5

Searches that found nothing 1

Negative, card index by spelling variant: the 77804 Ø section (red divider at canvas 2281; Å divider at 2329) has two Selbu 'Øvervik' cards: canvas 2321, 26/6-1691, Birgit Jonsdatter, widower Povel (Paul) Eriksen, her siblings Jon Jonsen and Guri Jonsdatter; and canvas 2323, 12/2-1732, Gunder Olsen Wigen (his son; see Gunnar (Gunner) Olsen Vigen). No probate after Ole Ingebrigtsen or his wife was found under Vigen, Overvik or Øvervik for 1689-1742. ¶10

Open questions and the next record to read 2

What would carry this further: the 1664 fogd's list at full resolution for his patronymic ('Abrm.' is uncertain); the 1647 and 1657 tax lists (kvegskatt 1657) for an Ingebrigt at Over-Vigen - not found transcribed; a Stjør- og Verdal probate after him (protocol 1, 1689-1701, source 24749, or card index 77804). ¶3

Biography gaps: his birth (1616-1630) and his marriage predate the 1699 Selbu register, and his wife is not named in any record read. His death, death place and burial are not found: the 77804 cards checked for Vigen and the O-run hold no probate for him, but that index covers 1689-1742 and only about 45% of it was sampled, so the next check is the unindexed Stjør- og Verdal protocol 1 (24749, 1689-1701). A burial after 1699 would be in 16310, which the drafts do not report searching for him. ¶4
