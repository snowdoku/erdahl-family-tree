About the family

Mother of Knut Rolfsen of Nordre Slettum (see his page), Probable; graded on her link to Knut. The 1694 skifte after Rolf Christensen at Ula (Nord-Gudbrandsdal skifteprotokoll 1690-1719, source 25155, fol. 23b, 26 Sep 1694, scan) is held 'imellem hans efterlatte Hustru Anne Arnesd: og deris 6 Børn, Tore, Christen, Oluf, Knud og Lars Rolfss: samt Magnild Rolfsdatter'. 'deris Børn' names her as the mother of all six (audit 8 rule 2). Probable rather than Confirmed only because the Knud Rolfsen of this record is identified with Knut Rolfsen of Nordre Slettum across farms and about 25 years (see Rolf Christensen Ula). The patronymic is read 'Arnesd:' from a bitonal scan; the letters after A are cramped, so the reading is not certain. The bygdebok (vol. 3 canvas 326) has 'Anne' in the entry. The eldest child Tore is given as ca. 1663 by the bygdebok, so she may be a second wife for the elder children; 'deris' covers all six as written, but if the bygdebok's 1663 is right she would be about 50 at Knut's birth ABT 1685, so the date for Tore or the identity of the mother of the eldest deserves a check. ¶1

Life story

Correction to the Test record. An exact-phrase search of the Lesja bygdebok (vol. 3 canvas 326) reads 'I 1723 var det skifte etter bror til Rolf', i.e. the 1723 skifte was after a BROTHER of Rolf (an uncle of Knut), not after an unmarried son; the entries for Rolf's children Kristen and Lars say 'død utan arvingar før skiftet etter [gap] i 1723' and Magnhild 'død før skiftet etter [gap] i 1723', so Rolf's surviving children (among them Knut of Nordre Slettum) were heirs in it. It would still bridge the gap if it names 'Knud Rolfsen Slettum' as a brother's son. Search so far: 25156 views 140-189 read header by header; 1723 entries with Lesja headers are at views 143, 146, 149 (21-24 May) and 179 (7 Oct), and none is at Ula or after a single man. The deceased may be filed under another farm. Test record (still open): the 1723 skifte after Rolf Christensen's brother (25156; unread so far: views 3-139 and 190-229; the sorenskriver Lauritz Brun's Lesja sessions were 21-24 May and 7 Oct 1723). ¶3

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Upgrade: Probable -> Confirmed, graded on her link to Knut Rolfsen of Nordre Slettum (see his page). re-read the 1694 entry (25155 view 27, fol. 23b): 'imellem hans efterlatte Hustru Anne Arnesd: og deris 6 Børn, Tore, Christen, Oluf, Knud og Lars Rolfss: samt Magnild Rolfsdatter'. 'deris 6 Børn' (their six children) is written in the protocol entry itself, not inferred from a card listing (audit 8 rule 2 asks for the opening of the protocol entry; the formula here is 'deris Børn' without 'avlede'). The 1723 skifte after Rolf's brother Hans Christensen at Ula then bridges the Knud of 1694 to Knut of Nordre Slettum: The 1723 skifte (25156 view 136, fol. 131b-132a; spring Lesja session of Lauritz Brun, May 1723) is after 'Sl. Hans Christensen' of 'Ulsjordet i Læssøe Sogn' (Ula), who left no children; his heirs are his brothers and sisters or their children, set out in groups A-G. Group B reads 'Arfvinger Sl. Rolf Christensens Børn 1. Thoer Rolfsen 60 aar 2. Ole Rolfsen... 40 aar 3. Knud Rolfsen Slett[?] 38 aar og 4. Arfvinger Magnild Rolfsdatter Slett[?]s Børn' (three Ingebrigtsen children). Knud's farm word is a cramped abbreviation (tall looped capital, as in this hand's 'Sl.', then -ett- and a flourish); the same word follows Magnild's name, and the Lesja bygdebok independently places Magnhild at Slettom ('ho hadde før vore [gift på] Slettom', vol. 3 canvas 326) and Knut at Nørdre Slettom from 1718 (vol. 1 canvas 370), so the reading 'Slett(om)' is well supported though not letter-certain. Knud's age 38 gives b. ABT 1685, as for Knut of Nordre Slettum, and Thoer's 60 gives b. ABT 1663, as the bygdebok has for Tore. This is a sibling-set bridge (audits 8/9): the 1694 skifte after Rolf Christensen at Ula names 'deris 6 Børn, Tore, Christen, Oluf, Knud og Lars Rolfss: samt Magnild'; the 1723 skifte at the same farm, naming the same father, lists Thoer, Ole and Knud Rolfsen and the children of the dead Magnild, while Christen and Lars are absent, as the bygdebok says ('død utan arvingar før skiftet... 1723'). No rival Knud Rolfsen is known in Lesja. On the age concern in an earlier note: the 1723 skifte gives Thoer 60 (b. ABT 1663) and Knud 38 (b. ABT 1685), a 22-year span. That allows Anne to be the mother of both (born about 1643, aged about 20 and 42), and if Rolf did have an earlier wife she would be the mother of the elder children, not of Knud, the second-youngest, unless Anne married Rolf after 1685, which the protocol's 'deris 6 Børn' contradicts. The concern is therefore resolved for her link to Knut; it may still matter for Tore. ¶4

Audit 12 (2 Nov 2026): Confirmed KEPT, graded on her link to Knut Rolfsen of Nordre Slettum (see his page). Ruling on the wording: the 1694 protocol opening divides the estate 'imellem hans efterlatte Hustru Anne Arnesd: og deris 6 Børn'. 'Deris Børn', written by the court in the opening of the protocol entry, names the widow as the mother of those children; the word 'avlede' is not required. Audit 8 rule 2 exists to stop a CARD that lists the wife and then the children from being read as naming her the mother; the protocol's own possessive does name her. The same reading applies to Berethe (Brit, Bethe) Lauridsdatter (1708, 'deris 6 Børn'), Kari Endresdatter (1681) and Jøran Steinarsdatter Tande (1660). Limit: where another record shows the man had an earlier wife, or the children's ages run back before the couple could have married, 'deris' is not enough for the elder children. That is the open point for Tore (b. about 1663): her link to Tore is not settled by this ruling, but Knud (b. about 1685, second youngest) is inside any window. Continuity is by the 1723 skifte after Hans Christensen at Ula (see the audit 12 note on Rolf Christensen Ula). ¶5
