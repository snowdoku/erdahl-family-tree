Life story

Bygdebok (Treungen vol., Heimdal nordre, canvases 359-361): 'Knut... Haugsjå' married 'Torbjørg Åsmundsdtr. Heimdal'; their children begin 'Hæge 17[..], [g. m.] Eivind Andersson Heimdal... halve garden', then [..] 1715, Margit 1720, [..] 1724 (Trond, Felle Vestgarden), Astrid 1728 (1759). Canvas 361: 'Eivind Andersson Heimdal [g. m.] Hæge Knutsdtr. Heimdal. Born: Åsmund 1732, [g.] 1761 [m...] Tarjeisdtr. Sønderland... halve [garden];... Signe; Knut 1736, [g.] 1768 [m...] Jonsdtr.; Anders 1744, [g.] 1771 Anne Jonsdtr. Tveit; Torbjørg 1748, [g.] 1775 Hølje Jonson; Nils'; 'Eivind Andersson d 1766, f 1701', with a skifte after him in 1767. Canvas 362: Åsmund Eivindson took over the farm and had shares from his brothers Anders and Nils Eivindson; his children include Torbjørg 1761 and Hæge 1779, named for his mother's mother and his mother. ¶1

Primary record for her parents: the July 1733 probate of Torbjør Aamundsdatter of nordre Heimdal (Øvre Telemark skifteprotokoll no. 4, fol. 338b; card DA 6856 img 478) names 'Hage g.m. Evend Anderssen Heimdal' as the eldest child of Knud Bjønnesen and Torbjør. So she was Eivind's wife by 1733, a year after Åsmund's birth by the book. ¶2

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 3

Graded on her link to Aasmund Evindsen Heimdal (see his page). ¶3a

Graded Probable: a bygdebok is a compiled secondary source and the passage was rebuilt from word boxes with gaps; dates from it are ABT. ¶3b

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed, graded on her link to her son Aasmund Evindsen Heimdal (see his page). Record: DA 25674 (skifteprotokoll no. 10, 1759-68) No. 775, view 735 left page: 'Anno 1767 den 20de May var efter lovlig Varsel forsamlede paa Gaarden Hiemdahl i Treungens Annex og Hvidesøe Præstegield' the lagrettemen Jon Fione, Gunder Homme and Torof Hiemdahl, 'for at Registere og Vurdere samt Skifte og Dele alt hvis Boen tilhørte efter afgangne Even Andersøn', to be divided 'imellem Enken Haye Knudsdaatter og deres sammenavlede Børn Nemlig: Aasmund Evensøn myndig, Knud Evensøn myndig, Anders Evensøn 22 1/2 aar gammel, Niels Evensøn 12 1/2 aar gammel, Gundbør Evensdaatter gift med Tiøstof Aarach, Torbiør Evensdaatter 19 aar gammel'. The protocol calls the children 'deres sammenavlede Børn' (their children begotten together), so it names Hæge as the mother of Aasmund (audit 8 rule 2 satisfied: the opening of the protocol entry was read, not a card). The same entry (fol. 726a) records her declaration that she will hand over the farm to 'sin ældste Søn Aasmund Evensøn' (her eldest son), who is to keep her for life. Continuity: Aasmund Evindsen Hiemdahl married at Treungen in 1761 and remarried in 1766, was named at his son Targie's baptism 1764 and was a landowner of nordre Heimdal in the 1770s (panteregister I 1 fol. 269b); this skifte is held on Heimdal in 1767, 6 years after his marriage, and no other Aasmund Evensen at Heimdal is known. The rival raised earlier (a possible second wife as mother of the late child Guri, b. ~1754) is resolved: the youngest living son Niels, 12 1/2 in 1767 (b. ~1754-55), is also listed among 'deres sammenavlede Børn', so Hæge was still bearing children then and was the mother of all the children named. The bygdebok statement (canvas 361) is thus borne out by the primary record. ¶5

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Rival: Eivind's daughter Guri was born about Dec 1754, when Hæge (married by 1732) would be about 45 or more; a second wife cannot be ruled out for the late children, but the book makes Åsmund 1732 her son and his children carry her family's names (Torbjørg, Hæge, Knut). ¶3c

Open questions and the next record to read 1
