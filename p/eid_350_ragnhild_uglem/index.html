About the family

She is therefore also the book's mother of Povel Svendsen Berge (see his page) and Ingri Svendsdatter (see her page). Her patronymic is not in the recovered words. A probate after her (d. 1742, Uglem) in Stjør- og Verdal skifteprotokoll 3 (24753, 1742-60) would name her heirs and could confirm this; not yet looked for. ¶2
