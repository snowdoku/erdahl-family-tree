Life story

Anne, wife and widow of Robert Barnes, M.D., of Oxford. His will of 1 Jan 1603/4 says 'his wife Anne survived him' (Wood's abstract, Survey of Oxford, vol. 3, p. 248), and the St Giles register as copied by Wood has 'Ann Barnes, widdow of Dr. Robert Barnes a phisitian, 21 Dec. 1612'. Her maiden name is unknown. Added. ¶1

How sure are we?

Speculative. A plausible candidate only. Treat this link as a question, not a fact.

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Speculative, graded on her link to Prudence Barnes: a plausible candidate only. The will abstract calls her only his wife and does not say which daughters were hers. It names two living daughters called Mary ('Mary the elder' and 'Marye the yonger'), which suggests two wives; Barnes was born about 1515 and was a fellow of Merton (unmarried) from 1538, while Prudence, the youngest daughter, was having children in 1603-04 and lived until 1652, so she was probably born in the 1570s. If there were two wives, the youngest daughter is the one most likely to be the last wife's, and Anne was the last wife. ¶2a

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Test record: the original will of Robert Barnes, proved in the Chancellor's Court of the University of Oxford in 1604 (Oxford University Archives, Hyp/B), for any wording such as 'my wife and our daughter'; the St Mary Magdalen and St Giles, Oxford, registers for his marriage(s) and Prudence's baptism; Prudence's own will, PROB 11/224/477 (1652). Added. ¶2b
