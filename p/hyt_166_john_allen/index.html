About the family

Father of Jeremiah (see his page): 'Jeremiah of John & Eliza. Allen born June 11' 1699 (Boston register, vol. 9), with an elder brother 'James of John & Elizabeth Allen' (1697). Graded Probable on his link to Jeremiah: the birth entry names a John and Elizabeth Allen, and the identification of that couple with the goldsmith and Elizabeth Edwards (married 1697) and of their Jeremiah with Rebecca Kilby's husband rests on the dates, the Waldo Genealogy's statement and the land, not on a record naming them together. ¶3

His father's will (Suffolk Probate vol. 17 pp. 101-102, docket 3272, dated 12 March 1705/6, proved 18 Oct. 1710; DGS 007703076 images 67-68, read) names 'my son John Allen' (the 18-acre farm 'in his present Tenure and Occupation', devised to the testator by 'my Uncle Penn', and the negro man Richard 'now living with him'), his brother Jeremiah, and his sister Hannah Danforth, and makes John and Jeremiah executors. This replaces the Gleaner abstract as the source for the farm. Grade unchanged (Confirmed, on his link to Jeremiah). ¶9

Life story

Goldsmith of Boston (partner of John Edwards as 'Allen & Edwards'), 1671-1760, 'Son of Rev. James Allen. Married Elizabeth Edwards' (Ensko, American Silversmiths III, and other silver reference books; compiled). ¶1

Birth: 'John of James & Elizabeth Allen born Feb. 29', in the 1671 section of the Boston register (that is, 29 Feb. 1671/2), and in the First Church baptisms of 1672, 'John Allen of our Teacher' (Record Commissioners vol. 9, which prints the First Church baptisms alongside; OCR read). His brother 'Jeremiah of James & Eliza. Allen born Mar. 29' 1673 ('Jeremiah of James Allen our Teacher') is Jeremiah Allen Esq., the Province treasurer. ¶5a

Enrichment: Katharine Penn's will of 25 Oct. 1679 (Suffolk probate case 1270, original read on the image) leaves 'to his Son John a bedlying on the trundle bedstead' among bequests to the children of 'my Kinsman Mr James Allen' (James, Hannah, John, Jeremiah, Sarah). A record naming John as a son of Rev. James Allen in 1679. ¶10

Land: by Rev. James Allen's deed of settlement (1706, Suffolk Deeds 23:8) and will (1710) John received 'all that his tract of land or farm... containing by estimation 18 acres, lying in the new fields', with two acres of meadow, the 'Allen farm' north of Cambridge Street at West Boston, later cut by Allen, McLean and Poplar streets (Gleaner XI). Capt. Job Prince's mansion stood on this ground by 1790. ¶2

Death: between 8 Dec. 1756 (date of his will) and 8 Jan. 1760 (probate); the widow Elizabeth Allen was sole executrix. Witnesses Ruth Barrell, Sarah Edwards, Jacob Sheafe. His heirs in 1765 were only three: the representatives of his eldest son Jeremiah (deceased), his daughter Elizabeth Blanchard, wife of Mr Hezekiah Blanchard of Boston, and his daughter Susanna Welch, widow, who had sons Ebenezer and Hezekiah (will; Suffolk Probate 64:465-469 and 67:342-344). The son James (b. 1697) is not among them and evidently died without issue. ¶7

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: graded on his link to Jeremiah Allen. His will (Suffolk Probate 55:293-295; 'I John Allen of Boston... Goldsmith', dated 8 Dec. 1756, proved 8 Jan. 1760, docket 12211; image read) leaves his dwelling house to 'my well beloved son Jeremiah' and £50 each to 'my two Grand Children named John & Elizabeth Children of my said son Jeremiah'; the division of his real estate (Suffolk Probate 64:465, 2 Aug. 1765) gives one third to 'the Children or legal Representatives of Jeremiah Allen eldest son of the deceased'. Continuity to the Jeremiah in the tree: Jeremiah and Rebekah Allen's Boston children include Elisabeth (22 Apr. 1725) and John (1729), exactly the two grandchildren the will names; Jeremiah died intestate by about 11 Apr. 1760, when 'Rebecka Allen of Boston Widow' was appointed administratrix of 'her late Husband Jeremiah Allen late of Boston Felt Maker' (Suffolk Probate 56:365, docket 12319), so he was alive at the will (1756) and dead soon after it was proved (1760), a gap of under four years. The only other Boston Jeremiah Allen of the age, the merchant son of Jeremiah Allen Esq., died by April 1755 leaving a widow Elizabeth and children Jeremiah, Martha and William (dockets 10999-11022), so he cannot be the son living in 1756 with children John and Elizabeth. ¶6

Corrections 1

Correction (to note on the 1781 West Boston proprietors): the 1765 and 1768 divisions concern 'the Real Estate of James Allen late of Boston Esqr deceased', a share of which was set off to John Allen's representatives; so the 'Heirs of James Allen' among the 1781 proprietors are most probably heirs of Rev. James Allen (John's father), not of a son James. ¶8

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Rival: another John and Elizabeth Allen had children in Boston in 1686-88 (Anne, Elizabeth, John); that couple is older and is not the goldsmith, who was 14-16 then. ¶5b

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Lead: Suffolk probate docket 12211 is a will of a John Allen in 1760 (docket book DGS 7703035, image about 114, not read). If it is the goldsmith's, it should name his surviving children and grandchildren, possibly 'Elizabeth Prince' as a daughter of his son Jeremiah, which would confirm Elizabeth Allen's parentage. ¶4
