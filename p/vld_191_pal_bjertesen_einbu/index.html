About the family

Father of Bjerte Pålsen (see his page), graded on his link to Bjerte: Probable. The skifte after 'Pofuell Biertesen' of Einbu i Lesje ('Inneboe'), 18 Dec 1676 (card 39945/2433, skifteprotokoll 1673-1688 fol. 48b), names his widow 'Sønne Christophersdtr.' and 'Barn: 1. Bierte Poffuelsen, 2. Christopher, 3. Lauritz, 4. Jon' (formue 85-1-20). The card labels Bierte as his child, so the relationship is from a faithful index; the grade is held at Probable because identifying that Bierte with Marit Biertesdatter's father (Bjerte of Nørdre Einbu, d. after 1727) spans 50 years with no record read in between, resting on the farm, the rare name and the bygdebok (vol. 1, c180: Pål ca. 1604 - ca. 1676, user of Sør-Einbu, 'Barn i live i 1676' with Bjerte ca. 1644 who took over). Bjerte's son Pål (Poul Biertes, b. about 1674) carries his name. The protocol entry (25154 fol. 48b, view 51) reads: skifte after S. Poffuell Biørtesen between his wife Sønne Christophersdatter 'og deris 7 Børn', the sons Bierte, Christopher, Lauritz and Jon and the daughters Marrit, Ragnild and one more; Bierte and Marrit were already married (they had had their hjemgave), which fits Bjerte's eldest child Synnøve being born about 1665. ¶1a

Parents entered from the Lesja bygdebok (vol. 1 canvas 180, Einbu): 'Magnhild og Bjerte hadde kanskje to søner, og kanskje ei dotter: 1. Pål, ca. 1604, tok over. 2. Ola, ca. 1608, sjå Nørdre Kvam. 3. Marit'; Magnhild held Einbu until 1628, when Pål took over. Father Bjerte (see his page) Probable (his patronymic Biertesen in the 1676 skifte plus the succession); mother Magnhild (see her page) Speculative. His probable brother Ola is Oluf Biertesen of Kvam (d. 1681, Oluf (Ola) Biertesen Kvam), a direct ancestor through Kari Endresdatter of Slettum. Pål's own grade is unchanged. ¶2
