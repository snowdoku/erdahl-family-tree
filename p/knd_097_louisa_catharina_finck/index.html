Life story

Baptism (identification Probable): Louisa Catharina Finckh, baptised at Illingen 13 Feb 1774, daughter of Johannes and Friederica Magdalena (Ancestry 61023 record 8396225, image 1187139-00301). The only Louisa Catharina Finck baptism in the index; she would be 29 at the 1803 marriage. A second Johannes Finck (wife Maria Barbara) was baptising at Illingen in 1774-75, but no Louisa Catharina is indexed to him. Siblings by the same parents: Johann Gottfried (bapt. 18 Jun 1772) and Georg Carl (bapt. 28 Jan 1780, m. Illingen 2 May 1809). ¶2

3 on the grade

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 3

Graded on that link: Probable. The daughter's 1822 marriage indexes name the mother 'Luisa Catharina' (Vaihingen), 'Katharina' (Bietigheim, Ancestry) and 'Katharina Kuffer' (Bietigheim, FamilySearch). Her identification as Louisa Catharina Finck rests on the Illingen marriage of 28 Apr 1803 of Johann Melchior Lindenmaier (son of Johann Melchior) and 'Louisa Catharina Fincken', daughter of Johannes (Ancestry 61023 records 8261325, 908261325; image 1187140-00632): same husband's name, same given names, two years before the daughter's birth. It crosses parishes (Illingen to Bietigheim) and conflicts with the 'Kuffer' surname in one index (probably the occupation Küfer), so Probable, not Confirmed. ¶1b

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed, graded on her link to her daughter Luise Catharina Lindenmaier (see her page). FamilySearch, 'Germany, Births and Baptisms, 1558-1898', V4LM-YDX: Louisa Catharina Lindenmajer, born 7 Apr 1805, christened Bietigheim 8 Apr 1805, Father's Name 'Johann Melchior Lindenmajer', Mother's Name 'Louisa Catharina Fink'. FamilySearch, 'Germany, Marriages, 1558-1929', VCYQ-MRR: the 1822 bride Louise Katharine Lindenmaier, Birth '1805 Bietigheim', parents 'Johann Melchior Lindenmaier, Katharina Kuffer' (the Lutheran twin QPGP-ZRG8 gives birth '17 Apr 1805', parents 'Johann Mathias Lindenmaier, Katharina'). Continuity (17 years, audit 7 rule 1): the father stays at Bietigheim in the gap (son Gottlob Melchior christened there 20 Aug 1809, V4L9-4ZJ, mother 'Louisa Catharina Finck'; his remarriage there 1813/14, VCYQ-S6T); the far record ties the bride to Bietigheim and to the father by name and birth year; no other Louisa/Luise Catharina Lindenmaier is named. Death: buried Bietigheim 19 Sep 1813, age 39, parents Johannes Finck and Magdalena (QPGL-X2R5). The 'Kuffer' reading stays explained as the father's trade (Küfer). Read signed in, 2 Nov 2026; labelled index fields, image not seen (partner-only). ¶3

Audit 10 (2 Nov 2026): Confirmed kept, graded on her link to her daughter Luise Catharina Lindenmaier (see her page). The 1805 baptism V4LM-YDX names her as the mother. Continuity runs through the father, who stays at Bietigheim (baptism 1809; remarriage 1814, image read; 17 years in all, audit 7 rule 1). Her husband was married to her from 1803 until her burial in 1813, so any daughter of his born in 1805 is hers. Clean-up: the note explains 'Katharina Kuffer', the mother's name in the 1822 'Germany, Marriages' row VCYQ-MRR, as the father's trade (Küfer). That no longer holds. The 1814 marriage image calls the father 'Bürger und Schuster' (shoemaker), and so does the 1774 entry for his father. 'Kuffer' is therefore unexplained, possibly an indexer's misreading. It does not change her grade, because the man had no other wife in 1805. Test record if wanted: the 1822 Bietigheim marriage image (Ancestry 1184901-00228). ¶4
