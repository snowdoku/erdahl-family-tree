Life story

1701 manntall (index): 'Madz Johansen', aged 24 (b. c.1677), servant and soldier ('Tienistekarle eller drenge, Soldat') on Moen, Romsdal fogderi (pc00000005080465). The other men listed on Moen were the tenants Endre (b. c.1671) and Iver (b. c.1665) with his sons Steen Iversen (b. 1697) and Erick Iversen (b. 1699), and a widow tenant with her sons Knud and Hovor Olsen. Steen Iversen of Moen stood godfather to Mads's son Endre in 1735, which places this Moen in Grytten. Mads was a servant, not a son of any tenant listed, so his parents are not on the farm in 1701; his father was a Johan, not found. ¶4

Marriages (index): his first wife (name not indexed) was buried from Moen 10 Dec 1729 (pg00000007161140); he married secondly Guri Ingebrichtsd. of Bøe at Eid church on 18 Oct 1730 (pv00000010058199). Children baptised at Vold church as 'Mads, Moen': Peder 1726, Birgitthe 1730 (d. 1731), Birgitthe 1732 (d. 1732), Birgitthe 1733, Endre 1735, Birgitthe 1738, Anna 1740 (index). This matches the 1749 probate (Peder and Marith of the first wife; Endre, Brith elder, Brith younger and Anna of Guri) and confirms the 1749 probate and the Moe baptisms concern one man. His link to the 1770 bride stays Probable (identification by patronymic and farm). ¶5

Probate (scan read): Romsdal sorenskriveri skifteprotokoll 3A 07 (1740-1751), s. 543b-544a (source 24616, view 563). On 8 Nov 1749 the appraisers Ole Knudsen Bøe, Peder Otterholm and Knud Rochnem met at the farm Moe to divide the estate of 'afgangne Mads Johansen Moe' between his widow 'Guri Ingebrethsdatter' and the children: Peder and Endre Madssønner, Brith the elder ('den ældste') and Brith the younger ('den yngste') Madsdøtre, and Anna and Marith Madsdøtre. Guardians: for Peder, Erich Sæbøe; for Endre, Knud Samset; for Brith the elder, Ole Brudeset; for Brith the younger, Elling Brudeset; for Anna, Hans i Bøe; for Marith, Lars i Bøe. Among the debts are Peder Madsen's and Marith Madsdatter's unpaid inheritance 'efter sin Moder' (from their mother), so Peder and Marith were children of an earlier, deceased wife and Guri Ingebrethsdatter was his second wife. A claim by Anna Endresdatter on the estate is also listed. No ages are given. ¶2

Burial (index): 'Mats Johansøn', Moe, buried 1 Nov 1749 at Vold church with a funeral sermon, birth year calculated 1680. Grytten ministerialbok 1725-1763 (1539P), p. 167 (pg00000007162177). A son Mads Madssøn of Moe, b. c.1743, was buried 14 Jan 1748 (p. 147, pg00000007162122). 'Mads Moe' and 'Mads Moes Qvinde' stood godparents at Vold church on 18 Aug 1748 (pd00000042624126). ¶1

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the grade

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Why Probable as father of Brit (Birgitte) Madsdatter: the 1770 marriage names the bride only as 'Mats D. Moe' (Madsdatter of Moe). Mads Johansen is the only Mads found at Moe in the Grytten church-book and probate indexes before 1770, apart from his son Mads who died in 1748, and his 1749 probate lists daughters Brith (two), Anna and Marith, which covers both first names the clerk gave the bride. This is identification by elimination and name, so it stays Probable until a record (e.g. a later probate of a Moe sibling listing 'Brit Madsdatter gift med Ole Svendsen Korsand') names the relationship. ¶3
