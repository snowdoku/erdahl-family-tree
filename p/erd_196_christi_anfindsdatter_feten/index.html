Life story

Eldest daughter of Anfind Andersen of Feeten: 'eldste Datter Christi Anfindsdatter 6 aar gl.' at his probate, 16 Aug 1768 (Ytre Sogn 8b p. 528, No. 36), with her mother Britte Endresdatter. Confirmed at Tjugum on 30 Nov 1777 as 'Christi Anfinds Dr' of Feten, 16, with her brother Ole Anfindsen Feten, 18 (Leikanger 11567, canvas 233). ¶1

Marriage: about 19-22 June 1797 at Tjugum, 'Unge-Karl Marcus Marcussen Thue' and 'Pigen Christi Anfinds Dr Fethen' (Leikanger 11568, canvas 93 right). ¶2

Daughter Giøri baptised at Tjugum on 24 Sep 1797, parents Marcus Marcussen Moland and Christi Anfindsdatter. The godparents were Ole Moland, Endre Thorsen Fethen, Britha Endresdatter ibid [Feten], Anna Johannesdatter Ulvestad and Guri Hansdatter Moland (11568, canvas 98 left). ¶3

1801 census: Christi Anfindsdatter, 38, wife of the cottager Marcus Marcussen, 38, at Esse (household 07), both in their first marriage, with Giøri 4. ¶4

Probate begun 30 Apr 1813 at Ytre Esse (Ytre Sogn 14 fol. 538a): widower Marcus Marcussen; heirs the daughters Giøri Marcusdatter 15 3/4 (her guardian a Chri[sten/stian] Christensen Fiærestad) and Sigrid Marcusdatter 9 3/4 (her father as guardian). ¶5

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the grade

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Probable as daughter of Anfind Andersen and Brite Endresdatter, graded on her link to Anfind Andersen. The marriage record places her at Feten, where the 1768 probate and 1777 confirmation place the only Christi Anfindsdatter of the right age; her age fits; her daughter's godparents were Britha Endresdatter of Feten and a woman of Ulvestad; and her daughter's name Giøri repeats her sister's. No record names her parents, and 1777 to 1797 is a gap of 20 years, so the link stays Probable. Added as a collateral relative, to show why she is not Christi Anfindsdatter. ¶6
