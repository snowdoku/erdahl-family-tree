About the family

Father of Gunnar Olsen (see his page): on 3rd Advent, 14 December 1727, 'Ole Gunders: Vigens barn Nom: Gunder' was baptised (Selbu ministerialbok 1699-1737, canvas 110 right page, crop). Rated Probable rather than Confirmed because the adult Gunnar is tied to this baptism by his patronymic and the farm form 'Vigen' alone, and the bygdebok word boxes also show an 'Ola 1709' among the children of the earlier holder, so a second Ole Gundersen at Overvik is not yet excluded. ¶3a

Parents: Gunnar (Gunner) Olsen Vigen (see his page) and Ulü[?] (Ulli Nielsdatter), both rated Probable. This replaces the earlier remark here that Gunnar Olsen was 'probably his father - inference, not added': the 1701 baptism and churching now name the father and mother. ¶5

Son Ingebret: on the 19th Sunday after Trinity 1736 (7 October 1736, computed), 'døbt Ole Gunderz Vigens barn Ingbret' (Selbu ministerialbok 1699-1737, canvas 140 right page). 'Ole Gundersen Vigen' is this Ola, the same form as at Gunnar's baptism in 1727; it is not a second adult Ole. The child is not added to the tree. ¶6

Life story

Baptism: on the 21st Sunday after Trinity, 16 October 1701, 'döbt Gönner Wigens barn, Oluff' - Olof, child of Gunner Vigen; godparents Hans Wigen, Bersvend and Olof of the same farm, Marit Moshelt[?] and Marit Wigen (Selbu ministerialbok 1699-1737, canvas 11 left page). His mother, Gunner's wife Ulü[?], was churched on 20 November 1701 'after the first child Olof'. He is identified with this Olof by his name and patronymic, the farm form 'Vigen' used at his burial, and his age of 58 at burial in December 1759, which points to late 1701. read 1699-1702 and found no other Ole son of a Gunder at Vigen. ¶4

Bygdebok: holder of the Overvik bruk 1729-1772, born 1701, died 1759, married 1726 to Kari, born 1705, died 1773 (Selbu i fortid og naatid vol. 6, p. 326, word boxes). The tenure running past his death is the usual widow's continuation. The previous holder, Gunnar Olsen (1701-1728, died 1731), is by the patronymic probably his father - inference, not added. ¶2

Burial: 2nd Sunday in Advent, 9 December 1759, 'Graufæst (1) Ole Gunars: Vigen 58 aar gl:' - buried Ole Gunnarsen of Vigen, 58 years old, which gives a birth about 1701 (Selbu ministerialbok 1757-1801, canvas 8 right page, crop). ¶1

Probate (skifte) after him, held 17 March 1760 on the farm Wigen in Selbu and entered as No. 1 of 1761 in Skifteprotokoll 5 (24755, fol. 102a-102b, views 118-119): 'Gjør vitterligt, at Anno 1760 d. 17 Martz blev paa gaarden Wigen i Selboe foretaget Skifte efter afgangne Ole Gundersen Wigen, som har efterladt sig hans igienlevende Enke Karen Torstens datter', and as heirs 5 children, 4 sons and a daughter: '1 Sønnen Gunder Olsen 34 aar, 2 Torsten Olsen 30 aar, 3die Sønnen Ingebrigt Olsen 25 aar, 4de Sønnen Hans[?] Olsen 17 aar, 5te Datter Siri Olsdatter 20 aar', all at home on the farm. Lensmann Ole Thomassen took part; the appraisers were [Peder?] Nielsen Wigen and Esten Hove. The widow's guardian ('lauværge') is named Gunder [surname not legible]. ¶8

His probate, 17 Mar 1760 (24755 fol. 102a-102b): 'Ole Gundersen Wigen', widow Karen Torstensdatter, children Gunder 34, Torsten 30, Ingebrigt 25, [Jens?] 17 and Siri 20. It confirms him as the father of Gunnar Olsen Overvik and dates his death before 17 Mar 1760. (Left at its current confidence, who was limited to generation 6; the record is enough for another worker to upgrade him.) ¶10

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed. The 1760 probate is a primary record that names Ole Gundersen Wigen, his widow Karen Torstensdatter and his son Gunder Olsen aged 34, which ties him to his son Gunnar (Gunder) Olsen Overvik (see his page, baptised Dec 1727 as 'Ole Gunders: Vigens barn Gunder') and to his wife Kari Torstensdatter. The ages fit his burial (9 Dec 1759, aged 58) and his sons' baptisms (Gunder 1727, Ingebret 1736). His own link to his parents Gunnar Olsen Vigen and Ulli is unchanged (Probable): the probate does not name them. ¶9

Open questions and the next record to read 2

Would settle it: Gunnar's 1767 burial with his age, or a read of vol. 6 pp. 325-327 on the page. ¶3b

Biography gaps: his exact birth and death dates are not known beyond the baptism and burial entries already cited. No probate is found, and since the 77804 index stops at 1742, the next check is the unindexed Stjør- og Verdal protocol covering 1759-60 (the one before skifteprotokoll 5, 24755, which begins 1760), not yet identified in the drafts. ¶7
