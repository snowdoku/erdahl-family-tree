Life story

Lifetime division: on 24 Feb 1762 (date from the Bamble card index, 7105 view 2457; the register heading reads 'Anno 176[?] den 1ste Febr[?]', Bamble skifteprotokoll I 6a, 1758-63), at Steensland, Joen Thoresen Steensland and his wife Anne Torbiørnsdatter, on account of old age, asked for their estate to be registered and divided among their children while they were still alive, and gave up the farm. Officials: Joen Nielsen [Myre?], probably the lensmann (rural sheriff), and the appraiser Jørgen Holte. A deed of 10 Jul 1752 for land the couple had bought is mentioned. Debts included 35 rd 1 ort 22 sk to Peder Poulsen in Kragerø; the net to divide was 667 rd 2 ort 20 sk, a brother's lot 146 rd 3 ort 17 7/9 sk and a sister's lot 73 rd 1 ort 20 8/9 sk. Fol. 252-253a. ¶2

Burial: 'Joen Toresen' of Steensland, aged '77 aar og nogle uger' (b. about 1687), buried 14 May 1764 (Drangedal ministerialbok 1697-1767, burials p. 26; index pg00000008554999). Name and farm match, and the age suits a man who divided his estate 'on account of old age' in 1762 and whose eldest daughter was born about 1728. He is the only Joen Toresen buried at Steensland in the index. Birth stays BEF 1708; the burial implies about 1687. ¶5

The case for this identification 1

Identity: Joen (Jon) Thoresen of the farm Steensland in Tørdal, an annex of Drangedal, Bratsberg (Telemark). Birth before about 1708 is inferred only from his eldest daughter Aasil, born about 1728; no baptism or census record has been found. ¶1
