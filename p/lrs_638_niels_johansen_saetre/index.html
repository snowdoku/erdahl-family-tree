Life story

Census 1701: 'Niels Johansen', 20 (b. about 1681), Bunde (farmer), on Settre (Sætre), Ytre Sogn, with 'Hermund Knudsen', 19, listed as son (probably a stepson) and the servant Mons Gullachsen, 28 (Digitalarkivet 206, pc00000000629471-73). A Knud Monsen of Sætre has a probate in 1700-1701 (index pa00000000519922, not read), so Niels had probably just married Knud Monsen's widow; Synneve Gregoriusdatter, mother of his children from about 1706, was then a later wife (inference). No Johan(nes) decedent at Sætre appears in the Ytre Sogn probate index 1698-1747, so his father is not found there. ¶4

Sætre in 1701: the probate after 'Knud Mognsen Sættre', 30 May 1701 (1b fol. 333b), divided between his widow Dorette Torsdatter and his children: by his first wife Olle and Sigri, by the last wife Hermund Knudsen and daughter(s); the widow's brother Anders Ladvig acted for her. The first wife's children had received their mother's inheritance at a division of 24 Mar 1680. This explains the 'Hermund Knudsen' 19 listed as a son in Niels Johansen's household at Sætre in the 1701 census: he was Knud Monsen's son, so Niels was probably his stepfather or foster-father on the farm (inference; the bygdebok, canvas 509, gives Hermund Knutsen of Sætre 1682-1716). Niels Johansen's own parents are still not found; the probate does not mention him. ¶5

Probate No. 31, Ytre Sogn 6b fol. 550b, 29 Jul 1748, on the farm Sætre (glebe land, 'Pres[te] gods', in Lavik skipreide) 'efter Niels Johansen Sætre, imellem Enken Synneve Gregori-datter og deris Børn, nemlig: Sønnen Gregorius Nielsen, myndig og tilstede; ældste Datter Dordej 42 aar; 2den Datter Borni Nielsd[atter] gift med Joen Gulløvsen Indre Hiertholm, nærværende; 3die Datter Mari 30 aar; 4de Datter Magdeli gift med Anders Lassesen Tackle paa Nordrevigen[?]; og 5te Datter Haldis Nielsdatter 21 aar gl.' Appraisers included Johannes Ejnersen[?] Krogen; Iver Olsen Sætre and Hans Endresen[?] Ladvig are named at the end of the heading. Gross estate 115 rd. Read from the scan. ¶1

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Graded on his link to his daughter Borni Nielsdatter: the probate names '2den Datter Borni Nielsd[atter] gift med Joen Gulløvsen Indre Hiertholm' as his and the widow's child. The tree's Borni Nielsdatter is the widow of Jon Gulløvsen of Indre Hjartholm in his probate of 1-2 Oct 1773 (Confirmed). Same couple named by full name and farm, 25 years apart; there is one Jon Gulløvsen of Indre Hjartholm (rival: none named). The probate of Borni's sister Haldis, 3 Sep 1750 (7a No. 67, fol. 16b), repeats the same five siblings with 'Borni Nielsdatter gift med Joen Gulløvsen Indre Hiertholm'. ¶2
