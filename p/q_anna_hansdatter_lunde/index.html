About the family

Her father's probate, 29 May 1713 at Lunde (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll 2b, fol. 444b-446a), names the children of the late Hans Hansen and his widow Brede (Brite) Olsdatter as Hans, Johannes, Britte and Anne, and gives a sister's share to '1 Søster Anna'. No age or guardian is written for her. This settles her patronymic Hansdatter and her parents: the 1729 probate after Brite Olsdatter names the same four children, so there was one Anna in this family, unmarried in May 1713, who is the 'Ane Lunde' betrothed to Ole Bale on 10 Feb 1715 and married 23 Jun 1715. Confidence stays Probable only because the church entries give no patronymic and more than one Ole lived at Bale; the parentage itself rests on the probate. ¶10

Life story

Betrothal: 'Dn. V post Epiph. / Ved Tiugum... Ole Bale / Ane Lunde } spon' (sponsalia, betrothal), Leikanger ministerialbok 1690-1734, IIIF canvas 112, left page; the year heading 'ANNO 1715' stands on canvas 111 R. The 5th Sunday after Epiphany 1715 fell on 10 Feb 1715 (computed from Easter 21 Apr 1715). The entry gives no patronymic, father or witnesses. It names the bride Ane (Anna) of Lunde, as Laberg p. 60 has it, and not Brita as on p. 116, so the given name Anna is kept. ¶7

Laberg 1985: the Bale section (p. 60) says Ola Andersson married in 1715 'Anna... Lunde' (the word after Anna is unread) and that their son Hans [stayed] at Lunde. The Lunde section (p. 116) lists the children of Hans Hansson of Lunde (born about 1638, died 1713; wife Brita, died 1728) as Hans, who stayed at Lunde (born 1685, married Gjertrud, died 1760), Johannes, and Brita, married to Ola Andersson Bale, mother of Hans and of Gurid who married Jon Bjørnson Bale. Hans Hansson fits the 1701 census Hans Hansen, 63, at Lunde with sons Hans (about 1685) and Johanis (about 1700). ¶3

Married at Tjugum church on 23 Jun 1715 (1st Sunday after Trinity, computed): 'Ole Bale / Ane Lunde } nupt' (Leikanger ministerialbok 1690-1734, source 11564, canvas 115 L). The marriage entry, like the betrothal, gives her only as Ane (Anna) of Lunde, with no patronymic or father, so her confidence stays Probable and the patronymic Hansdatter is still inferred. ¶9

Probate 12 Feb 1729 at Lunde in Tjugum skipreide after 'den Sal. Qvinde Brite Ols datter', between her children Hans, Johannes, Britte and Anna, all of age and present (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll 3b, fol. 339a). No husband is named, nor any son-in-law. Her sons Hans and Johannes match the sons of Hans Hansen Lunde in the 1701 census, so she is probably his widow (Laberg's 'wife Brita, d. 1728'). ¶5

This probate shows that BOTH a Brita and an Anna were daughters at Lunde, so each of Laberg's two names (Anna p. 60, Brita p. 116) belongs to a real sister. Neither this probate nor Ole Andersen Bale's 1760 probate (which names no widow) says which sister married Ola. Stays Speculative; name unchanged. ¶6a

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

2 on the grade · 1 alternative set aside · 3 open questions

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Stays Speculative: the book contradicts itself on her given name (Anna on p. 60, Brita on p. 116), so it cannot raise her or give her a father. If the Lunde section is right, she is Brita Hansdatter, daughter of Hans Hansson Lunde. ¶4a

Confidence raised from Speculative to Probable on the 1715 Leikanger betrothal entry 'Ole Bale / Ane Lunde' (source 11564, canvas 112): a primary record that matches the year, both farms and the given name of Laberg p. 60 and kgroenha.net. It stays short of Confirmed because the entry gives neither party a patronymic and more than one Ole lived at Bale. Her patronymic Hansdatter is still inferred: the 1729 Lunde probate after Brite Olsdatter names a daughter Anna, and Brite was probably the widow of Hans Hansen Lunde, but no record yet read names Anna's father. The marriage entry itself (nupt.), probably later in 1715 on canvas 113 or after, was not read. ¶8

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Candidate father, not entered: the 1701 census lists Hans Hansen, bonde, age 63 (born about 1638), at Lunde, Leganger parish, Ytre Sogn, page 31, with sons Hans (born about 1685), Olle (about 1697) and Johanis (about 1700) and a servant Zacharias Andersen. The 1701 manntall lists males only, so it cannot show a daughter Anna; he is a candidate on the farm name and the patronymic alone. He is not added as a person because Anna herself is Speculative (kgroenha.net only) and a guessed father would chain another Speculative generation. Whether this Leganger-parish Lunde is the Balestrand Lunde of kgroenha.net is also not checked. ¶2a

Open questions and the next record to read 3

What would settle it: Hans Hansen Lunde's probate (Ytre Sogn, early 1700s) naming a daughter Anna, or Anna's 1715 marriage entry naming her father. ¶2b

What would settle it: the 1715 Leikanger marriage entry of Ola Andersen, Ola Andersen Bale's 1760 probate, or Hans Hansen Lunde's 1713 probate naming his daughter and her husband. ¶4b

What would settle it: the 1715 Leikanger marriage entry, or an 18th-century probate after Ola's wife (before 1760, Ytre Sogn 4-7b). ¶6b
