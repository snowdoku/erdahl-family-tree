About the family

Parentage of Lars Trulsen (Probable): Aaland's succession marks Lars as Truls's son ('S.'). Independently, the 1728 probate after the unmarried Brynilde Trulsdatter of Aaning names her siblings as the late Lars Trulsen Aaning, Jørgen Trulsen Aaning and Christi Trulsdatter, so their father was named Truls and was of Aaning; Aaland's 'Br. Jørgen' (Lars's brother, died 1774 aged 84) matches. ¶2a

Parents (Probable): Laurits Simonsen (Brekke) and Kristi Lassesdatter (Brekke), entered as Laurits Simonsen Aaning and Kristi Lassesdatter Brekke. Aaland names them as his parents; the 1645 Nordfjord poll tax lists 'Lauriz' and 'Kristi' as the only couple on Aaningh (p. 5b); and the 1664 vicar's census gives his patronymic as Lauridtzsøn on the same farm. ¶5

Life story

Aaland 1932: Aaland's Aaning history (bruk nr. 2) lists 'Laurits Simonson Brekke, br. 1620-33 … * Kristi Lassed. Brekke, n. 1646. S. Truls, br. 1664 heile g. S. Lars, † 36 a. g. 1723, br. 1714 heile g.': Truls, son of Laurits Simonsen and Kristi Lassesdatter, farmed the whole of Aaning from 1664, and his son Lars succeeded in 1714. In Aaland's Brekke history a 'Truls Aaning' is a defendant in a 1706 odal suit over 3½ pund in Brekke. He was alive in 1706. Lars's taking the whole farm in 1714 suggests he had died or retired by then, and the 1728 probate after his daughter Brynilde names no parent among her heirs (auditor: death field set to AFT 1706, the only dated fact; the draft's BEF 1714 was an inference). ¶1

1664 vicar's census (p. 211): 'Halff gaard Aaning, ij Løber Smør', sole farmer 'Truels Lauridtzsøn 26 aar': Truls Lauritsen, aged 26, so born about 1638. This agrees with Aaland's note that Truls took the farm in 1664 and confirms his patronymic. No father Laurits is listed on Aaning, and none on Brekke (farmers there: Oluf Lassesen 40, Oluf Johannesen 45, Erik Olsen 48, Lasse Lassesen 42). ¶3

1701 census (Innvik, p. 250, Aaning): 'Troels Lars.', opsidder (occupier), born 1638, with sons Lars (1687) and Jørgen (1693). Born 1638 matches his age of 26 in the 1664 vicar's census. Nedre and Øvre Flo in 1701 had only one inderst (lodger) each, Joen Persen (1631) and Thore Persen (1638). ¶4

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade (regrade 3): Probable to Confirmed as father of Lars Trulsen Aaning (see his page); graded on his link to Lars Trulsen Aaning. Naming record: the 1701 census, Innvik prestegjeld p. 250, Aaning (Digitalarkivet pc624062 ff.): 'Troels Lars.', opsidder, b. 1638, with his sons Lars (b. 1687) and Jørgen (b. 1693). This is the record his own notes named as the test ('the 1701 census for Aaning, Truls with sons Lars about 14 and Jørgen about 11'); it also removes the auditor worry that Aaland's succession might skip a generation. Continuity to the tree's Lars: the tree's Lars is the man of the probate of 4 Jul 1728 after the unmarried Brynilde Trulsdatter of Aaning (Nordfjord skifteprotokoll L0006 fol. 146 and), which names as her heirs the children of the late 'Lars Troelsen Aaning', her brother 'Jørgen Troelsen Aaning' and her sister Christi Trulsdatter. The same pair of brothers, Lars and Jørgen, both Trulsen and both of Aaning, are the two sons of the 1701 household, so the 1728 record identifies Lars by his sibling set (the method audit 5 accepted for), not by name alone. Truls (b. 1638) is the only Truls on Aaning in 1664 and 1701; no rival is named. ¶6a

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Caution: a holder from 1664 would have been born by about 1640, so he was in his mid-40s or older when Lars (about 1687) and Jørgen (about 1690) were born. That is possible, but Aaland's succession could have skipped a generation or a second marriage. ¶2b

Open questions and the next record to read 2

What would confirm it: the 1701 census for Aaning (Truls with sons Lars about 14 and Jørgen about 11), or Truls's own probate (about 1705-1714) in the Nordfjord probate registers. ¶2c

Flag: the 27 years from 1701 to 1728 are bridged by the sibling set, not by a short gap; Aaland's 'br. 1714' is secondary support only. ¶6b
