Life story

Age caution: if she was really born about 1802, she was 43 at Mary Ann's birth (1845) and about 48 at Charles's (about 1850). That is possible, but her 1881 age may be overstated. ¶7

1841 census: she is probably the woman aged 16-45 in Richard Moore's household of ten at Lot 64 (1841 census of PEI, Kings County District 15, image 531), all entered as Baptists (image 532). The 1841 band puts her birth after about 1795, and the 1861 band (45-60) puts it between about 1801 and 1816. Her 1881 age of 79 (born about 1802) sits at the very start of that range. She is probably one of the six English-born members (image 533); the 1861 return counts two natives of England in the household, Richard and his wife. ¶8

Record naming her as a parent: the adult baptism of her daughter Mary Ann (born 20 September 1845, baptised 20 April 1877, parents Richard and Sarah) in the Murray Harbour Bible Christian register (PEI baptisms index, record 72100). ¶2

1861 census, Lot 64: the woman aged 45-60 in Richard Moore's household, one of its two natives of England. ¶3

1881 census, Lot 64: Sarah Moore, 79 (born about 1802), Baptist, English origin, living with William Moore (34), farmer, his wife Mary and their children, next door to her other sons Benjamin, Charles and Humphrey. The transcription gives her birthplace as PEI; the 1861 census, which counts two English-born persons in Richard Moore's family, points to England, and a woman born in 1802 on PEI would be one of very few - the 1881 image should be checked. ¶4

Death: after the 1881 census; not yet found. ¶5

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

The case for this identification 1

Identification: the probable mother of Grace Amelia Moore. Her maiden name is unknown. ¶1

Searches that found nothing 2

Not found in the 1891 census of Lot 64, when she would have been about 89; probably died between 1881 and 1891. Two grandsons were named for her husband (Richard Herbert, son of Benjamin, 1878; Richard, son of William, about 1885) and granddaughters Sarah were born to Grace (1864), Humphrey (about 1877), William (about 1887) and Albert (1887). ¶6

Maiden name test (negative): the middle name Wake in two grandsons (Humphrey Wake Moore, 1874; Joseph Wake Hayter, 1883) was tested as her possible maiden name. No Sarah Wake married a Richard Moore in FreeREG or in the England marriage index (Ancestry 9852) in 1812-1838. The Wake name may honour a neighbour, a minister or a godparent instead. Lead only; see Richard Moore for the English candidates (Martock 1826, Woodbury 1825-31). ¶9
