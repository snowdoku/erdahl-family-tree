About the family

Father of Peder Pedersen Fuglem (55 in 1701, buried 1715 aged 72): the son Peder Pedersen aged 16 at Fuglem in 1664 and 1666 is the only Peder Pedersen there; Peder Pedersen named his eldest son Bersvend (Bersvend Pedersen, 15 in 1701), and the name Bersvend recurs in the next generation (Bersvend Olsen Fuglem). Ages run 3-7 years younger in 1664/66 than the later records imply - usual for these lists. Rated Confirmed for this link. ¶2

Parentage: the 1624 leidang roll for Selbu (39890 view 76 right) lists 'Jon Fuglemb' and 'Bersuend ibid' as the Fuglem holding. Peder Bersvendsen held Fuglem in 1664-66 beside Jon Olsen, so Bersvend of Fuglem is made his father, graded Probable on Bersvend's record (farm succession and patronymic; no record names the relationship). Peder's own grade is unchanged. ¶6

Life story

1666 vicar's manntall, Selbu, no. 3 Fuglem: 'Peder Bersvendsen bruger 4 ørt er 52 Aar', with the sons 'Peder Pedersen 16 Aar, Olle Pedersen 12 Aar, Olle Pedersen 7 Aar' (Digitalarkivet 35528, view 21 left, crop checked by the orchestrator). The 1664 fogd's list has Peder (50) at Fuglem with his son Peder Pedersen (16) (35578 v18 R). Born about 1614; his father was a Bersvend. ¶1

Burial, probably his: 'graffæstet Peder Fuglum ætat 89' (buried 1st Sunday after Trinity 1701, which was 29 May 1701 in the new-style calendar Norway adopted in 1700), Selbu 16310 c10 left page. The age 89 (born about 1612) fits Peder Bersvendsen, who was 50 in 1664 and 52 in 1666, the ages these lists usually understate. It does not fit his son Peder Pedersen, who was 55 in the 1701 manntall and was buried in 1715 aged 72. Identified by name, farm and age only (Probable), so the death date is set as 'before 30 May 1701'. ¶5

What would carry this further: a Stjør- og Verdal probate after him (protocol 1, 1689-1701, source 24749; card index 77804). ¶3

Biography gaps: his birth (c.1614) and marriage (before his children, c.1640s) predate the 1699 Selbu register, so no parish record gives them. His death, death place and burial are not found, and any death before 1699 falls outside the register; the 77804 probate index (1689-1742, only about 45% sampled) has no probate for him read, so the next check is the unindexed Stjør- og Verdal protocol 1 (24749, 1689-1701). ¶4
