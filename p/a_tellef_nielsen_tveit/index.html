About the family

NEW ANCESTOR. His estate was settled at Tveit in Lille Tovdal on 27 May 1703 (Østre Råbyggelag skifteprotokoll no. 2, folio 60b). The card names him and his wife Aase Thorisdatter, and records that the estate — "ni notting sæd" (land sown with nine notting of seed) plus half a hud in Tveit and movables — had to be divided among the creditors. He died insolvent. ¶1

His brothers were Rolf Nielsen Tveit and Olle (Ole) Nielsen Tveit, named on the 1703 card: "Avd.s bror: Rolf Nielsen Tveit" and "Olle" the same, with reference to a purchase contract registered 4 November 1703. ¶4

Life story

The 1703 card names one son, Tjøll (Kjøll) Tellefsen Tveit, who died in 1747; a separate card for the 1747 settlement gives his parents as "? Tellef Nielsen Tveit (+ sk. 1703)" and "? Aase Thorisdtr." ¶2

Our ancestor Thore (Thor) Tellefsen of Tveit, whose estate was settled in 1740, was a second son. The proof is the kinship term used in the 1792-94 probate of Gunlek Aslaksen of Tveit: Gunlek was the son of Aslak Kjøllsen, grandson of Kjøll Tellefsen and great-grandson of Tellef Nielsen Tveit, and the heirs on the father's side are "tremenning" — third cousins — namely the children of Tellef Thorsen Tveit. Third cousins share great-grandparents, so Tellef Thorsen's great-grandfather must also have been Tellef Nielsen Tveit. That fixes the line Tellef Nielsen > Thore Tellefsen > Tellef Thorsen > Thor Tellefsen. ¶3

His parentage is Probable. A separate Ramse card records that Groe Evindsdatter, daughter of Even (Evind) Tellefsen Ramse, married a Nils and that their children were Olle Nielsen Tveit, Rolf Nielsen Tveit, Groe Nielsdatter of Høgeli and Thargier Nielsdatter. Two of those sons are exactly the two brothers named in his 1703 probate, on the same farm in the same years. He himself is absent from that list because he had already died in 1703, before the 1704 deed the card abstracts. ¶5

Corrections 1

Correction: Birth estimate widened from ABT 1665 to BET 1660 AND 1680. No record gives his age. He died in 1703 leaving young sons, Thore (born about 1700) and Kjøll. That suggests a birth in the 1660s or 1670s. His mother Groe Evindsdatter was born about 1640-1660. A birth about 1665 still fits, but only if Groe was born in the 1640s; if she was born about 1650-1655, he was more likely born about 1670-1680. ¶6
