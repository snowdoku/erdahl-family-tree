Life story

Aaland (Stryn vol., Nedre Flo bruk nr. 1 Bendiktbruket, label 404): 'Vs. Laurits (Lasse) Andersson, sk. 1631 p. 3 3/4 pd. Aatte 1 l. i Meira-Sunde. I 1658 aatte han og sonen Anders 1 l. * Helga Øysteinsd., n. 1646.' Followed by Jørgen Lasseson (br. 1674, moved to Bolstad) and Rasmus Lasseson (br. 1686). ¶1

Aaland's account of the 1717 odel case (Stryn vol., Flo, label 398): corporal Ivar Ivarson Flo sued 'Lasse R. N.-Flo' over 1 1/4 pd. in Øvre Flo sold by Ivar's father 'til Lasse sin far, Rasmus N.-Flo'. Lasse produced his title deeds: a 1639 exchange deed 'der bestefaren hans, Lasse Andersson N.-Flo' exchanged 18 mk. against 18 mk. his wife had inherited in Uppheim, and a 1641 deed; 'Sidan erva Lasse sine søner Rasmus N.-Flo eit pd. av same gods og Jørgen L. Bolstad 6 mk.' So Aaland, summarising the court record, makes Lasse Andersson the father of Rasmus Lassesen and grandfather of Lasse Rasmussen Flo. ¶2

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Probable as father of Rasmus Lassesen Flo (see his page), graded on that link: a bygdebok summary of a court record, not read. ¶3a

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed, graded on his link to his son Rasmus Lassesen Flo (see his page). Tingbok Nordfjord L0020 (source 30928), Stryn spring ting at Hilde, 28-29 Mar 1718, views 15R-16L (Aaland's '1717' is the year of the summons). Corporal Iver Iversen Floe sued 'Lasse Rasmussen Nedre Floe' to give up 1 pund 6 marker in Øvre Flo, which Iver said was his forefathers' odel, lost through poverty while Iver was in the King's service; until about five years ago his father, Old Iver Iversen, had used it, until a contract was made with 'Contrapartens Fader Rasmus Lassesen'. Lasse Rasmussen produced two title deeds of 5 Nov 1639 and 18 Nov 1641, 'hvormed hand beviste at hans afgangne Farfader Sal: Lasse Andersen Nedre Floe' had bought the 1 pund 6 marker from several people. He then stated 'at hans Fader Rasmus Floe arvede et heelt pund deraf, og de øvrige 6 marker tilfaldt hans Farbroder Jørgen Lassesen Bolstad', and that after Iver Floe had used the land for some years without lease, a contract was made 'imellem hans afgangne Sal: Fader Rasmus Floe' and the plaintiff's father Old Iver Floe, dated 26 Oct 1712. Judgment: the land had been Lasse Rasmussen's and his forefathers' odel; the suit was dismissed. So his grandson Lasse Rasmussen called him 'hans afgangne Farfader Sal: Lasse Andersen Nedre Floe' in court, and said that his father Rasmus Floe and his farbror Jørgen Lassesen Bolstad inherited the land from him: a primary record naming the relationship, read from the scan. Rasmus Floe of the record is the Rasmus Lassesen of the tree (the plaintiff calls him Rasmus Lassesen, and he is the father of Lasse Rasmussen Nedre Flo). Nothing in the record names Lasse Andersen's wife, so Helga Østensdatter stays Speculative. ¶4
