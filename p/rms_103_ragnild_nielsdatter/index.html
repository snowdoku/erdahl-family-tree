About the family

NEW ANCESTOR, graded on her link to Torjus Ormsen (see his page). She was betrothed on 15 Feb 1726 and married at Topdal on 21 Jun 1726 to Orm Tallachsøn of Skjeggedal (Åmli register 1711-1740, DA 9920 canvas 42, pp. 78-79, read on the scan). Orm Tallaksen is Torjus's father (Tallach Ormsen's 1796 estate names Torjus as his brother). Åmli baptisms of this period name only the father, so no record names her as Torjus's mother. She is the only known wife of Orm, but Torjus was born about 1742, 16 years after the marriage, and a second marriage of Orm in between has not been ruled out. So Probable. ¶1a

Life story

Card 39942/12331 (Tallak Ormsøn's probate, 1719) adds in the archivist's hand, after Orm Tallaksen, '(g.m. Ragnild Nielsdtr. Simonstad?)'; the bygdebok calls her 'Ragnhild Nilsdtr. Vrålstad f 1705 d 1783'. The farm reading is uncertain. Her probate (if she died in 1783) is not among the Skjeggedal cards 12426-12450 (1773-1786: Asborg Taraldsdatter 1773, Torbiørn Kiddelsen 1774, Gunild Knudsdatter 1777, Thore Svendsdatter 1773, Asborg Børgesdatter 1778, Svend Salvesen 1783-86). Still Probable on her link to Torjus. ¶2a

Her burial is found and read. Åmli register 1775-1796 (DA 9923), canvas 119 (book p. 122), first entry of the new church year 1783/84, no. 1: 'd. 2 Novbr Hillestad paa Konen Ragnil Nielsd. Øvre Skiggedal død d. 1 Octob. gamle 78 aar'. So she died at Øvre Skjeggedal on 1 Oct 1783, aged 78 (b. about 1705), as the bygdebok says. No kin are named. What this adds to her link to Torjus Ormsen (see his page): her grade note said that a second marriage of Orm Tallaksen between the 1726 wedding and Torjus's birth (about 1742-44) had not been ruled out. A Ragnild Nielsdatter of Øvre Skjeggedal, of the right age (21 at the 1726 wedding), was alive there until 1783, long after Orm's death (before or about 1762-64), so Orm had no other wife in Torjus's birth window unless the 1783 woman is a different Ragnild Nielsdatter (none is known on the farm). This removes the stated objection, but it is still an elimination argument: no record names her as Torjus's mother (Åmli baptisms of the 1740s name only the father). So she stays Probable, graded on her link to Torjus. ¶3a

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
