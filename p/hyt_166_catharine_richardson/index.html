About the family

Second wife of Capt. Pilgrim Simpkins of Boston (married after Nov. 1660); mother of 'Sarah of Pilgrim & Cathar. Symkins', born 21 Sept. 1668 (Boston register, vol. 9). Her maiden name Richardson is from TAG 27 (1951): 202 (compiled); no marriage record was read. Graded Probable on her link to Sarah Simpkins, for the same reason as her husband. ¶1

Parentage search, negative. None of these gives her parents: TAG 28:87-88 (Holman), Savage 4:101, or a Notes and Queries item in NEHGR 33 (Jan. 1879), pp. 103-104, on her granddaughter Katharine (Simpkins) Stanton. Savage dates the marriage '27 Mar. 1661'; the town register says 27 November 1661, and the register is followed. ¶5a

Life story

Verified from the original: Pilgrim Simpkins's will (Suffolk Probate vol. 22 pp. 60-61, DGS 007703078 image 41) names 'my loving wife Kathrine Sinkins' sole executrix and divides the residue among 'my Children... Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: graded on her link to Sarah Simpkins. Her marriage is in the Boston register: 'Pilgrim Sympkins & Katherine Richardson were married 27th November' 1661, by Gov. Bellingham (Record Commissioners vol. 9; also NEHGR 20 (1866), 'Pilgrim Simpkins was marryed to Katherin Richardson, ye 27th November, 1661'), which settles her maiden name. The birth record 'Sarah of Pilgrim & Cathar. Symkins born Sept. 21' 1668 names her as Sarah's mother; Sarah was baptised at the Old South Church 15 Dec. 1676 as a child of Pilgrim and Katharine, Katharine having been admitted 7 Dec. 1676. On 2 Jan. 1720/1, as widow and executrix of Pilgrim's will (which names 'Sarah Kilbe' among his children), she asked that 'her beloved son-in-law, Christopher Kilby' administer (Suffolk Probate 4383, TAG 28:88 abstract). Continuity: Pilgrim's first wife Miriam died in 1660 and Katharine was his wife from 1661 until his death in 1720, so every child born 1662-1677 was hers; the chain birth 1668 - baptism 1676 - marriage to Christopher Kilby 1691 - 'Sarah Kilbe' 1714 - son-in-law Christopher 1721 has no rival. ¶2a

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Caution: she died in 1721 'aged about 86', so she was born about 1635 and was about 26 at the marriage. 'Richardson' could therefore be a first husband's name rather than her maiden name. No record found settles this. ¶5b

Open questions and the next record to read 2

Flag: the will and petition were read in the TAG abstract, not the probate image. ¶2b

Test record: a Boston or Suffolk record before 1661 of a Katherine Richardson (a probate of a Richardson naming a daughter or widow Katherine). ¶5c
