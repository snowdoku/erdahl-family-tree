Life story

Aaland 1932: Aaland lists him directly after '(Br.) Rasmus Lasseson, br. 1686', who leased 18 merker of crown land 'which his father gave up' and married '?…… Øysteinsdatter' (given name left blank in print). Aaland does not mark Lasse as Rasmus's son ('S.'), so the parentage rests on the patronymic, the holding passing from Rasmus to Lasse, and Lasse's naming a son Rasmus: see Rasmus Lassesen Flo. ¶5

Probate continued: the eldest son's age reads 'Aamund Lassesen [?] 2[3] Aar', i.e. 23 (second digit read from the same raised 3-form as the year; Probable). Aaland's 'Amund, † 48 a. g. 1756' gives birth about 1708, age 23/24 in 1732, which agrees. Crop:. ¶7

Tingbok 30928 (Stryn ting at Hilde, 28-29 Mar 1718, views 15R-16L): as 'Lasse Rasmussen Nedre Floe' he won an odel suit brought by corporal Iver Iversen Floe over 1 pund 6 marker in Øvre Flo. He produced deeds of 1639 and 1641 of his late farfar Lasse Andersen Nedre Floe, and stated that his late father Rasmus Floe inherited one pund of it and his farbror Jørgen Lassesen Bolstad the other 6 marker. This names his father and grandfather in a primary record and shows he was holding the land in 1718. ¶10

Probate: Nordfjord sorenskriveri skifteprotokoll L0007 (1731-1734), fol. 151, read from the scan. Probate (skifte) after the late Lasse Rasmussen who lived at Nedre Flo in Stryn skipreide (court sub-district), held 10 October 173[1 or 2] (last year digit uncertain). Heirs: the widow [?]gne Aamundsdatter (given name only partly legible; her guardian Østen Hilde) and the children of the marriage: sons Aamund (age 2[?]), Ole (16), Peder (14) and Rasmus (1[1?]); daughters Mallene (married, husband Anders [?]), Helge (married to [?] Rasmussen of Øvre Flo) and Ragnilde, 'now 19 years old'. ¶2

Aaland 1932: Aaland's Nedre Flo history (bruk nr. 1, 'Bendiktbruket') reads 'Lasse Rasmusson, † 51 a. g. 1733, br. 1716. Aatte 3½ pd. og ymse sylv. * Malene Aam.d., † 76 a. g. 1757': he died in 1733 aged 51 (born about 1682), was tenant from 1716, owned 3½ pund and some silver, and married Malene Aamundsdatter. His death year supports 1733 for the uncertain last digit of his probate date. Aaland's son 'Amund, † 48 a. g. 1756, sk. 1739' is the probate's son Aamund. ¶4

'Lasse Floe', whose daughter married Rasmus Jørgensen Erdal about 1738 (guardianship note, 1740). Probably the Lasse Rasmussen of Nedre Flo whose probate was registered in 1731-34 (protocol 7). Identification Probable: the patronymic is not given in the 1740 note. ¶1

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Confidence moved Probable to Confirmed: the probate text shows that Lasse Rasmussen of Nedre Flo had an unmarried daughter Ragnilde aged 19 in 1731/32 (born about 1712/13). That matches Ragnilde Lassesdatter's burial age of 70 in 1783 and the 1740 note that Rasmus Jørgensen Erdal married 'Lasse Floes datter' about 1738. Ragnilde was the only unmarried daughter in 1731/32 (her sisters Mallene and Helge were already married), and Ragnilde's own children Malene and Brithe (Brithe later at Nedre Flo) repeat the family's names. He died shortly before October 1731/32. ¶3

Corrections 2

Correction: death date narrowed from 'BET 1731 AND 1734' to 'BEF 10 OCT 1732'. The year of his probate session, Nordfjord skifteprotokoll L0007 fol. 151 (image 155), re-read at x4-x6: 'Anno 17[3]2 den 10de Octob.'. The last digit is a closed blob-form 2, not a 1: this scribe's 1 is a single plain stroke (Peder '14', Rasmus '11' on the same page), and the same blob 2 and raised 3 forms recur in the son Aamund's age. Reading 1732 is Probable, not certain (bitonal 2304-px scan). Either reading puts his death before 10 Oct 1732, so Aaland's '† 1733' is wrong or approximate; the contemporaneous probate wins. Ragnilde 'now 19' in Oct 1732 gives birth about 1713, agreeing with her 1783 burial age 70. Crop:. ¶6

Correction: the 1738 settlement of his widow Mallene Aamundsdatter (Nordfjord A 8, 1738, 'Nedre Floe', 24225 view 46) dates the probate after the sons' 'Sal. Fader' as 'Skiftebrevet af dato 10de October 1733', written clearly by a different scribe. That agrees with Aaland's '† 1733'. Against it, the sons' ages fit 1732 slightly better (Peder 14 and Rasmus 11 in the probate, 20 and 17 in 1738). The death date is widened from BEF 10 OCT 1732 to BEF 10 OCT 1733, which holds under either reading. ¶8
