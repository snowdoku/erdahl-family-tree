About the family

NEW ANCESTOR, graded on his link to his daughter Guro Evindsdatter (see her page). Åmli ætt og heim II (Gjøvdal), Oland L.nr. 62 / bruk nr. 5 (canvas 264): 'Dette bruket fekk Eivind Gunnarson ved [?] 1640-åra.' Summary: 'Eivind Gunnarson Oland f 1594 d 1650. [g m] Birgit [?]. Born: [a son] 1637 m. Birgit [?] Askland; Ingeborg m. 1. Tolv Eivindson Oland, 2. [?] Eivind Homdrom, 3. [?] Olson; Guri [g m] Jørgen Olson Oland; Anne 1641 m. Halvor Olson Jørundland.' The widow Birgit then married Olav Eivindson (L.nr. 60, canvas 268: 'Olav Eivindson f 1627, skifte 1686', whose wife Birgit was the widow of Eivind Gunnarson, 'truleg dotter av Knut'). On the next page (canvas 265): 'Jørgen Olson [Harstveit] f 1640, skifte 1707, [g m] Guro [?] Oland... Guro hadde arva [jord] etter foreldra'. The book's L.nr. 57 (Harstveit, canvas 210) says an Eivind moved from Harstveit to Oland in the 1620s. Bygdebok only (compiled secondary source, text rebuilt from word boxes, dates ABT), so Probable at most. ¶1
