Daughter of Ole Gundersen Slind and Ingeborg Olsdatter, twin of Johan, born 21 February 1914 in Selbu. In the 1920 census of her father's farm at Kjosnes sondre; emigrated with the family on the Stavangerfjord on 7 June 1922. ¶6

Life story

She is Kristine Olsdatter Slind, b. 21 Feb 1914, the twin daughter of Ole Gundersen Slind and Ingeborg, enumerated in the 1920 census of Selbu at Kjøsnes søndre and brought to America with the whole household on the Stavangerfjord on 7 June 1922 (both transcribed by the sister agent selbu-slind). Her twin brother Johan was born the same day. Her married surname was EMERSON. ¶3

Her twin brother Johan (John) was born the same day, 21 February 1914, and is buried at Selbu Cemetery. ¶9

Her death is not in the Washington indexes under either Slind or Emerson in the years checked, so she probably died outside Washington or after 2024. The next record for her is her own obituary, or the Emerson plot at Selbu Cemetery. ¶10

Emigrated with her parents and five siblings, Trondheim 7 June 1922 on the Stavangerfjord: emigrant protocol no. 15, page 140, line 30, group 00883, 'barn', residence Selbu. She and her brother Johan on the next line are both entered with the birth year 1914, so they are either twins or one year is a slip of the clerk's pen. ¶1

Marriage: JOSEPH M. EMERSON JR, 10 SEPTEMBER 1934, filed 11 September 1934, Asotin County, Washington - Asotin County Auditor, Marriage Records 1899-2003, document eaasmr1743. She was twenty. Asotin County adjoins Whitman on the south, and the Emersons are one of the larger families in the Selbu Cemetery churchyard, with eleven memorials including "Joseph Sr Emerson, b. 1895 d. 1983, 'Uncle Joe'" and "Brown Emerson, b. 1893 d. 1984, Vet of WWI, s/w & h/o Clara Wigen m. Oct 16 1920". ¶8

HER OWN SOCIAL SECURITY APPLICATION NAMES HER PARENTS. NUMIDENT Application (SS-5) Files 1936-2007, SSN XXX-XX-0349, filed as "CHRISTINE SLIND EMERSON": date of birth 21 February 1914, place of birth SELBU, NORWAY, FATHER: OLE G SLIND, MOTHER: INGEBORE KLEGSETH, cycle date 1971. This single federal record proves her identity, her father's American name form (Ole G., not Ole O.), her mother's maiden name, and her birthplace, and it is the hinge that ties the Washington family to the Selbu parish register. ¶7

THE KEY RECORD OF THIS WHOLE SWEEP. Social Security application (SS-5), an ORIGINAL application, signed by her, filed April 1971: 'CHRISTINE SLIND EMERSON', born 21 February 1914, PLACE OF BIRTH CITY: SELBU, STATE OR FOREIGN COUNTRY OF BIRTH: NORWAY. FATHER'S FIRST NAME: OLE, FATHER'S MIDDLE NAME: G, FATHER'S LAST NAME: SLIND. MOTHER'S FIRST NAME: INGEBORE, MOTHER'S LAST NAME: KLEGSETH. Sex female, race white, 'SIGNATURE CODE: S — Client's signature'. NARA AAD, NUMIDENT Application (SS-5) Files 1936-2007 (Last Names E-G), rid 733121. One sheet of paper, filled in by a 57-year-old woman in America, states in her own hand that she was born in SELBU, that her father was a Slind and that her mother was a Klegseth. It is the first document in this project to put the surname SLIND and the parish SELBU in the same record, and it does so from the American end, independently of the Norwegian parish books. ¶2

That she filed an ORIGINAL application only in 1971, at 57, is itself a fact about her life: she had not needed a Social Security number before, which points to a woman who had not worked in covered employment — a farm wife applying when she came to claim on her husband's record, or when Medicare began to require a number. ¶4
