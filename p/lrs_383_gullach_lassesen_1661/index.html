About the family

Naming support: Iver Gullacksen of Engesætter (d. 1774) named sons Tosten (Torstein Iversen), Hermund, Jon and Gullow. A son Tosten fits a father Gullach Tostensen with an elder son Tosten; under the old reading 'Lassesen' no son Lasse was found, which was a point against. This strengthens, but does not prove, the link; confidence stays Probable. ¶5a

Father (Probable): Tosten, farmer on Engesetter in the bailiff's census of 21 Jun 1664 (35573 viewer 130, s.240), where had read the name as 'Østen[?] (Ehsten)'. Re-read at 2.2x, it opens with the same 'Th' ligature as the next farm name 'Thouning' and reads 'Thosten'. He is the only Tosten in Brekke sogn in 1664; Gullach (about 3) was too young to be listed. See Tosten for the argument and what is open. ¶8

Wife (Probable, bygdebok): Siri Iversdatter (about 1667-1737), married about 1693; Brekke herred bygdebok vol. 2 (canvas 510) gives '[Gullak] Torsteinson' b. 1662, d. 1742, user of Engesæter bnr. 52 1694-1733, with children Torstein 1694, Agate 1696, Ivar 1699, Ingeborg 1704, Ragnhild 1708 and a son b. 1711. Canvas 509: his father Torstein, user 1659-1694, married 1653 an Ingeborg [..] (gap) and had 9 children, among them Randi 1654, Kari 1656, [Gullak] 1662, Jon 1664, Johannes 1668, Jens 1673, Lisbet 1679 (m. 1706 Hermund Knutsen of Sætre) and Ragnhild 1680 (m. 1706 Jon Torkelsen, Engesæter bnr. 53). No probate after Gullak Tostensen was found in the Ytre Sogn index (Engeset, 1728-1750). ¶9

Life story

1701 census: 'Gullach Lassesen', aged 40, occupant on Engesetter (Bondegaard), Eivindvik (Gulen) parish, with sons 'Lasse Gullachs[en]' 7 and 'Iver Gullachs[en]' 2, and the servant Rognald Monsen, born in Sogn, 23. Birth about 1661 is inferred from the age of 40. Manntall 1701, Digitalarkivet 38455, viewer 55, s.93 and. ¶1

Engesætter in 1719: on 31 Mar 1719 at Brekke, at the baptism of Tosten, son of Jon Engesætter and Magnild, the godparents were 'Gullig Toens: Engesætter, Gullig ibid, Iver Engesætter og hans qvinde Birthe, Kari Iversdr: ibid' (Gulen ministerialbok 11438, canvas 34, right page). So two men named Gullak lived on Engesætter in 1719, and one of them was a son of a Toen (Tosten or Tor). Which of them is this person is not settled here. ¶6

Alive 22 Nov 1720: 'Gullich Tostens: Engesætter' was first godfather at Brekke on 22 Nov 1720 to Kari, child of Iver Engesætter and Birthe (Gulen 11438 c52 right page, foot). The 1701 census also had an Iver Torkilsen, 32, on Engesætter; the 1720 father is probably he rather than Gullach's son Iver (b. about 1699), but this is not settled. ¶7

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the grade · 1 record that disagrees · 1 correction · 1 alternative set aside · 2 open questions

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Confidence raised from Speculative to Probable, as father of Iver Gullacksen of Engesetter (see his page). The grade was held at Speculative because the other Gullach on Engesetter in 1701, Gullach Torkilsen (36, sons Jon 3 and Tour 1), could have had a son Iver after 1701. That rival is now excluded: the probate after Gullov of Engesætter, 22 Aug 1741 (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll 6a, No. 11, fol. 153a-154a), lists his sons as Joen Hiertholm, To[e]n[?] Hiertholm and Lars Engesætter only, and 'Iver Engesætter' sat in that probate as an appraiser, not as an heir. Gullach Lassesen's 'Iver Gullachs[en]', 2 in 1701, is thus the only Iver Gullachsen known on the farm. Still not Confirmed: no record yet names Gullach Lassesen as Iver's father (his probate has not been found in the index; the Engeset rows 1698-1772 hold none for him), and Iver's youngest daughter Ingeborg (b. about 1761) would make him about 62 at her birth. ¶3

Records that disagree 1

Against or open: a second Gullach lived on Engesetter in 1701, 'Gullach Torkils[en]' 36, with sons Jon 3 and Tour 1, who could have had a son Iver after 1701; Iver Gullacksen's youngest daughter Ingeborg was only 12 1/2 in 1774 (born about 1761), when a man born in 1699 would have been about 62, which fits a later-born Iver better; and none of Iver's other sons (Hermund, Tosten, Jon) is named Lasse. ¶2b

Corrections 1

Correction: his patronymic in the 1701 census reads 'Tostens[en]', not 'Lassesen', and his elder son is 'Tosten', not 'Lasse'. Re-read at 3x from the scan (38455 viewer 55, s.93): 'gullach Tostens: 40: Tosten gulachs. 7, Ifver gullachs. 2', servant Rognild Monsen 18. Digitalarkivet's transcription agrees independently: 'Gullach Tostensen' b.1661, 'Tosten Gulachsen' b.1694, 'Ifver Gulachsen' b.1699 (pc00000000629530-629532). The name is changed to Gullach Tostensen; the id is kept. The servant at the end of the next household, read earlier as 'Lasse', is 'Lasse Lau[r]sen'/'Lasse Gullachsen' 16 and belongs to Gullach To[e]nsen's household, which is likely where the 'Lasse' reading came from. ¶4

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Possible father of Iver Gullacksen of Engesetter (see his page), Speculative: for: the 1701 'Iver Gullachs[en]' 2 (born about 1699) on Engesetter is the only Iver Gullachsen found there, the 1774 probate places Iver Gullacksen on the same farm, an age of about 75 at death is plausible, and Iver named a son Gullow (Gullak) after his father. ¶2a

Open questions and the next record to read 2

Would settle it: a Gulen baptism of Iver about 1697-1700 or 1702-1715 naming his father, the continuation of Iver's 1774 probate on p. 360 (ages), or a probate after Gullach Lassesen or Gullach Torkilsen of Engesetter naming their heirs. ¶2c

Would settle it: a probate after Gullach Tostensen or his wife (none found under Engeset in the Ytre Sogn index 1698-1745, nor under firstname Gul*/lastname Tost*). ¶5b
