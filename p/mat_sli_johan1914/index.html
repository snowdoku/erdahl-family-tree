About the family

Son of Ole Gundersen Slind and Ingeborg Olsdatter, twin of Kristine, born 21 February 1914 in Selbu. In the 1920 census of his father's farm at Kjosnes sondre; emigrated with the family on the Stavangerfjord on 7 June 1922. ¶6

Life story

Arrival, Confirmed, and it dates the whole family's crossing: 'Johan Slind', male, aged 8, born 1914, single, residence 'Selbu, Norway', nationality 'Norway, Scandinavian', arrived ELLIS ISLAND on 20 JUNE 1922, ship Stavangerfjord, departure port Christiania, manifest line 20, NARA microfilm T715 roll 3129 frame 290. Thirteen days from the Trondheim police desk to New York. ¶2

Social Security: a DEATH CLAIM was filed on the account of JOHN SLIND, born 21 Feb 1914, at the SPOKANE, WASHINGTON field office in 1957, and the Death file records JOHN SLIND, b. 21 Feb 1914, died December 1957 (day not captured). NARA AAD, NUMIDENT Claim Files (Last Names R-T) rid 2602710, and Death Files (Last Names S-T) rid 2490719. A death claim is a survivor's claim, so he left a widow or children to file it. The Death file posting is dated 30 Dec 1987, thirty years after the event, which is the usual signature of a pre-1962 death folded into the file retrospectively. ¶3

A FLORENCE A. SLIND, b. 16 Nov 1909 (one record says 9 Nov 1909), filed a life claim at the SAME Spokane field office in 1972 and died 16 May 1991 at ZIP [withheld], Spokane. She is the obvious candidate for the widow who filed the 1957 death claim, but nothing read says so. ¶5

Twin of Kristine, born 21 February 1914 at Kjosnes sondre, Selbu; came to America with the household in 1922, aged eight. ¶7

Burial: Selbu Cemetery, Whitman County, "John Slind, b. 1914 d. 1957" (hrp-cem-whi-selbu_0124). He lies in the same block of ten Slind stones as his father, his father's two Ingeborgs, his brother Gilbert, Gilbert's wife and two of Gilbert's children. ¶10

Find a Grave 32434403 gives the full dates from the stone: "John Slind, 21 Feb 1914 - 23 Dec 1957", Selbu Cemetery, La Crosse, Whitman County, Washington. The birth date is exactly that of Johan Olsen Slind in the 1920 Selbu census and of his twin sister Kristine. ¶11

Emigrated with his parents and five siblings, Trondheim 7 June 1922 on the Stavangerfjord: emigrant protocol no. 15, page 140, line 31, group 00883, 'barn', residence Selbu, destination 'U.S. Amerika'. ¶1

Marriage: DARLENE CARDINAL, Whitman County, Washington - application 21 August 1944, licence 24 August 1944, Whitman County Auditor marriage-licence applications, document eawhmapp789. ¶8

Death: December 1957, aged 43. Social Security NUMIDENT death file, SSN XXX-XX-4046, "JOHN SLIND, b. 21 February 1914, d. December 1957" - the birth date matching the Selbu census to the day. ¶9

The case for this identification 1

The identification. The 1920 census of Selbu at Kjøsnes søndre lists JOHAN OLSEN SLIND, b. 21 FEBRUARY 1914, twin of Kristine, son of Ole Gundersen Slind and Ingeborg; the family emigrated together in June 1922. The birth date matches TO THE DAY, and his twin sister Kristine is independently fixed in the same Social Security series under the married name Emerson, with the same parents named (see mat_fed_christine_slind). He died at 43. ¶4
