About the family

Children named in the Haram probates of 1752 and 1775 and in Borgund baptisms: Anne (born about 1729/30), Ingeborg (about 1733), Jørgen (baptized 15 Jan 1735), Ole (about 1738-40), Anne (baptized 21 Feb 1740) and Jetmund (baptized 5 May 1743). ¶3

His father is now identified as Jørgen Aamundsen of Alvestad in Haram (see that record for the reasoning). Paul came to Store Kalvøy from outside the farm: the 1701 male census shows Kalvøen held by three tenants, Peder (born about 1652), Jetmund (about 1655) and Ole (about 1654), with Ole's sons Jetmund (1689) and Hans (1694), and no Jørgen anywhere on the island. ¶5

More children than previously recorded. The Borgund church book 1698-1739 has 'Povel Jørgenss.' of Store-Kalvøen as father of children baptized 15 September 1726, 25 November 1727, 15 January 1735 (Jørgen) and 20 July 1738, besides the baptisms of Anne (21 Feb 1740) and Jetmund (5 May 1743) already known. His wife was churched after childbirth at Store Kalvøen as 'Bereth Olufsdtr.' on 20 October 1726 and as 'Brith Olsdtr.' on 4 January 1728, which fits the births of September 1726 and November 1727 and confirms Berethe Olsdatter as the mother of his children. ¶6

Mother: entered as Anne Povelsdatter, who married Jørgen Aamundsen at Haram on 20 January 1694 (index). This link is Speculative and she carries that label. His own Confirmed confidence rests on his link to his father and on his own records and is not lowered. His name Povel fits naming after a maternal grandfather Povel, which supports the link but does not prove it; no record yet names his mother; see her notes. ¶10

Life story

As 'Povel Jørgensen', a bachelor of Store Kalvøen, he was engaged 22 Apr 1725 at Borgund to Berethe Olsdatter, a maid of Store Kalvøen. The witnesses were Jetmund Knudsen and Einer Einersen, both of Store Kalvøen. He was a witness at Store Kalvøen in 1742. ¶2

His neighbours on Store Kalvøen through these years were Jetmund Knudsen (his betrothal witness in 1725), Jetmund Olsson - who is the 'Jetmund Oels.' born about 1689 listed as a son on the farm in the 1701 census - and Einer Einersson, the other 1725 witness. He named a son Jetmund in 1743, which suggests how close these households were. ¶7

Named as father ('Povel Jørgensen, Store Kalvøen') in the 1735 baptism of his son Jørgen, so that link is Confirmed. The chain down to John Jørgensen is Probable. ¶1

Death: Speculative. A probate for 'Paul Jørgensen', Store Kalvøy, Borgund was registered 7 Nov 1749 (Sunnmøre, 3A 17, fol. 66b). That fits the 1752 and 1775 Haram probates, where he is entered together with his children ('m. barna'). The 1749 skifte could instead be for his wife. ¶4

Probate: the protocol page has now been read (3A 17, fol. 66b; image 24558/80). On 7 November 1749 a registration and probate was held at Store Kalvøen after the late Poul Jørgensen. The parties were his surviving wife Brithe Olsdatter, with her legal guardian (lauværge) Peder Erichsen Sevrig, and the deceased's children, perhaps described as 'begotten with her' (that phrase is uncertain): Jørgen Poulsen, 14, and Ole Poulsen, 11, both with guardian (formynder) Ole Olsen Gamlem; Jetmund Poulsen, 6, with guardian Hans Jetmundsen Kalvøen; Anne Poulsdatter, 20, with guardian Peder Sevrig; Ingeborg Poulsdatter, 16, with guardian Einer Kalvøen; and the younger Anne Poulsdatter, aged perhaps 9, with guardian Ole Olsen Velle (farm uncertain). The estate was appraised by Ole Gamlem and Peder Sevrig at about 138 riksdaler gross. ¶8

Corrections 1

Correction: the death note above called this probate Speculative and suggested it might be his wife's. The page shows that it is his own estate and that his wife survived him, so he died before 7 November 1749 (Confirmed by this record). The children's ages fit the baptisms of Jørgen (January 1735), Ole (July 1738), Anne (February 1740) and Jetmund (May 1743). The record names no parents or siblings of either spouse. ¶9
