Life story

Widow at Vangberg, Frosta; mother of Peder Olsen of Breiset (see his page). ¶1

Frostaboka VII p. 543 (Vangberg nedre, gn. 44) gives her husband as Ole Jakobsen, who took the bygsel in 1723 and died before 1762, and her death as about 1765; it lists the same children with birth years (Jakob 1710, Augustinus 1712, Kirsti 1717, Peder 1718, Nils 1719, Ole 1723, Ole 1724, Arnt 1726). ¶3

1 on the grade

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Graded on her link to her son Peder of Breiset (see his page). Her probate, held 14 Feb 1765 at Vangberg, Frosta (Stjør- og Verdal skifteprotokoll 5, source 24755, fol. 455b, scan), after 'afg. Enke Gunild Niels Dater', names her heirs as 7 sons and 1 daughter: '1. Jacob Olsen Wanberg 53 aar, 2. Augustinus Olsen Moenes, 3. Arn Olsen Frost 39 aar, 4. Peder Olsen Breset 47 aar, 5. Niels Olsen Breiset 46 aar, 6. Ole Olsen Frostad, 7. Ole Olsen Wangberg 41 aar, 8. Kirstie Ols Dater Wangberg 38 aar'. Continuity: 'Peder Olsen Breset, 47' (b. c.1718) is the Peder of Breiset in the tree, who had a son Jens baptised at Breiset in 1758 and is listed as the leilending there in the 1762 extra tax, three to seven years before the probate. His brother Niels Olsen also held part of Breiset, but there was no other Peder there, so there is no rival. ¶2
