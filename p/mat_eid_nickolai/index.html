Life story

Known as Nick. Minnesota death index: born 29 September 1904 in Minnesota, 'child of KULSETHMO (mother)', died 1 November 1981 in Olmsted County. SSN [number withheld] (Minnesota). Buried with Zelda Mae (Snook) Eidem, 1906–1989. ¶1

He is not buried at Zoar; his memorial is 104462973. 'Nick married Zelda Snook, and they adopted Jerry and Robert, as children and were thrilled to have the boys and their families.' ¶3

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

Corrections 1

Correction: his father was ANDERS ANDERSSON EIDEM (1852/3-1932), not Mikal Paulsen Eidem. Find a Grave memorials 136223223 and 139948155 both list Nikolai 'Nick' Eidem (1904-1981) among the children of Anders Andersson Eidem and Brynhild Kulsetmoen. ¶2
