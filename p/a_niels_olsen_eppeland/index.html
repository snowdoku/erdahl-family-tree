About the family

NEW ANCESTOR. Father of Christi (Kristi) Nielsdatter and so the great-grandfather of Tellef Torsen Ramse (b. 1795). ¶1

His parents are named on the archivist's cross-reference card: "Avds far: Ole Ormsen Eppeland" and "mor: Anna Svenningsdtr.", both with estates relinquished ("oppg. sk.") on 11 December 1747. His second wife Ingeborg Svenningsdatter was presumably a kinswoman of his own mother Anna Svenningsdatter. ¶4

Life story

Marriage: betrothed 20 Jun 1726 and married 12 Jul 1726 at Omblie (Åmli) to Inger (Ingri) Knudsdatter, 'Niels Olsøn Eppeland' (Åmli register 1711-1740, DA 9920, canvases 42-43, pp. 79-80, read on the scan). No fathers or sureties are named. ¶6

Second marriage: his wife Inger Knudsdatter was buried at Septuagesima (21 Jan) 1742, and on 7 Apr 1742 'Niels Olsøn Eppeland og Ingeborg Svenningsdatter Eppeland' were betrothed at Omblie, sureties Orm Olsøn and Evind Svenningsøn Eppeland (Åmli register 1741-1756, DA 9921 canvas 2, read on the scan). ¶7

His estate was settled on 19 July 1755 (Østre Råbyggelag skifteprotokoll no. 12b, folio 313). The card names his widow Ingebor Svenningsdatter and separates the children by marriage. "Barn av 1ste ekteskap med Yngri Knudsdatter": Christj Nielsdatter, 21 years old (our ancestor, born about 1734) and Anna, 18. "Barn med igjenlevende hustru" (children with the surviving wife): Ole Nielsen and Svenning Nielsen. ¶2

The estate was deeply insolvent: movables of 62 riksdaler 1 ort 6 skilling against debts of 513-1-18. ¶3

The same card adds the marriage that matters for us: "Christi Nielsdtr. senere g.m. Tergie Tiøstelsen + sk. på Eppeland 10/1-1769". ¶5
