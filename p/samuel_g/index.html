Life story

Baptized 15 Jan 1774 at Hilde (Innvik), son of Guttorm Andersen and Anna Larsdatter. Confirmed 1789 at Hilde (born about 1773). Servant at Innvik vicarage in 1801. Married Martha Halstensdatter of Loen on 20 Jun 1807 at Innvik (he was then of Hilde). Parentage Probable: he is the only Samuel Guttormsen in the parish. ¶2

Farmer at Loen, Stryn. Also father of Halsten (baptized 1807). ¶1

Probate, 1809: named as husband of Marthe Halstensdatter in the probate of her parents Halsten Rasmussen and Gunnild Andersdatter of Loen (Nordfjord skifteprotokoll A 21b, pp. 1033, 1040-1041). He was appointed guardian (formynder) of his sister-in-law Gjertrud Halstensdatter, 18. ¶3

Aaland 1932: Aaland's Hilde farm history (Del 2 3 2, pp. 26-39) does not mention Samuel; it lists only the holders of each bruk and their wives. It places Guttorm Andersson and Anne Larsdatter of Loen on Hilde bruk nr. 4 from 1763, with a successor Anders Guttormson (born about 1773), which fits the household of Samuel's 1774 baptism. The book therefore neither adds to nor contradicts his parentage, which stays Probable on the baptism, the 1789 confirmation at Hilde and the 1807 marriage 'of Hilde'. The Hilde bruk nr. 4 probate (skifte) after Guttorm Andersson, who died in 1817 per Aaland, should name his children and would settle it. ¶4

Probate, 1812: on 29 Apr 1812 his parents Guttorm Andersen Hilde and wife Anne Larsdatter had a lifetime division (skifte i levende live: they handed their property to their heirs, who took over their debts) and named their heirs as their children: 1. eldest son Anders Guttormsen, of age, on Hilde; 2. second son Samuel Guttormsen, of age, living on Loen; 3. third son Guttorm Guttormsen, 21; 4. daughter Giertrud, 30, whose guardian was her brother Samuel Guttormsen Loen; 5. daughter Anne, 19 (Nordfjord skifteprotokoll A 21b, pp. 1591-1593). ¶5

Probate, 1818: in the probate after his father, the late Guttorm Andersen Hilde, registered 27 May 1818, 'Sønnen Samuel Guttormsen, fuldmyndig og boende paa Gaarden Loen' (the son Samuel Guttormsen, of age, living on the farm Loen) is the second of four heirs. He was appointed guardian of the three children of his dead elder brother Anders Guttormsen Hilde, as their farbror (father's brother) (Nordfjord skifteprotokoll A 22b, fol. 595-596). ¶6

Aaland 1932: Loen bruk nr. 15, printed p. 322: 'Vs. Samuel Gutormson Hilde, † 59 a. g. 1832, sk. 1789, br. 1802. * Marte Hallst.d. L., † 81 a. g. 1857.' He was son-in-law of the tenant Hallstein Rasmussen, took the deed in 1789 and farmed the holding from 1802; he died in 1832 aged 59, which agrees with his 1774 baptism and 1832 burial. His father Guttorm A. Hilde had owned the holding since 1773 through Samuel's mother Anne Larsdatter, daughter of the previous owner Lars Samuelsen; this is how Samuel of Hilde came to live on Loen. ¶8

Aaland 1932: the successor was his son 'S. Hallstein, † 46 a. g. 1853, sk. 1840. * Anne Jørgensd. Erd., † 74 a. g. 1875' (Halsten, baptized 1807, deed 1840, died 1853 aged 46, married Anne Jørgensdatter of Erdal); then Anders Guttormsen Meling, who married the widow Anne (farmer 1855), and Samuel Hallsteinsen (deed 1856). ¶9

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Confidence raised from Probable to Confirmed: both probates (A 21b pp. 1591-1593, 1812, and A 22b fol. 595-596, 1818) name Samuel Guttormsen of Loen as the son of Guttorm Andersen Hilde and Anne Larsdatter. His parentage no longer rests on his being the only Samuel Guttormsen in the parish. ¶7
