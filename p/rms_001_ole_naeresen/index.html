About the family

Parents linked (Probable): Nære Joensen Tveit and Gunild Gunsteensdatter. Three cards support this. His mother's 1700 estate names a son 'Olle (senere Austenaa i Tovdal)'. His father's 1720 estate names Ole among the heirs. His brother Gunsten Næresen's 1758 estate names 'Ole Næresen Austenaa i Tovdal'. He was an heir in 1700, so he was born before 9 Jun 1700. The archivist's note on his own 1761 card names other parents, Mære Børulsen Smedland and Ingri Christensdatter, and another card calls him 'Ole Nerresson (Smeland)'. The protocol entries behind the 1700, 1720 and 1758 cards, or the 1761 entry itself (Skp. 12b fol. 439), would settle which is right. Note on age: born before 1700, he would have been in his late 40s or 50s when his children were born in 1738-52, which is possible but makes the Smeland identification worth testing. ¶5

Life story

The 1758 probate after Gunsten Næresen of Midtre Skjeggedal names 'Ole Næresen Austenaa i Tovdal' as his brother. The same card gives the deceased's father as Nære Joensen Tveit (probate 19 Mar 1720) and his mother as Gunild Gunstensdatter (probate 9 Jun 1700). Other siblings named are Joen Næresen Tveit (probate 1740) and Ingeborg Næresdatter, wife of Salve Svendsøn Askland (probate 1754). This supports the Tveit parentage of Ole Næresen against the Smeland note on his 1761 card (Østre Råbyggelag skiftekort, Skp. 12b fol. 374b, cards 12391-12397). ¶4

Protocol check (Skp. 12b read from the scans): Gunsten Næresen's estate at Skjeggedal, 17 Mar 1758 (fol. 374b), names 'Anden Broder Ole Nærresøn Østenaae' (second brother Ole Næresen of Østenaa/Austenaa) among the heirs, alongside the dead eldest brother Joen Nærresøn Tved (9 children; the eldest son Ole Joensøn Tved is guardian of his younger siblings) and the sister Ingeborg Nærresdatter, wife of Salve Svendsøn Askland. In 1758 Ole lives at Østenaa, not Oland, and no heir is called 'Ole Næresen Oland'. The first probate at Oland in Gjøvdal, 9 Feb 1761 (fol. 439a), is after 'Ole Nærresen'. It names his widow Ulvild Evensdatter, her son Even Michelsen (also written 'Even Michelsen Østenaae'), and the deceased's heirs: son Nære Olsen 12, and daughters Gunild 23, Sigri 20, Astri 17 (age uncertain) and Ingri 9. The guardian names are only partly legible (one may be Ole Halvorsen Dale). No Tveit relative, no 'farbroder' and no statement of origin was found in the opening paragraph, and nothing there mentions Smedland. The parentage stays Probable. Both texts tie Ole to Østenaa and to Ulvild's Michelsen family, which fits the Tveit identification, but neither text states that the 1758 brother and the 1761 decedent are the same man. ¶6

Guardian lines read (Skp. 12b fol. 439a, image 221; the scan is bilevel and several words stay uncertain). The heirs are numbered across both of the widow's marriages: 1 Even Michelsen, of age; 2 Nære Olsen, 12, guardian 'Engebret[?]...[?]laag' (surname uncertain: 'Hellelaag'/'Tellelaag'); 3 Anna Michelsdatter; 4 Gunnild Olsdatter, 23, unmarried, guardian 'Ole N[iel?]sen Dale' (uncertain); 5 Sigri Olsdatter, 20, unmarried, guardian Ole Halvorsen Dale; 6 Astri Olsdatter, c.17, guardian '...[?]land' (illegible, possibly 'Lichland'); 7 Ingri Olsdatter, 9, guardian apparently her half-brother Even Michelsen Østenaae. No guardian is called Tved/Tveit, Skjeggedal or Askland, no relation word (farbroder, brodersøn) was seen, and the deceased's origin is not stated. The widow Ulvild Evensdatter's lagverge (guardian at law) could not be read with certainty. The closing on fol. 439b (image 222) is the standard formula about the heirs' shares and names no kin. No confidence upgrade: the parentage stays Probable. ¶7

Probate: his estate was settled at Oland in Gjøvdal on 9 Feb 1761; his widow Ulvild Evensdatter (earlier married to Michel Christensen Oland/Austenaa, probate 14 Nov 1731) gave up the estate. Children together: Nære Olsen 12, Gunild 23 (unmarried), Sigri 20 (later wife of Ole Kiddelsen Austenaa), Astri 17 (later wife of Even Wrolsen Oland) and Ingri 9 (Østre Råbyggelag skiftekort, Skifteprotokoll 12b fol. 439). ¶1

The 1776 probate after his wife's nephew Even Tellefsen names 'Ingeborg Olsdatter, married to Torgius Ormsen Skjeggedal' among his and Ulvild's children (Skifteprotokoll 14a fol. 73). Ingeborg is probably the 'Ingri, 9' of 1761 (b. c.1752). ¶2

Records that disagree 1

Origin conflict: the archivist's note on the 1761 card gives his parents as Mære Børulsen Smedland (probate 1712) and Ingri Christensdatter, while the 1776 card calls him 'Ole Næresen (Tveit)', and the Tveit cards list an Ole, son of Nære Joensen Tveit (probate 1720) and Gunild Gunstensdatter (probate 1700), 'later Austenaa'/'Smeland'. His parents are left open. The archivist also queries a first wife Sigri Giermundsdatter Aamland. ¶3
