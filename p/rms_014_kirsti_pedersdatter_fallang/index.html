About the family

Wife of Peder Nielsen of Fallang, Gran parish; named as mother at the baptisms of Nielss (29 Sep 1737), Peder (31 May 1739) and Ole (5 Mar 1741) at Gran. Probable as the mother of Nils Pedersen of Fallang: see the note on Nils. Her birth, parents and death have not been searched. ¶1

Parents not decided: no baptism of a Kirsti daughter of a Peder is indexed in Gran 1707-1716. A Peder and an Ane of Fallang stood godparents together in 1709-1712 and could be her parents (her second son was named Peder in 1739), but nothing in the records ties them to her, so no link is made. Gran baptisms before 1707 are not indexed. ¶2

Life story

Marriage: betrothed to Peder Nielsen Fallanger at Gran on 4 Apr 1737 (sureties Jens Klæstad and Aasten Houg) and married at Gran on 9 Jul 1737. The entry gives neither her farm nor her father, so her parents remain unknown (Gran church book 1732-1744, p. 169b, read on the scan). ¶3

Her betrothal/marriage entry (Gran 1737, p. 169b, scan read) gives no residence or father for her: just 'Kirsti Pedersdatter'. Her parentage cannot come from this record. The Gran confirmation lists (DA 9341 from canvas 207) give name, farm and age only, no parents. ¶4

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
