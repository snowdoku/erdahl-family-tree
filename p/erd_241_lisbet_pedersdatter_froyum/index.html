About the family

Wife of Anders Davidsen of Frøyum, Stjørdal; died before 19 Jun 1741. Stjør- og Verdal skifteprotokoll 2C fol. 1070 (DA 24752 view 333; card 485 of the 77804 card index points to it): 'Aar 1741 d. 19 Juny' a skifte was held 'paa gaarden Frøjum' in Stjørdal after the late Lisbet Pedersdatter, between her surviving husband Anders Davidsen and their 7 children: 1 David Andersen, 19; 2 Ole Andersen, 16; 3 Sibor Andersdatter, 21; 4 Berit Andersdatter, 16; 5 Mali Andersdatter, 14; 6 Lisbet Andersdatter, 6; 7 Gollaug Andersdatter, 3, all at home on the farm. Guardians: the father, and Iver Thomesen Børset, 'the deceased woman's morbror' (mother's brother). Land: 1 øre 12 marklag in Frøyum (odel), valued 150 rd; gross estate 224-2-1, net to divide 201-1-12; each son 22-1-12, each daughter 11-0-18. Scan; the words 'og deres [tilsammen avlede]' before '7 Børn' are partly faint, but the entry is her own probate, so the children are hers. ¶1

Her mother's brother was Iver Thomesen Børset (guardian in 1741, 'morbror' of the deceased), so her mother was a Thomasdatter, probably from Børset. Her eldest child Sibor was 21 in 1741, so she was born about 1690-1700. Test record for her parents: a skifte on Børset (77804 B-cards) naming Iver Thomesen and a sister married to a Peder. ¶3

The case for this identification 1

Continuity to the tree's David Andersen (see his page): David Andersen, 19 and at home on Frøyum in Jun 1741 (born about 1721-22), is the 'ungkarl David Anderson Frøyum' betrothed 2 Oct 1743 (16370 c262) and the 'David Andersen Frøyam 22 1/2 aar' buried 21 May 1744 (16370 c54): same farm, 2-3 years, consistent age, and no other David on Frøyum. The 1745 Frøyum deed (pantebok A-2 fol. 460b, DA 19751 view 90) ties it directly: Anders Davidsen Frøyum, Berit Andersdatter and Sibor Andersdatter sell their Frøyum shares to Gunder Jonsen together with the share Gunder's wife Gjertrud had inherited after 'hendes afgangne Mand David Andersen' as his arvelod on the skifte after his mother. Also, David's sisters Sibor and Berit stood godmother ('Sibor Engen, Berit Frøyum') at his posthumous daughter Lisbet's baptism on 20 Sep 1744 (16370 c58), and he named that daughter after his mother. Graded on her link to her son David Andersen: Confirmed. ¶2
