How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

2 on the grade · 3 on the identification · 1 search with no result · 5 open questions

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Graded on that link: the groom row of Johann Adam's own marriage at Vaihingen an der Enz on 15 Nov 1703 reads 'Johann Adam Koodel', relatives 'Georg, Anna Margaretha' (father, then bride; record 4776330, image 1184562-00486). Continuity: the couple baptise at Vaihingen 1705-1714, including Georg Jacob (1711), the grandfather of the line. Ancestry 61023 index rows do not label roles (BRIEF audit 5 rule 3), so this rests at Probable at most until the image or a labelled FamilySearch twin is read; images not seen. ¶1b

Re-check, grade held at Probable. The Vaihingen images that would decide between the two Georg Knodels (the 1761 death of Johann Adam Knodel, Ancestry 1184565-00336, FS QPGK-2X5H; the 1703 marriage, Ancestry 1184562-00486) are not viewable on FamilySearch: the record pages offer only 'Check Image Availability', which points to partner sites. The labelled 1761 index gives age 82 (born 1679, the Vaihingen baptism, not the Ensingen one of 1683); under audit 8 rule 4 that age alone does not exclude the rival. ¶6a

The case for this identification 3

Which Georg (undecided): a Georg Knodel x Elisabethe had Johann Adam born at Vaihingen 4 Jul 1679 (record 4767377), and a Georg Knodel x Catharina had Johann Adam baptised at Ensingen 1 Apr 1683 (record 5148106). Both fit the marriage row, so his wife is not entered and his dates are unknown. A Johann Adam Knodel died at Vaihingen 3 Nov 1761 (4405534, image 1184565-00336); the age on that image would point to one baptism. ¶2a

Which Georg: the Ensingen candidate is eliminated. Johann Adam Knodel, baptised at Ensingen 1 Apr 1683 as a twin with Eva Catharina to 'Georg, Catharina' (5148106/5148107), was buried at Ensingen on 1 Jun 1683: row 'Johann Adam Knodels', burial, Ensingen, 1 Jun 1683, relative 'Georg' (record 5150844, image 1184592-00389). No later Johann Adam is indexed to Georg x Catharina at Ensingen (all Ensingen Knodel rows read: Balthas 1677, Hans Georg 1680, the twins 1683; that Georg, son of Balthas, married there 16 Jul 1676, 5150163, and 'Jerg Knodel' was buried there 24 Oct 1693, 5151018; his son Hanß Jorg married at Ensingen 1705 and stayed there). No early death of the Vaihingen boy of 4 Jul 1679 (4767377, 'Georg, Elisabethe') is indexed, and no other Johann Adam Knodel born 1675-85 appears anywhere in the index. So the 1703 groom is very probably the Vaihingen boy, son of Georg x Elisabethe; his wife is now entered (see her page). A Georg Knodel died at Vaihingen on 27 Feb 1690 (record 4397827, image 1184565-00126, no relatives indexed): probably this man, but not tied by the row, so no death date is entered. Grade unchanged (Probable): the 1703 marriage row is unlabelled (audit 5 rule 3) and the child's identity rests on elimination. ¶3a

Labelled evidence, grade held at Probable: QPGK-XCVT, Johann Adam Knodel, born Vaihingen 4 Jul 1679, Parents 'Georg Knodel, Elisabethe'; and QPGK-2X5H, Johann Adam Knodel died Vaihingen 3 Nov 1761 aged 82 (born about 1679). The test record named in (the 1761 death with age) is now read and fits. Still Probable: the 1761 death row names no relatives, so tying the decedent (and the 1703 groom) to the 1679 child rests on name, age and parish, i.e. elimination (the Ensingen boy of 1683 died in 1683; the Ensingen 'Hanß Adam Knodel', son of Georg, who married 22 Jan 1738, QPGF-WVPJ, is a younger man). The 1703 marriage was not found on FamilySearch. ¶5a

Searches that found nothing 1

Searched without result: a marriage of Georg Knodel 1670-80 (only the Ensingen marriage of 1676, the other Georg, is indexed); a widow 'Elisabetha Knodel' remarrying 1685-95; Georg/Hans Georg Knodel baptisms 1638-58 (none; Knodel baptisms of that period are indexed at Ensingen to Balthas x Barbara 1650-57, at Dürrmenz to Balthas 1654-57, and at Horrheim to Hannß x Judith 1648-59). The Vaihingen registers are indexed only from about 1679, so his marriage is probably in the unindexed years or in Elisabethe's home parish. ¶4a
