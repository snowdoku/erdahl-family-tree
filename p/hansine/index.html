Life story

Baptized 6 Apr 1856 at Borgund as Hansine Jakobine. Parents: Peder Johan Pedersen, farmer, and Gjertrud Birgitte Olsdatter of Røssevold. Both the parish register and the klokkerbok copy agree. ¶1

Servant girl at Roald, Vigra (Haram parish) in the 1865 and 1875 censuses (born 1856 in Borgund). ¶2

Confirmed 1 Oct 1871 at Roald church, Vigra, as Hansine Jacobine Pedersdatter Røssevold, born 1856 at Røssevold. Parents: Peder Johan Pedersen Røssevold, farmer, and Gjertrud Olsdatter. ¶3

Married Martinus Eliassen of Valderhaug 15 Jun 1879 at Borgund, age 23 1/4, born at Røsevold; her father is named as Peder Pedersen. The 1900 census also gives her birth year as 1856, which corrects the earlier estimate of about 1855. ¶4

Eleven recorded births, all at Valderhaug and all indexed in the Borgund and Giske church books with her as mother: a stillborn child in 1880; then children baptized 26 Jun 1881, 18 Nov 1883, 8 Feb 1885, 5 Jun 1887 (Peder Johan), 26 May 1890, 27 Nov 1892, 3 Jun 1895, 10 Apr 1898 and 30 Aug 1903, plus Martin, born 24 Aug 1899. Her age is given as 1855 in most of the church-book entries and as 1856 in the 1891 and 1900 censuses; her 1856 baptism settles it. ¶6

1910 census: 'Gaardmandskone' at Valderhaug, Giske, with four children at home while her husband was away in Ålesund. ¶7

Widowed in January 1912. In the 1920 census (1 Dec 1920) she was still at Valderhaug in Giske, entered as 'Hansine Valderhaug', born 1855, living as a dependent 'har Føderaad' - that is, on the retirement keep charged on the farm, the same arrangement her mother-in-law had had before her. She died 28 January 1927 and was buried in Giske on 2 February 1927. ¶8

Death register: the Nordre Sunnmøre district court's register of deaths (dødsfallsprotokoll no. 9, p. 7, 1927 no. 20, Valderøy) enters Hansine P. Valderhaug, a widow living on a farm pension ('kår'), died 28 January 1927. Her heirs are given as '7 m. barn hvorav 6 fraværende' (7 adult children, 6 of them absent), and her estate as nothing ('Do.', repeating 'Intet' from the lines above). The children are not named. ¶9
