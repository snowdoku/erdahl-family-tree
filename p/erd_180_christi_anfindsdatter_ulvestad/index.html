Life story

Widow at the probate of her husband Anders Truelsen of Ulvestad, 30 Apr 1753 (Ytre Sogn 7a fol. 162b, No. 12), with her son Anbiørn Andersen Esse as guardian; the nine children listed match her own 1761 probate, including 'Tredie Søn Anfind Andersen, myndig'. ¶3

Buried at Leikanger about 19-20 Mar 1761 as 'Christi Anfinds D. Ulvestad 67 an' (11566 c55 L). Probate 14 Apr 1761 at Ulvestad in Tjugum skipreide (Ytre Sogn 7b fol. 520a, No. 18): heirs her children - eldest son [Ambiørn] Andersen Ulvestad, of age and present; 2nd son Hans Andersen [Glavaa?], of age, absent; 3rd son Anfind Andersen, of age and present; 4th son Lars Andersen [Qvame?]; eldest daughter Synneve Andersdatter, dead, leaving children by Tollach [...]: Johannes, Truls 10, [Mari?] 19, Agathe 15, [Kari?] Tollachsdatter and Britte Tollachsdatter; 2nd daughter Britte Andersdatter married to Ole Olsen Indre Esse; 3rd daughter Randi Andersdatter married to Guttorm Samsonsen Langeteigen; 4th daughter Mari Andersdatter married to Siovard Knudsen [Skjøren?]; 5th daughter Ingeleif Andersdatter. Present: lensmann Ole Pedersen Wangsnes; appraisers Elling Erichsen Tjugum and Hans Andersen ibid. (several names cramped). ¶1

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Confirmed as mother of Anfind Andersen: her probate names her son Anfind Andersen, and his own probate (1768) names his children's Farbroder Ambiørn Andersen Ulvestad, her eldest son. Her husband's name, Anders, comes from the children's patronymic only. ¶2
