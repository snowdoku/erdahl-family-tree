Life story

Baptised 26 May 1795 in Treungen church: 'Tore Thronsen Felles Datter Ingebiør'. Godparents: Turi Targiesdatter Haugstøl, Tarjer Osmundsdatter(?) Felle, Torbør Halvorsdatter Haugstøl, Ole Biønnesen Felle and Evind Evindsen. The mother is not named. The entry follows Whitsun and 2nd Whit Day (24-25 May 1795) under the heading Børne Daab 1795 (Kviteseid ministerialbok 1787-1799, p.90, image 96). This fits her age of 6 in the 1801 census and 22 at her 1817 marriage. ¶5

Married (1) Orm Egildsen 3 Nov 1817, aged 22, of Felle. Married (2) Tellef Olsen of Heimdal (b. ~1798) on 7 Jul 1844 in Treungen, as a 48-year-old widow; one copy misindexes her as 'Ingeborg Thomasdatter' of Tvedt. ¶2

In the 1865 census she is Ingeborg Thorisdatter (b. ~1796, Nissedal) at Heimdal nordre, wife of Tellef Olsen. She is not at Heimdal in the 1875 census, and her death has not been found. ¶3
