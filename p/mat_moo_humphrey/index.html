About the family

Probable brother of Grace Amelia Moore (Mrs Thomas Hayter): one of the sons aged 16-21 in Richard Moore's 1861 household. ¶1

His son Humphrey Wake Moore was born 22 January 1874 and baptised 14 April 1875, parents Humphrey and Jane Eliza (PEI baptisms index, image FS_005240355_00171 - the same register film as the 1851 baptisms of Thomas and Martha Elizabeth Hayter's children). His wife Jane Eliza (the 'Janett' of 1881, Anglican, Scottish origin) was born 27 March 1842 (1901 census and Lauretta Brooks's diary), so she is not Jane Eliza Hayter (born 5 June 1845), the sister of Grace's husband, although the middle name Wake also occurs among the Hayters (Thomas Wake Hayter, born about 1909). ¶3

His son George H. Moore, b. 14 September 1867, was buried 14 May 1919 at Murray Harbour (PEI burials index). ¶6

Life story

1880 Meacham atlas, Lot 64: Humphrey Moore held a farm of about 87 acres at Toronto, Lot 64, adjoining the strips of Chas., Wm. and Benj. Moore. In 1890-91 he was Grand Guard of the Grand Lodge of PEI, Independent Order of Good Templars, of High Bank (Chappelle's PEI Almanac 1891, p. 87). ¶5

1881 census, Lot 64: Humphrey Moore, 42, Baptist, farmer; wife Janett, 38, Anglican; children George 13, Mary 11, Eliza 9, Humphrey 7, Sarah 4, Peter 2. ¶2

1901 census, Lot 64 (page 7, dwelling 66, LAC z000123387, read): Humphrey Moore, head, born 29 August 1839, aged 61, Baptist, farmer; wife Janet (born 27 March, about 1841); son George (born September 1867), farm son; daughter Adelaide (born January 1886). His neighbour in dwelling 68 was his brother Benjamin. ¶4

His widow: Lauretta Brooks's diary for March 1931 notes 'Mrs. Janet MOORE 89 years old March 27th', which puts Janet's birth on 27 March 1842. ¶7

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
