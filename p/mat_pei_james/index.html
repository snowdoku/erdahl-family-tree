Life story

Born 1878 (1901 census: Sep 17[?], 1878; 1911 index: Nov 1878), PEI. Farm son at home in 1881, 1891 and 1901. Alone in Kings County in 1911; head at Launching, Kings, in 1921. A James Hayter born about 1878 was buried 28 March 1951 (PEI burials index). ¶1

Death: 'Hayter, James, 74, 26 March 1951, Georgetown - Tom's son. High Bank' (death list in the diary of Lauretta Brooks of Murray Harbour). ¶2

Military: His gravestone reads 'SGT. James Hayter, MM - Bar, 78th Batt. C.E.F., Mar. 26, 1951'. He was a soldier of the 78th Battalion (Winnipeg Grenadiers) of the Canadian Expeditionary Force and was awarded the Military Medal and Bar. The stone gives his birth as 17 September 1877. His CEF personnel file at Library and Archives Canada would confirm the details. ¶3

Death: The Brooks diary records 'Hayter, James | 74 | 26-Mar-51 | Georgetown | Tom's son. High Bank', and the PEI burial index gives his burial on 28 March 1951. There are two Find a Grave memorials for him: memorial 245003277 at Georgetown Baptist Cemetery, which says he was the son of Thomas Jr. and Grace A. Moore Hayter, and memorial 287705631 at St Michael's Catholic Cemetery, Launching Place, which has photographs of the inscribed stone. The photographed stone is the stronger evidence for where he is buried. ¶4
