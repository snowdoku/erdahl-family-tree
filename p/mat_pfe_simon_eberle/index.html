Life story

Marriage: civil marriage act Standesamt Pfeilsdorf (Płużnica) 1903 no. 5 (1949 Polish certified copy, read at medium resolution): Szymon [Simon] Eberle, son of a settler, Evangelical, born 20 August 1882 at Fr[iedens]feld, Bessarabia, living at Płużnica, son of the settler Jan [Johann] Eberle and Dorota [Dorothea] née R[...], married Maria Hildebrandt in February 1903. Witnesses: the settler Jan Eberle, 60 (his father), and the settler Gottfried Frank, 21, both of Płużnica. ¶1

Pfeilsdorf: registered as a settler (osadnik) at the births of his children Daniel (1 Oct 1903) and Emilia (23 Oct 1907) at Pfeilsdorf (Standesamt Pfeilsdorf births, read by the w5 knodel-origin agent). ¶2

Emigration: Hamburg-America liner Prinz Adalbert, Hamburg to Quebec, ship's summary 29 Oct 1910, landed 30 Oct 1910 (manifest image read, LAC reel T-4773 frame 411, lines 25-29): Eberle, Simon, 28, married, farmer, with Maria 26, Emanuel 4, Emilie and Frieda, all born 'Germany', German, destination Winnipeg, Man. Daniel (b. 1903) is not with them. ¶3

Later life: farmer near Mendham and then Eatonia, Saskatchewan; 1931 census: immigrated 1910, naturalised 1913 (knodel-sask). Died 7 Nov 1931 at Eatonia, buried Eatonia Cemetery (Find a Grave 189010643). His daughters Emilia and Sarah married Gottlieb Knodel jr and Jacob Knodel, and his family were the Knodels' established neighbours when they arrived in 1928. ¶4
