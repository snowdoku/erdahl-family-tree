About the family

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 3

Graded on her link to Ole Jonsen Sandvik (see his page): the probate names her as the mother of both Oles; the continuity to the Ole Jonsen buried in 1767 is set out on Joen Povelsen's page (two brothers named Ole at Sandvik, ages 12 and 3 in 1728, matching burials at 50 in 1767 and 44 in 1768, and the 1762 'Farbroder Ole Jonsen Sandwig'). Her own parents are not named; she may have remarried after 1728. ¶2

Audit 4 (1 Nov 2026): lowered from Confirmed to Probable, graded on her link to her son Ole Jonsen Sandvik (see his page). The 1728 skifte names her as the mother of 'Ole Jonsen 12' and 'Ole Jonsen Junior 3'. As for her husband, joining those boys to the Ole Jonsen of the tree spans 34 years (1728 to the 1762 'Farbroder' skifte) with no bridging record, so it is Probable under the audit-3 gap detail. The evidence and the records that would confirm her are set out on Joen (Jon) Povelsen Sandvik. ¶3

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed, graded on her link to her son Ole Jonsen Sandvik (see his page). The 25 Feb 1728 skifte names her as the widow of Joen Povelsen and mother of their children 'Ole Jonsen 12' and 'Ole Jonsen Junior 3'. The bridge audit 4 asked for is now read: Selbu klokkerbok 16315 canvas 91, St Michael's Day 1746, 'Ole Sandwigens Kone Ingebor Michelsdatter' churched and 'Ole Junior Sandvig' among the communicants at the same farm. That joins the 1728/1736 sons (skifter of 1728 and 1736) to the Ole Jonsen whose wife Ingebor Michelsdatter's 1762 skifte names his brother Ole Jonsen Sandwig. Gaps: 8, 10 and 16 years, all at Sandvik; no rival. The full argument is on Joen (Jon) Povelsen Sandvik. A 'Marithe Sandvigen' was also a communicant that day, possibly she herself (not proved). ¶4

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Lead for her family, not entered as parentage: the probate card for her husband (77804 card c1713, 'Sandvik, Selbu, 25/2-1728, Jon Povelsen Sandvig', 24750 fol. 117b) names the guardians: for Ole 12 and Povel 8, Biørn Olsen Haarstad; for Gunder 6 and Ole jr. 3, 'Morbroren, Guttorm Gröten' (the children's mother's brother); for Ingeborg 1, 'Peder Poulsen Sandvigen, farbror'. So she had a brother Guttorm (Olsen) at Grøtem, Selbu - the Guttorm Olsen Grøtem whose skifte was held 30 Sep 1743 (24753 fol. 74, view 93 R: widow Siri Jacobsdatter; children of his first marriage Peder Guttormsen 25, Ole Guttormsen and Ingebor Guttormsdatter 21). Her father was therefore probably an Ole of Grøtem. The 'Morbroren' is an archivist's abstract (Probable-level only); read 24750 fol. 117b for the wording. The 77804 GRØTEM cards (c597-c601: Karen Jonsdatter 1700 x2, Jens Nielsen 1738, Peder Olsen 1740, Siri Eriksdatter 1741) contain no Ole Grøtem probate naming Guttorm and Marit. ¶5a

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Test record: a probate after an Ole of Grøtem before 1727 (gap in the protocols 1701-1727), or the Grøtem chapter of Selbu i fortid og nåtid. ¶5b
