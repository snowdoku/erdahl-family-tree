About the family

Probable father of Sivert (Siur) Jonssen (see his page): Siur son of a Jon, on the same farm, of the right age (23 in 1762). Birth year estimated. ¶2

Life story

Farmer of Eidem (Eidum, gl. matr. 720 - the same holding, later løpenr 62, that Sivert Jonssen held from 1787 and his son John bought in 1820) in the 1762 extra tax list, with wife Ingebor Olsdatter and children over 12 Ole, Siur, Brynild and Jon. The 1762 extra tax list names every person over 12 in each household, with spouses first. ¶1

Cross-line lead tested and refuted (overnight cross-line test): he is not the son of Siur Olsen Alset (see his page) and Gjertrud Jonsdatter (see her page). Graded on his link upward, so his own grade (Probable, as father of Sivert Jonssen) is unchanged and no parents are set. (1) The 1732 probate protocol after Gjertrud Jonsdatter (Stjør- og Verdal skifteprotokoll 2B, Digitalarkivet 24751, fol. 401b, view 29; 'Anno 1732 d. 11 Febr.', Alset) lists the eight children 'alle hiemme paa gaarden Alset': 1. Ole Siversen 30, 2. Anders Siversen 25, 3. Baro Siversen 15, 4. Ole Siversen 13, 5. Joen Siversen 13, 6. Mali 26, 7. Inger 20, 8. Berit 18. There is one Jon among them, aged 13 (born about 1719), not the Jon baptised in Oct 1707, who would have been 24. (2) That Jon lived at Garberg. At the probate after his brother Ole Syvertsen Alset (skifteprotokoll 6-1, 24756, fol. 92a, view 101; 4 Feb 1768) the guardian of Ole's daughter Giertrud Olsdatter was 'hendes Farbroder Joen Syvertsen Garberg' (also on line 7). (3) The 1762 extra tax list has that Jon at Garberg 736 (49639 canvas 132): 'Jon Siursen, Anne Ingebrigtsdatter', with 'Gjertrud Olsdatter' in the house, who is the niece of the 1768 probate. In the same 1762 list this man, Jon Siursen of Eidem 720, appears with a different wife, Ingebor Olsdatter (canvas 134). The 1732 probate has only one son Jon, so the Eidem Jon cannot be Siur Alset's son. The Sesseng rival is also a different man: Jon Sivertsen of Sesseng lost his wife Mali Jørgensdatter (probate 9 Sep 1760, 24755 fol. 103b, view 120; daughters Gunild and Karen; Esten Haldosen Eidum was one of the guardians), and then lost a second wife, Jertrue Arnsdatter (24755 fol. 244, 1762, per the typed register). So in 1762 Selbu had at least three men called Jon Siursen, at Garberg, Eidem and Sesseng, each with his own wife. His father is still unknown. ¶4a

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 correction · 1 alternative set aside · 2 open questions

Corrections 1

Correction: the 'marriage of 30 Oct 1740 to Ingebore Olsdatter' (16315 canvas 10, col. 3, 20th Sunday after Trinity) is not his. Read at full resolution, the groom is 'Jon Pa[u]lsen' (the capital is the looped P/B of this hand, as in 'Beder' and 'Børnedaab', not its S as in 'Seri'), married to Ingebore Olsdatter. His marriage is therefore still not found. ¶5

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Cross-line identity lead (overnight cross-line pass), Speculative, no link made. Line 7 evidence holds a baptism that fits him: 'Døbt Siur Alsets barn Jon', 16th Sunday after Trinity (about 9 Oct) 1707, with the churching of 'Siur Alsets... Gertrud efter det 3de barn Jon' (Selbu ministerialbok 16310). The father is Siur (Sivert) Olsen Alset (see his page, a line 7 ancestor, Confirmed), married 1702 to Gjertrud Jonsdatter, and a widower with eight children at her probate on 11 Feb 1732 (card 77804 c21; the children's names are not transcribed in the tree). For the identification: the patronymic Siursen; the date fits a man whose son Siur was 23 in 1762; his eldest son Ole would carry the name of Siur's father Ole Jonsen Alset, and his son Siur his own father's name (naming custom, an inference). Against it: other Siurs had sons in Selbu (a Jon Siursen of Sesseng stood forlover in 1777; a Gunder Siursen Haarstad and a Povel Siursen Haarstad were active in the 1770s), so a rival Jon Siursen exists, and nothing ties the Alset child to Eidem. Siur Mikkelsen Velve is ruled out as father: his 1742 probate (card 77804 c2131) names no son Jon. ¶3a

Open questions and the next record to read 2

What would settle it: the 1732 probate card 77804 c21 read for the eight children (a Jon there, of age about 24, and his later residence), a probate after Jon Siursen Eidem naming his siblings (Strinda/Stjør- og Verdal skifteprotokoller 1762-1790), or a full-resolution reading of the groom's patronymic at the 30 Oct 1740 marriage to Ingebore Olsdatter (16315 canvas 10, col. 3). ¶3b

Next records: a probate after Jon Siursen of Eidem or after Ingebor Olsdatter (Strinda og Selbu skifte 1762-1787; nothing under Eidem in the typed Stjør- og Verdal registers), and the baptisms of his children Ole, Siur (about 1739), Brynild and Jon (16310 to 1737, 16315 1740-46, godparents). ¶4b
