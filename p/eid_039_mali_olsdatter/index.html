Life story

Named as mother, with Ingebrigt Bjornsen of Store Evjen, at the baptisms of Ole (1758), Ingebrigt (1760), Gunnar (1762) and Jon (13 January 1765), Selbu ministerialbok 1757-1801, canvases 6, 10, 13 and 17 (the 1765 crop). ¶1

Death (Probable identification): the bygdebok (Selbu i fortid og naatid vol. 5, Stor-Evjen chapter, printed p. 182; word boxes only) gives Ingebrikt Bjørnsen's wife Mali the years '1727,... 1794' - born 1727, died 1794. That year agrees with the register's burial lead already noted here: 'Maglie Olsdatter Stor Efjen', 70, died 16 March and buried on Oculi Sunday 1794 (Selbu ministerialbok 1757-1801, canvas 162). Same name, patronymic, farm and year; the burial age (70, born about 1724) and the book's 1727 differ by three years, which is common for burial ages. Rated Probable, not Confirmed: the burial names no husband and was read only in overview. ¶4a

1762 extra tax list: listed at holding 681 as the wife of Ingebrigt, son of Biørn Ingebrigtsen, with their daughter Kari. ¶5

Burial, Oculi Sunday (23 Mar) 1794: 'L: J: p: Maglie Olsdatter Stor Effjen, gl: 70 Aar, død d: 16de Martii' - aged 70, so born about 1724 (16311 canvas 162). Confirms the death date already recorded. ¶6

Probate: registered 14 April 1794 at Stor-Evjen, Selbu, after 'Mali Olsdatter', wife of Ingebrigt Biørnsen, who survived her. Heirs: the late son Ole Ingebrigtsen (his son Ole Olsen), son Jon Ingebrigtsen, daughters Karen (married Erich Olsen), Ranni (married the cottager Jens Thomassen Eidum), Brynnild (26) and Ingeborg. The heading lines read do not name her parents. The next lines name the widower Ingebrigt Biørnsen of Stor-Evjen, the father, as guardian of the minor heirs (the daughters Brynnild and Ingeborg, and the grandson Ole Olsen). No mother's brother or other kinsman of hers appears, so her parents are still unknown (24763 fol. 537b, view 553 left; the register's folio 537 points to the verso). ¶7

Alternatives considered and set aside 2

Lead, not linked: 'Maglie Olsdatter Stor Efjen', 70, died 16 March and was buried on Oculi Sunday 1794 (Selbu ministerialbok 1757-1801, canvas 162). Born about 1724, she would fit as this Mali; the entry gives no husband. Zooming that entry, and checking it against any other Mali Olsdatter at Store Evjen, would settle it. ¶3

Namesake (cross-line sweep pass 2): not the same woman as Mali Olsdatter of Baar, Skatval (see her page, line 8), although both were born about 1724 and fleet.py dupes pairs them. That Mali was the widow of Peder Erichsen Baar at his probate of 1 Jul 1790 and was buried from Baar on 8 Feb 1791 aged 67; this one was the wife of Ingebrigt Bjørnsen of Store Evjen, Selbu, buried there in March 1794 aged 70, with her own probate of 14 Apr 1794 naming her children. Different husbands, farms, parishes and burials, so no pedigree collapse between lines 7 and 8 here. ¶8

What would confirm it: a zoomed reading of canvas 162, and a check that no other Mali Olsdatter lived at Store Evjen in 1794. ¶4b
