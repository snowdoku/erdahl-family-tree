About the family

Children, reconciled across the 1856 emigration list and the 1870, 1875 and 1905 censuses: (1) Ingeborg, born 21 March 1850, emigrated 1856, is not in the household in 1870 or 1875 and has not been traced in America; she would have been 20 in 1870, so she may have died or married by then. (2) Anders, born September 1852, is Andrew (1870 about 1853; 1875 aged 22). (3) Marie, born 25 April 1855, is Mary (see her page). (4) A daughter born in Wisconsin about 1857-58, 'Setone' aged 17 in 1875; the family list calls her Susan. She is probably the unnamed 1870 record 41887523, which stands between Mary and Hannah. (5) Johanna, called Hannah: 'Hannah', born about 1859 in Wisconsin (1870 index), is 'Johana', 14, born Minnesota (1875), and very probably 'Johane M. Johnson', 44 years 11 months, born Minnesota (1905 census), and Johanna Johnson, born 14 July 1860, died 2 January 1957 in Faribault County (death index). There is one daughter here, not two, so the family list's separate 'Annie (~1860)' and 'Johanna Hannah Johnson (~1861)' should be read as Anna and Johanna. (6) Anna, 12 in 1875, born Minnesota about 1862-63; probably the unnamed 1870 record 41887525. (7) Ole, born about 1865-66 in Minnesota (1870 about 1865; 1875 aged 9). The census places alone point to Wisconsin from 1856 to about 1860 and Minnesota from about 1861. Gjertrud's 44 years in Minnesota in June 1905 agrees. ¶10

Children, open question: Johanna's birthplace is Wisconsin in the 1870 index, Minnesota in the 1875 and 1905 censuses, and 'out of state' on the 1957 death index. With a birth in July 1860 and arrival in Minnesota about 1861, Wisconsin is the more likely. Her 1957 death certificate, which would name her parents, would settle both the birthplace and her identity. ¶11

Brother: Johannes Andersen, born 2 March 1832 to cottager Anders Olsen and Ingebor Gjestsdatter of Esse, emigrated with Ole's family on 12 April 1856 (see Johannes Andersen Esse). ¶15

Life story

Find a Grave gives birth 24 Jun 1820. Emigrated about 1856 via Wisconsin. ¶1

Baptism: the Leikanger register records under the 8th Sunday after Trinity (23 July 1820), at the summer service at Tjugum, '2. uægte Drengebarn Ole, født den 12te Julii. Forældrene Ingeborg Gjestsdr. Esse og Anders Olsen Esse' (illegitimate boy Ole, born 12 July; parents Ingeborg Gjestsdatter of Esse and Anders Olsen of Esse). Godparents: Hans Esse, Marcus Esse (his father's brother), Christie Esse, Unni Sjøthun and Kari Esse. His parents married on 16 January 1826. ¶12

Marriage 1846: the index of the Leikanger register (1846, p. 229, no. 11, 10 June 1846) names the groom Ole Andersen, 25, born at 'Esse un[der] Grøneng', his father Anders Olsen Esse; bride Gjertrud Johnsdatter, 23, of Kvalem, father John Bjørnsen Kvalem. ¶14

Emigration: the Balestrand parish register's list of people moving out ('Udflyttede') for 1856 records the whole family under 12 April 1856, nos. 55-59: 'Ole Andersen Grøneng, Huusm.' (husmand, a cottager), aged 36; 'Gjertrud Johnsd., hans Hustru' (his wife), 33; and 'deres Børn' (their children) Ingeborg (born 21 March, baptised 7 April 1850), Anders (born 11 September?, baptised 26 September 1852) and Marie (born 25 April, baptised 13 May 1855). Their destination is 'Nord-America'. This confirms the family-tradition date of about 1856 and fixes the departure to April 1856, from Grøneng in Balestrand parish. ¶5

Emigration, related entry: the next line in the same list, also dated 12 April 1856 and bound for North America, is the bachelor Johannes Andersen Esse, born 10 March 1832. The farm name Esse and the patronymic suggest he was Ole's younger brother, but the list does not say so; his baptism would settle it. ¶6

1875 Minnesota census (image read): family 54 in Emerald, enumerated as of 1 May 1875, is Ole Anderson, 55, Gjertru (indexed 'Gjeter'), 52, and Andrew, 22, all born in Norway; then Ole J. Quame, 21, and Mary, 20, born Norway; then 'Setone' Anderson, 17, born Wisconsin; Johana, 14, Anna, 12, and Ole, 9, all born Minnesota. Both parents of everyone are entered as born in Norway. The index's age 'F' for Setone is an indexing error; the sheet reads 17. ¶9

1880 U.S. census, Emerald Twp.: 'Ole Anderson', 60, wife 'Gertrude', 57. ¶2

1895 Faribault Co. register of deaths: 'Ole Anderson Flatten', died 30 May 1895 in Emerald, aged 74y 11m 6d (so born 24 Jun 1820), married, born Norway; parents 'Andrew & Ingeborg Olson'. ¶4

1870 U.S. census (index only): the Emerald, Faribault County household on image 4265057_00375 is indexed as Ole Anderson, born about 1829 [sic] in Norway, Getrude, about 1822, Norway, Andrew, about 1853, Norway, Mary, about 1856, Norway, Hannah, about 1859, Wisconsin, and Ole, about 1865, Minnesota (records 41887519-41887526). Records 41887523 and 41887525 fall inside the household but were not returned by any name search; the image has not been read. ¶8

Corrections 1

Correction: the baptism names Ole's mother as Ingeborg (Ingebor) Gjestsdatter of Esse, not Ingeborg Monsdatter. The 1895 death register's 'Ingeborg' fits her. His birth date is 12 July 1820 per the baptism register; the 24 June 1820 on Find a Grave was computed back from the death-register age (74y 11m 6d) and is wrong. ¶13

Searches that found nothing 1

Emigration, negative: no out-migration entry for this family was found in the Leikanger parish register for 1854 to April 1858. The departure was registered in Balestrand, not Leikanger. ¶7
