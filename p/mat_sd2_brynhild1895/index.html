About the family

She is the ninth child of the marriage and takes the count of births from eight to nine. Rated Probable, from a published gaardshistorie; her baptism and burial entries in the Selbu registers for 1895 and 1896 would confirm her. ¶2

Life story

Oline Slind's youngest sister, who lived about a year and whom nobody in the family in America can have remembered. Selbu i fortid og naatid vol. 7, p. 100, last in the children's list of Gunnar Olsen d.e. and Kari: 'Brynhild, f. 1895, d. 1896.' Her mother was forty-eight. ¶1

Brynhild was her paternal grandmother's generation's name on the Slind farm - her father's sister Brynild, born about 1856, bore it before her. ¶3

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
