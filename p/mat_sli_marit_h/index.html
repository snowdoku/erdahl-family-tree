About the family

Oline Slind's maternal grandmother, wife of the freehold farmer Ole Johnsen of Stokke in Selbu, aged 57 in the 1865 census. ¶1

Her children, from the 1865 census: John born about 1841, Haldo about 1844, Ole about 1849, Johanna about 1852, Christen about 1855, and Kari, born July 1847, who married Gunder Olsen of Slind in 1871 and became the mother of Oline Slind. ¶3

Her father was a farmer at Svinaas and her grandfather is now reached: the 1801 census of Selbu, rural residence 0015 Svinaas, has her father in household 4 as 'Haldo Torgersen, 30, Mand, Begge i 1te aegteskab, I faellesskab med faderen og national soldat ved indfanteriet' - in partnership WITH HIS FATHER, and a soldier of the national infantry - and her grandfather in household 3 as 'Torger Erikssen, 70, Husbonde, Bonde og gaard beboer'. ¶7

Her mother was Ingebor Jonsdatter, aged 28 in the 1801 census, so the couple had married by about 1794. Marit, born about 1810, was a later child; the three already born in 1801 were Ingebor 6, Torger 5 and Magli 2. ¶8

Life story

Baptism: 'Marite', born 22 March 1810, baptised on Midfaste Sunday (4th Sunday in Lent, 1 April 1810 by computation from Easter 22 April 1810), father Haldor Torgersen of Svinaas, mother Ingebor Jonsd. (Selbu ministerialbok 1801-1815, births and baptisms 1810, p. 135; Digitalarkivet index record pd00000009557803; the scan has not been read). Both parents and the farm agree with her 1831 marriage entry and the 1801 Svinaas household, and the year agrees with her burial age of 61 in 1871. Birth changed from ABT 1810 to 22 March 1810. Other 1810 Selbu baptisms of a Marit/Marite (to Ole Jensen of Aas, Ole Thomesen of Eidem, Niels Barosen of Lien) have different parents. ¶11

Her patronymic is written three ways in three records of the same family: 'Haldosdatter' in the 1865 census, 'Haavelsdatter' in her daughter Kari's baptismal entry of 25 July 1847 in the main register, and 'Halderd' in the parish clerk's duplicate of that same entry. Haldo and Haavel are different Selbu names, so this is a genuine ambiguity rather than a simple slip; two of the three readings say Haldo, and her eldest son at home in 1865 is named HALDO, which by the Selbu custom of naming a child for the mother's father settles it in favour of Haldosdatter. The variant is recorded here rather than hidden. ¶2

Printed source: the Stokke chapter of Selbu i fortid og naatid, bind 6 (1992) is on printed pages 8-16, and page 12 is the family page - carrying the year 1808 (her own and her husband's stated birth year), her given name Marit, her daughters' names Kari and Johanna, and the birth years 1841, 1847, 1849, 1851, 1853 and 1855. Her patronymic Haldosdatter is printed Haldor- in the 1992 orthography; 'Haldor' occurs on page 14. ¶4

A trap on this farm, recorded so that nobody falls into it. The 1801 census of Svinaas also contains, in household 5, a 'Haldo Pederssen, 51, Husm. med lidet jord og sygelig' - cottager with a little land, and sickly - a widower with four children of his own all surnamed Haldorsdatter or Haldorsen; and the Sovlvskatten rolls of 1816 and 1819 find that same Haldo Pedersen as a husmann AT STOKKE. A Marit Haldorsdatter of Stokke could very easily be assigned to him. She was not his: the 1831 marriage entry names her father as Haldo TORGERSEN of Svinaas. ¶9

Death and burial: 'Marit Haldosdr', Gaardmandskone (farmer's wife), residence Stokke, aged 61, died 20 November and buried 26 November 1871, Selbu burial register 1860-1877, p. 40; the same burial is in the 1869-1888 list, p. 315, as 'Marit Haldosdatter', 62. Identification strong: name, the farm Stokke, the status of a farmer's wife (her husband Ole Johnsen of Stokke lived until 1891), and the calculated birth year 1810 (1809 in the duplicate) all agree with her marriage entry of 1831. Index read; scan not read. ¶10

Corrections 2

Correction: she was born at SVINAAS, not at Stokke, and her patronymic is HALDORSDATTER. Her marriage entry of 21 June 1831 in the Selbu register, page 317 serial 19, names her 'Marite Haldorsdtr Svinaas', born 1810 in Saelboe, and her father 'HALDO TORGERS SVINAAS'. Stokke is her husband's farm, which she moved to on marriage and which the 1865 census then attaches to her name; Svinaas is where she came from. The wave-3 record gave her as 'Marit Haldosdatter Stokke' with her birthplace unknown. ¶5

Correction: the three-way ambiguity in her patronymic is resolved, and 'Haavelsdatter' is wrong. Wave 3 recorded that her daughter Kari's baptism of 25 July 1847 calls the mother 'Marit Haavelsdatter', the clerk's duplicate 'Marte Halderd' and the 1865 census 'Marit Haldosdatter', and judged between them on the naming custom. The marriage register settles it from the primary side: HALDORSDATTER, father Haldo Torgersen of Svinaas. Haavel is a misreading of Haldor. ¶6
