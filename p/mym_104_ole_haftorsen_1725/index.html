Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Speculative father of Guri Olsdatter (see her page). SPECULATIVE (orchestrator, line 8): Guri Olsdatter (b. about 1756) married Niels Olsen Langlie in 1777; her baptism falls in the 1747-56 register gap. The only Guri Olsdatter confirmed at the right age with a farm is 'Guri Olsd. Aunglum' (Uglem) in 1772 (another Guri Olsdatter, of Solum, was confirmed in 1775). In the 1762 extra tax list the only Uglem household headed by an Ole is matr. 702: Ole Haftorssen and Gunild Baardsdatter, with no children over 12 (Guri would have been about 6, so not listed). The chain rests on (a) the 1772 Guri being ours and (b) her being a daughter of the household rather than a servant there; neither is proved, and none of Guri's children carries the names Haftor, Gunild or Baard. Treat as a lead only. ¶2

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Counter-lead: the bygdebok's Langli chapter appears to give Guri Olsdatter's origin as Pigarden, not Uglem (word boxes only). Keep this Speculative link under review. ¶3

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Lead: the typed registers of the Stjør- og Verdal skifteprotokoller list Uglem in Selbu as 'Uglan': 'Marithe Olsdatter, Haftor Olsens hustru, Uglan, Selbu' (protocol 24753, fol. 537, about 1743-48) and 'Haftor Olsen, Uglan', 1758 (protocol 24755, fol. 74). These are very probably the probates of Ole Haftorsen's parents, and would test whether he is Haftor's son. They cannot settle Guri Olsdatter's parentage, because they come before her time. The registers have no probate for Ole Haftorsen or Gunild Baardsdatter, and no Pigarden entry. The untranscribed registers of 24754 and 24756 were not checked. Link stays Speculative. ¶4
