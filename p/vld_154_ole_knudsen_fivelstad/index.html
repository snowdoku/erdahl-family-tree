About the family

Son of Knut Olsen of Fivelstad: 'Olle Kds.', 18, in the 1701 manntall, and 'Ole Knudsen' in his father's probate of 11 Sep 1703 (see Knut (Knud) Olsen Fivelstad). ¶1

Father of Knud Olsen of Ringdal (see his page), Probable; graded on his link to Knud. No record names Knud's father. The case: (1) Knud Olsen was 'of Fivelstad' when he married in Dec 1742 and when his first son was baptised on 15 Apr 1743 (pd00000009560916); (2) his burial at Sunnylven on 5 Jun 1786 gives his age as 66, so he was born about 1720, when Ole Knudsen (b. about 1683/85) was the Ole of age on Fivelstad; the farm's other household of that generation was Arne Olsen's (Knut's stepson; Arne's sons are Arnesen), and Joen's (sons would be Jonsen); (3) Knud's first son was named Ole (1743), and a daughter Giertrud (1751), the name of Knut Olsen's widow in 1703; (4) 'Arne of Fivelstad' was godfather to Giertrud in 1751, and Arne Arnesen of Fivelstad was guardian of Knud's son Lars in 1759, which fits a family tie to Fivelstad. Not stronger than Probable: no Sunnylven baptism register survives before 1736, no probate after Ole is indexed, and an unrecorded Ole (a husmann or servant) on the farm cannot be excluded. ¶3

Life story

Burial (index record read): Ole Knudsen of Fivelstad, aged 54, buried at Sunnylven on 2 May 1739 (Norddal parish, Sunnylven 1736-1760, 1524P, p. 131 no. 33; pg00000001730893). No probate after him is indexed in the Sunnmøre probate index (Fivelstad rows: 1703, 1711, 1712, 1765, 1771, 1779; the 1711 and 1712 rows, 24547/126 and 218, read, belong to a Fivelstad in Dale skipreide, not this family). ¶2

The deed itself (scan read; Sunnmøre pantebok 3A 1744-1760, Digitalarkivet 22713, view 18, right page, fol. 16a): 'Jeg underskrevne Arne Olsen Fibelstad' sells to 'min kiære Søn Arne Arnesen Fibelstad' the land in Fivelstad, Sunnylven skipreide, that he held by deeds ('Skiøder') of 5 Nov 1735 and 2 Nov 17[39?] (last digits hard to read), on condition that the son keeps his old mother; dated Langeloe 6 Nov 1744, witnesses Rasmus Ned. Liaaen and Joen Olsen Langeland. So Arne Olsen held his share by purchase deeds, not by a stated inheritance, and the deed does not name his father. If the second deed is of Nov 1739, it falls just after Ole Knudsen's burial (1739) and could be a purchase from his co-heirs. ¶5a

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

3 open questions

Open questions and the next record to read 3

Lead (panteregister scan read): the Sunnmøre panteregister 1744-1802 (Digitalarkivet 22525, view 58, right page, p. 83) lists under 'Fibelstad i Sundelven' a 'Skiøde fra Arne Olsen... udi Fibelstad til Sønnen Arne Arnesen', dated 6 Nov 1744, pantebok fol. 16 (the reading of the patronymic Olsen is good but the middle words are faint). Arne Arnesen of Fivelstad was guardian of Knud Olsen's son Lars in 1759, and an 'Arne of Fivelstad' stood godfather to Knud's daughter Giertrud in 1751. If Arne Olsen was a son of Ole Knudsen of Fivelstad, he was a brother of Knud Olsen Ringdal, and the 1759 guardian was Lars's first cousin. Not added as a relationship. ¶4a

Next: the deed itself, Sunnmøre pantebok for 1744, fol. 16, which may say how Arne Olsen acquired his share of Fivelstad (inheritance from his father would name him). ¶4b

Next: those two deeds in Sunnmøre pantebok 1724-1743 (Digitalarkivet 22712), and the panteregister 22525 for its pre-1744 back-entries. ¶5b
