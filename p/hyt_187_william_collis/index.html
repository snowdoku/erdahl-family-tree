About the family

Parents, Speculative: Burke's Landed Gentry of Ireland (1912), 'Collis of Tieraclea', says that William Collis, an officer in Hierome Sankey's regiment of horse in Cromwell's army, left a son John Collis, who married Mary, daughter of Philip Corridon of Kilmacaha, and that John was the father of 'William Collis, of Lisedoge, co. Kerry'. Because the same Burke entry mangles William's own marriages, this is graded Speculative. ¶5a

Life story

Marriages: he married Mary Crosse by licence of the diocese of Cork and Ross in 1687. He had an elder son, the Rev. William Collis junior of Dingle (later Vicar-General of Ardfert, died 1754), by an earlier wife. Burke (1912) names that first wife as Martha Mullins (1685), but the Blennerhassett Pedigree shows that Martha Mullins married his grandson William, son of John Collis and Elizabeth Cooke. So Burke's first-marriage entry is a confusion, and the first wife's name is unknown. Added. ¶4

Memorial 420 read in full on the image (FamilySearch signed in, 2 Nov 2026; Registry of Deeds book 2 p. 215, registered 2 May 1709). Lease and release of 13 and 14 February 1708[/9]. The release is made between 'Samuell Bennett Sen[io]r of Cos[s] in the County of Limerick Gent and Susanna his Wife of the First part', 'the said William Collis' [of Liseaige, Co. Kerry, gent.] 'and Francis Bennet' [of Ballykendon, Co. Cork, gent.] 'of the second part', and 'Samuel Bennett Jun[io]r Son and Heir Apparent of the said Samuell Bennett Sen[io]r and Anne Collis Eldest Daughter of the said William Collis of the Third part'. It recites that 'a Marriage is soon to be had and solemnized' between Samuel junior and Anne, and that William Collis paid a portion of £300. The lands are Castlereagh (121 acres), 50 acres in Curroughrosty (parish of Galbally), 96 acres in Cloghasty and 7 acres in Mitchelstown, 'in the Parish of Ballingarry Barrony of Cosler [as transcribed; i.e. Connello] and County of Limerick'. Castlereagh and Curroughrosty, 'whereof the said Samuell Bennett Sen[io]r was hitherto seized in right of his said Wife', go to Samuel junior for life, then to his sons in order of seniority, 'and for want of such Issue then to the Heires Males of the Body of the said Susanna', and then to Susanna's right heirs. Cloghasty and Mitchelstown go to Samuel senior and Susanna for life and then to Samuel junior and his sons. In default of his sons they go to 'William Bennett Second Son of the said Samuell Bennett Sen[io]r', then to James Bennett, third son. After the deaths of Samuel senior and Susanna, those lands are charged with £300 payable to William, James and Charles Bennett and to Prudence Bennett, daughter of Samuel senior. The image reads 'Anne Collis Eldest Daughter of the said William Collis'. The abstract had only 'daughter', and 'eldest' agrees with the Blennerhassett Pedigree's 'first daughter'. His grade (Confirmed, on his link to Anne Collis) now rests on the image. The deed does not name Anne's mother. ¶6

William Collis, gent., of Liseaige (Lisodige, Lissodigue), near Tralee, Co. Kerry; living in 1730. He was the second party to the marriage settlement, 13 Feb 1708/9, of his daughter Anne with Samuel Bennett junior of Co. Limerick (Registry of Deeds memorial 420). In 1711 he took leases for the lives of his sons John, Robert and Henry (memorials 5062-5063, 6290). In 1714 his daughter Martha married (memorial 16968). In 1730 he and his wife Mary settled lands on the marriage of his son Robert (memorial 43069). The Blennerhassett Pedigree (about 1733-36) gives his children by Mary Cross as John, Thomas, Edward, Robert, Samuel and Henry, and Anne, Martha and Mary. ¶1

Will: Phillimore's index of Ardfert and Aghadoe wills lists 'William, Lisodige, co. Kerry, 1733'. The will itself was destroyed in 1922; no copy has been found. Added. ¶3

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Confirmed, graded on his link to his daughter Anne Collis: the 1709 settlement, read in the Registry of Deeds Index Project's labelled index of the memorial, names the bride as 'Anne Collis of Liseaige, daughter of' William Collis of Liseaige. Continuity: the same Anne is the wife of Samuel Bennett the younger of Ballincallow in 1713 (memorial 10967). The pedigree independently calls her the first daughter of William Collis and the wife of Samuel Bennett of Ballincollon. Added. ¶2

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Test record: Registry of Deeds memorials or Kerry chancery bills before 1740 naming William of Lisodigue as son of John; the 1659 'census' and the Down Survey/Books of Survey and Distribution for a Collis in Tralee barony. Added. ¶5b
