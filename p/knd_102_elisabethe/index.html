About the family

Mother of Johann Adam Knodel (see his page), wife of Georg Knodel (see his page). Graded on her link to Johann Adam: birth row 'Johann Adam Knodel', Vaihingen an der Enz, 4 Jul 1679, relatives 'Georg, Elisabethe' (record 4767377, image 1184562-00025). That this boy is the Johann Adam who married at Vaihingen in 1703 as son of Georg (4776330) rests on elimination: the only rival, Johann Adam son of Georg x Catharina baptised at Ensingen 1 Apr 1683, was buried there 1 Jun 1683 (5150844). Unlabelled index row and an elimination argument, so Probable at most (audit 5 rule 3). Surname not indexed; her marriage to Georg (before 1679) is not indexed at Vaihingen. ¶1a

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
