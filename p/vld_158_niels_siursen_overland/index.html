About the family

His father found: the 1701 manntall for Nordmøre (Census (manntall) 1701, no. 11: Nordmøre fogderi and Romsdal fogderi, p. 62) lists at Offuerland, Gjemnes, 'Siffuer Toresen' (b. 1647), farmer, with 'Niels Sifuers.' (b. 1683) and 'Tøris Sifuers.' (b. 1689) as his sons. This fits his patronymic (Siursen = son of Siur/Siffuer), his farm, his age (children aged 36-31 in 1742) and his naming of his eldest son Siur. The link is graded Probable on the father's record (41 years between 1701 and 1742). Birth estimated from the 1701 age. ¶4

Life story

Probate of his wife: Nordmøre skifteprotokoll 24642 (no. 8b, 1741-46), fol. 547b (view 120, left page), No. 64: 'Anno 1742 den 14de Septemb.' a registration was held on the farm 'Overland', belonging to 'Niels Siursen', after his wife 'Helge [Ols]datter', who had died some time before, between her surviving husband on the one side and on the other 'deris sammen avlede Børn', namely the son 'Siur Nielsen 36 aar gammel', 'Berit Nielsdatter 34 aar gl. gift med Peder Christophersen Strand' and 'Marith Nielsdatter 31 aar gammel' [unmarried]; appraisers included Jon Pedersen Overland. The index card (77795 view 4629) gives the same: 'Øverland, Gjemnes. 1742 14/9 Helge Olsdatter. Enkemann: Niels Siursen. Barn: 1) sønnen Siur Nielsen 36 år gl., 2) datteren Berite Nielsdr. 34 år, g.m. Peder Christophersen Strand, 3) Marite Nielsdr. 31 år gl. ugift. Fallitt.' ¶2

Farmer at Overland (Øverland), Gjemnes tinglag, Kvernes parish, Nordmøre; widower in Sep 1742, when his wife Helge Olsdatter's estate was registered (the estate was insolvent, 'Fallitt'). ¶1

Niels Øverland was still living in Jan 1747, when he stood godfather to Ane, daughter of Ole Nielsen Søvigen (16075 view 44). ¶6

Burial: Kvernes 16075 view 51, p. 49 left, Festo Passionis Christi (Good Friday, 12 Apr 1748; Easter was 14 Apr): 'graftested Enkemand Niels Siverts[en] Øverland 68 aar', so born about 1680 (the 1701 manntall gives about 1683). He is called a widower, as he was after Helge Olsdatter's death in 1742, and he was still alive in Jan 1747. The burial gives his patronymic as Sivertsen, the same name as Siursen (Sjur/Sivert), which fits his father Siffuer Toresen of the 1701 Overland household. ¶7

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Graded on his link to his son Sivert Nielsen Overland: Probable. Identification of the 1742 son 'Siur Nielsen' (b. about 1706) with Sivert Nielsen of Overland (1762 extra tax, 1767 godparent): Siur (Sjur) and Sivert are both forms of Sigurd and were used interchangeably for one man in Nordmøre records; same farm, same patronymic, and his age fits a man with a married son (Ole Sivertsen) and unmarried children over 12 (Niels and Ane) in 1762. His eldest known son Niels Sivertsen is named for Niels Siursen. But the two records are 20 years apart, with no record in between yet read, and the name form differs, so these parents are Probable, not Confirmed (graded on their link to Sivert Nielsen Overland). The Overland card series 1669-1746 (77795 views 4625-4629) has no other Niels or Siur. ¶3

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed, graded on his link to his son Sivert Nielsen Overland. Record: Nordmøre skifteprotokoll 24642 fol. 547b, 14 Sep 1742, probate after his wife Helge Olsdatter at Overland, naming 'Siur Nielsen 36 aar' among 'deris sammen avlede Børn' (the children of Niels Siursen and Helge together). The gap that kept this Probable (Siur in 1742 vs Sivert in 1762) is now closed by the Kvernes church book. Continuity, how the probate's 'Siur Nielsen' is followed to Sivert Nielsen of Overland in the tree (all Kvernes 16075, plus records already in the tree): (a) 14 Sep 1742 probate after Helge Olsdatter (24642 fol. 547b): children of Niels Siursen and Helge, 'Siur Nielsen 36', 'Berit Nielsdatter 34 gift med Peder Christophersen Strand', 'Marith Nielsdatter 31' unmarried; (b) 20 Feb 1742 (view 21): godmother 'Siur Øverlands hustru Ane'; (c) Judica 1744 (view 31): baptism of Iver, son of 'Sivert Øverland' and Ane, godmother 'Marrit Nielsd. Øverland' (the unmarried sister of the probate); the boy was buried as 'Iver Sivertsen Øverland', 14 weeks, in summer 1744 (view 33); (d) 4 p. Pascha 1745 (view 36): baptism of Tørris, son of 'Sivert Nielsen Øverland' and Ane (the patronymic now written out; the boy is named for Tøris Sifuersen of the 1701 Overland household); (e) 1 p. Epiph. 1748 (view 50): 'Sivert Nielsen Øverland' is first godfather to Knud, son of Peder Christophersen Strand and Berrit Nielsdatter, the sister and brother-in-law named in the 1742 probate; (f) 1762 extra tax (47939 view 57): Sivert Nielsen og hustru at Øverland, with children Niels and Ane (14 years after (e)); (g) 1767: Ane Iversdatter, wife of Sivert Nielsen Overland (16076 p. 122). Same farm throughout, gaps of 2 to 14 years, and the man is tied to the probate family by name, patronymic, wife and the sisters Marit and Berit standing in his circle. ¶5a

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

No rival: no other Siur or Sivert appears at Overland in the 1742 probate, the 1742-48 church book, or the 1762 list. Siur (Sjur) and Sivert are forms of one name, and (b)-(c) show the same man, with the same wife Ane, written both ways. ¶5b
