About the family

Father of Poul Haftorsen Garberg: the 1666 list names his son 'Paaffuel' (this scribe's spelling of Poul) Haftorsen, aged 3 (born about 1663), at Garberg; in the 1701 manntall Poule Haftorsen, aged 40, is a Garberg farmer, and he named his eldest son Haftor. No other Poul is at Garberg in 1666. Rated Confirmed for this link. ¶2

Life story

1624 leidang roll for Selbu (39890 views 76R-78): the only Garberg entry is 'Hanns(?) Gardbergh' (view 76 right; first name uncertain). There is no Jon Garberg and no Haftor, so the roll does not show Haftor's father on the farm. Garberg in 1664-66 had four users; the others may have been taxed under a different heading, or Haftor came to Garberg later. ¶7

Bygdebok (Selbu i fortid og nåtid vol. 3, Garberg 35/1, canvases 628 and 631): Haftor Jonsen became user of the bruk in 1634, was born about 1600, 'gift med Anne', son Pål b. 1663; 'Haftor døde [i] 1678'; Anne was still alive in 1690 and Pål took over 'etter moren' about 1693. 'Brukere' list: '-1647-1666- Haftord Jonsen, f. ca. 1600. [Barn:] Pål, f. ca. 1661; Mali, f. ca. 1662, [g....] Evensen Uglem'. Death 1678 is the book's date (no burial register before 1699). The words after 'bror av' (canvas 628, y1695) are not recovered: the book names a brother of Haftor there, a lead for his own parents. Earlier holders of the bruk: a Jon in 1557 and 1617 (canvas 628, y977-1117), not tied to Haftor in the recovered words. ¶8

1666 vicar's manntall, Selbu, no. 8 Garberg (3½ spann): 'Haftor Jonsen bruger 2 ørt 15ß er 64 Aar', with the son 'Paaffuel Haftorsen 3 Aar' - Poul (Paul) Haftorsen, aged 3 (Digitalarkivet 35528, view 21 left, crop checked by the orchestrator). The 1664 fogd's list gives a Haftor at Garberg aged 70, so his birth is set to 1594-1602. His father was a Jon. ¶1

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the identification · 1 correction · 3 open questions

The case for this identification 1

Supporting evidence: at the 1741 probate after Peder Paulsen Garberg (Poul Haftorsen's son), 'Havtor Olsen Uglem' acted as guardian and is called 'farens fetter' - the father's first cousin. A cousin named Haftor Olsen implies a shared grandfather Haftor, consistent with Poul Haftorsen being Haftor Jonsen's son (card 77804 c545). Still Probable because of Haftor's age. ¶5

Corrections 1

Correction: lowered from Confirmed to Probable. Haftor is 64 (1666) or 70 (1664), so he was about 60-67 when Poul was born about 1661-63 - possible (a late second marriage) but unusual. The list gives Poul the patronymic Haftorsen and places him in the son column under the Garberg users, and no younger Haftor is listed at Garberg in 1664-66, so Haftor Jonsen remains the only candidate; but a grandson by an unlisted son Haftor cannot be excluded. ¶4a

Open questions and the next record to read 3

What would carry this further: the 1647 and 1657 tax lists for a Jon at Garberg (not found transcribed); a probate after Haftor in Stjør- og Verdal protocol 1 (1689-1701, source 24749) or card index 77804. ¶3

Would settle it: a probate after Haftor Jonsen (Stjør- og Verdal protocol 1, 1689-1701, source 24749 / card index 77804), or the 1657 kvegskatt for Garberg. ¶4b

Biography gaps: his birth (1594-1602) predates every Selbu register, and his death and burial, if before 1699, fall outside the register; his last record read is the 1666 manntall. His probate is not found in the 77804 Garberg cards sampled (cards 531-545), and that index is only about 45% sampled, so the next checks are its unsampled gap cards and the unindexed Stjør- og Verdal protocol 1 (24749, 1689-1701). Selbygbogen vol. 1 surname hits at pp. 46, 65, 101, 112, 118, 284, 398 and 406 are not person-checked and are the next printed source. ¶6
