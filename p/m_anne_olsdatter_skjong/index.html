About the family

Parents: her father is entered as Ole Olsen of Skjong, whose daughter Anne was baptised at Borgund on 14 March 1734 (index). This link is Speculative, and the father carries that label; her own Probable confidence concerns her marriage and son and is unchanged. Name, patronymic, farm and age (about 25 at her 1759 engagement) agree, but other women named Anne Olsdatter lived at Skjong in the same years; see his notes for the limits and the record that would confirm it. ¶3

Life story

Checked (scan read): the probate of Ingeborg Rasmusdatter of Skjong, 4 Sep 1744 (Skifteprotokoll 12A, s. 227b-228a, source 24556 view 242), is not that of Ole Olsen Skjong's first wife. Her heirs are half-siblings with guardians (Lars.. Walderøen, Knud Olsen Walderhaug[?], Peder Andersen Schiong), and no Anne Olsdatter is named. Anne's mother is still unknown. ¶4

A maid of Skjong, engaged to Peder Olsen of Giske-Ødegård 3 Nov 1759. Presumed mother of his posthumous son Peder (1760), although the baptism names only the late father. Her later life is not traced. A widow Anne Pedersdatter (68) lived at Giske-Ødegård in 1801, but she is a different person. ¶1

Probate: as 'Enke Anne Olsdatter' she was the widow in the skifte after Peder Olsen of Giske-Ødegård on 28 November 1760 (Sunnmøre sorenskriveri, 3A 19, fol. 394a). Her legal guardian (lauværge) was Ole Jonsen of Giske-Ødegård, who had also witnessed her engagement in 1759. The entry does not say how he was related to her, and the lines read do not mention Skjong. The other heir was the infant son Peder Pedersen. The line that would say he was her child is partly illegible. An auditor's re-reading takes it as 'den afdødes og Enkens sammenavlede Børn' (the children of the deceased and the widow together), which would make the link explicit. Even without that reading, his age (about 18 weeks, matching his baptism of 24 July 1760) and the engagement of November 1759 leave little doubt. ¶2

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: Confirmed, graded on her link to her son Peder Pedersen Giske-Ødegård (see his page), not on her own parentage (her father Ole Olsen Skjong stays Probable). Her own probate, indexed in the Probate index for Sunnmøre 1690-1886 (Anne Olsd., Giskeødegård, registered 6 Feb 1816, Nordre Sunnmøre sorenskriveri, protocol 3A 10, fol. 201b) and read on the scan (source 24600, view 78, urn sk20090128680302, left page lower half): 'Aar 1816 den 6te Febr.' a registration and valuation was held at Giskeødegaard in Borgund skipreide 'efter den 13de Decbr. Sidstl. ved Døden afgangne Kaarkone Anne Olsdatter', whose estate was divided between her heirs: '1. Søn Ole Erichsen' (Eriksen), gardmann living on Giskeødegaard, and '2 Søn Peder Pedersen, boende her paa Gaarden, død og har i lovligt Egteskab sammenavlet følgende Livsarvinger': a. son Johan Pedersen, of age and present; b. son Knud Pedersen, a minor, curator Knud Knudsen Giskeødegaard; c. son Peter Pedersen, aged 12, guardian Peder Jensen Giskeødegaard; and, continuing on the right-hand page, d. son Jacob Pedersen, aged 8, and e. son Peder Pedersen, an infant (age read as about 1½ years), both with guardian Lars Andersen Giskeødegaard, followed by daughters [Marta?] and Anne Pedersdatter (lines partly illegible); the estate's other party includes Ole Eriksen Giskeødegaard. Continuity: same farm Giske-Ødegård throughout; the tree's Peder Pedersen was born there posthumously in 1760 to the late Peder Olsen and his widow Anne Olsdatter (1760 probate, fol. 394a), lived there, and died there in Aug 1814, i.e. dead by Feb 1816 as the probate says; the grandsons Knud and Peter match the sons of his first marriage (Knud about 1798, Peter about 1804) recorded as stepchildren in his widow Gurri's household in 1815, and the infant Peder fits his posthumous son Peder Johannes (see his page, baptised 30 Oct 1814). ¶7a

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

No rival: no other Peder Pedersen of Giske-Ødegård dying before 1816 is known. The son Ole Eriksen shows that after 1760 she remarried a man named Erik (not yet identified) and had a son Ole by him. Death date 13 Dec 1815 from the same probate; she was then a kårkone (retired, on the farm's support). The widow 'Anne Pedersdatter' aged 68 at Giske-Ødegård in the 1801 census, earlier judged to be a different woman, may in fact be her with a miswritten patronymic; not tested. A burial search (Anne, Giskeødegård, 1815-16) found no indexed entry. ¶7b

Searches that found nothing 1

Negative (scan read): the share list of the Mar 1746 probate after Gunhild Iversdatter of Skjong (24557/49, left page) gives the one joint child of Ole Olsen and Gunhild as 'Sønnen Jogn [Jon] Olsen', who received his share as mother's inheritance. The child was a son, so Gunhild Iversdatter was not the mother of Anne Olsdatter (b. about 1734). If Anne's father is the same Ole Olsen, Anne came from an earlier marriage. Anne's mother is still unknown. ¶6

Open questions and the next record to read 2

Lead (scan read): 'Schiong i Giskens Schibrede. Anno 1746 den [?] Martii' a probate was held after the late Gunnilde Iversdatter of Skjong 'imellem hendes igienlevende Mand Ole Olsen ibm. paa den ene Side, og deres sammen avlede 1 Barn' on the other. The opening breaks off at the foot of fol. 434a and fol. 434b begins with the inventory, so the child's name was not found; it may stand in the share list (not located on 24557/49). So an Ole Olsen of Skjong had one living child with Gunhild Iversdatter in 1746. If this is the Ole Olsen whose 1749 probate lists Anne aged 15 among six children, Gunhild could be Anne's mother, but there were several Ole Olsens at Skjong and the child's name cannot be read, so nothing is changed. ¶5a

Next: find the share list of this entry (24557/49 middle column has the closing; look for the child's lodd) or the Borgund 15995 baptisms of 1736-45 for a child of Ole Olsen Skjong. ¶5b
