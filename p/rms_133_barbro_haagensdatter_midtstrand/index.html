Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Graded on the link to the daughter Ragnild Helgesdatter (see her page): Confirmed. The probate after Helge Knudsen of Midtstrand, 11 Oct 1739 (DA 25286 p. 279, view 151; card 9297), is held between the widow Barbro Haagensdatter and 'deres sammen avlede 3de Børn' (their children together), the second being 'Datteren Ragnild Helgesdatter 4 aar gl.'. Continuity: the bride of 23 May / 1 Jul 1754, 'Ragnild Helgesdr. Mitstrand' (DA 9361 p. 93), has the same forename, patronymic and farm 15 years later (within the 15-year same-farm threshold); no other Ragnild Helgesdatter of Midtstrand is known, and the bygdebok's birth year 1735 for Ole Arnesen's wife agrees with the age 4 in 1739. Note that Ragnild's own link to her daughter Marit Olsdatter rests on the bygdebok and is Probable, so the line through these two to the tree is no stronger than Probable at that step. ¶2

Audit 9 (2 Nov 2026): Confirmed kept, graded on her link to her daughter Ragnild Helgesdatter (see her page). The 1739 probate is held between the widow Barbro Haagensdatter and 'deres sammen avlede 3de Børn', which names her as the mother (audit 8 rule 2). Continuity is as on her husband's record (15 years, same farm, no rival). Clean-up: the caveat that Ragnild's own link down is Probable is out of date (raised). ¶3
