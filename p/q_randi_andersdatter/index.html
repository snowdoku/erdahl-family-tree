Life story

Betrothal and marriage 1752: on Rogation Sunday, 5 May 1752, at Tjugum, 'Sponsalia Christen Peders: Sværen, Randi Andersd: Tiugum', with sureties (forlovere) 'Hans Æse' and 'Tollev Tenning Aasen'; married 19 June 1752 ('Nupt: Christen Pedersen Sværen, Randi Anders Daattr Tiugum') (Leikanger ministerialbok 1735-1756, folios 129-130). The entry gives her residence as Tjugum but does not name her father. Her first surety, Hans Esse, matches Hans Andersen Esse, whom the 1750 probate of Anders Andersen Tjugum names as his second son and as guardian of his sister 'Rangdi'; and at the next service (18 June 1752) a Claus Andersen Tiugum (the probate's third son is Claus Andersen) was betrothed with Hans Æse again as surety. This supports Anders Andersen Tjugum as her father. ¶6

1801 census: 74, mother of the farmer Peder Christensen at Sværen, 'widow after her 2nd marriage', living on a retirement allowance. Her estate is in the Ytre Sogn probate register for 1809-1813 at Sværen. kgroenha.net says she died in 1810 at Grøneng. ¶1

Probate (skifte): No. 26, held 28 May 18[10?] at Sværen (matrikkel no. 13), Tjugum skipreide, after 'Christen Pedersen Sværens Enke Randi Anders Datter Sværen', a føderaadskone (retired woman living on a farm pension). Her heirs were the seven children of 'sit 1ste Ægteskab' (her first marriage) with the long-dead Christen Pedersen Sværen: Peder Christensen, who lived and died at Sværen (his widow Ragnilde Olsdatter; children Christen 13, Ole 8, Randi and Ellie[?]); Kari, married to Ole Olsen Ytre Farnæs; Turi, married to Bjørn Johnsen Gjerde; Brithe; a fourth daughter [Brithe?], married to Marchus Marchussen Dahle; Randi, married to Arne Olsen Hopperstad of Vik skipreide; and Christi, married to Lasse Endresen Ham[mer]stad. Witnesses were Anders Andersen Sværen and Ole Iversen Dahle. 'First marriage' agrees with the 1801 census 'widow after her 2nd marriage'. Her own parents are not named. ¶2

Confidence raised from Probable to Confirmed as the mother of Turi Christensdatter and wife of Christen Persen Sværen: her 1810 probate (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll A 14, fol. 116a-117a, No. 26) names Turi Christensdatter, wife of Bjørn Johnsen Gjerde, as her daughter by Christen Pedersen Sværen. Her own parentage stays Probable (see Anders Andersen Tjugum). ¶3

Corrections 2

Correction: kgroenha.net says she died in 1810 at Grøneng. The probate places her at Sværen as a retired woman. She died before 28 May 1810 (the year digit of the probate date reads as 1810 but is not fully certain; the index range is 1809-1813). ¶4

Correction: the tree had Brita Knutsdatter Næsse (kgroenha.net) as her mother. The 1750 probate after Anders Andersen Tjugum names his widow, the mother of the daughter read 'Rangdi' (Randi), as [Kari?] Clausdatter (Brite Clausdatter). The link is Probable, as for the father. ¶5
