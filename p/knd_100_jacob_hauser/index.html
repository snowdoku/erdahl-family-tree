Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Graded on that link: her own marriage row at Illingen on 22 Apr 1738 reads 'Maria Catharina Hausers', relatives 'Jacob, Johann Gottfried' (her father, then the groom; record 908393752, image 1187139-00168). Ancestry 61023 index rows do not label roles (BRIEF audit 5 rule 3), so this rests at Probable until the image or a labelled FamilySearch twin is read; images not seen. No other record of him searched beyond this row. ¶1b

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed, graded on his link to his daughter Maria Catharina Hauser (see her page), wife of Johann Gottfried Finck. Illingen marriage register 1738 (image read), entry of 22 Apr: 'Johann Gottfried, weyl. Jacob Fincken gewesenen Burgers und Krämers allhier ehel. Sohn, mit Maria Catharina, Jacob Hausers Burgers und Gerichtsverwandten allhier ehel. Tochter, copulirt worden'. The register names her as the legitimate daughter of Jacob Hauser, burgher and member of the court (Gerichtsverwandter) at Illingen, and is the same record that makes her Johann Gottfried's wife in the tree, so no further identification step is needed. He was living in 1738 (no 'weyl.'), and was an Illingen burgher and court member, which corrects inference that she 'came from elsewhere': the family lived at Illingen in 1738 even though no Hauser baptism of hers is indexed there. Test record for her baptism: Illingen baptisms 1705-20 by image (film 1187139). Image read signed in on FamilySearch, 2 Nov 2026. ¶3

Searches that found nothing 1
