Life story

Brother or sister of Paul Ingebrigtsen (see his page), child of Ingebrigt Johnsen and Anne Paulsdatter. The couple's seven baptisms and one stillbirth were found by searching the whole Selbu baptismal index for children of a father Ingebrigt Johns- and a mother Anne Pauls-. The family was at ALSAET when John (1825) and Paul (1826) were born and at MARSTAD from 1831 onward, every later child carrying Marstad as a byname. ¶1

Baptism: born 23 March 1825, baptised 27 March 1825; Selbu parish register 1824-1842. Father Ingebrigt Johns, mother Anne Paulsd, residence 'Alfaet' (Alsaet). As the eldest son he is named for his father's father, a John - which is the naming custom's own evidence that Ingebrigt Johnsen's father was the Jon Ingebrigtssen of Store Evjen identified from the 1801 census. ¶2

Death and burial: died 28 March 1825 and buried 4 April 1825 at Selbu: 'Huusmands Søn John Ingebrigts. Alsæt' - a cottager's son, John Ingebrigtsen of Alsaet - aged a few days (the figure reads '7 Dg', days; the reading of the number is not certain, and he was born on 23 March). Minister's register (ministerialbok) 1824-1842, burials 1825, p. 362 no. 29 on 13 Oct 2026; the clerk's duplicate (1816-1831, pp. 260-261 no. 9) is indexed as 'Jon Ingebrigtssøn'. The index codes the first entry female; the scan says son. This entry had been carried as a possible burial of his grandfather Jon Ingebrigtssen; it is not. ¶3
