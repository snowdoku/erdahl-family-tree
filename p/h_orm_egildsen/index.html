About the family

Father of Gunbjør: named in her 1827 baptism and her 1849 marriage. ¶1

Children baptized in Treungen 1819-1833, including Gundbjør (1826, died young) and Gundbjør (1827). Hæge Ormsdatter (b. ~1822 at Tvedt, m. 1847) is probably also a daughter. A son, Torof Ormsen Tvedt, is named on Find a Grave. ¶3

His parentage is Probable: in the 1801 census, Orm Egildsen, aged 12, is at Tvedt as a child of Egild Ormsen and Gundbiør Toruvsdatter. ¶4
