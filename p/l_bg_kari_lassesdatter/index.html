Life story

Baptised 2 Jul 1828. Daughter of farmer Lasse Torkeldsøn and Ingeborg Monsdatter of Asheim. Her parents married on 22 Jun 1828, two days before her birth. ¶1

Married Rasmus Iversen of Botnen 24 Jun 1848. Mother of Ingeborg (1850). Died a widow ('kaarkone') in 1900. ¶2
