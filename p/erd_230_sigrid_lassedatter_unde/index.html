How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the grade · 1 open question

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Graded Probable on her link to her son Hermund Olsen of Gulteigen (see his page). The Vik bygdebok (H. 1, canvas 43, Unde chapter; text rebuilt from word boxes, so dates ABT) says Ola Ivarson (1665-1733) came to Unde and married Sigrid Lassedtr Undi (1660-1742), and lists their children: Hermund 1688 (who went to Gulteig, d. 1743 aged about 53), Brita, Ivar 1690, Lasse 1694, Sjur 1696 and Anna 1698. This matches the 1701 census of Vndi (Olle Ifversen 36 with sons Hermund 13, Ifver 11, Lasse 7, Siver 5; Digitalarkivet pc626694-8), which lists no wives. She seems to have been the daughter and heiress of Lasse, holder of Unde in 1667 (her second son Lasse is named after him). Why only Probable: the mother is named only in the compiled bygdebok, and the census does not name her. ¶1a

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Test record: her own probate at Unde about 1742 in the Ytre Sogn probate index (domicile Undi/Unde/Vndi, 1740-45), or Hermund Olsen's 1743 Gulteigen probate (6a fol. 324a) if it lists an inheritance from his mother; either would name her son Hermund Olsen. ¶1b
