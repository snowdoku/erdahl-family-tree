About the family

Named as the groom's father ('Ole Olssen' / 'O. Olsen') at the 1838 Nord-Aurdal marriage of Erik Olssen, whose birthplace is given as 'Bøe'. Nothing else is known. A speculative candidate (not adopted) is Ole Olsen of Garthus / Langødegaard / Foss, Sør-Aurdal, married to Andrea Østensdatter, who had a son Erich baptized 20 Oct 1811. ¶1

Life story

An 'Ole Olsen Bøe' stood godfather on 13 Oct 1811 in Nord-Aurdal, at the baptism of Svend, son of Bernt Christensen[?] Bøe and Ingri Tidemandsdatter (9365 p. 39). So an Ole Olsen lived at a Nord-Aurdal Bøe farm in 1811. No Erik was baptised to an Ole Olsen in Nord-Aurdal from Dec 1810 to Dec 1811, though. The Sør-Aurdal Ole Olsen (Garthus 1804-08, Langødegaard 1811, Foss 1816; wife Andrea Østensdatter) remains a candidate without a Bøe link. ¶3

Run 4: the 9380 baptism index (1825-1840) has an Ole Olsen, inderst (lodger), at Bøen in Sør-Aurdal with children baptised 1827 (mother Marit Andersdt), 1829 (Ingeborg Olsdt), 1832, 1835 and 1836/37 at Bøenseie (Sigri Olsdt). These may be one man with successive wives or several men. None has Andrea Østensdatter as wife, so they do not link Ole Olsen Langødegaarden (father of Erich b. 1811) to Bøen. Which Ole Olsen at Bøe was the father of the 1838 groom is not established. (Digitalarkivet pd00000003397093, pd00000003397957, pd00000003399619, pd00000003400837, pd00000003401761; index only, scans not read.) ¶4

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 open question

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Lead (Oct 2026): the 1801 census for Aurdal lists several men named Ole Olsen at Bøe farms. They include Ole Olsen (b. about 1757, huusmand at Bøe Vester) with a son Ole Olsen (b. about 1789), and Ole Olsen (b. about 1791), a stepson at Bøe. The 1816 silver tax lists Ole Olsen at 'Bøe nordre' and at 'Søm-Bøe'. One of the younger men could be Erik's father (b. about 1811), but no baptism links them. Not adopted. ¶2
