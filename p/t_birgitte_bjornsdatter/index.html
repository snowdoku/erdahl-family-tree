About the family

Her patronymic means her father was named Bjørn, a relatively uncommon name in Romsdal, which should make her family traceable in the Eid and Voll registers. Her own baptism, age and death have not been found. ¶3

Life story

She is the probable mother of Jon Tronsen of Sætnes. His baptism on 28 Dec 1751 at Eid church names only the father, 'Thron' of 'Jelden el. Strømme', but the clerk added that the parents were betrothed and should have been married that same day. Tron Jonsen's betrothal is recorded in 1751 and his wedding to Birgitte in April 1752, and the bride's described condition fits a child already born or on the way. No other wife of Tron Jonsen is recorded. ¶2

Married Tron Jonsen ('Thron Joensøn') of Jelden on 4 Apr 1752 at Eid church in Grytten parish, Romsdal. The register gives her residence as Eide and her status as 'Besvangrede' - that she had been got with child before the wedding. ¶1

Marriage scan read, Grytten p. 190, Easter Tuesday 1752 (4 Apr): 'Thron Joensøn Jelden og af ham beswangrede Birrite Bjørns D. Eide' - she had been got with child by Tron himself. This supports her as the mother of Joen, baptised in December 1751 as Thron Jelden's child, whose parents the clerk noted were betrothed. The entry gives no age, sureties or father for her. ¶5

A woman named Birgitte and another named Birgith appear as godmothers at Strømme in 1782 and 1783, which may be her, but the register gives no patronymic for either. ¶4

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
