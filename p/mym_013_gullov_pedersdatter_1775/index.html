About the family

Father linked at Probable: a Peder of Skiervold, whose daughter Golla was baptised 28 Jul 1771; she married from Skiervold as 'Gullov Petersdatter' in 1798. Her own baptism is not in the Skatval index. ¶6

Probable parents: Peder Jonsen of Schiervold (d. before 15 Jan 1772) and his second wife Marith Olsdatter. His estate registration of 15 Jan 1772 lists 'Gallov Peders Datter ½ Aar gl.' among his heirs (Stjør- og Verdal skifteprotokoll 6-2, fol. 428b-429a), which fits the Golla baptised at Skatval 28 Jul 1771; a 1773 note on the skifte names her as a minor (myndling). In 1801 her mother Marit Olsdatter, 67, is the wife of Peter Larsen, farmer at Schiervold (household 01), and Gullov is in household 02 on the same farm. If so she was born in mid-1771, about four years before her 1801 census age suggests (26). Read. ¶7

Life story

Marriage: As 'Pige Gullov Petersdatter Skiervolde' she married the soldier Peder Pedersen of Baar on 14 June 1798 (Stjørdal 1797-1815 marriages p. 606 no. 25, scan read). Her father is not named. ¶4

1801 census: Gullov Pedersdatter, 26, is listed as 'Hans kone' (his wife) of Peder Pedersen, inderst, at Schiervold (Skjervold), Størdalen. She is in her first marriage, and Daarethe Pedersdatter, 2, is listed as 'Deres barn' (their child). Her name is a form of Gullaug. ¶1

1802: She is named as mother, 'Gullov Petersd.', at the baptism of her son Peder at Skatval on 31 October 1802. Peder and Peter are interchangeable in these registers. ¶2

Widowed in 1806; as 'Gullov Pedersd., Enken' of Schiervold she married the bachelor Christopher Olsen of Øvervinge on 16 Oct 1806 (Stjørdal marriages p. 646 no. 62). Her own burial was not found in the Stjørdal index 1797-1830. ¶5

Motherhood of Daaret: Rated Probable. The census calls Daarethe the couple's child, but Daaret's own baptism has not been found, and the 1828 marriage index names only the bride's father. ¶3

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: Confirmed, graded on her link to her daughter Daaret Elisabet Pedersdatter (see her page), not on her own parentage (Probable). The record naming the relationship is the 1801 census of Schiervold (Skjervold), Størdalen, household 02: Peder Pedersen, 33, inderst, and 'Hans kone' Gullov Pedersdatter, 26, both in their first marriage, with 'Daarethe Pedersdatter', 2, 'Deres barn' (their child). Continuity: the tree's Daaret married in 1828 aged 29, born 1799 at Skiervold, the same farm, and the 1828 marriage index names her father Peder; Gullov was still at Skjervold as Peder's wife at the baptism of her son Peder on 31 Oct 1802 ('Gullov Petersd.'), and as his widow when she remarried there in 1806. ¶8a

Corrections 1

Correction: her father is Peter Larsen of Skjervold (see his page), Marit Olsdatter's second husband, not Peder Jonsen. Her baptism has now been found: Skatval, 11th Sunday after Trinity [14 Aug] 1774, '5 Peter Schjervolds b: Gudlov', godparents Ole, Malie and Anne Schjervold, Sivert Velvang, Malie Moxne[?] (Stjørdal 16371, canvas 251, p. 249 left; not in the online baptism index). Peder Jonsen's daughter Golla (bapt. 28 Jul 1771), the child the tree had matched to her, was buried in 1773 (index: 'Golla Pedersdatter, b. 1771, Skiervold, avdød, 1773'). Her age 26 in Feb 1801 fits Aug 1774, not Jul 1771. The Skatval bygdebok (vol. 3, canvas 273) agrees: the bride of Peder Pedersen (m. 14.06.1798) was 'datter av bruker, døpt 14.08.1774', and Peder got a deed from 'sin svigerfar' Peter Larsen in 1804. Her mother is still Marit Olsdatter (Peter Larsen married her in 1772). Her own grade (on her link to her daughter) is unchanged; the father link is graded on Peter Larsen's record (Probable). ¶11

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

No rival: Peder Pedersen and Gullov were each in their first marriage in 1801, so Daarethe cannot be a stepchild. ¶8b

Searches that found nothing 2

Land records, negative for her link: the Stjørdal panteregister quitclaim list 1762-1800 (22318 images 13-23) has 'Kiersten Pedersdatter til Joen Pedersen Schiervold' (C fol. 226, about 1781) and Anders Pedersen's(?) quitclaim to Peter Larsen Schiervold (C-3 fol. 616, 1792), but no quitclaim from a Gullov/Golla Pedersdatter to her guardian Lars Pedersen Wigen up to fol. ~650; a quitclaim of hers, if any, would fall after 1792 (C-3, fol. 650-934, images 22-23 of 22318, not yet read). ¶9

Negative: the quitclaim ('Afkald') list of the Stjørdal tinglag panteregister 1762-1800 (source 22318, images 22-23, pantebok C fol. 653-938, c.1793-1800) was read in full for a quitclaim by Gullov Pedersdatter (or her husband for her) to Lars Pedersen Wigen; there is none. With images 13-21, the whole Afkald list 1762-1800 has now been searched without finding her. ¶10

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Test record for the father link: the 14 Feb 1804 deed from Peter Larsen to his son-in-law Peder Pedersen (Stjørdal pantebok after 1800). ¶12
