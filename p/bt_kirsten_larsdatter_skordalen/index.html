About the family

Wife of Lars Andersen of Almoen, married 29 May 1792. The marriage entry calls her a maid of Skordalen; the 1801 census, which gives her age as 50 and her husband's as 36, records her patronymic as Lassedatter, probably a slip for Larsdatter. She was some fourteen years older than her husband. ¶1
