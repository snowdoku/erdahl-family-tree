How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Probable mother of Johannes Finck (see his page). ¶1a

Graded on that link: inferred, not named. She is the wife of Johann Gottfried Finck at Illingen in the baptisms of 27 Jan 1739 (Jacob Friderich, record 8393227) and 4 Sep 1749 (Johann Gottfried, record 8395371), the years in which Johannes (married 1771) was probably born; his baptism is not indexed, so an earlier or other wife cannot be excluded. Surname not indexed. ¶1b

The case for this identification 1

Surname and father: she is Maria Catharina 'Hausers' (Hauser), daughter of Jacob, who married Johann Gottfried Finck at Illingen on 22 Apr 1738 (bride row 908393752, relatives 'Jacob, Johann Gottfried'; groom row 8393752; image 1187139-00168). Whole window for her son Johannes (married 1771, so born about 1738-50): the marriage of Apr 1738 opens it; she is the mother named in the couple's indexed baptisms of 1739 and 1749 and at their son Johann Gottfried's burial in 1823 (7505918); no other wife of Johann Gottfried is indexed. Still Probable on her link to Johannes, because his own baptism is not indexed and the years 1740-48 are not fully read (a 1746 row 'Johann Gottlieb x Maria Catharina' may be this couple). ¶2
