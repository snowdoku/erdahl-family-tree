About the family

Mother of Ole Jensen Quame, as named on the index of Ole's Minnesota death certificate (1934-MN-004071): 'Bertha Quome'. 'Quome/Quame' is Ole's American surname, so her maiden name is not known; in Norway her given name would be Berte, Birthe or Britha. Graded on her link to Ole Jensen Quame: Probable (index only, informant unknown; image restricted). ¶1

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

Records that disagree 1

Against: birth 3 Oct 1853 vs 23 Nov 1853 on his gravestone (the 1900 census gives Sept 1853). Norway-side follow-up: Fjærland/Balestrand emigrants 1854-1861 (Jens Larsen, Britha Olsdatter, son Ole), the Fjærland marriage of Jens Larsen x Britha Olsdatter c. 1853-58, Ole's absence from the Fjærland confirmations c. 1868, and whether a Kvamme/Kvame farm lies in or near Fjærland. Crop:. ¶2b

Alternatives considered and set aside 3

Candidate Norwegian baptism (Speculative, not linked): Fjærland annex of Balestrand prestegjeld, Sogn (ministerialbok 1853-1879, 1418P, p. 2, 1853 no. 20; FS image DGS 107616644 img 2; index https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:687D-N8SV): Ole, born '3die Octbr', baptised 25 Oct 1853; parents read from the image as Jens Larsen [Nordal?], 'født paa Bøium' (born on Bøyum), and 'Pige' (unmarried girl) Britha Olsd[atter] Bøium/Supphellen; the legitimacy column appears to read 'Uægte' (illegitimate) [uncertain]. Sponsors: Johannes Bøium, Peder Eriksen ibid., Ingebrigt Iversen Sophellen, Gjøri Iversdatter ibid., Britha Olsdatter Bøium; right-hand remark 'Ole Ingebrigtsen Sophellen'. For: father Jens LARSEN and mother BRITHA = Bertha both match the 1934 death index exactly; year 1853 matches; and Fjærland is the parish earlier guessed for 'Quame'. ¶2a

Candidate test (1 Nov 2026), the Fjærland baptism re-read on the Digitalarkivet scan of the main Balestrand ministerialbok (8378, p. 25 no. 56; the Fjærland copy book 1418P p. 2 no. 20 is the same act): '56. [born] 3 Oct. [baptised] 25 Oct. Ole. uægte [illegitimate]. Ungk[arl] Jens Larsen Nordnæs af Sogndal / Pige Britthe Olsd[atte]r Bøjum / Nedkomst [delivered at] Bøjum. [church] M[undal].' Godparents: Sjur Johannesen Bøjum, Peder Eriksen ibid., Ingebrigt Iversen Supphellen, Gjøri Iversdatter Bøjum, Britthe Olsdatter Bøjum. Remark column: 'Anmeldt af hendes Fader Ole Ingebrigtsen Supphellen. Begges 1ste Leiermaal' (reported by her father Ole Ingebrigtsen of Supphellen; the first illegitimate child of both). Correction to the earlier reading: the father was NOT 'born on Bøyum'. He was a bachelor from the farm Nornes (Nordnæs) in Sogndal parish; Bøyum is where the mother lived and gave birth. ¶3

Candidate mother's father: the 1853 baptism was reported by 'hendes Fader Ole Ingebrigtsen Supphellen'; an Ole Ingebrigtsen, b. 1789 Balestrand, widower and føderaadsmand, was a lodger at Supphellen in the 1865 census. He is not added to the tree because the identification of Bertha Quome with this Britha is unproved. She is NOT the Britha Olsdatter of Bøyum who married Knud Olsen Vaadeng on 18 Jun 1855 (bride's father Ole Ellendsen Bøjum; that couple was at Bøium in 1865 with son Ole Knudsen b. 1855 and no stepson), nor the one who married Paul Kjeldsen on 15 Mar 1857 (bride's father Ole Eriksen). ¶4a
