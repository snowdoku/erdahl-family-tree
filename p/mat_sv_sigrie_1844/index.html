Life story

Baptism: baptised in Selbu on 27 OCTOBER 1844 - Selbu parish register, Selbu local parish 1843-1859, births and baptisms, page 18 serial no. 97, with the parallel entry at 1842-1858 page 135 serial 97. Child 'Sigrie', birth year 1844; father 'Sivert Johnsen'; mother 'Brynnild Johnsdatter', residence EIDEM. The duplicate register names the child in full as 'Segri Severtsdatter EIDEM'. ¶1

She is one of three children of this marriage who died in infancy - a Sigrie baptised 1844 and two boys baptised John in 1847 and 1854 - which is why eight baptisms produced five survivors. Infants nobody in the American family had ever heard of. ¶3

She died before 17 June 1849, when a second daughter was baptised Sigri to the same parents. Only one Sigri is in the 1865 household, and Norwegian naming practice did not give two living children the same name; re-use after a death was the norm. Her burial entry has not yet been read and is the record that would turn this from Probable to Confirmed. ¶2

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
