About the family

Parents added, Probable: Ole Bersvendsen Kjøsnes and Ingeborg. Baptism: 12th Sunday after Trinity, 27 Aug 1702, 'döbt Olaf Kiøsnæs barn Olaf' (16310 canvas 13 right; godparents Biørn Aftred, Tron ibid, Bersvend Vigen, Ingebor Kiøsnæs, Brynel ibid), and the mother's churching on 15 Oct 1702, 'Olaf Bers. Kiøsnæs qv. Ingebor efter det 7de barn Olaf' (canvas 14 left). The bygdebok (vol. 7, canvas 299) lists '7. Ola, f. 1702' as the seventh child of the holder Ola and Ingeborg, matching the churching. Kept Probable: no record links the 1702 child to the holder of 1728-1773 across the 26 years, and Ole Guttormsen's son Ole (b. about 1699, Kjøsnes 1701) is a named rival. The birth year is left as ABT 1702 until the identification is proved. ¶5

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Graded Probable on his link to his daughter Berit Olsdatter (see her page). Selbu i fortid og nåtid vol. 7, Kjøsnes (canvas 300): '1728-1773 Ola Olsen, [f.] 1702, d. 1774. G. 1728 m. Anne Persdt. [fra] Flora, f. 1706, d. 1779. De [bodde] først [...] sammen med bror hans'; children Ingeborg 1731, Ola 1733 (d. 1766, m. 1757 Ingeborg [...] Overvik), '3. Berit, f. 1735, [g.I m.] Per Anderssen [...] [g.II m....] Gunnarsen [Mølnhus, på] Sæter', Per 1737, Jon 1744, Marit 1747, Haldor 1750 (took over), and others. Vol. 6, Sæter, plass Sveen (canvas 394) matches from the other side: '-1761-1793 Per Anderssen [...] 1734, [d.] 1793 [...] G. ca. 1755 [m.] Berit [...] Kjøsnes, [f.] 1735, [d.] 1805', children Anders c.1755, Anne 1761 (m. Nils Eriksen Aunet), Ola 1767 (d. 1793, m. 1792 Ragnhild [...] Slindan; daughter Berit 1792), and '1794-1805 Berit [...] enke etter Per Anderssen [g.II] 1795 [...] Gunnarsen Mølnhus'. This is the tree's Berit Olsdatter, widow in the 1793 probate after Peder Andersen of Sveet (24763 p. 391), mother of Ole Pedersen Sæter and grandmother of Berit Olsdatter b. 1792. He is himself '7. Ola, f. 1702, [se] over' among the children of the earlier holder Ola [...] (b. c.1657) and Ingeborg (canvas 299). ¶1

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed, graded on his link to his daughter Berit Olsdatter (see her page). The probate after him, registered 27 Sep 1774 'paa gaarden Kiösnes i Selboe Tinglaug' (Stjør- og Verdal skifteprotokoll 24758 fol. 255, found through the typed register 'O. Ole Olsen, Kjøsnes'), names as the late Ole Olsen's heirs, after the widow Anne Peders Datter: 1 the son Peder Olsen, married; 2 the son Joen Olsen, married and living at Wigen; 3 the son Haldoe Olsen; 4 the son Bersvend Olsen, 25; 5 the son Ole Olsen, dead, leaving a son Ole Olsen and a daughter Anne Olsdatter; 6 'Datteren Berithe Ols Datter gift med Peder Andersen Sæter'; 7 the daughter Ingebor Olsdatter, dead, leaving a daughter Helge Jonsdatter. The tree's Berit is the wife of Peder Andersen of Sveen under Sæter (see his page; baptisms 1761 and 1767 'Per Anders. Sveen/Svee og Berit Olsd.', and his own 1793 probate naming 'Enke Berithe Olsdatter'). The probate names the relationship (daughter) and identifies her by her husband, so no cross-farm identification is needed; no other Berit Olsdatter married to a Peder Andersen Sæter is known. This is the bygdebok's test record, and it agrees with Selbu i fortid og nåtid vol. 7 (Kjøsnes, canvas 300). Death: before 27 Sep 1774 (registration date). ¶4

Corrections 1

Correction: the bygdebok's marriage year 1728 is not borne out; 16310 canvases 112-121 (Easter 1728 to autumn 1730) were read in full and this is the only Ole Olsen x Anne Persdatter marriage. The couple's first child per the bygdebok is Ingeborg, 1731, and the widow in his 1774 probate is Anne Pedersdatter, so the 1730 couple is very probably this one; graded as identification only. ¶7b

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Rival Ole: the bygdebok's family of Ola Guttormsen Kjøsnes (vol. 7, canvas 288: Ola Guttormsen, b. ca. 1667, d. 1707, and Ingeborg) lists '2. Ola, f. 1699, g.m. Eli [...]', so the book gives the rival Ole Olsen (b. about 1699) a wife Eli, not Anne Persdatter. This is secondary and does not yet exclude him by a primary record. Note also that on the 18th Sunday after Trinity 1730 (8 Oct) the wife of an 'Ole Kiøsnes' was churched (16310 c121 right, bottom), four months after the June 1730 wedding; this is probably the rival Ole and Eli, but may be this couple (no baptism of an Ole Kjøsnes child is entered in Aug-Oct 1730). Parents stay Probable. His brother Thomas married 'Gurru Joens D:' on the 3rd day of Pentecost 1729 (7 Jun 1729, 16310 c117 right: 'Thomes Ols: Kiøsnes'), matching the 1761 skifte after Gurie Jonsdatter, Thomas's widow. ¶8

Searches that found nothing 1

Negative: his marriage to Anne Persdatter, which the bygdebok dates 1728, is not in Selbu 16310 between Easter 1728 and Candlemas 1729 (canvases 112-114 read in full; the marriages there are Gunder Olsen x Kirstin Joensd., Peder Andersen Wigen x Birgitte Olsd., Sivert Olsen x Barbro Iversd., Joen Jonsen Andal x Ingebor Mattisd., Haldoe Pedersen x Anne Larsd., Ole Mi[...] x Ingeborg Persd., Peder Larsen x Gurru Olsd.). ¶6a
