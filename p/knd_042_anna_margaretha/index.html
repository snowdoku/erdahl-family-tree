Life story

Maiden name: Becher. Bride row 'Anna Margaretha Bechers' (Becher with the genitive/feminine -s), relatives 'Joh. Jacob, Johann Adam' (her father, then the groom), Vaihingen an der Enz 15 Nov 1703 (record 904776330, image 1184562-00486); groom row 'Johann Adam Koodel', relatives 'Georg, Anna Margaretha' (4776330). Continuity: she is the mother named in all four Vaihingen baptisms of Joh. Adam Knodel's children 1705-1714, the 1711 one being Georg Jacob. Her own grade (on Georg Jacob) is unchanged. Her baptism (about 1675-85) not found at Vaihingen under Becher/Böcher/Becker/Bächer. ¶3

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

2 on the grade · 2 on the identification · 2 open questions

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Upgrade: Speculative to Probable, graded on her link to her son Georg Jacob Knodel (see his page): the Vaihingen birth of 11 Jul 1711 names 'Joh. Adam, Anna Margaretha' (row 4768553). Georg Jacob, father of Christoph Adam (FamilySearch QPGK-HRCY, 1740), is the 1737 Vaihingen groom whose father is indexed 'Adam' (row 4777562), and the 1711 child is the only Georg Jacob Knodel baptised at Vaihingen in the index. She is the mother in all three indexed births of the couple (1705, 1711, 1714). Probable because the rows are unlabelled and the identification rests on name, parish and the father's name. ¶2

Re-check, grade held at Probable (on her link to her son Georg Jacob Knodel). The in-gap baptisms are now dated: Elisabetha Margaretha 21 Sep 1705 (died 13 Nov 1709), Georg Jacob 11 Jul 1711, Johannes 26 Oct 1714, all at Vaihingen to Joh. Adam Knodel x Anna Margaretha. No record of Johann Adam's family at Vaihingen 1715-1736 was found on FamilySearch (search for children of an Adam Knodel, Vaihingen area 1704-1745), so the gap to the 1737 groom (father 'Adam Knodels') is 23 years without an in-gap record. A rival of the right age exists: a Georg Jacob Knodel married Anna Maria Kieffer in 1734 ('Germany, Marriages', place given only as Württemberg), not yet placed; and a Georg Jacob Knodel of Derdingen, b. 1696, d. 15 Feb 1738. Supporting, not decisive: the burial of a Georg Jakob Knodel at Vaihingen in 1794 gives birth 1711 from age. The Vaihingen images are partner-only on FamilySearch. ¶5a

The case for this identification 2

Identity (Speculative): mother of the Georg Jacob Knodel born at Vaihingen an der Enz on 11 July 1711 and of Johannes born there 26 October 1714 (index rows 4768553 and 4768768, relatives 'Joh. Adam, Anna Margaretha'); her surname is not indexed. Entered as the mother of Georg Jacob Knodel only on the Speculative identification of that man with the 1711 child. The 1711 birth image (1184562-00144) may give her maiden name. ¶1

Labelled evidence, grade held at Probable: QPGK-DBXQ, Georg Jacob Knodel born Vaihingen 11 Jul 1711 to Joh. Adam Knodel and Anna Margaretha; the 1737 groom's labelled father is 'Adam Knodels' (QPGK-7T87); Johann Adam stayed at Vaihingen until his death there in 1761 (QPGK-2X5H). Held because the 26-year gap is bridged only by the father's name, and the dated in-gap baptisms of this couple (Johannes QPGV-P87B, Elisabetha Margaretha QPG2-ZDDW) were not opened. Her 1703 marriage (Becher) was not found on FamilySearch. ¶4a
