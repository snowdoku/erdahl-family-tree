Ingebrigt (Ingebricht) Hansen Bøec. 1654 – bef. 1741Probable

About the family

Father of Ingebrigt Hansen of Nedre Bøe, Grytten (see his page), known only from Ingebrigt's patronymic in the 1701 manntall ('Ingebrigt Hans.', pc00000005080454) and his 1741 burial ('Ingebricht Hansen', pg00000007161705). Born before about 1640, since his son was born about 1654-61. ¶1a

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Probable, not Confirmed: the patronymic names the relationship, but the identification of 'Ingebricht, Bøe' (Guri's father) with Ingebrigt Hansen of Nedre Bøe rests on farm, period and the naming of Ingebricht's son Hans, and Hans himself is not otherwise identified. ¶1b
