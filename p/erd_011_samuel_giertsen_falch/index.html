Life story

Probate: named in the probate (skifte) of her/his father Giert Nielsen Falck, former innkeeper at Balholmen, registered 9 October 1805 (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll A 12, fol. 510b-511a, No. 11), as one of the 7 children of Giert and his widow Citrone (Zithone) Samuelsdatter 'in lawful marriage'. Third son, 10 years old, living with his mother; guardian Christen Christensen Fiærestad the younger. Aged 6 in the 1801 census. ¶1
