About the family

Mother of Berethe Olsdatter (see her page), Probable; graded on her link to Berethe. The Haram bygdebok, Band 2b (nb.no b709b66866b959f610ebd56887cb57fe; text rebuilt from content-search word boxes) states it twice. In the Store Kalvøy bruk 5 chapter, p. 376 (canvas 376, right column): 'Berte, ca. 1695 - 1777, dotter [av] Karen [..] Ole Jetmundson, gifta seg [i] 1725 [med] Pål Jørgenson Alvestad, ca. 1702 - 1749'. On p. 370 Karen is named twice beside Ole Jetmundson just before the list of his children Jetmund (ca. 1689), Hans (ca. 1693) and Berte (ca. 1695). No patronymic is given (none of about 30 -dotter forms queried occurs on either page). A published secondary source only; no primary record names her. Not to be confused with Karen Johansdatter, wife of an Ole Olsen of Lille Kalvøy (probate 1741). ¶1

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Audit 7 (1-2 Nov 2026): grade unchanged (Probable, compiled source only). Under the audit-6 rule a bygdebok-only ancestor must name the primary record that would test the link; this note supplies it. ¶2a

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Test record: a probate after Karen herself under Store Kalvøy (Sunnmøre probate index; none after Ole Jetmundsen is indexed for 1691-1790), or a court or land record naming Ole Jetmundsen's wife. The 1749 probate after Berethe's husband Poul Jørgensen Alvestad may name her brothers Jetmund or Hans Olsen as guardians (morbrødre); that would test the father's side only. ¶2b
