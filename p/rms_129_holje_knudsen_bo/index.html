About the family

Father of Trond Høljesen (see his page); Probable, graded on his link to Trond, from the bygdebok alone. Valdres bygdebok I F, gnr 106 Bø (canvas 267, right column): 'Hølje Knutsson f [om] 1619, d [mellom] 1682 [og] 1685, g m Astrid Trondsdotter.... Born: 1 Trond f om 1647... 2 Knut f 1648, d 1727, g m Sigrid Andrisdotter 1649-1706...'. In the 1645 koppskatt his widowed mother Bergit paid for herself and her son Hølje; in the 1647 matrikkel Hølje held half of Bø, and he was the user of 1 hud 4 skinn in 1664/66 and in 1657/58 (canvas 267, left column). In his parents' chapter (canvas 266) he is '1 Hølje f om 1619, [fekk] halve garden'. Rebuilt from nb.no content-search word boxes; dates from the rebuild are given as ABT. ¶1a

Life story

Vicar's census 1664 for Aurdal (DA 35504, p. 10, view 14, left page, last farm): 'Helje Knudsøn 45 aar, bruger j hud iiij skind, halff Mele korn, j pund Smør[?]'; sons column 'Knud Helgesøn 16 aar' and 'Trond Helgeß. 1[2?] aar'; second holder on the same farm 'Eivind Knudß. 41 aar, bruger j hud iiij skind, j Mele korn, j pund Smør[?]'; among the servants 'Erland Knudß. Soldat 19 aar'. Ages and land fit the bygdebok exactly (Hølje b. about 1619 with sons Trond b. about 1647 and Knut b. 1648; brother Eivind b. about 1623; Hølje's share 1 hud 4 skinn in 1664/66). This is a primary record naming Trond as a son of Hølje Knudsen. Grade NOT raised: (a) the farm name on this page reads '[?]raberg' (first letter unclear: D/K/V), not Bø; a farm 'Bøe' (v huder j skind, Oluff Knudsøn 73 and Niels Bendixsøn 24) follows at the top of p. 11, so which farm this household sat on in 1664 needs resolving before the census is tied to the Bø family of the tree; (b) Trond's age reads 12, against the bygdebok's 'about 1647' (17); (c) the gap from 1664 to Trond's later records at Bø (1690s-1707) is over 19 years. Bailiff's list a) of 12 May 1664 (DA 35563, p. 53, view 36, right page) checked: it has 'Helge Aabreig' and 'Eivind Aabreig' on consecutive lines (no sons entered for either), followed by 'Oluff Bøe' with son 'Knud Olsen'. So both 1664 lists put Hølje and Eivind Knudsen on a farm written Aabreig/[?]raberg, distinct from the Bøe of Oluff Knudsen. The bygdebok's gnr 106 chapter (canvas 268) itself cites 'manntalet 1664' and a child '12 år gamal', so its author used this same household; whether gnr 106 is the farm the 1664 lists call Aabreig (the tree calls it Bø) must be settled from the bygdebok's chapter heading or the 1647 matrikkel before any raise. ¶2a

Bailiff's census list b) of 12 Nov 1664 for Aurdal (DA 35563, p. 212, view 114, left page): farm 'Aaberigs = 2½ huder 9 skind, 1 M. korn, 1 pund...': 'Helje Knudsen 45 aar', br. 1 hud 4½ skind ½ M.; 'Eivind Knudsen 41 aar', br. 1 hud 4½ skind ½ M.; sons column 'Knud Helgesen 16 aar' and 'Thrond Helgesen 12 aar'. The NEXT farm entered separately is 'Bøe = 5 huder 1 skind', with 'Olle Knudsen 73 aar' (bruger 2 huder) and Niels[?] Hendrichsen 24, son Knud Olsen 23. This settles the reading questions left: the vicar's '[?]raberg' and the bailiff's list a) 'Helge Aabreig' are the same farm, Aaberg, which both bailiff lists treat as a farm distinct from Bø. Correction (place, not parentage): the bygdebok files Hølje Knutsson under gnr 106 Bø and cites the 1664 manntall for him; the 1664 lists put him on Aaberg (perhaps a part or neighbour of Bø then taxed separately; not resolved). The census names Trond as his son (sons column, patronymic, same farm), which is the naming record for his link to Trond (see his page); but the tree's Trond enters as father of Marit Tronsdatter (b. about 1680) at Bø, so the step from the 1664 boy to the Bø farmer spans about 16 years and a farm-name difference. Grade kept Probable. ¶3a
