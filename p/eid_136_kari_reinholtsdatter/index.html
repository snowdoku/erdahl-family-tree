Life story

Married soldier Tosten Erichsen on 16 June 1765 ('Kari Reinholts.'); mother of Erich (bapt. 13 Jul 1766, 'Kari Reinholts D.') and Olle (bapt. 5 Jun 1770, 'Karen Reinholdsd.'), both of Uglum (Selbu ministerialbok 16311). Her father was evidently a Reinholt, whose name her son Reinal/Reinholdt carried. Mother of Erik Torstensen (Probable). Baptism and death not yet searched. ¶1

The name Reinholt is rare in Selbu: the 1701 manntall lists 'Reinholdt' (19, b. c.1682), son of Lieutenant Ulrich[?] Holst (56) at Over Horstad (canvas 116). Too old to be her father unless she was born late in his life; recorded as a weak lead only. ¶2

Widowed 11 Jun 1797 (Torsten's burial). New lead for her father: 'Niels Reinholdsen Gudset', died 3 Jul 1797 aged 66 (b. c.1731), and a 'Giertrud Reinholdtsdatter Røsset' sponsor in 1797 (16311 c202-213) - children of a Reinholt at Gudset or Røsset about 1730, the right generation to be Kari's siblings. Unproved; next: 16310 baptisms c.1729-37 (canvases 115-147) for children of a Reinholt. ¶5

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Speculative father: Reinold Olsen Kleiset, baptised 1716 (16310 c62 L) - the rare forename Reinholt clusters at Kleiset (an elder Reinol Kleiset d. 1717; Reinhol- names at Kleset in 1801). Name-only; see Reinold (Reinholt) Olsen Kleiset. A 'Reinholt Be[..]' stood godfather at Ola Jørnsen's baptism in 1717 (c69 L). ¶3

Upgrade (regrade 3): Probable to Confirmed as mother of Erik Torstensen (see his page); graded on her link to Erik Torstensen. Naming record: the Selbu baptism of 13 Jul 1766 (16311 c20 L) names the parents 'Torsten Erichs. Uglum, Kari Reinholts D.', so it names her as Erich's mother directly (no stepmother question). Continuity as for her husband Torsten Eriksen: the 1783 confirmation 'Erich Torstens. Uglum' (c110 R), Erik Torstensen of Uglem in 1796, and the 1801 Uglan household where his brother Ole (bapt. 1770 to Tosten Erichs. and Karen Reinholdsd.) lives with him; all on Uglem, no named rival. ¶10a

The case for this identification 1

Re-read at zoom: the 2nd Sunday after Trinity 1765 marriage line (16311 c18 L) reads '2.) Sold: Tosten Erichs: Kari Reinholtsd:'. The clerk wrote no farm for either party, so the marriage entry cannot give her home farm. Her father is still unknown. ¶9

Corrections 1

Correction: the Speculative father Reinold Olsen Kleiset (b. 1716) is withdrawn. The probate after his father, 'Kleset, Selbu, 31/7-1728: Ole Reinelsen. Enke: Mali Arntsdtr. Barn: Reinel Olsen, 6 uker; Ingeborg Olsdtr., 6 år; Ragnild Olsdtr., 3 år. Boet fallitt' (card 77804 c1011; read by the orchestrator), does not list the Reinold of 1716, so he had died, and the newborn Reinel of 1728 is too young to be the father of a woman married in 1765. Her father is unknown. Remaining leads: the forename recurs at Kleset (Reinel Olsen b. 1728, Reinholt Pederssen b. 1770) and Sæther (Reinolt Tomassen 1666); an online-tree title (unverified) names a 'Reinholt Kaspersen c.1715-1806'. ¶4a

Searches that found nothing 2

Negative (overnight 1762 frontier pass): the 1762 extra tax list for Selbu (Rentekammeret, Ekstraskatt 1762, Strinda og Selbu, DA 49639) was read for a Reinholt as householder on the farms where her possible siblings lived about 1797. Gulsæt (Gudset) 678, canvas 155: two households, Anders [?] and [Asborg?] Østensdatter with married son Østen, and Ole Østensen and Marit Pedersdatter with married son Østen (wife Anne Gundersdatter); no Reinholt. Røsæt (Røsset) 767 and 768, canvas 140: Ole Thomassen and [Sophie?] Olsdatter with children Thomas, Ole and Ingebor; Ole Olsen, widower; no Reinholt. Earlier readers found none at Klegset (canvases 152-153) or Uglem (133-134) either. Still unread for this purpose: Gulsæt 730 (canvas 156) and the servant columns of the other canvases, where she would appear with her patronymic if she was in service in 1762 (aged 15 or more). ¶7

Negative (scan read): the 1762 extra tax list for Selbu (DA 49639) canvas 156, p. 164, has no Kari Reinholtsdatter and no Reinholt. 730 Gulsæt (two households: Niels Arnesen? and Siri Halvorsdatter? with Birit Siursdatter in the others column; the widow Ragnild Jonsdatter with son Ole and cottagers Jon Siursen? and Ingebor Jonsdatter), 731 Aftræd (maid Kirsti Olsdatter), 689 Aftræd (maids Helga Arntsdatter, Siri Olsdatter) and 696 Kjesnes. The transcribed servant columns of the other Selbu canvases (127-166:) contain no Reinholtsdatter either; the only Karis are Olsdatter, Haagensdatter?, Torstensdatter, Biørnsdatter, Ellingsdatter?, Pedersdatter, Siursdatter?, Torkelsdatter, Jonsdatter and an unread widow. So in 1762 she was either under 12 (born after Sep 1750), living outside Selbu, or entered under an unread patronymic. Her father remains unknown. ¶8
