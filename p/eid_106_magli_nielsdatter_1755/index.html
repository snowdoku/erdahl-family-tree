Life story

1801 census: 'Magli Nielsdatter', 45, 'Hans kone' (his wife) of Jon Olssen, 42, at Stoche (Stokke) farm 0069, household 03, Selbu. The marital-status phrase 'Hans 1te, haendes 3die aegteskab' makes this his first marriage and her third. Listed children 'Deres born': Angneta Jonsdatter 10, Karen Jonsdatter 3 and Elen Jonsdatter 1. Also in the household: servants Ole Pederssen 16 and Ane Andersdatter 45, and a widow Kiersten Andersdatter 77. ¶1

Death and burial: 'Maglie Nielsd.', 'Konen' (the wife), residence Stokke, aged 52, died 6 December and buried the 3rd Sunday in Advent 1807, Selbu burials 1807, p. 98 serial 1 (index; scan not read). Age 52 gives birth about 1755, against 45 in Feb 1801 (birth about 1755-56). ¶2

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the grade

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Rated Probable as the first wife of John Olsen Stokke (see his page): the burial names no husband, but she is the only Magli Nielsdatter at Stokke, she died a wife at Stokke seven months before 'Enkemand' (widower) Jon Olsen Stokke remarried on 3 July 1808, and her husband in 1801 was the younger Jon Olssen (b. ~1758-59), the one whose age fits John's 1825 census and 1827 burial. Reading the 1807 burial scan for a husband's name would confirm it. Her two earlier husbands are not identified. ¶3
