Life story

1801 census: Ingeborg Giertsdatter, 10, in the household of the cottager with a little land (huusmand med lidt jord) Giest Giestsen, 40, and his wife Christi Anfindsdatter, 38, at Esse, with Anfind 7 and Anna 4. That gives a birth about 1790-91. She is not in her parents' household in 1815. ¶2

Name: written 'Ingeborg Gjestsdr.' (1820), 'Ingebor Gjestsd.' (1826, 1832) and 'Ingeborg Giertsdatter' (1801 census); Gjest and Giert are forms of the same name here. She was the mother of Ole Andersen 'Flaten' and the wife of Anders Olsen Esse. ¶1

Marriage: 16 January 1826, Leikanger (1826 no. 2), to the bachelor Anders Olsen of Esse, 40. She is entered as 'Pige' (unmarried woman), of Esse, aged 30 (understated; the 1801 census gives about 35), father Gjest Esse. ¶4

Searches that found nothing 3

Death: not found. She may be the woman buried 1 June 1846 as 'Ingeborg Monsdatter, Husmandskone', 56, of Esse (see Ingeborg Monsdatter). That is unproved. A search of the Leikanger and Balestrand index for any Ingeborg born 1786-1798 in 1846-1880 found only that burial. ¶5

Baptism search: her parents married at Tjugum on 11 Oct 1790 (Leikanger 11567, fol. 199). Every baptism in 11567 from the wedding to the end of the book (canvases 203-229, Oct 1790 to 26 Nov 1791) was read and none is hers, so she was probably born in Dec 1791 or 1792, which still fits '10' in Feb 1801. ¶6a

Baptism search continued: Leikanger ministerialbok 11568 begins in early March 1792 (canvas 1 right); its baptisms from March to August 1792 (canvases 1-6) have no Ingebor. 11567 ends on 26 Nov 1791, so no baptism register survives for about 27 Nov 1791 to early Mar 1792. Her baptism probably fell in that gap, which fits '10' in Feb 1801 (born late 1790 or 1791). Grade unchanged. ¶7
