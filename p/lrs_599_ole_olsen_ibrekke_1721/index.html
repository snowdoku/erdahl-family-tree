Life story

Marriage (probable): see his widow's notes. The 1756 betrothal of the bachelor Ole Olsen and Ingerbor Tor[c]elsdatter in Nissedal (7981 c108R; wedding 11 Apr 1757) probably belongs to this couple, since the probate's spelling 'Torchels' explains why that entry has no k. ¶7

Land register checked: panteregister I 2 (10916, p. 782, view 131 left), 'Mat. No 11 Ibrække i Skattelag med Øvre Tvetten', has entries from 1763 to 1803 (Ole Aadnesen 1780; Jon Tallefsen and Rannei Joensdatter; skifte entries of 1790 and 1803), but no deed to Ole Olsen from a father and no odel claim naming his parents. Together with I 1 fol. 126 (he bought from Niels Claussen in 1771 and Sigur Jonsen's odel right in 1772), the land registers suggest he bought into Ibrække rather than inherited it, so his parents should be sought elsewhere (his 1756/57 marriage entry names no farm). ¶8

Burial: in the Kviteseid ministerialbok 1787-1799 (source 7983, canvas 231, left page) a group heading reads 'Ved Nisseedals Kirke i April og Maj Maaneder kast Jord paa efterskrevne, der kort efter hinanden ere bortdöde af den der grasserende Flüs-Febr[?]' (at Nissedal church in April and May [1790] earth was cast on those listed below, who died one shortly after another of the fever then raging). The group includes 'Item Ole Olsen Ibræke ag: 69 Aar' (Ole Olsen of Ibrække, aged 69). The second digit of the age looks like 9; 6 is possible. Birth ABT 1721 is inferred from the age. ¶1

Probate (skifte) No 31, Øvre Telemark vestfjelske sorenskriveri skifteprotokoll 1790-94 (Digitalarkivet source 25679, view 84 right page): on 7 Jun 1790 the estate was registered and valued at the farm Ibreche (Ibrække), matrikkel no. 11, in Nissedal annex of Hvideseid (Kviteseid) prestegjeld, with the lagrettemenn (court jurors) Targe Halvorsen Grimstvedt and John Halvorsen Grimstvedt as appraisers, after the man Ole Olsen, who died on 27 April 1790. The heirs are his surviving widow Ingeborg Torchels Datter (Torkelsdatter) and the one child born to them in the marriage, a son Ole Olsen, then 22 years old (the age figure is cramped; 18 is a less likely reading), whose curator (guardian for a minor under 25) was John Halvorsen. Targe Halvorsen Grimstvedt was appointed the widow's lauverge (legal guardian). The final settlement, headed 'No 31, Anno 1790 den 1[2?]te Julii', was held at the sorenskriver's farm Nordre Sundbø in Flåbygda, Kviteseid. Crop:. ¶5

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed. The 1790 probate after Ole Olsen of Ibrække names his widow Ingeborg Torkelsdatter and their only son Ole Olsen, aged about 22. That matches the 1801 census household at Ibræke, where Ole Olsen, 33, has his widowed mother Ingebor Torkelsdatter, 68, living with him. The death date of 27 Apr 1790 also agrees with the burial of 'Ole Olsen Ibræke, 69' among the Nissedal burials of April-May 1790 (7983 c231L). The probate names no other children, so Ole Olsen b. about 1768 was the couple's only surviving child in 1790. ¶6

The case for this identification 1

Identification as the father of Ole Olsen of Ibrække (see his page) is Probable, not Confirmed. For: the son carries the patronymic Olsen and was 'Ole Olsen Ibrække', a bachelor, at his betrothal on 10 Apr 1791, eleven months after this burial; in 1801 the son's mother Ingebor Torkelsdatter lived with him at Ibrække as 'Enke efter 1te egteskab' (widow after her first marriage), so her only husband was an Ole who died before 1801. Lrs_525_ found no other Ole Olsen of Ibrække in the burial index for 1785-1814, and read every Nissedal burial in 7983 from Jan 1789 to Sep 1790 and found no other adult Ole of Ibrække. ¶2a

Records that disagree 1

Against: no record read names this Ole as Ingebor's husband or as the younger Ole's father. His probate (skifte) would settle it; see the next note. ¶2b

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Not to be confused with Ole Torsen of Bræke (aged 42) and Ole Aanundsen of Tvetene (42), both buried at Nissedal in the same weeks of 1790, nor with 'Ole Olsen Tvetene', father of an Ole baptised 10 Jul 1768 (not linked). ¶4

Searches that found nothing 1

Not found yet: his probate. The Vest-Telemark (Øvre Telemark vestfjelske) sorenskriveri probate protocol for 1790-1794 is Digitalarkivet source 25679 (scan only, no index; the protocol begins in Feb 1790 and has no register at its front). The skiftekort series (source 6856) has no cards for 1734-1809. Marriage: a betrothal of the bachelor Ole Olsen and the maiden 'Ingerbor Tor[..]sdatter' in Nissedal, about 17 Oct 1756, married 11 Apr 1757 (Kviteseid 7981 c108R), is a candidate only: the patronymic reads more like Toralds than Torkels. ¶3
