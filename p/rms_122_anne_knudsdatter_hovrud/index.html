Life story

Bygdebok (Svenes vol., gnr. 76 Hovrud, canvas 643): Anne Knutsdotter, 1655-1713, of gnr. 111, wife of Jon Eivindsson (d. 1699) and from 1700 of Erik Embriksson; mother of Eivind (Even) Jonsson, to whom his stepfather Erik gave the farm by deed in 1717; a date '14.2.1713' stands with her (her skifte). ¶1

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

The case for this identification 1

Primary support already in the tree: the 1728 Hovrud probate (DA 25285 fol. 914b-915a) recites a 'Skiftebrev' of 14 February 17[1/2]3 'efter [Enke]mandens [?] Moder Ane Knudsdaatter', the widower being Even Jonsen. Read with the bygdebok's 14.2.1713, that names Ane Knudsdatter as Even's mother. Kept Probable because the reading of the word before 'Moder' and of the year was flagged uncertain. ¶2

Searches that found nothing 1

Search note: Digitalarkivet's probate series for the district runs Hadeland, Land og Valdres skifteprotokoll no. 4, 1704-1709 (DA 25284), then no. 5, 1716-1737 (DA 25285); no sorenskriver probate protocol for 1709-1716 is scanned, and the prosti series (DA 35450) starts in 1716. So the 'uncarded 1708-1716 protocol' does not exist online, and a probate after Trond Høljesen (about 1712) or Anne Knudsdatter (about 1713) cannot be read there. The record that could still test these links is the mortgage book (pantebok) for Hadeland, Land og Valdres 1704-1715 (DA 10262), for a deed or division among the heirs, read page by page (not indexed). ¶5

Open questions and the next record to read 3

Test record: the skifte of 14 Feb 1713 after Ane Knudsdatter, Hovrud (Hadeland, Land og Valdres skifteprotokoll covering 1713), and the 1717 deed Erik Embriksen to stepson Even Jonsen. ¶4

Lead only: in the vicar's census of 5 Nov 1664 (DA 35504, p. 22, view 20) Hovrud ('Hoffvords-Rud, iij meler korn') has no resident holder and is 'Brugis aff Knud Næß', worked by Knud of Nes. A Knud farming Hovrud in 1664 is a candidate father for Anne Knudsdatter (b. about 1655), who later held Hovrud with Jon Eivindsen; the bygdebok makes her 'of gnr. 111' (farm name not recovered). Speculative; not added. The 1664 lists do not name daughters, so they cannot test this. ¶6a

Test record: a deed or tingbok entry naming her father (e.g. Jon Eivindsen's acquisition of Hovrud), or the skifte after her of 14 Feb 1713 if found. ¶6b
