Life story

Patronymic evidence: Leikanger 11566, 15 Nov 1760: 'Bapt: Einer Paulsøn Esse', godparents Ole Bottelsøn Esse, Ole Olsøn ibid., Ole Hermundsøn ibid.; Mari Hermunds D. Esse, Kari Hans D. Balle. Buried at Tjugum on 6 Jan 1761 as 'Einer Paulson Esse', an infant. Paul Olsen of Esse (her first husband, d. 1761/62) is the only Paul at Esse in these years (he is godfather 'Paul Olsøn Esse' on the same page), and his mother Mari Hermundsdatter stands godmother. A son named Einer, a name not found in Paul's paternal line (Ole Povelsen, Povel Hansen), supports the 1762 reading 'Sigri Ein[er]s datter' against the 1803 'Olsdatter': the boy would be named for her father Einar. Not proof; her father is still unidentified. Ruled out as kin: the probate of a Sigri Einersdtr at Berge Øvre 1707-1719 (index pa00000000520303, 2b p. 516b) is in Klævoll tinglag, a different Berge. ¶8

Probate: Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll A 7b, fol. 589b, No. 29 (24-25 May 1762). She is the widow of Pouel Olsen of Ytre Esse and mother of his children Ole Pouelsen and Ingebor Paulsdatter. Her patronymic begins clearly 'Ein-' and most probably reads 'Einers datter' (Einarsdatter), but the ending is blurred, so it is kept out of the name field. ¶1

Probably the 'Sigri Berge' whose probate on the farm Berge closed 13 April 1803: the 1804 probate of Peder Marchussen Tendingen (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll A 12, fol. 320b-321a, No. 46) calls Sigri Berge the 'Farmoder' (paternal grandmother) of Anders Olsen Esse, son of Ole Paulsen Esse. If so she was alive after 1762, lived at Berge, and probably remarried. Her own 1803 probate (Ytre Sogn A 12) would name her heirs and settle it. ¶2

Probate: 'Skifte No. 9 efter Qvinden Sigri Olsdatter Berge i Tjugums Skibrede', begun 12 and closed 13 April 1803 on the farm Berge (matr. no. 11), where her surviving husband Hermund Olsen was occupier (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll A 12, fol. 161b-163a, No. 9). She had been married three times: (1) to 'hendes forhen længst afdøde Mand Paal Olsen' (her long-dead husband Paal Olsen), by whom she had a son and a daughter; (2) to the late Lars Andersen, by whom she had a daughter Mare (Mari) Larsdatter, married to Ole Hermundsen, occupier of Distad; (3) to the surviving Hermund Olsen Berge, by whom she had a daughter Brithe Hermundsdatter, married to Hans Andersen, occupier at Berge. ¶4

Probate 1803, first-marriage heirs: a) the dead son Ole Poulsen, who lived and died at Esse in Tjugum skipreide, married to the surviving Ingerie Marchusdatter of Esse, represented by his six children Paal Olsen 25 (married, occupier of Ytre Esse), Marchus Olsen 20, Anders Olsen 16, Sigrid Olsdatter 22, Ingeborg Olsdatter 15 and Aase Olsdatter 10; b) the dead daughter Ingeborg Paalsdatter, who lived and died at Hjaastad[?] (reading uncertain), married to the surviving Sjur Hil[?]lougsen, tenant there, represented by her daughter Synneve Sjursdatter, married to Ole Larsen, occupier of Schaasæn[?] in Kvamsøy skipreide. The granddaughters' shares are called 'Farmoderarv' (inheritance from the paternal grandmother) and were paid partly in land rent in the farm Berge. ¶5

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

She is Probable as an ancestor because the link from Paul Olsen to Ole Paulsen Esse is Probable. Her granddaughter Sigrid Olsdatter bears her name. ¶3

Confidence moved Probable to Confirmed: her 1803 probate names her first husband Paal Olsen and their children Ole Poulsen of Esse (husband of Ingerie Marchusdatter, with the same six children as in his 1799/1800 probate and the 1801 census) and Ingeborg Paalsdatter, which matches Paul Olsen's 1762 probate (widow Sigri, children Ole and Ingebor). It is the 'Sigri Berge' skifte closed 13 April 1803 cited in Peder Marchussen's 1804 probate. ¶6

Records that disagree 1

Patronymic conflict: the 1803 probate writes her name clearly as 'Sigri Olsdatter' (margin heading and text), while the 1762 probate of her first husband reads 'Sigri Ein[er]s datter' (ending blurred). The same woman is meant in both (same husband and children). Which patronymic is right is unresolved; a Leikanger/Tjugum marriage or baptism record, or a burial entry about February-April 1803, could settle it. The name field is left as 'Sigri'. ¶7
