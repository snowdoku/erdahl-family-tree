About the family

Children named in the probate: Gunder Johnsen (of age), Peder Johnsen (of age), Ragnild Johnsdatter (married), Brynhild Johnsdatter (married), and a son Mikkel Jonsen, dead by 1821, whose children John (15), Ingeborg (12) and Sigrid inherited his share. The 1825 census of Moen, with Marit Jørgensdatter, 46, John Michelsen, 19, and Siri Michelsdatter, 11, is that son's widow and children. The sons-in-law are not named. Her parents (the Gunder of her patronymic) are not named in the probate. ¶7

Life story

Baptism of her daughter: 22nd Sunday after Trinity (8 November) 1789, 'Döbt Brynil. Par: Jon Mich: Moen Segri Gund:' - Brynil, parents Jon Michelsen of Moen and Segri (Sigrid, Siri) Gundersdatter; godparents Jon Evjen, N. Rinde, Geertrud of the same place, Ane Moen and a Ragn[hild] (16311 canvas 134 right; crop). Brynil is Brynild Jonsdatter, 11, of the 1801 household. ¶2

1801 census: Selbu, rural residence 0018 Moen, household 01 - 'Siri Gundersdatter, 44, Hans kone, Begge i 1te ægteskab' - wife of the householder Jon Mikkelssen, both in their first marriage; the six children in the household, including Gunder Jonssen, 15, are entered as 'Deres børn' (their children). Her age gives a birth about 1757. (Read.) ¶1

Burial: 'Siri Gundersd Moen', aged 65, died 11 March 1821, buried 18 March 1821 - Selbu burials 1821 no. 12, indexed twice, in the 1816-1824 book (pp. 210-211, pg00000007058834) and in the 1816-1831 book (pp. 220-221, 'Siri Gundersdatt.', pg00000007095785), with the same date, age and number; one burial entered in two registers. Index only; the scan has not been read. Taken to be hers: the name and the farm Moen agree, age 65 gives a birth about 1756 against about 1757 from her 1801 age of 44, and she is absent from the Moen household of the 1825 census, where her husband was still living. ¶4

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed. Her probate: Strinda og Selbu skifteprotokoll 10b (Digitalarkivet 24744), image 235, fol. 778, 'Skifte paa Gaarden Moen i Selbo Thinglaug, Matr. No 787, efter Kaarkone Sigrid Gundersdatter'; registration and valuation 6 April 1821, settled 5 October 1821. The heirs are: a) 'Enkemanden John Mikkelsen' (the widower); b) 'Sønnesøn John Mikkelsen, 15 Aar'; c) 'Sønnedatter Ingeborg Mikkelsdatter, 12'; d) [Sønnedatter] Sigrid; e) 'Sønnen Gunder Johnsen, myndig' (of age); f) [Sønnen] Peder [Johnsen], of age; g) 'Datteren Ragnild Johnsdatter, gift' (married); h) [Datteren] Brynhild [Johnsdatter], married. The guardian (Værge) for the minors was 'Gunder Johnsen Eidum'. This is a primary record naming Sigrid (Siri) Gundersdatter, wife of John Mikkelsen of Moen, as the mother of Gunder Johnsen, and the guardian's farm, Eidem, ties that son to Gunder Johnsen Eidem (see his page). It settles the conflict with the 1786 baptism that names the mother 'Elen Gund.': that entry is a clerk's slip, and Siri is his mother. ¶6

Records that disagree 1

Mother of Gunder Johnsen Eidem (see his page), rated Probable, against a conflicting baptism. Gunder's own baptism on 24 September 1786 reads plainly 'Par: Jon Mich: Moen, Elen Gund:' - mother Elen Gundersdatter (16311 canvas 123 right; crop). Siri is preferred because: (1) the 1801 census calls Gunder the child of Jon and Siri and states that both were in their FIRST marriage, so Jon had no earlier wife; (2) their eldest child Rangnil was born about 1779, so Siri was Jon's wife before and after 1786, and in 1789 the same register names her, 'Segri Gund.', as mother of the next child; (3) in 1801 Elen Gundersdatter, 54, was the wife of Ole Olssen, husmann on the same farm (Moen household 02), also in her first marriage - and the first godparent at Gunder's baptism is 'O. Moen', very probably that Ole - so the clerk could easily have written the godfather's wife's name for the mother; (4) the baptism carries no mark of illegitimacy, which a child of Jon by another man's wife would carry; (5) Gunder named his third daughter Siri (1819) and no daughter Elen. That Siri and Elen were both Gundersdatter, perhaps sisters, would make the slip easier still (inference only). ¶3a

Searches that found nothing 1

Baptism not found, 1757-1759: every baptism in Selbu ministerialbok 1757-1801 (source 16311) from 1 January 1757 to the end of December 1759, Selbu and Tydal, was read for a Siri or Sigri with a father Gunder or Gunnar (January-May 1757; 22 May 1757 - December 1759). None was found. The only Siris baptised were daughters of Ole Jørgensen and Marit Persdatter (Invocavit 1757), Guttorm Estensen Langlie and Mali Haldorsdatter (3rd Sunday in Advent 1757), Ole Bersvendsen Brafen[?] and Agnis Persdatter (13 January 1758) and Halvar Persen Solum and Marit Olsdatter (Laetare 1758). Her burial age points to a birth about 1755-56, before this book begins; the earlier Selbu register is the place to look. ¶5
