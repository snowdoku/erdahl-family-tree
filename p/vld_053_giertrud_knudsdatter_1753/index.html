About the family

Mother of Ole (Probable, inferred): the 13 Oct 1782 baptism of Ole names no mother (scan read). Gertrud Knudsdatter of Melset was churched (introdusert) on 10 Nov 1782, four weeks later, with Jon Arnesen of Melset as husband (index rows, not read on the scan). She was also churched at Mælset on the day of the Jan 1791 baptism of Birgte Eline, daughter of Jon of Mælset (index), and was Jon's wife in 1801, when the census gives both as in their 1st marriage (index). She is therefore very likely Ole's mother, but this is an inference: no record names her as his mother. A probate after her (1815) or after Jon (1818) naming the children would settle it. ¶2

Parentage: Giertrud was the daughter of Knud Olsen and Brithe Larsdatter of Store Ringdal, Sunnylven (Probable). Why this is our Giertrud: (1) Gertru Knudsdtr. of Rindalen (=Ringdal) married Joen Arnesen of Løvigen at Sunnylven on 7 Apr 1776 (index record read). (2) On 9 Nov 1777 Knud, son of Joen Arnesen and Gertru Knudsdtr. of Rindal, was baptised in Norddal parish. The godparents were Morten Persen Gierde, Lars Knudsen Rindal, Niels Arnesen Tryggestad, Marthe Knudsdtr. Rindal and Olave Knudsdtr. Rindal. Lars, Marthe and Olava are three of the siblings that the 1759 probate names alongside Giertrud. (3) Knud Jonsen of Melset, Ørskog, was confirmed in 1795 aged 17 and was 23 in the 1801 census, in the household of Jon Arnesen and Giertrud Knudsdatter of Melset, both in their 1st marriage. His age fits the 1777 Knud. Their next son Ole was baptised at Ørskog in 1782. (4) Giertrud Knutsdtr. of Ringdal was confirmed at Geiranger in 1770 aged 16. The ages vary: 8 in 1759, 16 in 1770, 48 in 1801 and 'b. 1750' at her 1815 burial, which points to about 1751-54. No single record names Giertrud of Melset as the daughter of Knud Olsen, so the parents are Probable: the case rests on the matching names and the godparent pattern. Her own grade, as Ole's mother, is unchanged. Her marriage was not at Ørskog, which is why earlier searches there failed. She married at Sunnylven in 1776, and the couple lived at Rindal until at least Nov 1777 and moved to Melset by 1782. ¶12

Life story

Baptism (index record read): baptised at Sunnylven on 12 Apr 1751 as 'Giertrud', daughter of Knud Olsen of Ringdalen (1524P p. 164 no. 296; pd00000009561840); godparents Arne Fivelstad, Gammel Øyene and Marta Lille Ringdalen. This matches her age 8 in her mother's probate of 26 Jul 1759. Her later stated ages (16 at confirmation in 1770, 48 in 1801) run two or three years young. A second Sunnylven Giertrud, daughter of Knud of Molschredal, baptised 17 Nov 1754 (pd00000009562258), is a rival only by name: the 1776 bride was of Rindalen, and her 1777 godparents were the Ringdal siblings Lars, Marthe and Olave Knudsdatter. Her own grade (as Ole's mother) is unchanged; the link to her parents stays Probable because the step from Rindal (Nov 1777) to Melset, Ørskog (1782) is a change of farm and parish. ¶13

1801 census (index): wife of Jon Arnesen, Melseth, Ørskog, household 03, age 48; children Knud 23, Ole 18, Birgte 10 in the household. ¶1

Burial (index row only): Giertrud Knudsd., gaardmands konen (the farmer's wife), of Mælseth, buried 1815 at Ørskog; birth year given in the index as 1750. Day not indexed; record not opened. ¶3

Probate register (scan read): the Sunnmøre probate index has 'John Arnesen, Melset, 1815, etter hustru Gjertrud Knutsd.' (pa00000000791888), registered 22 Jul 1815 at Nordre Sunnmøre 3A 10 fol. 169a. The entry is a copy of a royal licence (bevilling) allowing Jon Arnesen Mælsæt to keep the estate after 'hans afdøde Hustru Giertrud Knudsdatter' undivided (uskiftet) with their joint children. It confirms that she was Jon Arnesen's wife and had died by July 1815, but it names none of the children, so it does not prove the link to Ole Johnsen Røssevold. The division between the children must be the 1818 probate (3A 12 fol. 5a), whose heir list earlier workers found illegible. ¶11

Probate 1818 (partly read): the 5 June 1818 skifte at Mælsæth naming farmer John Arnesen also names a 'Giertrud', apparently with a date that may read 22 July 1814 and the word 'afdøde' (deceased) nearby. If that reading holds, she may have died in 1814 rather than 1815. This is not established; the passage needs a closer reading. ¶5

Probate 1818, closer reading: the opening of the 5 Jun 1818 skifte at Mælsæth (skifteprotokoll 3A 12, fol. 5a) appears to name 'gaardmand John Arnesen' together with his 'før[?] afdøde Kone Giertrud Knudsd[atte]r[?]' (his previously deceased wife), which, if the reading holds, makes her Jon Arnesen's late wife by June 1818. The continuation mentions 'Skifteretten den 22 Julii 1814' and 'afdøde... Kone Giertrud' together; this may be an earlier court sitting about her estate, which would put her death before 22 Jul 1814 and conflict with the indexed 1815 burial, but the grammar is not legible and the 1815 date is kept. No passage naming her as Ole's mother was read. ¶6

Ruled-out baptism (index): Ørskog 26 May 1750 (pd00000010637033), Giertrud, father Gullik of Melset (not Knud), so not this Giertrud Knudsdatter despite the Melset residence. Her baptism is still unfound. ¶9

Probate 1818, third reading: the 5 Jun 1818 entry at Mælsæth (Skifteprotokoll 6, 3A 12, s. 5a-5b, viewer 24602 images 7-8) names 'hans [John Arnesen's] forhen afdøde Kone Giertrud Knudsd[atter]' and refers to the court's decision of 22 Jul 1814 about her estate. It is a ruling on debts, not a division: no heirs list. 'Ole Johnsen Rødsevold' signs at the close with Knud Jakobsen Mælsæth and others. This supports, but does not prove, that Ole Johnsen Røssevold was her son; grade unchanged (Probable, graded on her link to Ole). ¶15

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

Corrections 1

Correction (probate scan read): the Jon Arnesen of Westre (Vestre), Ørskog, who fathered Arne (baptised 4 Apr 1778, Westre) and the 1784 Westre daughter indexed as Maren is a different man. He was dead by 28 Oct 1788, when the Sunnmøre probate court held a skifte (estate division) at Westre after 'afg[angne] Jon Arnesen' between the widow Inger Pauls d[atte]r and the children '1 Søn Arne Jonsen Westre gl. 11[?] Aar' and '2. Datter Martha Giertrue Jonsd[atte]r 3½[?] Aar' (guardian Arne Arnesen Waldermoe; ages faint). His widow's name matches the bride of the Borgund marriage of 22 Jun 1777 (Joen Arnesen, ungkarl, of Westre, and Inger Povelsdtr. of Lille Fylling), so that marriage is his too. Jon Arnesen of Melset was alive in the 1801 census and buried in 1818, with wife Giertrud Knudsdatter, so Arne 1778, the 1784 daughter and the 1777 Borgund marriage do not belong to the Melset couple. The daughter 'Martha Giertrud' is probably the child indexed as 'Maren' in 1784, but this is not proved. ¶8

Alternatives considered and set aside 2

Marriage (unlinked lead): an Ørskog marriage indexed for 1777/1778 (pv00000002198672) has a bride Gertrud Knudsdatter, pige (maid), with the groom's given name not transcribed and residence Moldvær. Not shown to be this couple. ¶4

Ruled out (index, record page read): the Ørskog marriage of 1 Jun 1777 (engagement 20 Apr 1777; Ørskog 1773-1809, 1527P, p. 49, serial 2) of Gertrud Gullichsd., Pige, was to Haldkield Gregoris[sen], K.S., of Melset (trulovar Ole Ch. and Helge, both of Melset), not to Jon Arnesen. That Gertrud Gullichsdatter of Melset is therefore a different woman, and the 26 May 1750 Ørskog baptism of a Giertrud with father Gullik of Melset (already set aside above) belongs to her, not to this Giertrud Knudsdatter. Do not retry that candidate. ¶10

Searches that found nothing 2

Probate search 1814 (negative): the sitting headings on images 205-210 of Nordre Sunnmøre skifteprotokoll 3 (1809-1814, s. 837b-843a), where a 22 Jul 1814 sitting at Mælsæth had been expected, were read on the scan. They are Borgund, Valde and other skipreide sittings dated 1811-1814, and none names Mælsæth, Ørskog, John Arnesen or Giertrud. The volume is not kept in date order, so the sitting may lie elsewhere in it (images 1-204, 211-223) or in a separate registration book. No heirs list was read; no change. ¶7

Negative: the Sunnylven churching of 1751 and the Melset 1818 probate were not productive this pass; her paternal line now runs Knud Olsen (b. about 1720, bur. Sunnylven 1786) - Ole Knudsen of Fivelstad (Probable) - Knut Olsen of Fivelstad (d. by 1703). Her own grade is unchanged. ¶14
