Life story

Born about 1872, PEI; daughter in her father's household in 1881 (as Mary) and 1891 (as Mary Jane). ¶1

Possible directory entry, about 1925–28: The Cummins Atlas rural directory for Lots 63 and 64 lists "McKenzie, Daniel. P.O. Hopefield R.R. 2." with his wife given as "Catherine Hater" and children Robert, James, Grace, [An]nie, John and George. Lauretta Brooks recorded the death of "Mrs Dan MacKenzie" as "Mary Jane Hayter", so this is probably the same couple, with the wife's first name misgiven in the directory. Unconfirmed. ¶4

Marriage and death: 'MacKenzie, Mrs Dan - Mary Jane Hayter - 15 September 1946, Iris' (death list in the diary of Lauretta Brooks of Murray Harbour). ¶2

Marriage and death: She became Mrs. Dan MacKenzie. Lauretta Brooks's list of local deaths records 'MacKenzie, Mrs Dan | | 15-Sep-46 | Iris | Mary Jane Hayter'. Iris is a settlement in Lot 62/63, near Murray River. ¶3
