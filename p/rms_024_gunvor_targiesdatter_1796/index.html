Life story

Baptized on the 6th Sunday after Easter (8 May 1796) in Treungen church as the daughter of Targie Osmundsen Hiemdal. Her godparents were Torbør Halvorsdatter Hiemdal, Gundbiør Osmundsdatter, Anne Egilsdatter Sundet, Ole Jensen(?) Almlien and Egil Olsen Kaase (Kviteseid ministerialbok 1787-1799, images 106-107). The mother is not named in the entry; Birgit Targiesdatter was Targie's wife from 1791 until her burial in Feb 1799. ¶1

In the 1801 census at Hiemdal she is Gunvor Targiesdatter, 4, one of 'hans børn' (his children) of Targie Aasmundsen. ¶2
