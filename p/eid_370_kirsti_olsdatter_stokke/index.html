About the family

Widow of Joen Gundersen of Stokkan, Selbu, at his probate on 22 May 1693 (24749 fol. 108a; card 77804 c1885): the skifte was held 'imellem hans efterleffuende Hustru Kirsti Ols Daatter paa den ene og deris barn Ingeborre Joensdatter paa den anden side, Myndig'. 'Deris barn' names her as the mother (audit 8 rule). The bygdebok (vol. 6, canvas 19) gives her as Kirsti, d. 1719 (ABT; book only). ¶1

Life story

Ingeborg Jonsdatter's burial is found: 'Ingebor Jonsd: Stockun 82 aar gl.', Sexagesima 1759 (16311 c7 R). It agrees with the 1693 'deris barn Ingeborre Joensdatter' by patronymic and farm only, so the link to Kirsti stays Probable. Test record (open): the probate after Ola Bjørnsen Stokke (about 1738; not in 77804) or after Ingeborg (1759, Stjør- og Verdal 24753-24754 typed registers, under Stokkan/Stochen). ¶4

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the grade · 1 open question

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Graded Probable on her link to her daughter Ingeborg Jonsdatter (see her page), for the same reason as her husband: the 1693 Ingeborg Joensdatter is tied to the wife of Ola Bjørnsen at Stokke only by first name and farm (1708 churching, 16310 c35) and by the bygdebok. Ingeborg named her daughter Kirsti (b. 1701, bygdebok), which fits. ¶2

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Test record: as for her husband, the probate after Ola Bjørnsen Stokke (about 1738) or Ingeborg's 1759 burial; and a probate after Kirsti herself about 1719 (none survives: the Stjør- og Verdal protocols have a gap 1702-1726, and no Stokkan card of that period is in 77804). ¶3
