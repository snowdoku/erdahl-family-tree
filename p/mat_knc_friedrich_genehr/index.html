Life story

A baker of 816 West 8th Avenue, Vancouver, aged 40, when he married Frieda Knodel on 12 August 1938. He was the son of Ernst Reinhold Genehr and Ida Scheer. ¶1

He later used the surname Gaynor. As Fredrick Reihnhold Gaynor, a retired baker of Surrey, he died 15 October 1989, born 27 June 1898 at Munich; his wife Frieda Knodel was the informant (BC 1989-09-018639). ¶2
