About the family

Parents entered: Christoph Adam Knodel (see his page) and Maria Dorothea (see her page), both Probable, who married at Bietigheim on 12 February 1759. They are named in his 1765 birth entry and his 1833 burial entry, and his father ('Stoph Adam') in his 1791 marriage entry. His elder siblings Jacobina Sophia (1761) and Ludwig Friederich (1762) were baptised at Bietigheim. Index only; images not seen. ¶6

Life story

Birth: Christoph Ludwig Knodel was born at Vaihingen an der Enz on 6 February 1765, son of Christoph Adam Knodel and Maria Dorothea. Index entry only; image not seen; not yet queued for the signed-in lane. ¶1

Unverified: the earlier confidence note says Christoph Adam and Maria Dorothea also had children Johann Friedrich (1769) and Johann Gottlieb (1781) at Vaihingen. No index row for either is in the or dumps; the only Vaihingen rows under those names are Christoph Ludwig's own sons, Johann Gottlieb (baptised 1795/96) and Johann Friedrich (baptised 1819), so the claim may confuse generations (inferred). Treat it as unsupported until a 1769 or 1781 row or image is found. ¶7

First marriage: he married Jacobina Friederike (surname not indexed) at Vaihingen on 25 January 1791; the index names his father as 'Stoph Adam' (Christoph Adam). Children indexed: Johann Christoph (bapt. 13 Oct 1794) and Johann Gottlieb (bapt. 14 Mar, indexed 1795, probably 1796). Index only; image not seen. ¶2

Wives' surnames: the bride rows of his two Vaihingen marriages give 'Jacobina Friederika Fessels' (25 Jan 1791; father Friedrich; record 904788300) and 'Margaretha Dorothea Bonnwetsch' (1807; father Jacob Friedr.; record 904256797, image 1184563-00226). His mother's bride row of 1759 gives 'Maria Dorothea Treuzen/Creubzen', father Jacob. ¶8

Second marriage: he married Margaretha Dorothea at Vaihingen in 1807 (no day indexed). Children indexed: Johann Matthäus (bapt. 22 Jan 1809), Ludwig Friedrich (bapt. 20 Oct 1813; m. 1839 Christiane Rosine, later at Güglingen) and Johann Friedrich (bapt. 28 May 1819, buried 3 Feb 1820). Index only; image not seen. ¶3

Burial: he was buried at Vaihingen on 14 August 1833; the index names his parents Christoph Adam and Maria Dorothea and his wife Margaretha Dorothea. Age not indexed. Index only; image not seen. ¶4

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Confidence: Probable as the father of Johann Gottlieb Knodel (see his page), resting on the identification of the Friedenstal man with the Vaihingen-born man (see his notes). His place in Ludwig Knodel's ancestry inherits the Speculative link between Ludwig and Johann Gottlieb. Not the Christoph Ludwig Knodel baptised at Horrheim on 16 Dec 1778 (father Johann Erhard), who was 12 in 1791. Parents Christoph Adam x Maria Dorothea are not entered; they also had children at Bietigheim (Ludwig Friederich, 1762) and Vaihingen (Johann Friedrich 1769, Johann Gottlieb 1781). ¶5
