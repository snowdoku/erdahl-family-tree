Life story

Jon (Joen) Torjusen of Jyserø, Drangedal. Betrothed on Whit Monday, 23 May 1763, and married on the 5th Sunday after Trinity, 3 Jul 1763, to the maid Gunnild Tellefsdatter of Frostved; sureties Svenum[?] Jyserø and Halvor Grytting (Drangedal ministerialbok 1697-1767, p. 33). ¶1

Death: dead before 30 Apr 1781, when his estate at Jyserød was registered ('afgangne Mand Joen Torgiusen'; Bamble skifteprotokoll nr. 10b fol. 535a, DA 23754 view 199; card DA 7105/1190, division dated 20 Aug 1783). Heirs: widow Gunnild Tellefsdatter and four under-age daughters Ragnild 16, Mari 13, Kari 11, Torberg 6. The entry is a second primary record of his link to his daughter Ragnild Johnsdatter. The Bamble card index's Jyserød run (DA 7105 views 1183-1191, filed as 'Joklerød'/'Jysereid') has no probate of his parents: 1699 Tor Biørnsen, 1733 Aslak Torjersen (sons Halvor and Jens), 1734 Ingeborg Torjersdatter (heir her brother Aslak), 1756 Knud Jensen, 1762 Aanund Svenningsen, 1762 Ingebor Gundersdatter (wife of Svenning Aanundsen), 1766 Torbiør Knudsdatter. ¶5

Burial: 'Jon Torjusen' of Jyserøe, aged '45 aar og 3 Mnd' (b. about late 1735), buried 5 Mar 1781 at Drangedal (Drangedal ministerialbok 1768-1814, p. 278; index pg00000008753197). Remark: 'Paa sin Tilbagereyse fra Kragerøe Kiørte Han ned i Isen paa Kammerfos[s]', on his way back from Kragerø he drove through the ice at Kammerfoss, i.e. he drowned. Name and farm match, and the date fits his estate registration of 30 Apr 1781 (widow Gunnild Tellefsdatter, daughters Ragnild 16, Mari 13, Kari 11, Torberg 6). The earlier negative search covered only 1790-1830. Birth stays BEF 1742; the burial implies about 1735. ¶6
