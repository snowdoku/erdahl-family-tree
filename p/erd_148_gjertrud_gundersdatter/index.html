Lisbet Davidsdatterc. 1744 – bef. 1825Confirmed · Frøyum

About the family

Widow of David Andersen of Frøyum, Stjørdal, at his probate registered 17 Nov 1744 ('Enken Gjertrud Gundersdatt. Frøyum'), with one infant daughter, Lisbet[?] Davidsdatter, 10 weeks old (Stjør- og Verdal skifteprotokoll 3, s. 102b-103a). Read. ¶1

Parentage: the 1744 Frøyum probate after her husband David Andersen names the guardian of her infant daughter as 'Morfaderen Gunder Johans[en]' (mother's father), so her father was Gunder Johansen (see his page), probably of Hemre in Hegra. Her own grade (her link to Lisbet Davidsdatter) is unchanged at Probable. ¶4

Probable brother (inference): the dragoon Johan Gundersen of Øvre Hemre, Hegra, d. by 10 Jan 1764, whose children Gunder (13), Anders (11), Ole (9), Lisbet (8?), Ingeborg (6?) and Lanie (4) had as guardian their farbror Gunder Gundersen (skifteprotokoll 5 fol. 385a-386a). Not proved. ¶6

Her father Gunder Johansen is now placed at Øvre Hemre, Hegra, which he bought from Burgomaster Hans Hagerup by deed of 19 Dec 1737 (Stjør- og Verdal panteregister 22315, image 62, pantebok fol. 242); the 1744 probate writes him 'Morfaderen Gunder Johans. Hamre'. So she was very probably born at Hemre about 1721. Her own grade is unchanged. ¶7

Life story

Bygdebøker: Hegra gårds- og slektshistorie B. 2, Frøyum (canvas 418): 'David Andersen Frøyum, [f.] 1722, gravlagt 21/5 1744, 22 [år]. [Gift] (22/11 1743) [m.] Gjertrud Gunnarsdtr. Hembre, f. 16/1 1721, gravlagt 1786.... Johan Gunnarsen Hembre. [Born:] Lisbet, f. 1744, [g. m.] John Kleven.' Hegra B. 1 (Hembre Øvre, canvas 183) lists her among the children of Gunnar Johansen Hembre and Ingeborg (m. 13 Jul 1718): 'Gjertrud, f. 16/1 1721, g. m. David Andersen... Stjørdal'. So the bygdebok also carries her daughter Lisbet to John Kleven, the tree's link (secondary only). Her mother is now entered as Ingeborg (see her page), Probable. Her own grade (her link to Lisbet Davidsdatter) is unchanged at Probable. The baptism date 16/1 1721 was not found in 16368's Jan 1721 Hegra entries (see Ingeborg's notes). ¶8

(near-miss attempt, not raised; graded on her link to Lisbet Davidsdatter, unchanged at Probable). Remarriage searched in the Stjørdal trolovede list (16370, viewer views 263-278 = pp. 261-276, Sep 1743 to Mar 1748, scan read). No betrothal of the widow Gjertrud Gundersdatter was found. The list has a gap: p. 267 (view 269) ends on 4 Oct 1744 and p. 268 (view 270) starts with October entries whose weddings fall in Nov 1745-Mar 1746, so betrothals from about Nov 1744 to Sep 1745 are not in the list. The marriage falls in that gap. The bygdebok (Hegra B. 2, nb.no cc80d7a812edad10bfde4eaaa4cba59b canvas 418, rebuilt from the harvest he2m.json) gives the second marriage: 'Gunnar Johnsen Lien Skatval', buried 25/1 1784, 'kjøpte' the farm 23/6 1745, married '1745' 'Gjertrud Gunnarsdtr., enkja etter David'. Their children were Gjertrud (b. 27/3 1746), Ingeborg (b. 25/2 1748, m. Haftor Larsen Kvål), David (b. 17/5 1750), Ragnhild (1751), Marit (28/3 1755), Ingeborg (13/12 1763) and John Gunnarsen Frøyum (20/6 1756). This explains the 1762 ekstraskatt household at Frøyum (48401 v216): 'Gunder og H[ustru]' with Lisbeth (her daughter by David), Giertrud and Ingborg. Reading (b) in the note (Lisbeth as Gunder's own child, a rival) is therefore unlikely, but only the bygdebok says so. In the trolovede list, on 31 Jan 1748 (p. 275, view 277) Gunder Jonsen is already 'odelsbonden Gunder Jonsen Frøyum' and stands cautioner for his farmhand Svend Jensen Frøyum. The chronological register (16370, e.g. view 70 = p. 68, 1744) records weddings by the groom's name only ('Copul: Johan Gundersen Hemren') and churchings without the wife's name ('Introd: Peder Torges qv[inde]'), so neither can name Gunder's wife. The 1780 'Contract og Forening' (19755 view 227, fol. 225b-226a) was fetched again at full size: it opens 'Jeg Gunder Jonsen Frøyum... min Søn Jon Gundersen', but the body is too faint to read the wife's name. Test record (would raise her): a record that names Gunder Jonsen's wife as Gjertrud Gundersdatter, or the widow as remarried. Candidates: Gunder Jonsen Frøyum's probate after his burial (25 Jan 1784; Stjør- og Verdal skifteprotokoll 24760/24761, Stjørdal F entries), which would name the widow and 'hendes Datter' Lisbet; or the 1745 deed (pantebok, about 23 Jun 1745) by which Gunder bought Frøyum. ¶12

Remarriage proved: pantebok A-2 fol. 460b-461a (DA 19751 view 90), deed no. 21 of 1745, 'Actum Frøjum d. 23 Junij 1745': Anders Davidsen Frøyum, Berit Andersdatter with her husband Johan Gundersen Hembre, and Sibor Andersdatter sell their parts of Frøyum to 'Gunder Jonsen og hans Hustrue', and the deed counts in the share that 'bemeldte Gunder Jonsens Hustrue Gjertrud [patronymic read as 'Lars'] datter' had inherited after 'hendes afgangne Mand David Andersen' as his arvelod on the skifte after his mother. Indexed in panteregister 22315 view 63 ('Gunder Joensens Skiøde paa gaarden Frøyum... folio 460'). The patronymic in this pantebok copy looks like 'Lars', not 'Gunders' (the scan is 2296 px wide; not legible beyond doubt). Resolved as a clerk's slip: the woman is fixed by her late husband, and David Andersen of Frøyum had one wife only - 'Geertrud Gundersd. Hemre', betrothed Oct 1743, married 28 Nov 1743, named as his widow 'Gjertrud Gundersdatt.' in his probate of 17 Nov 1744 - and 'Jertrud Gundersdatter' was buried from Frøyum in 1786. This also settles the earlier date question: she was Gunder Jonsen's wife by 23 Jun 1745. ¶13

Burial (overnight sweep F): Stjørdal ministerialbok 1781-1797 (1714Q), burials, 27 Jan 1786: 'Jertrud Gundersdatter', Frøjum, inderst (a lodger), indexed with birth year 1721 (i.e. an age of about 65), Digitalarkivet burial record pg00000002773447. She was still living on Frøyum 42 years after David Andersen's probate there, which is consistent with her daughter being 'Pigen Lisbet Davidsdatter Frøyum' at the 1765 marriage. The birth about 1721 fits a mother of a child born in Sep 1744. Graded on her link to Lisbet Davidsdatter: stays Probable. The 1744 probate names her as the mother of the infant Lisbet, but the step from that infant to the 1765 bride and 1825 widow of Kleven spans two decades with no intervening record naming mother and daughter together; her own probate (none found under Frøian in the typed Stjør- og Verdal registers) would settle it. ¶2

The case for this identification 2

Supporting (not an upgrade): her daughter's baptism is now read - Stjørdal 16370 canvas 58, Dom. 16 p. Trinit. (20 Sep) 1744 at Værnes: 'Sal. David Frøyums B. Lisbet' (the child of the late David of Frøyum), godparents 'Gunder og Ranni Hemre' (her father Gunder Johansen and, probably, her sister Randi b. 1723), Berit Frøyum and others. This settles the given name 'Lisbet', which the 1744 probate gave only as a probable reading, and puts the Hemre family at the font. The baptism names no mother (16370 omits mothers). Her link to Lisbet Davidsdatter stays Probable only because of the 1744-1765 gap (21 years) before 'Pigen Lisbet Davidsdatter Frøyum' marries; an intermediate record is still needed - Lisbet's confirmation about 1759-61 (16371, unindexed) or the 1762 ekstraskatt household of Frøyum (48401: not in the dragoon-quarter views 239-246 nor views 223, 232-233, 236; Frøyum's place in the 1762 numbering is still unknown). Her own baptism is now read too: 16368 canvas 83, Dom. Oculi (2 Mar) 1721, 'Gunder Hemræs b. Gjertrud. Mod. Ingborg' (see Ingeborg Rolfsdatter). ¶9

Supporting (not a change of her own grade): her betrothal is now read, Stjørdal 16370 canvas 262 (p. 261), 2 Oct 1743 at Værnes: 'ungkarl David Anderson Frøyum med pigen Geertrud Gundersd. Hemre', cautioners Jon Morset and Peder Mona, 'Copul. ved Wernes d. 28 Nov 1743'. It names her farm, Hemre, so she is the daughter of Gunder Johansen Hemre, and the 1717 Trøite Giertrud (daughter of Gunder Rolfsen Trøite and Mali, who were still having children in 1720: 16368 c78) is excluded. Her link to Lisbet Davidsdatter stays Probable for the separate reason already noted (1744-1765 gap). ¶10

Alternatives considered and set aside 5

Caution on the lead: it is not yet shown that Gunder Jonsen, owner of Frøyum 1762-1780, is her father (the 1744 guardian, her father, is written Gunder Johans[en]; Jon/Johan were interchangeable, so they may be one man). His deed of 26 Aug 1775 conveys Frøyum to 'min kiære Søn Jon Gundersen' and his 'Contract og Forening' with that son, dated Frøyum 14 Jan 1780, provides for himself and his wife; neither names a daughter Gjertrud in the parts legible to me (pantebok B-2 fol. 434b, source 19754 view 216; C-1 fol. 225b-226a, source 19755 view 227). Whether the 1744 'Gunder Johans[en]' and this Gunder Jonsen are one man is open. ¶5

Near-miss attempt, not raised: Frøyum is found in the 1762 extra-tax list for Stjørdal. Ekstraskatt 1762 (source 48401), view 216, farm No. 120 'Fröyom': 'Gunder og H[ustru]', children over 12 'Lisbeth, Giertrud, Ingborg', farmhand Jens Lars[en] 24, then the husmann/inderst column 'Niels og H[ustru]: Inderster Anders og H[ustru]: Marit Andersd.' (scan read). The list gives first names only and no relationship words, so it cannot raise anyone (audit 5 rule 3). Read with the panteregister lead (Gunder Frøyum's deed about 1762-63; Gunder Jonsen's deed of 1775 to 'min kiære Søn Jon Gundersen'), it allows two readings. (a) Gjertrud remarried a Gunder (Jonsen) of Frøyum: then 'H[ustru]' is she, Lisbeth is her daughter by David, and Giertrud and Ingborg are later daughters, named after her and her mother Ingeborg. (b) Lisbeth, Giertrud and Ingborg are Gunder's own children: then Lisbeth is a rival to Lisbet Davidsdatter. ¶11a

Upgrade (near-miss): graded on her link to Lisbet Davidsdatter, Probable to Confirmed. Linked records: (1) David Andersen's probate, Frøyum 17 Nov 1744 (24753 fol. 102b): widow Gjertrud Gundersdatter, only child 'Lisbet Davids datt.', 10 weeks; (2) Lisbet's baptism 20 Sep 1744 (16370 c58), godparents her father Gunder and her sister Randi Hemre; (3) NEW, the Frøyum deed of 23 Jun 1745 (19751 view 90): she is Gunder Jonsen's wife on Frøyum, the remarriage that named as the test record; (4) Gunder Jonsen on Frøyum in between, 1748 (16370 p. 275), about 1762-63, 1775 and 1780 (22318 image 83; 19754 v216; 19755 v227); (5) the 1762 ekstraskatt, Frøyum No. 120 (48401 v216): 'Gunder og H[ustru]' - she, by (3) and her 1786 burial on Frøyum - with children over 12 'Lisbeth, Giertrud, Ingborg', so Lisbet (18) was in her household 18 years after 1744, with the parent shown on the farm inside the gap and no named rival (audit 7 rule 1); (6) 'Pigen Lisbet Davidsdatter Frøyum' married 29 May 1765 (16371 p. 345), 3 years later; (7) her own burial, 'Jertrud Gundersdatter', Frøyum, 27 Jan 1786. Mother and daughter are on Frøyum at both ends and the mother stays there throughout. ¶14a

No rival: the 1765 patronymic Davidsdatter excludes a daughter of Gunder, and David Andersen was the only David on Frøyum. Lisbet named her children David and Gjertrud. ¶14b

Superseded notes: the 'Caution on the lead' and the reading (b) (Lisbeth in 1762 as Gunder's own child, a rival) are superseded. Gunder Jonsen of Frøyum (her second husband, from Lien in Skatval) and Gunder Johansen Hemre (her father) are two men, and the 1745 deed shows she was Gunder Jonsen's wife, so the 1762 'Hustru' is she and Lisbeth is her daughter. The statement that 'no primary record read names Gunder's wife' is superseded by the 1745 deed. Gunder Jonsen's own probate after his burial on 25 Jan 1784 is not in the typed registers of 24759-24762 (no Gunder at Frøian), nor is hers of 1786; he had handed the farm to his son Jon Gundersen in 1775/1780, which may explain why no skifte was held. ¶15

Open questions and the next record to read 2

Lead, not proof (panteregister sweep): the Stjørdal tinglag panteregister 1762-1800 (source 22318, image 83) shows a Gunder Jonsen as owner of Frøyum (gl. matr. no. 190, 1 øre 18 [?] skyld): 'Cancelliesecr. Hornemanns Skiøde paa 1 øre 12 mrk til Gunder Frøyum' (pantebok B, fol. 48, about 1762-63), 'Gunder Joensens Skiøde til Søn Joen Gundersen' (pantebok B-2, fol. 434, 1769-77) and 'Contract og Forening imellem Gunder Jonsen og Søn Jon Gundersen' (pantebok C-1, fol. 225, about 1781). A Gunder of Frøyum old enough to hand the farm to a grown son in the 1770s is of the right generation to be the father of Gjertrud Gundersdatter (b. about 1721), who stayed on Frøyum as a widow and died there as a lodger in 1786. Not yet proved: fol. 434a (source 19754 view 215) shows no Frøyum deed, so it is probably on fol. 434b (view 216); fol. 225 (19755 view 226) was too dense to read at the resolution tried. Read both at full resolution, and look for a skifte after Gunder Jonsen of Frøyum (not in the typed registers of 24753-24762, whose Frøyum rows are only David Andersen 1744 and Joen Hansen/Ingeborg Arntsdatter). ¶3

Test records: a marriage of the widow Gjertrud Gundersdatter Frøyum to a Gunder, about 1745-50 (16370; the trolovede list is at about c258-270); and the 1775 and 1780 Frøyum deeds read for the wife's name. Grade unchanged. Lisbet's own confirmation about 1759-61 was sought in 16371 c44-79 (Nov 1758 to about 1761): the 'Conf.' entries there are confirmations of home baptisms, not catechumen confirmations, and no named confirmation list was seen in those years. ¶11b
