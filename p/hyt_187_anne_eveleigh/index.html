Life story

Anne Eveleigh, second of the five daughters of Dean John Eveleigh of or near Bandon, Co. Cork, and Mildred Coldwell. She married Doctor Benjamin Cross, and their children were John, Robert, William, Tryphena, Mary and Elizabeth Cross (Blennerhassett Pedigree, in Hickson, Old Kerry Records, 1872, pp. 53-55). ¶1

Corroboration: Brady (vol. 2, p. 308) and Venn (Alumni Cantabrigienses, s.v. Crosse, Benjamin) both say that Benjamin Cross, Precentor of Cloyne, married 'Anne, daughter and co-heir of John Eveleigh, Dean of Ross'. Brady lists the same children as the Blennerhassett Pedigree: John, Robert and William (all died without issue), Tryphena, Mary (married William Collis in 1687) and Elizabeth (wife of John Blennerhassett). The Dean's will left his Oxford leasehold to Benjamin Cross. These are compiled sources, so the grade stays Probable. ¶3a

Benjamin Crosse's will read on the image (PROB 11/378/255, 1684): it names 'my deare wife Anne Crosse' (lease of Black Hall, St Giles, Oxford, with sons Robert and William) and 'my beloved wife Anne Crosse my whole and sole Executrix', and it calls the Dean of Ross the grandfather of her husband's eldest son John and of the eldest daughter Tryphena. It also names 'my second Daughter Mary Crosse' (500 pounds) and 'my youngest Daughter Elizabeth Crosse'. Grade unchanged (Probable, graded on her link to Mary Cross): the will lists wife and children but does not say the children were Anne's (audit 8 rule 2), and Mary's identity with Mary wife of William Collis is still carried by the Blennerhassett Pedigree (see Benjamin Cross). ¶4

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
