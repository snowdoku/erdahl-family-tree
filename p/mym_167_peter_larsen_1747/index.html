About the family

Father of Gullov Pedersdatter (see her page), graded Probable on that link. Record read: Skatval baptism, 11th Sunday after Trinity [14 Aug] 1774 (Stjørdal 16371, canvas 251, p. 249 left, entry 5): '5 Peter Schjervolds b: Gudlov, Fadd: Ole, Malie, Anne Schjervold, Sivert Velvang, Malie Moxne[?]'. Peder Jonsen of Skjervold was buried 15 Dec 1771, so in 1774 the Peter of Skjervold is Peter Larsen, who had married the widow Marit in 1772 (bygdebok). The only other Gullov/Golla Pedersdatter of Skjervold, Peder Jonsen's daughter (bapt. 28 Jul 1771), was buried in 1773 (Digitalarkivet index: 'Golla Pedersdatter, b. 1771, Skiervold, 1773, avdød'). The 1798 bride 'Pige Gullov Petersdatter Skiervolde' was 26 in Feb 1801, which fits Aug 1774 (the 1771 child would have been 29). The bygdebok also says her husband Peder Pedersen 'fikk skjøte av sin svigerfar 14.02.1804' (got a deed from his father-in-law). Kept Probable: 24 years lie between the baptism and the marriage, and the deed has not been read. ¶2

Life story

Farmer (bruker, possibly owner) of Skjervold (Schiervold) 77/3, Skatval. Bygdebok (Skatval vol. 3, 77/3 Skjervold, canvas 273 right): 'Peter Larsen [fra Holan] 24), døpt 15.01.1747, ble bruker/muligens eier... [g.] 20.04.1772 Marit Olsdtr., enke etter Peder Johnsen'; children include a daughter 'døpt 14.08.1774'; 2nd marriage 13.10.1809 Berit Eriksdtr. (children Lorents 1810, Erik 1813, Peter 1816). The 1801 census has him at Schiervold, 56, first marriage, with wife Marit Olsdatter, 67 (already in the tree on her record). ¶1

Burial: 'Peter Larsen', kårmann (retired farmer on a life-lease), of Skiervold, aged 71 (b. about 1745), died 27 Jul and was buried 6 Aug 1816 at Skatval (Stjørdal ministerialbok, Skatval 1815-1819, p. 115, no. 6; index pg00000001970360). Name and farm match the Skjervold farmer who married Marit Olsdatter in 1772 and deeded the farm to his son-in-law in 1804, which fits his kår status. The age is two years above his baptism of 15 Jan 1747, which is kept. ¶5

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
