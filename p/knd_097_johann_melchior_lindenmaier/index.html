About the family

Father of Luise Catharina Lindenmaier (see her page). Graded on his link to her: the FamilySearch 'Germany, Marriages, 1558-1929' index of her Bietigheim marriage of 1822 (1:1:VCYQ-MRB) names her parents in labelled fields as Johann Melchior Lindenmaier and Katharina Kuffer, and the Vaihingen index of the same marriage on 4 Jul 1822 (1:1:QPG2-871S) names her father Johann Melchior Lindenmajer. Both are indexes of the marriage record naming the relationship; the marriage is of the woman in the tree, so no bridge is needed. Variant: Ancestry's row of the Bietigheim entry reads 'Johann Mathias' (record 907054817); two of three readings give Melchior. Images not read (Bietigheim 1184901-00228; Vaihingen 1184564-00908). ¶1

Family at Bietigheim (index rows, inferred to be his): Friderich Ludwig Melchior Lindenmaier, buried Bietigheim 12 Sep 1815, parents 'Joh Melchior, Rosina Cathar' (record 7062944, image 1184901-01089); 'Rosina' may be a misreading of Louisa. Probably a cooper (see the 'Kuffer' note on his daughter). Birth about 1775-80, not found in the index. ¶3

Mother entered (Probable): Elisabetha Regina Render ('Renters'), wife from 8 Feb 1774 of Johann Melchior Lindenmaier the elder. A son Johann Melchior of 'Johann Melchior, Elisabetha' married at Bietigheim on 22 Feb 1813 (record 7054597) and is very probably this man remarried after Louisa Catharina Finck's death; see the note on his father. Born about 1775-82; his baptism is not indexed. ¶4

Life story

Marriage: Johann Melchior Lindenmaier, son of Johann Melchior, married Louisa Catharina Fincken, daughter of Johannes, at Illingen, Oberamt Maulbronn, on 28 Apr 1803 (Ancestry 61023 groom row 8261325, bride row 908261325, image 1187140-00632). Identified as Luise Catharina's father by name, by the mother's names (Luisa Catharina) and by date (Probable for that identification; it carries the mother's and his father's grades, not his). ¶2

Labelled records: baptism V4L9-W4H, born 12 Sep 1778, christened Bietigheim 13 Sep 1778, Father 'Johann Melchior Lindenmajer', Mother 'Elisabetha Reuter'; marriage QPGR-8VHN (Illingen 28 Apr 1803), Father 'Johann Melchior Lindenmaiers', Father-in-law 'Johannes Fincken'; first wife buried Bietigheim 19 Sep 1813 (QPGL-X2R5, spouse written 'Joh Mathias Lindermajer'). Remarriage to Rosina Catharina Kienzlin (b. 1778 Hofeneck, daughter of Johann Caspar Kienzlin and Johanna Maria Luz): 'Germany, Marriages' VCYQ-S6T dates it 1814 and gives the groom's birth as 1778 Bietigheim, previously married, parents Johann Melchior and Elisabetha Reitter; the Lutheran index QPGG-ZGWB and Ancestry 7054597 date it 22 Feb 1813, which is before the first wife's burial. Contradiction unresolved; 1814 fits the burial. Identifying the 1778 child with the 1803 groom is an elimination (the only Johann Melchior baptised to a Johann Melchior Lindenmaier in the FamilySearch indexes, 1774-85), so his MOTHER's link stays Probable. Birth not entered for that reason. Read signed in, 2 Nov 2026; labelled index fields, image not seen (partner-only). ¶6

Corrections 2

Correction: the burial of Friderich Ludwig Melchior Lindenmaier at Bietigheim, 12 Sep 1815, parents 'Joh Melchior, Rosina Cathar' (record 7062944), cited earlier as a child of his marriage to Louisa Catharina with 'Rosina' a misreading, belongs to his (probable) second marriage, to Rosina Catharina Frienzlin on 22 Feb 1813 (record 7054597). ¶5

Correction: the remarriage was on 22 Feb 1814, not 1813. The Lutheran index date 22 Feb 1813 (QPGG-ZGWB; Ancestry 7054597) is an indexing error; the image (FS DGS 101835616 item 3, image 19 = Ancestry 1184901-00191) puts it under 1814, with a dispensation of 19 Feb 1814, which agrees with 'Germany, Marriages' VCYQ-S6T (1814) and with the first wife's burial on 19 Sep 1813 (QPGL-X2R5). The date conflict noted is resolved and no longer blocks a grade. Bietigheim marriage register 1814, entry 3 (image read): groom 'Johann Melchior Lindenmaier', Bürger und Schuster allhier, born 12 Sept 1778 zu Bietigheim, status 'Wit[wer] der Louise Catharina geb. Fink'; his parents 'Johann Melchior Lindenmaier, B[ürger] und Schuster allhier' and 'Elisabetha geb. Reitter(in)'; bride 'Rosina Catharina geb. Kienzlin', ledig, born 13 Nov 1778 zu Hofeneck, daughter of the late Johann Caspar Kienzlin and Johanna Maria geb. Luz; married 22 Feb 1814; dispensation of the banns dated 19 Febr. 1814. The entry stands under the year heading 1814 on the page headed 1813/1814. The entry ties the tree's man (the widower of Louise Catharina Fink) to the birth of 12 Sep 1778 at Bietigheim and to his parents Johann Melchior Lindenmaier, shoemaker, and Elisabetha née Reitter. Image read signed in on FamilySearch, 2 Nov 2026. ¶7
