About the family

First wife of Rev. Erling Ludvig Tungseth and mother of his children. Her name comes from the 2007 obituary of their eldest son, which states that Rev. Erling Martin Tungseth junior was born on 6 Oct 1917 in Hawley, Minnesota, to Erling L. and Lydia (Larson) Tungseth. Her own dates, parents and death have not been found, and she is not on her husband's Find a Grave memorial, which names only his second wife, married in 1966. Since he remarried in 1966 she had presumably died or the marriage had ended by then. ¶1

Life story

The couple were living at Hawley, Clay County, in 1917, in his parents' neighbourhood, before he moved north to Thief River Falls. ¶2

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
