The case for this identification 1

Surname and father: bride row 'Maria Agnes Marcklins', Bietigheim 20 Jul 1706, relatives 'Johan Georg, Johann Albrecht' (her father, then the groom; record 907270163, image 1184899-00372). Indexed 'Marcklins' (genitive); spelled Märklin/Mercklin here. A Johann/Hanß Georg Merklin x Maria Agnes baptised Johann Jacob (8 Apr 1697, 7268047) and Johann Georg (29 Aug 1705, 7268466) at Bietigheim, probably her parents' family; her own baptism (about 1680-88) was not found in the index. ¶2
