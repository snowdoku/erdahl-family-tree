Life story

Death: 'Guri Olsd.', kaarenke and widow ('Ek') of Gidskeødegaard, born 1784, died 4 Feb and was buried 7 Feb 1872 at Borgund (Borgund ministerialbok 1864-1879; index pg00000000874137, indexed again as pg00000005474063). Farm, widowhood and birth year all match: she was 31 at Giske-Ødegård in the 1815 census (b. about 1784), and she is the only Guri Olsdatter born 1779-1789 in the Borgund/Giske burials. ¶8

Probably the Gurri Olesdatter, 16, an unmarried servant at Giske-Ødegård (household of Martinus Johansen) in the 1801 census. Her baptism (about 1784/85) and parents are not found. No Guri daughter of an Ole is indexed in Borgund, Giske, Vigra or Haram for 1782-1787. A Gurri Olesdatter of Brandal was confirmed 11 Oct 1801 at Borgund, but whether she is the same girl is unknown. ¶3

Married widower Peder Pedersen of Giske-Ødegård 18 Aug 1811 at Borgund. ¶1

Marriage: The Borgund pastor's register (ministerialbok) records that on Whit Sunday, 2 June 1811, banns were asked for the widower (enkemand) Peder Pedersen of Giske-Ødegård and the maiden (pige) Gurri Olesdatter of Giske-Ødegård; they married on the 10th Sunday after Trinity, 18 August 1811. The marriage witnesses (forlovere) were Lars Andersen and Ole Eriksen, both of Giske-Ødegård. The entry gives no ages, no fathers and no birthplace, so it does not settle her origin. The clerk's copy (klokkerbok 16007) has no entry for this marriage in its 1811 list. ¶4

Probate of her husband (scan read): 'Gidskeødegaard i Borgens Skibrede, Ao 1814 den 11te Augusti' the probate court met at Giske-Ødegård after the late farmer Peder Pedersen, between (1) the widow 'Gurie Olsd.' with her appointed guardian at law (antagne laugværge) Martinus Johannesen Gidskeødegaard, and (2) the deceased's children and heirs (Johan, Knud Andreas, a daughter, Jacob Andreas 6, Martha Andrine, Anne Laurine 13, and others; partly read). Her guardian is the Martinus Johansen in whose household she served in 1801, not a kinsman, so the probate gives no clue to her parents. Nordre Sunnmøre 3A 6 fol. 630b, view 24596/95. ¶5

In the 1815 census (30 Apr 1815) she is 31, wife of the pilot Hans Olesen at Giske-Ødegård. With her are 'Peder Pedersen, 1/2, her child' and her stepsons Knud (17) and Peter (11) Pedersen. ¶2

Confirmation checked, no upgrade: the Borgund confirmations of 3 Oct 1830 (Borgund ministerialbok 1828-1839, 1531P, p. 108 no. 19), read on the scan (canvas 104 of source 16004) and the index, have 'Peter Johan Pedersen', born 1813, birthplace 'Gidskeødegaard', parents 'Peder Pedersen og Gurri Larsdatter' (the index gives baptism 20 Jun and vaccination 23 Jun 1821). Peder Johan of the tree was baptised 30 Oct 1814 as the posthumous son of Peder Pedersen and Gurri Olesdatter (married 1811). The farm and the father's name agree, but the birth year (1813 against 1814) and the mother's patronymic (Larsdatter against Olesdatter) do not. Either the confirmation clerk erred twice, or this is another boy of a different Peder Pedersen household at Giske-Ødegård. Until the 1814 baptism scan, the 1815 census entry and this confirmation are compared, her grade stays Probable. A search of Borgund confirmations 1827-1833 found no other Peder Pedersen from Giske-Ødegård. ¶6

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: Confirmed, graded on her link to her son Peder Johan Pedersen Røssevold (see his page), not on her own parentage (unknown). The record naming the relationship is the 1815 census of Giske-Ødegård (household 9): no. 064 Hans Olesen, 28, pilot; no. 065 Gurri [Olesdatter], 31, 'H' (wife); no. 066 'Peder Pedersen', 1/2, family position 'B. hendes' (her child); nos. 067-068 Knud Pedersen 17 and Peter Pedersen 11, 'stedbørn' (stepchildren), who are the sons of her late first husband Peder Pedersen by his first marriage. That identifies her as Peder Pedersen's widow (married 18 Aug 1811; widow 'Gurie Olsd.' in his probate 11 Aug 1814) and the baby as the posthumous Peder Johannes baptised 30 Oct 1814. Continuity to the tree: the boy is confirmed in 1830 from Gidskeødegaard as 'Peter Johan Pedersen', son of 'Peder Pedersen og Gurri', and married in 1842 as a bachelor from Giske-Ødegård born about 1814, father Peder Pedersen. The 1830 entry's 'Gurri Larsdatter' and birth year 1813 are clerk errors rather than a rival family: no other Peder Pedersen couple and no other Peder-fathered baptism at Giske-Ødegård appears in the 1813-1814 Borgund/Giske index, and found no other Peder Pedersen from Giske-Ødegård among the 1827-1833 confirmands. The 1814 baptism scan was not reached (the index record has no scan link). ¶7
