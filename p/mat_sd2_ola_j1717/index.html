About the family

David Erdahl's maternal seventh-great-grandfather, and the man the 1801 census required but did not name. Jorgen (Jorn) Olssen Slind's patronymic made his father an Ole, and his mother is named in the 1801 census as Marit Pedersdatter; the Selbu gaardshistorie supplies the man himself. Selbu i fortid og naatid vol. 7, p. 116: holder of the bruk '1756-1774 [Ola], f. 1717, d. 1772. G. 1742 m. Marit..., f. 1720, d. 1808', and in the previous holder's list of children 'Ola, f. 1717,... over bruket' - Ola, born 1717, took over the holding. ¶1

His own father was JORN OLSEN, holder 1729-1755, which makes his patronymic JORNSEN - and that in turn is why his son was named Jorn after him. The naming alternates Jorn - Ola - Jorn - Ola down four generations of this bruk, which is the Norwegian custom of naming the eldest son for the paternal grandfather working exactly as designed, and it is also why the farm's holders are so easily confused with one another. ¶2

His seven children are listed under his wife Marit Pedersdatter. ¶4

Pedigree collapse (three routes; the third, through Brynhild, Probable): Ola Jornsen and Marit Pedersdatter of Slindan are ancestors of Gunder Olsen Slind and Oline, and so of Palmer Gilbert Eidem, three times: (1) Jorgen (Jorn) Olssen, b. 1752 -> Gunder Jorgensen Slind -> Ole Gundersen Slind -> Gunder Olsen Slind; (2) Ingeborg Olsdatter, b. 1749 -> Ingebor Jonsdatter of Sandvik -> Marit Haldorsdatter -> Kari Olsdatter, wife of Gunder Olsen Slind; (3) Brynhild Olsdatter, b. 1744 -> Magli Christensdatter -> Brynhild Eriksdatter -> Berith Gundersdatter Eidem, wife of Ole Gundersen Slind and mother of Gunder Olsen Slind. So Ole Gundersen Slind and Berith Gundersdatter (m. 24 May 1841) were second cousins once removed, and Gunder Olsen Slind descends from Ola and Marit through both his parents as well as through his wife. Route 3 rests on the identification of Magli's mother Brynild Olsdatter with Ola's daughter Brynhild (Probable); routes checked with fleet.py show. ¶10

Life story

Baptism (supports the Probable rating; no farm named): 4th Sunday in Advent, 19 December 1717, 'Ditto Jørgen Smeds barn, Olaf' - Jørgen Smed's child Olaf - godparents Baro Ütthus[?], Peder Grøtlie[?], Reinholt B[?], Brÿnel Ütthus[?] and Mali Efjen; and on the 3rd Sunday after Epiphany, 23 January 1718, 'Introd. Jørgen Smeds q. Brynel effter det ægte barn Olaf' - Jørgen Smed's wife Brynel (Brynhild) churched after the legitimate child Olaf (Selbu ministerialbok 1699-1737, canvas 69 left and right pages, crops). A Jørgen with a wife Brynhild and a son Olaf born in December 1717 matches the gaardshistorie's Jorn Olsen, married 1717 to Brynhild, and their son Ola born 1717. Birth replaced '1717' with 'before 19 December 1717'; place left as Selbu because the register names no farm. ¶7

Betrothal (possible, not proved): late 1741, before Advent, the same clerk's book has 'N1 Trolovet Ole Jørgensen' - betrothed, Ole Jørgensen - squeezed in afterwards in darker ink, with no bride, farm or sponsors (16315 canvas 27 col. 3). It is presumably the betrothal before the July 1742 wedding, but Ole Jørgensen is a common name. ¶13

Marriage: on the 8th Sunday after Trinity 1742 - 15 July 1742, computed from Easter 1742 = 25 March and Trinity Sunday = 20 May - the Selbu parish clerk's book enters 'Egte viet [Ungkarl og?] Soldat Ole Jørgensen med Pigen Marethe Pedersdatter': married, the [bachelor and?] soldier Ole Jørgensen and the maiden Marethe (Marit) Pedersdatter (16315 canvas 35 col. 1). No farm and no forlovere (sponsors) are written. The names and year agree with the gaardshistorie's 'G. 1742 m. Marit' and with the 1801 census naming his son's mother Marit Pedersdatter, so the entry is taken as his marriage; the register itself does not name Slindan. ¶11

Military service: the 1742 marriage entry calls him 'Soldat' - a soldier, i.e. an enrolled man of the national infantry levy, as were many farm sons of his generation. ¶12

Called 'Ole Jørgens[en] Slinden' at his daughter Marit's baptism, 14 Aug 1746 (16315 c90), which confirms his patronymic Jørgensen/Jørnsen and that he was at Slinden by 1746. ¶14

1762 extra tax: Ola lived in his parents' household at 706 Slinden, entered as 'Ole gift med Marit Tors[?]datter, Børn, Brynild, Marit, Ingebor' - Ole married to Marit, with children Brynild, Marit and Ingebor over 12. These are his daughters Brynhild (b. 1744), Marit (b. 1746) and Ingeborg (b. 1749, aged about 13); his son Jørn (b. 1752) and the younger girls were under 12 and not listed. The word earlier read as a son 'Biørn' is 'Børn' (children). So Ola had not yet taken the bruk over in 1762: his father Jørn was still alive and still head of household. ¶9

Context, flagged as context: he died in 1772 but the gaardshistorie runs his holding to 1774, which is the ordinary pattern - a widow kept the bygsel (lease) for a year or two after her husband's death until a son was of age to take a new one. His son Jorn took the holding in 1775 at twenty-three, and Marit lived on the farm another thirty-four years. ¶5

His bruk was SLINDAN VESTRE, whose holder list occupies pages 113-117 of Selbu i fortid og naatid vol. 7 under a heading at the foot of page 112. ¶6

Drowned 22 Jun 1772. Burial entered before the 3rd Sunday after Trinity 1772: 'Begravne: Olle Jørgensen Slinden, som d. 22 Jun: druknede i Gulset[?] Elven, gl. 55 aar' - buried Ole Jørgensen Slinden, who drowned on 22 June in the Gulset[?] river, aged 55 (Selbu 16311 canvas 51, right page, crop checked by the orchestrator). Age 55 agrees with his baptism of Dec 1717 (he was 54). The river name is uncertain. ¶15

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 3

Rated PROBABLE, not Confirmed. Two things are certain from primary records - that Jorgen Olssen born about 1750 had a mother called Marit Pedersdatter and a father whose given name was Ole - and the gaardshistorie supplies a man of that name, in the right bruk, married to a Marit of the right age, whose son Jorn born 1752 took over the farm. But a gaardshistorie is a published reconstruction and no register entry for this man has been read. The record that would confirm him is his burial in the Selbu register in 1772, or the probate (skifte) that followed it. ¶3

The identification rests on the names and the date, not on a farm. 'Smed' (smith) is an occupational byname and does not name Slinden; Selbu had smiths at the copper works as well, so a works smith is a possible rival. The other Brynel of 1717, wife of Siur Slinde, is a different woman: she was churched in July 1717 after a child Christen and bore a posthumous daughter Guru in 1719 after Siur's burial, which cannot be the mother of an Olaf born in December 1717. Later, Ole Gundersen Slind of this family was a smith - consistent with, not proof of, a smith byname in the family. ¶8a

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed. His probate, Skifteprotokoll 6-2 (24757) fol. 530: 'Anno 1772 den 17de October' registration and valuation on the farm Slinde in Selbu after the late 'Ole Jørgensen', between his widow 'Marith Peders Datter' and the seven children born to them in marriage: 1. the son Jørgen Olsen, married and living on the farm; 2. the daughter Brønnille Olsdatter, married to Christen Christensen Stor Efjen; 3. the daughter Marithe Olsdatter, married to Ingebrigt Christensen Stor Efjen; 4. the daughter Ingeborg Olsdatter, married to Jon Olsen Sandvig; 5. the daughter Sire Olsdatter, 15; 6. the daughter Margaretha Olsdatter, 12; 7. the daughter Maren Olsdatter, 7. This primary record names him as the husband of Marit Pedersdatter and the father of the direct ancestors Brynhild Olsdatter (wife of Christen Christensen) and Ingeborg Olsdatter (wife of Jon Olsen Sandvik), and of Jørgen Olsen Slinden. The register of the volume lists the entry as 'Ole Jørgensen, Slindan, Selbu'. It fits his burial of 22 Jun 1772. ¶16
