Life story

Hans's older brother, Gunhild's uncle. Baptized 8 Nov 1829. Married Sigrid Paulsdatter (b. ca 1822, Sandland; daughter of Paul Gundersen) on 19 Feb 1855. Owner-farmer of Rønningen in 1865. His wife is called 'Sigrid Paulsdatter Undeberg' as a godparent in 1862. Sons: Torjus (1856), Paul (1858) and Tellef (1863), later surnamed Rønningen/Undeberg. Witness at Hans's 1858 marriage. Included as evidence for the Undeberg link. ¶1
