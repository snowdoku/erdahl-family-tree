About the family

Wife of Cecil Elwood Hayter. At her 1938 marriage she was 19, did housework, was Pentecostal, lived at Kaleden and was born at Turtleford, Saskatchewan. Her father was Robert John Preston, born Pilot Mound, Manitoba; her mother was Hazel Inez Dunn, born Glen Ewen, Saskatchewan. She was living at Kaleden and reported Cecil's death in 1963. Her obituary (Okanagan Valley Newspaper Group, 2014, as posted on Find a Grave) gives her birth as 3 July 1919 at Turtleford and her death as 12 June 2014 at Haven Hill Retirement Centre, Penticton, aged 94. It says that 'Evelyn's home was always Kaleden' and that her husband Cecil had died before her. She was survived by four sons and four daughters, with 16 grandchildren, 13 great-grandchildren and two great-great-grandchildren. Her funeral was at Bethel Pentecostal Church, Penticton, and she was buried at Kaleden Cemetery. The children are presumably living and are not recorded here. ¶1
