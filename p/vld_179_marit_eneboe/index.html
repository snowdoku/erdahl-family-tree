About the family

Mother of Anne Pedersdatter of Slettum (see her page), Probable; graded on her link to Anne. Lesja bygdebok only (vol. 1, canvas 181/183, chapter Einbu): Peder Hansen 'g.m. Marit ca. 1683-?'; children Kari ca. 1717, Anne ca. 1718 (sjå Nørdre Slettom 1-7), Hans ca. 1720,... Peder ca. 1727. Her patronymic was not recovered (gap). Capped at Probable; the skifte after Peder Hansen (about 1745) would name her and the children. ¶1

Parents entered (both Probable): Bjerte (Bierte) Pålsen of Nørdre Einbu (b. about 1644, d. after Nov 1727) and Guro Jakobsdatter (bur. Eneboe 13 Dec 1733, b. about 1642), from the Lesja bygdebok vol. 1 c181 ('4. Marit, ca. 1683, sjå Nørdre, her' among Bjerte and Guro's children), her primary patronymic Biertesdatter, and indexed Lesja records placing Bierte and Guro on Eneboe in 1726-27. Her own grade (link to Anne) is unchanged. ¶5

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Upgrade: Confirmed, graded on her link to her daughter Anne Pedersdatter of Slettum (see her page). Skifte after her husband Peder Hansen of Eneboe (Einbu), 8 Feb 1745 (card 39945/2437, skifteprotokoll 1740-1751 fol. 701b): 'Peder Hansøn + g.m. Marit Biertesdtr.', children Hans 25, Bierte 20, Peder 18, Kari 28 and 'Anne g.m. Rolf Knudsøn Slethum' - the Anne Pedersdatter, wife of Rolf Knudsen of Nordre Slettum, whose 1759 skifte puts her in the tree (same couple, same farm, 14 years). Again in the skifte after her son Bierte Pedersen, 29 Mar 1758 (protocol 25158 fol. 380a, scan read), the first heir is '1. Moderen Marit Biertesdatter', and the sister 'D. Anne Pedersdatter gift med Rolf Slettum' is among the heirs. Her patronymic is thus Biertesdatter (two records); she was alive in March 1758. ¶3

Audit 8 (1-2 Nov 2026): Confirmed kept, graded on her link to Anne Pedersdatter of Slettum (see her page). The card (39945/2437) and the 1758 sibling probate do not by themselves show that the widow Marit was the mother of every child; compare Mons Torbersen in audit 7, whose second wife was correctly not linked. So the auditor read the protocol entry itself: Nord-Gudbrandsdal skifteprotokoll 1732-1751 (source 25157), fol. 701b, view 718, left page, lower half, headed Eneboe, 8 Feb 1745 (bitonal scan; crops in). The preamble divides the estate between the widow and their 'tilsammen avlede' children: 1. Hans Pedersen 25, 2. Bierte 20, 3. Peder Pedersen 18, 4. Kari Pedersdatter 28, and 5. 'Anne Pedersdatter gift med Rolf Knudsøn Slettum'. The record therefore names Anne as the joint child of Peder Hansen and Marit. ¶6

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Lead for her parents (not entered): the name Bierte is rare and belongs to Eneboe. An earlier Eneboe skifte, 18 Dec 1676 (card 39945/2433, skifteprotokoll 1673-1688 fol. 48b, 'Einbu i Lesje, Inneboe'), is after Poffuell (Pål) Biertesen, married to Sønne(?) Christophersdatter, children Bierte, Christopher, Laurits and Jon Poffuelsen (no ages). Marit Biertesdatter (b. about 1683) could be a daughter of that Bierte Pålsen, which would explain how she and Peder Hansen came to hold odelsgods in Eneboe (1745). Speculative only; test record: the 1701 manntall for Lesja (Eneboe) or a deed (the early Einbu cards 2431-2437 hold only skifter of 1667, 1676, 1708 and 1745, none after a Bierte). ¶4
