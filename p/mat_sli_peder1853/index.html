About the family

Younger brother of Oline Slind's father. 1865 census: 'Peder Olsen, 12, deres Son' in Ole Gundersen's household at Slinden. 1891 census: 'Peder Olsen, b. 1854, s ug hjelper faderen, smed' - an unmarried son helping his father, and a smith, in the same household at Slinden. He is one of the three smiths of Slinden nordre recorded in 1891. ¶1

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
