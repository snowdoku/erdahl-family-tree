About the family

Son's marriage: The 1828 marriage index for his son Peter Olsen names the groom's father as 'Ole Pedersen'. ¶3

Parents linked at Probable: Peder Pedersen and Mali Olsdatter, the retired couple on Valstad Lille in 1801. Probable, not proved (orchestrator, line 8): in the 1801 census Ole Pedersen (43) is the farmer of Valstad Lille (household 01), and the only other household on the farm (02) is that of the retired farmer (kaarmand) Peder Pedersen, 78, and Mali Olsdatter, 68, both in their first marriage, with children Mali (30), Anne Juditha (27) and Peter (24). At his marriage in 1791 Ole was called 'Odelsbaaren til Gaarden Walstad' (heir by birthright to Walstad), so his own family held the farm; a retired couple on the farm the son works is the classic father-to-son handover. Ole named his first son Peter and a daughter Mali, as the naming custom would predict for parents Peder and Mali. 'Peder Lille Walstad' had a daughter Ellen baptised 8 Jan 1758 and a Peder Walstad a son Halvor 24 Apr 1757 (Skatval); Ole's own baptism (about 1757-58 by his ages) has not been found in 16371 c13-c30, so his birth year may be off by one or two years. Peder's wife Mali Olsdatter could in principle be Malena Pedersdatter's (Ole's wife's) mother instead, but Malena was 'of Hegge' at marriage and the odel right ties the farm to Ole's line. No record states the relationship. ¶8

Life story

Baptism: 'Døbt 1 Peder Lille Walstads Barn Ole' at Skatvold church on the 1st Sunday after Epiphany, 8 Jan 1758 (16371 p. 25 no. 1, canvas c27). Godparents: Gunu[?] Wold, Kirsten Hougstad[?], [illegible] Jon Wold, Peder Hougstad[?], Engebrigt Wold. His mother was churched on 26 Feb 1758. The date fits both his ages exactly (43 at the census of 1 Feb 1801; 74 at death 13 Feb 1832). ¶9

Marriage: 'Ungkarl Ole Pedersen Walstad' was betrothed to 'Pigen Malena Pedersd. Hegge' on about 1 October 1791 and married her on 24 October 1791 (Stjørdal 1781-1797, marriages 1791 p. 560 no. 43). The sureties were Ole and Lars Wold. The entry calls him 'Odelsbaaren til Gaarden Walstad', the heir by odel right to the farm Walstad, which implies that his father owned it. The rest of that clause is only partly legible. ¶5

1801 census: Ole Pedersen, 43, is head of household 01 at Valstad Lille, Størdalen, as 'Huusbonde' and 'Bonde og gaardbeboer' (farmer and farm occupier), in his first marriage. The household is his wife Malena Pedersdatter, 31, and their children Peter (9), Malena (6), Mali (3) and Peder (1). Two other households lived on the farm. One is the retired couple Peder Pedersen, 78, kaarmand (living on a kår, a retirement agreement), and Mali Olsdatter, 68, with three unmarried children. The other is Peder Pedersen, 35, husmand med jord (a cottager with land), with his family. Ole's patronymic, Pedersen, and the name of his first son, Peter, would fit his being a son of the elderly Peder Pedersen, but no record read here says so. ¶2

Death: Ole Petersen, gaardmand (farm owner) of Lille Hvalstad, died on 13 February 1832 and was buried on 27 February 1832, aged 74, born 1758 (Stjørdal 1820-1832, burials p. 641 no. 32). No cause or remark is recorded. The age agrees with the 1801 census (43, born about 1758). ¶4

The case for this identification 1

Identity: Ole Pedersen (also written Petersen) was the farmer of Lille Hvalstad (Valstad Lille) in Skatval, Nedre Stjørdal. He was the father of Peter Olsen Hvalstad. His farm is the one his son carried as a byname and that his grandchildren took to Dakota as 'Walstad'. ¶1

Corrections 1

Correction: and the line-8 orchestrator first read the child's name on the scan as 'Elñ' (Ellen). In the Gothic (Kurrent) hand a lowercase e looks like an n; the Digitalarkivet index reads 'Ole', and the age fit makes Ole certain. There was no daughter Ellen in this entry. ¶10

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Possible father and sisters: Because he was odel heir to Walstad, his father was probably the Peder Pedersen, 78, kaarmand at Lille Valstad in 1801. A Peder Walstad was a godparent of Ole's son Peter in 1791. Three Pedersdatter women of (Lille) Walstad married in these years: Mali (12 April 1787, to Ole Jonsen Wald), Ane (1789, to Bjørn Pedersen Hegge) and Karen (18 November 1790, to Lars Halvorsen Wold, with Peder Walstad as surety). They may be his sisters. This is inferred and not stated in any record read. ¶6

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Lead (not proof) on his father: a Peder Walstad had a child, read as Halvor, baptised at Skatval on Misericordias Sunday (24 April) 1757; the godparents were Kirsten Hegge, Karen Walstad, Ole and Peder Walstad and Peder Haugsted[?] (Stjørdal 1755-1780, canvas 19). Peder Walstad's wife was churched on 30 May 1757. If this Peder was Ole's father, Ole was not born in spring 1757, so his census and burial ages (born about 1757-58) would be a year or more off. Ole's own baptism has not yet been found; the register for 1757-59 is still being read. ¶7
