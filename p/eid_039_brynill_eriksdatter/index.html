Life story

Married Peder (Per) Evensen on the 3rd Sunday after Trinity, 11 June 1758, after betrothal on 2 April 1758 (Selbu ministerialbok 1757-1801, canvas 6). Named as mother of Even (23 September 1759), Erik (1761) and Mali (1764), all to 'Per Evens. Garberg' (canvases 8, 11 and 15, the 1759 and marriage entries). ¶1

Identity lead tested and refuted (overnight cross-line test). Graded on her link to Even Pederssen (see his page), so her grade is unchanged and no parents are set. The rival 'Brynill Erichsd.', wife of Bersvend Pedersen of Slinden, did not die before June 1758: her probate was held on 11 Jun 1762 (Stjør- og Verdal skifteprotokoll, Digitalarkivet 24755, fol. 241b, view 258; 'efter afg. Brønild Erichsdatter', husband Bersvend Pedersen, Slinde). It names three children, Peder Bersvendsen 9, Mali and Guri. The guardian of the daughter Mali was 'Svend Ericksen Uglen', that is Svend Eriksen, aged 10 in his father Erik Svendsen Uglem's probate of 1741 and still at Uglem 761 with his widowed mother in 1762. The word for the relationship is not read with certainty. So two Brynild Eriksdatters were married in Selbu in 1758: the Slinden wife, dead by 1762, and this woman, alive at Uglem in 1769. The 1741 Uglem probate lists only one Brynild, aged 13, and she fits the Slinden wife, whose brother was her daughter's guardian. Bersvend Slinden's sponsorship at Torsten Eriksen Uglem's son's baptism in 1770 fits that too. This Brynill's father is therefore an Erik other than Erik Svendsen Uglem; her baptism (about 1725-1732) and her father are still open. Not read: the 1769 Uglem deed (Strinda og Selbu pantebok) and any probate after Guru Torstensdatter. No Uglem probate for her appears in the typed Stjør- og Verdal registers 24753-24765. ¶8

How sure are we?

Confirmed. A record that names this person and this family link has been read.

1 on the grade · 1 record that disagrees · 3 alternatives set aside · 1 open question

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Confidence raised from Probable to Confirmed: she is named as mother, with 'Peder Evens: Ugl:', at the baptism of Jon on Ascension Day, 4 May 1769 (Selbu ministerialbok 1757-1801, canvas 31 right page), which places the Garberg couple of 1758-64 at Uglem, the farm of her son Even Pedersen from 1784 to his death in 1812. See Per (Peder) Evensen for the full argument. This also answers part of the bygdebok contradiction: in 1769 Peder's wife was still Brynild Eriksdatter, so the book's 'Mali, b. 1728' can at most be a later wife; Brynild's burial is still not found. ¶5

Records that disagree 1

Contradiction: the bygdebok (vol. 4 p. 76, word boxes only) gives Per Evensen's wife as 'Mali, b. 1728'. The register is preferred for Even's mother; see the note on Per. Her birth and death are not found. ¶4

Alternatives considered and set aside 3

A namesake: a 'Brynill Erichsd.' was the wife of Bersvend Pedersen of Slinden at the baptism of their daughter Guri on the 9th Sunday after Trinity 1757 (canvas 4 right page). Whether that woman was widowed and remarried Per Evensen in 1758 is not known; the 1758 entry does not call the bride a widow. ¶3

Not alive in 1801: her husband Per (Peder) Evensen is a widower 'after his second marriage' in the 1801 census, so she - if his second wife - died before Feb 1801; no burial of a Brynhild Eriksdatter was found in 16311 burials 1759-1801 (all read). Possibly recorded without patronymic or farm. ¶6

Cross-record identity lead, Speculative, no link made. She may be Brynild Eriksdatter, aged 13, daughter of Erik Svendsen Uglem (see his page) and Guru Torstensdatter in his probate of 20 Jun 1741 (card 77804 c2055), and so a sister of the line 7 ancestor Torsten Eriksen Uglem (see his page). That Brynild is very probably the 'Erich Uglems Brønild' baptised at Michaelmas 1727 (Selbu 16310 canvas 109 R; card), the only Erik-to-Brynhild baptism in Selbu from Jan 1727 to Jun 1729. For the identification: (1) the age fits a bride of 1758 (about 30); (2) she and Per Evensen named their second son Erik (1761), which fits a father Erik; (3) the couple moved from Garberg to Uglem by 1769 (baptism of Jon, 4 May 1769), and the bygdebok has Per as holder on Uglan from 1769, which could have come through his wife's share of Erik Svendsen's 12 marklag of jordegods in Uglen (an inference). Against it, and why no link is made: a rival candidate exists. A second 'Brynill Erichsd.' was the wife of Bersvend Pedersen of Slinden at their daughter Guri's baptism in Aug 1757 (16311 c4 R), and Bersvend Slinden stood sponsor at the baptism of Torsten Eriksen Uglem's son Ole on 5 Jun 1770 (16311 c39 L), which is as consistent with his wife being Torsten's sister. No record read names this Brynill as Erik's daughter. ¶7a

Open questions and the next record to read 1

What would settle it: a probate or land transfer that names Per Evensen's wife as an Uglem heir (the 1769 Uglem deed in the Strinda og Selbu pantebok, or a probate after Guru Torstensdatter), or proof that Bersvend Slinden's wife Brynill lived on after June 1758. ¶7b
