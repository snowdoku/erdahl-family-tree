Life story

Engagement: he was engaged at Borgund on 14 October 1759 (18th Sunday after Trinity) as a bachelor of Lille Nørve. The sponsors (forlovere) were Jon Larsen of Lille Nørve, who may be a relative or an older resident of the farm, and Aslak Ingebrigtsen of Godøya. The wedding entry of 1 January 1760 (New Year's Day) names no sponsors. Both entries were read on the page images (Borgund ministerialbok 1739-1761, canvases 39 and 64). Their first child, Karen, was baptised on 1 February 1761 (index). ¶7

Baptism found (scan read): Borgund ministerialbok 1698-1739 (15995), canvas c195, under the heading 1735: 'Festo Novi anni dobt Olle Olsøn Lille Nørves Søn N. Lars', baptised New Year's Day (1 Jan) 1735. Godparents: Ole Knudsen, Jon Larsen, Andreas Iversen[?] and Karen Arentsdatter[?] (who carried the child), all of Lille Nørve, and Synnøve Gundersdatter of Godøy. The mother is not named. Identification with the groom of 1760 is Probable, not proved: same farm, and the age of 84 at burial in Aug 1818 (66 in 1801, 81 in 1815) gives birth about 1734. The godfather Jon Larsen of Lille Nørve is very likely the Jon Larsen Lille Nørve who was Lars's engagement sponsor (forlover) in 1759, which supports it. This also replaces the earlier note that his baptism is not in the index (index pd00000001508627). Audit (crop re-read): the reading is confirmed. Caution on the year: the entry sits directly under a '1735' heading, but the weekdays on the page fit Dec 1735 and Jan 1736, not Dec 1734 and Jan 1735. The next entry is 'Tiisdagen d. 3 Januari', and 3 Jan was a Tuesday in 1736 but a Monday in 1735; the December entries before the heading also fit 1735. So the baptism may be 1 Jan 1736 and the heading the clerk's slip. Birth is therefore given as ABT 1735. Either year fits his ages, which are about a year apart anyway (84 at burial 1818, 66 in 1801, 81 in 1815). Check the index entry pd00000001508627 for the date it assigns. ¶8

As 'Lars Olss. Ervig', a bachelor of Lille Nørve, he married Sidsele (Cæcilia) Andersdatter, a maid of Hessen, 1 Jan 1760 at Borgund. 'Ervig' is probably his birth farm. His baptism (about 1734) is not in the index. ¶2

'Ervig', the farm he is said to have come from in his 1760 marriage entry, is not a Borgund place name as far as the index goes: every record indexed under the residence Ervik or Ervig in western Norway belongs to Davik, Selje or Eid in Nordfjord, or to Aure and Frøya in Nordmøre, and none is a Lars Olsen born about 1734. Either the clerk's 'Ervig' is a small Borgund holding too minor to be indexed, or Lars came from outside the parish. ¶4

His baptism is not in the Borgund index, which is consistent with either explanation. The next source is the 1762 extra-tax list (ekstraskatt) for Nørve, which gives household members and sometimes their origins, and then the page image of the 1760 marriage entry. ¶5

1762 extra tax, Borgund, Lille Nørve household 4 (49637 view 37): 'M: Lars Olsen, H: Sidsle Andersdatter'; in the column for other persons supported: '2de Søster: Synneve og Birthe Olsdatt.' (his two sisters Synneve and Birthe Olsdatter); in the next column: 'Hans Forældre: F: Ole Olsen, M: Elen Ingebrigtsdatt.' (his parents: father Ole Olsen, mother Elen Ingebrigtsdatter); with a servant Hans Andersen Wolsdal; 7 persons taxed. This gives his mother's name, Elen Ingebrigtsdatter, and two sisters. His own grade (on his link to his son Knud) is unchanged. ¶12

Farmer at Nørve Lille in the 1801 census, age 66, in his first marriage. Children at home: Knud (30), Ole Andreas (18), Anders (12) and Cæcilia (21). ¶1

In the 1815 census he is 81, a retired farmer (kårmann) at Lille Nørve in his son Knud's household. Died 24 Aug 1818, age 84; buried 30 Aug 1818. ¶3

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: Confirmed, graded on his link to his son Knud Larsen Lille Nørve (see his page), not on his own parentage (Probable). The record naming the relationship is the 1801 census of Nørve Lille, Borgund (household 04, read on the index person pages): no. 019 Lars Olesen, 66, 'Husbonde', 'Bægges første ægteskab' (first marriage of both); no. 020 Cæcilia Andersdatter, 62, 'Hans kone'; no. 021 Knud Larssen, 30, 'Deres børn' (their children), unmarried; then Ole Andreas (18), Anders (12) and Cæcilia (21), all 'Deres børn'. Continuity: in the 1815 census Lars, 81, is a retired farmer (kårmann) in his son Knud's household on the same farm, and Cæcilia's probate of 21 Jul 1821 names as her first heir 'Knud Larsen, married, farmer at Lille Nørve'. ¶11a

Corrections 1

Correction: the Digitalarkivet index gives the groom of 1 January 1760 as 'Lars Olss. Ervig'. On the page image the wedding entry reads 'copulatus ungkarl Lars Olsøn Lille Nørve med Pigen Sidsele Andersdatter Hessen' (married the bachelor Lars Olsen of Lille Nørve to the maid Sidsele Andersdatter of Hessen). The engagement entry of 14 October 1759 reads 'Lars Olsen Lille Nørve'. Neither entry contains the word Ervig, so Ervig should not be treated as his birth farm. Possibly the indexer added it. As a lead only: the place Urvig appears as a Borgund residence on the 1759 marriage pages of the same book. ¶6

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

No rival: the other Knud Larssen on the farm in 1801 (household 01) was 75, too old to be the tree's Knud (born about 1771). ¶11b

Searches that found nothing 2

Negative: the Sunnmøre probate index has no Ole Olsen with farm Nørve 1736-1800. Two Lille Nørve probates were read on the scan and do not concern his parents: Bjørn Olsen, 11 Jan 1752 (3A 17 fol. 252b; widow Ragnhild Iversdatter, children Ole Bjørnsen and Marthe Bjørnsdatter), and Brynhild Olsdatter, 4 Feb 1773 (3A 24 fol. 608b; widower Ole Samundsen, heirs her siblings). In 1773 an Ole Olsen and a Lars Olsen of Lille Nørve appear in the second entry only as appraiser and debtor. ¶9

Negative: the Borgund churchings (introduserte kvinner) in ministerialbok 15995 were read on the scan for 1 Jan to about 10 Apr 1735 (canvas 233, left page) and 1 Jan to about June 1736 (canvas 233 right and canvas 234). No woman of Lille Nørve was churched in either window, so the churching does not name Lars's mother. The only Nørve woman, 'Magnille Jacobsdatter Store Nørve' (Maundy Thursday 1735), was the wife of Arne Iversen of Store Nørve (Sunnmøre probate index 1744, pa00000000779888), so she is not Ole Olsen's wife. Canvas map: c232 = late 1733 to Dec 1734; c233 = 1735 to early 1736; c234 = Lent to summer 1736. ¶10
