Life story

Probate: 'Anno 1691 d. 8 Junij' an inheritance skifte was held at Mebust in Selbu after the late Oluf Jonsen, 'som boede og døde paa bemt. Gaard', between his widow Gunnild Barroesdatter (Bårdsdatter) and their children Baaror (Bård), Joen and Oluf Olsønner and Ingeborr Olsdatter, all minors ('Umyndige'); guardians Bersvend Lille Qvien and Olluf Kolset (24749 fol. 14-15b; the 77804 card 1309 abstract agrees). Birth before about 1665 is inferred from four minor children in 1691. ¶1

1701 manntall (38461): the Mebust holding (written 'Mejum', c112) had Peder Olsen, 47, with no sons; an inderst Oller Pedersen, 90, is entered under 'Mejum' among the cottagers (c127). Neither is tied to Oluf Jonsen's family by the manntall, which lists males only. ¶4

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the grade · 2 alternatives set aside · 1 open question

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Graded Probable on his link to Ingeborg Olsdatter, widow of Erik Olsen of Øvre Haarstad (see her page). The 1691 skifte names a minor daughter Ingeborg Olsdatter, which fits the 'Ingebor Olsd. Mebust' buried 22 Jun 1760 aged 70 (born about 1690). The identification with the Haarstad widow of 1742 rests on: (1) the 1742 Haarstad skifte (24752 fol. 1116b) chose as guardians for her children 'Moderens Søskende Barn Ole Pedersen Kolset og Torgier Mebust', that is, children of her siblings, one of them living at Mebust; (2) the 1760 burial places an Ingeborg Olsdatter of the right age at Mebust after she had left Øvre Haarstad (absent there in the 1762 ekstraskatt); (3) the 1691 guardian 'Olluf Kolset' ties this family to Kolset as well as Mebust, matching the two farms of the 1742 guardians. ¶2a

Alternatives considered and set aside 2

Not Confirmed: no record names the 1742 widow as Oluf Jonsen's daughter, and the identification spans 51 years. ¶2b

Rival: none found; no other Ingeborg Olsdatter of Mebust is known. ¶2c

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Test record: a probate after Ingebor Olsdatter, Mebust, about 1760 (not in the typed registers of 24753/24755 under Mebust), or a deed or panteregister entry for Mebust naming Erik Haarstad's widow or children as heirs of Oluf Jonsen's line; also the 1742 skifte after Peder Olsen Mebust and the 1745 skifte after Torger Olsen Mebust (24753 fol. 1b, 114) read in full for a sister Ingeborg. If Torger Olsen Mebust (d. 1745) was the 1742 guardian, the literal reading 'the mother's siblings' child' makes him a son of her brother Oluf Olufsen; that is untested. ¶3
