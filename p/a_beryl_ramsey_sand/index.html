About the family

Eldest child of Theodore Ulysses Ramsey and Wilma Almira Nelson, and the elder sister of Wava Lorene Valder. ¶1

Children: Carole; Linnea, later Praml, of Los Angeles; Wanda, who married Peter Tweed of Ossian, Iowa; and Mark Sand, who was a pallbearer at his grandmother's funeral in 1992. ¶5

Life story

Her birth is fixed by a contemporary notice in the Lake Mills Graphic of 6 February 1918: "Mr and Mrs Theodore Ramsey are the proud parents of a baby girl born Saturday" — the Saturday before that issue was 2 February 1918. She was baptised at Beaver Creek Lutheran Church, where the register records "Beryl Phillis - 1918 - Theodor Ramsey / Wilma Nelson". ¶2

In the 1940 census she is 22, single, and working as a rural school teacher while still living at home. ¶3

She married Rev. Lloyd Vernon Sand (1906-2001). They served as Lutheran missionaries in Cameroun, at Djohong and Meiganga, and afterwards lived at Forest City and then Lake Mills, Iowa. Her mother's 1992 obituary lists her as "Beryl and Rev. Lloyd Sand" of Lake Mills. ¶4

She was a bridesmaid at her sister Wava's wedding to Palmer Valder on 14 February 1942. She died aged 97. ¶6
