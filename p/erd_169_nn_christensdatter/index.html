About the family

Naming evidence: Leikanger ministerialbok 11564, p. 348 (canvas 165 R), 1731, burial column about Lent: 'Christen Larsen Eitungierde 10 dage', an infant son of Lars Eitungjerde named Christen. With the daughter Christi (minor 1758), this fits a wife who was a Christensdatter in 1731, i.e. the woman betrothed to Lars in Oct 1724 (that betrothal is on p. 322, which is 1724: 'Die Veneris d. 21 Julij' on the same page fits 1724 only). Marcus Larsen's baptism was not found in 1724-1734 (pp. 322-353), so whether she was Marcus's mother is still open. Stays Speculative. ¶2

Life story

Betrothed to Lars Eitungjerde (Lars Marcussen, b. about 1691) at Tjugum about October 1724: 'Sponsalia Lars Eitungierde / N. Christensd.' (Leikanger ministerialbok 1690-1734, canvas 137 R, p. 322). Her given name is not written. Speculative as the mother of Marcus Larsen Thue (see his page), whom Lars's probate of 1758 calls his eldest son: no earlier marriage of Lars is known, Marcus married in 1751, and Lars's youngest daughter, a minor in 1758, was named Christi, as if after this wife's father Christen. ¶1a

A second son named Christen: Leikanger ministerialbok 11564, p. 351 (canvas 168 R), 1733, baptism about Feb 1733: 'Christen Lars. Eitungierde', sponsors Toller Botun and Mons Eitun. Two sons named Christen (1731, died at 10 days, and 1733) make it near certain that Lars's wife in 1731-33 was a daughter of a Christen. The open question remains whether she, or an earlier wife, bore Marcus. ¶3

Death bracket: Lars Marcussen remarried in autumn 1733 - canvas 169 R, Sponsalia 'Lars Marc. Eitun / Unni Biaastad' and Nupt. 'Lars Eitungierd / Unni Biaastad' - so the Christensdatter wife died between the baptism of the second Christen (Feb 1733) and autumn 1733. Her burial was not picked out in the 1733 list (burials there give given name and farm only). The daughter Christi (minor in 1758) is more likely a child of the second wife Unni, so the Christi naming argument in the first note is weak. ¶4

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the grade · 1 correction · 2 alternatives set aside

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: Speculative to Probable as mother of Marcus Thue. With the betrothal moved to Oct 1719, Lars Marcussen (b. about 1691) married the Christensdatter at about 28; his eldest son Marcus (named 'Ældste Søn' in Lars's probate of 1758, married 1751) and the daughter Brite (buried Jan 1734 aged 10, so b. about 1723) fall in 1720-1724, inside this marriage. The sons Christen (1731, 1733) show the wife was still this Christensdatter then. No earlier marriage of a Lars Eitungjerde was found in 11564 for 1715 to Sept 1718 (canvases 115-136 at 2800 px; the 1715-17 hand is difficult) and there is a register gap Sept 1718 to Mar 1719. ¶6a

Corrections 1

Correction: the betrothal 'Sponsalia Lars Eitungierde / N. Christensd.', Dom. XVII p. Trin. at Tjugum, sureties Siur Vestrez and Erich Eitun (11564 p. 322, canvas 137 R), is of October 1719, not 1724. Reasons: (1) p. 321 (canvas 136 R) is headed 1719, begins when the new priest arrived at Laetare 1719 after a gap from Sept 1718, and ends with entries 'ante Dom. Palmarum'; p. 322 (canvas 137 L) opens 'Dnca Palmarum' in the same hand and runs on to Dom. XVII p. Trin.; (2) 'Die Veneris d. 21 Julij' on p. 322 fits 1719 as well as 1724 (21 Jul 1719 was a Friday); (3) 'Siur Sværens, 70 aar' buried on p. 322 fits Siver Marchusen, b. 1650 by the 1701 census, in 1719-20 better than 1724; (4) canvas 138 L, which follows to Advent of the same year, holds the betrothals 'Elling Erichsøn Grøneng / Brite Nilsd. Tiugum' and 'Christen Grinde / N. Sylfestdatter', whose marriages are entered in 1721 (canvases 149 L-150 L), so that page must be before 1721; (5) the year 1724 is entered separately on pp. 341-343 (canvases 156-157). The heading 'Anno 172[5]' on p. 323 with the note 'refer ad pag. 341 ubi hic annus omissus' marks where the priest resumed after leaving 1720-1724 to be entered later. ¶5

Alternatives considered and set aside 2

Not proved: Marcus's baptism was not found in 1725-1727 (canvases 138-143) and no record names his mother. She was probably dead by 1758, when Lars's probate names no widow (inferred from the earlier reading of that probate). ¶1b

Not Confirmed: Marcus's baptism is still unfound (1720 to Jul 1721 on canvases 143 R-148 was not; 1722-23 are sparse), and no record names his mother. ¶6b
