About the family

Children: Svend 1808, Mali 1810 (named after his mother Mali Larsdatter), Lisbet 1813, Eli 1816 and Sigri 1821 (at Beitland, then Almlitrøen). Buried 27 Mar 1830, a cottager of Almliplads, born 1782. ¶4

Parents' marriage and his elder brothers: Erich Hansen, a bachelor drummer living at Christlock, married Mali Larsdatter at Hegre on 5 April 1774 (Stjørdal ministerialbok 1755-1780, marriage section p. 376). Their sons Hans (bapt. 15 Jan 1775) and Lars (bapt. 29 Sep 1776) were baptised as children of 'Eric, Christloch'. The first Lars apparently died in 1777 (Stjørdalsboka V 1), and the name was given again to this Lars, baptised in February 1782 as son of 'Erich, Schielstadgierdet'. The repeated name supports the identification with Erik Hansen and Mali Larsdatter, as does the name Mali he gave his own daughter in 1810. His confidence stays Probable: no record read yet names both him and his parents together (his 1797 confirmation at Værnes, 1 Oct 1797, enters him only as 'Lars Erichsen, Almogjerdet', p. 12 no. 24). ¶10

Life story

Husmann (cottager) at Almlitrøen, Hegra, 1810s. ¶1

Baptized 24 Feb 1782, son of Erich of Skjelstadgjerdet. 1801 census: 'Lars Erichsen', 19, a servant at Almoen (identification probable). ¶2

A different Erich Hansen, an unmarried soldier, married Margrete Kjelsdatter of Stoken on 17 June 1784; that man is not this Lars's father, whose marriage to Mali Larsdatter is still unlocated. ¶8

Confirmation checked, no upgrade: 'Lars Erichsen, Almogjerdet' was confirmed at Værnes on 1 Oct 1797 (Stjørdal ministerialbok 1797-1815, 1714Q, Digitalarkivet source 16373, p. 12 no. 24; index and scan, canvas 10, read). The 1797 Stjørdal list gives name and farm only, with no parents, age or birth date, so it cannot tie him to Erik Hansen and Mali Larsdatter. It does place him at Almo(en) at 15, where he was a servant in 1801. The Stjørdal confirmation lists of these years generally carry no parents, so this method will not settle Stjørdal or Hegra links before the printed-form registers. ¶13

His 1801 identification is now firm rather than guessed: the full 1801 household listing for Skjelstad shows his father Erik Hansen, 54, as 'Huusmand med jord og skomager' - a cottager with land and a shoemaker - with his wife Mali Larsdatter, 50, and their daughter Mali, 6, while the Almoen listing of the same census has 'Lars Erichsen', 19, serving as 'Tiener Ugift' in the household of Anders Larsen. Almoen and Skjelstad are neighbouring holdings in Hegra, and a 19-year-old cottager's son in service on the next farm is exactly what one expects. ¶5

A dragoon (cavalry soldier). On 9 Oct 1808 a son Svend was baptized: 'Lars Eriksen, bachelor, dragoon, of Rolseng' and 'Lisbet Joensd., maid, of Storkleven', the first out-of-wedlock child for both. As a bachelor dragoon of Skjelstad he married Lisbeth Joensdatter of Storkleven on 14 Nov 1808. His father Erik Hansen died in 1806 at Rolseng-gjerdet, which matches both places. ¶3

Marriage, 14 Nov 1808, read from the scan (Stjørdal ministerialbok 1797-1815, Digitalarkivet 16373, p. 654 no. 50): 'Ungkarl og Dragon Lars Erichsen Schielstad med Pigen Lisbeth Joensdatter Storkleven. Caverer Erik Schielstad og Hans Boraasen.' The surety Erik Schielstad cannot be his father, who was buried in 1806. He is evidently the farmer Erich Joensen of Skjelstad, under whose farm the family's cottage lay. ¶12

Probate of his mother-in-law, Dec 1810 (Stjør- og Verdal skifteprotokoll 19, s. 248): his wife is heir no. 6, 'Lisbeth Joensdatter, gift med Huusmand Lars Erichsen Kleven' - in 1810 he was a cottager, apparently living on or under Kleven. His own confidence is unchanged (his link to Erik Hansen rests on the 1782 baptism, which names only 'Erich of Skjelstadgjerdet'). ¶9

Two Eriks at Skjelstad: the indexed Stjørdal baptisms keep the farm and the cottage holding apart. 'Erich, Schielstad' (the farm) is father on 24 Apr 1768, 16 Jun 1771, 31 Mar 1774 and 19 Mar 1780. He is the farmer Erich Joensen, b. 1744, of the 1801 census, whose children there are Joen 1774, Ingeborg 1778 and Anne 1780. 'Erich, Schielstadgierdet' (the cottage) is father only on 24 Feb 1782 (this Lars) and 7 Nov 1794 ('Erich Hanssen', with Mali Larsdatter). So the 1782 entry belongs to the cottager Erik Hansen, not to the farmer, and that strengthens the link. Confidence stays Probable, because no record yet names Lars together with Erik Hansen and Mali Larsdatter. ¶11

At his daughter Eli's baptism in October 1816 he is entered as 'Husm: Lars Erichsen Aemlitrøen' - crofter at Almlitrøen. The godparents were Mali Fossen, Sigri Fossen, Marit Almli, Ole Almli and Ole Fossen. ¶6

Buried 27 March 1830, entered as a husmand of Almliplads, born 1782. He was 48 and left a widow with young children. ¶7

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: graded on his link to his daughter Eli Larsdatter (Eli Larsdatter, b. 16 Oct 1816). Her baptism, Hegra 27 Oct 1816 (Stjørdal parish, Hegra local parish, ministerialbok 1815-1819, p. 69 no. 45; Digitalarkivet index pd00000013102098), names the parents as 'Lars Eriksen, husmand, Almlitrøen' and 'Lisbet Johnsdatter, Almlitrøen'. Continuity to the tree person: the same Lars Erichsen married Lisbeth Joensdatter of Storkleven on 14 Nov 1808 (16373 p. 654 no. 50), is father with her of Svend 1808 and Mali 1810, is 'husmand' at Almliplads/Almlitrøen in 1816 and was buried from Almliplads on 27 Mar 1830 aged 48 (born 1782); no second Lars Eriksen married to a Lisbet Johnsdatter appears at Almli. This grade covers only his link to Eli and Mali. His own link upward to Erik Hansen and Mali Larsdatter is graded on their records and is still Probable (the 1782 baptism names only 'Erich, Schielstadgierdet'). ¶14
