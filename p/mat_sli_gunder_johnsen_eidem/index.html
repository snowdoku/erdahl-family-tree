About the family

Named as the bride's father in the Selbu marriage entry of 24 May 1841, when his daughter Berith Gundersdatter Eidem, born 1816, married Ole Gundersen Slinden. He is therefore an Eidem of Selbu living in the first half of the nineteenth century, and the ancestor through whom Eidem blood entered the Slind family two generations before Oline Slind married John P. Eidem in Minnesota. ¶1

His wife was BRYNHILD ERIKSDATTER, born about 1797, eleven years his junior, and two of her younger siblings were in service in the same household. His four daughters in 1825 were Beret about 1815, Maglie about 1816, Sigrid about 1819 and Marit about 1823. ¶9

Mother - two versions: his baptism names Elen Gundersdatter, the 1801 census and the 1789 baptism of his sister Brynil name Siri (Segri) Gundersdatter as Jon Mikkelsen's wife. The tree links Siri at Probable and keeps Elen as this note; Elen Gundersdatter, 54 in 1801, was the wife of Ole Olssen, husmann on the same farm, and is not entered as a person. The reasons are in the note on Siri (Sigri) Gundersdatter. ¶18

His mother's probate: 24744 image 235, fol. 778, Moen, Selbu, settled 5 October 1821, after 'Kaarkone Sigrid Gundersdatter': among the heirs is 'Sønnen Gunder Johnsen, myndig', and 'Gunder Johnsen Eidum' was appointed guardian of the minor grandchildren. His father, the widower John Mikkelsen, was alive. This primary record names Siri (Sigrid) Gundersdatter as his mother and resolves the conflict with his 1786 baptism, which names the mother 'Elen Gund.'. His siblings were Peder Johnsen, Ragnild Johnsdatter and Brynhild Johnsdatter (both married by 1821) and the late Mikkel Jonsen. ¶26

Life story

Printed source, and the page that should finally give him dates and a father: he is on printed page 203 of Selbu i fortid og naatid, bind 4 (1992), the last full page of the chapter EIDEM (YSTER-EIDEM) which runs pp. 184-204. The 1992 volume modernises his name to GUNNAR JONSEN, and the page carries Gunnar, Jonsen, Gunnarsen (his sons' patronymic), Jon twice, Berit twice, Ola twice, Olsen twice, the farm names Slind and Eidem, his daughter's two recorded birth years 1815 and 1816, her marriage year 1841, his grandson Gunder Olsen Slind's birth year 1844, and three holder tenures - 1785-1826, 1815-1838 and 1839-1874. The tenure 1815-1838 is almost certainly his own and 1785-1826 his father's. Since a gaardshistorie holder entry names the holder's own parents, this one page should supply his birth year, his death year, his wife, his children and his father - everything he presently lacks. ¶4a

THE SPELLING TRAP that hid him, recorded because it will hide others: the 1992 bygdebok writes Gunnar, not Gunder; Gunnarsen, not Gundersen; Jonsen, not Johnsen. The phrase 'Gunder Johnsen' has ZERO hits in the whole of volume 4, and the word Gunder, which has 31 hits in the volume, does not occur on page 203 at all. A search of the printed farm history on the spellings the 1841 parish register uses returns nothing and looks like proof that he is absent from it. ¶5

He was born about 1786 and was an INDERST - a landless lodger or sub-tenant - in another family's house at Eidem in 1825, not a farmer. This is worth stating plainly because the farm name Eidem in this tree has been assumed to imply standing on the farm, and in his case it implies only residence on it. ¶8

Birth year: 1786, from the bygdebok Selbu i fortid og naatid vol. 4 p. 203, holder block '1815-1838 Gunnar Jonsen', which carries '1786' and '1844' on the same line (read as born 1786, died 1844). This is a reconstruction from nb.no word boxes, not a reading of the page, so it is Probable only; it agrees with the tree's ABT 1786. ¶12

Baptism (Probable identification): 15th Sunday after Trinity, 24 September 1786, 'Döbt Gunder. Par: Jon Mich: Moen, Elen Gund: T. O. Moen, Hans Efj:, Michel Velve, Karen Sessaas, Guri Moen' - baptised Gunder, parents Jon Michelsen of Moen and Elen Gundersdatter; godparents O. Moen, Hans Evjen, Michel Velve, Karen Sessaas and Guri Moen (Selbu ministerialbok 1757-1801, canvas 123 right page, crop). Birth replaced 'ABT 1786, Selbu' with 'before 24 September 1786, Moen'. For why this Gunder is the Eidem man, and why the mother is entered as Siri, see Jon Mikkelsen Moen and Siri (Sigri) Gundersdatter. ¶15

1801 census: Selbu, rural residence 0018 Moen, household 01 - 'Gunder Jonssen, 15, Deres børn', son of the householder Jon Mikkelssen, 56, and his wife Siri Gundersdatter, 44, both in their first marriage; his siblings in the household are Rangnil 22, Mikkel 19, Brynild 11, Jon 8 and Peder 4. He is one of only two Gunder Jon*sens in the 1801 Selbu index; the other was born in 1780 at Overvik to Jon Nielssen. ¶17

Marriage: 11 June 1815, Selbu, to Brynhild Eriksdatter of Uglem; he is 'unge Mand Gunder Johns: Bromman' - an unmarried young man of Bromman (index: Brennan). The bygdebok's tenure dates 1815-1838 begin in the year of this marriage. ¶13

Death: before December 1865, when his wife Brynhild Eriksdatter is entered as a widow in their son Ole Gundersen's household at Grindhaugen. The bygdebok's 1844 is unverified. ¶14

At his daughter Bereth's home baptism (b. 15 Sep 1815; Selbu 16312 p. 222, read by the orchestrator) he is 'Gunder Johns. Brennan', and the first godfather is 'Michel Johns. Moen' - his brother Mikkel, a son of Jon Mikkelsen Moen (1801 census) - supporting his parentage. ¶25

Baptism of his son John (scan read by the orchestrator): Selbu ministerialbok 1824-1842 (source 16314), p. 115, baptisms 1835 no. 48 - 'John Gundersen Eidem', born 25 April, baptised 8 June 1835, legitimate, parents 'Hmd. Gunder Johnsen og Brygnild Eriksdatter' (husmann Gunder Johnsen and Brynhild Eriksdatter). The Digitalarkivet index reads the mother as 'Ragnild Eriksdatter'; the scan shows Brygnild, so the index is wrong and this John is a son of our couple, following Ole (1826) and two daughters named Brygnild (1830, 1832), all baptised at Eidem. ¶20

1865 census, a different widow: the Sletne household (Selbu, rural residence 0041, household 01, person 010) contains a Brynhild Eriksdatter, 70, widow, 'hans Moder' - mother of the farmer Ole Arntsen, 46. Her son's patronymic points to a husband named Arnt (inference), and Gunder's son Ole Gundersen is not in that household. Gunder's widow is the Brynhild Eriksdatter, 69, at Grindhaugen with her son Ole Gundersen, already noted; the Sletne woman is not evidence about Gunder. ¶24

His own patronymic makes his father a JOHN. No son is recorded for him in 1825, so the Eidem name did not pass down from him in the male line. ¶10

Burial (scan read by the orchestrator): Selbu klokkerbok 1843-1859 (Digitalarkivet source 15709), p. 360, burials 1845 no. 3 - 'Gunder Johnsen Uglum, Hmd.', that is a husmann (cottager), aged 60, residence Uglem, died 28 December 1844, buried 5 January 1845. No wife or parents are named. The index (pg00000006363272) carries the same name, age and dates but leaves residence and status blank. ¶19

Correction, pre-emptive - he is NOT the Gunder Johnsen of Uglem. WikiTree profile Johnsen-1845 offers a 'Gunder Johnsen aka Eidem, Uglum', b. 1786 Selbu, d. 28 December 1844 Selbu, with a wife Ragnhild Eriksdatter b. 1797 (Eriksdatter-1310), and it is tempting because our man has no dates at all. I chased the Digitalarkivet records that profile cites, and they do not support the identification: the burial entry is for 'Gunder Johnsen UGLUM', male, age 60, died 28 December 1844, buried 5 January 1845, Selbu parish 1843-1859, page 360 no. 3 - a man of the farm UGLEM, not Eidem; and the marriage the profile cites is that of his son John Gundersen, birthplace UGLEM, married 31 October 1864 in Selbu, Selbu parish 1860-1877 p. 15 no. 22, the groom's father given as 'G. Johnsen'. Uglem and Eidem are different Selbu farms and Gunder Johnsen is an unremarkable name combination in that parish. Do not merge the two men. Published trees are leads, never evidence - this one earned its keep only by handing over four primary-record links, which is all a published tree is for. ¶6

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 3

Rated Probable rather than Confirmed because the only record of him found so far is that one line in his daughter's marriage entry: a name and a farm, with no date of birth, no household and no baptism located. The next records to look for are the Selbu marriage of Gunder Johnsen at Eidem about 1810-1815, his daughter Berith's baptism about 1816 with his household named, and the 1801 census of Eidum in Selbu, which returns 77 people. ¶2

Rated Probable: Gunnar also occurs on pp. 200, 201 and 205 and Berit is common throughout the chapter, and I have not read the page. ¶4b

Confidence raised from Probable to Confirmed, 12 Oct 2026. Wave 3 knew him only as a name in his daughter's marriage entry of 24 May 1841 - 'Gunder Johnsen Eidem' - with no date, no household and no second record. The 1825 census of Selbu supplies all three: rural residence 0060 Eidem, household 1, 'Gunder Johns., 39, Indst.', with his wife 'Brynhild Eriksd., 28' and four daughters, the eldest of them 'Beret Gundersd., 10'. One Gunder Johnsen at Eidem in 1825 with a daughter Beret of exactly the right age, and a Gunder Johnsen Eidem named as that Beret's father sixteen years later. ¶7

Corrections 3

Correction: earlier worker notes (and briefs built on them) date this baptism 17 September 1786. The register heads the entry '15. p. Trin.', and with Easter 1786 on 16 April and Trinity Sunday on 11 June, the 15th Sunday after Trinity is 24 September 1786. ¶16

Correction: death changed from 'after 27 November 1825' at Eidem to 28 December 1844 at Uglem, burial 5 January 1845 at Selbu, rated Probable. He is identified with the husmann Gunder Johnsen Uglum because: (1) the 1835 scan shows he was a husmann (Hmd.) married to Brynhild Eriksdatter, and the burial gives the Uglem man the same status; (2) Brynhild came from Uglem - she married in 1815 as 'Pigen Brynhild Eriksd. Auglem' and her father Erik Torstensen held Uglan in 1801 - so a move of the family to her home farm is natural; (3) their son John, born April 1835, is very probably the John Gundersen, born 1836 (calculated from his age) at Uglem, whose father is 'G. Johnsen' at his marriage to Guri Nilsdatter on 31 October 1864 (Selbu 1860-1877, marriages p. 15 no. 22); the index gives Uglem as his birthplace, which more likely reflects where he grew up than where he was born (inference); (4) the bygdebok's Eidem holder block for him, '1815-1838 Gunnar Jonsen', carries 1786 and 1844, so his time at Eidem ended about 1838 and he died in 1844 (word-box reconstruction); (5) age 60 at death gives a birth about 1784, against his September 1786 baptism and his 1825 census age of 39 - a two-year overstatement, ordinary for a burial age; (6) Brynhild was a widow by 1865. What would make it Confirmed: a baptism at Uglem of a child of Gunder Johnsen and Brynhild about 1836-1844, Brynhild's burial in 1880 naming her late husband, or a probate (skifte) after Gunder Johnsen Uglem in 1845. ¶21

Correction: the earlier note 'Correction, pre-emptive - he is NOT the Gunder Johnsen of Uglem' is withdrawn. It rested on the farm names alone (Uglem against Eidem) and on WikiTree Johnsen-1845 giving the Uglem man a wife 'Ragnhild Eriksdatter'. That wife's name evidently comes from the misread index of the 1835 baptism, whose scan names Brygnild Eriksdatter, our Gunder's wife. The same correction applies to the LINE.md trap that Berith Gundersdatter Eidem's father is not WikiTree Johnsen-1845: the WikiTree man appears to be her father after all, although that profile's wife's name is wrong. ¶22
