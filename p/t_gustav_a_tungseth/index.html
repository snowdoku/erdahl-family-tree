Life story

Born 12 Jun 1885 in Kristiansund and enumerated there as a baby in the census taken on 31 Dec 1885 at Toldbodbakken, then again in the 1891 census at 47 Toldbodsmujet. He emigrated with his mother and the other children on 2 May 1894 for Minnesota, aged eight. ¶1

1895 Minnesota census: Gustav Tungseth, 10, born in Norway, in his father Peter's household in Skree Township, Clay County (p. 6, read from the image). ¶5

Find a Grave records two marriages: to Olga Pauline Dukleth, 1898-1921, in 1916, and to Frances Mildred Johnson Campbell, 1896-1992, in 1925. Three children are linked: Sylvia Naomi (O'Neill), 1918-1986; Theodore Harvey 'Buddy', 1921-1923, who is buried with the family at Zion Lutheran in Clay County; and Richard Gene, 1929-1979. His first wife died in 1921, the same year the little boy was born who died at two. ¶3

1900 census: Gustav Tungseth, son, born June 1885 in Norway, 14, single, immigrated 1894, at school, in his mother Caroline's household in Skree Township (ED 316, sheet 185A, line 4, read from the image). ¶6

1905 Minnesota census: 'Gustar' (Gustav) Tungseth, 19, born in Norway, farmer (ditto mark, inferred); by his birth on 12 Jun 1885 he was twenty, at home in his father Peder's household in Skree Township, 11 years in Minnesota (Enumeration District 10, schedule sheet III, line 33, read from the image). ¶7

1910 census: Gustav A. Tungseth, son, 25, single, born in Norway, immigrated 1894, naturalization column possibly 'Na' (not clear), speaks English, farmer on the home farm, own account, in his father Peter P O Tungseth's household in Skree Township (ED 59, sheet 187A, line 37, read from the image). ¶8

Death of his son: the Minnesota death index, as found by the signed-in lane, lists Theodore Harvey Tungseth, died 19 Apr 1923 in Becker County. This matches his son Theodore Harvey 'Buddy', 1921-1923, as Find a Grave gives him (index entry only; identification by the full name and year). ¶9

He died at Stillwater, Washington County, Minnesota on 23 Jun 1943, eleven days after his 58th birthday, and is buried in Fairview Cemetery there, block 3, lot 102 - the only one of the eight to settle at the far eastern side of Minnesota rather than in the Red River valley or the west. ¶2

His occupation and obituary have not been found; the Stillwater Gazette for late June 1943 is the next source. ¶4
