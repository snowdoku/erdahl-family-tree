Life story

Lewis (Lodovic, Lewes) Boyle of Bidney in Dilwyn and of the Friars in the city of Hereford. The 1569 heralds' visitation of Herefordshire (Harl. MS 1140, f. 103, printed by Weaver, p. 83) gives him two sons, John Boyle of Bidney and Hereford and '2. Roger Boyle', and a daughter Elinor, who married first Watkins Russell and secondly Hugh Apharry. Lodge (Peerage of Ireland, 1789, vol. 1, p. 144) gives the same family: two sons, John (who kept the Herefordshire estate) and Roger (who married Jane Pattishull), and the daughter Eleanor with the same two husbands. The eldest son John's will was proved in the Prerogative Court of Canterbury on 15 July 1551 (TNA PROB 11/34/206, John Boyle of St John the Baptist, Hereford). The birth year is an estimate: his son John died about 1551 and his grandsons were born about 1520-45. Added. ¶1

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the grade · 1 on the identification · 1 record that disagrees · 1 correction · 1 open question

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Probable, graded on his link to his son Roger Boyle. Two compiled sources agree. The visitation MS lists Roger as Lewis's second son, and a separate pedigree in the visitation papers (Haworth of Burghope, Harl. MS 615, ff. 19v-20, written after 1591) says 'Ales, maryed John Boyle, son of Roger second son of Lewes'. Lodge independently makes Roger the father of the same John, who married Alice Haworth, and of Michael, the London merchant, so the Roger of the visitation is the Roger of the tree. Neither source quotes a record. ¶2a

The case for this identification 1

Test record read: the will of John Boyle of St John the Baptist, Hereford (TNA PROB 11/34/206, register image read). Grade unchanged (Probable, on his link to Roger Boyle). The will names John's wife Anne and his children: 'Thomas Boyle my sonne th'elder' (heir, sole executor, already tenant of a house in All Saints that the testator 'bought of one Phillip Ap Ivy [?]'), 'John my sonne', 'Edward my sonne', 'William my sonne', 'Harry my sonne', 'Thomas my sonne the yonger', 'Elizabeth my doughter', and 'Maude my doughter... to be ordered by her two brethren Thomas and James'. It names NO father and NO brother, so it neither confirms nor refutes Roger as Lewis's second son. It does support the visitation in two ways. Thomas is the eldest son and heir (the Haworth pedigree has 'Thomas Boyle of Hereford, eldest son of John Boyle, son of Lewes Boyle and heir of Bidney'). A son James and a garden 'without the Gray Freres gate' fit the 1544-45 (36 Hen. VIII) grant of the Grey Friars to James Boyle. That James was thus Lewis's grandson, as noted above. Witnesses: Sir Robert Pryse [?], curate; Sir William Hillar, clerk; John Stryven [?]; John Gillam. Next test record: the will of Roger Boyle himself (Hereford diocesan probate), or a Hereford city deed naming Lewis with his sons. ¶5

Records that disagree 1

Earlier generations; still none added. The two compiled ascents above Lewis disagree. Lodge (1789) gives Lodovic (temp. Henry III), John, James, Lodovic, John, James, then Lodovic of Bidney. Duncumb (1804, from Collins) gives Ludowick (temp. Edward III), John, James, James, then Ludowick of Bidney. Grosart (Lismore Papers, 2nd series, vol. 4, Life, pp. 194-195) repeats Lodge and cites Harleian visitation MSS and Segar's baronage MS, not records. The Biuile of Pixley (Domesday) derivation he mentions is a guess. Free searches of the printed record calendars (patent and close rolls, inquisitions) found no Boyle of Hereford before Lewis. The first Boyle record found is the grant of the Grey Friars site to his grandson James Boyle in 36 Henry VIII (Duncumb, vol. 1, p. 378, citing the enrolled leases of the Augmentation Office). Do not confuse Lewis with a later 'Lewis de Boyle of Hereford', whose daughter Jane married John Hayward of Bodenham, the son of Thomas Hayward and Joan Morton (Duncumb, vol. 2, p. 53; Weaver, Hayward pedigree). Five generations after the Hayward of 12 Henry VI, that Lewis belongs to the later 1500s. Test record for Lewis's parents: a Hereford city deed or a feet of fines entry (CP 25/1 Herefordshire, 1460-1509) naming Lewis Boyle with his father. Added. ¶4

Corrections 1

Correction of a date: Lodge puts Lodovic 'in the reign of Henry VI' (1422-61), which rightly judged too early for Roger's father. The 1569 visitation, as edited, has him 'temp. Hen. VII' (1485-1509), and his eldest son's will was proved in 1551. Those dates fit a father of Roger, who was born about 1490-1510. Lodge's earlier chain (a Lodovic 'in the reign of Henry III', then John, James, Lodovic, John and James, father of Lodovic of Bidney) is not added. It is a single compiled descent with no dates, and the visitation begins with Lewis. Added. ¶3

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Test record: the will of John Boyle of Hereford, 1551 (TNA PROB 11/34/206), which may name his brother Roger; or the will of Roger Boyle himself (Hereford diocesan probate). Added. ¶2b
