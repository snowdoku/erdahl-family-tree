About the family

One of the ten children of James P. Eidem - baptised Ingebrigt Paulsen at Selbu in 1863, one of the two children of Paul and Kari Eidem whom the American family had never heard of - and his wife Ida Pederson, listed with this birth date in Selbygbogen (Minneapolis, 1921), p. 278. All ten were born in Roseau County, Minnesota. These are first cousins of Palmer Gilbert Eidem whose existence was unknown to the family. Not otherwise traced. ¶1

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
