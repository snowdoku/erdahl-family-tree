About the family

Husband of Ane Gundersdatter (see her page), who was a widow after her first marriage in 1801 and holder of Alsaet; their marriage has not been found. It is entered as before 1763 from Povel's birth (1762-63, from his 1801 and 1839 ages). read January 1757 - February 1767 in full with this marriage and Povel's baptism among its targets and found neither, so either both were missed at half-page resolution or the marriage predates 1757 and Povel was baptised outside this register. ¶3

Father of Povel Sivertssen (see his page), rated Probable. For: Povel was of Alsaet at his 1796 marriage and farmed Alsaet 'in common with his mother' Ane Gundersdatter in 1801; his patronymic Sivertsen fits Siur (Sivert); Siur Olsen of Alsaet was Ane Gundersdatter's husband in 1779; Povel's brother Gunder (born 1779 to Siur and Anne) was Povel's sponsor in 1799; and no other Siur or Sivert is known as householder at Alsaet in 1757-1801. Against or missing: Povel's own baptism (about 1762-63) has not been found - read 1757-February 1767 without it - so no record names Siur as his father. ¶4a

Life story

Baptism (Probable identification, one of two): 'Ole Alset' had a son Sivert baptised on Invocavit Sunday, 26 February 1730 (Selbu ministerialbok 1699-1737, canvas 120 left) and another Sivert ('Siverd') on 31 October 1731 (canvas 125 left), the mother churched after each, crops. Siur's burial age of 51 in March 1780 fits either; the 1731 child is the likelier if the 1730 Sivert died young, but no burial has been found to decide it, so the birth field is left at 'ABT 1729'. The father is linked at Probable; see Ole Sivertsen Alset for the reasoning and what would settle it. ¶6

1762 extra tax list: listed unmarried among the six sons of Ole Siursen and Ingri Svendsdatter at Alset 732. Ingri is added as his mother at Probable; she may be a second wife of Ole (see her record). ¶7

Burial: 'Begravne Siur Olsen Alset, død d. 19 Mart., 51 aar gl.' - buried Siur Olsen of Alsaet, died 19 March, aged 51, on Easter Monday, 27 March 1780 (date computed from Easter 26 March 1780). Selbu ministerialbok 1757-1801, canvas 101 right page, crop. Born, by that age, about 1728-29. Siur is the Selbu form of Sivert (Sigurd). ¶1

Baptism of his son: 'Döbt Gunder. Par: Siur Ols. Als[et], Anne Gund. T. Olle Berge, Engelbr. Kielstad, Niels Ovne, Mali ib., Brynil [Alset]' - Gunder, parents Siur Olsen of Alsaet and Anne Gundersdatter, 14th Sunday after Trinity, 5 September 1779 (canvas 98 right page); 'Siur Alsets hustru' was churched on 17 October 1779 (canvas 99 right). Read, crop. This Gunder is Gunder Sivertssen, 22, in the 1801 household of the widow Ane Gundersdatter at Alseth, and 'Gunner Siversen Alsæt' who stood godfather to his brother Povel's daughter Anne in 1799. ¶2

Siur's own parents are unknown; his patronymic shows his father was an Ole. An Ole Olsen Alset, buried Whit Monday 1781 aged 41 (canvas 105 left), may be a brother - an inference only. ¶5

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 3

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed. His father's probate, Skifteprotokoll 6-1 (24756) fol. 92a: 'Anno 1768 den 4de Februarii' on the farm Alset in Selbu, after the late 'Ole Syvertsen', between his widow 'Ingri Svends Datter' and their children: 1. Syvert Olsen, of age, living on the farm; 2. Svend Olsen, of age, living on the farm Sletne[?]; 3. Joen Olsen, married; 4. Ole Olsen, of age; 5. Erich Olsen, of age; 6. the daughter Giertrud Olsdatter, unmarried, 23. This names him as the son of Ole Siursen Alset and Ingri Svendsdatter and as the heir living on Alset. ¶8

Audit (1 Nov 2026): downgraded from Confirmed to Probable. The upgrade record, his father Ole Syvertsen's 1768 probate, proves Siur's own parentage, and Ole Sivertsen Alset and Ingri Svendsdatter stay Confirmed as his parents. It says nothing about the link that places Siur in the pedigree, his fatherhood of Povel Sivertssen. His notes rate that link Probable because Povel's baptism is not found, and the census evidence for it is indirect (see Ane Gundersdatter). ¶9a

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed, on the probate after him, Skifteprotokoll 8 (24759) fol. 291a-291b (views 310-311; typed register entry 'Sivert Olsen, Alset, Selbu, fol. 291'): 'Anno 1780 den 5te Maii' on the farm Alset in Selbu, registration, valuation and division after the late 'Sivers[!] Olsen Alset' between the surviving widow 'Anne Gundersdatter' on the one side and the children she had with the deceased in marriage on the other: '1. Sønnen Paul Siversen 17 aar gammel. 2. Sønnen Gunder Siversen 1 aar gammel', and on fol. 291b '3. Dotteren Ingrie Siversdatter 10 aar gammel. 4. Dotteren Karen Siversdatter 6 aar gammel'. The widow had a lauverge (co-guardian) Erich M[eeren?]; a Paul Pedersen Garberg was guardian for one of the children (relationship word not read with certainty). Paul (Povel) Siversen, 17 in May 1780 (born about 1762-63), is Povel Sivertssen of Alset (38 in 1801, 76 at his burial in 1839); his siblings Gunder, Ingri/Inger and Karen are the Gunder 22, Inger 30 and Karen 27 of the 1801 census, Alseth household 01. The probate names Siur as Povel's father, the link the audit of 1 Nov 2026 asked for. ¶10
