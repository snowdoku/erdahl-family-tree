About the family

NEW ANCESTOR. Daughter of Thor Jørgensen of Øvre Ramse in Tovdal and wife of Tellef Nielsen Tveit. She is the link that ties the Tveit family of our direct line back into the Ramse farm whose name the family carried to America. ¶1

She is named in the joint probate of her unmarried brother and sister, Ole Thoersøn and Anløv Thoersdatter, held at Øvre Ramse on 28 September 1715 (Skifteprotokoll no. 4, folio 280). The heirs are listed as: brother Jørgen Thoersøn of Øvre Ramse; brother Thallak, deceased; "Systera Aase Thoersdtr. +" with the note "(g.m. Thellef Tveit. Børn.)" — sister Aase Thorsdatter, deceased, married to Tellef Tveit, with children; sister Kari Thoersdatter, living as a kårkone (a retired dependant) with her brother at Øvre Ramse; sister Gjertrud, unmarried at Øvre Ramse; and sister Thargier, married to Knud Thargiersen Mjaaland. ¶2

Her son is named in the same probate file. The guardianship list for her brother Thallak Thorsen's children (Skifteprotokoll no. 4, folio 281b) appoints "Tiøel Thellefsøn Tved, systerson til avl." — Tjøll Tellefsen of Tveit, the deceased's sister's son. That is her son Kjøll Tellefsen, who died in 1747. ¶3
