About the family

Parents: Hans Siursen and Engebor Olsdatter, Probable. The naming pattern supports it: in the 1801 census Ole's sons are Jacob (17, his wife Siri Jacobsdatter's father's name) and Hans (13, the subject's father's name), and his daughter is Ingeborg Malene (7, his mother's name Engebor). ¶3a

Life story

1758 list: the 1758 soul register of Skudenes parish lists on the farm Langager (Ferkingstad annex) the household of 'Hans Siursen gal:' (gardmann, farmer), 34, and Engebor Olsdatter, 26, with 'Ole Hansen 3 aar' and 'Marte Hansdatter 5 aar' in the children column. The next household on the farm is Peder Hansen, 32, whose child is Hans Pedersen, 5, so the two Hansen children belong to Hans Siursen. Ole's age (3 in 1758, born about 1754-55) agrees with his 1801 age of 45 (born about 1755-56), and he was the farmer at Langager in 1801. Read from the scan (source 4501, view 9, p. 17). ¶2

The probate after his father Hans Siursen, Langager, 23 Feb 1785 (card index 39936 view 866; D 7b, source 23892, fol. 385a) names him as 'Ole, myndig' with his mother Ingeborg Olsdatter (widow), brother Torbjørn (21) and sister Marthe (31). This settles his parentage: Hans Siursen and Engebor Olsdatter are now Confirmed as his parents. ¶6

Farmer at Langager, Skudenes. In the 1801 census he is 45, with wife Siri Jacobsdatter and children Jacob (17), Hans (13), Ingeborg Malene (7) and John (4). ¶1

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: Confirmed as the father of Hans Olsen (b. 25 Mar 1788). Two primary records name him: the c.1840-42 Skudenes household register (source 1653, Akre, canvas 273) gives Hans Olsen's parents as 'Gaardm. Ole Hansen Langager og h. Siri Jakobsdatter', and the 1801 census of Langager lists Hans, 13, in the household of Ole Hansen, 45, and Siri Jacobsdatter. His own parentage (Hans Siursen and Engebor Olsdatter, from the 1758 soul register) stays Probable as. ¶4

Searches that found nothing 1

Search: parents' probate not found. The 12 Mar 1765 Langager estate of an 'Ingebor Olsdatter' (source 23888, view 95) has Rasmusdatter heirs and is not his mother's; the only Langager estate in the Karmsund designation register 1740-1754 (source 23887, view 78: Anne Jacobsdatter, 18 Mar 1752) does not name his family. A probate after Hans Siursen or Engebor Olsdatter would have to come from D 7a-D 15 (sources 23891-23897, 1774-1807), which have no typed register. ¶5
