About the family

He is the oldest person yet established on this quarter of David Erdahl's ancestry, and his patronymic says his own father was a JON of Eidem. ¶2

Parents linked at Probable from the 1762 extra tax list: Jon Siursen and Ingebor Olsdatter of Eidem (gl. matr. 720), whose children over 12 were Ole, Siur, Brynild and Jon. He later held the same farm (1787-1814) and named sons Jon (1781) and John. No record names his parents outright. ¶14

Life story

Marriage: betrothed (trolovet) in Selbu on Easter Monday, 20 April 1778 (Easter 1778 = 19 April): 'Trolovede Siur Jons. Eid. J. Helge Jonsd: Aftr: Sp. Olle Ped. Flønes Einer Povels. ib.' - Sivert (Siur) Jonsen of Eidem and Helge Jonsdatter of Aftreit; sponsors (Sp., forlovere - the men who stood surety that there was no impediment) Ole Pedersen Flønes and Einer Povelsen of the same farm, Flønes. Married on Exaudi Sunday, 31 May 1778: 'Copul. Siur Jons. Eidem J. Helge Jonsdatter.' Read from the scans (source 16311, canvas 88 left page and canvas 89 right page). This replaces the estimate 'before 1781'. Both sponsors were of Flønes, not of either party's farm; whether they were kin is not known. ¶8

Residence: called 'of Eidem' at his betrothal and marriage in 1778, but entered as 'Siur Jons. Aftreit' at the baptism of his son Jon on 16 May 1784 - the family lived at Aftreit, his wife's farm of origin, in 1784, and was back at Eidem by the 1801 census. ¶10

He named a daughter Ingebor twice over (b. about 1789 and about 1792) and both were alive in 1801, which is unusual in Selbu and worth noting: it means the elder was not dead when the younger was christened, so this is not the ordinary re-use of a dead child's name. ¶5

1801 census: person 001 at rural residence 0034 Eidum, Selbu - 'Sivert Jonssen', aged 62, 'Husbonde', 'Begge i 1. aegteskab' (both in their first marriage), occupation 'BONDE OG GAARDBEBOER' = farmer and occupier of the farm, i.e. he worked and held Eidem. Five children in the house: Jon 20, JON 18 (our line), Ole 15, Ingebor 12 and Ingebor 9, with a servant girl Magli Olsdatter 18. A second household on the farm was that of Jens Thomassen, huusmand med jord. ¶1

Probate, a daughter not in the 1801 census: the 1815 skifte names a daughter Marit Sivertsdatter (called Mali, married, in her mother's skifte of 1820), who is not in the 1801 Eidum household (Jon 20, Jon 18, Ole 15, Ingebor 12, Ingebor 9). Only one Ingeborg is among the heirs in 1815. Whether one of the two 1801 'Ingebor' entries is Marit, or Marit was living elsewhere in 1801, is not resolved; her baptism (Selbu book 16311, about 1778-1800) would settle it. ¶13

Death and burial: the Selbu burial register for 1814 (page 207) enters 'Sivert Johnsen, Gaardmand, Ejdem', aged 76, died 5 July and buried on the 5th Sunday after Trinity, which in 1814 fell on 10 July (date worked out from Easter 1814 = 10 April). A gaardmand is a farmer holding his own farm. ¶6

Two of his children stood godparents at his grandson Sivert's baptism in August 1815: 'Ole Siverts: Ejdem' and 'Ingebor Sivertsd:', which confirms the 1801 household grouping from an independent record. ¶3

Probate: the skifte (division of the estate) after 'Siwert Johnsen Eidem', gaardmand of Eidem, Selbu, was closed on 5 April 1815 (Strinda og Selbu district court, skifteprotokoll 10b, fol. 705b). He died 'i Julii f.A.' (in July of the previous year), which agrees with the burial-register date of 5 July 1814; the estate was registered on 30 September 1814 and the skifte meeting held on 24 February 1815 (proceedings protocol fol. 98). The heirs were his widow Helge Johnsdatter; his sons John the elder (eldre), John the younger (yngre) and Ole; and his daughters Marit Sivertsdatter and Ingeborg. This is the first record that names all his children as his heirs in one place. ¶11

Probate, the two Johns: the 1815 skifte lists two sons both called John, distinguished as 'eldre' (the elder, Jon Sivertsen Eidem) and 'yngre' (the younger, John Sivertsen Eidem, the direct ancestor and gaardmand of Eidem). They are two different men and must not be merged. ¶12

Three of his sons were still on Eidem at the 1825 census - John, 41, as gaardmand, John the elder, 45, as husmand, and Ole, 30, as husmand - and a daughter Ingebor, 33, was servant in the gaardmand's house. One farm, four households, all his. ¶4

1 on the identification · 1 alternative set aside

The case for this identification 1

Identification of the 1814 burial: the age of 76 gives a birth year of about 1738, against 62 in the 1801 census of Eidum (about 1739); the patronymic Johnsen is the same name as Jonssen; and the status and farm are those of the 1801 'Bonde og gaardbeboer' at Eidum. His widow is entered six years later as a gaardmand's widow at Eidem (see Haelge Jonsdatter). Rejected: Sivert Biornsen, b. about 1738, kaarmand (retired on a farm pension) at Eidem, buried 1807, because his patronymic is wrong; Sivert Rejertsen (Berge, 1807) and Sivert Olsen (Ostbye, 1805), also because of their patronymics. No other Sivert Jonsen or Johnsen of the right age appears in the Selbu burial index for 1801-1830. ¶7

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Not the same marriage: a different Siur Jonsen married Berthe (Birthe) Pedersdatter in Selbu on Trinity Sunday 1777 (betrothed about February 1777, sponsors Gunder Siursen Haarstad and Jon Siursen Sesseng). He is not this man, whose only wife in the 1801 census, Helge, was married to him in 1778 and was in her first marriage as he was in his. ¶9
