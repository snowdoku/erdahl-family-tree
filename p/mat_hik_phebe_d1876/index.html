Life story

Aunt of Lydia Adelaide (Hicken) Hayter. Gravestone at Sturgeon, as transcribed in 2002: 'Phebe daughter of George & Francis Hicken, died Aug 14, 1876 age 37 yrs.' (Find a Grave 75109550). Named for her paternal grandmother Phebe (Allbut) Hicken, by the compiled genealogy's account. ¶1
