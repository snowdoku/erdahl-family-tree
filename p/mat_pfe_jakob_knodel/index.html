About the family

Parentage (Speculative, 13 Oct 2026; audited; supersedes the earlier note that his relationship to Gottlieb Knodel was 'not established'): Jakob is entered as a candidate son of Ludwig Knodel and Wilhelmine Tarnowsky, and so as a possible brother of Gottlieb Knodel (b. 1879), Erna Knodel's father. The only evidence is indirect. In the 1928 Canadian passenger-list index (Ancestry dbId 1263), exact-keyword tests of the hidden index fields return 'Jakob' and 'brother' on Gottlieb's entry and on his wife Karolina's, and 'Jakob' and 'uncle' on the entries of their children Frieda, Erna and Otto. Neither the index fields nor the manifest image have been read; the words are known only to occur somewhere in each entry. The relative's surname and residence are not established: 'Knodel' is the passengers' own surname, and the same index failed to return a relative's surname on Wilhelm Knodel's 1928 entry ('uncle Simon', Eberle not matched). On the 1925-1935 Canadian form the relative named by a family travelling together is usually the nearest relative in the country left behind, so the words fit a brother named Jakob of either Gottlieb or Karolina (née Schmidt, who had several siblings); 'uncle' on the children's entries fits both. That the relative was this Jakob Knodel of Płużnica (Pfeilsdorf), the only Jakob Knodel found there in the acts read for 1900-1918, is an inference. No Jakob Knodel of his generation was in Canada in 1931. ¶3

Parentage, the case against and the alternatives: no son Jakob of Ludwig and Wilhelmine is among the Friedenstal birth extracts of the St Petersburg Consistory duplicates, 1860 to November 1885. Those extracts are incomplete. Ludwig's known children leave long intervals with no recorded birth (after Karolina, Dec 1863, until Ludwig, Oct 1869; 1871-1874; and between Anna Maria, Jul 1875, and Gottlieb, Apr 1879), and Jakob, whose first known child was born in January 1905, was probably born between about 1865 and 1882, so the silence is not a contradiction. Alternatives kept open: (a) the relative in the 1928 index was Karolina's brother, a Schmidt, and says nothing about Jakob Knodel; (b) a half-brother of Gottlieb, a son of Ludwig by another wife or of Wilhelmine by another husband; (c) no close kinship, or 'brother' used loosely: a Jakob Knodel was born at Friedenstal on 28 Aug 1878 to Jakob Knodel and Christina Sauter (FHL 1895623/2 p.191 no.40), of a different Friedenstal Knodel household, and is the right age to be the Pfeilsdorf settler (a Jakob Knodel b. 1878 is indexed on his own page, p.188, of the Friedenstal family book 1881-1900); (d) a brother-in-law, which is unlikely because his wife was a Reister and Gottlieb's wife a Schmidt. This link stays Speculative until a record read in full names Jakob's parents or the 1928 relative. ¶4a

Parentage, reassessed after the age evidence (13 Oct 2026; stays Speculative): (1) The rival candidate (c) in the note above, the Jakob Knodel born 28 Aug 1878 at Friedenstal to Jakob Knodel and Christina Sauter, is ruled out as this man: the Pfeilsdorf Jakob was born about 1865-66, twelve years earlier. (2) A birth in late 1865 or early 1866 falls in the longest interval in the indexed births of Ludwig Knodel and Wilhelmine Tarnowsky (married Feb 1860): after Karolina (Dec 1863) and before Ludwig (Oct 1869). A son then fits, but the St Petersburg Consistory extracts show no birth for this couple in those years, which may be a gap in the extracts or a sign that he was not their son. (3) Alternative (a), that the 1928 passenger-list relative 'brother Jakob' was a brother of Karolina née Schmidt, is not supported by any record found: no Jakob is known among the children of Wilhelm Schmidt and Marie Adam (Karolina 1881, Wilhelmine 1885, Friedrich 1886, Anna 1888, Rosa Therese 1890), nor in the 1879 Atmagea household of Wilhelm's mother; but the family book and Geni (a lead) suggest further children whose names have not been read, so (a) is not excluded. (4) The age also leaves room for a son of another Friedenstal Knodel household of the 1860s, for example that of Gottlieb Knodel (b. 1826). The link therefore stays Speculative: the age removes the one named rival and fits Ludwig's family, but no record yet names Jakob's parents. ¶7a

Parentage, raised to Probable (Oct 2026, from the 1928 manifest image): the Megantic manifest of 22 Jul 1928 (LAC T-14745 frame 00366, vol 15 p 110), now read, names Gottlieb Knodel's nearest relative left behind as 'Brother Jakob Knodel, Pliznica, Poland'. His wife Karolina gives the same man as 'Brother in Law' and the children as 'Uncle', so the relative is a Knodel and the brother is Gottlieb's own, not Karolina's (alternative (a) in the notes above is ruled out). This Jakob Knodel of Płużnica is the settler of this record: he is the only Jakob Knodel found there in the civil acts of 1900-1918. A brother of Gottlieb, who was a son of Ludwig Knodel and Wilhelmine (Confirmed), must be a son of Ludwig, of Wilhelmine, or of both. A half-brother is very unlikely: Ludwig was single at 30 and Wilhelmine single at 21 when they married in February 1860, so neither had earlier legitimate children, and Jakob's birth about Nov 1865 to May 1866 falls within their marriage, in the gap between Karolina (Dec 1863) and Ludwig (Oct 1869). He is therefore entered as their son at Probable. It is not Confirmed because his own baptism has not been found (the Consistory extracts show no birth for the couple in 1864-69) and 'brother' rests on a single informant. ¶10a

Life story

Pfeilsdorf: a second Knodel settler (osadnik) at Pfeilsdorf, husband of Katharina née Reister; children registered at Pfeilsdorf: Elisabeth (b. 20 Jan 1905, 1905 no. 5), Gottlieb (b. 25 Oct 1906, 1906 no. 45), Wilhelm (b. 4 Oct 1908, 1908 no. 43), and one more in 1911 (images read by the w5 knodel-origin agent). His 1905 act carries no signature. ¶1

Church: his children Elisabeth (baptised 23 Jul 1905), Gottlieb (30 Nov 1906), Wilhelm (15 Oct 1908) and Anna (24 Sep 1911) are indexed as baptised in the Evangelical parish of Villisaß, Kreis Culm, with the relatives given as Jacob (or Jakob) and Katharine (or Katharina). These are index entries only (Ancestry dbId 61250); the images, which would name the godparents, have not been read. Gottlieb Knodel's children were baptised in the same parish in the same years. ¶8

Witness, 1907: on 21 Sep 1907 the settler (osadnik) Jakob Knodel of Płużnica (Pfeilsdorf), aged 41, witnessed the marriage at the Standesamt (civil registry office) Pfeilsdorf of Jan Eichelberg and a bride surnamed Brandtner, who was born on 25 Nov 1889 at Cogealia near Constanța in Romania (Pfeilsdorf marriages 1907 no. 13; image read). This is the earliest record of him at Pfeilsdorf as a witness. His age, 41, places his birth between 22 Sep 1865 and 21 Sep 1866, which agrees with his ages of 45, 46 and 52 in 1911-1918; together they narrow his birth to between about 31 Oct 1865 and 25 May 1866. He witnessed a second Brandtner (Brandner) marriage in 1911, so he was probably close to that Cogealia family; the acts do not say how. ¶9

Birth of daughter Anna, 1911: on 4 Aug 1911 the settler (osadnik) Jakob Knodel of Płużnica, Evangelical, declared at the Standesamt (civil registry office) Pfeilsdorf that his wife Katharina née Reister had borne a daughter, Anna, in his dwelling at Płużnica on 1 Aug 1911 at 4 a.m. He signed the act himself (his 1905 act carries no signature). The act gives no age, birthplace or parents for him. Source: Pfeilsdorf births 1911 no. 34 (Polish certified copy, AP Toruń 69/1205/0/2.1/1011, scan 340). ¶5

Age, from three marriage acts at Pfeilsdorf (images read): as a witness he was 'Besitzer' (owner) Jakob Knodel, 45, of Pfeilsdorf on 30 Oct 1911 (1911 no. 11); 'Ansiedler' (settler) Jakob Knodel, 46, on 25 May 1912 (1912 no. 3); and settler Jakob Knodel, 52, of Pfeilsdorf, Kreis Briesen, on 10 Aug 1918 (1918 no. 5). If the stated ages are exact, he was born between 31 Oct 1865 and 25 May 1866, so 'ABT 1866' is entered as his birth date. The ages are self-consistent across seven years. He was about 14 years older than Gottlieb Knodel (b. Apr 1879), who gave his own age as 33 at a Pfeilsdorf marriage of 2 Nov 1912 (1912 no. 13), an age that matches Gottlieb's known birth date exactly; so the registrar recorded ages carefully. Jakob was still living at Pfeilsdorf in August 1918. In 1911, 1912 and 1918 his fellow witness was the settler Heinrich Schmidt (b. about 1865); their kinship, if any, is unknown. ¶6

1 alternative set aside · 3 open questions

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Relationship to Gottlieb Knodel (Erna's father): not established. He is very probably a brother or cousin - the same surname among a few dozen settler families, and he named a son Gottlieb in 1906 - but no record yet read names his parents. The Settlement Commission file or a death/marriage act would decide it. ¶2
