About the family

Parents: very probably Joen Knudsen of Dal and Marit Tronsdatter. At Joen's probate on 15 Feb 1738 (DA 39948 card 2043; DA 25286 s. 121) the youngest child was 'Astrie, 10'. She married in 1753 as Astri Jonsdatter Dahle. The parents are Probable; her own grade (as mother of Even Julsen) is unchanged. ¶4

Life story

Origin: in 1753 she was of the farm Dahl (Dale) in Aurdal, which is where her father Jon should be sought. ¶3a

Probate text read: DA 25286 p. 122 (view 72) names her '7de Datteren Astria Jonsdatter 10 aar gl.', among the children of Joen Knudsen and Marite Tronsdatter 'begge deris sammen avlede Børn'. Her uncle (farbror) Christen Knudsen Rogne acted for the widow. At her son Even's baptism in 1754 a 'Trond' stood godfather first, possibly her brother Tron Jonsen (22 in 1738); not proved. ¶6

Listed in the 1801 census at Haarud (Hovrud), Aurdal, as 'mandens moder' (the husband's mother) of Even Julsen. She was then 90, a widow of her second marriage, living on føderaad. Even's patronymic shows that her husband (first or second) was named Jul. Her granddaughter Astri Evensdatter (b. about 1780) was named for her. Her marriages are not yet identified. The Aurdal marriages for 1692-1730 are transcribed, but none for 1730-1760 were found. ¶1

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: her marriage is now found. Aurdal ministerialbok 1730-1762 (DA 9361) p. 88, canvas 313: betrothed 3 May 1753, 'Juel Evensøn Hovrud og Astri Joensd[atte]r Dahle', sureties Knud Re[bbe?]stad and Knud Siversen M[?]ansfest; married ('Copul.') 15 Oct 1753. This ties her by a primary record to her husband Jul Evensen of Hovrud, and the 1801 census (Haarud, Aurdal) names her as 'mandens moder' of Even Julsen, whose patronymic is Jul's. Her son Even was confirmed in 1771 as 'Even Juelsøn Haavrud', 17 (DA 9362 p. 405, canvas 193), so he was born about 1753-54, just after this marriage. The census and the marriage are both primary and name her relationships. ¶2

Records that disagree 1

Bygdebok discrepancy, not resolved: Valdres bygdebok I F, gnr 107 Dal (canvas 494, right column) lists her as '7 Astrid f 1721, g 1753 m Jul Eivindsson', whereas the 1738 probate gives 'Astrie, 10' (born about 1728) and the 1801 census age (90) points to about 1711. The bygdebok year 1721 is from a word-box rebuild (number and name on the same line, so the pairing is likely right) and may come from a baptism the bygdebok author read; it does not change her parents (the marriage to Jul Eivindsson in 1753 matches the tree). ¶7a

Corrections 2

Correction: a wedding in 1753 and a son born about 1754 sit badly with a birth about 1711 (from her age of 90 in 1801); she was more probably born about 1715-1725, and the 1801 age is likely overstated. The 1801 census says she was then a widow of her second marriage, so she married again after Jul's death. ¶3b

Correction: her birth is revised from 'about 1711' (census age 90 in 1801) to about 1728 (age 10 in 1738), which also fits her son's birth in 1754. ¶5

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Test record: an Aurdal baptism of Astri, daughter of Jon Dal, in 1721 (Aurdal ministerialbok before 1730, if extant), against the probate age. ¶7b
