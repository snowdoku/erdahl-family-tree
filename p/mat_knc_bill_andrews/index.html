About the family

Parents: William Arthur Andrews (1901-1975), a Canadian Pacific Railway telegraph operator and later agent-operator, and Elizabeth Alice Chadwick (1893-1974) of Standish, Lancashire, who married at St Saviour's Church, Nelson, on 17 March 1924, when both were living at Sirdar on Kootenay Lake. Bill was born at Cranbrook in October 1925 into this railway family and followed his father onto the CPR, where he worked 25 years as a locomotive engineer. ¶5

Life story

Military service: he is buried in the Veterans Section, and the shared marker carries the poppy-on-maple-leaf veterans' emblem. Born in 1925, he very probably served in the Second World War. His service file is not yet retrieved. ¶2

He was ten years younger than Erna. Find a Grave and Geni show no children of this marriage. ¶3

Voters lists (Ancestry index, dbId 2983): William Arthur Andrews Jr., Kootenay West, 1953; William Andrews of Oliver, a Canadian Pacific Railway employee, 1962; William A. Andrews, Oliver, 1965; William A. Andrews Sr., Okanagan-Boundary, 1974, with Erna Andrews. ¶9

He was not yet a householder at Oliver in 1954: the Oliver section of the 1954 Penticton City and District Directory lists no Andrews. ¶8

Federal voters' lists at Oliver: 'William Andrews' 1962, 'W A Andrews' 1963, 'William A Andrews' 1965, 'W A Andrews' 1968 (the same page as 'Mrs Erne Andrews') and 'William Andrews' 1974 (the same page as 'Erna Andrews'). These are anonymous index entries; the images, which name occupation and address, have not been read. ¶7

Oliver: he signed his mother's death registration in April 1974 as 'W A Andrews Jr.', son, of P.O. Box 308, Oliver, and his father's in August 1975 as 'W A Andrews', son, of #2 Road East, Oliver. His parents had retired to #2A Road East, about a mile south of Oliver, and both are buried in Oliver Cemetery. ¶6

Erna Knodel's second husband, known as Bill. Death registration, BC 2000-09-022261 (indexed as 'William Authur Andrews'): died 28 October 2000 at South Okanagan General Hospital, Oliver; residence 9332 338th Avenue, Oliver; married, spouse Erna Knodel; occupation engineer, 25 years, in rail transportation; born 12 October 1925 at Cranbrook, BC. His father was Arthur William Andrews, born London, England, and his mother Elizabeth Alice Chadwick, born Standish, England. The informant was his wife, Erna Andrews. He was buried 2 November 2000 in Oliver Cemetery (Graham Funeral Home). ¶1

Corrections 1

Correction: his own death registration of 2000, for which Erna was the informant, names his father as 'Arthur William Andrews, born London'. His father's own marriage (1924) and death (1975) registrations show that the father was William Arthur Andrews, born 22 May 1901 at Croydon, Surrey, and that Arthur William Andrews was Bill's grandfather. Bill bore exactly his father's name and was known as W. A. Andrews Jr.; on the 1974 voters' list his father appears as 'William A Andrews Sr'. ¶4
