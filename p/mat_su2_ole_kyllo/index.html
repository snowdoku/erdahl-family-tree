About the family

Husband of Barbara Ann Eidem, daughter of John P. Eidem and Oline Gundersdatter Slind, married 24 September 1950. He is therefore David Erdahl's great-granduncle by marriage. ¶1

Life story

Gravestone, Selbu Cemetery, Whitman County, Washington: "Ole Bernhart Kyllo, b. Jan 05 1919 d. Feb 11 2005, s/w & h/o Barbara Eidem m. Sep 24 1950". ¶2

He is the "Cle Kyllo" of Oline's 1955 obituary - a misreading of Ole. ¶3

KYLLO IS A SELBU FARM NAME and his people are one of the larger families in that churchyard: Henry P. (b. 17 Aug 1876, d. 15 Apr 1954), Ranghild (b. 18 Feb 1883, d. 4 May 1966), John (b. 2 Aug 1893, d. 13 Dec 1972, Pvt US Army WWI, Purple Heart), Thomas P. (1885-1966), Juliette M. (1898-1946), Regina (1891-1977), Sylvia (1915-2004), Bruce Maurice (b. 23 Mar 1927, m. Ruth Morasch 16 Apr 1949), Thomas Raymond (b. 12 May 1931, d. 12 Dec 2008, m. Carol Perkins 4 Sep 1959), an infant child of "M&M Henry" who died in 1916, and Prudence Olivia Aune nee Kyllo (b. 11 Dec 1917, d. 29 Nov 2000). His own parents are not yet established; Henry P. and Ranghild are the likeliest couple on age. ¶4
