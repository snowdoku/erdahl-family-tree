About the family

Wife of Anders Knudsen of søndre Heimdal. Her estate was held on 8 Nov 1727 (protocol 1722-34 fol. 190b), with children Torov 7, Biug 5 or 6 and Gunnild 9. The patronymic reads 'Jans.dtr.' on the index card (could be Jensdatter); the protocol page itself was not read. ¶1

Life story

Probate 8 Nov 1727 (protocol 1722-34, fols. 190b-192a) reads her name as 'Astrj Jons datter' (Jonsdatter; Jensdatter not excluded; the card index gave Jansdatter). Widower Anders Knudsen; children Torov 7, Biug 5, Gunnild 9. Guardians: father for Torov, Anund(?) Haagensen for Bjug; the daughter's clause is unclear. No parent or mother's brother of Astri is named. ¶2
