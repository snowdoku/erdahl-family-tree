About the family

Eldest son of Paul Ellingsen and Tørri Jakobsdatter, and the brother who stayed in Norway and took over the family farm while Peder Olaus emigrated. In the 1900 census he is head of the household at Tungset nedre, gnr. 129 bnr. 1, in Kornstad, entered as born 1839 in Kornstad herred and as 'Gaardbruger S.' - a farm owner working his own land (selveier), not a tenant. ¶1

His wife was Ingborg Torgersdatter, born 1847 in Kornstad. The five children at home in 1900 were Pål, born 1871, a skrei (winter cod) fisherman and field labourer; Tore Kristian, born 1877, a house carpenter and cabinetmaker; Elen Ingborg, born 1884, occupied with handicrafts (husflid); Hanna Marie, born 1887; and Ane Johanna, born 1891. The household also kept Kannutta Knutsdatter, born 1819, a widow living on a retirement agreement (føderåd) from the farm. ¶2

His son Pål Ellingsen, born 1871, is the 'Paul Ellingsen, Grdbrs' who stands as godfather at Tungset baptisms between 1889 and 1899 - not his grandfather Paul, who was by then an old man on a retirement agreement and died in 1894. ¶3
