About the family

Oline Slind's great-great-grandfather, named as the bride's father - 'Haldo Torgers Svinaas' - in the Selbu marriage register for 21 June 1831, page 317 serial 19, when his daughter Marite Haldorsdatter married Ole Johnsen Stokke. He is the man whose given name produced the patronymic that three earlier records garbled as Haldosdatter, Haavelsdatter and Halderd. ¶1

Father: Torger Erikssen (see his page). The 1801 census of Selbu, rural residence 0015 Svinaas, re-read 13 Oct 2026, enters Haldo Torgersen, 30, in household 4 as working the farm 'i faellesskab med faderen' - in partnership with his father - and the only possible father on the residence is household 3's Torger Erikssen, 70, 'Bonde og gaard beboer' (farmer in possession). His patronymic Torgersen fits, and his eldest son, Torger Haldorssen, 5, carries the name, as Selbu naming custom expects. The other farmer on the residence (household 1, Ole Jonssen) is 21, and the cottager Haldo Pederssen in household 5 is unrelated. The link is rated Confirmed: the census states the father relationship in words and only one man fits it. ¶6

Mother: not linked. Torger Erikssen's 1801 marriage to Magli Guttormsdatter was 'Hans 2det haendes 1te aegteskab' - his second marriage, her first - and the daughter in their household is written 'Hans datter', his daughter, not theirs. Haldo, born about 1770, was therefore almost certainly a child of Torger's unnamed first wife, and Magli Guttormsdatter is deliberately NOT entered as his mother. Haldo's baptism c.1769-70, in the untranscribed Selbu register 1757-1801, would name her. ¶7

Mother: Mali Haldorsdatter (see her page), named in his baptism and churched as Torger Eriksen Svinaasen's wife in late December 1770; Torger's first wife. He carries her father's name Haldor. This replaces the earlier note that his mother was not linked. A probable elder sister, Marit, was baptised on 27 September 1767 to 'Torger Erichs: Qvello' and Mali Haldorsdatter, when the family lived at Kvello. ¶13

Brother: Ole Torgersen, baptised in April 1774 (between the burials of early April and the 2nd Sunday after Easter, 17 April), 'Döbt: Ole. Foræld: Torger Erichsen Svinaas, Mali Haldorsd. Fadd: Ole B. Fossum[?], Povel Eidem, Erich Lien, Anne Solum, Kirsten Svinaas' - parents Torger Eriksen of Svinaas and Mali Haldorsdatter (Selbu klokkerbok 1766-1776, canvas 105 right page, col. 3, crop). The wife was churched in May 1774 (canvas 107). The first godfather is probably Ole Barosen Fossum, later a forlover (surety) at Torger's second marriage in 1787 and Haldo's in 1794. Ole is not in the 1801 Svinaas households; his fate is not traced. ¶15

Siblings so far known, all children of Torger Eriksen: Marit, baptised 27 September 1767 at Kvello to Torger and Mali Haldorsdatter (already noted); Haldo himself, November 1770; Ole, April 1774 (note above); and Ingebor, born about 1779 ('Hans datter' in Torger's 1801 household, Ingebor Torgersdatter Svinaas), presumably also Mali's since Torger's second marriage was not until 1787. Erich Torgersen Svinaas, buried 1787 aged 27, may be an elder brother (see Torger Erikssen Svinaas); not linked. ¶16

His mother's probate: Skifteprotokoll 9-3 (Digitalarkivet 24762), fol. 613b, October 1786, Svinaas: '2. Sønnen Haldo Torgersen 16 Aar gl.', son of the late Mali Haldorsdatter and the widower Torger Eriksen. A second primary record for his parentage, beside his 1770 baptism. ¶17

Life story

Death: a Haldo Torgersen born about 1769 was buried in Selbu on 8 July 1832, entered in the Selbu burial register 1824-1842. He is the only man of the name and age in the parish and the identification is secure, so he died in the first days of July 1832 aged about sixty-two, a year after giving his daughter in marriage. ¶4

Godparents Jon B[arosen?] Sandvig and Ingeborg Sandvig at his baptism tie the Svinaas family to Sandvig already in 1770; Jon Barosen Sandvig was later forlover for both Torger (1787) and Haldo (1794), and Haldo married Ingeborg Jonsdatter of Sandvig. The initial 'B.' is uncertain. ¶14

Betrothal and marriage: on the 15th Sunday after Trinity, 21 September 1794, the Selbu register enters 'Trolovet: Grenadier Haldor Torgersen Svinaas med Pigen Ingeborg Jonsdatter Sandvig' - betrothed, the grenadier Haldor Torgersen of Svinaas and the maiden Ingeborg Jonsdatter of Sandvig (Sandvik) - with 'Caut: Lovend[e]' (forlovere, sureties for the marriage) Jon Barosen Sandvig and Ole Barosen Fossan. The banns were read for the third time on 5 October, and the couple were married ('Aegteviet') at a weekday service on Monday 27 October 1794. A grenadier was a soldier of the enrolled grenadier company of the Trondhjem infantry; the 1801 census still calls him a national soldier. ¶9

The forlovere Jon Barosen (Bardsen) Sandvig and Ole Barosen Fossan had stood in the same role, in the forms Jon Baros. Sandvig and Olle Baros. Fossum, at his father Torger Eriksen's betrothal on 1 April 1787. They were therefore sureties connected with the Svinaas family rather than evidence of the bride's parentage; that they might be kin of Haldo's unnamed mother is a possibility only. ¶10

Baptism: 'Döbt: Haldor: forældr: Torger Erichsen Svinaasen, Mali Haldorsd.' - godparents Bersvend H. Solum, Jon B. Sandvig, Ole Olsen Svinaas, Marit Svinaas and Ingeborg Sandvig - in the entry just before the 1st Sunday in Advent (2 December) 1770, so about 18-25 November 1770 (Selbu klokkerbok 1766-1776, source 16316, canvas 65 column 4, crop). Birth replaced 'ABT 1770' with 'before 2 December 1770'. It fits his 1801 age of 30 exactly; his 1832 burial age of 63 is two years high. ¶12

1801 census: rural residence 0015 Svinaas, household 4 - 'Haldo Torgersen, 30, Mand, Begge i 1te aegteskab, I FAELLESSKAB MED FADEREN OG NATIONAL SOLDAT VED INDFANTERIET'. Both he and his wife were in their first marriage; he worked the farm IN PARTNERSHIP WITH HIS FATHER, who is Torger Erikssen in household 3 of the same residence; and he was serving as a soldier in the national infantry, the conscript militia raised on a quota from each farm. His household then was his wife Ingebor Jonsdatter, 28, and three children: Ingebor Haldorsdatter 6, Torger Haldorssen 5 and Magli Haldorsdatter 2. ¶2

He rose from his father's junior partner to the farm's master. The Sovlvskatten rolls enter him as 'Haldo Torgers., Gaardmand, Svinaas' on 7 September 1819 and again on 3 March 1821, and the 1825 census of Selbu as 'Haldo Torgers., 55, Gmd.' at Svinaas. ¶3

He is 'brudgommens far' - the groom's father - in the Selbu marriage register on 15 June 1835 and 20 January 1840 as well, so at least two of his sons married in Selbu after Marit did. ¶5

Records that disagree 1

Conflict noted: the 1801 census gives the couple a daughter Ingebor aged 6 (born February 1794 - January 1795), but they married only on 27 October 1794 and no baptism of a child of theirs is in the register up to Christmas 1794. Either Ingebor was born between November 1794 and January 1795 or her census age is a year high; her baptism (expected in canvases 173-176) would settle it. ¶11

Corrections 1

Correction: death was carried as 'BEF 8 JUL 1832' (the burial date). The burial entry (Selbu 1824-1842, p. 378 no. 27; index record re-read 13 Oct 2026) gives date of death 2 July 1832 and burial 8 July 1832, aged 63 (calculated birth 1769, against 30 in the 1801 census). The index gives no residence; the identification remains as stated in the existing Death note. ¶8
