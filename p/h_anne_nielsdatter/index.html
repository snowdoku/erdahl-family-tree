About the family

Mother of Tøri: named in her 1816 baptism. Married Ole Herjussen 27 Dec 1813, as a pige from Oland. ¶1

Her parentage is Probable: in the 1801 census, Anne Nielsdatter, aged 12, is at Ohland as a child of Tøri Olsdatter's first marriage. Her granddaughter Tøri Olsdatter (b. 1816) was named after that grandmother. ¶2

Life story

She was an infant when her father died in April 1789 and was brought up at Oland by her mother and her step-father Tallak Eriksen. Her brother Aadne Nielsen died about 1811. ¶5

Her exact age on the card is written as a figure 9 with the unit abbreviated; given her stated age of 12 in the February 1801 census, this must be 9 weeks or 9 months, placing her birth in the first half of 1789 and not, as 9 years would imply, in 1780 — which is in any case impossible, since her parents did not marry until June 1784. ¶6

An Anne Nielsdatter was buried at Åmli 19 Jan 1839, but one copy gives her birth year as ~1819, so that is probably a different woman. Her death is unresolved. ¶3

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

PARENTAGE RAISED FROM PROBABLE TO CONFIRMED (10 Oct 2026). It previously rested on her age in the 1801 census and a naming pattern. The Østre Råbyggelag probate card for the estate of Niels Aadnesen of Oland in Gjøvdal, settled 16 May to 18 November 1789, lists his children as "Aadne Nielsen, 4 år" and "Anne Nielsdtr.", an infant, and adds in the same hand "(g.m. Ole Herjusen Gangsei)" — married to Ole Herjussen of Gangsei. The record therefore names both her parents and her husband in one entry. ¶4
