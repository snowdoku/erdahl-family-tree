Life story

Married Kiersti Gulbrandsdatter of Hvinden at Gran on 3 Nov 1789, when he was an unmarried artillerist living at Fallang. They were betrothed 21 Sep 1789, and the forlovere were Morten Knarud and Iver Fallang. ¶1

In the 1801 census for Gran he is Peder Nilsen, 37, gaardmand, heading household 3 on Øvre Fallang with wife Kirsti Gulbrandsdatter (40) and children including Nils (11) and Eli (7). He is identified as a son of Nils Pedersen and Eli Olsdatter, who head household 1 on the same farm, by the patronymic and farm continuity. Further support: his eldest son is named Nils, his daughter Eli bears his mother's name, and Ole Nilsen and Mari Nilsdatter of Fallang were godparents to Eli. The census does not state the relationship directly, so it is Probable. ¶2

Probate (index card): the estate of Niels Pedersen of Fallang, held 22 Jan 1811 (Hadeland og Land skifteprotokoll 1802-1821, f. 547b), with the widow Elie Olsdatter, lists the children 1. Peder, of age; 2. Ole; 3. Gulbrand; 4. Kirsti, married to Christoffer [Mortensen] Enarud; 5. Mari, married to Jens Svensen Gamkind. This names him as the son of Nils Pedersen and Eli Olsdatter: confidence upgraded from Probable to Confirmed. Ole and Gulbrand match the unmarried sons in his parents' 1801 household. A second card (f. 222a) dated 7 Jan 1806 lists the same family. That date conflicts with his father's burial on 15 Jun 1810, so the 1806 reading needs checking against the protocol. ¶3
