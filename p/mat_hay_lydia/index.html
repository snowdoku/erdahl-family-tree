About the family

Mother of Raymond Hayter. Her maiden name is given as Hicken on her son's 1939 marriage registration and in full as Lydia Adelaide Hicken on his 1947 death registration; born Prince Edward Island. ¶1

Family: the eldest child of William J. Hicken (died 1891) and Bexey Jane Creed of Gaspereaux and St Mary's Bay, Kings County. Her father died when she was nine; her sister Edith Gordon Hicken (1887-1916) and brother George Howard Hicken (1889-1966) stayed on the Island. Two compiled Island Register genealogies (Hicken and Creed) give her name as 'Lydia Adella'; both of her son Raymond's BC records and her own death registration give Lydia Adelaide, which is preferred. ¶12

Life story

1881 context: she was born eleven months after the 1881 census found her parents, William (26) and Bexy (19), newly married and living in the household of her grandparents George and Frances Hicken on their farm at Gaspereaux, Township 61. Her father died in August 1891, when she was nine. ¶15

Gravestone, Lakeview Cemetery, Section N (photographed on Find a Grave and read): a flat marker reading 'Lydia A. Hayter / In Loving Memory / 1882 - 1969'. ¶7

Gravestone: a flat bronze marker in Section N of Lakeview Cemetery, Penticton, reads 'Lydia A. Hayter / In loving memory / 1882 - 1969' (Find a Grave memorial 155003998, photograph 2018). The Find a Grave contributor, transcribing the full death registration, gives the place of death as Penticton General Hospital and the informant's surname as Hermiston (the signature on the microfilm reads 'Rita Herniston' or 'Hermiston'). ¶11

1891 census (Township 61, enumerated 30 April 1891): 'Adilada' Hicken, 9, daughter of William and Bexy Hicken, in a household that included her grandfather George Hicken, 79, born in England. ¶16

Baptism: none found in the PEI Baptism Index or the BMB index under Hicken, Hickin, Hickens, Lydia, Adelaide or Adella. Her younger brother and sister were baptised together at Sturgeon on 15 May 1890, but she was not baptised with them. ¶17

Grave: the City of Penticton's Lakeview Cemetery register lists 'HAYTER, LYDIA ADELAIDE' in plot N-052-3. ¶18

Marriage: to Benjamin Hayter on 27 February 1901, according to both compiled Island Register genealogies (Hicken and Creed), which agree on the date. The PEI marriage register entry (place, church, witnesses) has not yet been read - the PEI Public Archives database is behind a CAPTCHA to automated access. The same compilers give Benjamin's death as 30 May 1936 at Charlottetown, which explains why she came to British Columbia a widow. The Hicken compiler lists ten children: Glen G. (1902), Rosebud (1904), Rex (1905-1929), Raymond (1907-1947), Cecil Elwood (1909-1963), Alice Vera May (1912-1982), Rita Edith (1919-1997), and three daughters shown only as living, married respectively Stevenson, Oland and Bourne - who match Bertha Olive, Winnifred Eunice and Rena Pearl Hayter of the Penticton marriages in the BC index. The compiler puts Raymond's birth at Montague and the rest at Gaspereaux, and gives Raymond's dates as 15 Aug 1907 - 15 Apr 1947, which his own BC records correct to 28 Aug 1907 - 19 Mar 1947. ¶13

Marriage notice: Messenger and Visitor (Saint John, NB), 6 March 1901, p. 13: married 'at the home of the bride, Feb. 27, by Rev. H. Carter', Benjamin Hayter of High Bank, Lot 64, to 'Lydia Adilda Hicker of Gaspereaux, Lot 61, P.E.I.' ('Hicker' and 'Adilda' are the printer's spellings of Hicken and Adelaide). ¶20

Her eight known children: Rex (1905-1929), Raymond (1907), Cecil Elwood (1909), Alice Vera May (1912), Bertha Olive (1914), Winnifred Eunice (about 1916), Edith Rita (1919) and Rena Pearl (1922). Each of the seven younger children is proved by a marriage or death registration naming both parents; Rex by a transcription of his death registration together with his gravestone. Every child whose record gives a town was born at Gaspereaux, Prince Edward Island; Raymond's records say only 'Prince Edward Island'. If, as a Find a Grave note says, the parents married in February 1901, there may be an older child born about 1902-1904 who has not yet been found. ¶5

Move west: in June 1931 Lydia and the younger children were still in Prince Edward Island (1931 census, Sturgeon and Gaspereaux, as found by the PEI researcher). Her sons Rex and Raymond had gone to British Columbia by 1929. Her 1969 death registration says 35 years in Penticton, which puts her arrival about 1934, and Raymond's 1947 record gives him 13 years in Penticton, which agrees. Rena's obituary says the family went west in 1930; the 1931 census shows that is too early. Benjamin evidently did not go. ¶8

Her name in her children's records: Lydia Adelaide (Alice 1937, Rena 1949), Lydia Adeline (Winnifred 1944, Bertha 1945), Lydia A. (Rita 1939), Della (Cecil 1938, Rita 1997) and Adella (Bertha 2000). Della and Adella are pet forms of Adelaide. Every child's record gives her birthplace as Gaspereaux, Prince Edward Island. ¶4

Known as Della: her son Cecil's marriage registration (BC 1938-09-465867, Kaleden, 11 Oct 1938; image read) gives his mother's maiden name as 'Della Hicken', born Gaspereaux, Prince Edward Island - Della being short for Adelaide (the compiled Island genealogies render it 'Adella'). The same record gives Benjamin Hayter's birthplace as High Bank, P.E.I.; the witness was 'Ray Hayter' of Penticton, her son Raymond. ¶14

Residence: in 1944 and 1945 two unmarried daughters gave their home as 312 Whyte Avenue, Penticton, and in 1949 a third gave 307 Whyte Avenue; Lydia died at 355 Whyte Avenue. Whyte Avenue was the family's street for at least 25 years. ¶6

She went by Della: the Summerland Review of 3 January 1952 calls her 'Mrs. D. Hayter, Penticton' in reporting her daughter Rita's second wedding. ¶19

Probable death: the BC death index has LYDIA ADELAIDE HAYTER dying at Penticton on 14 Dec 1969 aged 87 (registration 1969-09-017349), which fits her exactly in name, town and age. The registration image has not yet been read; until it is, the identification rests on the unusual full name in the same town as her son. ¶2

Death registration read, BC 69-09-017349 (Penticton district 167/69): Lydia Adelaide Hayter died at Penticton General Hospital on 14 December 1969, aged 87, born 21 March 1882 at Gaspereaux, Prince Edward Island. She was a widow, the wife of Benjamin Hayter, and lived at 355 Whyte Avenue, Penticton, where she had been 35 years. Her parents are given as William John Hicken and Bexie Creed, both born in Prince Edward Island. The informant was her daughter Rita Hermiston of Summerland. She was buried at Lakeview Cemetery, Penticton, on 18 December 1969 by Penticton Funeral Chapel. This record moves her 1969 death from probable to confirmed: the husband's name, the Prince Edward Island birth and the daughter who reported it all match. ¶3

Death registration, BC 1969-09-017349 (Penticton district no. 167/69), image read: Lydia Adelaide Hayter, widow, housewife, a Canadian, died at Penticton on 14 December 1969 aged 87; born 21 March 1882 at 'Gospereaux, P.E.I.' (Gaspereaux, Kings County); husband Benjamin Hayter; father William John Hicken, mother's maiden name Creed, given name typed 'Bexie' (almost certainly a slip for Bessie); both parents born Prince Edward Island. The informant was her daughter Rita, of Box 4, Summerland, B.C. She was buried on 18 December 1969 in Lakeview Cemetery, Penticton, where her son Raymond had been buried in 1947; undertaker Penticton Funeral Chapel. The top of the form (items 1-4: name, place of death, residence) is cut off the microfilm frame. ¶9
