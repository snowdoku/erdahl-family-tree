Two of the daughters married in the county: Lillian Pauline married Joseph M. Greenhalgh on 4 August 1946 (cert. L46198) and Helen Mae married Raymond A. Onken on 20 June 1950 (cert. M50102). ¶4

Parents, from the Find a Grave biography: 'Larsen Eliassen and Brynhild Gunnersdtr/Gundersdtr' — that is, Lars Eliassen and Brynhild Gundersdatter of Selbu. The name GUNDER is worth marking: Oline G. Slind's surviving brother in Selbu in 1955 was a Gunder Slind, and Elias's mother was a Gundersdatter. ¶7

HIS SIX CHILDREN ARE NOW EACH PROVED BY A FEDERAL RECORD. All six filed Social Security applications naming him as father and Emma B. Paulson as mother (NUMIDENT Application SS-5 Files 1936-2007, NARA AAD series 5138, file unit S-T): ¶12

His father's name was LARS. A Washington death certificate for a GUNDER SLIND who died at Port Blakely, Kitsap County on 30 January 1959, aged 68, names "Father: Lars Slind. Mother: Brynhild Hanstad" - so a Lars Slind had at least two sons, and Elias Larsen Slind, b. 20 November 1881, is very likely Gunder's elder brother and Brynhild Hanstad's son. Rated as a lead, not a fact: nothing names Elias and Lars in one document, and HANSTAD is a Selbu farm name, which is consistent but not proof. The record that would settle it is Elias's own South Dakota or Minnesota death certificate (he died March 1965, Revillo, Grant County, South Dakota). ¶20

Life story

A Slind farmer of Mehurin Township, Lac qui Parle County, born in Norway about 1881 - the same year as Oline Slind, in the same township, on the same prairie. A brother is the natural reading and the obvious next thing to test, but nothing retrieved yet states the relationship. ¶1

Married Emma B. Paulson, born 4 February 1887 in Grant County, South Dakota, died 2 April 1952 there (Grant County death certificate number 265225, page 394). Children on Find a Grave: Clara Beatrice Slind Wusthoff (1919-1984), Lillian Pauline Slind Greenhalgh (1921-1977), Ethel Slind Allen (1922-2007), Helen Mae Slind Onken (1931-1993) and Edward Lyle Slind (1933-1991). ¶8

Homestead, 1916: he received a United States land patent on 27 OCTOBER 1916, serial number 02169, accession SER-552242, for land in section 31, Township 17 North, Range 23 East of the BLACK HILLS MERIDIAN, DEWEY COUNTY, SOUTH DAKOTA - the east half of the south-west quarter, lot 3, and a third description. Dewey County lies on the Cheyenne River Indian Reservation, which the federal government opened to homestead entry in 1909-10; a young man from a crowded eastern county taking land on a newly opened reservation in the 1910s is the standard second-generation move, and it is two hundred miles west of the Zoar colony where he is buried. ¶11

Named as father on the birth certificates of six children registered in Lac qui Parle County between 1919 and 1933. The first of them, Clara Beatrice Slind, was read in full from the record image: 'County of Lac qui Parle, Township of Mehurin. Date of birth Oct 4, 1919. FATHER: Elias Slind, post office Marietta, W, age at last birthday 38, birthplace Norway, occupation Farming. MOTHER: full maiden name Emma B. Paulson, post office Marietta, W, age at last birthday 32, birthplace S.D., occupation Housework. Number of children born to this mother, including present birth: 1; now living 1.' Attended by Henry Duncan; filed 10 November 1919 at Marietta. The certificate carries a stamped note that it was amended by the State Registrar pursuant to affidavit. ¶2

Aged 38 in October 1919 he was born about 1881. ¶3

A Slind of Selbu, born within nine months of our Oline G. Slind, who settled in the same churchyard's congregation and was a PALLBEARER AT OLINE'S FUNERAL in October 1955. He is the nearest thing to a named Slind relative the project has. ¶5

Draft registration, Fourth (so-called 'old man's') Registration, D.S.S. Form 1 revised 4-1-42, read from the scan on his Find a Grave memorial: serial number U-572; 'Elias (none) Slind'; place of residence ADAMS TOWNSHIP, GRANT COUNTY, SOUTH DAKOTA; mailing address MARIETTA, MINNESOTA; age in years 60; date of birth Nov. 20, 1881; place of birth 'Seibo' — the registrar's spelling of Selbu — NORWAY; person who will always know his address, 'Emma Slind, Marietta, Minn. Wife'; employer 'Self. (Farmer)'; signed in his own hand, 'Elias Slind'. His farm therefore straddled the state line in the same way the Eidem farms did, lying in Grant County, South Dakota, with the post coming to Marietta in Minnesota. ¶6

1. CLARA BEATRICE SLIND, SSN XXX-XX-2259, b. 4 October 1919, MEHURIN, LAC QUI PARLE, Minnesota; father ELIAS SLIND, mother EMMA B PAULSON; cycle 1938. Later filings as CLARA BEA ILES and CLARA B WUSTHOFF, so she married twice - Frank Iles in King County, Washington on 5 February 1949, then a Wusthoff. ¶13

Correction - probable conflation of two men. Selbygbogen (1921) p. 149 gives Elias Larsen Slind, father of Kari (b. 1863) and Beret (b. 1872) Eliasdatter Slind, as BORN 1826, and p. 174 says he DIED IN SELBU before his widow emigrated in 1907. The dates recorded against this id - born about 1881, died March 1965 at Revillo, Grant County, South Dakota - belong to a different and much later man, most likely a grandson. See Elias Larsen Slind for the Norwegian Elias Larsen Slind, b. about 1826. ¶21

1923 accident, survivor: he is almost certainly the 'Elias Slind' who was buried alive with Mikal Paulsen Eidem and Gunder Harstad in a collapsing bank on Leroy Pratt's farm three miles south of Marietta on 9 March 1923, and who was dug out unconscious and revived. Minneapolis Tidende, 15 March 1923, p. 7. Elias Larsen Slind of the Zoar/Marietta colony is the right man in the right township at the right date, and no other Elias Slind is known in the settlement; the notice gives no patronymic, so this is Probable as an identification rather than certain. ¶22

HIS WHOLE FAMILY LEFT MINNESOTA FOR SEATTLE. Four of the six children married or died in KING COUNTY, WASHINGTON - Ethel (1947), Clara (1949), Paul (1949) and Edward (1956) - and Edward and his son Michael D. Slind (1959-2001) are buried side by side at Mountain View, Auburn. So Elias's children went west like Ole's, but to Puget Sound rather than to the Whitman County wheat country: two separate Slind migrations out of Lac qui Parle County to two separate parts of Washington. ¶19

Burial: Zoar Lutheran Cemetery, lot 22, with his wife Emma — South Dakota state burial register, 'ELIAS SLIND MAR 1965, lot 22' and 'EMMA SLIND 1952, lot 22'. Their daughter Clara lies in lot 26. ¶9

Relationship to Oline G. Slind is NOT established. The case for close kinship is circumstantial but strong: same farm name, same parish, born in the same year, emigrated to the same square mile, a member of the same congregation, and chosen to carry her coffin. Stated here as the lead it is, at Probable for kinship and Confirmed for everything else. ¶10
