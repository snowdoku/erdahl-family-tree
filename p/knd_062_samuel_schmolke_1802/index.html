Life story

Marriage: Samuel Schmolke, single, married Rosina Gierke, widow, at Beresina on 24 Jul 1825 (film 1767907-3 no.16). His children by Rosine née Bomm (Rosina Bomm) were born at Beresina: Johann Samuel 1 Jun 1826, Anna Maria 27 May 1829 and Louise 13 Dec 1831 (posthumous). That the 1825 bride 'Gierke, Rosina' is Rosine née Bomm is inferred: it is the only marriage of a Samuel Schmolke in the 1815-32 extracts (other Schmolke marriages there are of other people), the 1834 census lists a Karolina Jerke, born 1823, as Rosina's daughter, and the Drefs birth extract records Caroline Jerke, born at Beresina on 8 Feb 1823, mother 'Bum, Rosina' (extractor's query 'Bumm?'; film 1198701-2 no.27), which ties the widow Gierke/Jerke to Rosina Bomm. ¶2

Death: Samuel Schmolke died at Beresina on 6 Jul 1831 aged 29, so born about 1802 (Drefs death extract, film 1198706 no.55). The Wise/Stumpp emigrant index lists a Samuel Schmolke from Bavaria, settled at Beresina in 1815; this Samuel would then have been about 13, so the emigrant may be him with his family or an older namesake (not decided). ¶1

His widow married Johann Farowsky in 1835, and the Wahl index places his son Johann and daughter Luisa in Farowsky's household at Beresina. ¶3
