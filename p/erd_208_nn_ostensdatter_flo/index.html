Life story

Aaland (Stryn vol., Nedre Flo bruk nr. 1, label 404): '(Br.) Rasmus Lasseson, br. 1686; b. s. a. 18 mk. kg., som faren sa upp. * ? Øysteinsd.' The given name is left blank. Speculative as mother of Lasse Rasmussen Flo (see his page, b. about 1682): she is the only wife Aaland gives Rasmus, but Aaland gives the same patronymic to Rasmus's mother Helga Øysteinsdatter, so this may be a confusion. Lead (not proof): Østen Rasmussen Hilde was guardian for Lasse's widow (1733) and for Lasse's son Rasmus Lassesen (from 1738, guardianship protocol 109814 p. 59) and sat with Lars Frøyse and Anders Clementsen Kirkeeid as guardian at the 9 Jun 1738 division (A 8 image 46); if Østen was Lasse's brother, a son named Østen would fit an Øysteinsdatter mother. No record states the kinship. ¶1

How sure are we?

Speculative. A plausible candidate only. Treat this link as a question, not a fact.
