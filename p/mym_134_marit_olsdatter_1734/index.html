About the family

Second wife and widow of Peder Jonsen of Schiervold (Skjervold), Skatval. At the registration of his estate on 15 Jan 1772 she is 'hans igienlevende Enke Marith Olsdatter' (his surviving widow), with Christopher Pedersen Winge as her guardian (lagverge); her own children in the estate are Anders Pedersen, 3, and Gallov Pedersdatter, ½ year (Stjør- og Verdal skifteprotokoll 6-2, fol. 428b-429a, scan). ¶1

Mother of Gullov Pedersdatter (see her page): Probable. She is the proved mother of the Gullov born about mid-1771 (1772 skifte; Golla, baptised 28 Jul 1771); the identification of that child with the Gullov Pedersdatter who married Peder Pedersen from Skiervold in 1798 is inferred (same name, farm and father's name; in 1801 Gullov and her husband live as lodgers on the farm where Marit is the farmer's wife). ¶3

Life story

Death: 'Marit Olsd.' of Schjervold, aged 70 1/2 years (b. about late 1734), buried 19 May 1805 at Skatval (Stjørdal ministerialbok 1797-1815, p. 232; index pg00000001360272). Name, farm and age match the Marit Olsdatter, 67, wife of Peter Larsen at Schiervold in the 1801 census (b. about 1734). Another Marit Olsdatter of similar age buried in Stjørdal parish 1799-1805 lived elsewhere (Wold, Sortesgierdet, Kirkebye), so the farm decides it. ¶6

In the 1762 extra-tax list for Skatval (48401 image 204, farm no. 15 'Schiervold', two leilending households) the household of 'Ole og Hustru' (child Mali) has a maid 'Marit Olsd.', and the other household is 'Peder Jons[en] og Hu[stru]' with children Jonn Pedersen 21, Mali and Kirsten (Peder Jonsen's first marriage; his wife Golla Andersdatter died 1765), farmhand Halvor Jons[en] 15. A Marit Olsdatter, unmarried servant on Skjervold in 1762, fits the woman Peder Jonsen married as his second wife after 1765, and so his widow of 1772. The list gives no age or origin, so it does not name her parents. ¶5

Still the mother of Gullov Pedersdatter, but through her second marriage. Gullov was baptised 14 Aug 1774 as daughter of 'Peter Schjervold' (16371 canvas 251), i.e. Peter Larsen, whom the bygdebok (Skatval vol. 3, canvas 273) says married 'Marit Olsdtr., enke etter Peder Johnsen' on 20.04.1772; in 1801 Peter Larsen, 56, and Marit Olsdatter, 67, are husband and wife at Schiervold. The baptism does not name the mother, so her link stays Probable. The bygdebok gives her as 'Marit Olsdtr., [f.] 1735, [d.] 1805' (77/3-4, canvas 273 left). ¶7a

1801 census: Marit Olsdatter, 67 (b. about 1734), 'Hans kone', in her second marriage, household 01 at Schiervold, wife of the farmer (bonde og gaardbeboer) Peter Larsen, 56, who is in his first marriage. Identification with the 1772 widow rests on name, farm and 'second marriage'; inferred, not stated. ¶2

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
