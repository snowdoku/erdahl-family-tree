Life story

Birth (inference): before about 1631, because he was a farmer heading his own holding in 1664 and had a son aged 13 (born about 1651). His exact age is not recorded. ¶2

1645 poll tax (index rows): the koppskatt (poll tax) of 1645 for Brekke, Lavik and Bø parishes lists on the farm 'Opedahl' the husbond (householder) Lasse (pt00000000059709) with his kone (wife) Sigrj Thorbensdtr (pt00000000059710). The same list has a separate farm 'Ytteropedahl' (Ytre Oppedal), so 'Opedahl' is taken to be Indre Oppedal (inference). If this Lasse is the farmer Lasse of 1664, he was married by 1645 and his wife then was Sigrj Thorbensdtr (normalised Sigrid Torbjørnsdatter or Torbensdatter), possibly the mother of his son Iver, born about 1651 (inference; she is not linked or created). ¶7

1664 census: listed as the farmer (bonde) 'Lasse' on Indre Oppedal ('Indre=oppedall'), Brekke kirkesogn (church parish), Eivindvik prestegjeld, in the bailiff's census of 21 Jun 1664, with one farmer's son, 'Ifver' (Iver) aged 13. This list gives farmers no age and no patronymic, so his patronymic and age are unknown; only males are listed and boys under about 12 are omitted, so his wife and any younger children are not named. The other farmers on Indre Oppedal were Colben (son Guttorm 40), Johans (servants Johans Haldorsen 30 and Willum Johansen 15), Laurids (son Anders 24), Hans (sons Østen[?] 15 and Laurids 16) and Olluf (son Jorn[?] 22). Bailiff's census 1664-1666 no. 14, Digitalarkivet 35573, viewer 129, s.239 and the Lasse and Ifver 13 entries. ¶1

Death (inference): alive on 21 Jun 1664 (the census). No later record of him has been found; Gulen church books do not survive this early. ¶3

Name: the 1664 list writes only 'Lasse' (a common Sogn form of Lars). He is given here without a patronymic or farm byname because no record yet supplies one. ¶4

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 alternative set aside · 1 open question

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Not linked as father of Johannes Larsen (see his page): the farmer 'Johans' on the next row of the 1664 list could be Johannes Larsen (born about 1631), but nothing in the list ties the two men, and the 1699 probate after Iver Lassesen tells against Johannes being Iver's brother; see the note on Johannes Larsen. ¶6
