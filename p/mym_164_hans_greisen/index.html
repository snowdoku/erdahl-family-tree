About the family

Father of Greis Hansen (see his page); died before 24 Aug 1771. ¶1

Children named in the 1771 probate: a son [Jon?] Hansen (first name hard to read), a soldier in Copenhagen; Greis Hansen, 28; Martha Hansdatter, 24 (b. c.1747), servant at Nordre Lein. Hans Greisen was dead by 1771; no probate for him is in the typed Stjør- og Verdal registers 1742-1816. Greis named his first son Hans (b. c.1766/67, d. 1784), after this Hans. ¶3

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Graded on his link to his son Greis Hansen (see his page). The probate of Gurie Hansdatter, wife of Hans Nielsen of Riset, Frosta (opened 15 Mar 1771, continued 24 Aug 1771; Stjør- og Verdal skifteprotokoll 6-2, source 24757, fol. 381b, scan) lists her heirs on her father's side: '1. Farbroderen Greis Hansen, er død, men efterladt sig Børn: a) Hans Greisen, er død, og hans Arvinger ere 1. Sønnen [Jon?] Hansen, Soldat i Kiøbenhavn, 2. Sønnen Greis Hansen 28 Aar gl., Huusmand under Stor Lein her paa Frosten, 3. Datteren Martha Hans Datter 24 Aar gl., tienende paa Nor Lein.' The record names Hans Greisen as the father of Greis Hansen, 28 (b. c.1743), husmann under Storlein. Continuity to the tree's Greis: he is the 'Greis' of Storleinsvallet whose son Sivert was baptised on Almindelig Bededag 1768 (same farm, three years earlier), and his age matches the tree's Greis Hansen (confirmed 1761 aged c.18, 58 in 1801, 82 at burial 1825). All 28 Frosta index entries for a man Greis up to 1811 belong to this one man, so there is no rival. The 1765 dragoon roll also lists 'Greg: Hansen' among the young men at Nordre Lein, where his sister Martha served in 1771. ¶2
