About the family

Father of Kari Endresdatter (see her page), Confirmed; graded on his link to Kari. Primary: the skifte after 'Endre Qvam' held at Kvam in Lesjaskogen in May 1726 (Nord-Gudbrandsdal skifteprotokoll 1719-1726, source 25156, fol. 346b; probate card 39945 images 7531-7532) divides his estate among 'hans efterlatte 6 Børn' and names '5. Kari Endresdatter Knud Rolfsen Slettums Hustru' (card: 'Kari g.m. Knud Rolfsen Slettum'). The tree's Kari is the wife of Knut Rolfsen of Nordre Slettum (married 1717 per the bygdebok), so the record names the relationship to the very person in the tree; no continuity step is needed. Corroboration: the 1763 extra-tax list for Slettum has the inderst couple 'Knud Rolvsøn, Kari Endresd.', and the Lesja bygdebok (vol. 1 canvas 215) gives 'Kari, sjå Nørdre Slettom (1-6)' among Endre's children. ¶1

Family from the two probates: wife Berethe (Bethe, Birete) Lauridsdatter, d. before 3 Sep 1708 (Berethe (Brit, Bethe) Lauridsdatter); children Oluf (Ole) b. about 1688 (took over; m. 1713 Rønnaug Tostensdatter, d. 1720, Søndre Gudbrandsdal skifteprotokoll 1682-1721 fol. 233a, card 7529), Lauritz (Lars) b. about 1696, Bjerte (written 'Birete'/'Bierthe' Endressøn), Eli (m. Niclas Hansen Hampen), Kari, and Magnhild b. about 1703 ('23 aar ugift' in 1726). The six names agree on the 1708 and 1726 probates. Birth ABT 1649 is the bygdebok's (rebuilt text). ¶2

Life story

His own parents (Probable; see Oluf (Ola) Biertesen Kvam): the 1681 skifte at Kvam after Oluf Biertesen names 'Endre Olufsen' first among the children; the bygdebok makes this Endre (ca. 1649) the one who took over and married Brit Larsd. Test record for that link: a record between 1681 and 1708 naming Endre Olsen Kvam with his father's estate or his brothers Engebret and Povel (Nord-Gudbrandsdal tingbok 1680s-1690s; the 1681 protocol entry, source 25154 fol. 106a). ¶3

'Ender Qvam' was one of the two lagrettemenn who valued the estate of Jon Ingelbretsen at Qvam on 25 May 1696 (skifteprotokoll 1690-1719, s. 31a). Probably this Endre (he held Nordre Kvam by 1708), but the entry gives no patronymic and an Endre Amundsen is named in a 1681 Kvam probate, so it is not used for grading. ¶4
