About the family

Father of Birte (bapt. 7 Nov 1761, Eppeland, Åmli). ¶1

SIBLINGS, from the 1765 probate: Even Tjøstelsen, 27, who later settled at Austad in Mykland; Anne Tjøstolsdatter, married to Giermund Jonson of Rostveit, later of Indjorden; Ingeborg; Anne (a second of the name); and Oslou (Aaslöf). Two of the daughters, Ingeborg and Anna, died as minors and their joint estate was settled 10 March to 7 August 1768, naming their mother Berte Evensdatter and surviving siblings Tergie, Even, Anna and Aaslöf (18). ¶7

Life story

Betrothed 22 Nov 1755 'paa Eppeland', as Tergie Thiøstelsøn Rostved, to Kristi Nielsdatter Eppeland (Åmli 1741-1775). He was from Rostved (Rossevatn?) and moved to his wife's farm, Eppeland. ¶2

THE "ROSTVED" PUZZLE IS SOLVED. He was recorded at his betrothal on 22 November 1755 as "Tergie Thiøstelsøn Rostved" while the ceremony took place at Eppeland. His grandfather was Tergie Tjøstelsen of Rostveit, whose estate was settled 15 October 1728; Rostveit in Åmli was the family's farm of origin, and the family kept the byname. The earlier note in this record that "Rostveit" conflicted with his siblings being of Eppeland is therefore resolved: both are right. ¶6

Aslak, Eivind and Ingeborg Thiøstelsen/-datter of Eppeland, godparents in 1761, are probably his siblings, which would make Eppeland his own home farm too. That conflicts with 'Rostved' in the betrothal (unresolved). A Kirsti Thiøstelsdatter, b. ~1791, lived at Nedre Skjeggedal in 1801. His father was evidently a Tjøstolv (Thiøstel). ¶3

CORRECTION to the earlier note here. Aslak, Eivind and Ingeborg Thiøstelsen/-datter of Eppeland, godparents at his daughter's baptism in 1761, were taken to be his siblings. Eivind (Even) and Ingeborg are now confirmed as his siblings from the 1765 probate. Aslak is not among his father's children; he was Aslak Tellefsen of Nedre Eppeland, a neighbour whose own estate was settled 7 October 1767 and whose wife Eli Olsdatter was a sister of Christi Nielsdatter's father Niels Olsen. He was therefore a kinsman by marriage, not a brother. ¶8

DEATH ESTABLISHED. His own estate was settled on 10 January 1769 at "Nedre Eppeland" (Skifteprotokoll no. 13b, folio 220b). The card names him and his wife Christi Nielsdatter, notes that she later married Niels Joensen, and lists one child: "Berte Tergiesdtr., 8 år" — Birgitte Terjesdatter, aged eight, born about 1761. That is a direct documentary link between this man and our ancestor. The estate held 15 skinn in Eppeland, with assets of 453 riksdaler 2 ort against debts of 517 — insolvent. ¶5

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

PARENTAGE RESOLVED (10 Oct 2026), and confidence raised from Probable to Confirmed. He was the eldest son of Tjøstel Tergiesen of Nedre Eppeland and Berte Evensdatter. His father's estate, settled 2 March 1765 (Østre Råbyggelag skifteprotokoll no. 13a, folio 91), lists him first among the children as "Tergie Tjøstelsøn, 33 år", with the added notes "(+ sk. 10/1 1769)" and that he married Christine Nielsdatter "som brav odel til Eppeland" — who brought the hereditary right to Eppeland. Aged 33 in March 1765 gives a birth year of about 1732. ¶4
