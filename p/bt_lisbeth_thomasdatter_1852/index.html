Life story

Third child of Thomas Olsen and Eli Larsdatter. Baptized 12 September 1852 in the Hegra annex, entry no. 67 on page 16; her father is entered as a husmann at Beitlandsbjørgen. The index does not give her date of birth. ¶1

She bears her maternal grandmother's name, Lisbet Johnsdatter of Almlitrøen, who had died nine years before she was born. ¶2
