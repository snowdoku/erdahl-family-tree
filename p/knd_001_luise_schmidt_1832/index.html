Life story

Atmagea soul list, June 1879: in the first soul list of Atmagea (Atmadscha, Tulcea district, the oldest German colony of the Dobruja) under Romanian rule, household 4, whose head is marked 'tot' (dead), lists Karl Schmidt born 1853, Wilhelm Schmidt born 1854, 'Mrs Luise Schmidt' born 1832, and Karoline Schmidt born 1860. She was therefore a widow by June 1879. The list does not state relationships; that she was the mother of Karl, Wilhelm and Karoline is inferred from the household's make-up and her age (21 at Karl's birth). Her maiden name is not given. ¶1

Status after 1906 (13 Oct 2026): the Standesamt Pfeilsdorf death act of 1906 (no. 23), which names Anna Maria née Adam as the wife of Karolina's father Wilhelm Schmidt, raises his identification with the Wilhelm born 1854 at Atmagea to Probable. Her own link stays Speculative: the 1879 Atmagea soul list states no relationships, so that she was this Wilhelm's mother is still an inference from the household's make-up. Karl Schmidt's EWZ file (born 1853 Atmagea), naming his parents, would settle it. ¶5

Status after 1913 (13 Oct 2026): the Pfeilsdorf marriage act of 1913 (no. 15), which shows that Wilhelm Schmidt and Marie Adam had a daughter born at Atmagea in 1890, strengthens the identification of her (presumed) son Wilhelm with the Wilhelm born 1854 at Atmagea. Her own link stays Speculative: the 1879 Atmagea soul list still states no relationships, so that she was Wilhelm's mother rather than, for example, a stepmother or aunt remains an inference from the household's make-up. Karl Schmidt's EWZ file (born 1853 Atmagea), naming his parents, or Wilhelm's death act would settle it. ¶6

Context (1 Nov 2026): Traeger 1922 (Die Deutschen in der Dobrudscha, pp.52-53, read from the archive.org OCR) describes Atmagea's founding in 1848 from Akpunar. Adam Kühn's baptism book, Sept 1846 to Dec 1849, records 27 families with children; among them are the surnames Adam and Schmidt. Most of the parents were young couples born in Russia (Bessarabia) between 1816 and 1827, whose families came originally from West Prussia and Posen. The Atmagea church book records marriages from 1849. If Luise (b. about 1832) married at Atmagea about 1850-52, that marriage, and the baptisms of her sons Karl (1853) and Wilhelm (1854), should be in the Atmagea church book that Traeger used (the 'Kirchenakten von Atmagea'). That book is not on any free site. Its present repository is unknown: it may be with the Evangelisches Zentralarchiv in Berlin, which holds the Prussian Oberkirchenrat files for these congregations. ¶7

How sure are we?

Speculative. A plausible candidate only. Treat this link as a question, not a fact.

1 on the identification · 1 alternative set aside · 1 open question

The case for this identification 1

Identification (Speculative): she is entered as the mother of Wilhelm Schmidt only because he is identified, Speculatively, with the Wilhelm Schmidt born 24 Aug 1854 at Atmagea (1940 EWZ index; his birth year matches this soul list exactly, as does his brother Karl's, born 23 Sep 1853). That identification is not proved: no record yet names Karolina Knodel née Schmidt as that Wilhelm's daughter. Because Wilhelm's identification is itself unproved, this link already rests on two guesses (that Willem is the Atmagea Wilhelm, and that the household's widow was his mother). No parents for her, and no husband, should be added until Wilhelm's identification is proved. ¶2

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Husband, a candidate only: the Atmagea grain-levy list of 13 Feb 1877 has Michel Schmidt as no. 3 (half a plough), and he is not in the 1878 land register or the 1879 soul list, so he is probably the dead head of household 4 and her husband. This is not secure: household 5 in 1879 is also headed by a dead Schmidt (widow Wilhelmine, born 1854, children born 1873 and 1876), and a Matthias Schmidt (no plough) is in the 1877 list too. Michel is therefore not entered as a person. ¶3

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Next records: the full EWZ naturalisation file of her (probable) son Karl Schmidt, born 1853 at Atmagea, died 1940 at Cogealia (NARA A3342, EWZ51-B049 frame 0408; EWZ58-D020 frames 2278, 2300, 2370), which should name his parents; the Heimatortskartei cards for Atmagea (Bessarabiendeutscher Verein, Stuttgart); and the Atmagea Evangelical registers of the 1850s for Wilhelm's baptism, which would name both parents. ¶4
