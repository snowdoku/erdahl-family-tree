About the family

Parents (Probable): Torbjørn Tollefsen Steensland (d. before 23 Jun 1702) and his first wife Ingeri Erichsdatter. The probate of 23 Jun 1702 at Steensland in Tørdal (Bamble skifteprotokoll I 2, source 23742, fol. 204b) names 'Anna Torbiørnsdatter' as the only child of that marriage, with her grandfather Tollef Steensland acting for her. Identification (Probable): the 1702 Anna Torbjørnsdatter, a minor heir at Steensland, is taken to be Anne Torbjørnsdatter (see her page), wife of Joen Thoresen Steensland in 1762, because the name and farm match, Joen held land at Steensland as an inheritance would explain, and the couple's sons were named Torbjørn and Tollef, after her father and grandfather (naming pattern). ¶4a

Life story

Lifetime division: named with her husband in the 24 Feb 1762 division at Steensland (Bamble skifteprotokoll I 6a, fol. 252-253a), held at the couple's request because of old age, among their six children, the eldest daughter being Aasil Joensdatter, wife of Alf Halvorsen at Toe in Tørdal. She was alive at the time. ¶2

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Confidence: Confirmed as mother of Aasil Jonsdatter (see her page): the division names the children as the couple's. It is not stated whether she was Joen's only wife, but the division treats all six as children of both. Her father was named Torbjørn (from the patronymic; inference). ¶3a

The case for this identification 1

Identity: Anne Torbiørnsdatter (Torbjørnsdatter), wife of Joen Thoresen Steensland, Steensland, Tørdal annex, Drangedal. Birth range inferred only from her children, born about 1728 to about 1739-42 (the youngest son Tolleff was 20, or 23 on the card index, in Feb 1762); no baptism or census record found. ¶1

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Not Confirmed: the identification spans 1702 to 1762 with no record in between naming her parents, and her own ages are unknown (born before 1702; her youngest child about 1739-42 implies she was about 38-50 then, which is possible but late). Birth range narrowed from 'BET 1690 AND 1713' to before 1702 (she was born before her father's death). This grades her parents' link to her; her own grade (as mother of Aasil) is unchanged. ¶4b

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Next records: her death probate and burial (Drangedal/Tørdal, after 1763). ¶3b
