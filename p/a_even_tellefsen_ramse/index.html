About the family

NEW ANCESTOR, AND A DOUBLE ONE. Even (also written Evind) Tellefsen of Ramse in Tovdal is an ancestor of both Thor Tellefsen Ramse (1760-1800) and of his wife Birgitte Terjesdatter (1761-1812), by two separate descents. They were therefore distant cousins — on the present reconstruction, fourth cousins, which in the Lutheran church of the time was outside the degrees needing a royal dispensation, so their 1787 marriage required none. ¶1

He is named explicitly as a father on two Østre Råbyggelag probate cards. The abstract of the Ramse nedre ytre file says "Mannens far: Even Tollefsen Ramse" — the husband there being Svend Evensen Ramse, whose own estate was settled 13 March 1696. The same card lists Svend's sisters as "? Groe Evindsdtr. g.m. Niels" and "Signe Evindsdtr. g.m. Joen Aslaksen Gunelifjell". ¶2

His own father was evidently a Tellef, from the patronymic. Note that the 1664 priests' census of Lille Tovdal does not name him at Ramse; the Ramse occupants that year were Knud (36), Thoor (50) and Osmund (23) at Øvre Ramse and Osmund (77) and Tollef (25) at Ytre Ramse Nedre. He may be the Tollef's father's generation, or he may have been dead or at another farm in 1664. ¶6

Children: Re-reading the cards gives three more children. Rolf Evindsen was from the first marriage (card 10365). Knud Evindsen of Risland, whose estate was settled in 1725, was a brother of Svend (card 10383). Groe Evindsdatter was from the second marriage, to Anna Evindsdatter (card 10365). The cards do not say which marriage Svend, Knud and Signe came from. The deed abstracted on card 10365 was registered on 28 July 1704. ¶7

Life story

A separate handwritten card headed simply "Ramse" gives his family: "Evind Tellefsen Ramse +, g.2.g.m. Anna Evindsdtr., lever i 1704" (married secondly Anna Evindsdatter, living in 1704). Children of the first marriage: Rolf Evindsen, whose own children were Jørgen Rolfsen Ormstveit and Aslak (estate 1713). Children of the second marriage: Groe Evindsdatter, married to Nils, with children Olle Nielsen Tveit, Rolf Nielsen Tveit, Groe Nielsdatter of Høgeli and Thargier Nielsdatter. ¶3

DESCENT ONE (to Thor Tellefsen): Even Tellefsen Ramse > Groe Evindsdatter > Tellef Nielsen Tveit (d. 1703) > Thore Tellefsen Tveit (d. 1740) > Tellef Thorsen Tved (b. about 1728) > Thor Tellefsen (1760-1800). ¶4

1636-37 landskatt, Lille Topdal odelsbønder: 'Effuind ytter[?] Ramssøe, aff iiij huder Odel...; der aff hans Odels[?] part...' (first in the list). Odel holder of Ytre Ramse by 1636. No patronymic is written, so this roll does not link him to a Tellef (sum and one word uncertain). ¶14

1645 poll tax (DA 76706 view 122, p.14): under 'Øffre ytter Ramse' (a half farm): 'Tharal[d?] och hans quinde, en pige och to drenge, Biørn och hans quinde, Effuind och hans quinde, och en Encke ibid'. Effuind and his wife are this man and his wife then living, but the list does not name wives. The 1661 lead 'Olle Effuindsen Ouland' (odelsjordebok, his father alive) fits Ole Evindsen Hillestad better (see Ole Evindsen Hillestad). ¶19

1661 land register (Alminnelig jordebok for Råbyggelag, Lille Topdal, fol. 58a, DA 38714 view 62, read on the scan): 'Nedre Ramsøe 4 huder': the Crown owns ½ hud, a Svenning [farm name unclear] owns ½ hud, and 'Even Ramsøe eyer 3 huder, haaver bøxell oc bruger det' (owns 3 huder, holds the bygsel and works it). He was therefore alive and the main owner of Nedre (Ytre) Ramse in 1661, fitting the 80-year-old Effuind of the 1666 census. On the next page (fol. 58b, view 63) 'Even Ramsøe' also owns 5½ kalveskinn in Haugendal ('Hogendall 3 huder 5½ k.s.'), where a Knud and an Even each work half. The register gives no patronymic. ¶15

1661 odelsjordebok for Råbyggelag (DA 38723 view 6, Lille Topdal section, read on the scan): this register lists odel land outside the owner's farm. It has 'Olle Effuindsen [farm word, ?Ouland]', who 'angaff paa sin och sin faders vegne' 2 kalveskinn in a farm in Telemark and 7 kalveskinn in Lunde in Nissedal, and on his own account ½ hud in Risland in Åmli parish. His father was then alive. This fits the bygdebok's son Olav of Even of Ytre Ramse and the later Ramse holding at Risland (Even's son Knut, b. 1662, of Risland), but the farm word is unclear and Even is not named, so it is a lead only. The same section has 'Osmund Ramsøe' giving in his odel through his son Tollef Osmundsen (Tveiland 2 huder, Omblj, Borkenes, Øvre Eppeland, Grolleberg, Rorsland ½ hud, Bygdal, Vølstad, Bjelland, Øvre Løvrak, Haugendal 5½ k.s., Hesthaug, Vestre Tørkildsrud(?), land in Telemark and Kobstad), and Tollef Osmundsen giving in for his brother Rolleff Osmundsen (Nedre Løvrak 2 huder, Bås 1½ g.sk., Østad in Bygland 4½ k.s.). The 1661 land register (DA 38714 fol. 58b) read 'Even Ramsøe' as owner of 5½ k.s. in Haugendal; since the odel register gives exactly 5½ k.s. in Haugendal to Osmund Ramse, that owner name should be re-checked on the scan. ¶16

Re-check of the 1661 Haugendal owner (DA 38714 view 63, fol. 58b, read on the scan at full resolution): 'Hogendall 3 huder 5½ k.s.: [V?]lsborg Effuindsdaatter eyer ij hud, raader bøxell; Bunder eyer j hud 4½ k.s.; Osmund [?Wiffueland] med sin broder eyer iiij k.s.; Effuin Ramsøe eyer 5½ k.s. Opsiddere: Bunder och Effuin bruger hver sin halffue part.' The reading 'Effuin Ramsøe eyer 5½ k.s.' stands. The 1661 odelsjordebok's 5½ k.s. in Haugendal under Osmund Ramse is a separate entry; both men may have held shares of the same size, or the odel register lists the land Osmund's family claimed. Not resolved, but reading is confirmed. The woman who held 2 huder and the bygsel, '[?A]sborg Effuindsdaatter' (the 1664 vicar's census, DA 35510 view 18 p.14, has 'Asbor Effuindsdatter' as a holder at Haugendal with Bunner 30), was daughter of an Evind; the 1610 tax list has an 'Effuind' at 'Hagendall'. Not linked to Even's wife Anna Evindsdatter. ¶18

1664 vicar's census of Lille Topdal (annex of Åmli), farm 'Ytter Ramsøa Nedre, 4 huder': opsiddere Effuind 77 aar and Tollef 25[?] aar; in the sons column Rolff 20 aar (Sogneprestenes manntall 1664-66 bd 14, p.13). The 1664 bailiff's census lists at Nedre Ramse Tollef Effindsøn 33 aar and Rolf Effindsøn 17 aar; it lists men only up to age 50, which is why the old man is missing there (Fogdenes manntall 1664-66 Råbyggelaget, p.19). If Effuind is this Even/Evind Tellefsen (probable: same farm, a son Rolf, a son Tollef/Thellef; his patronymic is not written), he was born about 1587, not about 1610. Svend and Knud are not named on these pages. ¶10

1664 bailiff's census, Nedre Ramse (DA 35567 view 28, p.19): head Tollef Effuindsøn 33; sons/servants column (in farm order) Rolf Effuindsøn 17, a ? Tollefsøn 32 and Joen Gundersøn 13. Rolf Evensen fits the bygdebok's son Rolf (b. 1644). ¶17

1666 vicar's census of Lille Topdal (Sogneprestenes manntall 1664-66 bd 14, p.46), farm no. 3 'Ytre Ramsøa Nedra, 4 huder': opsiddere Effuind 80 aar (uses 3 huder) and Tollef 30 aar (uses 1 hud); sons column, against Effuind: Svend 10 aar and Knud 4 aar; servants: Throen Rasolfsøn 26, [Hal?]da 18[?], Rolff 26[?16]. Effuind is the same man as the 77-year-old of 1664 (same farm). If he is Even/Evind Tellefsen (probable; patronymic still not written), the page names his sons Svend (b. about 1656) and Knud (b. about 1662), matching the probate-card sons Svend Evensen Ramse and Knud Evindsen Risland; young sons of a man of 80 fit children of the second marriage. Stated ages 77 (1664) and 80 (1666) put his birth about 1586-87; old men's ages are often inflated. ¶11

Birth year (orchestrator, 13 Oct 2026): changed from ABT 1610 to ABT 1587, from his ages in two independent counts at Nedre Ytre Ramse: 77 in the 1664 vicar's census and 80 in the 1666 vicar's census (checked on the 1666 image: 'Effuind - 80 Aar, bruger 3 huder', sons 'Svend - 10 Aar' and 'Knud - 4 Aar'). Ages of old men in these counts are often overstated, and fathering Knud at about 75 is unusual, so the true birth may be somewhat later; the record is followed. ¶12

DESCENT TWO (to Birgitte Terjesdatter): Even Tellefsen Ramse > Svend Evensen Ramse (d. 1696) > Evind Svendsen Ramse (d. 1722) > Berte Evensdatter (m. Tjøstel Tergiesen Eppeland) > Tergie Tjøstelsen Eppeland (d. 1769) > Birgitte Terjesdatter (1761-1812). This descent rests entirely on probate cards that state each link in words. ¶5

Death: The 1715 Risland estate (Østre Råbyggelag Skifteprotokoll 4, fol. 247b, as abstracted on probate card 39942/11147) lists among its title deeds a "skiftebrev mellom borna til avl. Evind Ramse, dat. 27/5-1684" — a division deed among the children of the late Evind Ramse, dated 27 May 1684. He was therefore dead by 27 May 1684. That division deed, if it is copied in the protocol, should list all his children. ¶8

Marriages: The 1704 Ramse deeds show he married at least twice. Rolf Evindsen was a son of an earlier marriage; Anna Evindsdatter, his widow, was Rolf's stepmother and held part of the odel land for her lifetime after his death. She died before March 1704. ¶9

Corrections 1

Correction: birth widened to BET 1587 AND 1610. The census ages (77 in 1664, 80 in 1666) give 1586-87, but his sons Svend (10 in 1666) and Knud (4 in 1666) would then have been born when he was about 69 and 75, which is very unlikely; old men's ages in the 1664-66 counts are often overstated. The range keeps the census estimate as its lower end. ¶13
