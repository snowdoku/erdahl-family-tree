Life story

Possibly the 'Torsten Jonsen, reserve soldier, 23' serving in Jon Olsen's household at Kjøsnes in the 1701 manntall (canvas 114) - which would make him born about 1678 and Torsten Jonsen; not proved. ¶2

1665/66 manntall, Kjøsnes (no. 23; 35528 v24 L): users Bersvend Olsen 40, Per Nielsen 57, Biørn Olsen 59 and Thorsten Torgersen 54, with a son read 'Jon Olsen?' 26 (the 1665 list gives Thorsten 50 with a son Jon 25 - probably Jon Thorstensen). If the 1701 'Torsten Jonsen, 23' at Kjøsnes is this Torsten, his father would be that Jon and his grandfather Thorsten Torgersen - unproved; not added. ¶4

Bygdebok, Probable source only: Selbu i fortid og nåtid vol. 7, Kjøsnes (canvas 318): '1703-1719 Torsten Jonsen, f. ca. 1678, d. 1719 [...] 1704 [m.] Siri [...] Sæter, f. ca. 1680, d. 1744 [...] Hans Jonsen Renå [...] flyttet'; children Kari 1705 (m. Ola Gunnarsen Overvik), Goro 1707, Jon 1711, Gunnar 1713. This supports the 1701 identification with 'Torsten Jonsen, reserve soldier, 23' (b. c.1678) and gives his death as 1719, after which the widow moved to Renå. His parents are not stated in the recovered words (canvas 316 mentions a Jon Nilsen at Kjøsnes 'med sønner i 1701'). ¶10a

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the grade · 1 record that disagrees · 2 alternatives set aside · 1 search with no result · 3 open questions

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Why Probable: the baptism names the father and the child; Kari Torstensdatter of Overvik/Vigen (married 1726, died 1 Feb 1773 aged 68, so born 1704-05) fits the date exactly; she would have moved to Overvik on marriage. ¶3a

Records that disagree 1

Against: no later record of hers names Kjøsnes, and baptisms after mid-1705 are still being read for a rival Kari. ¶3b

Alternatives considered and set aside 2

Checked and ruled out as evidence for his parents: the probate after Gurue Jonsdatter, widow of Thomas [Eriksen?], at Kjøsnes, 1 Mar 1760 (No. 1 of 1761, Skifteprotokoll 5, 24755, fol. 100a-101a, views 116-117). Her heirs are only her own children: Ole Thomassen, Joen Thomassen 16, Ingeborg Thomasdatter (married to Joen Ammundsen[?]), Berit Thomasdatter and Marit Thomasdatter (married to Ole Findsen[?]); the minor's guardian was Bersvend Kjøsnes. No Torsten is named, so the estate neither proves nor disproves that Gurue was his sister. ¶7

Ruled out: the skifte after Guru Jonsdatter, widow of Thomas, at Kiøsnes, 1 Mar 1760 (24755 fol. 100a-b, views 116 R-117 L), was read as a possible estate of Torsten's sister. She left three sons and three daughters (Ole Thomassen, of age; Joen Thomassen 16; Ingeborg Thomasdatter married to Joen Olsen [..]; Marit Thomasdatter; a daughter married to Ole Findsen Clokvold[?]), so her own children were the heirs and no Torsten or Kari Torstensdatter is named. It does not help with Torsten's parents. Also re-read: the 1724 marriage 'Hans Renaas og Siri Garberg[?]' (16310 c94 R) at the archive's highest zoom; ink bleed from the facing page covers the bride's byname, which starts with G and is most like 'Garberg'. It cannot be read as Kiøsnes. ¶9

Searches that found nothing 1

Negative: the probate after Baard Joensen Kiøsnes, 12 Feb 1751 (Skifteprotokoll 3, 24753, fol. 310a, view 331), names only his widow Anne Einersdatter and three children, Joen Baarsen 17, Siri Baarsdatter 23 and Brynild Baarsdatter 20; no siblings, so it does not test whether Torsten Kjøsnes was a Jonsen. Not read: the 1760 probate after Gurue Jonsdatter, widow of Thomas, Kjøsnes (Skifteprotokoll 5, 24755, fol. 100, views 116-117), whose heirs are Thomassen children. ¶6

Open questions and the next record to read 3

Biography gaps: his exact birth date (before 1684) and baptism predate the 1699 Selbu register, so no surviving church book gives them. His burial is not identified in the 16310 entries for 1705-37, which were read, so a full-resolution recheck for 'Tosten Kiøsnes' is next. His death date and probate are not found: the 77804 Kjøsnes cards checked (only card 995, from 1689) hold nothing for him, so Stjør- og Verdal protocol 1 (24749, 1689-1701), unindexed and read page by page for Kjøsnes estates, is the next record. ¶5

Lead: the same 1692/93 Selbu debtor list (court book 28707 view 136 right) has 'Joen Tostensen Kiøsnes 2 D.' (patronymic read with moderate confidence). A Jon Torstensen at Kjøsnes about 1692 supports the earlier candidate (Jon, son of Thorsten Torgersen of Kjøsnes, 1664-66) as the father of Torsten (Tosten) Kjøsnes, who was 'Torsten Jonsen' 23 at Kjøsnes in 1701. Still unproved; not added. ¶8

Test record: the probate after Torsten Jonsen Kjøsnes, 1719-20 (77804 card index). ¶10b
