Life story

Baptism: Selbu, Oculi Sunday (3rd Sunday in Lent), 22 March 1778: 'Döbt Elen Par: Niels O. Langli Guri Olsd. T. Jon O. ib. Tosten Renaa Mali Langlie Anne Seth. Karen Langl.' - baptised Elen, parents Niels Olsen Langlie and Guri Olsdatter; godparents (T. = testes) Jon Olsen of the same farm, Tosten Renaa, Mali Langlie, Anne Seth[er] and Karen Langlie. Her mother was churched on Maundy Thursday 1778. Read from the scan (source 16311, canvas 87, right page). Birth date not given. She was the first child, born nine and a half months after her parents' marriage on 1 June 1777. ¶1

1801 census: 'Elen Nielsdatter', aged 23, unmarried (ugift), eldest child in the household of her parents Niels Olssen and Guri Olsdatter at Langlie, Selbu. The age fits the 1778 baptism exactly. She is a sister of John Nielsen Langli (see his page). ¶2

Probate (Probable identification): the skifte after her father Niels Olsen Langlie, closed 21 June 1816 (skifteprotokoll 10b, fol. 713), lists a married daughter whose name is faint and was read 'Elie[?]'. She is very probably Elen: Elen, Agnis and Ingebor were the only daughters in the 1801 household, and the other two are named separately. If so, Elen was married by 1816; her husband is not named. The registration of 28 December 1815 (57562) would confirm the name and may name the husband. ¶4
