Life story

1901 census (Township 61, Sturgeon, dwelling 150): Jackson, Augustine, head, born 6 March 1864, 37, with his wife Annie (born 6 March 1864) and children Margaret S. (1897), Shedrick (1898), Mark (1899) and Wilfred (1901). They lived next door to Bexy Johnston, the mother of his future second wife Edith Hicken. ¶1

Marriage (2): to Edith Gordon Hicken, 8 June 1906, Georgetown (PEI BMB index). ¶2

1911 census (Gaspereaux, dwelling 20): Austin Jackson, head, born September 1863, 47, Scotch, Baptist, fisherman, with his wife Edith and six children. ¶3

Birth at Point Pleasant, his mother's name (Angelina Jacklin), his middle name Locke and his death on 8 May 1936 at Montague are from the compiled Hicken genealogy. Confidence is Probable because the census ages vary (1863/1864) and his death has not been seen in a record. ¶4

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
