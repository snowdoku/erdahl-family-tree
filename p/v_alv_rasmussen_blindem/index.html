About the family

Married twice. His second marriage was on 29 July 1742 at Borgund, when he is entered as 'Alv Rasmuss., Enchemand' (widower) of Blindem and the bride as 'Martha Olsd., Pige' (maiden) of Uggedal. The transcription records no bondsmen (forlovere). His first wife's name has not been found; a child was baptized to him on 28 November 1742, four months after the wedding, so the first marriage must have ended shortly before. ¶2

Children baptized at Borgund with him as father, all at Blindem: 28 November 1742, 10 March 1745 (Anne), 25 December 1747 (Martha), 14 February 1751, 10 March 1754 and 6 January 1757. A son Rasmus, born about 1746, is not separately indexed but is required by the 1790 burial of Rasmus Alvsen of Blindem aged 44. ¶3

Mother found: Inger (Ingeborg) Knudsdatter, wife of Rasmus Alfsen of Blindem, married 1 Jan 1710, d. 1738; her probate of 13 May 1738 names 'Sønnen Alf Rasmussen' as heir (3A 12 fol. 481b, image 24553/216). See Inger (Ingeborg) Knudsdatter. ¶9

Life story

Farmer at Blindem (modern Blindheim) in Borgund parish, on the mainland side of the Ålesund district. Baptized 'Alf' at Borgund on 3 April 1712, son of Rasmus Alfsen; buried at Borgund on 8 November 1778 with his birth year given as 1712, which matches the baptism exactly. ¶1

1762 extra tax list (DA 49637 view 12, p. 11, Borgund sogn): at Blindem, household 4, 'M: Alv Rasmus., H: Marthe Olsd.', with children over 12 'Rasmus' and 'Anne'. So his second wife Martha Olsdatter was still living in 1762; the widow of his 1778 probate (not Martha) was therefore a later wife. Blindem then had six households (1 Matthis Iversen and Synneve Pedersdatter; 2 Knud Amundsen; 3 Niels Olsen; 4 Alv; 5 Ole Knudsen and Ragnild Willumsdatter; 6 Ole Iversen and Gjertrud Nielsdatter). ¶10

He was a man his neighbours trusted: the Borgund register lists him as a betrothal witness (trulovar) at weddings in 1766, 1769, 1772, 1774, 1775 and 1778 - six times in twelve years, which in a parish register is a mark of standing. ¶4

His estate was registered on 25 November 1778, seventeen days after his burial, at 'Blindheim' in Borgund, by the Sunnmøre sorenskriveri, protocol 3A 26, folio 193a. The index names no heirs; the protocol page would list them and is the next record to read. ¶5

Probate: the protocol page has now been read (3A 26, fol. 193a, continued on 193b; image 24567/194-195). The scan is bitonal and the hand is hard, so several words are uncertain. On 25 November 1778 a probate was held on Blindem in Borgund skipreide, with the court jurors (lagrettemenn) Ole Knudsen and Claus Clausen Blindem, after the late Alv Rasmussen. The parties were the widow, read as 'Inger Anders Datter' (Inger Andersdatter), with her chosen legal guardian (lauværge) Peder Olsen of Blindem, and the heirs: son Rasmus Alfsen, of age (myndig) and living at Blindem; son Ole Alfsen, whose age is not securely legible (perhaps 21), with a guardian whose name is unclear; and daughter Anne Alfsdatter, married to Einer Larsen (farm read tentatively as Nakken). The gross estate appears to be about 86 riksdaler. ¶6

Probate (interpretation): the widow in 1778 is not Martha Olsdatter, his 1742 bride, so Alv probably married a third time after Martha's death; no marriage record for this has been checked. A debt line on fol. 193b names a Martha (patronymic read as either Alfsdatter or Olsdatter), so it is unresolved whether daughter Martha or the late wife Martha is meant. Only three children are clearly named; one uncertain line before 'Datter Anne' might name another heir. A greyscale rescan or the Borgund burials 1757-1778 for a Martha Olsdatter of Blindem would settle this. ¶7

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: Confirmed, graded on his link to his son Rasmus Alvsen Blindem (see his page), not on his own parentage (Probable). The record naming the relationship is his probate, Sunnmøre sorenskriveri 3A 26, fol. 193a-193b, 25 Nov 1778 at Blindem (image 24567/194-195, scan read earlier), whose first heir is 'Søn Rasmus Alfsen myndig og boende paa Blindem' (son Rasmus Alvsen, of age, living at Blindem); the same legible line already carries Rasmus's own Confirmed grade. Continuity: Rasmus Alvsen is the farm holder ('Grdm.') of Blindem buried at Borgund on 1 Jan 1790 aged 44, on the same farm 11 years later, and the father of Anne Martha baptised at Blindem in 1776. ¶8a

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

No rival: Alv Rasmussen was the only Alv on Blindem in the eighteenth century, and no other Rasmus Alvsen appears there. ¶8b
