Life story

Birth: Find a Grave gives 12 February 1902, Deuel County, South Dakota. The project currently holds 2 December 1901; the conflict is noted, not resolved. A birth in February 1902 fits his parents' marriage of 6 May 1901 better than one in December 1901 does. ¶8

Death registered in Lac qui Parle County, Minnesota, 29 September 1962. ¶2

Death: 29 September 1962, Lac qui Parle County - Minnesota death certificate index 1962-MN-009570, mother's maiden name KULSETHMO. ¶5

Life: 'Anton's parents were both immigrants from Selbu, Norway. Many farmers in the area were from the same village in Norway. Anton was educated at the one-room school house; and was fluent in Norwegian and English... Anton, or Tony as most people called him, worked with his father on the farm most of his life. In his later years, he lived with his mother and sister, Benda in Rochester. Tony never married. He is buried at Zoar Lutheran, which his family helped to build.' ¶7

Burial: Zoar Lutheran Cemetery, lot 1 (South Dakota state burial register, 'ANTON B EIDEM 1962, lot 1'). ¶9

Corrections 3

Correction: his father was Anders A. Eidem, not Mikal Paulsen Eidem. His own birth registration says so. It is a delayed registration, received 17 June 1935 and filed 16 July 1935 at Marietta on the affidavit of 'Mrs. A. A. Eidem' and signed by Paul Eidem: 'County of Lac qui Parle, Township of Augusta. Full name of child: Anton Bernhard Eidem. Sex M. Date of birth December 2, 1901. FATHER: Anders A. Eidem, Augusta Twp, White, age at last birthday 48, birthplace Norway, Farmer. MOTHER: full maiden name Brynhild Kulsethmo, White, birthplace Norway.' Lac qui Parle County certificate 1901-10783. ¶3

Correction to Find a Grave, which gives his birth as 12 February 1902: the civil registration, made on his own mother's sworn affidavit, says 2 December 1901. ¶4

Correction: his father was ANDERS ANDERSSON EIDEM (1852/3-1932), not Mikal Paulsen Eidem. Find a Grave memorial 140436487 records his parents as Anders Andersson Eidem and Brynhild Kulsetmoen Eidem, and carries a biography by a descendant of Anders's brother Bardo: 'Anton Eidem was the first of three children born to Anders A. and Brynhild Kulsetmoen (spellings vary) Eidem, on their farm close to Marietta, MN. The farm was almost on the border between MN and SD. Anton's mother was a widow with three small daughters when she married Anton's father. (Anton's father Anders Eidem and my g.g.grandfather Bardo Eidem were brothers.)' ¶6
