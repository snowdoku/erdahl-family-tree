Life story

Name: patronymic Torgersdatter added from the 1763 Lesja manntall (45390 view 286), Eneboe: 'Peder Pederson, Guri Torgersdr.'. ¶3

Patronymic found. The 1763 Lesja extra-tax manntall (source 45390, view 286), farm No. 4 Eneboe, lists the couple 'Peder Pederson, Guri Torgersdr.' (second household of the farm, no children over 12 - their eldest, Marit b. 1754, was 9). So Peder Pedersen Eneboe's wife in 1763 was Guri Torgersdatter, the Guri churched in 1757. On the same farm the husmann household at Kiønsletten was 'Siver Pederson Kiønsletten, Marit Torgersdr., Børn Peder, Torgen, Anne; Moderen Gjøda Larsdotter'. Marit Torgersdatter of Kiønsletten is probably Guri's sister: 'Marit Kiønsletten' stood godmother to Anna in 1757, and Anne Pedersdatter was living 'of Kiønsletten' when she married in 1783, on the same day as Torger Siversen Kiønsletten (Marit's son Torgen). If so, Guri's parents were Torger ___ and Gjøda Larsdatter (not entered: the sister link is inferred). ¶4a

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 3

Speculative mother of Anne Pedersdatter b. 1757 (see her page); graded on her link to Anne. The Lesja bygdebok (vol. 1, canvas 183, chapter Einbu, word-box rebuild) makes Peder Pedersen of Eneboe (b. about 1727) marry 2nd in 1753 'Guri... her (6-1), 1730-?', with children Marit 1754, Anne 1757 (= the wife of Peder Engebretsen, entry 21-1) and Peder 1761. Contradiction: on the page of the 1757 baptism (9236 p. 433, canvas 209), the churching of Peder Eneboe's wife was read as 'Barbara', not Guri. Either that reading, or the bygdebok, or the identification of the 1757 child is wrong, so she is entered as Speculative only. ¶1a

Upgrade (to Probable): the Barbara contradiction is resolved. Re-reading 9236 p. 433 (canvas 209) at 3000 px: the Oculi 1757 churching reads 'Introd: Peder Eners. Qvinde Barbara' - the wife of Peder ENERSEN (Einarsen), a different man - directly above the baptism 'Peder Eneboes B: Anna fød d. 4 Martii'. Lower on the same page, at Dom. Palmarum [3 Apr] 1757 at Verket, the churchings read 'Introd: Ole Qvams Qvinde Giertrud, it. Peder Eneboes Guri, it. Elling Sandems Marit, Johan Trysils Ingeborg'. Guri was thus Peder Eneboe's wife and was churched four weeks after Anna's birth, so she is the mother of the 4 Mar 1757 baby, as the bygdebok says (Peder Pedersen m. 2nd 1753 Guri, b. 1730). Raised from Speculative to Probable, graded on her link to Anne (see her page): she is now proved mother of the baby Anna of 1757, but the identification of that baby with the 1783 bride Ane Pedersdatter Kiønsletten (26 years later, age 44 in 1801) still rests on the bygdebok and on age and place. ¶2a

Upgrade (near-miss):, graded on her link to Anne Pedersdatter b. 1757 (see her page). The 1757 baptism names 'Peder Eneboes' child Anna (b. 4 Mar 1757), and on the same page 'Peder Eneboes Guri' was churched at Palmarum [3 Apr] 1757, four weeks after the birth (9236 c209). The 'Barbara' churching on that page belongs to Peder Enersen, another man. In the 1763 manntall (45390 v286) the couple 'Peder Pederson, Guri Torgersdr.' are on No. 4 Eneboe. The confirmation list for 19 p. Trin. 1775 (9236 p. 47, canvas 25) has 'Anne Pedersd. Eneboe 18', and in 1783 'Ane Pedersd.' married from Kiønsletten, a husmann place under Eneboe (engagement indexed as 'of Eneboe'). Gaps: 18 years, with the parents on the farm in 1763 and the far end tied to the farm by patronymic plus farm, with no named rival (audit 7); then 8 years. See the matching note on Peder Pedersen Eneboe for the rival check. This supersedes the earlier caveat that the baby's identification with the 1783 bride rests on the bygdebok. ¶6
