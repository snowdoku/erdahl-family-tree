About the family

Wife of Peder Olsen of Skjong (engaged 20 Jul 1777) and mother of Elisabeth Pedersdatter. Age 50 in the 1801 census at Sjaang. ¶1

Mother (Probable, circumstantial): Siri Pedersdatter of Sunde (see her page) is added as her mother on a strong circumstantial fit with no contradicting evidence. Knud Jensen married Siri Pedersdatter of Sunde in Nov 1739. Every child baptised to Knud from 1740 to 1750, Christiana included, falls within that marriage, and no other marriage of Knud is known. Siri is the widow in the 1765 probate that names Christhana (15) as the deceased's daughter. Christiana's guardian there, Hendrich Olsen Sunde, came from Siri's home farm. Her baptism of 20 Oct 1750 names no mother in the index, and the probate calls the heirs only 'the deceased's children'. The records that would confirm the link are the scan of her 1750 baptism, if it names the mother, and the widow's share (Enkens udlæg) in the same probate, Skifteprotokoll 15A views 442-444, if it calls Siri the children's mother. Her own confidence stays Probable. ¶5

Life story

Baptism (Probable): Christiana, daughter of Knud Jensen of Mychlebust, was baptized 20 Oct 1750 at Borgund, and a Christiana Knudsdatter of Mychlebust was confirmed 18 Oct 1767. Date given is the baptism date. Died a retired wife (kårkone) at Schiong 10 Jun 1821, age 70; buried 16 Jun 1821. ¶2

1762 extra tax list (DA 49637 view 42, p. 71): listed as 'Christiana' (daughter, '1 D.') in the Myklebust household of Knud Jensen and Siri Pedersdatter, with her brother Jens. See Knud Jensen Myklebust for why her parents' grade stays Probable. ¶8

Probate of her father (scan read): at the probate after Knud Jensen of Myklebust, 3 Aug 1765 (Sunnmøre Skifteprotokoll 15A = 3A 21, fol. 428b), the heirs include '2de Datter Christhana Knuds datter gl. 15 aar' (second daughter, aged 15) with guardian Hendrich Olsen Sunde. This makes her link to Knud Jensen explicit and agrees with her baptism of 20 Oct 1750. Her sister Lisbeth (22) and brother Jens (of age) are the other heirs. The widow Siri Pedersdatter is named, but the children are described only as 'the deceased's children', so the record does not state that Siri was her mother. That is a strong inference: Knud married Siri in Nov 1739, every known child was baptised 1740-1750, and the probate mentions no earlier marriage. Her confidence is not raised, because her link to her mother is still not explicit. ¶4

Probate (scan read; audited): the joint probate of the old couple Peder Olsen and Christiane Knudsdatter of Skjong, 15 Sep 1821 (Nordre Sunnmøre 3A 13 s. 119b-120a), names as heirs the daughter Lisbeth Pedersdatter, wife of Jon[?] Jørgensen of Valderøy (= Elisabeth Pedersdatter), the daughter Kanutha (widow of Lars Olsen Skjong) and Malene (unmarried). This proves she is Elisabeth's mother; that link is carried on Elisabeth Pedersdatter's confidence. Her own confidence stays Probable, because it rests on the 1750 Myklebust baptism (father Knud Jensen), which the probate does not address. ¶3

2 on the grade

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Upgrade: the probate after her stepfather Peder Andersen of Schiong (Skjong), 9 Jul 1775 (Sunnmøre Skifteprotokoll 16C = 3A 25, fol. 843a-844a; scan), was held between his widow 'Siri Peders Datter', whose guardian at law was 'Jens Knudsen Mochelbust' (her son), and the deceased's own four children. Among the estate's debts on fol. 843b it lists a sum owed to 'Pigen Christhina Knuds Datter' (the unmarried girl Christina Knudsdatter), immediately followed by a sum owed to Jens Knudsen Mochelbust. The two children of Knud Jensen of Myklebust and Siri Pedersdatter thus appear together in the Skjong household two years before 'Pigen Christianna Knudsdtr.' of Schiong was engaged (20 Jul 1777) to Peder Olsen of Schiong. This bridges the 1765 probate of Knud Jensen (daughter Christhana aged 15) to the wife of Peder Olsen Skjong (1777 marriage; joint probate 1821 naming Elisabeth). With Siri's 1766 remarriage to Peder Andersen of Skjong, it settles the 1750 Myklebust baptism as hers. The exact wording of the debt (perhaps her paternal inheritance held by the estate) is only partly legible. ¶6

Audit (1 Nov 2026): left at Confirmed, on the 1821 joint probate that names her as Elisabeth's mother. Her link to her parents Knud Jensen and Siri Pedersdatter is Probable, not Confirmed: the 1775 Skjong debt entry and the shared name and age make a strong circumstantial case, but no record read names the wife of Peder Olsen Skjong as their daughter. Both parents are set to Probable to show this. ¶7
