About the family

Parents (bygdebok, Probable): Valdres bygdebok I D (Svenes), Hovrud gnr. 76 (canvas 643): 'Eivind Jonsson', b. about 1683, son of Jon Eivindsson (d. 1699) and Anne Knutsdotter (1655-1713), who married secondly in 1700 Erik Embriksson; Erik gave the farm by deed in 1717 to 'stesonen Eivind Jonsson'. The book's date 14.2.1713 for Anne resolves the year of the skifte letter recited in the 1728 probate ('14de Febr. 17?3... Moder Ane Knudsdaatter') as 1713, so search of the Feb 1723 skifter could not find it. Linked parents Jon Evindsen and Anne Knudsdatter. Birth set ABT 1683 from the book. ¶5a

Life story

The Feb 1723 skifter searched: in DA 25285 the run reaches 9 Sep 1722 at view 600 (fol. 583) and 25 Sep 1723 at view 625. Views 603-607 hold the skifter of mid-February 1723: view 603 (fol. 590b-591a), 'Rønnes[?]' in Aurdal, dated 13 or 14 Feb 1723 ('d. 1[3/4]de Febr.'), after Marit Bendixdatter, widower Erik Olsen, eldest daughter married to Ole Gulbrandsen; view 605, Quien in Vang, 19 Feb 1723; view 607, Førstad in Volbu, 27 Feb 1723. No skifte after an Ane Knudsdatter on or about 14 Feb 1723 appears there, so the letter cited in 1728 was either held under another court or the recital's name or date is misread. If the Rønnes skifte is the one meant, Ane Knudsdatter is not its deceased. The parents of Even Jonsen remain unknown. ¶4

Farmer at Haaverud (Hovrud), Aurdal, Valdres. Probate (index cards): his first wife Marit Juulsdatter died before 7 Oct 1728, when her estate was held (skifteprotokoll 1716-1737, fol. 914a) with children Juul 11, Jon 5, Lars 3 and Anne 8. His second wife Sigrid Olsdatter died before 12 Feb 1751; at her estate (skifteprotokoll 1744-52, fol. 353b) 'Even Johnsøn' is the living widower and the children of his first marriage are Juel, John, Lars and Anna. Birth inferred only from his eldest known child (about 1717). Confirmed as father of Jul Evensen (b. about 1717) by the 1728 card; Jul's own link to Even Julsen is Probable. ¶1

Protocol read: the 1728 Haaverud estate (DA 25285 fol. 914a-914b, views 931-932) is held after 'Marit Juulsdatter' between 'hendes efterlevende Egtemand Even Jonsøn og begge deres sammenavlede 4 Børn': sons Juul Evensen 11, Jon Evensen 5, Lars Evensen 3, daughter Anne Evensdatter 8 1/2. Lead for his own parents (not adopted; reading uncertain): the recital of how the farm was held, at the foot of fol. 914b and top of fol. 915a, cites a skifte letter dated 14 Feb 1723 and a deed of 12 Mar 1717, and names 'Enkemandens Olde[?] Moder Ane[?] Knudsdaatter', a 'Knud Engebretsen' and a 'Lars Engebretsen'. If the word is 'Moder', Even's mother was a Knudsdatter and the 14 Feb 1723 skifte is probably his parent's estate; it should be in the same protocol (5C, 1716-1737) and on a Haaverud card of 1723. ¶2a

Recital re-read at full scan resolution: the URN scan of DA 25285 view 932 (3226 px) is the full resolution available; the IIIF manifest returns 404 for this protocol. The top of fol. 915a reads 'Skiftebrevs Indhold af dato 14de Febr. 1723 formeldt efter [Enke]mandens Ole[?] Moder Ane Knudsdaatter...', followed by 'Knud Engebretsen'. The word before 'Moder' is written 'Ole' (or 'Olde') with a flourish; it is not 'Sal.' and the reading 'Moder' alone is ruled out. 'Oldemoder' (great-grandmother) cannot be literal for a man born before 1695 if she died about 1722, so the word is unresolved: either a scribe's 'Olde Moder' for a grandmother, or a person's name. The 1723 skifte is therefore after Ane Knudsdatter, an older relative of Even Jonsen (probably a grandmother), not after one of his parents. The protocol (DA 25285) reaches 9 Sep 1722 at view 600 (fol. 583) and 25 Sep 1723 at view 625 (fol. 612b), so the 14 Feb 1723 skifte lies between views 601 and 624. Not adopted as a parent. ¶3
