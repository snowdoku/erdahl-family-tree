Life story

Birth (index): she is one of the two surviving Birgitthes baptised to Mads of Moen at Vold church: 4 Aug 1733 (pd00000042618666, Brit the elder of the 1749 and 1758 probates) or 7 Apr 1738 (pd00000042620427, Brit the younger). Two earlier Birgitthes (1730, 1732) died as infants. Which of the two married Ole Svendsen is not known; either way her mother is Guri Ingebrigtsdatter. The stepsister Marith (first wife's child, born before Dec 1729) is very unlikely to be the bride by age: see the note on Guri Ingebrigtsdatter. 'Birgitte Matsd.' of Moe was godmother at Grytten in 1757, 1761, 1768 and three times in 1769, and not after the 1770 wedding. ¶5

Engagement (scan read): Grytten ministerialbok 1764-1806 (1539P), p. 110, 1770, Eid church, 3rd Sunday after Easter. The widower Ole Svendsøn of Korsen in Rødven parish ('Enkemand Ole Svendsøn Korsen av Rødvens Sogn') was betrothed to the maiden 'P. Birgite Mats D. Moe' (Birgitte Madsdatter of Moe). Sureties: Svend Botholphsøn Alfarnæs and Knud Olsøn Rødven. The Digitalarkivet index gives the engagement date as 16 Apr 1770 (pv00000010053250). ¶1

Marriage (scan read): same book, p. 111, Eid church, Sunday Jubilate 1770 (6 May 1770 in the index, pv00000010053270): 'NB. Brudevielse: Enkemand Ole Svendsøn Korsen av Rødven Sogn og P. Marite Mats D. Moe'. The clerk wrote 'Marite' here, against 'Birgite' at the engagement three weeks earlier; the letters match the 'Marite' of the line above on the same page, so it is not a misreading. Both names belong to daughters of Mads Johansen Moe (his 1749 probate names Brith the elder, Brith the younger, Anna and Marith). The name Brit (Birgitte) is preferred here because (1) the engagement entry is the fuller one, with both sureties; (2) a 'Birgite Madsd.' of Moe stood godmother at Grytten church on 9 Apr 1768 (baptism of Peder s. of Hans Larsøn Bøe, pd00000042635893), so an unmarried Birgitte Madsdatter was living at Moe two years before; (3) Ole's daughter of this marriage was named Guraa (1774), after Guri Ingebrigtsdatter, who was the mother of the Briths but not of Marith. The first name is therefore not certain; the identity of the woman is. ¶2

Death: no burial or probate found for a Brit/Birgitte/Marit Madsdatter at Korsand (Kaarsan, Korsan) 1774-97 in the Digitalarkivet indexes; the only Korsan burial in the window is Erich Olsøn, 1791. Searched by domicile Kaars*/Kors*/Korsan* and by name. ¶6

Bygdebok (Holm sokn bygdebok 2, 1999, nb.no item 7e82a0c0b53810567f93385b0678d4de, canvas 665, rebuilt from word boxes): Ole Svendsen Korsan's second wife (married 1770) is given as '... Moen, 1738-93'. If the compiler is right, she is the younger Birgitthe, baptised 7 Apr 1738 at Vold church (pd00000042620427), not the elder (1733), and she died about 1793. Both dates come from a compiled secondary source and a gapped rebuild, so the birth and death fields are left unchanged. ¶8a

The case for this identification 1

Why she is Confirmed as mother of Guri (Giøri, Guraa) Olsdatter: Ole Svendsen's 1806 probate (Romsdal skifteprotokoll 13, s. 172b-173a, read) names three children 'udi andet Ægteskab' (of the second marriage): Peder, Anne and the unmarried Gurie. His first wife Anne Steensdatter died in 1769 (probate 28 Feb 1770, read), and this marriage of May 1770 is his next one; the children of the second marriage were baptised May 1771 (Anne), Jan 1773 (Peder) and 20 Aug 1774 (Guraa), and his third marriage, to Anne Larsdatter, came only in 1797, when he was again a widower. The 1770 marriage record therefore makes this woman the wife of the second marriage, and the probate makes Guri a child of that marriage. Two primary records, each read on the scan, tie her to Guri; no step rests on elimination. ¶3

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Caution: the only Korsand burial of a married woman near that date is 'Berette Peders D.', Jan 1794, aged 63 (Veøy 16043 p. 430 as clearly 'Peders D.'), and the compiler may have taken her death year from that entry. Her grade (on her link to Guri (Giøri, Guraa) Olsdatter) is unchanged. ¶8b

Searches that found nothing 2

Death: not found. She died between Aug 1774 (churching after Guraa's baptism) and 1797 (Ole's third marriage as a widower). The only married woman buried from Korsand in those years in the Veøy index is 'Qvinden Berette Peders D. Kaarsan', buried Jan 1794 aged 63 (Veøy 16043 p. 430). Re-: the patronymic is clearly 'Peders D.', so that burial is not hers unless the clerk erred; it is more likely the wife of the other Korsand household. Her burial may be in Grytten or elsewhere, or missed by the index. ¶4

Negative (scan read): the Romsdal 1764 main tax roll (Rentekammeret, DA 49638, view 52, p. 173) for No. 313 Moe gives 'Lars Larsen, Birgithe Just[s]d[atte]r' (not a Madsdatter) as the couple, with Ole Larsen (corporal), Niels Arnesen, Agathe Larsdatter, Gunn Larsdatter and Agatha Olsdatter (inderste) among the others; 6 persons. No Brit or Birgitte Madsdatter is on this Moe, so the roll does not show which of the two Briths (b. 1733 or 1738) was the 1770 bride. Her Moe family was at 'Moen i Wolds Otting' (1758 probate), which may be a different matrikkel number from No. 313 in Grytten sogn; the next canvases to try are 49638 views 44-60 for another Moe/Moen. ¶7
