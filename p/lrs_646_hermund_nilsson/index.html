Life story

Ættebok for Lavik (1983, nb.no 8d133e3cb711f2ec5c05edb513806d88), Østerbø bruk 1, canvas 295 (gaps are words not recovered): the owner text says '... Mons [..] Grøneng kjøpte dette...' (Mons of Grøneng bought this [part of the farm]); the family table gives 'Mons [..] Grøneng', married 1705, '(1671—1745)'; '1. Elsa Hermundsdtr. ([16?8]—1740) dotter til Hermund Nilssson [..]'; '2. Synneva Andersdtr. (1719—1759)', married 1742 (children Johannes 1742, Ole 1743, Else 1744); among the first marriage's children are Nils 1723-1771 Østerbø, d. Gjertrud, and no. 4 b. 1712 d. 1793 Oppedal (Britte, wife of Torkel Iversen of Indre Oppedal). Dates are ABT (rebuilt text). Elsa's birth year is OCR-damaged ('1628'): it cannot be 1628, and with children to about 1724 it is more likely about 1680-85 than 1668, so no birth year is entered. ¶1

The Bruield candidate is now tied to a daughter Else by a primary record. Probate after 'dannequinde Sal. Dorethe Olsdaatter' on Bruheld in Kvamsøy skipreide, 27 May 1701 (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll A 1b, fol. 329a, Digitalarkivet 24154 viewer 113 right page; index pa00000000519920, where the farm is spelled 'Bruhjell'), 'imellem hendes efterlevende fornumstig dannemand Hermund Nielsen Bruheld paa dend eene, og deris tilsammen avlede børn Nafnlig Andfind, Samson, Gietlof, Elsse, Agaattha, Sara og Sigri, Hermunds Sønner og døttre, paa dend anden Side'. On fol. 329b the debts include 'Niels Hermundsen Arfve paa sin Moderne arf' (so Hermund also had a son Niels, apparently by an earlier wife), a loan from Samson Nielsen, and 'Gietlouf og Agaate Hermunds døttre' for long service in their parents' house. This is the man of the 1701 census (Hermund Nielsen, bunde, 60, Bruield) and the 1664 manntall ('Bruield, Hermund'). ¶6

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

2 on the grade · 5 alternatives set aside · 2 open questions

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Graded on his link to his daughter Else Hermundsdatter: Probable, capped (compiled secondary source only). The book names Elsa Hermundsdtr., first wife of Mons of Østerbø (m. 1705, she d. 1740), as 'dotter til Hermund Nilssson'; that is the same woman as the Else Hermundsdatter whose probate of 5 Apr 1740 on Østerbø divides between her widower Mons Torbersen and their children, Brite wife of Torkel of Indre Oppedal among them. The farm word after his name was not recovered (queried about 2,200 words, including about 250 Sogn farm names). Records that would test it: a probate after Hermund Nielsen in the Ytre Sogn probate index (none found 1690-1745 under that name), or a deed or tingbok entry naming Else Hermundsdatter or Mons Torbersen as heir of Hermund Nielsen. ¶2

Grade stays Probable on his link to Else Hermundsdatter: Else Hermundsdatter of Bruheld (unmarried at home in 1701) is identified with Else Hermundsdatter, wife of Mons Torbersen at Østerbø in Lavik (d. before 5 Apr 1740) across two farms and districts, so the identification is circumstantial, though strong: (1) the Ættebok for Lavik calls Elsa the daughter of 'Hermund Nilssson'; (2) Mons Torbersen came from Tyom og Grøneng in the same Tjugum sogn as Bruield (1701 census); (3) Else's children in her 1740 probate include a son Niels (Hermund's patronymic) and a daughter Dordej (Dorothea, after Dorethe Olsdatter), plus Giertrud (cf. her sister Gietlof/Giertrud). No other Else Hermundsdatter is known in Ytre Sogn 1700-1705. ¶7a

Alternatives considered and set aside 5

Not identified in the 1701 census: the indexed Hermund Nielsens of 1701 in Sogn (Bruield, 60, with Andfind Hermunsen 33; Leerum, 56; Hofland, servant 16) are in the indexed Indre Sogn rows and are not linked. He is a different man from Hermund Hendrichsen of Indre Takle (see his page), whose 1716 heirs do not include Else. ¶3

Candidate identification (inference only, grade unchanged at Probable): the only Hermund Nielsen/Nilsson of the right age in Ytre Sogn in 1701 is Hermund Nielsen, bunde on Bruield, 60 (b. about 1641), Leikanger prestegjeld (Tjugum), with Hans Nielsen, servant 19, and Andfind Hermunsen, bunde 33 (b. about 1668, possibly his son) on the same farm (1701 census p. 30, pc00000000627565-67). Correction of: Bruield is NOT in Indre Sogn; the 1701 row is in the Leganger parish of Ytre Sogn fogderi, on the same page as 'Tyom og Grøneng', where Mons Torbergsen, 28, was a servant. The 1664 manntall (35573 viewer 115, s.210) lists the farm 'Bruield' in 'Tiugum Kierche-Sogn' with farmer 'Hermund' (no age given for farmers), tjenestedreng 20 Olle Arnesen and husmann 54 Anders Hagen; Grøneng is on the facing page (s.211). So a Hermund held Bruield from 1664 to 1701 in the same parish as Grøneng, the farm Elsa's husband Mons came from, which fits the Ættebok's 'Hermund Nilssson'. ¶4a

Not proved: no record names Elsa as daughter of the Bruield Hermund. ¶4b

Rival: the other 1701 Hermund Nielsen of Sogn (Leerum, 56) is in Indre Sogn. ¶4c

Not to be confused with Andfind Hermundsen of Skrenes (b. about 1646; 1701 census), whose probate of 9 Oct 1715 (2b fol. 466a, view 151) names his widow Christi Hermundsdatter[?] and children Hermund, Gute[?], Anders, Niels[?], Lars[?], Ingeborg and Giertrud; that man is too old to be a son of Hermund Nielsen (b. about 1641). ¶5

Open questions and the next record to read 2

Test record: a probate after Hermund Nielsen of Bruield (not in the Ytre Sogn probate index under Bru*, 1698-1760, nor under 'Hermund Niel*'); the next is a tingbok/pantebok entry for Bruield, or a probate after Andfind Hermundsen of Bruield naming his sister Else, wife of Mons Torbersen. ¶4d

Test record: Mons Torbersen and Else's marriage (about 1705; Lavik church books do not survive so early), or a later probate of one of Else's siblings (Andfind, Samson, Gietlof, Agatha, Sara, Sigri Hermundsdatter) naming 'Else gift med Mons Torbersen Østerbø' as an heir. ¶7b
