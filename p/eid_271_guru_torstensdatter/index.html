About the family

Widow of Erik Svendsen Uglem at his probate, 20 Jun 1741: 'Enke: Guru Tostensdtr.' (card 77804 c2055); mother of Torsten Eriksen (8) and his five siblings. Her brother was Siver (Sivert) Torstensen Berge ('morbroren'). Her eldest daughter Ragnild was 17 in 1741, so she married about 1722 or earlier. ¶1

Her father is now identified (Probable) as Torsten Siursen of Berge (see his page). Chain of primary records: (1) her husband's probate, 20 Jun 1741 (card 77804 c2055), names her brother 'Siver Tostensen Berge' as 'morbroren' of her daughters Ragnild and Brynild; (2) the probate after Guri Mogensdatter at Berge, 21 Jun 1752 (24753 fol. 448b-449a), names Siver Tostensen Berge and Halder Tostensen Berge as 'farbrødre' of Ole Tostensen Berge's children, so Siver, Haldor and Ole were brothers; (3) the 1701 manntall lists at Berge Torsten Siursen 50 with sons Siur 10, Haldor 6 and Oller 4, the same three brothers. Her son Torsten Eriksen (b. about 1733) is named for him. Probable, not Confirmed, because no record names her as his daughter. ¶3

Life story

Mother added from the bygdebok (Selbu i fortid og nåtid vol. 5, Berge, canvas 21): Torsten Sjurdsen's wife was Brynhild, d. 1719, and their daughter '4. Goro, f. ca. 1698, g.m. Erik [...] Uglem'. The book's birth estimate (ca. 1698) fits the tree's 'BEF 1705' and her daughter Ragnild's age 17 in 1741. Brynhild is Probable (book only). ¶6

The protocol entry for her husband's skifte (24752 fol. 1049-1049b, 20 Jun 1741) names her 'Guri Tostensdatter' and calls Siver Tostensen Berge 'Børnenes Morbroder', which confirms from the protocol itself what card 77804 c2055 said. It gives no parent of hers. ¶5

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Possible burial: 'Begravne: 1. Guru Tostensd. død d. 12 Febr. 85 aar gl.' (buried Sexagesima Sunday 1783; died 12 Feb 1783 aged 85, so born about 1698), Selbu 16311 c110 left page (listed). No farm or husband is given. Her birth about 1698 fits Guru, the mother of Ragnild (17 in 1741), but the identification is unproved, so no death date is set. If she was born in 1698 her baptism falls before the Selbu register begins in 1699, which is why no baptism can confirm her father. ¶4

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Biography gaps: her exact birth date (before 1705) is not in any record read, and her marriage to Erik Svendsen Uglem (before about 1722) is not found because Selbu canvases c80-95 were read only at overview level, so a full read there is next. Her death, death place and burial are not found and the 77804 index holds only her 1741 appearance, so the next check is the 16315 burial entries for 1739-46 (canvases 0-94) under Guru or Gjertrud Torstensdatter. ¶2
