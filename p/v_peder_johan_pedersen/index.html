Life story

Baptism: Probable. A 'Peder Johannes', son of Peder Pedersen of Giske-Ødegård, was baptized 30 Oct 1814 at Borgund. The father had been buried 21 Aug 1814, and the index note 'K: afgangen Peder Pedersen' appears to mean 'wife of the late Peder Pedersen'. In the 1815 census, Peder Pedersen, aged 1/2, is listed as the child of Gurri Olesdatter at Giske-Ødegård. ¶4

Death: Probable. Peder Pedersen, farmer at Røssevold (born 1815 per the index), died 30 Jun 1876 and was buried 5 Jul 1876. Gjertrud was a widow by 1891. ¶5

Married Gjertrud Birgitte Olsdatter of Røssevold 18 Sep 1842 at Borgund. He was a bachelor from Giske-Ødegård, born about 1814; his father is named as Peder Pedersen. ¶2

Freeholder farmer at Røssevold: 'Peder J. Pedersen', age 52, in the 1865 census and born 1814 in the 1875 census. ¶3
