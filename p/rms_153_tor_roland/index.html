Life story

'Tor Rølang' (read as 'Rø[s?]land'; the zoomed scan shows 'Rølang'). In Feb 1707 he attended the probate after Helge Skafhelden of Søndre Strand as the children's grandfather ('Bestefaderen Tor Rølang', DA 25284 s. 331b, view 336), claimed 4 rdr 'som arfvingernes Udgiftspenge' (s. 336a, view 340), and was appointed guardian of the eldest son: 'først for den Eldste Søn Thor Helgesen dend dannemand Morfaderen Tor Rølang' (s. 338a, view 343). Morfaderen = the mother's father. ¶1

Farm: 'Rølang' is almost certainly Røllang (Rudlang, Rødlang), Ulnes, Nord-Aurdal (Valdres card index DA 39948, cards 11649-11693). That run has no card for a Tor (cards of 1698 Nordre Røllang, Tollef Samsonsøn; 1739-40 Søndre Røllang, Mogens Torsen and Marite Amundsdatter, children Tord, Amund, Ole, Turi, Jørand; later cards 1752-1886). Mogens Torsen of Søndre Røllang (d. 1740) is a candidate son (lead only, not entered). ¶3

Røllang/Rudlang search (negative for kinship). Every Røllang/Rudlang card in DA 39948 (11649-11693) was read: before 1800 there are only 1698 (Tollef Samsonsen, Nordre Røllang), 14 Oct 1739 (Marite Amundsdatter, wife of Mogens Torsen, Søndre Røllang, S. 297) and 9 Feb 1740 (Mogens Torsen, S. 358), and 1752 (Nye-Stølen). No card for a Tor. The 1739 and 1740 protocol entries (DA 25286 views 160-161, 191-193) were read: Mogens Torsen bought Søndre Rudlang in 1728 (from 'Peder... Witter') and a part of Nordre Rudlang on 18 Jun 1725, so he did not inherit the farm; his eldest son is Tord (18 1/2), which fits a father named Tor but proves nothing; the guardians of the five children (p. 363) are the maternal uncle ('Morbroderen') and unrelated men, with no Farbroder or Faster and no mention of Tor or of Helge Skafhelden's children. Mogens Torsen is therefore NOT added as Tor Rølang's son. ¶5
