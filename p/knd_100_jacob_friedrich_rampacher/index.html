Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Graded on that link: her own first marriage at Vaihingen on 4 Jul 1786 names her father, bride row 'Jacobina Friederika Kampacher' (= Rampacher), relatives 'Jacob Friedrich, Johann Christoph' (record 904787916, image 1184562-00922), and her Vaihingen birth on 6 Apr 1759 names 'Jacob Fridrich, Maria Jacobina' (record 4775787; FamilySearch QPGK-LDW2, labelled Father/Mother). Continuity to the woman in the tree: the 1786 bride had a Fressel child in 1788 and remarried as the widow 'Fessels' in 1791 (Vaihingen throughout; her sons' records call her Rampacher). Ancestry 61023 index rows do not label roles (BRIEF audit 5 rule 3), so this rests at Probable until the image or a labelled FamilySearch twin is read; images not seen. A labelled FamilySearch twin of the 1786 marriage would make this Confirmed. ¶1b

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed, graded on his link to his daughter Jacobina Friederike (see her page), the wife of Christoph Ludwig Knodel. Record naming the relationship: marriage at Vaihingen 4 Jul 1786 (QPGK-T6TY, labelled), groom Johann Christoph Fußel, Wife 'Jacobina Friederika Kampacher' (K for R), Father-in-law 'Jacob Friedrich Kampacher'. Continuity to the woman in the tree, all at Vaihingen, each step 2-4 years: 1786 she marries Fußel; 7 Oct 1788 a daughter Jacobina Wilhelmina is born to Christoph Friedrich Fressel x Jacobina Friederika (QPG2-WGRS); 25 Jan 1791 'Jacobina Friederika Fessels' (the widow, under her married name) marries Christoph Ludwig Knodel (QPGK-ZG18); 14 Mar 1795 and 1 Feb 1798 the Knodel children's baptisms name their mother Jacobina Friederike 'Rambach' / 'Rampacherin' (QPGV-4PT1, QPG2-43YM), the same maiden name as the 1786 bride. The contradiction that held this grade (: the 1791 index labels 'Friedrich Fessels' as her father) is resolved in the note on Jacobina Friederike Rampacher: the contemporary baptisms give her maiden name as Rampacher, so 'Fessels' is her first husband's name. Rivals named and excluded: Jacobina Friederika Rapp (dau. of a Jacob Friedrich, m. Brecht 20 Nov 1788); the other Jacob Friedrich Rampacher, of Enzweihingen (b. 1744, son of Johann Christoph, m. Agnes Maria Magenau, children from 1769), whose children found on FamilySearch include no Jacobina Friederika, and any daughter of his would have been 17 or younger in 1786. The 1786 father is this man (b. 1720, m. 1744), father of Jacobina Friderica b. Vaihingen 6 Apr 1759 (QPGK-LDW2), the only Jacobina Friederika Rampacher indexed at Vaihingen. Read signed in, 2 Nov 2026; labelled index fields; the 1786 and 1791 images are partner-only on FamilySearch. ¶5

The case for this identification 1

Labelled evidence, grade held at Probable: FamilySearch, 'Deutschland, ausgewählte evangelische Kirchenbücher 1500-1971' (= 'Germany, Lutheran Baptisms, Marriages, and Burials, 1500-1971'), QPGK-T6TY: marriage at Vaihingen 4 Jul 1786, groom 'Johann Christoph Fußel' (Father 'Johann Friedrich Fußel'), Wife 'Jacobina Friederika Kampacher', Father-in-law 'Jacob Friedrich Kampacher'. Her baptism QPGK-LDW2 (6 Apr 1759, Jacob Fridrich 'Ramprecht' x Maria Jacobina) was already labelled. Held because the 1786 bride is joined to the tree's Jacobina Friederike (wife of Christoph Ludwig Knodel, 1791) only through the widow chain (child 1788 to 'Christoph Friedrich Fressel' x Jacobina Friederika, QPG2-WGRS; 1791 bride 'Fessels'), and the 1791 Lutheran index labels 'Friedrich Fessels' as her FATHER (QPGK-ZG1P), a noted contradiction (audit 5 rule 4), even though her son's 1854 burial names her 'Jakobine Friedrike Ramsacher' (QPG2-WR7B). ¶4a
