Life story

The Stjør- og Verdal probate card index 77804 covers only 1689-1742 (viewer titles), so it cannot hold a probate of about 1746. The typed register of protocol 3 (24753, 1742-60) has no Jens Andersen or Ingeborg Pedersdatter; its Frosta entries for 1744-48 are Biørn Håversen (fol. 41), Berit Jonsdatter (57), Kari Einersdatter (105), Marit Olsdatter (109b), Joen Tostensen (110), Ole Olsen Hogstad (130b) and Marit Larsdatter (145b). ¶3a

Farmer on gn. 65, Frosta, from about 1710 (Frostaboka VIII p. 516). Probable father of Anne Jensdatter (see her page), graded on that link. ¶1

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the grade · 2 open questions

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Probable only: the link rests on the farm history Frostaboka VIII (2004) p. 516 (a compiled source, rebuilt from word boxes; dates ABT), which lists Anne Jensdtr., b. 1716, married to Ola Olsen of Hellan (63/1), and her sister Mali Jensdtr. (d. 1765, wife of Hans Jonsen of Hellan) among his children. The probate after Mali Jensdatter, 9 Oct 1765 (24755 fol. 495a, scan), names 'En kiød Søster Anne Jens Dater' married to Lars Olsen Hellan, so Anne and Mali were full sisters, and lists a half-brother Gunder Gundersen, which fits the bygdebok's statement that their mother had been married before to a Gunder. It does not name the parents. ¶2a
