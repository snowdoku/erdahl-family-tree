Life story

As a bachelor of Giske-Ødegård (Gidscheødegaard) he was engaged 3 Nov 1759 at Borgund to Anne Olsdatter, a maid of Schiong (Skjong). The witnesses were Rasmus Nilsen and Ole Joensen of Giske-Ødegård. He was dead by 24 Jul 1760, when his son Peder was baptized. A probate for Peder Olsen of Giske-Ødegård was registered 28 Nov 1760. ¶1

His probate is registered as: Peder Olsen, farm Giskeødegård, area Giske, registered 28 November 1760 by the Sunnmøre sorenskriveri, protocol 3A 19, folio 394a. The index gives only his name and no heirs, so the protocol page itself is the record that would name his widow and his children and settle whether the posthumous son Peder of 1760 was his only child. ¶2

Probate: the protocol page has now been read (Sunnmøre sorenskriveri, skifteprotokoll 14A, 3A 19, fol. 394a; image 24560/417). On 28 November 1760 the lawful probate and division (skifte og deling) after the late Peder Olsen of Giske-Ødegård (Gidsche Ødegaard) was held. The parties were his surviving widow Anne Olsdatter, with her legal guardian (lauværge) Ole Jonsen of Giske-Ødegård, and his son Peder Pedersen, about 18 weeks old (the digit is uncertain and could be read as 16, but 24 July to 28 November 1760 is 127 days, or 18 weeks, which fits his baptism exactly), with guardian (formynder) Ole Pedersen of Giske-Ødegård. No other child is named. No parents or siblings of the deceased appear as heirs, and the entry gives no relationship for Ole Pedersen. The gross estate was about 132 riksdaler. Debts included wages owed to Erik Olsen and Ingeborg Pedersdatter, and the net estate of about 65 riksdaler was divided between the widow and the son. ¶4

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Confidence raised from Probable to Confirmed. The 1760 probate, read from the scan, names him as deceased at Giske-Ødegård, with widow Anne Olsdatter and an infant son Peder Pedersen whose age matches the baptism of 24 July 1760. ¶5

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

A nearby red herring, checked and excluded: the Haram probate of 12 October 1787 for Brite Olsdatter of Skjelten (born 1721) names as her children Alv Pedersen 'Giskeødegaard' (born 1748), Ole Pedersen (1753) and Eli Pedersdatter (1768), with Rasmus Amundsen Skjelten as guardian. Those are the children of a different Peder - this Peder Olsen was still a bachelor in November 1759 - so that family does not belong to this line. ¶3

Searches that found nothing 1

Burial not found in the index (13 Oct 2026): the Borgund burial index has no Peder Olsen for 1758-1760 or 1750-1800, nor any Olsen or Giske* burial for 1758-1760. A control search (Olsen, Borgund, 1750-1800) returned a single row, of 1791, so Borgund burials are barely indexed before 1791; this is a coverage gap, not a negative. His burial must be read in the scanned Borgund book for 1759-1760. ¶6
