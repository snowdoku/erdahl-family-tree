About the family

Named as father on his daughter June's Social Security application of January 1958: 'FATHER'S FIRST NAME: HENRY / FATHER'S MIDDLE NAME: E / FATHER'S LAST NAME: TOSTENSON'. The same card gives the mother as ANNA E OLSON and June's birth as 4 Jun 1920 in Nicollet County, Minnesota, so the family was in Nicollet County, Minnesota in 1920. NARA AAD, NUMIDENT Application (SS-5) Files 1936-2007 (Last Names E-G), rid 421699. He is the father-in-law of Ingvald Eidem and no record of him was in the tree before. ¶1
