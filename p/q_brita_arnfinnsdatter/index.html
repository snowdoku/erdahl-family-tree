Life story

Laberg 1985: in the Sværen farm history (printed p. 139) Laberg writes that in 1686 the holder was Arnfinn, that he had a daughter Brita who in 1692 married Per Johannessen, and that Per got the holding after Arnfinn, who died in 1714. On p. 140 he gives Per's wife's death as 1742 (kgroenha.net, the source of this record's death date, has 1743). ¶2

Probate 7 Aug 1743 at Sværen in Tjugum skipreide after 'Brite Anfinds-datter', between the widower 'Peder Hansen Swæren' and their 5 children: Johannes, Christen, Giertrud (m. Aslack [?]), Brite (m. Tollef Findingaas[?]) and Kari (m. Olle Anfindsen Baale), all of age. Appraisers Mons Forrnes and Anders Swæren. Net estate 41 rd 2 ort 6 s. ¶4

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Confidence raised from Speculative to Probable on Laberg 1985, which states her parentage and her marriage. It is a compiled bygdebok read through nb.no word boxes (page images are Norway-only), so not above Probable. ¶3a

Confidence raised from Probable to Confirmed on the 1743 probate (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll 6a, fol. 317a, No. 63), which names her as mother of Christen; the children agree exactly with Laberg 1985. Her father Arnfinn is not named in it. ¶5

Corrections 1

Correction: the Leikanger register's marriage of 25 Jun 1692 at Tjugum (11564 canvas 10 left) reads 'Peder Johans[en] Vangnes / Sigri Andfinds d. Sværen'. The bride's first name is Sigri, not Brita. Brita Anfindsdatter is Peder's wife and the mother of the five children in her own 1743 probate (6a fol. 317a), so either the vicar wrote her sister's name or Peder married Sigri first and Brita after Sigri's death (before about 1697). Her grade (Confirmed, on her link to Christen Persen Sværen through the 1743 probate) is unaffected; only the 1692 marriage date attached to her is now in doubt. ¶6a

Open questions and the next record to read 2

What would confirm it: Arnfinn Sværen's 1714 probate (Ytre Sogn) naming his daughter Brita and son-in-law Per, or the 1692 Leikanger marriage entry. ¶3b

Test record: a baptism of a child of Peder Sværen in 1693-1697 (11564 canvases c11-c30) naming the mother, or Sigri Anfindsdatter's burial before 1698. ¶6b
