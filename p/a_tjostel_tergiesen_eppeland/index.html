About the family

NEW ANCESTOR. Grandfather of Birgitte Terjesdatter (1761-1812) and so the 4x-great-grandfather of Tellef T. Ramsey. Farmer at Nedre Eppeland in Åmli; the estate held 1½ huder in Eppeland. ¶1

His parents are named on the archivist's cross-reference card: "Avds far: Tergie Tiøstelsen Rostveit + sk. 15/10-1728", "mor: Oslaug Aanonsdtr.", "bror: Hylje Tergiesen Rostveit". This finally explains why his son Terje was recorded at betrothal in 1755 as "Tergie Thiøstelsøn Rostved" while living at Eppeland: Rostveit was the family's farm of origin. ¶4

His father's estate (Østre Råbyggelag Skifteprotokoll nr. 8 fol. 26, 15 Oct 1728, Rostveit, card 11395) lists him first among the children: 'Tiøstel Tergiesøn 25 år', so born about 1703. The archivist's note adds '+ sk. 1765 på Eppeland' and 'g.m. Bergitte Evensdtr. fra Ramse'. Brother: Hølje Tergiesen (21 in 1728), m. Guroe Kiddelsdatter from Tveit. ¶5

Life story

His estate was settled on 2 March 1765 (Østre Råbyggelag skifteprotokoll no. 13a, folio 91). His widow Berte Evensdatter "oppgir boet" — surrendered the estate to the court. Six children are named with their ages: Tergie Tjøstelsøn, 33 (our ancestor, who died in 1769 and had married Christine Nielsdatter, "som brav odel til Eppeland", who brought the hereditary right to Eppeland); Even, 27; Anne Tjøstolsdatter, married to Giermund Jonson Rostveit, later of Indjorden; Ingeborg, unmarried; Anne; and Oslou. Total assets 132 riksdaler 3 ort 14 skilling against debts of 23-1-0 — a modest but solvent holding. ¶2

Two of his daughters died young: the joint estate of "Ingeborg Tiøstelsdtr." and "Anna", described as under-age children, was settled 10 March to 7 August 1768, with their mother Berte Evensdatter and their brothers Tergie and Even and sisters Anna (married to Giermund Jonsen Indjorden) and Aaslöf (18) as heirs. ¶3
