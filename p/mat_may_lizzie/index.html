About the family

Sister of Anna Olene (Satter) Eidem, so Mayme (Eidem) Eidem's maternal aunt; she married an Abraham and is recorded as Lizzie Satter Abraham. ¶1

Her son is one of the men who carried her brother-in-law to the grave: 'GUST ABRAHAM' is among the pallbearers at Severt S. Eidem's funeral on 1 August 1940, and he is the Gust Abraham born 1889 in Union County whom the wave-3 agent found in the South Dakota birth index under mother's maiden name SATTER. ¶13

Life story

Her existence is the cross-check that validates the Satter identification. Searching the South Dakota birth index on mother's maiden name SATTER returns seven ABRAHAM children born in Union County - Gust 1889, Albert 1890, Anna 1892, Alfred 1894, Laura 1896, Edwin 1900 and Richard 1905 - alongside Anna's own three. The state's index and Find a Grave's Satter family agree with each other, and neither was built from the other. ¶2

Third child of Gunder and Guri, and the first born in Dakota Territory. Her own obituary states the place exactly: 'Lizzie Carrie Satter, daughter of Gunder and Anna Satter, was born May 30, 1870, on the Satter homestead, one mile north of the Spink store.' ¶3

Baptism and confirmation: 'The records of the Brule Creek Lutheran church show that she was baptized in the old log church and confirmed with the first class in the frame church built in 1883.' The congregation therefore had a log church standing by 1870 and its ministerial books were still consultable in 1934. ¶4

Her flush granite marker is photographed: MOTHER / LIZZIE ABRAHAM / 1870 - 1934. A captioned group photograph of about 1918, 'ANDREW ABRAHAM FAMILY', names all fourteen people in it. ¶9

She was president of the Ladies' Aid of the Brule Creek Lutheran church for a number of years. She was recorded variously as Eliza K., Elise, Lisa A. and even Mary. ¶10

Dates made exact from Find a Grave memorial 63284894, whose compiled biography gives her birth as 30 May 1870 in Union County, her death on 3 March 1934 at the Lutheran Hospital, Sioux City, Iowa, of carcinoma of the gall bladder following an operation, and her burial on 6 March 1934 at Brule Creek Cemetery. The file carried only the years. ¶11

1870 census: she is the infant 'Setter, Mary', aged 1/12, born in DAKOTA, with the enumerator's 'born within the year — MAY' against her name, in her father's household at dwelling 122, Sioux Valley, Union County, Dakota Territory, 21 August 1870. The month, the year and the territory agree exactly with her own 1934 obituary, which says she was born on 30 May 1870 on the Satter homestead one mile north of the Spink store. The given name 'Mary' is the enumerator's, and does not appear in any other record of her; it is the kind of error a marshal makes in a household that spoke no English. This entry, three weeks after her birth, is the earliest record of her existence, and it is the datum that fixes the whole household as the Satters. ¶15

1880 census: 'Eliza R.', daughter, 10, at school, born Dakota Territory, in Gunder Satter's household at Spink Township, Union County, 15-16 June 1880 (ED 5, sheet 407A, line 7). Age 10 fits her birth of 30 May 1870 and the birthplace agrees with Union County. The middle initial is written R. where this file has Carrie (C.); the letter is clear on the image, so either the enumerator erred or she bore another middle name as a child. ¶16

Marriage: 31 May 1888, in Union County, to Andrew 'Andy' Abraham (1863-1955), son of Abraham Olson and Berte 'Anna' Gjytri. He had emigrated in 1881. In 1899 they settled on the farm north of Spink, which remained her home. ¶5

Marriage, as a lead from the same source: she married ANDREW ABRAHAM, son of ABRAHAM OLSON and BERTE 'ANNA' GJYTRI, on 31 MAY 1888 in Union County - the day after her eighteenth birthday. She was confirmed at the Brule Creek church in 1883. Her given name is written variously Lizzie, Eliza, Elise, Lisa. ¶12

Thirteen children, twelve of them living when she died: Gustav Andreas 'Gust' (1889), Albert Oliver (1890), Anna Gustine (1892, m. Ronning), Alfred Rudolph (1894), Laura Kathrina (1896, m. Ronning), Edwin Selmer (1900), Obert Palmer (1903-1910, died aged seven), Richard Hjalmer 'Dick' (1905), Gladys M. (1907), George L. (1908), Evelyn S. (1910, m. Jolin), Eleanor Rosetta (1913, m. Clausen) and Florence Mildred (1917, m. Petersen). Eleven of the thirteen are independently in the South Dakota birth index with mother LIZZIE SATTER, seven of them registered in one batch of delayed registrations on 17 October 1942. ¶6

Death: 3 March 1934 at the Lutheran hospital, Sioux City, after an operation, of carcinoma of the gall bladder, 'aged 63 years, 9 months and 3 days' — exactly right from 30 May 1870. Funeral at the Brule Creek Lutheran church conducted by Rev. W. I. Aamoth before an estimated 500 people, six nephews acting as pallbearers. ¶7

Her obituary is the record that recovered her brother Lewis and corroborated her sister Anna: 'three brothers, Gust. Satter, of Vermillion, S. D.; Fred Satter, of Bainville, Mont., and Lewis Satter, of Grand Pass, Ore.; a sister, Mrs. S. S. Eidem, of Elk Point.' ¶8

She predeceased her sister Anna Olene by three years, which is why the 1937 obituary's survivor list names three brothers and no sister. ¶14
