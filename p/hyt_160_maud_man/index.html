Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Speculative mother of William Brewster of Scrooby (d. 1590): the will of Christopher Man of Scrooby, dated 11 Oct 1558, proved 13 Jan 1558/9 (York P&E 15 pt 3 fo. 186), as quoted by J. G. Hunt, NEHGR 124 (1970): 253 n.5, leaves 6s 8d 'to my sister Maud Bruister' and was witnessed by 'Mr. Thomas Simkinson, William Bruister and John Simkinson'. So a Maud (Man) Brewster, Christopher Man's sister, was alive at Scrooby in 1558. Hunt thinks she was the wife of William Brewster of Bentley cum Arksey and the mother of William of Scrooby, but says only that this 'seems' so. The other legatees: sisters Isabel and Annis, brother William Man, wife Alice, children Avery and John. No record read names her husband or her children. ¶1

The case for this identification 1

Index check: YAS Record Series 14, p. 105 (page image read) has 'Jan. 13, 1558. Man, Christopher, Scrowbie (Notts.), Oct. 11, 1558', register vol. 15 pt 3 fol. 186. The will date, the probate date and the register reference all match Hunt's citation. Her parents: no Man of Scrooby, Bawtry or Harworth appears in the York registry indexes for 1389-1514 or 1514-1553 (the only Man wills of 1514-1553 are from Cleveland, Mirfield, Normanby, Raskelf, Hickling and Newark). The Man family's earlier wills, if any, would have been proved in the Retford deanery and are not in the York registers. Her parents remain unknown. Grade unchanged (Speculative, graded on her link to William Brewster). ¶2a

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Test record: Christopher Man's original will (Borthwick, Prob. Reg. 15 pt 3 fo. 186), to see whether it says more of 'my sister Maud Bruister' (for example 'and her son William'). Also the Scrooby manor court rolls and rentals of the 1520s-50s. ¶2b
