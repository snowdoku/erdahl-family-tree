About the family

He is identified as Anne's father from her name, age and farm (Jyserød at her 1818 marriage). Drangedal baptisms before 1800 are not indexed, so this has not been confirmed. ¶2

Father of John, baptised 26 Jun 1785, and Anne, baptised 23 Aug 1795, in Drangedal (both at Jyserø). He was surety at the 1793 marriage of the soldier Gunder Towsen (Tovsen) Jyserø, who was also godfather to John in 1785; Gunder was probably his brother (inferred, not proved). ¶5

Parent link to Toruv Andersen Heimdal (see his page) is PROBABLE, not confirmed: his baptism is lost, because the Kviteseid ministerialbok 1754-1773 is missing its first 10 leaves and its baptisms begin only on 12 Sep 1756. The link rests on: (1) his 1784 marriage as 'Thorulf Torulfsen Hiemdal fra Treungen'; (2) the churching of 'Torofs Hiemdahls Qvinde' (Toruv Hiemdal's wife) in Treungen on 25 Jan 1756, which means a birth about Dec 1755, matching his age of 46 in 1801; (3) Toruv Hiemdal's son Toruv was buried 7 Feb 1755, and the name would be reused for the next son. Toruv is the only Toruv seen at Hiemdal in 1754-1786; the other Toruvs nearby lived at Fjone in Nissedal and at Kittilskaas in Kviteseid. Points (2) and (3) are inferences. ¶7

Pedigree collapse: if this link holds, Toruv Andersen Heimdal appears three times in Ingebor Heimdal's ancestry: through Gundbiør Toruvsdatter (mother of Orm Egildsen), through Hæge Toruvsdatter (mother of Ingebor Toresdatter), and through this Thoruf (father of Anne Thorufsdatter). ¶8

Mother added, with lower confidence than on her record: Gunild Olsdatter (see her page). 'Torof Torofsen' is an heir in the skifte after Gunild Olsdatter on søndre Hiemdahl, about 1774 (Panteregister I 1 fol. 318, DA 10915 view 361). He is identified with Thoruf Thorufsen of Jyserø (m. Drangedal 1784) across farm and parish, so for him this link is Probable at most. Her own grade is set on her link to Hæge Toruvsdatter. ¶10

Life story

Death: 'Torof Torofsen', aged 69 (b. about 1756), died 22 Feb and was buried 5 Mar 1825 in Drangedal (Drangedal ministerialbok 1814-1831, p. 106, no. 4; index pg00000008656002). No farm is given, but the age fits his 1801 census age of 46 at Jyserø (b. about 1755) and rules out his son Thoruf (14 in 1801); he is the only Toruv/Torolf born 1748-1762 in the Drangedal burials. ¶9

The skifte after Gunild Olsdatter, søndre Hiemdahl, 15 Feb 1775 (DA 25675 fol. 827a, No. 71, view 831) names a son 'Torrof Torrofsøn 19 aar' (born about 1755-56), with curator Jørgen Olsøn Hiemdahl. This fits the churching of Torof Hiemdal's wife in Jan 1756. His identification with Thoruf Thorufsen of Jyserø (Drangedal, m. 1784) crosses parishes, so that link stays as graded on the parents' side. ¶11

Betrothed in Drangedal 8 Aug 1784 and married there 26 Sep 1784, as 'ungkarl [bachelor] Thorulf Torulfsen Hiemdal fra Treungen', to the maid Ragnild Johnsdatter of Jyserø; sureties (forlovere) Knud Knudsen Jyserø and a second Knud whose surname is cut off at the page edge (Drangedal ministerialbok 1768-1814, p. 215). ¶3

He came from Hiemdal (Heimdal) in Treungen, so he was not born in Drangedal; his baptism should be in the Kviteseid prestegjeld books (Treungen). That he was a son of Toruv Andersen Heimdal (see his page) is suggested by the patronymic and farm, but is not yet proved. ¶4

1801 census, Jyserø, Drangedal: husband, aged 46, in his first marriage, with wife Ragnild Johnsdatter (37). Children: John (16), Thoruf (14), Gunnild (11), Gunnild (9), Anne (6), Mari (1) and Ole (3). Also in the household: his wife's sister Thorbær Johnsdatter (27). ¶1
