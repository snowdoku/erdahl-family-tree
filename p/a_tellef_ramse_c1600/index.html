Life story

Known only from the patronymic of his son, Even (Evind) Tellefsen Ramse of Tovdal, who was dead by 1696 and whose own son Svend had his estate settled in March 1696. No record of him has been found. ¶1

He would have been at Ramse in Tovdal in the early 1600s. The name Tellef then runs unbroken down the family for the next eight generations to Tellef T. Ramsey of Joice, Iowa (1853-1933). ¶2

1610 tax census (Nedenes len, Råbyggelag, Lille Thopdall [Tovdal] sogn; Digitalarkivet source 100550, pp. 35-36, indexed): 'Tholluff' was taxed 1 daler at ytre Ramßø, with Oluff (½ daler) on the same farm; øffre Ramßø had Effuind, Oluff and Jørgen, and nedre Ramßø Gunuold and Biørn. This Tholluff may be Tellef Ramse, or his father of the same name; the record gives no age or patronymic, so this is a lead only. ¶4

Birth bound revised (orchestrator, 13 Oct 2026): his son Evind was 77 in 1664 and 80 in 1666 (vicar's censuses), so born about 1586-87; Tellef must then have been born before about 1567, not 1575-1595. This also makes him a good fit for the 'Tholluff' taxed at ytre Ramse in 1610 (still a name-and-farm match only). ¶5

1636-37 landskatt (Lille Topdal): no Tellef/Tholluff at any Ramse farm; Effuind holds Ytre Ramse, Tiöstell is at Dale, and a Thelleff is at Hægendall (not linked). No entry names Even/Effuind as a son of Tellef; the parentage still rests on the patronymic in card 10365 only. ¶7

1655-56 odelsjordebok: the Lille Topdal section (DA 76715 views 23R-24) lists bønders odelsgods by owner. No Tellef and no Effuind/Even is listed as owning odel at Ramse there. Ramse men listed: Osmund Ramse (land at Rorsland in Åmli, Grolleberg, Bygdal 5¼ hpd, Øvre Løvrak, Øvre Eppeland, Bjelland and others); Tellef Osmundsen Ramse (Melstad in Vegusdal, Lille Lien in Lille Topdal, Vassbøgund in Bygland); a [?Knud] Osmundsen Ramse (Nedre Løvrak, Bås in Åmli, Østad); and 'O[lle] [struck word] Ramsse' (Aggeland in Iveland, ¼ b.). The list gives holdings outside the owner's own farm, so its silence does not show who held Ytre Ramse. ¶10

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the grade · 1 correction · 1 alternative set aside · 1 open question

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: Speculative to Probable. (1) His son's patronymic is stated twice by the archivist's cards ('Even Tollefsen Ramse', card 10383; 'Evind Tellefsen Ramse', card 10365) and by the 1704 deed of Groe's heirs ('her late father Evind Tellefsen'). (2) In the 1610 tax list a Tholluff holds 1 daler at yttre Ramse, and by 1636-37 Effuind is odel owner of Ytre Ramse, so Even held odel land at the farm where a Tellef paid tax in 1610. (3) The Tovdal bygdebok's 'Ytre Ramse' chapter (canvases 323-325) discusses the odel of Ytre Ramse through Tellef and Tone (an Olav's daughter): 'Tellef... Tone', 'Tone sitt... Ramse', a document naming 'Oluf, Svenning, Jon... Tellef... Tone', a quoted record with '...Tellevsen»', and 'Tone... gift med ein... Tjøstolvson'; it lists the heirs at a 1623 division as 'Mikkel, Jon, Tallak, Eivind, Torjus, Halvor, Anne... Tone', and says Eivind was born before 1610. (read from nb.no content-search word positions, not from a page image, so only the words listed are certain; the book is a published secondary source.) Birth bound moved from BEF 1590 to BEF 1567, about 20 years before his son Even's earliest birth estimate (1587). No primary record naming Tellef as Even's father has been read, so not Confirmed. ¶8

Corrections 1

Correction: birth bound set to BEF 1590 (not BEF 1567), to match the widened birth range of his son Evind (1587-1610). ¶6

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Bygdebok lead, not adopted: the reconstruction suggests the bygdebok makes Even's father a man with the patronymic Tjøstolvson, married to Tone Olavsdotter, dead before a division of 1623. The word before 'Tjøstolvson' was not recovered, and the 1628-29 tax roll's 'Tjøstolv' at Ytre Ramse may be a relative. A reading of pages 323-325 of the book (printed page numbers not yet mapped) by someone in Norway would settle his patronymic, wife and death. ¶9
