About the family

Eldest of the eight children of Peter and Olga Valder, born in Boone County, Iowa, about eighteen months after their 1913 marriage and about a year before they sailed for China. She was the 'first daughter' who went to China with them in 1916. ¶1

Life story

Married the Rev. Orvin Daniel Thompson (1914-1993) at Ferndale, Washington, on 31 December 1939. The Herald-news of Wolf Point, Montana, reported on 25 January 1940 both the Ferndale wedding ('Rev Orvin D Thompson Takes Bride At Ferndale Washington') and a reception held for the newly married couple at the Homestead, Montana, home of Mr. and Mrs. Anders Sunwall, attended by guests from Froid and Homestead, with devotions, a solo and a sermon. Her father was then farming at Ferndale. ¶2

She is buried apart from her parents, in Billings, Montana, where her husband served. ¶3

1930 census: Myrtle P. Valder, 15, daughter, born Iowa, at school, in her parents' household at Ferndale, Washington (ED 37-31, sheet 2B). Read from the census image. ¶4

Marriage: the Washington marriage index lists Orvin D. Thompson and Myrtle Pearl Valder, Whatcom County, with the years 1939 and 1940 (marriage and filing). This agrees with the 31 December 1939 Ferndale wedding already noted. Index entry only; the certificate was not read. ¶5
