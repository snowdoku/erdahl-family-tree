About the family

Naming: her granddaughter Kirsti Gulbrandsdatter (bapt. 1 Jan 1761), the first child of her son Gulbrand, bears her name, as Norwegian custom expected for a first daughter named after the paternal grandmother. ¶3

Father: the 25 Apr 1741 estate at Søndre Hvinden after the wife of Amund Hansen lists 'Kierste (g. Amund Gulbrandsen)' among the children, so her father is Amund Hansen of Søndre Hvinden. Her mother, the deceased of 1741, is not named on the index card. Her siblings were Halvor, Bergitte (m. Hans Nielsen Skierstad), Kari (m. Gulbrand Nielsen), Mari (m. Torger Olsen Racken) and Ane (m. Niels Gulbrandsen Skare). ¶5

Mother: the protocol entry for the 25 Apr 1741 estate at Søndre Hvinden (skifteprotokoll 1737-1743, s. 585) names 'Kirste Amundsdatter gift med Amund Gulbrandsen' as the first daughter of the deceased and the widower Amund Hansen. Her mother's forename is read, uncertainly, as Marit. ¶6

Life story

Baptisms: mother of Birthe (1733), Anne (1735), Gulbrand (1738) and Hans (1741), children of Amund Gulbrandsen of Hvinden, Gran (Gran church book 1732-1744, index). Her birth year is inferred only from her first known child. ¶1

Godparent: Kirsti Amundsdatter of Hvinden was godmother on 18 Oct 1744 to Ole, son of Ole Nielsen of Hvinden Ejer, alongside Ole of Griner. ¶2

Probate (index card): the estate of 'Kirstie Amundsd.' of Hvinden in Gran was held 19 Nov 1754 (Hadeland, Land og Valdres skifteprotokoll 1752-56, f. 686a). Children: Gulbrand 16 1/2, Hans 12, Amund 8 1/2, Mary 24, Berte 21, Anne 19, the same six as at her husband Amund Gulbrandsen's estate in 1751. Gross 1342-0-14, net 1260-3-15 rdr. The card does not name her parents. ¶4

Corroboration (already Confirmed; graded on her link to Gulbrand Amundsen). The 1762 deed in pantebok 8 (DA 10272, fol. 144a, No. 4) is made by her children, who call her 'vores sal. Moder Kirstie Amundsdatter' and recite her estate held at Søndre Hvinden on 19 Nov 1754; they sell their shares of Søndre Hvinden to 'vor kiære Broder Gulbrand Amundsen Hvinden, hans Hustru Elie Arnes Datter'. So a primary record names Gulbrand, husband of Elie Arnesdatter, as her son. Her daughters' marriages named in the deed: Kari Amundsdatter x Ole Andersen; Anne Amundsdatter x Jens Christophersen Haugtvedt; a third daughter x Tosten Andersen [Hvinden]. ¶8

1 on the grade

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: the protocol entry itself was read (Hadeland, Land og Valdres skifteprotokoll 1783-1802, fol. 305a-305b). 'Skifte Forretning paa Gaarden Hvinden i Grans Sogn efter Gulbrand Ammundsen', 16 Apr 1789, widow Ellie Arnesdatter; children Hans Gulbransen 16, Anders Gulbransen 12, Kirsti 28, Anne 23, Kari 21, Abigael 9 and Elline 7 1/2. On fol. 305b the guardian appointed for the son Hans and the two youngest daughters Abigael and Elline is 'deres Farbroder Ammund Ammundsen' (their father's brother Amund Amundsen). A son Amund (4 in 1751, 8 1/2 in 1754) appears at both the 1751 estate of Amund Gulbrandsen of Hvinden and the 1754 estate of Kirsti Amundsdatter of Hvinden, beside Gulbrand (13 and 16 1/2). The protocol thus makes the Gulbrand Amundsen who died in 1789 the brother of Amund Amundsen, which, with the same farm (Hvinden), the same 3 skippund holding and the ages, settles that he is the Gulbrand of the 1751 and 1754 estates. Kirsti Amundsdatter is therefore Confirmed as the mother of Gulbrand Amundsen (raised from Probable). ¶7
