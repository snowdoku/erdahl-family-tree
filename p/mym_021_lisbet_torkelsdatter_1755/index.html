Life story

Confirmation - possible only: the Selbu list for Quasimodogeniti Sunday, 2 April 1769, has 'Lisbeth Torkildsd.' (no. 42 of 49), with no farm or father. She is the only Lisbet Torkelsdatter in the Selbu confirmation lists 1764-1775 (source 16311 canvases 15-69), and the date fits a birth about 1754-55, but without a farm the entry cannot be tied to her. ¶7

Confirmation: the parish clerk's book names her 'Lisbeth Torchelsd. Klagset' among the confirmands of 2 Apr 1769 (no. 42), so she came from the farm Klagset, Selbu - not Hammer, the earlier lead. Her father was therefore probably a Torkel of Klagset (not yet identified). ¶8

Marriage: betrothed in Selbu on 1 March 1778 as 'Lisbeth Torchelsd.' to Bersvend Sivertsen (sponsors Arent Vig and Ole Bersvendsen Draxen), and married on 21 June 1778 as 'Lisbeth Torkiedsd.' to 'Bersvend Siurs: Drax:'. Neither entry names her farm. Read from the scans (source 16311, canvas 87 left page and canvas 89 right page). ¶5a

Named as mother at the baptisms of her son Sivert (12 Apr 1784) and her daughter Sigri (6 Aug 1786) in the Selbu register; churched (Introd.) on 2 Feb 1783 and 3 Sep 1786 as 'Bersv. Siurs. Draxens Hustru'. ¶3

Name: written 'Lisbete Torch:' (1786), 'Lesbeth Torchilsd.' (1784) and 'Lisbet Torkelsdatter' (1801 census) - the patronymic Torkelsdatter means daughter of Torkel. ¶1

1801 census: wife of Bersvend Sivertsen in household 04 of Draxen, Selbu, aged 46 (so born about 1754-55), with seven children. ¶2

1801 census: at Draxen, where Lisbet Torkelsdatter lived as Bersvend Sivertsen's wife, there were also Mikkel Torkilsen, 53 (b. about 1748), cottar with land and sawmaster at Draxen's sawmill, and a widow Marit Torkilsdatter, b. about 1744. A son Mikkel (over 12) is listed in Torkel Nielsen and Siri Mikkelsdatter's Klagset household in 1762, and a 'Marit Torchelsd.' of Draxen was confirmed in 1774 (a different, younger Marit or a misdated reading). Mikkel Torkilsen at Draxen is very probably Lisbet's brother, which supports the Klagset parentage (still Probable). ¶10

Her probable parents' marriage is found: Torkel Nielsen of Klagset and Siri Mikkelsdatter, c.1739-40 (16315 c3), which strengthens the Probable link (still not Confirmed: her baptism falls in the 1747-56 gap). ¶11

Not in her husband's household in the 1825 census, so probably dead by then. A search of the Selbu index 1801-1850 for Lisbet Torkelsdatter found only the 1801 census; Lisbeth, Elisabeth and Thorchelsdatter spellings in the burial lists 1801-1825 have not been worked through. Her parents are unknown. ¶4

Searches that found nothing 1

Baptism not found - no register survives: Selbu has no surviving baptism register for 1747-1756. Book 16310 ends in 1737, book 16315 covers only 1740-1746, and book 16311 begins on 1 January 1757 (who also read every baptism from 1 Jan 1757 to 25 Mar 1758 without finding her). Born about 1754-1755 by her age of 46 in the 1801 census, she falls in that gap, so her own baptism, and with it the names of her parents, is lost. What remains: the confirmation lists in book 16311 (about 1764-1775, now being read - they usually give the father or the farm), and probate (skifte) - the Strinda og Selbu probate protocols cover Selbu only from 1797, so a skifte after her death (date not found) could name heirs but not the parents. ¶6
