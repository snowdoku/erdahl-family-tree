Life story

Gran baptism index 1705-1720 by father Amund (m=0534): Halvor, 2 Sep 1714 (pd00000007066753), no domicile or mother indexed; no Kirsti/Kierste daughter of an Amund. The Gran 1707-31 index records no mothers, so Marit's patronymic is not obtainable this way; the scan of 2 Sep 1714 (DA 9339) is the next step. 1664 vicar's census for Hadeland (DA 35503, searchable at /en/search/64/35503 by gard/fornavn) has no Hvinden under Hvinden/Hvinnen/Vinnen/Quinden/Huinden; too early for him in any case. ¶4

The 2 Sep 1714 Gran baptism (14th Sunday after Trinity; Gran church book 1707-1731, p. 61b; index pd00000007066753) is of twins, Halvor and 'Eling' (Eli), children of Amund of Hvinden ('Huinden'). The index names NO mother, and this book's baptisms are indexed but not scanned, so the entry cannot be read for the wife's name. Godparents: Niels Guldbrandsen Fallang, Hans Knudstad, Hans Hansen Hvinden, Hans Stubstad, Lars Hansen Hvinden, dragoon Lars Andersen, Madame Jagt, and women Kari Hvinden, Karen Fallang, Guri Molstad and one of Hole. Hans Hansen and Lars Hansen of Hvinden share Amund's patronymic and farm and may be his brothers (inference only, not a relationship statement). The twin Eli is not among the 1741 heirs, so she probably died young. ¶5

Probate (index card,; read on the image by the orchestrator): on 25 Apr 1741 an estate was held at 'Syndre Vinden' (Søndre Hvinden) in Gran (Hadeland, Land og Valdres skifteprotokoll 1737-1743, p. 585) after the wife of Amund Hansen. He is entered as '(enkemand) Amund Hansen (lever, opgir bod)', the living widower. The deceased wife's name is not on the card. Children: 1. Halvor; 2. Kierste, married to Amund Gulbrandsen; 3. Bergitte, married to Hans Nielsen Skierstad; 4. Kari, married to Gulbrand Nielsen; 5. Mari, married to Torger Olsen Racken; 6. Ane, married to Niels Gulbrandsen Skare. Gross c.296 rdr, net 203 rdr, including land in Søndre Hvinden valued at 220 rdr. ¶1

Probate protocol read: DA 25286, s. 585-586. He is 'Enkemanden Amund Hansen' (the widower). His wife's forename is read, uncertainly, as Marit. The six heirs and their spouses agree with the index card. Under 'Odelsgods' (s. 586), the estate's land in Søndre Hvinden is described as his own odel (ancestral land), part of 3 [huder?] of skyld (tax assessment). The rest of the farm's skyld belongs to his son-in-law Amund Gulbrandsen. The inventory and the close on s. 586-587 were not fully transcribed. ¶3
