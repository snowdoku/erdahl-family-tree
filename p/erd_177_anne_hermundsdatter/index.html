About the family

Widow of the cottager Giest Guttormsen at Nauteskreen, Tjugum skipreide, named 'hands efterlevende Enke Anne Hermunds datter' at his probate registration on 18 Sep 1761, with their son Giest Giestsen, half a year old (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll 7b, fol. 546b, No. 57). ¶1

Her parents: not found. Ruled out: Hermund Sjursen, cottager at Rundingen under Menes (probate 5 May 1764, Ytre Sogn 8a p. 121, No. 19, 24165 view 123): widow Synneve Colbeensdatter, heirs only sons Siur and Hermund Hermundsen, no daughter Anne. Guttorm Gjertsen (index 'Gjestsen'), cottager at Vikøren, probate 9 Jun 1775 (9a p. 601, No. 67, 24167 view 302): widow Synneve Arnesdatter, a dead son Giert Guttormsen leaving a son Giert Giertsen 1 year old, daughters Rande, Christie and Britte: a different family, not her husband's kin. Her son's godparents came from Hammersnes, Farnes, Menes and Lillestølen; Farnes had a Hermund (bonde Hermund Hermundsen, b. 1659, Indre Farnes, 1701 census) and may be her family's farm, unproved. ¶4

Parents confirmed: her mother Eli Olsdatter's probate at Gulteigen, 1 May 1759 (Ytre Sogn 7b fol. 436a, 24164 view 101), names 'Ane Hermundsdatter [Nød]schreen myndig' among the children of Eli and the late Hermund Olsen. The bygdebok (Vik H. 1, c35) places Anna between Agata (b. 17[1x]) and Ola (b. 1721), so b. about 1714-20, which fits a first marriage to Kiel Østensen about 1742-43 and resolves age caution. Also, the probate after her sister Agaathe Hermundsdatter at Nedre Hove, Vik, 13-14 Sep 1771 (Ytre Sogn 8b fol. 720b, No. 81, 24166 view 341) names among the heirs 'Søsteren Anna Hermundsdatter afg. Giest Guttormsen Nødschreens [Enke]', with the brothers Ole Hermundsen Store Nummedal, Anders Hermundsen Rislaug and the late Siur Hermundsen Rislaug (whose son Ole Siursen was then at Gulteigen). With Hermund Olsen's 1743 Gulteigen probate (children Siur, Ole, Anders, Agaatha, Anna, Sigri, Ingerid; widow Ely Olsdatter, 'deris sammen avlede') this makes her the daughter of Hermund Olsen and Eli Olsdatter of Gulteigen. Her own grade (link to her son Gjest Gjestsen) is unchanged. In Sep 1771 she was alive and a widow (Gjest's widow, not remarried, unless the word after Nødschreens is not 'Enke'). ¶18

Life story

Parent candidates read, none proved. (1) Hermund Endresen, Refsdal, Vik, probate No. 28, 21 May 1743 (6a fol. 290b, 24161 view 292): widow [first name unclear] Rognaldsdatter, children a son Guttorm and a daughter Anna, 2 years old (b. about 1741). This Anna is almost certainly the 'Anna Hermundsdtr, Refsdal' who took a marriage licence with Lasse Botolfsen on 29 May 1761, when our Anne was Giest's wife and had just borne his son (baptised 17 May 1761), so she is not ours. One loose end: the second surety at the 1759 betrothal reads 'Guttorm Ordal[?]'; if the farm word were 'Refsdal' he would be this Guttorm Hermundsen, so the word should be checked against a better image before the Refsdal family is dismissed. (2) Hermund Olsen, Gulteigen, Vik, probate No. 73, 14 Aug 1743 (6a fol. 324a, 24161 view 325): widow [?] Olsdatter and seven children, the sons Siur, Ole and Anders (all of age) and the daughters Agaatha, Anna, Sigri (18) and Ingerid; Anna is listed before Sigri and so was born before about 1725, eight or more years older than Giest (b. 1732/33). A candidate only. The residence word in the betrothal, 'Klean/Kleen', matches neither farm, and no Kle* farm appears in the Ytre Sogn probate register. ¶13

First marriage found: the probate after Kiel Østensen at Nødeskreen (Nauteskreen), Tjugum skipreide, 23 Sep 1754 (Ytre Sogn 7a fol. 240b-241a, No. 67, 24163 view 241), is held between his widow 'Anne Hermundsdatter' and their children: eldest son Anfind Kieldsen (about 11), Hermund 9, Østen 5, Siur and the daughter Brithe Kieldsdatter. Her guardian was 'hendes Broder Ole Hermundsen Nummedale' (her brother, of Nummedal in Vik skipreide). This is the same farm and the same widow's name as in Giest Guttormsen's probate of 18 Sep 1761, seven years later, and it fits the bygdebok's Nautskreda chapter (c246: Kjell d. 1754 aged 37, five children, the widow then married a Gjest). The farm word after her name at the 1759 betrothal, read 'Kleen', may be the end of '[Nøde]skreen'. Her brother Ole was also the guardian of her son in 1775: in Guttorm Gjestsen's Vikøren probate the guardian of the grandson Giest Giestsen reads 'Ole H[ermundsen?] Numedale' (24167 view 302). Father added, Probable: Hermund Olsen of Gulteigen, Vik (probate 1743, children Siur, Ole, Anders, Agaatha, Anna, Sigri, Ingerid). Her own grade (link to Gjest Gjestsen) is unchanged. ¶16

Betrothal found: Leikanger 11566 canvas 34 left, Dom. III post Trin. (1 Jul 1759 by the church calendar; the entries run 21-29 Jun before it and the 1760 heading is on canvas 39), Tjugum: 'Spons: Giest Guttormsøn / Anna Hermunds D. Kleen[?]', with the sureties Mathias Vigøren and Guttorm Ordal[?]. Her residence word after the patronymic reads 'Kleen' or 'Klean' (not resolved; no Kle* domicile in the Ytre Sogn probate index 1720-1800). The same couple appear as widow and dead husband in the Nauteskreen probate of 18 Sep 1761, two years later and within Tjugum skipreide. No parents are named. A rival 'Anna Hermundsdtr' of Refsdal, Vik, took out a marriage licence with Lasse Botolfsen on 29 May 1761 (Bergen diocese licences, pc00000000178876); she is not ours, since Gjest was alive until July 1761. ¶11

The 1761 Nauteskreen probate heading (7b fol. 546b, 24164 view 212) names the lensmann Ole Pedersen Wangsnes and the appraisers Anders Christensen [Huene?]stad and Anders Hansen Meene; no guardian (laugverge) for the widow is named in the heading, so it gives no kinsman of hers. ¶14

Burials: Leikanger 11566 canvas 58 left (p. 58), Dom. VII p. Trin. 5 Jul 1761, Wangsnes and Tiugum: 'Fun: Giest Guttormß. Schreen 28 An.' and 'Hermund Kielson ibid. 15 An. et 2 D.' Her second husband was buried aged 28 together with her son Hermund Kjellsen of the first marriage (9 in Sep 1754, so the ages agree). The farm is written 'Schreen' here, which supports reading the 1759 betrothal word as '[S]kreen' rather than 'Kleen'. ¶17

Sources checked for her parents, with no result. The Sogn extra tax of 1762 (Fogderegnskap no. 3418, Digitalarkivet 47516) is described on its contents page (view 3) as a 'Hovedmanntall (bare hovedpersoner)', which lists heads of household only and not the children. The 1789 local census of Leikanger (source 58134, 10 views) is also a list of heads only. Neither will name a widow's father. Her son's daughter was named Anna in 1797 (Leikanger 11568, canvas 98 left), after her. Grade unchanged. ¶9

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 5

Probable as mother of Gjest Gjestsen, on the same identification as his father (unique Giest Giestsen born 1761 in Leikanger 1801). Her patronymic Hermundsdatter is shared with Mari Hermundsdatter, widow of Ole Povelsen of Ytre Esse (d. 1758), and Ole's daughter Randi had married a Hermund (sons Ole and Hans Hermundsen in 1758): a kinship is possible but nothing read states it. Not identified with the Anna Hermundsdatter, b. about 1754, wife at Bahle in the 1801 census (too young). ¶2

Upgrade: as mother of Gjest Gjestsen. Two primary records now agree: the Leikanger baptism of 'Giest Giestsøn Nødeschreen' on 17 May 1761 (11566 c56, p. 57) and the probate at Nauteskreen of 18 Sep 1761 naming the widow Anne Hermundsdatter and the son 'Giest Giestsen 1/2 aar gl.' (7b fol. 546b). The baptism fixes the birth to spring 1761, which matches exactly the 1801 (40) and 1815 (54) census ages of Giest Giestsen at Esse in the same Tjugum skipreide, the only man of that name in Leikanger in 1801; his daughter Anna (1801 census age 4) bears the grandmother's name. The link to Esse rests on that uniqueness, not on one record naming both places. ¶3

Coordinator review (1 Nov 2026): kept at Probable under the overnight grading rule: the 1761 baptism and the probate prove this couple's posthumous son Giest, but his identity with Giest Giestsen of Esse rests on the unique name and an exact age match, which is identification by elimination. ¶5

Re-proof attempt: no change. Her own later probate was not found: the two Anne Hermundsdatter probates in the Ytre Sogn register (Hanevik 1780; Hundsrøven under Bale 1810s) were read and are other women (see Gjest Guttormsen). ¶7

Upgrade: graded on her link to her son Gjest Gjestsen (see his page), Probable -> Confirmed. The probates after her husband's sisters at Vikøren, Brithe Guttormsdatter ([26?] Oct 1807, Ytre Sogn 13 p. 271b, 24173 view 274) and Christi Guttormsdatter (3 Oct 1809, 13 p. 551b, 24173 view 554), name Giest Guttormsen of Nødskreen in Tjugum skipreide 'gift med ligesaa afdøde Anna Hermundsdatter, med hvem han i et lovligt Egteskab har avlet og efterladt sig een Søn', Giest Giestsen, cottager at Indre Esse in Tjugum skipreide. This names her as the mother of the Esse Gjest Gjestsen (1801 and 1815 censuses at Esse, the only Gjest there). She was dead by 3 Oct 1809 ('ligesaa afdøde'). ¶19

The case for this identification 2

Supporting: 'Giest Giestsen', 18-19, living at Tue, Tjugum sogn, was confirmed on 26 Sep 1779 (Leikanger 11567, canvas 234, fol. 230), the only man of the name in the 1771-1781 confirmation lists. This carries the posthumous son of the 1761 Nauteskreen probate forward to 1779 within Tjugum sogn, but at a third farm and without parents' names, so the link to Gjest Gjestsen stays Probable (graded on that link). No probate at Tue in Tjugum 1761-1800 is indexed (Ytre Sogn index: the Tue rows of 1782-99 are Tue in Kvamsøy). ¶8a

Supporting: the probate after her unmarried sister Ingegier Hermundsdatter at Guldteig (Gulteigen), 13 Oct 1761 (Ytre Sogn 7b p. 550b, No. 61, 24164 view 216), names among the heirs 'anden Søster Ane Hermundsdatter Enke [Nau]deskreen' (widow three months after Gjest's burial), with the brothers Siur (dead), Ole and Anders and the sister Agaathe m. Howe; her guardian was 'hendes fuldbroder Anders Hermundsen Rislog'. A fourth probate placing her in Hermund Olsen's sibling set. ¶20

The wedding was therefore held elsewhere or not entered; it is not where a father's name could be found. ¶12
