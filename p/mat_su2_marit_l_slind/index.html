Life story

A Slind woman previously unknown to this tree, recovered by a reverse lookup in the Social Security applications file. Her daughter OLGA MARIE CARLSON, b. 21 October 1895 at "TRONBHLEM" (Trondheim), NORWAY, filed an application (NUMIDENT SS-5, SSN XXX-XX-1214, cycle 1946) naming FATHER: OLAUS LARSEN, MOTHER: MARIT L SLIND. ¶1

So she married an Olaus Larsen and bore a daughter at Trondheim in 1895, and the family later came to America. The middle initial L is presumably her patronymic LARSDATTER, which would make her a daughter of a Lars - possibly the same Lars Slind who fathered Elias Larsen Slind (b. 1881) and Gunder (b. abt 1890), in which case she is their sister. That is an inference from an initial and is not rated. ¶2

Note the Carlson connection: CARLSON is one of the largest families at Selbu Cemetery, Whitman County, Washington (nineteen memorials), and a "IDA CARLSON SLIND, b. 1901 d. 1934" is buried in the Slind block there. Whether Olga Marie Carlson belongs to that family is not established. ¶3
