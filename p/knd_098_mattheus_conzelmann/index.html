Life story

Marriage and father: 'Matthäuß Luntzelman' (= Conzelmann), son of Martin, married Agnes Catharina 'Hoffin' (Hoff), daughter of Joh. Conrad, at Illingen on 10 Jul 1725 (groom row 8393662, relatives 'Martin, Agnes Cathar.'; bride row 908393662, relatives 'Joh. Conrad, Matthäuß'; image 1187139-00162). Children at Illingen (index): Johann Conrad 'Kuntzelman' 27 Apr 1732 (8393204, father read 'Matrÿ'), Friderica Magdalena 7 May 1748 (8395322), Matthias 26 Dec 1750 (8395428). A widow Agnes Catharina (no surname indexed) married Eberhard Friedrich Behr at Illingen on 12 Feb 1753 (8396727); possibly his widow, not proved. ¶3

Death: 'Matthæus Contzelman' died at Illingen on 9 Apr 1753 (record 8397332); the only Matthäus Conzelmann indexed at Illingen. His baptism candidate remains Tailfingen (Balingen) 3 Jun 1701, Martin x Margaretha (8634059), the only Matthäus Conzelmann son of a Martin indexed 1694-1706 (another Matthias, 1705, is son of Hannß); the move of about 80 km is unproved, so Margaretha is not entered. ¶5

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 3

Graded on that link: the bride's own row of her marriage to Johannes Finckh at Illingen on 30 Apr 1771 reads 'Friderica Magdalena Conzelmanns', relatives 'Mattheus, Johannes' (father, then groom; record 908396924, image 1187139-00345). Her own marriage names her father. Images not seen; graded on the faithfully transcribed index row under the overnight rule for this collection. Possibly the Matthias Conzelmann who with Agnes Catharina baptised a son Matthias at Illingen on 26 Dec 1750 (record 8395428); not proved, so no wife entered. ¶1b

Coordinator review: held at Probable rather than Confirmed. Ancestry collection 61023 index rows do not label who is father, mother or spouse; the BRIEF audit 5 rule is that unlabelled index rows give Probable until the scan is read, or a FamilySearch index with labelled parent fields is found (queued S0233). The naming evidence in the note above is otherwise strong, so this person is a priority for an image read. ¶2

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed, graded on his link to his daughter Friederica Magdalena Conzelmann (see her page). FamilySearch, 'Deutschland, ausgewählte evangelische Kirchenbücher 1500-1971' (= 'Germany, Lutheran Baptisms, Marriages, and Burials, 1500-1971'), record QPGR-XQL7 (Illingen, 30 Apr 1771): bride 'Friderica Magdalena Conzelmanns', field Father-in-law (of the groom Johannes Finckh) = 'Mattheus Conzelmanns'. The tree's Friederica Magdalena is that bride (the couple's daughter Louisa Catharina is baptised to 'Friederica Magdalina, Johannes Finckh' at Illingen in 1774, QPG5-KNXH). Her own baptism is also labelled: QPGP-CH5H, Friderica Magdalena Kuntzelman, bapt. Illingen 7 May 1748, Parents 'Matthaeus Kuntzelman, Agnes Catharina'. Read signed in, 2 Nov 2026; labelled index fields, image not seen (partner-only). ¶6

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Possible origin (Speculative, not linked): the Conzelmann name belongs to the Balingen district (Tailfingen, Onstmettingen); a Matthæus Conzelmann, son of Martin x Margaretha, was baptised at Tailfingen (Balingen) on 3 Jun 1701 (record 8634059). The father's name agrees, but nothing ties that child to Illingen. ¶4
