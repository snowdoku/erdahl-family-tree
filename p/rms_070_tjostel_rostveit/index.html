About the family

NEW ANCESTOR. Known only from his sons' patronymic, which two primary records state: (1) the probate of Tergie Tiøstelsøn, Rostveit, 15 Oct 1728 (Skifteprotokoll nr. 8 fol. 26; card 39942/11395), which names the deceased's brothers Knud Tiøstelsen Homstøl in Froland and Ulf Tiøstelsen Kleivene in Åmli; (2) the heirs' quitclaim (avkallsbrev) copied into Pantebok A5, fol. 52b-53, in which 'Knud Tiøstelsøn Homstøl i Frolands Sogn' and 'Ulf Tiøstelsøn' acknowledge to 'vores kiære broder Tergie Tiøstelsøn Rostved' the settlement of their inheritance, dated Rostved 26 Oct 1711 and registered at court 10 Nov 1711. Three brothers sharing the patronymic Tiøstelsøn make their father a Tjøstel. The inheritance being settled at Rostveit, with Tergie keeping the holding, suggests Tjøstel held Rostveit and was dead by Oct 1711; the deed as far as it can be read on the scan does not name him or their mother. His farm is not stated, so no farm name is given. ¶1

Life story

1664-66 censuses of Rostveit. Bailiff's census 1664 (p.11, 'Roestued'): holders Tallach 24[?] and Svenning 30; sons/servants column: 'Thiøstel Thorgiersøn 30 aar' and 'Thallach Giermundsøn 31 aar'. Vicar's census 1664 (p.7, 'Rostued iij½ hud'): opsiddere Svenning 28, Tallack Thorgeirsøn 28; 'Tiøstell er dreng ad bruger gaard 32 aar' (a farmhand who works the farm); Tallak [Bier?/Gier?]mundsøn, also 'dreng ad bruger gaard', 32. Vicar's census 1666 (p.39, 'No 19 Roastued er iij½ hud'): 'Tiøstell er 34 Aar, bruger 10 kalfskind', son 'Knud ½ Aar'. These give his patronymic (Torgeirsen) and a birth about 1632-34. ¶2

1666 census, read on the scan at zoom: Omblie sogn no. 19, 'Roßtued er iij hud' (Rostveit, 3 huder): users Svenning 32 (son Knud 6), Tallack Giermundsøn 35, 'Tiøstell er 34 aar, bruger 10 kalvskinn' with son 'Knud ½ aar', and Tallack Torgeirsøn 32 with son Torgei 1½ (DA 35510 view 30, p. 39). Tiøstel, 34, born about 1632, fits his birth estimate of about 1633, and his infant son Knud (b. about 1665) is the Knud Tiøstelsøn of Homstøl in Froland who quitclaimed to his brother Tergie in 1711 (Pantebok A5 fol. 52b-53). Tergie (b. about 1668) was not yet born. The Tallak Torgeirsen, 32, on the same farm carries the same patronymic as Tjøstel Torgeirsen and may be his brother; no record says so. The 1664-65 bailiff's list of men under 50 (DA 35567 view 24, p. 11) gives the patronymics at 'Roestued': users Tallach 24(?) and Svenning 30, and 'Thiøstel Thorgiersøn 30 aar' and 'Thollach Giermundsøn 31 aar', so Tjøstel's patronymic Torgeirsen is on a 1660s record. ¶4

The case for this identification 1

Identification (Probable, inferred): the Tjøstel of these lists is taken to be the father of Tergie, Knud and Ulf Tjøstelsen because he worked Rostveit, where Tergie later held the farm and the brothers settled their inheritance in 1711, and because his son Knud, aged ½ in 1666, matches Knud Tjøstelsen Homstøl of the 1711 avkallsbrev. His son Tergie (Torgeir) bears the name of Tjøstel's own father. Tergie and Ulf were born after March 1666 (not yet in the list). The censuses do not name his wife. ¶3
