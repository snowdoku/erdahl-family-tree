About the family

Family reconstruction: children of a Thomas Crosse ('Mr' in 1633-34) baptised in Chester: Elisabeth (St Oswald, 18 Sep 1627), John (St Peter, 9 Apr 1630), Hanna (St Peter, 24 Feb 1631/2), Jonathan (St Peter, 15 Dec 1633), a daughter (St Peter, 12 Dec 1634), probably Sarah (St Peter, 1 Jan 1638/9, no father named), Wm (St Peter, 4 Apr 1640). With the freemen rolls (Thomas Crosse, clerk, and Jonathan Crosse, ironmonger, admitted 1656-7; Daniel, ironmonger, 1647-8), this is a family of at least four sons (Daniel?, Thomas?, John, Jonathan, William), consistent with a 4th son Benjamin, but Benjamin's own baptism was not found. The gaps where he could fall: 1628-29 (BT faint), 1636 (no St Peter BT in the film), the torn names of Jan 1638/9, or another parish. Grade unchanged (Probable, on his link to Benjamin Cross): no record read names Benjamin as Thomas's son except the Gray's Inn register, and the identification with the Cork precentor still has the Rostherne rival. ¶7

Life story

Chester St Peter bishop's transcripts read page by page (FamilySearch signed in, DGS 4008469 item 2, 'Chester St Peter. Bishop Transcript Records 1600-1879'; images 21-30 read on the image): (1) BT 1630 (image 22): 'John sonne of [?Mr] Thomas Crosse Aprill 9th' [1630]. Not in the FamilySearch index. (2) BT 1633 (image 25, headed 'Christenings in the parish of St Peters... in the yeare 1633'): 'Jonathan son of Mr Tho: Crosse was bapt ye fifteenth of December 1633'. The next line, 'Thomas son of Wm Crofts', is a different surname, written with a clear -fts; the Crosse entry has the long-s double s. A Jonathan Crosse, ironmonger, was admitted a freeman of Chester in 1656-7 (aged about 23), which fits this child. (3) Correction to: the BT on image 26 is for 1634, not 1633 (its burials are headed 'Burialls 1634' and its weddings 'Weddings 1634'; it follows the 1633 BT on image 25). So '... daughter of Mr Thom: Crosse was baptized December 12' is 12 Dec 1634; the FamilySearch index (1:1:NH8X-MQP) dates it 1633. (4) BT 1638 (image 29, headed 'Christenings in the parish of Saint Peter in Chester in the yeare 1638'; this year's entries give the child's name only, no father): 'Sarah Cross was baptised the first day of January' [1638/9]. Three January entries have their names lost at the torn left edge. (5) Read with no Crosse baptism: BTs 1632 (image 24; only 'mary & martha daughters of Raph Crosse[?]', 17 Mar 1632/3, a different father), 1637 (image 28), 1639 (image 30); 1635 (image 27) was. 1629 (image 21) is faint at the left edge, no Crosse seen. No St Peter BT for 1636 is in this image group (image 27 is 1635, image 28 is 1637). No Benjamin Crosse in any of them, and no Crosse burial in the 1632, 1634, 1637 or 1639 burial lists. ¶6

Chester records: a Thomas Crosse had children baptised in the Chester city parishes: Elisabeth at St Oswald, 18 Sep 1627 (England, Cheshire, Parish Registers, 1:1:F7VT-VXL); Hanna at St Peter, 24 Feb 1631[/2] (Cheshire BTs, 1:1:NH86-T7P); a daughter at St Peter, 12 Dec 1633, whose first name is lost at the torn margin, entered as '... daughter of Mr Thom: Crosse was baptized December 12' (image read: Chester St Peter BT for 1633, DGS 4008469 item 2, image 26; the index gives her residence as St Oswald); and Wm at St Peter, 4 Apr 1640 (1:1:NH8X-3P5). The 'Mr' marks a man of standing, which fits the father of a Gray's Inn and Oxford son. No baptism of a Benjamin Crosse is indexed in Cheshire 1625-1645, and the St Peter BT for 1635 (image 27, read) has no Crosse entry. Grade unchanged (Speculative): none of these records names Benjamin. ¶5a

St Oswald, Chester, register read on the image (FamilySearch signed in; film opened from 1:1:F7VT-VXL). (1) The 1627 baptism reads in full 'Elisabeth the daughter of Thomas Crosse of St peters p[ar]ish bap: 18o Sept 1627' (image 148). So the St Oswald and St Peter children are one family living in St Peter's parish. (2) St Oswald baptisms read entry by entry from May 1628 to 27 Mar 1636 (images 147-165, odd images; the even images are repeat shots): no child of Thomas Crosse. Other Crosse entries: 'Samuel the sonne of Raphe Crosse' bapt. 27 Jul 1634 (image 157), a different father. 'Benjamin the sonne of Tho: Heatsley', bapt. 28 Feb 1635[/6] (image 165), is NOT a Crosse (the same father has a son Samuel in Jan 1634/5). (3) After 27 Mar 1636 the register says 'Turne to the other ende of the Booke on the right hand for the next' (image 165). At the other end (image 451) the first surviving page is damaged and begins 'March 1639' [i.e. 1639/40], then 'Anno 1640'. So St Oswald baptisms from Apr 1636 to Feb 1639/40 are lost, which is the window (c.1635-40) for a 4th son Benjamin. Mar-Jun 1640 read: no Crosse. (4) St Oswald burials read for Sep 1642-Feb 1645/6 and Oct 1654-Aug 1657 (images 417-431): no burial of Thomas Crosse. The register has no burials from Mar 1645/6 to Oct 1654 (image 427 resumes 'William Jones buried the 17th Octo: 1654'). Collateral: 'Mary daughter of Daniell Crosse buried 10th of Aprill' 1657 (image 431), so Daniel Crosse (ironmonger, free 1647-8) had a family in Chester in 1657. Dec 1656: 'Wm Erollett [or Trollock?] buried the 15th', not a Crosse. Grade unchanged (Probable, graded on his link to Benjamin Cross): no baptism names Benjamin as his son. ¶8a

Thomas Crosse of Chester. Foster's Alumni Oxonienses notes a Benjamin Crosse, B.A. Hart Hall 1653-4, M.A. 1655, admitted to Gray's Inn in 1657 'as 4th son of Thomas, of Chester'. Venn independently describes Benjamin Crosse, the future Precentor of Cloyne and husband of Anne Eveleigh, as 'of Chester' when he was admitted to Emmanuel College in 1657. Added. ¶1

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

2 on the grade · 1 alternative set aside · 4 open questions

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Speculative, graded on his link to Benjamin Cross: that the Oxford and Gray's Inn Benjamin Crosse is the same man as the Emmanuel man and the Cork precentor is a guess based on the name, the city and the date. ¶2a

Upgrade: Speculative to Probable, graded on his link to Benjamin Cross. The Gray's Inn admission register, read in Foster's printed edition (1889), gives under July 1657: '13. Benjamin Crosse, fourth son of Thomas C., of Chester, gent., deceased'. So the relationship is named in a record, and Thomas was dead by 13 Jul 1657. The cap is the identification of this Benjamin with the Cork precentor: (1) Venn, independently, has the future Precentor of Cloyne and husband of Anne Eveleigh admitted at Emmanuel on 24 Jun 1657 'of Chester', three weeks before the Gray's Inn admission; (2) Foster's Alumni Oxonienses has the Gray's Inn man as B.A. of Hart Hall 1653-4 and M.A. 1655, and the precentor had Oxford ties (Blennerhassett calls him 'Doctor Benjamin Cross of Blackhall in Oxford'; Dean Eveleigh, whose kinsman John Eveleigh had been Principal of Hart Hall 1599-1604, left him the Blackhall lands). A named rival exists: a Benjamin Cross, clerk, of Rostherne, Cheshire, whose will was proved at Chester 1660-80 (Chester wills index, RS vol. 15). He could be the Gray's Inn man (though not the precentor, who died in Dorset in 1684). So this stays Probable. Added. ¶3

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Family candidates, not proved: the Chester freemen rolls show a family of Crosse ironmongers with biblical names: Thomas Crosse, ironmonger (master of an apprentice in 1634-5); Daniel Crosse, ironmonger (free 1647-8); and Thomas Crosse, clerk, and Jonathan Crosse, ironmonger, admitted together in 1656-7. A Thomas Crosse was also admitted in 1624-5 as apprentice of 'Thomas Tu...'. Thomas Crosse the ironmonger, with sons Thomas (clerk), Daniel, Jonathan and Benjamin (4th), would fit the Gray's Inn entry, but no record ties them. No will of a Thomas Crosse of Chester is indexed at Chester 1621-50 or 1660-80, or in PCC 1625-90. The Visitations of Cheshire 1580 and 1613 have no Crosse of Chester pedigree. ¶4a

Open questions and the next record to read 4
