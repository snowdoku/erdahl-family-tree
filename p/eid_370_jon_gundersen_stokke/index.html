Life story

Farmer at Stokkan (Midti-Stokkan), Selbu. His probate (in the card index 77804, canvas 1885, filed as 'Stokkan, Selbu'; read in the protocol 24749 view 106, fol. 108a, lower half, and view 107, fol. 108b): 'Anno 1693 den 22 May holtes skifte paa Stochen efter s[alig] Joen Gundersen... imellem hans efterleffuende Hustru Kirsti Ols Daatter paa den ene og deris barn Ingeborre Joensdatter paa den anden side, Myndig'. Estate 55 rd. 0 ort 18 sk.; after debts 13-3-4 to divide, 'Moderen og barnet' get 6-3-14 each. Ingeborg was the only child and of age. ¶1

Birth about 1623 is from the bygdebok only (ABT). ¶4

The card index has a second 1693 Stokkan card (canvas 1887, 'Stokkan ytre', 22/5-1693, Joen Joensen, widow Ellen Gundersdtr., heir his brother's son Anders Joensen, fol. 109b). That is a different estate on the outer farm, with no children. ¶3

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the grade · 1 on the identification · 1 open question

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Graded Probable on his link to his daughter Ingeborg Jonsdatter (see her page). The probate names the relationship ('deris barn Ingeborre Joensdatter'). What is inferred is that the 1693 heiress is the Ingeborg who held Midti-Stokkan with Ola Bjørnsen from 1697 (bygdebok vol. 6, canvas 19, which makes her Jon Gunnarsen's daughter). The nearest primary bridge is the churching of 'Olef B[iørnsen] Stokke's wife Ingebor after 6th child Jon' in 1708 (16310 c35), 15 years later on the same farm, but it gives only her first name, so the identity rests on name plus farm. ¶2

The case for this identification 1

Test record read: the burial of 'Ingebor Jonsd: Stockun 82 aar gl.' on Sexagesima [18 Feb] 1759 (Selbu 16311 canvas 7, right page; the same day 'Ane Persd: Marstad 17 aar gl.') gives the patronymic Jonsdatter and the farm Stokkan for the widow of Midti-Stokkan. Age 82 means born about 1677, which fits the bygdebok but makes the 1693 heiress only about 16, although the 1693 skifte calls her 'Myndig'; burial ages of this period are often inflated, so that is a noted tension, not a refutation. Graded still Probable on his link to his daughter: the 1759 burial adds patronymic plus farm (audit 3: Probable, never Confirmed) and does not name her husband or parents. ¶6

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Test record: a record naming Ingeborg Jonsdatter as Ola Bjørnsen's wife, such as the probate after Ola Bjørnsen Stokke (d. about 1738; no 1727-42 Stokkan card in 77804 c1877-1899) or her burial in 1759 (Selbu 16311, about canvases 7-10, 'Ingebor Joensd. Stokke'), or a Stokkan deed in the 1690s. ¶5
