Life story

Born 4 Feb 1826 and baptized 5 Feb 1826 in Treungen, son of Osmund Tarjesen and Anne Thorufsdatter of Hjemdal. Both church-book copies agree. ¶2

Married 26 Dec 1849 in Nissedal/Treungen. The record gives his birthplace as Heimdal and his father as Osmund Torjesen, and the bride as Gunbjør Ormsdatter, b. ~1827 at Tvedt, daughter of Orm Egiltsen. This confirms the Find a Grave parentage. ¶3

Lived at Tveit after the marriage (1851, 1853) and at Heimdal from 1856 to 1859. He is not in the 1865 Nissedal census. ¶5

Emigration: Gunbjør's Find a Grave biography says they registered to emigrate on 7 Jul 1861 and reached Perry, Dane Co., Wisconsin, that fall with three children (Osmund, Ingebor, Anne). They moved to Hartland Twp., Worth Co., Iowa, between 1864 and 1868, then to Fertile Twp. in 1873. The Norwegian emigrant list itself is not indexed, so this has not been checked against it. ¶6

Emigration: the Treungen out-migration list (Afgangs-Lister) for 1861, rows 9-13, records 'Thoruf Osmundsen Heimdal og Hustru Gunbjør Ormsdatter og deres Børn Osmund, Ingeborg, Anne', ages 36, 34½, 10, 7½ and 5½. He is listed as Gmd (farmer), the destination is America (ditto), and the entry is dated 7/4 61, which is 7 April 1861. Find a Grave's '7 Jul 1861' is evidently the same date read month-first. Others on the 1861 page who left the same spring for America were Rolf [O]smundsen[?] Tvedt, 23 (attest 6/4 61), Halvor Halvorsen, Tellef Guttormsen and John Holgersen[?] Mone with family. Gunbjør's brother Thoruf Ormsen Tvedt and Thoruf's sister Turi Osmundsdatter are not in this group. They are on the 1862 list (rows 43-46, dated 15/4 62), so they followed a year later rather than travelling together. ¶9

The Silver Lake (Worth Co., Iowa) church book lists 'Toruf Osmundsen' as father of Gunhild, b. 18 Nov 1868 and baptized at Shell Rock. The mother is transcribed 'Gunvor Ovinsd', almost certainly a misreading of Gunbjør Ormsd. This matches Gunhild H. Heimdal Rollefsen (1868-1893). ¶7

His death (16 May 1888, Fertile Twp.) and burial (Concordia Cemetery, Joice) come from Find a Grave only. ¶8
