Life story

Named as the surviving widow of Rasmus Olsen Erdal in his probate of 30 May 1714. The reading 'Christi(an) Biørnsdatter' is uncertain. ¶1

Aaland 1932: Aaland's Erdal history names Rasmus Olsen's wife as 'Brite (Jørgensd.)', with the patronymic bracketed as inferred, and does not mention a Kirsti. If Aaland is right, Brite was probably Rasmus's first wife and the mother of his children born about 1678-1700, and Kirsti the widow of 1714 a second wife. That would make Kirsti's motherhood of Jørgen less likely. The 1714 probate text (fol. 327-328) is the record to re-read: probates normally say which heirs were 'begotten with' the widow and name a guardian for children of an earlier marriage. ¶3

Probate 1714, division: the rest of Rasmus Olsen Erdal's probate of 30 May 1714, Nordfjord skifteprotokoll A 5b fol. 328b-330 (images 558-559), was read. The total estate ('Summa Summarum') was 217 rd 12 sk (rd = riksdaler, sk = skilling) and the debts and costs 32 rd 4 sk, leaving 'Den rene igienstaaende Boe' (the clear remaining estate) of 185 rd 8 sk. All of it was divided among the six children: two brother's lots ('Broderlod') of 46 rd 1 ort 2 sk and four sister's lots ('Søsterlod') of 23 rd 13 sk. The arithmetic closes exactly (8 x 23 rd 13 sk = 185 rd 8 sk). The widow Christiane received no half-share and no lot. No mother's inheritance (morsarv), earlier wife or first marriage is named in the parts read. ¶6

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Confidence stays Speculative for her motherhood of Jørgen (reader, 1714 probate fol. 327-327b read in full for the parties): the widow is one party and 'den Sal: [Mand]s efterladende samptlige Børn og Arvinger' (the late man's surviving children and heirs) the other: Ole (on military service, represented), Jørgen (of age, present), Barbra, Kari and Brite (married) and Mari (unmarried). The record never calls them her children or 'their' children, and it also never mentions an earlier marriage or a mother's inheritance. It neither proves nor disproves her motherhood. Aaland's 'Brite (Jørgensd.)' as Rasmus's wife has no support in this record; Brite appears only as a daughter. ¶5

Confidence stays Speculative as Jørgen's mother, and the 1714 division now weighs against it. Under Christian V's Norwegian Law a widow in community of property, and the mother of the heirs, normally takes half the estate. Her complete exclusion fits a later wife married under a marriage settlement or with separate property better than the children's mother; a renunciation not recorded in the legible parts cannot be excluded. Jørgen was born about 1681, when Rasmus was about 30. Recommendation: treat Christiane as Rasmus's last wife and probably Jørgen's stepmother. The parent link to Jørgen Rasmussen Erdal should be removed or kept only with this warning. A first wife of Rasmus (perhaps Aaland's 'Brite (Jørgensd.)') would be the more likely mother. ¶7

Corrections 1

Correction: given name changed from Kirsti to Christiane. Re-read of the 1714 probate preamble, Nordfjord skifteprotokoll A 5b fol. 327 (image 45), at x3: 'imellem den efterlevende Enke Christiane Biørnsdatter... med hendes Lauværge...'. The letters C-h-r-i-s-t-i-a-n-e are each legible (the scribe's n looks like u); Biørnsdatter is clear. Christiane is a learned spelling of the same name family as Kirsten/Kirstine. Crop:. ¶4
