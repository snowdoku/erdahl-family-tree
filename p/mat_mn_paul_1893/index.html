Life story

An Eidem man of Marietta, Lac qui Parle County, Minnesota who married a woman of his own surname — the same pattern as Palmer Gilbert Eidem and Mayme Eidem a generation later, in the same village, and therefore evidence about the shape of this community rather than a curiosity. Three of his children's Social Security applications name him: FATHER: PAUL EIDEM, MOTHER: GINA EIDEM. NARA AAD, NUMIDENT Application (SS-5) Files 1936-2007, rids 421612, 421682, 421686, 1896965, 755846, 3545646. ¶1

Marriage: to Gina Klegseth/Eidem (1891-1967) in 1913. ¶11

Social Security: a DEATH CLAIM was filed on the account of PAUL EIDEM, born 22 Apr 1893, at the MARSHALL, MINNESOTA field office in March 1957 — so he died shortly before that, leaving someone to claim as survivor. NARA AAD, NUMIDENT Claim Files 1936-2007 (Last Names D-G), rid 1352248. Marshall was the field office serving Lac qui Parle County. ¶2

The Paul Eidem of the younger generation, born 22 April 1893 (wave 2's Minnesota index extract, mother's maiden name 'Slind') and buried at Zoar. Wave 2 established that he cannot be a son of John P. Eidem, who was only 17 in 1893; this work adds that he is also not a son of Mikal and Beret, whose first recorded child is Minnie in 1895 — though a 1893 eldest son for that couple is not excluded and is the best candidate on the table, given Beret's maiden name of Slind. ¶10

1895 state census: Mehurin Township, Lac qui Parle County, page 184, line 10 - 'Eidem Paul M., 2 1/2, M, W, born Minnesota', in the household of Mike Eidem, 26, and Betsy Eidem, 23, with his grandmother Carrie P. Eidem, 61, and his uncle John Eidem, 19. Aged two and a half in June 1895 he was born about December 1892 or early 1893, which is close enough to the 22 April 1893 on his Minnesota death record for a census age. ¶5

1895 Minnesota state census: Mehurin township, Lac qui Parle County, page 0184 line 10 - 'Eidem Paul M., 2 1/2, M, W, born Minnesota'. An age of two and a half at 6 June 1895 points to about December 1892, a few months earlier than the registered birth date of 22 April 1893; I treat '2 1/2' as a rounded figure and do not let it overturn the birth record. ¶16

Marriage: Lac qui Parle County certificate F13207, 'EIDEM, PAUL' and 'EIDEM, GURINE', 29 December 1913. Gurine - Gina - was herself an Eidem, the daughter of Anders A. Eidem and Brynhild Kulsetmo, so this was a marriage of Eidem cousins. Their children registered in Lac qui Parle County were Bernice Marian (1914), a stillborn son (26 February 1923), Harriet Adeline (18 September 1924), Elaine Beatrice (28 August 1927), Carol Patricia (25 December 1928) and Marlys Ann (18-24 November 1934). ¶8

A 'Paul C. Eidem' of Marietta stood as informant on the 1933 Minnesota birth card of Edward Lyle Slind, son of Elias Slind and Emma Paulson (recorded by the sister agent slind-us) — an Eidem signing for a Slind birth, which is the two families in one document. The birth year 1893 and the Marietta residence make that man and this one very probably the same, which is why I carry the middle initial C. ¶3

Death: 16 March 1957, Lac qui Parle County - Minnesota death certificate index 1957-MN-008716, birth 22 April 1893, birthplace Minnesota, mother's maiden name SLIND. The mother's-maiden-name field is what ties him to Berit Slind, and the 1895 census is what ties him to Mikal's household. ¶6

Death: 16 March 1957, from the South Dakota state burial register — 'PAUL EIDEM 03-16-1957, lot 3, block 0'. This sharpens Find a Grave's bare '1957'. Wave 2 recorded a death of 16 March 1957 in Lac qui Parle County from the Minnesota index; the two agree exactly, which is a good cross-check on the register. ¶12

Burial plot: Zoar Lutheran Cemetery, lot 3, with Gina. ¶14

Two agents working in parallel created this man twice, as Paul C. Eidem from his Minnesota birth registration and as mat_zor_paul_1893 from his gravestone at Zoar Lutheran. Both give 22 April 1893 and they are one person; the records are merged here and the duplicate record was removed. ¶15

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

Corrections 1

Correction: wave 2 had reasoned, correctly, that Paul could not be a son of John P. Eidem, who was only seventeen in 1893, and guessed he was John P.'s brother. He is in fact John P.'s nephew - the eldest son of John's elder brother Mikal, born a month after his parents' wedding. ¶7

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Not yet connected to a parent. He is of Palmer Gilbert Eidem's own generation and farmed the same village, so he is most likely a cousin — a son of one of John Paulsen Eidem's brothers. The record that would place him is the 1900 or 1910 census of Mehurin or Augusta Township. ¶4
