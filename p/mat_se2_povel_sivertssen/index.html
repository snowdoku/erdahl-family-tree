About the family

Father of Anne Paulsdatter and therefore the maternal grandfather of Paul Eidem, who carries his name. 1801 census, Selbu, rural residence 0028 Alseth, household 02: 'Povel Sivertssen', 38, Mand (head of household), with his wife Marit Bersvendsdatter, 40, and their two-year-old daughter Ane Poulsdatter. His occupation is entered as 'I faellesskab med moderen' - farming in common with his mother, the widow Ane Gundersdatter, 65, who held Alsaet in her own right. ¶1

His father was a Sivert, dead before February 1801; his mother Ane Gundersdatter is in the house as a widow after her first marriage. His siblings in household 01 are Inger Sivertsdatter, 30, Karen Sivertsdatter, 27, and Gunder Sivertssen, 22, the last a 'National soldat ved skiloeberne' - a conscript in the Norwegian ski troops. ¶2

Daughter Anne's baptism: 'Povel Siversen Alsæt' is the father of Anne, born 12 January 1799 and baptised 20 January 1799, mother Marit Bersvendsdatter (Selbu ministerialbok 1757-1801, p. 230, no. 1). Confidence raised from Probable to Confirmed: the baptism names him and his wife as Anne's parents, matching the 1801 census household (Alseth 02, daughter Ane aged 2), and her birth date agrees with her age of 25 at her 1824 marriage to Ingebrigt Johnsen. ¶4

Kin at Anne's baptism: two of the five godparents were 'Gunner Siversen Alsæt' and 'Karen Siversdatter Alsæt' - his brother Gunder Sivertssen (22) and sister Karen Sivertsdatter (27) of the 1801 census, Alseth household 01, his mother's household. ¶5

Father, Probable: Siur (Sivert) Olsen of Alsaet (see his page), husband of Povel's mother Ane Gundersdatter, father of Povel's brother Gunder (baptised 5 September 1779, Selbu ministerialbok 1757-1801, canvas 98 right), buried 27 March 1780 aged 51 (canvas 101 right). Povel's own baptism has not been found; the argument and what would confirm it are on Siur's record. ¶8

Parents proved: in the probate after his father, 5 May 1780 (Stjør- og Verdal Skifteprotokoll 8, 24759, fol. 291a), he is the first heir, '1. Sønnen Paul Siversen 17 aar gammel', son of the late Sivert Olsen Alset and his widow Anne Gundersdatter. His age gives a birth about 1762-63, in agreement with the 1801 census (38) and his burial (76). Siblings: Gunder 1, Ingri 10, Karen 6. ¶9

Life story

Marriage: betrothed on the 4th Sunday after Easter, 24 April 1796: 'Trolovet unge mand Povel Sivers. Alsæt, med Pigen Marite Bersvends d: Fuglem' - betrothed, the young man (bachelor) Povel Sivertsen of Alsaet with the maiden Marit Bersvendsdatter of Fuglem; forlovere (sureties) Ole Ingebrigtsen Kieldstad and Erich Nielsen Aune (canvas 192). Banns read on 1 May, 5 May (Ascension Day) and 8 May 1796; the third calling names him 'Povel Sivertsen Garberg'. Married on the 4th Sunday after Trinity, 19 June 1796: 'item, unge Mand Povel Sivers. Alsædt, med Pigen Marite Bersvends datter Fuglem' (canvas 194 right page). Selbu ministerialbok 1757-1801. 'Unge mand' and 'pige' mean both were marrying for the first time, as the 1801 census also says. This replaces the inferred 'before 1799'; their daughter Anne was born on 12 January 1799, two and a half years after the wedding. ¶6

Death and burial: died 15 February 1839 and buried 24 February 1839: 'Paul Sivertsen Alset Kaarmand', aged 76, residence Alset - a kårmann, a retired holder living on a life-pension (kår) from the farm. Church book from Selbu parish 1824-1842, burials 1839, p. 395 no. 11; index entry, scan (the index leaves residence blank; the scan gives Alsaet). Age 76 gives a birth in 1762-63, exactly as the 1801 census (38). Rated Probable-strong: name, farm, standing and age all agree. The other candidate, 'Paul Sivertsen' buried 19 September 1841 aged 75 (p. 403 no. 44), is a husmann (cottager) of Haavsmoe, born about 1766 - probably the second Povel Sivertssen, born about 1768, in the 1801 census of Selbu - and is rejected. ¶7
