Life story

Baptism: Leikanger 1832, no. 51: Johannes, born 2 March, baptised 4 March 1832 (bracketed with nos. 46-51), legitimate; parents 'Husmd. Anders Olsen og Ingebor Gjestsd. Esse' (cottager Anders Olsen and Ingebor Gjestsdatter of Esse). Godparents: Paul Esse, Niels ib. [of the same farm], Anfind Bale, Anna Christensdatter Tjørestad[?] and Gjertrud Esse. ¶1

Emigration: the Balestrand list of people moving out records 'Johannes Andersen Esse', bachelor, born 10 March 1832, leaving for North America on 12 April 1856, the same day as his brother Ole's family. The '10 March' differs from the baptism register's 2 March; the name, farm, year and month agree. ¶2
