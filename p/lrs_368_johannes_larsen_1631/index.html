About the family

Father of Mons Johansen (see his page), confidence Probable: the 1701 census itself lists Mons Johansen 22 as his son. That this Mons is the Mons Johansen who died at Indre Oppedal before 16 May 1739 rests on: the same farm; the same name; an age (born about 1679) that fits a man whose youngest son Torkild was born about 1719 (the 1739 probate gives no ages for the other four sons); no other Mons Johansen on the Indre Oppedal or Ytre Oppedal pages of 1701 (the only other Mons at Indre Oppedal is a servant boy Mons Iversen, 10); and Mons Johansen being the only Mons in the Ytre Sogn probate index with domicile Oppedal 1720-1759. Mons's second son was named Johannes, which fits naming after this grandfather (inference). Not Confirmed, because no record yet links the 1739 decedent to Johannes Larsen by name. His place in this line also depends on Mons Johansen being the father of Torkild Monsen of Asheim, which is itself Probable. ¶2a

Son Lars and 1701 probate: on 30 May 1701 a probate was held on Indre Oppedal after his son Lars Johansen (see his page) and Lars's wife Eli Monsdatter, both dead; the heir on Lars's side was 'hans fader Johannes Larsen Indre Oppedal' (his father), so Lars was childless. Eli was the widow of Iver Lassesen and mother of Randi Iversdatter, later the wife of Johannes's son Mons Johansen. This shows Johannes alive on 30 May 1701 and a second son on the farm; it does not by itself prove that the Mons Johansen who died in 1739 was his son. Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll 1b, fol. 331b-333a. ¶4

Wife and 1709 probate: on 26 May 1709 a probate was held on Indre Oppedal after 'Sal. Anna Torchels datter' between 'hendis efterlevende Mand Johanes Lars[en]' and 'hans Børn og Arf[vinger]', among them 'Mons Oppedahl' (Ytre Sogn 2b fol. 370a, source 24156 view 64). This is a second record (after the 1701 census) naming a Mons of Oppedal as Johannes Larsen's child, 8 years later and 6 years after Mons Johansen's marriage to Randi Iversdatter. It strengthens but does not by itself settle his link to the Mons Johansen who died at Indre Oppedal in 1739 (still 30 years apart, identified by name and farm), so the grade stays Probable. Other heirs: Gutorm Oppedal, the children of the late Anders Erdal[?] (Lars, Niels, Sigtrud[?]), and Mari, wife of Jon Iversen[?] (readings uncertain). Johannes was alive on 26 May 1709. ¶14

Life story

1664 census: probably the farmer 'Johans' on Indre Oppedal in the bailiff's census of 21 Jun 1664, listed on the row after the farmer Lasse; Johans is the only adult Johannes at either Oppedal farm. The list gives farmers no age or patronymic, so the identification is inference. Two servants are bracketed on his row: Johans Haldorsen 30 and Willum Johansen 15. Willum is in the servants' column, not the sons' column, and a son aged 15 (born about 1649) would be very unlikely for a man born about 1631, so Willum is probably not his son. Digitalarkivet 35573, viewer 129, s.239. ¶6

1665 matrikkel: the land register of 1665 for Indre Oppedal (No. 6, fol. 75) names the users only by given name, 'Lars, Johannes, Olle og Johannes...'. No patronymics appear, so this does not identify Johannes Larsen's father. ¶9

1701 census: listed on the farm Indre Oppedal (Bondegaard, a farm held by peasants), Eivindvik (Gulen) parish, as 'Johans Lars:' (Johannes Larsen), aged 70, with the son 'Mons Johansen' aged 22 and the servant Iver Bendixsen, aged 20, 'fød paa gaarden' (born on the farm). Birth about 1631 is inferred from the age of 70. Only men and boys are listed, so his wife and any daughters are not named. Manntall 1701, Digitalarkivet 38455, viewer 54, printed s.91; reading of the name and both ages. ¶1

Burials at Brekke, 10 Dec 1717: at the service in Brekke church on 10 Dec 1717 (Comm: 35): 'graflagt Johannes Lars: Jnderopdal ætat: 84[?] Aar, Item Kari Torsdr: Engesætter ætat: 58 Aar, Item Kari Andersdr: Indreopdal ætat: 39 Aar, Item Tor Ifversøn Engesætter ætat: 10[?] uger og 5[?] Maaneder' (buried Johannes Larsen of Indre Oppedal aged 84, Kari Torsdatter of Engesetter 58, Kari Andersdatter of Indre Oppedal 39, Tor Iversen of Engesetter, an infant). Gulen ministerialbok 1716-1722 (source 11438), canvas 20, left page, lower third. The first digit of Johannes's age is probably 8 (9 is not excluded). ¶12

Heirs' line re-read at higher zoom (crops, a2.jpg): 'imellem hendis efterlevende Mand Johanes Lars: og hans Børn og Arf: Gutorm Oppedahl, Sal: Peder Opedals Børn Lars, Niels og Sigri[?], samt Brite, Peders Sønner og Døttre, Mons Oppedahl og Marj Jon Iversens[?], alle giftte og deris giftemaal[?]'. 'hans Børn' (his children) is clear; the word is not 'deris'. ¶16a

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed, graded on his link to his son Mons Johansen. Two primary records name the relationship: the 1701 census of Indre Oppedal lists 'Mons Johansen', 22, as the son of 'Johans Lars:', 70 (38455 viewer 54, s.91); and the probate of his wife Anna Torkelsdatter, 26 May 1709 (2b fol. 370a), divides between 'Johanes Lars:' and 'hans Børn' (his children), among them 'Mons Oppedahl'. Continuity to the tree's Mons Johansen (d. before 16 May 1739) through consecutive Indre Oppedal records, none more than about 15 years apart: 1701 census (son, 22) - 3 Jun / 5 Jul 1703 betrothal and marriage of 'Juv: Mons Johansøn Indreoppedahl' and Randi Iversdatter 'ibid.' - 15 Aug 1704 baptism of 'Mons Johans: Indreoppedahls Iver' - 1709 heir 'Mons Oppedahl' - 21 Oct 1719 baptism of 'Mons Indreopdals' son Torckel, mother Randi - 1739 probate of Mons Johansen, widow Randi Iversdatter, sons Iver and Torchel among them. The wife Randi carries the identity across 1704-1739. ¶15a

The case for this identification 1

Identity and death: 'Johannes Lars: Jnderopdal', buried 10 Dec 1717 aged 84[?], is this man: the 1701 census has 'Johans Lars:' 70 on Indre Oppedal with son Mons Johansen 22, and the 1701 probate after Lars Johansen names his father Johannes Larsen of Indre Oppedal; no other Johannes Larsen is known on the farm. He died before 10 Dec 1717. Age 84 gives a birth about 1633, against about 1631 from the 1701 age of 70; the birth field is left as ABT 1631. This record dates his death and does not by itself change the Probable link to Mons Johansen. ¶13

Records that disagree 1

Against: the 1701 census lists only Mons Johansen (22) and a servant with Johannes Larsen, so Colben was not in his household in 1701. Gulen ministerialbok 1699-1705 (Digitalarkivet 11433), canvas 96, right page, printed p. 92. ¶10b

Corrections 2

Correction: Colben of Indre Oppedal, who married the widow Giertrud Torsdatter of Myren at Ladvik (Lavik) on 28 Oct 1702, is not a candidate son of Johannes Larsen. Re-read at 2x and 3x, his patronymic is 'Jons' with a final long s: 'Jon' is clear and no 'h' is visible, so he was Colben Jonsen, not Johannessen. With the 1701 census already showing no Colben in Johannes Larsen's household, nothing ties him to this family. Gulen ministerialbok 1699-1705 (Digitalarkivet 11433), canvas 96, right page, p. 92 (crops). This supersedes the patch 036 note 'Possible son Colben (inference only, not linked)'; no link was ever set, so nothing has to be removed. ¶11

Correction: the earlier reading 'Sal. Anders Erdal[?]s Børn Lars, Niels og Sigtrud[?]' should be 'Sal: Peder Opedals Børn Lars, Niels og Sigri[?], samt Brite, Peders Sønner og Døttre' (the late Peder of Oppedal's children). The heirs are thus four children of Johannes: Guttorm, the late Peder (through his children Lars, Niels, Sigri and Brite), Mons, and Mari wife of Jon Iversen. This matches the Brekke bygdebok (canvases 71-72), which gives Johannes Larsson and Anna Torkelsdtr. six children: Guttorm b. 1662, Mari b. 1664 (m. Jon), Peder b. 1667 d. 1705 (children Lars, Nils, Siri, Brita), Lars b. 1670 (d. childless 1700-01, his probate naming his father as heir), Mons b. 1678, and Erik b. 1681 d. 1701 unmarried; exactly the surviving lines in 1709. ¶16b

Alternatives considered and set aside 5

A Gullov Johansen at Indre Oppedal in 1700 (not linked): the widower 'Gullowe Johansen [Indre?]' at the probate after his wife Agate Monsdatter on Indre Oppedal, 8 Oct [1700], could be another son of Johannes (same farm and patronymic; Mons and Lars Johansen are creditors in that estate). He is not linked as a son: see the note on Gullov Johansen of Asseim (see his page) for why he is probably not that man. ¶5

Father not linked: Johannes Larsen could in principle be a son of Lasse of Indre Oppedal (see his page), who farmed the next holding in 1664: same farm and a matching patronymic. He is deliberately not linked, even as Speculative, because: (1) the 1664 list gives no relationship between the two farmers, and Johannes appears as a farmer in his own right, not in Lasse's sons' column; (2) Lasse's only listed son is Iver 13, born about 1651, twenty years after Johannes, so Lasse would need to have been born by about 1610 and to have fathered sons twenty years apart; (3) at the 1699 probate after Iver Lassesen, a 'Johannes Oppedal' (probably this Johannes, who is called 'Johannes Larsen Indre Oppedal' in the 1701 probate; but the record does not say Indre, and Johans 13, son of the farmer Olluf at Ytre Oppedal in 1664, born about 1651, is another possible Johannes Oppedal) sat as one of the lagrettemenn (sworn court assessors), and the guardian present for Iver's under-age children was their mother's brother Ole Monsen, not a paternal uncle; a full brother of the deceased would normally have been the children's guardian and would not have sat on the court (inference from usual practice, not a stated rule in the record); (4) if Johannes and Iver were brothers, Johannes's son Lars Johansen would have married his own uncle's widow (Eli Monsdatter) and Johannes's son Mons Johansen his first cousin (Randi Iversdatter), both unions that would have needed a royal dispensation, of which no trace has been found. ¶7a

1645 poll tax (index rows; not linked): on 'Opedahl' (taken to be Indre Oppedal, inference) the 1645 poll tax also lists the husbond Laurids (= Lars) (pt00000000059715) with his kone Karen Pedersdtr (pt00000000059716). They are candidate parents for Johannes Larsen, born about 1631, who at about 14 might not be listed; this is inference only, and the farmer Lasse on the same farm is another candidate. ¶8

Possible son Colben (inference only, not linked): on 28 Oct 1702 at Ladvik (Lavik) church 'Copulered Juv: Colben Jons[?] Indreoppedahl, Vid: Giertrud Thorsd: Myren' (bachelor Kolbein of Indre Oppedal married the widow Gjertrud Torsdatter of Myren); the patronymic, read 'Jons', but the tall ascender of the third letter favours 'Johs' (an abbreviation of Johannessen), is uncertain. If it is Johannessen he may be a son of Johannes Larsen and a brother of Mons Johansen. ¶10a

Rival: Mons Iversen of Indre Oppedal (m. 1718 Giertlaug Lassesdatter) is not a Johannesen and had a different wife; no other Mons Johansen is known on the farm. The earlier objection (a 1709-1739 gap) is closed by the 1703 marriage, the 1704 and 1719 baptisms and the wife Randi. ¶15b

Searches that found nothing 1

Not found: no death, burial or probate for him has been searched; he was alive in 1701 at about 70. ¶3

Open questions and the next record to read 3

Would confirm: a probate after Johannes Larsen of Indre Oppedal (after 1701) naming Mons as heir, or a land or court record calling Mons Johansen of Indre Oppedal the son of Johannes Larsen. ¶2b

Would settle it: the 1661-1665 matrikkel or a court record giving the Indre Oppedal farmers' patronymics, or a probate after Lasse naming his heirs. ¶7b

Would settle it: a clearer reading of the patronymic, or Colben's own probate or children's baptisms. The Gregorius Colbensen Oppedal named as an appraiser in the probate after Lasse Hermundsen of Indre Oppedal, 3 Sep 1764 (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll 8a, No. 82) may be his son (inference). ¶10c
