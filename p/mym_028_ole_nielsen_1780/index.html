About the family

Children's baptisms: 'Ole Nielsen Langlie og Brynille Jonsdatter' had a son Niels, born 5 February 1812 and baptised on Shrove Sunday 1812, with godparents including Peder Nielsen and Agnis Nielsdatter of Langlie (his brother and sister) and Ingebrigt Jonsen and Helge Jonsdatter of Lien; and a daughter Brynhild, born 28 January 1815. He stood godfather to his brother John's son Nils in June 1815 and, as 'Ole Nilsen Langlie', to John's daughter Brynhild in 1818. ¶3

Life story

1801 census: 'Ole Nielssen', 21, one of 'Deres boern' (their children) of Niels Olssen and Guri Olsdatter at Langlie, Selbu, household 01; occupation 'National soldat ved skieloeberne', a national soldier in the ski troops. A younger brother also named Ole was 11. ¶1

Probate: 'Ole Nielsen' is the first-named son and heir in the skifte after his father Niels Olsen Langlie, closed 21 June 1816 (skifteprotokoll 10b, fol. 713), and as 'Sønnen Ole Nielsen' he attended the skifte meeting of 23 February 1816 and signed it (proceedings protocol fol. 102b). A younger brother also named Ole is a separate heir. ¶4

1825 census: residence 0047* Langlie, household 01, person 002 - 'Ole Nilsen', 46, 'Gift soen' (married son) of the widow Guri Olsdatter, 70, who heads the household. With him: his wife Brynhild Johnsdatter, 40, and their children Anne (18), Guri (16), Nils (14), Brynhild (11), Beret (8 1/2), Eli (5 1/2), John (3) and Peder (1). His brother John Nilsen's family lived in the same household. The ages (21 in 1801, 46 in 1825) agree on a birth about 1779-80. ¶2
