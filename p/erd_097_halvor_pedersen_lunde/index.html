Life story

Leikanger ministerialbok 1690-1734: a son 'Samuel Halvorson Lunde' was baptised at Dom. Invocavit 172[5] (p. 323, canvas 138 R) and 'Samuel Halvers. Lunde' was buried about June 1725 (p. 324, canvas 139 R). The 4th son Samuel of the 1733 probate is therefore a later namesake. Confidence left at Probable: the surviving Samuel's baptism (1725-33, canvases 140-170) is still unread. ¶4

Burial: Leikanger ministerialbok 11564, p. 349 (canvas 166 R), 1731, burial column near the end of Trinity season (about Nov 1731): 'Halver Pedersen Lun[de] 67'. The age implies a birth about 1664, which would make him about 64 at Samuel's birth in 1728 (possible with a much younger wife; ages at burial in this book are often rounded up, e.g. 113 for Ole Arnesen Hamre in 1728), so no birth date is set; he died about Nov 1731, which fits his probate of 20 Mar 1733 (opened 1731?). He is the only Halvor at Lunde in the book 1724-1734. ¶5

Note on the Samuel baptisms: the 'Samuel Halvorson Lunde' baptised at Dom. Invocavit on p. 323 (canvas 138 R, heading 'Anno 172[5]' with a correction over the last digit) and the one on p. 342 (1724) fall on consecutive Sundays if both are 1724 (Easter 16 Apr 1724: Quinquagesima 27 Feb, Invocavit 5 Mar). They may be one baptism entered twice, in which case the 'June 1725' burial on p. 324 may be of 1724. Either way a Samuel Halvorsen of Lunde was buried before 1728, so the Dec 1728 child remains the surviving Samuel; the year of p. 323 should be re-checked. No marriage of Halvor (Pedersen) of Lunde was found in 11564 for 1715 to Sept 1718 or Jul 1721 to Jul 1723. ¶7

Probate held 20 March 1733 on the farm Lunde in Tjugum parish after 'Salig Halvor Pedersøn' (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll 3b, fol. 505b-508a). Heirs: the widow Citrona Jørgensdatter and four minor sons, numbered in the division: 1 Peder, 2 Jørgen, 3 Ole, 4 Samuel, each with 61 rd 4 sk paternal inheritance. Guardians: for Peder, Mons[?] Esse[?]; for Jørgen, Kleng[?] Grøneng[?]; for the two youngest (Ole and Samuel), their mother. No ages given. The estate held land in Bøsager and Lagaard, Indre Sogn. Net estate 488 rd 2 ort. ¶1

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

4 on the grade · 1 alternative set aside · 3 open questions

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 4

Probable as father of Samuel Halvorsen: the 4th son Samuel was a minor in 1733 (fits b. ~1724); the 1752 probate of the widow's later son Halvor Jonsen names 'Samuel Halvorsen Lunde' as fourth half-brother with the same mother and brothers; and Samuel named his daughter Zithone/Citrone (b. ~1753) after this grandmother. ¶2a

Upgrade: to Confirmed as father of Samuel Halvorsen. The block named by the last note (the surviving Samuel's baptism) is now read: Leikanger ministerialbok 11564, p. 345 (canvas 160 R), Advent 1728, 'Samuel Halverson Lunde'. With the earlier Samuels of 5 Mar 1724 (p. 342) and 18 Feb 1725 (p. 323, buried June 1725), this Dec 1728 child is the minor 4th son Samuel of Halvor Pedersen's probate of 1733, and the burial 'Halver Pedersen Lunde 67' (1731) ties the name Halvor Pedersen to Lunde. The 1752 probate names 'Samuel Halvorsen Lunde' as half-brother, with the widow Sitron as mother, and Samuel named his daughter Zithone after her. Remaining caveat: the 1801 census age of Samuel at Balholmen (77) points to 1724, about four years early. ¶6

Audit (1 Nov 2026): downgraded from Confirmed to Probable. The records read prove that Halvor Pedersen of Lunde had a minor 4th son Samuel in 1733 (probate) and that a Samuel Halvorsen Lunde was baptised in Dec 1728. They do not tie that Samuel to the tree's Samuel Halvorsen of Balholmen (see his page). That identification still rests on the patronymic, the daughter named Zithone, an online tree (kgroenha.net) and Laberg's bygdebok, and his 1801 age of 77 points to 1724, four years before the surviving Lunde Samuel's baptism. The Dec 1728 baptism answers the question the earlier note asked (which Samuel survived) but not the link to Balholmen. ¶8a

Re-proof attempt (overnight 1-2 Nov 2026), no change: still Probable. Looked for a record joining Samuel Halvorsen Lunde to Samuel of Balholmen. The Leikanger church books for 1735-1791 are not indexed (person search for Samuel Halvors* 1745-1810 finds only the 1801 census at Balholmen; father search finds no baptism). Ytre Sogn probate register: no probate after Samuel, after his mother Sitron Jørgensdatter or after a Lunde brother is indexed. The Balholmen probate after Ole Samuelsen, 18 Nov 1778 (9b p. 1000, view 24167/503, scan read) is a different family: his heirs are sisters Kari Samuelsdatter (married... Ellingsen Hove) and Anna Samuelsdatter, 35, so their father was probably Samuel Nilsen of Balholmen (probate 1750-56), not this Samuel. ¶9a

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Not the son of Peder Ellingsen, Lysne under Lunde: Peder Ellingsen's probate of 30 Jul 1731 (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll 3b, fol. 480a) says he left no legitimate issue ('ægte Livs arvinger') and names as heir his brother Ingebrigt Ellingsen, of age; the widow was Aase Andersdatter. Halvor was alive in 1731 (his own probate is 1733), so he would have been heir had he been Peder's son. Halvor's father is still unknown. ¶3
