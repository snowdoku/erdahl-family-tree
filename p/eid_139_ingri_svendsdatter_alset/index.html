About the family

Parentage (Probable): at the probate after Erik Svendsen Uglem, 20 Jun 1741, her husband Ole Sivertsen Alset was guardian of two of the children as 'fasterens mann' - their father's sister's husband - so she was Erik Svendsen's sister and a daughter of Svend (Siund) Eriksen of Uglem (card 77804 c2055; 1701 manntall Uglem). This makes her the aunt of Torsten Eriksen Uglem, another direct ancestor: a pedigree collapse. Probable rather than Confirmed because her own baptism is not found and the relationship is inferred from the guardian's title. ¶3

Life story

Mother added from the bygdebok (Selbu i fortid og nåtid vol. 4, Uglan bruk 49, canvas 26): Svend Eriksen (1691-1722 user, b. ca. 1650, d. 1723) 'G. m. Ragnhild [...], f. ca. 1659, d. 1742', children Erik ca. 1688, Pål ca. 1693 (m. Ingeborg [...] Berge), Ola ca. 1697, Ingrid 1701 (m. Ola [...] Alset). Probable only (bygdebok). ¶9

Protocol wording checked: in the skifte after Erich Svendsen Uglen, 20 Jun 1741 (24752 fol. 1049, view 312 right), her husband appears as 'Børnenes Fastersmand Ole Siversen', guardian for Svend and Marit Erichsen. So the card's 'fasterens mann' is the protocol's own word. Her link to her father Svend Eriksen Uglem still rests on that relationship word plus her patronymic and stays as already graded. ¶8

1762 extra tax list: wife of Ole Siursen at Alset 732, Selbu (landlord Conferentz Raad Schiøller). The head cell reads 'Ole Siursen, Ingri Svendsdatter' (zoom crop; the S of the patronymic is the clerk's looped capital and 'Svend' is a fair but not certain reading). Their children over 12: six sons, Jon (married to Brynild Pedersdatter), Siur, Svend, Povel, Ole and Erik[?], and a daughter Gjertrud. ¶1

The probate names no brother or sister of hers, so it does not test her Probable father Svend Eriksen Uglem. The lensmann Ole Thomassen and the appraiser Ole Ingebrigtsen Kilstad took part. The births it implies are Jon about 1735, Ole about 1742, Erik about 1745 and Svend's daughter Ingri about 1769. ¶6

Widow in her husband Ole Syvertsen's probate at Alset, 4 Feb 1768 (24756 fol. 92a), with six children: Syvert, Svend, Joen, Ole, Erich and Giertrud (23). Her guardian was Anders Syvertsen Lang[set?], whose relationship to her is not stated. No relative of hers is named, so the record does not help with her mother. ¶7

2 on the grade · 2 open questions

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Why Probable as mother of Siur Olsen Alset (see his page): she is Ole's wife in 1762 and Siur is listed among the couple's sons; a son Svend fits naming after her father (inference). The risk is that she is a second wife: Siur was born about 1729-30 and 'Ole Alsets qvinde' was churched in 1730 without a name. ¶2a

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed (as the mother of Siur Olsen Alset). Her probate, Skifteprotokoll 7 (24758) fol. 477b, reads: 'Anno 1777 den 12te Januarii blev paa gaarden Aleseth i Selboe... forretaget Registering og Vurdering... efter den sammesteds ved døden afgangne Enke Ingrie Svends Datter, som til Arvinger haver efterladt sig følgende Børn, nemlig: 1. Sønnen Siver Olsen gift og boende her paa gaarden Aleseth, 2. Sønnen Joen Olsen [...] Aleseth 42 aar gammel, 3. Sønnen Ole Olsen 35 aar gammel, 4. Sønnen Erich Olsen 32 aar gammel, 5. Sønnen Svend Olsen [Rotnen?], som er død men haver efterlat sig en Datter ved Navn Ingrie Svendsdatter 8 aar gammel, 6. Datteren Giertrud Olsdatter, som er gift med Joen Olsen Lang[lie?]'. Siver, the eldest and the holder of Alset, is Siur (Sivert) Olsen (b. about 1729). This removes the earlier worry that she was a second wife and not Siur's mother. ¶5

Open questions and the next record to read 2

Would settle it: a probate after Ole Siversen Alset or Ingri, or a churching that names the wife (1730-37). ¶2b

Biography gaps: her birth date, birth place and baptism are not found, since 16310 (1699-1737, read in full) has no baptism of her and the 1701 manntall lists men only; her marriage to Ole Alset is undated. Her death and burial are not found after the 1762 list and no burial search is noted; a churching naming the wife in 1730-37 or a probate after Ole Alset would date the marriage and show whether she survived him. Her confirmation is not recorded, and her parentage depends on Svend Eriksen Uglem's probate (77804 Uglem cards 2039-2073, searched; Stjor- og Verdal protocols 24749-24753 unread). ¶4
