About the family

Named as Ole Tostensen's father in Ole's 1835 marriage. Also father of Birgit Tostensdatter (b. about 1804 at Medgaarden in Sata; married Hermand Aslesen of Helling 1832). ¶2

His patronymic shows his father was named Asle. In 1756 two men named Asle lived at a Medgaarden in Ål: Asle Olsen (a godparent, Apr 1756) and Asle Jakobsen (father of Ragnild, Sep 1756). A man named Asle at Medgaarden also buried a child in 1749. Tosten's own baptism was not found in the scanned Ål baptisms for 1755-1756, so his father is not identified and no parent is recorded here. ¶3

Parentage (Probable, strong): Tosten is identified with the 1758 baptism because (1) it is the only Tosten son of any man named Asle baptised in Ål from 1755 to Dec 1760 or from Jan 1762 to May 1763 (every entry read); (2) his unmarried brother Ole Aslesen, aged 38 in the 1801 census, matches exactly Ole son of the same Asle Knudsen of Medgaardehuus, baptised 10 Oct 1762, and the only other Ole son of an Asle in 1762 was the son of Asle Olsen of Mandshuus, who had no Tosten; (3) the family lived on Medgaarden land, where Tosten was later the farmer; (4) Tosten bears the name of his maternal grandfather, a Tosten of Sando, and the Sando Tostensens (his mother's brothers) stood godparents to him and to his brothers Knud (1757) and Ole (1762). His age, 44 in Feb 1801 and about 86 at his 1841 burial, implies a birth in about 1755-1757, one to three years earlier than the baptism; such drift is common in the 1801 census and in old people's burial ages. Not proved because no record states that the farmer Tosten Aslesen of Medgaarden is the child of 1758, the 1758 entry does not name the mother, and Ål baptisms for 1754 and 1761 were not read. ¶6a

Parentage: the confirmation supports the Probable link to the 1758 baptism. He is the only Tosten Aslesen confirmed in Ål in 1775-1778 (every list read), he was at Medgaarden, the farm he held in 1801, and his age at confirmation (18 years 7 months if born in Oct 1758) is typical. It gives only his patronymic, so it does not name his parents, and the link stays Probable. ¶9a

Parents (Confirmed): confidence of his links to Asle Knudsen (see his page) and Kari Tostensdatter (see her page) raised from Probable to Confirmed by the 1773 probate protocol and the index cards of 1773 and 1785. The protocol of 12 Oct 1773 (skifteprotokoll 19, fol. 651a) names 'Søn Tosten Aslesen 15 aar' (son Tosten Aslesen, 15 years) among the children of the late Asle Knudsen of Medgaarden and his widow Karie Tostensdatter, and the cards name Tosten as their son in 1773 (aged 15) and as Tosten Aslesen in 1785 (aged 26). Both ages fit his baptism of 22 Oct 1758, and the farm is Medgaarden, which he held in 1801. The 1773 protocol entry (prot. 19 fol. 651a) has been read and agrees with the card; the 1785 entry (prot. 20b fol. 674a) has not been read. ¶12

Siblings: the probates add two sisters, Rangdie (Ragndi), aged 13 in 1773 (born about 1760), married to Lars Tollefsen Opsata[?] by 1785, and Bergit, aged 8 in 1773 and 20 in 1785 (born about 1765), unmarried in 1785. ¶13

Life story

Birth date: changed from 'ABT 1756' (an estimate from his 1801 census age) to before 22 Oct 1758, his baptism date, on the Probable identification above. This supersedes the earlier note that his father was not identified and that his baptism was not found; the earlier search covered only 1755-1756. ¶7

Baptism: on 22 Oct 1758 in Ål, 'Asle Knudsøn Medgaardehuus S: Tosten' (Asle Knudsen of Medgaardehuus, son Tosten) was baptised. Godparents (faddere): Ole Kiberjord[?], Halvor Tostensen Sando, Tosten Tostensen of the same place (Sando), Bergith Bjørnsdatter [Kleiven?] and Haldis[?] Rasmusdatter [Torbu?]. His mother, not named in the entry, was churched (introduced) on 26 Nov 1758. *Medgaardehuus* means the house (cottar place, *husmannsplass*) of Medgaarden. An earlier reading of the father's patronymic as 'Gundersøn' was a misreading; a re-read of the scan at higher resolution shows 'Knudsøn', the same letterforms as the name Knud elsewhere on the page. ¶5

Probate of his father: the probate index card for Medgaarden in Ål records the skifte (estate division) of 12 Oct 1773 after Asle Knudsen, deceased, and his widow Karie Tostensdatter (Skifteprotokoll 19, fol. 651a). The children were Knud 17, Tosten 15, Ole 10, Rangdie 13 and Bergit 8. The widow's brother Tosten Tostensen of Sando and the children's first cousin (søskendebarn) Knud Olsen of Dybsjorde took part. The gross estate was 617 riksdaler 0 ort 4 skilling, including the farm Medgaarden, 6 løbel (a land-tax unit), valued at 350 riksdaler, held by deed (skjøte) of 1756; net 445 riksdaler 2 ort 14 skilling. ¶10

Confirmation: 'Tosten Asleson Medegaarden' (Tosten son of Asle, of Medgaarden) was confirmed at Ål church on Trinity Sunday (25 May) 1777, the third boy on the list. The list gives no ages and no parents. A girl 'Randi Asles d[atter] Medegaarden' was confirmed on the same day, and 'Knud Asles: Medgaarden' was confirmed on Ascension Day 1775; they were probably his sister and his elder brother Knud (bapt. 16 Jan 1757). Ål church book 1771-1806 (source 8545), p. 64. ¶8

Probate of his mother: the index card for the skifte of 18 Feb 1785 at Medgaarden after Kari Tostensdatter, deceased, wife of the late Asle Knudsen (whose skifte it cites as 12 Oct 1773), lists the children Knud Aslesen, of age; Tosten Aslesen, 26; Ole Aslesen, 23; Ragndie Aslesdatter, married to Lars Tollefsen Opsata[?]; and Bergit Aslesdatter, 20, unmarried (Skifteprotokoll 20b, fol. 674a). Medgaarden, 3 løbel, was valued at 175 riksdaler. ¶11

Married Birgit Olsdatter at Ål in 1789. The church book (1771-1806, p. 170, list of marriages for 1789) reads 'Tosten Aslesen Medgaarden og Pigen Birgith Olsd.'; the index misreads him as 'Tosten Ellevsen'. ¶4

Farmer (gaardmand) at Medgaarden, Ål. In the 1801 census he is 44 and in his first marriage with Birgit Olsdatter (33). Children then: Kari (10), Sidsil (7), Guri (5), Asle (3); his unmarried brother Ole Aslesen (38) lived with them. Still at Medgaarden in the 1816 silver-tax lists. ¶1
