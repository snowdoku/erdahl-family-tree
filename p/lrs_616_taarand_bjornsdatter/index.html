About the family

Widow of Halvor Torgiersen (see his page) of Nordre Grimstvedt, Nissedal. His probate of 17 May 1756 divides the estate 'Mellem hans efterlevende Enke Taarand Biørnsdatter og deris i Ægteskab tilsammen avlede Børn', naming Gunder Halvorsen (of age), Joen Halvorsen (22), Torgiei Halvorsen (13) and Margith Halvorsdatter (16). So she is the mother of Joen Halvorsen Grimstvedt (see his page); see the confidence note on Halvor for the identity of the son and the age caveat. Vest-Telemark sorenskriveri skifteprotokoll 1752-1759 (source 25673), fol. 300a, view 304R. ¶1

Named as mother: panteregister I 2 (source 10916, p. 802, view 141 left), Nordre Grimstvedt: Joen Halvorsen's 'Forligs Contract med sin Moder Taarand Biørnsdatter', dated 4 Mar, tingl. 5 Mar 1762 (No 340), about the shares that fell at the skifte on the farm of 17 May 1756. A second record that calls her the mother of Joen Halvorsen (see his page); it agrees with the 1756 probate. She was alive in March 1762. ¶3

Life story

Earlier marriage: the same probate names her first husband Gunder Svendsen and gives shares to her children by him (the eldest son Svend Gundersen and daughters), so she was twice married (fol. 300b-301a, read at overview scale only). The probate also records an agreement for her keep at Grimstvedt by her son Gunder Halvorsen. ¶2
