About the family

Second wife of Anders Larsen of Almoen, married 9 July 1792; the entry calls her 'Marie Søfrensdatter, Pige'. The forlovere were Eric Frigaard and Hans Boraasen. ¶1

Life story

She was about 31 and he about 59. Four children followed: Anne (1793), Søren (1795, who died that August aged a few weeks and was named for her father), Marit (1796) and Martha (1799). In the 1801 census she is 36, mistress of the farm, with three small daughters, while her husband's grown sons by his first marriage kept cottages on the same land. ¶2

Still at Almoen in April 1799 at the baptism of her last child. She was widowed in December 1807. ¶3
