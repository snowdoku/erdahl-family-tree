About the family

Parents found: the probate after Erik Svendsen Uglen, 20 Jun 1741, lists 'Tosten Eriksen, 8 år', with his mother Guru Torstensdatter (widow) and siblings Svend 10, Ragnild 17, Brynild 13, Anne 5, Marit 2 (card 77804 c2055; read by the orchestrator). This is Torsten Eriksen of Uglem who married Kari Reinholtsdatter in 1765 and named his first son Erik (1766); his brother Svend is the 'Sven Erichsen Uglem' who died in 1800 aged 70. He was named for his mother's father, a Torsten. ¶4a

Life story

A soldier ('Sold.') at his marriage on the 2nd Sunday after Trinity, 16 June 1765, to Kari Reinholtsdatter (Selbu ministerialbok 16311, canvas 18 left). Of Uglum (Uglem) at the baptisms of his sons Erich (13 Jul 1766) and Olle (5 Jun 1770). A son Reinal/Reinholdt (b. ~1771) lived at Slinden in 1801. Father of Erik Torstensen (Probable). His own baptism, parents and death are not yet searched; his patronymic points to a father Erik. ¶1

1701 manntall leads at Uglem (canvases 117-118): Ole Jonsen 28 with sons Jon 6 and Erich 2 (b. c.1699); Siund Erichsen 52 with son Erich Siundsen 13; servant Erich Olsen 17. Any of these Eriks could be his father (Torsten b. before 1745; his own son Erik); none is proved. A 'Sven Erichsen Uglem' died 1800 aged 70 (b. c.1730), possibly his brother. ¶2

Baptism 5 Jun 1770: son Olle, parents Torsten Eriksen Uglem and Karen Reinholdsdatter (16311 c39 L) - confirms the couple. 'Ole Eriksen Uglem', buried 14 Jul 1771 aged 39 (b. c.1732; c45 R), is probably his brother. ¶3

Burial, 1st Sunday after Trinity (18 Jun) 1797: 'Kastet Jord paa og Graffæstet: Manden, Torsten Erichsen Uglem, død d: 11te Junii, gl: 64 Aar, og 3 Maaneder' - died 11 Jun 1797 aged 64 years 3 months, so born about March 1733 (16311 canvas 210 left). This fits 'Tosten Eriksen, 8 år' in his father's probate of June 1741. He was alive as a sponsor in 1794-97 (c170, c192, c208). Sons named in the 1790s: Erich Torstensen Uglem (m. Jun 1792 Mali Christensdatter Lien), Reinholdt and Ole Torstensen Slinden (name-based). ¶5

Land record: the Selbu panteregister 22743 (image 72, under T) has 'Torsten Erichsen med flere arvinger udlagt Uglen paa Skifte efter Faderen Erich Svendsen', pantebok folio 361 (series A-1, c.1741). It agrees with the 1741 Uglem probate ('Tosten Eriksen 8 år') that his father was Erik Svendsen; it says nothing about his own children, so his graded link (Probable, on his link to his child in the tree) is unchanged. ¶6

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade (regrade 3): Probable to Confirmed as father of Erik Torstensen (see his page); graded on his link to Erik Torstensen. Naming record: the Selbu baptism of 13 Jul 1766 (7th Sunday after Trinity; ministerialbok 16311, canvas 20 left and re-read by the orchestrator): 'Döbt (1) Erich. Fædr: Torsten Erichs. Uglum, Kari Reinholts D.' Continuity, all on Uglem: (1) 'Erich Torstens. Uglum' among the confirmands of Quasimodogeniti, 27 Apr 1783 (16311 c110 R), 17 years after the baptism; (2) the introduction of 'Erich Torstensen's wife af Uglem' in Apr 1796 and the baptism of Brynil, 5 Mar 1796, to 'Erik Torstensen Uglum og Magli Christens d:' (13 years later); (3) the 1801 census of Uglan, where Erik Torstensen, 35 (b. about 1766), farms with 'Ole Torstensen 31, i fællesskab med husbonden' - the brother Olle baptised 5 Jun 1770 to 'Tosten Erichs. Ugl., Karen Reinholdsd.' (c39 L), who was buried in 1811 aged 41. Torsten himself stayed at Uglem from 1765 until his burial there on 18 Jun 1797, a sponsor in 1794-97. No other Torsten is known at Uglem who could father an Erik, and the rival hypothesis (Torsten Olsen Slinden) was withdrawn on dates. ¶7a
