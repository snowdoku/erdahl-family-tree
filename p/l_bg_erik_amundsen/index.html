Life story

1758 list: an 'Erich Amundsen', 26 (born about 1732), is listed in the servants column of the household of Hans Rasmussen, 58, and Borgele Halvorsdatter, 40, on the farm Hebnes in the Ferkingstad annex of Skudenes parish (1758 soul register, source 4501, p. 18). He is the only Erik Amundsen in the part of the list read (views 1-13: Skudenes main parish, Åkra and Ferkingstad annexes and the start of Bokn, sheet pages 1-25, folios 87-99), and the age fits our Erik (70 in 1801, born about 1731), so this is very probably him as an unmarried farmhand. A servant entry names no parents. Amundsdatters in the same list who could be sisters (unproved): Marte Amundsdatter, 36, wife of Ole Olsen at Ferkingstad (p. 15); Anne Amundsdatter, 15, servant at Langager (p. 17). In 1758 his later wife Olene Pedersdatter, 13, was living at Randvoldjord with Peder Knudsen (p. 13). ¶2

His own parents are still unknown. Skudenes church books before 1842 burned; the surviving 1770-1842 book (source 1653) is only a partial reconstruction ('P.g.a. brann... ufullstendig'), and Skudenes confirmations survive only from 1842, so no confirmation entry exists for him or his children. Searched without result: Karmsund skifteprotokoll A1 1793-1800 (source 23911), front register (letters E, H, I/J, views 4-5), and the back register of Karmsund skifteprotokoll A3 1804-1810 (source 23913, views 576-583, fol. 569-576); see the dossier for which skipreider those registers cover. ¶5

Bygdebok: Bygdebok for Karmøy: Åkra (1980), farm 21 Heiå (= Randvoldjord), bnr. 4, canvas 178 (rebuilt from nb.no word boxes): '1. 1765-1795: Erik [...] ca. 1731. G.m. [Oline] Pedersdtr., ca. 1745, dotter [av] Peder [og] Rannveig [...] bnr. 15. Born: a. [name not recovered], ca. 1772, [...] Vik [i] Skudenes; b. Peder, ca. 1772, [neste] brukar; c. Knut, ca. 1781, [tenar] hjå [...] Vik [i] 1801; d. Rannveig, ca. 1784, [...] Åkra. Erik fekk ta over halve [svigerfaren] sitt bruk [på] 4 spann ved [bygsel] av 2. [...] 1765.' User no. 2 of bnr. 4 (1795-1841) was Peder Eriksen, ca. 1772, d. 1820. The book gives no origin or parents for Erik (the gap after his first name holds about one word, presumably the patronymic). The Skudenes og Skudeneshavn volume (nb.no ddcac229fdbf08963d59a2e92b5cf1c7) has no 'Amundsen' in its farm chapters for Langåker. ¶6

The Åkra bygdebok (nb.no ce2b9f78ad841d2fc96d5f827749c503, canvas 178) has the word box 'Omundsen,' on the line of Heiå bnr. 4 user no. 1 ('1765-1795: Erik Omundsen, ca. 1731'), so the book gives his patronymic as Omundsen and no origin. The 1762 ekstraskatt for Skudenes (source 49646, view 606) lists an 'Erich Ommundsen' as a servant (tjenestekarl column) in the household of Torchild Hansen on farm 16 'Tiosvold' (Tjøsvoll, Åkra). Unmarried, at the right age, and three years before our Erik married into Randvoldjord, so very probably him (he was a servant at Hebnes in 1758). A servant entry names no parents; his origin is still open. ¶7a

Randvoldjord, Skudenes. In the 1801 census he is 70 and retired ('nyder pension'), with wife Olene Pedersdatter, 54, and daughter Randvej Karine, 17. ¶1

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed (upgrade sweep D), as father of Ranveig Karine Eriksdatter. Two records name the relationship. (1) The 1801 census of Skudenes (index, relationship words read) lists at Randvoldjord the household of Erich Amundsen (70, huusbonde, 'begge 1 gang', nyder pension), Olene Pedersdatter (54, 'hans kone') and Randvej Karine Erichsdatter (17, 'Datter'). (2) The c.1842 Åkra household register (Skudenes church book 1770-1842, source 1653, as read by an earlier wave) enters Ranveig, wife of Hans Olsen, as daughter of gaardmand Erik Randvoldsjord and Oline. The 1801 census gives the father's full name and the c.1842 register ties the tree's Ranveig (wife of Hans Olsen) to the same couple at the same farm. His wife Olene's identity is already Confirmed by the 1764 probate after her father Peder Knudsen of Randvoldjord. ¶3

Upgrade: Confirmed as the father of Ranveig Karine Eriksdatter (see her page) by two primary records. (1) 1801 census, Skudenes, district 003, Randvoldjord, household 05: 022 'Erich Amundsen', 70, Huusbonde, 'Begge 1 gang', 'Nyder pension'; 023 'Olene Pedersdatter', 54, 'Hans kone'; 024 'Randvej Karine Erichsdatter', 17, family position 'Datter'. The census relationship word makes her the daughter of the householder Erich Amundsen. (2) The c.1840-42 Skudenes household register (source 1653, Akre, printed p. 268, IIIF canvas 273): 'Ranveg Karine Eriks.datter', wife of husmann Hans Olsen, parents 'Gaardm. Erik Randvaldsjord og h. Olene'. The register names her father as the farmer Erik of Randvoldjord with wife Olene, and the census puts Erich Amundsen with wife Olene Pedersdatter and their daughter Randvej Karine on that farm. The two records agree on the farm, the wife's name and the daughter's name. ¶4

Searches that found nothing 1

Negative: no estate of an Omund (Amund) at Tjøsvoll found. The register 23888 lists at 'Tiosvold' only Guri Torkelsdatter (22 Jan 1766; heirs incl. Torchel Hansen), and none in the 1763 or 1765 Stangeland blocks; the 1774-88 card index has three Tjøsvold cards (925b Boel Andersdatter g. Fredrik Jonsen 1784; 926a Ole Gregoriussen and Sidsel Torkelsdatter 1786; 926b Joseph Johannsen 1786), none an Omund. His master in 1762, Torkild Hansen of Tjøsvoll, belongs to that Guri Torkelsdatter's family, not his own. Origin still open; the 1757-62 part of 23888 (views ~20-62, not reread here) and the Skudenes 1758 sjeleregister (servant at Hebnes) are the remaining leads. ¶8
