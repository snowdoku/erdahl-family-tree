About the family

Father of Niels Thorsen Buchen (Probable, not proved): Niels's 1777 baptism names no parents, only 'Niels Torsen Buchen'. This is the only marriage of a Tore/Thor of Buchen in Hjelmeland 1763-1777 (all marriages read). Godparents Peder, Reier and Marthe Halsnøe at Niels's baptism point to the bride's home farm, Halsnøy. A 1768 marriage fits Siri Thorsdatter of Buchen (b. c.1770) as the eldest child. A 'Tore Buchen' was godfather in Hjelmeland in late 1777. Not at Buchen in the 1801 census (probably dead or moved). ¶2

Probable daughter Siri: 'Siri Thors Datter fød i L[?] B[u/ö]cken[?]' baptised 1 Jan 1770 (Festo Circumcisionis), Hjelmeland, 2nd entry of 1770. Godparents include Niels Halsnøe and Anna Buchen. Born 18 months after the June 1768 marriage. Parents not named; the Halsnøe godparent supports the link. Confidence unchanged. ¶5

Life story

1762 extra tax: the Ryfylke ekstraskatt 1762 lists at Lille Buchen, Fister, 'Thore Thorsen, Opsidder' with 'Siri Tostensdatter, Hustr' and 'Marthe Sevathsdatter, til huuse'; no children over 12. So he held Lille Buchen by 1762 and was then married to Siri Tostensdatter, not to Anna Pedersdatter. Siri Tostensdatter's own probate is the 'Bukken Lille i Sjernerø' entry of 20 May 1767 in D 6 (DA 23890) fol. 59b, which read as having 'her husband and young Larsen/Larsdatter children' as heirs: the Larsen children were evidently hers by an earlier husband Lars, and the surviving husband was Thore. This makes Anna Pedersdatter (m. 26 Jun 1768) his second wife and Kari Erichsdatter (by 1791) his third, and explains Samuel Thorsen (of age in 1791, not a child of Anna in 1786-87) as perhaps a son of the first marriage. re-read D 6 fol. 59b at full resolution (view 89): 'Anno 1767 den 20de May' registration on Lille Buchen in Stiernerøe Schibrede after the late woman Siri Tostensdatter, between her surviving husband and the children, who carry Larsen/Larsdatter patronymics (among them a Tosten, about 10, and an Anne); the husband's name in lines 5-6 could not be read with certainty, and no Thorsen child is listed. ¶19a

Marriage (scan read): Hjelmeland marriages 1768, p. 233, no. 10: 'Thore Thorsen Butten [=Buchen] og Anna Peders Datter Halsnøe blev samme Dag copuleret i Fist[er] Kirk', the same day as no. 9, Dom. 4ta p. Trinit., 26 Jun 1768. No ages, status or sponsors are recorded. The farm is read as Buchen (the clerk's 'ch' looks like 'tt'). ¶1

On 7 Apr 1771 (Quasimodogeniti) Thore Buchen and Anna Buchen stood godparents to 'Peder Thorsen fød i Maaløen[?]' (baptisms no.28, canvas 24 left). As a godfather Thore is unlikely to be this Peder's father, so this is not the Per of Buchen (b. c.1774-76); perhaps a kinsman's child. ¶7

1777 marriage checked: Hjelmeland marriages 1777 no.7 reads in full 'Tosten Børgesen Buchen og Anna Peders Datter blev e[odem] d[ie] copulerede udi Hielm: Kirke' (15 Jun 1777, Dom. 3 p. Trin.). No farm is given for the bride and there is no 'Enke' (widow) note. It does not show that Thore was dead by 1777; nothing ties this Anna to Anna Pedersdatter Halsnøe. No correction made. ¶4

Probates of his wife and father-in-law (scans read): (1) Ryfylke skifteprotokoll D 7b (DA 23892) fol. 457a, Lille Buchen, Sjernerø tinglag, 10 Aug 1786, after Anna Pedersdatter: the heirs are her surviving husband 'Tore Toresen' and their 3 children, Peder Toresen 12, Neels Toresen 8 and Siri Toresdatter 16. (2) Fol. 507a, Halsnøe, 19 Mar 1787, after Peder Nielsen: his daughter 'Anna Peders Datter gift med Thore Thorsen Buchen', dead, left Peder Thorsen 13, Niels Thorsen 9 and Siri Thorsdatter 17, whose guardian is their father Thore Thorsen. These records name him as the father of a Niels Thorsen of Lille Buchen born about 1778 (the 1777 baptism) and show him alive in March 1787, which fits the 1791 burial. His farm was Lille Buchen. ¶13

Probate found and read: Ryfylke skifteprotokoll D 12 (DA 23895), fol. 142a, headed 'Skifte Forretning efter Thore Thorsen Lille Buchen, Stiernerøe Skibrede'. 'Aar 1791 den 2den Augusti' the deputy and two appraisers met on the farm Lille Buchen after the late man Thore Thorsen, between his surviving wife 'Karie Erichsdatter' and his children, 3 sons and a daughter: 1. the eldest son Samuel Torsen, of age; 2. the second son Peder Torsen, 18, guardian Peder [?Halle]; 3. the youngest son 'Nils Torsen', 15, guardian Neels Byre; 4. the daughter Siri Torsdatter, 23, married to Thore Clemensen [?Wignes]. The ages of Peder, Nils and Siri run about two years above those in their mother Anna Pedersdatter's probate of 1786 (12, 8 and 16), which is common, and the same three children are named. Samuel, of age in 1791, was probably a son of an earlier marriage. So Thore married Kari Erichsdatter between Aug 1786 and 1791. The register to this protocol (view 3) is handwritten and was not used by the earlier passes, which looked for this probate in D 8a/8b. ¶15

Mortgage register checked: Ryfylke panteregister II A 2 (DA 23069) has no Buchen entry at the Hjelmeland tings of 8 Jun 1790 (nos. 97-103), 12 Oct 1790 (nos. 139-142), 7 Jun 1791 (nos. 183-192), 6 Jun 1792 (nos. 346-354), 12 Oct 1792 (nos. 423-429) or 4 Jun 1793 (nos. 504-509). No skiftebrev for Buchen was read at a ting in 1791-93, so his estate probably held no freehold land, and the probate itself (if held) would be in a Ryfylke protocol for 1788-1797 that is not scanned (D 8a/8b and D 12 are Karmsund og Hesby). ¶10a

Burial: Hjelmeland burials p.159 (canvas 84 right): 'Thore Buchen blev begraven den 21de Julÿ gl.', age column 60 aar. Year 1791 from the page's opening date '26de Maÿ 91' and the 1791 heading on p.157. Birth c.1731 is estimated from the age. No wife named; he is the only Thore/Tore of Buchen in the burials 1776-1792 (name columns read). Confidence unchanged. ¶6

Court journal checked: Ryfylke tingbok C 5b (DA 39981; view n = urn rg10511109011+n) has the Hjelmeland ting of 12 Oct 1791, the first after his burial (21 Jul 1791), at views 178-180 (fol. 474-476). The list of documents read at that ting (nos. 1-12: Sande, Næsvik, Meland, Tøtland, Hauge, Steensland, Tuntland, Førmeland) has no Buchen entry. (The 1791 June ting is at about view 151; the case pages of the October session were not read word by word.) This closes the gap left, which had read the mortgage register for Jun 1790-Jun 1793 except Oct 1791. Probate cards 1774-1788 (DA 39936) end before his death; no Ryfylke card index for 1788-1800 is scanned. ¶11

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

3 on the grade · 2 alternatives set aside · 4 searches with no result · 6 open questions

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 3

Grade kept Probable on his link to Nils Thorsen, for the same reason as his wife's: the probates prove he was the father of Niels Thorsen of Lille Buchen (b. 1777), but the tree's identification of that Niels with the Madla Niels Thorsen spans the 1796-1816 gap and a move of parish, and is Probable in Niels's own record. His own 1791 probate should be in the Ryfylke protocol after D 7b under Sjernerø skipreide; the card index 39936 ends at 1788. ¶14

Grade kept Probable, graded on his link to Nils Thorsen. The 1791 probate names a son Nils Torsen, 15, of Lille Buchen, the same boy as in 1786 and 1787, but it carries him no further than 1791. The doubt is still the identification of the Buchen Niels with the Madla Niels Thorsen (1796 confirmation, 1816 marriage, later Madla). ¶16a

Grade kept Probable, graded on his link to Nils Thorsen, but the Madla half of the gap is now closed: the 1824-25 probate after Niels Olsen and Mallene Pedersdatter of Øye (Ryfylke D 19, fol. 293b) names their daughter 'Mallene Nielsdatter, gift med Niels Thorsøn Bukken', living under Sunde in Jæren og Dalane, so the Madla husband is the Niels Thorsen of Bukken who married in 1816. The remaining step is the Bukken farm alone: Niels Torsen, 15, son of Thore, Lille Buchen (probate 2 Aug 1791) and confirmed at Buchen in 1796, to Niels Thorsen, bachelor at Buchen, in 1816. No rival Niels Thorsen of Bukken is named in any record read, but 1796-1816 is 20 years with no record naming him, so under the audit-3 rule it stays Probable. Checked without result: Siri Thorsdatter's husband's probate (Lars Andersen, Bukken, D 19 fol. 221b, 6 Apr 1824: widow Siri Thorsdatter Bukken, children Sevend Larsen of age and Anna Larsdatter 20; no brother named) and the 'Peder Thorsen Skorpe' entry in the D 17a/b register (fol. 576b, not read; Skorpe, not Bukken). ¶18a

Alternatives considered and set aside 2

Caution: a 'Thore Thorsen' married Mallene Pedersdatter Wallen in Årdal church on 26 Jun 1763 (farm illegible, not Buchen as far as read); and in 1777 (no. 7) 'Tosten Børgesen Buchen' married an 'Anna Peders Datter' (her farm not read). If she were Thore's widow, Niels (bapt. Nov 1777) would be posthumous, which the 1777 godfather 'Tore Buchen' argues against. ¶3

Rejected father candidate: the probate after Thore Thorsen of Barkve, Idsø skipreide (D 11, DA 23888, fol. 99b, c.1763-64) has only young heirs (Thore Thorsen 3 1/2, Ingeborg 11, Gunnille 5 1/2), so that man was not the father of Thore Thorsen Buchen (b. c.1731). No probate after a Thor/Tore on Buchen appears in the typed registers DA 23971 (1731-54) or 23973 (1757-66). ¶20

Searches that found nothing 4

Probate search: Ryfylke D 8b (DA 23894) is the Karmsund og Hesby protocol, and its skipreide register (views 382-391) has no Hjelmeland section. His probate would be in the Ryfylke sorenskriveri proper, in a volume for 1789-1793 that is not on Digitalarkivet under the probate series (the series lists D 7b 1782-88, D 8a, D 8b, D 12 1786-1800, D 13, D 15). ¶8a

Probate search: no Ryfylke probate protocol for Hjelmeland 1789-1795 is scanned (D 8a 23893 and D 8b 23894 are Karmsund og Hesby). The Ryfylke mortgage register II A 2, 1790-1821 (DA 23069) is a chronological list of documents read at each ting (number, tinglag, date, pantebok folio, document type, farm). At the Hjelmeland ting of 6 June 1792 (s. 8, nos. 346-354, view 9) the farms are Øye, Tøtland, Tuntland, Sande and others, with no Buchen; s. 9 (view 10, nos. 355-370) has none either. ¶9a

Search note: the farm Buchen (Bukken, Lille and Store) lay in Sjernerø skipreide, not Hjelmeland skipreide: the typed register of Ryfylke skifteprotokoll D 6 (DA 23890, 1767-74) files 'Bukken Lille i Sjernerø' (Siri Tostensdatter, 20 May 1767, fol. 59b: heirs her husband and young Larsen/Larsdatter children) and 'Bukken Store' (Ragnille Larsdatter, 20 Oct 1767, fol. 60b: heirs her husband and a dead sister's children). Neither is Thore's family. Earlier passes looked for his 1791 probate under Hjelmeland skipreide; it should be sought under Sjernerø skipreide. The register of D 8a/8b (DA 23894, views 380-391) has sections for Skaare, Augvaldsnes, Hasbye, Aardahl, Jelsø, Idsø, Aschøe and Hausken only, with no Sjernerø, so the 1788-95 volume for Sjernerø is not that one. ¶12

Search note: the loose register 'A' to D 15 that earlier passes thought unscanned is DA 62748 ('Register til Ryfylke Skifteprotokoll nr. 3 (yngre) A 1798-1807 og nr. 4 (yngre) B 1807-1810'), ordered by farm with protocol letter and folio. Its B (Bøe, Bukken), H (Halsnø, Holle) and L (Lille...) pages have no entry for Bukken/Buchen or Halsnøy; Holle has Knud Olsen, Sophia Olsdatter and Ole Olsen only. So no probate after Kari Erichsdatter, Peder, Samuel or Siri Thorsen on Buchen was held 1798-1810, and D 15/D 16 cannot bridge Niels Thorsen to Madla. ¶17

The same list has 'Peder Thorsen, Huusmand' at Store Buchen with 'Ragnille Larsd, Hustr' (her 1767 probate, D 6 fol. 60b, names her husband and a dead sister's children); he is a possible brother of Thore, but nothing states it. Grade unchanged (Probable, graded on his link to Nils Thorsen). ¶19b
