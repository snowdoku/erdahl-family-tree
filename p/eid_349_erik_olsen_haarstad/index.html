Life story

Another child: 'døbt Erich Haarstads B. Nom: Ole', Rogate Sunday (22 May) 1729, Selbu ministerialbok 16310 c116 R; Erich Haarstad's wife was churched on the 1st Sunday after Trinity (19 Jun) 1729 (c117 R, beside the wives of Ole Hoem and Bersvend Fuglem). Ole is not among the children in the 1742 skifte, so he presumably died young. His name fits Erik's father or Ingeborg's father being an Ole. ¶4

(near-miss attempt, not raised; graded on his link to Torger Erikssen Svinaas, unchanged). The register row 'Anne Erichsd.... i Salboe 318' in Stjør- og Verdal skifteprotokoll 10 (24763, register view 2, right page) reads 'Anne Erichsd. Sanden i Salboe'. Fol. 318 was read (view 333, left page, lower half; continued on view 334 left = p. 319): skifte 6 Feb 1792 at Sanden in Selbu after 'afgangne Kone Anne Erichsdatter' between the widower Thorsten Olsen and their children Reinholdt Thorstensen 23, Ole Thorstensen 18 and Guru Thorstensdatter 7. The father acts for his minor daughter Guri. No brother Torger or any Haarstad kin is named. With children aged 7-23 in 1792, this Anne was probably born about 1745-65, too young to be a sister of Torger (b. about 1734). Not the sister sought. The 1785 Solemshagen probate (24761 fol. 405) is still the only candidate, and it has no relationship word. ¶6a

Farmer at Øvre Haarstad (Hastad), Selbu. His estate was settled on 29 March 1742 (Stjør- og Verdal Skifteprotokoll 2C, 24752, fol. 1116b, view 383 left, No. 20): 'efter afgangne Erich Olsen Øvre Haarstad' between his widow Ingeboer Olsdatter and 'deres sammen avlede og i live værende 4 børn', namely '1 Torgier Erichsen 8 aar gl:, 2 Anne Erichsdatter 17 aar gl:, 3 Ingeboer Erichsdatter 14 aar gl: og 4 Marit Erichsdatter 11 aar gl:', all living at home on the farm. He died before that date. Birth before about 1700 is inferred from a daughter aged 17 in 1742. ¶1

The Torger of the 1742 skifte is the 'Erich Haarstads barn Nom: Torger' baptised on the 17th Sunday after Trinity (27 Sep 1733) in Selbu (16310 c130 R), and his wife was churched on the 23rd Sunday after Trinity 1733. Baptism and skifte are on the same farm 8½ years apart, and the skifte age of 8 matches the baptism, so the Haarstad Torger of 1733 and 1742 is one boy. ¶2

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the grade · 1 alternative set aside · 2 open questions

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Graded Probable on his link to Torger Eriksen Svinaas (see his page). The skifte proves he was the father of a Torger Eriksen born 1733 at Øvre Haarstad. That this boy is the Torger Eriksen of Kvello (1767) and Svinaas (1770-1808) is an identification across farms and 25 years, so it can only be Probable. For it: (1) he is the only Torger baptised in Selbu between 7 p.Trin 1729 and 8 Mar 1737 (16310 c118-141), which covers the birth years implied by Torger Svinaas's later ages; (2) Torger Svinaas named his eldest son Erik (26 in Oct 1786, so born about 1760), a daughter Marit (1767, the name of a sister in the skifte), a daughter Ingeborg (about 1779, the name of this Erik's widow) and two sons Ole (1774 and 1775, the name of Ingeborg Olsdatter's father); (3) no rival Torger Eriksen of the right age has been found in Selbu. Against, and not resolved: Torger Svinaas's ages of 70 (1801 census) and 77 (1808 burial) put his birth in 1730-31, two to three years before this baptism; and Selbu baptisms before 7 p.Trin 1729 (16310 c107-117) have not been searched for another Torger. ¶3

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Near-miss attempt, not raised: a sister's probate was looked for in the typed Stjør- og Verdal registers (- reg_*.tsv, also 'Salb'). Selbu hits for the sisters' names: only 'Anne Eriksdatter, [B]ersvend Haldorsens hustru, Solemshagen' (24761 fol. 405) and an unread 'Anne Erichsd.? [?] i Salboe' (24763 fol. 318 [?]). No Ingeborg or Marit Eriksdatter in Selbu. Read: 24761 fol. 405 (view 191), skifte 14 Apr 1785 at Solemshagen after Anne Erichsdatter, between the widower Bersvend Haldorsen and 'deres sammen avlede 7 Børn': Erich Bersvendsen (of age, married), Haldor 18, Mali, Ingeborg (m. Ole Halvorsen), Marith 28, [Gjertrud?] 23 and Marithe 21. The guardians are the father and husbands; Torger is not named. The children's names Erich, Ingeborg and Marit fit Erich Olsen and Ingeborg Olsdatter of Haarstad as her parents, but the probate gives no relationship word linking her to Haarstad or to Torger. Grade unchanged. ¶5a
