About the family

Father of Maria Dorothea Creutz (see her page), wife of Christoph Adam Knodel. Both index rows of her 1759/1760 marriage (Bietigheim 12 Feb 1759, 'Maria Dorothea Treuzen'; Vaihingen 1760, 'Maria Dorothea Creubzen') give the relatives 'Jacob, Christoph Adam'. In bride rows of this index the first relative is the bride's father and the last the groom. ¶1a

The case for this identification 2

Identity and marriage (Probable identification): probably Johann Jacob Kreutz, who married Maria Dorothea Stierlen, daughter of Johann Caspar, at Bietigheim on 13 May 1738 (groom row 7053511 with the single relative 'Maria Dorothea'; bride row 907053511 'Maria Dorothea Stierlens', relatives 'Johann Caspar, Johann Jacob'; image 1184901-00013). He is the only Jacob of this surname indexed at Bietigheim; the Kreutz family there goes back to Martin Kreutz x Anna (children 1656-1672) and Hans Georg Kreutz x Margretha (children 1711-1712); his own baptism and parents were not found (the groom row names no parents). ¶3

Identity: now labelled. V4LM-7HM ('Germany, Births and Baptisms'): Maria Dorothea Creutz, christened Bietigheim 10 Nov 1740, Parents 'Johann Jacob Creutz, Maria Dorothea Stierler', so the father of the 1759 bride is the Johann Jacob Creutz who married Maria Dorothea Stierlen at Bietigheim in 1738 (VCYQ-KKB). Grade unchanged (Confirmed). Read signed in, 2 Nov 2026; labelled index fields, image not seen (partner-only). ¶7
