Life story

The wife of 'Engebret Søreneboe' was churched on 5 Aug 1753, the day their son Peder was baptised (9236 p. 379, canvas 182; the wife is not named there). Graded on her link to Peder (see his page): stays Probable for the same reason as Engebret (30-year step from baptism to the 1783 marriage, and her name comes from the 1763 manntall, not from the 1753 entry). ¶2a

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the grade · 1 alternative set aside · 2 searches with no result · 2 open questions

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Probable mother of Peder Ingebretsen (graded on that link). The 1763 Lesja manntall of the extra tax (Ekstraskatt Gudbrandsdal 1762-66, source 45390, view 287, read on the scan) lists at No. 6 'Søndre Eneboe', self-owner, the couple Engebret Larsøn and Guri Pedersd., with children over 12 Erich, Lars, Marit and Marit (Peder, about 10, was too young to be listed). The link is inferred, not named: Peder Ingebrigtsen married in 1783 as a soldier 'of Søreneboe' and lived there with his family in 1784-1801; Engebret Larsen is the only Engebret on either Eneboe farm in 1763; and Peder's first child (bapt. 1 Jan 1784) was named Gurri. Birth before 1730 is estimated from children over 12 in 1763. ¶1a
