Life story

Brekke herred bygdebok vol. 2 (1944), Engesæter gnr. 55 bnr. 52 (canvas 510, text rebuilt from word boxes): under the user 1694-1733 '[Gullak] Torsteinson' (b. 1662, d. 1742), married 1693 'Siri Iversdtr.' (b. 1667, d. 1737), daughter of Ivar Olson and Agate [..] (of bnr. 66, gap in the rebuild). Children listed: Torstein Gullakson b. 1694, Agate Gullaksdtr. b. 1696 (m. 1727 Johannes Andersson, Tynning bnr. 74), 'Ivar Gullakson' b. 1699 (user of bnr. 52), Ingeborg Gullaksdtr. b. 1704 (m. 1729 soldier Sjur Hansson, Instefjord), Ragnhild Gullaksdtr. b. 1708 (d. 1760, m. 1738 Gjert Larsson, Bergen) and a son [..] Gullakson 1711-1792, unmarried. Canvas 511: 1733-1773 'Ivar Gullakson' (1699-1773) on bnr. 52, married 1732 [Kari Clausdtr.] of Takle (1713-1794), daughter of 'Claus Hermundson' and 'Kari Johannesdtr.'. Canvases 323 and 840 repeat the parents 'Gullak Torsteinson og Siri (Sigrid) Ivarsdtr.' for the daughters Ingeborg and Agate. Dates are ABT (rebuilt text). ¶1

Still no probate after Gullak Tostensen or Siri Iversdatter of Engesætter: the Ytre Sogn probate index searched by domicile 'Enge*' and 'Inge*' (1733-1760) and by forename 'Gull*' (1738-1750) shows only Gullov [To]ensen (Engeset, 1740-43, the other Gullak) and men of other farms. ¶4a

Not the wife of Gullov Toensen of Engesætter (see his page, bnr. 55, probate 22 Aug 1741), whose wife was Kirsti Jonsdatter. ¶3

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Graded on her link to her son Iver Gullacksen: Probable (bygdebok only; capped). The book's Ivar Gullakson b. 1699, of Engesæter bnr. 52, m. Kari Clausdtr. of Takle, d. 1773, is the tree's Iver Gullacksen (probate 12-13 Mar 1774 with widow Kari Clausdatter); and its Gullak Torsteinson b. 1662 with sons Torstein 1694 and Ivar 1699 matches the 1701 census 'Gullach Tostens:' 40 with sons Tosten 7 and Ifver 2 (see his page). Records that would test it: a Gulen burial of 1737 for Siri Iversdatter of Engesætter (Gulen ministerialbok 11441, 1737), a division after her (the book cites 'T.', a tingbok), or the probate after Gullak Tostensen (d. about 1742; not found in the Ytre Sogn probate index under Engeset 1728-1750). Her father Ivar Olson is not linked (name only, from a gap-ridden passage). ¶2
