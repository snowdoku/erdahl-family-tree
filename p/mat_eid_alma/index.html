About the family

Second wife of Peder Conrad Eidem. Daughter of John and Ida (Dahl) Gilbertson; baptised and confirmed at Trinity Lutheran Church, Boyd, Minnesota; graduated Boyd High School 1929; worked at the First State Bank in Boyd and at KDMA radio in Montevideo. ¶1

Collateral note: she brought no Eidem descendants, so this branch of the Lac qui Parle Eidems ends with Peder Conrad. ¶3

Life story

First marriage: 31 October 1935 to Lawrence Oakland, who died in February 1954; two daughters, Irene (Talaasen) and Lorraine (Fokken). Second marriage: 22 October 1960 to Peder C. Eidem of Marietta, who died in 1995. ¶2
