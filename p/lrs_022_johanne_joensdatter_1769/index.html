About the family

Mother (Probable): Susanne Andersdatter (see her page), the widow whom her father Joen Halvorsen Grimstvedt married as a bachelor in Jan 1762 (7981 c125L). In 1801 he was a widower after his first and only marriage, so Susanne is the only possible mother of his children born after 1762. ¶7

Life story

Birth date: changed from 'ABT 1769' (from her 1801 census age) to before 22 Jul 1770, her baptism date, on the Probable identification above. Her mother is unknown. ¶5

Baptism: on the 7th Sunday after Trinity 1770 (22 Jul 1770; Easter was 15 Apr), Pastor Børresen baptised in Nissedal church 'Joen Halvorsen Grimstvedts Daatter Johanne' (Johanne, daughter of Joen Halvorsen of Grimstvedt). Godparents (testes): Kittil Nordboe, Amtjon Grimstvedt, Targe Grimstvedt, Anne Nordboe and Gunild Grimstvedt. The mother is not named. Kviteseid ministerialbok 1754-1773, baptisms 1770, p. 126. ¶3

Death: 'Johanne Johnsdat', aged 76 (b. about 1770), died 13 Nov and was buried 2 Dec 1846 in Nissedal (Nissedal ministerialbok 1814-1860, p. 123, no. 24; index pg00000006405339). No farm is given; the age matches her baptism of 22 Jul 1770 and her 1801 census age of 32 within a year, and she is the only Johanne Jonsdatter born 1766-1773 in the Nissedal burials. ¶9

Marriage (dates recomputed): betrothed on the 5th Sunday in Lent, 10 Apr 1791, at Kirkenes in Nissedal, as 'Pigen Johanne Joensd: Grimstvedt' (the maiden Johanne Joensdatter of Grimstvedt), to the bachelor Ole Olsen of Ibrække; cautioners Targie Halvorsen Grimstvedt and Hølje Tovsen Ibrække; married on Whit Monday, 13 Jun 1791, in Nissedal church. Dates computed from Easter 1791 = 24 Apr; the earlier card dates of 20 Mar and 23 May 1791 used a wrong Easter (3 Apr) and are superseded. Kviteseid ministerialbok 1787-1799 (source 7983), p. 188. ¶6

1801 census: Johanne Joensdatter, 32, wife of Ole Olsen at Ibræke, Nissedal (then Kviteseid prestegjeld), both in their first marriage, with children Ingebor 8, Ole 6 and Joen 3. ¶1

Baptism of son: mother of Torkild, baptised 9 Aug 1801 in Nissedal; she was churched on the 13th Sunday after Trinity (30 Aug 1801). Her link to Torkild Olsen is Probable for the same reasons as her husband's (see Ole Olsen). ¶2

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: Confirmed as the mother of Torkild Olsen (see his page). The Nissedal confirmation list for 1822 (ministerialbok 1814-1845, source 8019, p. 246, canvas 160, boys no. 12) reads 'Torkild Ibrek | Ole og Johanne Ibrek | døbt 1801 d. 10 Sønd. e. Trin.', with the verdict 'ringe i Kundskab men god i Dyd og Forhold' (poor in knowledge but good in conduct) and vaccination on 4 Mar 1806 by A. Hansen. This names his parents as Ole and Johanne of Ibrække and his baptism as the one of the 10th Sunday after Trinity 1801 ('Ole Olsen Ibrækes Søn Torkild', 9 Aug 1801). A year later 'Torkeld Olsen Ibrække', 22, married Aasild Gunleiksdatter of Midbø, so name, farm and age join the confirmand to the groom. Her own identification with the 1770 baptism of Johanne daughter of Joen Halvorsen Grimstvedt is unchanged: that link is carried by her parents' records and her 1791 betrothal as Johanne Joensdatter of Grimstvedt. ¶8

The case for this identification 1

Identification (Probable): she is the only Johanne with a father named Joen among the Nissedal baptisms read from Oct 1767 to Sep 1770 (canvases 48-55; part of canvas 56 is water-damaged and canvas 57 was not read). Her 1801 census age of 32 points to a birth in 1768/69, not 1770; a gap of a year or two is common in this census but is not proof. ¶4a

Searches that found nothing 1

Confirmation search: not in the Nissedal or Treungen confirmation lists of 1787 (7983 canvas 258 left, 15 children) or 1788 (canvas 260 left, 9 children). These Kviteseid lists give name, farm and age only, no parents. Her confirmation should be in 1785-86 (7982) or 1789 (7983 canvas 261). ¶10

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Would confirm: her confirmation (about 1785-1787) or her marriage to Ole Olsen (before 1793) in the Kviteseid registers, or a probate after Joen Halvorsen naming her. ¶4b
