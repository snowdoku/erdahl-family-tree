About the family

Son of George and Phebe Hicken according to the compiled Island Register genealogies (Hicken and Reynolds pages), born in England in March 1809. No baptism has been found; the family was then between Little Cowarne, Herefordshire (1806) and Doddenham, Worcestershire (1814). He named his eldest surviving son George Benjamin (born 1834), following the family pattern. ¶1

Life story

Marriage: married Hannah Reynolds (Rennels), born about 1812 in British North America, daughter of John Reynolds and Elizabeth Wolfe, at Gaspereaux, Prince Edward Island, on 3 September 1831 (compiled genealogy). Their sons George Benjamin and Thomas were baptised together on 19 September 1837 at Three Rivers (PEI baptism index). ¶2

1841 census of Prince Edward Island, Lot 61, as summarised in the Island Register Reynolds genealogy: Thomas, a carpenter, born in England, with Hannah and four children under sixteen; Church of England; living on land in the twentieth year of a 999-year lease - that is, leased from about 1821-22, when Thomas was a boy, which dates the family's settlement on Lot 61. ¶3

1841 census (image read): 'Thomas Hicken', carpenter, Lot 61 (1841 census of PEI, Kings County District 15, image 515), headed a household of six. It comprised one man and one woman aged 16-45, three boys and one girl under 16, all entered as Church of England. He held 50 acres on a 999-year lease with 20 years expired (so from about 1821), at 1s 2d an acre, a rent of 2 pounds 18s 4d (image 516). This confirms the Island Register summary. The three boys fit John (about 1832), George (about 1834) and Thomas (about 1837) of the 1850 Bath, Maine household; the girl may be one of its two unidentified members. His trade fits the shipwright or ship carpenter of the Newburyport directories as reported by the compiled genealogy. Together these strengthen the identification of the Lot 61 Thomas with the Bath and Newburyport Thomas, which stays Probable until his death record is read. ¶5

Later life: the family moved to Newburyport, Massachusetts, by about 1854; the Newburyport Directory lists Thomas as a shipwright at 251 Merrimac Street (1854) and a ship carpenter at 48 Warren Street (1858). He died at Newburyport on 7 December 1858 (compiled genealogy). His Massachusetts death registration is not in the online index; the original would give his birthplace and his parents' names. His widow Hannah stayed at 48 Warren Street until about 1869; sons John J. (c.1832-1889) and Lemuel (c.1845-1902) died at Newburyport, both recorded as children of Thomas and Hannah. ¶4

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed on his link to his father George Hicken (see his page): the 1847 will of George Hicken of Lot 61 recites that 50 acres on the east side of his leased farm had been 'sublet by me to my son Thomas under Lease bearing date the first day of March 1834' (typescript, PEI Genealogies file, image 97). Thomas Hicken, carpenter, headed a Lot 61 household in 1841, the only Thomas Hicken head there. His link onward to the Bath (1850) / Newburyport Thomas stays as graded there. ¶7

Searches that found nothing 1

Death record search (signed-in FamilySearch, 1 Nov 2026; negative): no death registration for Thomas Hicken was found for Newburyport in 1858 or 1859. (1) The Newburyport city clerk's register (DGS 007009577, 'Deaths 1833-1843; and, births, marriages, and deaths, 1844-1871') carries deaths only to December 1857: image 255 = Jan-Apr 1857 (p. 109), image 257 = Dec 1857 (p. 113), and images 258-261 are blank or end-of-roll. (2) In the state copy, 'Massachusetts. Death Records 1858' (Massachusetts, State Vital Records 1638-1927), the City of Newburyport returns are on images 176-181 of 738 (image 179 holds William C. Williams; image 181 holds only five late entries). None of the indexed names on those images is a Hicken or a similar spelling. (3) In 'Massachusetts. Death Records 1859', Newburyport is on images 180-185 of 810 (image 179 is the town of Newbury). None of the indexed names is a Hicken. Image 182 is not indexed, but it is a second shot of the image 181 page (May-June 1859). (4) A record search of State Vital Records for given name Thomas, deaths 1856-61 in Massachusetts, finds no death entry for any Thomas Hicken. It does find his son's marriage: Thomas W. Hicken, born about 1837, married Mary E. Saunders at Rowley, 3 Nov 1859, parents Thomas and Hannah Hicken. It also finds a New Hampshire death record for a daughter, Hannah E. Consodine, whose parents are given as 'Thos Hickon' and Hannah Reynolds (not read). The 7 Dec 1858 Newburyport death date comes only from the compiled Island Register. He may have died elsewhere, or his death may never have been registered. Image 176 of the 1858 volume has only 23 indexed names, so part of that page may be unindexed. Confidence unchanged (Probable). ¶6
