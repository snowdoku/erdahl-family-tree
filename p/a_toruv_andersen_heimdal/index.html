About the family

His daughter Hæge was baptized in Treungen church on 11 Mar 1759 as 'Toruvs Hiemdals daatter Hæge'. Godparents: Orm Tvedt, Joen Hiemdal, Joran Aarack, Margit Hiemdal, Margit Olsdatter (Kviteseid ministerialbok 1754-1773, p.49). ¶5

His son Toruv was buried at Treungen on 7 Feb 1755, no age given: 'Torofs Hiemdals Søn Torofs' (Kviteseid ministerialbok 1754-1773, burials p.2, image 153). This child of his first marriage died young. The same day Orm Tvedt's son Halvor was buried. ¶6

His wife (not named) was churched in Treungen on 25 Jan 1756 together with Orm Tvedt's wife: 'Item Torofs Hiemdahls Qvinde' (Kviteseid ministerialbok 1754-1773, introductions, image 72). So a child was born about Dec 1755; its baptism is in the lost first leaves of the book (baptisms survive only from 12 Sep 1756). That child is probably Thoruf Torulfsen Hiemdal (see his page), who married in Drangedal in 1784. Baptisms Sep 1756 - Jan 1758 (images 3-11) show no child of his; the first is Hæge, Mar 1759. ¶8

His wife was buried at Treungen on 22 Oct 1773: 'Samme dag samme sted jordfæstet Torofs Hiemdahls Hustrue Gunild[?], gl. 53 aar' (Kviteseid ministerialbok 1754-1773, burials p.59, image 183). She was born about 1720; her first name probably reads Gunild (possibly Gunlaug), with no patronymic. Her death explains why he remarried as a widower in Lent 1775. She is probably the wife churched in Jan 1756 and the mother of Hæge (bapt. 1759). Whether she or the candidate Margit Knudsdatter (bur. 1763) was the mother of his older children (Anders ~1745, Gundbjør ~1752) is not settled, because no baptism of 1756-1763 seen so far names a mother. The same day 'Ole Torofsen Hiemdahl's' infant daughter was buried; Ole is probably a married son of Toruv (not linked). ¶9

Life story

Probate: 'Torov', aged 7 at his mother Astri Jansdatter's probate (8 Nov 1727) and 'Torow Andersøn', 13, at his father Anders Knudsen's probate on søndre Heimdal (16 Jul 1733), so born about 1720. His full siblings were Biug (Bjug) Andersen (11 in 1733) and Gunnild Andersdatter (15 in 1733). ¶11

As an orphaned minor: Panteregister I 1 fol. 52b (søndre Hiemdall) records that 'Targei Sønderland paa sin Myndling Torof Andersøns vegne' bought a share of the farm for 20 Rd, and that Bendix Olsen gave up his claim to a share for 35 Rd to [Torof] Andersøn by a deed registered 8 Mar 1740 at Prestgaarden (DA 10915 view 95). This fits his father Anders Knudsen's 1733 probate (Torow, aged 13). His guardian Targe Sønderland is probably Targe Toroffsen Sønderland (bur. 1756); no relationship word is given. ¶14

Re-reading Panteregister I 1 fol. 52b (DA 10915 view 95 left) for the 1740 deed: the registration date 8 Mar 1740 belongs to the SECOND transaction, Bendix Olsen's renunciation of his claim (for 35 Rd) to '[Torof] Andersøn', 'efter brev Dat. 7de og tinglyst 8de Martij 1740 paa Prestgaarden'. The first transaction, 'Targej Sønderland paa sin Myndling Torof Andersøns vegne kiøbt af hans So[..]' for 20 Rd, was 'tinglyst paa Prestgaarden d. 1[..]' with the month and year lost in the gutter. Pantebok I 4 (DA 10869) views 6-18 were checked: the spring 1740 sessions there are Fyresdal and Seljord (10-29 Mar 1740), with no Nissedal-tinglag session of 8 Mar 1740; the Nissedal tinglag summer ting at Præstegaarden on 1 Jul 1740 (views 13R-15R, deeds 28-31: sale of Treungen church to Ole Torofsen Aarak and Even Evensen; Tallef Tiostolfsen Homme; Gjertrud(?) Witkinsdatter, Felle; Elef Nielsen, Groven) has no Heimdal deed. So the 8 Mar 1740 registration is probably in the last leaves of pantebok I 3 (DA 10868, 1723-39), not in I 4. Whether 'So[..]' is 'Søster' (his full sister Gunnild Andersdatter, 15 in 1733) remains unread. ¶17a

In Treungen baptisms of 1759-1762 he appears only as a godparent: Whitsun 1759 (Torof Andersen, image 19), Maundy Thursday 1760 (Torof Hiemdahl, image 22) and the 4th Sunday after Easter 1762 (Torof Hiemdahl, image 30). No child of his was baptized between Apr 1759 and Oct 1762 (images 19-31 read). The register names only fathers, so his baptism entries cannot identify the mother of his children. ¶10

Married (2) on Midfaste Sunday 1775 in Treungen, as a widower (betrothed Sexagesima 1775): Torof Andersen Hiemdal and the widow Liv(?) Halvorsdatter Homme. ¶2

Cautioner (surety) for about ten Treungen betrothals 1775-81, among them 30 Mar 1781 at Tvedsund, just before the betrothal of Thore Tronsen Fælle and Hæge Toruvsdatter Hiemdal (Kviteseid ministerialbok 1773-1786, images 109-130). He is the only Toruv seen at Hiemdal in that register. Another Toruv lived at Kittilskaas in Kviteseid (son Joen Toruvsen, m. 1782). ¶4

1801 census, Hiemdal (Treungen): Toruv Andersen, aged 81, the farmer's father, a widower after his 2nd marriage, with his son Anders Toruvsen (56) and family. ¶1

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 4

Probable father of Gundbjør Toruvsdatter of Hiemdal (m. 1775). Hæge Toruvsdatter (b. ~1758, wife of Tore Thronsen at Felle) may also be his daughter, but this is speculative and not linked. ¶3

Wife and children named together (grade unchanged at Probable). The Panteregister I 1 abstract of the skifte after his wife Gunild Olsdatter on søndre Hiemdahl (5 May, between her burial on 22 Oct 1773 and 1779) lays land out to 'Torof Andersen' and to 'Anders, Knud, Torof Torofsønner' and 'Astri, Gundbiør og Hæge Torofsdøttre' (DA 10915 fol. 318, view 361). This ties Torof Andersen, by patronymic, to both of his tree children, Gundbiør and Hæge, in one record on his own farm. It stays Probable under audit rule 3, because the abstract does not label the heirs; the protocol entry (DA 25675 or 25676) would confirm him on his link to Hæge. ¶13a

Upgrade (regrade 3): Probable to Confirmed as father of Hæge Toruvsdatter (see her page); graded on his link to Hæge Toruvsdatter. Naming record: her baptism in Treungen church, 11 Mar 1759: 'Toruvs Hiemdals daatter Hæge' (Kviteseid ministerialbok 1754-1773, p. 49, image 18, read on the image). Continuity, all at Hiemdal: (1) confirmation in Nissedal church, 12 Nov 1775, 'Hæge Torufs daatter Hiemdal, gl. 16 aar' (Kviteseid ministerialbok 1773-1786, canvas 212 right), 16 years after the baptism and of matching age; (2) the skifte after his wife Gunild Olsdatter on søndre Hiemdahl (held 5 May, between Oct 1773 and 1779; Panteregister I 1 fol. 318, DA 10915 view 361), which lays land out to 'Torof Andersen' and to 'Astri, Gundbiør og Hæge Torofsdøttre' - used here for continuity, which needs no relationship label; (3) the betrothal of 30 Mar 1781 of 'Pigen Hæge Toruvsd: Hiemdal' to Thore Tronsen Fælle, with Toruv Andersen Hiemdal cautioner in the entry just before. He is the only Toruv seen at Hiemdal in the registers read (another Toruv lived at Kittilskaas in Kviteseid, a different farm); no named rival. The skifte also ties the father's full name Torof Andersen to the 'Toruv Hiemdal' of the baptism. His link to Gundbjør (Gundbiør Toruvsdatter) is not regraded here; the mother's link (Gunild Olsdatter Heimdal) stays Probable, because only the unlabelled panteregister abstract names Hæge as her heir. ¶15

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed, graded on his link to his daughter Hæge Toruvsdatter (see her page). Record: skifteprotokoll 11 (DA 25675) fol. 827a, No. 71, view 831: 'Anno 1775 den 15de Februarii' at 'Gaarden Sønder Hiemdahl i Treungens Annex og Hvideseid Præstegield', to register and divide the estate 'efter den under 13de Aug.(?) 1773 afdøde Qvinde Gunild Ols Datter og igienlevende Enkemand Torrof Andersøn', between 'hendes 7 Børn, 4 Sønner og 3 Døttre, nemlig': Anders Torrofsøn, of age; Knud Torrofsøn, of age; Torrof Torrofsøn, 19 (curator Jørgen Olsøn Hiemdahl); Joen Torrofsøn, 17 (guardian 'Faderen Torrof Andersøn'); the eldest daughter Astri Torrofsdatter, dead, who in marriage with Halvor Targeisøn Sønderland left a daughter Astri Halvorsdatter, 12 (guardian her father); Gundbiør Torrofsdatter, 23 (guardian Joen Sigurdsøn Hiemdahl); and Hæge Torrofsdatter, 16, for whom 'Broderen Anders Torrofsøn' was appointed guardian. Valuers Kittil Torrofsøn(?) Nordbøe, Jørgen Sigurdsøn Hiemdahl and Jørgen Olsøn ibid. The death day reads '13de Aug.' (month abbreviation slightly uncertain); her burial was 22 Oct 1773. This is the protocol entry behind the panteregister abstract (DA 10915 fol. 318), which dates the division 5 May [1775]. The entry names 'Enkemand Torrof Andersøn' as Gunild's widower and as 'Faderen' (guardian of the son Joen), and Hæge Torrofsdatter, 16, as one of their children; it also names Gundbiør Torrofsdatter, 23, and Torrof Torrofsøn, 19. Continuity to the tree's Hæge (see her page): she was baptized 11 Mar 1759 as 'Toruvs Hiemdals daatter Hæge' (16 in Feb 1775 fits), was confirmed in Nov 1775 as 'Hæge Torufs daatter Hiemdal, gl. 16 aar', and was betrothed on 30 Mar 1781 as 'Hæge Toruvsd: Hiemdal' to Thore Tronsen Fælle: the same farm, gaps of 6 years or less, and no other Hæge Toruvsdatter on Hiemdal (rival: none named; Toruv Andersen is the only Toruv at Hiemdal in the 1773-86 registers). The skifte was held on 15 Feb 1775, just before his second betrothal (Sexagesima 1775), as the law required. ¶16

Corrections 1

Correction: the earlier candidate first wife, Margit Knudsdatter (bur. 1763), is now unlikely to be the mother of any of these six children. All six inherited from Gunild, so all were her children; that includes Anders (b. about 1745) and Gundbiør (b. about 1752). ¶13b

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Candidate first wife (NOT proved): 'Margit Knuds daatter Hiemdall, gl. 47 aar' was buried at Treungen on the 17th Sunday after Trinity (25 Sep) 1763 (Kviteseid ministerialbok 1754-1773, burials, image 168). She was born about 1716, which fits a mother of Gundbjør (b. ~1752) and Hæge (b. ~1759), and Toruv's son Knud would carry her father's name. But the entry names no husband, and Hiemdal also held the households of Evind and Sigur. A record naming Toruv's wife (a baptism 1754-1763 or a probate) is needed before she is linked. ¶7

Searches that found nothing 1

Negative: his wife Gunild's probate (bur. 22 Oct 1773) is not among Øvre Telemark protocol no. 11 (DA 25675) entries Nos. 26-31 at images 752-767: No. 26 is 5 Nov 1774 and Nos. 30-31 are 24 Jun 1774 at Nordre Dahlen and in the Omnerdal annex (Seljord); images 756-758 are 1775 continuations of Flatdal estates. With earlier passes, protocol 11 has now been read by heading from image 700 to 800 without a Hiemdal entry. The Kviteseid confirmation lists (7982 canvas 212, 1775) give only name, farm and age, so they cannot name Toruv as father. ¶12
