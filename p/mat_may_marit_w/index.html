About the family

Sister of Severt S. Eidem, so Mayme (Eidem) Eidem's paternal aunt. Recorded as Marit Eidem Wennblom; the wave-2 NUMIDENT harvest independently noted an Eidem woman married as 'Mary S. Wennblom'. Linked as a sibling on Severt's Find a Grave memorial. ¶1

Life story

Baptism: baptised in Selbu on 28 DECEMBER 1851 - Selbu parish register, Selbu local parish 1843-1859, births and baptisms, PAGE 71, SERIAL NO. 128. Child 'Marit'; father 'Sivert Johnsen'; mother 'Brynild Johnsdatter'. (The indexed residence reads 'Elderaas', another mangling of Eidem.) ¶2

Her Find a Grave year of 1851 is confirmed and the month supplied; she was baptised three days after Christmas, which in Selbu usually means a birth in the same week. ¶4

1865 census: person 007 at oestre Eidem, Selbu - 'Marit Sivertsdatter', aged 15, 'Deres Datter', unmarried; consistent with a December 1851 birth once this enumerator's habitual year of over-statement is allowed for. ¶3

She emigrated with the family as a girl of fourteen or fifteen, married a Wennblom in America and died in 1925. ¶5

Obituary, transcribed on Find a Grave memorial 121858039 and not yet re-read by this agent in the paper itself: 'Marit Wennblom, (nee Eidem) was born November 29th, 1851 in EAST EIDEM, SELBU, NORWAY of parents [Sivert] S. Eidem and Bry[n]hild Eidem. In the spring of 1866 she immigrated to America and came directly to Union County, S.D. On the 28th of January 1880 she was joined in marriage to JOHN WENNBLOM of Union County and shortly afterwards they settled on the farm north of Elk Point, which has since been her home. Her husband passed away April 12, 1912. Since that time she has lived with her son, Joel. Four children survive her namely: SAM, S. JOEL, RUBEN and FLORENCE, MRS. HALFRED FINSTAD, all of Union County. She is also survived by two brothers, OLE and SEVERT EIDEM, and by seven grandchildren.... Shortly before midnight last Saturday she was taken suddenly ill and passed away at 1:15 a.m. SUNDAY FEB. 22ND, 1925 at the age of 73 years, 2 months and 23 days. Mrs. Wennblom was one of the early members of the St. Paul church... Funeral services were held from the South St. Paul church at 2 p.m. Wednesday. Rev. C. B. Runsvold offici[ating].' ¶7

Two things in it matter beyond her own life. It independently names the family's Norwegian home as EAST EIDEM (ostre Eidem) IN SELBU - the same farm her brother's 1940 obituary describes as lying by the lake. And it says that in February 1925 she was survived by 'two brothers, Ole and Severt Eidem', which contradicts Severt's own 1940 obituary saying all four of his siblings had predeceased him. Her three sons Reuben, Sam and Joel Wennblom were pallbearers at Severt's funeral in 1940. ¶8

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
