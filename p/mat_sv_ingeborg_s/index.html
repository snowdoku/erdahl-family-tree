About the family

One of the two Ingebor Sivertsdatters of 1801 is this woman; which is not certain. The elder was born about 1789 and the younger about 1792, and the 1825 and 1865 ages both point to the younger. ¶4

Life story

She appears in three households over sixty-four years and never married. 1801: 'Ingebor Sivertsdatter', aged 9, one of the children of Sivert Jonssen and Haelge Jonsdatter at Eidum - and one of two daughters of that name, the elder aged 12. 1825: 'Ingebor Sivertsd.', aged 33, in her brother's house at Eidem as 'Tp.' (tjenestepike, servant). 1865: person 009 at rural residence 0058 oestre Eidem, aged 74, 'ug' (unmarried), occupation 'Foederaadslem' - living on the farm's pension in her nephew's house. ¶1

Probate: 'Ingeborg' is the only daughter of that name among the heirs of her father Sivert Johnsen Eidem (closed 5 April 1815) and of her mother Helge Johnsdatter (closed 22 September 1820); in 1820 she was unmarried and under the guardianship of her brother John Sivertsen the younger (skifteprotokoll 10b, fol. 705b and 765b). ¶5

The 1865 census gives her no family position, so the relationship is inferred from the three ages running consistently, the patronymic, and her presence in the same house as a dependant across two generations. Rated Probable. ¶2

An unmarried aunt kept on the foederaad is exactly what a Selbu freeholder's household looks like, and she would have been the great-aunt the children of that house grew up with. Severt S. Eidem, who died at Elk Point in 1940, shared a roof with her until he was at least six. ¶3

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Confidence raised from Probable to Confirmed: the 1815 and 1820 probates name Ingeborg as a daughter of Sivert Johnsen and Helge Johnsdatter, and as only one Ingeborg was then living, the unmarried Ingebor Sivertsdatter of the 1825 and 1865 Eidem households is she. Which of the two 1801 Ingebors she was is still open. ¶6
