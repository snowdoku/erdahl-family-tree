About the family

Parents (Probable): Lasse Monsen of Indre Oppedal (see his page) and Anna Gullaksdatter (see her page). The division of 3 Jun 1783 at Indre Oppedal, at the request of the living couple Lasse Monsen and Anna Gullachs[datter], names 'ældste Søn Mons Larsen' of Indre Oppedal, to whom they had long since sold and ceded their land at Indre Oppedal, and makes him guardian of his sister Elie. He was a skattebonde (farm-owning farmer) at Indre Oppedal in 1801, aged 60, and was buried in Dec 1822 aged 80 3/4 (born about Mar 1742), which fits a first son born after the couple's daughter Rand (baptised 16 Feb 1741) and named after his paternal grandfather Mons Johansen. No other Mons Larsen/Lassesen is known at Indre Oppedal. This supersedes the 'Possible father (not linked)' note: Lasse Gudormsen of Ytre Oppedal (probate 1746/48) had a son whose name only looks like Måns/Mons and whose patronymic appears as Gutterms-, at the other Oppedal farm; that candidate is set aside. Not Confirmed because no record names Mons's parents and him together with his later life (1801-1822); the 1783 eldest son is identified with him by name, farm and age only. ¶10a

Pedigree collapse (corrected by audit): through his father Lasse Monsen he is a grandson of Mons Johansen and Randi Iversdatter of Indre Oppedal (see his page, Randi Iversdatter). The same couple also enter the line through their son Torkild Monsen of Asheim (see his page), father of Randi Torkildsdatter (see her page), and through their son Iver Monsen of Botnen (see his page), father of Anna Iversdatter (see her page). His wife Else Torkildsdatter is the daughter of Torkel Iversen of Indre Oppedal (see his page), not of Torkild Monsen. Mons and Else are not known to be related. They would be related only if Else's mother Britte Monsdatter were a daughter of Mons Johansen and Randi, which is unproved (patch 035 on hold). ¶11

Parents (Confirmed,; raised from Probable, patch 039): Lasse Monsen of Indre Oppedal (see his page) and his wife Anne/Anna (see her page). The records that moved the grade: (1) his baptism at Brekke on 25 Feb 1742 (Gulen ministerialbok 11441, canvas 23 right), which names his father Lasse Monsen of Indre Oppedal and his mother Anne; and (2) the division of 3 Jun 1783 (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll A 11a, fol. 122b-123a), in which the living couple Lasse Monsen and Anna Gullachs[datter] of Indre Oppedal name 'ældste Søn Mons Larsen' (eldest son), to whom they had already ceded their land at Indre Oppedal. Identity of the child of 1742 with our Mons Lassesen: same name and patronymic; the same farm, Indre Oppedal, where he was farmer in 1783 and skattebonde (farm-owning farmer) in 1801; his age, 60 in 1801 and 80 3/4 at burial on 16 Dec 1822, points to a birth in early 1742, matching the baptism within weeks; he is the eldest son in 1783, as a first son born a year after the couple's daughter Rand (baptised 16 Feb 1741) would be; and he was named after his paternal grandfather Mons Johansen. The only other candidate on file, a son 'Måns?' of Lasse Gudormsen of Ytre Oppedal (probate 1746/48; name uncertain, patronymic written Gutterms-), belongs to the other Oppedal farm and cannot be the 1783 eldest son of Lasse Monsen; no other Mons Larsen/Lassesen is known at Indre Oppedal. (Only canvas 23 right of the 1742 baptisms was read for this find; saw no child of Lasse Gudormsen on canvases 19L-21L or 25-26.) Nothing contradicts the identification. This supersedes 'Parents (Probable)' and its 'Would settle it' sentence: the baptism it asked for has now been found. ¶14

Life story

Birth: changed from ABT 1741 to BEF 25 FEB 1742 from the baptism. Children were then usually baptised within days or a few weeks of birth, so he was probably born in Feb 1742 (inference). This agrees closely with his burial age of 80 3/4 in Dec 1822 (born about Mar 1742) and with his 1801 census age of 60. The id suffix _1741 is only a label from the earlier census estimate and is not changed. ¶13

Baptism (crop): baptised at Brekke church on Sunday Oculi 1742, which was 25 Feb 1742 (Easter 1742 fell on 25 Mar; Oculi is the third Sunday in Lent, four weeks before Easter; a later pencil '25/2' stands beside the heading). The entry, under 'Dom: Oculi tienistforretn. Brecke', reads 'dobt Lasse Monsen ind: Opdals b. N. Mons Mat: anne' (baptised Lasse Monsen of Indre Oppedal's child, named Mons; mother Anne). Godparents (Test.): Gregorius [of Indre Oppedal?], Hans of the same farm (ibid.), Lasse Pers[en] (or Ivers[en]; read 'Lasse Ivers:' among the sponsors of another Indre Oppedal baptism in Nov 1742) of the same farm, Aslak of the same farm, and Birthe Monsd[atter] Østerbø[e], followed by one or two illegible words. The margin 'Dom: qvasimo[do]genit:' records the mother's churching on Quasimodogeniti, 1 Apr 1742. Gulen ministerialbok 1736-1746 (Digitalarkivet 11441), canvas 23, right page (numbered 24), foot of page. ¶12

1801 census: Mons Lassessen, 60, head of household (huusfader), married for the first time, skattebonde (tax-paying farmer) at Indre Oppedal (Oppedal Indre), Brekke, with wife Else Torkieldsdatter, 53, and joint children Torkield 22, Giertrud 16 and Kari 12. Birth about 1741 is inferred from his age. ¶1

Grandchildren: As Mons Lassessøn of Indre Oppedal he was a godparent at the baptism of his grandson Lasse Torkildsen (20 May 1803) and home-baptised his grandson Mons (baptism confirmed in church 1 Jan 1808) (Gulen church book 1798-1808, index). ¶2

1808 barneskifte: on 26 Sep 1808 'Gaardmanden Mons Lassesen' (the farmer) and his wife Else Torchelsdatter, both living, had their estate registered and divided among their children Torkild, Anna, Giertrud and Kari (No. 39, a barneskifte or division with the children in the parents' lifetime; its purpose, perhaps handing over the farm, was not fully read). This is not a death record. ¶4

1809: he was lauværge (legal spokesman) for his widowed daughter-in-law Mari Tostensdatter at the probate after his son Torkild Monsen in Mar 1809. ¶5

1812: he was alive on 2 Jun 1812 as the surviving husband at the probate after his wife Else Torkildsdatter. ¶6

Probate: as 'Kaarmand Mons Lassesen Indre Oppedahl' (a kårmann, a retired farmer living on a pension from the farm) his estate was registered and valued on 22 Mar 1823 by lensmann Nils Andersen Ringereide and settled on 2 May 1823. The heirs were the children of his dead son Torkild (three sons and Else Torkildsdatter), the children of his dead daughter Anna (four sons and Eli Jensdatter), his daughter Giertrud, married to Iver Tostensen of Engesætter, and his daughter Kari, married to L[asse?] Pedersen of Ørneberg[?]. He therefore died between 2 Jun 1812 and 22 Mar 1823, probably shortly before the registration. None of the three probates states his origin or parents. ¶7

Burial (per index): 'Mons Lassesøn', Kaaremand (retired farmer on a pension), of Indreoppedahl, died 12 Dec and buried 16 Dec 1822, aged 80 3/4, serial no. 65. Gulen church book, burials 1822 (index pg00000001043810); the book was not read. The age gives a birth about Mar 1742, within a year of the 1801 census age of 60; the birth field is left at ABT 1741. This replaces the death window 'between 2 Jun 1812 and 22 Mar 1823'; his estate was registered on 22 Mar 1823. ¶8

Alternatives considered and set aside 3

Not to be confused with Mons Torkieldssen, 69, of Yttre Oppedal, father of the other Torkild Monssen (29 in 1801). ¶3

Possible father (not linked): the probate after Lasse Gudormsen of Ytre Oppedal, 27 May 1746 (last digit possibly 8; Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll 6b, Nº 18, fol. 496b), divides the estate between the widow Marite Andersdatter and '1 Søn' whose name looks like Måns or Mons (not certain; age not read) and '1 Datter Gertrud', 4; Torkild Gutormsen of Ytre Oppedal acts in the case. A son Mons of a Lasse, born about 1741, fits his age (60 in 1801, 80 3/4 at burial in 1822) and his patronymic, and he named a daughter Giertrud. ¶9a

Do not confuse: Mons Torkildsen, son of Torkil (Torkel Iversen) of Indre Oppedal and Birthe, was baptised about Feb 1741 (mother churched 17 Mar 1741) on the same farm. He is the eldest son 'Mons Torkelsen' of Britte Monsdatter's 1793 probate and the brother of this Mons's wife Else Torkildsdatter, not this Mons. ¶15
