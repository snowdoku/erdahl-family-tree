About the family

Children baptised at Terling to Richard Gaymer (FreeREG index): John 19 Mar 1591/92, Sara 20 Jan 1593/94, Elizabeth 31 Oct 1596, Andrew 2 Apr 1600, Lydia 18 May 1602 ('Lydia... father Richarde GAYMER', file line 163); and, by his second wife, Jeames 8 Jan 1603/4, Richard 31 Oct 1605, Judith 21 May 1607, Bridget 30 Mar 1609, Frauncis 7 Feb 1612/13. NEHGR 151 (1997) gives the same list from the Strutt transcript of the register, adding an earlier Frances bp. 20 Jan 1610/11, bur. 21 Jan 1612/13. ¶2

Parents added: Richard Gaymer of Coggeshall, fuller (will 3 Apr 1571, Emmison Bishop of London's Commissary Court no. 347: 'To Richard my son £26 6s.8d. which is in the hands of one John OYLY of Terling'), Probable; and Alice Webbe (m. Coggeshall 1561/2; probably the Alice Gaymer who married at Terling 25 Jun 1571), Speculative. See their notes. This upgrades the earlier Coggeshall lead to an entered pair; no record names the Terling miller as the fuller's son. ¶11

Life story

Overseer of his stepfather's will: John Thorison of Terling, miller, who had married Alice Gaymer (very probably Richard's mother) in 1571, made his will on 1 Jan 1590/1 and wrote 'I appoint Richarde Gaymer to be Overseer of this my last will & testament' (ERO D/ACW 5/51, original read on the free watermarked image). Richard married Margaret Mason at Terling six months earlier (23 Jul 1590). In 1608 'Richard Gaymor and John Road, millers, all of Terling' stood surety at Quarter Sessions (ERO Q/SR 186/48, catalogue). This ties the Terling miller to the Thorison household into which the Coggeshall fuller's widow and under-age son moved in 1571. His own grade (on his link to Lydia Gaymer) is unchanged: Probable, because of the Terling-to-Scituate step. ¶12

Will read on the original: ERO D/ACW 6/45, free watermarked image (Essex Archives Online id 863445). The small hand is barely legible at the free 400-pixel size, but cropped and enlarged it shows the opening 'Richard Gaymer of Terling... Miller', the bequest 'unto Sara Gaymer & Elizabeth Gaymer my daughters', and 'Also I give and bequeath unto Lidia Gaymer my daughter all that...', and it ends with his own signature 'Richard Gaymer' (not a mark). This matches the text printed in NEHGR 151: 288, which is cited in his notes. The witnesses' names could not be read at this resolution. His grade (Probable, on his link to Lydia Gaymer) is unchanged: the will settles that Lydia Gaymer of Terling was his daughter, but not that she was Lydia, wife of Humphrey Turner of Scituate. ¶13

Marriage (first): 'Richarde Gaymer' and Margaret Mason were married at All Saints, Terling, Essex, on 23 July 1590 (FreeREG transcription of the Terling register, file line 253). ¶1

Jury service: NEHGR 151 says Essex Quarter Sessions rolls show him on jury panels for neighbouring towns between 1595 and 1601 (ERO Q/SR, not read). ¶8

Will text read in print: NEHGR 151 (1997): 288 prints Richard Gaymer's will at length: 'Richard Gaymer of Terling in the county of Essex, Miller', 8 Oct 1613, proved 25 Oct 1613, with devises to 'John Gaymer my eldest sonne', 'Andrew Gaymer my second sonne', 'James Gaymer my third sonne', 'Richard Gaymer my fouerth sonne', 'Frances Gaymer my fift sonne', 'Judith Gaymer my daughter', 'Sara Gaymer & Elizabeth Gaymer my daughters' (houses in North Street, Colchester) and 'Liddia Gaymer my daughter' (part of the tenement called the Angell in Terling, on condition she release at 21 a GBP 5 legacy from William Gibson of Heybridge, brazier); wife Joan residuary legatee and executrix. The article also quotes the Terling register: 'Lydia Gaymer the daughter of Richarde Gaymer was baptised the xviiith' of May 1602. He was a juror for neighbouring towns 1595-1601 (Quarter Sessions rolls). So Richard is proved the father of Lydia Gaymer of Terling. ¶9

Marriage (second): Richard Gaymer married Johan Robinson at St Mary the Virgin, Fairstead, Essex, on 28 Feb 1602/3 (FreeREG, file line 76; NEHGR 151 gives 19 Feb 1602/3 from Strutt's Fairstead transcript). His widow 'Jone Gaymer' married Hierome Neale at Terling on 31 July 1614 (FreeREG). ¶3

Will: Richard Gaymer of Terling, miller, 'being frail in body', made his will on 8 October 1613. He left the Angell tenement in Terling, after his wife Joan's term, to 'John Gaymer my eldest sonne'; a Colchester tenement to 'Andrew Gaymer my second sonne'; his dwelling to 'James Gaymer my third sonne'; other Terling property to his fourth son Richard and fifth son Frances; land to his daughter Judith and a Colchester tenement to 'Sara Gaymer & Elizabeth Gaymer my daughters'; and part of the Angell 'unto Liddia Gaymer my daughter', on condition that at 21 she release a £5 legacy from William Gibson of Heybridge, brazier. Joan was residuary legatee and executrix; the will was proved 25 October 1613 (Essex Record Office D/ACW 6/45, as transcribed in NEHG Register 151 (1997): 288). The will was not read in the original. ¶4

Burial: 'Richard GAYMER' was buried at Terling on 10 Sep 1613 according to the FreeREG transcription (file line 94). NEHGR 151 gives 10 October 1613, two days after the will, from the Strutt transcript; the will date of 8 October favours October, so the FreeREG month is probably a slip. Death entered as before 25 Oct 1613 (probate). ¶5

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

2 on the grade · 1 alternative set aside

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Confidence Probable: the Terling baptism (FreeREG) names Richard as father of Lydia Gaymer (1602), and his will names 'Liddia Gaymer my daughter'. That this Lydia is Lydia, wife of Humphrey Turner of Scituate, rests on the Sandon marriage of 'Humphry Turner and Lidda Gamar', 24 October 1618, which was quoted in NEHGR 151 from the register and has not been read by this project, together with the Terling baptism of John, son of Humfry Turner and Lydia his wife (1621/22). So the line from Richard to the tree is Probable, not Confirmed. ¶6

Confidence unchanged (Probable) for one reason only: the step from Lydia Gaymer of Terling to Lydia, wife of Humphrey Turner of Scituate, rests on the Sandon marriage 'Humphry Turner and Lidda Gamar October 24' 1618 and the Terling baptism 'John the sonne of Humfry Turner & Lydia his wife' 24 Mar 1621/2 (both quoted verbatim in NEHGR 151: 287), matched by name and family pattern to the Scituate family (eldest son John in Humphrey's will). No record says the Scituate Humphrey came from Terling. Parents not found: Turner found no Gaymer baptism for him; a Coggeshall Richard Gaymer married Alice Webbe 1561/2 (FreeREG; lead only). ¶10

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Origin (lead only): Gaymers were numerous at Coggeshall, about 7 miles from Terling: a Richard Gaymer married Alice Webbe there on 8 Feb 1561/62 and a 'Ric Gaymer' was buried there on 4 Apr 1571 (FreeREG). Coggeshall baptisms in FreeREG do not begin until about 1600, so no baptism of this Richard (born probably about 1560-1568) was found. An Alice Gaymer married John Thorison at Terling on 25 June 1571. Not entered as parents. ¶7
