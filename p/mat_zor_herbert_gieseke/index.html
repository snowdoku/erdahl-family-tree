Burial: Zoar Lutheran Cemetery, lot 13 — South Dakota state burial register, 'HERBERT H GIESEKE 09-13-1967, lot 13, block 0'. Lot 13 otherwise holds Harstads: Olaf Harstad d. 1910 and Casppper [Casper] Harstad d. 1941. No Find a Grave memorial. ¶2

A German surname in an otherwise wholly Norwegian churchyard, which is itself a small marker of how far into the twentieth century the colony stayed endogamous. ¶3
