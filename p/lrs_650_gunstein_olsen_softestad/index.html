About the family

Father (bygdebok, Probable): Olav Gunsteinson of Vik, gnr. 7, Nissedal (see his page). Søftestad canvas 451: 'Gunstein Olavson, son [av] Olav Gunsteinson Vik'; in 1692 Gunstein sold 5 [merker] of his father's land in Vik. His brother Eivind's estate card (skiftekort 6856 view 1464, 1719, Even Olssen of Vik) fits the book's Vik family. ¶4

Life story

Bygdebok (Nissedal vol., Søftestad, canvases 451-452): 'Gunstein [..]', son of Olav Gunsteinson, married Sigrid Gjermundsdtr. (daughter of Gjermund Ormson, d. about 1676; she had first married a Tellev Tallakson); children 'Tellev omlag 1700 [g. m.] Aslaug Salvesdtr. [and 2.] Sigrid Torbjørnsdtr.' and 'Sigrid omlag 1701 [g. m.] Torgrim Vigleikson'. Sigrid Gjermundsdtr. died about 1701 (skifte). Gunstein married again about 1707 Bergit Kristoffersdtr. (d. 1712) and then Gunhild Pedersdtr. (children Kjetil 1716,.. 1719, Kristoffer 1721); he sold land in 1692 and 1712 and died about 1738. Canvas 452: 'Tellev Gunsteinson [g. m.] Aslaug Salvesdtr', children Sigrid about 1716 (m. 1758 Aslak Vigleikson), Olav about 1719 (m. Anne Johannesdtr.), Salve about 1721; Aslaug d. 1728; Tellev d. 1755 aged about 55. ¶1

An earlier record of the couple: pantebok I 2 (14504) No 305, entered about Oct 1692 (view 5 left): 'Kiendes Jeg Undertegnede Gunsten Olsen Søftestad paa Min Hustrues Sigri Giermundsdatters, samt Stifdatters Rannou(?) Tallefsdatters vegne' (I, Gunsten Olsen of Søftestad, on behalf of my wife Sigri Gjermundsdatter and my stepdaughter Rannou(?) Tallefsdatter). Knud Andersen joins for his wife Margit Tallachsdatter, about a mortgage their late father(-in-law) Tallach had given on half of 1½ tønder in Nedre Fione. So Gunstein was on Søftestad and married to Sigri by about 1692. With Tallef's daughter aged 12 in 1728, Tollef Gunsteinsen was born about 1685-1697; the bygdebok's 'omlag 1700' is too late. The stepdaughter reads Rannou here; the 1701 reading 'Kari(?)' is uncertain and is probably the same girl. The deed does not concern Vik. The bygdebok's 1692 Vik sale was not found in views 2-9 of 14504. ¶7

Gap still open (graded on his link to; stays Probable). Checked: (1) Tallef's own probate, 23 Oct 1755 (25673 No 154) names no parents and no sister; (2) the 7 Mar 1718 Søftestad probate after Jørgen Eigildsøn (25692 fol. 305b, view 316) has his stepdaughter Rannej Tollefsdatter as widow with laugverge Jon Fione, not Tollef; card 6856 v1261 says Jørgen's 1 td in Søftestad 'blev indløst av den odelsberettigede' (the deed of redemption, pantebok I 3 or the 1718 tingbok, could name Tollef as Sigrid's odel heir); (3) Vest-Telemark skifteprotokoll 1735-40 (25669, URN sk2009011565NNNN = view): the Nissedal session of May 1738 is Nos 143-145 (views 140-148: Nordbø 19 May, Brække 20 May, Nordre Grimstvedt 21 May) with no Søftestad entry; Mar-Apr 1739 entries (views 224-264) are Fyresdal, Vinje, Lårdal; 19 Aug 1739 at view 268. The bygdebok (canvas 452) says he owned nothing when he died in 1738 ('ikkje noko... då han døydde i 1738'), so there may be no probate. ¶8a

Gap still open (graded on the link to Tallef Gundstensen Søftestad; stays Probable). Checked: (1) Vest-Telemark skifteprotokoll 25669 views 264-386 (19 Aug 1739 to Sep 1740): the only Nissedal session is 6-9 Apr 1740 (views 350-359: Lintvedt, Grimstvedt, a Nissedal farm on 8 Apr with heirs Signe and Sigri Knudsdøttre, Midbø); no Søftestad probate after Gunstein Olsen or his widow Gunhild. (2) Panteregister I 1 (10915) s. 44b, the whole Søftestad entry: Gunsten Olsen got Søftestad land 1696 (deeds dated 7 Nov [1695?] tinglyst 15 Jun 1696, one from Egill Jørgensen Meule), mortgaged 1/2 td to Peder Ellefsen 1701, sold 1 td to Jørgen Egilsen Meule (deed 30 Jun 1712, tinglyst 15 Oct 1712); then a line dated 1718 in which Gunsten Olsen mortgages [pantsat] land 'i denne gaard' to Hans Ellefsen T[ong?] for 150 rd (obligation dated 22 Jun..., reading uncertain), so Gunstein himself held the land again by late 1718; the next owners are Anders Halvorsen Næs, who sold to Ole Tallefsen (deed tinglyst 24 Oct 1754), and Ole Tallefsen 1759-65. No deed in the register names Tollef Gunsteinsen, so the 'odelsberettigede' who redeemed Jørgen Eigilsen's 1 td (card 6856 v1261) was probably Gunstein, not Tollef, and the redemption does not bridge the gap. ¶9

Primary record found and read: skifte after his wife Sigri Giermundsdaatter, Søftestad, 8 Oct 1701 (østfjelske 25699 No 39, view 64): 'Octobris d. 8de Var paa Søfftestad i Neisedahls annex med tvende laugrettismend [..] at registere, vurdere og siden skifte og dele efter afgangne Sigri Giermundsdaatter imellem hendes efterlevende mand Gunsteen Olsen og sammen avlede børn Tollef Gunsteensøn og Sigri Gunsteensdaatter, og med første mand Tollef Tallachsen avlet Kari(?) Tollefsdaatter, overværende hendes formynder Egil Jørgensen Meule' (the estate to be divided between her surviving husband Gunstein Olsen and the children they had together, Tollef and Sigri Gunsteinsen, and Kari Tollefsdatter, her child by her first husband Tollef Tallaksen, whose guardian Egil Jørgensen Meule was present). This is the 'test record' named and it names the relationship: Gunsteen Olsen is the father of Tollef Gunsteensøn by Sigri ('sammen avlede børn'). Graded on his link to Tallef Gundstensen Søftestad: kept PROBABLE. Continuity to Tallef Gundsteensen of the 1728 Søftestad probate: same farm; the father Gunsten Olsen is on Søftestad throughout 1696-1718 (panteregister I 1, 10915 fol. 44b); Tallef named his eldest daughter Sigri (b. about 1716) after this mother; the only other Tallev Gunsteensen of the farm (son of Gunsteen Tallefsen and Ellen Asbjørnsdatter, Moen under Søftestad) was 17 in 1731 and cannot be the 1728 father of a girl of 12. BUT no record between Oct 1701 and Nov 1728 (27 years) names Tollef/Tallef himself, so by the audit-3 gap rule this stays Probable. ¶5a

The same skifte's Fastegods (view 64 left foot and right head, partly read) mentions land that came to 'Enchemanden Gunsteen Olsen' by inheritance after a father, and shares after a brother '[T]idre(?) Olsen, der døde barnløs' and a sister, and land 'i Brunckeberg'. Readings uncertain; whose father (his or Sigri's) is not settled, so it is not used for Olav Gunsteinson Vik. A careful transcription of view 64 (left, lines from 'Fastegods') and the head of the right page would test the Vik descent. ¶6

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
