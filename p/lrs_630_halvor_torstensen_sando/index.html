Life story

Probate (read on the scan): 'Ao 1719 d. 21 July' at the farm 'Sandøe udi Aals Hoved Sogn i Hallingdahlen', the estate of the late 'Dannemand Halvor Torstensen Sandøe, som boede og døde sammesteds', was divided 'imellem hans igienlevende Søn Torsten Halvorsen og... Rangdi Halvorsdatter', the widow 'Gunild Herbrandsdatter' giving up the estate (skifteprotokoll 7, fol. 211b). The card index adds: Torsten of full age, Rangdj 'ug.' (unmarried), the children's first cousin Ole Torckelsen Tvetter, and 9 løb odelsgods in Sando taxed at 90 rd; the division was concluded 15 Sep 1719. ¶1

Protocol wording: the 1719 skifte (fol. 211b) divides the estate 'imellem hans igienlevende Børn Torsten Halvorsen og Rangdi Halvorsdatter'. The protocol gives no marital status for Rangdi at this point; the 'ug.' (unmarried) on the index card is the card-maker's summary, which is not supported in the opening. The debts list (fol. 212a) includes small sums to Ole Torkelsen and to 'Rangdi Halvorsdatter' (readings uncertain). Land (fol. 212b, 'Odels og Pante Gods'): the 9 løb of Sando had been acquired by two deeds, one of 15 Oct 1653 for 6 løb (the seller apparently from Hol annex; names not read with certainty) and one of 29 Nov 1661 for 3 løb, valued 90 rd. These deeds, if found in a court book of 1653/1661, would probably name his father (a Torsten) as buyer. The 1723 skifte after his son appointed the cousin Ole Torkelsen Tvetter as guardian of the grandson Halvor Torstensen; Knud Aslesen does not appear in it, so it neither supports nor refutes the identification of his daughter with Rangdi of Dybsjorde. ¶3

Bygdebok: Aal bygdesoge, slektssoga 3, Sando øvre gnr 30 bnr 2 (canvas 251): 'Halvor Torsteinsson Sando 1620c-1719. Foreldre: truleg Torstein...' (the rest of the line not recovered). The bygdebok itself hedges ('truleg'), so no father is added. The earlier Sando chapter (canvas 250) treats Tolleiv Torsteinsson Sando (parents Torstein Larsson [øvre...]) and his son Torstein (about 1625-1705), who is too young to be Halvor's father; a Torstein of the generation before is meant. The birth year about 1620 looks early beside children born 1670-75 and a wife born about 1640; treat it as the bygdebok's estimate. Rebuilt (worker tag for line 2) from nb.no content-search word boxes; dates from the rebuild are given as ABT. ¶4a

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the identification · 1 alternative set aside · 1 open question

The case for this identification 1

Identification (Probable, inferred; graded on the parents' link to their daughter Rangdi Halvorsdatter; Rangdi's own Confirmed grade, on her link to her son Asle Knudsen, is unaffected): the 1719 skifte at Sando in Ål names 'Rangdi Halvorsdatter' as the daughter and heir of Halvor Torstensen Sandøe and Gunild Herbrandsdatter. Rangdi Halvorsdatter, wife of Knud Aslesen, died at Dybsjorde in Ål in 1742. The link rests on the same name and patronymic in the same parish, plus family ties: her brother Torsten Halvorsen of Sando (d. 1723) left the widow Berte (Bergith) Olsdatter, who remarried Tosten Thordsen of Sando, and Rangdi's son Asle Knudsen of Dybsjorde/Medgaarden married Kari Tostensdatter, Bergith's daughter by that second marriage. No other Rangdi Halvorsdatter of the right age is known in Ål, but no record names her as the same woman. ¶2a

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Caution: the card calls Rangdi 'ug.' (unmarried) in 1719, while her son Ole Knudsen was buried in 1761 aged 44 (b. about 1717) and is called [fuld]myndig in 1742. If the burial age is overstated by a few years (common) and Rangdi married Knud Aslesen about 1719-20, it fits; otherwise the two Rangdis differ. Different farms and a 23-year gap, so Probable at most. ¶2b

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Test record: the 1719 skifte after Halvor (Hallingdal sorenskriveri; panteregister DA 14666, Sando). ¶4b
