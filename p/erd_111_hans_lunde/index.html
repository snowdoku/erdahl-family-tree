About the family

Probable father of Hans Hansen Lunde: the bailiff lists a son Hans, 32, under Hans of Lunde, and Hans Hansen held Lunde by 1701 (63) and died there before 29 May 1713. Same farm, name and patronymic; the ages disagree (b. ~1632 here, ~1638 by 1701), and neither vicar's list names the son Hans. Not Confirmed. ¶2

Wife in 1645: the 1645 poll tax lists on Lunde, Tjugum sogn, the farmer 'Hanns' followed by 'Joran Hansdtr' (Digitalarkivet 100387, p. 23, pt00000000056620-056621). Entered as Joran Hansdatter; not linked as the mother of Hans Hansen Lunde (see her record). ¶3

Life story

Bygdebok (Laberg, Balestrand 1 canvas 137, Lunde; rebuilt text): he is 'Hans Olson', who came to Lunde [from a farm in a gap of the rebuild] with his wife '[...] Hansdtr.'; they had 5 children, among them the son Hans (Hans Hansson, b. 1638, d. 1713), and 'Hans Lunde hadde ein bror, Arne'. 1645 poll tax, Ytre Sogn, Lunde: 'Hanns' with 'Joran Hansdtr' (pt56620-56621). Grade unchanged. Test record for his father: a Hans Olsen's origin in the 1645 roll or the 1610s-1620s Lunde land lists named in the bygdebok. ¶4

Vicar's census 1666 (23 May), Tjugum sogn N.14: 'Lunde... Hans bruger 54 aar', sons 'Ole 17 aar', 'Johannes 5 aar'. Vicar's 1664 list: 'Lunde - Hans - 14 Ole Hansen'. Bailiff's census 21 Jun 1664: 'Lunde, Hans' with farmer's sons '32: Hans' and '12: Olluf', husmann Torsten Erichsen 34. Birth about 1612 from the 1666 age. ¶1

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
