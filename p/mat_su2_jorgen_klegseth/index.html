About the family

Brother of Ingeborg Olsdatter Klegseth, wife of Ole G. Slind, and therefore Ole's brother-in-law. He followed the same chain to the Selbu colony in Whitman County, Washington. ¶1

Life story

Burial: Selbu Cemetery, Whitman County - "Jorgen O Klegseth, b. Jul 15 1900 d. Jan 25 1945", reference hrp-cem-whi-selbu_0086, immediately beside his wife at _0085. ¶3

He married a Slind: his widow is buried as "Christine Slind Klegseth, b. Jan 07 1905 d. Jul 30 2002". So one Klegseth sister married into the Slind family and one Klegseth brother married out of it into the same family - see mat_su2_christine_slind. ¶4

Death: 25 January 1945 at Colfax, Whitman County, aged 44. Washington DOH death certificate, DA reference {8F0E7EF8-F608-4738-AD7C-731E2638E4D0}, image 342, document 26, doc. ref. id 13. Father: Ole J. Klegseth. Mother: Johanna Klegseth. Spouse: Christine Klegseth. ¶2
