About the family

NEW ANCESTOR, graded on his link to his son Ole Ormsen (see his page). The 1666 vicar's census of Omblie (Åmli) sogn (DA 35510 view 30, p. 38, read on the scan at zoom) lists under no. 17 'Oppaland Øffre er 2 huder' (Øvre Eppeland) the users 'Orm, er 36 aar, bruger ½ hud', Niels 26 (½ hud), Lauritz 40, Aanon 62 (son Olle 16) and Anders 41 (4 kalvskinn each); servant Joen Tollefsøn 13; husmann Oluf Ormsøn 68. Orm had no son aged 12 or more listed, which fits a man who married in the 1660s. His patronymic comes from the bailiff's list of men under 50 (1664-65, DA 35567 view 25, p. 12, read on the scan at zoom): 'Øffre Eppeland: Knud Olsøn 40 aar, Orm Olsøn 34 aar'. So he was Orm Olsen, born about 1630-31, and Knud Olsen (b. about 1624) on the same farm was probably his brother (not stated). Ole Ormsen in turn named a son Orm Olsen (card 2375, 1742), which fits the naming pattern. ¶1

Life story

Bygdebok corroboration (Åmli ætt og heim V, Eppeland gnr. 65, canvases 209-211): Orm [Olson] Eppeland f. (1630) d. (1686), son of Olav Ormson (from Askland, at Øvre Eppeland from 1626, f. 1598), g.m. Kirsti [?] Nedre Eppeland; sons Aslak and Olav (Ole) f. (1667?) g.m. Anne [?] Rostveit. In 1670 'Orm og Nils' are named on the bruk; Orm owed Jon Tveit 40 [rdl] from 1667; the debt was settled by Ole Ormsen in 1713. Father added: Olav Ormsen (Probable). His own grade on Ole is unchanged. ¶4

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the identification · 1 alternative set aside · 1 open question

The case for this identification 1

Why he is taken as Ole Ormsen's father: Ole Ormsen, born about 1670, was later lagrettemann at Øvre Eppeland ('Ole Ormssøn øvre Eppeland', card 2375, 1742), and his brother was Aslak Ormsen (card 2379, 1747). Orm is the only user named Orm on Øvre Eppeland in 1666, and the right age (b. about 1630) to be the father of sons born about 1670. The census names no relationship and Ole is first seen at Eppeland decades later, so this is patronymic plus farm: Probable. The husmann Oluf Ormsøn, 68 (b. about 1598), on the same farm may be a relative (an uncle?) but is not a candidate father of Ole, since Ole's patronymic is Ormsen. ¶2a

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Rival: none named; no other Orm in Åmli parish has been found in 1666 (the Gjøvdal Orm of Nordre Askland is Orm). ¶2b
