Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Graded Probable on his link to his daughter Anne Hermundsdatter (see her page). (1) The probate after Anne's first husband Kiel Østensen at Nødeskreen (Nauteskreen), 23 Sep 1754 (Ytre Sogn 7a fol. 240b, No. 67), names as the widow's guardian 'hendes Broder Ole Hermundsen Nummedale' (her brother; Nummedal is in Vik skipreide) and has among the creditors 'Agaathe Hermundsdatter'. (2) The probate after Hermund Olsen at Gulteigen, Vik skipreide, 14 Aug 1743 (6a fol. 324a, No. 73) names his seven children: sons Siur, Ole and Anders, daughters Agaatha, Anna, Sigri (18) and Ingerid. So a brother Ole, a sister Agaatha and Anne herself all appear among Hermund Olsen's children, in the same skipreide that Ole Hermundsen lived in. Her second son was named Hermund (b. about 1745). The Balestrand bygdebok (1985, c246) says Kjell's wife Anna Hermundsdtr. was 'syster [til] Ola [...] Vik'. The rival Hermund probates in Vik (Endresen at Refsdal 1743, Endresen at Vikøren 1743, Pedersen at Vikøren 1761) have no son Ole. Why only Probable: the link is inferred from a matching set of siblings' names, and the identification spans farms (Gulteigen 1743, Nummedal 1754). ¶1a

Upgrade: graded on his link to his daughter Anne Hermundsdatter (see her page), Probable -> Confirmed. Most direct record: the probate after his widow Eli Olsdatter at Gulteigen, 1 May 1759 (Ytre Sogn 7b fol. 436a, No. 11, 24164 view 101), held between the children she had 'med den forhen afdøde Mand Hermund Olsen', among them 'Ane Hermundsdatter [Nød]schreen myndig'. Also, the test record named in his notes (Agaatha's or Siur's probate) was: Agaathe Hermundsdatter's probate at Nedre Hove, Vik, 13-14 Sep 1771 (Ytre Sogn 8b fol. 720b, No. 81, 24166 view 341) names 'Søsteren Anna Hermundsdatter afg. Giest Guttormsen Nødschreens [Enke]' together with the brothers Siur (dead), Ole Hermundsen Store Nummedal and Anders Hermundsen Rislaug; and Siur Hermundsen's probate at Rislaug, 18 May 1746 (6b fol. 494a, 24162 view 142) names 'Farbroderen Ole Hermundsen Gulteig'. Continuity, how the 1743 daughter Anna is followed to the tree's Anne: (1) 14 Aug 1743, Gulteigen: Hermund Olsen's probate names the widow and 'deris sammen avlede 7 Børn', sons Siur, Ole, Anders and daughters Agaatha, Anna, Sigri, Ingerid. (2) 18 May 1746, Rislaug: Siur Hermundsen's probate names as his children's guardian 'Farbroderen Ole Hermundsen Gulteig' (Siur's brother, still at Gulteigen), with Agathe Hermundsdatter a creditor. (3) 23 Sep 1754, Nødeskreen: Kiel Østensen's widow Anne Hermundsdatter has as guardian 'hendes Broder Ole Hermundsen Nummedale'. (4) 18 Sep 1761, Nauteskreen: Giest Guttormsen's widow Anne Hermundsdatter and their son Giest Giestsen, 1/2 year. (4b) 1 May 1759, Gulteigen: the probate after the widow Eli Olsdatter names her and her late husband Hermund Olsen's children: Siur (dead; his son Ole Siursen at Gulteigen), Ole Hermundsen Store Nummedal, Anders Hermundsen, Agaathe Hermundsdatter (married at Hove), 'Ane Hermundsdatter [Nød]schreen myndig' and two younger daughters: this names the daughter Ane at Nødeskreen, between Kiel's probate (1754, Anne at Nødeskreen) and her second marriage (1759) and widowhood (1761, Nauteskreen): gaps of 5 and 2 years, same place. (5) 13-14 Sep 1771, Nedre Hove: the probate after Agaathe Hermundsdatter names as her heirs the late brother Siur Hermundsen Rislaug's children (the son Ole Siursen now at Gulteigen), the brother Ole Hermundsen Store Nummedal, the brother Anders Hermundsen Rislaug and 'Søsteren Anna Hermundsdatter afg. Giest Guttormsen Nødschreens [Enke]'. This 1771 record names Anne, widow of the tree's Gjest Guttormsen of Nauteskreen, as the sister of Siur, Ole, Anders and Agaathe, the same four siblings who with Anna are Hermund Olsen's children in 1743, and Siur's son is on Gulteigen. The bridge 1743-1771 is a sibling set of five named people found together at both ends, with a record in between (1746), so it is not a single-name match (audit 8 rule 3). ¶2a

Alternatives considered and set aside 2

Caution: the Gulteigen Anna was still unmarried and under guardianship in Aug 1743, while Kjell's eldest son Anfind is read as about 11 in Sep 1754 (b. about 1743); that fits only if Anfind was born in 1744 and his age was rounded up. ¶1b

No rival: no other Hermund in the Ytre Sogn register has children Siur, Ole, Anders, Agaatha and Anna (rival Vik Hermund probates 1743 and 1761 have no son Ole). Sigri and Ingerid are absent in 1771, presumably dead without issue; this does not affect the link. The earlier caution (Anna unmarried in Aug 1743 while Kiel's eldest son Anfind was read as about 11 in Sep 1754) concerns only whether Anfind was Anne's own son; the 1771 record ties Gjest Guttormsen's widow to this family directly, so the caution no longer bears on her parentage. (Daughters were routinely listed 'umyndig' under guardianship whatever their age.) ¶2b

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Test record: Ole Hermundsen Nummedal's own probate (9b p. 1319, 1781) names only his own children, so it does not test this; Agaatha Hermundsdatter's or Siur Hermundsen's probate, or the Vik ministerialbok for Kjell Østensen's marriage c.1743, would. ¶1c
