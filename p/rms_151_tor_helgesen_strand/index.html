About the family

The children's 'Bestefaderen Tor Røland' (grandfather) appears again among the debts (s. 336a, view 340): 'Tor Røland fordrer som arfvingernes Udgiftspenge 4 rdr'. Which side he is on is still not stated. The eldest son is named Tor, which fits either a paternal grandfather Tor (Helge would then be a Torsen) or a maternal one. The large mother's inheritance makes a well-off maternal family likely, but this is unproved. The guardians' section (after s. 337) was not reached. ¶5a

Mother found (forename unknown): the 1707 probate after his father calls her 'Børnenes afgangne Moder' and appoints for 'den Eldste Søn Thor Helgesen dend dannemand Morfaderen Tor Rølang' (DA 25284 s. 338a, view 343); see Torsdatter and Tor Rølang. His own grade is unchanged. ¶6

Life story

Born about 1692 from the 1707 probate after his father (eldest son 'Thor Helgesen 15 aar', Feb 1707). Died before 6 Oct 1722 (opening of his probate). ¶3

Division of his father's estate read (DA 25284 s. 336b, view 341, middle column): the net estate of about 5083 rdr is the heirs' 'Mødrene arf' (mother's inheritance), which had stood under the father's guardianship. Each of the 3 sons gets 1452 rdr and the daughter 726 rdr. 'Thor Helgesøn udi Fædrene og Mødrene arf for sin broderlod 1452 rdr og 2 ort, der til udlagt. Hvorved bekom Sørstrand, efter for hans i Skifte Retten fremviste adkomst...' (the reading of 'bekom Sørstrand' is fairly secure but not certain). So Tor took over Søndre Strand in 1707, the farm where he died in 1722. This is further continuity between the 1707 heir and the 1722 decedent. ¶4

Probate after him, Sør-Strand, Valdres card 13727 (Hadeland, Land og Valdres skifteprotokoll 1716-1737, fol. 559a, 1722): 'Tor Helgesen +, g. Ingebor Jonsdtr.; Barn: 1. Helge 14 aar, 2. Ingebor 8'. Protocol read (DA 25285 fol. 558b, view 571, lower third column, onto 559a): the court met 'paa Sørstrand udi Aurdahls Præstegield i Walders' on 6 Oct 1722 to hold 'Skifte og Deeling efter afgangne S[alig] Mand Tor Helgesen... imellem hans efterlatte og nu igienlevende Egte quinde Jngebor Joensdatter og begge deris Sammen Auflede og efter sig ladende 2de Børn, hvoraf er j Søn og j Daatter, Sønnen ved Navn Helge Torsen 14 aar gl., Daatteren Jngebor Torsdaatter 8te aar gl.'; present for the heirs 'dend afdøde S: Mands Brødre Giul Helgesen Skafwelden og Ole Hel[gesen]' (wording partly blotted; the names and ages are clear). ¶1

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Upgrade:, new person entered as Confirmed, graded on his link to his son Hølje (Helge) Tordsen (see his page). The 1722 probate names 'Sønnen... Helge Torsen 14 aar' (born about 1708) as his son. Continuity: the next record of the son is his marriage in Trinity 1728, 'Helje Thords. Sønderstrand' (DA 9360 p. 776), six years later on the same farm (Sønderstrand = Sør-Strand), and his 1729 child at Sønderstrand; his own probate of 1785-86 at 'Strande' (DA 25288 fol. 148b) calls him Helge Tordsen. No other Helge Tordsen is named on any Strand farm in 1722-1730 (the other Helge on Strand, Helge Knudsen of Midtstrand, has a different patronymic). Age 14 in Oct 1722 fits Hølje's birth about 1708-09. ¶2

Audit 12 (2 Nov 2026): Confirmed KEPT (1722 probate names 'Sønnen... Helge Torsen 14 aar', 'begge deris Sammen Auflede'; crop checked; 1728 marriage on the same farm six years later; no rival). Test record (added; every new person needs one): the 1728-1730 Aurdal baptisms or betrothals (DA 9360) naming 'Helje Thors.' with his mother Ingebor Jonsdatter or sister Ingebor Torsdatter as sponsor, or a Sør-Strand deed of about 1728-35 in the Valdres tingbok by which Helge Torsen took over the farm from his mother; either would show the 1722 heir and the 1728 groom as one man in a record. ¶7
