About the family

Daughter of Ole Gundersen Slind and Ingeborg Olsdatter, born 2 October 1916 in Selbu. In the 1920 census of her father's farm at Kjosnes sondre; emigrated with the family on the Stavangerfjord on 7 June 1922, aged five. ¶2

Life story

Emigrated with her parents and five siblings, Trondheim 7 June 1922 on the Stavangerfjord: emigrant protocol no. 15, page 140, line 32, group 00883, 'barn', residence Selbu. ¶1

Marriage: MAURICE E. BRYANT, 12 DECEMBER 1942, Spokane County, Washington - Spokane County Auditor, Marriage Records 1880-2026, document easpmca62233. He was a physician and a captain in the Army Air Corps during the war. ¶3

Her only known child died with her generation: FRANK ELBERT BRYANT, b. 17 November 1943, d. 18 November 1943, one day old, buried in the same yard ("Frank Elbert Bryant, b. Nov 17 1943 d. Nov 18 1943"). Her husband MAURICE BRYANT MD, b. 1912 d. 1997, is buried there too - he never left the colony although she had been dead fifty-one years. ¶5

SHE DIED IN 1946, AGED THIRTY. Her stone at Selbu Cemetery, Whitman County reads "Johanna Slind Bryant, b. 1916 d. 1946, s/by Maurice MD" (reference hrp-cem-whi-selbu_0022) - buried under her maiden name as a middle name, beside the plot kept for her husband. ¶4

Corrections 1

Correction to a possible confusion: a "Johanna Kristine Slind" married Noel Thanner Vaughn in Whitman County on 20 June 1970 (document eawhmc5720). That cannot be this woman, who died in 1946. The double forename combines both of Ole's daughters' names, so the 1970 bride is probably a granddaughter of Ole G. Slind. ¶6
