About the family

Parentage (Speculative): an 'Elisabet Susanna', daughter of Peder Olsen of 'Schiong' (Skjong), was baptized 16 Feb 1789 at Borgund. That matches her residence in 1813 and roughly matches her age. However, she is not in Peder Olesen's 1801 household at Sjaang. Another candidate is a Lisbeth Pedersdatter, born about 1789, a widow's daughter at Alnæs in 1801. ¶4

Parentage upgraded to Probable. 'Lisbeth Susanna Pedersdtr.' of Sjaang was confirmed 13 Oct 1805 at Borgund. That links the 'Elisabet Susanna' baptized 16 Feb 1789 (father Peder Olsen of Schiong) to the Elisabeth Pedersdatter of Sjaang who married in 1813. Her sister Mali (baptized 1782) is probably the Malene Pedersdatter living in her household at Valderøy in 1815. Date given is the baptism date. ¶5

Parentage now proved by a primary record (scan read; audited). The probate of Peder Olsen and Christiane Knudsdatter of Skjong, 15 Sep 1821 (Nordre Sunnmøre 3A 13 s. 120a), lists among their heirs 'Datteren Lisbeth Pedersdatter gift med Jon[?] Jørgensen Valderøen' (the daughter Lisbeth Pedersdatter, married to Jon Jørgensen of Valderøy). Her sisters Kanutha (widow of Lars Olsen Skjong) and Malene (unmarried) were the other heirs. Malene matches the Malene Pedersdatter in her household in 1815. This explicit naming justifies the Confirmed confidence on her link to Peder Olsen Skjong and Christiana Knudsdatter, and supersedes the earlier 'Probable' parentage notes (1789 baptism plus 1805 confirmation). ¶8

Life story

Married John Jørgensen 17 Jan 1813 at Borgund. She was unmarried and living at 'Sjaang' (Skjong farm, Borgund). ¶2

Age 24 in the 1815 census. Died 3 Jul 1835 at Valderhaug, age 45 (born about 1790); listed as 'Kaarkone'. ¶3

Widowed 25 Oct 1824. In 1825 'Elisabeth Pedersd., enke, Walderøen' married Ole Leenert Gammelsen, a bachelor of Bjørge, at Borgund. That explains why she is called 'kaarkone' (wife, not widow) at her burial in 1835. ¶6

Probate: as Lisbeth Pedersdatter she was the widow and heir in the estate of her husband John Jørgensen of Valderhaug, Valderøy, registered 10 December 1824 (Nordre Sunnmøre sorenskriveri, 3A 14, fol. 162a-163b). Her sons Peter Jørgen Martinus (10), Elias Peter Olaus (8) and Georg (Jørgen) Olaus (4) were the other heirs, with the guardians (formyndere) Mons Rasmussen Walderhaug, Lars[?] Hansen Skjong and Ole Siversen[?] Walderhaug. At the 1825 settlement the net estate came to about 95 speciedaler; she took half, and each son 15 speciedaler. ¶7

Named as mother on son Elias's 1832 confirmation. ¶1
