Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Graded on her link to her daughter Anne Hermundsdatter (see her page). Her own probate, 1 May 1759 at Gulteigen (Ytre Sogn 7b fol. 436a, No. 11, 24164 view 101), after 'afdøde Kone Eli Olsdatter', is held between her children with 'den forhen afdøde Mand Hermund Olsen', among them 'Ane Hermundsdatter [Nød]schreen myndig': it names Anne as her daughter at Nødeskreen, the farm of Anne's first and second husbands. The bygdebok gives her 1681-1759. Also, the probate after her husband Hermund Olsen at Gulteigen, Vik skipreide, 14 Aug 1743 (Ytre Sogn 6a fol. 324a, No. 73) is held 'imellem Enken Ely Ols-Datter og deris sammen avlede 7 Børn', naming the daughter Anna among them, so it names her as the mother (audit 8 rule 2 wording 'deris sammen avlede'). Name reading: the first name is written 'Ely' (or 'Ell[i]'), read from; earlier passes left it unread. Her son Siur named a daughter Eli (b. about 1737-40, probate 1746), which supports the reading. Continuity, how the 1743 daughter Anna is followed to the tree's Anne: (1) 14 Aug 1743, Gulteigen: Hermund Olsen's probate names the widow and 'deris sammen avlede 7 Børn', sons Siur, Ole, Anders and daughters Agaatha, Anna, Sigri, Ingerid. (2) 18 May 1746, Rislaug: Siur Hermundsen's probate names as his children's guardian 'Farbroderen Ole Hermundsen Gulteig' (Siur's brother, still at Gulteigen), with Agathe Hermundsdatter a creditor. (3) 23 Sep 1754, Nødeskreen: Kiel Østensen's widow Anne Hermundsdatter has as guardian 'hendes Broder Ole Hermundsen Nummedale'. (4) 18 Sep 1761, Nauteskreen: Giest Guttormsen's widow Anne Hermundsdatter and their son Giest Giestsen, 1/2 year. (4b) 1 May 1759, Gulteigen: the probate after the widow Eli Olsdatter names her and her late husband Hermund Olsen's children: Siur (dead; his son Ole Siursen at Gulteigen), Ole Hermundsen Store Nummedal, Anders Hermundsen, Agaathe Hermundsdatter (married at Hove), 'Ane Hermundsdatter [Nød]schreen myndig' and two younger daughters: this names the daughter Ane at Nødeskreen, between Kiel's probate (1754, Anne at Nødeskreen) and her second marriage (1759) and widowhood (1761, Nauteskreen): gaps of 5 and 2 years, same place. (5) 13-14 Sep 1771, Nedre Hove: the probate after Agaathe Hermundsdatter names as her heirs the late brother Siur Hermundsen Rislaug's children (the son Ole Siursen now at Gulteigen), the brother Ole Hermundsen Store Nummedal, the brother Anders Hermundsen Rislaug and 'Søsteren Anna Hermundsdatter afg. Giest Guttormsen Nødschreens [Enke]'. This 1771 record names Anne, widow of the tree's Gjest Guttormsen of Nauteskreen, as the sister of Siur, Ole, Anders and Agaathe, the same four siblings who with Anna are Hermund Olsen's children in 1743, and Siur's son is on Gulteigen. The bridge 1743-1771 is a sibling set of five named people found together at both ends, with a record in between (1746), so it is not a single-name match (audit 8 rule 3). ¶1a

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

No rival: no other Hermund in the Ytre Sogn register has children Siur, Ole, Anders, Agaatha and Anna (rival Vik Hermund probates 1743 and 1761 have no son Ole). Sigri and Ingerid are absent in 1771, presumably dead without issue; this does not affect the link. The earlier caution (Anna unmarried in Aug 1743 while Kiel's eldest son Anfind was read as about 11 in Sep 1754) concerns only whether Anfind was Anne's own son; the 1771 record ties Gjest Guttormsen's widow to this family directly, so the caution no longer bears on her parentage. (Daughters were routinely listed 'umyndig' under guardianship whatever their age.) ¶1b
