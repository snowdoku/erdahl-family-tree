About the family

Son of Jacob Isaak Sr. and Rosebud Hayter, born at American Falls, Idaho, on 7 April 1930 and named Rex a year after his uncle Rex Hayter's death in British Columbia. He married Ethelene Mae Ransom (1929-2018), lost an infant son, David Henry Isaak, in 1957, and died at Hagerman, Idaho, on 16 December 1989 (Find a Grave). ¶1
