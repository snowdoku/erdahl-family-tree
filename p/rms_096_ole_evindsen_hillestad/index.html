About the family

NEW ANCESTOR. Father of Ingeborg Olsdatter, wife of Svend Evensen of Nedre Ytre Ramse. Graded on his link to Ingeborg Olsdatter Nedre Ytre Ramse: Probable. ¶1

Life story

The skifte after his son Ole Olsøn Hillestad (Østre Råbyggelag Skifteprotokoll nr. 3 fol. 269b, 4 Mar 1712; probate cards 4991-5001, read on the images) refers to 'skiftebrev etter avl. Ole Olsøns far, Ole Evindsøn Hillestad, dat. 2/3 -1695' (card 4993), to the deceased's mother Birgitha Nielsdatter (skiftebrev 12/6 1699, card 5001), and to a 1681 deed to 'avl. Ole Olsøns far, Ole Evindsøn Hillestad' for land in Haugen under Hillestad 'som Ole Evindsøns mor, Wilborrig Olufsdaatter, hadde ått' (card 4995). So Ole Evindsen of Hillestad died before 2 Mar 1695, and his son Ole Olsen died in 1712. ¶2

1661 odelsjordebok (DA 38723 view 6 crop): 'Olle Effuindsen Ouland' gave in odel 'paa sin och sin faders vegne' (2 k.s. in a farm in Bratsberg len, 7 k.s. in Lunde in Nisdal), and 'paa sin egen vegne' ½ hud in Risland in Åmli. This fits this man better than a son of Even Ramse: his father Evind Hillestad was alive in 1661 (he is owner and user at Hillestad in the 1661 land register, DA 38714 view 63), and the Tovdal bygdebok (Hillestad chapter, canvas 210, word positions) puts his wife Birgit at Risland. If so, he lived at Oland in Tovdal in 1661 before taking over Hillestad, and held Risland land on his own (perhaps his wife's) account. ¶8a

Tovdal bygdebok (Hillestad chapter, canvases 207-210, word positions only): Olav Eivindson took over Hillestad after his brother Kjetil, who was dead by 1664 by the Åmli priest's statement; he served some years as lensmann in Tovdal; he married twice (the second wife a Birgit, probably from Risland); and his son Olav bought out the other heirs. The 1655-56 odelsjordebok entry '[?G]ulluff Hillestad eyer i Tved som hand nu selff bruger' (DA 76715 view 24) is probably this man. ¶5

Skifte protocol read in full (Skp. 3 fol. 271b-272, DA 24351 view 273, section 'Faste Oddels Jordegods'): the land is set out from 'skiftebrev effter salig Ole Hillestads fader... Ole Evindsøn Hillestad... dateret 2 Martij Anno 1695', and odel shares in Hillestad are laid out to the deceased's sisters 'Wilborrig Olsdatter' and 'Guroe Olsdatter', called 'disse tvende Søstre', whose husbands are Gunder Qvæste and Knud Løland. Ingeborg Olsdatter is NOT named anywhere in the protocol entry (fol. 269b-272). So the archivist's added line on card 4991 ('Ingeborg Olsdtr. g.m. Svend Evensen Ramse, se sk. 1712') is the card writer's inference, and her link to this man still rests on the cousin guardian 'syskinbarnet, Evind Svendsøn, nedre yttre Ramse' and the bygdebok. Still Probable on his link to Ingeborg Olsdatter Nedre Ytre Ramse. Her absence from the odel division would fit a daughter already paid out (for example at the 1695 skifte) or a half-sister by another mother; it does not count against the link. ¶6

1664 bailiff's census (DA 35567 view 28, p.19, read on the scan): at Hillestad the men under 50 are 'Olle 32 aar', Lauridtz 32 and Michel 25; in the sons/servants column 'Jacob Effuindsøn 16'. Olle, 32, is very probably this Ole Evindsen (b. about 1632), now holding Hillestad after his father; birth changed from BET 1615 AND 1640 to ABT 1632 on that basis (identification by name and farm only). The vicar's census of the same year (DA 35510 view 17, p.13) does not list him among the Hillestad opsiddere (Lauritz 37, Michell 24, Signi Kiedilsdatter, Sigri Torgeirsdatter; sons column 'Jacob Effuindsøn 20'), so his residence in 1664 rests on the bailiff's list alone. ¶7

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the grade · 1 on the identification · 1 search with no result · 2 open questions

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Probable, not Confirmed: the sister entry for Ingeborg on card 4991 is a later addition in blue ink, not part of the original heir list, and in the protocol heir paragraph on fol. 269b (read on the scan) only the children Thore 20, Ole 17, Kiddel, Tharan Olsdatter (m. Joen Næresøn Tveit, Lille Topdal) and Walborre Olsdatter (m. Ole Olsøn Tveit, Treungen) are named. The cousin guardian is a relationship word in the record, but it does not say through which parent. ¶4a

The case for this identification 1

Why Ingeborg is his daughter: (1) in the same 1712 skifte the guardian named for the deceased's son Ole Olsøn is 'syskinbarnet, Evind Svendsøn, nedre yttre Ramse' (card 4993), i.e. a first cousin of the children. Evind Svendsen is Ingeborg Olsdatter's son (see his page); a cousin relationship through his mother Ingeborg Olsdatter, sister of the deceased Ole Olsen, fits her patronymic exactly, whereas his father Svend Evensen (mother Anna Evindsdatter) cannot be a full brother of the widow Ingeborg Thoresdatter. (2) The archivist added to card 4991, under the deceased's sisters Wilborrig Olsdatter (m. Gunder Qvæsta, Valle) and Guroe Olsdatter (m. Knud Sigursøn Løeland, Valle): 'Ingeborg Olsdtr. g.m. Svend Evensen Ramse, se sk. 1712', i.e. a third sister, wife of Svend Evensen Ramse, cross-referenced to her own 1712 skifte. (3) The Tovdal bygdebok (Åmli: ætt og heim I, 1977, Hillestad chapter, canvas 210, read through word positions only) lists Olav Eivindson Hillestad's children as Olav, Ingeborg and Birgit, with Ingeborg married to an Eivindson of Ytre Ramse and Birgit at Tveit, and calls 'Ingeborg Ramse' a sister of the younger Olav. ¶3
