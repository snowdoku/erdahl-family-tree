About the family

Children: Ingeborg, baptised 20 Jan 1805, whose baptism names her as 'Kari Johanness Dr' of Assem. ¶3

Parents: Probable. She married from Tynjum, and the 1801 census lists one Kari Johannesdatter at Tynning, a daughter of Johannes Jenssen and Martha Johannesdatter. Her baptism (c.1778, Gulen book 1776-1787) or her burial with age would confirm it. A different Kari Johannesdatter, 34, unmarried, sister of Hermund Johannessen, lived at Asheim (Asseim, household 04) in 1801; the 1804 bride's residence, Tynjum, points to the Tynning daughter rather than to her. ¶4

Parents (update): The baptism names her father, Johannes Jensen of Tynning, matching the 1801 householder Johannes Jenssen, so the father link now rests on a primary record. Her mother is still identified only by the 1801 census (Martha Johannesdatter, with Kari as 'felleds datter'). The godmother 'Martha Johannes D. i Tynning' has the same name as the 1801 wife, but a mother did not stand sponsor at her own child's baptism and was not yet churched, so this is probably another Martha of Tynning and is not read as naming the mother. ¶7

Life story

Baptism: Baptised 15 Dec 1779 as 'Johannes Jenssen Tynnings D[atter] N[omine] Kari', daughter of Johannes Jensen of Tynning. Godparents: Lasse Olsen, Tor Lassesen, Ragnvald Jensen and Martha Johannesdatter, all of Tynning, and Ole Olsen of Engesetter. The mother is not named; her churching at Brekke on 1 Jan 1780 calls her only 'Johannes Jenssen Tynnings Hustrue'. Gulen ministerialbok 1776-1787, folio 35b. ¶5

1801 census: Kari Johannesdatter, 23, unmarried, joint daughter (felleds datter) of Johannes Jenssen, 56, skattebonde, and Martha Johannesdatter, 53, at Tynning (Tynjum), Brekke, with sisters Brite 24, Johanna 20, Orloug 18, Barbra 15, Martha 12 and Jørnille 8. Birth about 1778 is inferred from her age. ¶1

Marriage: Engaged 2 Jun 1804 and married 1 Jul 1804 at Brekke church, as Kari Johannesdatter, pige, of Tynjum, to Mons Johannessøn of Asseim (Gulen church book 1798-1808, marriage index). ¶2
