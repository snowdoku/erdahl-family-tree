About the family

Children: Brithe (~1834), Marte (~1837), Jørgen (~1839, took over Erdal), Samuel (1844), two baptized 1846 and 1848, Lasse (1850), Anne (1857). ¶3

Parentage confirmed: the probate after his mother Brite Olsdatter of Erdal (Nordfjord skifteprotokoll A 24, fol. 188b, registered 13 Oct 1834) names 'Sønnen Lasse Jørgensen Erdal' first among the five children and heirs of Jørgen Lassesen Erdal and Brite Olsdatter. At the division of 18-20 May 1835 he took a son's double share (30-2-10), and he acted as guardian for his unmarried sister Ingeborg. This replaces the earlier reasoning that he was 'the only Lasse baptized at Erdal in 1809'; his links to Jørgen Lassesen Erdal and Britha Olsdatter are now Confirmed by this record. ¶5

Life story

Baptized 7 Apr 1809, Innvik. Owner-farmer of Erdal in the 1865 census; died a widower on føderåd (retirement). ¶1

Married Marthe Samuelsdatter 9 Jun 1833 at Oppstryn. ¶2

Aaland 1932: Aaland's Erdal history (bruk nr. 4): 'S. Lasse, † 75 a. g. 1884, sk. 1840. * Marte Samuelsd. Loen, † 49 a. g. 1858': he died in 1884 aged 75, took the deed in 1840, and married Marte Samuelsdatter of Loen, who died in 1858 aged 49. All of this agrees with the tree. His son Jørgen (died 1921 aged 82, deed 1869) followed him on the holding. ¶6
