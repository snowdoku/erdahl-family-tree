About the family

NEW ANCESTOR, graded on his link to his son Tallak Ormsen (see his page). Østre Råbyggelag probate cards (DA 39942), Øvre Skjeggedal: the archivist's back of card 12321 (for Gro Mikkelsdatter's probate, Skp. 5 fol. 118, 1717) gives 'Syster til Thallak Ormssøn: Helge Skeiggedal. Far... Orm Aanonsen Tveitli + sk. 28/1-1699', and cites a deed, 'skiftekontrakt mellom Thallak Ormssøn og syskina hans, dat. 28/1-1699'. Card 12324 (back of Salve Ormsøn's probate, Skp. 5 fol. 178, 27 Sep 1717) gives 'Avd.s far: Orm Aanonsen Tveitli + sk. 28/1 1699' and 'Syster: Anna Ormsdtr.', and cites 'skiftekontrakt mellom Orm Tvedeliens born, dat. 28/1-1699'. In that probate (card 12323) Thallak Ormsøn is the children's 'Farbroren', which is a primary relationship word making Tallak and Salve brothers. The bygdebok, Åmli ætt og heim I (Tovdal), canvas 105 (a holding under Øvre Skjeggedal; rebuilt from word boxes, so ABT), agrees: 'Orm Ånonson' was there in 1666; 'Orm var fødd 1626. Då han [fall] frå i 1699 skifte borna seg' with 3 sons and 2 daughters: Salve (eldest), Gunnar (b. 1669, d. 1732) and Tallak, and Helga (m. Torjus Tallakson Skjeggedal) and Anne. Card 12317 names a 'Gunder Ormsøn Tvedlie' (1713). Archivist's notes and a bygdebok only, so Probable. ¶1

Life story

(1) Tallak Ormsen's own probate, Øvre Skjeggedal, 7 Jun 1719 (Skp. 5 fol. 272a-274a, DA 24353 views 279-281, read on the scan), does not name his father or the 1699 division in the pages read; it names a creditor 'Gunder Ormsøn Tvedalie' (Tvedelien) in a matter of land at Tvedelien, and the guardian Asuf Olsøn Løbdal as the children's first cousin on the father's side. The typed back of card 12333 repeats 'Avd's far: Orm Aanonsen Tveitli + sk. 28/1-1699' (archivist's note). Still Probable. (2) The 1699 deed: no Østre Råbyggelag pantebok survives for 1699 (Pantebok A4, DA 17714, begins 1702). The court journal (tingbok) nr. 1b, 1697-1699, is DA 28626 (151 views; view 120 = fol. 113b-114a; the autumn 1699 ting with numbered lists of documents read out (lyst) are at views 128 = fol. 120b-121a and 138 = fol. 130b-131a). Views 122-138 sampled, no Tvedelien entry seen. ¶3a

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
