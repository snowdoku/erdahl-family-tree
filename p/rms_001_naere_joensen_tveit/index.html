About the family

His wife's 1700 estate (Skp. 1 fol. 183b) lists their children Joen; Gunsteen (later Skjeggedal, estate 17 Mar 1758); 'Olle (senere Austenaa i Tovdal)'; Asbør (m. Ole Tellefsen Harstveit); Anna; and Ingebør (later m. Salve Svendsen Askland, d. 1754). The estate held 2 huder of odel land in Tveit. ¶2

Father of Ole Næresen (Probable): see Ole's notes. Gunsten Næresen's 1758 estate names his brother 'Ole Næresen Austenaa i Tovdal', and Ole Næresen of Oland had lived at Austenaa after marrying Ulvild, the widow of Michel Christensen there. The archivist's note on Ole's 1761 card instead gives Mære Børulsen Smedland as his father, so this is Probable, not Confirmed. ¶3

Life story

Probate (index card): the estate of Nære Joensøn of Tveit in (Lille) Tovdal was held 19 Mar 1720 (Østre Råbyggelag skifteprotokoll nr. 5, fol. 300). Heirs: Joen Næresøn on Tveit (his estate 22 Apr 1740), Gunsteen on nedre Skjeggedal, Ole (no farm named) and Ingeborg, unmarried; two daughters had died before him. The card refers back to the estate of his late wife Gunild Gunsteensdatter, 9 Jun 1700. ¶1

Protocol check: the 1758 estate of his son Gunsten Næresen (Skp. 12b fol. 374b) names the deceased's brothers Joen Nærresøn Tved (dead) and 'Ole Nærresøn Østenaae', and the sister Ingeborg Nærresdatter (m. Salve Svendsøn Askland). This confirms from the protocol that a son Ole lived at Østenaa in 1758. Whether he is the Ole Næresen who died at Oland by 9 Feb 1761 is still Probable: the 1761 entry (fol. 439a), as read, names no Tveit kin. ¶4

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
