Life story

Probate 1702: 'Anno 1702 den 23 Junij' the sorenskriver Peder Pedersen and two lagrettemenn met 'paa Steensland udi Tordal' to register and divide the estate 'efter afgangne Torbiørn Tollefsen Steensland' between his surviving wife Boel Jensdatter and their child Sifver Torbiørnsdatter. Before that division, because 'forn. Torbiørn Tollefsen tilforn været gift med Ingeri Erichsdatter' (had earlier been married to Ingeri Erichsdatter), with whom he had '1 barn, Nafnlig Anna Torbiørnsdatter', her inheritance after her mother was settled, 'og paa barnets vegne hendes beste faderen [grandfather] Tollef Steensland' attended (the word read 'beste faderen' is probable; the card index read only 'a phrase naming Tollef Stensland'). Bamble skifteprotokoll I 2, fol. 204b. Crop. ¶1

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the grade · 1 alternative set aside

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Confidence: Probable as father of Anne Torbjørnsdatter (see her page). Identification (Probable): the 1702 Anna Torbjørnsdatter, a minor heir at Steensland, is taken to be Anne Torbjørnsdatter (see her page), wife of Joen Thoresen Steensland in 1762, because the name and farm match, Joen held land at Steensland as an inheritance would explain, and the couple's sons were named Torbjørn and Tollef, after her father and grandfather (naming pattern). ¶2a

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Not Confirmed: the identification spans 1702 to 1762 with no record in between naming her parents, and her own ages are unknown (born before 1702; her youngest child about 1739-42 implies she was about 38-50 then, which is possible but late). ¶2b
