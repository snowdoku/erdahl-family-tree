About the family

Wife of Hermund Nielsen of Bruheld (Bruield). Probate after her on Bruheld, Kvamsøy skipreide, 27 May 1701 (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll A 1b, fol. 329a, Digitalarkivet 24154 viewer 113): between her widower Hermund Nielsen and 'deris tilsammen avlede børn' Andfind, Samson, Gietlof, Elsse, Agaattha, Sara and Sigri, so she was the mother of all seven, including Elsse. Graded Probable on her link to Else Hermundsdatter for the same reason as Hermund (see his page): the identity of Elsse Hermundsdatter of Bruheld (1701) with the wife of Mons Torbersen of Østerbø spans two farms. Supporting: Else's daughter Dordej (Dorothea, in Else's 1740 probate) carries her name. ¶1a

Life story

The step-son 'Niels Hermundsen' was owed his 'Modernearf' (inheritance from his mother) in this estate, so Hermund had been married before; Niels is not Dorethe's child and Else's son Niels may be named after Hermund's father Niels. ¶2

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
