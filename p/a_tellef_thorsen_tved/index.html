About the family

Father of Thor (bapt. Jan 1760, Topdal; 'Tellef Thorsøn og Margitte Olsdatter Tvedts barn Thor'). ¶1

He is named again, as an adult, in the second settlement of his mother's estate on 9 October 1761 (Skifteprotokoll no. 12b, folio 446), which lists "konens barn i 1. ekteskap" — the wife's children by her first marriage to "Tore Tellifsen + sk. 28/4 1740" — as Tellef Toresen the elder, now of age; Niels; Tellef the younger, 22; and Gunder, who had died after his father. ¶7

NEW SIBLINGS FOR THOR: Erich Tellefsen Tveit and Aase Tellefsdatter. Aase was named for her great-grandmother Aase Thorsdatter of Øvre Ramse. ¶10

Brothers: 'den eldre' and 'den yngre' were FULL brothers. Both are listed in 1761 as 'konens barn i 1. ekteskap' (the wife's children by her first marriage), i.e. sons of Torbor Tergiesdatter by Thore Tellefsen, whom she married on 2 July 1720. All five known sons (Tellef 1721, Tellef 1727, Niels 1731, Gunder 1735, Tellef 1739) were born after that marriage. No earlier marriage of Thore is recorded, and no child of one was an heir in 1740. ¶16

Life story

Baptism: on 26 July 1727 in Åmli church: 'Thoer Tellefsön Tveds barn Tellef' (Thor Tellefsen Tved's child Tellef), Åmli 1711-1740 p. 85 (canvas 45). The year comes from the page sequence: the next page but one is headed 'Anno 1728'. No godparents are recorded in this book. ¶14

A Gunsten Tellefsen Hillestad, aged 58 (b. ~1742), was buried on the same day as Thor in Apr 1800. He may be another son, though if so he was born before the 1755 betrothal (speculative). ¶3

Betrothed 'paa Ramse' on 4th Sunday in Advent (21 Dec) 1755 as Tellef Thorsøn Tved to Margitte Olsdatter Ramse (Åmli 1741-1775). His father was presumably a Thor, possibly of Tvedt or Ramse (speculative). A 'Thor Ramse's wife' was godmother in 1760. ¶2

He married Margitte Olsdatter of Ramse; they were betrothed at Ramse on the fourth Sunday in Advent, 21 December 1755. He was then about 27. ¶12

Tovdal entries in Åmli 1711-1740: Thor Tellefsen Tved had a son Tellef baptised Dom. 8 p. Trin. 1739 ('Thor Tellefsön Tveds barn Tellef', canvas 80), and in the same summer an elder 'Tellef Thorsön Tved 17½ aar' was buried (Dom. 12 p. Trin. 1739, canvas 80). So the elder Tellef (b. c. 1722) died unmarried, and the only Tellef Thorsen Tved alive after 1739 was born in 1739 — which conflicts with this card's 'ABT 1728' birth estimate. If he is the 1739 boy he married in 1756 aged about 17, which is unusually young; a Tellef son of another Thor at Tved is not excluded. Thor Tellefsen Tved's other children: Niels (lay-baptised Trinity 1731), Gunder (1 p. Trin. 1735). ¶13

DEATH AND CHILDREN ESTABLISHED. The probate of Gunlek Aslaksen of Tveit, running 7 December 1792 to 30 January 1794 (Skifteprotokoll no. 16b, folio 515), lists the heirs on the father's side as "Tremenning: Tellef Thorsen Tveit +. Hans barn: Thor Tellefsen Tveit, Erich [Tellefsen Tveit], Aase Tellefsdtr." — third cousin Tellef Thorsen Tveit, deceased, and his children Thor, Erich and Aase. He was therefore dead by December 1792. This is the first independent confirmation, outside the baptism register, that Thor Tellefsen (1760-1800) was his son, and it adds two previously unknown children. ¶8

The kinship term is itself proof of the pedigree. "Tremenning" means third cousin, and third cousins share great-grandparents. Gunlek Aslaksen's great-grandfather was Tellef Nielsen Tveit (died 1703). For Thor, Erich and Aase to be Gunlek's third cousins, their great-grandfather must have been the same man — which it is, through Thore Tellefsen. The arithmetic of the relationship term independently validates three generations of the reconstruction. ¶9

CORRECTION to the earlier note in this record. The suggestion that his father was "presumably a Thor, possibly of Tvedt or Ramse (speculative)" is now proved correct and specific: Thore Tellefsen of Tveit, died 1740. The separate caution about "Tellef Thorsen (b. ~1738) of Frigstad in Mykland" is also now explained — that man is very probably his own younger brother, Tellef Thorsen "den yngre", born about 1739, who was 22 in October 1761. ¶11

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

PARENTAGE RESOLVED (10 Oct 2026), and confidence raised from Probable to Confirmed. He was the eldest son of Thore (Thor) Tellefsen of Tveit in Tovdal and Torbor Terjesdatter. The probate of his father's estate at "Tvedt" in Tovdal, dated 28 April 1740 (Østre Råbyggelag skifteprotokoll no. 10, folio 45b), lists the children as "Tellew Thoreson d.e., 12 år; Niels, 10; Gunder, 5; Tellew d.y., 1". "D.e." is den eldre, the elder, distinguishing him from his baby brother of the same name. Twelve years old in April 1740 gives a birth year of about 1728. ¶6

The case for this identification 1

Identity of the husband of Margitte Olsdatter: he was this Tellef 'den eldre' (born 1727), not the younger brother. In December 1755 the elder was 28 and the younger 16. The 1761 probate calls the elder 'myndig' (of full legal age, 25 or over) and the younger 22. The 'Tellef Thorsen Tveit', dead by December 1792, whose children were Thor, Erich and Aase, cannot be the younger brother if he is the Tellef Thorsen, aged 63, who was a widowed cottager with land at Frigstad in Mykland in the 1801 census. That identification is Probable: the age fits and no other Tellef Thorsen of that age is known in Åmli. Even without it, a 16-year-old betrothed in 1755 would be very unusual. ¶17

Corrections 1

Correction: the birth estimate stays at 1727-28, and the doubt raised in the previous note is withdrawn. Thor Tellefsen Tved and Torbor Tergiesdatter had THREE sons named Tellef: (1) baptised 17th Sunday after Trinity 1721, buried 12th Sunday after Trinity 1739 'Tellef Thorsön Tved 17½ aar gl.'; (2) this man, baptised 26 July 1727; (3) baptised 8th Sunday after Trinity 1739. At the probate of 28 April 1740 the two survivors were listed as 'Tellew Thoersøn d.e. [den eldre, the elder], 12 år' and 'Tellew d.y. [den yngre, the younger], 1 år'. The 1739 burial was of the 1721 son, not of this man. Two Tellefs were alive together from 1727 to 1739, and again from 1739 until at least October 1761. ¶15

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Not to be confused with Tellef Thorsen (b. ~1738) of Frigstad in Mykland, whose wife Joran Tellefsdatter was buried in 1800. ¶4

Searches that found nothing 1

His death was not found (it falls before 1796, in the unindexed books). ¶5
