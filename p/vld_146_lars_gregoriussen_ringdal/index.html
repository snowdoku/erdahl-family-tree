About the family

Father of Brithe Larsdatter (see her page), Probable; graded on his link to her. The probate names a daughter Brit Larsdatter of Store Ringdal in 1727. 'Brite Larsdtr.' of Rindalen married Knud Olsen of Fivelstad at Sunnylven on 29 Dec 1742 (index pv00000002059244), and 'Brithe Lars Datter' died at Store Ringdal by 26 Jul 1759, wife of Knud Olsen (her probate). Same name, patronymic and farm throughout, but the 1727 record and the 1742 marriage are 15 years apart, the 1727 ages are unread, and no record names Brithe of 1742/1759 as Lars Gregoriussen's daughter, so this stays Probable. Supporting: Brithe's second son was named Lars (b. about 1747). Probate index rows at Store Ringdal for 1745 (Marthe Larsd., wife of Ingebrigt Amundsen) and 1749 (Gjertrud Arnesd.) are a different household: the 1749 skifte (3A 16 fol. 664b, 24557/281, read) is between Ingebrigt Amundsen and his children. ¶2

Life story

1701 manntall (scan read): 'Lauris', 8, son of the tenant Gregorius (53) on Stor Ringdall, Sunnylven (p. 124; pc00000001091848). This gives him a birth year of about 1693 and his father Gregorius (Probable; see Gregorius). His own grade (as Brithe's father) is unchanged. ¶3

Probate (scan read): 'Anno 1727 den 26 Septemb.' a registration, valuation and division was held on the farm 'Store Ringdahl udi Sundulvens Skibrede' after the late 'Lars Gregoriussen', between his widow 'Maret Andersdatter' (with a guardian, Ingebrigt [..] Moe[l]) and 'deres sammenavlede Børn' (their children together): the son Gregorius Larsen (guardian's name partly lost in the gutter) and the daughter 'Brit Larsdatter' (guardian [Tore?] Olsen). Valuers included [..] Pedersen Hellesylt. The children's ages run into the gutter and are not legible. Sunnmøre sorenskriveri skifteprotokoll 3A 09 fol. 787 (Digitalarkivet 24551 view 410, right page). ¶1

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Regrade 2 (1 Nov 2026): upgraded to Confirmed as father of Brithe Larsdatter (see her page); graded on his link to Brithe Larsdatter. Naming record: his probate at Store Ringdal, Sunnylven, 26 Sep 1727 (Sunnmøre skifteprotokoll 3A 09 fol. 787, Digitalarkivet 24551 view 410, scan), held between the widow Maret Andersdatter and 'deres sammenavlede Børn', the son Gregorius Larsen and the daughter 'Brit Larsdatter'. Continuity: 'Brite Larsdtr.' of Rindalen (= Ringdalen; Knud Olsen is 'of Ringdalen' in 1751 and 'Rindalen' in 1775) married Knud Olsen at Sunnylven on 29 Dec 1742, 15 years later, and 'Brithe Lars Datter' died at Store Ringdal by 26 Jul 1759 as Knud's wife. Same name, patronymic and farm, a gap of 15 years, and no named rival: the other Store Ringdal households of 1745/1749 (Ingebrigt Amundsen's) have no Brit Larsdatter. Under the audit-3 detail (about 15 years or less on the same farm with no named rival is continuity) this is Confirmed; held it Probable under the stricter reading. ¶4a
