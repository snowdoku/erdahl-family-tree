About the family

Wife of Rasmus Iversen of Botnen. Age 53 in the 1801 census. Mother of Iver by inference from the census household ('felleds søn', a child of both). ¶1

Parents: Unknown. Lead only, not a link: Joen Bendixsen, 75, kaaremand, with wife Sophie Andersdatter, 74, both in their first marriage, lived in Emanuel Botolfsen's household at Ynnesdal, Brekke, in 1801. His age fits a father of a woman born 1749, but no record connects him to Synneve. Her marriage (about 1778) or baptism (about 1749) in the Gulen books would test it. ¶6

Parents (Confirmed): the probates of 1-2 Oct 1773 at Indre Hjartholm (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll A 9, p. 282 and p. 286) name 'Synneve Jons datter', betrothed to Rasmus Iversen of Botnen, as the eldest daughter of the late Jon Gulløvsen and his widow Borne Nielsdatter. Her full siblings were Gulow, Christj, Kari and Haldis; Thor and Johannes Jonsen were half-brothers, sons of Jon's first wife Kari Iversdatter. Read from the scans. ¶13

Life story

Baptism: Baptised 3 May 1749 at Brekke church as 'Jon Hiertholms B[arn] N[omine] Synneve, Mat[er]: Born[i]', Jon Hjartholm's child named Synneve, mother Borni (the mother's name is a probable reading). Godparents: Iver Botnen (her future father-in-law), Johannes Olsen Hiertholm, Johannes Hansen in Taskle, Athlet[?] in Hiertholm and Agatha Engesetter. Gulen church book 1736-1749, canvases 72-73. ¶7

Marriage: Betrothed 30 May 1773 at Brekke to Rasmus Iversen of Botnen (forlovere Colbein Hiertholm and Johannes Taskle) and married at Brekke on 31 Dec 1773, four months after the burial of Jon Hiertholm. ¶12

1801 census: Synneve Joensdatter, 53, wife of Rasmus Iverssen at Botnen, both married for the first time, with the joint children Bornie 23, Randi 21, Johanne 19, Brite 17, Iver 16, Niels 13 and Lasse 9. ¶4

Burial: Synneve Jons Datter, widow and kårkone (enke og kaarekone: a widow living on a pension from the farm), of Botnen, died 16 Dec 1838 and was buried 21 Dec 1838, aged 89 2/3 years. Gulen church book 1837-1847, burials 1838, p. 286, no. 39 (index; the scan of that page has not been read). ¶2

How sure are we?

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Confidence: Upgraded from Probable to Confirmed. The 1801 census lists her as the wife of Rasmus Iversen and Iver as their joint son (felleds); the 1838 burial confirms the Botnen widow; and Iver's first daughter, baptised 9 Aug 1813, was named Synneve. ¶5

The case for this identification 1

Identity of the 1749 baptism: Probable (strong), not Confirmed, because no record states that the bride of 1773 is the child of 1749 and the 1773 betrothal gives a conflicting patronymic (see the next note). Her farm of origin (Hjartholm, from the 1773 betrothal), her father's name (Jon: 'Joensdatter' in 1801, 'Jons Datter' at her burial), and the birth implied by her burial age all agree, and the godfather Iver Botnen is the father of the man she married. No other Synneve was baptised at Hjartholm between 1741 and Oct 1751 (11442 canvases 30-73 and 11443 canvases 1-20 read). ¶9a

Corrections 4

Correction: The birth moves from 'ABT 1748' to 'ABT APR 1749', from the burial age of 89 2/3 years on 16 Dec 1838. This is consistent with her age of 53 in Feb 1801. An earlier inference that she died before her husband's probate of 18 Nov 1837 is wrong: she outlived him by over a year. The probate names no widow because, as a kårkone, she had no share in the estate. ¶3

Correction: The birth moves from 'ABT APR 1749' to 'BEF 3 MAY 1749', from the baptism, which agrees with her burial age of 89 2/3 in Dec 1838. ¶8

Correction: The betrothal of 30 May 1773 calls her 'Synneve Olsd[atter]: Hiertholm', against 'Joensdatter' in the 1801 census and 'Jons Datter' at her burial in 1838. The baptism of 1749 names her father as Jon, so Joensdatter is trusted and the 1773 'Olsd.' is taken as a clerk's slip. A plausible source of the slip: Ole Johannesen of Indre Hiertholm (buried 6 Mar 1774, aged 75 1/2) and Johannes Olsen Hiertholm, one of her godfathers, were Olsens of the same farm. ¶10

Correction: The earlier lead that her father might be Joen Bendixsen, 75, of Ynnesdal (1801 census) is ruled out: her father was Jon of Hjartholm, probably the Jon Hiertholm buried in Aug 1773. ¶11

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Would confirm: a probate (skifte) after Jon Hiertholm (1773-74) naming a daughter Synneve married to Rasmus of Botnen. ¶9b
