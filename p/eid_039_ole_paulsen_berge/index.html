About the family

Parents confirmed: 'Ole Poulsen, 6½ år', the only child of Povel Svendsen Berge and Ingeborg Bjørnsdatter at the probate of 29 Jul 1727; his guardian was his father's brother Erik Svendsen Uglem (card 77804 c145; read by the orchestrator). ¶7a

Life story

Baptism of his daughter: '(6) Dordi: Forældr: Ole Pauls: Berge, Kirsten Paulsd: faddr: Per Aftreit, Siur Berge, Mali Berge, Siri Flönes' - Dordi, parents Ole Paulsen of Berge and Kirsten Paulsdatter, baptised on Oculi Sunday, 22 February 1761 (computed from Easter 22 March 1761). Selbu ministerialbok 1757-1801, canvas 11 left page, top and the crop. Earlier daughters of the same couple: Ingebor, 3rd day of Pentecost 1757 (canvas 4), and Brynill, 3rd Sunday after Epiphany 1760 (canvas 9). ¶1

Berge had several households (a Siur Berge and Mali Berge stood godparents in 1761). His birth, parents and death are not found; his patronymic shows his father was a Paul. ¶3

With Kirsten Paulsdatter baptised a son Povel in spring 1770 (16311 c37 L) - so Kirsten Paulsdatter (see her page) was his wife; the son was named for Ole's father Poul/Paul. ¶6

Burial, 7th Sunday after Trinity 1778: 'Begravne 1, Olle Povels. Berge død d. 26 Jul. 62 aar gl.' - Ole Paulsen Berge, died 26 Jul 1778, aged 62 (the second digit might be 7), so born about 1716 (or 1711) (16311 canvas 90 right). Identification by name and farm (Probable). An 'old Mali Berge' received communion at home on 21 Oct 1778 (c92) - possibly his widow. A Paul Berge buried 1727 aged about 36 (b. c.1691) fits as his father; no Paul is at Berge in the 1701 manntall (the Berge households then were Torsten Siursen 50 and Lars 56). ¶4

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Why the link to Daardi Olsdatter (see her page) is Confirmed: she married Jon Ingebrigtsen in 1789 as 'Dorete Olsd. Berge' (of Berge); she was 39 on 1 February 1801, which a baptism on 22 February 1761 fits to the month; and this is the only Dordi daughter of an Ole in complete read of January 1757 - February 1767 (the other Dordi of 1761 is a daughter of Esten Olsen Kyllo). Her second child and first son was named Ole (1793), and a Halvor Berge was his godfather. ¶2

Speculative father: Poul (Paul) Berge, b. about 1691, buried 27 Apr 1727, the only Poul at Berge 1715-1727 (see Povel (Poul) Svendsen Berge). ¶5

Corrections 1

Correction: birth set to about Jan 1721 (his burial in 1778 gave his age as 62, which would mean 1716). He was a grandson of Svend Eriksen Uglem and a first cousin of Torsten Eriksen Uglem. ¶7b
