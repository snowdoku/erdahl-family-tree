About the family

Children in Scituate: the Scituate births list as children of 'John Sr.' Jonathan (20 Sept 1646), Joseph (12 Jan 1649), Lydia (24 Jan 1652), John (30 Oct 1654), Mary (10 Dec 1658) and Ruth (baptised 17 May 1662, Second Church records) (Vital Records of Scituate, vol. 1, pp. 377-381, Turner; Ruth's entry read on the page image, the others from OCR). His wife is not named in these entries. ¶1

Father: Humphrey Turner of Scituate, tanner, whose will of the last day of February 1669/70 left twelve pence to 'my eldest son John Turner' and ten shillings each to 'Jonathan Turner Joseph Turner and Ezekiell Turner, being the sonnes of my eldest sonne', and separately £5 to 'my son Young John Turner' (The Mayflower Descendant 24:42-43, read on the page image). Jonathan, Joseph and Ezekiel are the sons named in John Senior's own will, so he is the eldest son. Probable, not Confirmed, because Humphrey's will does not add 'Senior' to the eldest son's name. No mother is entered: the will names no wife. ¶7

Mother: Lydia, wife of Humphrey Turner (see her page), from the Terling baptism above. Probable, on the same identification. ¶10

Life story

Baptism (Probable identification): 'John', son of Humfry TURNER and Lydia, was baptised at All Saints, Terling, Essex, on 24 March 1621/22 (Old Style; 1622 in modern reckoning). Source: FreeREG transcription of the Terling parish register (file line 651), re-read on 13 Oct 2026; the Ancestry 9841 index (from the IGI) gives the same entry as 24 Mar 1622, Terling, parents Humfrey and Lydia. The register image was not seen. The birth date is entered as 'before' the baptism. ¶8

Terling baptism read in the index: the FreeREG transcription of the Terling register gives, at 24 Mar 1621/22, 'John', father 'Humfry TURNER', mother 'Lydia'. It agrees with the entry that NEHGR 151: 287 quoted from the register. There is no rival for this entry: the other John Turners baptised at Terling were the sons of Thomas and Frauncis (24 Aug 1623) and of John and Ellis (28 Jul 1625). No other child of Humfry Turner is indexed at Terling between 1618 and 1636, which fits a family that moved away soon after 1622 and was in New England by 1632-34. His own grade, which covers his link to his child in the tree, is unchanged. The baptism names his parents, but that he is the John Turner of Scituate is still shown only by the name pattern (eldest son John in Humphrey's will), not by a record that crosses the Atlantic. ¶13

Marriage: 'Mary Brewster marryed John Turner of Situate the Elder the 10th November. 1645', in the family record kept by her father Jonathan Brewster in the 'Brewster Book'. The editor says Jonathan made that entry at the same sitting and in the same ink as the births of his children, which include 'Mary Brewster the daughter of Johnnathan borne at Plymouth the 16th of Aprill 1627'. Source: The Mayflower Descendant vol. 1, pp. 7-8, a printed transcription; the page image of p. 8 was. The will's testator, 'John Turner Senr', is the same man as Bowman's 'John Turner, Senior, or the Elder' (MD 5:41). ¶4

No grade change: his marriage is also in the colony's register, 'John Turner and Mary Brewster marryed the xij Nouemb, 1645' (Records of the Colony of New Plymouth 2:94), and the colony's Scituate register gives Jonathan (20 Sep 1646), Joseph (12 Jan 1647/8, died 15 Jan 1647/8), Joseph (12 Jan 1649), Ezekiel (7 Jan 1650/1), Lydia (24 Jan 1652), John (30 Oct 1654) and Elisha (8 Mar 1656/7) as children of 'John Turner, Seni[o]r' (PCR 8). His link to Ruth (Turner) Prince stays Probable: the will names a daughter Ruth and a grandson Thomas Prince, and the 1704 Plymouth account names Ruth Prince's eldest son Thomas, but no record read calls Ruth Prince/Silvester his daughter (the step is by eliminating Isaac Prince, who had no son Thomas). Free full-text searches for 'Israel Silvester' or 'Ruth Silvester' with Turner, and for a grandson's deed of the Middleborough land, found nothing. ¶14a

(no grade change; graded on his link to Ruth Turner). Deane's History of Scituate (1831), family sketches p. 327 (Prince), says 'Thomas married Ruth, daughter of John Turner, sen. of Scituate, 1685' and 'Isaac married Mary, daughter of John Turner, sen. of Scituate, 1683'. Deane is a compiled source and cites no record, so this supports, but cannot confirm, the identification already made from the 1695/6 will (daughter Ruth, grandson Thomas Prince) and the 1704 Prince estate account (Ruth Prince's eldest son Thomas). The Second Church of Scituate records (printed abstract) have no baptism of a child of Thomas and Ruth Prince; the only Prince entry of the period is the 1681 baptism of Alce, daughter of Isaac Prince (as corrected in the errata). Free full-text searches of archive.org for Ruth Prince or Ruth Silvester with Turner, Israel Silvester with Turner, and a Prince deed of the Middleborough land found nothing new. Test record still needed: a Plymouth County deed by Thomas Prince of Duxbury or by Israel and Ruth Silvester reciting the Turner gift (queued S0254), or John Turner's probate file papers (S0246). ¶15

Plymouth County probate docket found (FamilySearch signed in, 2 Nov 2026): John Turner of Scituate, 1697, is old-series case no. 21382 in the Plymouth County Index and Docket (Sta-Wat, 1685-1881; FamilySearch collection 'Massachusetts, Plymouth County, Probate Records, 1633-1967', Dockets Sta-Wat image 343 of 474). The docket entries are: 1697, letter of administration [with will annexed?] to Jonathan Turner and Joseph Turner, executors, vol. 1 p. 267; will vol. 1 p. 266; inventory vol. 1 p. 267 (page figures as read on the image). No division, receipt or account is docketed. The recorded will was read on the image (Plymouth Probate vol. 1, pp. 266-268, images 142-144 of 'Probate records 1686-1702... vol 1-1F'). It agrees with Bowman's transcript in MD 5:41-45: the household goods after the widow Mary go 'betwixt my four Daughters', a gift to 'my Daughter Grace Christopher[s]', and the Middleborough half-share to 'my six Grandchildren... that is to say' Isa[ac] Turner, John James, Thomas Prince, Ma[r]y/Mar[garet] Turner, Alice Prince and the eldest child of my son Ezekiel Turner (the first and fourth names are hard to read on the image; MD prints Isaac and Margret). The probate certificate is dated 28 June 1697. The file papers (koha:1461478, arranged by case number) for no. 21382 are not in the browsable FamilySearch image set: its 'Probate case files 1686-1881' waypoints run only from no. 1881 to no. 16163, and the catalog lists films only to no. 7708. So no receipt naming 'Israel Silvester and Ruth his wife' could be looked for. ¶16a

His file papers (Plymouth case 21382) and his daughter's (Ruth Sylvester, case 20019, administration 1729) are both outside the FamilySearch image set of Plymouth probate case files (catalog 4092105). There the old series covers only nos. 147-186 and 9290-9876. Ruth's docket lists only the letter of administration and the inventory, with no division or receipt, and the 1729 Partridge v. Prince suit about her estate concerns wages and does not mention Turner. The record that would settle his link to her is still open: the original papers of case 21382 (at the Massachusetts Archives or the Plymouth Probate Registry), or a deed in which Thomas Prince sells the Middleborough land 'given by my grandfather John Turner'. ¶17

Grandchild Alice Prince (inference): the 'Alice Prince' of the will is probably Aallse, daughter of Isaac and Mary Prince, born at Hull on 17 December 1680, who was baptised as Alce, daughter of Isaac, at the Second Church of Scituate on 10 April 1681. If so, his daughter Mary (born 1658) was the wife of Isaac Prince, who married a Mary at Hull in December 1679. This is not shown by any record read here. ¶6

Will: John Turner Senior of Scituate, husbandman, made his will on 4 March 1695[/6]. He left his household goods to his 'dearly beloved wife Mary' for her widowhood, and after her death or remarriage to be divided equally 'betwixt my four Daughters; That is to say Lydia Mary Ruth & Grace'. He named his sons Jonathan, Joseph, Ezekiel, John, Elisha, Amos and Benjamin, his daughter Grace Christophers, and six grandchildren to share his Middleborough land: Isaac Turner, John James, Thomas Prince, Margret Turner, Alice Prince and the eldest child of his son Ezekiel. His sons Jonathan and Joseph were executors. The witnesses were Deodat Lawson, Deborah Lawson and Ephraim Little. The will was proved before Judge William Bradford on 22 and 28 June 1697, and the inventory of 'John Turner senior late of Sittuate deceased' was taken on 20 May 1697 (The Mayflower Descendant, vol. 5, pp. 41-45, transcribed by George Ernest Bowman from the original documents; recorded in Plymouth County Probate Records vol. 1, pp. 266-270). He therefore died between March 1696 and 20 May 1697. ¶3

Link to Ruth strengthened: the 1704 probate account of Thomas Prince's estate shows that his widow Ruth's eldest son was Thomas (Plymouth Probate Book 2, p. 47). That is the grandson Thomas Prince named in this man's will of 1695/6, alongside his daughter Ruth. ¶11

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

3 on the grade · 2 on the identification · 1 correction · 3 open questions

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 3

Confidence raised from Speculative to Probable, on the record of his will (Mayflower Descendant 5:41-45). The will names a living daughter Ruth and a grandson Thomas Prince. Thomas Prince, father of Thomas (born Scituate 10 July 1686), Benjamin and Job, had a wife Ruth, as the Boston birth entries of James (1687) and Benjamin (28 Feb 1693/4) show. Isaac Prince of Hull, the only other Prince who could have married a Turner daughter, had no son Thomas among the eleven children recorded at Hull from 1680 to 1701. So the grandson Thomas Prince is the son of Thomas Prince and a daughter of John Turner, and the daughter Ruth of the will is Ruth Prince. With Ruth, daughter of John Sr., baptised at Scituate on 17 May 1662, this makes him Ruth's father. It is not Confirmed because the will does not write 'my daughter Ruth Prince'. ¶5a

Grade unchanged: the daughter Ruth of the will is identified as Ruth Prince only by eliminating Isaac Prince as father of the grandson Thomas. Test record still open: the original file papers of Plymouth case 21382 (Massachusetts Archives / Plymouth Probate Registry, or a FamilySearch film not yet online), and a Plymouth deed by the grandson Thomas Prince of Duxbury of his share of the Middleborough land 'given me by my grandfather John Turner'. ¶16b

Audit 11 (2 Nov 2026): considered for a raise to Confirmed (AUDIT-QUEUE raise candidate) and kept at Probable, graded on his link to Ruth Turner. The will (image) names a daughter Ruth and grandchildren Thomas and Alice Prince, but it does not write 'Ruth Prince'. That the daughter Ruth is Ruth, wife of Thomas Prince, follows from the grandson Thomas being the son of Thomas and Ruth Prince (the 1704 account) and from eliminating Isaac Prince, who had no son Thomas. That is elimination, which stays Probable. The 1729 administration of 'Ruth Silvester' by Thomas Prince of Duxbury (her son) adds nothing about Turner. No rival Turner daughter married to a Prince is known, but the absence of a rival does not turn an inference into a naming record. Test records (unchanged): the original papers of Plymouth case 21382, for a receipt by 'Israel Silvester and Ruth his wife'; or a deed by the grandson Thomas Prince of the Middleborough land 'given me by my grandfather John Turner'. ¶18

The case for this identification 2

Identity (Speculative): proposed as the father of Ruth Turner, wife of Thomas Prince, because the 1902 Prince Genealogy (p. 32) calls her 'dau. of John Turner Sr. of Scituate' (son of Humphrey Turner, a lead not added) and Ruth, daughter of John Sr., was baptised at Scituate in 1662. What is missing: a record naming his daughter Ruth as Ruth Prince, such as his will or estate division in the Plymouth Colony or Plymouth County probate records. ¶2

Identity of the Terling baptism (Probable): The identification of this Terling John with John Turner Sr. of Scituate rests on: (1) the parents' names, Humfry and Lydia, which match Humphrey Turner of Scituate and the wife 'Lydia' that Vernon Dow Turner (NEHG Register 151, 1997) reports from a Scituate land record (Plymouth Colony Deeds vol. 3, not read here); (2) Humphrey's will of 1669/70, which names 'my eldest son John' and a daughter Lydia (Doughty), and a Lydia daughter of Humphry Turner baptised at Little Baddow, about 4 miles from Terling, on 17 Feb 1629/30 (Ancestry 9841 index); (3) age: baptised March 1622, John Sr. married Mary Brewster in November 1645 at about 23; (4) Humfry Turner is the only Turner father named Humfry in the transcribed Terling baptisms of 1617-1640 (all nine FreeREG Terling Turner baptisms of those years were opened; the last, William, 12 May 1632, was son of William and Joane), and the two other Terling John Turners of 1623 and 1625 had fathers Thomas and John. It is Probable, not Confirmed, because no record read ties the Terling family to Scituate. ¶9a

Corrections 1

Correction: the 'Identity (Speculative)' note above is superseded; he is Probable as Ruth's father, on his will of 1695/6. The 1704 Plymouth probate account then shows that Ruth Prince's eldest son was Thomas, the grandson Thomas Prince named in that will (Plymouth Probate Book 2 p. 47). ¶12
