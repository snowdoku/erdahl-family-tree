Life story

Baptized 13 May 1855; the date matches her gravestone exactly. ¶1

Came to America with her parents at age one (about 1856), via Wisconsin; family settled near Blue Earth, MN and fled by covered wagon during the 1862 Dakota War (1953 Mason City Globe-Gazette obituary). ¶2

1870 U.S. census (index only): 'Mary Anderson', born about 1856 in Norway, in her parents' household in Emerald, Faribault County (Ancestry db 7163, record 41887522, image 4265057_00375). ¶14

Charter member of Liberty Lutheran Church. Married Ole Jensen Quame about 1877. ¶3

Find a Grave links her to the wrong mother (Gertrude Halvorsen Laegereid of Lærdal, whose son Ole was born 4 months after Mary). Church records show her mother was Gjertrud Johnsdatter of Kvalem, Balestrand. ¶4

1885 MN census: on 10 June 1885 'Maia O Quame', 30, born Norway, was the wife in family 31, headed by Ole J. Quame, in Rome Township, Faribault County, with four daughters including 'Ohanna' (Annie), 2. 'Maia O.' is Maria Olsdatter, and age 30 fits her birth in April 1855. ¶6

1900 U.S. census: 'Maria', wife of Ole J. Quamne, born April 1855 in Norway, 45, married 25 years, mother of 10 children of whom 7 were living, immigrated 1856 and 44 years in the United States (Rome Township, sheet 5B). The April birth agrees with her baptismal record, and the 1856 arrival agrees with her obituary's account that she came at the age of one. ¶8

1910 U.S. census: 'Marria' (index surname Avaren, alternative Quame), the wife of Ole, born in Norway, was living in Rome Township with Cora, Minnie, Henry, Julius and Elmer. The index gives her birth as about 1865. That is ten years later than her 1855 baptism and her ages in the 1875 (20) and 1885 (30) censuses, so it is an indexing or enumeration error (perhaps 45 read for 55). The baptism date is kept. ¶7

1910 U.S. census: on the image she is 'Marrie', 45, wife, in her first marriage of 34 years, with 10 children born and 7 living, born in Norway. The age of 45 (ten years too young) and the immigration year written as 1844 are both enumerator slips: the 1844 date is before her birth, and her 1900 entry gives 1856. The 'about 1865' in the 1910 index reflects that age as written, not an indexing error. ¶10

1920 and 1930 U.S. censuses (index only): she was in Rome Township with Ole as 'Mary Quamme' (1920) and 'Maria Quarn' (1930), born about 1856 in Norway. In 1930 their daughter Cora was still at home. The images have not been read. ¶11

Emigration: entered as 'Marie' (no. 59), one of the children of Ole Andersen of Grøneng and Gjertrud Johnsdatter, in the Balestrand parish register's out-migration list for 12 April 1856, destination 'Nord-America'. The list gives her dates as born 25 April and baptised 13 May 1855, which match her baptism record exactly. She was eleven months old, which agrees with her obituary's statement that she came to America aged one. ¶13

Corrections 2

Correction: the earlier note that she married Ole Jensen Quame 'about 1877' is too late. The 1875 Minnesota census lists the couple together by May 1875, and the 1900 and 1910 censuses (25 and 34 years married) point to about 1875. ¶12

Correction: the earlier sibling list counts 'Susan (~1858 WI)', 'Annie (~1860 MN)' and 'Johanna Hannah Johnson (~1861)' as three. The 1875 census image gives 'Setone' 17 (Wisconsin), Johana 14 and Anna 12 (both Minnesota). Hannah and Johanna are one sister, born about July 1860 (1905 census; probably Johanna Johnson, d. 1957), and Anna/Annie was born about 1862-63, not 1860. The reasoning is on her father's record (Ole Andersen Flaten). ¶15
