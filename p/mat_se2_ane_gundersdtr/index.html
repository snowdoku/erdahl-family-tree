About the family

Her husband was a Sivert, dead before February 1801. Her children named in the census are Povel (38), Inger (30), Karen (27) and Gunder (22) Sivertsen. ¶2

Husband: Siur (Sivert) Olsen of Alsaet (see his page). She is named 'Anne Gund.' as mother of Gunder, baptised 5 September 1779 to 'Siur Ols. Als[et]', and was churched as 'Siur Alsets hustru' on 17 October 1779; Siur was buried on 27 March 1780 aged 51, which agrees with her being a widow of her first marriage in 1801 (Selbu ministerialbok 1757-1801, canvases 98, 99 and 101). ¶5

Father (Speculative): Gunder Vigen (see his page), a younger man than Gunnar Olsen of Overvik. His own parents are unknown; he is not linked to the Overvik family, so this does not make her a relative of Ola Gunnarsen Overvik. ¶7

Life story

The earliest ancestor so far identified on Paul Eidem's side. 1801 census, Selbu, rural residence 0028 Alseth, household 01, person 001: 'Ane Gundersdatter', 65, HUSMODER, 'Enke efter 1te aegtesk.' (widow after her first marriage), occupation 'Bonde med jord' - a farmer with land, holding Alsaet in her own right as a widow of sixty-five. Her son Povel Sivertssen farmed it jointly with her and his daughter Anne Paulsdatter, aged 2, was in the house: Paul Eidem's mother was being brought up in her grandmother's farmhouse. ¶1

Death and burial: died 11 November 1820 and buried 19 November 1820: 'Anne Gunders Datt. Alsæt', 'Kaar Enke', aged 83 - Anne Gundersdatter of Alsaet, a widow living on kår (a life-pension from the farm). Church book from Selbu parish 1816-1831, burials 1820, pp. 216-217 no. 24 (the same burial is indexed from the 1816-1824 book, pp. 208-209 no. 24, as 'Ane Gundersd', 83); index entries, scan. Rated Probable-strong: name, farm, widowhood and age (83, born about 1737; 65 in February 1801, born about 1735-36) all agree, and the only other Ane Gundersdatter of her age in the 1801 census of Selbu, the widow at Gulset born 1735, was buried in 1805. As a kår-widow in 1820 she had handed the farm on, presumably to her son Povel. ¶4

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 4

Rated Probable on the 1801 census household structure; her own identity before marriage is untraced and would need the Selbu register for 1757-1801, which is not transcribed. ¶3

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed, on the 1801 census text, re-read in full. Selbu, rural residence 0028 Alseth: household 01 is headed by 'Ane Gundersdatter', 65, 'Husmoder', 'Enke efter 1te ægtesk.', 'Bonde med jord', with 'Hændes børn' (her children) Inger Sivertsdatter 30, Karen Sivertsdatter 27 and Gunder Sivertssen 22. Household 02 is 'Povel Sivertssen', 38, whose occupation is entered as 'I fællesskab med moderen' (farming in common with his mother), with his wife Marit Bersvendsdatter and their daughter Ane Poulsdatter, 2. The census thus says Povel's mother was the joint holder of Alset, and the only holder there is the widow Ane Gundersdatter, whose own children carry the same patronymic, Sivertsen. Together with the 1779 baptism of Gunder to 'Siur Ols. Alset' and 'Anne Gund.', this ties her to Povel Sivertssen (see his page) by primary records. Her own parents remain unproved (Gunder Vigen is a speculative candidate). ¶8

Audit (1 Nov 2026): downgraded from Confirmed to Probable. The 1801 census does not name her as Povel Sivertssen's mother. Povel heads a separate household (02) whose occupation is 'I fællesskab med moderen', and her own household lists 'Hændes børn' Inger, Karen and Gunder but not Povel. Identifying the unnamed 'moderen' with her is a strong inference (she is the only holder at Alset, and her children are Sivertsen too), but it is an inference by elimination, not a stated relationship like 'Hans moder'. ¶9a

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed, on her husband's probate: the probate after him, Skifteprotokoll 8 (24759) fol. 291a-291b (views 310-311; typed register entry 'Sivert Olsen, Alset, Selbu, fol. 291'): 'Anno 1780 den 5te Maii' on the farm Alset in Selbu, registration, valuation and division after the late 'Sivers[!] Olsen Alset' between the surviving widow 'Anne Gundersdatter' on the one side and the children she had with the deceased in marriage on the other: '1. Sønnen Paul Siversen 17 aar gammel. 2. Sønnen Gunder Siversen 1 aar gammel', and on fol. 291b '3. Dotteren Ingrie Siversdatter 10 aar gammel. 4. Dotteren Karen Siversdatter 6 aar gammel'. The widow had a lauverge (co-guardian) Erich M[eeren?]; a Paul Pedersen Garberg was guardian for one of the children (relationship word not read with certainty). She is named as the widow and the four children as born to her and Sivert Olsen in their marriage, the first being 'Paul Siversen', 17, who is Povel Sivertssen of Alset. This is the stated relationship the audit of 1 Nov 2026 asked for, and it agrees with the 1801 census ('I fællesskab med moderen'). ¶10

Records that disagree 1

Against or open: her burial age of 83 in November 1820 points to about 1737, and the rest of 1737 and the years 1738-1739 have not been read for a rival Ane Gundersdatter; neither had all Gunders been checked for 1733. No record yet names her farm of birth. ¶6b

Alternatives considered and set aside 2

Baptism candidate (Speculative): on the Sunday after the Circumcision, 2 January 1735, 'döbt Gunder Vigens barn Nom: Anne' - Anne, child of Gunder Vigen, was baptised; her mother was churched at Septuagesima 1735 (Selbu ministerialbok 1699-1737, canvases 134 right and 135 left). Gunder Vigen had lost an earlier daughter Anne in 1729, so this Anne is the replacement name. For: it is the only Ane or Anne baptised to any Gunder in Selbu between January 1734 and March 1737, and her age of 65 in the 1801 census fits a birth at the turn of 1734-35 exactly. ¶6a

Ruled out: she is not a daughter of Niels Gundersen of Over Wigen. His probate of 26 Oct 1768 (24756 fol. 132) names his heirs as the children of his late son Gunder Nielsen, Jon Nielsen, Kirsti, Ingebor (a widow at Moksnes), Mali and Ingeborg; no Erich Nielsen and no Ane. ¶12

Open questions and the next record to read 3

What would settle it: her marriage to Siur Olsen of Alset (Selbu, about 1757-1762, ministerialbok 1757-1801, source 16311), which may give her residence before marriage, and Selbu 1737-1739 baptisms (source 16310 canvases 142-147 and the next book). ¶6c

Would confirm: her probate (Strinda og Selbu sorenskriveri, after her death on 11 Nov 1820) listing Povel as heir, or Povel's baptism or confirmation. ¶9b

Lead for her own family (reading uncertain): in the 1780 probate (24759 fol. 291b) the widow's lauverge (co-guardian) and the guardian of her son Paul appear to be 'Erich N[ielsen] Aune', and the word before his name reads like 'Mor-broderen' (mother's brother) split over the line. An Erich Nielsen Aune(t) was confirmed aged 15 in the 1760s, had a son Bersvend baptised in 1768 with Anne Torgersdatter, and was a surety at Povel's betrothal in 1796; 'Niels Ovne' and 'Mali ib.' were godparents of her son Gunder in 1779. If 'Morbroder' is right, Erich was a (half-)brother of Ane, which would point to her mother having remarried a Niels at Aune. Not a link; re-read the word at higher magnification before using it. It does not fit Gunder Vigen (see his page) as her father without a remarriage of his widow. ¶11
