Life story

Baptised in Selbu on the 5th Sunday after Trinity (25 June) 1815: 'et Drengebarn kaldet Nils fød d. 19de Junii. Forældrene John Nielsen Langlie og Marith Jens d.' Godparents: Ole Nielsen Langlie (his father's brother), Ole Estensen of Langlie, Peder Johnsen Lien, Elen Nilsdatter Hjelstad(?) and Ingebor Johnsdatter Lien. ¶1

Born four weeks after his parents' wedding of 21 May 1815, and named for his grandfather Niels Olsen Langlie, who died that September. Brother of Brynhild (b. 1818). His later life has not been searched. ¶2

1825 census: residence 0047* Langlie, household 01, person 014 - 'Nils Johns.', 11, 'Soen', son of John Nilsen and Marit Johnsdatter, living with his grandmother Guri Olsdatter. The age of 11 implies about 1814; he was born 19 June 1815 and so was 10 at the census. ¶3
