About the family

Father: probably a man named Tor/Toen (from his patronymic and his second son's name), not identified. Torkild Larsen of Engesetter (60 in 1701) is excluded: the probate after Torkild's wife Marte Johansdatter, Engesetter, 19 May 1713 (Ytre Sogn 2b fol. 440a, read from the scan) lists the widower 'Torchild Larsen' and their children 'Iver, Claus, Johannes og Joen Torchilsønner, alle myndige', with no Gullach. No Tor/Toer probate is in the Ytre Sogn index under Engeset 1698-1745 (the rows there are Peder Jonsen and Ingebrigt 1702-07, Marte Johansdtr 1713, Kari Torsdtr 1718 [wife of Iver Torkilsen, children as heirs], Erik Bottelsen 1728-33). ¶9

Father (Probable): Toen, farmer on Engesetter in the bailiff's census of 21 Jun 1664 (35573 viewer 130, s.240), name read 'T[ö]en' or 'T[ö]rn' (: 'Tørn[?]'). His patronymic To[e]nsen (1701, 1719, 1741) and his second son's name To[e]n fit; Toen is the only possible bearer of that name among the 1664 Brekke farmers. See Toen. ¶10

Life story

Bygdebok (Engesæter gnr. 55, canvas 551): brukar 1698-1741, d. 1741 aged 81 (b. about 1660), married Kirsti Jonsdtr. of Nedre Haugland; a division of 22 [month unread] 1741 followed. Agrees with the probate of 22 Aug 1741 and with his son Jon (b. 1696, Hjartholm). His own parents were not rebuilt; his forename is written 'Gullak' in this book. ¶11

1701 census (identification Probable): 'Gullach Torkils[en]', aged 36, householder on Engesetter, Eivindvik (Gulen), with the sons 'Jon Gullachs[en]' 3 and 'Tour Gullachs[en]' 1 and the servant Lasse Larsen, born on the farm, 16 (Manntall 1701, 38455 viewer 55, s.93). The sons Jon and Tor match the 1st and 2nd sons of the 1741 probate on the same farm; the other Gullach on Engesetter in 1701, Gullach Lassesen 40, had the sons Lasse and Iver instead. The surname Torkilsen comes from this census; it does not agree well with the '[T?]ensen' read in 1741, which may be a short form (Tor-) or a misreading. Birth about 1665 is from the census age. ¶4

1701 identification: with the patronymic corrected, the 1701 man and the 1741 decedent now agree on name, patronymic form, farm and the names and order of the two eldest sons (Joen, then To[e]n). The identification Gullov = 'Gullach To[e]nsen', 36 in 1701 (birth about 1665), is Probable on that basis, stronger than before; the 1741 probate itself remains the Confirmed link to his children. ¶8

Probate: 'Ao 1741 den 22de Aug. blev under Ladvigs Skibrede paa gaarden Engesætter skifted efter Gullov [T?]ensen' (in 1741 on 22 Aug, in Lavik skipreide, on the farm Engesætter, probate was held after Gullov [T?]ensen), between his widow ('Enken' in the lots) Christi Joensdatter and their jointly begotten children: 1st son Joen Hiertholm, 2nd son To[e]r[?] Hiertholm, 3rd son Lars Engesætter, all of age and present; a daughter read 'Agathe', married to Colbein[?] Johansen Engesætter; and a 2nd daughter 'Sirie', betrothed to Torke[?] Jonsen Engesætter. Appraisers: Lars [Hacke?] and Iver Engesætter. Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll 6a, No. 11, fol. 153a (Digitalarkivet 24161, view 153). The lot pages (fol. 153b ff., view 154) were not read. ¶1

Lot pages (fol. 153b-154a, view 154, read at reduced scale): the division gives 'Enken for den Halve Boe' (the widow, for half the estate), so Christi Joensdatter was Gullov's widow; then son lots headed 'Lars Gullacksen' (the patronymic Gullacksen for the children), a '2den Søn' and a '3die Søn' both headed '[J/T]oen Hiertholm' (the capitals are hard to tell apart at this scale), and the daughters 'Agatha' (1st) and a 2nd daughter read 'Brite' (the heading has 'Sirie'; one reading is wrong). A creditor 'Bottel Gullacksen' is listed, relationship not stated. The ordinal order of the son lots differs from the heading; the heading's order (Joen 1st, To?n 2nd, Lars 3rd) agrees with the 1701 ages (Jon 3, Tour 1, Lars not yet born). ¶2

Name: the patronymic in the probate heading is written with an ornate capital and was not read with certainty: '[T?]ensen'; the Digitalarkivet index has 'Gullov Loensen' (and codes him as female). Gullov, Gullach and Gullik are forms of one name (Gudleiv/Gullak), as with Gullov Johansen of Asseim, who is Gullach Johansen in 1701. ¶3

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Confidence: Confirmed as father of Jon Gulløvsen of Hjartholm (see his page) by the 1741 probate, which names Joen Hiertholm as his eldest son. ¶6

Corrections 1

Correction: the 1701 census does NOT call him Torkilsen. Re-read at 3x from the scan (38455 viewer 55, s.93), the third Engesetter householder reads 'Gullach To[e]ns[en]' (or 'Tours[en]'; the capital is the looped T this clerk also uses for 'Tosten' and 'Torchild'), aged 36, with sons 'Joen gullachs[en]' 3 and 'To[e]n gullachs[en]' 1. Digitalarkivet's own transcription gives 'Gullach Løensen' b.1665 with sons 'Joen Gullachsen' b.1698 and 'Løen Gullachsen' b.1700. Either way the patronymic matches the name of his second son, and it matches the 1741 probate heading 'Gullov [T?]ensen' (index 'Gullov Loensen') and the 1741 second son '[T?]oen Hiertholm' / 1742 guardian 'To[e]r Gullachsen'. The earlier reading 'Torkilsen' (and 'Tour' for the son) should be dropped. ¶7

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Possible father (not linked): Torkild Larsen, 60, also on Engesetter in 1701 with the sons Johannes 18 and Jon 16, and Iver Torkilsen 32 on the same farm. Gullach Torkilsen is not listed in Torkild's household, so the relationship is not shown by the census. ¶5
