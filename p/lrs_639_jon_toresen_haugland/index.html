Life story

Bygdebok (Engesæter gnr. 55, canvas 551): the wife of Gullak of Engesæter (brukar 1698-1741, d. 1741 aged 81) was 'Kirsti Jonsdtr. Nedre Haugland', daughter of 'Jon Toreson' and 'Brita Larsdtr.' of Nedre Haugland (bnr. 61). ¶1

The probate after his (bygdebok) wife Brite Larsdatter of Haugland, 1707 (Ytre Sogn 2b fol. 292a, view 6), names her children Jon, [..]sten, Lars, Brite, Kirsti (wife of Gullach Engesætter) and Eli, all of age, and no widower, so the father was probably dead by 1707. It does not name the father. The children's patronymic is Jonsdatter (Christi Joensdatter in 1741; 'Eli Jonsdtr, Haugland' in the probate index 1728-1733), which fits a father named Jon, and the eldest son is Jon. Grade unchanged (Probable): his patronymic Toresen and his identity rest on the bygdebok. Test record still needed: his own probate (before 1707; the Ytre Sogn index for Haugland 1698-1707 shows Jon Josefsen, Hans Jonsen and others at Nedre Haugland, no Jon Toresen) or the 1701 census of Nedre Haugland, Gulen (38455, scan only). ¶4

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
