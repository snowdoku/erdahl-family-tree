Life story

Olga's eldest sister. She appears as 'Anna Therese Tungsæt', born 1882 in Kristiansund, in the 1885 census at Toldbodbakken and the 1891 census at 47 Toldbodsmujet, and she emigrated with her mother and brothers and sisters from Kristiansund on 2 May 1894 for Minnesota. ¶1

1895 Minnesota census: Anna Tungseth, 13, born in Norway, in her father Peter's household in Skree Township, Clay County (p. 6, read from the image). ¶5

1900 census: probably the Annie Tungseth, servant, born May 1882 in Norway, living in the town of Hawley, Clay County. Index only, image not read. She is the only other Tungseth in the 1900 index, she fits Anna's age, and she accounts for the seventh living child her mother reported that year. If right, this gives her birth month, May 1882. ¶6

1910 census: probably the Anna Awrey, wife of J. H. Awrey, born 1882 in Norway, at Tempe, Maricopa County, Arizona; the index carries Tungseth as an alternate surname for her. Index only, image not read. Awrey is the first married name already recorded for her. ¶7

She died at Moorhead, Clay County, Minnesota on 12 Aug 1935, aged 52 or 53, and is buried in Hawley Cemetery in Hawley - the town cemetery, not the rural Zion Lutheran cemetery where her parents lie. ¶2

Her married life, as set out in the biography on her Find a Grave memorial (which is a contributor's text, not a record, and should be checked against county marriage and divorce files): she first married James Henry Awrey, 1864-1956, a widower with three daughters, and had five children by him before they divorced; she married Clarence Leroy Bodwell, 1889-1963, on 30 Oct 1918 in British Columbia, had three children, and divorced again; and she married Ole O. Halvorson, 1876-1943, on 14 Oct 1931 in South Dakota. The children linked on the memorial are Marie Theresa Awrey Rose, 1909-1976; Maurice Awrey, 1911-1984; Thelma Dagny Awrey Johnson, 1913-2004; Eldred Peter Awrey; Beatrice Ann Bodwell Aiello, 1918-1999; and Gustav Frederick 'Fred' Tungseth Bodwell, 1922-1981. The memorial text also names James Erling Awrey and Leonard Bodwell as children, though they are not linked. ¶3

Three marriages, two divorces and a death in town at 53 make her the most eventful of the siblings, and the British Columbia marriage is a reminder that the family's first American destination was Canadian - their father emigrated to Manitoba in 1893. ¶4
