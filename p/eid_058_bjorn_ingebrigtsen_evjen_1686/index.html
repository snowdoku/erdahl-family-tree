About the family

Father of Ingebrigt Bjørnsen (see his page): on Laetare Sunday, 11 March 1725, 'Døbt Biørn Efjens barn K[aldet]: Ingebrick' - baptised Bjørn Evjen's child, named Ingebrigt (Selbu ministerialbok 1699-1737, canvas 99 left page, crop); on Cantate Sunday, 29 April 1725, 'intod: Biørn Stor Efjens qvinde' - Bjørn of Store Evjen's wife was churched (canvas 100 left page), which places this Bjørn at Store Evjen. The son carries his father's father's name, Ingebrigt, as custom required. The mother is not named in this hand. ¶2

Other children (inference from the farm and patronymics): Marit, baptised Easter 1728 ('Biørn Efjens Marite', 16310 canvas 111 right); and probably Thomas, Halvor and Siur Bjørnsen of Store Evjen, who baptised children there 1758-1765. An unread lead: a 'Biørn E[f]jeng[?]' married a Brynel Fosheim[?] at Trinity 1716 (16310 c62, low-confidence reading). ¶4

Wife in 1762: Brynild Halvorsdatter (see her page). The tree has no mother for his son Ingebrigt, and this draft does not add one. Brynild may be Ingebrigt's mother or a second wife; his 1764 probate would settle it. ¶7

His father's probate, 19 Jun 1744 at Store Evjen (Skifteprotokoll 3, 24753, fol. 93b, view 114 from enhanced crops of very faded ink), names 'Biørn Ingebrigtsen Sønnen' as the first heir of 'afg. Ingebrigt Olsen'. This gives a second primary record, after the 1701 manntall, for his parentage. His own Probable rating is about the link to his son Ingebrigt and is unchanged. No probate after Bjørn himself (d. 1764) is in the typed register of Skifteprotokoll 5 (24755, 1760-66); it may be under a farm spelling the register does not show, or it was never held. ¶9

Mother added from the bygdebok, Probable: vol. 5 canvas 216 names Marit (b. ca. 1649), daughter of Bjørn Haldorsen of Stor-Evja, as wife of Ingebrikt Olsen Overvik at Stor-Evja; vol. 5 canvas 167: '-1719-1763 Bjørn Ingebriktsen fra [...] Stor-Evja, f. ca. 1691, d. 1764. G. 1716 [med] Brynhild [...] fra Mølnhus', children Marit 1716 (m. 1740 Sjurd Bjørnsen Flønes), Ragnhild 1720, Halvard 1721 (m. Berit [...] Fossan), Ingebrikt 1725 (m. Mali [...] Ner-Hoem), Marit 1728, Ola 1731, Tomas 1733. Inferred link: see the caution on Marit's page (a younger second wife of Ingebrigt existed). ¶11

Life story

1701 manntall (Selbu, Efvien, canvas 120): 'biørn Ingbrigtsen', aged 10, son of the farmer Ingebrigt Olsen (44) -, crop checked by the orchestrator. ¶8a

Bygdebok (Selbu i fortid og naatid vol. 5, Stor-Evjen chapter, printed p. 165; word boxes only): '-1719-1763 Bjørn... Stor-Evja' - holder of Stor-Evjen 1719-1763, followed in the book by '1764' (his death year, agreeing with his burial on 13 May 1764) and by the child 'Ingebrikt,... 1725,... Mali' on the same page. The holder block also has '1716... Brynhild'. read 1716 as Brynhild's birth year and ruled her out as Ingebrigt's mother; but this bygdebok series writes a marriage year in a holder block as 'G.' plus the year (for example 'G.1761' and 'G.1719' in vol. 3), and the 'G.' may simply be unqueried here, so 1716 may instead be the year Bjørn married Brynhild - which would agree with the low-confidence register lead of a 'Biørn E[f]jeng' marrying a 'Brynel Fosheim[?]' at Trinity 1716 (16310 canvas 62). An inference only; reading the printed p. 165 would settle whether Brynhild was his wife and Ingebrigt's mother. ¶5

Biography: on 18 Jun 1738 'Bjørn Ingebrigtsen Storefjen' was lauverge (guardian) for Berit Haaversdatter, widow of Ole Joensen Store Evjen, at the skifte after Ole (24752 fol. 805). Ole left no children; his heir lived at Kjølstad. No relationship between Bjørn and the couple is stated. Ole Joensen is therefore not the father of Haaver Olsen Store Evjen, and this estate is no route to Svend Eriksen Uglem's wife. ¶10

1762 extra tax list: 'Biørn Ingebrigtsen' heads the farmer-owned holding 681, with his wife Brynild Halvorsdatter and his married sons Ingebrigt (wife Mali Olsdatter, daughter Kari) and Halvor (wife Brit Olsdatter). The farm word is clearly 'Storesien' with a long s, not 'Storefjen': on the same page the clerk wrote 738 'Stor Eff[ien]' with a double f (comparison crop eid_130_c141_names.jpg). So 681 is a separate matrikkel unit, perhaps a Store Evjen part listed under another name or a farm Storsjøen (not verified). The identification still rests on the father, son and daughter-in-law all matching, and on his 1764 burial at Store Evjen. ¶6

Burial: Jubilate Sunday, 13 May 1764, 'graufæst Biørn Ingebr: St. Efjen gl: 78 aar' - buried Bjørn Ingebrigtsen of Store Evjen, aged 78, so born about 1686 (Selbu ministerialbok 1757-1801, canvas 16 left page, crop). ¶1

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the grade · 1 correction · 1 open question

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Rated Probable, not Confirmed: the adult 'Ingebricht Biørns. Stor Efjen' (1765) names a father Bjørn and the farm, and this Bjørn is the Bjørn of Store Evjen who had a son Ingebrigt in 1725; but Ingebrigt's own age is not known from any record (his burial is not found), and the 1725 baptism says only 'Efjen'. ¶3a

Corrections 1

Correction: birth widened to 1686-1691; the census age points to about 1691, his burial age (78 in 1764) to about 1686. Elderly burial ages in this register often run high. ¶8b

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Would confirm: Ingebrigt's burial with an age pointing to 1725, or his confirmation (about 1740) naming Bjørn. ¶3b
