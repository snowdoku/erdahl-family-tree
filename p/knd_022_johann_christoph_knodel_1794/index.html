Life story

Baptism: Johann Christoph Knodel was baptised at Vaihingen an der Enz on 13 October 1794, son of Christoph Ludwig and Jakobina Friederike. Index entry only; image not seen; S0109-S0111 queued for the signed-in lane. ¶1

Marriage: he married Magdalena Dorothea at Vaihingen on 2 July 1822, two days before his brother Johann Gottlieb married there; the index relatives are 'Christoph Ludwig, Jacobina Friederica, Magdalena Dorothea'. Children indexed at Vaihingen include Katharina Luise (1829), Karoline Luise Friedrike (1832), Johann Christoph Ludwig (1840) and Karl Gottlieb (m. 1857). Index only; image not seen. ¶2

Burial: he was buried at Vaihingen on 12 August 1854; the index again names his parents Christoph Ludwig and Jakobine Friedrike, and his wife Magdalena Dorothea. Entered as a collateral because his records confirm the parents' names of his brother Johann Gottlieb. Index only; image not seen. ¶3

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
