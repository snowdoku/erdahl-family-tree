Life story

Burial (per index): 'Torbiørn Larssøn', Mand, of Yst-Takle, aged 57 (b. about 1728), burial date 17 Jun 1785, funeral sermon (likprediken) on the 9th Sunday after Trinity, 24 Jul 1785. Gulen church book 1776-1787, burials 1785, p. 102a, no. 118 (index pg00000001734082); indexed again from the 1779-1788 book (pg00000000768569), the same burial. Death narrowed from before 25 Aug 1785 (the probate) to before 17 Jun 1785. The scan was not read. ¶5

Godfather 1751: on 1 Jan 1751 at Brekke a 'Torbiørn Larsen' of Takle stood godfather to Hermund, child of [?] Mittachle (Midt-Takle) and Barbra (Gulen ministerialbok 11443, canvas 14, right page; read from a strip only). His marriage to Giertrud Olsdatter was not found in 11443 c4-c17 (Oct 1749 - May 1751; read in strips of the left part of each page); the betrothal of Per Olsen of Takle and Eli Johansdatter of Midt-Takle (spring 1751, c17 R) is a different couple. ¶7a

Godfather 1752: in Oct 1752 'Torbiørn M[id]tackle' (Midt-Takle) was a godparent at Brekke to the twins Anne and Marthe, children of Johannes of Midt-Takle, and 'Gjertrud M[id]tackle' was among the godmothers (11443, canvas 30, right page top). She is not called his wife there. His marriage to Giertrud Olsdatter was not seen in 11443 canvases 18-36 (Jun 1751 - Aug 1753), read across the full width of both pages, nor in 11442 canvases 66-71 (Jul 1748 - Mar 1749); nor was the baptism of their son Ole (born about 1752-53) seen in canvases 22-36 (Jan 1752 - Aug 1753). ¶8a

Probate: on 25 Aug 1785 the probate court divided at Ytre Takle the estate of 'den ved Døden afgangne Dannemand Torbiørn Larsen' (the late yeoman Torbjørn Larsen) between his widow Giertrud Olsdatter and two sons and a daughter: the eldest son Ole Torbjørnsen of Ytre Takle, of age and present; the second son Lars Torbjørnsen, of age and present, who was the widow's laugværge (legal guardian); and the daughter Maritha Torbjørnsdatter, 22, whose guardian was her brother Ole. Appraisers and witnesses: Lasse Johannessen Kuusnæs[?], Hans Clausen of Ytre Takle and Mons Pedersen. Net estate 34 riksdaler 5 ort 5 skilling, from which wedding-outfit allowances were first set aside. Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll A 10a, fol. 135a, no. 105. ¶1

Death: before 25 Aug 1785, the date of his probate. A Torbjørn Larsen of Ytre Takle was curator for Iver Johannessen in the probate after Johannes Herløvsen on 25 Sep 1784, so he was probably alive then; that rests on name and farm. ¶2

Searches that found nothing 1

Parents not found: no Lars lived on Ytre Takle in 1701 (householders Baltzer Colbensen, Rasmus Jacobsen and Alf Christensen), and the Ytre Sogn probate index for Takle (41 rows, 1698-1790) has no Lars decedent, so he probably came to Ytre Takle from elsewhere, perhaps by marriage. The Takle probates after Ole Kolbeinsen (1771) and Ole Torsen (1728) do not name him. ¶6
