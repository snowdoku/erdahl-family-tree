Life story

Born 15 Oct 1869 (Old Style) at Friedenstal, son of Ludwig Knodel and Wilhelmine Farowskye (FHL film 1884110/3 p.243 no.40); died there 24 Jan 1870, the entry annotated 'not a colonist' (FHL film 1884111/2 p.267 no.7). ¶1

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Confidence raised to Confirmed (Oct 2026): the Consistory birth index (FHL 1884110/3 p.243 no.40) names both parents, Ludwig Knodel and Wilhelmina Farowskye, at Friedenstal. The earlier Probable level rested only on the identity of the child's brother Gottlieb, which is now Confirmed (1879 baptism image and 1928 Megantic manifest). ¶2
