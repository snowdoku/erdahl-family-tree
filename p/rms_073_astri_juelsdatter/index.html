About the family

Mother of a Juel baptised in Aurdal in January 1755 (DA 9361, p. 452, canvas 228), wife of Ole Knudsen; churched 9 Feb 1755. Speculative mother of Jul Olsen of Midtstrand, whose eldest daughter was named Aaste (Astri). Birth inferred only from motherhood in 1755. ¶1

Life story

Ole Juelsen of Garli: 'Ole Juelsen Gardlie' stood surety in 1740 for Ole Biørnsen Gardlie and Marit Knudsdatter (DA 9361 p. 46, canvas 292), and 'Ole Juelsen Garlie' in Nov 1742 for 'Niels Troensen Garlie og Guri Knuds[datter] Loe' (married 28 Dec 1742; canvas 295). Guri is the fourth child of the 1754 Lo division (married to Niels Tronsen); her own probate at Garli i Skrautvaal, 3 Oct 1752 (Valdres card, fol. 432a; protocol DA 25287 view 444) lists children Knud 9, Arne 4 1/2, Engebret 3 1/2 and Guri 1/2, named after her parents Knud Olsen and Marit Arnesdatter; the father Niels Tronsen Garlie is guardian, so no Lo uncle is named. Ole Juelsen standing for Guri fits his being already married to her sister Ragnild (married to Ole Juelsen in the 1754 division) by 1742, and places him at Garli. The Garli probate cards of 1735 (Nils Olsen), 1740 (Anne Christophersdatter), 1742 (Olle Biørnsen), 1744 (Ole Olsen, nedre Garli) and 1754 (Peder Knudsen) name no Juel, so Juel's estate is not under Garli either. 'Knud Loe' stood surety at two Svenes marriages in 1740-41 (canvas 293): Knud Olsen was alive and active then. For Astri: if she was a sister of Ole Juelsen (Garli) and Hendrik Juelsen (Maanum), her father Juel is still unplaced; the Aurdal marriages 1739-1744 (DA 9361 canvases 290-296, read in this pass) do not include Ole Knudsen x Astri Juelsdatter or Ole Juelsen x Ragnild Knudsdatter, so both marriages fall before mid-1739 (or after 1744 elsewhere); the 1735-39 marriages (canvases c284-289) are next. ¶5

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: Speculative to Probable on the 1769 Aurdal confirmation (DA 9362 canvas 191). Raised from Speculative to Probable with her husband Ole Knudsen: mother of the Juel baptised Jan 1755 (DA 9361 canvas 228), who was confirmed in 1769 as 'Juel Olsøn Lo[e]', 15 1/2 (DA 9362 canvas 191), the only Juel Olsen in the Aurdal confirmation lists 1768-1776, and Jul Olsen of Midtstrand named his eldest daughter Aaste (Astri). The marriage of Ole Knudsen and Astri Juelsdatter is not in the Aurdal marriages 1751-1753 (canvases 310-313); a different 'Ole Knudsen Korsbrek[?]' married Joran Jacobsdatter Granum in Oct 1753. ¶2

Searches that found nothing 1

Marriage search: Aurdal marriages DA 9361 canvases 284-289 (Jul 1735 to Sep 1739) and 312-313 (Sep 1752 to Dec 1753) read in full: no Ole Knudsen x Astri Juelsdatter and no Ole Juelsen x Ragnild Knudsdatter. With the earlier passes (c269-283, c290-296, c300-309, c311, c314-316) the Aurdal register 1730-1754 is now read except c297-299 (1744-45) and c310 (1752). Both marriages were probably held in another parish (the bride's), or in the unread canvases. ¶6

Open questions and the next record to read 2

Lead: in the 1754 lifetime division of Lo (Loe), Svenes, two daughters of Knud Olsen, her probable parents-in-law, were married to men with the patronymic Juelsen: Ragnild to Ole Juelsen and Gunild to Hendrik Juelsen (DA 39948 card 8651). Two Lo sisters marrying two Juelsen brothers, and their brother Ole marrying a Juelsdatter, would be a typical exchange between two farms. So Astri may be a sister of Ole and Hendrik Juelsen. Unproved: look for a Juel with children Ole, Hendrik and Astri, for example a probate with 39948 heirs of those names, or the Aurdal marriages of Ole Juelsen and Hendrik Juelsen in about 1740-1754 (DA 9361 marriages, c290-313; their sureties). ¶3

Lead: Hendrik Juelsen, who married Gunild Knudsdatter of Lo in 1748, was of 'Maanum' (Månum) (DA 9361 p. 70, canvas 304). If Astri was his sister, her father Juel was probably of Maanum too. The Valdres probate cards for Maano/Maanum i Aurdal (DA 39948 cards 9677-9693, 1693-1753) were read: no Juel among the deceased or heirs (deceased Halvor Iversen 1693, Sigri Olsdatter 1705, Ingeborre Tostensdatter 1708, Beret Nielsdatter 1718, Bergite Helgesdatter 1730, Ingeborre Christophersdatter 1736, Guri Helgesdatter 1741, Iver Jensen 1746, Jens Iversen 1753). So her father Juel's probate, if any, is not under Maanum; Hendrik may have married or moved in there. Her marriage to Ole Knudsen is not in the Aurdal marriages of 1745-1754 (DA 9361 canvases 300-309 and 314-316 read in this pass; 310-313 earlier). ¶4
