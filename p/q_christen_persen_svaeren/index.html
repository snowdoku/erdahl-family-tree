About the family

His mother's 1743 probate (6a fol. 317a, No. 63) names '2den Søn Christen', of age, son of Brite Anfindsdatter and the widower Peder [written Hansen] Swæren; siblings Johannes, Giertrud, Brite and Kari (m. Olle Anfindsen Baale). ¶4

His brother Johannes Pedersen's probate, 30 Apr 1772 (Ytre Sogn 9a p. 5-6, No. 18, Sværen), names him 'Broderen Christen Pedersen boende paa gaarden Sværen' and the sisters Giertrud (dead; m. Aslach, Ottergield), Brithe (m. Tollef Larsen Sandergaard) and Kari (dead; sons Ingebrigt and Ole Olsen), the same five children as their mother's 1743 probate. ¶6

Life story

Kgroenha.net no. 466: born 1718 (given as Grøneng), married 19 Jun 1752 Randi Andersdatter Tjugum, died 1785 at Grøneng. Known children: Peder Christensen (b. ~1761, farmer at Sværen in 1801; his son was named Christen), Britha (b. ~1756) and Turi (b. ~1754). ¶1

Probate 1810: the probate after his widow Randi Andersdatter Sværen (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll A 14, fol. 116a-117a, No. 26, 28 May 18[10?]) calls him 'for længst afdøde Christen Pedersen Sværen' (Christen Pedersen Sværen, long since dead) and names their seven children: Peder, Kari, Turi (wife of Bjørn Johnsen Gjerde), Brithe, a fourth daughter [Brithe?], Randi and Christi. Confidence raised from Probable to Confirmed as the father of Turi Christensdatter on this record. ¶2

Laberg 1985 (p. 140): Kristen, son of Per Johannessen of Sværen, took the holding after his brother Johannes; born 1718, married 1752 Randi of Tjugum, died 1785; his wife lived until 1810. Their children: Per (took the holding), Kari (married Ola...), a daughter married to Bjørn... Gjerde, Brita the [elder] (unmarried), Brita the [younger] (married), Randi and Kristi. This agrees in every point with Randi Andersdatter's 1810 probate, and the two Britas explain the probate's doubtful second 'Brithe'. ¶3a

Primary record, grade unchanged: his own probate, 3 Oct 1785 at Sværen ('Wiigs Præstebool', i.e. Vik glebe land), Ytre Sogn 10a fol. 202b-204a, No. 140: 'afgangne Christen Pedersen', widow Randi Andersdatter, children Peder Christensen (of age), Kari m. Ole Olsen Ytre Farnes, Thore m. Biørn Johnsen Gjære, Brithe 28, Brithe the younger 26, Randi 19 and Christie 15. This agrees with the 1810 probate of Randi and with Laberg. Death therefore before 3 Oct 1785 at Sværen (kgroenha.net's Grøneng is not supported by the probate). The witnesses Ole Olsen and Ingebrigt Knudsen were both of Vangsnes. ¶5

Baptism search: Leikanger 11564 canvases 133-136 (Apr-Sep 1718) and 137 (Mar-Oct 1719) have no baptism of a Christen at Sværen. The register stops in Sep 1718 at the vicar's death (Nov 1718) and resumes at Lætare 1719 (canvas 136 right), so he was probably born in the gap, Oct 1718-Mar 1719, and his baptism is unrecorded. Burial in the same book: 'Christen Vangsnes sep.' 17 Jun 1718 (canvas 134 right), his uncle Christen Johansen, whose probate followed on 4 Jul 1718. ¶7
