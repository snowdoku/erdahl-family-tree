Life story

His birth is in the South Dakota index of birth records over 100 years old as 'SAMUEL EIDEM, male, born 6 October 1901, Union County, mother's name ANNA SATTER, father's name SIVERT EIDEM' - but with the striking detail that the state file number is 771318 and the FILE DATE is 26 March 1957. That is a delayed registration taken out when he was 55, the classic Social-Security-era filing by a man born before South Dakota's registration system began in July 1905. ¶2

Named as 'Samuel Eidem' among the predeceased siblings in his sister Myrtle's obituary of 2013. He married Vera Belle Richardson (1903-1975); their children were Darlyene Belle (Eidem) Mikkelson (1929-2011) and Larry Richard Eidem (1939-1968), both also buried at Elk Point. He died 20 May 1997 aged 95. ¶3

Censuses: Samuel G. Eidem, son, b. 1902 South Dakota, in the 1910 federal census of Brule township; Samuel Eiden, b. abt 1902, in 1920. Gone from his father's household by 1930. His registered birth date is 6 October 1901; the index years of 1902 are census-day arithmetic. ¶7

At MECKLING, Clay County, South Dakota in 1940 (his father's obituary) and at YANKTON in 1937 (his mother's obituary), before returning to Spink, Union County, where his niece Kathryn (Eidem) Hayter visited him from California in September 1975 and again in August 1976. ¶8

Mayme (Eidem) Eidem's elder brother. His South Dakota birth certificate is a delayed registration - State File Number 771318, filed 26 March 1957 for a birth of 6 October 1901 - and names his father as SIVERT EIDEM and his mother as ANNA SATTER, the same pair as his sister Mamie's contemporaneous 1906 certificate. He is named as 'Samuel Eidem' among the predeceased siblings in Myrtle (Eidem) Overdahl's 2013 obituary. ¶4

Social Security number [number withheld], an area number issued in South Dakota; the NUMIDENT death file gives his residence ZIP [withheld], Elk Point. He died at Elk Point aged 95 and is buried in the family's churchyard there. He appears in the 1950 census in Brule Township, Union County, South Dakota, as Sam Eidem, born about 1902 in South Dakota. ¶5

He lived to 1997 and his sister Mayme to 2002, so each outlived nearly all their generation; the one record that would have made this family visible from the California end - his obituary, which would have listed 'a sister, Mayme Eidem of Arcadia, California' - is in an Elk Point or Sioux City paper and is not digitised. ¶6
