About the family

Mother of Alf Olsen of Nystuen (see his page), graded on her link to him: Probable (compiled source only). Lesja bygdebok vol. 1, canvas 141, Sør-Kvam entry 4-4 'Ca. 1710 - 1735': 'Brit, ca. 1682-1761, g.m. ein Ola Ols.... Dei tok over garden kring 1710', children '[1.] Ola, ca. 1700, tok over. 2. Alf, sjå Nystugu, [her] (3-3). 3. Ragnhild, sjå Kleivstugu (7-2). 4. Tore, 1729-?'. The Nystugu entry 3-3 (canvas 45) cross-refers back: 'Alf Ols. truleg frå [___] (4-4)', so the bygdebok itself says 'probably'. Her patronymic Alfsdatter is inferred from entry 4-3 (Brit, ca. 1682, daughter of Alf and Sigrid, took over the farm) and from her son's name Alf; it is not written in the rebuilt text.
