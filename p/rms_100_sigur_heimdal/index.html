Anders Sigurdsen Heimdal? – aft. 1733Confirmed

About the family

Father of Anders Sigurdsen of Heimdal, Treungen (division among his children 16 Jul 1725) and of Hæge Sigursdatter (widow of Gunner Evendsen, Heimdal, 1717). Known only from the patronymic of the two siblings, whose relationship is stated in the 1717 skifte ('hendes Broder... Anders Hiemdal', Øvre Telemark skifteprotokoll no. 3 fol. 276b-277a, DA 25692 view 285). Both siblings named their eldest son Sigur. ¶1a

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Probable, not Confirmed: no record names him or gives his farm; that he lived at Heimdal is an inference from both children being settled there. The 6856 Heimdal run (views 473-480) has no card from the 1689-97 or 1697-1703 protocols, so no probate after him survives in the index. ¶1b
