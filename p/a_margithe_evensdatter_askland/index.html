About the family

NEW ANCESTOR. Wife of Terje Salvesen of Søndre Askland in Gjøvdal and mother of Torbor Terjesdatter of Tveit. ¶1

Parents: Margithe was a daughter of Evind Biørnsøn of Ekra (Økre/Egren, a part of Søndre Askland) and Thodne Larsdatter. Evind's estate was settled on 3 Apr 1724 (Østre Råbyggelag Skifteprotokoll nr. 6 fol. 226a, read on the scan; probate card 65): the widow Thodne Larsdatter handed over the estate and the heirs were Biønne Evindsøn, Tallak 16, 'Marg[ith] Evindsdaatter gift med Tergie Salvesøn Askland', Thargier Evindsdatter wife of Ole Tollachsøn [nordre] Askland, and Margitta wife of Halvor Jensøn Hugsiaa in Treungen. The archivist's notes on the 1743 Askland card (109/111: 'Konens far: Evind Biørnsen Askland + sk. 3/4-1724; mor: Thodne Larsdtr.') and on the 1749 estate of her brother Biøne Evendssen (cards 123/125: sister 'Margitha Evindsdtr. g.m. Terge Salvesen Askland') say the same. ¶3

Life story

Named on the Østre Råbyggelag probate card for the 1761 Tveit settlement: "mor: Margithe Evensdtr. oppg. sk. 18/6-1743". Her estate was surrendered to the court on the same day as her husband's. Her father was an Even. ¶2

Chronology flag. In the 16 Mar 1708 Askland skifte (Skp. 3 fol. 46b) Tergie Salvesen is already married to 'Enkens Søsterdatter', so Margithe was married by early 1708 and born by about 1690. A daughter 'Birgithe the younger, 2' in the 1743 oppgitt skifte would then have been born when Margithe was over 50. Either the age 2 is misread, or that child was a grandchild or the child of another wife. The parentage is not affected, since Todne Larsdatter was Evind Bjørnsen's wife both in 1708 and at his death in 1724. ¶5

Corrections 2

Correction: the 18 Jun 1743 Søndre Askland record is an oppgitt skifte (Skifteprotokoll nr. 10 fol. 356a, read on the scan): 'Tergej Salvesøn og hans Kone Margithe Evensdatter', both living, handed their estate to their children. So she was alive on 18 Jun 1743, not dead before it. The children named are Salve Tergejsøn (married), Neri 23, Even 16, Torbor (wife of Salve Giermundsøn, Tvett in Lille Topdallen), Birgithe the elder (wife of Thore Aanonsøn, Birkeland in Bakke parish, Lister amt), Gunnild (wife of Ellef Olsøn, Askeland) and Birgithe the younger, 2 years old. A daughter aged 2 in 1743 means Margithe was born well after 1680, probably about 1695-1705; her brother Tallak was 16 in 1724 (born about 1708). ¶4

Correction: the chronology conflict is settled. Re-read at high magnification, the 18 Jun 1743 oppgitt skifte (Skifteprotokoll nr. 10 fol. 356a, DA 24358 view 359) ends its list of heirs '...og Birgithe Tergejsdatter den yngre 20 aar gammel': the younger Birgithe was 20, not 2, so born about 1723. In the same list Neri is 23 (born about 1720) and Even 16. Even's baptism was found: 'Dom. Oculi i Giøvedal döbt Tergie Asklands barn Evind' (30 Mar 1727; Åmli register 1711-1740, DA 9920 canvas 44, p. 83). So Margithe, married by Mar 1708, bore children until at least 1727, which fits a birth about 1685-1690. Her daughter Torbor married in Jul 1720, so Margithe's children span about 1700 to 1727; her birth 'ABT 1680' is left unchanged, though it makes her about 47 at Even's birth, and it is possible that the youngest children were by a later wife of Tergie (the 1743 skifte calls Margithe his wife and the heirs 'their children', which argues against that). The earlier flag of a child aged 2 in 1743 came from a misreading. ¶6
