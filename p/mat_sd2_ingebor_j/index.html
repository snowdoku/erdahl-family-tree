About the family

Oline Slind's great-great-grandmother, wife of Haldo Torgersen of Svinaas. The 1801 census of Selbu, rural residence 0015 Svinaas, household 4, enters her as 'Ingebor Jonsdatter, 28, Hans kone, Begge i 1te aegteskab' - his wife, both of them in their first marriage - with three children already born: Ingebor 6, Torger 5 and Magli 2. The couple had therefore married by about 1794 and Marit, born about 1810, was a later child. ¶1

Her patronymic makes her father a JON, born about the 1740s. He is not entered here. ¶2

Parents (Probable): father Jon Olsen of Sandvik (see his page), holder of Sandvik 1768-1811; mother his first wife Ingeborg Olsdatter (see her page), born 1749 at Slindan. The bygdebok's Sandvik chapter (vol. 3, p. 353, nb.no word boxes) lists among Jon Olsen's children 'Ingeborg, 1775, Haldor Torgersen Svinås' - her husband by name. Her 1794 marriage as 'Ingeborg Jonsdatter Sandvig', the 1801 Sandvig household of Jon Olssen, 55, and the register's children of Jon Olsen Sandvig and Ingeborg Olsdatter (Ole 1771, Marit 1773) agree. The arguments are on the parents' pages. ¶8

Life story

Origin and marriage: betrothed in Selbu on 21 September 1794 as 'Pigen Ingeborg Jonsdatter Sandvig' - the maiden Ingeborg Jonsdatter of Sandvig (Sandvik) - to the grenadier Haldor Torgersen Svinaas, forlovere Jon Barosen Sandvig and Ole Barosen Fossan, and married on 27 October 1794. So before her marriage she lived at Sandvik, and her father Jon should be sought there. ¶5

Father, not decided. The forlover Jon Barosen (Bardsen) Sandvig is NOT taken to be her father: (1) he and Ole Barosen had already stood as forlovere for the groom's father Torger Eriksen in 1787, so they were the Svinaas family's sureties; (2) the 1801 census of Selbu has no Jon Bardsen at Sandvig, and its only Jon Bardsen of suitable age is Jon Barossen, born about 1758, a farmer at Moegaard, who would have been about 14 at her birth in about 1772-73; (3) he was a frequent forlover in 1794, for at least two other couples. A better candidate, also unproved, is Jon Olssen of Sandvig, 55 in 1801 (born about 1746), a farmer in his second marriage, whose son Ole Jonssen, 30 (born about 1771), worked the farm in partnership with him - Ingeborg, born about 1772, would fit as Ole's full sister by Jon's first marriage. Against it, Jon Olssen had a living daughter Ingebor, 10, by his second marriage in 1801; two living half-sisters of one name is unusual but not unknown. ¶6a

Context for her father, not a link: a Jon Olsen of Sandvig and Ingeborg Olsdatter had a son Ole baptised in spring 1771 (Selbu klokkerbok 1766-1776, canvas 68 col. 1, godparents Torchel Sandvigen, Erich Sandvigen, Jørgen Slinden, Ingeborg Sandvig, Gidsille Sandvigen). He agrees with the 1801 Sandvig household of Jon Olssen, 55, in his second marriage, with a son Ole Jonssen, 30, beside him. A Jon Olsen at Sandvig with children in the early 1770s is a candidate father for her (born about 1772-73), but her own baptism has not been found (read Jan-Nov 1771 without it). Also, a Jon B. Sandvig and an Ingeborg Sandvig stood godparents to her future husband Haldor in November 1770. ¶7

Baptism: 'Ingeborg. Foræld. John Olsen Sandvig, Ingeborg Ols d. Fadd. Ole Olsen Sandvig, Ole Mejum[?], Marith Storeggen[?], Sigri Slinden, Marthe Sandvig' - Ingeborg, parents Jon Olsen of Sandvik and Ingeborg Olsdatter, godparents (faddere) Ole Olsen Sandvik, Ole Meium[?], Marit Storeggen[?], Sigrid Slinden and Marte Sandvik. Entered under the First Sunday after Epiphany 1775, which fell on 8 January 1775 (date inferred from the service heading; the clerk's book, not the ministerialbok). Her mother was churched (introdusert, the first church visit after a birth) on the Fourth Sunday after Epiphany, 29 January 1775, as 'John Olsen Sandvigens h[ustru]'. Selbu klokkerbok 1766-1776, canvas 115 right page, columns 3-4. ¶10

Godparents as kin evidence: the godmother Sigri Slinden, like Jørgen Slinden, godfather to her brother Ole in 1771, ties this family to Slindan, the farm the bygdebok gives as her mother's birthplace. This is consistent with the in-line pedigree collapse logged in LOG.md (Ola Jørnsen x Marit Pedersdatter of Slindan as ancestors twice). The baptism proves the Sandvik link (Ingebor's mother is Ingeborg Olsdatter, wife of Jon Olsen Sandvig). The Slind link (that this Ingeborg Olsdatter is Ola Jørnsen's daughter of 1749) still rests on the bygdebok and is Probable on Ola Jornsen Slind. The collapse therefore remains Probable. ¶13

1801 census: Ingebor Jonsdatter, 28, wife of Haldo Torgersen at Svinaas. Burial candidates only (indexed): 'Ingeborg Johnsdr', aged 79, 14 Jun 1853, residence blank (pg00000006364019 - best age fit); a 'kårkone' at Svinaas aged 95, 20 Dec 1863 (pg00000004447000 - farm fits, age does not). Unresolved. ¶16

The maternity of Marit Haldorsdatter, born about 1810, is rated Confirmed rather than Probable because the 1801 census states that this was the first marriage of BOTH parties and no record shows Haldo Torgersen with a second wife before 1825, when the census still has him married. ¶3

Death: NOT DECIDED. Two Selbu burials fit her name and approximate age (30 Dec 1772 - 1 Feb 1773 from her 1801 census age of 28), and neither index entry gives a residence, a husband or a widow's remark, so neither names Svinaas or Haldo Torgersen: (a) 'Ingeborg Johnsdr', 79, died 11 June, buried 14 June 1853 (1843-1859, p. 389 no. 48), calculated birth 1774 - the better age fit; (b) 'Ingeborg Johnsdr', 70, died 4 July, buried 14 July 1839 (1824-1842, p. 396 no. 64), calculated birth 1769. Other near misses (Ingebor Johnsd Drate, 1835; Ingeborg Johnsdatter, 84, 1851; Ingeborg Johnsdt Oxstad, 1844) are listed in dossier. The scans of entries (a) and (b) are the next thing to read; failing that, whether she appears in Svinaas households after 1839. ¶4

1825 census (27 November 1825), Svinaas: Haldo Torgers., born 1770 (calculated), Gmd. (married farmer), with two women named Ingebor Jonsdatter on the farm: Ingebor Johnsd., born 1774 (calculated), and Ingebor Jonsd., born 1777 (calculated); also sons and daughters Ole, Torger, Erik, John, Marit and Ragnhild Haldosen/Haldosdatter (index rows). The 1774 woman is presumably Haldo's wife (her baptism is January 1775), but the index rows do not state which woman was the wife; the 1777 Ingebor is another woman to keep apart. ¶14

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Parents raised from Probable to Confirmed, on the 1775 baptism. The record names her parents explicitly as Jon Olsen Sandvig and Ingeborg Olsdatter, the same couple as at the baptisms of Ole (1771) and Marit (1773). The adult is tied to this child as the audit standard requires: she married in 1794 as 'Pigen Ingeborg Jonsdatter Sandvig', naming the baptism farm, and her identity is unique. Every baptism block of the clerk's book was read for an Ingeborg daughter of a Jon from January 1771 to June 1774 and from December 1774 to May 1776. No other Sandvik Ingeborg was found; in particular there was none from November 1771 to February 1773, the window her 1801 census age implies. The only Ingeborg Jonsdatter left at Sandvik in 1801, Jon Olsen's daughter Ingebor, 10, by his second marriage, is a different person. The younger Jon Olsen of Sandvik, buried in September 1773 aged 20, cannot be this child's father. The bygdebok independently marries Jon Olsen's daughter Ingeborg of 1775 to Haldor Torgersen Svinås. Residual gap: canvases 109-114 (June - December 1774) have not been read, so a rival Ingeborg of another Sandvik Jon there is not formally excluded; a rival from that window would conflict with the bygdebok, and her parents' next child could not fall there. ¶12

Records that disagree 1

Birth year conflict: the 1801 census age of 28 gives 1772-73 (the date kept above); the bygdebok gives 1775. Her parents had Marit on 14 March 1773 and Ole in spring 1771, so a birth in 1772 or early 1773 is unlikely, and the burial candidate of 1853 (aged 79) points to about 1774. The book's 1775 is preferred in reasoning, but the birth field stays at the census estimate until her baptism is read (Selbu klokkerbok 1766-1776, canvases 109 onward, from June 1774). ¶9

Corrections 1

Correction: birth changed from 'ABT 1773' (her 1801 census age of 28) to 'before 8 January 1775' at Sandvik, on the baptism above. The census age is two years high: she was 26 on 1 February 1801. That is an ordinary census slip, and the 1775 date agrees with the bygdebok ('Ingeborg, 1775, Haldor Torgersen Svinås'), with the spacing of her siblings (Ole spring 1771, Marit 14 March 1773) and with the better burial candidate ('Ingeborg Johnsdr', 79, buried 14 June 1853, born about 1774). The earlier notes that leave her father undecided, or rate her parents Probable pending this baptism, are superseded by this note and the next. ¶11

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Burial candidates, none adopted: (a) 'Ingeborg Johnsdr', 79, died 11 June, buried 14 June 1853 (calculated birth 1774 - the best age fit, but no residence or husband in the index); (b) an Ingebor Jonsdatter, Kaarkone (pensioner on a farm's kår), residence Svinaas, birth year 1768 (calculated), buried 1864 (index date 1 January 1864, apparently a placeholder; Selbu 1859-1868 and 1860-1877). (b) has the right farm and the status expected of Haldo's widow, but its birth year is seven years early and she would have been about 89; the Ingebor Jonsd. born 1777 at Svinaas in 1825 is an equally possible identity for it. Because the candidate with the right age lacks a place and the candidate with the right place has the wrong age, no death is set. ¶15a
