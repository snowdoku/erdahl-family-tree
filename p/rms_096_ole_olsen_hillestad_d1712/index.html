Life story

Collateral, brother of Ingeborg Olsdatter Nedre Ytre Ramse; added because his skifte proves the Hillestad link. Graded on his link to his parents: the skifte after him (Skifteprotokoll nr. 3 fol. 269b, 4 Mar 1712; cards 4991-5001) names 'avl. Ole Olsøns far, Ole Evindsøn Hillestad' (skiftebrev 2/3 1695) and his mother Birgitha Nielsdatter (skiftebrev 12/6 1699). ¶1

Married Ingeborrig Thoresdatter (estate 1718), the eldest of three sisters from Tveit in Treungen; a contract of 5 Apr 1689 between him (then living at Tveit in Treungen) and his brothers-in-law Jørgen Knudsøn Skjeggedal (Vegusdal) and Knud Hiembedal (Treungen) is cited (card 4999). Children in 1712: Thore 20, Ole 17 (lame; estate 19 Jan 1713, Skp. 4 fol. 14), Kiddel 10, Tharan (m. Joen Næresøn Tveit, Lille Topdal) and Walborre (m. Ole Olsøn Tveit, Treungen). Sisters: Wilborrig Olsdatter (m. Gunder Qvæsta, Valle), Guroe Olsdatter (m. Knud Sigursøn Løeland, Valle) and, by the archivist's added note, Ingeborg Olsdatter, wife of Svend Evensen Ramse. Guardian for the son Ole: 'syskinbarnet, Evind Svendsøn, nedre yttre Ramse'. Land: 1 hud 10½ kalveskinn in Hillestad, land in Haugen under Hillestad, 5⅝ mæle in Tveit, Treungen. ¶2
