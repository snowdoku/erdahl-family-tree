How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the identification · 1 record that disagrees · 1 search with no result · 1 open question

The case for this identification 1

Identity: father of Johann Farowsky (see his page), named only in his son's marriage of 1834/35. The St Petersburg Consistory extracts record that marriage twice. Kulm, 31 Dec 1834 (FHL 1882643/1 p.68 no.173): 'Sarofski, Johann', 23, born in Poland, son of the late Andreas ('s +Andreas'), married Rosine Schmolke, widow, 38. Tarutino, 1 Jan 1835 (FHL 1882645/2 p.1 no.131): 'Farofski, Johann', 25, of Kulm colony, born in Poland, son of the late Andreas, married Rosina Schmolke née Bomm, 38, born in Poland, daughter of the late Michael. So Andreas was dead by 31 Dec 1834. ¶1

Records that disagree 1

Link (Probable): the groom of 1834/35 is the Johann Farowsky whose daughter Wilhelmine was born at Beresina on 7 Jan 1838 to 'Johann Farowsky, Colonist, u. dessen Ehefrau Rosina geb. Bomm' (baptism image read 1 Nov 2026), the bride of 1835 being Rosina Schmolke née Bomm. The father's name rests on two independent index extracts of the marriage (the Kulm and Tarutino entries), not yet on the register image; hence Probable, not Confirmed. The grooms' stated ages (23 and 25) disagree with Johann's later age (born 1803 by his 1881 death and the Wahl family-book index); that affects Johann's birth year, not the father's name. ¶2a

Searches that found nothing 1

Not found: no Andreas Farowsky (or Farofski, Sarofski, Tarowsky) in the Bessarabian deaths 1815-32 (R. Drefs, odessa3 early/link/deaths.txt) or births 1815-32, so he probably died in Poland before the family's emigration (Stumpp-type index: 'Farowsky, Johann, Poland, 1816'). No Farowski births in Geneteka for mazowieckie, łódzkie, wielkopolskie, kujawsko-pomorskie, podlaskie, lubelskie or świętokrzyskie (searched 1 Nov 2026). ¶3
