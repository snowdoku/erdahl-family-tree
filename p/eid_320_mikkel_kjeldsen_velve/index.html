About the family

Parentage: 'Michel Kielsen' aged 18 is listed as a son of 'Kiel Larss.' at Store Evjen, the bailiff's farm, in the 1666 vicar's manntall (35528 view 25 left). He is the only Mikkel Kjeldsen in the 1664-66 lists, so he is identified with Michel Kieldsen of Velve (d. by 26 May 1693). Birth moved from 'before 1665' to about 1648 on that identification. The link to Kjeld is graded Probable on Kjeld's record (two farms, 27 years apart, no record names the relationship); Mikkel's own grade, on his link to Siur Mikkelsen, is unchanged. ¶3

Life story

Probate (skifte) after him, 26 May 1693, at Velve (24749 fol. 117a-117b from the scan): 'Anno 1693 den 26 Maij blev skifte og deling paa... Welfue, efter Sl. Michel Kieldsen som bonde og boede paa forskrefne gaard'. Two valuation men were present (one read as Halvor Berg[e], the other Gunder Hoem). The estate came to 24 rd 2 ort and the debts equalled it, so nothing was left to divide. The entry closes by saying the estate was ceded to the man 'som nu gaarden beboer' (who now lives on the farm), read as Mogens...sen. No widow or children are named. The index card (77804 c2127) says the same: 'Ingen arvinger oppgitt' (no heirs given). ¶1

Proposed as the father of Siur (Siver) Mikkelsen Velve (see his page). The evidence is circumstantial, so Probable, not Confirmed. (1) In the 1701 manntall the household of Mogens Jonsen (57) at Velve has a servant 'Siver Michels.' aged 12 (38461 c120). Mogens took over Velve after Michel's insolvent estate of 1693. He has a son Christen aged 1, so he had a young wife, and she may have been Michel's widow. (2) No other Mikkel is known at Velve. (3) Siur Mikkelsen held Velve by 1709, and his 1742 probate passed land in Velve to his son. (4) Siur named his own son Mikkel (baptised 1711). ¶2a

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 alternative set aside · 1 search with no result

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Not proved: no record names Siur as Michel's son. ¶2b

Searches that found nothing 1

Searched, with nothing found: no Kjeld appears in the 1646-47 Selbu crown or diocese tenant lists (51306 view 181), and none among the 1645 poll-tax half-farm men (77038 views 269R-270R). The poll tax gives wives only as 'hans hustru', so it cannot name Mikkel's mother. In the Digitalarkivet transcription of the 1701 Selbu manntall, no man has a Kjeldsen patronymic (search lastname Kiel*/Kield*, kommune 1664), so Kjeld's other sons Lauritz, Poul and Jon do not appear in Selbu in 1701. The court book 28707 (1689-93) was sampled at the Selbu session of late 1692 (view 136, a merchant's debt list naming about 80 Selbu farmers). No 'Michel Velve' was read there, but the hand is poor, and views 133-135 were not fully read. ¶4
