Life story

Marriage: the index of Cork and Ross marriage licence bonds lists 'Collis, William, and Mary Crosse.. 1687' (image read). That agrees with Brady, who says Mary Cross married William Collis in 1687, and it contradicts Burke's Landed Gentry of Ireland (1912), which dates the marriage 1693. Added. ¶3

Mary Cross, second daughter of Dr Benjamin Cross and Anne Eveleigh. She was the wife of William Collis of Lissodigue, Co. Kerry, and was living in 1733. The Blennerhassett Pedigree, written by Captain John Blennerhassett, husband of her sister Elizabeth Cross (died 22 Mar 1732 aged 63), says: 'Mary Cross, second daughter of Doctor Cross and Anne Eveleigh, by William Collis has issue, anno 1733, six sons... and three daughters Anne, Martha, and Mary Collis', and names Anne Collis as 'first daughter of said William and Mary' (Hickson, Old Kerry Records, 1872, pp. 55-56). In 1730 William Collis of Lisodige had a wife named Mary (Registry of Deeds memorial 43069). ¶1

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the grade · 1 alternative set aside · 2 open questions

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Probable, graded on her link to her daughter Anne Collis: the only statement that she was Anne's mother is in the pedigree, a contemporary family manuscript by her brother-in-law. The 1709 marriage settlement names Anne's father but not her mother. ¶2a

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Caution, on her link to Anne Collis: William Collis had an elder son, the Rev. William Collis junior, already a clergyman at Dingle in 1709 (memorial 420) and named 'son of' William in a 1711 lease (memorial 5063). He is not among the six sons the Blennerhassett Pedigree gives to William and Mary, and Burke makes him the son of an earlier wife. So William Collis was married before 1687, and Anne's mother is fixed only by the pedigree's words 'first daughter of said William and Mary'. Anne married in 1709, which fits a birth after the 1687 marriage, but her birth year is not recorded. Grade stays Probable. ¶4a

Open questions and the next record to read 2

Test record: the full memorial 420, or a Collis of Lissodigue will or settlement naming Mary as Anne's mother. Added. ¶2b

Test record: the 1709 settlement in full (memorial 420), or William Collis's will (Lisodige, 1733, Ardfert diocese; Phillimore index), if any copy or abstract survives. Added. ¶4b
