About the family

Child of 1748 (scan read): 'Ole Johansen Asseims B[arn] N[omine]: Hermun[d], Mat[er]: Ingebor', baptised at Brekke 28 Jul 1748 (Gulen ministerialbok 11442, canvas 67, left page). So Ingeber was Ole Johansen's wife and still bearing children in 1748, which suggests she was born after about 1705. The entry does not concern Johannes (1742), so the confidence is unchanged. ¶5

Life story

Name: 'Ingeber' (Ingebjørg, Ingeborg), wife of Ole Johansen Asseim, named only as mother (Mat.) at the baptism of their son Johannes at Brekke, Gulen, on 6 May 1742 (11441 c24R). Her patronymic is not given. Birth range inferred only from a probable child born about 1732-33 and from Johannes, 1742 (about 1692-1727 if the elder boy was not hers). ¶1

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the grade · 1 alternative set aside · 1 search with no result · 1 open question

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Confidence: Probable, linked as mother of Johannes Olsen Asheim (see his page), whose identity with the 1742 baby is Probable. ¶3a

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Caution: she is not Ingeborg Gullaksdatter (see her page), the wife of Torkil (Torkild Monsen) Asseim, who is also written 'Ingeber' in the same book (mother of Randi, bapt. 12 Apr 1744). Both women lived at Asseim in the 1740s, so a godmother entered only as 'Ingeber Asseim' (3 Mar 1741, Mathias Asseim's child; 25 Jun 1741, Jens Tjønning's child Johanne) cannot be assigned to either. ¶2

Searches that found nothing 1

Rival search complete (assessed): no other Johannes son of an Ole of Asseim was baptised at Brekke from Jun 1739 to May 1742; her link as mother of Johannes Olsen Asheim stays Probable for the reasons given under Johannes. She is still to be kept apart from Ingeborg Gullovsdatter (see her page), who was the wife of Torkild Monsen by Apr 1740 and is named in her father Gullov Johansen's probate; nothing in that probate makes Ole Johansen's wife a daughter of Gullov. ¶4

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Would confirm: a probate after her or her husband naming a son Johannes; her burial with age; or her betrothal to Ole Johansen, which would give her patronymic. ¶3b
