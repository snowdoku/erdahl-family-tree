About the family

First wife: Ane Olsdatter of Loen, whose probate (A 10 fol. 335b, 12 [Mar?] 1749) names her 'efterlevende Mand Lars Samuelsen' and their sons Samuel Larsen, 8 1/2 years old (b. ~1740), and Ole Larsen, 1/2 year (b. ~1748). Marthe Rasmusdatter (see her page) was therefore his second wife, married after early 1749. Samuel and Ole are half-brothers of Anna Larsdatter (see her page); one of them may be the 'brother-in-law' from whom Aaland says Guttorm A. Hilde inherited in 1773 (inference). ¶6

Mother found, Probable: NN Hansdatter (see her page). The 1747 heirs paragraph of his father's probate (A 10 fol. 16b), enlarged, reads 'med hans tvende forhen afdøde Hustruer avlede Børn': all six children were born to Samuel's two deceased wives, not to the widow Ane Paulsdatter. Britte Rasmusdatter (d. 1709) bore only Britte and Synneve, and Lars (b. about 1714) is called the elder Brite's half-brother. So his mother was Samuel's other deceased wife, whom Aaland names only as '...Hansd.'. This resolves the conflict noted; the link is an elimination argument, so it stays Probable. ¶8

Life story

Named in his father Samuel Larsen's probate, 15 Sep 1747 (A 10 fol. 16b), as 'Lars Samuelsen gift og boende her paa Gaarden' (married, living on Loen), with a brother Hans Samuelsen and sisters Brite, Synneve, Brite and Ane. ¶5

Aaland 1932: his son-in-law (Vs.) Guttorm A. Hilde took the deed to the holding in 1773, 'erva etter verfaren og svogeren' (having inherited from his father-in-law and brother-in-law), but did not farm it; Hallstein Rasmussen was tenant from 1773. Guttorm's wife was Anne Larsdatter of Loen (Anna Larsdatter). ¶2

Aaland 1932 says Guttorm A. Hilde took the deed in 1773 having 'inherited after his father-in-law and brother-in-law', yet Lars was alive as a widower in Oct 1772 and, by Aaland, until 1789. The 1772 probate after Lars's wife Marthe (Guttorm's mother-in-law) is the more likely route of the inheritance, so Aaland's 'verfaren' (father-in-law) may be a slip for the mother-in-law; this is inference. ¶4

Aaland 1932: Loen bruk nr. 15 (later 'Hallsteinbruket'), printed p. 322: 'S. Lars, † 75 a. g. 1789, b. 1745 med P. H. Finde. Løyste br. 1767 fraa same for 90 rdl. Bykslepengane var ⅓ meir enn vanleg for sildefisket si skuld. * 1. Anne Olsd. Svingeset, † ikr. 1749. 2. Marte Rasmusd. Beinn., † 58 a. g. 1772. Eldste son. Samuel, † ug 26 a. g. 1772.' He was the son of the previous holder Samuel Larsen, died 1789 aged 75 (born about 1714), leased the holding in 1745 from P. H. Finde and bought it in 1767 for 90 riksdaler; the lease money was a third above the usual because of the herring fishery. He married first Anne Olsdatter of Svingeset (died about 1749) and second Marte Rasmusdatter of Beinnes (died 1772 aged 58). His eldest son Samuel died unmarried in 1772 aged 26. ¶1

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Confidence raised from Probable to Confirmed: the probate after his wife Marthe Rasmusdatter Loen, registered 5 Oct 1772 (Nordfjord skifteprotokoll A 15b, fol. 768b), was held between 'Enkemanden Lars Samuelsen' and her child 'Anne Larsdatter gift med Guttorm Andersen Hilde', which ties him to Anna Larsdatter as her father. His death in 1789 rests on Aaland only: no probate after a Lars Samuelsen of Loen is indexed for 1772-1800 (the only Nordfjord Lars Samuelsen, at Kroken in Davik skipreide, 1781-1785, is another man). His Innvik burial about 1789 has not been read. ¶3

Records that disagree 1

Mother still unknown, with a conflict to resolve. (1) Samuel Larsen's 2nd wife Britte Rasmusdatter died before 7 Jun 1709. Her probate (A 4a fol. 171a) names only 'deres sammen avlede Børn' Britte Samuelsdatter, 16, and Synneve Samuelsdatter, 6. Lars is not among them, so she was not his mother. (2) Aaland's death aged 75 in 1789 puts Lars's birth at about 1714, after that marriage, which points to the 3rd wife Ane Paulsdatter (widow in 1747, d. 1750 aged 71). (3) But the heirs paragraph of Samuel's 1747 probate (A 10 fol. 16b), seems to call all the children, Lars included, 'den Sal. Mands med hans for[rige?] Hustruer avlede Børn', i.e. children of his earlier wives. It also calls Lars the guardian of the elder Brite Samuelsdatter, apparently as 'Halvbroderen' (her half-brother). The reading 'forrige' is uncertain. If it is right, Ane Paulsdatter was not Lars's mother, and either Aaland's age is wrong (Lars would be a son of the 1st wife,...Hansdatter, born before about 1692) or there was another wife. No mother is linked until this is settled. Would settle: a clearer reading of A 10 fol. 16b (the words after 'med hans'), or Ane Paulsdatter's own probate (about 1750, Loen; not found in the Loen index 1747-1760, so search under Olden or by her name). ¶7
