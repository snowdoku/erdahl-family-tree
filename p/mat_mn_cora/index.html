About the family

The fourth child of John P. Eidem and Oline Slind, previously unknown to this project, and the child whose existence disproves the received account of this sibship. ¶1

Her mother's October 1955 obituary gives her married name and home: 'Mrs. Elmer Peterson (Cora) of Tracy, Minn.' Tracy is in Lyon County, Minnesota. ¶6

Life story

Birth registration: Minnesota birth card no. 11743 of 1907, Lac qui Parle County, read from the record image. Typed: 'Birth 1907 Sept 25 Mehurin tp L.Q.P / Cora Belinde Eidem / W / F / John P. " Farmer Nor 3o / Oline Slind Nor 26 / Dr. Duncan Marietta / G. L. Schckelford.' This is the first of the family's registrations to write both parents out in full - John P. Eidem and Oline Slind, both born in Norway, aged 30 and 26 - and it is therefore the single clearest documentary statement of the marriage that this project has. ¶2

Her birth on 25 September 1907 is what makes it impossible for Ludwig Eidem, born 30 October 1907, to be a child of this couple: thirty-five days separate them, and the 1909 certificate of the next child records five children born to this mother and five living. ¶4

A daughter of John P. Eidem and Oline G. Slind who was unknown to this project, and the only one of the eight whom Find a Grave itself records: memorial 166659963, 'Cora Belinde Eidem Peterson, 1907-1987', is listed as a child on both her father's and her mother's memorials, and as the sister of Peder Conrad Eidem. ¶5

She is not buried at Zoar and is not in the South Dakota state burial register. ¶7

Her birth year of 1907 is the same as Ludwig Eidem's, which is part of why the two cannot both be children of this couple; the obituary decides it for Cora. ¶8

Censuses: in her father's Mehurin household in 1910 ('Cora B Eidem', b. 1908), 1920 and 1930 (b. abt 1908), and gone by 1940 - so she married between April 1930 and April 1940 (to Elmer Peterson, per her mother's 1955 obituary). ¶9

Married name and death: she died 7 February 1987 in Lyon County, Minnesota, as Cora B. Peterson - Minnesota death certificate index 1987-MN-004068, birth date 25 September 1907, birthplace Minnesota, mother's maiden name SLIND. Day-exact match to the 1907 card. ¶3
