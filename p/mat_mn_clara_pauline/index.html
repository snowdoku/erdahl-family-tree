Life story

Birth registration: Minnesota birth card no. 11302 of 1902, Lac qui Parle County, read from the record image. 'BIRTH, 1902, July 7, Mehurin, L.q.P. Name: Clara Pauline Eidem. White. Female. Father's name: John Eidem. Occupation: Farmer. Birthplace: Nor. Age 25. Mother's maiden name: Lena. Birthplace: Nor. Age 21. Number of child: 1. Attending physician or midwife: None. Reporter: R. W. Hobbs, Marietta.' ¶2

Registered under a different name: the card was written up in 1902 as 'Alvira A. Eidem'. That name is struck through and 'Clara Pauline' written above it, and the card is stamped 'ALTERED COR. FILE AUG 28 1957 - F. Engelbrecht - Pursuant to Affidavit of Father'. So on 28 August 1957 her father John P. Eidem, then eighty, swore an affidavit to correct his daughter's birth record, fifty-five years after the event - most probably because she needed a birth certificate for Social Security. The household had been calling her Clara from the start: she is 'Eidem Clara, F, 3' on the 1905 state census. ¶3

1905 state census: Mehurin Township, Lac qui Parle County, enumeration district 1, schedule sheet 4, page 243, line 6 - 'Eidem Clara, F, 3, W, born Minn., father born Norway, mother born Norway', resident of the state 3 years 1 month. ¶4

1905 Minnesota state census: Mehurin township, ED 1, sheet 4, line 6 - 'Eidem Clara, F, 3, W, born Minnesota, father born Norway, mother born Norway'. Her first census appearance. ¶11

She is in her father's household in 1910 and 1920 and gone by 1930, so she married between January 1920 and April 1930 (to Julius Jornlin, per her mother's 1955 obituary). ¶12

A daughter of John P. Eidem and Oline G. Slind who was unknown to this project. Her mother's October 1955 obituary names her first of the eight children: 'Mrs. Julius Jornlin (Clara) of Thief River Falls'. ¶7

Burial: Zoar Lutheran Cemetery, Antelope Valley Township, Deuel County, South Dakota, lot 12 — South Dakota state burial register, 'CLARA PAULINE JORNLIN 04-23-1976, lot 12, block 0'. Her husband Julius G. Jornlin is in the same register, d. 13 May 1982, with no lot recorded. Neither has a Find a Grave memorial, so this churchyard's Find a Grave listing does not show them. ¶8

In 1955 the couple were living at Thief River Falls, Pennington County, Minnesota, a long way north of the colony; they came home to Zoar to be buried. ¶9

Married name and death: she died 23 April 1976 in Becker County, Minnesota, as Clara Pauline Jornlin - Minnesota death certificate index 1976-MN-008758, which gives her birth date as 7 July 1902, her birthplace as Minnesota and her mother's maiden name as SLIND. Birth date, birthplace and mother's maiden name all match the 1902 card exactly, which is what identifies the married woman with the registered child. Her husband's given name is not in the index; her marriage is not in the Lac qui Parle county marriage index, so she married outside the county. ¶5

Confidence: Confirmed. Two civil records sixty-four years apart agree on an uncommon birth date, a county of birth and a mother's maiden name, and the 1902 record names both parents. ¶6

Rated Probable: the relationship is from a printed obituary and the burial from a state register, but no birth record, marriage record or gravestone photograph has been retrieved. The middle name Pauline is from the burial register. ¶10
