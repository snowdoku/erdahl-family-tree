About the family

Probable father of Povel Hansen: the bailiff writes 'Povel Hansen', 30, in the sons column under Hans of Thue, and the 1666 list gives Povl 34. He is the only Povel in Tjugum parish in the three lists read, and Ytre Esse was held by two men named Anders in 1664-66, so Povel Hansen of Ytre Esse (1701: 66) came there later. ¶2a

Life story

1645 poll tax: 'Hanns' is the sole farmer on 'Thue' (Ytre Sogn, Quambsøe Skibredt, p. 50, pt00000000058599), listed with his wife Giertrud Andersdtr. With the 1666 age of 66 he was about 45 in 1645. The poll tax places Tue in Kvamsøy skipreide; by 1664 it was listed with Tjugum sogn, Leikanger. ¶3

Vicar's census 1666 (23 May), Tjugum sogn N.1: 'Tue... Hans bruger 66 aar', with sons 'Povl 34', 'Ander[s] 32' and 'Engelbret fosterson 9 aar' (foster son), servant Knud 16, and Oluf 28 and Hans 12 in the husmann (cottager/lodger) column. Bailiff's census 21 Jun 1664: 'Thue, Hans' with farmer's sons '30: Povel Hansen' and '26: Endre[?] Hansen', servants Erich [unread] 18 and Knud Mathiesen[?] 12. Vicar's 1664 list: 'Tue - Hans - 25 Anders, 18 Erich, 13 Knud'. Birth about 1600 from the 1666 age; patronymic not given. ¶1

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

Open questions and the next record to read 1

What would confirm it: a farm transfer or tingbok entry for Ytre Esse c. 1666-1701 naming Povel Hansen Tue, or Hans Tue's probate naming son Povel at Esse. ¶2c
