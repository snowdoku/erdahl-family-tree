About the family

NEW ANCESTOR. First wife of Niels Olsen Eppeland and mother of Christi Nielsdatter (born about 1734), who married Tergie Tjøstelsen and was the mother of Birgitte Terjesdatter. ¶1

Her father was a Knud. Her own 1742 probate has not yet been read. ¶3

Life story

Marriage search, negative. The Åmli register 1711-1740 (DA 9920) was read for Sep 1729 to May 1733 (canvases 50-63, pp. 92-121). It has no betrothal or marriage of Niels Olsen Eppeland and an Ingri Knudsdatter. In the same book, at 'Dom. 6 p. Pascha' 1731 in Omblie, 'Niels Olsøn Eppelands barn Knud' was baptised (canvas 55, p. 106). A son named Knud fits her father's name, but proves nothing. The marriage was therefore before Sep 1729: canvases about 40-49 (1725-1729) are not yet read for it. Or it took place in the bride's own parish outside Åmli. The 1733 Eppeland estate of Anna Knudsdatter (card 2371, wife of Tellef Aslaksen) is still the only Knudsdatter lead nearby. ¶7

Marriage found, Åmli register 1711-1740 (DA 9920), read on the scan. Betrothal: 'Den 20 ejusdem [June 1726] trolovet Niels Olsøn Eppeland og Inger Knudsdatter' (canvas 42, p. 79). Marriage: 'Den 12 July i Omblie copul. Niels Olsøn Eppeland og Inger Knudsdatter' (canvas 43, p. 80), so 12 Jul 1726 at the Åmli main church. Neither entry names her father, her farm or any sureties, so her parentage is still open. The name is written Inger here and Ingri/Yngri on the probate cards; these are the same woman (same husband, Eppeland, and the son Knud baptised 1731). Canvases 40-49 (late 1725 to Mar 1729) were read in full; this is the only marriage of Niels Olsen. Next for her father: a Knud's estate (Østre Råbyggelag card index) naming a daughter Inger/Ingri married to Niels Olsen Eppeland, or the protocol text of her own 25 Jan 1742 estate, which may name her guardian or brother. ¶8

Burial: 'Dom. Septuag. i Omblie... overstaaet Niels Olsøn Eppelands Kone Inger Knudsdatter 37 aar gl.' (Åmli register 1741-1756, DA 9921 canvas 2, 1742, read on the scan). Septuagesima 1742 was 21 Jan (Easter 25 Mar), four days before her estate was registered on 25 Jan 1742. Aged 37, so born about 1705, which matches the tree. The burial names her as Niels Olsen's wife, the same woman he married in Jul 1726. ¶9

Her estate was settled on 25 January 1742, as recorded on the card for the 1774 probate of her daughter: "mor: Ingri Knudsdtr. + sk. 25/1-1742". She is also named in her husband's 1755 probate, where the children of his first marriage are specified as being "med Yngri Knudsdatter": Christj, 21, and Anna, 18. ¶2

Her own estate found and read. Card 2375: 'Skifteprotokoll nr. 10, fol. 239b, 25/1 1742 - 3?/3 s.å. Eppeland i Åmli, "Nedre Eppeland". Ingier Knudsdtr. †, [g.m.] Niels Olssøn († sk. 19/7 1755). Kristi Nielsdtr. 8 år (senere g.m. Tergie Tiøstelsen, sk. på Eppeland 10/1 1769), Anna 3 år. Enkemannens far: Ole Ormssøn øvre Eppeland; bror: Orm Olssøn; svoger: Aslach Tellefssøn nedre Eppeland. Bruker 1½ hud i Eppeland.' The protocol (fol. 239b-240a, read on the scan) opens 'Anno Christi 1742 Løverdagen den 25 Januarij' at Neder Eppeland in Omblie annex parish, after the late Ingier Knudsdatter, between her widower Niels Olsøn and their children Kristi Nielsdatter, 8, and Anna Nielsdatter, 3. Present for the widower were his father, lagrettesmann Ole Ormsøn of Øvre Eppeland, his brother Orm Olsøn, and Aslach Tellefsøn of Nedre Eppeland. No relative on Ingri's side is named, so the estate does not identify her father Knud. It confirms her death before 25 Jan 1742, her marriage and her daughter Kristi. ¶4

Her own skifte read: Skifteprotokoll nr. 10 fol. 239b-240a, 25 Jan to 3 Mar 1742, Nedre Eppeland (DA 24358 view 241; card 39942/2375): after 'Ingier Knudsdatter', husband Niels Olsøn; children Kristie Nielsdatter 8 and Anna Nielsdatter 3; guardians and kin from the husband's side only (his father Ole Ormsøn øvre Eppeland, brother Orm Olsøn, brother-in-law Aslach Tellefsøn Nedre Eppeland). Her father or any of her own kin are not named. ¶10

Searches that found nothing 3

Search: the Eppeland card 2371 (Skp. 8 fol. 395, 28 May 1733) is the estate of an Anna Knudsdatter, wife of Tellef Aslachsøn, with children Aslach Tellefsøn, Anna (m. Gunsten Rolfsøn Kiilane) and Berthe, 36. This Anna Knudsdatter of Eppeland could be a sister or aunt of Ingri, and her son Aslach Tellefsøn attended Ingri's estate in 1742, but no record read states a relationship. Lead only. ¶5

Search: Eppeland card 2363-2366 (Skifteprotokoll nr. 6 fol. 8b, 29 May 1721, Nedre Eppeland) is the estate of a Knud Evindsøn, wife Kari Børuldsdtr., children Evind 18, Torkild 15, Tharald 12 and Maritha (unmarried). No Ingri is listed, although she would have been about 16, so this Knud of Nedre Eppeland is probably not her father. Guardians named there: Alf Aasuldsøn Sigridnes (the deceased's first cousin), Børru Gullaugsøn Haagendal, Joen Næresøn Tved, and Børru Børrusen Vraalstad (the widow's brother). ¶6

Negatives: she was not a daughter of Knud Evindsen of Nedre Eppeland, whose skifte of 29 May 1721 (Skp. 6 fol. 8b; card 2363) lists the children Evind 18, Torkild 15, Tharald 12 and Maritha only. The 18 Oct 1738 Nedre Eppeland skifte after Ingebor Knudsdatter (Skp. 9 fol. 329b, DA 24357 view 333, read on the scan) names as heirs only her sister 'Ingebor Knudsdatter, ugift' and the children of her dead sister Gunild Knudsdatter (wife of Svenning Olsen): Ole Svenningsen and Birgithe Svenningsdatter. Ingri, then married and living at Nedre Eppeland, is not among them, unless the second 'Ingebor... ugift' is the clerk's slip for her, which the word 'ugift' (unmarried) makes unlikely. Her marriage record (1726) gives no home, so she may have come from outside Åmli. ¶11
