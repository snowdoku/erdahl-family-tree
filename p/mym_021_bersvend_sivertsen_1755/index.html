About the family

Children traced in the Selbu registers, in addition to the 1801 household: Siur (Sivert), home-baptised, buried 2 Feb 1783 aged 14 days (his churching of the mother, 'Bersv. Siurs. Draxens Hust.', is entered the same day, so the child is taken to be theirs - inferred); Sivert, baptised 12 Apr 1784 (godparents all of Draxen); Sigri (Siri), baptised 6 Aug 1786, who married Jon Sivertsen of Eidem in 1812. The 1801 children Marit (b. about 1780), Helge (b. about 1789), Peder (b. about 1791), Beret (b. about 1796) and the younger Siri (b. about 1800) have not been checked against their baptisms; Helge and Sivert stood godparents together at their sister Siri's first child's baptism in October 1812. ¶5

Father: Probably Siur (Sivert) Bersvendsen of Draxen (see his page), buried 29 June 1766 aged 50 - the confirmation's patronymic and farm fit him, Bersvend carries his grandfather's name and gave the name Siur/Sivert to his own sons. ¶11a

Child not previously known: Torkild, baptised Dom. 4 p. Epiph. 1781 (28 Jan 1781): 'Döbt Torkild. Par: Bersv. Siurs. Lisbeth Torchelsd. T. Halv. Varmd[?] Niels ib. Bersv. O. Drax. Ingeb. ib. Marit ib.' Read from the scan (source 16311, canvas 104 left page). Not in the 1801 household, so probably died young; burial not yet sought. Named for the maternal grandfather. ¶12

Child not previously in tree: Siur, home-baptised, born c. 31 Dec 1782, died 14 Jan 1783 aged 14 days, buried Dom. 4 p. Epiph. 1783 (2 Feb 1783): 'Begravne... 2. Siur Bersv. Draxen død d. 14 Jan: 14 døgr gl. Hiemmedøbt.' The same Sunday: 'Introd.... 4. Bersv. Siurs. Draxens Hust.' (churching of Bersvend Siursen Draxen's wife). Read from the scan (source 16311, canvas 109 right page). No church baptism entry; the name was then reused for Sivert b. 1784 (see his page). ¶13

Father: upgraded from Probable to Confirmed - the 1766 probate after Sivert Bersvendsen of Draxen names his son Bersvend Sivertsen as heir with Ole Bersvendsen Draxen as guardian, the same Ole Bersvendsen Draxen who was Bersvend's forlover in 1778. Bersvend had one full sister, Ingebor Sivertsdatter, a minor in 1766. His age in the probate is not legible ('13[?]'). ¶14

Mother linked at Probable: Marit Andersdatter, wife of his father Siur Bersvendsen at Draxen in the 1762 extra tax list. ¶15

Life story

Confirmation: confirmed in Selbu church on Misericordias Domini Sunday, 3 May 1772, entered in Peder Holmen's school district as no. 5, 'Bersv. Siverts. Draxen' - Bersvend Sivertsen of Draxen. No age is given; the 1772 confirmands were mostly about 15-17, which fits a birth about 1755. He is the only Bersvend Sivertsen in any Selbu confirmation list from 1764 to 1775. Read from the scan (source 16311, canvas 51, left page). ¶10

Marriage to Lisbet Torkelsdatter not yet found; it should fall about 1778-80 (eldest child Marit about 1780) in the Selbu register 1757-1801, source 16311 canvases about 80-106. ¶7

Marriage: betrothed in Selbu on Esto mihi Sunday (Quinquagesima), 1 March 1778: 'Trolovede Bersvend Siurs: J. Lisbeth Torchelsd. Sp. Arent Vig Olle Bersv. Draxen' - Bersvend Sivertsen and Lisbet Torkelsdatter; sponsors (forlovere) Arent [of] Vig and Ole Bersvendsen of Draxen. Married on the 1st Sunday after Trinity, 21 June 1778: 'Cop. Bersvend Siurs: Drax: J. Lisbeth Torkiedsd.' Neither entry gives the bride's farm. Read from the scans (source 16311, canvas 87 left page and canvas 89 right page). This replaces the estimate 'before 1781' and the note that the marriage had not been found. Ole Bersvendsen of Draxen, his sponsor, was a householder at Draxen in 1757-58 and may be kin (Bersvend may be named for an older Bersvend of Draxen) - not proved. ¶8

Name: the registers write his patronymic both as 'Siurs.' and as 'Siverts.' - Siur is the Selbu spoken form of Sivert, and the 1784 baptism of his son Sivert spells it out as 'Bersv. Siverts. Draxen'. Draxen (Draksten, Draxten) is the farm he lived on all his recorded life; it is used here as a byname because every record attaches it to him. ¶1

1801 census: head of household 04 on rural residence 0001 Draxen, Selbu, 'Bersvend Sivertsen', aged 45, with his wife Lisbet Torkelsdatter, 46, and the children Marit 21, Sivert 17, Siri 14, Helge 12, Peder 10, Beret 5 and Siri 1. Draxen had five households and 27 people that year; household 02 was another, unrelated Bersvend - Bersvend Olssen, 46, with Karen Olsdatter. ¶2

Silver tax 1816: 'Bergsvend Sivertsen', Draxen (farm 421), leilænding (tenant), 5 spd, round of 27 Aug 1816 - alive and holding Draxen in Aug 1816 (the 1801 census called him farmer and occupier). (the 1816 silver tax was levied on property holders in several rounds, 1816-1821.) ¶16

1825 census: 'Bersvend Siverts.', aged 71, gaardmand (a farmer holding the farm), head of the household at Draxen og Tangvold. With him were his son Sivert (b. 1785 by that census) with wife Beret Olsdatter and five children, his son Peder Bersvendsen (b. 1791), a husmand (cottager), with wife Agnis Olsdatter and four children, and an inderst (lodger) Marit Torkelsdatter, b. 1752 - possibly a sister of his wife, not proved. His wife Lisbet is not in the household and was probably dead by then; no death or burial of hers has been found. ¶3

Death and burial: the Selbu register for 1827 (page 366, no. 30), read from the scan, enters 'Bersvend Siverthsen, Gaardmand', aged 75, residence Draxen, died 21 July and buried 26 August 1827. The index omits his farm and status; the scan supplies both, and they match the 1825 head of Draxen. Ages: 45 in Feb 1801 (b. about 1755-56), 71 in Nov 1825 (about 1754), 75 at death (about 1752) - the drift upward with age is usual; about 1755 is taken from the earliest record. ¶4

Searches that found nothing 1

Baptism not found - no register survives: Selbu has no surviving baptism register for 1747-1756. Book 16310 ends in 1737, book 16315 covers only 1740-1746, and book 16311 begins on 1 January 1757 (who also read every baptism from 1 Jan 1757 to 25 Mar 1758 without finding him). Born about 1752-1756 by his ages in 1801 (45), 1825 (71) and at burial in 1827 (75), he falls in that gap, so his own baptism, and with it the names of his parents, is lost. What remains: the confirmation lists in book 16311 (about 1764-1775, now being read - they usually give the father or the farm), and probate (skifte) - the Strinda og Selbu probate protocols cover Selbu only from 1797, so a skifte after his death in 1827 could name heirs but not the parents. ¶9
