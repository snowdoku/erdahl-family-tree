Life story

Burial: Selbu Cemetery, Whitman County - "Ingeborg A Slind, b. 1884 d. 1959, s/w Ingeborg O and Ole G", reference hrp-cem-whi-selbu_0121. The A is her patronymic ANDERSDATTER, Anders Mogard's daughter, just as the first wife's O was Olsdatter. ¶4

MOGARD (Mogaard, Mogard) is another Selbu farm name, so she was from the same parish. Her own birthplace in Norway, her emigration and her first marriage (if any) are not yet established; nothing read so far says whether she came to America independently or married Ole in Minnesota or in Washington. The marriage itself has not been found - it falls in the gap between 1930 and 1950 and would be in a Minnesota or Washington county register. ¶5

Independently confirmed on Find a Grave: "Ingeborg A MOGARD Slind, 1884 - 1959, Selbu Cemetery, La Crosse, Whitman County, Washington" (memorial 32434358). The maiden name Mogard is in the memorial's title, from the stone or from the family, and agrees with the 1959 death certificate's "Father: Anders Mogard". ¶7

SHE IS THE RECORD THAT TIES THE MINNESOTA HOUSEHOLD TO COLFAX. The 1950 census found her at Mehurin Township, Lac qui Parle County, Minnesota, line 10, "Ingeborg, wife, W F 65, married, born Norway" - and she died at Colfax, Whitman County, Washington on 1 March 1959, aged 75. The woman sitting beside Ole in a Minnesota farmhouse in April 1950 is buried under his stone in the Selbu churchyard in eastern Washington. That single pair of documents proves the move without appealing to the children, the obituary or his age, and it settles the question of whether the 1950 Mehurin "Ole Slind" is Oline's brother: he is. ¶2

Death certificate: "Ingeborg Slind, date of death 1 Mar 1959, age 75, female, death place Colfax, Whitman Co., Washington; note on death place: St. Ignatius Hospital. Father name: Anders Mogard. Mother name: Brynhild Mogard." Washington DOH Death Certificates, DA reference {C7283F3A-C170-44BA-838C-E3E2A7E5978A}, image 1540, document 32, doc. ref. id 6391/21. Age 75 in March 1959 gives a birth in 1883 or 1884. ¶3
