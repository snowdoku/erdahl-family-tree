About the family

Children by the 1756 probate: Gunder Halvorsen (of age in 1756), Joen Halvorsen (22), Torgeir (Torgiei, Targe) Halvorsen (13) and Margit Halvorsdatter (16). The widow Taarand Bjørnsdatter had earlier been married to Gunder Svendsen, and her children by him (the eldest son Svend Gundersen, and daughters, one married to Aslak Knudsen) also appear in the division (fol. 300b-301a, read at overview scale only). ¶3

Life story

Probate: 'Anno 1756 den 17 Maj' at 'gaarden Nordre Grimstvedt af Nissedals Annex og Hvideseid Præstegjeld', with lensmann Jacob Nordbøe and the appraisers Kittil Mule and Tallef Tvetten, to register and value the estate 'efter afgangne Halvor Torgiersøn'. The division was 'Mellem hans efterlevende Enke Taarand Biørnsdatter og deris i Ægteskab tilsammen avlede Børn, Nl.: Gunder Halvorsøn myndig, Joen Halvorsøn 22 aar, Torgiei Halvorsøn 13 aar, Margith Halvorsdatter 16 aar' (between his surviving widow Taarand Bjørnsdatter and the children they had together: Gunder, of age; Joen, 22; Torgeir, 13; Margit, 16; the ages of Torgeir and Margit are uncertain readings). The shares that follow name 'Joen Halvorsøn' and his land share 'udj Grimstvedt'. Vest-Telemark (Øvre Telemark vestfjelske) sorenskriveri, skifteprotokoll 1752-1759 (source 25673), fol. 300a (view 304, right page, foot) to fol. 301 (view 305); register entry 'Grimstvedt ibm efter Halvor Targiersøn', column 599 (register bound in 25675, view 862L). Read from the scan. ¶1

Guardians in the same probate (fol. 301b, view 307L): 'Gunder Halvorsøn ordineres til Curator for Broderen Joen Halvorsøn' (Gunder Halvorsen appointed curator for his brother Joen Halvorsen), and also guardian for the youngest brother Torgiei Halvorsen; Svend Gundersen, the widow's eldest son, was guardian for the daughters Guroe Gundersdatter and Margith Halvorsdatter. The shares are headed 'Ældste Søn' Svend Gundersen, 'Anden Søn' Gunder Halvorsen, [third son Joen] and 'Fierde Søn Targiej Halvorsen', counting the widow's sons by both marriages. ¶4

Deeds: panteregister I 1 (10915, fol. 11, view 52 right), Grimstvedt Nordre in Nissedal: Aslach Torbjørnsen sold land 'til Halvor B[iørg]usen', tingl. 16 Oct 1747; then 'Halvor [the same]... accorderet med Svend Gundersen og Gunder Halvorsen', tingl. 16 Oct 1752 at Nordbø; Gunder Halvorsen registered a deed for his 'Bødstue' (?) 11 Mar 1757 and a mortgage 17 Oct 1758. Svend Gundersen (the widow Taarand's eldest son by her first marriage) and Gunder Halvorsen (Halvor's son) are exactly the two elder sons of the 1756 probate, so this Halvor is surely Halvor Torgiersen; but the patronymic in the register looks like 'Biørgusen' (Bjørgulfsen; a Torgrim Bjørgulfsen held Nordre Grimstvedt in 1723), not Torgiersen. The probate of 1756 (primary, written at the estate) is preferred; the register is a later abstract. Discrepancy noted, no change to his grade. Earlier owners on the same farm: Torgier Wigleichsen (Vigleiksen), 1694-95, and Aslach Torgiersen, 1720; a Torgier is a possible father for Halvor Torgiersen (born about 1700), but nothing ties them (inference only, not added). ¶5

How sure are we?

1 on the grade · 1 alternative set aside

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Confidence: Confirmed as father of Joen Halvorsen Grimstvedt (see his page). The probate names a son Joen Halvorsen with a share in Grimstvedt. The tree's Joen Halvorsen Grimstvedt was an unmarried 'unge Karl' of Grimstvedt at his betrothal in 1762, lived and died there, and in 1790 sat as lagrettemann together with 'Targe Halvorsen Grimstvedt', who matches the son Torgiei Halvorsen of this probate. ¶2a

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Caveat: the probate gives Joen as 22 in 1756 (born about 1734), while his 1801 census age (75) and his 1808 burial age (82) give about 1726. Inflated ages in old age are common, and no other Joen Halvorsen is known at Grimstvedt. ¶2b
