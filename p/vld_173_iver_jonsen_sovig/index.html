About the family

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Graded Probable on his link to his daughter Ane Iversdatter. The Gjemnes farm history (Gards- og ættesoge for Gjemnes, bind 10, Søvik farm, canvases 351-352) gives 'Brukar 5: Iver Jonson... brukar frå ca. 1695 til ca. 1732', 'Iver f. ca. 1665, d. 1744, 85 år gamal', and continues '[det ser] ut som Iver hadde desse barna': 1. Jon, b. about 1695, m. about 1722 Gunhild Pålsdotter Stokke; 2. Eli, b. about 1696; 3. Ole, b. 1709 (brukar 6); 4. Beret, b. 1696, m. Gunder Pederson Søvik; 5. Iver, b. about 1708, m. 1747 Marit Olsdotter; 6. Elling, b. about 1711, m. 1743 Guri Pedersdotter; 7. Marit, b. about 1719, m. 1742 Peder...; 8. Knut, b. about 1725, m. Ellen Hansdotter; 9. 'Ane f. 1707, d. 1776, g. 1730 [med] Sivert Nilsson Øverland, f. ca. 1706'. The book itself hedges the list ('det ser ut som'). ¶1

The case for this identification 1

Supporting records (inferred, not a stated relationship): the 1701 manntall for Søeuig lists 'Ifuer Jons.' b. 1665 with his son 'Joen Ifuers.' b. 1695, matching the bygdebok's eldest son Jon (b. about 1695); he is the only Iver at Søvig in 1701. At the baptism of Sivert Øverland's daughter Ane on 21 Sep 1738 (Kvernes 16075 view 7, p. 8; mother Ane) the godparents were Peder Søvigen, Ole Søvigen, Elling Iversen 'ibid.', Iver Søvigen's wife Ragnhild and Marrit Iversdatter Søvigen, the bygdebok's Ole, Elling and Marit. In 1739 Ole Iversen Søvig was betrothed to Ane Jensdatter Astad on Dom. 3 Adv. 1739 (16075), as the bygdebok has it. This is a sibling pattern, so it stays Probable. A record naming Iver Jonsen as Ane's father (a probate after him, about 1744, or one after any of the siblings that names Ane as sister) would confirm it. No Søvig cards are in the Nordmøre probate card index 77795. ¶2

Records that disagree 1

Conflict to note: the bygdebok's 'd. 1744, 85 år gamal' implies a birth about 1659, against 1665 from the 1701 manntall. The Kvernes 1744 burial (16075, about views 31-36) has not been read. ¶3

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Lead closed: Synnøve Iversdatter, wife of Ole Ingebrigtsen of Berge, Gjemnes, is NOT a Søvig daughter. Her probate (Nordmøre skifteprotokoll 8b, 24642, no. 58, fol. 542b, 1745, view 115) names only her children Jacob and Marit as heirs, so it cannot name Iver Jonsen's children; and the Gjemnes bygdebok (bind 7, Høgset chapter, canvas 575) places her as a daughter of Iver Torsteinsen of Høgset (see his page). It does not bear on Ane Iversdatter Øverland. ¶7

Searches that found nothing 1

Negatives for his 1744 probate: the typed first-name register at the end of skifteprotokoll 8b, 1741-1746 (24642, views 409-417, read in full) has no Iver Jonsen and no entry at Søvig (the I page lists Iver Andersen Barlipen, Iver Olsen Kaasholmen, Iver Baarsen, Iver Estensen Stokke, Iver Knudsen Ellerhaug, Iver Iversen Gløsvog only); the 8c register (24643 view 362) has no Iver Jonsen either; and the Nordmøre probate card index 1668-1746 (77795) has no Søvig card at all (the S cards run Søfstad 4031-4035, Søisel 4041, Sørdalen 4051, Sørvik 4053, then T at 4057). So no probate after him survives in the sorenskriver's protocols 1741-50; his grade on Ane Iversdatter stays Probable. A Synnøve Iversdatter, wife of Ole Ingebrigtsen Berge, Gjemnes, has a probate in 24642 (register view 416, fol. 542b?); a possible daughter not in the bygdebok's list, unread. Panteregister 22675 views 93-116 (1744-1750) have no Søvig or Øverland deed. ¶6
