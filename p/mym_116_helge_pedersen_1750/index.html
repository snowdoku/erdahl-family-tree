Life story

Negative search of Beitstad 16433 (scan read). Marriages: right page of canvas 174 (Mar-Aug 1788), canvas 175 (Sep 1788-Aug 1789), and canvas 173 at overview resolution (Jul 1786-Oct 1787) have no groom Helge. Baptisms: canvases 206 (Oct-Nov 1799), 208 (Mar-Apr 1800) and 210 (Sep-Oct 1800), with 207 and 209, have no child Mogns and no father Helge. Mogns (1 in Feb 1801) was therefore not baptised in Beitstad between Oct 1799 and Oct 1800, and Helge did not marry there in 1788-89. The family seems to have come to Beitstad only after the 1801 census (Augdahl, Frosta), so the older children's baptisms and the marriage of c.1787-88 should be sought in Frosta's neighbours (Åsen, Skogn, Levanger, Stjørdal) rather than Beitstad. Canvas map: c155 = 1777, c172 = 1785-86, c173 = 1786-87, c174 = Nov 1787-Aug 1788, c175 = Sep 1788-Aug 1789, c176 = Sep 1789-Jul 1790. ¶6

The 1801 Frosta census household at Augdahl (Helge Pedersen 51, husmann uden jord, first marriage; Martha Olsdatter 39; Ole 6, Anne 3, Mogns 1) is joined by a daughter 'Martha Helgesdatter 12, tienere' in household 1 of the same farm (b. c.1789, so the marriage was c.1787-88). No Frosta baptism in 1778-1811 has a father named Helge (index search, father filter and first-name search), so the children of 1789-1800 were baptised elsewhere, most likely in Beitstad, which fits Karen's Beitstad baptism in 1802. Beitstad 16433 (chronological list 1722-1804, scan only) is mapped: canvas 195 = Dec 1796/Jan 1797, 205 = 1799 (fol. 203), 207 = Dec 1799-Feb 1800, 209 = May-Aug 1800, 215 = Jul-Sep 1801 (fol. 213); about four canvases a year. Canvases 207 and 209 were read: no Mogns son of Helge. ¶5a

Of Fosliepladsen, Beitstad (Nord-Trøndelag) in 1802; named as 'Helge Peders af Bedstaden' at his daughter Karen's marriage in 1831. Probable father of Karen Helgesdatter (see her page). Probable: Karen's marriage at Frosta on 3 Feb 1831 gives her birthplace as Bedstad (Beitstad) and her father as 'Helge Peders af Bedstaden' (a second index transcription reads 'Helge, Juberg'); the Beitstad baptism of 10 Oct 1802 of Karen, daughter of Helge and Martha of Fosliepladsen, fits her age (35 in Feb 1838). A rival Karen Helgesdatter baptised at Åmot on 13 Feb 1803 (father Helge Taralsen) does not fit the Beitstad birthplace. The Frosta 1801 couple Helge Pedersen 51 and Martha Olsdatter 39 (Augdahl) may be the same pair but nothing yet ties them to Beitstad; Martha's patronymic is unproved. Both scans (marriage p. 168, baptism p. 219a) are queued for reading. ¶1

Further support: a Frosta vaccination record of 1830 names 'Karen Helgesdr.' with father 'Helge Peders' of Foslien (pz00000000232699), agreeing with her 1802 Beitstad baptism at Fosliepladsen. The 1801 Frosta census family at Augdahl (Helge Pedersen 51, husmann/day labourer; Martha Olsdatter 39; Ole, Anne and Mogns Helgesen) is probably the same: Mogns b. c.1800 is probably the Mons Helgesen of Holdahl who married in Beitstad on 16 Apr 1827 with father Helge Pedersen (pv00000009034420). Still inferred; no record names Martha Olsdatter as Karen's mother. The Beitstad index starts in 1801 (baptisms) and 1802 (marriages), so Helge's marriage and the older children's baptisms are not indexed. A Helge Pedersen b. c.1750 buried at Molnaaen, Beitstad, 7 Jan 1810 (pg00000001355458) is probably a different man, unresolved. ¶4

Patronymic read on the scan: the Frosta 1831 marriage entry (p. 168) names the bride's father 'Helge Pedersen af Bedstaden' in full. The 1802 Beitstad baptism scan (p. 219) gives only 'Helge og Martha Fosliepladsens', no patronymic. Identity with the Helge Pedersen, 51, of Augdahl, Frosta (1801 census) is still an inference. ¶3

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: Confirmed, graded on his link to his daughter Karen Helgesdatter (see her page), not on his own identity before 1802 (the Augdahl, Frosta, 1801 identification stays unproved). The record naming the relationship is Karen's own marriage at Frosta, 3 Feb 1831 (Frosta ministerialbok 1827-1839, p. 168, scan): 'Pige Karen Helgesd[atter] Juberg 28 Aar Datter [af] Helge Pedersen af Bedstaden, fød i Bedstad efter Attest af 30te October 1830 af Pastor Fyhn' (daughter of Helge Pedersen of Beitstad, born in Beitstad by a certificate of 30 Oct 1830). Continuity to the Beitstad family: the Beitstad baptism of 10 Oct 1802 (p. 219) names 'Helge og Martha Fosliepladsens' as the parents of Karen, and a Frosta vaccination record of 1830 names 'Karen Helgesdr.' with father 'Helge Peders' of Foslien (pz00000000232699). ¶8a

Alternatives considered and set aside 3

Birth year is a rough estimate (possibly the Helge Pedersen, 51, of Augdahl, Frosta, in the 1801 census - unproved). ¶2

No rival: the only other indexed Karen with a father Helge in these years (Åmot, Hedmark, 13 Feb 1803) has a father Helge Taralsen, not Pedersen. ¶8b

Burial candidate, not adopted: 'Helge Pedersen', born 1750 per the index, of Mølnaaen, buried 7 Jan 1810 at Beitstad (Beitstad ministerialbok 1804-1816; index pg00000001355458). He is the only Helge Pedersen born 1742-1758 in the Beitstad burials and the age fits, but Mølnaaen is not Fosliepladsen, where the family lived in 1802, so no date is entered. ¶11

Searches that found nothing 3

Negative, index: no Helge appears as a father 1786-1800 in any indexed register near Frosta. The Frosta book 16379 (1778-1811, which also covers Åsen) has only one Helge in its baptism list before 1800, Helge of Buset, who was a godfather in 1782-88; he is probably the widower Helge Jacobsen of Buset, d. 1798 aged 88. The person index has only 3 Helges in Frosta and Åsen up to 1800, none married there. Stjørdal 1786-1801 has none either, and neither does Verdal 2481 (1781-1804). The nationwide baptism list (father Helge, 1786-1801) gives only Snåsa (Helge Olsen; and a Helge Pedersen in 1790, probably the father of Gollov at Svarva whose mother was Sigrid Pedersdatter, so a different couple) and Klæbu (Helge Larsen, 1795) for Trøndelag. So the children Martha (c.1789), Ole (c.1795), Anne (c.1798) and Mogns (c.1800) were baptised in an unindexed register. The candidates are Skogn 16391 (1747-1803) and Alstadhaug 16392 (1783-1809), whose baptism lists do not answer a search, and Beitstad 16433 before 1802 (scan only; already read for Oct 1799-Oct 1800 and 1786-89 marriages). Skogn fits: Karen later lived at Ekne in Skogn. Also unread: the 1800 local census of Frosta and Åsen (source 69454; names and ages only). ¶7

Scans read, negative: Skogn ministerialbok 1747-1803 (source 16391) has marriages for the whole parish (Alstadhaug, Levanger, Ekne) in a section that starts on p. 340. Canvases 166-170 (pp. 343-352) cover the 1786 betrothals through early 1789. There is no groom named Helge. Canvases 164-165 are an older block of marriages for 1747-51. The baptisms in 16391 end about 1785, and the canvases from about 200 on are confirmation lists. From 1783 the baptisms are in 16392 ('Alstadhaug', in fact the main Skogn book), which names both parents ('Forældre'). It has about 6-7 canvases a year: canvas 40 = Apr-May 1791 and canvas 79 = Sep-Oct 1797. That book is still unread for Martha (c.1789), Ole (c.1795), Anne (c.1798) and Mogns (c.1800); with no Skogn marriage, Skogn is now a weaker lead. 1762 extra tax (source 48401): Augdal, where the family lived in 1801, was in Åsen tinglag (view 185, row 44), so Helge was not there in 1762. ¶9

Scans read, negative: Skogn ministerialbok 16392 (the main Skogn book from 1783, both parents named) canvases 24-28, which cover Apr 1788 to Feb 1789 (the 1788 year-end summary is on canvas 28: 49 boys and 39 girls born in Skogn prestegjeld), have no child of a father Helge. So Martha Helgesdatter (12 in Feb 1801) was not baptised in Skogn between Apr 1788 and Feb 1789. Canvas map: c24 = Apr-Jun 1788, c25 = Jul-Aug 1788, c26 = Aug-Oct 1788, c27 = Oct-Dec 1788, c28 = Dec 1788-Feb 1789 (about 5 canvases a year). Not yet read: c22-23 (Jan-Apr 1788) and the later windows for Ole (Feb 1794-Feb 1795, about c55-60), Anne (Feb 1797-Feb 1798, about c70-76) and Mogns (Feb 1799-Feb 1800, about c80-86). With no Skogn marriage either, Skogn is a weak lead. Indexed searches found no baptism of a Helge with father Peder in 1744-57 in Frosta, Åsen, Skogn, Leksvik, Stjørdal, Verdal, Beitstad, Inderøy or Ytterøy, and no Helge-Martha marriage in 1785-91 in any indexed register. ¶10
