About the family

His wife's 1743 probate (6a fol. 317a, No. 63) names the widower clearly as 'Peder Hansen Swæren', not Johannessen, with their five children Johannes, Christen, Giertrud, Brite and Kari. This conflicts with Laberg and kgroenha.net (Per Johannessen). The eldest son's name Johannes favours Johannessen (naming after the paternal grandfather), so the clerk may have slipped, but the patronymic is now unproved. Name left unchanged; stays Probable. ¶4a

Father added, Probable: Johannes Hansen of Vangsnes (see his page), b. about 1610, 56 in the 1666 census with sons Anders 16, Ola 9, Christen 6. The probate after Christen Johansen, Vangsnes, 4 Jul 1718 (2b p. 539a, 24156 v223-224), read in full: the heirs are his widow Brithe and 'Hermund og Peder Johans Sønner' (full brothers) plus the half-siblings Hans, Anders, Brithe and Gyri; 'Broder Peder Johansen' and 'Broder Hermund Johansen' each receive 18 merker smør in Vangsnes. No farm is given for Peder in the probate. ¶10

Life story

Kgroenha.net no. 932: born 1672, married 1692 Brita Arnfinnsdatter, died 1759. Unsourced. The naming fits: Christen's son was Peder Christensen. ¶1

Laberg 1985: Per Johannessen married Brita, daughter of Arnfinn of Sværen, in 1692 and got the holding after Arnfinn died in 1714 (p. 139). He was born [about] 1669 and died 1759; his wife died 1742. Their children named in the reconstructed text are Johannes (born 1698, married 1732 Kari, died 1771; he gave the farm up to his brother after his wife died in 1750), Kristen (took the holding after his brother; born 1718, married 1752 Randi of Tjugum), Gjertrud (married on Leikanger), Brita, and Kari ([married] Ola... of Bale) (p. 140). The book's birth year 1669 differs from kgroenha.net's 'about 1672'; the birth date here is left as it was. ¶2

Bygdebok (Laberg, Balestrand 1 canvases 161-162, Sværen; rebuilt from word boxes, dates ABT): 'Per Johannessen Vangsnes' married in 1692 Brita, the daughter of Arnfinn of Sværen, and took over after Arnfinn, who died 1714; Per was born about 1669 and died 1759, his wife died 1742; 5 children: Johannes (took the farm; b. 1698, m. 1732 Kari Johannesdtr.), Kristen (b. 1718, m. 1752 Randi Andersdtr. Tjugum), Gjertrud (m. Aslak [...] at Leikanger), Brita, and Kari (m. Ola Arnfinnson Bale, i.e. Ole (Ola) Anfindsen's third wife Kari Pedersdatter). New lead for his parents: he came from VANGSNES (Vik prestegjeld), so his father was a Johannes at Vangsnes. Not found in the Vik H. 5 Vangsnes volume by a quick phrase search (its 'Per Johannesson Vangsnes' is a man of 1866). Grade unchanged. ¶8a

New primary records for him: (a) his marriage, Leikanger 11564 canvas 10 left, 25 Jun 1692 at Tjugum: 'Peder Johans[en] Vangnes' and '[bride] Andfinds d. Sværen' (nupt.); the year is from the heading 'Ao 1692 d. 1 Jan.' on canvas 8 right. This proves the bygdebok's 'Per Johannessen Vangsnes' and the 1692 date. (b) Census 1701, Sueren: 'Peder Johansen', b. 1669, bonde (pc627659); earlier notes that saw no Peder at Sværen in 1701 had searched another spelling. (c) His second son Christen was born in 1718 (bygdebok), the year his brother Christen Johansen of Vangsnes died, which fits naming after the dead uncle; the eldest son Johannes carries the paternal grandfather's name. ¶11

His probate (about 1759-60) is not on Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll 7b (source 24164) views 120, 125, 128 or 129: view 120 holds 1759 Nos 49-51 (15 Oct-30 Nov 1759), views 124?-128 a large Lem family estate, and views 128-129 the 1760 entries Nos 2-3 (23-25 Jan 1760). Still unread: views 121-124 (rest of 1759) and 1760 entries after view 130. The patronymic conflict (Peder Hansen in his wife's 1743 probate vs Johannessen) stays open. ¶5

1772 sibling probate witness detail: the 1785 probate of his son Christen was witnessed by Ole Olsen Wangsnæs and Ingebrigt Knudsen Wangsnes, and his wife's 1743 probate by 'Ole P. Wangsnes'. Repeated Vangsnes men at the family's probates fit a Vangsnes origin but name no kinship. ¶15

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the grade · 1 record that disagrees · 1 correction · 1 alternative set aside · 2 searches with no result · 6 open questions

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Confidence raised from Speculative to Probable on Laberg 1985, which states that Kristen was his son and Brita his wife. The book gives the patronymic Johannessen but the reconstructed text does not name his father. ¶3a

Records that disagree 1

Patronymic conflict, partly resolved: the 1743 probate of his wife (6a fol. 317a) writes 'Enkemanden Peder Hansen Sveren' (re-read; 'Hansen' is clear). Against it: the 1692 marriage and the 1701 census both say Peder JohanSen, at Vangsnes and at Sværen; Sværen had only two holders in 1701 (Siver Marchusen and Peder Johansen) and no Peder Hansen appears in any Sværen record; the children are named Johannes and Christen. The 1743 'Hansen' is best read as a clerk's slip (perhaps from his father's patronymic, Johannes HANSEN). Grade unchanged at Probable on his link to Christen Persen Sværen: the 1743 probate names 'Peder [Hansen] Sveren' as father of Christen, but the patronymic mismatch is not fully closed by a relationship record. ¶12a

Corrections 1

Correction: the 1692 marriage entry names the bride 'Sigri Andfinds d. Sværen', not Brita (high-resolution crop m4.jpg; the first name reads Sigri, with the same letter forms as 'Sigri' on canvas 8). His wife in 1743, the mother of his five children, was Brite Anfindsdatter. So either the vicar wrote the wrong sister's name, or Peder married first Sigri Anfindsdatter (d. before c.1697) and then her sister Brita. The tree's '1692 marriage to Brita' therefore rests on the bygdebok, not on the register. This does not touch his link to Christen (the 1743 probate names Brite as Christen's mother). ¶13

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Not in Ytre Sogn 7b views 121-123 either: Dec 1759 holds No. 52 (Pernille [Jørgensdatter?]), No. 53 (13 Dec, widow Agathe Hansdatter) and a short 20 Dec 1759 entry, none at Sværen; 1760 begins on fol. 458a (view 123 right) with the Lem estate. Remaining: 1760 entries after No. 5 (view 130 onward) and 1759 before No. 49 (before view 120). ¶6

Searches that found nothing 2

Search: the Ytre Sogn probate register (domicile Sv*, 1700-1800, filtered to Ytre Sogn) has no probate for a Peder/Per at Sværen in the 1750s-60s, and no Peder Hansen or Peder Johannesen 1755-1766 in the district apart from Brensdal and Mølmesdal. His probate was evidently never registered, so the Hansen/Johannessen patronymic conflict cannot be settled from probate. Sværen rows that exist: Brite Anfindsdtr (1740-43), Sjur Markusen (1743-50), Kari Johansdtr (1750-56, probably Johannes Pedersen's wife), Marie Andersdtr (1763-66), Anders Sjursen (1766-72), Johannes Pedersen (1772-76), Christen Pedersen (1783-86), Brite Aamundsdtr (1782-91), Peder Christensen (1800-05). ¶7

Negative tests, grade unchanged (Probable): (1) the probates of his sons Johannes Pedersen, Sværen, 30 Apr 1772 (9a p. 5-6, No. 18) and Christen Pedersen, Sværen, 3 Oct 1785 (10a fol. 202b-204a, No. 140) list no land in Vangsnes; Johannes's estate was 10 rd, wholly consumed, and Sværen itself was Vik glebe land ('Wiigs Præstebool'), so the 18 merker smør in Vangsnes that 'Broder Peder Johansen' inherited in 1718 cannot be traced to the Sværen family this way. Neither probate names the father. (2) No baptism of Christen in Leikanger 11564 Apr 1718-Oct 1719 (the register has a gap Oct 1718-Mar 1719), so the baptism cannot test the Hansen/Johansen patronymic. (3) Baptisms in 11564 in the 1690s name only the child and the father (with farm) and that decade has no churchings, so they cannot show whether the mother was Sigri or Brita. The Johannes Hansen identification and the 1743 'Peder Hansen' conflict stay open. Test record now: the Ytre Sogn tingbok (court book) 1718-20 for a sale or redemption of the 1718 Vangsnes share by 'Peder Johansen Sværen', or the 1711 or 1723 matrikkel / landkommisjon owner lists for Vangsnes. ¶14

Open questions and the next record to read 6

What would confirm it: his 1759 probate or Brita's 1742 probate (Ytre Sogn) naming the son Christen. ¶3b

What would settle it: his own 1759 probate (Ytre Sogn 7b, not yet located) or the 1692 Leikanger marriage. ¶4b

Test record: the 1692 Leikanger marriage (11564, unindexed) giving his origin, and the 1664/1666 census for Vangsnes (a Johannes with a son Peder about 1669 would appear only in 1666 if born by then). ¶8b

Lead, no grade change: the probate after Christen Johansen at Vangsnes, 4 Jul 1718 (Ytre Sogn 2b p. 539a, 24156 view 223), names as his heirs the full brothers 'Hermund og Peder Johans Sønner' and the half-siblings Hans, Andres, Brithe and Gyri Johansen/-datter. So a Johannes (Johan) at Vangsnes married twice and had a son Peder by the first marriage, alive in 1718. No farm is given for this Peder, so his identity with Peder Johannessen of Sværen (b. ~1669) is not shown. Found by the probate sweep by farm (domicile Vangsnes*, 1698-1745: 25 rows; no Johannes/Johan as decedent). ¶9a

Test record: the share-list payments or a later court entry naming 'Peder Johansen Sværen' as Christen's brother; the probate of Hermund Johansen (Vangsnes area); the 1701 census at Vangsnes for Christen and Hermund Johansen and their father; the Vangsnes bygdebok harvest for Christen Johansen d. ~1718. ¶9b

Test record: Christen's baptism in 1718 (Leikanger 11564: 1717 starts c125 L, 1718 starts c131 R; c132 (Jan-Mar 1718) was with no Christen at Sværen, so read c133-c136), which should give the father as 'Peder Johansen Sværen' or 'Peder Sværen'. ¶12b
