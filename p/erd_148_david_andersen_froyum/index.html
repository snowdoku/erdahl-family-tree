About the family

Parents: his mother Lisbet Pedersdatter's skifte, Frøyum 19 Jun 1741 (24752 fol. 1070, view 333), names him as 'David Andersen, 19', first of the 7 children of Lisbet and the widower Anders Davidsen. See the notes on Anders Davidsen for the continuity (betrothal 1743, burial 1744 aged 22 1/2, the 1745 Frøyum deed naming his widow's inheritance from him on his mother's skifte). The parents' grade is on their own records. ¶7

Life story

Burial: Stjørdal 16370 canvas 54 right (p. 53), 'D. 21 May Hegre Pastor. Parent[atio]: David Andersen Frøyam 22 1/2 aar', so he was born about 1721-22. The Nov 1743 marriage is not in the chronological list (canvases 47-48, Dom. 19 p. Trin. to Christmas 1743, contain no David Andersen copulation), so it should be in the 16370 trolovede list (1743 is about canvases 258-261). One godparent and appraiser lead: an 'Anders Davidsen Frøyum' appraised the Hammer estate in Mar 1738 (24752 fol. 771b), possibly his father or another kinsman. Not entered. ¶4

Bygdebok: Hegra gårds- og slektshistorie B. 2, Frøyum (canvas 418): 'David Andersen Frøyum, [f.] 1722, gravlagt 21/5 1744, 22 [år]', married 22 Nov 1743 Gjertrud Gunnarsdtr. Hembre; daughter Lisbet b. 1744, m. John Kleven. The left column lists an 'Anders Davidsen Frøyum, [f.] 1684' on the farm, probably his father (not added: the rebuilt text does not state the relationship). His grade is unchanged. Test: Stjørdal 16370 (1740-56) for the Nov 1743 marriage and May 1744 burial. ¶2

His betrothal is found, Stjørdal 16370 trolovede list canvas 262 (p. 261), 2 Oct 1743 at Værnes: 'ungkarl David Anderson Frøyum med pigen Geertrud Gundersd. Hemre', married at Værnes 28 Nov 1743 (the chronological list on c47-48 had omitted it). It names neither his father nor his birthplace. Test record for his parents: the Frøyum household before 1743 (Anders on Frøyum; an 'Anders Davidsen Frøyum' was an appraiser at the 1738 Hammer probate, 24752 fol. 771b, a strong candidate father by name pattern only) and a skifte after Anders Davidsen Frøyum. ¶5

Probate (skifte) registered 17 Nov 1744 on the farm Frøyum in Stjørdal (Værnes parish), between his widow Gjertrud Gundersdatter and their only child, a daughter read as 'Lisbet Davids datt.', 10 weeks old (Stjør- og Verdal skifteprotokoll 3, s. 102b; the register index names the deceased David Andersen, Frøian, 1744). The scan is heavily contrasted; 'Davids datt.' and '10 Uger gl.' are clear, the given name Lisbet is a probable reading. Read. ¶1

His posthumous daughter's baptism, 16370 canvas 58, 20 Sep 1744: 'Sal. David Frøyums B. Lisbet' - 'Sal.' (the late) shows he died before the birth, consistent with the bygdebok burial of 21 May 1744. His marriage (22 Nov 1743) and burial in 16370 c47-c54 were not read this pass. ¶3

Alternatives considered and set aside 2

Near-miss attempt, not raised; graded on his link to Lisbet Davidsdatter: see the note on Gjertrud Gundersdatter. His widow's 1745 remarriage to Gunder (Gunnar) Jonsen Frøyum comes from the bygdebok only. It falls in a gap in the 16370 trolovede list (Nov 1744-Sep 1745), and no primary record read names Gunder's wife. Grade unchanged. ¶6

Upgrade (near-miss): graded on his link to Lisbet Davidsdatter, Probable to Confirmed. Linked records: (1) his probate, Frøyum 17 Nov 1744 (24753 fol. 102b): widow Gjertrud Gundersdatter, only child 'Lisbet Davids datt.', 10 weeks; (2) her baptism 20 Sep 1744, 'Sal. David Frøyums B. Lisbet' (16370 c58); (3) NEW, the Frøyum deed of 23 Jun 1745 (pantebok A-2 fol. 460b, DA 19751 view 90): Gunder Jonsen's wife Gjertrud is the widow of 'hendes afgangne Mand David Andersen', so the mother and the infant stayed on Frøyum in Gunder Jonsen's household (the missing remarriage record that named as the test); (4) Gunder Jonsen on Frøyum in between: 'odelsbonden Gunder Jonsen Frøyum' 31 Jan 1748 (16370 p. 275), deed about 1762-63 (22318 image 83); (5) the 1762 ekstraskatt, Frøyum No. 120 (48401 v216): 'Gunder og H[ustru]' with children over 12 'Lisbeth, Giertrud, Ingborg', i.e. Lisbet, 18, in her mother's household 18 years after 1744, with the parent shown on the farm inside the gap (audit 7 rule 1); (6) 'Pigen Lisbet Davidsdatter Frøyum', married 29 May 1765 (16371 p. 345), 3 years later, patronymic plus farm. No rival is named: David Andersen was the only David on Frøyum, the 1762 Lisbeth cannot be Gunder's own daughter by the 1765 patronymic, and David's sister Lisbet Andersdatter (6 in 1741) carries a different patronymic. Her age at burial (81 in 1825) and her children David and Gjertrud fit. ¶8
