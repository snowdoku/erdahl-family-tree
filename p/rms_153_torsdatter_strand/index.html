About the family

Wife of Helge Skafhelden of Søndre Strand and mother of his four children; forename not recorded. She was dead before her husband's probate of 18-21 Feb 1707 (card 13725; DA 25284 s. 331b, view 336), which was attended on the children's side by 'Bestefaderen Tor Rølang, saa vel som Børnenes afgangne Moders Søstermand Niels Frøen' (the children's late mother's sister's husband), Haagen Siurbrygge and Erich Knudsen Rauden. The estate divided among the children was their 'Mødrene arf' (maternal inheritance; s. 336b, view 341). At the appointment of guardians (s. 338a, view 343, read): 'først for den Eldste Søn Thor Helgesen dend dannemand Morfaderen Tor Rølang'; for Jul Helgesen Ole V[i?]nstad; for Ole Helgesen Niels Jensen Frøen and Haagen Siurbrygge; for the daughter Jøran Helgesdatter Erich Rauden and Knud An[...]. Her patronymic Torsdatter follows from 'Morfaderen Tor Rølang' (her father). ¶1

Life story

No Frøen/Frøien card run exists in the Valdres card index DA 39948: the cards run Fristad (3397-3399), Frydenlund (3401-3405), Fuglei/Fugleim (3407-3431). So the probate of Niels (Jensen) Frøen's wife, her sister, is not carded in Valdres. ¶4a

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade:, new person entered as Confirmed (forename unknown), graded on her link to her son Tor Helgesen (see his page). The 1707 probate names her as 'Børnenes afgangne Moder' of the four heirs, 'den Eldste Søn Thor Helgesen 15 aar' first, and the estate is their 'Mødrene arf'; one mother for all four children. Continuity for Tor is as under Tor Helgesen (1722 probate on the same farm names his brothers Jul and Ole). ¶2
