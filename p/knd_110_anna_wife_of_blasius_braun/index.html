About the family

Mother of Anna Braun (see her page), wife of M. Blasius Braun (see his page). Graded on her link to Anna: Probable (compiled source). Pfarrerbuch Württemberg Nr. 834: Blasius Braun married at Tübingen on 16 Apr 1605 'Anna GRAMMER, geb....', previously married to Johann Konrad Grammer (Cramer), Dr. med. et physic. at Landau. So Grammer is her first husband's surname; her maiden name is not given. Anna Braun was baptised at Weinsberg on 19 Feb 1606, ten months after this marriage, and no other wife of Blasius is listed, so she is the mother. ¶1a

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
