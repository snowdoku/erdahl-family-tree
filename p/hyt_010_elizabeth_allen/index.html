About the family

Children: as 'Elizabeth his Wife' she is named as mother of the eight children of Job Prince born in Boston from 1749 to 1767 (Record Commissioners vol. 24, pp. 271-315), the first born about seven and a half months after the 1748 marriage; that she is the Elizabeth Allen of that marriage is inferred from these dates, as the birth entries give only her first name. ¶2

Parents entered (Probable; her own grade is unchanged, being graded on her link to her children and to Job Prince): Jeremiah Allen (b. 1699, son of John Allen, goldsmith, and grandson of Rev. James Allen) and Rebecca Kilby (b. 1701), married at the Brattle Church 18 Oct. 1722. Evidence: (1) the Boston birth of 'Elisabeth daughter of Jer. and Rebekah Allen', 22 April 1725, is the only Boston Elizabeth Allen of 1724-26 and fits her age at death exactly; (2) the Brattle Church baptismal list for 1724-25 shows 'Eliz. Allen' brought by 'Rebekah Allen' (Manifesto Church records, pp. 146-147); (3) Job Prince's house and estate lay on the twenty-acre West Boston farm settled in 1706-10 on John Allen, Jeremiah's father (Gleaner XI; Hezekiah Prince's diary); (4) in 1781 Job Prince, Hezekiah Blanchard and 'the Heirs of James Allen' (James b. 1697 was Jeremiah's elder brother) were joint proprietors of a twenty-acre tract at West Boston (Boston Town Records vol. 26, p. 263), consistent with a division of John Allen's farm among his heirs, Job holding in right of his wife; (5) her first son was named Hezekiah Blanchard Prince. No record yet states that she was Jeremiah's daughter. ¶14

Parentage settled: the 1814 deed of David Higgins and John and Sarah Tucker to Samuel Dunn (Suffolk Deeds register, DGS 007442718 image 570, p. 248) calls the South Allen Street pasture part of 'the Real estate of which Jeremiah Allen dec[ease]d seized', set off 'among the heirs of said Jeremiah Allen... to Elizabeth Prince whose heirs we are in part', the other heirs at law being James, Samuel and Thomas I. Prince and [Thomas] Williams; persons 'claiming under Elizabeth Creed late of Prince Edwards Islands, deceased' are among those whose claims the grantors warrant against, and a sixth part is 'in the heirs of the late Cap[tai]n Job Prince'. This makes her the daughter of Jeremiah and Rebecca (Kilby) Allen (born 22 Apr. 1725; see their notes). Her own grade (on her link to her daughter Elizabeth (Prince) Higgins Creed) stays Probable: the deed treats Elizabeth Creed's heirs (David Higgins and Sarah Tucker, evidently Elizabeth Creed's Higgins children) as heirs of Elizabeth Prince, which strongly implies that Elizabeth Creed was her daughter, but it gives no relationship word. Elizabeth Creed's administration (Suffolk docket 23931) was found by full text (record book 'Probate Records 1811-1812', ark 3:1:3Q9M-C9Y5-R96L-3, transcript only): inventory by David Higgins, administrator of 'Elizabeth Creed formerly of Boston... but late of Prince Edwards Island [transcribed 'singlewoman'] deceased intestate', 29 June 1812: 'Received of James Prince in Cash $1048.88', the whole personal estate. It names no relationship. ¶19a

Life story

Allen probate (S0107): the father of the leading birth candidate (Elisabeth, daughter of Jeremiah and Rebekah Allen, born 22 April 1725) appears to be Jeremiah Allen of Boston, merchant. He was the son and executor of Jeremiah Allen Esq. of Boston, whose will of 1736 (proved 1741/2) names his sons James and Jeremiah and leaves bequests to his 'daughters in law' Martha and Elizabeth Allen (Suffolk Probate vol. 35 pp. 603-604). The younger Jeremiah died intestate by early 1755: administration was granted (docket 11022; vol. 50 p. 96, inventory vol. 52 p. 66), and guardians were appointed that year for his under-age children Jeremiah, Martha and William (dockets 10999-11001; vol. 50 p. 83, which calls him 'Jeremiah Allen late of Boston … Merchant'). No division or settlement naming his adult children is docketed, so no probate record yet names Elizabeth Prince as his daughter, and she remains unlinked. Ruled out: John Allen senior of Medfield (will of 1750 names no children; vol. 44 p. 498). ¶8a

Marriage: Elizabeth Allen married Job Prince in Boston on 26 December 1748; the Rev. Jonathan Mayhew officiated, and the intention gives her name as 'Eliza.' (Boston Marriages, Record Commissioners vol. 28, p. 259). ¶1

Blanchard link (unresolved): her first son was named Hezekiah Blanchard Prince (1749). A Hezekiah Blanchard married an Elizabeth Allen in Boston on 28 November 1723 (Rev. Benjamin Wadsworth; Boston Marriages, vol. 28, p. 111). That bride cannot be Job's wife, who bore children until 1767, and the 1748 entry gives no 'Mrs.' or widow marker. The 1723 couple has no children in the Boston birth register and no Hezekiah Blanchard or Blancher appears in the Suffolk probate index. A family tie to Elizabeth (Allen) Blanchard, as niece or sister, is a possibility only; no record read links them. ¶6

Death notice (index): 'Elizabeth (Mrs.), consort of Capt. Job Prince, sen., d. in Boston, yesterday, in 61st yr', in the Massachusetts Centinel of 7 December 1785 (AAS obituary index, vol. 4 p. 3678; page image read; notice not yet read). So she died about 6 December 1785, before her husband, and was born about 1725. A widow 'Elizabeth, of the late Job Prince', who died in 1818 aged 65, is the son Job's widow, not her. ¶7

Deeds read: she joined her husband's deed to Nathaniel Patten of 12 March 1784 only to release dower ('I and Elizabeth my wife in Testimony that she releases all her right of dower'; acknowledged 13 Mar 1784 by 'Capt. Job Prince and Elizabeth his wife'; Suffolk Deeds 142:66-67, DGS 7442680 images 334-336), so she was living in March 1784; the deed (a former rope walk and distillery with Charles River flats) recites no title. In March 1793 'one fifth part of one third part' of an Allen marsh on Cambridge Street, next to Capt. Job Prince's land, was reserved as 'claimed by Capt. Job Prince' (Deeds 175:141); see Jeremiah Allen. Her parents stay Probable. ¶18

1815 release (supports the 1814 deed, no grade change): on 18 March 1815 the heirs of Job Prince Jr. (his widow Elizabeth Prince, James S. Colburn and Sarah D. his wife, B. C. Prince, and the minor children of Stephen D. Turner and Mary J. his wife, represented by 'Elizabeth Prince aforenamed their Grandmother') released to Samuel Dunn, for $500, their right 'as heirs at law of Job Prince late of Boston aforesaid Master Mariner, Deceased' in the West Boston land 'which has been upwards of twenty years in the occupation of said Dunn as a Cow Pasture by a parole lease from the heirs at Law of said Job Prince + Elizabeth his wife', adding that 'Five undivided Sixth parts of the above piece of Land were conveyed to said Samuel Dunn by David Higgins + others' (the 1814 deed) (Suffolk Deeds 1815-1819, image 356, ark 3:1:3QSQ-G9Z3-7X31; transcript read). So the pasture descended to the heirs at law of Job Prince and Elizabeth his wife in sixths, one sixth to the line of their son Job Jr. and five sixths conveyed by David Higgins (son of Elizabeth Creed) and others. The words are 'heirs at law' again, not 'daughter' or 'mother'. ¶21

Diary evidence: Hezekiah Prince's diary, 18 Nov 1793 (New England Magazine n.s. 9 (Feb. 1894): 727-728), says 'Capt. Job married Elisebeth Allen, died 1790', that his mansion house stood 'between McLean Street and Allen Street, West End, his estate reaching to Charles River', and that their children included a daughter 'Elisebeth'. The Allen Street location is suggestive of an Allen family estate in the West End (a lead for her parentage: Jeremiah Allen's West End lands), not evidence. Her parents remain unknown; confidence unchanged. ¶11

Allen probate, a lead and not proof: Suffolk docket 11022 is the estate of 'Jeremiah Allen late of Boston... Merchant', who died intestate. Administration was granted on 18 April 1755 to 'Elizabeth Allen of Boston... Widow', his widow (Suffolk probate records vol. 50 p. 96, DGS 7703692 image 58; docket book nos. 10803-18054, DGS 7703035 image 23; images read). The docket has only the bond, the letter and the inventory (52:66), with no distribution, so no children are named. A widow named Elizabeth fits the Jeremiah Allen Jr. who married (1) Rebecca Kilby in 1722, the parents of the leading birth candidate Elisabeth (b. 22 Apr 1725), and (2) Elizabeth Oulton of Marblehead. Nothing in the file ties him to Elizabeth (Allen) Prince, so no parents are entered. ¶13

Allen probate read, not yet proof: her grandfather-candidate John Allen, goldsmith, left £50 each to 'my two Grand Children named John & Elizabeth Children of my said son Jeremiah' (will dated 8 Dec. 1756, Suffolk Probate 55:294), so Jeremiah Allen had a daughter Elizabeth living in 1756; she is called only 'Elizabeth', with no married name, though Job Prince's wife had been married since 1748. The divisions of John's real estate (Suffolk Probate 64:465-469, 1765; 67:342-344, 1768-69) give Jeremiah's third to his 'Children or legal Representatives' without naming them, and Jeremiah's own estate (docket 12319, 1760) recorded no division. They do show that John Allen's daughter was Elizabeth Blanchard, wife of Mr Hezekiah Blanchard of Boston: so Hezekiah Blanchard was the husband of the aunt of Jeremiah's daughter Elizabeth, consistent with the naming of Hezekiah Blanchard Prince (1749). Her parents stay Probable. The deciding record is still a Suffolk deed in which Job Prince and Elizabeth his wife convey Allen land, or a release naming Jeremiah's heirs. ¶17

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Upgrade: graded on her link to her daughter Elizabeth Prince. The 1814 deed (Suffolk Deeds, DGS 007442718 image 570) calls David Higgins and John and Sarah Tucker heirs 'in part' of 'Elizabeth Prince' (this woman, wife of Capt. Job Prince, d. Dec. 1785) and covers persons 'claiming under Elizabeth Creed late of Prince Edwards Islands, deceased'. The records read now show that Elizabeth Creed was born Prince ('Elizabeth Creed otherwise Prince', 1806 power of attorney, Suffolk deeds 1809 image 529), was a co-owner with Job's sons James and Thomas I. Prince in 1809, and that her son David Higgins was an heir at law of old Capt. Job Prince (Samuel D. Parker's deposition, sworn 7 Nov 1823, Suffolk deeds 1819-1825 image 262). Elizabeth (Allen) Prince died before her husband, so her heirs were their children and the issue of dead children; Elizabeth Creed, who married in 1773, can only be a daughter. Her birth to 'Job Prince and Elizabeth his Wife' on 28 May 1753 (Boston Births) completes the link. The earlier note keeping her Probable 'because the deed gives no relationship word' is superseded. ¶20

Audit 11 (2 Nov 2026): Confirmed kept, graded on her link to her daughter Elizabeth Prince, on the same chain as Job Prince (see his audit 11 note). The 1814 deed makes David Higgins an heir in part of 'Elizabeth Prince' (this woman), and she died before her husband, so her heirs were her issue. Clean-up: the note's parenthesis '(David Higgins and Sarah Tucker, evidently Elizabeth Creed's Higgins children)' is wrong and superseded. Mrs Sarah Tucker, wife of John Tucker Esq., was Job and Elizabeth Prince's own daughter Sarah (1823 deposition: 'James Prince, Samuel Prince, Mrs Tucker the wife of John Tucker Esquire, David Higgins and others heirs at Law'; the 1793 diary lists a daughter Sarah). Only David Higgins is Elizabeth Creed's son. ¶22

The case for this identification 1

Identity (Probable): she is placed as the mother of Elizabeth (Prince) Higgins Creed on the same grounds as Job Prince; see his identity note. The same probate files would confirm it. A will of an Elizabeth Prince proved in Suffolk in 1818 (docket 25536) may be hers as a widow; it has not been read. ¶4

Corrections 1

Correction (to an earlier note's assumption): the Jeremiah Allen, merchant, who died intestate by April 1755 leaving widow Elizabeth (Suffolk docket 11022) is probably not her father. Her mother Rebecca (Kilby) Allen was living in 1738, and the Waldo Genealogy (1902) places the 1729 marriage to Elizabeth Oulton, and the minor children of 1755, with the other Jeremiah Allen, son of Jeremiah Allen Esq. ¶15

Alternatives considered and set aside 3

Birth: not established. The one Boston birth noticed so far, Elizabeth daughter of John and Mary Allen (c.1731, OCR only), has not been checked or linked. ¶3

Birth candidates (unresolved): two Boston-born Elizabeth Allens fit the dates, read from the page images: Elisabeth, daughter of Jer[emiah] and Rebekah Allen, born 22 April 1725 (23 at her marriage); and Elizabeth, daughter of John and Mary Allen, born 5 April 1732 (16 at her marriage; the earlier 'c.1731' was an OCR estimate). An Eliza., daughter of Jeremiah and Mary Allen (c.1715), is too old to have borne a child in 1767. Neither candidate is preferred: Boston also recorded marriages of at least four other Elizabeth Allens in the 1740s and 1750s, and none of Job's children is named Jeremiah, Rebekah, John or Mary. ¶5

Birth candidates narrowed (reasoning, no new parent entered): her death notice gives her age as 'in 61st yr' on about 6 December 1785, so she was born between about 7 December 1724 and 6 December 1725. Of the two Boston candidates, Elizabeth daughter of John and Mary Allen (5 April 1732) is therefore ruled out, and Elisabeth daughter of Jer[emiah] and Rebekah Allen (22 April 1725) fits exactly. A scan of the Boston birth register OCR for 1724-1726 (Record Commissioners vol. 24) found no other Elizabeth Allen; the only other Allen births there are Mary and Martha, daughters of John and Mary Allen. Jeremiah Allen married Rebecca Kilby in Boston in 1722 (Boston Marriages vol. 28, OCR). The parents are still not entered: Allen is a common name, she may have been born outside Boston, and no record ties Job Prince's wife to Jeremiah Allen's family (none of her children is named Jeremiah or Rebecca). Speculative only. ¶10a

Open questions and the next record to read 6

Next: the administrator named in vol. 50 p. 96, the guardians' accounts (vol. 57 p. 351; vol. 65 p. 31), and Suffolk deeds in which Job and Elizabeth Prince release rights as Allen heirs. ¶8b

Leads for the birth candidate Elisabeth, daughter of Jeremiah and Rebekah Allen, b. 22 Apr 1725 (OCR only, page images not read): Boston Marriages 1700-1751 has 'Jeramia Allen & Rebecca Kilby' (intention 'Jeramiah', 'Rebecka') in the 1722 section, and later 'Jeramiah Allen Junr. of Boston & Elizabeth Oulton of Marblehead'. If Rebecca (Kilby) died and Jeremiah Jr remarried an Elizabeth, that would explain the 'daughters in law Martha and Elizabeth Allen' of Jeremiah Allen Esq.'s will of 1736. Not linked: nothing yet ties Job Prince's wife to this family. ¶9

Next: the Suffolk guardianship and administration files of Jeremiah Allen, merchant, 1755 (dockets 10999-11022), and Suffolk deeds c.1755-70 for Job and Elizabeth Prince as Allen heirs. ¶10b

Lead, not evidence of parentage: in the 1780 Boston assessors' taking books, 'Capt Job Prince' (2 polls, £250) is listed between 'Hezekiah Blanchard' and 'John Allen', with 'Job Prince Junr' nearby, so a Hezekiah Blanchard and a John Allen were the Princes' immediate neighbours. Job's first son, born 1749, was named Hezekiah Blanchard Prince, and a Hezekiah Blanchard married an Elizabeth Allen in Boston in 1723. This makes a Blanchard-Allen family tie (aunt or sister) more likely, but no record names Elizabeth (Allen) Prince's parents. Her parents remain unentered. ¶12

Next records (to confirm): Suffolk probate docket 12211 (John Allen, will, 1760) and any division of his real estate; Suffolk docket 7782 (Christopher Kilby, 1742), executors' accounts for the share of 'daughter Rebecca Allen... and if she dies, her children'; Suffolk deeds 1760-1790 by Job Prince and Elizabeth his wife for West Boston land (grantor index Pa-Ry, DGS 7453726). ¶16

Test record: the docket 23931 file papers (petition for administration, which may name her heirs or mother) or a deed of David Higgins reciting 'my grandmother Elizabeth Prince'. ¶19b
