About the family

Children: the Borgund index lists children of Anders Olsen of Hessen baptised Ole on 1 June 1732, Henning on 29 November 1735, Joen on 3 May 1739, Sisille on 5 June 1740 and Ole on 24 January 1745. The 1739 (Joen) and 1740 (Sisille) entries were read on the page images. In 1739 the godparents were Anders Larsen, Elling Amundsen, Nils Clausen, Kirsten Pedersdatter and Ellen Olsdatter, all of Hessen. ¶2

Parents: his father is entered as Ole Jonsen of Hessen, whose son Anders was baptised at Borgund on 24 August 1704 (index). This link is Speculative, not Probable: the baptism fits name, father and farm, but it makes him about 45 at his drowning in 1750, against the burial age of 50. His own Probable confidence concerns his own identity, marriage and children, not this parent link, and is unchanged; the new father carries the Speculative label. See Ole Jonsen's notes for what would confirm it. ¶8

Life story

Bygdebok: Borgund og Giske bd. 4 (canvas 372, Hessen) has '4. Anders Olsen (1704-50)', son of Ole Jonsen (d. 1742, 73) and, by the bruk sequence, of Brite Larsdatter (widow of Anders Jonsen, d. 1699; remarried 1700); he took the bruk in 1731, married Sissel Olsdtr. that year, and drowned with his son. This supports the 1704 baptism as his and resolves the burial-age discrepancy in favour of 1704. Mother added as Brite Larsdatter (Probable); father Ole Jonsen Hessen raised to Probable. ¶10

Marriage: as the bachelor Anders Olsen of Hessen he was engaged at Borgund on 27 May 1731 to the maid Sissell Olsdatter, born at Wedde (Vedde) and living at Hessen. The sponsors were Ole Eriksen and Jens Nilsen, both of Hessen. They married on 8 July 1731. From the Digitalarkivet index; the page image was not read. ¶1

Death: he drowned just off Hessen ('Omkom paa Søen strax ved Hessen') together with his son Henning. Their burials are consecutive entries in the Borgund register, marked 'the father' (age 50) and 'the son' (14 years and 15 weeks). The burial date is not in the index. The entries follow a burial of 26 February 1750, and Henning's age, counted from his baptism on 29 November 1735, puts the drowning in about March 1750. His birth year of about 1700 is inferred from the burial age of 50. ¶3

Probate read on the scan (13 Oct 2026): his estate was registered at Hessen on 12 Sep 1750. The parties were his widow Sisille Olsdatter, with her guardian Henning Olsen (farm uncertain), and their child Cisille Andersdatter, apparently the only one ('eeniste', uncertain), whose guardians were Rasmus Gundersen of Godøya and Ole Rasmussen of Hessen. Ole Eriksen and Anders Larsen of Hessen appear next, apparently as appraisers. Henning is not among the heirs, which fits his drowning with his father. The opening of the probate does not mention how Anders died. This primary record names his wife and daughter directly, so his links to Sisille Olsdatter and to Cæcilia now rest on a record, not only on the index. ¶6

Probate continuation: fol. 137b of Sunnmøre skifteprotokoll 13A (1749-1753) carries the end of an estate, immediately before a new entry headed 'Biørneset Boe', dated 12 Sep 1750. It ends with the daughter's share: 'Og Datteren Cisille Andersdatter [...] Arvelod med Gang Klæder i alt 25 Rd 3 ß 8½ [?] udlagt' (to the daughter Cisille Andersdatter her inheritance, including clothing, about 25 rd 3 sk, was allotted), then the closing formula. This is very probably the end of his estate. No age for Cisille, no Henning, no drowning remark and no guardian's farm appear on this opening; those must be on the preceding page. ¶7

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Confidence is Probable because his link to Cæcilia rests on the identification of the 1740 baptism with her (see her notes). His own marriage, the baptisms of 1732, 1735 and 1745, and the burial rest on the Digitalarkivet index; the 1739 and 1740 baptisms were read on the page images. ¶5

Upgrade (regrade 3): Probable to Confirmed as father of Cæcilia (Sidsele) Andersdatter (see her page); graded on his link to Cæcilia Andersdatter. Naming records: (1) her baptism, Borgund, Whitsunday 5 Jun 1740: 'Samme dag læst ofver Anders Olsøn Hessens hiemme døbte Datter, N: Sisille' (read on the page image); (2) his probate at Hessen, 12 Sep 1750 (Sunnmøre 3A 17, p. 136b-137b, scan): 'efter afg. Anders Olsen... imellem hands efterladende Enke Sisille Ols Datter... og den Sal. Mands med hende avlede eeniste[?] Barn Cisille Anders datter'. Continuity, all at Hessen, through consecutive records: baptism 1740, probate 1750 (10 years), confirmation at Borgund 2 Oct 1757 'Siedsele Anders d: Hessen', 16 (7 years; the only Sidsele and the only Hessen confirmand of 1756-58), and her betrothal 1759 and marriage 1 Jan 1760 to Lars Olsen as the maid Sidsele Andersdatter of Hessen (2-3 years); in 1762 she is 'Sidsle Andersdatter', wife of Lars Olsen, at Lille Nørve (49637 view 37). The only other Anders with children at Hessen before 1761 married in 1756 (too late), so there is no named rival. Audit 2 kept this Probable because Cæcilia is joined to the 1750 heir 'only by name, farm and age'; under the later audit-3 detail (gaps of about 15 years or less on the same farm with no named rival are continuity) the chain 1740-1750-1757-1760 qualifies. ¶9a

The case for this identification 1

Identity: this man is distinct from the younger Anders Olsen of Hessen, a bachelor who married in 1756 and fathered children there in 1757 and 1760. He is also distinct from the Anders Olsen, cottager (husmand), who married at Hessen in 1703. ¶4

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Flag: the confirmation age (16 in Oct 1757) is a year short of the baptism (17 y 4 m), noted in her record as ordinary rounding; 'eeniste' is an uncertain reading and does not affect the link. ¶9b
