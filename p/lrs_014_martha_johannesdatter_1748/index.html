Life story

1801 census: Martha Johannesdatter, 53, wife of Johannes Jenssen at Tynning (Tynjum), Brekke, married for the first time. Birth about 1748 is inferred from her age. ¶1

Burial (read from the scan): Gulen ministerialbok 1809-1825 (11449), female burials 1815, p. 94 (canvas 80, left page), no. 2, interment 15 Jan 1815: 'Kone Marthe Johannesd. Tynning, gammel 64 [1/12?] Aar' (wife Marthe Johannesdatter of Tynning, aged 64 and a fraction, so born about 1750). Called 'Kone' (married woman), which fits her husband Johannes Jensen being alive (he died at Tynning in 1822). Her 1801 age of 53 gives about 1748, a two-year difference usual for these registers. Name, farm and marital status match; no other Marthe Johannesdatter of Tynning is in the 1815 list. ¶4

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Probable mother of Kari Johannesdatter (see her husband's note). A second Martha Johannesdatter, 52, wife of Ole Lassessen, lived on the same farm in 1801; she is a different woman. ¶2

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed (upgrade sweep D), as parent of Kari Johannesdatter. Three records name or fix the link. (1) Her baptism on 15 Dec 1779, 'Johannes Jenssen Tynnings D[atter] N[omine] Kari' (Gulen ministerialbok 1776-1787, fol. 35b), names Johannes Jensen of Tynning as her father; this register does not name mothers. (2) The 1801 census of Brekke (index, relationship word checked) lists Kari Johannesdatter, 23, unmarried, as 'Felleds dr' (daughter of both spouses) of Johannes Jenssen, 56, and Martha Johannesdatter, 53, at Tynning, household 01. (3) She married from Tynjum as a maid on 1 Jul 1804 (Mons Johannessøn of Asseim and Kari Johannesdatter, Gulen marriages 1804, index); the other Kari Johannesdatter of 1801 (34, at Asheim) did not live at Tynjum. For Martha the naming record is the 1801 census word 'Felleds dr', which states that Kari was a child of both spouses; Johannes was married for the first time. ¶3
