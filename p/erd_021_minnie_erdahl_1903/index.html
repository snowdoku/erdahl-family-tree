Life story

Birth: born 14 July 1903 in Ellsborough Township, Murray County, and registered as Minnie Bertina Erdahl, female, the second child of B. L. Erdahl, farmer, 25, and Annie Quam, 21. ¶1

Death: the family list recorded under her brother William (from Find a Grave) gives her life as 1903-1924, so she died at about 21, probably in Montana. No death record has been read yet. ¶3

1905 MN census: 'Minnie B Erdall', 1, in her parents' household in Ellsborough Township (the figure could also be read as 9, but 1 fits her birth). ¶2

1920 U.S. census (index only): 'Minnie B Erdahl', daughter, born about 1904 in Minnesota, with her mother indexed as 'Oliana', was in School District 18, Phillips County, Montana. This shows that she was alive and living with the family in Montana in early 1920, four years before her death about 1924. The head of the household is not in the index, and the image has not been read. ¶4
