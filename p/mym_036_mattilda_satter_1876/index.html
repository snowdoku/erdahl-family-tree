Life story

1880 census: 'Mattilda' (so spelt), daughter, white, female, 4, born Dakota Territory, in the household of Gunder Satter and his wife 'Anna' (Guri Anna) at Spink Township, Union County, Dakota Territory, 15-16 June 1880 (ED 5, sheet 407A, line 10). Birth about 1875-76 is inferred from the age. Confirmed as their daughter on this primary record, read on the image. ¶1

Later life unknown: she is in no later family record found so far. Her sister Anna Olene's 1937 obituary names only surviving brothers Lewis, Fred and Gust, and her sister Lizzie's 1934 obituary names only Anna as surviving sister; neither mentions a Sophia or a Mattilda. She may have died young, before 1885. ¶3a
