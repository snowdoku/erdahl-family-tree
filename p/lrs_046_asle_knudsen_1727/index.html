About the family

Children: by 1757 he lived at Medgaardehuus, a cottar place (*husmannsplass*) under Medgaarden. Three sons were baptised in Ål with the father written as Asle Knudsen of Medgaardehuus: Knud (16 Jan 1757), Tosten (22 Oct 1758) and Ole (10 Oct 1762). Godparents of all three came from Sando, his wife's home farm: Halvor Tostensen and Tosten Tostensen of Sando, and in 1762 also 'Enken Guro Dybsjorde', the widow Guro of Dybsjorde, his own home farm. In Apr 1762 'Asle Medgaardens Qv.' (Asle of Medgaarden's wife) stood godmother to Tosten, son of Ole Tostensen of Sando. A daughter 'Ragn[ild?]' of an 'Asle Medgaarden' baptised 14 Sep 1760 may be his, but two other Asles lived at Medgaarden (Asle Olsen and Asle Jakobsen), so she is not linked here. ¶3

Brother (inference from the probate): the card names Knud Olsen of Dybsjorde as the children's søskendebarn (first cousin) (index card wording; not yet found in the protocol text). Since Asle's wife was from Sando, a first cousin from Dybsjorde, Asle's home farm, must be a son of Asle's sibling; Ole Knudsen of Dybsjorde (died 1761, aged 44) is therefore very probably Asle's brother. ¶9

Mother (Confirmed): Rangdi (Rangde) Halvorsdatter, d. at Dybsjorde before 16 Jun 1742. Her probate of 16 Jun 1742 (skifteprotokoll 13, source 25548, image 289) names her widower Knud Aslesen Dybsjorde and their children Ole Knudsen, 'Asle Knudsen 14 aar' and Rangdi Knudsdatter (m. Thomas Aslesen). Continuity (no rival): Asle Knudsen, 14 at Dybsjorde in Jun 1742 (b. about 1727-28), is 'Asle Knudsøn Dybsjord' aged 20 at his confirmation on 1 Oct 1747 (five years later, same farm, same age); he married in 1756 as 'Asle Knudssen Dybsjorde', and his 1773 probate names Knud Olsen of Dybsjorde (son of his brother Ole Knudsen, d. 1761) as his children's first cousin. The other Asle Knudsens of Ål (Tangen, Skurdalen, Skarsgaard) have no Dybsjorde tie. ¶10

Life story

Confirmation: 'Asle Knudsøn Dybsjord', aged 20, was confirmed at Torpo church on the 18th Sunday after Trinity (1 Oct 1747), which places his birth in about 1727. On the same day Ole Tostensen of Sando, aged 18, was confirmed; he was probably the brother of Asle's later wife. Other Asle Knudsens in Ål were of Tangen (b. about 1726, buried 1754), Skurdalen (b. about 1735) and Skarsgaard (a father in 1763); none has a tie to Dybsjorde, Medgaarden or Sando. ¶1

Marriage: 'Ung K: Asle Knudssen Dybsjorde og Pig: Kari Tostensdtr: Sando' (bachelor Asle Knudsen of Dybsjorde and the maiden Kari Tostensdatter of Sando) were betrothed (*trolovet*) on 9 Apr 1756 and married on the 11th of a month beginning with J (June or July; the page is torn) 1756 in Ål. This register records no marriage witnesses (*forlovere*). ¶2

Probate: the probate index card for Medgaarden in Ål records the skifte of 12 Oct 1773 after 'Asle Knudsen †' (deceased), with his widow Karie Tostensdatter and five children: Knud 17, Tosten 15, Ole 10, Rangdie 13 and Bergit 8. The widow's brother Tosten Tostensen of Sando and the children's first cousin Knud Olsen of Dybsjorde are named. The estate included the farm Medgaarden, 6 løbel, valued at 350 riksdaler, held by deed (skjøte) of 1756 (day and month on the card uncertain), so by 1773 he owned Medgaarden rather than only a cottar place under it. Gross 617 riksdaler 0 ort 4 skilling, net 445 riksdaler 2 ort 14 skilling. Ringerike og Hallingdal skifteprotokoll 19, fol. 651a (index card; protocol text). The protocol names the children as 'Søn Knud Asle[sen] 17 aar', 'Søn Tosten Aslesen 15 aar', 'Ole Aslesen 10 aar', 'Rangdie Aslesdatter 13 aar' and 'Bergit Aslesdatter 8 aar', and 'Børnenes Morbroder Tosten Tostensen Sanda' (the children's maternal uncle) as their guardian. The widow's lauværge (legal guardian) was Knud Engebretsen Thrinne[?]; the widow was to use the farm rent-free for 5 years and raise the children. ¶6

Death: before 12 Oct 1773, the date of his probate. He was alive at the baptism of his son Ole on 10 Oct 1762. The Ål burials 1765-1769 and 1771-1773 have not been read on the scans and would give the date. ¶7

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Confidence: raised from Probable to Confirmed by the 1773 probate index card and the 1785 card after his widow. They name him with his wife Kari Tostensdatter, whose brother was a Tostensen of Sando, so he is the Dybsjorde bachelor who married Kari Tostensdatter of Sando in 1756; and they name his sons Knud, Tosten and Ole at ages that fit the baptisms of 1757, 1758 and 1762. The link to his own father, Knud Aslesen of Dybsjorde, stays Probable (see that record). ¶8

The case for this identification 1

Identification (Probable): the Asle Knudsen of Medgaardehuus who fathered Tosten is taken to be the Dybsjorde bachelor who married Kari Tostensdatter of Sando in 1756, because his first son was born nine months after the wedding, every son had Sando godparents, the widow of Dybsjorde stood godmother in 1762, his second son carries the name Tosten (Kari's father's name) and his first son the name Knud (his own father's name). No baptism names the mother. ¶4a

Searches that found nothing 1

Death: not found. He was alive in Oct 1762 (son Ole baptised). He is not enumerated anywhere in Ål prestegjeld (Ål, Hol, Torpo) in the 1801 census, so he had probably died or moved away by then. The Ål burials are indexed only to about 1764; those for 1765-1769 (source 8544) and 1771-1806 (source 8545) must be read on the scans. No Ål register survives for 1770, and none on Digitalarkivet before 1744, so his own baptism (about 1727) is not available. ¶5
