About the family

Wife of Povel Sivertssen of Alsaet and mother of Anne Paulsdatter; 1801 census, Selbu, rural residence 0028 Alseth, household 02, aged 40, 'hans kone'. A Brynil Bersvendsdatter, 26, heads the third household on the same farm with her husband Peder Jonssen, and is very likely her sister - two Bersvendsdatter sisters married onto one farm. ¶1

Daughter Anne's baptism: 'Marite Bersvensdatter' is the mother of Anne, born 12 January 1799 and baptised 20 January 1799, father Povel Sivertsen of Alsaet (Selbu ministerialbok 1757-1801, p. 230, no. 1). Confidence raised from Probable to Confirmed on that record; see Povel (Paul) Sivertssen. ¶2

Kin at Anne's baptism: one godmother was 'Brynille Bersvensd. ibidem' - Brynhild Bersvendsdatter of Alsaet, the Brynil Bersvendsdatter, 26, who heads Alseth household 03 in 1801 with her husband Peder Jonssen. Her standing sponsor to Marit's first child supports the earlier inference that the two were sisters; no record yet states it. ¶3

Life story

Her probable sister Brynil Bersvendsdatter of Alsaet: a daughter Brynild was baptised to the same parents on 29 January 1775, but so was a Brynild (twin) to Bersvend Christensen Kallar on 17 April 1775, and the 1801 census has two Brynild Bersvendsdatters aged 26 (Alseth and Punde). Which one stood godmother to Marit's daughter in 1799 is undecided. ¶8

Marriage: betrothed 24 April 1796 and married 19 June 1796 (4th Sunday after Trinity) to the bachelor Povel Sivertsen of Alsaet; she is entered three times, in two hands, as 'Pigen Marite Bersvends d: Fuglem' - the maiden Marit Bersvendsdatter of Fuglem; forlovere Ole Ingebrigtsen Kieldstad and Erich Nielsen Aune (Selbu ministerialbok 1757-1801, canvases 192 and 194). 'Pigen' means a first marriage, as the 1801 census also says. ¶4

Origin, a lead: Fuglem was her residence in 1796, and Fuglem is a distinct Selbu farm (the same pages have Haldor, Mons and Marite of Fuglem), not a slip for Uglem. It may be where she was born or only where she was in service at 35; it is not proof that her father Bersvend lived there. Search the register for a Bersvend of Fuglem with a daughter Marit baptised about 1760-61 (Selbu ministerialbok 1757-1801, canvases for 1760-61), and the Fuglem holders in the bygdebok. ¶5

Baptism, Probable: 'Döbt... (2) Marit. Forældr: Bersvend Ols: Fuglum, Anne Olsd: faddr: Guttorm Pers: Ole Ols: Berit Biørnsd:' - Marit, parents Bersvend Olsen of Fuglem and Anne Olsdatter, 13th Sunday after Trinity, 16 August 1761 (Selbu ministerialbok 1757-1801, canvas 11 right page). Birth replaced 'ABT 1761' with 'before 16 August 1761'. Parents linked as Probable: she married in 1796 as of Fuglem, her 1801 age of 40 fits, a Marit Bersvendsdatter, farm read 'Fugl[em?]' on an overview only, was confirmed in 1780, and the 1801 census places the main rival (Marit of 1760, daughter of Bersvend Nielsen Solum) unmarried in her parents' house at Solem. See Bersvend Olsen for why it is not Confirmed and what would confirm it. ¶7
