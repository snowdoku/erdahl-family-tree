About the family

First wife of Even Jonsen of Haaverud (Hovrud), Aurdal. Her estate was held 7 Oct 1728 (Hadeland, Land og Valdres skifteprotokoll 1716-1737, fol. 914a; index card 6179), with children Juul 11, Jon 5, Lars 3 and Anne 8. The 1751 estate card of Even's second wife (fol. 353b) names her as 'Marit Juelsdatter +', mother of Juel, John, Lars and Anna. Her patronymic shows a father named Jul; the name Jul recurs at Midtstrand (Jul Olsen), but no link is known. Her parents are not identified. ¶1

Father (bygdebok, Probable): Valdres bygdebok I D (Svenes): Hovrud gnr. 76 (canvas 643) has Eivind Jonsson marry 1715 'Marit Julsdotter' of gnr. 65, about 1690-1728; gnr. 65 Strand (canvas 310) lists her as '3 Marit about 1688', daughter of Jul Tordsson (wife uncertain in the rebuilt text). Linked father Jul Tordsen. Marriage year 1715 fits her eldest son Juul being 11 in 1728 (born about 1717). Birth set ABT 1688. ¶3a

Life story

Protocol read: DA 25285 fol. 914a-b (views 931-932) names her 'Marit Juulsdatter', with 'begge deres sammenavlede 4 Børn' by Even Jonsøn (Juul 11, Jon 5, Lars 3, Anne 8 1/2). The entry closes on fol. 915b (view 933, left page): the father Even is himself made guardian ('Faderen... for sine umyndige Børn'), so no morbroder (a Juulsen) is named and the estate does not identify her father Juul. Grade unchanged. ¶2
