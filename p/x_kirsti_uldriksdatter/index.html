About the family

Wife of Jul Olsen at Midt-Strand in the 1801 census, aged 42. Mother of Ole Juelsen, who named his daughter Kirsti (1808) after her. Ole had a brother named Uldrik, after her father. SPECULATIVE: Erik Uldriksøn of Fonnes, a forlover at Ole's 1803 wedding, was probably her brother, which would place her origin at Fonnes. ¶1

Parents: her father Uldrich Thommesen Fodnes (Fodnes nordre, Svenes, d. 21 Jul 1812) and mother Aaste Helgesdatter. His probate card lists, among the children, 'Kirstie enke efter Juel Olsen Midstrand' (Valdres skifteprotokoll 1810-1814, fol. 306b; DA 39948 card 3255). This also shows she was a widow and living in 1812-13. Her siblings were Thommes, Helge and Erich. ¶2

Life story

Confirmation: 'Kierst[i] Uldrichsdat[ter] Fonnes', 15 1/2, in the 1773 confirmation list for the Svenes annex (Aurdal ministerialbok 1763-1781, DA 9362, p. 407, canvas 194), so born about 1757-58 at Fodnes (Fonnes). ¶5

Baptism of her son Ole, 30 Jul 1780 (Aurdal ministerialbok 1763-1781, DA 9362, p. 307, canvas 151): 'Juel Olsøn Mitstrand og hustru Kiersti Uldrichsdatter'. So she was already married to Juel Olsen by mid-1780. ¶4

Probate (index card): she is the widow 'Kjerstie Ulrichsdatter' at her husband Juel Olsen's estate at Midtstrand in Svenes (2 Aug 1810 - 12 Jul 1811; Valdres skifteprotokoll 1810-1814, fol. 76). The children were Ole 30, Ulrich 26, Knud 20, Johannes 17 1/2, Juel 12 and Aaste 28 (m. Juel Evensen of Hovrud). Her son Ulrich bears her father's name. Together with her father Uldrich Thommesen Fodnes's 1812 probate, which names her as 'enke efter Juel Olsen Midstrand', confidence upgraded to Confirmed. ¶3
