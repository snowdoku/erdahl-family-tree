About the family

NEW ANCESTOR. Wife of Evind Svendsen of Nedre Ytre Ramse and, on the reconstruction set out under her daughter, the mother of Berte (Birgitha) Evensdatter who married Tjøstel Tergiesen of Nedre Eppeland. ¶1

Her patronymic is written both Gunnersdatter and Gunnusdatter (from Gunnulv) on different cards. ¶3

Life story

Her estate was settled at Ramse on 15 June 1720, noted on one card as "Sk. e. Evinds kone Anna Gunersdtr., Ramse 15/6-1720" and opening the joint probate of 15 June 1720 to 29 April 1721, which names the four children: Svend (11), Tellef (8), Birgitha and Ingeborrig. ¶2

Probate protocol reading: Skifteprotokoll no. 6, fol. 1, opens the estate on 15 June 1720 at Nedre Ytre Ramse in Lille Tovdal before sorenskriver (district judge) Lytke Mathiasen Crum and two lay assessors, Ole Giermundsen[?] and Jørgen Thorsen[?] of Øvre Ytre Ramse. It names her as Evind Svendsen's deceased wife "Anna Gun-/ner[?]s Daatter" (the word breaks across a line; at high zoom the second half reads -ners, so the spelling Gunnersdatter is kept). Heirs: the widower and their children Svend Evindsen, 11, Tellef Evindsen, 8, Birgitha and Ingeborrig Evindsdøtre. ¶4

Probate protocol reading: the movables were valued at about 340 rd (inventory sum 310-3-11; division figure 340 rd 3 mk 10 sk), of which Evind took half (170-1-17½), each son 56-3-5 5/6 and each daughter 28-1-14 11/12. The final sitting was held on 30 October (year not legible; later sittings in January and July 1721 are mentioned). The protocol names no kin of hers: no father, brother or morbror (mother's brother) appears, and Evind remained guardian of the children "efter Loven" (by law). Her parentage is therefore not established by this record. ¶5

Her estate closed on 30 October 1721: the archivist's card 10411 gives the sittings as 15/6 1720 – 29/7 1721 – 30/10 [1721], and her husband's own estate refers to the "skiftebrev etter fyrste kona 30/10 1721". The card index spells her name Anna Gunnusdatter on both cards (10411 and 10423); the protocol heading of 1722 reads "Anna Gunnu[r?]sdatter". Her husband's estate of 1722-23 names guardians only from his side of the family, so her parents and siblings remain unidentified. ¶6
