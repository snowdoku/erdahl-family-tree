Graded on that link: her own marriage row at Bietigheim in 1718 reads 'Elisabetha Margaretha Majer', relatives 'Tobias, Joh Caspar' (her father, then the groom; record 907270350, image 1184899-00384). Ancestry 61023 index rows do not label roles (BRIEF audit 5 rule 3), so this rests at Probable until the image or a labelled FamilySearch twin is read; images not seen. ¶1b

Corrections 1

Correction: not the father but the first husband of Elisabetha Margaretha (see her page). He is Tobias Mayer, baptised at Bietigheim 12 Mar 1692 to 'Martin, Anna Catharina' (7267975); married Elizabetha Margarethe Haubert, daughter of Johann Christoph, at Bietigheim 29 Oct 1715 (7270306 / 907270306); buried at Bietigheim 2 Jun 1718 (7276045). His widow married Joh Caspar Stierlin later in 1718, and her bride row names him ('Tobias', 907270350). He is therefore no longer an ancestor of the line; the father link from her to him is removed and a marriage to her entered. Graded Probable on that marriage link (unlabelled rows; the widow identification is inferred). ¶2
