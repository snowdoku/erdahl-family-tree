About the family

Mother added: Ane Tostensdatter, named with Endre Nielsen Grønset in the 22 Jan 1769 Kvernes baptism that the tree already uses for her father (Probable). ¶9

Family context (16077 c13, pp.20-21): Anders Andresen [Endresen] of Grønset and his wife Aleth Larsdatter buried a stillborn child on 21 Apr 1782 and she was churched on 9 May 1782. With Anders Endresen Grønset confirmed in 1768, he is probably her elder brother (inferred). ¶11

Parents' probate: the joint probate after Endre Nielsen and Anne Torstensdatter of Grønset, opened 17 May 1780 (Nordmøre skifteprotokoll nr 15, source 24650, fol. 624a), lists 'Datteren Ingeborg Endresdatter gammel 11½ aar' (born about late 1768) among their four children, with guardian Ole Nielsen Haaset[?]. Her full siblings were Torsten (13), Endre (7) and Mali (6); her half-siblings from her father's first marriage were Anders Endresen of Grønset and Anne Endresdatter (26). This supports the 1769 baptism as hers. ¶12

Life story

Kvernes 16076 canvases read: c63-c70, pp.120-135. Side note: c64 (p.122, Laetare 1767) records the baptism of Elling, son of Paul Ellingsen Stokke and Ane Sivertsdatter, likely her husband (already). ¶8

Baptism probably found (run 2): Kvernes ministerialbok 16076, p.135 (canvas c70), Dom. Septuagesima [22 Jan] 1769, 'Ingebor', daughter of Endre Nielsen Grønset and Ane Tostensdatter. Godparents include Jacob Olsen Stokke's wife Ane Aslachsdatter (Stokke being her husband's family farm). The only Ingeborg-daughter-of-Endre in Kvernes pp.120-135 (late 1766 to Feb 1769); it fits the 1801 census age (born about 1769). The link is Probable, not proved: no record yet ties this child to the wife of Elling Povelsen, and Kvernes after Feb 1769 and Kornstad 1766 and 1769 have not been searched for a rival candidate. ¶7

Named as mother of Ingeborg Anna (baptized 5 Dec 1813), with Elling as father. At Lillemark with Elling in the 1825 census. ¶1

Burial scan read: Kvernes local book 1819-1829, female deaths Advent 1827-Advent 1828, p. 201, entry 4: died 'Jan. 22de' [reading of day slightly uncertain] 1828, buried 23 Mar 1828, 'Ingeborg Endresdatter', '[G?]: [?]: Mork' (status abbreviation uncertain; residence Mork, matching Lillemork in 1825), aged 60, so born about 1768. The 23 Mar date in the index is the burial day and 22 Jan the death day; both are right. ¶4

Death: Probable. Ingeborg Endresdatter, age 60, died 22 Jan 1828 and was buried 23 Mar 1828 at Kvernes (no residence given). Historisk befolkningsregister links this burial to her but flags a possible linking error because of the age spread. Elling was a widower before 1840. ¶2

The Kornstad burial index has two entries dated 23 Mar 1828 for an Ingeborg Endresdatter: one aged 60 (born about 1768) and one aged 80 (born about 1748). These may be one entry indexed twice with different age readings, or two women. The 1801 census also has another Ingeborg Endresdatter (born about 1753) at Vevang. Her baptism (about 1769) and marriage (before 1796) fall in years not indexed for Kvernes. The Kornstad book 1750-1789 (source 16095/16096) is scanned but not indexed. ¶3

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed, graded on her link to her husband Elling Povelsen Lillemark. Kvernes betrothal register 16080, canvas 138, p. 275, 1796: 'Aug: 6te forlangte u[ng]k[arl] og Sold[at] Elling Povlsøn Stokke, som skal faae 1 Part i Gaarden Gulset til beboelse, at trolove i Kvernes d. 28 ejusd. s[om er] D[om]: 14 p. Tr[initatis] med p[ige] Ingbor Endresd[atte]r Grønset. For deres Egteskabs Lovlighed godsige Ole Ellings[en] Stokke [and] Anders Endres[en] Grønset.' The groom, Elling Povelsen of Stokke, was to take part of Guldset, and in the 1801 census Elling Povelsen and Ingeborg Endresdatter are husband and wife at Guldset, five years later on the farm the entry names; the 1813 baptism of Ingeborg Anna and the 1825 census (Lillemork) name her with Elling. No rival couple of these names exists in Kvernes. Marriage date: betrothed 6 Aug 1796, banns to be read and the wedding planned at Kvernes from 28 Aug 1796 (14th Sunday after Trinity); the wedding date itself is not in this register. ¶13

Upgrade: Confirmed, graded on her link to her son Paul Ellingsen (see his page), not on her own parentage (which stays Probable). The record naming the relationship is Paul's baptism, Kvernes ministerialbok 1780-1819 (1553P), pp. 460-461, 28 Apr 1805 (born 26 Apr): 'Elling Povelsen Guldsets og H[ustr]u', child 'Povel' (scan, kb20070912620442; the index pd00000006673381 omits the mother because the entry gives her only as 'Hustru', his wife). Continuity that identifies the wife: the 1801 census at Guldset has Elling Povelsen 35 and his wife Ingeborg Endresdatter 31, both in their first marriage; the 5 Dec 1813 baptism of Ingeborg Anna names Elling and Ingeborg as parents; the 1825 census at Lillemork has 'Ingebor Endresdatt', 60, as 'hu' (hustru) in Elling Paavelsen's household, with 'Paavel Ellingsen' 21 in the same household. Same couple at every record from 1801 to 1825, both in their first marriage in 1801 and both alive in 1825, so there is no rival wife who could be the 'Hustru' of 1805. ¶14
