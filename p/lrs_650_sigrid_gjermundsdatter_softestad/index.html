About the family

Life story

Bygdebok (Nissedal vol., Søftestad, canvas 451): daughter of Gjermund Ormson (d. about 1676, whose widow married Torjus Ulvson); married first Tellev Tallakson and then Gunstein [Olsen], to whom she brought odel in Søftestad; mother of Tellev (about 1700) and Sigrid (about 1701); died about 1701, with a skifte ('fyrste kona [til] Gunstein døydde omlag 1701... skiftet'). ¶1

An earlier record of the couple: pantebok I 2 (14504) No 305, entered about Oct 1692 (view 5 left): 'Kiendes Jeg Undertegnede Gunsten Olsen Søftestad paa Min Hustrues Sigri Giermundsdatters, samt Stifdatters Rannou(?) Tallefsdatters vegne' (I, Gunsten Olsen of Søftestad, on behalf of my wife Sigri Gjermundsdatter and my stepdaughter Rannou(?) Tallefsdatter). Knud Andersen joins for his wife Margit Tallachsdatter, about a mortgage their late father(-in-law) Tallach had given on half of 1½ tønder in Nedre Fione. So Gunstein was on Søftestad and married to Sigri by about 1692. With Tallef's daughter aged 12 in 1728, Tollef Gunsteinsen was born about 1685-1697; the bygdebok's 'omlag 1700' is too late. The stepdaughter reads Rannou here; the 1701 reading 'Kari(?)' is uncertain and is probably the same girl. The deed does not concern Vik. The bygdebok's 1692 Vik sale was not found in views 2-9 of 14504. ¶8

Gap still open for her too (graded on the link to Tallef Gundstensen Søftestad; stays Probable). Checked: (1) Vest-Telemark skifteprotokoll 25669 views 264-386 (19 Aug 1739 to Sep 1740): the only Nissedal session is 6-9 Apr 1740 (views 350-359: Lintvedt, Grimstvedt, a Nissedal farm on 8 Apr with heirs Signe and Sigri Knudsdøttre, Midbø); no Søftestad probate after Gunstein Olsen or his widow Gunhild. (2) Panteregister I 1 (10915) s. 44b, the whole Søftestad entry: Gunsten Olsen got Søftestad land 1696 (deeds dated 7 Nov [1695?] tinglyst 15 Jun 1696, one from Egill Jørgensen Meule), mortgaged 1/2 td to Peder Ellefsen 1701, sold 1 td to Jørgen Egilsen Meule (deed 30 Jun 1712, tinglyst 15 Oct 1712); then a line dated 1718 in which Gunsten Olsen mortgages [pantsat] land 'i denne gaard' to Hans Ellefsen T[ong?] for 150 rd (obligation dated 22 Jun..., reading uncertain), so Gunstein himself held the land again by late 1718; the next owners are Anders Halvorsen Næs, who sold to Ole Tallefsen (deed tinglyst 24 Oct 1754), and Ole Tallefsen 1759-65. No deed in the register names Tollef Gunsteinsen, so the 'odelsberettigede' who redeemed Jørgen Eigilsen's 1 td (card 6856 v1261) was probably Gunstein, not Tollef, and the redemption does not bridge the gap. ¶10

Her probate (died 1701 per the bygdebok, 'Ved skiftet etter [ho]': net estate 36 rd, Guri Nes a creditor) should be in Øvre Telemark østfjelske skifteprotokoll no. 4, 1700-1703 (Digitalarkivet 25699; Kviteseid/Nissedal were østfjelske then), not in the vestfjelske 25691 (read for Oct 1700-Nov 1701 with no Nissedal entries). Read 25699 views 55-85 (autumn 1701, skifter Nos 34-57) and 110-113 (Nissedal session 7 Oct 1702). If the entry says 'deris tilsammen avlede børn' and names Tallef (b. about 1700), it would confirm Gunstein Olsen and her as parents of Tallef Gundstensen Søftestad. ¶5

Her own skifte read: Søftestad, 8 Oct 1701 (østfjelske 25699 No 39, view 64): 'Octobris d. 8de Var paa Søfftestad i Neisedahls annex med tvende laugrettismend [..] at registere, vurdere og siden skifte og dele efter afgangne Sigri Giermundsdaatter imellem hendes efterlevende mand Gunsteen Olsen og sammen avlede børn Tollef Gunsteensøn og Sigri Gunsteensdaatter, og med første mand Tollef Tallachsen avlet Kari(?) Tollefsdaatter, overværende hendes formynder Egil Jørgensen Meule' (the estate to be divided between her surviving husband Gunstein Olsen and the children they had together, Tollef and Sigri Gunsteinsen, and Kari Tollefsdatter, her child by her first husband Tollef Tallaksen, whose guardian Egil Jørgensen Meule was present). It confirms her name Sigri Giermundsdaatter, her death before 8 Oct 1701, her husband Gunsteen Olsen, her first husband Tollef Tallachsen, and that Tollef Gunsteensøn was her child with Gunstein. Graded on her link to Tallef Gundstensen Søftestad: kept PROBABLE. Continuity to Tallef Gundsteensen of the 1728 Søftestad probate: same farm; the father Gunsten Olsen is on Søftestad throughout 1696-1718 (panteregister I 1, 10915 fol. 44b); Tallef named his eldest daughter Sigri (b. about 1716) after this mother; the only other Tallev Gunsteensen of the farm (son of Gunsteen Tallefsen and Ellen Asbjørnsdatter, Moen under Søftestad) was 17 in 1731 and cannot be the 1728 father of a girl of 12. BUT no record between Oct 1701 and Nov 1728 (27 years) names Tollef/Tallef himself, so by the audit-3 gap rule this stays Probable. ¶6a

Half-sibling of Tallef: Kari(?) Tollefsdaatter, her daughter by Tollef Tallachsen (guardian Egil Jørgensen Meule). Two days earlier (6 Oct 1701, 25699 No 37, view 61) a skifte at Grøvum in Nissedal after Torgier Tallachsen names a 'Farbroder' Tov(?) Tallachsen: likely kin of her first husband 'Tellev Tallakson Grovum-Fjone' (bygdebok); not added. ¶7

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

Graded on her link to Tallef Gundstensen (see his page). ¶2a

Graded Probable: a bygdebok is a compiled secondary source and the passage was rebuilt from word boxes with gaps; dates from it are ABT. His eldest daughter Sigri (12 in 1728) carries her name. ¶2b

Still Probable as mother of (the 1701-1728 gap). Her son's 1755 probate (25673 No 154) does not name her. Her daughter by her first marriage, Rannej Tollefsdatter, was the widow in the 7 Mar 1718 Søftestad probate after Jørgen Eigildsøn (25692 fol. 305b); her land in Søftestad was 'indløst av den odelsberettigede' (card 6856 v1261), which is a lead for a deed naming her son Tollef as odel heir. ¶9
