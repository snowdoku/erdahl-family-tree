About the family

Mother of Rolv Knudsen Slettum (see his page), Probable; graded on her link to Rolv. Source: the Lesja bygdebok only (vol. 1, nb.no item 8e238b3c93fae7ff68ee91598c918b4d, canvas 370, chapter Slettom): Knut Rolfs 'g. 1717 m. Kari ___ frå Nørdre Kvam'; their first child listed is 'Rolf, 1720, tok over'. Her patronymic falls in a gap of the word-box rebuild (a word not queried). Birth before 1700 is an estimate from her 1717 marriage. Capped at Probable (compiled source); the test would be a skifte after her or her husband (probate cards 39945) or a Nordre Kvam probate naming her as a daughter. ¶1

Life story

Name change: patronymic Endresdatter, and parents found. The skifte after 'Endre Qvam' at Kvam in Lesjaskogen, May 1726 (skifteprotokoll 1719-1726, source 25156, fol. 346b; cards 39945 images 7531-7532) names '5. Kari Endresdatter Knud Rolfsen Slettums Hustru'; the skifte after her mother 'Birete Lauridzd.' at Nordre Qvam, 3 Sep 1708 (1690-1719, fol. 73b) lists 'Karri' among 'deris 6 Børn' with Endre Olufsen. The Lesja bygdebok's Slettom entry reads 'Kari Endresd. frå Nørdre Kvam' (canvas 370, words recovered by exact-phrase search), and the 1763 extra tax has 'Knud Rolvsøn, Kari Endresd.' as inderst at Slettum. Her own grade (link to Rolv Knudsen) is unchanged at Probable: these records tie her to Knut Rolfsen, not to Rolv. Test record for her link to Rolv: a probate after Knut Rolfsen or Kari (after 1763) naming Rolv as their son. ¶2

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
