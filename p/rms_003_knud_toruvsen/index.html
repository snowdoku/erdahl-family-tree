About the family

Probable son of Toruv Andersen Hiemdal (see his page): patronymic and farm match, and Toruv Andersen is the only Toruv seen at Hiemdal in the 1773-86 marriage register. The entry does not name his father. Would be confirmed by his baptism (Kviteseid ministerialbok before 1773) or a probate (skifte) after Toruv. ¶2

Life story

Betrothed on the 1st Sunday after Epiphany 1774 in Treungen as 'Knud Torofsen Hiemdal', a soldier, to Ingri Joensdatter Lille Dale(?); cautioners Orm Tvedt and Jørgen Home; married in Treungen church on the 5th Sunday after Trinity 1774 (Kviteseid ministerialbok 1773-1786, image 106, p.101; difficult hand). In Nov 1775 he was cautioner as 'Knud Torofsen Lille Dale' (image 110). ¶1

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
