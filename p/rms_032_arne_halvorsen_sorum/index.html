Life story

Marriages (indexed church books): (2) betrothed 29 Dec 1734 and married 7 Mar 1735 at Jevnaker. He was Arne Halvorssen of Sørrum and the bride Ane Hansdatter of Biertnæs. The betrothal sponsors were Amun of Vaaker and Tron of Stadum. (1) Probably the 'Arne Hallvordsøn', a dragoon in Captain Gynter's company, who married Ane Hansdaatter of Raastad at Gran on 7 Jan 1723 (p. 145b). That fits his first wife, Anne Hansdatter Raastad, on the 1746 probate card, but the groom's farm is not given. ¶5

Baptisms: Arne Halvorsen of Sørum (Toverud in 1736), Gran parish, had children baptised at Gran: Halvor on 28 Sep 1732, Anne on 15 Jan 1736, Elli on 5 Jan 1738 (entered as father 'Arne', Sørum) and Rønnof on 23 Oct 1740. The mother is Anne Hansdatter in 1736, 1738 and 1740. Shared godparents (Embret Hansen of Dal, Dorthe Hansdatter of Igelsrud, the Stadom family) show the 1738 'Arne' is the same man. His birth year is inferred from his first known child. ¶1

Gran confirmation lists 1745-1758 (DA 9341, from canvas 207) give only name, farm and age, with no parents, so a confirmation of Eli c.1753-55 would not settle her identity with Elie of Sørum (b. c.1738). The 1664 Hadeland census (DA 35503) lists Knud Sørum 52 with sons Anders 44 [sic], Erick 25, Axel 14, Niels 9 at Sørum (Gran) - no Halvor; 1664 is too early to bear on Arne's parents. ¶8

Probate (index card,; read on the image by the orchestrator): the estate of Arne Halvorsen of Sørum in Gran was held 21 May 1746 (Hadeland, Land og Valdres skifteprotokoll 1744-52, fol. 91b). Wives: (1) Anne Hansdatter Raastad, deceased; (2) Anne Hansdatter Biørtnæs (Bjørtnes), the widow. Children: Hans 17 and Ingeborg 23, ruled off from the rest on the card as the first marriage's children; then Anne 10, Elie 8 and Rønaug 5. Gross 390-2-4, net 277-3-[?] rdr, including land in Sørum valued at 220 rdr. ¶3

Probate protocol read: DA 25287, fol. 91b, 21 May 1746, at Sørum in Gran. The protocol says Arne Halvorsen had been married twice. It lists 5 children of the two wives, 1 son and 4 daughters. By his first wife, the late Anne Hans Datter Raastad: the son Hans, 17, a minor (umyndig), and the eldest daughter Ingeborg, 23. By the surviving widow Anne: the 2nd daughter Anne, 10, the 3rd daughter Elie, 8, and the 4th daughter Rønaug, 5. The son Hans was represented by his morbror (maternal uncle) Niels Niels[søn?] Raastad. The widow's lagverge (her legal guardian at the estate) and the guardians of the younger daughters are in blotted lines and were not read. ¶7

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

2 on the grade

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Speculative as father of Eli Arnesdatter of Hvinden: see the note on Eli. ¶2

Confidence upgraded from Speculative to Probable by this probate card: it shows that Arne's daughter Elie, aged 8 in May 1746 (born about 1737/38, matching the baptism of Elli, daughter of Arne of Sørum, on 5 Jan 1738), was living in 1746. She remains the only Eli/Elli daughter of an Arne in Gran 1728-1747. The naming fits: Eli Arnesdatter of Hvinden named daughters Anne and Kirsti and a son Hans. No record yet states that Elie of Sørum is Eli of Hvinden (m. 1761), so this is not Confirmed. The full protocol entry at fol. 91b, or a confirmation c.1753-55, could settle it. ¶4
