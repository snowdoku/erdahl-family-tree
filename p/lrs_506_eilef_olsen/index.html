Life story

Probate: the probate (registering, valuation and division) after 'den Sal. afdøde dannemand Elløf Olsøn' (the late honest man Eilef Olsen) was held on the farm Botten in Ladvig skipreide on 20 Jun 1730 by Oluf Hansen Gøe, between his widow Maritte Endresdatter on the one side and their children Ole, Giertrud, Elizabeth and Britte on the other. Ole and Elisabeth were of age (myndig); Giertrud was dead and had left a minor daughter, Maritte Endresdatter; Brithe was a minor. The appraisers were Colben [?] Tafle and Claus Tafle. Shares: the widow half, 8 Rd 4 ß; the son Ole 3 Rd 2 ort 14 3/5 ß; each daughter 1 Rd 4 ort 7 1/5 ß. Endre Johansen Eiken, father of Giertrud's daughter, was made guardian of that granddaughter, and 'her sister's husband' Endre Johansen Eiken guardian of Brithe. Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll 3b, fol. 433b-434a. ¶1

1701 census (from the crop saved): on Botten (Botnen), Eivindvik (Gulen), the only householder is 'Heløf Olsen', aged 44, with no sons listed and the servant Johannes Tomasen, born in Sogn, 24 (Manntall 1701, 38455 viewer 56, s.95). Heløf is a form of Eilef (Elløf, as his 1730 probate spells it). He is taken to be this Eilef Olsen, whose probate was held at Botten on 20 Jun 1730: same name, patronymic and farm, and he is the only Eilef/Heløf at Botten. Birth about 1657 is inferred from the age of 44. The 1701 list counts only males, so the daughters (Giertrud, Elisabeth, Brithe) would not appear; the son Ole, an heir in 1730, is not listed and was probably born after 1701. ¶4

Death: before 20 Jun 1730, the date of his probate, at Botten. No burial has been looked for. ¶2

How sure are we?

Searches that found nothing 1

1664 census, negative: in the bailiff's census of 21 Jun 1664 the farm Botten (s.242, the last page of Brekke sogn, before 'Ehuenvigs Sogn'; had stopped at s.241) has one farmer, Hans, with the servant Clemmund Andersen 18, and no Ole. So Eilef Olsen (about 7 in 1664, too young to be listed) was probably not born on Botten to an Ole farming there, or his father had not yet come to the farm. Other farms on the page: Indre Hiertholm (Siffuer with sons Thor[?] 14 and Anders 12; Hans Olsen), Ytre Hiertholm (Niels; Peder Mathissen 20 in the sons' column; husmann Anders Mogensen 50), Sandvig (one farmer, name unread), Brevig (Niels, Jorn, Olluf). ¶5
