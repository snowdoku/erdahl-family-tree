About the family

Youngest of the six children, and the last of the family to be baptized in Norway. Born 23 August 1862 and baptized 12 October 1862 in the Hegra annex, entry no. 91 on page 74; her father is entered as a husmann at Beitlandsplads. She was an infant in arms when the family crossed the Atlantic. ¶1

Life story

Known in America as Christine T. Beitland. Married Hendrik 'Henry' Gullord (1858-1943). Four children are recorded: Edwin Bernhart (1881-1918), Theodore H. (1883-1973), Cora Marie (1891-1984, who married an Almlie - the Almli farm name from her grandmother's home in Hegra surfacing again in Minnesota) and Oscar Edward (1901-1946). ¶2

Died 5 August 1945 in Freeborn County, Minnesota, aged 82, and was brought back to Bricelyn to be buried with her parents. She outlived her sister Beret Martha by 67 years. ¶3
