Life story

Probate 1742: Ole Østensen, aged 21, was one of the three heirs at the probate held on 29 May 1742 after Ane Olsdatter, late wife of Østen Rasmussen of Hilde. The other heirs were his brothers Rasmus (23) and Arne (19). The age gives a birth about 1720/21, close to the 1722 implied by his burial age. His father, Østen Hilde, administered the sons' inheritance as their curator (guardianship protocol no. 3, p. 59). This confirms the parentage Aaland implies ('Ola Øysteinson Hilde'). His brother Arne Østensen was guardian of Ole's eldest son at Ole's own 1780 probate. ¶6

Farmer at Tiellov (Tjellaug). With Aase Rasmusdatter he had children baptized: Anna 1755, Østen 1757, Berthe 1759 (died as an infant), Rasmus 1760 and Berthe 1765. Probate registered 1777-80 at Tjellog. ¶1

Probate: Nordfjord skifteprotokoll A 16b, fol. 834b-836b (index pa00000000056595), read from the scan. The opening lines with the date and the deceased's name are lost in the black-and-white scan; the index puts Ole Østensen of Tjellog on this page. The heirs are the widow Aase Rasmusdatter (her legal guardian, laugverge, Cornelius Nielsen U[tvik?]) and the 5 children of the marriage: Østen Olsen 22[?], Rasmus Olsen 21[?], Ole Olsen 18[?], Anne Olsdatter 24[?] and Brithe Olsdatter 15 (ages other than Brithe's uncertain). Net estate 220 rd: the widow got half (110), each son 27 rd 3 ort and each daughter 13 rd 4 ort 8 s. ¶2

Guardianship record: Nordfjord Overformyndarprotokoll 3, p. 79. On 9 Jun 1755 'Ole Ostensen Tiellof som har ægtet Aase Tiellov' acknowledged receipt of her inheritance of 15 rd 14 s from the 6 Jun 1742 Tjellaug skiftebrev. So he was married to Aase Rasmusdatter by June 1755, the year Aaland gives for his deed to the holding. ¶7

Probate guardians (formyndere), 1780: for the eldest son Arne[?] Østensen [farm uncertain, Rise?]; for the second son Colben[?] (Kolbein?) Thomassen Gredung[?]; for the youngest son Lasse Andersen [farm uncertain]; for the eldest daughter Ab[?]slon (Absalon?) Andersen Erdahl; for the youngest daughter Brithe, Anthoni Thorsen Gredung[?]. Arne[?] Østensen shares Ole's patronymic and may be his brother. That is inferred only: guardians were often kin, but the record does not say so. ¶3

Aaland 1932: Aaland's Kjellog (Tjellaug) history, bruk nr. 1 'Uppigard', reads 'Vs. Ola Øysteinson Hilde, † 58 a. g. 1780, sk. 1755. * Aase Rasmusd. Kjell., † 73 a. g. 1797': Ole Øystein's son (Østensen) came from Hilde (on the Innvik side). He married into the farm as son-in-law (verson) of Rasmus Kristoffersen, took the deed in 1755, and died in 1780 aged 58. This agrees with his 1780 probate and the tree's birth about 1722. His successors were his sons Øystein (died 1817 aged 53, tenant 1789) and Rasmus (died 1816 aged 70). The 1755 deed in the Nordfjord mortgage register (pantebok) would confirm the transfer. For his father see Østen (Øystein) Rasmussen Hilde. ¶5

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Confidence moved Probable to Confirmed: the 1780 probate (A 16b, fol. 834b) is a primary record of his estate (the deceased's name in its opening lines is illegible; the entry is identified by the index line at fol. 834b and by the heirs), naming his widow Aase Rasmusdatter and his children Østen, Rasmus, Anne and Brithe, who match the Tiellov baptisms of 1755-1765. It also names a son Ole Olsen (born about 1762), whose baptism is not yet in the tree. ¶4
