About the family

Eldest daughter of Mikal Paulsen Eidem and Beret (Slind) Eidem. Married Conrad B. Lund (16 November 1889, South Dakota - 8 April 1979, Lac qui Parle County) in 1918; son Clifford Maurice Lund (1922-2011). ¶1

Life story

The Lund connection runs through the whole congregation: a Ludwig Lund was one of the six pallbearers at Oline Eidem's funeral in 1955. ¶3

Burial: Zoar Lutheran Cemetery, lot 2A — South Dakota state burial register, 'MINNIE LUND 02-02-1987, lot 2A, block 00'. The Lund lot also holds Conrad (d. 8 April 1979), Nels Lund (d. 23 October 1928) and Ray Kenneth Lund (d. 6 August 1949). ¶2

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
