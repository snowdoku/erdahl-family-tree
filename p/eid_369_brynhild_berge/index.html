Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Graded Probable on her link to her daughter Guru (Goro) Torstensdatter (see her page). Selbu i fortid og nåtid vol. 5, Berge 'Brukere' (canvas 21): '1685-1721 Torsten Sjurdsen, f. ca. 1652, d. 1722, [...] var bror[sønn] til Ola Torstensen. Kona [...] Brynhild d. 1719. Fra 1719 [...] bygslet [...]', then 'Barn: 1. Sjurd, f. ca. 1691 [...]; 2. Haldor, f. ca. 1694; 3. Ola, f. ca. 1696, g.m. Goro [...] Kyllo [...] Berge; 4. Goro, f. ca. 1698, g.m. Erik [...] Uglem'. The four children are exactly the sons Siur 10, Haldor 6 and Oller 4 of Torsten Siursen at Berge in the 1701 manntall and Guru Torstensdatter, wife of Erik Svendsen Uglem, whose brother 'morbroren Siver Tostensen Berge' is named at Erik's probate in 1741 (77804 c2055). So the book's Brynhild is the wife of the tree's Torsten Siursen and the mother it gives for Guru. ¶1

Why only Probable: the book alone names her; no record read gives her name or ties her to the children. Torsten was about 40-47 when the four children were born (1691-98), so an earlier wife cannot be ruled out from the book. ¶2

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Test record: her burial in the Selbu register 16310 in 1719 ('Tosten Berges qv. Brynild'; the 1717-18 entries are at about canvases 66-69, so 1719 is about canvases 72-80, free scan, not read), which would confirm the marriage and her death year; and, for the link to Guru, a probate after Brynhild or Torsten (none in the 77804 Berge cards; Torsten died 1722, in the 1701-27 protocol gap). A Digitalarkivet search for 'Bryn*' in Selbu 1718-20 returns no indexed burial. ¶3
