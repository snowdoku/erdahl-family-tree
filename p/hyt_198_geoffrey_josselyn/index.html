Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Graded on his link to his son John Josselyn. The only statement that John Josselyn of Sheering was 'son and heir of Geoffrey' is the heraldic pedigree recorded by Thomas Joscelin of London at the Visitation of London 1633-4 (College of Arms C.24 f.258b; printed by Howard and Crisp), a descendant of John's third son Geoffrey. French (NEHGR 71:240-241, nos. 16 and 18) identifies this Geoffrey with Jeffrey, younger son of Jeffrey Jocelyn of Hide Hall (d. 1425) and brother of Thomas of Hide Hall and of Sir Ralph Josselyn, Lord Mayor of London (d. 1478), and dates his death from the Sawbridgeworth brass. That fits the 1524 will of John Josselyn of High Roding, who calls Ralph son of John of Sheering his 'cousin' (French: second cousin) and puts that line next in the entail after his own and his brother's heirs, but no record read names Geoffrey as John's father. Which of his two wives (Katherine, Joan) was John's mother is unknown, so neither is entered. Speculative (visitation only). ¶1a

Searched again, grade unchanged (Speculative). No record found that names John Josselyn of Sheering as Geoffrey's son: TNA Discovery (Geoffrey/Jeffrey Josselyn, 1400-1530), the Calendar of Inquisitions post mortem Henry VII (vols 1-3) and archive.org full text give only compiled statements (Burke's Landed Gentry: 'The 2nd son, Geoffrey Josselyn, was father of John Joscelyn, of Sheering'; the 1634 visitation). The Hyde Hall inquisition of 22 Hen. VII (CIPM Hen. VII vol. 3 no. 933) shows the Hyde Hall line running George Josselyn of Sawbridgeworth (grant of 10 Mar. 21 Edw. IV) to Ralph (d. 23 Nov. 1504, wife Katharine) to George (aged 14 in 1504); it does not mention Geoffrey or Sheering. Test record unchanged, plus: ERO TS 557/1 (Joscelyne will transcripts from 1465) for a will of Geoffrey (d. 1470/1) or of John of Sheering. ¶2
