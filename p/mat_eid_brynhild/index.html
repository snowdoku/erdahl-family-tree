Life story

Buried as 'Brynhild Kulsetmoen Eidem'. Kulsetmo is a farm in Selbu, Sør-Trøndelag. Her maiden name survives in the Minnesota death index entries of her three children, which give the mother's surname as KULSETHMO. ¶1

First marriage: 'Brynhild was born in Selbu, Norway. She married Nils Klegseth and had three daughters. Nils died when the girls were very young; and Brynhild accepted her brother's invitation to come to Marietta, MN to live.' A granddaughter's account adds that Nils Klegseth 'died in a work accident in Selbu, Norway'. She therefore arrived at Marietta a widow with three small children, from Anders Eidem's own home village. ¶3

Later life: 'Brynhild and Anders farmed at Marietta until Anders died, at age 81. Then Brynhild moved to Rochester and lived with her daughter, Benda who was a life-long teacher.' She died at Rochester, Olmsted County, Minnesota, 21 March 1959, and was brought back to Zoar for burial. ¶5

The related Selbu farm name KULSETH does appear - and it connects to SLIND. A BESSIE KULSETH, b. 5 February 1879 in Norway, filed a Social Security application naming her father as SVEN JOHNSON and her mother as BERET SLIND (NUMIDENT SS-5, SSN XXX-XX-7175). That is the only documented Slind-Kulseth link anywhere in the American record and it is recorded under Beret Slind. Washington also holds a small Kulseth group - seventeen deaths and divorces, two Skagit County naturalisations, two Skagit military-eligibility registers - but none of them names a Slind or an Eidem. ¶9

Burial plot: Zoar Lutheran Cemetery, lot 16 (South Dakota state burial register, 'BRYNHILD EIDEM 03-21-1959, lot 16'). Her husband Anders lies in lot 15, with her daughter Benda and with Beret Kulsethmo (1841-1929). ¶6

Beret Kulsethmo, whose photographed stone at Zoar reads simply 'BERET KULSETHMO / 1841 - 1929' and who lies in lot 15 beside Anders and Benda, is of exactly the right generation to be Brynhild's own mother, and Kulsetmo is the Selbu farm Brynhild is named from. Speculative — no record has been found that states the relationship. ¶7

KULSETMO/KULSETHMO SWEEP, as the brief asked. The surname is effectively absent from the American indexes: ZERO Kulsetmo or Kulsethmo records of any kind in the Washington State Digital Archives (all 25 record series, from marriages and deaths to land and school censuses), and ZERO in the VA Nationwide Gravesite Locator. The only American trace of the name outside Deuel County, South Dakota is in the Minnesota death index's mother's-maiden-name field, where her three children Anton Bernhard, Nickolai and Benda Elvina Eidem are each entered as a child of "KULSETHMO". ¶8

So the brief's hypothesis that Kulsetmo would turn up alongside Slind in an American index is a clean negative for Kulsetmo itself and a narrow positive for Kulseth. ¶10

Corrections 1

Correction: her husband was ANDERS ANDERSSON EIDEM (1852/3-1932), not Mikal Paulsen Eidem. Find a Grave records the marriage as 6 May 1901, and the biographies on her own memorial and on those of Anders, Anton and Benda — all written by a descendant of Anders's brother Bardo Eidem — state it plainly: 'Brynhild met the bachelor farmer, Anders Andersson Eidem there, and they married at Zoar Lutheran Church May 6, 1901.' Mikal Paulsen Eidem's wife was Beret Eidem, 1872-1922, and the two couples' children are entirely different sets of people. ¶2

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Lead: Kulsetmo is spelled KOLSETMOEN in the 1992 farm history - Kulsetmo, Kulsetmoen, Kulseth and Kolsetmo all return nothing - and its chapters are in Selbu i fortid og naatid, bind 6, with all-caps KOLSETMOEN headings on printed pages 214, 218, 235, 245, 249 and 253, under the parent farm KOLSET whose headings are on pages 200, 202, 223, 225, 228, 229, 241, 242 and 250. Her exact birth date, 4 February 1870, returns no hit as '4.2.1870' even though volume 6 does print some full dates (19.10.1959, 25.3.1959, 23.8.1868 and 28.8.1900 all occur), so her entry gives the year only or she is not in these chapters. The pages carrying both the name Brynhild and the year 1870 are 227 and 228, and page 228 also carries Amerika, four separate occurrences of Brynhild, and the tenure 1850-1870; 1959, her death year, occurs on pages 233, 238, 247 and 249. Treat page 228 as Speculative only: Brynhild is one of the commonest female names in Selbu and I have not read the page. Order volume 6, pages 214-256. ¶11
