About the family

Father: see Ole Søftestad (see his page), Probable. His mother is unknown. ¶5

Probable brother: Johannes Olsen Søftestad, bachelor and soldier, married Groe Jørgensdatter Tvedt in Nissedal church on 19 Aug 1787 (betrothed 9 Jul 1787 at Søftestad), and stood cautioner at Tellev's betrothal in 1788. Tellev's 1801 household held 'hans broder søn' (his brother's son) Ole Johansen, 12, which fits a son of Johannes. Kviteseid ministerialbok 1787-1799. ¶7

Mother (Speculative): Anne Johannesdatter, wife of Ole Søftestad, buried 1798 aged 62 (b. about 1736). She was about 24 when Tellev was baptised in 1760, which is plausible, and Tellev's daughter Anne (baptised 1792) had 'Anne Søftestad' and 'Ole Søftestad' as godparents. But Ole was about 41 in 1760 and may have had an earlier wife, so she is not proved to be Tellev's mother. Would decide it: a probate after Ole Søftestad (died 1799) or after Anne (died 1798) naming Tellev, in the Øvre Telemark sorenskriveri records. ¶8

Parents named: Vest-Telemark panteregister I 2 (10916, p. 783, view 131 right), Søftestad: 'Tallef Olsen... Contract med hans Forældre Ole Tallefsen og Anne Johannesdatter', 13 Oct 1791, tingl. 3 Mar 179[2]. He bought 1 tønde in Søftestad from his brother Johannes Olsen in 1788 (deed tingl. 28 Feb 1788, No 1750); Johannes had it 'af sin Fader Ole Tallefsen' in 1787. The probate after his wife 'Kari Torjusdatter, Tallef Olsens' hustru' was begun 3 Sep 1803 and closed 3 Nov 1804; heirs listed in the register: Tallef Olsen Søftestad, Ole Tallefsen (two of the name), Torjus Tallefsen, Halvor Tallefsen, Jacob Tallefsen and a daughter [Ingeborg?] Tallefsdatter. ¶15

Life story

Baptism: on 24 Mar 1760 in Nissedal church 'Ole Søftestads Søn Tallef' (Ole Søftestad's son Tallef) was baptised. Godparents (testes): Ole Midbøe, Hans Næs, Sigri Søftestad, and Sigri and Aslaug Holt. The mother is not named. The year comes from the page: the facing page dates Midfaste Sunday (Laetare) 16 Mar, which fits 1760, and the previous canvas is headed from the 1st Sunday in Advent 1759. Kviteseid ministerialbok 1754-1773, p. 58. ¶3

Confirmation: on the Sunday after New Year 1778 in Nissedal church, 'Teller Olsen Søftestad', aged 16 (born about 1762 by that age). Kviteseid ministerialbok, source 7982, confirmations 1778, canvas 213 right page. Age conflict: his baptism of 24 Mar 1760 makes him 17 in Jan 1778, and the 1801 census (41) gives about 1760; the confirmation age is taken as loose. ¶10

Marriage: betrothed on the 5th Sunday after Easter (27 Apr 1788) in Nissedal, 'U:K: og Soldat Tallev Olsen Søftestad' (bachelor and soldier) and 'Pigen Kari Torjusd: Tvedt' (the maid Kari Torjusdatter of Tvedt); cautioners (sureties) Evind Torgiusen (Torjusen) Tvedt and Johannes Olsen Søftestad. Married on the 6th Sunday after Trinity (29 Jun 1788) in Nissedal church. Dates computed (Easter 1788 = 23 Mar). Kviteseid ministerialbok 1787-1799 (source 7983), Trolovede og Copulerede 1788. This replaces the earlier 'before 1789'. ¶6

Bonde og gaardbeboer (farmer and occupant) at Søftestad, Nissedal, age 41 in the 1801 census of Kviteseid prestegjeld. Wife Kari Torjusdatter, 40. Children: Ole 12, Ole 10, Anne 7, Torjus 5, Halvor 2. Also in the household: his brother's son Ole Johansen, 12. His identification as Torjus Tellefsen's father is Probable (see Torjus). ¶1

Widowed: his wife Kari Torjusdatter was found dead on 19 Aug 1803, probably murdered, and buried at Nissedal church on 18 Sep 1803. No second marriage is known; his 1810 probate names no widow. ¶14

Death (index card): 31 Mar 1808, per the probate index card 'Søftestad i Nissedal' (no. 1263), which writes '† 1808 31/3'; the date in the probate text itself could not be read. He is not in the 1808 burials of Kviteseid prestegjeld (source 7984, fols. 163a-164a), and the estate was not registered until 2 Nov 1809, so the card's year may be wrong (a death early in 1809 is possible); the 1807 and 1809 burials have not been read. ¶12

Probate: Vest-Telemark (Øvre Telemark) sorenskriveri, skifteprotokoll 17a (1810-1821), no. 29, fols. 60a-61a (Digitalarkivet source 25684, views 74-75). Registered 2 Nov 1809; skifte held 19 Jul 1810. No widow (the index card marks his wife as dead). Heirs: Ole Tallefsen, 21, and Ole Tallefsen, 19 (curator Gunder Poulsen Fjeldt[?]); Torjus, 13 (guardian Ole Olsen Brokke[?]); Halvor, 11 (Christopher Jensen Grimstveit[?]); Jacob, 9 (Tarje[?] Hauk[?]); Anne Tallefsdatter, 14 (Aasmund Tallachsen Wiig). No movables; Søftestad, matr. no. 12, valued at 700 rd; gross estate 842 rd. A debt of 600 rd under the 'Skiftebrev' of 5 Nov 1804 (the division after his wife Kari Torjusdatter) was secured on the farm. Creditors included Asløg Olsdatter Søftestad (possibly his sister; inference), Tallef Aslachsen Midtbø[?], Rasmus Nielsen Fjone and Halvor Torvildsen Bakken. Kari Torjusdatter is not named in the parts read. ¶13

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Confidence raised from Probable to Confirmed: the 16 Nov 1863 Kviteseid marriage entry of his son Torjus Tellefsen (scan read) names the groom's father as Tellef Olafsen and his birthplace as Søftestad, matching this 1801 household. Both he and Kari Torjusdatter were in their first marriage in 1801, with Torjus listed among 'deres børn' (their children). ¶2

The case for this identification 1

Identification (Probable): the 1760 Tallef is taken to be Tellev Olsen of Søftestad because (1) the father's farm, Søftestad, is the farm Tellev Olsen held in the 1801 census; (2) his age of 41 in Feb 1801 fits a birth early in 1760; and (3) he is the only Tellev/Tallef/Tollef son of an Ole found in the Nissedal-church baptisms read from Feb 1759 to Jun 1763 (every entry read). Birth date: changed from 'ABT 1760' (from the 1801 census age) to before 24 Mar 1760, the baptism date, on this identification. ¶4a

Corrections 1

Supersedes the earlier 'His mother is unknown': his Speculative mother is Anne Johannesdatter (see Anne Johannesdatter). ¶9

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Not his confirmation (probably): 'Telles Olsen Tvedt', 19 (born about 1765), was confirmed in Nissedal church on the 18th Sunday after Trinity 1784 (source 7982, confirmations 1784, pp. 248-253). suggested this was Tellev of Søftestad living at Tvedt, which would explain his bride's 1788 byname 'Tvedt'. Since Tellev Olsen Søftestad was already confirmed in 1778 and a person was confirmed once, and the age is five years off his baptism, the 1784 Telles is probably another man of the same name. ¶11

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Would confirm: his confirmation (about 1775-1777) or his marriage to Kari Torjusdatter (before 1789) in the Kviteseid books, or a probate after Ole Søftestad naming a son Tellev. ¶4b
