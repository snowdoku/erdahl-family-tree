About the family

Mother of Marit Tronsdatter (see her page); Probable, graded on her link to Marit, from the bygdebok alone. Life story

Her probate found and read: card 15291 (Hadeland, Land og Valdres skifteprotokoll 1698-1703, S. 360, 23 Sep 1701): 'Jøran Arnesdtr. +, g. Trond Helgesen; 1. Even 2 aar, 2. Arne 12, 3. Marit 19, 4. Astri 16, 5. Beril 8, 6. Ingbiør 6'; gross about 456 rdr, with 1 hud 4½ skind in Aabjør. Protocol (DA 25283 s. 360b-361a, view 364): 'Anno 23 Septemb. blev holden Registrering og Skifte paa Aabiør i Ourdals Præstegield i Walders efter afgangne Joran Arnesdatter, som haver efterladt sig med Sin Igienlevende Mand Trond Helgesen 6 Børn, Nafnlig Even Trondsen 2 aar, Arne Trondsen 12 aar gl., Marit Trondsdatter 19 aar, Astri 16 aar, [Berit] 8 aar gammel, og Ingebiør Trondsdatter 6 aar gl.', with the children's kinsman Ole Arnesen (probably her brother; the kin word not read) present. ¶2

Marriage read: Aurdal ministerialbok DA 9360, canvas 213 (p. 229, left page), under the service of 27 Dec 1701 ('Fer. 3 Nativ.', third day of Christmas; the last service of 1701 before 'Saa endis det gamle Aar' and the title page 'anno 1702'): 'Copulerede: Thron Siffuersen Bergier og Marthe Knudsdatter. Jon Knudsøn og Marthe Throndsdatter Dahl.' The Digitalarkivet index (pv00000000706837, 'Joen Knuds.' x 'Marthe Throensd.') gives the date as 27-11; the scan shows it is 27 Dec. No betrothal for the couple was found in the Aurdal entries of Oct-Dec 1701 (canvases 208-212). Grade kept Probable (graded on the link to Marit Tronsdatter, Marit Tronsdatter). The marriage shrinks the gap from 37 years to 3 months: an unmarried 'Marit Trondsdatter 19 aar', daughter of Trond Helgesen and Jøran Arnesdatter, at Aabjør on 23 Sep 1701, and a bride 'Marthe Throndsdatter' marrying Jon Knudsen, of Dal, in the same parish on 27 Dec 1701. But the marriage entry gives her patronymic only, not her father or her farm, so the identification still rests on name, parish and timing (audit 3: patronymic alone stays Probable). No rival Marit Trondsdatter of marrying age is named in the Aurdal records read so far. ¶6a

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the grade · 1 correction · 3 open questions

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Grade kept at Probable, graded on her link to Marit Tronsdatter (see her page). The probate names 'Marit Trondsdatter 19 aar' (born about 1682) as her daughter by Trond Helgesen, but the tree's Marit is the widow of Joen Knudsen at Dal in 1738: a different farm and 37 years later. The bygdebok's 'g 1701 m Jon Knutsson Dal' and the parallel of her sister Astri (16 in 1701), wife of Joen's brother Christen Knudsen (Rogne card 11107, 'Astri Tronsdtr.'), make the identification strong but inferred. Death 'd 1701, 52 år' (bygdebok) is now 'before 23 Sep 1701'. ¶3

Corrections 1

Correction (farm name): the 1701 probate is held 'paa Aabiør i Ourdals Præstegield' and the card files it under 'Aabjør i Aurdal i Nordre Aurdal' (card 15291), with land 'i Aabiør 1 hud 4½ skind'. This matches the 1664 bailiff's 'Aaberigs' (Helje Knudsen 45, son Thrond Helgesen 12) and the vicar's '[?]raberg'. So the family's farm is Aabjør (Aaberg), not Bø; the bygdebok's gnr 106 heading should be checked (it may be Aabjør itself). Places left unchanged pending that check. ¶5

Open questions and the next record to read 3

Test record: her burial in the Aurdal ministerialbok about 1701 (age 52) and the probate after her (about 1701-02, Valdres probate card index DA 39948, Bø i Nordre Aurdal), which should list her children with Trond Høljesen. ¶1b

Test record: the Aurdal marriage of Jon Knudsen Dal and Marit Trondsdatter, late 1701 (Marit is unmarried at the probate of 23 Sep 1701), in the Aurdal ministerialbok 1692-1730 (DA 9360, IIIF manifest at; 1701 lies roughly at canvases 150-190, not calibrated). A betrothal naming 'Marit Trondsdatter Aabiør' would bridge the farms. ¶4

Test record: a record naming Trond Helgesen (Aabjør) as father-in-law of Jon Knudsen Dal, or Marit as 'Trond Aabjørs datter', e.g. the 1741 Rogne probate continuation (DA 25286 view 295 on) or an Aurdal pantebok division 1708-1712 (DA 10262 views 140-260). ¶6b
