About the family

Wife of Halsten Rasmussen of Loen and mother of Marthe (baptized 1775). Listed as Gunille Andersdatter, 53, in the 1801 census. ¶1

Parents (Probable): the probate after Anders Guttormsen of Hilde, October 1760 (Nordfjord skifteprotokoll A 13, fol. 387-389; card), names among the five children he had with his widow Giertrud (Jartrud) Olsdatter a youngest daughter 'Gunilde Andersdatter', aged 1[2?], so born about 1748, with Guttorm [?]sen Malem as her guardian. That matches this Gunnild's age (53 in the 1801 census, so born about 1748) and her residence at Hilde when she married in 1773. No record yet seen states outright that Halsten's wife is Anders Guttormsen's daughter, so the link is Probable. It makes her a sister of Guttorm Andersen Hilde (see his page), whose son Samuel Guttormsen married her daughter Marthe Halstensdatter: first cousins (pedigree collapse). Confirming record: the probate after Jartrud Olsdatter Hilde (d. 1791), which should name her married daughter. ¶10

Life story

Marriage: 14 Nov 1773, Innvik parish, Halsten Rasmussen, bachelor, residence Rustøen, and Gunnild Andersdatter, maid, residence Hilde (Digitalarkivet indexed marriage pv00000000001199/pv00000000001200; no fathers or forlovere indexed). Their children were baptised as of Loen: Lars 1774, Marthe 1775, Giertrud 1778, Else Cathrina 1780, Randi 1787, Giertru 1791 (worker). ¶9

Aaland 1932: Loen bruk nr. 11 'Trulsbruket' (printed p. 321) lists 'Vs. Anders Andersson Nsd., † 60 a. g. 1794, br. 1774. * Else Trulsd. Loen, † 65 a. g. 1813. S. Truls, † 61 a. g. 1841, sk. 1794.' This Truls Andersen (born about 1780) is very probably the appraiser Truls Andersen Loen of the 1809 probate, and his father Anders Andersen (born about 1734, died 1794) very probably the 'Anders Andersen of Loen' of the 1792-1795 probate index. Anders was only about 14 years older than Gunnild, so neither is her father or brother on these ages; the earlier hypothesis that Truls Andersen was her brother is weakened. This is inference from Aaland's ages, not a statement of his. ¶8

Probate, 1809: the joint probate of 29 May 1809 (Nordfjord skifteprotokoll A 21b, pp. 1033, 1040-1041) treats her (Gunilde Andersdatter) and her husband Halsten Rasmussen Loen as both dead. She therefore died after the 1801 census and before 29 May 1809. Heirs: Lars, Else Catrine, Randi (21), Marthe (wife of Samuel Guttormsen Loen) and Gjertrud (18). ¶2

Probate, 1809: one appraiser was Truls Andersen Loen, who shares her patronymic and may be her brother. That is inferred only; the record does not say so. ¶3

Aaland 1932: Loen bruk nr. 15, printed p. 322, names Hallstein Rasmusson's wife 'Gunh. Andersd., † 84 a. g. 1834'. Aaland gives no farm of origin or parents for her. ¶6

Corrections 1

Correction: Aaland's death in 1834 aged 84 conflicts with the 1809 probate (A 21b, pp. 1033, 1040-1041), which treats her as dead by 29 May 1809. The probate wins. ¶7

Searches that found nothing 1

Parents, search: the probate after Anders Andersen of Loen, Olden skipreide, 1792-1795 (index: Nordfjord protocol '19b', p. 486) was looked for. The only scanned volume for those years is A 19 (catalogued L0019a, 1790-1795); its fol. 486a has a short entry naming an Anders Andersen, probably of Loen, but the binarised scan is too faint to read the heirs. Whether he was Gunnild's father, or Truls Andersen Loen's, is still unknown; this is not a negative result. ¶5
