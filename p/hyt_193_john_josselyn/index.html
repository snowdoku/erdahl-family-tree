About the family

His own father is entered as the Speculative Ralph Josselyn, following French's reconstruction (see there). ¶2

Mother entered: Helenor, widow successively of a Josselyn and of John Nevell of Fyfield; her will of 1547 names 'my 3 sons John Joslyn, Symond & John the younger' (John the elder and Symond executors), and Gilbert Nevell's will of 1550 calls 'John Josselyng the elder of Chykenayle' his brother-in-law. Graded on Helenor. This explains why he married at Fyfield and why his bride was a Nevell: his mother had married into the Fyfield Nevells. ¶4

Life story

Fyfield register (FamilySearch 'England, Births and Christenings, 1538-1975' extraction, index only): 'Helenora Joslyn or Joslin', daughter of 'Johis. Joslyn or Joslin', christened at Fyfield 1 Feb. 1546[/7]; and 'Radulphus Joslyn', son of 'Simonis Joslyn', christened at Fyfield 5 Dec. 1546. This puts John (married at Fyfield 15 Jan. 1544/5) and his brother Simon both at Fyfield having children in 1546, and John's daughter Helenor carries the name of Helenor Nevell, the widow whose 1547 will names John and Simon as her Josselyn sons, which supports that identification. At Roxwell, where John later lived, the index has Simon Josling bapt. 12 Oct. 1561, John Josline 27 Nov. 1563 and Phillip Joseline 21 Aug. 1566 (no parents indexed; film 571184). Simon fits Ralph's brother Simon named in the 1605/7 Rochford probate, but the index gives no father, so this is a lead only. No grade change. ¶3

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 3

Graded on his link to his son Ralph Josselyn. Of Fyfield (taxed there 16 June 1546), Chignal Smealy (taxed 1547-1552) and Roxwell (sessor 1566/7; bought the farm Bollinghatch in Newland, Roxwell, by fine of May 1573). Married at Fyfield 15 Jan. 1544/5 'Alice Nevell Widow' (Fyfield register, printed by Crisp 1896, as extracted in NEHGR 71:32). Buried at Roxwell 18 Feb. 1578/9. The inquisition taken at Stratford Langthorne 9 May 1580 after the death of 'John Josselyn of Roxewell, yeoman' (died 20 Feb. last), holding Searles, Bollinghatch and Thistledowns, finds 'that Thomas Josselyn is his son and next heir and was aged at the time of the death of John thirty years and more'. Thomas Josselyn of Rochford, yeoman, in his will of 5 Mar. 1604/5 leaves £150 to 'my brother Rafe Joslin's children of Roxwell' and names Rafe and Simon Joslin guardians; the PCC sentence of 17 June 1607 calls Ralph and Simon 'brothers of the testator'; the Chancery bill of 30 Apr. 1646 calls Ralph Josselyn of Roxwell the 'brother of Thomas Joslyn'. Ralph also succeeded to Bollinghatch, John's farm. All read as abstracts in French, NEHGR 71:19-33, 227-233. ¶1a

Probable, not Confirmed: no record read names Ralph as John's son; it is inferred from Ralph being the brother of John's son and heir, and holding his land. ¶1b

More evidence for Ralph as his son, grade unchanged: (1) the will of 'Jhon Joslin of Revenale', husbandman, 7 Feb. 1596/7 (Commissary of London, original will; NEHGR 71:24) says 'Jhon Josheline, my father, by his last will gave me £100, to be paid me within four years after my mother's decease out of one messuage called boiling hayche', and names 'my faithful and well beloved brother Thomas Josline of Rochforde'; Bollinghatch was this John's farm (fine 1573, inquisition 1580); (2) the will of John Joslynne of Mountnessing, yeoman, 21 Apr. 1602 (Archdeaconry of Essex, Stephen f. 350; NEHGR 71:24-25) names 'my brother Rafe Joslynne of Roxwell', 'my brother Thomas Josslynn of Rochford' and 'my brother Simon Joslynn', and the administration of 8 June 1602 was granted to 'Thomas Joslynn, uncle on the father's side' of the minor executor. So Ralph was a brother of at least two men, besides the heir Thomas, whom records tie to this John and his farm. Thomas of Rochford (1604/5) also names his sisters Elizabeth Sumner, Jone Lee (m. Roxwell 1576 Robert Lees) and Phillip Gooddy (bapt. Roxwell 21 Aug. 1566 'Phillip Joseline'; Henry Josline of Mountnessing calls John Gooday of Roxwell his uncle in 1611). Still Probable: no record read names Ralph himself as John's son. John's 1596/7 'after my mother's decease' shows that John's widow outlived him, which fits the 'Josselyn vid.' taxed at Roxwell in 1598 and the 'Widow Joslin' buried there 31 Jan. 1600/1. ¶5a

Open questions and the next record to read 2

Test record: the 1580 inquisition (TNA C 142) and the Chignal Smealy register c.1556 for Ralph's baptism; or Thomas of Rochford's original will (PCC 92 Stafford), for any wording that names their father. ¶1c
