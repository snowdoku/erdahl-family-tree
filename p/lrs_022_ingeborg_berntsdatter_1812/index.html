Life story

Marriage: as a spinster (pige), 'Ingeborg Berndtsd. Strand', 51, daughter of Bernt (Berent) Torjesen, married the widower Torjus Tellefsen, 66, of Rønningen, Nissedal, on 16 Nov 1863 in Kviteseid church. Her birthplace reads 'Strømnæs' (uncertain). ¶1

1865 census: as Ingebjørg Bendiksdatter, 55 (b. about 1810, Kviteseid), wife of Torjus at Rønningen in Nissedal. The patronymic and age conflict with the marriage entry; the marriage entry, which names her father, is preferred. Collateral: Torjus's second wife, not an ancestor. ¶2
