About the family

Father of Ane Tostensdatter, graded on that link: Confirmed. His probate, 18 Feb 1761 at Høkset (Nordmøre skifteprotokoll 10c, source 24646, fol. 615b, no. 27), names his widow Guri Olsdatter and their children Johannes Torstensen, Ingebor Torstensdatter (married) and the unmarried 'yngste datter Anne Torstensd[atter]' (age read as about 22, i.e. born about 1739, fitting the bygdebok's 1739). Anne is followed from the probate to the wife of Endre Nielsen Grønset through consecutive Hoksæt records with her brother Johannes in each (1762 extra tax; godmother 1764-65; marriage 1765 from Höckset; 1780 probate with 'Morbroderen Johannes Torstensen'); see her notes. ¶1

Life story

Bygdebok (Gards- og ættesoge for Gjemnes, bind 7, Høgset chapter, rebuilt from nb.no word boxes, canvases 576-577; dates ABT): brukar 5 on Høgset 'Torstein Iverson', farmer from 1731 to 1761, born about 1693, died 1761, married Guri Olsdotter (died 1771, 70 years old); he took over from Ole Toreson Høgset (his wife's half-brother) in 1731. Children include Johannes (b. about 1731, brukar 7, married 1762 Anne Iversdotter of Søvik) and Ane (b. 1739, m. 1765 Endre Nilsson Grønset). His widow Guri Olsdotter held the farm 1761-64 (brukar 6). ¶2

The 1771 probate after his sister, which would test his father Iver Torstensen, is not in the Nordmøre sorenskriveri registers 24648 (1766-72, read in full) or 24649 (1772-76, A-J). The only Gjemnes Iversdatter there, Ingebor Iversdatter of Berge (24648 fol. 747b, 1772), belongs to another family (brothers Mogens, Anders, Iver Iversen). ¶4a

His parents (lead, bygdebok only): the same chapter (canvas 574) has brukar 3 'Iver Torsteinsen', farmer 1693-1709, born about 1660, married a Pedersdotter, with a son 'Torstein f. 1690-åra' ('kanskje' the boy of 11 in the 1701 census) who is cross-referred to brukar 5. The 1701 census (Digitalarkivet 206, pc00000005073094) has 'Ifuer Taarstens.' b. 1661 at Høgset with a son 'Tøris Ifuers.' b. 1700 (pc00000005073097), but no Torsten in that household. Father added as Iver Torstensen Høgset, Probable on that record. ¶3a
