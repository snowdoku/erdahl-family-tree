Life story

Born 21 March 1884 (1901 census; March 1885 in 1911), PEI. At home in 1891 and 1901. In 1911 in Kings County with wife Mary E. and children Perry J., Florence M., Walter L. and Elva May. In 1931, as Charles Tolman Hayter, head at High Bank and Murray River South with wife Mary Adelaide and children including Russell, Lester and Forrest - the family that stayed on the home ground. ¶1

Rural directory, about 1925–28: The Cummins Atlas of Prince Edward Island lists "Hayter, Chas. P.O. High Bank. Wf. Mary Horton; Ch. Percy, Walter, Lester, Russell, Edna, May, Margaret." This gives his wife's maiden name, Mary E. Horton. Two of the sons are buried at Murray River: Lester Charles (1921–1998) and Private Russell E. (1918–1999). The daughter May is probably the Elva May of the 1911 census. ¶2
