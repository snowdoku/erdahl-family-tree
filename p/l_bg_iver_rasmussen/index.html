Life story

Baptised 31 Dec 1786 at Brekke, son of Rasmus Iverssøn of Botnen. He is Iver Rasmussen, 16, in his father's household in the 1801 census. Confirmed 1801. ¶1

1801 census age: The census (Feb 1801) gives Iver as 16, which implies a birth about 1784-85. His baptism on 31 Dec 1786 would make him 14. The baptism is trusted. Census ages in this part of Brekke often run high: Ole Torbjørnsen's children at Ytre Takle are listed about two years older than their baptisms. ¶4

Farmer at Botnen. Married 13 Jun 1813 (as a bachelor) to Marthe Olsdatter of Ytre Takle. Died 1860 (buried mid-May 1860). ¶2

Probate: As eldest son and estate holder (inventator), Iver Rasmussen Botten took all the movables in his father's 1837-38 estate and paid out his brothers and sisters. He was also guardian of his niece Synneve Hansdatter Hauge (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll A 20a, fol. 111a-111b). ¶5
