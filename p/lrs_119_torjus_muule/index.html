Life story

Baptisms of children in Nissedal church: as 'Torjus Muule' he had a daughter Kari baptised on 13 Oct 1759 (godparents included Joen Gundersen, Kittil Tostensen, Ragnild Wiig and Torbor Muule), and a son Wetle[?] on 17 Nov 1762. Kviteseid ministerialbok 1754-1773. The mother is not named. Muule is the farm Mule. ¶1

Relinked as Speculative father of Kari Torjusdatter of Søftestad (see her page),. The 1759 baptism, re-read from the crop, writes him 'Torgius Muule', and the first sponsor of the same entry 'Targe[?] Nie[l]sen', so the clerk kept Torgius and Targie apart; he is therefore not Targie Nielsen of Tvetene, father of the Mule/Tvetene Targiesens. Kari's proved brother Gunder Torjussen was living at Mule as a widower in 1783. See Kari's notes for the evidence against (her ages point to about 1761, two years after this baptism) and the records that would settle it. ¶5

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Speculative father of Kari Torjusdatter of Søftestad (see her page): his daughter Kari of 1759 is the only matching baptism found. Not proved; see Kari's notes for the Tvedt alternative. ¶2

Current grade (from patch 033, audit): he is linked as the Speculative father of Kari Torjusdatter of Søftestad (see her page). The note 'Unlinked as father of Kari Torjusdatter of Søftestad ' is superseded by the note 'Relinked as Speculative father'. ¶7

Alternatives considered and set aside 2

Unlinked as father of Kari Torjusdatter of Søftestad: she married from Tvedt in 1788 and nothing ties her to Mule. Muule's daughter Kari was baptised 13 Oct 1759; Kari of Søftestad's burial age (35) gives about 1768. Kept as a record of the candidate. ¶4

Possible further children (inference, not linked): Gunder Torjussen (born about 1751; Mule 1783, Lien 1793-1804, Søftestad 1801) and Eivind (Evind) Torjusen (born about 1753; Ulvsbrekke 1780, Tvedt 1788, Tvetene 1797, Grimstvedt Nedre 1801), the brother and probable brother of Kari of Søftestad. Their baptisms have not been found. ¶6

Searches that found nothing 1

Not found later: no Torjus lived at Mule in the 1801 census, and no Torjus burial at Mule is indexed for 1800-1815 (Kviteseid burials are indexed only from 1800),. ¶3
