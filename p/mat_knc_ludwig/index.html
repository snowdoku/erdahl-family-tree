Life story

Birth: Born at Płużnica (Pfeilsdorf) at 3.30 p.m. on 21 May 1910 and named Ludwik (Ludwig) in the Polish copy; registered 25 May 1910 as no. 29 by his father the settler Gottlieb Knodel, who signed; mother Karolina Knodel née Schmidt. His 1941 BC marriage record's 'born Poland' means this village, Polish since 1920. ¶5

Baptism (index): the Evangelical parish of Villisaß, Kreis Culm, has a baptism of Ludwig Knodel on 21 May 1910, with the relatives indexed as 'Gottlieb, Karoline'. The civil birth at Pfeilsdorf was on 21 May 1910, the same day as the indexed baptism date, and the relatives' names repeat the parents' given names, so this is very probably this child's baptism. A baptism on the day of birth would be unusual, so the index may have taken the birth date from the entry; the image would show the baptism date. This is an index entry only (Ancestry 'Germany, Lutheran Baptisms, Marriages, and Burials, 1567-1945', dbId 61250); the register image has not been read, so the index does not say whether the two names in its unlabelled 'Relatives' field are the parents or the godparents, and it gives no sponsors. Index recordId 3649558, image id 0245317-00176. ¶10

Emigration: As 'Ludwik Knodel', born about 1911, he landed at Halifax on 28 March 1927 from the White Star liner Doric out of Liverpool, with his sister 'Zofia' (Sophie). ¶7

Emigration: the passenger-list index (Ancestry dbId 1263) has 'Ludwik Knodel', b. abt 1911, and 'Zofia Knodel' on the same page, arriving at Halifax 28 March 1927 on a ship from Liverpool (rids 3003252-3; image CANIMM1913PLIST_2000908389-00163, not seen). The Polish spellings show they travelled on Polish papers. ¶9

1931 census: Ludwig Knodel, 21, single, born Germany, immigrated 1927, general labourer, living with his brother Jacob and sister Frieda at Oliver, BC. ¶6

Voters list 1940, Yale (BC): 'Louis Knodel', with a 'Mrs Ida Knodel' on a nearby page. Ludwig described himself as divorced when he remarried in 1941, so Ida was probably his first wife. That is not proved; the first marriage is not in the BC index under Knodel. ¶8

Known as Louie. Marriage, BC 1941-09-515249: on 6 September 1941 at the United Church, Oliver, Ludwig Knodel, a truck driver aged 31, divorced, Lutheran, of Oliver, born in POLAND, son of Gottlieb Knodel (born Russia) and Carolina Schmit (born Roumania), married Bertreme Schmunk, a fruit packer aged 22, of Oliver, born in Saskatchewan, daughter of Henry Jacob Schmunk and Magdalene Nietz. He was already divorced from an earlier marriage, probably made in Saskatchewan. ¶1

His own 1941 statement that he was born in Poland is the earliest and best evidence. It is the only record that places the family in Poland, and it agrees with Erna's family memory of a town 'then in Poland'. ¶3

Death, BC 1996-09-012114: an orchardist of 10364 343rd Avenue, Oliver; died 1 June 1996, aged 86; born 21 May 1910 in 'Germany'; parents Gottlieb Knodel and Karoline Schmidt, both said to be born in Russia; married to Bertreme Schmunk, who was the informant. He was cremated at Oliver. ¶2
