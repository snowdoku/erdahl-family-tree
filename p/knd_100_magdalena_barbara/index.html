About the family

Mother of Jacob Friedrich Rampacher (see his page). ¶1a

Life story

A first child, Jacob Heinrich 'Kangacher' (= Rampacher), was born at Vaihingen on 29 Apr 1718 to 'Joh Erhard, Magdalena Barbara' (record 4769004). Johann Erhard's first wife Anna Margaretha died 8 Jan 1717 (4400121), so they married between Jan 1717 and mid-1717; that marriage is not indexed at Vaihingen nor elsewhere under Magdalena Barbara with a groom Erhard (1709-24 searched). Lead, not a claim: a Magdalena Barbara Rambacher, daughter of Herr Paulus x Maria Magdalena, was baptised at Esslingen on 1 May 1675 (3314840), but she married at Esslingen on 17 Jun 1695 (903342502), so she would be a widow in 1717 if she is the one. ¶2

Probable remarriage (identification Probable, not a grade change): 'Magdalena Barbara Rambacher', bride, married at Eberdingen (Oberamt Leonberg, about 10 km SE of Vaihingen) on 9 Nov 1723; bride row relatives 'Johann Friedrich' (the groom; record 904729779), groom row 'Johann Friedrich' with no surname indexed, relative 'Magdalena Barbara' (record 4729779); image 1184575-00442. Her husband Johann Erhard Rampacher died at Vaihingen on 19 Jun 1722, so a widow Magdalena Barbara Rampacher remarrying 17 months later fits her exactly; no other Magdalena Barbara Rampacher is indexed in the district (search of Magdalena Barbara rows with keyword Vaihingen, and of Rambacher/Rampacher rows at Eberdingen). The bride row names no father, as usual for a widow. ¶5a

S0281 lead: the Eberdingen marriage of 9 Nov 1723 is on FamilySearch as QPGX-5ZVB ('Deutschland, ausgewählte evangelische Kirchenbücher', Eberdingen 'Taufen, Tote u Heiraten 1556-1925' p.45): groom 'Johann Friedrich', bride 'Magdalena Barbara Rambacher'; no image is linked (the page offers only 'Check Image Availability'). The 'Germany, Marriages' twin names the groom 'Hans Johan Friedrich Eisenkraemer' and the bride 'Magdalena Barbara' (1723, Eberdingen), with no parents. No children of Johann Friedrich Eisenkrämer and Magdalena Barbara are indexed 1724-40. Her maiden name and father are still not found. ¶6a

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

2 on the grade · 1 on the identification · 4 open questions

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Graded on that link: his Vaihingen birth row of 14 Feb 1720 names 'Joh. Erhard, Magdalena Barbara' (record 4769125, image 1184562-00180). Second wife of Johann Erhard Rampacher (his first wife Anna Margaretha, m. 1704). The 1744 groom Jacob Friederich (father 'Erhard') is identified with the 1720 child as the only Jacob Friedrich Rampacher of that age at Vaihingen. Ancestry 61023 index rows do not label roles (BRIEF audit 5 rule 3), so this rests at Probable until the image or a labelled FamilySearch twin is read; images not seen. Surname and marriage not indexed. ¶1b

Re-check, grade held at Probable. No in-gap record of her son Jacob Friedrich (b. 1720) before his 1744 marriage was found; FamilySearch search for Jacob Friedrich Rampacher at Vaihingen 1745-1800 (signed in) shows his children 1745-1759 but no burial of him with age. The 1744 marriage image (Ancestry 1184562-00542) is partner-only on FamilySearch. ¶4a

The case for this identification 1

Labelled evidence, grade held at Probable: QPGV-56RH, Jacob Friderich Rambacher, born Vaihingen 14 Feb 1720, Parents 'Joh. Erhard Rambacher, Magdalena Barbara'; the 1744 groom's labelled father is 'Erhard Rambachers' (QPGK-GFW8). Held because the gap is 24 years and the father died in 1722, so he cannot be shown staying (audit 9 rule 2); the identification is that the 1720 boy is the only Jacob Friedrich son of an Erhard Rampacher. ¶3a
