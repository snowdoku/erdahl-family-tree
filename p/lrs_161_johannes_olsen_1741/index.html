About the family

Parents, confidence Probable: the link of this 1742 baby to the Johannes Olsen Asheim who married in 1767, lived at Asheim in 1801 aged 60 and was buried in 1817 aged 75 1/2 rests on the same farm, the patronymic Olsen, and age agreement within about five months. Supporting: he was the only Johannes son of an Ole baptised at Brekke from about 15 Sep 1740 to May 1742 (all Brekke baptisms); the 1801 census patronymic 'Johannes Olssen' fits Ole Johansen; his eldest son was named Ole (b. about 1769, the Asheim head in 1801), which fits the custom of naming the first son after the paternal grandfather (inference); a daughter Ingerborg (b. about 1778) fits Ingeber but equally Randi's own mother Ingeborg Gullaksdatter, so it is not evidence either way; and Torkil Asseim, his future father-in-law, stood godfather. Against certainty: the Brekke baptisms of Feb - Sep 1740 (11441 c14-c19) are not yet read, so a rival Johannes born then is not excluded, and he might have been baptised elsewhere. ¶11a

Elder brother (probable): 'Barn Johannes Olss. Asseim gl. 8 aar' (the child Johannes Olsen of Asseim, aged 8) was buried at Brekke at the Dom. Reminiscere service, about 26 Feb 1741. He was very probably a son of Ole Johansen and Ingeber (b. about 1732-33), and the name was given again to the next son in 1742; the burial entry names no parent, so this is inference. ¶13

Life story

Other Ole at Asseim: Ole Clausen Asseim also lived on the farm in 1741 (he and Ole Johansen were cautioners at the same betrothal on 25 Jun 1741). He is not a credible father: his wife Brite Joensdatter's probate in July 1751 lists sons Joen, Claus (dead, with children aged 16-19), Iver, Lasse, Mons, Bendix and Thoer and daughters Brite and Ingebor, but no Johannes, and his children were then mostly adults. Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll A 7a, pp. 66a-66b, no. 36. ¶12

Baptism: on Dom. Exaudi, 6 May 1742 (Easter 1742 was 25 Mar), at the service in Brekke church, Gulen: 'Døbt Ole Johansen Asseims B[arn] N[omine] Johannes. Mat[er]: Ingeber' (baptised: Ole Johansen Asseim's child named Johannes; mother Ingeber). Godparents (testes): Johannes i Opdal, Torchil Asseim, Mons Ols[en] ibid., Binthe or Berthe [J...sd.] ibid. (forename uncertain), Siri Torsd. and Anne Niels[d.] ibid. Torchil (Torkild Monsen) Asseim was the father of Randi Torkildsdatter, whom Johannes married in 1767. Gulen ministerialbok 1736-1746, source 11441, canvas 24 right (fol. 25). ¶9

Marriage: Betrothed (trolovet) on Rogation Sunday (Dom. Rogate), 17 May 1767, at the service in Brekke church, Gulen, and married (Cop., copulated) on 5 Jul 1767, per the margin note: 'Trolovet Johans Olsen Asheim og Randj Torchilsd.' The groom is of Asheim; no residence is given for the bride and no parents are named for either. The cautioners (forlovere, men who vouched that there was no impediment) were both of Opdal (Oppedal farm, Gulen); their forenames are uncertain, read either as 'Johans Ivers. Opdal' and 'Lasse... Opdal' or as three men. The margin day '5 July' is confirmed by the preceding betrothal at the same service, which carries the same margin date (Gulen ministerialbok 1749-1776, canvases 124-125). No other betrothal of a Johannes Olsen or a Randi was found Feb 1767 - Feb 1768. ¶7

Godparent card: at the baptism of Niels, child of [forename uncertain] Asheim, on 11 Sep 1767, two months after the wedding, the godparents included a Johannes and 'Randj Asheim'. They are probably this couple, but Asheim had other households (Johannes Clausen), so the identification is only probable. ¶8

Baptism of son: On 7 Oct 1773 'Johannes Olsen Asheims Søn N: Mons' was baptised in Gulen. Johannes Olsen of Asheim was also a godparent at Asheim baptisms on 28 Jan 1773 (Lasse Matthiesen's daughter Marthe), where the other Johannes of the farm, Johannes Clausen, stood with him, and in Advent 1773 (Johannes Asheim's daughter Giertrud). ¶3

1801 census: Johannes Olssen, 60, married for the first time (Gift 1ste gang), living on a farm pension (lever af kaar) at Asheim, household 02, as the father of the head, Ole Johannessen, 32. His wife is Randi Torkieldsdatter, 58. Also in the household: Mons 29, Johannes 27, Torkield 16 and Ingerborg 23, all with the patronymic Johannessen/Johannesdatter but listed as servants. ¶2

Burial (per index): Johannes Olssøn, Gaardemand (farmer), of Assem, died 22 Jun 1817, buried 27 Jun 1817, aged 75 1/2 (Gulen church book 1816-1825, burials 1817, no. 24; index pg00000001043321, card). The age gives birth about Dec 1741, agreeing with his age of 60 in the 1801 census. Assem is the spelling of Asheim/Asseim in this book. No wife or children are named. ¶5

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 3

Confidence: Probable. The baptism names him as father of a Mons, and Mons Johannessen lives in his son's household in 1801; no record yet ties the two Mons entries together explicitly. Confirming records: his burial, a probate naming heirs, or a later record calling Mons his son. Searches so far: no estate under Asseim, Asheim or Assem in the Ytre Sogn probate index 1760-1830; Gulen indexed burial searches failed on a broken query shape and must be redone. ¶4

Confidence raised from Probable to Confirmed: the 7 Oct 1773 Gulen baptism, read from the scan, names him as father of Mons ('Johannes Olsen Asheims Søn N: Mons'), and the 1844 burial of Mons Johannesen of Asheim, aged 70 11/12, ties that baby to the adult Mons (see Mons Johannessen). The 1771 baptisms are now read with no rival Mons. Johannes Clausen Asheim's 1801 probate names no son Mons. His 1817 burial at Assem, aged 75 1/2, agrees with the 1801 census, where he is the retired father of the Asheim head Ole Johannessen. The audit condition of (read 1771 and find a burial) is met. ¶6

Confidence of the parent link kept at Probable: the identification of the adult Johannes Olsen Asheim (m. 1767, d. 1817) with the 1742 baby is now strong, by farm, patronymic, age and the absence of any rival, and the other Ole on the farm, Ole Clausen, is excluded by his wife's 1751 probate. But no record yet read names Ole Johansen or Ingeber as the adult Johannes's parents; the canvases 14-18 were scanned at a reduced 2400 px scale and not every line zoomed; a baptism in a neighbouring parish is not excluded; and the census age of 60 points to a birth in 1740-41, about a year before the 1742 baptism. By the fleet standard (Confirmed needs a record that states the link) it remains Probable, at the strong end. ¶15a

The case for this identification 1

Identity: Johannes Olsen of Asheim (Asseim), Brekke, Gulen parish, Sogn. Called 'Johannes Olsen Asheim' in the Gulen church book in 1773. Birth about 1741 is inferred from his age of 60 in the 1801 census. ¶1

Corrections 1

Correction: birth changed from ABT 1741 to before 6 May 1742 (baptism date; birth place inferred from the father's residence at Asseim). His 1817 burial age of 75 1/2 implies birth about Dec 1741, about five months earlier than the baptism allows; the 1801 census age of 60 (born Feb 1740 - Feb 1741) is also somewhat high. Ages at death in these books are often rounded or overstated, and the baptism is the primary record. ¶10

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Rival window closed (assessed): read every Brekke baptism on 11441 canvases 14-18 (Jun 1739 - Sep 1740) and found no Johannes son of any Ole of Asseim (the child Johannes[?] baptised on 30 Jul 1740 was the son of Johannes Olsen of Opdal, not of an Ole of Asseim). With (about 15 Sep 1740 - May 1742) and (Jul 1741 - Mar 1742), the whole span Jun 1739 - May 1742, which covers both his 1801 census age (60, b. Feb 1740 - Feb 1741) and his 1817 burial age (75 1/2, b. about Dec 1741), contains only one Johannes son of an Ole of Asseim: Ole Johansen and Ingeber's son, baptised 6 May 1742. The condition set by the audit for considering Confirmed has thus been met. ¶14

Open questions and the next record to read 2

Would confirm: a probate after Ole Johansen Asseim or after Ingeber naming a son Johannes, or a clean read of 11441 for Feb - Sep 1740. ¶11b

Would confirm: a probate after Ole Johansen Asseim or Ingeber (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoller after 1742) naming a son Johannes; Johannes's 1767 betrothal or marriage entry if it names his father; or a land record (Asheim) passing from Ole Johansen to Johannes. ¶15b
