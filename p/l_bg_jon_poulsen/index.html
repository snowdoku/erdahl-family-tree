Life story

1801 census, Odland, Skjold: husmann with land, 62, in his first marriage. Wife Inger Larsdatter. Children at home: Orm, 22, sailor, and Ane, 14. ¶1

Burial: Skjold ministerialbok, Tysvær section 1815-1830 (source 1652), burials 1819, p. 131 (index p. 128/131) no. 3, scan canvas 136 read: 'John Poulsen', died 13 Nov, buried 21 Nov 1819, 'Hsm. fra Lammevær(?) under Odland', 78 years (b. c.1741). Same farm (Odland) as the 1801 census household of Jon Poulsen, 62, with wife Inger Larsdatter; Inger was buried from Lamevig under Odland in 1824. Indexed as pg00000006254229 and pg00000006956263. The remarks column has a short note I could not read (possibly the sexton's name). ¶3

Bygdebok: Tysvær bd. 1, Gard og ætt, Tysvær sokn (1990), I. Odland, canvas 31, no. 49 (rebuilt from nb.no word boxes): '49. John Paulsen 1739-1819 [g.m.] Inger Larsdt[r]. 1745-1824. Dei budde som husmannsfolk i Lamme[vik] i 1801 og hadde enkja Eli [Olsdtr.] 70 år hjå seg [...]. Born: a) Paul 1769-91, han var fadder i 1794; b) Orm 1779; c) John 1780; d) Anne 1787, g. 1822 m. [...] stokke, flytte til Avaldsnes i 1824.' No origin or parents are given for John or Inger. (The 1801 census lists Eli Olsdatter, 70, widow, as 'Inderste' in the household, with no relationship stated.) ¶6

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed (upgrade sweep D), as parent of Orm Jonsen. The 1801 census of Skjold (index, relationship words read) lists at Odland household 08 Peder Aslagsen (69, mand), Angona Iversdatter (70, hans kone) and Ragnille Pedersdatter (23, 'Deres datter', unmarried), and household 12 Jon Poulsen (62, mand), Inger Larsdatter (56, hans kone), Orm Jonsen (22, 'Deres børn', sailor) and Ane Jonsdatter (14). On 1 Jan 1804 'Orm Johnssøn', bachelor, of Aadland (Odland) married 'Rognald Pedersdr.', maid, of Aadland, at Tysvær (Skjold marriages 1804, p. 101, index). Their son John was baptised 15 Aug 1804 to 'Orm Johssøn' and 'Ragnille Pedersdr., hustrue', of Adland (Skjold baptisms 1804, p. 105, index). The couple Orm Jonsen and Ragla Peersdatter are the parents named at Lars Ormsen's 1818 baptism at Ytre Eide, with sons John (1804), Peder (1806) and Poul (1809) named after both grandfathers. So the census names Ragnhild and Orm as children of these two Odland couples, and the 1804 marriage and baptism tie those two children, still of Odland, to each other as the couple of the tree. ¶2

Corrections 1

Correction: the two 'Odland estates' in Ryfylke skifteprotokoll D 6 (23890) fol. 362b (1772) and fol. 399 (1773), named in earlier 'next' pointers as the place to look for this family's parents, are 'Odland i Stangeland' (Stangeland skipreide = Karmøy/Skudenes), not Odland in Hetland skipreide (Skjold/Tysvær). The typed farm register of D 6 (view 18) lists them as: 1772 'Odland i Stangeland, ægtefaller Ole Oresen og Anne Siursdt.' (362b); 1773 'Odland i Stangeland, Morten Josephsen' (399). The register has no Odland/Aadland in Hetland 1767-74 (letters A view 4 and O view 18 read). So a greyscale copy of those pages would not help here. ¶7

Searches that found nothing 4

Probate negative: Skjold (Hetland skipreide) probates from 1817 are in KARMSUND sorenskriveri, not Ryfylke. Karmsund skifteprotokoll A5 1817-1825 (source 23916; view n = URN sk20081105680 + n) has a back register by skipreide and year at views 717-727 (Stangeland fol. 710, Skaare 711, Augvaldsnes 712, Hetland 713 = view 720 right + view 721 left, Hesbye 714, Aardal 715, Fitjar(?) 716, Hølle 717, Askøe 718, Hausken 719). The Hetland list for 1819-1825 has no John/Jon Poulsen and no Inger Larsdatter (it does have farms like Aadland, Alvestad, Grønnestad, Førre, Eike, Tysvær). The Ryfylke volume D 18 (23901, 1817-22; URN sk20081104690 + n; first-name register at views 3-9) also has no John Poulsen. So no formal skifte appears to have been held for this husmann couple; their parents will not come from their own probates. ¶4

1762 (negative): the 1762 ekstraskatt for Skjold prestegjeld (source 49646, view 234) lists at 'Odland No. 50' the farmer Lars Helgesen and Malene Thorsdatter (no children over 12), servants Thore Christophersen and Guro Halvorsdatter, Ranveg Olsdatter, and the cottars/lodgers Christopher Torbjørnsen, Brita Rejersdatter, Ole Christophersen, Kari Helgesdatter, Marita Ericsdatter, Borjila Gunnersdatter, Malene Halvorsdatter, Guro Christophersdatter, Guro Larsdatter (old and poor), Toller Ericsen, Ragna Knudsdatter, Jens Tormodsen and Susanna Biørnsdatter. None of Jon Poulsen, Inger Larsdatter, Peder Aslaksen or Angona Iversdatter was living on Odland in 1762; no separate Lammevig or Holen entry appears in the Skjold list (views 216-241, farm column read). They must have come to Odland after 1762, so their parents should be sought on other farms. ¶8

1762 (negative): the name columns (couples, children over 12, servants, others, cottars) of the 1762 ekstraskatt for Skjold (source 49646) were read for views 216-220, 225-226 and 229-237 (Odland itself, view 234). No Jon Poulsen, Inger Larsdatter, Peder Aslaksen or Angona Iversdatter appears. Not yet read: views 221-224 and 227-228. Aslaksen men seen, possible kin of Peder Aslaksen but unproven: Lars Aslaksen with Ingebor Hedersdatter at Haaskuld No. 34 (view 217), and Michel Aslaksen, farmhand, at Eggenæs No. 64 (view 229). A Larsdatter of the right age is not listed as a child of any Lars in the views read, apart from Torbor Larsdatter at Frøvig No. 55 (view 218). ¶9

Negative: (1) The 1762 ekstraskatt for Skjold prestegjeld (source 49646) has now been read in full: views 221-224 and 227-228 (Skjold skipreide end, Skaare skipreide, Vats from view 224), the rest. Jon Poulsen is not in it, so the family came into the parish after 1762. (2) The Ryfylke probate card index 1774-1788 (source 39936) files Odland under Hetland skipreide (Tysvær; views ~236-345). It holds one Aadland card (236a: 1 May 1780, husmann Nils Johnsen g. Elen Olsdatter) and no Lammevik card (L runs Lavøen, Lerevold, Lervik, Lundevold, Løvland, views 298-300), so no probate for this family or their kin on Odland 1774-88. Origin still open. ¶10
