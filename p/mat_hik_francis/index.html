Life story

Death: PEI death card, Francis A. Hicken, born 1857, died 28 February 1893 at Sturgeon. The Sturgeon register records his burial on 2 March [1893] (FamilySearch film 005240351, image 302). ¶3

Uncle of Lydia Adelaide (Hicken) Hayter, called Frank. 1881 census: Francis Hicken, 24, farmer, in his father George's household, Township 61. 1889 voters' list: Francis A. Hicken, Gaspereaux, owner and freeholder of 46 acres in Lot 61. Gravestone at Sturgeon (Find a Grave 75108880, photograph read): 'In memory of Francis A. Hicken, died Feb. 28, 1893, Æt. 36' - the same verse ('One in life and one in death...') and makers (Cairns & McLean) as his brother William J.'s stone of 1891. ¶1

1891 census (Township 61, dwelling 171): Hicken, Francis, 34, single, born P.E.I., father born England, mother born P.E.I., farmer. He lived next door to his brother William, 36, and their father George, 79. ¶2
