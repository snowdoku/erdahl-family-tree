About the family

Wife of Endre Nielsen Grønset and mother of Ingeborg, baptised at Kvernes on Septuagesima Sunday [22 Jan] 1769: 'Døbt Endre Nielsen Grønset og H[ustr]u Ane Tostensd[atte]r B[arn] Ingebor' (Kvernes ministerialbok 16076, p. 135, canvas c70). She is as the mother of Ingeborg Endresdatter on the same evidence, and with the same Probable confidence, as the father link already in the tree: the identification of this baptism with Ingeborg Endresdatter, wife of Elling Povelsen, rests on the name, the 1801 census age and the Stokke godparent, not on a record naming both. ¶1

Kin lead: one godfather at the 1769 baptism was a '[?] Tostensen Hoksæt', perhaps her brother. Possible burial: 'Ane Tostensd.', of Stokke, 'P. Lægdslem' (pauper on the parish rotation; 'P.' may mean Pige, maiden), died 11 Aug 1807 and buried 23 Aug 1807 aged 72 (b. c.1735), Kvernes 1780-1819 p. 393 (index pg00000001231449). Stokke was the farm of Ingeborg's husband's family, but 'P.' may mean she was unmarried, so the identification is unproved. ¶2

Her brother (reading partly uncertain): the guardian for her son Torsten was 'Morbroderen Johannes Torstensen', so she had a brother Johannes Torstensen (farm name read as Hogset or Hoaset, uncertain). 'Morbroderen' is read from the shape of the word; its first letters are not certain. This, with Knud Tostensen of Grønset, points to a Torsten as her father, probably at Hoksæt/Hogset or Grønset; at Ingeborg's 1769 baptism a godmother was the wife of '[?] Tostensen Hoksæt'. ¶8a

Her brother (inferred): she was godmother, as 'pigen Ane Tostensd[atte]r Höcksæt', at the baptism on Cantate Sunday (5 May) 1765 of Gurri, daughter of Johannes Tostensen Höckset and his wife Ane Iversdatter (view 58, p. 111), and 'Pigerne... Ane Tostensdatter Höckset' was godmother in Oct 1764 (view 56, p. 106). Johannes Tostensen's wife Ane Iversdatter was in turn godmother to Ane's daughter Ingebor in 1769, and 'Johannes Torstensen' was guardian of Ane's son Torsten in 1780, apparently called 'Morbroderen'. So Johannes Tostensen of Hoksæt was very probably her brother, and their father was a Torsten at Hoksæt in the 1730s-40s. His full name is not yet found. ¶10

Parents found: her father's probate. Nordmøre skifteprotokoll nr. 10c (source 24646), no. 27, fol. 615b (view 77, left page): 'Anno 1761 den 18de Feb.' the court registered and valued, on the farm Høkset [Hoksæt] in Kvernes prestegjeld, Gimnes tinglag, the estate of 'den Dannemand Torsten Iversen', between 'Enken Guri Olsd[atter]' and their children born in marriage: 'Sønnen Johannes Torstensen' (of age, present), the eldest daughter 'Ingebor Torstensd[atter]', married [husband's name read as Even(?) Pedersen, farm uncertain], and 'den anden og yngste datter Anne Torstensd[atter]', her age read as '22 aar' (reading uncertain; it fits the bygdebok's birth year 1739), unmarried, for whom a guardian (read as Torsten Frederichsen) was named. The probate is indexed in the volume's typed register (view 5) as 'Tosten Iversen Høkset i Gjemnes fol. 615b'. ¶16

Life story

How the 1761 Anne is followed to her: the probate leaves an unmarried Anne Torstensdatter at Hoksæt in Feb 1761, with her brother Johannes Torstensen. In 1762 the extra tax (47939 view 55, no. 52 Hoksæt) has 'Johannes Tostensen og hustru' with the girl 'Ane Tostensd[atte]r' and 'Enke Gurri' (the widow Guri) in his house. In 1764-65 'pigen Ane Tostensdatter Höckset' was godmother twice, once to Johannes Tostensen Hoksæt's daughter Gurri (16076 p. 106, p. 111). On 3 Nov / 29 Dec 1765 'pigen Ane Tostensd[atte]r Höckset' married the widower Endre Nielsen Grønset (16076 pp. 113-114). In the 1780 probate after Endre and 'Anne Torstensdatter' (24650 fol. 624a) the guardian of her son Torsten is 'Johannes Torstensen', called 'Morbroderen'. Each step is the same farm (Hoksæt) within four years, and her brother Johannes runs through all of them. The one named rival, Ane Tostensdatter of Stokke (daughter of Torsten Nielsen Stokke, alive in 1765), is excluded because our Ane married from Hoksæt and her children's guardian was Johannes Torstensen of Hoksæt. The Gjemnes bygdebok (bind 7, Høgset chapter, canvas 577) independently lists among Torstein Iverson's children '3. Ane f. 1739, d. 1780, g. 1765 med enkemann Endre Nilsson Grønset', with her children Torstein 1766, Ingeborg 1769, Nils 1772, Mali 1774, Endre 1778. ¶17

Her own grade (link to her husband Endre Nielsen and child) is unchanged. The parents' links to her are graded Confirmed on their own records. ¶18

1762 extra tax: Ekstraskatt 1762, Kvernes sogn, Gjemnes tinglag (source 47939), view 55, farm no. 52 Hoksæt ('Hocksæt'): the second household is 'Johannes Tostensen og hustru', with two farm lads and, in the column for girls over 12, 'Ane Tostensd[atte]r' and 'Marta Andersd[atter]'; in the column for others living in the house 'Enke Gurri' (the widow Gurri). So in 1762 an unmarried Ane Tostensdatter lived at Hoksæt in Johannes Tostensen's household, three years before our Ane married from Hoksæt (1765). This strengthens the inference that Johannes was her brother. The widow Gurri in the same house is a candidate for their mother (Johannes named his 1765 daughter Gurri), but no record yet names her relationship, so she is not added as a parent. The first household at Hoksæt is Elling Botolfsen with children Botolf, Ole and Marit, already shown not to be related to Paul Ellingsen. ¶12

Marriage found: Kvernes 16076 view 59, p. 113, betrothal 3 Nov 1765 'Enkemanden Andre Nielsen Grønset und pigen Ane Tostensd[atte]r Höcksæt', and view 60, p. 114, marriage on the Sunday after Christmas, 29 Dec 1765, 'Enkem[an]d Andre Nielsen Grønset og pigen Ane Tostensd[atte]r Höckset'. Graded on her link to her husband Endre Nielsen: already Confirmed; this is the marriage record itself. It gives her home farm as Hoksæt (Höckset), which resolves the earlier Stokke lead: a different girl, Ane Tostensdatter of Stokke (daughter of Torsten Nielsen of Stokke, see below), appears in the same book in 1765, so the two must not be merged. ¶9

Hand-over: the Kvernes people now go to the Nordmøre-probate worker. Nordmøre skifteprotokoll nr. 13 (1766-1772, source 24648) has a 20th-century alphabetical register (first name, farm, parish, folio) starting at view 3; nr. 14 (1772-1776) is 24649 and nr. 11 (1762-63) is 24647. A probate after Endre Nielsen or Ane Tostensdatter of Grønset would name Ingeborg. ¶3

Probate found: the joint probate after her and her husband Endre Nielsen, Grønset, opened 17 May 1780 (Nordmøre skifteprotokoll nr 15, source 24650, fol. 624a) names her as 'Konen Anne Torstensdatter', both spouses then dead, and lists her four children by Endre as her heirs: Torsten (13), Ingeborg (11½), Endre (7) and Mali (6). ¶6

Grønset context: Knud Tostensen (Torstensen) Grønset, a widower, was betrothed on 25 Jan 1793 to Ane Pedersdatter, raised at Hastad (16080 c120, p.239). His sureties were Anders Andresen [Endresen] Grønset and Johannes Olsen Grønset. His first wife Kirsti Paulsdatter's probate was held at Grønset in Gjemnes tinglag in Dec 1791 (source 24653 fol. 432a). Anders Endresen standing surety fits Knud being his mother's brother, but no relationship is stated. ¶5

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed, graded on her link to Ingeborg Endresdatter. She is named as mother at the 22 Jan 1769 baptism of Ingebor (16076 p.135) and as mother of 'Ingeborg Endresdatter gammel 11½ aar' in the 1780 probate at Grønset (24650 fol. 624a). Ingebor Endresdatter of Grønset was betrothed to Elling Povelsen of Stokke on 6 Aug 1796 (16080 c138 p.275), with her half-brother Anders Endresen Grønset (named in the same probate) as surety, and the couple were at Guldset with Ingeborg aged 32 in 1801, as the betrothal entry foresaw ('skal faae 1 Part i Gaarden Gulset'). Same farm at each step, no rival Ingeborg Endresdatter at Grønset. Full chain in the notes on Endre Nielsen. ¶7

Alternatives considered and set aside 2

Rival to keep apart: Torsten Nielsen of Stokke, with wife Ane Knudsdatter, was godfather in Dec 1764 (view 56, p. 107); his widow 'Ane Knudsdatter' was churched in 1766 (view 60, p. 115) after a posthumous child was baptised in Øre. His children Arne Tostensen Stokke (wife Marith Amundsdatter, 1765), Maren Tostensdatter Stokke (betrothed 1764 to Peder Tørgersen Strøm) and the girl Ane Tostensdatter Stokke (godmother 1765, view 58, p. 111) were at Stokke. He is NOT our Ane's father on present evidence, since our Ane was of Hoksæt at her marriage; his probate (about 1765-66, likely in 24648) would show whether he had a daughter Ane who married Endre. ¶11

Caution: the same 1762 list (view 56) has a second unmarried 'Ane Tostensd[atte]r' at no. 53 Grønset, in the household of Auden Andersen, next to Endre Nielsen's household. She may be the Stokke Ane Tostensdatter (daughter of Torsten Nielsen Stokke) or our Ane working on her future husband's farm; the list alone cannot decide which, so the Hoksæt identification above rests on the 1764-65 entries that call our Ane 'Höckset'. ¶13

Searches that found nothing 2

Probate searches for her father, negative: no Torsten at Hoksæt in the registers of Nordmøre skifteprotokoller 24644 (1750-55, T pages: Torsten Andersen Wiig, Torsten Nielsen Sætter i Wiig only), 24647 (1762-64, T pages; Tosten Olsen Sandnes i Gjemnes, p. 748, read: widow Marith Nielsdatter, only heir the son Ole Tostensen, not ours) or 24653 (1787-92). ¶14

Probate searches for her father Torsten (Hoksæt), negative: the Nordmøre probate card index 1668-1746 (source 77795) has no cards for Hoksæt/Høgset in Gjemnes (the Høgset cards, views 1909-1913, are Valsøyfjord; no Hogset between Hofset v1733 and Hol v1735); the back register of skifteprotokoll 24643 (1746-50, views 359-366) has no Torsten/Tosten at Hoksæt (T entries: Torsten Erichsen Schelvogen, Torsten Erichsen Strøm, Tøre Danielsen Omsen i Gjemnes only) and no Gurri there. ¶15

Open questions and the next record to read 2

Lead for her family (scan read): a Knud Tostensen lived at Grønset in 1782-88 (godfather 1782, 16077 c12-c14; married to Kirsti Povelsdatter of Stokke, daughter Ane baptised 26 Apr 1788, 16077 c44 p.82). He may be her brother (same patronymic and farm as her husband Endre Nielsen Grønset); a Tosten Knudsen at Grønset/Ruxset area should be checked as her father. Not proved. ¶4
