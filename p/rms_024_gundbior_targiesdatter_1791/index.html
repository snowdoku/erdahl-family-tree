About the family

She was named after her father's mother, Gundbjør Targiesdatter. Her godparents include Torbør Osmundsdatter of Hiemdal, probably her father's sister (inference); none of them comes from Frontved, so they do not identify Birgit's parents. ¶3

Life story

Baptized on the 3rd Sunday in Advent (11 Dec 1791) in Treungen church by the curate Storm: 'Døbt: Targie Osmundsen Hiemdals D. Gundbiør. Test: Gunild Home, Birgit Hiemdal, Torbør Osmundsd. ibid., Knud Evindsen Hiemdal, Jon Halvorsen Sønderlane.' (Kviteseid ministerialbok 1787-1799, p.52, image 54; the page is headed 'Børne Daab 1792' because the church year 1792 began in Advent 1791). The entry does not name the mother; she is placed as Birgit's daughter because her parents married on 14 Aug 1791 and the 1801 census lists her as Targie's child from his first marriage. ¶1

In the 1801 census at Hiemdal she is Gundbiør Targiesdatter, 9, one of 'hans børn' (his children) in the household of Targie Aasmundsen and his second wife Turi Halvorsdatter. ¶2
