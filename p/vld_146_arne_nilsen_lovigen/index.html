About the family

Father at Løvigen, Sunnylven (index records read): of 'Jon' (the name marked uncertain in the index), baptised 7 Feb 1751, godparents Rasmus Liøen, Ole Hellesylt, Guro Liøen (pd00000009561819); Anna, 10 Mar 1754 (pd00000009562167); Arne, 11 Sep 1756 (pd00000009562498); and children baptised 30 Aug 1761 and 13 May 1764. His wife was churched on 7 Feb 1751, 9 Mar 1754, 11 Sep 1756, 4 Jun 1759 and 30 Aug 1761, but the index gives only 'Arne, Løvigen' as husband, without her name. Other probable children: Ingeborg Arnesdtr. of Løvig, confirmed 1763 aged 16 (b. about 1747), Niels Arnesen of Løvig (godparent 1776-80), Anne Arnesdtr. of Løvig (godparent 1778), Peder Arnesen of Løvigen (confirmed 1782, b. about 1766) and Arne Arnesen of Løvigen (father 1784). ¶1

Father of Jon Arnesen (see his page), Probable; graded on his link to Jon. Jon, son of Arne of Løvigen, was baptised at Sunnylven on 7 Feb 1751; 'Joen Arnesen' of Løvigen married Gertru Knudsdtr. of Rindalen at Sunnylven on 7 Apr 1776 (pv00000002170650), and Niels Arnesen (of Løvig in 1776-80; of Tryggestad in Nov 1777) was godfather to their son Knud in 1777. Same farm, 25 years apart, and the only Jon son of an Arne in the Sunnylven baptism index 1740-60. Jon of Melset's age (b. about 1751) fits. ¶3a

Father (scan read): the probate after Niels Ellingsen of Berge, Dale skipreide, 10 Oct 1738 (3A 12 fol. 528b, view 24553/263) names his son 'Arne Nielsen', about 18, guardian Arne Weiberg[?]; siblings Elling Nielsen (of age), Giøre and Ragnilde Nielsdatter; the widow was Brite Olsdatter (not stated to be the children's mother). This is the Arne Nielsen of Berge who married Zidsele Ingebrigtsd. in 1745: same farm, seven years later, and he was then about 25. Niels Ellingsen is entered as his father (see his page), Probable. ¶8

Life story

1745 marriage read on the scan: Norddal, 'Dom: 1 Octobris... Copulerent 1. Arne Nielsen Berge og Zidsele Ingebrigtsd. Frøse' (Norddal ministerialbok 1736-1760, source 15967, canvas 48, left page; image). No widower note. The same book (canvas 47) has, on 7 p. Trinit. 1745 in Strand church, 'Døbt Arne Nielsen Berge sit Barn, Ingeborg', and on 8 p. Trinit. the churching of 'Arne Nielsen Berges' wife. So Ingeborg was born after the betrothal of 23 May and before the wedding of 1 Oct 1745; that fits one wife, Zidsele (Sidsel) Ingebrigtsdatter, rather than two Arne Nielsens or two wives. Arne of Berge was a godparent in Norddal on 6 Jan 1749 (pd00000009564881); Arne of Løvigen first appears in Feb 1751. ¶5

1751 churching read on the scan: Sunnylven, Septuagesima 1751: 'introducerent Ole Langelands, Jochum Midgaardens, Arne Løvigens og Rasmus Liøens qvinder', followed by the baptism of 'Arne Løvigens Barn... Jon', godparents Rasmus Liøen, Ole Hellesylt, Guro Liøen (source 17005, canvas 45, left page). The wife is not named. So Jon's mother is still unproved; if Arne of Løvigen is the Arne Nielsen of Berge (Ingeborg and Siri of Løvig fit Berge's daughters of 1745 and 1746, and the patronymic Nilsen fits), she was probably Sidsel Ingebrigtsdatter. Not added as a parent: the Berge-Løvigen identity rests on names alone. ¶6

Patronymic: an 'Arne Nilsen' of Løvig did public penance at Sunnylven on 28 Jun 1761 (ps00000000006900). He is the only adult Arne known at Løvigen then (the son Arne was 5), so the father is taken to be Arne Nilsen; a son named Niels fits. This rests on one record. ¶2

1762 extra tax (scan read): Løvigen, Sunnylven: 'Arne og Zitzele', child over 12 'Siri' (source 49637, view 239, p. 37). This joins Arne of Løvigen to Arne Nielsen of Berge, who married Zidsele Ingebrigtsd. in 1745 and had a daughter Siri baptised at Norddal on 9 Oct 1746. It strengthens the patronymic Nilsen (now from two records) and names his wife. His own grade (as Jon's father) is unchanged: Jon, aged 11 in 1762, is under the age at which children were listed. ¶7

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 alternative set aside · 2 open questions

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Not Confirmed: no record names Jon of Melset as Arne's son, and the identification crosses from Løvigen to Rindal to Melset. Probate for Arne of Løvigen not found in the Sunnmøre probate index (Løvik rows there belong to other districts). ¶3b

Open questions and the next record to read 2

Lead, earlier home (index records read; not used for any link): an Arne Nielsen of Berge (Stranda annex of Norddal) was father of Ingeborg, baptised at Strand on 1 Aug 1745 (legitimate; wife churched 8 Aug; pd00000009564191), and of Siri, baptised at Norddal on 9 Oct 1746 (pd00000009564508). An Arne Nielsen of Berge also married 'Zidzele Ingebregtsdtr.' of Frøse at Norddal on 1 Oct 1745 (engaged 23 May; pv00000002058348), which, given the churching in August, suggests either a second marriage or two men of that name. Ingeborg Arnesdtr. of Løvig (confirmed 1763) and a Siri of Løvigen (godparent 1773) fit these two daughters, so Arne of Løvigen may be this Arne Nielsen of Berge, and Jon's mother may be Sidsel Ingebrigtsdatter. Not added: the two-wife question is open. ¶4a
