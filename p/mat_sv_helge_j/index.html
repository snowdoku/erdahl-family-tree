Life story

Confirmation, probable: the parish clerk's book gives the farms of the confirmands of 2 Apr 1769 - no. 40 'Helge Joensd. Aftreiten' (the other Helge Jonsdatter that day, no. 35, was of Uglum). Her husband was 'Siur Jons. Aftreit' at the baptism of their son Jon in 1781, so no. 40 is the strong candidate, which makes her a daughter of a Jon of Aftreit. ¶11

Marriage: betrothed in Selbu on Easter Monday, 20 April 1778, as 'Helge Jonsd: Aftr:' to Sivert (Siur) Jonsen of Eidem; sponsors Ole Pedersen Flønes and Einer Povelsen of Flønes. Married on Exaudi Sunday, 31 May 1778: 'Copul. Siur Jons. Eidem J. Helge Jonsdatter.' Read from the scans (source 16311, canvas 88 left page and canvas 89 right page). ¶6

Farm of origin: 'Aftr:' in the 1778 betrothal is the farm Aftreit (Aftret) in Selbu - the same hand writes the name out in full ('Aftreit') in 1776 (expansion inferred from that spelling). She was of Aftreit when betrothed. The family was living at Aftreit in 1784, when her son Jon was baptised on 16 May as son of 'Siur Jons. Aftreit Helge Jonsd.' (source 16311, canvas 114), with a godparent 'Th. Aftr.' of the same farm; by the 1801 census they were at Eidem, her husband's farm. Aftreit is therefore the place to look for her father Jon. ¶7

1801 census: person 002 at rural residence 0034 Eidum, Selbu - 'Haelge Jonsdatter', aged 48, 'Hans kone', both in their first marriage, wife of Sivert Jonssen the farmer of Eidem. ¶1

Her name descends in the usual Selbu way: her granddaughter HELGE JOHNSDATTER, b. about 1812 and aged 13 at the 1825 census, is named for her. That the custom is being followed is itself corroboration of the link between the 1801 and 1825 households. ¶2

Probate (husband's): she is named as the widow and heir, 'Helge Johnsdatter', in the skifte after her husband Sivert Johnsen Eidem, closed 5 April 1815 (skifteprotokoll 10b, fol. 705b). ¶9

Death and burial: the Selbu register for 1816-1831 (women's burials 1820, page 213, no. 11) enters 'Helge Jonsdatt[er], Eidem', status 'g:mds Enke' (gaardmand's widow, a farmer's widow), aged 67, died 21 May and buried 28 May 1820. The online index leaves her residence and status blank. Both were read from the scanned page. ¶4

Probate: the skifte after 'Helge Johnsdatter', farmer's widow of Eidem, Selbu, no. 720, was closed on 22 September 1820 (Strinda og Selbu district court, skifteprotokoll 10b, fol. 765b-766); the estate was registered on 8 June 1820, after her death on 21 May 1820, and the skifte meeting held on 8 July 1820 (proceedings protocol fol. 136). Heirs: John Sivertsen the elder (of age), John the younger (of age), Ole (of age or under age, reading uncertain), Mali Sivertsdatter (married, husband not named) and Ingeborg (age partly illegible, '2[?]'), whose guardian was her brother John Sivertsen the younger. These are the same five children as in her husband's skifte of 1815, with Marit there written Mali here. ¶10

The case for this identification 1

Identification of the 1820 burial: her name and patronymic match; her age of 67 gives a birth year of about 1753, exactly that of the 1801 census (48); the farm is Eidem; and 'farmer's widow' fits the wife of Sivert Jonssen, the gaardmand of Eidem who was buried in 1814. Rejected: Helge Johnsdatter of Langlie, buried 1814 aged 56 (wrong farm and age, and her husband was then still alive); Helge Olsdatter (Voldsaeth, 1814), Helge Hansdatter (1816) and Helge Torkildsdatter (1831), because of their patronymics. ¶5

Searches that found nothing 1

Baptism not found - no register survives: Selbu has no surviving baptism register for 1747-1756. Book 16310 ends in 1737, book 16315 covers only 1740-1746, and book 16311 begins on 1 January 1757 (who also read every baptism from 1 Jan 1757 to 25 Mar 1758 without finding her). Born about 1752-1753 by her ages in 1801 (48) and at burial in 1820 (67), she falls in that gap, so her own baptism, and with it the names of her parents, is lost. What remains: the confirmation lists in book 16311 (about 1764-1775, now being read - they usually give the father or the farm), and probate (skifte) - the Strinda og Selbu probate protocols cover Selbu only from 1797, so a skifte after her husband's death in 1814 or hers in 1820 could name heirs but not the parents. ¶8
