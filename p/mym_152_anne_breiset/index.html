About the family

Wife of Peder at Breiset, Frosta, in the 1762 extra tax (source 48401, view 181, row 40: 'Peder, Anne'; children over 12 'Aren, Guri, Kiersti'). Speculative mother of Gurru Pedersdatter (see her page), graded on that link. ¶1

How sure are we?

Speculative. A plausible candidate only. Treat this link as a question, not a fact.

1 on the grade

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Why only Speculative: the household in 1762 was probably a blended one. The child 'Aren' is probably Arent Larsen (b. c.1732-36, later farmer at Breiset), not a son of Peder, and Peder of Breiset was still having children in 1758 and 1763. So Anne may be Gurru's stepmother rather than her mother. An 'Ane' of Breiset was godmother at Laugtu at Sexagesima 1759 (pd00000006473523). Her patronymic and her burial are not found. ¶2
