About the family

Father of Anne Pedersdatter of Slettum (see her page), Probable; graded on his link to Anne. Source: the Lesja bygdebok only (vol. 1, nb.no item 8e238b3c93fae7ff68ee91598c918b4d, canvases 181 and 183, chapter Einbu, word-box rebuild): 'Peder Hanss. (4-5), ca. 1685-1745, g.m. Marit ca. 1683-?. Dei kjøpte Nørdre Einbu 1718 eller 1720. Barn: Kari, ca. 1717... Anne, ca. 1718, sjå Nørdre Slettom (1-7)...', and in the farm history 'Dottera Anne som [var] gift [på] Slettom'. Entry 1-7 of Slettom is Rolf (Rolv Knudsen), 1720-83, 'g. 1745 m. Anne Pedersd...., 1718-59', the woman whose 1759 probate names Kari Rolvsdatter as her daughter. Her birth about 1718 also fits. Life story

He is also, by the same bygdebok, the father of Peder (b. about 1727, d. 1777), the probable father of Anne Pedersdatter b. 1757 (see her page), so he is a great-grandfather through that line too (see Peder Pedersen Eneboe). ¶2

Origin found. He came from Stor-Tande in Lesja main parish. The Lesja bygdebok vol. 3 (nb.no b5a76aea1b65fda9b28e08b3852f9e55, canvas 99, Stor-Tande entry 4-5) lists among the children of Hans (ca. 1645 - ca. 1698) and Kari '3. Peder, ca. 1685, sjå Nørdre Einbu (2-2)'; that is the '(4-5)' in vol. 1 canvas 181. The skifter at Stortande after Hans Jacobsen (26 Sep 1698, fol. 44a, 'Karri Sifuersd: og deris 6 umyndige Børn... Peder Hanss:', read) and after Kari Sifersdatter (23 Jun 1710, fol. 94b, Peder 'myndig') name a son Peder. Parents entered as Probable (identification across farms rests on the bygdebok, age and naming). His own grade (Confirmed, on his link to Anne) is unchanged. ¶4

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: Confirmed, graded on his link to his daughter Anne Pedersdatter of Slettum (see her page). The probate after him is found in the card index 39945, filed under the modern farm name 'Einbu i Lesje' (card 2437, not under 'Eneboe'): skifte 8 Feb 1745, skifteprotokoll 1740-1751 fol. 701b, 'Peder Hansøn' (dead), married to Marit Biertesdatter; children 1. Hans 25, 2. Bierte 20, 3. Peder 18, 4. Kari 28, 5. 'Anne g.m. Rolf Knudsøn Slethum'. The card names Anne as his child and as the wife of Rolf Knudsen of Slettum, the same couple as in the tree: Rolf Knudsen of Nordre Slettum and Anne Pedersdatter, whose own skifte of 22 May 1759 (card 12275) is the record that puts Anne in the tree, 14 years later on the same farm, and no other Anne married to a Rolf Knudsen Slettum is known. The 1758 skifte after the son Bierte Pedersen (protocol 25158 fol. 380a, read) repeats the family: mother Marit Biertesdatter, brothers Hans Pedersen Eneboe (dead) and Peder Pedersen Eneboe, sisters Kari Pedersdatter married to Erland Enstad and 'Anne Pedersdatter gift med Rolf Slettum', and refers back to the skifte of 8 Feb 1745. Ages give Hans b. about 1720, Bierte about 1725, Peder about 1727, Kari about 1717, matching the bygdebok. His own parents are not named in either record (the bygdebok's 'Peder Hanss. (4-5)' points to another farm entry for his origin). ¶3

Test record: the skifte after Peder Hansen of Nordre Eneboe, about 1745 (the bygdebok mentions it, net 70 daler), in Nord-Gudbrandsdal skifteprotokoll 1740-1751; it was not found among the Eneboe cards checked (source 39945, images 2623-2679, headers only), so read the cards' farm lines in full or look under 'Einbu'/'Enebø', or in the Lesja Jernverk protocol. ¶1b
