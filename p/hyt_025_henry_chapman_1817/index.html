Life story

Death: The PEI death-card index gives Henry Chapman, born 1817, died 1899. The compiled history gives the day as 4 February 1899, at Murray Harbour North. ¶6

Baptism (Probable identification): Henry, son of Henry Chapman, labourer, and Elizabeth, of Minster, was baptised at Minster, Cornwall, on 5 October 1817 (Exeter bishop's transcripts, Cornwall OPC index). This fits his death-card birth year 1817 and his age 74 in May 1891. See Henry Chapman for why this Minster family is taken to be the Murray Harbour one. Parents: Henry Chapman and Elizabeth Herring, married at Minster 19 Oct 1812. ¶7

1861 census: Henry Chapman, Lot 63, was a farmer with a household of 2 (himself and a wife, both aged 21-45), Bible Christian. He held 30 acres on a 999-year lease from Sir Samuel Cunard, with 8 years expired. His brother John's farm is the entry above his. ¶2

1881 census: The HGIS transcription of Township 63 gives this household as 'Thomas Chapman', 60, born England, Bible Christian, farmer, with 'Hellin', 50, born PEI, and Walter, 39. The 1891 image shows the same couple and Walter Miller, so 'Thomas' is a misreading of Henry. ¶3

1891 census: Henry Chapman, 74, married, born England (both parents England), Methodist, farmer, with Helen, 60, born PEI, and Walter Miller, 45, domestic, sailor, Lot 63, enumerated 14 May 1891. ¶4

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the identification

The case for this identification 1

Identity: Henry Chapman, farmer of Lot 63 (Murray Harbour), born in England about 1817 and died in 1899, the younger of the two Henry Chapmans at Murray Harbour. In 1855 The Islander called the elder man 'Henry Chapman, Sen.'. Probable as the son of Henry Chapman Sen. and brother of John Chapman (about 1816-1903): the 'Sen.' title and the compiled family history, which makes John and Henry brothers, support this, but no record names his father. ¶1
