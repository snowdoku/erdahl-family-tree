Life story

Origin (compiled, NEHGR 71:250-253, Elizabeth French 1917): identified as Thomas, son of Ralph Josselyn of Roxwell, Essex, yeoman, whose will of 1626 left him only £5; of Barham, Suffolk, just before emigrating (daughter Mary bapt. there 16 Mar. 1633/4). His baptism is not recorded. Married about 1615 Rebecca, who survived him. ¶2

Origin, strengthened: (1) a trust deed of September 1613 between 'Ralph Josselin Senior of Roxwell, Essex, and John Jude of Radwinter, Essex, as principals', with 'Thomas Josselin, one of the sons of me the aforesaid Ralph Josselin the elder, and Rebecca Jude' as contingent future beneficiaries (quoted in the Great Migration Newsletter's review of R. D. Joslyn, NEHGR 158 (2004):320-340); (2) the will of Symon Joceline of East Hanningfield, 1649 (abstract, NEHGR 71:30), recites a deed dividing his father's farm Bollinghatch made between 'Ralph Joceline of Roxwell, yeoman, Thomas Joceline of Barham, co. Suffolk, yeoman, and Rebecca his wife, and me, the said Symon'; (3) Barham register: '1633 Mary daughter of Thomas Josline and Rebecca his wife baptized 16 March [1633/4]' (NEHGR 71:227); (4) the Increase list of April 1635 has Thomas 43, Rebecca his wife 43, and among his children Mary aged 1. So a Thomas-and-Rebecca pair runs from the 1613 settlement on Ralph's son through Barham to the ship. This is the father link, graded on Ralph Josselyn. ¶5

Will read on the image: the record-book copy of Thomas Joslin's will in Middlesex Probate Records vol. 1, pp. 74-75 (FamilySearch 'Middlesex. Probate Records 1654-1661, 1657-1667, 1667-1672', image 223 of 586). Read on the page: 'after the death of my wife Rebeccah Joslin', 'my sonne Abram Joslin twenty shillings', 'my daughter Rebecca Nicholls twenty shillings', 'my sonne Nathaniel Joslin twenty acc[re]s of intervale land', 'my daughter Mary Soommer [Sumner] my house lott', 'I do likewise give unto my daughter Elizabeth Emons ten pounds', 'my sonne Abram Joslins sonne, my Grand child called also Abram Joslin ye younger', and 'my sonne Nathaniel Joslin, & my sonne in law Roger Sumner' as joint executors after the wife. Signed 'Thomas Joslin & a seale'; witnesses Mordecai Macelwad (his mark), James Butler (his mark) and Jacob Farrer. They swore to it 'the 29th of the 1st m[onth] 1661' before Simon Willard; 'exhibited at a Coun[ty] Court held at Cambridge Aprill 2. 1661' and 'Entred & Recorded Apr. 2. 1661' by Thomas Danforth, Recorder. The inventory follows on the same page (taken by Wm Kerly and Jacob Farrer, 25th of the 11th month 1660). This confirms on the original record the wording took from Holman. Grade unchanged (Probable, graded on his link to Elizabeth Joslin): the record names 'my daughter Elizabeth Emons' but no husband, so identifying her with the Elizabeth Joslin who married Edward Yeomans at Boston in 1652 still rests on the Emons/Yeomans spelling match across Lancaster and Boston (audit 4). The file papers of docket 12967 (FamilySearch film 7553487, 'Probate papers 12934-13004', not browsed to the folder) are the remaining test for a paper naming Edward Yeomans or Elizabeth Kilby. Crops: and...-p75-thomas-joslin-will-probate-2apr1661.png. ¶6

Probate docket: the Middlesex County probate docket index lists 'No. 12967 Thomas Josline of Lancaster 1661 Apr. 2', with record of will and record of inventory in the early record books (the transcript gives the volume and pages ambiguously, about pp. 73-75) (FamilySearch, 'Middlesex. Probate Records 1648-1871', image 415, ark 3:1:3QS7-99DL-FZWZ; transcript read). The will itself was not reached in this session. ¶3a

Will read in transcript: Holman (1948) quotes the will verbatim from Middlesex Probate 12967: 'This is the last will & Testament of Thomas Joslin of Lanchaster, made the 9th day of May in the yeare [1660]'... 'unto my wife Rebecca Joslin, all my goods, moveable & all my estate in lands & housing' [for life]... 'I do likewise give unto my daughter Elizabeth Emons ten pounds'... 'my sonne Abram Joslins sonne, my gradchild called also Abram Joslin ye younger twenty ac[res]'... 'my daughter Rebecca Nicholls twenty shillings'... 'my sonne Nathaniel Joslin twenty accrs of intervale land'... 'my daughter Mary Sumner my house lott, be it upland or intervale'... 'my sonne Nathaniel Joslin, & my sonne in law Roger Sumner all my meadowes'. Filed in court 2 Apr. 1661, the widow Rebecca Joslin having sworn to it 29 Mar. 1661. So the will itself says 'my daughter Elizabeth Emons'; the NEHGR 55:303 reading 'Elizabeth wife of Edward Yeamans' is the 1901 editor's gloss, not the will's words. Grade unchanged (Probable, graded on his link to Elizabeth Joslin): the will names a daughter Elizabeth Emons, but tying her to the Elizabeth Joslin who married Edward Yeomans at Boston in 1652 and John Kilby in 1662 rests on the Emons/Yeomans spelling match across Lancaster and Boston (audit 4: identifying the child across places stays Probable). No rival Elizabeth Joslin is known in New England then; the passenger list has only one daughter Elizabeth. ¶4a

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 3

Graded on his link to his daughter Elizabeth Joslin. The passenger list of the Increase of London (April 1635), as printed in NEHGR 71:253 from Hotten and Drake, has 'Tho: Jostlin, husbandman, aged 43, Rebecca his wife, aged 43' and, as 'children of the said Tho: Jostlin', Rebecca 18, Dorothy 11, Nathaniell 8, 'Eliza' 6 and Mary 1. He settled at Hingham, sold there in 1652/3 and moved to Lancaster, where he signed the civil compact 12 Nov. 1654 and died 3 Jan. 1660/1. His will, dated 9 May 1660 and proved 2 Apr. 1661 (Middlesex probate), as abstracted in NEHGR 71:253, names wife Rebecca executrix, sons Abraham and Nathaniel, grandson Abraham, and daughters Rebecca Nichols, wife of Thomas, Mary Sumner, wife of Roger, and Elizabeth 'Emons'; the NEHGR 55:303 abstract reads 'Elizabeth wife of Edward Yeamans'. Edward Yeomans or Emmons married Elizabeth Joslin at Boston 21 June 1652, and his widow Elizabeth Yeomans married John (register: 'Edward') Kilby 9 May 1662 (Boston Rec. Comm. 9). ¶1a

Probable, not Confirmed: the will and passenger list were read only as abstracts in compiled articles, and his daughter's own link to Christopher Kilby is Speculative. ¶1b

Audit 11 (2 Nov 2026): his father's link to him (Ralph Josselyn) is raised to Confirmed on the 1613 feoffment (ERO D/DHt T531) and the Thomas-and-Rebecca chain from Bollinghatch to Barham and the Increase (see the audit 11 note on his father). His own grade, on his link to Elizabeth Joslin, stays Probable: the will names 'my daughter Elizabeth Emons', and her identity with the Elizabeth Joslin who married Edward Yeomans at Boston in 1652 is still a match across places. ¶7

Open questions and the next record to read 3

Test record: Thomas Joslin's will, Middlesex County probate (docket for 1661), which should name 'my daughter Elizabeth Emons/Yeomans'. ¶1c

Test record: Middlesex probate file 12967 or the record-book copy of the will of 9 May 1660, for 'my daughter Elizabeth Emons/Yeomans'. Full-text searches of the will's wording (Nichols, Sumner, Rebecca, Lancaster) found no transcript, so it probably has to be read by eye. ¶3b

Test record: the will and inventory papers in Middlesex Probate docket 12967 (FamilySearch, Middlesex probate packets), for any paper naming 'Edward Yeomans' or 'Elizabeth Kilby'. ¶4b
