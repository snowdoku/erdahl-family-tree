About the family

Attached as Guri's father at Probable. The identification rests on the agreement of her given name, her patronymic, the Walstad farm byname she carried to America and her birth year, against a twelve-day discrepancy with the date on her American gravestone. ¶3

Father of Guri Anna Pettersdatter Walstad. Named as 'bruras far — Peter Olsen' in his daughter's marriage entry at Værnes, Nedre Stjørdal, 19 June 1863, and named again in the same entry as one of the two witnesses, 'Peder Ols[en] ibid[em]' — of Valstad. That independent naming is what moves this parentage from Probable to Confirmed. ¶5

Six children by Daaret Elisabet Pedersdatter, all recorded legitimate: Mette (b. 10 Mar 1828), Petrina (b. 16 Oct 1830), Olive (b. 27 Sep 1833), Guri Anna (b. 26 Apr 1836), Christopher (b. 4 Mar 1840) and Ole (b. 7 Jun 1842). ¶8

Parentage: His father is Ole Pedersen of Lille Hvalstad, rated Confirmed on three records that agree: the 1828 marriage index names the groom's father as Ole Pedersen; the 1801 census places Peter, aged 9, as a child of Ole Pedersen and Malena Pedersdatter on Valstad Lille; and Ole Petersen, gaardmand (farm owner) of Lille Hvalstad, born 1758, was buried in 1832, after which Peter rose from inderst (lodger) in 1830 to bonde (farmer) by 1833 on the same farm. 'Pedersen' and 'Petersen' are the same patronymic in these registers. His mother, Malena Pedersdatter, is Probable: the 1801 census calls Peter one of 'their children' and both parents are in their first marriage, but no baptism naming her has been read. A baptism of about 1791-92 would confirm it. ¶13

Parents' marriage: His parents, Ole Pedersen of Walstad and Malena Pedersdatter of Hegge, married on 24 October 1791, two months before his baptism. The Hegge godparents would fit his mother's home farm, but that link is inferred, not stated. ¶17

Life story

Farmer at Lille Hvalstad (Valstad) in Skatval, Nedre Stjørdal parish, Nord-Trøndelag. He is named as 'Peter Olsen Hvalstad, bonde' — a farm owner — at the baptism of Guri Anna Petersdatter on 10 July 1836, and as 'Peter Olsen, inderst' (a lodger, i.e. not yet holding the farm) at baptisms from the same house in 1828 and 1831. ¶1

Birth: About 1791 at Lille Hvalstad. His marriage entry of 1828 gives his age as 36½, birth year 1791 and birthplace Lillehvalstad, and the 1801 census lists him as Peter Olsen, aged 9, in his parents' household on the same farm. His baptism falls before the earliest Stjørdal baptism book in Digitalarkivet's index (1797) and has not been searched in scans. ¶10

Baptism: He was baptised at the Skatval annex church on Christmas Day, 25 December 1791, as 'Ole Lille Walstads B[arn]: Peter' (Stjørdal 1781-1797, chronological list p. 303). The godparents were Mali Wold, Mali Walstad, Jørgen Hegge, Ejner Hegge and Peder Walstad. This register names only the father, so his mother is not stated. The date agrees with his age of 9 in the 1801 census and 36 1/2 at his marriage in June 1828. ¶16

1801 census: Peter Olsen, 9, is listed as one of 'Deres børn' (their children) in the household of Ole Pedersen, 43, bonde og gaardbeboer (farmer and farm occupier), and his wife Malena Pedersdatter, 31, at Valstad Lille, Størdalen. Both parents are stated to be in their first marriage. His siblings in the house are Malena (6), Mali (3) and Peder (1). ¶11

Marriage: Married Doret (Daaret) Elisabet Pedersdatter at Værnes, Stjørdal, on 5 June 1828 (Stjørdal 1820-1832, marriages p. 353 no. 30). He is described as 'ungkarl gårdmandssøn' (a bachelor and farmer's son), aged 36½, residing at Lillehvalstad; the index names the groom's father as Ole Pedersen and the bride's father as Peder Pedersen. The index does not record the forlovere (marriage witnesses); the scan has not yet been read. ¶12

His status rises across his own children's baptisms: 'inderst' (a lodger with no land) in 1830, 'bonde' (farmer) by 1833, 'gaardmand' (farm owner) by 1840. Guri was born the daughter of a bonde at the moment the household was establishing itself on the land. ¶7

The rise from inderst in 1831 to bonde by 1836 suggests he took over the farm in between, probably on the death of Ole Petersen, gaardmand at Lille Hvalstad, who was buried in 1832 aged about 74 and is very likely his father. ¶2

He is the Petter the patronymic requires: Guri was Pettersdatter, and the family in South Dakota called her Guri Anna Pettersdatter Walstad long before this register was consulted. ¶4

Of LILLE HVALSTAD, later written VALSTAD, in Skatval, Nedre Stjørdal — the farm whose name the family carried to Union County, South Dakota as WALSTAD. ¶6

Death: Petter Olsen, gaardmand of Lille Valstad, died on 5 December 1849, aged 58 (born 1791), and was buried on 19 December 1849 (Stjørdal/Nedre Stjørdal 1845-1858, burials p. 494 no. 154). No cause or remark is recorded. The name, birth year, farm and status match him, and his wife is a widow on the same farm in 1865. ¶14

Corrections 1

Correction: The note above stating that the second witness at Guri Anna's wedding in 1863, 'Peder Ols[en] ibid', was her father Peter Olsen cannot be right, because Peter died in December 1849. The witness is very probably Peter's younger brother Peder Olsen, the infant of the 1801 household, who was farm owner (gaardbruger selveier) at Lillevalstad in the 1865 census. In the 1863 entry Peter Olsen is named only as the bride's father, which registers did for dead fathers as well. Guri Anna's parentage remains Confirmed on her 1836 baptism, which names both parents. ¶15
