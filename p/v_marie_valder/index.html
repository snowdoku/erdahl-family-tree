Life story

Sixth child of Peter and Olga Valder, born 25 February 1925, while her parents were still on the China field; her birthplace is not stated on her memorial. ¶1

Married Vern Elmo Nelson (1918-2005), a veteran; they are buried together in Fort Rosecrans National Cemetery, San Diego. A son, John V. Nelson (1948-1988), is linked to her memorial. ¶2

1930 census: Marie R. Valder, 5, daughter, born North Dakota, in her parents' household at Ferndale, Washington. Read from the census image. So she was born in North Dakota, not China, after the family's return. The 1940 census index shows her still at home at Ferndale (born about 1925, North Dakota); index only. ¶3

1940 census (read from the image): Marie R. Valder, daughter, 15, single, born in North Dakota, attending school, highest grade 'H-1', in her parents' household at Ferndale, Whatcom County, Washington, in the same house as in 1935. ¶4
