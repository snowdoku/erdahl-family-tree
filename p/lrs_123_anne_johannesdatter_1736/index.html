Life story

Burial: 'Ole Søftestads hustru Anne Johannesd. af 62 Aar' (Ole Søftestad's wife Anne Johannesdatter, aged 62, so b. about 1736) was buried in Nissedal in 1798. Kviteseid ministerialbok 1787-1799 (Digitalarkivet source 7983), burials 1798, canvas 252, right page, side note in dossier (overview image, not carded or cropped); the exact date was not recorded. ¶1

Bygdebok (Nissedal vol., Søftestad, canvases 306, 452): Olav Tellevson (about 1719) married Anne Johannesdtr.; the author writes that he does not find Anne Johannesdtr. baptised or confirmed in Nissedal or Treungen, so the book gives no parents for her (a Johannes is mentioned as a possible father, unread). 3 on the grade · 1 alternative set aside

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 3

Speculative mother of Tellev Olsen (b. 1760): she was about 24 at his baptism. A son named Johannes (Johannes Olsen Søftestad, m. 1787) fits naming after her father Johannes, and an 'Anne Søftestad' stood godmother to Tellev's daughter Anne in 1792. Against certainty: Ole was about 17 years older than her and may have had an earlier wife; no record names Tellev's mother. Would decide it: a probate after Ole or Anne naming Tellev. ¶3

Upgrade: Confirmed, graded on her link to her son Tellev (Tallef) Olsen (see his page). The panteregister I 2 (source 10916, p. 783, view 131 right), Søftestad in Nissedal, abstracts 'Tallef Olsen... Contract med hans Forældre Ole Tallefsen og Anne Johannesdatter', dated 13 Oct 1791, registered 3 Mar 179[2] (No 2252): a retirement contract between Tallef and 'his parents' Ole Tallefsen and Anne Johannesdatter. This answers the doubt in her earlier notes that Ole might have had an earlier wife who was Tellev's mother: the record calls Anne one of Tellev's parents ('Forældre'). Continuity to the tree's Tellev: same page, Tallef Olsen's wife Kari Torjusdatter (probate 1803-04) and his sons Ole, Torjus and Halvor, matching the 1801 census household at Søftestad. She was buried in 1798 as 'Ole Søftestads hustru Anne Johannesd.', aged 62, which agrees. ¶4a

Audit 7 (1-2 Nov 2026): Confirmed kept, graded on her link to Tellev Olsen (see his page): the panteregister abstract calls Ole Tallefsen and Anne Johannesdatter Tallef Olsen's 'Forældre', no earlier wife of Ole is named, and the caveat in her upgrade note is theoretical, not a contradiction. Partial check for that caveat: the auditor read the Kviteseid marriages (ministerialbok 7981, 0-based IIIF canvases 109-110, Jan-Jul 1757; 113, Apr-Jun 1758; 115, Nov 1758-Apr 1759) without finding Ole Tallefsen Søftestad's marriage. Canvases 111-112, 114 and 116 (mid-1757 to early 1758, and spring 1759) are unread. Finding the marriage, with Ole as a bachelor (ungkarl), would remove the stepmother caveat entirely. ¶5

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Caveat: 'Forældre' could in principle be used loosely of a stepmother, but the clerk's word is parents and no other wife of Ole is known. ¶4b
