About the family

Second wife of Anton Johannes Tungseth, married in 1926 according to his Find a Grave memorial, and buried as 'Thea Sophia Halverson Tungseth', 1887-1965, in Zion Lutheran Cemetery, Skree Township, Clay County, Minnesota. She and Anton both died in 1965. She was probably the mother of his two youngest children, Agnes Valborg (1927-2014) and Peter Ole (1928-2011); the 2014 obituary of Agnes Valborg Tungseth names her parents as Anton and Thea (Halverson) Tungseth. ¶1
