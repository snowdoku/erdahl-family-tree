Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Graded Probable on his link to his son Torsten Siursen Berge (see his page). The son's patronymic is primary: 'Torsten Siursen' 50 at Berge in the 1701 manntall (38461 c120), so his father was a Sjur (Siur), born before about 1630. The bygdebok (Selbu i fortid og nåtid vol. 5, Berge, canvas 21) calls Torsten 'Torsten Sjurdsen, f. ca. 1652' and says he 'var bror[sønn] til Ola Torstensen', the previous user of Berge (Ola Tostensen, 60 in 1665 per the book, canvas 19: 'Fra 1685 er det en ny Tosten, [...] etter Ola. Han het Tosten Sjursen og var f. i 1651'). So the book makes Sjur a brother of Ola Tostensen of Berge and a son of the older Tosten at Berge. ¶1

Open questions and the next record to read 2

Where he lived is not known. He is not a user with a son Torsten of about 14-15 anywhere in the 1666 Selbu vicar's manntall (35528 v21-25, all households listed in; the Siurs there are at Kolset, Neder Vigen, Slinde, Kvello, Sirem and Skjøbaard and have no son Torsten), so he had probably died, or Torsten was in service, by 1666. A 'knegt Thorsten...sen 15' at Grøtte in the 1664 fogd's list (35578 v22R) was read as Esthensøn; a re-read for 'Siurssøn' is a cheap test. ¶2

Test record: the 1664 fogd's list (35578 v22R, Grøtte servant 'Thorsten...sen 15'), the 1645 koppskatt half-farm and cottager lists for Selbu (77038 v269R-270R and from v285R), and a court or deed entry naming Torsten Sjursen's father or his right to Berge as nephew of Ola Tostensen (Stjørdal tingbøker before 1685). ¶3
