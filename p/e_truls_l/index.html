About the family

Parentage: the inheritance of 4 July 1728 (inheritance book fol. 146) is the probate after the unmarried Brynilde Trulsdatter of Aaning, held 4 July 1728 (skifteprotokoll L0006, fol. 146). Her heirs were 'Lars Troelsen Aaning's' two surviving children, one son and one daughter, her brother Jørgen Trulsen Aaning, and her sister Christi Trulsdatter. Truls Larsen of Aaning, credited with an inheritance from that very probate, is therefore Lars Trulsen's son and Brynilde's nephew. Supporting: Truls's own children include Jørgen and Christi, the names of Lars Trulsen's siblings. Probable rather than Confirmed because the probate does not name the son; the 23 October 1724 probate (probably his father's) is not in the scanned series, which has a gap from 1715 to 1727. ¶4

Life story

Aaland 1932: Aaland's Aaning history (bruk nr. 2) reads: 'S. Lars, † 36 a. g. 1723, br. 1714 heile g. * (Synnøve Olsd., † 92 a. g. 1768). Enkja br. 1724-35 heile, etter 1736 halve garden. Br. Jørgen, † 84 a. g. 1774, br. 1735 halva; … Selde 1751 2 pd. til brorsonen Truls. … Brs. Truls Larsson, † ett. 1783, sk. 1751 p. heile g., derav ½ pd. av farbroren Jørgen. * Marte Monsd., † 63 a. g. 1782. Garden vart so delt i to like br. i 1783 mill. sønerne.' Truls Larsen, his brother's son (brorson) of Jørgen, took a deed to the whole farm in 1751 (half a pund of it from his paternal uncle Jørgen), died after 1783, and married Marte Monsdatter, who died in 1782 aged 63. The farm was split in 1783 between his sons Lars and Mons. This agrees with the 1736-44 guardianship and the 1783 probate. In 1766 he lost a court case against the Nesje farmers over summer pasture. ¶6

Also written Truls Lassesen and Truels Larssen. Farmer at Ånning (Aaning), Oppstryn. He was a ward in 1736-44 and was married and living at Aaning by 1741. ¶1

Aaland 1932: Aaland names his mother only in brackets (his own inference) as Synnøve Olsdatter, who died in 1768 aged 92 and ran Aaning as a widow in 1724-35: see Synnøve Olsdatter. Aaland also gives the wife of Truls's uncle Jørgen Trulsen as '(Kristi Magnesdatter Tyva)'. That makes it likely that Truls's court-appointed guardian 'Jørgen Tyvem' in 1724 was this paternal uncle, then living at his wife's farm; this is an inference, not stated by Aaland. ¶7

Legal guardian of his daughter Birthe in Lasse Rasmussen's 1774 probate. At his own request, a probate was held on 30 Apr 1783 after the death of his wife. It names his children: Lars Trulsen (eldest son, at Aaning), Mons Trulsen, Jørgen Trulsen, Joen Trulsen, Salve Trulsen, Brite Trulsdatter (married to Arent Knudsen Nesje) and Christi Trulsdatter (dead, leaving 4 children). ¶2

Guardianship: Nordfjord overformynderiprotokoll no. 1 (1711-1735), fol. 76v. His guardian (formynder) Jørgen Tyvem (Tjugum) held the inheritance of 'Truels Larsen Aaning' from a probate of 23 October 1724 (inheritance book fol. 92), from 4 July 1728 (fol. 146) and from the probate after the late Ole [?] on 2 June 1735 (fol. 225), in all 19 rd 1 ort 2 sk. This was carried to book no. 2, fol. 198, where in 1741 it is noted that he had claimed nothing because he was now married and living at Aaning in Stryn. Since a living father was normally his child's guardian, a court-appointed guardian in October 1724 suggests his father was already dead by then (inferred). ¶3

1 alternative set aside

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Ruled out: he was not a son of Lasse Rasmussen of Nedre Flo (see his page). Lasse's probate (skifteprotokoll L0007, fol. 151) lists the sons Aamund, Ole, Peder and Rasmus and no Truls, and Truls was already a ward of Jørgen Tyvem in 1724, while Lasse Rasmussen lived until about 1731. So the marriage of Lasse Rasmussen Erdal and Birthe Trulsdatter was not a first-cousin marriage through Nedre Flo. ¶5
