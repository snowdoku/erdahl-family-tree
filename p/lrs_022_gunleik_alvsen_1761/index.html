About the family

Probable brother: 'Alf Taes [or Toes] Søn Haavor' (Halvor) was baptised in Drangedal on the 3rd Sunday in Advent, 17 Dec 1758 (date computed; Drangedal ministerialbok 1697-1767, p. 22). Same father and farm-name form, and a Kaasa woman stood godmother at both baptisms. No Halvor or Haavor Alfsen was found in the 1801 census of Drangedal and Tørdal. ¶6

Probable brother: John Alfsen (b. about 1755), farmer and householder at Thoe, Drangedal, in the 1801 census, in his second marriage (both spouses). He is the only Alfsen in Drangedal and Tørdal in 1801 and lived on the farm (Thoe = Toe) named at the 1758 and 1761 baptisms. His parents and baptism are not read. ¶7

Father link to Alf Halvorsen (Toe) raised back to Probable: the Drangedal baptisms for Nov 1759 to Dec 1760 (canvases 200-203) were and show no son of an Alf; the only Gunleik is a son of Halvor Klebbe (Apr 1760). This answers the check asked for by audit. ¶10

Mother (Speculative): Tore Torkildsdatter, b. about 1737, a widow at Thoe, Drangedal, in the 1801 census, mother of John Alfsen (b. about 1755), Gunleik's possible brother. She is taken to be Alf of Toe's wife because she was in her first marriage and her son is an Alfsen on the same farm. What is missing: Gunleik's 1761 baptism does not name his mother, and no record ties Gunleik to her. ¶11a

Probable sister: Aasil Alfsdatter of Toe, buried in Drangedal at Lady Day 1764 (index pg00000008555036). ¶12

Mother (Probable): Aasil (Åshild) Jonsdatter of Steensland, b. about 1728 (see her page). She married Alf Halvorsen (then of Dale) in Drangedal on 2 Jul 1753 and was buried from Toe on 17 Mar 1764, aged 36. No remarriage of Alf from 1753 to Jan 1763 and no burial of Aasil or of any wife of Alf from 1755 to 1763 was found in the Drangedal registers, so she was Alf's only known wife and alive when Gunleik was baptised in Aug 1761 and his mother was churched on 29 Sep 1761. Inferred: the baptism does not name the mother. ¶14a

Sister (died in infancy): Aasil Alfsdatter of Toe, aged 3 weeks, buried 25 Mar 1764, eight days after her mother Aasil Jonsdatter (see her page). This is the same 1764 burial as in the earlier 'Probable sister' note. ¶15

Parents raised to Confirmed: the 1764 probate names Gunlich, aged 3, as the son of Alv (Alf) Halvorsen and Aasel (Aasil) Jonsdatter of Toe, which matches the 1761 baptism of 'Alf Toes Søn Gundlick'. This supersedes the earlier notes 'Mother (Probable): Aasil (Åshild) Jonsdatter', 'Father link to Alf Halvorsen (Toe) raised back to Probable' and 'Father link... is Speculative'. Gunleik's own identification as the later Gunleik Alvsen of Midbøe, Nissedal, remains Probable, as stated in his 'Identification (Probable)' note. ¶17

Brothers (from the 1764 probate): Jon Alfsen, 11 in Oct 1764 (b. about 1753); Torgen Alfsen, 9 (b. about 1755), not previously known; Halvor Alfsen, 6 (b. about 1758, the Haavor baptised 17 Dec 1758). ¶18a

Father of Aasild proved: The Nissedal confirmation list for 1822 (ministerialbok 1814-1845, source 8019, p. 247, canvas 162, girls no. 13, in the group confirmed 28 Jul 1822) reads 'Aasil Midbøe | Gunlek og Turi Midbøe | døbt 1805 Midfaste Søndag', vaccinated 4 Mar 1806 by A. Hansen of Tvetene. This names her parents as Gunleik and Turi of Midbø and her baptism as the Midfaste Sunday baptism of 24 Mar 1805 ('Gunleik Alvsen Midböes Datter Aasild'). On 12 Apr 1823 'Aasild Gunleiksdatter Midbøe', 18, married Torkeld Olsen Ibrække, so name, farm and age join the confirmand to the bride. His link to Aasild is therefore proved. His grade stays Probable only because his identification with the 1761 Drangedal baptism and 1764 probate ('Gunlich Alfsen' of Toe) still rests on age and patronymic; no record yet names Midbø and Toe together. ¶20

Life story

Father link to Alf Halvorsen (Toe) is Speculative pending a check of Drangedal baptisms 1759–60. ¶9

Baptism: on the 14th Sunday after Trinity 1761 (23 Aug 1761; Easter fell on 22 Mar) 'Alf Toes Søn Gundlick' (Alf Toe's son Gunleik) was baptised in Drangedal. Godparents (testes): Marj Tellefsdatter Røgndalen, Ingebor Jonsdatter Bergenne, Kirstj Anundsdatter Kaasa, Tov Traae and Niels Traae. The mother was churched (introduced) on Michaelmas, 29 Sep; she is not named. The farm name is an uncertain reading (Toe, possibly Foe). Drangedal ministerialbok 1697-1767 (source 7848), baptisms 1761, p. 41. ¶4

Probate of his mother: on 2 Oct 1764 the sheriff (lensmann) of Drangedal and two appraisers registered at Toe in Tørdal the estate of the late Aasel Jonsdatter, for division between her surviving husband Alv Halvorsen and their four joint sons ('sammen avlede 4 Sønner'): eldest Jon Alfsen 11, Torgen Alfsen 9, Halvor Alfsen 6 and fourth son Gunlich Alfsen 3. Guardians (formyndere): Jon Dahle for Jon, Engvold Torredahl for Torgen, Thore Steensland for Halvor, and the father Alf Toe for Gunlich. Gunlich's inheritance, 37 rd 3 ort 20¼ ß, was laid out in the farm Thoe. Bamble skifteprotokoll I 6b, fol. 455a-457a. ¶16

1801 census: Gunlek Alvsen, 40, householder and farmer-occupier (bonde og gaardbeboer) at Midbøe Nordre, Nissedal (then Kviteseid prestegjeld), in his first marriage, with wife Turi Thoresdatter 25, farmhand Joen Tellevsen 23 and his wife's sister Guro Toresdatter 13. No children were in the household. ¶1

Baptism of daughter: father of Aasild, baptised 24 Mar 1805 (Midfaste Sunday) in Nissedal ('Gunleik Alvsen Midböes Datter Aasild'). ¶2

Burial (per index): Gunleik Alvsen, aged 59, died 2 May and buried 11 May 1820, Nissedal ministerialbok 1814-1845, burials 1820, p. 101, no. 7 (index pg00000008652021). The index leaves the residence blank; the link to Midbøe rests on the name, the age and the parish, and on index rows placing 'Gunlik Alfsen' at Midbøe in 1811 and 'Gunleik' at Midbøe in 1812 as father of buried children. The scan was not read. ¶8

Confirmation of daughter: Nissedal, 28 Jul 1822 ('Den 28de Julii 1822 confirmerede Piger'), girl no. 13: 'Aasild Midbøe' | parents 'Gunleik og Turi Midbøe' | 'født 1805 Midfaste Søndag'. Nissedal ministerialbok 1814-1860 (source 8016), Confirmerede Piger, p. 244 (IIIF canvas 220, right page). ¶21

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Current grade (from patch 020, audit): both parent links are Confirmed. His father is Alf Halvorsen (see his page) and his mother Aasil Jonsdatter (see her page): the probate of 2 Oct 1764 after Aasil names 'Gunlich Alfsen', 3, as their son. The grades in the older notes 'Father link to Alf Halvorsen (Toe) is Speculative pending a check', 'Father link... raised back to Probable' and 'Mother (Probable)' are superseded; see the note 'Parents raised to Confirmed'. His own identity with Gunleik Alvsen of Midbøe, Nissedal, stays Probable. ¶19

Upgrade: graded on his link to his daughter Aasild Gulliksdatter (see her page). This is the confirmation entry that the note 'Identification as the father of Aasild... is Probable' named as the record that would settle it. It names the parents Gunleik and Turi of Midbøe and gives her baptism as Midfaste Sunday 1805, which is the day of the 1805 baptism of 'Gunleik Alvsen Midböes Datter Aasild' (mother Turi Torsdatter). Continuity: baptism 1805 (Midbøe) -> confirmation Jul 1822 (Midbøe, same baptism day) -> marriage 12 Apr 1823 as 'Aasild Gunleiks D.r Midböe' to Torkild Olsen -> Aasild Gulliksdatter; same farm, gaps of under a year at the end, and no rival (she is the only Aasild with a father Gunleik in the Nissedal/Kviteseid baptisms 1802-1808). His identity with the 1761 Drangedal baptism (son of Alf Toe) is a separate link, graded on Alf's record. ¶22

The case for this identification 2

Identification as the father of Aasild Gulliksdatter (see her page) is Probable: she married in 1823 as 'Aasild Gunleiks D.r Midböe', and this is the only matching baptism. A skifte after Gunleik Alvsen of Midbø, or Aasild's confirmation entry, would confirm it. ¶3

Identification (Probable): the 1761 Gundlick is taken to be Gunleik Alvsen of Midbøe because he is the only son of an Alf (Alv) found in every Drangedal baptism read from late 1758 to Oct 1761, and the date fits his ages of 40 in Feb 1801 and 59 at burial in 1820. The only other Gunleik baptised in 1761, son of Lars Gundliksen Naas (p. 38), is ruled out. Birth date changed from 'ABT 1761' to before 23 Aug 1761, the baptism. ¶5a

Corrections 2

Correction: Tore Torkildsdatter (see her page) is removed as Gunleik's mother. The Drangedal marriage register shows that the widower (Enkke-Mand) Alf Halvorsen Toe was betrothed to the maiden Tore Torkildsdatter on 1 Apr 1766 and married her on 12 Jul 1766, five years after Gunleik's baptism in Aug 1761 (p. 43). She was his stepmother. This supersedes the earlier note 'Mother (Speculative): Tore Torkildsdatter'. ¶13

Correction: the earlier note 'Probable brother: John Alfsen (b. about 1755)' is superseded. John Alfsen, householder at Thoe in 1801, is taken to be the eldest son Jon (b. about 1753 by the probate; his 1801 age of 46 understates it by a year or two); same name and same farm. He is now a full brother named in his mother's probate, and the 1801 'Mandens moder' Tore Torkildsdatter was his stepmother. The earlier 'Probable brother: Haavor' note is likewise confirmed by the probate (Halvor, 6). ¶18b
