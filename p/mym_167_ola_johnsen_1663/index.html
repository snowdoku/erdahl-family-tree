Life story

Age check: born 1663 per the book, he would be about 61 at Mali's birth (c.1724; her wife-mother b. 1682 would be about 42). Possible but a flag: the 1663 may belong to another phrase in the gapped rebuild, which is one more reason to hold this at Probable. ¶3

Farmer of Vold (gnr. 29/1), Skatval. Bygdebok (Skatval vol. 1, 29/1, canvas 164): Hans Jakobsen sold the farm on 03.02.1720 to 'sin svigersønn' Ola Johnsen, who had held it on lease and got a deed 26.06.1728; 'Ola Johnsen, [f.] 1663, [d.] 06.08.1747', wife '[name not recovered] Olsdtr., [f.] 1682'; children John (owner), Ola døpt 06.12.1716, Berit døpt 14.12.1721 (m. Hans Olsen), Anne (m. Torstein, 27/1), a child døpt 02.12.1725, and 'Mali, [g.m.] enkemann Peder Eriksen [35]'. ¶1

Bygdebok children checked on the scan: 'døbt Oluf Joens. Volds b. Birrit. Mod. Gundbor' on Dom. 3 Advent [14 Dec] 1721 (16368 c90 R) confirms the bygdebok's 'Berit døpt 14.12.1721' and gives his patronymic Joensen at Vold in 1721; 'Døbt Olaf Volds b. Olaf, Mod. [G]undbor[?]' on 2 Advent [6 Dec] 1716 (c33 R) is 'Ola døpt 06.12.1716'. So the book's child list is reliable for these two. His grade on Mali stays Probable until her own baptism is read (Test record: 16368 c104-111, c116-118). ¶8

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Graded Probable as father of Mali Olsdatter of Baar (see her page): bygdebok statement only. It agrees with the Bå chapter (vol. 1, canvas 222 left: 'Peder Eriksen 2. gang 05.06.1753 [m.] Mali Olsdtr. [fra] 29') and with her burial age (67 in 1791, so b. c.1724). Godparents at Peder Lille Walstad's 1723 baptism and later Baar baptisms are Vold people, a further fit. The 'Olsdtr.' wife conflicts with 'svigersønn' of Hans Jakobsen, unless she was a stepdaughter or a second wife; her name was not recovered, so no mother is added. ¶2

Searches that found nothing 2

1666 vicar's census, negative: if he was born about 1663 in Skatval he should appear, aged about 3, in the 1666 list, which gives all sons. The Vold holdings in 1666 (35528 view 86, p. 153, nos. 143-144; ødegaard Vold view 87, p. 155) have 'Jon 31' with 'Tomes Johans. 6' and 'Oluf Christophers. 4'; 'Oluf Halfvers. 31' with 'Christopher Pers. 8' and 'Per Anders. 14'; Øster Vold 'Per 31' with 'Anders Ols. 17' and 'Hogen Ols. 12'; and ødegaard Vold 'Jon 52' with 'Jon Erichs. 7' and 'Jon Jons. 20'. No Ole Jonsen of about 3 is at any Vold holding, which fits the bygdebok's account that he came to Vold as Hans Jakobsen's son-in-law. His origin is still unknown. ¶6

Negative, card index by farm Vold/Volden/Volen, 77804 c2209-2245: the Skatval Vold cards are c2233 (3/10-1733, 'Dragonrider' Johan Pedersen, widow Guri Jonsdtr.) and c2235 (Vold øvre, dragonkvarter, 26/3-1738, Marit Ingebretsdtr., widower Haagen Halvorsen Vold). The others are Stjørdal (c2225, 17/4-1730 Gunder Larsen Wold), Skogn, Frosta and Verdal. No probate after Hans Jakobsen Vold or Ola Johnsen's first wife is in the index for 1689-1742. ¶7
