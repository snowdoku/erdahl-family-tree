Life story

Entered in the 1801 census of Selbu at rural residence 0015 Svinaas, household 3, as 'Ingebor Torgersdatter, 22, Hans datter, Ugift' - HIS daughter, in the household of Torger Erikssen and his second wife Magli Guttormsdatter. The census's choice of 'hans' over 'deres' is why she is given a father here and no mother: she was a child of Torger's first, unnamed marriage, as her half-brother or brother Haldo Torgersen very probably was too. ¶1
