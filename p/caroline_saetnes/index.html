Life story

Maid in the household of district physician Werring, Kristiansund, in 1875. ¶1

The marriage index garbles her surname as 'Solmør' and her father's as 'Solnøs'; the birth date matches. ¶2

The ages in that list are unreliable and should not be used as birth years. The clerk's calculated years run from one to three years late against the 1891 census and the known birth dates - Olga, who was four years and three months old, is entered as aged 2, and the mother's year is calculated as 1859 when she was born in February 1851. The 1891 census and the Find a Grave gravestone dates are the better evidence. ¶4

In the 1891 census she is 'Hendrikke Karoline Gurine Tungsæt', born 1851 in Kristiansund, wife of the head, living with her husband, seven children and one servant at 47 Toldbodsmujet in Kristiansund. Her youngest, Erling, was born there in September 1893, five weeks before her husband sailed for Manitoba and seven months before she followed with the children. ¶5

The timing of the emigration is grim. Her husband's mother Tørri died at Tungset on 20 Jun 1893; her husband left Kristiansund on 11 Oct 1893; her husband's father Paul Ellingsen died at Tungset on 14 Jan 1894; and she sailed with the eight children on 2 May 1894. Both of the children's Norwegian grandparents on the Tungset side died in the ten months between the father's departure and the mother's. ¶6

She brought all eight children out alone. The Nordmøre police emigrant protocol no. 3 for Kristiansund records a party of nine expedited on 2 May 1894 with the Dominion Line, destination Minnesota, tickets bought in Kristiansund: the mother, whose name the transcribers could not read and entered as '!! Tungset', married, and the children Paul, Anna, Karl, Gustav, Anton, Marie, Olga and Erling. They travelled in a group of eighteen emigrants from Nordmøre and Romsdal, the others bound for Montreal, Halifax and Dakota. No ship is named, only the line. ¶3

1895 Minnesota census: Caroline Tungseth, 44, born in Norway, is in her husband Peter's household in Skree Township, Clay County, with their eight children (p. 6, read from the image). Her age agrees with her birth in February 1851. ¶7

1900 census: on 14 Jun 1900 'Caroline' Tungseth (the first name is hard to read; the census index renders it 'Cho?A') is head of the household in Skree Township, Clay County - born February 1851 in Norway, aged 49, married 22 years, mother of eight children of whom seven were living, immigrated 1894, six years in the United States, a farmer on an owned farm free of mortgage. She can read and write but cannot speak English. Her husband is not in the household. With her are Paul (Feb 1881), Carl (Sep 1883), Gustav (Jun 1885), Anton (Jan 1887), Olga (Jan 1890) and Erling (Sep 1893), all born in Norway and all at school (ED 316, sheet 185A, lines 1-7, read from the image). ¶8

1900 census, the seventh child: she reported seven living children but six are at home. The seventh is very probably Anna, who appears in the 1900 index as Annie Tungseth, servant, born May 1882 in Norway, living in Hawley, Clay County (index only, image not read). ¶9

Death certificate: FamilySearch's index of her Minnesota death certificate ('Minnesota, Death Records and Certificates, 1900-1955', ref 2744) gives Carolina Gurine Tungseth, female, aged 77, died 14 Aug 1928 in Clay County, Minnesota (FamilySearch mis-standardises the place to Clay Township, Hubbard County), and names her parents as Peder Johnson and Ane Satness. The MNHS index used earlier leaves the parents blank. 'Peder Johnson' is her father Peder Johnsen Sætnes and 'Ane Satness' her mother Anne Larsdatter, here given the family's byname Sætnes; aged 77 agrees with her birth in February 1851. Confidence is unchanged: her father is already Confirmed, and her mother's record stays Probable because this certificate names the mother only by given name and married byname, adding nothing to the identification already made from the 1842 marriage. The certificate image was not viewed, so informant and cause of death are unread. ¶13

1905 Minnesota census: Caroline Tungseth, 55, born in Norway of Norwegian parents, 11 years in Minnesota and the United States, no occupation entered, in her husband Peder's household in Skree Township with five of the children (Enumeration District 10, schedule sheet III, line 31, read from the image). The age is a year high; see her husband's record for the comparison. ¶10

1910 census: Karolina P. Tungseth, wife, 59, married 27 years (a census error; the marriage was in 1877), mother of eight children (the number living is not legible on the image), immigrated 1894, speaks Norwegian only, no occupation, in Peter P O Tungseth's household in Skree Township (ED 59, sheet 187A, line 35, read from the image). Her middle initial P is probably her patronymic, Pedersdatter. ¶11

Death: the Minnesota death index records Carolina Gurine Tungseth, died 14 Aug 1928 in Clay County, certificate 1928-MN-002744; the parents' fields are blank. This is index-only: no certificate image is online, so her recorded age, the place within the county and her cause of death are not known. She outlived her husband by thirteen years. ¶12
