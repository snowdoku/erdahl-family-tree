Life story

Identified as Peder Olsen, b. about 1718, son of the widow Gunild Nielsdatter of Vangberg, Frosta: her probate of 14 Feb 1765 (24755 fol. 455b, scan read) names 'Peder Olsen Breset 47 aar' among her children, beside his brother 'Niels Olsen Breiset 46 aar'. Frostaboka VI p. 217 (Breiset) says that Peder Olsen (from Vangberg 44/1), b. 1718, took 1 1/2 [spann] of Breiset in 1756 from his brother Nils Olsen, and in 1762 had wife Anna and children Arent, Guri (confirmed 1756) and Kirsti; it lists Jens Pedersen Breiset b. 1758 (d. 1773 aged 16), Per Persen Breiset b. 1763, a Pedersdtr. Breiset d. 1773 aged 25, and in 1780 Anna, daughter of Gregers [and Gurru] Breiset. The farm historian thus places Gurru in this family, but that is a compiled source. His grade on the link to Gurru stays Probable: no record names her as his daughter. ¶6

Tenant (leilænding) at Breiset, Frosta, in 1762. Probable father of Gurru Pedersdatter (see her page), graded on that link. ¶1

Evidence. (1) Ekstraskatt 1762, Stjør- og Verdal fogderi (source 48401, view 181, row 40, scan and): 'Breisæt', leilænding, husband and wife 'Peder, Anne', children over 12 'Aren, Guri, Kiersti'. It is the only Breiset household in the list. (2) Frosta 1750-78, public absolution, 14th Sunday after Trinity 1763 (index ps00000000005836): 'Gurru Persdatter', of Breiset, did open penance for leiermål (an illegitimate child), so a Gurru Pedersdatter was living unmarried at Breiset a year after the tax. (3) A 'Gurru' of Breiset was godmother at Laugtu on the 4th Sunday after Trinity 1762 (pd00000006474491). (4) Gurru Pedersdatter married Greis Hansen of Vigleeret in 1766 (no home farm written for the bride). (5) In 1780 the couple lived at Breiset: baptism of Anna, 6th Sunday after Trinity 1780, parents 'Gregers' and 'Gurru Pedersdtr', Breyset (pd00000006455895), and churching of Gregers' wife at Breyset, 11th Sunday after Trinity 1780 (py00000000027416). Greis is a short form of Gregers; the same man is 'Gregers' of Vigleret in 1783-89. ¶2

The child 'Aren' in the 1762 household is probably Arent (Arnt) Larsen, later farmer at Breiset (Arn Breset 1780s-90s), kårmann at Breiset when buried 17 May 1818, aged 82-86 (pg00000002399855, pg00000007131527). His patronymic Larsen shows that the 1762 'children' column included at least one stepchild or foster child, so the household was probably a blended one. ¶5

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the grade · 1 alternative set aside · 1 search with no result

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Why Probable, not Confirmed: the 1762 tax names Guri as a child of the Peder-Anne household, but gives no patronymics, and the step from that Guri to the tree's Gurru rests on the matching name and patronymic and her return to Breiset with her husband by 1780. Rivals: other women called Gurru Pedersdatter married at Frosta in 1777, 1786 and 1798 (pv00000001561431, pv00000001558192, pv00000001559044); the 1777 bride could in principle be the 1763 penitent. The 1780 Breiset residence of Gregers and Gurru is the point that ties the tree's Gurru to this farm. ¶3

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Peder was probably not young in 1762: his daughter Guri was over 12 and is probably the Gurru born about 1737 (64 in the 1801 census), which puts his birth before about 1717. A Peder of Breiset also had sons Jens (bapt. 8th Sunday after Trinity 1758, pd00000006473357) and Peer (bapt. 8th Sunday after Trinity 1763, pd00000006474771), and stood godfather in 1770 (pd00000006476710). Whether these are the same Peder, or a younger Peder (for example a son), is unproved. No probate for a Peder or Anne at Breiset appears in the typed Stjør- og Verdal probate registers 1742-1802 (24753, 24755-24765); the only Breiset probate is Kari Einersdatter, wife of Willum Willumsen, in the 1740s (24753 fol. 105). ¶4

Searches that found nothing 1

His death is not found. A Peder of Vigleret (Greis and Gurru's cottage) was buried in Frosta on 10 Mar 1783 aged 67 (b. c.1716; pg00000001198630); he could be Peder Olsen living with his daughter, but this is unproved. Peder Olsen, husmann at Storlien under Asklund, buried 1782 aged 65, is ruled out: his probate (24759 fol. 448a, scan read) names only the children Ole 28 and Berethe 32 of his first marriage and Inger 6 of his second. ¶7
