Life story

Protocol re-read at full resolution: Hadeland, Land og Valdres skifteprotokoll 1737-1743 (DA 25286), view 225, s. 427, lower half. The estate was held 21 Jun 1740 at Fodnes in Svennes annex of Aurdal by the deputy judges Ole Arnesen and Ole Engelbretsen Svennes. It was held after 'afgangne... Kierstj Johans[datter]' between her and her surviving husband ('efterlevende Mand') Tommes Uldricksen, with their 6 children. These include a son Uldrich and daughters named Tommesdatter, the youngest a daughter read as 'Rangdi[?]'. The lines that follow name guardians for the minor children, but the names are not legible. The page then turns to the registration and debts: an obligation dated 14 Oct 1739, and a creditor at Søndre Fodnes, 22 rd. No father, brother (morbror) or other kin of Kiersti is named on s. 427. The entry continues on s. 428 (view 226), which was not read. Crops:. ¶2

Probate (index card 3247): estate of 'Kiersti Johansdtr. +', wife of Tommes Uldrichsen, at Fodnes Nordre i Svenes i N. Aurdal ('Nordre Faadnæs'), 21 Jun 1740 (Hadeland, Land og Valdres skifteprotokoll 1737-1743, s. 427). Children: 1. Uldrick 4 1/2, 2. Johannes 3, 3. Barbroe 13, 4. Marit 11, 5. Sigri 9, 6. Rangdie 5 1/2. Gross about 831 rdr, net about 651, besides odel land in Nordre Fodnes. The protocol entry on s. 427 was viewed (it names her as 'Kierstj Johans[datter]' with the six children); her parents could not be read from it. The son Uldrick (b. about late 1735) is Uldrich Thommesen of Fodnes (d. 1812), who was 'Uldrik Tommesen', b. 1735, on føderaad at Fodnes in the 1801 census. Her son Johannes carries her father's name. Her eldest daughter Barbro (b. about 1727) carries the name of Tommes's mother Barbro Knudsdatter. ¶1
