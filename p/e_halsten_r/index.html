About the family

Father (Speculative): Rasmus Haraldsen of Rustøen (b. about 1700, buried 17 May 1787 aged 88, widower). Halsten married in 1773 as a bachelor of Rustøen. The 1701 census of Røstøen has only two Rasmuses young enough to father a son in about 1737: Rasmus Christophersen, aged 7, whose 1760 probate names five heirs and no Halsten, and Rasmus Haraldsen, aged 1, whose household also held a Halsteen Rasmussen, aged 10, probably his half-brother. This is a candidate by elimination and naming only; no record names Halsten's parents. To test it, read the probate after Rasmus Haraldsen (about 1787; not in the Nordfjord index, so read the registers of skifteprotokoll A 18a/18b, 1786-90), and find Halsten as a godparent or witness among the children of Harald Rasmussen Røstøen in the Innvik baptisms of the 1750s-60s (godparents not indexed; scans only). ¶8

Mother: Aaland's Rustøen history gives his (Probable) father Rasmus Haraldsen the wife Marte Ingebrigtsdatter (d. 1787 aged 89); linked as Speculative. ¶12

Life story

Innvik baptisms of the children of Harald Rasmussen Rustøen and Siri Antonisdatter, read from the scan of Innvik ministerialbok 1750-1816 (IIIF 11506): Martha, 4 Jun 1760 (canvas 37, girls no. 39), godparents Peder Antonisen Sunde, Claus Rasmussen Rustøen, Anne Olsd. Aaberg, Siri Andersd. Rustøen, Synneve Antonisd. Sunde; Barbra, 12 Mar 1770 (canvas 73, girls no. 11), godparents Ole Andersen Sunde, Jacob Anthonisen Sunde, Birthe Micklebust, Susanna Qvame, Ellen Davidsd. Aabrecke. Halsten Rasmussen is not a godparent at either, so this source gives no support to a sibling link between Halsten and Harald Rasmussen (negative). The 1766 baptism (Elen, 7 Jun 1766, canvas ~61-62) was not read. ¶10

Innvik baptism 7 Jun 1766 (IIIF 11506 canvas 61, p. 62, girls no. 27): Elen, daughter of Harald Rasmussen Rustøen and Siri Anthonisdatter; godfather 'Halsteen Rasmus. Rustöen'. This places Halsten (then about 29) at Rustøen in 1766, seven years before his 1773 marriage as a bachelor of Rustøen, and ties him to Harald Rasmussen's household as a godparent. It supports, without proving, that Halsten and Harald were brothers, sons of Rasmus Haraldsen Rustøen (see his page, now Probable). ¶11

Marriage: 14 Nov 1773, Innvik parish, as 'Halsten Rasmussen', bachelor, residence Rustøen (Digitalarkivet indexed marriage pv00000000001199). He was therefore living at Rustøen before taking Loen bruk nr. 15 in 1773. The probates after Rasmus Jacobsen Loen (24 Apr 1745, Nordfjord A 9 fol. 251a) and Rasmus Olsen Loen (5 May 1756, A 12 fol. 367a) do not name a son Halsten (worker), so his father should be sought at Rustøen. The Halsten Rasmussen of Møklebust, born 1736 and buried 1777, is a different man. ¶7

Farmer at Loen. With Gunnild Andersdatter he had children baptized 1775 (Marthe), 1778, 1780, 1787 and 1791. Listed in the 1801 census at Loen aged 64, with wife Gunille Andersdatter (53). Probate registered 1808-13. ¶1

Nordfjord skifteprotokoll A 18b (1786-1790), fol. 308b-309a (Digitalarkivet 24238 view 3): at the registration of 9 Dec 1786 at Loen ('Loen i Oldens Skibr., Loens Sogn', estate connected with the late captain Christopher Raun), the sworn appraisers were lagrettemann Ole Faleide and 'Halsten Rasmussen Loen'. This places Halsten at Loen as a lawful court appraiser in 1786; it names no kin. ¶9

Probate: Nordfjord skifteprotokoll A 21b, p. 1033 and pp. 1040-1041 (index pa00000000059071), read from the scan. On 29 May 1809 the estate of the late Halsten (Halsteen) Rasmussen Loen and his wife Gunnild (Gunilde) Andersdatter, both then dead, was registered by the sheriff (lensmand) Rasmus Kierkhorn with the appraisers Mathis Olsen Setten[?] and Truls Andersen Loen. Heirs: 1 son and 4 daughters: Lars Halstensen, of age; Else Catrine Halstensdatter, married to Anders Johannesen Hong[?]; Randi (Randei) Halstensdatter, 21, guardian Mathis Olsen Setten[?]; Marthe Halstensdatter, married to Samuel Guttormsen Loen; Gjertrud (Giertrud) Halstensdatter, 18, guardian Samuel Guttormsen Loen. The probate was closed on 24 Jun 1809, with witnesses Lensmand Kierkhorn and Jens Olsen Beenes[?]. ¶2

Aaland 1932: Loen bruk nr. 15, printed p. 322 (checked on the image): 'Hallstein Rasmusson, † 81 a. g. 1818, br. 1773. Bruket hev namn etter han. * Gunh. Andersd., † 84 a. g. 1834.' He was tenant (brukar) of Loen bruk nr. 15 from 1773 and the holding is still named Hallsteinbruket after him. The owner from 1773 was Guttorm A. Hilde, whose son Samuel Guttormsen married Halsten's daughter Marte and took over the farming in 1802. Aaland gives no father or farm of origin for Halsten. ¶5

Confidence moved Probable to Confirmed on the 1809 probate (A 21b, pp. 1033, 1040-1041). It names him with his wife Gunnild Andersdatter and his children Lars, Else Catrine, Randi, Marthe (wife of Samuel Guttormsen) and Gjertrud. ¶4

Corrections 2

Correction: his death was given as 'BET 1801 AND 1813'. The 1809 probate opened on 29 May 1809 treats him as already dead, so he died after the 1801 census and before 29 May 1809. ¶3

Correction: Aaland's death 'died 81 years old 1818' conflicts with the probate of 29 May 1809 (Nordfjord skifteprotokoll A 21b, pp. 1033, 1040-1041), which treats Halsten and Gunnild as both dead. The contemporaneous probate wins. Aaland's age at death (birth about 1737) does agree with the 1801 census age of 64. ¶6
