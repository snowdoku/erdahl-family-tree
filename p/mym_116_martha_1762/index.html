Life story

Probably Martha Olsdatter, 39, wife of Helge Pedersen at Augdahl, Frosta, in 1801 (inferred from the family pattern). A Johanne Martha Olsdatter buried in Beitstad 14 Oct 1840 with no residence (pg00000006437190) is an unchecked candidate for her burial. ¶2

Mother 'Martha' at the Beitstad baptism of Karen, 10 Oct 1802 (index gives no patronymic). Probable mother of Karen Helgesdatter. Possibly the Martha Olsdatter, 39, wife of Helge Pedersen at Augdahl, Frosta, in 1801 (unproved). Probable: Karen's marriage at Frosta on 3 Feb 1831 gives her birthplace as Bedstad (Beitstad) and her father as 'Helge Peders af Bedstaden' (a second index transcription reads 'Helge, Juberg'); the Beitstad baptism of 10 Oct 1802 of Karen, daughter of Helge and Martha of Fosliepladsen, fits her age (35 in Feb 1838). A rival Karen Helgesdatter baptised at Åmot on 13 Feb 1803 (father Helge Taralsen) does not fit the Beitstad birthplace. The Frosta 1801 couple Helge Pedersen 51 and Martha Olsdatter 39 (Augdahl) may be the same pair but nothing yet ties them to Beitstad; Martha's patronymic is unproved. Both scans (marriage p. 168, baptism p. 219a) are queued for reading. ¶1

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: Confirmed, graded on her link to her daughter Karen Helgesdatter (see her page); her patronymic and origin are still unproved (the Martha Olsdatter of Augdahl, Frosta, in 1801 is only a candidate). The record naming the relationship is the Beitstad baptism of Karen on 10 Oct 1802 (scan p. 219): 'Helge og Martha Fosliepladsens' child Karen. Continuity to the tree's Karen: her 1831 Frosta marriage (scan read) gives her as the daughter of Helge Pedersen of Beitstad, born in Beitstad by a certificate issued 30 Oct 1830, and her 1830 Frosta vaccination record names her father as Helge Peders of Foslien, the same Foslie place as the 1802 baptism. No rival Karen daughter of Helge at Beitstad in the index; the Åmot Karen of 1803 has a father Helge Taralsen. ¶3
