About the family

Mother of Gunder Sivertsen. Named 'Anna Olsdatter' of Snytan as mother at his baptism at Frosta on 22 January 1809. She is written 'Anne Olsdr' at her marriage in 1801 and at her son Siver's baptism in 1803. Source note: the Frosta church book for 1778-1811 (Digitalarkivet source 16379) survives on Digitalarkivet only as a searchable index transcription; no scan is published, so page numbers are not given and the original handwriting could not be checked. ¶1

Parents (Probable): that she was the daughter of Ole Larsen and Magnhild Pedersdatter of Hiellan Øster rests on the 1801 census and on these agreeing facts. The bride of June 1801 was an Anne Olsdatter of the same farm, married four months after the census. One surety at the wedding was Ole of Hillan. At her son Siver's baptism in 1803 the godparents were Magnhild, Mallena, Ole and Lars, all of Hillan, matching her mother, her sister Malena, her father and her brother Lars. In 1809 the godparents Martha and Peder of Hilde match her siblings Martha and Peder. No record names her parents outright; a confirmation entry or a probate after Ole Larsen would settle it. ¶4

Children found: Siver (baptised 20 March 1803, Frosta) and Gunner/Gunder (born 16 January 1809, Frosta). Her death has not been searched. ¶5

Life story

Baptism: 'Anna', daughter of Ole and Magnild Pedersdatter of Hellan, baptised on Oculi Sunday (27 Feb) 1780 (Frosta index). With the 1801 census (Anne Olsdatter 21 as daughter in the Hiellan Øster household of Ole Larsen 45 and Magnhild Pedersdatter 42) and the 1779 marriage of Ole Larsen of Hellan to Magnild Pedersdatter, this names her parents: raised to Confirmed (two independent records). ¶6

Death (identification by parish and age): 'Anne Olsdatter', aged 70 (b. about 1780), died 21 Mar and was buried 4 Apr 1850 at Ekne (Skogn ministerialbok, Ekne 1850-1862, p. 354, no. 1; index pg00000005411509). The entry gives no farm or husband. It fits her: the family had moved from Frosta to Søndre Kjølen in Ekne (her son Gunder lived there from 1838, and her husband Sivert Greisen was buried in the same book, p. 355, in 1858), and the age matches her baptism of Feb 1780 exactly. She is the only Anne Olsdatter born 1776-1786 in the Skogn/Ekne burials apart from a widow of Langaasval, Alstadhaug, born about 1786, buried 1875, while Sivert was dead by then and Anne would have been 95. The Frosta burials of Anne Olsdatters born 1777-1782 (Alstadhaug 1817, Kroglund 1848 'pige', Raaen 1877) do not fit a wife living in Ekne. ¶7

1801 census: on 1 February 1801 Anne Olsdatter, 21, unmarried, was living at Hiellan Øster (Hillan), Frosta, as a daughter of the farmer (gaardmand) Ole Larsen, 45, and his wife Magnhild Pedersdatter, 42. Her birth year of about 1780 is calculated from this age. Her brothers and sisters at home were Malena 19, Lars 16, Peder 14, Martha 12, Mette 9, Gunild 5 and Siver 2. Siver Griesen, 34, a servant on the farm, became her husband. ¶2

Marriage: she married Siver Gresen (Sivert Greisen), landvern dragoon of Hillan, at Loktu church, Frosta, on 4 June 1801. She is described as 'pigen' (unmarried woman) of Hillan. ¶3
