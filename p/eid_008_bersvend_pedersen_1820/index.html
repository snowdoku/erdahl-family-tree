Life story

Baptism: born 27 March 1820 and baptised 3 April 1820, Selbu parish register 1816-1824, births and baptisms pp. 88-89 no. 20. Father Peder Bersvends; mother Gertrud Nilsd, residence Eidem. The index gives his surname as 'Peders Broman'; 'Broman' is unexplained and the scan has not been read. Half-brother of Kari Pedersdatter (see her page). ¶1

1825 census: 'Bersvend Peders., 5 1/2', in Selbu, 0060* Eidem, household 1, with his father Peder Bersvendsen, 36, and his widowed grandmother Kirsten Ingebrigtsdatter, 71. He carries his paternal grandfather Bersvend Pederssen's name, which Selbu custom gives a first son. He is entered here because he is the record that ties the census Peder Bersvendsen to the 1819 groom. ¶2
