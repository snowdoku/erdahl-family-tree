Life story

Death and burial, Probable: 'Gjertrue Pedersd.', aged 1, died 1 June 1823 and buried 8 June 1823 (Selbu 1816-1824, pp. 218-219 no. 10; parallel transcription pg00000007095968); no residence in the index. Name, age and date fit this child; she died twelve weeks before her mother. Half-sister of Kari Pedersdatter. ¶2

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 record that disagrees

Records that disagree 1

Baptism: baptised 27 January 1822 in Selbu, residence Eidem, father Peder Bersvendsen. Two transcriptions of the parallel church books disagree: Selbu 1816-1831, pp. 152-153 has 'Giertru Peders Datt.', born 7 January, mother 'Giertru Nielsdatt'; Selbu 1816-1824 has 'Gjertrue Pedersd Eidem', born 17 January 1822, mother 'Kjersten Nils D'. Index only; scans not read. Peder's wife from 1819 to 1823 was Giertru Nielsdatter, and no Kjersten Nielsdatter is known as his wife, so the 'Kjersten' reading is probably a transcription or clerical slip; the mother link is Probable until the scan is read. ¶1
