About the family

Widow of Trond Olsen of vestre Felle, Treungen. Called old and aged ('gamle og bedagede') on 22 Jul 1721, when she divided her property at Vester Felle among her children Tore, Alf, Johannes, Halvor and Jens Trondsønner and Torbiør Trondsdatter (Øvre Telemark skifteprotokoll no. 3, fol. 456a; card DA 6856 image 244). Her son Alph Trondsen's 1731 probate calls the land he got by this skifte his 'Moders arv'. Her son Halvor was probably the Halvor Trondsen Ormestad, lensmann, of Alph's 1731 probate; she may have named him after her own father Halvor (inferred). ¶1

1 open question

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Lead: on the same day as her own division, 22 Jul 1721, the court held a skifte at vestre Felle after 'afg. Inger Halvorsdatter' (Øvre Telemark skifteprotokoll no. 3 fol. 453b-454a, DA 25692 view 466; card 6856/243): widower Tollef Haavarsen; children Ole and Torbior Svenningsbørn by her first husband Svenning Alfsøn (Ole guardian for his sister) and Haavar Tollefsen by the second. The entry speaks of odelsgods in Felle in the estate. Ingeri Halvorsdatter of the same farm, divided the same day, was very probably Gunvor's sister, which would make their father a Halvor who held odel in Felle; the entry gives no kin term, so no father is added. The Felle run in card index 6856 (views 243-254) has no card from the 1689-1703 protocols. ¶2
