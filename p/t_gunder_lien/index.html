Knud Gundersen LienProbable

Life story

Known only from his son's patronymic. His son Knud Gundersøn was living at the farm Lien in the Voll annex of Grytten parish, Romsdal, when he married Elri Sørensdatter there on 28 Oct 1751, so Gunder was probably born in the first quarter of the 1700s and lived in the Grytten or Voll area. Nothing else about him has been found; the Grytten register begins only in 1725 and the surviving pre-1750 pages do not identify him. ¶1

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 alternative set aside

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Rejected candidate: the 1701 male census has a 'Gunder Ols.', aged 9 (born about 1692), son of the tenant Oluf Sifuersen (born about 1654) at a farm Lien, with brothers Sifuer (1685) and Elling (1689). The census page puts this Lien under the bailiff (fogd) of Nordmøre, while Grytten, where Knud Gundersøn lived at Lien in 1751, was in Romsdal. Lien is a common farm name, so this is a different farm and no parent has been added for Gunder. His father is still unknown; the next records are the Grytten church book 1725-1763 burials for Lien and the Romsdal probate index. ¶2
