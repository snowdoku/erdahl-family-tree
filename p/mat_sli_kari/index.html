About the family

Oline Slind's mother. Baptised in Selbu on 25 July 1847 as 'Karen', daughter of Ole Johnsen and Marit Haavelsdatter of Stokke - entered at page 39 serial 78 of the Selbu local parish register 1843-1859 and at page 153 serial 76 of the clerk's duplicate, where the mother is written 'Marte Halderd'. The 1865 census gives her mother's patronymic as Haldosdatter, and the eldest son of the Stokke household is called Haldo, which is the Selbu naming custom confirming it. ¶1

Life story

Printed source: her birth year 1847 stands with her husband's 1844 and all seven children's on page 100 of Selbu i fortid og naatid, bind 7 (1992), SLINDAN NORDRE chapter. Her own birth farm is written up separately in volume 6: see the note on her parents. ¶6

Gravestone, Selbu Kirke Cemetery, Mebonden, Selbu, Section A, Row 03, No. 016: 'Kari O. Slind, 20 JUL 1847 - 23 MAY 1935'. This is a near-perfect convergence of three independent sources on a date that wave 3 could only bracket. Wave 3 reasoned from her baptism on 25 July 1847 that she must have been born in the first three weeks of July 1847 and entered ABT 1847; the stone says 20 JULY 1847, five days before the baptism. And the Selbu gaardshistorie's 23 May 1935 is the stone's death date exactly. ¶11

Her birth date is therefore 20 July 1847 and her baptism five days later, which is unusually prompt even for Selbu and suggests a weak child. ¶13

1865 census: 'Kari Olsdatter, 19, Datter paa Gaarden' in the household of her father Ole Johnsen at Stokke, Selbu - a holding worked by its owner, with fifteen cows, fifteen sheep, sixteen goats and two horses. She married into a tenancy: her husband's family at Slinden were leilendinger, leaseholders, and never owned their land. ¶3

Her farm is named exactly: MIDTI-STOKKAN, the MIDDLE Stokke holding, which is the bruk her father Ole Johnsen held as a freeholder (lopenr 150 in 1865, residence 0147 in 1825). The censuses call it only 'Stokke'. ¶8

Marriage: 17 July 1871 in Selbu to Gunder Olsen of Slind. She bore at least seven children between 1872 and 1890, of whom Oline, born 1881, and Ole, born 1884, both emigrated to Marietta, Lac qui Parle County, Minnesota. ¶4

Widowed between 1910 and 1920. The 1920 census finds her at Slind nordre as 'hp e' - head of household, widow - 'Driver bygslet gaard', working the leased farm herself at the age of seventy-three, with her unmarried daughter Marit the knitter and her son Gunder, recorded in 1910 as 'udygtig til erhverv', unfit for gainful work, still in the house. Her birth date is there given as 28 July 1847. ¶5

Her birth date is given three different ways and none of them is her own testimony. The 1920 census says 28 July 1847, which falls three days after her own baptism and so cannot be right. The 1910 census says 28 June 1846. Her age in the 1865 census implies 1846, while the marriage entry and the 1891 and 1900 censuses say 1847. The baptism of 25 July 1847 is the only primary date, so she was born in the first three weeks of July 1847. ¶2

Her status in the 1920 census - 'Driver bygslet gaard', works a leased farm - is therefore the last year of the tenancy, not a permanent condition. ¶10

SHE BOUGHT THE FARM. The Nedpaa-Garden narrative on page 98 of the same volume reads, in reconstruction, 'Enka Kari... Slind kjopte Nedpaa-Garden av... 1921 for... og... bruket til sonnen...' - the widow Kari Slind BOUGHT Nedpaa-Garden in 1921 and conveyed the holding to her son. That is the moment four generations of leaseholding ended: her husband, his father, his grandfather and his great-grandfather had all farmed Slind land on bygsel without owning a foot of it, and the widow bought the freehold five years after she was left with it. The holder list's next line is '1921-1963 Gunder... Slind, f. 1887', her son. ¶9

HER DEATH DATE, from the Selbu gaardshistorie: 23 MAY 1935, aged eighty-seven. Selbu i fortid og naatid vol. 7, p. 100: 'G. 1871 m. Kari... fra Midti-Stokkan, f. 1847, d. 23.5.1935.' Wave 3 had no death date at all; the 1920 census was the last trace of her. Rated Probable because a gaardshistorie is a published reconstruction; the Selbu burial register for 1935 would confirm it. ¶7

Correction, and it explains a contradiction wave 3 recorded without being able to resolve: the 1910 census's '28 June 1846' for her is a DIFFERENT WOMAN. Selbu kirkegaard holds a second 'Kari O. Slind, 28 JUN 1847 - 19 MAR 1936' in Section G. The 1910 enumerator, or the transcriber, took one Kari Olsdatter Slind's date for the other's. The 1920 census's '28 July 1847', three days after her own baptism, is a separate slip of the same kind. ¶12
