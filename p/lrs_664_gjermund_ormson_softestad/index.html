Life story

Bygdebok (Nissedal sokn, Søftestad, canvas 451): 'Gjermund Ormson [..] kom [til Søftestad] etter Jørgen'; he married a daughter of Jørgen (the previous holder; the 1664 vicar's census lists 'Jørgen 60' as the farmer of Søftestad, 35508 view 77 left). 'Born: Sigrid [g. m. 1.] Tellev Tallakson, [2.] Gunstein Olavson'. 'Gjermund døydde [omlag] 1676. Kona hans vart gift [att] med Torjus Ulvson'; they moved away. Gjermund Ormson is also named on canvases 472 (Nes) and 690. Dates ABT. ¶1

The bygdebok (canvas 451, re-harvested) gives him as 'Gjermund Ormson Nigard-Fjone', i.e. from Nigard-Fjone, and her first husband as 'Tellev Tallakson Grovum-Fjone'. Test record moved: Sigrid Gjermundsdatter's probate (she died 1701 per the book) belongs in Øvre Telemark østfjelske skifteprotokoll no. 4, 1700-1703 (25699), most likely in the autumn-1701 sessions at views 55-85 (skifter Nos 34-57) or the Nissedal session of 7 Oct 1702 (view 110 on). located that Nissedal session (view 110: 'Octobris d. 7de Var paa Tvedten Nordre i Nissedals annex udi Hvideseid præstegield', skifte after Kari Aslachsdatter) but ran out of budget before reading views 56-84 entry by entry. ¶4a

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
