About the family

Father of Gunder Sivertsen. Named as 'Hmd. Sivert Greisen Kjølen' (Hmd. probably husmand, cottager) in the column for the groom's father at Gunder Sivertsen's second marriage, Ekne, 5 September 1847. The patronymic is spelled as written. 'Greisen' is probably a contracted form of a name such as Gregersen and has not been interpreted further. ¶1

Life story

Baptism: probably 'Sivert', son of 'Greis' of Storleinsvallet, Frosta, baptised on Almindelig Bededag (the general day of prayer) 1768. Parents linked at Probable: Probable, not proved: Sivert (b. about 1767: aged 34 in 1801, 91 at burial 1858) fits the baptism of 'Sivert', son of 'Greis' of Storleinsvallet, Frosta, on Almindelig Bededag 1768 (no mother indexed). found Greis Hansen the only Greis in the 1801 census of Frosta (58, cottager at Loksteen, b. about 1743). Greis Hansen of Vigleeret married Gurru Pedersdatter in 1766 and had a son Jens there in 1776; a 'Jens Greisson' stood godfather to Sivert's son Siver in 1803 and a 'Greis' of Vigeleret to Sivert's son Gunder in 1809. The 1768 farm (Storleinsvallet) differs from Vigleeret, so the identification rests on the unique given name, age and the godparents. ¶10

1801 census: on 1 February 1801 'Siver Griesen', aged 34 (born about 1767), unmarried, a servant (tiener) and landvern (militia) soldier, lived in the household of the farmer Ole Larsen at Hiellan Øster (Hjelle, written Hillan in the church book), Frosta. Anne Olsdatter, 21, the farmer's daughter, was in the same household. ¶4

Marriage: on 4 June 1801 at Loktu church, Frosta, 'Siver Gresen', landvern dragoon (Ldv: Dragoun) and bachelor of Hillan, married Anne Olsdr, unmarried woman (pigen) of Hillan. The sureties (trulovere) were Ole of Hillan, probably the bride's father, and a vagtmester (cavalry sergeant) of Hogstad whose name is not indexed. Source note: the Frosta church book for 1778-1811 (Digitalarkivet source 16379) survives on Digitalarkivet only as a searchable index transcription; no scan is published, so page numbers are not given and the original handwriting could not be checked. ¶5

1803: as 'Siver Greisson', inderst (lodger) at Hillan, he and Anne Olsdr had a son Siver baptised at Loktu on 20 March 1803. Jens Greisson stood godfather. ¶6

1809: as 'Sivert Greysen' of Snytan he and Anna Olsdatter had a son Gunner (Gunder) born on 16 January and baptised on 22 January 1809 at Loktu, Frosta. ¶7

The spellings Greisen, Gresen, Griesen, Greisson and Greysen are forms of one patronymic, 'son of Greis' (Greis is probably a short form of Gregers; this is an interpretation, not stated in any record). In the Frosta baptism index 1778-1811 only two men carry it as fathers: Siver Greisson (1803) and Sivert Greysen (1809). Both had a wife Anne/Anna Olsdatter, so they are one man. In the 1801 census of Frosta he is the only Siver or Sivert with this patronymic. ¶8

His age of 91 makes him about 42 when his son Gunder was born. A Mette Sivertsdatter of Kjølen, unmarried, stood godmother to Gunder's daughter Hanna in 1838. She may have been another of his children, but nothing proves it. ¶3

Death: 'Kaarmand Sivert Greisen', aged 91, of Søndre Kjølen, died on 10 June 1858 and was buried at Ekne on 17 June 1858 (Ekne Begravede 1858, p. 355, no. 2). A kårmann is a man retired on a pension from the farm. That he is the same man as the 1847 groom's father is Probable, from the same uncommon name and the same farm, Søndre Kjølen, where Gunder lived from 1838. To settle it, find a record of Sivert Greisen at Frosta about 1809, such as a baptism of his children, which needs the Frosta church book before 1827, or the 1801 census of Frosta (searching Sivert b. 1762-72 there found only Sivert Jonssen of Hallum, Skogn). ¶2

1 on the grade

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Confidence raised from Probable to Confirmed. That he was Gunder Sivertsen's father is stated in two separate primary records: the 1847 Ekne marriage names Sivert Greisen of Kjølen as the groom's father, and the 1809 Frosta baptism names Sivert Greysen of Snytan as the father of the child Gunner. Snytan was where Gunder himself lived in 1831-33. The burial of 'Kaarmand Sivert Greisen', aged 91, at Søndre Kjølen in June 1858 fits the 1801 census age of 34 exactly (both give birth in 1767), and Søndre Kjølen is the farm to which Gunder moved in 1838. One point remains an inference: no record names the 1858 decedent as Gunder's father. The 1865-era Ekne records or a Skogn probate (skifte) for 1858 would settle it. ¶9
