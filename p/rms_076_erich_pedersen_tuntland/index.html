About the family

Father of Peder Erichsen of Tuntland (Probable): his only heir in 1742 was his son Peder Erichsen, aged 12 (born about 1730), who inherited land in Tuntland. Peder Erichsen was holder of a share of Tuntland in the 1762 ekstraskatt, and his estate still held Tuntland land in 1782. The same name, the same farm, the land and the age fit, and Peder named his eldest son Erich (b. about 1750). The link rests on that identity, not on a record that names both generations together, so it is Probable. Added. ¶2

Parents found: the probate after Peder Erichsen of Tuntland, 27 Sep 1725 (Ryfylke D 4, DA 15920, fol. 246b), names as first of six children 'Erich Pedersen', of age, who had built houses on Tuntland and received his brother's share in Tuntland land; the widow was Anna Danielsdatter. Of age in 1725, so born before about 1705. Father Probable (identity by name, farm and land), mother Speculative (possibly a stepmother). His son Peder Erichsen (b. about 1730) carries the grandfather's name. ¶3

[1725], g m Anna Danielsdtr. f ca. 1658', children Anna ca. 1689, Kari ca. 1690, Erik ca. 1696, Anna, Ingrid. Grade unchanged (Probable); this is a compiled source. Skipreide confirmed: the card index DA 39924 to 'Ryfylke (eldre) Pantebok nr. 4, Hemnes 1754-1775' (DA 23017) files Hjelmeland farms (e.g. 'Tøtland i Hjelmeland', view 1211; 'Tuntland i Hjelmeland'), so Tuntland was in Hemnes jurisdiction and this is the right volume. Only three Tuntland cards exist: fol. 161, lodseddel after Anne Johannesdatter Askvik, 22 Oct 1762 (shares between Sjur Birkvik and Johannes Dahle); fol. 200b, 're Birke...', skifte after John Larsen; fol. 231, skjøte 4 Nov 1767 from Iver Olsen Herre (Jæren) and Kleng Pedersen Birke to Jacob Olsen Vige on 12 ? marklag. None names Peder Erichsen or Erich Pedersen, so the deed by which Peder held Tuntland is not in pantebok 4 (1754-75). ¶10a

1701 manntall (index): Ryfylke fogderi, Hjelmeland, 'Thuntland', p. 177: 'Erich Pedersen', søn, aged 5, at home with his father Peder Erichsen, 52. Born about 1696; so aged about 29 at his father's probate in 1725 (of age, as stated) and about 34 when his son Peder was born about 1730. ¶6

Probate (scan): Ryfylke skifteprotokoll D 9 (DA 23886), the 'Extract' of probates held in Hemnes sorenskriveri and Ryfylke fogderi in 1742, fol. 118a, no. 3, Hjelmeland skipreide: 'Anno [1742] 31te January blev Skifte holden paa Tuntland efter afgangne Mand Erich Pedersen'. The heirs' column names one child only: 'den afdødes Søn Peder Erichsen, 12 aar gammel'. His inheritance is entered as movable and real property ('udi Løst og fast gods'), about 23 rd. The guardian column names two men whose names are not fully legible on the binarised scan. The typed register to the Ryfylke designations 1731-1754 (DA 23971) lists the same probate as 'Tuntland i Hjelmeland, Erick Pedersen, 31/1 1742', fol. 25b and fol. 118. He died before 31 Jan 1742. ¶1

Guardians in 1742: the designation copy of his probate, D 9 (DA 23886) fol. 25b-26a, Hjelmeland no. 9 (view 28), repeats '31te January... paa gaarden Tuntland... afdøde Erich Pedersen', heir 'den afdødes Søn Peder Erichsen 12 Aar gammel', inheritance about 23 rd in movable and real property. The guardians' column, fully visible there (it is cut by the binding on fol. 118a), reads 'Aanen [?Opsangen] og [?Niels] Valle': two men, with no kinship stated. So it does not name Peder's uncles. ¶4

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

(near-miss attempt, not finished; grade unchanged). DA 39924 is not a register in the pantebok: it is a 20th-century card index to 'Ryfylke (eldre) Pantebok nr. 4, Hemnes 1754-1775' (= DA 23017), filed by farm with 2 cards per view, each card citing the pantebok folio. Sampled: view 6 Åbø (start), 1150 Sørde, 1252-1258 Vatn/Vatnekvam, 1264-1270 Vegge/Vetle, 1275 Vestbø, 1320 Vorre. So T farms lie between views about 1150 and 1250, not yet read. The Hemnes cards seen are for Sauda/Suldal/Vats farms (Sørde, Vegge, Vatne). Check that Tuntland (Hjelmeland) belongs to Hemnes skipreide before reading further: if not, the right volume is the Hjelmeland/Jelsa pantebok of the same years. ¶9a

The case for this identification 1

Re-reading of his father's probate: D 4 fol. 246b, 27 Sep 1725, names him as the only son among six children 'sammen avlede' by Peder Erichsen and Anna Danielsdatter, of age; his sisters were Anna the elder (m. Ole [?]), Britta (m. Peder [?Braaten]), Ingre (married), Anna the younger (m. [?Rasmus] Osmundsen) and Kari (unmarried). As the only son he took the brother's share in Tuntland land. ¶5

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Near-miss attempt, not raised: the Hjelmeland baptism of about 1750-52 that REGRADE-4 asked for cannot exist on Digitalarkivet: the earliest Hjelmeland church book is source 1599 (1760-1799); the source list for Hjelmeland has no church book before 1760. So the 1742-1762 gap cannot be closed from baptisms. Remaining tests: the Hemnes mortgage book 1754-1775 (DA 23017) and mortgage register (DA 39924) under Tuntland, for the deed by which Peder became owner of the whole holding about 1760 (bygdebok), which may recite his inheritance after his father Erich Pedersen; and the court journal Bb 51 (1751-54, DA 39975). Grade unchanged. ¶8
