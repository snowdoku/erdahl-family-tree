Life story

Baptisms: Amund Gulbrandsen of Hvinden, Gran parish, Hadeland, and his wife Kirsti Amundsdatter had children baptised at Gran: Birthe on 24 Jun 1733, Anne on 26 Jun 1735, Gulbrand on 20 Apr 1738 and Hans on 5 Feb 1741 (Gran church book 1732-1744, index by GDS-Hadeland). His birth year is inferred only from his first known child (1733); his own baptism, marriage and death have not been searched. ¶1

Probate (index card): the estate of 'Ammund Gulbrandsen' of Hvinden in Gran was held 29 Apr 1751 (Hadeland, Land og Valdres skifteprotokoll 1744-52, fol. 325b), with his wife Kirsti Amundsdatter. Children: Gulbrand 13, Hans 10, Amund 4, Marie 20, Beret 18, Anne 16. Gross 1323-3-6, net 1190-0-22 rdr. He held 3 skippund of odel land. The ages fit the Gran baptisms of Birthe 1733, Anne 1735, Gulbrand 1738 and Hans 1741. The card does not name his parents. ¶3

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: the protocol entry itself was read (Hadeland, Land og Valdres skifteprotokoll 1783-1802, fol. 305a-305b). 'Skifte Forretning paa Gaarden Hvinden i Grans Sogn efter Gulbrand Ammundsen', 16 Apr 1789, widow Ellie Arnesdatter; children Hans Gulbransen 16, Anders Gulbransen 12, Kirsti 28, Anne 23, Kari 21, Abigael 9 and Elline 7 1/2. On fol. 305b the guardian appointed for the son Hans and the two youngest daughters Abigael and Elline is 'deres Farbroder Ammund Ammundsen' (their father's brother Amund Amundsen). A son Amund (4 in 1751, 8 1/2 in 1754) appears at both the 1751 estate of Amund Gulbrandsen of Hvinden and the 1754 estate of Kirsti Amundsdatter of Hvinden, beside Gulbrand (13 and 16 1/2). The protocol thus makes the Gulbrand Amundsen who died in 1789 the brother of Amund Amundsen, which, with the same farm (Hvinden), the same 3 skippund holding and the ages, settles that he is the Gulbrand of the 1751 and 1754 estates. Amund Gulbrandsen is therefore Confirmed as the father of Gulbrand Amundsen (raised from Probable). His own parents remain unknown. ¶4
