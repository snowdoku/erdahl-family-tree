About the family

His father was probably named Erich (patronymic; the twin Erich follows the custom of naming a son after the paternal grandfather). ¶2

Life story

Baptism of twin sons (scan read, zoomed): 'Aug: 8de Døbt i Weedøen Kirke Ole Erichsen Wiikes og Hustrues Berrit Lasse datter 2de Tvillinger begge Drænge-børn, Nafnlig: Erich og Lasse' = 8 Aug 1741, baptised in Veøy church: twins, both boys, of Ole Erichsen Wiike and his wife Berrit Lassesdatter, named Erich and Lasse. Ten godparents (*faddere*), most of Wiike: Ingebor Nielsdatter Stad[?], Marith Pedersdatter Wiike, Ole Olsen Bierke[?], Lars Knudsen Wiike, Morten Andersen Wiike, Synov[?] Børgesdatter Wesnes, Marith [?]datter Wiike, Christopher Pedersen Gjelland, Lasse Thorsen[?] Wesnes and Niels Nielsen Wiike. Veøy ministerialbok 1721-1764, IIIF canvas 108, right page (printed p. 215). ¶1

Tested: the probate after Bridt Erichsdatter of Wiiche, 8 Mar 1797 (Romsdal skifteprotokoll 3A 12, 1789-1802, fol. 259b-260a, scan read), lists only her own children by a Johan: Hans Johansen 11, Kari Johansdatter 17, Marithe Johansdatter and Bridt Johansdatter. It names no siblings, so it says nothing about Ole Erichsen Wiike or Anders Olsen. Link stays Speculative. ¶5a

How sure are we?

Speculative. A plausible candidate only. Treat this link as a question, not a fact.

1 on the grade · 1 alternative set aside · 1 open question

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Speculative father of Anders Olsen Vike (b. c. 1734, later farmer at Wiike). For: Anders held Wiike in 1801; Anders's eldest son was named Ole, a daughter Bereth and a son Lasse (b. 1784), which matches the custom of naming children after grandparents Ole and Berrit and after Berrit's father Lasse; this family had a son Lasse in 1741. Against or unproved: no baptism of an Anders son of Ole Wiike was found. Searched Jan 1733-Oct 1734 (canvases 66 right-73); the only Anders baptisms found were sons of Just paa Laven (Oct 1733), Joen Darø[?] (Feb 1734) and Siur[?] Vesnes (Aug 1734). Hands before Aug 1741 do not name mothers. Wiike had several households (godparents of four other Wiike surnames in 1741), so another Ole at Wiike could be Anders's father. 1732, Nov 1734-1736 and 1728-31 not read; no marriage of Ole Erichsen and Berrit Lassesdatter found (only Jan 1733-Oct 1734 marriages seen; an Ole Erichsøn Fren[?] who married Jertrud Povelsdatter in 1734 is a different man). ¶3

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Ruled out: the probate of Bridt Eriksdatter of Vike, Romsdal skifteprotokoll 3A 12, s. 260a, 18 Jan 1797 (source 24621, view 278, scan read), is not that of a sister of Ole Erichsen. Her heirs are her own children by a Johannes (Hans Johannesen 11, Erich Johannesen, Kari, Marithe and Bridt Johannesdatter), so she was born about the 1750s, a generation after Ole Erichsen, who had twins in 1741. ¶4
