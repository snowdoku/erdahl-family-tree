Life story

Baptized 5 Nov 1809, Innvik, as Martha. Buried 7 Jul 1858, aged 49. ¶1

She would have been about 47 when daughter Anne was baptized in 1857; worth checking against the original page. ¶3

Death register, 1857: named as 'Marte Samuelsdatter, about 40, married to Lasse Jørgensen Erdal', a daughter and heir of Marte Halstensdatter Loen (died 16 Feb 1857), in the Stryn og Innvik lensmann death register 1856-1861, 1857 no. 3. This confirms her parentage. ¶4
