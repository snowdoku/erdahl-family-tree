Life story

1801 census, Bale (Balestrand): Mons Erichsen, 54, inderst (lodger), married twice, with his wife Anna Hermundsdatter, 47, and children Erich 15, Ingeborg 13 (b. ~1788), Anders 6 and Hermund 3. This Ingeborg Monsdatter is the only one of the right age found nearby in 1801. Linking her to the Ingeborg of Esse (b. ~1789-90) rests only on name and age. A second Ingeborg Monsdatter (b. ~1786, buried 5 Nov 1841, Leikanger) also existed. ¶1
