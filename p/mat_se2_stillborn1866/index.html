Life story

A stillborn daughter, Dodfodt (stillborn), 6 November 1866, buried from Selbu church the same year. Selbu parish register 1860-1877, burials page 22; duplicate in the 1859-1868 volume, burials page 193. The entry gives no name, only the gender (k, female) and the position 'Dodfodt', and names the parents as paaroerande (next of kin): 'Poul Ingebrigtsen, Hsmd.' (husmand, cottager), RESIDENCE EIDEM, and 'Karen Pedersdr', residence Eidem. ¶1

This entry is what proves that Braendhaugen was an Eidem holding and not a farm of its own: the pastor wrote the family's address as plain 'Eidem'. ¶2

She is the sixth confinement and the only one that did not live. She is not among the 'five children' of the 1904 Minnesota biography, which counted the living. ¶3
