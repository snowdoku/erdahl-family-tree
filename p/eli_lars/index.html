Life story

Baptized 27 Oct 1816. Confirmed 1833 at Meråker. ¶1

Birth and baptism read from the register page itself (Hegra annex, 1816, page 69, entry no. 45 in the women's column): born 16 October 1816, baptized 27 October 1816. Her parents are entered as 'Husm: Lars Erichsen Aemlitrøen' - husmand, a crofter at Almlitrøen - and Lisbet Jonsdatter. ¶4

Her godparents were Mali Fossen, Sigri Fossen, Marit Almli, Ole Almli and Ole Fossen - neighbours from the Almli and Fossen holdings. ¶5

She bore six children between 1848 and 1862, all baptized in Hegra: Ole, Beret Martha, Lisbeth, Lorentz, Matias and Kirstine. Through all of them she is entered as 'hustru' - a married woman - living on one Beitland croft after another. ¶7

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgraded to Confirmed. Her parentage is now proven by a primary record and not only inferred: the entry for her marriage on 18 June 1846 names the bride as Eli Larsdatter, born 1817 in Stjørdal, then living at Husebye, and gives her father as Lars Eriksen. ¶3

Records that disagree 1

Find a Grave gives 14 Oct 1816; the church book gives 16 Oct. ¶2

Corrections 1

Correction, refined: Find a Grave gives her birth as 14 Oct 1816 and the church book gives 16 Oct. The two are not independent. Her gravestone states her age as '76 yr, 7 mo, 25 day'; counted back from her death on 8 June 1893 that arithmetic lands exactly on 14 October 1816. So the American date is the family's own reckoning, two days adrift from the Norwegian register. The register, written eleven days after the birth, is the better authority. ¶6

Searches that found nothing 1

Emigration, negative: no out-migration entry for her, her husband Thomas Olsen or their children was found in the Hegra parish register's out-migration list for 1863 to 1866 (folios 175-177), read row by row. ¶9
