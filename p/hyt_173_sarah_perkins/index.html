Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Speculative candidate for the mother of Thomas Jolls; graded on that link. Married Thomas Jolls at Stepney (St Dunstan) 9 Sep 1641 (FreeREG, Other Transcript); named as 'Sarah' or 'Sara', mother of the Ratcliff Jolls children baptised 1642-1651 (see Thomas Jolls), including Thomas bp 17 Jun 1642. Speculative for the same reason as her husband: nothing yet ties Capt. Thomas Jolls of Boston to this baptism. ¶1
