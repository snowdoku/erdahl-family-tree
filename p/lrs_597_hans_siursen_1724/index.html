About the family

Father of Ole Hansen of Langager (see his page), Probable: Ole Hansen, 3, is in his household on the farm Ole farmed in 1801, at a matching age, and Ole named a son Hans. 'Siursen' = son of Siur (Sjur/Sigurd). ¶2

Life story

In the 1758 soul register of Skudenes parish he heads a household on Langager (Ferkingstad annex) as 'Hans Siursen gal:' (gardmann, farmer), aged 34 (born about 1724, inferred), with Engebor Olsdatter, 26, evidently his wife, and the children Ole Hansen 3 and Marte Hansdatter 5. Read from the scan (p. 17). ¶1

Bygdebok: Bygdebok for Karmøy: Skudenes og Skudeneshavn (1982), Langåker bnr. 7, canvas 130: user no. 6, '17[..]-1780: Hans Sjursen, ca. 1724, d. ca. 1785', married Ingeborg Olsdtr., daughter of the previous user Ola Anderssen; children Marta ca. 1753, Ola ca. 1755 ('brukar', no. 7: 'Ola Hanssen, ca. 1755, son til førre... G.m. Siri Jakobsdtr., ca. 1757'; children Jakob 1784, Hans 1788, Jon 1794, Ingeborg Malene ca. 1796) and Torbjørn ca. 1764. Hans and Ingeborg took over after Ola and Malene, and Hans held the lease until his son Ola took over in 1780. The book gives no parents or origin for Hans. Secondary source; his grade (Probable, on his link to Ole Hansen) is unchanged. ¶3

Death: his estate was registered on 23 Feb 1785 (D 7b fol. 385a), so he died shortly before; the bygdebok's 'd. ca. 1785' agrees. Net estate only 3 rd 3 ort 4 s. His own origin (patronymic Siursen) is still open; no Langager probate in the card index names his parents. ¶6

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed, graded on his link to Ole Hansen. The Ryfylke probate card index 1774-1788 (source 39936, view 866) has a card 'Langaker i Stangeland, side 385, 1785 23/2, + Hans Sjursen g. Ingborg Olsdatter; Børn: 1. Ole, myndig; 2. Torbjørn 21 år; 3. Marthe 31 år' (net estate 3 rd 3 ort 4 s). The card has labelled fields (deceased, widow, 'Børn'), and the protocol entry it cites was read: D 7b (source 23892) fol. 385a (view 98), 'Anno 1785 den 23 Februarii', registration and valuation on the farm Langager in Stangeland skipreide after 'Hans Siursen' (underlined), between the widow Ingeborg Olsdatter and their children; the binarised scan is hard to read beyond the heading, but the names and ages agree with the card. Continuity to Ole in the tree: 1758 soul register, Langager, Hans Siursen 34 with 'Ole Hansen 3' and 'Marte Hansdatter 5'; 1785 probate, Langager, son Ole (of age, so 25 or more: b. by 1760) and daughter Marthe 31 (b. ~1754, matching Marte 5 in 1758); 1788, Ole Hansen of Langager has son Hans (c.1840 household register: 'Gaardm. Ole Hansen Langager'); 1801 census, Langager, Ole Hansen 45. Same farm throughout, no gap over 3-13 years from 1785. ¶5a

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Rival: the only other Hansen household on Langager (Peder Hansen, d. 1780, card 865b) had children Hans, Tore and Asbjørn, no Ole; no rival Ole Hansen is named. The card lists the children without a split by marriage (the indexer writes 'Børn av 1 gifte' where there were two), so all three were children of Hans and Ingeborg. ¶5b
