About the family

Sister of Severt S. Eidem, so Mayme (Eidem) Eidem's paternal aunt. She married a Thomson and is recorded as Sigri Sivertsdtr 'Sarah' Eidem Thomson. Linked as a sibling on Severt's Find a Grave memorial. ¶1

Life story

Baptism: baptised in Selbu on 17 JUNE 1849 - Selbu parish register, Selbu local parish 1842-1858, births and baptisms, PAGE 166, SERIAL NO. 58. Child 'Sigri'; father 'Severt Johnsen'; mother 'Brynild Johnsd'. (The indexed residence reads 'Culion', which is one of this transcription's several manglings of Eidem - see the dossier.) ¶2

She is the SECOND Sigri of this marriage. An elder sister, 'Sigrie' / 'Segri Severtsdatter Eidem', was baptised 27 October 1844 and must have died in infancy, because only one Sigri is in the 1865 household and Norwegian families did not duplicate a living child's name. ¶3

Her Find a Grave year of 1849 is therefore right and the baptism supplies the month; the exact day is on the scanned page of the Selbu 1842-1858 register, page 166 entry 58. ¶5

1865 census: person 006 at oestre Eidem, Selbu - 'Sigri Sivertsdatter', aged 17, 'Deres Datter' (their daughter), unmarried. The age is a year high in the usual way of this enumerator and is consistent with a June 1849 birth. ¶4

She emigrated with the family as a girl of sixteen or seventeen, married a Thomson in America and died in 1921. ¶6

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
