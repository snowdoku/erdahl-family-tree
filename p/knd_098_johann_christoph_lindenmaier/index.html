About the family

Father of Nicolaus Lindenmaier (see his page) of Bietigheim. ¶1a

Mother (Speculative): Anna Maria, on the baptism of 26 Jun 1671 (Hanß Christoph, son of Hanß Christoph x Anna Maria, record 7267002). The rival named (the 'Herr Joh Christoph Lindenmaÿer' who married Maria Löffler on 19 Aug 1694, 7269987) may be the father himself as a widower: an 'Anna Maria', relative 'Christoph', was buried at Bietigheim on 12 Mar 1694 (7275179, image 1184899-00493); the 1694 groom row names no father (as for a widower); and 'Herr Christoph Lindenmaÿer' was buried on 16 Dec 1694 (7275242), two years before the 1696 wedding of the son, whose row still names his father Johann Christoph. That sequence fits one older man (wife d. Mar 1694, remarried Aug 1694, d. Dec 1694), but the March burial carries no surname, so the rival is not excluded and the link is Speculative. His own grade (on Nicolaus) is unchanged. ¶6

Life story

Occupation and residence (Landesarchiv Baden-Württemberg finding aid): a 'examinierter Feldtmesser [sworn land surveyor] Johan Christoph Lindenmajer von Biettigh[eim]' drew a map for the ducal Rentkammer dated 29 Sep 1721 (HStA Stuttgart A 249 Bü 270), and a Lindenmaier, Feldmesser zu Bietigheim, is credited by the archivist with a tithe map of [1731] (A 249 Bü 268 Qu. 40; the attribution is in brackets, so an archivist's inference). A surveyor of standing fits the title 'Hn' (Herr) given to Nicolaus's father in the 1737 marriage. The finding aid does not say which Johann Christoph Lindenmaier he was; the man of 1721 was an adult practising surveyor, which fits the 1696 groom (born about 1671) and not the Johann Christoph, son of Johann Christoph, who married in 1737. It shows a Johann Christoph Lindenmaier living at Bietigheim inside the 1704-1737 gap discussed under Agnes Johanna Jenisch, but it does not by itself tie the 1704 child Nicolaus to the 1737 groom, so no grade changes. ¶9

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 4

Graded on that link: the groom row of Nicolaus's own marriage at Metterzimmern on 3 Sep 1737 reads 'Nicolaus Lindenmajer', relatives 'Hn Johann Christoph, Catharina' (father, then bride; record 5517933, image 1184926-00850). Continuity: the same couple, Nicolaus x Catharina, baptise children at Bietigheim from 5 Dec 1738; no other Nicolaus Lindenmaier is indexed at Bietigheim in the 18th century. 'Hn' (Herr) marks a man of standing. Images not seen; graded on the faithfully transcribed index row under the overnight rule for this collection. ¶1b

Coordinator review: held at Probable rather than Confirmed. Ancestry collection 61023 index rows do not label who is father, mother or spouse; the BRIEF audit 5 rule is that unlabelled index rows give Probable until the scan is read, or a FamilySearch index with labelled parent fields is found (queued S0233). The naming evidence in the note above is otherwise strong, so this person is a priority for an image read. ¶3

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed, graded on his link to his son Nicolaus Lindenmaier (see his page). FamilySearch, 'Deutschland, ausgewählte evangelische Kirchenbücher 1500-1971' (= 'Germany, Lutheran Baptisms, Marriages, and Burials, 1500-1971'), QPGY-D23L: marriage at Metterzimmern 3 Sep 1737 (Taufen, Konfirmationen u Heiraten 1645-1911, pages 60-61), groom 'Nicolaus Lindenmajer', field Father = 'Hn Johann Christoph Lindenmajers', Wife 'Catharina Schopfen', Father-in-law 'Hn Jacob Schopfen'. Also V4LM-GV4: Nicolaus christened Bietigheim 24 Jan 1704 (born 23 Jan), Parents 'Johann Christoff Lindenmeyer, Agnes Jo[hanna]', which identifies him as the groom of 1 Dec 1696 (QPGP-8VBK). Continuity for Nicolaus: baptism 1704, marriage 1737, children at Bietigheim 1738-61, burial there 23 Jul 1767 'birth 1704' (QPGP-PDG3); no other Nicolaus Lindenmaier of that age in the indexes. Read signed in, 2 Nov 2026; labelled index fields, image not seen (partner-only). ¶7

Audit 10 (2 Nov 2026): Confirmed kept, graded on his link to his son Nicolaus Lindenmaier (see his page). The 1737 marriage QPGY-D23L, labelled, and the image, name the groom Nicolaus as his son. ¶8a

The case for this identification 4

Which Johann Christoph (undecided): two are indexed at Bietigheim: 'Herr Joh Christoph Lindenmaÿer', who married Maria Löffler ('Lofler', daughter of Jacob) on 19 Aug 1694 (records 7269987/907269987), and Joh Christoph Lindenmaier, son of Johann Christoph, who married Agnes Johanna (daughter of Herr Johann Jacob; surname read 'Jamschauer') on 1 Dec 1696 (records 7270021/907270021). A Hanß Christoph, son of Hanß Christoph x Anna Maria, was baptised there 26 Jun 1671 (record 7267002). Nicolaus's baptism (about 1705-15) is not indexed, so his mother is not entered. ¶2

Which Johann Christoph (resolved): Nicolaus's baptism at Bietigheim on 23 Jan 1703 names 'Johann Christoph, Agnes Johanna' (record 7268393), so he is the Joh Christoph Lindenmaier, son of Johann Christoph, who married Agnes Johanna 'Jamschauer', daughter of Herr Johann Jacob, at Bietigheim on 1 Dec 1696 (groom row 7270021 'Joh Christoph Lindenmaier', relatives 'Johann Christoph, Agnes Johanna'; bride row 907270021 'Agnes Johanna Jamschauer', relatives 'Herrn Johann Jacob, Joh Christoph'; image 1184899-00367), not the 'Herr Joh Christoph' who married Maria Löffler in 1694. Other children (index): Sophia Rosina Catharina 22 Feb 1706 (7268511); probably Johann Christoph, son of Johann Christoph, who married at Bietigheim on 5 Feb 1737 (7053500); a Christoph Jacob, relative Johann Christoph, buried 29 Aug 1697 (7275304). A Johann Christoph Lindenmajer was buried at Bietigheim 2 Jan 1760 (7058522, no relatives; he or his son). ¶4

Identity (images read): he was a coppersmith (Kupferschmid) and sworn land surveyor (Feldmesser) at Bietigheim. The caveat of (Audit 10) that his identity as the 1696 groom is only Probable is superseded: the 1696 groom is 'Joh. Christoph Lindenmaier, Kupferschmidt', son of the late Burgermeister Johann Christoph, marrying Agnes Johanna; the 1704 baptism of Nicolaus names 'H. Johann Christoff Lindenmeyer, Kupfferschmid' and 'ux. Agnes Johanna'; the 1737 Metterzimmern marriage names Nicolaus's father as 'Hn Johann Christoph Lindenmajer, Feldmesser und Kupferschmid zu Bietigheim'; and Agnes Johanna was buried in 1748 as the widow of '[Johann] Christoph Lindenmeier, gewesener Burger und Kupferschmid'. He died before 19 Apr 1748. Image read signed in on FamilySearch, 2 Nov 2026. ¶10

The chain, all from images read: (1) 1696 Bietigheim marriage: 'Joh. Christoph Lindenmaier, Kupferschmidt, H. Johann Christoph Lindenmaiers, gewesenen [...] und Burgermeisters allhier hinterlassener ehelicher Sohn' and 'Agnes Johanna, Herrn Johann Jacob J[amschauer/Jenisch]s, gewesenen Gerichtsverwandten und Kupferschmids zu Brackenheim hinterlassene eheliche Tochter'. (2) 1704 Bietigheim baptism, 23 Jan [born] / 24 Jan [baptised]: child 'Nicolaus', parents 'H. Johann Christoff Lindenmeyer, Kupfferschmid [...], ux. Agnes Johanna'. (3) LABW finding aids: 'examinierter Feldtmesser Johan Christoph Lindenmajer von Biettigh[eim]', map of 29 Sep 1721 (HStA Stuttgart A 249 Bü 270), and a Feldmesser Lindenmaier of Bietigheim [1731] (A 249 Bü 268), so the man is at Bietigheim inside the gap. (4) 1737 Metterzimmern marriage (p.60): 'Nicolaus Lindenmajer, Kupferschmid, Hn Johann Christoph Lindenmajers, Feldmessers und Kupferschmids zu Bietigheim ehl. Sohn' married Catharina, daughter of Hn Jacob Schopf, gewesener Schultheiß there. (5) 1748 Bietigheim burial: 'd. 19. [April] Starb Agnes Johanna, [a forename struck through, apparently 'Nicolai', with a correction written above, apparently 'Christoph'] Lindenmeiers gewesenen Burgers und Kupferschmids allhier [widow], aetat. 77 Jahr [7?] Monath, und wurde d. 21. begraben'. (6) 1767 Bietigheim burial: Nicolaus Lindenmajer died 21 Jul, buried 23 Jul 1767, aged '63 Jahr 2 Mon. [..] Tag'. ¶11

Alternatives considered and set aside 2

Possible baptism (Probable identification,; not entered as a date): Hanß Christoph Lindenmeier, baptised at Bietigheim 26 Jun 1671 to Hanß Christoph x Anna Maria (record 7267002, image 1184899-00176); he would be 25 at the 1696 marriage, and the father's name agrees with the 1696 groom row. ¶5

Caveat: his identity as the 1696 groom (husband of Agnes Johanna Jamschauer, son of Hans Christoph) is Probable, not Confirmed. It rests on the 1704 baptism, whose child is tied to the 1737 groom only by name, father's forename and burial age, and other men of his name lived at Bietigheim (see the audit 10 notes on Agnes Johanna Jenisch and Hans Christoph Lindenmaier). The wording 'which identifies him as the groom of 1 Dec 1696' should be read in that light. ¶8b
