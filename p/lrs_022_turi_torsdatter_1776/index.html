Life story

Birth date: changed from 'ABT 1776' (an estimate from her 1801 census age) to before 6 Jan 1777, her baptism date, on the Probable identification above. ¶5

Baptism: on 6 Jan 1777 in Nissedal church, 'Thore Joensen Midböes Datter kaldet Turi' (Tore Joensen of Midbø's daughter named Turi) was baptised. Godparents (*testes*): Hans Præstegaarden, Jørgen Jonsen Midbøe, Ragnild Præstegaarden, Ingier Johansdatter Midbøe and Tone Jonsdatter Midbøe. The mother is not named; this register never names mothers. Kviteseid ministerialbok 1773-1786, Kirke-Aaret 1777, p. 14. ¶3

1801 census: Turi Thoresdatter, 25, wife of Gunlek Alvsen at Midbøe Nordre, Nissedal, both in their first marriage; her sister Guro Toresdatter, 13, lived with them. ¶1

Baptism of daughter: mother ('Turi Torsd.') of Aasild, baptised 24 Mar 1805 in Nissedal; she was churched on Easter Day, 14 Apr 1805. Her link to Aasild Gulliksdatter is Probable for the same reasons as her husband's (see Gunleik Alvsen). ¶2

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: from Probable to Confirmed. The probate after her uncle, the bachelor Jørgen Johnsen of Nordre Midbø (Vest-Telemark skifteprotokoll 1790-94, skifte No 21, registered 12 Jun 1790), lists as heirs the five daughters of his dead eldest brother Thore Johnsen Midbøe: 'Ældste Datter Thuri Thoresdatter 13 Aar gammel' (guardian the lensmann Ansteen Hansen Nøst), Thone 10, Anne 8, Gunnild 5 and 'Femte Datter Guroe Thoresdatter' 2. Age 13 in June 1790 fits her baptism of 6 Jan 1777, and the youngest sister Guroe, 2 in 1790, is the Guro Toresdatter, 13, who lived in 1801 in Turi's household at Midbøe Nordre as 'Konens søster' (the wife's sister). That ties our Turi, wife of Gunleik Alvsen, to Thore Johnsen Midbøe by a record that names her father. ¶7

The case for this identification 1

Identification (Probable): Turi, wife of Gunleik Alvsen at Midbøe Nordre in 1801, aged 25 and called Thoresdatter, is taken to be this child because she is the only Turi baptised at Midbø in Nissedal between Apr 1775 and Jan 1777, her patronymic and farm agree, and her age of 25 in Feb 1801 is within a year of the baptism. The other Turi baptised nearby, the daughter of Anders Pedersen Feeden (Christmas Day 1776), is ruled out by her father's name. Not proved, because no record states that the 1801 wife is the 1777 child. ¶4a

Corrections 1

Correction: an earlier lead proposed Tor Egilsen of Midbø, a widower who remarried in 1796, as her father. The 1777 baptism names the father as Tore Joensen, and no Tor Egilsen was found in the 1801 census of Kviteseid or Nissedal (search for the exact spelling only). The Egilsen lead is set aside. ¶6

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Burial candidate, not adopted: a woman aged 51 (b. about 1777) died 7 Mar and was buried 20 Mar 1828 in Nissedal. One transcription (pg00000008652157, Nissedal 1814-1845, p. 106, no. 3) names her 'Thuri Thoresdr', which fits Turi Torsdatter (baptised Jan 1777); the other copy of the same burial (pg00000006404886, Nissedal 1814-1860, p. 103, no. 3) reads 'Turi Hans D:'. Until the scan settles the patronymic, no date is entered. ¶9

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Would confirm: Turi's confirmation (about 1791-1793) or her marriage to Gunleik Alvsen (about 1795-1800) in the Kviteseid registers, either of which should name her father or farm, or a probate (*skifte*) after Tore Joensen naming her. ¶4b
