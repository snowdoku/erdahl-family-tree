About the family

Children baptised to 'Bersvend Ols. Fuglum' and Anne Olsdatter: Marit, 13th Sunday after Trinity, 16 August 1761 (canvas 11 right, no. 2; godparents Guttorm Pedersen, Ole Olsen, Berit Bjørnsdatter; the child's name and the parents); Kirsti, 21st Sunday after Trinity 1763 (canvas 15 left); Ane, Epiphany 1766 (canvas 19 left) - all; and Brynild, 4th Sunday after Epiphany, 29 January 1775, godparents Peder J. Fuglum, Ole Th. of the same farm, Marit Grytthem, Karen of the same farm and Brynild Høybyen, with the mother churched about 26 February 1775 (Selbu klokkerbok 1766-1776, canvas 116, crop). ¶2

Father of Marit Bersvendsdatter (see her page), rated Probable. For: she married in 1796 as 'Pigen Marite Bersvends d: Fuglem', of Fuglem; she was 40 in February 1801, which a baptism in August 1761 fits; a 'Marit Bersvendsd. Fugl[em?]' was confirmed at Trinity 1780 (canvas 102 left, overview reading). The two rival Marits are accounted for: the Marit of 21 September 1760 (Bersvend Nielsen Solum x Giertrud Andersdatter) is in the 1801 census still unmarried at Solem in her parents' household ('Marit Bersvendsdatter, 40, Mandens og konens born'), and the Marit of January 1765 (Bersvend Haldorsen Solum) is four years too young. Why not Confirmed: Fuglem in 1796 may have been her place of service at 35 rather than her parents' home, and no record names her parents after 1761. ¶3a

Daughter Brynild (1775) and the Alsaet Brynil: Marit's probable sister Brynil Bersvendsdatter (godmother to Marit's daughter Anne in 1799, and wife of Peder Jonssen at Alseth in 1801) may be his daughter Brynild of 29 January 1775, but a second Brynild Bersvendsdatter was born in 1775 - a twin of Ingeborg, daughter of Bersvend Christensen Kallar and Marite Thomasdatter, 17 April 1775 (canvas 117). The 1801 census does not separate them: it has two Brynild Bersvendsdatters aged 26, one at Alseth (wife of Peder Jonssen) and one at Punde (household 04, wife of Ole Nielssen, with a son Niels, 1), and neither household names her parents. The question stays open; Brynil's 1797 marriage entry or her burial (with age and farm) may decide it. ¶4

Life story

Of Fuglem (Fuglum), Selbu. Married Anne Olsdatter on the 4th Sunday after Trinity, 14 June 1761 ('Copul. (4) Bersvend Ols. og Ane Olsd.', date computed from Easter 22 March 1761), Selbu ministerialbok 1757-1801, canvas 11 right page. ¶1

Bygdebok (Selbu i fortid og naatid vol. 3, Fuglem chapter p. 218; nb.no word boxes only): '1761-1785... Olsen,... 1737,... 1805.' and 'G.1761... Anne... 1740,... 1800.' - a holder of Fuglem 1761-1785, born 1737, died 1805, married 1761 to Anne, born 1740, died 1800. The holder's first name is not in the boxes (it may read Bersvend or Ola), but the marriage year 1761, his birth year and the children below all agree with this man. Under 'Barn:' the book lists Marit 1761 (married Pål of Alset), a child of 1763 (married an Ola), Anne 1766 (married Ola of Ustigarden), a child of 1769, Ola 1772-1777, Brynhild 1775 (married a Jonsen of Alset) and Ola 1780-1785. Birth 1737 and death 1805 rest on the book alone (Probable). ¶6

Effect on the links: the book places Marit (1761, wife of Pål/Povel of Alset) and Brynhild (1775, wife of a Jonsen of Alset - Peder Jonssen of Alseth in 1801) in this Fuglem family, which supports both register identifications and makes the Brynhild of the Kallar twins (1775) the less likely sister. It also agrees with the 1801 census lead: 'Bersvend Olssen', 64, a widower at Grøttem - born 1737 and widowed by Anne's death in 1800. The link to Marit Bersvendsdatter (see her page) stays Probable, now stronger, because the book is a compiled source read only as word boxes and the holder's first name is missing. ¶7a

1762 extra tax list (Probable): at Fuglem 691 he and his wife Anne Olsdatter lived in the household of his widowed father Ole Pedersen, with his brother Peder (married to Brit Brynildsdatter[?]) and sister Marit. A probate after Ole Pedersen would confirm the link. ¶8

With Anne Olsdatter baptised a son Ole at Michaelmas 1772 (Selbu 16311 c53); she was churched 1st Sunday in Advent 1772 (c54). The name Ole for the son fits Bersvend's father being an Ole - possibly Ole Pedersen Fuglem (d. 1773 aged 77), not proved. ¶9

Probate of his father Ole Pedersen Fuglem, 17 Apr 1773 (Skifteprotokoll 7, 24758, fol. 141a): '2 Sønnen Bersvend Olsen gift', one of four living children with Peder, Ole and Ingebor. This confirms his parentage, which earlier rested on the 1762 extra tax. ¶12

Detail from a re-read of the 1773 Fuglem skifte (24758 fol. 141, view 156 R, bottom): Ole Pedersen's heirs are five, not four - 1 Peder Olsen, 2 Bersvend Olsen (married), 3 Ole Olsen (of Øvre Solem), 4 Ingebor Olsdatter (married, under Lechvolden[?]), and 5 a deceased daughter who left a daughter Karen. The mother is not named (Ole was a widower); her probate is not under Fuglem in the typed registers of 24753, 24755 or 24756. ¶14

Pointer: the probate 'Ole Pedersen, enkemann, Fuglem, Selbu' is entered in the typed register of Stjør- og Verdal skifteprotokoll 7 (24758) at fol. 141 (1776-78 section). This is the folio for the probate that his notes name as the next record; not read here (Selbu probates are another worker's assignment). ¶13

Later life, a lead only: the 1801 census has a 'Bersvend Olssen', 64, widower of his first marriage, at Grøttem, household 08 (with the cottager Peder Iverssen, 47, and his wife Gunild Bersvendsdatter, 32), and a 'Marit Grytthem' was godmother to Brynild in 1775. Born about 1737, he is of the right age, which would make Anne Olsdatter dead before 1801 - unproved; there were several Bersvend Olsens in Selbu. ¶5

Probable burial: indexed Selbu burials 1805: 'Bersven Olsen', widower, inderst (lodger), Fuglem, aged 68 - born about 1737, matching the bygdebok's 1737 (record pg00000001727316). In the 1801 census 'Bersvend Olssen', 64, widower, lived at Grøttem; the return to Fuglem as a lodger is inferred. ¶11

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

A son 'Olle Bersvends. Fuglum' was buried March 1777 aged 4½ (16311 c80). ¶10
