Life story

Birth: if her children were born 1656-1662, she was born about 1615-1640, as already estimated. ¶9

Marriage: Second wife of Even (Evind) Tellefsen Ramse of Ramse in Tovdal. The Østre Råbyggelag probate-card abstract of the Ramse deeds registered 28 July 1704 reads "Evind Tellefsen Ramse +, g. 2. g. m. Anna Evindsdtr. lever i 1704" — Evind Tellefsen Ramse, deceased, married the second time to Anna Evindsdatter, who was living in 1704. ¶1

Dates (inferred): No birth or death record survives. She was alive at the July 1704 deed. Her daughter Groe had a son, Tellef Nielsen Tveit, who was himself a father by about 1700, so Groe was born about 1640-1660 and Anna by about 1640 at the latest. The earliest bound assumes she was no more than about 90 in 1704. These are estimates from the family's generations, not from any record of her age. ¶3

1704 deeds: Two deeds in the Østre Råbyggelag mortgage book (pantebok A4, fol. 76-78), registered at the summer ting of July 1704, name her. Rolf Evindsen's sons sold their father's share of "his late father's odel farm, which Anna, the late Evind Ramse's [widow], had for her use as long as she lived"; the clerk's registration note calls her "hans Stifmoder S. Anna Evind Ramses" — his (Rolf's) stepmother, the late Anna. Groe Evindsdatter's heirs sold their share of the odel land "which Anna, the late Evind Ramse's [widow], has had since his death as maintenance property (underholdningsgods) for her lifetime"; the note calls her "S. Anna Evindsdaatter". This confirms her name, that she was Evind Tellefsen Ramse's widow, and that she was his later wife: she was stepmother to Rolf, a child of an earlier marriage. ¶5

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Confidence: Probable, not Confirmed. The card states the marriage, but the archivist marked Groe only as "? søster" of Svend Evensen Ramse on card 10417, i.e. queried whether this deed's "Evind Tellefsen Ramse" is the same man as Svend's father "Even Tollefsen Ramse". Same farm, same name and patronymic (Even and Evind are one name), and a daughter Groe who married into Tveit make the identification strong. What would settle it is the 28 July 1704 deed (skjøte) itself in the Østre Råbyggelag tingbok/pantebok, which should name the sellers and their relationship. ¶4

Confidence: Her marriage to Evind (Even) Tellefsen Ramse is now Confirmed by the deeds. Her motherhood of Groe stays Probable: the heirs' deed claims only through Groe's father and never calls Anna Groe's mother or their grandmother. The archivist's "Barn i 2. ekteskap" (child of the 2nd marriage) for Groe is an inference, though the contrast with Rolf's deed, where Anna is expressly "stepmother", supports it. ¶7

Corrections 2

Correction: She was dead by 7 March 1704, not living in 1704. Both deeds call her "S." (salig, the late) and speak of her life interest in the past tense; the sales were made because that life interest had ended. The card abstract's "lever i 1704" is a misreading. ¶6

Correction: The Tovdal bygdebok (Åmli: ætt og heim I, 'Ytre Ramse', canvases 326-327) gives Eivind Ramse three marriages: Signe (estate divided about 1639; children incl. Olav, Jon and Gro), a second wife about 1640 (a son Rolf, b. 1644), and Anne, married before 1655 ('Eivind... med Anne... før 1655'; 'Tovdal kyrkje... 1654'), whose children are 'Born:... 1656... Ingeborg... Hillestad', 'Knut... 1662... Risland', and 'Signe... Jon Aslakson... Austenå'. So Anna was the mother of Svend (b. about 1656, wife Ingeborg), Knud Evindsen Risland (b. about 1662) and Signe (wife of Joen Aslaksen), not of Groe. This matches the 1666 vicar's census, where Effuind's sons are Svend 10 and Knud 4.
