About the family

Widow of Hermund Hendrichsen of (Indre) Takle in his probate of 19 May 1716 (Ytre Sogn 2b fol. 492b): 'hans efterlevende quinde Gietr.[?] Claus Datter'. The first name is abbreviated and the reading is not certain: 'Gietr.' (Giertrud) is the best reading; the first letter has a looped descender like the q of 'quinde' and differs from the b of the daughter's name 'Brit' two lines below. Read from the scan. ¶1

Life story

The same probate closes (fol. 493b, view 178, top left) with 'Saaledis Moderen med hendis børn beskiftet' (thus the mother and her children were divided), again calling the widow the mother of the heirs. ¶3

Birth and death not known; alive 19 May 1716. ¶4

1 on the grade

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Graded on her link to her son Claus Hermundsen: the 1716 probate divides the estate between her and 'deris tilsammen avlede Børn' (their jointly begotten children) Hendrich, Claus, Brite and Haldis. Continuity to the tree's Claus Hermundsen as under his father Hermund Hendrichsen (1701 census, 1716, 1726, 1750, all Indre Takle; no rival). Her father was probably a Claus, and her son Claus carried that name (inference). Hermund was about 48 at Claus's birth; no earlier wife is known, but the probate's wording makes her the mother of all four children. ¶2
