Life story

Probate after Ane Olsdatter, who lived on Loen, held 12 [Mar?] 1749 (Nordfjord skifteprotokoll A 10, fol. 335b; month uncertain): present her 'efterlevende Mand Lars Samuelsen' and their children Samuel Larsen, 8 1/2 years old, and Ole Larsen, 1/2 year. Appraisers Niels Jørgensen Loen and Arne [?] Pedersen [Bødal?]. First wife of Lars Samuelsen Loen; not an ancestor of the line (David descends from Lars's daughter Anna by Marthe Rasmusdatter). Not the same person as Ane (Anne) Olsdatter. Read. ¶1
