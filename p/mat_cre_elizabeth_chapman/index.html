About the family

Eldest daughter of Henry Chapman Sen. of Murray Harbour; married Henry Colcroft at Murray Harbour on 23 October 1855 before John Dalziel, J.P. (The Islander, 9 November 1855; PEI marriage register index, 'Elizabeth Chapman' and 'Henry Coalcroft'). Henry 'Calcraft' was a head of household in Lot 59 in 1861, two census pages from James and Jane Creed; in 1881 Henry Colcraft, 71, born England, Bible Christian, was living in Lot 59 with Frances, 40, so Elizabeth had probably died by then. Speculative as Mary Jane (Chapman) Creed's sister. ¶1

Life story

Baptism (Probable identification): Elizabeth, daughter of Henry Chapman, labourer, and Elizabeth, was baptised at Minster, Cornwall, on 26 February 1822, the first daughter baptised to that couple, matching 'Elizabeth, eldest daughter of Mr. Henry Chapman, Sen.' in the 1855 Islander notice of her marriage to Henry Colcroft. See Henry Chapman. ¶3

1861 census: Henry 'Calcraft', farmer, Lot 59, headed a household of 3: himself (male 45-60), a wife (female 21-45) and a girl under 5. He lived next to the Johnson Clarke and John Clarke households. Henry Colcraft had married Frances Ann Clark on 23 August 1858, so the wife in 1861 was most likely Frances. Elizabeth (Chapman) Colcroft had probably died between October 1855 and August 1858. That is inferred: no death or burial of hers has been found, and searches of the 61503 death cards and the Kings County cemetery transcriptions for Colcroft, Colcraft and Calcraft found nothing. As 'eldest daughter' in 1855 she was probably born before about 1830. ¶2
