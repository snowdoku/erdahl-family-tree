Life story

Birth registration, two cards: two typed Minnesota birth index cards name the same child: certificate 1905-16626 'Otis Marine Erdahl', 'Jan 1' 1905, and certificate 1906-16229 'Otis M. Erdahl', 'Jan.1' 1906. Both give Ellsborough Township, Murray County, the father B. L. Erdahl, farmer, 27, the mother Annie Quam, 23, and the reporter W. L. Rogde of Ruthton. 'Jan 1' is probably a placeholder for an unknown day. ¶1

Otis, one child or two: there was one Otis, born in 1905 or early 1906. The 1908 certificate makes Bernice the mother's fourth child and the 1910 certificate makes Maynard the fifth, with five living, which leaves room for only one child between Minnie (the second) and Bernice. The census of 1 June 1905 lists Oliver and Minnie but no Otis, which suggests a birth after June 1905; the family list gives 1905. The exact date is unproved. The 1910 census page (Cameron Township) or a later record with a full birth date would settle it. ¶2

Name and death: the family list recorded under his brother William (from Find a Grave) calls him Otis Merin Erdahl, 1905-1975; the certificates read 'Marine' or 'M.'. No death record has been read yet. ¶3

Census sightings: 'Otis', son, 4, born Minnesota, in the 1910 household of Berendt Erdahl at Cameron Township, Murray County, Minnesota (index); and 'Otis M.', son, 14, born Minnesota, in his mother's household at South Malta, Phillips County, Montana, in January 1920 (read from the image). Both ages fit a birth about 1905-06 and confirm he lived to at least 1920 as the child between Minnie and Bernice. ¶5
