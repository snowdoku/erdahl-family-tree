Life story

Town and death found. (1) The 1667 Brackenheim entry (no. 752) reads, on the crop: 'Heinrich Friderich Casten, [seel.?] Schiffhandlers zu H[ö]rdten hinderlassene eheliche Tochter'; the letter shapes of the place name fit 'Hördten' (had guessed 'Lo...'). The place is Hördten, i.e. Hörden on the Murg near Gernsbach. The Archion forum helpers read it the same way ('Hördten an der Murg', 'Schiffshändler'), and the Pfarrerbuch entry for his son-in-law's father (Nr. 3905) has 'Maria Juliane Kast, Va. Heinrich, Schiffshändler Hördten bei Gernsbach'. A Schiffhandler here was a member of the Murg timber-rafting trade (the Murgschifferschaft of Gernsbach). (2) His burial, Gernsbach Lutheran register 1649, p.39 (DGS 102078085 image 283, image read): 'Den 8ten Martij H. Heinrich Friederich Kast zu Hördten, so von einem Soldaten erstochen worden 6.[?] ejusd[em] [...]', i.e. stabbed by a soldier, apparently on the 6th, and entered on 8 Mar 1649. The FamilySearch index (QP6J-BMMV) misreads this entry as the death of a son 'J. Jacob Kast' with father Heinrich Friedrich Kast. The image shows one entry for H. F. Kast himself; the words after the date of the stabbing are not fully read. So the 'J. Jacob Kast' lead in his note is superseded: there is no son of that name in this entry. Identification: the same full name, the same village (Hördten) and the title Herr in the 1649 entry, and the 1667 entry calls him dead ('hinderlassene'). No other Heinrich Friderich Kast appears in the Gernsbach indexes. His daughter's burial age (33 in March 1681) puts her birth about 1647-48, before his death. Surname spelling set to Kast, as the Gernsbach register writes it; Brackenheim wrote the genitive 'Casten'. His grade, on his link to Maria Juliana through the 1667 entry, is unchanged (Confirmed). His wife, Maria Juliana's mother, was not found: no Heinrich Kast marriage is indexed at Gernsbach or Scheuern. ¶2a

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Graded on that link: Confirmed. Naming record: the Brackenheim marriage of 1667, no. 752 (image read): 'Maria Juliana, Heinrich Friderich Casten, [a short word, perhaps 'seel.', i.e. late] Schiffhandlers [reading uncertain: boat-trader] zu [place unread, beginning 'Lo' or 'La'] hinderlassene eheliche Tochter'; the FamilySearch extraction VCYM-NRF labels him 'Spouse's Father's Name: Heinrich Friderich Casten'. 'Casten' is the genitive, so the surname is Cast. 'Hinterlassene' means he was dead by 1667. Continuity: the bride of this record is the Maria Juliana of the 1670 baptism, because the groom of the same entry is the coppersmith Hans Jacob Jenisch, whose only wife between 1667 and 1682 was Maria Juliana (see her notes). His occupation (apparently 'Schiffhandler', a river boat-trader, which would suit a Neckar town such as Lauffen) and his town were not certainly read; the place is left blank. Lead, not entered: a 'J. Jacob Kast' died at Scheuern near Gernsbach on 8 Mar 1649, indexed as son of 'Heinrich Friedrich Kast' (QP6J-BMMV, no image read); Loffenau, a Württemberg village beside Gernsbach, would fit a place beginning 'Lo'. Not proved to be the same man. ¶1b
