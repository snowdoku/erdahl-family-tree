About the family

Father of Tallach Ormsen of øvre Skjeggedal, Torgius (Torjus) Ormsen, A[m]borg Ormsdatter (married Aslak Terjesen Ramse; see her estate 1789) and Guro (married Aslak Salvesen Skjeggedal, d. 1766): the archivist's card for Tallach Ormsen's estate, øvre Skjeggedal i Tovdal, 6 Sep 1796 (Austre Råbyggelaget Skifteprotokoll nr. 17, fol. 164; card 39942/12483 and checked by the orchestrator) gives 'Avd.s far: Orm Tallaksen' and 'bror: Torgius Ormsen'. The 1766 estate after Aslach Salvesen of Skjeggedal (Skp. 13a fol. 170) also names Guro's father as Orm Tallaksen. ¶1

Wife unknown. His own father was presumably a Tallak (patronymic); not researched. ¶3

Parents and birth from the bygdebok (Probable): Åmli ætt og heim I (Tovdal), Øvre Skjeggedal (canvases 92-93): 'Orm Tallakson [Øvre] Skjeggedal f 1693 d 1764, g 1726 m Ragnhild Nilsdtr. Vrålstad f 1705 d 1783', son of Tallak Ormson (1665-1719) and Gro Mikkelsdtr. (1671-1717). Children per the book: Guro 1726 (m. 1759 Aslak Salveson d.y. Skjeggedal), Tallak 1729 (m. 1757 Torborg Sveinungsdtr.; d. 1796, = the Tallach Ormsen of the 1796 estate), a daughter 1738 (m. 1758 Aslak Terjeson Ramse), Birgit 1741 (m. 1765 Aslak Olson Ramse), Torjus 1744 (m. 1776 Ingeborg Olsdtr. Austenå). Contradiction noted, date not changed: the book gives his death as 1764 and says he 'gav opp til skifte 1762, men det er ikkje [funne] i [protokollane]', i.e. the 12 Jun 1762 estate report was a handover (oppgjevingsskifte) by a living man, so 'BEF 12 JUN 1762' may be wrong. ¶6a

Parents proved by his mother's probate. Østre Råbyggelag Skifteprotokoll nr. 5 (DA 24353, view 123), fol. 118a: 'Anno 1717 dend 25 Septemb.' at Øfre Skieggedal, probate after 'Guroe Michelsdaatter', divided between her surviving husband 'Thallach Ormsøn' and 'deris sammen avlede Børn': Orm Thallachsøn 24 aar; Jacob Thallachsøn 21 aar (a soldier in Capitain Sommerschiel's company); Knud Thallachsøn 17; Gundsten Thallachsøn 11; Knud the younger, 6; Errich ½; and Ingeborrig Thallachsdaatter, unmarried. The father is guardian. Card 39942/12319 indexes it the same way and adds '(+ sk. 1719)' for Tallak. He is the eldest son, 24, so b. about 1693. ¶7

Life story

Baptisms of children, Åmli register 1711-1740 (DA 9920), read on the scan: 'Orm Tallachsøn øfre Skiegedals barn Tallach', Dom. Palmarum 1729, Tovdal (canvas 50, p. 93), the Tallak Ormsen whose 1796 estate names Orm as his father; and 'Orm Tallachsøn øfre Skiegedals barn Margitte', 23 p. Trin. 1732, Tovdal (canvas 62, p. 118). These place him at Øvre Skjeggedal from 1729 to 1732. ¶4

Marriage, Åmli register 1711-1740 (DA 9920), read on the scan: betrothal 'Den 15 ejusdem [Feb 1726] trolovet Orm Tallachsøn Scheggedal og Ragnild Nielsdatter' (canvas 42, p. 78), and marriage at Topdal 'Den 21 Junij... Copul: Orm Tallachsøn og Ragnild Nielsdatter' (21 Jun 1726, canvas 42, p. 79). No fathers or sureties named. His children Tallak (1729) and Margitte (1732) were baptised to 'Orm Tallachsøn øfre Skiegedal'. ¶5

Death: the same card says an estate after Orm Tallaksen was reported ('oppg.') on 12 Jun 1762 but was not found in the probate protocols; so he died before 12 Jun 1762. ¶2
