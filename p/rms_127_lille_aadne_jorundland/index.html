About the family

NEW ANCESTOR, graded on his link to his son Børuf Aadnesen (see his page). Åmli ætt og heim II (Gjøvdal), Jørundland L.nr. 35 / bruk nr. 4 (canvases 86-87): in the 1680s there was a man called 'Lille-Ådne' Jørundland; the author does not know whether he is the Ådne who owned part of the farm in 1670 or another Ådne, nor how he came to the farm (perhaps married a granddaughter of Gjermund); 'Det var skifte etter han i 1689, men det stod ikkje [vel] til med buet. Alt var panta [bort] til Peder Eilevsen.' 'Etter denne Ådne veit vi om 5 born': Anne b. 1680 d. 1767; Olav d. 1712; [Børuf] b. 1682 m. Anne [Vrålsdtr.]; Olav b. 1684 (m. 1708 Birgit Svennsdtr.); a child b. 1687 d. 1765. The next summary is '[Børuf] Ådneson Jørundland f 1682 d 1728, g m Anne [?] f 1681 d 1743', with children Ingeborg 1711 (m. 1733 Tallak Eivindson Ekra), Asborg (m. 1730 Olav Halvorson Jørundland), Ådne 1714 (m. 1736 Gunvor Taraldsdtr.), Tellev 1718, Olav 1721, Olav 1724 d. 1724, Signe 1725 (m. 1754 Olav Olson Mjåland) — exactly the children of Børuf in the church book and his 1728 probate. He is distinct from 'Stor-Ådne' Ådne Olson (b. 1624, skifte 1699, bruk nr. 1), so the 1666 census 'Aadne 52' is probably Stor-Ådne, not Børuf's father. Bygdebok only (compiled secondary source, text rebuilt from word boxes, dates ABT), so Probable at most. ¶1
