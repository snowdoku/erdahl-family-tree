About the family

Father of Oline Pedersdatter (see her page), Probable: she is the only child bearing his patronymic in his household, on the farm where she lived as Erik Amundsen's wife in 1801, at a matching age. No baptism (Skudenes registers before 1770 are not on Digitalarkivet) or probate yet names him as her father. ¶2

Life story

In the 1758 soul register of Skudenes parish he heads the household on 'Hejen eller Ranvoldsjord' (Heien or Randvoldjord), aged 58 (born about 1700, inferred), with Ranved Holgersdatter, 60, evidently his wife (listed second in the householders column, the usual place of the wife). In the children column: Olene Pedersdatter 13, Rasmus Rasmussen 20 and Engebor Rasmusdatter 39 (the last two evidently his wife's children by an earlier husband Rasmus). Servants Engebor Tormodsdatter 40 and Gunele Hansdatter 33. Read from the scan. ¶1

The heir column names Olene alone, so Rasmus Rasmussen and Engebor Rasmusdatter, who lived in his household in 1758, were not his children (consistent with their being his wife's children by an earlier husband). Whether a widow survived is not shown in the part of the row read; the deceased column names him alone. ¶4

Bygdebok: Bygdebok for Karmøy: Åkra (1980), farm 21 Heiå (the book's name for 'Hejen eller Ranvoldsjord'; it quotes '... eller Randvoldjord' from 1723), old-farm section, canvas 186: user no. 7, 1733-1756, 'Peder Knutsen, ca. 1705, d. ca. 1764, son [av] førre' (no. 6, Knut Kolbeinsen, user 1699-1733), 'G.m. Rannveig Holgersdtr., ca. 1698'; children Sjur ca. 1734 (next user), Knut ca. 1745 (bnr. 6) and a daughter ca. 1745 (bnr. 4, i.e. Oline, wife of Erik). 'Peder bygsla 8 spann [ved bygselbrev av] 5. [...] 1733. Han gav garden frå seg [...] 30. [...] 1756', when Sjur took the lease. His father Knut Kolbeinsen is added as Probable from this book. ¶5

1762: the 1762 ekstraskatt for Skudenes (source 49646, view 607), farm 39 'Randvoldiord', lists 'Peder Knudsen / Randveg Holgersd. / Olene Pedersd.' together in the column for people living on their own means, beside the farmer Siur Pedersen and Kirstine Hansd. He was therefore alive in 1762 and had handed the farm to Siur (the bygdebok's son Sjur, b. ca. 1734). His death falls between 1762 and 18 Apr 1764. ¶6

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: the Ryfylke probate designation register 1757-1766 (source 23888, view 88) lists, under Stangeland skipreide 1764, a probate (skifte) held 18 April 1764 at 'Ranvoldiord' after the deceased 'Peder Knudsen', whose only heir is 'Olene Pedersdatter', aged 17; her inheritance 10 rd 0 mk 20 s, her guardian Sivert Pedersen Ranvoldjord. Together with the 1758 soul register (Peder Knudsen 58 with Olene Pedersdatter 13 on the same farm), this is a primary record naming him as the father of Olene and placing his death before 18 April 1764.
