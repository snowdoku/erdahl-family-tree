Life story

Marriage and father: 'Joh Caspar Stierlein', son of Jacob, married Elisabetha Margaretha 'Majer', daughter of Tobias, at Bietigheim in 1718 (groom row 7270350, relatives 'Jacob, Elisabetha Margaretha'; bride row 907270350, relatives 'Tobias, Joh Caspar'; image 1184899-00384). She is the Elisabetha Margaretha of the 1728 baptism (7269723), so not necessarily a second wife as supposed. He is the only Johann Caspar Stierlin indexed at Bietigheim; his own baptism (about 1690-98) is not indexed there. ¶3

Images not seen; graded on the faithfully transcribed index row under the overnight rule for this collection. Probably the Hanß (Johann) Caspar Stierlin of Bietigheim who with Elisabetha Margaretha baptised Maria Justina there on 27 Nov 1728 (record 7269723) and buried a child on 18 Jan 1736 (record 7057255); Elisabetha Margaretha may be a second wife, so no wife entered. ¶1b

Coordinator review: held at Probable rather than Confirmed. Ancestry collection 61023 index rows do not label who is father, mother or spouse; the BRIEF audit 5 rule is that unlabelled index rows give Probable until the scan is read, or a FamilySearch index with labelled parent fields is found (queued S0233). The naming evidence in the note above is otherwise strong, so this person is a priority for an image read. ¶2
