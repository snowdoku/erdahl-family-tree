Life story

Not in the 1664 fogd's list (35578 views 17-27 read; Store Evjen, the fogd's own farm, is not listed) or the 1665 list. His probate would predate the 1689 start of the Stjør- og Verdal protocols; the Storevjen cards in 77804 (c1897, c1899) are 1731 and 1738 and are not his. ¶3

1666 vicar's manntall, Selbu (35528 view 25 left and on the crop): under 'Foegdens Gaard' (the bailiff's farm) Store Evjen ('Stor Effjen', skyld 3½ spann 1 ørtug) the users are Jon Torrissen, 'Kiel Larss.' (½ ørtug, aged 60 or 90 - the figure is ambiguous on the scan), Jon Olsen 60 and Østen Olsen 30. The sons column lists 'Lauritz Kielss.' 38, 'Michel Kielsen' 18, 'Paaffuel Kielss.' 35 and 'Joen Kielsen' 30, then Olluf Jonsen 28 and Lauritz Olsen 20. Kiel Larsen is the only man named Kjeld in the 1664, 1665 or 1666 Selbu lists, so the four Kielsen sons are his. Birth is set to about 1606 on the reading 60; the alternative reading 90 would make him 72 at the birth of his son Mickel, so it is set aside, and the date is approximate. ¶1

Proposed (Probable) as the father of Mikkel (Michel) Kjeldsen of Velve (see his page), graded on his link to Mikkel: 'Mickel Kielsen' aged 18 (born about 1648) is listed as Kiel Larsen's son at Store Evjen in 1666, and 'Michel Kieldsen', farmer at Velve, died before his insolvent estate was settled on 26 May 1693 (24749 fol. 117). The patronymic Kjeldsen is rare in Selbu; no other Mikkel Kjeldsen and no other Kjeld appears in the 1664-66 lists, the 1701 manntall or the 77804 card index searches. ¶2a

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
