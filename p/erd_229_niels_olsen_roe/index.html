1 on the grade · 1 on the identification · 1 alternative set aside

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: the probate after the widow Anna Jørgensdatter at Røe, 28 Aug 1769 (Ytre Sogn 8b p. 561b, 24166 view 180), is held between 'hendes... med... afdøde Mand Niels Olsen sammen avlede 2de Sønner og 4re Døttre' and names 'Ingebor Nielsdatter gift med Anfind Olsen Tiugum' (share heading 'Anden Datter Ingebor Nielsdatter'). Continuity to the tree's Ingeborg: 12 years later, on 18 Oct 1781, the probate after Ingeborg Nielsdatter at Kalvetræet under Tjugum is held with her widower Anfind Olsen (24167 view 739), same couple, same place (Tjugum), with her brother 'Ole Nielsen Røe' (named in 1745 and 1769) as curator of her daughter Christie. ¶1a

The case for this identification 1

Supporting: the probate after Kari Christensdatter, 13 Jun 1726 (Ytre Sogn 3a fol. 187a, 24157 view 189; index 'Rød'), is held between 'hendes efterladte Mand Niels Olsen Røe' and their daughter Gidlov Nielsdatter. So Kari Christensdatter was his first wife and Gidlou's mother, and he was at Røe by 1726; Anne Jørgensdatter married him after Jun 1726. No other probate at Røe/'Rød' in Ytre Sogn 1698-1775 besides Kari 1726, Niels 1745 and Anne 1769, so no probate of his father Ole at Røe is indexed. ¶2

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

No rival: no other Ingeborg Nielsdatter married to an Anfind Olsen is known in Tjugum skipreide. Graded Confirmed on his link to his daughter Ingeborg Nilsdatter (see her page). Farmer (on Bergen bishop's land) at Røe, Tjugum skipreide, Leikanger; dead before his probate of 17 Sep 1745, which names him 'Niels Olsen Røe' and lists his children: Gidlou (20) of a former marriage, and with his widow Anne Jørgensdatter Knud 15, Ole, Kari 18, Ingebor 12, Brithe the elder 9 and Brithe the younger. Røe identified: Røe is a farm in Leikanger parish, Tjugum skipreide (1801 census 'Leganger: Røe'); the Ytre Sogn probate index spells it 'Rød' (Lars Olsen and Magdeli Hansdtr of the 1801 Røe household appear there as 'Rød' 1809-13), which is why searches for 'Røe' found nothing. The probate after Niels Olsen Røe, 17 Sep 1745 (Ytre Sogn 6b fol. 480a-481a, No. 5, 24162 views 128-129) names among the children he had with his widow Anne Jørgensdatter '3die Datter Ingebor Nielsdatter', 12 (b. about 1733), with her brothers Knud Nielsen (15) and Ole Nielsen. Continuity: (1) 1745, Røe: Ingebor Nielsdatter 12, brothers Knud and Ole Nielsen; (2) 11 Apr 1757, Tjugum: betrothal of 'Anfind Olsøn Bahle & Ingeborg Røe' (11566 c8), 12 years later, same farm, no patronymic; Knud Nielsen Røe betrothed in 1758 (11566 c20); (3) 18 Oct 1781, Kalvetræet: probate after Ingeborg Nielsdatter, wife of Anfind Olsen, with 'Ole Nielsen Røe' as curator of her daughter Christie (24167 view 740). So the 1745 sister pair Ingebor + Ole Nielsen of Røe recurs in 1781 as Ingeborg Nielsdatter and Ole Nielsen Røe, curator for her child (curators were usually the nearest kin), and the 1757 'Ingeborg Røe' links the farm to the marriage. (This 1745-1757-1781 chain alone would be Probable; the 1769 probate of her mother, above, is what confirms it.) Her age fits (b. ~1733; daughter Christie b. ~1762, son Niels b. ~1766, named after her father). Further records: Ole Nielsen Røe's probate, 30 May 1810 (Ytre Sogn 14 fol. 119a, 24174 view 121; the heirs there are his own widow and children, read only at heading level), the probate after Anne Jørgensdatter (not yet found; search domicile 'Rød' 1745-1790), or Knud Nielsen's probate, any of which might name 'Søsteren Ingeborg Nielsdatter' or her children Niels and Christi Anfindsbørn. ¶1b
