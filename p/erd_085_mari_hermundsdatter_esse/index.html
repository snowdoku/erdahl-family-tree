About the family

Widow of Ole Povelsen of Ytre Esse (see his page). His probate, held 6 and 8 May 1758 (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll A 7b, fol. 545b, No. 56; read from the scan and audited), names the widow twice as 'Mari Hermunds datter' and says she moved in with 'sin Søn' (her son) Paul Olsen at Ytre Esse. The probate closed on 14 Sep 1761 with Paul Olsen present. ¶1

Father added as Speculative: Hermund Hermundsen, bonde at Indre Farnes in 1701 (b. 1659), the only Hermund holding a Farnes farm; rival Hermund Olsen, cottar at Farnes in 1701 (b. 1646). See Hermund Hermundsen Farnes. ¶7a

Life story

Bygdebok (Laberg, Balestrand 1 canvas 97, Ytre-Ese bruk 1; rebuilt from word boxes, dates ABT): Ola Paulson (b. 1675) married in 1718 Mari Hermundsdtr.; he died 1758 and his wife in 1764; they had 4 children, Paul (who took the farm), Anders (m. a Kristendtr. from Grinde), Randi (m. Hermund) and [Magdeli]. The four children agree with Ole Povelsen's 1758 probate, so the bygdebok treats Mari as the mother of all four, Paul included. Her origin falls in a gap of the rebuild (phrase tests 'Hermundsdtr. <farm>' for about 75 Sogn farms were negative). Grade unchanged (Probable). ¶5a

Primary record: her marriage. Leikanger 11564, canvas 133 right, betrothal at Tjugum on Misericordias Sunday (Apr) 1718: 'Ola Esse / Mari Farnes'; canvas 135 left, wedding at Tjugum on the 5th Sunday after Trinity (Jul) 1718: 'Ole Esse / Mari Farnes } nupt.' This is the 1718 marriage of Ole Povelsen of Ytre Esse given by the bygdebok, and it shows that she came from FARNES. Grade unchanged (Probable on her link to Paul Olsen Esse): the marriage date makes her the mother only if Paul was born after Jul 1718, and his birth year is not known; Ole (b. about 1675) was 43 at this wedding and an earlier marriage of his has not been searched (11564, 1700-1717). The ages of her daughter Randi's sons in 1758 (10 and 6) fit children born after 1718 but do not fix Paul's birth. ¶6

Godmother 'Mari Hermunds D. Esse' at the baptism of Einer Paulsøn Esse on 15 Nov 1760 (Leikanger 11566 c50 R), the infant son of Paul Olsen Esse: consistent with her being the child's grandmother. Not further evidence of her own parents. Whether Anne Hermundsdatter of Nødeskreen (see her page) was her sister is not shown by anything read: neither stands godparent to the other's child in Jan-May 1761, and Anne's son's godparents (Hammersnes, Farnes, Menes, Lillestølen) are from the inner Tjugum farms, not Esse. ¶3

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the grade · 1 search with no result · 4 open questions

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Probable as mother of Paul Olsen Esse: the probate calls Paul 'her son', but Danish-Norwegian clerks sometimes used 'Søn' for a stepson, and the entry does not say whether she was Ole's only wife. ¶2a

Searches that found nothing 1

Checked: her son Paul Olsen's probate, No. 29, Ytre Esse, 25 May 1762 (7b fol. 589b-590a, 24164 view 255), names his widow Sigri Einersdatter and their children Ole Paulsen and Ingebor Paulsdatter. His mother is not named on these two pages; a creditor 'Ole Hermundsen' appears in the debts and could be kin (unproved). Grade unchanged. ¶4a

Open questions and the next record to read 4

What would confirm it: her own probate (Ytre Sogn, after 1761; the probate index lists no Mari Hermundsdatter at Esse, so check the protocols' farm registers), or Paul Olsen's 1762 probate (A 7b, fol. 589b) if it names his mother. The only woman's estate at Ytre Esse in the index for 1755-1790 is 'Ragnilde Clausdtr' (1756-63, A 7b fol. 454a), a different woman. ¶2b

Lead only: a 'Mari Hermundsdatter', under age, was the younger daughter of Hermund Endresen and Marta Knudsdatter of Vikøren, Vik skipreide (probate No. 71, 12 Aug 1743, 6a fol. 322b, 24161 view 324). Nothing ties her to Esse, and she may be too young to be the mother of a man who had children by 1758. ¶4b

Test record: her probate about 1764 at Ytre Esse (Ytre Sogn index, domicile Esse/Ese, 1762-1766) naming her son Paul Olsen's children as heirs, or the 1718 Leikanger marriage (11564). ¶5b

Test record: her baptism (11564, c.1690-1700, 'Mari Hermunds d. Farnes'). ¶7b
