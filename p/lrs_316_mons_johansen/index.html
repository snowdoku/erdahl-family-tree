About the family

Son Iver linked (Probable): his eldest son Iver is Iver Monsen of Botnen (see his page). The probate after Iver's first wife at Botten on 8 Oct 1739 (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll 5, Nr. 78) names 'Farbr: Peder Monsøn Indre Tackle' as the children's paternal uncle; Peder is Mons's third son in Nr. 76 and Nr. 77, and a 'Peder Monsøn Tackle' is listed among the creditors of Mons's own estate (fol. 122a). See Iver's parentage note for the full reasoning and what would confirm it. (Supersedes the 'Possible identity of his son Iver... not linked' note.) ¶7

Pedigree collapse: Mons Johansen is now an ancestor of this line through two sons, Torkild Monsen of Asheim and Iver Monsen of Botnen, and through Iver by both of Iver's marriages: three lines of descent in all. Torkild's link to Mons is itself Probable (see the place-in-the-line note). ¶8

Son Torkild's baptism (grade raised): on 21 Oct 1719 'Mons Indreopdals bar[n] N: Torckel, Mat: Randi' was christened (Gulen ministerialbok 11438, canvas 41), and on the 1st Sunday in Advent (3 Dec) 1719 at Brekke 'Mons Indreopdals q[vin]de' was churched after a boy named Torchild (canvas 42). With his 1739 probate (widow Randi Iversdatter, 5th son Torchel), this makes him Confirmed as the father of Torkild Monsen of Asheim (see his page). See Torkild's note 'Confidence of parents raised' for each record. ¶10

Son Iver, link raised from Probable to Confirmed: the baptism of 15 Aug 1704 in Eivindvik church, 'Mons Johans: Indreoppedahls Iver' (Gulen ministerialbok 11433, canvas 150), names him as father of Iver, and its date fits Iver Monsen of Botnen's burial age of 81 years [9] months on 15 May 1786 to the month; Nr. 76 and Nr. 77 (1739) name Iver as his eldest son, and Nr. 78 names Iver Monsen of Botten's 'Farbroder' Peder Monsen of Indre Takle, Mons's third son. See the 'Father raised' note on Iver Monsen. (Supersedes the 'Son Iver linked (Probable)' note as to the grade.) ¶12

Son Johannes: his second son Johannes is probably the Johannes Monsen who lived at Østerbø (Østrebø), Brekke, by Dec 1741 and died before Sep 1742 (identification inferred; see below); probate Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll 6a, Nr 70 (fol. 242b-243a, Sep 1742), widow Gunilde Lassesdatter (surname partly legible), children Mons, Anna and Marita. Guardians: the mother for the son, 'Lasse Indre Oppedal' for Anna, and 'Ande[r]? Indre Tackle' for Marita. The identification with Mons Johansen's son Johannes rests on the brothers named as guardians (see the 1754 note), not on a direct statement. ¶14

Son Lasse linked: Lasse Monsen of Indre Oppedal (see his page), about 1718-1788, is linked as his son (Confirmed), from the 1739 probates, the 1739 betrothal and the 1754 'Farbroderen Lasse Monsen' guardianship. He is an ancestor of the line through his sons Lasse (→ Mons Lassesen), Torkild (→ Randi Torkildsdatter) and Iver of Botnen (→ Anna Iversdatter) (pedigree collapse; see the notes on Lasse Monsen). ¶22

Mother (Probable): Anna Torkelsdatter, wife of Johannes Larsen of Indre Oppedal, d. before 26 May 1709. Her probate on Indre Oppedal that day (Ytre Sogn 2b fol. 370a) names 'Mons Oppedahl' among 'hans Børn og Arf[vinger]', the husband being Johannes Lars[en]. Probable because the entry says 'his' children, not 'their', and identifies Mons by farm only. Torkild, Mons's youngest son, carries her father's name (inference). ¶24

Life story

1701 census: 'Mons Johansen', aged 22, is listed as the son of 'Johans Lars:' (Johannes Larsen), aged 70, on Indre Oppedal (Bondegaard), Eivindvik (Gulen) parish. Manntall 1701, Digitalarkivet 38455, viewer 54, printed s.91. His birth is therefore about 1679 (inferred from the age of 22), replacing the earlier estimate 'before 1700'. Identity with the Mons Johansen who died at Indre Oppedal before 16 May 1739 is Probable: same farm and name, a fitting age, and no other Mons Johansen at Oppedal in 1701 or in the probate index 1720-1759; his second son Johannes fits naming after Johannes Larsen. See the father's notes for what would confirm it. ¶5

Marriage date (inference): the wedding (copulation) itself has not been found; read the Gulen register for Jul 1702-May 1704 in part without it (for the canvases still unread). It took place after the betrothal of 3 Jun 1703 and before 19 Sep 1704, when 'Mons Indreoppedahls Qvinde' (his wife) was churched (11433, canvas 152), and very probably between Jul and Dec 1703; the other couple betrothed at Brekke the same day married there on 23 Sep 1703 (11433, canvas 122). The marriage date is entered as 'BET 3 JUN 1703 AND 19 SEP 1704'. (Supersedes 'a first wife is not excluded' in the 'Married by 1704 ' note: the betrothal shows him a bachelor in Jun 1703.) ¶19

Marriage: he was married at Brekke church, Gulen parish, at the Brekke service for the feast of the Visitation of Mary (Fest: Visit: Mariæ; the feast day is 2 Jul), dated '5/7' in the margin, so 5 Jul 1703, as couple no. 1 under 'Copulered' (married): 'Juv: Mons Johanssøn Inderoppedahl, Virg: Rannei Iversd: ibid' (the bachelor Mons Johansen of Indre Oppedal and the maiden Randi Iversdatter of the same farm). The service heading 'Fest: Visit: Mariæ: Brecke Comun: 42' (42 communicants) stands at the foot of p. 130 and the marriages head p. 131; the next service, the fifth Sunday after Trinity at Eivindvik, is dated '8/7' in the margin, which fits 1703 (Trinity Sunday 3 Jun 1703). The margin figure beside the heading, in the same pale ink as the '8/7' of the next service, reads '5/7' on a 4x crop (confirmed; had read the digit as 2 or 5). Brekke was an annex church, where feasts were often kept on a weekday after the feast itself, so the wedding is dated 5 Jul 1703 (a Thursday); 2 Jul is only the feast day. Gulen ministerialbok 1699-1705 (Digitalarkivet 11433), canvas 116, left page, p. 131, and canvas 115, right page, p. 130. This supersedes the date 'BET 3 JUN 1703 AND 19 SEP 1704' and the note 'Marriage date (inference)' of patch 036; the marriage entry is now dated 5 JUL 1703 at Brekke. ¶20

Betrothal: on Trinity Sunday, 3 Jun 1703, at Brekke church, Gulen parish, 'Finitis sacris trolovet' (betrothed after the service): '1) Juv: Mons Johansøn Indreoppedahl, 2) Virg: Rannei Iversdatter ibid.' (bachelor Mons Johansen of Indre Oppedal and the maiden Randi Iversdatter of the same farm). Sponsors (forlovere, who vouched that there was no impediment to the marriage): 'Anders Lassesen Indre-oppedahl, Gutorm ibid., Thor Gutormsen ibid., Ole Lassesøn Ytrebradahl' (the patronymic Lassesen is read from the double long s; Ytrebradahl is an uncertain reading). He was therefore unmarried until at least Jun 1703, and Randi Iversdatter is his only known wife. Gulen ministerialbok 1699-1705 (Digitalarkivet 11433), canvas 112, left page, printed p. 123. ¶18

Married by 1704: his wife, not named, was churched at Brekke on 19 Sep 1704 after the birth of Iver (Gulen ministerialbok 11433, canvas 152). He is the earliest-recorded Mons Johansen of Indre Oppedal as a father. That this wife was Randi Iversdatter rests on the 1739 probates only (see the 'Mother kept at Probable' note on Iver Monsen); a first wife is not excluded. ¶13

Index: Mons Johansen is the only Mons in the Ytre Sogn probate index with domicile Oppedal between 1720 and 1759. A separate index row 'Randi Iversdtr, Oppedal Indre, 1738-39' (pa00000000520993, not opened) is probably his widow indexed as a party to the same probate. ¶3

Probate: registration and appraisal after the late Mons Johansen were held on the farm Indre Oppedal in Lavik skipreide on 16 May 1739, between his widow Randi Iversdatter and their children, the sons Iver, Johannes, Peder (3rd), Lasse and Torchel (5th), 'alle Monses Sønner' (all sons of Mons); no daughters and no ages are named. The appraisers were Henrich Indre Oppedahl and Gulleck Ytre Oppedahl; Anders Bøe was present in a role not legible. The probate was closed on 7 Oct 1739: assets 13 rd 4 ort 8 s against debts of about 23 rd 5 ort 8 s, so the estate was insolvent and fol. 122a lists only payments to creditors. Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll 5, fol. 121b-122a. ¶1

Death and birth (inference): he died before 16 May 1739 at or near Indre Oppedal. Birth before about 1700 is inferred from his five sons, the youngest (Torkild) born about 1719. ¶2

Place in the line: father of Torkild Monsen of Asheim (see his page), confidence Probable. Confidence Probable: (1) the 1739 probate names a son Torchel (Torkel/Torkild) of Mons Johansen and Randi Iversdatter of Indre Oppedal, and Torkild of Asheim's patronymic is Monsen; (2) at the 1754 probate after Torkild's first wife the guardian beside the maternal uncle was read as 'Farbroderen Lasse Monsen Indre Oppedal' (paternal uncle Lasse Monsen of Indre Oppedal), and the 1739 probate names a son Lasse; Lasse Monsen of Indre Oppedal's own probate followed in 1783; (3) Mons Johansen is the only Mons in the Oppedal probate index before 1760; (4) Torkild's only known son by his first wife was named Mons (b. about 1753), which fits naming after the paternal grandfather, and his second daughter was named Randi (b. 1744); his eldest daughter, however, was Sigri, so the naming evidence for Randi Iversdatter is weak (inference). ¶4a

Probate entry number: his probate of 16 May 1739 on fol. 121b is numbered Nr. 76 (the 7 is faint; Probable), followed by Randi's lifetime division Nr. 77 (7 Oct 1739) and the Botten probate Nr. 78 (8 Oct 1739). ¶9

Grandson Mons Johansen: the probate of 'ungdomsmand' (unmarried young man) Mons Johansen of Østerbø, Mar 1754 (skifteprotokoll 7a, No. 22, fol. 205b-206a), is that of Johannes Monsen's son. Heirs: his mother Gunilde Lassesdatter, now married to Anders Nielsen Østerbøe, and his sisters Anne and Maritte Johansdatter Waage, both unmarried; guardians 'Farbroderen' (paternal uncle) Lasse Monsen Indre Oppedal for Anne and 'Farbroderen' Andr[?] Monsen Indre Takle for Maritte. This proves that Johannes Monsen had brothers Lasse Monsen (Indre Oppedal) and a Monsen at Indre Takle; that Johannes is Mons Johansen's son Johannes is inferred from these brothers. ¶15

Current grade of his links as a father: Torkild Monsen of Asheim (see his page) is Confirmed (patch 032, audit); Iver Monsen of Botnen (see his page) is Confirmed (patch 034, audit); Britte Monsdatter (see her page) is not linked, a Speculative hypothesis (patch 035, audit). These supersede the Probable grades in 'Place in the line ', 'Pedigree collapse ' and 'Son Iver linked (Probable)', the 'not linked' in 'Possible identity of his son Iver', and the clause in 'Married by 1704' that says her being that wife rests on the 1739 probates only. His own father link to Johannes Larsen stays Probable (patch 027). ¶21

Records that disagree 1

Takle guardian discrepancy: Mons Johansen's third son was Peder, and 'Peder Monsøn Indre Tackle' was named as Farbroder in 1739 (Nr. 78). The 1754 entry reads 'Andr[?] Monsen Indre Takle' (zoom: looped capital, no P-descender, medium confidence), and the 1742 word, zoomed and (-4.jpg), reads 'Ande[r]?' (medium-low; first letter A-like, no P-descender). Mons's sons in 1739 included no Anders, so either the forename is misread, or the Takle Monsen of 1742-54 is a different man. Not resolved; would settle it: a Takle probate for Peder or Anders Monsen, or a Takle entry naming Peder Monsen's death or departure before 1742. ¶16

Corrections 1

Correction: the 'Possible daughter, not linked' hypothesis is resolved against him. Britte Monsdatter (see her page), wife of Torkel Iversen of Indre Oppedal, was a daughter of Mons Torbersen of Østerbø, whose probate of 10 May 1745 (Ytre Sogn 6b fol. 433a) names '3die Datter Brite gift med Torchel Iversen Indre Oppedal'. ¶23

Alternatives considered and set aside 4

Not Confirmed: no record read calls Torkild of Asheim a son of Mons Johansen; the 1739 probate gives no ages and no residence for the sons; and the 'Farbroderen' reading of 1754 is marked uncertain by its reader. ¶4b

Possible identity of his son Iver (not linked): Iver Monsen of Botnen (see his page, born about 1705, buried 15 May 1786) is a candidate for the eldest son Iver named in his 1739 probate. The 1739 probate after Mons Johansen of Indre Oppedal (skifteprotokoll 5, fol. 121b-122a) names his eldest son only as 'Sønnen Iver': it gives no age and no residence for any son, and the estate was insolvent, so no shares were assigned that might name farms. A probable reading 'og som umyndige' (and, as minors) was judged doubtful and cannot be used to exclude or include an adult Iver. The dates fit: Mons Johansen was 22 in the 1701 census (born about 1679) and Iver Monsen of Botnen was born about 1705 (burial index), when Mons would have been about 26; Iver of Botnen named sons Mons and Johannes by his second marriage, which fits Mons Johansen and his father Johannes Larsen, but these are common names. Against or neutral: nothing places Iver of Botnen at Indre Oppedal, none of his daughters (Anna, Eli, Brithe, Lisbeth) is named Randi after Mons's widow Randi Iversdatter, and his first son was named Endre. If they were the same man, Mons Johansen and Randi Iversdatter would be ancestors of this line both through Torkild Monsen of Asheim and through Iver Monsen of Botnen, who already appears twice (through Rasmus Iversen and through Anna Iversdatter): a further pedigree collapse. Records that would settle it: the probate index row for Randi Iversdatter, Oppedal Indre, 1738-39 (pa00000000520993); the probate of Lasse Monsen, Indre Oppedal, 1782-91 (pa00000000525057), which might name his brothers or their heirs; Iver Monsen's own probate (after May 1786); or his first marriage (about 1727, inferred from his daughter Anna's birth about 1728), if a Gulen register for those years survives. ¶6

Homonym (inference): a 'Mons Ifvers: [Indre?]opdal' was betrothed in 1717 and married on 2 Jan 1718 at Brekke to Giertlaug Lassesdatter of Ytre Brekke (Gulen ministerialbok 11438, canvases 16 and 21; 'Mons Iffvers:' and 'Giertlaug Lassesdr' re-read on the c21 crop). He is Mons Iversen, not Mons Johansen, because his bride was not Randi. He may be Randi Iversdatter's brother Mons Iversen, named in her father's probate of 1699 (inference only). ¶11

Possible daughter, not linked (audited; Speculative hypothesis): Britte Monsdatter (see her page), wife of Torkel Iversen of Indre Oppedal, may be his daughter, but his 1739 probate names only five sons and no daughter or son-in-law. See her 'Parentage hypothesis' notes. ¶17
