Life story

Ruled-out baptisms (index, recorded so they are not repeated): Ørskog 24 Oct 1751 (pd00000010638222) is a girl, Marthe, daughter of Per Jons. Brudevold; 15 Apr 1752 (pd00000010638659) Jon son of Anders (Hove); 12 Aug 1752 (pd00000010638918) Jon son of Lars (Gieskemoe). None is a son of Arne. Not yet opened: Jon rows of 2 Jul 1750, 6 Jul 1751, 22 Aug 1751, 13 Jan 1754 and 17 Feb 1754. ¶10

Baptism (index record read): Sunnylven, 7 Feb 1751, 'Jon' (the index marks the name as uncertain), legitimate, father Arne of Løvigen; godparents Rasmus Liøen, Ole Hellesylt, Guro Liøen (Norddal parish, Sunnylven 1736-1760, 1524P; pd00000009561819). This fits 'Joen Arnesen' of Løvigen, who married at Sunnylven in 1776, and Jon of Melset's age. Father added as Probable (see Arne Nilsen Løvigen). The test candidate 'Jan Arnesen b. 1754, Tronstad' is not needed: the 1751 Løvigen baptism is the only Jon son of an Arne in the Sunnylven index 1740-60. Mother unknown: the churchings of Arne's wife do not give her name in the index; the next record is the scan of the 1751 churching (Sunnylven 1736-1760, p. 164 no. 305) or Arne's marriage about 1745-46. ¶15

Marriage found: 'Joen Arnesen, Løvigen' married Gertru Knudsdtr. of Rindalen at Sunnylven (Norddal parish) on 7 Apr 1776, engaged 25 Feb 1776; the trulovere (sureties) were Morten of Gierde and Peder of Hellesylt (index record read). Their son Knud was baptised on 9 Nov 1777 with the parents at Rindal; one godfather was Niels Arnesen of Tryggestad, possibly Jon's brother. Jon came from Løvigen in Sunnylven: an Ingeborg Arnesdtr. of Løvig, b. 1747, was confirmed in Norddal parish in 1763, and an Ingebor Arnesdtr. of Løvigen was a mother at Stranda in 1775 and 1779. She may be a sister. His father Arne of Løvigen is not yet identified. ¶13

Baptism of son: father of Ole, baptised at Ørskog 13 Oct 1782 as 'Jon Arnesen Melsets Barn: Ole' (scan read). Probable as the father of Ole Johnsen of Røssevold; see Ole's notes for the grounds and the conflicting 1819 age. ¶1

Churching (index): on 10 Nov 1782, four weeks after Ole's baptism, Jon Arnesen of Melset (ektemann, husband) and Gertrud Knudsdatter of Melset were entered for her churching (introdusert, a mother's first return to church after a birth). Index rows only; not read on the scan. ¶2

1801 census (index): Melseth, Ørskog, household 03: Jon Arnesen, 50, husbonde (householder), in his 1st marriage, bonde og gaardbeboer (farmer-occupier); wife Giertrud Knudsdatter, 48; children Knud 23, Ole 18 and Birgte 10; lodger Inger Madsdatter, 28. ¶3

Silver tax 1816 (index): Jon Arnesen, gaardbruger (farmer), Mælseth. ¶4

Burial (index record read): Jon Arnesen, vilkaarmand (retired on a farm pension), of Mælset, age 66, buried 21 Apr 1818 at Ørskog (born about 1751-52). A Sunnmøre probate of 5 Jun 1818 indexed under 'John Arnesen' with no residence is a candidate for his estate; not read. ¶5

Probate (partly read): the Nordre Sunnmøre probate register (skifteprotokoll 3A 12, p. 5a-5b) records a skifte (estate division) held on Saturday 5 June 1818 at the farm Mælsæth (Melset), whose opening names 'Gaardmand John Arnesen' (farmer John Arnesen). This fits his burial at Ørskog on 21 Apr 1818. The cramped, faded hand could be read only in fragments: a 'Giertrud' is named (with a date that may be 22 July 1814), but whether as his late wife is not legible, and the list of heirs was not read. No son Ole could be identified in it. A closer line-by-line reading of the continuation (image 7, right-hand column, and image 8, first column) is still needed. ¶6

Probate, closer reading (5 Jun 1818 skifte at Mælsæth, skifteprotokoll 3A 12, fol. 5a-5b): the opening at the foot of fol. 5a reads, as far as legible, '... gaardmand John Arnesen... hans før[?] afdøde Kone Giertrud Knudsd[atte]r[?]...', so the estate appears to be that of John Arnesen and his previously deceased wife. The continuation refers back to an earlier court sitting ('Skifteretten den 22 Julii 1814' [reading uncertain]) and to a date '11 Junii 1788' [context unread], and discusses debts (Gjeld) and the Skifteforvalter. A man 'Ole...' appears with the words 'den afdødes [Søn?]' and '33 Aar' nearby, but the word after 'afdødes' and his patronymic and residence cannot be read. The close of the skifte on fol. 5b names men who appear to be witnesses or appraisers, among them Knud Jakobsen [Mælsæth?], Ole Jakobsen [?], Magnus Olsen Mælsæth [?] and Knud Edvardsen [?]. No formal list of heirs with ages and residences was found in these columns; the scan is a high-contrast black-and-white copy that loses the fainter strokes. The next skifte begins 'Aar 1818 Torsdagen den 11te Junii' (11 Jun 1818 was a Thursday). ¶7

Probate 1818, third reading (full-resolution scans of images 7 and 8 read column by column): the entry is not a division with an heirs list. It opens at the foot of s. 5a ('Mælsæth i Walde Skibrede... Anno 1818'), names 'Gaardmand John Arnesen' and 'hans forhen afdøde Kone Giertrud Knudsd[atter]', refers back to the court's decision of '22 Julii 1814' about the estate after Giertrud, and then deals with debts, a claim and the court's ruling (s. 5a col. 2 to s. 5b col. 1). One line names 'den afdødes' child with an age of 33 [the given name is unclear, possibly Peder]. It closes (s. 5b col. 1, just above the next entry 'Ao 1818') with the people who signed with the court: Knud Jakobsen Mælsæth, Ole Johnsen Rødsevold [= Røssevold], Ole Jakobsen [?], Magnus Olsen Mælsæth and Knud Edvardsen [?]. So Ole Johnsen of Røssevold took part in the settlement of John Arnesen's and Giertrud Knudsdatter's estate in 1818, which fits his being their son. The record does not call him an heir or a son, so the grade stays Probable (graded on the link to Ole Johnsen). The 1815 royal licence (3A 10 fol. 169a) shows that the children kept the estate undivided, which is why no formal heirs list was made. ¶17a

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

Corrections 1

Correction (probate scan read): the Jon Arnesen of Westre (Vestre), Ørskog, who fathered Arne (baptised 4 Apr 1778, Westre) and the 1784 Westre daughter indexed as Maren is a different man. He was dead by 28 Oct 1788, when the Sunnmøre probate court held a skifte (estate division) at Westre after 'afg[angne] Jon Arnesen' between the widow Inger Pauls d[atte]r and the children '1 Søn Arne Jonsen Westre gl. 11[?] Aar' and '2. Datter Martha Giertrue Jonsd[atte]r 3½[?] Aar' (guardian Arne Arnesen Waldermoe; ages faint). His widow's name matches the bride of the Borgund marriage of 22 Jun 1777 (Joen Arnesen, ungkarl, of Westre, and Inger Povelsdtr. of Lille Fylling), so that marriage is his too. Jon Arnesen of Melset was alive in the 1801 census and buried in 1818, with wife Giertrud Knudsdatter, so Arne 1778, the 1784 daughter and the 1777 Borgund marriage do not belong to the Melset couple. The daughter 'Martha Giertrud' is probably the child indexed as 'Maren' in 1784, but this is not proved. ¶9

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Ruled out: Jon Arnesen of Røssevold, Borgund (confirmed 1758, b. 1739), is a different man. He was a father at Røssevold to Siver (16 Feb 1766), Arne (29 Sep 1771), Baar (22 May 1774), Einer (7 Jul 1776) and Arne (1 Apr 1779), all index records read, while the Melset Jon was at Rindal in Sunnylven in 1776-77. In 1776 the Røssevold Jon was a trulovar (surety) for Gunnilde Knudsdtr. of Røssevold, not a groom. Also ruled out: the Ørskog marriage of 1778 (pv00000002198672) of a Gertrud Knudsdatter was to Ole Knudsen of Moldvær. ¶14

Searches that found nothing 2

Probate search 1814 (negative): the sitting headings on images 205-210 of Nordre Sunnmøre skifteprotokoll 3 (1809-1814, s. 837b-843a), where a 22 Jul 1814 sitting at Mælsæth had been expected, were read on the scan. They are Borgund, Valde and other skipreide sittings dated 1811-1814, and none names Mælsæth, Ørskog, John Arnesen or Giertrud. The volume is not kept in date order, so the sitting may lie elsewhere in it (images 1-204, 211-223) or in a separate registration book. No heirs list was read; no change. ¶8

Negative: the 29 Jul 1749 probate after Anne Arnesdatter of Melset (24557/356, scan read) is between her widower Ole Clementsen and their children (Margrethe Olsdatter and others); the facing entry, after Gjertrud Andersdatter of Melset, concerns the household of Gullik Olsen. Neither names an Arne with children. The Sunnmøre probate index has no Arne or Knud among the ten deceased at Melset 1740-1800 (Ole Larsen 1741/1743/1745, Anne Arnesd. 1749, Gjertrud Andersd. 1749, Ole Olsen 1755/1782, Guri Hansd. 1766, Margrete Olsd. 1775, Ole Clementsen 1778). ¶11

Open questions and the next record to read 2

Lead (index only): a Jon Arnesen 'b. 1739, Røssevold' was confirmed at Borgund on 8 Oct 1758 (pk00000002392695), and a Jon Arnesen of Røssevold was a father at Borgund in 1762 and 1771. Røssevold is the farm Ole Johnsen later married into in 1819. If the Melset Jon's age (b. about 1751) proves unreliable, this Røssevold man, son of an Arne of Røssevold, would be a candidate; untested. Anne Arnesdatter of Melset (d. 1749) could also be an aunt. Neither is added. ¶12

What would confirm it: a record that names Ole Johnsen as John Arnesen's son, for example a later probate in the same family or a census or church entry that uses a relationship word. ¶17b
