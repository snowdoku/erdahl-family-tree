About the family

Life story

His birth is in the South Dakota index of birth records over 100 years old as 'EIDEM, AMOS, male, 15 January 1909, UNION' county, and he is named as 'Amos Eidem' among the predeceased siblings in his sister Myrtle's obituary of 2013. He married Ruth Erickson (1912-2002) and is buried beside her in the family churchyard at Elk Point. ¶2

Mayme (Eidem) Eidem's younger brother. South Dakota birth certificate 39448, born 15 January 1909 in Union County, filed 27 February 1909; the father is entered as 'G EIDEM' - a clerk's slip for S. - and the mother as ANNA SATTER. He is named as 'Amos Eidem' among the predeceased siblings in Myrtle (Eidem) Overdahl's 2013 obituary. ¶4

Social Security number [number withheld], an area number issued in Iowa, which is unremarkable at Elk Point, twenty miles from Sioux City. The NUMIDENT death file gives his residence ZIP [withheld], Elk Point. ¶5

Censuses: Amos S. Eidem, son, b. 1909 South Dakota, in the 1910 federal census of Brule township (he was three months old); Amos Eiden, b. abt 1910, in 1920; 'Amos Stefan Eidem, son, b. abt 1909' in 1930; 'Amos S Eidem, b. 1910' in the 1925 South Dakota state census. The 1930 index supplies his MIDDLE NAME, Stefan, which was not previously known. ¶6

1940 federal census: by 1940 he had moved the few miles to Spink township, Union County, and was head of his own household with his wife Ruth A. (NARA T627 roll 3869 image 313). ¶7

He hosted his niece and her husband at Spink in September 1975: 'Friday through Saturday guests of Mr. and Mrs. Amos Eidem were Mr. and Mrs. Harry (Kathryn Eidem) of Pasadena, California' - Leader-Courier, 4 September 1975. She came back in August 1976 and 'visited in the Amos and Sam Eidem homes'. He is therefore the documented link between the California Hayters and the South Dakota Eidems. ¶9

His obituary would have run in a Union County or Sioux City paper in June 1991 and is not online; it would name his sister as 'Mayme Eidem of Arcadia, California' and would be a second independent proof of the identification. ¶3

Obituary: Leader-Courier (Elk Point, South Dakota), June 1991, listing among his survivors 'three sisters, MRS. RAY (SELMA) NELSON OF STURGIS, MRS. PALMER (MAYME) EIDEM OF PASADENA, CALIFORNIA, and MRS. ERNEST (MYRTLE) OVERDAHL of [Worthington, Minnesota]'. The notice settles his sister Selma's husband's given name as RAY - the 1937 obituary of their mother called him Roy and the 1940 obituary of their father called him Raymond - and places Selma at Sturgis, Meade County, by 1991, having moved from Faith in the same county. ¶8
