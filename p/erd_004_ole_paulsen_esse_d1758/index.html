About the family

Mother: Randi Olsdatter of Ytre Esse. Her probate of 20 June 1721 (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll A 3a, fol. 61a-62a) names 'Olle Povelsen' as her 2nd son, with brothers Hans, Anders and Knud Povelsen and sisters Britte (wife of Olle Menes) and Guri (wife of Mons Esse). The brothers match Povel Hansen's 1701 household exactly. This also supports his identification with the 1701 Olle Povelsen. ¶7

Life story

1701 census (probable identification): Olle Povelsen, son (Sønn), age 26 (born about 1675, calculated), in the household of his father Povel Hansen, 66, bonde at Esse (Ytre Esse), Leganger parish, Ytre Sogn, page 31, with his brothers Hans (born about 1669), Anders (about 1683) and Knud (about 1686). He is the only Ole Povelsen at Esse in 1701. The identification with the Ole Povelsen whose probate was held at Ytre Esse in May 1758 rests on farm, patronymic and naming (eldest son Povel after Ole's father; second son Anders, the name of Ole's brother); no record yet states it. Birth year and birthplace are inferred from this census: the census gives no place of birth, and Ytre Esse is assumed because he was living in his father's household there. ¶4

Probate: Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll A 7b, fol. 545b, No. 56, read from the scan. Probate held 6 and 8 May 1758 at Ytre Esse after the late Ole Povelsen, recently buried; it was closed in 1761. His 4 children were the heirs: eldest son Povel Olsen of Ytre Esse (of age); second son Anders Olsen (of age); eldest daughter Randi Olsdatter, dead, who left 2 sons, Ole Hermundsen (10) and Hans Hermundsen (6), with their morbror (mother's brother) Paul Olsen Esse as guardian; and Magdeli Olsdatter, married to Hermund [?]sen Listad (surname uncertain). His widow was 'Mari Hermunds datter' (read twice in the entry, audit); the entry says she moved in with 'sin Søn' (her son) Paul Olsen at Ytre Esse, so she was probably Paul's mother, though a stepmother is not ruled out. The probate closed 14 Sept 1761 with Paul Olsen Esse present. ¶1

Name: the 1758 probate writes his name 'Ole Povelsen' and the 1701 census 'Olle Povelsen'. Povel is the old written form of Paul (Pål), so 'Povelsen' and the tree's 'Paulsen' are the same patronymic. This is a spelling difference, not a conflict. ¶5

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

He is Probable as an ancestor because the link from his son Paul Olsen to Ole Paulsen Esse is Probable (see Paul Olsen Esse). ¶2

Corrections 1

Correction: an earlier note says he is Probable only because the link from his son Paul Olsen to Ole Paulsen Esse was Probable. That link is now Confirmed by Sigri Olsdatter Berge's probate of 12-13 April 1803 (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll A 12, fol. 161b-163a). His own link to Paul is a primary record: his probate of 6-8 May 1758 (A 7b, fol. 545b, No. 56) names his eldest son 'Povel Olsen Ytre Esse', with whom his widow Mari Hermundsdatter went to live. Paul Olsen of Ytre Esse, who died 1761-62, is the only man of that name on the farm. Confidence is therefore raised to Confirmed as Paul's father. ¶6

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Not the same man as Ole Paulsen Esse (Ole Paulsen of Ytre Esse, d. 1799/1800): this Ole died before May 1758, leaving adult children. He is Ole Paulsen Esse's probable grandfather, a namesake under the usual naming pattern. ¶3
