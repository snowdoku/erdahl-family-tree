About the family

Parentage (Probable): in the 1801 census at Wiike (Vike), Veøy, Lasse Andersen, 17, is listed among the children of the farmer Anders Olsen (67, in his second marriage) and Anne Olsdatter (57). His siblings there are Anders (24) and Bereth (26). This fits 'Lasse Anderssen, Wiche', the bachelor who married in 1806. The naming pattern supports it: his children were Berit (1806), Anders (1813), Anne (1816) and Ole (1819). Veøy baptisms for about 1784 are not indexed. ¶5

Life story

Baptism (scan read): on the 2nd Sunday in Advent, 5 Dec 1784, at Vesnes church, Veøy, 'Anders Olsen Wiikes Søn, kaldet Lasse' (Anders Olsen Wiike's son, named Lasse) was baptised. Godparents (sponsores): Kari Knudsdatter Aas, Ingebor Larsdatter Wiike, Erich Mortensen of the same farm, Baard Hansen Leixernes and Christopher Madsen Gilsteen. The mother is not named; this register does not name mothers. The birth was presumably in late Nov or on 1 Dec 1784, since his mother died in childbed on 1 Dec (inference); the date recorded is the baptism bound. ¶8

Marriage (Probable): Lasse Anderssen, a bachelor of Wiche (Vike), married Guraa Olsdatter, maiden, of 'Kaarsan' 3 Dec 1806 in Veøy church. Their daughter Berit had been born at Skorgen on 17 Oct 1806, when the mother is described as 'forlovede' (engaged), which fits this marriage. ¶2

One baptism (Helle, 1810) gives the father as 'Lasse Knuds.' with the same mother and farm. This is probably a transcription slip. ¶3

His status is recorded as 'Hmd.' - husmann, a cottager who rented a small plot and cottage from a larger farm and paid partly in labour - at Skorgen in Grytten parish when his daughter Anne was baptized at Hen church on 21 Jul 1816. He held no land of his own, and the same is true of the two generations of the family that follow. ¶6

Skorgen lies in the Hen part of Grytten parish, which is why his children were baptized at Hen church rather than at Grytten. ¶7

Cottar (husmann) at Skorgen, Grytten. Named as Anne's father in her 1816 baptism and 1831 confirmation. ¶1

Probate (scan read): the estate of Anders Olsen Wiige was registered 19 Dec 1818 and divided at a sitting dated 20 Apr (the clerk wrote 1818, probably for 1819). The heirs are the deceased's six children 'af første Ægteskab' (of his first marriage); no. 5 is 'Lasse Andersen', of age (myndig), followed by a word read 'Kaalmand[?]' or possibly 'Kaarsand[?]' (uncertain). The siblings are Ole (dead, of Gjermundnæs, leaving Hans, Hille and Ellen[?]), Knud Andersen Willa, Anders Andersen Vikbakken[?], Ole Andersen Wiige and Bereth Andersdatter, married to Ole...sen Brøeghaugen[?]. This names Lasse explicitly as Anders Olsen's son. ¶9

Death and burial (index, re-read): 'Lasse Anderssen Skorgen', 'Huusmand' (cottar), aged 45, died 23 Feb 1829. Cause: 'omkom i Elven mellom Weblungsnes og Næs den 23 Februar om Eftermiddagen' (drowned in the river between Veblungsnes and Næs on the afternoon of 23 February). Buried 1829, day not transcribed in the index. Grytten church book, Grytten local parish 1818-1853 (1539P), p. 198, no. 9 (pg00000006506958). ¶11

Records that disagree 1

Age conflict: his baptism on 5 Dec 1784 makes him 44 years and about 2 months old at death, not 45. The register's age is one year high, a common rounding; the index birth year 1784 is calculated from the age and is not independent evidence. ¶12

Corrections 1

Correction: his mother was Hille (Hilde) Knudsdatter (see her page), not Anne Olsdatter (see her page). He was baptised 5 Dec 1784, and Hilde, the wife of Anders Olsen Wiike, died in childbed on 1 Dec 1784 and was buried the same day he was baptised. Anne Olsdatter married Anders only on 30 Jan 1786, as his second wife, and the 1818 probate lists Lasse among the children of Anders's first marriage. Anne, in the house in 1801, was his stepmother. ¶10
