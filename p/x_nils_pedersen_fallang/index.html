Life story

Baptism: Nielss, son of Peder Nielsen of Fallang and Kirsti Pedersdatter, was baptised at Gran church on 29 Sep 1737 (15th Sunday after Trinity, Michaelmas). Godparents: Ole Fallang, Ole Knarud, Hans Pedersen Gamkind, Marie Gamkind, Anne Hansdatter Knarud and Anne Tollofsdatter Knarud. Mother churched 3 Nov (Gran church book 1732-1744, fol. 73v; scan). ¶3

Married Eli Olsdatter of Griner (Grini) at Gran on Thursday 8 Mar 1764, when he was living at Fallang. They had been betrothed at Gran on Tuesday 31 Jan 1764, and the forlovere were Povel Erichsen of Gouttvet and Jens Christophersen of Høgtvet. ¶1

In 1767 Niels Pedersen Fallanger bought out the co-heirs of Peder Nielsen of øvre Fallang (skifte 21 Mar 1767): Peder Pedersen Fallanger, minors Gulbrand and Ole Pedersen (their 'Broderlod', by guardian Anders Gulbrandsen) and Marte Pedersdatter (by her husband), 504 rd 13 s in all, for 2 skippund in Fallang (pantebok 8, DA 10272, fol. 596a-b, No. 6). This places him as Peder Nielsen's successor on the farm alongside his presumed brother Peder (b. 1739); the deed does not call him a son in the lines read. ¶8

1801 census for Gran, Øvre Fallang: Nils Pedersen, 63, gaardmand, with wife Eli Olsdatter (58) and unmarried children Ole (32), Gulbrand (30), Anne (18) and Ragnild (15). His probable son Peder Nilsen heads household 3. Burial: Niels Pedersen of Fallang, buried 15 Jun 1810, aged 78 (b. about 1732). Birth estimates therefore range from about 1732 to 1738. ¶2

Probate (index card): his estate at Fallang was held 22 Jan 1811 (skifteprotokoll 1802-1821, f. 547b), with widow Elie Olsdatter. Gross 562-2-13, net 464-2-0, with 400 rdr standing in Fallang. Children: Peder, Ole and Gulbrand (all of age), Kirsti (m. Christoffer Enarud) and Mari (m. Jens Svensen Gamkind). A card at f. 222a, read as 7 Jan 1806 (gross 2821-1-21), lists the same heirs. The 1806 date conflicts with his burial of 15 Jun 1810 and is unresolved. Probates for his parents Peder Nielsen and Kirsti Pedersdatter were not found among the Fallang cards (DA 39947, images 1643-1661). ¶6

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: the probate protocol itself was read (Hadeland og Land skifteprotokoll 1802-1821, fol. 547b-548a, view 571). Heading 'Skifte efter Niels Pedersen Fallang i Grans Præstegield', 22 Jan 1811, with the widow Elie Olsdatter. It states that Niels Pedersen left these children 'i Ægteskab avlede' (born in the marriage): 1. the son Peder Nielsen, 2. the son Ole Nielsen, 3. the son Gulbrand Nielsen, 4. the daughter Kiersti Nielsdatter, married to Christopher [Enarud], 5. the daughter Mari Nielsdatter, married to Jens Svendsen Gamkind, 6. the daughter Anne Nielsdatter, married to Iver Albretsen Bergerud, and 7. the daughter Ragnild Nielsdatter, 22, unmarried, living at Fallang. The heirs' shares include 'Peder Nielsen Fallang' 200 rdr. The record names Peder Nielsen of Fallang as his son born in the marriage with Eli Olsdatter, which settles his link to Peder Nilsen; confidence raised from Probable to Confirmed. His own parentage (Peder Nielsen and Kirsti Pedersdatter of Fallang) remains Probable, resting on the 1737 baptism. The protocol lists seven children where the index card gave five (adding Anne and Ragnild, both in the 1801 household). ¶7

The case for this identification 1

Identification: he is the only Niels baptised in Gran 1729-1744 to a father Peder living at Fallang, the farm he lived on at his 1764 marriage; the other sons of a Peder (Oppegaarden 1735, Smebølle 1739) are from other farms. His 1801 census age of 63 fits 1737 exactly; his burial age of 78 is about five years high. His eldest son was named Peder. Probable rather than Confirmed because no record yet states the link outright; a skifte (probate) after Peder Nielsen of Fallang naming Nils as heir would confirm it. ¶4

Searches that found nothing 1

Search trap: the Gran index spells the name 'Nielss' in 1733-1738, and a search for Nils or Niels does not return those entries. ¶5
