About the family

Mother of Anders Olsen of Hessen (see his page), Probable; graded on her link to Anders. Borgund og Giske bd. 4 (canvas 372, Hessen, rebuilt from word boxes) gives her as the wife of Anders Jonsen (d. 1699, aged 30), the brukar before Ole Jonsen; she married again in 1700, and the next brukar is Ole Jonsen (d. 1742), whose son Anders Olsen (1704-50) took over in 1731. So she was Ole Jonsen's wife when Anders was born in 1704 (baptised 24 Aug 1704, father Ole Jonss., Hessen). Her son's name Anders follows her first husband, a common custom. Probable only: the marriage to Ole and her motherhood rest on the compiled book; dates and the remarriage word are in rebuilt text. ¶1

Her family (scan re-read): the 1700 probate after Anders Jonsen of Hessen (Sunnmøre skifteprotokoll 3A 01 fol. 299b, 24544/311) names the guardian of her son Olle Andersen as 'barnets Mosters Mand' Iver Nielsen of Hessen. So a sister of Brite was married to Iver Nielsen of Hessen. The appraisers were Iver Larsen and 'Olle Jonssen Hessen'. ¶6

Life story

Engagement (index): Borgund 1700, engagement date 14 Jan, page 3 no. 5: groom Ole Jonsen of Hessen, bride 'Berte Larsdtr.' (no residence), sureties Jon Olsen of Ousnæs and Ole Jonsen of Hessen (pv00000010160817). This is the remarriage the farm history dates to 1700. Her first husband's probate was registered four months later, on 4 May 1700. ¶4

Not a duplicate of Brite Larsdatter (Sogn, d. 1702-07) or Brithe Larsdatter (Sunnylven, m. 1742): different place and generation. ¶2

Churchings (index): 'Brite', wife of Ole, Hessen, churched (introdusert) at Borgund on 5 Oct 1704, p. 443 no. 34 (py00000000089258; a duplicate row is py00000000091683). That is six weeks after the baptism of Anders, son of Ole Jonss. of Hessen, on 24 Aug 1704, the usual gap before a mother was churched. A Brite of Hessen was also churched on 19 Nov 1702 and 19 Jun 1707, matching the Ole Jonss. Hessen baptism of 8 Oct 1702. ¶5

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the identification · 2 open questions

The case for this identification 1

Why still Probable (graded on her link to Anders Olsen, Anders Olsen Hessen): the churching names her as Ole's wife but not the child. She is tied to the 1704 Anders only by timing, and the adult Anders Olsen (m. 1731, d. 1750) is tied to the 1704 baptism by name and farm across 27 years. A possible rival: Hilde, daughter of Ole Jonss. of Hessen, was baptised 5 Feb 1699, before the January 1700 engagement, so by an earlier wife. The 1700 engagement row also lists a second Ole Jonsen of Hessen, as surety. That suggests either a wife between Oct 1696 and 1699 or two men named Ole Jonsen at Hessen; it is not resolved. ¶7

Open questions and the next record to read 2

Test record: the Borgund marriage of 1700 (Ole Jonsen and the widow Brite Larsdatter of Hessen, Borgund ministerialbok 1698-1739) and her churching after 24 Aug 1704; her probate (Hessen) naming Anders Olsen. ¶3

Test record: the scan of the 5 Oct 1704 churching (Borgund ministerialbok 1698-1739, p. 443 no. 34) and of the 24 Aug 1704 baptism (godparents, mother's name if given); a probate after Brite Larsdatter at Hessen naming Anders Olsen (the Sunnmøre probate index has none at Hessen 1700-1771, ids pa00000000781075-781098). ¶8
