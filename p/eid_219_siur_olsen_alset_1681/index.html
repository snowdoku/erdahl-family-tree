Life story

1701 manntall, Selbu, farm Aldset (Alset) (canvas 118, crop checked by the orchestrator): 'Siur Olsen 20', son of the farmer Ole Jonsen (80); his brother 'Biørn, Soldat, 28'. ¶1

Widower at his wife Gjertrud Jonsdatter's probate, 11 Feb 1732, as 'Siver Olsen Alset', with eight children; the guardian of the daughters was 'farbroren Biørn Olsen' - his brother Bjørn, the soldier aged 28 at Alset in 1701 (card 77804 c21). ¶5

The 1732 probate protocol entry has now been read (Stjør- og Verdal skifteprotokoll 2B, Digitalarkivet 24751, fol. 401b, view 29; 'Anno 1732 d. 11 Febr.', Alset, after 'afgangne Giertrud Joensdatter'). The widower is Siver Olsen Alset, and the children are 'alle hiemme paa gaarden Alset': 1. Ole Siversen 30, 2. Anders Siversen 25, 3. Baro (Bård) Siversen 15, 4. Ole Siversen 13, 5. Joen Siversen 13, 6. Mali Siversdatter 26, unprovided for, 7. Inger Siversdatter 20, 8. Berit Siversdatter 18. The card (77804 c21) copies these correctly. The Jon of 1732 is 13 (born about 1719, apparently a twin of the younger Ole), so the Jon baptised in Oct 1707 (16310 c31) is not among the heirs and probably died young. Collateral: the Jon of 1732 is 'Joen Syvertsen Garberg', guardian as 'Farbroder' at his brother Ole's probate in 1768 (24756 fol. 92a), and Jon Siursen at Garberg 736 in the 1762 extra tax list (49639 c132), with his niece Gjertrud Olsdatter in the house. The cross-line lead that made him the Jon Siursen of Eidem on line 8 (see his page) is refuted: that man had a different wife in 1762. ¶7

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Speculative father of Ole Sivertsen Alset (see his page): he is the only Siur/Sivert at Alset in 1701, and Ole Sivertsen, who held Alset from about 1730, gave his first two sons (1730, 1731) the name Sivert, as a first son was named for the paternal grandfather. Nothing names Ole's father directly, so this rests on name, farm and naming custom only. ¶2

Corrections 1

Correction: raised from Speculative to Confirmed by the baptism of his son: 'Siür Alsets barn Olaf', 28 Oct 1703, with the churching of 'Siür Alsets qv. Gertrud efter det 1ste barn' (16310 c17;, crop checked by the orchestrator). He is the only Siur at Alset in the 1701 manntall (Siur Olsen, 20, son of Ole Jonsen), and Ole Sivertsen Alset is the only Ole born to a Siur at Alset; Ole's own records (1737) call him 'Ole Siverthsen Alset'. Married 4th Sunday after Trinity (9 Jul) 1702 to Gjertrud (16310 c13 left). A daughter Mali followed in 1705 (c22-23). ¶4

Open questions and the next record to read 2

What would raise it: Siur Olsen Alset's burial or marriage in 16310 (1699-1737), or a probate in the Stjør- og Verdal series (24749-24764) naming Ole as his son. ¶3

Biography gaps: his exact birth date (c.1681) predates the 1699 Selbu register, so his baptism is not in any surviving church book. His death, death place and burial after his February 1732 widowerhood are not found: the 16310 Alset entries (canvases 118-147) and the 16315 burials were read without a match, and any burial in 1747-56 falls in the Selbu register gap. His probate is next in Stjør- og Verdal skifte 3 (24753, 1742-60, unindexed), since the 77804 index stops at 1742. ¶6
