About the family

Father of Giertrud Knudsdatter (see her page), Probable. Why this is our Giertrud: (1) Gertru Knudsdtr. of Rindalen (=Ringdal) married Joen Arnesen of Løvigen at Sunnylven on 7 Apr 1776 (index record read). (2) On 9 Nov 1777 Knud, son of Joen Arnesen and Gertru Knudsdtr. of Rindal, was baptised in Norddal parish. The godparents were Morten Persen Gierde, Lars Knudsen Rindal, Niels Arnesen Tryggestad, Marthe Knudsdtr. Rindal and Olave Knudsdtr. Rindal. Lars, Marthe and Olava are three of the siblings that the 1759 probate names alongside Giertrud. (3) Knud Jonsen of Melset, Ørskog, was confirmed in 1795 aged 17 and was 23 in the 1801 census, in the household of Jon Arnesen and Giertrud Knudsdatter of Melset, both in their 1st marriage. His age fits the 1777 Knud. Their next son Ole was baptised at Ørskog in 1782. (4) Giertrud Knutsdtr. of Ringdal was confirmed at Geiranger in 1770 aged 16. The ages vary: 8 in 1759, 16 in 1770, 48 in 1801 and 'b. 1750' at her 1815 burial, which points to about 1751-54. No single record names Giertrud of Melset as the daughter of Knud Olsen, so the parents are Probable: the case rests on the matching names and the godparent pattern. ¶2

Parents not found. Leads: a Knut Olsen of Fivelstad died by 11 Sep 1703 leaving a widow Giertrud Olsdatter and two children, Ole Knudsen and Anne Knudsdatter (Sunnmøre skifteprotokoll 3A 02 fol. 7a, 24545/17, scan read). If Knud (married 1742) was a son of that Ole Knudsen, Knud would be named for his grandfather and his daughter Giertrud for his grandmother, but no record of Ole Knudsen at Fivelstad after 1703 has been found, so nothing is added. The 1759 guardian of Knud's son Lars was Arne Arnesen of Fivelstad (married at Sunnylven 11 Apr 1746), possibly a relative. ¶7a

Parents: probably the son of Ole Knudsen of Fivelstad (b. about 1683/85, buried 1739), grandson of Knut Olsen of Fivelstad (d. by 1703). See Ole Knudsen Fivelstad for the grounds; Probable only. Mother unknown. His own grade (as Giertrud's father) is unchanged. ¶11

Life story

Marriage (index record read): Knud Olsen of Fivelstad, Sunnylven, married Brite Larsdtr. of Rindalen on 29 Dec 1742 (engaged 23 Jun) at Sunnylven (1524P p. 138 no. 35, pv00000002059243). So he came from Fivelstad and married into Store Ringdal. ¶4

First child (index record read): Ole, son of Knud of Fivelstad, baptised at Sunnylven on 15 Apr 1743; godparents Iver Eriksen and Magrete of Sæteren, Lars of Karbøen (1524P p. 140 no. 119; pd00000009560916). This is the 'Ole Knudsen 16' of the 1759 probate. A son Anders, child of Knud of Ringdalen, was baptised at Sunnylven in the 1750s (scan 17005 canvas 52, p. 171; not in the 1759 probate, so presumably dead). ¶10

Baptism of Giertrud (index record read): Sunnylven, 12 Apr 1751, 'Giertrud', legitimate, father 'Knud Olsen' of Ringdalen; godparents Arne of Fivelstad, Gammel of Øyene, Marta of Lille Ringdalen (Norddal parish, Sunnylven 1736-1760, 1524P, p. 164 no. 296; pd00000009561840). This is the 'Giertrud Knuds Datter gl. 8 Aar' of the 1759 probate. Rival noted: a Giertrud, daughter of Knud of Molschredal, was baptised at Sunnylven on 17 Nov 1754 (pd00000009562258); she is not of Ringdal and not in the 1759 probate. ¶5

Probate (scan read): 'Store Ringdahl udi Sunelvens Schibr[ede]. Anno 1759 d. 26 July' a skifte was held after the late 'Brithe Lars Datter' between her surviving husband [Knud] Olsen (the left edge of the name is in the gutter; the share list on fol. 232a names 'Enkemanden Knud Olsen', the widower) and their 6 children: Ole Knudsen 16 years (guardian Haagen Thorsen Lille Ringdahl), Lars Knudsen 12 (guardian Arne Arnesen Tibelstad), Marthe Knudsdatter 17 (guardian Anders Olsen Tryggestad), 'Giertrud Knuds Datter gl. 8 Aar' (guardian Ole Andersen [Sætre?]), Synneve Knudsdatter 6 (guardian Ole Jensen Lange Landskoug) and Olava Knudsdatter 3 (guardian Haagen Thorsen Lille Ringdahl). The share list on fol. 232a has 'Sønnen Ole Knudsen', 'Sønnen Lars Knudsen', 'Datteren Marthe Knudsdatter' and 'Datteren Giertrud Knudsdatter'. Image. ¶1

He was alive and a widower on Store Ringdal, Sunnylven, in Jul 1759. A Lars Knutsen of Store Ringdal had a probate registered in 1815 (3A 10 fol. 176a) and a Marthe Knutsd. of Store Ringdal in 1819 (3A 12 fol. 205a). These are probably Giertrud's siblings, and their heirs might name her children; not read. ¶3

Remarriage (index record read): Knud Olsen of Ringdal married Eli Stephansdtr. of Korsbreche at Sunnylven on 20 Jul 1760 (engaged 24 Jun); banns asked by Haagen of Ringdal, trulovar Ingebrigt of Ringdal (1524P p. 194 no. 158; pv00000002059693). A 'Knud Olsen' of Rindalen was a godparent on 1 Oct 1775 (pd00000010324668). ¶6

1762 extra tax (scan read): Store Ringdalen, Sunnylven, kirkegods: 'Knud Olsen og Elen' (his second wife Eli Stephansdatter, m. 1760), children over 12 'Ole Knuds. og Marta' (source 49637, view 235, p. 29). These are the Ole Knudsen (16) and Marthe Knudsdatter (17) of the 1759 probate after Brithe Larsdatter. Giertrud (b. 1751) was under 12 and is not listed, so this does not change his grade. ¶12

Burial (index record read): Knud Olsen of Rindal, aged 66, buried at Sunnylven on 5 Jun 1786 (Norddal ministerialbok 1774-1816, 1524P, p. 56 no. 258; pg00000001819256). Born about 1720. No probate after him is indexed at Ringdal (rows jump from 1775 to 1803). ¶9

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
