Life story

Aaland (Innvik 1974 ed., Rustøen, Haraldbruket): 'Harald Klausson, f. ikr. 1661, b. 1698 1 1/2 l. med presten H. Schreuder; sk. 1709...; sk. 1732 på 7 mk. krg.... K.: e. etter R. (Barbrå Olsd. Kårstad). Bruket har namn etter Harald.' ¶2a

1701 census (Innvik parish, Røstøen): 'Harald Claes.', opsidder, b. 1661, with 'Sønn' Rasmus b. 1700, Halsteen Rasmus. b. 1691 and Olle b. 1693 (Halsten, by his patronymic, is a stepson). ¶1

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the grade · 1 alternative set aside · 2 searches with no result · 1 open question

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Probable as father of Rasmus Haraldsen Rustøen (see his page), graded on that link: the 1701 index labels Rasmus b. 1700 as Harald's son on Rustøen, and Rasmus Haraldsen (d. 1787 aged 88, so b. about 1699) took over the same bruk from his father in 1737 (Aaland). ¶3a

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Not Confirmed: 36 years between the 1701 census and the 1737 takeover with no record read in between. ¶3b

Searches that found nothing 2

Negative, grade unchanged: Nordfjord probate index by farm (domicile Rust*/Røst*/Rystø*, 1690-1800, probate filter). The only Rustøen rows are Marite Olsdtr (A 9, 1744), Rasmus Christoffersen (A 13, 1758-62) and Sigri Antonsdtr (A 20b, 1799-1803). The 'Røst' rows are another farm: A 7 p. 398 (Kari Pedersdtr, 1731-34) is next to a Røst estate with widow Else Olsdatter and son Simen Rasmussen. So no indexed probate survives for Harald Klausen or for Barbro Olsdatter; theirs would be in the unscanned Arvebok (1715-27) or never registered. Guardianship protocol 1, fol. 169b (scan db50002645700173, read): 'Rasmus Haraldsen Røstøen' held 12 rd 1 ort 6 s for 'Christi Rasmusdatter Avlem' from the skifte of 19 May 1734 (Arvebok fol. 269). On 8 Jul 1735 he was relieved and 'barnets fader Rasmus Avleid' took over. Protocol 3, p. 196 (db50002645900199, read): Rasmus Avlem holds the money for 'hans egen Datter Christi Rasmusdatter' and for his stepdaughter Giertrud Andersdatter (13 Jun 1725). No relationship word links Rasmus Haraldsen to the girl, and the deceased of 19 May 1734 is not named. Test record for Harald as Rasmus's father: the Nordfjord tingbok deed of 1737, when 'faren sa opp' the bruk to Rasmus (Aaland), or Barbro Olsdatter's skifte in the Arvebok (Statsarkivet i Bergen). ¶4

Negative, grade unchanged: Nordfjord tingbok L0027 (1735-38, DA 30935, no IIIF manifest; viewer images read). Olden skipreide sessions read for deeds, leases and odel declarations: spring ting 21-22 Mar 1737 (v397; deeds only for Oppheim and Eide), autumn ting 8 Nov 1737 at Faleide (v471R-473; deeds for Oppheim, Løken/Loen, Eide and Malene, then administrative items), spring ting 20 Mar 1738 at Tonning (v500R-501L; only an Oppheim skiftebrev). The neighbouring Stryn skipreide sessions of 18-20 Mar 1737 (v394R-396) and 2-6 Jul 1737 at Tonning (v422-425) were also read. No entry names Røstøen/Rustøen, Harald Klausen or Rasmus Haraldsen, so the 1737 change of holder (Aaland: Rasmus 'b. 1737... som faren sa opp') was not read at these tings; a lease (bygselbrev) from the priest was often never tinglyst. No Olden summer session for 1737 was found between v416 and v426. Test record (unchanged): Barbro Olsdatter's skifte in the Nordfjord Arvebok (1715-27, Statsarkivet i Bergen), or the Innvik parish priest's bygsel copy book; also the Olden sessions of the autumn of 1736 (before v380) and the 1738-41 books (L0028 = DA 30936 starts 1741, so 1739-40 may be missing). ¶5
