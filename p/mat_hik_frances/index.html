Life story

Baptism: Frances Carpenter Thompson, born 13 November 1813 and baptised 1 January 1814, daughter of Gordon and Catharine Thompson. The entry is in the Charlottetown Anglican register (FamilySearch film 005240361, image 117; Ancestry 61501 record 17738 and PEI Baptism Index 9195 record 102330). The film is identified as St Paul's, Charlottetown, from the entries it carries. ¶4

Maiden name: Gordon according to the compiled Hicken genealogy (married George Hicken 31 January 1837 at Gaspereaux); Thompson according to the compiled Creed genealogy. Gordon is preferred: her 1881 census origin is Scottish, and her granddaughter was named Edith Gordon Hicken. Her daughters' gravestones spell her name 'Francis'. Death 22 April 1887 at Gaspereaux is from the compiled Hicken genealogy; no gravestone found. ¶2

Marriage entry: George Hicken, bachelor, and Frances Thompson, spinster, married by licence on Tuesday 31 January 1837 by J. L. Lewellin, J.P.; witnesses Philip Beers, George Hicken Senr. and Thomas Reynolds Senr. No place, residence or parents are given (PEI marriage register p. 249, FamilySearch film 004594917 image 146; image read). ¶10

Grandmother of Lydia Adelaide (Hicken) Hayter. 1881 census (Township 61, household 38): Frances Hicken, 67, Wesleyan Methodist, of Scottish origin, born Prince Edward Island, wife of George Hicken. ¶1

1881 census (Township 61, household 38): Frances Hicken, 67, born P.E.I., origin Scottish, Wesleyan Methodist, wife of George Hicken, 69. ¶7

Death: on 22 April 1887 at Gaspereaux, per the compiled Hicken genealogy. She is absent from the 1891 census, where George is a widower. No PEI death card or church burial entry for her has been found in the indexes, and no gravestone in either Sturgeon cemetery transcription. ¶8

The case for this identification 2

Why 'Gordon': Gordon was her father's given name. Her son William John named his daughter Edith Gordon Hicken (b. 1887), apparently after this great-grandfather, and a later compiler took 'Gordon' for Frances's surname. ¶5

Identification: the link between the Charlottetown baptism of 1813 and George Hicken's wife rests on four points. The name matches the 1837 marriage register. The birth date fits her age of 67 in the 1881 census. She was born in P.E.I., as the 1881 census and her son's 1891 entry say. And she is the only Frances Thompson of that age in the PEI baptism index. Her 'Scottish' origin in 1881 is her paternal origin. ¶6

Corrections 1

Correction: her maiden name was Thompson, not Gordon. The PEI marriage register for 31 January 1837 calls her Frances Thompson (Ancestry 61502, record 5377). The compiled Hicken genealogy on islandregister.com calls her 'Frances Gordon'; the compiled Creed genealogy has Thompson. ¶3
