Life story

1701 census: Anders Monsen, 52, farmer (bonde) at Tyom og Grøneng (Tjugum/Grøneng), with sons Anders (12) and Mons (8). The census confirms he was the father of Anders Andersen. kgroenha.net no. 1868 gives his wife as Brita Hansdatter Møland. He is rated speculative only because the link from Anders Andersen down to Randi is unsourced. ¶1

1701 census, re-read: Anders Monsen, bonde, age 52, heads the 'Tyom og Grøneng' entry (Leganger parish, page 31) with sons Anders Andersen (12) and Mons Andersen (born about 1693), servants Anders Hermundsen, Michel Endresen and Hans Olsen, then a second bonde, Niels Monsen (born about 1653), with sons Mons and Andfind Nielsen and servants Mons Torbergsen and Gutorm Erichsen. The record does not say which holding (Tjugum or Grøneng) each bonde farmed, or whether Niels Monsen was Anders's brother. Stays Speculative for the same reason as his son: the link from the 1701 boy Anders Andersen down to Randi Andersdatter is unsourced. ¶2

Laberg 1985 (Tjugum, printed pp. 102-104): by the 1666 census Mons [of Tjugum] had [sons] Anders, Nils and Endre. Anders Monsson became holder from [about] 1677; he was born [about] 1654 (the 1701 census age 52 gives about 1649) and married Brita, who died 1685, and then Ragnhild. His brother Nils is named. Anders Monsson died 1730, leaving a widow and children including Anders, who got the farm, and Jon; Anders Andersson, who got the farm, was born [about] 1688. ¶3

Probate 1-2 May 1730 at Tjugum after 'Anders Monsøn, forhen betroed Lensmand for Tiugums Skibrede': widow Ragnilde Olsdatter and her children with him Anders and Jon (of age), Ole and Siri[?] (under age; guardian Truels Ulvestad, their father's sister's husband); and the children of his first marriage with the late Bridtte Hansdatter, Mari (m. Omund Korpedall) and Marithe (m. Elling [surname unread]), of age. ¶5

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Confidence raised from Speculative to Probable: the 1701 census lists Anders Monsen with a son Anders aged 12, and Laberg 1985 states that this Anders Andersson succeeded him; the son is now confirmed as Randi Andersdatter's father by the 1750 probate. By the book's dates the first wife Brita died in 1685, before Anders Andersson's birth (about 1688), so his mother would be the second wife Ragnhild, not kgroenha.net's 'Brita Hansdatter Møland' ('Møland' has 0 hits in the book). ¶4a

Confidence raised from Probable to Confirmed on the 1730 probate (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll 3b, fol. 419a), which names him at Tjugum with a son Anders of age, as in the 1701 census. ¶6a

Records that disagree 1

Vicar's census 23 May 1666, Tjugum N.13: 'Mogens bruger 42 aar' with 'Anders 12 aar', 'Niels 7 aar' and a 3-year-old (name reads like 'Anders'; Laberg: Endre) in the sons column. Anders 12 gives birth about 1654, agreeing with Laberg 1985; the 1701 census age (52) gives about 1649. Birth left at ABT 1649. Father Mogens (Mons), lensmann in 1664, added as Probable. ¶7

Corrections 1

Correction: kgroenha.net's 'Brita Hansdatter Møland' was his FIRST wife (the probate's 'Bridtte Hans Datter') and mother only of Mari and Marite; Anders Andersen's mother was the second wife Ragnhild Olsdatter. ¶6b
