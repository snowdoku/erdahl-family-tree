Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Speculative mother of Pål Bjertesen of Einbu (see his page); graded on her link to Pål. Only the Lesja bygdebok (vol. 1 canvas 180) names her: 'Magnhild og Bjerte hadde kanskje to søner, og kanskje ei dotter: 1. Pål, ca. 1604, tok over. 2. Ola, ca. 1608, sjå Nørdre Kvam. 3. Marit', and has her holding Einbu until 1628, when Pål took over. The author's own 'kanskje' (perhaps) and the lack of any record naming her as Pål's mother keep her Speculative. Pål named a daughter Magnhild (bygdebok, children alive 1676: '6. Magnhild'), and Oluf Biertesen of Kvam's son Endre also had a daughter Magnhild, which fits but proves nothing. ¶1

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Test record: the Gudbrandsdal lensregnskap/skattemanntall of the 1620s showing 'Magnild Inneboe' as widow on Einbu, and a court or land record of 1628 transferring Einbu to her son Pål. ¶2
