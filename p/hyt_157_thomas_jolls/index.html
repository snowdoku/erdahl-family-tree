About the family

Wives and Sarah's mother: the Boston register has Thomas, born 25 Apr 1672, and Jonathan, born 21 Mar 1674, both 'of Thomas & Abigail Jolls', and Robert, born 2 Jun 1677, 'of Thomas & Susanna Jolls' (Record Commissioners 9: 123, 133, 142). No Boston marriage of Thomas Jolls is recorded and no Sarah Jolls birth. The Prince Genealogy (pp. 23-25) names Abigail as Sarah's mother, though its own narrative says only 'probably a daughter Sarah... perhaps b. in 1675'. Sarah married Christopher Kimball in 1701 and bore a child in 1703/4, so a birth between Mar 1674 and the Susanna marriage (before mid-1676) fits best, which makes Abigail the likeliest mother. Entered as Speculative. ¶5

Life story

Origin lead (Speculative parents entered): a Thomas, son of Thomas Jolls, mariner of Ratcliff, and Sarah (Perkins, m. 9 Sep 1641), was baptised at Stepney, St Dunstan, 17 Jun 1642 (FreeREG). It fits his age, his trade, his London ship and his children's names, but no record ties him to Stepney; his own grade (on his link to Sarah) is unchanged. Also: PROB 11/427/237 (PCC, 7 Oct 1695), 'Thomas Jolls, belonging to His Majesty's Hired Ship the Samuel and Henry, Mariner and Shipwright', is very probably his son Thomas (Boston will 13 Oct 1693, proved 3 Oct 1696), proved first in London. ¶11

Corroboration from printed records: (1) Boston births (Record Commissioners vol. 9) record Thomas (1672) and Jonathan (1674), children of Thomas and Abigail Jolls, and Robert (1677), son of Thomas and Susanna Jolls; the book gives Thomas's mother as Rebecca, but the register says Abigail. (2) Suffolk Deeds XIII:106 has land 'in the occupation of Capt. Thomas Jolls', and XIV:321 land 'in the Occupacon of Hannah Jolls relict of Thomas Jolls Decd', so he was dead by then and his widow was Hannah. (3) At the Suffolk Inferior Court of Pleas sitting of 2 April 1695, both 'Thomas Jolls, Boston, shipwright' and 'Sarah Jolls, Boston, spinster' sued 'Hannah Jolls, widow, admx.' for money due (Sarah claimed £80 and was awarded £49 4s 6d). Two Jolls children suing their father's widow and administratrix is what heirs claiming their portions would do, and it puts an unmarried Sarah Jolls in Boston six years before 'Sarah Joles' married Christopher Kimball there (3 Apr 1701). (4) The 1713 Boston intention of 'Sarah Kemball of Boston' with Thomas Shurtleff of Plymouth fits the book's account of her later life. ¶2

Fourth marriage produced a son (Correction to the Prince Genealogy, which says he had no issue by Hannah): the will of Richard Winslow, merchant, of Boston, 31 Mar 1697, proved 26 Feb 1707 (Suffolk Probate 16:413), leaves everything to 'my Honrd Mother Hannah Jolls of Boston Widdow' and after her to 'my Brother John Jolls', and failing him to the children of 'my Uncle John Briggs late of Cohassett', 'my uncle James Briggs' and 'my Uncle Cornelius Briggs' (Mayflower Descendant 10:55-56). So Thomas's widow Hannah, administratrix in 1695, was Hannah (Briggs), widow of Capt. Samuel Winslow, and she and Thomas had a son John Jolls, born after their marriage of 1681 and alive in 1697. Hannah is not Sarah's mother. ¶4

Estate: docket 1452 (Suffolk probate docket book): administration and inventory 1686 at vol. 9 p. 265 (not read), account 13:523, distribution 13:524 (read, 10 Jan 1694/5). Jonathan (b. 1674) is not among the four surviving children in Jan 1694/5, so he died young. A piece of land valued at £40 in the inventory was left undivided; it is the Black Horse Lane lot that Sarah quitclaimed to Robert in 1706/7. The distribution does not name the children's mother. ¶8

Will of his son Thomas read (signed-in lane): Suffolk Probate vol. 11 pp. 213-214, docket 2334. 'I Thomas Jolls of Boston... Shipwright being bound out to sea' leaves all to 'my Dear and welbeloved Sister Sarah Jolls of Boston Spinster', 'Except what plate shall come from my Fathers Estate and belong unto me, and all my Wearing Cloaths and Working Tooles, which I give to my Brother John Jolls'; Sarah sole executrix. Dated 13 Oct 1693; proved 3 Oct 1696, administration to 'Sarah Jolls of Boston... Spinster, Sister of the said deceased'. Correction to the Prince Genealogy: the testator was of Boston, not of New York. John Jolls is Capt. Thomas's son by Hannah (Briggs) Winslow (Richard Winslow's will), and the shipwright is the Thomas born 1672 to Thomas and Abigail, so the will makes Sarah Jolls a sister of two of Capt. Thomas's sons and shows their father dead with an unsettled estate by 1693. On its own the will does not name the father and calls her only 'Sarah Jolls, spinster'; the 1706/7 deed (Suffolk Deeds 23:215v) closes both gaps. ¶9

Administration: 'At a County Court for Suffolk holden at Boston 26th January Ao 1685/. Full Power and Authority to administer all and singular the goods Chattells Credits and Estate of Thomas Jolls late of Boston Marriner decd intestate was granted unto Hannah his Relict widow; And She hath exhibited an Inventory... April 15. 1686', followed by 'An Inventory of the Estate of Thomas Jolls Shipmaster taken February 10th 1685' (Suffolk probate records vol. 9 p. 265, docket 1452; FamilySearch DGS 7703072 image 438; image read). No children are named. It confirms that his widow in 1686 was Hannah, his third recorded wife after Abigail (children 1672, 1674) and Susanna (1677). It does not say which wife was the mother of Sarah (see her page). ¶10

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Confidence Probable, not Confirmed: the will of the younger Thomas Jolls (Suffolk Probate Records vol. 11 p. 213, c.1696) and the 1706 deed to Robert Jolls, which would tie Sarah to this family by name, have not been read. The court abstract does not state the relationship. Sarah's mother is not entered: if she was 21 when she sued in 1695 she was born by 1674, so her mother was Rebecca or Abigail; the book guesses Abigail. ¶3

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed, graded on his link to his daughter Sarah Joles. Suffolk Deeds Lib. 23 fol. 215v-216 (deed of 15 Jan 1706/7, recorded 3 Apr 1708; page image read on FamilySearch): 'Sarah Kimball of Boston in the County of Suffolk in New England Widow only Daughter of Thomas Jolls late of Boston Marriner deced' quitclaims to 'Robert Jolls of Bridgewater in the County of Plymouth... Yeoman Only Surviving Son of my said Father Thomas Jolls deced' her share of her father's lot in Black Horse Lane, Boston, which 'Thomas Jolls Eldest Son of the said Thomas Jolls in and by his Last Will and Testament gave unto her' (that will is Suffolk Probate 11:213, read). The deed names the father-daughter relationship, and so does the distribution of his estate (Suffolk Probate 13:524, docket 1452, 10 Jan 1694/5, read): on the account of 'Hannah Jolls Relict Widow and Administratrix' of 'Thomas Jolls late of Boston Mariner deced Intestate', £439 7s 5d is divided, one third to the widow and the rest to 'Thomas, Sarah, Robert and John Jolls the four surviving Children of the said deced', Thomas as 'the Eldest Son' taking a double share and 'Sarah Jolls, Robert Jolls and John Jolls' £58 11s 8d each, saving the widow's claims 'for Education... since the death of the said Thomas Jolls their Father'. Continuity: Sarah Joles married Christopher Kimball in Boston on 3 Apr 1701, and by Jan 1706/7 Sarah Kimball of Boston is a widow born Jolls; there is no other Jolls-Kimball marriage in Boston, so there is no rival. The deed also shows that his son John (by Hannah) was dead by Jan 1707, Robert being 'only surviving son'. ¶7

The case for this identification 1

Identification as father of Sarah (Jolls) Kimball (Probable): the Prince Genealogy (c.1902), pp. 23-24, says 'Sarah Jolls was the only dau. of Capt. Thomas Jolls, a retired ship-master and merchant of Boston'; that he had four wives (Rebecca, Abigail, Susanna, and Hannah, widow of Capt. Samuel Winslow, married 1681) and children Thomas (b. 25 Apr 1672), Jonathan (b. 21 Mar 1674), 'probably a daughter Sarah (whose birth is not recorded), perhaps b. in 1675', and Robert (b. 2 Jun 1677); that he died in 1686 at his wife's house in Black Horse Lane (later Prince Street), his estate appraised at £566; that 'his dau. Sarah inherited by will (vol. 11, p. 213) all the property of her brother Thomas, a shipwright of New York who d. in 1696'; and that in 1706 she deeded a Prince Street lot to her brother Robert of Bridgewater and moved there with her children Christopher and Abigail, marrying Thomas Shurtleff in 1713. ¶1

Searches that found nothing 1

Origin (negative): nothing found on his English origin. As a ship-master he brought passengers from England to Nantasket (Diaries of John Hull) and commanded the Richard from London (Ford, The Boston Book Market 1679-1700). His son Robert Jolls settled in Bristol, Rhode Island (selectman and constable there in the 1720s-30s; Bristol records). ¶6
