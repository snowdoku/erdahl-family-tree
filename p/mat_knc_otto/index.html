About the family

Youngest child of Gottlieb Knodel and Anna Karolina Schmidt. He is buried in Eatonia Cemetery beside his parents under a shared family monument: 'MARY 1926-1992 | OTTO 1918-1973'. Find a Grave (memorial 229408781) says that he married Marie Mary (Mary Dorothy) Knippelberg on 15 April 1945 at Eatonia, Saskatchewan. He apparently stayed on the Saskatchewan farm when most of his siblings moved to British Columbia. ¶1

Life story

Birth: Born at 10 a.m. on 11 January 1918 at Pfeilsdorf (Płużnica), West Prussia, in his father's dwelling; registered 15 January 1918 as no. 2 by the midwife Antonie Kunkowski née Naß, who was present at the birth; mother Karoline Knodel née Schmidt, wife of the farm owner (Besitzer) Gottlieb Knodel, both Evangelical. Deputy registrar Felske. The youngest child found. ¶3

Emigration: landed at Quebec on 22 July 1928, aged 10, from the Megantic (from Southampton) with his parents; last residence Płużnica. ¶5

1931 census: Otto Knodel, 12, born Germany, immigrated 1928, a student (8 months' schooling in the year), in his parents' household on NW 27-25-24-W3, RM of Newcombe No. 260, Saskatchewan. ¶4

Voters lists: Otto Knodel and 'Mrs Otto' Knodel of Eatonia appear on the Kindersley federal lists of 1957, 1958, 1962 and 1965. He was still farming in the Eatonia district. ¶6

Emigration: Canadian passenger-list index (Ancestry 'Canada, Incoming Passenger Lists, 1865-1935', anonymous search) lists Gottlieb Knodel (b. abt 1879), Karolina (abt 1881), Frieda (abt 1912), Erna (abt 1915) and Otto (abt 1918) on one manifest page, arriving 22 July 1928, destination Winnipeg, Man. Index keywords that match this record (tested one by one) are Quebec, Southampton, Megantic, Polish, German, Farmer and Married: i.e. the White Star-Dominion liner Megantic, Southampton to Quebec, the family travelling as Polish nationals of German 'race'. The manifest image (Quebec reel, 1925-35 series) has not been seen; it should give their last residence (expected Plużnica) and the relative they were joining. ¶7
