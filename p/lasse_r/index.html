Life story

A brother, Jørgen Rasmussen (born about 1741), was a soldier at Erdal. He married Christi Rasmusdatter of Folven in 1763 and was buried 6 Jan 1767 aged 26; he left no surviving heirs by 1783. ¶7

Burial: 'Lasse Rasmussen' of Erdahl, aged 28 (b. about 1746), buried 2 May 1774 (Innvik ministerialbok 1750-1816, burials; index pg00000000462202). Farm, name and year fit his probate of 11 Oct 1774, and the age matches Aaland's '† 28 a. g. 1774'. He is the only Lasse Rasmussen buried in the Innvik index 1770-1780. Birth range BET 1739 AND 1749 left as it is; the burial points to about 1746. ¶9

Bachelor of Erdalen; married Birthe Trulsdatter 6 Jan 1773 at Innvik (betrothed 22 Nov 1772). ¶1

Probate at Erdal 1774-77 suggests he died young. ¶2

The probate (skifte) after Lasse Rasmussen Erdalen was registered on 11 Oct 1774, so he died before that date. His heirs were his widow Birthe Trulsdatter, whose legal guardian was Truls Lassesen Aaning, and their only child Jørgen Lassesen. Arne(?) Trulsen Aaning was appointed the boy's guardian. The net estate was 106 rdl, split 53/53 between widow and son. ¶5

Aaland 1932: Aaland's Erdal history (bruk nr. 4, split off from nr. 3 in 1786) lists 'Lasse Rasmusson, † 28 a. g. 1774, sk. 1773 p. ½ av farens br. * Brite Trulsd. Aan.': he died in 1774 aged 28, which puts his birth about 1746. He had taken a deed in 1773 to half his father's holding, and married Brite Trulsdatter of Aaning. This agrees with the 1774 probate and his 1773 marriage. The age is Aaland's, without a cited source; his 1774 burial in the Innvik register (which survives from 1750) would confirm it. ¶8

Speculative: possibly son of Rasmus Jørgensen of Erdal (~1720-1797). ¶3

Confirmed son of Rasmus Jørgensen Erdal and Ragnilde Lassesdatter. In the probate after Ragnilde (registered 29 Apr 1783), the heirs are listed as her husband Rasmus Jørgensen Erdal and their children: the eldest son, the late Lasse Rasmussen Erdalen, 'who left one son Jørgen Lassesen, 10½ years old'; the second and youngest son Lars Rasmussen, married and living at Erdalen; the elder daughter Brithe Rasmusdatter at Nedre Flo (8 children); and the youngest daughter Malene Rasmusdatter, married to Lars Trulsen Aaning. ¶4

Searches that found nothing 1

No baptism survives: Innvik church books before 1750 are lost. Birth range: he was older than his brother Lars (born about 1749), and his parents married about 1738. ¶6
