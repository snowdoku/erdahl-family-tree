Life story

Widow (reported): WikiTree (Tolderbury-2) reports that the Latin administration record of 8 Dec 1630 for Walter Toldervey of Inkpen names his wife Ann, citing J. B. Prince, The Prince Family of Hull and Boston (1976), Appendix 2, and Ancestry's UK Extracted Probate Records (record 68804). Not read here. If correct, it shows that Walter's wife in 1630 was Ann, which supports the 1907 Noyes-Gilman statement, but it does not show that she was the mother of Elizabeth, born about 1585 (married 1606). Walter's daughters Ursula (m. 1621/2) and Katherine (m. 1626, third wife of Edward Garrard) were probably born about 1600, so Ann may have been a second wife. ¶2

Named as the wife of Walter Toldervey and mother of Elizabeth (Toldervey) Prince by Noyes-Gilman Ancestry (1907), p. 306, without a cited record. Maiden name unknown. Speculative, on the same footing as Walter. ¶1

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Grade: stays Speculative, graded on her link to her daughter Elizabeth (see her page): the only statement that Ann was Elizabeth's mother is Noyes-Gilman (1907, unsourced). ¶4a

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Surname candidate, not entered: John Jenkins of Adderbury, Oxfordshire (will 6 May 1599, proved Oxford 18 Oct 1603, OHC W. 191.431) left 'unto my daughter Anna Tolederbery iijL'. So an Anne Jenkins had married a Toldervey before 1599. The will does not name her husband, and several Toldervey men were living in and around Oxford (Henry, James, John, Thomas, Christopher), so she is only a candidate for Walter's wife. Adderbury is about 17 miles north of Oxford. ¶3

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Test record: the 1630 administration bond (Berkshire RO, D/A1) for her name as widow; the Oxford marriage of Walter Toldervey and Anne Jenkins (Adderbury or an Oxford parish, c.1580-1599), and the Adderbury register for Anne's baptism. ¶4b
