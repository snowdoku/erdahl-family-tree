About the family

Mother of Ane Gundersdatter (see her page): named as 'Kari Andersd.' beside 'Gunar Ols: Vigen' at Ane's baptism on 3 December 1758 (Selbu ministerialbok 1757-1801, canvas 7 left page, crop). The name 'Kari Andersd.' also ends the godparent list - either the clerk repeated the mother or a woman of the same name stood sponsor. ¶1

Life story

Anne named her second daughter Kari (baptised 21 May 1789), which fits a mother called Kari. Her birth, marriage and death are not found. She is not Kari born 1705, the wife of Ola Gunnarsen, who would have been 53 in 1758. Rated Probable on the same grounds as her husband (Gunnar (Gunder) Olsen Overvik). ¶2

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 open question

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Biography gaps: her birth date and place, her baptism and her confirmation are not found; her father is named Anders (estimated born about 1718) and is unidentified, and 16310 c96-102 was read in full without her baptism. Her marriage to Gunnar Olsen Vigen (about 1750-57) falls in the 1747-56 register gap, so the next record is her father's probate in Stjor- og Verdal protocols 24750-24753 (unindexed, not read), if he died before 1760. Her death, burial and any probate of her own are not found, and no search for them is recorded. ¶3
