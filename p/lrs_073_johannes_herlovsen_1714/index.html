About the family

Son-in-law of Iver Monsen of Botnen: he is named as husband of Anna Iversdatter in Iver's probate of 25 Sep 1779 (no. 46). As 'Johannes Herlevsen Inste-Taskle' a man of this name stood godfather at Brekke on 1 May 1774 with Botnen people; the farm form differs, so that is not linked. ¶3

Life story

Burial (per index): 'Johannes Herleivssøn', Mand, of Yst-Takle, aged 69 3/4 (b. about 1714), burial date 30 Jun 1784, interment 9 Jul 1784. Gulen church book 1776-1787, burials 1784, p. 88a, no. 69 (index pg00000001733895); indexed again without age as pg00000000768445. The scan was not read. ¶5

Herløv of Takle's daughter Barbra (scan read; still not a proof for Johannes): on the second day of Whitsun 1752 (29 May 1752) at Lavik 'trolovet Ole Mathiesen Stølsbotnen og Barbra Herløfsd[atter] i Tachle, Caut[ionister]: Hans Andersen Lovig, Iver Bog', married 15 Nov 1752 (margin '15 Nov Copul.'; Gulen ministerialbok 11443, canvas 26, left page, foot). This ties the Barbra Herløfsdatter of Stølsbotten (probate 1776, wife of Ole Mathiesen) to Takle and to a father named Herløv. Caution on her age: if she is the Barbra baptised to Herløf of Ytre Takle and Anna on 2 Jul 1721, she would have been about 43 to 50 when the children named in her 1776 probate (aged 12, 10 3/4, 8 and 5) were born, which is unlikely. So she may be a younger daughter of the same name (the 1721 Barbra having died), or the daughter of another Herløv; her burial or the 1721 Barbra's would decide it. ¶10

Johannes Herløvsen at Takle in 1752: in Oct 1752 'Johans Herl[øvsen?] i Tackle' was a godparent at Brekke at the baptism of the twins Anne and Marthe, children of Johannes of Midt-Takle and Marta, beside Torbiørn M. Tackle and Gjertrud M. Tackle (11443, canvas 30, left page foot and right page top; crops, c30-Rtop.jpg). So Johannes Herløvsen and a Barbra Herløfsdatter both lived at Takle in 1752. That fits their being brother and sister, but no record names Johannes as a son of Herløv and Anna. No parent is set. Against the link: none of Johannes's known sons (Jørgen by his first wife; Iver and Anders by his second) is named Herløv. Records still to read: Herløv's burial or probate (Gulen 11439-11442, 1723-1749, burials are unindexed; the Ytre Sogn probate index has no Herløv decedent), and Johannes's betrothal to Sigri Iversdatter (before about 1756, not in 11443 c18-c36, Jun 1751 - Aug 1753). ¶11

First marriage: probate No. 39, held in 1757 at Ytre Takle, Lavik skipreide, after 'den afdøde Kone Sigri Iversdatter' between her widower 'Johannes Herløfsen' and their son Jørgen Johannessen (a minor; age not read with certainty), whose guardian in the lots was his father Johannes Herløfsen (Ytre Sogn 7b fol. 387b, read from the scan). So Anna Iversdatter was his second wife and he was at Ytre Takle by 1757. The estate has no land and names no siblings or parents of Johannes. ¶6

Probate: on 25 Sep 1784 at Ytre Takle the probate court registered and valued the estate of 'den … afgangne Johannes Herløvsen' for division between his widow Anna Iversdatter and their four children of the marriage: Iver Johannessen 19 (curator Torbjørn Larsen of Ytre Takle), Anders Johannessen 11 (guardian Torkel[?] Monsen Aseim), Sigri Johannesdatter, married to Ole Torbjørnsen of Ytre Takle, and Lisbeth Johannesdatter 7 (guardian Endre Rognaldsen of Midt-Takle). Gross estate 46 riksdaler 5 skilling[?]. Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll A 10a, fol. 5b. ¶1

Death: before 25 Sep 1784, the date of his probate. Probably the 'Mand Johannes Herleivsøn yst-Takle gammel 69 3/4 aar' (Johannes Herleivsen of Ytre Takle, aged 69 3/4) who died 30 Jun and was buried 9 Jul 1784 in Gulen (fol. 88, no. 69); the name and farm agree, which would put his birth about Sep 1714. ¶2

Searches that found nothing 1

Parents not found: (1) The 1701 census of Ytre Takle (Ytre Tachle) has householders Baltzer Colbensen, Rasmus Jacobsen and Alf Christensen 40 each and no Herløv. (2) The Ytre Sogn probate index has no Herløv/Herlof/Heløf decedent in Gulen to 1780; the only Herløvsen/-datter rows there are Barbra Herlofsdtr 1776 and Johannes himself. (3) Barbra Herløfsdatter of Stølsbotten (probate No. 26, 8 May 1776, A 9 p. 659, read from the scan) was the wife of Ole Mathiesen with children Mathias 10 3/4, Giertrud 12, Anna 8 and Dordei 5; nothing in the entry ties her to Johannes, and her children's ages put her a generation younger, so she is not shown to be his sister. (4) Eilef (Heløf) Olsen of Botnen, d. 1730, had heirs Ole, Giertrud, Elisabeth and Britte only, so he was not Johannes's father. ¶7a

Open questions and the next record to read 4

Lead only: a 'Herløf Jonsen', 40, leilending on Nørland in Gulen skipreide (Nordhordland fogderi) in 1701, and a Herløv of Nørland was guardian for his daughter Ragnilde Herløvsdatter in a probate after Kari Olsdatter, 10 Aug 1737 (Stiftamtmannen extracts for Nordhordland, fol. 29b-30a). Nothing yet links Johannes to Nørland; the Nordhordland skifteprotokoll entry of 1737 (heirs in full) is the next record. ¶7b

Lead for his father (scan read; not a proof): the Stiftamt extract of minors' shares for Gulen skipreide, Nordhordland, 10 Aug 1737, after 'Karj Olsdatter' of Nørland, lists only minor heirs: 'Ragnilde Herløvsdatter' 17 Rd 3 mk 13 ß, guardian 'Herløv Nørland', and 'Anne Christiansdatter' 3 mk 13 ß, guardian 'Christian [F?]edje i Lindaas Schibrede'. So Herløv of Nørland was alive in 1737 and was the father of a minor daughter Ragnilde, apparently by Kari Olsdatter. Johannes Herløvsen (born about 1714) would have been about 23 in 1737, of age, and so would not appear in a list of minors. Herløv of Nørland is a candidate father, but the extract cannot prove it. The probate itself would list adult heirs. The ordinary Nordhordland probate protocol for 1737 is not in the Digitalarkivet probate index: a domicile=Nørland search for 1730-45 returns only these Stiftamt extract rows (Lars 1736, Herløv 1737). ¶8

Lead for his father (scan read; not a proof): on 2 Jul 1721 at Brekke 'Christned Herløf ytstetackles b: N: Barbra, Mat: Anna, Test: Anders Mittackle, Ole ibid, Johannes ibid, Birthe Gudormsdatter ytstetackle, Synneva Eilifsdatter botnen' (Gulen ministerialbok 11438, canvas 60, left page, foot), and the mother was churched in Aug 1721 as Herløf Ytre Takle's wife (c62 L). So a Herløv, wife Anna, lived on Ytre Takle in 1721, the farm where Johannes Herløvsen lived from 1757 and died in 1784, aged 69 3/4 (born about Sep 1714). The daughter Barbra (b. 1721) fits the Barbra Herløfsdatter of Stølsbotten whose probate was held in 1776. Herløv is a rare name in Gulen, so Herløv of Ytre Takle x Anna are the likely parents of Johannes, but no record names Johannes as their son; no parent is set. Johannes's baptism (about 1714) is earlier than the surviving Gulen book 11438 (begins 1716). ¶9a

Next: Herløv's probate or burial (Gulen 11439-11441, 1723-1746); the Herløv of Nørland of 1737 may be the same man after a move. ¶9b
