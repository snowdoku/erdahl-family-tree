About the family

Widow of Lars Marcussen of Eitungjerde in his probate of 1 May 1758 (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll A 7b, fol. 408a-408b): 'Enken Unni Hermunds Datter for hendes Hovedlod og halve Boe'. Given name read as Unni (possibly Ungi). Not proved to be the mother of Marcus Larsen Thue; the probate calls the children 'Arvinger' without saying which marriage they came from in the parts read. Not linked as mother. ¶1
