Life story

Probate, read from the scan: Ryfylke skifteprotokoll 1798-1807 (D 15, Digitalarkivet 23897), 'Skifter for Aaret 1805 / I. For Hetlands Skibrede 1805', No. 1, fol. 493b-494a (view 500): 'Skifte efter Huusmandskone Angona Iversdatter, paa Pladset Holen, under Gaarden Aadland', begun 5 Jan and closed 28 Feb 1806. She died 4 July of the previous year (read as 1805; the year is abbreviated in the text). Widower Peder Aslachsøn. The estate was divided between the widower (half) and 'hans fem Børn', all of age, 'med den afdøde Hustrue avlede': a. eldest son Iver Pedersøn, seafarer from Bergen (absent); b. second son Aslak Pedersøn, at Christianssand (absent); c. third son Peder Pedersøn, husmann under Gismervig; d. eldest daughter Marthe Pedersdatter, married to Helge Olsen Alvestad in Hetland skipreide; e. 'anden og yngste Datter Rongnilla Pedersdatter, gift med Orm Jonsøn under Aadland'. Guardian for the absent sons: husbonde Hellier (Hellik?) Halvorsøn Aadland. This primary record names the relationship directly: Angona and Peder Aslaksen are the parents of Rongnilla (Ragla), wife of Orm Jonsen of Aadland, the couple of the tree (married 1 Jan 1804). It independently confirms the existing Confirmed grade, graded on her link to Ragla (Ragnhild) Pedersdatter. Her eldest son is named Iver, consistent with her patronymic. Note: Skjold (Hetland skipreide) probates c.1798-1810 are in RYFYLKE sorenskriveri (D 15 = 23897, D 16 = 23898), not Karmsund; each year in D 15 opens with the Hetland skipreide section. ¶4

Bygdebok: Tysvær bd. 1 (1990), I. Odland, canvas 34, no. 72, names her 'Angunna Iversdt[r]., f. 1731', wife of Peder Aslaksen, with no origin or parents. Searched volumes 2, 3, 5, 6 and 7 of the Tysvær series and Skjold gard og ætt 1-2 for 'Angona'/'Ango*': only one unrelated hit. ¶5

1801 census, Odland, Skjold: wife of Peder Aslagsen, 70. ¶1

She died between the 1801 census (Angona Iversdatter, Odland, 'Hans kone', both in their first marriage) and 15 Jun 1806, when 'Peder Aslakson', Odland, 'Enkemand' (widower), married again (Skjold ministerialbok 1786-1815, marriages, indexed). A widower had to hold probate with his children before remarrying, so a skifte after her, c.1801-1806, should name her heirs, among them Ragla Pedersdatter (married Orm Jonsen 1 Jan 1804). It is not in the back register of Karmsund skifteprotokoll A3 1804-1810 (source 23913, views 576-583), but that register does not visibly include a Skjold block. ¶3a

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed (upgrade sweep D), as parent of Ragla (Ragnhild) Pedersdatter. The 1801 census of Skjold (index, relationship words read) lists at Odland household 08 Peder Aslagsen (69, mand), Angona Iversdatter (70, hans kone) and Ragnille Pedersdatter (23, 'Deres datter', unmarried), and household 12 Jon Poulsen (62, mand), Inger Larsdatter (56, hans kone), Orm Jonsen (22, 'Deres børn', sailor) and Ane Jonsdatter (14). On 1 Jan 1804 'Orm Johnssøn', bachelor, of Aadland (Odland) married 'Rognald Pedersdr.', maid, of Aadland, at Tysvær (Skjold marriages 1804, p. 101, index). Their son John was baptised 15 Aug 1804 to 'Orm Johssøn' and 'Ragnille Pedersdr., hustrue', of Adland (Skjold baptisms 1804, p. 105, index). The couple Orm Jonsen and Ragla Peersdatter are the parents named at Lars Ormsen's 1818 baptism at Ytre Eide, with sons John (1804), Peder (1806) and Poul (1809) named after both grandfathers. So the census names Ragnhild and Orm as children of these two Odland couples, and the 1804 marriage and baptism tie those two children, still of Odland, to each other as the couple of the tree. ¶2

Corrections 1

Correction: the two 'Odland estates' in Ryfylke skifteprotokoll D 6 (23890) fol. 362b (1772) and fol. 399 (1773), named in earlier 'next' pointers as the place to look for this family's parents, are 'Odland i Stangeland' (Stangeland skipreide = Karmøy/Skudenes), not Odland in Hetland skipreide (Skjold/Tysvær). The typed farm register of D 6 (view 18) lists them as: 1772 'Odland i Stangeland, ægtefaller Ole Oresen og Anne Siursdt.' (362b); 1773 'Odland i Stangeland, Morten Josephsen' (399). The register has no Odland/Aadland in Hetland 1767-74 (letters A view 4 and O view 18 read). So a greyscale copy of those pages would not help here. ¶6

Searches that found nothing 3

1762 (negative): the 1762 ekstraskatt for Skjold prestegjeld (source 49646, view 234) lists at 'Odland No. 50' the farmer Lars Helgesen and Malene Thorsdatter (no children over 12), servants Thore Christophersen and Guro Halvorsdatter, Ranveg Olsdatter, and the cottars/lodgers Christopher Torbjørnsen, Brita Rejersdatter, Ole Christophersen, Kari Helgesdatter, Marita Ericsdatter, Borjila Gunnersdatter, Malene Halvorsdatter, Guro Christophersdatter, Guro Larsdatter (old and poor), Toller Ericsen, Ragna Knudsdatter, Jens Tormodsen and Susanna Biørnsdatter. None of Jon Poulsen, Inger Larsdatter, Peder Aslaksen or Angona Iversdatter was living on Odland in 1762; no separate Lammevig or Holen entry appears in the Skjold list (views 216-241, farm column read). They must have come to Odland after 1762, so their parents should be sought on other farms. ¶7

1762 (negative): the name columns (couples, children over 12, servants, others, cottars) of the 1762 ekstraskatt for Skjold (source 49646) were read for views 216-220, 225-226 and 229-237 (Odland itself, view 234). No Jon Poulsen, Inger Larsdatter, Peder Aslaksen or Angona Iversdatter appears. Not yet read: views 221-224 and 227-228. Aslaksen men seen, possible kin of Peder Aslaksen but unproven: Lars Aslaksen with Ingebor Hedersdatter at Haaskuld No. 34 (view 217), and Michel Aslaksen, farmhand, at Eggenæs No. 64 (view 229). A Larsdatter of the right age is not listed as a child of any Lars in the views read, apart from Torbor Larsdatter at Frøvig No. 55 (view 218). ¶8

Negative: (1) The 1762 ekstraskatt for Skjold prestegjeld (source 49646) has now been read in full: views 221-224 and 227-228 (Skjold skipreide end, Skaare skipreide, Vats from view 224), the rest. Angona Iversdatter is not in it, so the family came into the parish after 1762. (2) The Ryfylke probate card index 1774-1788 (source 39936) files Odland under Hetland skipreide (Tysvær; views ~236-345). It holds one Aadland card (236a: 1 May 1780, husmann Nils Johnsen g. Elen Olsdatter) and no Lammevik card (L runs Lavøen, Lerevold, Lervik, Lundevold, Løvland, views 298-300), so no probate for this family or their kin on Odland 1774-88. Origin still open. ¶9
