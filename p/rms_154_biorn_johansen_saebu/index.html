Life story

Farmer at Sæbu (Sæboe) in Skrautvål, Aurdal; first husband of Ingebiør Reyersdatter. The 1698 Sæbu skifte (DA 25282 s. 133b, view 137) was held after her second husband Ole Østensen and herself, and lists her children 'med den Sal. Qvindes første Egtemand' (inserted above the line: 'Biørn Johansen') '3 Børn, Johannes er 19 aars alder, Rangnilde er 21 aar gammel og Giertrude er 14 aars alder', and 'med Ole Østensen 1 barn ved nafn Østen Olsen som er 12 aars alder'. He was therefore dead by about 1685 (Østen Olsen was 12 in 1698), certainly before 17 Mar 1698. ¶1

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade:, new person entered as Confirmed, graded on his link to his daughter Ragnild Biørnsdatter of Midtstrand (see her page). Ragnild's own probate at Midtstrand, 9 Oct 1721 (DA 25285 fol. 480a, view 489, read), records that land in Sæbu fell to her by inheritance 'efter hendis S. Forældre Biørn Johansen og Ingebor[..]daatter', cites 'Skiftebrevet som er forrettet efter Biørn Johansen... dat. den 17de Martij 1698', and names her brother 'Johannes Biørnsen Sæbur'. The 1698 skifte names 'Rangnilde 21' and 'Johannes 19' as his children. The relationship is written in a record of Ragnild herself, so no cross-farm identification is needed; the 1698 ages (Ragnild b. about 1677) fit her marriage in 1702. No rival. ¶2
