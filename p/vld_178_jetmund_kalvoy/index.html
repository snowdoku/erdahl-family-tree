Ole Jetmundsen Kalvøyc. 1654 – c. 1735Probable · Sunnmøre

About the family

Father of Ole Jetmundsen of Store Kalvøy (see his page), Probable; graded on his link to Ole. Ole's patronymic is given by the Haram bygdebok (Band 2b p. 370: 'Ole Jetmundson... son [of] Jetmund') and by the probate after his sister Berette Jetmundsdatter of Kalvøen, 8 Jun 1691 (3A 01 fol. 34b, view 24544/47, scan read), whose heirs were her brothers Joen, Peder, Knud and Olle Jetmundsønner and the children of a dead brother Søffren Jetmundsen. So their father was a Jetmund, and Ole named his eldest son (b. about 1689) Jetmund. Jetmund's own dates, farm and wife are unknown: he was probably dead by 1691, since the probate does not mention a father. Do not confuse him with Jetmund Knudsen, Berette's widower, who was 'Jetmund 49' on Store Kalvøy in 1704 (b. about 1655). ¶1a

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the identification · 1 record that disagrees · 1 alternative set aside

The case for this identification 1

Probable identification (index): the 1666 vicar's census for Sunnmøre has only one Jetmund on Kalvøy: 'Jetmund', bonde, b. about 1606, on Lille Kalvøy (Lille Kalfføen), with sons Johan (b. about 1659) and Knud (b. about 1663) (pc00000001599533-35); the 1664 bailiff's census also has a Jetmund farming Lille Kalfføen (pc00000003915152). Johan (Jon) and Knud are two of the four brothers named as heirs in 1691 (Joen, Peder, Knud and Olle Jetmundsønner), which makes this Jetmund their likely father. Ole (b. about 1654-58), Peder and Søren are not in his 1666 household, so they were away or the census missed them (it lists males only and not always all sons). The birth year and farm are entered on this ground; they rest on the name match and on Kalvøy, not on a record that names Ole as his son. The other Kalvøy farmers in 1666 were Knud (b. 1620) with sons Oluff (b. 1657) and Tosten (b. 1664), Knud (b. 1618) and Arne (b. 1596). That Oluff is an Ole Knudsen, not an Ole Jetmundsen, so he is not a rival. ¶2

Records that disagree 1

Conflict with the bygdebok, noted: the Haram bygdebok, Band 2b, p. 376 (bruk 5) reads the 1691 skifte differently. It says Jetmund Knutson (ca. 1655 - 1727, probably son of Knut Jonson) had five sons with Berte, who died in 1691 (the skifte 'etter mora' in 1691), lists children '... Jon, 3. Peder, 4. Knut, 5. Ole', and says Jetmund Knutson remarried in 1692 at Haram, an Ingeborg (of Hildre?); his own skifte was in 1728. On that reading Ole would be the son of Jetmund Knudsen and Berte. The protocol does not support it: the scan clearly calls the heirs 'den Sal: Qvindes Brødre' (the deceased woman's brothers), one of them (Søffren Jetmundsen) was already dead leaving two children, and the guardian of those children is called their 'fader broder' (father's brother). A dead son with two children is impossible for a husband born about 1655, and Ole himself was born about 1654 (50 in 1704), so he cannot be Jetmund Knudsen's son. This entry follows the protocol: Ole was Berette Jetmundsdatter's brother, and his father was an older Jetmund. Someone with access to the printed page should check whether the bygdebok gives a source for its reading. ¶3

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Not Confirmed: the step from the Olle Jetmundsøn heir of 1691 to Ole of Store Kalvøy in 1704 rests on farm and name. ¶1b
