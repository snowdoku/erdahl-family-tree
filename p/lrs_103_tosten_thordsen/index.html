Life story

Contract of 16 Oct 1759 read: Ringerike og Hallingdal pantebok nr. 11 (1755-1764), s. 303 left page, no. 22 (registered at Ål, 1759): 'Kiendes jeg underskrevne Tosten Thordsen Sando og jeg Halvor Tostensen'... 'at som jeg Tosten Thordsen og min kiere Hustru er... gamle Folk' they have contracted with 'vores... Søn' [one word before Søn not read with certainty] Halvor Tostensen that he take over 'vores halve Anpart i gaarden Sando', with a retirement contract (kår) for the couple; dated Sando 16 Oct 1759, witnesses Torchild Olsen Welland and Ole Stensen Hammersbøen. The wife is not named in the contract itself; the 1781 skifte names the contracting couple as Tosten Thordsen and Bergith Olsdatter. Kari is not named in the contract, so the grade stays Probable. ¶4

Probate of a stepson: the probate index card of 12 Feb 1781 after Halvor Tostensen of Sando in Ål names among the deceased's kin 'Stefar(?): Tosten Thordsen Sando' (stepfather, queried by the card-maker), with the mother 'Bergith Olsd.' (also queried) and a half-brother Tosten Tostensen of Sando (Ringerike og Hallingdal skifteprotokoll 20a, fol. 270a; card, protocol text not read). ¶1

Death: before 4 Oct 1791 (the deed calls him 'Salig afdøde'). He and his wife had given half of Sando to her son Halvor Tostensen in 1759 as 'gamle Folk'. Children named in 1791: Tosten Tostensen (of Sando), Ole Tostensen Skjerven, Astri Tostensdatter (m. Knud Engebretsen Skrinde) and, through her heirs, Kari Tostensdatter (d. by 1785). Panteregister p. 190 shows no skifte after him or his wife, only this heirs' deed. ¶7

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 3

Confidence: Speculative, as father of Kari Tostensdatter of Sando (born about 1736). Plausible because Halvor's mother Berte Olsdatter was widowed by Feb 1723, Halvor's half-brother was a Tosten Tostensen of Sando (so the son of a Tosten), and Kari's brother in 1773 was a Tosten Tostensen of Sando. ¶2a

Upgrade to Probable, as father of Kari Tostensdatter of Sando. The 1781 skifte after Halvor Tostensen of Sando was read on the scan (Ringerike og Hallingdal skifteprotokoll 20, source 25555, fol. 270a-271a, images sk20090127340277 and 340278). (1) The division (fol. 270b) says the deceased held part of Sando under a 'Contract af Dato 16 October 1759' made 'med Tosten Thordsen og Bergith Olsdatter', so Tosten Thordsen and Bergith Olsdatter were a couple who held Sando and passed it to Halvor in 1759; the contract is entered in pantebok no. 11 fol. 302 (cited on fol. 271a). (2) The same text says Halvor had inherited a share of Sando from his 'Farmoder Gunnild Olsdatter Sandoe' (his father Torsten Halvorsen's mother, d. by 1723). (3) An obligation of 1770 copied into the skifte (fol. 271a) has Halvor Tostensen borrowing 550 rd from 'min Broder Tosten Tostensen Sandoe'. Halvor's own father Torsten Halvorsen died before Feb 1723 leaving Halvor as only child, so Tosten Tostensen is Halvor's brother through their mother Berte/Bergith Olsdatter by her second husband, a Tosten: this is Tosten Thordsen, whose marriage to her the 1759 contract now shows. Kari Tostensdatter's guardian in 1773 was 'Børnenes Morbroder Tosten Tostensen Sanda', so Kari was Tosten Tostensen's sister. Taken together, Kari belongs to the Sando family of Tosten Thordsen and Bergith Olsdatter. Still not Confirmed: no record names Kari's father, and the skifte's own words do not use 'stepfather'; the registration (fol. 270a) and heirs list (fol. 270b: widow Marie/Marith Michelsdatter and Halvor's children Tosten, Kari, Guri and Gunnild Halvorsen/-datter) name neither Tosten Thordsen nor Bergith as kin. ¶3a

Upgrade: Confirmed, graded on his link to his daughter Kari Tostensdatter. The deed (no. 8, entered 'Extimus' 1791) opens: 'Underskrevne Arvinger efter Salig afdøde Tosten Thorsen og Birgit Olsdatter Sandoe, nemlig Jeg Tosten Tostensen paa egne Vegne, Jeg Ole Tostensen Schierven paa egne Vegne, Jeg Knud Engebretsen Schrinde paa min Hustrue Astrie Tostensdatters Vegne, og Jeg Tosten Aslesen Meedgaarden paa egne og mine Søskendes[?] Birgit Aslesdatters og Ole Aslesens Vegne, alle Boende udi Aals Præstegield i Hallingdahlen'. They sell to Tosten Halvorsen Sandoe the share of Øvre Sando that fell to them 'som Arv efter benævnte vores Salig afdøde Forældre og Bedste Forældre Tosten Thorsen og Birgit Olsdatter Sandoe' (as inheritance after our late parents and grandparents), 11 løb, for 663 rd 1 ort 8 sk; Tosten Tostensen also refers to 'min Salig afdøde Broder Halvor Tostensen'. Dated 4 Oct 1791, tinglyst 14 Oct 1791 (pantebok 15, s. 308; Ål panteregister p. 190). Tosten Aslesen of Medgaarden (see his page), Birgit Aslesdatter and Ole Aslesen are the children of Asle Knudsen and Kari Tostensdatter named in Kari's 1785 probate (Tosten 26, Ole 23, Bergit 20) and they inherit here as grandchildren ('Bedste Forældre') of Tosten Thorsen and Birgit Olsdatter. Their father Asle Knudsen was the son of Knud Aslesen and Rangdi Halvorsdatter of Dybsjorde (1742 probate), so the descent can only run through their mother Kari, who had died by 1785; she was therefore a daughter of Tosten Thorsen and Birgit Olsdatter. This agrees with the 1773 probate naming 'Børnenes Morbroder Tosten Tostensen Sanda' and with the 1781 skifte in which Halvor Tostensen calls Tosten Tostensen 'min Broder'. Continuity: the same Sando family, the same Medgaarden heirs as 1785, six years apart; no rival. ¶6
