About the family

Probable father of Torstein Iversen of Engesætter (see his page): same farm, the name Tosten Iversen, and the right age. ¶3a

His patronymic Gullacksen (son of Gullak) fits a son of his named Gullow (Gullak); a Jon Gullachsen/Gulløvsen of Indre Hjertholm (see his page) is a possible brother, which is untested. ¶4

Father of Torstein Iversen of Engesætter (see his page): see the confidence note. (Supersedes the earlier 'Probable father... Would confirm' note.) ¶6

Father (Speculative): possibly Gullach Lassesen of Engesetter (see his page), whose son 'Iver Gullachs[en]' was 2 in the 1701 census (born about 1699). The competing candidate is Gullach Torkilsen, 36 in 1701 on the same farm, who had no son Iver then but could have had one later; Iver's youngest daughter born about 1761 fits a later birth better. See Gullach Lassesen's notes for the full reasoning. The 1701 census also shows that Jon Gullachsen 3 (perhaps Jon Hiertholm, unproven) was the son of Gullach Torkilsen; he would be Iver's full brother only if Iver was also Gullach Torkilsen's son, so the 'possible brother' question now depends on which Gullach was Iver's father (still untested). ¶7

Father's name: the 1701 census entry for his probable father reads 'Gullach Tostens[en]' 40 with sons Tosten 7 and Iver 2, not 'Gullach Lassesen' as earlier transcribed (see Gullach Tostensen, renamed Gullach Tostensen). Iver's son Tosten fits this. Gullov To[e]nsen of Engesætter (d. 1741), the other Gullach in 1701, is still excluded as his father (Iver was an appraiser, not an heir, in 1741). ¶9

Mother (Probable, bygdebok): Siri Iversdatter (about 1667-1737), wife from 1693 of Gullak Torsteinson (Gullach Tostensen) of Engesæter bnr. 52; Brekke herred bygdebok vol. 2, canvases 510-511, lists 'Ivar Gullakson' b. 1699 among their children and as the next user of bnr. 52 (1733-1773), married 1732 to Kari Clausdatter of Takle. Birth about 1699 agrees with the 1701 census (Iver Gullachsen, 2). ¶10

Life story

Probate: on 12 and 13 Mar 1774 the probate court registered and appraised at Engesetter the estate of the late 'Dannemand' (an honest yeoman) Iver Gullacksen, between his widow Kari Clausdatter and his 4 sons and 3 daughters: Hermund Iversen of Ytre(?) Brekke, Tosten Iversen (of age), Jon Iversen, Gullow Iversen (of age), Kari Iversdatter (married to Iver Larsen Haugeland), Agathe Iversdatter (23) and Ingeborg Iversdatter (12 1/2). Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll A 9 (1772-1782), p. 359, no. 8. The heirs' list continues on p. 360, not read. ¶1

Death: before 12 Mar 1774, the date of the probate. No burial has been searched for. ¶2

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Confidence: raised from Probable to Confirmed as father of Torstein Iversen (see his page). His probate of 12-13 Mar 1774, a primary record read from the scan, names Tosten Iversen, of age, as his second son by the widow Kari Clausdatter. The widow's own probate of 14 Apr 1799 (Ytre Sogn 11b fol. 546, no. 21) names the same seven children in the same order, with the second son now 'Tosten Iversen Engeseter', living on the farm. This places the heir at Engeseter, where Torstein Iversen lived in 1801 with his wife Sigrid Larsdatter, and where the 1814 probate after Tosten Iversen of Engeset names his widow Sirie Larsdatter. He is the only Tosten Iversen among the Engeset probate-index rows. ¶5

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Not a son of Gullov of Engesætter: in the probate after Gullov [T?]ensen of Engesætter, 22 Aug 1741, 'Iver Engesætter' was one of the two appraisers (Wurderingsmænd), and the sons were Joen Hiertholm, To[e]n[?] Hiertholm and Lars Engesætter. Jon Gulløvsen of Hjartholm, earlier suggested as a possible brother, was therefore Gullov's son and not Iver's full brother. Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll 6a, No. 11, fol. 153a. ¶8
