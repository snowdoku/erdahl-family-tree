About the family

Child of Ola Jornsen and Marit Pedersdatter. Selbu i fortid og naatid vol. 7, p. 116: 'Ingeborg, f. 1749, g.m. JON OLSEN [N.]' ¶1

Mother of Ingebor Jonsdatter (see her page), rated Probable on the same footing as the father link (see Jon Olsen Sandvik): Ingebor, born about 1774-75, came between Marit (1773) and Jon's second marriage, so she is a daughter of the first marriage. This makes Ingebor Jonsdatter a niece of Jørgen Olssen Slind (Jorgen Olssen Slind) - the Slind family enters line 7 twice (pedigree collapse). ¶4a

Pedigree collapse, updated: the 1775 baptism confirms the Sandvik half of the second route to Ola Jørnsen x Marit Pedersdatter, namely Kari Olsdatter <- Marit Haldorsdatter <- Ingebor Jonsdatter <- this Ingeborg Olsdatter. Its godmother Sigri Slinden, like the godfather Jørgen Slinden at her son Ole's baptism in 1771, supports a Slindan family tie. The collapse (Oline Slind's parents Gunder Olsen Slind and Kari Olsdatter as third cousins) remains Probable until a primary record names this Ingeborg as Ola Jørnsen's daughter: her own baptism in 1749 (a gap year in the Selbu books) or her burial, about 1786, with an age (Selbu ministerialbok 1757-1801). ¶7

Her father's probate, Slinde, 17 Oct 1772 (24757 fol. 530): '4. Datteren Ingeborg Olsdatter gift med Jon Olsen Sandvig'. ¶12

Life story

A brother or sister of Jorn (Jorgen) Olssen Slind, born 1752, David Erdahl's maternal fifth-great-grandfather. ¶2a

Marriage and children (Probable identification). The two bygdebok chapters name each other. (1) The Slindan chapter (Selbu i fortid og naatid vol. 7, printed p. 116 = nb.no image 0118, word boxes re-queried): in the children list of Ola (1717) and Marit (1742 marriage), between 'Marit, 1746' and 'Jørn, 1752,... bruket', the line at y2262 carries 'Ingeborg,... 1749,... Jon Olsen... Sandvik,' - her husband is Jon Olsen of SANDVIK, not just 'a Jon Olsen'. (2) The Sandvik chapter (vol. 3, p. 353, word boxes) gives Jon Olsen Sandvik (see his page) a wife on the line at y1945-1960: '1770... Ingeborg... Slind... 1749, død' - married 1770, from Slind, born 1749. (3) The register names Jon Olsen Sandvig's wife 'Ingeborg Olsd.' at the baptisms of Ole (spring 1771) and Marit (14 March 1773), and a 'Jørgen Slinden' - probably her brother Jørgen Olssen Slind (Jorgen Olssen Slind), though Slinden had several households - stood godfather to Ole in 1771. Marit's name fits naming after her mother Marit Pedersdatter. Rated Probable, not Confirmed, because the Slind parentage rests on the compiled book (read as word boxes, not page images); no primary record yet names her father. ¶3

Burial: Selbu ministerialbok 1757-1801 (source 16311), canvas 121 left page, under 'Dom: Palmarum' (Palm Sunday, which with Easter on 16 April fell on 9 April 1786): 'Ingeborg Olsd. Sandvig død 3. april 37 aar. og barnet 12 dag gl.' with a last word not read with certainty - Ingeborg Olsdatter of Sandvik, died 3 April, 37 years old, and the child, 12 days old. She was buried with her baby. Taken to be her: Sandvik is her husband Jon Olsen's farm, age 37 gives a birth in 1748-49 against the bygdebok's 1749, and the bygdebok has Jon widowed in 1786 and remarried in 1787. The death is set from this entry; the earlier note that her 1786 burial 'has not been read' is superseded. ¶8

1762 extra tax: listed as 'Ingebor' among Ole's children over 12 in her grandfather Jørgen (Jørn) Olsen's household at 706 Slinden; she was about 13, which fits the bygdebok's birth year 1749. (A maid 'Ingebor Ols datter' in the same household is listed separately and is a different person.) ¶11

Death (book only, word boxes): the Sandvik chapter's line after her entry reads '... 1786. G.II 1787 m. Ingrid... Sandvik' - she apparently died in 1786 and Jon remarried in 1787 (his 1801 wife Inger Olsdatter, 34). No death field is set; her burial in 1786 (Selbu ministerialbok 1757-1801) has not been read. ¶5

She probably died after childbirth (inference): she was buried with a child 12 days old, which puts that child's birth about 22 March 1786. The child is not yet in the tree; its baptism (16311, Annunciation block on canvas 120 right or canvas 121 left), or her churching if she lived to it, would name the mother. ¶9

Other Ingeborg Olsdatters buried in 1786 and rejected: 'Ingeborg Olsd. Slinden', died 19 May 1786 aged 36 (16311 canvas 122 left, Ascension Day) - at her birth farm with no husband named, so probably a namesake of another Slinden household; 'Ingeborg Olsd. Eidem', aged 84 (canvas 120 right, Reminiscere); and an 'Ingeborg Olsd. Kytto' of 9 February struck through and corrected to Marit Olsdatter. Every burial on canvases 120-122 (2nd Sunday in Advent 1785 to the 4th Sunday after Trinity 1786) was read. ¶10

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Rated Probable: from the Selbu gaardshistorie, read by reconstruction from the National Library's word coordinates; no register entry has been read for her. ¶2b

Confidence raised from Probable to Confirmed, as mother of Ingebor Jonsdatter (see her page). Her daughter's baptism on 8 January 1775 names the mother 'Ingeborg Ols d.' with the father John Olsen Sandvig, and she was churched on 29 January 1775 as 'John Olsen Sandvigens h[ustru]' (Selbu klokkerbok 1766-1776, canvas 115 right page). Ingebor is therefore a daughter of Jon's first marriage. This rating covers her motherhood of Ingebor only. Her own birth at Slindan in 1749, as a daughter of Ola Jørnsen and Marit Pedersdatter, still rests on the bygdebok and stays Probable on Ola Jornsen Slind. ¶6
