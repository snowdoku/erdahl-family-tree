About the family

Son (Confirmed): the head of the household is Ole Olsen of Ibrække (see his page): same name, age 33 (b. about 1768), same farm, and wife Johanne Joensdatter, whom he married in 1791 as Ole Olsen of Ibrække. The census states the mother-son relation directly, and no second Ole Olsen with a wife Johanne is known at Ibrække, so the link is graded Confirmed. Her granddaughter Ingebor Olsdatter, 8, in the same household, is named after her (inference). ¶2

Husband (unknown): her late husband, Ole Olsen's father, must have been named Ole (from Ole's patronymic), but the census does not name him and he has not been identified. 'Widow after her 1st marriage' means she married only once and was widowed, so Ole's father was very probably her only husband (inference). His burial (before 1801) and her marriage (before about 1768, possibly as early as the 1750s) have not been searched. The 1768 baptism of 'Ole Olsen Tvetenes Søn Ole' (noted on Ole Olsen) names no mother and is not linked. ¶3

Husband (Probable): the elder Ole Olsen of Ibrække (see his page), buried at Nissedal in Apr/May 1790 aged 69 (Kviteseid 7983 c231L). That fits her status in 1801 as a widow after her first marriage. Their marriage date is not proved; a Nissedal betrothal of Ole Olsen and 'Ingerbor Tor[..]sdatter', about 17 Oct 1756, married 11 Apr 1757 (7981 c108R), is a candidate only. ¶5

Life story

Probate of husband: the 1790 skifte after Ole Olsen of Ibrække (25679, skifte No 31, view 84R) calls her 'Ingeborg Torchels Datter', the surviving widow, which settles her patronymic as Torkelsdatter (not Toraldsdatter). Her lauverge (legal guardian) was the lagrettemann Targe Halvorsen Grimstvedt. The probate does not name her father or her family. ¶6

Burial, Probable (assessed by audit): the Digitalarkivet burial index for Kviteseid ministerialbok 1800-1814 (0829P), p. 159a, has 'Ingeborg Torkildsd.', 'Enken' (the widow), of Ibræke, aged 77, buried 1806 (record pg00000001619222; index only, exact date and church not read from the scan). Same farm, widowed, Torkild = Torkel, and no other Ibrække woman is buried in 1800-1814, so she is very probably the Ingebor Torkelsdatter of the 1801 census. The ages conflict: 77 in 1806 gives birth about 1729, against 68 in 1801 (about 1733); a gap of this size is common in ages of elderly people. Would settle the exact date: the scan of 0829P 1800-1814, p. 159a. ¶4

1801 census: Ingebor Torkelsdatter, 68 (so born about 1733), lived at Ibræke (Ibrække), Nissedal (then Kviteseid prestegjeld), in household 2, headed by Ole Olsen, 33, farmer-occupier (bonde og gaardbeboer), with his wife Johanne Joensdatter, 32. Her relation to the head is given as 'Husbondens moder' (the householder's mother) and her marital status as 'Enke efter 1te egteskab' (widow after her first marriage). She was alive in 1801; probably buried 1806 (see the burial note). ¶1

1 alternative set aside

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Marriage candidate strengthened: the 1790 probate spells her patronymic 'Torchels', with ch and no k. The 1756 Nissedal betrothal of 'unge Karl Ole Olsen og Pigen Ingerbor Tor[..]s doettr' (about 17 Oct 1756, married 11 Apr 1757; 7981 c108R) was as T-o-r, a closed bowl, a minim group, one looped ascender, then s, with no k ascender. That is exactly 'Torcels' (c, e, l, s), a k-less spelling of Torkels like the probate's 'Torchels'. So the 1756 entry is probably her marriage to Ole Olsen (see his page), who was then about 35 and a bachelor. Not added as a marriage date, because no farm is named in the entry. ¶7
