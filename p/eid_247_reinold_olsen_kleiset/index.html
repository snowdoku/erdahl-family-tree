Life story

Baptised Jubilate Sunday (about 3 May) 1716: 'døbt Olaf Kleisets barn Reinold, fadd. Olaf Vigen, Torger Kleiset, Olaf Kolset, Mali Vigen, Mari Kleiset' - Reinold, son of Ole Kleiset (16310 canvas 62 left). Probably named for 'Reinol Kleiset', buried 11th Sunday after Trinity 1717 aged 65[?] (c67 left) - his grandfather. ¶1

1801 census (indexed): Reinholt Pederssen, b. 1770, heads Kleset with wife Beret Evensdatter - the name persisted at Kleiset; and Marit Reinholtsdatter, b. 1762, lives at Stoche (Stokke) - possibly a younger daughter of this Reinold (who would have been 46). Both are consistent with, but do not prove, a Reinholt family at Kleiset. ¶4

No Reinold at Kleiset in 1664-66. Other Reinolts then: Reinholt (45 in 1664) / Reinolt Tomassen (50 in 1666) at Sæther, and Reinolt Jonsen (10) at Neder Lien (b. c.1656 - could be the 'Reinol Kleiset' buried 1717 aged 65). The name was established in Selbu but its link to Kari Reinholtsdatter stays Speculative. ¶5

How sure are we?

Speculative. A plausible candidate only. Treat this link as a question, not a fact.

1 on the grade · 1 correction · 1 open question

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Speculative father of Kari (Karen) Reinholtsdatter (wife of Torsten Eriksen Uglem, married 1765; born before 1747). The forename Reinholt/Reinold is rare in Selbu and clusters at Kleiset: this Reinold (b. 1716), the elder Reinol Kleiset (d. 1717), and in 1801 the census index has Reinhol- names at Kleset. A Reinold born 1716 is the right age to father a daughter born about 1740-46. Nothing yet links Kari to Kleiset; this rests on the name alone. ¶2

Corrections 1

Correction: almost certainly died young - he is not among the heirs in his father Ole Reinelsen's probate of 31 Jul 1728 (children Reinel Olsen 6 weeks, Ingeborg 6, Ragnild 3; widow Mali Arntsdatter; card 77804 c1011), and a new son was given his name in 1728. No longer linked as father of Kari Reinholtsdatter. His father Ole Reinelsen (Reinholtsen) of Kleset died 1728; the 'Reinol Kleiset' buried 1717 aged 65 was presumably his grandfather. ¶6

Open questions and the next record to read 1

What would raise or reject it: Kari's baptism (c.1740-46; 16315 for 1740-46 has no such baptism found so far, and 1747-56 has no register), Torsten and Kari's 1765 marriage entry (16311) if it names her farm, or godparents from Kleiset at Kari's children's baptisms. ¶3
