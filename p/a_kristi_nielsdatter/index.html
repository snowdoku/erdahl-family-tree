Life story

The archivist's cross-reference card to that estate adds, in so many words, the marriage that concerns us: "Christi Nielsdtr. senere g.m. Tergie Tiøstelsen + sk. på Eppeland 10/1-1769." She was betrothed to him on 22 November 1755, four months after her father's estate was settled. ¶3

DEATH ESTABLISHED. Her own estate was settled between 29 July 1774 and 28 July 1775 (Skifteprotokoll no. 14a, folio 14b). The card reads: "Sk. e: Christj Nielsdatter, g.2.g.m. Niels Joensen (+ sk. 22/9 1780), g.1.g.m. Tergie Thiostelsen Eppeland + sk. 10/1 1769. Barn i 1. ekteskap: Birgittha Thergiesdtr." Birgitte Terjesdatter is named as her only child, and no children of the second marriage are listed. The estate held 1 hud 6 skinn in Eppeland. She had brought the odel, the hereditary right to Eppeland, into the family. ¶4

SECOND MARRIAGE: to Niels Joensen of Eppeland, who outlived her and remarried Anne Aslaksdatter. His estate, settled 22 September 1780 to early 1781 (Skifteprotokoll no. 14b, folio 361), names his two small daughters by that second marriage, Christj (4¾) and Signe (born October 1780) — Birgitte's step-siblings, not blood relations. His brother was Gunder Jonsen; his second wife's father was Aslak Tellefsen of Eppeland, who died in 1767. ¶5

PARENTAGE RESOLVED (10 Oct 2026), and confidence raised from Probable to Confirmed. She was a daughter of Niels Olsen of Eppeland by his first marriage, to Ingri (Yngri) Knudsdatter. Her father's estate, settled 19 July 1755 (Østre Råbyggelag skifteprotokoll no. 12b, folio 313), lists under "barn av 1ste ekteskap med Yngri Knudsdatter": "Christj Nielsdatter, 21 år gl." and "Anna, 18". Aged 21 in July 1755 gives a birth year of about 1734, close to the estimate of 1735 previously carried in this record. ¶2
