About the family

Wife of Torger Erikssen of Svinaas at the 1801 census of Selbu, entered at rural residence 0015, household 3, as 'Magli Guttormsdatter, 68, Hans kone, Hans 2det haendes 1te aegteskab' - his second marriage, her first. ¶1

Her patronymic makes her father a GUTTORM, an uncommon Selbu name. ¶3

Life story

Marriage: betrothed 1 April 1787 as 'Mali Guttormsd. Stamnes' - of the farm Stamnes before her marriage - to Torger Eriksen Svinaas, and married 17 June 1787 in Selbu. She was about 54 at the marriage, which confirms that she cannot have been the mother of Haldo Torgersen, born about 1770. ¶4

Burial: 'Maglie Guttormsd.', 'Qvinden' (the wife), of Svinaas, aged 72, buried in Selbu on the 15th Sunday after Trinity 1802 (26 September 1802, computed from Easter 18 April 1802), by the Digitalarkivet index of the church book 1801-1815 (scan not read; the index date-of-death field is garbled). Svinaas, the surname and her status as wife agree with her 1801 census entry; age 72 implies birth about 1730 against about 1733 from the 1801 age of 68. The only Magli or Maglie burial at Svinaas in that register. The death is entered as before the burial day. ¶5

1 alternative set aside

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

She is deliberately NOT entered as the mother of Haldo Torgersen, Oline Slind's ancestor. The census says this was Torger's second marriage, and the daughter living with them is written 'HANS datter' - his daughter - rather than 'deres'. Haldo was born about 1770, when Magli would have been thirty-seven and, on the census's own statement, in her first marriage; the natural reading is that Torger's children were by the first, unnamed wife and that Magli married him later. Recording her as Haldo's mother would be a guess, so the link is left out and the reasoning written down instead. ¶2
