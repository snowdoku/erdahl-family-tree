Han hadde [i] 1645 kone, en sønn [og...] døtre.'; '[Ola] Jonsen fikk [i] 1650 [...] faren [...] Alset'; '[mens] faren levde, [...] fikk han bygsel [i] 1658. [Han var] født [ca.] 1625'. 'Brukere' (canvas 774): '-1661-1701 [Ola] Jonsen, f. ca. 1625, [...] etter faren.' The 1666 list has 'Olluff Jonsen' 42 at Alset and the 1701 manntall 'Oller Jonsen' 80, consistent. ¶1

Audit 7 (1-2 Nov 2026): grade unchanged (Probable, compiled source only). Under the audit-6 rule a bygdebok-only ancestor must name the primary record that would test the link; this note supplies it. ¶4a

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Test record: no probate survives for his death in 1658, so the test is the record the bygdebok cites for Ola Jonsen's bygsel of Alset 'after his father' in 1650/1658 (Trondhjems len accounts or the Selbu/Stjørdal court book for 1650-58). The 1645 koppskatt for Selbu names wives only as 'hans hustru' and does not help. ¶4b
