About the family

Wife (Probable): Randi Olsdatter of Ytre Esse, whose probate of 20 June 1721 (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll A 3a, fol. 61a-62a) names her children as Hans, Olle, Anders and Knud Povelsen, Britte (wife of Olle Menes) and Guri (wife of Mons Esse). The four sons match his 1701 household exactly, but the entry does not name her husband, and no widower is named, so Povel was evidently dead by June 1721. The daughters Britte and Guri are new to the family; the 1701 census lists males only. The 'Brite Olsdatter, Ese, 1728-1733' probate lead is a different woman (patronymic Olsdatter). ¶5

Mother (Probable): the 1645 poll tax lists on Thue (Tue) the farmer 'Hanns' and his wife 'Giertrud Andersdtr' (Ytre Sogn, Kvamsøy skipreide, p. 50); see Giertrud Andersdatter. His brother's name Anders fits her patronymic. The link to Povel is inferred (the poll tax omits children), so Probable. ¶7

Life story

1664-1666 censuses: bailiff's census 21 Jun 1664 lists '30: Povel Hansen' as a son of Hans at Thue (Tue), Tjugum parish, and the vicar's census of 23 May 1666 lists 'Povl 34' as a son of Hans (66) at Tue, with brother Anders 32 (bailiff: Endre[?] 26). Ytre Esse was held by two men named Anders in 1664-66 and no Povel lived there, so Povel came to Esse after 1666. Father Hans of Tue added as Probable: he is the only Povel in the parish in the lists read, but no record read joins Povl of Tue to Povel of Esse. Ages: 30 (1664) and 34 (1666) give b. ~1632-1634; 1701 gives ~1635. Birth left at ABT 1635. ¶6

1701 census: Povel Hansen, 66 (born about 1635, calculated), bonde (farmer) at Esse, Leganger (Leikanger) parish, Ytre Sogn, census page 31. The page carries the remark 'lensmandsgaard' (the farm of the lensmann, the rural sheriff); the census does not say whether Povel himself held the office. Listed under him as sons (Sønn): Hans Povelsen (born about 1669), Olle Povelsen (26, born about 1675), Anders Povelsen (born about 1683) and Knud Povelsen (born about 1686). The 1701 manntall lists men and boys only, so his wife and any daughters are not named. Povel is the old written form of Paul (Pål). ¶1

Esse here is Ytre Esse (inferred): the 1701 census lists 'Esse' and, as the next household on the same page, 'Indr Esse' (Indre Esse, Lars Elingsen); the farm without the prefix is the outer farm. ¶2

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the grade · 1 search with no result · 1 open question

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Probable as the father of Ole Paulsen Esse. The parent-son link to the 1701 Olle Povelsen is stated by the census itself (primary). What is Probable is that this Olle is the Ole Povelsen of Ytre Esse whose probate was held in May 1758: same farm, same patronymic, he named his eldest son Povel (the usual pattern of naming the first son after the paternal grandfather) and his second son Anders (also the name of Olle's brother), and no other Ole Povelsen is at Esse in 1701. He would have been about 83 at death. ¶3a

Searches that found nothing 1

Probate search: the Ytre Sogn probate register 1698-1853 on Digitalarkivet has no Povel, Paul or Poul Hansen at any Ese farm. All 19 Ytre Sogn probates at Ese farms from 1698 to 1760 were listed, and there is none for him, so his probate cannot be used to prove the link to Ole Povelsen. The link stays Probable. Unread leads: Randi Olsdatter, Ytre Ese, 1720-1728 (skifteprotokoll A 3a, fol. 61a) and Brite Olsdatter, Ese, 1728-1733, either of whom could be his widow or a daughter-in-law. ¶4

Open questions and the next record to read 1

What would confirm it: a Ytre Esse probate, mortgage-book (pantebok) or farm-transfer entry between 1701 and 1758 that names Povel Hansen's son Ole as the holder of Ytre Esse, or Povel Hansen's own probate naming his son Ole Esse. ¶3b
