About the family

Father of Ulvil, born 25 Nov 1782 at Tvet and baptised on the 3rd Sunday in Advent (15 Dec) 1782 in Åmli parish, with wife Ingebor Olsdatter (Åmli ministerialbok 1775-1796, image 35). ¶1

Father of Gunsteen (Gunstein), born 20 Jun 1785 at Tvet and baptised at Lille Topdal on 17 Jul 1785, with wife Ingebor Olsdatter (Åmli ministerialbok 1775-1796, image 50). ¶3

Father of Ole, born 4 Oct 1788 at Tvet and baptised at Lille Topdal on 19 Oct 1788 (same book, image 63 right). He was still at Tvet in Oct 1788. ¶10

Probable family: the 1766 probate after Aslach Salvesen of Skjeggedal names the widow Guro Ormsdatter, her brother 'Tallach Ormsen Skjeggedal (+ sk. 1796)' and her father 'Orm Tallaksen' (Østre Råbyggelag skiftekort, Skp. 13a fol. 170, cards 12413-12419). Guro Ormsdatter of Øvre Skjeggedal was buried in 1788 aged 62, and Tallak Ormsen of Øvre Skjeggedal in 1796 aged 65. Torjus Ormsen of Øvre Skjeggedal (born about 1742) was probably a younger brother, and so a son of Orm Tallaksen. The card does not name him, so this is inferred from his patronymic, farm and age. ¶17

Father (Confirmed): 'Torgius Ormsen' is named as brother of the deceased Tallach Ormsen of øvre Skjeggedal, whose father was Orm Tallaksen, in the estate of 6 Sep 1796 (Skifteprotokoll nr. 17 fol. 164; card 39942/12483). He is the 'E. M. Torgius Ormsen øvre Skieggedal' buried in 1798 aged 56 (same farm). His siblings were Tallach (d. by 1796, wife Torborg Svenningsdatter), A[m]borg (wife of Aslak Terjesen Ramse, probably the 'E. M. Aslak Tergiesen øvre Ramse' buried 7 Oct 1798 aged 73) and Guro (wife of Aslak Salvesen Skjeggedal). ¶19

Mother, Probable: Ragnild Nielsdatter, who married Orm Tallaksen of Skjeggedal on 21 Jun 1726 (Åmli register 1711-1740, DA 9920 canvas 42). Inferred: no record names her as his mother (see Ragnild Nielsdatter). ¶23

Life story

Bygdebok (Åmli ætt og heim I, Øvre Skjeggedal, canvas 93): 'Torjus Ormson f 1744 d 1798, g 1776 m Ingeborg Olsdtr. Austenå'; children Olav 1776 d. 1787, Ragnhild 1779 d. 1787, Orm 1781 d. 1781, Ulvhild 1782 (m. 1816 [?] Torson Vrålstad), Gunstein 1785 (m. 1808 Sigrid Aslaksdtr. [Ytre] Ramse), Olav 1788 (m. 1825 Gunvor [?] Mjåland). This gives Ingebor Olsdatter (see her page) as Ingeborg Olsdtr. from Austenå, married 1776 (test record: Tovdal/Åmli marriages 1776, DA 9923; and the Austenå chapter, vol. I canvases 137-183, for her parents). Note the book has Gunstein marrying Sigrid Aslaksdtr. Ramse in 1808, while identified a Gunsteen Torjussen with wife Gunvor Gregoriusdatter; the 1808 marriage is the better test for which Gunstein is Torjus's son. ¶31

Date check (orchestrator, from the page images): both the betrothal (img 89 left, after the 27 Dec 1775 betrothal of Herjus Olsen and Anne Halvorsdatter, under a '1776' heading) and the marriage (img 89 right, under '1776', after the 24 Mar 1776 marriage of Herjus Olsen) belong to 1776, not 1775. The worker's first reading of 1775 is superseded. ¶12

Betrothal: 'Torgius Ormsen Øvreskieggedal og Ingeborg Olsdatter Østenaae' were betrothed in Topdal on 1 January 1776; no sureties or fathers are named (Åmli ministerialbok 1775-1796, betrothals, entry no. 3 under the heading 1776, image 89 left). So he came from the farm Øvre Skjeggedal in Tovdal. ¶8

Marriage: Torjus Ormsen and Ingebor Olsdatter, 'both of Topdal', were married on the 7th Sunday after Trinity 1776 (21 Jul 1776, computed from Easter 7 Apr 1776), the same day as Tolv Jørgensen and Berte Torjusdatter; no fathers or sureties named (same book, marriages, entry no. 5 under the heading 1776, image 89 right). ¶9

Called 'Torgius Ormsen Skjeggedal', husband of Ingeborg Olsdatter, in the probate of her cousin Even Tellefsen, Hushovd in Mykland, 18 May-21 Jun 1776 (Østre Råbyggelag skiftekort, Skp. 14a fol. 73). ¶13

A 'Torjus Tvet' stood godfather at Tvet in Aug 1783 and at Øvre Ramse in Nov 1784, and 'Torjus Tvet's wife' at Skaaledalen under Tvet in Jul 1784 (same book, images 39, 45, 47). This is probably the same man, living at Tvet; that is inferred from the farm name, not stated. ¶2

A 'Torgius Ø. Ramse' (Torjus of Øvre Ramse) stood godfather at Lille Topdal on New Year's Day 1787, and a 'Torgius' of Østenaa in Mar 1788, in the same Ramse-Østenaa-Vrolstad circle that sponsored Ulvil and Gunsteen (same book, images 56 and 60). These may be Torjus Ormsen after leaving Tvet; the records give no patronymic, so this is unproven. ¶6

In the burial register, 'Torgius Tvet's' son Ole (11) and daughter Ragnild (9) burned to death on 4 Nov 1787 and were buried at Lille Topdal on 25 Nov 1787 (Åmli ministerialbok 1775-1796, image 124 left). These are probably his children from the 1776 marriage: the father's patronymic is not given. ¶11

Burial: 'E. M. Torgius Ormsen øvre Skieggedal', a widower aged 56, died 3 Oct 1798 and was buried at Lille Topdal on 27 Oct 1798 (Åmli ministerialbok 1796-1816, burials 1798 no. 73, p. 200, image 105 right). The month is read from the ditto marks after the previous entry (died 1 Oct, buried 21 Oct). His age gives a birth about 1742. This explains his absence from the 1801 census. He was back on his home farm, Øvre Skjeggedal, by then. ¶15

Because he was a widower in Oct 1798, his wife Ingebor Olsdatter had died before him. No Åmli burial of hers was found from Aug 1789 to Oct 1798 (Åmli ministerialbøker 1775-1796 and 1796-1816, read entry by entry). ¶16

Sibling lead for the Ulvild link, not evidence: the 1865 census of Øyestad lists 'Gunsten Torjusen', husmann at Skibehei, born 1780 in 'Omlid Prgj.' (Åmli), pf01038163003760, and the 1801 census lists Gunsteen Torjussen, 18, servant at Evenstad in Øyestad (pf01058326004663). He may be Torjus Ormsen's son Gunsteen (b. 20 Jun 1785). A probate or marriage record of this man naming his sister Ulvild (wife of Tellef Torsen) would tie the 1782 baptism to Ulvild Torjusdatter. No probate index hit for Ulvild Torjusdatter as heir (probate person search, 0 hits). ¶24

Sibling cluster for the Ulvild link (still inference, so his grade on Ulvild Torjusdatter stays Probable): his three surviving children by Ingebor Olsdatter were Ulvil (b. 1782), Gunsteen (b. 1785) and Ole (b. 1788) (Åmli 1775-1796, images 35, 50, 63). In the next generation all three names turn up together at Øvre Ramse in Tovdal: 'Ulvil Torjusdr', mother, Øvre Ramse, 1820 onward; 'U.K. Ole Torjussen Øvre Ramse, 30' (so b. about 1790) married Signe Tellefsdatter of Øvre Ramse on 25 Jun 1820 (Åmli 1816-1829 p. 225; Tovdal p. 188 no. 1, pv00000009098558); and 'Gunsteen Torjussen', Ramse, aged 38 (so b. about 1787), married Gunvor Gregorsdatter of Mjaaland on 25 Sep 1825 (Åmli 1816-1829 p. 226 no. 1, pv00000009594230) and named his son Tørjus (bapt. 9 Jul 1826, Tovdal p. 9 no. 4, pd00000037678161). Three Torjus-children with the same names, in the same birth order, on one farm, fit the 1782-88 baptisms well and make the Øyestad Gunsten Torjusen (b. 1780/1783) an unlikely match for the Gunsteen of 1785. No record read names their father, so this does not settle the link. Åmli confirmations 1796-1816 (DA 9924, e.g. canvas 81) give only name, farm and age, so Ulvild's confirmation will not name her parents either. ¶26

Sibling trace for Ulvild, no change of grade. Gunsteen Torjussen, who with Gunvor Gregoriusdatter of øvre Ramse had Tørjus baptised in Tovdal on 9 Jul 1826 (Åmli 1816-1829 p. 78 no. 4), moved to Øyestad: the same couple (Gunsteen Torjusen and Gunilde Gregoriusdatter, Asal Eje) baptised Gunild Serine there on 16 Oct 1831; the 1865 census has Gunsten Torjusen 86, born Omlid (Åmli), husmann at Skibehei in Øyestad, with wife Gunvor Gregoriusdatter 66, born Omlid; and he was buried at Øyestad on 1 Jun 1873 (died 21 May), 'Enkemand Gunsten Torjesen', aged 96, of Asdal, born Omlid (Øyestad 1872-1886 p. 279 no. 48, read on the scan). His Øyestad death means any registration or probate is in Nedenes sorenskriveri, death register no. 2 1863-1889 (DA 35242), May-June 1873, not yet read; he left children, so it will list widow and children, not his sisters. Contradiction to note: his ages (86 in 1865, 96 in 1873) imply birth about 1777-1780, whereas Torjus's son Gunsteen was baptised in 1785 (born 20 Jun 1785). Ages in old age are often inflated, but the identification of the 1826 Gunsteen as Torjus's son is an inference only and is not strengthened by this. ¶28

Ole Torjussen: fathered children with Signe Tellefsdatter in Tovdal in 1821 (øvre Ramse), 1822, 1824 (Ramse), 1826 and 1828 (ytre Ramse) (Åmli 1816-1829, index). Signe Tellefsdatter, gaardmandskone, 63, of ytre Ramse, was buried 31 Jul 1858 (Åmli 1852-1869 fol. 397 no. 5), so Ole was presumably alive then. Ole's own burial was NOT found: the Tovdal burials 1830-1850 (DA 16870 canvases 28-33) and 1852-1869 (DA 855 canvases 404-411) were read page by page with no adult Ole Torjussen, and the two indexed 'Ole Torjussen' burials (27 Mar 1858, 23 May 1867) are infants of ytre Ramse aged 11 weeks and 4 weeks (presumably grandsons). He is not in the 1865 census of Åmli. He presumably died 1858-1865 outside Tovdal, emigrated, or is in a page gap (Tovdal Aug-Dec 1861). Ole Torjussen of (ytre) Ramse is named as a parent at marriages in 1858 and 1863 (Åmli 1852-1869 index, not read). ¶29

1801 census: no Torjus Ormsen (or Torgius/Tørjus/Torjes) appears anywhere in Åmli parish, including Tovdal, Gjøvdal and Mykland (indexed search). He had probably died or left the parish by 1801; that is inferred from his absence. ¶4

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
