Life story

Younger brother of Oline Slind's father, and confusingly bearing the same given name: Ole Gundersen and Beret Gundersdatter of Slinden had two sons called Gunder, born 1844 and about 1848, and both are entered as 'deres Son' in the 1865 census of the same household. That is unusual but it is what the record says, and it is a trap for anyone matching 'Gunder Olsen Slind' in American or Norwegian records without a date. ¶1

1865: aged 17, helping his father with the farm. 1875: 'Gunder Olsen, b. 1850, Deres s g Hjelper Foraeldrene' at Slinden - married and helping his parents. 1878: a child baptised to a Gunder Olsen, b. 1849, 'Ind' (inderst, a lodger holding no land) of Slinden. 1891: a widower, 'hjelper faderen, smed' - helping his father, and a smith. 1896: remarried, 3 June 1896, entered as 'Grdmd' (farmer) of Slindan. 1900 and 1910: head of a household at Slindan sondre / Slindan nordre, 'Gaardbruger, Smed og Sagbygger' - farmer, smith and sawmill builder - with a second family including Gurina born 1896, Olina born 1897, Ole born 1899, Thomas born 1903 and Berntine born 1905. ¶2

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Rated Probable because the man's stated birth year moves between 1848, 1849 and 1850 across the records and because two Gunder Olsens of the same farm have to be told apart by trade and household; the smith who remarried in 1896 is consistently distinguished from Oline's father, who was a tenant farmer at lopenr 184 and married only once. ¶3
