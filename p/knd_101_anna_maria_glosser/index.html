About the family

Mother of Maria Jacobina Glosser (see her page). ¶1a

Wife of Jacob Glosser/Gloss (see his page); mother named in his Enzweihingen baptisms 1719-1731. Surname unknown; possibly Anna Maria Schmidgall, daughter of Hanß Adam, who married a 'Hanß Jacob Schlosß', son of Martin, at Enzweihingen in 1719 (904607563/4607563, image 1184569-00135): Speculative, as the groom's surname reading does not match. ¶2a

Life story

Possible origin, Speculative: the only marriage of a Jacob to an Anna Maria indexed at Enzweihingen in 1718-20 is 'Hanß Jacob Schlosß', relatives 'Martin, Anna Maria' (record 4607563), with bride row 'Anna Maria Schmidgall', relatives 'Hanß Adam, Hanß Jacob' (904607563, image 1184569-00135), entered between rows of 1717 and 16 May 1719, so early 1719, which fits a first child on 4 Dec 1719. The other Jacob of that year, Jacob Bluss son of Conrad, married Catharina on 21 Oct 1719 (4607568) and baptised with Katharina 1724-36. The bride is the Anna Maria Schmidgall baptised at Enzweihingen on 16 Feb 1697 to 'Joh. Adam, Anna' (record 4605650); the other Anna Maria Schmidgall there (b. 5 Feb 1702 to Johann Georg x Maria Agnes, 4605767) married in 1723 as daughter of 'Hß. Jerg' (904607581). Speculative because the groom's surname is read Schlosß, not Gloß/Bloß, and nothing but the first names and the date joins him to the father of Maria Jacobina. Her parents are entered at Speculative and her name left without a surname. ¶3a

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
