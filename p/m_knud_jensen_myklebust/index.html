About the family

His patronymic shows his father was named Jens. No baptism, marriage or burial for him is indexed; the Borgund registers for 1739-1800 would have to be read page by page. ¶3

Heirs against the baptisms (inference): Christhana aged 15 in Aug 1765 fits Christiana baptised 20 Oct 1750; Lisbeth aged 22 fits Elisabeth baptised 21 Oct 1743; Jens of age fits the Jens baptisms of 1740-1741. The children Pernille (1746) and any other Jens do not appear as heirs and presumably died before 1765. No earlier marriage of Knud is mentioned in the probate. ¶6

Life story

Of Mychlebust (Myklebust), Borgund. Named as father of Christiana, baptized 20 Oct 1750. Nothing further found. ¶1

He was still at Myklebust seventeen years after his daughter Christiana's baptism: 'Knud Jens.' of Mychlebust had another child baptized at Borgund on 24 December 1767. Together with the 1750 baptism that places his married life at Myklebust across at least 1750-1767 and puts his own birth about 1720-1725. ¶2

1762 extra tax list (scan read): Myklebust is on DA 49637 view 42, pp. 71-72 (Borgund sogn, after Grytebust, Rotsvik and Lyngsten). Household 5 reads 'M: Knud Jens[en], H: Siri Pedersd[atter]' with children over 12 '1 S[øn]: Jens, 1 D[atter]: Christiana', and a maid Margrethe Arnesdatter; 5 persons. So in Sep 1762 Christiana (bapt. 20 Oct 1750, then just under 12 but listed anyway) was living at Myklebust as the daughter of Knud Jensen and his wife Siri Pedersdatter. Lisbeth (about 19) is not in the household and was presumably in service elsewhere. Grade unchanged (Probable), graded on his link to Christiana Knudsdatter: the list names the relationship, but it is the same Myklebust girl that the 1765 probate already names. The weak step the audit flagged is still the move from Myklebust (1762, 1765, confirmation 1767) to Skjong (debt in the 1775 probate, engagement 1777), a change of farm through the mother's 1766 remarriage, with no record that names the Skjong bride as Knud's or Siri's daughter. The other Myklebust households in 1762 (1 Iver Abrahamsen and Lisbeth Iversdatter; 2 Niels Olsen and Marthe Pedersdatter; 3 Peder Olsen and Birthe Olsdatter; 4 Johannes Larsen and Inger Hovardsdatter; 6 Knud Colbeinsen and Birthe Olsdatter) have no Christiana, so the list adds no rival. ¶12

1762 extra tax (scan read): Borgund sogn, Mychlebust, household 5: 'M: Knud Jensen; H: Siri Pedersd.'; 'Børn over 12 aar': '1 S: Jens, 1 D: Christiana' (source 49637, view 42, p. 71). This places Christiana (b. Oct 1750, just 12) at home with both Knud and Siri in Oct 1762, but, like the 1765 probate, it does not reach her later identity as the wife of Peder Olsen Skjong, which is what keeps this link Probable. No grade change. ¶13

Probate (scan read): Sunnmøre sorenskriveri, Skifteprotokoll 15A (3A 21), fol. 428b-430b. On 3 Aug 1765 the estate of the late Knud Jensen of Mychelbust (Myklebust), Borgund skipreide, was registered and valued for division between his surviving wife Siri Peders datter (her guardian, laugverge, Guttorm Larsen) on one side, and 'den afdødes efterladte Børn' (the deceased's surviving children) on the other: a son Jens Knudsen, of age (myndig); a daughter Lisbeth Knudsdatter, aged 22, guardian (formynder) Ole Johansen [reading uncertain]; and a second daughter Christhana Knudsdatter, aged 15, guardian Hendrich Olsen Sunde. The estate is divided and each child's share listed (Jens 68 rd.; Christhana 32 rd. [reading uncertain]); the entry ends with the signature of Melchior Falch. ¶4

The division page of his 1765 probate (3A 21 fol. 429b-430a, view 24562/443) was: it is the itemised inventory and the start of the shares (a heading for 'Jomfruen Lisbeth Knuds d.' is legible); it contains no statement about the children's mother beyond the opening already noted. ¶8

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

3 on the grade · 1 correction · 1 search with no result · 2 open questions

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 3

Upgrade: his 1765 probate names a daughter Christhana Knudsdatter aged 15, and the 1775 probate after his widow's second husband, Peder Andersen of Skjong (3A 25 fol. 843a-844a, scan), shows his children 'Christhina Knuds Datter' (unmarried) and Jens Knudsen Mochelbust as creditors of the Skjong estate, with Jens as guardian of his mother Siri Pedersdatter. Christiana was engaged from Skjong in 1777 to Peder Olsen Skjong, so the father of the tree's Christiana (wife of Peder Olsen Skjong) is this Knud Jensen. ¶7

Audit (1 Nov 2026): downgraded from Confirmed to Probable. His 1765 probate proves a daughter Christhana, aged 15. Her identity with Christiana Knudsdatter, wife of Peder Olsen Skjong (engaged from Skjong 1777), rests on a combination: same name and age, the widow's 1766 remarriage to Skjong, and a debt in the 1775 Skjong probate owed to 'Pigen Christhina Knuds Datter' next to one owed to Jens Knudsen. That debt entry is only partly legible and does not state her parentage. This is a strong circumstantial case, which is the standard for Probable. ¶9a

Re-proof attempt (overnight 1-2 Nov 2026), no change: still Probable. Searched for a record naming the wife of Peder Olsen Skjong as his daughter. (1) Her engagement, Borgund 20 Jul 1777 (pv00000010144082), gives only 'Pigen Christianna Knudsdtr.' of Schiong; the trulovere are Hans Pedersen and Peder Joensen of Schiong; no father named. (2) Her confirmation, Borgund 18 Oct 1767 (pk00000002551841), gives name, residence Mychlebust and age 16 only, no parents. (3) The 1801 census household at Sjaang has no kin of hers. (4) The 1775 Skjong debt line (3A 25 fol. 843b, view 24566/157) was re-read at full resolution: 'Pigen Christhina Knuds Datter har tilgode [?] 7 rd 5 ort 2 sk'; it states no parentage. (5) The Sunnmøre probate index has no probate after the widow Siri Pedersdatter (d. 1785, Skjong) and none for Jens Knudsen Myklebust or Lisbeth Knudsdatter. ¶10a

Corrections 1

Correction: the earlier notes that no burial is indexed for him and that he was born about 1720-1725 are superseded: he was buried at Borgund 9 Jun 1765 from Myklebust, age 51 (pg00000002548394, index), so born about 1714, and his probate was held 3 Aug 1765. He married Siri Pedersdatter of Sunde at Borgund on 22 Nov 1739 (pv00000002822979, index). The baptism of a child Else on 24 Dec 1767 with father 'Knud Jens.' of Myklebust cannot be his child, since he was dead by Jun 1765; it is either another Knud Jensen at Myklebust or an index error, and should be read on the scan before it is used. ¶5

Searches that found nothing 1

1762 extra tax list: Myklebust was not found in DA 49637 views 8-20, 44 or 46-50 (Borgund sogn pp. 3-27, 75, 79-88); the unread Borgund sogn openings are views 42, 43 and 45 (pp. 71, 73, 77). The list would not settle his link to the tree's Christiana in any case: she was baptised 20 Oct 1750 and so was under 12 on the tax date (23 Sep 1762), and only children over 12 are named. A 'Torbiørn Abrahamsen Mychlebust' was a farmhand on Store Kalvøy (view 47). ¶11
