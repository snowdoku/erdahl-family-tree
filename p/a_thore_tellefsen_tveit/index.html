About the family

NEW ANCESTOR (identified 10 Oct 2026). Thor Tellefsen of Tveit in Tovdal is the father of Tellef Thorsen Tved (b. about 1728), and so the great-grandfather of Thor Tellefsen Ramse (1760-1800). His estate was settled on 28 April 1740 in the Østre Råbyggelag probate court (Skifteprotokoll no. 10, folio 45b), under the farm heading "Tvedt" in Tovdal. ¶1

His patronymic, Tellefsen, and his son's name, Tellef, continue the strict Tellef-Thor alternation that runs down this family for seven generations: Tellef Nielsen - Thor Tellefsen - Tellef Thorsen - Thor Tellefsen - Tellef Thorsen - Torjus Tellefsen - Tellef Torjusen (Tellef T. Ramsey). ¶5

Children baptised (Åmli 1711-1740): Tellef, 17th Sunday after Trinity 1721 (buried 1739 aged 17½); Tellef, 26 July 1727 in Åmli church; Niels, lay-baptised at Trinity 1731; Gunder, 1st Sunday after Trinity 1735; Tellef, 8th Sunday after Trinity 1739. The 1740 probate lists the survivors as Tellef 'd.e.' (12), Niels (10), Gunder (5) and Tellef 'd.y.' (1). The probate ages run slightly high: Niels was really about 8¾ and Gunder about 4¾. ¶9

Life story

Marriage: he was betrothed on the second day of Christmas 1719 to Torbor Tergiesdatter (the betrothal adds 'Aschland'). They married at the Feast of the Visitation, 2 July 1720, in Tovdal: 'Thoer Tellefsön Tved og Torbör Tergiesdatter' (Åmli 1711-1740, canvases 24-25). ¶8

The 1740 settlement names his widow, Torbor Terjesdatter, and four sons: Tellew (Tellef) Thoreson "d.e." (den eldre, the elder), aged 12; Niels, aged 10; Gunder, aged 5; and Tellew "d.y." (den yngre, the younger), aged 1. Two sons were given the same name because the elder was expected to inherit the farm and the younger was named for a different relative — a common Norwegian practice. The eldest, Tellef the elder, born about 1728, is our ancestor. ¶2

A second probate confirms the family. When his widow's second estate was wound up on 9 October 1761 (Skifteprotokoll no. 12b, folio 446), the record lists "konens barn i 1. ekteskap" (the wife's children by her first marriage): Tellef Toresen the elder, now of age; Niels; Tellef the younger, aged 22; and Gunder, "død etter faren" (died after his father), whose share was settled at the same time. The card explicitly says she was "g.1.g. m. Tore Tellifsen + sk. 28/4 1740" — married first to Tore Tellefsen, whose estate was settled 28 April 1740. ¶3

Burial found: 'Thor Tellefsön Tved 58 aar' buried in Tovdal c. Feb 1740 (1740 entry 12, Åmli 1711-1740 pp. 152-153, canvas 81) → born c. 1682 (card says ABT 1700). Children baptised: Niels (Trinity 1731), Gunder (1735), Tellef (8 p. Trin. 1739); a son Tellef aged 17½ was buried 1739. ¶6

His own parentage is Probable: the archivist's cross-reference card for the 1747 Tveit probate gives the father of his brother Kjøll Tellefsen as "? Tellef Nielsen Tveit (+ sk. 1703)" and the mother as "? Aase Thorisdtr.", with question marks showing the archivist inferred it. The inference is confirmed independently by a kinship term: in the 1792-94 probate of Gunlek Aslaksen of Tveit, Thor's grandchildren are called "tremenning" (third cousins) of the deceased. Third cousins share great-grandparents, and that is exactly true only if Thor's father was Tellef Nielsen Tveit, the deceased's own great-grandfather. ¶4

Corrections 1

Correction: the birth date is changed from ABT 1700 to ABT 1682. His burial in Tovdal about February 1740, as 'Thor Tellefsön Tved 58 aar' (Åmli 1711-1740, canvas 81), gives a birth year of about 1682. He was therefore about 38 when he married in 1720. ¶7
