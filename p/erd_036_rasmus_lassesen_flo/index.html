Life story

1664 vicar's census: Nedre Flo (p. 208) had five farmers: Colben Jacobsøn 46, Østen Lassesøn 26, Olluff Lassesøn 50, Jon Pedersøn 30 and Jochum Torgiersøn 36; no sons listed. Øvre Flo (p. 210) had Knud Jonsøn 50 with Anders Rasmussøn 30 ('Ehr døff', is deaf) in the sons' column. No man named Lasse farmed either Flo in 1664, so Rasmus's father Lasse is not identified there. The two Lassesens on Nedre Flo (Oluf, born about 1614, and Øystein, born about 1638) may be kin; nothing is stated. ¶3

Aaland 1932: Aaland's Nedre Flo history (bruk nr. 1) lists '(Br.) Rasmus Lasseson, br. 1686; b. s. a. 18 mk. kg., som faren sa upp. * ?……… Øysteinsd.': Rasmus Lassesen, probably a brother of the previous holder Jørgen Lassesen (who took the holding in 1674 and moved to Bolstad), became tenant in 1686. In the same year he leased 18 merker of crown land that his father had given up. He married an Øysteinsdatter whose given name Aaland left blank. Aaland's Flo history, under another holding (label 407), adds that in 1712 Ivar Ivarsen gave up a third of his holding to 'Rasm. Lasseson N.-Flo', so Rasmus was alive in 1712 (auditor: death field changed from the inferred BEF 1716 to AFT 1712). The next holder listed is Lasse Rasmussen (from 1716, died 1733 aged 51). ¶1

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Upgrade: Speculative to Probable, graded on his link to his son Lasse Rasmussen Flo (see his page). New evidence: Aaland's account of the 1717 odel case between corporal Ivar Ivarson Flo and 'Lasse R. N.-Flo' (Stryn vol., Flo, nb.no label 398) says the land had been sold 'til Lasse sin far, Rasmus N.-Flo', and that Lasse inherited it; it also names Lasse's grandfather Lasse Andersson N.-Flo and says 'Lasse sine søner Rasmus N.-Flo... og Jørgen L. Bolstad' inherited from him. Earlier workers had only the brukar list, which does not mark Lasse as Rasmus's son. Stays Probable: it is the bygdebok's summary of a court record that has not been read. The record would raise it to Confirmed: the 1717 tingbok entry (not in the Stryn summer ting 16 Jul 1717, 30927 v528-529; try spring 1717 or 30928). ¶5

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed, graded on his link to his son Lasse Rasmussen Flo (see his page). Tingbok Nordfjord L0020 (source 30928), Stryn spring ting at Hilde, 28-29 Mar 1718, views 15R-16L (Aaland's '1717' is the year of the summons). Corporal Iver Iversen Floe sued 'Lasse Rasmussen Nedre Floe' to give up 1 pund 6 marker in Øvre Flo, which Iver said was his forefathers' odel, lost through poverty while Iver was in the King's service; until about five years ago his father, Old Iver Iversen, had used it, until a contract was made with 'Contrapartens Fader Rasmus Lassesen'. Lasse Rasmussen produced two title deeds of 5 Nov 1639 and 18 Nov 1641, 'hvormed hand beviste at hans afgangne Farfader Sal: Lasse Andersen Nedre Floe' had bought the 1 pund 6 marker from several people. He then stated 'at hans Fader Rasmus Floe arvede et heelt pund deraf, og de øvrige 6 marker tilfaldt hans Farbroder Jørgen Lassesen Bolstad', and that after Iver Floe had used the land for some years without lease, a contract was made 'imellem hans afgangne Sal: Fader Rasmus Floe' and the plaintiff's father Old Iver Floe, dated 26 Oct 1712. Judgment: the land had been Lasse Rasmussen's and his forefathers' odel; the suit was dismissed. The court record names him twice as Lasse Rasmussen's father ('hans Fader Rasmus Floe', 'hans afgangne Sal: Fader Rasmus Floe' and, in the plaintiff's words, 'Contrapartens Fader Rasmus Lassesen'), and names Jørgen Lassesen Bolstad as Lasse's farbror, so Rasmus was a Lassesen and a brother of the Jørgen Lassesen whom Aaland has moving from Nedre Flo to Bolstad. Continuity to the man in the tree: the 1718 Lasse Rasmussen of Nedre Flo is the Lasse Rasmussen of Nedre Flo whose probate was held 10 Oct 1733 (L0007 fol. 151) and who held the bruk from 1716 (Aaland); same farm, 15 years apart, and no rival Lasse Rasmussen on Nedre Flo is known. Death: alive at the contract of 26 Oct 1712, dead ('afgangne Sal.') by 29 Mar 1718; this also settles the 1717 odel case that looked for in tingbok 30927. ¶8

Records that disagree 1

Bailiff's census 1664-66 (p. 140, view 78): 'Heel gaard Nederflou' lists five farmers by first name only: Colben, Lasse, Oluff, Jon, Rasmus. The vicar's census of the same years (p. 208) has Colben Jacobsøn, Østen Lassesøn, Olluff Lassesøn, Jon Pedersøn and Jochum Torgiersøn instead, so the two lists disagree on two of five shares. The bailiff's 'Lasse' on Nedre Flo is the first record of a farmer of that name there in the 1660s and a lead for Rasmus Lassesen's father; no relationship is stated and he could be a clerk's slip. Øvre Flo (p. 142): farmer Knud, son Anders only. ¶4

Searches that found nothing 1

Negative: the 1717 Flo odel case that Aaland summarises was not found in tingbok 30927 (L0019). The Stryn winter ting of 22-24 Feb 1717 at Hegdal (v495R-500L) was read in full, with no Flo case; had already read the summer ting of 16 Jul 1717 (v528-529). The autumn round of 1717 runs Stad 2 Nov (v538R), Davik (v539R), Eid and Hornindal 12-13 Nov (v542-545L), Olden 19 Nov at Loen (v545R), Gloppen 22 Nov (v546R) and Breim 27 Nov (v551), with no Stryn session in v538-551. Next places to look: 30927 v552 to the end (a December 1717 Stryn ting), then the start of 30928. Grade unchanged (Probable). ¶7
