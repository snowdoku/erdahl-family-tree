Life story

Born to Lutheran Brethren missionaries in China; grew up in China, Norway, Mayville ND and Ferndale WA. ¶1

1930 census: on 5 April 1930 Palmer M. Valder, 10, son, born in China and marked 'Am Cit' (an American citizen born abroad), lived with his parents and seven brothers and sisters in Ferndale town, Whatcom County, Washington (ED 37-31, sheet 2B, line 89); he was attending school. Read from the census image; the Ancestry index of the same entry gives 'Palmer M Valder', born about 1920, China. ¶7

Graduated Lutheran Brethren Schools, Fergus Falls 1939; a year at Iowa State Teachers College. ¶2

1940 census: the 1940 census index shows Palmer M. Valder, son, born about 1920 in China, in his father Peter's household at Ferndale, Whatcom County, Washington, with his brothers Otto and Carol and his sister Marie. Index only; the image was not read. ¶8

Draft registration: on 16 October 1940 he registered for the draft at Ferndale, Whatcom County, Washington, as Palmer Mascus Valder, born 16 April 1919 in China. Index entry only (Ancestry collection 2238); the card itself was not read. 'Mascus' is the indexer's reading of his middle name and is unverified; it may be a misreading of a Norwegian name such as Magnus or Martinus. ¶9

1940 census (read from the image, replacing the earlier index-only entry): Palmer M. Valder, son, 20, single, born in China, marked as an American citizen, was at home in his parents' house in Ferndale town, Whatcom County, Washington, where he had also lived in 1935. He had not attended school since March 1940; his highest grade reads as 'H-4', the fourth year of high school (the reading is uncertain), which fits his graduation from the Lutheran Brethren Schools in 1939. He was entered as a 'New Worker' looking for work in farming, with no previous employment, no weeks worked in 1939 and no income. ¶11

U.S. Army Air Corps 1942-1945. Furniture sales in Waterloo IA; sales in Minneapolis 1957-~1992; retired to Joice, IA. ¶3

He was the third of eight children. His seven siblings were Myrtle Pearl Thompson (1915-1976), Gladys Margie Behnke (1916-1991), Frances Esther Walla (1921-2013), Rev. Otto Trygve Valder (1922-2013), Marie Rose Nelson (1925-2009), Carol David Valder (1926-1997) and Fred Martin Valder (1928-2015). Three of them - Palmer himself, Frances and Otto - were born in China. ¶6

1950 census: on 1 April 1950 Palmer M. Valder, 30, born China, married, was head of his own household in Otter Tail County, Minnesota (ED 56-35), next to his parents' household on the same sheet. He worked 48 hours a week as a travelling salesman for a musical instrument company. With him were his wife Wava L., 27, born Iowa, and their two daughters Sharon and Joanne. Read from the census image. ¶10
