About the family

Second wife and widow of Siuffuer (Syver) Knudtzøn of Dal. His probate (card 2041; protocol DA 25281 s. 312a, view 322, 'Anno 1664 den 30 Novemb.', read) divides the estate 'imellem hans efterlatte Hustru... Audbiør Haldorsdaatter, och hans Kiere Kiødbørn som hand Auflet ved den første Quinde Dorte Erichsdaatter, Nemblig Erich Siuffuerssøn Kiørsta[d], Sampt Rangnild Siuffuersdaatter hendis efterlatte Børn Engebret, Knud och Halder...; Och Andre Kiødbørn som hr Auflet ved den Igienleff[ue]nde Quinde: Nemblig Knud Siuffuerssøn Dahl, Sampt Dorte Siuffuersdaatter..., Marit och Ingeborre Siuffuersdøtter'. So the record sorts the children by mother, and Knud is a child of the living second wife (some words of the opening lines are uncertain; 'den første Quinde', 'den Igienleffuende Quinde' and the names are clear). ¶1

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade:, new person entered as Confirmed, graded on her link to her son Knud Syversen of Dal (see his page). The probate names 'Knud Siuffuerssøn Dahl' among the children 'Auflet ved den Igienleffuende Quinde', the widow Audbiør Haldorsdatter, which names her as his mother (the 'hendes med ham avlede' form of audit 8, rule 2). Continuity: the vicar's census of 5 Nov 1664 (DA 35504 p. 22) has 'Knud Siffuersß. 26' as the holder of Dal, the same weeks and the same farm; no rival Knud Syversen at Dal. ¶2
