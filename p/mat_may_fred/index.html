Life story

Second child of Gunder and Guri. His birth at Sioux City, Iowa in May 1868 is the record that dates the family's arrival in America: they were in Woodbury County, Iowa between the 1865 emigration and the taking of the Dakota homestead before May 1870. ¶2

1870 census: almost certainly the 'Setter, Godfrey', aged 3, in his father's household at dwelling 122, Sioux Valley, Union County, Dakota Territory, 21 August 1870. 'Godfrey' is plausibly Gotfred or Godfred, the Norwegian name behind the American 'Fred', though no document proving that has been found. Two fields are wrong for him: his age should be 2, and his birthplace should be Iowa rather than the Norway dittoed down the column. The enumerator ran the birthplace ditto unbroken down lines 17 to 19 and only broke it for the newborn on line 20, so this is a careless ditto and not a statement. The next record to settle it is the 1880 census of Spink Township, where his birthplace should read Iowa. ¶8

Married Ida Regina Stylen (1876-1946) in 1910. ¶3

1880 census: 'Godfried', son, 12, farmer, born IOWA, in Gunder Satter's household at Spink Township, Union County, Dakota Territory, 15-16 June 1880 (ED 5, sheet 407A, line 6; indexed 'Badfred'). Age 12 fits a birth in May 1868 and the Iowa birthplace fits Sioux City - correcting the 1870 census, which gave Dakota. It also supports the reading of the 1870 'Godfrey' as Gotfred/Godfried, the Norwegian name behind 'Fred'. ¶9

He gave $5 at his brother Gust's wedding about 1894, and was still living in 1934, when his sister Lizzie's obituary places him at Bainville, Montana. He went west with the Montana homestead boom. ¶4

Bainville is in Roosevelt County, in the far north-east corner of Montana. His sister's 1937 obituary already placed him in Montana. He left a son George and an adopted daughter. ¶7

Obituary, Great Falls Tribune, 16 December 1944: 'Wolf Point — Funeral services were held Tuesday, Dec. 12, for Fred Satter, old time resident of Bainville, who died Saturday at Williston, N. D. Services were conducted at the Lutheran church with the Rev. A. H. Bachman officiating. Satter is survived by an adopted daughter and a son, George, of Bainville.' ¶5

Obituary: Great Falls Tribune (Montana), 16 December 1944 - 'FRED SATTER. Wolf Point - Funeral services were held Tuesday, Dec. 12, for Fred Satter, old time resident of BAINVILLE, who died Saturday at WILLISTON, N. D. Services were conducted at the Lutheran church with the Rev. A. H. Bachman officiating. Satter is survived by an ADOPTED DAUGHTER and a SON, GEORGE, of Bainville.' Transcribed on Find a Grave memorial 26045600. The Saturday before a Tuesday 12 December 1944 funeral is 9 December 1944, so that is his death date; the file carried only the year. ¶6

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
