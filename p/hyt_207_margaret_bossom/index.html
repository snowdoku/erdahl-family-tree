Life story

Margaret, daughter of John Bossom (OCR 'Bossond') of Bossom, Devon; wife of Nicholas Southcott and mother of John Southcott of Bovey Tracey (Visitation of Devon 1620, Harleian Society vol. 6, p. 268). The surname is read from OCR text and should be checked on the page. Added. ¶1

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
