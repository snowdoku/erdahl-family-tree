About the family

Widow of Ole Jonsen of Sandvigen, Selbu, at his probate on 9 Oct 1767 (Skifteprotokoll 6-1, 24756, fol. 86b): 'hans efterlevende Enke Kari Olsdatter', with her guardian Ole Flønes. The only heir was 'en Søn... Jon Olsen'. Her birth is given only as before 1730, inferred from a son aged about 20 in 1767. Probable as the mother of Jon Olsen Sandvik for the same reasons as her husband (see Ole Jonsen). The probate does not say whether Jon was her son or a stepson; it is written as a skifte between the widow and 'the heirs he left'. Her death and parents are not found. ¶1

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 correction

Corrections 1

Correction: she was the second wife of Ole Jonsen of Sandvik and the stepmother, not the mother, of Jon Olsen Sandvik. The skifte after Ole's first wife, Ingebor Michelsdatter, at Sandvigen, 17 Jun 1762 (24755 fol. 246a), names Jon (Joen Olsen, 14) as Ingebor's only child. Kari married Ole between June 1762 and 1767 and was his widow in October 1767. She is therefore not a direct ancestor; her own parents are no longer a target. ¶2
