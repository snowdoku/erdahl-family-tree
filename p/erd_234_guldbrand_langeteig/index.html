Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Probable as father of Marethe Guldbrandsdatter (see her page), graded on that link. Farmer at Langeteig in 1645 ('Guldbrandt', with his wife 'Gurj Joensdtr') and still 'Gulbrand bruger... 86 aar' there in 1666. Other children listed in 1645: Johans, Peder, Olle and Astri Guldbrandsen/-datter. Langeteig evidence. (1) 1645 poll tax, Langeteig in Tiugum sogn: four farmers with wives, 'Guldbrandt' + 'Gurj Joensdtr', 'Peder' + 'Brite Olsdtr', 'Østen' + 'Ingier Frederichsdtr', 'Lauridtz' + 'Ellj Jonsdtr' (p. 24); the farm's sons and daughters (p. 29) include five children of Guldbrand: Johans, Peder, Olle, Astri and 'Marete Guldbrandsdtr'. No other Guldbrand at Langeteig. (2) 1666 vicar's census, Tiugum sogn N. 21 Langeteig (35519 view 170, p. 314): holders Ola 59, Harmund 45, 'Gulbrand bruger... 86 aar' (b. ~1580) and 'Frich bruger... 26 aar' (b. ~1640) with his son 'Niels 1 1/2 aar' (b. ~1664/65). No Peder is left on the farm. (2b) 1664 vicar's census, Tiugum sogn, Langeteig (35519 view 152, p. 281): farmers 'Ola Erichsen', 'Gulbrand', 'Harmund', 'Frich'; servant 'Lasse Andbiørnsen' 17; husmann 'Engelbart'; again no Peder. (3) 21 Mar 1701, Langeteig: joint probate after Frich Pedersen and his wife Marethe Guldbrandsdatter, heirs their son Niels 'boende Langeteig' and daughter Britte, wife of Jørgen Langeteig (1b p. 275a). So in 1666 the young holder Frich (= Frich Pedersen, d. by 1701) farms beside the old Gulbrand, with a son Niels (= the 1701 heir Niels of Langeteig), and his wife Marethe Guldbrandsdatter carries the patronymic of the only Guldbrand on the farm, whose daughter Marete is listed there in 1645. Grading: Probable, not Confirmed. No record names Marethe as Guldbrand's daughter in so many words beyond the 1645 patronymic list of the farm's children, and the step from the 1645 'Marete Guldbrandsdtr' to Frich's wife of 1701 rests on name + farm across 56 years (bridged in 1666 only by Frich farming beside Gulbrand). She would have been born before ~1633 (listed with the taxable children in 1645), some years older than Frich (b. ~1640): possible, but it keeps the link Probable. ¶1a

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Test record: a Langeteig deed or tingbok entry 1660-1700 naming Frich Pedersen's 'Svigerfader Guldbrand', or the 1664 bailiff's census (35573 views 115-116, Tjugum), which gives sons' ages under each farmer. ¶1b
