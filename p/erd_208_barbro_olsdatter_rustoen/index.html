Life story

Aaland (Innvik 1974 ed., Rustøen, Haraldbruket) says Harald Klausson married the widow of Rasmus Hallsteinson (d. about 1697), whom he gives in brackets (uncertain) as 'Barbrå Olsd. Kårstad'. Her son by Rasmus was Halsten Rasmussen (b. 1691 in 1701). Speculative as mother of Rasmus Haraldsen (b. about 1699-1700): she married Harald about 1698, so fits, but Aaland marks the name as uncertain and no record names Rasmus's mother. ¶1
