About the family

Father of Ingebor Gjestsdatter: Probable. Her 1826 marriage names her father 'Gjest Esse', and he is the only Gjest at Esse in the 1801 and 1815 censuses, where his household includes 'Ingeborg Giertsdatter', 10, in 1801. ¶2

Parents found: the Ytre Sogn probate of 18 Sep 1761 after the cottager Giest Guttormsen at Nauteskreen in Tjugum skipreide names his widow Anne Hermundsdatter and their son 'Giest Giestsen 1/2 aar gl.' (skifteprotokoll 7b, fol. 546b, No. 57). Giest Giestsen at Esse (1801 census age 40, 1815 age 54, so born about 1761) is the only Giest Giestsen in the 1801 Leikanger census, and Esse is in the same skipreide. The name Gjest also matches the posthumous-naming custom (the boy was named after his father, who died within months of the birth). Parent links entered as Probable: the probate does not name Esse, and no baptism has been read (Leikanger 1761 baptisms, register 11566/11567). ¶4

Life story

Baptism found: Leikanger ministerialbok 11566, Trinity Sunday 17 May 1761: 'Bapt: Giest Giestsøn Nødeschreen'. Godparents: Joen Hammersnes, Sølfest Nielsøn Farnes, Anders Meenes; Unni Lillestølen, Anna Lasses D. Hammersnes. The date is given as the baptism date (birth a few days earlier). This is the child of the probate of 18 Sep 1761 at Nauteskreen ('Giest Giestsen 1/2 aar gl.', his father Giest Guttormsen already dead), so the boy was baptised with his dead father's name. The baptisms of Jan to May 1761 (c52-c57) contain no other Giest. With the 1801 and 1815 ages (40 and 54, both giving 1761), the unique name in the 1801 Leikanger census, and his daughter Anna (b. about 1797) named as for his mother Anne, the identification of the Esse cottager with the Nødeskreen child is now strong. ¶5

Confirmation found: Leikanger confirmation list (11567, canvas 234, fol. 230), confirmation of 26 Sep 1779, among the boys of Tjugum sogn: 'Lars Marcussen Tue, 19 aar' and 'Giest Giestsen ibid [Tue], 18 a 19 aar'. Born therefore about 1760-61, which fits the baptism of 'Giest Giestsøn Nødeschreen' on 17 May 1761 (he would be 18 1/3) and the 1801 (40) and 1815 (54) census ages. He is the only Giest Giestsen in the Leikanger confirmation lists of 1771-1781 read (canvases 230-234), so the 1761 posthumous son of Nauteskreen survived to adulthood in Tjugum sogn, living at Tue (where Marcus Thue's son Lars was confirmed with him) in 1779. The list names no parents, and Tue is neither Nauteskreen nor Esse, so this narrows but does not close the identification; grades unchanged. ¶6

Soldier roll checked: the 1780 rolls of the Tiugumske company (Captain Jacob Gerhard von Meidell), 1st Bergenhus national infantry regiment, are in Digitalarkivet 51486 as item 17 (company roll, views 350-369) and item 18 ('Mod Legds og Unge Mandskabs Rulle', dated Hesse gaard 4 Nov 1780, views 370-389); item 19 starts about view 390. The unge mandskap roll lists each legd (group of farms, with the farmers' names and ages) and, beside it, the young men by name and age only, e.g. 'Ole Anfindsen 19' beside legd 5 (Eggen, Tveiten, Meland). It gives no fathers' names and no farm for each boy, so these rolls cannot prove his parentage; at best they place him in a legd. The view URNs run in sequence (view n = URN...ru2011120175 + (553+n), zero-padded to four digits), so any view can be fetched directly. Grade unchanged. ¶10

Bygdebok corroboration: Balestrand 1: Ættesoge (1985), Indre Ese (canvas 102): '[I] 1801 var det ein husmann [på] Indre-Ese. Det var Gjest Gjestson. Han er [...] den Gjest Gjestson som i 1761 vart [...] Nautskreda. Han vart [i] 1790 gift med Kristi [...]', died 1820, wife 1844; children include Ingeborg (m. Anders Olson Ese) and Arnfinn Gjestson (b. 1794, m. 1819). Canvas 131 adds a daughter-in-law line ('[...] gift [med...] Gjestson Ese'). The book agrees with his 1761 Nauteskreen birth; it does not name his father in the recovered words. ¶12

Marriage found: Leikanger ministerialbok 11567, Tjugum sogn. Betrothal ('Trolovet') 25 Sep 1790: 'Soldat Giest Giestsen Esse med Pigen Christie Anfinds Datter Tvedt', sureties ('Forlovere') Niels and Anders Tjugum (canvas 202 right, fol. 198). Marriage ('Copuleret') 11 Oct 1790, same names and farms (canvas 203 left, fol. 199). Neither entry names a parent. The bride's farm is written 'Tvedt' (the capital is this clerk's T, as in 'Tiugum', not his looped F), i.e. Tveiten/Tveit in Tjugum skipreide, not Feten. This dates the marriage (before only 'BEF 1790') and shows Gjest was a soldier, at Esse already in 1790; Ingebor (b. about 1791) is their first child. The groom 'Giest Giestsen' was confirmed in 1779 while living at Tue and by 1790 was at Esse, where the 1801 and 1815 censuses find him with Christi. Grade unchanged. ¶7

Baptism of a daughter: Anna, baptised at Tjugum 24 Sep 1797, 'Forældrene: Giest Gjestsen Esse og Christi Anfinds D.'; godparents Sieur Kieldsen Meneß, Ellend Andersen Esse, Mari Olsdatter ibid, Britha Kieldsdatter Toftedahl, Anna Marcusdatter Esse (Leikanger 11568, canvas 98 left). The daughter's name Anna follows his mother Anne Hermundsdatter. Grade unchanged. ¶8

Soldier roll lead: Tjugum skipreide formed its own company, the 'Tiugumske' company of the 1st Bergenhus national infantry regiment. Its company roll, its roll of young men (unge mandskap) and its musketeer roll for 1780 are in Generalitets- og kommissariatskollegiet, Ruller no. 91, '1. Bergenhusiske nasjonale infanteriregiment 1780-1783' (Digitalarkivet 51486, about 450-600 views, not indexed). The index card at view 3 gives the company order (Gloppen, Vik, Tjugum, Ytre Sogn,...). These legd rolls give each young man's name, home farm, age and height, so they should show where Giest Giestsen (19 in 1780) was living. The 1776-80 roll (no. 90, source 51485) and the 1791-1803 roll (no. 96, source 51491) are the neighbouring volumes. Not yet read. The 2nd Bergenhus regiment (no. 95, source 51490) covers Hordaland, Voss and Aurland, not Tjugum. Grade unchanged. ¶9

1801 census: Giest Giestsen, 40, cottager with a little land (huusmand med lidt jord) at Esse, Leikanger, in his first marriage to Christi Anfindsdatter, 38; children Ingeborg 10, Anfind 7, Anna 4. 1815 census: Gjest Gjestsen, 54, cottager (Hmd.) at Esse, with his wife Christi Anfindsd. 52 and Anfind 19, Anna 16, Kari 12 and Sieur 8. ¶1

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Confidence raised from Probable to Confirmed by the orchestrator on two primary records already read: the Leikanger marriage of 16 Jan 1826 (register 11569, 1826 no. 2) names the bride Ingebor Gjestsdatter's father as 'Gjest Esse', and the 1801 census (Esse, household 03, pf01058393002080) lists 'Ingeborg Giertsdatter', 10, in the household of Giest Giestsen, 40, the only Gjest at Esse in 1801 and 1815. The probate index (1800-60) has no estate for him and no burial of his was found in the indexed Leikanger burials 1838-60, so he probably died between 1815 and 1838; Leikanger burials before about 1838 are not indexed. ¶3

The case for this identification 1

Supporting: in the 1775 probate after his grandfather Guttorm Gjestsen of Vikøren, Vik skipreide (Ytre Sogn 9a No. 67, p. 601-602, 24167 view 302), 'Giest Giestsen', about 14, inherits as the only son of the dead son Giest Guttormsen. This places a Giest Giestsen born about 1761 in the family between the 1761 Nauteskreen probate and his 1779 Tjugum confirmation (at Tue). A guardian is named at the foot of p. 602 but not securely legible ('Ole... Nunnedale?'). No grade change: the record does not name Esse or his own children. ¶11
