Life story

Betrothal or marriage, late 1739 or 1740: 'Soldat Torkel Nielsen Klagstad og pigen Seri Mickelsdatter' (the soldier Torkel Nielsen of Klagset and the maid Siri Mikkelsdatter), Selbu klokkerbok 16315 canvas 3 (0-based). The names are clear; whether it is the betrothal or the wedding, and the exact Sunday, need a zoom. This is the same couple who head Klagset 749 in the 1762 extra tax list, and it fits a daughter Lisbet b. about 1753-55 (confirmed 1769 'of Klagset'). ¶4

Farmer of Klagset (Klegset, matr. 749), Selbu, in the 1762 extra tax list, with wife Siri Mikkelsdatter and children over 12 Niels, Mikkel and Bir[ith?] - the only Torkel on Klagset that year (the other two Klagset households, matr. 733 and 687, have none). The 1762 extra tax list names every person over 12 in each household, with spouses first. ¶1

1801 census: at Draxen, where Lisbet Torkelsdatter lived as Bersvend Sivertsen's wife, there were also Mikkel Torkilsen, 53 (b. about 1748), cottar with land and sawmaster at Draxen's sawmill, and a widow Marit Torkilsdatter, b. about 1744. A son Mikkel (over 12) is listed in Torkel Nielsen and Siri Mikkelsdatter's Klagset household in 1762, and a 'Marit Torchelsd.' of Draxen was confirmed in 1774 (a different, younger Marit or a misdated reading). Mikkel Torkilsen at Draxen is very probably Lisbet's brother, which supports the Klagset parentage (still Probable). ¶3

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
