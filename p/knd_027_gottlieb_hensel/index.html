Life story

Marriage: on 9 Nov 1913 at the Standesamt Pfeilsdorf the farmhand (Knecht) Gottlieb Hensel, Evangelical, living at Pfeilsdorf, Kreis Briesen, married Rosa Therese Schmidt of Bilau, Kreis Culm. He was the son of the farmer (Landwirt) Gottlieb Hensel and his wife Christine née Kohls (surname reading uncertain), living at Tariverde in Romania. Witnesses: Gustav Eichelberg, 25, and Rosa Eichelberg née Lingnau, 23, of Pfeilsdorf (Pfeilsdorf marriages 1913 no. 15; image read). ¶1

Birth: the act gives his birth as 14 February 18?? at Tariverde in Romania. The year is written out in words, and the words after 'acht hundert' are not securely legible on the image (first read as 1880s; re-read by the auditor without a secure result); the date is therefore not entered. Tariverde is a German colony in the Constanța district of the Dobruja. ¶2
