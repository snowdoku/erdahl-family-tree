Life story

Her maiden name is unknown. 'Nelson' is her married surname, and the 'K.' may be a patronymic initial such as Knudsdatter or Kristiansdatter. The 1958 obituary of her son John William confirms her first name, Ellen. Her dates and the 1863 marriage come from Find a Grave. ¶1

Still unresolved this round (searched in Oct 2026): her maiden name and origin. She is not named in Gust S. Nelson's 1953 obituary (Lake Mills Graphic, 1 Jul 1953, p. 2), which names only his children and brother 'Willie'. The only Fillmore County church book transcribed on Digitalarkivet (Rushford) has no 1863 marriage for her. Best remaining sources: Peter S. Nelson's Civil War pension file (widow and child affidavits often name the first wife's maiden name and the 1863 marriage place); the 1863 Fillmore County marriage record; and the Iowa marriage registrations of her sons Gust (to Annie Hanson, about 1889) and John William (to Amelia Hanson, 1894), which name both parents. ¶4

1870 census (index only; the image has not been read): she is indexed as 'Caroline', 34, born Norway, wife in the household of Peter S Nelson, Iowa Township, Winnebago County, Iowa, with Nicholis 8, Gustave 6, Andrew 4 and Thomas M 2. Age 34 in June 1870 means born about 1835-36, which agrees with the Oakland Cemetery list (born 1835, died 1874 aged 39). ¶8

Biography (1884): her husband's sketch in the History of Kossuth, Hancock and Winnebago Counties (1884) says that in 1861 Peter S. Nelson 'was united in marriage with Ellen Caroline Gudman', and that Mrs. Nelson died in 1874, leaving six children: Nicholas, Gus, Andrew, Thomas M., John W. and Melinda. ¶5

Maiden name, and how far to trust it: 'Gudman' appears in only one source, a county history printed ten years after her death and probably supplied by her widower. It is not a usual Norwegian surname. It may be a shortened patronymic, such as Gudmundsdatter (daughter of Gudmund), or a farm name, or a misprint. Until a primary record (the pension file, a marriage record, or an image of a son's marriage register) repeats it, her name field is left as 'Ellen K. /Nelson/', and 'Gudman' is recorded here as a lead, not a fact. ¶6

Name forms: 'Ellen K.' (gravestone, as transcribed by Find a Grave and the Oakland Cemetery lot list); 'Ellen' (son John William's 1958 obituary); 'Caroline' (1870 census index); 'Ellen Caroline' (1884 sketch). Together they show that the 'K.' is a second given name, Karoline or Karine, spelt with K in Norwegian, not a patronymic initial as an earlier note guessed. This is an inference from the agreement of the four forms. ¶7

Sons' marriage registers (index only): Winnebago County gives the groom's mother as 'Karina Nelson' for Gust (20 May 1889) and 'Karine' for John William (7 Jun 1894). This could be her second name Karine, or it could be the stepmother Kari Monsdatter, who raised the boys from 1878; the index alone cannot tell them apart, and neither entry gives a maiden name. The register images have been queued (S0094, S0095). ¶9

Maiden name still unknown: neither death certificate gives a maiden surname (John W.'s writes 'Caroline' and dashes; Gust's writes 'Ellen Nelson', her married name). So the Speculative identification with Elen Karine Gudmundsdatter of Nedstrand (and her parents) is neither helped nor harmed by these certificates; the 'Caroline/Karine' given name is at least consistent with it. ¶23

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade (signed-in lane, 2 Nov 2026): graded on her link to John William Nelson. John W. Nelson's Iowa death certificate, State File No. 114-58-20610 (Iowa Death Certificates 1958, FS image 615 of 5,010, read on the image), gives his birth as 8 Sept 1870 in Winnebago Co., his father as 'Peter Nelson' and his mother's maiden name as 'Caroline -----------' (given name only; informant his son Vern E. Nelson). Continuity: the 1870 US census, taken in June 1870, three months before his birth, lists Peter S. Nelson's wife as 'Caroline', 34, born Norway; the 1884 county-history sketch names Peter's first wife as 'Ellen Caroline', mother of John W., who died in 1874; her gravestone reads Ellen K. The only named rival is the stepmother Kari (Carrie) Monsdatter, whose marriage to Peder S. Nilsen is entered in the Winnebago church register on 23 May 1878, eight years after John William's birth, so she cannot be his mother. His full brother Gust S. Nelson's Iowa death certificate (28 Jun 1953) gives the mother's maiden name as 'Ellen Nelson' (informant his son Russell Nelson); together the two certificates give her two given names, Ellen and Caroline. ¶21

Records that disagree 2

Conflicting record: her son John William's 1925 Iowa census entry gives his mother's maiden name as 'Johnson Andria', born Norway, dead, and says his parents married in Norway. This disagrees with every other source (Ellen/Caroline/Karine; 'Ellen Caroline Gudman' in 1884; marriage about 1861 in Minnesota) and does not fit the Gudmundsdatter candidate. It is weak evidence: on the very next line the same informant gives the wife Emilia's mother as 'Erickson Annie', whose real name was Andrea (Andrine) Erickson, so 'Andria' may be carried over from her. John William was four when Ellen died and was raised by his stepmother. No change in confidence. A second informant is available on his brother Gust S. Nelson's 1925 card (indexed 'G S Nelson', Winnebago County), not yet read. ¶19

Resolving the noted contradiction: John William's 1925 Iowa census entry gave his mother's maiden name as 'Johnson Andria' and a marriage in Norway. The 1958 death certificate (Caroline) and Gust's 1953 certificate (Ellen) both disagree with it, as do the 1870 census and the 1884 sketch, while 'Andria' matches the name of John William's mother-in-law Andrea (Andrine) Erickson given on the very next line of the same 1925 sheet. The 1925 reading is therefore treated as an informant's slip and no longer blocks the grade. The earlier note's reading of 'Karine/Karina' in the sons' marriage indexes remains ambiguous between Caroline and the stepmother Kari. ¶22

Corrections 1

Correction: marriage year 1861, per the 1884 sketch, which the eldest son Nicholas's age (born about 1861-62, Minnesota) supports. The 1863 date earlier taken from Find a Grave is replaced. Place unknown; Peter was in Fillmore County, Minnesota, from 1857. ¶10

Alternatives considered and set aside 8

Candidate identification (Speculative): she may be Elen Karine Gudmundsdatter, born 28 Jul 1836 at Nedstrand, Rogaland, Norway, daughter of Gudmund Gudmundsen and Johanne Wilhelmsdatter. For: both given names match (Elen Karine = 'Ellen K.' on her stone, 'Ellen Caroline' in the 1884 sketch, 'Caroline' in the 1870 census index, and possibly 'Karine/Karina' as the mother in her sons' marriage indexes); the 1884 surname 'Gudman' reads naturally as a shortened Gudmundsdatter (daughter of Gudmund); the 1836 birth fits the 1870 age of 34 within two months; the Nedstrand Elen Karine was entered in May 1859 on the North America passport of the Hegland family of Sørbø, Rennesøy, and a Hegland family that is probably the same (the 1865 schedule gives no ages) was living in Canton Township, Fillmore County, Minnesota, by 1865, the county where Peter lived from 1857 and where Ellen married him in 1861; and no Norwegian record places the Nedstrand Elen Karine in Norway after the passport. A Norway-wide index search found no other Elen/Ellen Gudmundsdatter born 1833-37 who emigrated. ¶12

Candidate identification (Speculative), why it is not yet Probable: no record places Elen Karine in America (she is not in the 1865 Hegland household, and neither she nor the Hegland family is found in the 1860 census index), so the link to Fillmore County runs only through her employer's family. Ellen's stated ages run one to two years older than the candidate (1870 census 34, where the candidate was 33; death in 1874 'aged 39', where the candidate was 37 or 38). 'Caroline' is the usual American rendering of Karine but is strictly a different name. 'Gudman' rests on one secondary source. Nothing in the Norwegian records ties the Nedstrand woman to Ellen K. Nelson directly; the match rests on names, age and timing. ¶13

Candidate identification (Speculative), a same-name woman excluded: a godmother written 'Elen Karine Gudmundsdt.', of Ferkingstad on Karmøy, stands sponsor at the Skudenes (Åkra) baptism of Johan Martin, son of Niels Endresen of Stave, on 21 Oct 1860, after the passport date. The scan (Skudenes ministerialbok A 3.1, fol. 132, no. 154) shows that she stood with Jetmon Jonassen of Ferkingstad, and the 1865 census of Ferkingstad lists Jetmund Jonasen, 43, a cottager and pilot, with his wife Elen Karine Gundersdatter, 42, born in Skudenes, and children born from about 1849, one a son named Gunder. She was the only Elen Karine at Ferkingstad, and no Elen Karine Gudmundsdatter appears in Skudenes in the indexes. The godmother was therefore this older, married Elen Karine Gundersdatter, and 'Gudmundsdt.' was the clerk's slip. She was not the Nedstrand woman born in 1836, so the 1860 entry does not place the candidate in Norway after 1859. ¶14

Candidate identification (Speculative), what would settle it: (1) the Winnebago County, Iowa, marriage register images for her sons Gust (20 May 1889) and John William (7 Jun 1894), queued as S0094 and S0095, if they give the mother's maiden name or birthplace; (2) Peter S. Nelson's Civil War pension file (Co. H, 2nd Minnesota Infantry, NARA), whose widow's or marriage affidavits often name the first wife's maiden name and the 1861 marriage; and (3) an 1861 marriage entry in a Fillmore County church book (Norwegian Lutheran or Methodist Episcopal) naming the bride as Elen Karine Gudmundsdatter or giving her origin. ¶15

Candidate identification (Speculative), baptism: if the identification holds, she was born on 28 Jul 1836 and baptised on 7 Aug 1836 in Nedstrand parish (Sjernarøy local parish), Rogaland, as Elen Karine Gudmundsdatter, daughter of Gudmund Gudmundsen and Johanne Wilhelmsdatter, inderster (lodgers, cottars without their own holding) at Ellingsnæs under the farm Lekjeletnæs. Her godparents were Tomas[?] Bjergaa[?], Knud Wingehaven, Anna Wilhelmsdatter Bonden[?] (probably her mother's sister, an inference from the shared patronymic), Marta Ellingsnæs and Ingeborg Helle. ¶16

Candidate identification (Speculative), confirmation: if the identification holds, she was confirmed in Nedstrand in 1850 as 'Elen Karine Gudmundsdatter Helle' (living at Helle), daughter of Gudmund Gudmundsen and Johanne Wilhelmsdatter, huusfolk (cottagers) at Valland[?]; her knowledge was marked 'Godt' (good), and she had been vaccinated in 1837. There is no emigration note in the margin. ¶17

Candidate identification (Speculative), emigration: if the identification holds, she left Norway on a passport issued on 13 May 1859 by the Ryfylke bailiff to Tosten Jørgensen Hegland of Sørbø, Rennesøy, aged 51, his wife Bertha Jacobsdatter, 39, and their six children, for North America; an interlined addition reads 'samt T[jenestep]ige[?] Elen Karine Gudmundsdatter 23 Aar' (and the servant-girl[?] Elen Karine Gudmundsdatter, 23 years). Her age of 23 fits the 1836 baptism exactly. A Hegland family (Fosten/Tosten, Betsey, Michael, Malina, Elizabeth, Betsey, Thomas, and George with his own family), probably the passport group although the 1865 schedule gives no ages or birthplaces, was enumerated in the Township of Canton, Fillmore County, Minnesota, on 1 June 1865. No Elen Karine is in their household, which would fit a marriage before 1865. ¶18

Ruled out as her family: the Gudmundsen family of Norway Township, Fillmore Co. (Hans Gudmendson, 49, and Ingbor A., 49, in the 1875 Minnesota census, with Johanne 14, Berthe M. 11 and Emma L. 8, all born in Norway; parents of Gustava Gudmansen, who married Gaarder in 1872 per the 1912 county history) came from Nittedal (Hakadal), Akershus: Digitalarkivet indexes Johanne, b. 4 Feb 1861, and Emma Lovise, b. 15 Apr 1867, as children of a Hans Gudmundsen there, so the family arrived after 1867. Hans and Ingeborg (b. about 1826) are too young to be parents of Ellen (b. about 1835). The Martin Gudmundsen of the same township is probably Martin, b. 15 Feb 1831 at Hougeie, Nittedal, son of Gudmund Hansen and Berthe Hansdatter. No Nittedal daughter of a Gudmund born 1833-37 named Elen, Karen or Karine is indexed (only Ingebor Dorthea, b. 1834). The 'Gudman' lead for Ellen therefore does not run through this family, unless she was an elder sister who emigrated alone before 1861, for which there is no evidence. ¶20

Searches that found nothing 1

Still unresolved after this round of research: her maiden name and origin are unknown. She married Peter S. Nelson (Peder Nilsen) in 1863, apparently in Minnesota (Find a Grave links a Minnesota marriage index entry). Her son Gust was born 8 Mar 1864 in Fillmore County, MN. The Oakland Cemetery transcription calls her Peter's '1st wife' (b. 1835, d. 1874, aged 39). No Norwegian-language church entry naming her has been found. The 'K.' is probably a patronymic initial (for example Knudsdatter or Kristiansdatter). Best leads are Gust S. Nelson's 1953 Iowa death certificate, John William's 1958 death certificate, and the 1863 Fillmore County marriage record. ¶3
