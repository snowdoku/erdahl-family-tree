About the family

Widow of Lasse Rasmussen of Nedre Flo (see his page) in his probate of 10 October 173[1 or 2] (Nordfjord skifteprotokoll L0007, fol. 151), present with her legal guardian (lagverge) Østen Hilde. Her surname Aamundsdatter is clear; only the end of her given name, '-gne', is legible on the black-and-white scan (Ragne, Signe and Ingeborg-type forms all possible). Probably died after 1731. ¶1

Mother of Ragnilde Lassesdatter (Probable, auditor). The argument: (1) the probate lists all seven children (Aamund, Ole, Peder, Rasmus, Mallene, Helge, Ragnilde) as one group after naming her, apparently as the late man's children 'med hende' (with her; that phrase is only partly legible), and it names no children of an earlier marriage and no maternal guardian for them, as such probates normally did; (2) the eldest son is named Aamund, which fits the custom of naming a son for the maternal grandfather; Aamund was apparently adult (age 2[?], 'og self myndig' partly legible), so she was married to Lasse by about 1706, before Ragnilde's birth about 1712/13. Against it: a widow can be a second wife, and her given name and Aamund's age are only partly read. ¶2a

Life story

1738 settlement: Nordfjord skifteprotokoll A 8 (probate extracts), 1738, Stryns Skibbred, under 'Nedre Floe': 'Hos Enken Mallene Aamundsdatter blev efter hendes egen begiering gjort hendes Liqvidation og Deeling, nemlig: at hendes andre Søn Ole Lassesen, som nu er 22 aar gammel, tilfalt paa sin Andeel 36 rd..., Tredie Søn Peder Lassesen 20 aar... 36 rd..., og den fierde og yngste Søn Rasmus Lassesen 17 aar... 36 rd', and these 'tre yngste Sønner' had earlier inherited after 'deres Sal. Fader' by the skiftebrev of 10 October 1733 [sic]; their guardians were Lars Frøise[?], Anders Clementsen Kirkeeid and Østen Hilde. This is the widow's own settlement with her sons (she was alive; it is not a probate after her death, although the index lists her as 'arvelatar'). It NAMES her as mother of Lasse Rasmussen Flo's sons Ole (about 1716), Peder (about 1718) and Rasmus (about 1721), and calls Ole her SECOND son, so the eldest son Aamund (about 1708-10) was hers too. Graded on her link to Ragnilde Lassesdatter: stays Probable. Ragnilde (born about 1713) falls between two sons this record makes Malene's, which leaves no room for an earlier wife of Lasse as Ragnilde's mother, but the record does not name the daughters, so the link is still an inference. ¶7

Aaland 1932: Aaland names Lasse Rasmussen of Nedre Flo's wife 'Malene Aam.d., † 76 a. g. 1757' (checked on the page image): Malene Aamundsdatter, died 1757 aged 76, born about 1681. The patronymic agrees with the probate; the given name conflicts with the ending '-gne' read from the probate scan. Aaland's dates make her old enough to be the mother of all seven children (born about 1708-1720), which supports her motherhood of Ragnilde. Re-reading fol. 151 for '-lene' against '-gne', or finding her 1757 burial (Innvik), would settle the name. If she was Malene, the daughter Mallene bore her mother's name. ¶3

Name changed from '[?]gne' to Malene: re-reading the probate scan (Nordfjord L0007 fol. 151, image 155) across the line break, the line before her surname ends '... Hustrue Mal-' (wife Mal-), and the next line begins with the '-lene'/'-gne' fragment read before, then 'Aamundsdatter med hendes [Lagværge]', then 'Østen Hilde'. 'Mal-' is clearly legible; the fragment that follows fits '-lene' (the same scribe writes the daughter 'Mallene'). With Aaland's independent 'Malene Aam.d.', the given name is Malene (Mallene). Crops: and -widow-mal.jpg. The id keeps its old form. ¶4

Name confirmed from the probate itself: on fol. 153 (image 157) of the same probate the widow's share is headed in large chancery letters 'Encken Mallene Aamundsdatter' (the widow Mallene Aamundsdatter), fully legible. Malene/Mallene is now read from the primary record, not only inferred from 'Mal-' plus Aaland. The session date is 10 Oct 1732 (Probable; see Lasse Rasmussen Flo). Crop:. ¶5

Name reading: in Lasse Rasmussen Flo's probate, Nordfjord skifteprotokoll L0007 fol. 151 (Digitalarkivet 24224 view 153), the widow's name is split across a line break as 'Mal-' / '-lene Aamundsdatter', so Mal[l]ene Aamundsdatter. 'Mal' is clear and the rest faint; the earlier '[?]gne' reading was the start of the second line. This supports the name Malene. ¶6

Burial: Innvik burials, 7 May 1757, 'Malene Amundsdatter', Floe, born 1681 (index). This agrees with Aaland's 'Malene Aam.d., † 76 a. g. 1757' and confirms that she outlived the 1738 settlement. ¶8

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
