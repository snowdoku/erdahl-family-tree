Life story

Birth (Probable identification): 'Anna Sibylla Hoehner' (= Herner), born at Vaihingen an der Enz on 25 Nov 1689 to 'Johann Carolus, Anna Margaretha' (record 4767591). Her 1713 marriage row names her father Carl ('Anna Sibylla Hernery', relatives Carl, 904776645), and Joh. Carl Herner x Anna Margaretha baptised Susanna (11 Jan 1702, 4768025) and Maria Ursula (1 Feb 1704, 4768140) at Vaihingen. She is the only Anna Sibylla of this surname found (Vaihingen births 1684-1700 and nationwide Anna Sibylla daughters of a Carl checked); age 23 at marriage. Her own grade (on her daughter Susanna Margaretha) is unchanged. ¶6

Death (Probable identification): 'Anna Sibylla', relative 'Joh. Christoph', died at Vaihingen an der Enz on 4 Apr 1719 (record 4400295, image 1184565-00176); no surname or age indexed. ¶3

Coordinator review: held at Probable rather than Confirmed. Ancestry collection 61023 index rows do not label who is father, mother or spouse; the BRIEF audit 5 rule is that unlabelled index rows give Probable until the scan is read, or a FamilySearch index with labelled parent fields is found (queued S0233). The naming evidence in the note above is otherwise strong, so this person is a priority for an image read. ¶4

The case for this identification 2

Surname and father: she is Anna Sibylla 'Hernery' (Herner), daughter of Carl, who married Johann Christoph 'Schumeze' (= Schumayer), son of Jacob Christoph, at Vaihingen an der Enz in 1713 (bride row 904776645, relatives 'Carl, Johann Christoph'; groom row 4776645, relatives 'Jacob Christoph, Anna Sibylla'; image 1184562-00494). The couple's children follow at Vaihingen from Jan 1714 (Christian Margaretha, 4768716; Susanna Catharina Dec 1714, 4768783; Susanna Margaretha 18 Sep 1716, 4768896). A Joh. Carl Herner x Anna Margaretha had children at Vaihingen 1702-1706 (Susanna 1702, 4768025; Maria Ursula 1704, 4768140; Christoph Carl 1706, 4768292), and a Margaretha Herner, daughter of Carl, married Tobias there in 1716 (904776736): probably her father's family (her mother not entered; Anna Margaretha may be a later wife). Her own baptism is not indexed. Her grade (on her link to Susanna Margaretha) is unchanged. ¶5

Labelled evidence, grade held at Probable: FamilySearch, 'Deutschland, ausgewählte evangelische Kirchenbücher 1500-1971' (= 'Germany, Lutheran Baptisms, Marriages, and Burials, 1500-1971'), QPGK-45ZQ: Susanna Margaretha Schunaya, born Vaihingen 18 Sep 1716, Parents 'Anna Sibylla, Joh. Christoph Schunaya'; and QPGK-7T8Y / QPGK-7T87 (marriage 3 Aug 1737) label the bride's father 'Joh. Christoph Schaemayers' and the groom's father 'Adam Knodels'. The index-label objection is gone, but the gap from the 1716 birth to the 1737 bride is 21 years, and the father did not stay a widower (he remarried at Vaihingen 15 Aug 1719 to Maria Judith, Ancestry 4776817). A daughter of the second marriage could not be a Susanna Margaretha born before 1720, but the record of her as the only Susanna Margaretha of the father in 1716-1737 is not read (audit 9 rule 3 would need the name-reuse check). ¶7a
