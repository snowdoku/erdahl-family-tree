Life story

Ralph's half of the continuity, the executors 'Philippe Josselyn [and] Geffrey Josselyn my brethern', was checked in the low-resolution online image of the original will (ERO D/ABW 21/3). The 1524 PCC will is not at the Essex Record Office, so the link stays Probable until PCC 3 Porch is read. ¶2

Second abstract of the 1524/5 will found, grade unchanged (Probable): F. A. Crisp, Fragmenta Genealogica vol. 4 (private press), 'Joslin Family', abstracts the same will (P.C.C. 3 Porch) independently of French and gives the remainders as '(iii.) my cousin Rauffe Josselyn, son of John Josselyn late of Sheryng, and his heirs male; (iv.) Philip and Geffrey, brothers of last named Rauffe, and their heirs male'. The clause is thus reproduced in the same original spelling by two separate abstracts made from the PCC register, which makes misreading of the relationship very unlikely. Crisp dates the will '12 July 17 Henry VIII' [1525] where French gives 1 Aug. 1524; French's date is probably that of the land-will part (it follows a marriage indenture of 18 June 16 Henry VIII), Crisp's that of the testament; both say proved 13 Feb. 1525[/6], so this is not a contradiction about the relationship. Not raised to Confirmed because this note's earlier test record (the registered copy, TNA PROB 11/22/61) is still unread and both readings are abstracts. Test record unchanged: PROB 11/22/61 (PCC 3 Porch), remainder clause. ¶3

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 4

Graded on his link to his son Ralph Josselyn. The will of John Josselyn of High Roding, Esq., 1 Aug. 1524 (PCC 3 Porch, proved 13 Feb. 1525/6; abstract in NEHGR 71:20) entails his lands, failing his own and his brother Rauffe's heirs male, 'to my cousin Rauffe Josselyn, son of John Josselyn late of Sheryng... and for lack of such to his brother Philip... and for lack of such to Geffry, brother of the said Rauffe and Philip'. Continuity to the Ralph in the tree: the will of 'Rauffe Josselyn of Moche Canfeld', 31 May 1525, names as executors 'Philippe Josselyn and Geffery Josselyn my brothers', the same three-brother set (Ralph, Philip, Geoffrey); Jeffrey Josselyn was taxed at Sheering in 1513/14 and 1523/4, and Philip at Braintree in 1524/5 (lay subsidies, NEHGR 71:227-228). No other Ralph Josselyn with brothers Philip and Geoffrey is known; the only other Ralph of the family then, John of High Roding's brother (d. 1504), is named separately in the same will. The 1634 Visitation of London pedigree independently gives 'John Joscelin = Anne, son and heir of Geoffrey', with sons 'Rafe Joscelin = Elizabeth', 'Philipp' and 'Geoffrey Joscelin, third son'. ¶1a

Probable, not Confirmed: both wills were read only as printed abstracts. 'Late of Sheryng' means he was dead by Aug. 1524 (a John Josselyn taxed at Sheering on 20s. in 1523/4 may be him or a younger man). ¶1b

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed, graded on his link to his son Ralph Josselyn. Record: the registered copy of the will of John Josselyn of High Roding, Esq., TNA PROB 11/22/61 (PCC 3 Porch), dated 1 Aug 1524, proved 13 Feb 1525/6, read on the image. The remainder clause reads: 'And for lack of suche Issue I wolle that all the said premisses shall remayne to the heires males of the bodye of my brother Rauffe Josselyn lawfully begotten... And for lack of suche heires males then the said manors londes and tenementes and other the premisses with their appurtenaunces shall remayn unto my cousyn Rauffe Josselyn sonne of John Josselyn late of Sheryng and to the heires males of his body lawfully begotten', and, failing them, 'shall remayn unto Philip Josselyn brother unto the said Rauffe my said cousyn... And yf the said Philip Josselyn my cousyn dye without Issue male... then the said premisses shall remayn unto Geffrey Josselyn brother unto the said Rauff and Philip my cousyns'. ¶5

Audit 12 (2 Nov 2026): RAISED to Confirmed, graded on his link to his son Ralph Josselyn. Continuity: Ralph of Great Canfield's will of 31 May 1525, ten months later and a few miles away, names 'Philippe Josselyn [and] Geffrey Josselyn my brethern' (a three-brother set at both ends; audit 8 rule 3). The only rival Ralph (the testator's brother) is named separately in the same clause. ¶7

The case for this identification 1

Continuity: the will names the relationship (Rauffe, son of John late of Sheering) and gives Rauffe two brothers, Philip and Geoffrey. Ten months later the will of 'Rauffe Josselyn of Moche Canfeld' (31 May 1525, ERO D/ABW 21/3, key phrases checked on the original image) names 'Philippe Josselyn [and] Geffrey Josselyn my brethern' as executors: the same three-brother set, in the same corner of Essex (Great Canfield, High Roding and Sheering are a few miles apart), with Jeffrey taxed at Sheering in 1513/14 and 1523/4. The only rival Ralph, the testator's own brother, is named separately in the same clause. Supersedes the note that the link 'stays Probable until PCC 3 Porch is read'. ¶6
