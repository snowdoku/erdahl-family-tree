About the family

Her husband anglicised Genehr to Gaynor. His BC death registration (1989-09-018639) is for Fredrick Reihnhold Gaynor, a retired baker of Surrey, born 27 June 1898 at 'Muchen' (Munich), Germany, who died 15 October 1989 and was buried at Valley View Memorial Gardens, Surrey; it names his wife as Frieda Knodel, and she was the informant. In January 1974 her mother died at Frieda's house, 12496 72nd Avenue, Surrey, and Frieda, signing 'Frieda Gaynor', reported the death. ¶2

Life story

Death: Find a Grave memorial 104610625 records Frieda Knodel Gaynor (1912 - 30 March 2009), buried in Cardston Cemetery, Alberta, with her husband Fred Gaynor (1898-1989) linked as spouse. ¶3

Birth: Born at 3 a.m. on 4 March 1912 at Pfeilsdorf (Płużnica), West Prussia, in her father's dwelling, and named Frida; registered 9 March 1912 as no. 12 by her father, the settler (Ansiedler) Gottlieb Knodel, who signed; mother Karoline Knodel née Schmidt, his wife, both Evangelical. Registrar Fengler. ¶4

Baptism (index): the Evangelical parish of Villisaß, Kreis Culm, has a baptism of Frida Knodel on 30 Mar 1912, with the relatives indexed as 'Gottlieb, Caroline'. The civil birth at Pfeilsdorf was on 4 Mar 1912, 26 days earlier, and the relatives' names repeat the parents' given names, so this is very probably this child's baptism. This is the last Knodel baptism in the index for Villisaß. This is an index entry only (Ancestry 'Germany, Lutheran Baptisms, Marriages, and Burials, 1567-1945', dbId 61250); the register image has not been read, so the index does not say whether the two names in its unlabelled 'Relatives' field are the parents or the godparents, and it gives no sponsors. Index recordId 3649751, image id 0245317-00209. ¶8

Passenger-list index, birthplace (Oct 2026): her Ancestry dbId 1263 record 2638381 matches the keyword 'Pluznica' and not 'Fluznica', so the index's hidden birthplace field reads Pluznica (Płużnica), agreeing with the 1928 manifest. ¶9

Emigration: landed at Quebec on 22 July 1928 from the Megantic (from Southampton) with her parents; her index entry gives last residence Płużnica and occupation 'servant'. ¶6

Emigration: Canadian passenger-list index (Ancestry 'Canada, Incoming Passenger Lists, 1865-1935', anonymous search) lists Gottlieb Knodel (b. abt 1879), Karolina (abt 1881), Frieda (abt 1912), Erna (abt 1915) and Otto (abt 1918) on one manifest page, arriving 22 July 1928, destination Winnipeg, Man. Index keywords that match this record (tested one by one) are Quebec, Southampton, Megantic, Polish, German, Farmer and Married: i.e. the White Star-Dominion liner Megantic, Southampton to Quebec, the family travelling as Polish nationals of German 'race'. The manifest image (Quebec reel, 1925-35 series) has not been seen; it should give their last residence (expected Plużnica) and the relative they were joining. ¶7

1931 census: Frieda was counted twice. In her parents' household in Saskatchewan she is 'Freda', 18, born Germany, immigrated 1928, with the note 'abt Oliver BC'. At Oliver, BC, she is 'Freida', sister, 19, homemaker, keeping house for her brothers Jacob and Ludwig in a rented one-room wooden house; immigrated 1928. ¶5

Marriage, BC 1938-09-462373: on 12 August 1938, at the clergyman's house, 824 12th Avenue East, Vancouver (Church of Jesus Christ), Frieda Knodel, a domestic, spinster, aged 26, Lutheran, of 1050 Jervis Street, Vancouver, married Friedrich Reinhold Genehr, a baker aged 40, born in Germany. She was born at 'PFEILSDORF, Germany' - the town written in small capitals over an erased word, with a correction mark in the margin. Her father Gottlieb Knodel was born in Russia and her mother Karolina Schmidt in Roumania. She signed 'Frieda Genehr'. ¶1
