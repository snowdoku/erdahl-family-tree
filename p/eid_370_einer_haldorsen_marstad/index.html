Life story

Farmer at Marstad, Selbu. His probate (in the Stjør- og Verdal card index 77804, canvas 1305, and read in the protocol, 24751 view 326, fol. 694b, left page, skifte No. 1): 'Ao. Christi 1736 d. 19 Juny blev... Skifte foretaget og holden paa gaarden Marstad udi Selboe Præstegield efter afg: Einer Haldorsen Marstad imellem hans efterladte [Børn]': A. 'Ole Einersen Uglem', living on Uglem; B. 'afg: Haldor Einersen Marstad hans efterladte Børn': 1. Einer Haldorsen 12, 2. Peder Haldorsen [5], 3. Brynild Haldorsdatter [7], 4. Kiersti Haldorsdatter 3; C. 'afg: Gunild Einers D[atte]r efterladte Børn': 1. Torkild Povelsen 18, 2. Einer Povelsen 6, 3. Guru Povelsdatter 15, 4. Kiersti Povelsdatter 10. 'Børnenes Fader' Povel Torkildsen was made curator/guardian for Gunild's children; Peder and Haldor Bersvendsen Skinde were guardians for Haldor's children. (Bracketed ages are from the card where the protocol is hard to read.) ¶1

Birth before about 1665 is inferred: his son Haldor had a son aged 12 in 1736 and his daughter Gunhild was born about 1687 (burial age 41 in 1728). His wife (Gunhild's mother) is not named in the probate and is not added. ¶3

Earlier Marstad cards in 77804: canvas 1301 (B, 4/6-1689, Gunnild Marstad, prot. 1 fol. 6b) and canvas 1303 (24/2-1733, Haldor Einersen, widow Mali Pedersdtr., children Einer 9, Peder 3, Brynild 4, Kirsti 1/2; guardian 'Farfaren, Einer Marstad', prot. 2). The 1689 card (Gunnild Marstad) may be his wife or mother; not read. ¶4

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Graded Confirmed on his link to his daughter Gunhild (Gunild) Einersdatter (see her page). The probate names 'Gunild Einers Dr.' as a branch of his heirs, through her children, and so names her as his daughter. Continuity: her own probate, 30 Jul 1728 at Flackne/Flaknan (24750 fol. 193), is after 'Gunild Einers datter' between her widower Povel Torchelsen and their children; in 1736 the same widower, Povel Torkildsen, is 'Børnenes Fader' of Torkild, Einer, Guru and Kiersti Povelsen/-datter; and the 1756 probate after Povel Torchilsen (24753 fol. 732b) again lists Torchel, Einer 40, Guru about 34 and Kiersten, married to Ole Poulsen Berge (the tree's Kirsten (Kirsti) Paulsdatter). The children named in 1736 are therefore the tree's Gunhild's children, and no other Gunild Einersdatter is named. The bygdebok (vol. 5, Flora, canvas 509) also says Pål married Gunhild 'from Marstad'. ¶2

Audit 12 (2 Nov 2026): Confirmed KEPT, graded on his link to his daughter Gunhild (see her page). Ruling on the flag: the 1689 Marstad probate ('Ejner Olsen og Kirsti Olufs Datter', children of the widow Gunnild Marstad) does not contradict the link. The link rests wholly on the 1736 probate after 'Einer Haldorsen Marstad', which names 'afg: Gunild Einers Dr efterladte Børn' as one branch of his heirs, and on the widower Povel Torkildsen, 'Børnenes Fader', who carries her children to her own 1728 probate and his 1756 probate. Whatever his patronymic, the man whose estate was divided in 1736 is Gunhild's father. The conflict (Olsen in 1689, Haldorsen in 1736; or two Einers on the farm) matters only for his own parents and his name. Until a record joins them: do not add Gunnild Marstad (or an Ole or a Haldor) as his parent, and keep the name as the 1736 probate writes it. ¶8a

The case for this identification 1

Test record read; flag: the 1689 Marstad probate (24749 view 7, fol. 6b, card c1301) reads: 'Anno 1689 d. 4de Junij holtes Arffveskiffte og Delling efter afgangne Gunnild Marstad, som døde og boede paa Marstad i Selboe Prestegield, imellem hendis 2de efterlatte Børn, nemblig Ejner Olsen og Kirsti Olufs Datter... Formyndere for den Umyndige [Kirsti] var Ejner Olsen'. In the debts, 'Ejner Marstad' is repaid 3 rd. for 'den sal. qvindes Begravelse', and the closing line assigns the rest to 'Ejner Marstad'. So in 1689 the Einer at Marstad was Einer OLSEN, son of Gunnild and of an Ole. The bygdebok (Selbu i fortid og nåtid vol. 5, Marstad, canvas 117, rebuilt from word boxes) has '[...] 1680 enka Gunhild Marstad, skifte etter [henne] 1689. Einar Haldorsen [...]', i.e. it makes the widow Gunhild the holder before Einar Haldorsen. Either the 1689 heir Ejner Olsen and the 1736 decedent 'Einer Haldorsen Marstad' are one man under two patronymics (his children were named Ole and Haldor, and his daughter Gunhild, b. about 1687, bears Gunnild's name), or they are two men on the farm. This does NOT affect his Confirmed link to his daughter Gunhild, which rests on the 1736 probate. It does block adding Gunnild Marstad as his mother: the patronymics conflict and no record joins the two. ¶6
