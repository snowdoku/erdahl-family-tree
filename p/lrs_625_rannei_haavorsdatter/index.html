About the family

Second husband's probate read from the scan: 25668 skifte no. 91, fol. 138a, view 141 (foot of right page) and fol. 138b-139b, view 142. 'Anno 1725 den 19de July var paa gaarden Mellum Tvedt i Treungens annex og Hvideseid Præstegield', with lensmann Hans Ellefsen Lung and the appraisers Torgier Sønderland and Tov Tallefsen Homme, to register and divide the estate 'efter afgangne Staale Jonsøn, imellem hans efterladte hustru Rannej Haavorsdatter og deris tilsammen avlede barn, Nafnlig Torbiør Staalesdatter'. The widow's lauverge is 'Gullov Tvedt' (also a creditor of 28 rdl), the same man who was her lauverge in 1720 and Margith Bjørnsdatter's husband in 1729. The widow's share is 'Enken Rannej Haavorsdatter' 37 rdl 2 ort 6 sk; the daughter 'Torbiør Staalesdatter' the same, with odelsgods 5 5/8 setting in Tvedt bought (skjøde 6 Jul 1720) for 100 rdl; her guardian is Torgrim Leone. The record does NOT name Rannei's father Haavor or say how Gulløv was related to her, so it adds nothing on her parents. Her farm in 1725 was Mellom-Tvedt. Death changed to after 19 Jul 1725. ¶5

Life story

Probate 1720 (read from the scan, 25692 fol. 420a foot to 420b, views 431-432): the entry opens 'Anno 1720' with the sorenskriver Cornelius Hansen, the lensmann Hans Ellefsen Lung and two Nissedal appraisers, and continues: 'forsamblet paa... gaarden Tvedt i Treungens annex under Hvideseid Præstegield... for at [holde] Registering, Vurdering, Skifte og Deling... efter den Salig Mand Eigild Biørensøn... imellem hans efterladte hustru Rannej Haawarsdatter', her lauverge the dannemand Gulløug [Haavorsøn?], 'og deris tilsammen avlede børn een Søn og 3 døtre, Nafnlig Orm Eigildsøn', his guardian the dannemand Ewend Dale(?), 'Tone og Kari samt Walborg Eigildsdøtre, af hvilke den første er 9 aar, den anden 7 aar og den 3die ½ aar'. Odelsgods in the farm Tvedt is in the estate. The card (6856 view 1310) gives the date 26 Apr 1720. Some words are faint; the names, the relationships ('efterladte hustru', 'tilsammen avlede børn') and the patronymic 'Biørensøn' are clear. ¶1

Remarried by 1723-25 to Staale Jonsen of Tvedt in Treungen, who died 1725; his probate (card 6856 view 1313, skifteprotokoll 1722-34 S. 138) names 'Ranny Haavorsdtr' and one child Torbiør. Gulløv Haavorsen (her lauverge 1720, husband of Egil's sister Margith) may have been her brother (inference from the patronymic only; not added). Her son Orm named a daughter Rannei (see her page), which fits. ¶4

Correction (BRIEF rule 'Don't grade on a spouse to get round a Probable link to the child'): her Confirmed grade rested on her link to her husband Egil, but she enters the tree as the mother of Orm Egilsen Tvedt (see his page). ¶7a

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

3 on the grade · 1 on the identification · 1 open question

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 3

Confidence: Confirmed, graded on her link to her husband Egil (see his page): the 1720 probate names her 'hans efterladte hustru' of Eigild Biørensøn, and their children together ('deris tilsammen avlede børn') Orm, Tone, Kari and Walborg. Egil enters the tree through the same family (sister's probate 1729 names the same four children). Her place as mother of Orm Egilsen Tvedt carries the same Probable identification of the probate's Orm with the later Orm Egilsen Tvedt as Egil's does. ¶3

Audit 4 (1 Nov 2026): lowered from Confirmed to Probable, graded on her link to her son Orm Egilsen Tvedt, who is the person who puts her in the pedigree. The 1720 probate names her as Eigild Biørensøn's widow and mother of their children Orm, Tone, Kari and Walborg. Grading her on the spouse link to Egil does not lift her above Probable, because Egil himself (see his page) enters the tree only as Orm's father, and that link is Probable: joining the probate's Orm (12 in 1729) to the later Orm Egilsen Tvedt is an identification across years. Her own note already says her place as Orm's mother 'carries the same Probable identification'. A spouse link to a person whose own pedigree link is Probable cannot make her Confirmed. ¶6a

Graded on that link: Probable. The 1720 probate names her and Egil's son Orm Eigildsøn, but the step from that boy to the later Orm Egilsen Tvedt is by patronymic and farm across about 17 years (1729 to 1746, see Orm's notes), the same as Egil's. Lowered from Confirmed to Probable. A probate after her (after 1725; Vest-Telemark protocols 25669-25672, unindexed, no registers at either end of 25669 or 25671) naming Orm as her son, or any record of Orm 1730-45, would restore Confirmed. ¶7b

The case for this identification 1

Continuity: the four children of 1720 (Orm, Tone 9, Kari 7, Walborg ½) are the four heirs of Margith Bjørnsdatter at Tvedt in Treungen in Nov 1729 (Orm 12, Tone 20, Kari 17, Walborg 10, 25668 skifte no. 188), 9½ years later on the same farm, so the two probates are one family. Gulløv Haavorsen, Rannei's lauverge in 1720, was Margith Bjørnsdatter's husband in 1729. ¶2

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Would confirm: the record that confirms Egil as Orm's father (a record of Orm Egilsen between 1729 and his marriage that names his parents or the Tvedt heirs). ¶6b
