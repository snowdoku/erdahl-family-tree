Life story

Mother added from the bygdebok (Selbu i fortid og nåtid vol. 4, Uglan bruk 49, canvas 26): Svend Eriksen (1691-1722 user, b. ca. 1650, d. 1723) 'G. m. Ragnhild [...], f. ca. 1659, d. 1742', children Erik ca. 1688, Pål ca. 1693 (m. Ingeborg [...] Berge), Ola ca. 1697, Ingrid 1701 (m. Ola [...] Alset). Probable only (bygdebok). ¶7

He is the 'Poule', 8, son of Siund (Svend) Erichsen at Uglem in the 1701 manntall (canvas 118), brother of Erich Siundsen (13) - so born about 1693, a son of Svend Eriksen Uglem. His burial age 'ætat 3[6?]' (1727) is better read as 34. ¶5

Buried Jubilate Sunday (27 Apr) 1727: 'Gfæstet Pohl Berge ætat 3[6?]' - aged 36 (second digit unsure; possibly 30), so born about 1691 (16310 canvas 108 right). Alive and baptising at Berge in 1715: 'døbt Poul Berges barn Gurol[?]', 13th Sunday after Trinity 1715 (c59 R); godfather 'Pal ibid' at a Berge baptism in 1724. ¶1

Probate (skifte) after him, 29 Jul 1727: 'Berge, Selbu. 29/7-1727: Povel Svendsen Berg. Enke: Ingeborg Biørnsdatter. Barn: Ole Poulsen, 6½ år gl.... Formynder for Ole Poulsen: Farbroren, Erich Svendsen Uglen.' (card 77804 c145; read by the orchestrator). This names Ole Poulsen (aged 6½, born about Jan 1721) as his only child and heir, and his brother ('farbroren' = the child's father's brother) as Erik Svendsen of Uglem. ¶4a

1 on the grade · 1 correction · 2 open questions

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Speculative father of Ole Paulsen Berge (see his page), who died at Berge in 1778 aged 62 (born about 1716): he is the only Poul/Paul at Berge in 1715-1727 and was having children there in 1715. Ole's own baptism (c.1716) has not been found in the re-read of 1716-17 (16310 c61-69), and no record names Ole's father. Not in the 1701 manntall at Berge (he would have been about 10; the Berge farmers then were Torsten Siursen 50 and Lars 56 - neither had a son Poul listed). ¶2

Corrections 1

Correction: raised from Speculative to Confirmed as father of Ole Paulsen Berge; name set to Povel (Poul) Svendsen Berge. ¶4b

Open questions and the next record to read 2

What would raise it: Ole Paulsen Berge's baptism (1716-17; 16310 c64-69 partly bled through), or a Stjør- og Verdal probate after Poul Berge in 1727 (skifteprotokoll 2a, source 24750) naming his children. ¶3

Biography gaps: his birth (about 1692) predates the 1699 start of the Selbu register, so it cannot be found in the parish books; the 1701 manntall (38461 c118) shows a 'Poule', 8, at Uglem, which fits. His marriage to Ingeborg Bjornsdatter (about 1716-20) is not identified in 16310 c56-102, so the next step is a targeted re-read of the 1717-20 marriages (c64-80). His death date is only 'before 27 April 1727' from the burial entry (16310 c108 R), and his confirmation is not recorded. ¶6
