Life story

Identified (Probable) as Hans Thorjussen/Torjussen (Tarjesen), gaardbruger and selveier (owner-farmer) of Ibrække østre, Nissedal. He is the father named in the 1862 baptism and 1878 confirmation of daughter Gunhild. ¶1

Baptism: Hans, born 16 Apr 1833 and baptized 16 May 1833 in Nissedal, son of Torjus Tellefsen (indexed as 'Telleven') and Gunnil Halvorsdatter of Nordgarden. The parentage is Probable. It rests on matching age, his brother Halvor Torjussen of Rønningen (son of Torjus Tellefsen) witnessing his 1858 marriage, and his daughter Gunhild being named after Gunnil. ¶5

The surname 'Underberg' comes from the farm Undeberg, part of the Rønningen holding where he grew up. ¶6

Married 12 Jul 1858 in Nissedal: 'Ungkarl Hans Thorjussen Rønningen, 26 aar' to 'Pige Thuri Thorkildsdatter Midbø, 28 aar'. The witnesses were Thor Gulliksen Midbø and Halvor Thorjussen Rønningen, read from the scan. ¶2

Censuses: 1865 at Ibrække øvre, east part (age 32); 1875 at Ibrække, east part (b. 1831); 1891 at Ibrække (b. 1834), with his wife only. Buried 25 Feb 1899 in Nissedal as Hans Tarjesen, b. 1833 (Probable same man). ¶3
