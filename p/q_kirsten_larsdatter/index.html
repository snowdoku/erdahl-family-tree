Life story

From Stræte, Stjørdal, at her marriage in 1787. 1801 census: 42, wife of Ole Arntsen at Geiteråsen. ¶1

The forlovere at her marriage on 25 June 1787 were Thomes Ydstie and Bersvend Nodheng. ¶2

At her daughter Marit's baptism in 1795, one of the godparents was 'Peder of Stræte' - from her own home farm, and very likely a brother. Her father was named Lars, which is why her second son was given that name. ¶3

Stræte in the 1801 census (Stjørdal from the index): the farm had two farmer households. One was Peder Erichsen, 56, farmer and shoemaker, with his wife Marit Larsdatter, 57, and sons Erich, 26, and Lars, 22. The other was Ole Pedersen, 51, with Mali Joensdatter, 49, and sons Peder, 17, and Joen, 9. Also living there were the widow Beret Gundersdatter, 70, and her son Lars Erichsen, 28, a tailor. No Lars of the right age to be Kirsten's father was there. The 'Peder of Stræte' who stood godfather to her daughter Marit in 1795 is therefore most likely Peder Erichsen, whose wife Marit Larsdatter (b. about 1744) could be Kirsten's elder sister. This is an inference, not a proved link, and it corrects the earlier guess that Peder was her brother. ¶5

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade (as mother of Maret Olsdatter): Records: (1) marriage at Værnes 4 Jun 1816 (Stjørdal ministerialbok 1815-1819, p. 195 no. 35): bride 'Mærit Olsdatter', Pige (unmarried woman), born 1795 in Stjørdal, residence Gedderaas; groom Ole Tomesen, bachelor cottager of Almoeaasen. (2) 1801 census, Stjørdal, rural residence 0007 Giedderaas: household 01 is Ole Arntsen 45, gaardmand, and Kirsten Larsdatter 42, both in their 1st marriage, with 'Deres børn' (their children) Gunnil 12, Arnt 9, Marit 6, Marit 4 and Lars 2; household 02 is the cottager Iver Iversen 61, his wife Gullov Joensdatter 69 and their daughter Anne Iversdatter 27. No other Marit Olsdatter lives at Giedderaas. (3) baptism 1 Feb 1795: Marit, daughter of Ole Arentsen and Kirsten Larsdatter of Gedderaas. Together with her marriage to Ole Arntsen on 25 Jun 1787, these primary records make her the mother of Maret Olsdatter, so she moves from Probable to Confirmed. Her own parents (father Lars, from Stræte) are still unknown. ¶4

Searches that found nothing 1

Baptism search: the indexed Stjørdal register for 1755-1763 has only one Kirsten (spelled Kirsten, Kiersten, Kjersten or Kjersti) whose father was a Lars. She was 'Kiersten', daughter of Lars Lunden, baptised at Skatval on 9 Apr 1758 (Stjørdal ministerialbok 1755-1780, p. 28; godparents read as Brant[?] Gaarden, Anna Lein[?], Gjertrud Ølstad[?], Eric Gaarden and Jens Lein[?]). Her age fits, but nothing ties her to Stræte, so she is a speculative candidate only and is not linked. ¶6
