About the family

Son Iver linked (Probable): her eldest son Iver is Iver Monsen of Botnen (see his page), on the 'Farbroder Peder Monsøn Indre Tackle' evidence of 8 Oct 1739 (see Iver's parentage note). Iver named his second daughter by his first marriage Randi (in Nr. 78, 1739), which fits naming after her. She is therefore an ancestor of this line by three routes (through Torkild Monsen of Asheim, and through Iver by both his marriages): a pedigree collapse. ¶7

Parents (Probable): Iver Lassesen (see his page, d. before 1 Jun [16]99 at Indre Oppedal) and Eli Monsdatter (see her page, d. before 30 May 1701 at Indre Oppedal). Iver's probate of 1 Jun [16]99 (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll 1a, fol. 45a-46a) names an under-age daughter 'Rendj', lot 5 'Randj'; the probate of 30 May 1701 after Eli and her second husband Lars Johansen (skifteprotokoll 1b, fol. 331b-333a) names 'Randj' among Eli's children by Iver. Lars Johansen was a son of Johannes Larsen of Indre Oppedal and so, if her husband Mons Johansen is Johannes's son Mons (Probable), his brother; she would then have grown up as the stepdaughter of Mons's brother on the same farm. ¶8a

Siblings: brothers Hindrich, Lasse and Mons Iversen; sisters Torbiør, Britha, Anna and Ingeborg (1699 and 1701 probates). Her maternal uncle Ole Monsen ([?]dal in 1699, [Leirh?]eland in 1701) was her guardian from 1701. Not created. ¶9

Son Torkild's baptism and her churching (confidence raised from Probable to Confirmed): on 21 Oct 1719 'Mons Indreopdals bar[n] N: Torckel, Mat: Randi' (mother Randi) was christened (Gulen ministerialbok 11438, canvas 41). On the 1st Sunday in Advent (3 Dec) 1719 at Brekke church 'Mons Indreopdals q[vin]de' (Mons Indre Oppedal's wife, unnamed) was churched after a boy named Torchild (canvas 42). With the 1739 records naming her as Mons Johansen's widow and mother of a 5th son Torchel, this makes her Confirmed as the mother of Torkild Monsen of Asheim (see his page). Her own parentage (Iver Lassesen and Eli Monsdatter) stays Probable. ¶11

Son Iver, link kept at Probable: Iver's baptism of 15 Aug 1704 (Gulen ministerialbok 11433, canvas 150) names only his father, Mons Johansen of Indre Oppedal, and the churching of Mons's wife on 19 Sep 1704 (canvas 152) does not name her. Her motherhood of Iver rests on the 1739 probates: Nr. 76 calls the five sons, Iver first, the children she and Mons begot together ('sammenavlede Børn'), and Nr. 77 divides her estate 'imellem hendes 5 Sønner, 1. Søn Iver'. Against Confirmed: she was unmarried in 1699 and probably 1701 and may have been born about 1689, and a 'Randi Ifversdr' of Indre Oppedal was an apparently unmarried godmother on 10 Apr 1712, so a first wife of Mons as Iver's mother is not excluded. See the 'Mother kept at Probable' and 'Godmother of 1712' notes on Iver Monsen. ¶13a

Son Iver, link raised from Probable to Confirmed: Records that moved the grade: (1) the betrothal of 3 Jun 1703 (Gulen ministerialbok 11433, canvas 112) styles Mons Johansen 'Juv.' (bachelor) and her 'Virg.' (maiden), so Mons had no earlier wife who could have borne Iver; (2) Iver, 'Mons Johans: Indreoppedahls Iver', was baptised on 15 Aug 1704 (11433, canvas 150), fourteen months after the betrothal; (3) Mons's wife was churched on 19 Sep 1704 (11433, canvas 152), so the mother of Iver was Mons's wife, and the only wife possible between Jun 1703 and Sep 1704 is the woman he was betrothed to; (4) the probate after Mons of 16 May 1739 (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll 5, Nr. 76) divides the estate between 'Enken Randi Iversdatter' and 'deris sammenavlede Børn' (the children they begot together), naming Iver first, and her own division of 7 Oct 1739 (Nr. 77) names him '1. Søn Iver'. The gap left open (a possible unrecorded first wife of Mons) is closed by record (1), and the 1739 probate and the 1704 sequence now agree independently. Residual doubt: no record of Iver's birth names his mother, and a betrothal broken off and replaced within months by another marriage would leave no trace in the canvases read; nothing suggests it. (Supersedes the 'Son Iver, link kept at Probable ' note as to the grade.) ¶17

Son Lasse linked: Lasse Monsen of Indre Oppedal (see his page), about 1718-1788, is linked as her son (Confirmed), from her 1739 division (Nr. 77, '4. Søn Lasse'), the 1739 betrothal and the 1754 'Farbroderen Lasse Monsen' guardianship. She is an ancestor of the line through Lasse, Torkild and Iver (pedigree collapse; see the notes on Lasse Monsen). ¶21

Life story

Birth, corrected (inference; bound widened): before about 1690, probably about 1680-1686. Her eldest son Iver Monsen of Botnen was born about 1705 (birth year from a burial index, approximate), and her youngest son Torkild about 1719; her husband Mons Johansen was born about 1679 (1701 census, age 22). (Supersedes the 'Birth (inference): before about 1700' note.) ¶5

Birth bounds (inference, note only; the birth field set is left unchanged): (1) 'u-myndige' (under age) in 1699 and 1701 does not bound a daughter's age closely, because under the Norwegian Law of 1687 an unmarried woman stayed under a guardian whatever her age; it shows that she was unmarried on 1 Jun 1699 and very probably still on 30 May 1701, so her marriage to Mons Johansen was probably after May 1701, which fits a first son Iver born about 1705. (2) She is the second of five daughters in both probates; if they are listed by age and her elder sister Torbiør is the 'Torberg[?] Iversen' aged 12 in the 1701 census, she would be born about 1689 or later, which is within the birth field 'BEF 1690' but in tension with the 'probably about 1680-1686' estimate of, based on Iver Monsen of Botnen's index birth year 1705 (she would have been about 16 at his birth; that index year is not backed by an age in the burial record). The census entry is doubtful (a girl in a males-only list, name marked [?]) and the index birth year is approximate, so this is a tension, not a contradiction. Her siblings' ages, or Iver Botnen's age at burial from the scan, would sharpen it. ¶10

Birth (inference): before about 1700, from her five sons, the youngest born about 1719. Her later life and death were not searched; a Randi Iversdtr of Oppedal Indre appears in the probate index for 1738-39 (pa00000000520993, not opened), probably as widow in her husband's probate. ¶2

Place in the line: mother of Torkild Monsen of Asheim, confidence Probable on the same grounds as for her husband Mons Johansen. Torkild's second daughter Randi (b. 1744) and his brother Lasse's daughter Randi both carry her name (naming inference; Torkild's eldest daughter was Sigri). ¶3

Betrothal: on Trinity Sunday, 3 Jun 1703, at Brekke church, Gulen parish, she was betrothed after the service as 'Virg: Rannei Iversdatter ibid.' (the maiden Randi Iversdatter of Indre Oppedal) to 'Juv: Mons Johansøn Indreoppedahl' (the bachelor Mons Johansen of Indre Oppedal). Sponsors: 'Anders Lassesen Indre-oppedahl, Gutorm ibid., Thor Gutormsen ibid., Ole Lassesøn Ytrebradahl'. Gulen ministerialbok 1699-1705 (Digitalarkivet 11433), canvas 112, left page, printed p. 123. The wedding date is not yet found (between 3 Jun 1703 and 19 Sep 1704; see the marriage note on Mons Johansen). ¶15

Marriage: she was married at Brekke church, Gulen parish, at the service for the feast of the Visitation of Mary, margin-dated 5 Jul 1703, as 'Virg: Rannei Iversd: ibid' (the maiden Randi Iversdatter of Indre Oppedal) to 'Juv: Mons Johanssøn Inderoppedahl' (the bachelor Mons Johansen of Indre Oppedal), a month after their betrothal of 3 Jun 1703. Gulen ministerialbok 1699-1705 (Digitalarkivet 11433), canvas 116, left page, p. 131, under the heading 'Fest: Visit: Mariæ: Brecke' at the foot of canvas 115, right page, p. 130. The feast day itself is 2 Jul; the Brekke service is dated '5/7' in the margin (see the marriage note on Mons Johansen). This supersedes the words 'The wedding date is not yet found (between 3 Jun 1703 and 19 Sep 1704...)' in the patch 036 betrothal note. ¶19

Godmother of 1712: the 'Randi Ifversdr: ibid' (Randi Iversdatter of Indre Oppedal) who stood godmother at Brekke on 10 Apr 1712 without a husband named (Gulen ministerialbok 11436, canvas 23) was probably a different Randi Iversdatter (inference). This Randi was betrothed to Mons Johansen in Jun 1703, was his wife by Sep 1704, bore his son Torkel in 1719 ('Mat: Randi') and was his widow in 1739, so she was a married woman in 1712, and a wife standing godmother is usually entered as 'N.N.s qvinde' (the wife of N.N.), although the same entry of 10 Apr 1712 names the other godmother 'Kari[?] Jonsdr' only by her own name, so the missing husband does not by itself prove that this Randi was unmarried. The 1712 godmother may have been a daughter of another Iver on the farm (for example the 'Iver Indreoppedahl' of 1704, or Iver Gutormsen, father of the child baptised that day, though she would then be godmother to her own brother). The circularity noted no longer arises: the mother link no longer depends on who the 1712 godmother was, because the 1703 betrothal shows Mons unmarried before Randi. ¶18

Probate of husband: widow (Enken) of Mons Johansen of Indre Oppedal at his probate of 16 May 1739, mother of his sons Iver, Johannes, Peder, Lasse and Torchel ('hendes... sammenavlede Børn', the children she had with him). She was alive on 16 May 1739. Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll 5, fol. 121b-122a; reading of her name. ¶1

Probate entry No. 77: on 7 Oct 1739, the day her husband Mons Johansen's estate was closed, a skifte og deling (estate division) was held at Indre Oppedal, Lavik skipreide, concerning 'Randi Iversd.' and divided between 'hendes 5 Sønner' (her five sons): 1st Iver, 2nd Johannes, 3rd [read Mons? or Peder], 4th [Lasse?], 5th Torchel, 'Monses Sønner' (sons of Mons); the appraisers were Henrich Indre Oppedahl and Gullach Ytre Oppedahl. Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll 5, fol. 123b-124a, No. 77 (Digitalarkivet 24160, view 126; index pa00000000520993). Whether she is named here as a living widow or as the deceased is not settled: read 'Enke', while reads the heading as a probate after 'Dannekone[?] Randi Iversd.', which would mean she died between 16 May and 7 Oct 1739. A clean re-read of the heading (line 3) is needed before any death date is set. ¶4

Lifetime division, settled: Nr. 77 of 7 Oct 1739 (fol. 123b-124b) is a division of her estate in her lifetime, not her probate: the closing on fol. 124b reserves 'Moderen' (the mother) her maintenance and keep. She was therefore alive on 7 Oct 1739. The third son's name, written 'Mons' in the heading, is a scribal slip for Peder: the allotment on fol. 124b is headed '3 Søn Peder'. No residence is given for any son. (Supersedes the unsettled 'Enke' vs 'Dannekone' question in the 'Probate entry No. 77' note.) ¶6

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Current grade of her links as a mother: Torkild Monsen of Asheim (see his page) is Confirmed (patch 032, audit), and Iver Monsen of Botnen (see his page) is Confirmed (patch 036, audit). Britte Monsdatter is not linked; that is a Speculative hypothesis (patch 035, audit). These supersede the Probable grades in 'Place in the line ', 'Son Iver linked (Probable)' and 'Son Iver, link kept at Probable '. Her own parent links to Iver Lassesen and Eli Monsdatter stay Probable (patch 029, audit). ¶20

The case for this identification 1

Identity: in Jun 1703 she was unmarried ('Virg.', maiden) and of Indre Oppedal ('ibid.'), which fits Randi ('Randj'), daughter of the late Iver Lassesen of Indre Oppedal, who was unmarried and under guardianship in the probates of 1699 and 1701 (the parent link set by patch 029 stays Probable). The betrothal adds the farm and her unmarried state at the right date but does not name her father, so it supports that link without proving it. Inference only: the sponsors Anders Lassesen of Indre Oppedal and Ole Lassesen may be brothers of her father Iver Lassesen (son of Lasse of Indre Oppedal, Lasse), that is, her paternal uncles (but the 1664 list names only Iver, 13, as Lasse's son, so they would have to be sons born after 1664, or sons of another Lasse); the 'wife of Anders Larsen' of Indre Oppedal who was godmother to her son Iver on 15 Aug 1704 may be the wife of the same Anders (Lasse and Lars are often interchanged). Neither is proved. ¶16

Alternatives considered and set aside 3

Not Confirmed: no record read names Mons Johansen's wife as Iver Lassesen's daughter, and their marriage record has not been found. See Iver Lassesen's notes for the full reasoning and what would confirm it. ¶8b

Possible brother (inference): the 'Mons Ifvers: Indreopdal' who was betrothed in 1717 and married Giertlaug Lassesdatter of Ytre Brekke at Brekke on 2 Jan 1718 is probably her brother Mons Iversen, named among Iver Lassesen's under-age children in the probate of 1 Jun [16]99 (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll 1a). The 'Anne Ifversdr Indre Opdall' betrothed at Brekke on 12 May 1719 to Ole Pedersen Indre Tackle (Gulen ministerialbok 11438, canvas 35) may be her sister Anna of the same probate. Dorthe Iffversdr (Iversdatter) of Indre Oppedal, a godmother at Torkild's baptism, is not among the children named in 1699 (inference only for all three). ¶12

Possible daughter, not linked (audited; Speculative hypothesis): Britte Monsdatter (see her page), wife of Torkel Iversen of Indre Oppedal, may be her daughter, but her own lifetime division of 7 Oct 1739 (Nr. 77) is 'imellem hendes 5 Sønner' and names no daughter. See Britte's 'Parentage hypothesis' notes. ¶14
