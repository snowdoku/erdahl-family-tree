About the family

Husband of Winnifred Eunice Hayter. In 1944 he was a railway section foreman of Penticton, aged 36, a Baptist, still a Swedish citizen, son of Oscar Oland and Emma Okvest. His death registration (BC 1997-09-008770) gives his birth as 19 September 1908 at Alsbyn, Sweden, his father's name as Oscar Ohlund, and his work as a railroad foreman. He died at Penticton on 18 April 1997, aged 88, still married to Winnifred, who reported the death. He was cremated. ¶1
