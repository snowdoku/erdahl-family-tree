Life story

The baptism scan (Kvernes, 6 Sep 1812, p. 400) reads 'Jacob Estens. Hosets og Hu[stru]', son Esten, born 'Aug: 28.'. The mother is not named and there is no godparent column, so the entry cannot tie him to Marith Gorrisdatter. ¶4

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the grade · 1 on the identification

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Probable eldest child and only known son of Jacob Estensen, and therefore Tørri Jakobsdatter's elder brother. He was born 28 Aug 1812 and baptized 6 Sep 1812 in Kvernes, the son of a Jacob Estensen then living at Hoset. His mother is not named in the index. ¶1

The case for this identification 1

The identification is Probable rather than Confirmed because the baptism names the father's farm as Hoset, not Kongshaug, and because the mother is not recorded, so he cannot be tied to Marith Gorrisdatter. Jacob Estensen appears at three different places in the register between 1812 and 1836 (Hoset, Kongshaug and Dale), which is normal for a landless man but makes each identification rest on the name alone. ¶3
