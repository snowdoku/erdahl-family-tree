Life story

NEW, father of Targie Asgiersen Frontved (see his page); graded on that link: Probable. Panteregister I 1 fol. 299a (DA 10915 view 342, read on the scan) registers the skifte after 'Asgier Tarjesen', begun 26 Oct 1781 and closed 19 Feb [1782?], in which shares of Frontved were laid out to Kittel Kristoffersen [Flaa?], Tarje Asgiersen, Hans Asgiersen and a [..]giersen of Lund. The panteregister summary gives no relationship words; that Tarje and Hans Asgiersen were his sons is inferred from patronymic, farm and heirship, so Probable until the protocol entry (skifteprotokoll 13, DA 25677) is read. Earlier: an 'Asgier Targjesen' holds a mortgage in Frontved in 1742 (fol. 41a, DA 10915 view 83), and a court forretning of 19 Dec 1777 was held against Asgier Tariesen's property (fol. 299a). Whether the 1742 man is the same Asgier (or his father) is not settled. ¶1

The 1765 skifte after his wife Kirsten Joensdatter (DA 25674 No. 695, 11 Nov 1765, øvre Frontvedt, view 575) names him as 'Enkemand Asger Targeisøn' and their children Targei, Joen, Lars (24), Hans (19), Knud and Joen the younger: the same six sons as at his own 1781 skifte, which bridges the two records on the same farm. ¶4

Family from skifte No. 448 (1781): his children were Tarje (eldest named, long of age), Joen (of age, but under his brother Tarje's guardianship), Lars, Hans, Knud and Jon the younger, all of age. His first wife was Kirsten Jonsdatter, whose skifte was concluded on 11 Nov 1765 (to be read in skifteprotokoll 10, DA 25674, 1759-68); he was a widower again at his death, so a later wife also died before him (not named on this page). He owned Store Mule (Matr. No. 9, Nissedal) as well as part of øvre Frontved. Test record for Kirsten as Tarje's mother: the 1765 skifte after Kirsten Jonsdatter, Frontved, in DA 25674, which will list her children. ¶3

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed, graded on his link to his son Targie (Tarje) Asgiersen Frontved (see his page). Record: skifteprotokoll 13 (DA 25677) No. 448 (view 229, right page, fol. 225a): 'Anno 1781 den 26de October' the lensmann for Nissedal tinglag, Kittil Tiøstolsen Fone, with the witnesses Ole Olsen Tvedt and Søren Torvildsen Fone, came 'paa Gaarden øvre Frontvedt, Matr. No. 19 i Nissedals Annex til Hvidesøe Præstegield' to register and value what the 'den 25de Martii næst forhen afdøde Enkemand Asgier Tarjesen' left, for division 'imellem hans efterlevende Børn som ere Tarje Asgiersen forlængst myndig, Joen Asgiersen ligeledes myndig' (for whom 'hans Broder forbemeldte Tarje Asgiersen' was named guardian), 'Lars Asgiersen ligeledes myndig, Hans Asgiersen og Tarje Asgiersen' [sic, repeated], 'Knud Asgiersen ligeledes myndig og Jon Asgiersen den yngre ligeledes fuldmyndig'. Tarje Asgiersen then declared that he had bought 4 1/2 'Skind' (part) of Frontved from his father Asgier Tarjesen 'ved Skifte efter hans first nævnte forhen afdøde Hustrue Kirsten Jonsdatter afhandlet 11te Novbr 1765', and that his late father also owned the outlying farm Store Mule, Matr. No. 9, in Nissedal annex. The protocol names Tarje Asgiersen among 'hans efterlevende Børn', and in his own declaration calls Asgier 'hans Fader'. Continuity: the son is the same Tarje Asgiersen of Frontved who was cautioner in 1778-80 and received Frontved land in this very skifte (panteregister fol. 299a); the farm and the year are the same, and no other Tarje Asgiersen is named. The panteregister's lack of relationship words (the reason for Probable) is now resolved. He died a widower on 25 Mar 1781. ¶2
