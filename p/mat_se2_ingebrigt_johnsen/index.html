About the family

Probable parentage from the 1801 census: Selbu, rural residence 0050 STOREVJEN, household 06, person 026 - 'Ingebrigt Jonsen', aged 2, child of Jon Ingebrigtssen, 37, 'Husmand med jord' (cottager with land), and Daardi Olsdatter, 39, with elder siblings Siri Jonsdatter, 11, and Ole Jonssen, 7. In the WHOLE 1801 census of Selbu there are exactly two males christened Ingebrigt born 1795-1803 - this one and an Ingebrigt Olssen at Belle - and only this one is a Jonsen. His calculated birth year of 1799 is exactly the year our Ingebrigt Johns gave at his wedding in June 1824, where he stated his age as 25. ¶5

Store Evjen is the census's next residence after Marstad (0049 and 0050), the literal neighbouring farm, so a Store Evjen cottager's son taking up at Marstad is the shortest move in the parish. The naming pattern supports it across four generations: Jon Ingebrigtssen's father was an Ingebrigt; his son is Ingebrigt; and Ingebrigt's grandsons are Ingebrigt (1863) and John (1876). ¶6

Children: seven baptisms and one stillbirth, all in Selbu - John (23 Mar 1825), Paul (31 Jul 1826), Dortie/Daardi (bapt. 9 Jan 1831), Marit (bapt. 31 Mar 1833), Siri (b. 14 Oct 1835), and the twins Kjersten and Anne (bapt. 4 Aug 1839), plus a stillborn child buried 1842. The family was at ALSAET for the first two and at MARSTAD from 1831, every later child carrying Marstad as a byname. That reconciles the 1921 Selbygbogen's statement that Paul was born 'at the farm Marstad' with the 1826 register's Alsaet: he was born at his mother's farm and brought up at his father's from the age of four. ¶8

Life story

Age: born about 1799 on his own stated age of 25 at marriage in June 1824. His exact baptism has not yet been found - see the findings dossier for the search and the next record. ¶4

The naming of those children names all four grandparents in the order Selbu custom prescribed - John for the father's father, Paul for the mother's father, Daardi for the father's mother, Marit for the mother's mother - and every one of the four is a person identifiable in the 1801 census of Selbu. See the findings dossier, section 11. ¶9

Baptism: born 11 February 1799 and baptised 17 February 1799 (the Second Sunday in Lent), Selbu ministerialbok 1757-1801, 1799, page numbered 232, baptism no. 2: 'et Drengebarn, kaldet Ingebrict, foed d: 11te Februar. Forældre: Jon Ingebrictsen Stor Efjen og Dordi Olsdatter' - a boy named Ingebrigt; parents Jon Ingebrigtsen of Store Evjen and Dordi (Daardi) Olsdatter. Faddere (godparents): Sivert Rejersen (Reiersen) Berge, Thomes Einersen Lien, Jon Jonsen Mebust, Guru Povelsdatter Efjen and Anne Sivertsdatter Berge. Read and re-read from the scan crop on 13 Oct 2026; the reading of child, date, parents and farm is clear. The register is scanned but not transcribed on Digitalarkivet. This supersedes the earlier note that his baptism had not been found. ¶11

Marriage, read from the scans: The minister's book (ministerialbok 1816-1831, pp. 416-417 no. 25) dates the wedding 22 June 1824, in church: groom 'Bonde-Søn og Artillerist og Ungkarl Ingebrigt Jonsen Marstad' - a farmer's son, an artilleryman (soldier in the artillery) and a bachelor - age left blank; bride 'Gaardmd. Datt. Pigen Anne Poulsd. Aldsædt' - a farmer's daughter, unmarried, of Alsaet - age blank; forlovere (sureties for the marriage) 'Gmd. Ole Einersen Marstad og' with the second name never written. The form has no column for the fathers. The parish clerk's copy (klokkerbok 1816-1824, p. 406 no. 24) dates it 21 June 1824 and adds ages: 'Ingebrigt Johns. Mar[stad], Ungkarl og S[oldat?], 25 Aar' and 'P[igen] Anne Paulsd. Alsæth, 23 Aar' (the 3 is not certain), with forlovere 'Ole Einers. Mar[stad]' and a second name read only as 'Joh[n?] Olsen Sye[?]'. Read from the scans and the crops. ¶13

Marriage: married at Selbu church 22 June 1824 (minister's copy) or 21 June 1824 (parish clerk's copy) Anne Paulsdatter of Alsaet. He is entered as 'Ingebrigt Jonssoen, residence Margstad' in the ministerialbok 1816-1831, marriages pages 416-417 serial no. 25, and as 'Ingebrigt Johns, b. 1799 calculated, age 25, residence Magth' in the klokkerbok 1816-1824, marriages pages 406 and 463 serial no. 24. Margstad and Magth are both the clerk's rendering of MARSTAD. Neither index carries the fathers' names: the Selbu marriage transcriptions only begin to index brudgommens far and bruras far with the 1843-1859 volume, so his own father - a John, on the patronymic - must be read off the scan. ¶2

Marriage, what the scans add: (1) his farm in 1824 is written MARSTAD in full in the minister's book (the index's 'Margstad' and 'Magth' are misreadings), and the forlover Ole Einersen was of the same farm, so he was living at Marstad before his marriage - the farm where his son Paul was confirmed in 1841; (2) he was an artilleryman as well as a farmer's son, his military service presumably not yet complete; (3) no father is named in either copy, so the link to Jon Ingebrigtsen of Store Evjen rests on his baptism and the 1801 census, not on the marriage. His age of 25 agrees with his baptism of February 1799. ¶14

Paul Ingebrigtsen's father, named in two independent records: as 'Ingebrigt Johns[en]', father, in Paul's baptismal entry of 13 August 1826, and as 'Ingebrigt Johnsen', brudgommens far (the groom's father), in Paul's marriage entry of 31 May 1858. ¶1

He was of Marstad and his bride of Alsaet, and the couple were living at Alsaet when Paul was born there in 1826: the same marry-into-the-wife's-farm pattern that his son Paul would repeat at Eidem thirty-four years later. Paul was back at Marstad, his father's farm, when he married in 1858. ¶3

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Parents raised from Probable to Confirmed, and why. The baptism names Jon Ingebrigtsen of Store Evjen and Dordi Olsdatter, exactly the couple of the 1801 census, Storevjen household 06, who have a son Ingebrigt aged 2. Born 11 February 1799 agrees with his age of 25 at his marriage in June 1824 and the clerk's 'b. 1799 calculated'. No other Ingebrigt Jonsen was baptised in Selbu in 1798 or January-February 1799 (all entries), and the 1801 census has no other Ingebrigt Jonsen of his age in the parish. His children's names repeat Jon (John, 1825) and Dordi (Dortie/Daardi, 1831). The step from the 1799 child to the 1824 groom of Marstad still rests on name, patronymic, exact age and naming pattern rather than a record that states it, but there is no competing candidate. ¶12

Records that disagree 1

Against: the 1801 census has NO Jon and no Ingebrigt at Marstad at all (eight people, headed by Anders Pederssen, 53, with Thomas Jonssen and Ole Jonssen), so Ingebrigt Johnsen was not born at Marstad - he came to it, as his son Paul later came to Eidem. The link is rated Probable, not Confirmed; the record that would close it is his own baptism of about 1798-99 in the Selbu parish register 1757-1801, which Digitalarkivet has scanned but not transcribed. ¶7

Searches that found nothing 1

Death: alive in 1842, when he and his wife are named as next of kin at the burial of their stillborn child, and absent from the 1865 census, so he died between those dates. His burial is not in the Digitalarkivet index: a search of Selbu burials 1830-1885 for a male Ingebr- Johns- returns thirteen entries, all children or a man born 1788 at Oeraas who is eleven years out. He is presumably indexed under a form the search does not reach, such as Ingebrigt Jonsen Marstad. ¶10
