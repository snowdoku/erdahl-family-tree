About the family

NEW ANCESTOR, graded on her link to her son Ole Ormsen (see his page). Åmli ætt og heim V, Øvre Eppeland (canvas 209): Olav Ormson's son 'Orm f. (1630) g.m. Kirsti [?] Nedre Eppeland'; canvas 210: 'Orm Eppeland f. (1630) d. (1686) g.m. [?] Nedre Eppeland f. (1636?) d. 1712?', children Aslak and [Olav] f. (1667?) g.m. Anne [?] Rostveit. The book notes that a woman aged 76 died at Åmli in 1712, but as her name is not given it is not certain she is this Kristi; in 1713 Olav's mother is said to be dead. Patronymic unknown.
