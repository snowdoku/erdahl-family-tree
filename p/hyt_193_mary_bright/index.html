Life story

Bright family of Roxwell, new references, grade unchanged (Speculative): (1) ERO D/DQ 41/337 contains the 'will of William Bright of Stonehill of Roxwell, proved 1592' (bundled with a 1644 agreement of Henry Bright of Stonehill, yeoman); this William is very probably the William Bright buried at Roxwell 7 Apr. 1591, and his will is the record most likely to name a daughter 'Mary Joslin' or her children. No online image; catalogue entry only. (2) ERO D/DQ 41/295: quitclaim, 13 June 1586, John Cutler of London to William Bright of Roxwell, yeoman, of a messuage and the close Morrells in Roxwell. (3) TNA C 3/7/17 (1558-1579), Bright v Bright: Henry Bright against William Bright and Robert Perker and Margaret his wife, over property in Roxwell; this fits the fine of 1555/1572 in which John Bright conveyed the reversion of 28 acres in Roxwell and Writtle 'which Robt. Parker & w. Margt. hold for her life' (Feet of Fines for Essex vol. 5), Margaret apparently a Bright widow with a life interest. None of these names Mary. William Bright is a candidate father only and is not entered. ¶5a

How sure are we?

Speculative. A plausible candidate only. Treat this link as a question, not a fact.

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Graded on her link to her son Thomas Joslin. Married Ralph Josselyn at Roxwell, Essex, 21 May 1583 ('Ralfe Joslin and Marie Bright', Roxwell register as extracted by French, NEHGR 71:33). Ralph's children were baptised or born from 1584 (John, about 1584; Marie bapt. 4 Sept. 1586; Agnes 25 Aug. 1588; Ralph 8 July 1590) to Elizabeth (bapt. 23 Sept. 1604); Thomas was born about late 1591. Ralph's second wife Dorothy survived him. French says 'lack of evidence makes it impossible to determine which wife was the mother of each child'. Zubrinsky (2026) calls Mary the 'probable' mother of Thomas and all his siblings, on the regular two-year spacing, and dates her as living after 1603 (source not seen). The 1646 Chancery answer of Simon Josselyn speaks of 'their mother Dorothy' for himself (born about 1594) and his brother Ralph (bapt. 1590), which, if not a stepmother, would put Dorothy as mother even of children born before Thomas. Speculative: a plausible candidate; no record names her as Thomas's mother. ¶1a

The case for this identification 1

The Dorothy question, new points, grade unchanged: (1) the 'Dorothie Josselin' buried at Roxwell 17 Jan. 1606/7 was Ralph's DAUGHTER, not his wife: the 1646 Chancery bill says Thomas of Rochford's legacy to Joseph (bapt. 1600) grew 'by the death of his sister Dorothy' (NEHGR 71:230). So Ralph named a daughter Dorothy before 1605, which does not tell us whom he had married by then. (2) French's Roxwell extracts have no Josselyn baptism at all between Ralph (8 July 1590) and Joseph (6 June 1600), though Thomas (about late 1591), Simon (about 1594), Richard (about 1598), Anne and Dorothy were born in those years, and no Josselyn marriage or burial for those years either. Either the register is defective for the 1590s or the family's events then were recorded elsewhere; Mary's burial and Ralph's marriage to Dorothy (not at Roxwell) most likely fall in that gap, so neither can be dated from the printed extracts. (3) A possible Bright family: in 1602 Ralph's brother John of Mountnessing had £22 'in the hands of Thomas Bright of Rettingdon' (NEHGR 71:24), and Ralph's son Symon later held lands in Rettendon (will 1649). Thomas Bright of Rettendon is a candidate kinsman of Mary, nothing more. ¶3a

Searches that found nothing 2

Register search (FamilySearch Essex index, signed in): no burial of a Mary/Marie Josselyn (any spelling) is indexed in Essex 1586-1600 other than 'Marie Joselin' 4 Sep. 1586 at Roxwell, which is the date French gives for the BAPTISM of Ralph's daughter Marie and is very probably the index's mislabelled baptism. No marriage of Ralph Josselyn to a Dorothy is indexed in Essex 1584-1600. Ralph's children Symon (1 Apr. 1594) and Dorathie (2 Apr. 1598) were baptised at Good Easter (index; father only). A daughter named Dorathie in 1598 fits Dorothy being Ralph's wife by then, and Simon's 1646 answer makes Dorothy his own mother, so the second marriage is probably before April 1594 (inference). Mary is still not excluded as Thomas's mother (born about April 1591 to April 1592 by his age 43 in April 1635). The Bright family was then at Roxwell (William Bright buried 7 Apr. 1591; Elizabeth Bright bapt. 26 Jul. 1590; Dorothie Bright 20 Oct. 1591; Henrie Bright 28 Oct. 1592; index only). Stays Speculative. ¶2a

Essex Record Office search: no will survives for a Bright of Roxwell before 1610. Roxwell lay in the Peculiar of Writtle, whose wills survive only from 1618; the first Roxwell Bright will is that of Henry Bright senior, yeoman, 1639 (D/APwW 1/18). Brights were at Roxwell from the 16th century: deed D/DSx/411 names 'Hy. Bright (s. of William B., decd.)', yeoman of Roxwell. The Rettendon will is that of Arthur Bright, husbandman, 1594 (D/AEW 10/285), not yet read. William Bright of Roxwell (deceased before Henry's conveyance) is a candidate father for Mary, nothing more. ¶4a
