About the family

Father of Catharina Schopf (see her page), wife of Nicolaus Lindenmaier. ¶1a

Life story

Office and family (index): 'Herr Schultheis Jacob' Schopf, Schultheiß (village mayor) of Metterzimmern, with Maria Catharina had Jacobina (5 Jul 1715, record 5509222) and Anna Christina (8 May 1722, record 5509394); 'Anna Catharina' in 1718 is taken as the same wife (variant reading). A Jacob Schopf buried Metterzimmern 14 Oct 1732 (record 5504925, no relatives) may be he; a younger Jacob Schopf (wife Anna Maria 1732-33, then Catharina Hörnle m. 9 Nov 1734) is a different man. Possibly the Jacob baptised there 9 Aug 1664 to Simon Schopf and Christina (record 5508415) and the Jacob x Susanna with children 1692-1703 (not proved; not entered). ¶2

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Possible parents (Speculative): Jacob Schopf, baptised at Metterzimmern on 9 Aug 1664 to 'Simon, Christina' (record 5508415). Naming pattern: the Schultheiß and Maria Catharina named a son Simon (9 Mar 1717, 5509255) and a daughter Anna Christina (8 May 1722, 5509394). He would have been 50-58 at the births of 1715-1722 and 68 at the 1732 burial (5504925). Rival kept open: a Jacob Schopf x Susanna baptised at Metterzimmern in 1696 and 1703 (5508899, 5509004); either he is the 1664 man in a first marriage, or the Schultheiß is a younger man (e.g. his son) who named children after his grandparents. Hence Speculative. ¶4a

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Test record: the Metterzimmern marriage of Jacob x (Anna) Maria Catharina (c.1704-14, film 1184926) and the 1732 burial image (age). ¶4b
