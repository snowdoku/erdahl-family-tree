About the family

NEW ANCESTOR, graded on her link to her daughter Todne Svendsdatter (see her page). Named as the widow's mother, 'Asbor Thallaksdtr. + sk. 1707', in the archivist's note on Askland card 79 (Skp. 8 fol. 67, 1729). Todne named her daughter Asbor after her. Archivist's note only, so Probable. ¶1a

Parents from the bygdebok (Åmli ætt og heim II, Jørundland L.nr. 36, canvas 78; Askland L.nr. 50, canvas 143): 'Asborg Tallaksdtr. Jørundland', daughter of Tallak Olsen of Jørundland (b. 1631, skifte 1701), whose second wife was Anne Eivindsdtr.; her sister Gunvor married Knut Jørgenson Mjåland. Father Probable, mother Speculative (see their notes). Her own grade is unchanged. ¶5

Her parents are now proved by her father's probate. Østre Råbyggelag Skifteprotokoll nr. 1 (DA 24349, view 278), fol. 274a, 22 Nov 1701: 'bleve paa Jørunland Registerit og werderit hvis ringe middel som fandtis efter salige Thallack Oelsen... imellem Creditorerne, saa som hans Efterlatte Hustru, Anna Evindsdaatter, og deris sammen avlede Børn, Nemlig Asbør og Gundvor Thalacksdøtter som nu er giffte, efter Svend Ormsen Askland[s] beretning (som har dend Eldste Daatter til Ehte)'. Card 39942/5977 indexes it as 'Fol. 274... Thallack Oelsen + / Anne Evindsdatter, Barn Asbør, Gundvor'. (The probate on fol. 274b-276a of the same day is a different one, after Gunild Osmundsdatter, widow of Olle Thallacksen and Halvor Ormsen, card 5979.) She is the eldest daughter, married to Svend Ormsen Askland. Her sister was Gundvor Tallaksdatter (married by 1701; the bygdebok says to Knut Jørgensen Mjåland). ¶6

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: graded on her link to her daughter Todne Svendsdatter. She is the widow 'Asbør Thallaksdatter' in the 13 Oct 1705 Askeland skifte after Svend Ormsøn, whose children are Salve, Anløv, Thoene and Birgitha (Skifteprotokoll nr. 2 fol. 147a, DA 24350 view 147, read on the scan), and her own skifte followed on 28 Mar 1707 (Skp. 2 fol. 196b; card 39942/35, children Salve, Thoene, Birgithe, Anløv). Continuity to Todne Svendsdatter, wife of Evind Jørgensen of Askland, through her brother Salve Svendsøn named in the 1729 skifte (Skp. 8 fol. 67, cards 77-79). ¶2

The case for this identification 1

Re-read at full resolution: the headings of the 1707 skifte after her (Skp. 2 fol. 196b, DA 24350 view 197) and of the 1705 skifte after her husband (fol. 147a-b, views 147-148) name the children Salve, Anløv, Thoene and Birgitha Svendsdøtter and, in 1705, the nominated guardians Lau[ritz] and Halvor Ormssøn (excused), Svend Torbiørnsøn Askeland and Knud...søn Omland. No 'farbror', 'morbror' or Tallaksen appears in either heading or guardian paragraph. Her father remains unknown. ¶4

Searches that found nothing 1

Search for her father: the 1705 and 1707 skifter (Skp. 2 fol. 147a and 196b) were re-read for kin. The headings name the deceased, the widow/children and the court's men; Svend Torbiørnsøn (a neighbour on Nordre Askland, aged 10 in 1666) attends in 1705, and in 1707 Lauritz Ormsøn acts as guardian for the children. No Tallaksen (morbror) is named in either heading as read. The 1666 census has no Tallak at Askland; Tallaks of the right age are at O-enland (Tollak 60) and Jørundland (Tallak 35), neither tied to her by any record. ¶3
