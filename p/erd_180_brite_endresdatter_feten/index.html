About the family

Widow of Anfind Andersen at Feeten, named at his probate on 16 Aug 1768 (Ytre Sogn 8b p. 528, No. 36) as 'Enken Britte E[ndre?]s datter' - the patronymic looks like 'Eldres' on the scan and is most probably Endresdatter. Her guardian (lagverge) was Ole [Endresen?] Torsnes, possibly her brother; the 1701 manntall has an Endre Olsen, b. 1700, son of Olle Olsen at Torsnes (a son of Ole Torsnes), which would fit a father Endre Olsen Torsnes, but that is unproved. Probable as mother of Christi Anfindsdatter on the same evidence as her husband. ¶1

Life story

Patronymic re-read at full resolution: the 1768 Feten probate writes 'Britte E[n/l]dres datter' for her and 'Ole E[n/i]dresen Toersnes' for her lagverge in the same hand and letter shapes, so the two share the patronymic Endresen/-datter; her guardian was very probably her brother, though the record does not say so. Ole Endresen of Torsnes was a godfather at Christmas 1761 (11566 c63 L, 'Ole Endresøn Thorsnes'). ¶2

Ole Endresen Torsnes's probate, 18 Aug 1790 (Ytre Sogn 11a p. 334, No. 99): widow [Ma?]rie Ellingsdatter; children Ole Olsen 12, Elling Olsen 9, Anna Olsdatter 18 and Maria Olsdatter 15. No siblings or parents named, so it neither proves nor disproves the sibling link. ¶3

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the identification · 1 correction · 1 alternative set aside

The case for this identification 1

Supporting/caution: the bride of 1790 was 'Christie Anfinds Datter Tvedt' (Leikanger 11567), no parent named; she was not a daughter of Anfind Monsen Tveiten (his probate, Ytre Sogn 9b p. 1255, lists no Christi), so the Feten identification survives, but her daughter Kari (b. about 1803) could equally be named for Kari Anfindsdatter of Tveiten. Graded on her link to Christi Anfindsdatter: stays Probable. ¶5

Corrections 1

Correction: the Christi Anfindsdatter of Feten belongs to another family, the household of Marcus Marcussen. (1) Leikanger ministerialbok 11568, canvas 93 right (fol. 91), Tjugum sogn, about 19-22 June 1797: 'Copuleret Unge-Karl Marcus Marcussen Thue med Pigen Christi Anfinds Dr Fethen'. (2) On 24 Sep 1797 at Tjugum (11568, canvas 98 left) two girls were baptised one after the other. The first was Anna, 'Forældrene: Giest Gjestsen Esse og Christi Anfinds D.', with godparents Sieur Kieldsen Meneß, Ellend Andersen Esse, Mari Olsdatter ibid, Britha Kieldsdatter Toftedahl and Anna Marcusdatter Esse: no one from Feten or Ulvestad. The second was Giøri, 'Forældrene: Marcus Marcussen Moland og Christi Anfinds D.', with godparents Ole Moland, Endre Thorsen Fethen, Britha Endresdatter ibid [Feten], Anna Johannesdatter Ulvestad and Guri Hansdatter Moland. Britha Endresdatter of Feten is the name of the widow in the 1768 Feten probate, and Ulvestad was Anfind Andersen's home farm. The name Giøri repeats the Feten probate's youngest daughter '[Giøre?] Anfindsdatter 2'. (3) In the 1801 census Marcus Marcussen's wife Christi Anfindsdatter is 38 (Esse, household 07). That is the same age as the Feten child (6 in Aug 1768, 16 in Nov 1777). She died before 30 Apr 1813, when her probate was held (Ytre Sogn 14 fol. 538a; daughters Giøri 15 3/4 and Sigrid 9 3/4). Gjest Gjestsen's wife, by contrast, was 'Christie Anfinds Datter Tvedt' when she married in 1790, and she was still alive in 1815. The only Christi Anfindsdatter in the 1768 Feten probate and the 1777 Feten confirmation is therefore, on present evidence, the Christi who married Marcus Marcussen, not Gjest's wife. She is therefore no longer linked as the mother of Christi Anfindsdatter. Her daughter is recorded as Christi Anfindsdatter. 'Britha Endres D.' of Feten, a godmother in 1797, is very probably she herself, still at Feten and so alive in Sep 1797. Endre Thorsen of Feten, the godfather beside her, may be a son of a second marriage named for her father Endre; that is unproved. ¶6

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Ruled out: the probate indexed under Brite Endresdatter, Lunde (Tjugum), 23 Mar 1799 (Ytre Sogn 11b p. 755b-756a): its heirs are sisters (Ingeleu Endresdatter m. Anders Hansen Brelien; Gjertrud Endresdatter 49 m. Lasse Nielsen Eitthun; Synneve Endresdatter 42 m. Ole Pedersen Fedje; Kari Endresdatter m. Anders Ingebrigtsen Fedje) and nieces Gjertrud 12, Ragnilde 10 and Agnes 6 Hansdatter, with no children. This Brite cannot be the Feten widow, whose daughter Christi was alive in 1815. ¶4
