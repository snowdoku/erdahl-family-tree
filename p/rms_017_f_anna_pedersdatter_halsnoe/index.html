About the family

Mother of Niels Thorsen Buchen (Probable, not proved): the 1777 baptism names no mother; the link rests on the marriage of Thore Thorsen of Buchen (the only Tore/Thor of Buchen to marry 1763-1777) and on three Halsnøe godparents (Peder, Reier, Marthe) at Niels's baptism, likely her kin (a Peder Halsnøe could be her father or brother). ¶2

Parents: Peder Nielsen of Halsnøy (d. before 19 Mar 1787) and Sophie Biørnsdatter, both Confirmed as her parents. Their probate (Ryfylke skifteprotokoll D 7b, DA 23892, fol. 507a, Halsnøe, 19 Mar 1787) lists among the children 'Anna Peders Datter gift med Thore Thorsen Buchen', dead, leaving 3 children: Peder Thorsen 13, Niels Thorsen 9 and Siri Thorsdatter 17, with their father Thore Thorsen as guardian. Her siblings: Biørn, Niels and Peder Pedersen; Marthe (m. Jon Rasmussen Hageberg); Britha (m. Aage Ogesen, Halsnøe). ¶12

Grandparents found: her mother Sophie Biørnsdatter was the daughter of Biørn Reyersen and Kari Jonsdatter of Halsnøy (probates 1731 and 1751), so Anna's eldest brother Biørn Pedersen carried his maternal grandfather's name. ¶16

Life story

Confirmation (index): Hjelmeland ministerialbok 1760-1799 (source 1599), confirmations 1766, p. 173, no. 22, Fister church, 16 Nov 1766: 'Anna Pedersdatter', of Halsnøe, aged 17 (born about 1749). She is the only Anna Pedersdatter of Halsnøe confirmed 1760-97, so very probably the bride of Thore Thorsen Buchen in 1768 (Probable identification, by name and farm). The list gives names, farms and ages only, no parents (checked on the scan of pp. 176-177, canvas 95). ¶8

1762 ekstraskatt (index): 'Ane Pedersd', Datter, on Halsnøe, in the household of Peder Nielsen (Opsidder) and Sophie Biørndatter (Hustr). Over 12 in 1762, which fits a birth about 1749. Her grade on her link to her son Nils Thorsen is unchanged (Probable; see the note). ¶15

Marriage (scan read): 'Anna Peders Datter Halsnøe' married Thore Thorsen Buchen in Fister church on 26 Jun 1768 (Hjelmeland marriages 1768, p. 233, no. 10). Her farm is Halsnøy (Halsnøe) in Fister/Hjelmeland. ¶1

At the 1 Jan 1770 baptism of Siri Thors Datter (probably of Buchen) a godparent was Niels Halsnøe, a further Halsnøe godparent for a probable child of this couple. ¶5

Godmother as 'Anna Buchen' on 7 Apr 1771 with Thore Buchen (Peder Thorsen, baptisms no.28). ¶7

Possible later marriage (unverified lead): Hjelmeland marriages 1777, p. 242, no. 7, 'Tosten Børgesen Buchen og Anna Peders Datter' (her farm not read). Not adopted. ¶3

1777 marriage of 'Tosten Børgesen Buchen og Anna Peders Datter' (Hjelmeland, 15 Jun 1777, no.7) read in full: the bride has no farm and no widow note, so it is not evidence that she is the widow of Thore Thorsen Buchen. Unresolved; a common name. ¶4

Her own probate (D 7b fol. 457a, scan read; card DA 39936 view 948): 'Aar 1786 den 10de Augusty' registration and valuation on the farm Lille Buchen under Siernerøe tinglag after the late woman 'Anna Pedersdatter', between her surviving husband 'Tore Toresen' and their 3 children born in the marriage: 1. the eldest son Peder Toresen, 12; 2. the younger son 'Neels Toresen', 8; 3. the daughter Siri Toresdatter, 16. Card: net estate 340 rd 1 ort 1 sk. This settles that she died in 1786, so the Hjelmeland burial of 26 Jul 1786 ('Ane Peders D: Buchen', aged 36) is hers, and she is NOT the Anna Pedersdatter who married Tosten Børgesen Buchen in 1777 (she was then still Thore's wife), nor the Anna Pedersdatter buried in 1824. ¶13

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

2 on the grade · 2 alternatives set aside · 1 search with no result · 3 open questions

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Grade kept Probable on her link to her son Nils Thorsen, but the link is now much stronger. The 1786 and 1787 probates name her as the mother of Niels Thorsen of Lille Buchen, aged 8 in Aug 1786 and 9 in Mar 1787, which fits exactly the baptism 'Niels Torsen Buchen' of 16 Nov 1777 and the 1796 confirmand 'Niels Thorsen Buchen'. What holds the grade at Probable is the tree's identification of that Buchen Niels with the Madla cottager Niels Thorsen (Peter S. Nelson's father). That identification rests on the 1816 Fister marriage of a bachelor Niels Thors. of Buchen to Malene Nielsdatter of Øye, which is twenty years after the 1796 confirmation with no record in between, and on the move to Madla, which is graded Probable in his own notes. Naming supports it: the Madla couple's children include Anna (1816), Thore (1821) and Peder (1831, 1833), the names of Niels's mother, father and brother. ¶14

Grade kept Probable, graded on her link to Nils Thorsen, but the Madla half of the gap is now closed: the 1824-25 probate after Niels Olsen and Mallene Pedersdatter of Øye (Ryfylke D 19, fol. 293b) names their daughter 'Mallene Nielsdatter, gift med Niels Thorsøn Bukken', living under Sunde in Jæren og Dalane, so the Madla husband is the Niels Thorsen of Bukken who married in 1816. The remaining step is the Bukken farm alone: Niels Torsen, 15, son of Thore, Lille Buchen (probate 2 Aug 1791) and confirmed at Buchen in 1796, to Niels Thorsen, bachelor at Buchen, in 1816. No rival Niels Thorsen of Bukken is named in any record read, but 1796-1816 is 20 years with no record naming him, so under the audit-3 rule it stays Probable. Checked without result: Siri Thorsdatter's husband's probate (Lars Andersen, Bukken, D 19 fol. 221b, 6 Apr 1824: widow Siri Thorsdatter Bukken, children Sevend Larsen of age and Anna Larsdatter 20; no brother named) and the 'Peder Thorsen Skorpe' entry in the D 17a/b register (fol. 576b, not read; Skorpe, not Bukken). ¶17a

Alternatives considered and set aside 2

POSSIBLE burial, not applied: Hjelmeland burials 1786 no.62 (canvas 81 right): 'Ane Peders D: Buchen blev begraven d: 26de Julÿ', 36 aar (b. c.1750). She would have been 18 at the 1768 marriage, and another Anna Pedersdatter married Tosten Børgesen Buchen in 1777 and fits equally. Not stated as wife or widow. No death date added until her husband is named. ¶6

Burial candidate, not adopted: 'Anna Pedersdr', aged 75 (b. about 1749), buried 7 Jul 1824 at Hjelmeland (Hjelmeland ministerialbok 1816-1841, p. 100, no. 5; index pg00000006650222; the index's death year 1827 is an error for 1824). Age fits and she is the only Anna Pedersdatter born 1744-1754 in the Hjelmeland burials, but the entry gives no farm or husband, and the 1777 marriage of a second Anna Pedersdatter to Tosten Børgesen Buchen (see above) means there may have been two women of the name. No date entered. ¶9

Searches that found nothing 1

Search note: the Halsnøe probate of 10 Jan 1771 in D 6 (DA 23890, fol. 349b, image sk20081104640381; heading 'Sierneröe Schibrede, Anno 1771 den 10de Januarij' at the foot of the left column, continued at the head of the right column) was read: it is after the woman Kari Rasmusdatter, shared between her surviving husband Ole Larsen and their 3 children, Lars aged 17, Rasmus 14 and a third child about 8 (name not read); gross estate 187-2-12. No Peder or Anna Pedersdatter appears, so Kari Rasmusdatter is not Anna's mother. The other Halsnøe probate in D 6 (Karen Biørnsdatter, unmarried, 1768, fol. 156b-157a) has Biørnsen sibling heirs and was not pursued. ¶11

Open questions and the next record to read 3

Lead: Halsnøy is filed under Sjernerø skipreide in the D 6 register (DA 23890, 1767-74). Two Halsnøy probates there: Karen Biørnsdatter, an unmarried woman ('pige'), 24 Feb 1768 (fol. 156b-157a; heirs are her siblings, names mostly illegible on the binarised scan), and Kari Rasmusdatter, 10 Jan 1771 (fol. 349b-350a; heirs not yet read, the closing mentions an Ole Larsen). Either could concern Anna's family; neither was read far enough to name heirs. ¶10a

Next: read fol. 349b bottom and 350a lines 1-4 for the heirs of Kari Rasmusdatter, and look for 'Anna Pedersdatter gift med Thore Buchen'. ¶10b

Test records: the 1801 census servant Niels Thorsen, 22, at Meele (needs a link to Bukken), the 1815 military roll (legdsrulle) for Sjernerø/Fister, or a later probate after Siri Thorsdatter Bukken (after 1824; registers to D 20a/D 20b) naming her brother Niels Thorsen at Sunde. ¶17b
