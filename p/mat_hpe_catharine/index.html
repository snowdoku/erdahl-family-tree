About the family

Wife of Gordon Thompson and mother of Susannah (bapt. 1809), Peter (bapt. 1811) and Frances Carpenter Thompson (bapt. 1814), all baptised in the Charlottetown Anglican register. Her maiden name is unknown. Frances's middle name 'Carpenter' may come from her, since a Carpenter family (Joseph and Hannah) had children baptised in the same register in 1796-1819, or from a sponsor. This is a lead, not a finding. ¶1

Life story

1798 census (context): Curry heads of household on the Island in 1798 were Patk. Curry (Lot 35), Alex'r Curry (Lot 36), Angus and Call Curry (Lot 38) and Dond. and Angus Curry (Lot 48), none in Charlottetown and Royalty. A Duncan Curry, born on PEI about 1770, petitioned for land in Cape Breton in 1814. None of these is linked to her; they are the Island's Curry families of her generation's parents. ¶9

Deeds: she joined her husband, 'Gordon Thompson of Charlotte Town... Mariner and Catharine his Wife', in a sale of 8 May 1812 to James Macintosh (PEI Land Registers 1810-1814, deed no. 237), and in other conveyances of 1810-12 (see Gordon Thompson). No deed by her as widow, and none by the Hickens as Thompson heirs, turned up in the FamilySearch full-text search. Grade unchanged, Probable. ¶11

She was widowed on 30 July 1815. No remarriage or burial has been found yet in the indexes. ¶2

Maiden name: all three baptisms of her children in the Charlottetown (St Paul's) Anglican register call her 'Catharine Curry his wife': 'Susannah, Daughter of Gordon Thompson and of Catharine Carry [Curry] his wife, born the 15th June 1809 and baptized 14th July following'; Peter, born 27 June 1811, baptised 2[2?] July 1811; 'Frances Carpenter, daughter of Gordon Thompson and of Catharine Curry his wife, Born 13th of November 1813, Baptized the 1st January 1814' (FamilySearch DGS 005240361 images 98, 106, 117; images read). The 1809 entry can be read 'Carry'; the later two read 'Curry'. The register gives no sponsors, occupation or residence. Name changed from 'Catharine' to 'Catharine Curry' on this evidence. The earlier lead that 'Carpenter' was her maiden name is set aside; Frances's middle name may come from the Joseph Carpenter family baptised on the same page. ¶6

Widow and administratrix: she petitioned on 1 August 1815 for administration of the estate of 'my Husband', who 'died intestate', and gave bond on 3 August 1815 as 'Catharine Thompson Widow' (PEI Probate Estate Case Files, T intestate no. 10, Estate of Gordon Thompson, images 978-982). She signed with her mark, spelled 'Chatharine Thompson'. The file names no children. Grade unchanged (Probable) on her link to Frances (see her page), for the same reason as her husband's. ¶10

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

Alternatives considered and set aside 2

Remarriage candidate (unconfirmed): the PEI marriage index has Catherine Thompson married to William Pickering on 20 November 1835 (Ancestry collection 61502, from the register on FamilySearch DGS film 004594917, frame 107). The index gives no widow marker, parents or place, so it is not known to be Gordon Thompson's widow; the register image would show whether she was styled widow. ¶3

Remarriage candidate weakened: the register entry itself (PEI marriage register, FamilySearch DGS film 004594917, image 107, register p. 170) styles the bride 'Catherine Thompson, Spinster' and the groom 'William Pickering, Bachelor', married 20 November 1835 by James Campbell, J.P., under licence from the Lieutenant Governor; witnesses John Grahame and Charles Anderson; no place or parents given. Taken at face value a spinster is not Gordon Thompson's widow, so this marriage is not hers; the bride could be an unrecorded daughter or an unrelated Catherine Thompson. ¶5

Searches that found nothing 3

Death search (negative): no Catharine, Catherine or Kitty Thompson in the PEI death card index 1810-1913, no burial 1815-1870 in the PEI baptism/burial index, and no match in the Island Register death and marriage transcriptions. ¶4

Origins search (negative): no baptism of a Catharine Curry/Currie/Currey/Corry/Carry c.1775-1795 in the PEI baptism indexes (Ancestry collections 9195 and 61501); the earliest Curry-family entry there is 1811, so her birth was probably outside the Island or before its surviving registers. No Gordon Thompson x Catharine Curry marriage appears in the indexed PEI marriages, though these index rows show only one party, so the search is not conclusive. Curry/Currie households on the Island 1811-1835 (e.g. John and Ellen Currie, Lot 45 and West River, children 1823-1829) are listed in the line's working notes; none is linked to her. ¶7

Marriage search (negative): the St Paul's Anglican register at Charlottetown (FamilySearch DGS 005240361, marriages section 'Marriages 1780-1819', images 566-584, covering 1780 to 1812) has no marriage of Gordon Thompson to Catharine Curry under any spelling (Currey, Carry, Corry); the surname margins of every page were read. They probably married elsewhere, before coming to the Island, or before a gap in the register (image 577 stops at 3 November 1806). Some small squeezed-in entries on images 566-567 were hard to read. ¶8
