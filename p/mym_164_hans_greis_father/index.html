Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Probable, graded on his link to Greis Hansen the elder: inferred from the patronymic only. The 1771 Riset probate (24757 fol. 381b) shows that Greis Hansen (the elder), Anna Hansdatter and the father of Gurie Hansdatter were siblings ('Farbroderen Greis Hansen', 'Faster Anna Hans Datter'), and both named siblings carry the patronymic Hansen/Hansdatter, so their father was a Hans. No record names him. ¶2a

Corrections 1

Correction: this person ('Hans', inferred only from the patronymic 'Greis Hansen' in the 1771 probate) was never linked as Greis Hansen the elder's father, and is now doubtful. The 1693 probate of Aren Thoresen at Alstad østre, Frosta (card index 77804 view 23) lists his children Hans Arensen, Greigers Arensen and Anne Arensdatter. That is the same sibling set as Gurie Hansdatter's father Hans, her farbror Greis and her faster Anna in 1771, so Greis's father may have been Aren Thoresen (see his page, linked at Speculative), and this 'Hans' may not exist. Kept unlinked. If the 1693 protocol entry (prot. 1 fol. 90) or another record confirms that Greis was Greigers Arentsen, this record should be merged into Aren Thoresen or retired. ¶3
