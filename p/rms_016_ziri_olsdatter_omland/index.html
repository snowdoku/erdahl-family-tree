About the family

Parents from the bygdebok: Åmli ætt og heim II, gard 16 (Øy), canvas 59: 'Sigrid g 1. m Vrål Bendikson, g 2. m Eivind Eivindson Jørundland', daughter of Olav Knutson Øy (skifte 1682) and (by the book's grouping) his second wife Ingeborg [?] Engenes (skifte 1707). The Omland chapter (canvas 203) calls Vrål's wife 'Sigrid [Olsdtr.] (Øy)'. Ziri is the same name as Sigrid. Father Probable, mother Speculative. ¶3

Life story

'Enkens mor: Ziri Olsdtr. (Olufsdtr.) Aamland + sk. 1688' (archivist's note, card 5999). Married (1) Vraal Bendixsen Aamland (+ sk. 1687) and (2) Evind Evindsen of Jørundland (from Hommedrum), whose probate of 18 Oct 1704 - 21 Nov 1705 (Skifteprotokoll 2 fol. 148, card 5981) names '1.g. Zire Olsdatter +' and the child of that marriage, Bendix (= Bendix Evindsen Mjåland, the half-brother of her daughter Anna in 1728). Her own probate (1688) not yet read. ¶1

New card. Askland card 43 (Skp. 5 fol. 25, 23 Apr 1716, the estate of her daughter Zigne Wraalsdatter, wife of Ole Tiøelsøn of Askland) cites the skiftebrev 'etter avd.'s mor, Siirj Olufsdtr. Ombland, dat. 15/11-1688', and the one after the father Wraal Bendixøn Ombland, 7 Sep 1687. This fixes her estate date. It fits the 1704-05 Jørundland record: she remarried Evind Evindsen soon after Vraal's death in 1687, bore him the son Bendix, and was dead by Nov 1688. The grade is unchanged. ¶2

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
