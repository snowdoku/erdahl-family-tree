About the family

Named for his grandfather Jorgen Olssen, and the man who held the old Slind bruk after him. Entered as 'Jorgen Olsen, 14, Son' in his parents' part of the 1825 census household at Slinden; as 'Jorgen Olsen Slinden, b. 1811 Saelboe, brudgom' - groom - in the Selbu marriage register on 26 November 1832, three weeks before his grandfather's funeral year closed; and as 'Husfader g Gaardbr. Leilaending' - head, married, tenant farmer - at Slinden, lopenr 188's neighbourhood, in the 1865 census, still there as 'hf g Gaardbr. og Leilaending' in 1875. ¶1

He is Oline Slind's father's second cousin's father, which is to say: the senior male line of the farm. His branch and hers both carried the Slind name in Selbu through the nineteenth century and the two must be kept apart. ¶2

His wife Ragnhild of Lille-Evjen, born 1811, outlived him by twenty-three years and died 2 December 1907. Their children, from the same page: a child f. 1833 who married Nils Persen; one f. 1836 who married a Kari of Uglan; Berit f. 1839; Torsten f. 1841; Jon f. 1844; Jorgen f. 1845; a younger child f. 1850 who died unmarried 28 September 1916 having had a son Peder in 1886; Anne f. 1853 (d. 1870); Arnt f. 1856, who took over the bruk; and Gunder f. 1860, who married a Marit Stokke. ¶4

Life story

He held the bruk 1835-1880 and married in 1832. Selbu i fortid og naatid vol. 7, p. 117: '1835-1880 [Jorn] Olsen, f. 1811, d. 1884. G. 1832 m. RAGNHILD [N.] LILLE-EVJEN fra..., f. 1811, d. 2.12.1907.' His death in 1884 matches the Selbu burial index's 'Jorgen Olsen, b. 1811 Slinden, avdod 24 Aug 1884' exactly, which is a clean independent check on the gaardshistorie and on the whole holder list. ¶3

That last son, Gunder f. 1860, is the 'Gunder Jorgensen, b. 1860, Slind' who recurs all over the Selbu index as an inderst and then a tenant farmer and who must NOT be confused with Gunder Jorgensen b. 1792 of this tree. Two Gunder Jorgensens on the Slind farms, sixty-eight years apart, both of this family. ¶5
