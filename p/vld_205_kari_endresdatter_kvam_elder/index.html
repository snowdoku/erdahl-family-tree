About the family

Mother of Endre Olufsen Kvam (see his page), Probable; graded on her link to Endre. The 1681 probate card (39945 image 7523; skifteprotokoll 1673-1688 fol. 106a, skifte 5 Jul 1681 at Kvam i Lesje) names her as the widow of Oluf Biertesen ('g.m. Karj Endresdtr.') and then lists the children Endre Olufsen, Ingelbret and Pofuel. A card that lists the widow and then the children does not by itself make her their mother (audit 8 rule 2), and the identification of the 1681 Endre with the tree's Endre spans 27 years; so Probable. The bygdebok (vol. 1 canvas 215) also gives Ola's wife as 'Kari Endresd.', and Endre bore her father's name and named a daughter Kari. ¶1

Life story

The protocol entry was read (skifteprotokoll 1673-1688, source 25154, view 108, fol. 106a, 5 Jul 1681): 'Skifte paa Quamb... efter Sl: Oluf Bier[te]ss: imellem hans hustru Karri Endersd: og deris 3 Børn, Ender, Ingelbret og Poffuell Olufss:'. 'deris Børn' names her as the mother of these three sons (audit 8 rule 2 met), so whichever Endre this is, she is his mother. Grade unchanged (Probable, graded on her link to Endre Olufsen, Endre Olufsen Kvam): the step from the Endre of 1681 to Endre Olufsen of Nordre Kvam in 1708 still spans 27 years, the only record between (the 1696 lagrettemann 'Ender Qvam') gives no patronymic, and an Endre Amundsen is named in the other 1681 Kvam probate. Note: on the facing page (fol. 105b) a different 'Karrj Endresdatter' died in 1681 at another farm (her husband 'Ender...sen', her brother Ingelbret Endresen heir); she is not this woman, who is alive as widow on the same day. ¶3

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
