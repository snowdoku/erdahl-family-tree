About the family

Children and residences: The couple's son Hans was baptised on 25 Aug 1771 with the father given as Christian Hansen of Sogn (p. 232). Their son Christoffer was baptised on 21 Aug 1774 at Lunner with the father given as Christian Hansen of Veloreien, a plot under the Velo(r) farm (p. 252). Kari's brother Jacob Christoffersen and Halvor Rolfsen were godparents at both baptisms. By 1801 the family was at Bjørgesæter. ¶5

Life story

DA 9334 canvases 73-78 (12 Nov 1741 - 19 May 1743, pp. 142-153) read: no Christian son of a Hans. The only Christian baptised is the son of 'Soldat Christian Syrestad[?]', 29 Aug 1742. With (none Jun 1739 - Nov 1741), there is no Christian son of Hans Pedersen and Kari Olsdatter (m. Apr 1739) to May 1743. His burial age (82 in Dec 1821) points to 1739, where the two candidates are Christen son of Hans Græsthagen (22 Mar 1739) and Christian son of Hans Christensen (24 Apr 1739). Related: on 14 Jul 1768 'Christopher Tomeraas' stood godfather to Gunder, son of Halvor Rolfsen Veloe and Anne Hansdatter, who had married on 7 Jul 1768 (DA 9335 p. 198, Anne 'Søgn'; no father named), the year before Christian married Kari Christoffersdatter of Tømmeraas. Deeds for Sogn (gnr. 163) in pantebok 8 (DA 10272), from the typed register DA 266260 p. 3: skjøte 1762 fol. 102b, obl. 1762 102b, skjøte 1762 132b, two skjøter 1767 fol. 602a, skjøte 1769 fol. 737a. Fol. 737a (view 739) No. 11 is a deed by Gunder Joensen [?] of Sogn; the hand was not legible at my resolution, so whether it names Hans Pedersen's family is open. ¶12

PROBABLE marriage: Christian Hansen, bachelor, of Sogn, married Kari Christoffersdatter of Tømmeraas on 2 Nov 1769 at Jevnaker main church. The son named Christoffer (b. about 1775) fits Kari's father's name. Kari's census age (48, i.e. b. about 1753) would make her only 16 in 1769. Either her age is understated or this is a second wife, so the link is Probable. ¶2

Marriage: He was betrothed on 31 Aug 1769 to Kari Christophersdatter of Tømmeraas. He is called Ungkarl (bachelor) and the forlovere (sureties) were Bent Sogn and Ole Dahl[e] (p. 213). They married on 2 Nov 1769 at Jevnaker main church as "Ungkarl Christian Hansen Sogn" and "Pigen Kari Christophersdatter Tømmeraas" (p. 215). Because he was a bachelor in 1769, this was his first marriage, and Kari was the mother of his children. ¶3

1801 census, Jevnaker, Biørgesætren: Christian Hansen, aged 70, farmer (bonde og gaardbruger), with wife Kari Chrestoffersdatter (48) and sons Chrestoffer (26) and Christian (19). Walborg Pedersdatter, widow, is listed as 'deres sønnekone' (their son's wife). ¶1

Probate: estate of Christian Hansen, Bjørgesæteren i Lunner, 11 Feb - 1 May 1822 (Hadeland og Land skifteprotokoll 1800-1843, fol. 47b; index card DA 39947 no. 659). Widow Kari Christophersdatter; children 1. Hans (deceased) with his children Hans Bjørgesæter (of age) and Marte (24, unmarried), 2. Christian, 3. Christopher, both of age. Gross 510-1-11, net 443-0-9; farm share 1 1/2 skind valued 300 spd. So he died about 1821-22, aged about 90. The card names none of his own kin. ¶8

Burial: 'Christian Hansen', buried 6 Jan 1822 in Jevnaker parish, died 28 Dec [1821], aged 82 (Jevnaker ministerialbok 1815-1837, index pg00000006236536; no residence given in the index). The date fits his estate at Bjørgesæteren, registered 11 Feb 1822 (card DA 39947 no. 659), so this is very probably him. Age 82 puts his birth about 1739-40, not about 1731 as the 1801 census age of 70 implies; he would then have been about 30 when he married as a bachelor in 1769. The birth field is left unchanged until a baptism is found. ¶9

The Sogn lead: no Hans appears at Sogn in the indexed Jevnaker marriages 1720-1770 (22 Sogn hits: Gulbrand Pedersen 1733 and 1744, Bent/Bendt as surety 1744-47, Anne Hansdatter bride 7 Jul 1768, Christian Hansen groom 1769). A 'Hans Peders.' with no farm married Kari Olsdatter of Nykleby at Jevnaker on 2 Apr 1739 (betrothed 3 Mar; sureties Børe Berger and Torgrim Rønrud; pv00000002195713, p. 217). If he is the Hans Pedersen of Sogn buried 8 Jan 1772 aged 78, a son born in 1740 would fit the burial age of 82, but no baptism of a Christian to this couple was noticed up to early Nov 1741 (c72). ¶11a

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Probable first child: On 10 Sep 1770 "Christian Hansens dødfødt Pigebarn" (stillborn girl) was buried at the main church. On 7 Oct 1770 the wives of Christian Hansen and Hans H[iø?] were churched. No farm is given, but the stillbirth falls about ten months after the marriage, and no other Christian Hansen is seen as a father in 1769-1770 (pp. 223-224). ¶4

Confidence: Upgraded from Probable to Confirmed. The bachelor of Sogn who married in 1769 is the father named at the 1771 and 1774 baptisms of Hans and Christoffer, and both sons appear with him in 1801. The 1801 census age of 70 (born about 1731) is still unchecked against a baptism. ¶7

Searches that found nothing 1

Baptism search: Jevnaker ministerialbok 1725-1751 (DA 9334; baptisms are NOT indexed, as a domicile search for Tømmeraas 1730-1760 returns only a 1755 marriage) read on the scan for Feb 1739 - early Nov 1741 (IIIF canvases 62-72, pp. 120-141). Two boys of a Hans were baptised in 1739, both at the main church: (1) Palm Sunday 22 Mar 1739 (p. 120, c62): 'Hans Græsthagens barn döbt ved Navn Christen', godparents Morthen Gaarerud, Ole Enger, Brynil Olim, Gubiør Kierstad and Pernille Græsthagen; (2) 24 Apr 1739, Almindelig Bededag (p. 122, c63): 'Hans Christensens barn döbt ved Navn Christian', godparents Ivar Sjå, Marthe Sjå, Iver Kiørren, Christoffer Olsen and Rønnaug Christerud. Neither entry names Sogn or a Pedersen, and the two are rivals for each other, so neither is adopted. No other Christian/Christen son of a Hans was noticed in Jun 1739 - early Nov 1741 (c64-72). ¶10

Open questions and the next record to read 2
