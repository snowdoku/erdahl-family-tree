About the family

The third child of John P. Eidem and Oline Slind, previously unknown to this project. ¶1

Her husband was a Lutheran minister: at Oline's funeral, 'Rev. Peder Lerseth of Hendricks, Minn., sang the Norwegian hymn "Jeg ved mig en sovn i Jesu Navn," and also spoke on behalf of the family.' Hendricks is in Lincoln County, Minnesota, forty miles south of Marietta and likewise on the South Dakota line. ¶6

Life story

Birth registration: Minnesota birth card no. 12001 of 1906, Lac qui Parle County, read from the record image. Typed, not written: 'Birth 1906 May 22 Mehurin tp L.Q.P / Olga J. Eidem / W / F / John " Farmer Nor 29 / Oline Nor 25 / G. S. Schackelford Marietta.' Father John, farmer, born Norway, aged 29; mother Oline, born Norway, aged 25. ¶2

Censuses: in her father's Mehurin household in 1910 (indexed 'Olga J Eidem', b. 1907) and 1920 ('Olga', b. abt 1907), and gone by 1930 - so she married between January 1920 and April 1930 (to Peder Lerseth, per her mother's 1955 obituary). ¶9

A daughter of John P. Eidem and Oline G. Slind who was unknown to this project. Her mother's October 1955 obituary names 'Mrs. Peder Lerseth (Olga) of Hendricks, Minn.' among the eight children. ¶5

That one of John P. and Oline's daughters married a Norwegian-speaking Lutheran pastor is worth noting for the church-records side: Rev. Peder Lerseth's own ministerial papers and the Hendricks congregation's records are a route into this family. ¶7

She is not buried at Zoar. No dates found; rated Probable on the obituary alone. ¶8

Married name and death: she died 24 January 1978 in Lincoln County, Minnesota, as Olga J. Lerseth - Minnesota death certificate index 1978-MN-000974, birth date 22 May 1906, birthplace Minnesota, mother's maiden name SLIND. The birth date matches the 1906 card to the day. Lincoln County adjoins Lac qui Parle to the south, so she married a Lerseth and settled two counties from home. ¶3
