Life story

Her given name is less certain (Speculative to Probable). Geni names the mother as Brite Larsdatter. A Brite Lassesdatter of Erdal had probate in 1731-34 (protocol 7, p. 511), and Rasmus's guardianship account cites the inheritance book 'fol. 513' at 9 Jun 1734. ¶2

Aaland 1932: Aaland names Jørgen Rasmussen Erdal's wife as 'Brite Lassed. Hj.', Brite Lassesdatter of Hjelle. The given name Brite agrees with Geni and with the 1731-34 probate index (Brite Lassesdatter, Erdal). The farm conflicts with the primary record: the 1727 guardianship entry names the boys' maternal grandfather (morfar) as 'Lars Grønuur', i.e. of Grønfur. Aaland's own Hjelle owner lists show no daughter Brite married to Erdal, and Aaland names only one wife for Jørgen, so he does not address the half-sister Brite Jørgensdatter. The contemporaneous 1727 record is trusted for her father's farm; Aaland's 'Hjelle' is unsupported. A Lars or Lasse who had moved between Hjelle and Grønfur could reconcile the two, but nothing found shows that. ¶4

Probate 1731-34 of Brite Lassesdatter, Erdal: Nordfjord skifteprotokoll A 7 (L0007) fol. 511 is scan image 517 (fol. 509 is image 515). The page heading 'Erdahl' is visible. The preamble with the names of the widower and heirs cannot be read: the bitonal microfilm scan has lost most strokes, and contrast, unsharp and erosion (stroke-thickening) filters did not recover them. Fol. 511b-512 (image 518) is inventory, and fol. 513 (image 519) holds the debts and lots, likewise illegible apart from a few headings. Whether she was a daughter of Lars Grønfur, and who her mother and heirs were, remains unanswered. ¶8

Nordfjord A 8 (24225), Jul-Dec 1743 specification, image 126 left. 'Erdal den 9 Octobr [1743], efter en afgangen liden pige, ved navn Brit Jørgensdatter, arvede den eene hendes umyndige halfbroder, som var Ole Jørgensen 18 aar', 5 marker smørs leie in the farm Grønfur, plus loose goods and money, 30 rd in all; guardian Rasmus Folvem. This confirms that the girl Brit(e) Jørgensdatter was only a HALF-sister of Ole Jørgensen. So Jørgen Erdal had children by two wives, which agrees with this woman (mother of Rasmus, Lars and Ole, dead by Oct 1723) and a later wife, probably the Brite Lassesdatter of Erdal whose probate is dated 1731-34. Brit owned land in Grønfur, which suggests that Grønfur land came into the second marriage too, either through Jørgen's estate or because the second wife was also of Grønfur stock. That cannot be told from this extract. Ole's age of 18 is too young for a boy who inherited in Oct 1723 (he would be at least 20). Ages in these lists are rounded, but note the discrepancy. No grade change. ¶9

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 record that disagrees · 3 corrections

Records that disagree 1

CONFLICT: Brite Jørgensdatter (born about 1731/32) is called Rasmus's half-sister. So either the Brite Lassesdatter who died about 1733 was Jørgen's second wife (and Rasmus's mother was a different Lars-daughter who died about 1723), or the half-sister came from a later wife. The 1734 skifte text is too faded on the scan to read. Treat the name as uncertain. ¶3

Corrections 3

Correction: she died before 10 Oct 1723, not about 1731-34. Nordfjord guardianship protocol no. 1, fol. 7v: 'Jørgen Erdale formynder for sine Børn, nemlig: Rasmus Jørgensen for 21 rd, Lars Jørgensen for 21 rd, Ole Jørgensen for 21 rd, effter Skiftebrev af dato 10 Octob. 1723 og Arvebogen fol. 526' ('Jørgen Erdal, guardian for his children Rasmus, Lars and Ole Jørgensen, 21 rd each, after the probate of 10 Oct 1723'). Their father was alive and acting as their guardian, so the estate they inherited in 1723 was their mother's (an inference, but the only usual one). Their inheritance in 1727 from their maternal grandfather (morfar) 'Lars Grønuur', rather than through a living mother, agrees. ¶5

Correction: her given name is not known, so it is changed from Brite to NN. 'Brite' came from Geni, from Aaland's 'Brite Lassed. Hj.', and from the probate index entry 'Brite Lassesdtr, Erdal, 1731-1734' (protocol 7, p. 511). A woman who died before October 1723 cannot be the subject of a 1731-34 probate. So Brite Lassesdatter of Erdal is a different woman, probably Jørgen's second wife and the mother of the half-sister Brite Jørgensdatter (born about 1731/32). That inference cannot be checked, because the 1734 probate text is illegible (see below). ¶6

Correction: the guardianship reference to 'fol. 513' and June 1734 is not an inheritance. The same entry (fol. 7v, middle column) reads 'Rasmus Jørgensen, hans Arvemidler var opløben til den 8 Junij 1734... Arvebogen fol. 513... 35-5-6; og Ole Jørgensens... 34-1-6' ('Rasmus Jørgensen's inherited means had grown to 35 rd 5 mk 6 sk by 8 June 1734 per the inheritance book fol. 513; Ole Jørgensen's to 34-1-6'). It adds that 'Lars Jørgensen var nu ved døden afgangen' (Lars Jørgensen had now died). This is a balance statement and does not name Brite Lassesdatter. ¶7
