About the family

Wife of Jorgen Olssen of Slinden at the 1801 census of Selbu, aged 41, the marriage being his second and her first. Mother, on the reading taken here, of Gunder Jorgenssen, born about 1792, Oline Slind's great-grandfather. ¶1

Her children, as the gaardshistorie separates them from her husband's first family: Ingeborg f. 1787 (d. 1788), Kari f. 1789 - the 'Karen Jorgensdatter, 12' of the 1801 census - and GUNNAR f. 1792, David Erdahl's ancestor. Three children, the first of whom died at a year old. ¶8

Life story

Baptism (Probable identification): 1st Sunday in Advent, 3 December 1758, '(3) Ane. Forældr: Gunar Ols: Vigen, Kari Andersd:' - Ane, daughter of Gunnar Olsen of Vigen (= Overvik) and Kari Andersdatter (Selbu ministerialbok 1757-1801, canvas 7 left page, crop). Birth replaced 'ABT 1758' with 'before 3 December 1758'. For why 'Vigen' is Overvik see Gunnar (Gunder) Olsen Overvik. ¶12

SHE CAME FROM OVERVIK, she married in 1787, and she died in 1841. Selbu i fortid og naatid vol. 7, p. 116: '1775-1814 Jorn Olsen... G.II 1787 m. ANNE [N.] OVERVIK, se..., f. 1758, d. 1841.' The book's 1758 is exactly the birth year the 1825 census's age of 67 gives, which is a clean independent agreement, and it corrects the ABT 1760 that the 1801 census's age of 41 implied. Her death year of 1841 replaces the AFT 27 NOV 1825 I had from the census alone: she outlived her husband by nine years and died at about eighty-three. ¶7

Her farm of origin, OVERVIK (Overvik), is on the north shore of Selbusjoen a short way west of Slindan; the book's 'se...' is a cross-reference to its chapter, where her father - a Gunder, by her patronymic - will be named. That is the next record on this branch. ¶9

The gaardshistorie also records what she did with the farm at the end: the narrative on p. 113 says 'Jorn Olsen og Anne [drev] bruket med soennen [Ola] og kona... Anne var [stemor] til [Ola] og... [det] var... den [yngste] hennes... soenn, GUNNAR [som skulle ha hatt en del] av bruket. Men han flyttet [i] 1821 til AUNET som gardbruker. [I] 1825 [var Ola] og Anne [fort opp] som brukere' - she and her stepson ran the holding together, and her own son Gunnar, who might have had a share of it, moved to Aunet as a farmer in 1821. That is the gaardshistorie independently confirming the move the Soelvskatt roll of 3 March 1821 shows, and it says why: there was not room for both sons. ¶10

Marriage: betrothed 22 April 1787 as 'Anne Gundersd. Slinden' - already of Slinden before her marriage - to Jorgen Olsen Slinden, and married 28 May 1787 (Whit Monday) in Selbu. Her children Ingeborg (baptised 30 December 1787) and Karen (21 May 1789) precede Gunder (1792), which supports her maternity of Gunder: he was born five years into her marriage, after two sisters of the same parents. ¶11

She is not the Anne Gundersdatter who bore children at Slinden in 1816, 1820 and 1823. That woman is entered in the same 1825 census, in the same farm listing, as 'Anne Gundersd., 35, Hustru' - born about 1790 - wife of the cottager Ole Christophersen. Two women of identical name on one farm, a generation apart, is the commonest trap on a Norwegian farm and both are recorded here so that the next researcher does not merge them. ¶5

Her burial has not been found. A search of the indexed Selbu deaths for an Ane or Anne Gundersdatter born between 1750 and 1770 returns only one woman, born about 1765 and buried 22 February 1824, who cannot be her because she was alive in November 1825. The entry is on the scanned pages of the Selbu burial register for 1826-1842. ¶6

Found again twenty-four years on, which settles that she was Jorgen Olssen's wife and not a short-lived second match: the 1825 census of Selbu enters her as 'Anne Gundersd., 67, Hustru' at the head of the Slinden household beside 'Jorgen Olsen, 75, Gmd.' She was therefore still alive and still his wife on 27 November 1825, and she was born about 1758 rather than the about 1760 implied by her stated age of 41 in 1801. ¶4

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

2 on the grade · 1 record that disagrees · 2 searches with no result · 2 open questions

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Rated Probable for the maternity rather than Confirmed: the 1801 census lists the four children of the household collectively as 'Deres born' without separating the issue of the husband's first marriage from that of his second, so while Gunder born about 1792 falls well inside Ane's marriage, the census does not say so outright. Her own patronymic, Gundersdatter, is the likeliest reason her son was named Gunder. ¶2

Why this Ane is her: she is the only Ane daughter of a Gunnar/Gunder baptised in read of every Ane in January 1758 - December 1759 (16311 canvases 5-8); her 1825 census age of 67 gives 1758, and her 1801 age of 41 is about a year low; the bygdebok puts 'Anne... 1758... Jorn Olsen Slind' under Gunnar of Overvik (vol. 6 p. 327) and calls Jorn Olsen's second wife 'Anne... Overvik, f. 1758, d. 1841' (vol. 7 p. 116); she named her second daughter Kari, her mother's name, and her son Gunnar/Gunder, her father's. ¶13a

Records that disagree 1

Against: the indexed 1841 burial of 'Anne Gundersdt' gives age 72 (born about 1769), eleven years off, so it does not support the 1758 birth; and no primary record calls the adult Anne 'Overvik' - she married in 1787 as 'Anne Gundersd. Slinden'. Hence Probable. Not yet excluded: an Ane daughter of a Gunnar baptised in 1757 or 1760 (16311 canvases 2-4 and 9-10 not read for this). ¶13b

Searches that found nothing 2

Probate search, negative: the A page of the Strinda og Selbu skifteutlodningsprotokoll 2, 1841-1853 (Digitalarkivet 24746), handwritten register at the back, one letter per image (A = image 479, B 480, C 481, F 484, J 487, L 490, R 495, S 496) has no estate of Anne Gundersdatter of Slinden (died 1841); the many Anne entries there are of other farms. A probate naming her son Gunder Jorgensen, which would confirm her maternity, is not in this protocol. ¶14

Baptism search for her son Gunder (negative so far, no grade change): Gunder's own baptism would name both parents and settle her link to him, which the 1801 census 'Deres børn' cannot (it was Jorgen Olssen's second marriage). Selbu ministerialbok 1757-1801 (source 16311) canvases 141 (Feb-May 1791), 142 (Apr-Jul 1791), 143 (Aug-Dec 1791) and 144 (1 Jan-Mar 1792) were read at 2000 px: no Gunder with a father Jorgen/Jørgen. The two Gunders baptised in that span are sons of Ole Gundersen Sesaas (14th Sunday after Trinity 1791) and of an Ole of Gulset[?] (Sexagesima-Invocavit 1792). The bygdebok gives Gunnar's birth as 1792, so the next canvases are 145-148 (Mar-Dec 1792; c148 = Sep 1792). Images:. ¶15

Open questions and the next record to read 2

Lead, not evidence: she should be printed in the SLINDAN NORDRE chapter of Selbu i fortid og naatid, bind 7 (1992), whose heading stands on printed page 92. The year 1760 occurs on page 91, with 1748, 1749, 1750, 1761 and 1762 - a sibling group of the right span - and page 92 carries the holder tenure 1751-1773 and the years 1753 to 1773. The phrase 'Ane Gundersdatter' and the abbreviated 'Ane Gundersdtr' both return nothing in volume 7, so the book does not spell her name out in either form; her entry will be under her husband's. Order volume 7, pages 86-112. ¶3

Would settle it: a read of 1757 and 1760 for a rival, or a record linking Anne of Slinden to Overvik (for example an Overvik probate naming her). ¶13c
