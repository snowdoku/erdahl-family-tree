About the family

Mother of Ingebor Gjestsdatter: Probable, inferred from the 1801 household. No baptism of Ingebor has been read (born about 1790-91; Leikanger register 1792-1809 does not cover it). ¶2

Parents found: the probate of Anfind Andersen at Feeten (Fetene), Tjugum skipreide, 16 Aug 1768 (Ytre Sogn 8b p. 528, No. 36) names 'eldste Datter Christi Anfindsdatter 6 aar gl.' (born about 1762), with her mother Britte [Endres]datter, brothers Anders and Ole and a younger sister, and the Farbroder Ambiørn Andersen Ulvestad. The age matches her 1801 (38) and 1815 (52) census ages exactly, Fetene lies in the same Tjugum skipreide as Esse, and she named her elder son Anfind. Her paternal grandmother Christi Anfindsdatter of Ulvestad, d. March 1761 aged 67, was her namesake. Entered as Probable: no record yet names her both at Feten and at Esse. Ruled out on the way: Anfind Gundersen Njøs, probate 13 Nov 1784 (Ytre Sogn 11a p. 318, No. 69, 24170 view 319): children Gunder, Anders, Hans, Anfind and Mons Anfindsen and Ingeri Anfindsdatter 30, no Christi; mother Marthe Andersdatter (probate 25 Mar 1776). ¶7

Parents still unknown after the remaining Tjugum Anfind probates were read; none lists a daughter Christi who could be this woman (b. about 1762, confirmed 1780 at Tvet aged 18, married Gjest Giestsen 1790): (1) Anfind Stillaugsen, Hove, No. 26, 19-20 Feb 1766 (8a fol. 342b, 24165 view 345): widow Birgitte Togersdatter Nitter, sons Jens 22, Toger, Niels, Henric and daughter Dorothea Catharina; (2) Anfind Svendsen, Grindeland (8a fol. 200b, 24165 view 203): widow Anna Nielsdatter, sons Svend and Niels, daughters Marithe (m. Niels Ericsen), Kari (m. Anfind Monsen Tveiten), Christie (already married to Peder S...) and Siri; that Christie was married by about 1764 and so is not ours; (3) Anfind Olsen, Hove, No. 53, 6 May 1771 (8b p. 700a, 24166 view 318): an unmarried farmhand whose heirs were his siblings; (4) Anfind Olsen, Sætre, No. 81, 16 Jul 1785 (10a p. 103a-104a, 24168 views 103-104): daughters Unni, Ingeri 23 and Gioa 18 by a first wife Giertrud, and Ole 8 and Unni 3 by the widow Synneve Ellingsdatter; (5) Anfind Olsen, Hov, 17 Apr 1795 (11b p. 597b, 24171 view 203): widow Giertrud Tollefsdatter and one daughter Christi Anfindsdatter aged 20 (b. about 1775, too young, and unmarried in 1795); (6) Anfind Pedersen, Mundal, 1795 (11b p. 620b-621b, 24171 views 226-227): childless, with siblings as heirs. A probate-index search for Christi/Kirsti Anfindsdatter as an heir 1763-92 finds only Indre Sogn women (Amble/Kaupanger 1770 aged 19; Engesetter Haugen 1771, a daughter in service at Dahlager, age not given; Kopanger Holten 1777 aged 6 weeks), none tied to Tjugum. Her father probably died outside Tjugum skipreide, or after she married, or left no probate. ¶15

Parents added from the bygdebok, Probable: Balestrand 1: Ættesoge (1985), canvas 132, names 'Kristi som vart gift [med] Gjest Gjestson Ese' as a child of Arnfinn Olson Bale (b. 1724, m. 1757, d. 1800) and Ingeborg Nilsdtr. (d. 1781), with a brother Nils Arnfinnson (b. 1766, m. 1795 Sigrid Johannesdtr.). Canvas 102 (Indre Ese) has Gjest Gjestson, husmann there in 1801, born 1761, 'vart [i] 1790 gift med Kristi [...]'. This is a new lead after every Ytre Sogn Anfind probate of 1762-99 had been read without result: her father died only in 1800. ¶17a

Parents confirmed: the probate after her mother Ingeborg Nielsdatter, 18 Oct 1781 (Ytre Sogn 9b p. 1479, 24167 view 739), at Kalvetræet under Tjugum, names 'Datteren Christie Anfindsdatter 19 aar gammel', her father the widower Anfind Olsen, and her brother Niels Anfindsen 15. ¶18a

Life story

More Anfind probates read; none is her father. With the Tjugum ones, every Anfind in the Ytre Sogn probate register 1762-1799 has now been read except Anfind Bottelsen of Østrebø, Lavik (9a p. 331). (1) Anfind Knudsen, Skeie or Vaarstølen, Tjugum, No. 16, 3-4 May 1764 (8a fol. 117b, 24165 view 120): widow Randi Joensdatter; sons Knud 5 and Joen 3, daughters Giøe, Giøe(?), Gierthru and one more, all small; no Christi. (2) Anfind Hansen, Fyli, Vik, No. 26, 11 Apr 1785 (10a p. 35a, 24168 view 35): widow Marthe Nielsdatter; son Hans 20, daughters Brithe 23 and Brithe 17. (3) Anfind Samsonsen, Grinde, Sjøstrand, No. 48, 4 May 1785 (10a p. 60b-61a, 24168 view 61): widow Britte Joensdatter; daughters Christi 12, Brithe 9, Kari 7 and Agaete 2. This Christi was born about 1773, ten years too late for ours (18 at her confirmation in 1780). (4) Anfind Thorsen, Vikøren under Sæbø, No. 104, 1790 (11a fol. 342b, 24170 view 344): widow Ingeborg Johnsdatter; children of his earlier marriage Johannes (of age) and Gjørand 25. (5) Anfind Monsen, Verken, No. 6, 7 Feb 1794 (11b p. 537a, 24171 view 143): widow Brithe Nielsdatter, two sons Anbjørn and Niels. (6) Anfind Ellingsen, Fosse, Sjøstrand, No. 16, 14-15 Apr 1762 (7b fol. 578b, 24164 view 244): widow Gjertrud [Heines?]datter, one son Elling, 9 days old. (7) Anfind Nielsen, Henjesanden, No. 6, 19 Feb 1757 (7b fol. 357a, 24164 view 22): widow Britte Omundsdatter; sons Omund and Ole, daughters Malene, Dordei 17, Britte 15 and Marite 12; it predates her birth anyway. Still unread: Anfind Guttormsen, Midlang, Vik (7b fol. 371b, 24164 view 37, image saved at but not read). If her father is not there, he probably outlived the 1790s protocols or died outside Ytre Sogn. ¶16

Confirmation found: Leikanger confirmation list (11567, canvas 233, fol. 229), 1st Sunday in Advent, 30 Nov 1777, at Tjugum, confirmed by the chaplain Vigeboe: among the Tjugum girls 'Kari Erichs Dr Feten 17', '[Lucie?] Erichs Dr ibid 16', 'Christi Anfinds Dr ibid 16 aar', and among the boys 'Ole Anfindsen Feten 18'. So Christi Anfindsdatter was still at Feten aged 16 in 1777 (born about 1761), with her brother Ole, matching the 1768 Feten probate (Christi 6, brother Ole) nine years on, same farm, and the 1801/1815 census ages (b. 1762/63). The list gives no parents' names, and it is the only Christi Anfindsdatter in the Tjugum lists of 1771-1779 read. What is still missing is the step from Feten to Esse: her marriage to Gjest Gjestsen (not in the 11567 Actus list late Apr 1789-May 1790, canvases 180-196). A rival, another Christi Anfindsdatter b. about 1763, wife of Marcus Marcussen at Esse in 1801, keeps her parentage at Probable. Graded link unchanged. ¶9

Marriage found: Leikanger ministerialbok 11567, Tjugum sogn. Betrothal ('Trolovet') 25 Sep 1790: 'Soldat Giest Giestsen Esse med Pigen Christie Anfinds Datter Tvedt', sureties ('Forlovere') Niels and Anders Tjugum (canvas 202 right, fol. 198). Marriage ('Copuleret') 11 Oct 1790, same names and farms (canvas 203 left, fol. 199). Neither entry names a parent. The bride's farm is written 'Tvedt' (the capital is this clerk's T, as in 'Tiugum', not his looped F), i.e. Tveiten/Tveit in Tjugum skipreide, not Feten. This dates the marriage (before only 'BEF 1790') and shows Gjest was a soldier, at Esse already in 1790; Ingebor (b. about 1791) is their first child. Graded link (to Gjest and Ingebor) unchanged. ¶10

Baptism of a daughter: Anna, baptised at Tjugum 24 Sep 1797, 'Forældrene: Giest Gjestsen Esse og Christi Anfinds D.' (Leikanger 11568, canvas 98 left). She is the Anna, 4, of the 1801 household and Anna, 16, of 1815. ¶13

Confirmation found: Leikanger ministerialbok 11567, canvas 234, third column (fol. 230 verso), 'D: 22de Søndag efter Trinit: i Tjugum med Hr. Vigeboe confirmeret 28 børn' (22nd Sunday after Trinity, 22 Oct 1780, at Tjugum, by the chaplain Vigeboe), among the girls: 'Christi Anfindsdr: Tvet [the word could also be read Troet] 18 aar'. Next to her: 'Brita Anfindsdr: Ege=Støen 17 aar'. This is the only Christi Anfindsdatter in the 1780 lists for Leikanger, Fresvik, Rinde and Tjugum on this page (Leikanger, Fresvik and Rinde girls in column 2 were also checked: none). Age 18 in Oct 1780 gives a birth about 1762, matching the 1801 (38) and 1815 (52) census ages of Gjest Gjestsen's wife, and the farm is the one she married from ten years later ('Christie Anfinds Datter Tvedt', 25 Sep / 11 Oct 1790). So she was already at Tveit as a confirmand. The Feten Christi (Marcus Marcussen's wife) was confirmed separately in 1777, so the two women are now distinct in the confirmation lists too. The list names no parents. Tveit (Tvedt) had two households in 1801: Mons Anfindsen's (with his mother Kari Anfindsdatter, b. about 1727, widow of Anfind Monsen) and Simon Guttormsen's (b. about 1721, with his son Lars Simonsen, who was confirmed in the same 1780 list). Anfind Monsen's probate (Ytre Sogn 9b p. 1255-56, 28 Apr 1781) and Kari Anfindsdatter's (14 p. 549b-550a, 1813) list no Christi, so she was not their daughter by that marriage; her father was another Anfind, perhaps kin of the Tveit family (her children Kari and Sjur repeat names of that family). Grade unchanged (graded on her link to Gjest and Ingebor). ¶14

1801 census: Christi Anfindsdatter, 38, wife of the cottager Giest Giestsen at Esse, Leikanger, both in their first marriage, with Ingeborg 10, Anfind 7 and Anna 4. 1815 census: Christi Anfinds., 52, wife of Gjest Gjestsen at Esse. ¶1

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Stays Probable as mother of Ingebor Gjestsdatter: the 1801 census shows her and Giest Giestsen both in their first marriage with Ingeborg 10, Anfind 7 and Anna 4, but no record read so far names her as Ingebor's mother (no baptism read; Leikanger 1790-91 baptisms are in register 11567, canvases about 193-230, unindexed -). A second Christi Anfindsdatter, also born about 1763, was the wife of Marcus Marcussen at Esse in 1801 (household 07, pf01058393002109). Candidate burial: 'Kristi Anfindsdtr', buried 9 Sep 1852 (Leikanger p. 183 no. 43, index pg00000004420954, no age or residence in the index; not yet read). ¶5

Upgrade: Confirmed as the mother of Ingebor Gjestsdatter (see her page). The 1801 census for Leikanger (1419P), census district 005, farm 0004 Esse, household 03, read on the Digitalarkivet transcription, lists 'Giest Giestsen', 40, 'Mand', 'Huusmand med lidt jord'; 'Christi Anfindsdatter', 38, 'Hans kone'; both 'Begge i 1ste ægteskab' (both in their first marriage); and as 'Deres børn' (their children) Anfind, 'Ingeborg Giertsdatter' [sic, for Giestsdatter], 10, and Anna. The census names the relationship directly: Ingeborg, born about 1791 at Esse, is the child of Giest and his wife Christi, and both parents were in their first marriage, so she is not a stepchild. This is the relationship-word evidence the earlier note asked for in place of the unread 1790-91 baptism. The grade covers only her link to Ingebor; her own parentage is unknown and nothing here bears on it. ¶8

Corrections 2

Correction: the Christi Anfindsdatter of Feten belongs to another family, the household of Marcus Marcussen. (1) Leikanger ministerialbok 11568, canvas 93 right (fol. 91), Tjugum sogn, about 19-22 June 1797: 'Copuleret Unge-Karl Marcus Marcussen Thue med Pigen Christi Anfinds Dr Fethen'. (2) On 24 Sep 1797 at Tjugum (11568, canvas 98 left) two girls were baptised one after the other. The first was Anna, 'Forældrene: Giest Gjestsen Esse og Christi Anfinds D.', with godparents Sieur Kieldsen Meneß, Ellend Andersen Esse, Mari Olsdatter ibid, Britha Kieldsdatter Toftedahl and Anna Marcusdatter Esse: no one from Feten or Ulvestad. The second was Giøri, 'Forældrene: Marcus Marcussen Moland og Christi Anfinds D.', with godparents Ole Moland, Endre Thorsen Fethen, Britha Endresdatter ibid [Feten], Anna Johannesdatter Ulvestad and Guri Hansdatter Moland. Britha Endresdatter of Feten is the name of the widow in the 1768 Feten probate, and Ulvestad was Anfind Andersen's home farm. The name Giøri repeats the Feten probate's youngest daughter '[Giøre?] Anfindsdatter 2'. (3) In the 1801 census Marcus Marcussen's wife Christi Anfindsdatter is 38 (Esse, household 07). That is the same age as the Feten child (6 in Aug 1768, 16 in Nov 1777). She died before 30 Apr 1813, when her probate was held (Ytre Sogn 14 fol. 538a; daughters Giøri 15 3/4 and Sigrid 9 3/4). Gjest Gjestsen's wife, by contrast, was 'Christie Anfinds Datter Tvedt' when she married in 1790, and she was still alive in 1815. The only Christi Anfindsdatter in the 1768 Feten probate and the 1777 Feten confirmation is therefore, on present evidence, the Christi who married Marcus Marcussen, not Gjest's wife. The Probable parent links to Anfind Andersen and Brite Endresdatter are therefore withdrawn. Her parents are unknown again. Kari Anfindsdatter of Tveit's probate: Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll 14, No. 28, p. 549b-550a, 13 May 1813 (Digitalarkivet 24174 view 552), read at the full viewer resolution (3327 px; the scan is bilevel, so finer crops add nothing). She was the widow of Anfind Monsen. Her heirs are her children: the eldest son Mons Anfindsen of Tvedt, who was dead and is represented by his widow Sigrid and his under-age children, 'Anfind Monsen' and a daughter '... Monsdatter'; the 2nd son Anfind Anfindsen (Grindedal); the 3rd son Sieur Anfindsen; and 'eneste Datter' (the only daughter) Anna Anfindsdatter, married to... Nielsen [Hollestad?]. There is no Christi. Taken with Anfind Monsen's probate of 1780-81 (five children, no Christi), this shows that the 1790 bride 'Christie Anfinds Datter Tvedt' was not a daughter of the Tveiten couple. She was evidently in service at Tveit in 1790. Clues to her origin: her children Ingebor, Anfind, Anna, Kari and Sjur; the godparents of Anna in 1797 included the siblings Sieur Kieldsen of Menes and Britha Kieldsdatter of Toftedal, who may be kin. ¶12a

Correction: her farm in the 1780 confirmation list reads 'Troet' (Trøet, i.e. Kalvetræet/Treet under Tjugum), not 'Tvet', and the 1790 marriage word read 'Tvedt' is better read 'Tr[ø]det', the same place; she was not in service at Tveit. Her own grade (link to Gjest and Ingebor) is unchanged. Lead (bygdebok, Balestrand 1 c102, rebuilt text, ABT): Gjest d. [1]820 and his wife ('kona') d. [1]844; the 1844-45 Ytre Ese probate after 'Christi Anfindsdatter' (A 21 fol. 293a) was shown to be Povel Olsen's wife, so her own death record is still to be found. ¶18b

Alternatives considered and set aside 4

Not her: the Ytre Sogn probate of 30 Apr-1 May 1813 after a 'Christi Anfindsdatter, Ytre Ese' (skifteprotokoll A 14, fol. 538a) concerns the wife of a cottager M[athias?] M...sen, whose heirs are daughters with his patronymic (eldest about 15 3/4). Gjest Gjestsen is not named. This fits the 1815 census, where Gjest's wife Christi Anfindsdatter, 52, is still alive. Read. ¶3

Not her either: the Ytre Sogn probate begun 17 Jun 1845 after 'Christi Anfinds Dr. Ytre Ese' (skifteprotokoll A 21, No. 4-47, fol. 293a-293b) has as heirs the sons Anfind, Lars and Johannes Paulsen (Paulsønnerne) Ese, and Christi Povelsdatter; it is the estate of Povel Olsen's wife (b. about 1777). No Gjest or Gjest's children are named. Also not her: the Leikanger burials of 'Christi Anfindsdatter, Huusbarn' at Njøs, 6 Nov 1846 (aged 2 weeks) and 7 Nov 1847 (aged 11 days) (ministerialbok 1838-1851, p. 296 no. 89 and p. 298 no. 59). Read. Her own death after 1815 is still unfound. ¶4

Ruled out: Anfind Nielsen of Vangsnes, Tjugum skipreide, probate 15 Aug 1774 (Ytre Sogn 9b p. 518, No. 14): children Niels 7 1/2, Lasse 5, Synneve 11 and Marthe; no Christi, so not her father. Other Anfind probates 1763-1800 in Ytre Sogn (Njøs [Sjøstrand], Hove, Fyli, Grinde, Mundal, Tveiten, etc.) not read. ¶6

Rival checked: the probate after Anfind Monsen of Tveiten, Tjugum skipreide (Ytre Sogn 9b p. 1255-1256, about 1780-81) names his widow Kari Anfindsdatter and 'deres sammen avlede Børn, 3 Sønner og 2 Døttre': Mons Anfindsen (of age), Anfind Anfindsen, Sjur Anfindsen 20, Anna Anfindsdatter 15 and Sigrie Anfindsdatter 11. There is no Christi, although a daughter born about 1761-63 would have been 17-20 and listed. So the 1790 bride 'Christie Anfindsdatter Tvedt' was not a daughter of the Tveiten Anfind; most likely she was in service at Tveiten (unmarried women were entered at the farm where they served). Two cautions remain: the names of her later children (Anfind, Anna, Kari, Sjur) echo the Tveiten family (Kari, Sjur), which might point to kinship with Kari Anfindsdatter; and the step Feten (1768 probate, 1777 confirmation) to Tveiten (1790) is a change of farm with no record naming her parents. Kari Anfindsdatter Tveit's own probate (Ytre Sogn protocol 14 = source 24174, p. 549b, 1809-13) would list her heirs and test this. The 1790 marriage therefore narrows the field (the bride is not the Tveiten daughter) but does not by itself tie her to Feten: her parents stay Probable. ¶11
