About the family

Parents (Probable on the parents' records): son of the dragoon Joen Olsen Hammer (d. by 11 Mar 1738) and Siri Larsdatter. The 1738 probate card (77804 image 665) lists 'Joen Joensen, 6 år' with guardian 'Joen Kleven'; Hegra B. 2 (Kleven canvas 77) has him 'frå [Hammer] 119, Stjørdal', b. 1732. His own grade (as father of his children) is unchanged. ¶9

Life story

Stjørdalsboka V 1 (1953), Kleven, as rebuilt from nb.no word boxes: John Johnsen, b. 1732; first wife Ane Larsdatter of Hofstad (b. 1731, d. 1764), children Beret 1758, John 1760, Ane 1760, Svend 1761 (d. 1762); second marriage 1765 to Lisbet Davidsdatter (b. 1744), children Svend 1766 (d. 1773), Sigrid 1768 (m. Hans), Gjertrud 1772 (m. John), Beret 1774, Lisbet 1778 (m. Lars Eriksen, Almlitrøen), David (Kleven), Mali 1782 and John 1783. A compiled history - a lead, not proof; the 1801 census says he was in his third marriage, which the book does not show. ¶3

The 1757 marriage in Stjørdal 16371, marriage section p. 322 (canvas 324 right, top): 'eodem Die et loco trolovet Ungkarl og Dragoun af Liv Compagniet Jon olsen[?] Hamer, som haver... Dragoun Qvarteret Kleven og havde Obriste-Lieutenant Møllerops Tilladelse at ægte Pigen Siri Svendsdatter Kleven'; forlovere Haagen Hamer and Baar Jonsen Øyen; 'Copuleret ved Værnes d. 30te Junij' [1757]. So the dragoon who took over the Kleven dragoon quarter and married Siri Svendsdatter Kleven came from Hammer, and a Hammer man stood surety. This is the couple of the 1761 probate (Sirie Svendsdatter, wife of Joen Joensen, Stor Kleven). Contradiction, unresolved: the groom's patronymic in the 1757 entry reads 'olsen' (lower-case o, not the J of 'Jonsen' written by the same hand in 'Baar Jonsen'), although every later record (the 1761 and 1810 probates, the 1765 marriage and the 1801 census) calls him Joen Joensen. It is probably a slip (his father was Joen Olsen), but under the audit-8 rule a noted contradiction blocks the raise until it is resolved. His own grade (as father of his children) is unchanged. It bears only on his parents' grade. also read the 1738 Hammer probate in the protocol: his guardian there was 'Joen Kleven[?], den afg. Mands Søskende Barn', a first cousin of his father. ¶10

No grade change; bears on his parents' grade only: the baptisms of his children by his first wife are read in 16371. 17 Sep 1758, Værnes: '2 Jon Klevens Barn Berethe', godparents 'Randi Hamer[?], Gunnil[?] ibid, Giertrud Kleven, Haagen Hamer, Rolluf Biørngaard' (c33 right). 15 Jun 1760, Hegre: '2 Jon Klevens barn Jon', godparents 'Ellen Biørngaard, Siri Trøytta, ole Hamer, Lars ibid, Lars Biørngaard' (c55 right; this son Jon was buried 3 Aug 1760, index pg00000007157502). 5 Aug 1761, Hegre: 'Siri Svendsdatter Kleven 27 aar' buried and, the same day, 'Jon Klevens barn Svend' baptised (c69 left). The register writes the father as 'Jon Kleven' every time, with no patronymic, so the baptisms neither confirm nor refute the 1757 'Jon olsen Hamer'. What they do show: (1) the 1757 groom is this man beyond doubt: he married 'Siri Svendsdatter Kleven' in 1757, she died at Kleven aged 27 in Aug 1761, and the Sept 1761 probate after 'Sirie Svendsdatter, Joen Joensens hustru' at Stor Kleven names their children Svend and Beret, i.e. the Berethe of 1758 and Svend of 1761; (2) the Hammer link recurs: Hammer people stand godparent at two of the three baptisms, and in 1760 they are 'ole Hamer' and 'Lars ibid', the same names as the two elder sons of the 1738 Hammer probate (Ole 12, Lars 8, besides Joen 6); this fits brothers but the register gives no patronymics or relationship. (3) The older 'Joen Joensen Hammergierdet' (betrothed 24 Aug 1743 to Siri Svend[s]d. Gedderaas, married 19 Mar 1744) is a different man: a 'Jon Hammer' had a son Haagen baptised at Hegre on Rogate (30 Apr) 1758 and his wife was churched at Pentecost 1758 (16371 c30), while our Jon was living at Kleven with Siri Svendsdatter (b. about 1734, so she cannot be the Gedderaas Siri of 1743). So the 1743 man cannot be the 1757 groom or the Kleven man. Net: the patronymic contradiction stands as a single 1757 reading ('olsen', most simply the clerk writing the father's patronymic) against the 1761 probate, the 1765 marriage ('Enkemand og Dragoun Jon Jonsen Kleven'), the 1801 census and the 1810 probate. No 'Jon Olsen' of Hammer of marrying age has been found in the 1755-66 index (57 Hammer entries; fathers on Hammer are indexed by first name only). His parents stay Probable. ¶12

First marriage: Stjør- og Verdal skifteprotokoll 5 (1760-66), s. 209b-210a (Digitalarkivet 24755 view 226; register entry 'Sirie Svendsdatter, Joen Joensens hustru, Kleven, Hegra, 1761'): 'Anno 1761 d. 2[3?] Septembr.' at Stor Kleven, probate after Sirie Svendsdatter, wife of Joen Joensen; the widower Joen Joensen and their minor children Svend Joensen and Birgit/Beret Joensdatter (ages illegible; the scan is badly degraded). So Joen married three times: Sirie Svendsdatter (d. 1761), Ane Larsdatter (d. by early 1765; probate 24755 s. 333b) and Lisbet Davidsdatter (m. 29 May 1765). This fits the 1801 census, which describes the Kleven marriage as his third. Stjørdalsboka assigns Beret (1758) and Svend (1761) to Ane Larsdatter; the probate shows they were Sirie's. No parent of Joen is named in the legible parts. ¶6

Betrothed at Værnes church on 1 or 12 March 1765 (day digits unclear) and married at Hegre church on 29 May 1765: 'Eodem Die et loco trolovet Enkemand og Dragoun Jon Jonsen Kleven, som havde holdt Skifte med sine Arvinger, og havde Sessionens Tilladelse at ægte Pigen Lisbet Davidsdatter Frøyum... Copuleret ved Hegre d. 29de May' (Stjørdal ministerialbok 1755-1780, source 16371, marriage section, p. 345, IIIF canvas 347 right). He was a widower and a dragoon, had settled the probate (skifte) of his late wife with her heirs, and had the military session's leave to marry; sureties (forlovere) Haagen Hemer and Ole[?] Hemer. This confirms Stjørdalsboka's second marriage in 1765 and makes Lisbet his 2nd wife. The 1801 census's '3rd marriage' for him is unexplained (no further marriage of his found 1765-66); a different Joen Joensen Ree, widower, married the widow Ingebor Jonsdatter in 1765 (p. 347) and is not him. ¶4

Farmer at Storkleven (Kleven Stor), Stjørdal. 1801 census: 69, in his 3rd marriage, with wife Lisbet Davidsdatter 55; daughters Beret 26 and Beret 23 (from an earlier marriage); and children of both, David 20, Mali 18 and Joen 17. Possibly the 'Joen, Kleven' whose daughter Lisbet was baptized 20 Sep 1778. ¶1

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 3

Confidence raised from Speculative to Probable as father of Lisbet Johnsdatter: the 1778 baptism names the father as Joen of Kleven; Stjørdalsboka V 1 (Kleven section) gives John Johnsen (b. 1732, skjøte on Kleven) a daughter 'Lisbet, 1778', married to Lars Eriksen of Almlitrøen; and in 1808 Lisbet Joensdatter was of Storkleven with 'Joen Kleven' as godfather of her son Svend. The only other Kleven family in the book, that of Håkon Olsen, has a Lisbet born 1783, not 1778. A probate (skifte) after Joen naming his heirs would make it Confirmed. ¶2

Probable burial (per index, Hegre parish p. 195): buried 22 Jan 1804, 'Joen Joensen', gaardmand, residence 'Stor' (read as Storkleven by inference; not confirmed), aged 72, so born about 1731–32, which fits. A rival index row, 'John Johnsen' of Eidom, buried 28 Jun 1813 aged 81 (pg00000001361611), is on another farm and is not taken as his. His first wife Ane Larsdatter was buried at Kleven on 18 Aug 1763 (index pg00000007157871). Found; the 1804 record page has not been opened. ¶5

Upgrade: Stjør- og Verdal skifteprotokoll 19, s. 247b-248 (Digitalarkivet 57508 view 263): skifte at Stjørdalshalsen in early December 1810 (day read as the 3rd, uncertain) in the 'opgivende Boe' of Lisbet Davidsdatter of Kleven - a widow handing her estate over to her heirs in her lifetime. The heirs are listed as the children she had 'med førhen afdøde Mand John Johnsen Kleven' (with her late husband John Johnsen Kleven): 1. David Johnsen Kleven, of age, living on Kleven; 2. Joen Johnsen, of age; 3. Sigrie Joensdatter, dead, who left seven children by her husband Hans Olsen Forsen (Mallie 16, Sigrie 14, Ole 13, Joen 11, Lisbet 9, Svend 7, Ole 1½); 4. Giertrud Joensdatter, married to Joen Hafstad in Verdalen; 5. Berethe Joensdatter, married to Ole Pedersen Moe[?]; 6. Lisbeth Joensdatter, married to the cottager (huusmand) Lars Erichsen Kleven; 7. Mallie Joensdatter, married to Mads Andersen Forbregd[?]. The record calls him the late husband ('førhen afdøde Mand') of Lisbet Davidsdatter and father of Lisbeth Joensdatter, wife of the cottager Lars Erichsen, so he is Confirmed as Lisbet Johnsdatter's father. It also fits (but does not itself prove) the index burial of a Joen Joensen, 72, gaardmand, on 22 Jan 1804. His own parents remain unknown. ¶7

Records that disagree 1

The 1757 groom is re-read at native resolution as 'Jon olsen Hamer' (16371 c324), so the patronymic contradiction stands and his parents stay Probable (see Joen Olsen). ¶11a

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Caution: another 'Joen Joensen Hammergierdet' (a cotter at lille Lund under Hammer) was betrothed on 24 Aug 1743 to a Siri, possibly 'Svendsd.', from Gedderaas (16370 c261). He was adult in 1743, so he is not this man (b. about 1732), but records naming 'Joen Joensen Hammer' between 1743 and 1760 must be checked against both. ¶11b

Searches that found nothing 1

Negative: the two Kleven farm pages of the Stjørdal tinglag panteregister 1762-1800 (22318 image 144 right, N:o 219 Kleven, blank; image 147, N:o 326 Kleven) list only an auction deed to Lars Andersen (B fol. 437), an auction deed from Resch to Major Christian Fredrik Meyer (C fol. 342), and Major Meyer's leases to Haagen Larsen and Isach Pedersen (C fol. 368). Nothing in Joen Joensen's name, so he held his part of Kleven without a registered deed or lease, and no land record names his parents. Grade unchanged. ¶8
