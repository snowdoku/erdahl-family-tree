About the family

Her eldest daughter was named Gunhild Maria (1841), after Gunhild Frantsdatter rather than after Paul's mother Ingeborg. That suggests Gunhild was a close relative or foster mother of Tørri, possibly her mother or aunt (Speculative). ¶4

Her brothers and sisters, as far as they can be traced: Esten, born 28 Aug 1812 (father then at Hoset); Barbro or Berit, born 15 Apr 1815 at Kongshaug, her full sister by the same mother; and two much younger half-sisters by her father's later partner Ragnild Antonsdatter of Dale, Berit Guri (1834) and Ingeborg (1836). ¶8

Son's death certificate: the Minnesota death certificate of her son Peder O. Tungseth (died Clay County, Minnesota, 2 Jan 1915; FamilySearch index, page 100, certificate 2539) names Peder's mother as 'Torri Jakobsdatter', born in Norway, independently corroborating the parent link already Confirmed from his 1853 baptism. ¶10

Life story

Her birth year is given as 1817 in the 1865 and 1875 censuses and as 1818 in the 1838 marriage and 1891 census. The 1875 census says she was born in Kornstad sokn. ¶2

In 1825, age 8, living at Tungset with Knud Andersen Tungset and Gunhild Frantsdatter, apparently as foster child or heir; Gunhild was still living with Paul and Tørri in 1865 as a retired widow. The farm probably came into the family through Tørri (speculative). ¶1

In 1825, age 8, she lived at Tungset with Knud Andersen (41), Gunhild Frantsdatter (40) and Karig Frantsdatter (64). By 1865 Gunhild was a widowed retiree (føderaadskone, 81) living with Paul and Tørri on Tungset nedre, which fits Paul and Tørri having taken over the farm from Knud and Gunhild. ¶5

Baptism found in the scanned Kornstad parish book 1789-1819 (Digitalarkivet source 16097, births 1818, p. 389). She was born 20 Apr 1818 and baptized 26 Apr 1818, daughter of Jacob Estensen Kongshaug and his wife Marith Gorrisdatter. The book's spelling of the mother's patronymic is uncertain; the 1815 index reads it as 'Orisd.' This baptism is not in the online index. ¶6

In the 1825 census, when Tørri was fostered at Tungset, her sister Berit was living at Kongshaug in the household of a man the transcribers could read only as '... Esstensen', aged 33, with his wife Kristine Nielsdatter. Jacob's children were therefore split between two households by 1825, which supports the reading that their mother Marith had died or the couple had separated by then. ¶9

Death found in the scanned Kornstad parish book 1885-1900 (source 5311), burials 1893, women no. 12. 'Tørri Jakobsdtr., g[ift]' died 20 Jun 1893 and was buried 26 Jun 1893 (committal 22 Oct 1893). Husband: 'Kaarmand Paul Ellingsen'. She was born 1818 at Kongshaug and lived at Tungset. No doctor was called. This independently confirms her birthplace and father's farm. ¶7

Death register (scan read): the Nordmøre probate court's death register (dødsfallsprotokoll no. 3, p. 127, Kvernes 2nd quarter 1893, no. 71) records 'Tørri Jakobsdtr. Tungset', kaark[one] (a wife on a farm retirement pension), aged 75. She died 20 Jun 1893 and the death was reported on the 25th. Her surviving spouse was Paul Ellingsen. The heirs were '6 myndige Børn, hvoraf 1 i Amerika' (6 adult children, 1 of them in America), and the remark is 'Efterlader Intet' (she left nothing, so there was no estate to divide). The children are not named. Age 75 fits her 1818 baptism. ¶11
