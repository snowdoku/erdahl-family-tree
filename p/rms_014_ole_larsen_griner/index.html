Life story

Gran marriages Sep 1737 - Sep 1739 read on the scan (DA 9340 canvases 163-167, pp. 171-175; c163 also covers Sep-Dec 1737 and Jan-Mar 1738): no betrothal of an Ole Larsen with a Kirsti Olsdatter (canvases 165-167 read at reduced resolution, so a soft negative). With (1737 p.169b, 1739-40 c168-169), the Gran marriage scans 1737-1740 are now covered. The pantebok 8 register (DA 266260 p. 10) lists 'Grini, gnr. 233.234' (bygselseddel 1766, fol. 459b): Griner is the matrikkel farm Grini, gnr. 233-234, distinct from Grinåker gnr. 109-110. The Hadeland probate cards for Grini may be filed under 'Grini' rather than in the Grindaaker run. ¶5a

Gran marriage scans 1739-1740 (DA 9340 canvases 168-169, pp. 175-177) read: no Ole Larsen with a Kirsti Olsdatter. The only Ole Larsen is 'Soldat Ole Larsen Sætter[?]' betrothed 8 Nov 1739 to a [Kirsten?] Johansdatter (not our couple). Ole Andersen Grinager and Hans Aagesen Grinager appear as forlovere in Dec 1739 and 1740, so Grinager (Griner) had several households. Earlier years (1735-38, canvases 163-167) not read. ¶4

Lived at Griner (Grini) in Gran parish, Hadeland, when his daughter Eli by Kirsti Olsdatter was baptised at Gran on 5 Mar 1741 (Gran church book 1732-1744, fol. 124v, read on the scan). Probable as the father of Eli Olsdatter of Griner, who married Nils Pedersen of Fallang in 1764: see the note on Eli. His other children, birth, parents and death have not been searched; the Gran index 1730-1744 is the next place to look (search related_first_name=Ole, domicile Griner). ¶1

Second child: Ole, son of Ole Larsen of Griner and Kirsti Olsdatter, was baptised at Gran on 7 May 1744 (Ascension Day; p. 151). Godparents Nielss Smedsrud, Jenss Olsen Hoftved, Ole Hansen Mortved, Gubiør Gunmundsdatter Dal and Kari Hansdatter Aasserud. ¶2

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
