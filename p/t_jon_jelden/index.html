Tron (Thron) Jonsen JeldenProbable

Life story

Known from his son's patronymic. His son Tron Jonsen ('Thron Joensøn') was living at Jelden in the Eid part of Grytten parish, Romsdal, when he was betrothed in 1751 and married on 4 Apr 1752, so Jon was probably born in the first quarter of the 1700s in Grytten or Eid. ¶1

Living at Jelden in October 1729 (scan read): the Grytten register, p. 26, records the baptism on 16 Oct 1729 at Eid church of 'Jon Jeldens barn kaldet Troen' (Jon Jelden's child called Troen), probably his son Tron Jonsen. His wife is not named in the entry. The godparents were Peder Lerem, the cottager Peder of Lerem, Peder Strømme, Ingebor Ora and Birgitthe Lerem, so the family had ties to the farm Lerem. A father of a child in 1729 was probably born before about 1705, earlier than the first-quarter-1700s estimate. ¶4

A possible second son: Halvor Joensøn of Mættit stood first godfather at the baptism of Tron's son Joen on 28 Dec 1751. First-named godparents are usually close kin, and a Halvor Jonsen would be a brother of Tron Jonsen. This is a suggestion from the record, not a proven link. ¶2

He is most likely the father of the eldest son's name as well: Tron Jonsen's son was named Joen (Jon), and that grandson in turn named his own son Jon - the standard Norwegian pattern of naming the eldest son after the paternal grandfather, repeated over three generations in this family. ¶3

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
