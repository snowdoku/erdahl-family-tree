Life story

Probable origin: on 8 Oct 1745 Tommes Ulbrichsen of Fodnes nordre in Aurdal divided his estate in his lifetime (Hadeland, Land og Valdres skifteprotokoll 1744-52, fol. 69a). The children were Ulbrich 16 (reading uncertain, possibly 10), Johannes 8, Barbro 18, Marit 16, Sigri 14 and Rangdie 12. Same farm and patronymic, and his eldest son Thommes carries the name. The birth year c.1729 rests on the uncertain age of 16. ¶3

Marriage read: betrothed at Svenes 11 Aug 1755 and married 13 Oct 1755 to 'Aaste Helgesdatter Strand' (Aurdal church book DA 9361 p. 100, canvas 319, left page, last entry; groom 'Ulrich Thommesøn Fodnes'). ¶5

Probate (index card,; read on the image by the orchestrator): 'Fodnes nordre i Svenes, Nord Aurdal', Valdres skifteprotokoll 1810-1814, fol. 306b. 'Uldrich Thommesen Fodnes +, g.m. Aaste Helgesdtr. +' died 21 Jul 1812, and the probate closed 16 Jan 1813. 'Barn' (children): 1. Thommes; 2. Helge; 3. Erich; 4. 'Kirstie enke efter Juel Olsen Midstrand'. Gross 1010-.-4, net 769-3-10. ¶1

Corrections 1

Correction: born about late 1735, not about 1729. His mother's estate of 21 Jun 1740 gives him as 'Uldrick 4 1/2 aar' (card 3247), the 1801 census as 'Uldrik Tommesen', b. 1735, on føderaad at Fodnes, and the 1745 lifetime-division card, re-read at full size, gives his age as 10 rather than 16. Mother: Kiersti Johansdatter (d. before 21 Jun 1740), named on that card as the wife of Tommes Uldrichsen. ¶4
