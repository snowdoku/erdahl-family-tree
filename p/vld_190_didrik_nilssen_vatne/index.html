How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the identification · 1 open question

The case for this identification 1

Supporting pattern: Paul of Indre Hildre named his son Didrik (Dirick, b. 1665 in the 1666 census; the bygdebok's child no. 5, 'Didrik, ca 1665, d. før 7.1.1707'), the usual naming after the paternal grandfather. Supporting only. ¶2

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Test record: the 1657 kvegskatt or 1645/1647 tax lists for Vatne (Sunnmøre, not indexed) showing Didrik Nilssen or his widow Ingeborg; a skifte after Didrik (c. 1657, before the surviving Sunnmøre protocols, which begin 1689) does not survive, so a tingbok entry on the Indre Hildre odel that Paul inherited from Ingeborg (the bygdebok mentions a dispute at 'tinget' about 1652) would be the direct test. The 1666 census at Indre Hildre ('Poffuel', 50, son Dirick b. 1665) is consistent but does not name Paul's parents. ¶3
