About the family

Widow of Børu (Børuf) Aadnesen of Jørundland at the probate of 11 Oct 1728 (Østre Råbyggelag Skifteprotokoll 8 fol. 21; index cards 5997-5999): 'Børu Aadnesön + g.m. Anna Vraalsdtr.' Children named: Aadne Børusön 14, Tellef 11, Ole 7, Asbør 22, Ingeborg 19, Zigne 3. Her brother: Giermund Vraalsen Omland. Her half-brother: Bendix Evindsøn Miaaland (son of her mother's 2nd marriage to Evind Evindsen of Jørundland, probate 1704-05, card 5981). The archivist's note gives her father as Wraal Bendixsen Aamland (+ probate 1687) and mother Ziri Olsdtr. (Olufsdtr.) Aamland (+ probate 1688), so she was born by 1688. Her own death/probate not yet looked up (Jørundland cards after 5999). ¶1
