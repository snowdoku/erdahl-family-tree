About the family

Parentage (Probable): Alv Rasmussen was the only man of that name at Blindem in the eighteenth century. He was baptized in 1712, farmed at Blindem all his life, married as a widower in July 1742, and had children baptized there in 1742, 1745, 1747, 1751, 1754 and 1757. A son Rasmus born about 1746 fits into that sequence - the gap between the daughters Anne (March 1745) and Martha (December 1747) - and the patronymic and the farm both agree. No other candidate exists. ¶3

His father died in 1778 and his father's estate was registered on 25 November 1778; his own death twelve years later, at only 44, left his daughter Anne Martha, then about 14, an orphan on the father's side. ¶5

His daughter's baptism (24 Mar 1776, read on the scan) has as first godfather 'M: Alv Rasmuss: ibm' - Alv Rasmussen of Blindem, his father - and as second the young man Anders Olsen Uggedal, who carries the patronymic and farm of Alv's wife Martha Olsdatter Uggedal (perhaps her brother; unproved). ¶8

Life story

1762 extra tax list (DA 49637 view 12, p. 11): listed as 'Rasmus', son over 12, in the Blindem household of Alv Rasmussen and his wife Marthe Olsdatter (with his sister Anne). This is the record that names Martha Olsdatter as his mother (see Martha Olsdatter Uggedal). ¶11

Of Blindem, Borgund. Named as father of Anne Martha, baptized 24 Mar 1776. Not traced further. ¶1

Probate: the 1778 probate after Alv Rasmussen of Blindem (3A 26, fol. 193a) names as first heir 'Søn Rasmus Alfsen myndig og boende paa Blindem': the son Rasmus Alvsen, of age and living at Blindem. That legible line names his father in a primary record, so the confidence is raised from Probable to Confirmed for him and his father link. His mother is not named there: the widow in 1778 was Inger Andersdatter, and his descent from Martha Olsdatter still rests on his birth about 1746 falling inside Alv's 1742 marriage to her. ¶6

Engagement read on the scan (13 Oct 2026): Norddal, 'd. 1ste Julii [Dom. 3tia?] p. Trin. Rasmus Alvsen Blinden og Kari Rasmusd: Schmauge. T. Peder Alstad og Ener Larsen Nacken' - engaged 1 Jul 1775 to Kari Rasmusdatter of Schmauge, with sureties Peder Alstad and Ener Larsen Nacken. The wedding date in the margin is illegible; no ages or fathers are given. Blinden is the clerk's spelling of Blindem/Blindheim in Borgund. ¶7

His own record is now filled in. 'Rasmus Alvss.', 'Grdm.' (gaardmand, farm holder) of Blindem, aged 44, was buried at Borgund on 1 January 1790, which puts his birth about 1746. He was therefore about 30 when his daughter Anne Martha was baptized in 1776. ¶2

His widow was Kari Rasmusdatter, who married Jon Olsen of Blindem and died before 8 Sep 1798; her probate names Anna Martha Rasmusdatter, 24, as her daughter by her former marriage. So he did not marry again after Kari, and the widow Anne Olsdatter of Blindem who married Ole Larsen of Blindem in 1790 (Borgund copulerede 1790 p. 88, read on the scan; no first husband named) was another man's widow. His burial 1 Jan 1790, age 44, is an index fact (pg00000002551124, card in vld_027). ¶9

Probate read on the scan (13 Oct 2026): his estate was registered at Blindem on 30 Jan 1790, four weeks after his burial, between 'Enken Kari Rasmusd.' (her guardian possibly Ole [?]sen of Blindem; faded) and 'den Afdødes [eeniste? avlede?] Datter Anne Martha Rasmusd. gl. 14 Aar med Formynder Willum Olsen Blindem'. The appraisers were Rasmus Arnesen of Spilchevig giære and Poul Poulsen of Nedre Spilkevig. The ink is faded and some small words are lost, but only one child is named. This proves that Kari Rasmusdatter was his widow and Anne Martha his daughter. Her guardian, Willum Olsen Blindem, is the same man who was her guardian at her mother's probate in 1798. ¶10

The case for this identification 1

Supporting detail from the naming pattern: Alv Rasmussen's second wife was Martha Olsdatter and his daughters were Anne (1745) and Martha (1747). Rasmus named his own daughter, baptized 1776, 'Anne Martha' - the two names together. ¶4
