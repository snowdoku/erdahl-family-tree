Life story

Bygdebok (Selbu i fortid og nåtid vol. 3, Fuglem canvases 216-217): '-1661-1666- Per [Bersvendsen], f. ca. 1617, d. 1701. Barn: 1. [...] død; 2. Per, f. ca. 1651; 3. Ola, f. ca. 1654; 4. Ola, f. 1658', then '1701-1711 Per Persen, f. ca. 1651, d. 1715, kona Ingeborg'. The book's birth year (ca. 1651) differs from 1646 (1701 age 55); his burial 1715 'aet. 72' implies about 1643. His mother's name is not in the recovered words. ¶7

1664 and 1666 manntall: 'Peder Pedersen 16 Aar', son of Peder Bersvendsen (52) at Fuglem (35528 v21 L; 35578 v18 R) - born about 1648-50 by these lists, about 1643-46 by his 1701 and burial ages. Brothers Ole Pedersen (12) and a second Ole (7). ¶5

1701 manntall, Selbu, farm Fuglum (canvas 112): farmer Peder Pedersen, aged 55 (so born about 1646); sons Bersvend Pedersen 15 and 'Olle' 12. The neighbouring Fuglum holding had a servant Siur Pedersen, soldier, 22 - possibly another son. ¶1

Taken as father of Ole Pedersen Fuglem (rated Probable): Ole aged 12 in 1701 is the only Ole at Fuglem; Ole Pedersen Fuglem named his sons Peder (after this man) and Bersvend (after his brother Bersvend Pedersen of 1701). ¶2a

Probable burial: Pentecost (9 Jun) 1715, 'begraffuet Jon Lechvold[?] ætatis 52[?] item Peder Fuglem æt. 72' (16310 canvas 58 right) - aged 72, so born about 1643, close to the 1701 census age 55 (born about 1646). Identification by name and farm. He was godfather at Fuglem in 1699 (c4 R). ¶4

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 record that disagrees · 2 open questions

Records that disagree 1

Against: Ole's burial in 1773 gives his age as 77 (born about 1696), seven years younger than the census implies (born about 1689); elderly burial ages in this register are often approximate. ¶2b

Open questions and the next record to read 2

What would confirm it: a Stjør- og Verdal probate after Peder Pedersen Fuglem (scanned series 24749-24764) naming his heirs; his burial in 16310 with an age. ¶3

Biography gaps: his exact birth date (c.1646) predates the 1699 Selbu register, and his marriage and wife are not named in any record read. His burial rests on a probable Pentecost 1715 entry (16310 canvas 58 R), matched by name and farm, so a full-resolution check of that entry would confirm it. His probate is not found: the 77804 card index (1689-1742) is only partly sampled, so its gap list (cards.md) is the next check, then the 1702-26 protocols (series not yet confirmed). ¶6
