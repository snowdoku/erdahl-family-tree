Life story

Anne Collis, daughter of William Collis, gent., of Liseaige (Lissodigue, near Tralee), Co. Kerry. She married Samuel Bennett junior, son and heir apparent of Samuel Bennett senior of Coss, Co. Limerick, under a marriage settlement of 13 Feb 1708/9 (Registry of Deeds memorial 420). She was the 'Ann now wife of' Samuel Bennett the younger of Ballincallow, a life in his 1713 lease (memorial 10967). The Blennerhassett Pedigree (about 1733-36) calls her 'first daughter' of William Collis and Mary Cross, and the widow of Samuel Bennett of Ballincollon, with three sons and four daughters. ¶1

(no grade change; graded on her link to Mary Bennett). Memorial 420, read on the image, calls her 'Anne Collis Eldest Daughter of the said William Collis'; the abstract said only 'daughter'. The marriage was 'soon to be had' in February 1708/9, with a £300 portion and a jointure on lands in Ballingarry parish, Co. Limerick. Her link to Mary still rests on the pedigree. ¶3

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the grade · 1 open question

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Probable, graded on her link to her daughter Mary Bennett: the pedigree names Mary as 'eldest daughter of Samuel and Anne'. It is Probable for the same reason as her husband: the place name 'Ballindall' has to be read as Ballyneal. ¶2a

Test record: the Ballingarry baptisms of 1733 and 1735, or a Bennett of Ballincollow will naming Mary Creed. Added. ¶2b
