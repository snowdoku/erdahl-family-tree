Life story

Death: Michael Bom died at Beresina on 2 Oct 1822 aged 60, so born about 1762 (Drefs death extract, film 1198706 no.28). No wife or origin is printed in the extract. ¶1

Origin lead (1 Nov 2026): his children's birthplaces 'Miliski/Milisky' (1834 census) and 'Lenschuetz' (son Christoph, 1837 marriage) point to the old Łęczyca (Lentschütz) district of central Poland. Other Kulm-group settlers give 'Milask', 'Miliske' and 'Mileski' as their birthplace in the Consistory death extracts. Mileszki, near Łódź, is a candidate village but is not verified. See the notes on his daughter Rosina (see her page). ¶5

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

2 on the grade · 2 alternatives set aside · 1 search with no result · 1 open question

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Speculative link: entered as the father of Rosina Bomm (see her page). Her 1835 marriage entry names her father as the late Michael (FHL 1882645/2 p.1 no.131); this Michael Bom is the only one in the 1815-32 Beresina deaths and was dead by 1835. The identification is inferred, not stated. Christoph Bomm, 'son of the late Michael' at his Beresina marriage of 15 Aug 1837, and Christoph's brother Martin, both from Milisky, Poland (1834 census; Christoph's 1837 marriage instead gives his birthplace as Lenschuetz), were probably also his children, which places the family's origin at Miliski/Milisky, Poland. ¶2a

Upgrade: Speculative to Probable (1 Nov 2026), as the father of Rosina Bomm. Three index-level facts point to one man: (1) Rosina's marriage at Tarutino on 1 Jan 1835 calls her 'daughter of the late Michael', née Bomm, born in Poland (FHL 1882645/2 p.1 no.131); (2) Christoph Bomm's marriage at Beresina on 15 Aug 1837 calls him 'son of the late Michael' (FHL 1882647/3 p.49 no.107), and the 1834 Beresina census gives Christoph (b. 1802) and his brother Martin (b. 1805) the same birthplace, Milisky, Poland, as Rosina (b. 1794, Miliski); (3) Michael Bom, who died at Beresina on 2 Oct 1822 aged 60 (film 1198706 no.28), is the only Michael Bom, and the only adult male Bom, in the Bessarabian deaths of 1815-32, and his age fits the father of children born 1794-1805. A Michael Bomm who was the father of a Beresina woman and dead by 1835 is therefore very probably this man. It stays short of Confirmed: no register image read, and the 1822 death extract names no wife or children (image FHL 1198706 entry 28, queued S0175). ¶3

Alternatives considered and set aside 2

Lead, not entered: the 1834 Beresina census lists, among the landless, a widow Elisabetha Falk, born 1773 at 'Glokau, Prussia', 'living in the house of Christoph Bom'. Her age would fit the mother of Rosina (1794), Christoph (1802) and Martin (1805), remarried after Michael's death in 1822, but no record links her to Michael Bom; no Falk-Bom marriage is in the 1815-32 Bessarabian marriage extracts (odessa3 early/link/marriage.txt). Rosina's mother remains unknown. ¶4

Sister-in-law or relative, ruled out again: Katharina Schramm née Bomm (d. Beresina 23 Apr 1837 aged 44) was born at 'Schlowig, Poland', not Miliski (FHL 1882647/3 p.119 no.56; WikiTree Bomm-11 repeats this). This matches reading. No link to Michael is shown. ¶7

Searches that found nothing 1

Negative: Geneteka has a marriage at Łęczyca on 12 Feb 1804 of Michał 'Bomowski?' to the widow Elżbieta Schuberthin, aged 32. The groom was a soldier of the Oldenburg Legion aged 29, so born about 1775, and is not this Michael (aged 60 in 1822, so born about 1762). Ruled out. A Michael Bom baptised at Wałdowo (West Prussia) in 1759, son of Martinus, is the right age but in the wrong region, given the Łęczyca evidence. Not pursued. ¶6
