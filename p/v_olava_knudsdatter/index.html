About the family

Wife of Jon Tronsen at Sætnes in the 1801 census (age 50). She is a speculative mother of John Johnsen Sætnes. ¶1

Mother of Søren (1790) and Joen (1792) at Sætnes-Ødegård. Married Jon Tronsen 7 Dec 1782 at Grytten. ¶2

Her mother is now identified (Probable) as Elri Sørensdatter, the bride whom Knud Gundersøn of Lien married at Grytten on 28 Oct 1751, seven and a half months before Olava's baptism on 11 Jun 1752. ¶4

Life story

Baptism (Probable): Olava Knudsdatter, daughter of Knud of Lien 'af Wolds Sogn', baptized 11 Jun 1752 at Grytten. An Olava Knudsdatter of Lien, born 1752, was confirmed in 1769. She was 50 in the 1801 census, which fits. Date given is the baptism date. Her death is not found. ¶3

Marriage re-checked (13 Oct 2026): the Grytten marriage register 1764-1806, p. 220, has the marriage on 7 Dec 1782; the bride is indexed as 'Olava Knutsd.' (Knutsdatter, a spelling of Knudsdatter). The date agrees with the tree. ¶7

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Confidence lowered from Probable to Speculative (audit, 13 Oct 2026). The records about this person stand, but her identification as the mother of John Johnsen Sætnes is now Speculative: see the note of the same date on John Johnsen Sætnes, which names the records (Grytten confirmation 19 Oct 1834, p. 495 no. 5, 'Anne Pedersd. fra Lesjøe'; Lesja marriage 13 Mar 1816, pp. 834-835; Grytten marriage of the soldier Jon Jonsen Setnes, 15 Oct 1816, p. 613). ¶5

Confidence adjusted: Speculative restored to Probable (13 Oct 2026). The lowering by audit expressed doubt about whether John Johnsen Sætnes is her son; that doubt now sits on John Johnsen Sætnes (Speculative). Her own level rests on her link to Knud Gundersen of Lien and Elri Sørensdatter: the Grytten baptism of Olava, daughter of Knud of Lien, 11 Jun 1752, with an Olava Knudsdatter of Lien, born 1752, confirmed in 1769 and her 1801 census age of 50 fitting. That remains a Probable identification (no record names her parents together with her husband). ¶6

Searches that found nothing 1

Burial not found (13 Oct 2026): the Grytten burial index (indexed from 1818) for 1790-1870 has no Olava Knudsdatter (0 rows), and none of the 6 Olava burials of any surname carries a Knud or Knuts patronymic. Near miss, not linked: Olava Hansdtr. Setnæs, buried 21 Apr 1828 at Grytten, age 77 (born about 1751), a lægdslem (a pauper boarded round the farms). Setnæs is the Sætnes farm, but her patronymic is Hans, not Knud, so she is not this Olava without a parentage check. ¶8
