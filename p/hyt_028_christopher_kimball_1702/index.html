About the family

Family (Speculative): brother of the Abigail born in Boston in 1703/4. He is recorded because he helps fix the family. His own birth record is primary; he is Speculative only because the link of this family to our Abigail Kimball (wife of Job Prince) is, and that link would be raised by a Plymouth County deed or probate record for Christopher or Sarah Kimball about 1704-1725. Nothing further is known of him. ¶3

Life story

Birth: 'Christopher Son of Christopher Kemball and Sarah his Wife, [born] 2 September 1702' (Boston Births 1700-1800, p. 15, under 'Births - 1702', page image read). This is the son, not his father Christopher Kimball (who married Sarah Joles in 1701 and whose birth is unknown). ¶1

How sure are we?

Speculative. A plausible candidate only. Treat this link as a question, not a fact.

1 correction

Corrections 1

Correction: the 1897 Kimball Family in America (vol. 1 p. 54) gives his birth as 'Dec. 2, 1702'. The Boston town record, read on the page image, says 2 September 1702; the book is wrong on this date. ¶2
