Life story

Baptised 26 May 1828, entered at page 36 serial 48 of the Selbu register 1824-1842, mother Beret Olsdatter of Aunet. RAGNILD is the key name in this family: it is the name of Rangnil Jonsdatter of Slinden, who on the reading taken here was her maternal grandmother. See the note on Beret Olsdatter. ¶1

A brother or sister of Ole Gundersen Slind, born 10 December 1816, who was Oline Slind's grandfather. ¶2
