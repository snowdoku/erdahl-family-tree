Life story

1825 census: residence 0047* Langlie, household 01, person 016 - 'John Johnsen', 3, 'Soen', listed directly after Brynhild Johnsdatter (8) in the family group of John Nilsen (42) and Marit Johnsdatter (42). His patronymic Johnsen and his place in the list make him their son; the head of the household is his grandmother, the widow Guri Olsdatter. Birth year about 1822 is calculated from the census age, and the ages in this household run one to two years high. ¶1

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the grade

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Rated Probable: the census states 'Soen' but the household head is his grandmother, so the parentage rests on his patronymic and his position after John and Marit. His baptism, about 1822-1824 in the indexed Selbu register 1816-1824, would confirm it. Later life not searched; a husmand Jon Olsen, 44, at Langli in 1865 is not he (different patronymic). ¶2
