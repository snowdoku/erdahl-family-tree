About the family

Wife: Alva Moore, born 1 October 1894 and died 6 February 1973, is buried beside him at New Glasgow. They married on 23 December 1908. The PEI marriage index gives that date, which would make her 14, so the 1894 date should be checked. 'Wake' recurs as a middle name in the family: William Henry's son Thomas Wake Hayter was born in 1908. It may preserve the surname of a grandparent, which is a lead to test. ¶4

Life story

Born February 1883 (1901 census), PEI. At home in 1891 and 1901. Married Alva on 23 December 1908 (PEI marriages index). By 1921 the Rev. Joseph Hayter, a clergyman at Milton East, Queens County, Nova Scotia, with wife Alva and children Lois (b. 1918) and James (b. 1920), both born PEI. ¶1

Full name and death: His full name was Joseph Wake Hayter. His gravestone in New Glasgow Cemetery, Queens County, gives 6 February 1883 to 3 April 1948 (Find a Grave memorial 262244978). The Brooks diary records 'Hayter, Rev Joseph W. | 67 | 03-Apr-48 | Charlottetown'. He was a clergyman (Baptist, like his family). In 1921 he was at Milton East, Queens County, Nova Scotia, with his wife Alva and children Ruth (b. about 1913 in NS), Lucile (b. about 1916 in Ontario), Lowis (b. about 1918 in PEI) and James (b. about 1920 in PEI). ¶3

Death: 'Hayter, Rev Joseph W., 67, 3 April 1948, Charlottetown' (death list in the diary of Lauretta Brooks of Murray Harbour). ¶2
