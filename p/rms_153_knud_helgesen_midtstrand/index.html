Life story

Born before about 1680: in Feb 1701 he was 'myndig' (of age) and present at his mother's probate, and claimed pay for his work on the farm. Alive 13 Oct 1727; his own probate is not in the Midtstrand cards (9283-9301). ¶3

His own parents (Probable): see Helge Gulbrandsen. ¶4a

Farmer at Midtstrand (Mitstrand), Svenes. Married 18 Jan 1702 Ragnild Biørnsdatter (Aurdal church book DA 9360, index pv00000000706841: 'Knud Heljes.' of Strand and 'Ragnild Biørnsd.'). At her probate, 9 Oct 1721 (card 9283; DA 25285 fol. 479a, view 488, right page, read), the court divided the estate after 'afgangne S: Quinde Ragnild Biørnsdatter... imellem hendes efterladte og den Igienlevende Egtemand Knud Helgesen [og] begge deris sammen auflede og i Live værende 4re Børn, hvor af er 1 Søn og 3de Døttre, Sønnen med Nafn Helge Knudsen 12 aar gammel', then Marit 17, Ingebiør 8 and Gertrud 6 (card). On 13 Oct 1727 (card 9287; DA 25285 fol. 900b, view 917, read) a second skifte was held at Mitstrand at the request of the 'Enkemand Knud Helgesen', who 'formedelst Alderdom og Skrøbelighed' (old age and frailty) could no longer run the farm, for his '4 Børn... 1 Søn og 3 Døttre, Sønnen ved Nafn Helge Knudsen 18 aar gl., 2den Datteren Mary Knudsdatter gift med Biørn Knudsen', then Ingebør 14 and Giertru 12; odelsgods 9 skind. ¶1

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade:, new person entered as Confirmed, graded on his link to his son Helge Knudsen of Midtstrand (see his page). The 1721 probate names 'begge deris sammen auflede' children, 'Sønnen... Helge Knudsen 12 aar', with Knud Helgesen as the surviving husband; the 1727 skifte, on the same farm, again names 'Sønnen... Helge Knudsen 18 aar' and hands the farm over in Knud's old age. Continuity: Helge Knudsen's own probate at Midtstrand, 11 Oct 1739 (DA 25286 p. 279; card 9297), is 12 years after 1727, on the same farm; no other Helge Knudsen is named at Midtstrand (the Midtstrand cards 9283-9301 have none), so no rival. ¶2

Open questions and the next record to read 1
