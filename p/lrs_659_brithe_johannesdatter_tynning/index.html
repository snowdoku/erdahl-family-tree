Life story

Earliest record of her: as 'Berthe', mother of Jørgen, son of Jens of Tynning, baptised at Brekke in April 1743 (Gulen 11441, canvas 28 right). That son died before 1772. Her marriage to Jens is not among the Brekke, Bø and Lavik betrothals of Aug 1742 to Jun 1743 (11441 canvases 26-28), nor among the Eivindvik betrothals of Sep 1742 to Jan 1743 (Gulen ministerialbok 11442, canvases 35-36). So it was held before Aug 1742 (11441 canvases 0-25 and 11442 canvases 0-34 for 1736-42 remain unread for it) or in her home parish, which is not yet known. Her farm of origin is therefore still unknown, and a farm-based probate search for her father Johannes cannot yet be made. ¶2a

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Graded on her link to Johannes Jensen. Widow of Jens Jørgensen of Tynning in his probate of 13 Mar 1772 (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll A 9, pp. 363-364, Digitalarkivet 24167 viewers 184-185): the division is between 'hans efterlatte Enke' and 'deres udi et kiærligt Ægteskab tilsammen avlede' children (Johannes, Rognald, Lasse, Agaathe), which names her as the mother of all four (audit 8, ruling 2); on p. 364 'Enken Brithe Johannes[datter]' takes her 'Hovedlod og halve Boe', 38 Rd. Continuity as for her husband: Johannes Jensen, eldest son, of age, at Tynning in 1772, is Johannes Jenssen of Tynning, father of Kari (bapt. 15 Dec 1779). Johannes Jensen's eldest daughter in 1801 was Brite (24), named after her. Her own parents and birth are not yet found; she was alive on 13 Mar 1772. ¶1
