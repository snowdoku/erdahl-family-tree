Life story

Birth: he was 'myndig' (of age) in his father's probate of 3 Sep 1731, so born before about 1711; no baptism searched. His children were born about 1745-1752, which fits a late marriage. Not found yet: a 1762 ekstraskatt or a baptism for him. ¶2

Baptism of a son, April 1743 (Gulen 11441, canvas 28 right): 'dobt Jens Tynnings b. N. Jørgen. Mat: Berthe' at Brekke; godparents Iver[?] Tynning, Ole Frembrecke, Lars ibid., Jon Hans. ibid. and Ingebor Jensd. Tynning. The son is named after Jens's father Jørgen Jensen (d. before 3 Sep 1731). This Jørgen is not among the heirs in 1772, so he died before then. Nine months earlier, on Dom. 9 p. Trin. (22 Jul 1742), the Brekke burials (canvas 25 right) include 'Jørgen Jens. Tynning', aged 7, together with the wife ('qvinde') Siri Iversdatter of Tynning, aged 27. If that boy was Jens's son, Jens was married by about 1735, and Siri Iversdatter may have been a first wife who died in 1742. This is an inference only: no record seen names Siri as Jens's wife, another Jens may have lived at Tynning (Ingebor Jensdatter of Tynning was a godmother in 1743), and the 1772 probate mentions no children of an earlier marriage. ¶4a

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Graded on his link to Johannes Jensen. The probate after him, 13 Mar 1772, on Tynning ('Tøiningen'), Lavik skipreide (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll A 9, p. 363, Digitalarkivet 24167 viewer 184), names his widow Brithe Johannesdatter and 'deres udi et kiærligt Ægteskab tilsammen avlede' children: eldest son Johannes Jensen (of age, present), second son Rognald Jensen (of age), third son Lasse Jensen (23) and daughter Agaathe Jensdatter (19 1/2). Continuity to the tree: the eldest son Johannes Jensen, of age, at Tynning in 1772 is the 'Johannes Jenssen Tynnings' who had his daughter Kari baptised on 15 Dec 1779 (7 years later, same farm) and the householder Johannes Jenssen, 56, of Tynning in 1801. No other Johannes Jensen is named at Tynning. Among the estate's debts is wages to 'Barbra Jørgensdatter', his unmarried sister (see his father's probate). ¶1

Upgrade note on the link to his parents: the probate after Jørgen Jensen of Tønning, 3 Sep 1731 (A 3b fol. 486a), names 'hendes med den Salig mand avlede Børn, Nafnlig Jens, Lisabeth, Britha og Barbra Jørgens Søn og Døttre', 'Jens myndig'. The 41-year gap to his own probate (1772) is bridged by a pair on the same farm (audit 8, ruling 3): Jens and his sister Barbra Jørgensdatter appear together as Jørgen's children at Tønning in 1731, and again in 1772 when Jens Jørgensen dies at Tynning and his estate owes wages to Barbra Jørgensdatter. His only daughter is named Agaathe after his mother Agatha. No rival Jens Jørgensen is named at Tynning (Ytre Sogn probate index, domicile Tyn* and Tau*, 1698-1800). ¶3

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Test record: Jens Jørgensen's marriage(s), about 1733-35 (Gulen ministerialbok 11440, 1729-35, scan-only) and 1742-43 (not among the Brekke/Bø/Lavik betrothals of Aug 1742 to Jun 1743 in 11441, canvases 26-28, nor among the Eivindvik betrothals of Sep 1742 to Jan 1743 in Gulen ministerialbok 11442, canvases 35-36, all). Because no remarriage appears in either Gulen book between the July 1742 burials and the April 1743 baptism, the marriage to Berthe was more probably held before mid-1742 or outside Gulen, which weakens the idea that Siri Iversdatter was a first wife. ¶4b
