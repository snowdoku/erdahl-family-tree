About the family

Father of Hans (Johann) Jacob Jenisch (see his page). ¶1a

Parents: Paul Jenisch and Helene Keller (Pfarrerbuch Nr. 3905). The Leube-Liste (the Tübingen Stift roll) also gives 'V.: Paul J.' and his birthplace Lauingen. Indexed Stuttgart christenings of 1609 and 1611 name the parents 'Paulus Jenisch, Helena', which shows the couple at Stuttgart after 1608, as the Pfarrerbuch says. The link rests on compiled sources: Probable (graded on the parents' records). ¶3a

Life story

Life (Pfarrerbuch Württemberg Nr. 3905, compiled): born 26 Nov 1606 at Lauingen. Matriculated at Tübingen on 31 Jul 1627, Stift scholar 1630 (from Maulbronn), Magister 1631, vicar at Lichtenstern 1634, deacon at Böblingen 1635, pastor of Münchingen from 1637 until his death on 7 Apr 1675. Married (I) in 1635 Anna Braun, daughter of his predecessor at Münchingen, M. Blasius Braun; she died at Stuttgart on 3 Mar 1645 (the family was at Stuttgart during the war: children christened there 1638-1648). Married (II) at Reutlingen on 13 Aug 1645 Agnes Engel (born there 29 Dec 1624, died at Münchingen 25 Jun 1692), daughter of Paul Engel, of the council and burgomaster of Reutlingen. The Pfarrerbuch counts 25 children of the two marriages. ¶2a

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Graded on that link: Confirmed. Naming record: the Brackenheim marriage of 1667, no. 752 (image read): 'Hanß Jacob Jenisch, H. M. Joseph Jenischs, wohlverordneten Pfarrers zu Münchingen [...] ehelicher Sohn, Kupferschmid und Burger allhier'. The FamilySearch extraction VCYM-NRF labels him 'Father's Name: M. Joseph Jenisch'. Continuity: the groom of this record is the man in the tree, because the same entry makes him the coppersmith of Brackenheim and the husband of Maria Juliana, the mother named in Agnes Johanna's 1670 baptism, and the 1682 and 1695 entries carry the coppersmith on to the 1696 marriage (see his son's notes). 'M.' is Magister (a university degree); he was pastor of Münchingen (the entry writes 'Grüninger Ampts', i.e. the district of Markgröningen). Indexed children of a Joseph Jenisch and Agnes/Anna christened at Stuttgart 1643-1648 (Johann Heinrich 1643, Eberhard Friderich 1646, Paulus Ludwig 1648) are probably his, which would make Eberhard Friderich Jenisch at Brackenheim in 1679 a brother, but this is not proved. A death of a Joseph Jenisch at Münchingen on 7 Apr 1675, indexed age 37 (QPLM-VLKJ, no image linked), may be him with a misread age or a younger namesake; not entered. ¶1b

Corrections 1

Correction: the Münchingen death of 7 Apr 1675 (QPLM-VLKJ, indexed age 37), which his note left aside, is his own: the Pfarrerbuch gives him the same death date and place. The indexed age must be a misreading (he was 68). His note is superseded on this point. ¶2b
