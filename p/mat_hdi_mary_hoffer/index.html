About the family

Wife of Glenn Gordon Hayter, Raymond Hayter's eldest brother. Their shared headstone at Falls View Cemetery, American Falls, read from the Find a Grave photograph, gives her as 'MARY MARGUERITE / FEB. 27, 1911 / JAN. 11, 1976' and records 'MARRIED MAY 18, 1929'. ¶1

Parents, per her Find a Grave memorial (compiled, not a primary record): Ludwig Hoffer (1888-1924) and Marie Anna 'Mary' Langheinrich Hoffer (1884-1972). Siblings listed there: Anna Marie Hoffer Brantner, Louise Isaak Hoffer Gossen, Lloyd Ludwig Hoffer, Jacob Hoffer and Dorothy Hoffer Mayer. Her maiden name rests on that memorial; rated Probable until an Idaho marriage or death record is read. ¶2
