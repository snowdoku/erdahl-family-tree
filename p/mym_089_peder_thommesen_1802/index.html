About the family

Second husband of Sigri Bersvendsdatter (see her page): as 'Peder Thommesen Eidne', born 1802, son of Thomas Johnsen Lien, he married the widow 'Siri Bersvendsdtr Eiden', aged 46, in Selbu on 21 Jun 1832 (16314 p. 319 no. 20), four and a half months after her first husband John Sivertsen died (3 Feb 1832). ¶1

Life story

Probably the husband meant in Panteregister 18's line 'Sigrid Bersvendsdatter, gift med Ole Thomassen' (from a poor binarised scan; the index has Peder, not Ole). She was a widow again by the 1854 deed; his burial (1832-54) is not yet identified. His father Thomas Johnsen Lien was probably the 'Thomas Jonssen' (20) of the 1801 Lien household of Jon Thomassen, which would make him a nephew of Marit Johnsdatter (see her page) (inference). ¶2
