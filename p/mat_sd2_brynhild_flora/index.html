About the family

David Erdahl's maternal eighth-great-grandmother, born in 1700 at FLORA in Selbu and married in 1717, at seventeen, to Jorn Olsen. Selbu i fortid og naatid vol. 7, p. 116: '1729-1755 Jorn Olsen... G. 1717 m. Brynhild... fra... Flora, f. 1700, d. 1765.' Her patronymic is in the printed text at a point my reconstruction could not recover; it is on page 116 of that volume. ¶1

Life story

The narrative on p. 113 of the same volume puts her first: 'Brynhild... var fra... Flora. Hun og [Jorn] Olsen [kom] til [bruket] etter [at den forrige brukeren] dode. 1729 fikk de... bygselbrev' - SHE was from Flora, and the lease of 1729 was granted to the two of them. A bygselbrev naming the wife as well as the husband usually means she brought something to the bargain. ¶2

Context, flagged as context: FLORA is a farm cluster at the eastern end of Selbu, up the Nea valley from the lake, and the Slindan farms are on the north shore - a day's walk, which in 1717 made this a marriage between districts rather than between neighbours. ¶4

Betrothed in June 1717 to 'Jørgen grof-smed' (the blacksmith) as 'Brynel Olsd.' (16310 c66 right;, crop checked by the orchestrator); her son Olaf was baptised 19 Dec 1717. No farm is named in either entry, so her origin at Flora rests on the bygdebok. No farm Flora appears in the 1701 manntall of Selbu (it may be a sub-holding listed under another name). ¶8

Probably the 'Brynel', wife of Jørgen Smed, churched on 23 January 1718 after 'det ægte barn Olaf', baptised 19 December 1717 (Selbu ministerialbok 1699-1737, canvas 69, crops). Supports, does not raise, her Probable rating; see Ola Jornsen Slind. She is not the Brynel wife of Siur Slinde (child Christen 1717, widowed 1719). ¶5

Context for the 1729 takeover of Slindan: the previous holder, 'Bersvend Slinden', left a bankrupt estate ('Boet fallitt') at his probate of 18 Oct 1728, with widow Ingeborg Olsdatter and children Ole Bersvendsen 12, Ole Bersvendsen jun. 10, Peder 5, Beret 13 (card 77804 c1801; read by the orchestrator). The bygdebok says Brynhild and Jørn came to the farm after he died. That the widow was another Olsdatter could mean she was Brynhild's sister - not proved; recorded as a lead. ¶9

Burial, 2nd Sunday in Advent (8 Dec) 1765: 'Graufæst Brynjuld Olsd. Slinden gl 64 Å' - Brynhild Olsdatter Slinden, aged 64, so born about 1701 (16311 canvas 18 right on a zoom). Confirms the bygdebok's death year 1765 and birth about 1700-01. She stood godmother as 'Brynill Slinden' at Rogate 1765 (c18 L). ¶10

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the grade · 1 correction · 1 search with no result · 2 open questions

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Rated Probable: a gaardshistorie read by reconstruction. Her patronymic, and her father at Flora, are the next thing to recover from that page. ¶3

Corrections 1

Correction: name changed from 'Brynhild Slind' to 'Brynhild Olsdatter Slind'. The 1762 extra tax list names Jørn (Jørgen) Olsen's wife at 706 Slinden 'Brynild Ols datter', so her father was an Ole. She was alive in 1762, which agrees with the bygdebok's death year 1765. ¶7

Searches that found nothing 1

Negative search: Selbu 16310 c10-c11 (Exaudi 1701 to Septuagesima 1702) read in full at 1500 px. They hold no baptism of a Brynild/Brynhild and no Flora entry. With c8 (to Christmas 1700) and c9 (to Ascension 1701) already read, no Selbu baptism of her is found for 1700 to January 1702, although her burial age (64 in Dec 1765) points to 1700-1701. Either the age is off, or she was baptised outside Selbu. ¶11

Open questions and the next record to read 2

Lead only - an Uthus origin. The only Brynhild baptised in Selbu in 1700, her bygdebok birth year, is 'Brÿnil', child of Olof Ütthus (Uthus), on Misericordias 1700 (16310 canvas 7 left). A 'Brynel Uthaus/Uthus' stood godmother at Selbu in 1720-1721, apparently unmarried (16310 canvases 79-84). Against identifying her with our Brynhild: a 'Brÿnel Ütthus[?]' was also a godmother at Olaf's own baptism in December 1717, alongside 'Baro Ütthus[?]' (reading of the farm; read 'Ålhus[?]'), and a mother did not stand sponsor to her own child. So the Uthus Brynhild looks like a different woman - perhaps a kinswoman, which would itself point to an Uthus connection for Olaf's mother. The bygdebok says our Brynhild was 'fra Flora'; whether Olof Uthus was ever at Flora is unchecked. ¶6a

Would settle it: the Flora and Uthus chapters of Selbu i fortid og naatid vol. 6, or Brynhild's 1765 burial age. ¶6b
