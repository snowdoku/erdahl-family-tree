Life story

Baptism: Gottlieb Friedrich Knodel was baptised at Vaihingen an der Enz on 5 May 1823, son of Johann Gottlieb Knodel and Luisa Catharina (the index date may be the birth or the baptism date). This matches his Friedenstal death entry (1856: son of Johann Gottlieb, born 'Vaihingner Wurtt.'). No other Gottlieb Friedrich Knodel with a father Johann Gottlieb is in the Vaihingen index. Index entry only; image not seen; S0109-S0111 queued for the signed-in lane. His mother is linked at the confidence of this index entry (Probable); her separate link to Ludwig Knodel is Speculative. ¶2

Death: Gottlieb Friedrich Knodel died at Friedenstal on 18 June 1856 (Julian date as extracted). The entry names his father as Johann Gottlieb, gives his birthplace as 'Vaihingner Wurtt.' (Vaihingen, Württemberg) and adds the remark 'not a colonist'. His age was not extracted. Index extract, FHL 1884101/1 p.186 no.35; image not seen. Entered because his 'not a colonist' status is the main evidence for the speculative link between his father and Ludwig Knodel (see his page). ¶1

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
