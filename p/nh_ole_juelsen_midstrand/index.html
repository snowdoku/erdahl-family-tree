About the family

Named as the bride's father ('Ole Juulss.' / 'O. Juulsen') at the 1838 Nord-Aurdal marriage of Marit Olsdatter and Erik Olssen. He was living at Midstrand (Milstrand), Nord-Aurdal, when his daughter Marit was baptized on 25 Sep 1814. His dates and parents are not yet researched. ¶1

Children baptized to Ole Juelsen and Astri Evensdatter: Juel (bapt. 18 Sep 1803, Svennes, Mitstrand); Juel (bapt. 15 Apr 1805, Ulnes, Mitstrandeje; probably the Juel Olsen of Midstrand, aged 4, buried July 1810); Kirsti (bapt. 24 Jan 1808, Ulnes); Marit (bapt. 2 Feb 1812, Svennes; evidently died young); Marit (bapt. 25 Sep 1814, the later wife of Erik Olsen Dakken); and Aaste (bapt. 28 Oct 1821, mother at Soerstrand). Sørstrand is where Erik and Marit lived when their daughter Andrea was born in 1839. ¶4

The children's names follow the usual pattern: Juel for his father, Kirsti for his mother, and Marit for Astri's mother Marit Olsdatter. His death has not been identified. 'Ole Bryn Juelsen' (b. about 1781, buried 22 Feb 1851, Aurdal) is a possible but unconfirmed match. ¶5

Life story

1801 census, Aurdal, Midt-Strand: Ole Jullsen, 21, unmarried son, living with his parents, the farmer Jull Olsen (46) and Kirsti Uldriksdatter (42). His siblings in the household were Uldrik (16), Knudt (10), Johanes (8), Jull (4), Aasse (18), Astri (13) and Marit (5). ¶2

Marriage: 'Ole Juelsøn, ungkarl, Mitstrand' married 'Astri Evensdatter, pige, Hovrud' on 11 Apr 1803 at Ulnes church. The forlovere were Juel Nordstrand and Erik Uldriksøn Fonnes, the latter probably his mother's brother. ¶3

Probate (index card): 'Ole 30 aar' is the first child at the estate of his father Juel Olsen of Midtstrand in Svenes, Nord-Aurdal (2 Aug 1810 - 12 Jul 1811; Valdres skifteprotokoll 1810-1814, fol. 76), with his mother Kjerstie Ulrichsdatter. The age fits his birth c.1780. Confidence upgraded from Probable to Confirmed as their son. His sister Aaste (28) had married Juel Evensen of Hovrud, probably the Jul Evensen (19 in 1801) of Even Julsen's Hovrud household and so a brother of Ole's own wife Astri Evensdatter. That likely double tie between Midtstrand and Hovrud supports the identification. ¶6

Baptism: Aurdal ministerialbok 1763-1781 (DA 9362), legitimate baptisms 1780, p. 307 (IIIF canvas 151). Under 'dito 30te' (30 Jul 1780), Svenes: 'Item Döbt Juel Olsøn Mitstrand og hustru Kiersti Uldrichsdatter barn Ole'. Godparents: Ragnild Nordstrand, Ingebor Olsdatter Lo[?], Knud Lo, Erik Olsen ibid. and Helje Haraldsøn Agervold. The mother was churched (introduced) the same day. This is the baptism itself, and it names both parents, Juel Olsen of Midtstrand and Kiersti Uldrichsdatter. His birth date can be narrowed to shortly before 30 Jul 1780. ¶7
