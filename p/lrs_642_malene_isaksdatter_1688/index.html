Life story

Bygdebok for Karmøy: Skudenes og Skudeneshavn (1982), Langåker bnr. 7, canvas 129: wife of Ola Anderssen (user from 1718), 'Malene Isaksdtr., ca. 1688, dotter til Isak [og] Ingeborg [på] Langåker bnr. 37'; their children listed as Anna ca. 1719, Anders ca. 1722 and Ingeborg ca. 1732 (who married Hans Sjursen). ¶1

1762 ekstraskatt: Skudenes manntall (Digitalarkivet 49646, view 601), farm '2 Lang Ager': the couple 'Hans Sieursen / Ingebor Olsd.' (total 5 persons) with 'Ana Olsd.' in the girls-over-12 column and 'Ole Andersen / Malene Isachsd.' in the next column (other people kept in the household). So in 1762 Ole Andersen and Malene Isaksdatter were living in the household of Hans Siursen and Ingebor Olsdatter on Langager, as the bygdebok's son-in-law succession implies. The list does not write the relationship. ¶3

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

2 on the grade · 1 open question

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Graded on her link to Engebor Olsdatter: Probable (bygdebok statement plus co-residence). The 1762 ekstraskatt puts 'Ole Andersen / Malene Isachsd.' in the household of Hans Sieursen and Ingebor Olsd. at Langager, and Engebor's son Ole Hansen named a daughter Ingeborg Malene. Cautions: Malene would have been about 44 at Ingeborg's birth (ca. 1732), no record names the relationship, and the bygdebok itself queries Ingeborg ('Ho var ikkje mor til borna hans?'). ¶2

Audit 8 (1-2 Nov 2026): grade unchanged (Probable, compiled source only). Under the audit-7 rule every bygdebok-sourced ancestor must carry a test record; this note supplies it. ¶4a

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Test record: as for her husband Ola Anderssen: a probate after Malene or Ola of Langåker (after 1762) naming Ingeborg Olsdatter, wife of Hans Sjursen, as her own child. This must also answer the bygdebok's question whether Malene (about 44 in 1732) was the mother of Ola's children, and whether there was an earlier wife. ¶4b
