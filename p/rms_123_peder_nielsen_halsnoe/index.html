About the family

Children named in the probate: Biørn, Niels and Peder Pedersen; Marthe (m. Jon Rasmussen Hageberg); Anna (m. Thore Thorsen Buchen, d. 1786); Britha (m. Aage Ogesen, Halsnøe). Anna, confirmed in 1766 aged 17, was born about 1749, so Peder was born before about 1725. Test record for his own parents: the Ryfylke probates before 1767 (D 5 and the 1757-66 protocol behind the designations D 11, DA 23888) under Halsnøy in Sjernerø skipreide. ¶4

Parents not found. In the 1701 manntall (Ryfylke fogderi), Halsnøe had an 'opsidder' Peder Nielsen, 37 (b. about 1664), with a son Niels Pedersen, 5 (b. about 1696). This Niels is the right age to be the father of our Peder Nielsen, who would then carry his grandfather's name. Rasmus Biørnsen's first wife on Halsnøy, Kari Nielsdatter (d. before 20 Mar 1760, D 11 fol. 36), could be Peder's sister. Nothing ties them yet, and nobody has been added. The typed registers to the Ryfylke designations 1731-1754 (DA 23971) and 1757-1766 (DA 23973), and the register to D 4 (1719-1732), list no probate after a Niels or a Peder on Halsnøy (the only Halsnøy entries are Ifver Ifversen, D 4 fol. 151b; Biørn Reyersen, 1731; Kari Jonsdatter, 1751; Kari Nielsdatter, 1760). ¶6a

Life story

That index is filed by skipreide and then farm; the Sjernerø section (which holds the Fister farms Byre, Buchen/Bukken, Halsnøy, Hille, Fure and others) runs at about views 946-1000. Image urls: view n = urn sk(20110328670000 + n + 335). Earlier passes had sampled only the Hjelmeland section. ¶1

1762 ekstraskatt (index): Halsnøe, 'Peder Nielsen, Opsidder' with 'Sophie Biørndatter, Hustr', and the children over 12 Biørn, Niels, Marthe and Ane Peders-. Ane is his daughter Anna (b. about 1749). ¶5

Probate (D 7b fol. 507a, scan read): 'Aar 1787 den 19de Martii' registration and valuation on the farm Halsnøe in Siernerøe skibrede after the late 'Peder Nielsen', between his widow 'Sophie Biørnsdatter' and their jointly begotten children ('sammen avlede Børn'): 1. the eldest son Biørn Pedersen, of age and married; 2. the second son Niels Pedersen, also married and of age; 3. the youngest son Peder Pedersen, of age; 4. the eldest daughter Marthe Pedersdatter, married to Jon Rasmussen Hageberg; 5. the second daughter 'Anna Peders Datter gift med Thore Thorsen Buchen', dead, who left 3 children, 2 sons and 1 daughter: the eldest son Peder Thorsen 13, the second son Niels Thorsen 9, and the daughter Siri Thorsdatter 17, whose guardian is their father Thore Thorsen; 6. the youngest daughter Britha Pedersdatter, married to Aage Ogesen, living on Halsnøe. Valuation men Mikel Aadsen Oe[?] and Aamund Pedersen Wendje[?]. The card (DA 39936, view 961) gives the net estate as 257 rd 3 ort 14 sk. ¶2

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Graded Confirmed on his link to his daughter Anna Pedersdatter. The probate names her as 'Anna Peders Datter gift med Thore Thorsen Buchen', dead, leaving 3 children: that is the same woman as 'Anna Peders Datter Halsnøe' who married Thore Thorsen Buchen in Fister church on 26 Jun 1768, identified in the record itself by her husband. Her own probate (D 7b fol. 457a, Lille Buchen, 10 Aug 1786) names the same husband and the same three children, Peder 12, Niels 8 and Siri 16, one year younger, so there is no rival Anna. The Halsnøe godparents at the baptisms of her children (Niels Halsnøe 1770; Peder, Reier and Marthe Halsnøe 1777) match her brothers Niels and Peder and her sister Marthe in this probate. ¶3
