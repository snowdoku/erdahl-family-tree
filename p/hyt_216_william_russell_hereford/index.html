Life story

William Russell of Hereford (of '....' in co. Hereford), father of Elizabeth, wife of Lewis Boyle of Bidney and of the Friars, Hereford. Three compiled sources name him: Lodge (1789; 'William Russel of the same [city]'), Duncumb (1804; 'William Russell, esq.'), and Robinson's Mansions and Manors of Herefordshire, p. 94, as carried into Weaver's edition of the 1569 Herefordshire visitation. A fourth, independent of the Boyle peerage tradition, is the Russell of Strensham pedigree in the Worcestershire visitation (Harleian Society vol. 27, p. 119), where Richard Mundy's later transcript (Harl. MS 1566) adds 'Will'm Russell of.... in com. Hereford', son of Kinard Russell, with a daughter 'Elizebeth vx. Lewes Boyle of the Citty of Hereford a quo Boyle Earle of Cork'. The words 'Earle of Cork' show the addition was written after 1620. He was probably born about 1450-60, judging by his daughter's marriage under Henry VII. Added. ¶1

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the grade · 1 open question

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Probable, graded on his link to his daughter Elizabeth Russell. Several compiled pedigrees agree, and the Worcestershire one comes from a separate family's papers. None quotes a record, and the Strensham entry is a late addition in italics, not part of the 1569 text. Its generations also conflict with Lewis Boyle's dates (see Kinard Russell), so the grade rests on Lodge, Duncumb and Robinson naming 'William Russell of Hereford'. ¶2a

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Test record: a Hereford city deed or Hereford diocesan will of William Russell naming his daughter Elizabeth Boyle; or the will of John Boyle of Hereford, 1551 (TNA PROB 11/34/206), for Russell kin. Added. ¶2b
