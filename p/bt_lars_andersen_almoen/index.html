About the family

Elder brother of Thomas Andersen, baptized 30 September 1764 at Almoen. He carried the name of an infant brother buried in 1761, and, by the usual Trøndelag rule, of his paternal grandfather. ¶1

Life story

Married 29 May 1792 as an 'Ungkarl soldat' - an unmarried enlisted soldier - of Almoen to Kiersten Larsdatter, a maid of Skordalen; the forlovere were Ole Schej and Ole Fossan. His father remarried six weeks later, on 9 July. ¶2

In the 1801 census he is 36, 'Mand 1te egteskab, Huusmand med jord' at Almoen, with his wife Kirsten (recorded there as Lassedatter, aged 50) - a cottager with land on his father's farm, alongside his brother Thomas's household. ¶3

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
