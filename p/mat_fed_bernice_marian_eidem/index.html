Notes on method and coordination

Her two cards are the cleanest demonstration in the whole file of the Minnesota/South Dakota confusion that also afflicts Barbara Ann Eidem's birthplace: one and the same birth, recorded nine years apart by the same woman, is 'Marietta, Deuel County, South Dakota' on one card and 'Marietta, Lac qui Parle County, Minnesota' on the other. Marietta village is in Minnesota, on the line, with Deuel County, South Dakota directly across it. I carry the Minnesota reading because that is where the village is. ¶3
