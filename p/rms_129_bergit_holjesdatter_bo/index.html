About the family

Mother of Hølje Knudsen (see his page); Probable, graded on her link to Hølje, from the bygdebok alone. Valdres bygdebok I F, gnr 106 Bø (canvas 266, right column): Knut 'd 1643, g m Bergit Høljesdotter', children Hølje (b. about 1619), Eivind (b. about 1623, had half the farm 'etter mora') and Marte (b. about 1631). Canvas 267: 'Då mannen døydde, [dreiv] enkja Bergit garden med sonen Hølje. [I] 1645 [betalte] ho koppskatten for ein... son Hølje'. Her son Hølje carries her father's name.
