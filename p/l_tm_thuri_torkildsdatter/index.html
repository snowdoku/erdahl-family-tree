Life story

Born 13 Jan 1831 and baptized 23 Jan 1831 in Nissedal, daughter of Torkel Olsen and Aasild Gulliksdatter of Midbø. This parentage is Probable. She lived at Midbø when she married in 1858. Her ages are 28 at marriage, 36 in 1865, b. 1830 in 1875 and b. 1829 in 1891/1900. Her first daughter was named Aasild. ¶2

In 1900 she was a widow (føderaadsenke) at Grimstveit nedre, Nissedal (indexed as Turi Tarkelsdatter, b. 1829). The 1875 census misnames her 'Thuri Hansdatter'. ¶3
