About the family

Father of Guru Torstensdatter (see her page), Probable. Her brother was 'Siver Tostensen Berge', named as 'morbroren' (mother's brother) of her daughters at her husband Erik Svendsen Uglem's probate, 20 Jun 1741 (card 77804 c2055). Siver Tostensen Berge was in turn the brother of Ole and Haldor Tostensen Berge (1752 probate), and the only Torsten at Berge in 1701 is Torsten Siursen, whose sons were exactly Siur, Haldor and Ole. Siur and Siver are the same name in Selbu records (Siur Olsen Alset is 'Siver Olsen Alset' in his 1732 probate). Guru's son Torsten Eriksen (b. about 1733) carries his name. ¶3a

Life story

Selbu i fortid og nåtid vol. 5, Berge (gnr. 75), canvases 19 and 21 (rebuilt from word boxes): '1685-1721 Torsten Sjurdsen, f. ca. 1652, d. 1722, [...] var bror[sønn] til Ola Torstensen. Kona [...] Brynhild d. 1719'; canvas 19: 'Fra 1685 er det en ny Tosten [...] etter Ola. Han het Tosten Sjursen og var f. i 1651. Han hadde [sønnene] Sjur 1691, [Haldor] 1695 [og] Ola 1697'. The book gives his death as 1722 (the tree has no death date; ABT, book only) and his wife as Brynhild (added as Brynhild). Father Sjur added (see his page, Probable). His own grade is unchanged. ¶8

1701 manntall, Berge in Selbu (38461 canvas 120): 'Torsten Siursen' 50, farmer, with sons 'Siur Torstensen 10, Haldor 6, Oller 4' and a servant Øvind Eriksen, reserve soldier, 20. So born about 1651, son of a Siur (Sivert). ¶1

More support for his line at Berge: the probate card after Marit Mogensdatter Berge, 13 Feb 1741 (77804 c149; earlier read as 'Haldor Torkelsen'), names the widower as 'Haldor Tostensen Berge'. Their children were Arn[?] Haldorsen 13, Tosten Haldorsen 10, Mogens Haldorsen 6, Brynild Haldorsdatter 17 and Marit Haldorsdatter 2½. The guardians were 'Faren, Haldor Tostensen Berge', Ole Eriksen Kyllo[?], who was married to the children's mother's sister Brynild Mogensdatter, 'samt farbroren Ole Tostensen Berge' (and the father's brother, Ole Tostensen Berge). So Haldor named his second son Tosten. ¶4

Correction to the lead note (full-resolution re-read of 24752 fol. 1037b, the skifte after Marit Magnusdatter at Berge, 13 Feb 1741): it is NOT Ole Tostensen who was married to the children's moster. The protocol names 'Ole Er[ich]sen [of] Kyllo[?]' as the man 'gift med deres Moster Brynild Magnusdatter', and then 'Børnenes Farbroder Ole Tostensen Berge', as card 77804 c149 says. 'Siver Tostensen [fra] Berge... antog sig Wergemaalet' (took on the guardianship); the words just before his name are hard to read ('den yngre Broder'?) and no relationship word for Siver can be read with confidence. So this entry proves Ole Tostensen a brother of Haldor Tostensen, which the 1752 probate (24753 fol. 448b) already showed; it adds nothing on Guru Torstensdatter (see her page), whose link to Torsten stays Probable. ¶7

His three sons are proved brothers, all of Berge, by the probate after Guri Mogensdatter, wife of Ole Tostensen Berge, held at Berge 21 Jun 1752 (Skifteprotokoll 3, 24753, fol. 448b-449a, view 472). The heirs are the widower 'Ole Tostensen Berge' and four children, sons Torsten Olsen 25 and [Mogens?] Olsen 20 and daughters Marithe Olsdatter 28 and Brynild Olsdatter 17; the guardians named for the minors are the children's 'farbroder' (father's brother) 'Halder Tostensen Berge' and 'farbroderen Siver Tostensen Berge'. Haldor Tostensen died at Berge before 10 Jun 1766 (probate, 24755 fol. 565a): widow Malie Thomasdatter, sons Arnt and Torkild, daughters Marithe (married to Peder Thomassen Grøtum), Ingeborg, Kari and Berethe; one of the guardians was Torsten Olsen Berge, his nephew. ¶2

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Not Confirmed: no single record names Guru as Torsten Siursen's daughter. She was not in the 1701 list, which names only males; she was born before 1705 and could be a daughter born after 1701 or a daughter omitted from it. ¶3b

Searches that found nothing 2

No probate after Torsten Siursen himself is in the Stjør- og Verdal card index. The whole Berge run (77804 c133-149, odd canvases; even canvases are blank card backs) was. It holds only 1701 (A, Ole Sivertsen Buum, husmann under Berg, Åsen), Verdal and Stjørdal Berg cards, Haldor Baarsen (Selbu, 1697), Povel (1727) and Marit Mogensdatter (1741). So he died between 1701 and 1727, when no Stjør- og Verdal skifte protocol survives (series A ends in 1714 and protocol 2 begins in 1727), or he left no probate. ¶5

Negative for the 1719 Berge probate: no probate after Brynhild (d. 1719, bygdebok) or Torsten Siursen (d. 1722) can survive in the Stjør- og Verdal series. The card index 77804 abstracts protocol 1 (1689-1701), the stiftamt designations 1692-1714 (series A) and protocol 2 (1727-1742) only, and read the whole Berge run (c133-149). The protocol gap 1702-1726 covers both deaths. The remaining test for Brynhild is her 1719 burial (16310, about canvases 72-80). ¶9

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Lead, not graded: the skifte after Marit Magnusdatter, wife of Haldor Tostensen, at Berge, 13 Feb 1741 (24752 fol. 1037b) names their children, including Tosten Haldorsen 10, Brynild Haldorsdatter 17 and Marit Haldorsdatter 2 1/2. Among the guardians are 'Siver Tostensen fra Berge', who took on guardianship for the children, and 'Ole Tostensen fra Berge', who was married to the children's moster Brynild Magnusdatter. The word that gives Siver's relationship to the children is not clear at this zoom (it may read 'Farbroder'). If it does, Haldor, Siver and Ole Tostensen of Berge were brothers. That matches the 1701 Berge household of Torsten Siursen, 50, with sons Siur 10, Haldor 6 and Oller 4, and it supports Torsten as the father of Siver Tostensen Berge, the 'morbroder' of Guru Torstensdatter's children in 1741. Confidence is not changed: Guru herself is not named here. Next step: a full-resolution crop of fol. 1037b, lines 7-10. ¶6
