Life story

Known only from his son's patronymic: Jørgen Aamundsen, tenant at Alvestad in Haram parish, born about 1662 and buried there in 1736. A man whose son was born about 1662 must himself have been born before about 1640. ¶1

Jørgen named his eldest recorded son Aamund (born about 1698), which in this region is the ordinary way of naming a boy after his paternal grandfather, and so supports the patronymic. ¶2

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
