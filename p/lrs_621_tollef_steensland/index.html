Life story

Probate 1702: 'Anno 1702 den 23 Junij' the sorenskriver Peder Pedersen and two lagrettemenn met 'paa Steensland udi Tordal' to register and divide the estate 'efter afgangne Torbiørn Tollefsen Steensland' between his surviving wife Boel Jensdatter and their child Sifver Torbiørnsdatter. Before that division, because 'forn. Torbiørn Tollefsen tilforn været gift med Ingeri Erichsdatter' (had earlier been married to Ingeri Erichsdatter), with whom he had '1 barn, Nafnlig Anna Torbiørnsdatter', her inheritance after her mother was settled, 'og paa barnets vegne hendes beste faderen [grandfather] Tollef Steensland' attended (the word read 'beste faderen' is probable; the card index read only 'a phrase naming Tollef Stensland'). Bamble skifteprotokoll I 2, fol. 204b. Crop. ¶1

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the grade

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Confidence: Probable as father of Torbjørn Tollefsen Steensland (see his page). In 1702 Tollef Steensland, alive, attended the probate on behalf of his grandchild Anna Torbjørnsdatter ('hendes beste faderen', probable reading), and Torbjørn's patronymic is Tollefsen on the same farm. The probate does not say 'his son' in words, and the reading 'beste faderen' is not certain, so Probable. Birth before 1660 is estimated (a grandfather in 1702). ¶2
