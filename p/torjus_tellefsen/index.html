Life story

Baptized 11 May 1817 (identification probable): father Tellef Torsen, mother Ulvild Torjusdatter, of Østenaae; his 1838 marriage gives birthplace Øvre Ramse. ¶1

BIRTHPLACE RESOLVED: his baptism (11 May 1817, Åmli 1816-1829 p.73, born 6 Apr) gives the parents' residence as Østenaae. By his brother Thor's baptism on 25 Jun 1820 the family was at Øvre Ramse, and his siblings Ingeborg (1823) and Birgitte (1826) were also baptized there. He was most likely born at Østenaa. The 1838 marriage's 'øvre Ramse' is the farm he grew up on. ¶4

Married Tøri Olsdatter 15 Jul 1838 in Tovdal. Children: Ingeborg (1839), Anne (1841), Ragnild (1844), Anne (1847), Ulvild (1850), Tellef (1853), Ole (1855), Thorid (1859). ¶2

Emigrated with family to Winnebago County, Iowa between 1859 and 1868. Death and burial not yet found. ¶3

Emigration narrowed: Torjus Tellefsen of Øvre Ramse is named as bride's father at the Åmli weddings of daughters Anne (29 Dec 1861) and Ingeborg (13 Aug 1862). He and Tøri are not in the 1865 Åmli census. The family was in Winnebago Co., Iowa by Dec 1868 (Uldvil's confirmation), so they emigrated between Aug 1862 and 1868, probably before Dec 1865. ¶5

Emigration: the Tovdal out-migration list (clerk's book, source 9929), under the year 1862, rows 4-11, records 'Torjus Tellefsen 46 Leilænding' (tenant farmer), 'Thorid Olsdatter 48 Leilændingskone', and children Ragnel 18, Anne 14, Ulvild 11, Tellef Torjusen 9, Ole 7 and Thorid 4, all to 'Amerika'. No day, month or attest is given. The children's ages fit a departure after Jul 1862. Torjus at 46 and Thorid jr. at 4 fit spring 1863, which agrees with Tellef's 1900 census immigration year of 1863. The year is therefore 1862 per the register, possibly 1863 (inferred). Listed immediately before them, also to America, were the lodgers Gunder Gundersen T[?]edt, 64, Torborg Olsdatter, 63, and Kari Gundersdatter, 29. The family is not in the Åmli main-parish out-migration list for 1858-1869 (source 855), which was read in full. ¶8
