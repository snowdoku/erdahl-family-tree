Life story

1801 census, Langager: Hans Olsen, 13, son of Ole Hansen and Siri Jacobsdatter. ¶3

The c.1842 Åkra household register (Skudenes church book 1770-1842, image 272) reads: 'Hans Olsen (husmand), b. 25 March 1788, parents gaardmand Ole Hansen Langager and Siri Jakobsdatter'. His wife was Ranveg Karine Eriksdatter. Children at home: Erik Hansen (1815) and Oline (1825). ¶2

Husmann at Akre (Åkra). Named as the bride's father ('Huusmand Hans Ols. Akre') at Siri Karine's 1845 marriage. ¶1

Burial: Skudenes ministerialbok 1847-1863 (source 1657), burials 1853, p. 402 no. 1, scan read: 'Huusmand Hans Olsen', died 25 Dec 1852, buried 1 Jan 1853, age '64 3/4' (the first digit after 6 is unclear; 64 3/4 fits his birth on 25 Mar 1788 exactly), 'Akre'. Index pg00000005064038. This is the husmann at Akre of the c.1842 register; Ranveig was a widow at Akre in 1865. ¶5

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: Confirmed as the father of Siri Karine Hansdatter. Her marriage of 3 Apr 1845 (Skudenes ministerialbok 1841-1846, p. 186 no. 31) names the bride's father as 'Hans Olss Akre' (the scan reads 'Huusmand Hans Ols. Akre') and gives her birthplace as Akre. The c.1840-42 Skudenes household register (source 1653, Akre, canvas 273, re-read from the saved crop) has exactly this man at Akre: 'Hans Olsen (Huusmand)', born 25 Mar 1788, married 1815 to Ranveg Karine Eriksdatter, and it names his parents as 'Gaardm. Ole Hansen Langager og h. Siri Jakobsdatter'. searched the name columns of every Akre page of that register (canvases 266-294) and recorded no other Hans Olsen household. Two primary records therefore join the bride's father to this Hans Olsen, born 1788, by name, status (husmann) and farm. ¶4
