Life story

Burial scan read (Stjørdal ministerialbok 1755-1780, p. 253): 'd. 5 Dec. ved Schatvold graffestet 1. Peder Arends[en] Hegge 60 Aar'. No remark, spouse or death date. On 25 Feb 1770 two children of a 'Peder Hegge' (Malena and Marit) were baptised at Skatval in consecutive entries; whether both are his, or a second Peder lived at Hegge, is unresolved. ¶3

Of Hegge, Skatval; buried 5 Dec 1774 aged 60 (index, Schatvold p. 253) - the only Peder buried from Hegge 1755-1832. ¶1

Bygdebok, Skatval vol. 2, 50/1 Hegge, canvas 206 right: 'Peder Arntsen [?]Frosta[?], [d.] 05.12.1774, korporal. Fikk [bygsel?] av... [g.] 28.11.1759 enke Mali Nilsdtr.' (b. 30.05.1730, d. 18.08.1788), children from 1760, including Malena (m. Ola Pedersen Lillevalstad 37). If right, he married only in Nov 1759, which fits the 1762 tax list (no children over 12) and argues against the hypothesis that he is the Peder Arntsen of Baar (a widower who remarried in 1755); the word 'Frosta' beside his name may give his origin, but the rebuild has gaps there. The 1756-59 baptisms indexed to 'Peder Hegge' would then belong to the other Peder at Hegge (Peder Olsen). ¶13a

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the grade · 2 records that disagree · 4 alternatives set aside · 4 searches with no result · 2 open questions

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Probable father of Malena Pedersdatter (see her page), baptised 25 Feb 1770 as daughter of 'Peder', Hegge. Children indexed to a Peder of Hegge: Ole 1756 (bapt. 28 Nov 1756, 'Peder Hegges Barn Ole'), Niels 1759, Jørgen 1760 (father entered as 'Peder Corporal'), Arnt 1763 (fits a father Arent/Arnt), Ejner 1766, Malena 1770, Petter 1773. Jørgen (41) and his brother Ejner (36) farmed Hegge in 1801, and both stood godfather to Malena's son Peter in 1791. More than one Peder may have lived at Hegge in 1756-73 (Niels 1759 and Jørgen 1760 are close), so the sibling list is provisional. His wife is not named in the index; a widow Anne Pedersdatter b. about 1742, lodger at Hegge in 1801, is the best candidate (unproved). ¶2

Records that disagree 2

Correction (age conflict): in the 1765 manntall- og legdsrulle of the Nordre Stjørdal dragoon company (source 51337, image 49, quarter no. 33 'Heggen', Skatval, owned by Kancelliraad Hornemann in Trondheim), the holders are Ole Olsen, 35, and 'Peder Arensen', 33, 'tient' (has served). That gives a birth year about 1732, not 1714 as the 1774 burial age (60) implies. The same roll is about right for his neighbours (Peder Pedersen of Lille Walstad 39 for an actual 42; Peder Joensen of Skjervold 51). He is the only Peder Ar(e)ntsen at Hegge, and 'tient' fits the 'Peder Corporal' of the 1760 baptism. If the roll age is right, (1) the birth date ABT 1714 is about 18 years too early, and (2) the hypothesis that he is the Peder Arntsen of Baar who was of age in 1743 is weakened. His link to Malena (via the Hegge baptisms) is not affected, so the grade stays Probable. The young men listed at Heggen are Ole Paalsen (two of that name, 21, cottagers' sons) and Hans Joensen 19 and Peder Joensen 16, farmers' sons: these are the sons of the dragoon Joen Pedersen Hegge (d. 1752, probate 24753 fol. 465a: sons Hans, Peder, Joen). ¶9

Bearing on the age conflict: in the 1762 extra-tax list (48401 image 203, Skatval no. 13 'Hegge', leilending) the household is 'Peder Corporal ved Dragonerne og Hu[stru]' (Peder, corporal of the dragoons, and wife) with NO children over 12, farmhand Ole Jons[en] 16 and maid Mali Olsd.; lodgers Lars Erichs[en] and wife and the widow Anne. No children over 12 fits a man born about 1732 (33 in the 1765 roll) and marrying in the 1750s, better than a man born about 1714 (burial age 60). The conflict is not resolved; it weighs against identifying him with Peder Arntsen of Baar (of age by 1743). The list gives no wife's name. ¶11

Alternatives considered and set aside 4

Possible widow (unproved): the 1801 census of Hegge lists Karine Torrisdatter, 83 (b. c.1718), lodger, 'Enke 1ste gang' (widow of a first marriage), in household 11 with Sivert Jorgensen 39 and Giertru Pedersdatter 38. Her age fits a wife of Peder Arentsen (b. c.1714, bur. 1774), and Giertru Pedersdatter could be a daughter, but the census gives no relationship and no husband's name. Anne Pedersdatter, 59, widow, is too young to be his wife. No indexed marriage of any Peder 1755-1770 at Hegge, Baar or Skiervold. Read. ¶4

Hypothesis (unproved, for the next worker): Peder Arentsen of Hegge (b. about 1714, buried 1774) may be the Peder Arntsen who lived at Baar in 1743 as son of Arnt Joensen and Berit Pedersdatter (24753 fol. 46b) and who, as a widower, married Berethe Jacobsdatter of Röan in 1755. He is the only Peder Ar(e)ntsen of suitable age buried in Skatval 1755-1800 (the Peder Arnsen of Hembre, buried 1757, was buried at Hegra); a son Arnt (1763) fits. If true, the 'Peder Baar' children of 1758-68 belong to Peder Erichsen. ¶6a

Not proved: needs a record placing Peder Arntsen at Hegge (e.g. a Hegge baptism naming the mother Berethe, or a 1774-75 skifte after Peder Arentsen, which the typed registers do not list). ¶6b

Hypothesis only. If Peder Arentsen of Hegge (b. c.1714) is the Peder Arntsen of Baar who was of age in 1743, his parents were Arnt Jonsen and Berit Pedersdatter of Baar, and he had a sister Berit Arntsdatter, b. c.1730 (probate after Berit Pedersdatter, 4 Jul 1743, 24753 fol. 46b). No record yet links the Baar man to the Hegge man, so no parents are added. ¶7

Searches that found nothing 4

Negative: the probate card index for 1689-1742 (source 77804) has no card filed under Hegge: the cards run Havstein (Leksvik, views 715-719) - Hegle (Levanger, view 721). The 1692-1714 skifte designations name 'Olluf Hansen Hegge' only as an heir (of Gundborg Olufsdatter Kierche Skatvold, 28 Feb 1703, view 1741). So no pre-1742 probate after an Arnt of Hegge is indexed. ¶5

Negative (panteregister sweep): the Hegge pages of the Stjørdal tinglag panteregister 1762-1800 (source 22318, image 104, both halves of 'N:o 106 Hegge') list no deed, lease or skifte in the name of Peder Arentsen. The owners were Hans Jonsen and Ole Olsen (deeds from Cancelliraad Hornemann, B fol. 312 and 89), then Hans Olsen, Lars Olsen and Jørgen Pedersen (C fol. 653, 681, 465); the only probate entered is the skifte after Mali Hansdatter (B fol. 279), widow of the dragoon Joen Pedersen Hegge. So Peder Arentsen was not an owner of Hegge; he was probably a tenant or cottager, and his estate (d. 1774) did not convey land. One lease is unread: 'Capit. Israel Jørgen Poldevin B[ygsel] B[rev] til...', B fol. 17 (about 1762), name not written on the farm page. ¶8

Negative: the 25 Feb 1770 Skatval entries (16371 canvas 192, left page) read: '1 Peder Hegges Barn Malena. Test: Mali Hegge, Berit ibid., Karen Alstad, Hans Hegge,... ibid.' and '2 Peder Hegges barn Marit. Test: Kirsten Moxnæs[?], Maria Fosvig[?], Maren Forbordsaune, Ole Alstad, Lodden[?] Hegge'. No mother and no patronymic in either, so the page does not settle whether the two girls are twins or children of two Peders of Hegge. ¶10

Negative, farm-by-farm probate search for Hegge: every Hegge probate in the typed Stjør- og Verdal registers 1742-1802 has now been read, and none names Peder Arentsen as heir, son-in-law, guardian or appraiser. (1) 12 Oct 1751, the dragoon Ole Joensen Hegge (24753 fol. 426b-427a, view 450): widow Marithe Baalsdatter [register: Bårdsdatter], six children Baal Olsen 40, Jon Olsen 38, Ole Olsen 36, Lars Olsen 33, Peder Olsen 30 and Mali Olsdatter, unmarried, 25. (2) 19 May 1758, the girl Malj Larsdatter (24753 fol. 789b, view 820): heirs her mother Mali Hansdatter Hegge (widow of the dragoon Joen Pedersen) and her half-brothers Hans, Peder and Joen Joensen. (3) 8 Jan 1759, Marithe Baardsdatter, Ole Joensen's widow (24753 fol. 829a, view 859): heirs Baard Olsen 48, Jon Olsen husmann under Hegge, the late Ole Olsen's children Ole 13 and Marit 8 (at Sve), Povel[?] Olsen husmann under Hegge, Peder Olsen 37, and Mali Olsdatter, widow of Jon Michelsen Røkke. (4) 6 Aug 1765, Ole Lødensen Hegge (24755 fol. 482a, view 503): widow Margretha Christophersdatter, son Joen Olsen and daughter Mali Olsdatter, married to Hans Olsen. So Peder Arentsen did not marry into either Hegge owner family (Joen Pedersen's or Ole Joensen's), nor into Ole Lødensen's; his wife (Malena's mother) must come from outside the farm. The 1774 burial age conflict is unresolved, but note that the 1765 roll that gives him 33 also gives Peder Jonsen of Skjervold 51, against a burial age of 67 in 1771 (16371 p. 212), so the roll's ages for older men who had served look unreliable; this weakens the roll as evidence against the burial age. ¶12a
