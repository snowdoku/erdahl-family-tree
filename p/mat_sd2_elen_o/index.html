About the family

Wife of Ole Jorgensen of Slinden, entered as 'Elen Olsd., 39, Hustru' in the 1825 census of Selbu at rural residence 0182 Slinden, household 1, immediately after her husband. Her children in that household are Ingebor Olsd. 18, Jorgen Olsen 14, Helge Olsd. 10 and Ole Olsen 4, so she had been married since about 1806. ¶1

Life story

Her birth farm and both her dates come from the Selbu gaardshistorie, Selbu i fortid og naatid vol. 7, p. 116: '1815-1845 [Ola Jornsen], f. 1783, d. 1848. G. 1807 m. [Elen], [fodt] paa STOR-LANGLI 1787, d. 1845.' Married in 1807, born at Stor-Langli in 1787, died 1845. The 1825 census's age of 39 implies 1786 and the book says 1787. ¶3

STOR-LANGLI - great Langli - is a farm on the north side of Selbu that this project has had reason to look for on other branches. This is the marriage that connects the Slind line to it. ¶4

She is probably the 'Elen Olsdatt' entered as mother in a Selbu baptism at Slinden on 29 July 1821, which would be a child who died before 1825. ¶2

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
