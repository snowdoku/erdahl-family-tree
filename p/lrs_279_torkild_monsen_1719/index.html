About the family

Daughter: Randi Torkildsdatter (see her page), wife of Johannes Olsen Asheim. The probate's 'Randei Torkildsdatter, gift med Johannes Olsen Asheim' is taken to be her without doubt: the name, the husband and the farm agree with her 1767 marriage, the 1801 census (Randi Torkieldsdatter, 58, wife of Johannes Olssen at Asheim) and her 1821 burial at Assem, and no other Johannes Olsen of Asheim with a wife Randi is known. Confidence Confirmed as Randi's father. His place in the direct line runs through Randi, whose link as mother of Mons Johannessen is Probable. ¶6

Naming (inference): Randi's son Mons (b. 1773) may be named after Torkild's father Mons, and her son Torkield (b. about 1784) after Torkild himself. The patronymic Monsen is the only evidence for a father Mons; he is not identified. ¶8

Parents: Mons Johansen of Indre Oppedal (d. before 16 May 1739) and Randi Iversdatter. Probate 16 May - 7 Oct 1739 after Mons Johansen at Indre Oppedal, Lavik skipreide, between the widow Randi Iversdatter and their children: 'Sønnen Iver, Sønnen Johannes, 3die Peder, Sønnen Lasse, 5te Søn Torchel, alle Monses Sønner' (the son Iver, the son Johannes, the 3rd Peder, the son Lasse, the 5th son Torchel, all sons of Mons). No daughters and no ages are given. The words that follow read 'og som [u]myndige' (and being minors, or of age); the 'u' is not clearly visible, so the reading is uncertain either way. Torkild, the 5th son born about 1719, was under 25 and so a minor in 1739; his elder brothers may well have been of age. The estate was insolvent (assets 13 rd 4 ort 8 s, debts about 23 rd 5 ort 8 s), so the heirs received nothing. ¶9

Brothers (from the 1739 probate, not created as persons): Iver, Johannes, Peder and Lasse Monsen. Lasse Monsen of Indre Oppedal, very probably the 'Farbroderen' of 1754, died before 3 Jun 1783, when his probate was held at Indre Oppedal (opening also names Anna Gullachsdatter, widow or co-deceased, reading uncertain). His heirs: sons Mons Larsen (eldest, Indre Oppedal), Niels Larsen (in another skipreide) and Johannes Larsen (of age); daughters Randi Larsdatter (m. Peder Olsen [Ytre?] Berge), Gurbind[?] Larsdatter (deceased, leaving daughters Elisabeth Monsdatter, about 11, and Brithe Monsdatter, 11, whose guardians or fathers were read as Mons Danielsen[?] and Mons Torkildsen, both of Indre Oppedal), Magnilde Larsdatter (m. Hendrich Gut[tormsen?] of Indre Oppedal) and Elie Larsdatter, 23. Lasse also named a daughter Randi, which fits a shared mother Randi Iversdatter (inference). ¶11

Son Gullik (zoom-): in July 1740 at Brekke 'Torchil Monsen Asheims B. N. Gullig, Mat. Ingeber' (Torkil Monsen Asheim's child named Gullik, mother Ingeborg) was baptised; godparents Mathis Asheim, Guttorm Asheim, Lisbe of the same place, Ingeber Olsd. and Binthe Olsd. of the same place (Gulen ministerialbok 11441, canvas 18 left; 9 or 10 Jul 1740). Named after Ingeborg's father Gullov Johansen of Asseim. Not among the heirs at Ingeborg's 1754 probate, so very probably died young (inference). This is the earliest record found that gives Torkild's patronymic together with the farm Asheim and his wife Ingeber. ¶14

Brother Lasse: the 1754 probate after his first wife Ingeborg (Ytre Sogn A 7a, fol. 196a, No. 10) names 'Farbroderen Lasse Monsen Indre Oppedale' as guardian for the children, read clearly from the scan. Lasse Monsen of Indre Oppedal was therefore the children's father's brother, so Torkild and Lasse were brothers. This settles the 're-read of Farbroderen Lasse Monsen' asked for in the confidence note; it ties Torkild to a son Lasse of Mons Johansen of Indre Oppedal (1739 probate), but the confidence of the parent link is left for the orchestrator to review. ¶21

Mother's churching: on the 1st Sunday in Advent (3 Dec) 1719 at Brekke church 'Introduc: Mons Indreopdals q[vin]de effter at drengebarn N: Torchild' (churched: Mons Indre Oppedal's wife, after a boy named Torkild). Gulen ministerialbok 11438, canvas 42. Only the child's name is on the saved crop; the words naming the mother are reading of the full canvas and have not been re-checked. ¶25

Life story

Burial (per index): 'Torkild Monssøn', a married man (Mand) of Assem, aged 74 (birth year 1719 in the index), buried in Brekke in 1793; the register gives the dates 'Dom. XXI p. Trin. den 20de Oct.' and 'den 8de Nov.' (Gulen church book 1788-1798, index pg00000000769542; scan not read; card). He is the Torkild Monsen of the 1793 probates: same name, same farm, and he was alive on 13 May and dead by 11 Dec 1793. Death is set before 8 Nov 1793, the later of the two register dates; birth about 1719 is inferred from the age. ¶3

Baptism: on Saturday 21 Oct 1719, the eve of Dom. 20 p. Trinit., 'Sammen Dag ad aften Christned Mons Indreopdals bar[n] N: Torckel, Mat: Randi' (the same day in the evening Mons Indre Oppedal's child was christened, named Torkel; mother Randi). Godparents (Test.): Henrich Indreopdal, Hans ibid (of the same farm), [Erin? Kirsten?] [?]lacksdr ibid, Alid Ellingsdr and Dorthe Iffversdr (Iversdatter) ibid (surname re-read on the full canvas). The church is not named: the entry follows the confirmation examination of the young people who were to take communion the next day, and the next day's service (Dom. 20 p. Trinit.) was held at Eivindvik. Gulen ministerialbok 1716-1722 (Digitalarkivet 11438), canvas 41 (godparents). ¶23

Birth: before 21 Oct 1719, very probably in October 1719, at Indre Oppedal (inferred from the baptism, his father's farm, and his mother's churching on 3 Dec 1719). This agrees with his age of 74 at burial in 1793. ¶24

Remaining caveats: the baptism gives no patronymic for the father, and two godparent names are partly illegible. Gulen ministerialbok 11438 canvases 45, 47, 48 and 50 (spring-summer 1720) show no baptism to a Mons Indre Oppedal; on 4 Jun 1720 (c48) Mons Indreopdal was a godfather. Canvases 43, 44, 46 and 49 were not read. The other Torkil of Opdal with a wife Anne in the Brekke books of 1739-1744 is a different man; the Torkel Iversen Indre Opdal betrothed in 1737 is also a different man. ¶27

Second 1739 probate entry: on 7 Oct 1739 at Indre Oppedal the estate division No. 77 concerning his mother 'Randi Iversd.' was made between her five sons, the 5th being 'Torchel', all 'Monses Sønner' (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll 5, fol. 123b-124a, No. 77; Digitalarkivet 24160, view 126). This names Torchel as Randi Iversdatter's own son, not only Mons's. It does not by itself tie this Torchel to Asheim; the link to Torkild Monsen of Asheim stays Probable as argued above. ¶16

Marriage: betrothed at Brekke on Dom. Quasimodogeniti (5 Apr, computed) 1739 as 'Torchil Monsen Indre Opdal' to 'Ingebor Gulligsd: Osseim'; cautioners Colbein Osseim and Torcel[?] Breche; married 24 Jul 1739 (margin 'd. 24 Jul. Copul.'). Gulen ministerialbok 11441, canvas 13. The bride is his proven first wife Ingeborg Gullovsdatter: her parents' probate of April 1740 names her as married to Torkel Asheim, and the couple had a son Gullik baptised in July 1740 as children of 'Torchil Monsen Asheim' and Ingeber. ¶17

Residence in 1739: at his betrothal in April 1739 he lived at Indre Oppedal (Indre Opdal), and he had moved to his wife's farm Asheim by 13 Nov 1739 ('Torkil Asseim', cautioner). This is the first record that puts Ingeborg's husband Torkild Monsen at Indre Oppedal, the farm where Mons Johansen died before 16 May 1739 leaving a 5th son 'Torchel' (probate closed 7 Oct 1739, No. 77 also under Randi Iversdatter). It supports the identification of Torkild as Mons Johansen's son (patch 023): name, patronymic and farm now agree within a few weeks, and his paternal uncle-in-law guardian of 1754 was read as Lasse Monsen of Indre Oppedal. ¶18

Marriage before Apr 1740: the probate of 29 Apr 1740 after his father-in-law Gullov Johansen Asseim names the heir 'Ingeborre gift med Torkel Asheim', so he was married to Ingeborg by then, aged about 21. On 13 Nov 1739 'Torkil Asseim' stood surety (cautioner) at the betrothal of Iver Monsen Botnen and Lisbet (11441 canvas 15). ¶15

Baptism of daughter Randi: on Quasimodogeniti, 12 Apr 1744, at Brekke church 'Torchil Assejms born N. Rand[i]' (Torkil of Asheim's child named Randi) was baptised; mother 'Ingeber'; godparents Guttorm Mathisen, Bendict Assejm, Ingeber Olsdatter and Siri Assejm; the mother was churched on Exaudi Sunday, 17 May 1744 (Gulen ministerialbok 1736-1746, source 11441, canvas 32). The patronymic is not written; this Torkil of Asheim with wife Ingeborg is taken to be Torkild Monsen of Asheim, whose first wife was Ingeborg Gullaksdatter and whose daughter Randi married Johannes Olsen Asheim. ¶4

Correction (from audit; extends the 'Current grade ' note): in the note 'Brothers', the statement that Lasse Monsen 'died before 3 Jun 1783, when his probate was held' is wrong: the 1783 record at Indre Oppedal was a division in the lifetime (skifte i levende live) of the living couple Lasse Monsen and his wife Anna Gullachsdatter, and Lasse was buried at Brekke on 12 Feb 1788. Lasse Monsen is Lasse Monsen. ¶29

Probate: on 11 Dec 1793 the probate (skifte, estate division) after the late Torkild Monsen was held on the farm Asheim, between his widow Brithe Olsdatter and his heirs. Heirs by his first wife Ingeborg Gullaksdatter ('for lang Tid siden ved Døden afgangne', long since dead): (a) eldest daughter Sigri Torkildsdatter, married to Mons Torkildsen of Ytre Oppedal; (b) second daughter 'Randei' Torkildsdatter, married to Johannes Olsen Asheim, present; (c) third daughter Anna Torkildsdatter, married to Iver Nielsen(?) of Plougland, present. Heirs by the widow Brithe Olsdatter: (a) Ingeborg Torkildsdatter, married to Lars Knudsen, farmer on (a share of) Asheim, present; (b) Belaug Torkildsdatter, 25, whose guardian (formynder) was her brother-in-law Lars Knudsen Asheim, who was also the widow's guardian-at-law (lauverge). Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll A 11b, fol. 533b-534a, No. 79. ¶1

Division in life: on 13 May 1793, at the written request of the living couple Torkel Monsen and Brithe Olsdatter, their joint estate at Asheim was divided among the same heirs, listed in the same order ('Rannei Torkelsdatter, gift med Johannes Olsen Asheim, tilstede'; Belaug then 24). Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll A 11b, fol. 502b, No. 25. ¶2

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 4

Confidence Probable: (1) the 1739 probate names a son Torchel (Torkel/Torkild) of Mons Johansen and Randi Iversdatter of Indre Oppedal, and Torkild of Asheim's patronymic is Monsen; (2) at the 1754 probate after Torkild's first wife the guardian beside the maternal uncle was read as 'Farbroderen Lasse Monsen Indre Oppedal' (paternal uncle Lasse Monsen of Indre Oppedal), and the 1739 probate names a son Lasse; Lasse Monsen of Indre Oppedal's own probate followed in 1783; (3) Mons Johansen is the only Mons in the Oppedal probate index before 1760; (4) Torkild's only known son by his first wife was named Mons (b. about 1753), which fits naming after the paternal grandfather, and his second daughter was named Randi (b. 1744); his eldest daughter, however, was Sigri, so the naming evidence for Randi Iversdatter is weak (inference). ¶10a

Confidence of parents kept at Probable: the betrothal strengthens but does not settle the link. It names no parents; Torkel Monsen was a common name, and notes another Torkil of Opdal (with a wife Anne) in the Brekke books of 1739-1744, who could equally be from Indre Oppedal; and a young man at Indre Oppedal in 1739 could be a servant there rather than a son of the house. Confirmed still needs a record that states the relation: a clean re-read of 'Farbroderen Lasse Monsen' in the 1754 probate (24163 view 196), a land or probate record naming Torkild Monsen Asheim as Mons Johansen's son or Lasse Monsen's brother, or identification of the other Torkil of Opdal (wife Anne) as a different man. ¶19

Confidence of parents raised from Probable to Confirmed. Records that moved the grade: (1) the baptism of 21 Oct 1719 (Gulen ministerialbok 11438, canvas 41) is a primary record naming the child Torkel's father as Mons of Indre Oppedal and his mother as Randi; this is the direct parent record the audit said was missing. (2) Mons Johansen of Indre Oppedal's probate of 16 May 1739 (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll 5, Nr. 76) and Randi Iversdatter's lifetime division of 7 Oct 1739 (Nr. 77) name the widow Randi Iversdatter and a 5th son Torchel, so Mons Johansen is the Mons of Indre Oppedal married to a Randi. (3) The other Mons known at Indre Oppedal then, Mons Iversen, was betrothed in 1717 and married on 2 Jan 1718 at Brekke to Giertlaug Lassesdatter of Ytre Brekke, not a Randi (Gulen ministerialbok 11438, canvases 16 and 21). (4) The child is Torkild Monsen of Asheim: he was 'Torchil Monsen Indre Opdal' at his betrothal at Brekke in April 1739 (Gulen ministerialbok 11441, canvas 13); his burial in 1793 at 74 gives birth in 1719; and at his wife Ingeborg's probate in 1754 (Ytre Sogn A 7a, fol. 196a, No. 10) 'Farbroderen Lasse Monsen Indre Oppedale', a son of Mons Johansen named in the 1739 probate, was the paternal uncle of his children. The earlier note 'Confidence of parents kept at Probable ' is superseded. ¶26

Current grade (from patch 032, audit): both parent links are Confirmed. His parents are Mons Johansen of Indre Oppedal (see his page) and Randi Iversdatter (see her page), from his baptism of 21 Oct 1719 read with the 1739 probates. The grades in the notes 'Naming (inference)' ('he is not identified'), 'Confidence Probable ' and 'Confidence of parents kept at Probable ' are superseded. The note 'Homonym, unresolved' is also out of date: Iver Monsen of Botnen (see his page) is now linked as a son of the same parents, so he is Torkild's brother (patches 034 and 036). ¶28

Corrections 3

Correction: the earlier note that his father Mons 'is not identified' is superseded; he is now identified, as Probable, as Mons Johansen of Indre Oppedal (see above). ¶13

Correction (applied): in the note 'Baptism of daughter Randi', the baptism on Quasimodogeniti 1744 was on 5 Apr 1744, not 12 Apr, and the churching of his wife on Exaudi Sunday on 10 May 1744, not 17 May. Denmark-Norway's improved calendar put Easter 1744 on 29 Mar, a week before the Gregorian Easter used for the earlier dates. Probable until the '5/4' margin in 11441 c32 is read at full size. ¶20

Correction (, agreeing with): the wedding of Torkild Monsen and Ingeborg Gullaksdatter was on 4 Jul 1739, not 24 Jul 1739. The margin beside their betrothal in Gulen ministerialbok 11441, canvas 13, reads 'd. 4 Jul. / Copul.' (d. = den, the; Copul. = married). The first glyph is the scribe's looped 'd', the same glyph that opens every other margin note in that column ('d. 18 April introd.', 'd. 17 April introd.', 'd. 25 Jul. sepult'); took it for a '2'. This supersedes the date '24 Jul 1739' in the 'Marriage ' note and in patch 025; the marriage is set to 4 JUL 1739 at Brekke church. High confidence. ¶22

Alternatives considered and set aside 4

Second wife (note only, not linked): Brithe Olsdatter, his widow in Dec 1793, mother of his daughters Ingeborg and Belaug (Belaug b. about 1768 from her age of 25). The marriage was therefore between Mar 1754 (Ingeborg's probate) and about 1767. Not searched in the Gulen marriages. In the 1801 census a Brite Olsdatter, b. about 1734, widow for the second time (Enke 2dn gang), lived at Asseim (card); probably she, which would make Torkild her second husband (inference). ¶5

Possible further children (not linked; sibling notes only): Son (stated): Mons Torkildsen, an infant at his mother Ingeborg's probate of 6 Mar 1754 (b. about 1753), very probably the Mogens Torkildssøn, a bachelor (Dreng) of Assem, b. about 1754 per index, buried 7 Jan 1783, which would explain his absence from the 1793 heirs. Possible further children: Magnilde Torkildsdatter, an unmarried woman (Pige) of Assem, b. about 1761 per index, buried 3 Jan 1783 (Gulen church book 1776-1787, burials p. 69b, nos. 19 and 16; index only). Both are dead before 1793, which is why they are not heirs; no parents are named. Also Orloug Torkieldsdatter, b. about 1769-70, in 1801 a servant at Asseim in the household of Lars Knudsen (Torkild's son-in-law), buried 2 Jan 1826 as 'Pige og Inderste' (unmarried lodger) of Asseim; if Torkild's, she would be a daughter of Brithe Olsdatter, yet she is not named among the heirs in 1793, which counts against it. All three are inferred from patronymic and farm only. ¶7

Not Confirmed: no record read calls Torkild of Asheim a son of Mons Johansen; the 1739 probate gives no ages and no residence for the sons; and the 'Farbroderen' reading of 1754 is marked uncertain by its reader. ¶10b

Homonym, unresolved: Iver Monsen of Botnen, Brekke (see his page, b. about 1705 per burial index, married by 1735, father of a daughter b. about 1728 and of a son Mons by his second marriage) shares the name of the eldest brother Iver, and his dates fit an eldest son of Mons Johansen. But nothing links him to Indre Oppedal or to Mons Johansen, and none of his known daughters is named Randi. Not linked and not excluded; the 1739 'umyndige' reading is too uncertain to decide it. If he were the brother, he would appear twice in this line (pedigree collapse). ¶12
