Life story

Probate after Bjørn Jonsen, Skåsheim, 27 May 1729 (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll 3b, fol. 343a): 'hans efterlatte Hustrue Brithe Anders datter' and 'hendes med den Sal. Mand avlede Børn, Jon 17 aar gl. og Anders 15 aar gl.'. Her brother (the boys' morbroder) Anders Røsem[?] was present. She was judged able to be guardian of her sons. ¶1

The 1765 probate of a 'Brite Andersdatter' at Hagen (Ytre Sogn 8a fol. 290b, No. 46, 26 Jul 1765) is not hers: it is after two children, Anders Andersen and Britte Andersdatter, of the late Anders Larsen and his widow Synneve Knudsdatter. Her own later probate is still not found. ¶3

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the grade · 1 alternative set aside

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Probable: the probate proves she was Bjørn's widow and the mother of his son Jon; the identification of that Jon with Jon Bjørnsen Bale is by name and age (see Jon Bjørnsen). ¶2

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Ruled out: the probate of 'Brite Andersdatter' at Valsvik, Kvamsøy skipreide, 31 May 1742 (Ytre Sogn 6a fol. 216a, No. 34; index pa00000000521254) is not hers: she was the wife of Roland Nielsen Walswig and died childless, her heirs being her siblings (brothers Eric and Storch[?] Andersen, sister Giertrud, and others). Other Brite Andersdtr rows in the Ytre Sogn register 1729-1790 (Hagen, Tungodden [Utvær], Eiken, Nese, Hove, Bilsbakke, Torsnes, Ulvestad) were not read; Tungodden is outside the district. No Anders Bjørnsen probate is indexed 1735-1800. ¶4
