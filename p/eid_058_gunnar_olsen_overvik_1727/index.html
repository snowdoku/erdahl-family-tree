About the family

Father of Ane Gundersdatter (see her page). Baptism of his daughter on the 1st Sunday in Advent, 3 December 1758: '(3) Ane. Forældr: Gunar Ols: Vigen, Kari Andersd: faddr: Per Antons: Tosten Ols: Kari Andersd: etc.' - baptised Ane, parents Gunnar Olsen of Vigen and Kari Andersdatter, godparents Per Antonsen, Tosten Olsen, Kari Andersdatter and others (Selbu ministerialbok 1757-1801, canvas 7 left page, crop). 'Vigen' (the bay) is this clerk's short form of Overvik (see the next note). In 1758 he had stood godfather ('Gunar Vigen', 3rd Sunday after Trinity) to a daughter of Per Antonsen, who is first sponsor here. ¶1

His parentage is now proved by a primary record: the probate after Ole Gundersen Wigen, 17 March 1760 (Skifteprotokoll 5, 24755, fol. 102a-102b), lists 'Sønnen Gunder Olsen 34 aar' first among the heirs, with his mother 'Enke Karen Torstens datter' and his siblings Torsten Olsen 30, Ingebrigt Olsen 25, Hans[?] Olsen 17 and Siri Olsdatter 20. The godfather 'Tosten Ols:' at his daughter Ane's baptism in 1758 is therefore probably his brother. His own confidence is left at Probable because it rests on the link to Ane Gundersdatter, which this record does not touch. ¶7

Life story

Baptism (inferred to be his): 14 December 1727 (3rd Sunday in Advent), 'døbt Ole Gunders: Vigens barn Nom: Gunder' - baptised Ole Gundersen of Vigen's child, named Gunder (16310 canvas 110 right). Taken as his own baptism because the adult of 1758 is 'Gunar Ols. Vigen' - patronymic Olsen and the same farm form - and the bygdebok gives Ola Gunnarsen's son Gunnar the birth year 1727. This 1727 hand records no mother or godparents. The other Gunnar Olsen of 1759, a land soldier who married Agnis Jonsdatter, is never called Vigen and is a different man. ¶3

Death: 1767, from the bygdebok only (vol. 6 p. 327: 'Gunnar,... 1727, død... 1767', word boxes). His burial is not yet read; complete read of the 1757-1801 register runs only to February 1767. Children per the same page include Kari (1756) and Anne (1758), with further children born 1760-1768; the Ola Gunnarsen born 1765 who held part of Overvik 1792-1815 (p. 334) is a probable son. Inference only. ¶4

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

3 on the grade · 1 on the identification · 1 search with no result · 1 open question

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 3

Rated Probable. Primary records name him as Ane's father and as Ole Gundersen Vigen's son; what keeps the whole chain at Probable is that no primary record yet ties the Ane of 1758 to Anne Gundersdatter of Slinden explicitly (see Ane Gundersdatter Slind), and that the Overvik equivalence rests on one clerk's usage plus the bygdebok. Records that would settle it: his burial in 1767 (16311, canvases about 20-24) with an age near 40, and any record calling Anne of Slinden 'Overvik' or 'Vigen'. ¶5

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed. The skifte after his father, Skifteprotokoll 5 (24755) fol. 102a-102b, entry No. 17: 'Anno 1760 d. 17 Marts' a skifte was held on the farm Wigen in Selbu after the late 'Ole Gundersen Wigen' between his surviving widow 'Karen Torstens Datter' and the five children born to them in marriage, four sons and a daughter: 1. the son Gunder Olsen, 34; 2. Torsten Olsen, 30; 3. the son Ingebrigt Olsen, 25; 4. the son [Jens?] Olsen, 17; 5. the daughter Siri Olsdatter, 20. All were at home on the farm. The typed register of the volume lists it as 'Ole Gundersen, Øvervik, Selbu, 1760, fol. 102'. Gunder Olsen, 34 in 1760 (born about 1726), fits his baptism before 14 Dec 1727 and his place as Gunnar Olsen of Overvik. Niels Gundersen Wigen took part in the skifte. ¶8

Audit (1 Nov 2026): downgraded from Confirmed to Probable. The 1760 probate of Ole Gundersen Wigen proves Gunder's parentage and places him at Wigen/Overvik. That is a link upward, and Ola Gunnarsen and Kari Torstensdatter stay Confirmed as his parents. The link that places him in the pedigree, his fatherhood of Ane Gundersdatter of Slinden (see her page), still rests on identifying the Ane baptised in 1758 to 'Gunar Ols: Vigen' with Ane of Slinden. His own earlier note said his confidence stays Probable for exactly this reason, and the upgrade record does not touch it. ¶9

The case for this identification 1

Why 'Vigen' is Overvik: the same clerk buried 'Ole Gunars: Vigen 58 aar gl.' on 9 December 1759 (canvas 8 right page). The Overvik gaardshistorie has exactly one such man - the holder Ola Gunnarsen, born 1701, died 1759 (Selbu i fortid og naatid vol. 6, p. 326) - so name, patronymic, age and death year all agree, and the household the register calls Vigen is the bygdebok's Overvik household. The same form is used thirty years earlier: 'Ole Gunders. Vigens barn Nom: Gunder' was baptised on 3rd Advent, 14 December 1727 (Selbu ministerialbok 1699-1737, canvas 110 right page, crop), and the bygdebok's Gunnar, son of Ola Gunnarsen, is born in 1727 (vol. 6 p. 327). No separate farm 'Vigen' has turned up in any Selbu reading on this line. ¶2

Searches that found nothing 1

Searched without result, for a record tying him to Ane Gundersdatter of Slinden: the typed registers of Stjør- og Verdal skifteprotokoller 24753-24762 list no probate after a Gunder/Gunnar Olsen of Overvik/Vigen (d. 1767) or after his widow Kari Andersdatter; the Over Wigen probate of 1768 is Niels Gundersen's, with no Gunnar heirs (24756 fol. 132). The handwritten register of Strinda og Selbu 24745 (1827-40) has no Ole Gundersen Overvik (his probable son Ola, b. 1765). Selbu confirmation lists give only name, farm and age, so Ane's confirmation cannot name him. Still Probable. Remaining routes: a probate after Kari Andersdatter or after one of Ane's siblings (for Ola Gunnarsen b. 1765, holder until 1815, look in the unindexed Strinda og Selbu 24744, 1807-27), naming 'Anne Gundersdatter, married to Jørgen Olsen Slinden'. ¶10

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Biography gaps: his exact birth date rests on the 14 Dec 1727 baptism taken as his (Probable), and his marriage to Kari Andersdatter (before 1758) falls in the 1747-56 register gap. His death in 1767 is from the bygdebok only and his burial is not read; the 1767 burial would be in 16311 (canvases about 20-24, past the February 1767 end of the complete read), and an age near 40 there would settle it. His confirmation and any probate are not recorded. ¶6
