About the family

Father of Dordi Johnsdatter Stokke (see her page), rated Probable. The chain: (1) the 1801 census shows his daughter Daardi Jonsdatter, unmarried, 28, at Velve, the only Dordi Jonsdatter in the 1801 Selbu census of the right age who was still single (the others at Kiosnaes and Slinden were married or married in 1802); (2) the 1808 marriage register names the bride of John Olsen Stokke as 'Pigen Dordie Jonsdatter Welve' - an unmarried girl of Velve; (3) at the baptism of her son Ole in 1809 a godfather was 'Michel Jonsen Velve', who fits her brother Mikkel Jonssen b. ~1791 of this household (buried 1864 as Mikkel Johnsen, gaardbruker at Velve, b. 1791); (4) at the baptism of her son Jon in 1813 the first godfather was 'Jon Halvorsen... aelder' (the elder), the name of her 1801 father, though the farm word after his name is uncertain (read as Evjen or Efjen, not Velve). Her census age 28 (Feb 1801) and burial age 67 (Aug 1839) agree on a birth in Feb-Aug 1772. ¶2

Life story

1762 extra tax list (Probable): 'Jon Halvorsen' is listed with his sister Mali Halvorsdatter as a child over 12 of Helge[?] Olsdatter, probably a widow, at Velve 797. His father Halvor was probably dead by 1762. Why Probable: a single Jon Halvorsen at Velve whose age fits; his baptism (about 1743) is not yet found, and a probate after Halvor or Helge would confirm. ¶7

Probate of his mother, 17 Dec 1764 (Skifteprotokoll 5, 24755, fol. 451a): heirs 'Søn Jon Halvorsen 20 aar gl., D. Mallie Halvors Datter 16 aar', at Velve. That puts his birth about 1744 and confirms his parents, Halvor Jonsen Velve and Helge Olsdatter, which the 1762 extra tax had shown only probably. ¶10

A soldier. Betrothed Whit Monday, 8 June 1772 ('Trolovede Soldat Jon Halvorsøn & Birthe Michelsdatter') and married 7th Sunday after Trinity, 26 July 1772 ('Copuleret S. Jon Halvors. & Birthe Micheldd.'), Selbu ministerialbok 16311, canvases 51 right and 52 left. Their daughter Dorethe was baptised 4 Apr 1773 with father 'Jon Halvors. Velve'. Confidence raised to Confirmed as Dordi's father on that baptism. ¶5a

Named as 'Jon Haldorsen Welve', husband of Berithe Michelsdatter, in the probate of his mother-in-law Brynille Pedersdatter, Velve, 20 Feb 1786 (24762 fol. 521b). ¶11

Alive in 1792 and Aug 1798 as 'Jon Halvorsen Welve', a sponsor (16311 c146, c226-236). No burial before 1801. ¶8

1801 census: 'Jon Halvorssen', 58, Husbonde (head of household), 'Bonde og gaardbeboer' (farmer and farm occupier), at Waelve (Velve) farm 0048, household 05, Selbu, with marital status 'Hans 2det, haendes 1. aegteskab' - his second marriage, his wife's first. Birth about 1743 is inferred from the age 58 on 1 Feb 1801. Household: wife Beret Mikkelsdatter, 54; 'Deres born' (their children) Daardi Jonsdatter 28 (unmarried), Brynil Jonsdatter 22, Marit Jonsdatter 14 and Mikkel Jonssen 10; a servant, Ole Baarssen, 26. ¶1

The 1801 census calls this his second marriage and her first; with the 1772 marriage to Birthe Mikkelsdatter being his, he had an earlier wife before 1772 (not found; Selbu has no register 1747-56 and 1757-72 not searched for him). ¶6

1801 census: Jon Halvorssen, 58, head of a household at Wælve (Velve), with Beret Mikkelsdatter, 54. Burial not found in the indexed Selbu burials 1801-70 (only a name-only 'Halvorssøn' of 1825, pg00000007096220). ¶9
