Life story

Smith, later boilermaker, in Årstad and Laksevåg (Bergen area). ¶1

Baptism: John, born 28 Jul 1853, son of husmann Lars Aanensen Skreaa and his wife Serine Hansdatter. He was baptised at Skåre church. The baptism was entered in the Avaldsnes register on 6 Dec 1853 under the date 14 Aug 1853 (entry 173, page 106). Godparents came from Nordre Eike, Skreaa and Holmes. The 1910 census gives exactly the same birth date (28 Jul 1853), born Avaldsnes. ¶3

The surname Skrede comes from the place Skreå/Skreaa in Avaldsnes parish, where his father was a husmann in 1853. It is spelled Skrean or Skraaen in the baptism registers. It has nothing to do with Skrede in Jølster/Sunnfjord. It appears later as Skræ (1875), Skrene/Skreå/Skreau (1886-1890), Skrede (1891) and Skreo (1910). ¶5

In the 1865 census he is 'John Larsen', 13, at Slettetræ (Kolnes district) with Lars Auensen and Seri K. Hansdatter. This CONFIRMS the earlier speculative link. ¶6

Married the widow Ingeborg Rasmusdatter 19 Nov 1874, St. Jørgens Hospital parish, Bergen. ¶2

The 1874 marriage scan (St. Jørgens Hospital parish, page 82, entry 32) reads: 'Ungkarl, Værkarbeider John Larsen', born Avaldsnes, living at Solheimsvik, age 21. The bride was 'Enke Ingeborg Rasmusdatter', born Ladvik, age 24. Groom's father: 'Huusmand Lars Aanonsen Skreaa'. Bride's father: 'Gaardmand Rasmus Iversen Botnen'. ¶4

1875 census: John Larsen Skræ, born 1853 in Avaldsnes, head of household, married, indoor smith at the same workshop (indr. smed i.d.m. verksted), with his wife Ingeborg Rasmusdatter at Viken, Aarstad sogn, Årstad prestegjeld. ¶8

1891 census: Jahn Larsen Skrede, born 1852 in Avaldsnes herred, head of family, married, with his wife Ingebarg Rasmusdtr. Botn and seven children, at Laksevåg, tellingskrets 1, Askøy, listed after the household of Peder Hansen Hage. His occupation is blank in the index. ¶9

1910 census: kjedelsmed (boilermaker) at the works, Nordre Laksevåg, Askøy. His wife is listed as Engeborg Larsen, b. 8 Aug 1851. ¶7

1910 census: John Larsen Skreo, born 28 Jul 1853 in Avaldsnes, head of household, married, boilermaker at the works (kjedelsmed paa værket), at Nordre Laksevaag, Askøy, with his wife Engeborg, Josefine Larsen (born 1897, daughter) (not established as John's daughter: a Josefine Johnsen b. 1897 Aarstad was in the Lille Damsgård household of the other John Larsen in 1900, lrs_028/lrs_095) and Herman Johannesen (his wife's son) (listed b. 1841, which conflicts with a baptism of 1 Sep 1871; a son of Ingeborg born before her 1874 marriage is not yet explained, lrs_095). ¶10

The case for this identification 1

Identity: he is not the John Larsen born 1872 in Manger who lived at Lille Damsgård with another Ingeborg Rasmusdatter; every census above gives his birthplace as Avaldsnes and a birth year of 1852-1853, matching his 1853 baptism. ¶11
