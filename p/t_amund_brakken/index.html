Life story

A man recorded only as 'Amund' of Brækken, born about 1723, was buried at Grytten in 1799. He is a speculative father of Peder Amundsen of Brækken: the patronymic, the farm and the age all fit, and he is the only Amund recorded at Brækken. The burial entry gives no patronymic of his own, so the link is not proven. ¶1

An Ole Amundsen of Brækken stood godfather at Grytten on 1 Jul 1800, and would on this reading be another of his sons. ¶2

How sure are we?

Speculative. A plausible candidate only. Treat this link as a question, not a fact.
