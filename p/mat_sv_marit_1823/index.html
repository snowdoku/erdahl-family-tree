Life story

1825 census: 'Marit Johnsd.', aged 2, Datter, in the household of Joh Siverts., gaardmand at Eidem. The youngest of the four children in the house and the youngest sister of Sivert Johnsen b. 1815. ¶1

Probate: 'Marith Johnsdatter', 8 1/2 years, daughter, is an heir in the skifte after her father John Sivertsen Eidum, closed 8 November 1832 (skifteutlodningsprotokoll 1, fol. 129b-130). Her age gives a birth about autumn 1823, which agrees with her age of 2 in the 1825 census. Her guardian was her paternal uncle John Sivertsen Eidem. ¶2

Married by 1854 to Erik Torstensen Uglem, who signed the 1854 deed of Eidem as 'gift med Afdødes Datter Marit', husband of the late John Sivertsen's daughter Marit (Pantebok 11 fol. 167b). ¶4
