About the family

Oline Slind's father, and the man whose given name is the G in 'Oline G. Slind Eidem'. Baptised in Selbu on 11 February 1844 as 'Gunder Olsen Slinden', born 13 January 1844, son of Ole Gundersen and Berete Gundersdatter of Slinden; entered at page 130 serial 18 of the Selbu local parish register 1842-1858. The 1910 census gives his birth date as 11 January 1844, two days earlier than the register; a baptismal entry made within a month of the birth is the better authority. ¶1

Nine children, not eight. Wave 3 listed seven living plus two more implied. The gaardshistorie's list for him is: Ole d.e. 1872, Berit 1875, Marit 1878, [Oline] 1881, Ole d.y. 1884, Gunder 1887, Karen 1890 and BRYNHILD 1895 - who died in 1896 - which with the Beret born 1875 who died in 1895 makes nine births and seven children who reached adulthood. ¶13

Life story

Printed source: his birth year 1844, his wife's 1847, and the birth years of all seven of his children - 1872, 1875, 1878, 1881, 1884, 1887, 1890 - occur together on page 100 of Selbu i fortid og naatid, bind 7 (1992), within the SLINDAN NORDRE chapter whose heading stands on page 92. The same page carries the given names Gunder (twice), Kari, Oline, Marit (twice), Karen and Berit, the farm name Slind, and Eidem, Amerika and Minnesota, with the emigration years 1900, 1902 and 1922. This is the printed account of his household. ¶6

Context - the holding: the Slindan chapters of volume 7 begin on printed pages 86, 92 (SLINDAN NORDRE), 97, 112 (VESTRE), 132 (OESTRE), 147, 149 (a second NORDRE) and 156 and 161 (SOENDRE); the farm register for the volume is on page 424. The holder tenures printed in the Slindan nordre chapter run 1701-1712, 1713-1742, 1743-1750 (p. 91), 1751-1773, 1774-1783, 1774-1805, 1806-1811 (p. 92), 1812-1836 (p. 94) and 1828-1845 (p. 97) - the backbone of the farm's descent, and the quickest way to place each generation of this line. The matrikkel number 141/2 is printed on page 141. ¶7

Context - how this page citation was obtained without reading the page: every volume of the Selbu bygdebok is access-restricted to Norwegian IP addresses on nb.no, so neither the page images nor the OCR text can be downloaded from the United States. The National Library's IIIF Content Search service, however, is open to anyone and returns, for any quoted phrase, the exact page and the pixel box of every occurrence, with the surrounding context blanked. A page's contents can therefore be profiled by asking it a long series of yes/no questions - one phrase search per name, per year, per place - and the printed folio number is itself OCR'd in the bottom margin, which fixes the page number exactly. Nothing below is read off a page image; every item is a retrieved search hit. Confidence is accordingly Probable, never Confirmed. ¶8

The gaardshistorie's dates for the couple are a near-perfect match to the parish registers already in hand - f. 1844 against a baptism of 13 January 1844, g. 1871 against a marriage of 17 July 1871, and his wife f. 1847 against a baptism of 25 July 1847 - which is why its two new death dates are worth having. ¶10

Gravestone, Selbu Kirke Cemetery, Mebonden, Selbu, Section J: 'Gunder O. Slind, 11 JAN 1844 - 3 AUG 1916'. The stone settles two things wave 3 left open. His BIRTH DATE: the baptismal register says 13 January 1844 and the 1910 census says 11 January; the stone says 11 January, so two independent records now agree against the register, and both readings are recorded here rather than one being preferred. His DEATH DATE: the Selbu gaardshistorie says 8 August 1916 and the stone says 3 August 1916; the stone is the primary object and is preferred, with the book's date noted. He was 72. ¶14

His plot is in Section J of Selbu kirkegaard, his widow Kari's in Section A row 3 no. 16, his daughter Marit's in Section L row 6 no. 24 and his daughter Karen's in Section K row 2 no. 13 - the family is scattered across the churchyard rather than gathered in one plot, which is normal for a Norwegian parish burial ground where graves were reused on a cycle. ¶15

Tenure and occupation, census by census. 1865: son at home at Slinden, aged 22, 'hjaelper Faderen med Gaardbruget' - helping his father with the farm. 1875: already head of his own holding at Slinden, rural residence 0006, lopenr 184, 'Gaardbruger og Leilaending' - farmer and tenant; he sowed one barrel of barley, two of oats and three of potatoes and kept a horse, a foal, three cows, four calves, five sheep, nine goats and a pig. 1891: 'grdbr. leilaending' at the same lopenr 184. 1900: 'Gaardmand Leilaending, Jordbrug' at Slinden nordre, gnr 141 bnr 2. 1910: 'gaardbruger' at Slindan nordre, same matrikkel number. A leilending is a tenant holding land on a lease, as against a selveier who owns it; Gunder Olsen was a tenant for the whole of his recorded life. ¶3

Marriage: 17 July 1871 in Selbu, aged 27, to Kari Olsdatter, born 1847 at Stokke. The entry, at page 32 serial 14 of the main register and page 253 serial 14 of the clerk's copy, names the groom's father as 'O. Gundersen' and the bride's father as 'O. Johnsen', which is what proves both parent links. The forlovere (witnesses) are on the scanned page but were not transcribed. ¶2

The farm and the tenancy, precisely. He held the bruk from 1871, THE YEAR HE MARRIED, to 1920, and the bruk is named in the gaardshistorie as SLINDAN NORDRE, NEDPAA-GARDEN - 'the farm down below' - gnr 141 bnr 2, a holding split off from Slindan nordre, whose chapter runs from page 97 to page 101 of volume 7. He did not inherit it: his father Ole Gundersen was tenant of a different Slind bruk, lopenr 188, until his death in 1897, and the gaardshistorie says Gunnar came to Nedpaa-Garden 'fra... Slindan' - from elsewhere on Slindan - in 1871, taking over from a holder born 1821 who had died in 1868. He took a lease, not a patrimony. ¶11

There is a PHOTOGRAPH of him. Page 98 of the same volume carries a picture whose caption, reconstructed from the word positions, names 'Gunder G. Slind, Kari Olsdatter... Olsdatter Slind, Marit Gundersdatter Slind' under a heading mentioning Vikvarvet - so his widow Kari, his son Gunder G. (born 1887), his daughter Marit (born 1878) and a fourth woman. It is the only known likeness of Oline Slind's mother and of three of her siblings. ¶12

In the Selbu baptismal index he appears as the father of children at Slinden on 17 March 1878, 18 April 1881 and 6 April 1884, at 'Slinde' on 30 October 1887 and at 'Slind' on 15 February 1891, each time described as gaardmand or gaardbruger. ¶4

Death: he is alive and head of the household at the 1910 census of 1 December 1910, and by the 1920 census of 1 December 1920 his wife Kari Olsdatter is entered at Slind nordre as 'hp e' - head of household, widow - 'Driver bygslet gaard', working a leased farm. He therefore died between those two dates. The Selbu burial transcription on Digitalarkivet stops with the register that ends in 1902, so the exact date is not in the index; it is on the unindexed pages of the Selbu burial register for 1903-1940. ¶5

HIS DEATH DATE, from the Selbu gaardshistorie: 8 AUGUST 1916. Wave 3 could only bracket it as between 1 December 1910 and 1 December 1920, from the censuses. Selbu i fortid og naatid vol. 7, page 100, in the holder list of the bruk Slindan nordre (Nedpaa-Garden): '1871-1920 Gunnar Olsen d.e. fra... Slindan, f. 1844, d. 8.8.1916. G. 1871 m. Kari... fra Midti-Stokkan, f. 1847, d. 23.5.1935.' The book spells him GUNNAR, the Trondelag form of Gunder. 'd.e.' is den eldre, the elder, distinguishing him from a younger namesake. Rated Probable rather than Confirmed because a gaardshistorie is a published reconstruction, not a register; the Selbu burial register for 1916 is the record that would confirm it. ¶9
