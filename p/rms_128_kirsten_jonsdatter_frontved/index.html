Life story

NEW, mother of Targie (Tarjei) Asgiersen Frontved (see his page); graded Confirmed on that link. Record: skifteprotokoll 10 (DA 25674) No. 695, fol. 573a (view 575, right page): 'Anno 1765 den 11te Novbr.' the sorenskriver's court met 'paa Gaarden øvre Frontvedt i Nissedals annex og Hvidesøe Præstegield' with the lagrettemenn Joen Carlsøn(?) Fone and Targei Alphsøn Tretten and Asbiørn Halvorsøn Grimstvedt, to register and divide what 'afgangne Kirsten Joensdatter' left, 'imellem hendes efterlevende Enkemand Asger Targeisøn og deres udi Ægteskab tilsammen avlede Børn, Sønner Targei Asgiersøn myndig, Joen Asgiersøn myndig, Lars Asgiersøn 24 aar gl., Hans Asgiersøn 19 aar gl., Knud Asgiersøn [age not certain] og Joen Asgiersøn den yngere [about 15] aar gl.'; the father and the sons Targei, Joen and Lars were present. The skifte names Targei Asgiersøn as one of the children she and her husband Asger Targeisøn had together. Continuity to the tree's Targie: the same six sons, in the same order, are the heirs at Asgier Tarjesen's own skifte on the same farm, øvre Frontved, 16 years later (26 Oct 1781, DA 25677 No. 448), where Tarje Asgiersen says he bought his share of Frontved 'ved Skifte efter hans [the father's] forhen afdøde Hustrue Kirsten Jonsdatter afhandlet 11te Novbr 1765'. The sons Targie and Joen were already of age in 1765, so Targie was born before about 1740. No rival Targie on Frontved is named. ¶1
