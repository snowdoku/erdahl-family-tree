Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Speculative as father of Kristi Lassesdatter Brekke (graded on that link). For: (1) Aaland (1932) calls her 'Kristi Lassed. Brekke', i.e. a Lasse's daughter connected with Brekke; (2) he lived on Brekke itself, the farm named in her byname; in the part of the Oppstryn sogn section read (rows 66000-66210: Gudal, Skåre, Sunndal, Folven, Hjelle, Røgh, Erdal, Berrie, Tjellaug, Gredung, Grønfur, Mork, Doreflot, Fosnes, Sandvik, Nesje, Aaning, Brekke, Tunold, Ornes, Melland, Berstad, Holmevik, Dispen) the only other Lasse is 'Lase' on Jelde (Hjelle, pt00000000066034, listed with Karen), a rival candidate with no Brekke connection; (3) Brekke is the next farm after Aaning in the list, where Kristi lived with Laurits Simonsen. ¶2a

Records that disagree 1

Against or open: the Hjelle Lase is a rival; Aaland's byname Brekke may belong to her husband (also called Brekke), not to her birth; this Lase could equally be her brother or unrelated; no record names her father. A placeholder candidate only. Birth before about 1605 assumes he was her father (she was born before about 1620). ¶2b
