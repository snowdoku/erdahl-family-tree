Life story

1825 census: 'Anders Johns.', aged 5 and a half, Soen, in the household of Joh Siverts., gaardmand at Eidem. Younger brother of Sivert Johnsen b. 1815. ¶1

Probate: 'Anders', 11 1/2 years, son, is an heir in the skifte after his father John Sivertsen Eidum, closed 8 November 1832 (skifteutlodningsprotokoll 1, fol. 129b-130); his guardian was his paternal uncle John Sivertsen Eidem. ¶4

Named in the 1854 deed of Eidem as 'Anders Johnsen Eidum, Afdødes anden Søn', the second son of the late John Sivertsen, joining his mother and siblings in conveying the farm to his brother Sivert (Pantebok 11 fol. 167b). ¶7

He is very probably the ANDERS JONSEN entered at the 1865 census as Husmand med Jord at VOKKEN, rural residence 0063 in the Eidem cluster, five doors from his brother's freehold - the ordinary fate of a farmer's younger son in Selbu, a cottage on the family farm rather than a share of it. ¶2a

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Rated Probable: the name, age and farm agree but no record states the relationship. ¶2b

Confidence raised from Probable to Confirmed: the 1832 probate names him as a son of John Sivertsen Eidum and Sigrid Bersvendsdatter. ¶5

Corrections 1

Correction: birth moved from about 1819 to about 1820. His age of 11 1/2 at the registration of 8 March 1832 gives a birth about mid or late 1820, and his age of 5 1/2 in the 1825 census (November 1825) gives about mid 1820. His baptism in the Selbu register (1816-1824) would give the exact date. ¶6
