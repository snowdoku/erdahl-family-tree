About the family

His mother died giving birth to him; raised by his father and stepmother Brita. ¶1

Half-siblings: Lawrence M. (1880-1962), Josephine Johnson (1884-1920), Martha Gertrude Eberlein (1885-1964), Andrew C. (1887-1967), Ludwig Bertunis (1889-1969, Minnesota state representative 1945-1962). ¶5

Half-siblings in the records: the Blue Earth church book records five baptisms of children of Lasse L. Erdahl and his second wife Britha/Brithe: Laurits Martines (1880), Marthe Gjertine (1882), Josefine Berthine (1884, mother 'Brithe f. Myklebust', that is, born Myklebust) and Anders Kornelius (1887). The 1885 and 1895 censuses add Ludvick (about 1889) and Albert (about 1892). The baptism and the 1885 census age (2) put Martha Gertrude's birth in 1882, not 1885 as in the family list. Albert (born about 1892) is missing from the family list. ¶20

Life story

Baptism and parentage: the Blue Earth congregation's church book (Faribault County, transcribed on Digitalarkivet) records the baptism in 1878 of 'Bernt Ludvik', son of Lasse L. Erdahl and Martha Thomasdatter Erdahl. 'Martha Thomasdatter' is his mother Beret Martha Thomasdatter Beitland under her second given name; the same book calls her 'Berthe T. Erdahl' at the baptism of her first son Laurits in 1877. This index of a primary record is the first record, rather than a memorial, that names both his parents, and it confirms the existing links to lasse and beret. The index gives no day, godparents or scan. ¶6

1880 U.S. census (index): in June 1880 'Berent L. Erdahl', son, 1, born Minnesota, was in Rome Township, Faribault County, in the household of his father 'Lewis L. Erdahl', 30, born Norway, and 'Brette', 21, born Norway (his stepmother Brita). Sheet 48C, ED 55. ¶28

1885 MN census: on 5 June 1885 'Bentt L Erdahl', 6, born Minnesota, was living in Rome Township, Faribault County, in family 19 headed by his father 'Lewis L Erdahl', 34, born Norway, with 'Butte', 25, born Norway (his stepmother Brita), and the half-siblings 'Laurie M' 4, 'Marty G' 2 and 'Jacob B' 0. ¶7

Confirmed at Bethany Lutheran Church (Frost, MN) 1893. ¶2

1895 MN census: 'Berent Erdahl', 16, born Minnesota, is listed in Rome Township under 'Berthe L Erdahl', 36, born Norway (his stepmother), with Lauris 14, Martha 12, Josephie 10, Andrew 8, Ludvick 6 and Albert 3. His father is not on that page; the household starts at the top of the page, so the head may be on the previous page, which has not been read. ¶8

1895 MN census, head found: the last line (line 40) of the page before his stepmother's page in Rome Township, enumerated about 20 June 1895 by Leopold Oelke, is 'Lous' (probably Louis) 'Eardahl', 45, born Norway, farmer, 26 years in the state and in the district. The next page opens with 'Berthe L Erdahl' and Berent, 16. So the household was complete, headed by his father, and simply ran across the page break. ¶21

1900 U.S. census (probable): 'Bennie L Erdahl', born October 1878 in Minnesota, was a servant in another household in Rome Township, Faribault County. The birth month and year, the township and the initial L fit Berent. The head of the household he worked in is not in the index; read image 4120274_00097 to confirm. ¶9

1900 U.S. census, head identified (index): 'Bennie L Erdahl', servant, 22, born October 1878 in Minnesota, single, was in the household of Andrew Male, 46, born February 1854 in Wisconsin, widowed, farmer's household in Rome Township (ED 94, sheet 2A, line 31). Also in the household: James T Doyle 43, Peter J Erdal 26, Michael Mikkelson 20 and Allen S Miller 25. This answers the earlier note that the head was not indexed. ¶29

Birth of son Oliver (1901): the certificate for 'L. Oliver Erdahl', born 7 July 1901 in Ellsborough Township, Murray County, names the father 'B. L. Erdahl', farmer, 23, born Minnesota, and the mother 'Annie Quam', 19, born Minnesota. It was their first child. The birth was reported by W. L. Rogde of Ruthton. ¶11

Residence 1901-1910: the records place the family in Ellsborough Township in 1901-1906, in Lake Wilson village in 1908 and in Cameron Township in 1910, all in Murray County near Lake Wilson. This refines the family account that they 'lived in Lake Wilson'. The five children named on the certificates match the first five of the eleven listed under William. ¶16

Birth of daughter Minnie (1903): the certificate for Minnie Bertina Erdahl, born 14 July 1903 in Ellsborough Township, again names 'B. L. Erdahl', farmer, 25, and 'Annie Quam', 21, both born in Minnesota. She was their second child. ¶12

1905 MN census: on 1 June 1905, 'Bernt Erdall', 26, a farmer born in Minnesota, was living in Ellsborough Township, Murray County, with 'Annie', 22, born Minnesota, and the children 'Lurince O' (Oliver, 3) and 'Minnie B' (1). No son Otis is listed (see the note on Otis). ¶10

Birth of son Otis (1905 or 1906): two typed index cards, certificates 1905-16626 ('Otis Marine Erdahl', 1 Jan 1905) and 1906-16229 ('Otis M. Erdahl', 1 Jan 1906), name the same parents ('B. L.', farmer, 27, and 'Annie Quam', 23), the same township of Ellsborough and the same reporter. They are taken to be one child registered twice, because the later certificates count only one child between Minnie and Bernice (see the note on Otis). ¶13

Birth of daughter Bernice (1908): Berent himself registered this birth late, signing on 14 June 1944 from Custer, Washington, and it was filed on 24 November 1944. In this registration he gives his full name as Berent Ludwig Erdahl, farmer, age 29, born in Rome Township, Minnesota; the mother is 'Annie Quame', 25, housewife, born 'Faribault, Minn.'. Bernice Olivia was born on 26 February 1908 at 4 a.m. in the village of Lake Wilson, Murray County, and was the mother's fourth child. ¶14

Birth of son Maynard (1910): the certificate for Maynard Edwin Erdahl, born 8 September 1910 in Cameron Township, Murray County (post office Lake Wilson), names the father 'Berent Erdahl', farmer, 30, born Minnesota, and the mother 'Anna Quame', 27, a farmer's wife, born Minnesota. It was the mother's fifth child, and all five were then living. ¶15

1910 U.S. census: 'Berend Erdahl', head, born about 1879 in Minnesota, and his wife 'Anna', born about 1883 in Minnesota, were living in Cameron Township, Murray County. The children's rows were not captured from the index. ¶17

1910 U.S. census, children (index): 'Berendt Erdahl', head, 31, with Anna 27, Laurence (Luren) 9, Minnie 6, Otis 4 and Bernice 2, all born Minnesota, Cameron Township, Murray County (ED 133, sheet 9B). Maynard was born in September 1910, after census day. ¶30

Lived in Lake Wilson, MN (5 children born); moved to Froid, MT 1911; homesteaded at Bennett Lake south of Malta, MT 1913 (6 more children). Emmanuel Lutheran Brethren Church. ¶3

1920 U.S. census: his household in Phillips County, Montana has not been found in the index. Only his daughter Minnie B. was found, in School District 18, with her mother indexed as 'Oliana'. ¶24

Death certificate, read from the image: his father is written simply 'Louis Erdahl'. The form has no field for the father's birth date or birthplace. The '10/31/1878' and 'Minnesota' on the certificate are Berent's own date of birth (item 8) and birthplace (item 11), not his father's. His marital status was marked by typing over every choice except 'Widowed' (reading); his wife Annie had died in 1952. The informant was 'Bert Erdahl', probably his son Bert Rueben. He was the owner of a dairy farm, living on RFD 2, Ferndale rural. He had been in the Whatcom County Hospital for 8 days. He was buried on 15 May 1956 at Greenacres, Whatcom County, which agrees with the tree, and Monroe Mortuary of Ferndale handled the funeral. 'Ann Quann' for his mother is his wife's name given in error; see the contradiction note. ¶25

1920 U.S. census (read from the image): Berent is not listed. At School District 18 (South Malta), Phillips County, Montana (ED 160, sheet 5A, dwelling 95), his wife is entered as head, 'Oliana Erdahl', 37, married, farmer, born Minnesota, home owned with a mortgage, with Loren O. 18 (farm labourer), Minnie B. 16, Otis M. 14, Bernice O. 11, Maynard E. 9 (all born Minnesota), Inez E. 6, Elma F. 4 5/12, Frances E. 2 3/12 and Burt R. 2/12 (born Montana). He was presumably away on enumeration day (January 1920); this is inferred. The children's father is entered as born in Norway, an error, since Berent was born in Minnesota. This supersedes the note that only Minnie was found in 1920. ¶31

1930 U.S. census (index only): 'Berent Erdahl', head, born about 1879 in Minnesota, with his wife Annie (about 1883, Minnesota), was in South Malta, Phillips County, Montana. The indexed children are Bernice O. (about 1908, Minnesota), 'Magnard E.' (Maynard, about 1911, Minnesota), Elma F. (about 1915, Montana), 'Francis E.' (Frances, about 1917, Montana), Bert R. (about 1920, Montana), 'Gene W.' (Gene, about 1922, Montana) and 'William I.' (William, about 1924, Montana). The children's names and the order of their births agree with the family list. His eldest son Luren O. Erdahl headed his own household on the same South Malta page. The image has not been read. ¶22

Moved to near Custer, WA in 1939, later Ferndale; Ebenezer Lutheran Brethren Church. ¶4

1940 U.S. census (index only): Berent's own row was not found under Erdahl. The South Malta household is indexed as 'Edell': the parents 'Ben L' and Annie with their son William. 'Ben L.' fits Berent Ludvig. In the same census Annie and their son Gene ('Jean A.') are indexed at Custer, Whatcom County, Washington (see Annie). The family seems to have been split between the Malta farm and Custer in April 1940, during the 1939 move. This is inferred; the images are requested (S0113). ¶23

1950 U.S. census: on 1 April 1950 he was head of a household on LaBounty Road in rural Whatcom County, Washington (enumeration district 37-37, dwelling 25, a farm). The enumerator wrote his name as 'Erdahl, Bernt L.', age 71, married, born in Minnesota, and marked him 'U', unable to work. With him were his wife Annie, 67, and four unmarried children: Maynard E., 39, a farmer working the farm; Inez E., 37, a bookkeeper at a wholesale feed store; Elma F., 35, a nurse's helper at a private hospital; and Frances, 33, office worker at a wholesale feed store. Maynard was born in Minnesota and the three daughters in Montana. His son Gene A. Erdahl, 29, lived next door (dwelling 26) with his own family. Read from the page image. ¶26

1950 census, agreement with known dates: his age of 71 fits his birth on 31 October 1878, and Minnesota fits his birth at Frost. By 1950 the whole family was together in Whatcom County, which supports the view that the split between the Malta, Montana farm and Custer, Washington seen in the 1940 census index was a staged move that was over by then. LaBounty Road is also the address on Annie's 1952 death certificate. The dwelling number reads as 25 written over another figure (perhaps 24); the sequence 24, 26, 27 on the sheet supports 25. ¶27

WA death record: the Washington death certificate index records Berent Ludvek Erdahl, male, died 12 May 1956, aged 77, at Bellingham, Whatcom County. It names his father 'Louis Erdahl' and his mother 'Ann Quann'. The age agrees with his birth on 31 October 1878. The tree gives the death place as Ferndale, which is in the same county; the certificate image (behind a reCAPTCHA) has not been read. ¶18

Records that disagree 1

Contradiction, mother's name: the 1956 death record calls his mother 'Ann Quann', but Ann (Annie) Quame was his wife. His own mother, Beret Martha Thomasdatter, died the day he was born. The informant, probably one of his children, almost certainly gave the name of the wife instead of the mother. The 1878 baptism, made at the time, names the mother as Martha Thomasdatter Erdahl and is trusted. His mother stays as beret. The father's name 'Louis' is the usual American form of Lasse/Lewis and agrees with the tree. ¶19
