About the family

Her father is now Confirmed (Erik Amundsen) by the 1801 census, where she is 'Datter' in his household, and by the c.1842 register naming 'Gaardm. Erik Randvaldsjord og h. Olene'. Her own grade (Probable) covers her link to Siri Karine Hansdatter and is unchanged: Siri's baptism (c.1817-19) and confirmation (c.1833-35) would settle it, but Skudenes registers before 1842 burned (Skudenes confirmations survive from 1842 only), so the next sources are a probate after Hans Olsen or Ranveig in Karmsund skifteprotokoll A5/A6 (sources 23916, 1817-1825, and 23917, 1825-1847), which would name the children. ¶3

Life story

In the c.1842 Åkra register as the wife of Hans Olsen, daughter of gaardmand Erik Randvoldsjord and Oline. The 1801 census lists Randvej Karine Erichsdatter, 17, at Randvoldjord with Erich Amundsen and Olene Pedersdatter. ¶1

The 1865 census of Skudenes (Akre, household 02, person 007) lists 'Ranvæg Eriksdatter', 82, b. 1784 Skudenes, widow ('e'), føderaadskone, living with Tollef Ariansen (43) and his wife Olene Hansdatter (43, b. Skudenes) and their children Marthe Karine, Karen Marie, Hans Ole and Albert. Olene is evidently the daughter Oline (b. 1825) of the c.1842 Akre register. So Hans Olsen died between c.1842 and 1865. Candidate burial: 'Ranvig Eriksdtr', b. 1779, buried 5 Jul 1874 (Skudenes ministerialbok 1864-1881, index; not opened, age differs). Her grade is unchanged (Probable on her link to Siri Karine Hansdatter). A probate after Hans Olsen would name Siri Karine: Karmsund skifteprotokoll Skud/Torv/Tysv/Stang/Skår 1830-1854 (Digitalarkivet 23921; view n = URN sk20081106630 + n) has a back register by skipreide (views 680-682), but its Stangeland (Skudenes) section stops in 1841 at fol. 327; fol. c.330-677 (1843-1854) are unregistered and would have to be read page by page. ¶4

Death (probable identification): 'Ranvig Eriksdtr', died 1 Jul and buried 5 Jul 1874 in Skudenes (Skudenes ministerialbok 1864-1881, p. 361, no. 65; index pg00000005305772). The index gives birth year 1779 and no farm or age, against her census ages (17 in 1801, 82 in 1865 = b. about 1784), so the birth estimate is not changed. She is the only Ranveig Eriksdatter born 1774-1792 in the 1865 census of Skudenes (pf01038219001423, widow at Akre; the only other in Norway lived in Oppdal) and the only one in the national burial index born 1779-1789. ¶5

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
