About the family

Eldest daughter of Ole Gundersen Slind (b. abt 1872) and Sigrid T. 1910 federal census, Whitman County, Washington, Lacrosse precinct, ED 276, page 78A line 1: "Charoline Slind, 7, born Norway". ¶1

Her name is the American spelling of KAROLINE, and she is named for her paternal grandmother KARI OLSDATTER of Stokke - the naming pattern that identifies her father. ¶2
