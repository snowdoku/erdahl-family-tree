About the family

NEW ANCESTOR, graded on his link to his son Evind Jørgensen (see his page). The Askland probate card 79 (Skp. 8 fol. 67, Evind Jørgensøn's estate of 3 Aug 1729) carries the archivist's note 'Avd.s far: Jørgen Olsen Oland; mor: Guroe Evindsdtr. + sk. 18/3 1707'. This is a typed/handwritten archivist's annotation, not a reading of the protocol, so Probable. ¶1a

Life story

Bygdebok (Åmli ætt og heim II, Oland L.nr. 62, canvases 264-265): 'Jørgen Olson Harstveit f 1640. Skifte 1707.', husband of Guro (Eivindsdtr.) Oland; he came to the bruk after 1670. The Harstveit chapter (canvas 210) only guesses at his parents: in 1671 half a hud of Harstveit went to Nils Pederson and Jørgen [Olson], and 'Jørgen Olson [og Tallak?] Olson... kunne [vere] søner av Olav [Torbjørnson] Harstveit' (Olav Torbjørnson Harstveit is named at Jørundland in 1651-61, canvas 78). No parent added: the book's 'kunne' is a guess, and found a Jørgen Olsøn of Harstveit dead by 4 Mar 1701 (card 4249), which conflicts with 'skifte 1707' unless the book has merged two Jørgen Olsens. ¶7a

1664/1666 censuses, read on the scans: Jørgen Olsen is NOT among the users of O-enland (Oland), 8 huder, in 1666 (Oluf 39, Tolf 37, Tollak 60, Knud 47, Angrim 73; sons Tron 8, Knud 4, Svend 10, Gunner 4; husmann Orm Gunnersen 80) (DA 35510 view 32, p. 42), nor in the 1664 bailiff's list ('Ommeland 8 hud': Ole 39, Knud 40, Tollef 58, Angrim 70, Effuin 70; DA 35567 view 57, p. 78). So he came to Oland after 1666, probably by marrying Guro Evindsdatter. ¶4a

He was living on 18 Mar 1707, when the skifte after his wife names him as the children's father (same entry). ¶3

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: graded on his link to his son Evind Jørgensen. The skifte after his wife Guroe Evindsdatter, held at Oenland (Oland) in Gjøvdal on 18 Mar 1707 (Skifteprotokoll nr. 2 fol. 186b, DA 24350 view 187, read on the scan; card 39942/10017 types the same heirs), divides the estate among 'hindes efterlatte børn' Evind Jørgensøn Askeland, Salve Jørgensøn Oenland, Ole Jørgensøn, Andgrim Jørgensøn and Siri Jørgensdatter, and names 'Faderen Jørgen Olsen', who was still alive, as their father. Continuity to the Evind Jørgensen of Askland who died in 1729: in the 1723 Oland skifte after Ole Evindsøn (Skp. 6 fol. 165b; cards 10033/10035) the guardian of the children of the widow Sigri Jørgensdatter is 'Morbroren Evind Jørgensøn Askland', with Salve [Jørgensøn] Oland, which matches the 1707 sibling group (Siri, Evind, Salve). The 1729 Askland skifte (Skp. 8 fol. 67) is then after this same Evind Jørgensøn. No other Evind Jørgensen is known at Askland. So the archivist's note on card 79 is now backed by the protocol. ¶2

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Not him either: the Oland card 10053 lists 'Jørgen Olssøn +' at the plass Hunnemoen under Oenland, skifte 4 Oct 1736 (Skp. 9 fol. 131b). The protocol (DA 24357 view 134, read at zoom) shows a younger man of the same name: widow Martha Olsdatter; children Sigri Jørgensdatter (dead, leaving Ellev Olsøn 6 and Kiersti Olsdatter 11), Birgitha Jørgensdatter (m. Svend Larsøn), Ingebor Jørgensdatter 23 and Asbør Jørgensdatter 12; appraisers Tolv Ewindsøn Hommedrum and Orm Olsøn Eppeland. Our Jørgen's daughter Siri (w. of Ole Evindsøn Oenland, then of Salve Evindsen) was alive until 1744 (card 10055), and his other children were grown by 1707, so this 1736 Jørgen is a different man (perhaps a kinsman), and his estate names no parents or siblings. Our Jørgen Olsen's own death and estate are still not found. ¶6

Open questions and the next record to read 4

Next: the 18 Mar 1707 skifte after Guro Evindsdatter (Skp. 2 or 3), which should name Evind as her son. ¶1b

Lead only: the 1664-65 bailiff's list of men under 50 has, at 'Harstued' in Gjøvdal, the user Jørgen Pedersøn 34 and the sons/servants 'Jørgen Olsøn 24', Halvor Olsøn 16 and Oluf Olsøn 30 (DA 35567 view 26, p. 14). Also: in 1666 a 'Jørgen Olufsøn, 30 aar, bruger ½ hud' sits on Gjøvdal no. 3 (4 huder, read 'Haßbuen', = 'Harstued' of 1664), where an Oluf aged 74 has a son Jørgen 26 as well (DA 35510 view 32, p. 43). That Jørgen Olufsen (b. about 1636) is a possible match for our Jørgen Olsen, but there are two Jørgens there and no record joins either to Oland, so no parent is added. ¶4b

Lead eliminated: noted 'Jørgen Olsøn 24' at Harstued (Harstveit), Gjøvdal, in the 1664-65 men-under-50 list as a possible match. The Harstveit probate card 4249 (Skp. 1 fol. 229b, skifte 4 Mar 1701) records 'Jørgen Olsøn (død)' with widow Siire Halvorsdatter and son Olle; card 4251 (fol. 231, same day) is the estate of 'Olle Jørgensen Harstveit +' with widow Hild Aslacksdatter. So the Harstveit Jørgen Olsen was dead by 1701, while our Jørgen Olsen of Oland was alive at his wife's skifte on 18 Mar 1707. He is not our man. In 1666 Gjøvdal no. 3 (= Harstued) had two Jørgens, the user Jørgen Olufsøn 30 and Oluf 74's son Jørgen 26; one of them is presumably this Harstveit man, and the other remains an unproven candidate for our Jørgen. ¶5

Test record: the 18 Mar 1707 Oland skifte (Skp. 2, DA 24350) for Jørgen's own origin, and the Harstveit 1671 deed (Råbyggelaget pantebok). ¶7b
