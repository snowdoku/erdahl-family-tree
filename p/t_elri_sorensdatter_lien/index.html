About the family

Married Knud Gundersøn of Lien on 28 Oct 1751 at Grytten; the register gives her residence as Lien as well, so she was probably a servant or a daughter on the same farm. She is the probable mother of Olava Knudsdatter, who was baptized 11 Jun 1752 as the daughter of 'Knud of Lien af Wolds Sogn' (the Voll annex of Grytten parish) - the first child of that marriage, born about seven and a half months after the wedding. ¶1

Her patronymic means her father was named Søren. Her own baptism, her age and her death have not been found; the Grytten register before 1725 is lost, and the 1701 census for the district lists no family at Lien with a Søren. ¶2

Naming support, offered as an inference only: her granddaughter generation in this line kept the name Olava rather than Elri, so the link rests on the farm, the date and the absence of any competing Knud at Lien rather than on naming. ¶3

Life story

Engagement (index): the Norddal (Sunnmøre) register 1736-1760 (1524P), page 80, serial 184, has the engagement on 18 Sep 1751 of Knud Gundersen of Aarset and Eldri Sørensdtr. of 'Grøniningsætter' (Grøningsæter), with Ole of Berdalen as trulovar (betrothal witness); no marriage date is entered. This is very probably the earlier betrothal 'af Hr. Astrup paa Sundmeur' mentioned in the Grytten marriage entry of 28 Oct 1751: same names, same year, a Sunnmøre parish, and the Norddal engagement precedes the Grytten wedding by six weeks. The index entry does not name the priest, so the identification is an inference from names and dates; a reading of the Norddal scan (source 15967) would settle it. ¶5

Marriage scan read, Grytten p. 187, 28 Oct 1751: 'Elri Sørens D. ibid' (of Lien). No age or father is given. The couple had been betrothed earlier by Hr. Astrup in Sunnmøre, so Sunnmøre registers are a place to look for her origin (inference). ¶4

Origin lead (inference): if the Norddal engagement is hers, her home farm in 1751 was probably Grøningsæter in Norddal, and her father Søren should be looked for there - in Norddal baptisms about 1715-1732 (daughter Elri/Eldri, father Søren, Grøningsæter), and in Sunnmøre probate for a Søren of Grøningsæter. Not yet searched. ¶6

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
