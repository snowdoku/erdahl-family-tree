About the family

Father of Guri Olsdatter Høgset per the Gjemnes bygdebok only, graded on that link: Probable. Bind 7, Høgset chapter, canvas 576, under brukar 5 Torstein Iverson: 'Gift med Guri Olsdotter... d. 70 år gamal 1771. Foreldra hennar var Ole Halvorson og Anne Sveinsdotter'; the same page says Ole Toreson (brukar 4, b. about 1683) got inheritance in 1732 'etter mor si, Anne Sveinsdotter', so Anne was married first to a Tore and then to Ole Halvorsen, and Guri (b. about 1701) was Ole Toreson's half-sister. The exact phrase was verified with the nb.no content search, so this is not a gap guess. Where Ole Halvorsen lived and when he died is not in the rebuilt text. ¶1

Parents not proved. A probate card for Hjorten (77795, view 1725) of 8 Nov 1679 after Olle Halfuorsen, widow Dorrette Torsdaatter, children Thor, Halfuor and Jon Olsen, 'Alle myndige' (all of age; Romsdal skifteprot. 2, fol. 159b-160), shows the Hjorten family he belongs to: he is perhaps a son of that Halvor Olsen (grandson of the elder Ole), but the rebuilt bygdebok text gives no parents for him. ¶5a

Life story

Residence correction: he lived on Hjorten (Gjemnes), not Høgset; the Gjemnes bygdebok (bind 8, Hjorten, canvas 30) makes him brukar 7 there c.1697-1742, b. 1669 (date ABT, rebuilt text), m. 1 c.1698 Anne Sveinsdatter (d. 1731, probate 1732), living on Hjorten from 1705, m. 2 1735 Berit Tronsdatter Sletnes (b. about 1715, d. 1775); children of the 2nd marriage Ane 1736, Halvor 1738, Tron 1740. The 1742 probate names the widow's stepfather Siur Joensen Sletner as her guardian and her brother Anders Tronsen Sletner for the children. ¶4

1701 census checked (the Test record named in his notes). Kvernes, Hiorten: 'Oluf Halfuors.', 32 (b. 1669), opsidder, heading a household with 'Sønner' Oluf Tores. (b. 1683) and Suend Tores. (b. 1690), i.e. his stepsons, the sons of his wife Anne Sveinsdatter by her first husband Tore Olsen of Hjorten, exactly as the Gjemnes bygdebok (bind 8, canvas 29) says. No older man is in the household, so the census does not show his father. The bygdebok's candidate, Halvor Olson of Berge (Gjemnes gnr. 47), is in the same census at Berge as 'Halfuor Ols.', 62 (b. 1639), opsidder, with no sons at home (only the soldier-servant Inguold Erichs., the 'Erikson, 48 år, soldat' of the bygdebok), so the census neither supports nor rules out his being Ole's father. The Berge (Gjemnes) cards of the Nordmøre probate card index (77795, views 137-153: 1671 Knut Toresen, 1688 Marit Toresdatter, 1688 Peder Toresen, 1734 Dorte Jakobsdatter, 1737 Sidsel Torisdatter, 1741, 1742 Synnøve, 1743 x2) have no probate for Halvor Olsen or a Jonsdotter wife; Halvor left Berge about 1710 and the Nordmøre probate protocols for 1696-1714 are lost. Bind 8 canvas 271 rebuilt again: Halvor 'f. ca. 1645 var truleg frå [gap]... Gift 1667 [med...] Jonsdotter Berge' (wife a daughter of Jon, Berge brukar 4); his origin is in an unrecoverable gap (none of about 200 Nordmøre farm names matched there, and Hjorten does not occur on that page). An alternative candidate is the Halvor Olsen of Hjorten who was of age in 1679 (son of Ole Halvorsen d. 1679, card 77795 v1725), which would explain the naming Ole Halvorsen and Ole's return to Hjorten in 1697 by marrying the widow of Tore Olsen (Halvor's brother); the two Halvors may even be one man, but no record says so. Father not added. ¶6a

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: graded on his link to Guri Olsdatter Høgset. His own probate, Nordmøre skifteprotokoll 8b (Digitalarkivet 24642) fol. 543b, no. 59, held 12 Sep 1742 on the farm Hiorten in Gjemnes, names among his heirs 'datteren Guri Olsdatter', married to Torsten Iversen Høgset, begotten in his earlier marriage with Anne Svendsdatter; the other heirs are Halvor Olsen (5) and Tron Olsen (2) by the widow Berite Tronsdatter. The card index (77795, view 1727) abstracts the same entry with the relationships labelled, and the heading and heir lines were read on the scan (view 116). The record names Guri by her husband Torsten Iversen Høgset, who is Torsten (Torstein, Tosten) Iversen Høgset in the tree, so the tie to the tree person is direct, with no rival. ¶3
