About the family

Mother of Karen Poulsdatter (see her page); see the note on Paul (Povel) Pedersen for why the link is Confirmed. Karen named two daughters Giertrud (1786 and 1792). Her own birth, parents and death are unknown; her patronymic shows her father was a Niels. ¶2

Parents added (Probable): Niels Eriksen Aune and Kari O[la]fsdatter. Their probate of 26 Sep 1732 (24751 fol. 481b) names a daughter Giertrud Nielsdatter, 6, who is the 'Niels Aunes barn Nom: Giertrud' baptised on 23 Feb 1727 (16310 c107 R). The identification with this Giertrud, the wife of Paul Pedersen Garberg from about 1757, rests on there being no other Giertrud with a father Niels in the 1699-1737 baptisms and on her naming her first daughter Kari (1758) and a son Niels (1761). Different farm and a 26-year gap, so Probable, not Confirmed. Birth date is taken from the 1727 baptism on that inference. ¶6

Support for her Aune parentage, not an upgrade. At the baptism of Niels, son of 'Erich Niels: Aune og Ane Thomasd:', on Invocavit Sunday (27 Feb) 1757, the godparents were 'Ole Aune, Elle[n?] itm., Jon Samstad, Kari Brøndhaugen[?], Giertrud Garberg' (16311 c3 left page). Erik Nielsen Aune is the 'Erik Nielsen 7 aar' of the 1732 skifte after Niels Eriksen Aune (24751 fol. 481b), so he was Giertrud Nielsdatter's brother if she is the Aune Giertrud of 1727/1732; he named his son for their father, and the second godmother is probably their sister Elen Nielsdatter (9 in 1732) 'of the same place'. A Giertrud of Garberg standing godmother at this Aune baptism fits Giertrud Nielsdatter, wife of Paul Pedersen Garberg, being Erik's sister, and it shows she was already living at Garberg by February 1757, so her marriage falls in the 1747-56 register gap (the 1757 copulations in 16311 c3-4 were checked: no Paul Pedersen). Grade unchanged at Probable for her link to her parents: the entry names no relationship, and another Giertrud at Garberg in 1757 is not excluded. ¶7

Life story

Named as mother, with Paul Pedersen of Garberg, at the baptisms of Kari (12 November 1758), Niels (1761) and Jon (1764), Selbu ministerialbok 1757-1801, canvases 7, 11 and 16 (the 1758 crop). She was godmother as 'Giertrud Nielsd.' at a Garberg baptism on Judica Sunday 1759, and the 'Giertrud ibid. [Garberg]' who was godmother to Even Pedersen in September 1759 - her future son-in-law - is probably she. ¶1

Further support for her Aune parentage, not an upgrade. Her husband stood godfather at Aune: on the third day of Christmas 1765 'Døbt Jon. Kald: Erich Oune, Anne Thomasd:', godparents 'Jon Samstad, Gunner Svends: Mogaard, Paul Garberg, Helge Oune, Marit Kallar' (16311 c18 R, last entries for 1765). This is the second child of Erik Nielsen Aune whose baptism has a Garberg sponsor (after 'Giertrud Garberg' in 1757). It fits Erik being her brother and Paul Pedersen Garberg his brother-in-law. Grade unchanged (Probable for her link to her parents): no relationship is named. ¶8

1 alternative set aside · 3 open questions

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Not the same person as Giertru Nielsdatter (see her page), born about 1794 at Garberg, who married Peder Bersvendsen of Eidem in 1819: this Giertrud was already a mother in 1758, a generation and more earlier. The younger Giertru may be her granddaughter (a daughter of Niels Povelsen Garberg, married 1794, presumably her son Niels of 1761), but that is an inference not yet tested. ¶3

Open questions and the next record to read 3

Biography gaps: her birth date and place, baptism and parents are not found; 16310 c3-147 was read in full without her baptism, and the one candidate, the 23 Feb 1727 'Niels Aunes barn Giertrud' (c107 R), names only her father, so the mother and churching lines need a zoom read before the Aunet household in bygdebok vol. 7 pp. 55 and 66 (Norway-only). Her marriage to Paul Pedersen (about 1754 per the bygdebok) falls in the 1747-56 gap and is not in the register. Her death, burial and any probate are not recorded and no search is noted; Paul's probate (not located) would name her if she survived him. ¶4

Lead, not a link: the skifte after Niels Eriksen Aune, 26 Sep 1732 (24751 fol. 481b), names a daughter 'Giertrud Nielsdatter 6 aar' (born about 1726, so the 'Niels Aunes barn Giertrud' baptised 23 Feb 1727, 16310 c107 R), with the widow Kari Ch[ristoph?]ersdatter. Giertrud's first daughter with Paul Pedersen Garberg was named Kari (1758) and a son Niels (1761), which fits Niels and Kari as her parents. Not added as parents: nothing ties the Aune child of 1732 to the Garberg wife of 1758 (different farm, 26-year gap), and rival Giertrud Nielsdatters of 1720-35 have not been excluded. ¶5a

Would settle it: her burial with an age, or the skifte after Paul Pedersen Garberg or after Kari at Aune naming her. ¶5b
