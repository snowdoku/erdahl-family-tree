About the family

Father (not added): Alf Tvetene was first godfather at Osmund's baptism in 1757 and was still a godfather in 1768 and on 17 Aug 1770. By the patronymic Alfsen he is probably Torgius's father, but no record says so, so he is not entered as a person. ¶6

Children Alf and Sigri (supersedes the 'Probable earlier children' note): the 1769 burial names Sigri, aged about 10, as Torgius Alfsen Tvetene's daughter, so the Sigri baptised on 26 May 1760 as the daughter of 'Torjus Tvetene' is his, and with her the son Alf of the same 'Torjus Tvetene' baptised in spring 1759. Inference: same farm, same forename, ages fit. The burial age of 10 years 8 weeks fits a birth in about May 1759 better than the May 1760 baptism; the age is probably loose. Alf's later life has not been traced. ¶12

Children, summary (supersedes the child lists in earlier notes where they differ): Osmund, bapt. 16 Jan 1757, bur. 14 Jul 1769 aged 12; Alf, bapt. spring 1759; Sigri, bapt. 26 May 1760, bur. 14 Jul 1769; Marte, bapt. 26 Feb 1764 (Probable = Marte Torjusdatter of Tvetene Nordre, 1801); Kari, bapt. 1 Feb 1767, bur. 24 Jun 1768 aged 1; Kari, bapt. about 28 Apr 1769, not buried in the parish to 15 Nov 1770, possibly Kari Torjusdatter of Søftestad (Speculative) or Kari Torjusdatter, 32, of Grimstvedt in 1801 (unresolved). The mother of none of them is named in the entries read. ¶15

Daughter Kari of 1769, probable later identity (unproved): his Kari, baptised about 28 Apr 1769, is probably the 'Kari Torjusd: Tvetene', 16, confirmed in Nissedal church on 5 Jun 1785 (the age fits to the month), and probably the widow 'Enken Kari Torjusd: Viig' betrothed on Easter Monday (25 Apr 1791) in Nissedal to the bachelor Christoffer Joensen Grimstved, with cautioners Niels[?] Hansen[?] Viig and Ole Torjusen[?] Tvetene (Kviteseid ministerialbok, source 7983, p. 188, canvas 194 right). She is the Kari Torjusdatter, 32, at Grimstvedt in 1801, in her second marriage, with a son Tallak Biørnsen, 13. For: her age, and a Torjus-son of Tvetene as her cautioner in 1791. Against or missing: no record names her father; the 1791 entry calls her 'of Viig'; the cautioner's patronymic is read Torjusen or Torgersen. ¶19a

Life story

Marriage: betrothed 8 May 1756 at Nissedal church as 'ungkarl Torgius Alfsen Tvettene' (bachelor) to 'Piigen Aslet Aasmundsdaatter Fjone' (the maiden Asløg Asmundsdatter of Fjone; the forename is read as Aslet/Aslev, taken to be Asløg), with cautioners (sureties) Jacob Nordbøe and Tor Tvettene; married in Nissedal church on 11 Jul 1756 (4th Sunday after Trinity). Kviteseid ministerialbok 1754-1773 (source 7981), p.9. Their first known child, Osmund (Jan 1757), carries her father's name Asmund (Osmund), by naming custom. No other marriage of a Torgius Alfsen was found from Oct 1754 to Sep 1756. ¶9

Baptism of son Osmund: 16 Jan 1757 (2nd Sunday after Epiphany), Nissedal church, 'Torgius Alfsen Tvetenes Søn, kaldet Osmund' (Torgius Alfsen Tvetene's son, named Osmund). Godparents Alf Tvetene, Tor Tvetene, Tallev Danielsen, Susana Nordbøe and Ingebor Nordbøe; the mother is not named (Kviteseid ministerialbok 1754-1773, source 7981, p.25). ¶2

Baptism of daughter Marte: on 26 Feb 1764 at Nissedal church 'Torgius Tvetens daatter Marte', who had been home-baptised (hjemmedøbt, an emergency baptism at home) by Alf Tveten, was baptised; godparents Jøren[?] Fjone, Kittil [illegible], Susanna Nordbøe, Ragnild Grovum, Anne Nordbøe (Kviteseid ministerialbok 1754-1773, source 7981, p.87, canvas 36). Susanna Nordbøe was also a godmother of his son Osmund in 1757. ¶13

Daughter Marte (Probable, supersedes the 'Possible daughter' note): she is taken to be Marte Torjusdatter, b. about 1765, wife of Alv Toresen at Tvetene Nordre in the 1801 census, the only Torjusdatter on Tvetene in 1801; her son is named Torjus Alvsen. For: the 1764 baptism gives Torgius Tvetene a daughter Marte, the 1801 age is one year off, and the farm and her son's name agree. ¶14a

Father (not added;, supersedes the earlier 'Father (not added)' note): Alf Tvetene (Tveten) is probably Torgius's father. He home-baptised Torgius's daughter Marte in 1764, was the first godfather of his son Osmund in 1757, and was still a godfather in 1768 and on 17 Aug 1770. The inference rests on the patronymic Alfsen and on these family roles; no record states the relationship. He is not entered as a person, so as not to add a further generation on an inference. ¶16

Baptisms of daughters named Kari: 1 Feb 1767 (4th Sunday after Epiphany), Nissedal church, 'Torgius Alvsen Tvetenes Datter Kari', godparents Ambjøn Grimstvedt, Targe Tvetene, Aslach Tvetene, Sigri Fjone, Sigri Tvetene (p.106); and on about 28 Apr 1769 (day digit uncertain), Nissedal church, 'Torgius Alfsen Tvetenes Daatter Kari', godparents Joen Fjone, Ole Tvetene, Gunvor Frontvedt, Anne Tvetene, Signe Fjone (p.118). Both. The repeat of the name suggests the 1767 Kari died young (inference; her burial has not been read). ¶3

Burials of children: on 24 Jun 1768 (St John's Day) at Nissedal church 'Torgius Tvete[ne]s Datter Kari, gl. 1 aar' (Torgius Tvetene's daughter Kari, aged 1) was buried. This is the Kari baptised 1 Feb 1767 (inference from age and farm; the farm word is cramped and read as probable). On 14 Jul 1769 at Nissedal church 'Torgius Alfsen Tvetenes Søn Osmund, gl. 12 aar' and 'Torgius Alfsen Tvetenes daatter Sigri, gl. 10 aar 8 uger' (son Osmund aged 12, daughter Sigri aged 10 years 8 weeks) were buried the same day. Kviteseid ministerialbok 1754-1773 (source 7981), burials, canvases 173 and 174. This supersedes the 'Baptisms of daughters named Kari' note's statement that the 1767 Kari's burial has not been read. ¶11

Probate: Vest-Telemark skifteprotokoll 1784-90 (source 25678), skifte No 591, view 196. On 16 Oct 1786 the estate of 'afgangne Mand Torjus Alfsen' was registered at 'Gaarden Tvedtene i Nordbygden udi Nissedahls Annex', to be divided between his widow, read 'Aslet Osmundsdatter' (Asløg Asmundsdatter), and 'deres i Egteskab sammen avlede Børn, som ere 2de Døttre, nemlig: Ældste Datter Margith Torjusdatter gift med Alf Thoresen' and 'Yngste Datter Kari Torjusdatter 19 Aar gammel', for whom Kittil Tjøstolfsen [Tvene?] was appointed guardian; the case was closed at Sundbø on 24 Oct 1786. So Torjus Alfsen of Tvetene died in 1786 leaving only two daughters, and his wife was Asløg Asmundsdatter (married 1756). His Kari, 19 in 1786 (born about 1767-69), is the Kari baptised about 28 Apr 1769 and very probably the 'Kari Torjusd: Tvetene', 16, confirmed in 1785. This also names his wife: the widow Asløg (Aslet) Asmundsdatter (see her page). ¶22

1801 census: no Torjus Alfsen (any spelling searched: Torjus, Alfsen, Alvsen) is indexed on Tvetene or Tvedt in 1801, and no widow there has children with the patronymic Torjussen or Torjusdatter. He may have died before 1801; not checked under the spellings Torgius, Torjer or Tor. ¶7

1801 census (adds to the '1801 census' note): the Tvedtene list (the 'Tvedtene' spelling, missed by a 'Tvet*' search) was read in full, 44 residents: no Torjus, Torgius, Torjussen or Torjusdatter. With (Tvetene and Tvedt), he does not appear on either farm in 1801 and was probably dead by then (inference). His death, burial and probate have not been found. ¶17

How sure are we?

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 3

Probable earlier children: 'Torjus Tvetene' had a son Alf baptised in spring 1759 and a daughter Sigri on 26 May 1760 in Nissedal church. The farm and the son's name Alf (after Torgius's father, by naming custom) make it probable, not proved, that this is the same man. ¶4

Confidence: Speculative, as the father of Kari Torjusdatter of Søftestad (see her page). See her 'Father ' note for the reasoning. Not to be confused with Torjus Muule (see his page) or Torjus Kittilsen of Tvedt (daughter Turi, Jul 1769; probably also a Turi in Dec 1767, father's forename uncertain) or the Torjus family of Margit and Tone Torjusdatter at Tvedt in 1801. ¶8a

Confidence: stays Speculative as the father of Kari Torjusdatter of Søftestad (see her page). The test set in patch 016 is now met: the 1763-1767 baptisms of all churches hold no rival Kari, and his 1767 Kari died in 1768. However, his surviving Kari (bapt. 28 Apr 1769) has a closer-fitting adult match: Kari Torjusdatter, 32 in 1801, wife of Christopher Joensen at Grimstvedt. The full reasoning is in Kari's 'Father ' notes. This note supersedes the 'Confidence: Speculative' note's list of what would raise the grade. ¶10

The case for this identification 1

Identity: Torgius (Torjus) Alfsen of Tvetene, Nissedal (then Kviteseid prestegjeld), Telemark. Written 'Torgius Alfsen Tvetene' or 'Torgius Alvsen Tvetene' in the Kviteseid ministerialbok 1754-1773. Born before about 1737: inferred only from his fathering a son baptised in Jan 1757; no baptism, age or death of his has been read. ¶1

Records that disagree 1

Against: no record links the two directly. Would confirm it: her confirmation or her marriage to Alv Toresen, or a probate after Torgius naming her. ¶14b

Alternatives considered and set aside 4

Possible daughter: Marte Torjusdatter, b. about 1765, wife of Alv Toresen at Tvetene Nordre in the 1801 census, is the only Torjusdatter on Tvetene in 1801; her son is named Torjus Alvsen. Inferred from patronymic and farm only; her baptism (Jun 1763 - Jan 1767) has not been read. ¶5

Not an ancestor: he is no longer linked as the father of Kari Torjusdatter of Søftestad (see her page). She was confirmed in 1778 as 'Kari Torjusd. Bakken', 17 (born about 1761), and her brother Gunder Torjussen was born about 1751, before this man married as a bachelor in 1756. His record is kept as a collateral record of the Tvetene family. His confidence grade of Speculative referred to that link; as a person he is well attested in the baptisms, marriage and burials read. This note supersedes the 'Confidence: Speculative' and 'Confidence ' notes and the 'possibly Kari Torjusdatter of Søftestad' clause of the 'Children, summary' note. ¶18

Possible daughter (unproved): 'Aszier (Aster?) Targiesd. Tvetene', 17, was confirmed on the 3rd Sunday in Advent 1778 in the same service as Kari Torjusd. Bakken (born about 1761), and a maiden 'Askier Targiesd. Tvetene' married bachelor Ole Eriksen Fione in Nissedal on 19 Aug 1786. She is not among his children found in the baptisms read; her baptism (about 1761) has not been looked for under this name. ¶20

Possible son (unproved): 'Egild Torjusen Tvetene', 19 (age read at overview scale), confirmed on the Sunday after New Year 1778 in Nissedal church, so born about 1759. Whether he is the Egild Torjusen, 45, at Grimstvedt Øvre in 1801 is not settled. ¶21

Open questions and the next record to read 2

Would settle it: her first marriage (to a Biørn, about 1786-88) or Tallak's baptism, or a probate after her first husband naming her kin. ¶19b
