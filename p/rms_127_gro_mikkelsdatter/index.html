About the family

NEW ANCESTOR, graded on her link to her son Orm Tallaksen (see his page). Åmli ætt og heim I (Tovdal), Øvre Skjeggedal (canvas 92): 'Tallak Ormson f 1665 d 1719, g m Gro Mikkelsdtr. [?] f 1671 d 1717', with Orm b. 1693 first among their children. (Her farm of origin is a gap in the rebuilt text.) Bygdebok only (compiled secondary source, text rebuilt from word boxes, dates ABT), so Probable at most. ¶1a

Her brothers are named. In her husband's probate (Thallak Ormsøn, Øvre Skjeggedal, 7 Jun 1719, Skp. 5 fol. 273b-274a, DA 24353 view 281, read on the scan; card 39942/12331-12333), the guardian of her son Gundsten is 'Morbroderen Paal Michelsøn Haartved i Nisdal' and another 'Morbroder', Aane Michelsøn, becomes guardian for Knud the younger. So Poul (Paal) Mikkelsen of Haatveit in Nissedal and Aane Mikkelsen were her brothers, and the three share the patronymic Mikkelsen/Mikkelsdatter. This gives her father as a Mikkel (see his page, Probable), and suggests that she came from Haatveit (Hårtveit) in Nissedal. Her own grade (Confirmed, on her link to Orm Tallaksen) is unchanged. ¶3

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: graded on her link to her son Orm Tallaksen (see his page). Østre Råbyggelag Skifteprotokoll nr. 5 (DA 24353, view 123), fol. 118a: 'Anno 1717 dend 25 Septemb.' at Øfre Skieggedal, probate after 'Guroe Michelsdaatter', divided between her surviving husband 'Thallach Ormsøn' and 'deris sammen avlede Børn': Orm Thallachsøn 24 aar; Jacob Thallachsøn 21 aar (a soldier in Capitain Sommerschiel's company); Knud Thallachsøn 17; Gundsten Thallachsøn 11; Knud the younger, 6; Errich ½; and Ingeborrig Thallachsdaatter, unmarried. The father is guardian. Card 39942/12319 indexes it the same way and adds '(+ sk. 1719)' for Tallak. Continuity to Orm Tallaksen in the tree (see his page, b. about 1693): Orm Thallachsøn, aged 24 in 1717 (so b. about 1693), at Øvre Skjeggedal; then 'Orm Tallachsøn Scheggedal', betrothed 15 Feb 1726 and married 21 Jun 1726 (DA 9920 canvas 42); then 'Orm Tallachsøn øfre Skiegedals barn Tallach', 1729 (canvas 50). That is 9-12 years on the same farm, and no other Orm Tallaksen is known in Tovdal. Confirmed. She died before 25 Sep 1717 (the bygdebok's 'd 1717' agrees). Her parents (a Mikkel) are not named in the probate. ¶2
