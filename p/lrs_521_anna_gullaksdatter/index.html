About the family

Parents: father Gullak Nielsen of Yttre Oppedal (see his page), Confirmed by the 1766 probate, which names her as his eldest daughter. Mother: probably his widow Britte Iversdatter (see her page); this link is Probable only, because the heading's 'deres udi[?]... [Æg]teskab avlede' (their... begotten in marriage) is not fully read and a first marriage of Gullak is not excluded (see Britte's note). Her siblings, as read in the 1766 probate, are listed on her father's record. ¶8

Mother confirmed: her mother Britte Iversdatter's own probate, 16 Sep 1769 (Ytre Sogn 8b No. 55, fol. 563b), names 'Datteren Anna Gullachsdatter gift med Lasse Monsen Indre Oppedale' as heir to a half share of Moderarv (mother's inheritance), 11 Rd 10 2/3 s. ¶10

Twins of 1718: the 6 May 1718 twins Anna and Kari of 'Gullig Opdal' with mother Marthe are not this couple's children: in 1719 and 1720 Gullig of Ytre Oppedal's wife is Birthe, and a 'Marthe ytter opdal' stood godmother beside her in 1720. The 1718 entry belongs to another Gullig of Oppedal, or the mother's name is a slip; either way it does not affect Anna's parentage. ¶15

Life story

Elder sister of the same name died (burial check): an earlier Anna of the couple was baptised about the end of June 1719 ('Christned Gulig ytter Opdals barn N: Anna, Mat: Birthe, Test: Colben Asseim, Harald ytter Opdal, Rand[?] Hans: ibid, Anna Vatne, Birthe Lassesdr ytter Opdall', 11438 c37 right page, after an entry dated 3 p. Trin. = 25 Jun 1719), the mother was churched on 12 Aug 1719 ('Introduceret Gulig ytter Opdals qvinde Birthe efter et Pigebarn N: Anna'), and on the same day was buried 'Anna Gulicksdr ytter Opdal ætat 7 [uger? or dager?]' (11438 c39 left page; crops, c39-grav.jpg). The 1739 bride is therefore the second Anna, baptised 16 Jun 1720. ¶14

Baptism: at Brekke church on Dom. 3 post Trinitatis 1720 (16 Jun 1720): 'Christned Gulich ytter opdals barn N: Anna, Mat: Birthe, Test: Johannes Vatne, Harald ytter opdal, Marthe ytter opdal, Anna N[iels?]dr ibid, Birthe Lassesdr ibid' (Gulen ministerialbok 11438, canvas 48, right page, foot). Father Gullak of Ytre Oppedal and mother Birthe match Gullak Nielsen and Britte Iversdatter, and she is the only Anna of this couple who survived infancy (see the next note), so this is the eldest daughter Anna who married Lasse Monsen in 1739, aged 19. Birth set to BEF 16 JUN 1720 from this baptism (previously BET 1716 AND 1721). The mother's churching, at Brekke on 3 Aug 1720, names the father with his patronymic: 'Introduc: Gulig Niels[en] ytre opdals q[vind]e Birthe efter et pigebarn N: Anna' (11438 c50 left page, lower part). So the 1720 Anna is the daughter of Gullak Nielsen of Ytre Oppedal and his wife Birthe, which a baptism record now ties directly to both parents. ¶13

Marriage: betrothed about 4 Oct 1739 and married 1 Nov 1739 at Brekke to Lasse Monsen of Indre Oppedal (see his page); she was then of Indre Oppedal ('ij Opdal'). Mother ('Mat: Anna') at the baptism of Rand, 16 Feb 1741. ¶2

Birth bound (inference): she married on 1 Nov 1739, so she was born before about 1722 (a bride then was rarely under 17 or 18). As eldest daughter, with a brother Iver named first among the heirs, she may have been born well before that. Her father was alive until 1766, so she was not a posthumous child. The existing bound BEF 1722 is kept. No baptism has been found; the Gulen church book used so far (11441) begins in 1736, after her birth, and whether an earlier Gulen register survives has not been checked. ¶9

Birth window (inference): her parents Gullach Nielsen of Ytre Oppedal and Birthe Iversdatter were betrothed at Brekke on 1 Jan 1716 (Gulen 11438 c1 left), and she married in Nov 1739, so she was born between about 1716 and 1721. Her baptism was not found in the canvases. ¶11

Name, 1742: at the baptism of her son Mons on 25 Feb 1742 she is named only 'anne' (Mat: anne), with no patronymic, as she was 'Anna' with none at Rand's baptism in 1741. Her patronymic remains unsettled between Gullaksdatter (1783 division, 'Anna Gullachs-', clear) and the uncertain 1739 betrothal reading ('Guttormsd.'; 'Gul[l]hagsd.' / 'Gut[?]h[?]gsd.'; 'gul[?]hagsd', which favours Gullaksdatter). She was churched on Quasimodogeniti, 1 Apr 1742. ¶5a

Name: the 1766 probate after Gullak Nielsen of Yttre Oppedal settles her patronymic as Gullaksdatter. It names 'ældste Datter Anna Gullachsdatter gift med Lasse Monsen Jndre Oppedall, begge tilstede'. This agrees with 'Anna Gullachs-' in the 1783 division and with the 1739 betrothal reading 'gul[?]hagsd', a form of Gullaksdatter. The earlier reading 'Guttormsd.' is set aside. ¶6

Name: the 1783 division calls her 'Anna Gullachs-' (Gullaksdatter); re-read the heading crop and the letters 'Gullachs' are clear. The 1739 betrothal entry was as 'Anne Guttormsd. i[ndre] Opdal'; on re-reading the crop sees 'anne Gul[l]hagsd.' (audit also sees no 'orm'), which does not clearly contain 'orm' and could be a form of Gullagsd./Gullachsd. She is entered as Gullaksdatter from the clearer 1783 record; Guttormsdatter is a possible variant reading. She is not among the heirs of Gullov Johansen of Asheim in 1740 (patch 024), so she is not known to be a sister of Torkild Monsen's first wife Ingeborg Gullaksdatter. Her own parents are not identified. ¶1

Living in 1783: on 3 Jun 1783 she and her husband, both living and old, asked the court to divide their estate among their children (reading; see the note on Lasse Monsen). Her death and burial are not found; she died after 3 Jun 1783. ¶3a

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Confidence Probable: her identity as the Anne of 1739, the Anna of 1741 and the Anna Gullachs[datter] of 1783 rests on the same husband and farm; the patronymic differs between the readings. ¶4

Confidence: raised from Probable to Confirmed. Her Probable grade concerned only her patronymic and the identity of the 1739 Anne (see the 1742 name note). The 1766 probate (Ytre Sogn 8b Nº 68) now names her in full, Anna Gullachsdatter, as wife of Lasse Monsen of Indre Oppedal. The same couple appear as Lasse Monsen and Anna Gullachs[datter] of Indre Oppedal in 1783, and Lasse Monsen of Indre Oppedal married Anne in 1739. No other Lasse Monsen is known at Indre Oppedal in this period. ¶7

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Caution: a baptism that looks like hers is probably not hers. On 6 May 1718 (11438 c24, right page) 'Gullig Opdalens börn, tvillinger, N: Anna, N: Kari' were baptised, 'Mat: Marthe', with godparents Niels Kirkebiid[?], Jonn Klout[?]niten, Jon Opdall, Lars ibid, [?] Kirkebiid, Kari ibid, Kari Opdall, [?] Larsdr and Magneli Jonsdr. The mother was churched on Dom. 4 p. Trin. 1718: 'Gulig [Yttre?] Opdals q[vinde] Mar[the] efter at Pigebørn tvillinger N: Anna, N: Kari' (11438 c27, right page). The mother is Marthe, not Britte Iversdatter, so this Anna is not taken as the eldest daughter of Gullak Nielsen and Britte unless a priest's error is shown. Either a second Gullak lived on Oppedal in 1718, or the name is a slip. No baptism of a child of Gullak and Britte was found from Apr 1718 to Jun 1719 (11438 c24-c36, read in strips). ¶12

Open questions and the next record to read 2

Next record: Gulen burials 1783-1800 for an Anna (Gullaksdatter/Guttormsdatter) of Indre Oppedal, and any probate after her. ¶3b

Would settle it: a further child's baptism or a sponsor entry giving her patronymic, her burial after 1783, or a probate after her or Lasse Monsen. Her link as mother of Mons Lassesen (see his page) is now Confirmed by this baptism together with the 1783 division; her own Probable grade concerns only her patronymic and the identity of the 1739 Anne, and is not changed. ¶5b
