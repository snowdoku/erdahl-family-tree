Life story

Fifth child of Thomas Olsen and Eli Larsdatter. Born 5 June 1858 and baptized 4 July 1858 in the Hegra annex, entry no. 61 on page 50; his father is entered as a husmann at Beitlandsbjørgen. ¶1
