About the family

Father of Lars Gregoriussen (see his page), Probable; graded on his link to Lars. The 1701 census names Lauris as Gregorius's son on Store Ringdal, and Lars Gregoriussen died on Store Ringdal by 26 Sep 1727 (his probate); the patronymic, farm and age (Lars would be about 34, with young children in 1727) all fit, and there is no rival Lars on the farm. Not Confirmed because the two records are 26 years apart and no record names Lars Gregoriussen of 1727 as this Gregorius's son. Lars's son Gregorius Larsen (1727 probate) is named for him. No probate for Gregorius is indexed at Ringdal (the Sunnmøre index has only Håkon Abrahamsen, Lille Ringdal, 1708, before 1727). ¶2

Life story

1666 vicar's census (index): no Gregorius was on Ringdal in 1666. Store and Lille Ringdal then had Niels Samundsen (sons Samund, Knud), the husmann Endre Olsen, Lauridtz Pedersen, Rasmus Knudsen (sons Knud, Endre), Siver Ingebrichtsen (son Peder), Anders Pedersen (sons Peder 16 and Halkiel 14) and the husmann Arne Østensen (pc00000001602810-836). Peder Andersen (b. about 1650) is the Peder with sons Joen, Rasmus, Halchild and Anders on Store Ringdal in 1701. Of the Gregoriuses in the 1666 Sunnmøre census aged about 14-22, the nearest by place is Gregorius Haldkieldsen, 22 (b. about 1644), a farmer (bonde) on Hole in Sunnylven (pc00000001602919); the name Halkjeld recurs at Ringdal. The ages differ by four years and nothing links him to Ringdal, so he is only a lead, not added. Others: Gregorius Gregorisøn 16, Nordall; Gregorius Pouelsen 20, servant at Eidem. ¶3

1701 manntall (scan read, p. 124; index pc00000001091847): on 'Stor Ringdall', Sunnylven, the leilending (tenant) 'Gregorius', aged 53 (b. about 1648), with one son listed under 'Søner': 'Lauris', aged 8 (b. about 1693). The other Store Ringdal households in 1701 are Rasmus (sons Halchield, Ingebrit, Siver), Oelle (stepson Tore Ingebritsen) and Peder (sons Joen, Rasmus, Halchild, Anders); no other Lars and no other Gregorius on the farm. ¶1

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
