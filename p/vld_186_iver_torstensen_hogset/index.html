About the family

The full 1701 household at Høgset (Kvernes) is 'Ifuer Taarstens.' 40 (b. 1661) with 'Sønner' Knudt Tores. (b. 1685), Laurids Tores. (b. 1691) and Tøris Ifuers. (b. 1700). Knut and Lars Toresen are the bygdebok's Knut (b. 1684) and Lars (b. 1691) Tørrisson, children of the previous brukar Tørris Tørrisson and his wife, a Pedersdotter Høgset; so Iver had married Tørris's widow, the 'Pedersdotter' of the bygdebok. That puts Iver's marriage about 1692-93 and fits a son Torsten born in the 1690s, but no Torsten is in the 1701 household, though the census lists boys down to age 1. The only Torsten Iversen of the right age in the Nordmøre 1701 census, 'Taarsten Ifuers.' 12 (b. 1689) at Grip fishing station (pc00000005076979), is the son of the cottar 'Ifuer Ifuers.' 50 there (pc00000005076978), with a brother Ifuer Ifuers. 6; so he is not Iver Torstensen's son, and the bygdebok's 'kanskje' boy of 11 in 1701 is not found. Torsten (b. ABT 1693 per the bygdebok) was therefore living away from home in 1701 or was born after the census. This is a gap, not a contradiction, but it means the census gives no support to the link: grade stays Probable. Test record unchanged (a probate after Iver Torstensen or his wife, 1714-1730s, 24639/24640, or the 1771 probate after Torsten's sister). ¶8

Life story

Primary records seen: the 1701 census has 'Ifuer Taarstens.' b. 1661 at Høgset with son 'Tøris Ifuers.' b. 1700 (Digitalarkivet 206, pc00000005073094, pc00000005073097), which fits the bygdebok's Iver and his son Tørris, but no Torsten in the household. The 1745 probate after Synnøve Iversdatter of Berge, wife of Ole Ingebrigtsen (24642 fol. 542b, no. 58) names only her children Jacob and Marit, matching the bygdebok's no. 4, but does not name her father or siblings. So no record yet names Iver as Torsten's father: Probable on the bygdebok statement. ¶2

The first-name register of Kristiansund byfogd skifteprotokoll nr. 1, 1742-1785 (24671, views 563-573, A-W) was read in full. It has no entry for a wife of Jens [of] Flatset and no Flatset entry. Its two Iversdatter entries were read and neither is Iver Torstensen's daughter: (1) 'Margaretha Iversdatter' fol. 366 (view 369, right page): probate 18 Jun 1771 in Kristiansund after Margaretha Iversdatter, whose heirs are her children Iver Pedersen (17) and Bertie Maria Pedersdatter, so she was the wife of a Peder (probably the 'Peder Halvorsen' indexed under P) and was born about 1720-30, too young to be a daughter of Iver born about 1661; (2) 'Christina Iversdatter' fol. 384 falls inside the 1771 probate after Niels [..]sen Møklebostad of Kristiansund (fol. 383-385, views 386-388), with town-born children Jacob Christian, Andreas, Karen Magdalena, Anna Fridrica and Inger Catharina, so again not ours. The sister who married Jens of Flatset therefore has no probate in the Kristiansund byfogd volume under her own name; she may be indexed under her husband in the Nordmøre sorenskriveri volumes, or she died outside town. Test record unchanged; grade unchanged (Probable). ¶6

Negative checks for the probate of his daughter (wife of Jens of Flatset, Frei). The typed register of skifteprotokoll 13 (24648, views 8-9, I/J-L) lists the deceased by first name with tinglag (Halse, Surendalen, Wig, Meke, Gjemnes, Gangnat, Brøske, Smølen, Sundalen): no Jens or Flatset entry and no Frei tinglag at all, so Frei probates of 1766-72 are probably not in this series; the registers of 24647 (view 3, A) and 24650 (view 3, A-B) show no Frei entries either. Also read: 24648 fol. 154a (view 168), 8 May 1767, Høgset, after Elling Bottolvsen (d. 14 Aug [1766]), widow Anne Olsdatter, children Marithe Ellingsdatter (m. Frederich Olsen), Bottolf Ellingsen 19, Ole Ellingsen 17, Anne Ellingsdatter 13, with Ouden Oudensen Grønset among the guardians: no Iversen or Torstensen kin named, so a different family. 24649 fol. 343a (view 361): 18 Apr 1774, Stokke, after Ingebor Knudsdatter, heirs her children Knud and Halvor Halvorsen, Johanna, Anne and Marithe Halvorsdatter: unrelated. Nordmøre guardianship protocols (overformynderi) are not transcribed or searchable on Digitalarkivet: there are no 'Public guardian register' sources for Gjemnes, Kvernes, Frei, Tingvoll or the county, and person searches by farm return only church books, censuses and probates. ¶7

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
