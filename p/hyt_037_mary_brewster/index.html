Life story

Birth and parents: 'Mary Brewster the daughter of Johnnathan borne at Plymouth the 16th of Aprill 1627' (MD 1:7), and 'Johnnathan Brewster marryed Lucretia Oldam. of Darby. the 10th Aprill. 1624. who had children: William Mary...' (MD 1:8). Both entries were read on the page images (the p. 7 birth had previously been OCR only). Her parents Jonathan Brewster and Lucretia Oldham are entered from her father's own record; her own confidence stays Speculative because it measures whether she was Ruth Turner's mother, which no record states. This supersedes the earlier 'Lead only, not added' note: Jonathan Brewster and Lucretia Oldham are now entered (audited); Elder William Brewster is still deliberately not entered. ¶5

Marriage: 'Mary Brewster marryed John Turner of Situate the Elder the 10th November. 1645' (The Mayflower Descendant vol. 1, p. 8). The entry is in the family record of Jonathan Brewster (the 'Brewster Book'), transcribed in print; the page image was. The same record gives 'Mary Brewster the daughter of Johnnathan borne at Plymouth the 16th of Aprill 1627' (p. 7, OCR). Bowman's introduction to Turner's will repeats the marriage and cites this page (MD 5:41). ¶1

No grade change: the colony's own marriage register also has the marriage: 'John Turner and Mary Brewster marryed the xij Nouemb, 1645' (Records of the Colony of New Plymouth 2:94; the Brewster Book gives the 10th and adds 'of Situate the Elder'). The colony's Scituate register (PCR 8) lists the children of 'John Turner, Seni[o]r' from Jonathan (20 Sep 1646) to Elisha (8 Mar 1656/7) without naming their mother, and no entry in PCR 1-8 (OCR searched for Mary Brewster, Mary Turner and John Turner Senior) records her death or a second wife. Her rating as Ruth's mother stays Probable: no record read names Ruth's mother. ¶9a

Widowhood: John Turner Senior's will of 4 March 1695[/6] provides for 'my dearly beloved wife Mary' during her widowhood, so his wife Mary was living in 1696 (The Mayflower Descendant, vol. 5, pp. 41-45). ¶2

(no grade change; graded on her link to Ruth Turner). Ruth Sylvester's Plymouth probate (case 20019, 1729: administration and inventory only) and the 1729 court suit over her estate (Partridge v. Prince, a wage claim) do not name Ruth's mother. No record found here names Mary (Brewster) Turner as Ruth's mother. ¶10

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the grade · 1 on the identification · 1 correction · 1 search with no result · 3 open questions

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Confidence raised from Speculative to Probable (orchestrator, 13 Oct 2026): John Turner the Elder married Mary Brewster on 10 November 1645 (Brewster Book, MD 1:8); the Scituate births list his children by 'John Sr.' in an unbroken run from Jonathan (20 September 1646) to Mary (10 December 1658) and Ruth (baptised 17 May 1662), when Mary Brewster was 35; his will of 4 March 1695/6 still provides for 'my dearly beloved wife Mary'; and no record of a death of the 1645 wife or of a second marriage has been found. Caulkins's History of New London (1852) also calls his son Ezekiel Turner 'grandson of Mr. Brewster' (a compiled statement, used here as support only). With a wife named Mary at both ends of the marriage and no intervening wife known, Mary Brewster is very probably Ruth's mother. ¶7a

The case for this identification 1

Identity (Speculative): proposed as the mother of Ruth Turner, baptised at Scituate on 17 May 1662 as daughter of John Sr. The reasons: Ruth was born 17 years into John Turner the Elder's 1645 marriage to Mary Brewster; his wife in 1696 was still a Mary; and the will's daughters Mary, Ruth and Grace repeat the names of Mary Brewster's sisters Ruth and Grace (an inference from naming only). What is missing: no record read names Ruth's mother, and none rules out a second wife also named Mary. The baptism names only the father. A Scituate or Plymouth Colony death record for Mary (Brewster) Turner, or a record of the widow Mary Turner after 1697 giving her age (she would be about 70), would settle it. ¶3

Corrections 1

Correction: the 'Identity (Speculative)' note above is superseded; her rating as Ruth Turner's mother is Probable. It rests on three records: her marriage to John Turner the Elder on 10 November 1645 (Brewster Book, MD 1:8); his children entered as 'John Sr.' in an unbroken run at Scituate from 1646 to 1662, Ruth being baptised on 17 May 1662; and his will of 1695/6, which provides for 'my dearly beloved wife Mary' and names a daughter Ruth. No record names Ruth's mother, and no second wife of John Turner is known. That gap is why the link is Probable rather than Confirmed. ¶8

Searches that found nothing 1

Death search (negative): no death of Mary Turner that fits Mary (Brewster) Turner after 1697 was found in the printed Vital Records of Scituate, vol. 2 (marriages and deaths), nor in the printed Duxbury or Pembroke vital records; the Mary Turners found there are other women (search of the OCR text; not repeated on images). ¶6

Open questions and the next record to read 3

Lead only, not added: the Brewster Book makes her a daughter of Jonathan Brewster and Lucretia Oldham, and Jonathan was a son of Elder William Brewster of the Mayflower. Those generations are deliberately not entered from this pass. A famous descent needs its own audited pass (Mayflower Society 'Five Generations' Brewster volume, Plymouth Colony records). ¶4

What would confirm it: a record naming Ruth's mother (for example a Plymouth County probate settlement of John Turner's estate naming his widow Mary, formerly Brewster, or Mary Turner's own will naming her daughter Ruth Sylvester/Prince). ¶7b

Test record: (1) Bowman's article 'John Turner, Senior, and Mary Brewster: Their Marriage and Their Children' (MD 32 (1934): 28 ff.), lending-only, which may cite a record naming Mary as mother of the later children; (2) the Plymouth County probate file papers of John Turner Sr. (1697), for a receipt or division of the household goods among 'my four Daughters' after the widow Mary's death, which would show whether Mary was still living and name Ruth Silvester. ¶9b
