About the family

Widow of Claus Hermundsen of Indre Takle: named as 'Enken Kari Johansdatter' in his probate No. 68 (Ytre Sogn 7a fol. 17a, September 1750). ¶1

Life story

Birth not known. Her eldest daughter's son Torstein Iversen was born about 1734, so she was probably born before about 1690 (inference). ¶4

Lifetime division No. 69 (Ytre Sogn 7a fol. 17b-18a, September 1750, same session): at the request of 'den Dannequinde Kari Johansdatter, afgangne Claus Hermundsen Tackles efterlevende Enke', her estate (97 rd 3 ort 13 s) was divided 'imellem hendes Børn'. 'Hendes Børn er: første Søn Claus Claussen Mittackle, anden Søn Hermand Claussen Nesse, tredie Søn Hans Claussen, 4de Søn Ole Claussen og 5te Søn Johannes Claussen [the last two apparently twins], alle myndige og tilstede. Ældste Datter [K/M]ari Clausdatter gift med Iver Engesætter, 2den Datter Magdeli Clausdatter gift med Niels [Horager?] og 3die Datter Brite Clausdatter gift med Rasmus Bendixsen [..]dal, alle myndige og nærværende.' Her laugværge (legal guardian) was her eldest son Claus Claussen of Midt-Takle. Read from the scan. Her shares were drawn as 'Moder-gave' for the children. ¶2
