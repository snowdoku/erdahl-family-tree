About the family

Father of Aasmund Evindsen Hiemdal (from the patronymic). An 'Evind Hiemdal' was a marriage cautioner at Treungen in 1761 and 1762, and a 'Torbor Evindsdatter Hiemdal' married Hølje Joensen Tvetene in 1775; she is probably Aasmund's sister. Not otherwise researched. ¶1

His daughter Guri, aged 15 weeks, was buried at Treungen on 22 Mar (Lady Day) 1755: 'Evind Hiemdals daatter Guri gl. 15 uger' (Kviteseid ministerialbok 1754-1773, burials p.2, image 153). So he had a child born ~Dec 1754, about 20 years after his son Aasmund (m. 1761). The burial register for 1754-1759 does not show his own burial. ¶2

His son Jørgen, aged 16, was buried at Treungen on 4 May 1766 (5th Sunday after Easter): 'Evind Hiemdalls Søn Jørgen, gl. 16 aar' (image 172). ¶4

Parents (Probable): the 1725 Heimdal skifte (Øvre Telemark skifteprotokoll no. 4, fol. 88a-89a, no. 54, 16 Jul 1725) names 'Evend Anderssøn', of age, as the second son of the aged couple Anders Sigurdsen and Sigri Tallevsdatter of Heimdal, and lays out his share in nordre Heimdal. This fits Evind Andersen Hiemdal: born about 1701 (65 at his burial in 1766), so of age by 1725; living at nordre Heimdal by 1733, where his wife Hage was heir at the probate of her mother Torbjør Aamundsdatter; and the only Evind Andersen at Heimdal in the records read (Anders Knudsen of søndre Heimdal had no son Evind). His daughter Guri (bur. 1755) bears the name of his sister Guri Andersdatter. Probable rather than Confirmed because no single record names both the 1725 heir and the 1766 decedent; the link is by name, farm, land share and age. A younger Evind Andersen at Tvedt is a different man. ¶7

Life story

Deed: Pantebok I 3 (DA 10868) no. 504, fol. 139a-b, read on the scan. At the ting held at Prestegaarden for Nissedal tinglag on 4 Mar 1730 was read a deed dated Hiemdahl 11 Oct 1729 in which 'Anders Evendsøn, boende paa gaarden Tvedt i Treungens annex', with the consent of his wife 'Ingeborg Andersdaatter', sells to 'min kiære Svoger Evend Andersøn boende paa Hiemdahl', 'min hustrues Ingeborg Andersdaatters arvelod efter skifte brev passeret 16de July 1725 udi gaarden Nordre Hiemdahl', 1½ maller with buildings, for 66 rd 1 ort 8 s. Witnesses Ole Aarack and Anders Knudsøn Hiemdal. This ties the daughter Ingeborg and the son Evend of the 16 Jul 1725 division by Anders Sigurdsen and Sigri Tallevsdatter to the Evend Andersen living on nordre Heimdal in 1729 (Svoger = brother-in-law), and so bridges 1725 to the Evend Andersen Heimdal of 1733 and later. It also shows that Ingeborg Andersdatter was married to Anders Evendsen of Tvedt by 1729. His own grade (link to his child) is not changed by this deed. ¶9

Land in the same skifte (DA 25674 No. 775, fol. 726a, view 735 right, 'Faste Gods'): his part of N[ordre] Hiemdahl is described as 1 1/2 tønde bought 14 Oct 1765 (tingl. 3 Mar 1766), 3 maller inherited 'efter Skifte brev af 16 Junij 1725', and 1 1/2 maller 'tilkiøbt' from 'sin Søster Ingebor Andersdaatter' of her inheritance under the same skifte. This is a primary statement, in his own probate, that the decedent of 1767 was the brother of Ingeborg Andersdatter and an heir of the 16 Jul [sic: 'Junij'] 1725 Heimdal skifte, so it directly bridges him to his parents Anders Sigurdsen and Sigri Tallevsdatter (their own grades are not changed here). The widow Haye Knudsdatter declared that she would hand the farm to 'sin ældste Søn Aasmund Evensøn', who was to give her kår (board and lodging) for life; Aasmund and Knud each received 154 rd 18 s, paid in land in Hiemdahl (4 1/2 Bdtg each). ¶11

Buried at Treungen church on the 22nd Sunday after Trinity (26 Oct) 1766: 'kast Jord paa Evind Andersen Hiemdal, gl. 65 aar' (Kviteseid ministerialbok 1754-1773, burials, image 172). So he was born about 1701 and his patronymic was Andersen. Identified with this Evind because he is the only Evind seen at Hiemdal in the 1754-1778 registers (inferred). A younger Evind Andersen lived at Tvedt (son Anders buried 1767, image 173) and is a different man. ¶3

Name: the patronymic Andersen is added from the 1766 burial. Toruv Andersen Hiemdal (see his page, b. ~1720) shares the patronymic and farm and may be a younger brother, but no record says so. ¶5

Probate: 'Hage [Hæge] g.m. Evend Anderssen Heimdal' was the eldest child of Knud Bjønnesen and Torbjør Aamundsdatter of nordre Heimdal at Torbjør's probate in July 1733 (protocol 1722-34 fol. 338b). So Evind was married to Hæge Knudsdatter by mid-1733. Whether she was Aasmund Evindsen's mother is not stated. Evind is not a son of Anders Knudsen of søndre Heimdal. ¶6

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed, graded on his link to his son Aasmund Evindsen Heimdal (see his page). Record: his own skifte, DA 25674 (skifteprotokoll no. 10, 1759-68) No. 775, view 735 left page: 'Anno 1767 den 20de May var efter lovlig Varsel forsamlede paa Gaarden Hiemdahl i Treungens Annex og Hvidesøe Præstegield' the lagrettemen Jon Fione, Gunder Homme and Torof Hiemdahl, 'for at Registere og Vurdere samt Skifte og Dele alt hvis Boen tilhørte efter afgangne Even Andersøn', to be divided 'imellem Enken Haye Knudsdaatter og deres sammenavlede Børn Nemlig: Aasmund Evensøn myndig, Knud Evensøn myndig, Anders Evensøn 22 1/2 aar gammel, Niels Evensøn 12 1/2 aar gammel, Gundbør Evensdaatter gift med Tiøstof Aarach, Torbiør Evensdaatter 19 aar gammel'. The record names Aasmund Evensøn (of age) as a child of the deceased Even Andersen of Heimdal and his widow Hæge Knudsdatter. Continuity: Evind Andersen Hiemdal was buried at Treungen 26 Oct 1766 aged 65; the skifte follows seven months later on the same farm; his son is the Aasmund Evindsen Hiemdal married 1761, with no rival Aasmund Evensen at Heimdal. Also gives the full child list: Aasmund, Knud (of age), Anders 22 1/2, Niels 12 1/2, Gundbør (married to Tiøstof Aarach), Torbiør 19 (she is the Torbor Evindsdatter who married Hølje Joensen in 1775). Son Jørgen (bur. May 1766) and daughter Guri (bur. 1755) had died before him. ¶10
