About the family

Wife of Rolv Knudsen of Slettum, Lesja (see his page). Lesja ministerialbok 1732-1776 (source 9236), p. 421 (canvas 203), read on the scan: 'Bapt: Rolv Knudsøn Slettum og Anne Pedersdatter[s Barn]', born 31 May [1756], godparents including people of Raanaa and Lesstad (child's name not clearly read). Her churching follows on p. 422 (canvas 204): 'item Rolf Slettums Anne' (Dom. 7 p. Trin. 1756). Birth about 1725 is an estimate only (a child in 1756). ¶1

Parents (Probable, from the Lesja bygdebok vol. 1, canvases 181/183, chapter Einbu): Peder Hansen (ca. 1685-1745) and Marit (ca. 1683-?) of Nordre Eneboe (Nørdre Einbu), whose children include 'Anne, ca. 1718, sjå Nørdre Slettom (1-7)'. This makes her the aunt of Anne Pedersdatter b. 1757 (see her page), the wife of Peder Ingebretsen of Søndre Eneboe. ¶7

Her parents are proved: skifte after Peder Hansen of Eneboe (Einbu), 8 Feb 1745 (card 39945/2437, fol. 701b), married to Marit Biertesdatter, child '5. Anne g.m. Rolf Knudsøn Slethum'; and skifte after her brother Bierte Pedersen, 29 Mar 1758 (25158 fol. 380a, read): mother Marit Biertesdatter, brothers Hans (dead) and Peder Pedersen Eneboe, sisters Kari (married Erland Enstad) and 'Anne Pedersdatter gift med Rolf Slettum'. She was therefore already married to Rolf by Feb 1745 (the bygdebok gives 1745). ¶8

Life story

The 1763 Lesja extra-tax manntall (source 45390, view 287) shows Rolv Knudsen at Slette with his second wife Guro Jesbr.d. and the children over 12 Peder and Kari, so Anne Pedersdatter had died between 1756 and the 1760 betrothal (Lesja 9236 c230: 'Despons: Rolv Knudsøn Slettum og Goro Jes[bre]ts d[atter]', Trinity 1760, Verket). Kari's grade link to Anne is unchanged (Probable): Anne is the wife churched as 'Rolff Slettums Anne' on 10 Mar 1748, two weeks after Kari's baptism, but no record names her as Kari's mother. ¶4

The Lesja bygdebok (vol. 1, canvas 370, chapter Slettom, entry 1-7) gives her as 'Anne Pedersd. frå ___ Nørdre ___, 1718-59', married 1745 to Rolf (1720-83); her home farm was in a gap of the word-box rebuild. Her parents are still unknown; the next record is her own baptism about 1718 (Lesja books before 1724 are lost or unindexed) or a probate on her home farm. ¶6

Burial: 'Anne Pedersd[atter]' of Slettom, aged 42 (b. about 1717), buried 4 Feb 1759 at Lesja (Lesja ministerialbok 1732-1776; index pg00000000936641). Name, farm and age fit: her probate at Nordre Slettum was held 22 May 1759, and the bygdebok gives '1718-59'. She is the only Anne Pedersdatter buried from Slettum in the index. Birth stays BEF 1730; the burial implies about 1717. ¶9

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 3

Speculative mother of Kari Rolvsdatter (graded on her link to Kari). She is the only wife of Rolv Knudsen named in any record found (1756); Kari was born about 1748-51 and a son Hans on 20 Mar 1750 (9236 p. 348, canvas 167, 'Rolff Slettums Hans'). An earlier wife of Rolv cannot be excluded, since no marriage of Rolv Knudsen has been found and Kari's own baptism has not been read. The eldest known son, Peder (b. about 1745), may be named after her father Peder. ¶2

Upgrade: Speculative to Probable, graded on her link to Kari Rolvsdatter. Kari's baptism (Lesja 9236, p. 325, canvas 156, Dom. Esto Mihi [25 Feb] 1748, 'Rolf Slettums Kari', born 19 Feb) is followed on the same page by the churching of the mother, 'item Rolff Slettums Anne', on Dom. Oculi [10 Mar] 1748 at the Verket church. A churching two weeks after a birth is the mother's, so Kari's mother was Rolv Slettum's wife Anne. Rolv's wife was again 'Anne Pedersdatter' at the baptism of 13 Jun 1756 and 'Rolf Slettums Anne' at that churching. ¶3a

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed, graded on her link to Kari Rolvsdatter. Record: the probate after her, Nord-Gudbrandsdal skifteprotokoll 1752-1764, fol. 428a, 22 May 1759, 'Sletten nordre i Lesjeskogen (Nordre Slettum)', as indexed on the probate cards (source 39945, images 12275-12277, read): deceased Anne Pedersdatter, married to Rolf Knudsen; children (Barn) 1. Peder 14, 2. Knud 13, 3. Hans 9, 4. Endre 5, 5. Rolf 1, 6. Kari 11, 7. Marit 7, 8. Anne 1/4. The card's fields label the deceased, her husband and their children, so it names the mother-daughter relationship. Continuity to the tree's Kari, all on Nordre Slettum with no rival Kari: baptism of 'Rolf Slettums Kari', 19/25 Feb 1748, with 'Rolff Slettums Anne' churched 10 Mar 1748 (9236 c156) -> Kari 11 in this probate, May 1759 (age exact) -> 'Kari' a child over 12 of Rolv Knudsøn at No. 12 Slette in the 1763 manntall (45390 v287) -> Kari Rolvsdatter of Slettum, bride of Jon Alvsen 1775 -> buried 1833 aged 85. Gaps 11, 4 and 12 years, same farm. The probate also confirms the other children named in her notes (Peder b. about 1745, Knud 1746, Hans 1750, Endre 1754, Rolf, Marit, and a daughter Anne born about Feb 1759, so she probably died in or after childbirth early in 1759). Death set BEF 22 MAY 1759 (from 'BET 1756 AND 1760' reasoning in earlier notes). ¶5

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Not Confirmed: the 1748 entry gives the mother's first name only, and the 1748 baby is tied to the 1775 bride Kari Rolvsdatter of Slettum across 27 years. ¶3b
