Life story

1801 census: wife of Giert Falch, 48, in her first marriage. Her father Samuel Halvorsen (77, 'the householder's father-in-law') and her brother Johannes Samuelsen (37, smith) lived with them. ¶1

Probate 1805: widow 'Citrone Samuelsdatter' in the probate of Giert Nielsen Falck at Balholmen, 9 October 1805 (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll A 12, fol. 510b), with Christen Christensen Fiærestad the elder as her lauverge (legal guardian of a woman). She and Giert had 7 children together: Niels, Giert, Samuel, Kari, Citrone, Marie and Guri. Her son Samuel, 10, lived with her. ¶2

Laberg 1985 (Balestrand 1: Ættesoge): a whole-book word search for Zithone, Sitrone, Citrone, Sitrona, Zitrone, Sidonia, Falch and Falck gave 0 hits. Her father appears only as 'Samuel Halvorson Lunde' on p. 109, as father of another daughter married in 1785. The book does not mention her. ¶3
