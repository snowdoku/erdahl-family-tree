Life story

Primary record: betrothed in October 1692 to Giest Mattesen (Leikanger 11564 canvas 10 right: 'Giest Mattes[en] / Dorethe Samsons d. [Hy?]dall } spon.'). The farm word after her name is probably her home farm; it reads like '[Hy]dall' or '[Y]dall' and is not identified yet. Grade unchanged. Test record for her parents: a Samson at that farm in the 1664/1666 censuses (35519 / 35573), and the nupt. entry (11564 c10-c12), which may give her farm more clearly. ¶3

Primary record: married Giest Mattisen at Leikanger, Dom. 25 post Trin. 1692 (11564 canvas 11 left: 'Dorethe Samsons d.', no farm). Grade unchanged. ¶4

Her farm word '[Hy?]dall' (betrothal, Oct 1692) is probably Hydal/Ydal in Vangsnes sogn: the 1664 bailiff's census (35573 v115, p. 210) lists 'Hydall' in Vangsnes sogn with the farmers Mogens, Lasse and Lasse (no Samson), and the Leikanger register buries 'Hans Ydall' at Vangsnes on the 1st Sunday in Advent 1694 (11564 c16 R). A Samson aged 12 in 1664 was a son of Elling of Hofland, the next farm (too young to be her father). ¶5a

Reading: the 1750 probate heading of Samson Giestsen (7a fol. 37b, 24163 view 38) was re-cropped at high resolution. The phrase after the brothers' names reads 'begge myndige og tilstede paa egne og deres Moders Vegne'; 'Moders' is legible. So the record calls Dordej Samsonsdatter the mother of the brothers Mathias and Gutorm Giestsen, not only of the deceased Samson. Grade stays Probable (graded on her link to Guttorm Gjestsen, Guttorm Gjestsen): the 1750 record gives no farm for Gutorm, and no later record ties the tree's Guttorm of Vikøren to his brother Mathias by a relationship word. Checked: Guttorm's own probate, 9 Jun 1775 (9b p. 601, 24167 view 302), names only the widow Synneve Arnesdatter, their children and the guardians Mads Hansen Solbje and others, no 'Farbroder'; Mathias Giestsen's probate, 22 May 1778 (9b p. 939, 24167 view 473), names only his widow Ingeleif Olsdatter, the children of his two marriages and the guardian Niels Nielsen Refsdal, no sibling. The bridge is still name + parish + Mathias Vigøren as surety at Gjest Guttormsen's betrothal in 1759. ¶2a

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Probable as mother of Guttorm Gjestsen (see his page), graded on that link. Named 'Dordej Samsons Datter', 'Den afdødes Moder', heir with 'tvende Brødre Mathias Giestsen og Gutorm Giestsen' in the 1750 probate after her son Samson Giestsen at Hagen under Hopperstad, Vik (Ytre Sogn 7a fol. 37b, No. 92, 24163 view 38). Her name reading 'Dordej' is fairly clear in the heading; the share head is fainter. Primary record found: the probate after the cottager Samson Giestsen at Hagen under Hopperstad in Vik, 1750 (Ytre Sogn 7a fol. 37b, No. 92, 24163 view 38), names as heirs, besides his widow Ingebor Iversdatter, his mother Dordej Samsonsdatter and 'tvende Brødre Mathias Giestsen og Gutorm Giestsen, begge myndige og tilstede paa egne og deres [Moders] Vegne' (share heads: 'Den afdødes Moder', 'Den afdødes Broder Mathias Giestsen', 'Anden Broder Gutorm Giestsen'). So Guttorm and Mathias Giestsen were brothers of Samson Giestsen, and their father was dead by 1750 (the mother inherits with the brothers; Samson left no children). The 1701 census for Vik lists at Torvold (parish 'Wiigs', so Torvold is in Vik) 'Giest Matiesen', inderste, 43, with his son 'Samson Giestsen', 2 (b. ~1699): the only Samson Giestsen in the Ytre Sogn records, and Samson is the name of the mother's father (Dordej SAMSONSdatter), so the 1750 Samson is the 1701 boy. Guttorm (b. ~1706) and Mathias (b. ~1701) were born after the census. Grading: Probable, not Confirmed. The 1750 probate names the brothers and their mother but not their father, so Giest Matiesen as father is inferred (shared patronymic Giestsen + the 1701 Samson, 49 years and a change of farm earlier). The tree's Guttorm is identified with the 1750 'Gutorm Giestsen' by name, parish and his brother Mathias Giestsen (Mathias of Vikøren stood surety at the 1759 betrothal of Guttorm's son Gjest, 11566 c34); the probate does not give Guttorm's farm. The word read '[Moders]' in 'deres [Moders] Vegne' is partly legible; if confirmed it names Dordej as the brothers' (not only Samson's) mother. No rival Guttorm Giestsen is indexed in Ytre Sogn 1698-1790. ¶1a
