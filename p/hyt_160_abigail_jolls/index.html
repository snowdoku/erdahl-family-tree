About the family

Wife of Capt. Thomas Jolls of Boston by 1672: the Boston register records 'Thomas of Thomas & Abigail Jolls born Apr. 25' 1672 and 'Jonathan of Thomas & Abigail Jolls born Mar. 21' 1674 (Record Commissioners 9: 123, 133). By 2 Jun 1677 Thomas's wife was Susanna (birth of Robert). Her maiden name, marriage and death are not recorded in Boston. ¶1

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Speculative mother of Sarah (Jolls) Kimball: the Prince Genealogy (pp. 23-25) says 'Capt. Thomas Jolls and wife Abigail of Boston were the parents of Sarah', but elsewhere on the same page only 'probably'. Sarah's birth is not recorded; a birth about 1675 between Jonathan (Mar 1674) and Thomas's marriage to Susanna fits, which would make Abigail her mother. That Sarah inherited the estate of her brother Thomas (b. 1672, son of Abigail) in 1696 is consistent with full siblinghood but does not prove it. ¶2

Searches that found nothing 1

Negative search: FreeREG has no marriage of Thomas Jolls in Middlesex or London 1660-1690, and no marriage of any Abigail Jolls anywhere 1655-1690 (fuzzy). Her maiden name and marriage place are still unknown. The Stepney Jolls families of 1652-1675 have no Thomas with a wife Abigail. Grade unchanged (Speculative). ¶3
