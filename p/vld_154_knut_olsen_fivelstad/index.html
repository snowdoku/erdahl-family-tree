About the family

Father of Ole Knudsen of Fivelstad (see his page), Confirmed; graded on his link to Ole. Two primary records name the relationship: the 1701 census lists Olle Kds. (18) as Knud's son on Fivelstad, and the 1703 probate names the son Ole Knudsen. Continuity: same farm, two years apart; Ole Knudsen of Fivelstad was buried at Sunnylven on 2 May 1739 aged 54 (b. about 1685, within two years of the census age). No other Ole Knudsen is recorded at Fivelstad. ¶3

Life story

1666 vicar's census (index): Knut was not on Fivelstad in 1666. The farm then had two holders: Oluf Arnesen, 70 (b. about 1596), with sons Oluf Olsen 29 (b. about 1637) and Arne Olsen 20 (b. about 1646), and Lauridtz Arnesen, 60, with sons Oluf 11 and Arne 6 (pc00000001602797-802). Knut's 1701 stepson 'Arne Oels.' (b. about 1673) fits a son of Oluf Olsen (b. about 1637), named after his grandfather Oluf Arnesen, which suggests Knut married the widow of Oluf Olsen and came to Fivelstad by marriage; nothing names her. At least three Knud Olsens born about 1646 appear elsewhere in the 1666 Sunnmøre census (Fitten, Lundenæs Setter, Affraa), so his origin cannot be picked from the census alone. ¶4

1701 manntall (scan read, p. 126; index pc00000001091911): on 'Fibbelstad' (Fivelstad), Sunnylven, 'Knud', aged 55 (b. about 1646), opsidder/leilending and selfeier (part-owner), with 'Olle Kds.' aged 18 (b. about 1683) under 'Søner' and 'Stifsøn Arne Oels.' aged 28 (b. about 1673). The other household on the farm was Joen, 36. Image. ¶1

Probate (scan read): a skifte after Knut Olsen of Fivelstad was held on 11 Sep 1703, between his widow Giertrud Olsdatter and the children Ole Knudsen and Anne Knudsdatter (Sunnmøre skifteprotokoll 3A 02 fol. 7a, 24545/17). The 1701 'Stifsøn Arne Oels.' (b. about 1673) shows that Knut's wife in 1701 had an earlier husband named Ole; whether she was Giertrud and whether Giertrud was Ole Knudsen's mother is not stated in what has been read, so no mother is added. Arne Olsen stayed on Fivelstad: 'Arne Olsen Fivelstad' was buried at Sunnylven about 1751-52 (scan 17005 canvas 52; age read as 58 but possibly 78), and his wife (b. about 1677) was buried there on 11 Mar 1753 (pg00000001731174). ¶2

1703 probate wording (scan re-read): the skifte of 11 Sep 1703 is between 'hans efterlatte quinde Giertrud Ols datter' and 'den Sal: Mands 2de børn Ole Knudsen og Anne Knuds datter' (the late man's two children), not 'deres' (their) children. That wording leaves open whether Giertrud was the mother of Ole Knudsen, so she is still not entered as his mother. ¶5
