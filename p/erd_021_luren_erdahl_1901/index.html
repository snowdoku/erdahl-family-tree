Life story

Birth: born 7 July 1901 in Ellsborough Township, Murray County, and registered as 'L. Oliver Erdahl', male, the first child of B. L. Erdahl, farmer, 23, and Annie Quam, 19, both born in Minnesota. The birth was reported by W. L. Rogde of Ruthton. ¶1

Death: the family list recorded under his brother William (from Find a Grave) gives his life as 1901-1983. No death record has been read yet. ¶4

1905 MN census: 'Lurince O Erdall', 3, in his parents' household in Ellsborough Township. ¶2

1930 U.S. census (index only): a 'Luren O Erdahl' household is indexed in South Malta, Phillips County, Montana, on the same page as his parents' household (image 4610691_01025). The household members were not recorded by the searcher, and the image has not been read. ¶5

Marriage (probable): the Whatcom County, Washington marriage index lists 'Luren O Erdahl' and Mary Alta Gawley, 1945. The name and initial agree; the index has no ages or parents. ¶3
