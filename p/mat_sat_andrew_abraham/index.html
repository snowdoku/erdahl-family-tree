Life story

Married Lizzie Carrie Satter on 31 May 1888 in Union County; son of Abraham Olson and Berte 'Anna' Gjytri. He emigrated in 1881 and settled on the farm north of Spink in 1899, where he farmed through the censuses of 1900, 1910, 1920 and 1930 and outlived his wife by twenty-one years. ¶1

He gave $5 at his brother-in-law Gust Satter's wedding about 1894, and his brothers Ole, Sivert and Andy Abraham are in the same published gift list. ¶2

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
