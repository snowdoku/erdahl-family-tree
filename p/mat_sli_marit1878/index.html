About the family

Sister of Oline G. Slind Eidem, still living in SELBU, NORWAY when Oline died in October 1955: 'Gunder, Marit and Karen Slind of Selbu, Norway.' She is distinct from Mrs. Marit E. Slind (1832-1917) buried at Zoar, who is two generations older. ¶4

Life story

Oline Slind's elder sister, born 4 January 1878 and baptised in Selbu on 17 March 1878, her father then entered as Gunder Olsen, gaardmand, of Slinden. She never married and never left the farm. ¶1

Gravestone, Selbu Kirke Cemetery, Mebonden, Selbu, Section L, Row 06, No. 024: 'Marit G. Slind, 4 JAN 1878 - 21 MAR 1963'. The birth date agrees to the day with her baptismal entry of 17 March 1878 in the Selbu register, and she DIED 21 MARCH 1963 - which is the year the Selbu gaardshistorie's holder line for gnr 141 bnr 2 ends ('1921-1963'). She, not her brother, was the last of the family to hold the farm: Gunder died in the 1940s and Marit carried the bruk on alone for another sixteen years, to her death at eighty-five. She never married. ¶7

Her working life, as the censuses record it: 1900, 'Datter, Husgjerning' - daughter, housework; 1910, 'strikkerske' - knitter; 1920, 'd, uten kost' with the occupation 'Strikkerske', that is a daughter living at home but not boarded at the family's expense, earning her own keep by knitting. Selbu is the home of the Selbu mitten and its two-colour knitting, so a woman entered as strikkerske in that parish in 1910 is describing a real trade, not idleness. ¶2

She is in her widowed mother's household at Slind nordre at the 1920 census, aged forty-two. ¶3

She was a holder of the farm, not merely a daughter on it. Selbu i fortid og naatid vol. 7, p. 100: the bruk's holders are given as '1921-1963 Gunder... Slind, f. 1887, d.... 1947,... bruket med... Marit... Slind, f. 1878' - Gunder worked the holding WITH his sister Marit. The same page's earlier entry for her, in the children's list, likewise says she 'had the bruk'. She never married, worked as a strikkerske (knitter) in the 1910 and 1920 censuses, and ran the family farm with her unmarried brother for more than twenty-five years after their mother bought it in 1921. ¶5

She is one of the four siblings Oline's 1955 obituary names as surviving in Selbu, and she appears in the photograph on page 98 of the same volume, captioned 'Marit Gundersdatter Slind'. ¶6
