Life story

Birth and death (inference): born before about 1680, since he was married by 1701 and his father was born about 1631; died at or near Indre Oppedal before 30 May 1701. He is not in the 1701 census of Indre Oppedal, which fits a death before it was taken. ¶4

Marriage: he married Eli Monsdatter (see her page), widow of Iver Lassesen, between 1 Jun 1699 (her first husband's probate) and 1701; no marriage record (none survives in Gulen this early). He was thus the stepfather of Randi Iversdatter, later the wife of his brother Mons Johansen (Probable). ¶2

Creditor in 1700: 'Lars Johansen' (1 ort 4 s) is a creditor in the probate after Agate Monsdatter at Indre Oppedal on 8 Oct [1700], beside Mons Johansen (loan and interest, 1 rd 3 ort). Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll 1b, fol. 258b. ¶3

Probate: on 30 May 1701 the lensmann Niels Hansen with the lagrettemenn Jørgen Aspnæs, Mongs Næsse and Iver Sætter[?] (or Dahle[?]) met on Indre Oppedal to divide the estate of the late married couple Lars Johansen and Elj Mognsdaatter (Eli Monsdatter), both dead; heirs on the husband's side 'hans fader Johannes Larsen Indre Oppedal' (his father Johannes Larsen of Indre Oppedal), and on the wife's side her children by her first husband Iver. Lars left no children (his father inherited). Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll 1b, fol. 331b-333a. ¶1

Collateral: brother of the Mons Johansen 22 listed as Johannes Larsen's son in the 1701 census of Indre Oppedal, who is probably (Probable) Mons Johansen d. 1739 (see his page). Created only to carry Eli Monsdatter's second marriage; no descendants. ¶5
