Life story

Bygdebok (Treungen vol., Heimdal nordre, canvases 358-359): 'Hølje [..] Heimdal [g. m.] Ragnhild Åsmundsdtr.'; children Gunstein (about 1650), a [..] Larsdtr. (daughter-in-law?), Margit, Signe, Margit; 'Hølje døydde etter 1692, fødd omlag 1620'. A skifte of 1667 is mentioned, at which Hølje, Sigrid and Berit each got a share of Heimdal. Åsmund Høljeson, who bought Heimdal in 1693, is his son by the book. ¶1

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
