About the family

Parentage (Probable): the identification of this 1801 baptism with Torkild Olsen rests on (1) the only Torkild son of an Ole of Ibrække in the indexed Nissedal/Kviteseid baptisms 1799-1803; (2) his burial age of 45 on 9 Aug 1846, which a birth in early Aug 1801 matches exactly, and his marriage age of 22 on 12 Apr 1823; (3) residence continuity, since he married in 1823 as 'Torkild Olsen Ibrække' and died at Ibrække; and (4) the first marriage witness in 1823, Ole Olsen Ibrække, who is either his father or his elder brother Ole (b. about 1795). The other two Torkild sons of an Ole (1802, Tvedt and Fiellestad) have the wrong year and farm. Not yet read and would confirm: the 1823 marriage scan names no father; a confirmation entry about 1816-1818, a skifte (probate) after Ole Olsen of Ibrække, or the skifte after Torkild himself in 1846 (Nedre Telemark sorenskriveri) would name the relationship. ¶3

Life story

Baptism: 'Ole Olsen Ibrækes Søn Torkild' was baptised by Mr Storm in Nissedal church on the 10th Sunday after Trinity 1801 (9 Aug 1801, computed from Easter 5 Apr 1801). His mother Johanne Joensdatter was churched (introduced) on the 13th Sunday after Trinity (30 Aug). Godparents: Tone Tallaksdatter Møle(?), Turi Halvorsdatter(?) Tveiten, [G?]uri Davidsdatter of the same place, Targjei Nielsen Møle(?) and Torgrim(?) Olsen Ulvsbrot(?); the farm names and some patronymics are uncertain readings of the scan. ¶2

1801 census: born after the census date (1 Feb 1801). His parents' household at Ibræke (household 2) then held Ole Olsen 33, farmer-occupier (bonde og gaardbeboer), Johanne Joensdatter 32, children Ingebor 8, Ole 6 and Joen 3, a servant, and Ole's widowed mother Ingebor Torkelsdatter, 68. Torkild was probably named after her father Torkel (inference from Norwegian naming custom). ¶4

Of Ibrække. Married Aasild Gulliksdatter of Midbø on 12 Apr 1823 in Nissedal. Children were baptized in Nissedal in 1825, 1829, 1831 (Thuri), 1834, 1837, 1839 and 1840, with the family at Midbø in 1829-1834. His Ibrække origin fits his daughter Thuri and her husband Hans later owning Ibrække østre. His own parents were not identified; in the 1801 census there are a Torkild Olsen b. ca 1787 at Fjellestad øvre and an older one at Osmundtvedt. Unresolved. ¶1

Marriage: he married Aasild Gunleiksdatter of Midbø on 12 Apr 1823 in Nissedal, as a bachelor (ungkarl); she was a spinster (pige). The marriage witnesses (forlovere) were Ole Olsen Ibrække and Gunder B[or?]ussen Midbøe; the second patronymic is an uncertain reading. No fathers are named. The copy in Nissedal ministerialbok 1814-1845 (p. 199) gives no legible ages, but the second register, Nissedal ministerialbok 1814-1860 (p. 196, same entry no. 1), gives the groom ('Torkeld Olsen Ibrække') as 22 and the bride as 18, with the same witnesses. ¶5

Death: 'Torkel Olsen, Ibrække' died 9 Aug and was buried 13 Aug 1846 in Nissedal, age 45 (Døde 1846 no. 18; the index gives burial 19 Aug, the scan 13 Aug). A different Torkil Olsen, age 43, of Fjellestad, died 9 Mar 1846. ¶6

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Upgrade: Confirmed as the son of Ole Olsen and Johanne Joensdatter of Ibrække. The Nissedal confirmation list for 1822 (ministerialbok 1814-1845, source 8019, p. 246, canvas 160, boys no. 12) reads 'Torkild Ibrek | Ole og Johanne Ibrek | døbt 1801 d. 10 Sønd. e. Trin.', with the verdict 'ringe i Kundskab men god i Dyd og Forhold' (poor in knowledge but good in conduct) and vaccination on 4 Mar 1806 by A. Hansen. This names his parents as Ole and Johanne of Ibrække and his baptism as the one of the 10th Sunday after Trinity 1801 ('Ole Olsen Ibrækes Søn Torkild', 9 Aug 1801). A year later 'Torkeld Olsen Ibrække', 22, married Aasild Gunleiksdatter of Midbø, so name, farm and age join the confirmand to the groom. This is the confirmation entry that the earlier 'Parentage (Probable)' note asked for. His link to his daughter Thuri rests on her 1831 baptism. ¶8

Audit 2 (1 Nov 2026): Confirmed kept, but on a different record from the one the upgrade note cites. Under the overnight grading rule his grade covers his link to his daughter Thuri (see her page), not his link to his own parents. The 1822 confirmation list proves the upward link to Ole Olsen and Johanne Joensdatter, which is graded on their records. The link to Thuri rests on her baptism of 23 Jan 1831 at Nissedal, which names her parents as Torkel Olsen and Aasild Gulliksdatter of Midbø, joined to the bride 'Thuri Thorkildsdatter' of Midbø, aged 28, who married Hans Thorjussen on 12 Jul 1858 (Nissedal ministerialbok 1814-1860, p. 218 no. 11; index read by the auditor, which gives no fathers). Same name, patronymic, farm and age. ¶9a

Corrections 1

Correction: an earlier note offered the 1801-census Torkild Olsens at Fjellestad øvre (b. about 1787) and Osmundtvedt (b. about 1747) as candidates. Both are ruled out by his burial age of 45 in 1846 (b. about 1801). ¶7
