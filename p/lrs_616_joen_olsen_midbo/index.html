About the family

Sisters and half-brothers named: Vest-Telemark pantebok I 5 (source 10870), entry No 311, read at the Nissedal ting at Nordbø on 28 Jun 1753 (views 98 right to 99 left). The sellers are 'Anstan Halvorsøn Nass, Targiei Halvorsøn Giøveland, Ole Jonsøn Midbøe, Torgrim Olsøn Grimstvedt, Niels Aslachsøn [..] og Hans Targiesøn Søftestad', and the buyer is 'vores k[iære] Broder Jon Olsøn' of Midbø. Ole Jonsen, Torgrim Olsen, Niels Aslachsen and Hans Targiesen sign 'paa vore Hustruer Kari, Torbior, Inger og Ragnild Olsdøttres Vegne' (for their wives Kari, Torbjør, Inger and Ragnild Olsdøtre). The shares came to them at the skifte held 9 Apr 1740 after 'vore Hustruers [Moder?]' (the word after Hustruers is unclear; Moder is the best reading). So Jon Olsen's full sisters were Kari, Torbjør, Inger and Ragnild Olsdøtre, and Anstan and Targei Halvorsen, who sell their own shares, are most likely his half-brothers by his mother's other marriage (inference: they are not listed as acting for wives). This matches the panteregister I 1 abstract (fol. 21) that Jon Olsen redeemed land in Midbø from 'Anstan Halvorsøn Nes' and others, tingl. 28 Jun 1753. ¶7

Parents found: the probate of 9 Apr 1740 at Midbø (Vest-Telemark skifteprotokoll 1735-40, source 25669, fol. 539, views 359-361) after 'Ole Torsøn og Aasild Torsdatter' names 'Sønnen Jon Olsøn' first among the heirs, with his sisters [Turi/Kari] (wife of Ole Jonsen Midbø), Torbjør (wife of Torgrim Olsen), Inger and Ragnild, and his half-brothers Ansten and Targier Halvorsen, sons of Aasild by an earlier marriage. This is exactly the group that sold him their shares in 1753 (pantebok I 5, No 311). It settles the question left open: his father was Ole Torsen (the 1707 buyer of Midbø), not Ole Jonsen. His share is headed 'Sønnen Jon Olsøn' (view 360). He was of age in 1740 (no guardian named for him; guardians were named only for Inger and Ragnild), so born before about 1715, as already given. ¶9

Life story

Probate: 'Anno 1756 den 13 Maj' at 'gaarden Midbøe i Nissedals annex af Hvideseid Præstegjeld', with lensmann Jacob Nordbøe and the appraisers Halvor Tvetten and [..] Brekke, to register and value the estate of 'afgl. Turi Jørgensdatter', to be divided 'imellem hendes igienlevende Mand Joen Olsøn og deris udi Ægteskab tilsammen avlede Børn, Nl.: Tore Joensøn [2?]1 aar, Jørgen Jonsøn 18 aar, Halje Joensøn 13 aar, Ole Joensøn 8 aar, Halvor Joensøn [.] aar, Gunnil Joensdatter [.] aar, Anne Joensdatter [.] aar, Todne Joensdatter 10 aar og Aase Joensdatter [.] aar' (Tore's age is partly under a blot; several ages were not legible). The shares are headed 'Ældste Søn' (Tore), 'Anden Søn Jørgen Jonsøn', 'Tredie Søn Helje Jonsøn', 'Fierde Søn Ole Jonsøn', 'Yngste Søn Halvor Jonsøn', 'Ældste Datter Gunnil', 'Anden Datter Anne', 'Tredie Datter Todne' and 'Yngste Datter Aasild'. Guardians: Torgiei Halvorsen [Øyeveland?] curator for the eldest son Tore Joensøn; Ole Bracke curator for Jørgen; Joen Haatvedt for Helje; Ole Fiellestad for Ole; Knud Fiellestad for Halvor; Poul Grovum for Gunnil and Anne; Hans Søftestad for Todne; the father Joen Olsøn for Aasild. Tore and Jørgen had curators, not ordinary guardians, which fits their being over 18. Vest-Telemark sorenskriveri skifteprotokoll 1752-1759 (source 25673), fol. 304b-306a (views 309L to 310L); register entry 'Midbøe ibm efter Turi Jørgensdatter', column 606. Read from the scan (crops and -v310L-midbo-guardians-1756.jpg). ¶1

Later record: 'Joen Olsen Midbøe' stood godfather at Nissedal church on 18 Apr 1760 to Hans, son of Aslach Grovum (Kviteseid ministerialbok 1754-1773, p. 58), so he was alive in 1760. Whether he remarried after 1756 was not checked. ¶4

Land register: panteregister I 1 (10915 fol. 21, view 63) has 'Jon Olsøn' redeeming land in Midbø for 180 rdl from Anstan Halvorsen Nes and others (deed dated 6 May, tinglyst 28 Jun 1753 at Nordbø); land laid out at the 13 May 1756 skifte after Turi Jørgensdatter; then his son Tore Jonsen redeemed 1½ tønde (tinglyst 10 Oct 1766) and, by a contract noted in the register, undertook to keep 'sin gl. fader' (his old father), with land in Midbø as security (marginal date 28 Nov 1776). So Joen Olsen was probably still alive in the 1770s. Father proposed as Speculative: Ole Jonsen Midbø (see his page), who bought out his sisters' shares of Midbø after their father Jon Tollefsen's death in 1722; see his notes for the case and the rival (Ole Toresen, 1707). ¶6

Burial: 'Den 27 Oct. ved Nissedals K. kast Jord paa Joen Olsen Midbøe gl. 83 Aar' (Kviteseid ministerialbok 1773-1786, 7982, p. 190, canvas 184 right). Born about 1703, which agrees with his being of age in 1740 and with the 1776 contract by which his son Tore undertook to keep 'sin gl. fader'. He is the only Joen Olsen known at Midbø (his son Ole Joensen was 8 in 1756), so the identification is sound; this adds dates only and does not change his grade. ¶10

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Confidence: Confirmed as father of Tore Joensen Midbø (see his page). The 1756 probate names Tore Joensøn as the eldest son of Joen Olsøn and Turi Jørgensdatter of Midbø, and the 1790 probate of Tore's brother Jørgen ties that sibling group to Tore Joensen Midbø. ¶3

The case for this identification 1

Identity of the eldest son: the 1790 probate after the bachelor Jørgen Johnsen of Nordre Midbø (25679 skifte No 21) names his 'Ældste Broder Thore Johnsen Midbøe', dead, with Rannei Omundsdatter as widow, and the brothers Haavor, Ole and Helje and sister Gunnild. That is the same set of siblings as the 1756 probate (Tore eldest, Jørgen, Helje, Ole, Halvor, Gunnil), and Jørgen's age (18 in 1756) agrees with his burial age of 52 in 1790. So the Tore Joensøn of 1756 is Tore Joensen Midbø (see his page), the father of Turi Toresdatter. ¶2

Corrections 1

Correction: the same deed shows that Ole Jonsen of Midbø as the Speculative father of Jon Olsen, was in fact his brother-in-law: he signs for his wife Kari Olsdatter, Jon's sister, and calls Jon 'Broder' (brother[-in-law]) together with the other sisters' husbands. Ole Jonsen and Jon Olsen of Midbø also witnessed a deed side by side at Nordbø on 15 Oct 1753 (10870 view 109 right). Jon's father was an Ole (from his and his sisters' patronymic), not Ole Jonsen. The father link to Ole Jonsen Midbø should be removed. The remaining named candidate is Ole Toresen, who bought land in Midbø in 1707 (panteregister I 1 fol. 21). The record that would settle both parents: the skifte of 9 Apr 1740 after Jon's mother, in the Vest-Telemark skifteprotokoll 1735-1740 (Digitalarkivet source 25669, scan only; April 1740 should lie near the end of the volume, about views 350-386). ¶8

Searches that found nothing 1

Parent search: the 1722 Midbø probate (card 6856 view 794) was read from the scan, 25692 fol. 535a, view 547 right page top (it begins 'Anno 1722 den 5te Augusti' at the foot of the left page, 534b). It is held after 'afgangne Jon Tollefsøn' between 'hans efterlevende hustru Guro Torbiørnsdatter' and 'deris sammen avlede børn Ole Jonsøn myndig, Hage Jonsdatter gift med Torbiørn Jacobsøn Hauger(?), Anne Jonsdatter 20 aar og Torbiør Jonsdatter 17 aar'; guardians Ole Grovum and Tollef Brekke; the widow also had a son by an earlier husband, Even Aslaksen (of age). Ole Jonsen, of age in 1722 (born before about 1697), is the only adult son, and Joen Olsen (born before 1715) at Midbø could be his son named after this grandfather Jon. Nothing names that link, so Ole Jonsen is NOT added (Speculative only). ¶5a
