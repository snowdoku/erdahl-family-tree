About the family

Wife of Gunder Eidem of Mehurin Township. Her maiden name is written out on the 1912 birth certificate of their son Konrad Bertrand - 'full maiden name Emma Wold, post office Marietta, White, age at last birthday 25, birthplace S.D.' - and the Lac qui Parle birth index records it again, as Wold in 1919 and Vold in 1922, on the registrations of her two short-lived sons Gordon Alvin. ¶1
