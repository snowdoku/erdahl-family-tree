About the family

Brother of Severt S. Eidem, so Mayme (Eidem) Eidem's paternal uncle, and the longest-lived of the immigrant generation at Elk Point. Linked as a sibling on Severt's Find a Grave memorial. ¶1

His parents are therefore DETACHED. Attaching him to Sivert Johnsen Eidem and Brynhild Jonsdatter Langli would assert a descent the registers contradict, and leaving him unparented is merely incomplete rather than wrong. His six children, his wife, his death and his burial are unaffected and remain documented. The record that would settle him is the Selbu baptism of 25 October 1857 read in full, which names his father, and the Baktroen chapter of Selbu i fortid og naatid. ¶15

Life story

Baptism: baptised in Selbu on 12 OCTOBER 1856 - Selbu parish register, Selbu local parish 1842-1858, births and baptisms, PAGE 210, SERIAL NO. 2. Child 'Ole'; father 'Sivert Johns[en]'; mother 'Brynild Johnsdr', residence EIDEM. The parallel register (1843-1859, page 107, serial 102) carries the same entry with the mother read as 'Borghild Johnsdatter' and the residence as 'Gagnum' - the clearest single demonstration that the indexed residence and name spellings for Selbu are not to be trusted without the scan. ¶5

FLAG FOR THE COORDINATOR, not acted on here: on that evidence the 'Ole Sivertson Eidem 1857-1948' of Saint Paul Cemetery, Elk Point, with wife Maret and sons Sivert O. and Einar O., is most likely OLE SIVERTSEN, SON OF SIVERT OLSEN AND MARIT TORSTENSDATTER OF BAKTROEEN - a cottager's son on the next holding - and NOT Severt S. Eidem's brother. If that is right, Find a Grave has attached the wrong Ole to this family, the project has inherited it, and Severt's actual brother Ole (b. Oct 1856) has yet to be traced in America. I have not made that change, because it would unpick a whole American household on Norwegian evidence alone and the American side is not my territory. ¶10

1865 census: person 004 at oestre Eidem, Selbu - 'Ole Sivertsen', aged 10, son, unmarried. ¶6

CAUTION - there were TWO Ole Sivertsens on this farm, born a year apart, and the American identification may have the wrong one. A second Ole Sivertsen was baptised at Eidem on 25 OCTOBER 1857, son of SIVERT OLSEN and MARIT TORSTENSDATTER, husmann folk at BAKTROEN - the cottage holding immediately next door, residence 0059 to our 0058 in the 1865 census. Find a Grave 121775505 gives 'Ole Sivertson Eidem, b. 8 OCT 1857, d. 29 Jan 1948, Saint Paul Cemetery, Elk Point', and 8 October 1857 fits the Baktroeen boy's baptism of 25 October 1857, not this boy's of 12 October 1856. ¶7

The Baktroeen Ole married MARIT EINERSDATTER of Stavnes in Selbu on 24 APRIL 1882 - Selbu parish register 1878-1891, marriages, page 315 serial 13, groom 'Hsmd', b. 1858, birthplace and residence Eidem; bride b. 1852, birthplace and residence Stavnes; BRIDE'S FATHER 'E. JOHNSEN, GMD.' - and baptised a son SIVERT on 26 MARCH 1883 at Eidem, the father then entered as 'Ind' (inderst, lodger), the mother as 'Marit Einarsdr' b. 1853. ¶8

FOUR independent hooks now fit the Baktroeen man rather than this one, and the fourth comes out of this tree's own American data. The tree holds Ole's wife as Maret Eidem, 'Maret Eidem, born DECEMBER 1852 in NORWAY' - which is Marit Einersdatter of Stavnes, b. 1852, to the year. It holds his son Sivert O. Eidem, 'Sivert O. Eidem, b. about 1883 in NORWAY' - which is the Sivert baptised at Eidem on 26 March 1883. The next son is EINAR O., b. 1884 - and Einar is exactly the name the Selbu custom gives a second son, for the mother's father EINER JOHNSEN of Stavnes. And the gravestone date 8 October 1857 fits the Baktroeen boy's baptism of 25 October 1857. Wife, eldest son, second son's name and birth date all point one way. ¶9

The Norwegian-born son Sivert O., b. 1883, is the hard evidence that Ole married in Norway and crossed with at least one child - so his emigration falls after 1883 and before September 1884, when Einer was born in Dakota. ¶4

Censuses: he emigrated and settled beside his brother Severt. 1900 federal census, Brule township, Union County, South Dakota, Ancestry image 4120605_00156 - 'Ole S Eidem, head, born OCTOBER 1857, Norway', with wife Maret (b. Dec 1852, Norway) and five South-Dakota-born children: Einer (Sep 1884), Magnus (Jan 1887), Anna (Apr 1889), Minnie (Jan 1892) and Joseph T. (Mar 1894). The stated month and year of his birth agree exactly with the 8 October 1857 in the tree, which is what identifies him. ¶2

1910 federal census, Brule township, Ancestry image 4449870_00450 - head, b. 1858 Norway, with sons Sivert O. (b. 1883 NORWAY - a child he brought with him), Martin O. (1887), daughter Anna R. (1889) and son Joseph T. (1894). 1920, image 4390883_00436 - head, with son Joseph and Joseph's wife May, and his son Martin as 'brother' of the head. 1930, image 4548146_00093 - now living as FATHER in his son Martin's household. 1940, NARA T627 roll 3869 image 133 - indexed 'Ole I Eidem', head, b. abt 1858 Norway, with Martin and daughter-in-law Elora. ¶3

Correction, and it splits one tree record into two men. This record had been built as both the Elk Point farmer who died in 1948 and a brother of Severt S. Eidem, and the Selbu parish registers show those are not the same person. There were TWO Ole Sivertsens on the Eidem farms. Severt's own brother was baptised 12 October 1856 and is recorded here separately. This man, whose gravestone at Elk Point gives 8 October 1857 and whose household the American censuses record, fits the baptism of 25 October 1857 in the BAKTROEN branch instead. His wife, in this tree as Marit Einersdatter of Stavnes, married the Baktroen Ole on 24 April 1882, and their son Sivert was baptised at Eidem on 26 March 1883 - which is the Sivert O. Eidem born 1883 in Norway who appears in this tree as his son. So the household below him stands; it is the link upward that fails. ¶14

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

Records that disagree 2

Against that: the only Ole S. Eidem of Selbu in the whole Trondheim emigrant index left on 29 MAY 1884 for 'Elk Point Dak', aged 27 - which fits an October 1856 birth better than an October 1857 one - and the ledger names his wife RAGHILD, 25, and his son HANS, eleven months, neither of which matches either Selbu couple. Neither Ole, and no Marit Einersdatter, is in the 1891 or 1900 census of Selbu, so both left the parish. ¶11

I have NOT changed his parents. The Find a Grave attribution stands at Probable with this caution recorded against it. Three records would settle it: the klokkerbok scan of the 1882 marriage (Selbu 1869-1888, page 276 entry 13) for the groom's father's name in full, since the two transcriptions disagree and the ministerialbok scan is smudged at that word; the immigration year for Ole Eidem in the 1900 US census of Brule Township; and the name of his wife on her gravestone or in the Union County church book - Marit against Ragnhild decides it outright. ¶12

Corrections 2

Correction, independent of all that: if this is Severt's brother, he was born about 8 OCTOBER 1856 and not 1857. A child cannot be baptised fourteen months before he is born, and the 1865 census makes him 10 at 31 December 1865. ¶13

Correction: several earlier notes on this record describe the other Ole and are superseded - the opening line calling him 'Brother of Severt S. Eidem', the 'Baptism' note (12 October 1856, page 210 no. 2) and the '1865 census' note (aged 10 at oestre Eidem). Those facts belong to Ole Sivertsen Eidem, as are the two 1856 baptism sources and the 1865 census source listed here. His own parents are left unlinked: the Baktroen identification is Probable, and the record that would settle it is the Selbu baptism of 25 October 1857 (page 217 no. 126) read for his father's name against the 1882 marriage entry (klokkerbok 1869-1888, page 276 no. 13) that names the groom's father. The 1884 emigrant 'Ole S. Eidem, 27', with wife Raghild and son Hans, bound for Elk Point, matches neither Ole's known family and remains unexplained. ¶17

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Namesake: not the same man as Ole Sivertsen (see his page), Severt S. Eidem's brother, baptised 12 October 1856 at Eidem, son of Sivert Johnsen and Brynhild Johnsdatter, and aged 10 in their household at oestre Eidem in the 1865 census. This man fits the other Ole Sivertsen of the farm, baptised 25 October 1857, son of Sivert Olsen and Marit Torstensdatter of the cottage Baktroen, who was still with those parents at Baktroen in the 1875 census ('Ole Sivertsen', b. 1857, helping his parents), married Marit Einersdatter of Stavnes on 24 April 1882 and had a son Sivert baptised at Eidem on 26 March 1883. The Elk Point stone's 8 October 1857, the wife Maret b. December 1852 and the son Sivert O. b. 1883 in Norway all fit him and none fits the 1856 boy, whose family had left Selbu for Union County by 1872 at the latest (his father died there on 15 April 1872). ¶16
