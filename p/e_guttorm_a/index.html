Life story

Burial: 'Guttorm Andersen' of Hilde, aged 78 (b. about 1739), died 2 Mar and was buried 5 Mar 1817 (Innvik ministerialbok 1816-1821; index pg00000000322648, indexed again from the klokkerbok as pg00000006848365). Farm and age match Aaland's '† 78 a. g. 1817' and fall inside the probate window (29 Apr 1812 - 27 May 1818); he is the only Guttorm Andersen in the Innvik burial index. The age is three years younger than the 1760 probate (24 in 1760 = b. about 1736); birth estimate kept at ABT 1740. ¶9

Probate, 1760: in the probate after his father Anders Guttormsen of Hilde (October 1760), the widow Giertrud Olsdatter and 'the 5 children the late man had with her' are named: Guttorm Andersen, then 24 (so born about 1736), Ole Andersen 18, Hans Andersen 1[5?], Kari Andersdatter 25 and Gunnild Andersdatter 1[2?]. All five were under age (under 25); Guttorm, the eldest son, had Rasmus Guttormsen Hilde as guardian (Nordfjord skifteprotokoll A 13, fol. 387-389). His age of 24 in 1760 is about three years older than his ages in 1801 (61) and at death (78 in 1817, per Aaland). ¶4

Farmer at Hilde, Innvik. Married Anna Larsdatter of Loen on 3 Jun 1772 (he was a bachelor). Children baptized 1772 (Anders), 1774 (Samuel), 1778, 1780, 1785, 1788, 1791 and 1793. Listed in the 1801 census at Hilde, aged 61. ¶1

Aaland 1932: in the Stryn volume (Loen bruk nr. 15, printed p. 322) Aaland names 'Gutorm A. Hilde' as son-in-law (Vs.) of Lars Samuelsen of Loen: he took the deed to the holding in 1773, inheriting after his father-in-law and brother-in-law, but did not farm it. This identifies his wife Anne Larsdatter's father. ¶8

Probate, 1812: on 29 Apr 1812 he and his wife Anne Larsdatter, both living, divided their property among their children in their lifetime (skifte i levende live): Anders (eldest son, on Hilde), Samuel (living on Loen), Guttorm (21), Giertrud (30) and Anne (19) (Nordfjord skifteprotokoll A 21b, pp. 1591-1593). ¶5

Aaland 1932: Jacob Aaland's farm history of Hilde (Nordfjord fraa gamle dagar til no, Del 2 3 2, p. 33, bruk nr. 4 'Guttormbruket') lists 'Guttorm Andersson, † 78 a. g. 1817, sk. 1763. * Anne Larsd. Loen, † ett. 1812': he died in 1817 aged 78, took the deed (skøyte) to bruk nr. 4 in 1763, and married Anne Larsdatter of Loen. This agrees with the 1772 marriage and with his age of 61 in the 1801 census (born about 1739-40). The death year 1817 rests on this compiled source only; his Innvik burial entry would confirm it. ¶2

Aaland 1932: on the same bruk Aaland lists before him Anders Guttormson (farmer from 1715, deed 1740, died 1760 aged 71), married to Jartrud Olsdatter Hilde (died 1791 aged 84), then Ola Andersson (farmer 1761, died about 1763), who married the widow Jartrud. After Guttorm came Anders Guttormson (deed 1804, died 1813 aged 40), which fits Guttorm's son Anders baptized in 1772. Aaland lists holders, not children, so Guttorm's parentage is inferred from the succession: see the notes on his father and mother. ¶3

Probate, 1818: the probate after him was registered at Hilde on 27 May 1818 by lensmann Rasmus Kierkhorn, so he died before that date (Aaland gives 1817, aged 78). He is called a [?]kaarmand, probably a retired farmer living on a pension (kår) from the farm; the estate was only about 5 riksdaler, because he had handed the farm over (a document of 15 Oct 1804 is cited, matching his son Anders's deed of 1804). Heirs: the three children of his dead eldest son Anders Guttormsen Hilde (Guttorm, Ole and Anna Andersen/Andersdatter, with their farbror Samuel as guardian), the son Samuel Guttormsen of Loen, the youngest son Guttorm Guttormsen and the daughter Anne Guttormsdatter. No widow is named, and the daughter Giertrud (30 in 1812) is not listed (Nordfjord skifteprotokoll A 22b, fol. 595-596). ¶6
