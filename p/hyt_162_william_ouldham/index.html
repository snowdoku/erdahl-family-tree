About the family

Brother of the bride: Stratton, Plymouth Colony (1986), sketch of John Oldham, cites Jonathan Brewster's letter of 30 Apr 1636 to John Winthrop Jr., 'and if my brother Oldam be at the rivers mouth' (Winthrop Papers). This supports the old statement (Bradford's 1623 land division; Banks) that Lucretia was John Oldham's sister. If so, a baptism of John, son of William Ouldham, at All Saints, Derby, about 1589-1595 would be strong supporting evidence for William's fatherhood of the Plymouth Lucretia. ¶6a

Children of William Ouldam baptised at All Saints, Derby (FamilySearch index, England, Derbyshire, Church of England Parish Registers, father only named): Johannes 14 Jul 1592 (1:1:NGWZ-L5B); Elizabetha 24 Feb 1594[/5] (1:1:NGWZ-FJB); Jacobus 12 Jun 1597 (1:1:KBD6-2KW); Lucretia 14 Jan 1600[/1] (1:1:KBD6-JQW, image read); Grace 15 Jul 1604 (1:1:KBD6-GRC). Marriage 'Wilms Ouldam' x Philippa Sowter, 17 Nov 1588 (1:1:KBD6-XWY, NJH6-YMT). Burial of a William Ouldam at Derby, 26 Jun 1636 (1:1:KBDX-M7V): possibly him, not used. A Thomas Oldham married Elizabeth Roades at Derby 25 Jan 1615[/16] and had children there 1617-1631 (one named Phillippa, bp 10 Aug 1617, 1:1:KBD6-RRL): probably another son of William and Philippa, unproved. ¶8

Wife's burial: 'Buried Phillip[a] the wife of William Oldham', All Saints, Derby, 22 March 1616/17 (FamilySearch DGS image 99 of 772, ark 3:1:S3HY-DTM3-KZ8; index 1:1:KBD6-YSL). So he was still living and married to Philippa (Sowter) in 1617. That makes it more likely he is the William Ouldam buried at Derby on 26 Jun 1636 (index 1:1:KBDX-M7V), but this is not proved. ¶9

Parents not found (free sources). The Lichfield consistory calendar, Series IV, gives no places for 1561-1652, so its Oldham entries ('Oldham, William, ad.' 1593-95; 'Oldham, Thomas' c.1596-98; 'Oldham, John' 1607/8; 'Oldham, Anthony' 1613-15; 'Oldham, Thomas, ad.' 1622-24; 'Oldam, Elizabeth' c.1616-23; 'Oldham, Arthur' 1620/1) cannot be placed from the print; only after 1627, when places are given, do Oldhams appear (John of Leek 1628, Thomas of Chesterfield 1638/9), none of Derby. FreeREG (Derbyshire, Nottinghamshire, Lancashire, Cheshire) has no William Oldham baptism 1550-1570 that fits. Archive.org full text for 'Ouldam' finds only Lancashire families (Manchester, Salford, Eccles), not linked. ¶10a

Life story

Marriage: 'Married 17th November 1588, William Ouldham and Philippa Sowter', All Saints, Derby, quoted by Hunt from the published All Saints register. ¶2

Lichfield calendar: an administration of 'Oldham, William' was granted in 1594 or 1595 (Lichfield consistory calendar, place lost in the OCR). If he was of Derby he cannot be Lucretia's father, who was alive at her baptism in January 1600/1, so it is a different man or a different place. Not used. ¶5

Baptism of his daughter: 'Baptism solemnized in the Parish of All Saints in the Diocese of Derby and County of Derby in the year 1600. Lucretia, daughter of William Ouldham, baptised fourteenth January 1600 by Edward Bennett (Minister).' This is the text of a certificate from the All Saints register supplied by the Provost of Derby Cathedral, R. A. Beddoes, and printed verbatim by John G. Hunt, NEHGR 111 (1957): 242 (read in the Zaniewski96 photocopy). The date is 14 Jan 1600/1. Anderson, Great Migration Begins (Jonathan Brewster), and Stratton, Plymouth Colony, accept this baptism as that of Lucretia, wife of Jonathan Brewster. ¶1

Bridge search (2 Nov 2026), after the Audit 11 lowering, graded on his link to his daughter Lucretia Oldham: no record was found that ties the Derby family to Lucretia (Oldham) Brewster or to her brother John Oldham, so William stays Probable. Read: (1) John Oldham's estate in Massachusetts Bay: the Court of Assistants (Records 2:63) only names a committee (Haynes, Harlakenden, Nowell) on 'Mr Ouldams estate, & debts' and has Hutchinson and Mayhew gather the debts; no heir, widow, child or kin is named. Shurtleff, Mass. Bay Records vol. 1, has nothing on the estate beyond land matters. (2) His estate in Connecticut (Public Records of the Colony of Connecticut 1:4-6, 43-44, 1636-1641): inventory, custody of his goods by Clement Chaplin, a creditors' call and an account of debts and credits (Craddock, Pynchon, Chaplin and others) to be divided among creditors; again no heir or kinsman is named. (3) Winthrop Papers vol. 3: besides Jonathan Brewster's 'my brother Oldam' (1636), the Oldham letters concern trade and his murder; none gives his origin. (4) Lichfield consistory calendar, O section 1624-1652: no will or administration of any William Ouldham/Oldham (the only Derbyshire Oldham entry is Thomas Ouldham of Chesterfield, 1639), so there is no Lichfield probate for the William Ouldam buried at Derby 26 Jun 1636. (5) The Great Migration Begins vol. 2 (Oldham sketch) is not on archive.org; vol. 1 (William Brewster) states the Derby baptism and that Lucretia 'was sister of JOHN OLDHAM' without a further record. TAG 42 (1966): 212-215 is not online (index only; its index entries centre on Nathaniel and John Sowter). Supporting, circumstantial only (Stratton, Plymouth Colony, Oldham sketch): the All Saints, Derby, register shows children of Thomas and Elizabeth Oldham, John bp 9 Apr 1622 and Thomas bp 23 Aug 1624; Pope lists John Oldham aged 12 and Thomas aged 10 on the Elizabeth and Ann, May 1635; and Bradford says '2 litle boys that were his kinsmen' were saved when John Oldham was killed in 1636. If these are the same boys, the Derby Oldhams were John Oldham's kin; Thomas (m. 1615, named a daughter Phillippa in 1617) is probably William's son. This is a chain of name and age matches with no record naming any relationship, so it adds weight but does not reach Confirmed under Audit 11. Test records (unchanged): TAG 42:212-215; the Great Migration Begins Oldham sketch (vol. 2), for any English record of John Oldham's origin; the All Saints, Derby, baptisms of John 1622 and Thomas 1624 read on the image (to confirm the parents' names); Derby borough or Lichfield court records of 1601-1624 naming Lucretia or John with William. ¶13

Derby film read (FamilySearch signed in, 2 Nov 2026), graded on his link to his daughter Lucretia Oldham; grade unchanged (Probable). (1) The two boys. The All Saints register, read on the images (FHL film 1041144, 'Baptisms, marriages, burials 1558-1712 (vol 1)'), has under 'February 1622' [1622/3]: 'Bap. John Oldham sonne of Thomas 9 day' (image 101 of 272), and under 'June Anno 1624': 'Bapt. Thomas Oldame sonne of Thomas the 23 day' (image 103). So the dates are 9 Feb 1622/3 and 23 Jun 1624, not 9 Apr 1622 and 23 Aug 1624 as given by Stratton. Both entries name the father only; neither names the mother (Elizabeth is inferred from Thomas Oldham's marriage to Elizabeth Roades, 25 Jan 1615/16) and neither has any marginal note. The corrected dates still fit 'John 12' and 'Thomas 10' on the Elizabeth and Ann in May 1635 exactly (John 12 from Feb 1635, Thomas 10 from Jun 1634, under 11 until Jun 1635). (2) William's own entries, read on the images: 'Johes filius wilmi Ouldam bap. 14 die', July 1592 (image 51); 'Elizabetha filia wilmi Ouldam bap. 24 die', February 1594[/5] (image 60). No marginal notes; father only named. (3) His burial, read on the image (117 of 272), last entry before 'July 1636': 'Sep: William Ouldam buried the six & twentieth day' (26 Jun 1636). No age, wife, occupation or note, so the register itself does not say whether this is Lucretia's father (he was living and married in 1617, when 'Phillip[a] the wife of William Oldham' was buried). (4) A Thomas son of William: no baptism of a Thomas Ouldham/Oldham/Ouldam son of William is indexed at Derby or anywhere in Derbyshire 1586-1602 (FamilySearch, fuzzy surname, father William). The indexed children of William are John 1592, Elizabeth 1594/5, James 1597, Lucretia 1600/1 and Grace 1604. A Thomas born 1589-91 (between the 1588 marriage and John) or 1598-99 would fit, but none is indexed, so Thomas Oldham of Derby (m. 1615/16, a daughter Phillippa bp 1617) is still not shown to be William's son. Result: nothing on the film ties the Plymouth Oldhams to William; the 'kinsmen' chain (Bradford; Pope) now rests on correct dates but is still a name and age match to Thomas's sons, and Thomas is not linked to William by any record. No raise under Audit 11. ¶14a

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Why Probable, not Confirmed, as father of Lucretia (Oldham) Brewster: the baptism, read as a verbatim certificate, names William as Lucretia's father, but nothing in it ties this Derby child to the 'Lucretia Oldam of Darby' who married Jonathan Brewster at Plymouth in 1624 except the rare forename, the surname and the town. That identification is accepted by Anderson and Stratton and is very likely, but no record states it. Sherwood (Pilgrim, a biography of William Brewster) notes that John Oldham's wife was also named Lucretia; this does not affect the baptism but is a reminder that the family used the name more than once. ¶3

Upgrade: graded on his link to his daughter Lucretia Oldham. (FamilySearch signed in, 2 Nov 2026) I read the All Saints, Derby, register image myself: under 'Januarius 1600' it has 'Bap. Lucretia filia Willimi Ouldam bap. 14 die' (FamilySearch DGS image 75 of 772, 'Derby. Baptism Records 1558-1841...', image ark 3:1:S3HY-DTM3-N2X; index record 1:1:KBD6-JQW, England, Derbyshire, Church of England Parish Registers, FHL film 1041144). The identification with the Plymouth bride is secured on two grounds: (1) the Plymouth marriage entry itself calls her 'Lucretia Oldham of Darby' (Mayflower Descendant 1: 8), and the FamilySearch indexes have exactly ONE Lucretia of any surname baptised, married or buried in Derbyshire 1580-1615 (exact-forename search), so there is no rival; (2) the same register has 'Johes Ouldam', son of 'Wilmi Ouldam', baptised 14 Jul 1592 (index 1:1:NGWZ-L5B), and Jonathan Brewster wrote of 'my brother Oldam' in 1636 (Winthrop Papers; Stratton), matching John Oldham of Plymouth. The earlier 'Why Probable' note is superseded. ¶7

Corrections 1

Correction: Audit 11 (2 Nov 2026): lowered from Confirmed to Probable, graded on his link to his daughter Lucretia Oldham. The raise rests on the All Saints, Derby, baptism of 14 Jan 1600/1 ('Lucretia filia Willimi Ouldam', image read). The tree's Lucretia is the bride of Jonathan Brewster at Plymouth on 10 Apr 1624 ('Lucretia Oldham of Darby'). The step from the Derby infant to the Plymouth bride crosses 23 years and the Atlantic, and no record in between names Lucretia. It rests on (1) the place 'of Darby' in the marriage entry, (2) her being the only Lucretia in the FamilySearch Derbyshire indexes 1580-1615, and (3) a brother: Jonathan Brewster's 'my brother Oldam' (1636) against John, son of William Ouldam, baptised 1592. Point 2 is elimination, point 1 ties her to the town but not to a parent, and point 3 needs the Plymouth John Oldham to be identified with the Derby John of 1592, which is itself a name match. Audit 3 gives Probable for 20 years or more with no record in between; audit 4 puts identification across places in the parents' grade; audit 9 rule 2 does not apply, because the father is not named at the far end; and audit 10 lowered a 33-year identification of the same kind (Agnes Johanna Jenisch). The case is strong and stands at the top of Probable. ¶12a

Searches that found nothing 2

Searches made for a record tying the Derby baptism to Lucretia, wife of Jonathan Brewster: (1) the only record placing the bride is Plymouth's marriage entry (Mayflower Descendant 1: 8), '10 April 1624... Jonathan Brewster and Lucretia Oldham of Darby', which names no parent. (2) Hunt's note (NEHGR 111: 242), reread, offers no evidence beyond the Derby certificate and the 1588 marriage. (3) The printed All Saints registers that Hunt cites (probably Cox and Hope, Chronicles of the Collegiate Church or Free Chapel of All Saints, Derby, 1881) are not on archive.org; the Phillimore Derbyshire marriage volumes cover St Werburgh, St Peter and other parishes, not All Saints before 1600. (4) Sherman, Pilgrim: a biography of William Brewster, cites the same baptism and adds only that Lucretia came over with John Oldham. No document read names William as the father of the Plymouth bride, so he stays Probable, graded on his link to Lucretia. ¶4

Origin, negative (FamilySearch indexes, 2 Nov 2026): no baptism of a William Ouldham/Oldham/Ouldam is indexed at Derby (All Saints or the other parishes) 1555-1570, and the All Saints indexes have no Ouldham entry at all before his marriage in 1588. So he probably came to Derby from elsewhere. Derbyshire Oldam families of the period are at Pleasley (John Oldam, children 1556-1560), Heanor (from 1559) and Horsley; the nearest William candidate in the indexes is a William Oldham who married Elizabeth Tockett at Tickhill, Yorkshire, 26 Oct 1567, but nothing links him to Derby. His parents are not identified. ¶11
