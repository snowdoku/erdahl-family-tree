About the family

Wife of Tellev Olsen at Søftestad, Nissedal, age 40 in the 1801 census. Both were in their first marriage. ¶1

Parents (not identified): a possible candidate is Kari, daughter of Torjus Muule (Mule), baptised in Nissedal church on 13 Oct 1759; godparents included Joen Gundersen, Kittil Tostensen, Ragnild Wiig and Torbor Muule. She is the only Kari daughter of a Torjus in the Nissedal-church baptisms read from Feb 1759 to Jun 1763. ¶3a

Father (Speculative): Torjus Muule (Mule), Nissedal. On 13 Oct 1759 in Nissedal church 'Torjus Muules daatter Kari' was baptised (Kviteseid ministerialbok 1754-1773, pp. 53-54). It is the only baptism of a Kari daughter of a Torjus found in the Nissedal-church baptisms read from Feb 1759 to Jun 1763, and in the other churches of the parish from Nov 1759 to Oct 1762. What is missing: nothing ties Kari of Søftestad to the Mule farm. ¶6

Probable brother: Eivind (Evind) Torjusen. He was cautioner at her betrothal in 1788 as 'Evind Torgiusen Tvedt' and godfather to her son Torjus in 1797 as 'Evind Torjusen Tvetene', so Tvedt and Tvetene are taken to be the same farm (Tvetene is the plural form of Tveit/Tvedt; inference). In 1801 an Evind Torjusen, 48 (b. about 1753), husmann with land in his third marriage, lived at Grimstvedt Nedre; that he is the same man is probable but not proved. Other possible siblings in 1801: Gunder Torjusen, 50, husmann at Søftestad; Margit Torjusdatter, 51, and Tone Torjusdatter, 34, at Tvedt. ¶9

Father (Speculative): linked to Torgius (Torjus) Alfsen Tvetene (see his page). For: he had a daughter Kari baptised in Nissedal church on 1 Feb 1767 and another Kari on about 28 Apr 1769; these are the only baptisms of a Kari daughter of any Torjus found in the Kviteseid book from Feb 1767 to Easter 1771. In the Nissedal-church baptisms of Feb 1759 to Jun 1763 the only one found is Torjus Muule's Kari of 13 Oct 1759, and nothing ties her to Søftestad. Her son Torjus's first godfather in 1797 was Evind Torjusen Tvetene, and the only Torjusdatter on Tvetene in 1801, Marte (b. about 1765), may be Torgius's daughter (patronymic and farm only), so Kari's family has ties to Tvetene. ¶11

Father, against and unresolved: (1) Farm. She married in 1788 as 'Kari Torjusd: Tvedt', with Evind Torgiusen Tvedt as surety. Tvedt and Tvetene appear as separate farms in the 1760 baptisms and in the 1801 census. Tvedt had Torjus families of its own: a Torgius Tvedt was a godparent there in 1768; Torjus Kittilsen Tvedt had a daughter Turi in Jul 1769, and a Kittilsen of Tvedt whose forename may be Torjus had a Turi on 26 Dec 1767; and in 1801 Margit (b. about 1750) and Tone (b. about 1767) Torjusdatter lived at Tvedt. Kari's father may therefore be a Torjus of Tvedt. Evind may simply have lived at Tvedt in 1788 and at Tvetene in 1797, so the farm difference weakens but does not rule out the link. This supersedes the sentence in the 'Probable brother' note that takes Tvedt and Tvetene to be the same farm. (2) Age. The 1801 census gives her 40 (b. about 1761); her 1803 burial index gives 35 (b. about 1768). The two Tvetene Karis fit the burial age, the census age fits neither, and no Kari daughter of a Torjus was baptised in 1760-1763 apart from Muule's. The burial index has not been checked against the scan. (3) Which Kari. If this link is right, she is the Kari of 1767 or the Kari of 1769; which one is unknown until the 1767-1769 Nissedal burials are read (one Kari presumably died, since the name was reused). (4) Gap. No one has read the baptisms of Jun 1763 to about Sep 1766 (source 7981, canvases 34-44). For Oct 1766 - Jan 1767 (canvas 45), only the Nissedal-church entries have been read (an overview listing every Nissedal child, in which the only Kari is Torgius Alvsen Tvetene's of 1 Feb 1767); the other churches' entries on canvas 45 are unread, so a rival Kari daughter of a Torjus born then, for example of Torjus Kittilsen Tvedt, is not excluded. ¶12

Father, grade: Speculative, not Probable, because the farm and the census age both point away from Tvetene and the Jun 1763 - Sep 1766 baptisms (and the non-Nissedal entries of Oct 1766 - Jan 1767) are unread. Alf Tvetene, probably Torgius's father, is not added; there is no further Speculative generation. Would raise it: no rival Kari in the Jun 1763 - Jan 1767 baptisms of all churches (c34-c45), and a 1767-1769 burial of one of the two Tvetene Karis; or her confirmation entry (about 1783-1786) or a probate after Torgius Alfsen naming her. ¶13

Father, Speculative (not raised): Torgius (Torjus) Alfsen Tvetene (see his page). She is taken to be his daughter Kari, baptised in Nissedal church about 28 Apr 1769. For: (1) His earlier Kari, baptised 1 Feb 1767, was buried on 24 Jun 1768 aged 1, and the 1769 Kari was not buried in the parish from her baptism to 15 Nov 1770. (2) The baptisms of all churches of the parish from Nov 1759 to Oct 1762 and from Jun 1763 to Apr 1771 hold no other Kari daughter of any Torjus; the three 1764-65 Kari entries that were re-cropped have fathers or a mother not named Torjus. So the 1769 Kari is the only Kari daughter of a Torjus in this book known to be alive after 1768. (3) Her 1803 burial age of 35 gives a birth in about 1768, one year from the 1769 baptism. (4) Ties to Tvetene: her son Torjus's first godfather in 1797 was Evind Torjusen Tvetene, and Marte Torjusdatter of Tvetene Nordre (1801) is now a probable daughter of Torgius (baptism 26 Feb 1764), so probably Kari's sister. ¶14

Father, what stands against it: (1) She married in 1788 as 'Kari Torjusd: Tvedt' with Evind Torgiusen Tvedt as cautioner, and Tvedt is a different farm from Tvetene, with Torjus families of its own (see the 'against and unresolved' note of). (2) Her 1801 census age of 40 (b. about 1761) does not fit a 1769 birth. The only Kari daughter of a Torjus found baptised that fits it is Torjus Muule's Kari of 13 Oct 1759, and baptisms of Oct 1754 - Jan 1759 have not been searched for a Kari. (3) A rival claimant to the 1769 baptism: Kari Torjusdatter, 32 in the 1801 census (b. about 1768-69), wife in her 2nd marriage of Christopher Joensen at Grimstvedt, Kviteseid, with a son Tallak Biørnsen, 13, from her first marriage and a son Torjus, 8. Her age fits the 1769 Tvetene Kari better than this Kari's census age does. (4) If Evind Torjusen, her probable brother, is the Evind Torjusen, 48, of Grimstvedt Nedre in 1801 (b. about 1753), he was born before Torgius married as a bachelor in 1756, which points to a different Torjus, perhaps the father of Margit (b. about 1750) and Tone (b. about 1767) Torjusdatter at Tvedt. (5) The burial age of 35, the only age that fits 1769, is from the index; the scan of p.154b is unread. The grade therefore stays Speculative. ¶15

Father, what remains open, and what would confirm it: (0) The first marriage of Kari Torjusdatter of Grimstvedt (to a Biørn, about 1786-87) and the baptism of her son Tallak Biørnsen (about 1787-88), Kviteseid books of 1773-1799. Her marriage entry should give her farm of origin, and so show whether she is the Tvetene Kari of 1769. (1) Non-Nissedal baptisms of Feb - Nov 1759 and Oct 1762 - Jun 1763 (source 7981, canvases 17-19 and 31-33; read only the Nissedal entries there, read all churches Nov 1759 - Oct 1762). (2) Burials after 15 Nov 1770 (canvas 177 onward and the next book), which could show the 1769 Kari dying as a child. (3) The fate of Torjus Muule's Kari of 1759 (burials 1759-1766 unread). (4) Her confirmation, about 1783-1786, naming her father or Tvetene. (5) A probate (skifte) after Torgius Alfsen Tvetene, Øvre Telemark sorenskriveri, naming a daughter Kari. (6) The scan of her 1803 burial entry, to check the age 35. Items (4) or (5) would confirm the link. ¶20

Brother (Confirmed by her estate): Gunder Torjussen, living on the plass Lien under Søftestad. Her probate calls him 'den Afdødes Broder' (the deceased's brother); he acted for the minor children and signed 'Paa de umyndiges Vegne Gunder Torjussen Lien'. He had no assets to show for the estate. He is very probably the Gunder Torjusen, 50, husmann (cotter) at Søftestad HH02 in the 1801 census (same name and farm; inference), so born about 1751. ¶26

Probable brother (inference): Evind (Eivind) Torjusen. He was cautioner (surety) at her betrothal in 1788 as 'Evind Torgiusen Tvedt' and first godfather to her son Torjus in 1797 as 'Evind Torjusen Tvetene'. Probably the Evind Torjusen, 48 (born about 1753), husmann with land in his third marriage at Grimstvedt Nedre in 1801; a widower 'Evind Torgiusen Ulsbæk[?] fra Nissedal' married Gunild Eriksdatter Oen in 1788, which may be the same man. An 'Even Torjussen' was paid for six days' work in her estate accounts. No record states he was her brother. ¶27

Father, for: (1) The 13 Oct 1759 baptism, reads 'hiemdøbt Torgius Muules daatter Kari', with sponsors 'Targe[?] Nie[l]sen, Joen Gundersen, Kittel Tjøstolfsen, Ragnild Wiig, Torbor Muule' (the first sponsor's name is cut at the page edge and uncertain). The clerk writes the father as Torgius and the first sponsor as Targe/Targie Nielsen in the same entry, so in this hand the father is not Targie Nielsen of Tvetene. (2) It is the only baptism of a Kari daughter of any Torjus found in the parish from Nov 1759 to Apr 1771 in all churches, apart from the two Kari daughters of Torgius Alfsen Tvetene of 1767 and 1769, who are ruled out. Small gaps remain in the non-Nissedal entries (Oct 1762 - Jun 1763; 'what remains open'). (3) Her brothers fit the same family: Gunder (born about 1751) and the probable brother Eivind (born about 1753) are of an age to be older children of Torgius Muule, who also had a son Wetle[?] baptised 17 Nov 1762 (inference). (4) Gunder lived at Mule in 1783. (5) In 1780 she stood godmother together with Gunder at Eivind's child, and in 1785 together with Eivind at Gunder's child, which shows the sibship acted together. ¶37

Father, against and what is missing: (1) Age. She was 17 at her probable confirmation on 13 Dec 1778 and 40 in the 1801 census, both giving about 1761; the 1759 Kari would have been 19 and 41. Her 1803 burial age of 35 (about 1768) points later still, though it fits no candidate. The census difference is within normal error. The confirmation difference of two years is a real conflict, softened only a little because the same 1778 register entered Tellev Olsen (born 24 Mar 1760) as 16 when he was 17. This conflict is why the grade is Speculative and not Probable. (2) Farms. Her bynames are Bakken (1778), Grimstved (1785, probable), Tvedt (1788) and Søftestad (1788-1803); none is Mule. Gunder was a mobile cotter (Borrestad 1778, Mule 1783, Tvetene 1785, probably Midbø 1792 and Lien 1793, Søftestad 1801), so his stay at Mule may say nothing about where he was born. (3) Rival owner of the 1759 baptism: Kari Targiesdatter, 42 in 1801 (born about 1759), wife of Gulloug Ormsen at Tvedt. She is a claimant only if the clerk's 'Torgius Muule' was a Targie. Against that: the 1759 entry itself distinguishes Torgius from Targie, and Kari Targiesdatter named a son Nottov with Torbjørn Targiesen as godparent, which places her in or near the Targiesen sibship of Mule and Tvetene, probably the children of Targie Nielsen; she cannot easily be his, since his son Tallef was baptised in Feb 1759 and her census age gives about 1758-59. Targie Nielsen also cannot be the father of the Oct 1759 Kari, for the same reason. Her own baptism has not been found; her 1778 marriage calls her 'Kari Targiesd: Tvedt', betrothed at Tvedt in Treungen, not Mule. (4) No record names Kari's father. ¶38a

Brother Gunder at Mule (entry): 'Den 3 Maji paa Hvideseöe Prästegaard trolovede Enkemand Günder Torgiüsen Mule og Pigen Güro Nielsd: Grovum. Caut: Ole Tovsen[?] Bræ[kke]...'; margin 'Copulerede d: 11 Jul: i Nissedals Kirke' (on 3 May 1783 at the Kviteseid parsonage the widower Gunder Torjussen of Mule was betrothed to the maiden Guro Nielsdatter of Grovum; married 11 Jul 1783 in Nissedal church). Kviteseid ministerialbok 1773-1786 (source 7982), canvas 138, left page, p. 134. That the groom is her brother Gunder is probable, not proved: he was a widower (his first marriage in 1778 was as Gunder Torgiusen Borrestad), an Ole Bræk(ke) was cautioner at both marriages (patronymic read 'Elsen[?]' in 1778 and 'Tovsen[?]' in 1783), and Eivind and Kari stood godparents to his child in 1785. ¶39

Life story

Birth: ABT 1761, from her confirmation at 17 on 13 Dec 1778 (born Dec 1760 - Dec 1761, computed) and her age of 40 in the 1801 census. Her 1803 burial gives 35 (born about 1768), which conflicts by about seven years; the scan confirms that the priest wrote 35. The census and the probable 1778 confirmation agree on about 1761, so that is preferred. Her baptism has not been found. ¶23

Which Kari (supersedes point (3) of the 'Father, against and unresolved' note): if the link is right, she is the Kari baptised about 28 Apr 1769, not the Kari of 1 Feb 1767, who died in 1768. ¶16

Confirmation (identification probable, from age and name only): on the 3rd Sunday in Advent 1778 (13 Dec 1778, computed) in Treungen church, among the confirmands listed 'Af Nissedal', 'Kari Torjusd. Bakken', aged 17; knowledge marked 'maadelig' (moderate). She is the only Kari Torjusdatter confirmed in 1777-1778. Kviteseid ministerialbok, source 7982, confirmations 1778, canvas 215 right page, p. 241. Her husband Tellev Olsen was confirmed in Nissedal church in the same year as 'Teller Olsen Søftestad', 16. ¶24

Godmother 1785 (entry): on the 12th Sunday after Trinity 1785 (14 Aug 1785, computed) Hr Storm baptised in Nissedal church 'Günder Torgiüsen Tvetenes Datter Ingeborg'; godparents 'Eivind Ulvsbrak, Targie Nielsen Grovum, Torbör Bräkke, Högne Tvetene, Kari Torgiusd:' with the byname 'Grimstved' on the following line (the crop ends at 'Torgiusd:'). Kviteseid ministerialbok 1773-1786 (source 7982), canvas 244, right page. She is very probably this Kari (inference: the same pairing with Eivind and Gunder as in 1780). ¶40

Marriage entry re-read (from the crop of source 7983, canvas 186, left page, last entry): '5 Sönd: n: P[?]: i Nissedal trolovede [U?]: K[?]: og Soldat Tallev Olsen Söftestad og Pigen Kari Torjusd: Tvedt. Caut: Evind Torgiusen Tvedt og Johannes Olsen Söftestad'; margin 'Copulerede 6 Sönd: n: Tr: i Nissedals Kirke'. So she married from Tvedt (written Tvedt, not Tvetene), and the groom was a soldier. The betrothal is on the 5th Sunday after Easter (27 Apr 1788, Easter 1788 being 23 Mar), as already in the tree; reading '5th Sunday in Lent' (9 Mar 1788) does not fit the abbreviation 'n:' (after), which the same clerk uses in the margin for 'after Trinity', and a betrothal in March is not supported. The marriage is the 6th Sunday after Trinity, 29 Jun 1788 (computed). The two cautioners are Evind Torgiusen of Tvedt, her probable brother, and Johannes Olsen of Søftestad, probably the groom's brother (inference from the patronymic and farm). ¶41

Guardian of a son: the Nordre Grimstvedt page of panteregister I 2 (10916, p. 802, view 141 left, right column) notes 'Christopher Joensen er Formynder for Halvor Tallefsen', who had an inheritance from the skifte at Søftestad (matr. 12) closed 3 Nov 1804, i.e. her probate. Christopher Joensen of Grimstvedt was married to the other Kari Torjusdatter (32 in 1801, Grimstvedt H02). Guardians were usually kin, so this may point to a family tie between the two Karis or their husbands, but the register states none (inference only). Her probate itself (begun 3 Sep 1803) would list all guardians with their relationship words (farbror, morbror) and could settle her parentage; it is not in the scan-only protocols 25669-25682 read so far. ¶43

Alternative: in 1788 she married from Tvedt, with Eivind Torjussen of Tvedt as cautioner, and in 1801 the Tvedt farm (Kviteseid) held two Torjusdatters, Margit (51) and Tone (34), probably her sisters. A Torjus family is also seen at a Tved farm in Brunkeberg in 1761 (Ole Torjesen Tved). Kari's father may therefore be a Torjus of Tvedt rather than Torjus Muule. Would decide it: her confirmation entry (about 1774-1776) or a probate after a Torjus naming Kari. ¶7

Marriage: betrothed 27 Apr and married 29 Jun 1788 in Nissedal church to Tallev (Tellev) Olsen Søftestad, as 'Pigen Kari Torjusd: Tvedt'; cautioners Evind Torgiusen (Torjusen) Tvedt and Johannes Olsen Søftestad (Kviteseid ministerialbok 1787-1799). ¶8

Marriage context: she married in 1788 as 'Pigen Kari Torjusd: Tvedt', so she was living at Tvedt then, ten years after her confirmation from Bakken. suggested that 'Telles Olsen Tvedt', 19, confirmed in Nissedal in 1784, was her husband living at Tvedt. That is doubtful: Tellev Olsen was already confirmed in 1778 as 'Teller Olsen Søftestad', and a person was confirmed once, so the 1784 Telles is probably another man. Her own stay at Tvedt (probably in service; inference) is what the 1788 entry records. Tvedt had Torjus families of its own in 1801 (Margit, born about 1750, and Tone, born about 1767, Torjusdatter); they are not shown to be her sisters. ¶29

1801 census, Kviteseid 'Hvidesøe: Baken': the farm Baken in Kviteseid parish holds 14 persons in three households in 1801, and none is a Torjus (Torgius) of any age or a widow of a Torjus; nor is anyone named Torjussen or Torjusdatter. The nearest forms are Ole Torgrimsen, 59, and the widow Torbiør Targiesdatter, b. about 1749, whose father 'Targie' is unidentified. This farm is in Kviteseid, while Kari's 1778 byname 'Bakken' was recorded among the Nissedal confirmands, so the Nissedal Bakken remains unsearched in the census. ¶34

Burial (per index): 'Kari Torjusd.', Hustrue (wife), of Søftestad, aged 35, next of kin Tollev Ols. of Søftestad, interred on the 15th Sunday after Trinity 1803 (18 Sep 1803, computed). Remark: found dead 200 paces from the farm on 19 Aug, when she was going up to the summer pasture (stølen), 'probably murdered in a cruel way'. Kviteseid ministerialbok 1800-1814, burials 1803, p. 154b (index pg00000001618943). The death date is the date she was found. The scan was not read. ¶4

Age: the 1803 burial index age of 35 gives b. about 1768; the 1801 census age of 40 gives b. about 1761. Neither has been checked against a scan of the burial. The birth field stays ABT 1761 from the census. If the Tvetene link is confirmed, the birth should become 'BEF 28 APR 1769, Nissedal'. ¶17

Death and burial (scan read; supersedes the 'Burial (per index)' note): buried on the 15th Sunday after Trinity 1803 (18 Sep 1803, computed) at Nissedal church: 'Tollev Olsen Søftestads Hustrue Kari Torjusdatter gl: 35 Aar, som blev funden død 200 Skridt fra Gaarden, d: 19 Aug: da hun vilde reise paa Stølen, rimeligviis paa en grusom Maade myrdet' (Tollev Olsen Søftestad's wife Kari Torjusdatter, aged 35, who was found dead 200 paces from the farm on 19 Aug, when she was going up to the summer pasture; probably murdered in a cruel way). Kviteseid ministerialbok 1800-1814, source 7984, p. 154b. The entry names no father and no farm of origin. The death date is the day she was found. ¶30

Probate: Øvre Telemark vestfjelske sorenskriveri, skifteprotokoll 19, no. 76 (source 25683, views 171-173, fols. 162b-165a). Inventory and appraisal (registrering) held 3 Sep 1803 at Søftestad by the deputy Christen Dahl; division (skifte) held 5 Nov 1804 by sorenskriver (district judge) Ole Kiell. Deceased 'afdøde Kone Kari Torjusdatter' of Søftestad, Nissedal; widower Tallef (Tallev) Olsen. Net estate 1478 rd 2 ort 17 sk: half to the widower; the sons Ole the elder (13 in 1803), Ole the younger (11), Torjus (7), Halvor (5) and Jacob (3) 134 rd 1 ort 16 2/3 sk each; the daughter Anne Tallefsdatter (age read '8[?]', uncertain) 67 rd 20 1/3 sk, half a son's share. Søftestad came to the widower by deeds of 27 Feb 1786 (read in court 28 Feb 1788) and a contract of 3 Oct 1791; Kari did not inherit it. No parents of hers and no inheritance from them ('arv efter') are named. ¶31

Probate, guardians (several names partly illegible): Ole the elder, [?] Hovland[?] of Tvedene; Ole the younger, Torarin [Torkil?/Niel?]sen of Tvedtene; Torjus, Ole Olsen [Veraase/Fjeraase?]; Halvor, Christopher Jensen/Jonsen Grimstveed; Jacob, Tore[?] Nielsen Mi[?]le; Anne, Asmund Tallefsen (probably Tellev's brother; inference). Correction: father unlinked. Torgius (Torjus) Alfsen Tvetene (see his page) is no longer given as her father. Three records point to a different, older Torjus. (1) She is very probably the 'Kari Torjusd. Bakken', aged 17, confirmed on 13 Dec 1778 (the only Kari Torjusdatter confirmed in 1777-1778, and her age matches the 1801 census age of 40; inference), so born about 1761; Torgius's surviving Kari was baptised about 28 Apr 1769 and would have been 9. (2) In the same service a separate girl, 'Aszier Targiesd. Tvetene', 17, was confirmed under Tvetene, which is where Torgius's children are entered; Kari is entered under a different farm, Bakken.
