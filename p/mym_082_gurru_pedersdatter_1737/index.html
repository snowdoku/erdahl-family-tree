About the family

Stronger, but still Probable. The 1810 local census of Frosta lists 'Guri Pedersdtr', the wife of Greis Hanssen, as 'hans Kone i første Ægteskab' (his wife, in her first marriage), at 'Loksteen Plads Wigeleret eller Haugen', and Greis as 'Mand i første Ægteskab' (pf01051132000133). The 1801 census also gives Greis as in his first marriage. Greis therefore had one wife, Gurru, from their betrothal in 1766 until after 1810. Sivert (bapt. 1768, son of Greis) was confirmed in 1787 as 'Siwert Gregersen Vigleret' (16379 p. 87), so he was Greis's son, born after the 1766 marriage. That makes Gurru his mother unless he was born outside the marriage, and his baptism does not say so. It is still an inference: no record names Sivert's mother. A churching of Greis's wife at Storleinsvallet on the 3rd Sunday after Trinity 1768 (py00000000031705) names only the husband. The record that would settle it is the scan of Sivert's 1768 baptism in 16378, if the priest wrote the mother there and the indexer left her out. ¶5

Parents found at Probable (father) and Speculative (mother): Peder and Anne, tenants at Breiset, Frosta, whose 1762 extra-tax household lists a daughter 'Guri' over 12 (source 48401, view 181, row 40). A 'Gurru Persdatter' of Breiset did open penance for leiermål on the 14th Sunday after Trinity 1763 (ps00000000005836), and in 1780 Gurru and her husband (written 'Gregers') were living at Breiset, where their daughter Anna was baptised on the 6th Sunday after Trinity (pd00000006455895). Her own grade (on her link to Sivert Greisen) is unchanged. ¶10

Life story

Gurru's parents: the 1759 Aspgutt probate (24753 fol. 824a, re-read at full resolution) names the first heir as 'Anne Pedersdatter gift med Arent' and continues on the next line with a word read as 'Ovne' (perhaps a farm name, e.g. Aune; the start of the word is in the gutter). The widow's guardian at the same probate was 'Arent Ovne', i.e. this son-in-law. Malena Arntsdatter (b. c.1744, 'konens søskende barn' in 1801, a widow living with Greis and Guri in 1810) may be a daughter of this Arent and Anne Pedersdatter. If so, Gurru's parent was a sibling of Arent 'Ovne' (Gurru is not an heir of Peder Arentsen, so her parent was not one of his children). This is unproved. ¶6a

1762 extra tax, Frosta tinglag; Stjørdal og Verdal fogderi, ekstraskatt 1762, source 48401, views 180-183, scan read: this list gives first names only. Row 40, Breiset (selveier): 'Peder, Anne'; children over 12: 'Aren, Guri, Kiersti'. Gurru Pedersdatter would have been about 25 in 1762, so this Guri, daughter of a Peder, is a candidate. Unproved: the list has no patronymics, and other Guris may be in service elsewhere in the parish. If Gurru belonged to this family, her brother Arent Pedersen could be the 'Arndt Breiset' who stood godfather in 1771 (pd00000006477235). Row 85, Asput (Aspgutt): 'Aren, Anna', no children over 12, servants 'Mali' and 'Anne'. This is Peder Arentsen's son-in-law Arent and his daughter Anne Pedersdatter, with Anne's unmarried sister Mali (the 'Malie Pedersdatter 78' of 1801), three years after the 1759 probate. Malena Arntsdatter (b. c.1744, about 18 in 1762) is not in their household. The Frosta index has an Arnt of 'Aune' as father of Lars (22nd Sunday after Trinity 1758, pd00000006473452) and Peder (Thanksgiving Day 1763, pd00000006474619), and as godfather at Fastelaven 1768 (pd00000006476086). The son Peder, born 1763, would fit a grandson named after Peder Arentsen (d. 1759), so Arnt Aune is probably 'Arent Ovne' of the 1759 probate. The 1762 list has no farm called Aune, so Aune was probably a cottar's place, perhaps under Aspgutt. The typed registers of Stjør- og Verdal probates (24753-24765) have no probate for an Arnt or Arent of Aune, nor for a Peder of Breiset. ¶8

Married Greis Hansen of Vigleeret, Frosta (betrothed 1766); mother of Jens (bapt. 1776, 'Guru Pedersd.'); aged 64 at Loksteen in the 1801 census. ¶1

Also in the Frosta index, a woman 'Guru' of Viglæret was churched on the 4th Sunday after Epiphany 1767 (py00000000031614), which fits the birth of her son Hans Gregersen (b. c.1766, buried 1784 at Vigleret). ¶11

1801 census, Loksteen, Frosta, household 2: Greis Hansen 58, Gurrue Pedersdatter 64, their daughter Anne Greisdatter 21, and 'Malena Arentsdatter 57, Konens søskende barn' (the wife's sibling's child, or cousin), inderst. In household 3 next door: Arent Joensen 58, wife Jødda Arentsdatter 55, and 'Malie Pedersdatter 78, Konens moster'. A Pedersdatter kin group (Malie b. c.1723, Gurru b. c.1737, and the mother of Malena Arentsdatter) is a lead to Gurru's father Peder; unproved. Negative: the 1773 probate after the widow Ingeborg Pedersdatter, Presthammer under Logstein (24758 fol. 175a) names only her daughters Jodda Arntsdatter 26 and Anne Arntsdatter 22, with no Gurru and no morbror or moster guardians, so it does not prove a link. ¶3a

Frostaboka XI p. 624 (Lokstein 93/2) gives her death as about 1827 (b. c.1737), and Frostaboka VI p. 217 places her in the family of Peder Olsen of Breiset (see Peder Olsen); compiled sources, rebuilt from word boxes. ¶12

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the grade · 2 alternatives set aside · 1 search with no result · 3 open questions

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Probable mother of Sivert Greisen; the 1768 baptism of Sivert names no mother. See the argument on her husband. ¶2

Alternatives considered and set aside 2

Candidate ruled out. Probate 14 Feb 1759 at Aspgutt (Aspgutu), Frosta, after Peder Arentsen; widow Anne Pedersdatter; heirs four daughters: Anne Pedersdatter, married to Arent [surname unclear]; Mali Pedersdatter, unmarried; Marit Pedersdatter, married to Christopher Flegstad; and Ingeborg Pedersdatter, married to Sergeant Arent [Pres]hammeren (Stjør- og Verdal skifteprotokoll 3, 24753 fol. 823b-824a, scan read). This is the family of 'Malie Pedersdatter 78' (b. c.1723) and of the widow Ingeborg Pedersdatter of Presthammer (d. 1773), mother of Jødda Arentsdatter, next door to Gurru in 1801. Gurru (then about 22) is not among the heirs, so she was not a daughter of Peder Arentsen. In the 1801 census Malena Arentsdatter 57 is 'konens søskende barn', i.e. Gurru's first cousin; if Malena is a daughter of Anne Pedersdatter and her husband Arent, Gurru's own parent was a sibling of that Arent (or of Anne's husband's family), not of the Pedersdatter sisters. Also negative: Peder Arntsen of Banlien, Frosta, probate 15 Sep 1753 (24753 fol. 511b): children Rasmus 20, Arnt 2[3], Malena 3[2], Margrethe and Maren; no Gurru. The 77804 card index has no card for Lokstein/Logstein (views 1243-1253 run Lillevik, Linreiten, Lo (Åsen), Lund). ¶4

Breiset candidate, continued: the Frosta index has Peder of Breiset as father of Jens on the 8th Sunday after Trinity 1758 (pd00000006473357). So the Peder and Anne of Breiset in 1762 were still having children in 1758, while their children over 12 (Aren, Guri, Kiersti) were born before 1750. If Guri is Gurru (b. c.1737), Anne bore children over about 21 years, or was a second wife. Gurru's own son Jens (bapt. 1776) shares a name with this half-brother or brother, a weak naming hint only. Still a candidate, not added to the tree. ¶9a

Searches that found nothing 1

Scans read, negative: (1) Sivert's baptism, Almindelig Bededag 1768 (Frosta ministerialbok 1750-78, source 16378, canvas 51, p. 102), reads 'Døbt Sivert Greisen Storleins Wallet', followed by the godparents. No mother is written. In this book the priest gives the child and the father only (e.g. 'Døbt Maren Pevelsdatter Risem' on the same page), so the record that would settle Gurru as Sivert's mother does not exist in this register. She stays Probable on her link to Sivert. (2) The betrothal on the 15th Sunday after Trinity 1766 (16378 canvas 46, p. 91) reads 'Trolovet Greis Hansen Wigleeret med Gurru Peders Dr., f[orlovere] Niels Haugen og Hans Risset'. No home farm is given for the bride. ¶7

Next: a farm 'Ovne'/'Aune' in Frosta c.1740-1760, and Arent's probate. ¶6b

Next: a probate after Peder or Anne of Breiset in the protocols that have no typed register (24754, 24756), and Breiset in the 1762 extra tax cross-checked against the Frosta burials 1760-1800. Also read, negative for Malena: Malena Arntsdatter, 'Pigen', married Søren Arntsen of Fjæren on 17 Jun 1773 (pv00000001561111), and a Malena Arntsdatter of Haneboe was confirmed in 1774 (pk00000001000058). Søren Arntsen's probate, 25 Apr 1783, Reichebosveet (24759 fol. 528a, view 550, read at overview resolution), lists his siblings and their children as heirs (the first, a brother Arnt, with sons Ole and Arnt and a daughter Maren). No widow is visible on that page, so this may not be the 1801 Malena (b. c.1744). Unresolved. ¶9b
