Life story

A stillborn child (Dodfodte) of Ingebrigt Johnsen and Anne Paulsdatter, buried from Selbu church in 1842; Selbu parish register 1824-1842, burials page 408, with both parents named as paaroerande (next of kin). The entry is also the last record that shows the couple alive and together: Anne was about 43. ¶1
