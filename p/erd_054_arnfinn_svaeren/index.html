Life story

Laberg 1985: holder of a bruk (holding) on Sværen by 1686; his daughter Brita married Per Johannessen in 1692, and Per got the holding after Arnfinn, who died in 1714 (printed p. 139). His patronymic and birth were not read; the page's earlier lines name a Sjur and a Kari (widow of Sjur, 1663) and an Ola (1666) as earlier holders, without a readable link to Arnfinn. ¶1

Bygdebok (Laberg, Balestrand 1 canvas 161, Sværen; rebuilt text, dates ABT): in 1686 [the holder of Sværen was] Arnfinn; he had a daughter Brita, married 1692 to Per Johannessen from Vangsnes, who took over after Arnfinn, who died 1714. Before him (1663-1676) the holders were Sjur Markusson's widow Kari Larsdtr. and an Ola; the text does not give Arnfinn's patronymic or origin (gap words), so the identification with the 1701 cottager 'Andfind Nielsen' (b. 1631) is still untested. Grade unchanged. ¶6a

Primary record: the marriage at Tjugum on 25 Jun 1692 (Leikanger 11564 canvas 10 left) of 'Peder Johans[en] Vangnes' and 'Sigri Andfinds d. Sværen' shows an Anfind at Sværen with a marriageable daughter in 1692 (Sigri, possibly a sister of Brita, or a slip for Brita). Grade unchanged. Test record for his patronymic: the 1714-16 Sværen probate (domicile Sv*/Sueren in the Ytre Sogn register) and the 1701 census row of 'Andfind Nielsen' (b. 1631) at Sværen. ¶7

His probate (about 1714) is not on Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll 2b (source 24156) views 122-125: those pages hold May-June 1713 entries only (folio 439 = view 123 right), mostly from the Lavik skipreide circuit. 1714 entries must come after view 125; not yet read. ¶3

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

Probable: a compiled bygdebok read through nb.no word boxes; no primary record read. ¶2a

Searches that found nothing 2

Not found in the 1664 vicar's census for Leikanger hovedsogn (35519 views 146-147, pp. 268-271) or the start of the 1664 bailiff's list for Leikanger Kirke-Sogn (35573 view 118, p. 217), nor in Vangsnes or Tjugum sogn 1664/1666. The farm names on p. 271 are lost in the binding gutter, and the 1666 Leikanger pass (35519 v159-163) and the bailiff's views 119+ are unread, so Sværen may still be there. Read. The Andfind (53 in 1666, holder at Vangsnes) and Andfind (54 in 1666, Indre Esse) are both b. ~1612-13, too old for Andfind Nielsen of Sveren 1701 (b. 1631). ¶4

Search: no Arnfinn/Anfind probate at Sværen in the Ytre Sogn register 1700-1720 (the only Sv* row before 1720 is Elling Larsen, Svaet; the only Anfind 1708-20 is Anfind Ellingsen, Veim). His 1714 probate was not registered. ¶5

Open questions and the next record to read 2

What would confirm it: his probate (Ytre Sogn sorenskriveri, about 1714) or the 1701 census for Sværen (Leikanger parish), which should list him with his sons. ¶2b

Test record: Arnfinn's probate 1714 (Ytre Sogn 2b, 1713-15) naming his daughter Brita and son-in-law Peder Johannessen. ¶6b
