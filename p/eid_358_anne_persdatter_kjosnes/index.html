Life story

Marriage: Trinity Sunday 1730 (4 Jun 1730), 'Copulerede Ole Ols: Anne Peders D:' (Selbu ministerialbok 16310, canvas 121 left). No farm or father is written, so the entry does not name her father (Per) or her home farm (the bygdebok's Flora). The bygdebok's 1728 marriage year is not borne out (16310 c112-121 read). Her parents stay unfound. ¶6

Probate of her husband Ole Olsen, Kjøsnes, 27 Sep 1774 (24758 fol. 255): she is the 'efterlevende Enke Anne Peders Datter'; the heirs include 'Datteren Berithe Ols Datter gift med Peder Andersen Sæter' (see her page). Kept Probable, graded on her link to Berit: the entry calls the heirs Ole's children ('har... til Arvinger efterladt sig'), and I could not read a 'deres' or 'sammenavlede' that makes them hers too. Her birth about 1706 (bygdebok) fits a mother of children born about 1735-1749, but the marriage of Ole Olsen Kjøsnes and Anne Persdatter (before about 1735) is unread, so an earlier wife of Ole cannot be excluded. ¶4a

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Graded Probable on her link to her daughter Berit Olsdatter (see her page). Selbu i fortid og nåtid vol. 7, Kjøsnes (canvas 300): Ola Olsen 'G. 1728 m. Anne Persdt. [fra] Flora, f. 1706, d. 1779'; Berit (b. 1735) falls inside the marriage. 'Persdt.' is an exact-word hit on her line. ¶1

Searches that found nothing 1

Negative: (1) her marriage to Ola Olsen Kjøsnes (bygdebok: 1728) is not in 16310 canvases 112-114 (Easter 1728 to Candlemas 1729); the only 'Persdatter' bride there is 'Ingeborg Persd.', married to an 'Ole Mi[...]' on the 8th Sunday after Trinity 1728 (canvas 113 left), not Anne. (2) The typed-register entry 'Jon Thomasen, Kjøsnes, fol. 229' (24759) is the skifte of 19 Oct 1779 after Jon Thomasen of Kjøsnes (view 248 right) and is not her estate. Her own probate (d. about 1779) is still unfound; she is kept Probable on Berit under audit 8 rule 2. ¶5
