About the family

Siblings baptized in Jevnaker to Hans Hansen and Eli Pedersdatter of Bjørgesæter: Kirsti (1 Dec 1822), Ingeborg/Valborg (27 Mar 1826; the two transcriptions differ), Hans (31 May 1830), Mari (3 May 1833), Peder (Feb 1836) and Eli (16 Sep 1838). Karen Hansdatter of Bjørgesæter (b. 1821) married Ole Larsen in 1848. Valborg Hansdatter of Bjørgesæter (b. 1826) married Johannes Hansen Snellingen in 1849. ¶9

His daughter Marie Pedersdatter (later Mrs. P. K. Reveland) was born 24 Aug 1861 in Perry, Dane Co., Wisconsin, according to her 1876 Winnebago confirmation record. So the family lived on the Dane/Iowa County line in Wisconsin. ¶10

Life story

Norwegian name and farm: the Winnebago Lutheran church book records the 1867 baptism of a son Peder with the father written as 'Peder Hansen Bjørgesætren' and the mother as 'Andrine Eriksd.' (Digitalarkivet pd00000009781755). Bjørgesæter (Bjørgesætra) is farm no. 117a in Lunner, then part of Jevnaker parish, Hadeland, Oppland, about 45 km north of Christiania. ¶6

Birth date RESOLVED from the scan of the Jevnaker main church book 1815-1837, baptisms 1836, page 179, no. 20. The entry reads: born 'Febr 2den', child Peder, parents 'Hans Hansen og Eli Pedersdr. Bjørgesæteren'. The baptism date is 'Do' (ditto) for Feb 21 [1836] at Lunner church, the same as entry no. 17, which reads 'Febr 21' and 'Lunder'. The second transcription's '24 Feb' (klokker's book p. 151) is a misreading or a duplicate. The 1853 emigration entry ('1836, 2/2') agrees. So Peder was born 2 Feb 1836 and baptized 21 Feb 1836. Every US source gives 2 Jan 1834, which keeps the day ('2') but shifts the month and year. That suggests the American date came from memory and was adjusted, perhaps to make him older for land or military purposes. The US date is kept only as an alternative. ¶11

From the 1884 History of Winnebago and Hancock Counties (Mount Valley Twp. chapter). Born near Christiania, Norway, on 2 Jan 1834, and raised on his father's farm. Sailed in 1853 and landed at Quebec after 68 days. Homesteaded in Iowa County, Wisconsin, until 1866. Married Anna Erickson in 1855. In July 1866 he became the first settler of Mount Valley Township (section 13, later section 26) and named the township. Lutheran and a Republican. Served six months in Co. E, 11th Wisconsin, and was a GAR member. ¶1

The same history says he had 12 children, six living in 1884: H. P., Mary (Mrs. P. K. Reveland), Peter Jr., Anna, Amelia and Clara. Amelia's 1934 obituary agrees: 'Mr. and Mrs. Peter Hanson', sisters Anne (Mrs. Gust Nelson) and Clara, and Mrs. Knudt Reveland. ¶2

As a patronymic, 'Hanson' suggests his father was named Hans. In Norway he would have been 'Peder Hansen' with a farm name that is not yet known. Find a Grave gives his birthplace as 'Oslo fylke'. A Digitalarkivet search for a Peder Hansen born 2 Jan 1834 in the Christiania/Akershus parishes found no clear match. Identifying his Norwegian parents is an open lead. ¶3

The 1922 obituary says he came to the United States in 1853 'with his parents', settled in Iowa County, Wisconsin, and married 'Andrina Erickson' in 1855. He enlisted in 1865 in Co. E, 11th Wisconsin. Five of his twelve children were living in 1922: Hans, Peter Jr., Mrs. Gustav Nelson, Mrs. Wm. Nelson and Mrs. M. O. Weber. ¶5

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

PROBABLE Norwegian identification: Jevnaker parish recorded on 15 Apr 1853 that Hans Hansen (57), his wife Eli Pedersdatter (58) and their son Peder of Bjørgesæter were leaving for 'Amerika'. This matches the 1853 emigration 'with his parents' and the farm name used in Winnebago. In the 1845 census for Jevnaker the household at Bjørgesæter was Hans (50), Eli Pedersd. (48), and children Peder (10), Kari (25) and Eli (7). ¶7

Records that disagree 1

CONFLICT on his birth: Norwegian records point to early 1836, not 2 Jan 1834. The 1853 emigration entry gives Peder's birth as '1836, 2/2' (2 Feb 1836?). A Peder, son of Hans Hansen and Eli Pedersdatter of Bjørgesæter, was baptized in Jevnaker on 21 or 24 Feb 1836 (the two transcriptions differ; pd00000038730908 / pd00000038824100). His sister Mari was baptized 3 May 1833, which rules out a sibling born 2 Jan 1834. Every US source (1884 history, 1922 obituary, grave) gives 2 Jan 1834. The ages were probably shifted in America. This conflict is why the Norwegian identification is rated Probable, not Confirmed. ¶8

Corrections 1

Correction to the death date: his obituary (Lake Mills Graphic, 6 Sep 1922, p. 4) says he died 29 Aug 1922, aged 88 years, 7 months and 27 days. That age matches a birth on 2 Jan 1834 exactly. The 30 Aug 1922 notice (p. 8) says he died 'Tuesday morning', and 29 Aug 1922 was a Tuesday. Find a Grave's 20 Aug 1922 is probably a typo. ¶4
