About the family

Parents (Confirmed): the skifte of 22 Jul 1721 at vestre Felle (Øvre Telemark skifteprotokoll no. 3, fol. 456a; card DA 6856 image 244) names 'Tor' (Tore) Trondsen, of age, as the eldest son of the aged widow Gunvor Halvorsdatter and the late Trond Olsen (skifte 7 Jul 1711), and appoints him guardian of his under-age brother Jens. The identification with Tore Trondssen of vestre Felle (d. 1733) rests on the same farm and on the family in his own probate: his 1733 estate (fol. 402b-403a, no. 309) counts Felle land 'for Torbior Trondsdatter' (his sister of the 1721 skifte); the guardian of his younger children in 1733 and the curator of his son Jon in 1743 was Halvor Ormestad, and the 1731 probate of Alph Trondsen of Vestgaarden Felle (fol. 335b) names 'Halvor Trondsen Ormestad, lensmand' as the brother of Alph, so Halvor was Tore's brother too. In 1733 the other guardian (for Trond and Helje) was Jørgen Haugstoel; no kin term is given for either guardian. ¶5

Siblings: Alf (Alph) Trondsen of Vestgaarden Felle, d. before 23 May 1731 (widow Kari Levsdatter; children Trond 11, Biørn 9, Helgie 6 1/2, Jørgen 4, Niels 2); Johannes; Halvor (Ormestad, lensmann); Jens (under age in 1721); Torbiør. ¶6

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 3

Speculative as father of Tron Torsen Felle (see his page): the son 'Trond 18' (b. about 1715) has the right name, patronymic and farm, but Tron's burial in 1757 gives his age as 47 (b. about 1710), and a 1756 churching entry calls him 'Troen Torofsen' (son of a Toruv). ¶2a

Upgrade (Speculative to Probable): the 1757 probate of Tron of Felle names him 'Tron Toresøn Fælle', which settles the patronymic as Toresen (the 1756 'Torofsen' is a slip). A creditor in the same estate is 'Joen Toresøn Fæle', who fits the 'Jon 11' among Tore Trondssen's children at the 1733 Felle probate. The estate also says Tron had undertaken to pay 'sin Moders Gield' (his mother's debt) at a skifte passeret 7 Aug 1743, which fits a widowed mother at Felle. Name, farm, patronymic and a brother named Jon at Felle make the link Probable. It stays below Confirmed because no record names Tron as Tore Trondssen's son, and his ages disagree (18 in 1733, but 47 at his burial in 1757). ¶3a

Upgrade: the 1743 probate of his widow Ingebor Jonsdatter on Felle (skifteprotokoll nr. 6, image 283, 7 Aug 1743) names her children as Trond Toresøn, Jon Toresøn and Helje Toresdatter (with her husband Knud Knudsøn), the same children as at Tore Trondssen's own 1733 probate (Trond 18, Jon 11, Helge 16). The heir Trond Toresøn takes over Felle, and he is the Tron Toresøn Fælle whose 1757 probate cites this 1743 skifte for his title to the farm. Together these two probates tie Tore Trondssen to his son Tron: the 1733 probate gives Tore's children, and the 1743 probate shows that the Trond among them is the man who held Felle until 1757. Read. ¶4
