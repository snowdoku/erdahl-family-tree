About the family

Eldest of the four children entered collectively as 'Deres born' in Jorgen Olssen's household at Slinden in the 1801 census: 'Marit Jorgensdatter, 21, Ugift' (unmarried). Oline Slind's great-grandfather Gunder was her youngest brother. ¶1

Her mother is left blank rather than guessed. The 1801 census records the marriage of the heads of this household as 'Hans 2det haendes 1. aegteskab' - his second marriage, her first - but lists all four children together as theirs without separating the issue of the two marriages. A daughter born about 1780 to a wife born about 1758 is possible but tight, and the likelier reading is that Marit and her brother Ole were children of Jorgen's first, unnamed wife. Recorded as a father-only link for that reason. ¶2

Her mother is now known and it was NOT Ane Gundersdatter. Selbu i fortid og naatid vol. 7, p. 116, gives Jorn Olsen's children by his first wife Ingeborg Kjosnes d.y. (m. 1771, d. 1786) as Marit f. 1773 (d. 1774), 'Marit d.e.' f. 1775, Marit f. 1777 (d. 1778), 'MARIT d.y.' f. 1780 and Ola f. 1783. The Marit Jorgensdatter aged 21 in the 1801 census is Marit d.y., born 1780, and the gaardshistorie says she married Mikkel Jonsen. Her elder sister Marit d.e., born 1775, married first a Torstensen of Uglem and then a Jonsen - and so there were TWO surviving sisters called Marit, of whom only the younger was still at home in 1801. ¶4

Life story

Nothing further has been found for her. She is not in the 1825 census of Slinden, so she married away or died between 1801 and 1825. ¶3

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
