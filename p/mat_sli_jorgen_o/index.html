About the family

The children entered under them are Marit Jorgensdatter, 21; Ole Jorgenssen, 18; Karen Jorgensdatter, 12; and Gunder Jorgenssen, 9 - the last of whom is Oline Slind's great-grandfather. Because the census records this as the husband's second marriage and the wife's first, the two eldest children may be his by an earlier wife; the census writes them all collectively as 'Deres born', their children, and does not distinguish. Gunder, born about 1792, falls comfortably within Ane Gundersdatter's marriage and is treated as her son. ¶2

His mother is named: the 1801 census enters as the last member of his household 'Marit Pedersdatter, 83, Hans moder, Enke efter 1. aegteskab' - HIS MOTHER, widow of her only marriage. She was therefore born about 1718, and because her son's patronymic is Olssen her husband was an OLE who had died before February 1801 and whom she never replaced. That single census line is one generation further back than anything previously held on this line. ¶10

His father's given name is therefore known - Ole - but nothing else about him is. He is not entered as a person here, because a patronymic is not a record. ¶11

HIS FATHER IS NAMED IN PRINT, and he is the Ole the patronymic required. Selbu i fortid og naatid vol. 7, p. 116, gives the holder list of this bruk: '1756-1774 [OLA, f.] 1717, d. 1772. G. 1742 m. Marit..., f. 1720, d. 1808', and among that couple's children 'JOERN, f. 1752,... over bruket' - took over the holding. The next holder line is '1775-1814 JOERN OLSEN, f. 1752, d. 1832.' So the gaardshistorie, reached independently of the census, gives exactly the father the 1801 census's 'Hans moder Marit Pedersdatter' and the patronymic Olssen between them required: OLA (Ole), born 1717, died 1772, who married Marit in 1742. ¶14

His father's probate, Slinde, 17 Oct 1772 (24757 fol. 530): '1. Sønnen Jørgen Olsen', married and living on the farm. ¶22

Life story

He held the bruk for thirty-nine years, 1775 to 1814, and lived another eighteen years after handing it to his son Ola, born 1783, who held it 1815-1845. His grandson Joern (Joergen) Olsen, born 1811, held it 1835-1880. ¶17

His bruk was SLINDAN VESTRE - the heading 'SLINDAN VESTRE GNR...' at the foot of printed page 112 of Selbu i fortid og naatid vol. 7, with the holder list on pages 113-117. This matters because it is NOT the bruk his great-grandson Gunder Olsen Slind held from 1871, which was Slindan nordre, Nedpaa-Garden (pp. 97-101), the gnr 141 bnr 2 of the 1900 and 1910 censuses where Oline Slind was born. The family held two different Slind holdings in succession, and the 1801 census shows ten households on the single enumerated residence 0083 Slinden, so the farm must always be read bruk by bruk and never as one unit. ¶19

In 1785 he was forlover, with Peder Bersvendsen Slinden, at the betrothal of Marit Olsdatter Slinden to Christopher Olsen, who later lived at Slinden; Jorgen was godfather to their son Ole in 1786. That Marit was his sister is an inference from the shared patronymic and farm only. ¶21

Second marriage: betrothed on Misericordias Sunday, 22 April 1787, 'Joergen Olsen Slinden & Anne Gundersd. Slinden', forlovere Tosten (Torsten) Slinden and Siur (Sivert) Haftorsen of the same farm, and married on Whit Monday, 28 May 1787, in Selbu. This is the marriage the 1801 census calls his second and her first. Children of the marriage read in the register before Gunder (baptised 1792): Ingeborg, baptised 30 December 1787 (read at overview only), and Karen, baptised Ascension Day, 21 May 1789 (godparents Peder and Kejser of Slinden, Aula[?] Gulset, Guri Slinden). Karen Jorgensdatter, 12 in 1801, is this child. ¶20

The oldest ancestor reached on the Slind line, and the man who held the farm at the 1801 census. 1801 census of Selbu, rural residence 0083 Slinden, household 3: 'Jorgen Olssen, 49, Husbonde, Hans 2det haendes 1. aegteskab, Bonde og gaardbeboer' - householder, his second marriage and her first, farmer and occupier of the farm. His wife is Ane Gundersdatter, 41. ¶1

Context: 'Bonde og gaardbeboer' in 1801 means a farmer in possession of a farm, without stating whether he owned or leased it. Residence 0083 Slinden in 1801 held three households - his own, that of the farmer Peder Bersvendsen, and that of the husmand (cottager) and 'nyebygger' (new settler) Jon Olssen. Seventy-four people were enumerated at Slinden in Selbu in 1801. ¶3

Context - how this page citation was obtained without reading the page: every volume of the Selbu bygdebok is access-restricted to Norwegian IP addresses on nb.no, so neither the page images nor the OCR text can be downloaded from the United States. The National Library's IIIF Content Search service, however, is open to anyone and returns, for any quoted phrase, the exact page and the pixel box of every occurrence, with the surrounding context blanked. A page's contents can therefore be profiled by asking it a long series of yes/no questions - one phrase search per name, per year, per place - and the printed folio number is itself OCR'd in the bottom margin, which fixes the page number exactly. Nothing below is read off a page image; every item is a retrieved search hit. Confidence is accordingly Probable, never Confirmed. ¶5

Context, flagged as context: 'Bonde og gaardbeboer' in 1801 and 'Gmd.' in 1825 both mean a farmer in possession of a farm without saying whether he owned or leased it, and the Selbu gaardshistorie shows the Slind bruk to have been church and crown land worked on bygsel (a lifetime lease) through this whole period. A LEILENDING - tenant farmer - held his farm on a bygselbrev, a lease deed, which gave him and his widow the farm for life and in practice allowed a son to take a new lease on the same holding, but conveyed no ownership and could not be sold or willed. That is why four generations of this family farmed Slind and none of them owned it until 1921. ¶13

THE BOOK SPELLS HIM 'JOERN', not Joergen. Joern is the Troendelag short form of Joergen, and the same book calls his great-grandson Gunder 'Gunnar'. This is worth recording because a search of any printed Selbu source for 'Joergen Olsen' will miss him: in the bygdebok he is JOERN OLSEN throughout. ¶15

HE MARRIED TWICE AND THE GAARDSHISTORIE DATES BOTH, which settles the question the 1801 census raised and could not answer. '1775-1814 Joern Olsen, f. 1752, d. 1832. G.I 1771 m. INGEBORG [KJOESNES] d.y., f. 1750, d. 1786, soester til Ingeborg Kjoesnes... G.II 1787 m. ANNE [of] OVERVIK, f. 1758, d. 1841.' The 1801 census said only 'Hans 2det haendes 1. aegteskab' and listed all four surviving children together as 'Deres boern'. The book separates them: by the first wife Marit 1773 (d. 1774), Marit d.e. 1775, Marit 1777 (d. 1778), Marit d.y. 1780 and OLA 1783; by the second wife Ingeborg 1787 (d. 1788), Kari 1789 and GUNNAR 1792. That is a printed confirmation of the reading I had taken from the census's own arithmetic - that the two eldest surviving children were by an earlier wife and that Gunder born 1792 was Ane Gundersdatter's. ¶16

Sovlvskatten 1816 - the forced loan in silver levied on every Norwegian householder in 1816 to capitalise the new Norges Bank. On 31 August 1816 'Jorgen Olsen, Gaardmand, Slinden' in Selbu main parish was assessed at 9 spesidaler 0 ort 84 skilling, with the remark 'Felles med sonnen Ole' - jointly with his son Ole. For scale, his neighbour Ole Pedersen, also gaardmand, paid 16-0-20 and Thomas Sivertsen 9-0-84. At the third round, 3 March 1821, 'Jorgen Ols., Gaardmand, Slinden' and his son were assessed at only 0-4-16. This is the only surviving valuation of what the household was worth. ¶9

1825 census household at Slinden, rural residence 0182, household 1, taken 27 November 1825, in full: Jorgen Olsen 75 (gaardmand); Anne Gundersd. 67 (hustru, wife); Ole Jorgensen 43 (gaardmand - his son, by then co-holder); Elen Olsd. 39 (hustru, Ole's wife); Ingebor Olsd. 18, Jorgen Olsen 14, Helge Olsd. 10 and Ole Olsen 4 (Ole's children); Karen Estensd. 21 (tp., tjenestepike, maidservant). The remark in the source gives his lopenummer as 2780. By 1825 his mother Marit Pedersdatter was dead and his son had taken the working headship of the farm while the old man kept the title. ¶12

Death: he died 28 September 1832 and was buried 7 October 1832, aged 82, entered at page 379 serial 47 of the Selbu burial register for 1832 (Selbu local parish 1824-1842). He had held the farm for at least thirty-one years and was still styled gaardmand in the census seven years before he died. ¶8

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Confidence raised from Probable to Confirmed, 12 Oct 2026, and this is why: the man entered in the 1801 census of Selbu as 'Jorgen Olssen, 49, Husbonde, Bonde og gaardbeboer' at rural residence 0083 Slinden, household 3, is found again in the CENSUS OF 1825 FOR SELBU at rural residence 0182 Slinden, household 1, as 'Jorgen Olsen, 75, Gmd.' (gaardmand, farmer) with his wife 'Anne Gundersd., 67', and a third time in the Selbu burial register for 1832, which records the death of a Jorgen Olsen aged 82. Three records twenty-four and thirty-one years apart, all on the same farm, all agreeing on a birth about 1750, and no other Jorgen Olsen of that age in Selbu (the only other, of Haave, was buried in March 1829). The earlier rating rested on the 1801 census alone. ¶6

Records that disagree 1

Birth year: the three primary records disagree by two years. The 1801 census makes him 49 at 1 February 1801 (born 1751-52); the 1825 census makes him 75 at 27 November 1825 (born 1750); his burial entry gives his age as 82 at his death on 28 September 1832 (born 1749-50). Two of the three say 1750, so ABT 1750 is entered here, replacing the ABT 1752 taken from the 1801 census alone. ¶7

Corrections 1

Correction to the chronology I wrote earlier in this patch: the gaardshistorie's '1775-1814' means he took the holding at twenty-three, nine years before the Soelvskatt assessed him and his son Ole jointly in 1816 - by which year the title had in fact already passed. The 1816 remark 'Felles med soennen Ole' is therefore the tail of a handover, not the start of one. ¶18

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Lead, not evidence - where he should be printed: the SLINDAN NORDRE chapter of Selbu i fortid og naatid, bind 7 (1992) begins on printed page 92, and its holder tenures are printed as year-ranges. Page 92 carries the tenure 1751-1773 and the years 1753, 1754, 1755, 1757, 1759, 1762, 1769, 1773, with the tenures 1774-1783, 1774-1805 and 1806-1811; page 91 carries 1748, 1749, 1750, 1760, 1761 and 1762 and the tenures 1701-1712, 1713-1742 and 1743-1750. A Joergen Olssen born about 1752 with a wife Ane Gundersdatter born about 1760 and a son Gunder Joergensen born about 1792 would be printed on exactly those two pages. The word Joergen occurs in this part of volume 7 on pages 101, 106, 115, 116, 117, 118, 131, 133, 134, 136, 151 and 159. Note that the phrase 'Joergen Olsen' has no hit anywhere in volume 7, so the book does not write his patronymic in that form. Ordering volume 7, pages 86-112, puts the whole descent of Slindan nordre from 1701 to 1900 in hand on twenty-seven pages, and is the cheapest route to raising this man above Probable. ¶4
