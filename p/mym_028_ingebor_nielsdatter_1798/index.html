Life story

1801 census: 'Ingebor Nielsdatter', 2, the youngest of 'Deres boern' (their children) of Niels Olssen and Guri Olsdatter at Langlie, Selbu, household 01. ¶1

Confirmation: Selbu, 1813, girl no. 5 - 'Ingebor Nielsd: Langlie, 15 Aar', who had had natural smallpox ('nat: Kopper'), attested by Ole Olsen Langlie and 'Jon Nielsen ibidem' (her brother John, of the same farm). Her age of 15 agrees with 2 in 1801. ¶2

She stood godmother as 'Ingebor Nilsd. Langlie' to her brother Ole's daughter Brynhild in 1815. She is not in the 1825 household at Langlie; later life not searched. ¶3

Probate: 'Ingeborg' is a daughter and heir in the skifte after her father Niels Olsen Langlie, closed 21 June 1816 (skifteprotokoll 10b, fol. 713). ¶4
