About the family

Named as Rangdi's father in her 1813 baptism and her 1835 marriage. In the 1813 baptism he and his wife Ingebor Olsdatter lived at Kaarud. Other children found: Ole (bapt. 26 Jan 1817, at Kaarud under Sata) and Kari (bapt. 11 Jun 1826, at 'Langefmyren', which is probably Kanafedmyren; Kari Salvesdatter, b. 1826 at Kanafelinger, married in 1847). ¶1

Life story

Two other Sølve Knudsens lived in Ål at the same time, so take care. One was b. about 1786 at Kolsrud and married Aagot Sandersdatter in 1817. The other was Solve Knudsen, b. about 1768, a farmer at Helling in 1801 with wife Guri Olsdatter. Our Sølve may be the man buried 21 Feb 1837 aged about 65 (b. about 1772), and possibly the same as the Helling man after a second marriage. Both identifications are speculative. ¶2

Marriage: 'Ungkarl Sølve Knudsen Sandoe og Pigen Ingebor Olefsd: Sata' (the bachelor Sølve Knudsen of Sando and the maiden Ingebor Olefsdatter of Sata) married in Ål on 28 Apr 1807, entry no. 6 of the 1807 marriages. The register gives only status, names, farms and date: no ages, no fathers and no witnesses (*forlovere*). ¶3

Burial: 'Sølve Knudsen, Huusmand, 65 [years], Kanafetmyren under Guldhagen' died 10 Feb and was buried 21 Feb 1837 in Ål. *Huusmand* (*husmann*) means cottager. The remark column gives a circumstance of death (wording partly uncertain; see dossier findings/.md). Kanafetmyren (also written Kanafedmyren) was the birthplace of his daughters Rangdi (1813) and Kari (1826), so the man buried is Rangdi's father. This replaces the earlier note that called the identification speculative. His age at burial gives a birth in about 1771-1772 (inferred). ¶4

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Ruled-out namesakes: (1) Sølve, son of Knud Olsen of Halling, baptised at Torpo 4 Sep 1774 and probably the 'Sølve Knuds: Heling' confirmed in 1787 and the farmer Solve Knudsen at Helling in the 1801 census, married then to Guri Olsdatter. Our Sølve was a bachelor (*ungkarl*) in 1807; a widower would have been entered as *enkemand*. (2) Sølve Knudsen of Kolsrud, b. about 1786, son of Knud Sølvesen, who married Aagot Sandersdatter on 29 Dec 1817, while Ingebor was still bearing Sølve's children in 1826. ¶6

Searches that found nothing 3

Origin: not found. No Sølve was baptised in Ål in 1769 or 1771-1773, and Ål baptisms for 1770 are lost (not in either surviving register), so a birth in Ål about 1771-1772 cannot be checked. No Sølve Knudsen aged about 29 is indexed in the Ål 1801 census; he may have been a servant under a variant spelling or outside the parish. In 1807 he lived at Sando; the only Knud there in 1801 was Knud Paulsen (b. about 1750, *husmann med jord*, cottager with land), a possible but unproven father. ¶5

Searched (lead only): no Sølve/Solve/Salve Knudsen b. 1765-1778 is indexed in Ål outside the Helling farmer (b. 1768). The only other Hallingdal hit is 'Sølve Knudsen', 25, unmarried servant (tjenestefolk) at Garnaas in Nes, 1801 census; he would fit a Sølve who was a bachelor at Sando in 1807 if the 1837 burial age (65) is overstated by about 4 years. Not identified; Nes baptisms 1764-1786 are not name-indexed. The Ål panteregister (14666) pages for Nedre and Øvre Sando (p. 189-190) name no Knud as owner, so his father was not a Sando farmer. ¶9

Searched (negative): the Sando i Ål skiftekort (3917 images 3183-3189: 1719 Siver Arnesen, 1719 Halvor Torstensen, 1723 Torsten Halvorsen, 1726 Lars Larsen, 1739 Bjørn Olsen, 1761 Ambjør Engebretsdatter, 1781 Halvor Tostensen, 1808 Guri Pedersdatter) include no skifte after a Knud of Sando (such as the cottager Knud Paulsen) naming a son Sølve. ¶10
