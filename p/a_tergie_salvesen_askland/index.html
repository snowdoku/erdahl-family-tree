About the family

NEW ANCESTOR. Farmer at Søndre Askland (South Askland) in Gjøvdal, a side valley of Åmli. Father of Torbor Terjesdatter, who married Thore Tellefsen of Tveit in Tovdal. ¶1

His father was evidently a Salve. His own estate record (Østre Råbyggelag skifteprotokoll, 18 June 1743) has not yet been read and should name all his children. ¶3

Son's baptism: 'Dom. Oculi i Giøvedal döbt Tergie Asklands barn Evind' (30 Mar 1727; Åmli register 1711-1740, DA 9920 canvas 44, p. 83). This is the Even Tergiesen aged 16 in the 1743 oppgitt skifte. In that skifte (Skp. 10 fol. 356a, re-read) the younger Birgithe is 20, not 2. ¶7

Life story

Named, with his wife Margithe Evensdatter, on the Østre Råbyggelag probate card for the 9 October 1761 settlement at Tveit: "Konens far: Tergie Salwesen, søndre Askland i Gjøvdal, oppg. sk. 18/6-1743". "Oppgitt skifte" means the estate was formally surrendered to the court rather than divided privately — usually done when there were debts or under-age heirs. His and his wife's estates were handled on the same day, 18 June 1743, which normally means they died close together. ¶2

Married by 16 Mar 1708. At the Søndre Askland skifte after Stian Madsøn (Skp. 3 fol. 46b, read on the scan; card 37), 'Thargier Salvesøn, som haver Enkens Søsterdatter' was appointed guardian for the daughter Thargier Stiansdatter. His wife Margithe was the daughter of the widow's sister Todne Larsdatter. He was an appraiser at the 23 Apr 1716 Askland skifte after Zigne Wraalsdatter (Skp. 5 fol. 25a, preamble: 'Evind Jørgensøn og Tergie Salvesøn Askland'). ¶5

Father search, negative. All the Askland i Gjøvdal probate cards before 1724, from the block start (card 27) to card 63 (1651-1724), were read. None is the estate of a Salve who could be his father. Lead only, not a link: card 49 (Skp. 6 fol. 73b, 22 Sep 1722, the estate of Ole Svenkesøn, Askland) records that the widow Guro Salvesdatter's parents were 'Salve Jørgensøn Askland og Gunild Gjermundsdtr.' A Salve Jørgensen at Askland is the right name and place for Tergie's father, but nothing ties Tergie to him. The 1722 protocol preamble (read on the scan) does not name Tergie. ¶6a

Father, graded Probable on the new father's link to him. The 1664 manntall for Gjøvdal (Råbyggelaget fogderi, s. 80, DA 35567 view 58), read on the scan, lists three users of Askeland søndre (3 huder): Torkild 65 (sons Knud 23 and Asmund 20), Salve 30 and Laurids 40. Salve, aged 30, is the only Salve on Askland, and the household has no son of 12 or more, which fits Tergie being born about 1675. Askland card 49 (Skp. 6 fol. 73b, 1722) names a 'Salve Jørgensøn Askland' and his wife Gunild Gjermundsdatter as the parents of Guro Salvesdatter. Tergie Salvesen held a part of Søndre Askland from before 1708 to 1743. The link rests on patronymic, farm and age only; no record names Salve as Tergie's father, so it is Probable. Gunild Gjermundsdatter is not added as his mother: she is named only as Guro's mother, and Salve may have had more than one wife. ¶8

Corrections 1

Correction: the 18 Jun 1743 Søndre Askland skifte is an oppgitt skifte by the living couple 'Tergej Salvesøn og hans Kone Margithe Evensdatter' (Skifteprotokoll nr. 10 fol. 356a, read on the scan; card 109). He was alive on 18 Jun 1743, not dead before it. Children: Salve (married), Neri 23, Even 16, Torbor (m. Salve Giermundsøn, Tveit in Lille Tovdal), Birgithe the elder (m. Thore Aanonsøn, Birkeland, Bakke), Gunnild (m. Ellef Olsøn, Askland), Birgithe the younger, 2. The record does not name his parents; the card has no note on his father, so 'Salve' still rests on the patronymic only. ¶4
