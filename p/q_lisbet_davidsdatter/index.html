About the family

Wife of Joen Joensen at Storkleven in 1801, aged 55, in her 1st marriage. Her son David (b. ~1781) carries her father's name. ¶1

Parents (Probable): the probate after David Andersen of Frøyum, registered 17 Nov 1744, names his widow Gjertrud Gundersdatter and their only child, a daughter read as 'Lisbet Davids datt.', 10 weeks old, so born about early September 1744 (Stjør- og Verdal skifteprotokoll 3, s. 102b). This fits her description as 'Pigen Lisbet Davidsdatter Frøyum' at her 1765 marriage and her age 81 at burial in 1825; her children David and Gjertrud carry the names of both parents. Probable rather than Confirmed because the given name in the probate is a probable reading on a poor scan and the record does not itself link forward to the 1765 bride. ¶7

Life story

Stjørdalsboka V 1 (1953), Kleven, gives her birth year as 1744 and her marriage to John Johnsen as 1765, and lists Lisbet (1778, later wife of Lars Eriksen of Almlitrøen) among the children born after that marriage. Kept Speculative as Lisbet Johnsdatter's mother because the 1801 census labels the household's two Berets (26 and 23) as 'Mandens børn' - the husband's children - and the 1778 baptism names no mother. Joen Joensen's probate, or her own, would settle it. ¶2

Baptism: Stjørdal 16370 canvas 58, Dom. 16 p. Trinit. (20 Sep) 1744, Værnes: 'Sal. David Frøyums B. Lisbet', godparents Gunder and Ranni Hemre (her mother's father and aunt), Lars Uthelberg[?], Sibor[?] Engen, Berit Frøyum. This confirms the given name in her father's 1744 probate. Her parents' grades are set on their own records. ¶9

Both her parents are now graded Confirmed on their link to her, via the Frøyum deed of 23 Jun 1745 (pantebok A-2 fol. 460b, DA 19751 view 90), which shows her widowed mother as Gunder Jonsen's wife on Frøyum; with the 1762 ekstraskatt household ('Gunder og Hustru', children Lisbeth, Giertrud, Ingborg) and her 1765 marriage as 'Pigen Lisbet Davidsdatter Frøyum', mother and daughter are on Frøyum from 1744 to 1765 without a break. The 'Lisbeth' in 1762 is therefore no longer read as a rival. Her paternal grandparents are Anders Davidsen and Lisbet Pedersdatter of Frøyum (skifte 19 Jun 1741, 24752 fol. 1070), after whom she was named. Her own grade (her link to Lisbet Johnsdatter) is unchanged. ¶11

A 'Lisbeth' is listed among the children over 12 of 'Gunder og Hustru' on Frøyum (No. 120) in the 1762 extra-tax list (48401 view 216), with a 'Giertrud' and an 'Ingborg'. She is probably this Lisbet (b. Sep 1744, so 18 in 1762). But the list gives first names only and no relationship words, so it does not bridge her parents' 1744-1765 gap. See the note on Gjertrud Gundersdatter. ¶10

1801 census scan, Kleven Stor, Stjørdal (source 58452, view page 61): Joen Joensen, 69, is in his 3rd marriage and Lisbet Davidsdatter, 55, in her 1st; two daughters both named Beret (26 and 23, the second clearly written 'Beret Joensd.') are marked as the husband's children, and the couple's joint children begin with David, 20 (born about 1781). Read literally, Lisbet Davidsdatter married Joen about 1779–80, after Lisbet Johnsdatter's baptism of 20 Sep 1778, so Lisbet Johnsdatter's mother may be Joen's second (unnamed) wife rather than Lisbet Davidsdatter. The bygdebok (Stjørdalsboka V 1) has Joen marrying her in 1765 and only two marriages. She stays Speculative as mother; what would settle it: Joen Joensen's marriages 1765–80 in the marriage section of Stjørdal register 16371, or a wife's burial about 1775–79. ¶3

Burial (per index, Stjørdal church book p. 431): buried 3 May 1825, 'Lisbet Davidsdatter', kaarkone (a widow living on a retirement agreement), of Kleven, aged 81, so born about 1743–44 rather than the earlier estimate of about 1746. ¶6

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Confidence raised from Speculative to Probable on the marriage record: betrothed at Værnes 1 or 12 March 1765 and married at Hegre 29 May 1765 as 'Pigen Lisbet Davidsdatter Frøyum' (an unmarried woman of Frøyum) to the widower Jon Jonsen Kleven (Stjørdal ministerialbok 1755-1780, source 16371, p. 345, IIIF canvas 347 right). This is a primary record of the 1765 marriage that Stjørdalsboka gives, and it overturns the reading of the 1801 census that she married Joen only about 1779-80: she was his wife from 1765, and in 1801 she was still in her 1st marriage with him. Unless Joen was widowed and remarried a different Lisbet Davidsdatter (no sign of that), she was his wife when Lisbet Johnsdatter was baptised in 1778 and is probably her mother; the 1801 census label 'Mandens børn' on the two Berets remains a conflict to note, not resolved. Residence before marriage: Frøyum (farm, Stjørdal). ¶4

Upgrade: Stjør- og Verdal skifteprotokoll 19, s. 247b-248 (Digitalarkivet 57508 view 263): skifte at Stjørdalshalsen in early December 1810 (day read as the 3rd, uncertain) in the 'opgivende Boe' of Lisbet Davidsdatter of Kleven - a widow handing her estate over to her heirs in her lifetime. The heirs are listed as the children she had 'med førhen afdøde Mand John Johnsen Kleven' (with her late husband John Johnsen Kleven): 1. David Johnsen Kleven, of age, living on Kleven; 2. Joen Johnsen, of age; 3. Sigrie Joensdatter, dead, who left seven children by her husband Hans Olsen Forsen (Mallie 16, Sigrie 14, Ole 13, Joen 11, Lisbet 9, Svend 7, Ole 1½); 4. Giertrud Joensdatter, married to Joen Hafstad in Verdalen; 5. Berethe Joensdatter, married to Ole Pedersen Moe[?]; 6. Lisbeth Joensdatter, married to the cottager (huusmand) Lars Erichsen Kleven; 7. Mallie Joensdatter, married to Mads Andersen Forbregd[?]. This names her as the mother of Lisbeth Joensdatter, wife of Lars Eriksen, and as widow of John Johnsen Kleven, so she is Confirmed as Lisbet Johnsdatter's mother. It also shows that Berethe Joensdatter (heir no. 5) counted as her child, which weakens the 1801 census label 'Mandens børn' on the two Berets. Her own parentage (David Andersen and Gjertrud Gundersdatter of Frøyum) is still Probable; this record does not touch it. ¶8

Corrections 1

Correction: the earlier note (from the 1801 census) that she 'married Joen about 1779–80, after Lisbet Johnsdatter's baptism' is wrong. The marriage record shows she married him on 29 May 1765, so she was his wife when Lisbet Johnsdatter was baptised on 20 Sep 1778. Still unexplained: the 1801 census calls Joen's marriage his 3rd (this record makes her his 2nd wife, after Ane Larsdatter d. 1764) and labels the daughters Beret 26 and Beret 23 'Mandens børn' (the husband's children). She stays Probable, not Confirmed, as Lisbet Johnsdatter's mother until a record names her as the mother. ¶5
