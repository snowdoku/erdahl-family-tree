Life story

Maid (pige) Gunnild Tellefsdatter of Frostved (also read Fostved; probably the Drangedal farm later written Tostved/Tostvedt) married Jon Torjusen of Jyserø in Drangedal on 3 Jul 1763, after a betrothal on 23 May 1763 (Drangedal ministerialbok 1697-1767, p. 33). ¶1

Probably the mother of Ragnild Johnsdatter (baptised 16 Sep 1764). The baptism does not name the mother, so the link is inferred from the marriage 14 months earlier. The farm suggests she was kin to Anund Tellefsen Frostved (who had a child baptised in 1763) and so to Knud Anundsen Tostved, a godfather of Anne Thorufsdatter in 1795; that is a further inference. ¶2

Bamble 1781 entry: the widow chose as her laugverge (guardian at law) Anund Tellefsen of Tjøstvedt(?) (farm name uncertain), who was also made guardian of the youngest daughter Torberg. His patronymic suggests he was her brother, but the entry gives no kin term, so this is only a lead to her parents. Other guardians: Knud Knudsen Jyserød for Ragnild, Rolf Torgiersen Moldal(?) for Mari, Ole Knudsen Fostvedt for Kari. Appraisers Biørn Opsahl and Halvor Grytting. She was alive on 30 Apr 1781. ¶6

Living at Jyserø in 1787-89: 'Gunnild Tollefsdatter Jyserøe' was godmother to her grandson Thorulf Thorulfsen on 22 Apr 1787 and to John, son of Abraham Knudsen Gulbrands and Mari Johnsdatter, on 26 Sep 1789 (Drangedal ministerialbok 1768-1814, pp. 80, 98). Her standing beside Ragnild's sister Kari Johnsdatter supports, but does not prove, that she is Ragnild's mother. ¶3

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade (Probable to Confirmed, graded on her link to her daughter Ragnild Johnsdatter and her husband Jon Torjusen): Bamble skifteprotokoll nr. 10b, fol. 535a (DA 23754 view 199), read on the scan. On 30 Apr 1781 the court registered the estate at Jyserød in Drangedal after 'afgangne Mand Joen Torgiusen', to be divided between 'hans efterladte Enke Gunnild Tellefsdatter' on one side and 'deres med hverandre i Ægteskab sammenavlede 4 umyndige Børn' on the other: '1. ældste Datter Ragnild Jonsdatter 16 aar, 2. Mari Jonsdatter 13 aar, 3. Kari Jonsdatter 11 aar, og 4. Torberg Jonsdatter 6 aar', all living at home with their mother. The card (DA 7105/1190) agrees and gives a second date, 20 Aug 1783 (the division). Continuity: the couple married in Drangedal in 1763, Ragnild was baptised there 16 Sep 1764 (aged 16 in Apr 1781 fits), and in 1801 Ragnild Johnsdatter (b. 1764) lived at Jyserø with her husband Thoruf Thorufsen and her sister Thorbær (Torberg) Johnsdatter b. 1774 (aged 6 in 1781 fits). Same farm, under 20 years, no rival couple. ¶5
