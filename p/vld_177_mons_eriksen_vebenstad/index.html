About the family

Mother of Anne not added. The 1738 card makes Anne Monsdatter a full sister of Erich, Jens, Mari, Helge, Guri and Toril, so they had one mother, and the half-sister Anna came from the father's other marriage. The bygdebok names Anne Jensdotter as his SECOND wife, so Anne Monsdatter's mother was probably the unnamed first wife. ¶2a

Son Erik (bygdebok only): the Ekkilsøya bygdebok gives Erik Monsen, b. about 1685, d. 1747 (probate 7 Apr 1747), married about 1709 a Gundersdatter (about 1686-1755), brukar at Vebenstad 1705-1747; children Gunder (about 1710), Mons (about 1712, m. 1738 Anne Toresdatter), Knut (about 1714, m. 1741 Ane Nilsdatter Søbstad), Anne (about 1718), Jens (about 1720), Guri (about 1722), Erik (about 1728). This fits the 1749 probate (24643), where the brother Erich Magnusen Webenstad is dead and his sons Gunder, Knud and Erich are named. ¶6

Life story

Probate cards read: Mons Eriksen of Søbstad (Søfstad), Bremsnes, was a widower twice over by 1674, both times of a Synnøve Knutsdotter (Nordmøre probate card index 77795). (1) View 4031, 18 Oct 1669: probate after 'Synnøve Knutsdotter', 'Enkemann: Mons Eriksen', the deceased's children Berit Aslaksdatter Opsal and Guri Aslaksdatter Sunde (from her first marriage), net 200 rdl; sk.pr. 1668-76 fol. 43-45. (2) View 4033, 18 May 1674: probate after 'Synnøve Knutsdotter', 'Enkemann: Mogens Eriksen', 'Barn: Synnøve Mogensdotter, Guro Mogensdotter (twins; Guro died after her mother), Anne Mogensdotter'; land in Dale and Ødegård; sk.pr. I 1668-76 fol. 201b-202. (The two cards probably describe one estate settled in two sittings, or the card-writer repeated the wife's name; either way Mons/Mogens Eriksen had a daughter Anne Mogensdotter before 1674.) The Ekkilsøya bygdebok (vol. 2004, Vebenstad chapter, canvas 234) says Synnøve, daughter of Knut Vebenstad, married 1) Halvor Aslaksen Søbstad and 2) Mons Eriksen Søbstad. ¶3

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

3 on the grade · 1 search with no result · 3 open questions

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 3

Probable father of Anne Monsdatter Stokke (graded on that link). Primary record: the 1749 Stokke probate after the childless Marit Magnusdatter Boxaspen (Nordmøre skifteprotokoll 8c, 24643 fol. 1084a-b, views 240-241) names her brothers and sisters as heirs: Erich Magnusen Webenstad (dead), Helge Magnusdatter, Gurie Magnusdatter, 'Anne Magnusdatter gift med Niels Endresen Stoche', a sister [Sofie?] Mogensdatter and the full sister Anne Mogensdatter. The 1738 probate card after their brother Jens Mogensen of Vebenstad, Bremsnes (77795 view 4367, citing sk.pr. 1736-41 fol. 202) names the same set as children: full brother Erich Mogensen Vebenstad, full sisters Mari (wife of Iver Boxaspen), Helge, Gari (Guri) Fladset, Toril Søbstad and 'Anna Mognsdr. g.m. Niels Endresen Stokke', and a half-sister Anna. That proves Anne was a daughter of a man named Mons (Magnus/Mogens), and the brother's byname points to the farm Vebenstad. The Gjemnes bygdebok (bind 9, canvas 213, words) identifies him: 'Foreldra til Anne var Mogens Erikson Vebenstad, [f.] 1636, d. 1707, gift 2. g. med Anne Jensdotter', and says the deceased sister 'var frå Vebenstad'. The patronymic is proved by the probate; his own identity (Eriksen, Vebenstad, dates) rests on the bygdebok, so Probable. His eldest son was named Erik, as the naming custom expects for the paternal grandfather. ¶1

Why this matters for his link to Anne Monsdatter Stokke: the 1738 probate card after Jens Mogensen of Vebenstad (77795 view 4367) names, besides the full siblings (Erich, Mari, Helge, Guri, Toril and 'Anna Mognsdr. g.m. Niels Endresen Stokke'), a dead HALF-sister Anna whose daughter Anna Andersdatter served with Erich Mogensen Vebenstad. The Ekkilsøya bygdebok (canvas 237, rebuilt text) says that Anne Andersdatter had inherited from 'mor si, Anne Monsdatter Søbstad'. So the half-sister is the Anne Mogensdotter of the 1674 Søbstad card, daughter of Mogens Eriksen and Synnøve Knutsdotter. A half-sister who is also a Mogensdatter shares the FATHER, so the father of the whole sibling set, Anne the wife of Niels Endresen included, was Mons (Mogens) Eriksen of Søbstad and later Vebenstad. Anne Monsdatter Stokke (b. about 1682) and her full siblings came from a later marriage (the Gjemnes bygdebok names his second wife Anne Jensdotter). The 1740 card after Peder Toresen Søbstad (77795 view 4035) names the guardian 'morbroren Erich Mogensen Vebenstad' for the sons of Peder's first wife, i.e. Toril Mogensdatter of the 1738 card, which ties the Vebenstad siblings to Søbstad. ¶4

Grade unchanged, Probable, graded on his link to Anne Monsdatter Stokke. The identification now rests on records (1674 card, 1738 card) rather than on the bygdebok alone, but the bridge is an inference across 64 years (the half-sister of 1738 = the child of 1674, by name and by the bygdebok's statement about Anne Andersdatter's mother), so the audit rules keep it Probable. ¶5a

Searches that found nothing 1

Negative: the Nordmøre skifteprotokoller jump from nr. 4 (1688-1696, Digitalarkivet 24638) to nr. 5 (1714-1720, 24639); there is no surviving protocol for 1696-1714, so the 1707 probate after Mons Eriksen that the Ekkilsøya bygdebok mentions is lost (which is also why the 1668-1746 card index 77795 has no card for it). His link to Anne Monsdatter Stokke must rest on other records, e.g. a Vebenstad deed or tingbok entry of about 1707 naming his heirs (Nordmøre tingbøker c.1705-08), or the 1738 protocol entry sk.pr. 1736-41 fol. 202 (24641) behind the Jens Mogensen card. ¶7

Open questions and the next record to read 3

Lead: card 77795 view 4359, Vebenstad 15 Feb 1694, Peder Baruchsen, widow 'Anne Joensdtr.', guardian Ole Mogensen Hoaset (Nordmøre sk.pr. 1688-97 fol. 218). This widow may be the Anne Jensdotter who became Mons's second wife. No Mons/Mogens probate card for Vebenstad about 1707 exists in 77795 (Vebenstad cards views 4357-4367: 1669, 1694, 1738, 1738). The 1664/66 manntall has an Erich (b. 1614/15) as opsidder at Webenstad (pc00000005050806, pc00000005051745), with no son Mogens at home; he could be Mons's father, but nothing links them. ¶2b

Next: an Averøy (Bremsnes) bygdebok on nb.no for Vebenstad; Kvernes 1664/66 for a 'Mogens Erichsen' elsewhere in the parish. ¶2c

What would settle it: the 1707 probate after Mons Eriksen, which the Ekkilsøya bygdebok (canvas 236) says was held before his son Erik took over a part of Vebenstad on 30 [?] 1707; it has no card under Vebenstad or Søbstad in 77795 (Søbstad cards: views 4031, 4033, 4035 only) and should be in the Nordmøre skifteprotokoll covering 1707 (Digitalarkivet 24637-24640; read its register or folios for 1707). Also the protocol entries sk.pr. 1668-76 fol. 43-45 and 201b-202 (Nordmøre skifteprotokoll nr. 1, DA 24635) to back the two cards. ¶5b
