About the family

Widow of Halvor Torstensen of Sando in Ål at his skifte of 21 Jul 1719 (skifteprotokoll 7, fol. 211b), at which she gave up the estate to the children Torsten Halvorsen and Rangdi Halvorsdatter. Still living on 1 Feb 1723, when the card for the skifte after her son Torsten Halvorsen names 'avdødes mor: Gunild Herbrandsdtr. (lever)'. An Olle Herbrandsen, probably her brother, is named on the same card (reading of his role uncertain). ¶1

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the identification · 1 record that disagrees · 1 alternative set aside · 1 open question

The case for this identification 1

Identification (Probable, inferred; graded on the parents' link to their daughter Rangdi Halvorsdatter; Rangdi's own Confirmed grade, on her link to her son Asle Knudsen, is unaffected): the 1719 skifte at Sando in Ål names 'Rangdi Halvorsdatter' as the daughter and heir of Halvor Torstensen Sandøe and Gunild Herbrandsdatter. Rangdi Halvorsdatter, wife of Knud Aslesen, died at Dybsjorde in Ål in 1742. The link rests on the same name and patronymic in the same parish, plus family ties: her brother Torsten Halvorsen of Sando (d. 1723) left the widow Berte (Bergith) Olsdatter, who remarried Tosten Thordsen of Sando, and Rangdi's son Asle Knudsen of Dybsjorde/Medgaarden married Kari Tostensdatter, Bergith's daughter by that second marriage. No other Rangdi Halvorsdatter of the right age is known in Ål, but no record names her as the same woman. ¶2a

Records that disagree 1

Conflicting patronymic: in the 1781 skifte after her grandson Halvor Tostensen of Sando (skifteprotokoll 20b, fol. 270b) the heirs stated, without documents, that the deceased had inherited 1 løb in Sando 'efter sin Farmoder Gunnild Olsdatter Sandoe'. His paternal grandmother was Gunild Herbrandsdatter (1719 and 1723 skifter, read on the scans), so the 1781 'Olsdatter' is a slip in an oral statement made some 60 years later. The card for this skifte (3917 image 3189) copies it as 'Farmor: Gunnild Olsd.' and adds 'Avdøde var hennes eneste arving' (the deceased was her only heir); the protocol sentence was read only in part, and that phrase may come from the 1723 skiftebrev, in which he was the only heir of his father. If the protocol does call Halvor her ONLY heir, Rangdi of Dybsjorde's children did not share, which would count against identifying her daughter Rangdi with Rangdi Halvorsdatter of Dybsjorde. Not settled; the grade stays Probable. No skifte after Gunild is on the Sando cards (3917 images 3183-3189: 1719, 1719, 1723, 1726, 1739, 1761, 1781, 1808). ¶4

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Caution: the card calls Rangdi 'ug.' (unmarried) in 1719, while her son Ole Knudsen was buried in 1761 aged 44 (b. about 1717) and is called [fuld]myndig in 1742. If the burial age is overstated by a few years (common) and Rangdi married Knud Aslesen about 1719-20, it fits; otherwise the two Rangdis differ. Different farms and a 23-year gap, so Probable at most. ¶2b

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Test record: the 1719 skifte after Halvor Torstensen Sando and the 1723 skifte after Torstein Halvorsen (Hallingdal sorenskriveri; panteregister Ål tinglag DA 14666, farm Sando), and a skifte after Herbrand Amundsen naming a daughter Gunhild. ¶5b
