About the family

Parents (SPECULATIVE): a Juel, son of 'Ole Knudsen Loes[?] og Astri Juelsd.', was baptised in Aurdal in January 1755 (DA 9361 p. 452, canvas 228; mother churched 9 Feb 1755), with godparents Helge Strand[?], Tor Helgesen ibid., Ole Knudsen, Birthe Haavorsdatter and Guri Olsdatter. The date fits Jul's ages (46 in 1801, 55 at burial in 1810). Jul's eldest son Ole (b. about 1780) and eldest daughter Aaste (b. about 1782) carry the names a first son and daughter would take from paternal grandparents Ole and Astri, and he had a son Knud. An Ole Knudsen Mitstrand was a marriage surety in 1748 (DA 9361 c304) and a Knud Olsen Mitstrand in 1775 and 1777. The farm in the baptism reads Loes/Lees rather than Midtstrand, and the baptisms 1753-56 were not read in full, so the identification is possible only. This replaces, as the better candidate, the 1747 marriage of Ole Thorsen and the widow Barbara Knudsdatter of Midtstrand. ¶5

Life story

Burial: 'Juel Olsen, Midstrand, manden', aged 55 (b. about 1755), buried July 1810 in Nord-Aurdal. His grandson Juel Olsen of Midstrand (aged 4) was buried the same month. His parents are unknown, because Aurdal baptisms for 1750-1800 are not transcribed. A Marit Olsdatter of Mitstrand (b. about 1705) was buried in April 1775, but there is no evidence linking her to him. ¶2

Confirmation: 'Juel Olsøn Lo[e]', 15 1/2, at the 1769 confirmation at the Aurdal main church (DA 9362 p. 401, canvas 191) is the only Juel Olsen in the Aurdal lists of 1768-1776 and is probably he; the list gives farm and age only, not parents. This raises his Speculative parents Ole Knudsen and Astri Juelsdatter to Probable (see their notes). His wife Kiersti Uldrichsdatter Fonnes, 15 1/2, was confirmed in 1773 (DA 9362 p. 407, canvas 194). ¶6

1801 census, Aurdal, Midt-Strand: Jull Olsen, 46, husbonde and gaardbruger, in his first marriage, with wife Kirsti Uldriksdatter (42) and children Ole (21), Uldrik (16), Knudt (10), Johanes (8), Jull (4), Aasse (18), Astri (13) and Marit (5). The census states directly that Ole is his son. ¶1

Probate (index card,; read on the image by the orchestrator): 'Mit Strand i Svenes, Nord Aurdal', Valdres skifteprotokoll 1810-1814, fol. 76. Sittings 2 Aug 1810 to 12 Jul 1811. 'Juel Olsen +', with Kjerstie Ulrichsdatter, the widow. 'Barn' (children): 1. Ole 30 aar; 2. Ulrich 26; 3. Knud 20; 4. Johannes 17 1/2; 5. Juel 12; 6. Aaste 28, married to Juel Evensen of Hoverud (Hovrud). Gross 3910-1-8, net 239-.-22. As father of Ole Juelsen (b. c.1780) he is now Confirmed (confidence upgraded from Probable by this card). The card does not name his own parents. ¶3

Alternatives considered and set aside 2

Lead for his parents (not adopted): on 29 Feb 1747 'Ole Thorsøn og Enken Barbara Knudsdaatter Midtstrand' were betrothed at Svenes, married 23 May 1747, surety Thore Sinde Strand (Aurdal ministerialbok 1730-1762, DA 9361, p. 66, canvas 302). Jul, b. about 1755 at Midtstrand, named a son Knud, and Ole Knudsen Midtstrand was a surety in 1748. Ole Thorsen and Barbara Knudsdatter are therefore candidates for Jul's parents, but no record links them; Jul's baptism c.1754-56 in DA 9361 (baptisms about 10 canvases a year, c207 = Jan 1753) would settle it. Separately, Juel Larsen of Midtstrand died childless in 1739 (card DA 39948 no. 9295), leaving his 2 hides to his sisters and to his wife Gunild Monsdatter's siblings, including an Ole Monsen. ¶4

Rival noted: in March 1755 (churched 11 March), at Skrautvål ('Ved Schrutvold'), 'Ole Juelsøn Garlie og Ragnild Knudsd.' had a son 'Juel' baptised (DA 9361 p. 455, canvas 230, 0-based, top of the right page); godparents Helge Ophus[?], Astri Strand and others. This Juel Olsen, born in the same months as the Juel of 'Ole Knudsen Loes og Astri Juelsd.' (January 1755, canvas 228), is a named rival for the identity of Jul Olsen of Midtstrand. The 1769 confirmation 'Juel Olsen Lo[e]' still favours the Lo family, but the Garlie Juel should be traced (burial, confirmation c.1769-71) before any raise. ¶7
