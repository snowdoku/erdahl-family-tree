About the family

Father of Anne Pedersdatter b. 1757 (see her page), Probable; graded on his link to Anne. (1) Primary: Lesja ministerialbok 1732-1776 (9236), p. 433 (canvas 209), Dom. Oculi [13 Mar] 1757 at Verket, 'Peder Eneboes B[arn]: Anna, fød d. 4 Martii', godmother Marit Kiønsletten (scan). (2) Lesja bygdebok (vol. 1, canvas 183, chapter Einbu, word-box rebuild): Peder, son of Peder Hansen of Nordre Eneboe, 'ca. 1727-77', first married to Brit (daughter Brit 1750), 'g. 2. g. 1753 m. Guri... her (6-1), 1730-?', got a deed to half the farm in 1750; children Marit 1754 (Sør-Einbu 13-4), 'Anne, 1757, sjå... her (21-1)' - entry 21-1 being Peder Engebretsen and his wife Anne Pedersd. (1757-?) - and Peder 1761-1822. The bygdebok thus identifies the 1757 baby with the 1783 bride Ane Pedersdatter, married from Kiønsletten (a plass under Einbu). ¶1a

Parents (Probable, bygdebok only): Peder Hansen and Marit of Nordre Eneboe, listed with 'Peder, ca. 1727' among their children (canvas 181/183). ¶2

His parents are now proved by probate (graded on their records): the skifte after Peder Hansen, 8 Feb 1745 (card 39945/2437, fol. 701b), lists son 'Peder 18' (b. about 1727), and the skifte after his brother Bierte Pedersen, 29 Mar 1758 (25158 fol. 380a), names 'Broderen Peder Pedersen Eneboe' beside 'Moderen Marit Biertesdatter' and the sister Anne married to Rolf Slettum. So Kari Rolvsdatter's grandmother Anne and Anne Pedersdatter b. 1757's father were brother and sister. ¶5

Life story

Correction to the earlier reading of 9236 p. 433: the churching of 'Barbara' on that page belongs to 'Peder Eners.' (Peder Enersen/Einarsen), not to Peder Eneboe. Peder Eneboe's wife Guri was churched at Palmarum [3 Apr] 1757 (same page). This removes the contradiction with the bygdebok's second wife Guri (m. 1753). He stays Probable (graded on his link to Anne, Anne Pedersdatter): the 1757 baptism names him as father of Anna, but Anna's identity with the 1783 bride rests on the bygdebok and age across 26 years. ¶3

Burial: 'Peder Peders[en]' of Eneboe, aged 50 (b. about 1727), buried 5 May 1777 at Lesja (Lesja ministerialbok 1777-1819; index pg00000000422585). Name, farm and age fit exactly: 'Peder 18' in his father's 1745 skifte, and the bygdebok's 'ca. 1727' and death about 1777. He is the only Peder Pedersen buried at Eneboe in the index. Replaces the ABT 1777 death; it also dates the search for his skifte (from May 1777 in skifteprotokoll 25159). ¶12

1763 extra-tax manntall for Lesja (45390, view 286), No. 4 Eneboe: 'Peder Pederson, Guri Torgersdr.', a separate household on Eneboe beside Hans Siverson and Hans Pederson. This places him and his second wife at Eneboe between Anna's 1757 baptism and her 1783 marriage (when she lived at Kiønsletten, a plass under Eneboe, with the probable aunt Marit Torgersdatter). Still Probable: Anne is not named in 1763 (under 12). ¶4a

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade (near-miss):, graded on his link to Anne Pedersdatter b. 1757 (see her page). New record: Lesja ministerialbok 1732-1776 (source 9236), confirmation list p. 47 (canvas 25), 19th Sunday after Trinity 1775, 'Confirmation ved Lesøe' (Lesja church): 'Anne Pedersd. Eneboe - 18' (scan read at 2400 px, plain reading). Chain: (1) baptism 4 Mar 1757, 'Peder Eneboes B[arn]: Anna' (9236 c209), with his wife Guri churched at Palmarum 1757 on the same page; (2) 1763 Lesja manntall (45390 view 286), farm No. 4 Eneboe: 'Peder Pederson, Guri Torgersdr.', the only Peder heading a household on Eneboe (the others are Hans Siverson and Hans Pederson); (3) the 1775 confirmation, Anne Pedersdatter of Eneboe, aged 18, which fits a birth in March 1757 exactly; (4) the 1783 marriage, 8 years later: 'Ane Pedersd. Kiønsletten' (9237 c84), engagement indexed with the bride 'of Eneboe' (pv00000001983480). Kiønsletten is a husmann place under Eneboe, entered inside the No. 4 Eneboe entry of 1763. The gaps are 18 years (1757-75) and 8 years (1775-83). The 18-year step meets all three audit-7 conditions: (a) the parents are shown on Eneboe inside the gap (1763); (b) the far-end record ties the child to that farm by patronymic plus farm (Anne Pedersd. Eneboe); (c) no named rival. The only other Anne on Kiønsletten, Anne Syversdatter, died in 1777 (skifte 6 Sep 1777, 25159 image 776) and had a different patronymic; 'Anne Andersd. Søreneboe 15' (confirmed 1774, p. 43) also has a different patronymic. This supersedes the earlier caveats that the identification 'rests on the bygdebok and age across 26 years'. ¶11
