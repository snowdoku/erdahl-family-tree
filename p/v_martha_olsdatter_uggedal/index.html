Life story

Engagement (index): the same Borgund book (Borgund local parish 1739-1761, 1531P) has an engagement on 30 Jan 1742 of 'Ole Rasmussen', Enchemand (widower), of Blindem, and Martha Olsdtr., Pige (maiden), of Uggedal, trulovar Elling Størchersen and Willum Olsen, both of Blindem (pv00000010136224). Her 29 Jul 1742 marriage record (pv00000002823155) reads Alv Rasmuss., Enchemand, Blindem, and Martha Olsd., Pige, Uggedal. The two entries come from the same book but were indexed by different groups (engagements by Slekt og Data, marriages by Sulasoga, per the record page). Same year, same patronymic, same status and farm for both principals: this is almost certainly one couple, engaged in January and married in July 1742, and 'Ole' is most likely an indexing misreading of Alv (Alf/Aalf) in one of the two transcriptions. Neither index shows which given name is right; the scan of the 1742 engagement and marriage would settle it. No change to the tree. ¶4

A maiden (Pige) of Uggedal in Borgund parish when she married the widower Alv Rasmussen of Blindem on 29 July 1742 at Borgund. The marriage transcription gives no bondsmen and no ages. ¶1

She is the probable mother of Alv's later children - the daughters Anne (baptized 10 March 1745) and Martha (25 December 1747), the children of 1751, 1754 and 1757, and the son Rasmus born about 1746 who is the ancestor of the Røssevold and Valderhaug line. That Rasmus named his own daughter 'Anne Martha' in 1776 supports it: Martha was his mother's name. ¶2

Re-proof (overnight 1762 frontier pass), graded on her link to her son Rasmus Alvsen Blindem; grade stays Confirmed, but it now rests on a record that names the relationship. The 1762 extra tax list for Sunnmøre (Rentekammeret, Ekstraskatt 23.09.1762, DA 49637, view 12, p. 11, Borgund sogn, Blindem household 4) reads, in the columns 'Mand og Hustrue' and 'Børn over 12 aar og deres Navne': 'M: Alv Rasmus[sen], H: Marthe Olsd[atter] | 1 S[øn]: Rasmus, 1 D[atter]: Anne'. So in 1762 the son Rasmus (about 16) and the daughter Anne (bapt. 10 Mar 1745) were living as children of Alv Rasmussen and his wife Marthe Olsdatter. Continuity: Rasmus Alvsen of Blindem was engaged in 1775 (13 years later, same farm), and his father's 1778 probate names 'Søn Rasmus Alfsen myndig og boende paa Blindem'; he named his daughter Anne Martha (1776). ¶6a

Confirmed. A record that names this person and this family link has been read.

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: the probate after Marthe Pedersdatter, wife of Ole Knudsen of Uggedal, 1742 (Sunnmøre sorenskriveri Skifteprotokoll 11 = 3A 14, fol. 142a; scan), names as the third heir 'Martha Olsdatter gift med Alf Rasmussen Blindem'. This ties the woman who married the widower Alv Rasmussen of Blindem in 1742 to her mother Marthe Pedersdatter and her father Ole Knudsen of Uggedal, and so explains her farm name. It also settles the engagement-index question in favour of Alv: the husband is 'Alf Rasmussen Blindem', not Ole. She was already called married in this entry although the church marriage was 29 Jul 1742; the engagement of 30 Jan 1742 probably explains the wording. Her siblings were a brother [Knud?] Olsen, dead by 1742, and Peder Olsen, aged 20 (born about 1722). ¶5

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

No rival: the only other Blindem households in 1762 (no. 1 Matthis Iversen, view 11; nos. 2, 3, 5, 6, view 12) have no son Rasmus, and Rasmus's burial age (44 in 1790, so born about 1746) puts his birth inside Alv's 1742 marriage to Martha, not in Alv's first marriage. ¶6b

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Her own parents are not identified. Her patronymic shows her father was named Ole, of Uggedal. ¶3
