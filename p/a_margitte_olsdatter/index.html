About the family

Mother of Thor (bapt. 1760, Tovdal). Betrothed to Tellef Thorsøn Tved in Dec 1755, when she was of Ramse. ¶1

Her parents were searched for and NOT found in this round. The Østre Råbyggelag probate card file for "Ramse i Tovdal" (118 cards, covering 1700 to 1830) was read card by card, and no estate of an Ole at Ramse in the 1730s-1750s names a daughter Margitte. The Ramse heads of household in that period were Torvild Torvildsen, Tellef Gunsteensen, Tallak Olsen, Aslak Olsen, Tergie Aslaksen and Svend Thorsen, none of them an Ole with a recorded daughter of that name. ¶3

Life story

Married 14 Jul 1756 in Tovdal: 'Item cop: Tellef Thorsen Tved og Margitte Olsdatter' (1756 marriage no. 14, canvas 50; same day as no. 13, dated 14 July, Topdal). No farm or parents given. ¶7

Åmli 1711 - Feb 1724 (canvases 2-35) has now been read too (round 6). The only Margitte daughter of an Ole in that period is the illegitimate Margitte baptised 21 Jan 1720 in Tovdal. Her mother was Torbor Olsdatter Hestekleiv and her father the married man Ole Aslaksen Vraalstad (canvas 24). She is very probably the 'Margitte Olsdatter 10 aar og 17 uger' buried in Jun 1730 (canvas 53), so she is not this Margitte. The 1738 Øvre Ramse baptism therefore remains the only candidate in 1711-1740, apart from Feb-Oct 1724 (canvases 36-37, unread). ¶10

Note: her husband was Tellef Thorsen 'den eldre', baptised 26 July 1727, not the Tellef baptised in 1739. The earlier argument that the 1739 groom-age favoured a bride born about 1738 no longer holds. The candidate baptism of 2 March 1738 (daughter of Ole Aslaksen Øvre Ramse) stays Speculative. It is now no more likely than a bride born about 1726-30, which the possible 1806 burial at age 80 would suggest. ¶11

Åmli 1711-1740 Feb-Oct 1724 (canvases 36-37) read: one Margitte daughter of an Ole - 'Ole Troensön Onelands barn Margitte', baptised Festo Joh. Bapt. (24 Jun) 1724 at Tovdal church (p. 68). She is the Margitha Olsdatter of Oland i Gjøvdal (d. 1762, daughter of Ole Tronsen Oland and Signe Johnsdatter) already rejected. The whole book 1711-1740 has now been read: the only remaining candidate is the 1738 Øvre Ramse baptism. ¶12

Possible death (Speculative): Margitthe Olsdtr., widow on poor relief, of Tvet, aged 80, buried 15 Jun 1806 at L. Topdal (Åmli 1796-1816 p.206 #30). Thor's family lived at Tvet. Several women of this name lived in Åmli in 1801 (one aged 70 at Hillestad and one aged 61 at Ytre Ramse), so this is not certain. ¶2

She may simply be the daughter of a cottager or a servant at Ramse whose estate was too small to be registered. The remaining source is the Åmli church book 1741-1775, read page by page for baptisms about 1725-1735, which is scanned but not indexed. ¶5

Source check (13 Oct 2026): a baptism register covering c. 1725-1735 DOES survive — 'Church book from Åmli parish 1711-1740' (Digitalarkivet source 9920), a single chronological list 1711-1740, scanned but not indexed. Not yet read for her. ¶6

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed (upgrade sweep D). Her link to her son Thor Tellefsen (1760-1800) rests on his baptism, which names both parents: Dom. 3 p. Epiph. (27 Jan) 1760, Tovdal, 'Tellef Thorsøn og Margitte Olsdatter Tvedts barn Thor' (Åmli church book 1756-1775, p. 32, no. 12, read from the scan by an earlier wave). That Thor is the Thor Tellefsen Tveit of the tree is proved by the probate after Gunlek Aslaksen of Tveit, 7 Dec 1792 - 30 Jan 1794 (Østre Råbyggelag skifteprotokoll 16b, fol. 515, card 13869), which names 'Tellef Thorsen Tveit +. Hans barn: Thor Tellefsen Tveit, Erich, Aase Tellefsdtr.', and by his burial in 1800 aged 39. Tellef Thorsen Tved married Margitte Olsdatter of Ramse in July 1756 (betrothed at Ramse 21 Dec 1755), so the baptism's 'Margitte Olsdatter' is this wife, named directly as Thor's mother. Her own parentage stays Speculative (graded on Ole Aslaksen's record). ¶13

Alternatives considered and set aside 3

One candidate was examined and rejected: a Margitha Olsdatter whose estate was handled at Oland in Gjøvdal on 10 July 1762, identified on her card as a daughter of Ole Tronsen Oland and Signe Johnsdatter. That woman was of Oland in Gjøvdal, a different valley, and died in 1762, whereas our Margitte was recorded at Ramse in Tovdal in 1755 and bore a child at Tvedt in January 1760. They are not the same person unless further evidence appears. ¶4

Candidate baptism (Speculative): Dom. Invocavit (2 Mar) 1738, Tovdal: 'i Topdal döbt Peter Nielsön Wraalstads barn Margitte, Ole Aslachsön Övre-Ramses barn Margitte, og Ole Knudsön Östenaaes barn Targiar' (Åmli 1711-1740 pp. 144-145, canvas 75; patronymic 'Aslachsön' read with good but not full confidence, farm certain). Every Margit/Margitte baptism Oct 1724 - Dec 1740 was checked (canvases 38-85): this is the only daughter of an Ole, and the only one at Ramse. She would be 18 at the 1756 marriage. ¶9a

Caveat: 1711-1724 not yet read; if she was born c. 1726-30 (as the speculative 1806 burial suggests) she is not in this book at all, since no Margitte of an Ole was baptised 1724-37. Correction to an earlier note: Ole Olsen Mjåland married Marthe Aslaksdatter Øvre Ramse on 3 p. Trin. 1732 (not 1733); their children seen are Ole (Feb 1733) and Christi (Jan 1736), no Margitte. ¶9b

Searches that found nothing 1

Searched: Åmli 1711-1740 read for every Margit/Margitte baptism Oct 1724 - Jul 1733 and Feb - Oct 1735 (canvases 38-58, 60, 63-64; 1733 Jul - 1735 Feb not yet read): NO Margitte daughter of an Ole was baptised in those periods (Margittes seen were daughters of Terchel Knudsen Eppeland, Birg Omundsen Neergaarden, Salve Børulfsen Gjerustad, Tellef Torbjørnsen Simonstad, Gisle Bjørnsen Fiskvatn). A 'Margitte Olsdatter 10 aar og 17 uger' was buried Jun 1730 (Mykland list) — not her. Ramse leads: two Oles of Øvre Ramse married 1730-31 (Ole Aslaksen x Helge Pedersdatter, Tovdal Nov 1730; Ole Tallaksen x Asbor Taraldsdatter, Gjøvdal Jul 1731, later at Harstved), and Ole Olsen Mjåland married Marthe Aslaksdatter of Øvre Ramse in Jun 1733. A daughter of any of these would be born 1731+; the gap Jul 1733 - Feb 1735 and 1736-40 remain to read. ¶8
