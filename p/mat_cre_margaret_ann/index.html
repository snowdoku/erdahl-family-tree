Life story

Born 3 October 1873, baptised 23 July 1874, daughter of James and Mary Jane Creed (PEI baptism index, as 'Cread'). Wife of Cephas Davey, blacksmith, in 1891 and 1901 (Township 59, next to her father); among her children was Bexey J. Davey, born 26 December 1896, named after her aunt. Later married Ernest S. Griffin; died 19 February 1957 at Sturgeon (compiler). ¶1
