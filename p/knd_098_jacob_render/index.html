About the family

Father of Elisabetha Regina Render (see her page), of Bietigheim. ¶1a

Wife and identity: his daughter's burial at Bietigheim on 17 Apr 1821 names her parents 'Jakob, Maria' (7063442). The only Jacob Render couple indexed at Bietigheim with a wife Maria is Conrad Jacob Render, son of Johann Conrad, who married Maria Sophia there on 30 Jan 1731 (groom row 7270554, image 1184899-00394), and as 'Jacob Conrad Rencker' x Maria Sophia baptised Sophia Benedicta on 25 Apr 1739 (7277161). So he is probably Conrad Jacob Render; Probable, as it joins records 1731-1774 by name and wife's first name only (unlabelled rows). Other Render men at Bietigheim (Georg Friedrich x Anna Sabina 1726, 7269557; Johann Conrad, son of Conrad, m. 1774, 7053962) are not Jacobs. His own grade (on Elisabetha Regina) is unchanged. ¶4

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 3

Graded on that link: the bride's own row of her marriage to Johann Melchior Lindenmaier at Bietigheim on 8 Feb 1774 reads 'Elisabetha Regina Renters', relatives 'Jacob, Johann Melchior' (father, then groom; record 907053963, image 1184901-00062). Images not seen; graded on the faithfully transcribed index row under the overnight rule for this collection. Probably the Jacob Render whose daughter Philippina Friderica was buried at Bietigheim on 29 Jul 1754 (record 7058234). Surname Renter/Render (index 'Renters', genitive). ¶1b

Coordinator review: held at Probable rather than Confirmed. Ancestry collection 61023 index rows do not label who is father, mother or spouse; the BRIEF audit 5 rule is that unlabelled index rows give Probable until the scan is read, or a FamilySearch index with labelled parent fields is found (queued S0233). The naming evidence in the note above is otherwise strong, so this person is a priority for an image read. ¶2

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed, graded on his link to his daughter Elisabetha Regina Render (see her page). FamilySearch, 'Deutschland, ausgewählte evangelische Kirchenbücher 1500-1971' (= 'Germany, Lutheran Baptisms, Marriages, and Burials, 1500-1971'), QPGL-8R9S (Bietigheim, Heiraten u Tote 1733-1896, page 1): marriage 8 Feb 1774, groom Johann Melchior Lindenmajer, Wife 'Elisabetha Regina Renters', Father-in-law 'Jacob Renters'. Twin VCYQ-F1W ('Germany, Marriages') reads the bride 'Elisabetha Regina Reuter'. The labelled field names the relationship; the coordinator's audit-5 hold is superseded. His identity as Conrad Jacob Render (m. 1731) is still Probable (see below). Read signed in, 2 Nov 2026; labelled index fields, image not seen (partner-only). ¶5

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Lead (not linked): Conrad Jacob Render, son of Johann Conrad, married Maria Sophia at Bietigheim on 30 Jan 1731 (record 7270554, image 1184899-00394); he could be the Jacob Render of 1754-1774, but no Jacob Render baptism or marriage of about 1740-55 is indexed at Bietigheim, so this is only a candidate. Another daughter: Margaretha 'Renters', daughter of Jacob, married Johannes at Bietigheim on 3 Nov 1772 (bride row 907053939). ¶3
