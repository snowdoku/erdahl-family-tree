About the family

Widow of Oluf Jonsen at Mebust, Selbu, at his skifte on 8 Jun 1691: 'hans efterlefuende Quinde Gunnild Barroesdatter' with 'deris sammenauflede børn' Bård, Joen and Oluf Olsønner and Ingeborr Olsdatter, minors (24749 fol. 14; card 77804 c1309 'Gunnild Barroesdtr'). Barroe/Baaror is the clerk's form of Bård. The eldest son was named Bård after her father. A debt entry on fol. 15 R ('Joen Baarsøn udi fædrene arf 17-2-16') shows a Jon Bårdsen's paternal inheritance held in the estate, perhaps her brother's; untested. ¶1

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Graded Probable on her link to Ingeborg Olsdatter Haarstad (see her page), for the same reasons as her husband Oluf (Ole) Jonsen Mebust: the 1691 skifte names her daughter Ingeborg Olsdatter, but the identification with Erik Haarstad's widow of 1742 rests on the 1742 guardians from Mebust and Kolset and the 1760 Mebust burial, across 51 years. ¶2a
