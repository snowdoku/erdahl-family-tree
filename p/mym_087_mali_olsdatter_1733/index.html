About the family

Wife (first marriage for both) of Peder Pedersen, kaarmand at Valstad Lille, Skatval, aged 68 in the 1801 census. ¶1

Parents: her father's probate. On 21 Mar 1754 an arveskifte was held at the dragoon quarter Arnstad, Skatval annex of Stjørdal, after Ole Larsen Arnstad, between his widow Kari Pedersdatter and 'deres sammen avlede 6 børn' (their six children together): 1. Hans Olsen, farmer on the farm Brenset[?], 2. Johan Olsen, present, 3. [name at the page edge, not read], 4. Anne Olsdatter, wife of [?], 5. Guru Olsdatter, married to Ole Olsen Wold, 6. Mali Olsdatter, under age; her sister's husband Ole Olsen Wold attended for her (Stjør- og Verdal skifteprotokoll 3, 24753 fol. 522a, scan; the right edge of the page is bent and some words, including the children's ages, were not legible). She is identified with the Mali Olsdatter 'of Arnstad' who married 19 months later; age (b. c.1733) fits an unmarried daughter in 1754. ¶7

Parentage flag (does not affect her Confirmed link to her husband). A second Mali Olsdatter of Arnstad was baptised at Skatval in spring 1727, daughter of Ole Pedersen Arnstad and Kirsti (Stjørdal 16368 canvas 160). Her ages (68 in 1801, 70 at burial in 1803) fit Ole Larsen's daughter (b. c.1733) better, but until the 1727 child is accounted for, her parents Ole Larsen and Kari Pedersdatter are Probable, not Confirmed. ¶9

Parentage. The rival Mali Olsdatter of Arnstad (bapt. spring 1727, daughter of Oluf Pedersen Arnstad and Kirsti) was buried at Skatval on 14 Sep 1727 as an infant ('Ole Pederss. Arnstads b. Mali', 16368 canvas 164). Her baptism of 2 Jun 1733 ('Oluf Arnstads barn Mali, Moderen Kari', 16369 canvas 41) and the 1754 probate of Ole Larsen Arnstad therefore identify her parents as Ole Larsen and Kari Pedersdatter; their link to her is raised to Confirmed. Her own grade (link to her son Ole Pedersen Walstad) is unchanged. ¶11

Life story

Death: 'Mali Olsd.', Lille-Walstad, aged 70 (b. 1733), buried 24 Mar 1803 (index) - age and farm match the kaarmand's wife of 1801. ¶4

Probable baptism, Skatval, 2 Jun 1733 (3rd day of Pentecost): 'Item Oluf Arnstads barn Mali, Moderen Kari', godparents Jens[?] Skatvold, Ane and Thore Skatvold, Marit and Siri Myhr (Stjørdal 16369 canvas 41, p. 564). Matches her ages in 1801 (68) and at burial in 1803 (70) exactly, and Ole Larsen Arnstad and Kari Pedersdatter's 1754 probate names a daughter Mali Olsdatter. The other Mali Olsdatter of Arnstad (b. 1727, mother Kirsti) would have been 73-76; she is not yet accounted for. ¶10

Betrothal: as 'Pigen Malli Olsdatter Arnstad' (the unmarried Mali Olsdatter of the farm Arnstad, Skatval) she was betrothed at Skatval on 14 Oct 1755 to the bachelor and dragoon quartermaster Peder Pedersen Walstad; sureties Jon and Christopher Store Walstad (Stjørdal 16370, betrothals 1755, scan). ¶5

In the 1765 manntall- og legdsrulle of the Nordre Stjørdal dragoon company (source 51337, image 49, quarter no. 34 'Arnstad', Skatval), the holders are 'Peder Olsen', 28, dragoon, and 'Johan Olsen', 33 (status word read as 'Død'? or 'Dr.'). Two Olsen men holding Arnstad in 1765 fit sons of Ole Larsen of Arnstad (d. before 1753), and so possible brothers of Mali Olsdatter Arnstad. This is a lead only: the roll names no parent. ¶12

Bygdebok (Skatval vol. 1, canvas 237 left): 'Peder Pedersen Valstad, [f.] 1724, [d.] etter 1801, skjøte 17.04.1753. [G.] 1755 [m.] Mali Olsdtr., døpt 26.05.1733, [d.] etter 1801, 40/2' - her baptism date 26 May 1733 and a pointer to 40/2 (her farm of origin, probably Arnstad) for her parents. Child listed: 'Mali, døpt 29.08.1756, [g.m.] Ola Johnsen'. ¶13a

Ole's baptism (legitimate) names only the father, Peder of Lille Walstad. In 1801 both Peder and Mali Olsdatter are recorded in their first marriage, so - if that is accurate - she was Peder's only wife and therefore Ole's mother. Kept at Probable because the baptism does not name her. ¶3

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

3 on the grade · 2 open questions

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 3

Probable mother of Ole Pedersen Walstad; see the argument on her husband. Ole's second daughter was named Mali. ¶2

Upgrade: Probable -> Confirmed as mother of Ole Pedersen Walstad (b. Jan 1758). The 1755 betrothal shows Peder Pedersen Walstad marrying Mali Olsdatter two years before Ole's baptism to 'Peder Lille Walstad' (8 Jan 1758), and in 1801 both Peder and Mali are recorded in their first marriage, still together at Valstad Lille. So she was Peder's only wife throughout Ole's birth year. ¶6

Audit 2 (1 Nov 2026): downgraded from Confirmed to Probable. Her grade covers her link to Ole Pedersen Walstad (b. Jan 1758), and no record read names her as his mother: Ole's baptism of 8 Jan 1758 names only the father, 'Peder Lille Walstad'. The upgrade rests on her 1755 betrothal to Peder Pedersen Walstad and on the 1801 census calling both spouses 'in first marriage', from which it follows that she was Peder's only wife in 1758. That is an argument by elimination, which stays Probable however strong it is (her own earlier note said exactly this). ¶8a
