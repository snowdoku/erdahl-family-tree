About the family

Her parents (Probable): the probate on 2 Jun 1701 'paa Tønning' in Lavik skipreide after 'Sal. Jone Johansen Tønning og hans hustru Sal. Britte Olsdaatter' names 'deris efterleffuende tilsammen avlede børn Nafnlig Lasse, Johannes, Olle, Sigri, [Kari?], Agaatte og Giertrud, Jons sønner og døttre, alle vagsne fuldmyndige', of whom 3 sons and 2 daughters were married and 2 daughters unmarried. An Agatha Jonsdatter of Tynning in 1701 and Agatha Jonsdatter, widow on Tynning in 1731, are on the same farm but 30 years apart with no record yet between them, so the identification is Probable (patronymic plus residence). Her brothers Olle Joensen (24) and Lasse Joensen (38) were bunder on Tøniom in 1701. ¶2a

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Graded on her link to Jens Jørgensen. Widow of Jørgen Jensen of Tønning (Tynning) in his probate of 3 Sep 1731 (A 3b fol. 486a): 'hans efterladte Hustrue Agatha Jonsdatter... og hendes med den Salig mand avlede Børn, Nafnlig Jens, Lisabeth, Britha og Barbra' (audit 8 ruling 2: 'hendes med ham avlede' names her as the mother). Continuity to Jens Jørgensen of Tynning (d. 1772) as in his note; his daughter was named Agaathe. ¶1
