Life story

Doctor Benjamin Cross. By Anne Eveleigh, second daughter of Dean John Eveleigh, he had three sons, John, Robert and William (all died without issue), and three daughters: Tryphena (died unmarried), Mary (wife of William Collis) and Elizabeth (died 22 Mar 1732 aged 63, wife of Captain John Blennerhassett). Source: the Blennerhassett Pedigree, 'The Pedigree of my wife Elizabeth Cross and her connexions' (Hickson, Old Kerry Records, 1872, p. 55). ¶1

Birth estimated: if he is the Benjamin Crosse of Hart Hall, Oxford (B.A. 1653-4), he was born about 1633; if only the Emmanuel man (admitted 1657), about 1637-40. Added. ¶5

Career: Benjamin Crosse 'of Chester' was admitted pensioner at Emmanuel College, Cambridge, 24 Jun 1657, B.A. 1661 (Venn). Admitted Precentor of Cloyne 16 Jun 1664 on the resignation of John Eveleigh; Prebendary of Holy Trinity (Christ Church), Cork, 1664-83 (Brady, vol. 2, p. 308). About 1683 he became Rector of Spettisbury, Dorset, succeeding William Zouch, and he died there in 1684: his will, 'Benjamin Crosse, Rector of Spetisbury, Dorset', was proved in the Prerogative Court of Canterbury on 2 Dec 1684 (TNA PROB 11/378/255). His inventory showed over £1,200 owed to him in bills, bonds and debts in London and Ireland (McCall, Church and People in Interregnum Britain, p. 95). Brady says he inherited from Dean Eveleigh the lands of Blackhall and Coplands-meade near Oxford, which is why Blennerhassett calls him 'Doctor Benjamin Cross of Blackhall in Oxford'. Added. ¶3

Gray's Inn: Foster's printed Register of Admissions to Gray's Inn has, under July 1657, '13. Benjamin Crosse, fourth son of Thomas C., of Chester, gent., deceased'. With Venn's Emmanuel admission of 24 Jun 1657 'of Chester', this makes Thomas Crosse his probable father (graded on that person). Grade here unchanged: Probable on his link to Mary Cross. Possible rival for the Gray's Inn identity: Benjamin Cross, clerk, of Rostherne, Cheshire, will proved at Chester 1660-80. ¶6

His will read on the image: TNA PROB 11/378/255, 'I Benjamin Crosse Rector of Spettisbury in the County of Dorset', dated 27 Aug 1684, codicil 7 Sep 1684, proved 2 Dec 1684. Children named: 'my eldest sonne John Crosse' (Craford farm, Spetisbury), sons Robert and William, 'my eldest daughter Tryphenah Crosse', 'my second Daughter Mary Crosse' (500 pounds), 'my youngest Daughter Elizabeth Crosse' (500 pounds); 'my deare wife Anne Crosse' and 'my beloved wife Anne Crosse my whole and sole Executrix'; the Dean of Ross is called the grandfather of his son John. Trustees: Dr Arthur Pomeroy, Dean of Cork; Richard Power of Carrigaline, gent.; Randall Hull of Cork, merchant; and others in Dorset. Bequests to the poor of Spetisbury and of St Giles in Oxford. This matches the Blennerhassett Pedigree exactly (three sons John, Robert, William; daughters Tryphena, Mary, Elizabeth in that order; wife Anne, daughter of the Dean). ¶8

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 3

Probable, graded on his link to his daughter Mary Cross: the pedigree was written by his own son-in-law, but it is a family manuscript, not an official record. ¶2a

Grade unchanged (Probable, on his link to Mary Cross). The Cork and Ross marriage licence bond of 1687 ('William Collis and Mary Crosse') shows a Mary Crosse marrying William Collis three years after Benjamin's death, but it does not name her father. ¶4a

Grade unchanged (Probable, graded on his link to his daughter Mary Cross),. The will names 'my second Daughter Mary Crosse', unmarried in 1684. The tree's Mary is the wife of William Collis, whose marriage licence bond is Cork and Ross 1687 ('William Collis and Mary Crosse'). The step from the Dorset will to the Cork marriage three years later is a name match across places (audit 4); the only statement joining them is the Blennerhassett Pedigree. The Cork trustees in the will show the family kept its Cork ties, and no rival Mary Crosse is known, so this is strong Probable. ¶9a

Searches that found nothing 3

Baptism search: no Benjamin Crosse/Cross baptism is indexed in Cheshire 1625-1645 on FamilySearch (England Births and Christenings; Cheshire Parish Registers; Cheshire BTs). A Thomas Crosse, styled 'Mr' in 1633, had children baptised at St Peter and St Oswald, Chester, 1627-1640 (see Thomas Crosse); the gaps (1628-30, 1634-39) are where Benjamin's baptism should be looked for. Grade unchanged. Added (FamilySearch, signed in, 2 Nov 2026). ¶7

Baptism search continued: the Chester St Peter bishop's transcripts 1629-1639 were read page by page on FamilySearch (DGS 4008469 item 2, images 21-30). They add two sons of Thomas Crosse, John (9 Apr 1630) and Jonathan (15 Dec 1633), but no Benjamin. 1636 is missing from the St Peter BTs, and three names of Jan 1638/9 are torn away. See Thomas Crosse. Grade unchanged. ¶10

Baptism search continued: the St Oswald, Chester, register was read on the image for May 1628-Mar 1636 with no Benjamin Crosse; its baptisms for Apr 1636-Feb 1639/40 are lost (the book was turned and the first leaves at the other end are missing). A 'Benjamin the sonne of Tho: Heatsley', bapt. 28 Feb 1635/6, is a different family. Grade unchanged. See Thomas Crosse. ¶11
