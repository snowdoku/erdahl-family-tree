Life story

THE EARLIEST AMERICAN-BORN PERSON IN THIS WHOLE SURNAME GROUP, and she pushes the Slind presence in Lac qui Parle County back two decades. Her Social Security application (NUMIDENT SS-5, SSN XXX-XX-9342, cycle 1936, filed as HILDA O SLIND) gives date of birth 16 April 1879, PLACE OF BIRTH DAWSON, LAC QUI PARLE [County], MINNESOTA, FATHER: GUNDER P GORBERG, MOTHER: JULIA KJOSNESS. ¶1

GORBERG is GARBERG and KJOSNESS is KJOSNES - both Selbu farm names, and both are surnames in the Selbu Cemetery churchyard in Whitman County, Washington (four Garbergs, six Kjosneses). So Selbu people from the Garberg and Kjosnes farms were already settled at Dawson, Lac qui Parle County by 1879 - nine years before Peder P. Eidem married Kari Slind and twenty-two years before Oline arrived. ¶2

Three Minnesota birth cards in CLAY County 1916-1920 name a mother whose maiden name was SLIND and a father named GARBERG (Mildrid Barine 1916, Bernhard Palmer 1918, Hilma Tilborg 1920), so the Garberg and Slind families intermarried in both directions. ¶4

Recorded under her maiden name because her husband is unknown. The next record is the Minnesota or South Dakota marriage return, or the Recaboth Lutheran burial register. ¶5
