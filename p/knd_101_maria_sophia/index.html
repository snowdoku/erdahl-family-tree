How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the grade · 2 on the identification · 2 open questions

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Graded on that link: Elisabetha Regina's burial at Bietigheim on 17 Apr 1821, 'Elisabetha Regina Reitter', relatives 'Jakob, Maria, Melchior' (record 7063442, image 1184901-01166), names her mother Maria. Maria Sophia is the wife of the only Jacob Render couple at Bietigheim: Conrad Jacob Render married Maria Sophia there on 30 Jan 1731 (7270554) and they baptised Sophia Benedicta on 25 Apr 1739 (7277161). Probable: the burial gives only 'Maria', the rows are unlabelled, and Elisabetha Regina's own baptism (about 1740-52) is not indexed. Ancestry 61023 index rows do not label roles (BRIEF audit 5 rule 3), so this rests at Probable at most until the image or a labelled FamilySearch twin is read; images not seen. ¶1b

The case for this identification 2

Surname and parents: the bride row of her marriage to Conrad Jacob Render at Bietigheim on 30 Jan 1731 reads 'Maria Sophia Haussmanns', relatives 'Johann Albrecht, Conrad Jacob' (her father, then the groom; record 907270554, image 1184899-00394). She was baptised at Bietigheim on 20 Feb 1711: 'Maria Sophia Haussmann', relatives 'Johann Albrecht, Maria Agnes' (record 7268715, image 1184899-00280). She is the only Maria Sophia Haussmann in the Bietigheim index, so the two rows join. Her own grade (on Elisabetha Regina Render) is unchanged. ¶2

Labelled evidence, grade held at Probable: her marriage (QPGG-23PM, Bietigheim 30 Jan 1731: groom Conrad Jacob Render, Father 'Johann Conrad Renders'; Wife 'Maria Sophia Haussmanns'; Father-in-law 'Johann Albrecht Haussmanns') and baptism (V4LM-GQK, 20 Feb 1711) are now labelled. Her link to Elisabetha Regina still rests on identifying the 1774 father 'Jacob Renters' (QPGL-8R9S) with Conrad Jacob Render, and on the unlabelled 1821 burial row ('Jakob, Maria'); no labelled baptism of Elisabetha Regina (Bietigheim 1738-54) or labelled 1821 burial was found on FamilySearch. ¶3a
