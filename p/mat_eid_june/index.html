Life story

Her Social Security application names her father as HENRY E TOSTENSON and her mother as ANNA E OLSON, and gives her birthplace as Nicollet County, Minnesota. SSN [number withheld]. Wife of the veteran Ingvald Eidem and buried in the same grave. Died Christmas Day 1990. ¶1

Burial, and a federal statement of her marriage: EIDEM, JUNE E I, b. 4 Jun 1920, d. 25 Dec 1990, FORT SNELLING NATIONAL CEMETERY, SECTION Y, SITE 1484, recorded as 'WIFE OF EIDEM, INGVALD', himself T5 US Army, World War II. US Department of Veterans Affairs, Nationwide Gravesite Locator. Husband and wife share one grave. ¶3

Social Security death record: JUNE EIDEM, b. 4 Jun 1920, d. 25 Dec 1990. NARA AAD, NUMIDENT Death Files (Last Names E-G), rid 313898. ¶4

Social Security application (SS-5), filed January 1958 under her married name: 'JUNE ELITOSTENSON EIDEM' (the file has run her middle name and maiden surname together), born 4 Jun 1920 in NICOLLET COUNTY, MINNESOTA, former name 'JUNE EI TOSTENSON'. FATHER: HENRY E TOSTENSON. MOTHER: ANNA E OLSON. NARA AAD, NUMIDENT Application (SS-5) Files 1936-2007 (Last Names E-G), rid 421699. This names both of her parents and gives her birth county, neither of which the tree had. ¶2
