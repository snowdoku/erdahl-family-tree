Life story

Baptism index: born 27 Aug 1861 (Old Style) at Friedenstal, child of Ludwig Knodel and Wilhelmine Tarnowsky/Farowsky (St Petersburg Consistory extract, FHL film 1884084/2 p.408 no.43). A probable elder sibling of Gottlieb Knodel (b. 1879); probable because Gottlieb's own identification with this family is probable. Later life unknown. ¶1

Baptism, the register image (read Oct 2026, S0154): born 27 August and baptised 30 August 1861 (Old Style) at Friedensthal, legitimate daughter of 'Ludwig Knodel, Ausländer' (a foreign national) and his wife Wilhelmine née [T/F]arowsky, both Evangelical Lutheran. Godparents: Friedrich H?eiser (surname uncertain), Jakobine Litz and Maria Knodel, colonists. The baptism was confirmed by Pastor Bienemann at Friedensthal, so it was probably first performed at home. St Petersburg Consistory duplicate, FHL 1884084/2 (DGS 4280505, image 400), stamped p.408, entry 43. ¶5

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Confidence raised to Confirmed (Oct 2026): the Consistory birth index (FHL 1884084/2 p.408 no.43) names both parents, Ludwig Knodel and Wilhelmina Farrowski. The earlier Probable level rested only on her brother Gottlieb's identity, which is now Confirmed. ¶3

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Possible later record (lead only): Katharina, the illegitimate daughter of a 'Wilhelmine Baumann', was born at Friedenstal on 25 July 1885 and died there on 28 August 1885. The death extract names the mother as Wilhelmine Knodel. Wilhelmina, born 1861, is the likeliest Wilhelmine Knodel of the right age at Friedenstal, but her mother, the elder Wilhelmine Knodel, cannot be excluded. Not proved. ¶2

Open questions and the next record to read 2

Lead, Katharina Baumann 1885 (unproved): Katharina, recorded at birth as the illegitimate daughter of Wilhelmine Baumann, was born at Friedenstal on 25 Jul 1885 (FHL 1897694/2 p.340 no.40) and died there on 28 Aug 1885 aged 1 month 2 days, when the mother is entered as Wilhelmine Knodel (p.376 no.24). ranks this Wilhelmina (then 23) as the best fit on surname and age, against her mother (about 46) and a Wilhelmina Baumann in the Knodel household entry of the Friedenstal family book 1881-1900 (FHL 1767875 item 2 p.63 no.281). No Baumann marriage of a Wilhelmine was found in 1879-85. ¶4a

What would settle it: the two 1885 entries on film (queued S0086) and the family-book page. ¶4b
