About the family

Her sister's probate names her and her siblings. Skifte 9 Oct 1765 'paa Gaarden Hielden paa Frosten' after Mallie Jensdatter, who left no children; heirs her widower Hans Jonsen and her siblings: '1. En heel broder Gunder Jensen', dead, leaving two daughters Mette Gundersdatter 4 and Ingebor Gundersdatter 3/4; '2. En halvbroder Hr. Leut. [Benjamin] Gundersen'; '3. En heel Søster Anne Jens Datter gift med Lars Olsen Jellan', who attended with her husband (Stjør- og Verdal skifteprotokoll 5, 24755 fol. 495a, scan read). So Anne's father was a Jens; Mali and Gunder Jensen were her full siblings and Lieutenant Benjamin Gundersen her half-brother. The half-brother's patronymic suggests their shared parent was the mother, first married to a Gunder (Benjamin, b. c.1705-12, had daughters aged 40 and 36 in 1779) and then to Jens (Anne b. c.1716; the son Gunder Jensen was named for her first husband). This is an inference; the mother is not named. ¶3

Parents added at Probable: Jens Andersen (b. c.1678, d. c.1746) and Ingeborg Pedersdatter of gn. 65, Frosta, from Frostaboka VIII p. 516. The probate after her full sister Mali Jensdatter, wife of Hans Jonsen of Hellan, 9 Oct 1765 (24755 fol. 495a, scan read) names 'En kiød Søster Anne Jens Dater' married to Lars Olsen Hellan. Frostaboka VIII p. 429 says Anne was first married to Ole Olsen, bruker of Hellan from 1723 (whose first wife Berit Jonsdatter died 1744), and married his successor Lars Olsen in 1751. ¶7

Life story

Gunder Jensen's own probate, 15 Mar 1765 at the dragoon quarter Qvarme (Kvarme), Frosta (24755 fol. 461b, scan read): widow Mette Andersdatter, daughters Mette Gundersdatter 3 and Ingebor Gundersdatter; guardian for Mette 'hendes farbroder Hr. Leutenant Beniamen Gundersen'. Benjamin Gundersen, dimitted lieutenant, died at the lagrette farm Walberg (Valberg), Frosta, before 13 Oct 1779 (24759 fol. 212b): widow Cecilia Rig[?], children Karen (m. sergeant Jon Johansen[?]), Martha 40, Mette 36. In 1775 he was lauverge for his half-sister the widow Anne Jensdatter at Lars Olsen's probate (24758 fol. 369b). ¶4

The Stjør- og Verdal probate card index 1689-1742 (77804) has only two Frosta Hjellan cards (views 763, 765): 'Hjellan, vestre', 20 Jan 1733, Arnt Jensen, widow Berit Pedersdatter, children Arnt 22, Ole 12, Marit 28, Marit 23, Gunild 18 (skifteprot. 2, fol. 484), and 3 Jul 1736, Berit Pedersdatter, widow of Arn Jensen (fol. 678b). Neither names a Jens, a Gunder, Anne or Benjamin; no card is filed under Hille/Hilde (views 749-759 run Hernes-Hindrem). So no pre-1742 probate after Anne's father Jens or her mother's first husband Gunder is indexed under Hillan. Also read: 24755 fol. 217b, 22 Aug 1761, Vestre Hilde: probate after Guri Gundersdatter, wife of Arnt Arntsen; the only heir is their son Gunder Arntsen, so it says nothing of Benjamin Gundersen's family. Also negative: 24755 fol. 458b-459a, 11 Mar 1765, Juberg: probate after Kirstie Gundersdatter, wife of Arn Otersen; her heirs are her own children Otter Arnsen, Karie (m. Ole Erichsen Risan), Kirstie (m. Arn Larsen Staxet) and Anne 33, so no siblings are named and a link to Benjamin Gundersen cannot be tested from it. ¶6

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Probable mother of Ole Larsen (b. 1756). She married Lars of Hillan in 1751 (Frosta, index pv00000001560077). Ole's 1756 baptism names only his father, Lars of Hillan, so her motherhood is inferred from that marriage five years earlier. Probably the Anne Jensdatter of Hille buried 13 Mar 1776 aged 60 (b. c.1716); that burial names no husband, and the identification rests on name, farm (Hille = Hillan) and age. ¶1

Upgrade: the 1775 probate after her husband Lars Olsen of Øver Hilde (Øvre Hillan), Frosta, names the widow Anne Jensdatter and their only son, Ole Larsen, 19 (Stjør- og Verdal skifteprotokoll 7, fol. 369b-370a, 23 Aug 1775, scan). This confirms her as Ole's mother and as the Anne Jensdatter who married Lars of Hillan in 1751. Her burial at Hille before 13 Mar 1776 (index) fits a widow who died within a year of the skifte. ¶2

Searches that found nothing 1

Negative: Jens Vegalsen (Wigalsen) of Kvarme, the obvious Jens on Gunder Jensen's farm, is not her father. His probate of 14 Sep 1773 (24758 fol. 158a) names only his widow Anne Amundsdatter and their young children (among them Anne Jensdatter, 9), with no Gunder Jensen heirs; his previous wife Marit Larsdatter's probate of 8 Jul 1748 (24753 fol. 145b) lists only her children by an earlier husband Ole. ¶5
