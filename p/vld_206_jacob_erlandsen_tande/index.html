About the family

Father of Hans Jacobsen of Stor-Tande (see his page), Probable; graded on his link to Hans. The skifte after him at Tande on 16 Apr 1660 (skifteprotokoll 1658-1673, fol. 41a; card 39945/14047, read; the protocol page itself not read) names his widow Jøran Steenersdatter and one child, '1. Hans Jacobsen umyndig'. Hans Jacobsen held Stortande from about 1664/1670 until his death in 1698 and named his eldest son Jacob and a daughter Giøran (Jøran), which fits. ¶1a

Life story

Jacob Erlandsen paa Thande i Lessøe Prestegield, Imellem hans efterlatte Hustru Jøran Steenersdatter, og deris Barn, Hans Jacobsen, umyndig'; Hans is the only child. Grade unchanged (38-year gap to the 1698 skifte). ¶3

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the grade · 1 open question

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Probable, not Confirmed: 38 years lie between the 1660 card and Hans's own 1698 skifte, with no record read in between (the bygdebok says he is listed as user in 1664), and the 1660 card writes 'Tande' while the later cards write 'Stortande'. The Lesja bygdebok (vol. 3 canvases 97-99, Stor-Tande entries 4-4 and 4-5) makes Hans (ca. 1645) the son of the Jakob of entry 4-4 and notes a skifte after Jakob in 1660. ¶1b

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Test record: the 1664-66 manntall for Lesja (Hans Jacobsen on Tande, with age) and the protocol entry at fol. 41a of skifteprotokoll 1658-1673 (opening and heirs). ¶2
