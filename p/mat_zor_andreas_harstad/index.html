Life story

ANOTHER SELBU IMMIGRANT IN THE SAME CONGREGATION. Find a Grave gives his birthplace as 'Selbu kommune, Sør-Trøndelag fylke, Norway', 30 November 1857. Harstad is a Selbu farm name, and his household is a third Selbu family — after the Eidems and the Slinds — in this one township. ¶1

1900 United States census, AUGUSTA TOWNSHIP, LAC QUI PARLE COUNTY, MINNESOTA, enumeration district 0117, sheet 7, dwelling and family 97, transcribed in full on his Find a Grave memorial: Andreas Harstad, head, 41, b. January 1859 Norway, immigrated 1882, 18 years in the US, NATURALIZED, FARMER, OWNS HIS FARM FREE OF MORTGAGE, can read, write and speak English, married 20 years (so married 1880), both his parents born in Norway. Wife Marie, 40. Children: John 20, Anton 16, Mikkel 15, Bernhard 12, Ingeborg 10, Nils 8, Gunder 6, Emma 3 and a second Emma aged 6/12. And living with them, 'G Brynhild Garberg, 76, Grand Mother' — GARBERG is another Selbu farm name, and a woman of 76 in 1900 was born about 1824, so a grandmother brought out of Norway with the family. ¶2

Death: Minnesota death index 1908-2017, 'Andreas Harstad', 5 June 1938, Lac qui Parle, Minnesota, certificate number 007024, record number 784866. ¶4

Burial: Zoar Lutheran Cemetery, lot 24, with his wife Marit — South Dakota state burial register, 'ANDREW HARSTAD 1938, lot 24' and 'MARIT HARSTAD 1939, lot 24'. ¶5

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 record that disagrees

Records that disagree 1

The census's stated birth of January 1859 conflicts with the Find a Grave date of 30 November 1857; a census age is weaker evidence and the indexed date is kept. ¶3
