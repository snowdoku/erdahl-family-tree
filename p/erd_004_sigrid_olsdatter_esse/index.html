Life story

Probate: eldest daughter of Ole Paulsen of Ytre Esse in his probate, opened 1 April 1799 as written (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll A 12, fol. 548a). She was aged 16 or 18 (the second digit is uncertain), and her guardian was Biørn Johnsen Gierde. She was not in the family's 1801 household at Esse. ¶1

Probate of her paternal grandmother, 1803: 'Sønnedatter Sigrid Olsdatter 22 Aar gammel, ugift' (granddaughter, 22, unmarried; so born about 1780-81), serving on the farm Distad and not present; her guardian was Ole Hermundsen, occupier of Distad (husband of her father's half-sister Mari Larsdatter). Her share was called 'Farmoderarv' (paternal grandmother's inheritance) and paid partly in land rent in Berge (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll A 12, fol. 161b-163a, No. 9). ¶2
