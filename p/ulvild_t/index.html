About the family

Her parents were searched for again and NOT found. The Østre Råbyggelag probate cards for Vraalstad in Tovdal were read — she and Tellef Torsen were both "of Wrolstad" at their marriage in July 1816 — and no estate of a Torjus at Vraalstad names a daughter Ulvild. The Vraalstad families of that period are Peder Nielsen Vraalstad and Todne Giermundsdatter; their daughter Marthe Pedersdatter and her husband Børge Olsen (estate 1781-82); and Toruf Pedersen Vraalstad (estate 7 July 1773) and Gunild Hansdatter. ¶5

Brothers traced, no change: Gunsteen Torjussen (presumed brother, of øvre Ramse 1826) moved to Øyestad and was buried there 1 Jun 1873 aged '96', born Åmli; Ole Torjussen (presumed brother, m. Signe Tellefsdatter, ytre Ramse) has no burial in Tovdal 1830-1869. Neither record names Ulvild. Her own death (19 May 1853) is not in Nedenes death register no. 1, which does not cover Åmli. See Torjus Ormsen. ¶14

Life story

Probable baptism: 'Ulvil', a girl from Tvet, was born 25 Nov 1782 and baptised on the 3rd Sunday in Advent (15 Dec) 1782 in Åmli parish, the daughter of Torjus Ormsen and Ingebor Olsdatter. Her godparents were Aslak [Øvre Ramse], Reiel(?) Ytre Ramse, Ole Jørgensen Tvet, Kari Hillestad and Gunild Gunlefeld (Åmli ministerialbok 1775-1796, image 35, read in full). The identification rests on the rare forename, the patronymic, the farm Tvet (where the 1801 census lists Ulvild Torgiusdatter, 17, as a servant) and the Ramse and Hillestad godparents. The 1801 age of 17 implies about 1784, so the census is one year off. No other Ulvild with a father named Torjus was baptised in Jan 1782–Dec 1784 (images 29–47, read page by page); 1785–1786 not yet read. ¶8

Re-proof attempt, no change to her parents' grade. The Åmli confirmation lists in the ministerialbok 1796-1816 (DA 9924; e.g. IIIF canvas 44, 1807) give only birthplace farm ('Fødested'), name and age, with no parents, so her confirmation (about 1797-1800, earlier canvases of the same chronological book, before canvas 44) can at most show her birthplace as Tvet. That would join the 1782 Tvet baptism to the 1801 Tvet servant but not to the 1816 bride 'of Vraalstad', who sits on another farm 15 years later. A record naming her father is still needed: Torjus Ormsen's estate (died Oct 1798, Øvre Skjeggedal), perhaps filed under Tvet or Østenaa in the Østre Råbyggelag protocols, or the 1816 marriage scan (sureties). ¶11

Possible 1801 census entry (speculative): Ulvild Torgiusdatter, aged 17 (b. ~1784), servant at Tvet, Åmli. Her age matches. Birgitte Tergiesdatter, Tellef's mother, was living at Tvet when she died in 1812. ¶2

The surname Ulvild is rare, and an "Ulvild Evensdatter" appears in 1776 at Hushovd in Mykland — the Oland cards note a 1776 settlement there "etter enken, Ulvild Evensdtr., søstersønn Even Tellefsen, hvor der er masse slektsoppl." (after the widow Ulvild Evensdatter, her sister's son Even Tellefsen, where there is a great deal of genealogical information). That record is explicitly flagged by the archivist as unusually full and is the best untried lead for the name in this district, though it is a different woman from ours. ¶6

Marriage re-read on the scan: 7 Jul 1816, Topdal, 'Ungk. Tellef Torsen Wroolstad, 20 aar' and 'Pige Ulvild Torjusd. Vrolstad, 32 aar'; forlovere (sureties) Børuf Olsen Vrolstad and Tolf Gundersen Hillestad (Åmli kirkebok 1816-1829, p. 225, no. 2). No father is named, so the marriage does not settle her parentage. ¶13

Probable burial: Ulvild Torjusdatter died 19 May and was buried 28 May 1853 in the Åmli church book 1852-1869, which includes Tovdal. No age is indexed, but the name is rare. ¶1

Burial re-read on the scan: 'Ulvild Torjusdat.', inderstekone, of Øvre Ramse, died 19 May, buried 28 May 1853, aged 72 (the second digit is a little unclear; 70 is possible) (Åmli kirkebok 1852-1869, burials 1853 no. 11, fol. 396). That points to a birth about 1781-83, which fits the Ulvil baptised 15 Dec 1782 (born 25 Nov 1782) better than the about 1784 implied by her marriage age. ¶12

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: graded on her link to her son Torjus Tellefsen Ramse (and her husband Tellef Torsen Ramse). His baptism, Åmli 11 May 1817 (Åmli ministerialbok 1816-1829, p. 73 no. 2; Digitalarkivet index pd00000036078640 for), names the parents as 'Tellef Torsen' and 'Ulvild Torjusd', residence Østenaae. Continuity: Tellef Torsen and Ulvild Torjusdatter, both of Vraalstad, had married at Åmli on 7 Jul 1816, ten months earlier; the same couple then appear at Øvre Ramse with the baptisms of Thor (1820), Ingeborg (1823) and Birgitte (1826), and Torjus's 1838 marriage gives Øvre Ramse as his home. No second couple Tellef Torsen and Ulvild Torjusdatter appears in Åmli. This grade covers only her link to Torjus and Tellef. Her own link upward to Torjus Ormsen and Ingebor Olsdatter (the 1782 Tvet baptism of 'Ulvil') is graded on their records and is unchanged. ¶10

Searches that found nothing 3

Her baptism (~1784) and parents were not found: Åmli baptisms before 1796 are not indexed. ¶3

Further search: no 1784 baptism for Ulvild Torjusdatter was found in a skim of the 1783-1784 Åmli baptisms (scanned book 1775-1796, images 40-44). A full page-by-page reading of 1782-1786 is still needed. Her parents remain unknown. ¶4

Probate search (negative): Østre Råbyggelag skiftekort views 12484-12491 (39942), which follow the 1796 card for Tallach Ormsen of Øvre Skjeggedal (12483), hold only Nedre Skjeggedal estates of 1801 (Tellef Knudsen, Skp. 19a fol. 263b and Skp. 17 fol. 182) and 1807 (Nere Aslaksen and Anne Terjesdatter, Skp. 19b fol. 543). No card for an estate after her father Torgius Ormsen (died Oct 1798, Øvre Skjeggedal) or her mother Ingebor Olsdatter sits in that run, so the probate route to proving her 1782 baptism looks closed unless the estate was filed under Tvet or Østenaa. ¶9
