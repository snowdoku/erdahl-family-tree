Life story

Bygdebok (Svenes vol., gnr. 65 Strand, canvas 310): 'Jul Tordsson' (son of Tord, b. about 1604, d. 1689, and an Arnesdotter of Tveit), brukar; married first Marit Knutsdotter (d. 1685), with a son Tord b. 1678, and secondly a [forename unread] Larsdotter (from Tveit, about 1652-1704); children listed after the second marriage: 1 [unread] about 1681 (m. 1702 Knut Andrisson, gnr. 44), 2 Lars about 1684 (d. aged 12), '3 Marit about 1688, [g. m.] Eivind Jonsson, 76'. A 'd 1690' stands on his line. The Hovrud chapter (canvas 643) has Eivind Jonsson marry 1715 'Marit Julsdotter' of gnr. 65, about 1690-1728. ¶1

Vicar's census 1664 for Aurdal (DA 35504, p. 8, view 13): on Sør-Strand, 'Juel Tordß.' is listed in the sons column under the holder 'Tord Erlandß. 60 aar', with a brother 'Olle Tordß. 1[2?] aar'. Juel's age is covered by an ink blot. This supports the bygdebok's statement that Jul was the son of Tord (b. about 1604) of Strand, and adds Tord's patronymic (Erlandsen). The bailiff's list of 12 May 1664 (DA 35563, p. 52) agrees: 'Tord Sørstrand' with sons 'Ju[e]l og Ole Thorsønner'. Neither changes Jul's own grade, which is on his link to Marit Juulsdatter (see her page) and stays Probable. ¶5

The bailiff's census list b) of 12 Nov 1664 (DA 35563, p. 210, view 113) gives 'Juel Tordsen = 21 aar' in the sons column under 'Thore Erlandsen 60' on Sørstrand, so this Jul was born about 1643. Enrichment only; his grade (link to Marit Juulsdatter, b. about 1688) is unchanged. ¶6

Context: a 'Juul Strand' was a godfather at Svenes on 12 Oct 1732, at the baptism of a son Anders of Biørn Gilmann (DA 9361 p. 51, canvas 25, right page, first entry); 'Ingri Strand' was godmother the same day. Whether this Jul is Jul Tordsen (father of Marit Juulsdatter, b. about 1688) or a younger man is not shown; it only places a Jul at Strand in 1732. ¶4

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
