About the family

Father of Lydia Adelaide (Hicken) Hayter, named as William John Hicken, born P.E.I., on her 1969 BC death registration (informant her daughter Rita). ¶1

Parentage (Probable, not Confirmed): no record yet read names his parents. The compiled Hicken genealogy makes him a son of George Hicken (c.1812-1899) and Frances Gordon of Gaspereaux; the 1889 voters' list has a John W. Hicken qualifying as an owner's son in Lot 61 next to George Hicken sr; his daughter Edith was named Edith Gordon; and he is buried in the same cemetery as George's daughters Jane and Phebe, whose stones name their parents as George and Francis Hicken. ¶7

Parentage confirmed: the 1891 census of Township 61 (enumerated 30 April 1891) lists George Hicken, 79, born in England, as 'Fath.' (father) in the household of William Hicken, 36. The household also holds William's wife Bexy and their children Adelaide, Edith and George H. Together with the 1855 baptism naming George and Frances as his parents and the 1881 census household, this upgrades his parentage from Probable to Confirmed. ¶11

Life story

Birth: the Hicken compiler gives 12 May 1853 at Gaspereaux, as a twin of Lydia Levina, with baptism on 15 July 1855 at the Vernon River Bible Christian Church. The age of 36 on his gravestone fits a birth about 1855 rather than 1853, so the twin pairing is probably a compiler's error; ABT 1855 is used here. ¶4

Death: he died at 36 leaving a widow of 29 and three children aged nine, four and two. His widow remarried twice. ¶6

Baptism: John William Hicken (indexed 'Hickins'), born 24 January 1855, baptised 15 July 1855, son of George and Frances (PEI Baptism Index, Ancestry 9195). The compiled Hicken genealogy places this baptism at the Vernon River Bible Christian Church. ¶9

Death: PEI death card gives William Hicken, born 1855, died 18 August 1891 at Sturgeon. His gravestone says 19 August. The Sturgeon church register records the burial of William Hicken, aged about 36, on 20 August 1891 at Sturgeon (FamilySearch film 005240351, image 302; Ancestry 61501). ¶14

1861 census of P.E.I. (Lot 61): he was one of the ten people in his father George Hicken's household, a Methodist family holding 100 acres on leasehold. The 1861 return names only heads of household. ¶13

1881 census (Township 61, King's County, sub-district S, household 38; Wesleyan Methodist): William Hicken, 26, farmer, of English origin, born P.E.I., living with his new wife Bexy (19) in the household of George Hicken (69) and Frances Hicken (67), with George (29), Francis (24) and Alice (22). Their daughter Lydia was born eleven months later. This is the record evidence that he was George and Frances's son. ¶8

Marriage 1881: no Hicken-Creed marriage of 1881 is indexed in the PEI Marriage Registers 1832-1888 (Ancestry 61502), under any spelling of Hicken, Creed, Bexey, Bexy, Bexie or Rebecca. The conflict between 12 January and 10 February 1881 at Summerside is therefore still unresolved; see the dossier for the record that would settle it. ¶15

Gravestone, Sturgeon Baptist Cemetery, Albion (Find a Grave 75109750, photograph read): 'In memory of William J. Hicken, died Aug. 19, 1891, Æt. 36. Blessed are the dead who die in the Lord.' - the same verse and the same monument makers (Cairns & McLean) as the stone of Francis A. Hicken, died 1893 aged 36, beside him. Aged 36 puts his birth about 1854-55. ¶2

Name: his daughter's 1969 death registration calls him William John Hicken; the Creed compiler also has William John; the Hicken compiler has John William. The 1889 voters' list for Sturgeon (Township 61) has both a 'William J.' and a 'John W.' Hicken - the latter at Gaspereaux post office, qualifying as an owner's son on 108 acres in Lot 61 - which may be the same man listed twice or two different men; unresolved. ¶3

1891 census (Kings District 133, sub-district S, Township 61, page 41, dwelling 170, family 168): Hicken, William, 36, married, born P.E.I., father born England, mother born P.E.I., Methodist, farmer; Bexy [written 'Berry'], 29, wife; Adilada [Adelaide], 9, daughter; Edith, 4, daughter; George H., 2, son; George, 79, father, widower, born England of English-born parents. Next door (dwelling 171) was his brother Francis, 34, single, farmer. ¶12

Corrections 1

Correction: the compiled Hicken genealogy (islandregister.com) gives his birth as 12 May 1853, as a twin of Lydia Levina. The baptism index gives 24 January 1855, which agrees with his age of 26 in the 1881 census, 36 in the 1891 census and 36 on his gravestone. The twin pairing is a compiler's error. ¶10
