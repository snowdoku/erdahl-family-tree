How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the grade · 1 on the identification · 4 open questions

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Graded Probable on his link to his son Anfind (Arnfinn) Olsen (see his page). His probate (Ytre Sogn 8a fol. 211b-212a, No. 101, Oct 1764, at Bale) names among his heirs, 'udi første Egteskab med den sal. Kone Maritte Olsdatter avlet 1 Søn ved Navn Anfind Olsen myndig og tilstede', then a son Ole Olsen by his 2nd wife Inge[borg?] Knudsdatter, sons Ole Olsen 21 and Ingebrigt Olsen 19 by his 3rd wife Kari Pedersdatter, and four daughters Kari, Mari, Guri and Christie Olsdatter by his widow Torbiør Samsonsdatter. The bygdebok (Balestrand 1, c77-78) has Ola Arnfinnson, b. 1696, who came to Bale (bruk 1) after Hans, was married four times (the 2nd wife Ingrid Knutsdotter, d. 1736; the 3rd Kari Persdotter Sværen, d. 1752), had the son Arnfinn by his first wife, and died in 1764. Identification of the 1764 heir with the tree's Anfind Olsen of Kalvetræet (Arnfinn (Anfind) Olsen Bale) rests on the Balestrand bygdebok, which calls him 'Arnfinn Olson Bale' (c132, came to his place in 1757, b. 1724, d. 1800) and lists Arnfinn among Ola Arnfinnson's children ('Av borni hans Ola... Arnfinn... døydde i 1800', c78), and on the 1764 probate's note that Anfind Olsen had received goods at his marriage (fol. 212a), consistent with a son already married off the farm (m. 1757). It spans two places (Bale 1764, Kalvetræet under Tjugum 1781) and other Anfind Olsens lived in Tjugum skipreide (Anfind Olsen Hov d. 1795, Anfind Olsen Sætre d. 1785), so the link is Probable. ¶1a

The case for this identification 1

Supporting, grade unchanged: the groom of the 11 Apr 1757 betrothal with Ingeborg was 'Anfind Olsøn Bahle' (Leikanger 11566 c8), and 'Ole Anfindsøn Bahle' himself appears as a surety on 7 Aug 1757 (c11). This narrows the rivals (Anfind Olsen at Hov and at Sætre) for the husband of Ingeborg to the Bale man. Still Probable: no single record names Ole as father of the Kalvetræet Anfind. ¶2a

Open questions and the next record to read 4

Test record: a deed or tingbok entry naming Anfind Olsen of Kalvetræet as Ole Anfindsen's son, or Anfind Olsen's own probate c.1800 if one exists (not in the Ytre Sogn index under Kalvetreet, Tjugum or Bale). ¶1b

Test record: Christi Anfindsdatter's baptism c.1762 (11566 c61-68), if it calls the father Anfind Olsen Bale, would close the gap. ¶2b

Lead, no grade change: his origin before Bale is not known. The Balestrand bygdebok's sentence 'Ola Arnfinnson ___ som kom til Bale etter Hans' (Laberg 1985, canvas 77) has a gap word after the name. Phrase searches for Sværen, Ese, Mundal, Kvamme, Tjugum, Fjærland and others all returned 0. The 1701 census for Ytre and Indre Sogn (lastname Andfind*) lists these sons named Olle Andfindsen born 1695-97: Medie (b. 1696, pc628582), Quamme (b. 1696, pc632181), Mundahl (b. 1697, with brother Hans b. 1700, pc627757), Berdahl (b. 1697, pc628048) and Onstad (b. 1695, pc628824). ¶3a

Test record: a probate after an Anfind on one of these farms (1701-1740) naming 'Ole Anfindsen Bale' as an heir. ¶3b
