About the family

Family: he was married twice. Children of the first marriage: Anders Endresen (of age in 1780, living at Grønset; the Anders Endresen Grønset confirmed 1768) and Anne Endresdatter (26, born about 1754). Children with Anne Torstensdatter: Torsten (13, born about 1767), Ingeborg (11½), Endre (7, born about 1773) and Mali (6, born about 1774). ¶6a

Father: Niels Endresen of Stokke, guardian in a Stokke probate in May 1742 (77795 view 3791; 24642 fol. 522-523); graded Probable on the father's record. Endre's patronymic, his home farm Stokke in 1748, and his own name (Niels's father was an Endre) all fit; no record yet states the relationship. ¶12

Mother added: Anne Monsdatter Stokke, Probable, from the Gjemnes bygdebok (bind 9, canvas 213). It lists 'Endre f. 1716, g. 1748 med Ingeborg Andersdotter Grønset' among the children of Nils Endreson Stokke and his wife Anne (m. 1714), and gives his birth year as 1716. ¶14

Life story

First marriage found: Kvernes church book 1731-1760 (source 16075), view 1, p. 2, in the running list of marriages ('Copulerede'), among the 1748 entries (after Easter-summer 1748, before the entries copulated in Jan 1749 and the 1749 heading; the year is not written on the line itself): '5 Oct tr[olovet] Endre Niels[en] Stokke Ingbor Anders[datte]r Grønset — cop[uleret] 11 N[ov]'. So Endre came from Stokke and married into Grønset; his first wife Ingeborg Andersdatter had died by June 1764, when he married again as a widower. His daughter Ingeborg (b. 1769) by his third wife Ane Tostensdatter bears the first wife's name. ¶11

The 1761 Grønset sale at pantebok 2 fol. 202 was made jointly by 'Endre Nielsen med flere' and 'Tosten Nielsen Stoche med flere' (panteregister 22676, views 54 and 230). With the 1755 probate of Torsten's wife Anne Andersdatter (24645 no. 54, guardian 'Morbroderen Knud Andersen Skardet'), this makes Endre and Torsten Nielsen husbands of two daughters of Anders Knudsen Grønset. It is consistent with the bygdebok's Torstein, son of Nils Endreson, but no record yet calls Endre and Torsten brothers. Test: 24648 fol. 347a, the probate after Anders Tostensen Grønset (about 1768). ¶16

1762 extra tax: Ekstraskatt 1762, Kvernes sogn, Gjemnes tinglag (source 47939), view 56, farm no. 53 Grønset (continued): 'Endre Nielsen og hustru' (his first wife, still living in 1762), with one son over 12 in the children's column, read as '[Endre or Andor] Endresen' (Anders Endresen of the 1780 probate fits), and a girl over 12 and 'Enke Ane' in the house; next household Auden Andersen og hustru. This dates the first wife's death to between 1762 and June 1764. ¶9

Baptism read: Kvernes ministerialbok 16076, p.135 (canvas c70), Dom. Septuagesima [22 Jan] 1769: 'Døbt Endre Nielsen Grønset og H[ustr]u Ane Tostensd[atte]r B[arn] Ingebor'. Godparents: Ole Nielsen Halset, Botolf Ellingsen Hoksæt, Jacob Ols[en] Stokkes H[ustr]u Ane Aslachsd[atte]r, Auden Audens[en] Grønsets H[ustr]u Ingebor Monsd[atte]r, and [Jo? / Lange?] Tostens[en] Hoksæts H[ustr]u Ane Iversd[atte]r (last godparent's husband's first name uncertain). Ane Tostensdatter was churched 18 Feb 1769 (same page). Endre Nielsen Grønset was also a godparent at Advent 1768 (p.134). ¶1

Probate found: Nordmøre skifteprotokoll nr 15 (source 24650), fol. 624a. 'Anno 1780 Løverdagen den 17de Maij' a registration and valuation was held on the farm Grønset 'beliggende i Gimnes Tinglag og Kvernes Præstegield' after 'de... afgangne Egtefolk nemlig Manden Endre Nielsen Grønsetle og Konen Anne Torstensdatter'. The heirs were the husband's six children 'i 2de Ægteskaber avlede', of whom four were born in the marriage with Anne Torstensdatter and so were her heirs too. Of the first marriage: '1 Sønnen Anders Endresen Grønsetle', of age, living on the farm, and '2 Datteren Anne Endresdatter gammel 26 aar', unmarried, guardian Anders Knudsen Scharset of Mek tinglag. Of the last marriage (2 sons, 2 daughters): '3 Sønnen Torsten Endresen gammel 13 aar', guardian 'Morbroderen [reading of the first letters uncertain] Johannes Torstensen' of Hogset[?]; '4 Sønnen Endre Endresen gammel 7 aar', guardian Ole Nielsen Haaset[?]; '5 Datteren Ingeborg Endresdatter gammel 11½ aar', guardian Ole Nielsen Haaset[?]; '6 Datteren Mali Endresdatter gammel 6 aar', guardian Nils Johnsen Haastad[?]. Valuers Knud Monsen Øvrig[?] and Knud Halvorsen Stokke. ¶4

Marriages found: Endre Nielsen of Grønset married three times. (1) His first wife, mother of Anders Endresen and Anne Endresdatter (26 in 1780, so born about 1754), had died before June 1764; her name is still unknown. (2) Kvernes 16076 view 54, pp. 102-103: under 'Fer. 2 Pentecost.' [11 Jun 1764] 'trolovet Andre Nielsen Grönset und Enken Elen Nielsd[atte]r Aaset[?]', and among those 'Copuleret' after the Visitation feast [2 Jul 1764] '4 Enkemand Andre Nielsen Grönset und Enken Elen Nielsd[atte]r Aaset[?]'. (3) View 59, p. 113: 'Dom. XXII a Trin.' [3 Nov 1765] 'trolovet Enkemanden Andre Nielsen Grønset und pigen Ane Tostensd[atte]r Höcksæt', and view 60, p. 114: 'Dom. p. Natalit.' [29 Dec 1765] 'Copuleret Enkem[an]d Andre Nielsen Grønset og pigen Ane Tostensd[atte]r Höckset'. Elen Nielsdatter must have died between July 1764 and Nov 1765, apparently childless, since the 1780 probate speaks of children from only two marriages. ¶7

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 3

Why Probable: this is the only baptism of an Ingeborg with father Endre found in Kvernes 16076 pp.120-135 (end 1766 to Feb 1769) and in Kornstad 16095 for 1767-68 (run 1). It fits her 1801-census birth year (about 1769) and her name. The burial age (60 on 22 Jan 1828) would put birth in about 1767-68, one year early; rounded burial ages are common. Not checked: Kvernes before Christmas 1766 and after Feb 1769 (the 1801 census age fits a birth later in 1769 equally well, so a rival Ingeborg Endresdatter baptised in Mar-Dec 1769 is not excluded), and Kornstad 1766 and 1769. ¶2

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed, graded on his link to Ingeborg Endresdatter. (1) Baptism 22 Jan 1769, Kvernes 16076 p.135: 'Endre Nielsen Grønset og H[ustr]u Ane Tostensd[atte]r B[arn] Ingebor'. (2) His joint probate with Anne Torstensdatter, Grønset, 17 May 1780 (24650 fol. 624a), names 'Datteren Ingeborg Endresdatter gammel 11½ aar' (born late 1768, so the same child) and her half-brother 'Sønnen Anders Endresen Grønsetle', of age and living on the farm. (3) Kvernes betrothal register 16080, canvas 138, p. 275, 1796: 'Aug: 6te forlangte u[ng]k[arl] og Sold[at] Elling Povlsøn Stokke, som skal faae 1 Part i Gaarden Gulset til beboelse, at trolove i Kvernes d. 28 ejusd. s[om er] D[om]: 14 p. Tr[initatis] med p[ige] Ingbor Endresd[atte]r Grønset. For deres Egteskabs Lovlighed godsige Ole Ellings[en] Stokke [and] Anders Endres[en] Grønset.' The bride is still 'of Grønset' 16 years later, the same farm, and her surety is the same half-brother Anders Endresen Grønset named in the probate; the groom's surety Ole Ellingsen Stokke is the 'Farbroder' named in Elling's father's 1779 probate (24650 fol. 365a). (4) The groom was to take part of Guldset, and the 1801 census has Elling Povelsen and his wife Ingeborg Endresdatter (32) at Guldset. Each step is the same farm within a short gap, and no other Ingeborg Endresdatter is found at Grønset (the 1801 Ingeborg Endresdatter at Vevang, born about 1753, does not fit). This closes the gap his notes named (the marriage, or a probate tying the daughter to the husband). ¶5

Graded on his link to his children (unchanged, Confirmed by the 1780 probate). The 1764 and 1765 marriage entries add that he was already a widower in 1764, so Anders Endresen's mother died before June 1764; look for her burial and for Endre's first marriage before about 1754 (16076 begins 1753; earlier Kvernes book needed). ¶8

Corrections 1

Correction: Anders Endresen was Ingeborg's half-brother, not a full brother as earlier notes supposed. He died, with his second wife, before 17 May 1780. ¶6b

Searches that found nothing 2

Negative: the Grønset, Gjemnes cards of the Nordmøre probate card index 1668-1746 (source 77795, views 1353-1355) hold only Olluf Olluffsen (1677) and Ole Andersen (1694, sons Anders and Ole); no Niels at Grønset before 1746, so his father is still unidentified. ¶10

Deed read, negative for his parentage: Pantebok nr. 2, 1751-1765 (Digitalarkivet 20327), view 203, right page, No. 15, dated at the thing [place name unclear] 19 Jun 1761: the heirs of the late Anders Knudsen Grønset (his widow Anne Andersdatter, the son Knud Andersen Scharset, Torsten Nielsen Stokke and Endre Nielsen Grønset, apparently through their wives: Endre's first wife Ingeborg Andersdatter was of Grønset, and found Torsten Nielsen's wife to be an Anne Andersdatter of Stokke; the deed's opening lines are only partly legible) confirm the sale of his part of Grønset to Knud Pedersen Kiøl, citing the probate after Anders Knudsen of 10 Nov 1751. Signed by Knud Andersen Scharset, Torsten Nielsen Stokke, Endre Nielsen Grønset and Anne Andersdatter; witnesses Ole Nielsen Stokke and Halvor Knudsen Stokke. So the 'med flere' of the panteregister are his wife's family, not Niels Endresen's heirs. Torsten Nielsen and Ole Nielsen of Stokke stand beside him (the bygdebok's brothers Torstein and Ole), but the deed states no relationship among them, so it does not raise Niels Endresen Stokke or Anne Monsdatter Stokke. The first lines of the deed are hard to read; the wives' names are not written out on the part read. ¶15
