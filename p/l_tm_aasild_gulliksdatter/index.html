About the family

Parentage (Probable): she married in 1823 as 'Aasild Gunleiks D.r Midböe', so her father was a Gunleik (Gullik is a later normalised form) of Midbø. This is the only baptism of an Aasild daughter of a Gunleik (or Gullik/Gulleik) indexed in Nissedal/Kviteseid 1802-1808, and its year fits her marriage age of 18 on 12 Apr 1823 (Nissedal ministerialbok 1814-1860, p. 196) and her burial age of 53 in March 1858. In the 1801 census Gunlek Alvsen, 40, farmer-occupier, and his wife Turi Thoresdatter, 25, were at Midbøe Nordre with no children yet, and with Turi's sister Guro Toresdatter, 13. Thor Gulliksen of Midbø, a witness at her daughter Thuri's 1858 marriage, would be her brother named after their maternal grandfather Tore (inference). ¶3a

Life story

Baptism: 'Gunleik Alvsen Midböes Datter Aasild' was baptised by Mr Storm in Nissedal church on Midfaste Sunday (Laetare, 24 Mar 1805, computed from Easter 14 Apr 1805). Her mother Turi Torsdatter was churched on Easter Day, 14 Apr 1805. Godparents: Ragnil(?) Ormsdatter(?) Groven, Marthe Tveiten, Tone Thorsdatter Groven, Jon Aslaksen of the same place, and Alv Hans.(?) Tveiten; several readings are uncertain. ¶2

Of Midbø, Nissedal. Married Torkild Olsen on 12 Apr 1823. She is indexed variously as Gulleiksdatter, Gunliksdatter and Gulleksdatter. Thor Gulliksen of Midbø, a witness at her daughter Thuri's 1858 marriage, was probably her brother. ¶1

Death: 'Aasild Gulliksdatter, Ibrække' died 3 Mar and was buried 14 Mar 1858 in Nissedal, age 53, described as a gaardmandsenke (farmer's widow; probable reading). The index misreads her as 'Aasild Gullbranddatt' with death '11-02'. ¶4

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Upgrade: Confirmed as the daughter of Gunleik Alvsen and Turi Torsdatter of Midbø. The Nissedal confirmation list for 1822 (ministerialbok 1814-1845, source 8019, p. 247, canvas 162, girls no. 13, in the group confirmed 28 Jul 1822) reads 'Aasil Midbøe | Gunlek og Turi Midbøe | døbt 1805 Midfaste Søndag', vaccinated 4 Mar 1806 by A. Hansen of Tvetene. This names her parents as Gunleik and Turi of Midbø and her baptism as the Midfaste Sunday baptism of 24 Mar 1805 ('Gunleik Alvsen Midböes Datter Aasild'). On 12 Apr 1823 'Aasild Gunleiksdatter Midbøe', 18, married Torkeld Olsen Ibrække, so name, farm and age join the confirmand to the bride. This is the confirmation entry that the earlier 'Parentage (Probable)' note asked for. Her link to her daughter Thuri rests on Thuri's 1831 baptism. ¶5

Audit 2 (1 Nov 2026): Confirmed kept, but on a different record from the one the upgrade note cites. Under the overnight grading rule her grade covers her link to her daughter Thuri, not her link to Gunleik Alvsen and Turi Torsdatter, which the 1822 confirmation list proves and which is graded on their records. The link to Thuri rests on the 23 Jan 1831 Nissedal baptism naming Torkel Olsen and Aasild Gulliksdatter of Midbø, joined to the 1858 bride Thuri Thorkildsdatter of Midbø, aged 28 (p. 218 no. 11). ¶6a
