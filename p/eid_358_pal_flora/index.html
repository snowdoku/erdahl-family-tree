Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed, graded on his link to his daughter Kirsten (Kirsti) Paulsdatter (see her page). The joint probate after 'Povel Torchilsen og Hustrue Brynild Jensdatter', held 23 Nov 1756 in Selbu (Stjør- og Verdal skifteprotokoll 24753 fol. 732b, entry No. 2, view 762 left, lower half; found through the typed register entry 'Povel Torchilsen og hustru Brynild Jensdatter, Flaknan, Selbu, 1756, 732 b'), lists their children: 1 the son Torchel Poulsen; 2 Einer Povelsen, 40; 3 Jens Poulsen, 25, with a guardian; 4 the daughter Guru Povelsdatter, about 34, with her brother Einer Poulsen as guardian; 5 'Datteren Kiersten Povelsdatter gift med Ole Poulsen Berge'; 6 the daughter Gunild Povelsdatter, married [husband not read]. Kirsten is identified by her husband: the tree's Kirsten Paulsdatter is the wife of Ole Paulsen of Berge (baptisms 1757-1761), so no cross-farm identification is needed. The family matches the bygdebok's Pål of Flora exactly: second wife Brynhild Jensdatter (m. 1729, 'visstnok d. ca. 1756'), next holder Einer (b. 1717), Goro (b. 1721), Kirsti, Gunhild (b. 1730), Jens (b. 1733). The farm name in the protocol margin is hard to read ('Flagnum'?; the typed register has 'Flaknan'); the identification does not rest on it. His patronymic Torkilsen (Torchilsen) is new and his eldest son Torkel carries it. Death: before 23 Nov 1756. ¶5

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Caution: the book's birth year 1725 for Kirsti differs from her burial age (81 in Aug 1803, born about 1722) and the 1801 census (born 1721); both fall inside Pål's first marriage (1716-1728), so the mother is Gunhild either way, but the book's year needs checking against the Selbu baptisms of 1721-1725 (16310). ¶2
