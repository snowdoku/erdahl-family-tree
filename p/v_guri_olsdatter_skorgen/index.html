About the family

Named as Anne's mother in her 1816 baptism and 1831 confirmation. Probably the 'Guraa Olsd.' of 'Kaarsan' who married Lasse Anderssen at Veøy 3 Dec 1806. Her parents are not identified. ¶1

Father (Speculative): Ole Svendsen of Korsand (Kaarsan), Veøy (see his page) is entered as her father with the Speculative label on him. The link is Speculative only; her own confidence (Confirmed, as Anne's mother) is not a judgment on it. See his notes for the argument and caveats. This supersedes the notes 'Origin candidate (Speculative, not linked)' and 'Why no father is entered', and the earlier statement that her parents are not identified: the objection there (a birth at about 47 in 1819) is answered by her own burial age, and the link is entered as Speculative rather than left out. It would be confirmed by a Veøy baptism of about 1771-74 of Guraa, daughter of Ole Svendsen Korsand (Veøy ministerialbok 1765-1799, source 16043, scans unindexed), or by the probate of Ole Svendsen Kaarsan, d. 1806 (Romsdal sorenskriveri probate index). ¶12

Father raised to Probable: the probate of Ole Svendsen Kaarsan, 18 Jul 1806 (Romsdal skifteprotokoll 13, s. 172b-173a), names among his children by his 2nd marriage 'Datteren Gurie Olsd.', unmarried, with a guardian named Lasse (byname read Holmanvig[?]). She is very probably the 'Pigen Guraa Olsd. Kaarsan' who married Lasse Andersen Wiiche on 3 Dec 1806: same name, farm and year, and unmarried. A half-sister, also Gurie, was then wife of Bottolf Hunnes, probably the forlover 'Bukolf Bukolfsen Hundnes' at her wedding. Her mother, Ole's 2nd wife, is not named in the probate. Full siblings: Peder Olsen and Anne Olsdatter (b. 1771). Half-siblings by the 1st marriage: Steen, Ole, Ingeborg (m. Halvord Pedersen), Gurie (m. Bottolf Hunnes) and Anne. As a full sister of Anne (May 1771) and Peder (Jan 1773) she was born before about mid-1770 or after mid-1774, so neither her 1801 age (29) nor her burial age (68) can be exact; see Ole Svendsen Korsand. ¶14

Mother still unknown: she was Ole Svendsen's 2nd wife (probate 1806), not Ane Larsdatter, his wife in 1801. Her name should appear in the Veøy marriage register about 1765-70 (16043) or in a probate after her death before about 1790 (Romsdal probate index, free). ¶17

Mother identified: Brit (Birgitte) Madsdatter of Moe, Eid (Grytten), who married the widower Ole Svendsen of Korsand at Eid church in 1770 (engaged 16 Apr, married 6 May; Grytten ministerialbok 1764-1806 pp. 110-111, scans read). That was his second marriage, and his 1806 probate names Gurie among the three children of the second marriage. The wedding entry gives the bride's first name as Marite, the engagement as Birgite; see the mother's notes. The earlier lead Berette Pedersdatter Kaarsan (bur. 1794) is dropped: her patronymic is clearly Pedersdatter on the scan. ¶18

Life story

Marriage (scan read): Veøy ministerialbok 1799-1818, p. 113 left (folio 112b), 3 December 1806: 'I Wedøe Kirke copuleret Ungkarl Lasse Andersen Wiiche og Pigen Guraa Olsd: Kaarsan. Forlovere: Bukolf Bukolfsen Hundnes og Ingebrigt Andersen Hammervold.' Married in Veøy church: the bachelor Lasse Andersen of Vike and the maiden Guraa Olsdatter of Kaarsan. The entry gives no ages and names no parents. The forlovere (sureties) are not stated to be kin; Ingebrigt Andersen Hammervold also stood as forlover at another wedding on the same page (25th Sunday after Trinity 1806), so he was probably a regular witness, not a relative. ¶5

Her origin is still unresolved. She was 'Guraa Olsd.' of 'Kaarsan' at her 1806 Veøy marriage. That farm name does not match any 1801 census place in Grytten or Veøy, and there are many Guri Olsdatters of the right age (for example Gurri, 18, at Kyllingen, Grytten, and Guri, 16, a servant at Sellesvold, Veøy). Not identified. ¶2

Her origin was searched again and remains unresolved. The indexed registers for Grytten, Hen and Veøy contain thirteen women named Guri (Gurri, Gurie) Olsdatter born between 1778 and 1790, at Sellesvold and Redstadbakken in Veøy and at Hen, Sæbe, Kyllingen, Moa and Brudelistuen in Grytten. None of them can be matched to the 'Guraa Olsd.' of 'Kaarsan' who married Lasse Anderssen at Veøy church on 3 Dec 1806, and no farm in the 1801 census for Grytten or Veøy is written anything like Kaarsan. Her baptism, parents and death are therefore still unknown. ¶3

A possible reading worth testing: 'Kaarsan' may be a clerk's rendering of Kors or Korsen, the name of one of Grytten parish's annexes and of farms within it. The Kors local-parish register (Grytten, Kors sokn) would be the place to look for her baptism about 1780-1788 and would need to be read page by page. ¶4

Burial (index, re-read): 'Guri Olsdatter Skorgen', position 'HmdsEnke' (husmannsenke, widow of a cottar), aged 68, died 9 Jan 1842, buried 19 Jan 1842. Grytten church book, Grytten local parish 1818-1853 (1539P), p. 217, no. 1 (pg00000006507178). The residence field is blank; the farm name Skorgen is written as part of her name. Her husband is not named. Age 68 at death gives a birth between 10 Jan 1773 and 9 Jan 1774 (the index calculates 1774). ¶9

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 3

Why this burial is hers: she is the only Guri Olsdatter buried with the farm name Skorgen, and her status as a husmann's widow fits Lasse Andersen, husmann at Skorgen, who drowned in 1829. Two other burials of the same name were considered and set aside: Guri Olsd. of Alnæs, Kors local parish, Grytten, d. 26 Feb 1840, aged 66 (a different farm, and no widow status given); and Guri Olsdatter, Veøy local parish, buried 24 Oct 1841, aged 66, residence blank (a different parish, with no tie to Skorgen). Neither is ruled out absolutely, since their scans have not been read, but the Skorgen entry is the one that matches her farm and status. ¶10

Confidence adjusted (13 Oct 2026, line-3 orchestrator on auditor recommendation): Confirmed → Probable. In this tree a person's own level expresses the evidence for her link to her parents; her father link to Ole Svendsen of Kaarsan rests on the 1806 probate naming an unmarried daughter 'Gurie Olsd.' plus the same name, farm and year as the Dec 1806 bride — Probable, not Confirmed. Her identity (marriage 1806, children, 1842 burial) is not in doubt. ¶15

Upgrade: baptism found and read on the scan. Veøy ministerialbok 1765-1799 (1541P, Digitalarkivet source 16043), canvas 66, p. 130 (headed 'Anno 1774 Julius et Augustus'): 'Den 20de Aug. Døbte i Weedøe Kirke Ole Svendsen Kaarsands Datter, kaldet Guraa. Sponsores: Anne [Peders?]datter Øvre-Hundnæs, Guraa Børgesdatter Hou[s]seberg[?], Ingebor Pedersdatter Haresvold[?], Ole Olsen Bax[x]en[?] og Jon Pedersen Øvre-Hundnæs' - baptised in Veøy church 20 Aug 1774, Ole Svendsen of Kaarsand's daughter, named Guraa. The mother is not named; the facing page (p. 131, 14th Sunday after Trinity) records the churching of Ole Svendsen Kaarsand's wife, again unnamed. Why this settles the link: the bride 'Pigen Guraa Olsd. Kaarsan' of 3 Dec 1806 is the unmarried 'Datteren Gurie Olsd.' of Ole Svendsen's 2nd marriage in his probate of 18 Jul 1806, and her burial of Jan 1842 'aged 68' puts her birth in 1773-74; this baptism of Aug 1774 is the only Guraa baptised to Ole Svendsen Kaarsand (his other children Anne, May 1771, and Peder, 1 Jan 1773, were, and no Guraa in Feb-May 1771 or Feb 1773-Jun 1774). Baptism, probate, marriage and burial age now agree, so her link to Ole Svendsen is Confirmed. The 1801 census age of 29 (born about 1772) is two years high. The godparents from Øvre-Hundnes fit the later tie to Bottolf Hunnes, husband of her half-sister Gurie. ¶16

The case for this identification 1

Why no father is entered: the marriage entry adds no age and no father, so it neither confirms nor rules out the Korsand Guraa. If she is that woman, she was about 34 at the wedding and at Berit's birth in October 1806, and about 47 when her son Ole was baptised at Grytten on 15 Oct 1819 (pd00000040482713). A birth at 47 is rare. In this tree a person's own confidence also carries the link to their parents. Her confidence is Confirmed, which rests on her identity as Anne's mother. Linking Ole Svendsen would therefore show him as a Confirmed father, which the evidence does not support. Her confidence is left unchanged, and the link to Ole Svendsen is withdrawn. A record giving her age would settle it, such as her burial (Grytten, after 1819) or the 1815 or 1825 census. ¶7

Corrections 2

Correction: the earlier notes on this person looked for her only among women born 1778-1790, and they said no 1801 farm resembled Kaarsan. The age window was an assumption; no record about her gives an age. The farm is Korsand in Veøy, not Kors in Grytten. Her birth is left unset. ¶8

Correction: earlier notes placed her birth in 1778-1790, and doubted a birth about 1772 because she would then have been about 47 at her son Ole's baptism in 1819. The 1842 burial age of 68 puts her birth in 1773-74, which matches the Guraa Olsdatter, 29, at Korsand, Veøy in the 1801 census (b. c.1772) within one or two years, and excludes the 1778-1790 window. Whatever her origin, the burial age makes her about 45-46 at that 1819 baptism, so the late-birth point no longer tells against the Korsand Guraa. ¶11

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Origin candidate (Speculative, not linked): 'Kaarsan' is the farm written Korsand in the 1801 Veøy census. The same Ole Svendsen appears in both places: he is 71 at Korsand in 1801, and he was buried 18 May 1806 at Rødven as a kårmann (retired farmer on a retirement agreement) of Kaarsan, aged 76 (Veøy p. 167). The only Guraa on the farm in 1801 is Guraa Olsdatter, 29 (born about 1772), unmarried. She was the sister of the head, Ole Olsen, 32, and so by inference a daughter of Ole Svendsen. ¶6

Searches that found nothing 1

Baptism searched, not found: the Veøy register for 1765- (source 16043) was read at full resolution for Feb - May 1771 and late Feb 1773 - early Jun 1774. No Guraa, Gurri, Gjøri or Guri was baptised to an Ole in those pages. The pages do contain baptisms of two other children of Ole Svendsen Kaarsand: Anne, May 1771, and Peder, 1 Jan 1773. As a full sister, Guri therefore cannot have been born between about Aug 1770 and Oct 1773. That conflicts with both of her recorded ages: 29 in 1801 (b. 1771-72) and 68 at burial in 1842 (b. 1773-74). She could still be a daughter of Ole Svendsen born before mid-1770 or after mid-1774; the pages for those years have not been read. See Ole Svendsen Korsand for details. The Speculative father link is unchanged. ¶13
