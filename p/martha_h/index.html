Life story

Probate, 1809: in the probate of 29 May 1809 after Halsten Rasmussen Loen and his wife Gunnild Andersdatter (Nordfjord skifteprotokoll A 21b, pp. 1033, 1040-1041), 'Marthe Halsteensdatter gift med Samuel Guttormsen Loen' is named as a daughter and heir. Her siblings were Lars, Else Catrine, Randi and Gjertrud. ¶3

Aaland 1932: Loen bruk nr. 15, printed p. 322: 'Vs. Samuel Gutormson Hilde, † 59 a. g. 1832, sk. 1789, br. 1802. * Marte Hallst.d. L., † 81 a. g. 1857.' Aaland confirms her as Hallstein Rasmussen's daughter and Samuel Guttormsen's wife. ¶5

Aaland's death of 1857 aged 81 is right. The Stryn og Innvik lensmann death register (1856-1861, 1857 no. 3) records Marte Halstensdatter Loen, retired widow (vilkaarskone), died 16 Feb 1857 aged 81, survived by her children Halsten Samuelsen (dead, leaving children), Anders Samuelsen and Marte Samuelsdatter married to Lasse Jørgensen Erdal. The 4 Apr 1834 Erdal burial the tree gave her is a 4-day-old farmer's daughter of the same name (see the Correction note in). ¶6

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Probable: Marthe, daughter of Halsten Rasmussen and Gunnild Andersdatter of Loen, was baptized 27 Dec 1775. A Marthe Halstensdatter of Loen was confirmed in 1789 (index age implies born about 1774). Married Samuel Guttormsen on 20 Jun 1807. She died at Erdal (buried 4 Apr 1834), presumably living with her daughter Marthe Samuelsdatter, who had married Lasse Jørgensen Erdal in 1833. ¶2

Confidence moved Probable to Confirmed: that probate names her as Halsten's and Gunnild's daughter and as Samuel Guttormsen's wife, which ties the 1775 baptism to the 1807 marriage. ¶4

Corrections 2

Correction: she died at Loen on 16 Feb 1857 aged 81, not in 1834. The Stryn og Innvik lensmann death register (1856-1861, 1857 no. 3; reported 17 Mar 1857 by Samuel Halstensen Loen) records 'Vilkaarskone Marte Halstensdatter Loen, 81 Aar gl.' (a retired widow living on a farm pension). Her heirs were her children: 1. Halsten Samuelsen Loen, dead, leaving Samuel Halstensen Loen (20), Jørgen Halstensen (12) and Marte Halstensdatter (18); 2. Anders Samuelsen Kirke-Eide (about 37); 3. Marte Samuelsdatter (about 40), married to Lasse Jørgensen Erdal. This agrees with Aaland 1932 ('† 81 a. g. 1857'). ¶7

Correction: the Innvik burial of 4 Apr 1834 (died 31 Mar 1834, Erdal) formerly given as hers is entry no. 84 among the female burials of 1834: 'Gaardmandsdatter Marthe Halstensdatter Erdal', aged 4 days (Dage), of Erdal in Oppstryn. It is an infant farmer's daughter, not Martha Halstensdatter. The index omitted the age. The old 1834 death date and Erdal place are replaced; the earlier note that she died at Erdal living with her daughter is withdrawn. ¶8
