About the family

Mother of Anders Andersson Eidem (1852/3-1932) of Zoar Lutheran, and of six older children including Bardo, Erik, Kari and Brynhild. Find a Grave memorial 229712462 indexes her as 'Brynhild Barosdatter "Mogard" Mosletta Eidem, 1809-1888'; her son's biography gives the name as 'Brynhild Bardosdatter Mogarn'. Mogarn / Mogard / Mosletta are Selbu farm bynames. ¶1

Life story

Widowed in 1852 while carrying her youngest child, and 'never remarried'. She stayed in Norway; four at least of her seven children emigrated to Minnesota between 1874 and about 1880. ¶2

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
