About the family

Parents (bygdebok, Probable): Valdres bygdebok I D (Svenes), Fodnes gnr. 67 (canvas 423): Ulrik, 1735-1812, married 1755 'Åste Høljesdotter' of gnr. 65 (Strand), 1732-1809. The gnr. 65 chapter (canvas 310) lists her as '3 Åste 1732, g 1755 m Ulrik', daughter of Hølje Tordsson (1709-1785) and Kari Haraldsdotter (m. 1728); an elder sister Åste (1730) died in 1731. Linked as Hølje (Helge) Tordsen and Kari Haraldsdatter. Dates set from the book as ABT. The death year 1809 fits her being marked dead on the 1812-13 probate card of her husband. ¶2a

Life story

Probable baptism: 16 Mar 1732 at Svenes, 'Hælge Strands barn Aaste' (DA 9361 p. 37, canvas 18). It is the only 1732 baptism of an Aaste whose father is a Helge of a Strand farm. The other Strand Helge (Helge Knudsen of Midtstrand) had no Aaste in his 1739 probate. The link to the 1755 bride 'Aaste Helgesdatter Strand' is by name and farm across 23 years, so the identification stays Probable (it belongs to her father's grade; see Hølje (Helge) Tordsen). ¶4

Burial: 'Aaste Helgesd' of Fodnæs, aged 77 (b. about 1732), buried at Svennæs (Svenes) in May 1809, day not given in the index (Nord-Aurdal ministerialbok 1808-1815; index pg00000001088336). Name, farm and age match her 1732 baptism, her marriage to Uldrich Thommesen Fodnes and the bygdebok's '1732-1809'; she is one of only two Aaste Helgesdatters in the national burial index for those birth years. Replaces the bygdebok ABT 1809. ¶5

Confirmed at Aurdal on Quasimodogeniti 1751 as 'Aaste Helgesd: Strand', aged 22 (written; her 1732 baptism makes her 19), in the same group as her future husband 'Ulrich Thomesen Fodnæs', 18 (9361 confirmation section p. 14, canvas 373). This bridges her 1732 baptism to her 1755 marriage; her father is raised to Confirmed on it. Also in 1745 an 'Ingebor Helgesd: Strand', 17, was confirmed (p. 10, canvas 371): probably the elder sister whose name the bygdebok rebuild left unread (b. 1729). Her own grade, on her link to Kirsti, is unchanged. ¶6

Marriage read: Aurdal church book DA 9361 p. 100 (canvas 319, left page, last entry): at Svenes, betrothed 11 Aug 1755 'Ulrich Thommesøn Fodnes og Aaste Helgesdatter Strand', sureties [H...] Knudsøn Mittstrand and Thore Tor[d]søn[?] Strand; married 13 Oct 1755. This is the test record named on her father's entry: it confirms the bygdebok's marriage year 1755, her patronymic (Helgesdatter = Høljesdotter) and her home farm Strand. It does not name her father, so her parents stay Probable (patronymic plus residence only). ¶3

Probate (index card): wife of Uldrich Thommesen Fodnes, marked deceased (+) on the card for his estate (d. 21 Jul 1812, probate closed 16 Jan 1813; Valdres skifteprotokoll 1810-1814, fol. 306b). The children were Thommes, Helge, Erich and Kirsti, the widow of Juel Olsen Midstrand. Mother of Kirsti Uldriksdatter (Confirmed by the card's 'Barn' list). Her son Helge is named for her father, Helge. ¶1
