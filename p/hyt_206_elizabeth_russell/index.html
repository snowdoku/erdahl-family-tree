About the family

Her father is now added (William Russell, Probable) and her mother tentatively (Elizabeth Bleney, Speculative). The Russell of Strensham pedigree in the Worcestershire visitation (Harleian Society vol. 27, p. 119) has, in Richard Mundy's italic addition of after 1620, 'Elizebeth vx. Lewes Boyle of the Citty of Hereford a quo Boyle Earle of Cork', daughter of William Russell of co. Hereford and Elizabeth Bleney. That is the first statement of her parentage outside the Boyle peerage tradition, but it is still a compiled source. Her own grade (on her son Roger) is unchanged. Added. ¶3

Life story

Elizabeth, daughter of William Russell of Hereford, and wife of Lewis (Lodovic) Boyle of Bidney and of the Friars, Hereford (Lodge, Peerage of Ireland, 1789, vol. 1, p. 144: 'married Elizabeth, daughter of William Russel of the same [city], and had two sons, and one daughter'). Weaver's edition of the 1569 Herefordshire visitation adds her to the Boyle pedigree in editorial brackets, 'Eliz., d. of Wm. Russell of Hereford', from Robinson's Mansions and Manors of Herefordshire, p. 94. Her daughter Elinor married Watkins Russell, perhaps a kinsman. Added. ¶1

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the grade · 1 open question

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Probable, graded on her link to her son Roger Boyle. Lodge's wording makes her the mother of both sons, Roger included. Robinson's pedigree agrees on the marriage. Both are compiled works; the visitation MS itself does not name Lewis's wife, and no second wife is recorded anywhere. Her father William Russell is not added (Lodge and Robinson only, with nothing to place him). ¶2a

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Test record: the will of John Boyle of Hereford, 1551 (TNA PROB 11/34/206), for a mother or Russell kin; or a Hereford city deed of Lewis and Elizabeth Boyle. Added. ¶2b
