About the family

NEW ANCESTOR. Mother of Tellef Thorsen Tved (b. about 1728) and so the great-great-grandmother of Torjus Tellefsen Ramse. ¶1

Her parents are named on the archivist's cross-reference card to that 1761 settlement: "Konens far: Tergie Salwesen, søndre Askland i Gjøvdal, oppg. sk. 18/6-1743" and "mor: Margithe Evensdtr. oppg. sk. 18/6-1743" — her father Terje Salvesen of Søndre Askland in Gjøvdal and her mother Margithe Evensdatter, whose estates were both relinquished on 18 June 1743. ¶4

Her sons by Thore Tellefsen were Tellef (1721-1739), Tellef 'den eldre' (baptised 26 July 1727), Niels (1731), Gunder (1735, died after 1740) and Tellef 'den yngre' (1739). All are full siblings, and the 1761 card calls those then alive 'konens barn i 1. ekteskap' (her children by her first marriage). ¶7

Life story

Married first Thore Tellefsen of Tveit in Tovdal, whose estate was settled 28 April 1740. The 1740 probate (Skifteprotokoll no. 10, folio 45b) names her as the widow and lists their four sons: Tellef the elder (12), Niels (10), Gunder (5) and Tellef the younger (1). ¶2

She married secondly Salve Gjermundsen, and had at least one further son, Tore Salvesen, who was 15 when the joint estate of Salve Gjermundsen and "hustru Torbong [Torborg] Torgiesdatter" was wound up on 9 October 1761 (Skifteprotokoll no. 12b, folio 446). Her son by the second marriage was named Tore after her first husband. ¶3

Second marriage: 'D. 2 Jan. i Topdal cop: Salve Giermundsøn og Torbor Tergiesdatter Tved' (2 Jan 1742; Åmli register 1741-1756, DA 9921 canvas 2, read on the scan). It dates her remarriage, 20 months after Thore Tellefsen's estate of Apr 1740. On the 1743 chronology flag: the 'Birgithe the younger, 2' of the 1743 oppgitt skifte is a misreading; the protocol reads 20, so her siblings were born from about 1700 to 1727 and her own birth by about 1702 stands. ¶9

Her name explains her granddaughter's: Thor Tellefsen (1760-1800) and Birgitte Terjesdatter had no daughter Torbor, but the Terje/Tergie element recurs through the family. ¶5

Corrections 2

Correction: the birth date is changed from ABT 1705 to between 1695 and 1702 (inferred). She married Thore Tellefsen on 2 July 1720, so she was born by about 1702. Her son Tore Salvesen was 15 in October 1761 (born about 1746), so she was born no earlier than about 1695. ¶6

Correction, flagged not changed: her parents' oppgitt skifte of 18 Jun 1743 (Skifteprotokoll nr. 10 fol. 356a) lists her as wife of Salve Giermundsøn at Tveit in Lille Tovdal, among siblings aged 23 (Neri), 16 (Even) and 2 (Birgithe the younger), and her mother Margithe Evensdatter was the sister of a boy of 16 in 1724. A birth of 1695-1702 is therefore probably too early; about 1715-1722 fits the family better. Check her own records before changing the date. ¶8
