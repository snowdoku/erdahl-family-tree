About the family

Wife of Bersvend Olsen of Fuglem (see his page), married on the 4th Sunday after Trinity, 14 June 1761 (Selbu ministerialbok 1757-1801, canvas 11 right page, as 'Ane Olsd.'). Named as mother of Marit (16 August 1761), Kirsti (1763), Ane (1766) and Brynild (29 January 1775); churched as Bersvend Olsen Fuglum's wife about 26 February 1775 (Selbu klokkerbok 1766-1776, canvas 116). ¶1

Mother of Marit Bersvendsdatter (see her page), rated Probable; the argument, the rivals and the records that would confirm it are on Bersvend Olsen. Marit named her only known daughter Anne (1799), which agrees, though Anne was also the name of her husband's mother (Ane Gundersdatter). Her birth, parents and death are not found. ¶2

Life story

Bygdebok (Selbu i fortid og naatid vol. 3, Fuglem chapter p. 218; word boxes only): 'G.1761... Anne... 1740,... 1800.' - married in 1761, born 1740, died 1800, in the holder block of an Olsen of Fuglem (1761-1785, born 1737, died 1805) whose children are Marit 1761, Anne 1766 and Brynhild 1775, matching her register children. Birth 1740 and death 1800 rest on the book alone (Probable); her place of origin, in an unqueried gap, is not known. Her death before 1801 agrees with Bersvend Olssen being a widower in the 1801 census. ¶4a

Burial found: entered under Midfaste Sunday 1800 (c. 23 Mar 1800): 'L: J: p: og jordfæstet Qvinden, Anne Olsdatter Fuglem, død d. 31te Febr.: gl. 61 Aar' - funeral sermon and burial of the woman Anne Olsdatter Fuglem, died '31 Feb.' (a clerk's slip, so late February or early March 1800), aged 61, which puts her birth about 1738-39. This confirms the bygdebok's death year of 1800 and agrees with her husband Bersvend Olsen being a widower in the 1801 census. Selbu ministerialbok 16311, canvas 254 (book p. 250). Her parents are still unknown. ¶5

Birth estimate (overnight sweep F): her burial entry under Midfaste Sunday 1800 (already on her record) gives 'gl. 61 Aar', so she was born about 1738-39; entered as ABT 1739. The Selbu bygdebok's birth year 1740 (vol. 3 p. 218) agrees within a year. No change to her grade (Probable, on her link to Marit Bersvendsdatter). ¶7

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 alternative set aside · 1 search with no result · 1 open question

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Not the same woman as the three Anne Olsdatters on other lines that share her name: Anne Olsdatter (line 1) was born about 1760 and married Thomas Andersen in Stjørdal in 1794; Anne Olsdatter (line 3) married Peder Olsen Giske-Ødegård in Borgund, Sunnmøre, about 1759-60 and bore a son there in July 1760; Anne Olsdatter (line 3) was the wife of Anders Olsen Vike in Veøy, Romsdal, with a son born about 1784. This Anne was in Selbu, married to Bersvend Olsen from 1761 and still his wife in 1775. ¶3

Searches that found nothing 1

Probate search, negative: Stjør- og Verdal skifteprotokoll 11, 1796-1802 (24764), register. The 'Anne Aulsd.' row on the A page reads 'Anne Auls d. Schnan[?]', which is not Fuglem. The B-page row 'Birsvend... af Salboe, fol. 98' is an estate of 8 Dec 1797 after a Bersvend whose heirs include Thomas and Erich Bersvendsen and Berethe Bersvendsdatter, not Fuglem (fol. 98b, view 117). No estate for Anne Olsdatter or Bersvend Olsen of Fuglem appears on the A or B pages. ¶6
