Life story

Minnesota death index: born 9 May 1908 in Minnesota, 'child of KULSETHMO (mother)', died 9 September 1999 in Olmsted County. SSN [number withheld] (Minnesota); her Social Security claim was filed at Rochester, Minnesota on 26 February 1973. Last ZIP [withheld] (Rochester). ¶1

Social Security, retirement claim: BENDA E. EIDEM, b. 9 May 1908, filed a life claim at the ROCHESTER, MINNESOTA field office in 1973. NARA AAD, NUMIDENT Claim Files (Last Names D-G), rid 1352244. ¶2

Social Security death record: BENDA E EIDEM, b. 9 May 1908, d. 9 Sep 1999, residence ZIP [withheld], which is ROCHESTER, Olmsted County, Minnesota. Posted 14 Sep 1999. NARA AAD, NUMIDENT Death Files (Last Names E-G), rid 311593. Both dates match the tree exactly and both confirm the Olmsted County residence. ¶3

Life: 'Benda earned a teaching degree and taught school all her working life, the last couple of decades in Rochester, MN. Benda was a talented, wonderful woman who treasured her family history. She wrote to relatives in Norway every year, since her childhood. She was fluent in Norwegian and English; her parents both immigrated from Norway. Benda never married. She cared for her half-sister, Oline, for 20 years; and their mother previous to that, until each died.' The half-sister is Oline Klegseth Eidem Rask (1892-1993), a child of their mother's first marriage. ¶5

Born at Marietta, Lac qui Parle County, Minnesota, 9 May 1908; died at Rochester, Olmsted County, Minnesota, 9 September 1999. Buried Zoar Lutheran Cemetery, lot 15, with her father Anders and with Beret Kulsethmo. The South Dakota state register gives her death as 14 September 1999, which is the burial rather than the death; Find a Grave and the family give 9 September. ¶6

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 correction

Corrections 1

Correction: her father was ANDERS ANDERSSON EIDEM (1852/3-1932), not Mikal Paulsen Eidem. Find a Grave memorial 139948277 names her parents as Anders Andersson Eidem and Brynhild Kulsetmoen Eidem, and its biography begins 'Benda was the youngest of three children born to Brynhild and Anders Eidem at the family farm close to Marietta, MN.' ¶4
