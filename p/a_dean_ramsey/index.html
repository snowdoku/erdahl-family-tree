About the family

He is named in both his parents' obituaries among those who predeceased them — his father's of 1975 refers to "an infant son, Dean" and his mother's of 1992 to "son Dean". He was born between the 1920 and 1930 censuses and so appears in neither. ¶2

He carried his father's middle name, Ulysses. ¶3

Life story

Third child of Theodore Ulysses Ramsey and Wilma Almira Nelson. He died at three months old; his gravestone at Beaver Creek Cemetery records the age as "3 months", which places his birth in March 1927. ¶1
