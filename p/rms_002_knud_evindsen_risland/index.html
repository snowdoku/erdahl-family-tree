About the family

Family: Brother of Svend Evensen Ramse, and so a son of Even (Evind) Tellefsen Ramse. The archivist's card for the 1712 Ramse nedre ytre estate (Skifteprotokoll no. 4, fol. 41) gives "Mannens far: Even Tollefsen Ramse" and "Mannens bror: Knud Evindsen Risland + sk. 1725" — the husband's brother Knud Evindsen of Risland, deceased, whose estate was settled in 1725. ¶1

Siblings: The 1725 card names his sister Signe Evindsdatter, married to Joen Aslaksen Gunleifjeld (estate 1720), and his brother Svend Evensen Ramse (estate 1696). A man married to his brother's daughter, Tiøstel Olsen Løvrach, was present. The 1715 card names another brother, Thellef Evindsen of Oustad, who sold him land in Risland in 1693-1706. Because Knud left children, his siblings were not his heirs, and the card does not say whether they were full or half siblings. It does not settle which marriage he or Svend came from. ¶4

First wife's estate read (Skifteprotokoll 4 fol. 247b-254b): held at Risland 28 January 1715 after Guroe Nielsdatter; children Evind 23, Groe 14, Birgitha 13 and Niels 7, all at home with their father. Among his title deeds is a "skiftebrev imellem S. Evind Ramses børn de dato 27 Maij 1684", cited only with riksdaler values and not copied, so it does not name Evind's children or their mothers. Deed no. 7 is a sale to him by Thellef Evindsen of Oustad dated 12 November 1706, of odel land in Risland, apparently the share inherited by Thellef's wife Anna Niels[?]datter (reading uncertain). ¶5

Life story

1666 vicar's census of Lille Topdal (Sogneprestenes manntall 1664-66 bd 14, p.46), farm no. 3 'Ytre Ramsøa Nedra, 4 huder': opsiddere Effuind 80 aar (uses 3 huder) and Tollef 30 aar (uses 1 hud); sons column, against Effuind: Svend 10 aar and Knud 4 aar; servants: Throen Rasolfsøn 26, [Hal?]da 18[?], Rolff 26[?16]. Knud, aged 4 (born about 1662), is listed as a son at Nedre Ramse under Effuind. Identification with Knud Evindsen Risland is probable: brother Svend beside him, father Effuind; patronymic not written. Risland 1664/65 (fogd, DA 35567 view 54) has no Knud. ¶6

Dates (inferred): Dying with an estate in 1725, he was probably born about 1645-1665. His mother is not stated; a birth in that window fits Even's second marriage, to Anna Evindsdatter, better than the first, but no card says so. His 1725 estate settlement should name his heirs, including any surviving half-siblings, and could show which marriage each of Even's children came from. ¶2

Estate: Settled 19 July 1725 (Østre Råbyggelag Skifteprotokoll 7, fol. 183; probate cards 39942/11153 and 11155). The card gives "Avd's far: Even Tollefsen Ramse". Widow Sigri Aslaksdatter of Epletveit, whose own estate was settled 20 September 1758; she later married Tellev Davidsen Gjeveland. His first wife was Guri (Guroe) Nielsdatter, whose estate was settled 28 January 1715 (Skifteprotokoll 4, fol. 247b). Children with Guri: Eiven, living at Fiøne in Øvre Telemark; Niels, 18 (estate 1737); Groe, married Eiven Tolfsen Hummedrum; Birgitha, dead, married Herjus Halvorsen Walleberg. Children with Sigri: Astri 8, later married Tergie Rolfsen Smørli; Svend 6, later at Tviland; Ane 4; Bergithe 1 month (estate 1740 at Tviland). Estate about 1,100 riksdaler. ¶3
