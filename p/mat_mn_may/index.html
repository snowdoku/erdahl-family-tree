Life story

Birth certificate: State of Minnesota, Division of Vital Statistics, Record of Birth no. 12791, County of Lac qui Parle, Township of Mehurin, registration district 27/2, no. in registration book 30. 'Full name of child: May Chrisine Eidem. Sex F. Number in order of birth 6th. Legitimate yes. Date of birth June 5, 1911. FATHER: John Eidem, post office Marietta, W, age at last birthday 34, birthplace Norway, occupation Farmer. MOTHER: full maiden name Lena Slind, post office Marietta, W, age at last birthday 30, birthplace Norway, occupation Housewife. Number of child of this mother 6th. Number of children of this mother now living 6.' Attended by Henry Duncan, who dated his return 13 June 1911; filed 28 June 1911 by C. B. Ufford, registrar, Marietta. ¶2

This certificate is where the mother's maiden name is spelled out as 'Lena Slind' - the pairing of the household name Lena with the surname Slind that resolves the 1902 and 1904 cards. ¶3

Censuses: in her father's Mehurin household in 1920 ('May', b. abt 1912) and 1930 ('May E Eidem', b. abt 1912), and gone by 1940 - so she married between April 1930 and April 1940 (to Herbert Gieseke, per her mother's 1955 obituary). She was born 5 June 1911, so the index years of 1912 are census-day arithmetic, not a contradiction. ¶8

Marriage and later life, from the same obituary: 'May 7, 1945, she married Herbert Gieseke in Marietta'. The couple lived in Mankato and in Billings, Montana, then farmed near Marietta for many years and owned and ran the Marietta Laundry and the Y-Go Lunchroom. Herbert died 13 September 1967. In 1971 she moved to Montevideo and worked at Lane's Superette until she retired in 1981; she moved to Detroit Lakes in 1982 and to Greenbush in 1984, where she lived in the Greenbush nursing home. She was survived by a son and a daughter, two grandchildren, her brothers Palmer (South Pasadena, California), Peder (Montevideo) and John A. (Marietta) and her sister Barbara Kyllo, and predeceased by her husband, her parents and her sisters Clara Jornlin, Olga Lerseth and Cora Peterson. ¶10

A daughter of John P. Eidem and Oline G. Slind who was unknown to this project. Her mother's October 1955 obituary names 'Mrs. Herbert Gieseke (May)' among the eight children, living in the Marietta vicinity. ¶5

Burial: Zoar Lutheran Cemetery, Antelope Valley Township, Deuel County, South Dakota, LOT 2 — South Dakota state burial register, 'MAY CHRISTINE GIESEKE 06-14-1987, lot 2, block 0'. Lot 2 is the old Eidem lot, holding Peder P. Eidem, Karen P. Eidem, Kari P. Eidem and Mrs. Marit E. Slind: she was buried among the pioneers rather than beside her own parents in lots 1 and 1A. Her husband Herbert H. Gieseke lies in lot 13, d. 13 September 1967. ¶6

Neither she nor her husband has a Find a Grave memorial. ¶7a

Obituary: Thief River Falls Times, Wednesday 17 June 1987, page 4, 'May Gieseke, 76'. She died on Sunday (14 June 1987, the date in the South Dakota burial register) at United Hospital, Grand Forks, North Dakota, a resident of Greenbush, Minnesota; the funeral was on Friday at 11 a.m. at Zoar Lutheran church in rural Marietta, the Rev. Bradley L. Danielson officiating, with interment in the Zoar church cemetery, and visitation was at the Gieseke-Eeg chapel in Greenbush. She is named as the daughter of John P. and Oline Slind Eidem, born in rural Marietta; she attended elementary school there and graduated from the West Central School of Agriculture at Morris. ¶9

Corrections 1

Correction: the obituary prints her birth as 'June 11, 1945', an obvious misprint - she was 76, and her Minnesota birth certificate gives 5 June 1911. The certificate is trusted for the date. The marriage year 1945 in the next paragraph is printed the same way and is not corroborated; it fits her absence from her father's household in 1940 only loosely (she may have been away at work), so it is recorded as the obituary states it and should be checked against the Lac qui Parle County marriage register. The earlier note's 'married between April 1930 and April 1940' was an inference from that 1940 absence and is superseded by the obituary unless the register says otherwise. The surname is spelled Gieseke throughout the printed obituary; the 'Gleseke' of the OCR text is a misreading. ¶11

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

What became of her is not established. She is not in the Minnesota death index under any surname with a 5 June 1911 birth date and a Slind mother, so she either died in Minnesota before 1955 (when that index begins to carry the mother's maiden name), died after 2001, or died outside the state. ¶4a
