About the family

Wife of Joen Olsen Midbø (see his page). Her probate at Midbø, Nissedal, 13 May 1756, divides her estate between 'hendes igienlevende Mand Joen Olsøn' and their nine children: Tore (eldest son), Jørgen 18, Helje 13, Ole 8, Halvor, Gunnil, Anne, Todne 10 and Aasild. See her husband's record for the full reading and the identity of the eldest son with Tore Joensen Midbø (see his page). ¶1
