About the family

Family: the 1762 skifte names 'hans Farbroder Ole Jonsen Sandwig' as the guardian of the son Joen, so the Ole Jonsen of Øster Sandvik (buried 1768 aged 44, so born about 1724) was this Ole's brother. Two brothers both named Ole Jonsen suggests half-brothers or a naming after a dead child; the reading 'Farbroder' is clear. Their father, a Jon, is not named. ¶5

Parents found: the skifte after Joen Povelsen Sandvik, 25 Feb 1728 (Stjør- og Verdal Skifteprotokoll 2a, 24750 fol. 117b), names the widow Marit Olsdatter and their children Ole Jonsen 12, Povel 8, Gunder 6, 'Ole Jonsen Junior' 3 and Ingebor. Ole, 12, born about 1716, is this Ole (buried 1767 aged 50); 'Ole Junior', 3, is his brother Ole Jonsen of Øster Sandvik (buried 1768 aged 44), the 'Farbroder Ole Jonsen Sandwig' of the 1762 skifte. So the two Oles were full brothers. Siblings: Povel (born about 1720), Gunder (about 1722) and Ingebor (about 1726-27). Paternal uncle: Peder Poulsen Sandvik. ¶7

Life story

Biography: on St Michael's Day (29 Sep) 1746 'Ole Sandwigens Kone Ingebor Michelsdatter' was churched in Selbu (klokkerbok 16315 canvas 91), so he was married to Ingebor Michelsdatter by early 1746 and a child was born to them in summer 1746 (baptism not yet found; it should be on 16315 canvases 88-90). His younger brother appears the same day as communicant 'Ole Junior Sandvig'. The Selbu books have a gap 1747-1756, so this is the last church record of the couple before the 1762 skifte. ¶8

Marriages: (1) Ingebor Michelsdatter, by about 1747, who died before 17 Jun 1762; (2) Kari Olsdatter, between 1762 and 1767, his widow in October 1767. ¶6

Probate, Skifteprotokoll 6-1 (24756) fol. 86b: 'Anno 1767 den 9de October blev paa Gaarden Sandvigen i Selboe... forretaget lovlig Skifte og Deeling efter den ved døden afgangne Mand Ole Jonsøn, imellem hans efterlevende Enke Kari Olsdatter, og de Arvinger hand efterlod sig, en Søn, nemlig: Jon Olsen [20?] aar gl.' The widow's guardian was Ole Flønes; the guardian or curator for the son Jon Olsen was Niels Belle. Lensmann Ole Thomassen was present, and the appraisers were Niels Belle and Halvor Thomassen Bugen. The entry also mentions a deed to Sandvigen of 4 Nov 1751. ¶1

He is the 'Ole Jonsen Sandvig' buried 18 Jun 1767 aged 50 (Selbu ministerialbok 16311 c21), so born about 1717. Not to be confused with the Ole Jonsen of Sandvik østre buried Jun 1768 aged 44 (probate in the same volume, fol. 126b and 134b). ¶2

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Why Probable as father of Jon Olsen Sandvik (see his page): the probate names one son, Jon Olsen, aged about 20, at Sandvik in 1767, which fits the Jon Olsen born 1747 who held Sandvik from 1768 (the year after this probate) until 1811. The other Ole Jonsen of Sandvik (d. 1768) also had a son Jon Olsen, but the typed register of Skifteprotokoll 7 (24758) lists 'Jon Olsen, ungkarl, Sandvik østre, 1777, fol. 556b', with a cross-reference to the 1768 skifte after Ole Jonsen in protocol 6, fol. 126 and 134. So that Jon died unmarried in 1777 and cannot be the Jon who lived to 1818. Neither the 1768 nor the 1777 entry was; reading them would make this Confirmed. ¶3

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed as the father of Jon Olsen Sandvik (b. about 1747). The skifte after his first wife Ingebor Michelsdatter, 17 Jun 1762 (24755 fol. 246a), names their one son, Joen Olsen, 14. His own probate of 1767 names one son, Jon Olsen, about 20. The rival Jon Olsen of Øster Sandvik, son of the other Ole Jonsen (probate begun 1 Sep 1768, 24756 fol. 126b: widow Gurue Pedersdatter, children Jon 16, Peder 8, Marithe and Ingri), died a minor and unmarried before the 1777 summer ting (endorsement, 24758 fol. 556b). The 1772 Slinde probate (24757 fol. 530) calls Ingeborg Olsdatter's husband 'Jon Olsen Sandvig'. All. ¶4
