About the family

Parents and wife of William: not found. The Bury calendar also has 'Howchin, Thos. (sen.)' of Rickinghall Superior in register 'Harold' (1620-23) (page image read, leaf n136); if William senior was born about 1585-1595 he could be a son of that Thomas or of the William Howchen with children at Rickinghall 1578-1591 (a William Howchin was buried there 18 Aug 1611), but nothing names his parents. The Norwich consistory will index 1550-1603 (archive.org indextowillsprov0000perc, full-text snippets) has no Houchin of Redenhall, Harleston or Rickinghall; FreeREG (Norfolk and Suffolk, Howchin fuzzy, baptisms 1590-1625) has no Jeremy and does not cover Redenhall or Rickinghall. Nothing entered. ¶9

Life story

Birth of the son: Sumner reports a deposition of 1665 in which Jeremiah gave his age as about 49 (born about 1616). Lead only, not entered: the History of the Houchin Family (p. 9) lists, from the register of Rickinghall Superior, Suffolk (about 12 miles from Harleston), a baptism '1 Dec. 1617 - Jeremy s. of William Houchin', and a burial 18 Aug. 1611 of a William Howchin there. The place does not match Harleston, and a second 'Jeremy s. of William Howchen' was baptised there in 1647, so the 1617 entry is not taken as the emigrant's baptism. FreeREG (searched 1 Nov 2026, Norfolk and Suffolk, Houchin/Howchin fuzzy, baptisms 1600-1625) does not cover Redenhall/Harleston or Rickinghall: no Jeremy found. ¶3

Place evidence (supporting only): Jeremiah Houchin's will of 1670 makes 'Mr John Westgate of Harleston, Norfolk' his executor for the English estate. A John Westgate and 'John Westgate his son' are named among the inhabitants of Redenhall with Harleston, Norfolk, in the feoffment of the Harleston chapel dated 4 Feb. 8 Charles I [1632/3], and the Redenhall overseers' accounts for 1639-40 pay 'Mr Keene for phisicke for goodwife Houchin' (Candler, Notes on the Parish of Redenhall with Harleston, 1896). So a Houchin household and the Westgates were both at Redenhall with Harleston in the 1630s. ¶6a

Deposition age read: Jeremiah's deposition about Gov. Endecott's estate is headed 'the testimony of Jeremy Howchin agead about forty nine yeers' and dated 29 Jan. 1664[/5] (Colonial Society of Massachusetts, Transactions, December meeting 1918, printed transcript). That gives a birth about 1615-16. The Rickinghall Superior baptism of 1 Dec 1617 would make him 47, which is within the usual looseness of 'about'. No contradiction, no proof. ¶8

Harleston context: Candler's Notes on the Parish of Redenhall with Harleston (1896) prints the feoffment of Harleston chapel dated 4 Feb 1632/3, whose feoffees include 'John Westgate, John Westgate his son' among the inhabitants of Redenhall with Harleston; one of them is very probably the 'Mr John Westgate of Harleston' whom Jeremiah Houchin made executor of his English estate in 1670. The overseers' accounts printed in the same book pay 'Mr Keene for phisicke for goodwife Houchin' in 1639-40, so a Houchin household was living in Redenhall with Harleston then. Neither entry names William. These support Harleston as the family's home but do not prove William lived there. ¶5

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 3

Graded on his link to Jeremiah Houchin. Three compiled works agree that Jeremiah Houchin, tanner of Dorchester and Boston (d. 1670), was 'the son of William Houchin of Harleston, Norfolk' (Knoff, Thirty-one English Emigrants, p. 131, FTS snippet: 'Jeremiah Houchin was the son of William Houchin of Harleston, Norfolk. According to a deposition...'). Sumner (Coltman ancestry, pp. 110-111, read) gives the basis: Jeremiah's will of 7 Apr. 1670, proved 31 May 1670, 'refers to his estate in England, given him by his father William Howchin at Harleston', and leaves a bequest to 'Mr. John Westgate of Harleston, Norfolk', who had charge of his business there. The History of the Houchin Family (p. 11, read) says the same ('He mentioned his father, William, of Harliston, Norfolk Co.'). ¶1

Why Probable, not Confirmed: the will itself was not read; the relationship comes from compiled abstracts of it. ¶2a

Upgrade: graded on his link to Jeremiah Houchin. Jeremiah's own will (7 Apr. 1670, proved 31 May 1670; attested copy, Suffolk Court Files vol. 36 case 3126, DGS 008293221 image 42, read) leaves son Nathaniel 'all my Lands, Housing and Estate due and appertaining unto me by Will and Testament of my deceased Father William Howchin left unto me by him'. The testator names his father. Place: the will does NOT say William lived at Harleston; it only appoints 'Mr John Westgate of Harleston in the County of Norfolk' executor for the English estate. Harleston as William's residence remains from compiled sources (Knoff p. 131; Sumner). William was dead by 7 Apr. 1670 and left a will (English probate not found). Test record for place: William Howchin's will in the Norwich consistory or Archdeaconry of Norfolk/Suffolk courts, c.1630-70. The earlier note that William is Probable because the will was read only in compiled abstracts is superseded. ¶4

Records that disagree 1

Against: Candler's Redenhall overseers' accounts pay for 'phisicke for goodwife Houchin' at Redenhall with Harleston in 1639-40, and the executor lived at Harleston, Norfolk (about 13 miles from Rickinghall). Neither place is proved; the Rickinghall identification is a candidate only. ¶7b

Alternatives considered and set aside 2

Caution: there is also a Harleston in Suffolk (near Stowmarket), where a William Houchin paid tax on 4 hearths in 1674 (Suffolk Hearth Tax 1674, snippet), so 'Harleston' alone does not fix the county; the will's 'Norfolk' does. The goodwife is not named, and no record ties her to William. ¶6b

Place, a rival home in Suffolk (free sources, 2 Nov 2026). Graded on his link to Jeremiah Houchin; grade unchanged (Confirmed, by Jeremiah's 1670 will naming 'my deceased Father William Howchin'). The residence 'Harleston, Norfolk' is from compiled works only (Knoff; Sumner; Houchin Family History), apparently inferred from the will's executor 'Mr John Westgate of Harleston in the County of Norfolk'. A Suffolk family fits at least as well: (1) Calendar of Wills at Bury St Edmunds 1520-1650 [and after], vol. 2 (archdeaconry of Sudbury), page image read (archive.org calendarofwillsa02sudb, leaf n288): register '1650 etc. "Ashton" (Part 2.)', fol. 587 'Howchin, Wm. (sen.)' of 'Rickinghall S.' [Superior], and fol. 595 'Howchin, John (sen.)' of Rickinghall S.; the nearby margin dates on that page are 1650-1652, so William senior's will was registered about 1652. Jeremiah's will says his English land came 'by Will and Testament of my deceased Father William Howchin', so his father left a will, as this man did. 'Senior' implies a William junior living in the parish c.1652. (2) Rickinghall Superior register extracts printed in History of the Houchin Family pp. 8-9 (compiled; register not seen): bp '1 Dec. 1617 - Jeremy s. of William Houchin'; bp '8 Mar. 1647 - Jeremy s. of William Howchen'; bp '5 Apr. 1663 - ffrancis s. of William Houchin'; bur. '18 Aug. 1611 - William Howchin'; earlier William Howchen children Margaret 1578, Robert 1590, Abigail (bur. 1591). A William junior naming a son Jeremy in 1647 fits a brother of an emigrant Jeremy. (3) Ship-money return for Rickinghall Superior, 1639-40 (Harl. MS, ed. Redstone, djvu text read): 'Houchin, Jn. 27s 10d', 'Houchin, Wm. 6s 8d', and unassessed 'Houchin, Jn., sen.', 'Houchin, Jas.'. (4) The family were TANNERS, Jeremiah's trade: Suffolk Archives FB121/G5/3, 2 Jun 1618, 'Ellen Crosse to Robert Howchin, tanner, of Rickinghall Superior' (TNA Discovery catalogue entry). (5) Chancery suits: C 8/136/11 (1659) Browne v Howchin, Anne Howchin widow, property in Rickinghall Superior; C 9/55/61 (1672) Howchin v Howchin, bill and answer, which may be the 1671 Suffolk suit that Houchin Family History pp. 6-7 says mentions witnesses 'in remote places beyond the Seas' (i.e. after Jeremiah's death, perhaps over Nathaniel's English inheritance). ¶7a

Searches that found nothing 1

Register search, negative (FamilySearch, 2 Nov 2026): the Rickinghall Superior/Inferior and Redenhall with Harleston registers are not in the FamilySearch indexes, and the catalogue has no Rickinghall films. No baptism of a Jeremy/Jeremiah Houchin/Howchin 1608-1622 is indexed anywhere in England, so the 'Jeremy s. of William Houchin, 1 Dec 1617' entry from the printed Houchin history cannot be checked on FamilySearch. Indexed Houchin/Howchin families of the period are in Essex (Bocking: Thomas, children 1583-1598, including a Jeremy bapt. 3 Nov 1583 who was buried 7 Aug 1589; Shalford; Copford; Bradwell-on-Sea, William and Mary, children 1627-1630) and none is tied to Harleston. ¶10a
