About the family

Mother of Jon Gulløvsen of Hjartholm (see his page), Confirmed: the probate calls the children hers and Gullov's together. Her eldest son was named Jon, after her father Jon (from her patronymic). Her own probate (after 1741, Ytre Sogn, Engesætter) and burial have not been searched. ¶2

Parents and dates (bygdebok, Probable): Brekke herred bygdebok vol. 2 (1944), Engesæter (canvas 551): 'Kirsti Jonsdtr. Nedre Haugland', d. 1747 aged 82 (b. about 1665; the rebuilt text also shows '1655', taken as a misread), daughter of Jon Toreson and Brita Larsdtr. of Nedre Haugland (bnr. 61). Children named: Agate 1694, Jon 1696 (Hjartholm bnr. 79, m. 1728), Kari 1697 (m. Ivar Torkelson), a child of 1700 (Hjartholm bnr. 81, m. 1739 the widow Brita Andersdtr.), Lars 1702 and Brita 1707. 'Enka Kirsti Jonsdtr. skiftet 22.. 1741' (the widow made a division in 1741, after her husband's death). Linked parents Jon Toresen and Brita Larsdatter. ¶3

Mother's probate read: the probate after Brite Larsdatter of Haugland, 1707 (Ytre Sogn 2b fol. 292a, view 6), names among her children 'Kirsti Gullach Engesettre' (Kirsti, wife of Gullach of Engesætter), with Jon, [..]sten, Lars, Brite and Eli. This confirms the bygdebok mother Brita Larsdtr. and shows Christi married to Gullach/Gullov by 1707. Her own grade (Confirmed, on her link to Jon Gulløvsen) is unchanged. ¶4

Life story

Named in the probate after Gullov [T?]ensen of Engesætter, 22 Aug 1741, as the surviving wife (the word before her name is read 'Qvinden' or 'Konen', uncertain) 'Christi Joens Datter', with 'deres sammen avlede Børn' (their jointly begotten children) Joen Hiertholm, To[e]r Hiertholm, Lars Engesætter, Agathe and Sirie. Alive on 22 Aug 1741. Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll 6a, No. 11, fol. 153a. ¶1
