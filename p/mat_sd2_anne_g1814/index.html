About the family

Eldest known child, baptised the third Sunday after Trinity 1814 and entered at page 207 serial 1 of the Selbu ministerialbok for 1801-1815, the father 'Gunder Jorgensen' of Slinden, the mother 'Berith Olsd.' Her baptism is the record that pushes her parents' marriage back before 1814. She was probably named for her paternal grandmother Ane Gundersdatter. A second Anne was baptised to the same couple in 1825, which usually means the first had died. ¶1
