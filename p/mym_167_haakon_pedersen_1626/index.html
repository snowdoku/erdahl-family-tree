Life story

Farmer of Holan (gnr. 24), Skatval. Bygdebok (Skatval vol. 1, canvas 132 left): 'Håkon Pedersen, [f.] 1626, [d.] 18.11.1718', wife 'Marit Larsdtr., [f.] 1635?, [d.] 25.12.1731'; children Peder (husmann on the farm), Ingeborg 1678 (m. Hans Olsen, Skatval 41), Lars 1683 (bruker), Anne 1684 (m. Halvor Olsen, 26), Mali (m. Greis Johnsen, 20). ¶1

1664-66 vicar's census, read on the scans: in 1665 (35528 view 37, Skatval p. 54) the second holding of 'Nederholum' (Nedre Holan, 2 spann 18 merker) has the user 'Amund 42' with the farmhand (knegt column) 'Hogen Pers. 30 aar'; in 1666 (view 87, p. 154, no. 145 Nederholum) the same holding has 'Amund 43' with 'Hogen Pers. 3[?]' in the servants column (second digit unclear, probably 1 or 2). So a Håkon Pedersen born about 1635 was a hired man at Nedre Holan in 1665-66, before he held the farm. No Peder is a user at any Holan holding in either year, so the lists do not show his father. This points to a birth about 1635 rather than the bygdebok's 1626, which would make him about 48 at Lars's birth in 1683 and close to the age the book gives his wife Marit Larsdatter ('1635?'). Not used to change the grade (still Probable on his link to Lars Håkonsen). ¶4

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
