About the family

Mother of Ane Tostensdatter, graded on that link: Confirmed. Her husband Torsten Iversen's probate (24646 fol. 615b, 18 Feb 1761, Høkset) was held between 'Enken Guri Olsd[atter]' and 'deres i Ægteskab avlede' children, among them the unmarried youngest daughter Anne Torstensdatter, who is followed to the wife of Endre Nielsen Grønset through consecutive Hoksæt records (see Ane Tostensdatter). The 1762 extra tax (47939 view 55, no. 52 Hoksæt) lists 'Enke Gurri' in her son Johannes Tostensen's household with Ane Tostensdatter. ¶1

Parents added, Probable on the bygdebok: Ole Halvorsen and Anne Sveinsdatter (see his page, Anne Sveinsdatter). The phrase 'Foreldra hennar var Ole Halvorson og Anne Sveinsdotter' was verified by exact-phrase content search on Gjemnes bind 7, canvas 576, so it is no longer a gap reading. Her own grade (link to Ane Tostensdatter) is unchanged. ¶3

Her parents are now proved by her father's probate, Nordmøre skifteprotokoll 8b (24642) fol. 543b, no. 59, 12 Sep 1742, Hjorten, Gjemnes: 'datteren Guri Olsdatter', married to Torsten Iversen Høgset, child of Ole Halvorsen and his earlier wife Anne Svendsdatter; half-brothers Halvor Olsen (5) and Tron Olsen (2). Through her mother she was half-sister of Ole Toresen (Høgset), Svein, Dorthe and Gunhild Toresdatter of Hjorten (Gjemnes bygdebok bind 8, canvas 29). ¶6

Life story

Bygdebok (Gjemnes bind 7, Høgset chapter, canvas 576, rebuilt text, gaps): Guri Olsdotter died in 1771, 70 years old; her parents are given as Ole Halvorson and Anne Sveinsdotter, and Anne Sveinsdotter was also the mother of Ole Toreson, brukar 4 on Høgset c. 1710-31, who in 1732 'fekk arv etter mor si, Anne Sveinsdotter'. So Guri was Ole Toreson's half-sister. Not added as parents: bygdebok only, gaps in the rebuilt text. ¶2a

Her probate (she died 1771 per the bygdebok) is not under G in the typed register of Nordmøre skifteprotokoll nr. 13, 1766-1772 (24648, view 7: the Gjemnes G entries are Gurie Endresdatter Ødegaarden fol. 79b, Gunnild Andersdatter Wrvang, Guraa Johnsdatter Ørsetli fol. 739a), nor in 24649 (1772-76). The same register's E page (view 6) has 'Elling Bottolvsen Høgseth i Gjemnes fol. 154a' (about 1767), the only Høgset entry: unread lead (perhaps the husband of her daughter Ingebor Torstensdatter). ¶5
