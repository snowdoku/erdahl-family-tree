Life story

1701 manntall: at Indre Oppedal (Indr. Oppedale, a Bondegaard, that is a farm held by peasants) the census of men and boys lists 'Iver Gutorms: 30' (Iver Gutormsen, 30), with no sons. The birth ABT 1671 is inferred from that age. That he is the Iver Gutormsen of Indre Oppedal of 1706-1712 is an inference from name and farm; other men named Iver Gutormsen lived nearby (one at Øvridale[?], 30, and a soldier at Hestefiord[?], 26, in the same census, and a groom of Sæter[?] in 1706). ¶1

1706: his wife Kari Andersdatter was churched (introduceret, the mother's first return to church after a birth) at Brekke in 1706 after a boy named Lars: 'Introduceret Ifver Gutermsøn Inderoppedals qvinde Kari Anders-datter efter et drengebarn N: Lars' (Gulen 11435 c33, left page). So he was married before 1706. The marriage of 'Ifver Gutermsøn Sæter' and Giertrud Hansdatter, entered just above, is another man. ¶2

Godparent, 26 Sep 1711 (11436 c27,; year chronology, read as 1712): 'Iver Gutormsøn ibid' (of Indre Oppedal) stood godparent, with Gutorm of Indre Oppedal, to Lasse, child of Grimmund[?] of Indre Oppedal. Godparent, 19 Sep 1712 (11436 c41 left page): 'Iver Guttormsøn ibid' stood godparent, with 'Jon Guttormsøn ibid', to Anna, daughter of Mons Johannessen and Randi Iversdatter of Indre Oppedal. ¶4

Churching, 2 Jan 1718: at the service in Brekke church on 2 Jan 1718: 'Introduceret Ifver Gutormss: Inderopdalens qv[in]de Kari, efter at [hun fø]dt[?] barn N: Lasse' (churched the wife Kari of Iver Gutormsen of Indre Oppedal after a child named Lasse). So Kari was alive on 2 Jan 1718 and had borne a son Lasse in late 1717; the son Lars of 1706 had probably died, since the name was reused. Another son of his, Lasse, born late 1717, is added to the children named in the records (not entered as a person). ¶7

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

4 alternatives set aside

Alternatives considered and set aside 4

Children (named in the records; not entered as persons except Torkel): Lars, born about 1706 (churching, 11435 c33); Torkel (Torchel), baptised at Brekke on 22 Dec 1708 (11436 c3); Anders, baptised at Brekke on 10 Apr 1711 (the year of 11436 c23 is contested: 1711 by its margin and the feast sequence,; 1712), mother Kari Andersdr., godparents Iver Hansøn, Hendrich, Hans, Randi Ifversdatter and Kari[?] Jonsdatter, all of Indre Oppedal (11436 c23); probably died young (inference from the name being reused); a second Anders, baptised at Brekke on 11 Nov 1712, mother Karj, godparents Guttorm of Indre Oppedal, Iver Hansøn and Mons of the same farm, Giri[?] and Ragnilde of Ytre Oppedal[?] (11436 c42 left page; the child's name read with medium confidence). ¶3

Do not confuse: Iver Guttormsen of Engeset (buried 30 Apr 1785, born about 1740; probate 18 Oct 1785 names his brothers Hendrich Guttormsen of Indre Oppedal and Anders Guttormsen) is a man of a later generation. Death of this Iver: not found; no probate after him has been searched. ¶6

Do not confuse: a 'Kari Andersdr: Indreopdal', aged 39, buried at Brekke on 10 Dec 1717 (11438 c20 left), is not proved to be his wife: his wife Kari was churched on 2 Jan 1718. See the note on Kari Andersdatter. ¶8

1664 census (not linked): Indre Oppedal in the bailiff's census of 21 Jun 1664 has two adult Guttorms, Guttorm (Colbensen), 40, son of the farmer Colben, and the husmann Guttorm Olsen, 42; elsewhere in Brekke sogn are Guttorm Lassesen 14 (Hudisdall), Guttorm Magnussen 18 (Wirckeland) and the servants Guttorm Haldorsen 18 (Asseim), Guttorm Bendixsen 14 (Asseim) and Guttorm Aslaksen 15 (Ytre Takle) (transcription). Iver, born about 1671, was not yet born. Guttorm Colbensen, son of a farmer on the farm where Iver later farmed, is the likelier father, but with Guttorm Olsen on the same farm and no Colben among Iver's known children (Lars, Torkel, Anders, Lasse) no father is entered. The 1701 manntall of Indre Oppedal (index, 17 rows) has no Guttorm; a Gutorm of Indre Oppedal stood godfather beside Iver in 1711 (11436 c27), probably a brother or kinsman rather than his father. ¶10
