About the family

Parentage: probate no. 46 (25 Sep 1779) names 'ældste Datter Anna Iversdatter, gift med Johannes Herløffsen … Ytre Takle' (eldest daughter Anna Iversdatter, married to Johannes Herløffsen of Ytre Takle) among Iver Monsen's children by his first marriage. Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll A 9b, p. 1044. ¶1

Mother: not linked. Iver's first wife was probably Lisbeth Eilevsdatter (see Iver's notes), but Anna, born about 1728, may be older than that marriage, which is known only from 1735. ¶2

Mother: Lisbeth Eilevsdatter (see her page), Iver Monsen's first wife. The probate after Lisbeth at Botten on 8 Oct 1739 (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll 5, Nr. 78, fol. 125a) names 'Datter Anna' among the minor children she had with Iver Monsøn Botten ('deres sammenavlede Børn'), beside Endre and Eli, who are again Anna's full siblings of the first marriage in 1779. Anna, born about 1728, was about 11 and a minor in 1739, which fits. She is not a daughter of Iver's second wife Lisbet Rasmusdatter, who married him after Oct 1739. (Supersedes the note 'Mother: not linked... may be older than that marriage'; no mother link was set before, so nothing had to be removed.) ¶7

Grandparents: through her father Iver Monsen she is probably a granddaughter of Mons Johansen and Randi Iversdatter of Indre Oppedal (see Iver's parentage note). ¶8

Life story

Burial (per index): 'Anna Iversdatter', Kone, of Yttre Takle, aged 75 (b. about 1728), buried 3 Jan 1803 at Brekke. Gulen church book 1798-1808, burials 1803 (index pg00000000770768). The 1801 census gave her 73, about two years off. Identity Probable (name, farm, age). The scan was not read. ¶6

Birth estimate (inference): her parents' earliest child found is Sijnneve, baptised at Eivindvik on 23 Oct 1730 and not living in 1739 (Gulen ministerialbok 11440, canvas 24, right page), and her mother was churched ('Introduceret') in Mar 1734 (canvas 64, right page). With her mother apparently unmarried in Jun 1730, Anna was most likely born 1731-1734, not about 1728; the 1801 and 1803 ages are probably 3-5 years too high. Her baptism (Gulen ministerialbok 11440, 1731-1734; check first the weeks before the Mar 1734 churching) would settle it. ¶9

Widow: in the probate after Johannes Herløvsen of Ytre Takle (25 Sep 1784) she is his widow, 'Enke Anna Ivers-Datter', with two sons and two daughters: Iver Johannessen 19, Anders Johannessen 11, Sigri Johannesdatter (married to Ole Torbjørnsen of Ytre Takle) and Lisbeth Johannesdatter 7. ¶3

1801 census: 'Anna Iversdatter, Hustruens moder, 73, Enke' (the wife's mother, a widow, 73) in Ole Torbjørnsen's household at Ytre Takle, Brekke. The age gives a birth about 1728. That would make her about 49 at the birth of her daughter Lisbeth (about 1777), so the census age may be too high. ¶4

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Confidence: Confirmed. Her parentage and her marriage are stated in two probates read from the scans (1779 and 1784), and the 1801 census places her with her daughter Sigrid. ¶5

Current grade (from patch 028, audit): both parent links are Confirmed. Her father Iver Monsen of Botnen (see his page) is named in his division of 1779 (probate no. 46) as her father. Her mother Lisbeth Eilevsdatter (see her page) is proved by the probate after Lisbeth of 8 Oct 1739 (Nr. 78), which names 'Datter Anna' among the children she had with Iver, and by the 1779 division, which again puts Anna among the first-marriage children; audit passed the grade as Confirmed. The note 'Mother: not linked' is superseded (see 'Mother '). Its doubt about her birth before the marriage is also answered: puts her birth at about 1731-1734. ¶10
