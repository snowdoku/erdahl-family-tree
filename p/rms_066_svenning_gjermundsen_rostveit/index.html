About the family

NEW ANCESTOR. Probate card read on the scan: 'Austre Robyggjelaget Skifteprotokoll nr. 3. Rostveit i Åmli. Fol. 223. 26/5-1712. Svening Giermundsøn †, g.m. Øli Sveningsdtr. †(1690). Børn: 1. Knud Sveningsøn Rostved. 2. Anna Sveningsdtr. (g.m. Ole Ormsøn Eppeland). [later hand:] Oppg. sk. 1747. Jordegods ½ ksk. i Rostveit søre.' The card refers to (1) a contract between the late Svening Giermundsøn and his son Knud Sveningsøn dated 3 Dec 1686, (2) a settlement (forlik) between Knud Sveningsøn and Joen Gundlefield about the land in Rostveit dated 15 Nov 1697, and (3) the skiftebrev after the late Øli Sveningsdatter dated 29 Oct 1690 (cards 11385, 11387). ¶1

Children: Knud Svenningsen Rostveit (wife Tørbør Eilefsdatter, whose estate was also settled 26 May 1712, Skp. 3 fol. 245, card 11389; their daughter Tørbør m. Torbjørn Ulvsøn Rostveit) and Anna Svenningsdatter, wife of Ole Ormsen of Eppeland. ¶3

Brother (probable): a Tallak Gjermundsen lived on Rostveit at the same time: 'Thallach Giermundsøn 31 aar' in the 1664 bailiff's census, and in the 1666 vicar's census 'Tallack [G/B]iermundsøn er 35 Aar, bruger 5 kalfskind' as a holder beside Svenning, each using 5 kalfskind of the farm. Two men of about the same age sharing the holding, one of them a Gjermundsen, suggests brothers who inherited a father's share (inferred; the lists state no relationship). Tallak had no sons in 1666. ¶6

Wife's family: the censuses do not list women. No older Svenning (a possible father of his wife Øli Svenningsdatter) lives at Rostveit in 1664-66, so she was probably not a daughter of the farm (inferred). ¶7

Life story

1664-66 censuses: Svenning is the first holder of Rostveit in all three lists: 28 in the 1664 vicar's census (p.7, 'Suenning 28 aar'), 30 in the 1664 bailiff's census (p.11), and 32 in the 1666 vicar's census (p.39, 'Suenning 32 Aar, bruger 5 kalfskind'), with a son 'Knud er 6 Aar' (b. about 1660). The son Knud fits Knud Svenningsen Rostveit, to whom Svenning Gjermundsen handed land by contract in 1686, so this is the same man (inferred: same farm, same name, a son Knud of the right age). Birth revised from BEF 1650 to ABT 1635 on these ages. No patronymic is written for him in these lists. ¶5

1666 census: Omblie sogn no. 19, Rostveit (3 huder): 'Svenning 32 aar, bruger 5 kalvskinn', son Knud 6; also on the farm 'Tallack Giermundsøn, 35 aar' (DA 35510 view 30, p. 39, read on the scan). Svenning's age gives a birth about 1634, close to his estimate of about 1635. Tallak Gjermundsen, with the same patronymic on the same farm, is a likely brother (not stated). ¶8

Birth is estimated: he handed land to his adult son Knud by contract in 1686, so he was born before about 1650. His patronymic makes his father a Gjermund (not identified). ¶2

Protocol entry read: Skifteprotokoll nr. 3 fol. 223a, 'Anno 1712 d. 26 May' at Rostved in Omblie parish, registration and valuation of the estate after the late 'Svening Giermundsøn' between the heirs Knud Svenningsøn Rostved and Anne Svenningsdatter, whose husband is Ole Ormsøn Eppeland. The entry lists among the documents the contract of 3 Dec 1686. This is the primary record behind cards 11385/11387, and it confirms the card's reading of the heirs. The scan does not name Svenning's own parents. ¶4
