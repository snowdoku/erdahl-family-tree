About the family

Her own sons were born in IDAHO - LaVerne Hubert Dalberg at Kendrick in 1917 and Glen Howard Dalberg at Deary in 1923, both in LATAH COUNTY, which adjoins Whitman County, Washington across the state line. So a second, unrelated Slind family also ended up on the Idaho side of the same wheat country. Recorded to keep the two apart, not because a connection is claimed. ¶2

His son was JOHN MARTIN SLIND (Aug 1872 - 1 Sep 1929), who married MARIE E. LEE: their daughter Jeanette Slind was born at Spokane, Washington in 1907 (Spokane County Auditor birth returns, which name both parents), and the Minnesota birth index then records six more children of a Slind father and a LEE mother born in Hennepin County between 1913 and 1934 - Johan Helmer 1913, Leslie A. 1914, Gladys Olive Bernice 1916, Shirley 1919, an unnamed child 1922, Orvin Lea 1928 and Richard Lee 1934. So this family's track is Windom (Jackson Co., Minnesota) to Deary (Latah Co., Idaho) to Spokane to Minneapolis - a THIRD distinct American Slind lineage that must be kept apart from the Lac qui Parle and the Whitman County families, and which also ended up on the edge of the same Palouse wheat country. ¶4

Life story

Head of a THIRD, separate Minnesota Slind family, at WINDOM, JACKSON COUNTY - a hundred and thirty miles south-east of Lac qui Parle and nothing to do with the Marietta colony as far as any record shows. His daughter PETRA SLIND DALBERG, b. 23 January 1896 at Windom, Jackson County, Minnesota, filed a Social Security application (NUMIDENT SS-5, SSN XXX-XX-6288, cycle 1940) naming FATHER: JOHN J SLIND, MOTHER: MARY OVERSON. ¶1

Find a Grave 24241547: "John J. Slind, 11 Sep 1843 - 15 Oct 1925, Bethany Cemetery, Deary, Latah County, Idaho". Four Slinds lie in that one Idaho churchyard: with him, John Martin Slind (Aug 1872 - 1 Sep 1929), Edin J. Slind (25-27 Feb 1906) and Kelmar Laddie Slind (16 Sep - 5 Oct 1916). ¶3
