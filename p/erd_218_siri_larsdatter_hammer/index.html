About the family

New mother of Joen Joensen Kleven (see his page), graded on her link to him: Probable. The probate card (77804 image 665) for the skifte of 11 Mar 1738 after Joen Olsen Hammer names 'Enke: Siri Larsdtr. Hammer' and the children Ole 12, Lars 8, Joen 6 (guardian Joen Kleven) and Guru 3; the bygdebok (Hegra B. 2, canvas 401) has 'Gift 1724 m. Sigrid Larsdtr.' and the same children. The 1724 marriage covers all four children (b. about 1726-35), so she is the mother of each. Probable for the same reason as her husband: the 6-year-old Joen of Hammer is identified with the tree's Joen Joensen Kleven across farms and 19 years. ¶1a

Life story

The probate protocol entry itself: Stjør- og Verdal skifteprotokoll 2C (Digitalarkivet 24752) fol. 771b, view 33 left, headed 'N:o 11... 1738'. The district judge Laurits Bruun records a skifte held on 17 Mar 1738 (the card's 11/3 is probably the registration date) at the dragoon quarter 'Gaarden Hammer udi Stiørdals Præstegield, Hegre annex', under Obristl. Motz's regiment, after the late dragoon 'Joen Olsen Hammer', 'imellem hans efterladte Enke Siri Lars datter Hammer og deres tilsammen avlede nu i Live værende 4 Børn': A. Ole Joensen 12, B. Lars Joensen 8, C. Joen Joensen 6, D. Guru Joensdatter 3. The widow chose as her guardian her sister's husband ('hendes Søsters Mand', name not securely read). The registration on the same folio is signed by Captain P. Zincke, with appraisers A. Eeg, Anders Davidsen Frøyum and Mads Bang. The division (fol. 772, view 33 right) gives the widow half (41 rdl.) and each child about 11 rdl. The guardians (fol. 773a, view 34 right) are a man named as 'den afg. Mands Moster Mand' (the deceased's mother's sister's husband) for Ole; Mads Bang for Lars; 'Joen Kleven[?], den afg. Mands Søskende Barn' (the deceased's first cousin) for Joen Joensen; and Joen Kaall[?] for Guru. This settles the audit-8 rule: the protocol says 'deres tilsammen avlede' Børn, so Siri Larsdatter is the mother of all four, Joen Joensen included. ¶2

The 1757 marriage in Stjørdal 16371, marriage section p. 322 (canvas 324 right, top): 'eodem Die et loco trolovet Ungkarl og Dragoun af Liv Compagniet Jon olsen[?] Hamer, som haver... Dragoun Qvarteret Kleven og havde Obriste-Lieutenant Møllerops Tilladelse at ægte Pigen Siri Svendsdatter Kleven'; forlovere Haagen Hamer and Baar Jonsen Øyen; 'Copuleret ved Værnes d. 30te Junij' [1757]. So the dragoon who took over the Kleven dragoon quarter and married Siri Svendsdatter Kleven came from Hammer, and a Hammer man stood surety. This is the couple of the 1761 probate (Sirie Svendsdatter, wife of Joen Joensen, Stor Kleven). Contradiction, unresolved: the groom's patronymic in the 1757 entry reads 'olsen' (lower-case o, not the J of 'Jonsen' written by the same hand in 'Baar Jonsen'), although every later record (the 1761 and 1810 probates, the 1765 marriage and the 1801 census) calls him Joen Joensen. It is probably a slip (his father was Joen Olsen), but under the audit-8 rule a noted contradiction blocks the raise until it is resolved. ¶3a

No grade change: see Joen Olsen. The 1760 baptism of Jon Kleven's son Jon has 'ole Hamer, Lars ibid' as godfathers, consistent with her sons Ole and Lars (1738 probate) standing for their brother's child, but the register names no relationship. ¶5a

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Grade unchanged: Probable, graded on her link to Joen Joensen Kleven (see his page). Lead for her own parents (not entered): the next protocol entry, No. 12 (fol. 773a, view 34 right), is the skifte of 17 Mar 1738 after Lars Pedersen of Hammertrøen, a cottage under Hammer, with widow and children. A Lars of a Hammer cottage dying the same day is a natural candidate for her father. Read that entry's heirs before entering him. ¶3b

Searches that found nothing 1

Negative: the probate No. 12 on the same day, 17 Mar 1738, after Lars Pedersen Hammertrøen (24752 fol. 773a, view 34 right) is read. It is held 'imellem hans efterladte' widow Ingeborg [..]sdatter 'og deres tilsammen avlede og nu i Live værende 2de Børn: A. Peder Larsen 3 1/2 aar gl., B. Niels Larsen 1 1/2 aar gl.', with the children's farbror Lars Pedersen as guardian. No adult daughter Siri, and no first-marriage children are named, so this Lars Pedersen (whose children were born about 1734-36) is not Siri Larsdatter's father. Her father is still unknown. ¶4
