Life story

Probate (skifte) after her, Stjør- og Verdal Skifteprotokoll 2a (24750) fol. 366-366b (typed front register 'Giertrud Iversdatter "Avildgård", Evind Eriksens hustru, Garberg, Selbu, 1731, fol. 366'): 'Anno Christi 1731 den 15 Feb.' a skifte was concluded in Selbu parish 'efter afg. Giertrud Iver datter imellem hendes efterladte Mand Evind Erichsen Avildgaard boende paa huusmands pladzen under Garberg' and 'deres tilsammen avlede 4re Børn: 1. Peer Evindsen [5?] aar, 2. Erich Evindsen 11 aar, 3. Anders Evindsen 1 aar... og 4de Guru Evindsdatter 19 aar gl.' The digit of Peer's age is uncertain: it reads most like '13' but may be '5'. The estate was registered earlier (a document dated 23 Oct 1730 is cited in the debts). ¶1

Probably the 'Even Garbergs qvinde Giertrud æt: 53' buried 7 Nov 1728 (16310 c114 L), with the skifte concluded only in 1731; if so, 'Anders 1 aar' is an age taken at the registration or a misreading. The burial age 53 (born about 1675) sits badly with children of 1726 (and 1729-30); one of the ages is probably wrong. ¶2

Death dated: her husband Even Eriksen remarried on 2 Jul 1730 (16310 c121 L), and no other burial of a Garberg wife occurs Jul 1729 - Oct 1730 (c118-121 read in full), so the burial of 'Even Garbergs qvinde Giertrud' on the 24th Sunday after Trinity (7 Nov) 1728 (c114 L) is hers; the skifte concluded 15 Feb 1731 was held over two years after her death. The burial age 53 cannot be right if she bore Anders (and Peer, if 5): it is probably a clerk's error. 'Anders 1 aar' in Feb 1731 is also impossible for a mother buried Nov 1728, so the skifte ages were probably copied from an earlier registration or misread. Peer's age (5 or 13) is not settled by the burials; in the skifte he is listed before 'Erich 11', which in an age-ordered list favours 13. Grade unchanged (Probable). ¶4

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Graded Probable on her link to Per Evensen (see his page): the skifte names a son 'Peer Evindsen' of Even Eriksen at Garberg, and Per was baptised 13 Mar 1726 as 'Even Garbergs Barn' (16310 c103 R); his burial age (88 y 10 m in Dec 1812) and the 1801 census (born 1725) agree with an age of 5 in 1731, not with 13. Not Confirmed because the age digit is uncertain and nothing links the 1731 Peer to the Per of 1758 by a record within 15 years. ¶3

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed, graded on her link to her son Per (Peder) Evensen (see his page). The skifte concluded 15 Feb 1731 (24750 fol. 366b; card 77804 c535 agrees) names 'Pedr Evindsen 4 aar og 4 [m.]' among the children 'tilsammen avlede' (jointly begotten) by Giertrud Iversdatter and Even Erichsen, cottar under Garberg - a primary record naming her as Peder's mother. Peder aged 4 years 4 months in Feb 1731 matches the baptism of 'Even Garbergs Barn Peder' on 13 Mar 1726 (16310 c103 R), and his mother was churched as 'Even Garbergs qvinde' in May 1726 while Giertrud was Even's wife (buried as 'Even Garbergs qvinde Giertrud' 7 Nov 1728). That Peder is the tree's Per is shown by the 1762 ekstraskatt (49639 c132, Garberg 735): 'Peder Evensen med Qvinde Brynild Erichsdatter samt hans Moder Anne Madsdatter' - the tree's Per with his wife and his stepmother, Even's second wife Anne Mattis/Madsdatter (m. 2 Jul 1730). No rival Peder son of Even exists. See Even Eriksen Garberg. ¶6

Records that disagree 2

Correction and resolved contradiction: the 1731 skifte after Giertrud Iversdatter (24750 fol. 366b, view 377 L) was re-read at full resolution. The children are '1 Jvor Evindsøn 13 aar, 2 Erich Evindsen 11 aar, 3 Pedr Evindsen 4 aar og 4 [months?], Guru Evindsdatter 19 aar'. That is Iver 13, Erik 11, Peder 4 and Guru 19, not 'Peer [5?] or 13... Anders 1' as. The Stjør- og Verdal card index (77804 c535) independently reads Iver 13, Erik 11, Peder 4, Guri 19. Peder aged 4 years 4 months fits the baptism of 'Even Garbergs Barn Peder' on 13 Mar 1726 if the ages were taken at the registration about July 1730, just before Even remarried on 2 Jul 1730. So the age contradiction is resolved, and there was no son Anders. Further continuity: Iver Evensen of Børset, d. 1753, named a daughter Giertrud, and his sons' guardian was 'deres Farbroder Erich Evensen' (24753 fol. 514b). At Erich Evensen's skifte on 3 Feb 1764 (24755 fol. 378b) his daughter Giertrue was put under the guardianship of 'Peder Evensen Garberg' (no relationship word). So the 1731 brothers Iver and Erik are followed to 1753 and 1764, and a Peder Evensen of Garberg acted for Erik's children. Grade kept Probable on her link to Per Evensen (see his page). The skifte names her son Peder, and the age now agrees with the 1726 baptism. But nothing ties that child to the Per Evensen of the 1758 marriage, or to Peder Evensen Garberg of 1764, by a record within about 15 years: the 1764 guardianship gives no relationship word, and the gap from 1731 is 27-33 years. ¶5a

Contradiction resolved: the burial age 'æt: 53' (1728, implying birth about 1675) cannot stand beside the skifte's explicit statement that Peder (4 years in 1731) and Iver, Erich and Guru (13, 11, 19) were her children by Even; burial ages of this period were often rounded or inflated, and the skifte, an inventory made with the family present, is the better witness. Her birth stays ABT 1690, inferred from the children (Guru born about 1712). ¶7

Searches that found nothing 1

Negative, test record read: the rest of her 1731 skifte (24750 view 377, fol. 366b and the top of fol. 367a) was read for guardians and kin. After the children (Iver 13, Erich 11, Peder 4, Guru 19) the entry gives only the proclamation, the appraisers ('Lauerettesmanden Thomas Koe[?]' and others, not kin words), the inventory and the creditors (among them 'Peder Biørnsen Garberg'); the estate was insolvent and no guardian (formynder/værge), farbror or morbror is named. So the skifte gives no clue to her own parents. Test record for her parents: none in 77804 for 1689-1742 under Garberg (c529-545, already read); next would be the 1702-26 gap, which has no protocols. ¶9

Open questions and the next record to read 2

Test record: Per Evensen's probate, or Even Eriksen's own probate (after 1751), naming Peder as a son or brother. Birth changed from ABT 1675 to ABT 1690: a daughter of 19 in 1730/31 and a son born in 1729/30 rule out the burial age 53. ¶5b

Lead: her probate card, 77804 canvas 535, 'Garberg, plass under denne gård, Selbu, 15/2-1731: Giertrud Iversdtr.; Enkemann: Even Eriksen Hvildsgaard[?]; Barn: Iver Evendsen 13 år gl., Erik Evensen 11 år, Peder Evensen 4 år, Guru Evensdtr. 19 år gl.'; estate 13-3-10, debts 19-2-23, insolvent (prot. 2, fol. 366). The child Peder, 4, fits Peder bapt. 13 Mar 1726 (16310 c103) roughly. The skifte was registered more than two years after her burial on 7 Nov 1728. The card names no guardians. Test record for her parents: the protocol entry, 24750 fol. 366b (view 374, URN sk20090213660620; view 373 = fol. 365b-366a holds the preceding Frosta skifte of 4 Dec 1730), for the guardians, perhaps a 'morbroder' Iversen. ¶8
