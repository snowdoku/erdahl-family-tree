Life story

Probate: Anders Olsen, third son, aged 11, is an heir in his father Ole Paulsen's probate at Ytre Esse, opened 1 April 1799 as written (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll A 12, fol. 548a). His guardian was Peder Marchussen of Tenningen, and after Peder's death Niels Olsen Balle. This gives a birth about 1788. ¶7

1801 census: age 13 at Esse with his widowed mother Ingri and siblings Povel, Marcus, Ingeborg and Aase (identification probable). ¶2

Probate of his paternal grandmother, 1803: in the probate of Sigri Olsdatter Berge (12-13 April 1803, Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll A 12, fol. 161b-163a, No. 9) he is 'Umyndig tredie Sønnesøn Anders Olsen 16 Aar gammel' (under-age third grandson, 16, so born about 1786-87), present, serving on the farm Tendingen. His guardian was 'Myndlingens Morbroder Peder Marchussen Leilænding paa Gaarden Tendingen' (the ward's mother's brother, tenant at Tendingen). ¶8

Probate 1804: in the probate of his guardian Peder Marchussen of Tendingen (23 June 1804; Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll A 12, fol. 320b-321a, No. 46) the widow reported that Anders's inheritance from his father Ole Poulsen Esse's 1800 probate was held by his brother Poul Olsen, then living on Esse, together with Anders's share of 7 rd 5 ort 6 22/45 s from the probate on the farm Berge, closed 13 April 1803, after his farmor (paternal grandmother) Sigri Berge. His brother Poul Olsen Esse was appointed his guardian in Peder's place. ¶6

1815 census: Anders Olsen, 27 (b. ~1788), servant and day laborer in his brother Povel Olsen's household at Esse, with his siblings Marcus 32 and Ingeborg 25. ¶3

Marriage: on 16 January 1826 (Leikanger register 1826, p. 313, no. 2) 'Ungk: Anders Olsen', of Esse, 40, father Ole Esse, married 'Pige Ingebor Gjestsd.', of Esse, 30, father Gjest Esse. Sponsors (forlovere) Lars Trulsen Esse and Anders Aa[?]; banns requested by Lars Esse; both had had natural smallpox in 1801. Ungkarl means bachelor, so this was his first marriage. ¶10

Buried 17 Jan 1847, age 56. Probate in Ytre Sogn 1843-48, residence Grøneng. ¶1

Burial 1847 (scan read): no. 5, 'Anders Olsen, Huusmand', 56, of Esse, died 12 January, buried 17 January 1847. Huusmand means cottager. The age is understated: his 1803 probate age and 1826 marriage age give a birth about 1786. ¶12

Corrections 2

Correction (structural, Oct 2026): this record previously listed his wife Ingeborg Monsdatter as his mother. The 1801 census household at Esse shows him aged 13 with his widowed mother Ingri Marcusdatter; Ingeborg Monsdatter is the woman he married about 1819. The link has been corrected, which restores Ingri Marcusdatter to the direct ancestral line. ¶5

Correction: the tree's marriage 'about 1819' to Ingeborg Monsdatter (see her page) is contradicted by the records. He was still a bachelor (Ungkarl) when he married Ingebor Gjestsdatter in 1826, and the mother of his sons Ole (1820) and Johannes (1832) is Ingebor Gjestsdatter. No record links him to Ingeborg Monsdatter; that link was an inference from the 1815 census and her 1846 burial. ¶11
