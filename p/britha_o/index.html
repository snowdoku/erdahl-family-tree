Life story

Probate guardian, 1780: Anthoni Thorsen Gredung[?] (farm reading uncertain) was appointed guardian of her inheritance. Another guardian in the same probate was Ab[?]slon Andersen Erdahl, of the farm Erdal where she married in 1796. ¶7

Of 'Tielov' (farm name as transcribed; uncertain) when she married Jørgen Lassesen in 1796. ¶1

'Tielov' is the Oppstryn farm Tjellaug (Tiellov/Tjellog). ¶2

1801 census, Erdahlen, household 3: Jørgen Lassesen 28 (national soldier and farmer), Brithe Olsdatter 34, children Brithe 3 and Anna 1. ¶4

Probate: Nordfjord skifteprotokoll A 16b, fol. 834b-836b (index pa00000000056595), read from the scan. The opening lines with the date and the deceased's name are lost in the black-and-white scan; the index puts Ole Østensen of Tjellog on this page. The heirs are the widow Aase Rasmusdatter (her legal guardian, laugverge, Cornelius Nielsen U[tvik?]) and the 5 children of the marriage: Østen Olsen 22[?], Rasmus Olsen 21[?], Ole Olsen 18[?], Anne Olsdatter 24[?] and Brithe Olsdatter 15 (ages other than Brithe's uncertain). Net estate 220 rd: the widow got half (110), each son 27 rd 3 ort and each daughter 13 rd 4 ort 8 s. ¶5

Death and probate: she died before 13 Oct 1834, when the lensmann (sheriff's deputy) and two court assessors registered and valued the estate of 'afg. Gaardmands Kone Brite Olsd.' at Erdal, with her widower Jørgen Lassesen Erdal present. Her heirs were their five children: Lasse Jørgensen Erdal, Britha Jørgensdatter (married to Ole Pedersen Sandvig), Anna Jørgensdatter (married to Halsteen[?] Samuelsen Loen), Elisabeth Jørgensdatter (28) and Ingeborg Jørgensdatter (25[?]). Nordfjord skifteprotokoll A 24, fol. 188b-189b; the division was made on 18-20 May 1835, after Jørgen's own death. Her burial entry has not been looked for. ¶8

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Confidence moved Probable to Confirmed: her father's probate (Nordfjord skifteprotokoll A 16b, fol. 834b-836b, 1780) names 'Brithe Olsdr 15 aar' as the youngest child of the deceased and his widow Aase Rasmusdatter. The deceased's name in the opening lines is illegible on the scan; he is identified as Ole Østensen of Tjellog by the probate index (A 16b, fol. 834b, residence Tjellog) and by the heirs, a widow Aase Rasmusdatter with children Østen, Rasmus, Anne and Brithe Olsen/Olsdatter, who match the Tiellov baptisms. Age 15 in 1780 fits the baptism of 31 Mar 1765 and outweighs the 1801 census age of 34. ¶6

The case for this identification 1

Probable identification: Berthe, daughter of Ole Østensen and Aase Rasmusdatter, was baptized 31 Mar 1765 at Tiellov. A Birthe Olsdatter (born about 1765) of Tiellov was confirmed in 1781. 'Britha Olsd.', maid of Tielov, married Jørgen Lassesen in 1796. An elder sister Berthe (born 1759) died as an infant, so the name was reused. Her age in the 1801 census (34, i.e. born about 1767) differs slightly. ¶3
