About the family

NEW ANCESTOR. Known only from his son's patronymic. The estate protocol after 'Svening Giermundsøn' of Rostveit (Østre Råbyggelag Skifteprotokoll nr. 3 fol. 223a, 26 May 1712, read on the scan; card 39942/11385) gives the patronymic, so Svenning's father was a Gjermund. Nothing else is known: not his farm, dates or wife. The 1686 contract and 1697 forlik cited in the estate concern the land in Rostveit and do not name him on the card. Death date is only a bound (his son died before 26 May 1712). ¶1

Life story

1645 poll tax (koppskatt olsok 1645, Råbyggelag, p.9 'Omblj Sogen', DA 76706 view 119, right page, read on the scan): 'Roestued: Thorgier och hans quinde, [G/B]iermund och hans quinde och en daatter'. So a Gjermund (spelled as in the 1664 vicar's census 'Tallak [Bier/Gier]mundsøn') was a married householder at Rostveit in 1645, with a daughter, alongside Torgeir. This is very probably Svenning Gjermundsen's father (Svenning b. about 1635 was then about 10 and would not be taxed); identification by name and farm only. It gives him a farm: Rostveit in Åmli, by 1645. His wife is not named. ¶3

1664-66 censuses: no Gjermund is listed at Rostveit in the 1664 vicar's census (which lists men of all ages) or in 1666, so he was dead or living elsewhere by 1664 (inferred). His son Svenning was born about 1635 (ages 28, 30 and 32), so Gjermund was born before about 1615 (inferred). A Tallak Gjermundsen, born about 1631-33, shared Rostveit with Svenning in 1664-66 and was probably another son. Two other Gjermundsens, Aslak (26) and G[unmund?] (20), were at Vestre Tveit (Vestertued) in 1664 (bailiff's census p.11); they may be sons of a different Gjermund, and nothing links them to Rostveit. ¶2
