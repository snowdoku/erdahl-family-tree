About the family

Wife of Jon Johansen of Tynning; both were dead at the joint probate of 2 Jun 1701 (A 1b fol. 342b), which divides between 'deris... tilsammen avlede børn' (Lasse, Johannes, Olle, Sigri, [Kari?], Agaatte, Giertrud). Probable on her link to Agatha Jonsdatter for the same reason as her husband (identity of Agaatte 1701 with the 1731 widow rests on name and farm). ¶1a

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
