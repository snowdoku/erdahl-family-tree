About the family

Children: Millie (1878), Bertha (1880-1895), Olianna 'Annie' (1882), Cora (1885), Minnie (1887), Julius (1892), Henry, Elmer. ¶3

Children's names (daughters Millie, Bertha, Olianna, Cora, Minnie) give no clear Norwegian naming-pattern clue. Mary's mother was Gjertrud, and the 1885 census lists Millie as 'Jorttems E.' (Gjertrud?). ¶8

Daughter Bertha's baptism, 1880 (transcription only): the Blue Earth Norwegian Lutheran church book for Faribault County, 1859-1888 (Digitalarkivet source 100624, transcribed by Lars E. Øyane in 2000, with no scan, page, day or sponsors), has a child 'Berthe Josephine', born 1880, father 'Ole Johnsen', mother 'Mari Olsdatter'. Bertha Josephena Quame was born in 1880 to Ole J. Quame and Mary Olsdatter, so this is probably her baptism (Probable: the name, year and mother fit, and no other Berthe Josephine is in the book). The father is entered as Ole JOHNSEN, not Jensen. That could mean the J. in 'Ole J. Quame' stands for Johnsen or Jonsen (father Jon or Johannes) rather than Jensen, or that the pastor or transcriber anglicised Jens as John. Future searches for his Norwegian origin should try a father named Jon, John or Johannes as well as Jens. The earlier nationwide exact-date sweep (: every indexed Ole born 23 Nov 1853, whatever the father) already found no son of a Jon or Johannes either. ¶22

Parents named (index): the FamilySearch index of his Minnesota death certificate (cn 4071 = 1934-MN-004071; 'Minnesota, Deaths and Burials, 1835-1990', https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:FDDX-1JY) gives 'Ole J. Quome', 80, died 15 Apr 1934 in Rome Township, married; father 'Jens Larson'; mother 'Bertha Quome'; wife 'Mary Anderson'. The certificate image is restricted to FamilySearch centers, and the informant is not known. This confirms the J. = Jensen (father Jens) rather than Johnsen, and gives his father's own patronymic, Larsen. ¶24

Life story

Father's name was Jens (from the patronymic). Norwegian origin NOT yet found: no indexed baptism on 23 Nov 1853 fits. 'Quame' is an Americanized Kvam/Kvamme, likely a Sogn farm (Fjærland, Balestrand, Sogndal or Lærdal), but this is speculative. ¶2

1875 MN census, Emerald: 'Ole J. Quame', 21, living in the household of his future in-laws Ole and Gjertrud Anderson. 1885 MN census, Rome Twp.: age 31. ¶1

1875 MN state census (Emerald, Faribault Co., 1 May 1875; cert. 1875-mnsc_6-0391-32/33): 'Ole J. Quame' 21 and 'Mary' 20 are listed with ditto marks under the surname Quame inside Ole Anderson's household, so Ole and Mary were already married by May 1875 (not about 1877). ¶4

1880 U.S. census (FamilySearch index https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:MZ9Q-F32; T9 roll 618, Rome, ED 55, sheet 48D): Ole J. Quame 26, farmer, born Norway, both parents born Norway; wife Marie 25, born Norway; daughter Emelia 2, born Minnesota. No other relative in the household. ¶26

Signed-in check (2 Nov 2026): the FamilySearch index of his Minnesota death certificate (cn 4071, Rome Township, 15 Apr 1934) was re-read; it still offers only 'Check Image Availability' (image restricted), so the informant and any Norwegian birthplace remain unread. FamilySearch Full-Text search for "Ole J Quame" returns four hits only: his daughter Annie's delayed birth record (Faribault Co., affidavit by Mrs. Mary Quame, mother), a Faribault County deed of 1889-91 naming 'Ole J. Quame', a Faribault deed index entry, and an unrelated 'Ole J. Quame' and 'Jorgen J. Quame' in an 1880s Wisconsin county history (Crawford/Richland/Dane). No naturalization paper for him was found by full text. ¶28

1885 MN census, full household: family 31 in Rome Township, Faribault County, enumerated on 10 June 1885, is 'Ole J Quame', 31, born Norway, with 'Maia O Quame', 30, born Norway, and four children, all born in Minnesota: 'Bottens E' (?) 7, 'Bent J' 4, 'Ohanna' 2 and 'Korri J' 0. Read with the family list, these are Millie (born 1878; her name is hard to read), Bertha Josephena (1880), Olianna 'Annie' (1882) and Cora J. (1885). The MNHS index leaves Ole himself and the eldest child out, so a search for his name in the index does not find this household. Kund Quamme, 50, and Ellen J., 58, in family 21 on another page of the same township are not part of this household, and how they are related is not known. ¶10

1895 MN state census (Rome Twp., Faribault Co.): 'Ole J. Quanne' 42, born Norway, with wife Mary 39 and daughter Millie 18. The 'years in Minnesota' column reads 30, which points to arrival in Minnesota about 1865, aged about 11. The 1905 census (Rome, 'Ole Quamme' 51) is faint and seems to give about 25 years, which conflicts with his presence in 1875, so 1865 is preferred. ¶5

Immigration year: the 1900 U.S. census (Rome Twp., ED 94, sheet 5B, dwelling 84; FamilySearch https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:M9SW-1WX, indexed 'Ole J Quamne') gives born Sept 1853, immigrated 1860, 40 years in the US, naturalised; the 1910 census (sheet 7A, dwelling 101; https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:M2LT-LCR, indexed 'Ole Avaren') gives immigrated 1856. Both read from the images by the signed-in lane (fleet result S0002). Either year places his arrival in America at age about 3-7, before 1865, which also rules out every 1865 emigrant candidate, the Helene Ole included. The 1895 MN census 'years in Minnesota 30' may count from a later move into Minnesota rather than from his arrival in America. ¶15

1900 U.S. census, household detail: on sheet 5B of Rome Township (ED 94, dwelling 84) 'Ole J. Quamne', 46, born September 1853 in Norway, farmer, owned his farm, which was mortgaged; he had been married 25 years and was naturalised. His household was his wife Maria and the children Anna J. (October 1882), Cora J. (January 1885), Minnie J. (July 1887), Henry O. (October 1889), Julius M. (September 1892) and Elmer B. (October 1896), all born in Minnesota. The census month, September, differs from the 23 November 1853 on his gravestone; the gravestone is kept. These are the first records giving Henry's and Elmer's birth months and middle initials. ¶16

Marriage date, from the census: 25 years married in June 1900 and 34 years in April 1910 put the marriage about 1875-1876. The 1875 Minnesota census already shows Ole and Mary together under the name Quame on 1 May 1875, so the marriage was probably early 1875 or late 1874 (inferred). ¶18

1910 U.S. census: the Rome Township household is indexed under the surname 'Avaren' (alternatives Avance, Goawe, Quame): Ole, head, born Norway about 1855; his wife 'Marria', born Norway about 1865; and the children Cora (about 1885), Minnie (1888), Henry (1890), Julius (1893) and Elmer (1897), all born in Minnesota. This is the only record so far that gives Henry's and Elmer's approximate birth years. A search for 'Quame' alone misses it. ¶11

1910 U.S. census, household detail: on sheet 7A of Rome Township (dwelling 101) 'Ole Quame', 55, was in his first marriage, married 34 years, naturalised and English-speaking. The children at home were Cora 25, Minnie 22, Henry 20, Julius 17 and Elmer 13, all single. The image reads 'Quame', although the index has 'Avaren'. ¶17

1920 U.S. census (index only): 'Ole Quamme', head, born about 1854 in Norway, and his wife Mary, born about 1856 in Norway, were still in Rome Township, Faribault County. The image has not been read. ¶19

1930 U.S. census (index only): indexed as 'Ole Quarn', head, born about 1854 in Norway, with his wife 'Maria Quarn' and daughter 'Cora Quarn' (indexed as born about 1892, which conflicts with Cora's January 1885 birth in 1900), in Rome Township. This is his last census; he died in April 1934. The image has not been read. ¶20

MN death certificate index: Ole J. Quame, died 15 Apr 1934, Faribault Co., certificate 1934-MN-004071. Parents are not in the index; the original certificate (MN Historical Society) should name them and is the best next step. ¶6

Death certificate 1934-MN-004071: MNHS has no free image (the index row's image hashes are null, as for every Minnesota death row checked), so the parents and Norwegian birthplace it may name are still unread (Oct 2026). A non-certified copy costs $9 from MNHS (shop.mnhs.org, Digital Death Certificate) or $13 from MDH. ¶9

Records that disagree 2

Birth year, a contradiction weighed: his age gives a birth in 1853 or 1854 in the 1875 census (21), the 1885 census (31 on 10 June), the 1895 census (42) and the 1905 census (51), and his gravestone gives 23 November 1853. Only the 1910 census index (about 1855) disagrees, and that index also puts his wife ten years too young. The 1910 figure is treated as an indexing or enumeration error, and 23 Nov 1853 is kept. Reading the 1910 page image would also give his year of immigration. ¶12

Against: birth 3 Oct 1853 vs 23 Nov 1853 on his gravestone (the 1900 census gives Sept 1853). Norway-side follow-up: Fjærland/Balestrand emigrants 1854-1861 (Jens Larsen, Britha Olsdatter, son Ole), the Fjærland marriage of Jens Larsen x Britha Olsdatter c. 1853-58, Ole's absence from the Fjærland confirmations c. 1868, and whether a Kvamme/Kvame farm lies in or near Fjærland. Crop:. ¶25b

Corrections 2

Correction (Helene 1865 hypothesis re-tested and refuted, Oct 2026): the 'Ole Qvamme', 12, of Lærdal on the ship Helene (Bergen-Quebec, 1 May 1865) is Ole Knudsen, born 3 Nov 1852 and baptised 6 Jan 1853 in Lærdal, son of Knud Knudsen and Gunilda Olsdatter (Lærdal church book 1850-1866, p. 29 no. 5); his siblings Ragnilde (bapt. 1848) and Jacob (b. 2 Nov 1859) were on the same ship with their parents Knud and Gunnilde, both born about 1819. That family went to Otter Tail County: 1870 U.S. census St Olaf ('Krame': Knud ~1817, wife ~1818, Ole ~1853, Jacob ~1860); 1875 MN census Rush Lake, family 25, read from the image (Knud Kvamme 56, Gunnild 56, Ole Kvamme 23, Emma 19, Jacob 16); 1895 Polk Co., Lowell (Kunte Quamme 76, with Jacob 36). Their Ole was in Otter Tail on 1 May 1875, the day Ole J. Quame, 21, was enumerated in Emerald, Faribault County, so they are two different men. Do not re-test this candidate. ¶13

Correction: the 'Kund' (read Knud) Quamme, 50, and Ellen J. Quamme, 58, of Rome Township in the 1885 MN census (family 21) are not the Helene couple: that Knud was born about 1835, not 1819, and the household includes 'Comst' (or 'Ornst'), 15, born in Norway about 1870, and Inga, 8, also born in Norway (ditto), so this family came to America after about 1870-77. They appear in no other Minnesota state census (1875, 1895, 1905), and neither has an indexed Minnesota death from 1904 onward. Whether Ellen (born about 1827) was Ole's widowed mother remarried is untested: it would require Ole to have emigrated in 1856-60 without her. It remains an open lead, not a link. ¶14

Alternatives considered and set aside 2

Candidate Norwegian baptism (Speculative, not linked): Fjærland annex of Balestrand prestegjeld, Sogn (ministerialbok 1853-1879, 1418P, p. 2, 1853 no. 20; FS image DGS 107616644 img 2; index https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:687D-N8SV): Ole, born '3die Octbr', baptised 25 Oct 1853; parents read from the image as Jens Larsen [Nordal?], 'født paa Bøium' (born on Bøyum), and 'Pige' (unmarried girl) Britha Olsd[atter] Bøium/Supphellen; the legitimacy column appears to read 'Uægte' (illegitimate) [uncertain]. Sponsors: Johannes Bøium, Peder Eriksen ibid., Ingebrigt Iversen Sophellen, Gjøri Iversdatter ibid., Britha Olsdatter Bøium; right-hand remark 'Ole Ingebrigtsen Sophellen'. For: father Jens LARSEN and mother BRITHA = Bertha both match the 1934 death index exactly; year 1853 matches; and Fjærland is the parish earlier guessed for 'Quame'. ¶25a

Verdict on the Fjærland identification: NOT established; it stays Speculative and the death-index parents are not re-identified with the Fjærland couple. For: the parents' given names and patronymic (Jens Larsen, Britha/Bertha) and the year 1853 match the 1934 death index; Ole's in-laws (see his page) were Balestrand emigrants of April 1856, and the 1910 census gives Ole's arrival as 1856. Against or unproved: (1) the birth day, 3 Oct 1853, against 23 Nov 1853 (gravestone) and Sept 1853 (1900 census); (2) no emigration record: the Balestrand out-migration lists for 1854, 1855 and 1856 (nos. 1-119 of 1856, which include the Ole Andersen Grøneng family, nos. 55-59) contain no Jens Larsen, no Britha Olsdatter of Bøyum/Supphellen and no boy Ole born 1853; the indexed emigrant sources (Bergen passports 1842-60, Bergen-Quebec ship lists 1865-73, Bergen police protocol 1874-) have no matching Jens Larsen, Britha Olsdatter or Ole Jensen b. 1853; (3) a Jens Larsen Nornes was still in Sogndal on 11 Mar 1860, when his son Erik by Guri Jørgensdatter was baptised there (1420P p. 22 no. 28), so the father did not emigrate with the boy before 1860 if he is the same man; (4) no couple Jens Larsen x Britha Olsdatter married in Balestrand or Sogndal 1853-66 (index); (5) no farm Kvam/Kvamme/Qvam exists in Balestrand in the 1865 census, so 'Quame' is not explained (Sogndal has a husmannsplass Kvammen, with no link to either parent). Absence evidence consistent with emigration: no Ole Jensen b. 1852-54 is in the Balestrand 1865 census; no Ole son of Jens Larsen is buried in Balestrand 1853-66; Britha (daughter of Ole Ingebrigtsen) has no Balestrand marriage 1850-75 (bride's-father index) and none of the Fjærland burials of a Britha Olsdatter in 1857, 1859 and 1864 is her (read on the scans: an infant of Jordal, a child of 6 at Rødbotten, a pauper aged 89). The Balestrand out-migration lists after 1856 were not found in 8378 (canvases 196-263 are vaccinations and the day register), so emigration in 1857-66 is unchecked. Graded: no change to Ole Jensen Quame (Confirmed on his link to annie and Mary (Maria) Olsdatter Anderson). ¶27

Searches that found nothing 3

Norwegian origin still not found (2026 search). Ruled out: (a) 'Ole Qvamme', 12, of Lærdal on the ship Helene, 1 May 1865, who was the son of Knud Qvamme; (b) Ole Jenssen Amble of Sogndal, who left Bergen 1 Jun 1875, too late for the 1875 census; (c) the Lærdal Hunderi family (Jens Nilsen, his wife Kari Lassesdatter and his father Nils Jenssen), who were neighbors in Emerald in 1875 but emigrated in 1869 without any son born in 1853; (d) all indexed Ole baptisms in Lærdal from Nov 1853 to Mar 1854; (e) 1865 census Ole b. 1852-55 living on a Kvam/Kvamme/Qvam farm (none had a father named Jens). Because he probably emigrated about 1865 as a child, he is unlikely to appear in the Norwegian 1865 census. Look for a Jens family in the 1865-66 Bergen-Quebec or Christiania lists, and in the 1870 U.S. census. ¶7

Search (negative, so nobody repeats it): his family before 1875 has not been found in any census index. 1857 MN territorial census (MNHS index): Faribault County has no Ole at all, and no head named Jens, Jenst or Jence on any of the 55 pages with a Norway-born Ole aged 2-14 in Faribault, Freeborn, Fillmore, Mower, Houston or Goodhue counties (fleet). 1860 U.S. census (FamilySearch index, signed in, result S0108): no Quam, Quamme, Kvam or Qvam household anywhere; the only Qu*m* Norwegians are the Quamer family of Irving, Jackson Co., WI, with no child of about 1853; father-name filters do not work on the 1860 index. 1870 U.S. census (FamilySearch index, result S0003): no Jens household with an Ole of about 16 in Faribault County, and no Ole Jensen of the right age anywhere in Minnesota except a lone 19-year-old hired hand in Viola, Olmsted Co.; no Kvam or Qvam surname at all. Still open from S0003: the two Emerald Oles of the right age, Ole Olesen (b. 1854) and Ole Thompson (b. 1850), whose households need reading (queued as S0137). ¶21

Search (negative, so nobody repeats it): in the Blue Earth church book (Digitalarkivet 100624, baptisms 1859-1888), no other child of this couple was found. The searches were father Ole with surname Jens*, Joh* or Q*, mothers named Mari* (all 70 rows), and children named Olian*, Kor* and Cor*. The 'Ole O. Quam' with 'Maria J. Quam' (daughter Anne Maria, 1878) is the same couple as 'Ole O. Kvam' with 'Kristine Marie J. Kvam' (son Martin Kornelius, 1880), not Ole J. Quame and Mary. The other children were probably baptised in the Emerald or Bethania congregation, whose books are not on Digitalarkivet (signed-in lane S0188). ¶23
