About the family

Widow of Biørn Reyersen in the probate after him on Halsnøen, 23 May 1731 (D 9, DA 23886, fol. 50; D 4, DA 15920, fol. 355b: 'hans efterladte Enke Kari Joensdatter'), mother of their four children Rasmus 11, Sophie 17, Siri 6 and Anne 3. ¶1

Mother of Sophie Biørnsdatter (see her page), Confirmed, graded on her link to her. Both the 1731 probate and her own probate of 1751 name Sophie as her daughter. Continuity from these records to Sophie Biørnsdatter in the tree (see her page, widow of Peder Nielsen of Halsnøy, probate 19 Mar 1787): (1) 1731, Halsnøy: Sophie Biørnsdatter, 17, daughter of Biørn Reyersen and Kari Jonsdatter, with her brother Rasmus Biørnsen, 11. (2) 1751, Halsnøy, 20 years later but at her mother's probate on the same farm: 'Sofi Biørnsdatter', married, with the same brother Rasmus Biørnsen and sisters Siri and Anna. (3) 1762 ekstraskatt, Halsnøy, 11 years later: 'Sophie Biørndatter', wife (Hustr) of the farmer Peder Nielsen, listed beside her brother Rasmus Biørnsen, also a farmer on Halsnøy; the couple's children over 12 are Biørn, Niels, Marthe and Ane Peders-. (4) 1787, Halsnøy, the same couple: the probate after Peder Nielsen names his widow Sophie Biørnsdatter and the children Biørn, Niels, Peder, Marthe, Anna and Britha. No other Sophie Biørnsdatter is named on Halsnøy or in the Sjernerø probates in these years. The couple's eldest son was named Biørn, after her father. ¶3

Life story

Her own probate (scan): D 9 (DA 23886), Ekstrakt for 1751, fol. 206, Stiernerøe Schibrede no. 40: 'Anno [1751] 17de May' a probate was held on the farm Halsnøen after the late woman Karj Jonsdatter. Her children: 1. Rasmus Biørnsen, of age; 2. Sofi Biørnsdatter, married; 3. Siri Biørnsdatter, married; 4. Anna Biørnsdatter, married; 5. a son of about 19 and 6. a daughter of about 13, whose names (apparently Olsen/Olsdatter, from a second marriage) are only partly legible on the binarised scan. The year 1751 comes from the typed register to the designations 1731-1754 (DA 23971: 'Halsnø i Sjernerø, Kari Jonsdtr., 17/5 [1751], fol. 206'). She died before 17 May 1751. ¶2

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Not the same person as the 'Kari Joensdatter' who was the wife (Hustr) of Rasmus Biørnsen on Halsnøy in the 1762 ekstraskatt. This Kari died in 1751, and Rasmus's first wife Kari Nielsdatter died in 1760, so the 1762 Kari is Rasmus's second wife. ¶4
