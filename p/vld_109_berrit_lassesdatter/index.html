About the family

Wife of Ole Erichsen Wiike (see his page): named as mother of the twins Erich and Lasse, baptised 8 Aug 1741 in Veøy church ('Ole Erichsen Wiikes og Hustrues Berrit Lasse datter'). Veøy ministerialbok 1721-1764, IIIF canvas 108, right page. ¶1

Her father was named Lasse (patronymic; the twin Lasse probably named after him). ¶2
