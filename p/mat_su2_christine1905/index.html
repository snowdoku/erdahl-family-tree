Life story

Buried at Selbu Cemetery, Whitman County, Washington as "CHRISTINE SLIND KLEGSETH, b. Jan 07 1905 d. Jul 30 2002" (reference hrp-cem-whi-selbu_0085), immediately beside her husband. ¶1

Her birth date and birthplace match the "Christina Slind, 5, born Norway" of the 1910 Whitman County census - the second daughter of Ole Gundersen Slind (b. abt 1872) and Sigrid T. - so she is almost certainly that child. ¶2a

Marriage: JORGEN O. KLEGSETH, b. 15 July 1900, d. 25 January 1945 at Colfax, Whitman County, whose own death certificate names his wife as "Christine Klegseth" and his parents as Ole J. Klegseth and Johanna Klegseth. She was widowed at forty and lived another fifty-seven years, dying at ninety-seven. ¶3

THIS IS THE SECOND KLEGSETH-SLIND MARRIAGE, and it ties the two Ole Slinds' households together. Her uncle Ole G. Slind (b. 1884) married INGEBORG KLEGSETH, Ole J. Klegseth's daughter; she married JORGEN KLEGSETH, Ole J. Klegseth's son. Uncle and niece married a sister and a brother out of the same Selbu farm. ¶4

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
