Life story

Census 1701: no Jørgen Jensen is indexed at Tøniom (Tynning); the farm had Olle Joensen (24), Olle Johansen (60), Jørgen Jonsen (50) and Lasse Joensen (38) as bunder. Whether 'Jørgen Jonsen, 50' is this man with a misread patronymic is not known; he is not identified with him here. ¶2a

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Graded on his link to Jens Jørgensen. Probate after 'den Salig afdøde Dannemand Jørgen Jensen' on the farm Tønning in Lavik skipreide, 3 Sep 1731 (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll A 3b, fol. 486a, Digitalarkivet 24158 viewer 199, right page; index pa00000000520838), 'imellem hans efterladte Hustrue Agatha Jonsdatter paa den eene, og hendes med den Salig mand avlede Børn, Nafnlig Jens, Lisabeth, Britha og Barbra, Jørgens Søn og Døttre, paa den anden Side; Jens myndig, Lisabeth gift og med hendes mand tilstede', the other two daughters minors. The son Jens is followed to Jens Jørgensen of Tynning (d. before 13 Mar 1772) by the pair Jens + Barbra Jørgensdatter on the same farm at both ends (see Jens's note), and by Jens's daughter Agaathe named after Jørgen's wife. ¶1
