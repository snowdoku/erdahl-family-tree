About the family

Naming: Kari's eldest son was Hermund Iversen (named first in Iver Gullacksen's 1774 probate), after her father's father's name Hermund, which fits (inference). ¶3

Sister: probate at Indre Takle, 18 Sep 1726, after 'Haldis Hermunds Datter', between her widower Kolbein Takle and their three children Gregorius 16, Hermund 1[?] and Ole 10, 'Kolbeinssønner'; the minors' guardians were 'deres Morbroder Claus Tackle' (their mother's brother Claus of Takle) and Thor Asseim (Ytre Sogn 3a fol. 202a, source 24157 view 204; index pa00000000520543). So Claus Hermundsen of Takle was a brother of Haldis Hermundsdatter, wife of Kolbein (Johansen) of Takle; their father was a Hermund, probably of Indre Takle (not named in the entry; no father added). ¶4

Parents: Hermund Hendrichsen of (Indre) Takle (b. about 1633, d. before 19 May 1716) and Giertrud[?] Clausdatter. Hermund's probate at Takle, 19 May 1716 (Ytre Sogn 2b fol. 492b), names the widow and 'deris tilsammen avlede Børn: Hendrich, Claus, Brit[e] og Haldis', all of age. The 1701 census of Inder Tachle lists Claus Hermundsen, son, 20, in the household of Hermund Hnerichsen, 68. Birth about 1681 from the census age. His sister Haldis is the Haldis Hermundsdatter of the 1726 probate with 'Morbroder Claus Tackle'. ¶5

Life story

Probate No. 68, Ytre Sogn 7a fol. 17a, held in September 1750 (heading 'A. 1750 d. 4 og 5 September') on the farm Indre Takle in Lavik skipreide 'efter afdøde Claus Hermundsen Tackle, imellem Enken Kari Johansdatter og ved Egteskab avlede Børn, som ere: Første Søn Claus Claussen Mit Tackle, anden Søn Hermand Claussen Nesse, tredie Søn Hans Claussen, fierde Søn Ole Claussen og 5te Søn Johannes Claussen, alle myndige og tilstede. Ældste Datter [K/M]ari Clausdatter gift med Iver Gullacksen Engesætter, anden Datter Magdeli Clausdatter gift med Niels [Horager?], og tredie Datter Brite Clausdatter gift med Rasmus [Bendixsen...dal]'. Appraisers included Johannes [?] Krogen. Gross estate 246 rd. Read from the scan; the first letter of the eldest daughter's name is uncertain (K or M), which matches the 1774 'Kari' and 1799 'Mari' spellings of the same woman. ¶1

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Graded on his link to his daughter Kari Clausdatter: this probate names 'Ældste Datter [K/M]ari Clausdatter gift med Iver Gullacksen Engesætter' as his child and heir. Iver Gullacksen of Engesætter (d. before 12 Mar 1774) had only one wife, Kari (Mari) Clausdatter, named in his own 1774 probate and her 1799 probate at Engesætter; no other Iver Gullacksen of Engesætter is known (rival: none named). Continuity: same named couple, 24 years apart, linked by the husband's full name and farm, which is the same pair the tree already holds. ¶2
