Life story

Marriage (index): as Ane Hansdatter of Biertnæs (Bjørtnes) she was betrothed 29 Dec 1734 and married 7 Mar 1735 at Jevnaker to Arne Halvorssen of Sørrum, a widower. She is named as the mother at the Gran baptisms of Anne (15 Jan 1736) and Rønnof (23 Oct 1740). Her own baptism (father Hans of Bjørtnes) was not found in the Gran or Jevnaker indexes. Her burial and any remarriage after 1746 are also not found; an Anne Hansdatter of 'Elckeneyer' who married Ole Jørgensen at Gran on 25 Apr 1748 is unlinked. ¶4

Baptisms: wife of Arne Halvorsen of Sørum, Gran parish; named as mother of Anne (15 Jan 1736), Elli (5 Jan 1738) and Rønnof (23 Oct 1740) at Gran. Her birth year is inferred from her children. ¶1

Probate (index card): at Arne Halvorsen's estate on 21 May 1746 (fol. 91b), Arne had two wives both named Anne Hansdatter: (1) Anne Hansdatter Raastad, deceased by 1746; (2) Anne Hansdatter Biørtnæs (Bjørtnes), the widow. The card rules off Hans 17 and Ingeborg 23 from Anne 10, Elie 8 and Rønaug 5. So Elie (b. c.1738) is a child of the second marriage, and this Anne Hansdatter is Anne Hansdatter Bjørtnes, living in 1746. Confidence upgraded from Speculative to Probable together with her husband (see Arne Halvorsen's note). Her own estate after 1746 was not found among the Sørum cards (DA 39947, images 7551-7593). ¶3

Probate protocol: at her husband Arne Halvorsen's estate at Sørum on 21 May 1746 (DA 25287, fol. 91b), she is 'den sidste og nu igienlevende Enke' (the surviving widow). The protocol names her children: Anne, 10, Elie, 8, and Rønaug, 5, all Arnesdatter. Her lagverge and any statement of her own kin were not read; the lines are blotted and lie on view 94, left page, lines 7-10 of the entry. ¶5

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
