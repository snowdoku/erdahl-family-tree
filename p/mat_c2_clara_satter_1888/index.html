Life story

She is not on any known list of this family and I cannot place her from the index alone, but a Satter child born in Spink township in 1888 is almost certainly a grandchild of Gunder Gundersen Satter. The likeliest father is Fred Satter, b. 1868, who is otherwise absent from Union County in 1900. SPECULATIVE until a vital record or the 1880/1885 schedules place her. ¶2

The census month of September 1888 and the registration date of 1 November 1888 differ, which is ordinary for a census taken twelve years later; the registration is the primary record and is preferred here. ¶6

1900 federal census: Spink township, Union County, South Dakota, Ancestry image 4120605_00249 - 'Clara Satter, GRANDDAUGHTER, b. September 1888, South Dakota'. She is living under her own surname in a household headed by grandparents who are not Gunder Satter (Gunder is a boarder three sheets earlier in the same township). ¶1

She married Henry Numsen: the South Dakota birth index records LLOYD NUMSEN, born 22 August 1918 in Union County, father HENRY NUMSEN, mother 'CALRA SATTER' — a clerk's transposition of Clara. ¶8

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Confidence raised from Speculative to Confirmed in this patch. The reason is her South Dakota birth registration, which names both parents and so removes her from the 'unplaced Satter' category she was merged in under. ¶4

Corrections 1

Correction and answer to the open question: Clara Satter, found unplaced in the 1900 census of Spink at Speculative, is the daughter of GUSTAV SATTER by his FIRST wife CHRISTINE CARLSON, and so a granddaughter of Gunder Gundersen Satter. The South Dakota birth index records CLARA SATTER, born 1 November 1888 in Union County, father GUSTAVE SATTER, mother CHRISTINE CARLSON — the only one of Gust's ten registered children whose mother is not Sophia Larson. The research summary on Gust's own memorial independently lists 'Child of Gustav Parelius Satter and Christine Carlson: Clara Matilda Satter, b. 1 Nov 1888, d. Jun 1972'. ¶5
