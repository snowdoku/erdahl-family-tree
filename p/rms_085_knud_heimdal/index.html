About the family

Father of Anders Knudsen of søndre Heimdal, Treungen (d. before 16 Jul 1733) and of Torbiør Knudsdatter (wife of Ole Salvesen), known from their patronymics and from Anders's 1733 probate (Øvre Telemark skifteprotokoll no. 4, fol. 401b-402a, DA 25668 image 406), which calls Torbiør Anders's sister and Hage Tordsdatter her mother (Swermoderen of Ole Salvesen). Knud is therefore probably Hage Tordsdatter's husband. He is not named in the 1733 entry and was probably dead by then, since Hage's keep was laid on her son-in-law. A document of 27 Jun 1712 about Heimdal, cited in the same entry, may be the skifte after him; it is not in the court protocol (DA 25692). ¶1

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 alternative set aside

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Not to be confused, without proof, with Knud Bjønnesen of nordre Heimdal, husband of Torbjør Aamundsdatter (her probate, July 1733, protocol 4 fol. 338b), whose daughter Hage married Evind Andersen. That Knud had a different wife. ¶2
