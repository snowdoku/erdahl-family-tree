Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Speculative as father of Sigrid Lassedatter (see her page). Her patronymic shows her father was a Lasse. The Vik bygdebok (H. 1, canvas 43, Unde chapter, rebuilt from word boxes with gaps) says that in 1667 Lasse [held Unde] and that [he?] had [daughters?] Sigrid and Brita, who 'kom på [...] sitt bruk på Undi'; Sigrid's husband Ola Ivarson was one of the holders of Unde in 1701, and she named a son Lasse (b. 1694). The words linking Lasse to the daughters fall in gaps of the rebuild, so this is a candidate only. The holders before him were Sjur and Ingrid (1635-47). ¶1a

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Test record: the 1664/1666 census for Vik (Unde) giving Lasse's age, and a probate or deed by which Unde passed from Lasse (or his widow) to Ola Ivarson and Sigrid Lassedatter (Ytre Sogn tingbok/pantebok, 1685-1701). ¶1b
