About the family

Husband's will read on the original (ERO D/ABW 16/203, free image): the legacies to 'Rychard my sonne' revert, if he dies under 20, 'to Alis my wiffe', and Alice is residuary legatee and 'sole executrix'. The original matches the Emmison abstract and, like it, does not call her the son's mother. Her grade (Probable, on her link to Richard Gaymer) is unchanged. ¶9

Her father (not settled): the will of William Webbe of Coggeshall, beer brewer, made 13 Apr 1542 and proved 20 Apr 1542 (ERO D/ACR 4/190/2), survives only as a registered copy in the Archdeaconry of Colchester register 'Sargant'. Essex Archives Online has no image of it (searchroom microfiche only), and it is earlier than Emmison's printed abstracts (from 1558), so whether it names a daughter Alice could not be read. Other Webbe testators near Coggeshall in the ERO catalogue, 1480-1620, are John Webbe of Cressing, 1561 (D/ACR 5/212, also a register copy with no image); none at Kelvedon, Feering, Terling or Witham. A Thomas Webbe of Kelvedon, yeoman, occurs in 1504 (ERO D/DH VF34). Queued for sign-in as S0265 (the Archdeaconry of Colchester will-register microfilm on FamilySearch). ¶10a

Life story

Will of her husband read in full: Alice is named three times in Emmison no. 347 (contingent heir of the son Richard if he died under 20, a legacy of yarn and a bed, and residuary legatee and sole executrix: 'The rest of my goods to my Wife, whom I make ex'rix'). Overseer William Fuller; witnesses William Finche, George Browning, John Catterall. No Webbe is named, so the will does not identify her family. The searches of Emmison's other Essex volumes for a Webbe of Coggeshall (Archdeaconry and Commissary volumes, full-text) found none. Still Speculative as the son's mother: the will calls her 'my wife' only, not the son's mother. ¶2

S0263: the Coggeshall baptisms of 1560-1572 are not in FamilySearch's Essex index (a place search returns only other parishes) and no Gaymer of any Essex parish before 1590 is indexed except the Terling marriages and four Shalford entries (Elizabethe Gaymer x George Allen 20 Jul. 1581; Barnabye Gaymer bur. 10 Feb. 1590/1; Joone Gaymer x John Allgar 18 Sep. 1592; Johane Gaymer bur. 16 Sep. 1604). So the test record cannot be read on FamilySearch; it needs the ERO Coggeshall register (D/P 36/1/1) through Essex Ancestors. ¶8

Second marriage and death: as 'Alice Gaymer' she married John Thorison at Terling on 25 Jun 1571, eleven weeks after the burial of her husband Richard Gaymer at Coggeshall (4 Apr 1571). John Thorison's children Colet and Jane were baptised at Terling on 11 May 1572 (FreeREG, father John), and 'Alice Thorison, wife of John' was buried there on 20 May 1572, nine days later, evidently after childbirth. No other Alice Thorison and no other John Thorison occur at Terling. So she was still of child-bearing age in 1572, which fits a first marriage in Feb 1561/2 and a son Richard born in the 1560s. ¶3

John Thorison's will (ERO D/ACW 5/51, original read on the free watermarked image): 'I John Thorison of Terling in the county of Essex... Miller', 1 Jan 1590/1; legacies to his son John Thorison; the residue to 'my wellbeloved wife Ursula Thorison' and 'my daughter Collet Thorison' (bp. 1572, Alice's daughter) to share his new dwelling house in Terling; and 'I appoint Richarde Gaymer to be Overseer of this my last will & testament'. John was buried 20 Jan 1590/1. So the Richard Gaymer of Terling who married Margaret Mason in July 1590 was the trusted overseer of the will of Alice's second husband, a miller of Terling, and was himself a miller of Terling by 1608 (Q/SR 186/48) and at his death in 1613. He had evidently grown up in the Thorison household and learned the trade there. Collet, the overseer's half-sister on this reading, married John Clarke at Terling 23 Jan 1591/2. ¶4

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

2 on the grade · 1 alternative set aside · 3 open questions

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Speculative mother of Richard Gaymer of Terling: Alice Webbe married Richard Gaymer at Coggeshall, Essex, on 8 Feb 1561/2 (FreeREG). An Alice is named in his will of 3 Apr 1571 (Emmison no. 347, index 'Gaymer, Al'), and an Alice Gaymer married John Thorison at Terling on 25 Jun 1571 (FreeREG). Their son Richard was under age in 1571, so she was probably his mother, but an earlier wife of Richard senior cannot be ruled out; no baptism of the son was found (Coggeshall baptisms in FreeREG begin about 1600). ¶1

Upgrade: Speculative to Probable, graded on her link to her son Richard Gaymer (Richard Gaymer of Terling, miller). Evidence: (1) Richard Gaymer of Coggeshall's will of 3 Apr 1571 leaves goods to 'Richard my son', under 20, with remainder 'to Alice my wife' if he died under age, and makes Alice executrix; (2) Alice took the household to Terling on her remarriage in June 1571; (3) the Terling Richard Gaymer appears there as overseer of the will of her second husband, John Thorison, and followed Thorison's trade as miller; (4) Alice was bearing children in 1572, so the Feb 1561/2 marriage covers a son born about 1562-1570, which fits Richard's first marriage in 1590. Why not Confirmed: no record calls her Richard's mother. The 1571 will says only 'my wife', and a son born to an earlier wife of Richard senior (between about 1551 and 1561) cannot be excluded, because the Coggeshall baptisms of the 1550s-60s have not been read (FreeREG starts about 1600). No earlier wife is named anywhere, so there is no named rival. ¶5

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Her own family (lead only): a William Webbe of Coggeshall, beer brewer, made his will on 13 Apr 1542, proved 20 Apr 1542 (ERO D/ACR 4/190/2, a registered copy; no image online). He is probably the William Webbe who took a 99-year lease of Coggeshall Abbey tenements in 27 Hen. VIII (TNA LR 14/662) and was sued for Coggeshall tithes in 1536-44 (TNA E 321/15/82). He is the only Webbe testator of Coggeshall found in the ERO catalogue before 1650, and an Alice born about 1535-1542 could be his daughter. No record seen names her, so he is not entered. ¶7a

A baptism after Feb 1561/2 would settle her as his mother; one before it would refute her. ¶6
