Life story

Birth: Born at Płużnica (Pfeilsdorf) at 1.30 a.m. on 30 September 1908 and named Zofia (Sophie) in the 1949 Polish copy; registered 7 October 1908 as no. 42 by her father the settler Gottlieb Knodel, who signed; mother Karolina Knodel née Schmidt. ¶3

Baptism (index): the Evangelical parish of Villisaß, Kreis Culm, has a baptism of Sophia Knodel on 25 Oct 1908, with the relatives indexed as 'Gottlieb, Karoline'. The civil birth at Pfeilsdorf was on 30 Sep 1908, 25 days earlier, and the relatives' names repeat the parents' given names, so this is very probably this child's baptism. The same register image also carries the baptism of Wilhelm, son of Jakob Knodel and Katharina, and of Frieda Friederike Eberle, daughter of Friedrich and Friederike Eberle (the latter on the same day). This is an index entry only (Ancestry 'Germany, Lutheran Baptisms, Marriages, and Burials, 1567-1945', dbId 61250); the register image has not been read, so the index does not say whether the two names in its unlabelled 'Relatives' field are the parents or the godparents, and it gives no sponsors. Index recordId 3649395, image id 0245317-00148. ¶7

Emigration: indexed as 'Zofia Knodel' (age given as abt 1914 in the index, perhaps a misreading) with her brother 'Ludwik', arriving at Halifax 28 March 1927 from Liverpool (Ancestry dbId 1263, rid 3003253). ¶6

Emigration: As 'Zofia Knodel' she landed at Halifax on 28 March 1927 from the Doric (from Liverpool) with her brother Ludwig. ¶5

1931 census: Sophie Knodel, 22, single, a house maid (servant) in the household of a farmer, Henry Lofsted [reading uncertain], in the Rural Municipality of Fairview No. 258 (township 25, range 18, west of the 3rd meridian), Kindersley district, Saskatchewan. Born Poland, parents born Rumania [the father's entry slipped to 'Armenia'], immigrated 1927, alien, nationality Poland, German, Lutheran, English-speaking, wage earner. ¶4

Marriage, BC 1938-09-460222: on 18 June 1938 at St. Philip's Church, Dunbar Heights, Vancouver (Anglican), Sophie Knodel, spinster, aged 29, Anglican, of Penticton, born in Germany, daughter of Gottlieb Knodel and Caroline Schmidt, married Dudley William Warwick Hatcher. He was a police constable of Penticton, aged 24, born at Enderby, BC, son of William Hatcher and Isabel Helen Warwick. The witnesses were N. and J. Warwick of Ruskin, BC. A year later her sister Erna was a housekeeper at Penticton. ¶1

Death, BC 2001-09-027662: Sophie Hatcher, a retired florist of 46898 McGuire Road, Chilliwack, the widow of Dudley William Warwick Hatcher, died at Parkholm Lodge, Chilliwack on 25 December 2001, aged 93, and was cremated. She was born 30 September 1908 in 'PRUSSIA'; her father Gotlieb Knodel and her mother Anna Carolina Schmidt are both recorded as born in Prussia. The informant was her daughter. ¶2
