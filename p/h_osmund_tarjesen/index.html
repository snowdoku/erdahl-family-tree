About the family

Father of Thoruf: named in Thoruf's 1826 baptism (Osmund Tarjesen, Hjemdal) and in his 1849 marriage (Osmund Torjesen). ¶1

Other children include Tarje (b. ~1818, m. 1846) and John (b. ~1835). Turi Osmundsdatter (m. Torof Ormsen Tvedt) is named as a daughter on Find a Grave. ¶5

Mother identified (Probable): Birgit Targiesdatter of Frontved, who married Tarje Osmundsen in 1791. Osmund (b. ~1794/95) was a child of Tarje's first marriage, and there is no evidence of an earlier wife. His own baptism has still not been found in the scanned Kviteseid book. ¶6

Baptized on 2nd Easter Day (Easter Monday, 21 Apr 1794) in Treungen church by the curate Storm: 'Døbt: Targie Osmundsen Hiemdals S. Osmund. Test: Gunild Home, Sigri(?) Hogsiaa(?), Torbør Osmundsd. Hiemdal, Niels Evindsen(?) Støl, Evind Osmundsen Hiemdal' (Kviteseid ministerialbok 1787-1799, left page facing p.78, image 80; read from a full-opening image, two godparent names uncertain). The entry does not name the mother, but in April 1794 Targie's wife was Birgit Targiesdatter (married Aug 1791, buried Feb 1799), so she is now linked as his mother. This replaces the birth estimate of about 1794 and answers the earlier note that his baptism had not been found. ¶7

Life story

In the 1801 census, aged 6, at Hiemdal (then in Kviteseid prestegjeld) as a child of Targie Aasmundsen's first marriage. His siblings there were Gundbiør (9) and Gunvor (4). ¶3

Married 5 Jul 1818 in Treungen: Osmund Tarjesen/Torgiesen, aged ~24, of Hiemdal, to Anne Torafsdatter, aged 22, of Jyserød. ¶2

In the 1865 census: farmer and owner at Heimdal nordre, aged 72, born in Nissedal. His household included his wife Anne Thorufsdatter (b. 1795, Drangedal) and their son John (b. ~1835). ¶4
