About the family

Widow of Torkel (Torchild) Iversen of Indre Oppedal, named as his widow (Enken) in his probate of 7 Jun 1775 (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll A 9, no. 47). ¶1

Mother of Else Torkildsdatter (Probable, not proved): the 1775 probate calls her the widow, not the mother. See Else's notes for the reasoning and the record that would confirm it. ¶3

Husband: the 1793 probate does not name her husband; all five heirs are Torkelsen/Torkelsdatter, so their father was a Torkel. 'Torkel Iversen' is not written in it. The husband link rests on Torkel Iversen's own probate of 7 Jun 1775, which calls Britte Monsdatter his widow; the two probates agree on 2 sons and 3 daughters, an eldest son Mons and a second daughter Else married to Mons Lassesen. The youngest daughter is Dordei in both probates (1793 re-read by audit; an earlier reading 'Gjertrud' was a misreading), so the two probates agree on all five children. ¶7

Parentage, against: Mons Johansen's probate of 16 May 1739 (skifteprotokoll 5, Nr. 76) divides his estate between the widow and 'deres sammenavlede Børn' and names five sons, 'alle Monses Sønner'. Randi Iversdatter's lifetime division of 7 Oct 1739 (Nr. 77) is 'imellem hendes 5 Sønner'. Britte was married and alive in 1739. Under the Norwegian Law of 1687 a daughter inherited a half share (søsterlod), and a married daughter was normally named through her husband (general rule, inference; not a statement in these records), so her absence from both records is a real contradiction. Further: no baptism of a Brite to Mons Indre Oppedal was found in Jan-Apr 1711, Apr-Dec 1712 or Jul-Dec 1713. An apparently unmarried 'Randi Ifversdr' of Indre Oppedal was a godmother in 1712. None of Britte's three daughters (Kari, Else, Dordei) was named Randi (naming inference, weak). Rival father: Mons Iversen of Indre Oppedal (b. about 1691; m. 2 Jan 1718 Giertlaug Lassesdatter), Randi's probable brother. A daughter of his born after 1718 would be younger than Britte's burial age implies, but a son of his could have been named Lasse after Giertlaug's father, so a second Lasse Monsen at Oppedal in 1775 is possible (inference). A second Brite Monsdatter, born about 1735 and a widow at Indre Oppedal in 1801, shows that another Mons family there used the name Brite. ¶13

Parentage, what would settle it: the Gulen baptisms of Jan-Jul 1713 (Digitalarkivet 11437, canvases 0-10, unread) and, if missing, May 1711 - Mar 1712, for a Brite daughter of Mons Indre Oppedal; the probate after Lasse Monsen, Indre Oppedal (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll 11a p. 123, 1782-91, index pa00000000525057), which would name Britte or her children if he died childless; a re-read of Mons Johansen's Nr. 76 (fol. 121b-122a) for a son-in-law Torkel Iversen among creditors or receipts; and the probate after Mons Iversen, Breivik (7b p. 632b, 1756-63), for his children. If the hypothesis holds, Mons Johansen and Randi Iversdatter would enter the line a fourth way, through Britte's daughter Else Torkildsdatter (see her page) (inference). ¶14

Father: Mons Torbersen of Østerbø, Lavik skipreide (d. before 10 May 1745). His probate No. 9, 10 May 1745 (Ytre Sogn 6b fol. 433a), names '3die Datter Brite gift med Torchel Iversen Indre Oppedal' among his children. Siblings: Niels (of age), Ingeri[?] (m. Hans Gramer[?]), Giertrud (m. Hans Nesse), Dorte (d. by 1745, leaving a son Hans Johansen, 8) and Else (a minor). This fits her betrothal as 'Birthe Monsd: Østerbøe' in 1737. ¶17a

Mother: Else Hermundsdatter, first wife of Mons Torbersen of Østerbø, d. before 5 Apr 1740. Her probate (Ytre Sogn 6a No. 5, fol. 6a, 5 Apr 1740, Østerbø) names '4de Datter Brite gift med Torchel [Iversen] Indre Oppedahl' among the children of Else and Mons Torbersen. This settles the question left open in Mons Torbersen's 1745 probate, whose widow Synneve Andersdatter was a second wife. ¶18

Life story

Burial (per index): 'Brite Monsdr', Kone (wife), of Indre Oppedal, aged 80 3/4 (b. about 1712), buried 1793 'den 25de April den 28de Junii' at Brekke; Gulen church book 1788-1798, index pg00000000769573. The index gives two dates; 25 Apr is taken as the burial. Identity Probable: name, patronymic and farm fit, but the row does not name her husband. The scan was not read. ¶2

Marriage: betrothed at Lavik church on 15 Jun 1737 as 'Birthe Monsd: Østerbøe' to 'Torchil Iversen Indre opdal'; cautioners Guttorm Lassesen of Indre Oppedal and Mons of the same place; married 3 Jul 1737 (margin '3 Julij Copul.', day numeral medium confidence). Gulen ministerialbok 11441, canvas 6, right page. She was then about 25 (from her burial age). The farm name Østerbøe (Østerbø, also Østrebø) is read clearly on the crop. ¶10

Origin (inference, Speculative): she lived at Østerbø before her marriage, so her father Mons was probably of Østerbø, but no record names him. Leads, index only and not read: the probate after a Johannes Monsen of Østrebø, 1740-43 (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll 6a p. 242b, index pa00000000521294), who could be a brother; and a Johannes Østerbør whose son Mons was baptised at Lavik on 9 Dec 1741 (mother Gunild; 11441). In 1775 her daughter Dordei's guardian was her maternal uncle (Morbroderen) at Oppedal, so a brother of hers then lived at Oppedal. The cautioner 'Mons ibid.' of 1737 was of Indre Oppedal, not Østerbø, and is not shown to be her father. Reading Johannes Monsen Østrebø's probate would settle whether he was her brother. ¶11

Collateral, no grade change: The probate (skifte) of 11 Jan 1766 after Geertrud Lassesdatter of Indre Oppedal, the first wife of Britte's son Mons Torkelsen, was found as an extract in pantebok 12446, pp. 205-206 (published 8 Jul 1777). It divides Indre Oppedal 1 løb 12 merker (300 Rd) between 'Enkemanden Mons Torkildsen', the eldest daughter Elisabeth Monsdatter and the second daughter Britte Monsdatter. This agrees with Britte's own 1793 probate, which names Mons Torkelsen's daughters by 'the late Gjertrud Larsdatter' as Lisbeth (m. Hans Olsen Indre Oppedal) and Britte, and it dates Gjertrud's death to before 11 Jan 1766. It answers the lead: this family is Britte Monsdatter's (through her son Mons), not Lisbet Rasmusdatter's. ¶19

Probate: on 29 Aug 1793 the probate court sat at Indre Oppedal, Lavik skipreide, Brekke parish, to register and value the estate of the late plasskone (cottar's wife) Britte Monsdatter for division among 'hendes efterladte Børn' (her surviving children). The heading gives 2 (numeral not certain; earlier read as 3) sons and 3 daughters, and five heirs are listed: I. the eldest son Mons Torkelsen, dead, who left 4 daughters by three wives: by the late Gjertrud Larsdatter, Lisbeth Monsdatter (m. Hans Olsen Indre Oppedal) and Britte Monsdatter, 20; by Johanne [surname unread], Gjertrud Monsdatter, 14; and by Mari Hermundsdatter (now m. [?] Pedersen Raae), Magnilde Monsdatter, 8. II. the second son Anders Torkelsen Ringereide[?], lensmann (rural sheriff) of Lavik skipreide, present. III. the eldest daughter Kari Torkelsdatter, dead, through her children Torkel Hansen (of age), Synneve Hansdatter (m. Gunder Andersen Østerbø) and Ingeri Hansdatter (m. Mons Andersen Østerbø). IV. the second daughter Else Torkelsdatter, m. Mons Lassesen Indre Oppedal, both present. V. the youngest daughter Dordei ('Dördei') Torkelsdatter, m. Peder Aslaksen Indre Oppedal, Appelbotten, present (re-read by audit; earlier read as possibly Gjertrud). Appraisers Hermund [?] Brekke and Peder Larsen Indre Oppedal. Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll 11b, pp. 524a-524b, no. 58; the lots on 524b (right column) and 525a were not read. ¶5

Death: the 1793 probate calls her 'ved Døden afgangne' (deceased) on 29 Aug 1793. This agrees with, and does not narrow, the existing death before 25 Apr 1793 taken from the burial index; the death field is left unchanged. The probate confirms the burial identification as far as name, farm and year go. ¶8

The earlier note that her own estate (11b p. 524) would confirm her as Else's mother is now settled by that record. ¶9

The Brite Monsdatter alive in 1801 is a different woman (aged 66, a Mons's widow). Her own estate, Ytre Sogn 11b p. 524 (1791–99; fits the 1793 burial), is the record that would confirm her as Else's mother. ¶4

How sure are we?

2 on the grade · 1 correction · 2 alternatives set aside

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Confidence: raised from Probable to Confirmed. Her own 1793 probate, a primary record read from the scan, names 'Else Torkelsdatter gift med Mons Lassesen Indre Oppedahl' as her daughter and heir. This proves her as the mother of Else Torkildsdatter (see her page), which the 1775 probate after Torkel Iversen had left implicit. ¶6

Current grade: no parents are linked. The hypothesis that she was a daughter of Mons Johansen and Randi Iversdatter of Indre Oppedal is Speculative and is not linked (patch 035,; audit HOLD), because their 1739 probates name five sons and no daughter. The 'Origin (inference, Speculative)' note about a father Mons of Østerbø is an older lead that is still open; neither origin is proved. As a mother, her link to Else Torkildsdatter is Confirmed by her own 1793 probate (patch 013), which supersedes 'Mother of Else Torkildsdatter (Probable, not proved)'. ¶15

Corrections 1

Correction: this settles the 'Origin (inference, Speculative)' note (her father was indeed a Mons of Østerbø) and rules out the hypothesis that she was a daughter of Mons Johansen of Indre Oppedal (see his page), whose 1739 probate names no daughter. Mother not established: the widow in 1745 was Synneve Andersdatter, but the heading calls the heirs the deceased man's children, so she may be a stepmother. ¶17b

Alternatives considered and set aside 2

Parentage hypothesis (audited; Speculative, not linked): she may be a daughter of Mons Johansen of Indre Oppedal (see his page, d. before 16 May 1739) and Randi Iversdatter (see her page). For: (1) her patronymic Monsdatter; (2) at her betrothal on 15 Jun 1737 she was 'Birthe Monsd. Østerbøe', and Østerbø was the farm of Johannes Monsen by Dec 1741 (whether he held it in 1737 is not shown). His brothers, the 'Farbrødre' (paternal uncles) of his children in 1754 (7a No. 22), were Lasse Monsen of Indre Oppedal and a Monsen of Indre Takle whose forename reads 'Ande[r]?'/'Andr[?]' (6a Nr 70, 1742; 7a No. 22, 1754), so two of the three, Johannes and Lasse, match Mons Johansen's sons, while the Takle man does not match his son Peder unless the forename is misread; (3) a cautioner at her betrothal was 'Mons ibid.', a Mons of Indre Oppedal, but not identified: both Mons Johansen and Mons Iversen were of that farm; (4) in 1775 the guardian of her daughter Dordei was 'Morbroeren Lasse[?] Oppedale' (forename medium confidence, no patronymic), so she had a brother, probably named Lasse, at Oppedal. He may be Lasse Monsen of Indre Oppedal, Mons Johansen's son (inference). The link is not made because of the evidence in the next note. ¶12

Godmother, 1742 (no link made): 'birthe Monsd: Østerbøe' was a godmother at the baptism of Mons, son of Lasse Monsen of Indre Oppedal, at Brekke on 25 Feb 1742. This is the same name and farm under which she was betrothed in 1737 ('Birthe Monsd: Østerbøe'), so she is probably the godmother (the place-name is read with medium certainty), although by 1742 she had been married five years to Torkel Iversen of Indre Oppedal and might be expected to be called his wife or 'ibid.'; a different, unmarried Birthe Monsdatter of Østerbø (for instance a sister of Johannes Monsen of Østerbø) cannot be ruled out. Sponsorship ran both ways between the two households: her husband Torchil was a godfather at Lasse Monsen's daughter Rand's baptism on 16 Feb 1741, and a Lasse (with a Lasse Iversen) was a godfather at her own son Mons's baptism about Feb 1741. She was NOT a godmother at Rand's 1741 baptism (godparents Torchil, Guttorm, Gregorius, Hans Hansen, Eli Hendrichsd.). Godparents were often siblings, so this is consistent with the Speculative hypothesis that she was Lasse Monsen's sister, a daughter of Mons Johansen and Randi Iversdatter (patch 035). It strengthens but does not prove it: neighbours on the same farm also stood for each other, and the 1739 probates of Mons Johansen and Randi Iversdatter (Nr. 76 and 77) still list only five sons. The hypothesis stays Speculative and unlinked; what would settle it is listed in the note 'Parentage, what would settle it'. ¶16
