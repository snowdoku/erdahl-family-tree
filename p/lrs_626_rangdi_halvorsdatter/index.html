About the family

Other children named in the probate (not added as people): Ole Knudsen (of age in 1742; d. 2 Feb 1761 at Dybsjorde aged 44) and Rangdi Knudsdatter, married by 1742 to Thomas Aslesen (farm read as Rui[?]). Asle's eldest daughter Rangdie (b. about 1760) bears her name. Her own parents (a Halvor) are not identified; her baptism (about 1690) predates the surviving Ål registers. ¶4

Parents (Probable): Halvor Torstensen of Sando in Ål (d. before 21 Jul 1719) and Gunild Herbrandsdatter. The skifte after Halvor Torstensen Sandøe, 21 Jul 1719 (skifteprotokoll 7, fol. 211b; skiftekort 3917 image 3184), names his son Torsten Halvorsen and 'Rangdi Halvorsdatter' (card: 'Rangdj, ug.'). Her brother Torsten Halvorsen died at Sando in 1723, leaving the widow Berte Olsdatter (Bergith (Berte) Olsdatter) and a son Halvor Torstensen. Identification (Probable, inferred; graded on the parents' link to their daughter Rangdi Halvorsdatter; Rangdi's own Confirmed grade, on her link to her son Asle Knudsen, is unaffected): the 1719 skifte at Sando in Ål names 'Rangdi Halvorsdatter' as the daughter and heir of Halvor Torstensen Sandøe and Gunild Herbrandsdatter. Rangdi Halvorsdatter, wife of Knud Aslesen, died at Dybsjorde in Ål in 1742. The link rests on the same name and patronymic in the same parish, plus family ties: her brother Torsten Halvorsen of Sando (d. 1723) left the widow Berte (Bergith) Olsdatter, who remarried Tosten Thordsen of Sando, and Rangdi's son Asle Knudsen of Dybsjorde/Medgaarden married Kari Tostensdatter, Bergith's daughter by that second marriage. No other Rangdi Halvorsdatter of the right age is known in Ål, but no record names her as the same woman. ¶5a

Life story

Test of the 'ug.' conflict: the 1719 protocol calls Torsten and Rangdi only 'hans igienlevende Børn', with no marital status, so the 'ug.' on the index card is the card-maker's and is not a firm reading of the record. The 1723 skifte at Sando names neither Rangdi nor Knud Aslesen (guardian: Ole Torkelsen Tvetter). One counter-indication was found: the 1781 skifte after Halvor Tostensen of Sando says he inherited 1 løb from his farmor 'Gunnild Olsdatter' (properly Herbrandsdatter), and the card adds that he was 'hennes eneste arving'. If that is so, Rangdi's line had no share in Gunild's estate, which would argue against the identification. The grade of the parents stays Probable, with this caution. ¶6

Probate 1742 (read on the scan, skifteprotokoll 13, source 25548, image 289 right page): 'Anno 1742 den 16de Junij' the court met 'paa gaarden [Dybsjorde] i Aals Præstegield i Hallingdalen' to divide the estate 'efter den afgangne Dannekvinde Rangde Halvorsd., som boede og døde paa gaarden Dybsjorde her i Hallingdalen, og det imellem hendes efterlevende Mand Knud Aslesen Dybsjorde og deris i Ægteskab sammen avlede Børn Ole Knudsen [fuld]myndig, Asle Knudsen 14 aar og Rangdi Knudsdatter gift med Thomas Aslesen Rui[?]'. At the skifte appeared 'Enkemanden Knud Aslesen', the eldest son Ole Knudsen and Thomas Aslesen. The registration had been held on 19 Mar 1742 at Dybsjorde. The skiftebrev abstract in pantebok 9 fol. 162a says the farm (7 løb, less 1½ løb redeemed) went to the children, with life use for their father. ¶1

Skiftebrev 1742 (read on the scan): Ringerike og Hallingdal pantebok nr. 9 (1740-1747), fol. 162a, read at the autumn ting at Søndre Tingstue in Ål on 15 Oct 1742: 'Et Skifte Brev forrettet den 16de Junij 1742, efter afgangne Rangdi Halvorsdatter, som boede og døde paa gaarden Dybsjorde', by which the farm Dybsjorde, 7 løb (less 1½ løb that had been redeemed, 'indløst'), was laid out to her children ('Børnene'), who were to let 'Faderen' (their father, her widower) use it for his lifetime. The abstract names neither the widower nor the children. The Ål tinglag mortgage register (Hallingdal sorenskriveri panteregister I 1, 1736-1811, p. 48, Dybsjorde gl. matr. no. 146) indexes the same deed: 'Skifte efter Rande Halvorsdatter, fs. 16 Juni 1742, hvorved 7 Løbe i Gd. Dybsjorde er udlagt, hvoraf 5½[?] Løbe til Sønnen [or Sønnerne]', tinglyst 15 Oct 1742, pantebok 9 fol. 162. ¶3

1 on the grade · 1 alternative set aside

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: Confirmed, graded on her link to her son Asle Knudsen (and to her husband Knud Aslesen). The 1742 probate names her husband Knud Aslesen Dybsjorde and their children Ole Knudsen, Asle Knudsen 14 and Rangdi Knudsdatter. Continuity (no rival): Asle Knudsen, 14 at Dybsjorde in Jun 1742 (b. about 1727-28), is 'Asle Knudsøn Dybsjord' aged 20 at his confirmation on 1 Oct 1747 (five years later, same farm, same age); he married in 1756 as 'Asle Knudssen Dybsjorde', and his 1773 probate names Knud Olsen of Dybsjorde (son of his brother Ole Knudsen, d. 1761) as his children's first cousin. The other Asle Knudsens of Ål (Tangen, Skurdalen, Skarsgaard) have no Dybsjorde tie. ¶2

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Caution: the card calls Rangdi 'ug.' (unmarried) in 1719, while her son Ole Knudsen was buried in 1761 aged 44 (b. about 1717) and is called [fuld]myndig in 1742. If the burial age is overstated by a few years (common) and Rangdi married Knud Aslesen about 1719-20, it fits; otherwise the two Rangdis differ. Different farms and a 23-year gap, so Probable at most. ¶5b
