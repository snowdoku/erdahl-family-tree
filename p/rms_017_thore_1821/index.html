Life story

Baptism: Thore (Tohre), son of Niels Thorsen and Malene Nielsdatter of Nordre Sunde, Malde (Madla) sogn, Håland. The two registers are indexed with different dates: the klokkerbok (clerk's copy) 1815-1829 gives baptism 22 Jul 1821, the ministerialbok 1815-1845 gives 22 Jan 1821; the scan would settle which. Confirmed as their child (both parents named). ¶1
