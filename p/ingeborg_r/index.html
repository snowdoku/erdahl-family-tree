About the family

First husband Johannes Hansen (m. 29 Apr 1871 in Brekke). A Johannes Hansen, 28, died 3 Aug 1871 and was buried 7 Aug 1871 in St. Jørgens Hospital/Årstad parish. Their posthumous son Herman Olai Mathias was born 1 Sep 1871 and baptised 24 Sep 1871 at Nedre Solheim, with father Johannes Hansen of Ravneberg. Herman Johannesen (b. 1 Sep, the census writes 1841, i.e. 1871) was living with her in 1910. ¶4

Named after her maternal grandmother Ingeborg Monsdatter. ¶5

Life story

Baptised 6 Oct 1850 in Gulen parish (Brekke). Daughter of Rasmus Iversen and Kari Lassesdatter of Botnen. This matches her 1871 marriage (b. Botn in Brekke, father Rasmus Iversen) and her 1874 marriage (father 'Gaardmand Rasmus Iversen Botnen'). ¶2

1865 census: Ingeborg Rasmusdatter, 16, unmarried, born Brække sogn, daughter (deres datter) helping her mother in the house, in the household of her father Rasmus Iversen, 47, owner-farmer (gaardbruger, selveier), at Botn (Botnen), Taklestranden district, Lavik prestegjeld. ¶6

First married 29 Apr 1871 to Johannes Hansen; widowed by 1874. ¶1

1875 census: Ingeborg Rasmusdatter, born 1850 in Lavik prestegjeld, housewife (husmoder), wife of John Larsen Skræ, born 1853 in Avaldsnes, at Viken, Aarstad sogn, Årstad prestegjeld. ¶7

1891 census: Ingebarg Rasmusdtr. Botn, born 1850 in Ladvig (Lavik), wife (hustru), with her husband Jahn Larsen Skrede and seven children (Karl Jahan 1874 (b. 1874 Haugesund per 1891, before the Nov 1874 marriage and against a tree baptism of 1876; unresolved), Jahan Ingvald 1881, Lavise 1883, Sigvald 1885, Josefine 1884, Albert Mandus 1889 and Rasmus 1890), at Laksevåg, tellingskrets 1, Askøy. ¶8

1910 census: Engeborg Larsen, born 8 Aug 1851 in Lavik, Sogn, married, worker's wife (arbeiderkone), wife of John Larsen Skreo, at Nordre Laksevaag, Askøy. The household also lists Josefine Larsen, born 1897, as daughter (not established as hers: a Josefine Johnsen b. 1897 Aarstad was in the Lille Damsgård household of the other John Larsen in 1900, lrs_028/lrs_095), and Herman Johannesen, listed b. 1841, which conflicts with her son Herman's baptism of 1 Sep 1871 (lrs_095). ¶9

The case for this identification 1

Identity: these four censuses are the same woman, not the Ingeborg Rasmusdatter of Lille Damsgård, Askøy, whose husband John Larsen was born in 1872 in Manger and who was herself born in 1852 in Askøy herred. Every census here gives a birth in Lavik/Brekke in 1850-1851, the father's farm Botn (1865, and the 1891 name 'Botn'), and a husband born in 1852-1853 in Avaldsnes (1875, 1891, 1910); 1891 also lists Albert and Rasmus, whose baptisms name her and John. ¶10

Records that disagree 1

CONFLICT: the 1910 census gives her birth date as 8 Aug 1851. Other censuses and registers give 1850 or 1851. The 1850 baptism is preferred. ¶3
