About the family

Second wife of Gust Satter, married at the Dale church in Union County about 1894: the newspaper report of the wedding survives as a photograph on his Find a Grave memorial. 'The contracting parties were Gustave Setter and Miss Sophia Larson, Rev. Dale officiating. The bridesmaids were Miss Anna Setter and Bessie Helseth of Sioux City, with Albert Larson and Henry Lewis as groomsmen.' Miss Anna Setter is the groom's sister Anna Olene, who married Severt Eidem four years later. ¶3

Life story

She was born in December 1871 in South Dakota, daughter of Lars Larson Jr. and Ellen A. Helseth, and was living as a daughter in her father's household at Spink in the 1880 census; the Helseths of Sioux City at her wedding are her mother's people. ¶4

Her marriage was Gust's second: his first wife Christina Carlson had died in October 1889. ¶6

She must have married Gust Satter before September 1894, when their eldest child Ellen was born. ¶2

She bore nine children between 1894 and 1913 and raised Gust's daughter Clara as well. She died 1 June 1942 and is buried at Brule Creek Cemetery. ¶5

1900 federal census: Sioux Valley township, Union County, South Dakota - wife of Gustav Satter, born December 1871 in South Dakota (so a settler's daughter, like her sister-in-law Anna Olene). 1910 federal census: same township, b. 1872 South Dakota, with seven children in the house. ¶1
