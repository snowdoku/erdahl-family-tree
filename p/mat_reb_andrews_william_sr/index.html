Life story

Marriage: on 17 March 1924 at St Saviour's Church, Nelson, BC (Anglican), by licence, he married Elizabeth Alice Chadwick. He was then a telegraph operator, a bachelor aged 22, Anglican, living at Sirdar, BC, and gave his birthplace as London, England; his parents were Arthur William Andrews and Alice Hannah Longes, both born in England. The witnesses were Charles Allibone (reading uncertain) and Emily Dorothy Bostock of Nelson. ¶2

Cranbrook: the 1953-54 Cranbrook and East Kootenay Directory lists 'Andrews Wm A agt CPR r 422 12th Av S', a CPR station agent at Cranbrook, which fits his career as a CPR agent-operator; a William (A.) Andrews and Mrs William Andrews are also on the Kootenay East (Cranbrook) federal voters' lists of 1935 and 1940, and 'Mrs W A Andrews' of Cranbrook in 1953. Identifying these entries with him rests on name, occupation and place, and is probable rather than proved. By 1974-75 he and his wife had retired to Oliver. ¶5

He is 'William A Andrews Sr' on the 1974 federal voters' list at Oliver. ¶4

Death: he died on 8 August 1975 at the South Okanagan General Hospital, Oliver, aged 74, a widower, of #2A Road East, Oliver. He had worked most of his life as an agent-operator (station agent and telegrapher) for the Canadian Pacific Railway. The registration gives his birth as 22 May 1901 at Croydon, Surrey, his father as Arthur William Andrews and his mother as Alice Longes, both born in London. His son W. A. Andrews was the informant. He was buried on 12 August 1975 in Oliver Public Cemetery. ¶3
