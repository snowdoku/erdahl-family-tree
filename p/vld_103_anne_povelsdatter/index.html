About the family

Parents: the bygdebok, Indre Hildre bruk 4-4, p. 175, lists among the children of Paul of Indre Hildre (c. 1656-1675) '6 Anne, ca 1668, sjå Alvestad (8-1)'. See Paul (Povel). Test record for her own link to Paul Jørgensen: no probate after Jørgen Aamundsen (d. 1736) or after her (d. about 1738) is in the Haram or Sunnmøre probate indexes (Alvestad searched by farm,: nothing between Iver Olsen 1732 and Størker Ellefsen 1742); the Sunnmøre panteregister for Alvestad, or a skifte after her registered 1738-1740, would name her children. ¶6

Life story

Marriage: the Haram index has the engagement on 26 December 1693 and the marriage on 20 January 1694 (page 16, serial 48, marriage place Roald) of Jørgen Aamundsen, residence Ildre, and Anne Povelsdatter, whose residence is blank. The betrothal witnesses (trulovar) were Hans of Myren and Jetmund of Ildre. The page image was not read, so her parish and her father's farm are unknown. ¶1

Card index: the Sunnmøre probate card for Store Kalvøy, 10 Jan 1728 (77797/1499), is the skifte after Jetmund Knudsen of Kalvøen, whose widow was 'Ingebore Poulsdtr.' and whose only other heir was his sister Marithe Knudsdatter (no children). Jetmund Knudsen had married Anne's sister Inger Paulsdatter in 1692 (Haram Band 5 p. 192), so his second wife Ingeborg Paulsdatter, Band 2b's 'Ingeborg of Hildre', was probably another daughter of Paul of Indre Hildre, though the card does not say so. Not used for any grade. The card series has no Alvestad card at all (images 33-39 run Alnås, Alstad, Alstrup, Alvik), so no probate after Jørgen Aamundsen's parents (Aamund of Alvestad) falls in 1689-1728. ¶8

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

3 on the grade · 1 on the identification · 1 alternative set aside · 1 search with no result · 1 open question

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 3

Speculative mother of Paul (Povel) Jørgensen of Store Kalvøy (see his page) and wife of Jørgen Aamundsen of Alvestad (see his page). Why plausible: this is the only marriage of a Jørgen Aamundsen in the Haram index; it falls 15 months before the baptism of his daughter Berrete at Alvestad on 27 April 1695, the right interval for a first child; and the order of the sons fits the usual naming pattern, the first son Aamund (born about 1698) after the paternal grandfather Aamund and a later son Povel after a maternal grandfather Povel, which her patronymic supplies. The naming fit is supporting evidence only, not proof. What is missing: the groom's residence Ildre differs from Alvestad, so it is not proved that he is the same man; no record names the mother of any of Jørgen's children; and two daughters both named Berte (1695 and 1705) were alive together in the 1750s, which could mean Jørgen married twice and that Paul, born after the 1701 census (he is not in the household then, only Aamund, 3), had a different mother. ¶2a

Audit: the Ildre/Alvestad difference is narrowed by the witnesses. At the 1694 marriage the trulovar were Hans of Myren and Jetmund of Ildre. At the baptism of Berrete, daughter of Jørgen Aamundsen of Alvestad, on 27 April 1695, the godparents included Hans Andersen of Myren and Knud Erichsen of Ildre, and also Marrete Hansdatter of Myren and Karen Didrichsdatter of Alvestad (index). The same circle from Myren and Ildre appears at both events, so the groom of 1694 and the father of 1695 are very probably the same man, and Anne Povelsdatter very probably was Jørgen Aamundsen Alvestad's wife in 1694-1695. Her label stays Speculative because it is her motherhood of Paul that remains unproved: Paul was born after the 1701 census, and the two daughters named Berte (1695 and 1705) leave open a second marriage before his birth. ¶3

Upgrade (Speculative to Probable; graded on her link to her son Paul Jørgensen, Paul (Povel) Jørgensen Store Kalvøy): the Haram bygdebok, Band 5 'Hildrestranda', Alvestad bruk 8-1 '1695-1734', p. 296 (nb.no 1a85520cfb9659617e20d02dae47a323, canvas 296, rebuilt from word boxes) has 'Jørgen Amundss., ca 1662, d. 1736', married 26.12.1694 [the betrothal date in the church book] 'Anne Paulsd.' of Indre [Hildre], 'ca 1668, d. 1738', and lists their children on p. 298 as Brite (bapt. before 27.4.1695), Amund (1698 - before 7.3.1748), Ole (1700-1704), a child of 1701 (bruk 8-2), Paul (1702-1749), married 1725 Berte [Olsd.] of Store Kalvøy (ca 1695-1777), and Berte (bapt. before 24.7.1705 - 1751). This answers the earlier doubt: the bygdebok knows of only one wife of Jørgen, and Anne lived until about 1738, after the births of Paul (about 1702) and the second Berte (1705), so there was no second marriage before Paul's birth. A compiled source, so Probable only. ¶4

The case for this identification 1

Supporting primary index: the Haram probate index (source with heirs listed) for 1752, after Brite Jørgensdatter of Store Kalvø (b. 1705), farm Alvestad, names her brother ('bror') Paul Jørgensen Store Kalvø (b. 1702) with his children (Jørgen Paulsen b. 1734, Ole 1740, Jetmund 1743, Anne 1730, Ingeborg 1733, Anne 1740) and her sisters ('søster') Berte (b. 1692) and Synøve (b. 1698) Jørgensdatter; the 1775 probate after Brite Jørgensdatter Alvestad (b. 1692) names the same sibling set. This proves Paul was a full or half brother of the Alvestad daughters; it does not name their mother. Paul's eldest daughter is Anne (b. 1730), which fits a paternal grandmother Anne (naming pattern; supporting only). ¶5

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Burial candidate, not adopted (dates sweep 2): 'Anne Povelsd.' of Alvestad, interred 29 Mar 1738 at Roald (Haram ministerialbok, Roald 1689-1738, p. 134, no. 100; index pg00000001329490). Farm and year match the bygdebok's death about 1738, but the index gives her age as '30 aar' (b. about 1708), against about 70 for a woman married in 1694. Either the index misread the age (70 as 30?) or this is a younger Anne Paulsdatter of Alvestad. Read the scan of p. 134 before adopting it; death left at ABT 1738. ¶9

Searches that found nothing 1

Negative: her Test records were checked without result. No probate after her husband Jørgen Aamundsen (d. 1736) or after her (d. about 1738) is in the typed register to Sunnmøre skifteprotokoll 9B (1735-1739, source 24553, all entries read for Alvestad, Jørgen and Anne Paulsdatter) or 9A (1732-1735, 24552). The Sunnmøre panteregister series begins only in 1744 (22525/22526; no earlier register exists on Digitalarkivet), so it cannot list 1736-38 probates. The skifte after her brother Didrik (c. 1707) was not found either (see Paul (Povel)). Her grade stays Probable. ¶7

Open questions and the next record to read 1

What would confirm it: the Haram baptism of Paul (about 1701-1705) with the mother named, or Jørgen Aamundsen's probate of 1736 naming his widow or first wife. ¶2b
