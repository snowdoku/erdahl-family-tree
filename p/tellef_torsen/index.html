Life story

The godparent 'Torgius Tvet og Kone' at his 1795 baptism may be Torjus Ormsen, then at Tvet, and his wife Ingebor Olsdatter, the parents of the Ulvild he married in 1816. But the 1801 census also lists a Torgius Olsen, 37, farmer at Tvet (household 3, where Ulvild Torgiusdatter, 17, was a servant), so the godparent could equally be him. Not used as evidence. ¶8

In the 1801 census as a child of the widow Birgitte at Øvre Ramse. Married Ulvild Torjusdatter 7 Jul 1816 at Åmli; both of Wrolstad. ¶1

His marriage record of 7 Jul 1816 gives his age as ~20 and Ulvild's as 32. ¶5

Marriage scan read (DA 854 p.225): bride 'Pige Ulvild Torjusdatter Vroolstad, 32 aar'; forlovere (sureties) Børuf Olsen Vroolstad and Tolf Gundersen Hillestad; married 7 Jul 1816 in church. The entry names no parents. ¶7

Residence Østenaae at Torjus's baptism in 1817; Øvre Ramse from 1820. ¶4

A Tellef Torsen buried in Åmli 26 Mar 1865 (died 8 Mar) may be him (speculative). ¶3

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: baptism read on the scan (Åmli ministerialbok 1775-1796, DA 9923, p.107, canvas 105): '1 Søndag efter Trinitatis Prædiken L. Topdahl. Samme Dag döbt et Barn fra Tvet, Nom: Tellef, föd d. 3 Maij. Parent: Thor Tellefsen og Birthe Tergiesdatter. Test: Torgius Tvet og Kone, Salve Skieggedahl og Giermund ditto, og Guro Ramse' (baptised 7 Jun 1795). This primary record names his parents. It is tied to the man in the tree by the 1801 census (son Tellef, about 6, in the household of the widow Birgitte at Øvre Ramse, Thor having been buried 19 Apr 1800) and by his marriage of 7 Jul 1816 (Åmli 1816-1829, DA 854, p.225, Topdal no. 2: 'Ungkarl Tellef Torsen Wroolstad, 20 aar'), whose age fits a May 1795 birth exactly. ¶6
