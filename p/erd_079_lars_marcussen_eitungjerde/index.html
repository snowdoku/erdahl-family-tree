About the family

Wife at death: Unni Hermundsdatter. The probate does not say whether she was the mother of all four children; the second son's name Hermund suggests she was at least his mother (inferred). ¶3

Parents (Probable): Marchus Nielsen and Brite Larsdatter of Eitungjerde. The 1701 census lists Lars Marchusen, age 8, son of Marchus Nielsen (46) at Eitunsgierde, and the probate after Brite Larsdatter (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll 2a, fol. 187b-188a, 1702-1707) names her husband Marchus Nielsen and their son Lars, with daughters Gjertrud[?], Ragnhild, [unread] and Gyri. The census age (b. ~1693) is two years later than his burial age implies (b. ~1690-91); approximate ages of this period often differ by that much. ¶6

Life story

Betrothal: Leikanger ministerialbok 1690-1734, canvas 137 R (printed p. 322), 1724, Dom. XVII post Trin. at Tjugum (about October 1724): 'Sponsalia Lars Eitungierde / N. Christensd.', sureties ('Sponsores') Siur Vestrez[?] and Erich Eitun. The bride's given name was not known to the writer ('N.'). No wedding (nupt.) entry for the couple was found from Advent 1724 to Advent 1727 (canvases 138-143), so the wedding may have been held in the bride's parish. A daughter Christi Larsdatter, still a minor at his probate in 1758, carries the name of a maternal grandfather Christen, which fits this marriage. ¶7

Second marriage: Leikanger ministerialbok 11564, canvas 169 R, 1733: betrothal 'Lars Marc. Eitun / Unni Biaastad' and marriage 'Lars Eitungierd / Unni Biaastad', autumn 1733. Children by the first wife (a Christensdatter, betrothed Oct 1724): Christen, buried 1731 at 10 days (canvas 165 R), and Christen, baptised Feb 1733 (canvas 168 R). No baptism of Marcus Larsen found in Leikanger 1724-1734. ¶8

Burial: canvas 170 L, Jan 1734: 'Brite Larsd. Eitungiarde 10 aar' - a daughter Brite, born about 1723-24, named like Lars's mother Brite Larsdatter. If the age is right she was born before or at the Oct 1724 betrothal, which leaves room for an earlier wife (and an earlier birth of Marcus, the eldest son). ¶9a

Very probably the 'Lars Eithungierde' who was godfather at the baptism of Peder Marcussøn Thue, 16 Sep 1753 (inferred: same farm, his grandson). His patronymic Marcussen comes from the probate index ('Markusen') and the faint heading ('Lars Mar...'); his son Marcus was named for his own father (inferred). ¶2

Burial: 'Festo Gratiarum actionis pro recuperata Pace / Tiugum - Fun: Lars Marcussøn Eithungierde: 67 an:' (Leikanger ministerialbok 1756-1770, IIIF canvas 17, left page, first entry). The feast heading comes just before Dom. II post Epiph. (15 Jan 1758); canvas 15 ends 18 Dec 1757, so the burial lies between those dates (inferred). Aged 67, so born about 1690-91 (inferred from the age). This confirms his patronymic Marcussen, which before rested on the probate index. ¶4

Eitungjerde has no entry in the 1664 or 1666 censuses for Vangsnes sogn (35519 v150 p. 277 and v168 pp. 310-311; 35573 v114 p. 209) or Tjugum sogn, and no holder named Niels appears in Vangsnes in either year, so the father of Marchus Nielsen (b. ~1655, Eitungjerde 1701) is not identified from these lists. The only Marcus is 'Marcus 9 aar' (1666) beside Johannes, holder at Vangsnes, aged 60, i.e. Marcus Johannessen b. ~1657 (= 1701 Wangsnes 'Marchus Johansen' b. 1653), not Marchus Nielsen. Read. ¶5

Probate after him held 1 May 1758 at Eitungjerde, church-owned land (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll A 7b, No. 14, fol. 408a-408b; index name 'Lars Markusen, Eitorngjerde'). Heirs: widow Unni Hermundsdatter (hovedlod and half the estate); 'Ældste Søn Marcus Larsen Thue'; 'Ældste Datter Giøe [reading uncertain] Lars Datter', married to Niels [H]ansøn[?] Eitungjerde; 'Anden Søn Hermund Larsen'; and the minor daughter Christi Larsdatter, whose share her brother-in-law (svoger) Niels Eitungjerde received as guardian. Read from the scan; the heading narrative is only partly legible. ¶1
