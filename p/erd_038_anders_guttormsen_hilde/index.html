Life story

1701 census: Anders, aged 6, son of Guttorm Olsen, opsidder at Hilde. This gives a birth about 1695 and conflicts with Aaland's death age of 71 in 1760 (about 1689). The census also lists his brothers Hans (16) and Rasmus (3). Rasmus Guttormsen Hilde was guardian of Anders's son Guttorm in 1760. Father link Confirmed by the census; mother (Kari Hansdatter, per Aaland only) Speculative. ¶5

Guardianship protocol no. 1 (1711-1735), p. 8: Anders Guttormsen Hilde held as guardian 32 rd 1 ort 12 s for 'Arne Hegdal for his wife[?] Kari Guttormsdatter' under a probate deed of 14 June 1727. Kari Guttormsdatter is probably his sister. That is an inference: the entry does not name the deceased. ¶6

Aaland 1932: Aaland's Hilde farm history (Del 2 3 2, p. 33, bruk nr. 4 'Guttormbruket') lists 'Anders Guttormson, † 71 a. g. 1760, br. 1715; sk. 1740 p. br. Dømd 1727 for aa ha flutt merki. * Jartrud Olsd. Hilde, † 84 a. g. 1791': he died in 1760 aged 71, farmed the bruk from 1715, took the deed to it in 1740, and was convicted in 1727 of moving boundary marks. He married Jartrud Olsdatter of Hilde. ¶1

Aaland 1932: Aaland's previous holder of the bruk is Guttorm Olson, born about 1668, farmer before 1688, married Kari (Hansdatter), who owned bruk nr. 2 at Lunde in Stryn; in 1701 Guttorm Olson had sons Hans (16), Anders (6) and Rasmus (3). Anders Guttormson is presumably that son. Aaland's figures disagree on his birth: 6 years old in 1701 gives about 1695, but 71 at death in 1760 gives about 1689. The 1701 census (manntall) and his 1760 burial entry would settle it. ¶2

Probate: Nordfjord skifteprotokoll A 13, fol. 387-389, October 1760 (day unclear). Probate after Anders Guttormsen Hilde. His widow Giertrud Olsdatter was present with 'the 5 children the late man had with her': Guttorm 24, Ole 18, Hans 1[?], Kari 25 and Gunnild 1[?]. Rasmus Guttormsen Hilde was Guttorm's guardian. Confidence raised from Probable to Confirmed. The probate names Guttorm, aged 24, as his son by Giertrud Olsdatter, and that Guttorm took over the holding (Guttormbruket) in 1763 according to Aaland 1932, p. 33. ¶4

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Probable as the father of Guttorm Andersen (see his page): Aaland does not say 'son', but Guttorm Andersson took the deed to this same bruk in 1763, after Anders's death in 1760 and the short tenure of the widow's second husband. His patronymic matches, and he named his own eldest son Anders. A compiled bygdebok supports Probable at most. Guttorm's baptism (about 1739, Innvik) or the 1763 deed in the Nordfjord mortgage register (pantebok) would confirm it. ¶3
