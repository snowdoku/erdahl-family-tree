Life story

Heir as 'Sønnen Biørn Joensen' in his father Jon Bjørnsen's probate at Bale, 1 Oct 1770, with mother Guri Olsdatter and sister Brithe Joensdatter (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll 8b, fol. 636a-636b). Direct record of both parent links. ¶9

Lokalhistoriewiki (article on Marie Falch) calls John Bjørnsen (1780-1873) a son of Bjørn Jonsen and Turid Kristensdatter of Gjerde, and Jon/John later ran the trading post and inn at Balholmen (1803-1811) before buying Kvalheim. ¶4

Farmer at Gjerde, Leikanger in 1801. ¶1

1801 children: Hans (~1778), John (~1780), Torbjør (~1782), Christen (~1787), Ole (~1790), Johannes (~1794), Bjørn (~1797), Guri (~1799). Christen and Guri follow the grandparents Christen and Gurid. His sister Britha Johnsdatter, 45, lived with them. ¶3

Probate 1804: 'Gaardmand Biørn Johnsen Gierde', a sworn lagrettemann (court juror), was one of the two appraisers and witnesses at the probate of Peder Marchussen of Tendingen on 23 June 1804 (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll A 12, fol. 320b-321a, No. 46). ¶5

Probate 1810: 'Gaardmand Biørn Johnsen Gierde' was present as the husband of the heir Turi Christensdatter at the probate after her mother Randi Andersdatter Sværen (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll A 14, fol. 116a-117a, No. 26). ¶7

Probate 'Bjørn Jonsen, Gjerde' is in the Ytre Sogn probate register for 1828-1833. A genealogy page (kgroenha.net, ahnentafel no. 232) gives him as born 1751 at Bahle (Bale), Balestrand, married 23 Jun 1777 to Thuri Christensdatter, and died 1828 at Gjerde. The Leikanger church books before 1792 are not indexed, so the birth and marriage could not be checked against the records. ¶2

Probate (skifte): No. 46, taken up 21 October 1828 at the district judge's office at Vange and closed 5 November 1828, after 'afdøde Gaardmand Bjørn Johnsen Gjerde af Tjugums Skibrede og Tjugums Sogn' (the late farmer). Witnesses were the lensmann John Opdahl and Hans U[sting?]. Heirs: the widow Thuri Christensdatter Gjerde (half); the sons John Bjørnsen Kvalem ('Myndig Søn', a son of age), Christen Bjørnsen Skaasen, Ole Bjørnsen Teigen and Johannes Bjørnsen Hagen; the daughters Torbjør and Guri Bjørnsdatter; and the children of a son Hans (inferred dead): Bjørn Hansen, Bothilde, Thuri and Brithe Hansdatter Gjerde, whose guardians were Ole Aamundsen Fjærestad, Thor Andersen Tjugum and Christen Bjørnsen Skaasen. ¶6

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Confidence raised from Probable to Confirmed as the father of John Bjørnsen Kvalem and husband of Turi Christensdatter: his 1828 probate names 'Myndig Søn John Bjørnsen Kvalem' and the widow Thuri Christensdatter.
