About the family

Father of Bjørn Ingebrigtsen of Store Evjen: the census names his son Bjørn at Evjen; Bjørn appears later as 'Biørn Ingebr. St. Efjen' (holder 1719-1763, buried 1764) and named his own son Ingebrigt (1725); the other three Evjen households of 1701 have no Ingebrigt and their only Bjørn is 'Biørn Jonsen' 12. Rated Confirmed for this link; note that Bjørn's own link to his son Ingebrigt is Probable. ¶2

Father added from the bygdebok, Probable: Selbu i fortid og nåtid vol. 6, Overvik (canvases 324-325): 'Ola Ingebrigtsen [...] født [ca.] 1610 [...] hadde [i] 1665 [sønnene] Bersvend [1656], Gunder 1659 [og] Ingebrigt 1664'; 'Brukere': '-1665-1686- Ola Ingebriktsen, f. ca. 1610 [...] 3. Ingebrikt, f. 1665, [g.m.] Marit [...] Lien, [på] Stor-Evja'; and vol. 5 (canvas 216) has Marit Bjørnsdatter (b. ca. 1649) married to 'Ingebrikt Olsen Overvik' at Stor-Evja. This settles the lead (b) - the Over-Vigen 'Ingebricht Olsen 6 maaneder' of 1666 - in the book's view. ¶10

Life story

Origin leads from the 1666 vicar's manntall (no Ole with a son Ingebrigt at Store or Lille Evjen in 1664-66): (a) 'Ingebrigt Olsen 7' at Kolset (no. 36), son of Olle Tolsen[?] (60) - born about 1659, close to the 1701 age (44, born about 1657); (b) 'Ingebricht Olsen 6 maaneder' at Over-Vigen, son of Ole (Olluf) Ingebrichtsen - which would make him a brother of Gunnar Olsen Vigen, but born about 1665, eight years later than his 1701 age implies. Neither is linked; the next record is his burial in 16310 (1699-1737) with an age, or a probate (card index 77804). ¶4

1701 manntall, Selbu, farm Efvien (Evjen) (canvas 120, book p. 219, crop checked by the orchestrator): farmer 'Ingbrigt Olsen', aged 44 (so born about 1657); son 'biørn Ingbrigtsen' 10; servant Haller[?] Jonsen 30. His father was an Ole. ¶1

Probate (skifte) after him, held 19 Jun 1744 on Store Evjen in Selbu, entered as No. 1 of 1744 in Skifteprotokoll 3 (24753, fol. 93b, view 114). The ink is very faded; read it from the full-resolution scan (3405 px) in enhanced strips (grey-scale curves). Reading: 'Giør Vitterligt at A° 1744 den 19 Juny blev... forretaget paa gaarden Store Evjen udi Selbo... efter afg. Ingebrigt Ols[en], som boede og døde paa forn[evnte] gaard, imellem hans... Arvinger... 1 Biørn Ingebrigtsen Sønnen...'. The heirs that follow include a child who had died, leaving children ('efterladte Børn'), one of them Thomas, connected with a husmann (cottager) under the farm Grelloe[?]; then '3 Marit Ingebrigtsdatter', married ('hustru'); '4 Ragnild Ingebrigtsdatter 28 aar gl.', '5 Ingebor Ingebrigtsd. 26 aar gl.', '6 Kari Ingebrigtsd. 24 aar gl.'; and a Ragnild or Ingebor named as the wife of Haaver Olsen Store Evjen. The guardians include the brother Biørn Ingebrigtsen. The numbering and the middle heirs are uncertain, but the name of the first heir, the son Biørn Ingebrigtsen, is clear. ¶7

The probate settles his death (before 19 Jun 1744, at Store Evjen, aged about 87) and confirms from a second primary record, after the 1701 manntall, that Bjørn Ingebrigtsen was his son. The daughters aged 24-28 in 1744 (born about 1716-1720, when he was about 60) suggest a second, younger wife; the opening lines name no widow, so she was probably dead by 1744. His parents are still unknown: the probate does not name them. ¶8

Correction to the earlier reading of his 1744 probate (24753 fol. 93b): the married daughter named as the wife of Haaver Olsen Store Evjen is 'Mali Ingebrigtsdatter', not Ragnild or Ingebor. Re- from the full-resolution scan, upscaled strips: '[Ma]li Ingebrigtsdatter Haavar Olsen Stor Efjens Hustrue', and a few lines later 'ligesaa Mali Ingebrigtsd.'. Ragnild (28), Ingebor (26) and Kari (24) are the unmarried daughters. Mali is added as his daughter, and she is the probable mother of the direct ancestor Christen Christensen Lien. ¶9

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Probable lead, faded text: Skifteprotokoll 3 (24753), fol. 93b (view 114), entry of 1744, listed in the volume's typed register as 'Ingebrigt Olsen, Storevjen, [Selbu], 1744'. The opening reads '...efter afg. Ingebrigt Olsen, som boede og døde paa forn. gaard...', and the next line appears to name a son '[Sr?] Biørn Ingebrigtsen'; further heirs include daughters Marit, Ingebor, Kari and Ragnild Ingebrigtsdatter (ages 24-28 where legible) and a Haagen Olsen. The ink is very faint and the reading of Biørn is not certain. If it is right, Ingebrigt died between 1701 and 1744, aged up to about 87, and the young daughters point to a second marriage. Needs an expert re-read before any link or date is changed. ¶6

Records that disagree 1

Contradiction to resolve before any raise: the 1701 manntall gives him 44 (born about 1657) and the 1744 probate implies about 87, against 1664-65 in the 1666 list and the bygdebok. Kolset's 'Ingebrigt Olsen 7' (1666) remains a rival by age. A record naming his father (e.g. a skifte after Ole Ingebrigtsen Overvik, protocol 1, 24749, 1689-1701) is needed. ¶11

Open questions and the next record to read 2

What would carry this further: Ingebrigt Olsen's burial in 16310 (1699-1737) with an age; a Stjør- og Verdal probate after him (scanned series 24749-24764); the 1664/1666 manntall for an Ole at Evjen with a son Ingebrigt. ¶3

Biography gaps: his exact birth date (c.1657) and his marriage, which falls before the 1699 register, are not in any record read, and his wife is not named. His death, death place and burial are not found: the 16310 Evjen entries for 1724-38 were read with no burial of him noted, 1699-1723 are not reported checked, and the 77804 probate index (1689-1742) has not yet been searched for him. The next records are that index, then the unindexed Stjør- og Verdal protocols 24752 (1737-42) and 24753 (1742-60). ¶5
