Life story

Baptized 6 Nov 1814 at Borgund as 'Birte Maria Andrine', daughter of Knud Larsen of Lille Nørve. In the 1815 census (30 Apr 1815) she is Berte Marie Knudsdatter, aged 1/2, in Knud Larssen's household at Lille Nørve. ¶3

Confirmed 18 Oct 1829 at Borgund as Berthe Maria Knudsdatter, born 1814 at Lille Nørve. Parents: Knud Larsen and Catrine Mortensdatter. ¶2

Married Elias Peter Olaus Jonsen 13 Jul 1846 at Borgund as Berte Marie Knutsdatter of Lille Nørve, born about 1815; her father is named as Knut Larsen. ¶1

Went by Marie in later records. Widow at Valderhaug in 1900 (born 1814). ¶4

Widow at Valderhaug in the 1900 census. ¶5

She lived to a very great age. The 1910 census (1 December 1910) still finds her at Valderhaug in her son Martinus's household, entered as 'Berte H. Nørve', widow, born 2 October 1813 in Borgund, Søndmør, a 'føderaadskone' - a woman kept on the farm on a retirement allowance. The surname is the farm she came from, Lille Nørve, and the initial should be K for Knudsdatter. She was then about 96. ¶6

Her burial has not been found. It is not indexed for the farm Valderhaug and not among the indexed Berte Knudsdatter burials for Borgund, so it must be read from the Giske or Borgund burial register for 1911 onwards. ¶8

1 correction · 1 alternative set aside

Corrections 1

Correction: the 1910 census gives her birth as 2 October 1813. Her baptism on 6 November 1814 and the 1815 census, where she is 'aged 1/2' on 30 April 1815, both show she was born in the autumn of 1814, so the 1910 year is one too early; the day and month, 2 October, may well be right and would place her birth about five weeks before her baptism. ¶7

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Not our person: the Bergen probate court death register 1918-1919 (folio 191, pp. 378-379, no. 245) entry for Berthe Marie Knutsdatter Heggernæs, died 17 Feb 1919, gives her as a former servant girl (fhv. tjenestepige), aged 79, living in an old people's home in Bergen. Age 79 means she was born about 1839-40, while this Berte Marie was born in 1814 and was the widow of Elias Johnsen at Valderhaug, so the Bergen woman is a different person. ¶9
