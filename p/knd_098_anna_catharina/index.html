Confidence: the grade and how it changed 3

Graded on that link: the Metterzimmern baptism of 1 Sep 1718 names the parents 'Jacob, Anna Catharina' (record 5509298, image 1184926-00079). Continuity: the 1737 Metterzimmern bride Catharina Schopf names her father 'Hn Jacob' (record 905517933); same parish, matching age, no rival Catharina Schopf baptised 1700-1730. The same couple's other baptisms give the wife as 'Maria Catharina' (1715, 1722); the variant is taken as the same woman, so her first name may be Maria Catharina. Surname not indexed. Images not seen; graded on the faithfully transcribed index row under the overnight rule for this collection. ¶1b

Coordinator review: held at Probable rather than Confirmed. Ancestry collection 61023 index rows do not label who is father, mother or spouse; the BRIEF audit 5 rule is that unlabelled index rows give Probable until the scan is read, or a FamilySearch index with labelled parent fields is found (queued S0233). The naming evidence in the note above is otherwise strong, so this person is a priority for an image read. ¶2

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed, graded on her link to her daughter Catharina Schopf (see her page). FamilySearch, 'Germany, Births and Baptisms, 1558-1898', V4RW-6HS (twin FamilySearch, 'Deutschland, ausgewählte evangelische Kirchenbücher 1500-1971' (= 'Germany, Lutheran Baptisms, Marriages, and Burials, 1500-1971') QPGY-685L): Catharina Schopf, christened Metterzimmern 1 Sep 1718, Parents 'Jacob Schopf, Anna Catharina'. Continuity (19 years, audit 7 rule 1): the father stays at Metterzimmern in the gap (children of Jacob Schopf the Schultheiß there 1717 and 1722); the far record, the Metterzimmern marriage of 3 Sep 1737 (QPGY-D23L), ties the bride to him by the labelled Father-in-law field 'Hn Jacob Schopfen', same village; no rival named (the Anna Catharina Schopf baptised 1719 at Ditzingen to Jacob x Regina, QP2Y-DLZQ, is another parish and another mother). Read signed in, 2 Nov 2026; labelled index fields, image not seen (partner-only). ¶3
