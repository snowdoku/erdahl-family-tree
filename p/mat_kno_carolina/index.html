About the family

Mother of Erna Knodel. Her daughter's 1939 marriage registration gives the mother's maiden name in a hand that reads 'Carolina Arndt Schmidt' (the middle word is uncertain; it may be a second given name or a second surname), born Germany. An Anna Karoline Knodel died at Surrey, BC on 20 Jan 1974 aged 92 (born about 1881-82) and is a candidate for her; that is unproven. ¶1

Mother (Speculative): Maria née Adam is entered as her mother as the leading candidate only. She was the wife of a Wilhelm Schmidt (identified, Speculatively, with the Wilhelm born 24 Aug 1854 at Atmagea) and mother of Wilhelmine Schmidt, born 9 Jan 1885 at Ciucurova, confirmed at Constanța in 1899 while living at Cogealia, who married Daniel Frank at Cogealia in 1905. The name fits Geni's 'Anna Maria' (a lead). No record yet names Karolina as Maria Adam's daughter; her confirmation in the Constanța church book (FHL film 1768339) would settle it. Rival mothers, all named Karoline, are listed on her father's record. ¶16

Sister: her unmarried sister Anna Schmidt, born 24 Nov 1888 in Romania, died on 31 July 1906 aged 17 3/4 in Gottlieb Knodel's house at Pfeilsdorf. Gottlieb registered the death on 1 Aug 1906 and called her his unmarried sister-in-law (niezamężna szwagierka). The act names Anna's parents as Wilhelm Schmidt, then living in Romania, and his wife Anna Maria née Adam (Standesamt Pfeilsdorf deaths 1906 no. 23, AP Toruń 69/1205/0/2.3/1013, scan 221). ¶17

Life story

Burial: her BC death registration says that her remains were removed to 'Kinderseley, Saskatchewan'. Find a Grave memorial 229408825 and the family gravestone ('KAROLINA 1881-1974') place her in Eatonia Cemetery, in the Kindersley district, beside her husband. The memorial gives her birth as 9 November 1881 in 'Ukraine'. The BC registration, informed by her daughter, says 19 November 1881 in Romania, and that is preferred. ¶2

Emigration: As 'Karolina Knodel' (index birth about 1881) she landed at Quebec on 22 July 1928 from the Megantic (from Southampton) with her husband and three youngest children, bound for Winnipeg. Keyword tests of her index entry return 'Roumania' (birthplace, no village matched among more than 60 Bessarabian and Dobruja colony names tried), 'Polish', 'German' and 'married'. ¶13

Dobruja origin: Paul Traeger's survey of the Dobruja Germans (1922, pp.137-138) names Pfeilsdorf in Kreis Briesen, where she and Gottlieb Knodel were settlers from 1905, as 'an almost pure Dobruja peasant colony'. Peasants from Anadolchioi, Cogeala (Cogealia), Fachria, Cobadin, Sarighiol, Mangalia and other Dobruja villages took farms there from the Prussian Settlement Commission. This, with her BC record's birthplace 'Romania' and her husband's 'Russia', makes it very probable that she was a Dobruja German, born in the Romanian Dobruja in 1881. Her birth record has not been found. ¶8

Birthplace, the 1928 manifest (village uncertain): on the Megantic manifest of 22 Jul 1928 (LAC T-14745 frame 00366) Karolina Knodel, wife, aged 47, gives her birthplace as 'Roumania' and a faintly typed town read 'Tu?s?za', best reading 'Tulsza' (alternatives Tuisza, Tulcza). This may be Tulcea (German Tultscha), the Northern Dobruja district town, but that is an inference only. Two of her sisters' records point the same way: her sister Anna's 1888 birth record gives a village re-read as 'Tulcke' (Anna Schmidt), and her sister Rosa Therese was born in 1890 at Atmagea, a German village in Tulcea district (Rosa Therese Schmidt). Her stated age of 47 is one year more than her recorded birth on 19 Nov 1881 gives (46 on 22 Jul 1928). Her birth field is left unchanged ('village not yet proved') until a clear reading is found. What would settle the spelling: exact-keyword tests of 'Tulsza', 'Tulcza' and 'Tuisza' on her Ancestry dbId 1263 index entry, or her baptism in a Dobruja register. ¶20

Birthplace, index keyword tests (Oct 2026): her Ancestry dbId 1263 record 2638380 matches the keyword 'Roumania', but none of 23 town spellings tested matches it, among them Tulsza, Tulcza, Tuisza, Tuiza, Tusza, Tulcea, Tultscha, Tulcia, Atmagea, Atmadscha, Cogealac, Ciucurova, Tschukurowa and Dobrudscha. Each miss rules out only that exact word, so the manifest's faint 'Tu?s?za' remains unread; the manifest image (LAC T-14745 frame 00366) is what would settle it. ¶22

Cogealia: the family's remembered birthplace of her son Wilhelm, 'Cogelea', is closer in form to Cogealia (German Kodschalie; Cogealia until 1929, then Valea Neagră, and Lumina since 1965, about 15 km north of Constanța) than to Cogealac (Kodschalak). The candidate Schmidt family lived at Cogealia from the 1890s. Cogealia belonged to the civil commune of Cicârciu (Sibioara) from 1880 to 1908, and its Evangelical families were served from Constanța. ¶10

1931 census: Listed as 'Cataline' Knodel, wife, 50, born Rumania, both parents born Rumania, immigrated 1928, alien, German-speaking Lutheran homemaker who spoke no English, in her husband's household on NW 27-25-24-W3, RM of Newcombe No. 260, Saskatchewan. ¶12

Name: the daughter's 1939 marriage reading 'Carolina Arndt Schmidt' is very probably 'Carolina Anna Schmidt' or a garbled 'Anna Karolina'; she is Anna Karoline or Anna Karolina in every later record. ¶4

At Pfeilsdorf: The Standesamt Pfeilsdorf registers name her as Karolina/Caroline Knodel née Schmidt, Evangelical, wife of the settler Gottlieb Knodel, at the births of Gottlieb (1905), Anna (1906), Sophie (1908), Ludwig (1910) and Erna (1915). In the 1906 act her name is written 'Karoline A[n]e Knodel z d. Schmidt', a short second given name beginning with A - almost certainly the 'Anna' of her later name Anna Karolina, and very probably what was written as 'Arnt'/'Arndt' on Erna's 1939 marriage record, which is therefore not a surname. ¶6

Widowhood: She appears as 'Mrs G. Knodel' on the 1958 voters list for Kindersley, and as 'Mrs K. Knodel' in 1962, so she stayed in the Eatonia district for some years after Gottlieb's death in 1958. Her 1974 death registration says she had been in British Columbia only nine months, living with her daughter Frieda at Surrey. ¶14

Emigration: Canadian passenger-list index (Ancestry 'Canada, Incoming Passenger Lists, 1865-1935', anonymous search) lists Gottlieb Knodel (b. abt 1879), Karolina (abt 1881), Frieda (abt 1912), Erna (abt 1915) and Otto (abt 1918) on one manifest page, arriving 22 July 1928, destination Winnipeg, Man. Index keywords that match this record (tested one by one) are Quebec, Southampton, Megantic, Polish, German, Farmer and Married: i.e. the White Star-Dominion liner Megantic, Southampton to Quebec, the family travelling as Polish nationals of German 'race'. The manifest image (Quebec reel, 1925-35 series) has not been seen; it should give their last residence (expected Plużnica) and the relative they were joining. ¶15

Church: the family belonged to the Evangelical (Lutheran) parish of Villisaß, Kreis Culm, which served the Protestant settlers of Pfeilsdorf (Meyers Gazetteer lists only a Catholic parish church at Pfeilsdorf itself). The Ancestry index of German Lutheran registers (dbId 61250) has Villisaß baptisms of five of the couple's children, each with the relatives indexed as Gottlieb and Karoline (or Caroline): Gottlieb on 24 Dec 1905, Anna on 6 Jan 1907, Sophia on 25 Oct 1908, Ludwig on 21 May 1910 and Frida on 30 Mar 1912. Each follows the child's civil birth at Pfeilsdorf by 0 to 52 days (Ludwig's indexed date is the same as his birth date, so the index may give his birth rather than his baptism). Erna (1915) and Otto (1918) are not in the index. These are index entries only; the register images, which would name the godparents, have not been read. The same parish baptised the children of Jakob Knodel and Katharina Reister (1905-1911) and of Friedrich Eberle and Friederike née Knodel (1904-1910). ¶19

Her birthplace 'Romania' (her BC death record, 1974) points to the Dobruja, Romanian from 1878, where Bessarabian Germans founded colonies such as Cogealac (Kodschalak), Tariverde and Fachria in the 1870s-80s. Her birth record has not been found; Dobruja Lutheran registers are not in the St Petersburg Consistory extracts. A Karolina Schmidt born 19 Nov 1881 at Lichtenfeld (Güldendorf parish, Kherson) - the same day and month as her stated birth date - died there aged 21 days on 10 Dec 1881, so is not her. ¶7

Corrections 1

Correction: parents. The earlier notes on her saying that no record names her as Wilhelm Schmidt's daughter, that her parent link 'is not made in the tree', and that her mother Maria née Adam is Speculative are superseded. Her father 'Willem Schmidt' is named on her 1974 BC death registration (Confirmed), and her mother Anna Maria née Adam is now Probable, from the 1906 Pfeilsdorf death act of her sister Anna, which names Anna's parents. Her own mother is still not named in any record found; her confirmation or marriage entry in the Constanța church book (FHL film 1768339) would settle it. ¶18

Alternatives considered and set aside 2

Geni (lead only, not evidence): Geni's public search listing names her parents as Wilhelm Schmidt and Anna Maria Schmidt and gives her about ten siblings, including a Theresa Schmidt. Her BC death record independently names her father as 'Willem Schmidt'. ¶3

Parents, a candidate only (Speculative): the likeliest match is Wilhelm Schmidt, born 24 Aug 1854 at Atmagea, the oldest German colony of the Dobruja, and his wife Maria née Adam. The mother's name Maria fits Geni's 'Anna Maria', and the father's fits her BC death record's 'Willem Schmidt'. In June 1879 Wilhelm was still single, living in his widowed mother Luise's house at Atmagea (Atmagea community book, printed 1975), so a first child born in November 1881 fits. His daughter Wilhelmine was born at Ciucurova on 9 Jan 1885 (28 Dec 1884 Old Style). She was confirmed at Constanța in 1899, living at Cogealia, and married Daniel Frank at Cogealia in 1905 (EWZ 1940 files; Ciucurova village family book). No record yet names Karolina as Wilhelm's daughter, so the link is not made in the tree. ¶9

Open questions and the next record to read 2

Next record: the Evangelical church book of Constanța (Family History Library film 1768339, filmed 1991, which includes confirmations 1895-1940 and is cited by the Ciucurova family book) should hold her confirmation (about 1895-96) and her marriage to Gottlieb Knodel (about 1900) if the family belonged to that parish. Such entries normally name the parents. ¶11

Lead only (index, Villisaß burials): Hildegard Olga Schmidt was buried in the Villisaß parish in 1914 with the relative indexed as 'Gottl.' (Ancestry dbId 61250, image 0245316-00478, not read). No connection to Karolina's Schmidt family is known; recorded so the image (queued as S0165) can be checked for the parents. ¶21
