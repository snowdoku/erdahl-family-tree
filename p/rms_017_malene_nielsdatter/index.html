About the family

Mother of Peer (Peder Nielsen), born 22 Feb 1833 at Nordre Sunde, Madla, Håland, and wife of Nils Thorsen (baptism 1833, 'Malene Neilsdr'; confirmation 1847, 'Malene Neelsdatter'). ¶1

Parents (Speculative): the 1801 census of Hjelmeland has a Malene Nielsdatter aged 10 (b. about 1791) at Øye, daughter of Niels Olsen (57, farmer with land) and Malene Pedersdatter (49), with siblings Niels (23), Martha (20), Siri (18) and Peder (12). The 1816 bride lived at Øye, and the 1865 widow was 73 (b. about 1792) and born in Hjelmeland, so the ages agree within a year. No record yet names her father, so this is Speculative: her own baptism in Hjelmeland about 1790-91, or the scan of the 1816 marriage, would settle it. The names of her children (Niels 1825, Anna Malene 1828, Peder 1831 and Peer 1833) fit naming after Niels Olsen, Malene Pedersdatter and her brother Peder, which supports but does not prove it. ¶5

Parents' probate: Ryfylke skifteprotokoll D 19 (DA 23902), fol. 293a-b, No. 7 (scan): 'Skifteforretning efter de afgangne Selvejer Niels Olsøn og Hustrue Mallene Pedersdatter af Gaarden Øye i Hielmelands Skibrede', begun 12 Oct 1824 and closed 27 May 1825. The heirs are 'Boets Arvinger, nemlig': 1. the eldest son Niels Nielsen Øye, farmer and freeholder; 2.-3. the sons of a dead son Peder Nielsen (by his marriage with [?Gjertrud] Pedersdatter of Næs under [?Sørbø] in Årdal skipreide): Niels Pedersen 6 and Peder Pedersen 4; 4. the eldest daughter Susanna Nielsdatter, married to the cottager John Andersen, Houen under Gehland, Hjelmeland; 5. the second daughter Siri Nielsdatter, married to the farmer Niels Nielsen Øvrebøe, Årdal; 6. 'Tredie Datter Mallene Nielsdatter, gift med Niels Thorsøn Bukken, boende paa en Plads under Gaarden S[u/a]nde i H[?] Sogn under Jæderen og Dalernes Skriveri'; 7. the fourth and youngest daughter Martha Nielsdatter, married to Michel Larsen, Haneraand, Hausken skipreide. (The farm name reads 'Sande' or 'Sunde' and the parish name is cramped; 'under Jæderen og Dalernes Skriveri', the husband's name and Malene's name are clear.) This names her parents and shows her, in 1824, as the wife of 'Niels Thorsøn Bukken' living on a place under Sunde in Jæren og Dalane, i.e. the Madla household. ¶13

Life story

Birth year: aged 10 in Feb 1801 (born about 1790-91) and 73 at the census of 31 Dec 1865 (born about 1792). ¶6

Out-migration checks: the Høyland out-migration list (Høyland ministerialbok 1815-1825, DA 15798, IIIF canvases 157-160, pp. 578-585) has no Niels Thorsen or Malene Nielsdatter in 1821 nos. 3-12 or 1822 nos. 1-13. Together with reading of canvases 153-156, the Høyland list is negative from its start to the end of 1822, so the origin on the Håland 1822 in-migration entry is still unresolved. The Hjelmeland out-migration list (Hjelmeland ministerialbok 1816-1834, DA 15782, canvas 171) begins in 1824, with a note that the previous priest failed to keep it for 1818-1822, so it cannot record the couple's departure. ¶10

Re-proof attempt, no change to her parents' grade. (1) Thore's baptism read on the scan (Håland ministerialbok 1815-1845, DA 15813, pp. 76-77, no. 15): born 16 Jul and baptised 22 Jul 1821 at Malde (Madla) church (the index gives the baptism date as 01-22, which misreads 'ditto' = July); parents 'Niels Thorsen og Malene Niels Datter', of Nor Sunde, Malde Sogn; godparents Anna... of Haga, Marthe Samuelsdatter, Sven Jonasen gaardmand of Sunde, Samuel... of Sunde and Niels Peersen gaardmand of Nor Sunde. No godparent from Øye or Hjelmeland, and no birthplace given, so it does not bridge the 1816 Hjelmeland bride to the Madla wife. (2) CAUTION, a named namesake at the same farm: the Håland index shows a second Malene Nielsdatter at Sunde, wife of Peder (Peer) Thorsen, with children Peder (bapt. 7 Dec 1810, Sør Sunde), Rasmus (1812, Nor Sunde), Anna Mallene (4 Dec 1814, Nor Sunde) and Peer (born 17 Nov, bapt. 24 Nov 1816, 'Peer Thorssen' and 'Malene Nielsdat', Malde Sogn; also indexed with a daughter Inger the same month). Peder Thorsen of Nor Sunde is probably Niels Thorsen's brother. She was married to Peder by 1810, so she cannot be the Hjelmeland bride of Niels Thorsen in 1816, and she is not a rival for Peter's mother (his 1833 baptism names Niels Thorsen). But she IS a rival for any record at Nordre Sunde that names only 'Malene/Malli Nielsdatter' without a husband: the 1868 burial of 'Huusmands Enke Malli Nielsdatter', 72, of Nordre Sunde, needs checking against Peder Thorsen's widow before it is used as evidence for this Malene. The 1865 census entry (widow, born Hjelmeland, in the household of Niels Nielsen, born 1825 to Niels Thorsen) is the record that points to Niels's widow. ¶12a

Marriage: as Malene Nielsdatter, an unmarried woman of Øye, she married Niels Thorsen of Buchen on 17 Nov 1816 in Fister church, Hjelmeland (marriages 1816, pp. 256-257, no. 26, index). ¶4

Migration (scan): she is entered as 'Malin Nilsdatter' with Neils Thorsen in the Håland in-migration list for 1822 (p. 627), arriving apparently from Høyland parish together with a Hans Nilsen and Kisti Andersdatter. Hans Nilsen may be her brother (inference only). ¶7

Baptism (scan): Hjelmeland ministerialbok 1760-1799 (DA 1599), baptisms 1791, p. 114, no. 8 (IIIF canvas 61): 'Mallene Niels D. Øye døbt den 23de Januarij'; godparents Jone Westersøe, Søren Øye, Enger Brevig, Susane Tontland and Metta Øye. The entry gives the father only by patronymic. Niels Olsen was the only Niels living at Øye in the 1801 census (all 33 Øye residents checked), and his daughter Malene was then 10, so this is his daughter. The godparents fit his family: Søren Øye (his stepfather Søren Sørensen was head of N. Øye in 1762, or a namesake), 'Susane Tontland' from his wife's home farm Tuntland, and Metta Øye (Mette Hansdatter, a lodger at Øye in 1801). That this child is the Madla widow remains Probable, as before (1816 bride 'of Øye', 1865 widow born in Hjelmeland). Her ages drift later (73 in Dec 1865, 72 at death in 1868), so the 1791 baptism sits two to four years earlier than those ages imply; that drift is common for elderly people and does not outweigh the farm and family match. ¶8

Death (scan): Håland ministerialbok, Madla 1854-1870 (DA source 1618), burials 1868, p. 245, no. 6 (IIIF canvas 64): 'Huusmands Enke Malli Nielsdatter', 72 years, of Nordre Sunde, died 29 Jan 1868 and buried 4 Feb 1868. The index (pg00000004706331) gives the year of death as 1867, but the page is headed 1868 and the entry stands among the January 1868 burials. A cottager's widow named Malene Nielsdatter at Nordre Sunde is her: in the 1865 census she was the widowed retirement-allowance holder in her son Niels Nielsen's household there. ¶9

In the 1865 census she is a widow (føderaadskone, an elderly person living on a retirement allowance from the holding), aged 73 and born in Hjelmeland prestegjeld, in the household of her son Niels Nielsen, a widowed cottager with land at Nordre Sunde, Håland. The birth year about 1792 (aged 73 at the census date of 31 Dec 1865) and the birthplace come from that census (index). ¶2
