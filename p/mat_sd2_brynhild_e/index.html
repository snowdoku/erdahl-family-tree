About the family

Oline Slind's great-great-grandmother, and a person wave 3 did not know existed. The 1825 census of Selbu, rural residence 0060 Eidem, household 1, person 004: 'Brynhild?? Eriksd., 28, Hustru' - the question marks are the transcriber's doubt about the given name, not about the patronymic - wife of the lodger Gunder Johnsen, with their four daughters Beret 10, Maglie 9, Sigrid 6 and Marit 2. ¶1

Parents: Erik Torstensen (see his page) and Magli Christensdatter (see her page) of Uglem. Why Probable, and what would settle it: no single record names Erik Torstensen as the father of Gunder Johnsen's wife. The link is a chain of primary records: (1) she married Gunder on 11 Jun 1815 as 'Pigen Brynhild Eriksd. Auglem' - a maid of Uglem; (2) she is the widowed mother of Ole Gundersen (bapt. 1826, son of Gunder Johnsen and Brynhild Eriksd.) at Grindhaugen in 1865 and 1875, born 1796; (3) her 1880 burial gives her birthplace as Uglem and birth year 1795; (4) the only Brynhild Eriksdatter born at Uglem in the 1790s is Brynil, born 5 Mar 1796 to Erik Torstensen Uglum and Magli Christensdatter, who is 5 in their Uglan household in 1801; (5) the bygdebok (vol. 4 p. 203) calls Gunnar Jonsen's wife Brynhild of Uglan, b. 1796. The one rival, Brynhild Eriksdatter born 12 May 1797 at Langli to Erik Pedersen, is eliminated: she married Arnt Olsen of Kval on 4 Jun 1819 with her father as witness, and was his widow at Sletne in 1865. Her second daughter was named Maglie (1816), after Magli Christensdatter, as Selbu naming custom expects. A probate (skifte) after Erik Torstensen of Uglem, d. 1829, naming his heirs (Strinda og Selbu sorenskriveri, scan-only) would make it Confirmed. ¶5

Children with Gunder Johnsen, from the baptism index: Bereth, baptised on the 20th Sunday after Trinity (8 October) 1815, father indexed as 'Gunder Jens.' of Brennan; Maglie, baptised 28 July 1816; Sigrid (Siri), baptised 17 October 1819 at Eidem; Ole, baptised 29 October 1826 at Eidem. The bygdebok vol. 4 p. 203 (word-box reconstruction, Probable) adds Marit 1823, Brynhild 1830 (died), Brynhild 1832 and Jon 1835. ¶11

Life story

Born about 1797, she was about eighteen when her eldest daughter was born, which is young but not remarkable. ¶2

Two young servants in the same household carry her patronymic: 'Svend Eriks., 19' and 'Elen Eriksd., 24'. A brother and sister of hers in service on the farm where she lodged with her husband is the natural reading, and it is the sort of cluster that will identify her own father - an ERIK born about the 1760s - when the Selbu baptisms of the 1790s are read on the scan. ¶3

Marriage: 11 June 1815 (3rd Sunday after Trinity; Easter 1815 fell on 26 March), Selbu, to Gunder Johnsen. The entry reads 'unge Mand Gunder Johns: Bromman og Pigen Brynhild Eriksd: Auglem' - the young (unmarried) man Gunder Johnsen of Bromman and the maid Brynhild Eriksdatter of Auglem, i.e. Uglem/Uglan. No parents, ages or witnesses are written. The index reads Gunder's farm as Brennan. ¶4

At her daughter Bereth's home baptism (b. 15 Sep 1815; Selbu 16312 p. 222) a godfather is 'Tosten Eriks. Auglan' - her brother Tosten Eriksen of Uglem (b. ~1793, married 1819; in Erik Torstensen's 1825 household) - supporting her descent from Erik Torstensen. ¶12

1865 census: Grindhaugen, Selbu, household 01, 'Brynild Eriksdatter, 69, Moder, e[nke], Kaarkone' (a widow living on a retirement agreement) in the household of her son Ole Gundersen, 39, husmann with land, and his wife Sigri Severinsdatter. Gunder Johnsen was therefore dead by December 1865. ¶8

1875 census: Grindhaugen (plads), Selbu, household 01, 'Brynild Eriksdatter', born 1796 in Selbu, widow, 'hfs Moder' (the head's mother), 'Moder og Kaarmenneske', still with her son Ole Gundersen (b. 1826); his daughter Brynild Olsdatter (b. 1867) is named for her. ¶9

Corrections 2

Correction: her birth was carried as 'ABT 1797' from her age of 28 in the 1825 census. Her 1880 burial gives 1795 and her birthplace as Uglem, the 1875 census gives 1796, she was 5 in February 1801, and the baptism identified with her gives 5 March 1796. 'ABT 1796' with birthplace Uglem is used. ¶6

Correction: her death was carried as 'AFT 27 NOV 1825' at Eidem. She was alive as a widow in 1865 and 1875 and was buried in 1880: 'Husmandsenke Brynel Eriksdr.' (cottager's widow), born 1795 at Uglem, residence Uglem, died 31 March and buried 4 April 1880 (Selbu burials 1880, p. 373 no. 11, scan read). The bygdebok's death year 1879 is one year off; the register is preferred. ¶7

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Not to be confused with: the other Brynhild Eriksdatter born 1797 in Selbu - born 12 May 1797 at Langli to Erik Pedersen and Giertrud Olsdatter - who married Arnt Olsen of Kval on 4 June 1819 (her father a witness), was his wife at Kvaelloe in 1825, and was the widowed mother of Ole Arntsen at Sletne in 1865. ¶10
