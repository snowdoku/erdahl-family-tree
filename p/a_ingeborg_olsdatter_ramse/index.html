About the family

NEW ANCESTOR. Widow of Svend Evensen of Nedre Ytre Ramse and mother of Evind Svendsen Ramse. ¶1

Her father was an Ole. Her son Evind was appointed guardian for her daughter Birgitha. ¶4

Father: Ole Evindsen Hillestad (d. before 2 Mar 1695), added as Ole Evindsen Hillestad, Probable. Evidence: the skifte after her brother Ole Olsøn Hillestad (Skp. 3 fol. 269b, 4 Mar 1712; cards 4991-5001) names her son 'syskinbarnet, Evind Svendsøn, nedre yttre Ramse' as guardian for the deceased's son, and the archivist's added line on card 4991 lists 'Ingeborg Olsdtr. g.m. Svend Evensen Ramse' among the deceased's sisters; the Tovdal bygdebok (Hillestad chapter) agrees. This replaces the earlier leads (the 1655 'O[lle] Ramsse' of Aggeland, and the widow Inger of Hillestad, who was probably the widow of Ole Evindsen's brother Kjetil). Her mother is not settled: Ole Evindsen's wife Birgitha Nielsdatter (d. before 12 Jun 1699) is the mother of her brother Ole, and Ingeborg's children Niels and Birgitha fit her, but the bygdebok gives Ole Evindsen two wives. ¶10

Life story

1661 land register (DA 38714 view 63, fol. 58b): 'Hillestad iij huder': owners Åmli prestebol, a [?Osmund] (1 hud 2 k.sk., with the bygsel), 'Inger Hyllestad... med sinne børn' (Inger of Hillestad with her children, 5½ k.sk.) and Michell (3 k.sk.); users [?Osmund] 2 huder 3½ k.sk., a [?Lauwig] 5½ k.sk. and Michell 3 k.sk. No Ole holds land at Hillestad in 1661. Inger with her children looks like a widow; if her late husband was an Ole, Ingeborg (b. about 1650) could be one of the children, but nothing names them. Lead only. ¶9

1664 vicar's census (35510/17 p.13): no Ole at Nedre Ytre Ramse (Effuind 77, Tolleff 25[?], son Rolff 20, servants Tron Rasolfsøn and Tarald Helgesøn). Oles elsewhere in Lille Topdal 1664: Østenaa opsidder Oluff 60, Østenaa husmand Oluff 60, Tveit husmand Oluff 77, Oluff Biørnsøn 27[?] at Øvre Ramse. None is linked to her by any record. ¶7

Her estate was settled on 25 June 1712 (Østre Råbyggelag skifteprotokoll no. 4, folio 41), the record noting that it "skifter arven mellom borna" — divides the inheritance among the children. Six are named: Evind, Tellef, Niels, Signe, Anna and Birgitha (17). ¶2

She was buying land in her own name as a widow: the file refers to a purchase deed from Hans Aamundsøn Kruse of Arendal "til Ingeborrig Olsdtr. på 3 ksk. i nedre yttre Ramse, dat. 30/10-1711". Widows in Norway held full legal capacity over property, and she used it. ¶3

Probate protocol reading: the estate opened on 25 June 1712 (Skifteprotokoll no. 4, fol. 41) and the division was concluded on 29 April 1713 (fol. 43; the same date is cited for "Skiftebrev... efter deris Moder Ingeborrig Ramse" in protocol no. 6, fol. 3). The protocol calls Niels Svendsen her youngest son and Birgitha the youngest sister. Her daughter Vilbør Svendsdatter, widow of Tellef Gullovsen Haugedal, is listed in the 1712 protocol itself, set apart from the other heirs. No parents or siblings of Ingeborg are named in the heading or heir list. ¶5

The protocol of her brother Ole Olsøn Hillestad's skifte (Skp. 3 fol. 269b-272, 4 Mar 1712, DA 24351 views 271-273) was read through. It names only two sisters of the deceased, 'Wilborrig Olsdatter' and 'Guroe Olsdatter' ('disse tvende Søstre'), with odel shares in Hillestad. Ingeborg is not named, so the protocol does not confirm her as Ole Evindsen's daughter. Her father stays Probable, on the cousin guardian ('syskinbarnet, Evind Svendsøn, nedre yttre Ramse') and the bygdebok. Her mother is still unknown: the 1695 skiftebrev after Ole Evindsen and the 1699 skiftebrev after Birgitha Nielsdatter are cited only, before the surviving protocols (Skp. 1 = DA 24349 begins 1697, and the Hillestad card series 39942/4989-5051 has no entry before 1712). ¶11

Alternatives considered and set aside 2

Caution: the guardian clause on fol. 43 could not be read securely, so the statement that her son Evind was appointed guardian for Birgitha rests on the archivist's card index alone; the protocol neither confirms nor contradicts it. ¶6

Lead for her father, not adopted: the 1655-56 odelsjordebok, Lille Topdal section (DA 76715 view 23, right page), lists 'O[lle] [struck word] Ramsse eyer i Aggeland i Iffueland Sogen ¼ b.' (an Ole of Ramse owning odel in Aggeland, Iveland). The initial is an unusual looped capital also used for two 'O[leff?] Tved' entries on the same page, so the reading Olle is probable, not certain. The same section has '[?G]ulluff Hillestad eyer i Tved som hand nu selff bruger ij hpd' (view 24, left page). Either could be the Ole who was Ingeborg's father; neither record names a daughter. ¶8
