About the family

Kari Pedersdatter's father, named as 'Peder Bersvendsen' in her baptismal entry of 29 October 1834, Selbu parish register 1824-1842 p. 105 no. 86, alongside her mother Mali Evensdatter. ¶1

Parent links: both are now supported by two censuses that state the relationship - 1801 Eidum household 07 ('Deres boern', the child of Bersvend Pederssen and Kiersten Ingebrigtsdatter) and 1825 Eidem household 1 ('Son' of the widow Kirsten). He is tied to the 1819 groom by his son Bersvend, born 27 March 1820 to Peder Bersvends and Gertrud Nilsd of Eidem, who is the boy aged 5 1/2 in the 1825 household. ¶10

Child of his first marriage: Bersvend Pedersen, born 27 March 1820 at Eidem and baptised 3 April 1820, mother Gertrud (Giertru) Nilsdatter - Kari Pedersdatter's half-brother. Giertru is absent from the 1825 household; whether she had died is not yet looked for (burials 1820-25). ¶11

Children of his first marriage, to Giertru Nielsdatter: Bersvend (born 27 March 1820) and Giertru/Gjertrue (baptised 27 January 1822, died 1 June 1823, Giertru Pedersdatter). Of his second marriage, to Mali Evensdatter: Giertrug (born 23 October 1830, Giertrud Pedersdatter) and Kari (born 20 October 1834). No other child of either couple is in the Selbu baptism index 1816-1845 under any spelling searched. ¶27

Life story

Birth: the 1790 baptism gives no birth date - the entry reads only 'Döbt Peder Par: Bersv. Ped. Eid kerst. Engelb:' with four godparents (crop). The birth therefore stays 'before 14 February 1790'. He was born after the elder Peder died on 19 June 1789, and no baptism of a Peder to this couple is in the register from July to December 1789 (canvases 133-134), so he was most likely born in January or early February 1790. The birth is now firm rather than probable: the alternative kept open in the earlier note - an elder Peder born about 1786-87 who survived - is ruled out by the 1789 burial. ¶17

Bygdebok (Probable source only): the word-box reconstruction of Selbu i fortid og naatid vol. 4, p. 206 (page image not read) lists, among the children of the Brennhaugen holder of 1792-1825, 'Per, 1790', and then a holder '1826-1867 Per... 1790,... 1855... 1819', with a wife '1794,... 1823' and a second wife 'Mali... 1796,... 1872'. The 1819 marriage, the first wife's birth year 1794 (Giertru Nielsdatter was 'b. 1794 calculated' at the 1819 wedding) and Mali Evensdatter's years fit him. Two points conflict and must be read on the page image: the death year 1855 matches none of the three burial candidates (9 May 1841, 19 December 1852, 5 August 1857); and a first wife dying in 1823 would explain her absence from the 1825 household but no burial has yet been found. The year 1826 on the same block may be the date of his second marriage, to Mali; it is not set as a fact. ¶19

Second marriage: Mali Evensdatter, born about 1795, was his second wife, and she survived him; the 1865 census finds her a widow holding the Braendhaugen cottage in her own right. Their wedding is not in the indexed Selbu marriage register - see the findings dossier for that negative and the next record to try. ¶4

1801 census: Selbu, rural residence 0034 EIDUM, household 07 - 'Peder Bersvendsen', 14, child of Bersvend Pederssen, 47, HUUSMAND MED JORD (cottager with land), and Kiersten Ingebrigtsdatter, 46, with a younger brother Torsten Bersvendsen, 5, and Bersvend's widowed mother Marit Jonsdatter, 73, entered as 'Fattig' (poor). This is the only Bersvend Pedersen household at Eidem and the only Peder Bersvendsen there. Six Peder Bersvendsens appear in the 1801 census of Selbu - at Flakne, Slinden, Draxten twice, Solem and Eidum - and only this one is at Eidem, which is where our Peder was living when he married in 1819 and where his daughter was born in 1834. ¶6

The household is a HUSMANN household, which is the same standing the family still had seventy-four years later when his son-in-law Paul Ingebrigtsen was entered as Husmand med Jord at Braendhaugen. The cottager's status runs unbroken down three generations of this line. ¶7

First marriage: married at Selbu church 14 June 1819 Giertru Nielsdatter of Garborg (Garberg). Selbu parish register 1816-1831, marriages pages 390-391 serial no. 9. He is entered aged 39, birth year 1780 calculated, RESIDENCE EIDEM; she birth year 1794 calculated. He was therefore established at Eidem fifteen years before Kari's birth and thirty-nine years before Paul Ingebrigtsen married into the farm - the Eidem link in this family descends through him. ¶3

1825 census: Selbu, rural residence 0060* Eidem, household 1, 'Peder Bersvends., 36, Son' (index reading marked '??'), listed directly after his widowed mother 'Kirsten Ingebrigtsd., 71, Hmd. Enk.' and followed by his son 'Bersvend Peders., 5 1/2'. No wife is with him. Age 36 gives a birth year of about 1789, which agrees with the 1801 census (14) rather than the 1819 marriage (39). ¶9

Baptism, probable: 'Doebt Peder. Par: Bersv. Ped. Eid. Kerst. Engelb.' - baptised Peder, parents Bersvend Pedersen of Eid(em) and Kirsten Engelbrechtsdatter (Engelbrecht is a form of Ingebrigt). Godparents ('T.', testes): Povel Volds[?], Anders Berg, Ole Ny[?]l, Helga Eid(em). Selbu ministerialbok 1757-1801, 1790, the first baptism on the right page after the heading 'Dom. Esto mihi' (Quinquagesima Sunday), which was 14 February 1790. Read and re-read from the scan crop; the names of child and parents are clear, the godparents' farm names are not. ¶12

The alternative, and why it is not preferred: an elder brother Peder born about 1786 who either died before 1790 (so the 1790 child replaced him) or survived. If the elder died, the 1801 boy is still the 1790 child. If the elder lived, the 1790 child would be a second living son of the same name, which Selbu custom almost never allowed, and he would appear as a boy of 11 in the 1801 household; he does not - the household has only Peder 14 and Torsten 5. So on present evidence the 1801 Peder is the 1790 child. Not yet done: a read of Selbu baptisms about 1785-1788 and burials 1786-1790 (scans of canvas about 118-135 of source 16311) for an earlier Peder; that would settle it. Until then the birth is a probable date. ¶14

Marriage: married in Selbu church on 5 June 1826, Peder's second marriage. Two parallel church books record it. (1) Selbu 1816-1831, pp. 428-429 no. 10 (scan source 16317, canvas 177): groom 'Huusmd. og Enkemd. Peder Bersvendsen Eidem, gl. 36 aar' - cottager and widower Peder Bersvendsen of Eidem, aged 36; bride 'Bonde Datter Pigen Magl. Evensd. Auglan[d], gl. 30 aar' - the farmer's daughter, the maiden Magli (Mali) Evensdatter of Auglan (Uglem), aged 30; forlovere (sureties) 'g.md. Niels Paulsen Garberg' and 'Huusmd. Gunder Johnsen Eidem'; performed in the church; 'Huusmd.' and 'Enkemd.' in the groom's line are read from abbreviations. (2) Selbu 1824-1842, p. 305 no. 10 (scan source 16314, canvas 224): 'Enkem. Peder Bersv. Eidem' and 'Pig. Maglie Evensd. [farm]', both 'Selbo', aged 36 and 30; groom's father 'Bersvend Peders.', bride's father 'Even Peders.'; forlovere 'Gunder Johns. Eid.' and 'Niels Pauls. Garb.'; banns read in April and on 1 May 1826 (column partly read); remark 'Efter afsluttet Skifte' - after the probate (of the first wife's estate) was completed. The bride's farm in book (2) is written with a looped capital that the index renders 'Hylem'; book (1), in a different hand, plainly reads 'Auglan', the form used for Uglem in the 1792 baptism of her sister Giertrud ('Even Pedersen Auglem'). Scans; crops in. The two books agree on date, names, ages and sureties. ¶23

Death, Probable: 'Husm. Peder Bersvendsen' (index 'Peder Bergsvendsen'), cottager, residence Eidum, aged 65, died 2 June 1855 and buried 7 June 1855 (Church book 1843-1859, burials 1855, p. 394 no. 27; scan). Agreeing: age 65 fits his baptism of 14 February 1790 (65 years 3 months); the standing (husmann, cottager) is his, as in his 1826 marriage; Eidum is the farm where he lived in 1801, 1819, 1825 and 1826 and where his widow was buried in 1872; the bygdebok skeleton for Brennhaugen (vol. 4, p. 206) gives the holder 'Per, 1790... 1855'; and his wife is a widow in 1865 and a cottager's widow (Husmandsenke) in 1872. ¶25a

Consequence for his death: the burial of 5 August 1857 'aged 65' (born about 1792) was set aside as too young for a man born in 1780 or 1787. Against a birth in 1790 it is only two years out, so it is now a third live candidate with those of 9 May 1841 and 19 December 1852. Mali Evensdatter, widowed by 1865, is the test: the right burial should precede her widowhood and any record of her as a widow. ¶15

Elder brother of the same name, who died: a first Peder, son of 'Bersv. Ped. Eid.' (Bersvend Pedersen of Eidem) and 'Kirst. Engelbr.' (Kirsten Engelbrechtsdatter), was baptised on the 16th Sunday after Trinity, 23 September 1787 (Selbu ministerialbok 1757-1801, canvas 127 left page), and buried on the 2nd Sunday after Trinity, 21 June 1789: 'Peder Bersvends. Eidem død 19 Jun, 2 aar gl.' - Peder Bersvendsen of Eidem, died 19 June, 2 years old (canvas 133 left page). Read and the crops on 13 Oct 2026. Kari's father is the second Peder, baptised 14 February 1790, given the name of the brother who had died eight months earlier. ¶16

Death: before the 1865 census (31 December 1865), at which his widow is enumerated. Three Peder Bersvendsen burials in Selbu fall in the window and none states a residence: buried 9 May 1841 (d. 29 April, 1824-1842 vol. p. 403 no. 22); buried 19 December 1852 (d. 15 December, 1843-1859 vol. p. 386 no. 80); and buried 5 August 1857 aged 65, hence b. about 1792, which is too young for our man (1843-1859 vol. p. 398 no. 27). The 1841 and 1852 entries are both possible and the scans must be read to choose between them. ¶5

Records that disagree 1

A conflict in his age, recorded rather than resolved: the 1801 census makes him 14 (b. abt 1787), his 1819 marriage entry makes him 39 (b. abt 1780), and a Peder Bersvendsen buried in Selbu on 5 August 1857 is given as 65 (b. abt 1792). The census age, taken from the head of household with the boy in the room, is ordinarily the most reliable, so 1787 is the likelier true year; I keep ABT 1780 from the one record unambiguously his. His baptism, in the untranscribed Selbu register 1757-1801, would settle it. ¶8

Corrections 5

Correction: the 1858 marriage entry of his daughter Kari renders him 'Peder Richardsen'. That is a transcription error. Rikard/Richard was not a Troendelag given name in the 1780s, whereas Bersvend is common in Selbu and Tydal, and the 1834 baptism is the direct and contemporaneous record. ¶2

Correction: the birth date was carried as ABT 1780, from his age of 39 at his 1819 marriage. The 1790 baptism, read 13 Oct 2026, is the only baptism of a Peder to Bersvend Pedersen and Kirsten Ingebrigtsdatter that has been found, and the birth is now set to 'before 14 February 1790'. The ages conflict and are kept on record: 1801 census 14 (born about 1786-87); 1819 marriage 39 (about 1780); 1825 census 36 (about 1789, within a year of the baptism); and the Peder Bersvendsen buried 5 August 1857 aged 65 (about 1792). The 1825 age agrees with the baptism; the 1801 age is three years high and the 1819 age ten years high. ¶13

Correction: the earlier notes that preferred 1787 (from the 1801 census age of 14) as 'the likelier true year' are superseded. The boy born in 1787 died in 1789. The 1801 census age of 14 is about three years high and the 1819 marriage age of 39 about ten years high; the 1825 census age of 36 is within a year of the truth. ¶18

Correction: the earlier note that his second marriage 'is not in the indexed Selbu marriage register' is superseded. It is indexed under the bride's spelling 'Magli'/'Maglie' (5 June 1826, above). He married after the 27 November 1825 census, in which he is without a wife, and after the probate of his first wife's estate. ¶24

Correction: death changed from 'before 31 December 1865' to 2 June 1855 (Probable, see the note above). The bygdebok's '1855', which an earlier note said matched none of the burial candidates, now matches this burial. ¶26

Alternatives considered and set aside 3

Why the 1857 burial is set aside: it gives his residence as Draxen, not Eidem or Braendhaugen, and does not name a wife. Its age of 65 (born about 1792) fits the two boys named Peder Bersvendsen, each aged 10 (born about 1791), in households 02 and 04 at Draxen in the 1801 census - sons of Bersvend Olsen and of Bersvend Sivertsen - better than our man, who was baptised in February 1790 at Eidem. It is very probably one of the Draxen Peders. This replaces the earlier note that called it a third live candidate. His death therefore remains unknown, between 1834 (Kari's birth) and the 1865 census, where his widow Mali Evensdatter is enumerated; it is not in the indexed Selbu burials under the spellings searched so far. ¶21

Death candidates narrowed: the burial of 'Peder Bersvendsen', 9 May 1841 (Church book 1824-1842, p. 403 no. 22, pg00000007126443; no age in the index) is ruled out. The scan reads 'Peder Bersvendsen Krogstad Hdb.' - a cottager's child of Krogstad, a twin of 'John Bersvendsen Krogstad', both a few days old (nos. 21-22). The candidates left are 19 December 1852 and 5 August 1857. ¶22

Not proved: the entry names no wife, and Brennhaugen (the cottage under Eidem where his widow lived in 1865) is not named. The other Peder Bersvendsens of his age are ruled out: Draxen (buried 1857, and one alive at Draxten in 1865), Solem (buried 13 May 1855, aged 80), and the infants of 1841 and 1852. ¶25b

Searches that found nothing 1

Death, negative: none of the three Peder Bersvendsen burials in Selbu between 1834 and 1865 is his. Read from the scans: (1) buried 9 May 1841 (1824-1842 vol. p. 403 no. 22) is Peder Bersvendsen Krogstad, a cottager's child one day old, bracketed with no. 21 John Bersvendsen Krogstad as twins; (2) buried 19 December 1852 (1843-1859 vol. p. 386 no. 80) is a child of six weeks at Eidum, perhaps a grandson (a child of his son Bersvend Pedersen), which is untested; (3) buried 5 August 1857 (1843-1859 vol. p. 398 no. 27) is 'Hsmd. Peder Bersvendsen', cottager, aged 65, of Draxen. ¶20
