About the family

Son Gullach (Probable): father of Gullach Tostensen of Engesetter (see his page). For: (1) Gullach's patronymic is Tostensen in the 1701 manntall ('gullach Tostens:' 40; the Digitalarkivet index agrees), and Gullach farmed on Engesetter in 1701; (2) this Tosten is the only Tosten on Engesetter in 1664, and the only one in the whole Brekke sogn list (s.239-241); (3) Gullach, born about 1661, would have been about 3 in 1664, below the age at which this list records sons, so his absence from the sons' column is expected; (4) Gullach named his elder son Tosten (Tosten, 7 in 1701, born about 1694). ¶3a

Life story

1664 census: farmer (bonde) 'Thosten' on Engesetter ('Ingesetter'), Brekke kirkesogn, Eivindvik prestegjeld, in the bailiff's census of 21 Jun 1664. No sons aged 12 or over and no servants are listed with him. The list gives farmers no age or patronymic; birth before about 1640 is inferred only from his being a householder in 1664, and he was alive on 21 Jun 1664. ¶1

Reading: the Engesetter farmers in the 1664 bailiff's list are, in order, '[Th]osten', 'Claus' (sons Hans 14 and Jørgen 12 Claussønner), 'T[ö]rn' or 'T[ö]en', and 'Jorn' (servant Olle Knudtzen 44). read the first as 'Østen[?] (Ehsten)' and the third as 'Tørn[?]'. On a 2.2x crop the first name opens with the same looped 'Th' ligature as the next farm heading 'Thouning' directly below, so it is 'Thosten' (Tosten). The third opens with the same looped capital T without the h; the letters after it are 'ö' and then 'rn' or 'en', which this hand barely separates. This list gives farmers no age and no patronymic and omits boys under about 12. ¶2

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 record that disagrees · 1 alternative set aside

Records that disagree 1

Against or open: no record names Tosten as Gullach's father; Tosten is not on Engesetter in the 1645 poll tax (only Mogens, Knud and Claus), so he came to the farm between 1645 and 1664; and he is not in the 1701 manntall there (dead or gone by then). ¶3b

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Lead only (not linked): in the 1645 poll tax a boy 'Thosten Botolffs.' (Tosten Botolfsen) is listed among the children on Aasem (Asseim) in Brekke. He could be this Tosten before he took a holding on Engesetter, but nothing in the records joins them. ¶4
