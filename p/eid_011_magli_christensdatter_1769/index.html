About the family

Mother of Brynhild (Brynil) Eriksdatter, born 5 March 1796 at Uglum, by the baptism entry (Selbu ministerialbok 1757-1801, canvas 190). Godparents there include Christen Christensen Lien and Sofie Christensdatter Lien, possibly her siblings (inference only). ¶2

Parents: Christen Christensen Lien (see his page) and Brynild Olsdatter (eid_123_brynild_olsdatter), rated Probable, not Confirmed. The baptism names them explicitly, and the fit is good: name, a 1769 birth against her 1801 age of 32, her daughter Brynil (1796) named after Brynild Olsdatter, and godparents Christen Christensen Lien and Sofie Christensdatter Lien at that 1796 baptism. But none of her own adult records names the farm Lien, and the search for a rival Mali Christensdatter covered only Ascension 1769 to spring 1770. Her marriage entry (about 1792-1793) naming her of Lien would make it Confirmed. ¶8

Mother identified: her mother Brynild Olsdatter, wife of Christen Christensen Lien, is Brynhild Olsdatter born 1744 at Slindan, daughter of Ola Jornsen and Marit Pedersdatter (Brynhild Olsdatter Slind; Probable). The bygdebok names Brynhild's first husband as Kristen Kristensen and her second as Jon Persen of Lien; in 1801 she was at Lien as Jon Pederssen's wife, aged 57, with Kristensdatter daughters. Brynild's separate record eid_123_brynild_olsdatter is folded into Brynhild Olsdatter Slind. ¶10

Pedigree collapse (third route, Probable): Ola Jornsen and Marit Pedersdatter of Slindan are ancestors of Gunder Olsen Slind and Oline, and so of Palmer Gilbert Eidem, three times: (1) Jorgen (Jorn) Olssen, b. 1752 -> Gunder Jorgensen Slind -> Ole Gundersen Slind -> Gunder Olsen Slind; (2) Ingeborg Olsdatter, b. 1749 -> Ingebor Jonsdatter of Sandvik -> Marit Haldorsdatter -> Kari Olsdatter, wife of Gunder Olsen Slind; (3) Brynhild Olsdatter, b. 1744 -> Magli Christensdatter -> Brynhild Eriksdatter -> Berith Gundersdatter Eidem, wife of Ole Gundersen Slind and mother of Gunder Olsen Slind. So Ole Gundersen Slind and Berith Gundersdatter (m. 24 May 1841) were second cousins once removed, and Gunder Olsen Slind descends from Ola and Marit through both his parents as well as through his wife. Route 3 rests on the identification of Magli's mother Brynild Olsdatter with Ola's daughter Brynhild (Probable); routes checked with fleet.py show. ¶11

Parents proved: the probate after Christen Christensen of Lien, 23 May 1789 (24763 p. 149), names 'Datteren Mali Christensdatter 20 aar gl.' with the widow Brønnille Olsdatter. With her 1792 marriage as 'Mali Christensd. Lien' to Erich Torstensen Uglem (16311 c146), this closes her link to Christen Christensen Lien and Brynhild Olsdatter. Her own rating stays Probable because it reflects the link to her daughter Brynhild (see Erik Torstensen's notes), which this record does not touch. ¶14

Life story

Baptism: 'Mali', daughter of Christen Christensen Lien and Brynild Olsdatter, baptised in Selbu on the 8th Sunday after Trinity, 16 July 1769; godparents Hovar Effjen, Baro Lien, Ragnild Stien[?], Marit Stor-Effjen and Ingeborg [farm cut off]. The birth date in the tree is 'before 16 July 1769' and the birthplace Lien is inferred from her father's farm name in the entry. This replaces the estimate 'about 1769' taken from her 1801 age of 32, which it agrees with. ¶7

1801 census: Selbu, rural residence 0030 Uglan, household 01, 'Magli Christensdatter, 32', wife of Erik Torstensen, both in their first marriage, with children Torsten 7, Brynil 5 and Christen 2. ¶1

1801 census, person page: Selbu, rural residence 0030 Uglan, household 01 - person 001 Erik Torstensen, 35, Husbonde, 'Bonde og gaard beboer' (farmer and occupier of the farm); person 002 Magli Christensdatter, 32, 'Hans kone' (his wife), born about 1769; both 'i 1. ægteskab' (in their first marriage). Their children in the household were aged 7, 5 and 2 (Torsten, Brynil and Christen). ¶5

1801 census: Magli Christensdatter, 32, at Uglan (Uglem) with Erik Torstensen. No indexed burial found under Magli/Mali Christensdatter/Kristensdatter 1801-70. ¶12

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Why Probable, and what would settle it: no single record names Erik Torstensen as the father of Gunder Johnsen's wife. The link is a chain of primary records: (1) she married Gunder on 11 Jun 1815 as 'Pigen Brynhild Eriksd. Auglem' - a maid of Uglem; (2) she is the widowed mother of Ole Gundersen (bapt. 1826, son of Gunder Johnsen and Brynhild Eriksd.) at Grindhaugen in 1865 and 1875, born 1796; (3) her 1880 burial gives her birthplace as Uglem and birth year 1795; (4) the only Brynhild Eriksdatter born at Uglem in the 1790s is Brynil, born 5 Mar 1796 to Erik Torstensen Uglum and Magli Christensdatter, who is 5 in their Uglan household in 1801; (5) the bygdebok (vol. 4 p. 203) calls Gunnar Jonsen's wife Brynhild of Uglan, b. 1796. The one rival, Brynhild Eriksdatter born 12 May 1797 at Langli to Erik Pedersen, is eliminated: she married Arnt Olsen of Kval on 4 Jun 1819 with her father as witness, and was his widow at Sletne in 1865. Her second daughter was named Maglie (1816), after Magli Christensdatter, as Selbu naming custom expects. A probate (skifte) after Erik Torstensen of Uglem, d. 1829, naming his heirs (Strinda og Selbu sorenskriveri, scan-only) would make it Confirmed. ¶4

Upgrade: Confirmed, graded on her link to her daughter Brynhild Eriksdatter Eidem (see her page); her own parentage is already proved by the 1789 probate after Christen Christensen Lien. The record naming the relationship is the Selbu baptism at the Annunciation service 1796 (Selbu ministerialbok 1757-1801, source 16311, canvas 190) of Brynil, born 5 Mar 1796, daughter of 'Erik Torstensen Uglum og Magli Christens d:'. Continuity to the tree's Brynhild Eriksdatter Eidem (see her page), all on the farm Uglem: the 1801 census of Uglan (Uglem), household 01, has Erik Torstensen, 35, and his wife Magli Christensdatter, 32, both in their first marriage, with their children Torsten 7, Brynil 5 and Christen 2; she married Gunder Johnsen on 11 Jun 1815 as 'Pigen Brynhild Eriksd. Auglem' (a maid of Uglem); her 1880 burial gives her birthplace as Uglem, and the 1865 and 1875 censuses her birth as 1796. ¶15a

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

No rival: the only other Brynhild Eriksdatter of that age in Selbu, born 12 May 1797 at Langli to Erik Pedersen, married Arnt Olsen of Kval in 1819 with her father as witness and was his widow at Sletne in 1865 (eid_ notes on Erik). The probate after Erik (1829) that earlier notes asked for is not in the Strinda og Selbu skifteutlodningsprotokoll 1827-1840 register, so the grade rests on the baptism naming both parents and the unbroken Uglem residence 1796-1815. ¶15b

Searches that found nothing 4

Death: not found. She is not in her husband's household at Uglem in the November 1825 census, which suggests she had died by then (inference); a burial under Magli/Mali Christensdatter or Kristensdatter was not found in a quick index search and is still to be looked for. ¶3

Death still not found. The Digitalarkivet person index for Selbu church books, events 1801-1870, has no burial for Magli Christensdatter (3 hits), Mali Christensdatter (10 hits; the two burials are infants born 1867), Magli Kristensdatter (3 hits) or Malene Christensdatter (0). Census searches: 1825, Magli Christensdatter gives three women born 1799, 1809 and 1813, none her; 1865, no hit. Her husband Erik Torstensen is not found in the 1825 index under Torstensen or Thorstensen, nor under the domicile Uglan, so the 1825 index is too thin to show whether she was still alive then (inference). Not yet searched: Selbu burial lists year by year, the scanned burial books 1801-1829, and Erik's own burial of 27 January 1829. ¶6

Death: not found to 1830. She is not in the November 1825 census household of her husband Erik Torstensen at Uglem (household 01), where he is listed with no wife, and the Digitalarkivet person index for Selbu, events 1801-1830, has no 1825 census entry, burial or other church-book entry for a Magli or Mali Christensdatter or Kristensdatter born about 1769; the Magli/Maglie Christensdatters it returns were born 1799-1813. She probably died between 1801 and 1825 (inference), but her burial is not indexed under these spellings; the Selbu burial registers 1801-1825 (sources 16312 and the 1816-1831 book) need reading by scan. ¶9

Probate search, negative: the typed register of Strinda og Selbu skifteutlodningsprotokoll 1, 1827-1840 (Digitalarkivet 24745, images 3-4, A to D) has no estate for her mother Brynhild Olsdatter Lien (died 5 Jul 1827), so no heirs list naming Magli's children was found there. The register does list 'Christen Lien Christensen', Selbu, fol. 405 (probably her brother Christen Christensen Lien, a godfather in 1796; not read) and 'Bereth Christensdr, Stor Evjen', Selbu, fol. 33. That second estate was read (fol. 33b-34, images 47-48): 'Skifte efter Gaardmands Kone Berette Christensdatter', Selbu, opened 27 Aug 1829, farm Storevjen matr. no. 691; her heirs are the widower Halvor Halvorsen and their sons Halvor Halvorsen (of age), Christen (13 1/2) and John (7). Her father lived at Stor-Evjen in 1772, so she may be Magli's sister, but the probate does not say so and it names no siblings. ¶13
