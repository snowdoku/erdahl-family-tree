About the family

NEW ANCESTOR, graded on his link to his daughter Gro Mikkelsdatter (see her page). In the probate after her husband Thallak Ormsøn, Øvre Skjeggedal, Tovdal, 7 Jun 1719 (Østre Råbyggelag Skifteprotokoll nr. 5 fol. 273b-274a, DA 24353 view 281; card 39942/12333 typed 'Poul Mickelsøn Haartved i Nissedal som er morbror; en annan morbror Aane Michelsøn'), the children's two 'Morbroder' are 'Paal Michelsøn Haartved i Nisdal' and Aane Michelsøn. Gro (Guroe Michelsdaatter in her own 1717 probate, Skp. 5 fol. 118a) and these two brothers share the patronymic, so their father was a Mikkel. The relationship words (morbror) are primary; the father's name rests on the shared patronymic only, and no record names him or his farm, so Probable. Gro was born about 1671, so he was born before about 1651. Probably of Haatveit (Hårtveit) in Nissedal, where his son Poul lived in 1719 (inferred from the son's residence only). ¶1

Life story

The Øvre Telemark (vestfjelske) probate card index (DA 6856) was searched for Haatveit in Nissedal. The Haatveit cards are filed by parish at views 534-550 (Hjartdal, Kviteseid, Laardal, Mo, then Nissedal). The Nissedal cards are only: view 547, 1722 (fol. 8), the unmarried Ole Olsen, heirs his mother Aasild Torjusdatter, married to Torgier Torgiersen Haatveit, and his half-brothers Helie and Jon Torgiersen; view 548, 1729 (fol. 270b), Pôul Michelsen (deceased) and Anne Sigarsdtr., children Gunder 24 (soldier), Niels 20 (soldier), Birgith 17 and Hagge 14; view 549, 1730 (fol. 315), Torgier Torgiersen; and a card for 1810. So there is NO probate after a Mikkel at Haatveit (or after his wife) in this index, which in any case has no Nissedal Haatveit cards before 1722. The 1729 card is the probate of his son, Poul Mikkelsen, the 'Paal Michelsøn Haartved i Nisdal' who was a guardian in 1719 as the children's 'Morbroder'. It proves that Poul lived and died at Haatveit, but it lists only Poul's own children and does not name his father. Still Probable. ¶3a

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

Open questions and the next record to read 2

Test record: a probate after a Mikkel at Haatveit (or after his wife) in Nissedal, c.1690-1730, in the Nedre Telemark / Bamble probate card index that covers Nissedal (DA 6856, H-block; read only the 1729/30 Haatveit cards of Torgier Torgiersen), naming the children Gro, Poul and Aane; or the 1664/1666 censuses for Nissedal (Haatveit), for a Mikkel with sons. ¶2

Test record: the protocol entry itself (Øvre Telemark skifteprotokoll 1722-34, fol. 270b, 1729), whose land section may say how Poul came by his share of Haatveit (for example by inheritance 'efter sin fader Michel'); or a Nissedal tax list (1711 or 1723 matrikkel) naming the user of Haatveit before Poul. ¶3b
