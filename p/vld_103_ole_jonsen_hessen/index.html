About the family

Attested (scan read): at the probate after 'Sal. Anders Jonssøn Hessen', 4 May 1700 (Sunnmøre Skifteprotokoll 01, s. 299b, source 24544 view 311), the parties were his widow Britte Larsdatter and their only child Olle Andersen, with Hans Larsen Fischer, 'Olle Jonssøn Hessen' and the child's guardian [..] Nielsen Hessen, who is called the child's mother's sister's husband (Mosters Mand). So an Ole Jonsen lived at Hessen in 1700, and he was very probably a brother of the deceased Anders Jonsen. That fits Ole naming a son Anders, baptised at Hessen 24 Aug 1704, after a brother who died in 1700 (naming pattern; inference). ¶3

First wife (scan read): Sunnmøre skifteprotokoll 3A 01 fol. 172b (24544/186), 9 Oct 1696: probate after 'Sal. Marthe Olsdatter Hessen' between her surviving husband Olle Jonssen and her two sons Sønnemund and Claus Corfitzsønner, from her earlier marriage. No children of Ole are named. So the Ole Jonsen of Hessen who married Brite (Berte) Larsdatter on 14 Jan 1700 (engagement, pv00000010160817) had been a widower since 1696. Claus Corfitsen stayed at Hessen (his wife Berte Ivarsdatter died there in 1725). ¶9

Children with father Ole Jonss. of Hessen in the Borgund baptism index: Hilde 5 Feb 1699, a child 25 Mar 1701, a child 8 Oct 1702, Anders 24 Aug 1704. ¶10a

Life story

Burial (index): Ole Jonss. of Hessen, aged 73 (b. 1669), buried at Borgund on 15 Apr 1742 with a funeral sermon (pg00000002546306). This matches the farm history's 'Ole Jonsen d. 1742, 73 år' exactly. ¶8

Baptism of a son: the Borgund index has Anders, son of Ole Jonss. of Hessen, baptised 24 August 1704 (legitimate). The same index has Ole Jonss. of Hessen as father of Hilde, baptised 5 February 1699. A card notes an 'Oelle Joens', born about 1660, as a tenant at Hessen in the 1701 male census (name and farm only; that row was not re-read here). ¶1

Probate of Jon Jonsen, Hessen, 20 Apr 1733 (Skifteprotokoll 9A, s. 50b, source 24552 view 63; scan read, hand hard): the heirs are his siblings. They are 1) brother Jon Jonsen [Lote?], dead, whose children were sons Jon, Hans, Jon (26) and Hans, and daughters Inger, Karen (married to Arne Ødegaard) and Ragnild; 2) 'den 2den Broder Ole Jonsen [farm unclear], myndig' (of age); 3) brother Christopher Jonsen; 4) a half-brother and his children; and sisters, one married to [Nils?] Morevig, one dead with a son Niels Clausen. This makes a living Ole Jonsen in 1733, brother of Jon Jonsen of Hessen. Because Ole was alive, his own children are not named, so the record does not prove that Anders Olsen Hessen was his son. Confidence stays Speculative. ¶4a

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

3 on the grade · 1 alternative set aside · 2 searches with no result · 4 open questions

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 3

Speculative father of Anders Olsen of Hessen (see his page). Why plausible: the 1704 Anders is the only Anders with a father Ole and residence Hessen in the Borgund index for 1697-1704, and Anders Olsen lived and married at Hessen in 1731. Anders named his sons Ole (1732, and again 1745), Henning (1735) and Joen (1739), which fits the usual pattern of naming sons after the paternal grandfather (Ole) and great-grandfather (Jon); this is supporting evidence only, since Ole and Jon were among the commonest names in the parish. What is against it: Anders's burial in 1750 gives his age as 50, which implies birth about 1700, while this baptism makes him about 45; burial ages are often rounded, but four to five years is a real gap. Hessen also had other men named Anders Olsen (a cottager married there in 1703 and a younger man married in 1756), and only 25 of 87 Hessen index rows for 1697-1704 were read, so another candidate is not excluded. ¶2a

Upgrade: Speculative to Probable, graded on his link to his son Anders Olsen of Hessen (see his page). The Borgund farm history, Borgund og Giske bd. 4 (nb.no fa7c024e74b4e6a813036e44872d5863, canvas 372, Hessen; rebuilt from content-search word boxes), lists on one bruk: '2. Anders Jonsen d. 1699, 30 år', married to Brite Larsdtr., who remarried in 1700; '3. Ole Jonsen d. 1742, 73 år', who held the bruk until 1731, 'Då bygsla sonen 4. Anders Olsen (1704-50)', married 1731 Sissel Olsdtr.; 'Anders [og] sonen drukna'. That is our Anders exactly (married Sissel Olsdatter 8 Jul 1731; drowned with his son Henning off Hessen in 1750), and the book calls him Ole Jonsen's son and gives his birth year as 1704, matching the baptism of Anders son of Ole Jonss. of Hessen on 24 Aug 1704. This also resolves the noted age conflict (burial age 50 against the 1704 baptism) in favour of 1704: the compiler, who used the church books, accepted the 1704 baptism. ¶6a

Probable, not Confirmed: a compiled source. ¶6b

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Caution: Hilde comes before the 1700 marriage, the 1701 manntall gives the Hessen Ole Joens as born about 1660, against 1669 from the burial, and the 1700 engagement has an Ole Jonsen of Hessen as both groom and surety. There may have been two men named Ole Jonsen at Hessen. Not resolved, so he stays Probable (graded on his link to Anders Olsen, Anders Olsen Hessen). ¶10b

Searches that found nothing 2

Negative / reading: in the 1733 probate after Jon Jonsen of Hessen (24552/63) the heir line reads '2den Broder Ole Jonsen [farm] myndig'. Read at full resolution, the farm word begins with a capital E or G and ends in -nim/-nem (possibly Ekrem or Ellingsøy); it is not Hessen. So in 1733 this brother Ole Jonsen lived away from Hessen, which weakens (but does not break) the identification with the Ole of Hessen who had a son Anders baptised in 1704. The Sunnmøre probate index has no Ole Jonsen among the 16 deceased at Hessen 1730-1775 (the Ole entries are Ole Pedersen 1743, Ole Eriksen 1754, Ole Rasmussen 1767, Ole Olsen 1775). Eli Olsdatter of Hessen, 4 Oct 1751 (3A 17 fol. 244a, scan read), was the wife of Ole Larsen with two small children, so not a sister of Anders Olsen Hessen. ¶5

Negative: no Hessen (Borgund) card exists in the 77797 card set. Images 1232-1240 are Herdalen (Stranda) and Hesseberg (Skodje), not Hessen. The Sunnmøre probate index has no probate after him (d. 1742). ¶11
