Life story

Burial (index, probable identification): 'Grejs Hansen', deceased, husmann, of Haugen under Lochsteen, aged 82 (b. c.1743), died 26 Nov 1825, buried 5 Dec 1825, Frosta parish book 1817-1827 p. 216. Name variant, age, status and place match Greis Hansen, 58, husmann med jord at Loksteen in the 1801 census (wife Gurrue Pedersdatter, 64). The burial names no wife. Gurru's own burial was not found in the Frosta index for 1801-1830; two 1826 'Guru Pedersdatter b. 1749' burials at Solem do not fit. ¶3

Greis's own confirmation is in the Frosta index: 'Gres Hansen', of Wiigleeret, confirmed at Laugtu on the 5th Sunday after Trinity 1761 (pk00000000999788), aged about 18, which fits his birth about 1743. So he was already at Vigleret before his marriage. No man named Hans appears at Vigleret in the Frosta index 1750-78 (the residents named there are Jert 1758 and 1767, Anders Arensen 1752, Niels Pedersen 1768-76, Peder 1769-75, Christen d. 1776 aged 67, Anders and Jon 1773-78), so Greis was probably a servant there and his father Hans lived elsewhere. Still to find: his baptism about 1743 (Frosta books before 1750 are lost). ¶8

Legdsrulle sweep: the 1765 manntall- og legdsrulle of the Nordre Stjørdal dragoon company (source 51337, image 51, quarter no. 43 'Nor Lein', Frosta; holder Aage Torsen 24) lists among the young men of the farm 'Greg: Hansen' (Gregers Hansen), no age entered, remark 'insat i Reserve No 13' (put into the reserve as no. 13). Greis is the short form of Gregers, and the 1787 confirmation calls his son 'Siwert Gregersen'. This puts a Gregers Hansen at Nordre Lein in 1765, three years before 'Greis' of Storleinsvallet had his son Sivert baptised (1768). It narrows the audit's objection that Storleinsvallet is a different place from Vigleret: the same patronymic-bearing Gregers was attached to the Lein farm in 1765. It does not name a relationship, so the grade on his link to Sivert (see his page) stays Probable. The roll gives no father: the farm's holder was Aage Torsen, and the husmenn were Ole Olsen 47 and Niels Olsen 47, so no Hans was on the farm. ¶10

Of Vigleeret, Frosta, when betrothed to Gurru Pedersdatter on the 15th Sunday after Trinity 1766 and at his son Jens's baptism in 1776; cottager (husmann) at Loksteen, aged 58, in the 1801 census. ¶1

New father. The same 1771 probate names him as a son of Hans Greisen (deceased by 1771), who was a son of Greis Hansen the elder, the 'Farbroder' of the deceased Gurie Hansdatter. His siblings in 1771: [Jon?] Hansen, a soldier in Copenhagen, and Martha Hansdatter, 24, a servant at Nordre Lein. His aunt was Gunild Greisdatter, wife of the husmann Ole Hansen of Narvigen. This explains why Greis was named in the 1771 Riset probate (Frostaboka VII p. 72 lists him among the heirs) and corrects reading that it had no bearing on him: read only the opening of 15 Mar 1771 (fol. 380b), not the continuation on fol. 381b where the heirs are listed. ¶12

The farm history Frostaboka XI (2007) p. 624 (Lokstein, plass 93/2; nb.no item 5b1f2f560edbac3e705cc8eccecac709, word-box rebuild) lists Greis Hansen, b. c.1743, d. 1825, m. 1766 Gurru Pedersdtr. (b. c.1737, d. ABT 1827), with children Hans 1767, Sivert 1768, Jens 1776, Anna 1777 (confirmed 1796) and Karen Margrete 1783. A compiled source; it agrees with the records above. ¶13

The 1810 local census of Frosta (source 51132) lists 'Greis Hanssen', husmann, 'Mand i første Ægteskab' (in his first marriage), at 'Loksteen Plads Wigeleret eller Haugen' (the Lokstein cottage called Vigleeret or Haugen), with 'Guri Pedersdtr, hans Kone i første Ægteskab' and 'Malena Arntsdatter, Enke af 1ste Ægteskab, Inderste' (pf01051132000133). This proves that Vigleeret (1766, 1776), Loksteen (1801) and Haugen under Lokstein (burial 1825) are one cottage, and that the 1766 couple, the 1801 couple and the man buried in 1825 are the same people. Both spouses were still in their first marriage in 1810. ¶5

Further support for the Greis-Sivert link, though still not a record naming the relationship. All 28 Frosta index entries for a man Greis up to 1811 belong to one man (Vigleeret, Storleinsvallet 1768, Breiset 1780, Vigleret/Vigeleret 1775-1810), apart from a Greis Iversen at Øvne in the 1816 silver tax. In 1780 the long form 'Gregers' is used for Gurru Pedersdatter's husband at Breiset (pd00000006455895), confirming that 'Gregers' in the Frosta book is this Greis, as in Sivert's 1787 confirmation 'Siwert Gregersen Vigleret'. His son Jens Greisen's second marriage at Frosta, 10 Jun 1839, names the groom's father as 'Greis Hansen, afdøde Huusmand, Loksteen' (pv00000002951769). The 1768 churching of Greis's wife at Storleinsvallet (3rd Sunday after Trinity, py00000000031705) does not name her. Grade stays Probable on the link to Sivert. ¶9

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 4

Probable father of Sivert Greisen (see his page). Probable, not proved: Sivert (b. about 1767: aged 34 in 1801, 91 at burial 1858) fits the baptism of 'Sivert', son of 'Greis' of Storleinsvallet, Frosta, on Almindelig Bededag 1768 (no mother indexed). found Greis Hansen the only Greis in the 1801 census of Frosta (58, cottager at Loksteen, b. about 1743). Greis Hansen of Vigleeret married Gurru Pedersdatter in 1766 and had a son Jens there in 1776; a 'Jens Greisson' stood godfather to Sivert's son Siver in 1803 and a 'Greis' of Vigeleret to Sivert's son Gunder in 1809. The 1768 farm (Storleinsvallet) differs from Vigleeret, so the identification rests on the unique given name, age and the godparents. ¶2

Upgrade: Sivert's confirmation names him as Greis's son on Greis's own farm. The Frosta confirmation list for the 6th Sunday after Trinity 1787 at Lochtue (Frosta ministerialbok 1778-1811, source 16379, canvas 45, p. 87, scan read) includes 'Siwert Gregersen Vigleret' among the boys (index pk00000001005249 reads 'Sivert Gregersen'). Greis is a short form of Gregers, and this record uses the long form: the same index has 'Gregers' of Vigleret as a husband at a churching in 1783 (py00000000027649) and as a betrothal surety in 1789 (pv00000001558429), and a son 'Hans Gregersen', b. c.1766, of Vigleret, buried 1784 (pg00000001198685). Hans was named for Greis's father Hans. Greis Hansen is the only Greis or Gregers on Vigleret: betrothed from there in 1766, father of Jens there in 1776, and still there in 1810 (see below). Sivert, b. c.1767-68, was baptised on Almindelig Bededag 1768 as son of Greis of Storleinsvallet; the confirmation moves him to Vigleret under the patronymic Gregersen. Together these name Sivert as the son of Greis (Gregers) Hansen of Vigleret. ¶4a

Audit 3 (1 Nov 2026): downgraded from Confirmed to Probable, graded on his link to his son Sivert Greisen (see his page). No record names Greis Hansen as Sivert's father. (1) The 1768 baptism names the father only as 'Greis' of Storleinsvallet, a different place from Greis Hansen's Vigleret (1766, 1776). (2) The 1787 confirmation 'Siwert Gregersen Vigleret' gives a patronymic and residence but names no parent. So the link rests on a unique given name, a patronymic and a shared residence, and the upgrade note flags exactly that ('the link rests on the patronymic plus residence'). Under the operative grading rule (BRIEF.md, clarified after audit 2), a link that is inferred, or that crosses farms, stays Probable. 'The only Greis in Frosta' is an elimination argument, however strong. The 1810 census proving that Vigleret, Loksteen and Haugen were one cottage is sound, but it does not reach Storleinsvallet in 1768. ¶7a

Upgrade: Confirmed, graded on his link to his son Sivert Greisen (see his page). The audit's objection was that the 1768 baptism names the father only as 'Greis' of Storleinsvallet, a place different from Greis Hansen's Vigleret, and no record called the Storlein Greis 'Greis Hansen'. That record is now found: the probate after Gurie Hansdatter of Riset, continued 24 Aug 1771 (Stjør- og Verdal skifteprotokoll 6-2, 24757 fol. 381b, scan), names among the heirs 'Sønnen Greis Hansen 28 Aar gl., Huusmand under Stor Lein her paa Frosten'. Chain: (1) the baptism on Almindelig Bededag 1768 (16378 p. 102) names Sivert's father as Greis of Storleinsvallet; (2) three years later on the same farm the husmann there is Greis Hansen, aged 28 (b. c.1743); (3) this age matches the tree's Greis Hansen (confirmed 1761 aged c.18 at Vigleret, betrothed 1766 from Vigleret, 58 in 1801, 82 at burial 1825); (4) Sivert, aged 19-20, was confirmed in 1787 as 'Siwert Gregersen Vigleret', on Greis's own cottage. ¶11a

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

No rival: all 28 Frosta index entries for a man Greis or Gregers up to 1811 belong to one man, and there is no other Greis at Storlein. The links are consecutive records on the same farms with gaps of 3 years or less. ¶11b

Searches that found nothing 1

Negative: Hans of Risset, a surety at the 1766 betrothal, is not Greis's father. The probate of 15 Mar 1771 at Riset after Gurie Hansdatter, wife of Hans Nielsen (Stjør- og Verdal skifteprotokoll 6-2, 24757 fol. 380b, scan read) says their children died at birth. Hans Nielsen's own probate, 3 Jan 1776 at Riset (24758 fol. 422a, scan read), names his widow Marithe Andersdatter and his siblings and their children as heirs (sister Lisbeth Nielsdatter, wife of Jon Halvorsen, and others), with no children. The other surety was Niels of Haugan. The Stjør- og Verdal typed registers 1742-1816 have no probate for a Greis or Gregers in Frosta. ¶6

Open questions and the next record to read 2

Flag: the link rests on the patronymic plus residence, not on a record that names Greis Hansen in full as Sivert's father. ¶4b

Would confirm: a Frosta confirmation list that names Sivert's parents, a probate after Greis or Gurru naming Sivert, or a record calling Greis of Storleinsvallet 'Greis Hansen'. ¶7b
