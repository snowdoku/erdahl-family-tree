Life story

Betrothed 1791 at Kirkenes (Nissedal) to Targie Osmundsen Hiemdal; married 1791 in Treungen church. She is listed as pige Birgit Targiesdatter Frontved, and Knud Halvorsen Frontved was a cautioner. Kviteseid 1787-1799 is scanned only. ¶1

Alive 11 Dec 1796: she was godmother as 'Birgit Targiesd. Hiemdal' at the baptism of Jon Kristoffersen Hogsiaa's son Kristoffer (3rd Sunday in Advent, Treungen church; Kviteseid ministerialbok 1787-1799, p.106, image 113). She was also godmother on 28 Oct 1792 (image 64) and 7 Aug 1796 (image 110). Her daughter Gunvor was baptised 8 May 1796 in Treungen church (image 106-107). No burial for her was found in the burials from Advent 1796 to about Aug 1798 (images 248-252); Aug 1798-1799 (images 253-257) not yet read. The 1797 burials include the widow Gunvor Olsdatter Frontved, aged 61 (image 249); she may be Birgit's mother, but nothing links them (Speculative). ¶5

Buried 3rd Sunday in Lent (24 Feb 1799) at Treungen church: '3 Sønd. i Faste Treungens K: paa Targie Osmundsen Hiemdals Hustrue Birgit Targiesdatter af 29 Aar' (wife of Targie Osmundsen Hiemdal, aged 29). Heading Liigbegiengelser 1799, Kviteseid ministerialbok 1787-1799, p.257 (image 254). Her age gives birth about 1769-70. This replaces the earlier death range (between 12 Dec 1796 and Feb 1801); the death place is inferred from her residence at Hiemdal. ¶6

Baptism found. Kviteseid ministerialbok 1754-1773 (7981), p.120 (canvas 53, right page): on a Sunday after Trinity in Nissedal church, 'Samme dag, samme sted døbt Targie Frontveds daatter Birgit', godparents Ambiør(?) Grimstvedt, Kristofer Wiig, Ingebor..., Groe Wiig and Hæge Frontvedt. The year is 1769: the facing opening (canvas 52, pp.117-118) runs Juledag, Epiphany, Septuagesima, Sexagesima, then Kyndelmisse, then Dom. 3 i Fasten. Kyndelmisse (2 Feb) falls after Sexagesima only when Sexagesima is before 2 Feb, i.e. Easter before about 30 Mar; Easter 1769 was 26 Mar (1768: 3 Apr; 1770: 15 Apr), and the page-rate from the known anchors (p.49 = Mar 1759, p.89 = May 1764) also gives 1768-69. Her entry follows '14 Augusti' (Treungen) and precedes 'Dom. 14 p. Trinit.' and '25 Aug.', so the Sunday is Dom. 12 or 13 p. Trinit. (13 or 20 Aug 1769; the number reads like 13). This fits her burial age exactly (29 in Feb 1799, so born 1769-70). Her own grade is unchanged: it covers her link to her child in the tree. ¶9

She died before Feb 1801, when Tarje was in his 2nd marriage with Turi Halvorsdatter. Her youngest child, Gunvor, was b. ~1797. ¶2

Frontved (Fronteid?) farm: in the 1801 census, 'Trontvedt' in Kviteseid had several Targiesen/Targiesdatter adults (Nottov 38, Jørgen 30, Else 25, Ole 28). These may be her siblings (Speculative). ¶3

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the identification · 1 alternative set aside · 1 search with no result

The case for this identification 1

Re-read on the image (Kviteseid ministerialbok 1787-1799, p.187, image 195): betrothed 5th Sunday in Lent (10 Apr) 1791 at Kirkenes in Nissedal, bachelor Targie Osmundsen Hiemdal and the maid Birgit Targiesdatter Frontved. Her cautioners were Torjus Mule and Knud Halvorsen Frontved. They married on the 8th Sunday after Trinity (14 Aug 1791) in Treungen church. The entry does not name her father beyond the patronymic. Leads: Targie Asskiersen Frontved appears as cautioner in 1778-80, and Nottov Targiesen Frontved (soldier) married in Nissedal on 9 May 1791, recorded on the same page. Neither is proved to be her kin. ¶4

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Father candidate (Speculative): Targie (Tarje) Asgiersen Frontved, heir at the 26 Oct 1781 skifte after Asgier Tarjesen of Frontved (Panteregister I 1 fol. 299a, DA 10915 view 342). Patronymic and farm only; see his record. ¶8

Searches that found nothing 1

Negative: card index DA 6856 has no Frontved (Fronteveit) card at all; the F runs (views 238-286 and 291-314: Felle, Findalstveit, Fjalestad, Fyldet, Fjone, Fjaagesund, Flekstveit, Flaaten, Folsa, Fosse, Frangaarden, Frøland) were read. The 1776-98 protocols are carded in 6856 only for some parishes (Seljord), not Treungen, so her father's estate must be read in DA 25676-25680 directly. ¶7
