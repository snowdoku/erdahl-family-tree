Life story

Bygdebok (Botnen gnr. 65 bnr. 78, canvas 885): 'Lisbet Rasmusdtr.' (1715-1779), married 1739 Ivar Monsson of Botnen, was daughter of 'Rasmus... Brita Monsdtr.' (Indre Oppedal 47/48). Canvas 406 (Indre Oppedal): a division of 1708 after Rasmus Klausson's wife Lisbet Lassesdtr., then 'Rasmus Klausson barn med Brita Monsdtr.': 'Mons Rasmusson 1712-1789' (m. 1736 Marta Jonsdtr. of Øvre Opdal) and others. Canvas 886: Mons Rasmusson was 'morbror' (mother's brother) of Johannes Ivarson of Botnen, Lisbet's son, at a deed (skøyte) of 1777. ¶1

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
