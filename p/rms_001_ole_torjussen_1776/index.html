Life story

Burial: 'Torgius Tvets' son Ole, aged 11, died 4 Nov 1787 and was buried at Lille Topdal on 25 Nov 1787, together with a sibling and with Anne, the 20-year-old daughter of Even Oland. 'Alle 3 bleve indebrændte og fundne i Asken': all three burned to death and were found in the ashes (Åmli ministerialbok 1775-1796, burials 1787/88, image 124 left). The birth year is computed from the age at death. The father is named only as 'Torgius Tvet'. He is taken to be Torjus Ormsen because he was the only Torjus with a family at Tvet in 1782-88, the age fits his marriage of July 1775, and a new son baptised Ole at Tvet in Oct 1788 replaced this Ole. That identification is inferred, not stated. ¶1

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
