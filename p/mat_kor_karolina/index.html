Life story

Baptism index: born 29 Dec 1863 (Old Style) at Friedenstal, child of Ludwig Knodel and Wilhelmine Tarnowsky/Farowsky (St Petersburg Consistory extract, FHL film 1884124/2 p.247 no.63). A probable elder sibling of Gottlieb Knodel (b. 1879); probable because Gottlieb's own identification with this family is probable. Later life unknown. ¶1

Later life: no marriage or death of a Karolina Knodel of Friedenstal was found in the Bessarabian Consistory extracts to 1885. She was born 29 December 1863 and would have married about 1882-1888, after the extracts end. ¶2

Baptism, the register image (read 1 Nov 2026 by the signed-in lane,; S0189): St Petersburg Consistory duplicate, Arzis parish, Friedenstal births, Dec 1863 entered at the head of the Friedenstal section of the 1864 volume (vol. 828-14/135; FamilySearch coll. 1469151, waypoint 'Arzis: Friedenstal' > 1864, image 26 of 57; FHL 1884124/2 p.247), entry 63: born 29 December 1863 [Julian] at 11 o'clock midday(?), baptised 1 January 1864; 'Knodel, Karoline'; parents 'Ludwig Knodel, Ausländer; Ehefr[au] Wilhelmine geb. [T/F]arowsky; beide ev.-luth.' (Ludwig Knodel, foreign national; wife Wilhelmine née Tarowsky/Farowsky; both Evangelical Lutheran); godparents Friedrich H?eise, Karoline Ri?efle and Katharine K?luge (surnames uncertain), colonists; remark 'vom Schullehrer getauft, bestätigt vom Past[or] loci' (baptised by the schoolteacher, confirmed by the local pastor). Agrees with the odessa3 index date. ¶4

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Confidence raised to Confirmed (Oct 2026): the Consistory birth index (FHL 1884124/2 p.247 no.63) names both parents, Ludwig Knodel and Wilhelmina Farowski, at Friedenstal.
