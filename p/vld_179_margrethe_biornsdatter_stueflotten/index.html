About the family

Mother of Aaste (Aase) Iversdatter (see her page), Probable; graded on her link to Aaste. Primary (index of the probates, read on the cards): (1) skifte 18 Jul 1732 after her first husband Iver Andersen of Stueflaaten (card 39945/13171, fol. 116b), 'g.m. Margrethe Biørnsdtr.', children Biørn 4, Anders 1 3/4, Aaste 11; (2) her own skifte 19 Feb 1744 (card 13173, fol. 663a), married 2nd to Iver Iversøn and 1st to Iver (dead), children Biørn Iversøn 16, Anders Iversøn 13, Aase Iversd. 22. Her burial: 'Margrete Biørnsd.' of Stuflotten, buried 9 Jun 1743, given as b. 1689 (Lesja burial index). ¶1

Life story

Same grade reasoning as for Iver Andersen (see his page): the probate names Aaste as her daughter, but the tie to the tree's Aaste of Nystuen rests on name, patronymic, age (22 in 1744 = b. 1722 at the 1797 burial) and the farm complex (bygdebok), so Probable. Bygdebok for Lesja 1, canvas 42, entry 1-6 (see Iver). ¶3

Patronymic settled from the protocol itself. The 1732 skifte after Iver Andersen (skifteprotokoll 1732-1751, source 25157, fol. 116b, bottom of the page) names the widow 'Margrethe Biørns Datter' and says that she and Iver 'sammen avlede 3 Børn' (together had three children): 1. Biørn, 4, 2. Anders, 1 3/4, and 3. the daughter, 11. With the 1743 burial index ('Margrete Biørnsd.') that makes Biørnsdatter certain; the 1744 card's 'S...sd.' is a card-writer's slip (the 1744 protocol opening, fol. 663a, is too faint in the bitonal scan to read her patronymic). The 1744 heir list on fol. 663a reads '1 Biørn Iverson 16 Aar, 2. Ander Iverson 13 Aar og 3. Aase Iversdatter 22 aar'; no husband's name is legible after Aase's. A 'Peder Joensen Stueflatten' is named at the head of the 1744 entry, perhaps as guardian or the widower (the card's 'Iver Iversøn' as second husband is not confirmed by this reading). Grade unchanged (Probable, graded on her link to Aaste, Aaste Iversdatter): the protocol confirms Aaste/Aase as her daughter, but the step from the 22-year-old Aase Iversdatter of Stueflotten in 1744 to Aaste wife of Alf of Nystuen rests on name, age and the farm complex. ¶4a

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 record that disagrees · 1 open question

Records that disagree 1

Name conflict: the 1744 card writes her patronymic as something like 'S[igmund]sd.' (hand unclear), against 'Biørnsdtr.' on the 1732 card and 'Biørnsd.' in the 1743 burial index. The children and their ages agree exactly on both cards (Biørn b. about 1728, Anders b. about 1730-31, Aaste b. about 1721-22), so it is the same woman; Biørnsdatter is preferred (two sources, and her eldest son was named Biørn). The bygdebok gives her as 'ca. 1698-1743', against b. 1689 in the burial index. ¶2

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Test record: Aaste and Alf's marriage (1738 per the bygdebok, Lesja 9236 about canvases 100-110), if it names the bride as of Stueflotten or her father. ¶4b
