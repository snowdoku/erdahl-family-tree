Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Graded on her link to her son Ralph Josselyn. Wife of John Josselyn of Sheering only in heraldic pedigrees: the Visitation of London 1633-4 (College of Arms C.24 f.258b, as printed by Howard and Crisp), 'John Joscelin = Anne, son and heir of Geoffrey', and French's pedigree no. 18 (NEHGR 71:241, 'He married Anne ___', no source given). Wessler (1961) calls her 'Anne (Lavenham)' without a source; that surname is not used here. No will, fine or court record read names her, or names her as Ralph's mother. Speculative. ¶1a

Searches that found nothing 1

Searched for her test record, nothing found; grade unchanged (Speculative). Feet of Fines for Essex vol. 4 (1423-1547), where a fine by 'John Josselyn and Anne his wife' would be, is not on archive.org or any free site; vols 3 (1327-1422) and 5 (1547-1580) are, and hold no such fine. Neither Fragmenta Genealogica's nor French's abstract of the 1524/5 will of John Josselyn of High Roding names John of Sheering's wife. The ERO catalogue's TS 557/1 (transcripts of 29 Joscelyne wills, 1465-1619) and T/G 159/3 (all ERO Jocelyn wills, c.1400 onwards) are searchroom-only. Test record unchanged, plus: TS 557/1 at the Essex Record Office, for any 15th-century Josselyn will naming Anne. ¶2

Open questions and the next record to read 1
