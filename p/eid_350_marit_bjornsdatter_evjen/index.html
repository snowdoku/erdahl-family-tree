1 on the grade · 1 alternative set aside

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Graded Probable on her link to her son Bjørn Ingebrigtsen Evjen (see his page). Selbu i fortid og nåtid vol. 5, Stor-Evja, 'Brukere' (canvas 216): '-1647-1666- Bjørn Haldorsen, f. ca. 1598, død [...] 1690, [og] det var skifte' with eleven children, the last '11. Marit, f. ca. 1649, [g.m.] Ingebrikt Olsen Overvik, [på] Stor-Evja'. Vol. 6, Overvik 'Brukere' (canvas 325): '-1665-1686- Ola Ingebriktsen, f. ca. 1610 [...] sønner: [...] ca. 1654; 2. Gunnar, f. ca. 1658, [tok] over; 3. Ingebrikt, f. 1665, [g.m.] Marit [...] Lien, [på] Stor-Evja'. So the book makes her the wife of Ingebrigt Olsen of Store Evjen, whose son Bjørn (b. 1686-91) bore her father's name. ¶1

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Cautions: (1) the recovered words do not list Ingebrigt's children under her, so her motherhood of Bjørn is inferred from the marriage and the name; (2) the 1744 probate after Ingebrigt names daughters aged 24-28, so he had a younger second wife, and Mali Ingebrigtsdatter (b. about 1692, when Marit was about 43) could belong to either marriage - she is not linked here; (3) vol. 6 adds 'Lien' after Marit, not explained in the recovered words. Bjørn Haldorsen's 1690 skifte (Stjør- og Verdal protocol 1, 24749, 1689-1701) would list Marit and her husband among the heirs: the next record to read. Her father Bjørn Haldorsen is not added (bygdebok only, one step further). ¶2
