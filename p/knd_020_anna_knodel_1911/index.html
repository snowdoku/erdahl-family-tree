Life story

Birth: born at Płużnica (Pfeilsdorf) in her father's dwelling on 1 Aug 1911 at 4 a.m., daughter of the settler (osadnik) Jakob Knodel and his wife Katharina née Reister, both Evangelical. Her father registered the birth on 4 Aug 1911 at the Standesamt (civil registry office) Pfeilsdorf and signed the act (1911 no. 34). She is the fourth known child of the couple, after Elisabeth (1905), Gottlieb (1906) and Wilhelm (1908). Nothing further is known of her. She would be a first cousin of Erna Knodel only if her father was Gottlieb Knodel's brother, which is unproved (see her father's parentage note). ¶1

Baptism (index): the Evangelical parish of Villisaß, Kreis Culm, has a baptism of Anna Knodel on 24 Sep 1911, with the relatives indexed as 'Jakob, Katharina'. The civil birth at Pfeilsdorf was on 1 Aug 1911, 54 days earlier, and the relatives' names repeat the parents' given names, so this is very probably this child's baptism. This is an index entry only (Ancestry 'Germany, Lutheran Baptisms, Marriages, and Burials, 1567-1945', dbId 61250); the register image has not been read, so the index does not say whether the two names in its unlabelled 'Relatives' field are the parents or the godparents, and it gives no sponsors. Index recordId 3649702, image id 0245317-00201. ¶2
