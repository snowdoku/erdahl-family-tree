Life story

NEW. Mother of Gundbjør Targiesdatter (see her page), graded on that link. Record: the probate after her husband Targei Halvorsen at Sønderland, 25 May 1759 (skifteprotokoll 9, DA 25673 s. 472b, view 481, No. 321), divided the estate between 'hands efterlevende Enke Torbor Halvorsdatter og deres med hinanden avlede Børn', naming Gundbiør Targeisdatter, 17, among them. 'Deres med hinanden avlede Børn' (their children together) names her as Gundbjør's mother. Continuity: Gundbjør Targiesdatter of Sønderland married Aasmund Evindsen Hiemdal two years later (1 Jul 1761) and was buried aged 22 in 1764, which fits 17 in 1759; no other Gundbjør Targiesdatter is known on the farm (Targe Toroffsen's 1756 probate lists no Gundbjør). The other children in 1759 were Halvor (20), Kittil (19, reading uncertain), Aasild (15) and Sigri (11). ¶1

Burial: 'Enken Torbor Halvorsdatter Sonderland', aged 40, buried at Treungen 25 Jan 1760 (Kviteseid ministerialbok 7981, burials p.19, image 162). The age 40 gives birth about 1720, which fits an eldest child aged 20 in 1759. Her parents are not known. ¶2
