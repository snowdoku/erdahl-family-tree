Life story

First marriage: the probate index card for Sando in Ål records the registration (1 Feb) and skifte (3 Jun 1723) after Torsten Halvorsen of Sando, whose widow was Berte Olsdatter; their only child was Halvor, a minor. Torsten's mother Gunild Herbrandsdatter was living, and his maternal uncle was Ole Herbrandsen (skifteprotokoll 9, p. 152b). ¶1

First marriage (scan read): the skifte at Sando in Ål on 1 Feb 1723 after 'Dannemand Torsten Halfvorsen, som boede og døde sammesteds' was held 'imellem hans efterlatte Hustru Berte Olsdatter' and their son 'Halfvor Torstensen' (skifteprotokoll [8] 1721-1726, fol. 152b, source 25544 view 162; card 3917 image 3185: child Halfvor umyndig, the deceased's mother Gunild Herbrandsdatter living, 9 løb in Sando inherited by the skifte of 1719 after his father Halvor Torstensen Sandoe). This explains the 1781 skifte after Halvor Tostensen, which names Bergith Olsdatter as his mother and Tosten Thordsen as his stepfather: she was Berte Olsdatter, widow of Torsten Halvorsen, and she brought the Sando land into her second marriage with Tosten Thordsen. Her first husband Torsten Halvorsen was the brother of Rangdi Halvorsdatter of Dybsjorde (probable identification), mother of Asle Knudsen, who later married Bergith's daughter Kari Tostensdatter. Her own parents remain unknown (the 1723 entry was read only at its opening). ¶8

Contract of 16 Oct 1759 read: Ringerike og Hallingdal pantebok nr. 11 (1755-1764), s. 303 left page, no. 22 (registered at Ål, 1759): 'Kiendes jeg underskrevne Tosten Thordsen Sando og jeg Halvor Tostensen'... 'at som jeg Tosten Thordsen og min kiere Hustru er... gamle Folk' they have contracted with 'vores... Søn' [one word before Søn not read with certainty] Halvor Tostensen that he take over 'vores halve Anpart i gaarden Sando', with a retirement contract (kår) for the couple; dated Sando 16 Oct 1759, witnesses Torchild Olsen Welland and Ole Stensen Hammersbøen. The wife is not named in the contract itself; the 1781 skifte names the contracting couple as Tosten Thordsen and Bergith Olsdatter. Kari is not named in the contract, so the grade stays Probable. ¶5

Second marriage (Speculative): the 1781 probate index card after her son Halvor Tostensen of Sando names his mother as 'Bergith Olsd.' and his stepfather as 'Tosten Thordsen Sando', both queried (?) by the card-maker, and a half-brother Tosten Tostensen of Sando (skifteprotokoll 20a, fol. 270a). She is taken to be the same woman as Berte Olsdatter of 1723. ¶2

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 3

Confidence: Speculative, as mother of Kari Tostensdatter of Sando (born about 1736), whose brother was a Tosten Tostensen of Sando. If Kari and Tosten were children of the second marriage, the marriage was after Feb 1723. ¶3a

Upgrade to Probable, as mother of Kari Tostensdatter of Sando. The 1781 skifte after her son Halvor Tostensen of Sando (skifteprotokoll 20, source 25555, fol. 270b-271a, read on the scan) shows (1) a contract of 16 Oct 1759 between Halvor and 'Tosten Thordsen og Bergith Olsdatter' over Sando, so she was by then married to Tosten Thordsen and still at Sando, which bears out the 1781 card's identification of her with Berte Olsdatter, widow of Torsten Halvorsen in 1723; and (2) a 1770 obligation in which Halvor calls Tosten Tostensen of Sando 'min Broder'. Since Halvor was the only child of the first marriage, Tosten Tostensen was her son by Tosten Thordsen, and Kari Tostensdatter's 1773 guardian was 'Børnenes Morbroder Tosten Tostensen Sanda'. Kari is therefore very probably her daughter by Tosten Thordsen. ¶4a

Upgrade: Confirmed, graded on her link to her daughter Kari Tostensdatter. The deed (no. 8, entered 'Extimus' 1791) opens: 'Underskrevne Arvinger efter Salig afdøde Tosten Thorsen og Birgit Olsdatter Sandoe, nemlig Jeg Tosten Tostensen paa egne Vegne, Jeg Ole Tostensen Schierven paa egne Vegne, Jeg Knud Engebretsen Schrinde paa min Hustrue Astrie Tostensdatters Vegne, og Jeg Tosten Aslesen Meedgaarden paa egne og mine Søskendes[?] Birgit Aslesdatters og Ole Aslesens Vegne, alle Boende udi Aals Præstegield i Hallingdahlen'. They sell to Tosten Halvorsen Sandoe the share of Øvre Sando that fell to them 'som Arv efter benævnte vores Salig afdøde Forældre og Bedste Forældre Tosten Thorsen og Birgit Olsdatter Sandoe' (as inheritance after our late parents and grandparents), 11 løb, for 663 rd 1 ort 8 sk; Tosten Tostensen also refers to 'min Salig afdøde Broder Halvor Tostensen'. Dated 4 Oct 1791, tinglyst 14 Oct 1791 (pantebok 15, s. 308; Ål panteregister p. 190). The grandchildren Tosten Aslesen Medgaarden, Birgit Aslesdatter and Ole Aslesen are the children of Asle Knudsen and Kari Tostensdatter (Kari's 1785 probate); their paternal grandparents were Knud Aslesen and Rangdi Halvorsdatter of Dybsjorde (1742 probate), so the inheritance from Tosten Thorsen and Birgit Olsdatter came through Kari, who was thus their daughter. Continuity: same Medgaarden heirs as in 1785, six years apart; no rival. Her own parents remain unknown; she died before 4 Oct 1791. ¶7

(2) The 1739 probate after the bachelor Biørn Olsen of Sando (skifteprotokoll 11, source 25546, image 574, fol. 552b-553a) lists his full siblings Erik Olsen Guldhagen, Niels Olsen Skinnemoen, Kari, Guri, Guri the younger and Gro Olsdatter (m. Kittel Guttormsen Bergsgaard) and a half-brother Iver Olsen Tormodsgaard, but no Berte/Bergith Olsdatter, so she was not his sister. ¶6
