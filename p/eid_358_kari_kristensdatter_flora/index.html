Life story

Named as the widow 'Karen Christensdatter' in the probate after Ole Jonsen, Stokke, 25 Oct 1768 (24756 fol. 219b), with their children Jon 10, Christen 3, Mette 11 and Berete 7. Her patronymic Kristensdatter, which the bygdebok rebuild had left in brackets, is now read in a primary record, and her son Kristen (b. 1765) carries her father's name. Kept Probable on her link to John Olsen Stokke (see his page) for the same 33-year gap as her husband. The entry may also be read 'deres sammenavlede Børn' (their children together), but the fold makes it uncertain. Her own probate (d. 1769 per bygdebok) is not in the typed 24756 register (K section checked) or under Stokkan, Selbu in 24757. ¶4

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

Patronymic: an exact query for 'Kristensdt.' returns a box on the same printed line as her name (canvas 19, y 2908), and her son Kristen (b. 1765) supports a father Kristen; the patronymic is still a reading from word boxes, so treat it as tentative. She had a son Jon (b. 1747) before the marriage, father '[...] Eidem'. ¶1
