Lars Olesen Nørvec. 1735 – 1818Confirmed · Lille Nørve

About the family

Father of Lars, baptised at Borgund on New Year's Day 1735 (or 1736: see the weekday note on Lars Olesen Nørve): 'Olle Olsøn Lille Nørves Søn N. Lars' (Borgund ministerialbok 1698-1739, canvas c195, scan). Resided at Lille Nørve, Borgund, in 1735. Godparents at the baptism, possibly kin or neighbours: Ole Knudsen, Jon Larsen, Andreas Iversen[?] and Karen Arentsdatter[?] of Lille Nørve, and Synnøve Gundersdatter of Godøy. His wife is not named. Birth 'BEF 1715' is only a bound inferred from his fathering a child in 1735. Probable as the father of Lars Olesen Nørve, because the identity of the 1735 child with the 1760 groom rests on age and farm. ¶1

Life story

1762 extra tax, Borgund, Lille Nørve household 4 (49637 view 37): 'M: Lars Olsen, H: Sidsle Andersdatter'; in the column for other persons supported: '2de Søster: Synneve og Birthe Olsdatt.' (his two sisters Synneve and Birthe Olsdatter); in the next column: 'Hans Forældre: F: Ole Olsen, M: Elen Ingebrigtsdatt.' (his parents: father Ole Olsen, mother Elen Ingebrigtsdatter); with a servant Hans Andersen Wolsdal; 7 persons taxed. ¶4

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 3

Upgrade: Confirmed, graded on his link to his son Lars Olsen Nørve (see his page). The record naming the relationship is the Borgund baptism (ministerialbok 1698-1739, source 15995, canvas c195, scan, audited) of New Year's Day 1735 or 1736: 'dobt Olle Olsøn Lille Nørves Søn N. Lars', with godparents Ole Knudsen, Jon Larsen, Andreas Iversen[?] and Karen Arentsdatter[?] of Lille Nørve. Continuity: the next record of Lars is his engagement at Borgund on 14 Oct 1759 as a bachelor of Lille Nørve, and his sponsor there was Jon Larsen of Lille Nørve, the godfather of 1735/36; he then farms Lille Nørve for life (1801 census, 66, first marriage; 1815 census, 81; buried 1818 aged 84), ages that all point to a birth about 1734-35. ¶2a

Audit 3 (1 Nov 2026): downgraded from Confirmed to Probable, graded on his link to his son Lars Olsen Nørve (see his page). The relationship is named by a primary record, the Borgund baptism of New Year's Day 1735/36 ('dobt Olle Olsøn Lille Nørves Søn N. Lars', 15995 c195). But the next record of that boy is the engagement of 14 Oct 1759, about 24 years later, with no record in between. That gap is bridged only by the farm name, the sponsor Jon Larsen of Lille Nørve (a godfather in 1735/36) and the fit of later ages. Under the operative grading rule (BRIEF.md, clarified after audit 2), an identification that spans decades without a bridging record stays Probable. This is the same reason Marit Olsdatter (25 years) and Ole Unknown Sunde were left at Probable in the regrade. The note itself names the caveat. ¶3a

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed, graded on his link to his son Lars Olsen Nørve (see his page). The 1762 extra-tax list for Lille Nørve (49637 view 37, household 4) names the relationship: under 'Lars Olsen' and his wife 'Sidsle Andersdatter' it lists 'Hans Forældre: F: Ole Olsen, M: Elen Ingebrigtsdatter'. Continuity to the tree: the Lars of this household is identified by his wife, Sidsle (Cæcilia) Andersdatter, whom Lars Olsen of Lille Nørve married at Borgund on 1 Jan 1760, two years earlier, and by the farm, where he stayed to the 1801 and 1815 censuses. It also closes the 1735-1759 gap that the audit noted: the Ole Olsen of Lille Nørve who had Lars baptised in 1735 is on the same farm in 1762 as Lars's named father. There is only one Lars Olsen and one Ole Olsen in the Lille Nørve households of 1762 (households 1-3 are Knud Thorsen, Iver Nielsen and Knud Larsen). ¶5

Alternatives considered and set aside 2

No rival: the Borgund baptism index for 1725-1745 has six other boys Lars with a father Ole, all on other farms (Ålesund, Skjong, Hessen twice, Sandvika, Drevika), and the Skjong boy of 1727 is absent from his father Ole Olsen's 1749 probate; the index has no other Lars Olsen at Nørve 1745-1758. ¶2b

Caveat: the 24-year gap between baptism and engagement is bridged by the farm and the Jon Larsen sponsorship, not by an intermediate record; a Borgund confirmation entry for Lars of Lille Nørve about 1750-1753 (lists give name, farm and age) would close it. ¶2c

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Would confirm: a Borgund confirmation entry for Lars of Lille Nørve about 1750-53, a probate after Ole Olsen Lille Nørve naming Lars, or any record of 1736-1759 that names Lars as Ole's son. Lars's own grade (as father of Knud) is unaffected. ¶3b
