Life story

Collateral, added to prove a link: sister of Todne Larsdatter (see her page). She was the widow of Stian Madsøn of Søndre Askland, Gjøvdal, whose estate was settled on 16 Mar 1708 (Skifteprotokoll nr. 3 fol. 45b-46b, read on the scan; card 37). Their children were Lars Stiansøn and Thargier Stiansdatter. The guardians were Evind Biødnesøn, 'som haver Enkens Søster', and Thargier Salvesøn, 'som haver Enkens Søsterdatter'. Card 37 says the land was 4 ksk. in Askland. ¶1
