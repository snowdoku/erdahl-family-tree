About the family

Daughters: three daughters are known or likely: Anna Karolina (b. 19 Nov 1881, Anna Karolina Schmidt; Probable as her daughter), Wilhelmine (b. 9 Jan 1885 at Ciucurova, m. Daniel Frank 1905; Probable, resting on the compiled Ciucurova village family book, which names 'Maria Adam' as her mother; not entered as a person) and Anna (b. 24 Nov 1888 in Romania, d. 31 Jul 1906 at Pfeilsdorf, Anna Schmidt; Confirmed from her own death act). Geni (a lead) claims about ten children in all. ¶10

Life story

Dates: the village family book gives her only as 'born before 1869', which is the compiler's estimate, not a record; no birth date or place is entered here. A marriage to Wilhelm Schmidt between June 1879 (when he was listed without a wife in his widowed mother's household at Atmagea) and early 1884 is inferred from the Atmagea soul list of 1879 and Wilhelmine's birth in January 1885; if Karolina (born 19 Nov 1881) is their child, the marriage was before about February 1881. ¶3

1909: the Lissewo marriage act of her son Friedrich (1909 no. 6, Heimbrunn, 13 May 1909; image read) names her as 'Marie, geborene Adam', wife of the settler Wilhelm Schmidt of Bilau, Kreis Culm; Friedrich was born 3 Nov 1886 at Ciucurova, where the Ciucurova village family book places her with Wilhelm in 1885. She was alive in May 1909. ¶12

1913: the Pfeilsdorf marriage act of her daughter Rosa Therese (1913 no. 15, 9 Nov 1913; image read) names her as 'Marie geborene Adam', wife of the settler Wilhelm Schmidt, living at Bilau, Kreis Culm, West Prussia. Rosa was born at Atmagea in Romania on 30 June 1890. This is a second primary record naming her as the wife of Wilhelm Schmidt and the mother of a sister of Karolina, and it shows that she was alive in November 1913 and had moved with her husband to West Prussia. It does not name Karolina, so her status as Karolina's own mother stays Probable for the reason given above (a half-sister scenario through an unknown first wife is not excluded). ¶13

Death, lead only (Oct 2026): the Ancestry dbId 61250 index has a burial of a Marie Schmidt at Thorn on 24 Dec 1909, relative Friedrich (record 3173084, image 0245567-00202, not read). This is a name-only candidate and weak: Friedrich could be her son (b. 1886), but Thorn is a town parish outside the Culm-area parishes where the family appears. ¶15

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

2 on the grade · 1 on the identification · 2 corrections · 3 alternatives set aside · 2 open questions

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Upgrade, Speculative to Probable (13 Oct 2026), moved by the Standesamt Pfeilsdorf death register, 1906 no. 23 (Polish certified copy of 1949, AP Toruń 69/1205/0/2.3/1013, scan 221): on 1 Aug 1906 Gottlieb Knodel registered the death in his house at Płużnica of his 'unmarried sister-in-law' Anna Schmidt (b. 24 Nov 1888 in Romania), 'daughter of Wilhelm Schmidt and his wife Anna Maria née Adam'. An unmarried Schmidt sister-in-law of Gottlieb can only be a sister of his wife Karolina née Schmidt, whose father is named 'Willem Schmidt' on her 1974 BC death registration. So Karolina's sister's parents were Wilhelm Schmidt and Anna Maria Adam. This supersedes the earlier statement that she was only the leading candidate. ¶7

Why Probable and not Confirmed: no record yet names Karolina's own mother. The 1906 act names the mother of Karolina's sister. If Wilhelm Schmidt had an earlier wife who died between Karolina's birth (Nov 1881) and the birth of his daughter Wilhelmine (Jan 1885), Karolina and Anna would be half-sisters and Karolina's mother would be someone else. Nothing points to such a first wife: Wilhelm (if he is the Atmagea man born 1854) was listed without a wife in June 1879, leaving little room for one, and Geni's listing (a lead only) independently gives Karolina's mother as Anna Maria. What would confirm: Karolina's confirmation (about 1895-96) or marriage (about 1900) in the Evangelical church book of Constanța (Family History Library film 1768339), or the Heimatortskartei card of Wilhelm Schmidt and Maria Adam (Bessarabiendeutscher Verein, Stuttgart), either of which would name Karolina with her mother. ¶8

The case for this identification 1

Identification (Speculative): she is entered as the mother of Anna Karolina Schmidt (see her page) only as the leading candidate, not as a proved fact. No record yet names Karolina as her daughter. Karolina's own 1974 BC death registration names only her father, 'Willem Schmidt'; her mother's name there is not given. ¶1

Corrections 2

Correction: name. The Standesamt Pfeilsdorf death act of her daughter Anna Schmidt, 1906 no. 23, calls her 'Anna Maria z domu Adam' (Anna Maria née Adam), wife of Wilhelm Schmidt. Her display name is therefore changed from 'Maria Adam', the form in the Ciucurova village family book, to Anna Maria Adam, which also matches the 'Anna Maria' of Geni's listing. ¶6

Correction: the earlier notes on her that say no record names her as a mother of Karolina's family, and that Karolina's 1974 registration is the only record naming a parent, are superseded by the 1906 Pfeilsdorf death act of her daughter Anna, which names her as 'Anna Maria née Adam'. It remains true that no record names her as Karolina's own mother. ¶11

Alternatives considered and set aside 3

Evidence for the candidate: the Ciucurova village family book (Online-OFB Ciucurova, compiled by Axel Eichhorn, entry I10393, citing the Heimatortskartei, the Bessarabian-German home-town card index in Stuttgart, card series Cogealia) names Maria Adam as the wife of Wilhelm Schmidt and the mother of Wilhelmine Schmidt, born 9 Jan 1885 (28 Dec 1884 Old Style) at Ciucurova in the Dobruja. The 1940 resettlement (EWZ) index independently gives Wilhelmine's father as Wilhelm Schmidt, born 24 Aug 1854 at Atmagea. Geni's public listing (a lead only) names Karolina's parents as Wilhelm Schmidt and Anna Maria Schmidt, which fits 'Maria'. The family lived at Cogealia (Kodschalie, now Lumina, Constanța county) by 1899, which fits the family memory 'Cogelea' for the birthplace of Karolina's son Wilhelm in 1904. ¶2

Rival candidates for Karolina's mother: (1) Karoline née Liebelt (born 1838 Tarutino), wife of Wilhelm Schmidt born 1833 at Katzbach (died 1917 at Cataloi), who had children in the Atmagea-Cataloi-Ciucurova area 1859-1884 with a gap 1879-84, but who already had a daughter named Karoline; (2) Karoline née Siebert, wife of a Wilhelm Schmidt whose son Johann was baptised in 1867 at 'Fardan, Walachei' (St Petersburg Consistory extracts, film 1884091/3 p.221 no.59); (3) the unnamed wife of a Schmidt of the colony Fachria, whose families lived at Cogealac 1877-84. None of these is named Maria, which is the main reason the Adam candidate leads. ¶4

Rival mothers: the 1906 act removes the rival candidates listed earlier (Karoline Liebelt, Karoline Siebert, and the unnamed wife of a Fachria Schmidt), unless Karolina was a half-sister through a first marriage of her father; none of those women is known to have been married to a Wilhelm Schmidt who later married an Anna Maria Adam. ¶9

Open questions and the next record to read 2

What would confirm or refute: Karolina's confirmation (about 1895-96) or her marriage to Gottlieb Knodel (about 1899-1901) in the Evangelical church book of Constanța (Family History Library film 1768339, confirmations 1895-1940), which normally names both parents; the Heimatortskartei family card of Wilhelm Schmidt and Maria Adam (Bessarabiendeutscher Verein, Florianstraße 17, 70188 Stuttgart); or the full EWZ naturalisation file of Daniel and Wilhelmine Frank (NARA A3342, EWZ-51 B048 frames 2286/2928; EWZ-58 D020 2454), which may name Wilhelmine's mother and siblings. A confirmation naming other parents would refute it. ¶5

Lead, an Adam family at Tschukurow (Ciucurova): the chronicle of Tschukurow by Johann Adam (Jahrbuch der Dobrudschadeutschen 1972, p. 33) lists 'Witwe J. Adam mit ihren Söhnen (Preußen)', the widow of J. Adam with her sons, from Prussia, among the families there by 1877. Ciucurova is where her daughter Wilhelmine (1885) and son Friedrich (1886) were born, so she may have been a daughter of that widow, but the chronicle names no daughters. In the Atmagea soul list of June 1879 (Jahrbuch 1975, pp. 69-72) the only Adam households are no. 8 (Christian Adam, born 1853, with his wife Katharina and a daughter Karoline born 1877) and no. 26 (Johann Adam, born 1835, and Elisabeth, born 1842, with children born 1861-1879); neither has a Maria, Marie or Anna Maria, although a daughter already married out by 1879 would not appear. These are leads only. ¶14
