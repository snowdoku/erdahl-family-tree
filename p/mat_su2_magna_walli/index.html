About the family

Wife of Gilbert Olsen Slind, married 25 January 1934. Her stone at Selbu Cemetery, Whitman County, reads "Magna Walli Slind, b. Jul 21 1909 d. May 01 2005, s/w & w/o Gilbert m. Jan 25 1934" (reference hrp-cem-whi-selbu_0126). ¶1

She is named as the mother on two of her children's Washington death certificates - Oris Jerome Slind's (1953) and Irene Ann Slind's (1943) - both of which give her as "Magna Walli", her maiden name, which is how her parentage is anchored at all. ¶3

Life story

Probably the daughter of Johan Walli (1885-1959) and Anna Walli (1882-1971) of the same churchyard; her widowed father-in-law Ole G. Slind married Anna Walli in 1961. The relationship is an inference from the cemetery and the marriage, not from a record that states it; the birthplace "Washington" is inferred from a 1909 birth date in a family already settled at Lacrosse by 1908. ¶4
