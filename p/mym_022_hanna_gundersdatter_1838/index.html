Life story

Baptism: born 6 January 1838 and baptised at Ekne on 28 February 1838 as Hanna, legitimate daughter of 'Huusmand Gunder Sivertsen Søndre Kjølen[val]' and wife Karen Helgesdatter (Ekne Døbte 1838, p. 201, no. 1). Godparents: the farmer Augustinus Jochumsen Dahlingen and his wife Martha Larsdatter, the farmer Lorentz Iversen L[...] (farm name partly read), Ingebor Martha Nielsdatter, the bachelor Lars Johnsen Kjølen and the unmarried Mette Sivertsdatter Kjølen. Mette Sivertsdatter shares her father's patronymic and lived on the same farm. She may have been his sister, but nothing proves it. ¶1

She was born in Skogn seven weeks before the Frosta moving certificate of 26 February 1838 was issued, which is why the 1838 in-migration list names only her three older siblings. ¶2

Marriage: on 19 November 1860 at Frosta she married Endre Petersen of Laberget, a bachelor born at Frosta, 27, son of Peter Mortensen Laberget. She is entered as 'Pige Hanna Gundersd.', of Laberget, born in Skogn, 23, and her father as 'Gunder Siversen Kjølen'. Witnesses were Stefen Larsen Moxnæs and Peter Mortensen Laberget (Frosta Viede 1860, p. 362, no. 22). ¶3
