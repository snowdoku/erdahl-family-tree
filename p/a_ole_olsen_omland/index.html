Life story

Burial: 'Ole Olsen den Ældre' (the elder), Enkemand (widower), of Omland, aged 79 (b. about 1722), died 9 Mar and was buried 7 Apr 1801 at Gjøvdal (Åmli ministerialbok 1796-1816, p. 202, no. 21; index pg00000001931028). Name and farm match the Ole Olsen of Omland who married Signe Børjusdatter in 1754 and was at Omland by 1761; 'the elder' distinguishes him from a younger Ole Olsen there (probably his son Ole, b. 1754 or 1756). He is the only Ole Olsen buried from Omland in the index. His birth is not entered: the age points to about 1722, between the two Mjåland baptism candidates (1725 is nearer). ¶11

Baptism candidates — TWO boys named Ole son of an Ole Olsen Mjåland were baptised in Gjøvdal: (a) Dom. Sexagesimae (11 Feb) 1725: 'Ole Olsön Miaalands barn Ole' (Åmli 1711-1740 pp. 70-71, canvas 38); this father, Ole Olsen Mjåland, was buried in Gjøvdal 27 Dec 1729 aged 49 (canvas 51); (b) Dom. Esto mihi (20 Feb) 1735: 'Item Giævedal döbt Ole Olsön Miaalands barn Ole' (pp. 120-121, canvas 63), presumably son of the Ole Olsen Mjåland who was betrothed 1 Jan 1732 and married Jun 1733 to Marthe Aslaksdatter of Øvre Ramse, Tovdal (canvases 58, 60). Which of the two married Signe Børusdatter in 1754 (and which married Kari Aslaksdatter by 1752) is not settled; the 1725 boy is the same age as Signe (bapt. 1725). ¶8

Background on the Mjåland Ole Olsens (round 6, Åmli 9920 canvases 3, 7, 9, 18, 19, 26, 27): the elder Ole Olsen Mjåland (bur. 27 Dec 1729 aged 49) married 1st on 2 Jul 1711 in Gjøvdal Kristi Jacobsdatter, who was buried in Sep 1713 aged 24; their son Ole was baptised 29 Nov 1712. He married 2nd in Jul 1717 (Dom. 6 p. Trin.) Sigri Aslaksdatter; their children were Kristi (21 May 1718), Ole (1 Dec 1720, buried Mar 1721 aged 12 weeks), Gunild (1722), Ole (11 Feb 1725) and Asbor (d. 1728). So Ole Olsen Omland is either the 1712 son of Kristi (then the probable husband of Marthe Aslaksdatter in 1732) or the 1725 son of Sigri, or the 1733 son of the younger Ole × Marthe. This is still unresolved. ¶10

YEAR CORRECTION (round 5): canvas 62 carries 'Anno 1733' and canvas 65 'Ao 1734', so the second baptism 'Ole Olsön Miaalands barn Ole' was Dom. Esto mihi 1733 (= 15 Feb 1733), not 1735; and Ole Olsen Mjåland x Marthe Aslaksdatter Øvre Ramse married Dom. 3 p. Trin. 1732 (canvas 60), not 1733. That couple also had Christi, bapt. Dom. 2 p. Epiph. 1736 Gjøvdal (canvas 70). The candidates for Ole Olsen Omland are therefore the Ole bapt. 11 Feb 1725 and the Ole bapt. 15 Feb 1733 (aged 21 at the 1754 marriage). ¶9

MARRIAGE FOUND: 'Cop: Ole Olsön Miaaland og Signe Börusdatter Jörenland' — Åmli 1741-1775, 1754 marriage no. 12 (canvas 43), entered directly after the Gjøvdal baptism of 3 p. Trinit. (30 Jun) 1754; the day is inferred. He was then of Mjåland (Miaaland), Gjøvdal; at Omland by 1761. ¶5

The Oland households that do appear in that period are those of Ole Tronsen Oland (estate relinquished 8 June 1748) and Signe Johnsdatter (estate 1 August 1729); their son Joen Olsen Oland (estate 27 November 1778) and his wife Kari Tellefsdatter; Even Olsen; Even Vraalsen; and the Knudsen family of Sigridnes. He may have been a cottager or a younger son whose estate was never registered. ¶3

First child: Ole, baptised in Gjøvdal 27 Dec 1754 (3rd day of Christmas): 'Ole Olsön Miaalands og Signe Börüsdatter barn Ole'; godparents Tallach and Evind Miaaland, Tellef Asland, Kari and Gunild Miaaland (Dec 1754 no. 5, canvas 45). An Ole Olsen Mialand was also a godfather in 1749 to Gullov son of Aadne Larsen Gangsei. ¶6

Betrothed 25 Mar 1754 in Gjøvdal to Signe Børusdatter Jørundland as 'Ole Olsön Miaaland', by royal licence; sureties Aadne Jørundland and Evind Bendixsen Mjåland (canvas 42). Son Ole baptised 7 Mar 1756 (canvas 49). ¶7a

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

Caution: another Ole Olsen at Mjåland, married to Kari Aslaksdatter, had a son Aslak baptised Epiphany 1752 (canvas 35). ¶7b

Searches that found nothing 1

Searched for and NOT found in this round. All 74 Østre Råbyggelag probate cards for "Oland i Gjøvdal" (covering about 1700 to 1830) were read card by card. No estate of an Ole Olsen at Oland names a daughter Tøri, and no estate at all matches a couple Ole Olsen and Signe in the 1760s. ¶2
