About the family

Parents linked (this parent link is PROBABLE, not Confirmed): Anders Knudsen and Astri Jansdatter of søndre Heimdal, Treungen. Evidence: (1) their probates of 8 Nov 1727 and 16 Jul 1733 name a daughter Gunnild Andersdatter aged 9 and 15 (b. about 1718); (2) she was 84 in the 1801 Felle census (b. about 1717), same parish; (3) her full brother Torof Andersøn Hiemdahl was a creditor (33 rd) in her husband Tron Toresøn Fælle's estate, 19 Nov 1757; (4) her son Tore Thronsen married Torof's daughter Hæge Toruvsdatter in 1781 with a dispensation for kinship 'i andet Leed' (first cousins), which this identification explains. No other Gunild Andersdatter of the right age is known in Treungen (the older Gunnild Andersdatter, widow of Gunsten Evensen, nordre Heimdal, 1717, is ruled out by age). Still Probable because no record names her parents and her husband together. A probate after her (she died 1801-10, Øvre Telemark protocols 18-19, DA 25682-25683, not carded) or after Torof would settle it. ¶4

Life story

Probably the Gunnild Andersdatter aged 9 in 1727 and 15 in 1733 who was the daughter of Anders Knudsen and Astri Jansdatter of søndre Heimdal, Treungen, and full sister of Toruv Andersen (probate 8 Nov 1727 and 16 Jul 1733). Her age (b. about 1718) matches her age in the 1801 census (84). The probate is too early to name her husband, so the identification rests on name, age and parish. If correct, it explains the 1781 marriage of her son Tore Thronsen to Hæge Toruvsdatter: they were first cousins. Parents not linked in this draft for that reason. Do not confuse her with an older Gunnild Andersdatter, widow of Gunsten Evensen, Heimdal nordre (probate 1717). ¶2

Listed in the 1801 census at Felle as 'husbondens moder', mother of Tore Thronsen: aged 84, widow after her first marriage. Astri Thronsdatter (62) is listed after her as her child. ¶1

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: widow 'Gunnild Andersdaatter' at the probate of her husband Tron Toresøn Fælle, 19 Nov 1757, with children Tore 16, Anders 4, Astri 19, Anne 13, Ingeborg 11, Hage 6 and Gro about 1 (Øvre Telemark skifteprotokoll nr. 9, skifte no. 244). This confirms her as Tore Thronsen's mother and Tron's wife. Her parentage is still unproved: Torof Andersøn Hiemdahl appears in the estate as a creditor (33 rd), which fits but does not prove that she was his sister. ¶3
