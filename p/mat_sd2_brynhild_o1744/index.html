About the family

Eldest child of Ola Jornsen and Marit Pedersdatter, and so an elder sister of Jorn (Jorgen) Olssen born 1752. Selbu i fortid og naatid vol. 7, p. 116: 'Brynhild, f. 1744, g.I m. [N.], [g.] II m. JON PERSEN [N.], se NORDSTO' - married twice. ¶1

Pedigree collapse (third route, Probable): Ola Jornsen and Marit Pedersdatter of Slindan are ancestors of Gunder Olsen Slind and Oline, and so of Palmer Gilbert Eidem, three times: (1) Jorgen (Jorn) Olssen, b. 1752 -> Gunder Jorgensen Slind -> Ole Gundersen Slind -> Gunder Olsen Slind; (2) Ingeborg Olsdatter, b. 1749 -> Ingebor Jonsdatter of Sandvik -> Marit Haldorsdatter -> Kari Olsdatter, wife of Gunder Olsen Slind; (3) Brynhild Olsdatter, b. 1744 -> Magli Christensdatter -> Brynhild Eriksdatter -> Berith Gundersdatter Eidem, wife of Ole Gundersen Slind and mother of Gunder Olsen Slind. So Ole Gundersen Slind and Berith Gundersdatter (m. 24 May 1841) were second cousins once removed, and Gunder Olsen Slind descends from Ola and Marit through both his parents as well as through his wife. Route 3 rests on the identification of Magli's mother Brynild Olsdatter with Ola's daughter Brynhild (Probable); routes checked with fleet.py show. ¶9

Mother of Mali (Magli) Christensdatter, baptised in Selbu on 16 July 1769 (8th Sunday after Trinity) as daughter of Christen Christensen Lien and 'Brynild Old:' (Brynild Olsdatter). ¶10

Mother of Magli Christensdatter (see her page), rated Probable on the same chain as her husband Christen Christensen Lien (see his notes). Supporting it: Magli named her daughter born 1796 Brynil, the Selbu habit of naming a first daughter after a grandmother. Her own birth, marriage and death have not been searched. ¶11

Life story

Death and burial: Brynhild Olsd Lien, born 1744, was buried in Selbu on 8 July 1827 (Selbu local parish church book 1824-1842, burial index). She died before that date, presumably at Lien where she lived in 1825 (inference); the index gives no residence or age, and the page image should be read to confirm. ¶8

Merged duplicate record `eid_123_brynild_olsdatter` into this one: Same woman: Selbu i fortid og naatid vol. 7 p. 116 says Brynhild, b. 1744, daughter of Ola Jornsen of Slindan, married I Kristen Kristensen and II Jon Persen of Lien; the 1769 baptism of Mali names her parents as Christen Christensen Lien and Brynild Olsdatter; the 1801 census has Brynil Olsdatter, 57, at Lien as Jon Pederssen's wife in her second marriage, with Kristensdatter daughters Soffi 21 and Brynil 17; Sofie Christensdatter Lien was godmother to Magli's daughter Brynil in 1796; Brynhild Olsd., b. 1743, widow at Lien 1825; Brynhild Olsd Lien, b. 1744, buried 8 Jul 1827. Kept id has the parents and the bygdebok source. ¶12

Baptism read by the orchestrator (zoom of 16315 canvas 57 col. 1, first noted at overview): 'Børnedaab N:1 Ole Slindens barn ved Nafn: Brynill. Test: Jonn Slinden, Ole Renaaen, Mali Slinden, Berete Slinden, Mali Slinden, madamn Marithe Pedersdatter' - early 1744, entered just before Fastelavn Sunday (16 Feb 1744). The 'madame' at the end of a Selbu godparent list in this book is the woman who carried the child, usually the mother (found the same pattern for Guttorm Langlien's twins and his wife Mali Haldorsdatter). This gives Brynhild's mother as Marit Pedersdatter and supports the bygdebok's 'Brynhild, f. 1744' as the eldest child of Ola Jornsen; it also supports Marit's patronymic Pedersdatter against the 1762 ekstraskatt reading 'Tors-'. Her parent link stays Probable: her adult records (1801, 1825, 1827) do not name Slinden, and the identity rests on the bygdebok's account of her two marriages. ¶13

Death and burial, from the full index record: 'Brynhild Olsd Lien', age 83, birth year 1744 (calculated), died 5 July 1827 (index 'Date of death 07-05', year 1827, read as month-day as the burial date 07-08 confirms), buried 8 July 1827 (Selbu local parish 1824-1842, burials, record pg00000007125403). Residence and position are blank. Death changed from 'before 8 July 1827' to 5 July 1827; the place Lien remains an inference from her name form and the 1825 census. ¶14

A brother or sister of Jorn (Jorgen) Olssen Slind, born 1752, David Erdahl's maternal fifth-great-grandfather. ¶2a

Marriages: first to Christen (Kristen) Christensen of Lien, before 16 July 1769, by whom she had Magli (1769) and, by the 1801 census, Sofie (about 1780) and Brynil (about 1784); second, after Christen's death and before 1 February 1801, to Jon Pedersen (Persen) of Lien, about thirteen years her junior. Neither marriage entry has been read. ¶5

1801 census: Selbu, rural residence 0051 Lien, household 02 - Jon Pederssen, 44, husbonde (householder), in his first marriage; Brynil Olsdatter, 57, 'hans kone' (his wife), in her second marriage; Soffi Kristensdatter, 21, unmarried, and Brynil Kristensdatter, 17, entered as 'deres born' (their children) although their patronymic shows a father Kristen; also Jon Pederssen, 49, his brother, and two servants, Siri Pedersdatter 24 and Anders Larssen 11. ¶6

1825 census (27 November 1825): Brynhild Olsd., born 1743, a widow ('Enke') at Lien, Selbu, on the same farm as Sophie Christensd., born 1780, a married woman - presumably her daughter Sofie. Her widowhood implies that Jon Pedersen had died before November 1825 (inference; his burial has not been searched). ¶7

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 3

Rated Probable: from the Selbu gaardshistorie, read by reconstruction from the National Library's word coordinates; no register entry has been read for her. ¶2b

Why Probable and not Confirmed: the link to Slindan rests on the bygdebok, which was read by reconstructing the restricted page from the National Library's word coordinates, not from a page image; no register entry yet names her as Ola Jornsen's daughter. Rival Brynhild Olsdatters of about this age in Selbu (buried 1814 at Moslet Pladsen, b. 1746; buried 1818, b. 1752) do not fit the Lien residence or the 1825 census. ¶4a

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed. Her father's probate, Slinde, 17 Oct 1772 (Skifteprotokoll 6-2, 24757, fol. 530), names '2. Datteren Brønnille Olsdatter, gift med Christen Christensen Stor Efjen' as a child of Ole Jørgensen and Marith Pedersdatter. This ties her both to her parents and to her husband Christen Christensen (later of Lien). In 1772 the couple lived at Stor-Evjen, not Lien; her sister Marithe was married to Ingebrigt Christensen, also of Stor-Evjen. ¶15

The case for this identification 1

Identity: the Brynhild born 1744 at Slindan to Ola Jornsen and Marit Pedersdatter is the Brynild Olsdatter who was the wife of Christen Christensen Lien and the mother of Magli (Mali) Christensdatter, baptised 16 July 1769. Agreeing facts: (1) the bygdebok, Selbu i fortid og naatid vol. 7 p. 116, says she married first Kristen Kristensen and second Jon Persen of Lien (cross-referenced to the Lien Nordsto holding); the earlier reading of this line had left the first husband as [N.], and the word boxes now give Kristen Kristensen; (2) the 1769 baptism names Mali's parents as Christen Christensen of Lien and Brynild Olsdatter; (3) in the 1801 census Brynil Olsdatter, 57 (born about 1744), is at Lien as the wife of Jon Pederssen, in her second marriage, with Soffi Kristensdatter 21 and Brynil Kristensdatter 17 - daughters of a Kristen; (4) Sofie Christensdatter Lien and Christen Christensen Lien stood godparents at Magli's daughter Brynil's baptism in 1796, and that daughter bears this woman's name; (5) Brynhild Olsd., born 1743, is a widow at Lien in 1825, and Brynhild Olsd Lien, born 1744, was buried 8 July 1827. Name, birth year, both husbands and the farm all agree, and no conflicting fact was found. ¶3
