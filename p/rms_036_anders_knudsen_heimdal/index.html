Life story

Not the father of Evind Andersen Heimdal (b. abt 1701): Evind is not among his heirs in 1727 or 1733. ¶2

Farmer on søndre Heimdal (odelsgods = allodial land), Treungen annex of Kviteseid. Widower at the probate of his wife Astri Jansdatter, 8 Nov 1727 (children Torov 7, Biug 5 or 6, Gunnild 9). His own estate was held on 16 Jul 1733 at søndre Heimdal, between his children Torow Andersen, Biug Andersen 11 and Gunnild Andersdatter 15; gross 273 rdl, net 159 rdl. ¶1

End of his 1733 probate (fol. 401b): 'Ole Salvesøn derimod gift med afdøde Anders Knudsøns Søster Torbiør Knudsdaatter skal forsørge S[ver?]moderen Hage Tordsdaatter til sin dødsdag forsvarligen baade med Klæde og Føde samt fornøden opvartning', against which he enjoys certain land in Heimdal (a deed or skifte of 27 Jun 1712 is cited). So Anders had a sister Torbiør Knudsdatter, married to Ole Salvesen, and an old woman Hage Tordsdatter was to be kept for life. If the uncertain word is 'Svermoderen' (mother-in-law, i.e. Ole's wife's mother), Hage Tordsdatter was the mother of Torbiør and so probably of Anders; she is added as a Speculative mother for that reason. Guardians appointed: the dannemand Torgier Sønderland for Torov Andersen; Ole Nielsen Storedahl for Biug Andersen, in place of Anund(?) Haagviaal; Gunnil Andersdatter stays under the guardian appointed before, Tallev Homme. No kinship terms are given for the guardians, so the entry does not prove that Gunild Andersdatter of Felle (b. c.1717) is this Gunnil. ¶3
