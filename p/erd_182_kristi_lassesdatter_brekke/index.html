Life story

Aaland (bygdebok, 1932) on Aaning bruk nr. 2 names her as the wife of Laurits Simonsen Brekke, 'n. 1646' (named in a record of 1646), with their son Truls, holder of Aaning from 1664. The byname Brekke is Aaland's; it may mean she, or her husband, came from Brekke in Oppstryn. Birth before about 1620 is inferred from her son Truls being born about 1638. ¶1

1645 poll tax: 'Kristi' is listed on Aaningh, Oppstryn, directly after 'Lauriz' (The 1645 polltax for Nordfjord, p. 5b, pt00000000066160). ¶2

Court book: at the Stryn ting at Tonning on 26 Feb 1703 (Nordfjord tingbok L0016, fol. 29b-30, DA 30924 v30-31) Lars Larsen Lien sued Lars Olsen Tunold over an exchange deed (mageskiftebrev) of 18 Mar 1668 between Lars Larsen, 'then of Brekke', and Ole Carlsen (Tunold's father), copied and confirmed by sorenskriver Peder Raadt on 22 Nov 1687. It recites that Lars Larsen exchanged half a laup in Brekke, 'which his younger brother Ollef Larsen uses', against ½ laup in Bakkelien (Lien), Ole Carlsen's odel, and that Ole Carlsen was to redeem the rest of the Brekke land 'from Lars Aaning'; later Ole Larsen exchanged 21 skilling-rent back and himself 'redeemed in Brekke from Lars Aaning 1 pund' rent. The court gave the Bakkelien half laup to Lars Larsen's children and heirs as odel, and the 1 laup 18 marks in Brekke to the late Ole Larsen's son Jørgen Olsen 'who is odelsmann to all of it', half of it then used by Jørgen's stepfather (name read as Rasmus Tonnesen, uncertain). Reading: the brothers Lars (Lasse) and Ole Lassesen are the 'Lasse Lassesen 42' and 'Oluf Lassesen 40' farming Brekke in the 1664 vicar's census, so the Brekke odel family of the 1660s were sons of a Lasse. A 'Lars Aaning' held Brekke land in pledge in 1668; Lars is the usual spoken form of Laurits, and Laurits Simonsen of Aaning (Aaland's 'Laurits Simonson Brekke', wife Kristi Lassesdatter Brekke) is the obvious candidate. If so, the pledge fits a Brekke inheritance through Kristi Lassesdatter, which would make Lasse and Ole Lassesen her brothers, and also fits Aaland's 1706 odel suit against Truls Aaning over 3½ pund in Brekke. This is inference only: the deed does not call Lars Aaning Laurits, Simonsen, or Kristi's husband, and Truls already held Aaning from 1664. No confidence change. ¶5

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
