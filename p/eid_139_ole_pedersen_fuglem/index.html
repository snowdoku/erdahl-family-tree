Life story

Possible second marriage: 'Ægte viet agtbare mand Ole Pedersen og pigen Anne Arents datter', 5th Sunday after Trinity (17 Jul) 1740 (16315 c6) - no farm given, so name-only; if this is he, Anne Arentsdatter was his second wife (he was a widower by 1762) and not the mother of Bersvend (b. c.1737). ¶5

Mother added from the bygdebok (Selbu i fortid og nåtid vol. 3, Fuglem canvas 217): Per Persen (b. ca. 1651, d. 1715) 'kona Ingeborg'; children [Bersvend] ca. 1686 and Ola ca. 1689. The book's own entry for him: '1736-1760 Ola Persen, f. 1689, d. 1773, korporal. G.1 1726 [med] Anne [...] 1703, død [på] Garberg 1735'; 'G.2 1736 [med] Marit [...] Fuglem, enke etter [...] Olsen' (canvas 217). Probable only. ¶7

1762 extra tax list: head of holding 691 Fuglum (Fuglem), Selbu (landlord Conferentz Raad Schiøller), as 'Ole Pedersen Enkemand', a widower. His children over 12: Peder, married to Brit (Berit) Brynildsdatter[?]; Bersvend, married to Anne Olsdatter; and a daughter Marit (crop viewed by the orchestrator). ¶1

Burial under Palm Sunday 1773: 'Begravne 1. Olle Pedersen Fuglum død 28 Mart. 77 aar' - died 28 Mar 1773, aged 77, so born about 1695-96 (Selbu 16311 canvas 56, left page). The same day 'Ped. Olsen Fuglums Hustru' was churched (a son Peder Olsen at Fuglem). Identification with this tree person rests on name and farm only (Probable); no other Ole Pedersen at Fuglem of this age is known. ¶3

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Why Probable as father of Bersvend Olsen Fuglem (see his page): the list names Bersvend and his wife Anne Olsdatter as Ole Pedersen's son and daughter-in-law at Fuglem, matching the couple who baptised children as Bersvend Olsen Fuglum and Anne Olsdatter from 1761. ¶2a

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed. His probate, Skifteprotokoll 7 (24758) fol. 141a, reads 'Anno 1773 d. 17de Aprilis blev Skifte forretted paa gaarden Fugelum i Selboe efter... afg. Enkemand Ole Pedersen' and lists his heirs as '1 Sønnen Peder Olsen gift og boende paa gaarden [...], 2 Sønnen Bersvend Olsen gift og boende paa gaarden [...], 3 Sønnen Ole Olsen boende paa gaarden Over-[Bolem?], 4 Datteren Ingebor Olsdatter gift [...] under gaarden [...]', and a fifth child, a daughter who had died leaving a daughter Karen [Sivers?]datter (farm words partly illegible). This is the probate the earlier note asked for: it names Bersvend Olsen (see his page) as his son and heir, matching the 1762 extra tax and his burial of 28 Mar 1773. ¶6

Corrections 1

Correction: birth widened to 1689-1696. The 1701 manntall lists 'Olle', aged 12, son of Peder Pedersen (55) at Fuglum (canvas 112), which points to about 1689; his 1773 burial says 77, about 1696. Brother: Bersvend Pedersen, 15 in 1701 - which explains his son's name Bersvend. ¶4

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Would confirm: Ole Pedersen Fuglem's burial (age) or a probate after him naming Bersvend as heir. His wife had died before 1762; her name is not known. ¶2b
