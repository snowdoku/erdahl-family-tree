About the family

Her son Jon Mikkelsen Moen named daughters Brynild (1789) and a son Peder, which fits a mother Brynild Pedersdatter (naming pattern, inference). ¶3a

Pedigree collapse: this draft makes Jon Mikkelsen Moen (see his page) and Beret (Birthe) Mikkelsdatter (see her page) brother and sister, both children of Mikkel Siursen and Brynild Pedersdatter of Velve 791. Both are already direct ancestors of Palmer Eidem: Jon > Gunder Johnsen Eidem > Beret Gundersdatter > Gunder > Oline > Palmer, and Beret > Dordi Jonsdatter Stokke > Ole Jonsen > Kari > Oline > Palmer. So Gunder Johnsen Eidem and Dordi Jonsdatter were first cousins, Oline's parents Gunder and Kari were third cousins, and Mikkel and Brynild stand twice in Palmer's tree as his 4x-great-grandparents. This rests on two Probable links, not on a record that names the relationship; if either link fails, the collapse disappears. ¶4

Life story

Churching: about Michaelmas 1745 as 'Michel Wel[?]stads Qv[inde] Brynill Pedersdatter', after the baptism of her son Jon on 5 September 1745 (Selbu klokkerbok 16315, canvases 77 and 79). In the baptism entry the name Brynill Pedersdatter also stands at the end of the sponsors as 'madame', the woman who carried the child; the churching makes clear she was the mother. ¶2

1762 extra tax list: wife of Mikkel Siursen at Velve 791, Selbu, with six children over 12: Siur, Peder, Osmund[?], Jon, Ingebor and Brit. ¶1

Burial, second Christmas Day (26 Dec) 1785: 'Begravnen E. Brynild Pedersd. Welve, død d. 18[?] Dec: 72 aar gl.' - the widow Brynild Pedersdatter of Velve, died about 18 Dec 1785 aged 72, so born about 1713 (16311 canvas 120 left). Widow of Mikkel Sivertsen Velve (d. 1767). Two of her sons died in 1780: Siur Michelsen Velve (17 Feb, aged 41) and Peder Michelsen Velve (27 Aug, aged 38½) (16311 c101, c103). ¶5

Her own probate, Skifteprotokoll 9-3 (24762) fol. 521b: 'Anno 1786 den 20de Februarii' at the farm Welve in Selbu, registration and division after the deceased widow 'Brynille Pedersdatter' among her children and grandchildren: 1. the son Siver Michelsen, dead, leaving six children (Michel Siversen 18, Ole Siversen 13, Peder Siversen 10, [Jon?] Siversen 8, Ingeborg Siversdatter 16, Brynille Siversdatter 4); 2. the son Svend Michelsen, of age, living on Welve; 3. the son Jon Michelsen, of age, living on the farm Moen; 4. the daughter Berithe Michelsdatter, married to Jon Haldorsen Welve; 5. the daughter Ingeborg Michelsdatter, 38; 6. the daughter [Brithe?] Michelsdatter, 3[?]; 7. the daughter Anne Michelsdatter, 30. Guardian of the unmarried daughters Ingeborg, Beritte and Anne: their brother 'Jon Michelsen Moen'. ¶8

The 1786 probate names no brother, sister or other relative of hers, so it does not identify her parents. Her father, a Peder, is still unknown. The guardians Ole Pedersen Fu[glem?] and Baroe Pedersen Hegset from the 1767 probate remain the only leads; neither is called her brother in either record. ¶9

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed. The probate after her husband Michel Sivertsen at Velve, 21 May 1767 (Skifteprotokoll 6-1, 24756, fol. 65b), names 'hans efterlevende Kone Brönild Peders Datter' and eight children 'med hende', including John Michelsen and Berit Michelsdatter, aged 18. These are the direct ancestors Jon Mikkelsen Moen and Beret Mikkelsdatter Velve. ¶6
