Life story

Born 10 April 1895 at Valderhaug (date from the 1910 census) and baptized at Borgund on 3 June 1895. ¶1

In the 1910 census, aged 15, his occupation is already 'Fisker' (fisherman) and he was recorded as away from the farm on census day - the ordinary pattern on Valderøy, where boys went to sea with the Ålesund fishing fleet as soon as they were confirmed. ¶2

He is one of the 'four brothers in Norway' - Elias, Ole, John and Martin - named as survivors in his brother Peter's 1967 obituary, so he stayed on Sunnmøre. ¶3
