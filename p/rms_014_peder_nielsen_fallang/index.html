About the family

Father (Speculative): Niels Guldbrandsen, sergeant, of Fallang (godfather 1709-1721) is the only Niels at Fallang in the Gran index and is of the right generation. His first son was named Niels (1737), as custom expected. No baptism of Peder has been found; it probably predates 1707, where the Gran index begins. ¶6

Life story

Godparent: Peder Nielsøn of Fallang was godfather at Gran on 18 Sep 1729 (Gran church book 1707-1731, p. 255b), so he was an adult by 1729. ¶5

Scan read: Gran church book 1732-1744 (DA 9340), canvas 162, p. 169b. 'Torsdagen den 4de April blef troelovet ved Gran Peder Neilsen Fallanger og Kirsti Pedersdatter, forlovere Jens Klæstad og Asten Houg. Copulerede d. 9de July ved Gran' [1737]. The scan gives no residence or father for the bride and no father for the groom; it adds nothing to the index (pv00000001433850). Same page: Peder Nielsen Fallanger is forlover (marriage surety) with Ole Fallanger for Dragoon Ole Mathisen and Marthe Olsdatter (betrothed 6 May 1737); c168 (15 Sep 1739) 'Ole og Peder Fallanger' are forlovere for Thore Povelsen Lyne and Kari Andersdatter Gamkind. ¶8

Lived at Fallang in Gran parish, Hadeland, where children of his and his wife Kirsti Pedersdatter were baptised at Gran: Nielss on 29 Sep 1737, Peder on 31 May 1739 and Ole on 5 Mar 1741 (Gran church book 1732-1744, index by GDS-Hadeland; the 1737 and 1741 entries were also read on the scan). ¶1

Godparents to his children included Ole Fallang (Fallanger), Christopher Skattum, Hans Pedersen and Peder of Gamkind, Ole and Anne Hansdatter of Knarud, Joen Jørgensen Undelie, Kari Gautved and Gorroe Olsdatter Fallanger. Hans Pedersen Gamkind may be a brother of Peder or of Kirsti (inferred from the patronymic only). ¶2

Marriage: 'Peder Nielsen Fallanger' and Kirsti Pedersdatter were betrothed at Gran on Thursday 4 Apr 1737, sureties (forlovere) Jens Klæstad and Aasten Houg, and married at Gran on 9 Jul 1737. The entry names no parent of either party and no farm for the bride (Gran church book 1732-1744, p. 169b, read on the scan). ¶7

Deed read (pantebok 8, DA 10272, fol. 596a-596b, No. 6, registered at the court of 18 Nov 1767; register DA 266260 p. 10 lists it under Fallang gnr. 184-185, 1767, skjøte 2 hd 1 skinn). Heading: 'Peder Pedersen Fallanger med fleeres Skiøde til Niels Pedersen Fallanger'. Peder Pedersen Fallanger, Anders Gulbrandsen [Torgerstuen?] and [Hans?] Gulbrandsen [Øvre...] acknowledge payment from Niels Pedersen Fallanger for the inheritance that fell to them 'efter Skiftebrevs formeld af Dato 21de Martii 1767... udi gaarden øvre Fallanger, som Arv efter Peder Nielsen[s] F[allanger, last word uncertain]'. Peder Pedersen sells his own arvelod; Anders Gulbrandsen acts for 'mine Myndlinger' (wards) Gulbrand Pedersen and Ole Pedersen for their 'Broderlod'; [Hans] Gulbrandsen acts for his wife Marte Pedersdatter Fallanger for her share; the shares total 504 rd 13 s and Niels takes over 2 skippund in Fallang. So Peder Nielsen of Fallang died before the skifte of 21 Mar 1767 (no Hadeland probate protocol survives for 1757-82, so this deed is the only witness to it). Heirs named: sons Peder, Gulbrand and Ole Pedersen (Gulbrand and Ole still minors in 1767, so perhaps from a later marriage, or a second Ole), daughter Marte Pedersdatter. Niels Pedersen, the buyer, is not called 'Broder' in the parts read, so the deed does not by itself name his relationship; it shows him buying out Peder Nielsen's other heirs, among them Peder Pedersen (b. 1739, the son baptised with Nielss in 1737). Link to Nils stays Probable (graded on his link to Nils Pedersen). ¶9a

(near-miss attempt, not raised; graded on his link to Nils Pedersen, unchanged). The skiftebrev of 21 Mar 1767 cannot be read on Digitalarkivet. The Hadeland, Land og Valdres sorenskriveri probate protocols listed there are no. 7 (1743-1756) and then no. 8 (1783-1802); there is no protocol for 1757-1782, which matches the gap in the Hadeland card index (DA 39947) noted. The surviving 1767 deed (pantebok 8, DA 10272 fol. 596a-b) calls the minors Gulbrand and Ole Pedersen's share their 'Broderlod', but it gives Niels no relationship word. Remaining tests: the Hadeland district court journal no. 65, 1764-1767 (scan-only, Hadeland, Land og Valdres sorenskriveri), around the spring 1767 sessions, for a mention of the skifte; and the 1801 or later probates of Niels's siblings Gulbrand, Ole or Marte Pedersdatter naming 'Broderen Niels Pedersen Fallang'. ¶11

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the grade · 2 alternatives set aside · 1 open question

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Probable as the father of Nils Pedersen of Fallang (b. 1737): see the note on Nils. His own birth, parents and death have not been searched. ¶3

Alternatives considered and set aside 2

Namesake: not the same man as Peder Nilsen (b. about 1764), who is his grandson — the son of his son Nils Pedersen; the grandson's name follows the Norwegian custom of naming the eldest son after the paternal grandfather. ¶4

Near-miss attempt, not raised: the rest of the 1767 deed read (pantebok 8, DA 10272, fol. 596b, cached scan, left page). After the shares (Marte Pedersdatter's husband 60 rd 5 s; total 504 rd 13 s), the sellers declare that 'Niels Pedersen Fallanger' shall be the rightful owner of øvre Fallang, 2 skippund 1 skinn, warrant it for him, his wife and heirs, and sign 'Fallanger den 12te Novemb. 1767': Peder Pedersen Fallanger, Anders Gulbrandsen Torgersrud, Hans Gulbrandsen [Øien?], witnesses Erich Jensen [Hegrebøl?] and Povel Erichsen [..]. No 'Broder', 'Søn' or other relationship word for Niels is legible anywhere in the deed. Grade unchanged (graded on his link to Nils Pedersen). Remaining test: the skiftebrev of 21 Mar 1767, if it survives among loose papers. ¶10

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Test record: the rest of fol. 596b (the end of this deed and its signatures) or the 1767 skifte brev itself, if it survives among loose papers. ¶9b
