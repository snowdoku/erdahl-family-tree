Life story

Her marriage to Peder Erichsen found. Stjørdal ministerialbok 16370 (1740-1756), betrothals 1753, IIIF canvas 235 (0-based), left page: 'd. 17 Marty ved Wernes trolofvet Enkemand [Per] Erichsen Baar med Pigen Mali Ols[datter] Vold', sureties '[Per] Jonsen Skervauran' and 'Andreas [Olsen?] Skervauran'; margin 'Copul. ved Wernes d. 5 Junii' [1753]. The groom's first name is under an ink blot, but 'Enkemand... Erichsen Baar' fits only Peder Erichsen, widowed when Mali Andersdatter died (skifte 3 Jul 1752, 24753 fol. 449b). So she was an unmarried girl living at Vold (Skatval/Værnes) in March 1753, and married on 5 Jun 1753; her first child Ole followed in Jul 1756. The record does not name her father. This adds a marriage date only; her graded link (to Peder Pedersen, Peder Pedersen) stays Confirmed on the 1790 skifte. ¶5

Mother added as Gundbor (Probable), the mother named at the Skatval baptisms of Ola Johnsen Vold's children Olaf (6 Dec 1716) and Berit (14 Dec 1721) (16368 c33 R, c90 R). Mali's own baptism (about 1723) is still unread; see the mother's Test record. ¶7

The skifte (Stjør- og Verdal skifteprotokoll 10, 24763 p. 222 bottom - 223, scan): 'Anno 1790 den 1te Julii blev paa Selveiergaarden Baard i Schatvolds Annex og Nordahlens [?] Tinglag' registration and valuation held 'efter den samme Tid ved Døden afgangne Mand Peder Erichsen' between 'den efterlevende Enke Mali Olsdatter' on the one side and 'den Afdødes... avlede og efterladte Børn' on the other, namely 'af 1te Egteskab: 1. Sønnen Anders Pedersen fuldmyndig, 2. Sønne-datteren Mali Olsdatter 8 aar gl.' and 'af 2det Egteskab med den igienlevende Enke: 3. Sønnen Ole Pedersen, fuldmyndig og boende paa Gaarden, 4. Sønnen Peder Pedersen 21 aar gl., 5. Datteren Mali Pedersdatter gift med Ole Christophersen Wal[stad]trøen, 6. Datteren Marithe Pedersdatter [age not clearly read]'. The widow's lagverge was Christian Gravold; Jon Pedersen Schiervold was appointed curator and guardian for the son Peder Pedersen and the granddaughter Mali Olsdatter, and the brother Ole Pedersen Baard guardian for Marithe Pedersdatter (the same guardians who paid them out in the June 1792 quitclaims, pantebok C-3 fol. 614b). Valuers Otter Wibstad and Ole Christophersen Winge. In the debts on p. 223, items 12-14 are shares 'tilfalden udi Arv efter Faderen Peder Erichsen' (to the sons Anders and Ole), showing the settlement was continued after a later death (the session of 13 Mar 1791, after the widow's burial of 8 Feb 1791, not read). ¶1

She is the Mali Olsdatter of Baar buried 8 Feb 1791 aged 67 (b. c.1724; Stjørdal burial index). Her second-marriage children with Peder Erichsen, from the skifte and the Skatval baptisms: Ole (bapt. 25 Jul 1756; gaardmand of Baar, d. 1806), Mali (bapt. 16 Jul 1758; m. Ole Christophersen 1786), Ingbor (bapt. 1 Feb 1761; not in the 1790 skifte, so probably dead), Marit (bapt. 27 Mar 1766), Peder (bapt. 16 Oct 1768). Her parents and the marriage date are not yet known. She is not Mali Olsdatter (same name and birth year, but that woman died 16 Mar 1794 at Store Evjen, Selbu, while this one was buried from Baar, Skatval, 8 Feb 1791). ¶3

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Graded on her link to her son Peder Pedersen (see his page): Confirmed. The skifte of 1 Jul 1790 at Baar names her as the surviving widow of Peder Erichsen and lists 'af 2det Egteskab med den igienlevende Enke' the son Peder Pedersen, 21 years old. Continuity to the man in the tree: Peder, son of 'Peder Baar', baptised 16 Oct 1768 at Skatval -> Peder Pedersen, 21, heir at Baar 1790 under guardian Jon Pedersen Schiervold -> 'Peder Pedersen Baard' paid out by the same guardians in June 1792 (pantebok C-3 fol. 614b) -> the soldier of Baar who married Gullov Pedersdatter in 1798 -> buried 1806 aged 37 with the farm Baar. Same farm throughout, gaps of 2-6 years, and only one householder at Baar in 1762, so no rival Peder Pedersen. ¶2

Searches that found nothing 1

Negatives for her father, probate by farm Vold: (1) 24753 fol. 779b (view 810), skifte 23 May 1758 at Wold i Wærnes Sogn after Lisbet Larsdatter, wife of Ole Erichsen: her only heir is the daughter Anne Olsdatter (aged about 9); Ole Erichsen bought his part of Vold at auction in Oct 1739. (2) 24755 fol. 552b (view 575), skifte 6 May 1766 at Wold after Ole Erichsen: widow Sirie Pedersdatter; children Ole Olsen (of age), Lucia Olsdatter, and Anne Olsdatter, married to Jon Pedersen Hegre. No Mali, so she was not Ole Erichsen's daughter. (3) 24755 fol. 182b (view 199), skifte 26 May 1761 after Ole Olsen of Ytre Vold: widow Marit Larsdatter; children Berit (married to Hans Pedersen Wigen), Lars Olsen 22 and Sara Olsdatter 4½; guardian Ole Pedersen Schiervold. No Mali. Untested leads: the surety Anders Olsen Skjervauran may be a brother; the 1753 residence at Vold may have been as a servant. ¶6a
