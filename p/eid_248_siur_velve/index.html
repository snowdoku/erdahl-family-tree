About the family

Father of Mikkel (Michel) Siursen Velve: 'ditto Siur Welfuer[?] barn Michel, fadd: Ingebrict Lien, Haldo Welfuer, Ole ibid, Marit Efjen, Guru Welfuer' - Misericordia Sunday (about 3 May) 1711, and 'Siur Welfuet[?]s qv. Ingebor efter 3die barn Michel' churched at Trinity 1711 (16310 canvas 43, baptism crop checked by the orchestrator). Earlier child: Birit, baptised 25th Sunday after Trinity 1709, Ingebor churched after her 2nd child at Christmas 1709 (c38-39). ¶1

Father: probably Michel Kieldsen of Velve (Mikkel (Michel) Kjeldsen Velve), who died before 26 May 1693. His insolvent estate was settled at Velve that day (24749 fol. 117), and the farm passed to the man then living there, apparently Mogens Jonsen. In 1701 the boy Siver Michelsen was a servant in Mogens's house (Probable: a match on name, farm, age and naming pattern, with no record naming the father). ¶8

Life story

1701 manntall, Velve (canvas 120): a widow's household with sons including Haldor (20) and a son read as 'Siur' (18, so born about 1683) - the godfather 'Haldo Welfuer' of 1711 fits his brother Haldor; a second Velve head (26) was as 'Øvind Olsen' (possibly Siur). His patronymic is therefore unknown (possibly Arnesen, if the widow's sons share 'Lars Arnesen's' patronymic - unproved). ¶3

Probate (skifte) after him, 18 Jun 1742: 'Velve, Selbu. 18/6-1742: Siver Michelsen Velve. Enke: Ingebor Svendsdatter. Barn: Michel Siversen, 31 år gl.; Kirsti Siversdtr., gift m/ Niels Halvorsen, bor på gården; Beret Siversdtr., 23 år - verge: fetteren, Peder Andersen Vigen; Mali Siversdtr., 20 år - broren, Michel Siversen.' Jordegods in Velve passed to Michel Siversen. (card 77804 c2131; read by the orchestrator). This gives his patronymic Mikkelsen (Michelsen) - his father was a Mikkel - and his wife's patronymic Svendsdatter, and names 'Michel Siversen, 31 år' (born 1711) as his son and heir to the farm. ¶5a

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Why Probable: the farm is written 'Welfuer/Welfuet/Welsøe' in this decade - the old spelling Welfue = Velve, the godparents come from the Mebonden farms around Velve, and Mikkel later named a son Siur (Siur Mikkelsen Velve, active 1772-1778); Mikkel is the only Michel born to a Siur at Velve in the read years. ¶2a

Records that disagree 1

Against: the farm spelling never appears as plain 'Velve' in 1709-1712, so the identification of the farm rests on the spelling argument. ¶2b

Corrections 3

Correction: raised from Probable to Confirmed. Mikkel's own burial (26 Apr 1767) calls him 'Michel Siverts[en] Velve', aged 56 - born 1711 - which matches 'Siur Welfuer barn Michel' of May 1711 in name, patronymic, farm and year (16311 c21). This settles that 'Welfuer/Welsøe' is Velve. ¶4

Correction: name set to Siur (Siver) Mikkelsen Velve. Kirsti (married Niels Halvorsen) is the Kirsti baptised 1713; the guardian 'fetteren Peder Andersen Vigen' fits the son of Anders Vigen and Helge Welffer (married 1703), so Helge of Velve was probably his sister. ¶5b

Correction: the 1701 manntall entry was misread. Re-read at full resolution (38461 c120), the Velve widow's household is 'Lars Arnesen 26, Jon (reserve soldier) 22, Haldor 20, Øvind, Arne 18'. It has no Siur, so these sons are Arnesens, not his brothers. The only Siur at Velve in 1701 is 'Siver Michels.', aged 12, a servant in the household of the farmer Mogens Jonsen (57). That fits his patronymic Michelsen (1742 probate) and makes him born about 1689, so his birth estimate moves from 'before 1686' to about 1689. He would then have been about 20 when his daughter Birit was born in 1709. ¶7

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Biography gaps: his exact birth date (before 1686) predates the 1699 Selbu register, so his baptism is not in any surviving church book. His burial is not in any record read; the 16315 burial entries for 1739-42 (canvases 0-94) are the next check. His marriage to Ingebor is not identified in the drafts, so the 16310 marriage entries from 1699 to 1709 are the next check. ¶6
