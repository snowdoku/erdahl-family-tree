About the family

Mother of Wilhelmina Farofsky, 1838: she is named as the mother of Wilhelmina Farofsky, born at Beresina on 7 Jan 1838, daughter of Johann (FHL 1882648/2 p.72 no.3). Her husband Johann Farowsky (see his page) married again in 1851. ¶2

Father (Speculative): the 1835 marriage names her father as Michael, deceased. Michael Bom died at Beresina on 2 Oct 1822 aged 60 and is the only Michael Bom in the 1815-32 death extracts; he is entered as her father (Michael Bomm). Christoph Bomm (b. 1802/03), 'son of the late Michael' at his 1837 marriage, and Martin Bom (b. 1805), Christoph's brother, both from Milisky, Poland, are probably her brothers (inferred; no record links her to them directly). ¶8

Life story

Birth (estimated): about 1796. She was 38 at her marriage to Johann Farowsky on 1 Jan 1835 (and 38 in the Kulm entry of 31 Dec 1834), which gives 1796-97. The Beresina 1834 census and the Wahl index give 1794, and her birthplace as Miliski, Poland (census household 84, 'Schmolke, Rosina 1794, Miliski, Pol., Widow, Born Bom'). The 1850 death extract gives no age (the '49' there is the entry number). Inferred from the stated ages. ¶4

Earlier marriages: she was the wife of Samuel Schmolke (see his page) by 1826. The births of Johann Samuel (1 Jun 1826), Anna Maria (27 May 1829) and Louise Schmolke (13 Dec 1831) at Beresina name the parents as Samuel Schmolke and Rosine née Bomm. Samuel Schmolke married a widow 'Gierke, Rosina' at Beresina on 24 Jul 1825, the only marriage of a Samuel Schmolke in the 1815-32 extracts, so she was probably already widowed once, from a husband Gierke or Jerke (inferred: the 1834 census lists 'Jerke, Karolina, 1823, daughter of Rosina' in her household, and Caroline Jerke was born at Beresina on 8 Feb 1823 to a mother 'Bum, Rosina', film 1198701-2 no.27; the father's column of that extract is garbled). Samuel died at Beresina on 6 Jul 1831 aged 29, before Louise's birth, so she was a widow from 1831; the 1834 census and the Kulm marriage entry both call her a widow. ¶5

Marriage to Johann Farowsky: as Rosina Schmolke née Bomm, widow, aged 38, daughter of the late Michael, she married Johann Farofski at Tarutino on 1 Jan 1835 (FHL 1882645/2 p.1 no.131). Their daughter Wilhelmina was born at Beresina on 7 Jan 1838. ¶6

Death, the register image (read 1 Nov 2026 by the signed-in lane,; S0171/S0172): the St Petersburg Consistory duplicate for Beresina (Klöstitz parish), deaths of December 1850 entered in the 1851 volume (vol. 828-14/63, stamped p.307, entry 49; FamilySearch coll. 1469151, image 299 of 678, index 1:1:VT91-16W) reads: 'Rosina Farowsky geb. Bomm, Ehefr[au] des hies[igen Colonisten] Johann Farowsky' (Rosina Farowsky née Bomm, wife of the local colonist Johann Farowsky), died 13 December 1850, family-book page 83; birthplace 'in Polen' (in Poland); age '56 Jahr 10 Monat' and some days; married (verheirathet); buried at Beresina by the local pastor. The earlier note that the record gives no age is superseded: '49' is the entry number, and the age column reads 56 years 10 months. This gives a birth about February 1794 (Julian), agreeing with the 1834 Beresina census (born 1794, Miliski, Poland) and two to three years earlier than the age of 38 stated at her marriage on 1 Jan 1835 (which implied 1796-97). FamilySearch indexes the entry as 'Rosina Bomm Farowsky', age 56, birth year 1794, birthplace Polen. ¶9

Second marriage, register details (read 1 Nov 2026,; S0170/S0174), partial: the Klöstitz retrospective list of 1835 marriages (Consistory duplicate vol. 828-14/32 p.158, image 156; index 1:1:Q242-X96P) gives, as no. 1, 1 January: 'Farowsky, Johann, mit Schmolke, Rosina', names only. The Tarutino 1835 register (vol. 828-14/10, image 25, fol. 121 right page, entry 1) has a marriage of 1 January in the Tarutino prayer house of a single man aged 23 (or 25) and a widow aged 38, which by date and the bride's age is probably theirs (inference: the names page is not in the filmed sequence). Her maiden name Bomm and her father's name are not on any page read. ¶10

Origin lead (1 Nov 2026): the birthplace written 'Miliski' (Rosina) and 'Milisky' (her brothers Christoph and Martin) in the 1834 Beresina census recurs, spelled differently, for other settlers of Kulm, Beresina and Borodino in the St Petersburg Consistory death extracts (odessa3, Drefs): Daniel Albrecht, d. Kulm 7 Oct 1836 aged 78, b. 'Milask, Poland' (FHL 1882646/2 p.172 no.97); Louisa Schulz, wife of Christoph, d. Beresina 9 Mar 1837 aged 44, b. 'Miliske, Poland' (FHL 1882647/3 p.118 no.46); Eva Margaretha Knodel, d. Borodino 15 Oct 1841 aged 37, b. 'Mileski/Poland' (FHL 1883192/2 p.172 no.19). So 'Miliski' was a real home village shared by several Kulm-group families, not a clerk's slip. ¶11

Origin lead, district: her brother Christoph's 1837 marriage gives his birthplace as 'Lenschuetz', and 'Lenschütz' / 'near Lenschütz, Poland' (Łęczyca, German Lentschütz) is one of the commonest origins in the same death and marriage extracts (Blum, Engel, Graessle, Skalei, Strohschein, Markus, Jesse, Lenz, Kletke). The family therefore probably came from the old Łęczyca district west of Łódź, not from the Chełmno (Kulm) area of West Prussia. A village of that district whose name fits every spelling is Mileszki (now part of Łódź). This identification is a hypothesis, not checked in a gazetteer. Geneteka (łódzkie) shows a Bom family nearby in Brzeziny parish: Anna Marianna Bom, baptised 1799, and Anna Róża Bom, baptised 1801 at Lisowice Olędry, daughter of Andrzej Bom and Anna Doak. They are not linked to her. ¶12

Death (extract): Rosina, wife of Johann, née Bomm, died at Beresina on 13 Dec 1850; the birthplace column reads 'Poland'. The extract prints her married surname as 'Jaronsky', a variant reading of Farowsky (FHL 1883181/1 p.307 no.49; 49 is the register entry number). The extract gives no age, so her birth year is unknown; she was of child-bearing age in 1838. ¶1

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Speculative link: she is entered as the mother of Wilhelmine (see her page), wife of Ludwig Knodel, on the same footing as her husband Johann: the 1838 Beresina baptism names her daughter Wilhelmina, the only Wilhelmina Farowsky birth found in 1830-59. It is not Probable because a second Wilhelmina Farowsky was a married mother at Beresina in 1858 and either woman could be the 1838 child, and because the 1838 birth makes the bride 22 rather than the 21 stated in 1860. ¶3a

Link to Wilhelmine (see her page) upgraded from Speculative to Probable (Oct 2026), together with her husband's: see the argument on Wilhelmine's record. The 1838 baptism names her as Wilhelmina's mother explicitly; the Probable level reflects only the identification of that child with the 1860 bride. ¶7
