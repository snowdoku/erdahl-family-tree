About the family

His brother was Ole Rasmussen of Voldsnes (Vollsnes), whose probate was registered in 1741. In it, 'his brother's son Ole Jørgensen Erdahl' and 'his brother's daughter Brite Jørgensdatter' inherited, with her brother and guardian Rasmus Jørgensen Erdahl. This confirms Jørgen as the brother of Ole Rasmussen. ¶3

Life story

Aaland 1932: Aaland's Erdal history (bruk nr. 3): 'S. Jørgen, f. ikr. 1681, † 1736, sk. 1715 p. br.; sk. 1718 p. odr. J. tente ei tid p. Røyros. * Brite Lassed. Hj.': born about 1681, died 1736, deed to the holding in 1715 and to the odal right in 1718; he served for a time at Røros (the mining town). Birth and death agree with the 1701 census and the 1736 probate. Aaland names only one wife, Brite Lassesdatter of Hjelle; see the note on NN Larsdatter for the conflict over her farm. ¶6

1701 census (manntall), Erdal: Jørgen, aged 20, son of the occupier Rasmus Olsen (50), with brother Olle (23). ¶1

Named among the heirs in the probate after his father Rasmus Olsen Erdal, 30 May 1714: 'Jørgen Rasmussen, of age'. ¶2

Guardian (as father) in 1723 'for his three sons' Rasmus, Lars and Ole. That suggests the death of his first wife, a daughter of Lars Rasmussen. By a later marriage he had a daughter, Brite Jørgensdatter (aged 4½ in 1736, with land in Grønfur), described as the half-sister of Rasmus. In 1727-32 he was also guardian of Rasmus Monsen and Marrite Monsdatter of Grønfur, possibly stepchildren. ¶4

Marriages: his first wife, the mother of Rasmus, Lars and Ole, was a daughter of Lars Grønuur (Grønfur), and she died before 10 Oct 1723 (guardianship no. 1 fol. 7v, the probate of 10 Oct 1723). The 'Brite Lassesdatter, Erdal' of the 1731-34 probate index is therefore probably a second wife, perhaps the mother of the half-sister Brite Jørgensdatter. That is an inference: her probate text (A 7 fol. 511) is illegible. ¶10

Aaland 1932: Aaland's Grønfor history says Sjur Olsen Hjelle 'selde 1729 halve br. til Jørg. R. Erdal' (sold half of Grønfor bruk nr. 1 to Jørgen Rasmussen Erdal in 1729). That explains why his daughter Brite Jørgensdatter held land in Grønfur in 1736. The same history lists Mons Andersen of Grønfor (deed 1720), 'married Brite Rasmusdatter of Erdal', whose widow remarried by 1735. She is probably the married Brite Rasmusdatter among Rasmus Olsen Erdal's heirs in 1714. That makes it likely that Rasmus Monsen and Marrite Monsdatter of Grønfur, Jørgen's wards in 1727-32, were his sister's children rather than his stepchildren (an inference; the guardianship entries do not state the kinship). ¶7

Aaland 1932: Aaland's Voldsnes history lists 'Ola Rasmusson Erd., br. 1730. * e. Anne': Ole Rasmussen from Erdal became tenant of Voldsnes in 1730 by marrying the previous holder's widow Anne. This agrees with the 1741 Voldsnes probate, in which Jørgen's children were Ole Rasmussen's heirs. ¶8

Guardianship: Nordfjord guardianship protocol no. 1, fol. 7v, says of Jørgen 'Samme Mand døde 1735' (the same man died 1735; the last digit is Probable). After that his children's guardianship passed to Rasmus Rasmussen Erdahl and Rasmus Folven, 'i anden Bog paa fol. 15' (in the second book, fol. 15). Book 2 fol. 15 (scan image 17): 'Rasmus Rasmussen Erdale den Curator for Rasmus Jørgensen Erdahl, hvis fulde Arvemidler nu er efter Skiftebrev af dato 2den Junij 1736 Capital 81 rd 5 mk 1 sk' (Rasmus Rasmussen Erdal, curator for Rasmus Jørgensen Erdahl, whose full inheritance per the probate of 2 June 1736 is a capital of 81-5-1). Part of it was 1 pund 5 [?] of land in the farm Erdal. ¶9

Probate (arvelatar) registered in 1736 (skifteprotokoll A 8). A guardianship session of 2 Jun 1736 lists his minor children; Ole was then 12. ¶5
