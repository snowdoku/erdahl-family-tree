Life story

Michael Boyle, merchant of London, third son of Roger Boyle of Hereford. He married Jane, daughter and coheir of William Peacock, mercer of London, 'by whom he left eleven sons and two daughters'. They were William; Richard, Archbishop of Tuam (died 1645); Michael, Bishop of Waterford and Lismore (died 1635); Sir George; Leonard; Henry of London; Thomas; James; Joshua; Roger; Samuel; Blanch (wife of Richard Williamson of London); and Jane (wife of Thomas Caldwell of Newbury, Berkshire) (Lodge, Peerage of Ireland, 1789, vol. 1, p. 144). Cotton's Fasti also makes 'Michael Boyle, a merchant of London' the eldest son of Roger Boyle of Herefordshire, and the father of Archbishop Richard and Bishop Michael. Lodge says all the children were born in London. His younger brother Roger was the father of Richard, 1st Earl of Cork, so the two bishops were the Earl's first cousins. The birth year is estimated from his sons' births in the 1570s-80s. Added. ¶1

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the grade · 1 on the identification · 2 searches with no result · 2 open questions

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Probable, graded on his link to his daughter Jane Boyle. Lodge's printed peerage, a compiled source, names her as his daughter and the wife of Thomas Caldwell. Brady independently calls Mildred's mother 'Jane, youngest dau. of Michael Boyle'. The 1733 Blennerhassett Pedigree independently makes Mildred 'cousin german to Primate Boyle', which is exactly right if Jane was a sister of the Primate's father. No primary record naming the relationship was found. ¶2a

The case for this identification 1

Test record read: the will of James Boyle of London, mercer, 'nowe resident at Middelburgh in Zealand' (TNA PROB 11/117/199, register image read). Grade unchanged (Probable, on his link to Jane Boyle). The will, dated at Middelburg 23 Dec 1608, names NO parent and NO sister, so it says nothing about Jane Caldwell. It names 'my lovinge children Michaell Boyle and Katherine Boyle', 500 li. each; 'my lovinge wief Mrs [Cicely ?] Boyle', 1,200 li.; 'Samuell Boyle my man', 10 li.; his godson Jacob van [Densberge ?]; Mary Ailmer; his [300 ?] li. bond in the Orphans' Court to Edmond Burton; and Mr Giles Flotterbooke [?], his partner in trade, as overseer. His son Michael is executor. Administration with the will was granted on 23 Feb 1610/11 to the overseer during the minority of 'Michaelis Boyle filii naturalis et legitimi'. A son named Michael, and a Samuel Boyle in his service, would fit Lodge's sons of Michael the merchant (James, Samuel), but that is a naming pattern only. Remaining test records: Michael's own will in the London church courts (LMA), or a Mercers' Company record of James's apprenticeship naming his father. ¶5

Searches that found nothing 2

Searched without result for a primary record: no Boyle pedigree in the London visitations of 1568 and 1633-35, and no PCC will of Michael Boyle in the TNA catalogue (it may be in the Commissary Court of London). The PCC has the will of 'James Boyle, Mercer of London', proved 23 February 1611 (PROB 11/117/199). Lodge names a James among Michael's sons, so this will may name the testator's father, mother and sisters, Jane Caldwell included. The Lismore Papers (1st series, vol. 2, notes p. 419) repeat Brady's statement that Dean Eveleigh's wife Mildred was the daughter of Thomas Caldwell and 'Jane, youngest daughter of Michael Boyle'. The Earl's diary also calls Henry Boyle the scrivener 'my cozen' (Lodge's 'Henry of London', son of Michael). Grade unchanged. ¶3a

FamilySearch: no baptism of a child of Michael Boyle in London 1560-1600 is indexed (England Births and Christenings; the London Boyle entries are children of John Boyle at Clerkenwell 1584 and St Giles Cripplegate 1580, and two unparented Boyll baptisms at St Olave Old Jewry, 1588 and 1591). No Boyle x Peacock marriage is indexed. The City registers for the 1570s-80s are on Ancestry (London Metropolitan Archives), not on FamilySearch indexes. Added (FamilySearch, signed in, 2 Nov 2026). ¶4
