About the family

Parents: the 1735 probate after Ole Olsen Hilde (A 8, image 12) names his 'Ældste Datter Giertrud Olsdatter', then unmarried, and his son Anthonius (Anton) Olsen Hilde. Father linked as Probable, mother (Aaland's Gunnhild Andersdatter Skåden) as Speculative; see their notes. Her own grade (as mother of Guttorm Andersen) is unchanged. Note: the index row 'Gjertrud Olsdtr, Hilde, protocol 15a p. 377 (1766-70)' may be her own probate, which would contradict Aaland's death in 1791. ¶4

Life story

Guardianship protocol no. 2 (1736-1744), p. 267: as 'Giertrud Olsdatter Hilde, som arvede den 26 Maij 1735' (from her father Ole Olsen Hilde's probate), her 13 rd were paid on 18 Jul 1742 to 'Anders Guttormsen Hilde, som denne pige har ægtet'. So she married Anders Guttormsen before 18 Jul 1742, and the record bridges her father's 1735 probate to her husband. ¶6

Probate of her husband: Nordfjord skifteprotokoll A 13, fol. 387-389, October 1760. She is the widow 'Giertrud Olsdatter' of Anders Guttormsen Hilde and mother of all five children named, including Guttorm, 24. Aaland's 'Jartrud' is the same name. Confidence raised from Speculative to Confirmed as Guttorm Andersen's mother, because the probate states the children are 'with her'. ¶3

Aaland 1932: Aaland's Hilde farm history (Del 2 3 2, p. 33, bruk nr. 4) names 'Jartrud Olsd. Hilde, † 84 a. g. 1791' as the wife of Anders Guttormson. She died in 1791 aged 84, so she was born about 1707. After Anders died in 1760 she married Ola Andersson ('* e. Jartrud', e. = enkja, widow), who held the bruk from 1761 and died about 1763. Jartrud is a form of Gjertrud. ¶1

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Speculative as the mother of Guttorm Andersen (see his page, born about 1739): she is the only wife Aaland gives Anders Guttormson, and her age fits. But Anders farmed from 1715, about 24 years before Guttorm's birth, so an earlier wife is possible, and Aaland does not name the children. Guttorm's Innvik baptism (about 1739), if the register survives, or a probate after Anders Guttormsen (1760) would settle it. ¶2

Records that disagree 1

Correction of a noted conflict: the index row 'Gjertrud Olsdtr, Hilde, protocol 15a p. 377 (1766-70)' is NOT her death probate. It is a lifetime division (A 15a fol. 376b-377a, Hilde, Stryn skipreide, 2 May 1769) held 'efter den velagte Qvinde og Enke Gierdrud Olsdatters forlangende' (at the request of the widow Giertrud Olsdatter herself) between her and her four children: 'Guttorm Andersen fuldmyndig og boende paa gaarden Hilde', Hans Andersen now 23, Karie Andersdatter married to Peder [Diub?], and Gunilde Andersdatter 20. So she was alive in 1769, consistent with Aaland's death in 1791 (aged 84). The record again names Guttorm Andersen of Hilde (see his page) as her child, adding a second primary record for her Confirmed link to him; it does not name her parents. The 1769 division was presumably made after the death of her second husband Ole Andersen (probate A 14 fol. 251b-252a, Hilde, 1762-66; scan very faded, read only in part: it names the widow Giertrud and Guttorm, Ole, Hans, Kari and Gunilde Andersen). ¶5
