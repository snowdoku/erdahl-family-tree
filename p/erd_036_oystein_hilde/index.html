Life story

Aaland 1932: Aaland names Ole Østensen Tjellaug as 'Ola Øysteinson Hilde': his father was called Øystein (Østen) and the family was of Hilde, on the Innvik side of the fjord. Aaland's Kjellog history gives no more about the father. ¶1

Guardianship register 109814, image 61 left: Østen Hilde accounts as guardian for 'Inger Rasmusdatter Dahle(?)' (inheritances of 5 Oct 1736 and May 1737, 25 rd, raised 11 Jul 1741; she died 1750 and her means were divided among her surviving brother and 2 sisters) and for Rasmus Lassesen Flo, who inherited 72 rd 1 ort 8 s at the division of 9 Jun 1738. A further entry 'efter Skiftebrev af dato 29 Maij 1742' lists 52 rd 10 s each for his own three sons, with Rasmus Østensen [Hilde] as curator, and a note on the right says Østen died in 1741 [reading uncertain; conflicts with the 1742 probate date, so perhaps the year of his wife's death] when his sons were already of age. No kinship word links Østen to Rasmus Lassesen Flo or to Inger Rasmusdatter, so this does not show that Østen was a brother of Lasse Rasmussen Flo; that stays a lead only. ¶10

Probate 1742: On 29 May 1742 Østen Rasmussen of Hilde held probate after his late wife ('hans Sal: Qvinde') Ane Olsdatter. The heirs were his three sons: Rasmus Østensen, aged 23, Ole Østensen, aged 21, and Arne Østensen, aged 19. The sons were past the age of minority (lavalder), so their inheritance stayed in their father's keeping 'under Faderen Østen Hildes Curation og Tilsyn' (under the curatorship and supervision of their father, Østen Hilde). Read from the scan (A 8, 1742, image 104); the amounts are partly unclear. ¶4

Guardianship protocol: Østen Hilde's account in the Nordfjord guardianship register (overformynderi) no. 3, p. 59, cites the same probate deed (skiftebrev) of 29 May 1742, by which he held 52 rd 10 s each 'for sine egne tre Sønner, alene som Curator' (for his own three sons, only as curator): Rasmus, Ole and Arne Østensen. He also held money as guardian for Inger Rasmusdatter Dahle (from 1736) and for Rasmus Lassesen Floe (from 1738). A later note says 'Samme Mand døde 174[4 or 7]' (the same man died in 174[4 or 7]; the last digit is unclear). Because his three sons were then all of age (selvmyndige), no probate was held after him, and his eldest son Rasmus Østensen Hilde took over the ward's money. ¶5

Birth: not settled. Aaland gives about 1683, but no Øystein or Østen appears at Hilde in the 1701 census. The only 1701 Østen Rasmussen in Nordfjord/Sunnfjord is a boy of 8 (born about 1693), stepson of a servant at 'Niestad'. He is an unproved candidate. Sons born about 1719-1723 fit either birth year. ¶7

Deed of 16 Feb 1747: pantebok 12498 fol. 94, deed no. 7383, dated at Hilde. 'Arne Østensen Hilde', together with his brother 'Ole Østensen', settles with their eldest brother ('vores ældste Broder') 'Rasmus Østensen' over the share that the three brothers inherited 'efter vores afg. Sal. Fader Østen Rasmussen Hilde', with reference to the skiftebrev of [2]9 May 1742. Østen was therefore dead by 16 Feb 1747, which narrows the guardianship register's 'døde 174[4 or 7]'. The deed is a second primary record naming Ole Østensen as his son (already Confirmed from the 1742 probate). ¶9

Probate 1753 (eldest son): the probate after his son Rasmus Østensen of Hilde (begun 25 May 1753) names Rasmus's widow Ane Absolonsdatter and sons Østen (about 9), Absolon (about 5) and Rasmus (15 weeks). Ole Østensen is not named in it. ¶8

Placeholder, Speculative: known only from Ole's patronymic and Aaland's 'Hilde'. Ole named his eldest son Østen (1757), which fits. A guardian in Ole's 1780 probate, 'Arne[?] Østensen', may be Ole's brother. The Hilde farm history in Aaland's Innvik volume (Del 2 3 2, pp. 26-39) should list an Øystein among the holders or their sons around 1700-1730; also search the 1701 census for Hilde. ¶2

Corrections 1

Correction: confidence moved Speculative to Confirmed, and the name changed from the placeholder 'Øystein (Østen) Hilde' to Østen (Øystein) Rasmussen Hilde. The 1742 probate after his wife names Ole Østensen, aged 21, as his son. This fits Ole Østensen Tjellaug (burial age 58 in 1780, born about 1722; Aaland calls him 'Ola Øysteinson Hilde'). His brother Arne Østensen is the 'Arne Østensen' who was guardian of Ole's eldest son at Ole's 1780 probate. His eldest son Rasmus Østensen Hilde succeeded on Hilde bruk nr. 8, which Aaland assigns to Øystein Rasmusson. ¶6

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Leads, not proof: (1) Aaland's Innvik volume, Hilde bruk nr. 8 'Øysteinbruket', lists 'Øystein Rasmusson, f. ikr. 1683, † f. 1750, br. f. 1710' (born about 1683, died before 1750, tenant before 1710), married 'Anne Olsd., † ikr. 1741'; and an 'Øystein Hilde' bought part of bruk nr. 9 in 1720. Ole Østensen is not listed among his children there, but Ole's first daughter was Anna (1755) and first son Østen (1757), which fits Øystein Rasmussen and Anne Olsdatter as Ole's parents. (2) 'Østen Hilde' was the legal guardian (lagverge) of Lasse Rasmussen Flo's widow at his probate in 173[1-3] (Nordfjord L0007 fol. 151). To settle: Øystein Rasmussen Hilde's probate (before 1750), or a 1730s-40s Innvik record naming Ole Østensen of Hilde. ¶3
