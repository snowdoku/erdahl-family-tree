Life story

1664 vicar's census (p. 210): 'Halff gaard Grønfürd' had three farmers, Jon Rasmussøn 50, Lucas [?] Olsøn 60 and Jon Pedersøn 51, and no sons or servants. No Lars and no Rasmus farmed Grønfur in 1664. ¶4

Aaland 1932: Aaland's Grønfor (Grønfur) farm history (bruk nr. 1-3) names no Lars among the holders around 1700-1727: Sjur Olsen Hjelle held nr. 1 (deed 1706, died 1740), Mons Andersen nr. 2 (deed 1720) and Nils Knutsen nr. 3 (from 1708). Aaland therefore does not identify 'Lars Grønuur' or name his parents. A bygdebok's silence is weak evidence: Lars may have been a retired man (kårmann), a cottager (husmann), or a holder Aaland missed. His probate of 3 July 1727 (inheritance book fol. 494) remains the record that would identify him. ¶3

Maternal grandfather (morfar) of Rasmus Jørgensen Erdal, who inherited from him (guardianship protocol, 1727). His residence and farm are not stated in the index. ¶1

Guardianship text read (Nordfjord overformynderiprotokoll no. 1, fol. 7v): 'Nok arvede disse Sønner efter deris Morfader Lars Grønuur den 3 July 1727' (inheritance book fol. 494), meaning 'these sons further inherited after their maternal grandfather Lars Grønuur on 3 July 1727'. The clerk names him by farm, not by patronymic; the patronymic Rasmussen comes only from the index. The farm is probably Grønfur (Grønfor) in Oppstryn: in the same entry Jørgen Erdal is also guardian of Rasmus Monsen Grønfur, whose inheritance comes from a probate of the same date (fol. 495), probably as another grandson. Farm Probable; his death is placed before 3 July 1727. The Digitalarkivet index of this entry (pa00000001187387) gives 'Lars Rasmussen', but that patronymic is not on the page and may be an indexer's error; treat it as unverified until the 3 July 1727 probate (inheritance book fol. 494, in the 1715-1727 gap) is found. ¶2

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 correction

Corrections 1

Correction: an earlier note took Nordfjord skifteprotokoll 24223 views 486-488 to be the 3 July 1727 probate, Arvebogen fol. 494, that should identify Lars Grønuur. Reading those images shows they are fol. 492-493 of 24223, and they hold the end of a Sande estate (heirs Simen's children, with guardians), then a probate at Ytre Hauge in Stryn dated 6 May 1731 after Else Monsdatter, widower Erich Pedersen. No Grønfur estate is there. Folio 494 of 24223 therefore falls in 1731, not 1727, and the guardianship protocol's 'Arvebogen fol. 494' must be the separate, unscanned inheritance book (Arvebok). Lars Grønuur's probate is still unfound. ¶5
