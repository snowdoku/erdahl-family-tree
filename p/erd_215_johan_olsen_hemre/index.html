Life story

New father of Gunder Johansen Hemre (see his page), graded on his link to Gunder: Probable (compiled secondary source only). Hegra gårds- og slektshistorie B. 1, Hembre Øvre (canvas 183 from word boxes): 'Johan Olsen, [f.] 1659, gravlagt 4/4 1717. Var frå Hembre Nedre.... brukar [frå] 1705. Gift m. Gjertrud. Born: Gunnar, brukar. Mali, [f.] 1693, g. m. Henrik... Skatval. John, g. m. Randi.' The next brukar is 'Gunnar Johansen Hembre, [f.] 1688, gravlagt 20/5 1760', whose daughter is 'Gjertrud, f. 16/1 1721, g. m. David Andersen' (Frøyum, Stjørdal) - i.e. our Gunder, whom the 1744 Frøyum probate calls 'Morfaderen Gunder Johans. Hamre'. Dates are ABT because the text is rebuilt from word boxes. Consistent with the land records: Gunder Johansen already occupied Øvre Hemre when he bought it from Hans Hagerup in Dec 1737 (pantebok A-1 fol. 242b). Primary records that would test it: a 1701 manntall for Stjørdal fogderi (not found among Digitalarkivet's 1701 volumes 38450-38466; no. 13 = 38460 is Orkdal/Gauldal); Johan Olsen's probate after 1717 (falls in the 1714-1727 gap in the Stjør- og Verdal skifteprotokoller; the 77804 card index has a single Hemre card, 1733, not his); Gunder's own baptism c.1688 (Stjørdal church books begin 1714). ¶1

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the identification · 1 search with no result · 1 open question

The case for this identification 1

Supporting evidence (not an upgrade): the bygdebok's daughter of Johan Olsen, 'Mali, f. 1693, g. m. Henrik... Skatval', is in the primary record: Stjørdal ministerialbok 16368 canvas 52, Trinity Sunday (5 Jun) 1718, 'Copul. Henrick Olsen Hollan[?] med Mali Johansd. Hemra'. Five weeks later 'Gunder Johansen Hemra' married (canvas 53, 13 Jul 1718). So two Johansen/Johansdatter children were leaving Hemre in 1718, the year after Johan Olsen's burial (4 Apr 1717), as the bygdebok says. His link to Gunder remains Probable (patronymic plus residence; no record names the relationship). ¶2a

Searches that found nothing 1

Negative: the typed register row 'Anders Johansen, Hemre øvre, Hegra, 1765, fol. 504' (Stjør- og Verdal skifteprotokoll 5, 24755 view 525 right, read) is the probate of 24 Jul 1765 after the boy Anders Johansen, 8 years old, son of the late Johan Gundersen Over-Hembre. His heirs are his siblings: Gunder Johansen 11, Ole Johansen, Berit Johansdatter (m. Joen Olsen), Ingebor Johansdatter and a fifth sister. It is a grandson's estate and names no one of Johan Olsen's generation. ¶3

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Test record: a probate after Johan Olsen (1717-18) - falls in the 1714-1727 gap of the Stjør- og Verdal skifteprotokoller; a tingbok (Stjørdal) odel/åsete case for Hemre about 1717-20 naming Gunder as his son. ¶2b
