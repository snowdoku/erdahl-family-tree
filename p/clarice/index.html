Life story

Obituary index: a notice for 'Clarice Louise Erdahl', birth about 1927, relatives Clara and William, was published in Bellingham, Washington on 29 Mar 1985, eight days after her death in Portland. The newspaper itself has not been read. U.S., Newspapers.com Obituary Index. ¶5

1930 census (index only, image not read): 'Claice L Larson', daughter, born about 1927 in North Dakota, in the household of Albert M and Clara Larson, West Bank Township, Williams County, North Dakota. Age 3 at the April 1930 enumeration fits her birth on 19 Dec 1926; 'Claice' is read as a misspelling of Clarice, to be checked on the image (S0115). ¶3

Marriage: the Whatcom County Auditor's marriage index lists William E Erdahl and Clarice L Larsen, married 1 Jun 1945 and filed 4 Jun 1945 (document nwwtcmcv38_246). This places the marriage in Whatcom County, Washington, and agrees with 'Married 6-1-45' on their shared headstone. ¶2

1950 census: 'Erdahl, Clarice L', wife, white, female, 23, married, born North Dakota, in the household of her husband William E. Erdahl, 26, born Montana, in Whatcom County, Washington (ED 37-21, sheet 12), with their two sons. Transcribed from the sheet image. ¶4
