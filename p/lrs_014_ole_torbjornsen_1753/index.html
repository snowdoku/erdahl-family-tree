About the family

Children baptised as children of Ole Torbiørnssøn and Sigrid Johannesdatter of Ytre Takle (Gulen church books, index): Johanna 21 Oct 1784; Torbiørn 25 Jan 1786; Martha 9 Jul 1788; Iver 20 Aug 1790; Giertrud 8 Sep 1793; Anne 27 Dec 1796; Torbiørn 27 Apr 1800. The 1801 census has a Torbiørn aged 18 although the second Torbiørn was baptised in 1800; whether the first Torbiørn died is unchecked. ¶2

Children in the 1824 division: 2 sons and 4 daughters. Named on the pages read: Iver Olsen, Marthe Olsdatter (wife of Iver Rasmussen Botten), Giertrud Olsdatter, 31, and Anna Olsdatter, all of Ytre Takle except Marthe. ¶8

Father (Confirmed): the probate after Torbjørn Larsen of Ytre Takle, 25 Aug 1785, names 'Ældste Søn Ole Torbiørnsen Yttre Tackle, myndig og tilstede' (the eldest son, Ole Torbjørnsen of Ytre Takle, of age and present), who was also guardian of his sister Maritha, 22. His brother was Lars Torbjørnsen. The widow Giertrud Olsdatter is his mother: the probate calls the heirs 'deres sammen-avlede 2de Sønner og een Datter' (their jointly begotten two sons and one daughter), as verified by audit. Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll A 10a, fol. 135a, no. 105. ¶9

Life story

Marriage: Engaged (trolovet) 1 Jun 1784 at Brekke church: 'Ole Torbiørnssøn og Sigrid Johannes D[atte]r yst-Takle. For Ægteskabets Lovlighed bleve ansvarlige [answerable for the lawfulness of the marriage, i.e. forlovere]: Mons Pedersøn indst-Takle og Ole Baarsøn[?] Mit-Takle.' Married at Eivindvik church on Dom. VII p. Trin. 1784, which is 25 Jul 1784 (a later pencil note also says 25/7). He was a bachelor (karl) and she unmarried (pige); no ages or parents are given. Gulen ministerialbok 1776-1787, folios 87 and 89b. The wedding is missing from the separate marriage list of the Gulen book 1779-1788, which has only the engagement. ¶4

1801 census: Ole Torbiørnssen, 48, head of household (huus fader), married for the first time, skattebonde (tax-paying farmer) at Ytre Takle (Takle Yst), Brekke, with wife Sigrid Johannesdatter, 43, their joint children, and his wife's mother Anna Iversdatter, 73, a widow of her second marriage, on a farm pension (kaar). Birth about 1753 is inferred from his age. ¶1

Death: 'Ole Torbjørnsøn', kaaremand (retired farmer on a life-lease) of Yttretakle, aged 73 1/4 years, died 16 May and was buried 20 May 1831 (Gulen ministerialbok 1826-1836, p. 228, no. 35; index pg00000002037846). Name and farm match the 1801 census householder of Ytre Takle. The burial age implies birth about early 1758, against about 1753 from his 1801 age of 48; the birth estimate is left at ABT 1753 until his baptism is found. ¶10

Corrections 1

Correction: The marriage date moves from 'BEF 21 OCT 1784' (inferred from the first child's baptism) to 25 Jul 1784, from the wedding entry. Their daughter Johanna, baptised 21 Oct 1784, was conceived before the engagement. ¶5

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Status on 15 Jun 1824 unresolved: an 'Ole Torbjørnsen ytre Tackle' appeared and signed at the probate session of 15 Jun 1824 at Larsendahl, Lavik skipreide (registration and appraisal 17 May 1824). But the heading may describe an estate after death, and the Ole who signed could be a namesake (e.g. a grandson). No death date is set until his burial or the opening session is found. ¶7
