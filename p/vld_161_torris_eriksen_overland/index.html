Life story

Bygdebok support: Gjemnes bind 10, canvases 423-424: Tørris Erikson, brukar about 1644-1683, born about 1605. His first wife's name is unknown; their children were Halvor (b. about 1645), Sivert (b. about 1647, brukar 3) and Berit. His second wife was Berit Pedersdotter, d. 1669, daughter of Anne Knutsdotter. Grade unchanged (Probable). ¶6

Farmer (opsidder) at Overland (Offuerland), Gjemnes, Kvernes parish, Nordmøre, in 1664, 1666 and 1669. ¶1

1664: Vicar's census 1664-66 no. 28 (Nordmøre deanery), Kvernes, p. 131, Offuerland: 'Tørris', opsidder, b. 1604; 'Halffuor Tørrisen', b. 1644, soldier (knegt); also on the farm Johan (b. 1622) and Siffuer (b. 1617), other holders. ¶2

1666: the same census, Kvernes, p. 167, Offuerland: 'Thorris', opsidder, b. 1605, with 'Siffuer Thorriss.' b. 1656 and 'Peder Thorriss.' b. 1665 as 'Sønner' (sons); then Johan (b. 1623) with sons Peder and Joen Johanssen. ¶3

1669: Nordmøre probate index card (source 77795, view 4625), 'Øverland, Gjemnes. 1669 2/5 Berit Pedersdotter. Enkemann: Tørris Eriksen. Barn: Peder Tørrisen, Kari Tørrisdotter', with the children's maternal grandmother Anne Knutsdotter present. This gives his patronymic Eriksen. Siffuer is not among Berit's heirs, so he was a son of an earlier wife, whose name is not known. The 1666 son Peder (b. 1665) is Berit's son Peder. ¶4

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the grade

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Graded on his link to his son Siffuer (Sivert, Siur) Toresen Overland: Probable. The 1666 census names 'Siffuer Thorrissen' as Tørris's son at Overland, and in 1701 'Siffuer Toresen' held Overland, with a son Tøris (b. 1689) named after him. Same farm and patronymic, but 35 years apart with nothing read in between, and the two ages disagree (b. 1656 in 1666, b. 1647 in 1701), so Probable, not Confirmed. A second Siffuer (b. 1617) held part of Overland in 1664; he is not this man, whose patronymic is Toresen. ¶5
