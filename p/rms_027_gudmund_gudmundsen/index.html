Life story

Speculative parent: he is the father of Elen Karine Gudmundsdatter (b. 1836, Nedstrand), who is a candidate, not a proved identification, for Ellen K. Nelson, wife of Peter S. Nelson. The link from him to Ellen K. Nelson stands or falls with that identification; see her notes for the argument and the records that would settle it. ¶1

Baptism of his daughter, 1836: Gudmund Gudmundsen and his wife Johanne Wilhelmsdatter, inderster (lodgers) at Ellingsnæs under Lekjeletnæs, Nedstrand parish, had their daughter Elen Karine baptised on 7 Aug 1836 (born 28 Jul 1836). ¶2

Confirmation of his daughter, 1850: Elen Karine's 1850 confirmation entry in Nedstrand names her parents Gudmund Gudmundsen and Johanne Wilhelmsdatter as huusfolk (cottagers) at Valland[?]. ¶3

Emigration of his daughter, 1859: Elen Karine Gudmundsdatter, 23, was entered on the 13 May 1859 Ryfylke passport of the Hegland family of Sørbø, Rennesøy, bound for North America. Nothing found shows that he emigrated himself. ¶4

How sure are we?

Speculative. A plausible candidate only. Treat this link as a question, not a fact.

1 open question

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Lead, unproved: a Gudmund Gudmundsen Hodne (25) moved from Rennesøy to Stavanger in July 1859 with his wife and children, and a Gudmund Gudmundsen (b. about 1849), lodger at Sørbø, Rennesøy, had a daughter Elen Karine baptised in 1875 with Gudmundsdatter godmothers. Either could be a son of this couple; neither has been checked. ¶5
