About the family

Husband: Johannes Larsen of Indre Oppedal (see his page), alive at this probate (he was buried 10 Dec 1717). Her patronymic suggests a father Torkel (Torkild), and Mons Johansen's youngest son was named Torkild (b. about 1719), which fits naming after her (inference). ¶3

Life story

Probate, Ytre Sogn 2b fol. 370a (right page of view 64): 'Inder Oppedall udj Ladvig Schibr. Den 26 May 1709' the court met 'at skifte efter Sal. Anna Torchels datter, imellem hendis efterlevende Mand Johanes Lars[en] og hans Børn og Arf[vinger]: Gutorm Oppedahl, Sal. Anders Erdals[?] Børn Lars, Niels og Sigtrud[?], samt Brite Anders[?]..., Mons Oppedahl og Marj Jon Iversens[?], alle giftte...'. Estate 49 rd 4 ort 9 s; 'Mons Oppedahl' also appears among the creditors. Read from the scan (crops). Several words of the heirs' line are uncertain; the heirs appear to be given partly through sons-in-law (Gutorm Oppedal; the children of the late Anders Erdal[?]; Mari, wife of Jon Iversen[?]) and the son Mons Oppedal. ¶1

Heirs' line re-read at higher zoom (crops, a2.jpg): 'imellem hendis efterlevende Mand Johanes Lars: og hans Børn og Arf: Gutorm Oppedahl, Sal: Peder Opedals Børn Lars, Niels og Sigri[?], samt Brite, Peders Sønner og Døttre, Mons Oppedahl og Marj Jon Iversens[?], alle giftte og deris giftemaal[?]'. 'hans Børn' (his children) is clear; the word is not 'deris'. ¶4a

Bygdebok (secondary, rebuild, canvas 71): 'Anna Torkelsdtr., som døde 1709 67 år', so born about 1642 (ABT; from rebuilt word boxes); the children listed are born 1662-1681, all within her child-bearing years. Grade kept at Probable (graded on her link to Mons Johansen): her motherhood rests on the inheritance rule (a wife's estate passes to her own heirs, and the heirs are listed as children) and on the bygdebok; the record itself says 'hans Børn', and no marriage record dates the start of the marriage before Guttorm's birth (about 1662), so an earlier wife of Johannes cannot be excluded from the record (audit-5 rule 1). ¶5

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the grade · 1 record that disagrees · 1 correction

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Graded on her link to her son Mons Johansen: Probable, not Confirmed. For: the probate names 'Mons Oppedahl' among the heirs of Anna Torkelsdatter, wife of Johannes Larsen of Indre Oppedal, and the 1701 census lists Mons Johansen, 22, as Johannes Larsen's son on Indre Oppedal; a wife's estate passed to her own heirs, so an heir listed as a child should be her child (inference from the inheritance rule). ¶2a

Records that disagree 1

Against: the scribe wrote 'hans Børn' (his children), not 'deris Børn' (their children), so the record itself does not state that Anna was the children's mother; and no other record names her as Mons's mother. Identity of 'Mons Oppedahl' with Mons Johansen: the only Mons at Indre Oppedal in 1701 besides a servant boy Mons Iversen, 10, who was not Johannes's child. ¶2b

Corrections 1

Correction: the earlier reading 'Sal. Anders Erdal[?]s Børn Lars, Niels og Sigtrud[?]' should be 'Sal: Peder Opedals Børn Lars, Niels og Sigri[?], samt Brite, Peders Sønner og Døttre' (the late Peder of Oppedal's children). The heirs are thus four children of Johannes: Guttorm, the late Peder (through his children Lars, Niels, Sigri and Brite), Mons, and Mari wife of Jon Iversen. This matches the Brekke bygdebok (canvases 71-72), which gives Johannes Larsson and Anna Torkelsdtr. six children: Guttorm b. 1662, Mari b. 1664 (m. Jon), Peder b. 1667 d. 1705 (children Lars, Nils, Siri, Brita), Lars b. 1670 (d. childless 1700-01, his probate naming his father as heir), Mons b. 1678, and Erik b. 1681 d. 1701 unmarried; exactly the surviving lines in 1709. ¶4b
