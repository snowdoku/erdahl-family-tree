Life story

Probate 1750: widow of Anders Andersen Tjugum at the probate held 7-9 December 1750 at Tjugum (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll A 7a, fol. 34a, No. 90). The patronymic Clausdatter is clear. The given name is not: it is a flourished capital followed by about four small letters, read as 'Karie' (Kari), with 'Brite' also possible on the card; the auditor could not settle it on the binarised scan, so the name field gives the patronymic only. The probate calls the heirs 'deres sammenavlede Børn' (their joint children), so she was the mother of all of them, including the daughter read 'Rangdi' (Randi) Andersdatter. A son named Claus, and a grandson Claus (son of the eldest son Ole), fit a mother named Clausdatter. Randi Andersdatter's own eldest daughter was named Kari (1810 probate), which would fit 'Kari' but proves nothing. ¶1

Given name settled: the division in the probate after Anders Andersen Tjugum, 7-9 Dec 1750 (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll A 7a, fol. 34b, view 35), heads the widow's allotment 'Udlæg Enken Brite Clausdatter Tiugum for hendes Hovedlod og Halve Boe 149 rd' (allotted to the widow Brite Clausdatter Tjugum for her main share and half the estate). The name is written in large display script and reads clearly as Brite; the earlier reading 'Karie' (fol. 34a) is superseded. Her parents are still unnamed in this probate. ¶3

Probate (overnight sweep F): her own probate is indexed in the Ytre Sogn probate register (Brite Clausdtr, Tjugum, 1763-1766, protocol 8a p. 258) and was read on the scan: 'Anno 1765 d. 24 April' at the farm Tjugum in Tjugum skipreide, registration and division 'efter... Brite Clausdatter afg[angne] Anders Andersens Enke' (after Brite Clausdatter, the late Anders Andersen's widow), between her children. Heirs read: sons Hans Andersen Tjugum, Anders Andersen Berdall (of age, present) and Mons Andersen Haugland, and 'Datteren Rande Anders-datter gift med Christen S[?]rn, Manden tilstede' (the daughter Randi Andersdatter, married to Christen [surname partly illegible, probably Sværen], the husband present). The preamble also names Claus Olsen Nesse, Else Olsdatter and Mons Olsen, 18; whether they are children of an earlier marriage of hers (Claus would bear her father's name) or grandchildren through a dead child is not clear at this reading. Appraisers Ole Pedersen Wangsnes and Jacob Larsen Wangsnes. Gross estate 18 rd 4 [ort]. She died before 24 Apr 1765. ¶4

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Probable as the mother of Randi Andersdatter Tjugum, for the same reason her husband is Probable as Randi's father: the daughter in the 1750 probate is read 'Rangdi' (Randi), aged 2[3?], but no record yet joins her to the Randi who married Christen Persen Sværen in 1752. ¶2

Upgrade: graded on her link to Randi Andersdatter Tjugum. Her own probate of 24 Apr 1765 (Ytre Sogn 8a fol. 258a, No. 12) names 'Datteren Rande Andersdatter', married to a Christen whose husband was present. Continuity with the tree's Randi: the 1750 probate on the same farm Tjugum names Brite as Anders Andersen's widow and Randi ('Rangdi'), 23, as their daughter; in 1752 'Randi Andersd. Tiugum' married Christen Pedersen Sværen (Leikanger 1735-1756, fol. 129-130); in 1765 the widow's probate on Tjugum again has her daughter Randi Andersdatter, now the wife of a Christen; and Randi's 1810 probate at Sværen names her as Christen Pedersen Sværen's widow. Same farm, same names, records 2, 13 and 45 years apart, and no rival Randi Andersdatter of Tjugum is known. The husband's byname is not fully legible (it is read as 'S..rn' / 'H..rn'), which is the only soft point; the given names, patronymic and farm agree throughout. ¶5
