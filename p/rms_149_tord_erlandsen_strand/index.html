Life story

Vicar's census 1664 for Aurdal (DA 35504, p. 8, view 13, left page), farm 'Sør-Strand, iiij huder 4 skind ij meler korn': first holder (opsidder) 'Tord Erlandß. 60 aar, bruger j hud viij skind ij meler korn'; in the sons column (Sønner) 'Juel Tordß. [age blotted by an ink spot] aar' and 'Olle Tordß. 1[2?] aar'. The other holders on Sør-Strand are a widow (Enche) [Rønnaug?] and [Hoffuerk?] Eivindß. 80. This is the first primary record found for this family: it names Juel Tordsen as a son of Tord Erlandsen on Søndre Strand in 1664. Tord's age, 60, fits the bygdebok's 'Tord, b. about 1604, d. 1689' (Valdres bygdebok I D, Svenes, gnr. 65 Strand, canvas 310), which also gives his wife as an Arnesdotter from Tveit (forename not recovered; not added). His patronymic gives his own father's name, Erland (not added as a person). ¶1

Second, independent 1664 list: the bailiff's census list a) of 12 May 1664 for Aurdal (DA 35563, p. 52, view 36, left page) has 'Tord Sørstrand' with, in the sons column, 'Ju[e]l og Ole Thorsønner' (Jul and Ole, Tord's sons; the first name is cramped above the line), followed by 'Rønnaug ibid' (the widow Rønnaug also on Sør-Strand in the vicar's list). So both 1664 lists name Jul as Tord's son on Søndre Strand. ¶2

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the grade · 1 on the identification · 2 open questions

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Graded on his link to his son Jul Tordsen (see his page): Probable. The 1664 census names the relationship (son column, patronymic, same farm), but the tree places Jul through his daughter Marit Juulsdatter (b. about 1688, m. 1715 Even Jonsen of Hovrud), and no record between 1664 and about 1688 has been read that shows the 1664 Juel Tordsen still at Strand. That is a gap of about 24 years, beyond the 15-19 year zone, so the identification of the census son with the tree's Jul rests on the farm, the rare forename and the bygdebok. No rival Jul Tordsen is known in Aurdal. Death about 1689 is from the bygdebok rebuild (ABT). ¶3

The case for this identification 1

Test record (1) read: the bailiff's census list b) of 12 Nov 1664 for Aurdal (DA 35563, p. 210, view 113, left page), farm 'Sørstrand': 'Thore Erlandsen 60 aar', bruger 1 hud 9 skind; then the widow (Enchen) Rønnaug and [Hoffuer?] Eivindsen 80; in the sons column (Sønner) 'Juel Tordsen = 21 aar' and 'Olle Tordsen = 12 aar'. So all three 1664 lists (vicar 5 Nov, bailiff a) 12 May, bailiff b) 12 Nov) name Jul as Tord's son on Søndre Strand, and Jul's age, blotted in the vicar's list, is 21 (born about 1643). Grade unchanged (Probable): the step from the 1664 son to the tree's Jul, father of Marit Juulsdatter (b. about 1688), is still about 24 years with no record between; a 45-year-old father in 1688 is possible but adds nothing. Remaining test records as in the note: a probate after Tord (about 1689) or Jul at Strand, or a 1680s tingbok entry naming father and son. ¶5

Open questions and the next record to read 2

Test record: (1) the bailiff's census 1664-66 for Hadeland og Valdres (DA 35563, Aurdal section, Sør-Strand), which should give Juel's age (blotted in the vicar's list) and confirm the household; (2) a probate after Tord Erlandsen (about 1689) or Jul Tordsen (about 1690) at Strand in the Hadeland, Land og Valdres skifteprotokoll covering 1689-90, or the Valdres card index DA 39948 under Strand i Svenes, naming Jul as Tord's son or Marit as Jul's daughter; (3) the Hadeland, Land og Valdres tingbøker of the 1680s for a deed or odel case at Strand naming father and son. ¶4

Test record (2) searched, negative so far. The Valdres card index (DA 39948) files this farm as 'Sør-Strand i Svenes i nordre Aurdal' at cards 13721-13739 (read in full: 1661, 1701, 1707, 1722, 1731, 1753, 1810, 1880s) and as 'Strand, søndre' at 13055 (1786). There is no card for a probate after Tord Erlandsen (about 1689) or Jul Tordsen. The protocol series has no volume for about 1667-1697 among the cards seen (1659-1666, then 1698-1703), so a probate of about 1689-90 is probably lost. Context: card 13721 (1659-1666 protocol s. 64b, 24 Mar 1661) is the probate after Effuind Olsen of Sør-Strand, 'g.1 Astri Erichsd. +, g.2 Rønnoff Nielsdtr.', one daughter Tharand; this Rønnaug Nielsdatter is the widow 'Rønnaug' who sat beside Tord on Sør-Strand in the 1664 lists. ¶6
