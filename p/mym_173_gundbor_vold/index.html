About the family

Wife of Ola (Oluf) Johnsen of Vold, Skatval, and mother of his children. 3 Advent [14 Dec] 1721, Skatval (Pastor Værnæs), 'døbt Oluf Joens. Volds b. Birrit. Mod. Gundbor. fad. Carl Bergnæsset, Peder Otters. Svee, Mali Hollum, Marrit Om[?]vold, Kari Boleium' (canvas 90 R). This is the Berit 'døpt 14.12.1721' of the bygdebok's list of Ola Johnsen's children. On Dom. 2 Advent [6 Dec] 1716, Skatval, 'Døbt Olaf Volds b. Olaf. Mod: [G]undbor[?]. Faddr: Otter Svee, Joen Hollen, Peder Bolden[?], Gurru Vold, Gunnild Svee' (canvas 33 R; the first letter of the mother's name is unclear) is the bygdebok's 'Ola døpt 06.12.1716'. The same day 'Halvar Ols. Volds b. Olaf, Mod. Gunnild' was baptised: a different father, so not a rival. ¶1

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the grade · 1 open question

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Graded Probable on her link to Mali Olsdatter (see her page), who is in the tree as Ola Johnsen's daughter. The relationship is inferred: Gundbor is named as the mother of Ola's children in Dec 1716 and Dec 1721, Mali was born about Feb 1723 - Feb 1724 (burial age 67 in Feb 1791), and the bygdebok gives Ola Johnsen only one wife ('[...] Olsdtr., f. 1682'). Mali's own baptism has not been read, and Aug-Dec 1723 (16368 c112-115) has no Vold baptism of a Mali, so the window is not closed. Birth ABT 1682 and the patronymic Olsdatter are the bygdebok's, not added to the name. ¶2
