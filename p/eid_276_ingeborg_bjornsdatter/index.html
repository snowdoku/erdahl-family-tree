About the family

Widow of Povel Svendsen Berge at his probate, 29 Jul 1727 ('Enke: Ingeborg Biørnsdatter'), with their son Ole Poulsen, 6½ (card 77804 c145). Mother of Ole Paulsen Berge. Her father was a Bjørn; not identified. ¶1

Biography gaps: her exact birth date, baptism (about 1700-02) and confirmation are not found, and her father, a Bjorn, is unidentified; the 1699-1701 baptisms in 16310 (c3-9) are not recorded as searched for her. Her marriage to Povel Svendsen Berge (about 1716-20) is not identified after a full read of 16310 c56-102, so the next record is a targeted re-read of the 1717-20 marriages (c64-80) or a 77804 card search for a Bjorn's probate naming a daughter Ingeborg. Her death, burial and any probate of her own are not recorded and no search is noted; she was still a widow at the July 1727 Berge probate (card 77804 c145). ¶2
