About the family

HIS PARENTS ARE NOW PROVED, and they are not John P. Eidem and Oline Slind. His own Social Security application (NUMIDENT Application SS-5 Files 1936-2007, SSN XXX-XX-0085, cycle date 1945) reads: "INGVALD EIDEM, date of birth 26 January 1916, place of birth DEUEL COUNTY, SOUTH DAKOTA. FATHER: MIKAL EIDEM. MOTHER: BERTHA SLIND." ¶15

Parentage FOUND. Selbygbogen (Minneapolis, 1921) p. 150 lists the children of Mikal P. Eidem and Beret Eliasdatter Slind and the tenth and last is 'INGVALD, f. 26 Jan. 1916'. That is this man's recorded birth date to the day, and he was the youngest of the family. He had been left unattached and Speculative because the Minnesota death index's 'mother: SLIND' could not distinguish between the three Slind wives in the colony; the printed family list settles it. His confidence moves from Speculative to Confirmed and he is attached to Mikal and Beret. ¶20

Life story

Minnesota death index: born 26 January 1916, 'child of SLIND (mother)', died 8 January 1995 in Hennepin County. ¶1

Military service and burial, from the federal record: INGVALD EIDEM, Technician Fifth Grade (T5), UNITED STATES ARMY, war period WORLD WAR II, b. 26 Jan 1916, d. 8 Jan 1995, buried FORT SNELLING NATIONAL CEMETERY, Minneapolis, SECTION Y, SITE 1484. US Department of Veterans Affairs, Nationwide Gravesite Locator. His wife June is in the same grave and is recorded there as 'WIFE OF EIDEM, INGVALD'. ¶4

1940 Alien Registration: not present, as expected for a man born in South Dakota. ¶8

That makes his parentage genuinely uncertain. He could still be the eighth child of John P. Eidem and Oline Slind, born during a spell across the state line - Marietta is three miles from the South Dakota border and the family's burial ground is at Zoar Lutheran in Deuel County - but the seven children of that couple whose registrations survive were all born in Mehurin Township, Minnesota, and the only other documented Eidem-Slind couple, Mikal Eidem and Berit Slind, were living in Antelope Valley Township, Deuel County, South Dakota in exactly this period and have a son Ludwig born out of state in 1907 and a daughter Christina Paulina born in Deuel County in 1912. He fits either family. Nothing retrieved so far names his parents. ¶10

Military service: Technician Fifth Grade (T5) in the United States Army during the Second World War, enlisted 26 April 1941 and released 6 July 1945 — more than four years. Buried at Fort Snelling National Cemetery with his wife. ¶2

Social Security application (SS-5), an original application signed by him, filed July 1945: INGVALD EIDEM, born 26 Jan 1916, PLACE OF BIRTH CITY: DEUEL COUNTY, STATE: SOUTH DAKOTA. Sex male, race white. 'SIGNATURE CODE: S — Client's signature.' NARA AAD, NUMIDENT Application (SS-5) Files 1936-2007 (Last Names E-G), rid 421597. This corrects the tree's birthplace from 'Minnesota' to Deuel County, South Dakota. ¶5

1950 census: in Lac qui Parle County, enumeration district 37-23. ¶3

Marriage: Nicollet County, Minnesota, certificate Cert-6553, 'EIDEM, INGVALD' and 'TOSTENSON, JUNE ELIZABETH', 30 June 1957. ¶12

This is the record the wave-3 brief asked for. The Minnesota death index's "mother: SLIND" field could not identify which of the three Slind wives in the colony was meant; a federal form filled in by Ingvald himself in 1945 names her as BERTHA, not Oline, and names his father as Mikal, not John. The correction the brief insisted must not be undone is now independently confirmed from the other direction. ¶17

It also makes him a FULL BROTHER of Emma Clara Eidem (10 Jan 1899 - 3 Jan 1946), already in the tree as a child of Mikal and Beret, and strengthens the Probable reassignment of Ludwig Eidem (1907-1979) to the same couple. ¶18

Burial, from the VA Nationwide Gravesite Locator: Fort Snelling National Cemetery, Minneapolis, Section Y, Site 1484, T5 US Army, World War II, b. 26 Jan 1916, d. 8 Jan 1995, with his wife June E. I. Eidem (4 Jun 1920 - 25 Dec 1990) in the same grave. ¶19

Burial, Confirmed from a photograph of the stone: FORT SNELLING NATIONAL CEMETERY, Minneapolis, Minnesota. The marker is the plain government-issue upright: 'INGVALD EIDEM / TEC 5 / US ARMY / WORLD WAR II / JAN 26 1916 / JAN 8 1995'. Find a Grave memorial 617675, photograph uploaded 2021. ¶21

Negative on the question the wave-4 brief asks of him - whose son he was. A national-cemetery government marker carries rank, branch, war and dates and NOTHING ELSE: no parents, no wife, no birthplace. His Find a Grave memorial has no biography beyond 'T5, US ARMY' and no attached obituary. He is not on Legacy.com (102 Eidem notices since 1990, none his), and he died in January 1995 in a county whose newspaper is not digitised anywhere free. HIS PARENTAGE WILL NOT COME FROM A NEWSPAPER OR A GRAVESTONE. The records that would settle it are his WWII enlistment record or his veteran's burial application at the National Cemetery Administration (which names next of kin), and the Minnesota death certificate itself rather than the index. ¶22

Corrections 4

Correction: the same SS-5 names his parents as FATHER: MIKAL EIDEM and MOTHER: BERTHA SLIND — not John P. Eidem and Oline Slind, to whom the tree currently attributes him on circumstantial grounds. This is a primary document, an original application in the applicant's own hand, and it is the only record found anywhere that states Ingvald's parentage in one sentence. The supporting geography agrees with it: he was born in Deuel County, South Dakota, whereas every dated birth of John P. and Oline's children falls in Mehurin Township, Lac qui Parle County, Minnesota. 'Mike Eidem' married to a 'Betsy Slinde' in Antelope Valley Township, Deuel County, South Dakota is already recorded in held/findings/eidem-us.md, and Antelope Valley Township is where Zoar Lutheran Cemetery stands. I have NOT repointed his father and mother fields, because resolving 'Bertha/Betsy Slind' against the tree's Brynhild Kulsetmoen — who may be the same woman under a second Selbu farm byname, or may be a first wife of the same man — needs a record I do not have. The exact next record is the South Dakota birth registration for Deuel County, January 1916, or the Zoar Lutheran parish baptismal register. ¶6

Correction, and a downgrade: his Minnesota death certificate index entry (1995-MN-000742, d. 8 January 1995, Hennepin County) gives his birth as 26 January 1916 and his birthplace as 'OUT OF STATE' - not Minnesota, as this tree had it. His mother's maiden name is given as SLIND. ¶9

Correction: Ingvald Eidem is NOT a son of John P. Eidem and Oline G. Slind. Oline's 1955 obituary names her eight surviving children and he is not one of them; he was alive at the time and did not die until 8 January 1995. As with Ludwig, the wave-2 attribution came from a 'mother: Slind' index field, and there were at least three Slind wives in this colony. His parentage should be treated as unknown pending a birth or baptismal record. ¶13

Correction: this man is detached from John P. Eidem and Oline G. Slind, to whom wave 2 of this project assigned him. Three independent findings reject it. Oline's own obituary of October 1955 names her eight surviving children and he is not among them, though he lived until 1995. The Minnesota death index gives his birthplace as OUT OF STATE, while all seven documented births of that couple were registered in Mehurin Township, Minnesota. And the Minnesota death index field that wave 2 relied on - mother's maiden name SLIND - does not point uniquely at Oline, because there were at least three Slind wives in this one Selbu colony: Oline G. Slind who married John P., Kari "Carrie" Slind who married his brother Peder P., and Beret "Betsy" Slind who married Mikal. He fits Mikal and Beret, who were in Antelope Valley Township, Deuel County, South Dakota by 1912, but no record names his parents, so he is left unattached at Speculative rather than moved on a guess. The record that would settle it is his Minnesota death certificate, which carries both parents' names. ¶14
