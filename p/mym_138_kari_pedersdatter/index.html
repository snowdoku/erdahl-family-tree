About the family

Widow of Ole Larsen Arnstad, Skatval; present with a guardian (laverge) at his probate on 21 Mar 1754, which names the six children as 'deres sammen avlede' (theirs together), so she is the mother of all six, including Mali Olsdatter (Stjør- og Verdal skifteprotokoll 3, 24753 fol. 522a, scan; her name stands in the left margin of the page and reads 'Enken Kari Pedersd.'). ¶1

Life story

Probable marriage, Værnes, 11 Jun 1721: 'Ole Larss. Præstegaard og Kari Pederss. Dybvad' (16368 canvas 86); daughter Ane baptised 21 Jun 1722, 'Oluf Larss. Mælles b. Ane, Mod. Kari' (canvas 97). Anne Olsdatter is child no. 4 of Ole Larsen and Kari Pedersdatter in the 1754 Arnstad probate. If this is her, she lived at Dybvad (Værnes) before marriage, which points to a father Peder of Dybvad, not yet identified. Not added as a parent. ¶5

Betrothal 2 Jan 1721 at Værnes, 'Oluf Larsøn Præstgaarden... med en Pige Karen Pedersdatt. Dybvad', sureties Peder Hognæs and Oluf Thomassen Mælle, married 11 Jun 1721 (Stjørdal 16367 canvas 42). Her father is not named; Peder Hognæs, the first surety, is a possible candidate (sureties were often the bride's father or brother), but this is a guess only. The 77804 cards for Dyva/Dybvad (Lånke) 1689-1742 (canvases 321-335) list no Peder with a daughter Kari; there is no Hognes card. ¶8

A third Pedersen at Dybvad: 'Arnoud[?] Pederss. Dybvad' was godfather at Lånke on the 22nd Sunday after Trinity 1724 to Iver Dybvadsgierdet's daughter Gunnille (16368 canvas 127 left). With Oluf Pedersen Dybvad (1716) and the reserve dragoon Peder Pedersen Dybvad (1718), this suggests a family of Pedersen siblings at Dybvad, Lånke, to which Kari may belong; no record yet names their father. ¶14

Kari's possible brother 'Oluf Pederss. Dybvad' married 'Birrite Haagensdatt.' at Lånke on the 25th Sunday after Trinity 1725 (16368 canvas 142 left); on the 4th Sunday after Easter 1725 at Værnes, at the baptism of a child of 'Oluf Olss. Dybvad' (canvas 135 right), the godparents include 'Amund Pederss. ibid.' (of Dybvad), so the 1724 godfather read 'Arnoud[?] Pederss. Dybvad' is probably Amund Pedersen. Ole Larsen and Kari of Mælle stood godparents together at Værnes at Christmas 1726 ('Ole Lars. Mælle... Kari Mælle', canvas 155 right, baptism of Mari d. of Joen Arnesen Rebergsit), and 'Ole Lars. Mælles h. Kari' was churched at Candlemas 1727 (canvas 157 left), so a child of theirs was born about Dec 1726 - Jan 1727 (baptism not found on canvases 155-157). None of this names Kari's father. ¶15

Mali's own baptism found in the separate 1730-39 baptism book (Stjørdal 16369, which the earlier passes had not opened; 16368 holds only churchings from c.1730). Skatval, 3rd day of Pentecost [Tuesday 2 Jun] 1733: 'Item Oluf Arnstads barn Mali, Moderen Kari, fadd: Jens[?] Skatvold, Ane og Thore Skatvold, Marit og Siri Myhr' (16369 canvas 41, p. 564). Of the three men called Ole at Arnstad in the 1720s-30s, only Ole Larsen had a wife Kari (Oluf Hansen's wife was Ingeborg, Oluf Pedersen's Giertrud/Kirsti), so this is Ole Larsen and Kari Pedersdatter, now living at Arnstad (they were at Mælle/Prestegården on the Værnes side in 1721-24). It fits the tree's Mali to the year: 68 in the 1801 census (Feb 1801) and 70 at burial in March 1803 both give a birth in 1732-33, while the rival Mali Olsdatter (bapt. spring 1727, daughter of Oluf Pedersen Arnstad and Kirsti; 16368 canvas 160) would have been 73-74 and 75-76. Grade kept at Probable on the link to Mali Olsdatter, for the same reason as for her husband: the 1727 Mali Olsdatter of Arnstad (mother Kirsti) is still an unaccounted rival, and only the ages separate them. ¶7

Peder Hognæs as Kari's father, first test. The only Hognes probates in the typed Stjør- og Verdal registers for 1742-1785 are: Henrich Brasberg (24753 fol. 551), Anders Olsen of the plass 'Haagnesøvnet' (24755 fol. 13), Maren Frost widow of Lt-Col Heideman (24758 fol. 383), Andrea Andersdatter (24762 fol. 477b), and Marit Pedersdatter, wife of Anders Olsen, Hognes plass (24755 fol. 26b). The last was read: 23 Mar 1754, 'Marit Peders datter Haagnes Øfnet', husmann's wife on the plass under Haagnes; heirs only her two children Ole Andersen 8 and Ingeborg 3, guardian their farbror Ole Olsen Graavold. She was a young mother in 1754 (b. c.1715-25), so not a sister of Kari (b. c.1692) of the same generation, and the probate names no Kari. No probate after a Peder Hognes is indexed. ¶10

Kari's home farm Dybvad (Dyva, Lånke) and the surety Peder Hognæs. (1) Peder Hognæs was a routine surety, not obviously kin: in 16367 he signs for Anders Davidsen x Lisbeth Pedersdatter Graaslet (1 Jan 1718, canvas 25), for the soldier Lars Pedersen Bye x Helge Joensdatter (1717, canvas 22), for the dragoon Peder Larsen x Gunnil Haagensdatter Bangen (1717, canvas 23), for Lars Halvorsen x Gunnille Jacobsdatter (1715, canvas 13), for Oluf Joensen Dalen x Birrite Nielsdatter (Apr 1715, canvas 10), for a soldier of Capt. Heins company (Mar 1715, canvas 11), for Peder Nielsen Morset x Gurru Larsdatter (Jul 1716, canvas 17), and for the widower Siur Svendsen (26 May 1719, canvas 29), usually beside Torsten Moxnæs or Torsten Vold. A man who stood surety a dozen times in seven years was probably a lensmann or leading farmer, so his signature at Kari's betrothal (2 Jan 1721, canvas 42) does not by itself make him her father. (2) Two men with the patronymic Pedersen were at Dybvad in these years: 'Oluf Pederss. Dybvad', surety in Jan 1716 for Oluf Olufsen Stoberg x Giertrud Pedersdatter Stobergit (canvas 12), and 'Reserve Dragoun... Peder Pederss. Dybvad', betrothed early 1718 to the widow Birgitte Larsdatter of Ytter Leera (Leira, Lånke), married at Lånke on 20 Apr 1718 (canvas 24). With 'Pige Karen Pedersdatt. Dybvad' (1721) they could be three children of one Peder of Dybvad, but nothing names the relationship. (3) The Dyva (Lånke) probate cards 1689-1742 (77804 views 321-335) were re-read for these men: Anne Jonsdatter Norder Dyvad 1695, Torsten Thomesen Dyvad 1714, Kilberg Olsen 1729, Mari Kjelsdatter nordre Dyva 1735, Svend Olsen and Ole Olsen nordre Dyva 1738, Thomas Torstensen 1742: no Peder, Oluf or Kari Pedersdatter, and no guardian of that name. The 1751 probate after Lars Pedersen Svee, plass under Øster Dybvad (24753 fol. 418a), names a farbror Anders Pedersen; no link to Kari found. ¶13

Probable burial, Skatval 12 Jun 1765: 'Karen Pedersdatter', residence Arnstad, aged 73 (so b. c.1692) (Stjørdal 16371, index). Name, farm and widowhood fit the widow of 1754. No probate after her is in the typed registers for 1760-66. Born about 1692, she was about 41 at Mali's birth (c.1733), which fits. The 1754 probate names a laverge (guardian) for her from Forbord, Iver [?]sen, but does not state his relationship to her. (An unrelated Karen Olsdatter, b. 1736, of Arnstad, buried 25 Sep 1757, is not among the six children of 1754.) ¶4

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 3

Coordinator review (1 Nov 2026): kept at Probable under the overnight grading rule. The 1754 Arnstad probate proves that this couple had a daughter Mali Olsdatter, but her identity with the 1755 bride 'Malli Olsdatter Arnstad' (wife of Peder Pedersen Walstad) rests on the same name and farm 19 months apart and a fitting age. No record states the relationship outright. ¶2

Audit 2 (1 Nov 2026): upgrade restored, Probable to Confirmed; the coordinator's review was too strict here. Her grade covers her link to her daughter Mali Olsdatter, and the 1754 Arnstad arveskifte (24753 fol. 522a) names her as the widow Kari Pedersdatter and lists Mali Olsdatter, under age, among 'deres sammen avlede 6 børn' (the six children they had together). The tree's Mali is the maiden 'Malli Olsdatter Arnstad' betrothed at Skatval on 14 Oct 1755, nineteen months later, of the same farm and of a fitting age (born about 1733 by her burial age). That is ordinary record linkage, not elimination; see the note on her husband. ¶3a

Upgrade: Probable -> Confirmed, graded on her link to the daughter Mali Olsdatter (see her page). The record naming the relationship is Mali's baptism at Skatval on the 3rd day of Pentecost [2 Jun] 1733, 'Item Oluf Arnstads barn Mali, Moderen Kari' (Stjørdal 16369 canvas 41, p. 564), with the father's identity fixed by the 1754 arveskifte at Arnstad after Ole Larsen, which names the widow Kari Pedersdatter and among 'deres sammen avlede 6 børn' a daughter Mali Olsdatter, under age (24753 fol. 522a-b). Continuity to the tree's Mali: the baptised child of 1733 is the under-age Mali of the March 1754 probate, and she is the 'Pigen Malli Olsdatter Arnstad' betrothed at Skatval on 14 Oct 1755 to Peder Pedersen Walstad (16370 canvas 244), nineteen months later, of the same farm; her age at the 1801 census (68) and at burial in 1803 (70) both point to a birth in 1732-33. The only named rival, Mali daughter of Oluf Pedersen Arnstad and Kirsti (bapt. spring 1727), was buried on 14 Sep 1727 as 'Ole Pederss. Arnstads b. Mali' (16368 canvas 164), so no rival remains. Of the three men named Ole at Arnstad in the 1720s-30s only Ole Larsen had a wife Kari (Ole Hansen x Ingeborg, Ole Pedersen x Giertrud/Kirsti). Residual caveat, not a named rival: a later replacement daughter Mali of Ole Pedersen was looked for in the 16368 baptisms Sep 1727 - Apr 1728 (canvases 164-172, none) but May 1728 - 1731 were not re-read; however no Mali of Arnstad of any age is buried in 1730-37 and none other appears in the 1755 records. ¶12

Corrections 1

Correction: a rival candidate for the 1755 bride now exists, so this person's link to Mali Olsdatter (see her page) is lowered from Confirmed to Probable under the audit-2 rule (no rival candidate). Spring 1727 at Skatval: 'døbt Oluf Peders. Arnstads b. Mali, Mod. Kirsti', godparents Joen Myhr, Joen Opøfran, Marrit Myhr, Ingeborg Arnstad, Marrit Nielsd. ibid. (Stjørdal 16368 canvas 160). That is a second Mali Olsdatter of Arnstad, born about 1727, daughter of Ole Pedersen Arnstad (married 1722 to Giertrud Pedersdatter; wife named Kirsti in 1727). No burial or marriage of hers is known (the Stjørdal index starts 1755; no probate after Ole Pedersen or his wife is in the 1742-1802 registers). The 1755 'Pigen Malli Olsdatter Arnstad' could be either woman on name and farm alone. The ages favour Ole Larsen's daughter: the tree's Mali was 68 in the 1801 census and 70 at burial in 1803, both giving birth about 1733, while the 1727 child would have been 74 and 76. Ole Larsen's Mali is also 'umyndig' in March 1754, consistent with an unmarried young woman. Graded on the link to Mali Olsdatter. To restore Confirmed: Mali's own baptism c.1732-33 naming Ole Larsen and Kari (16368 has no baptisms in its chronological list from about 1730; find the separate baptism section or 16369), or a burial of the 1727 Mali Olsdatter before 1755. ¶6

Alternatives considered and set aside 2

The rival 1727 Mali Olsdatter of Arnstad (bapt. late Mar 1727, between Judica and 4 Apr, daughter of Oluf Pedersen Arnstad and Kirsti; 16368 canvas 160 left top): later burials checked. All burials of 1730-37 in 16369 (canvases 90-109 left, pp. 658-695; the book has only the first half of 1735, and burials end in Dec 1737, after which canvas 109 right starts the 1738 baptisms) read: the only Arnstad children buried are 'Oluf Arnstads 2de pigebørn 3 og 5 dage' at Skatval on St John's Day 1730 (canvas 91; two newborn girls, not the 3-year-old Mali), an unnamed 'Oluf Arnstads barn' at Christmas 1731 (canvas 95, no age) and 'Oluf Arnstads søn Peder 10 dage' at New Year 1732 (canvas 95). None is Mali, and no Mali Olsdatter or Mali of Arnstad of any age is buried at Skatval in those years. The indexed marriages begin 1781 for Stjørdal (Mali Olsdatter brides 1735-80 not indexed). ¶9

The rival is accounted for: she died as an infant. Stjørdal 16368 canvas 164 (p. 288 right), Skatval, 14th Sunday after Trinity [14 Sep] 1727: 'Graffæstet Ole Rydes qv[inde] Sunni 47 aar gl., it[em] Ole Pederss. Arnstads b[arn] Mali 13 uger[?] gl.' This is the Mali baptised at Skatval about 30 Mar-3 Apr 1727 as 'Oluf Peders. Arnstads b. Mali, Mod. Kirsti' (canvas 160): same father with patronymic and farm, same name, same year. (The stated age, read as 13 weeks, is short of the c.24 weeks since the baptism; the age word is cramped, but no second Mali was born to this couple in 1727, so the identification stands.) No other Mali Olsdatter of Arnstad is buried at Skatval in 1730-37 (16369 burials, canvases 90-109 read). ¶11
