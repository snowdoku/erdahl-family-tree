About the family

First husband of Ingeborg Rasmusdatter, married 29 Apr 1871 in Brekke. The marriage record reads 'Rannboerg i Gindaas' (probably Ravneberg). His father was Hans Nilsen. ¶1

Life story

Probably the Johannes Hansen, 28, who died 3 Aug 1871 in St. Jørgens Hospital/Årstad parish. Ingeborg's son Herman Olai Mathias was born four weeks later (1 Sep 1871) and baptised there with father Johannes Hansen of Ravneberg. No children with John Larsen are recorded before 1875. ¶2

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
