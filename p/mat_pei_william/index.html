Life story

Born November 1864 (1911 census; 1865 in 1881/1891), PEI. In his father's household in 1881 and 1891 (as William H.). In 1901 a farmer at Murray River South owning 80 acres. In 1911, as 'William Haytor', in Kings County with wife Mary Ann (b. Sep 1874) and children Maggie A. (1896), Henry W. (1900), Euphemia C. (1903), Raleigh J. (1906) and Thomas W. (1908). His son Thomas Wake Hayter (b. 1908) married in Kings County in 1931, naming his parents William Henry and Mary Ann. ¶1

Marriage: He married Mary Ann Blue on 20 March 1895, according to the Find a Grave memorial. Mary Ann was born on 1 September 1875 in PEI, the daughter of John Blue (1830–1926) and Margaret Blue (1842–1884). She died on 9 February 1951, buried at Little Sands; Lauretta Brooks's diary records her death at Beech Grove aged 77 and notes 'Mary Anne Blue. High Bank'. ¶3

Death and burial: Lauretta Brooks's contemporary diary records his death as 'William | 87 | 26-Feb-51 | Alliston | High Bank'. His gravestone in Little Sands Cemetery gives his dates as 14 November 1864 to 26 February 1951, which makes him 86 at death (Find a Grave memorial 38890043, with a photograph of the stone). His wife died seventeen days before him. ¶4

1921 census: The index lists a High Bank household under the head 'Charlott Burt', with wife Mary (b. about 1877), sons 'Collette' and 'Stone' Burt, and daughters Marion E. (about 1912), Florence J. (about 1914) and Merna H. (about 1918) Hayter (images e003057013/14). The daughters' names and ages fit William and Mary Ann's family, Merna H. being Myrna Harriet. The household was probably mis-transcribed and should be checked against the image. ¶6

Rural directory, about 1925–28: The Cummins Atlas of Prince Edward Island lists "Hayter, Wm. H. P.O. High Bank. School Trustee. Wf. Mary A. Blue; Ch. Margaret, Henry, Euphemia, Raleigh, Thom[as]…". The same directory lists a Sencabaugh whose wife was "Margaret Hayter", very probably his daughter Maggie, born 1896. ¶7

Death: 'William, 87, 26 February 1951, Alliston - High Bank'; his wife 'Mrs. Will Hayter (Mary Anne Blue), 77, 9 February 1951, Beech Grove' (death list in the diary of Lauretta Brooks of Murray Harbour). ¶2
