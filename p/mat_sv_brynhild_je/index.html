Life story

She is on age and patronymic the 'Brynil Jensdatter, 10' of household 02 at rural residence 0034 Eidum in the 1801 census - the daughter of Jens Thomassen, huusmand med jord on the Eidem farm, and Rangnil Ingebrigtsdatter. ¶2a

Named as the mother in the baptism of Erik Johns Eidem, 9 November 1817, Selbu parish register 1816-1824, pages 44-45 serial 26, residence Eidem. In the 1825 census she is the 'Brynhild', 34, Hustru of John Eriks., husmann at Eidem. ¶1

Her granddaughter Brynild Eriksdatter, b. 1843, who married John Sivertson Eidem in 1866 and was widowed in Union County, South Dakota, is named for her. ¶3

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
