Life story

Kinard (Kynard) Russell, third son of Robert Russell (of Strensham, Worcestershire) and Joan, daughter of Sir Kinard de la Bere, knight. He married Elizabeth, daughter and heir of Thomas Coningsby, and had sons John Russell of Witley (who married Alice Lee), Anthony and William (Harleian Society vol. 27, pp. 118-119). His own entry and his sons John and Anthony are in roman type, so they belong to the base manuscript, Harl. 1043. Only the son William 'of.... in com. Hereford', and through him Elizabeth Boyle, are Mundy's later italic addition. Added. ¶1

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Speculative, graded on his link to his son William Russell. Only a late italic addition attaches William of Herefordshire to Kinard's family. The dates also conflict. In the same pedigree, Kinard's brother Robert (who married Elizabeth Baynham) is the father of Sir John Russell (who married Edith Unton) and the grandfather of Sir John Russell of Strensham, who died in 1574. That puts Kinard's granddaughter Elizabeth in the same generation as the Sir John who died in 1574. Yet her husband Lewis Boyle was active under Henry VII, and their son John died in 1551. So the italic addition probably attaches Lewis's wife a generation too low, or to the wrong Russell. Treat this as a candidate family only. ¶2a

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Test record: a Worcestershire or Herefordshire deed or will of Kinard Russell or of his son William (a Russell of Strensham settlement naming William of Herefordshire), or an inquisition post mortem of a Russell of Strensham listing Kinard's issue. Added. ¶2b
