Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Graded on that link: her own marriage row at Illingen on 10 Jul 1725 reads 'Agnes Cathar. Hoffin', relatives 'Joh. Conrad, Matthäuß' (her father, then the groom; record 908393662, image 1187139-00162). Ancestry 61023 index rows do not label roles (BRIEF audit 5 rule 3), so this rests at Probable until the image or a labelled FamilySearch twin is read; images not seen. ¶1b

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed, graded on his link to his daughter Agnes Catharina Hoff (see her page), wife of Matthäus Conzelmann. Illingen marriage register 1725 (image read), entry of 10 July: 'Matthäus Kuntzelman, ein Wagner[?], Martin Kuntzelmans Burgers [...] ehl. Sohn, und mit Ihme Agnes Catharina Hoffin, weyl. Joh. Conrad Hoffen Burgers und Becken[?] allhier hinterlassene ehl. Tochter' (trades partly uncertain). The register names the relationship (legitimate daughter left behind by the late Joh. Conrad Hoff, burgher here), in the same record that makes her Matthäus's wife in the tree. 'weyl.' shows he died before July 1725, which fits the lead in (burial of 'Hanß Cunrad Heff', Illingen, 11 Oct 1719, Ancestry 8394448) and makes the Bönnigheim 1700 candidate less likely unless he moved; 'allhier' makes him an Illingen burgher. Image read signed in on FamilySearch, 2 Nov 2026. ¶4

Alternatives considered and set aside 2

Lead, not linked: 'Hanß Cunrad Heff' was buried at Illingen on 11 Oct 1719 with no relatives indexed (record 8394448, image 1187139-00195); possibly this man (Hoff misread), which would make him dead before his daughter's marriage of 1725. No baptism of Agnes Catharina Hoff (about 1695-1705) is indexed. ¶2

Lead, not linked: a Johann Conrad 'Hohf' x Anna baptised Eva Catharina at Bönnigheim on 3 Dec 1700 (record 6652571, image 1184904-00459); no other Hoff row of that period is indexed at Bönnigheim. Illingen baptisms are indexed only from about 1700 and show no Hoff before 1740; no Agnes Catharina Hoff/Hof/Hauff baptism 1692-1708 is indexed anywhere; no marriage of a Conrad Hoff 1687-1707 near Illingen (only Hannß Conradt Hauff, Beutelsbach 1698, too far). ¶3a
