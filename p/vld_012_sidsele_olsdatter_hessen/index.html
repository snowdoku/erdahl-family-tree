About the family

Parents (audit, 13 Oct 2026): her Probable confidence now also covers her link to Ole Endresen and Sidsel of Vedde. The argument is index-only (no scan read), but each index row is a faithful transcript of the Borgund church book, and two further points support it: her churchings run to 1748, so she was born after about 1700 and her baptism should fall inside the 1698-1739 register, where Zidzel (1703) is the only Sidsel with a Vedde father; and her son Henning (bapt. 1735) and her 1750 guardian Henning Olsen repeat the name Ole Endresen gave two sons in 1709 and 1710. The mother link is weaker than the father link: it rests on a churching six weeks after the baptism, and Ole may have had an earlier wife Ingebor (churched 1698). The baptism scan (15995, 4 Nov 1703) would settle both. ¶8

Life story

Baptism (Probable identification): Zidzel, daughter of Ole Endress. (Endresen) of Vedde, was baptised at Borgund on 4 Nov 1703 (serial 58). Her mother is not named in the index; a Sidsel, wife of Ole at Vedde, was churched six weeks later on 16 Dec 1703. This is taken to be Sidsele Olsdatter, born at Wedde per her 1731 marriage: the patronymic fits (Olsdatter, daughter of Ole; Endresen is Ole's own patronymic), Ole Endresen is the only Ole indexed as a father at Vedde 1698-1712, and she would have been 28 at marriage. Her brothers by the index are Hans (bapt. 1699) and Henning (bapt. 1709, died an infant; and bapt. 1710). Confirm on the 15995 baptism scan (probably canvases c75-c77). ¶7

Baptism read on the scan: the Borgund register for the 22nd Sunday after Trinity, 4 Nov 1703, reads 'Ole Endresson Vedde en Zidzel', meaning that Ole Endresen of Vedde had a daughter Zidzel baptised. The godparents were Knud Olsen of Jafnen [farm uncertain], Poul Olsen of Vedde, Berete Andersdatter of Vaagnes (who carried the child), Mette Sørensdatter [patronymic uncertain], a maid at Vaagnes, and Ernder [?] Hansdatter, a maid of Frantzholmen. The mother is not named; this priest wrote only the father. This makes the father link to Ole Endresen of Vedde scan-read for Zidzel. That Zidzel is the Sidsele Olsdatter born at Wedde who married in 1731 is still an inference: no record says so. She stays Probable. ¶9

Re-proof attempt (overnight 1-2 Nov 2026), no change. She was 28 at marriage in 1731, before confirmation lists began (1736), so no confirmation entry exists. Probates searched for one naming her as a daughter or sister: the Sunnmøre probate index for Vedde 1714-1790 has no probate after her mother Sidsel Hansdatter and none after a Henning Olsen of Vedde (rows: Didrik Paulsen 1722, Didrik Amundsen 1722, Anna Sjursd. 1723, Hans Olsen 1724 [index: 'finner ikke skiftet'], Sjur Didriksen 1733, Sidsel Knutsd. 1738, Marthe Pedersd. 1742, Berte Eriksd. 1753, Peder Einersen 1764, Brite Torbjørnsd. 1766, Elida Larsd. 1771, Anne Larsd., Siri Jonsd. and Åge Pedersen 1788). The probate after Henning Olsen of Kalvestad, Borgund, 29 Nov 1768 (3A 23 fol. 176b-177a, view 24564/178, scan read) lists a widow Ragnilde Nielsdatter and nine children (Johannes, Ole, Peder, Anders, Hans, a daughter Sidsille Henningsdatter and others), so no siblings are named; whether he is her brother Henning is unproved (lead only: he named a daughter Sidsille). Note for the coordinator: under the grading rule, her parents Ole Endresen and Sidsel Hansdatter (Confirmed) carry this link to her, which the coordinator review here judged circumstantial; one of the two grades should be reconciled. ¶13

Burial: 'Sidsele Olsd.' of Hessen, aged 66 (b. 1703), buried 17 Dec 1769 at Borgund (Borgund ministerialbok 1762-1800; index pg00000002548873). Farm and age fit her 1703 birth and her life at Hessen (married there 1731 and again, as a widow, 1751). The other Sidsele Olsdatter of the same age buried at Borgund (Gidschøe, 12 May 1767, pg00000002548640) lived on a different farm, so the farm decides it. This also fits Jetmund Hansen of Hessen having a different widow (Brithe Knudsdatter) at his death in 1788. ¶16

Marriage: as the maid Sissell (Zidsele) Olsdatter, born at Wedde (Vedde) and living at Hessen, she was engaged on 27 May 1731 and married on 8 July 1731 at Borgund to Anders Olsen of Hessen. From the index. ¶1

Churching: a Sidsele/Sissele Olsdatter of Hessen was churched (introdusert, a mother's first return to church after a birth) on 6 July 1732, 21 February 1734, 1 January 1736, 12 October 1738, 3 July 1740, 14 March 1745 and 8 September 1748. These dates fit Anders Olsen's children (1732, 1735, 1739, 1740, 1745). The churching entries do not name her husband, so linking them to her is an inference. If they are hers, she was alive after her daughter Sisille was born in 1740, which means the daughter was given her living mother's name. ¶2

Widow in 1750 (scan read): the 12 Sep 1750 probate after Anders Olsen of Hessen names her as his widow 'Sisille Ols Datter', with guardian Henning Olsen (farm uncertain), and their child Cisille Andersdatter as heir. This proves she was Anders's wife and outlived him. Henning Olsen is probably her brother (same patronymic; unproved), which would explain the name Henning among her son and her descendants. ¶5

Jetmund Hansen's probate (scan read): when the estate of Jetmund Hansen of Hessen was registered on 5 Jun 1788, his widow was Brithe Knudsdatter (guardian Rasmus Sørensen Alnes). His heirs were his sister's children, the Knudsen siblings of Gudøya (Hans, Sallamund, Ole of Roald, Lisbet, and the three children of the late Elie). He left no children of his own, and no step-children are named, although step-children did not inherit and would not be expected. If this is the Jetmund Hansen who married the widow Sidsele Olsdatter in 1751 (same name and farm; not proved), she had died before 1788 and he had remarried. Her own probate has not been found (negative for 1751-1800). ¶6

Second marriage (inferred): Sidsele Olsdatter, a widow (enke) of Hessen, was engaged on 12 April 1751 to the bachelor Jetmund Hansen of Hessen. The sponsors were Ole Rasmussen of Hessen and Rasmus Gundersen of Godøya, and the couple married on 5 July 1751. This fits Anders Olsen's drowning in 1750. From the index; that this widow is Anders's widow is an inference. ¶3

Checked in the 1762 extra tax, Hessen (49637 view 33, 13 households): household 5 is 'M: Jetmund Hansen, H: Sidsle Olsdatt.', possibly she remarried, but the entry lists no child over 12 and her daughter Cæcilia, then married at Lille Nørve, is not in it. No change to her grade. ¶14a

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 5

Confidence is Probable for the same reason as her husband's: her link to Cæcilia rests on the identification of the 1740 baptism. The baptism does not name the mother; she is the mother by the 1731 marriage and the churchings. ¶4

Upgrade: three primary records now tie her to Ole Endresen and Sidsel Hansdatter of Vedde. (1) Her 1731 marriage calls her Sissell Olsdatter, born at Wedde (Vedde). (2) The probate after Ole Endresen of Vedde, 17 Jun 1713 (Sunnmøre 3A 04 fol. 270b; scan), names among the joint children of Ole and his widow Sidzelle Hansdatter 'Zidselle Ols datter gammel 10 aar' (born about 1703, matching the 4 Nov 1703 baptism) and a son Henning Olsen. (3) In the 1750 probate after her husband Anders Olsen of Hessen her guardian was Henning Olsen, who is thus her brother as named in 1713. Ole Endresen was the only Ole with children at Vedde in the index for 1698-1712. Coordinator: the step from the 1713 heir to the 1731 bride rests on her birthplace and the brother Henning, not on a record naming Hessen; lower to Probable if that is judged short of Confirmed. ¶10

Coordinator review (1 Nov 2026): kept at Probable, not Confirmed. No single record names her parents; the link rests on three records together (her 1731 marriage giving her birthplace as Vedde, the 1713 probate listing Zidselle with a brother Henning, and Henning Olsen acting as her guardian in 1750). The evidence is strong, but it is circumstantial. ¶11

Audit 2 (1 Nov 2026): Probable kept, but for a different reason from the coordinator's review. Under the overnight grading rule her grade covers her link to her daughter Cæcilia (see her page), not her link to Ole Endresen and Sidsel Hansdatter of Vedde, which is graded on their records (Confirmed: the 1731 marriage gives her birthplace as Vedde, the 1713 probate names 'Zidselle Ols datter gammel 10 aar' with a brother Henning, and Henning Olsen is her guardian in 1750). The link to Cæcilia is Probable because the 1750 probate after Anders Olsen names the widow Sisille Olsdatter and 'Datteren Cisille Andersdatter', but Cæcilia of Lille Nørve (d. 1821) is joined to that Cisille and to the 1740 Hessen baptism only by name, farm and age. ¶12a

Upgrade (regrade 3): Probable to Confirmed as mother of Cæcilia (Sidsele) Andersdatter (see her page); graded on her link to Cæcilia Andersdatter. Naming record: the probate after Anders Olsen at Hessen, 12 Sep 1750 (Sunnmøre 3A 17, p. 136b, scan), divides the estate between 'hands efterladende Enke Sisille Ols Datter' and 'den Sal. Mands med hende avlede... Barn Cisille Anders datter' - the child he had with her, so it names her as the mother, not a stepmother. Continuity as for Anders: Cisille, baptised at Hessen 5 Jun 1740, confirmed 1757 as 'Siedsele Anders d: Hessen', 16, and married 1 Jan 1760 as the maid Sidsele Andersdatter of Hessen to Lars Olsen; all on one farm, gaps of 10, 7 and 3 years, no named rival. Audit 2 held this Probable on a stricter reading of continuity than the audit-3 detail now allows; see the note on Anders Olsen Hessen. Her own parentage (Ole Endresen and Sidsel Hansdatter of Vedde) is graded on their records and is not changed. ¶15
