About the family

Mother of Aase Rasmusdatter: Probable, on the same evidence as Rasmus Kristoffersen: Aaland's succession, and Aase's daughters named Berthe in 1759 and again in 1765. Aaland names only one wife for Rasmus. To confirm: her 1765 burial (Innvik) and the probate after her (about 1765), which would list her children. ¶2

Life story

Aaland 1932: Aaland's Kjellog (Tjellaug) history names Rasmus Kristoffersen's wife 'Brite Rasm.d. Brekke, † 77 a. g. 1765': Brite Rasmusdatter of Brekke, who died in 1765 aged 77, born about 1688. ¶1

1664 vicar's census (p. 211): 'Heel gaard Brecke' had four farmers, Oluf Lassesen 40, Oluf Johannesen 45, Erik Olsen 48 and Lasse Lassesen 42, and no sons listed. Her father Rasmus does not appear; he was probably a child then or not yet born. ¶3

Guardianship protocols: protocol 3, p. 150 calls her 'deris Enkemoder Brite Tiellov' (the widowed mother of the Tjellaug wards Aase, Ingebor, Brite and Else Rasmusdatter, whose 1742 inheritance she held). So her husband was dead before her own death in 1754. Under the skiftebrev of 6 Jun 1742, the wards were Ingebor 23, Brite 20, Aase 18, Else 12 and Rasmus Rasmusen 9, plus Synneve, who were each given shares in 6 marks of land in Tjellaug (protocol 2, pp. 238, 256, 269). ¶8

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: Nordfjord skifteprotokoll A 12 (1753-1757), fol. 77b-78a (Digitalarkivet 24229 view 80; index pa00000000054522 'Brite Rasmusdtr, Tjellog, prot. 12 p. 78'). On 17 May 1754 the lensmann Niels Larsen Falleide and two appraisers held a probate 'efter afgangne Britte Rasmusdatter, som boede paa den gaard Tiellau' in Oppstryn, at her heirs' request, between her six children and heirs, namely Rasmus Rasmussen [farm or remark unclear], 2[6?] years old, Synneve Rasmusdatter 41, Ingebor Rasmusdatter 35, Brite Rasmusdatter 32, Aase Rasmusdatter 30 and Else Rasmusdatter 24. Aase Rasmusdatter of Tjellaug, aged 30 (born about 1724), is Aase Rasmusdatter, so the probate names Brite as Aase's mother, and her motherhood moves from Probable to Confirmed. All six children are Rasmus's children by patronymic, which fits Aaland's husband Rasmus Kristoffersen, but the lines read do not name the husband; his link stays Probable. ¶5

Corrections 2

Correction (caution): an overnight target summary said Brite is named as a married daughter of Rasmus Olsen Erdal in his probate of 30 May 1714 (A 5b fol. 327b). Re-reading the saved crop of fol. 327b, that daughter is 'Brite Rasmusdatter gift med' a man whose name reads roughly 'Gulow/Olow B...son'. It is not Rasmus Kristoffersen. Aaland also places this Brite at Brekke, not Erdal. So the 1714 probate does not show her as Rasmus Olsen's daughter, and she must not be linked to Rasmus Olsen Erdal on that evidence. Her father, a Rasmus of Brekke, is still unidentified. ¶4

Correction: Aaland gives her death as 1765 aged 77. The probate after her was held on 17 May 1754, so she died before that date. The death date is changed to BEF 17 MAY 1754. Her birth about 1688 rests on Aaland's age and is left as it is; her eldest listed child, Synneve, was 41 in 1754 (born about 1713), which is consistent with it. ¶6

Searches that found nothing 1

Negative: the 1735 estate of Cornelius Rasmussen at Brekke (A 8, 1735 section, image 12 right to 13 left) went to 'hans Avlede umyndige Halvbroder Børn' under a testament: his half-brother's son Hans Jonsen, whose guardian was his farbror (father's brother) Erich [Skjegg?], and his half-brother's daughter Ingebor Jonsdatter, whose guardian was 'hendes næste Frænde Ole Brecke'. Hans's share includes what he inherited from his late father on 7 Jun 1734. So the half-brother was a Jon (d. by 1734) with a full brother Erik. If Cornelius had been a full brother of Brite Rasmusdatter (alive until about 1754) she or her children would have been heirs; they are not, so Cornelius was not her full sibling. The record gives nothing on her mother. ¶7
