About the family

The young daughters Christie and Brithe (about 7 and 2 in 1775) were born some 35-40 years after Gjest (b. about 1733), so Synneve Arnesdatter was probably a second wife and Gjest's mother an earlier wife, unnamed (inference). By the naming pattern his own father was a Gjest (inference only). ¶4

Parents: father Giest Matiesen (Gjest Mathiassen) raised to Probable and mother Dordei Samsonsdatter added (Probable). His own grade (link to Gjest Guttormsen) is unchanged. Primary record found: the probate after the cottager Samson Giestsen at Hagen under Hopperstad in Vik, 1750 (Ytre Sogn 7a fol. 37b, No. 92, 24163 view 38), names as heirs, besides his widow Ingebor Iversdatter, his mother Dordej Samsonsdatter and 'tvende Brødre Mathias Giestsen og Gutorm Giestsen, begge myndige og tilstede paa egne og deres [Moders] Vegne' (share heads: 'Den afdødes Moder', 'Den afdødes Broder Mathias Giestsen', 'Anden Broder Gutorm Giestsen'). So Guttorm and Mathias Giestsen were brothers of Samson Giestsen, and their father was dead by 1750 (the mother inherits with the brothers; Samson left no children). The 1701 census for Vik lists at Torvold (parish 'Wiigs', so Torvold is in Vik) 'Giest Matiesen', inderste, 43, with his son 'Samson Giestsen', 2 (b. ~1699): the only Samson Giestsen in the Ytre Sogn records, and Samson is the name of the mother's father (Dordej SAMSONSdatter), so the 1750 Samson is the 1701 boy. Guttorm (b. ~1706) and Mathias (b. ~1701) were born after the census. Grading: Probable, not Confirmed. The 1750 probate names the brothers and their mother but not their father, so Giest Matiesen as father is inferred (shared patronymic Giestsen + the 1701 Samson, 49 years and a change of farm earlier). The tree's Guttorm is identified with the 1750 'Gutorm Giestsen' by name, parish and his brother Mathias Giestsen (Mathias of Vikøren stood surety at the 1759 betrothal of Guttorm's son Gjest, 11566 c34); the probate does not give Guttorm's farm. The word read '[Moders]' in 'deres [Moders] Vegne' is partly legible; if confirmed it names Dordej as the brothers' (not only Samson's) mother. No rival Guttorm Giestsen is indexed in Ytre Sogn 1698-1790. ¶12a

Negative checks for his parents and siblings. (a) Ingegier Guttormsdatter, Seim, May 1759 (7b p. 438b, No. 14, 24164 view 104): heirs are her widower Ingebrigt Endresen Seim and her siblings, incl. a brother Simon Guttormsen and an under-age sister Brithe Guttormsdatter 8. Siblings, not a father, inherit, so her father was dead by 1759; Guttorm Gjestsen lived to 1775 and had no son Simon, so she is not his daughter (the sister Brithe, b. ~1751, is a namesake of his daughter Brithe, b. ~1751). (b) Helga Giestsdatter, Njøs, Oct 1771 (8b p. 731a, No. 96, 24166 view 349): a possible sister, but the probate is between her widower Eric Lassesen and their son, with no siblings named. (c) Probate index by farm, 1700-1790: no probate after Gjest Mathiassen or Dordei Samsonsdatter under Hagen*, Hop*/Hopperstad*, Vikør*, Torvold/Tor* (Torvold has no index rows; Torvund is a different farm) or by name (Gjest* + Mat*/Mad*, Dor* + Sam*). ¶14

Life story

Bygdebok (Vik H. 3 canvas 14, Vikøyri; rebuilt from word boxes, dates ABT): 'Guttorm Gjestson «Seim» (1706-1775)', married 1732; the child list begins with [Gjest] 1732-61, then Randi 1733, [...] 1743-56, [Kristi] 1744 and Brita 1747-1807; 'Synneva hadde truleg ein bror Jon'; 'Sonen Gjest gifte seg og kom til plassen [...] han Gjest 1761'. The bygdebok's son Gjest, born 1732, died 1761, married and moved to a plass where his son Gjest was born in 1761, matches Gjest Guttormsen (buried Nauteskreen 5 Jul 1761 aged 28, posthumous son Giest baptised 17 May 1761). This supports the identification but it is a compiled source, so the grade stays Probable (graded on his link to Gjest Guttormsen). The children's dates agree with the 1775 probate as (Brithe 24, b. about 1751: bygdebok Brita 1747; Christie 3-: Kristi 1744). ¶11a

Marriage found: Vik i Sogn ministerialbok 11752, canvas 30 left, 28 Dec 1732: 'ung Karl Gutorm Giestsøn Seim og sin besvangrede Synneve Arnes Daatter Seimsøren'. He was then a bachelor living at Seim (Vik). By 1736 he lived at Vikøren: '9. Døbt Gutorm Giests: Vigøren Barn kaldet [blank]', March 1736 (c51 right). Birth year estimated from the marriage. ¶5

Bridge evidence: at the Tjugum betrothal of 'Giest Guttormsøn' and 'Anna Hermunds D.' on 1 Jul 1759 (Leikanger 11566 c34 left), the first surety was 'Mathias Vigøren'. Mathias Giestsen, cottager at Vikøren (probate 22 May 1778, 9b p. 939), is probably this man's brother. The groom and bride are the couple of the 1761 Nauteskreen probate. A surety from Vikøren supports the identification of the Tjugum Gjest with this man's son 'Giest Guttormsen' of the 1775 probate, but sureties are not labelled kin, so the link to Gjest Guttormsen stays Probable. The Mathias Giestsen probate pages read (p. 939) do not mention Guttorm or his heirs. ¶7

Probate registered 9 Jun 1775 at Vikøren in Vik skipreide (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll 9a, No. 67, p. 601-602): 'efter afdøde huusmand Guttorm Gie[s]thsen imellem Enken Synneve Arnesdatter og... Livsarvinger... Sønnen Giest Guttormsen, død og efterladt sig 1 Søn navnlig Giest Giestsen [14, possibly 13] aar gl.; ældste Datter Randi Guttormsdatter gift med Ole Iversen Wiigdnen(?), anden Datter Christie [7?] aar, og 3die Datter Brithe 2 aar gl.' The share heads on p. 602 read 'Sønne Sønnen Giest Giestsen', 'ældste Datter Randi Guttormsdatter Wiigdnen', 'anden Datter Christie Guttormsdatter', 'tredie Datter Brithe Guttormsdatter'. A guardian (verge) for Giest Giestsen is named at the foot of p. 602, 'Ole... Nunnedale(?)', not securely legible. Lensmann Mads Hansen Sebje(?); appraisers Ole Sebøe and Erich.... ¶1

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 3

Probable as father of Gjest Guttormsen (graded on that link). The probate names his son 'Giest Guttormsen', dead by 1775, whose only child was 'Giest Giestsen', about 14 in June 1775. Our Gjest Guttormsen of Nauteskreen, Tjugum skipreide, was buried 5 Jul 1761 aged 28 and left one posthumously-named son 'Giest Giestsøn', baptised 17 May 1761 (14 years and three weeks before this probate). Father's name, the rare given name Gjest repeated over three generations, the son's death before 1775 and the grandson's age all agree; no other Gjest Guttormsen is known in Ytre Sogn. But the probate does not name Nauteskreen, Tjugum, or the widow Anne Hermundsdatter, and Vikøren (Vik) is a different skipreide across the fjord, so the identification spans farms and stays Probable under the grading rule. ¶2

Upgrade: graded on his link to his son Gjest Guttormsen (see his page), Probable -> Confirmed. His own probate at Vikøren, 9 Jun 1775 (Ytre Sogn 9a No. 67, 24167 view 302), names 'Sønnen Giest Guttormsen', dead, with 1 son Giest Giestsen 14, among his and his widow's children; the probates after his daughters Brithe (1807) and Christi (1809) Guttormsdatter at Vikøren name their 'Fuldbroder Giest Guttormsen, der boede og døde paa gaarden Nødskreen', husband of Anna Hermundsdatter and father of Giest Giestsen of Indre Esse. That closes the cross-farm gap that kept him Probable. Correction of an old reading: notes on Anne Hermundsdatter give the 1775 heirs as 'Giert Guttormsen... Giert Giertsen 1 year old'; the heading (crops h2.jpg) has 'Giest Guttormsen' and 'Giest Giestsen 14 aar', as read it. Continuity: the probates after his unmarried daughters at Vikøren, Brithe Guttormsdatter ([26?] Oct 1807, Ytre Sogn 13 p. 271b, 24173 view 274) and Christi Guttormsdatter (3 Oct 1809, 13 p. 551b, No. 54, 24173 view 554), each name as first heir their 'Fuldbroder Giest Guttormsen, der boede og døde paa gaarden Nødskreen i Tiugum Skibrede, og var gift med... Anna Hermundsdatter', and his one son 'Giest Giestsen, boende som huusmand paa g. Indre Esse i Tiugums Skibrede', present (he signed the 1809 entry 'Giest Giestsen Indre Esse'). Brithe, Christi and Rande are the daughters, and Giest Guttormsen (dead, son Giest Giestsen 14) the son, of Guttorm Giestsen and Synneve Arnesdatter in the 1775 Vikøren probate, held 'imellem Enken Synneve Arnesdatter og deres tilsammen avlede Børn'. The 1807/1809 records name the same sibling set (Giest, Rande m. Ole Iversen, Christi, Brithe) at the same farm Vikøren 32-34 years later, so this is a pair/sibling-set bridge (audit 8 rule 3), and 'Fuldbroder' makes Giest a child of both parents. The Nødeskreen husband of Anna Hermundsdatter is the tree's Gjest Guttormsen (probate 18 Sep 1761, Nauteskreen; burial 5 Jul 1761). ¶10a

Audit 9 (2 Nov 2026): Confirmed kept, graded on his link to his son Gjest Guttormsen (see his page). The 1775 Vikøren probate, held 'imellem Enken Synneve Arnesdatter og deres tilsammen avlede Børn', names 'Sønnen Giest Guttormsen', dead, with his son Giest Giestsen, 14. The probates after the daughters Brithe (1807) and Christi (1809) name their 'Fuldbroder Giest Guttormsen, der boede og døde paa gaarden Nødskreen', husband of Anna Hermundsdatter, and his son Giest Giestsen of Indre Esse. Together these name the relationship and place the son at Nødskreen, so no cross-farm identification is left. Clean-up: the later bygdebok note on this record says 'the grade stays Probable'. It was written beside the raise and is superseded; the bygdebok only supports the Confirmed grade. Its 'Test record' (Gjest's Vik baptism about Jan 1733) would add the birth, but the link no longer needs it. ¶13

The case for this identification 1

Supporting, grade unchanged: the guardian of the grandson Giest Giestsen in this 1775 probate, read 'Ole... Nunnedale', reads 'Ole H[ermundsen?] Numedale' (24167 view 302). In 1754 'Ole Hermundsen Nummedale' was the brother and guardian of Anne Hermundsdatter at Nauteskreen (Ytre Sogn 7a fol. 240b), and Anne was the widow of Giest Guttormsen in 1761. So the 1775 grandson's guardian was Anne's brother, which ties the 1775 'Giest Giestsen' to the Nauteskreen family across the change of farm (Vikøren 1775 vs Nauteskreen 1761). Still Probable, because the guardian's identity rests on a matching name and a partly read word, not a stated relationship. ¶9

Corrections 1

Correction: the 1775 probate ages were misread. A re-read of 24167 view 302 (p. 601 foot) gives 'anden Datter Christie 3[-] aar' (second digit unclear) and 'og 3die Datter Brithe 24 aar gl.', not 7 and 2. Brithe was born about 1751 and Christie about 1740-45. With the 1732 marriage to the same Synneve Arnesdatter who was the widow in 1775, the earlier inference that Synneve was a second wife and that Gjest's mother was an unnamed first wife is withdrawn. Grade unchanged (Probable as father of Gjest Guttormsen): neither the marriage nor the probate names Nauteskreen or Anne Hermundsdatter, so the identification of the dead son Giest Guttormsen with the Nauteskreen man still spans farms. ¶6

Alternatives considered and set aside 2

Rival checked: the other Guttorm probate suggested for Gjest, Guttorm Pedersen of Vangsnes, Tjugum skipreide (10a p. 239b-240a, 1785, 24168 view 240), has as heirs his widow Brithe Hamundsdatter and his sisters and their children (Ragnilde Pedersdatter, 80; Gutlaug and Brithe Nielsdatter, among others). No son and no Giest Giestsen appear, so Guttorm Gjestsen of Vikøren is the only Guttorm known whose probate names a son Giest and a grandson Giest Giestsen. ¶3

No rival: no other Giest Guttormsen is known in Vik or Tjugum. ¶10b
