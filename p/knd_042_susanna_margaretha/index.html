Life story

Surname, father and birth: the bride's own row of the Vaihingen marriage of 3 Aug 1737 reads 'Susanna Margaretha Schämayers', relatives 'Joh. Christoph, Georg Jacob' (her father, then the groom; Ancestry 61023 record 904777562, image 1184562-00524). A Susanna Margaretha 'Schunaya' was born at Vaihingen on 18 Sep 1716 to Joh. Christoph and Anna Sibylla (record 4768896, image 1184562-00166), and a sister Susanna Catharina 'Schumaÿer' on 26 Dec 1714 to Johann Christoph and Anna Sibÿlla (record 4768783). The three spellings are one surname, entered as Schumayer (Schuhmaier). Same parish, same father's name, aged 20 at the marriage, no other candidate found: the 1716 birth is entered as hers (identification Probable). Her own grade, on her link to Christoph Adam Knodel, stays Speculative. ¶3

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: Speculative to Probable, graded on her link to her son Christoph Adam Knodel (see his page). Christoph Adam's baptism, which earlier passes could not find in the Ancestry index, is in FamilySearch record QPGK-HRCY (FamilySearch, 'Deutschland, ausgewählte evangelische Kirchenbücher 1500-1971' (also shown as 'Germany, Lutheran Baptisms, Marriages, and Burials, 1500-1971')): Christoph Adam Knodel, born 4 Oct 1740, Vaihingen an der Enz, mother Susanna Margaretha, her husband Georg Jacob Knodel. Georg Jacob married Susanna Margaretha Schämayers at Vaihingen on 3 Aug 1737 (QPGK-7T8Y). ¶4a

The case for this identification 1

Identity (Speculative,; revised by audit): wife of the Georg Jacob Knodel who married at Vaihingen an der Enz on 3 August 1737 (index row 4777562, relatives 'Adam, Susanna Margaretha'; surname not indexed) and mother of his Vaihingen children born 1742-1757 (rows 4771653, 4772165, 4772465, 4773391, 4775358). Entered as the mother of Christoph Adam Knodel (married 1759) only Speculatively, and only on top of a second Speculative step, that Christoph Adam's father is this Vaihingen Georg Jacob at all. For her: she is the only wife of the Vaihingen Georg Jacob in the index, and no birth of the couple is indexed for 1738-1741, where a son born in mid-1738 or later would fall. Against her, and for an earlier wife: as her son Christoph Adam would have married in February 1759 aged at most about 20, unusually young for a Württemberg man; a son of an earlier marriage of Georg Jacob, born about 1730-1736, would have married at a more usual 23-29. If Georg Jacob is the man born in 1711, he was 19-25 in those years, so an earlier marriage is possible though early; the 1737 marriage row does not show whether he was then a widower. A third possibility is that Christoph Adam's father is a different Georg Jacob Knodel altogether (see Georg Jacob's 'Possible birth' note), in which case she is unrelated. On the index alone neither the 1737 marriage nor an earlier wife is the better reading; no baptism of Christoph Adam is indexed, and the 1759 and 1760 marriage rows name only his father. She was still bearing children in 1757, so she was probably born after about 1712 (inferred). Generation count upward from Christoph Ludwig Knodel (1765): Christoph Adam (Probable), then she (Speculative), the first Speculative generation on this path. The 1737 marriage image (1184562-00524) would give her surname and whether either party was widowed; a Vaihingen birth of Christoph Adam (film 1184562, about images 00285-00316, 1738-41), or an age at his 1813 burial (1184565-00603) if that is he, would prove or disprove her motherhood. ¶1

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Not Confirmed: identifying the 1740 baby with the groom of 1759/60 rests on name, father and parish. Under audit-5 rule 1, a burial check for an infant Christoph Adam (1740-1759) and a check for a second wife of Georg Jacob are still needed. He would also have been only 19 at marriage, which is young for Württemberg. ¶4b

Searches that found nothing 1

Death: not found in the index rows read. ¶2
