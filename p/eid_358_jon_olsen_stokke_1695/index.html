Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Graded Probable on his link to his son Ola Jonsen Stokke (see his page). Selbu i fortid og nåtid vol. 6, Midti-Stokkan (canvas 19): '1729-1752 Jon Olsen, f. ca. 1695, d. 1764. G. 1729 m. Marit Per[sdt.] f. ca 1697, d. 1751. Barn: 1. Jon, f. 1726, g.I m. Agnete [Olsdt.] [...] m. enke Mali [...]; 2. Per, f. 1729; 3. Ola, f. 1733, [se] over'. He is the son 'Jon, f. ca. 1695' of Ola Bjørnsen and Ingeborg (same canvas), and canvas 18 adds 'Ola hadde i 1701 sønnen Jon, født 1696'. Canvas 18 also mentions a skifte after Jon Olsen. ¶1

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Test record: the probate after Jon Olsen Stokke (d. 1764) in the Stjør- og Verdal skifteprotokoll 1760s (24755, typed register; 'Salb'/'Stok' entries) listing sons Jon, Per and Ola; and the 1701 manntall for Selbu (Ola Bjørnsen at Stokke with son Jon aged 5). ¶2
