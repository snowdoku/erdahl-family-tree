Life story

A Slind of Selbu, carrying the same patronymic as Oline Gundersdatter Slind, settled as a farmer at Madison, the county seat of Lac qui Parle County, Minnesota, by 1906. Entered as returning from a visit home: Trondheim police office emigrant protocol no. 12 (1904-1907), page 215, line 18, group 00721, expedited 4 July 1906, ship Tasso, Cunard - 'Kristian Gundersen Slind', b. 1867, married, occupation 'farmer', residence AMERICA, purpose of emigration 'Reise hjem' (travelling home), destination 'Madison, Minn', ticket paid in Trondhjem. On line 19 his wife 'Hanna Gundersen Slind', b. 1877, 'hustru', residence America, and on lines 21 and 22 their children Mimi (b. 1903) and Henry (b. 1905), entered with the residence Strinden. ¶1

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Rated Speculative as a brother of Oline: the patronymic, the parish and the county all fit, but the 1891 Norwegian census of the household of Gunder Olsen at Slind lists the children as Ole 1872, Beret 1875, Marit 1878, Oline 1881, Ole 1884, Gunder 1887 and Karen 1890, with no Kristian - he would have to be an elder child who had already left home by 1891, which a birth of 1867 makes possible but not proved. There was more than one Gunder at Slind. The Selbu baptismal register for 1867 would settle it. ¶2
