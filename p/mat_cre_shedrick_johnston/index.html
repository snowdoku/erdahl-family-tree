About the family

Second husband of Bexey Jane (Creed) Hicken. 1901 census (29 May 1901, Township 61, Sturgeon, page 18, image read): Johnston, Shedrick W., head, married, born 12 March 1859, 42, born PEI, Baptist, farmer on his own account, with his wife Bexy and her children Edith and George Hicken. They married between August 1891, when her first husband died, and May 1901; the marriage record has not been found. ¶1

Parents: William Johnstone, who married Mary Dewar (daughter of Donald Dewar and Jessie Forbes) on 6 May 1851, son of William Johnstone (born 1778, a soldier of the 42nd Highlanders, died 1856 at Cumberland Hill, Kings County). The parents' given names are confirmed by the Massachusetts death index; the rest is from the compiled genealogy. He had no children. His siblings included Isabella (born 1852), Dr William J. Johnstone of Boston, and two sisters married in Ashburnham, Massachusetts. ¶3

Life story

Death: Massachusetts death register index, 'Shadrach W. Johnstone', born about 1860, died 12 January 1906 at Boston, son of William and Mary. According to the compiled Johnstone genealogy he was a mariner and ship's captain, died at Boston, and was buried on 17 January 1906 in the Lower Montague Methodist Cemetery, PEI. Bexey is a widow in the 1911 census. ¶2
