Life story

He is almost certainly the Lars whose patronymic ELIAS LARSEN SLIND (b. 20 Nov 1881, d. Mar 1965, buried Zoar Lutheran, Deuel County, South Dakota) carries, and who was a pallbearer at Oline Slind Eidem's funeral in 1955. A third candidate son is GUNDER LARSON SLIND, PFC US Army in the First World War, b. 30 November 1886, d. 6 December 1947, buried Fort Snelling National Cemetery, Minneapolis, Section A-20 Site 5039 - the Larson being the same patronymic again. Two sons named Gunder, nine years apart (1886 and 1890), is possible in Selbu practice if the elder had been given away to another household or if one of the stated ages is wrong, and I flag it rather than resolving it. ¶2

Named as the father on the Washington death certificate of GUNDER SLIND, who died at Port Blakely, Kitsap County, Washington on 30 January 1959 aged 68 (so born about 1890-91): "Father Name: Lars Slind. Mother Name: Brynhild Hanstad" (DA reference {052531D4-9FFF-4570-B006-CA665B846C06}, image 1410, document 8, doc. ref. id 1096). ¶1

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the grade

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Rated Probable. The link to Elias is an inference from the patronymic and the shared surname and parish, not from a document naming both. Hanstad/Hanstadmoen is a Selbu farm name, which supports a Selbu origin for the whole group. ¶3
