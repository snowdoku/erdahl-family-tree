Life story

John Eveleigh, esq., of Holcombe in Ottery St Mary and later of Clyst St Lawrence, Devon; living at the 1564 heralds' visitation; died 1586, when administration of his estate was granted to his widow Joane, with his son George and Robert Connante of Bovey Tracey as bondsmen (Worthy, Devonshire Wills, pp. 8-9, as cited by the Heverly family history). He married first Alice, daughter of Henry Collyns of Ottery St Mary, and second Joane, daughter of John Southcott of Bovey Tracey. Added. ¶1

Vivian, Visitations of Devon (1895), p. 336, has now been read from the page image (facsimile in the Heverly history, leaf n59). It makes him 'John Eveleigh of Halcombe in the parish of St. Mary Otrie in Com. Devon, son & heir' of Thomas Eveleigh of Clyst St Lawrence and Thomazine Moore, and shows 'John Eveleigh, 2 son' (husband of 'Prudence, da. of Dr. Barnes, mar. in Oxford') among his sons by Joane Southcott. Westcote (about 1630, pp. 563-564) gives the same, naming John among the issue of the second wife and adding 'John Evelegh married, in Oxford, Prudence, daughter of Doctor Barnes'. Worthy's Devonshire Wills (pp. 8-9) was also read: '1586. Administration to the effects of John Eveleighe, Esqr., of Clist St. Lawrence, Intestate. Granted to Joan his relict. George Eveleighe his Son and Robert Connante of Bovey Tracy join the bond'. Grade unchanged (Probable, on his link to John Eveleigh): these are compiled pedigrees, and the 'Test record' asking for Vivian p. 336 is now met only to the extent Vivian is itself a compiled source. Test record still outstanding: a record naming the Principal as his son, e.g. Nicholas Evelegh's will, PROB 11/131/535 (1618), or Sir Edwin Sandys's will (1629, which names 'my brother in law, James Evelegh'); Boase, Register of Exeter College (1894), p. 79. Added. ¶5

Nicholas Evelegh's will read (TNA PROB 11/131/535, image read). Grade unchanged (Probable, on his link to John Eveleigh). The will names three of the brothers in Vivian's chart of his sons by Joane Southcott: 'John Evelegh my brother late of Oxford deceased' (Vivian's 2nd son, the Principal), 'my brother James Evelegh' (7th son, Sir James), and Josias Evelegh as a witness (3rd son). It also leaves 'my brethren' 20s. tokens. Nicholas's lands lay in Bovey Tracey (the park of Bovey Tracey and the grist mills there), his mother's family's parish. So a primary record now shows the sibling set that Vivian puts under John of Holcombe, but it does not name their father. The father-son link stays a compiled statement (Vivian, Westcote). ¶6a

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

2 on the grade · 3 open questions

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Speculative, graded on his link to John Eveleigh: Foster says only that the Principal of Hart Hall was 'probably 2s. John, of Holcombe'. The Heverly family history, following Vivian's Visitations of Devon, places him as a son of the second marriage. ¶2a

Upgrade (Speculative to Probable), graded on his link to his son John Eveleigh. No primary record names the Principal of Hart Hall as his son, so this is not Confirmed. But three independent pieces of evidence now support the Heverly family history (from Vivian's Visitations of Devon, 1895, vol. 1, p. 336), which places the Principal among John of Holcombe's sons and gives his wife as Prudence, daughter of Dr Robert Barnes. (1) Foster: the Principal matriculated at Exeter College, the Devon college, with the entry dated 9 Dec 1575, aged 16, 'of Devon, gent.'. A Richard Eveleighe, 'of Devon, gent.', matriculated in the same week (3 Dec 1575, aged 19) and entered the Middle Temple in 1577 as a son of 'John, of Ottery, Devon'. That Richard is not listed among John of Holcombe's sons in the Heverly account, so the two matriculations are only suggestive. (2) The Middle Temple admissions printed by Foster call George (1574) the '2s.' and Nicholas (1598) the '4s.' of 'John, late of Holcombe, Devon, esq., deceased'. That is a family of several sons sent to Oxford and the Inns at exactly this time. (3) The Ottery St Mary register (Tapley-Soper's printed edition, quoted in Heverly's footnote 59) has 'Rebecca, dau. of John and Mildred Eveleigh, bapt. Dec. 23, 1635', with further Eveleigh baptisms of Margaret and Prudence (1643) and Jane (1645). The Principal's son, Dean John Eveleigh, married Mildred Caldwell and had daughters Rebecca and Jane, and his mother was Prudence. So the Dean's family apparently baptised children in the Holcombe family's own parish, probably as refugees after the 1641 rising in Munster. Heverly gives these baptisms to a different John Eveleigh, a clergyman with a wife Frances, but notes that the 1635 entry 'shows a different wife'. Foster's own wording stays 'probably 2s.', and the Vivian page itself was not read: no free scan was found on archive.org (only the 1620 visitation, Harleian Society vol. 6, which has no Eveleigh of Holcombe pedigree). Added. ¶3

Added. ¶4
