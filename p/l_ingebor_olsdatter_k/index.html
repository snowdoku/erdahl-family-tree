About the family

Named as Rangdi's mother in the 1813 baptism. Also mother of Ole (1817; recorded as 'Ingebor Olevsd.') and Kari (1826; recorded as 'Ingeborg Olufsdatter'). ¶1

Children (more found): Knud, baptised early 1808 (month read as Feb[?]), when she and Sølve Knudsen lived at Sata, and Rangdi ('Landie'), baptised on the 8th of Oct or Nov 1813 at Kanerud. Her patronymic is written 'Olefsd.' in 1808 and 'Olsd.' in 1813. Ål ministerialbok 1807-1814 (source 8546). ¶5

Parents: not identified. She was at Sata when she married in 1807 and her first son had Sata godparents, but no Ingebor Olsdatter lived at Sata in the 1801 census, and no record names her father. ¶6

Life story

Marriage: as 'Pigen Ingebor Olefsd: Sata' (the maiden Ingebor Olefsdatter of Sata) she married the bachelor Sølve Knudsen of Sando in Ål on 28 Apr 1807. The register gives no age and no father; her patronymic is written Olefsdatter here, Olevsdatter in 1817 and Olufsdatter in 1826, all forms of Olsdatter (daughter of Ole). ¶2
