About the family

Second husband of Barbro Knudsdatter of Fodnes nordre, Svenes, Aurdal; living as widower at her estate of 9 Oct 1708 (index card 3243,; Hadeland, Land og Valdres skifteprotokoll 1704-1709, s. 458b). Her children listed after the line drawn under the first three (the first-marriage children) are Thommes 12, Knud 7, Ole 5, Erich 6 months, Ingeleff 16, Bergite 14 and Siri 9. Probable father of Tommes Uldrichsen of Fodnes nordre (the farmer of 1740-45): same farm, the patronymic Uldrichsen, and Thommes (b. about 1696) bears Uldrich Thommesen's father's name. A Knud Uldrichsen, probably the Knud of 1708, appears as a debtor in the 1740 Fodnes estate (s. 427). No record states the link outright. Birth inferred from the eldest presumed child (Ingeleff, about 1692). ¶1

Life story

Bygdebok (Svenes vol., Fodnes gnr. 67 gardpart 2, canvases 415, 420): 'Ulrik', b. about 1652, married Barbro Knutsdotter (of gnr. 50, 1668-1708), widow of Ola Olsson of Vestre Slidre; the children listed (canvas 420) are Berit about 1694 (m. 1726 Erik...), a son 1695 brukar (Tommes, d. 1750), Siri 1698 (m. 1726 Embrik Knutsson), Knut 1701, a son 1703 (d. 1778, 'Lisbet-Ola', soldier) and Erik 1708, which matches the 1708 probate. The 1664 holder of gardpart 2 was a '[..] Ulriksson' (forename not rebuilt; it is not Tomas, Tord or Tormod under any spelling queried), so the book does not give his father here in readable form. ¶3a

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: the 1740 Fodnes estate of Kiersti Johansdatter, wife of Tommes Uldrichsen (Hadeland, Land og Valdres skifteprotokoll 1737-1743, DA 25286, p. 428, view 226), recites that the widower's odel in Nordre Fodnes came to him by the skifte of 9 October 1708 after his father 'Uldrich Tomes[en]', and names his brothers Knud, Ole and Erich as co-heirs. These are exactly the children Knud 7, Ole 5 and Erich (6 months) beside 'Thommes 12' on the 9 Oct 1708 estate of Barbro Knudsdatter, wife of Uldrich Thommesen (card DA 39948 no. 3243, protocol 1704-09 s. 458b). So the Tommes Uldrichsen of 1740 (see his page) is the Thommes of 1708, and Uldrich Thommesen is his father. The p. 428 hand is difficult: the date, the farm and the brothers' names are clear; some connecting words are not read. ¶2
