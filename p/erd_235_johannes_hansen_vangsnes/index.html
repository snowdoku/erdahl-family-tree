How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

2 on the grade · 1 alternative set aside · 1 open question

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Graded Probable on his link to his son Peder Johannessen Sværen (see his page). The chain, all from primary records: (1) the 1666 vicar's census of Vangsnes sogn (35519 v168, p. 310), farm Vangsnes, has 'Johannes bruger', 56, with sons Anders 16, Ola 9 and Christen 6; the 1664 list (v150, p. 277) calls him 'Johannes Hans[en]' and has his son '14 Anders Johans[en]'. (2) The probate after Christen Johansen at Vangsnes, 4 Jul 1718 (2b p. 539a, 24156 v223-224), names as his heirs his full brothers 'Hermund og Peder Johans Sønner' and the half-siblings Hans, Anders, Brithe and Gyri Johansen/-datter, so Christen (6 in 1666) and Anders (16 in 1666) appear together at both ends as brother and half-brother: a pair match on the same farm, which ties the 1718 sibling set to this Johannes (the other Johannes at Vangsnes in 1666, aged 60, had the sons Lauritz 18 and Marcus 9 and is Johannes Markusson of the bygdebok). Johannes thus married twice: Anders, Hans, Brithe and Gyri by the first wife; Christen, Hermund and Peder by the second (neither wife is named). Peder (b. 1669 by the 1701 census) was born after the 1666 list. (3) The tree's Peder: the Leikanger register (11564 c10) has the marriage at Tjugum 25 Jun 1692 of 'Peder Johans[en] Vangnes' with a daughter of Anfind of Sværen, and the 1701 census has 'Peder Johansen', 32, bonde at Sværen; he named his sons Johannes and Christen (Christen b. 1718, the year his brother Christen died). ¶1

Why only Probable: the 1718 probate gives the brother Peder no farm, so his identity with Peder Johansen of Sværen rests on name, patronymic, origin (the 1692 marriage says Vangsnes) and naming, across farms (audit 4). ¶2a

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Rival: the Vik bygdebok H. 5 (canvas 9) tentatively ('Kanskje') gives 'Per 1668, som kom til Sværen' to the OTHER Johannes, Johannes Markusson (1606-1675); no record supports that, and the 1718 probate shows a Peder Johansen of Vangsnes who belongs to this Johannes. Contradiction to keep in view: Peder's wife's 1743 probate calls him 'Peder Hansen Sveren' (see his notes). The name Hansen may echo this Johannes Hansen, but that is not proved. ¶2b

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Test record: a probate or deed after Peder's death that lists land 'udi Vangsnes' among the Sværen family's property (the 18 merker smør Peder Johansen inherited from his brother Christen in 1718), e.g. the probate after his son Johannes Pedersen, Sværen (Ytre Sogn register 1772-1776) or Christen Pedersen, Sværen (1783-1786); or the Ytre Sogn tingbok 1718-20 for a sale of that land by 'Peder Johansen Sværen'. Either would tie the Sværen man to the 1718 heir and so to Johannes. ¶3
