About the family

Widow of Peder Erichsen of Tuntland, Hjelmeland: the probate after him held 27 Sep 1725 (Ryfylke D 4, DA 15920, fol. 246b, scan) names 'den efterlevende Hustru Anna Daniels Datter' on one side and six children on the other, the eldest being Erich Pedersen. She was alive on 27 Sep 1725. Graded Speculative as the mother of Erich Pedersen: the lines read do not say whether the six children were hers or children of an earlier marriage of Peder's, and Erich was already of age (born before 1705) in 1725. Added. ¶1

Life story

Preamble re-read, D 4 fol. 246b: 'Anno 1725 d. 27 Septembr.' at 'Tundtland udi Hielmelands [Skibrede]' after 'den Sl. Mand Peder Erichsen', between 'hans efterlevende Hustrue Anna Daniels Datter paa den ene side og deris [?Echteskabs] sammen avlede 6 Børn paa den anden side, som er 1 Søn og 5 Døttre, nemlig Erich Pedersen, [?3x] Aar myndig; Anna Pedersdatter den ældre, gift med Ole [?Gunnersen/Gudmundsen], boende paa [?Eckersvold] gaard i Hielmelands Skibrede; Britta Pedersdatter gift med Peder [?Braaten]; Ingre [Peders]datter gift med [?] [?Eidrevsen/Andersen]; Anna Pedersdatter den yngre gift med [?Rasmus] Osmundsen, boende paa [?Grane] gaard; og Kari Pedersdatter ugift'. So there was only ONE son (the earlier reading of a 'second son' was wrong; the 'Broderlod' in the division is Erich's own son's share, the double share of a brother), and the six children are 'deris sammen avlede', i.e. children of Peder Erichsen AND Anna Danielsdatter together. ¶2

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Upgrade: Speculative to Probable, graded on her link to Erich Pedersen. The 1725 probate (D 4, DA 15920, fol. 246b) calls the six children, the first being Erich Pedersen, the children begotten together ('sammen avlede') by Peder Erichsen and his widow Anna Danielsdatter, so she was Erich's mother, not a stepmother. It stays Probable, not Confirmed, only because the identity of this 1725 Erich (the sole son, of age, holding houses and land on Tuntland) with the Erich Pedersen of Tuntland who died before 31 Jan 1742 rests on name, farm and land across 17 years, the same basis as her husband's grade. No rival Erich Pedersen on Tuntland is known; Erich was the only son. ¶3

Upgrade (regrade 3): Probable to Confirmed as mother of Erich Pedersen of Tuntland (see his page); graded on her link to Erich Pedersen. Naming record: the 27 Sep 1725 Tuntland probate after Peder Erichsen (D 4, DA 15920, fol. 246b) divides the estate between her, 'hans efterlevende Hustrue Anna Daniels Datter', and 'deris... sammen avlede 6 Børn', the only son being Erich Pedersen, so it names her as his mother, not a stepmother. Continuity as for her husband: Erich, the only son, holding houses and land on Tuntland in 1725, is the Erich Pedersen who died on Tuntland by 31 Jan 1742 leaving real property there to his son Peder, 12 (D 9 fol. 25b, 118a); no named rival. ¶8a

Searches that found nothing 4

Search note on her father: the bailiff's census of 10 May 1664 for Hjelmeland skipreide (DA 35571, pp. 57-65, viewers 36-40, read on the scan) lists no Daniel as farmer, son or servant on any Hjelmeland farm. In the 1701 manntall for Ryfylke (DA 38452, index) the Daniels old enough to be her father (born before about 1645) are Daniel Jacobsen (b. about 1629, husmann under Øvre Vigedal), Daniel Knudsen (b. about 1630, opsidder on 'Indre Eye'), Daniel Olsen (b. about 1638, Holte) and Daniel Larsen (b. about 1640, Herbache); none is on a Hjelmeland farm near Tuntland in the index order (Tuntland is pc...5119598; Indre Eye is pc...5117712, a different parish). No candidate is added. ¶4a

Search note: the Ryfylke probate card index 1682-1700 (DA 39935) was read card by card (see sources for the range) for a deceased Daniel or a Danielsdatter widow with an heir Anna married to Peder Erichsen. Only two Daniels appear as deceased: (1) Daniel Torbjørnsen, Aaboe in Sauda, 3 Jun 1699 (fol. 154b), heirs Marite and Birgitte, minors under their father's brother Halle Østhus; not her. (2) Daniel Olsen, Føre in Hjelmeland, 29 Mar 1700 (fol. 156b). The protocol entry (D 3, DA 15919, fol. 156b, scan read) names the widow Sidselle Olsdatter and 'deres tilsammen avlede børn': Olle, noch Olle, Jonas and Rasmus Danielsønner, and Anne, Ragnilde, Siri and Sidselle Danielsdøttre, with guardians (Olle Fister and Osmund...). This Anne is a child of Sidselle and is listed without a husband, whereas Anna Danielsdatter was married to Peder Erichsen and had a son Erich by about 1696; so she is not this Anne. Anna's father probably died before 1682, outside the card index.
