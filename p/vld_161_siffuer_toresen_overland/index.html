Life story

Bygdebok support: Gards- og ættesoge for Gjemnes, bind 10, canvas 424, Øverland: 'Brukar 3: Sivert Tørrisson Øverland [var] brukar frå 1686 til 1709. Han [er] truleg fødd [i] 1647, [for] han [er i] manntalet [i] 1701 54 år gamal. Han hadde [desse] barn: Knut, fødd ca. 1682..., 2. Nils, fødd ca. 1683. Sjå brukar 4, 3. Tørris, fødd ca. 1689.' Brukar 4 is Nils Sivertson Øverland (1710-34), who died in 1748 aged 68 and was married to Helga Olsdotter (b. about 1678, d. 1742 aged 64), the father of Sivert (b. about 1706). On canvases 423-424 Sivert is the child (b. about 1647) of Tørris Erikson (brukar 2, about 1644-1683, b. about 1605) by his first wife, whose name is unknown ('Gift første gong [med] ei... av ukjent [namn?]'). Tørris's second wife was Berit Pedersdotter (d. 1669; her mother was Anne Knutsdotter), with children Peder (b. about 1662), Kari and Lars (1669 probate). This agrees with reading of the 1701 and 1666 lists; it is a published secondary source, so the grade stays Probable. ¶5

Farmer (opsidder or leilending) at Overland (Offuerland), Gjemnes tinglag, Kvernes parish, Nordmøre, in 1701. His age is given as 54 in 1701 (born about 1647) but as 10 in the 1666 vicar's census (born about 1656); ages in these lists are rough. ¶1

1701 manntall (read in the Digitalarkivet transcription, Census (manntall) 1701, no. 11: Nordmøre fogderi and Romsdal fogderi, p. 62, record pc00000005073164): farm 'Offuerland' (between Søeuig and Krechuig, in the Gjemnes run of farms that also holds Giemnes, Høgset, Grønset and Stoche on p. 61): 'Siffuer Toresen', opsidder eller leilending, b. 1647; under him as 'Sønner' (sons) 'Niels Sifuers.' b. 1683 and 'Tøris Sifuers.' b. 1689; then 'Knudt Sifuers.' b. 1682, listed as a separate opsidder on the farm (probably another son who already held part of it). The census lists males only. ¶2

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Graded on his link to his son Niels Siursen Overland: Probable. The 1701 census names 'Niels Sifuers.' (b. about 1683) as Siffuer Toresen's son at Overland. Niels Siursen, farmer at Overland, was a widower in Sep 1742 with children aged 36 to 31 (24642 fol. 547b), which fits a man born about 1683. Same farm, same name and patronymic, and the 1701 list has no other Niels on Overland, but the two records are 41 years apart with nothing read in between, so the identification is Probable, not Confirmed. Niels's eldest son was named Siur (b. about 1706), after this man: Siffuer, Siur and Sivert are forms of one name (Sigurd). ¶3

Searches that found nothing 1

No probate for him is in the Nordmøre probate card index 1668-1746 (source 77795): the Øverland, Gjemnes cards (views 4625-4629) are 1669 Berit Pedersdatter, 1738 Ane Pedersdatter and 1742 Helge Olsdatter only. He probably died after 1746, or his estate was not probated. ¶4
