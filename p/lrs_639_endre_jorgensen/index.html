Life story

Bygdebok (Botnen gnr. 65 bnr. 78, canvas 884): under the wife of Eilef [Olson] of Botnen, '... Endresdtr. døde 1739 82 år' (b. 1657): 'Foreldre... Endre Jørgenson... 1701... Agate Andersdtr.', with a division 'mellom' Endre Jørgenson's widow 'Agate Andersdtr.' and children, among them Johannes Endreson, Kari Endresdtr. (died before the division) and Gjertrud Endresdtr. ¶1

Probate index (Ytre Sogn, Digitalarkivet source 27): 'Endre Jørgensen', residence Saagnes, tinglag Lavik, 1700-1701, skifteprotokoll 1b p. 343b (pa00000000519930). The heirs are not indexed; reading 1b p. 343b would test whether a daughter Maritte, wife of Eilef Olsen of Botnen, is named. ¶2

Probate, Ytre Sogn 1b fol. 343b (lower half of the left page of view 128): 'Sognes udi Ladwig Skibrede. Den 2 Juny 1701' the court met 'at skifte efter Sal. Endre Jørgensen Sognes, imellem hans efterlevende danne Qvinde Aagaatha Anders datter, paa den ene, og deris tilsammen avlede børn Nemblig Johannes Endresen Sognes, Mar[...] Eillefs Botnes, Sal. Lars Ødegaards Søn Niels, Ingerborg og Giertrud Botolfs [Alveien?], paa den anden Side'. The daughter's forename runs into the binding after 'Mar' (only 'Mar' is visible) and the next line begins 'Eillefs Botnes', i.e. Mar[itte], Eilef's [wife], of Botnes. Estate 54 rd 3 ort, including land in Brendingsdal. Read from the scan (crops, en1-en4.jpg). ¶4

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 4

Graded on his link to Maritte Endresdatter (see her page). ¶3a

Graded Probable: a bygdebok is a compiled secondary source and the passage was rebuilt from word boxes with gaps. ¶3b

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed, graded on the link to the daughter Maritte Endresdatter. The 1701 probate (a primary record, read) names a daughter 'Mar[...]' of Endre Jørgensen and Agatha Andersdatter as of 'Eillefs Botnes' (Eilef's, of Botnen). Continuity: Eilef Olsen held Botnen from the 1690s to his death (bygdebok canvas 884; probate 20 Jun 1730, 3b fol. 433b-434a), and that 1730 probate names his widow 'Maritte Endres datter' as the mother of his children, so the 'Mar[...]' of 1701 is the same woman by husband, farm and patronymic. ¶5a

Audit 8 (1-2 Nov 2026): Confirmed kept, graded on his link to Maritte Endresdatter (see her page). This rules on the AUDIT-QUEUE flag about the forename cut off after 'Mar' in the 1701 probate. The forename is not needed for the identification, and it is not a contradiction, because no other reading competes with it. The heir is a jointly begotten child of Endre and Aagaatha, the wife of Eilef of Botnes. In 1730 Eilef Olsen of Botnen's widow is 'Maritte Endres datter', and her children with him include Lisbeth (born about 1697), so she was Eilef's wife before 1701. Husband, farm, patronymic and the first letters of the forename all agree, and no rival Eilef on Botnen is named. The spouse makes the 1701-1730 bridge, as audit 5 allowed. ¶6

Alternatives considered and set aside 2

Rival: none (no other Eilef on Botnen and no other Mar- Endresdatter is known). ¶5b

Caveat: the forename is cut by the gutter after 'Mar'. ¶5c
