About the family

David Erdahl's maternal great-grandmother. Mother of Kathryn, Stewart and Charles, named as such on her own gravestone: 'EIDEM / MAYME O. 1906-2002 / PALMER G. 1904-1990 / PARENTS OF KATHRYN, STEWART, CHARLES', in Zoar Lutheran Cemetery, Antelope Valley Township, Deuel County, South Dakota. Deuel County is in South Dakota and she was born in South Dakota, so the family stone stands in her home county rather than in the Minnesota township where the family farmed. ¶1

Her middle initial explained: she was Mamie OLENE, named for her mother Anna Olene (Satter) Eidem, in the ordinary Norwegian-American way. Minnesota and California records later rendered her first name as Mayme. ¶21

Her father's farm was on Brule Creek, near Elk Point, Union County, South Dakota, and he had settled it himself as a Norwegian immigrant. The family's church was St Paul Lutheran, rural Elk Point (31903 475th Avenue, Elk Point, South Dakota), whose churchyard Myrtle's obituary calls 'her family's burial ground for three generations and 150 years'. Mayme is the one of the six children who is not buried in it; she lies at Rose Hills Memorial Park, Whittier, California, with her name also cut on the family stone at Zoar Lutheran in Deuel County. ¶22

Her middle initial: her mother was Anna OLENE Satter, and the 'O.' that Mayme used all her adult life is her mother's middle name, in the ordinary Norwegian-American pattern of naming a daughter for her mother. It is not, as the American convention for a married woman would suggest, a maiden surname kept as an initial - which is what had made researchers look for an O- surname. ¶32

Family at her death: she was survived by three children, two sisters, eight grandchildren and nine great-grandchildren, and had been preceded in death by her husband, a sister and two brothers (obituary, 2002). The two surviving sisters are Selma (Nelson) and Myrtle (Overdahl); the sister and brothers who died before her are Bertha (Leer), Samuel and Amos - which agrees with the six children of Severt S. Eidem named in his 1940 heirs notice. ¶54

Life story

Birth: 18 December 1906 in South Dakota (birthplace from the 1950 census, date from the Social Security Death Master File). Her Social Security number, [number withheld], was issued in Minnesota. ¶7

Social Security, retirement claim: MAYME O. EIDEM, born 18 Dec 1906, filed a life claim on 9 September 1971 at the Social Security Administration field office in PASADENA, CALIFORNIA — the same field office at which her husband Palmer had filed two years earlier. She was 64. A life claim at that age is a retirement or wife's benefit, so she had either her own covered work record or a claim on his. NARA AAD, NUMIDENT Claim Files 1936-2007 (Last Names D-G), rid 1352267. Her account was issued in Minnesota. ¶12

Social Security, death record: MAYME O EIDEM, b. 18 Dec 1906, d. 16 Jan 2002, residence ZIP [withheld], which is ARCADIA, Los Angeles County, California. 'PROOF OF DEATH: P' = Proven; source of death 'FO' = reported by a Social Security field office; posted 17 Apr 2002; one death posting. NARA AAD, NUMIDENT Death Files (Last Names E-G), rid 294523. She outlived Palmer by eleven years and died four miles from where he died. ¶13

Clean negative on the single most wanted record in this project, with its structural reason. Mayme has NO record in the NUMIDENT Application (SS-5) file — she appears only in the Death file and the Claim file, neither of which carries parents' names. I enumerated every one of the 291 Eidem SS-5 application records across all nine letter-range file units of that series and she is not among them. So the NUMIDENT route to her parents is closed, not merely unsearched: NARA's own scope note says the Application extract 'does not contain records of all Social Security Number applications'. Her maiden name was recovered instead from her South Dakota birth certificate by the sister agent sd-mayme (MAMIE EIDEM, b. 18 Dec 1906 Union County SD, father SIVERT EIDEM, mother ANNA SATTER), and nothing in the federal indexes contradicts it. ¶14

Corroboration for the Union County, South Dakota Eidems that sd-mayme's birth certificate points at: the NUMIDENT SS-5 file contains a distinct colony of Eidems born at ELK POINT, UNION COUNTY, SOUTH DAKOTA — Einer Eidem (b. 16 Sep 1884 Elk Point, father OLE S EIDEM, mother MARIT STAVNAS), Anna Regenal Eidem (b. 19 Apr 1889 Elk Point, father OLE S EIDEM, mother rendered 'MARY E STARNES', evidently the same Marit Stavnas), Blanche Carmillia Eidem (b. 30 Sep 1908, father GILBERT J EIDEM, mother MARY OLSON), Bernice (b. 5 Mar 1920, same parents), James Orlyn Eidem (b. 17 Feb 1921, father JOSEPH T EIDEM, mother MAE B PAULSON), Frances A. Eidem (b. 19 Apr 1922, same parents) and Jerald Herbert Eidem (b. 2 Nov 1931, father HERBERT J EIDEM, mother JOSEPHINE A WOLLESEN). Elk Point is the county seat of Union County. A 'Sivert Eidem' is not among the named fathers, but his generation would mostly pre-date the SS-5 parent fields; OLE S. EIDEM is the one candidate whose middle initial could stand for Sivert. This is where to look for Mayme's family. ¶15

Social Security record, new in wave 3: 'EIDEM, MAYME O was born 18 December 1906, was assigned Social Security number [number withheld] (indicating Minnesota), and died 16 January 2002, while residing in ZIP [withheld]. The Social Security field office at PASADENA CA received the claim 9 September 1971.' ZIP [withheld] is Arcadia - the town in which her granddaughters Brigetta, Inga and Sigrid went to school. A widow of ninety-five at an Arcadia address is very probably living at or beside her daughter Kathryn Hayter's house, so her death place can be narrowed from 'Los Angeles County' to Arcadia. Her own Social Security number was issued in Minnesota and her claim was filed at the Pasadena office in September 1971, two years after her husband's. ¶16

The Social Security record confirms her birth date of 18 December 1906 independently of the gravestone, but like every other source it gives no maiden name: the Death Master File has no such field and no SS-5 application for her has been released, so the mother's-maiden-name gap on her remains open. ¶17

MAIDEN NAME SOLVED, 11 Oct 2026: it was EIDEM. Mayme O. Eidem was born an Eidem and married an Eidem, which is exactly why ninety years of looking for a different surname found nothing - her surname never changed. She was born MAMIE EIDEM on 18 December 1906 in Union County, South Dakota, the daughter of SIVERT EIDEM and ANNA (SATTER) EIDEM of Brule Creek near Elk Point, and she married Palmer Gilbert Eidem of Lac qui Parle County, Minnesota, who carried the same Norwegian farm name from the same parish of Selbu. ¶18

The record: South Dakota Department of Health index of birth records over 100 years old (published under SDCL 34-25-8), state file number 14515, filed 24 December 1906 - six days after the birth, so a contemporaneous original registration and not a delayed filing under a later name. It reads: MAMIE EIDEM, female, born 18 December 1906, Union County, mother's name ANNA SATTER, father's name SIVERT EIDEM. She is the only person named Mamie or Mayme born anywhere in South Dakota on that date; her own gravestone, Find a Grave 144072623 and the California Death Index all independently fix her birth at 18 December 1906 in South Dakota. ¶19

Independent confirmation from a second, wholly different kind of record: the obituary of her youngest sister MYRTLE 'SADIE' (EIDEM) OVERDAHL, who died at St Cloud, Minnesota on 3 October 2013 aged 102, says Myrtle 'was born January 4, 1911 near Elk Point, SD to Sivert and Anna (Satter) Eidem' and that she 'was preceded in death by her parents, all of her five siblings; Bertha Leer, Samuel Eidem, Selma Nelson, MAYME EIDEM, and Amos Eidem, and her husband.' The sister is named with the surname Eidem, which is only possible for a married sister if she married a man of the same name. A birth registration and an obituary 107 years apart therefore agree. ¶20

Her five brothers and sisters: Bertha Agnes (Eidem) Leer 1900-1957; Samuel Gerhard Eidem 6 Oct 1901 - 20 May 1997; Selma (Eidem) Nelson; Mayme herself 1906-2002; Amos Eidem 15 Jan 1909 - 7 Jun 1991; Myrtle 'Sadie' (Eidem) Overdahl 4 Jan 1911 - 3 Oct 2013. The case for this identification 2

Identification: the birth date on the certificate, 18 December 1906, matches to the day the date carried by her Social Security record (SSN [number withheld]) and by her gravestone, and the 1950 census of Mehurin Township, Lac qui Parle County, Minnesota gives her as born in South Dakota. An enumeration of every given-name variant in the whole South Dakota birth index - Mamie (256 records), Mayme (113), May (896), Mae (393), Marie (1,920) and Maime (3), 3,581 rows in all - produced exactly one person in the state born on 18 December 1906 under any spelling of her name, and that is this certificate. A separate enumeration of all 41 South Dakota births registered on 18 December 1906, whatever the surname, contains no other candidate. ¶30

The identification is clinched independently by the obituary of her youngest sister Myrtle 'Sadie' (Eidem) Overdahl, who died at St. Cloud, Minnesota on 3 October 2013 aged 102. It states that Myrtle 'was born January 4, 1911 near Elk Point, SD to Sivert and Anna (Satter) Eidem' and that she was preceded in death by her parents and by all five of her siblings - 'Bertha Leer, Samuel Eidem, Selma Nelson, Mayme Eidem, and Amos Eidem'. Mayme is named there under the surname Eidem, not a married name, and she died in 2002, eleven years before Myrtle. ¶31

Correction: her middle name was OVIDIA, not Olene. Her obituary prints it in full - 'Mayme Ovidia Eidem' (Leader-Courier, Elk Point, 7 February 2002, page 4) - and the 1930 census of Brule township, read independently and seventy years earlier, indexes her as 'Minnie Ovedia Eidem', which an earlier note in this record explained away as a misreading of 'Mamie Olene'. 'Mamie', the spelling of her birth certificate, the 1910-1925 censuses and the 1940 heirs notice, is kept as her nickname. ¶47

Correction: earlier notes in this record (and on her memorial-stone entry) still say that her maiden name is 'not established' or 'remains unknown'. That is superseded. Her birth surname was EIDEM: her South Dakota birth certificate, state file number 14515, registers 'MAMIE EIDEM', born 18 December 1906 in Union County, father Sivert Eidem, mother Anna Satter; and her own obituary (Leader-Courier, Elk Point, 7 February 2002, page 4) says she was born on that date 'to Severt S. Eidem and Anna O. Eidem of Elk Point'. She was born an Eidem and married an Eidem, Palmer G. Eidem, in 1940. ¶55

Alternatives considered and set aside 2

MAIDEN NAME NOT ESTABLISHED. She is entered here under her married surname because nothing retrieved gives her birth name. She died in 2002, five years after the California Death Index closes, and South Dakota's free pre-1925 birth search was unreachable. ¶6

Caveat, stated plainly: the 1950 census establishes that Mayme was the wife of the head of household and that Kathryn, Stewart and Charles were the head's children. It does NOT establish that Mayme bore them. If Palmer married twice, she could be their stepmother. Their ages — Mayme would have been 36, 38 and 40 at the three births — are compatible with her being their mother but are late enough to be worth a second look. ¶10

Searches that found nothing 2

Not found, and the search is now closed at this end: Rose Hills publishes no obituary text, no next of kin and no maiden name for her - it prints a biography only for recent deaths - so her maiden name cannot be got from the cemetery. Her 2002 death fell five years after the California Death Index closes to the public, and California sealed its vital-record indices outright from 1 January 2003 under SB 1614 (Health and Safety Code section 102230). Within Los Angeles County there is no further free record of her. ¶27

Negative, documented: Palmer G. Eidem and Mayme O. were not married in Lac qui Parle County, Minnesota. The Minnesota Official Marriage System indexes twelve Eidem marriages for that county, from 1893 to 1987, including ones in 1935, 1940, 1942 and 1946, so the register is digitised for exactly the years in which this couple must have married - their eldest child Kathryn was born about December 1942. There is no Palmer Eidem in it, and none anywhere in Minnesota: the only two Palmer Eidem marriages in the whole system are in Hennepin County, in 1923 and 1973, and neither is him. Her maiden name is therefore not recoverable from Minnesota records. ¶28a
