Life story

1666 vicar's census: at 'Vggedall', Borgund, p. 336, 'Peder' aged 16 is listed in the sons column under the holder 'Matts' aged 70 (pc00000001599303). This is very probably Peder Madsen, which supplies his father Mads (added as Mads (Matts) Uggedal, Probable). ¶5a

Manntall 1701 (index): Peder, 62, of Uggedall, Borgund, 'Opsider eller Leilending, Legsmand' (leaseholder; legdsmann, a farmer in a military levy district), remark 'verit Soldat' (has been a soldier), with son Mads Pers., 16 (born about 1685). A 'Peder Madtzen' of Uggedal was a witness (trulovar) at a Borgund engagement in 1699 (pv00000010160798), which supplies the patronymic if the same man. ¶1

1704 list of marines (index): Uggedal, Peder, bonde, 65 (b. about 1639), with son Mads Pedersen, 24 (b. about 1680). Confirms his age and a son named for his father Mads. Daughters are not listed in this roll, so his grade (as father of Marthe Pedersdatter) is unchanged. ¶6

Contrary hint, recorded for balance: at the baptism of Peder son of Ole Knudsen Uggedal, 14 Sep 1721 (15995 c158, Dom. 14 p. Trin.), the godparents were Rasmus Knudsen Borge[?], 'Mads Andersen[?] Uggedal', Peder Størchersen tjener paa Bræse[?], Henriche Olsdatter Hansund (carried the child) and Ragnille Ingebrigtsdatter Engeset[?]. The patronymic reads Andersen, not Pedersen, so this Mads may be a different man. At twins' baptism of Thore Andersen Halsnesholmen, 20 Apr 1720 (c152), 'Marithe Pedersdatter Uggedal', 'Mads Uggedal' and 'Ole Knudsen Uggedal' stood together as godparents. ¶4

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

2 on the grade · 1 correction · 1 open question

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Speculative father of Marthe Pedersdatter (b. about 1688, wife of Ole Knudsen of Uggedal): same farm, right patronymic and age; no record names her father. ¶2a

Upgrade: Speculative to Probable, on the Borgund baptism scans read tonight. Borgund ministerialbok 1698-1739 (15995) canvas c128, right page, 20 Oct 1715 (18 p. Trin.): 'Ole Knudsen Uggedal en datter til daaben', godparents 'Peder Madsen Uggedal', Siver[?] Pedersen Sporre[?], Margrete i Lerrgaard[?] (carried the child), Synneve Olsdatter Uggedal, Jertrud Iversdatter tjener i Lortgaarden[?]. The daughter's name is not written (the index guessed 'Barbro??'). The first godfather of Ole Knudsen's daughter is a Peder Madsen of Uggedal, which fits his being the child's maternal grandfather (Ole's wife was Martha Pedersdatter of Uggedal). Same page, left: Iver Olsen Solevaag's son Peder, godparents 'Peder Uggedal, Mads Persen ibm.' and others, which matches the 1701 manntall pair Peder (62) and son Mads Pers. (16) of Uggedal and supplies the patronymic Madsen. Still Probable, not Confirmed: no record states that Martha was his daughter. ¶3

Corrections 1

Correction: the 1666 age (16, born about 1650) conflicts with the 1701 age (62, born about 1639); birth widened to BET 1639 AND 1650. A birth about 1650 fits his children (son Mads about 1685, daughter Marthe about 1688) better. His own grade (on his link to Marthe Pedersdatter) is unchanged. ¶5b
