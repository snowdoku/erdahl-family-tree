About the family

Father found (Probable): the 1742 probate after Erich Pedersen of Tuntland (Ryfylke D 9, fol. 118a, no. 3, held 31 Jan 1742) names as sole heir 'den afdødes Søn Peder Erichsen, 12 aar gammel'. This gives him a birth about 1730 on Tuntland. He inherited Tuntland land and was holder there in 1762; his eldest son was named Erich. ¶8

Mother link Probable (bygdebok). Rebuilt from nb.no content-search word boxes (harvests and ar2.json, 3,011-word vocabulary); dates from the rebuild are given as ABT. ¶10a

Holder (opsidder) of a share of Tuntland, Hjelmeland, in the 1762 ekstraskatt (extra tax), listed with his wife Marthe Steensdatter (index). ¶1

Probate of his first wife (card): Ryfylke probate card index 1774-1788 (DA 39936, view 441), Tuntland i Hjelmeland, protocol side 396b, 16 Nov 1785: deceased Marthe Steensdatter, wife of Peder Eriksen; children Erik (of age), Sten (married) and Mallene (married). This names him as Mallene's father. Mallene is the 'Mallene Peders Datter Tuntland' who married Niels Olsen of Øye in 1775 (Hjelmeland marriages, p. 240, no. 6). The rest of the chain down to the Madla family is Probable (see Malene Nielsdatter). ¶3

Probate of his first wife read in the protocol: D 7b fol. 296b, 16 Nov 1782; he is named as the surviving husband ('Ectemand Peder Erichsen') and the heirs are 'deres Børn' Erich Pedersen (of age), Steen Pedersen (married) and Mallene Pedersdatter (married). ¶7

Second marriage (card): the same card index (view 442) has a card for Tuntland, protocol side 569b, 1788: deceased Berthe Pedersdatter, wife of Peder Eriksen. Her heirs are her children by an earlier marriage, Anna Gautesdatter (married, at Kvam) and Kari (17). Her estate held 1 løp smør in Tuntland; net estate 175 rd 3 ort 10 s. So he married Berthe after 1785 and was alive in 1788. ¶4

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Probable father of Mallene Pedersdatter (b. about 1752, confirmed from Tuntland in 1768, married Niels Olsen of Øye in 1775), of Erich Pedersen (b. about 1750) and of Steen Pedersen (confirmed 1771), all of Tuntland. He is the only Peder on Tuntland in 1762, and the sons' names fit his patronymic and his wife's. Inferred; no record names him as a parent. Added. ¶2

Corrections 1

Correction: the probate card (DA 39936, view 441) reads 'side 296b' and '1782 16/11', not 396b and 1785; the card-writer's 2 looks like a 5 or 3 (compare '2. Sten' on the same card). Ryfylke skifteprotokoll D 7b (DA 23892) fol. 396b (view 110) holds two unrelated 1785 entries, while fol. 296b (view 9) holds the Tuntland entry, headed 'Anno 1782 den 16de Novbr.'. So his first wife died before 16 Nov 1782, not 1785. ¶6

Searches that found nothing 1

Search note on his mother: the typed register to the Hemnes designations and extracts 1731-1754 (DA 23971, T section) has only one Tuntland probate in those years, after Erick Pedersen, 31 Jan 1742 (fol. 25b and 118). There is no probate for a wife of Erich Pedersen on Tuntland 1731-1754, and the 1742 extract (D 9, fol. 118a, re-read on the scan) names only 'den afdødes Søn Peder Erichsen, 12 aar' and not a widow. So his mother either died before 1731, when no Tuntland probate after a woman is indexed in D 4 either, or survived Erich and remarried; she is still unknown. The 1762 ekstraskatt household (ids pt...482946-482958) has no older Steen or Erich with him, so it gives no in-law. ¶9
