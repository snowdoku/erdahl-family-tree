Life story

Marriage and family: Caroline Zarbock married Johann Tarowsky/Farowsky at Tarutino on 7 September 1851 and had children at Beresina from 1853 (once extracted as 'Zarbock, Julian', 1868). The Wahl family-book index gives her as Karoline Farowske, born 1827, with the remark 'Tarutino', which is probably her place of origin. She was named as his wife at his death in 1881. Born about 1827, she cannot be the mother of Wilhelmine Farowsky (born about 1840) and is at most her stepmother. ¶1

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
