About the family

Probable father of Ane Sivertsdatter (inferred): (1) Ane was married on 20 May 1764 as 'pigen Ane Sivertsdatter Øverland' (16076 view 53, p. 101), so she was an unmarried daughter of the house at Overland; (2) Sivert Nielsen is the only Sivert known at Overland in these years, and his wife stood godmother to Ane's first surviving son in 1767; (3) Ole Sivertsen of Øverland, who was farming there by 1764-65, was guardian of Ane's son Ole in both 1779 and 1785, and Ane named a son Sivert. No record yet names Sivert as her father (no 'Morbroder', no probate of Sivert), so this stays Probable. Graded on his link to Ane Sivertsdatter. ¶2

Children named in the 1762 list: Niels Sivertsen (over 12 in 1762, unmarried, at home) and Ane Sivertsdatter. Ole Sivertsen, married and heading his own household at Overland in 1762, and Petter Sivertsen of Søvig (guardian of Ane's son Sivert in 1779) were very probably also his sons, but the list does not say so. ¶5

Parents found (Probable): Nordmøre skifteprotokoll 24642 (no. 8b, 1741-46), fol. 547b (view 120, left page), No. 64: 'Anno 1742 den 14de Septemb.' a registration was held on the farm 'Overland', belonging to 'Niels Siursen', after his wife 'Helge [Ols]datter', who had died some time before, between her surviving husband on the one side and on the other 'deris sammen avlede Børn', namely the son 'Siur Nielsen 36 aar gammel', 'Berit Nielsdatter 34 aar gl. gift med Peder Christophersen Strand' and 'Marith Nielsdatter 31 aar gammel' [unmarried]; appraisers included Jon Pedersen Overland. The index card (77795 view 4629) gives the same: 'Øverland, Gjemnes. 1742 14/9 Helge Olsdatter. Enkemann: Niels Siursen. Barn: 1) sønnen Siur Nielsen 36 år gl., 2) datteren Berite Nielsdr. 34 år, g.m. Peder Christophersen Strand, 3) Marite Nielsdr. 31 år gl. ugift. Fallitt.' Identification of the 1742 son 'Siur Nielsen' (b. about 1706) with Sivert Nielsen of Overland (1762 extra tax, 1767 godparent): Siur (Sjur) and Sivert are both forms of Sigurd and were used interchangeably for one man in Nordmøre records; same farm, same patronymic, and his age fits a man with a married son (Ole Sivertsen) and unmarried children over 12 (Niels and Ane) in 1762. His eldest known son Niels Sivertsen is named for Niels Siursen. But the two records are 20 years apart, with no record in between yet read, and the name form differs, so these parents are Probable, not Confirmed (graded on their link to Sivert Nielsen Overland). The Overland card series 1669-1746 (77795 views 4625-4629) has no other Niels or Siur. Birth year about 1706 from 'Siur Nielsen 36 aar' in Sep 1742. His sisters: Berit Nielsdatter (b. about 1708, m. Peder Christophersen Strand) and Marith Nielsdatter (b. about 1711). His own grade (on his link to Ane Sivertsdatter) is unchanged. ¶7

His wife: the Overland cards (77795, 1669-1746) show no probate for a wife of Sivert/Siur before 1746, and earlier waves found none in the registers of 24644-24652 (1750-81), so no first wife's death is recorded at Overland; this slightly favours Ane Iversdatter (his wife in 1767) being Ane Sivertsdatter's own mother, but no record says so (see her page stays Speculative). 24643 (1746-50) register not yet read for Overland. ¶8

Children's baptisms (Kvernes 16075): Ane, 21 Sep 1738 (view 7, mother Ane); Niels, 23 Apr 1741 (view 18, mother Ane; this is the Niels Sivertsen of the 1762 tax); Helge, Whit Sunday 2 Jun 1748 (view 52, mother 'Ane Iversd.', named for his mother Helge Olsdatter, d. 1742). With Iver (1744) and Tørris (1745), both died infants. His father Niels Sivertsen was buried as a widower on Good Friday 1748 (view 51). ¶9

Life story

Of Overland, Kvernes parish (Gjemnes tinglag), Nordmøre. Named only as the husband of Ane Iversdatter when she stood godmother to Elling, son of Paul Ellingsen Stokke and Ane Sivertsdatter, on 29 Mar 1767 (16076 p. 122). ¶1

Bygdebok account: Gards- og ættesoge for Gjemnes, bind 10, canvas 425, Øverland brukar 5: Sivert Nilsson, brukar 1734-1760, b. about 1706, d. 1775, m. 1730 Ane Iversdotter Søvik (1707-1776). Children Ole 1735, Ane 1738 (m. 1764 widower Paul Ellingson Stokke), Nils 1741 (d. 1807, m. 1768 Kari Persdotter). The book notes that some take him to be the 'Sjur' recorded at Bremsnes. Secondary source; no grade change. ¶10

Probate searched without success: no Sivert at Overland in the registers of 24644 (1750-55, S pages), 24647 (1762-64, S pages) or 24653 (1787-92, S and A pages; no Ane Iversdatter either). 24654-24655 remain. ¶6

Probate searched without success: no entry for Sivert at Overland in the registers of Nordmøre skifteprotokoller 24645 (1755-60), 24646 (1760-62), 24648 (1766-72), 24649-24651 (to 1781) or 24652 (S and O pages). 24647 (1762-64) and 24653-24655 remain. ¶3

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed, graded on his link to his daughter Ane Sivertsdatter. Record: Nordmøre fogderi, Ekstraskatt 1762, hovedmanntall (source 47939), view 57, Kvernes sogn, Gjemnes tinglag, farm no. 61 Øverland: the second household is 'Sivert Nielss[en] og hustrue', and in the column headed 'Børn over 12 aar og deres Navne' (children over 12 and their names) stand 'Niels Sivertss[en]' and 'Ane Sivertsd[atte]r'; a farm lad Lars Olsen completes the household of five. Before it is a household headed by an Olsen (with the girl Maria Olsdatter) and after it 'Ole Sivertss[en] og hustrue'. So the list names Ane Sivertsdatter as Sivert Nielsen's child. Continuity: two years later, on 20 May 1764, 'pigen Ane Sivertsd[atte]r Øverland' married the widower Paul Ellingsen Stokke (16076 view 53, p. 101): same farm, same name, still unmarried, and the 1762 list shows no other Ane Sivertsdatter and no other Sivert at Overland, so there is no rival. Sivert's wife Ane Iversdatter then stood godmother to Paul and Ane's son Elling in 1767, and Ole Sivertsen Øverland (the next household in 1762) was guardian of Ane's son Ole in 1779. ¶4
