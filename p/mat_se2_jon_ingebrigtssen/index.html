About the family

Probably Paul Eidem's paternal grandfather. 1801 census, Selbu, rural residence 0050 Storevjen, household 06: 'Jon Ingebrigtssen', 37, Mand, HUSMAND MED JORD (cottager with land), with his wife Daardi Olsdatter, 39, and their children Siri (11), Ole (7) and Ingebrigt (2). Storevjen in 1801 was a large multi-family farm of nine or more households. ¶1

Son Ingebrigt's baptism: 'Jon Ingebrictsen Stor Efjen' is the father of Ingebrigt, born 11 February 1799 and baptised 17 February 1799 (Selbu ministerialbok 1757-1801, p. 232, no. 2), with Dordi Olsdatter as mother. Confidence raised from Probable to Confirmed: see Ingebrigt Johnsen for the full argument that this child is Paul Eidem's father. ¶3

Kin: Jon Jonsen of Mebust was a godfather at Ingebrigt's baptism, and Jon Ingebrigtsen Efjen had stood godfather in September 1798 to the child of Jon Jonsen Mebost and Guru Ingebrigtsdatter (same register, canvas 228). The reciprocal sponsorship and Guru's patronymic suggest Guru Ingebrigtsdatter was Jon's sister; this is an inference only. ¶4

Children before 1799 (Selbu ministerialbok 1757-1801): Sigri, baptised 1st Sunday in Advent, 29 November 1789, parents 'Jon Engelbr. Efjen' and 'Dorete O[lsd.]' (canvas 134 right page) - the Siri Jonsdatter, 11, of the 1801 household, born two months after the wedding; and Ole, baptised 18th Sunday after Trinity, 29 September 1793, parents 'Jon Engelbrechtsen Efjen og Doreth Olsdatter', godparents Engelbrict Efjen, Peder Flønes, Halvor Berge, Marite Qvelloe and Mali Baarsgaard (canvas 157 right page) - the Ole Jonssen, 7, of 1801. With Ingebrigt (1799) these are the three children of the 1801 Storevjen household, each baptised to this couple. ¶6

Brothers as adults: the grenadier Ole Engelbrechtsen of Store Evjen, died 17 August 1784 aged 27, and Gunder Engelbrechtsen of Store Evjen, buried March 1789 aged 26, fit Ole of 1758 and Gunnar of 1762. 'Engelbrict Efjen', godfather to Jon's son Ole in 1793, is probably his brother Ingebrigt of 1760 (inference). Guru Ingebrigtsdatter, wife of Jon Jonsen Mebost, remains a possible sister (see the earlier kin note); her baptism, after 1767, is not yet read. ¶10

Life story

His own patronymic shows his father was an Ingebrigt, which is where the Ingebrigt-Jon-Ingebrigt-Jon alternation in this family starts; his great-grandson John Paulsen Eidem, born 1876, carries his name. See Ingebrigt Johnsen for why the link is rated Probable rather than Confirmed. ¶2

Baptism: 'Jon', son of Ingebrigt Bjornsen of Store Evjen and Mali Olsdatter, baptised on 13 January 1765 (Die Eucharistiae), godparents Siur Bjornsen, Thomas Jonsen and Guri Haarsdatter[?]; Selbu ministerialbok 1757-1801, canvas 17 left page (crop). Brothers Ole (1758), Ingebrigt (1760) and Gunnar (1762). Parents linked as Confirmed; reasoning on Ingebrigt (Engelbrecht) Bjørnsen (the farm Store Evjen, the only Jon son of an Ingebrigt in the complete read of 1757-67, his son Ingebrigt of 1799). His 1801 age of 37 is a year more than this baptism gives. Birth date is now 'before 13 January 1765' in place of ABT 1764. ¶8

Marriage: betrothed on the 5th Sunday after Trinity, 12 July 1789, 'Jon Engelbreths. Stor Efjen' and 'Dorete Olsd. Berge' - Jon Engelbrechtsen (Engelbrecht is a form of Ingebrigt) of Store Evjen and Dorete (Dordi) Olsdatter of Berge; forlovere Mogens Christensen of Store Evjen and Engelbrecht Christensen of the same farm (canvas 133 right page). Married on the 16th Sunday after Trinity, 27 September 1789: 'Copul. Jon Engelbr. Stor Efjen & Dorete Olsd. Berge' (canvas 134 left page). Selbu ministerialbok 1757-1801 and the betrothal crop. This replaces the inferred 'before 1790'. ¶5

Probate of his mother: 'Anno 1794 den 14de April' at Stor-Evjen in Selbu tinglag, the estate of the late Mali Olsdatter, shared between the widower Ingebrigt Biørnsen and their children: 1. the son Ole Ingebrigtsen, dead, leaving a son Ole Olsen; 2. 'Sønnen Jon Ingebrigtsen'; 3. the daughter Karen Ingebrigtsdatter, married to Erich Olsen [farm unclear]; 4. the daughter Ranni Ingebrigtsdatter, married to the cottager Jens Thomassen Eidum; 5. the daughter Brynnild Ingebrigtsdatter, 26; 6. the daughter Ingeborg Ingebrigtsdatter. This is a second primary record naming him as the son of Ingebrigt Bjørnsen and Mali Olsdatter (24763 fol. 537b, view 553). ¶13

Death (Probable, from a burial index row): the Selbu burial index (1801-1815) has 'Jon Ingebrictsen', a married man, of Marstad, birth year 1764 (calculated), buried 1813 (no day in the parsed row). This is taken as his burial because: the calculated birth year fits his January 1765 baptism; his son Ingebrigt married in 1824 as a farmer's son 'of Marstad', so the family had moved from Store Evjen to Marstad; and in the 1825 census his wife Daardi Olsdatter, born 1760 (calculated), is at Marstad with the status 'Gm. E.', apparently a farmer's widow (gaardmands enke). The same index has him as a father at Efjen in 1808, so the move came after that. ¶11a

Corrections 1

Correction: an earlier working inference (card on the 1789 betrothal) suggested his father might be Engelbrecht Engelbrechtsen of Store Evjen, who died 13 October 1783 aged 73 1/2. The baptism names his father as Ingebrigt (Engelbrecht) Bjornsen, so that suggestion is withdrawn. It never reached the tree. Engelbrecht Engelbrechtsen was a different man of the same farm. ¶9

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Rejected burial candidate: 'John Ingebrigtsen Evjen', birth year 1764 (calculated), buried 28 August 1836 (Selbu 1824-1842, index, no age or position). The farm name Evjen and the birth year fit, but if Daardi was already a widow at Marstad in November 1825 this cannot be her husband; it is probably another man of the Evjen farms. Not ruled out absolutely until the 1813 and 1836 scans are read. ¶12

Searches that found nothing 1

Death not found; the 1825 burial is ruled out. The two index entries 'Jon Ingebrigtssøn' and 'John Ingebrigts Alsæt', died 28 March and buried 4 April 1825, were carried as a possible burial for him. The scan of the minister's register (1824-1842, p. 362 no. 29 on 13 Oct 2026) reads 'Huusmands Søn John Ingebrigts. Alsæt', aged a few days ('7 Dg', the figure not certain): a cottager's son, John Ingebrigtsen of Alsaet. That is his grandson John (see his page), born at Alsaet on 23 March 1825 to Ingebrigt Johnsen and Anne Paulsdatter, not Jon himself. His death remains unknown; the burial indexes after 1801 need a search for a man of about his age (born about 1764). ¶7

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Would confirm: the scan of the 1813 entry (age, farm, any spouse) in Selbu ministerialbok 1801-1815. ¶11b
