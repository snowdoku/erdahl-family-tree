About the family

Parent added: father William Smythe of Stainforth, Hatfield parish (see his page), Confirmed on the quoted wills (see his notes). Mother not known; Hunt suggests William had two wives. ¶6

First husband's burial, index only (FamilySearch, 2 Nov 2026): the Doncaster St George register (Doncaster Archives P1/1/C1), as indexed in 'England, Yorkshire, Parish Registers, 1538-2016 (FamilySearch index; images with the partner only)', has a burial of John Symkinson in 1562 (page 5; index 1:1:6618-BPS2; year only, the image is not viewable on FamilySearch). The same index has Thomas Symkinson buried 1560 (page 3; 1:1:6618-5Z1X), which fits Thomas Simkinson, alderman, whose will (29 Jan 1558/9) was proved 2 May 1560; 'Mris Margaret Symkynson' 1562; Richard son of John Symkinson 1563 (1:1:6618-Y4ZJ); and 'Mris Issabell Symkinson' 1570. If the 1562 burial is that of her first husband John Simkinson of Doncaster, she was widowed in 1562 and free to marry William Brewster by about 1563-64, which fits Hunt's date (about 1564) and the Chancery bill calling her 'the late wife of John Symkinson late of Doncaster'. No grade change: the entry gives no description, and the identification is by name and place only. ¶9a

Life story

Superseded view: older writers (Dexter 1905 and works following him) made Prudence, the widow who renounced administration of William Brewster's estate in 1590, the Elder's mother. The 1590 act calls Prudence only 'relict', and on Hunt's dating she was a second wife. Prudence is not entered as a parent. ¶3

Hunt's article read: the full text of J. G. Hunt, NEHGR 124 (1970): 250-254, with its notes, was found in a photocopy in the Zaniewski collection on archive.org (Zaniewski96) and read. Its notes quote the documents. (a) TNA C 2 Eliz. B31/1, a Chancery bill to Sir Nicholas Bacon (Lord Keeper 1558-1579): 'Bill of Complaint by William Bruester and Mary his wife, the late wife of John Symkinson late of Doncaster, Yorks. The said John was seized of lands in Doncaster and in his lifetime did convey them to the said Mary then his wife for the term of her life and afterwards he died at Doncaster.' (b) John Simkinson was buried at Doncaster 25 Dec 1562 (parish register, per Hunt), so the Brewster marriage was after that date; William Brewster was 'dwelling in Scrooby' in the will of Bartholomew Bryan, 6 May 1564 (York P&E 17:389). (c) Her father's will, 1558/9 (York P&E 16:46), names 'John Symkinson and Marie his wife' among his executors. (d) Her brother John Smythe of Hull's will, 8 Aug 1592 (York P&E 25:1051-1052), names 'John Grene, John Smythe, William Brewster and Thomas Simkinson, my nephews' as executors and leaves a legacy 'to Dorothy of Scrobie my neece'. By John Simkinson she had Thomas Simkinson (c.1555-1611), merchant of Hull, and Dorothy (c.1557-after 1591). ¶5

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the grade · 2 on the identification · 1 alternative set aside · 3 open questions

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Confidence as the Elder's mother kept at Probable: the Chancery bill proves her marriage to William Brewster and the 1592 will calls William Brewster and Thomas Simkinson nephews of her brother together, which, with the Elder's age (about 42 in June 1609) and the 1609 Leiden power of attorney to Thomas Simkinson, makes her the Elder's mother; but no record read calls him her son in so many words. Her death is not known: Hunt guesses soon after 1566; the Chancery bill shows her alive at some date between 1563 and 1579, and William's widow in 1590 was Prudence. ¶7

The case for this identification 2

Identification (Probable, compiled sources): John G. Hunt, 'The Mother of Elder William Brewster of the Mayflower', NEHG Register 124 (October 1970): 250-254, identified the Elder's mother as Mary, daughter of William Smythe of Stainforth (Yorkshire) and widow of John Simkinson of Doncaster, who married William Brewster, bailiff of Scrooby, about 1564 and bore the Elder about 1566. Nick Bunker, Making Haste from Babylon (2010), follows Hunt ('William Brewster the bailiff married a widow called Mary Simkinson', about 1564, with the Elder born about 1566), and the General Society of Mayflower Descendants' Silver Book (Merrick, 2000, pp. 1-5, as cited by Wikipedia) gives the Elder's parents as 'William and Mary (Smythe) (Simkinson) Brewster'. Hunt's article itself was not read. ¶1

Evidence reported in those works (not read in the original): the will of her brother John Smythe, alderman and mayor of Hull (1592), naming 'my nephews William Brewster, John Smythe and Thomas Simkinson' as executors, which makes the Elder and Thomas Simkinson half-brothers through their mother; and the 1558/59 will of William Smythe of Stainforth naming 'John Symkinson and Marie his wife'. Consistent with this, the Leiden power of attorney of 12 June 1609 shows the Elder acting with 'Thomas Simkinson, a merchant of Hull' (Burgess 1920, p. 102), though it states no kinship. ¶2

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Lead, not entered: 'Symkenson, Margaret, Doncaster, widow', will 15 Dec 1562, proved 8 Nov 1564 (York Prob. Reg. 17 fol. 397; YAS RS 14 p. 158). Her will was made ten days before John Simkinson's burial at Doncaster (25 Dec 1562). She may have been Mary's mother-in-law, and her will may name 'Mary' or the Simkinson children Thomas and Dorothy. Grade unchanged (Probable, graded on her link to William Brewster). The link still rests on 'nephew' in her brother's 1592 will. ¶8a

Open questions and the next record to read 3

Next record: John Smythe of Hull's will, 1592 (Borthwick Institute, York, Prerogative and Exchequer Court probate register; index free), and William Smythe of Stainforth's will, proved 2 May 1560; reading either would raise this to Confirmed. ¶4

Test record: Borthwick Prob. Reg. 17 fol. 397, and John Smythe of Hull's will (Prob. Reg. 25 fols. 1051-1052), to see the full wording of 'my nephews'. ¶8b

Test record: the Doncaster register image for 1562 (Findmypast, Yorkshire Baptisms/Burials) and the Brewster x Simkinson marriage, which is not in the FamilySearch indexes (Doncaster index searched 1557-1600 for Brewster: no marriage). ¶9b
