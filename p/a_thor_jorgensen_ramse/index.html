About the family

NEW ANCESTOR, AND THE DEEPEST SO FAR ON THIS LINE. Farmer at Øvre Ramse in Lille Tovdal. His estate was settled on 26 June 1681 — the date is quoted twice in later probate files, as "skiftebrev etter avl.'s far, Thoer Jørgensøn Øvre Ramse, dat. 26/6-1681". ¶1

Life story

1636-37 landskatt (Lille Topdal): Øvre Ramse odelsbønder are Olluff and Harall; no Thor and no Jørgen. Thor, then about 22, was not a taxpayer. ¶6

1645 poll tax (DA 76706 view 122, p.14 'Lille Thopdal', Øvre Ramse a full farm): a first couple (name unclear) with a son, 'Biørn och hans quinde', a widow (?) with her son, 'Thord och hans quinde', and one more couple (name unclear). Thord with his wife is very probably this Thor (b. about 1614). Wives are not named in this list, so it does not give his mother or wife; no Jørgen is listed. ¶7

He appears in person in the priests' census of 1664. Under the heading "Lille Topdall er Jtz Annex till Omli prestegield" (Lille Tovdal is now an annexe to Åmli parish), the farm "Øffre Ramse, 4 huder" lists among its occupants "Thoor - 50 aar" with two sons, "Jørgen - 14 aar" and "Tallak - 13 aar". That gives him a birth year of about 1614 and matches exactly the two sons, Jørgen and Thallak Thoerssønner, who in 1685 made a contract dividing the farm between them ("kontrakt mellom brørne Jørgen og Thallak Thoerssønner om gardbyte, dat. 30/10-1685"). ¶2

Eight of his children are named across the 1715 probate file at Øvre Ramse: Jørgen Thorsen (b. about 1650); Thallak Thorsen (b. about 1651, estate settled 6 April 1715); Ole Thorsen and Anløv Thorsdatter, who died unmarried and were settled together on 28 September 1715; Aase Thorsdatter, married to Tellef Nielsen Tveit (our ancestor); Kari Thorsdatter, a kårkone at Øvre Ramse; Gjertrud Thorsdatter, unmarried, whose estate was settled 29 October 1715; and Thargier Thorsdatter, married to Knud Thargiersen Mjaaland. ¶3

The farm was valued at 1 hud in his estate. A "hud" (hide) was the old Norwegian unit of land assessment; Øvre Ramse as a whole was rated at 4 huder in 1664, shared between several households. ¶4

Context: Tovdal (Lille Topdal) is a narrow forest valley on the Tovdalselva in inland Agder. In the 1664 priests' census it was an annexe of Åmli parish and held only a handful of farms — Østenå, Øvre Ramse, Ytre Ramse Nedre and Hillestad among them — each rated at only 3 to 4 huder, the Norwegian land unit used to assess tax. Holdings were tiny, split repeatedly between heirs, and the probate inventories are mostly livestock, a share in the farm and small debts. Households supplemented farming with timber floated down the Tovdalselva to the coast and with work for the Agder ironworks. The same dozen families intermarried for generations, which is why the same given names — Tellef, Thor, Tjøstol, Terje, Aslak, Gunsten — recur on every farm. ¶5

1664-65 men-under-50 list (DA 35567 view 28, p. 19, read at zoom): 'Øffre Ramse': users Aswold(?) 36, Jørgen 35, Effuind Gunnulfsøn 27; in the sons/servants column 'Oluf Børresøn 20' and 'Jørgen Thorsøn 20'. Jørgen Thorsen is the son Jørgen (14 in the vicar's 1664 count) of Thor Jørgensen, himself over 50 and so not listed. Consistent with the tree; no new parent. ¶8
