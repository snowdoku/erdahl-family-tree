Life story

Note on his stated age: he was baptized in 1851 and the church books and the 1865, 1875, 1891 and 1910 censuses keep that year, but the 1900 census gives 1852 and his son's 1887 baptism gives 1853. 1851 is the year to trust. ¶10

Later records give his birth year as 1852 or 1853. ¶2

1865 census: Elias Johnsen's household at Valderhoug (rural residence 20, holding 135) in the Valderøen district of Borgund. Martinus was 15, 'deres Søn', with his parents Elias (50, gaardbruger) and Maria Knudsdatter (52) and his brother Karl (7). ¶4

Confirmed 1866. Farmer and fisherman at Valderhaug (farm 183, holding 29, in 1900); tenant farmer and fisherman in 1910. ¶1

Married Hansine Jakobine Pedersdatter 15 Jun 1879 at Borgund. The record calls him 'Martinius Kristian Eliassen', born and living at Valderhaug, and names his father as Elias Johnsen. ¶3

His first child with Hansine was stillborn in 1880. The Borgund register of stillbirths (dødfødte) for 1880, page 205, entry 2, names the father Martinus Eliassen, gaardmand at Valderhaug, born 1851, and the mother Hansine Pedersdatter, his wife, born 1855, with the midwife Marie Moe as informant. ¶5

Eleven recorded children in all, 1880-1903: a stillborn child (1880), Elias Oluf (baptized 26 Jun 1881), Maria Petrine (baptized 18 Nov 1883), Elias Karl Oluf (baptized 8 Feb 1885), Peder Johan (born 13 Apr 1887 - the later Rev. Peter Valder), Marie Josefine (baptized 26 May 1890), Karoline (born 2 Oct 1892), Ole (born 10 Apr 1895), Elias (born 14 Jan 1898), Martin (born 24 Aug 1899) and Johan Olaf (baptized 30 Aug 1903). The first three had all died before the 1891 census. ¶6

1891 census: head of family at Valderhaug, holding 135aab, in Valderøy enumeration district 18, with his wife, the children Elias (1885), Peder (1887) and Marie (April 1890), and two servants, Johan Hanssen (1872) of Borgund and Karn Hansdatter (1872) of Hareide in Ulstein. His parents Elias Johnsen and Marie Knutsdatter kept a separate household on the same farm with their son Karl (1859). ¶7

1900 census: 'Gaardbr. og Fisker' (farmer and fisherman) at Valderhaug, farm number 183, holding 29, with his wife, five children at home, a maid, and his widowed mother on a retirement pension charged on the farm. Fishing and farming together were the normal living on these outer-coast islands - a fiskarbonde, a fisherman-farmer. ¶8

1910 census (1 Dec 1910): 'Gaardbruker leilænding og fisker' - a tenant farmer and fisherman - at Valderhaug, gnr 183 bnr 29, now in Giske parish, district 3 Valderø. He was recorded as absent from the farm that day, with his whereabouts given as Ålesund. At home were his wife Hansine, the children Karoline (2 Oct 1892), Elias (14 Jan 1898), Martin (24 Aug 1899) and Johan (28 Jun 1902); his son Ole (10 Apr 1895), a fisherman, was also away. His mother, entered as 'Berte H. Nørve', widow, born 2 Oct 1813, lived with them as a 'føderaadskone'. ¶9
