About the family

Parents (Confirmed by probate card): the Ryfylke probate card index 1774-1788 (DA 39936, view 441, card read on the image) has a card for Tuntland i Hjelmeland, protocol side 396b, dated 16 Nov 1785: deceased Marthe Steensdatter, wife of Peder Eriksen; children 1. Erik, of age (myndig), 2. Sten, married, 3. Mallene, married; land: 2 løp smør in the farm; gross estate 496 rd. A married daughter Mallene of Peder Eriksen of Tuntland in 1785 is the 'Mallene Peders Datter Tuntland' who married Niels Olsen of Øye in 1775: Peder Erichsen was the only Peder on Tuntland in the 1762 ekstraskatt, and the brothers Erik and Sten match the confirmands Erich Pedersen (1768) and Steen Pedersen (1771) of Tuntland. Background, before the card was found: Peder Erichsen and Marthe Steensdatter of Tuntland. In the 1762 ekstraskatt for Tuntland, Peder Erichsen is a holder (opsidder), listed with Marthe Steensdatter as his wife; he is the only Peder on Tuntland. Her brothers by the same patronymic fit them: Erich Pedersen (b. 1750, named for the paternal grandfather), confirmed from Tuntland in 1768 and still on Tuntland in 1801, and Steen Pedersen, confirmed from Tuntland on 1 Dec 1771 (named for the maternal grandfather). Her own children include Martha (b. about 1781) and Peder (b. about 1789), and her brother Erich's daughter of 1791 is also Martha. Her own baptism of about 1752 would be in the Hjelmeland book before 1760, which is not on Digitalarkivet. ¶5

Life story

Confirmation (index): 'Mallene Pedersdatter', of Tuntland, confirmed in Hjelmeland on 1 May 1768 (index birth year 1752), together with Erich Pedersen of Tuntland (b. 1750). This agrees with her age of 49 in Feb 1801 and with her home farm at marriage. ¶4

Probate of her mother read: Ryfylke skifteprotokoll D 7b (DA 23892), fol. 296b, 16 Nov 1782, Tuntland: deceased Marthe Steensdatter; heirs her husband Peder Erichsen and their children Erich Pedersen, Steen Pedersen and Mallene Pedersdatter, married. (The card's '1785, s. 396b' is a misreading of 1782, s. 296b.) Her husband is not named in the legible lines, so the link to Niels Olsen rests on the 1775 marriage entry, as before. ¶6

1801 census: Malene Pedersdatter, 49, wife of Niels Olsen at Øye, Hjelmeland. ¶1

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 6

Linked as mother of Malene Nielsdatter as Speculative only (see Niels Olsen). ¶2

Upgrade to Probable: she married Niels Olsen of Øye in Hjelmeland church in 1775, as 'Mallene Peders Datter Tuntland' (marriages 1775, p. 240, no. 6, DA 1599, IIIF canvas 138, scan read). This is a primary record of her marriage to Niels Olsen, and the 1801 census lists her with him at Øye and with their daughter Malene (10), baptised 23 Jan 1791 as 'Mallene Niels D. Øye'. The link stays at Probable only because the identity of that daughter with the Madla widow Malene Nielsdatter is Probable. ¶3

Re-proof attempt (for), no change: still Probable, graded on her link to Malene Nielsdatter. The 1801 census names Malene, 10, as 'Deres børn' of Niels Olsen and Malene Pedersdatter at Øye, but the step from that child to the 1816 bride of Niels Thorsen (also 'of Øye', no rival in the Hjelmeland index 1803-1817) spans 15 years with no record between. The 1816 marriage scan names no father and no age (sureties Lars and Niels Buken). See Niels Olsen's note for the detail and the next record. ¶7

Upgrade: upgraded to Confirmed by the coordinator (1 Nov 2026), graded on the link to Malene Nielsdatter, applying the audit-3 threshold (about 15 years or less on the same farm, no named rival). The 1801 census for Øye lists Malene, 10, as 'Deres børn' (their child) of this couple. The 1816 marriage has the bride Malene Nielsdatter 'of Øye', and no other Malene Nielsdatter at Øye appears in the index. That is 15 years on one farm with no rival. Whether this Malene is the later Madla widow is graded on her own record, not here. ¶8

Audit 4 (1 Nov 2026): the coordinator's raise to Confirmed is reversed; Probable, graded on her link to her daughter Malene Nielsdatter. The 1801 census 'Deres børn' and the 1816 bride 'of Øye' are 15 years apart on one farm, which alone would pass. But the tree's Malene is the Madla widow of Nordre Sunde, Håland. Joining the Hjelmeland couple of 1816 to the Madla couple of 1821 onward crosses parishes and rests on matching names, Anna's 1816 Hjelmeland baptism and the 1865 birthplace. Under the grading rule this upward link is graded on the parent's record, not on Malene's. So it is Probable, as audit 3 found. The argument and the records that would confirm her are set out on Niels Olsen. ¶9

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed, graded on her link to her daughter Malene Nielsdatter. The joint probate after her and her husband is the record: Ryfylke skifteprotokoll D 19 (DA 23902), fol. 293a-b, No. 7 (scan): 'Skifteforretning efter de afgangne Selvejer Niels Olsøn og Hustrue Mallene Pedersdatter af Gaarden Øye i Hielmelands Skibrede', begun 12 Oct 1824 and closed 27 May 1825. The heirs are 'Boets Arvinger, nemlig': 1. the eldest son Niels Nielsen Øye, farmer and freeholder; 2.-3. the sons of a dead son Peder Nielsen (by his marriage with [?Gjertrud] Pedersdatter of Næs under [?Sørbø] in Årdal skipreide): Niels Pedersen 6 and Peder Pedersen 4; 4. the eldest daughter Susanna Nielsdatter, married to the cottager John Andersen, Houen under Gehland, Hjelmeland; 5. the second daughter Siri Nielsdatter, married to the farmer Niels Nielsen Øvrebøe, Årdal; 6. 'Tredie Datter Mallene Nielsdatter, gift med Niels Thorsøn Bukken, boende paa en Plads under Gaarden S[u/a]nde i H[?] Sogn under Jæderen og Dalernes Skriveri'; 7. the fourth and youngest daughter Martha Nielsdatter, married to Michel Larsen, Haneraand, Hausken skipreide. (The farm name reads 'Sande' or 'Sunde' and the parish name is cramped; 'under Jæderen og Dalernes Skriveri', the husband's name and Malene's name are clear.) Continuity to the tree's Malene Nielsdatter (see her page): she is the Malene, 10, 'Deres børn' of this couple at Øye in 1801; she married Niels Thorsen of Buchen in Fister church on 17 Nov 1816 as an unmarried woman of Øye; she and Niels Thorsen entered Håland parish (Jæren og Dalane sorenskriveri) in 1822, and their children were baptised from 1821 at Nordre Sunde, Madla, where she died a widow in 1868. The 1824-25 probate places her, by name and with her husband Niels Thorsen, on a cottar's place under the farm Sunde in Jæren og Dalane, two to three years after the move, exactly where the tree has her. No other Malene Nielsdatter married to a Niels Thorsen is known in Jæren; the 1801 sibling list (Niels, Martha, Siri, Peder, Malene) matches the probate heirs (Niels, Peder, Siri, Malene, Martha, plus Susanna). ¶10
