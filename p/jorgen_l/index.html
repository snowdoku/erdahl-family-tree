Life story

Baptized 14 Mar 1773, Innvik. Married Britha Olsdatter 12 Jun 1796, Innvik. ¶1

Probate at Erdal 1833-39 (Nordfjord probate register, protocol 24, p. 188) is probably his. ¶3

Buried at Erdal 27 Apr 1835 (index age implies born 1775), so he died 1835. ¶5

Probate: Nordfjord skifteprotokoll A 24, fol. 188b-189b (index pa00000000061150, filed under his name), read from the scan. It is the probate after his wife, 'afg. Gaardmands Kone Brite Olsd.' of Erdal. At the registration and valuation (Registrerings- og Vurderingsforretning) at Erdal on 13 Oct 1834 'Enkemanden Jørgen Lassesen Erdal' (the widower) was present and named the heirs as their five children: the son Lasse Jørgensen Erdal; Britha Jørgensdatter, married to Ole Pedersen Sandvig; Anna Jørgensdatter, married to Halsteen[?] Samuelsen Loen; Elisabeth Jørgensdatter, 28, with her legal guardian (laugverge) Iver[?] Samuelsen Berge; and Ingeborg Jørgensdatter, 25[?], with her brother as guardian. ¶6

Probate, continued: when the probate court sat at Erdal on 18 May 1835 the heirs reported that the widower Jørgen Lassesen Erdal had died since the registration. The joint estate (91 spd 2 ort 6 s to divide) went to the five children: the son Lasse Jørgensen 30-2-10 (a son's double share) and each of the daughters Brithe, Anne, Lisbeth and Ingeborg 15-1-5. The estate owned a share of the farm Erdal, with its buildings, by a recorded deed (date only partly legible). So he died between 13 Oct 1834 and his burial on 27 Apr 1835. ¶7

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Confirmed son of Lasse Rasmussen and Birthe Trulsdatter. He is named in his father's 1774 probate, his grandmother Ragnilde's 1783 probate (10½ years old) and his grandfather Rasmus Jørgensen's c.1794 probate (21 years old). ¶4

Confidence moved Probable to Confirmed: the 1834/35 probate above (A 24 fol. 188b) shows Jørgen Lassesen Erdal, husband of Brite Olsdatter, with five children including the son Lasse Jørgensen Erdal. The daughters Britha and Anna are the children aged 3 and 1 in his 1801 census household, and his own parentage was already proved by his father's 1774, his grandmother's 1783 and his grandfather's probates. ¶8

Corrections 1

Correction: Aaland 1932: Aaland's Erdal history (bruk nr. 4) lists 'S. Jørgen, † 86 a. g. 1858, sk. 1793. * Brite Olsd. Kjell., † 70 a. g. 1834': a deed in 1793 and a wife, Brite Olsdatter of Kjellog (Tjellaug), who died in 1834. The deed and wife agree with the tree. The death 'aged 86 in 1858' is in the printed text (checked on the page image, not an OCR error). It is contradicted by the probate of 1834-35, where on 18 May 1835 the heirs reported the widower Jørgen Lassesen Erdal dead, and by his burial on 27 April 1835. The primary records stand; Aaland's 1858 is an error. Aaland also records the 1786 court division in which he, as 'eldest son's son', received half of his grandfather's holding (it became bruk nr. 4, the 'Jørnbruket'). ¶9
