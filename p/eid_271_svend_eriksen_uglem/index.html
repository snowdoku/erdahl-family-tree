About the family

Father of Erik Svendsen Uglem and - through the 1741 probate, where Ole Sivertsen Alset is the guardian as 'fasterens mann' - of Ingri Svendsdatter, wife of Ole Sivertsen Alset. Both Erik and Ingri are direct ancestors (through Torsten Eriksen Uglem and through Ole Sivertsen Alset), so he appears twice in the pedigree: a pedigree collapse (Probable). Why Probable, not Confirmed: the 1701 reading 'Siund' and the identification of Ingri as a daughter rest on the guardian relationship rather than on her own baptism. ¶2

Life story

Bygdebok (Selbu i fortid og nåtid vol. 4, Uglan, canvases 23 and 26): 'Svend Eriksen [...] født ca. 1650, fikk i 1691 bygsel' (23); 'Brukere': '1691-1722 [Svend] Eriksen fra [...] i Sverige, f. ca. 1650, d. 1723. G. m. Ragnhild [...], f. ca. 1659, d. 1742'. The book says he came from Sweden (the farm name before 'i Sverige' is not recovered). This explains why no Erik is found at Uglem in the 1640s and weakens the lead that Esten Eriksen of Uglem was his brother. Death 1723 is the book's date; the burial (16310, 1723) is not yet read. His parents would have to be sought in Swedish (Jämtland/Härjedalen?) records. ¶7

1701 manntall, Selbu, Uglem (canvas 117-118): 'Siund Erichsen', aged 52 (born about 1649), with sons Erich Siundsen 13, Poule 8 and Olle 4. 'Siund' is taken as the clerk's form of Svend: his son Erik appears in the 1741 probate as 'Erik Svendsen Uglen' (card 77804 c2055). ¶1

Corrections 1

Correction: raised from Probable to Confirmed. The probate after Povel Svendsen Berge (29 Jul 1727) names 'farbroren Erich Svendsen Uglen' as the child's guardian, so Povel and Erik were brothers and both Svendsen - exactly the 'Erich Siundsen 13' and 'Poule 8' listed as sons of Siund Erichsen at Uglem in the 1701 manntall (card 77804 c145; manntall c118). 'Siund' is thus Svend. He is an ancestor three times over: through Erik Svendsen (-> Torsten Eriksen Uglem), Povel Svendsen (-> Ole Paulsen Berge) and Ingri Svendsdatter (wife of Ole Sivertsen Alset; that link is Probable). ¶3

Searches that found nothing 1

Searched, no Erik found at Uglem in the 1640s: (1) the 1646-47 kongsjordebok (51306 view 181) lists the diocese tenants ('Stichtens Gods') in Selbu; the Uglem holding is entered as 'Bersuend och Olle Uglemb' (reading of the first name fairly certain). (2) The 1645 poll tax (koppskatt), Selbu half-farm men (77038 views 269R-270R), has 'Jørn Uglemb' with wife and one daughter. Selbu's full-farm men in the 1645 poll tax were not found in views 250-263 and remain unread, so Uglem's full holders in 1645 are still unknown. The 1666 vicar's list (35528 v21R, no. 12) has no Erik at Uglem either; the widow there has a son Erich Østensen 2, so the Uglem lead runs through Esten, not an Erik. Svend's father may not have been at Uglem. ¶6

Open questions and the next record to read 2

Biography gaps: his exact birth date (c.1649) and baptism predate the 1699 Selbu register, and his marriage (before about 1688) and wife are not identified, since the 1701 manntall lists men only. His death, death place and burial are not found, and the 16310 burial entries for 1699-1737 are not reported searched for him, so they are the next check. His probate is not among the 77804 Uglem cards 2039-2073, which were searched in full, so the next record is protocol 1 (24749, 1689-1701) and protocol 2a (24750), both unindexed. ¶4

Lead: the 1664 fogd's list (35578 view 23 left, p. 24) has an Uglem holding (½ ørtug, øde) used by 'Esten Erichsøn' aged 36 (born about 1628); the 1665 list has 'Esten 40' at Uglem, and by 1666 an Uglem widow has a son 'Erich Østensen' aged 2. Esten Eriksen is a possible elder brother of Svend Eriksen (b. c.1649), which would place their father Erik at Uglem; unproved. The 1624 Selbu leidang roll (39890 views 76-78) lists 'Esten Uglemb', 'Baard ibid', 'Oluff Uglemb', 'Biørnn Uglem' and 'Poulle Uglemb', but no Erik at Uglem. ¶5
