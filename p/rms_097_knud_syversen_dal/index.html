Life story

Birth about 1638 and death after 1693 are from the bygdebok rebuild (ABT). ¶3

Vicar's census of 5 Nov 1664 for Aurdal (DA 35504, p. 22, view 20, left page): farm 'Daell, j hud iiij skind', holder (opsidder) 'Knud Siffuersß. 26 aar'; no sons, servants or housemen entered. So Knud Syversen held Dal alone in Nov 1664, aged 26 (born about 1638, as the bygdebok has it), within weeks of his father Siuffuer Knudtzøn's probate of 30 Nov 1664 (card 2041, which names 'Knud Dahl' among the children). Enrichment; his grade (link to Joen Knudsen) stays Probable on the bygdebok, since no record joins him to Joen. On the same page, just above: 'Hoffvords-Rud, iij meler korn - Brugis aff Knud Næß' (Hovrud, worked in 1664 by Knud of Nes); possibly relevant to Anne Knudsdatter of Hovrud (see her page, b. about 1655, 'of gnr. 111' per the bygdebok), but nothing ties them, so no person added. ¶8

Mother found. His father's probate of 30 Nov 1664 (DA 25281 s. 312a, view 322) lists him, 'Knud Siuffuerssøn Dahl', first among the children 'Auflet ved den Igienleff[ue]nde Quinde', i.e. by the widow Audbiør Haldorsdatter (see her page), not by the first wife Dorte Erichsdatter (whose children were Erich Siuffuerssøn Kiørsta[d] and Rangnild). His own grade (link to Joen Knudsen) is unchanged. ¶9

1707 Dal deed read: DA 10262 (pantebok nr. 4) s. 130-131 (views 132-133), 'Obristlieutenant Jørgen Otto Brockenhuuses udgifne Skiøde til Joen Knudsen Dahl', dated Toxstad 24 Jun 1707, read at the Aurdal ting (Soerum) of 11 Jul 1707. Brockenhuus sells to Joen Knudsen his right of odel and redemption in 'Dahle med Qvamfoss', which his late father had held. It calls Joen 'nærmeste Odels og Løsnings Mand' (the nearest odel heir) and names Joen's wife and children only generally. It does NOT name Joen's father, so it gives no primary link from Knud Syversen to Joen. His grade stays Probable on the bygdebok. That Joen was the nearest odel heir to Dal fits his descent from the old Dal owners (Syver Knudsen d. 1664), but it does not prove it. ¶6

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: Speculative to Probable, graded on his link to his son Joen Knudsen (see his page), on the bygdebok statement alone. Valdres bygdebok I F, gnr 107 Dal (canvas 494): under 'Sjugurd Knutsson Dal' (d. 1664; m. 1 Dorte Eriksdotter, 2 [Audbjørg] Haldorsdotter, which matches probate card 2041) the children include '3 Knut f om 1638, brukar', and the chapter continues 'Sonen... etter far: Knut Sjugurdsson Dal f om 1638, d etter 1693, g m [forename not recovered] Kristensdotter frå [farm] 109', with children '[1] Jon... f om 1665' and '2 Kristen, g 1706 m Astrid Trondsdotter', and '3 Guri, g m Andris Andrisson, sjå Kjørstad 94'; 'Sonen Jon kjøpte Dal' and is then treated as 'Jon Knutsson Dal, f om 1665, d 1737, g 1701 m Marit Trondsdotter frå [Bø] 106'. So the bygdebok names Joen and Christen as Knut Sjugurdsson's sons, which fits the 1738 probate's 'den sal. Mands Broder Christen Knudsen Rogne' and both brothers' sons named Syver. The bygdebok also says Jon bought (part of) Dal in 1707. Rebuilt from nb.no content-search word boxes; dates from the rebuild are given as ABT. ¶2a

Alternatives considered and set aside 2

Graded on his link to his candidate son Joen Knudsen: Speculative. 'Knud Dahl' is the third-listed child (no age, so probably of age) at the probate of his father Siuffuer Knudtzøn of Dal i Nordre Aurdal, 30 Nov 1664 (Valdres probate card 2041; Hadeland, Land og Valdres skifteprotokoll 1659-1666 s. 312, not read). Joen Knudsen of the same farm Dal (d. before 15 Feb 1738) was a son of a Knud: his brother Christen Knudsen Rogne is named 'den sal. Mands Broder' in Joen's probate (DA 25286 p. 122), and both brothers named a son Syver. No record yet joins Knud to Joen; the gap is 74 years, so this stays Speculative. ¶1

Not Confirmed: no primary record yet names Knud as Joen's father. ¶2c

Searches that found nothing 2

Search note: the mortgage book for Hadeland, Land og Valdres 1704-1715 (DA 10262, 'Pantebok nr. 4') is scanned two pages per view, with view n carrying printed page about n-2 (view 2 = s. 1, 28 Oct 1704; view 40 = s. 39; view 100 = s. 97-98, September-November 1706). The deeds are copied in full under short underlined headings ('X og Consortes... til Y'), grouped by tinglysing session with a parish heading. So 1707 starts at about view 105, and 1712-13 is well past view 200. Not read further this pass; the next reader should scan the session headings for 'Ourdals Prestegield' from view 105 (1707, Dal) and from about view 220 (1712-13, Bø). ¶5

Search note: the Aurdal deed sessions of 3 Mar 1707 (DA 10262 views 112-116) and 11 Jul 1707 (views 130-134) were read. The only Dal entry is the Brockenhuus deed. Brockenhuus deeds to other Aurdal farmers follow on the same pages (Trond Helgesen Kabiør and others). ¶7

Open questions and the next record to read 2

Test record: the 1707 deed (skøyte) by which Jon Knutsson bought Dal (Valdres pantebok/tingbok 1707), which may name the seller as his father; or the 1664/66 manntall for Dal. ¶2b

Next record: the 1707 Dal deed should be in the mortgage book for Hadeland, Land og Valdres 1704-1715 (DA 10262; not indexed). Not read this pass. ¶4
