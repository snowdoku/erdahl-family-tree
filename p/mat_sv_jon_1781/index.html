About the family

His wife Elen Sivertsdatter, b. about 1784, was in all probability the 'Elen Sivertsdatter, 17, Tieneste pige' in the 1801 household of Niels Olssen at LANGLI - so the Eidem and Langli farms were already exchanging people a generation before Sivert Johnsen married Brynhild Johnsdatter Langli. ¶3

Life story

The elder of the two brothers both named Jon Sivertssen in the 1801 census of Eidum - aged 20 against his brother's 18. By the 1825 census he is 'John Siverts.', 45, HMD. (husmand, cottager) on the same farm, married to 'Elen Sivertsd.', 41, with children Helge Johnsd. 10 and Sivert Johns. 4. ¶1

The parish clerk distinguished him in 1815 as 'John Siverts. AELDRE' - John Sivertsen the elder - when baptising his daughter Helge on the second Sunday in Advent that year, the mother entered as 'Elen Siversd., Hustrue'. His younger brother's son Sivert had been baptised in the same book three months before. ¶2

He matters because he is the reason two men called John Sivertsen and two called Sivert Johnsen were on Eidem at once, which is the principal trap on this farm. ¶5

Baptism: Selbu ministerialbok 1757-1801, Dom. 17 p. Trin. 1781 (7 Oct 1781): 'Döbt Jon. Par: Siur Jons. Afth. Helge Jonsd. T. Th. Afth. Jon Mölnh. Th. Afth. Marit ib. Marit ib.' - baptised Jon, parents Sivert (Siur) Jonsen of Aftreit and Helge Jonsdatter; godparents Th. Aftreit, Jon Mølnhus, Th. Aftreit and two Marits of Aftreit. Read from the scan. Afth. = Aftreit, Helge's farm at her 1778 betrothal, where the family also lived at the May 1784 baptism of the younger Jon. Age 20 in Feb 1801 fits. Birth changed from 'ABT 1781, Eidem' to 'BEF 7 OCT 1781, Aftreit'; confidence can move to Confirmed for the parentage. ¶8

Probate: he is 'John eldre' (John the elder), son and heir, in the skifte after his father Sivert Johnsen Eidem, closed 5 April 1815, and 'John Sivertsen eldre', of age, in the skifte after his mother Helge Johnsdatter, closed 22 September 1820 (skifteprotokoll 10b, fol. 705b and 765b). ¶9

Lease of a plass, 1820: Lt-Col Georg Frederik von Krogh, owner of Gaarden Eidem, leased him 'Pladsen Eidum under Gaarden Eidum' for his and his wife's lives, signed at Kroghsminde on 1 June 1820 and read at the Selbu sommerting on 7 July 1820. The rent was one barrel of oats a year from 1822, with labour duty for the farm. He signed 'John Sivertsen Eidum Ældr.' (Pantebok 5 fol. 374b), which agrees with his status as husmand in 1825. ¶12

Probate, guardian: in the skifte after his younger brother John Sivertsen Eidum, closed 8 November 1832, 'Barnenes Farbroder John Sivertsen Eidum' (the children's paternal uncle) was appointed guardian of his nephews and nieces Severt, Anders, Helge and Marith. As the brother of the same name was the deceased, the guardian is this man (inference from the record). He was still living on 8 November 1832. ¶11

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Rated Probable: the 1801 household and the 1825 household agree in name, age and farm, and the 'Aeldre' of 1815 explains the duplication, but no single record states the brotherhood. ¶4

Confidence raised from Probable to Confirmed: the 1815 and 1820 probates of Sivert Johnsen and Helge Johnsdatter of Eidem name him explicitly as their son 'John eldre'. ¶10

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Namesake: not the same man as his younger brother John Sivertsen Eidem (see his page), Mayme Eidem's great-great-grandfather. The 1801 census of Eidum, Selbu (rural residence 0034, household 01) lists two sons of Sivert Jonssen and Haelge Jonsdatter, both written 'Jon Sivertssen', side by side as persons 003 (aged 20, ugift = unmarried) and 004 (aged 18). The 1825 census of Eidem keeps them apart again on the same page: this man as 'John Siverts.', 45, Hmd. (husmand, cottager), with his wife Elen Sivertsd. 41 and children Helge 10 and Sivert 4; his brother as 'Joh Siverts.', 41, Gdm. (gaardmand, farmer), with wife Sigrid Bersvendsd. 39 and children Helge 13, Sivert 10, Anders 5 1/2 and Marit 2. Two men alive in the same census with different ages, wives, children and tenure cannot be one person, so the two ids must not be merged. The parish clerk made the same distinction in 1815 by writing this man as 'John Siverts. Aeldre', the elder. ¶6

Searches that found nothing 1

Death not found: the 1842 Selbu burial of 'John Sivertsen', aged 62 (b. about 1780), matches his age, but the scanned entry (1842, page 404, no. 11) reads 'John Sivertsen Krogstad, Hmd., 62 Aar, Krogstad'. He was a cottager like this man, but at Krogstad, not Eidem. Nothing yet links this man to Krogstad, so that burial is NOT assigned to him. A Krogstad household with a wife Elen Sivertsdatter (in the 1835 tax rolls, a child's baptism after 1825, or Elen's own burial) would decide it. ¶7
