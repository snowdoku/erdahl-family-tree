About the family

Wife of Ola Gunnarsen of Overvik: the bygdebok gives 'G. 1726 m. Kari, f. 1705, d. 1773' (Selbu i fortid og naatid vol. 6, p. 326, word boxes; her patronymic is not in the boxes). ¶1

Mother of Gunnar (see his page), now Probable: if she is the Karen who married Ole Gundersen in June 1726, she was Ola's wife when Gunnar was baptised in December 1727; no burial of a wife of Ole Gundersen Vigen is known before then. The baptism itself names only the father. ¶6

Her parents are still Probable, not Confirmed: the probate names no kin of hers. Naming pattern, inference only: her second son Torsten and her only daughter Siri carry the names of Torsten Kjøsnes and his wife Siri, the parents of the Kari baptised at Kjøsnes 8 Mar 1705, which supports that identification. ¶12

Life story

Probable baptism: Reminiscere Sunday (8 Mar) 1705, 'Tosten Kiøsnes barn Kari', first child of Torsten Kjøsnes and Siri (married 1704) - 16310 canvas 22. Fits her burial age (68 at 1 Feb 1773) and patronymic Torstensdatter; the only other Torsten fathering children in 1702-05 (of Qvello/Korsted?, married Barbro Efjen 1702) had Ingebor (1703) and Johanna (1704). ¶10a

Marriage: Whit Sunday, 9 June 1726 (date computed from Easter, 21 April 1726), 'Copuleret... Ole Gunderz: [og] Karen Torstens D:' - married Ole Gundersen and Karen Torstensdatter; no farm and no sponsors are given (Selbu ministerialbok 1699-1737, canvas 104 right page, crop). Karen is the written form of Kari. ¶4

Widow 'Karen Torstens Datter' in her husband Ole Gundersen Wigen's skifte, 17 Mar 1760 (24755 fol. 102a-102b), as the mother of all five children, among them Gunder Olsen, 34. The record does not name her father or her birthplace. (Confidence left unchanged, who was limited to generation 6.) ¶13

Weighing the 1762 widow against the 1726 marriage of 'Karen Torstens D:'. For her being Ola Gunnarsen's widow: the farm Vigen, where Ola was called 'Ole Gunders Vigen' in 1727 and 1736; widowhood after Ola's death in 1759; and sons Gunnar (the paternal grandfather's name) and Tosten. A son Torsten would be the expected second son if her father was Torsten, so it supports Torstensdatter - but she also has a son Jon, which would equally fit a father Jon. ¶8a

Burial under Septuagesima 1773: 'Begravne: Karen Torsend. Vigen død d. 1 Febr. gl. 68 aar' - Karen Torstensdatter Vigen, died 1 Feb 1773, aged 68, so born about 1704-05 (Selbu 16311 canvas 55, left page, crop checked by the orchestrator). Name, patronymic, farm (Vigen/Overvik) and age all fit; the entry names no husband or father, so her parentage is still open. ¶9

How sure are we?

Confirmed. A record that names this person and this family link has been read.

2 on the grade · 1 on the identification · 3 records that disagree · 2 corrections · 2 open questions

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Speculative as the mother of Gunnar (baptised 14 December 1727): the 1726 marriage and a first son in December 1727 fit, and the bygdebok places Gunnar 1727 under this couple, but no primary record names Gunnar's mother - the 1727 baptism gives the father only. ¶2a

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed as the wife of Ola Gunnarsen and mother of Gunnar Olsen Overvik. The probate after her husband, 17 March 1760 (Skifteprotokoll 5, 24755, fol. 102a-102b), names 'hans igienlevende Enke Karen Torstens datter' and their children Gunder Olsen 34, Torsten Olsen 30, Ingebrigt Olsen 25, Hans[?] Olsen 17 and Siri Olsdatter 20. This settles her patronymic (Torstensdatter, against the 'Jons-' reading in the 1762 ekstraskatt) and her marriage, which before rested on the bygdebok and the 1726 marriage of 'Karen Torstens D:'. ¶11

The case for this identification 1

Identification of this Karen with Kari, wife of Ola Gunnarsen of Overvik (see his page), rated Probable. For: the bygdebok gives Ola's marriage as 1726 to Kari; the clerk wrote Ola's father's name as Gunder as well as Gunnar ('Ole Gunders: Vigen' at his son Gunnar's baptism in 1727 and 'Ole Gunderz Vigen' in 1736), so 'Ole Gunderz' is the clerk's usual form for Ola; and the marriage, in June 1726, fits a first son baptised in December 1727. ¶5a

Records that disagree 3

Against: a second Ole Gundersen (farm read Setter[?]) married in the same spring, at Rogate 1726 (canvas 104 left), but his bride was Gjertrud Larsdatter, not a Kari, so he does not compete. Only canvases 104 left (bottom) and 104 right of 1726 have been read for marriages, so another Ole Gundersen marrying a Kari later in 1726 is not yet excluded. ¶5b

1762 extra tax (conflict, name not changed): at 675 Wiigen (Vigen) stands 'Kari Jons datter Enke' (widow), with children over 12 'Jon[?], Tosten, Gunnar, Ra..[?]'. Zoomed beside the 727 Wiigen rows directly above ('Siur Jonsen, Anne Jons datter'; 'Jon Jonsen, Berit Jons datter'), her patronymic is written with the same letterforms and reads Jonsdatter, not Torstensdatter. ¶7

Against: the written patronymic Jons-; and Gunnar is listed third of the sons, whereas Ola's Gunnar was born December 1727, the first child after the 1726 marriage (the clerk did not always list children by age). The two records cannot both give her father's name. The parish register entry is kept, because a marriage entry names the bride directly, while the 1762 clerk may have confused her with the Jonsdatters next door. Her confidence stays Probable for the 1726 marriage; the identification of the 1762 widow with her is Speculative, not proved. Her burial in 1773 (Selbu register 16311 or 16316) or a probate after Ola would settle it. ¶8b

Corrections 2

Correction: name changed from 'Kari' to 'Kari Torstensdatter', and confidence raised from Speculative to Probable, by the marriage of 'Ole Gunderz:' and 'Karen Torstens D:' on Whit Sunday 1726 (16310 canvas 104 right), for the reasons below. ¶3

Correction: birth set to before 8 Mar 1705 at Kjøsnes. ¶10b

Open questions and the next record to read 2

Would settle it: her churching after Gunder (16310, canvases 110-111, January-February 1728) if it names her, the 1726 marriage entry (16310, canvases about 103-106), or her 1773 burial. ¶2b

What would confirm it: her burial in 1773 with an age or patronymic (Selbu ministerialbok 1757-1801, source 16311), or a probate after Ola (1759) or after her naming her as Torstensdatter. Her parents are not identified. ¶5c
