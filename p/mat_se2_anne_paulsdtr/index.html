About the family

Paul Ingebrigtsen's mother, named as 'Anne Pauls[datter]' in his baptismal entry of 13 August 1826, with the residence Alsaett. ¶1

Her son Paul is named for her own father, a Paul, on the ordinary Selbu custom; and her granddaughter, Paul's eldest daughter, born 1860, was named Anne for her. Neither marriage index carries her father's name, so the Paul who was her father must be read off the 1824 marriage scan. ¶3

Her son Paul, born 1826, is named for her own father Povel (Paul) Sivertssen, on the Selbu custom that put the second son's name on the mother's father. Her granddaughter Anne, born 1860, is named for her. ¶6

Life story

Age: born about 1799 on the stated age of 25 at her marriage in June 1824. ¶4

Baptism: born 12 January 1799 and baptised 20 January 1799 (Septuagesima Sunday), Selbu ministerialbok 1757-1801, 1799, page numbered 230, baptism no. 1: 'et Pigebarn, kaldet Anne; foed d: 12te Januar. Forældre Povel Siversen Alsæt og Marite Bersvensdatter' - a girl named Anne; parents Povel Sivertsen of Alsaet and Marit Bersvendsdatter. Faddere (godparents): Ole Ingebrigtsen Kielstad, Gunner Siversen Alsæt, Brynille Bersvensdatter 'ibidem' (of the same farm), Giertrud Povelsdatter Stamnæs and Karen Siversdatter Alsæt. Read and re-read from the scan crop on 13 Oct 2026. Born 12 January 1799 agrees with her age of 2 in the 1801 census and 25 at her marriage in June 1824. No other Anne was born to a Povel at Alsaet in 1798-99 (all entries). ¶7

1801 census: Selbu, census district 001, rural residence 0028 ALSETH, household 02, person 008 - 'Ane Poulsdatter', aged 2, 'deres datter' (their daughter) of Povel Sivertssen, 38, and Marit Bersvendsdatter, 40. Povel is entered as head of the second household on the farm, 'I faellesskab med moderen' - farming jointly with his mother, the widow Ane Gundersdatter, 65, who held the land in her own right as 'Bonde med jord'. Aged 2 on 1 February 1801 means born 1798 or 1799, which agrees with the 25 she gave at her wedding in June 1824; and she is the only Ane Poulsdatter of that age at Alsaet. ¶5

Marriage, read from the scans: The minister's book (ministerialbok 1816-1831, pp. 416-417 no. 25) dates the wedding 22 June 1824, in church: groom 'Bonde-Søn og Artillerist og Ungkarl Ingebrigt Jonsen Marstad' - a farmer's son, an artilleryman (soldier in the artillery) and a bachelor - age left blank; bride 'Gaardmd. Datt. Pigen Anne Poulsd. Aldsædt' - a farmer's daughter, unmarried, of Alsaet - age blank; forlovere (sureties for the marriage) 'Gmd. Ole Einersen Marstad og' with the second name never written. The form has no column for the fathers. The parish clerk's copy (klokkerbok 1816-1824, p. 406 no. 24) dates it 21 June 1824 and adds ages: 'Ingebrigt Johns. Mar[stad], Ungkarl og S[oldat?], 25 Aar' and 'P[igen] Anne Paulsd. Alsæth, 23 Aar' (the 3 is not certain), with forlovere 'Ole Einers. Mar[stad]' and a second name read only as 'Joh[n?] Olsen Sye[?]'. Read from the scans and the crops. ¶8

Marriage, what the scans add: she is described as a gaardmandsdatter, a farmer's daughter, of Alsaet, which agrees with her baptism as a daughter of Povel Sivertsen of Alsaet. The clerk's age of 23 (if the digit is a 3) would put her birth about 1801, two years later than her baptism of January 1799; the minister's copy leaves her age blank. The baptism is preferred. No father is named in either copy. ¶9

Marriage: married at Selbu church 22 June 1824 (minister) / 21 June 1824 (parish clerk) Ingebrigt Johnsen of Marstad. She is entered as 'Anne Poulsd, residence Aldsaedt' in the one copy and 'Anna Paulsd, b. 1799 calculated, residence Alsaeth' in the other - Alsaet, the farm she came off and where the couple then lived. ¶2

Records that disagree 1

Against: the age of 48 gives a birth about 1805, six years after her baptism of 12 January 1799 (she would have been 54); the position Husm. (husmann, cottager) does not fit a woman who married as a farmer's daughter and whose husband was a farmer's son; and no husband is named. No death is set. ¶10b

Alternatives considered and set aside 2

Burial candidate, not adopted: 'Husm. Anne Paulsdatter', aged 48, of Alset, died 30 September and buried 16 October 1853 (Selbu burials 1843-1859, p. 390 no. 64; scan). For: the exact name, the residence Alset (her birth farm and residence at her 1824 marriage and 1826), and her absence from the 1865 census. ¶10a

Burial candidate, not adopted: the only Anne Paulsdatter buried in Selbu 1826-1900 as an adult in the index is one aged 48 (b. about 1805) who died 30 Sep and was buried 16 Oct 1853 (Selbu ministerialbok 1843-1859, p. 390, no. 64; index pg00000006364035). No farm or husband is given, and the age is six years short of her 1799 baptism, so no date is entered; she was also not found in the 1865 census of Selbu (none of the 36 Anne Paulsdatters born 1795-1803 there lived in Selbu), which fits either a death before 1865 or emigration. ¶11
