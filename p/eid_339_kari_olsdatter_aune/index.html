About the family

Widow of Niels Eriksen Aune at his probate, Aune, Selbu, 26 Sep 1732 (24751 fol. 481b): 'hans efterladte Hustrue Kari O[la]fsdatter'. The patronymic is split over a line end ('O-/lafsdatter'); read it as 'Ch..ersdatter', and reads Olafsdatter (Olsdatter) at higher zoom, with some doubt. The record calls the five children 'deres tilsammen avlede', so she was the mother of all of them, including Giertrud, 6. ¶1

Her son Erik Nielsen Aune (7 in 1732), with his wife Ane Thomasdatter, had a son Niels baptised 27 Feb 1757 (16311 c3 L); the godmothers included 'Elle[n?] itm.' (of Aune, probably her daughter Elen) and 'Giertrud Garberg', which supports the identification of her daughter Giertrud with Giertrud Nielsdatter of Garberg. Grade unchanged (Probable). ¶3
