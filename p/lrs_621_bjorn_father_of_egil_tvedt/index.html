Life story

Probate 1729 (skifte no. 188): '1729 Novembris 7de' at the farm [Tvedt] in 'Treungens annex og Hvideseid Præstegield', the sheriff Hans Ellefsen Lung and the appraisers Ole Aarak(?) and Andbiørn Bræche registered the estate 'efter afdøde Margithe Biørnsdaatter, som ingen livsarvinger [efterlod]' (who left no children of her own), to be divided between her surviving husband 'Gulowe Haavorsøn' and her [late?] brother's children, 'Nafnlig: Orm Igildsøn 12 aar, Tone Igilsdaatter 20 aar, Kari og Walborg døttre 17 og 10 aar gamle', on whose behalf their guardian Salve [..]land attended. The index card (6856 view 1315) heads the heirs 'Broderbørn' (brother's children); the scan's margin and the line ending are faint, and 'broders' is read from the card more than the scan. Land 'part i Tvedt' is in the estate. Crops and -6856-v1315-tveit-treungen-1729.jpg. ¶1

Strengthened: Egil's own probate, 26 Apr 1720 at Tvedt in Treungen (25692 fol. 420b, view 432), calls him 'Eigild Biørensøn', so his father was named Bjørn, which agrees with his sister Margith Bjørnsdatter (1729). Still Probable: Bjørn himself is known only from his children's patronymics; no record of him was found. The 6856 Tveit cards (views 1302-1317) hold no probate for a Bjørn at Tveit 1689-1734, so he probably died before 1689 or elsewhere. ¶3a

Bygdebok (Treungen vol., Tveit nordre, canvas 104): 'Bjørn Tveit fødd omlag 1620', 44 in the 1664 count of Tveit, skifte after him 1672; wife Kari Torbjørnsdotter; children Orm, Eigil, Gro and Margit (m. into Felle). The book thinks it 'rimeleg' that he was a son of Eivind Tveit (from whom he inherited land) and that his farfar was Bjørn Torbjørnson (Tveit 1588); that is the author's inference, so no parents are linked. Birth set ABT 1620, death ABT 1672 from the book. ¶5a

1664 vicar's census: Treungen annex opens on p. 127 of 35508 (view 77, right page) with the full farms Aarak, Aarak, then a farm of 5 tønner 4 mæler whose name reads 'Tvede' (probably Tvedt; the size fits the large Tvedt farm), with four occupiers 'Bent 70, Biørn 44, Torr.. 34, Kari', and then a farm (read 'Frøer'?) with Alf 50, Biørn 44 and Knud 54. No son named Bjørn appears at Tvedt in 1664. So Bjørn, father of Egil and Margith, is either the occupier Biørn aged 44 (born about 1620; then he was about 55-65 when Egil was born, which is possible but late) or a man born after 1664. Not identified; this is a lead only. ¶4a

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the grade · 3 open questions

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Confidence: Probable as father of Egil (see his page). The 1729 probate of Margith Bjørnsdatter at Tvedt in Treungen names as her heirs her brother's children Orm Igildsøn (12, born about 1717), Tone, Kari and Walborg Igilsdøtre, so their father Egil (Igild) was her brother, and a full brother of a Bjørnsdatter was a son of Bjørn. Inferred, not stated: Egil's own patronymic is not written, and a half-brother through the mother cannot be excluded. Birth before 1680 is estimated from his grandchildren's ages (Tone 20 in 1729). ¶2
