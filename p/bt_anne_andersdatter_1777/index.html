About the family

Daughter of Anders Larsen of Almoen, buried 2 May 1802 as 'P.' - pige, an unmarried woman - of Almoen, born 1777. A child was baptized at Almoen to a father Anders on 23 February 1777, which is almost certainly her. ¶1
