About the family

Mother of Bexey Jane (Creed) Hicken. Inscribed 'Jane Chapman Creed, died June 17, 1906' on her husband James Creed's obelisk at Sturgeon (age weathered). 1881 census: Mary Creed, 48, of English origin, born P.E.I., Bible Christian, wife of James, Township 59. Married James Benjamin Creed on 22 February 1854 at Georgetown per the compiled Creed genealogy. Her own parents are not yet known. ¶1

Parentage (Speculative): probably a daughter of Henry Chapman Sen. of Murray Harbour. She was 'of Murray Harbour' when she married in 1854, and the only Protestant Chapman household there old enough to have a daughter born about 1832 is his. His English-born (Cornish, per a compiled family history) sons John (about 1816-1903) and Henry (about 1817-1899) farmed at Murray Harbour North and Henry belonged to the Bible Christian church, as the Creeds did. His eldest daughter Elizabeth married Henry Colcroft in October 1855 and was living two census pages from James and Jane Creed in Lot 59 in 1861, and Jane named her own second daughter Elizabeth Chapman Creed. No record yet read names Jane's father. ¶6

Parentage analysis: Her father is still unproved, and Henry Chapman Sen. of Murray Harbour stays a Speculative candidate. In favour: (1) she was born on PEI about 1832 and was 'of Murray Harbour' when she married in 1854, and the only Chapman family at Murray Harbour was that of the English brothers John (born about 1816) and Henry (born 1817), whose father was evidently Henry Sen. (an inference from his title 'Senior' in 1855); (2) the ages fit one family that left Cornwall after 1817 and before her birth about 1832, with Grace Chapman born in England about 1827-30; (3) her religion (Bible Christian in 1881, a Cornish and Devon denomination) matches Henry junior's household in 1861 and 1891; (4) Henry Sen.'s eldest daughter Elizabeth married Henry Colcroft in October 1855, twenty months after Jane married into Lot 59, and Colcroft is found in Lot 59, James Creed's own township, in 1861 and 1881; (5) Jane named her second daughter, born 1857, Elizabeth Chapman Creed, and her first son, born 1860, William Henry. ¶9a

Mother (Speculative): Elizabeth (Herring) Chapman, wife of Henry Chapman Sen. (married Minster, Cornwall, 1812), entered on the same Speculative footing as Henry Sen.'s fatherhood. The Chapman family of Minster and Forrabury had children baptised 1815-1828 (William, Henry, Elizabeth, Grace) and then none, consistent with emigration to PEI before Mary Jane's birth about 1832; Mary Jane named children Elizabeth Chapman Creed and William Henry Creed. No record names Mary Jane's parents; her 1906 PEI death registration (PARO, H0016) would settle both. ¶11

Life story

Wedding notice, The Islander, 10 March 1854: married at Georgetown on Wednesday 22 February 1854 by W. B. Aitken, J.P., to James Creed of Lot 59, Three Rivers; she is described as 'Miss Jane Chapman, of Lot 61, Murray Harbour' (the lot number as transcribed sits oddly with Murray Harbour, which lies in Lots 63 and 64). ¶3

Marriage: Jane Chapman married James Creed on 22 February 1854 (PEI marriage register index). She appears as Jane, Mary Jane and 'Jane Mary' in the baptism entries of her children and in the 1891 census. ¶4

1854 marriage residence: The Islander's notice (transcribed by R. J. Reid) calls the bride 'Miss Jane Chapman, of Lot 61, Murray Harbour', married at Georgetown on 22 February 1854 by W. B. Aitken, J.P. Seven years earlier, on 30 June 1847, the same justice married 'Mr. Samuel Moris, of Lot 61, to Grace Chapman, of Lot 63' at Georgetown. 'Lot 61' may therefore be a real residence rather than a slip: Jane may have been living in Lot 61, where Samuel and Grace (Chapman) Moore lived, while still counted as of Murray Harbour. ¶7

Marriage register: 'James Creed, B[achelor]' and 'Jane Chapman, S[pinster]', married by licence on 22 February 1854; witnesses William Creed and Philip Lees; certified by W. B. Aitken, J.P.; recorded 2 June 1854 (PEI marriage registers, p. 319; image read). The register gives no residence or parents. Her sister-candidates in the same registers: Elizabeth Chapman married Henry Coalcroft on 23 October 1855 with Henry Chapman as a witness (p. 486), and Henry Colcroft was a widower when he remarried on 23 August 1858, so Elizabeth died between those dates (inferred); Grace Chapman married Samuel Moore on 30 June 1847 by W. B. Aitken, J.P. (p. 279). ¶10

Chapman households 1861-1901: The 1861 census index lists only two Chapman heads in southern Kings County, both in Lot 63 (Murray Harbour): John Chapman (born about 1816 in Cornwall per a compiled family history; household of 7, wife Margaret Miller of Scotland) and Henry Chapman (born 1817 in England; household of 2, Bible Christian). In the 1881 and 1891 censuses they are still the only Chapman households there, joined by John's son Captain Edward Chapman (born 1846). The other PEI Chapmans of 1861 were in Lot 37, Queens County, and in Lot 46, a Catholic family. The 1881 transcription's 'Thomas Chapman, 60' of Lot 63 is Henry: the 1891 census shows the same couple, Henry, 74, and Helen, 60, with the same lodger, Walter Miller. ¶8

Gravestone (Find a Grave 75268579, photograph read): 'Jane Chapman Creed, died June 17, 1906, aged 74', on the obelisk of her husband James Creed. Aged 74 puts her birth about 1832. The 1901 census gives her birthday as 24 May, birthplace PEI and her origin as English; the year is hard to read (indexed 1835). In 1881 she was 48. ¶2

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Confidence moved from Probable to Confirmed by the marriage-register index (Jane Chapman to James Creed, 22 February 1854), the baptism entries of her children (Bexey Jane's among them) and the 1901 census. ¶5

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Against or unproven: no record names her parents; 'eldest daughter' does not mean she had a sister Jane; Elizabeth Colcroft had probably died by 1858, so the 1861 proximity of Henry Colcraft to the Creeds reflects his second marriage; and no other Chapman man at Murray Harbour was old enough to be her father. John (born 1816) and Henry (born 1817) were 15 or 16 when she was born, and Captain Edward was born in 1846, so all three are ruled out as her father. No Chapman head elsewhere in Kings County fits her place or religion. ¶9b

Open questions and the next record to read 1

What would settle it: her PEI death registration of June 1906, a June 1906 death notice, Henry Sen.'s estate file, an early Bible Christian baptism or membership entry, or the 1841 census of Lot 63. ¶9c
