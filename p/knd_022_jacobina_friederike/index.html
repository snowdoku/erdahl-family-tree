About the family

Children 1798-1799: Sabina Regina Knodel was baptised at Vaihingen on 1 February 1798 (parents 'Christoph Ludwig, Jakobina Friederika'), and Jacob Friderich Knodel on 4 November 1799 (parents 'Christoph Ludwig, Jacobina'). The 1799 mother is the same woman: Jakob Friedrich Knodel's marriage at Vaihingen on 8 October 1826 names his parents 'Christoph Ludwig, Jakobina Friederike'. So she was alive on 4 November 1799. Index only; images not seen. ¶3

Daughter's burial: Jacobina Dorothea Knodel was buried at Vaihingen on 19 June 1799, with the single relative 'Christoph Ludw.' (the father; the mother is not indexed). She was probably a daughter of this couple (inferred from the father's name and place); her baptism has not been found. The burial image (1184565-00481) would name the mother and give the age. ¶4

Life story

Birth (identification Probable): Jacobina Friderica 'Ramprecht', born Vaihingen 6 Apr 1759 to Jacob Fridrich x Maria Jacobina (record 4775787, image 1184562-00470; FamilySearch QPGK-LDW2). She is the only Jacobina Friederika of the Rampacher family indexed at Vaihingen (all Rampacher/Rambacher/Ramprecht rows at Vaihingen paged), and the only adult Jacob Friedrich Rampacher there was the father of this child (his son Jacob Friedrich, b. 1752, married only in 1776). Age 27 at the 1786 marriage, 31 at the 1791 one. Rival excluded: Jacobina Friederika 'Rappen' (Rapp), also daughter of a Jacob Friedrich, married Johann Christoph Brecht at Vaihingen on 20 Nov 1788 (records 904788133/4788133); she was a different woman (Jacobina Friederika Fressel was then a wife with a child born 7 Oct 1788), and the later records call our woman Rampacher, not Rapp or Brecht. ¶10

Death (inferred): her husband remarried in 1807, so she probably died between 1796 and 1807. Her burial has not been searched. ¶2

Death (narrowed): previously 'before 1807'. She was alive at her son Jacob Friderich's baptism on 4 November 1799, and her husband remarried at Vaihingen in 1807, so she died between 4 November 1799 and 1807 (inferred). Her burial is not found under Jacobina or Friederike Knodel (negative searches). ¶5

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

The case for this identification 4

Identity: first wife of Christoph Ludwig Knodel, married at Vaihingen an der Enz on 25 January 1791 (index spelling 'Jacobina Friederika'; also 'Jakobina Friederike', 'Jacob Friedrica', 'Jakobine Friedrike'). Her surname and parents are not in the index. Mother of Johann Christoph (1794) and Johann Gottlieb (1796) Knodel. Index entry only; image not seen; S0109-S0111 queued for the signed-in lane. ¶1

Surname and father (Nov 2026): the bride's own row of the 25 Jan 1791 Vaihingen marriage in the Württemberg Lutheran index (Ancestry 61023, record 904788300, image 1184562-00936) reads 'Jacobina Friederika Fessels', relatives 'Friedrich, Christoph Ludwig' (her father, then the groom). 'Fessels' is the genitive of Fessel (possibly Feßler); spelling to be checked on the image. Her grade (on her link to Johann Gottlieb Knodel) is unchanged. ¶6

Surname caution and lead: in Kurrent script 'ss' and 'y' are easily confused, so 'Fessels' may stand for 'Feyels'. A child of Friederich and Margaretha 'Feyel' (given name not indexed) was baptised at Enzweihingen, next to Vaihingen, on 18 Nov 1762 (Ancestry 61023 record 4608559, image 1184569-00194); she would be 28 in 1791. Not identified with her; the image would show the child's name. No Jacobina Friederika Fessel/Feyel baptism is in the index. ¶7

Maiden name and first marriage: Jacobina Friederika 'Kampacher' (= Rampacher, K/R misread), daughter of Jacob Friedrich, married Johann Christoph 'Fußel' (= Fessel/Fressel), son of Johann Friedrich, at Vaihingen an der Enz on 4 Jul 1786: bride row 904787916 'Jacobina Friederika Kampacher', relatives 'Jacob Friedrich, Johann Christoph'; groom row 4787916 'Johann Christoph Fußel', relatives 'Johann Friedrich, Jacobina Friederika' (image 1184562-00922). A daughter Jacobina Wilhelmina Fressel was born at Vaihingen on 7 Oct 1788 to 'Christoph Friedrich' Fressel x Jacobina Friederika (record 4785060; FamilySearch QPG2-WGRS). As 'Jacobina Friederika Fessels' (her married name) she married Christoph Ludwig Knodel at Vaihingen on 25 Jan 1791 (record 904788300); her sons' later records give her maiden name as Rampacher/Ramsacher (FamilySearch QPGK-RPRG, QPG2-WR7B). So the 'Friedrich' of the 1791 row is not her father but most likely her first husband's name or his father Johann Friedrich Fessel. This removes the contradiction noted. ¶9

Records that disagree 1

Contradiction resolved: the 1791 Vaihingen marriage label 'Friedrich Fessels' as the bride's Father (QPGK-ZG18/QPGK-ZG1P) is an indexing interpretation, not her parentage. The image is not viewable on FamilySearch (the record page offers only 'Image Available... on our partner's sites'; checked signed in), so it was not read; but two baptisms of her children by Christoph Ludwig Knodel, made while she was his wife, give her own surname: Johann Gottlieb Knodel, Vaihingen 14 Mar 1795, mother 'Jacobina Friederike' indexed surname 'Rambach' (QPGV-4PT1), and Sabina Regina Knodel, Vaihingen 1 Feb 1798, mother 'Jakobina Friederika' surname 'Rampacherin' (QPG2-43YM). So her maiden name was Rampacher, and 'Fessels' in 1791 (genitive) was her married name as the widow of Christoph Friedrich Fressel/Fußel (marriage 4 Jul 1786, QPGK-T6TY; their daughter Jacobina Wilhelmina Fressel b. 7 Oct 1788, QPG2-WGRS). In this index the genitive name after a bride is routinely put into the Father field; for a widow it is the late husband. The later records (son's marriage 1822 QPGK-RPRG 'Rampacher', son's burial 1854 QPG2-WR7B 'Ramsacher') agree. Test record if wanted: the 1791 image at Ancestry (1184562-00936), expected to read '...Fessels... hinterlassene Wittib'. Her own grade (on her link to her son Johann Gottlieb Knodel, who is Speculative in the tree as the Bessarabian emigrant) is not changed here. ¶12

Corrections 1

Correction: her maiden name was very probably Rampacher, not Fessel. 'Fessels' (Fressels) in the 1791 marriage is probably the surname of her first husband, Christoph Friedrich Fressel; a daughter Jacobina Wilhelmina Fressel was born to him and a Jacobina Friederika at Vaihingen on 7 Oct 1788 (FamilySearch QPG2-WGRS). Her son Johann Christoph Knodel's marriage (2 Jul 1822, QPGK-RPRG) indexes her as Rampacher, and his burial (12 Aug 1854, QPG2-WR7B) as 'Jakobine Friedrike Ramsacher'. See the Correction on Friedrich Fessel. Candidate baptism (not proved, so no parents entered): Jacobina Friderica 'Ramprecht', born 6 Apr 1759 at Vaihingen to Jacob Fridrich Ramprecht x Maria Jacobina (QPGK-LDW2). The same couple, indexed Rampacher, had Jacob Friederich on 19 Sep 1752 (QPGK-CB53). She would then have been six years older than Christoph Ludwig (born 1765), which fits a widow's remarriage. ¶8a
