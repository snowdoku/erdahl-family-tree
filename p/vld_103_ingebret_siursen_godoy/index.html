Life story

Baptism of a son: the Borgund index has Siur, son of Ingebret Siurss. of Godøen (Godøy), baptised 7 September 1701 (legitimate). The father's own patronymic says his father was a Siur, so naming his son Siur follows the usual pattern of naming the eldest sons after the paternal grandfather. That fits the hypothesis but proves nothing by itself. ¶1

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

3 on the grade · 1 search with no result · 3 open questions

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 3

Speculative father of Sivert (Siur) Ingebrigtsen of Djup (see his page). Why plausible: the 1701 baptism gives the right name (Siur), the right father's name (Ingebret, a spelling of Ingebrigt) and a birth year that fits Sivert's inferred birth about 1700 (from his first child in 1728). Godøy is the island next to Giske, where Djup lies, and the 1701 male census has no Ingebrigt at Djup, so a move from a neighbouring farm is natural. Of 23 Borgund baptisms 1695-1706 with a father Ingebret, this is the only child named Siur. What is missing: no record yet places this Siur at Djup; Sivert's known children (Nils, Hans, Elisabeth, Ingeborg, Marthe and a possible Synneve) include no Ingebrigt; and only 20 of 67 index rows for the name Siur in 1698-1702 were read. ¶2a

Upgrade: Speculative to Probable, graded on his link to his son Sivert (Siur) Ingebrigtsen of Djup (see his page). The Giske farm history (Borgund og Giske bd. 3, nb.no ff134a2c9670bf6b5cbe848b7a7f9404, canvas 496, Dyb bruk, rebuilt from content-search word boxes) names the Djup tenant as 'Sjur Ingebrigtsen Godøy (1701-66)', brukar 1727-66, married 1728 Ingeborg Pedersdtr. and 1743 Inger Rasmusdtr. Karlsbakk, with the next brukar Jørgen of Store Kalvøy (1735-84) married 1768 to 'Ingeborg Sjursdtr.' (f. 1744). Every detail matches Sivert of Djup in the tree (d. 1766, second wife Inger Rasmusdatter m. 1744, daughter Ingeborg b. 1744 m. 1768 Jørgen Paulsen). So the book places Sivert's birth in 1701 on Godøy, which fits the Borgund baptism of 7 Sep 1701 of Siur, son of Ingebret Siurss. of Godøen. The same book (canvas 469, Godøy) has 'Ingebrigt Sjursen (f. ca. 1660)', brukar 1690-1730, 'g. m. Margrete' (d. 1735, 75 år), and a son Lars Ingebrigtsen (ca. 1695-1728, canvas 476). ¶3a

Probable, not Confirmed: the bridge from the 1701 baptism to the Djup tenant rests on a compiled source (the gap words around 'Godøy' were not all recovered), not on a record naming Sivert as Ingebret's son. ¶3b

Searches that found nothing 1

Negative and lead: the Godøen cards in the Sunnmøre probate card index 1689-1728 (77797, images 957-975, probates 1697-1727) have no probate after Ingebrigt Sjursen or his wife Marrete. He may be a son of Ingebrigt and brother of Sjur Ingebrigtsen (unproved; Lars is not in the tree). ¶5
