Life story

Birth: the 1665 age (50) gives about 1615, the 1666 age (40) about 1626; the bygdebok has ca. 1625. Death: after 20 Mar 1666; there is no Kjøsnes probate card for him in 77804 (1689-1714 and 1727-42; the only Kjøsnes card is c995, Marithe Pedersdatter 1689), so he probably died before 1689. Name: the 1702 godfather 'Bersvend Vigen' is not he; the 1666 list has a Bersvend Olsen, 9, at Over-Vigen (son of Olluf Ingebrichtsen). ¶3

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

2 on the grade · 1 open question

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Graded Probable on his link to his son Ole (Olaf) Bersvendsen Kjøsnes (see his page). The vicar's manntall of 20 Mar 1666 (35528 view 24 left, Selbu no. 23 'Kiøsnæs') has 'Bersvend Olßen bruger 2 ørt er 40' as the first user, and the sons column, which lists all sons with their patronymics, begins 'Olle Bersvendss 9 aar' (the 9 is written over an 8). He is the only Bersvend at Kjøsnes and Olle the only Bersvendsen, so the 1666 list ties Olle Bersvendsen to Bersvend Olsen by patronymic and farm. The 1665 list (35528 view 17 left, p. 14) has 'Bersten' 50 on the first Kjøsnes holding (sons listed there only from about 12). ¶1

Why Probable: the step from the boy of 1666 to the farmer 'Ole Bersvendsen' 52 at Kjøsnes in the 1701 manntall (38461 c114) spans 35 years with no record between, and the ages disagree (1666 gives birth about 1657, 1701 about 1649). The bygdebok (Selbu i fortid og nåtid vol. 7, canvas 299) agrees with the 1666 age and makes the same identification: '-1661-1666- Bersven Olsen, f. ca. 1625. Sønner: 1. Ola, f. ca. 1657, [tok] over; 2. Tomas, f. ca. 1666', and '-1701-1713 Ola Bersvensen, f. ca. 1657'. No other Ole Bersvendsen is known in Selbu in 1666-1701. Patronymic plus farm across decades is Probable only. ¶2

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Test record: a probate after Bersvend Olsen Kjøsnes or his wife (Stjør- og Verdal skifteprotokoll 1, 1689-1701, Digitalarkivet 24749, if he died after 1689), or a court or land record (tingbok 28709 for 1698-1704, or the 1680s odel cases) naming Ole Bersvendsen as his son. His wife is unknown. ¶4
