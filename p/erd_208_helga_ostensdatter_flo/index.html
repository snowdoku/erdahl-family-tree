Life story

Aaland (Stryn vol., Nedre Flo bruk nr. 1) names Helga Øysteinsdatter (Østensdatter) as wife of Lasse Andersson Flo in 1646. Speculative as mother of Rasmus Lassesen Flo (see his page): Aaland does not say which wife bore Rasmus, and the 1639 deed speaks of a wife who inherited in Uppheim (from Oluf Uppheim's children), not named. Aaland also gives Rasmus Lassesen a wife '? Øysteinsd.', which may be a confusion with Helga. ¶1
