About the family

Wife of Hans Hansen of Bjørgesæter, Jevnaker parish. She was 48 in the 1845 census, which implies b. about 1797, and 58 in the 1853 emigration entry, which implies b. about 1795. She emigrated to America with her husband and son Peder on 15 Apr 1853. ¶1

Life story

PROBABLE baptism: Eli, baptized 29 Dec 1793 at Gran church ('Søndag mellom Juul og Nyt Aar'), daughter of Peder Nielsen of Fallang and Kiersti Gulbrandsdatter. The godparents were Jøel Hansen and Hans Gulbrandsen of Hvinden Eie, Ole Nielsen of Fallang, Kiersti Nielsdatter of Knarud and Mari Nielsdatter of Fallang. Her mother was churched on 9 Feb 1794. ¶3

In the 1801 census for Gran, farm Øvre Fallang, Eli Pedersdatter (7) lives in household 3 with her parents Peder Nilsen (37, gaardmand) and Kirsti Gulbrandsdatter (40). Household 1 on the same farm is her grandparents Nils Pedersen (63) and Eli Olsdatter (58), with their unmarried children Ole, Gulbrand, Anne and Ragnild Nilsen/Nilsdatter. ¶4

She was living at Fallang when she married Hans Hansen of Bjørgesæter on 7 Dec 1820 (age 25). One of the sponsors was Guldbrand Fallang, her uncle Gulbrand Nilsen. Her age fits the 1793 baptism (48 in 1845, 58 in 1853). She named her first daughter Kirsti (1822) after her mother. Her death in America is not found. ¶5

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Probable mother of Peter Hanson. Her parents (father Peder...) are not identified, and her marriage to Hans Hansen (about 1820-1822) was not found among the transcribed Jevnaker marriages. ¶2

Upgrade: Confirmed as the mother of Peter Hanson. The 1836 baptism (Jevnaker ministerialbok 1815-1837, p. 179 no. 20, scan read by an earlier worker) names Peder's parents as 'Hans Hansen og Eli Pedersdr. Bjørgesæteren'; the Jevnaker out-migration list of 15 Apr 1853 enters Hans Hansen (57), his wife Eli Pedersdatter (58) and their son Peder of Bjørgesæter, with Peder's birth as '1836, 2/2'; and in America Peter Hanson had his son baptised in 1867 in the Winnebago Lutheran church as 'Peder Hansen Bjørgesætren', and his 1922 obituary says he came in 1853 'with his parents'. Peter Hanson (see his page) is already Confirmed on this chain, with the birth-date conflict resolved from the 1836 scan; these primary records name the parent with him, so the parent link is upgraded with it. She is the 'Elie Peders Datter, pigen, Fallang', aged 25, who married Hans Hansen of Bjørgesæter on 7 Dec 1820 (Gran ministerialbok 1815-1824, p. 429 no. 4), which ties her to the Fallang family. ¶6
