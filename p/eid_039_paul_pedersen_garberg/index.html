Life story

Baptism of his daughter: 'Döbt (2) Kari. Forældr: Paul Pers: Garberg og Giertrud Nielsd:' - baptised Kari (Karen), parents Paul Pedersen of Garberg and Giertrud Nielsdatter, on the 25th Sunday after Trinity, 12 November 1758 (date computed from Easter 26 March 1758). Godparents Per Bjornsen Garberg, Haftor Pedersen, Ingebor Garberg, Guri of the same farm and Ane Ovne[?]. Selbu ministerialbok 1757-1801, canvas 7 left page and the crop. ¶1

Baptism (Probable identification): Jubilate Sunday, 8 May 1729, 'döbt [Peder, struck over] Pouls: Garbergs B: Nom: Poul' - Peder Poulsen Garberg's child Poul (Selbu ministerialbok 1699-1737, canvas 117 left page, crop); the mother was churched as 'Peder Pouls Garbergs' wife on Whit Monday, 6 June 1729 (canvas 117 right). The bygdebok's Pål Persen, holder of Garberg 1760-1800, born 1729, married about 1754 Gjertrud (vol. 3, p. 632, word boxes), agrees with the year, the farm and his wife Giertrud Nielsdatter. ¶5

Named 'Povel Pedersen, 12 år gl.' among the children in the probate after his father Peder Paulsen Garberg, 20 Jun 1741 (card 77804 c545); his guardian was Ingebrigt Ingebrigtsen Kjelstad, his mother's sister's husband. ¶10

Named in his mother's probate: the skifte after the widow Mali Haaversdatter Garberg, 21 Oct 1761 (24755 fol. 110b-111a), lists 'Sønnen Povel Pedersen 30 aar' first among her six children by her late husband Peder. This confirms his parents a second time, after his father's 1741 probate. No probate after Paul himself is in the typed registers of 24753-24762. ¶13

Alive through the 1790s: surety or sponsor as Povel Pedersen Garberg in 1792 (16311 c146, c149), Apr 1794, May 1795, Apr 1796, and as 'Povel Pedersen ælder [the elder] Garberg' in Apr-Sep 1797 and Jun 1798 (c207, c211, c213, c225; -298_). His burial is not in 16311 up to Mar 1799 - he died after mid-1798 (1799-1801 burials in c237-260 also read without him; next: the indexed burials from 1801). ¶11

Later life (lead): a 'Povel Pedersen Garberg' stood cautioner at Selbu marriages in 1797 and 1798 (canvases 207 and 225) and at baptisms in the 1780s. This is probably Paul himself, though a son Peder Povelsen also lived at Garberg (married 1784), so the name could also be read as belonging to a grandson. His death and probate are not yet found. ¶4

1801 census: a second Poul Pedersen lived at Garberg - household 08, 'Poul Pederssen', 68, farmer and holder, with wife Marit Guttormsdatter, 64, both in their first marriage, son Guttorm 24; his son Peder Poulssen, 30, farmed jointly with him (household 10). That Poul never married Giertrud Nielsdatter, and Paul himself is absent from the 1801 census (the bygdebok gives Pål Persen's dates as 1729-1800). Two Pouls therefore grew up at Garberg, and the ages fit best with the 1735 Poul (Peder Bjørnsen's) being the 1801 husband of Marit Guttormsdatter (68 in 1801 = born about 1733) and the 1729 Poul (Peder Poulsen's) being Paul, husband of Giertrud (bygdebok: born 1729). The parent link to Peder Poulsen Garberg is kept at Probable. ¶8

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Why the link to Karen Poulsdatter (see her page) is Confirmed: she married in 1784 as 'Karen Povelsd. Garberg' (of Garberg); she was buried in January 1843 aged 84 as 'Karen Paulsdatter Uglum', which fits a birth in late 1758 (her age of 43 in February 1801 is one year more than a November 1758 baptism gives, a usual census error); Kari of 1758 is the only Kari/Karen with a Paul/Povel father at Garberg in complete read of the baptisms January 1757 - February 1767. Her own children carry this family's names: two daughters Giertrud (1786, 1792) after Giertrud Nielsdatter, and sons Povel (1795) and Poul (1799) after Paul. Her brothers stood sponsor to her children: Niels Garberg (1787) and Jon Garberg (1790); 'Jon Povelsen Garberg' was godfather to her daughter Mali in 1796 - the Jon baptised to Paul and Giertrud in 1764. ¶3

Alternatives considered and set aside 2

Rival baptisms (review): two other Pouls were baptised to a Peder at Garberg. (1) Maundy Thursday, 13 April 1724: 'Peder Garbergs barn Pouel' (Selbu ministerialbok 1699-1737, canvas 93 right), almost certainly Peder Poulsen's first son of the name; the bygdebok (vol. 3, p. 632) lists 'Pål, 1724, død' and 'Pål, 1729... bruket' under Per married 1719 to Mali, i.e. the 1724 boy died and the 1729 namesake took over the farm. His burial has not been found. (2) 16th/17th Sunday after Trinity (25 September or 2 October) 1735: 'döbt Peder Biörnz Garberg Nom: Poul' - Peder Bjørnsen Garberg's son Poul (same register, canvas 137 right). ¶7

Not identified in the 1801 census: the only Garberg candidate is 'Poul Pederssen' b. 1733 with a different wife, and no indexed burial 1801-70 fits; he was alive as 'Povel Pedersen ælder' in Jun 1798, so he probably died 1798-1801 in a burial entered without his name or farm, or elsewhere. ¶12

Open questions and the next record to read 1

What would settle the parentage: Paul's burial about 1800 (Selbu ministerialbok 1757-1801, Digitalarkivet 16311, last canvases; not indexed online), whose age at death would distinguish a 1729 from a 1735 birth. Against the 1729 identification: Per Bjørnsen Garberg and a 'Guri' of Garberg headed the godparents of Paul's daughter Kari in 1758, which could mean they were her grandparents (Peder Bjørnsen's second wife was Guri Poulsdatter, married 1733); they were also neighbours on the same farm. ¶9
