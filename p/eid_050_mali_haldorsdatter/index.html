About the family

Mother of Haldo Torgersen (see his page). Baptism of her son: 'Döbt: Haldor: forældr: Torger Erichsen Svinaasen, Mali Haldorsd. Fadd: Bersvend H. Solum, Jon B. Sandvig, Ole Ols. Svinaas, Marit Svinaas, Ingeborg Sandvig[en]' - baptised Haldor, parents Torger Eriksen of Svinaas and Mali Haldorsdatter, in the entry just before the heading for the 1st Sunday in Advent (2 December) 1770, so about 18-25 November 1770. Selbu klokkerbok 1766-1776 (source 16316), canvas 65, column 4, crop. She was churched ('introd.') as 'Torger Erichsen Svinaasens K[one] Mali Haldorsd.' at the late-December 1770 service (canvas 66, column 1). ¶1

Daughter, probable: Marit, baptised on the 15th Sunday after Trinity, 27 September 1767, 'Par: Torger Erichs: Qvello, Mali Haldorsd.', godparents Jon Samstad, Peder Solum, Ingeborg Lien, Birthe Qvello, Ingeborg Flønes (Selbu ministerialbok 1757-1801, canvas 23 left page, crop). In 1767 the couple lived at Kvello, not Svinaas; the identical pair of names three years apart, with the 1770 entry calling Torger 'Svinaasen', makes them almost certainly the same couple who moved from Kvello to Svinaas between 1767 and 1770. Marit is therefore a probable elder sister of Haldo; not entered as a separate person. 'Torger Qvellos hustru' was churched in October-November 1767 (canvas 23 right). ¶4

Life story

Marriage: betrothed to Torger Eriksen on Palm Sunday (30 Mar) 1760 and married on the 3rd day of Pentecost (27 May) 1760 (16311 c9 R, zoomed): 'Trolov: (1) Torger Erichs: og Mali Haldorsd:' and 'copul: (1) Sold: Ingebricht Ols: og Ingebor Haldorsd: (2) Torger Erichs: og Mali Haldorsd:'. No farm, father or sponsors are given, so the entry does not name her father. On the same day the soldier Ingebrigt Olsen married an Ingebor Haldorsdatter, a possible sister (lead only). A 'Mali Haldorsd.' was also godmother on the 25th Sunday after Trinity 1760 at the baptism of Haftor, son of Jon Andersen and Kari Haftorsdatter, with Per Gunnersen[?] Garberg (16311 c10 R). Their son Erik was baptised on 18 Oct 1761 (16311 c11 R). ¶12

Burial, 10th Sunday after Trinity (20 Aug) 1786: 'Begravne: 1, Mali Haldorsd. Svinaas, død 16. aug: gl. 57[?] a.' - died 16 Aug 1786 aged 57 (second digit possibly 2), so born about 1729 (or 1734) (16311 canvas 123 left). This narrows 'before 1 Apr 1787': her widower Torger Eriksen Svinås was betrothed again on 1 Apr 1787 (c125 L). ¶5a

Probate read: Stjør- og Verdal sorenskriveri, Skifteprotokoll 9-3 (Digitalarkivet 24762), fol. 613b-614a, typed register entry 'Mali Haldorsdatter, Torger Eriksens hustru, Svinås, Selbu, 1786, fol. 613 b.' The entry opens 'Anno 1786 den 12te[?] October' on the farm Svinaas in Selbo Tinglag, after the late 'Mali Haldorsdatter', between the widower 'Gaardmand Torger Eriksen' and the children born to the deceased in her marriage to him, two sons and one daughter: '1. Sønnen Erik Torgersen 26 Aar gl., 2. Sønnen Haldo Torgersen 16 Aar gl., 3. Datteren Ingeborg Torgersdatter 7 Aar gl.' The father acted for the minors; the witnesses (vurderingsmenn) were Ole Baardsen Kjøsnes and Jon Olsen Sandvigen. No guardian from her own family (morbror) is named, so the probate does not identify her parents. It does make Haldo Torgersen (see his page) and Ingebor Torgersdatter (see her page) her children by a primary record, and it adds an elder son Erik, born about 1760. ¶7

Marriage date moved earlier: her son Erik Torgersen was 26 in October 1786, so she and Torger Eriksen were married by about 1760, not 1766-67 as earlier searches assumed. The copulation should be looked for in Selbu ministerialbok 16311 canvases 2-11 (1757-61), or before 1757 in the 1747-56 gap with no surviving book. Canvas 19 (January-July 1766) was without a Torger Erichsen marriage. ¶8

Torger Eriksen's first wife. The 1801 census calls Torger's marriage to Magli Guttormsdatter (married 1787) his second; Mali Haldorsdatter is the earlier wife and must have died before his betrothal on 1 April 1787. Her burial has not been found. Her patronymic shows her father was a Haldor; her own birth is unknown (probably about 1730-1745). ¶3

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Why the link to Haldo is Confirmed: the baptism names Torger Eriksen of Svinaas, the only Torger at Svinaas in the 1801 census, where Haldo Torgersen, 30 (born 1770-71, matching November 1770), works the farm 'in partnership with his father' Torger Erikssen; Haldo carries her father's name Haldor; and his 1832 burial age of 63 is within the usual error. The record states the mother explicitly. ¶2

Corrections 1

Correction: death set to 16 Aug 1786. A 'Mali Haldorsd. Langsæt', d. 26 Apr 1785 aged 71 (c118), is a different woman. ¶5b

Searches that found nothing 4

Marriage search, partial negative: 16311 canvases 20-21 (Selbu ministerialbok, 6th Sunday after Trinity 1766 to Cantate Sunday 1767, both pages read at full resolution) contain no betrothal or marriage of Torger Erichsen with Mali Haldorsdatter. With their daughter Marit baptised 27 Sep 1767, the marriage is earlier than July 1766 (16311 c2-19) or took place in another parish. ¶6

Probate search, negative: the handwritten register of Stjør- og Verdal skifteprotokoll 10, 1785-1796 (24763), was read for the M page (view 8) and the S/T page (view 11). There is no estate for Mali Haldorsdatter or for Torger Erichsen of Svinaas. The Selbu entries are Mali Olsd. Mogaard (fol. 216), Malie Olsd. Store Evjen (537), Sivert Halvorsen Berge (206), Sivert Bergersen Haave (333), Thomas Thomassen Vangen (207) and Torkil Pedersen Eidum (248). So her 1786 estate, if one was held, is not in this volume's register. ¶9

Marriage search, partly negative: 16311 canvas 21 left (6th-21st Sunday after Trinity 1766, July to October) has no marriage of Torger Erichsen. The 1766 marriages on this page are recorded only as 'Copulerit' with the bride's and groom's names, with no farm and no father, so finding the entry would add little beyond the date. ¶10

Negative, testing the Berge candidate: the skifte after Haldor Tostensen at Berge, 10 Jun 1766 (24755 fol. 565, view 587 right), names his widow Malie Thomasdatter and his children: of the first marriage (to Marit Mogensdatter) Arnt Haldorsen, a second son, and 'Marethe Haldorsdatter gift med Peder Thomassen Grotum'; of the second marriage Marithe 24, Ingeborg 21, Karie 20 and Berethe 17. There is no Mali and no daughter married to Torger Eriksen, so the 'Maret Haldorsdatter' of the 1741 Berge division (pantebok A-1 p. 354) is Peder Grøtem's wife, and Haldor Tostensen of Berge is ruled out as Mali's father. ¶13

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Lead only, no link: the Berge candidate family is now documented from a land record. The pantebok A-1 (19750, p. 354, view 356) records the division ('udlæg') after Marit Mogensdatter of Berge, skifte dated 3 May 1741, to her widower Haldor Tostensen and their children Arn, Tosten and Mogens Haldorsen and Brynild and Maret Haldorsdatter. A 'Maret Haldorsdatter' born before 1741, with parents Haldor and Marit, would fit the naming of Torger Eriksen's first two children, Marit (1767) and Haldor (1770). But the record says Maret, not Mali, and does not reach 1767, so this is a candidate only. The test is Haldor Tostensen Berge's own probate, Stjør- og Verdal skifteprotokoll 5 (24755) fol. 565, c.1765 (typed register), which should name his married daughters and their husbands; or the Torger-Mali marriage (16311). ¶11
