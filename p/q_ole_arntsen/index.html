About the family

Children, now complete from the registers and the 1801 census: Gunnil (b. about 1789), Arnt (bapt. 1792), Marit (bapt. 1 Feb 1795 - the ancestor), a second Marit (b. about 1796-97), an infant Marit who died in September 1801, Lars (bapt. 19 Apr 1799) and Paul (bapt. 1802). ¶2

The naming of his children follows the Trøndelag pattern exactly: the first son Arnt for his own father, the second son Lars for his wife's father, and two daughters called Marit - which points to his own mother being a Marit. ¶3

Life story

Baptism: no Ole son of an Arnt (spelled Arnt, Arent, Arnd or Arndt) was baptised in the indexed Stjørdal register between November 1755 and 1762. That register begins in November 1755, so a baptism in 1755 would be in the unindexed register for 1740-1756 (Digitalarkivet source 16370), which has not yet been read. ¶7

Military rolls, continued: the 1769 roll of the company's young men (hovedlegdsrulle, ungt mannskap) does not list him; at about 14 he was probably below roll age, since the youngest men listed are about 17-19. In 1769 the holder of a quarter of Geiteråsen was Svend, aged 40, not an Arnt. He is also absent from the 1789 roll of march-ready men. None of the surviving rolls for the company in the Generalitets- og kommissariatskollegiet series, 1769-1789, names his father. ¶6

Military rolls: Ole does not appear in the 1780 company roll of the Stjørdal company (Stjørdalske kompani) of the 1st Trondhjem national infantry regiment, which lists every soldier by legd (recruiting district) with age, year enrolled and farm but no father. The Geiteråsen (Gedderaas) legd soldier in 1780 was Peder Jonsen, 21. The only Ole Arntsen in that roll is legd 32 in Skatval, 'Ole Arnsen Ældstad[?]', 19 - six years too young and from another parish, so probably a different man. Ole of Geiteråsen therefore probably enlisted between 1781 and 1787, when his marriage entry calls him a soldier. ¶5

As a bachelor and soldier he married Kjersten Larsdatter of Stræte on 25 Jun 1787. Farmer at Geiteråsen (Gedderaas). 1801 census: 45, with children Gunnil 12, Arnt 9 (bapt. 1792), Marit 6, Marit 4 and Lars 2. Buried 1811, born 1755. Godparents from Stræte at his children's baptisms fit his wife's origin. ¶1

Land records: he took Geiteråsen (Gidderaas, in Hegra annex) on a lease (bygselbrev) of 17 Mar 1787 from its owner, Cancelliraad Erich Müst, as 'Soldat Ole Arntsen' (Stjør- og Verdal pantebok C-2 fol. 431b, source 19756 view 128; indexed in the Stjørdal panteregister 1762-1800, source 22318 image 87). The farm had earlier been used by a man read as Iver Iversen (an Iver was father at Geiteråsen baptisms 1769-1777). He therefore came onto the farm from outside in the year of his marriage, so his father Arnt need not have lived at Geiteråsen; look for him in the soldier's home legd elsewhere in Stjørdal or Hegra. ¶13

Appraiser in 1790: 'Ole Arntsen Giederaas' was one of the two appraisers at the probate after Anne Jonsdatter of Almoen, 13 Nov 1790 (Stjør- og Verdal skifteprotokoll 10, p. 232, Digitalarkivet 24763 view 246). So he was living at Geiteråsen and of age by 1790. ¶11

Buried 16 June 1811, entered as 'Gaardmand' of Gedderaas, born 1755. He had risen from the 'Ungkarl soldat' - unmarried enlisted soldier - of his 1787 marriage entry to the working farmer of the 1801 census and of this burial. ¶4

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade (as father of Maret Olsdatter): Records: (1) marriage at Værnes 4 Jun 1816 (Stjørdal ministerialbok 1815-1819, p. 195 no. 35): bride 'Mærit Olsdatter', Pige (unmarried woman), born 1795 in Stjørdal, residence Gedderaas; groom Ole Tomesen, bachelor cottager of Almoeaasen. (2) 1801 census, Stjørdal, rural residence 0007 Giedderaas: household 01 is Ole Arntsen 45, gaardmand, and Kirsten Larsdatter 42, both in their 1st marriage, with 'Deres børn' (their children) Gunnil 12, Arnt 9, Marit 6, Marit 4 and Lars 2; household 02 is the cottager Iver Iversen 61, his wife Gullov Joensdatter 69 and their daughter Anne Iversdatter 27. No other Marit Olsdatter lives at Giedderaas. (3) baptism 1 Feb 1795: Marit, daughter of Ole Arentsen and Kirsten Larsdatter of Gedderaas. These primary records together make him the father of Maret Olsdatter, wife of Ole Tomasen, so he moves from Probable to Confirmed. His own parentage (mother Marit Larsdatter, father unknown) is not affected and stays as it was. ¶12

Corrections 1

Correction: the 1755 Skatval candidate is ruled out. Ole son of 'Arn Fløg' - Arn Olsen of Fløan/Fløgjerdet in Skatval, whose name is given in full at the burial of another of his children on 5 May 1754 - was buried at Skatval on 5 May 1756 from Fløgjerdet as 'Ole Arnsen', aged 1 year 2 months (indexed Stjørdal register 1755-1780, burials 1756 p. 8 no. 4), and Arn's next son, Jon, was baptised there on 6 Feb 1757. No other Ole son of an Arn, Arnt or Arent appears in the Stjørdal registers from January 1754 to 1762, and no Arn(t) or Marit is recorded at Geiteråsen before 1781 (residents then: Gunder and Iver families). Ole's father is still unknown; he may have come to Geiteråsen from elsewhere after 1769. ¶9

Searches that found nothing 2

Baptism search, 1755: the unindexed Stjørdal register for 1740-1756 (Digitalarkivet source 16370, chronological list) was read entry by entry for all of 1755 up to its last entry on 31 October 1755 (canvases 218-228). The only Ole whose father was an Arn(t) is a child of 'Arn Fløg[s]' - Arn of Fløan, a farm in the Skatval annex - baptised at Skatval on 8 March 1755. His godparents were Margret Stoen[?]rigg, Sara Forbord, Lars Fløg, Jens Alstad and Peder Hegge, all Skatval names; the mother is not named, and Arn's wife was churched at Skatval three weeks after Easter. This Ole is a possible, not a proved, identification with Ole Arntsen of Geiteråsen in Hegra: the name and year fit, but the family lived in Skatval, not Hegra, and the entry gives no mother. Ole's baptism in 1753-1754, if he was born earlier than his burial suggests, has not yet been searched. ¶8

Father, probate search: no probate for an Arnt, Arent or Arn of Geiteråsen, or of anywhere in Hegra, appears in the registers of the Stjør- og Verdal probate books for 1742-1802. Arent Joensen of Saltøen in Skatval (died 1773) is ruled out because his widow was Marit Pedersdatter. Possible lead only: at Medkiil (Mekiln) in Hegra, the probate after Berit Arntsdatter on 24 February 1752 names her widower, Ole Arntsen of Medkiil, and two sons, Arnt, aged about 16, and a younger boy aged about 3½ (the ages and the younger son's name are uncertain readings). An Arnt Olsen of Medkiil born about 1736 could have named a son Ole after his own father. Against this, Marit Larsdatter's burial age makes her about 24 years older than him. This is unproved, and it would be settled by a record naming the wife of Arnt Olsen of Medkiil. ¶10
