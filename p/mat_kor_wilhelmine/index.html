About the family

Parentage (Speculative, 2026 review): Johann Farowsky (c.1803-1881) of Beresina is entered as her father by a first, unnamed wife. This is a candidate, not a proved link. For: her children's baptisms spell her maiden name Farowski, Farrowski and Farowskye, the Beresina form, and Johann's is the only Farowsky household indexed at Beresina; a Wilhelmina Farowsky bore Louisa Dorsch at Beresina in December 1858; and the 1860 marriage extract's remark 'PB 2-84' points to a Beresina family-book house, so her family was probably at Beresina rather than at Tarutino (the Tarnaske rival). ¶6a

Mother of Gottlieb Knodel (1879-1958), reassessed (Oct 2026; stays Probable): the Friedenstal baptism of 1879 (St Petersburg Consistory duplicate, FHL film 1895629 item 1, p.300, entry 12; image read) names the mother as Wilhelmine née Turowsky, wife of Ludwig Knodel. The link stays Probable because Gottlieb's identity with that child is Probable (see his notes). Her marriage to Ludwig on 4 February 1860 is read in the Klöstitz register (entry 11) and the Consistory duplicate (fol. 450 no. 17). Her parentage remains unproved. ¶10

Parentage, reassessed (Oct 2026; stays Speculative): Johann Farowsky (c.1803-1881) of Beresina remains her candidate father, by an unnamed first wife. The new evidence does not weaken the link, and on balance it helps. Her age of 21 in 1860 (born about 1838/39) fits a daughter born when Johann was about 35, before his 1851 marriage to Caroline Zarbock. The age also rules out the rival candidate Wilhelmina Tarnasky, born at Tarutino on 10 February 1843, who would have been 16. The readings Turowsky, Jarowsky and Tarowsky all lack the 'n' of Tarnasky/Tarnowsky and are compatible with Farowsky given the usual T/F/J confusion. Two caveats remain. The first letter is unresolved on both images. The family-book reference II/84 points to the Roessler household at Beresina, not to Johann Farowsky's household (house 83). ¶11a

Mother of Gottlieb Knodel (1879-1958), upgraded to Confirmed (Oct 2026): the Friedenstal baptism of 1879 (FHL 1895629/1 p.300 no.12; image read) names the mother as Wilhelmine née Turowsky, wife of Ludwig Knodel, and the Megantic manifest of 22 Jul 1928 (LAC T-14745 frame 00366) gives the Canadian Gottlieb Knodel's birthplace as Friedenstal and his age as 49, which ties the baptised child to Erna Knodel's father. Her maiden-name spelling (Tarowsky, Turowsky, Farowski and variants) is not affected. ¶12

Parentage, upgraded from Speculative to Probable (Oct 2026): Johann Farowsky (see his page) and Rosina née Bomm (see her page) are entered as her probable parents. For: (1) Wilhelmina Farofsky, born at Beresina on 7 Jan 1838 to Johann and Rosina née Bomm (FHL 1882648/2 p.72 no.3), is the only birth of a Wilhelmina of this surname in any spelling in the Bessarabian extracts 1815-59, and Johann's is the only Farowsky household indexed at Beresina; (2) the 1860 marriage was performed by Klöstitz parish, which served Beresina, and every image reading of her maiden name ends in '-rowsky'; Johann's own family is written both ways (Johann Tarowsky at his 1851 marriage; his daughter Christina entered as 'Faroske [Tarowsky]' in 1861; Friedrich Farowski m. Karolina Betzlaff 1882 and Jakob Tarowsky b. 1883 to Friedrich and Karoline Retzlaff); (3) her stated age of 21 in February 1860 rules out the Tarutino rival Wilhelmina Tarnasky (b. 1843, then 16), and is within one year of the 1838 birth (she would have been 22 years 4 weeks), a common size of error in marriage ages. The Louisa Dorsch entry was weighed: a Wilhelmina Farowsky is mother of Louisa Dorsch, born at Beresina on 4 Dec 1858, father 'Friedrich' (FHL 1884077/1 p.135 no.94). No Dorsch x Farowsky marriage is in the 1850-59 marriage extracts, and the Wahl index places 'Dorsch, Luisa 1858' in Johann Farowsky's own household entry (Beresina book 83a p.100 no.106, FHL 1768263 item 1), together with his Schmolke stepchildren. A married Wilhelmina Dorsch would have raised her daughter in a Dorsch household; a child kept in the grandfather's household points instead to Johann's own daughter as the mother, most probably unmarried (inferred; the extract carries no illegitimacy remark, and its omission is not conclusive). The main friction: the child is entered under the father's surname Dorsch, which normally marks a legitimate birth, whereas illegitimate children were usually entered under the mother's surname. If Louisa was legitimate, the 1838 daughter was a married woman in 1858 and could not be the single (ledig) bride of 1860, and the bride would have to be an otherwise unrecorded namesake. The Probable level therefore rests on reading the 1858 birth as illegitimate; no Friedrich Dorsch is found at Beresina in the extracts or the Wahl index (the Dorsch family is at Gnadental and Lichtental). No second Wilhelmina Farowsky appears in any birth, census or family-book entry. The 1858 mother is therefore best read as the 1838 daughter, and that reading fits rather than contradicts the 1860 bride being single (never married): she married Ludwig Knodel 14 months later and left Louisa with her father at Beresina. Against, still open: the one-year age difference; the 1860 extract's family-book reference 'PB 2-84' points to Samuel Roessler's house, not Johann's (83), which may mean she was living or in service there; and no record yet names the bride's father. It is not Confirmed because the identity rests on a single-candidate argument. ¶14a

Life story

Birth (estimated): about 1838. She was single and 21 years old at her marriage on 4 February 1860 (Klöstitz marriage register 1860, entry 11; image read), so she was born between February 1838 and February 1839 if the stated age is exact. ¶9

Neither candidate is proved, and no parents are entered. The decisive record is the 1860 marriage entry itself (Kloestitz register no. 191, FHL film 038902; or Consistory duplicate FHL 1884080/1 p.450 no.17), which normally gives the bride's age and father. The Beresina family book 1841-60 (Johann Farowsky, house 83; FHL 1766557 item 2 p.114) would also settle it. ¶5

The probable mother of Gottlieb Knodel (b. 1879). Her surname is spelt Tarnowsky, Tarowsky, Farowski, Farrowski and Farowskye in the extracts (the T/F confusion is a transcription of the Cyrillic/German script). Married Ludwig Knodel on 4 February 1860 (Klöstitz parish, Bessarabia). A Farowsky/Tarowsky family lived at Beresina, Bessarabia: Johann Farowsky, born in Poland, died there on 28 Jan 1881 aged 78, husband of Karoline Zarbock (married at Tarutino 7 Sep 1851). He may be Wilhelmine's father - not proved. ¶1

Spelling: her children's entries spell her surname Farowski, Farrowski, Farowskye, Farowsky, Tarowsky and Tarnowsky. Farowsky is the form used for the Beresina family of that name. The Kloestitz register extractor read only 'F...'. ¶2

Against: house 84 is Samuel Roessler's household, not Johann Farowsky's (his children are marked 'pb 83'), so the remark does not put her in his house. ¶6b

Corrections 1

Correction (name): the surname 'Tarnowsky', taken from the odessa3 extract of her son Gottlieb's 1879 baptism, is not supported by the images. The 1879 baptism image reads 'Turowsky' (first letter uncertain; possibly 'Furowsky'). The 1860 Consistory marriage duplicate reads 'Jarowsky' (or 'Tarowsky'; its reader judged 'Jarowsky' the more likely). Earlier extracts give Tarowsky, Farowsky, Farowski, Farrowski and Farowskye, and the Walker extract of the Klöstitz register gives 'F...'. Every reading of an image has the ending '-rowsky', with no 'n', and only the first letter varies (T, F or J, often confused in German script). The name is entered as Tarowsky, the form common to the Consistory marriage extract and one reading of its image. Jarowsky, Turowsky and Farowsky remain possible; the form is provisional until the left page of the 1860 marriage entry or a family book is read. ¶8

Alternatives considered and set aside 5

Candidate family (B), the better fit: Johann Farowsky (Tarowsky), born in Poland about January 1803, died at Beresina on 28 January 1881 aged 78 years 23 days. He married Caroline Zarbock (b. 1827 Tarutino) at Tarutino on 7 September 1851, and they had children at Beresina from 1853 to 1868. A Wilhelmine born about 1840 could only be his daughter by an earlier wife. In favour: Beresina belonged to Kloestitz parish, which performed the 1860 marriage, and brides normally married in their home parish. A 'Wilhelmina Farowsky' is named as mother of Louisa Dorsch, born at Beresina on 4 December 1858 to a father 'Friedrich'. If that is the same woman, she had a child about 14 months before her marriage to Ludwig Knodel. ¶3

Candidate family (A): Wilhelmina Tarnasky was born at Tarutino on 10 February 1843. Her parents were Gottlieb Tarnaske (b. Poland about 1808, 'Jankow, Prussia', d. Tarutino 5 April 1851) and Susanna Schmalz, who had married at Alt Posttal on 25 February 1834. This Wilhelmina would have been 16 years 11 months old at the 1860 wedding. Tarutino had its own parish, which makes a Kloestitz marriage less likely. The Tarutino Tarnaskes and the Beresina Farowskys were very probably one clan from Prussian Poland: Zarbock wives occur in both. ¶4

Friedenstal 1885, a lead (unproved, probably not this woman): Katharina Baumann, an illegitimate child, was born at Friedenstal on 25 Jul 1885 and died there on 28 Aug 1885 aged 1 month 2 days. The birth extract names the mother 'Baumann, Wilhelmine' (FHL 1897694/2 p.340 no.40); the death extract names her 'Knodel, Wilhelmine' (p.376 no.24). The two extracts may describe one woman under two surnames. The Wahl index of the Friedenstal family book 1881-1900 (FHL 1767875 item 2) lists a Wilhelmina Baumann on page 63 (frame 281), the same page as Gottlieb Knodel (b. 1826) and Wilhelm Knodel (b. 1860) of the other Friedenstal Knodel family; she is the most likely mother, and the death extract's 'Knodel' may reflect that household. Ludwig's daughter Wilhelmina (b. 27 Aug 1861) is a weaker candidate, and this Wilhelmine, then in her forties, the weakest. The extracts give no age or marital state, and no Baumann marriage to a Wilhelmine appears in the 1880s marriage extracts. ¶7a

Birth candidate, Beresina 1838 (parentage stays Speculative): the St Petersburg Consistory extract records Wilhelmina Farofsky, born at Beresina on 7 Jan 1838 and baptised 12 Jan, daughter of Johann and Rosina née Bomm (FHL 1882648/2 p.72 no.3). This is the only birth of a Wilhelmina Farowsky (any spelling) found in the 1830-59 Bessarabian extracts, and it names the 'unnamed first wife' of Johann Farowsky. For the identification: the Beresina Farowsky household is the only one of that name found; the bride of 1860 was single, which fits; and Johann's later wife was a Zarbock, matching the Johann Tarowsky who married Caroline Zarbock at Tarutino on 7 Sep 1851 after Rosina's death. Against, or unresolved: (1) a girl born 7 Jan 1838 would have been 22, not 21, on 4 Feb 1860, a one-year discrepancy of a kind common in marriage ages; (2) a second Wilhelmina Farowsky was the mother of Louisa Dorsch, born at Beresina on 4 Dec 1858, father Friedrich (FHL 1884077/1 p.135 no.94). The child bears the father's surname, so the entry reads as a legitimate birth to a married woman, not an illegitimate one; since the 1860 bride was single, that mother was probably a namesake, but no Dorsch-Farowsky marriage was found in 1815-59 and either woman could be the 1838 child. The link to Johann and Rosina therefore stays Speculative. ¶13a

Ruled out (1 Nov 2026,; S0086): the infant Katharina Baumann (born 25 Jul 1885, died 28 Aug 1885 at Friedenstal) was not her daughter. The death register image (St Petersburg Consistory duplicate 1885, DGS 004139516 item 2, image 369; FHL 1897694/2 p.376 no.24; index 1:1:6RYG-3F97) reads 'Baumann, Katharina, Tochter der Wilhelmine Knodel geb. Baumann' (daughter of Wilhelmine Knodel née Baumann), family-book page 63, Friedensthal, a child, died of convulsions (Gichter), buried by the sexton (Küster). Her mother was therefore born Baumann, not Farowsky/Tarowsky, and the family-book page 63 is that of household 281 (Gottlieb Knodel b. 1826, Wilhelm Knodel b. 1860, Wilhelmina Baumann), so she was probably the wife of Wilhelm Knodel b. 1860 (inference). The lead that Ludwig's widow had a child in 1885 is closed. ¶16
