About the family

Father of Johann Gottfried Finck (see his page). ¶1a

Life story

A Jacob Finck was buried at Illingen on 5 Mar 1734 (record 8394864, no relatives), before his son's marriage of 1738. Two Jacob Fincks had children at Illingen: Hanß Jacob x Elisabetha (Philipp Friederich, 1 Feb 1719, 8392563) and Johann Jacob x Juditha (1725-1728, 8392767, 8392837, 8392895); Johann Gottfried's own baptism (about 1705-16) is not indexed, so his mother is not entered. Earlier Fincks there: Martin Finckh, son of Melchior, m. 13 Jul 1697 (8393464); Melchior buried 29 Apr 1706 (8394124). ¶3a

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Graded on that link: the groom row of Johann Gottfried's own marriage at Illingen on 22 Apr 1738 reads 'Johann Gottfried Finken', relatives 'Jacob, Maria Catharina' (father, then bride; record 8393752, image 1187139-00168). Continuity: the 1738 couple are the Johann Gottfried x Maria Catharina whose son Johannes married in 1771 naming his father 'Joh. Gottfried' (8396924). Ancestry 61023 index rows do not label roles (BRIEF audit 5 rule 3), so this rests at Probable until the image or a labelled FamilySearch twin is read; images not seen. ¶1b

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed, graded on his link to his son Johann Gottfried Finck (see his page). Illingen marriage register 1738 (image read), entry of 22 Apr: 'Johann Gottfried, weyl. Jacob Fincken gewesenen Burgers und Krämers allhier ehel. Sohn, mit Maria Catharina, Jacob Hausers Burgers und Gerichtsverwandten allhier ehel. Tochter, copulirt worden'. The register names the relationship (son of the late Jacob Finck, burgher and shopkeeper); the unlabelled-index objection (audit 5 rule 3) is removed by reading the image. Continuity to the man in the tree: the 1738 couple Johann Gottfried x Maria Catharina baptise at Illingen from 27 Jan 1739 (Jacob Friderich) to 1749-50 (Johann Gottfried), and their son Johannes names his father 'Joh. Gottfried Finckh' at his own Illingen marriage of 30 Apr 1771 (labelled QPGR-XQL7; image 346 read: 'H. Joh. Gottfried Finckhs... Handelsmanns allhier ehel. lediger Sohn'). No second Johann Gottfried Finck of that generation at Illingen is indexed. 'weyl.' (late) in 1738 fits the burial of a Jacob Finck at Illingen on 5 Mar 1734 (Ancestry 8394864). Not to be confused with Hr. Jacob Finck, Schultheiß, who married the widow Maria Catharina at Illingen in June 1737 (same image 169, left page): a living man in 1737, so of the next generation (the Johann Jacob of 1737 in). Image read signed in on FamilySearch, 2 Nov 2026. ¶4
