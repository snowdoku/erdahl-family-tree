Life story

PEI records: baptised in PEI on 2 July 1934 (born 15 April 1914), as a young adult; in the 1931 census of Gaspereaux as Olive Hayter, daughter, 17. ¶4

Marriage, BC 1945-09-587218: Bertha Olive Hayter, aged 31, a stapler in a box factory, United Church, of 312 Whyte Avenue, Penticton, born Gaspereaux, Prince Edward Island; father Benjamin Hayter, mother Lydia Adeline Hicken, both born PEI. She married Leonard Curtis Stevenson, a fruit farmer of Summerland born in the United States, on 20 June 1945 at Penticton United Church, by banns. The witnesses were her sister and brother-in-law, Alice Vera Mae and Kristian Verner Bak Thomsen of Naramata. ¶2

Death, BC 2000-09-002152: Bertha Olive Stevenson, a packing-house labourer, of Summerland, widow of Leonard Curtis Stevenson, died at Summerland General Hospital on 29 January 2000, aged 85, born 15 April 1914 at Gaspereaux, PEI; father Benjamin Hayter, mother Adella Hicken. A daughter reported the death. She was cremated at Penticton. ¶3
