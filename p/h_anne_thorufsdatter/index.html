Life story

Baptised in Drangedal on the 12th Sunday after Trinity, 23 Aug 1795: girl Anne, parents Thoruf Thorufsen Jyserø and Ragnild Johnsdatter. Godparents: Helvig Knudsdatter Jyserø, Thorbær Johnsdatter, Harald Halvorsen Grytting, Hans Larsen Gulbrands[rud?] and Knud Anundsen Tostved. Her mother was churched 13 Sep (Drangedal ministerialbok 1768-1814, p. 145). ¶6

Her parentage is now Confirmed by this baptism, not only by the 1801 census. ¶7

Married Osmund Tarjesen 5 Jul 1818; the record gives her as aged 22 and of Jyserød/'Iyserød'. ¶2

In the 1865 census, aged 71 and born in Drangedal, at Heimdal nordre. In the 1875 census she is a widow at Heimdal søndre, 'his mother', with føderåd (retirement support). ¶3

Her death is not confirmed. An Anne Thorafsdatter was buried 20 Jan 1880 in Nissedal (Nissedal local parish); she may be this Anne (speculative). ¶4
