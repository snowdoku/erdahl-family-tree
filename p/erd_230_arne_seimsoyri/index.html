Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Speculative as father of Synneve Arnesdatter (see her page). Her patronymic shows her father was an Arne, and she lived at Seimsøren (Seimsøyri, Vik) when she married in Dec 1732. The Vik bygdebok H. 3 says 'Synneva hadde truleg ein bror Jon' (canvas 14), and has 'Jon Arneson Seimsøyri (1703-1776)', married 1732 Ragnhild Oladtr (canvas 22; dates ABT, rebuilt text). So her father was probably an Arne living at Seimsøren about 1700-1730. This is a candidate built from patronymics and residence only. ¶1a

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Test record: the 1701 census for Vik (an Arne at Seim or Seimsøren), and a probate after Arne at Seim/Seimsøren (Ytre Sogn index, 1720-1760) naming the children Jon and Synneve. ¶1b
