Life story

Baptism: Henning, son of Anders Olsen of Hessen, was baptised at Borgund on 29 November 1735. From the index. ¶1

Death: he drowned with his father just off Hessen in about March 1750, aged 14 years and 15 weeks. From the index. He is added because his rare name recurs among Cæcilia Andersdatter's descendants (her daughter Henninge Larsdatter and granddaughter Henninge Sidsine Knudsdatter, from her 1821 probate), which supports the identification of Cæcilia as his sister. ¶2

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 alternative set aside

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Not an heir in 1750 (scan read): the 12 Sep 1750 probate after his father Anders Olsen of Hessen names only the widow Sisille Olsdatter and the child Cisille Andersdatter, apparently the only one. His absence fits his death before the probate. The probate opening does not mention the drowning. The account that father and son drowned together still rests on the consecutive burial entries in the index, which have not been read on the scan. The widow's guardian was a Henning Olsen, probably his mother's brother and his namesake (unproved). ¶3
