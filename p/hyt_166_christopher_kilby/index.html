Life story

Will dated 16 Sept. 1738, proved 2 Aug. 1742 (Suffolk Probate docket 7782): to wife Sarah the personal estate and use of the real estate; to son Thomas Kilby 5s.; 'to daughter, Rebecca Allen, but 5s.', with further provision for 'daughter, Rebecca Allen, and if she dies, for her children'; to daughters Meriam Clark and Mary Perkins; son-in-law Benjamin Clark and William Tyler Esq. executors; inventory £2117 (TAG 27:203, abstract). Graded Probable on his link to Rebecca Kilby: the will names her as his daughter, but it was read only in a published abstract; the will itself (Suffolk Probate, docket 7782) would confirm. ¶2

Estate: the docket (7782) holds only the will, probate and letter (36:106) and the inventory (36:120); no executors' account, division or distribution was recorded, so the estate never names Rebecca's children (docket book DGS 7703034 image 599). ¶6

Administrator of his father-in-law (enrichment): the account of 'Christopher Kilby Admin to the Estate of Pilgrim Simpkins late of Boston' (inventory of 22 Oct 1722, about £88, of which real estate £30) charges cash paid to John Kilby for the funeral charges, sundries at Pilgrim's wife's funeral, and the costs of proving the will and taking out administration (Suffolk probate record book 1719-1724, ark 3:1:3Q9M-C9Y5-27W9; transcript read, image not checked). The entry gives no relationship words. ¶8

Baker of Boston, son of John Kilby Sr.; died 27 July 1742 aged 77 (Boston News-Letter, 29 July 1742); married in Boston, 20 March 1691, Sarah Simpkins; member of the Old South Church 1695 (TAG 27 (1951): 202). ¶1

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Regrade 2 (1 Nov 2026): upgraded to Confirmed as father of Rebecca Kilby (see her page); graded on his link to Rebecca Kilby. Naming record: his will, dated 16 Sept. 1738 and proved 2 Aug. 1742 (Suffolk Probate docket 7782), leaves 'to daughter, Rebecca Allen, but 5s.' and makes further provision for 'daughter, Rebecca Allen, and if she dies, for her children' (read in the TAG 27:203 abstract, which quotes the will; a faithful abstract of the primary record). Continuity: 'Mr. Jeremiah Allen' and 'Mrs. Rebecca Kilby' were married at the Brattle Church in October 1722 (Manifesto Church records, marriages nos. 539-540, read), and 'Jer. and Rebekah Allen' had Elisabeth born 22 April 1725, so the married daughter Rebecca Allen of 1738 is the Rebecca Kilby of the 1722 marriage, the only Rebecca among Kilby's children, with no named rival. Flags: the will image itself (docket 7782) is still unread, and Rebecca's own link to Elizabeth (Allen) Prince stays Probable on its own grade. ¶4

Upgrade: graded on his link to Rebecca Kilby. His will, read in the record book (Suffolk Probate 36:106-107, docket 7782; image read), by 'Christopher Kilby of Boston... Baker', gives 'to my Daughter Rebecca Allen Five Shillings with what I have already given her', and a quarter of the proceeds of his real estate to be put at interest, the interest payable 'to my Daughter Rebecca Allen during her Coverture', the principal to her if she survived her husband, 'but if she should dye before her Husband then the said Quarter part to be applyed towards bringing up her Children'. Continuity: his daughter Rebecca Kilby (b. 27 Dec. 1701) married Jeremiah Allen at the Brattle Church on 18 Oct. 1722, sixteen years before the will; she is the only Rebecca Kilby to marry an Allen, and her husband was indeed living in 1738 (his widow Rebecca administered his estate in 1760, Suffolk Probate 56:365). The will itself, which this note's predecessor named as the settling record, now confirms the abstract. ¶5

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Lead: the will provides for Rebecca Allen's children if she died; the executors' accounts in docket 7782 may name them (for example Elizabeth Prince), which would tie Job Prince's wife to this family. ¶3
