About the family

Other children: Sivert Povelsen of Stokke, about 14 in 1785, was betrothed on 24 Jul 1795 to Lisbet Gudmundsdatter Søvig (16080 c134, p.266); he had taken a lease on part of Hakvig. ¶13

First wife and daughters: Nordmøre skifteprotokoll 1762-64 (source 24647), register 'Anne Haagensdatter, Povel Ellingsen Stoches hustru i Gjemnes... 735', and p. 735 (view 385), entry No. 95: 'Anno 1763 den 11te Maj' a registration was held 'paa gaarden Stoke i Gimnes Tinglaug' after the late Anne Haagensdatter, between 'Enkemanden Povel Ellingsen' and their two daughters: '1 Anne Povelsdatter', [age read as 3 or 5] years, with guardian 'Farbroderen Johan Ellingsen' [farm name uncertain], and '2 Christie Povelsdatter 1½ aar' [reading of the age slightly uncertain], with guardian Auden Andersen Grønset. So Paul was first married to Anne Haagensdatter, who died before 11 May 1763, and married Ane Sivertsdatter between May 1763 and Feb 1766 (her first churching). ¶16

His brother: the guardian for his son Povel was 'Farbroderen Ole Ellingsen Stoche', so Ole Ellingsen of Stokke (in the 1801 census as a widower lodger at Stokke, buried 1807 aged about 72) was his brother; the 1763 probate after his first wife names another brother, 'Farbroderen Johan Ellingsen'. This proves that his father was an Elling (the patronymic shared with a named full or half brother) and points to that Elling farming Stokke in the 1730s-40s. The father is not yet named in any record read, so he is not added as a person. ¶18

Parents found: Nordmøre skifteprotokoll 24644 (1750-55), fol. 74a (view 83, right page), No. 100: 'Anno 1751 den 11te Novemb.' a registration was held 'paa gaarden Stocke' in Gimnes tinglag after the deceased 'Elling Johansen', whose widow was 'Anne Povelsdatter'; the children are listed as the son 'Povel Ellingsen' (age read as 25), 'Johan Ellingsen' (about 20), 'Arne Ellingsen', 'Ole Ellingsen', and the daughters 'Christie Ellingsdatter' and '[Synnøve] Ellingsdatter' (ages after the first two only partly legible). The typed register of the volume lists it as 'Elling Johansen Stoche i Gjemnes 74a'. Birth year about 1726 is from his age (read as 25) in Nov 1751. The parents are graded Confirmed on their own records; his own grade (on his link to his son Elling) is unchanged. ¶23

Life story

Baptism found (scan read): Kvernes chronological book 1753-1780 (source 16076), p. 122, Laetare Sunday 1767 (29 Mar 1767, computed from Easter 19 Apr 1767; the neighbouring entries are Oculi and Judica): 'Paul Ellingsen Stokkes og hustru Ane Sivertsd: b[arn]: Elling'. Godparents: Hørcken[?] Olsen of Stokke, Ole Johnsen Exstran, Arne[?] Ellingsen Stokke, Ane Iversdatter wife of Sivert Nielsen Overland, and the girl Ane Olsdatter Stokke. Confidence moved Speculative to Probable because this record names a son Elling of the couple, and no other Poul/Paul Ellingsen is indexed in eighteenth-century Kvernes, so the 1767 Elling is the natural candidate for Elling Povelsen. It is not Confirmed: no record yet ties this child to Guldset or Lillemork, and the 1801 census age (35 in Feb 1801, so born 1765-66) is about a year earlier than this baptism. The Feb 1766 churching shows an earlier child born c. Dec 1765-Jan 1766, baptism not found; if that child was also Elling and died, the link would still hold through the 1767 namesake. ¶5

1762 extra tax: Nordmøre fogderi, Ekstraskatt 1762 (source 47939), view 56, Kvernes sogn, Gjemnes tinglag, farm no. 54 Stokke: 'Paul Ellingsen og hustru' (his first wife Anne Haagensdatter), with 'Arne Ellingsen', 'Ole Ellingsen' and '[?] Ellingsdatter' in the column for children over 12 and 'Enken Ane' among the others in the house. Since Paul's own children were then infants, these are his brothers and sister and his widowed mother, the household of the 1751 probate continued on the farm. Also at Stokke: Jacob Olsen (wife Ane Aslaksdatter), Tosten Nielsen and others. ¶24

Second marriage found: Kvernes 16076 view 53, p. 101 (1764): under 'Fer. 2 Pasch.' [Easter Monday, 23 Apr 1764] 'trolovede: 1 Enkemand Paul Ellings[en] Stokke med pigen Ane Sivertsd[atte]r Øverland'; and under 'Dom. 4 a Pasch.' [20 May 1764] 'Copuleret Paul Ellingsen Stokke og pigen Ane Sivertsd[atte]r Øverland'. (The clerk's capital St in Stokke looks like H, as in 'Hoxxe'; the same hand writes Stokke this way for Jacob Olsen and others known to live at Stokke.) This fixes the date of his marriage to Ane Sivertsdatter (20 May 1764), confirms he was already a widower (his first wife Anne Haagensdatter died before 11 May 1763), and gives her home farm as Overland. Their first child was born about Dec 1765 (churching 16 Feb 1766). ¶19

Other entries read: p. 112, 20th Sunday after Trinity (20 Oct 1765), Paul Ellingsen of Stokke was godfather to Iver[?], child of Jacob Olsen Stokke and Ane Aslaksdatter; fellow godparents included Ane Paulsdatter Stokke and Sønov[?] Ellingsdatter wife of Ole Eriksen of Stokke (perhaps Paul's sister). p. 122 (14 Mar 1767) he was again a godfather. Arne[?] Ellingsen Stokke, a godfather at Elling's 1767 baptism, may be a brother (inferred from the patronymic only). ¶6

Possible kin at Stokke (inferred): at a Stokke baptism on the 20th Sunday after Trinity 1765 (view 59, p. 113; child of Jacob Olsen Stokke) Paul Ellingsen 'ibid' stood godfather beside 'Enken Ane Paulsdatter Stokke' and the wife of a godfather, Sønov Ellingsdatter; at the Advent 1764 baptism of Ane, daughter of Ole Jonsen Eikrem and Tønnør (Synnøve) Ellingsdatter (view 56, p. 107), the godparents were 'Paul E[?] Stokke' and the widow Ane Paulsdatter of Stokke again. Synnøve Ellingsdatter may be Paul's sister and the widow Ane Paulsdatter his mother (which would make Paul named for her father), but no record says so. His brothers Ole and Johan Ellingsen were both active in the parish 1764-65 (Johan Ellingsen 'Hytt[?]' with wife Ane Erichsdatter, view 55-56). ¶20

Of Stokke, Kvernes. His wife Ane Sivertsdatter was churched in Feb 1766, and he was godfather at a Stokke baptism (Dom. XX p. Trin. 1765). He is a speculative father of Elling Povelsen (born about 1766). No Povel Ellingsen appears in the 1801 Kvernes census index. ¶1

What the 1801 census does show at Stokke, and why it matters: the farm held several households, and among them were Ole Ellingsen, born about 1745, entered as 'Huusbondens fader, enkemand, inderst' - the householder's father, a widower, living as a lodger - and Elling Olsen, born about 1769, a farmer and head of his own household there. Elling Olsen's patronymic makes him the son of an Ole, and Ole Ellingsen's makes him the son of an Elling; the two fit together as father and son, with the grandson named Elling after his great-grandfather. An Ole Ellingsen of Stokke, born 1735, was buried in 1807 as a widower. So Stokke was home to an Ellingsen family in the generation before 1800, and a Poul Ellingsen there in 1765-66 would most naturally be Ole Ellingsen's brother, both of them sons of an Elling farming Stokke in the 1730s or 1740s. That is a coherent picture but it is not proof, and it does not yet connect Poul to Elling Povelsen of Guldset. ¶3

Own probate found: Nordmøre skifteprotokoll nr 15 (source 24650), fol. 365a. 'Anno 1779 den 25de Januarii' a registration and valuation was held 'paa gaarden Stoche beliggende i Gimness Tinglaug, Kvernes Præstegield' after the late 'Povel Ellingsen Stoche', between 'hans efterlevende Kone Enken Anne Sivertsdatter paa den eene og deres med hinanden udi Egteskabet sammenavlede og i live værende Syv Børn, som ere 4 Sønner og 3 Døttre' on the other. Heirs: '1 Sønnen Elling Povelsen gammel 12 aar', guardian Størcher Olsen Stoche; '2 Sønnen Sivert Povelsen gammel 8 aar', guardian Peter Sivertsen Søvig; '3 Sønnen Ole Povelsen 6 aar', guardian Ole Sivertsen Øverland; '4 Sønnen Povel Povelsen gammel 4 aar', guardian 'Farbroderen Ole Ellingsen Stoche'; '5 Datteren Anne Povelsdatter gammel 10 aar', guardian Ole Monsen Hoset; '6 Datteren Christie Povelsdatter gammel 17 aar', guardian Auden Grønset; '7 Datteren Maria Povelsdatter gammel 3 aar', guardian Niels Røvig. The widow's lavverge was Knud Halvorsen Stoche. This probate names him, his widow Ane Sivertsdatter and their son 'Elling Povelsen gammel 12 aar' (born about 1766-67, matching the 29 Mar 1767 baptism) in one record, and the 1785 probate after Ane (24652 fol. 321b) names the same Elling (17½) at the same farm six years later. No grade change: he was already Confirmed on his link to Elling Povelsen Lillemark; this record is the direct one his notes asked for and puts that grade beyond doubt. The step from this Stokke heir to Elling Povelsen of Guldset is now shown by the 6 Aug 1796 betrothal (16080 c138 p.275) of 'Elling Povlsøn Stokke, som skal faae 1 Part i Gaarden Gulset' to Ingebor Endresdatter Grønset, with Paul's brother Ole Ellingsen Stokke as surety; see Elling Povelsen Lillemark. ¶14

Death narrowed: he died before 25 Jan 1779, after the birth of his son Povel (4 in Jan 1779, so born about 1774-75) and probably after the conception of Maria (3 in Jan 1779). The earlier bracket 'about 1775 to about 1783' is replaced by about 1776-78. ¶15

Likely daughters at Stokke (scan read, Kvernes 16077): 'Kirsti Povelsdatter av Stokke' was a godmother in Jun 1782 (c14, p.22). On 26 Apr 1788 (c44, p.82) 'Knud Tostensen paa Grønset og h[ustru] Christi Povelsdatter' had a daughter Ane baptised; godparents Ole Ellingsen Stokke, Johannes Olsen Grønset, Mogns Olsen Hoset, Niels Olsen's wife Kari Olsdatter and the girls 'Ane Povelsd[atter]' and Marit Jacobsdatter, 'alle 3 av Stokke'. A Povel/Paul at Stokke with daughters Kirsti and Ane (the second perhaps named for Ane Sivertsdatter), and Kirsti naming her own daughter Ane, fits Paul Ellingsen's family, so Elling had probably two sisters. Inferred from patronymic and farm only; no record yet names their father in full. Ole Ellingsen Stokke as godfather fits him as Paul's brother. ¶8

Death bracket: his youngest child Maria was 9 in Jan 1785, so he was alive about 1775. His widow had remarried Erich Olsen Stokke and died before Jan 1785, so he died between about 1775 and about 1783. His own probate should be in Nordmøre skifteprotokoll nr 15 (1776-81, source 24650) or nr 16 (1781-83, source 24651), under P or E. The registers of both were fetched (views 1-15) but not read. The register of nr 14 (24649, 1772-76; views 3-11, letters A-M) has no Paul/Povel. ¶11

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 3

Still Speculative after a systematic search, and the negative evidence is now documented so that nobody has to repeat it. Every indexed appearance of the name Poul, Povel or Paul Ellingsen in Kvernes and Kornstad was checked (70 hits, all spelling variants, all years): the earliest is 'Poul Ellings.' as a father in 1839, and every one of them belongs to the nineteenth century. There is no Poul Ellingsen in the 1801 census for Kvernes and none in the indexed parish registers of the eighteenth century. The only record of him remains the two entries the previous round found in the scanned chronological Kvernes book for 1753-1780: his wife Ane Sivertsdatter being churched in February 1766, and his appearance as a godfather in October 1765. ¶2

Confidence basis restated (13 Oct 2026): his level is kept at Probable. The raise was argued from his son Elling's 1767 baptism, but under the rule that a person's confidence carries the link to his own parents, that link is carried by Elling Povelsen Lillemark (now Probable). His own parents are unknown, so his level expresses only his identity as Paul Ellingsen of Stokke, attested in the Kvernes chronological book 1753-1780 (source 16076) as godfather on 20 Oct 1765 (p. 112), as husband of Ane Sivertsdatter at her churching on 16 Feb 1766 (p. 114) and as father at the 29 Mar 1767 baptism (p. 122). Whether such a parentless person should be graded Confirmed on those entries is left to David. ¶7

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed. The 29 Mar 1767 baptism (16076 p.122) names him as father of Elling with Ane Sivertsdatter. The 1785 probate after Ane Sivertsdatter at Stokke (Nordmøre skifteprotokoll 1783-87, source 24652, fol. 321b-322a) lists her children as 'Elling Paavelsen 17½ aar', Sivert Paavelsen 14, Ole Paavelsen 11 and Maria Paavelsdatter 9. That proves the 1767 Elling lived to adulthood as a Povelsen of Stokke. His son is identified with Elling Povelsen of Guldset by name, parish and age (born about 1766-67; no other Elling Povelsen in Kvernes), and Elling named his own children Ane and Paul. See Ane Sivertsdatter for the full transcription. ¶10

Corrections 2

Correction: Kirsti and Ane Povelsdatter 'of Stokke' are NOT among Ane Sivertsdatter's heirs in 1785. If they were Paul's daughters, they came from an earlier marriage. Kirsti was an adult godmother in 1782, so she was born before about 1765. Kirsti's own probate (source 24653 fol. 432a, Grønset, Dec 1791) names only her widower Knud Torstensen and daughters Ane (3) and Ingebor (½, guardian Nils Olsen Stokke), and not her father. Their link to Paul stays a guess. Paul's probate would settle it. ¶12

Correction: this explains the Kirsti/Ane puzzle. Christie (Kirsti) and Anne Povelsdatter were Paul's daughters by his first wife Anne Haagensdatter, so was right that they came from an earlier marriage and right that they were not Ane Sivertsdatter's heirs in 1785. Christie, 1½ in May 1763 and 17 in Jan 1779 (born about 1761-62), is the same girl both times, and Auden Grønset was her guardian both times; she married Knud Tostensen of Grønset (probate 24653 fol. 432a, Dec 1791). The 1779 clerk's phrase that all seven children were born to Paul and Ane Sivertsdatter is therefore loose. Anne's age in 1779 (read as 10 or 8) conflicts with her age in 1763, and the reading should be checked; she is probably the 'Ane Povelsdatter' of Stokke who was a godmother in 1788. ¶17

Open questions and the next record to read 1

What would settle it, named exactly: (1) the Kvernes chronological parish book 1753-1780, Digitalarkivet source 16076, read page by page for the baptisms of 1765-1767 at Stokke - the previous round read pages 113-114 and found the churching but not the baptism; (2) the Nordmøre sorenskriveri probate protocols (skifteprotokoller) for Kvernes in the 1770s-1800s, which would name Poul Ellingsen's heirs and so prove or break the link to Elling; (3) the extra tax of 1762 (ekstraskatten) for Kvernes, which lists every adult by farm and would show whether Poul and Ole Ellingsen were both at Stokke; (4) Averøy bygdebok, volume 1, the Kvernes sokn volume, under the farm Stokke - readable only from a Norwegian internet connection on nb.no. ¶4
