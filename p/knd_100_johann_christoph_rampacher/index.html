Confidence: the grade and how it changed 3

Speculative father of Johann Erhard Rampacher (see his page). ¶1a

Graded on that link: a Johann Erhardt 'Ramprecht' was born at Vaihingen on 19 Oct 1682 to Johann Christoph x Maria Margaretha (record 4767443, image 1184562-00033). Identifying that child with the 1720 father rests on name and parish only (his second marriage is not indexed). Other children of the couple at Vaihingen: Adam Christoph b. 24 Dec 1677 (4767350), Christina Barbara b. 26 Dec 1684 (4767487; married 26 Sep 1702, bride row 904776298 naming father 'Christoph'), Wilhelm Heinrich b. 23 Oct 1687 (4767552). 'Hl. Johann Christoph Rampacher' died at Vaihingen 3 Aug 1720 (record 4400382). Ancestry 61023 index rows do not label roles (BRIEF audit 5 rule 3), so this rests at Probable until the image or a labelled FamilySearch twin is read; images not seen. ¶1b

Upgrade: Speculative to Probable. Graded on his link to his son Johann Erhard (see his page). The birth row of 19 Oct 1682 at Vaihingen, 'Johann Erhardt Ramprecht', relatives 'Johann Christoph, Maria Margaretha' (record 4767443), is now tied to the 1720 father by a chain of Vaihingen records: the 1704 groom 'Joh. Erhard Rampacher' (4776351) is shown to be the 1720 father because his first wife died in Jan 1717 and Magdalena Barbara's first child followed in Apr 1718 (4400121, 4769004), and he carries the title Herr throughout. No other Johann Erhard was born at Vaihingen; the Cannstatt namesakes (b. 1687, 1691, 1692) were 12-17 in 1704 and so cannot be the groom. This is an elimination argument on unlabelled index rows, so Probable, not Confirmed. The 1704 groom row gives no father; a labelled FamilySearch twin of the 1704 marriage or the image 1184562-00487 would test it. ¶2

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Origin (lead only, not linked): a Johann Christoph Rampacher was baptised at Cannstatt on 1 Sep 1650 to 'Herr Johann Michel, Anna Barbara' (record 3293260), and his brother Johann Erhard on 3 Sep 1655 (3293296, 'Herr Joh Michael, Frau Anna Barbara'); that Johann Erhard married Maria Elisabetha and stayed at Cannstatt (children 1682-1702). The shared names Johann Christoph and Johann Erhard and the title Herr point to the Cannstatt family, but a 'Johann Christoph Ramprecht' was buried at Cannstatt on 10 Jun 1701 (3296311, no relatives, perhaps the boy of 1684), and his marriage to Maria Margaretha (about 1676) is not indexed. Not entered as parents. ¶4
