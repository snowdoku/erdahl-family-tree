About the family

Daughter Lisbeth: the probate gives the younger daughter Lisbeth as 27 in 1779 (born about 1752), four years later than the baptism of Lisbeth, daughter of Iver Botnen, on 5 Jan 1748. Either the probate age is low or the 1748 child died and a later Lisbeth was born; not resolved. ¶7

Parents (bygdebok, Probable): Brekke herred bygdebok vol. 2 (1944), Botnen bnr. 78 (canvas 885): Ivar Monsson (1704-1786) married first in 1728 Elisabet [Eilevsdtr.] of Botnen (1700-1739), and in 1739 'Lisbet Rasmusdtr.', b. 1715, d. 1779, daughter of Rasmus [Klausson] and Brita Monsdtr. of Indre Oppedal. A division of 22.. 1779 was held 'etter avd.kone Lisbet Rasmusdtr. Botnen' (canvas 886). Her brother Mons Rasmusson (1712-1789) is called morbror of her son Johannes Ivarson in 1777. Linked parents Rasmus Klausen and Brita Monsdatter. Birth narrowed to ABT 1715 from the book (was ABT 1718). ¶12

Life story

Name: Recorded only as 'Lispet', mother, in the baptisms of her children Lisbeth (5 Jan 1748) and Rasmus (24 Feb 1750), children of Iver Botnen. She was churched on 17 Feb 1748 and 30 Mar 1750. ¶1

Patronymic: Unknown. A 'Lisbeth Rasmus D[atter] Botnen' stood godmother on 9 Jul 1774 beside 'Iwer Mogensen Botnen', and a 'Lisbet Botnen' was godmother on 15 May 1775. She may be this woman, which would explain why her son was named Rasmus, but that is a hypothesis only: no record names her patronymic as Iver's wife. ¶2

Godmother: she is the 'Lisbeth Rasmus D[atter] Botnen' who stood godmother on 9 Jul 1774 beside her husband 'Iwer Mogensen Botnen'. ¶6

Burial record: the Digitalarkivet record page pg00000001733239 (Gulen ministerialbok 1776-1787, source 11444, page 31a) gives 'Lisbeth Rasmusd', Qvinde (woman), of Botnen, Brekke, aged '61 aar' (61 years), birth year 1718, buried 1779 with interment 08-01 (1 Aug) and the remark 'Dom: 9 p: F Trin' (the 9th Sunday after Trinity). In 1779 Easter fell on 4 Apr, so Trinity Sunday was 30 May and the 9th Sunday after Trinity was 1 Aug 1779, which agrees with the interment date. Birth about 1718 follows from the age of 61 (born 1717-1718); death before 1 Aug 1779, which narrows the earlier bound of before 22 Sep 1779 (her probate). Index-level: the row on the scan (source 11444, canvas 31, left page = 31a, or canvas 30) has not yet been located; the bands of page 31a hold baptisms of about 13-18 Aug 1779. ¶10

Name: Lisbet Rasmusdatter. The probate (*skifte*) no. 45, held at Botten on 22-23 Sep 1779, divided the estate of 'den forhen afdøde Kone Lisbet Rasmusdatter' (the late wife Lisbet Rasmusdatter) between her widower Iver Monsen and their six children of the marriage, four sons and two daughters: Mons Iversen (eldest, of Hembre[?]), Rasmus Iversen (living at Botten), Bendix Iversen, Johannes Iversen (of Leirnes[?] or Øvre Opedal), Brithe Iversdatter (married to Gulov[?] … of Hiertholm) and Lisbeth Iversdatter, aged 27. Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll A 9b, pp. 1041-1044. ¶4

Death: before 22 Sep 1779, the date of her probate. ¶5

Burial (per index): 'Lisbeth Rasmusd:', of Botnen, Brekke, buried 1779 (year only); one index row gives Qvinde and a birth year of 1718, a second row (Gulen book 1779-1788) gives Kone (wife). Index-level; the book was not read. This fits her probate of 22-23 Sep 1779. Birth about 1718 is taken from the index birth year and is approximate; it would make her about 30 at her daughter Lisbeth's baptism in Jan 1748. ¶9

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Confidence: Probable. She is named as the mother in two baptisms read from the scans, but her patronymic, age and death are unknown. Her marriage to Iver (probably before Sep 1741), her burial with age, or a probate would identify her. ¶3

Confidence: upgraded from Probable to Confirmed. Probate no. 45 (1779), a primary record read from the scan, names her as the wife of Iver Monsen and the mother of Rasmus Iversen of Botten. She was Iver's second wife: probate no. 46 lists his children by a first marriage separately (see Iver's notes). ¶8
