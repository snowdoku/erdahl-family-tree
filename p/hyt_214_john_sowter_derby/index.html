Philippa Sowterbef. 1568 – bef. 1617Probable · Derby

About the family

Father of Philippa Sowter (see her page), graded on his link to her (FamilySearch signed in, 2 Nov 2026). The All Saints, Derby, register has, under 'July 1568', 'Bap. Philippa filia Johis Sowter bap. x° die' (Philippa daughter of John Sowter, baptised the 10th day), read on the image (DGS image 19 of 772, ark 3:1:S3HY-DTM3-VSM; index 1:1:KBD6-8Q4). The record names him as her father. ¶1

Other children baptised at All Saints as 'son of John Sowter' (FamilySearch index, father only): Ricus [Richard] 23 Dec 1564 and Thomas 1 Apr 1567. So John was a householder in All Saints parish 1564-1568. The register (from 1558) has no John Sowter marriage, so the mother is not known; the baptisms name only the father. ¶3

Life story

Death, not settled: the All Saints register has, under 'July 1574', 'Johes Sowter sepultus 26 die' (John Sowter buried the 26th), with no description (image 29 of 772, read; index 1:1:KBD6-8VK). It is the only John Sowter burial in the All Saints index between his children's baptisms (1564-1568) and Philippa's marriage (1588), so it is probably his, but the entry does not say so. The Lichfield consistory calendars also list two administrations of a John Sowter in 1570/1, perhaps of another parish; a Find a Grave entry giving a John Sowter died Oct 1571 at Derby has no matching entry in the register index for that month (the FamilySearch name index for the image covering Sept 1571 to Jan 1572/3 was checked). 'Agneta Sowter vidua' (Agnes Sowter, widow) was buried at All Saints on 23 January 1572/3 (image 25 of 772, read; index 1:1:KBD6-8G4); she was a widow before the 1574 burial, so she was not the wife of the John buried then. No death is entered. Thomas Sowter, who married Alice Turnar at All Saints 19 May 1560 and had children there 1560-1574, was another Sowter householder in the parish (perhaps a brother); he is not Philippa's father. ¶4

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the grade · 1 open question

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Why Probable, not Confirmed: the baptism (July 1568) and Philippa's marriage to William Ouldham in the same church (17 Nov 1588) are twenty years apart with no record of her in between, so the identification of the bride with the 1568 child rests on the name alone. It is strong: she is the only Philippa Sowter baptised anywhere in the FamilySearch England indexes 1550-1580 (searched 2 Nov 2026), the marriage is at the same church, and she would have been 20 at marriage; no rival is known. ¶2a

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Test record: a Sowter will or administration naming 'my daughter Philippa' (Lichfield consistory calendars list John Sowter administrations in 1570/1 (two) and a will in 1573/4; Lichfield Record Office B/C/11), or TAG 42 (1966): 212-215, which discusses the Sowters of Derby. ¶2b
