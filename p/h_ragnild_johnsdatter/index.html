About the family

Wife of Thoruf Thorufsen at Jyserø in the 1801 census, aged 37 (transcribed 'Ragnild Johnsen'). Her sister Thorbær Johnsdatter (27) lived with them. ¶1

Her sister Thorbær Johnsdatter (named as sister in the 1801 census) was godmother to Anne in 1795, and in Jul 1795, of Grytting, godmother to Gunnild, daughter of Harald Halvorsen Grytting and Kari Johnsdatter. Kari and a Mari Johnsdatter of Jyserø (godmother to John in 1785) may also be sisters; that is inferred, not proved. ¶3

Her mother is PROBABLY Gunnild Tellefsdatter of Frostved, whom Jon Torjusen of Jyserø married on 3 Jul 1763, 14 months before this baptism. The register does not name the mother, so this is an inference from the sequence; no other wife of Jon Torjusen was seen. ¶6

Life story

Baptised in Drangedal on the 13th Sunday after Trinity, 16 Sep 1764: 'Joen Torjusen Jyserøes Datter Ragnild'. Godparents: Marta Chr[istina] Rynning, Anne Wellicksdatter Jyserø, Torjus Jyserø, Halvor Grytting and Knud Nilsmundsen Wøje. The mother was churched on the 17th Sunday after Trinity; this register does not name mothers (Drangedal ministerialbok 1697-1767, p. 61). Her father Jon Torjusen is Confirmed by this baptism. ¶5

Burial date entered from the record already cited in her notes: 'Ragnild Johnsd.', Kone, aged 42 (b. 1764), wife of Thoruf Thorufsen of Jyserø, buried 17 Nov 1806 at Drangedal (Drangedal ministerialbok 1768-1814, p. 308; index pg00000001577956, the deceased's row; pg00000001577957 is the husband's row). Death stays BEF 17 NOV 1806. ¶9

Named first among the heirs of her father Joen Torgiusen at Jyserød, 30 Apr 1781: 'ældste Datter Ragnild Jonsdatter 16 aar', her mother the widow Gunnild Tellefsdatter; guardian Knud Knudsen Jyserød (Bamble skifteprotokoll nr. 10b fol. 535a, DA 23754 view 199; card DA 7105/1190).. ¶8

Married Thoruf Torulfsen Hiemdal of Treungen in Drangedal 26 Sep 1784 (betrothed 8 Aug), as 'Pigen [maid] Ragnild Johnsdatter Jyserøe' (Drangedal ministerialbok 1768-1814, p. 215). Mother of Anne, baptised 23 Aug 1795. ¶2

Death: buried in Drangedal on 17 Nov 1806 as 'Ragnild Johnsd.', wife (Kone) of Thoruf Thorufsen of Jyserø, born 1764; remark 'døde i Barselseng' (died in childbed). Drangedal ministerialbok 1768-1814, burials 1806, p. 308 (indexed transcription, Digitalarkivet pg00000001577957). ¶7
