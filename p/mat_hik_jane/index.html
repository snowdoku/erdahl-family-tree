Life story

Aunt of Lydia Adelaide (Hicken) Hayter. Gravestone at Sturgeon (Find a Grave 75109199, photograph read): 'In memory of Jane, dau. of George & Francis Hicken, died April 22, 1868, Æt. 27 yrs.', with the verse 'Death was to her no gloomy shade, For Jesus' love she knew; O that these ashes might persuade Others to love him too.' The stone itself names her parents. The compiled Hicken genealogy gives her birth as about 1843. ¶1
