About the family

First wife of Gustav Parelius 'Gust' Satter and mother of Clara Matilda Satter. Her gravestone at Brule Creek Cemetery is photographed and reads CHRISTINA / wife of / GUST SATTER / BORN June 3, 1867 / DIED Oct. 15, 1889, with SATTER cut into the base. Plot Lot 86, Grave 4. ¶1

Life story

Born in SWEDEN about 1867, daughter of Bengt Carlson; she was living as a daughter in Bengt Carlson's household at Spink, Union County, Dakota Territory in the 1880 census. The Satters therefore married into a Swedish family, not a Norwegian one, at the first generation born in America. ¶2

She died 15 October 1889, less than a year after Clara's birth, aged 22. Her own family were still close enough to the Satters five years later that 'Mr. and Mrs. Charley Carlson' gave $7 — the third largest gift in the published list — at Gust's second wedding. ¶3
