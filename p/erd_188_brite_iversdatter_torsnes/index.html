Life story

The 1645 poll tax (koppskatt) for Ytre Sogn, Tjugum skipreide, lists at the farm Toesznesz (Torsnes) two farmers with their wives under 'Gaardmender och derris Quinder': Omund with Ingebret Johansdtr, and 'Olle' with 'Brite Iffuersdtr' (Iversdatter); a servant Olle N. is listed separately (pt00000000056635-56639, 57190). The 1664 bailiff's list (Olluf) and the 1666 vicar's census (Ola bruger, 60) show one Ole as farmer at Torsnes, so the 1645 Olle is very probably Ole Torsnes, and Brite Iversdatter was his wife in 1645. ¶1
