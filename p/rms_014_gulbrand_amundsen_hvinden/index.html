About the family

Parentage argument: a search of every Gran baptism 1728-1747 with a father named Amund (29 records, each read) found this as the only Gulbrand son of an Amund at Hvinden; the other Gulbrand (1737) was son of Amund Hansen of Horgen. Amund Gulbrandsen had no later Gulbrand to 1744. Lars Torgersen of Hvinden, godfather in 1738, was a sponsor at Gulbrand Amundsen's betrothal in 1760. Gulbrand's first child (1761) was named Kirsti, after Kirsti Amundsdatter, as custom expected for the paternal grandmother. ¶3

Parents (still Probable, now stronger): the estate of Amund Gulbrandsen of Hvinden, held 29 Apr 1751 with widow Kirsti Amundsdatter, lists a son Gulbrand aged 13. That fits the 20 Apr 1738 baptism. The 1751 estate also held 3 skippund of odel land, the same size as Gulbrand's own holding in 1789. Kirsti Amundsdatter's estate of 19 Nov 1754 lists Gulbrand 16 1/2. Neither card ties this son to the Gulbrand who died in 1789 by any statement; the link still rests on name, farm, holding and age. ¶6

Parents now Confirmed: at his own estate (16 Apr 1789, fol. 305b) the guardian of his minor children is 'deres Farbroder Ammund Ammundsen', i.e. Gulbrand's brother Amund Amundsen, who is the son Amund of the 1751 and 1754 Hvinden estates of Amund Gulbrandsen and Kirsti Amundsdatter. The name, farm, holding, ages and this brother together tie him to those parents. The same passage names Amund Erichsen Skølebakken [reading uncertain] as guardian of Anders and Kirsti. ¶7

Life story

Betrothed at Gran on Tuesday 18 Nov 1760 to Eli Arnesdatter of Hvinden and married there on Thursday 15 Jan 1761. The sponsors were Lars Torgersen of Hvinden and Christopher Mortensen of Hotved. His own farm is not stated. He and Eli had their daughter Kirsti baptised on 1 Jan 1761. He lived at Hvinden, where the index shows him as a betrothal witness in 1770-1786 and a godparent in 1788. He was buried at Gran on 12 Feb 1789, aged 48. His parents and his baptism (c.1741; Gran baptisms 1733-1744 are indexed) have not been searched. A different Gulbrand Olsen, a dragoon of Hvinden, married in 1763. ¶1

Baptism (Probable): Gulbrand, son of Amund Gulbrandsen of Hvinden and Kirsti Amundsdatter, was baptised at Gran on 20 Apr 1738 (2nd Sunday after Easter; Gran church book 1732-1744, p. 89, index). Godparents: Lars Torgersen, Gulbrand Larsen, Halvor Amundsen, Gubiør Gulbrandsdatter and Kirsti Larsdatter, all of Hvinden. ¶2

Deed read (pantebok 8, DA 10272, fol. 144a, entry No. 4, 1762; listed under Hvinden gnr. 192-193 in the typed register DA 266260 p. 12). The co-heirs - Ole Andersen [farm unclear] for his wife Kari Amundsdatter, Tosten Andersen [Hvinden] for his wife [?] Amundsdatter, Amund Amundsen for himself, Jens Christophersen Haugtvedt for his wife Anne Amundsdatter, and others - sell 'til vor kiære Broder Gulbrand Amundsen Hvinden, hans Hustru Elie Arnes Datter og... Arvinger' their shares of the odel farm Søndre Hvinden in Gran (3 skippund tunge), which had fallen to them 'paa Skiftet... Søndre Hvinden den 19de November 1754... efter vores sal. Moder Kirstie Amundsdatter'. Shares named: Ole Andersen for his wife 88 rd 3 ort 13 s; Tosten Andersen and Jens Christophersen Haugtvedt each 66 rd for their wives. Reading of a difficult hand at 2x; the names Gulbrand Amundsen Hvinden, Elie Arnes Datter, Kirstie Amundsdatter, 'Broder', 'Moder', Søndre Hvinden and 19 Nov 1754 are clear. This independently ties the Gulbrand who married Elie Arnesdatter to the children of Kirsti Amundsdatter's 1754 estate (corroborates Farbroder argument). ¶8

Probate (index card): his estate was held at Hvinden on 16 Apr 1789 (Hadeland, Land og Valdres skifteprotokoll 1783-1802, f. 305a), with his widow Ellie Arnesdatter. Children: Hans 16, Anders 12, Kirsti 28, Anne 23, Karie 21, Abigael 9, Elline 7 1/2. Gross 1593-3-18, net 1012-2-13 rdr. He held 3 skippund of land in Hvinden, valued at 1400 rdr. The widow's estate in April 1798 (f. 924a) names the same children and shows Kirsti married to Peder Nilsen Fallanger. As Kirsti's father he is now Confirmed. ¶5

Records that disagree 1

Age discrepancy: the burial register gives his age as 48 in Feb 1789 (read on the scan, p. 651), which implies birth about 1740-41, two to three years after the 1738 baptism. Ages at burial in this register are often approximate, so this is noted but not treated as a contradiction. A probate (skifte) after him in 1789, or a confirmation entry c.1754-56, would confirm the link. ¶4
