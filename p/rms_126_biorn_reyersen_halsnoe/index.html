About the family

Father of Sophie Biørnsdatter (see her page), Confirmed, graded on his link to her. The 1731 probate names her as his daughter. Continuity from these records to Sophie Biørnsdatter in the tree (see her page, widow of Peder Nielsen of Halsnøy, probate 19 Mar 1787): (1) 1731, Halsnøy: Sophie Biørnsdatter, 17, daughter of Biørn Reyersen and Kari Jonsdatter, with her brother Rasmus Biørnsen, 11. (2) 1751, Halsnøy, 20 years later but at her mother's probate on the same farm: 'Sofi Biørnsdatter', married, with the same brother Rasmus Biørnsen and sisters Siri and Anna. (3) 1762 ekstraskatt, Halsnøy, 11 years later: 'Sophie Biørndatter', wife (Hustr) of the farmer Peder Nielsen, listed beside her brother Rasmus Biørnsen, also a farmer on Halsnøy; the couple's children over 12 are Biørn, Niels, Marthe and Ane Peders-. (4) 1787, Halsnøy, the same couple: the probate after Peder Nielsen names his widow Sophie Biørnsdatter and the children Biørn, Niels, Peder, Marthe, Anna and Britha. No other Sophie Biørnsdatter is named on Halsnøy or in the Sjernerø probates in these years. The couple's eldest son was named Biørn, after her father. ¶2

Other children: Rasmus Biørnsen (b. about 1720) was a farmer on Halsnøy in 1760-62. His first wife Kari Nielsdatter died before 20 Mar 1760 (D 11, DA 23888, fol. 36), leaving Biørn 14, Niels 10, Reyer 6 and Rasmus 3 1/2, so Rasmus named sons after his father Biørn and his grandfather Reyer. Siri and Anne were both married by 1751. ¶3

Life story

He was not on Halsnøy in the 1701 manntall, where the farmers were Ifver Ifversen, Peder Nielsen and Biørn Steensen, so he probably came from elsewhere. His father was a Reyer; not searched further. Test record for his parents: a probate with a Biørn Reyersen among the heirs, in the Ryfylke protocols D 3-D 4 (1700-1732). ¶4

Probate (scan): Ryfylke skiftedesignasjon D 9 (DA 23886) fol. 50, Stiernerøe Schibrede no. 19: 'Anno 1731 den 23de Maji' a probate was held on Halsnøen after Biørn Reyersen, between his widow Kari Jonsdatter and their children, entered in the protocol at fol. 355. The heirs: 1. Rasmus Biørnsen, 11; 2. Sophie Biørnsdatter, 17; 3. Siri Biørnsdatter, 6; 4. Anne Biørnsdatter, 3; all at home with their mother. The full protocol entry (D 4, DA 15920, fol. 355b) says the same in its preamble: the estate was divided between 'hans efterladte Enke Kari Joensdatter' and 'deres tilsammen avlede 4re Børn, nemlig Rasmus Biørnsen 11 aar gammel, Sophi Biørnsdatter 17 aar gl., Siri Biørnsdatter 6 aar gl. og Anne Biørnsdatter 3 aar gl.' The gross estate was 127 rd 4 ort 6 sk. He died before 23 May 1731. ¶1

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Father candidates (not adopted): the 1701 manntall has two Biørn Rejersens in Ryfylke. (a) Biørn Rejersen, 20 (b. c.1681), stepson of Olle Jacobsen, opsidder at Nordre Knudsvig, Hjelmeland parish, with brothers Sten Rejersen 12 and Hermand Rejersen 7, so his father Reyer died c.1694-1701 and his mother remarried Ole Jacobsen; (b) Biørn Rejersen, 24 (b. c.1677), servant at Reyelstad in Finnøy, born in that parish. Candidate (a) lived in the same parish as Halsnøy and his age fits a first child Sophie in 1714, but nothing ties either to Halsnøy, and there are two rivals, so no father is added. His son Rasmus named a son Reyer (b. c.1754), which fits a grandfather Reyer but does not choose between them. The Ryfylke probate cards 1682-1700 (DA 39935) have no Knudsvig card (Klungtvedt is followed by Kodde). ¶5a
