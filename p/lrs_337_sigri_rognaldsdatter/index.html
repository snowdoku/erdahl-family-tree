Life story

Probate: 'No. 16. Anno 1740 d. 2[6 or 9] April' a probate (skifte, estate division) was held in Lavik skipreide on the farm Asheim 'efter den dannequinde Sigri Rognaldsdatter' (after the honest woman Sigri Rognaldsdatter), between 'hendes efterlevende Mand Gullov Johansøn Asheim' (her surviving husband Gullov Johansen of Asheim) and their children: 1st son Philippus Dahle; 2nd son Siur, of a place read Indre-[?] or Medelfiord[?]; 1st daughter Johanne, married to Jens[?] Tinning; 2nd daughter Kari, married to Niels of the same place as Siur; 3rd daughter 'Ingeborre gift med Torkel Asheim' (Ingeborg, married to Torkel of Asheim); all of age ('ere myndige'). Present: the lensmann Johannes Eicken; the appraisers were Kolbein and Ole Asheim, appointed by the King's fogd (bailiff) S. Harberg (the same officials as in No. 17). Estate 136 rd 5 ort 2 sk, debts 16 rd 2 ort 8 sk, net about 120 rd. Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll 6a, fol. 18a, No. 16. The second digit of the day is unclear, more probably 6 than 9; she died before her husband and before 29 Apr 1740. ¶1

Birth (inference): her daughter Johanne was buried 3 Jul 1740 aged 35 (b. about 1705), so Sigri was born before about 1690. Her father was named Rognald (Ragnvald), from her patronymic; he is not identified. ¶2

Reading of Johanne's husband: read 'Hans Tinning[?]' in this probate, while read 'Jens Tynning' in No. 17. On a 3x zoom of fol. 18a reads the forename as 'Jens': the capital matches the J of 'Johanne' two lines up. The conflict is therefore probably a misreading and not a second husband; the forename is still marked uncertain. ¶4

Dates of No. 16 and No. 17: No. 17, the probate after Gullov himself with the same five heirs and the same lensmann, fogd and appraisers, is clearly dated 'Anno 1740 d. 29e April'. The day of No. 16 is '2' and a second digit that is more probably 6 than 9. No. 16 calls Gullov 'hendes efterlevende Mand' (her surviving husband): he outlived Sigri, which does not mean he was still alive on the day of the division. The two estates were evidently divided at the same visit, or within days of each other, hers first; No. 17 names no spouse. Sigri therefore died before Gullov, and both died before 29 Apr 1740. This does not affect Sigri's identity or her link to Ingeborg, which No. 16 states directly. ¶5

Alternatives considered and set aside 4

Possible brother (unproved, not linked): Lasse Rognaldsen of Asem (Asheim), whose estate was divided at Asem on 15 May 171[4 or 5] (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll 2b, viewer s. 460b, view 145, read from the scan): heirs his widow Laren [Karen?] Monsdatter and their children Rodn[?] (perhaps Rognald), Mathias[?], Jens[?], Kirsti and Britta, represented by their paternal uncle (Fader broderen) Ole Michland[?], so Ole was Lasse's brother. Lasse's patronymic and his farm Asheim fit a brother of Sigri, but as only his widow and children inherit, the record cannot name his sisters. The rest of the entry (view 146) is unread. ¶6

Probably not her sister: Anne (Anna) Rognaldsdatter of Brekke, Kvamsøy, whose probate of 26[?] Oct 1744 (Ytre Sogn 6b, No. 60, fol. 419a, view 67; heading read from the scan) lists as heirs her full brothers Siur[?] Rognaldsen and Johannes Kopperstad, full sisters Kari[?] (55, unmarried) and Brite, and half-siblings Anders[?] and Mari Endresen/-datter (father Endre Johansen Kvamme). No Sigri and none of Sigri's children (Philippus, Siur, Johanne's heirs, Kari, Ingeborg) appears, as they would if Sigri had been a full or half sister; only the heading was read, so this is probable, not certain. Her parents remain unidentified. ¶7

Two possible Rognalds at Asseim; father not linked: the 1701 census of Aasem (Asseim) lists Rognald Andersen, aged 45 (born about 1656), with Mons Larsen[?] 16 (patronymic Larsen, so probably a stepson) and his own sons Lasse 8 and Anders 4; it also lists, as a separate householder, the soldier Lasse Rognaldsen, aged 28 (born about 1673). Rognald Andersen would have been about 17 at the soldier's birth, so the soldier is probably the son of an older Rognald who is not in the 1701 list (a father aged 17 is possible but unlikely). The Lasse Rognaldsen of Asem whose estate was divided in 1714/15, with five children and a paternal uncle Ole, is almost certainly the soldier: Rognald Andersen's son Lasse would then have been only about 21, too young to leave five children. Sigri's eldest son Philippus was 3 in 1701 (born about 1698), so she was born about 1665-1680, the same generation as the soldier Lasse. She therefore fits a sister of the soldier Lasse and of Ole (children of the older Rognald) at least as well as a daughter of Rognald Andersen, who would have been about 9-24 at her birth. Rognald Andersen is not linked as her father, even at Speculative: the census does not list daughters, and there were evidently two Rognald families at Asseim. ¶8a

Not the 1733 Rognald of Asseim: the Stiftamtmann's table of Ytre Sogn probates for 1733 (24159 view 20, right page) has an estate on 'Assem' dated the 30th after 'Rognald' with heirs only the widow 'Giertrud Enke' (6 Rd 6 ß) and two sons Ole and Endre (3 Rd 3 ß each; the shares add up, so no other heir is omitted). Sigri, alive until 1740, is not among the heirs, so this Rognald was not her father. Her father is still unidentified; Rognald Andersen (45 in 1701) is ruled out only if he is the 1733 man, which the table does not say. ¶9
