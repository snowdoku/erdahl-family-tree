2 on the grade · 2 open questions

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Speculative mother of Hans Jacobsen of Stor-Tande (see his page); graded on her link to Hans. The 1660 card for the skifte after Jacob Erlandsen at Tande (39945/14047, fol. 41a of skifteprotokoll 1658-1673) lists 'g.m. Jøran Steenersdtr.' and then 'Barn: 1. Hans Jacobsen umyndig'. Under audit 8 rule 2 a card that lists the wife and then the children does not prove she is the mother (she could be a second wife), and the protocol opening was not read. Hans named a daughter Giøran, which fits. Speculative rather than Probable because both the mother link and the 1660-1698 identification of Hans are unproved. ¶1

Upgrade (to Probable): graded on her link to Hans Jacobsen of Stor-Tande (see his page). The protocol entry behind card 14047 was: skifteprotokoll 1658-1673 (source 25153), fol. 41a (view 43), 16 Apr 1660, 'Skifte effter afgangne Sl. Jacob Erlandsen paa Thande i Lessøe Prestegield, Imellem hans efterlatte Hustru Jøran Steenersdatter, og deris Barn, Hans Jacobsen, umyndig'. 'deris Barn' names her as the mother of Hans (audit 8 rule 2 now met; this supersedes the earlier note that the card alone left her Speculative). She stays Probable, not Confirmed, for the same reason as Jacob Erlandsen: the minor Hans Jacobsen of Tande in 1660 is identified with Hans Jacobsen who died at Stortande in 1698 across 38 years with no record read in between (same farm, and Hans named children Jacob and Giøran). ¶3a

Open questions and the next record to read 2

Test record: the opening of the protocol entry at fol. 41a, skifteprotokoll 1658-1673 (Nord-Gudbrandsdal sorenskriveri), looking for 'deris (sammen avlede) Børn'. ¶2

Test record: the 1664-66 manntall for Lesja (Hans Jacobsen on Tande, with his age) or a Nord-Gudbrandsdal tingbok entry 1660-1698. ¶3b
