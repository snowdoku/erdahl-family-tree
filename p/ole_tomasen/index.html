Life story

Husmann (cottager) in Stjørdal, 1820s. ¶1

Baptized 15 Mar 1795, son of Thomas Andersen and Ane Olsdatter of Almogjerdet. The 1801 census lists him, aged 6, as a child of both Thomas Andersen and Anne Olsdatter at Almoen. ¶2

The 1801 census listing for Almoen puts his whole family in one view: his father Thomas Andersen, 30, 'Mand 2det egteskab, Huusmand med jord'; his mother Anne Olsdatter, 41, in her first marriage; and the children Ole 6, Jon 3 and Randi 1. The same listing has Thomas's father Anders Larsen, 67, as 'Huusbonde' and owner-occupier of the farm itself, and Thomas's brother Lars Andersen, 36, as a second cottager - three generations and two households on one farm. ¶4

Confirmed 1 Oct 1809 (Almoåsen). Married 4 Jun 1816 at Stjørdal (Værnes), a bachelor and cottager of Almoåsen born 1795, to Mærit Olsdatter of Gedderaas (Geiteråsen), born 1795. Buried 31 May 1822, a cottager of Almoåsen, born 1795, months after his son Thomas was born. Known children: Kirsti (bapt. 19 Mar 1820) and Thomas (b. 2 Jan 1822). ¶3
