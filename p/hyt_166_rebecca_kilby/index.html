About the family

Her father's will (16 Sept. 1738, proved 2 Aug. 1742, Suffolk docket 7782) leaves 'to daughter, Rebecca Allen, but 5s.' because she had already had her share, then 'provides further for daughter, Rebecca Allen, and if she dies, for her children' (TAG 27:203, abstract with quotations). ¶2

Mother of Elisabeth (Elizabeth Allen): the Brattle Church baptisms for 1724-25 list the child 'Eliz. Allen' with the parent 'Rebekah Allen' (Manifesto Church records, pp. 146-147; the child's date falls in the spring of 1725 but cannot be aligned exactly in the OCR), matching the civil birth of 'Elisabeth daughter of Jer. and Rebekah Allen', 22 April 1725. Graded Probable on her link to Elizabeth (Allen) Prince: the 1725 child's identity with Job Prince's wife is inferred (see Jeremiah Allen). ¶3

Life story

Birth and baptism: born 27 Dec. 1701, baptised at the Old South Church 28 Dec. 1701, daughter of Christopher and Sarah (Simpkins) Kilby; admitted to the Brattle Church 1 April 1722; married there 18 Oct. 1722 Jeremiah Allen; living 1738 (TAG 27 (1951): 203, compiled from the Boston and church records). The Brattle Church marriage list prints the couple as 'Mr. Jeremiah Allen' and 'Mrs. Rebecca Kilby' (Manifesto Church records, marriages nos. 539-540, October 1722). ¶1

Civil birth found: the Boston register of births for 1701 enters 'Rebecca Daughter of Christopher Kelbey and Sarah his Wife', aligned in the OCR with 27 December 1701 (Record Commissioners vol. 24). Her own grade (on her link to Elizabeth Allen) is unchanged. ¶5

Widow: 'Rebecka Allen of Boston Widow' was appointed administratrix of 'her late Husband Jeremiah Allen late of Boston Felt Maker', about 11 April 1760 (Suffolk Probate 56:365, docket 12319; image read). This confirms that Rebecca's husband lived until 1756-60 and so was not the merchant Jeremiah Allen who died by 1755 leaving a widow Elizabeth. Her grade (Probable, on her link to Elizabeth Allen) is unchanged: no record read names Elizabeth Prince as her daughter. ¶4

1 on the grade

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: graded on her link to Elizabeth Allen. The 1814 deed (Suffolk Deeds, DGS 007442718 image 570, read) shows Elizabeth Prince, wife of Capt. Job Prince, as an heir of Jeremiah Allen, deceased, taking a share of his West Boston land; his father's will (1756) names her as Jeremiah's child Elizabeth. Her mother: the only Elizabeth born to Jeremiah is 'Elisabeth daughter of Jer. and Rebekah Allen', 22 Apr. 1725 (Boston births vol. 24; Brattle Church baptism 'Eliz. Allen', parent 'Rebekah Allen'). Window: Elizabeth Prince died about 6 Dec. 1785 'in 61st yr', so she was born Dec. 1724-Dec. 1725, wholly inside Jeremiah's marriage to Rebecca (18 Oct. 1722 to his death 1756-60, Rebecca his widow in 1760); Jeremiah had no earlier wife (married at 23, first child 1723). The relationship is named by the birth record and the identity by the deed and will, so the earlier Probable notes are superseded. ¶6
