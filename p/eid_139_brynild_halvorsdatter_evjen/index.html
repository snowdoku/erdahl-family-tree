Life story

Not set as mother of Ingebrigt Bjørnsen (b. 1725). The 1762 list does not prove she was his mother, since stepchildren were listed in the same column. Supporting evidence: the Selbu bygdebok holder block for Stor-Evjen shows '1716... Brynhild' beside Bjørn, which may be a marriage year in 1716, and her son Halvor carries her father's name. ¶2a

1762 extra tax list: wife of 'Biørn Ingebrigtsen' at the farmer-owned holding 681, written 'Storesien', Selbu. With them were the married sons Ingebrigt (wife Mali Olsdatter, daughter Kari) and Halvor (wife Brit Olsdatter). ¶1

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
