About the family

Daughter of Ingebrigt Olsen of Store Evjen. In his probate, 19 Jun 1744 (Skifteprotokoll 3, 24753, fol. 93b), she is listed among the heirs as '[Ma]li Ingebrigtsdatter Haavar Olsen Stor Efjens Hustrue' (wife of Haavar Olsen of Store Evjen); a few lines later the record names her again, 'ligesaa Mali Ingebrigtsd.', and her brother 'Biørn Ingebrigtsen' acts as a guardian. Her link to her father is therefore from a primary record she is named in. ¶1

Life story

In the 1762 extra tax (canvas 141, Store Evjen no. 738) she and her husband 'Høvar Olsen' head the farm with three sons over 12: 'Mogens, gift med Johanne Nielsdatter', 'Christen' and 'Ingebrigt'. The sons are given no patronymic. Christen and Ingebrigt cannot be Håvard's sons, because in 1772 the same pair appear at Store Evjen as 'Christen Christensen Stor Efjen' and 'Ingebrigt Christensen Stor Efjen' (24757 fol. 530). The third son is a Christensen too: Mogens, married in 1762 to Johanne Nielsdatter, is the 'Mogens Christensen' who with 'Johanna Nielsd.' had a son Niels baptised in 1772 (16311 c89), and 'Mogens Christensen Stor Efjen' and 'Engelbrecht (= Ingebrigt) Christensen ibid.' stood as cautioners together in 1789 and 1794. So all three were her sons by an earlier husband named Christen, who died before 1744, when she was already Håvard's wife. Naming fits: her son Ingebrigt bears her father's name, and the direct ancestor Christen Christensen (Lien) named his daughter Mali (b. 1769). ¶2

Burial: on the 21st Sunday after Trinity (27 Oct) 1765, 'graufæst Mali Ingebrichtsd: Stor Efjen gl: 73½ aar' (16311 c18 R, read at full resolution; the age is clearly 73½). So she was born about 1692 and died in October 1765. The identification rests on name and farm: she was the Mali Ingebrigtsdatter heading Store Evjen with Håvard Olsen in 1762, and no other Mali Ingebrigtsdatter at Store Evjen is known. Birth changed from BEF 1720 to ABT 1692 on this age. This fits her father Ingebrigt Olsen (born about 1657). It makes her about 43 at the birth of Christen Christensen (born about 1735), which is late but possible. Her grade on her link to Christen stays Probable. ¶4

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Confidence Probable: her parentage (daughter of Ingebrigt Olsen) is stated in the 1744 probate, but her motherhood of Christen Christensen rests on the 1762 household plus the 1772 names, not on a record that names her as his mother. Her first husband, a Christen, is not entered. ¶3a
