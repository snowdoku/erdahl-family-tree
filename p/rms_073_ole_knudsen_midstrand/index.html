About the family

Father of a Juel baptised in Aurdal in January 1755 (Aurdal ministerialbok 1730-1762, DA 9361, p. 452, canvas 228: 'Ole Knudsen Loes[?] og Astri Juelsd:'). Possibly the Ole Knudsen Mitstrand who stood surety at a 1748 Svenes marriage (DA 9361 canvas 304). Speculative father of Jul Olsen of Midtstrand (b. about 1755, d. 1810): see the note on Jul Olsen for the argument and its gaps. Birth inferred only from fatherhood in 1755. ¶1

Parents: very probably Knud Olsen and Marit Arnesdatter of Lo (Loe), Svenes, whose lifetime division of 19 Feb 1754 names a son Ole first among their children (DA 39948 card 8651; DA 25287 fol. 539b). He baptised his son Juel as 'Ole Knudsen Loes' 11 months later. The parents are Probable; see their notes. His own grade (as father of Jul Olsen) is unchanged. This also explains the farm word Loe at Jul's 1769 confirmation ('Juel Olsøn Lo[e]'): the family was at Lo, and Jul moved to Midtstrand later. ¶3

Life story

Ole Knudsen Mitstrand stood surety for Gunild: at the betrothal on 4 Jun 1748 of 'Henrick Juulsøn Maanum og Gunild Knudsd. Loe' (Svenes; married 1 Jul), the sureties were 'Klokkeren Ole Halbiørnsen og Ole Knudsøn Mitstrand' (DA 9361 p. 70, canvas 304). Gunild is the fifth child of the 1754 Lo division (married to Hendrik Juelsen), and a bride's brother was the usual surety, so this joins Ole Knudsen 'Mitstrand' to the Lo family six years before Ole Knudsen 'Loes' baptised his son Juel. It does not name him as her brother, so the grade stays Probable, but it removes the doubt in about whether the Mitstrand Ole and the Lo Ole were one man. ¶4

Context: 'Ole Knudss. Mitstrand' was the first godfather at the baptism of Marit, daughter of Ole Arnesen and Ragnild Helgesdatter of Midtstrand, at Svenes on 6 Oct 1754 (DA 9361 p. 435, canvas 225 left page). So an Ole Knudsen lived at Midtstrand in October 1754, three months before 'Ole Knudsen Loes og Astri Juelsd.' had their son Juel baptised (January 1755). This does not settle whether the two are one man. ¶6

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: Speculative to Probable on the 1769 Aurdal confirmation of Juel Olsen Lo[e] (DA 9362 canvas 191) plus the re-read 1755 baptism. Re-read and new evidence: the Jan 1755 baptism (DA 9361 p. 452, canvas 228, read at full resolution) reads 'Item Ole Knudsen Loes [or Løs] og Astri Juelsd., b[arn] N[omine] Juel', godparents 'Helge Braa[?]/Strand[?], Tor Helgesen ibid., Ole Knudsen Fi[?]venger[?], Birthe Halvorsd. Draag og Guri Olsd. Gausager'. Loes/Løs is a farm name: 'Barbara Knudsd[atte]r Løs' was a bride in Aurdal in 1752 (DA 9361 p. 84, canvas 311), the same surname as the widow Barbara Knudsdatter Midtstrand of 1747, so the Knud-children of Løs and Midtstrand look like one family. 'Ole Knudsøn Mitstrand' stood surety in Aug 1752 (p. 85) as well as 1748. The Aurdal confirmation of 1769 (DA 9362 p. 401, canvas 191) lists 'Juel Olsøn Lo[e]', 15 1/2, so this Juel survived. I read the Aurdal confirmation lists of 1768-1776 (canvases 191-196), and he is the only Juel Olsen in them, the right age for Jul Olsen of Midtstrand (46 in 1801, 55 at burial in 1810). Jul's first son was Ole, his first daughter Aaste (Astri), and he had a son Knud, which is the naming you would expect from paternal grandparents Ole Knudsen and Astri. Raised from Speculative to Probable. It is not Confirmed: no record yet names Jul Olsen of Midtstrand as the son of Ole Knudsen, and Loes is not Midtstrand. The 1755 baptism pages either side (c222-225, c229) hold one other Juel, son of Gulbrand Christophersen H[aug]rud and Ingeborg Juelsdatter (Feb 1755, c229), confirmed 1773 as Juel Gulbrandsen Haugsrud, so not ours. ¶2

Searches that found nothing 1

Marriage search: Aurdal marriages DA 9361 canvases 284-289 (Jul 1735 to Sep 1739) and 312-313 (Sep 1752 to Dec 1753) read in full: no Ole Knudsen x Astri Juelsdatter and no Ole Juelsen x Ragnild Knudsdatter. With the earlier passes (c269-283, c290-296, c300-309, c311, c314-316) the Aurdal register 1730-1754 is now read except c297-299 (1744-45) and c310 (1752). Both marriages were probably held in another parish (the bride's), or in the unread canvases. His grade is unchanged. ¶5
