Life story

Marriage: Nicolaus Lindenmajer, son of 'Hn Johann Christoph', married Catharina 'Schopfen', daughter of 'Hn Jacob', at Metterzimmern (next to Bietigheim, the bride's parish) on 3 Sep 1737 (groom row 5517933, bride row 905517933, image 1184926-00850). Children at Bietigheim with Catharina (index): Christina Catharina (5 Dec 1738, father 'Johann Nicolaus'), Maria Barbara (16 Dec 1739), Sophia Rosina (4 Mar 1759), Nicolaus (17 Mar 1761); grown children married there: Christoph Jacob (1764), Catharina (1777) and Rosina Catharina (1783). Burial: Nicolaus Lindenmajer, Bietigheim, 23 Jul 1767 (record 7059003; no relatives or age indexed; the only Nicolaus Lindenmaier of the period there). Baptism (about 1705-15) not indexed. Images not seen; graded on the faithfully transcribed index row under the overnight rule for this collection. ¶2

Baptism (identification Probable): 'Nicolaus Lindenmeister' (misread surname), baptised at Bietigheim on 23 Jan 1703, parents 'Johann Christoph, Agnes Johanna' (Ancestry 61023 record 7268393, image 1184899-00264). This fits the 1737 groom, whose father was 'Hn Johann Christoph' (record 5517933), and he would be 34 at that marriage and 64 at his burial in 1767. A sister Sophia Rosina Catharina was baptised there on 22 Feb 1706 to the same parents (record 7268511). The only other Nicolaus Lindenmaier at Bietigheim is an older man (a daughter Anna Maria, father 'Nicolai', married there on 18 Jan 1729, record 907270504), of the previous generation, and his own son Nicolaus (b. 1761). Index row only; image not seen. ¶3

Burial image: Bietigheim burial register 1767, 'Nicolay Lindenmajer', died 21 Jul, buried 23 Jul 1767, aged '63 Jahr 2 Mon. [..] Tag'; no parents named; a short description follows the name that I could not read with confidence. The age gives a birth about May 1704, not quite the baptism of 23/24 Jan 1704 (63 years 5 months); the year agrees and there is no other Nicolaus baptism of 1703-04 at Bietigheim, so this is not treated as a contradiction. The 1737 marriage image calls him 'Nicolaus Lindenmajer, Kupferschmid', son of 'Hn Johann Christoph Lindenmajer, Feldmesser und Kupferschmid zu Bietigheim'; with the coppersmith trade at his 1704 baptism (father 'Kupfferschmid', mother Agnes Johanna) this bridges 1704-1737 (see Agnes Johanna Jenisch). Image read signed in on FamilySearch, 2 Nov 2026. ¶6

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Graded on that link: Probable. The groom row of the elder Johann Melchior's marriage at Bietigheim, 8 Feb 1774, reads 'Johann Melchior Lindenmajer', relatives 'Nicolaus, Elisabetha Regina' (record 7053963), so the 1774 groom's father was Nicolaus; but the identification of the 1774 groom with the father of the 1803 Illingen groom (the man in the tree) rests on elimination, so this link stays Probable. ¶1b

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed, graded on his link to his son Johann Melchior Lindenmaier the elder (see his page). Record: Bietigheim marriage register, 8 Feb 1774, entry 3 (image read): 'Johann Melchior Lindenmajer, Meister des Schuhmacher Handwerks, weil. Nicolaus Lindenmajers Bürger u. Kupferschmids allhier ehel. led. Sohn' married Elisabetha Regina, daughter of Jacob Reuter/Renter. The record names the relationship (son of the late Nicolaus, coppersmith). Continuity to the man in the tree: the 1814 marriage image of the tree's younger Johann Melchior (widower of Louise Catharina Fink, born 12 Sep 1778 Bietigheim) names his parents as Johann Melchior Lindenmaier, Bürger und Schuster, and Elisabetha née Reitter; his 1778 baptism names Johann Melchior x Elisabetha Reuter. Same name, trade, wife and town as the 1774 groom, 4 years later, and no rival Johann Melchior (sweep of about 60 Bietigheim Lindenmaier rows). The 1813/1814 conflict that held this grade is resolved: the remarriage was 22 Feb 1814 (image). Identity of the 1774 groom's father with this Nicolaus: the 1737 Metterzimmern marriage image calls him 'Nicolaus Lindenmajer, Kupferschmid, Hr Johann Christoph Lindenmajers... zu Bietigheim ehl. Sohn', the same trade as 'weil. Nicolaus... Kupferschmid' in 1774, and the only other Nicolaus at Bietigheim was of the previous generation (daughter married 1729) or his own son born 1761. He was dead by 1774 ('weil.'), which fits his burial of 23 Jul 1767. The earlier notes holding him at Probable are superseded. Image read signed in on FamilySearch, 2 Nov 2026. ¶5

The case for this identification 1

Labelled evidence, grade unchanged (Probable): the 1774 groom's father is labelled, QPGL-8R9S (Bietigheim 8 Feb 1774), Father 'Nicolaus Lindenmajer'; and V4LM-H8L ('Germany, Births and Baptisms'): Johann Melchior Lindenmajer, christened Bietigheim 1 Feb 1748, Parents 'Nicolaus Lindenmajer, Catharina'. So Nicolaus is certainly the father of the 1774 groom; but the tree's elder Johann Melchior is identified with the 1774 groom only through his son's 1778 baptism and the conflicting 1813/1814 remarriage dates (see Johann Melchior Lindenmaier). Under audit 4 rule 1 that identification belongs to this grade, so Probable. Own baptism, labelled: FamilySearch, 'Germany, Births and Baptisms, 1558-1898' V4LM-GV4, born 23 Jan 1704, christened Bietigheim 24 Jan 1704, Parents 'Johann Christoff Lindenmeyer, Agnes Jo[hanna]' (Ancestry 7268393 gave 23 Jan 1703; 1704 is the labelled reading). Burial: QPGP-PDG3, Bietigheim 23 Jul 1767, birth 1704 from age. Marriage, labelled: QPGY-D23L, Metterzimmern 3 Sep 1737 (pages 60-61), Father 'Hn Johann Christoph Lindenmajers', Wife 'Catharina Schopfen', Father-in-law 'Hn Jacob Schopfen'. Note: a FamilySearch row QP2T-PTD2 names a Nicolaus Lindenmaier with wife 'Ursula Katharina Schopfin' (child Juliana); probably the same wife's fuller name, not checked. Read signed in, 2 Nov 2026; labelled index fields, image not seen (partner-only). ¶4
