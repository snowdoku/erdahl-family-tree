About the family

Father of Rasmus Kristoffersen Tjellaug: Probable. Aaland (1932, Kjellog bruk 1 'Uppigard') names him as holder in 1697 and husband of Synnøve Jensdatter, who later married Eirik Eiriksen. The 1714 deed (pantebok 12493 pag. 146) proves Rasmus 'Christophersen' was the stepson of Eirik and son of Synnøve's line at Tjellaug, which fits a first husband named Kristoffer; Rasmus's son Christopher Rasmussen (named in Brite Rasmusdatter's 1754 probate) fits naming after him. No record read names Kristoffer himself, hence Probable. ¶1

Life story

1645 poll tax: 'Thielouff' (Tjellaug), Oppstryn sogn, p. 5, lists Christoffer, Agata, Marite, Marite, Siffuer, Dorete, Siffuer, Iffuer, Marite (pt00000000066094-66102), with no Rasmus and no Jens. So neither Kristoffer's father (a Rasmus) nor Synnøve Jensdatter's father farmed Tjellaug in 1645. The Christoffer here is presumably the 'Christoffer Rasmussøn, 60, widower' of the 1664 vicar's census, and Agata, listed next, presumably his wife then (the Nordfjord list gives no positions). If Kristoffer Rasmussen was born about 1650-70, his father Rasmus may have been a son of this Christoffer Rasmussen too young to be taxed in 1645 or living elsewhere; nothing states it. ¶3

Possibly a son of the Christoffer Rasmussøn, 60, widower, at half Tjellaug in the 1664 vicar's census (p. 210); naming only, not linked. ¶2

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
