Life story

1645 poll tax (koppskatt) for Ytre Sogn, Quambsøe Skibredt (Kvamsøy skipreide), local parish 'Quambsøe och Arenefiord Sogene', p. 50, under 'Gaardmend och derris Quinder' (farmers and their wives): farm 'Thue', 'Hanns' (pt00000000058599) followed by 'Giertrud Andersdtr' (pt00000000058600). They are the only couple listed on Thue; the rows before are Stochebøe (Botollff x Aneche Enersdtr) and after BruJelle (Joen x Gietleff Nielsdtr) and Skosem (Anders x Marj Thosensdtr). Read. ¶1

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the grade · 1 on the identification · 1 record that disagrees · 1 open question

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Probable as mother of Povel Hansen (graded on her link to that son). For: (1) she is Hans of Thue's wife in 1645, when Povel (b. about 1632-35) was about 10-13; (2) Hans (b. about 1600) is the same man on the same farm 19-21 years later, with no rival Hans there; (3) the second son is Anders, her father's name by the usual naming pattern. ¶3a

The case for this identification 1

Identification of the farm: the 1645 poll tax is arranged by skipreide, and Thue sits among the Kvamsøy-skipreide farms on the north shore (Stokkebø, Skåsheim), which by 1664-1701 belonged to Leikanger prestegjeld; the 1701 census places Thue (the sorenskriver's farm) in Leganger parish a few rows from Esse. set this row aside as 'Kvamsøy, Vik, not Povel Hansen's father', but the farm is the same Tue: no other Thue/Tue appears in the Ytre Sogn or Leikanger 1645 rows, and the farmer is again a Hans in 1664 (bailiff: 'Thue, Hans', son Povel Hansen 30) and 1666 (vicar: Tue, Hans bruger 66, sons Povl 34 and Anders 32). ¶2

Records that disagree 1

Against or open: the 1645 Ytre Sogn poll tax does not list Povel or Anders (children under 12-15 were not taxed), so no record names her as their mother; Hans could have had an earlier wife before about 1632. ¶3b

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Would confirm: Hans Tue's or Giertrud's probate (none before 1698 survives), or a tingbok entry naming Povel Hansen's mother. ¶3c
