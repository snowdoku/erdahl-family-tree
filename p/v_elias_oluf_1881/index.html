Life story

First surviving child of Martinus Eliassen and Hansine Pedersdatter, baptized at Borgund in 1881; the parents' first child had been stillborn in 1880. The date given is the baptism date, 26 June 1881, which is the only date the index supplies. ¶1

He had died before the 1891 census, in which the only Elias at Valderhaug is the brother baptized in 1885 - the family reused the name, as they did again in 1898. ¶2

The Borgund transcription names only the child and the parents; no godparents are indexed for this book. ¶3
