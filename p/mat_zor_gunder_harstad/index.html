About the family

Son of the Norwegian-born Andreas A. Harstad and Marit Jonsdatter, and husband of Emma Clara Eidem, whom he married 21 September 1922 in Lac qui Parle County, Minnesota. Through this marriage the Harstads become the largest non-Eidem family in Zoar churchyard. ¶1

Six children: Blanche Mildred (1923-1924), Arne Milton (1925-1988), Gordon Erling (1927-2005), Arlene Delores Woolery (1931-2022), Joan Marian Goens (1934-2010) and Kenneth Leroy (1938-2017). ¶3

Life story

He appears aged 6 in his father's household in the 1900 census of Augusta Township, Lac qui Parle County, Minnesota. ¶2

A neighbour of the Eidems south of Marietta, Lac qui Parle County, Minnesota, in 1923. Minneapolis Tidende, 15 March 1923, p. 7: he and Elias Slind were sheltering with Mikal Paulsen Eidem in a hollow on Leroy Pratt's farm when the bank collapsed on 9 March 1923; both were dug out unconscious and revived, while Eidem suffocated. ¶5

Recorded because HAARSTAD is a Selbu farm name and the Selbulag - the American society of emigrants from Selbu - had an 'Ole S. Haarstad' as its local subscription collector at Windom, Minnesota in 1914. A Gunder Harstad walking home from a farm auction with a Slind and an Eidem is another member of the same parish colony. No kinship to this family is claimed or implied; he is here as a documented associate only, which is why the confidence is Speculative. ¶6

Burial: Zoar Lutheran Cemetery, lot 5, block 1 — South Dakota state burial register, 'GUNDER HARSTAD 10-03-1960, lot 5, block 1'. ¶4
