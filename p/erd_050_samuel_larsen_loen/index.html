Life story

Aaland 1932: Loen bruk nr. 15, printed p. 321-322: 'Samuel Larsson, † 79 a. g. 1744, b. 1690 1 l. — 2⅓ pd. krg., n. b. 1721. Pass 1710. * 1. e[nkja]... Hansd. 2. Brite Rasmusd. 3. Anne Paulsd., † 71 a. g. 1750. Son. Hans var utanlands i 1747.' He died in 1744 aged 79 (born about 1665), leased 1 laup of crown land at Loen in 1690 with a new lease in 1721, had a passport in 1710, and married three times: first a widow,... Hansdatter (OCR unclear), then Brite Rasmusdatter, then Anne Paulsdatter (died 1750 aged 71). A son Hans was abroad in 1747. His successor was 'S. Lars' (son Lars). ¶1

Death date: Aaland gives 1744, but the probate was held 15 Sep 1747. A probate can follow the death by some time, so the death is at least before 15 Sep 1747; the year 1744 is unconfirmed. ¶6

Probate of 15 Sep 1747 (A 10 fol. 16b): widow Ane Paulsdatter (Aaland's 3rd wife 'Anne Paul[sd.]'); sons Hans Samuelsen and Lars Samuelsen; daughters Brite Samuelsdatter (apparently unmarried, with a guardian, perhaps '[half-]brother Lars Samuelsen Loen'), Synneve Samuelsdatter, wife of Rasmus Olsen [farm unclear], Brite Samuelsdatter, wife of Paul [?] [Mardal?], and Ane Samuelsdatter, wife of Rasmus Olsen Loen. Several residences and the guardian clause are uncertain readings. ¶5

Aaland 1932: Loen bruk nr. 6 (printed p. 319) lists Rasmus Olsen (died 1756 aged 55) whose second wife was 'Ane Samuelsd. Loen', who later married Jakob Olsen of bruk nr. 16 and died in 1783 aged 70 (born about 1713). She is probably Samuel Larsen's daughter, but Aaland does not say so. ¶2

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Confidence: Probable as father of Lars, on Aaland's 'S.' (son). A probate after a Samuel Larsen of Loen (Olden skipreide) is indexed in the Nordfjord probate register for 1747-1750, protocol 10, fol. 16b (Digitalarkivet pa00000000053734); it has not been read and should name his son Lars. The 1701 census of Loen, where Samuel should appear aged about 36, would also help. Which of his wives was Lars's mother is not stated. ¶3

Confidence raised from Probable to Confirmed: the probate after Samuel Larsen of Loen in Olden skipreide, held 15 Sep 1747 (Nordfjord skifteprotokoll A 10, fol. 16b), names among his children 'Lars Samuelsen gift og boende her paa Gaarden' (married and living here on the farm). The shares on fol. 17b-18a include 'Lars Samuelsen', and Hans Samuelsen's share mentions 'sin Broder Lars Samuelsen Loen'. Read from the scan. ¶4
