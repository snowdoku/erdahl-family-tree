About the family

Father of Halvor (bapt. 8 Nov 1829), Hans (b. 16 Apr 1833) and Ole (bapt. 14 Feb 1836) with Gunnil Halvorsdatter, at Nordgarden, Nissedal. Named as Halvor's father at Halvor's 1855 marriage. Lived with Halvor at Rønningen in the 1865 census (age 69, føderaadsmand) with his later wife Ingebjørg Bendiksdatter (b. ca 1810, Kviteseid). ¶1

Life story

Birth date changed from 'ABT 1796' to before 10 Feb 1797, the baptism. Ages conflict slightly: 5 at the census of 1 Feb 1801 (b. about 1795-96) and 66 at his marriage in Nov 1863 (b. about 1797). The baptism is preferred. Godfather Evind Torjusen Tvetene is taken to be his mother's brother (see Kari Torjusdatter). ¶9

Baptism: on 10 Feb 1797 in Nissedal church 'Tallev Olsen Søftestads Søn Torjus' was baptised. Godparents (Test.): Torbør Witth, Jorand Ivarsd.[?] Tingstoe, Torgild Ormsd.[?] Grimstved, Evind Torjusen Tvetene and Anders Johansen Grimstved. The mother is not named (usual in this book). Kviteseid ministerialbok 1787-1799 (source 7983), Børne Daab 1797, p. 107 verso (canvas 115, left page). This replaces the earlier note that his baptism was not found. ¶8

His parentage is Probable. He is the only Torjus Tellefsen of the right age in the 1801 census of Kviteseid prestegjeld, which then included Nissedal: Torjus Tellevsen, age 5, at Søftestad, with parents Tellev Olsen and Kari Torjusdatter. He is named after his maternal grandfather Torjus. His brother Halvor Tellefsen (b. ca 1799) of Søftestad married in 1829. ¶3

Second marriage: on 16 Nov 1863 in Kviteseid church, 'Enkemand' (widower) Torjus Tellefsen, 66, born Søftestad, living at Rønningen in Nissedal, son of Tellef Olafsen, married spinster (pige) Ingeborg Berntsdatter Strand, 51, born 'Strømnæs(?)', daughter of Bernt (Berent) Torjesen. Banns were read 1, 8 and 15 Nov; the marriage witnesses were T[?] Jo. Gundersen and Olaf Olafsen Bugten. The remarks cite 'Skifterettens Attest af 31te Oct. 1863', the probate court's certificate that the estate after his first wife had been dealt with. ¶5

Died 8 Mar 1872 and buried 19 Mar 1872 in Nissedal, age about 73, as 'Thorjus Tellefsen, føderaadsmand' (scan read; residence hard to read). ¶2

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Confidence raised from Probable to Confirmed: his 1863 marriage entry, read from the scan, states that he was born at Søftestad and that his father was Tellef Olafsen (= Olsen), and his age (66) agrees with the 1801 census (Torjus Tellevsen, 5, at Søftestad, child of Tellev Olsen and Kari Torjusdatter, both in their first marriage). The same entry places him at Rønningen, where he lived with his son Halvor in 1865, and his son's 1855 marriage names him as father. This replaces the earlier reliance on the 1801 census alone. A different Torjus Tellefsen, 22, of Tjane, married Margit Olsdatter on 3 Dec 1846. ¶7

Records that disagree 1

Contradiction: the 1865 census calls his wife Ingebjørg Bendiksdatter, 55 (b. about 1810), against Ingeborg Berntsdatter, 51 (b. about 1812), in the 1863 marriage entry. The marriage entry names her father, so its patronymic is preferred; the age differs by four years either way. The Kviteseid index misreads him as 'Tojus Tellefsen' and her father as 'Beret Torjasen'. ¶6

Searches that found nothing 1

Kviteseid/Nissedal baptisms before 1800 are not indexed, so his baptism (ca 1795-96) was not found. ¶4
