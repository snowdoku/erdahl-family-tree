Life story

Husmann at Øvre/Ytre Eide (Eidehagen), Avaldsnes. In records he is variously Orm Jonsen, Orm 'Arent' Johnsen and Arent Johnsen. His son Lars's patronymic was also written Aanensen and Aarentsen. A grandson was named Aanen (1839). ¶1

1801 census, Odland (Skjold): Orm Jonsen, 22, unmarried sailor (matros), son of Jon Poulsen and Inger Larsdatter. Identified as the same man by place, age, his 1804 marriage at Aadland, and his sons John (1804) and Poul (1809), named after Jon Poulsen and his father. ¶3

Married 1 Jan 1804 at Tysvær, Skjold parish: 'Orm Johnssøn, ungkarl, Aadland' and 'Rognald Pedersdr., pige, Aadland'. Son John Ormsen baptised 15 Aug 1804. ¶2

Widowed 1824. Remarried 14 Jul 1825 in Avaldsnes to Ane Jansdatter, as 'Orm Johnsen yttre', aged about 47. Possibly the 'Arent Johnsen fra Akre' who was godparent in 1839. Death not found. ¶5

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: Confirmed as the father of Lars Ormsen (see his page). Lars's baptism on 16 Dec 1818 (Avaldsnes ministerialbok 1816-1825, p. 26 no. 4) names the father as Orm Jonsen and the mother as Ragla Peersdatter of Ytre Eide. Lars married in 1845 as 'Lars Ormssen Yttreeide', aged 25 and born at Ytre Eide (Skudenes ministerialbok 1841-1846, p. 186 no. 31), which fits a birth in Dec 1818. Patronymic, farm and age join the baptism to the groom. His own link to the 1801 Odland household of Jon Poulsen stays Probable. ¶6
