Life story

Probate 1729 (skifte no. 188): '1729 Novembris 7de' at the farm [Tvedt] in 'Treungens annex og Hvideseid Præstegield', the sheriff Hans Ellefsen Lung and the appraisers Ole Aarak(?) and Andbiørn Bræche registered the estate 'efter afdøde Margithe Biørnsdaatter, som ingen livsarvinger [efterlod]' (who left no children of her own), to be divided between her surviving husband 'Gulowe Haavorsøn' and her [late?] brother's children, 'Nafnlig: Orm Igildsøn 12 aar, Tone Igilsdaatter 20 aar, Kari og Walborg døttre 17 og 10 aar gamle', on whose behalf their guardian Salve [..]land attended. The index card (6856 view 1315) heads the heirs 'Broderbørn' (brother's children); the scan's margin and the line ending are faint, and 'broders' is read from the card more than the scan. Land 'part i Tvedt' is in the estate. Crops and -6856-v1315-tveit-treungen-1729.jpg. ¶1

Collateral: sister of Egil (see his page), added because her probate is the record that ties Egil to the patronymic Bjørnsen. Wife of Gulløv Haavorsen; childless. Her own existence and death are from the probate (primary); her link to her father Bjørn is her patronymic. ¶2

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
