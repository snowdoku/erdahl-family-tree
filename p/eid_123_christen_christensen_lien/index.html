About the family

Father of Magli Christensdatter (see her page), rated Probable. The 1769 baptism names him explicitly as Mali's father, and it fits Magli, wife of Erik Torstensen of Uglan: Mali is the Selbu form of Magli; a mid-1769 birth fits her age 32 in the 1801 census; her daughter born in 1796 was named Brynil (Brynhild), after this Brynild Olsdatter, as Selbu naming custom expects; and that 1796 baptism had among its godparents Christen Christensen Lien and Sofie Christensdatter Lien, who fit as this man (or a son of the same name) and a daughter (inference). No other Mali or Magli daughter of a Christen was found in the Selbu baptisms from Ascension 1769 to spring 1770. ¶2

His wife Brynild Olsdatter is identified as Brynhild born 1744 at Slindan, daughter of Ola Jornsen (Probable). The bygdebok, Selbu i fortid og naatid vol. 7 p. 116, calls him Kristen Kristensen, her first husband. He was dead by 1 February 1801, when she was in her second marriage to Jon Pederssen at Lien (inference from the census; his burial has not been found). Daughters Sofie (about 1780) and Brynil (about 1784) were with her in 1801, so he was probably alive about 1783 (inference). ¶5

Mother, Probable: Mali Ingebrigtsdatter of Store Evjen. The 1762 extra tax, Store Evjen no. 738 (canvas 141), lists 'Høvar Olsen, Mali Ingebrigtsdatter' with sons Mogens (married), Christen and Ingebrigt. Ten years later, in his father-in-law's probate (24757 fol. 530, 17 Oct 1772), he and Ingebrigt appear together as 'Christen Christensen Stor Efjen' and 'Ingebrigt Christensen Stor Efjen', husbands of the sisters Brønnille and Marithe Olsdatter. The third son, Mogens, married to Johanne Nielsdatter in 1762, is likewise 'Mogens Christensen' (16311 c89, 1772; cautioner with Engelbrecht Christensen of Store Evjen in 1789 and 1794). Their patronymic shows that their father was a Christen, not Håvard, so they were Mali's sons by a first marriage; Mali was already Håvard Olsen's wife in 1744 (24753 fol. 93b). His daughter Mali (b. 1769) carries her name. Father: a Christen of Store Evjen, d. between about 1735 and 1744 (not entered). ¶9

Pedigree collapse: through his mother he is a grandson of Ingebrigt Olsen Evjen (d. 1744), who is already an ancestor in this tree through Bjørn Ingebrigtsen and Ingebrigt Bjørnsen of Store Evjen. ¶10

Life story

Possible identity of his wife, not tested: the Slindan farm history (Selbu i fortid og naatid vol. 7 p. 116) has a Brynhild Olsdatter born 1744 at Slindan (see her page) whose first husband is not named there. Nothing yet connects her to this Brynild Olsdatter, and no link is made. ¶4

Baptism of his daughter: on the 8th Sunday after Trinity 1769 (16 July 1769) 'Mali' was baptised in Selbu, 'Par: Christ. Christ: Lien, Brynild Old:' - parents Christen Christensen of Lien and Brynild Olsdatter; godparents (Test:) Hovar Effjen, Baro Lien, Ragnild Stien[?], Marit Stor-Effjen and an Ingeborg whose farm is cut off at the margin. The date is computed from Easter 26 March 1769. ¶1

In the probate after his father-in-law Ole Jørgensen Slinde, 17 Oct 1772 (24757 fol. 530), he is 'Christen Christensen Stor Efjen', husband of Brønnille Olsdatter. So he lived at Stor-Evjen in 1772 and moved to Lien later (he is at Lien in 1769 and at his death in 1789, so the family may have moved more than once). Her sister Marithe Olsdatter was married to 'Ingebrigt Christensen Stor Efjen'. That two Christensens held Stor-Evjen together suggests, but does not prove, that they were brothers and that their father, a Christen, was at Stor-Evjen. ¶8a

Probable burial, Misericordias Sunday (3 May) 1789: 'Begravne 1. Christen Christens: Lien død 21 April 54 a. gl.' - died 21 Apr 1789 aged 54 (first digit possibly 6), so born about 1735 (16311 canvas 132). He and Brynild Olsdatter were still baptising children in 1771 and Oct 1780 (c47), which fits a man born about 1735 rather than 1710. The 'Christen Christensen Lien' who stood godfather in 1800 is then a son of the same name. ¶7

Alive on 5 Feb 1800: first godfather 'Christen Christensen Lien' at the baptism of Ole, son of Ole Olsen Kiøsnæsnæsset and Ingebor Pedersdatter (Selbu 16311 c252). In spring 1799 'Christen Christensens hustru af Lien' was churched (c239), which may point to a younger man of the same name (a son?) rather than the frontier Christen; not resolved. ¶6

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Why Probable and not Confirmed: none of Magli's own adult records names the farm Lien - the 1801 census and her daughter's 1796 baptism call her only Magli Christensdatter - and the search for other candidate Malis covered only Ascension 1769 to Cantate 1770. ¶3a

Upgrade: his probate, Stjør- og Verdal Skifteprotokoll 10 (Digitalarkivet 24763), p. 149, view 163 right (found through the volume's handwritten register, canvas 3, 'Christen Christensen Lien i Salb.', where the page reads 179 or 149). It opens 'Anno 1789 den 23de May' on the farm Lien in Selbo, after the late Christen Christensen, between the widow Brønnille Olsdatter and the children of their marriage: '1. Sønnen Christen Christensen 18 aar gl., 2. Datteren Mali Christensdatter 20 aar gl., 3. Datteren Marithe Christensdatter, 4. Datteren Sophie Christensdatter' and '6. Datteren Brønnille' [the ages of the younger daughters and child no. 5 were not read]. Mali, 20 in May 1789, is the Mali baptised 16 Jul 1769 to Christen Christensen Lien and Brynild Olsdatter, who married Erich Torstensen Uglem in June 1792 as 'Pigen Mali Christensd. Lien' (16311 c146) and was Magli Christensdatter, 32, his wife at Uglan in 1801. A primary record now names her as his daughter; Confirmed. ¶11

Open questions and the next record to read 2

What would confirm it: her marriage to Erik Torstensen (about 1792-1793, Selbu ministerialbok 1757-1801, source 16311) if it calls her 'of Lien', or a probate (skifte) after Christen Christensen Lien naming her among his heirs; a wider search of 1765-1772 baptisms for a rival Mali Christensdatter would also help. ¶3b

Next: a probate for a Christen at Stor-Evjen (the typed registers of 24753-24758 list none under Selbu) and the 1762 extra tax for Stor-Evjen. ¶8b
