About the family

Wife of Ole Larsen of Griner, Gran parish; named as mother at the baptism of their daughter Eli at Gran on 5 Mar 1741 (read on the scan). Probable as the mother of Eli Olsdatter of Griner: see the note on Eli. Her birth, parents and death have not been searched. ¶1

Parents not decided: no Ole other than her husband is recorded at Griner in the Gran index 1700-1760, and no baptism candidate has been identified; her parents remain unknown. ¶4

Life story

Second child: mother of Ole, baptised at Gran on 7 May 1744, son of Ole Larsen of Griner. ¶3

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 alternative set aside · 1 search with no result

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Namesake: not the same woman as Kirsti Olsdatter (b. 1820, Stjørdal), another line's ancestor. ¶2

Searches that found nothing 1

No marriage to Ole Larsen in the Gran marriage scans for 1739-1740 (DA 9340 c168-169). A 'Kirsti Olsdatter Daug[?]' married Gunder Andersen (betrothed 4 Sep 1740); she is not this Kirsti. ¶5
