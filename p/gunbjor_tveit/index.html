Life story

An elder sister, also named Gundbjør, was born 10 Mar 1826 and buried 1 Jul 1826. ¶3

Born 26 Jun 1827 and baptized 8 Jul 1827 in Treungen, daughter of Orm Egildsen and Ingebor Thoresdatter of Tvedt. Find a Grave gives 24 Jun 1827; both church-book copies index the date as 26 Jun. ¶2

Married Thorof in 1849 (Find a Grave). ¶1

Her 1849 marriage record gives her as b. ~1827 at Tvedt, daughter of Orm Egiltsen. Her children's baptisms list her variously as Ormsdatter, 'Onnesdatter' and 'Osmundsdatter'. ¶4

According to Find a Grave, her brother Torof Ormsen Tvedt married Thoruf's sister Turi Osmundsdatter, and the two couples emigrated together in 1861. ¶6

Emigration: the Treungen out-migration list (Afgangs-Lister) for 1861, rows 9-13, records 'Thoruf Osmundsen Heimdal og Hustru Gunbjør Ormsdatter og deres Børn Osmund, Ingeborg, Anne', ages 36, 34½, 10, 7½ and 5½. He is listed as Gmd (farmer), the destination is America (ditto), and the entry is dated 7/4 61, which is 7 April 1861. Find a Grave's '7 Jul 1861' is evidently the same date read month-first. Others on the 1861 page who left the same spring for America were Rolf [O]smundsen[?] Tvedt, 23 (attest 6/4 61), Halvor Halvorsen, Tellef Guttormsen and John Holgersen[?] Mone with family. Gunbjør's brother Thoruf Ormsen Tvedt and Thoruf's sister Turi Osmundsdatter are not in this group. They are on the 1862 list (rows 43-46, dated 15/4 62), so they followed a year later rather than travelling together. ¶7
