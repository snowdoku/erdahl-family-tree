Life story

Land patent, 1889: he received a United States land patent on 18 MAY 1889, General Land Office document number 9585, accession STA-SD1630__.078, for the SOUTH HALF OF THE NORTH-WEST QUARTER, LOT 3, AND THE NORTH-EAST QUARTER OF THE NORTH-WEST QUARTER OF SECTION 2, TOWNSHIP 117 NORTH, RANGE 48 WEST OF THE 5TH PRINCIPAL MERIDIAN, DEUEL COUNTY, SOUTH DAKOTA. He is the only Slind patentee in Deuel County. ¶1

His land, T117N R48W section 2, is in northern Deuel County. Townships 117 North of ranges 46 West (Mehurin, Minnesota), 47 West (Peder Paulsen Eidem's 1898 Deuel County patent) and 48 West (this one) are three congressional townships in a row along one line, and the Selbu colony held land in all three. ¶3

The records that would settle the parentage: the Deuel County probate of Lars Olsen Slind at Clear Lake, South Dakota, whose heir list would name his children; or a baptism at Slinden in the Selbu parish register for a Lars Olsen born about 1845-55. ¶4

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the identification

The case for this identification 1

Why he is in this tree: his patronymic is OLSEN, which is the patronymic of the whole Slind line in this family - Gunder Olsen Slind (b. 1844), father of Oline, was a son of Ole Gundersen Slind of Slinden in Selbu. Lars Olsen Slind is therefore a strong candidate to be a brother of Gunder Olsen Slind. Separately, Elias LARSEN Slind (b. abt 1881, d. Mar 1965), who is buried at Zoar Lutheran Cemetery in this same county, carries the patronymic of a son of a Lars, and Lars Olsen Slind is very probably his father. Both links are rated Probable: the patent proves the man, the place and the date and nothing more. No parent or child link has been asserted in the data. ¶2
