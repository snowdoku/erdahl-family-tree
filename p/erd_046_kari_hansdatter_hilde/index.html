Life story

Aaland 1932: Aaland gives the wife of Guttorm Olsen of Hilde (bruk nr. 4) as 'Kari (Hansd.)'. The brackets mean the patronymic is Aaland's own inference. She owned bruk nr. 2 at Lunde in Stryn. No primary record read names her. Nothing shows whether she, or an earlier wife, was the mother of the sons Hans (born about 1685), Anders (about 1695) and Rasmus (about 1698). So her link as Anders's mother is Speculative. A probate after her, or after Guttorm, would settle it; none survives in the indexed Nordfjord protocols. ¶1

How sure are we?

Speculative. A plausible candidate only. Treat this link as a question, not a fact.
