About the family

Father of Johann Christoph Lindenmaier (see his page). ¶1a

Family (index; identification Probable): Hanß Christoph Lindenmeier x Anna Maria baptised a son Hanß Christoph at Bietigheim on 26 Jun 1671 (record 7267002), probably the 1696 groom. 'Herr Joh Christoph Lindenmaÿer' married Maria at Bietigheim on 19 Aug 1694 (record 7269987; bride Maria Löffler, daughter of Jacob) and 'Herr Christoph Lindenmaÿer' was buried there 16 Dec 1694 (record 7275242); these may be this man as a widower, remarried and dying the same year, but that is not proved. A Justina Barbara Lindenmaÿer, daughter of Joh Christoph, married Herr Heinrich at Bietigheim on 9 Sep 1694 (record 907269985), possibly a daughter. Wife Anna Maria not entered (link to the 1696 groom only by the 1671 baptism). ¶2

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 3

Graded on that link: the groom row of the son's own marriage at Bietigheim on 1 Dec 1696 reads 'Joh Christoph Lindenmaier', relatives 'Johann Christoph, Agnes Johanna' (father, then bride; record 7270021, image 1184899-00367). Ancestry 61023 index rows do not label roles (BRIEF audit 5 rule 3), so this rests at Probable until the image or a labelled FamilySearch twin is read; images not seen. ¶1b

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed, graded on his link to his son Johann Christoph Lindenmaier (see his page). FamilySearch, 'Deutschland, ausgewählte evangelische Kirchenbücher 1500-1971' (= 'Germany, Lutheran Baptisms, Marriages, and Burials, 1500-1971'), QPGP-8VBK (Bietigheim, Taufen, Heiraten u Tote 1586-1822): marriage 1 Dec 1696, groom 'Joh Christoph Lindenmaier', field Father = 'Johann Christoph Lindenmaier', Wife 'Agnes Johanna Jamschauer'. The groom is the tree's Johann Christoph through Nicolaus's labelled baptism of 1704 (parents Johann Christoff x Agnes Johanna, V4LM-GV4). Read signed in, 2 Nov 2026; labelled index fields, image not seen (partner-only). ¶3

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed, graded on his link to his son Johann Christoph Lindenmaier (see his page). Naming record: the 1696 Bietigheim marriage image calls the groom 'Joh. Christoph Lindenmaier, Kupferschmidt, H. Johann Christoph Lindenmaiers, gewesenen [...] und Burgermeisters allhier hinterlassener ehelicher Sohn' (so this man was dead by 1696 and had been Burgermeister). Continuity to the man in the tree (the father of Nicolaus named in 1737): the 1696 groom is a coppersmith marrying Agnes Johanna; the 1704 baptism shows Johann Christoph Lindenmeyer, coppersmith, with wife Agnes Johanna; the 1737 marriage names Nicolaus's father as Johann Christoph, surveyor and coppersmith at Bietigheim (the surveyor is at Bietigheim in 1721 and 1731); and Agnes Johanna dies in 1748 as the coppersmith's widow. Same name, same trade and same wife at each step (a pair match, audit 8 rule 3 and audit 10 point 3), with gaps of 8 years or less to the surveyor records, and no other coppersmith Johann Christoph named. This answers the Audit 10 test record ('the 1737 image's description of the father'). Image read signed in on FamilySearch, 2 Nov 2026. ¶5

Corrections 2

Correction: Audit 10 (2 Nov 2026): lowered from Confirmed to Probable, graded on his link to his son Johann Christoph Lindenmaier (see his page). The labelled 1696 marriage QPGP-8VBK names him as father of the groom Joh Christoph, husband of Agnes Johanna. But the tree's Johann Christoph is the father of the 1737 groom Nicolaus ('Hn Johann Christoph'), and the 1696 groom is identified with that man only through the baptism of a Nicolaus in 1704 to Johann Christoff and Agnes Johanna. As explained on Agnes Johanna Jenisch, the step from the 1704 child to the 1737 groom crosses 33 years by name, the father's forename and a burial age, while other men named Johann Christoph Lindenmaier lived at Bietigheim at the same time. Identifying the child across a gap belongs to the parent's grade (audit 4), so this link is Probable. ¶4a

Correction to the reading: the 1696 entry gives the groom's father as Burgermeister ('gewesenen... und Burgermeisters'), deceased by 1696. That fits the elder Johann Christoph who died in Dec 1694. The Correction note of (Audit 10) lowering him to Probable is superseded. ¶6
