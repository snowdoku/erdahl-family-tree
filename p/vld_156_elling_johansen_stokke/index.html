Life story

1734: in his father-in-law Povel Olsen's probate at Stokke, 8 Dec 1734 (24640 fol. 988, view 500), he appears as 'Elling Johansen Bøge[?]' (card 77795 v3789: 'Bogen'), husband of the daughter Anne Povelsdatter. The byname suggests he came from a farm Bøge/Bogen. The Nordmøre probate card index (77795) has a Bogen in Bremsnes (card v315: Joen Gullichsen d. 1744, children Ole, Marit, Anne, Beritte, Giertrud; not his family) but no Johan at Stokke 1677-1744 (cards v3777-3793 all read). So his father Johan is still unidentified; next: cards for Bøe/Bøgge farms in Kvernes/Gjemnes and the Kvernes church book before 1726 for his marriage to Anne Povelsdatter (about 1724-26). ¶5

Bygdebok account: Gjemnes bind 9, Stokke, canvases 376-377: 'Brukar 6: Elling Johanson, 1734-1751. Elling f. ca. 1698... på [?], d. 1751. Gift 1723 [med] Ane Paulsdotter Stokke f. 1701, d. 1779.' Children: Paul 1726 (brukar 8), Johan 1732, Kirsti 1734, Synnøve 1736 (m. 1762 widower Ole Jonson Røvik), Ane 1739, a son 1740, and Ole 1743. His farm of origin fell in a gap of the rebuilt text (none of Bøgge, Bøge, Bogge, Bugge or Boge matched). ¶7a

Probate found: Nordmøre skifteprotokoll 24644 (1750-55), fol. 74a (view 83, right page), No. 100: 'Anno 1751 den 11te Novemb.' a registration was held 'paa gaarden Stocke' in Gimnes tinglag after the deceased 'Elling Johansen', whose widow was 'Anne Povelsdatter'; the children are listed as the son 'Povel Ellingsen' (age read as 25), 'Johan Ellingsen' (about 20), 'Arne Ellingsen', 'Ole Ellingsen', and the daughters 'Christie Ellingsdatter' and '[Synnøve] Ellingsdatter' (ages after the first two only partly legible). The typed register of the volume lists it as 'Elling Johansen Stoche i Gjemnes 74a'. ¶2

Farmer at Stokke, Gjemnes tinglag, Kvernes parish, Nordmøre. Died before 11 Nov 1751, when his estate was registered. ¶1

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Graded on his link to his son Paul (Poul) Ellingsen Stokke: Confirmed, because the probate names Povel Ellingsen as his son. Continuity, shown record by record with no rival: (1) the 1751 son Povel Ellingsen of Stokke is the only Povel/Paul Ellingsen at Stokke or in Kvernes in the eighteenth century (70 index hits, all nineteenth century); (2) in the 1762 extra-tax list for Stokke (source 47939, view 56) 'Paul Ellingsen og hustru' head a household whose 'Børn over 12 aar' column holds 'Arne Ellingsen', 'Ole Ellingsen' and '[?] Ellingsdatter', with 'Enken Ane' (the widow Ane) at the same line in the column for others living in the house: the same siblings and widowed mother as in 1751, eleven years later on the same farm; (3) the widow 'Ane Paulsdatter' of Stokke stood godmother beside Paul in 1764 and 1765 (16076 views 56 and 59); (4) Paul's own probates name 'Farbroderen Johan Ellingsen' (1763, 24647 p. 735) and 'Farbroderen Ole Ellingsen Stoche' (1779, 24650 fol. 365a) as his children's father's brothers, matching the 1751 sons Johan and Ole. Paul's son Johan/Elling naming also fits (son Elling b. 1767 named for this grandfather). ¶3

Searches that found nothing 1

Searches: (1) The Kvernes church books before Easter 1737 burned with the parsonage on 17 Apr 1737 (pastor Hans Ston's note, Kvernes 16075 view 1, p. 2), so his marriage to Anne Povelsdatter (about 1724-26) cannot be found in the parish register; the reconstructed marriage list 1731-36 and the running list 1737-49 (16075 views 1-3) were read and do not contain it. (2) The 1701 manntall for Gjemnes (Census (manntall) 1701, no. 11: Nordmøre fogderi and Romsdal fogderi, pp. 61-62) has no Elling at Stokke, Grønset, Høgset or Overland. The only Elling Johansen in the Nordmøre 1701 list young enough to be him is 'Elling Johans.', b. 1700, a son at Slatlem (p. 118, not in Gjemnes); not linked. (3) No Bøge/Bøgge/Bogen farm appears in the 1701 Nordmøre index. His origin and his father Johan stay unidentified. ¶6

Open questions and the next record to read 3

His own parents are not yet found. The patronymic makes his father a Johan; his son Johan Ellingsen (b. about 1731) fits a naming after him. ¶4a

Next: Nordmøre probate registers 24642 (1741-46) and 24643 (1746-50) and the probate index cards 1668-1746 (source 77795) for a Johan at Stokke; the 1701 manntall for Kvernes (not indexed) would show a boy Elling Johansen at Stokke. ¶4b
