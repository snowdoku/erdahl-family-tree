About the family

Children: her first child Elen was baptised 22 March 1778 in Selbu, 'Par: Niels O. Langli Guri Olsd.' (source 16311, canvas 87, right page), and she was churched on Maundy Thursday 1778. Elen Nielsdatter, 23, is in the 1801 household at Langlie. ¶7

Parents proposed at SPECULATIVE only: Ole Haftorsen and Gunild Baardsdatter of Uglem. SPECULATIVE (orchestrator, line 8): Guri Olsdatter (b. about 1756) married Niels Olsen Langlie in 1777; her baptism falls in the 1747-56 register gap. The only Guri Olsdatter confirmed at the right age with a farm is 'Guri Olsd. Aunglum' (Uglem) in 1772 (another Guri Olsdatter, of Solum, was confirmed in 1775). In the 1762 extra tax list the only Uglem household headed by an Ole is matr. 702: Ole Haftorssen and Gunild Baardsdatter, with no children over 12 (Guri would have been about 6, so not listed). The chain rests on (a) the 1772 Guri being ours and (b) her being a daughter of the household rather than a servant there; neither is proved, and none of Guri's children carries the names Haftor, Gunild or Baard. Treat as a lead only. ¶13

Life story

Bygdebok lead: directly under Nils Olsen's entry on vol. 3 p. 718 the boxes '1756', '1832' sit on a line with 'Pigarden' - suggesting the book gives Guri's origin as Pigarden (a Langli holding; a 'Kari, 1759, Olsen, Pigarden' appears on p. 715, possibly her sister). This is an ALTERNATIVE to the Speculative Uglem parents (Ole Haftorsen x Gunild Baardsdatter, from her 1772 confirmation 'Aunglum'); 'Haftorsen', 'Gunhild' and 'Bårdsdt.' have no boxes on pp. 714-727. Settling it needs the page text (Norwegian IP or library) - see S0081. ¶15

Marriage: Selbu church, 1 June 1777, to Niels Olsen of Langlie: 'Copul. Niels Ols. Langlie J. Guri Olsd.' (married: Niels Olsen Langlie and Guri Olsdatter; the abbreviation 'J.' between the names, used in every pairing on this page, is left unexpanded). The entry gives no age, farm of origin or sponsors for her. Read from the scan (source 16311, canvas 82, left page). ¶6

Baptism of her son Jon: 'Döbt. Jon. Par: Niels Ols. Langlie Guri Olsd.', Selbu, Dom. 1 p. Trin. 1785 (29 May 1785); she was churched 26 June 1785 ('Niels Ols. Langl. Hust.'). Selbu ministerialbok 1757-1801, read from the scan. ¶11

1801 census: person 002 at rural residence 0026 Langlie, Selbu - 'Guri Olsdatter', aged 45, 'Hans kone', both in their first marriage, wife of Niels Olssen the farmer of Langlie, with seven children in the house. ¶1

Probate (husband's): no widow's share is shown in the share list of the skifte after her husband Niels Olsen Langlie, closed 21 June 1816 (skifteprotokoll 10b, fol. 713), though she lived until 1832; the reason is not given on that page. ¶12

Silver tax: 'Guri Olsdat', Langlie, widow 'Med Sønner' (with her sons), 4 ort 102 sk, round of 31 Aug 1816; in the 1821 round assessed jointly with her son Ole. (the 1816 silver tax was levied on property holders in several rounds, 1816-1821.) ¶14

Death and burial: the Selbu register for 1824-1842 (burials 1832, page 378, no. 9) enters 'Gaardmands Enke Gurru Olsd[atte]r Langlie', aged 77, residence Langlie, died 12 March and buried 18 March 1832. Gaardmands enke means a farmer's widow. The online index lists her as 'Guru Olsdtr', with no farm or status, so a search for the forename Guri does not find her. Read from the scanned page. ¶3

1825 census: residence 0047* Langlie, household 01, person 001 - 'Guri Olsd.', 70, 'Gmd. Enke' (gaardmand's widow), first in a household of 16: her married son Ole Nilsen (46) with his wife Brynhild Johnsdatter (40) and eight children, and her son John Nilsen (42, 'Hmd.', husmand or cottar) with his wife Marit Johnsdatter (42) and three children. Her age of 70 gives a birth year of about 1755, consistent with 45 in 1801 and 77 at her burial in 1832. The census does not state John's relationship to her; it gives his status ('Hmd.') in that column instead. ¶10

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Rated Probable on the same grounds as her husband: the link to her son Jon Nielssen is a census household plus the godparent pattern of 1818, not a document that states the parentage. ¶2

Confidence raised from Probable to Confirmed: the 1801 census of Langlie lists 'Jon Nielssen', 16, as one of 'Deres boern' (their children) of Niels Olssen and his wife Guri Olsdatter, and in 1825 she, as a farmer's widow, still kept house with John Nilsen and with her son Ole Nilsen, whom the 1825 list calls 'Gift soen' (married son). The identification of the 1801 Jon with the later John Nielsen Langlie is set out on her husband's record; no record contradicts it. ¶9

The case for this identification 1

Identification of the 1832 burial: her age of 77 gives a birth year of about 1755, against 45 in the 1801 census (about 1756). The farm is Langlie, and she is a farmer's widow, which fits the widow of Niels Olsen, gaardmand of Langlie, who was buried in 1815. Rejected: 'Guri Olsdatter', aged 82, buried 1 September 1839, whose scanned entry (page 398, no. 110) reads 'Guri Olsdatter Overviig... 82... Overviig', of the farm Overvik; and 'Guri Ols Datt', aged 78 years 7 months, buried 27 September 1829 (1816-1831 register, page 289, no. 31), whose scanned entry gives a farm that is not Langlie. As with her husband, the burial identifies the 1801 farmer's wife of Langlie, but it does not prove that she was John Nielsen's mother. The rating stays Probable, and the same marriage or probate record would settle it. ¶4

Searches that found nothing 1

Baptism not found - no register survives: Selbu has no surviving baptism register for 1747-1756. Book 16310 ends in 1737, book 16315 covers only 1740-1746, and book 16311 begins on 1 January 1757 (who also read every baptism from 1 Jan 1757 to 25 Mar 1758 without finding her). Born about 1755-1756 by her ages in 1801 (45) and at burial in 1832 (77), she falls in that gap, so her own baptism, and with it the names of her parents, is lost. What remains: the confirmation lists in book 16311 (about 1764-1775, now being read - they usually give the father or the farm), and probate (skifte) - the Strinda og Selbu probate protocols cover Selbu only from 1797, so a skifte after her husband's death in 1815 or hers in 1832 could name heirs but not the parents. ¶8

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Lead, not read: Selbu bygdebok vol. 3, p. 718 (word-box profile, not read) appears to give her as born 1756 and died 1832, possibly with the place word 'Pigarden'. The death year agrees with this register entry. ¶5
