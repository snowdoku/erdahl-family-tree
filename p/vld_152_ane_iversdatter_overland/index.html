About the family

Wife of Sivert Nielsen of Overland in 1767, when she stood godmother to Elling, son of Paul Ellingsen Stokke and Ane Sivertsdatter (16076 p. 122). ¶1

Her family (inferred): her brothers and sisters were evidently the Søvig Iversens: Knud, Ole (betrothed Dom. 3 Adv. 1739 to Ane Jensdatter Astad; his son Iver baptised Feb 1742), Elling, Iver (confirmed 1739, so born about 1723), Peder Iversen Søvig and Marrit Iversdatter Søvigen. Her father was therefore an Iver at Søvig. In the 1701 manntall (no. 11, Nordmøre, Søeuig) the only Iver is 'Ifuer Jons.' born about 1665, opsidder, with son Joen Ifuers. born about 1695, next to Joen Knuds. born about 1621, probably his father. Iver Pedersen Søvig, whose wife Ragnhild was churched on Good Friday 1740 (16075 view 14) and stood godmother to Ane in 1738, is not in the 1701 list and so was probably too young to be her father. The widow Synnøve Ellingsdatter Søvig, buried Trinity Sunday 1741 aged 71 (16075 view 19), is a possible mother (her son Elling would carry her father's name), but nothing names her children. Leads only; no parent added. ¶5

Father added: Iver Jonsen Søvig, Probable (bygdebok child list, the 1701 manntall, and godparents who were her siblings in 1738); the reasoning is on his record. ¶9

Life story

No Søvig (Gjemnes) cards exist in the Nordmøre probate card index 77795 (1668-1746): the S-ø section runs Söndalen (view 4051), Sörvik Bremsnes (4053), then T (checked views 3501-3591 and 4001-4099). A probate after Iver Jonsen Søvig, if any, would be after 1746 (24643 onward). ¶6

Bygdebok account: Gards- og ættesoge for Gjemnes, bind 10. Under Søvik, brukar 5 Iver Jonson (canvases 351-352), child no. 9 is 'Ane f. 1707, d. 1776, g. 1730 [med] Sivert Nilsson Øverland, f. ca. 1706'. Under Øverland, brukar 5 (canvas 425): 'Sivert Nilsson [var] brukar frå 1734 til 1760. Han [var] fødd ca. 1706, død 1775. Gift [i] 1730 [med] Ane Iversdotter Søvik, [f.] 1707, [d.] 1776.' It lists their children as 'Ole, fødd 1735', '2. Ane, fødd 1738, død 1784... Gift 1764 [med] enkemann Paul Ellingson Stokke' and '3. Nils, fødd 1741, død 1807', with no earlier wife of Sivert. Her own grade is NOT raised. The audit 5 objection was the 24-year gap from the 1738 baptism to Ane Sivertsdatter in 1762, and a possible earlier wife of Sivert. A marriage in 1730 would put Ole (b. 1735) and Ane (b. 1738) in one marriage, but this is the bygdebok's statement, not a record we have read. Kvernes church books before Easter 1737 burned, and the reconstructed marriage list 1731-36 (16075 views 1-3) has no Sivert. So the 1730 date must come from another source, perhaps a tingbok or the bishop's records. ¶8a

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

4 on the grade · 2 alternatives set aside · 2 open questions

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 4

Speculative mother of Ane Sivertsdatter: she was Sivert Nielsen's wife in 1767, but nothing shows whether she was his only wife or a later one, so she may be Ane Sivertsdatter's stepmother. Do not confuse her with Ane Iversdatter, wife of Johannes Tostensen Hoksæt, who appears in the same book in 1765 and 1769. Graded on her link to Ane Sivertsdatter. ¶2

Upgrade: Speculative to Probable, graded on her link to Ane Sivertsdatter. Sivert (Siur) Nielsen of Overland's wife is named Ane at every mention from Feb 1742 to 1767: 'Siur Øverlands hustru Ane' (16075 view 21, 20 Feb 1742), mother Ane at the baptisms of Iver (Judica 1744, view 31) and Tørris (4 p. Pascha 1745, view 36), churching of 'Siwert Øverlands hustru Ane' (views 32 and 37), 'Sivert Øverlands hustru Ane' godmother in Jan 1747 (view 44), and Ane Iversdatter, wife of Sivert Nielsen Overland, in 1767 (16076 p. 122). The son born 1744 was named Iver, as the custom expects for the mother's father, and two of his godparents were Knud Iversen Søvig and Ole Iversen Søvig, probably her brothers. So the Ane of 1742-47 is very probably the Ane Iversdatter of 1767, and she was Sivert's wife through the years when Ane Sivertsdatter (over 12 in 1762, unmarried in 1764) was born. ¶3a

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed, graded on her link to her daughter Ane Sivertsdatter. Record: Kvernes 16075 view 7, p. 8, Dom. 16 p. Trin. (21 Sep) 1738: 'Døbt Sivert Øverlands b[arn]: Ane. Mod[er]: Ane', the baptism of Ane, daughter of Sivert of Overland and his wife Ane. This is the record her earlier notes named as missing. Continuity (all Kvernes 16075, same farm Overland, same husband Sivert Nielsen, gaps of 1 to 3 years): mother Ane at the baptisms of Ane (21 Sep 1738, view 7) and Niels (23 Apr 1741, view 18), churchings of 'Sivert Øverlands hustru Ane' 26 Oct 1738 and Whit Sunday 1741; 'Siur Øverlands hustru Ane' Feb 1742 (view 21); mother Ane of Iver (1744) and Tørris (1745) and churchings 1744-45; 'Sivert Øverlands hustru Ane' Jan 1747 (view 44); then the patronymic is written out: mother 'Ane Iversd.' at the baptism of Helge, Whit Sunday 1748 (view 52), and churching of 'Sivert Øverlands gt. Ane Iversd.' Dom. 5 p. Trin. 1748 (view 53); and 'Ane Iversdatter, wife of Sivert Nielsen Overland' in 1767 (16076 p. 122). The Søvig Iversen siblings stand godparents throughout (Elling Iversen and Marrit Iversdatter Søvigen 1738; Iver Iversen Søvig and Ole Iversen Søvig's wife 1741; Knud and Ole Iversen Søvig 1744; Peder Iversen Søvig 1748). ¶4a

Audit 5 (1 Nov 2026): lowered from Confirmed to Probable, graded on her link to her daughter Ane Sivertsdatter. The 21 Sep 1738 baptism (Kvernes 16075 view 7, 'Døbt Sivert Øverlands b[arn]: Ane. Mod[er]: Ane') names the relationship. Its mother Ane is followed to Ane Iversdatter through consecutive records on the same farm, 1738 to 1748, and that step is sound. The weak step is the child. The baptised Ane of 1738 is next seen only as 'Ane Sivertsd[atte]r' over 12 in the 1762 extra tax, 24 years later, with no record of her in between. The audit-3 rule makes a gap of 20 years or more with nothing in between Probable, and audit 4 holds that identifying the child in the tree belongs to the parent's grade. The worker's fallback (any Ane born 1738-1750 had this mother) does not cover a birth before 1738. Sivert had a family before then: Ole Sivertsen, very probably his son, was married and heading a household at Overland by 1762 but is not among the 1737-48 baptisms. Sivert's marriage has not been found either, and the earliest Kvernes baptisms, 16075 views 1-4 (c.1731-37), are unread. ¶7a

Alternatives considered and set aside 2

Not Confirmed: Ane Sivertsdatter's own baptism has not been found (16075 read for 1741 to Jan 1748, views 20-50, with no baptism of an Ane to Sivert; 1737-40 and 1748-50 unread), so a birth before Feb 1742 to an earlier wife is not excluded. ¶3b

No rival: Sivert's wife is called Ane at every mention 1738-1767 and no other wife of his is named. The child is followed to Ane Sivertsdatter by the 1762 extra tax (47939 view 57: Sivert Nielsen's children over 12 at Øverland include Ane Sivertsdatter, so born by 1750) and by the 1764 marriage of 'pigen Ane Sivertsd. Øverland' (16076 view 53). No other daughter Ane was baptised to Sivert in 1737-48 (16075 views 4-56 now read), and no burial of a child Ane Sivertsdatter was seen in those years. Even if the 1738 child had died and a later Ane been born by 1750, her mother would still be this Ane Iversdatter, who was Sivert's wife from 1738 to 1767. ¶4b

Open questions and the next record to read 2

Would confirm: Sivert Nielsen's marriage to Ane Iversdatter (16075 views 1-4, or the bride's parish) dated before Ole's birth; or a record of Ane Sivertsdatter between 1738 and 1762 (a confirmation, or service as a godparent with her mother). ¶7b

Next: find the bygdebok's source for the 1730 marriage. Also read the Kvernes burials of 1775-76 (16076) for Sivert and Ane Iversdatter of Øverland, with ages. ¶8b
