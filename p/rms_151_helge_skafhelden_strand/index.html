Life story

Probate after him at Søndre Strand, Valdres card 13725 (Hadeland, Land og Valdres skifteprotokoll 1704-1709, s. 331b, 18-19 and 21 Feb 1707): 'Helge Skafheldsen +, g.m. ? ? +; 1. Thor 15 aar, 2. Jul 13, 3. Olle omtrent 10 aar, 4. Jøran 8½ aar'; gross about 5253 rdr, net about 5084, including 2 huder in Rebne i Slidre. Protocol read (DA 25284 s. 331b, view 336, middle column): 'Skifte Bref passerit paa Søndre Strand udi Svenes Annex i Ourdals Præstegield i Walders, efter afgangne Helge Skafhelden, Aar 1707 d. 18-19-21 February'. He died 'for omtrent 3 Uger siden udi Christiania' (about three weeks before, at Christiania; reading of the place word fairly secure). His heirs are '3de Sønner og j Datter, den Eldste Søn Thor Helgesen 15 aar gl., den anden Søn Jul Helgesen 13 aar, den 3die Søn Ole Helgesen omtrent 10 aar, den yngste og sidste Datteren... Helges datter 8½ aar' (the daughter's forename is hard to read; the card has Jøran). Present: the children's grandfather 'Bestefaderen Tor Rø[s?]land' (side not stated) and 'Børnenes afgangne Moders Søstermand Niels...'. So the children's mother was dead by Feb 1707; her name is not on the card ('g.m. ? ?'). ¶1

'Skafhelden' is written as a byname (the card turns it into a patronymic 'Skafheldsen', which the protocol does not support). It is probably a farm or place name (his son Jul carries it in 1722); his true patronymic is not given. Not identified with any Aurdal farm here. ¶3a

The 'Tor Rø[s?]land' lead is resolved, and supersedes the earlier note that left his side open: the guardianship passage of the 1707 probate (DA 25284 s. 338a, view 343) appoints for the eldest son 'dend dannemand Morfaderen Tor Rølang', so Tor Rølang (Røllang, Ulnes) was the father of Helge's late wife, not Helge's own father. Helge's own parents remain unknown. The same passage confirms the daughter's name as 'Jøran Helgesdatter' and names the other guardians: Ole V[i?]nstad (for Jul), Niels Jensen Frøen and Haagen Siurbrygge (for Ole), Erich Rauden and Knud An[...] (for Jøran). ¶5

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade:, new person entered as Confirmed, graded on his link to his son Tor Helgesen (see his page). The 1707 probate names 'den Eldste Søn Thor Helgesen 15 aar' on Søndre Strand. Continuity: fifteen years later, on the same farm, the 1722 probate after Tor Helgesen (DA 25285 fol. 558b) names 'dend afdøde S: Mands Brødre Giul Helgesen Skafwelden og Ole Hel[gesen]', who are the 1707 siblings Jul (13) and Ole (about 10): a sibling-set match at both ends, with Jul also carrying the byname Skafhelden. No rival Tor Helgesen is known on Sør-Strand. ¶2

Open questions and the next record to read 2

Lead: the grandfather 'Tor Rø[s?]land' present in 1707 may be Helge's father (the eldest son is named Thor) or the children's mother's father; unresolved, not added. ¶3b

Test record: the 1664/66 manntall and the 1690s Aurdal tingbok for a Helge at Skafhelle/Skavhelle or at Søndre Strand; a marriage before 1692 (church books for Aurdal start 1692, DA 9360). Note that the Strand farm also held Tord Erlandsen and Jul Tordsen in 1664-1690; how Helge came to Søndre Strand (by marriage to a daughter of the farm?) is not shown. ¶4
