About the family

Oline Slind's great-grandfather. Named as the father in the Selbu baptism of 26 December 1816 of his son Ole Gundersen, born 10 December 1816, the mother being Beret Olsdatter of Slinden; and as the groom's father, styled 'Gunder Jorgensen Aune', in his son's marriage entry of 24 May 1841. ¶1

Life story

He is identified with the 'Gunder Jorgenssen, 9' entered in the 1801 census of Selbu as one of the children of Jorgen Olssen and Ane Gundersdatter at rural residence 0083 Slinden, household 3. He is the only Gunder Jorgensen on the Slind farms in 1801, and a boy of nine in February 1801 was born about 1792, which fits a man fathering a son in 1816 and giving a daughter in marriage - or rather a son - in 1841. ¶2a

The Selbu church-book transcription for 1801-1815 does not index marriages under his name, so his marriage to Beret Olsdatter - which would have named her father and opened a further line - has not been found in the index. It is in the register. ¶4

When he moved, and how early. He was a cottager's or farmer's son at Slinden when his first two children were baptised - 1814 and 1819, both with the residence given as Slinden - and the Sovlvskatten of 3 March 1821 already enters a 'Gunder Jorgensen, Gaardmand' at Aune. The 1822 baptism gives the residence as Aunet, the 1825 and 1828 ones as Aunet and the 1833 one as 'Stednet' (the same place). So the move from Slind to Aune happened between August 1819 and March 1821, and he went up in the world doing it: from a son on his father's leasehold to a gaardmand of his own. ¶6

The two farm names. He is at Slinden when his son is baptised in 1816 and styled 'Aune' when that son marries in 1841, so either the family moved between Slind and Aune or he held land at both. Both are recorded. ¶3

His marriage is EARLIER than wave 3 had it. The wave-3 patch entered the marriage as BEF 1816 on the strength of the 1816 baptism of his son Ole. The Selbu ministerialbok for 1801-1815 carries an earlier child: 'Anne', baptised the third Sunday after Trinity 1814 at page 207 serial 1, father Gunder Jorgensen of Slinden, mother Berith Olsd. The marriage is therefore BEFORE 1814, most likely 1813 or early 1814. The marriage entry itself is not in the Digitalarkivet index - the Selbu marriage register for 1801-1815 is effectively untranscribed - and must be read on the scan. ¶7

He was alive on 3 May 1847, when the Selbu marriage register names 'Gunder Jorgensen' as the bride's father at the wedding of one of his daughters; he was then about fifty-five. He is 'brudgommens far' - the groom's father - at three weddings, 24 May 1841 (his son Ole), 6 June 1843 and 28 October 1844, so at least three of his sons married in Selbu. His own burial has not been located. ¶10

Death and burial, Probable: 'Gunder Jorgensen', aged 66, died 31 May and buried 6 June 1858, Selbu burial register 1843-1859, p. 400 (index; scan not read). For: the calculated birth year 1792 is his (1801 census age 9; 1825 census 'b. 1792'), and no second Gunder Jorgensen born near 1792 appears in the Selbu indexes. ¶11a

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Rated Probable: the chain is a unique name match in the right place at the right date, not a document that states the relationship. ¶2b

Confidence raised from Probable to Confirmed, 12 Oct 2026. The wave-3 identification chained a nine-year-old boy in the 1801 census to a man styled 'Gunder Jorgensen Aune' in an 1841 marriage entry and rated the chain Probable because no document joined the two. The CENSUS OF 1825 FOR SELBU joins them: 'Gunder Jorgens., b. 1792, Domicile Selbu: Aune, Gmd.' - gaardmand, farmer, at Aune, born 1792. One man born 1792, on the Slind farm as a child in 1801, farming at Aune by 1825 and called 'Gunder Jorgensen Aune' when his sons marry in 1841, 1843 and 1844. There is no second Gunder Jorgensen of that birth year anywhere in the Selbu index. ¶5

Records that disagree 2

A discrepancy I am not smoothing over: the 1825 baptism of Anne names the mother as 'Kirsti Olsd' of Aunet, not Beret Olsdatter, while the 1822, 1828 and 1833 baptisms all name Beret Olsdatter of Aunet. Either the 1825 clerk or the AMF transcriber wrote the wrong given name, or Gunder briefly had a second wife between 1822 and 1828 and then a third - which the continuous run of Beret's own children makes very unlikely. The scan of page 12 of the 1824-1842 register, entry 44, settles it. ¶9

Against: residence and wife are not given, so Aune or Slinden is not shown. Reading the scan for a residence would confirm it. ¶11b

Corrections 1

Correction: death was carried as 'AFT 3 MAY 1847' (alive as a bride's father); the burial index now gives 31 May 1858, at Probable. ¶12
