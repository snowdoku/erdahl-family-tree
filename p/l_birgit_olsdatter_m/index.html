About the family

Wife of Tosten Aslesen at Medgaarden, age 33 in the 1801 census. Named as Ole's mother in his 1805 baptism. ¶1

Her father's name was Ole. Her death has not been identified; several Birgit Olsdatters born 1765-1769 died in Ål between 1822 and 1847. ¶2

Life story

Married Tosten Aslesen of Medgaarden at Ål in 1789 as 'Pigen Birgith Olsd.' (church book p. 170). ¶3

1801 census caution: a second Bergit Olsdatter, aged 34 and in her first marriage, lived on the same farm in 1801 as the wife of Sølve Olsen (50, in his second marriage), household 1 at Medgaarden. Tosten Aslesen's wife is the one aged 33 in household 2. ¶4

Burial candidates (not identified): the indexed Ål burials of a Birgit Olsdatter born 1764-1770 are 19 Jan 1822 (b. 1765), 27 Feb 1822 (b. 1766), 29 Apr 1837 (b. 1765) and 4 Apr 1840 (b. 1766); two more Birgit Olsdatter burials, 24 Sep 1822 and 4 Aug 1827, carry no age. Searches that found nothing 2

Searched (negative): the Ål panteregister p. 140 (Øvre Medgaarden, gl. matr. 132) lists the 1756 auction deed to Asle Knudsen Dybsjorde, the skifter after Asle Knudsen (1773), Kari Tostensdatter (1785) and Knud Aslesen (14 Feb 1789, pantebok 15 fol. 180), and the deed of 17 Jul 1789 from Knud Aslesen's heirs to Tosten Aslesen (pantebok 15 fol. 182). None names Birgit Olsdatter's family. In the 1801 census the lodger Tosten Olsen (36) at Medgaarden was the brother of Sølve Olsen of household 1, not of Birgit. ¶6

Searched (negative): the Medgaarden skiftekort (3917 images 2470-2481) show no skifte after an Ole of Medgaarden naming a daughter Birgit. The 1808 card after Sølve Olsen of Medgaarden (prot. 24 fol. 120a) names his second wife 'Birgith Olsdtr.', the other Bergit Olsdatter of the 1801 census, not Tosten Aslesen's wife. ¶7
