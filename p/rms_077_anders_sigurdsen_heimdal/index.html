About the family

Sister: the 1717 skifte after Gunner Evendsen at Heimdal, Treungen (Øvre Telemark skifteprotokoll no. 3 fol. 276b-277a, DA 25692 view 285, read on the scan) names the widow 'Hæge Sigursdatter' and as her guardian 'hendes Broder tilstedeværende Anders Hiemdal'. Anders Hiemdal is thus Anders Sigurdsen, and Hæge Sigursdatter (b. before 1694; children Sigur 24, Knud 20½, Helje 15, Mari 12, Asloug, Ragnild) was his sister. Gunner's own brothers: Ole Evendsen (farbroder, guardian of Knud) and very likely Gunsten Evensen of nordre Heimdal (skifte 1717, card 6856/477). ¶4

Life story

Farmer at Heimdal in Treungen (annex of Kviteseid), Telemark. On 16 Jul 1725 the court held a skifte (division of property) at Heimdal of all the movable and fixed property of 'de bedagede folk Anders Sigurdsøn og Sigrj Tallevsdatter' (the aged couple), both still living, among their children: Sigur and Evend Anderssønner, both of age, and two unmarried daughters Ingeborg and Guri Andersdøttre (guardians: Tallev Tallevsøn(?) Homme for Ingeborg, and her brother Sigur Andersen for Guri). The land counted was 4 maal with buildings in søndre Heimdal and 3 'maller' in nordre Heimdal. The second son 'Even Anderssøn' received his share in nordre Heimdal (3 maller). The parents kept their maintenance (føderåd) on the farm; Sigur took over. Gross estate 680 rd 3 ort 8 s. (Øvre Telemark skifteprotokoll no. 4, fol. 88a-89a, no. 54; card index DA 6856 image 475.) ¶1

Deed: Pantebok I 3 (DA 10868) no. 504, fol. 139a-b, read on the scan. At the ting held at Prestegaarden for Nissedal tinglag on 4 Mar 1730 was read a deed dated Hiemdahl 11 Oct 1729 in which 'Anders Evendsøn, boende paa gaarden Tvedt i Treungens annex', with the consent of his wife 'Ingeborg Andersdaatter', sells to 'min kiære Svoger Evend Andersøn boende paa Hiemdahl', 'min hustrues Ingeborg Andersdaatters arvelod efter skifte brev passeret 16de July 1725 udi gaarden Nordre Hiemdahl', 1½ maller with buildings, for 66 rd 1 ort 8 s. Witnesses Ole Aarack and Anders Knudsøn Hiemdal. This ties the daughter Ingeborg and the son Evend of the 16 Jul 1725 division by Anders Sigurdsen and Sigri Tallevsdatter to the Evend Andersen living on nordre Heimdal in 1729 (Svoger = brother-in-law), and so bridges 1725 to the Evend Andersen Heimdal of 1733 and later. It also shows that Ingeborg Andersdatter was married to Anders Evendsen of Tvedt by 1729. Graded link unchanged (already Confirmed on his link to Eivind (Evind) Andersen Heimdal); this deed adds a second record that names Evend's sister and her share from the 1725 skifte. ¶5

He is probably the 'Anders S[igurdsen] Hiemdahl' who sat as a lagrettemann (lay assessor) at the probate of Anders Knudsen on søndre Heimdal, 16 Jul 1733 (reading of the patronymic uncertain). ¶2

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Regrade (1 Nov 2026): upgraded to Confirmed as father of Eivind Andersen Heimdal (see his page); graded on his link to Eivind (Evind) Andersen Heimdal. The naming record is the division by the living, aged couple Anders Sigurdsøn and Sigri Tallevsdatter among their children, Heimdal, 16 Jul 1725 (Øvre Telemark skifteprotokoll no. 4, fol. 88a-89a, no. 54; DA 25668 images 90-91; card DA 6856 image 475), which names 'Evend Anderssøn', of age, as their second son and lays out his share, 3 maller in nordre Heimdal. Continuity: the same man is followed at Heimdal by consecutive records: in Jul 1733 'Evend Anderssen Heimdal' is husband of Hage, heir at her mother's probate on nordre Heimdal; 'Evind Hiemdal' buries children at Treungen in 1755 and 1766; and 'Evind Andersen Hiemdal, gl. 65 aar' is buried there on 26 Oct 1766 (born about 1701, so of age in 1725). ¶3a

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

No rival: he is the only Evind Andersen at Heimdal in the records read; Anders Knudsen of søndre Heimdal had no son Evind, and the younger Evind Andersen of Tvedt is a different man on another farm. ¶3b
