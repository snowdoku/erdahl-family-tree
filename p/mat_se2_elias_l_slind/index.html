About the family

Father of the two Slind sisters who married the two Eidem brothers: Kari (b. 7 Sep 1863), who married Peder P. Eidem in 1888, and Beret 'Betsy' (b. 6 May 1872), who married Mikal P. Eidem about 1892-93. Named with his birth year in Selbygbogen (1921) p. 149: 'Elias Larsen Slind (f. 1826)'. ¶1

Life story

He never emigrated. Selbygbogen p. 174 says of his widow: 'Marit Estensd. Slind... was married to Elias Larsen Slind SOM DOEDE I SELBU (who died in Selbu). She came to America in 1907 and lived here with her daughters and died 7 Dec. 1917.' He was therefore dead before 1907. ¶2

A third child of his is named on p. 174: Lars Eliason Slind of Slind (Reiemoen), whose sons Gunder L. Slind (b. 30 Nov 1886) and Gunnerius L. Slind (b. 23 Nov 1890) both emigrated and settled at Madison, Minnesota; Gunder served with the American army in the Argonne in 1918. ¶3

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

Corrections 1

Correction and a warning to the coordinator: the tree already holds an id Elias Larsen Slind, 'Elias Larsen /Slind/', with birth ABT 1881 and death MAR 1965 at Revillo, Grant County, South Dakota. That cannot be this man - a man born about 1881 cannot father daughters born 1863 and 1872, and this Elias died in Selbu. The two records have been conflated somewhere upstream. I have given the Norwegian Elias his own id rather than overwrite the existing one; they should be reconciled, and the 1965 man is most likely a grandson. ¶4
