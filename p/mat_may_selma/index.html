About the family

Elder sister of Mayme O. Eidem, third of the six children of Sivert and Anna (Satter) Eidem, and so a great-great-aunt of David Erdahl. She lived to be 100 and died on 2 November 2004. ¶1

The sixth child of Severt S. Eidem and Anna Olene Satter, and the one wave 3 could not place. Her existence was known only from her sister Myrtle Overdahl's 2013 obituary, which names 'Selma Nelson' among the predeceased siblings; she is absent from the South Dakota birth index under Eidem, and wave 3 concluded her birth had gone unregistered. ¶8

Life story

Birth record: none found. The South Dakota birth index over 100 years has no entry for a Selma (or any girl born about 1902-1905) with father Eidem or mother Satter. It was searched under surname Eidem, Eiden and Eidesen, under mother's maiden name Satter and Sather, and under father Severt Eidem, on 13 Oct 2026. ¶13

Inference, not proved: the missing birth record fits the family's two and a half years in Canada after their 1898 marriage (Anna's 1937 obituary), if that stay fell between Samuel's birth in October 1901 and the 1905 state census, where Selma is aged 2. The absence is weak evidence, though. Her elder sister Bertha (b. 1900) is not in the index either, and Samuel (b. 1901) is there only through a delayed certificate filed in 1957, so births in the family before 1905 may simply never have been registered. Her birthplace column in the 1910, 1920 and 1930 censuses would settle it. ¶14

Censuses: Selma O. Eidem, daughter, b. 1904, in the 1910 federal census of Brule township; Selma Eiden, b. abt 1904, in 1920; 'Selma Olivia Eidem, daughter, b. abt 1904' in 1930, still at home aged 26. The 1930 index supplies her MIDDLE NAME, Olivia, which was not previously known - and it is her mother's own middle name as that enumerator wrote it. ¶6

1905 South Dakota state census: 'Eidem, Salma, 2', card 0031, in Brule Township, Union County, Section 1, T92N R50W — the fourth name in the household after Sivert 45, Anna 33, Bertha 5 and Samuel 4. That places her birth about 1902 or 1903, in exactly the gap between Samuel (October 1901) and Mamie (December 1906) that wave 3 predicted. This is the first record of her found anywhere. ¶9

Named as 'Selma Nelson' among the predeceased siblings in her youngest sister Myrtle's obituary of 2013, and identified with the Find a Grave memorial for 'Selma "Sally" Eidem Nelson, 1903 - 2 Nov 2004, Faith Cemetery, Faith, Meade County, South Dakota', who married Walter R. Nelson (1901-1964) in 1930. The identification is Probable rather than Confirmed because the two records are tied together only by the name Selma, the maiden name Eidem and a South Dakota death at the right time; her stone gives only the year 1903 and her memorial carries no parents. ¶2

Mayme (Eidem) Eidem's sister, known so far only from one line: she is named as 'Selma Nelson' among the five predeceased siblings in Myrtle (Eidem) Overdahl's obituary of October 2013. Nelson is her married surname. She has no entry in the South Dakota birth index under the surname Eidem, which means either that her birth went unregistered - she was probably born between 1902 and 1906, before registration became general in the state - or that she was registered with no given name, as six Eidem children in that index were. ¶4

She later married a man named Nelson and died before October 2013. Her married name and her dates are still unknown. ¶10

Two agents created this woman separately, as Selma Olivia "Sally" Eidem from her sister's 2013 obituary and as mat_sat_selma from the 1905 South Dakota state census. They are one person and the records are merged here; the duplicate was never added to the tree. ¶12

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

Records that disagree 1

Contradiction: the tree has her born ABT 1903, but three federal censuses (1910, 1920, 1930) all point to 1904. I have not changed the date, because the ABT 1903 may rest on a record I have not seen; but if nothing better exists, 1904 is the better estimate. ¶7
