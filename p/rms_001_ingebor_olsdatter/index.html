About the family

Mother of Ulvil, born 25 Nov 1782 at Tvet, Åmli, and wife of Torjus Ormsen (Åmli ministerialbok 1775-1796, image 35). She was churched (introduced) on New Year's Day 1783. ¶1

Mother of Gunsteen (Gunstein), born 20 Jun 1785 at Tvet, wife of Torjus Ormsen; she was churched on the day of the baptism, 17 Jul 1785 (Åmli ministerialbok 1775-1796, image 50). ¶3

Mother of Ole, born 4 Oct 1788 at Tvet; she was churched on the 26th Sunday after Trinity (16 Nov 1788) (same book, image 63 right). ¶5

Parents: the probate of Even Tellefsen, Hushovd in Mykland (18 May-21 Jun 1776), lists among the children of his maternal aunt Ulvild Evensdatter by her second husband Ole Næresen (Tveit), probate at Oland 9 Feb 1761: 'Ingeborg Olsdtr. g.m. Torgius Ormsen Skjeggedal' (Østre Råbyggelag skiftekort, Skifteprotokoll 14a fol. 73, cards 5507-5508). Her sisters were Sigri (wife of Ole Kiddelsen Austenaa) and Astri (wife of Even Wrolsen Oland); her half-brother was Even Michelsen Austenaa. She is presumably the 'Ingri, 9 years' in her father's 1761 probate, so born about 1752. Her daughter Ulvild (1782) bears her mother's name; 'Even Østenaa' (her half-brother) was a godfather to her children. ¶6

Her sister Siri (Sine) Olsdatter, widow of Ole Kiddelsen, died 1 Apr 1795 at Østenaa aged 61 (probate 1795, card 601). Their sister Astri's daughter Anne Evensdatter Oland, 20, burned to death with Ingebor's children Ole and Ragnild at Tvet on 4 Nov 1787. ¶8

Her husband Torgius Ormsen was a widower when he died on 3 Oct 1798, so she died before that date (Åmli ministerialbok 1796-1816, burials 1798 no. 73). No Åmli burial of hers was found from Aug 1789 to Oct 1798. ¶9

Life story

Betrothed in Topdal on 1 January 1776 to Torjus Ormsen of Øvre Skjeggedal; she is entered as 'Ingeborg Olsdatter Østenaae', so she lived at (and probably came from) Østenaa in Tovdal (Åmli ministerialbok 1775-1796, image 89 left). Married 21 Jul 1776 (same book, image 89 right). ¶4

Date note: the 1776 probate card already calls her married to Torgius Ormsen by May-June 1776, before the 21 Jul 1776 marriage date; the card may use 'g.m.' for the betrothal of 1 Jan 1776. ¶7

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the grade · 1 alternative set aside

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade check, graded on her link to Ulvild Torjusdatter (see her page); the grade stays Probable. The Åmli register 1775-1796 (DA 9923, canvas 34, p. 36, 3rd Sunday in Advent 1782, Lille Topdal) reads 'Ulvil' from Tvet, born 25 Nov, 'Parent: Torjus Ormsen og Ingebor Olsdat.', so the baptism does name her as the mother of the infant Ulvil. What is still inferred is that the infant of 1782 at Tvet is the Ulvild Torjusdatter who married Tellef Torsen in 1816. That identification spans 34 years and several farms, resting on the 1801 census (a servant at Tvet, aged 17) and on naming. No record found names her parents at confirmation or marriage. The Åmli confirmation lists (for example 1807, DA 9924 canvas 44) give only farm and age, and the 1816 marriage names no parents. The same reasoning applies to her husband Torjus Ormsen's link to Ulvild Torjusdatter, which is graded Confirmed. The coordinator may want to review that grade. ¶10

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Namesake: not the same woman as Ingebor Olsdatter (line 2), who was a mother in Ål, Hallingdal, in 1813-1826; this Ingebor Olsdatter was a mother at Tvet in Tovdal, Åmli, in 1782. ¶2
