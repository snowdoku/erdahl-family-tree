Life story

Baptism: Baptised 20 Jan 1805 in Gulen parish (Brekke), daughter of Mons Johannessøn and Kari Johannesdatter, both of Asheim (Assem); the mother was churched 15 Feb 1805. Godparents: Johannes Pederssøn, Matthias Lassessøn, Lars Knudssøn, Thor Lassessøn and Mari Olsdatter, all of Asheim. Gulen church book 1798-1808, p. 283, index; the scan was not read. ¶2

Death: 'Ingeborg Monsdatter Asheim', kaarenke (widow on a life-lease) of Indre Oppedal, born 1805 at Asheim, died 15 Feb and was buried 21 Feb 1883 in Brekke (Lavik ministerialbok, Brekke 1882-1914, burials 1883 no. 2; index pg00000000343712). Name, birth year and birthplace Asheim all match her 1805 baptism; she is the only Ingeborg Monsdatter born 1802-1808 in the indexed burials of the Gulen/Lavik area. ¶4

Married Lasse Torkildsen 22 Jun 1828. Her father is named as Mons Johannessøn of Asheim. Mother of Kari (1828). ¶1

Corrections 1

Correction: The birth date moves from 'ABT 1805' to 'BEF 20 JAN 1805', from the baptism. This is the only child of a Mons Johannessøn in the Gulen baptisms 1803-1808, and the father's farm, Asheim, matches the 1828 marriage. The baptism names her mother, Kari Johannesdatter, for the first time; that link is Confirmed by this record. The Ingerborg baptised 30 Aug 1805 at Asheim is a daughter of Ole Johannessøn and Orloug Henriksdatter, not of Mons. ¶3
