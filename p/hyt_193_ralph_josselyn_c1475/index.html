About the family

Parents entered: the 1524 will of John Josselyn of High Roding (PCC 3 Porch) names 'my cousin Rauffe Josselyn, son of John Josselyn late of Sheryng', with his brothers Philip and Geffry; Ralph's own will of 1525 names 'Philippe Josselyn and Geffery Josselyn my brothers' as executors. Graded on John Josselyn. His mother Anne is from the 1634 Visitation of London pedigree only (Speculative). This corrects the earlier note 'His parents are not entered'. He was bailiff of the manor of Great Canfield 1520-1524 ('Mr. Ralph Josselyn, the bailiff', At the Courts of Great Canfield, read as snippets). Grade unchanged. ¶3

Life story

Great Canfield register (FamilySearch Essex index, image not read): 'Dennis Joslyn', with father 'Ralfe Joslyn', married Thomas Browne at Great Canfield 8 Aug. 1541. Ralph of Great Canfield (will 1525) is the only Ralph of the family known there, so Dennis is probably his daughter (not named in the 1525 will abstract). Also indexed there: Thomas Joslyn, son of Jeffery, married Elizabeth Husterly 20/28 Jun. 1540. Collateral leads; not entered. ¶2

Original will seen: Essex Record Office D/ABW 21/3. The 400-pixel online image is just legible enough to check the abstract's key phrases: the life estate to 'Elizabeth my wif', remainders to 'Philippe my son', 'Rauffe my son' and 'Nicholas my son', then 'Agnes & Denyse'; the tenement in Canfield called 'Louedens' to Rauffe my son; and, near the end, executors 'Elizabeth... Philippe Josselyn Geffrey Josselyn my brethern and John Wysman my son in lawe'. A higher-resolution copy (searchroom) is needed for a faithful transcript. ¶4a

Note: his father's link is now Confirmed from the image of PROB 11/22/61 (see John Josselyn): 'my cousyn Rauffe Josselyn sonne of John Josselyn late of Sheryng', with remainders to 'Philip Josselyn brother unto the said Rauffe' and 'Geffrey Josselyn brother unto the said Rauff and Philip'. His own grade (on his link to Ralph Josselyn) is unchanged. ¶5

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

3 on the grade · 1 open question

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 3

Graded on his link to his son Ralph Josselyn. Will of 'Rauffe Josselyn of Moche Canfeld', the last day of May 1525 (Commissary Court of London for Essex and Herts, original will; abstract in NEHGR 71:20-21): to be buried in the church of St. Saviour of Canfield; lands to 'my wife Elizabeth' for life, then freehold to 'Philyppe my son', remainder to 'Rauffe my son', then 'my son Nicholas', then 'my two daughters Agnes and Denyse'; 'To my son Rauffe... my tenement in Canfeild called louedens'; executors Elizabeth his wife, 'Philippe Josselyn and Geffery Josselyn my brothers', and John Wysman his son-in-law. Taxed on goods worth £26 in 1523/4 and 1524/5. French (no. 20) makes him son of John Josselyn of Sheering and Anne. ¶1a

Probable, not Confirmed: the will names a son Ralph, but that this son is the Ralph who French makes the father of John of Fyfield is the reconstruction graded on Ralph Josselyn, and the will was read only as an abstract. ¶1b

Grade unchanged: the open question is whether this son Ralph was John's father (graded on Ralph Josselyn). ¶4b

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Test record: the original will (Essex Record Office, Commissary Court of London for Essex and Herts, 1525). His parents are not entered. ¶1c
