About the family

Children: Henry (b. October 1895 in the 1911 census, or 21 October 1897 by the baptism index; baptised 3 April 1925) married Lottie and lived at Mitchell River / Cardigan North; Lauretta Brooks's diary notes that he 'came home with Moth Aeroplane' in September 1930. Life story

Born January 1870 (1911 census), PEI. In his father's household 1881 and 1891. In 1911 in Kings County with wife Flora A. (b. Oct 1873) and children Henry (1895), Jennet (1896), Nelson (1899), Grace (1902), Thomas (1905), Catherine (1909). ¶1

Death and burial: His gravestone in St Andrew's Presbyterian Cemetery, Cardigan, gives 27 January 1870 to 17 February 1926 (Find a Grave memorial 220358623, with photographs). He lies with his wife Flora Ann MacSwain, who was born in October 1874 and died on 31 March 1941. The memorial's compiler names his parents as Thomas Jr. Hayter and Grace A. Moore. That is a compiled statement, but it agrees with the 1881 and 1891 censuses. ¶2
