About the family

Mother of Guri Olsdatter Høgset per the Gjemnes bygdebok only, graded on that link: Probable. Bind 7, Høgset chapter, canvas 576: 'Foreldra hennar var Ole Halvorson og Anne Sveinsdotter' (exact phrase verified by content search); also 'i 1732 fekk [Ole Toreson] arv etter mor si, Anne Sveinsdotter', so she died about 1732 (date ABT, from the rebuilt text) and was the mother of Ole Toresen, brukar 4 on Høgset c. 1710-31, by an earlier husband Tore. Guri's husband Torsten Iversen took over Høgset from Ole Toresen, his wife's half-brother, in 1731. ¶1

Her parents: the bygdebok says only that her origin 'er usikkert' and that she 'kan ha' had the parents Sven [gap] and Gunhild Olsdotter (canvas 29, line about y=1600; patronymic and farm not recovered). Too weak to add. ¶6a

Life story

The 1701 census of Hiorten (Kvernes) lists her second husband 'Oluf Halfuors.' (32) with 'Sønner' Oluf Tores. (b. 1683) and Suend Tores. (b. 1690), her sons by her first husband Tore Olsen, confirming the bygdebok's account of her two marriages (wives are not named in this census). Her own 1732 probate in 24640 was looked for but not found: views 83-89 and 90-110 hold June-August 1732 sessions in Sundalen and Surendalen (e.g. view 90, 4 Aug 1732, Surendal; view 105, Aug 1732), view 120 a Trondheim-related estate dated Feb 1732, and views 135-155 one long town-style inventory; no Gjemnes session seen. The Gjemnes 1732 session is probably before view 83 (spring 1732) or after view 155. Her parents remain unknown: the bygdebok phrase 'kan ha foreldra Sven [gap] og Gunhild Olsdotter på [gap]' (bind 8, canvas 29) could not be completed (about 200 farm and name words queried with no hit in the gaps). Test record unchanged: her 1732 probate (24640), which may name guardians who were her brothers. ¶7

Death and residence corrected from the Gjemnes bygdebok (bind 8, Hjorten, canvases 29-30): she d. 1731 (probate 1732) and lived on Hjorten from 1705 with Ole Halvorsen, her second husband (m. about 1698). Her first husband was the Hjorten brukar 6 (c.1682-97), [Tore] Olson, son of Ole Halvorsen d. 1679, who d. about 1697; their children: [Ole Toresen] b. about 1683 (later on Høgset, m. a Pedersdotter Høgset), Svein b. about 1690 (d. before 1732), Dorthe (d. 1750; m. 1 Ola Torsteinson, m. 2 Fredrik Olson), Gunhild (m. Knut Toreson), and a daughter m. Ole Olson. Dates ABT, rebuilt text. ¶5
