Life story

Maiden name: Jude (or Judd), from the trust deed of September 1613 between Ralph Josselin senior of Roxwell and John Jude of Radwinter, Essex, which names 'Thomas Josselin, one of the sons of me the aforesaid Ralph Josselin the elder, and Rebecca Jude' as contingent beneficiaries, evidently a settlement before their marriage (R. D. Joslyn, NEHGR 158 (2004):320-340, as reviewed in the Great Migration Newsletter; followed by Zubrinsky 2026, 'REBECCA JUDE (not Marlow)'). The name 'Rebecca Marlowe, born in London, 1592' in Wessler (1961) and earlier popular works (e.g. Genesis of the White Family) gives no source and is rejected by Joslyn. Her parents are not entered: Joslyn calls John Jude of Radwinter only 'a close relative' (Zubrinsky: 'Perhaps John JUDE of Radwinter'). Grade unchanged (Probable, on her link to Elizabeth Joslin): Thomas's will names 'my wife Rebecca Joslin' and 'my daughter Elizabeth Emons' but does not say Rebecca was Elizabeth's mother; a wife Rebecca from about 1613-15 to 1660 with no other wife known makes it likely. ¶2a

Will image read: Thomas Joslin's will as recorded in Middlesex Probate Records vol. 1, p. 74 (FamilySearch image 223) has 'after the death of my wife Rebeccah Joslin' and leaves 'my daughter Elizabeth Emons ten pounds'. The will calls Rebecca his wife but does not say she is the mother of his children; that rests on the 1635 Increase list ('Rebecca his wife' with 'Eliza' 6 among the 'children of the said Tho: Jostlin'). Grade unchanged. ¶3

Radwinter registers: FamilySearch has no pre-1800 Radwinter register (its catalog has only the bishop's transcripts 1800-1866), and there is no Radwinter entry for Jude in its Essex index, so her baptism and the 1614/15 marriage cannot be reached there. No marriage of Thomas Josselyn/Joslin to a Rebecca is indexed anywhere in England 1610-1620. The Jude families in the Essex index are at Great and Little Canfield (Thomas son of John Jude bapt. Great Canfield 25 Apr. 1591; John Jude father of Thomas bapt. Little Canfield 2 Feb. 1571/2; Thomas Jude x Jone Mead, Little Canfield, 30 Sep. 1582) and Henry Jude's children 1602-1625 (parish not indexed; one is a Rebecka, 7 Jun. 1625). Great Canfield was a Josselyn parish too, so a John Jude there is a lead for the 'John Jude of Radwinter' of the 1613 deed (not entered). ¶4a

1613 deed found in the Essex Record Office catalogue: ERO D/DHt T531, dated 29 September 1613 (feoffment to conditional uses). The archivist's calendar reads: 'Ralph Josselin senior and Simon Josselin, both of Roxwell, to John JUde of Radwinter and Ralph Josselin, jun., of Roxwell, all yeomen, to the use of Ralph Josselyn, sen, for life with successive remainders to Thomas Josselyn, a son of the feoffor, and Rebecca Jude for life in survivorship and their issue', with the land 10 acres of Apsonfield in Roxwell, and a condition that Ralph's heirs pay '£100 within 1 year after his decease at the mansion house called Bollinghatche in Roxwell' to Thomas and Rebecca. This is the deed Joslyn (NEHGR 158) used, now seen in an archive calendar rather than a review. It confirms the surname Jude and the settlement of Thomas and Rebecca as a couple. It does not name her parents, and does not say what John Jude was to her. Grade unchanged (Probable, graded on her link to Elizabeth Joslin), because the deed does not touch that link. ¶5

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the grade · 1 search with no result · 4 open questions

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Graded on her link to her daughter Elizabeth Joslin. 'Rebecca his wife, aged 43' sailed with Thomas Joslin and their children, including 'Eliza' aged 6, in the Increase in April 1635 (passenger list as printed in NEHGR 71:253). She was his executrix in 1660/1, married second at Lancaster 16 May 1664 William Kerley, and is not named in his will of 26 July 1669, so she died before then (NEHGR 71:253, compiled). Thomas married her about 1615, before the birth of the eldest child Rebecca (about 1616), and no other wife is known, so she is taken as the mother of all the children. Probable: no record read names her as Elizabeth's mother. Her maiden name is unknown. ¶1a

Searches that found nothing 1

Parents, search (negative so far): (1) Giles Jude of Radwinter, husbandman, nuncupative will 18 Dec. 1598 (ERO D/ACW 3/245; Emmison's abstract, read through archive.org snippets) names his wife Margaret, his eldest son William, his 2nd son Giles, and his daughters Anne, Margaret and Elizabeth. It does not name a Rebecca, who would have been about 6, so Giles was probably not her father. One of the witnesses was John JUDE, probably the John Jude of Radwinter of the 1613 deed. (2) John Jude senior of Great Canfield, husbandman, will 29 Sept. 1595, proved 29 July 1597 (ERO D/ABW 21/182; Emmison, Bishop of London's Commissary Court 1596-1603, no. 526) names his wife Joyce, his sons John, Thomas, Philip, William, Thomas the elder and Harry, his daughters Joan Flecher, Margaret Jude, Audrey Cramphorne and Constance Hickman, and godchildren 'William and John sons of Henry JUDE, John son of John JUDE, Matthew and Priscilla daughter of Philip JUD'. No Rebecca. (3) Earlier Radwinter Jude wills, Henry Jude, tailor (made 26 Oct. 1584, ERO D/ACR 7/265), and William Jude, husbandman (made 9 Nov. 1583, ERO D/ACR 7/276), are registered copies with no image online. Both men died before Rebecca was born (c.1592), so either could be a grandfather only. (4) FreeREG's Radwinter transcript has Jude entries only from 1640 (the surviving register seems to start about 1638). FreeREG has no Rebecca Jude baptism anywhere in England 1580-1620. Essex Jude families of the right date are at Saffron Walden (John Jude m. Ellin Clarke 5 Jun 1593 and Agnes Cooke 13 Jan 1594/5; both too late for a c.1592 birth unless her age of 43 in 1635 is too high), Helions Bumpstead, Great Sampford and Great Canfield. ¶6a
