Life story

Marriage: Niels Nielsen, born 1824 at Nordre Sunde, son of Niels Thorsen, married Synneve Jacobsdatter (b. about 1804, Haale, Avaldsnes) at Madla on 21 Jul 1847 (index). ¶2

Baptism: Niels, born 7 Feb 1825, baptised 20 Feb 1825 (ministerialbok index gives 20 Jan), son of Niels Thorsen and Malene Nielsdatter of Nordre Sunde, Malde sogn, Håland. Confirmed as their child. ¶1

1865 census: Niels Nielsen, 38, widower, cottager with land (huusmand med jord) at Nordre Sunde, with his mother Malene Nielsdatter, widow, 73, born Hjelmeland. The age 38 implies about 1827, two years off the baptism. ¶3

1875 census (Probable): Nils Nilssen, born 1825 in Malde sogn, farmer-owner (gaardbruger selveier) at Nordre Sunde, with wife Berthe Marie Thorsdatter (b. 1832) and children Serine 1867, Thore 1869 and Marie 1872. The son Thore fits naming after Niels Thorsen's father. Probable, as no record in that household names his parents. ¶4
