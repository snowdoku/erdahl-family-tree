Life story

Primary record: his betrothal in October 1692, Leikanger register 11564 canvas 10 right: 'Giest Mattes[en] / Dorethe Samsons d. [Hy?]dall } spon.' (the same bracket holds a second couple, Hans Hellen and Solvei Holland). This is the first record that names him and Dordei Samsonsdatter as a couple, ~7 years before the 1701 census shows him at Torvold with a son Samson aged 2. Grade unchanged (Probable on his link to Guttorm): the 1750 probate names the brothers' mother Dordei, not their father, so his fatherhood still rests on the patronymic Giestsen and on this marriage. ¶3a

Primary record: the wedding, Leikanger 11564 canvas 11 left, Dom. 25 post Trin. 1692 (late November 1692): 'Giest Mattis[en] / Dorethe Samsons d. } nupt.' No farm is written for either; the betrothal (canvas 10 right, Oct 1692) remains the only entry with her farm word '[Hy?]dall'. Grade unchanged. ¶4

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

2 on the grade · 3 open questions

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Speculative as father of Guttorm Gjestsen of Vikøren (see his page). The Vik bygdebok H. 3 (canvas 28, Vikøyri; rebuilt from word boxes, dates ABT) gives the father of Matias Gjestson of Vikøyri (1701-1777) as 'Gjest Matiasson 1657-)', who was [living] at [a farm in a gap] in 1701. Guttorm Gjestson «Seim» (1706-1775) is on the same Vikøyri pages (canvas 14), and Mathias Gjestsen of Vikøren stood surety at the 1759 Tjugum betrothal of Guttorm's son Gjest (Leikanger 11566 c34), which suggests the two were brothers; Guttorm also named his first son Gjest. But nothing read states that Guttorm was Gjest Matiasson's son: the brotherhood of Guttorm and Mathias is an inference, so this is a candidate only. ¶1a

Upgrade: Speculative -> Probable, on the 1750 probate of Samson Giestsen (Ytre Sogn 7a fol. 37b), graded on his link to his son Guttorm Gjestsen (see his page). Primary record found: the probate after the cottager Samson Giestsen at Hagen under Hopperstad in Vik, 1750 (Ytre Sogn 7a fol. 37b, No. 92, 24163 view 38), names as heirs, besides his widow Ingebor Iversdatter, his mother Dordej Samsonsdatter and 'tvende Brødre Mathias Giestsen og Gutorm Giestsen, begge myndige og tilstede paa egne og deres [Moders] Vegne' (share heads: 'Den afdødes Moder', 'Den afdødes Broder Mathias Giestsen', 'Anden Broder Gutorm Giestsen'). So Guttorm and Mathias Giestsen were brothers of Samson Giestsen, and their father was dead by 1750 (the mother inherits with the brothers; Samson left no children). The 1701 census for Vik lists at Torvold (parish 'Wiigs', so Torvold is in Vik) 'Giest Matiesen', inderste, 43, with his son 'Samson Giestsen', 2 (b. ~1699): the only Samson Giestsen in the Ytre Sogn records, and Samson is the name of the mother's father (Dordej SAMSONSdatter), so the 1750 Samson is the 1701 boy. Guttorm (b. ~1706) and Mathias (b. ~1701) were born after the census. Grading: Probable, not Confirmed. The 1750 probate names the brothers and their mother but not their father, so Giest Matiesen as father is inferred (shared patronymic Giestsen + the 1701 Samson, 49 years and a change of farm earlier). The tree's Guttorm is identified with the 1750 'Gutorm Giestsen' by name, parish and his brother Mathias Giestsen (Mathias of Vikøren stood surety at the 1759 betrothal of Guttorm's son Gjest, 11566 c34); the probate does not give Guttorm's farm. The word read '[Moders]' in 'deres [Moders] Vegne' is partly legible; if confirmed it names Dordej as the brothers' (not only Samson's) mother. No rival Guttorm Giestsen is indexed in Ytre Sogn 1698-1790. ¶2a
