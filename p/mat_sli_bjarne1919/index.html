About the family

Youngest child of Ole Gundersen Slind and Ingeborg Olsdatter in Norway, born 17 April 1919 in Selbu. In the 1920 census of his father's farm at Kjosnes sondre, aged one; emigrated with the family on the Stavangerfjord on 7 June 1922, aged three. ¶2

Youngest of the six children who crossed on the Stavangerfjord in June 1922, aged three. ¶3

His middle initial in the American records is J., not O., so by the 1970s he had dropped the Norwegian patronymic Olsen. ¶8

Life story

Emigrated with his parents and five siblings, Trondheim 7 June 1922 on the Stavangerfjord: emigrant protocol no. 15, page 140, line 33, group 00883, 'barn', residence Selbu, destination 'U.S. Amerika'. He was three years old. ¶1

Marriage 1: MARY KATHRYN MEAD, 7 APRIL 1942, Kittitas County, Washington - Kittitas County Auditor, Marriage Records 1891-2005, reference cektsmarcert0007668, document 75990, groom entered as "Bjarn Slind"; a duplicate licence is filed in King County. She was b. 1919 and d. 1972 in King County, and is buried at New City Cemetery, Kittitas County, plot B46-L1, as "Mary Mead Slind, b. 1919 d. 1972". ¶4

1950 census, Snohomish County, Washington, ED 31-62E (NARA schedule 6342053): "Slind Bjarne, Mary K., Mary V., Eric P., Margreth B." - three children. All three married in King County: Mary Victoria to Sidney John Flor in 1965; Eric Paul to Judith Ann Cochrane in 1968 and to Twila Marie Annis in 1978; Margreth B. (entered in the county book as "Bridget"/"Briget") to Peter A. Poole in 1970. ¶5

Marriage 2: DOROTHY MAE BYERS, 1973, King County (DOH index "SLIND BJARNE J x BYERS DOROTHY M, 1973"). She was b. 18 September 1921 at Tacoma, Pierce County, and d. 15 February 2004 (NUMIDENT, SSN XXX-XX-8186, ZIP [withheld]). ¶6

Death: 2008, King County, Washington - Washington DOH Death Index 1907-1960;1965-2024, "SLIND, BJARNE, 2008, King County". He was eighty-nine. ¶7
