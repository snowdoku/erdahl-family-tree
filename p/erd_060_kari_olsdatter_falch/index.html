Life story

Probate: Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll A 9a, p. 520, No. 15, read from the scan. Probate (skifte) held in April 1775 (day read as 5, uncertain) at the inn (giestgiversted) Balholmen, Kvamsøy skipreide, after Kari Olsdatter, between her widower Giert Nielsen Falck, privileged innkeeper (priviligeret giestgiver) there, and her heirs. She left no children: the heirs are her mother Marithe Claudi[?]datter (named together with an Ole Larsen Husebø, probably her husband or guardian), a brother [Giert?] Olsen Husebø, and sisters Ase Olsdatter (married to Jon Thorsen [?]Vamre, dead, leaving daughters Kari and Ma[rithe] Jonsdatter, 12), [Gieblaug?] Olsdatter (married to [Ivar?] Helliesen Sande) and Dordei Olsdatter (dead, leaving a daughter Marithe Andersdatter). The scan is low-resolution (1425 px), so the family's names and the farm names Husebø, Vamre and Sande are uncertain readings. Gross estate 382 rd, net 322 rd; the widower took half, 161 rd. ¶1

Marriage: she was the first wife of Giert Nielsen Falck (see his page), who was 'married twice' by the 1801 census. The marriage date and place are unknown; she died before the April 1775 probate. ¶2
