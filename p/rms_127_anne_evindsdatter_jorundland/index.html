NEW, Speculative, graded on her link to Asbor Tallaksdatter (see her page). Åmli ætt og heim II, Jørundland L.nr. 36 (canvas 78): 'Tallak Olson Jørundland. Skifte 1701. g 2. m Anne Eivindsdtr. Born: Asborg g m Svenn Ormson Askland; Gunvor...'. The text also says Tallak had a son Olav by his first wife, whose name is unknown, and the rebuilt page has a gap where a 'g 1.' line might stand, so it is not certain that Asbor (who married about 1682 and so was born about 1655-62) is the second wife's child rather than the first wife's. Speculative until a record names Asbor's mother. Bygdebok only (compiled secondary source, text rebuilt from word boxes, dates ABT), so Probable at most. ¶1a

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: graded on her link to her daughter Asbor Tallaksdatter (see her page). Østre Råbyggelag Skifteprotokoll nr. 1 (DA 24349, view 278), fol. 274a, 22 Nov 1701: 'bleve paa Jørunland Registerit og werderit hvis ringe middel som fandtis efter salige Thallack Oelsen... imellem Creditorerne, saa som hans Efterlatte Hustru, Anna Evindsdaatter, og deris sammen avlede Børn, Nemlig Asbør og Gundvor Thalacksdøtter som nu er giffte, efter Svend Ormsen Askland[s] beretning (som har dend Eldste Daatter til Ehte)'. Card 39942/5977 indexes it as 'Fol. 274... Thallack Oelsen + / Anne Evindsdatter, Barn Asbør, Gundvor'. (The probate on fol. 274b-276a of the same day is a different one, after Gunild Osmundsdatter, widow of Olle Thallacksen and Halvor Ormsen, card 5979.) 'deris sammen avlede Børn' (their children together) names Anna Evindsdaatter as the mother of Asbør and Gundvor. This resolves doubt about whether Asbør was a first wife's child: the son Olle Thallacksen is not among the heirs, so he may have been the first wife's son, but Asbør is Anna's. Asbør is tied to Svend Ormsen Askland in the same sentence. Anna was alive on 22 Nov 1701. Confirmed. Her own parents (an Evind) are unknown; the bygdebok gives none. ¶2
