About the family

Youngest child and only surviving son of Theodore Ulysses Ramsey and Wilma Almira Nelson, and the brother who kept the land. His father's 1975 obituary describes him as living "on the original Ramsey farm". ¶1

Children: Wendy, later Mikkelson, of Hurricane, Utah; Bonnie, who married Steve Rodberg of Joice; and Rhonda Renae (18 Sep 1954 - May 1981), who died before her grandmother and is named in the 1992 obituary as the predeceased granddaughter. ¶4

Life story

His birth date is corroborated by the 1930 census, taken in April 1930, which records him as aged 0 years 11 months. ¶2

He was a ring-bearer or attendant at his sister Wava's wedding in February 1942, when he was twelve. ¶5

He married Norma on 5 October 1947. His mother's 1992 obituary lists them as "Lynn and wife Norma Ramsey" of Joice, and he was appointed executor of her estate in the Iowa District Court for Winnebago County on 21 September 1992, under a will she had made on 16 June 1960. ¶3
