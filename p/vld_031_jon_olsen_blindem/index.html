Life story

Widower (enkemand) of Kari Rasmusdatter in her probate at Blindem, Borgund, 8 Sep 1798 (Sunnmøre skifteprotokoll 21B). Her second husband; they married between 1 Jan 1790 (burial of her first husband Rasmus Alvsen) and 1798. Not the father of Anne Martha Rasmusdatter. Birth, parents and marriage date not yet found; the Borgund marriages 1790-1798 (15997) should hold the wedding. ¶1
