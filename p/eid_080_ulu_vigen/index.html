Life story

Marriage (betrothal/wedding), 4th Sunday after Trinity 1699 (2 Jul 1699, Old Style): 'desponsati Anders Kiste[?] og Ingebor Kroppel[?]; ditto Gunner Vigen og Ulli[?] Nielsd.' (16310 canvas 4 right). This gives her patronymic Nielsdatter; the forename (Ulli / Aali / Eli?) is still uncertain - the churchings of 1709 and 1712 read 'Ali[?]' and 'Ula[?]'. ¶4a

Churching: on the 26th Sunday after Trinity, 20 November 1701, 'Introd. Gönner Wigens q. Ulü[?] efter det 1ste barn Olof' - Gunner Vigen's wife was churched after her first child Olof (Selbu ministerialbok 1699-1737, canvas 11 left page). The given name is uncertain: the clerk wrote something like 'Ulü'. The same form appears for a godmother 'Ulü Wigen' on the 24th Sunday after Trinity 1700 (canvas 8 right page), so she was already at Vigen a year before the birth. ¶1

Churchings name her as Gunnar Vigen's wife: 'Gunner Wigens qv. Ali[?] efter sit 2det barn' (Quasimodogeniti 1709, 16310 c37 L) and 'Gunner Vigens qv. Ula[?] efter sit 4de barn Marit' (12th Sunday after Trinity 1712, c47) -. The forename may be Eli (Ellen) or Ulla; still uncertain. ¶3

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the grade · 1 correction · 1 search with no result · 3 open questions

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Rated Probable for the same reason as her husband: she is named in a primary record as the mother of Olof, and Olof is identified with Ola Gunnarsen Overvik by name, farm and age. Her name, origin and dates are unknown. The marriage entry (Selbu 1699-1700, canvases 3-7, not all read) would give her full name and patronymic. ¶2

Corrections 1

Correction: name set to Ulli Nielsdatter (forename uncertain). ¶4b

Searches that found nothing 1

Negative, test record read: the opening of her husband's probate, Stjør- og Verdal skifteprotokoll 2B (24751) view 30, fol. 403a, skifte No. 1: 'Anno Christi 1732 d. 12te Febr. blev en Skifte foretaget og holden paa gaarden Øvervigen i Selbou Præstegield efter afgangen Gunder Olsen Wigen imellem hans efterladte 3 Børn: 1. Ola Gundersen 30 aar, 2. Nils Gundersen 28 aar og 3. Giertrud Gunders Datter 25 aar gl. og u-forsørget, hiemme paa gaarden Wigen'. No widow is named and the entry does not name the children's mother, so it neither confirms nor names Ulli; she had probably died before Feb 1732. Her grade is unchanged (Probable). Correction to the folio map: 24751 view 34 is fol. 407 (not 400b-401a); the offset here is view = fol. - 373 (view 30 = fol. 402b-403a, view 33 = fol. 406, view 36 = fol. 409). ¶7

Open questions and the next record to read 3

Biography gaps: her birth, before the 1699 start of the Selbu register, cannot be traced because the 1664-66 and 1701 manntall list men only, so her father is unidentified. Her death and burial are not found in the drafts; the next check is the 16310 burial entries (1699-1737) under the spellings Ulli, Ali or Ula, then 16315 (1739-46) if she died later. Her husband's 1731 probate (Stjør- og Verdal 24750-24751, unindexed) may name her and her kin. ¶5

Test record: 24751 fol. 403 (about view 36-37), the opening of that skifte. ¶6b

Test record (still open): her burial in Selbu 16310 (1699-1737) under the forms Ulli/Ali/Ula Vigen, Gunner Vigens qvinde, between 1712 (her last churching, c47) and Feb 1732. ¶8
