About the family

Wife of Bersvend Pederssen, cottager at Eidem, and probably Kari Pedersdatter's paternal grandmother. 1801 census, Selbu, rural residence 0034 Eidum, household 07, aged 46, 'hans kone', with sons Peder, 14, and Torsten, 5. ¶1

Children: Marit (baptised 22 May 1785, buried February 1786 aged 1); Peder (baptised 23 September 1787, buried June 1789 aged 2); Peder (baptised 14 February 1790); Torsten (born 10 September 1795); and a child about early 1784 known only from her churching. Two of the godparents at Torsten's baptism were of Berge (Anders Torstensen Berge and Giertrud Andersdatter Berge), which may be her own kin. See Bersvend Pederssen for the references. ¶5

Twins: on 5 November 1796 she bore twins, Karen and Jon, baptised 13 November 1796 (25th Sunday after Trinity), parents 'Bersven Pedersen Eidem, og Kirsten Engelbricts d:'; Karen's godparents were Friderich Torstensen Berge, Gunner Erichsen Samstad, Berete Olsdatter Nyskjelmoen[?], Doreth Olsdatter Krosnæsset[?] and Kirsten Sivertsdatter Eidem; Jon was baptised at home (hiemmedöbt) (Selbu ministerialbok 1757-1801, canvas 199 right page). Karen died on 2 August 1797 aged nine months ('3 Fjerdingaar') and was buried 6 August 1797 (canvas 212 right page). Jon is not in the 1801 household (only Peder, 14, and Torsten, 5), so he probably died before 1801 - an inference; his burial has not been seen (canvases 200-208 unread). ¶8

Life story

Death, a lead only: the word-box reconstruction of Selbu i fortid og naatid vol. 4, p. 206 gives the holder's wife 'Kirsti' the years '1755,... 1836'. The birth year matches hers (46 in 1801, 71 in 1825). If the death year is right, her burial should be in the Selbu burials of 1836; the burial index has not yet been searched for 1831-1845. Not set as a date. ¶6

Marriage and origin: betrothed 17 April 1783 and married 29 May 1783 (Ascension Day) to soldier Bersvend Pedersen of Eidem, entered as 'Kirsten Engelbrechtsd. Berge' - she was of the farm Berge before her marriage (Selbu ministerialbok 1757-1801, canvases 110-111). Her father was therefore an Engelbrecht (Ingebrigt), to be sought at Berge. ¶4

1825 census: Selbu, rural residence 0060* Eidem, household 1 (the farm of Ole Olsen, Gaardmand), entered as 'Kirsten Ingebrigtsd., 71, Hmd. Enk.' - husmandsenke, a cottager's widow - followed immediately by 'Peder Bersvends., 36, Son' and 'Bersvend Peders., 5 1/2', Peder's son. The census was taken on 27 November 1825, so she was alive then, aged about 71 (born about 1754, against about 1755 from her age of 46 in 1801). The transcription flags the reading of Peder's name with '??'; the scan was not read. ¶2

Death and burial: died 11 May 1836 and buried 23 May 1836: 'Kirsten Ingebrigtsd: Eidem Huusmandsenke', aged 83, residence Eidem - Kirsten Ingebrigtsdatter of Eidem, a cottager's widow. Church book from Selbu parish 1824-1842, burials 1836, p. 389 no. 14; index entry, scan. ¶7a

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Confidence raised from Probable to Confirmed, and why. The doubt was whether the Peder Bersvendsen who was her son in 1801 is the Peder Bersvendsen who married Giertru Nielsdatter at Eidem in 1819 and fathered Kari Pedersdatter in 1834. The 1825 census closes it: her son Peder there has with him a son Bersvend aged 5 1/2, and the Selbu baptism register records exactly one Bersvend born 1818-22 to a father Peder Bersvendsen - born 27 March 1820 to Peder Bersvends and Gertrud Nilsd of Eidem (1816-1824 register, pp. 88-89 no. 20). So the 1825 Peder is the 1819 groom, Kari's father, and the 1825 census names him the son of this widow. Two censuses (1801 'Deres boern', 1825 'Son') state the relationship directly. The remaining conflict is the groom's age of 39 in 1819 against 14 in 1801 and 36 in 1825; two censuses outweigh one marriage entry. ¶3

Corrections 1

Correction: this replaces the death 'after 27 November 1825'. The identification is Probable, close to certain: same name and patronymic, same farm, the same standing as in the 1825 census ('Hmd. Enk.', cottager's widow, at 0060* Eidem), and age 83 gives a birth in 1752-53 against 1754-55 from the censuses (46 in 1801, 71 in 1825). The bygdebok skeleton gives 'Kirsti 1755... 1836'. found no other Kirsten Ingebrigtsdatter burial in Selbu 1826-1846. ¶7b
