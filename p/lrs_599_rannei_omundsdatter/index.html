About the family

Mother of Turi Torsdatter (see her page), Probable: the probate names her as Thore's wife in the marriage that left the five daughters, Turi the eldest among them. It does not say in so many words that she bore each daughter, and a first marriage of Thore before 1777 has not been excluded, so the link is Probable, not Confirmed. Whether she was alive in 1790 is not stated in the lines read; she is not in the 1801 Midbø households as read by earlier workers (not checked here). ¶2a

Father (Probable): Orm Tvedt of Tvedt in Treungen (see his page). Her byname at the betrothal was Tvedt and her patronymic was Ormsdatter. She was betrothed in Treungen on the day Egild Ormsen Tvedt married there, and an Orm [T..lefsen?] Tvedt stood cautioner at the Treungen betrothal entered just before hers. Her daughters' guardians in 1790 were 'Gulov Omundsen' and '.. Omundsen' of Treungen, that is Gulloug Ormsen of Tvedt and probably Egild Ormsen. Orm Tvedt is the only Orm known at Tvedt in Treungen in 1755-1775. Not proved, because her baptism (before 1754, when no Kviteseid book survives) is not available. A probate after Orm Tvedt in the Øvre Telemark probate protocols would confirm it. ¶6

Mother linked at Probable: Anne Targiesdatter (see her page), wife of 'Orm Egilssøn Tvedt af Treungen' in 1756 by her father Targiej Torrofsen's probate (25673 view 331). No record names her as Rannei's mother; Rannei's birth year is not known, so whether she falls inside Anne's marriage cannot yet be shown. Her own grade is unchanged. ¶7

Life story

Probate: in the skifte after her brother-in-law, the bachelor Jørgen Johnsen of Nordre Midbø (registered 12 Jun 1790), the first heir is 'Ældste Broder Thore Johnsen Midbøe, Død og i Egteskab med [R/K]annej Omundsdatter, efterladt sig 5 Døttre' (the eldest brother Thore Johnsen Midbøe, dead, who in his marriage with Rannei Omundsdatter left five daughters): Thuri 13, Thone 10, Anne 8, Gunnild 5 and Guroe 2. The first letter of her name is R or K; 'Rannei' is the Telemark form of Ragnhild, so the name is read as Rannei (uncertain). Gulov (Gulleik?) Omundsen of Treungen was appointed guardian of the third daughter Anne and an '... Omundsen' of Treungen of the fifth, Guroe; they were probably her brothers (inference from the patronymic). ¶1

Betrothal: 'Samme Dag Samme Sted Trolovet [Enkemand or Lægdsmand?] Tore [..]son Midbøe og Pigen Rannei Orms datter Tvedt. Cautions-Mænd: Hans Ansteensen Nøs og Knud [..]sen [Lille?] Dale.' The preceding entry is dated 10 Nov 1775 in Treungen church ('i Triungen'), so they were betrothed in Treungen on 10 Nov 1775, the day on which Egild Ormsen of Tvedt married there. The copulation column gives the wedding in Nissedal church in 1776; the day and month are blotted. The groom's patronymic is cramped and partly illegible (the end reads '-son'). He is identified as Thore Joensen Midbø by the farm and by the cautioner Hans Ansteensen Nøs, whom the 1790 probate names as the husband of Thore's sister Gunnild. Kviteseid ministerialbok 1773-1786 (source 7982), canvas 109 right page, read from the scan. The word before the groom's name is cramped: it may be Enkemand (widower) or Lægdsmand (enrolled soldier). A Tore Joensen, ungkarl and soldier, was betrothed in Nissedal on 26 Feb 1762 to Tone Olsdatter, with Ole Midbøe as cautioner (7981 c125L, 2nd entry). That may have been a first marriage of Thore Midbø, but it is not proved. ¶3

Name: the marriage entry calls her 'Rannei Orms datter Tvedt', and the 1790 probate calls her '[R/K]annej Omundsdatter'. The same probate names 'Gulov Omundsen' of Treungen as one daughter's guardian. In 1801 Gulloug Ormsen, 60, lived at Tvedt in Treungen beside Egild Ormsen. So this probate scribe wrote Omund for Orm, and her patronymic is Ormsdatter. The first letter of her name is settled as R by the marriage entry. The record name is changed from Rannei Omundsdatter to Rannei Ormsdatter. ¶4

1 on the grade · 1 open question

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed as mother of Turi Torsdatter. Thore Joensen Midbø was betrothed to her on 10 Nov 1775 and married her in 1776. Turi, their eldest daughter, was baptised on 6 Jan 1777, 14 months after the betrothal. The 1790 probate names all five daughters, Turi 13 included, as left by Thore 'i Egteskab med' (in his marriage with) Rannei. Any earlier wife (see the 1762 Tore Joensen betrothal above) left no child among the heirs, so she cannot be Turi's mother. ¶5

Open questions and the next record to read 1
