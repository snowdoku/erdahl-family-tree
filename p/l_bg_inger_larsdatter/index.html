Life story

1801 census, Odland, Skjold: wife of Jon Poulsen, 56. ¶1

Burial: Skjold ministerialbok, Tysvær section 1815-1830, burials 1824, p. 134/135 no. 10 (index, two transcriptions): 'Inger Larsdatter, Lamevig undre Odland' (also 'Odland Harlamevigen'), died 19 Jun, buried 4 Jul 1824, aged 82 (b. c.1742; the 1801 census gave 56, i.e. c.1745). Same place as her husband Jon Poulsen's burial in 1819 (husmann, Lammevig under Odland). No skifte after her in the Karmsund A5 Hetland register for 1824-25 (see Jon Poulsen's note). ¶3

Bygdebok: Tysvær bd. 1 (1990), I. Odland, canvas 31, no. 49, gives 'Inger Larsdt[r]. 1745-1824' as the wife of John Paulsen, husmannsfolk in Lammevik, with no origin or parents. ¶4

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed (upgrade sweep D), as parent of Orm Jonsen. The 1801 census of Skjold (index, relationship words read) lists at Odland household 08 Peder Aslagsen (69, mand), Angona Iversdatter (70, hans kone) and Ragnille Pedersdatter (23, 'Deres datter', unmarried), and household 12 Jon Poulsen (62, mand), Inger Larsdatter (56, hans kone), Orm Jonsen (22, 'Deres børn', sailor) and Ane Jonsdatter (14). On 1 Jan 1804 'Orm Johnssøn', bachelor, of Aadland (Odland) married 'Rognald Pedersdr.', maid, of Aadland, at Tysvær (Skjold marriages 1804, p. 101, index). Their son John was baptised 15 Aug 1804 to 'Orm Johssøn' and 'Ragnille Pedersdr., hustrue', of Adland (Skjold baptisms 1804, p. 105, index). The couple Orm Jonsen and Ragla Peersdatter are the parents named at Lars Ormsen's 1818 baptism at Ytre Eide, with sons John (1804), Peder (1806) and Poul (1809) named after both grandfathers. So the census names Ragnhild and Orm as children of these two Odland couples, and the 1804 marriage and baptism tie those two children, still of Odland, to each other as the couple of the tree. ¶2

Corrections 1

Correction: the two 'Odland estates' in Ryfylke skifteprotokoll D 6 (23890) fol. 362b (1772) and fol. 399 (1773), named in earlier 'next' pointers as the place to look for this family's parents, are 'Odland i Stangeland' (Stangeland skipreide = Karmøy/Skudenes), not Odland in Hetland skipreide (Skjold/Tysvær). The typed farm register of D 6 (view 18) lists them as: 1772 'Odland i Stangeland, ægtefaller Ole Oresen og Anne Siursdt.' (362b); 1773 'Odland i Stangeland, Morten Josephsen' (399). The register has no Odland/Aadland in Hetland 1767-74 (letters A view 4 and O view 18 read). So a greyscale copy of those pages would not help here. ¶5

Searches that found nothing 3

1762 (negative): the 1762 ekstraskatt for Skjold prestegjeld (source 49646, view 234) lists at 'Odland No. 50' the farmer Lars Helgesen and Malene Thorsdatter (no children over 12), servants Thore Christophersen and Guro Halvorsdatter, Ranveg Olsdatter, and the cottars/lodgers Christopher Torbjørnsen, Brita Rejersdatter, Ole Christophersen, Kari Helgesdatter, Marita Ericsdatter, Borjila Gunnersdatter, Malene Halvorsdatter, Guro Christophersdatter, Guro Larsdatter (old and poor), Toller Ericsen, Ragna Knudsdatter, Jens Tormodsen and Susanna Biørnsdatter. None of Jon Poulsen, Inger Larsdatter, Peder Aslaksen or Angona Iversdatter was living on Odland in 1762; no separate Lammevig or Holen entry appears in the Skjold list (views 216-241, farm column read). They must have come to Odland after 1762, so their parents should be sought on other farms. Inger, about 17 in 1762, is not in Lars Helgesen's household on Odland (he has no children over 12 listed), so he is not shown to be her father. ¶6

1762 (negative): the name columns (couples, children over 12, servants, others, cottars) of the 1762 ekstraskatt for Skjold (source 49646) were read for views 216-220, 225-226 and 229-237 (Odland itself, view 234). No Jon Poulsen, Inger Larsdatter, Peder Aslaksen or Angona Iversdatter appears. Not yet read: views 221-224 and 227-228. Aslaksen men seen, possible kin of Peder Aslaksen but unproven: Lars Aslaksen with Ingebor Hedersdatter at Haaskuld No. 34 (view 217), and Michel Aslaksen, farmhand, at Eggenæs No. 64 (view 229). A Larsdatter of the right age is not listed as a child of any Lars in the views read, apart from Torbor Larsdatter at Frøvig No. 55 (view 218). ¶7

Negative: (1) The 1762 ekstraskatt for Skjold prestegjeld (source 49646) has now been read in full: views 221-224 and 227-228 (Skjold skipreide end, Skaare skipreide, Vats from view 224), the rest. Inger Larsdatter is not in it, so the family came into the parish after 1762. (2) The Ryfylke probate card index 1774-1788 (source 39936) files Odland under Hetland skipreide (Tysvær; views ~236-345). It holds one Aadland card (236a: 1 May 1780, husmann Nils Johnsen g. Elen Olsdatter) and no Lammevik card (L runs Lavøen, Lerevold, Lervik, Lundevold, Løvland, views 298-300), so no probate for this family or their kin on Odland 1774-88. Origin still open. Lead, unproven: card 1100a, Ekenes in Vats, 3 June 1778 (D 7a fol. 135b, view 136), Marthe Larsdatter, unmarried, heirs 'hendes søskende og deres børn'; with card 1100b (Brønille Larsdatter, 1778), these were probably daughters of lensmann Lars Ekenes (card 1099b). The protocol entry is too high-contrast to read the sisters' names, so it is not known whether Inger was among them. ¶8
