About the family

Father of Gundbjør Targiesdatter of Sonderland, who married Aasmund Evindsen Hiemdal in Treungen on 1 Jul 1761 (Kviteseid ministerialbok 1754-1773, p. 39, image 123). Known only from her patronymic and farm: the entry gives 'Targies datter Sonderland', not his own name in full. Her son, baptised 11 May 1764, was named Targie (Tarje (Targie) Osmundsen Heimdal), which fits the custom of naming a son after the maternal grandfather. ¶1

Probable kin: Halvor Targeisen Sonderland, who married Asløg(?) Torofsdatter Hiemdal in Treungen in 1761 (same book, p. 41), was probably another of his children (inferred from patronymic and farm; not stated). What would confirm: Gundbjør's baptism c. 1742 in the Kviteseid book before 1754, or a probate after Targie Sonderland. ¶2

Life story

Name: His own name is given in full in his probate, 'afg. Targei Halvorsøn' at Sønderland (DA 25673 view 481, No. 321), and in his burial, 'Targgi Halvorsen Sonderland', 4 Feb 1759 (7981 p.16). The surname field is set to the patronymic Halvorsen and the farm is spelled Sønderland. ¶7

Land history of Sønderland (lead for his own parents). Panteregister I 1 (DA 10915) fol. 43b, view 86, the only Sønderland folio in the volume's alphabetical farm index (view 19): Ole Olsen bought Sønderland in 1702; Ole Sønderland sold to Targej Torrofsøn by deed of 8 Mar 1713. Then 'Torgej Halvorsøn' acquired 13 Bdtg of the farm for 583 Rd 48 s by a deed dated 30 Jun 1758, registered 6 Jul 1759 (reading of the verb uncertain, but the sum is a purchase price). No Halvor appears as owner of Sønderland before him. So he probably came to Sønderland from another farm, and the probate of his father Halvor should be sought on other Treungen/Nissedal farms, not on Sønderland. Later: Orm Egilsen Tvedt transferred 1 Td to Halvor Targjesen by deed of 11 Oct 1779 (same folio). ¶9

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed, graded on his link to his daughter Gundbjør Targiesdatter (see her page). Record: his probate, skifteprotokoll 9 (DA 25673) s. 472b, view 481, No. 321, held 25 May 1759 at Sønderland in Treungen annex 'efter afg. Targei Halvorsøn', between his widow Torbor Halvorsdatter and 'deres med hinanden avlede Børn': Halvor Targeisøn 20, Kittil Targeisøn 19 (age not clearly read), Gundbiør Targeisdatter 17, Aasild Targeisdatter 15 and Sigri Targeisdatter 11. The record names the father-daughter relationship. Continuity to the tree's Gundbjør: two years later, on 1 Jul 1761, 'Pigen Gundbjør Targies datter Sonderland' married Aasmund Evindsen Hiemdal (7981 p.39), the same farm and name; she was buried on 11 May 1764 aged 22 (born about 1742), which matches her age of 17 in May 1759. Rival ruled out: the only other Targie on Sønderland, Targe Toroffsen (buried 10 Jul 1756), had his probate on 15 Sep 1756 (DA 25673 view 331, No. 195), and his children there were Gunnil, Anne, Asloug and Margith, all married, with no Gundbjør. His burial is 'Targgi Halvorsen Sonderland', 4 Feb 1759 (7981 p.16, image 161); this settles which of the two buried Targies was her father. His son Halvor Targeisen (20 in 1759) is the Halvor Targeisen Sonderland who married in 1761 and again in 1763. ¶6

Corrections 1

Correction: The earlier note guessed that the widow Torbor Halvorsdatter Sonderland (buried 25 Jan 1760, aged 40) was Targe Toroffsen's widow. The 1756 and 1759 probates show she was the widow of Targie Halvorsen; Targe Toroffsen's widow was Sigri Evensdatter. Where his probate was found: the register to protocol 9 is bound at the end of DA 25675 (views 860-863). Its numbers are PAGE numbers (two per folio), so page 944 = s. 472b = view 481, and page 651 = s. 326a = view 331. ¶8

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Caution: TWO men named Targie were buried from Sonderland at Treungen. (1) 'Targ[e] Toroffsen Sonderland', 10 Jul 1756, no age (burials p.8, image 156). (2) 'Targgi Halvorsen Sonderland gl. 65[?] aar', 5th Sunday after Epiphany (4 Feb) 1759, so b. ~1694 (burials p.16, image 161). Also buried: the widow Torbor Halvorsdatter Sonderland, aged 40, 25 Jan 1760 (p.19, image 162), probably Targe Toroffsen's widow. Which Targie was Gundbjør's father is unresolved, and no pre-1754 Kviteseid church book survives on Digitalarkivet. Halvor Targeisen's name could honour either a paternal grandfather Halvor (Targie Halvorsen) or a maternal grandfather Halvor (Torbor Halvorsdatter). Gundbjør was in any case fatherless when she married in 1761. Probate (skifte) after either Targie would settle it. ¶3

Searches that found nothing 2

Search note: the Digitalarkivet probate index has no entries for the domicile Sonderland/Sønderland in Telemark (a Sønderland search returns only Synnaland in Haram, Sunnmøre), and the Vest-Telemark probate cards (DA 6856, 'Øvre Telemark vestfjelske sorenskriveri, skiftekort 1689-1821', 1602 views) do not cover Treungen, which lay in the østfjelske district. The estates of Targe Toroffsen (bur. 10 Jul 1756) and Targgi Halvorsen (bur. 4 Feb 1759) of Sonderland must be read in the Øvre Telemark østfjelske skifteprotokoller for 1756-1760 (scan only). Grade unchanged. ¶4

Negative: card index DA 6856 has no Sønderland card; the Su-, Sv-, Sy- and Sø- runs (views 1207-1274) were read. Protocol-9/10 Treungen estates are not carded. ¶5
