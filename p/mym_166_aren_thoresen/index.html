Life story

Negative, 1665 vicar's census, Frosta, 35528 views 137-140 = pp. 255-261, read on the scans; view 141 not read: no son Aren (Arent) of a Thore is listed in the sons columns of the first quarter (pp. 255-259, ending 'Ende paa den første Fierding i Frosten Sogn') or the first page of the second quarter (p. 261). Lille Hogstad (p. 259) has only 'Olluf 36' as user, with no sons. On p. 261 a farm whose name begins '..ůckstad' (3 spand) has the user 'Thoren 62' (b. about 1603) with no sons listed. Neither Alstad nor (Store) Hogstad was found on these pages. ¶5a

Negative, 1665 vicar's census, Frosta, 35528 view 141 = pp. 262-263, read on the scan: p. 262 lists about a dozen farms of the second quarter (among them Hygen, Nygaard, Riset and Lauden; several names are hard to read) with users such as Jon 48, Niels 64, Ifver 53 and Berger 60; the sons column has only three or four entries (a Jon 18, two sons aged about 20 and 15 at Hygen, and a stepson Jon 16). No Thore with sons Aren and Gunder, and no Hogstad or Alstad. p. 263 is blank. ¶7a

Farmer (bruker) at Alstad østre, Frosta, from at least 1669 (Frostaboka X p. 11); lagrettemann (juror) 1690, 1691 and 1693; died before his probate of 22 Apr 1693. Speculative father of Greis Hansen the elder (see his page), graded on that link. ¶1

Evidence. The probate card for Alstad, Frosta, 22/4-1693 (card index 77804, view 23, read; protocol 1 fol. 90) lists the deceased Aren Thoresen, his widow Ragnild Mortensdatter, and 'the husband's children from earlier [marriage]': Hans Arensen, of age; Greigers Arensen and Anne Arensdatter, minors; guardian for Greiger and Anne their father's brother Gunder Hogstad. Frostaboka X pp. 11-12 gives the same heirs and puts the next bruker Sivert (1693-1730, m. Marit Olsdtr.) on the farm with daughters Lisbet (married in Trondheim) and Ingeborg Sivertsdatter. ¶2

Card index by farm, Hogstad, Frosta; 77804 c785-799 read: the guardian of 1693, 'farbror Gunder Hogstad', has his own probate card, canvas 793: 'Hogstad, Frosta, 14/4-1730: Gunder Thoresen'. Children by his first marriage with Ellen Giøersdtr.: Giøres (Greis) Gundersen, who went to Sjælland 8 years before, and Ellen Gundersdtr., wife of Ejner Colsen, Fosen in Nordmøre. Children by his last wife Berit Larsdtr.: Tor Gundersen Berg (married, in Frosta), Lars Gundersen (soldier in Coldevin's company) and Kari Gundersdtr. (married to Christen Hogstad, Frosta). Estate insolvent (prot. 2, fol. 299). This proves the farbror's patronymic Thoresen, so Aren Thoresen and Gunder Thoresen were sons of a Thore, as the 1693 'farbror' implies. The name Greis (Giøer) recurs in Gunder's family through his first wife Ellen Giøersdatter. Nothing here settles the 1771 'Greis Hansen' conflict, so the grade is unchanged (Speculative). Other Hogstad cards: c795 Hogstad lille 28/3-1737 Lars Johansen; c797 Hogstad nedre (see Holmberget); c799 Hogstad øvre 2/10-1693 Gunild Andersdtr., widower Kolbein Andersen, her children Joen and Niels Pedersen. No card for a Thore Hogstad. ¶6

How sure are we?

Speculative. A plausible candidate only. Treat this link as a question, not a fact.

1 on the grade · 1 on the identification · 2 open questions

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Why only Speculative: the 1771 probate calls the father's siblings 'Greis Hansen' and 'Anna Hansdatter' and the mother's brother 'Sivert Olsen'. Taken at face value, that makes Greis the son of a Hans, and conflicts with the 1693 patronymic Arensen. The clerk in 1771 was recording relatives about 80 years after 1693, and may have given Greis and Anna the patronymic of their niece Gurie Hansdatter, but that is an inference. Greis also named his son Hans, not Aren, which suits a father called Hans. The identification spans 78 years, and the patronymics contradict each other, so it stays Speculative until a record gives Greis's patronymic as Arentsen, or links Greis Hansen the elder to Alstad. The protocol entry itself (24749 fol. 90a, view 89, scan) agrees with the card: 'Efter afdøde Aren Thoresen, imellem hans efterlatte Hustru Ragnild Mortensdatter... og hans [forrige] Børn, Hans Arensen myndig, Greigers Arensen og Anne Arensdatter umyndige'. It gives no ages and no mother's name. Records that would test the link to Greis: the probate of Greis Hansen the elder (d. before 1771; farm unknown); Frosta tingbøker c.1700-1730 (Greigers Arentsen as a party). ¶4

The case for this identification 1

Why he may be Greis's father: the 1771 Riset probate after Gurie Hansdatter (24757 fol. 381b) names her father's siblings as Greis and Anna, and her mother's brother as Sivert, whose daughters were Ingeborg Sivertsdatter, a widow living in Trondheim, and Lisbet Sivertsdatter, married in Trondheim. So Gurie's father Hans and his siblings Greis and Anna form exactly the 1693 trio Hans, Greigers and Anne, children of Aren Thoresen at Alstad. Her mother's brother Sivert, with daughters Ingeborg and Lisbet, matches Sivert of Alstad østre, the next bruker after Aren. Gregers/Greis is a rare name: it occurs only 3 times in the whole of Frostaboka X. ¶3
