Life story

Lokalhistoriewiki names Jon Bjørnsen (d. 1770) as a son of Bjørn Jonsen of Sande and Skåsheim in Balestrand. The 1701 census lists 'Biørn Joensen', 29, born in the parish, as a servant (tjener) at Sande. That is possibly the same man, matched on name, age and farm only. ¶1

1701 census, re-read: 'Biørn Joensen', tjener (servant), age 29, place of birth 'føed i sognet' (born in the parish), at Sande, Leganger parish, page 30. The household is not a farmer's: its head is Dorethe Schielderup, whose position is transcribed 'Sl. hr Jens Bugis' (read here as the widow of the late Herr Jens Bugge; 'hr' is the title of a clergyman; the word widow is not written), with the remark 'præsteenchegaard' (the farm set aside for the parish priest's widow), and her sons Olle Bugge and Peder Bugge are lieutenants. A second servant, Erich Ingebrigtsen (born about 1681), is also born in the parish. The census adds nothing that ties this Biørn to Jon Bjørnsen (born about 1711): Biørn was a servant in 1701, not a holder at Sande or Skåsheim. Stays Speculative. ¶2a

Laberg 1985 (Sande, printed p. 38): Bjørn was first [in service] at Sande, born [about] 1672; in 1701 the farm's owner, the widow of [the priest] at Leikanger, who owned Sande and Skåsheim, lived at Sande. Later Bjørn leased a holding at Skåsheim, where he died in 1729 ('Bjørn Sande' came to Skåsheim). This fits the 1701 census servant 'Biørn Joensen', 29. His patronymic was not read on the page. ¶3

Orchestrator review: kept at Probable, not Confirmed. The 26 Apr 1711 baptism ('Joen Biørnss. Sande') and the 1729 probate (son Jon, 17) prove that Bjørn of Sande had a son Jon born 1711. But the identification of that Jon with Jon Bjørnsen of Bale (see his page, Gurid's husband) rests on Laberg's 'Jon Bjørnson frå Sande' (bygdebok) plus the matching name, birth year and parish, not on a record that names both. ¶8a

1664-1666 censuses: Sande and Skåsheim had no farmer of their own; both were worked by the king's bailiff ('Brugis bægge af Kong. Mayts Fogit', 1664; 'Brugis af Fogden', 1666), with husmenn Hans Knudsen[?] 26 and Lasse Olsen[?] 28 (1664) and Oluf 36, Lasse 40 and a boy Kollack[?] 6 (Sande 1666). No Jon is listed, so Bjørn Jonsen's father is not found there. ¶9

Probate 27 May 1729 at 'Schaasem udi Qvamsøe Skibrede' after 'den Sal. Mand Biørn Jonsøn', between his widow Brithe Andersdatter and their children Jon, 17, and Anders, 15, both sons; their maternal uncle (morbroder) Anders Røsem[?] was summoned for them; appraisers Christen Maalsnes and Anders Stockebø. The estate's closing lines (fol. 348b) make the widow guardian of her sons under the supervision of Mons Forness[?]. ¶5

Burial: Leikanger ministerialbok 11564, canvas 161 L, year headed 1729, burial column about the Annunciation (late Mar 1729): 'Biørn Sande 59'. Age 59 gives a birth about 1670, which fits the 1701 census servant 'Biørn Joensen', 29, at Sande. Byname Sande although Laberg says he died at Skåsheim. Gives age only, no parents. ¶10

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

3 on the grade · 3 open questions

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 3

Stays Speculative: Laberg calls the 1740 bridegroom 'Jon Bjørnson frå Sande' (p. 60), but the reconstructed text does not state that Jon was this Bjørn's son; the link rests on the patronymic and the farm. ¶4a

Confidence raised from Speculative to Probable on the 1729 probate (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll 3b, fol. 343a), which names a son Jon aged 17 (born about 1711/12), matching Jon Bjørnsen Bale (born about 1711). Not Confirmed because no single record joins the Skåsheim boy to the Bale husband, and the link of this Bjørn to the 1701 Sande servant rests on name, age and Laberg. ¶6

Confidence raised from Probable to Confirmed on the 1711 baptism 'Joen Biørnss. Sande' (Leikanger ministerialbok 1690-1734, source 11564, canvas 89 R, Jubilate 26 Apr 1711, at Vangsnes). It places a Jon son of Bjørn at Sande in 1711, which joins three primary records: the 1701 census servant 'Biørn Joensen' at Sande, the 1729 Skåsheim probate after Biørn Jonsøn naming his son Jon aged 17, and the 1760 Bale probate naming Jon Biørnsen as husband of Gurid Olsdatter. It also matches Laberg's 'Jon Bjørnson frå Sande'. The baptism does not name Bjørn's patronymic, so the 1701 servant link still rests on name, age and farm. ¶7

Open questions and the next record to read 3

What would settle it: a Sande or Skåsheim probate, or a farm-transfer entry, naming Bjørn Jonsen and his son Jon. ¶2b

What would settle it: Bjørn's 1729 probate (Ytre Sogn sorenskriveri) naming a son Jon. ¶4b

What would confirm it: Jon Bjørnsen Bale's 1770 probate or burial (age), or a Bale record naming Bjørn Sande/Skåsheim or Brithe Andersdatter as his parents. ¶8b
