About the family

Parents (Confirmed): the probate after Johannes Herløvsen of Ytre Takle, 25 Sep 1784, names his widow Anna Iversdatter and their eldest daughter 'Sigri Johannes-Datter, gift med Ole Torbiørnsen Yttre-Tachle' (Sigri, married to Ole Torbjørnsen of Ytre Takle), who was present. Her mother Anna was the daughter of Iver Monsen of Botnen by his first marriage (probate no. 46, 1779), so Sigrid's daughter Marthe and Marthe's husband Iver Rasmussen both descend from Iver Monsen (pedigree collapse; see Iver Monsen). Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll A 10a, fol. 5b. ¶5

Life story

Marriage: Engaged (trolovet) 1 Jun 1784 at Brekke church: 'Ole Torbiørnssøn og Sigrid Johannes D[atte]r yst-Takle. For Ægteskabets Lovlighed bleve ansvarlige [answerable for the lawfulness of the marriage, i.e. forlovere]: Mons Pedersøn indst-Takle og Ole Baarsøn[?] Mit-Takle.' Married at Eivindvik church on Dom. VII p. Trin. 1784, which is 25 Jul 1784 (a later pencil note also says 25/7). He was a bachelor (karl) and she unmarried (pige); no ages or parents are given. Gulen ministerialbok 1776-1787, folios 87 and 89b. The wedding is missing from the separate marriage list of the Gulen book 1779-1788, which has only the engagement. ¶3

Named as mother at the baptisms of Torbiørn (1786), Martha (1788), Iver (1790), Giertrud (1793), Anne (1796) and Torbiørn (1800) at Ytre Takle (Gulen church books, index). Probable mother of Marthe Olsdatter, wife of Iver Rasmussen. ¶2

1801 census: Sigrid Johannesdatter, 43, wife of Ole Torbiørnssen at Ytre Takle (Takle Yst), Brekke, married for the first time. Her mother, Anna Iversdatter, 73, widow of a second marriage, lived with them on a farm pension. Birth about 1758 is inferred from her age. ¶1

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Confidence: Probable to Confirmed, from the estate division of 15 Jun 1824 (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll A 16, fol. 358a-358b). The heading names 'Ole Torbjørnsen ytre Tackle og Hustrue Sigrid Johannesdatter ytre Tackle' as the estate holders, and the record names Marthe Olsdatter, wife of Iver Rasmussen Botten, as their daughter. Whether Sigrid was alive in 1824 is not stated in the parts read: the heading uses 'Efterladenskaber' (what is left), but her husband was present and alive. ¶6

Current grade (from patch 006,; stale-note list section A): her father link is Confirmed, to Johannes Herløvsen (see his page), and her mother is Anna Iversdatter (see her page); see the note 'Parents (Confirmed)'. This supersedes the sentence 'Not linked: nothing names him as Sigrid's father' in the older lead note about Johannes Herleivsøn, buried 9 Jul 1784. ¶7

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Parents (lead only): Johannes Herleivsøn of Ytre Takle, 'gammel 69 3/4 aar', was entered on 30 Jun 1784 and buried on 9 Jul 1784, four weeks after Sigrid Johannesdatter of Ytre Takle was engaged (Gulen ministerialbok 1776-1787, folio 88). He was born about Sep 1714 and is a candidate father: Sigrid's mother Anna Iversdatter (73 in 1801) was then a widow of her second marriage. Not linked: nothing names him as Sigrid's father. A probate after him (1784-85) or Sigrid's baptism about 1757-58 would test it. ¶4
