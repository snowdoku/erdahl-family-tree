Life story

1801 census: head of household 02 at rural residence 0034 Eidum, Selbu - 'Jens Thomassen', aged 46, 'Mand', first marriage, HUUSMAND MED JORD (cottager with land) on the Eidem farm, with 'Rangnil Ingebr.datter' 45 and children Brynil Jensdatter 10, Rangnil 7 and Karen 3. An unmarried Karen Jensdatter, 67, lived with them as 'Inderst vanfoer og fattig' - a lodger, crippled and poor. ¶1

He is placed here as the father of Brynhild Jensdatter, wife of John Eriksen and mother of Erik Johnsen Eidem of Union County. Rated Probable on age, patronymic and farm. ¶2

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
