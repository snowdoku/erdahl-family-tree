About the family

Her husband's probate is now read: 23 Nov 1756, after 'Povel Torchilsen' and his second wife Brynild Jensdatter (24753 fol. 732b), listing Torchel, Einer 40, Jens 25, Guru about 34, Kiersten (married to Ole Poulsen Berge) and Gunild. It is a joint probate with the second wife and does not say which children were the first wife's, so it does not prove Gunhild as Kirsten's mother. Kept Probable. Test record (unchanged): the probate after Gunhild herself (d. 1728), Stjør- og Verdal card index 77804 under the farm, which would list Kirsti as her child. ¶4

Father found: Einer Haldorsen Marstad (see his page). His probate, 19 Jun 1736 at Marstad (24751 fol. 694b; card 77804 c1305), lists among his heirs 'afg: Gunild Einers D[atte]r efterladte Børn': Torkild Povelsen 18, Einer Povelsen 6, Guru Povelsdatter 15 and Kiersti Povelsdatter 10, with their father Povel Torkildsen as guardian. This bears out the bygdebok's 'from Marstad' and her patronymic. Kiersti's age of 10 in 1736 points to birth about 1726 (the bygdebok gives 1725). ¶8

Life story

Name: the probate gives her patronymic, 'Gunild Einers datter', so her father was an Einer; her son Einer (b. about 1721) carries the name. The bygdebok (vol. 5, Flora, canvas 509) has her from Marstad; her father is not yet identified. The farm the probate calls 'Flackne' (card: Flaknan) is the farm the tree calls Flora, as in the 1756 probate's typed register ('Flaknan, Selbu'). The estate was insolvent ('Boet fallitt' on the card). ¶6

Burial: 8th Sunday after Trinity 1728 (18 Jul 1728; Easter 28 Mar), 'Begravet... Gunille Einers D: Flackne æt: 41' (Selbu ministerialbok 16310, canvas 113 left page, after the 7 July prayer day). Name, patronymic and farm match the probate after 'Gunild Einers datter paa gaarden Flackne', registered 30 Jul 1728 (24750 fol. 193); the age of 41 gives birth about 1687, as in the tree. Death date narrowed from before 30 Jul to before 18 Jul 1728. ¶7

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Graded Probable on her link to her daughter Kirsten (Kirsti) Paulsdatter (see her page). Selbu i fortid og nåtid vol. 5, Flora (canvas 509): Pål [...] 'gift [...] Marstad [i] 1716 med [...] der, Gunhild [...] ca. 1687, [d.] 1728'; Pål remarried in 1729, so the children born 1717-1728, including 'Kirsti, f. 1725, [g.m.] Ola Pålsen Berge', are hers. She was of Marstad; her patronymic is not recovered. ¶1

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed, graded on her link to her daughter Kirsten (Kirsti) Paulsdatter (see her page). This is the test record her notes named: the probate after her, registered 30 July 1728 (Stjør- og Verdal skifteprotokoll 24750 fol. 193, view 200 right; card 77804 c435 under 'Flaknan, Selbu'). The protocol reads that the skifte was held 'efter afg. Gunild Einers datter paa gaarden Flackne... udi Selboe Præstegield imellem hendes efterladte Mand Povel Torchelsen Flackne og Deres tilsammen Avlede Børn': 1 Torchell Powelsen 9, 2 Einer Powelsen 7, 3 Gorroe Powelsdatter 6, 4 Kirste Powelsdatter 3 years old. 'Deres tilsammen avlede Børn' names her as the mother (audit 8 rule 2). Continuity to the tree's Kirsten: the probate after the same father, Povel Torchilsen of Flaknan, 23 Nov 1756 (24753 fol. 732b), lists the same sibling set - Torchel, Einer (40), Guru (about 34) and 'Datteren Kiersten Povelsdatter gift med Ole Poulsen Berge' - plus Jens (25) and Gunild of the second marriage; four of the 1728 children reappear together, which bridges the 28 years like a sibling set. Birth window: Kirsten's ages give birth 1721 (1801 census), 1722 (burial age 81) or 1725 (3 in July 1728); all of 1721-1725 lies inside Povel's marriage to Gunhild (1716 per the bygdebok) and before his remarriage in 1729, so no daughter of the second wife can be this Kirsten. No rival named. ¶5
