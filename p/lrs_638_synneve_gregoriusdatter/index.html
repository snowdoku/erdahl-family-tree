About the family

Widow of Niels Johansen of Sætre, Lavik skipreide: named 'Enken Synneve Gregori-datter' in his probate No. 31 (Ytre Sogn 6b fol. 550b, 29 Jul 1748), which divides the estate between her and 'deris Børn' (their children): Gregorius Nielsen, Dordei (42), Borni (m. Joen Gulløvsen Indre Hiertholm), Mari (30), Magdeli (m. Anders Lassesen Takle) and Haldis (21). ¶1

Life story

Probate: No. 44, Ytre Sogn 7b fol. 452b, Aug 1759 (day read as 7, not certain), on the farm Sætre, prestegods in Lavik skipreide, 'efter den afdøde Kone Synneve Gregorii Datter', between her surviving son and four daughters: the son Gregorius Nielsen Sætre, of age; the eldest daughter Dordei Nielsdatter; the second daughter 'Borni Niels Datter gift med Joen Gulløvsen Indre Hiertholm'; the third daughter Mari Nielsdatter; and the fourth daughter Magdeli Nielsdatter, married to Anders Lassesen [Takle]. Read from the scan (crops). This repeats her motherhood of Borni (already Confirmed from the 1748 probate) in a record of her own estate, and puts her death before Aug 1759. Haldis (d. 1750) is absent, as expected. ¶4

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Graded on her link to her daughter Borni Nielsdatter: the 1748 probate names Borni, wife of Joen Gulløvsen of Indre Hjartholm, among 'deris Børn', i.e. the children of Niels and Synneve. ¶2a

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Rival: none. Her son was named Gregorius after her father (her patronymic), and Borni named a daughter Synneve in 1749. ¶2b

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Lead: the probate index has 'Synneve Gregoridtr', Sætre, 1756-1763 (pa00000000522550, not read); it is probably her own estate and would give her death and heirs. Her father was a Gregorius (patronymic). ¶3
