About the family

Wife of Jacob Estensen Kongshaug. Mother of Tørri (born 20 Apr 1818, Kongshaug) and, very probably, of Barbro (baptized 30 Apr 1815, indexed with mother 'Marithe Orisd.'). ¶1

Her patronymic was re-read on the scans in October 2026 to test whether she was the Marit Thoresdatter of Nøren who married a Jacob Estensen of Tufsingdal in Tolga on 13 Aug 1809. It does not read as Thoresdatter. At Barbro's baptism (Kvernes, 30 Apr 1815, p. 406) the priest wrote 'og H[ustru]: Marith[e] [G?]orisd:', and at Tørri's (Kornstad, 26 Apr 1818, p. 388) 'og H[ustru] Marith [G?]orisd.'. In both the patronymic starts with a small looped capital, most likely a G, followed by 'oris'; there is no h and the vowel before the s is i, not e. Gorisdatter (Gorris, a form of Gregorius) remains the best reading; the index's 'Marithe Orisd.' is the same letters divided differently. A capital T ('Torisd.') cannot be ruled out completely from the hand, but it would be an odd spelling of Thoresdatter, so the 1809 Tolga marriage is not accepted as hers. ¶3

Life story

The patronymic is read from the handwriting as Gorrisdatter (Gorris = Gregorius) and is uncertain. No other record of her is indexed. She was probably dead by about 1833, when Jacob had a new partner. ¶2

Esten's baptism in Kvernes on 6 Sep 1812 (p. 400), the other test, names no mother at all: the clerk wrote every couple on that page as '<father> og Hustru', here 'Jacob Estens. Hosets og Hu[stru]', and the register has no godparent column. So that entry neither supports nor rules out a Tolga marriage. ¶4

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: Confirmed, graded on her link to her daughter Tørri Jakobsdatter Tungsæt (see her page); her own parents are unknown. The record naming the relationship is Tørri's baptism in the scanned Kornstad parish book 1789-1819 (Digitalarkivet source 16097, canvas 158, p. 388-389, baptisms 1818): born 20 Apr, baptised 26 Apr 1818, child of 'Jacob Estensen Kongshaug og H[ustru] Marith [G?]orisd.' (re-read on the scan in Oct 2026). Continuity is the same as for the father Jacob Estensen (see his page, Confirmed on this baptism): Tørri's 1893 burial gives her birthplace as Kongshaug, her 1838 marriage names her father Jacob, and her censuses give her birth as 1817/1818 in Kornstad; no other Tørri born at Kongshaug about 1818 is known. The uncertain spelling of the patronymic (Gorisd./Torisd.) does not affect the link: she is the wife named in the baptism of the tree's Tørri. A search for her burial (first name Mari*, domicile Kongshaug, 1818-1840) found nothing indexed; Nordmøre probates for the period are scan-only (sources 24645-24664) and no index entry for her was found, so her death date is still open (probably before about 1833). ¶5
