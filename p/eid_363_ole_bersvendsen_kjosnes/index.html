Life story

Re-read of the 1761 skifte after Gurie Jonsdatter, widow of Thomas Kjøsnes (24755 view 117 left, top): the guardian line reads 'Bersvend Kiösnes for Sönnen Jon Thomesen', and the two appraisers are 'Peder Kl[...] og Ole Kiösnes'; 'Odelsmanden Ole Thomesen' was present. No relationship word ties Bersvend or Ole Kiösnes to the deceased's husband, so the skifte does not bridge Ola Olsen to this family. ¶7

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Graded Probable on his link to his son Ola (Ole) Olsen Kjøsnes (see his page). The primary records name him as the father of an Olaf born in 1702 at Kjøsnes: the baptism on the 12th Sunday after Trinity 1702 (27 Aug), 'döbt Olaf Kiøsnæs barn Olaf' (16310 canvas 13 right), and seven weeks later the churching on the 19th Sunday after Trinity (15 Oct 1702), 'Olaf Bers. Kiøsnæs qv. Ingebor efter det 7de barn Olaf' (canvas 14 left), which gives his patronymic and his wife's name and says Olaf was her seventh child. The other Ole at Kjøsnes in 1702, Ole Guttormsen, had a daughter Siri baptised on Invocavit Sunday 1702 (canvas 12 left) and his wife Ingebor churched after that third child on Palm Sunday 1702 (canvas 12 right), so the August 1702 Olaf cannot be his. ¶1

Why Probable and not Confirmed: the link from the 1702 child to the tree's Ola Olsen (holder 1728-1773, d. 1774) rests on name, farm and the bygdebok. There is no record between 1702 and his marriage (bygdebok: 1728), a gap of more than 15 years, and a named rival exists: Ole Guttormsen's son Ole, aged 1 1/2 in 1701 (b. about 1699), also of Kjøsnes. The bygdebok, Selbu i fortid og nåtid vol. 7 (Kjøsnes, canvas 299), lists '7. Ola, f. 1702, se over' as the youngest child of the holder Ola (b. c.1657) and Ingeborg, and the same book (canvas 300) has the holder of 1728-1773 'Ola [Olsen], f. 1702', who first lived together with his brother (Tomas, b. 1699, holder 1723-1759). Its seventh-child numbering agrees exactly with the 1702 churching, which is good evidence that the bygdebok family is right, but the book is a secondary source. ¶2

The case for this identification 2

Identity of the bygdebok holder with Ole Bersvendsen: the 1701 manntall (38461 canvas 114) has at Kjøsnes household 3 'Ole Bersvendsen' 52, with sons Bersvend 19, a son of 4 (name read 'Bersvend?') and Thomas 1 1/2, and the servant Bjørn Jonsen 52. The bygdebok's holder 'ca. 1657' had in 1701 'Bjørn Jonsen, ca. 1649' with him and sons b. c.1682 (who took over), c.1697 and Tomas 1699, so it is the same household; the birth year here follows the 1701 age (52), not the bygdebok's 'ca. 1657'. Death: the bygdebok has him as holder 1701-1713; the next holder (his son b. c.1682) from 1714. ¶3

Supporting, not used for grading: the skifte after Iver Olsen of Såmstad, 2 Aug 1728 (24750 fol. 189), names 'Ole Iversens Farbroder Peder Olsen Kiösnes' and 'Bersvend Olsen Kiösnes' as the widow's lauverge, so in 1728 the brothers Iver and Peder Olsen and a Bersvend Olsen were living at or from Kjøsnes. This fits Ole Bersvendsen's sons (a Bersvend, b. c.1682, is in his 1701 household), and Ola Olsen named sons Peder and Bersvend, but no record names Iver or Peder as Ole Bersvendsen's children. ¶4

Searches that found nothing 1

Negative: no Stjør- og Verdal probate protocol survives for 1702-1726. Digitalarkivet lists only skifteprotokoll 1 (1689-1701, source 24749) and 2a (1727-1731, source 24750) for that period; the probate-designation card index 77836 (1692-1714) repeats the abstracts in 77804. A probate after him or his wife Ingeborg (d. after 1713) is therefore not to be found in the protocols; the remaining sources are the bailiff accounts (48302-48306, 1714-18, probate fees) and the court journals 31207 (1717-32) and 31208 (1724-28). ¶8

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Test record: a probate after Ole Bersvendsen Kjøsnes or his wife Ingeborg (d. after 1713; the 1714-1726 Stjør- og Verdal protocols are not in card index 77804), or a probate after one of his children that names Ola Olsen Kjøsnes as a brother. The 1761 skifte after Tomas Olsen's widow Gurie Jonsdatter (24755 fol. 100b-101, views 116-117) was checked: it names only her children Ole, Jon (16), Ingeborg and Marit, with a Bersvend Kjøsnes as a guardian and no relationship word, so it does not bridge. ¶5
