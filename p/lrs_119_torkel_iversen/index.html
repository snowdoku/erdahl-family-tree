About the family

Father, not supported: Iver Lassesen of Indre Oppedal, whose probate was held there on 6(?) Jun [16]99(?), is not shown to be his father. The probate names the widow Eli Monsdatter (her brother Ole Monsen present) and their minor children Hindrich, Lasse, Mons, Torbiørn, H?endi, Britte, Anna and Gyri(?); there is no Torkel among them. The name read earlier as 'Torkel?' is Torbiørn (Torbjørn) on a closer reading (confirmed by audit). Inferred: a son born before the probate would have been about 41-60 at the births of Torkel's children (about 1740-1751); only a child born after the probate remains possible, and nothing points to one. Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll 1a (1698-1700), fol. 44b-45a. ¶4

Parents (Probable): Iver Gutormsen of Indre Oppedal (see his page) and Kari Andersdatter (see her page). For: (1) the 1708 child Torkel, son of Iver Guttormsen, was baptised from Indre Oppedal, the farm where Torkel Iversen lived at his betrothal in 1737 and died before 1775; (2) the patronymic Iversen fits; (3) the age fits: about 28 at the 1737 marriage, about 42 at the birth of his son Anders (about 1751); (4) naming: his eldest daughter was Kari, the name of the 1708 child's mother (eldest daughters were customarily named after the paternal grandmother), and he had a son Anders, the name of Kari's father and of Iver's sons Anders (b. 1711 and 1712); (5) a Guttorm of Indre Oppedal was a godparent in 1708, and a Guttorm Lassesen of Indre Oppedal was Torkel's cautioner (surety) in 1737 (relationship not shown). ¶13a

Life story

Baptism (identity Probable): at Brekke church on 22 Dec 1708 (a Saturday; the margin abbreviation 'Fr:' is unexplained) the priest baptised 'Ifver Guttorms[ens] barn fra Indre Opdal N: Torche[l]' (the child of Iver Guttormsen of Indre Oppedal, named Torkel; the last letter is lost in a flourish). Godparents (Patrini): Han[n/r]ich[?] (probably Hendrich) Oppedal, Grimmund of the same farm, Gutterm (Guttorm) of the same, Randi Oppedal, and Ra[?] Oppedal. The year is 1708: the next entry is the 4th Sunday in Advent, 23 Dec. Gulen ministerialbok 11436, canvas 3, right page. The record does not say that this child is the Torkel Iversen who married in 1737; that is the identification argued in the note 'Parents (Probable)'. ¶10

Birth: set to BEF 22 DEC 1708 at Indre Oppedal from the baptism above. Children were then usually baptised within days of birth (inference). This replaces the note 'Birth (inference, no record found)', which said no birth field was set. The birth rests on the Probable identification of the 1708 child with this man; if that identification fails, the birth reverts to unknown (about 1700-1715). ¶12

Churching, 17 Feb 1709: on Invocavit Sunday 1709 at Brekke 'Ifvers Indreopdals qvinde Karj' (Kari, wife of Iver of Indre Oppedal) was churched (introduceret) after a boy named Torchel. The same day Ane Torchelsdatter of Indre Oppedal, aged 67 (born about 1642), was buried; she may be the source of the name Torkel (a relative of Iver or Kari), but nothing states it. Gulen ministerialbok 11436, canvas 5, left page. ¶11

Marriage: at Lavik church on 15 Jun 1737 (Lavik communion service, 27 communicants) 'Torchil Iversen Indre opdal' was betrothed (trolovet) to 'Birthe Monsd: Østerbøe' (Britte Monsdatter of Østerbø); cautioners (sureties) 'Guttorm [his mark] Lassesen Indre opdal' and 'Mons [his mark] ibid.' (Mons of the same place, Indre Oppedal). The margin reads '3 Julij / Copul.': they were married on 3 Jul 1737. The day numeral is read with medium confidence; the margin does not name the church, so the wedding place is left at parish level. Gulen ministerialbok 11441, canvas 6, right page. He already lived at Indre Oppedal in 1737, the farm where he died before 1775. ¶6

Birth (inference, no record found): not known. He married in 1737; his wife was born about 1712; his son Anders was 24 in 1775 (born about 1751) and his youngest daughter Dordei was still a minor then. A birth about 1700-1715 fits best; no birth field is set. ¶7

Probate: on 7 Jun 1775 the probate court divided at Indre Oppedal the estate of the late Torchild Iversen between his widow Britte Monsdatter and 2 sons and 3 daughters: Mons Torchildsen of Indre Oppedal, of age; Anders Torchildsen, 24; the eldest daughter Kari Torchelsdatter, dead, who left children Torchel, Sinneva, Ingeri(?) and Britte(?) Haagensen/-datter; the second daughter Else Torchildsdatter, married to Mons Lassesen of Indre Oppedal; and the third daughter Dordei Torchelsdatter, a minor, whose guardian was her maternal uncle (Morbroderen) at Oppedal. Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll A 9, no. 47, pp. 575-576; several ages and guardians are partly illegible. ¶1

Death: before 7 Jun 1775, the date of the probate. No burial was found: the Gulen burial index has no Torkel or Torkild Iversen in 1772-1775 and no rows at all for 1775. ¶2

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Confidence: Confirmed as Else Torkildsdatter's father, from the 1775 probate, a primary record read from the scan. ¶3

Records that disagree 2

Against: (1) Torkild's role in 1756 is explained by his living on the same farm, and he appears beside Guttorm Larsen of the same farm; because the father inherited, siblings could not be named as heirs, so the probate cannot show whether he was a brother; (2) other Ivers lived at Indre Oppedal in the right years: Iver Gutormsen (a child baptised at Brekke 10 Apr 1712), Iver Jensen (probate 1720-1728, not read) and an 'Iver Johannessen' whose wife was churched on 2 Jan 1718 (perhaps a misreading of Josephsen); (3) Torkel married in 1737 and had a son born about 1751, so a birth about 1705-1716 fits, while Iver Josephsen's only known wife, Giri Jonsdatter, is first seen in 1718; Torkel would be her son only if born after their marriage, or the son of an earlier wife; (4) Torkel named no known child Iver or Giri (his known children are Mons, Anders, Kari, Else and Dordei). Decision: kept as a note, not a Speculative link, because nothing points to Iver Josephsen more than to the other Ivers of the farm. ¶9b

Against or open: (1) no record names the 1737 groom as this child; (2) he had no known child named Iver or Guttorm, though his eldest known son Mons (b. about 1741) was baptised about four years after the marriage, so earlier children may be missing; (3) the scan for a rival Torkel with a father Iver is incomplete: 11436 c3-c5 (Dec 1708-Mar 1709) were read, and c40-c42 (Sep-Nov 1712), but 11436 c6-c39 (1709-1712), 11435 c1-c32 (1705-06), 11434 (1700-05) and 11437 onward (1712-) have NOT been scanned for another Torkel son of an Iver. Rivals among the Ivers of the farm: Iver Lassesen is ruled out (patch 031); Iver Jensen died childless in 1720; Iver Josephsen is first seen at Asheim in 1713 and at Indre Oppedal in 1718 with his wife Giri Jonsdatter, too late for a son born in 1708 unless by an unknown earlier wife; Iver Tollaksen (26, soldier) and Iver Bendixen (20) of Indre Oppedal in 1701 Iver Hansen (godparent 1711 and 1712) and an 'Iver Johannessen' of Indre Oppedal whose wife was churched on 2 Jan 1718 (perhaps a misreading of Josephsen) have no known son Torkel. ¶13b

Corrections 2

Correction: Iver Lassesen of Indre Oppedal (see his page) is ruled out as his father. Iver died before 1 Jun 1699 and his probate lists eight under-age children with no Torkel. The probate of 30 May 1701 after Iver's widow Eli Monsdatter and her second husband Lars Johansen again names her children by her first husband, the same eight ('Hindrich, Lasse, Mognus, Torberg, Randj, Britte, Anne og Bigri, Ifvers Sønner og døtter'; Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll 1b, fol. 331b-333a), so a son born after Iver's death would also have been listed there; none was. A son born before 1699 would have been about 38 or more at the 1737 wedding and about 52 at Anders's birth, which is also unlikely. This supersedes the words 'only a child born after the probate remains possible' in the note 'Father, not supported'. His father remains unknown; the patronymic Iversen only says his father was an Iver. ¶8

Correction: the note 'Possible father Iver Josephsen (not linked)' lists among the other Ivers of Indre Oppedal 'Iver Gutormsen (a child baptised at Brekke 10 Apr 1712)'. That wording is wrong: the child baptised on 10 Apr (Gulen 11436 c23; the page is 1711 by its margin and feast sequence, not 1712) was Anders, son of Iver Gutormsøn of Indre Oppedal and Kari Andersdr.; a second son Anders was baptised at Brekke on 11 Nov 1712 (11436 c42 left page); Iver Gutormsen was the father, an adult already married by 1706 (11435 c33). The same note's decision to leave the father unlinked is superseded by the Probable link to Iver Gutormsen above; Iver Josephsen remains a weaker rival (see 'Parents (Probable)'). ¶14

Alternatives considered and set aside 3

Not related: the probate of Agate Monsdatter of Indre Oppedal, 8 Oct [1700] (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll 1b, fol. 257b), is between her widower Gullov(?) Johansen of Indre Oppedal and her father Mons S?sen as heir; she left no children and was not Iver Lassesen's widow. It has no bearing on Torkel's parentage. His father remains unknown. ¶5

Possible father Iver Josephsen (not linked): Iver Josephsen is a candidate for his father but no record states the relation. For: (1) the patronymic and the farm fit: Iver Josephsen was a sponsor at Asheim on 21 Aug 1713 ('Iver Josephsen Asheim', Gulen ministerialbok 11437, canvas 13) and lived at Indre Oppedal by 1718, when he and Giri Jonsdatter had a son Joseph baptised at Brekke on 13 Aug 1718 (11438, canvas 29; the mother's churching of 9 Sep 1718 names the father 'Ifver Josephs: Indre-Opdal'); (2) at the probate of 10 Apr 1756 after that son, Josef Iversen of Indre Oppedal, who died childless and whose heir was his living father 'Iwer Josepsen', 'Torkild Iwersen Indre Oppedal' is named, at least as an appraiser (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll 7a, fol. 334a, Nº 27); (3) Iver Lassesen is ruled out (patch 031). ¶9a

Siblings (not entered as persons): Lars, born about 1706 (mother churched in 1706, Gulen 11435 c33), Anders, baptised at Brekke on 10 Apr 1711 (11436 c23; year contested), and a second Anders, baptised at Brekke on 11 Nov 1712 (11436 c42), all sons of Iver Gutormsen of Indre Oppedal and Kari Andersdatter. They are his siblings only if the Probable identification holds. ¶15
