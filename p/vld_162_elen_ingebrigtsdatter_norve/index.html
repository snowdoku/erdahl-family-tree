Life story

1762 extra tax, Borgund, Lille Nørve household 4 (49637 view 37): 'M: Lars Olsen, H: Sidsle Andersdatter'; in the column for other persons supported: '2de Søster: Synneve og Birthe Olsdatt.' (his two sisters Synneve and Birthe Olsdatter); in the next column: 'Hans Forældre: F: Ole Olsen, M: Elen Ingebrigtsdatt.' (his parents: father Ole Olsen, mother Elen Ingebrigtsdatter); with a servant Hans Andersen Wolsdal; 7 persons taxed. ¶1

New person: mother of Lars Olsen Nørve (see his page) and wife of Ole Olsen of Lille Nørve (see his page). Confirmed, graded on her link to her son Lars: the 1762 extra-tax list names her as his mother ('Hans Forældre: F: Ole Olsen, M: Elen Ingebrigtsdatt.') in the household of Lars Olsen and his wife Sidsle Andersdatter at Lille Nørve, the couple married in 1760 who are in the tree. She and Ole were then living with their son, apparently as retired parents. Her birth is estimated from her son's birth (1735); her own baptism, marriage and burial are not yet found. The same entry names two daughters, Synneve and Birthe Olsdatter. ¶2
