Life story

Bygdebok (canvases 885, 406): second wife of Rasmus Klausson of Indre Oppedal (after 1708) and mother of Mons Rasmusson (1712-1789) and Lisbet Rasmusdtr. (1715-1779, wife of Ivar Monsson of Botnen). ¶1

The 1777 deed has been found and read. It is in pantebok 12446, pp. 239-240 (views 120-121), published at the Gulen skipreide ting on 3 Dec 1777: Mons Rasmussen 'Ofre[?] Opdahl' (the farm word was read as written; probably Indre Oppedal) sells to 'velagte Johannes Iversen Botten' 12 1/2 merker in his holding in Oppedal, which Lars Iversen had sold to him by deed of 23 Nov 1736. It is dated at Oppedal on 11 Sep 1777 and signed 'Mons Rasmussen Opdahl'. The deed as read uses NO relationship word: neither 'morbror' nor 'søstersøn' appears in the text or the signatures. So it does not prove that Lisbet Rasmusdatter (Johannes Iversen's mother) was Mons's sister, and Brita's grade stays Probable. The bygdebok's 'morbror' presumably comes from another record (the tingbok entry, Ytre Sogn tingbok pag. 552 for Dec 1777, A 41/42 range, 30875-30877, unread) or is the bygdebok author's own inference. The sale from Mons Rasmussen to his putative nephew is consistent with the bygdebok family but does not name the relationship. ¶4

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
