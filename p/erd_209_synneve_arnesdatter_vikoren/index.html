Life story

Bygdebok (Vik H. 3): 'Synneva hadde truleg ein bror Jon' (canvas 14), i.e. Jon Arneson Seimsøyri (1703-1776), married 1732 Ragnhild Oladtr (1698-1742), remarried 1743 a Hermunddtr of Vikøyri (canvas 22). The bygdebok's line for Guttorm's marriage in 1732 ends '[...] 1781)', which may be her death year (gap in the rebuild; not entered as a date). Her father (an Arne at Seimsøren) is added as Speculative. Grade unchanged. Test record for her own link: the Vik baptism of Gjest about Jan 1733 naming his mother. ¶5

Married 28 Dec 1732 (the Sunday between Christmas and New Year) in Vik i Sogn: 'ung Karl Gutorm Giestsøn Seim og sin besvangrede Synneve Arnes Daatter Seimsøren' (Vik ministerialbok 11752, canvas 30 left). She was pregnant at the wedding. As 'Enken Synneve Arnesdatter' she was the widow in the probate after the cottager Guttorm Gjestsen of Vikøren, 9 Jun 1775 (Ytre Sogn 9a No. 67), which names the children: the son Giest Guttormsen (dead, leaving one son Giest Giestsen, 14), and the daughters Randi (m. Ole Iversen Wiigdnen), Christie (3-, second digit unclear) and Brithe (24). So she was his wife for the whole span 1732-1775, and no second wife is needed. Birth year estimated from the 1732 marriage only. ¶1

Her residence at marriage was Seimsøren (Vik). No Arne at Seim or Seimsøren appears in the Ytre Sogn probate index 1698-1780 (domicile=Seim*), so her father is not found yet. ¶3

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Probable as mother of Gjest Guttormsen (graded on that link). She married Guttorm Gjestsen in late Dec 1732 while pregnant, and Gjest Guttormsen was buried 5 Jul 1761 aged 28, so born between Jul 1732 and Jul 1733: the expected child of that pregnancy. The 1775 probate names her as widow alongside the son 'Giest Guttormsen' and does not say any of the children came from an earlier marriage. But no baptism naming Gjest and his mother has been read, and Gjest's identity with the Nauteskreen man spans farms (as on his father's record), so the link stays Probable. Two candidate 1733 baptisms in 11752 were seen: '2. Døbt Sexagesima Søndag [8 Feb 1733] Gutorm Hofsvigs Barn' (c31 left, child's name and mother not given; the churching of 'Gutorm Hofsvigs Hustru' on 25 Mar 1733, c31 right), and the churching on Ascension Day [21 May] 1733 of 'Gutorm Gil[ch?]s[en]... Hustru' (c32 right, surname unclear). Neither is clearly Gutorm Giestsøn Seim. ¶2

Upgrade: graded on her link to her son Gjest Guttormsen (see his page), Probable -> Confirmed. Guttorm Giestsen's Vikøren probate of 9 Jun 1775 (Ytre Sogn 9a No. 67, 24167 view 302) is held 'imellem Enken Synneve Arnesdatter og deres tilsammen avlede Børn' (heading, crop h5.jpg), and the first child listed is 'Sønnen Giest Guttormsen', dead, with his son Giest Giestsen 14: this names her as Giest's mother (audit 8 rule 2 wording). The probates after her daughters Brithe (1807) and Christi (1809) Guttormsdatter call Giest their 'Fuldbroder' and place him at Nødskreen with his wife Anna Hermundsdatter. Her 1732 marriage to Guttorm 'besvangrede' fits Gjest's birth (burial age 28 in Jul 1761). Her own probate was not found (no Synneve Arnesdatter under domicile Vikøren 1775-1813). Continuity: the probates after his unmarried daughters at Vikøren, Brithe Guttormsdatter ([26?] Oct 1807, Ytre Sogn 13 p. 271b, 24173 view 274) and Christi Guttormsdatter (3 Oct 1809, 13 p. 551b, No. 54, 24173 view 554), each name as first heir their 'Fuldbroder Giest Guttormsen, der boede og døde paa gaarden Nødskreen i Tiugum Skibrede, og var gift med... Anna Hermundsdatter', and his one son 'Giest Giestsen, boende som huusmand paa g. Indre Esse i Tiugums Skibrede', present (he signed the 1809 entry 'Giest Giestsen Indre Esse'). Brithe, Christi and Rande are the daughters, and Giest Guttormsen (dead, son Giest Giestsen 14) the son, of Guttorm Giestsen and Synneve Arnesdatter in the 1775 Vikøren probate, held 'imellem Enken Synneve Arnesdatter og deres tilsammen avlede Børn'. The 1807/1809 records name the same sibling set (Giest, Rande m. Ole Iversen, Christi, Brithe) at the same farm Vikøren 32-34 years later, so this is a pair/sibling-set bridge (audit 8 rule 3), and 'Fuldbroder' makes Giest a child of both parents. The Nødeskreen husband of Anna Hermundsdatter is the tree's Gjest Guttormsen (probate 18 Sep 1761, Nauteskreen; burial 5 Jul 1761). ¶4a
