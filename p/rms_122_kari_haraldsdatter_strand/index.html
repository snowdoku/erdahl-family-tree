Life story

Bygdebok (Svenes vol., gnr. 65 Strand, canvas 310): wife from 1728 of Hølje Tordsson (1709-1785) and mother of 'Åste 1732, g 1755 m Ulrik' (of Fodnes). Her son Harald (1737) carries her father's name. Her own origin and dates are not in the rebuilt text. ¶1

Primary record found for the marriage: 20 Jun 1728, 'Helje Thords.' of Søndrestrand married 'Karen Haraldsd.' (Aurdal 1692-1730, DA 9360, index pv00000000708027). This replaces the bygdebok as the source for the marriage. Grade stays Probable, graded on her link to Aaste Helgesdatter (see her page): the 1730 and 1732 baptisms name only the father, and no record names her as Aaste's mother. Inferred from the marriage of 1728 alone. ¶6a

The 1732 baptism of Aaste (DA 9361 p. 37, 16 Mar 1732, 'Hælge Strands barn Aaste') names only the father, as every 1732 entry in this book does. It neither confirms nor refutes Kari Haraldsdatter as the mother. ¶5a

Graded Probable: a bygdebok is a compiled secondary source and the passage was rebuilt from word boxes with gaps; dates from it are ABT. ¶2b

Upgrade:, Probable to Confirmed, graded on her link to her daughter Aaste Helgesdatter (see her page). Her husband's probate (card 13055/13056; DA 25288 fol. 148b-149a, view 155, 12 Oct 1785 - 8 Feb 1786, 'paa Gaarden Strande i Ourdahl') is held between 'hans Efterlevende Enke Kari Haraldsdatter og deres i Ecteskab tilsammen avlede... 5 Børn', among them '4. Datteren Aaste Helgesdatter gift med Ulrich Fodnæs'. This names her as Aaste's mother (audit 8, rule 2: 'tilsammen avlede') and names Aaste by her husband, who is the tree's Uldrich Thommesen of Fodnes. No continuity gap: the record itself ties the child to the spouse in the tree. ¶7
