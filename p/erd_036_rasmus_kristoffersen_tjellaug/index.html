About the family

Father of Aase Rasmusdatter: Probable. Aaland lists Ole Østensen as Rasmus's son-in-law and Ole's wife as Aase Rasmusdatter of Kjellog. Independently, Ole and Aase named their children Rasmus (1760) and Berthe (1759, 1765) after Rasmus and Brite, and Østen (1757) after Ole's father, the usual Nordfjord naming pattern. To confirm: the 1755 deed from Rasmus Kristoffersen to Ole Østensen (Nordfjord pantebok), or Rasmus's probate (after 1755), which would name Aase and her husband as heirs. ¶2

Life story

Aaland 1932: Aaland's Kjellog (Tjellaug) history, bruk nr. 1 'Uppigard': 'Rasmus Kristoferson, sk. 1714 p. 16 mk. med styvfaren E. * Brite Rasm.d. Brekke, † 77 a. g. 1765. Vs. Ola Øysteinson Hilde, † 58 a. g. 1780, sk. 1755. * Aase Rasmusd. Kjell.' He was the son of Kristoffer Rasmussen (holder 1697) and Synnøve Jensdatter (Grov), whose second husband Eirik Eiriksen moved to Nesje in 1716. He took a deed in 1714 to 16 merker together with his stepfather Eirik, and married Brite Rasmusdatter of Brekke. His son-in-law Ole Østensen took the deed to the holding in 1755. ¶1

1664 vicar's census (p. 210): 'Halff gaard Tielouff': farmer 'Christoffer Rasmussøn 60 aar, Enchemand' (Kristoffer Rasmussen, 60, widower); in the sons' column 'Rasmus [O]lsøn [?] 18 [?] aar'; servant Hans Olsøn 32. This Kristoffer, born about 1604, is two generations above Rasmus Kristoffersen (born before 1696); he could be the father of the Kristoffer Rasmussen who held the farm in 1697, but that is an inference only and no link is made. ¶3

Bailiff's census 1664-66 (p. 142): 'Halff gaard Tielouff' had farmers Christoffer and 'Enchen' (the widow, unnamed), a farmer's son Rasmus and a servant Hans; no ages or patronymics. The son is not tied to either farmer. The vicar's list gives the son as 'Rasmus [O]lsøn[?] 18[?]' (reading uncertain). If that patronymic is right he is the widow's son; if it was misread he could be a son of Kristoffer born ~1646. Not enough to make a link. ¶4

Deed read: Nordfjord pantebok 12493 pag. 146-147, dated and read at the Stryn ting at Hegdal 30 Oct 1714. 'Kiendes jeg underskrevne Erich Erichsen Tiellov... at jeg... haver solgt og afhendt til min kiere Stifsøn den Dannemand Rasmus Christophersen Tiellov... som den rette Odelsmand... [16] Marcker Smørs Leye, som mig efter og ifølge Skifte-Brev efter min sal. Hustrue Synneve Jensdatter tilfalt udi gaarden Tiellov', conveyed to 'min kiere Stifsøn Rasmus Christophersen, hans Hustrue og Arvinger'. Witnesses Ole... Loen(?) and Ole... Aaland. Translation: Eirik Eiriksen of Tjellaug sells to his dear stepson Rasmus Kristoffersen of Tjellaug, as the rightful odel heir, the 16 marks of butter-rent in Tjellaug that fell to Eirik by the probate after his late wife Synnøve Jensdatter. A contract of the same day (pag. 145-146) between Eirik and 'Rasmus Lauritsen Brekke, on behalf of my brother-in-law (Svoger) Rasmus Christophersen Tiellov' has Eirik give up the third of Rasmus's holding that he had for life, to leave it in spring 1716, for 20 rdl. This is the primary record behind Aaland's 'sk. 1714 p. 16 mk med styvfaren' and it fixes his mother: he is the stepson of Eirik Eiriksen, whose late wife was Synnøve Jensdatter, and he is the odel heir to her share in Tjellaug. He was married by 30 Oct 1714 ('hans Hustrue'). ¶6

Clue (not a link): Rasmus Lauritsen Brekke acted for him in 1714 and called him 'min Svoger' (brother-in-law). His wife Brite Rasmusdatter is called 'Brekke' by Aaland, so Rasmus Lauritsen Brekke is probably her brother or her sister's husband, or married to Rasmus Kristoffersen's sister. If he is Brite's brother she would be a Lauritsdatter, which conflicts with her probate's 'Rasmusdatter'. More likely he married a sister of one of them. The Brekke deeds or probates around 1700-1720 would settle it. ¶7

Guardianship protocols (scans read), graded on his link to Aase Rasmusdatter (see her page): he stays Probable. (1) Protocol 1, p. 81: 'Rasmus Christophersen Tiellov', in place of Erich Erichsen Tiellov, administers 'sin Søster Ane Erichsdatters Midler' under the skiftebrev of 4 Jun 1712. (2) Protocol 2, p. 205 carries the same account: 'Rasmus Tiellov for sin Søster Ane Erichsdatter' (22 rd 2 ort 7 s, partly in 6 marks of land in Tjellaug). A margin note of 1740 says the girl died and 'hendes eeniste broder Rasmus Tiellov' (her only brother) inherited her land and money. So Rasmus Christophersen was still living at Tjellaug in 1740 (death changed from AFT 1714 to BET 1740 AND 1754), and Rasmus Christophersen and Rasmus Tiellov are one man. (3) Protocol 3, p. 150: by 1745-54 the wards' mother is 'deris Enkemoder Brite Tiellov'. (4) Under a skiftebrev of 6 Jun 1742, Tjellaug wards Ingebor 23, Brite 20, Aase 18, Else 12, Rasmus Rasmusen 9 and Synneve Rasmusdatter inherited shares in 6 marks of land in Tjellaug (protocol 2, pp. 238, 256, 269). The protocol does not name whose estate this was. It may be Rasmus's own probate, or that of the son Christopher Rasmussen, after whom Brite's 1754 probate says she inherited 6 marks. Rasmus Christophersen is the only Rasmus shown as a householder at Tjellaug from 1712 to 1740, and the children carry the patronymic Rasmussen and were born there between about 1713 and 1733. That is still patronymic plus residence, so it remains Probable. ¶11a

Brite Rasmusdatter's probate (Tjellaug, 17 May 1754; Nordfjord A 12 fol. 77b-78a) lists six children, all with the patronymic Rasmussen/Rasmusdatter: Rasmus, Synneve 41, Ingebor 35, Brite 32, Aase 30 and Else 24. So Aase's father was a Rasmus, and he had died or was otherwise gone by 1754. The heirs passage does not name him. Under 'Jordegods' (land), fol. 78b-79a (view 81), the estate owned 1 pund butter-rent in Tjellaug, which she had inherited after her late [Søn? the word is probably 'son'] Christopher Rasmussen (6 marks rent), plus land bought by a deed of 6 June 1742 and a mortgage deed of 8 July 1743. A son named Christopher fits naming after a paternal grandfather Kristoffer, which supports Aaland's statement that her husband was Rasmus Kristoffersen, son of Kristoffer Rasmussen of Tjellaug. This is naming evidence only, so the link stays Probable. The farm-history detail in the deeds of 1742 and 1743 (Nordfjord pantebok) may name the seller and settle it. ¶5

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

Corrections 1

Correction: his mother Synnøve Jensdatter died before 30 Oct 1714 (Eirik calls her 'min sal. Hustrue'), so she is not alive at the 1716 move to Nesje that Aaland mentions; Eirik left Tjellaug in spring 1716 by this contract. ¶8

Searches that found nothing 3

Negative: the 1755 deed from Rasmus Kristoffersen to Ole Østensen (Aaland 'sk. 1755') is not in Nordfjord pantebok 12498 (1745-55). That volume ends at fol. 367 with deeds read on 13 Jun 1755 and has a farm register at fol. 368-381 (views 369-382). The register has no Tjellaug/Kjellaug/Tiellov entry under K or T (the T entries are 'Tætlov' Anders Ottesen's lease, fol. 130, and Ole Pedersen's deed, fol. 65). Nor is the deed among those read at the Stryn autumn ting at Faleide on 4-6 Nov 1755, pantebok 12499 (1755-72) views 7-11 (s. 6-10): the Stryn deeds there are nos. 3653-3667 (Øvrebø, Hilde, Aarolen, Erdal/Brekke, Hegdal, Ytre Sunde, Solberg-Faleide), and the Gloppen ting follows on 8 Nov. 12499 has no register (its end, views 380-388, is deeds and the 1755 authorisation). If the deed is dated 1755 it was probably read at the spring 1756 Stryn ting: next read 12499 from about view 12 onward. ¶9

Negative: the 1755 deed to Ole Østensen that Aaland cites ('sk. 1755') is not among the deeds published at the Stryn ting at Faleide on 21-22 Jun 1756 (pantebok 12499 fol. 19-21, views 20-22: Aaland, Hegdal, Ytreeide, Lødemel and a Bergen charter). found it missing from the Nov 1755 Stryn ting too. ¶10a

Negative: the Tjellaug skifte of 6 Jun 1742 (the guardianship protocols' source for the six minors Ingebor, Brite, Aase, Else, Rasmus and Synneve) is NOT in Nordfjord A 8 (24225). That volume is the sorenskriver's half-yearly specifications of minors' inheritances, not a protocol. The Jan-Jun 1742 list (images 98-105, signed Totland 13 Aug 1742) has a Stryn run of Lyslo 26 May, Hilde 29 May and Sølvberg 4 Jun only (images 103R-104L), then Olden 30 May-2 Jun. The Jul-Dec 1742 list (images 106-121) has no Tjellaug entry in its Stryn run (image 117R) or elsewhere in the pages read. The 1744 list (image 126 top) cites other inheritances 'den 6 Junij 1742' at Aarnes/Nesje in Innvik, so a sitting was held on 6 Jun 1742, but it was left out of the Jan-Jun 1742 specification. Rasmus stays Probable. ¶12a
