About the family

Second wife: Brita Myklebust "Betsy" Engebrigtson, married 17 Jan 1880. ¶4

Children's baptisms 1877-1887: the Blue Earth congregation's church book (Faribault County; Digitalarkivet source 100624, index only, no days or godparents) names Lasse L. Erdahl as the father at seven baptisms: Laurits (1877) and Bernt Ludvik (1878) with his first wife, entered as 'Berthe T. Erdahl' and 'Martha Thomasdatter Erdahl'; then Laurits Martines (1880), Marthe Gjertine (1882), Josefine Berthine (1884), Anders Kornelius (1887) with his second wife, entered as 'Britha A.', 'Britha', 'Brithe f. Myklebust' (born Myklebust) and 'Brithe A.'. ¶8

Second wife, Brita = 'Betsy', resolved: the second wife's given name appears as Britha/Brithe at the baptisms (1880-1887), as 'Butte' 25 in 1885, as 'Berthe L' 36 in 1895 and as 'Betsy' in 1900. These are the same woman: the children follow from one record to the next (Laurits Martines 1880 = 'Laurie M' 4 = 'Lauris' 14; Marthe Gjertine 1882 = 'Marty G' 2 = 'Martha' 12; Josefine Berthine 1884 = 'Josephie' 10 = Josephine B., born December 1884, with 'Betsy' in 1900), and her ages agree (born about 1859-60). 'Betsy' was the usual American name for Brita, Berit or Beret. The 1884 baptism gives her maiden name as Myklebust, which agrees with the family's 'Brita Myklebust'. ¶12

Life story

Baptized 10 Nov 1850, Stryn parish. 1865 census: age 16 at home on Erdal (bnr 78). ¶1

Find a Grave gives his birthplace as 'Erdal, Førde kommune'; the baptism and 1865 census show Erdal in Stryn. ¶5

Brother Samuel Lasseson Erdahl (1844-1916), buried South Blue Earth Lutheran Cemetery, came to America first. ¶6

Emigrated 1866 via Quebec to his brother Samuel near Frost, MN; railroad work at Garner, Iowa; later farmed near Rake, Iowa. ¶2

Klokker (song leader) at Bethany Lutheran Church. ¶3

1885 MN census: on 5 June 1885 'Lewis L Erdahl', 34, born Norway, headed family 19 in the Town of Rome, Faribault County, with 'Butte', 25, born Norway, and the children 'Bentt L' 6, 'Laurie M' 4, 'Marty G' 2 and 'Jacob B' 0, all born in Minnesota. These are Berent, Laurits Martines, Marthe Gjertine and (inferred) Josefine Berthine. A 'Jacob' does not appear in any other record, and Josefine B., born December 1884, would have been 0, so 'Jacob B' is probably a misreading of 'Josefine B'. The same page has the households of 'Sain L Erdahl', 41 (probably his brother Samuel), and 'Rasmus M Erdahl', 40. ¶9

1895 MN census: his wife 'Berthe L Erdahl', 36, born Norway, is first on a page in Rome Township with Berent 16, Lauris 14, Martha 12, Josephie 10, Andrew 8, Ludvick 6 and Albert 3. Lasse himself is not on that page. Because he heads the household again in 1900, he was probably entered at the foot of the previous page, which has not been read. ¶10

1895 MN census, found: he is the last line (line 40) of Rome Township page 0612, enumerated about 20 June 1895, as 'Lous' (probably Louis) 'Eardahl', 45, male, white, born in Norway, a farmer employed 12 months, with both parents foreign-born. He had been resident 26 years in Minnesota and 26 years in the district. His wife 'Berthe L', with Berent and the other children, follows at the top of the next page (0613). The age is one year high for a birth in October 1850. The 26 years date his arrival in Minnesota to about 1869. That is three years after his emigration in 1866, which fits the family account that he first worked on the railroad at Garner, Iowa (inferred). ¶13

1900 U.S. census: 'Lewis G Erdahl', head, born October 1850 in Norway, was living in Rome Township, Faribault County, with his wife 'Betsy' and the children Josephine B. (born December 1884) and Ludwick B. (born January 1889). The birth month and year match his baptism record. The middle initial 'G' is probably an index error for L. ¶11

MN death certificate index: 'Lewis L Erdahl', died 7 Aug 1930, Faribault County, certificate 1930-MN-003988 (the only Erdahl/Erdal death in Minnesota in 1930). The index gives no parents or birthplace; MNHS publishes no free image of pre-2002 death certificates, so the certificate itself (MNHS copy $9) is still unread. ¶7

Emigration, negative: neither Lasse Lassesen Erdal nor his brother Samuel appears in the out-migration lists of the Stryn parish register for 1862 to 1867 or the Innvik parish register for 1865 to 1867, all read row by row.
