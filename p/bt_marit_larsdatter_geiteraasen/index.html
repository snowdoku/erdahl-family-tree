Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Probable mother of Ole Arntsen of Geiteråsen (1755-1811). She was buried from Gedderaas on 7 May 1797, entered as 'Enken, Fledf.' - the widow, on fledføring, meaning she had signed the farm over in return for board and lodging for life. A widow kept on the farm on those terms is, nearly always, the mother of the man then working it, and the man then working Geiteråsen was Ole Arntsen. ¶1

Records that disagree 1

Three things support the identification and nothing so far contradicts it: her age fits (born about 1712, she would have been 43 at Ole's birth in 1755); Ole named two of his daughters Marit; and no other household at Geiteråsen in those years could have produced a widow on retirement terms there. ¶2

Corrections 1

Correction (panteregister sweep): the land records contradict the argument that the widow on fledføring at Geiteråsen in 1797 must be Ole Arntsen's mother. The Stjørdal tinglag panteregister 1762-1800 (source 22318, image 87) shows Gedderaas as a tenant farm (it went to Cancelliraad Müst by the probate after his wife Lydia Müst, pantebok fol. 267) and lists 'Cancelliraad Müsts B[ygsel] B[rev] til Ole Arentsøn', fol. 431. That lease (pantebok C-2, fol. 431b, no. 4, dated 17 Mar 1787, the year of Ole's marriage) lets the farm to the soldier ('mandhaftig Soldat') Ole Arntsen as a new tenant; the farm had before been used ('brugt') by a man read as Iver Iversen (reading uncertain). The church-book index confirms an Iver of Gedderaas/Giederaas as father at baptisms on 17 Dec 1769, 22 Sep 1771, 7 Feb 1773 and 10 Aug 1777, and no Arnt there. So Ole came to Geiteråsen in 1787 as an incoming tenant, not as the heir of an Arnt on the farm, and the widow Marit Larsdatter (b. about 1712) on fledføring in 1797 belongs more probably to the Iver household (the former tenant's mother or widow, kept on the farm under the lease) than to Ole. Graded on her link to Ole Arntsen (see his page): lowered from Probable to Speculative. ¶6a

Searches that found nothing 3

Probate, negative: Stjør- og Verdal district court (sorenskriveri) kept the probate records for Stjørdal and Hegra. The alphabetical register of its probate book for 1796-1802 (skifteprotokoll 11) has no estate for a Marit Larsdatter at Geiteråsen (Gedderaas). The only Larsdatters it lists are at Borstadbroen[?] and Paasenes[?] in Stjørdal, Hembre-Haugen in Nordalen, and in Verdal. As a widow on fledføring she had already handed over the farm in return for her keep, so there was probably no estate to divide. No record yet names Ole Arntsen as her son. ¶4

Her husband, probate search: the registers of every Stjør- og Verdal probate book from 1742 to 1802 (nos. 3 to 11) were read under A. None lists an Arnt, Arent or Arn at Geiteråsen, or anywhere in Hegra. One Skatval man of the right age, Arent Joensen of Saltøen (buried 1773), is ruled out: the probate opened on 15 October 1773 names his widow as Marit Pedersdatter, not Larsdatter. ¶5

Negative: the end of the Geiteråsen lease of 17 Mar 1787 (pantebok C-2 fol. 432a, source 19756 view 129, top) contains only the usual tenant conditions (no husmenn or lodgers to be taken in without the owner's consent; dues to Trondheim at midsummer) and the owner's signature, Erich Müst. It has no føderåd clause and names no widow, so it does not identify Marit Larsdatter. Grade unchanged (Speculative). ¶7
