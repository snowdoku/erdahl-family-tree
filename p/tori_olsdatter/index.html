Life story

Baptism: Åmli church book 1796-1816, p.113: 'Tori', born 18 Jan 1816, father Ole Herjusen, mother Anne Nielsdr. ¶2

Sisters include Anne (bapt. 1814), Signe (bapt. 1817, of Gangaas, m. 1851 with 'Ole Herjusen' as bride's father), Sigri (1820) and Asborg (1827). Brothers: Nils (1822) and Herjus (1824). ¶3

Named 'Turi Olsd.' as mother of Uldvil (b. 1850 Tovdal) and Tollef (b. 1853 Omlid) at their Winnebago Co., Iowa confirmations in 1868 and 1871. Her death in America has not been found. ¶4

Emigrated with her family to Iowa between 1862 and 1868; named as Tellef's mother in his 1871 Winnebago confirmation. ¶5

Emigration (with husband Torjus Tellefsen): the Tovdal out-migration list (clerk's book, source 9929), under the year 1862, rows 4-11, records 'Torjus Tellefsen 46 Leilænding' (tenant farmer), 'Thorid Olsdatter 48 Leilændingskone', and children Ragnel 18, Anne 14, Ulvild 11, Tellef Torjusen 9, Ole 7 and Thorid 4, all to 'Amerika'. No day, month or attest is given. The children's ages fit a departure after Jul 1862. Torjus at 46 and Thorid jr. at 4 fit spring 1863, which agrees with Tellef's 1900 census immigration year of 1863. The year is therefore 1862 per the register, possibly 1863 (inferred). Listed immediately before them, also to America, were the lodgers Gunder Gundersen T[?]edt, 64, Torborg Olsdatter, 63, and Kari Gundersdatter, 29. The family is not in the Åmli main-parish out-migration list for 1858-1869 (source 855), which was read in full. ¶6

Corrections 1

CORRECTION: her father's name is Ole HERJUSSEN (Hergjus/Herjus, an old Agder given name). The 'Horsusen' in the Tovdal marriage index is a misreading. Other indexes of the same family read Horjusen, Hørjusen, Herjusen, Horiussen and Hergiusen. ¶1
