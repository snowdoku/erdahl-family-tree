Life story

Baptism: 'Lucretia, daughter of William Ouldham, baptised fourteenth January 1600 [1600/1]' at All Saints, Derby, by Edward Bennett, minister (certificate from the register printed by J. G. Hunt, NEHGR 111 (1957): 242). This supersedes the negative FreeREG search noted earlier: the entry exists but is not in FreeREG. Her parents are entered as William Ouldham (Probable) and Philippa Sowter (Speculative); her own grade, as wife of Jonathan Brewster, is unchanged. ¶9

Baptism read on the image: All Saints, Derby, 'Bap. Lucretia filia Willimi Ouldam bap. 14 die' [January 1600/1], FamilySearch image 75 of 772 (ark 3:1:S3HY-DTM3-N2X). Her brother Johannes, son of William Ouldam, was baptised there 14 Jul 1592 (index 1:1:NGWZ-L5B); she is the only Lucretia in the Derbyshire indexes 1580-1615. ¶10

Note (2 Nov 2026): her parents' link stays Probable after Audit 11. A search of her brother John Oldham's estate records (Mass. Bay Court of Assistants 2:63; Public Records of the Colony of Connecticut 1:4-6, 43-44) and the Lichfield calendar found no record naming the Derby family. Circumstantial support only: Thomas Oldham of All Saints, Derby, had sons John (bp 1622) and Thomas (bp 1624), matching the two boys, John 12 and Thomas 10 (1635), whom Bradford calls John Oldham's 'kinsmen' (Stratton; Pope). ¶11

Note (2 Nov 2026): the All Saints, Derby, baptisms of John and Thomas, sons of Thomas Oldham, read on the images, are 9 Feb 1622/3 ('John Oldham sonne of Thomas') and 23 Jun 1624 ('Thomas Oldame sonne of Thomas'), not 9 Apr 1622 and 23 Aug 1624; they name the father only. The dates fit the boys John 12 and Thomas 10 of 1635, John Oldham's 'kinsmen', but no record links Thomas Oldham to William Ouldham, so her parents stay Probable. ¶12

Marriage: 'Johnnathan Brewster marryed Lucretia Oldam. of Darby. the 10th Aprill. 1624. who had children: William Mary Johnnathan Ruth Beniamin Elizabeth Grace Hanna' (Jonathan Brewster's family record, The Mayflower Descendant 1:8). 'Darby' is as written; it is probably Derby, England (inference). No birth, parents or death are given in the record. ¶1

Widowhood (compiled history): Caulkins quotes a release of 14 February 1661/2 in which John Picket gives up his interest as trustee of the Brewster estate, stipulating only 'that my mother-in-law, Mrs. Brewster, the late wife of my father Mr. Jonathan Brewster, shall have a full and competent means out of his estate during her life'. This shows Lucretia was living, as a widow, in February 1661/2. Caulkins describes her as always given the title Mrs. and often recorded as nurse, doctress or midwife and as a witness to wills (History of New London, 1852, p. 276; the original deed has not been read). Her death date was not found in this source. ¶3

Widow and midwife: after her husband's death she witnessed a New London indenture in April 1662 and, as 'Lucresia Brewster, midwife', the will of Richard Hartley on 5 August 1662 (Caulkins, History of New London, pp. 279-280; the Manwaring digest of Connecticut probate, vol. I p. 123, abstracts the same will with 'Mrs Lucresiah Brewster, midwife'). ¶4

Burial (probable): the diary of Thomas Minor of Stonington records 'the.5. day of march 78-79 mrs bruster was buried the 12. day ffather avery was buried', that is 5 March 1678/9 (printed transcription, 1899, p. 153). The entry gives no first name. Caulkins (History of Norwich, p. 212) identifies this Mrs. Brewster with Lucretia, and a New London town clerk's memo she quotes dates Christopher Avery's death 'near the death of mother Brewster' (History of New London, p. 331), which fits the diary's 'father Avery' buried a week later. Caulkins also says no record of Lucretia's death is to be found in the town books. The identification is an inference from compiled history, not proved. ¶5

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Confidence: Confirmed as the mother of Mary Brewster (born Plymouth 16 April 1627, married John Turner the Elder 1645), on her husband's own family record, which lists Mary among the children of Jonathan and Lucretia. Her descent to Raymond Hayter depends on the Speculative link Mary Brewster -> Ruth (Turner) Prince (see Mary Brewster): if Ruth's mother was another wife of John Turner, Lucretia Oldham is not an ancestor. ¶2

Records that disagree 1

Conflict (minor): Caulkins (History of New London, p. 277) mentions a 'Lucretia' as a daughter of Jonathan Brewster in 1627; Jonathan's own family record (The Mayflower Descendant 1:8) lists no daughter Lucretia, only his wife. The family record is preferred. ¶6

Searches that found nothing 2

Baptism search (negative): FreeREG has no baptism of a Lucretia, Lucrece or Lucresia Oldham (also Oldam, Ouldham) 1585-1610 in any county, and no marriage of hers 1620-1625; its coverage of the Derby town parishes is very thin, so this rules little out. Leads only: John Oldham of Allestree, whose wife Lucie was buried 10 October 1596; William Ouldam married Philippa Sowter at Derby All Saints on 17 November 1588. The Derby and Allestree registers themselves are the next record. ¶7

Probate search (negative): Manwaring's Digest of Early Connecticut Probate Records vol. II covers Hartford County only and has no Lucretia or 'Mrs. Brewster'; a New London estate of 1678-1680, if one exists, would be in the New London probate district files (Connecticut State Library), not in Manwaring. ¶8
