Life story

Baptism (index): 'Malena', daughter of Peder of Hegge, baptised in Skatval on 25 Feb 1770. Fits her age 31 in Feb 1801 and 'Malena Pedersd. Hegge' at her 1791 marriage. Father linked at Probable as Peder Arentsen of Hegge (bur. 1774); her brothers Jørgen and Ejner Pedersen Hegge were godfathers to her first son in 1791. ¶5

Baptism scan read (Stjørdal ministerialbok 1755-1780, p. 189, Skatval, Dominica Esto mihi = 25 Feb 1770): '1 Peder Hegges Barn Malena, Test: Mali Hegge, Berit[?] ibid, Karen Alstad, Hans[?] Hegge, Jørn ibid.' No mother, no patronymic. ¶6a

Baptised 25 Feb 1770 (Esto mihi), Skatval baptism book p. 189, serial 1, together with Marit (serial 2, pd00000042582147), whose father row is also 'Peder, Hegge'. Same father, date and page with consecutive serials fits twins, but the index records no twin remark and the scan gives different sponsors, so this is not proved. The third child on the page (Niels) is son of a different Peder, of Floegierdet. Neither Malena nor Marit is in the 1801 Hegge census block. Read (index only). ¶7

Mother added at Probable from the Skatval bygdebok (vol. 2, 50/1 Hegge, canvas 206 right): Malena, who married Ola Pedersen of Lille Valstad, is listed among the children of Peder Arntsen and the widow Mali Nilsdtr. (m. 28.11.1759). ¶9a

Marriage and home farm: She married Ole Pedersen of Walstad on 24 October 1791 as 'Pigen Malena Pedersd. Hegge', an unmarried woman of the farm Hegge in Skatval (Stjørdal 1781-1797, marriages 1791 p. 560 no. 43). Her father was therefore a Peder, probably of Hegge; he is not named. Her son Peter was baptised two months later, with Jørgen and Ejner Hegge among the godparents. His baptism names only the father, so her motherhood of Peter stays Probable. ¶4

1801 census: Malena Pedersdatter, 31, is listed as 'Hans kone' (his wife) of Ole Pedersen at Valstad Lille, Størdalen. She is in her first marriage, as he is. The children Peter (9), Malena (6), Mali (3) and Peder (1) are listed as 'Deres børn' (their children). ¶1

Motherhood of Peter Olsen: Rated Probable. The 1801 census lists Peter as the couple's child and states that both parents are in their first marriage, and she would have been about 21-23 at his birth. However, no baptism naming her as his mother has been read, because his birth in about 1791 is before the indexed baptism books. His baptism would confirm it. ¶2

Death: Malene Pedersdatter, kaarenke (a widow living on a kår, a retirement agreement from the farm) of Lillevalstad, died on 16 February 1847 and was buried on 26 February 1847, aged 79, born 1768 (Stjørdal/Nedre Stjørdal 1845-1858, burials p. 473 no. 30). She is identified with Ole Pedersen's wife by her name and farm, and by her status as a retired widow after his death in 1832. The 1801 census age of 31 implies a birth in about 1770, two years later than the burial age implies. ¶3

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: Confirmed, graded on her link to her son Peter Olsen Hvalstad (see his page), not on her own parentage (Probable). The record naming the relationship is the 1801 census of Valstad Lille, Størdalen: Ole Pedersen, 43, bonde og gaardbeboer, and 'Hans kone' Malena Pedersdatter, 31, both in their first marriage, with Peter (9), Malena (6), Mali (3) and Peder (1) as 'Deres børn' (their children). Continuity: the tree's Peter Olsen gave his age as 36½, birth year 1791 and birthplace Lillehvalstad at his 1828 marriage and farmed Lille Hvalstad for life; Malena married Ole Pedersen of Walstad on 24 Oct 1791 and died a widow on Lillevalstad in 1847. Same farm throughout, no rival: both parents were in their first marriage in 1801, so Peter cannot be a stepchild, and he was born two months after their marriage. ¶8
