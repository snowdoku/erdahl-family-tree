Life story

Her own Social Security application exists twice over, under both names, and both copies name her parents: "BARBARA ANN EIDEM, b. 9 May 1926, LACQUIPARLE, Minnesota, father JOHN P EIDEM, mother OLINE G SLIND" (cycle 1946) and "BARBARA EIDEM KYLLO, b. 9 May 1926, SIOUX FALLS, SOUTH DAKOTA, father JOHN P EIDEM, mother OLINE G SLIND" (cycle 1955). The two give different birthplaces; I keep Lac qui Parle, from the earlier filing, which agrees with every other record of the family, and note Sioux Falls as a discrepancy that is probably a later clerk's error or a hospital of birth across the state line. ¶13

Censuses: in her father's Mehurin household in 1930 (aged three, indexed 'Barbara') and 1940 (indexed 'Borbara A Eidem', b. abt 1927) - by 1940 she and her brother Peter were the only children left at home. ¶7

1945 South Dakota state census: 'Barbara Ann Eidem, b. 1926, Lac Qui Parle Co Minnesota, residence Codington County, South Dakota'. At nineteen she had crossed into South Dakota and was living in Codington County (Watertown). This is the only record so far of where she was between 1940 and her marriage to Cle Kyllo. ¶8

Social Security application (SS-5), filed July 1946: BARBARA ANN EIDEM, born 9 May 1926, place of birth given as 'LACQUIPARLE', Minnesota. FATHER: JOHN P EIDEM. MOTHER: OLINE G SLIND. NARA AAD, NUMIDENT Application (SS-5) Files (Last Names E-G), rid 421654. ¶3

The contradictory birthplace, resolved as far as the evidence allows. Her 1946 card says Lac qui Parle County, Minnesota; her 1955 card, which she signed and which is in every other respect the fuller of the two, says Sioux Falls, South Dakota. I carry SIOUX FALLS because it is the specific statement (a named city, not a county) and because it is the later and more complete filing, but the Minnesota reading is recorded here and not suppressed. The same Minnesota/South Dakota wobble shows up elsewhere in this exact community: Bernice of the Paul Eidem family has one card saying 'MARIETTA DEU, South Dakota' and another saying 'MARIETTA LAC, Minnesota' for the same birth. Marietta village sits on the state line with Deuel County, South Dakota directly across it, and people there habitually gave either state. Her birth is therefore 'Probable' as to place and certain as to date. ¶5

Marriage: OLE BERNHART KYLLO, 24 SEPTEMBER 1950. Her gravestone at Selbu Cemetery, Whitman County, Washington reads "Barbara Eidem Kyllo, b. May 09 1926 d. Nov 14 1998, s/w & w/o Ole Bernhart m. Sep 24 1950" (reference hrp-cem-whi-selbu_0087); his, on the same stone, reads "Ole Bernhart Kyllo, b. Jan 05 1919 d. Feb 11 2005". ¶10

SO ONE OF OLINE'S EIGHT CHILDREN FOLLOWED HER UNCLE WEST. Barbara married into a Kyllo family of thirteen memorials at Selbu Cemetery, Whitman County, headed by Henry P. Kyllo (b. 17 Aug 1876, d. 15 Apr 1954) and Ranghild Kyllo (b. 18 Feb 1883, d. 4 May 1966), and she is buried about thirty yards from her uncle Ole G. Slind. Kyllo is a Selbu farm name and also the name of a Lac qui Parle family, so her marriage joined the two American Selbu colonies. ¶12

Her married name was KYLLO. A second, later NUMIDENT record — a replacement card, August 1955, signed by her — is filed under 'BARBARA EIDEM KYLLO', keeps the former name 'BARBARA ANN EIDEM' in the OTHER NAME fields, repeats FATHER: JOHN P EIDEM and MOTHER: OLINE G SLIND, and gives her place of birth as SIOUX FALLS, SOUTH DAKOTA. NARA AAD, NUMIDENT Application (SS-5) Files (Last Names K-L), rid 2637622. This is the first record found that gives her a married surname. ¶4

SHE DIED ON 14 NOVEMBER 1998 AND IS BURIED IN THE SELBU COLONY IN WASHINGTON. The tree carried her as living with no death date. Washington Dept. of Health death certificate DE_1998042841, Whitman County, county file no. 144: "BARBARA EIDEM KYLLO, death date 11/14/1998, death county Whitman, residence county Whitman, age 72. FATHERLASTNAME: EIDEM. MOTHERMAIDENNAME: SLIND." The DOH Death Index duplicate (reference 31D1219E-786A-624B-9889-D175A56C500A) gives the same date and county. ¶9

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Confirmed as a daughter of John P. Eidem and Oline G. Slind, and her married name and 1955 home are now known: her mother's October 1955 obituary names 'Mrs. Cle Kyllo (Barbara) of LaCrosse, Washington' among the eight surviving children. This settles the Kyllo marriage and places her in Whitman County, Washington — the same corner of Washington as her uncle Ole Slind of Colfax. ¶6

Corrections 1

Correction: her mother Oline's 1955 obituary names this daughter as "Barbara (Mrs. Cle Kyllo)". "Cle" is a misreading or a mis-setting of OLE - Ole Bernhart Kyllo. The husband is now identified by name, birth date, death date and burial. ¶11

Searches that found nothing 1

She is treated as possibly living and her detail is kept minimal: born 1926, she is absent from the Social Security Death Master File, but that file was frozen in November 2011 and so proves nothing about a death after that date. ¶2
