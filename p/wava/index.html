Life story

Baptized at Beaver Creek Lutheran Church; confirmed at Bethel Lutheran Brethren Church, both rural Joice. ¶1

Baptism (Beaver Creek, 1922): 'Wava Laurene', parents Theodor Ramsey and Wilma Nelson. Siblings: Beryl Phyllis Sand (1918-2015), Dean Ulysses (1927-1927), Lynn M. (1929-2010). ¶3

1930 census: Wava, aged 7, in her father Theodore Ramsey's household in Fertile Township, Worth County, Iowa, with her mother Wilma, her sister Beryl and her brother Lynn (IAGenWeb transcription, also summarised on her father's record). ¶4

1940 census: Wava, aged 17, still in her parents' household (family 31) in Fertile Township; her father owned the farm; her sister Beryl, 22, was a rural school teacher and her brother Lynn was 11 (IAGenWeb transcription). ¶5

Education: She attended Larson country school, did ninth grade at Augustana Academy in Sioux Falls, South Dakota, and tenth to twelfth grades at the Lutheran Brethren Schools (LBS) in Fergus Falls, Minnesota, graduating in 1939 in the same class as Palmer Valder; she then had one year of college at Augsburg in Minneapolis (Wava Valder obituary, 2018; Palmer Valder obituary, 2014). ¶6

Music: Her obituary describes her as a pianist, organist and singer who began playing and singing in her home church as a girl and continued all her life, able to play any song by ear in any key, and much in demand as an accompanist at LBS (Wava Valder obituary, 2018). ¶7

Marriage: She married Palmer Valder of Ferndale, Washington, at 8 p.m. on Saturday 14 February 1942 (Valentine's Day) at Zoar Lutheran church in Lake Mills, Iowa. Pastor E. L. Reisem read the double-ring service and her father gave her away; the reception for the bridal party and immediate family was at her parents' home (Lake Mills Graphic, 18 Feb 1942, p. 6). Palmer's obituary explains that they married at Zoar Lutheran Brethren Church because their home church, Bethel Lutheran Brethren at Joice, had burned down. ¶8

Wedding party: Her attendants were her sister Beryl Ramsey and the groom's sister Marie Valder; the groom was attended by his brother Otto Valder and Rev. Joseph Levang; the junior ushers were her brother Lynn Ramsey and her cousin Donald Ramsey. Her cousin Orlette Amundson played hymns before the ceremony, and the waitresses at the reception were her cousins Lois Tweed, Iona May Tweed and Esther Rusley (Lake Mills Graphic, 18 Feb 1942, p. 6). ¶9

Wedding photograph: The same page printed her bridal photograph beside that of Mrs. James Nelson (the former Helen Dahlby), married within the same ten days; the caption calls the two brides lifelong friends, both born and reared in the Joice community (Lake Mills Graphic, 18 Feb 1942, p. 6). ¶10

Residences after marriage: Palmer served in the Army Air Corps from 1942 until 1945. They then lived in Cedar Falls, Iowa, for a year while he attended Iowa State Teachers College, and afterwards in Waterloo, Iowa, where he sold furniture. In 1957 they moved with their three daughters to Minneapolis, where they lived about 35 years and belonged to Ebenezer Lutheran Brethren Church. After retiring they lived for some years at Joice, attending Bethel LB again, and in their nineties lived at Pioneer Cottages / PioneerCare in Fergus Falls (Palmer Valder obituary, 2014; Wava Valder obituary, 2018). ¶11

1950 census: on 1 April 1950 Wava L. Valder, 27, born Iowa, was in the household of her husband Palmer M. Valder, 30, born China, a travelling salesman for a musical instrument company, in Otter Tail County, Minnesota (ED 56-35), next door to his parents' household, with their two daughters (names only, living). Read from the census image by line 3 (patch 002_vld_009_). ¶15

Work: She worked at General Mills in Minneapolis and, after retiring from there, for several years at the Billy Graham Association (Wava Valder obituary, 2018). ¶12

Death and funeral: She died on Wednesday 17 January 2018 at PioneerCare in Fergus Falls, aged 95. Funerals were held at 11 a.m. on Monday 22 January 2018 at Bethel Lutheran Church, Fergus Falls, and at 1 p.m. on Wednesday 24 January 2018 at Bethel Lutheran Brethren Church, Joice, with interment in the Bethel Lutheran Brethren Church Cemetery, Joice (Wava Valder obituary, 2018). ¶14
