About the family

Mother of Jonathan (Probable, not Confirmed): the 1609 affidavit shows Mary as William's wife and Jonathan as his son in one household; her age (about 24 in August 1593) fits Jonathan's birth at Scrooby on 12 Aug 1593; no record names any earlier wife of William; Bradford lists 'Mary, his wife' with sons Love and Wrestling on the Mayflower in 1620, with 'the rest of his children' following later. No record read calls her Jonathan's mother, and the marriage date is unknown, so the link stays Probable. ¶2

Life story

Leiden affidavit, 25 June 1609 (Getuigenisboek/Groot Procuratieboek, Leiden, translated by Dexter p. 505): 'There appeared before the undersigned sheriffs William Brewster, Englishman, aged about forty-two years; Mary Brewster, his wife, aged about forty years; and Jonathan Brewster, his son, aged about sixteen years', testifying about a bale of English cloth carried to 'their house' in St Ursula's lane. Birth ABT 1569 inferred from 'about forty' in June 1609. ¶1

Death: 'Mary Brewster the wyfe of william Brewster' died at Plymouth on 17 April 1627 (Jonathan Brewster's family record, The Mayflower Descendant 1:7, as already cited on William Brewster). Her maiden surname is unknown; it is not given by Bradford or in any record read. ¶3

No grade change; graded on her link to her son Jonathan Brewster: the colony court of 5 June 1644 granted 'letters of administracon of all the goods and cattells of Mr Wiliam Brewster, deceased' to Jonathan Brewster and Love Brewster, who exhibited the inventory on oath (Records of the Colony of New Plymouth 2:72-73, OCR text read). The entry names the two sons only; it does not mention a wife or their mother (Mary had died in 1627). Dexter (England and Holland of the Pilgrims, pp. 514-515) finds no Leiden record of Jonathan's marriage, only the burial of his wife on 10 May 1619 and his citizenship in 1617 as 'Jonathan William's son', so no Leiden betrothal entry exists to name his mother. No record found names Mary as Jonathan's mother; she stays Probable on the 1609 Leiden affidavit (Mary 'his wife', about 40, and Jonathan 'his son', about 16, in one household). ¶5

Maiden name reviewed: still unknown, and no parents are entered. (1) The Wentworth theory: J. G. Hunt, TAG 41 (1965): 1-5, 63, argued from circumstance that she was Mary, daughter of Thomas Wentworth of Scrooby manor and Grace Gascoigne. The American Ancestors Mayflower sketch reports that Rubincam and others rejected it, and that Hunt himself later gave it up. (2) The Wyrrall theory: Hunt's pamphlet Of Mary Brewster (1984) rests on a will's bequest to 'Mary Butho', which he read as a garbled 'Brewster'. E. A. Stratton reviewed it negatively (Detroit Society for Genealogical Research Magazine 48:135-36, 1985), and Hunt issued a supplement. (3) Other names in circulation (Love, Stubbe, Butler, Smythe) come with no evidence. The Great Migration and American Ancestors sketches both treat the surname as unproven. No marriage record has been found in the Nottinghamshire registers or bishop's transcripts. A Wentworth family did live at Scrooby: the York index (YAS RS 11) lists 'Wentworthe, Christoffer, Scrobye (Notts.), gent.', will 10 Dec 1550, and 'Wentforth, William, Bawtrie strete, par. Harworthe (Notts.)', 4 May 1546. These only show that the family was present, which is a lead and nothing more. Grade unchanged (Probable, graded on her link to Jonathan Brewster). ¶6a

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

Maiden name (negative): Dexter's England and Holland of the Pilgrims (1905), which prints the 1609 Leiden affidavit naming 'Mary his wife', about 40, gives no maiden name and does not discuss one; the Scrooby passages mention Thomas Wentworth only as the occupant of the manor house, not as a relative of Brewster. Maiden names offered for her in compiled works were not read in any primary record by this project, so none is entered and no parents are added. A marriage record (about 1590-1593, probably in Nottinghamshire or adjoining counties, before Jonathan's birth at Scrooby in August 1593) would be needed. ¶4

Marriage search, negative (FamilySearch indexes, 2 Nov 2026): no marriage of a William Brewster 1585-1596 is indexed in Nottinghamshire or Yorkshire. The only English William Brewster marriages indexed for those years are at Timberland, Lincs (Elizabeth Tompson 1594; Isate Gyllate 1596), Tixover, Rutland (Isabel Pitts, 13 Nov 1592) and Ab Kettleby, Leics (Dorothy Blanklye, 1589), none with a Mary. The Scrooby, Babworth, Sutton-cum-Lound and Mattersey registers for the 1590s are not indexed on FamilySearch at all (a Nottinghamshire-wide Brewster search 1558-1610 returns no register entry). Her maiden name stays unknown. ¶7a
