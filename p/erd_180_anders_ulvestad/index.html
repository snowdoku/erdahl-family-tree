About the family

Husband of Christi Anfindsdatter and father of her nine children, all named Andersen/Andersdatter in her probate of 14 Apr 1761 (Ytre Sogn 7b fol. 520a, No. 18), which treats her as a widow. Known only from that patronymic and the farm. Very probably the Anders Trulsen of Ulvestad whose probate is indexed for 1750-1756 (index pa00000000522152, not read): a grandson is named Truls (Truls Tollachsen, 10 in 1761), which fits a Truls in the family. Reading that probate would settle his patronymic and his death. ¶1

Life story

Name corrected from Anders /Ulvestad/ (farm only) to Anders /Truelsen/, the patronymic written in his probate. Probate: Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll 7a fol. 162b, No. 12, held at Ulvestad 30 Apr and 1-2 May 1753 'efter... afgangne Sal. [Hæders]mand Anders Truelsen Ulvestad' between his widow 'den ærlige Dannekone Christi Anfindsdatter', with her son and lawful guardian Anbiørn Andersen Esse, and the children they had together in marriage: eldest son Anbiørn Andersen Esse, present; second son Hans Andersen [Slaveir?], of age and present; 'Tredie Søn Anfind Andersen, myndig og nærværende'; fourth son Lars Andersen, under age (umyndig) and present; eldest daughter Synneve Andersdatter, married to Tollach Johansen Meel; second daughter Brithe Andersdatter, married to Ole Olsen Esse; third daughter Randi Andersdatter, 24; fourth daughter Mari Andersdatter, 22; fifth daughter Ingeleif Andersdatter, 20. Present: the lensmann Ole Pedersen Wangsnes; appraisers Anders Siursen Sværen and Torald Olsen Eiden. Son Anfind's share is listed on fol. 163a as 'Sønnen Anfind Andersen'. ¶2

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: Confirmed as father of Anfind Andersen. The 1753 probate names 'Tredie Søn Anfind Andersen' as the son of Anders Truelsen and his widow Christi Anfindsdatter; it is the same family as her own probate of 1761 (the same nine children in the same order), and the Farbroder Ambiørn Andersen Ulvestad of the 1768 Feten probate is the eldest son Anbiørn of this one. His patronymic, Truelsen, is now read from the record; his death falls before 30 Apr 1753 (not 1761 as first entered). ¶3
