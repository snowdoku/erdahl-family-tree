About the family

Wife of Jacob Knodel; she was the informant on his 1980 BC death registration, signing 'Sarah M. Knodel', and that registration gives her maiden name as Eberle. Find a Grave (memorial 219751850) gives her birth as 7 August 1916 at Mendham, Saskatchewan, and her death as 20 February 2009 at Oliver; she is buried next to Jacob. It names her parents as Simon Eberle (1882-1931) and Maria Hildebrandt (1884-1933), and her sisters as Emelia 'Millie' (1907-1984, wife of Gottlieb Knodel), Frieda (1909-1959) and Elsie Elizabeth Kelley (1922-2012). Two Knodel brothers therefore married two Eberle sisters. ¶1

Her parents, Simon Eberle (20 August 1882 - 7 November 1931) and Mary Hildebrandt (3 March 1884 - 5 April 1933), both died at Eatonia, Saskatchewan, and are buried in Eatonia Cemetery, as are her husband's parents. A Find a Grave biography of her youngest sister Elsie Kelley says that both parents were born in Bessarabia. A 1959 obituary abstract for her sister Frieda Eberle names among the surviving sisters 'Mrs. Jack Knodel, Oliver' and 'Mrs. Gus Knodel, Dawson Creek, BC'. ¶3

Parents: Simon Eberle and Maria Hildebrandt (Find a Grave family links and her own memorial text; she was born in Saskatchewan in 1916, after their 1910 emigration from Pfeilsdorf). Probable from the memorial; consistent with the census. ¶4

Life story

Context: Emilia was born in Romania in 1907 and Sarah at Mendham, Saskatchewan in 1916, so the Eberle family, from Romania, emigrated between 1907 and 1916 and settled in the Mendham-Eatonia district a decade and more before the Knodels came. They are likely chain-migration contacts, and possibly from the same village. ¶2
