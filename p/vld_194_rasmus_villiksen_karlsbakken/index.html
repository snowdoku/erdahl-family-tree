About the family

Father of Inger Rasmusdatter (see her page), Probable; graded on his link to Inger. (1) Borgund baptism 24 Jul 1707: Inger, daughter of Rasmus Vilichss. of Karlsbakken (index pd00000001504963), and 7 Apr 1709: Marrete, daughter of Rasmus Vigleichss. of Karlsbakken (pd00000001505226); a third child was baptised to him on 2 Aug 1711 (pd00000001505480). (2) Inger Rasmusd., Pige, of Carlsbachen married the widower Sivert Ingebrigtsen of Diub on 1 Jan 1744 (pv00000002823274). (3) The Giske farm history (Borgund og Giske bd. 3, nb.no ff134a2c9670bf6b5cbe848b7a7f9404, canvas 496) calls her 'Inger Rasmusdtr. Karlsbakk (1707-80)', and on canvas 154 has a 'Marit Rasmusdtr. Karlsbakk (1709-55)' married 1739: the two birth years match the 1707 and 1709 baptisms exactly. ¶1a

Life story

1701 census (index): Karlsbache, Rasmus, b. 1656, leaseholder (opsider/leilending), with sons Mathias Rasmusen (1691), Arne Rasm. (1695) and Villig (1699, the name of his own father, Villik); the son 'Willich' (b. 1695 in the index) of Karlsbachen was buried 20 Dec 1705. His first wife Inger Arnesdatter (b. about 1651) was buried at Borgund 11 Jun 1699 and her probate was registered in 1700 ('Rasmus Williksens hustru', pa00000000781310). He remarried; 'Marrete', wife of Rasmus of Karlsbachen, was churched on 26 Dec 1700, 27 Aug 1702, 11 Nov 1703 and 18 Sep 1707 (eight weeks after Inger's baptism), so Marrete was probably Inger's mother; not added, because a Martha was churched as wife of a Rasmus of Karlsbakken on 12 May 1709, five weeks after Marrete's baptism, and the two names may be one woman or two. ¶2

Bridge (index): Borgund marriage 1 Jan 1744: groom Sifvert Ingebrigtss., widower ('Enchemand'), of Diub (Djup), and bride Inger Rasmusd., unmarried ('Pige'), of Carlsbachen (Karlsbakken) (pv00000002823274). So the Inger Rasmusdatter who became Sjur (Sivert) Ingebrigtsen's second wife was living, unmarried, at Karlsbakken, the farm where Rasmus Vilichss. had his daughter Inger baptised on 24 Jul 1707. Graded on his link to Inger (see her page): the match is patronymic plus farm across 37 years with no record in between, so he stays Probable. ¶5

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
