About the family

Child: Gunleik Alvsen (see his page), later of Midbøe Nordre, Nissedal. Probable other sons: Haavor (Halvor), baptised 17 Dec 1758, and John Alfsen, b. about 1755, householder at Thoe in 1801. ¶3

Child buried 1764: the Drangedal burial index for Lady Day (Annunciation) 1764 names the deceased as Aasil Alfsdatter of Toe, with 'Alf' of Toe as her father (role 'far'); church book 1697-1767, burials 1764, p. 26 (index pg00000008555036; scan not read). This settles the 1764 row as the burial of a daughter, not of Alf's father. Gunleik Alvsen named his own daughter Aasild (baptised 1805), which fits naming after a dead sister; that is an inference only. ¶8

Wife (Probable): Tore Torkildsdatter (see her page), b. about 1737, a widow in her first marriage living at Thoe in the 1801 census as the mother (Mandens moder) of the householder John Alfsen. As she had only one husband and her son is an Alfsen of Thoe, her husband was an Alf of Thoe, taken to be this Alf. Inferred, not stated. ¶9

First wife's death: Aasil Jonsdatter Toe was buried on 17 Mar 1764, aged 36, and their daughter Aasil, 3 weeks old, on 25 Mar 1764 (burials p. 26). ¶13

Father (Speculative): Halvor Alfsen Dale, buried in Drangedal on 14 Dec 1759 aged 67 (Halvor Alfsen). See his record for the reasons and what is missing. ¶17

Wife's probate, 2 Oct 1764 at Toe in Tørdal: 'efterlevende Mand Alv Halvorsen' (surviving husband) of Aasel Jonsdatter, with their four sons Jon 11, Torgen 9, Halvor 6 and Gunlich 3; he himself, as 'Alf Toe', was guardian for Gunlich. He took half the estate, laid out in 8 Bet. of Toe with the buildings. Bamble skifteprotokoll I 6b, fol. 455a-457a. ¶18

Father (Probable): Halvor Alfsen Dahle of Dale in Tørdal (see his page), buried 14 Dec 1759 aged 67. His probate of 20 Feb 1760 names a son 'Alf Halvorsen', of age, his second and youngest son. That Alf is taken to be Alf of Toe because (1) Alf Toe was 'Alf Halvorsen Dale' at his 1753 marriage; (2) the guardian of Alf Toe's eldest son in 1764 was Jon Dahle, matching Halvor's eldest son Joen Halvorsen Dahle, the boy's paternal uncle; (3) Halvor Torredal, a cautioner at Alf's 1753 betrothal, had been married to Halvor's eldest daughter Groe; (4) Tov Traae, a godfather of Gunleik in 1761, was the husband of Halvor's daughter Live; (5) Alf named sons Halvor and Jon. No record states that Alf of Toe is Halvor's son, so this is Probable. ¶21a

Life story

Bynames: he appears as 'Alf Halvorsen Dale' at his first marriage (1753), as 'Alf Halvorsen Toe' as a cautioner in 1758 and at his second marriage (1766), and as 'Alf Toe' or 'Alf Taes/Toes' at the baptisms of his sons in 1758 and 1761 and the burial of his daughter in 1764. So he moved from the farm Dale to Toe (Thoe) between 1753 and 1758. The 1758 and 1766 entries name him in full, which confirms that the 'Alf Toe' of the baptisms is an Alf Halvorsen. ¶10

First marriage: betrothed on Whit Tuesday 1753 and married on 2 Jul 1753 in Drangedal, as Alf Halvorsen Dale, to Aasil Jonsdatter of Steensland (see her page); cautioners Halvor Torredal and Jørgen Steensland. Drangedal ministerialbok 1697-1767, p. 18. ¶11

Baptisms of sons: as 'Alf Taes/Toes' he had a son Haavor baptised in Drangedal on 17 Dec 1758 (p. 22), and as 'Alf Toe' a son Gundlick (Gunleik) on 23 Aug 1761 (p. 41), Drangedal ministerialbok 1697-1767. The mother is not named in either entry. The farm name is an uncertain reading (Toe, Tae, possibly Foe); the 1801 census spells the Drangedal farm Thoe. ¶1

A 1764 Drangedal burial row (index pg00000008555036, Lady Day 1764) names 'Alf', role 'far', residence Toe. In this index the role 'far' marks the father of the deceased, so the row most likely records the burial of an unnamed child of Alf of Toe. It has also been suggested that it is the burial of Alf's own father; the index row alone does not show that. The scan was not read. ¶4

Second marriage: on the 3rd Easter Day (1 Apr 1766) the widower (Enkke-Mand) Alf Halvorsen Toe was betrothed in Drangedal to the maiden (Pigen) Tore Torkildsdatter (see her page); cautioners Torr Fraae and Joen Dale. Married 12 Jul 1766. Drangedal ministerialbok 1697-1767, p. 43. ¶14

John Alfsen and the 1801 census: John Alfsen, 46 (b. about 1755), householder at Thoe in 1801, is recorded with Tore Torkildsdatter, 64, widow in her first marriage, as 'Mandens moder' (the householder's mother). Tore married Alf only in 1766, so if John's age is right he was Aasil's son and 'moder' means stepmother. Otherwise his age is overstated by at least 11 years. The scan of the 1801 census was not read; John's baptism (about 1754-55, Drangedal baptisms) would settle it. ¶16

Burial age: the Digitalarkivet record page for his burial (pg00000001577529) gives 'Alf Halvorsen', of Tho, buried 1 Feb 1801, aged 73, Drangedal church book 1768-1814, burials 1801, p. 296. Age 73 gives birth about 1727-28, matching the birth field ABT 1728. ¶22

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 3

Confidence lowered to Speculative: the Drangedal baptisms for late 1759 to Dec 1760 (7848 canvases 200–203) were not read for a rival Gunleik, and his census and burial ages point to ~1760. No record yet ties him to Drangedal. Raise to Probable if those canvases show no rival. ¶6

Confidence raised from Speculative back to Probable: the Drangedal baptisms for Nov 1759 to Dec 1760 (source 7848, canvases 200-203, pp. 28-35) were. They hold no son of an Alf (Alv), and the only Gunleik is 'Gunlich', son of Halvor Eyulfsen(?) Klebbe, baptised about 7 Apr 1760, a different patronymic. So the 1761 Gundlick, son of Alf Toe, has no rival in the Drangedal list for Nov 1759 to Dec 1760; Jan-Aug 1761, 1758-Oct 1759 and any separate Tørdal list were not read. Still not proved: no record names Gunleik's father directly. ¶7a

Confidence raised from Probable to Confirmed as father of Gunleik Alvsen (see his page): the probate names Gunlich, 3, as his son with Aasel Jonsdatter. His identity with the Alf Halvorsen 'of Tho' buried in 1801 stays as stated in the 'Identity (Probable)' note. Her place in the direct line runs through Gunleik, whose identification as Gunleik Alvsen of Midbøe, Nissedal, remains Probable. ¶19

The case for this identification 1

Identity (Probable): Alf of Toe is taken to be the Alf Halvorsen, born 1728 (index), of 'Tho', buried in Drangedal on 1 Feb 1801 (Drangedal church book 1768-1814, index pg00000001577529; scan not read). He is the only Alf buried in Drangedal and Tørdal from 1765 to 1800 who could be on the Toe farm, his age fits a father of sons born 1755-1761, and the eldest-known son's name Haavor (Halvor) fits naming after a grandfather Halvor (an inference). 'Tho' is taken to be Toe/Thoe, not proved. ¶2a

Corrections 3

Correction: the earlier note 'Wife (Probable): Tore Torkildsdatter', and the link making her Gunleik's mother, are superseded. Tore was his second wife from 1766. Gunleik (1761), Haavor (1758) and, by date, John (about 1755) were born while Aasil Jonsdatter was his wife. The earlier note 'His wife is not identified' is also superseded. ¶15

Correction: the note 'Child:... Probable other sons: Haavor (Halvor), baptised 17 Dec 1758, and John Alfsen, b. about 1755' and the note 'John Alfsen and the 1801 census' are superseded. The 1764 probate names four sons by Aasil: Jon (b. about 1753), Torgen (b. about 1755), Halvor (b. about 1758) and Gunlich (b. about 1761). John Alfsen of Thoe in 1801 is taken to be the eldest, Jon, so his 1801 'Mandens moder' Tore Torkildsdatter was his stepmother, as that note proposed. ¶20

Correction (applied): in the note 'Identity (Probable)', 'scan not read' should read 'record page (transcription); scan not read'. The age of 73 now comes from the record page, not only the index row. ¶23

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Cautioner 1758: 'Alf Halvorsen Toe' and Jørgen Steensland were sureties at the Candlemas 1758 betrothal of Lars Pedersen Frontvet of Nissedal and Ingri Jonsdatter Steensland, probably his wife's sister (p. 24). ¶12
