About the family

Wife of Mikal Paulsen Eidem, and so the sister-in-law of John P. Eidem. Lac qui Parle County marriage certificate B93398: 'EIDEM, MIKAL' and 'SLIND, BERETH E.', 22 March 1893. Her name is also written Betsy and Betsey in American records. ¶1

Her eldest son Paul M. Eidem was born on 22 April 1893, one month after the wedding, and is on the same census page aged 2 1/2. ¶2

Wife of Mikal Paulsen Eidem (1869-1923), and so a daughter-in-law of Paul and Kari Eidem of Selbu. Her maiden name is SLIND: wave 2's extract of the Minnesota birth index for her youngest daughter, Christine Paulina, born 23 December 1912 at Antelope Valley, Deuel County, gives the parents as 'EIDEM MIKE' and 'BETSY SLINDE'. ¶5

Children, from the Find a Grave family panels of both parents: Minnie Eidem Lund (1895-1987), Emma Clara Eidem Harstad (1899-1946), Mable Berntine Eidem Clemensen (1904-1975) and Christine Pauline Eidem Clemensen (1912-2002). Two of her daughters married Clemensens, and her eldest two are buried at Zoar. ¶9

Her son INGVALD EIDEM, born 26 January 1916 in Deuel County, South Dakota, named her on his own Social Security application as "BERTHA SLIND", with his father as "MIKAL EIDEM" (NUMIDENT SS-5, SSN XXX-XX-0085, cycle 1945). Bertha is the ordinary American form of Beret/Berit. That federal record settles the reassignment of Ingvald to this couple, which the wave-3 work had rated only Probable from the Minnesota death index's mother's-maiden-name field. ¶12

Life story

She is the second Slind woman to marry an Eidem brother, eight years before Oline Slind married John P. Eidem, and a third, Kari Slind, had married the eldest brother Peder P. in 1888. Three Slind sisters or cousins marrying three Eidem brothers is the Selbu chain-migration pattern working itself out on the Minnesota prairie, and it is the reason the Minnesota death index throws up so many Eidems whose mother's maiden name is Slind. ¶3

Gravestone, photographed and read: a flat rose-granite marker with ruled bars down each side, 'MOTHER / BERET EIDEM', '1872' down the left margin and '1922' down the right. ¶7

She is the THIRD Slind woman in this one settlement to marry an Eidem brother - Kari/Karen Slind married Peder P. Eidem in 1888, she married Mikal Paulsen Eidem, and Oline Gundersdatter Slind married John P. Eidem in 1901 - and a fourth Bernice, married Oline's son John Alvin Eidem in the next generation. Her own parentage is still unknown; see the slind-us2 dossier for the records that would settle it. ¶14

Two Eidem brothers married two Slind sisters: Peder married Kari Eliasdatter Slind in 1888, and Mikal married Beret about 1892-93. Their mother Marit Estensdatter followed them to Marietta in 1907. ¶16

1895 Minnesota state census: Mehurin township, Lac qui Parle County, page 0184 line 9 - 'Eidem Betsy, 23, F, W, born Norway, father of foreign birth yes, mother of foreign birth yes'. Wife of 'Mike' Eidem. An age of 23 at 6 June 1895 points to a birth about mid-1871 to mid-1872, consistent with the ABT 1872 already in the tree. ¶11

The Social Security application file of her daughter Christina Paulina (b. 23 December 1912, Antelope Valley, Deuel County, South Dakota) names her parents as 'EIDEM MIKE' and 'BETSY SLINDE', which is the independent confirmation of the 1893 marriage index entry. ¶4

She was therefore still bearing children in 1916, at forty-four if the Zoar stone's 6 May 1872 is right, and she died on 13 November 1922, four months before her husband. ¶13

Selbygbogen (Minneapolis, 1921) pp. 149-150: Mikal P. Eidem 'soon after [1886] married Beret Eliasdatter Slind, born 6 May 1872 at Slind (Reiemoen) in Selbu, and SISTER TO MRS. PEDER P. EIDEM. She had come from Selbu in 1892.' Her birth date and birth farm, her parentage and her emigration year all come from that entry; the tree previously had only 'about 1872, Norway'. ¶15

Burial plot: Zoar Lutheran Cemetery, lot 1 — South Dakota state burial register, 'BERET EIDEM 11-13-1922, lot 1, block 0'. The same lot holds her husband Mikal, Anton B. Eidem, Marlys Ann Eidem, an undated Mable Eidem, and Oline G. Eidem. ¶8

She is the THIRD Slind wife in this one colony, after Oline G. Slind who married John P. Eidem and Kari 'Carrie' Slind who became Karen P. Eidem and married Peder P. Eidem. Three Slind women married three Eidem brothers' households in the same township — which is why a 'mother: Slind' field in a Minnesota index proves nothing about which Eidem a person belongs to. ¶10

Corrections 1

Correction to the project's picture of this generation: Mikal Paulsen Eidem's wife was Beret, not Brynhild Kulsetmoen. Brynhild married ANDERS Andersson Eidem on 6 May 1901. Beret and Mikal's matched pair of markers at Zoar — 'MOTHER / BERET EIDEM / 1872 - 1922' and 'FATHER / MIKAL EIDEM / 1869 - 1923' — were cut together and lie side by side, which settles the couple on the ground. ¶6
