About the family

Parents, Confirmed: Frich Pedersen and Marethe Guldbrandsdatter of Langeteig. Continuity: (1) 21 Mar 1701, Langeteig: the joint probate after Frich Pedersen and his wife Marethe Guldbrandsdatter names their children 'Niels... boende Langeteig, og Britte, Jørgen Langeteigs [hustru], deris Sønne og daatter' (Ytre Sogn 1b p. 275a, 24154 view 59); (2) 1701 census: the only Jørgen at Langeteig is 'Jørgen Knudsen', bonde, 31 (pc00000000627700); (3) 14 Feb 1750, Langeteig: probate after Jørgen Knudsen Langeteig 'imellem Enken Brite Friksdatter og deris sammen avlede 3de Døttre' (6b fol. 635a). The pair Jørgen + Brite at Langeteig at both ends (audit 8 rule 3) and her patronymic Friksdatter (= daughter of Frich) bridge the 49-year gap; no other Jørgen or Brite pair is known at Langeteig. Her own probate is indexed next to her husband's ('Brite Friksdtr, Langeteig, 1743-1750, 6b p. 636a', pa00000000521915; probably the same 1750 entry). Married by 1701, so born before ~1683; her husband was 31 in 1701. ¶2

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Graded Confirmed on her link to her daughter Anne Jørgensdatter (see her page). Widow at the probate after Jørgen Knudsen Langeteig, 14 Feb 1750 (Ytre Sogn 6b fol. 635a, No. 27, 24162 view 283), held 'imellem Enken Brite Friksdatter og deris sammen avlede 3de Døttre' (the audit-8 wording that names the widow as mother), the eldest 'Anne den eldre Jørgensdatter gift med Lars [Siur?]sen Røe'. Continuity: (1) 17 Sep 1745, Røe: the widow Anne Jørgensdatter of Niels Olsen Røe (6b fol. 480a; her guardian there was a Jørgen of Langeteig); (2) 14 Feb 1750, Langeteig: Jørgen Knudsen Langeteig's probate names 'Eldste Datter Anne den eldre Jørgensdatter gift med Lars [Siur?]sen Røe' (5 years later, same farm Røe, now remarried); (3) 28 Aug 1769, Røe: the probate after Anna Jørgensdatter is held between 'hendes efterladende Enkemand Lars [Siur?]sen Røe' and her children with her late husband Niels Olsen (19 years later, same farm, same second husband). So the 1750 daughter married to Lars of Røe is the same woman as the 1745 widow and the 1769 decedent: pair match (Anne + Lars at Røe at both ends, audit 8 rule 3) plus the Langeteig guardian in 1745. ¶1a

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Rival: her younger sister, also Anne Jørgensdatter, but the 1750 record places her at Langeteig as wife of Michel Johansen, so she is excluded by name of husband and farm. Reading caution: Lars's patronymic is unclear in both entries (1750 'Lars O/S..ürsen', 1769 'Lars..rson'); the farm Røe and the first name agree. ¶1b
