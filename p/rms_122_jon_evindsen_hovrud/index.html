Life story

Probate cards checked: in the Valdres card index (DA 39948) the cards just before the Bø run (1657-1663) are Byffellien i Bruflat, Etnedalen (1834-1870); the Bø i Aurdal run begins at card 1665 (1693). So there is no carded probate after Eivind Knutsson of Bø (b. about 1623) or his wife. His fatherhood of Jon stays on the bygdebok (Probable). Test record unchanged: the 1664/1666 manntall for Bø, or a deed in the mortgage books. ¶5

Bygdebok (Svenes vol., gnr. 76 Hovrud, canvas 643): 'Jon Eivindsson' (from gnr. 106), d. 1699, married Anne Knutsdotter (of Øvre [..], gnr. 111, 1655-1713); after Jon's death Anne married in 1700 Erik Embriksson (gnr. 80, about 1673-1751), and in 1717 Erik gave a deed (skøyte) of the farm to 'stesonen Eivind Jonsson' (his stepson). 'Eivind Jonsson', b. about 1683, brukar, married 1715 'Marit Julsdotter' of gnr. 65 (about 1690-1728) and in 1729 Sigrid Olsdotter; children Jon 1716 d., Jul 1717 brukar, Anne 1720 (m. 1746 soldier Trond Tordsson), Jon 1723, a son 1725 (soldier, d. 1754). A Rønnaug Eivindsdotter is 'kanskje syster'. ¶1

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
