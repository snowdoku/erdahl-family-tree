Life story

Probate 1735 (Nordfjord skifteprotokoll A 8, probate extracts, 1735 section, image 12 right page): 'Hilde, efter Sal: Ole Olsen' his estate passed to his children as follows: 'Ældste Datter Giertrud Olsdatter' 13 rd (land in Hilde and movables), with a kinsman ('hendes næste Frænde') appointed her guardian, so she was then unmarried; 'Yngste Datter Kar[en?] Olsdatter' 13 rd, for whom 'hendes Broder Anthonius Olsen Hilde' was appointed guardian. The heading words between the name and the heirs are partly unclear. ¶1

Aaland (Innvik 1974 ed., Hilde bruk nr. 3 Antonbr., p. 225): 'U. Ola Olson, d. 65 år g. 1735, br. 1702; 1705 løyste han 21 mk. frå Vib. Fux. Utkom. Pass 1710. K.: Gunnh. Andersd. Skåden, d. f. 1750. Br. Anton d. e., d. ikr. 28 år g. 1702.' Next holder: his son Anton Olson (d. 1770 aged 69, took the bruk 1737), who married Kari Guttormsdatter Hilde, sister of Anders Guttormsen. Aaland's death year 1735 matches the probate. ¶2

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Probable as father of Jartrud (Giertrud) Olsdatter (see her page), graded on his link to her. For: the 1735 probate names his eldest daughter Giertrud Olsdatter of Hilde; Jartrud/Giertrud Olsdatter is the widow of Anders Guttormsen Hilde in his 1760 probate, on the same farm; Aaland calls her 'Jartrud Olsd. Hilde'; her children (Guttorm b. about 1739, Gunnild b. about 1748) fit a marriage after 1735, when she was still unmarried; and the double marriage (Anton Olsen x Kari Guttormsdatter, Anders Guttormsen x Jartrud Olsdatter) is a sibling exchange between the two Hilde bruk. No rival Ole is found at Hilde 1700-1760. Why not Confirmed: the gap from 1735 to the 1760 probate is 25 years with no record in between naming her, and the Nordfjord index has a 'Gjertrud Olsdtr, Hilde' probate (protocol 15a p. 377, 1766-70), which conflicts with Aaland's death of Jartrud in 1791 (it is probably Jartrud herself, the widow of Ole Andersen who d. about 1763, but it is unread). ¶3a

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed, graded on his link to his daughter Jartrud (Giertrud) Olsdatter (see her page). This is the 1735-1760 bridge that the earlier notes asked for. Guardianship protocol no. 2 (1736-1744), p. 267: 'Anders Andersen Foesnes for Giertrud Olsdatter Hilde, som arvede den 26 Maij 1735: Capital 13 rd.' and opposite: 'Disse modstaaende 13 rd: forklarede formynderen d: 18de July 1742 at være leveret til Anders Guttormsen Hilde, som denne pige har ægtet.' The 13 rd and the date match his 1735 probate (A 8, 1735 section, image 12R), where his eldest daughter Giertrud Olsdatter receives 13 rd under a kinsman as guardian. Her sister's account confirms the date and the family: Barbra Olsdatter of Hilde inherited 27 May 1735 with 'Anthonius Olsen Hilde' as guardian, noted as 'Formynders søster' (protocol no. 1 p. 89), which is the probate's youngest daughter whose guardian is her brother Anthonius. Chain: probate May 1735 (Giertrud his daughter) -> guardianship account 1735-1742 (the same 13 rd paid to her husband Anders Guttormsen Hilde, 1742) -> Anders Guttormsen's 1760 probate (widow Giertrud) and her 1769 lifetime division (children Guttorm, Hans, Kari, Gunilde). Same farm, gaps of 7 and 18 years, and each step names the relationship; no rival Giertrud Olsdatter at Hilde is known. ¶5

Records that disagree 1

The conflict noted above is resolved: the 1766-70 'Gjertrud Olsdtr, Hilde' entry (A 15a fol. 376b-377a, 2 May 1769) is a lifetime division requested by the living widow Giertrud Olsdatter among her 4 Andersen children, not her death. It does not name her father or brother, so the 1735-1760 bridge is still missing and the grade stays Probable. A rival to note: an Anthoni Olsen, husmann on 'Hildes Grund', died before 12 May 1760 (A 13 fol. 295a; widow Marthe Stenersdatter, only daughter Brite Anthonsdatter about 48); he is not the farmer Anton Olsen Hilde (Aaland d. 1770), who was Giertrud's lavverge in Oct 1760, but it shows two Anton Olsens at Hilde, so the 'Anthonius Olsen Hilde' of 1735 must be matched with care. ¶4

Corrections 1

Correction: the youngest daughter in the 1735 probate as 'Kar[en?] Olsdatter', is Barbra Olsdatter in the guardianship protocols (inherited 27 May 1735, guardian her brother Anthonius Olsen Hilde; married by 5 Nov 1757 to Anders Rasmussen Loen, protocol no. 3 p. 50). ¶6

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Would confirm: Jartrud's marriage record or a deed or probate between 1735 and 1760 naming Anders Guttormsen's wife as Ole Olsen's daughter or Anton Olsen's sister; or the 15a p. 377 probate if it names her brother. ¶3b
