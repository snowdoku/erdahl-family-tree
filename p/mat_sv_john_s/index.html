About the family

Named as the groom's father in the Selbu marriage entry of 8 NOV 1841: 'brudgommens far: John Sivertsen Eidem'. ¶1

His wife is named in two independent records: 'Siri Bersvendsd., Hustrue' in his son Sivert's baptism of 1815, and 'Sigri Bersvendsdatter, 81, Husfaderens Moder, enke' in the 1865 census of oestre Eidem. ¶5

His own father was a Sivert, from the patronymic - the Sivert/John alternation runs down five generations of this family: Sivert Jonssen b. abt 1739, John Sivertsen b. abt 1783, Sivert Johnsen b. 1815, John Sivertsen b. 1842 and Sivert Sivertsen b. 1859. ¶7

Life story

Baptism: Selbu, Rogate Sunday (5th after Easter) 1784, which fell on 16 May (Easter 1784 = 11 April): 'Doebt. Jon. Par: Siur Jons. Aftreit, Helge Jonsd. T. Ped. Sath., Olle Berge, Th. Aftr., Karen Eidam, Sigri Ugl.' - baptised Jon, parents Sivert (Siur) Jonsen of Aftreit and Helge Jonsdatter; godparents Peder Sather, Ole Berge, Th. Aftret, Karen Eidem and Sigri Uglem. His mother was churched as 'Siur Jons. Aftreits Hustru' on the 2nd Sunday after Trinity (20 June) 1784. Read from the scan (source 16311, canvas 114). ¶13

1801 census: person 004 at rural residence 0034 Eidum, Selbu - 'Jon Sivertssen', aged 18, 'Deres boern', in the household of Sivert Jonssen (62, Bonde og gaardbeboer) and Haelge Jonsdatter (48). His brothers and sisters in the house are Jon 20, Ole 15 and two girls both called Ingebor, 12 and 9. ¶2

Marriage: banns in Selbu church on 19 April, 26 April and 7 May 1812 for 'Jon Siverthsen Eidem og Pigen Sirie Bersvensd: Draxen', sureties (forlovere) Erich Olsen Eidem and Christen Olsen of the same farm, who also attested that both had had smallpox; married 26 July 1812 (9th Sunday after Trinity) as 'Ungkarl' (bachelor). His bride was Sigri Bersvendsdatter of Draxen (see her page). The other Jon Sivertsen of Eidem, his elder brother, married Elen Sivertsdatter. ¶15

He and his elder brother were both called John Sivertsen and both lived at Eidem, which is why the parish clerk in 1815 distinguished one of them as 'John Siverts. AELDRE' - John Sivertsen the elder. Ours is the younger, the gaardmand; the elder was a cottager married to Elen Sivertsdatter, who had been a servant girl at Langli in 1801. ¶4

Dead by 31 December 1865: his widow is entered at that census as a foederaadslem - a person living on the pension of board and lodging that a Norwegian farm deed carried with it when the farm was handed over to the next generation. ¶6

A second Sivert Johnsen was living at Eidem at the same time as his son, married to Ingeborg Pedersdatter and baptising children in 1853, 1857 and 1859; by the 1865 census that man is husmand med jord at Eidemsgjerdet, residence 0062. Two men of the same name on one farm is a trap on this family and the mother's name is the only discriminator in the baptismal index. ¶8

Probate (parents'): he is the 'John yngre' (John the younger) among the heirs of his father Sivert Johnsen Eidem (closed 5 April 1815) and of his mother Helge Johnsdatter (closed 22 September 1820); in 1820 he was of age and was appointed guardian of his unmarried sister Ingeborg. His elder brother of the same name is the 'John eldre' of both skifter. ¶16

The 1820 fæsteseddel (lease) entered under Eidem in the land register does not concern him. The deed itself (Pantebok 5 fol. 374b) is a cottar's lease of 'Pladsen Eidum under Gaarden Eidum', dated 1 June 1820, from the landowner Lt-Col Georg Frederik von Krogh of Kroghsminde. The lessee signed it 'John Sivertsen Eidum Ældr.', meaning his elder brother John, the husmand. It also shows that von Krogh owned Gaarden Eidem in 1820. How the gaardmand John came to hold the farm is not yet shown. ¶20

Panteregister 18 for Selbu (Digitalarkivet 22744, fol. 41, canvas 43) lists the holders of Eidem gl. matr. 720 / ny matr. 51 løpenr 62 as: Sivert Jonsen; then 'John Sivertsen Sk[jøde] af 19 Aug. 1820, thingl. 2[?] Febr. 1821, f. 416'; then 'John Sivertsen, Sk. 1824 […], f. 110'; then 'Sigrid Bersvendsdatter, gift med Ole Thomassen'; then 'Sivert Johnsen, Sk. dat. og thingl. 23 Febr. 1854, fol. 167'. The farm was held by deed (skjøde), not by lease. The seller is not named in the register; G. F. v. Krogh owned the farm in 1807, when he mortgaged it (27 May 1807, released 7 Jul 1824). That the 1820/1824 John is this man rather than his elder brother is inferred (the elder brother's plass lease is registered separately at PR 18 fol. 293, and this man is gaardmand in 1825); the deeds themselves (pantebok fol. 416 and fol. 110) have not been read. ¶21

Silver tax 1821 (round of 3 Mar 1821): 'Joh. Siverts. Ældre og yngre', Eidem, 2 ort - the two brothers John Sivertsen the elder and the younger assessed together. (the 1816 silver tax was levied on property holders in several rounds, 1816-1821.) ¶22

Death and burial: the Selbu register for 1824-1842 (burials 1832, page 378, no. 6) enters 'Gmd. John Sivertsen Eidem', aged 48, residence Eidem, died 3 February and buried 12 February 1832. Gmd. means gaardmand, a farmer. The index omits the farm and status; both were read from the scanned page. ¶10

1825 census: head of household 01 at rural residence 0059* Eidem - 'Joh Siverts.', aged 41, occupation 'Gdm.' = gaardmand, farmer, with 'Sigrid Bersvendsd.' 39 as Hustru and four children, Helge 13, Sivert 10, Anders 5 and a half, and Marit 2. His unmarried sister Ingebor Sivertsd., 33, is in the house as Tp. (tjenestepike, servant). Three further households on the same farm are his brothers': John Siverts. 45, Hmd. (husmand, cottager) with Elen Sivertsd. 41, and Ole Siverts. 30, Hmd., with Anne Johnsd. 29. ¶3

Probate: the skifte after 'Gaardmand John Sivertsen Eidum af Selboe' was closed on 8 November 1832 (Strinda og Selbu district court, skifteutlodningsprotokoll 1, fol. 129b-130). The estate was registered on 8 March 1832, after his death on 3 February 1832; on 6 July 1832 the farm Eidum (matrikkel no. 120) was valued at 500 speciedaler and a skifte meeting held the same day (proceedings protocol fol. 391b); the public notice to creditors (proclama) followed on 30 July 1832. Heirs: the widow Sigrid Bersvendsdatter; the son Severt Johnsen, 16 1/2 years; the son Anders, 11 1/2; the daughter Helge Johnsdatter, 19 1/2, unmarried; and the daughter Marith Johnsdatter, 8 1/2. The farm Eidum was laid out to the widow, who also kept the movables. The children's guardian was 'Barnenes Farbroder John Sivertsen Eidum' - their paternal uncle, his elder brother of the same name (Jon Sivertsen Eidem). Read again on the saved crop. ¶17

Estate and heirs: the 1854 deed of Eidem (Pantebok 11 fol. 167b) is made by 'Sigrid Bersvendsdatter Eidum med Lavværge, Enke efter John Sivertsen Eidum' together with his heirs. They are Anders Johnsen Eidem, his second son; Ole Ingebrigtsen, husband of his daughter Helge; and Erik Torstensen Uglem, husband of his daughter Marit. The farm went to Sivert Johnsen, his eldest son. These four children are the only heirs the deed names. ¶19

1 on the identification · 1 correction · 3 alternatives set aside

The case for this identification 1

Identification of the 1832 burial: he is the GAARDMAND John Sivertsen of Eidem, and his age of 48 gives a birth year of about 1784. That agrees with this man (18 in 1801, and 41 as 'Joh Siverts., Gdm.' in 1825), not with his elder brother John (20 in 1801, and 45 as a husmand, a cottager, in 1825), who would have been about 52. This replaces the earlier 'dead before 31 December 1865'. He was already dead when the 1841 marriage register named him as the groom's father, which registers commonly do. ¶11

Corrections 1

Correction: his birth place was given as Eidem, because the family lived at Eidem in 1801 and after. In 1784 his parents were at Aftret (Aftreit), another Selbu farm, so he was born there; they moved to Eidem between 1784 and 1801. His birth year moves from about 1783 to 1784, agreeing with his age of 48 at burial in 1832 and 41 in 1825. ¶14

Alternatives considered and set aside 3

Namesake: not the same man as his elder brother Jon Sivertsen Eidem (see his page), b. about 1781. Both sons of Sivert Jonssen are named Jon Sivertssen in the 1801 census of Eidum - person 003 aged 20 and person 004, this man, aged 18 - and both appear again as separate householders in the 1825 census of Eidem: this man 'Joh Siverts.', 41, gaardmand (farmer), married to Sigrid Bersvendsdatter, and the elder 'John Siverts.', 45, husmand (cottager), married to Elen Sivertsdatter, each with his own daughter Helge and son Sivert. The wife's name is the discriminator for every record of either man; the two ids must not be merged. ¶9

Rejected candidates for his burial: 'John Sivertsen', aged 62 (b. about 1780), buried 20 March 1842, whose scanned entry (1842, page 404, no. 11) reads 'John Sivertsen Krogstad, Hmd., 62 Aar, Krogstad' - a husmand of KROGSTAD, not of Eidem, and too old; and 'John Olsen, Kaarmand, Eidem', died 29 December 1861 aged 78, whose patronymic is wrong and who matches the separate 1825 Eidem gaardmand John Olsen, b. about 1783. ¶12

Namesake reminder from the probates: the 1815 and 1820 skifter of his parents list two sons named John, 'eldre' and 'yngre', and in 1832 his own children's guardian was their uncle John Sivertsen Eidem. That uncle is his elder brother Jon Sivertsen Eidem, not himself; the two ids must stay separate. ¶18
