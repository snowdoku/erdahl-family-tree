Speculative father of Christina Catharina, wife of Jacob Christoph Schumayer (see her page). ¶1a

Graded on that link: the bride row of the marriage at Großsachsenheim on 3 Jun 1679 reads 'Christina Catharina Lugers', relatives 'Johann Burkhard, Herr Jacob Christoph' (her father, then the groom; record 906178196, image 1184619-00244); the groom is indexed 'Herr Jacob Christoph Schwenger' (6178196). Joining that bride to Schumayer's wife rests on the matching pair of given names and the date (13 months before their son's baptism, 1680), not on the surname, so Speculative. Unlabelled index rows. Surname read from the genitive 'Lugers' (Luger, perhaps Lieger/Leger). No Luger, Lieger or Leger rows are indexed at Großsachsenheim, and no Johann Burkhard appears there, so the family may have come from elsewhere (Lugers/Liegers are indexed at Horrheim 1657-60: Jasius Luger x Barbara, Leonhard Lieger x Maria). ¶1b
