Life story

1645 poll tax: 'Joran Hansdtr' is listed on Lunde, Tjugum sogn, Leikanger prestegjeld, directly after the farmer 'Hanns', in the section of farmers and their wives; Hans is the only farmer on that Lunde in 1645. He is taken to be Hans Lunde (see his page), farmer there in 1664 and 1666 (54 in 1666), so she was his wife in 1645. Birth before about 1620 is inferred from her son Truls being born about 1638. ¶1

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the grade · 1 record that disagrees · 1 open question

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: Speculative -> Probable on the Balestrand bygdebok (Laberg 1985, canvases 137-138), graded on her link to her son Hans Hansen of Lunde (see his page, b. about 1638), and now linked as his mother. New evidence: the Balestrand bygdebok (Laberg 1985, canvases 137-138, Lunde; rebuilt from word boxes, dates ABT) says Hans Olson came to Lunde, that his wife was '[...] Hansdtr.' (first name in a gap of the rebuild), that they had 5 children, and that 'Etter Hans Olson kom sonen Hans Hansson', b. 1638, d. 1713, wife Brita Olsdtr. So the bygdebok makes the mother of Hans Hansen a Hansdatter, which answers the open point in the note above (a possible earlier wife of another name): the only Hansdatter wife of the Lunde farmer on record is 'Joran Hansdtr' of the 1645 poll tax. Why only Probable: the mother-son link rests on the compiled bygdebok, and her first name is supplied by the 1645 roll, not the bygdebok text. ¶3a

Records that disagree 1

Against or open: Hans Lunde was only about 20-26 when his son Hans was born, so an earlier wife is quite possible; none of Hans Hansen's known children (Hans, Johannes, Brite, Anna) is named Joran; no record names his mother. Not linked as mother for that reason; recorded as a candidate. She may well be the mother of Hans Lunde's younger sons Ole (17 in 1666) and Johannes (5 in 1666), who are not in the tree. ¶2b

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Test record: a probate or tingbok entry naming Jøran Hansdatter's children (no Ytre Sogn probate protocol survives before 1698; Ytre Sogn tingbøker 1650s-1690s), or the 1666 census, which lists Hans Lunde's sons Hans 32, Ole 17 and Johannes 5 without wives. ¶3b
