About the family

Siblings: Millie Eastvold (1878-1972), Bertha Josephena (1880-1895), Cora J. (1885-1979), Minnie J. Monson (1887-1980), Julius Marvin (1892-1974). ¶1

Parentage: the 1885 census places her as a child in Ole J. and Maia (Mary) Quame's household, and her 1952 Washington death record names her father as 'Ole Guame', a misspelling of Quame. The two records together confirm the link to Ole Jensen Quame. The death record leaves the mother's name blank, so the link to Mary (Maria) Olsdatter Anderson rests on the 1885 household and on the family memorials. ¶3

Children's birth certificates 1901-1910: as the mother she is named 'Annie Quam' in 1901, 1903 and on both Otis cards (1905/1906), 'Annie Quame' in 1908 (born 'Faribault, Minn.', housewife) and 'Anna Quame' in 1910 (farmer's wife, born Minnesota). Five children were born in Murray County: Oliver 1901, Minnie 1903, Otis 1905 or 1906, Bernice 1908 and Maynard 1910. ¶5

Life story

1885 MN census: on 10 June 1885 'Ohanna Quame', 2, born Minnesota, was in family 31 in Rome Township, Faribault County, with her parents 'Ole J Quame', 31, and 'Maia O Quame', 30, both born Norway, and her sisters (see Ole Quame). The age fits her birth in October 1882. ¶2

1900 U.S. census: 'Anna J.' Quamne, daughter, born October 1882 in Minnesota, 17 and single, was living with her parents Ole J. and Maria in Rome Township, Faribault County (sheet 5B, dwelling 84). Seven months later, on 9 February 1901, she married Berent Erdahl. The month and year agree with her birth on 13 October 1882, and the initial J agrees with 'Olianna J.'. ¶9

1905 MN census: on 1 June 1905 'Annie Erdall', 22, born Minnesota, was living with her husband 'Bernt' in Ellsborough Township, Murray County. ¶4

1910 U.S. census: 'Anna Erdahl', wife of Berend, born about 1883 in Minnesota, was living in Cameron Township, Murray County. ¶7

1920 U.S. census (index only): the index entry for her daughter 'Minnie B Erdahl', in School District 18, Phillips County, Montana, gives the mother's name as 'Oliana'. This is the only census so far that uses her baptismal name. Her own row has not been found in the index, and the image has not been read. ¶10

1920 U.S. census (image): enumerated as head 'Oliana Erdahl', 37, married, farmer, born Minnesota, parents born Norway, at School District 18 (South Malta), Phillips County, Montana, with nine children; her husband Berent is not on the page. 1910 (index): 'Anna', wife, 27, Cameron Township, Murray County, Minnesota. ¶17

1930 U.S. census (index only): 'Annie Erdahl', wife of Berent, born about 1883 in Minnesota, was in South Malta, Phillips County, Montana (see Berent for the household). ¶11

1940 U.S. census, a double entry (index only): she is indexed twice. In South Malta, Montana she is 'Annie Edell' (alternative Erdahl), wife of 'Ben L', born about 1883 in Minnesota, with their son William. In Custer, Whatcom County, Washington she is 'Annie Erdahl' (alternative Eidahl), head of household, born about 1883 in Minnesota, with her son 'Jean A', born about 1921 in Montana. Both entries are probably her. The Custer entry agrees with the family account that they moved to Custer in 1939, and Jean A. is her son Gene Archie (married in Whatcom County in 1945 as 'Jean Archie Erdahl'). The Montana entry probably records the household that still stood on the Malta farm, with Annie listed as a usual member although she was already in Washington. This is inferred, not proved. Reading the two page images (signed-in request S0113) would show who was present and each person's residence in 1935. ¶12

Death certificate, read from the image: her father is written 'Ole Quame'. The capital Q is formed like a G, which explains the index reading 'Guame'. For her mother's maiden name the informant wrote two words that appear to read 'No record' (uncertain), not a surname. The informant was 'B. L. Erdahl', her husband Berent Ludvig, who used the same initials on his children's birth certificates. The certificate therefore links her to Berent. She was married, did housework in her own home, and lived at LaBounty Road outside Bellingham (house number read as 1326, uncertain). She had been in the Whatcom County Hospital for 14 days. Monroe Mortuary of Ferndale handled the funeral. ¶13

1950 U.S. census: on 1 April 1950 she was in her husband Bernt (Berent) L. Erdahl's household on LaBounty Road in rural Whatcom County, Washington (enumeration district 37-37, dwelling 25, a farm), listed as Annie, wife, 67, married, born in Minnesota, keeping house. Four of her unmarried children lived with them: Maynard E., Inez E., Elma F. and Frances. Her age fits her birth on 13 October 1882, and the address is the LaBounty Road home given on her 1952 death certificate. Read from the page image. ¶16

WA death record: the Washington death certificate index records Annie Erdahl, female, died 10 July 1952, aged 69, at 'Bellingham - Rural', Whatcom County, with the place note 'Whatcom Co. Hospital'. It names her father as 'Ole Guame' and leaves her mother blank. The age agrees with October 1882. The tree's death place, Ferndale, is in the same county; she probably died in the county hospital. The image has not been read. ¶8

Burial: the death certificate records burial on 15 July 1952 in 'Greenacres Memorial Pk.', Whatcom County, which agrees with the tree's Greenacres Memorial Park, Ferndale. An earlier reading of the cemetery as 'Whatcom Memorial Park' is not supported by the image. ¶14

How sure are we?

Records that disagree 1

Age, a small contradiction: the 1901 certificate gives her age as 19 and the 1903 certificate as 21, each a year more than her birth on 13 October 1882 allows. Her husband's ages on the same two certificates are also a year too high, so the reporter probably subtracted birth years. All the later records agree with October 1882: 22 in June 1905, 25 in February 1908, 27 in September 1910 and 69 at her death in July 1952. The 1910 census index gives her birth as about 1883. The birth date of 13 Oct 1882 is kept. ¶6

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Same-name records ruled out: two census index entries for an Annie or Anna Erdahl are not her. In 1910 an 'Annie Erdahl' born about 1871 in Minnesota is indexed in Rome Township, Faribault County, but Annie Quame was born in 1882 and was then in Cameron Township, Murray County with her husband. In 1940 an 'Anna J Erdahl', born about 1883 in Minnesota, is indexed as a head of household in Rochester, Olmsted County, Minnesota. The name, middle initial and age fit, but she has no husband or children with her, and Annie is indexed that year in both South Malta, Montana and Custer, Washington, so this is probably another woman. Both are index entries only; the Rochester image would settle the second. ¶15
