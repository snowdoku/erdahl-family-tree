About the family

Husband (Probable): Povel Hansen of Ytre Esse (see his page). The probate does not name her husband, but all four of her sons are Povelsen, and they are exactly the four sons listed under Povel Hansen, 66, at Esse in the 1701 census: Hans, Olle, Anders and Knud. No other Povel at Esse is known. A record naming Povel Hansen as her husband (a Ytre Esse farm transfer or court entry 1701-1721, or Povel's own probate) would confirm it. ¶2

Life story

Probate: Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll A 3a, fol. 61a-62a, read from the scan. On 20 June 1721 sorenskriver (district judge) Niels Wiborg, with the lensmann and two lagrettesmenn (lay assessors), met at Ytre Esse to divide the estate of the late Randi Olsdatter between her children and heirs: 1st son Hans Povelsen, 2nd son Olle Povelsen, 3rd son Anders Povelsen, 4th son Knud Povelsen, 1st daughter Britte, [wife of] Olle Meenes (Menes), and 2nd daughter Guri, [wife of] Mons Esse, all of age and present. The estate held land (jordegods) in Esse, shared among the heirs; gross 156 rd 4 s, debts (bortgang) about 13 rd. No widower is named, so her husband was dead by June 1721. ¶1
