Life story

Probate 1742: Probate was held at Hilde on 29 May 1742 after 'Ane Olsdatter', the late wife ('Sal: Qvinde') of Østen Rasmussen. Her heirs were their three sons, Rasmus Østensen (23), Ole Østensen (21) and Arne Østensen (19). The name is read from the scan of the probate extract (A 8, image 104) and fits Aaland's 'Anne Olsd., † ikr. 1741' (died about 1741) as wife of Øystein Rasmusson of Hilde bruk nr. 8. Her own parents are not named. ¶1
