About the family

NEW ANCESTOR. Farmer at Rostveit in Åmli. His estate was settled on 15 October 1728, as recorded on the archivist's cross-reference card to the 1765 Eppeland probate: "Avds far: Tergie Tiøstelsen Rostveit + sk. 15/10-1728". ¶1

His wife was Oslaug Aanonsdatter. His son Tjøstel Tergiesen moved to Nedre Eppeland and is our ancestor; another son, Hylje Tergiesen, stayed at Rostveit and is named on the same card as the deceased's brother. ¶2

His own father was a Tjøstel, from the patronymic — a name that then alternates Tjøstel/Terje down the family: Tjøstel > Tergie > Tjøstel > Tergie > Birgitte Terjesdatter. ¶3

Life story

Burial: 'Dom. 10 a Trinit. i Omblie overstaaet Tergie Tiöstelsön Rostved 60 aar gammel' (Åmli register 1711-1740, DA 9920 canvas 48, p. 91, read on the scan). The 10th Sunday after Trinity 1728 was 1 Aug 1728 (Easter 28 Mar). Aged 60, so born about 1668. His estate followed on 15 Oct 1728. ¶8

His own probate of 15 October 1728 has not yet been read and should name all his children and his widow. ¶4

Own estate found, card read on the scan: 'Austre Robyggjelaget Skifteprotokoll nr. 8, fol. 26, 15 okt. 1728. Rostveit i Aamli. Tergie Tiøstelsøn †, g.m. Oslog Aanonsdtr. Born: Tiøstel Tergiesøn 25 år [archivist: + sk. 1765 på Eppeland; g.m. Bergitte Evensdtr. fra Ramse], Hølgie Tergiesøn 21 år [g.m. Guro Kidelsdtr. fra Tveit]. Inkje jordegods. Avd.s bror: Knud Tiøstelsen Homstøl i Froland; bror: Ulfve [Tiøstelsen] Kleivene i Omli. Medel 153 rd, gjeld 10. Se avkallsbrev tgl. 10/11 1711 på Rostveit.' The probate itself states his patronymic and names his widow and two sons, and two brothers, Knud Tjøstelsen of Homstøl in Froland and Ulv Tjøstelsen of Kleivene in Åmli. Together they make their father a Tjøstel. It does not name the father's farm. The 1711 avkallsbrev (heirs' quitclaim) registered at the court on 10 Nov 1711 for Rostveit may be his father's or his wife's inheritance: Østre Råbyggelag tingbok for 1711 (not read). ¶5

Son Hølje Tergiesen's wife Guroe Kiddelsdatter died before 14 Dec 1754 (Skp. 12b fol. 285b, card 11397); that card repeats 'Enkemannens far: Tergie Tiøstelsen Rostveit + sk. 15/10 1728; mor: Oslaug Aanonsdtr.' and names 'Enkemannens bror: Tiøstel Tergiesen Rostveit (sk. på Eppeland 2/3 1765)'. ¶6

The avkallsbrev referred to on his 1728 probate card ('Se avkallsbrev tgl. 10/11 1711 på Rostveit') is found and read: Østre Råbyggelag Pantebok A5, fol. 52b-53, registered 'Paa Birkeland(?) den 10de ejusdem [Nov 1711] lovligen tinglyst'. The grantors are Knud Tiøstelsøn Homstøl of Froland parish and Ulf Tiøstelsøn (signing with the mark VTS); they acknowledge to 'vores kiære broder Tergie Tiøstelsøn Rostved' the settlement of their share of the inheritance. Dated Rostved 26 Oct 1711. This is a primary record of the three brothers, and of their father's name Tjøstel through the shared patronymic. The parents' names are not legible on the scan. ¶7

Protocol read: Skifteprotokoll nr. 8 fol. 26a-28a (DA 24356 views 41-43; view = folio + 15): Lars Bruun, sorenskriver, with lagrettemenn, 15 Oct 1728 at Rostved in Omblie sogn, 'efter... afgangne Tergie Tiøstelsøn Rostwed', to divide between 'hans efterlevende Enke Oslaug Aanonsdatter og deris med hinanden... auflede Børn, nafnlig Tiøstel Tergiesøn 25 aar, Hølgie Tergiesøn 21 aar'. This agrees with card 11395. Among the debts paid are claims of Martha Tiøstelsdatter (3 rd) and Aaslov(?) Tallaksdatter (4 rd); Martha may be a sister of the deceased, but no relationship is written. The widow's own kin (her father Aanon) are not named on the pages read. ¶9

The Rostveit chapter (gnr. 68) is in Åmli ætt og heim V (Vestbygda), canvases 496-502, not in vols VI/VII (had missed it). Rebuilt text (gaps; dates ABT): canvas 498 gives Tjøstolv [Torgeirsen] Rostveit, b. about 1632, d. about 1695/96, with Anne [Aslaksdotter] of Ramse, b. about 1639, d. 1729, and children b. about 1665 (Knut, 'Kalla', Froland, 1711), about 1668 (Terje, married to Aslaug, '(Sigridnes?)'), about 1683 (married 1711 to Ingeborg) and about 1686 (living 1728). The author says 'Tjøstolv må ha vore gift to gonger' and that the children were most likely by two wives, so the book does not say which wife was Terje's mother. Canvas 499: 'Terje Tjøstolvs. Rostveit f.(1668) d.1728', wife Aslaug, 'Sigridnes?', d. 1747; sons Tjøstolv b. about 1703 (married 1730 to Berte of Ramse) and [Hølje] b. about 1706 (married 1732 to Guro of Tveit). Aslaug gave up the whole estate ('mora Aslaug gav opp heile buet'). The book gives no parents for Oslaug Aanonsdatter beyond the guess 'Sigridnes?'. Test record for Terje's mother: the probate after Anne Aslaksdatter, Rostveit, 1729 (Østre Råbyggelag Skp. 8 or 9; Rostveit cards after 11395). ¶10

The 1729 burial is found and read. Åmli register 1711-1740 (DA 9920), canvas 51, p. 94, Dom. Exaudi 1729 (5 Jun 1729), at Omblie (Åmli church), among the burials: 'Anne Aslacksdatter Rostved 90 aar gl.' (b. about 1639, as the bygdebok gives). The entry has no status or kin word (not 'enke', no son). It shows that an Anne Aslaksdatter of Rostveit died in 1729 aged 90, but not that she was his mother; his mother remains unproved. If she was his mother she bore him at about 40 or later, which fits the bygdebok's doubt that Tjøstolv married twice. ¶12
