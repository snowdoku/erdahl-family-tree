Life story

Baptism: 'Giertrug Pedersdtr Eidem', born 23 October 1830 and baptised 14 November 1830; father 'Peter Bersvendsen', mother 'Magli Evensdtr', residence Eidem (Selbu 1824-1842, p. 62 no. 105; index only). Kari Pedersdatter's full sister, named for Mali's elder sister Giertrud of Uglem (and for Peder's first wife). The mother is certainly Mali (the 1826 bride's spelling, at Eidem); the father is entered as 'Peter', not found elsewhere for him, so the link is Probable until the scan is read. Her later life (burial, confirmation, marriage) has not been searched. ¶1

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
