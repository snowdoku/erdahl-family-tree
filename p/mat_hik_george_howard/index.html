About the family

Younger brother of Lydia Adelaide (Hicken) Hayter. Born 27 or 28 February 1889 at Sturgeon (the two compiled genealogies differ by a day). Gravestone at Sturgeon Baptist Cemetery (Find a Grave 75109393, photograph read): 'Hicken / In loving memory / George H. Hicken / died Oct. 19, 1966 / age 77 yrs. / The Lord is my shepherd' - age 77 agrees with a February 1889 birth. Married Georgia (Georgie W.) Sencabaugh in 1923 (Creed compiler); her stone in the Sturgeon Methodist/United Cemetery reads 'Georgie W. Sencabaugh Hicken, Dec 6, 1902 - May 21, 1984', with their son Gerald H. Hicken (7 Aug 1926 - 10 Jun 1959). ¶1

Family: he married Georgie W. Sencabaugh (6 December 1902 - 21 May 1984), per the Sturgeon Methodist/United cemetery transcription and the compiled genealogy. Their son Gerald Howard Hicken was born 7 August 1926, baptised 11 July 1954 and died in 1959 (PEI BMB index, film 005240344, image 89). ¶7

Life story

Baptism: George Howard Hicken, born 28 February 1889 at Sturgeon, baptised 15 May 1890, son of William J. and Bexy (PEI Baptism Index; Sturgeon register, FamilySearch film 005240351, image 213; a second copy of the register, image 260, gives 1887 in error). ¶2

1891 census: George H., 2, son, in his father William Hicken's household, Township 61, with his grandfather George, 79. 1901 census: Hicken, George, son, born 27 February 1889, 12, with his mother Bexy and stepfather Shedrick W. Johnston at Sturgeon. ¶4

Birth date: the 1901 census gives 27 February 1889, and the compiled Creed genealogy agrees. The baptism index gives 28 February. ¶3

1911 and 1921 censuses (LAC e002040198 and e003057191, read by the creed researcher): in 1911 he was single, 22, a Methodist farmer, living at Gaspereaux with his widowed mother Bexey Johnston. In 1921 he was 'George N. Hicken', stepson, 32, in the household of James Cleary and Bexey. ¶5

Burial: 22 October 1966 at Albion (PEI BMB index, FamilySearch film 005240351, image 621). His gravestone in Sturgeon Baptist Cemetery gives his death as 19 October 1966, aged 77. ¶6
