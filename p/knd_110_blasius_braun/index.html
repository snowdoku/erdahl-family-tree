Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Graded on that link: Probable (compiled source). Pfarrerbuch Württemberg Nr. 834: M. Blasius Braun, born about 1576 at Stuttgart, matriculated at Tübingen 29 Aug 1592 (Maulbronn scholar), B.A. 1594, Stift 1596, Magister 15 Feb 1598, deacon at Weinsberg from Oct 1604, pastor of Münchingen from 1 Nov 1609 until his death there on 20 Aug 1635 ('nach großem Schrecken', after a great fright: the year after the battle of Nördlingen). Married at Tübingen on 16 Apr 1605 Anna, widow of Dr Johann Konrad Grammer (Cramer), physician of Landau. Children: 1. Anna, baptised 19 Feb 1606 at Weinsberg, married 1635 Joseph Jenisch; 2. died young; 3. Johann Jakob, born 5 Oct 1608; 4-7 died young. His son-in-law Joseph Jenisch succeeded him at Münchingen in 1637. The Leube-Liste (Stift roll) agrees on birthplace, Magister year and posts. ¶1b
