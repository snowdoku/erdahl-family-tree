About the family

Wife of Aadne Larsen Gangsei; named as mother at the baptism of their son Gullov, Sexagesima (16 Feb) 1749, Åmli church: 'Aadne Larsön og Asbor Evindsdatter Gangseys barn Gulöu' (Åmli 1741-1775, 1749 no. 13, canvas 26). Mothers were not named in this book before 1747, so she is assumed (not proved) to be the mother of Niels (bapt. 1746) too; an 'Ambor Evindsdatter Gangsei' stood godmother at Niels's baptism — possibly a sister or a slip for the mother. ¶1

Family evidence (card 3469/3471, DA 39942): Todne Nielsdatter of Gangsei, died by 21 Sep 1763, was married to 'Angrim Aanesen' (Angrim Aadnesen, the son of Aadne Larsen and, probably, Asbor, b. 1740), and the card names 'Enkemannens bror: Even Aadnesen' (the widower's brother Even, i.e. the Evind baptised 1737). So two of Aadne's sons were at Gangsei in 1763. The heirs were her mother's brother Aasul Alfsen Sigridnes's children, so the marriage was childless. ¶5

Parents found: Evind Jørgensen of Nordre Askland (estate 3 Aug 1729) and Todne Svendsdatter. The oppgitt skifte of 6 Oct 1736 at Nordre Askland (Skifteprotokoll nr. 9 fol. 134b, DA 24357 view 137, read on the scan; card 87) lists the children 'Todne Svendsdatter... med forrige Mand Ewind Jørgensøn tilsammen auflede': Jørgen Ewindsøn 25, Gullow Ewindsøn 21, 'Asbør Ewindsdatter gift med Aadne Larsøn Søndre Ascheland' and Anlow Ewindsdatter 14. Her 1729 age on card 77 (12) gives a birth about 1717. The 1762 skifte card 5149 repeats her parents, and one guardian there is Jørgen Evensen Askland, her brother. She named sons Even (Evind) and Gullov after her father and brother. ¶7

Life story

Of Asceland (Askeland/Åsland), Gjøvdal, at her betrothal 2 Apr 1736 and marriage 24 Jun 1736 to Aadne Larsen (Åmli 1711-1740 canvases 70-71). First son Evind (1737) presumably named for her father Evind. ¶2

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed, graded on her link to her son Niels Aadnesen (see his page). The skifte held at Hommedrum (Homdrum) in Gjøvdal on 10 Nov 1762 after Aane Larsen (Skifteprotokoll nr. 13a fol. 11b, DA 24362 view 26, read on the scan; cards 5147/5149) divides the estate between 'hans efterladte Enke Asbor Evendsdatter' and their children: Even Aanesen 24, Angrim 21 (serving at Gangsei), Niels 17, Gullov 15, Ole 10 and Guroe Aanesdatter 19. Continuity: Niels, 17 in Nov 1762, is the 'Aadne Larsön Gangseys barn Niels' baptised 2 Feb 1746, and the Niels Aadnesen of Oland who died in 1789, whose probate names his father's brothers Angrim Aadnesen Engenes and Gulleug Aadnesen of Morgedal/Valleberg, the same Angrim and Gullov as in 1762. No rival Asbor or Aadne Larsen fits. ¶6

Corrections 1

Correction (re-read, 13 Oct 2026): the marriage of Aadne Larsen and Asbor Evindsdatter, 'Dom. 5 p. Trinit.' 1736 in Gjøvdal (Åmli 1711-1740 pp. 136-137, canvas 71), fell on 1 Jul 1736, not 24 Jun: Easter 1736 was 1 Apr, so Trinity Sunday was 27 May and the 5th Sunday after Trinity was 1 Jul. The entry follows ones dated Fest. Joh. Bapt. (24 Jun), 28 Jun and 29 Jun. Crop:. ¶3

Searches that found nothing 1

Negative (cards read): the Østre Råbyggelag probate card index (DA 39942) block 'Gangsei i Gjøvdal' runs from about card 3431 to 3500 in date order. Cards 3461 (Skp. 8 fol. 280, 26 Feb 1732, Gunild Ejelsdatter), 3463 (Skp. 8 fol. 418b, 17 Sep 1733, Gunder Taraldsen), 3465/3467 (Skp. 10 fol. 163b, 7 Jun 1741, Niels Jensen, widow Birgitha Alvsdatter), 3469/3471 (Skp. 13a fol. 38b, 21 Sep 1763 - 9 Jan 1764, Todne Nielsdatter) and 3473 (Skp. 13a fol. 39b, 24 Mar 1763, Ole Olsen 'Selletred' under Gangsei) are followed by 3475 (Skp. 16a fol. 401b, 1790, Ole Bjørnsen). So no estate after Aadne Larsen, after Asbor Evindsdatter or after Lars Gangsei (Aadne's father) was held at Gangsei between 1741 and 1790; her own probate, which would name Niels, must be sought under another farm (Omland/Oland was read in full by earlier workers without finding it). Her grade on the link to Niels Aadnesen Ohland stays Probable: the 1746 baptism names only the father, and her motherhood of Niels rests on the 1736 marriage and her being named mother of Gullov (1749) and Ole (1753). ¶4
