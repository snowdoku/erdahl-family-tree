About the family

Family: his parents are named exactly as the couple the tree holds as Karolina Knodel née Schmidt's parents (Wilhelm Schmidt x Anna Maria / Marie née Adam in the Pfeilsdorf acts of 1906 and 1913), and his sister Rosa Therese gave the same parents and the same residence, Bilau, in 1913. So he was Karolina's brother; the act does not name Karolina. His birth at Ciucurova in 1886 matches the Ciucurova village family book's placing of Wilhelm Schmidt x Maria Adam there in 1885. ¶2

Life story

Marriage: on 13 May 1909 at Heimbrunn, seat of the Standesamt Lissewo, Kreis Culm, the settler's son (Ansiedlersohn) Friedrich Schmidt, Evangelical, identified by documents he produced, born 3 Nov 1886 at Ciucurova in Romania, living at Bilau, Kreis Culm, son of the settler Wilhelm Schmidt and his wife Marie née Adam of Bilau, married Anna Carolina Schmidt, born 25 Mar 1888 at Cogealia in Romania, also of Bilau. Witnesses: the settler Wilhelm Schmidt, 54, of Bilau, and the settler Heinrich Habermann, 36, of Bilau, whose identity Wilhelm vouched for (Lissewo marriages 1909 no. 6; image read). The act does not call the witness the groom's father, but the name, the title 'Ansiedler' and the residence Bilau are the same as the father's, so he was almost certainly the father. ¶1

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Cousin marriage (possible, unproved): his wife's father was a Karl Schmidt of Cogealia; if this was his father's (probable) brother Karl, born 1853 at Atmagea, the couple were first cousins. The bride's mother is 'Julianna, geborene Bandau' in this act; Karl's wife Juliana (b. 2 May 1859) is 'geb. Bondo' in three EWZ index entries and 'Ponto' in two and in the Ciucurova family book, so the names are probably variants of one surname. See the note on his father. ¶3
