About the family

Family: Son of Even (Evind) Tellefsen Ramse by his first, unnamed, wife. The Østre Råbyggelag card abstracting the Ramse deeds of 28 July 1704 lists him as the only child under "Barn i 1. ekteskap" (children of the first marriage): "Rolf Evindsen. Hans barn: Jørgen Rolfsen Ormstveit" and "Aslak" Rolfsen Ormstveit, whose estate was settled in 1713. A pencil addition says Jørgen lived at another farm (name hard to read) in 1680 or 1690. ¶1

Life story

Birth year: ABT 1645 from his ages in the manntall (20 and 17 in the two 1664 counts; 16 or 26 in 1666), replacing BEF 1640. ¶6

1666 vicar's census of Lille Topdal (Sogneprestenes manntall 1664-66 bd 14, p.46), farm no. 3 'Ytre Ramsøa Nedra, 4 huder': opsiddere Effuind 80 aar (uses 3 huder) and Tollef 30 aar (uses 1 hud); sons column, against Effuind: Svend 10 aar and Knud 4 aar; servants: Throen Rasolfsøn 26, [Hal?]da 18[?], Rolff 26[?16]. Rolff appears in the servants column, not the sons column, in 1666 (age read 26, possibly 16; he was 20 in the 1664 vicar's list and 'Rolf Effindsøn 17' in the bailiff's list). Probably the same Rolf Evindsen. ¶5

Dates (inferred): He is represented by his children in 1704, so he had probably died by then. If his son Jørgen had his own holding by about 1680-90, Rolf was born before about 1640. That fits a first marriage of Even's in the 1630s. ¶2

1704 deed: His sons Jørgen and Aslak Rolfsen of Ormstveit sold, by deed dated 7 March 1704 at Ormstveit, "all the right and share that our late father Rolf Evindsen could claim in his late father's odel farm" after "our grandfather the late Evind Ramse", to Nere Jonsen Tveit. The clerk's registration note calls Anna (Evindsdatter) "hans Stifmoder" — his stepmother. So Rolf was a son of Evind (Even) Tellefsen Ramse by an earlier wife than Anna, and he was dead by March 1704. Confidence raised to Confirmed on this primary record. ¶4
