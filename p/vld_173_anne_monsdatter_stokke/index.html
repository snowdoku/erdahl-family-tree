About the family

Her family (bygdebok, same page): she was one of six siblings who inherited from a childless sister, a Monsdotter, in a probate at Stokke in 1749. That sister had been married to an Iver... who died in 1738. The heirs were '1. Erik Monsson', '2. Helge Monsdotter', '3. Guri g. med Knut Flatset', '4. Anne g. med Nils Endreson Stokke', a fifth sibling married to Peder Toreson, and '6. Anne g. med Anders...'. 'Anne Monsdotter arva 24 rdl 13 skilling etter syster si.' 'Foreldra til Anne var [Mons] Erikson... 1636, d. 1707, gift 2. g. med Anne Jensdotter.' The first name of the father fell in a gap of the rebuilt text; Mons follows from the children's patronymics. ¶2a

Her father is now identified through records as well as the bygdebok. Her 1738 half-sister Anna (77795 view 4367) is the Anne Mogensdotter named in the 1674 Søbstad probate card as daughter of the widower Mogens Eriksen and Synnøve Knutsdotter (77795 view 4033); the Ekkilsøya bygdebok (canvas 237) names that half-sister's daughter's mother as 'Anne Monsdatter Søbstad'. See the notes on Mons (Magnus, Mogens) Eriksen Vebenstad. Her own grade (on her son Endre Nielsen) is unchanged, Probable: no record yet names Endre as her son. Checked without result: skifteprotokoll 10c 1760-62 (24646) register, views 2-5, has no Niels Endresen and no Anne at Stokke; 10b (24645) lacks Jan 1757 to Jun 1760, so a probate after Niels (d. 1760) is probably lost. ¶7

Life story

Probate read: Nordmøre skifteprotokoll 8c, 1746-1750 (24643), no. 46, fol. 1084a (view 240, right page foot) to 1084b (view 241, left page top). 'Anno 1749' the appraisers Elling Johansen Stoche and Ole Størchersen Stoche met 'paa gaarden Stoche i Gimnes Tinglaug' to register the estate after the late 'Marit Magnusdatter Boxaspen', who left no children ('ingen Livs Arvinger'), so the heirs were her brothers and sisters: 1. the brother Erich Magnusen Webenstad, dead, whose children are named (sons Gunder, Knud, Erich, Jens(?)...); 2. the sister Helge Magnusdatter, dead, whose children are named (Peder Pedersen, daughters married to Jacob Olsen Henden(?), Lars Halvorsen Aal, Peder Jensen Myrset, Arnt Arntsen Hoeset); 3. the sister Gurie Magnusdatter, dead, whose children are the sons Magnus Knudsen Flatsett and Ole Knudsen Fladset and daughters Anne Knudsdatter married to David Sandøen and Elrie Knudsdatter, unmarried; '4de den sal. qvindes Søster Anne Magnusdatter gift med Niels Endresen Stoche' (the scribe wrote this item twice, the second time numbered 5); 5. the sister Sofie(?) Mogensdatter, dead, whose children are named; '6te og endelig den sal. qvindes hel Søster Anne Mogensdatter', dead, whose daughter Anne Andersdatter is married to Christian Ingebrigtsvig. (Magnus and Mogens are the written forms of the spoken name Mons.) The bygdebok adds that Anne Monsdatter inherited 24 rdl 13 sk from her sister. The word 'hel Søster' (full sister) used only for no. 6 suggests that some of the sibling set were half-siblings (the father married twice, per the bygdebok). ¶4

Probate card read: an earlier probate in the same family, after her brother Jens Mogensen of Vebenstad, Bremsnes, who had lived and died abroad in Amsterdam (Nordmøre probate card index 77795, view 4367, with the back at view 4368; card dated 12 Dec 1738, citing Nordmøre sk.pr. 1736-41 fol. 202). Heirs: '1 fullbroren comparenten Erich Mogensen Vebenstad, myndig'; '2. fullsösteren Mari Mognsdr. g.m. Iver Boxaspen'; '3. [fullsösteren] Helge, enke'; '4. [fullsösteren] Gari Fladset, död, 3 sönner og 2 döttre' (Mogens, Knud and Ole Knudsen, Anna Knudsdatter 24, Alvi 20); '5. fullsösteren Toril Mognsdr. Söbstad, död, 2 sönner' (Mogens Pedersen 20 and Joen 14, at home with their stepfather Peder Toresen Søbstad); '6. fullsösteren Anna Mognsdr. g.m. Niels Endresen Stokke'; '7. halvsösteren Anna, död, 1 datter: Anna Andersdatter, umyndig, tjenende hos Erich Mogensen Vebenstad'. So Anne, the wife of Niels Endresen of Stokke, was a FULL sister of Erich, Jens, Mari, Helge, Guri and Toril Mogensdatter of Vebenstad; the other Anne was a half-sister. This is the 'Iver d. 1738' of the rebuilt bygdebok text: Mari's husband was Iver Boxaspen, and Jens died in 1738. ¶5

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

2 on the grade · 2 open questions

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Probable mother of Endre Nielsen, from the Gjemnes bygdebok (bind 9, Stokke, canvas 213): Nils Endreson, brukar about 1714-1760, 'Gift 1714 med Anne [...] ca. 1682, d. 1756, 82 år gamal'. 'Nils Endreson [og] Anne sine barn: 1. Ole f. 1714 (brukar 9); 2. Endre f. 1716, g. 1748 med Ingeborg Andersdotter Grønset. Dei [busette] seg på Grønset bnr. 5; 3. Torstein f. 1719.' Child no. 2 is Endre Nielsen, who married Ingeborg Andersdatter of Grønset in 1748 as 'Endre Niels. Stokke' (16075 view 1, p. 2). The link rests on the bygdebok, so it is Probable. ¶1

Graded on her link to her son Endre Nielsen: grade unchanged, Probable. The 1749 probate is a primary record that names her as 'Anne Magnusdatter gift med Niels Endresen Stoche', so her MARRIAGE to Niels Endresen of Stokke is now confirmed (1749), and her sibling set and patronymic (daughter of a Mons/Magnus) are proved. It does not name her children. Endre Nielsen Stokke (b. about 1716 per the bygdebok; married 1748) was born before the Kvernes register begins (books before 1737 burned), and the 1714 marriage is known only from the bygdebok, so the audit 5 rule (show the marriage covers the whole birth window, with no earlier marriage) cannot yet be met from a primary record. ¶6a

Open questions and the next record to read 2

Next record: the 1749 probate at Stokke, Nordmøre skifteprotokoll 8c (source 24643; first-name register at the back, views 359-366). It names her as heir 'g.m. Nils Endresen Stokke', which would confirm her marriage and her sibling set. ¶2b

Next: a probate after Anne (d. 1756) or Niels (d. 1760); the 24645 volume lacks 1757-Jun 1760; a deed from Niels to a son 1743 (Nordmøre panteregister 22675, views 53-116). ¶6b
