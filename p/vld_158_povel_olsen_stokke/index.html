Life story

Bygdebok account: Gjemnes bind 9, Stokke, canvas 376: 'Brukar 5: Paul Olson [var] brukar frå 1696 til 1734', b. about 1670, d. 1734. The book says he was 'truleg bror til [...] Olson Stokke' (the first name fell in a gap), and that he moved there about 1700. No parents are named in the rebuilt text. ¶6

More: (1) 'Paul Stoche' (Povel Stokke) was one of the guardians named in the probate after Svend Olsen of Stokke, 30 Mar 1719 (77795 view 3785), so Povel was at Stokke by 1719. (2) He is not in the 1701 manntall for Stokke (Census (manntall) 1701, no. 11: Nordmøre fogderi and Romsdal fogderi, p. 61, record pc00000005073108; the men listed are a widow's sons Oluf, Iver, Iver and Halvor Knudsen, Amund Lauresen, the widow Elen's sons, Svend Olsen, Aslach Torgiersen and servants), so he came to Stokke after 1701, perhaps through his wife Synnøve Iversdatter. (3) His daughter Kirsti Povelsdatter Stokke (37 and unmarried in Dec 1734) married Sven Ellingsen Dalen at Trinity 1735 (Kvernes 16075 view 2, p. 3, reconstructed marriage list 1731-36). His own father Ole remains unidentified: the Stokke men of 1666 (Vicar's census 1664-66 no. 28, Kvernes, record pc00000005051540) were Oluff Pederssen (b. 1615, son Peder b. 1656), Peder (b. 1617) and Iffuer (b. 1605, son Erich b. 1654), with a servant Iffuer Olssen (b. 1657); no Povel. ¶5

Deed, Afset 1725 (panteregister and pantebok scans read): the Nordmøre panteregister 1692-1750 (Digitalarkivet 22675, view 32, p. 43) lists under 'Afset, Aeg (Kvernes)' a 'skjøte fra Povel Olsen Stocke, Halvor Knudsen Stocke, Erich Iversen Kaldset, Iver Joensen Echrem, Størcher Ellingsen Røevigen og Størcher Pedersen Myrset til svogeren John Iversen', 27 Oct 1725, pantebok fol. 145b. The deed itself (Nordmøre pantebok B 1712-1731, source 20324, view 147, left page foot) begins 'Kiendes Vi underskrevne... Povel Olsen Stocke, Halvor Knudsen Stocke, Erich Iversen Kaldset, Iver Joensen Echrem, Størcher Ellingsen Røevigen, og Størcher Pedersen Myrset' who with their wives sell 'Vores odelsgods udi den gaard Afset' in Gimnes tinglag, Kvernes parish, to 'Vor Kiere Svoger Jon Iversen, hans hustru Gun[il]de Povelsdatter og deres sande Arvinger'; dated 27 Oct 1725, sealed by all six. So Povel Olsen (or his wife) held a share of odel land in Afset jointly with five other men, and his daughter Gunild Povelsdatter (dead by Dec 1734, leaving two children) was in 1725 the wife of Jon Iversen of Afset. The deed does not say through whom the odel came, so it does not name Povel's parents; it shows a group of co-heirs whose common ancestor held land in Afset. Halvor Knudsen Stocke was a son of Knud Stokke, son-in-law of Ole Iversen of Stokke (d. 1679). ¶7

Probate found: Nordmøre skifteprotokoll 24640 (no. 7, 1731-36), fol. 988 (view 500, right page): 'Anno 1734 den 8de December' a registration was held 'paa gaarden Stocke' in Gimnes tinglag after 'afgangne Povel Olsen', between his surviving widow 'Synnøve Iversdatter' on the one side and on the other 'deres sammen avlede Døttre' (their daughters begotten together, reading of the last word slightly uncertain) and the daughters' children, namely: the late Gunild Povelsdatter's two children (a son Povel, 8, and a daughter Marite, 11, per the index card); 'Kirsti Povelsdatter 37 aar gl.'; and 'Anne Povelsdatter gift med Elling Johansen Bøge[?]' (the card reads the byname as 'Bogen'), for whom her husband Elling Johansen acted. The index card (77795 view 3789) adds the widow's statement that her late husband had before his death conveyed to his third daughter what little he owned, and that the unmarried daughter Kirsti had received 1 øre of land rent in the farm Stokke against her married sister Anne for 22 rdl. The typed card: 'Stokke, Gjemnes. 1734 8/12 Pouvel Olsen. Enke: Synnøve Iversdatter. Barn: 1 Afgangne Gunild Povelsdatters 2 barn... 2 datteren Kirsti Pouvelsdatter 37 år gl, 3 [datteren] Anna [Pouvelsdatter] g.m. Elling Johansen Bogen'. ¶2

Farmer at Stokke, Gjemnes tinglag, Kvernes parish, Nordmøre. Died before 8 Dec 1734, when his estate was registered. Wife Synnøve Iversdatter. Daughters Gunild (died before 1734, leaving two children), Kirsti (b. about 1697, unmarried in 1734) and Anne (married Elling Johansen). ¶1

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Graded on his link to his daughter Anne Povelsdatter: Confirmed, because the probate names 'Anne Povelsdatter gift med Elling Johansen' as his daughter and heir. Continuity to the Anne of the tree: 17 years later, on 11 Nov 1751, the estate of 'Elling Johansen' at the same farm Stokke was registered with his widow 'Anne Povelsdatter' (24644 fol. 74a): the same couple, same farm, no other Elling Johansen or Anne Povelsdatter at Stokke in either probate or in the Stokke card series 1677-1744 (77795 views 3777-3793, all read). The 1734 entry also explains how Elling came to Stokke: through his wife's share of her father's farm. Anne's children carry her parents' names: eldest son Povel (b. about 1726) and a daughter Synnøve Ellingsdatter (1751 probate). ¶3

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Not the same man as Paul Olsen Esse (Leikanger, Sogn, d. 1761-62). His own parents are not found; a father Ole is implied by the patronymic. ¶4a

Open questions and the next record to read 2

Next: Stokke cards 77795 views 3777-3783 (1677-1688) show Ole Iversen (d. 1679, sons Iver and daughters Ingeborg and Anne) and Peder Pedersen; no Povel among Ole Iversen's children, so no link is proposed. ¶4b

Lead: the same register (22675) lists for Stokke a deed of 13 Oct 1725 (fol. 142) from Halsten Pedersen Afset to his brother-in-law Ole Olsen on 20 marklag in Stokke; the deed (20324 view 143, right page) says Halsten and his wife Elisabet Jacobsdatter of Afset sell 'min Odels Jord i Stocke' to 'min Svoger Ole Olsen som har min kiære Søster Marit Peders Datter'. And a deed of 22 Jul 1729 (view 50, fol. 277b) from Knud Erichsen, Ole Svendsen and Ole Størchersen Stocke to their brother-in-law Halvor Knudsen Stocke on 1 øre 14 marklag. Neither names Povel. Register views 53-116 (1730-1750) not yet read for Stokke. ¶8
