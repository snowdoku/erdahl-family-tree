About the family

Wife of Mons Andersen Grønfur and then of Paul Kristensen Grønfur (Aaland: 'Mons Andersson Grf.... * Brite Rasm.d. Erd. Paul Kristenson, br. 1735. * e. Brite'). Aaland calls her 'Erd.', from Erdal in Oppstryn. Not the same woman as Brite Rasmusdatter, daughter of Rasmus Olsen Erdal, who was married to Rasmus Kristoffersen at Tjellaug by 1714; whether this Brite was another daughter of the Erdal family is untested. ¶1

Life story

Guardianship protocol no. 1, fol. 176a (scan read): the ward Joen Andersen Grønfur inherited 22 rd 5 ort 7 s at the 3 Jul 1727 skifte, 'men disse Midler var hos Moderen paa Grønfur', and the child died in 1728; Marrite Monsdatter then inherited from him on 16 May 1729 ('efter sin Halfbroder'; Rasmus Monsen's entry, fol. 7b, says the same). So the Monsen children's mother was alive at Grønfur in 1727-28 and had earlier had a son Joen by a husband named Anders. That fits the widow of Mons Andersen who married Paul Kristensen (Paul is 'Stefader' of both Rasmus and Marrite in protocol no. 2), and so supports Brite as the mother. Unresolved point, which blocks any raise: on the same day, 3 Jul 1727, Jørgen Erdal's children inherited 'efter deres Morfader Lars' (fol. 7b, Arvebog fol. 494), and Rasmus Monsen's and Marrite's shares sit at Arvebog fol. 495-497 beside it. If the 3 Jul 1727 skifte was after Lars Rasmussen (see his page) rather than after Mons Andersen, the Monsen children could be Lars's grandchildren, and their mother a Larsdatter, not Aaland's 'Brite Rasm.d. Erd.'. The equal 22 rd shares of Joen Andersen and Rasmus Monsen are more like a mother's or stepfather's estate than a grandfather's, so this is noted, not adopted. Jørgen Erdal as Rasmus Monsen's guardian and Rasmus Folvem (earlier guardian of Mari Rasmusdatter Erdal, 1714) as Marrite's fit an Erdal-born mother either way. ¶3a

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the grade · 2 open questions

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Probable as mother of Marthe Mogensdatter (see her page), graded on her link to her: the 1743 guardianship note (protocol no. 2, p. 111) calls Paul Grønfur Marrite Monsdatter's stepfather ('hendes Stiffader Poul Grønfur'), so her mother was Paul's wife; that Paul's wife was Brite Rasmusdatter, Mons Andersen's widow, comes only from Aaland. Marthe named her daughter Birthe (Brithe Thrulsdatter, eldest daughter in the 1782 probate), which fits. ¶2a

Open questions and the next record to read 2

Would confirm: Mons Andersen's probate, or Paul Kristensen's or Brite's own probate (Grønfur, after 1743) naming her children. ¶2b

Test record: the 3 Jul 1727 Grønfur skifte (Nordfjord Arvebok fol. 491-497, unscanned) or Paul Kristensen's or Brite's own probate (Grønfur, after 1743). ¶3b
