About the family

Parents added, Confirmed: Jørgen Knudsen Langeteig and Brite Friksdatter. Her own grade (link to Ingeborg Nilsdatter) is unchanged. Second marriage: by 14 Feb 1750 she was the wife of Lars [Siur?]sen Røe, who survived her as 'efterladende Enkemand' at her probate of 28 Aug 1769. Continuity: (1) 17 Sep 1745, Røe: the widow Anne Jørgensdatter of Niels Olsen Røe (6b fol. 480a; her guardian there was a Jørgen of Langeteig); (2) 14 Feb 1750, Langeteig: Jørgen Knudsen Langeteig's probate names 'Eldste Datter Anne den eldre Jørgensdatter gift med Lars [Siur?]sen Røe' (5 years later, same farm Røe, now remarried); (3) 28 Aug 1769, Røe: the probate after Anna Jørgensdatter is held between 'hendes efterladende Enkemand Lars [Siur?]sen Røe' and her children with her late husband Niels Olsen (19 years later, same farm, same second husband). So the 1750 daughter married to Lars of Røe is the same woman as the 1745 widow and the 1769 decedent: pair match (Anne + Lars at Røe at both ends, audit 8 rule 3) plus the Langeteig guardian in 1745. ¶2a

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: the probate after the widow Anna Jørgensdatter at Røe, 28 Aug 1769 (Ytre Sogn 8b p. 561b, 24166 view 180), is held between 'hendes... med... afdøde Mand Niels Olsen sammen avlede 2de Sønner og 4re Døttre' and names 'Ingebor Nielsdatter gift med Anfind Olsen Tiugum' (share heading 'Anden Datter Ingebor Nielsdatter'). Continuity to the tree's Ingeborg: 12 years later, on 18 Oct 1781, the probate after Ingeborg Nielsdatter at Kalvetræet under Tjugum is held with her widower Anfind Olsen (24167 view 739), same couple, same place (Tjugum), with her brother 'Ole Nielsen Røe' (named in 1745 and 1769) as curator of her daughter Christie. ¶1a

Alternatives considered and set aside 2

No rival: no other Ingeborg Nielsdatter married to an Anfind Olsen is known in Tjugum skipreide. Graded Confirmed on her link to her daughter Ingeborg Nilsdatter (see her page). Widow of Niels Olsen Røe at his probate, 17 Sep 1745 (Ytre Sogn 6b fol. 480a, No. 5), held between her and 'de med hende' children Knud 15, Ole, Kari 18, Ingebor 12, Brithe the elder 9 and Brithe the younger (the eldest daughter Gidlou, 20, was of Niels's former marriage and took mother's inheritance separately), so the probate names her as Ingebor's mother. The bridge from that Ingebor to the tree's Ingeborg Nilsdatter is the same as on her husband's record. Røe identified: Røe is a farm in Leikanger parish, Tjugum skipreide (1801 census 'Leganger: Røe'); the Ytre Sogn probate index spells it 'Rød' (Lars Olsen and Magdeli Hansdtr of the 1801 Røe household appear there as 'Rød' 1809-13), which is why searches for 'Røe' found nothing. The probate after Niels Olsen Røe, 17 Sep 1745 (Ytre Sogn 6b fol. 480a-481a, No. 5, 24162 views 128-129) names among the children he had with his widow Anne Jørgensdatter '3die Datter Ingebor Nielsdatter', 12 (b. about 1733), with her brothers Knud Nielsen (15) and Ole Nielsen. Continuity: (1) 1745, Røe: Ingebor Nielsdatter 12, brothers Knud and Ole Nielsen; (2) 11 Apr 1757, Tjugum: betrothal of 'Anfind Olsøn Bahle & Ingeborg Røe' (11566 c8), 12 years later, same farm, no patronymic; Knud Nielsen Røe betrothed in 1758 (11566 c20); (3) 18 Oct 1781, Kalvetræet: probate after Ingeborg Nielsdatter, wife of Anfind Olsen, with 'Ole Nielsen Røe' as curator of her daughter Christie (24167 view 740). So the 1745 sister pair Ingebor + Ole Nielsen of Røe recurs in 1781 as Ingeborg Nielsdatter and Ole Nielsen Røe, curator for her child (curators were usually the nearest kin), and the 1757 'Ingeborg Røe' links the farm to the marriage. (This 1745-1757-1781 chain alone would be Probable; the 1769 probate of her mother, above, is what confirms it.) Her age fits (b. ~1733; daughter Christie b. ~1762, son Niels b. ~1766, named after her father). Further records: Ole Nielsen Røe's probate, 30 May 1810 (Ytre Sogn 14 fol. 119a, 24174 view 121; the heirs there are his own widow and children, read only at heading level), the probate after Anne Jørgensdatter (not yet found; search domicile 'Rød' 1745-1790), or Knud Nielsen's probate, any of which might name 'Søsteren Ingeborg Nielsdatter' or her children Niels and Christi Anfindsbørn. ¶1b

Rival: her younger sister, also Anne Jørgensdatter, but the 1750 record places her at Langeteig as wife of Michel Johansen, so she is excluded by name of husband and farm. Reading caution: Lars's patronymic is unclear in both entries (1750 'Lars O/S..ürsen', 1769 'Lars..rson'); the farm Røe and the first name agree. ¶2b
