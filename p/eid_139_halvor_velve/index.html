Life story

Marriage, Trinity Sunday (12 Jun) 1740: 'Ægte viet mandhaftig soldat Haldvor Jansen Roldsæt[?] med pigen Helge Ols datter Stødren[?]' - married the soldier Halvor Jansen (= Jonsen) [of Roldset?] and the maid Helge Olsdatter [of Stødren?] (16315 c3). Taken to be this couple (Probable): the names match the Velve couple whose children Marit (1741) and Jon (1743) were baptised at Velve, and no other Halvor x Helge marriage is known; the farm names are uncertain readings and Velve is not named. This gives him the patronymic Jonsen (correction: name set to Halvor Jonsen Velve). He was dead by 1762 (his widow heads Velve 797 in the extra tax). ¶3

The 1761 entry is not a death probate. It records that Halvor Jonsen Velve, 'Delinqventen', had confessed to stealing 52 riksdaler, which he took from a storehouse ('stabur') on the farm Stubbe on 31 Aug 1758 during the summer ting in Selbu. The money belonged to Peder Povelsen Stubbe. He was sentenced under the Norwegian Law of 1687, book 6, ch. 17, art. 37, and his estate was seized to repay Peder Povelsen and to pay fines. Present, among others: Haldor Berge, Anders Moeslette, Jon Stokken, Niels Belle, Bjørn Moesletten, Ole Stokken, Peder Jonsen Moeslet and Ole Røset. He was dead by Dec 1764, when his wife's probate calls her his widow; he was probably dead by 1762, when the extra tax lists her as a widow. His death date and place are not known; if he was sentenced to forced labour in irons, he may have died at a fortress outside Selbu. ¶6

Known only from the patronymics of his children: in the 1762 extra tax list his widow[?] Helge Olsdatter heads Velve 797, Selbu, with their children Jon Halvorsen and Mali Halvorsdatter. His own patronymic is not given. ¶1

Death: probably before 1762, since his wife heads the household that year and seems to be marked as a widow (inference from the 1762 list only). ¶2a

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed. Two probate-court records tie him to his son Jon. (1) Skifteprotokoll 5 (24755) fol. 231b, 2 Sep 1761: a registration on the farm Welve in Selbu 'over Halvor Joensen Welves Boe og Formue', in the presence of his wife, the lensmann Ole Thomassen and lagrettemann Syver Welve. (2) The probate after his wife Helge Olsdatter, 17 Dec 1764 (fol. 451a), names her children as Jon Halvorsen, 20, and Mallie Halvorsdatter, 16, and the volume's register calls her 'avd. Halvors hustru', the late Halvor's wife. With the 1740 marriage of Haldvor Jansen and Helge Olsdatter, this settles that Jon Halvorsen Velve (b. about 1744) was his son. Both records. ¶5

Open questions and the next record to read 2

Would confirm: his burial before 1762 (the Selbu books have a gap for 1747-56) or a probate after him. ¶2b

Biography gaps: his birth date, birth place and baptism have not been searched, and his confirmation is not recorded. His death (probably before 1762) and burial are not found: 16315 (1739-46) and 16311 (1757-61, c2-94 read) show no burial, and the Selbu book has no 1747-56 entries that could hold it. The next record is his probate in Stjor- og Verdal skifte 3 (source 24753, 1742-60, unindexed, not read), which would fix the death year and name the heirs. ¶4
