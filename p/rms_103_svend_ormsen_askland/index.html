About the family

NEW ANCESTOR, graded on his link to his daughter Todne Svendsdatter (see her page). Askland card 79 (Skp. 8 fol. 67, 1729) notes 'Enkens far: Svend Ormsen Askland + sk. 1705; mor: Asbor Thallaksdtr. + sk. 1707', and card 78 names her brother Salve Svendsøn, who was a lagrettemann from Nordre Askland in 1736. Archivist's note only, so Probable. ¶1a

Father, Probable: Orm of Nordre Askland, aged 46 in the 1666 vicar's census, whose sons that year were 'Svend er 12 aar', Laurits 6 and Halvor 4 (DA 35510 view 33, p. 44). That gives Svend a birth about 1654. A Lauritz Ormsøn was guardian of Svend's children at the 1707 skifte after his widow (Skp. 2 fol. 196b). See Orm. This raises the parent link only; his own grade (on his link to Todne Svendsdatter) is unchanged. ¶3

Life story

Bygdebok (Åmli ætt og heim II, Nordre Askland L.nr. 50, canvas 143): '[Svenn] Ormson f 1654 d 1705, g m Asborg Tallaksdtr. Jørundland d 1707'; children Salve b. 1692 (m. 1718 Sigrid Eivindsdtr. Jørundland?, 2. 1724 Ingeborg [?] Tveit, Tovdal), a child b. 1683 d. 1718, Tone b. 1688 (m. 1. 1710 Eivind Jørgenson Askland, 2. 1736 Gunstein Olson, 3. 1745 [?] Halvorson), Birgit (m. Salve Olson Askland). Agrees with the 1705 and 1707 skifter. No grade change. ¶6

Guardians at his skifte: Skp. 2 fol. 147b (DA 24350 view 148), 13 Oct 1705: 'Lau[ritz] Ormssøn og Halvor Ormssøn' were nominated as guardians of his children and excused for incapacity; Svend Torbiørnsøn Askeland (for Salve and Anløv) and Knud...søn Omland (for Thoene and Birgitha) were appointed instead. Lauritz and Halvor match Orm of Nordre Askland's sons in 1666 (Svend 12, Laurits 6, Halvor 4), which strengthens Orm (see his page) as his father; the record does not say 'farbror', so the parent link stays Probable. His own grade (on his link to Todne Svendsdatter) is unchanged. ¶4

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: graded on his link to his daughter Todne Svendsdatter. His skifte, held at Askeland in Gjøvdal on 13 Oct 1705 (Skifteprotokoll nr. 2 fol. 147a, DA 24350 view 147, heading read on the scan; card 39942/33), names his widow Asbør Thallaksdatter and 'deris sammen auflede børn' Salve, Anløv, Thoene and Birgitha. Continuity to the Todne Svendsdatter of Askland in the tree: in 1729 the skifte after her husband Evind Jørgensøn of Askland (Skp. 8 fol. 67, cards 77-79) names 'bror til kona: Salve Svendsøn', so the same brother-sister pair, Salve and Todne, children of a Svend, on the same farm. No other Todne Svendsdatter is known in Gjøvdal. The 24-year span is bridged by that named brother, not by the farm alone. ¶2

Corrections 1

Correction to reading of the 1707 skifte: the guardian paragraph of the 28 Mar 1707 skifte after his widow (Skp. 2 fol. 197a, DA 24350 view 197, read at zoom) only says that the guardians named at Svend's skifte are to receive the children's shares; it names nobody. The 'Lauritz' on fol. 196b-197a appears in each child's share as a debt standing out 'hos Lauritz...' (surname not clearly read), not as a guardian. The 1705 entry (fol. 147b) is the record that names Lauritz and Halvor Ormssøn. ¶5

Open questions and the next record to read 2

Lead: the 1664 manntall lists an Orm, 44, among the users of Askeland nordre (Råbyggelaget fogderi s. 80), a possible father. ¶1b

Next: his 1705 skifte (Skp. 2, 1697-1706). ¶1c
