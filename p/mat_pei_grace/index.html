About the family

Mother of Benjamin Hayter. Married Thomas Hayter on 4 December 1862 (PEI marriage registers index, as Grace Amelia Moore). The Island Register's Hicken lineage names Benjamin's mother as Grace Moore. ¶1

Parents (Probable): Richard Moore and his wife Sarah, both born in England, tenant farmers in Lot 64. The 1861 census of Lot 64 lists Richard Moore, farmer, as the only Moore head in the township, with a household of eight: a man over 60 and a woman aged 45-60 (the two persons in the family born in England) and six PEI-born unmarried children - two daughters, one aged 16-21 and one aged 21-45, and four sons, two aged 16-21 and two aged 5-16. Grace, aged 23 and unmarried until December 1862, fits the elder daughter. A baptism index entry (Murray Harbour Bible Christian register, record 72100) names 'Richard and Sarah' as the parents of Grace's probable sister Mary Ann, born 20 September 1845, which ties the couple to this household. Rated Probable, not Confirmed: the 1861 PEI census names only the head, and Grace's own birth (as a Baptist she had no infant baptism) has no record naming her parents. ¶6

Siblings (Probable): the 1881 census of Lot 64 shows four consecutive Baptist, English-origin Moore households of PEI-born men - Humphrey (42), Benjamin (39), William (34) and Charles (31) - with Sarah Moore, 79, living with William. Their ages fill exactly the four sons of Richard Moore's 1861 household, and Grace named her own sons William, Humphrey, Benjamin and Charles, and her first daughter Sarah. ¶7

Sister: Mary Ann Moore, born 20 September 1845 to Richard and Sarah (baptism index), who married John Machon of Murray Harbour in 1877. Her daughter Lauretta (Machon) Brooks recorded in her diary the deaths of Grace's sons Benjamin (30 May 1936), Thomas George (4 April 1930), Rev. Joseph W. (3 April 1948), William and James (both 1951), and of Grace's daughter Mary Jane (Mrs Dan MacKenzie, 15 September 1946). ¶12

Life story

Born 13 January 1838 (1901 census), in Prince Edward Island (1881 and 1891 censuses; the 1901 enumerator's ditto marks carry 'England' down the column to her and her sons, which is an error). Her parents are not yet identified; Moore families lived in Lot 64 and the Murray River area. ¶2

1841 census: she is probably one of the three girls under 16 in Richard Moore's household of ten at Lot 64 (1841 census of PEI, Kings County District 15, image 531), aged 3. The household had 4 natives of the Island (image 533), which fits her birth on the Island in January 1838. The family was entered as Baptist (image 532). Only the head is named. ¶14

Marriage: the register entry itself has now been read (PEI Marriage Registers, DGS 004594919, image 82). Thomas Hayter, bachelor, and Grace Amelia Moore, spinster, were married by licence on 4 December 1862 by Daniel Gordon, J.P. The witnesses were Martin Martin and Neil Mathewson. The entry was recorded at the Provincial Secretary's office on 2 February 1863. The printed form has no columns for residence, age or parents. No Hayter or Moore signed as a witness, so the entry says nothing about either party's parents. The Islander's notice places the wedding at Georgetown. ¶15

Religion: the family was Baptist in 1861 (two members), 1881 and 1901, which is why no infant baptism exists for Grace or her brothers; Baptists baptised believers, usually as adults. ¶9

Marriage notice: The Islander of 19 December 1862 reported that "Miss Grace Amelia Moore" of Murray Harbour had married Mr. Thomas Hayter, also of Murray Harbour, at Georgetown on 4 December 1862. The ceremony was performed by D. Gordon, Esq., a justice of the peace. ¶13

Childhood home: Lake's 1863 map shows 'R. Moore' and 'T. Hayter' on adjoining farms at Toronto, Lot 64 - Grace married the neighbour's son. ¶10

Censuses: wife of Thomas Hayter in Lot 64 in 1881 (aged about 43), 1891 (as Grace A.) and 1901 (aged 63, at High Bank). Mother of at least ten children born 1864-1884. Not found in the 1911 index; date of death unknown. ¶5

Corrections 1

Correction: the England-born Grace Moore, aged 40 in 1881 at Gaspereaux (Township 61), is Grace (Chapman) Moore, who married Samuel Moore of Albion on 30 June 1847 (PEI marriage register index). Samuel was a son of George Moors and Sarah Sabine of Portsea, Hampshire, a separate Methodist Moore family. She is not this Grace and no blood link between the two Moore families has been found. ¶8

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Not to be confused with the Grace Moore, aged 40 and born in England, whom the 1881 census lists in the Gaspereaux township: in 1881 this Grace was already Grace Hayter, wife of Thomas, in Lot 64, and she was born in PEI. ¶3

Searches that found nothing 1

Death (not found): she was living at High Bank in the 1901 census (31 March 1901, aged 63). In the 1911 census her unmarried son James, b. 1878, lived alone in the same district, and no Grace Hayter born about 1838 appears anywhere in the 1911 census index, so she probably died between 1901 and 1911. There is no entry for her in the PEI death card index, the PEI baptisms-marriages-burials index or Find a Grave. The PEI death registrations from 1906 (Public Archives and Records Office, Charlottetown) are the next record. ¶11

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Lead for her parentage, not evidence: the 1881 census of Lot 64 shows a large Moore family there, including a Humphrey Moore born about 1839 and a Sarah Moore born about 1802. Grace named a son Humphrey (b. 1870), a rare name, which suggests a Moore relative of that name. Her baptism (about January 1838) or a Moore family record is needed. ¶4
