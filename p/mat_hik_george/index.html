Life story

Grandfather of Lydia Adelaide (Hicken) Hayter. A farmer at Gaspereaux, Township 61 (the Sturgeon district), Kings County, and a Wesleyan Methodist. ¶1

Marriage and family: married Frances Gordon on 31 January 1837 at Gaspereaux and had eleven children, according to the compiled Hicken genealogy - Eliza (1837-1861, d. Newburyport, Mass.), Phebe (c.1839-1876), Thomas, Jane (c.1841-1868), George L., Mary, Margaret Whitman (1849), Lydia Levina (1853), William John (c.1855-1891), Francis A. (c.1857-1893) and Alice (m. William A. Cross 1885 'at the house of the bride's father at St Mary's Bay, Sturgeon'). The gravestones of Jane and Phebe at Sturgeon each call them a daughter 'of George & Francis Hicken'. He outlived at least four of his adult children. ¶4

Marriage: George Hicken married Frances Thompson on 31 January 1837 (PEI Marriage Registers 1832-1888, Ancestry 61502, record 5377; FamilySearch film 004594917, image 146). The compiled genealogy places the marriage at Gaspereaux and calls the bride Frances Gordon. The register calls her Thompson. ¶7

1841 census: 'George Hicken Junr', a farmer (by ditto mark), was head of a household of five on Lot 61 (1841 census of PEI, Kings County District 15, image 512). It comprised one man and one woman aged 16-45 and three girls under 16. That matches George, Frances and their daughters Eliza (born 1837), Phebe (about 1839) and a third girl, probably Jane (about 1841). All five were entered as Church of England (image 513), before the family's later Methodism. No land is entered against him, so he probably worked on his father's leasehold. He is listed immediately after 'George Hicken Senr' (a man and a woman over 60), which supports, without proving, that the elder George was his father. ¶18

1861 census of P.E.I. (Kings County, Lot 61): George Hicken, farmer, head of a household of ten (five males, five females; two married, eight single), all Methodists. He held 100 acres on leasehold at about a shilling an acre. His neighbours included Jacob Sensabaugh and James and Robert Dixon. ¶8

1881 census (Township 61, household 38): George Hicken, 69, farmer, of English origin, with his wife Frances (67), sons George (29), William (26) and Francis (24), daughter Alice (22), and William's wife Bexy (19). The census gives his birthplace as Prince Edward Island; his sister Mary Ann (Mrs Moore, 67) and brother William (75), listed nearby, are both given as born in England, and the compiled Hicken genealogy has all the siblings born in England, so England is preferred here and the census entry is taken to be an enumerator's slip. Not proved. ¶2

1881 census (Township 61, household 38): George Hicken, 69, farmer, of English origin, recorded as born in P.E.I. (an enumerator's slip; 1891 says England), Wesleyan Methodist. With him were his wife Frances, 67, and their children George, 29, William, 26, Francis, 24 and Alice, 22, and William's wife Bexy, 19. ¶9

Context: the 1883 history of the Bible Christian Church in P.E.I. records that about 1848-50 the new Sturgeon church was still unplastered. 'Mr. Hicken, of St May's [Mary's] Bay' offered to plaster it if someone would attend him, and the young preacher Jesse Whitlock mixed the lime and carried the mortar himself. George's farm was at St Mary's Bay, but the book does not give the man's first name. ¶14

1889: on the list of voters for Sturgeon (Lot 61) as 'Hicken, George, sr', with his sons John W. (owner's son, 108 acres) and Francis A. (owner and freeholder, 46 acres), post office Gaspereaux. ¶3

1891 census (Township 61, enumerated 30 April 1891): George Hicken, 79, a widower, born in England, his father and mother both born in England, Methodist. He was living as 'father' in the household of his son William, 36. This census entry names the father-son relationship. ¶10

Birthplace: England, per the 1891 census; his siblings William (75) and Mary Ann Moore (67) were also English-born per the 1881 census. His age (69 in 1881, 79 in 1891) puts his birth about 1811-1812. ¶11

Death: 19 May 1899 at Gaspereaux, per the compiled Hicken genealogy. No gravestone for him or his wife appears in the 2002 transcription of either Sturgeon cemetery or on Find a Grave. ¶5

Death: 19 May 1899 at Gaspereaux, per the compiled genealogy; he was alive at 79 in April 1891. No PEI death card, church burial entry or gravestone has been found for him. The death falls after the death-card series thins out and before civil registration began in 1906. ¶15

Origin: his granddaughter Lydia Hayter wrote in 1966 that her grandfather "came from Worcestershire, England in 1812" (BC Pioneer Medallion application). He was very probably born in Worcestershire about 1812 - the family was at Doddenham, Worcestershire, in 1814 - and brought to Prince Edward Island as a child between 1815 and about 1822. ¶17

Birth and baptism: not found. He gave his age as 69 in 1881 and 79 in 1891, born England, so he was born about 1811-12, when the family was probably living in north Herefordshire or the Teme valley of Worcestershire (his brother William was baptised at Little Cowarne in 1806 and his sister Mary Ann at Doddenham in 1814). No baptism of a George to George and Phebe Hicken between 1807 and 1813 is in the England Births and Christenings index or FreeREG. An elder brother George was baptised at Deal, Kent, in December 1797 to the same parents; the Island George, of the 1881 and 1891 ages, cannot be that child unless he understated his age by fourteen years, so the first George probably died young and the name was reused. The registers of Little Cowarne and of the parishes round Doddenham, Knightwick and Martley for 1807-1813 (Herefordshire Archive and Records Centre; Worcestershire Archive) are where his baptism should be. ¶16
