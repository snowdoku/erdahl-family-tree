About the family

Her motherhood is now supported by the churching entries, not only by the engagement. The Borgund church book records her churched after childbirth at Store Kalvøen as 'Bereth Olufsdtr.' on 20 October 1726 and as 'Brith Olsdtr.' on 4 January 1728 - about five and six weeks after Povel Jørgensen's children were baptized on 15 September 1726 and 25 November 1727. 'Introdusert' means the mother's formal readmission to church after a birth, so these entries identify her as the mother of his children. ¶2

Parents: the Haram bygdebok, Band 2b 'Gamlem til Store Kalvøy', p. 370 (nb.no b709b66866b959f610ebd56887cb57fe, canvas 370; text rebuilt from content-search word boxes) lists among the children of Ole Jetmundson, brukar on Store Kalvøy from about 1690 (ca. 1658 - 1735): '3. Berte, ca. 1695 - 1777, [g.] 1725 [m.] Pål Jørgenson, ca. 1702 - 1749'. Her brothers were Jetmund (ca. 1689 - 1761, the Jetmund Olsen of Store Kalvøy whose probate was held in 1761) and Hans (ca. 1693 - 1763; 'Hans Ols. 8' in 1704). The 1704 list of marines has 'Ole, bonde, 50' on Store Kalvøy with sons Jetmund Ols. 14 and Hans Ols. 8. Ole Jetmundsen (see his page) is entered as her father, Probable (bygdebok statement, consistent with the 1704 roll and with her being of Kalvøy in 1720 and 1725). The bygdebok also calls her 'dotter [av] Karen [..] Ole Jetmundson' in the bruk 5 chapter (p. 376), so Karen (see her page) is entered as her mother, Probable. Her own grade (as mother of Jørgen Paulsen Djup) is unchanged. ¶10

Life story

A maid of Store Kalvøen, engaged to Povel Jørgensen 22 Apr 1725 at Borgund. She is the probable mother of his children born 1729-1743, including Jørgen (1735). The baptism entries name only the father. Her parents are unknown. ¶1

Probate: the 1749 probate after her husband Poul Jørgensen of Store Kalvøen (3A 17, fol. 66b) names her as his surviving wife 'Brithe Ols Datter', with her legal guardian Peder Erichsen Sevrig. She was therefore alive in November 1749. The record does not name her parents or brothers. Two of the children's guardians, Ole Olsen Gamlem and Ole Olsen Velle (farm uncertain), share her patronymic. That could point to brothers but is not evidence. Her confidence is unchanged. ¶3

Godmother (scan read): 'Berethe Olsdatter Kalfoen, en pige' (an unmarried woman of Kalvøy) stood godmother at the baptism of Johanna, twin daughter of Thore Andersen Halsnesholmen, 20 Apr 1720 (Borgund 15995 canvas c152), with Mads Uggedal and Ole Knudsen Uggedal. This places a single Berethe Olsdatter at Kalvøy five years before the 1725 engagement, which fits her being of Store Kalvøy rather than the Humlen Brite of 1701. Parentage still unproved. ¶4

Remarriage (index): on 6 Oct 1750 at Borgund 'Jon Larss.', bachelor (ungkarl) of Store Kalføen, married 'Birthe Olsd.', widow (enche) of Store Kalføen (Borgund ministerialbok 1739-1761, pv00000002823721). Her husband Poul Jørgensen's probate had been held 7 Nov 1749 at Store Kalvøy, naming his widow Brithe Olsdatter, so this is her remarriage eleven months later on the same farm. This settles the lead: the 'Joen Larsen and Birthe Olsdatter' of Stor Kalvøen household 3 in the 1762 extra tax list (DA 49637 view 47, two persons, no children over 12) are she and her second husband, and her son Jørgen (then about 27) had left the household. Jon Larsen was buried from Stor Kalvøen 13 Sep 1774, birth year calculated 1718 (pg00000002549407), so he was about 17 years younger than she was. ¶7

1762 extra tax list (DA 49637 view 47, p. 81, Borgund sogn, 'Stor Kalvøen'): four households, none headed by a widow Brithe and none with a son Jørgen. 1 Hans Jetmundsen and Birthe Einersdatter; 2 Niels Einersen and Anne Clausdatter; 3 Joen Larsen and Birthe Olsdatter (2 persons, no children over 12); 4 Joen Jonsen and Anne Paalsdatter, with a maid entered as 'Hendes Søster Anne Paalsd.' (her sister). Household 4 holds Paul Jørgensen's daughters (Anne Paulsdatter of Store Kalvøy died 1790), and Joen Jonsen of Store Kalvøen was a witness at Jørgen's 1768 engagement. Lead, not proof: the 'Birthe Olsdatter' of household 3 may be this widow remarried to Joen Larsen; a Borgund marriage of Jon Larsen and Brithe Olsdatter about 1750-1762 would test it. Her grade (Probable, as mother of Jørgen Paulsen Djup) is unchanged, since the list does not tie her to Jørgen. ¶6

Burial (index): 'Birthe Olsd.', widow ('Ek'), of Stor Kalvøen, buried at Borgund 16 Nov 1777, birth year calculated 1695 (Borgund ministerialbok 1762-1800, pg00000002549697). It fits her: a widow Birthe Olsdatter of the same farm three years after Jon Larsen's death, and she was the only Birthe Olsdatter on Store Kalvøy in 1762 (the other wife there was Birthe Einersdatter). The age (about 82) puts her birth about 1695, so she was about 30 at her 1725 engagement and about 48 at the 1743 baptism, which is late; the burial age may be inflated. Her grade (Probable, as mother of Jørgen Paulsen Djup) is unchanged, since neither record names Jørgen. Her parents are still unknown. The 1761 probate after Jetmund Olsen of Store Kalvøy (b. about 1688; 3A 19 fol. 471b, 24560/495, read) lists only his own children (Ole Jetmundsen Uggedal, Hans Jetmundsen Kalvøen, Anne Jetmundsdatter dead with 3 children) and no siblings, so it does not show whether he was her brother. ¶8

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

Open questions and the next record to read 2

Leads, no change. (1) 1704 list of marines, Store Kalvøy ('Kalføen'): Ole, bonde, 50 (b. about 1654), with sons Jetmund Ols. 14 and Hans Ols. 8. This Ole is the only Ole farming Store Kalvøy in 1701-1704 and is the likeliest father of the Berethe Olsdatter who was an unmarried woman of Kalvøy in 1720 and a maid there in 1725, but nothing yet names her as his daughter. (2) The probate after Jetmund Olsen of Store Kalvøen (b. 1688/90; 9 Jul 1761, 3A 19 fol. 471b, view 24560/495) was read: his heirs are his own children and grandchildren, so it names no siblings and does not reach Berethe. (3) 1762 extra tax, Store Kalvøen (source 49637 view 47): household 3 is 'Jon Larsen, H: Birthe Olsdatt.' and household 4 'Jon Jonsen, H: Anne Paalsdatt.' with 'Hendes Søster Anne Paalsd.'; the Paals-daughters are probably Paul Jørgensen's; Birthe Olsdatter wife of Jon Larsen may be Berethe remarried after 1749 (a Borgund marriage of Jon Larsen and Brithe Olsdatter c.1750-55 would test it). (4) The Sunnmøre probate index has no probate after an Ole of Store Kalvøy (rows 1691-1790); the probate after Brite Guttormsdatter of Store Kalvøy, 7 Nov 1749 (3A 17 fol. 67a, view 24558/80, scan read), held the same day as Paul Jørgensen's, is for the wife of Jetmund Olsen and names only their children (Anne, Ole, Hans, Giertrud Jetmunds-children), so it is not Berethe's mother. ¶9a
