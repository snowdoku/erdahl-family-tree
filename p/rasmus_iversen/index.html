Life story

Baptised 28 Feb 1819. Son of farmer Iver Rasmussen and Marthe Olsdatter of Botnen. Confirmed 5 Oct 1834 (both parents named). ¶2

Married 24 Jun 1848 in Gulen parish to Karie Lassesdatter (b. 1828, Asheim). His father is named as Iver Rasmussøn Botnen and hers as Lasse Torkildsøn Asheim. ¶3

1865 census: farmer and freeholder at Botn, Lavik, b. 1819 Brekke. Died 17 Jun 1883 as Rasmus Ivarsen Botnen, farmer. ¶4

Of Botn, Brekke, Sogn. Named as Ingeborg's father in her 1871 marriage. ¶1
