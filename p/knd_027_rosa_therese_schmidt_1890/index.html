About the family

Family: her parents are named exactly as the couple the tree holds as Karolina Knodel née Schmidt's parents. The 1906 Pfeilsdorf death act of her sister Anna names 'Wilhelm Schmidt and his wife Anna Maria née Adam'; this 1913 act names 'Wilhelm Schmidt and his wife Marie née Adam'. Both acts come from the same small Dobruja-German settler group at Pfeilsdorf, and no second Wilhelm Schmidt x Adam couple is known. Her link to these parents is therefore taken as Confirmed: her own marriage act names both. The act does not name Karolina, so that Rosa was Karolina's full sister (and not a half-sister) rests on the same reasoning as Anna's. ¶3

Probable son, 1908: she was very probably the 'unmarried Teresa Schmidt' who on 10 Nov 1908 gave birth to a son Jakob at Bilau, in the dwelling of her father, the settler Wilhelm Schmidt (Lissewo births 1908 no. 124, declared by Wilhelm, who calls Teresa his daughter). The boy was baptised at Lissewo on 16 Dec 1908 (index only), died at Bilau on 31 Mar 1909, aged 4 months 21 days, as the son of 'the unmarried settler's daughter Therese Schmidt' (Lissewo deaths 1909 no. 17), and was buried at Lissewo on 3 Apr 1909 (index only). The reasons for identifying the mother with Rosa Therese: Therese is her second given name; the father is named as the same settler Wilhelm Schmidt of Bilau; she was unmarried and living at Bilau with her parents when she married in 1913; and no other daughter Therese of Wilhelm is known. Neither act names Teresa's mother or gives her age or birth date, so the identification is probable, not proved. The child is not entered in the tree. ¶4

Life story

Birth: 30 June 1890 at Atmagea (Atmadscha), the oldest German colony of the Northern Dobruja, as stated in her 1913 marriage act. No baptism has been seen; the Atmagea Evangelical register of 1890 would give her godparents. ¶2

Marriage: on 9 Nov 1913 at the Standesamt (civil registry office) Pfeilsdorf she married the farmhand (Knecht) Gottlieb Hensel. The act calls her 'die ledige Ansiedlertochter Rosa Therese Schmidt' (the unmarried daughter of a settler), Evangelical, born 30 June 1890 at 'Atmagia' in Romania, living at Bilau, Kreis Culm, daughter of the settler (Ansiedler) Wilhelm Schmidt and his wife Marie née Adam, living at Bilau. The side-register, a copy certified by the registrar as agreeing with the main register, gives her signature as 'Rosa Therese Hensel geborene Schmidt' (in the copyist's hand, not her own). Witnesses: the labourer Gustav Eichelberg, 25, and his wife Rosa Eichelberg née Lingnau, 23, both of Pfeilsdorf (Pfeilsdorf marriages 1913 no. 15; image read). ¶1
