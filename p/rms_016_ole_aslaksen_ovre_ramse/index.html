Life story

Of Øvre Ramse, Tovdal. Betrothed 7 Oct 1730 and married Dom. 25 p. Trin. 1730 (c. 19 Nov 1730) in Tovdal to Helge Pedersdatter: 'Copul: Ole Aslachsön Övre Rambse og Helge Pedersdatter' (Åmli 1711-1740 pp. 102-103, canvas 54). Children baptised in Tovdal: Gundvor, Dom. 8 p. Trin. 1732 ('Ole Aslachsön Övre-Rambses barn Gundvor', canvas 61); Aslak, Dom. 2 p. Epiph. 1735 (canvas 68); Margitte, Dom. Invocavit (2 Mar) 1738 ('Ole Aslachsön Övre-Ramses barn Margitte', canvas 75). The book names fathers only, so Margitte's mother is presumably Helge Pedersdatter (no remarriage seen 1730-40). Probably a son of Aslak Olsen Øvre Ramse, buried Exaudi 1735 in Tovdal aged 67 (canvas 68). ¶1

Proposed (Speculative) as father of Margitte Olsdatter of Ramse (m. 1756 Tellef Thorsen Tved): the 1738 Margitte is the ONLY Margitte daughter of an Ole baptised in Åmli Oct 1724 - Dec 1740 (every canvas 38-85 read), and she is of Ramse, as the bride was in 1755. ¶2a

1762 extra-tax roll, Tovdal sogn, farm 518 Øvre Ramse, canvas 164: 'Ole og H: Helge' with children over 12 Aslak, Ole and Gro, and cottars 'Ole og H: Gunvor'; 7 persons. This fits Ole Aslaksen and Helge Pedersdatter (married 1730; son Aslak baptised 1735): same farm, both given names and the son's name (Probable). The cottar wife Gunvor may be the daughter Gundvor (baptised 1732); not stated. Margitte is not in the household, which is consistent with her marriage to Tellef Thorsen at Tveit in 1756 but proves nothing. ¶3

How sure are we?

Speculative. A plausible candidate only. Treat this link as a question, not a fact.

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Not proved: the 1711-1724 years (canvases 1-37) were not read for Margittes, and the bride's estimated birth (ABT 1730; a speculative 1806 burial aged 80) would put her earlier than 1738. ¶2b
