Life story

Speculative baptism: Peder, son of Ole Ingebrigtsen of Søvikreiten, baptized 26 May 1740 at Borgund. Not linked. ¶6

Peder Olsen, a bachelor of Schiong, was engaged 20 Jul 1777 at Borgund to Christianna Knudsdatter, a maid of Schiong. She was churched as his wife in 1778, 1780, 1782 and 1786. ¶3

In the 1801 census a Peder Olesen, 59, farmer at Sjaang, was married to Christiana Knudsdatter (50), with daughter Kanuta (20). There was also an unmarried Peder Olesen, 30, a soldier, on the same farm. Which Peder fathered the 1789 child is not proven. ¶2

Died 20 Aug 1821, a retired former farmer (kårmann) at Skjong, age 81; buried 23 Aug 1821. ¶5

Probate (scan read; audited): the estate of the old married couple Peder Olsen and Christiane Knudsdatter of Skjaang (Skjong) was registered 15 Sep 1821 (Nordre Sunnmøre sorenskriveri, skifteprotokoll 3A 13, s. 119b-120a). The heirs were (1) the daughter Kanutha Pedersdatter, widow of Lars Olsen Skjong, with Lars Hansen Skjong as her guardian at law (laugværge); (2) Malene Pedersdatter, unmarried, with Ole Andersen Skjong appointed her guardian (formynder); (3) the daughter Lisbeth Pedersdatter, married to Jon[?] Jørgensen of Valderøy, who is Elisabeth Pedersdatter (see her page, m. John Jørgensen 1813). This settles the 1801 question of two Peder Olsens at Skjong: the farmer aged 59 in 1801 is her father. The earlier note calling him her 'speculative' father is superseded. That link is recorded on Elisabeth Pedersdatter's own confidence. His own confidence stays Probable, because it concerns his own parentage, and the probate names no parents or siblings of the deceased. The entry gives no ages. The sons Knud (b. 1778) and Salmand (b. 1786) are not heirs, so they presumably died before 1821 without issue (inference). The appraisers were David Olsen and Christopher Johnsen, both of Tørlen[?]. ¶7

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Speculative father of Elisabeth Pedersdatter. Father of 'Elisabet Susanna', baptized 16 Feb 1789 at Borgund (residence Schiong = Skjong/Sjaang). ¶1

Upgrade: Confirmed, graded on his link to his daughter Elisabeth Pedersdatter (see her page), not on his own parentage (unknown). The record naming the relationship is the joint probate of 'the old married couple Peder Olsen and Christiane Knudsdatter' of Skjaang (Skjong), registered 15 Sep 1821 (Nordre Sunnmøre sorenskriveri, skifteprotokoll 3A 13, s. 119b-120a; scan, audited), whose heirs are the couple's children, among them the daughter Lisbeth Pedersdatter, wife of Jon Jørgensen of Valderøy. The same record already carries his wife Christiana's Confirmed grade. Continuity: Elisabet Susanna was baptised at Borgund 16 Feb 1789 to Peder Olsen of Schiong; Elisabeth Pedersdatter is the wife of John Jørgensen at Valderøy in the 1815 census, and John's probate of 10 Dec 1824 names his widow Lisbeth Pedersdatter. ¶10a

Corrections 1

Correction (scan read): the only baptism candidate (26 May 1740, Peder son of Ole Imbrigtsen of Søvikreiten) is ruled out. Ole Ingebrigtsen of Søvikreiten's first wife Ingeleiv Pedersdatter died in 1741 (probate 10 Apr 1741, 3A 13 fol. 33b). At Ole's own probate, 27 Apr 1756 (3A 18 fol. 815b, view 24559/355), and at a second probate the same week 'efter afg. Peder Olsen' of Søvikreiten (fol. 817a, view 24559/356), Peder Olsen is dead: his heirs are his full sisters Anne Olsdatter (20) and Lisbet Olsdatter (18) and his half-siblings Ingebrigt (9), a brother about 6, Ingeleiv(?), Berthe (5) and Johanne (3), children of Ole by his widow Mali Knudsdatter. So the Peder born 1740 at Søvikreiten died before April 1756 and cannot be Peder Olsen of Skjong (d. 1821). His parents remain unknown. ¶13

Alternatives considered and set aside 2

Parentage lead, not linked (audited): Peder, son of Ole Imbrigts. (Ingebrigtsen) of Søvikreiten, was baptised at Borgund on 26 May 1740 (index, serial 36; https://www.digitalarkivet.no/en/view/255/pd00000003228330). It is the only Peder with a father named Ole found in the Borgund baptism index for 1737-1743, and it fits his burial age of 81 in 1821. It was not added as a parent because nothing beyond the common name and year connects the two: no record places this Peder at Skjong, the 1801 census age of 59 gives about 1742, Peder named no child Ole or Ingebrigt, at least two men named Ole Ingebrigtsen were baptising children in Borgund in 1745, and an Ole Olsen lived at Skjong itself in 1725-1734, so Peder may belong to a Skjong household whose baptism is missing from the index. ¶8a

No rival: the unmarried soldier Peder Olesen, 30, on Skjong in 1801 is excluded because the probate is the joint estate of Peder Olsen and his wife Christiane Knudsdatter. ¶10b

Searches that found nothing 3

Negative: the probate after Ole Olsen of Skjong, 16 Dec 1749 (Sunnmøre 3A 17 fol. 77a, scan read), lists six children (Ole, Siur, Barbra, Synneve, Eli, Anne) and no Peder, so he was not a son of that Ole Olsen. The Søvikreiten candidate (father Ole Ingebrigtsen) remains untested: a probate index search for any deceased with farm Søvikreit* 1740-1800 returned 0 hits. ¶9

Negative: the 1762 extra tax list for Skjong ('Schiong', DA 49637 view 50, pp. 87-88, the last farm of Borgund sogn) has seven households and no son Peder of a man named Ole: 1 Erich Pedersen[?] and Marthe Gundersdatter; 2 Elling Gundersen and Marthe Pedersdatter; 3 Ole Olsen and Karen Olsdatter, 1 son Lars; 4 Peder Andersen and Marthe Larsdatter, 1 son Niels; 5 Hans Pedersen and Anne Olsdatter; 6 Anders Hansen and Mali Nielsdatter; 7 Peder [Pedersen?] and Birthe Henriksdatter, with old parents Henrik Jonsen and Anne Rasmusdatter in the others column. No Peder Olsen is among the servants either. So Peder Olsen (b. about 1740) was not living at Skjong as a child in 1762 and came to the farm later (he was 'bachelor of Schiong' in 1777); his parents must be sought on another farm. The untested Søvikreiten candidate (Peder, son of Ole Ingebrigtsen, bapt. 26 May 1740) remains the lead; Søvikreiten was not found in views 8-20, 44, 46-50 of this list. ¶11

Negative: the last unread Borgund sogn openings of the 1762 extra tax list (DA 49637 views 42, 43 and 45, pp. 71-74 and 77-78: Grytebust, Rotsvik, Lyngsten, Myklebust; Sperre, Vik, Langøy, Hagen, Bjørgeholm, Bjørge; Krogset, Lille Aarset, Gjerset, Urkedal, Holmeset, Grytten) contain no Søvikreiten and no son Peder of an Ole. With views 8-20, 44 and 46-50 already read, Søvikreiten does not appear as a named farm in the Borgund sogn section; it may be entered under its main farm Søvik (view 46) without a separate heading. Sons Peder listed elsewhere: Rotsvik hh 3 'Arent Iversen and Ingeborg Olsdatter, 1 son Peder' and Urkedal hh 1 'Iver Olsen and Marthe Pedersdatter, 1 son Peder', neither with a father named Ole. His parentage stays unknown; the Søvikreiten candidate remains untested. ¶12
