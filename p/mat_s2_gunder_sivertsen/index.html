About the family

Father of Gunder Gundersen Satter. Named outright as 'brudgommens far — Gunder Sivertsen' in his son's marriage entry at Værnes, Nedre Stjørdal, 19 June 1863, and the father of the only matching baptism in Frosta. ¶1

Mother: his mother was Anne (Anna) Olsdatter, named in his 1809 baptism. His parents had married at Frosta on 4 June 1801, and an elder brother Siver was baptised at Frosta on 20 March 1803. ¶18

Life story

Status: an INDERST — a lodger in another man's house, holding no land of his own, the lowest rung of rural Norwegian society above the destitute. He is recorded as inderst at SNYTAN in 1831 and 1833 and at MOXNES by 1835. ¶2

Baptism: born on 16 January 1809 and baptised 'Gunner' at Loktu (Loctue) church, Frosta, on 22 January 1809. The parents were Sivert Greysen and Anna Olsdatter, both living at Snytan. The godparents were the widow Berith of Vangberg, Martha of Hilde, Gudmund of Qvarme, Greis of Vigeleret and Peder of Hilde. Martha and Peder are probably the mother's sister and brother (see Anne Olsdatter). Source note: the Frosta church book for 1778-1811 (Digitalarkivet source 16379) survives on Digitalarkivet only as a searchable index transcription; no scan is published, so page numbers are not given and the original handwriting could not be checked. ¶15

Birth date: this replaces the earlier estimate 'about 1809', which was calculated from his ages in 1838 and 1847. ¶17

Emigration lead, UNDECIDED: a 'Gunder Sivertsen Bardsøeidet', 60 (b. about 1810), with wife Kirsti (48) and son Martin (10), residence 'Åhre' - apparently Åre in Jämtland, Sweden, just across the border from Meråker/Stjørdal - left Trondheim on 5 May 1870 for Sioux City, Iowa, via agent Ennis. For: age, the name, and the destination next to Union Co., SD, where his son Gunder Gundersen Satter settled; his second wife Martha probably died in Dec 1859, so a third marriage (to Kirsti) and a son Martin b. about 1860 are possible; he vanishes from the Norwegian 1865 census and burials. ¶20a

He is the only Gunder Sivertsen recorded as a father anywhere in the Frosta church book of 1827-1839, which is what allows his son to be identified without ambiguity. ¶4

Three children baptised at Frosta, all to the same wife Karen Helgesdatter: Anne Cecilie (b. 14 Mar 1831, bapt. 20 Mar 1831, page 25), Mette (b. 1 Jan 1833, bapt. 13 Jan 1833, page 35) and Gunder (b. 12 Jun 1835, bapt. 28 Jun 1835, page 53). The 1835 entry carries the pastor's remark 'Lægd Moxnæss' — the household was on the parish poor-relief rotation. ¶3

Out-migration: on 26 February 1838 'Gunder Sivertsen Moxnes, Inderst, b. 1809' and his wife 'Karen Helgesdatter, aged 35' left Frosta for Skogn prestegjeld (Frosta parish out-migration list, page 246, no. 2). His son Gunder, then two and a half, was therefore born at Frosta but raised in Skogn — which is why every later record of the son says 'born Frosta' while nothing else of him is at Frosta. ¶5

Move to Skogn, 1838: the Skogn in-migration list for 1838 (no. 2) enters 'Gunder Sivertsen Moxnes', 29, his wife Karen Helgesdatter, 35, and the children Anne Cicilie (7), Mette (5) and Gunder (3). They came from Frosta 'to support themselves as husmannsfolk' (cottagers) on Søndre Kjølen in Ekne sokn, carrying the Frosta pastor's moving certificate (attest) of 26 February 1838. The family was in fact already there: their daughter Hanna was born at Søndre Kjølen on 6 January 1838. ¶8

Residence 1838-1853: from 1838 he was a husmann (cottager) on the cottar holding Kjølenvald, under the farm Søndre Kjølen in Ekne sokn, Skogn. The baptisms of 1838 and 1842 call him 'Huusmand' of Søndre Kjølen. Karen's 1842 burial and his own 1847 marriage give Kjølenvald, and his daughter Gunelie Margrethe's 1853 baptism gives Kjølen. Earlier readings of the farm name ('Søndre Tjølle', 'Søndred Skjulund' in the index) misread the Gothic K; the Ekne index elsewhere has Nordre and Søndre Kjølen and Kjølenval. ¶9

1842: his daughter Oline Margrethe was born on 7 June and baptised at Ekne on 31 July 1842. His wife Karen Helgesdatter died at Kjølenvald on 10 November 1842, aged 40, and was buried at Ekne on 20 November. ¶10

Birthplace: his second marriage entry (Ekne, 5 September 1847) says he was 'født paa Frosten' (born at Frosta) and gives his age as 38, which fits the age of 29 in the February 1838 Skogn in-migration list. Both put his birth about 1809. ¶7

Second marriage: on 5 September 1847 at Ekne church, as 'Enkemand og Huusmand' (widower and cottager) of Kjølenvald, aged 38, he married the spinster Martha Erichsdatter Skaaraas, 35, daughter of the farmer Erik Hansen Skaaraas. His father is named as Sivert Greisen of Kjølen. The witnesses were the farmers Ole Olsen of Søndre Kjølen and Hans Erichsen Skaaraas. The pastor noted that his first marriage had ended with the death of his wife Karen Helgesdatter on 10 November 1842, and that a certificate from the probate court (skifteretten), dated 19 April 1847, had been shown. A daughter of this marriage, Gunelie Margrethe, was baptised at Ekne on 28 August 1853 (index only), with the mother at Kjølen. ¶11

Named as father 'Gunder Sivertsen Kjølenvald' at the confirmation of his daughter Karen Oline (b. 1847, by Martha Eriksdatter) at Skogn on 11 Oct 1863; the entry does not say whether he was then alive. The family is gone from the Kjølen farms by the 1865 census; his death and burial are still not found (searched: Skogn, Frosta, Levanger, Nedre Stjørdal burials to 1895; 1865/1875 censuses; Trondheim emigrant protocols - a 1870 'Gunder Sivertsen Bardsøeidet', 60, of Åre(?), to Sioux City, is a weak, unlikely candidate). ¶19

1852: at his son Gunder's confirmation at Alstadhaug on 26 September 1852 the parents are written 'Hmd. Gunder Sivertsen, K. Karen Helgesdatter', meaning husmann (cottager) Gunder Sivertsen and wife Karen Helgesdatter, with no deceased mark. Karen had been dead for ten years by then. Confirmation registers copied the parents' names from the baptism and often ignored deaths, so this entry is not evidence that she was alive. The confirmand's byname 'Sæthe' is the son's own residence, probably where he was in service, and does not show that the father lived at Sæther. ¶12

1860: at the Frosta weddings of his daughters he is named as the bride's father both times and is not marked deceased either time. At Hanna's wedding on 19 November 1860 he is 'Gunder Siversen Kjølen'. At Mette's wedding on 30 November 1860 he is 'Gunder Sivertsen Snytan', Snytan being the Frosta place where he had lived in 1831-33. The two residences contradict each other, so it is not settled where he lived in 1860, or whether he was still living. ¶13

Residence 1860: at his daughter Mette's marriage at Frosta on 30 Nov 1860 the bride's father is entered as 'Gunder Sivertssen', residence Snytan (Frosta) - the farm where he was born in 1809. So after his second wife Martha's probable death at Ekne in Dec 1859 he seems to have moved back to Snytan, Frosta. The index alone does not say whether he was alive. This weakens the 1870 Åre emigrant lead (that man's wife Kirsti and son Martin b. about 1860 do not fit), and points the search for his death to Frosta after 1860: the indexed Frosta burials 1858-95 have no Gunder Sivertsen, so the next records are the Frosta burial scans 1860-80 and the 1865 census for Snytan (read on the scan). ¶21

Neither he nor Karen Helgesdatter appears anywhere in the 1865 census of Norway under these names, so both were probably dead by then; their deaths have not been traced and Skogn is where to look. ¶6

The case for this identification 1

Identification of the 1809 baptism: 'Gunner' is a common written form of Gunder. He is the only child with a Gun- name among the 47 children baptised at Frosta in 1809. The birth year matches every later record of Gunder Sivertsen: 'b. 1809' and age 29 in the February 1838 moving lists, age 38 in September 1847, and 'born at Frosta' in the 1847 marriage. The father's name Sivert Greysen matches 'Sivert Greisen' named as his father in 1847. The farm Snytan matches his own residence as inderst (lodger) at Snytan in 1831-33 and the name 'Gunder Sivertsen Snytan' at his daughter Mette's wedding in November 1860. The baptismal date is therefore taken as his. ¶16

Records that disagree 1

Against: no marriage of Gunder Sivertsen and a Kirsti found in Trøndelag 1853-62; 'Bardsøeidet' is unexplained. ¶20b

Searches that found nothing 1

Death not found: no burial of a Gunder Sivertsen born 1805-1812 appears in the indexed Skogn (Alstadhaug, Ekne), Levanger, Verdal, Frosta, Åsen or Trondheim burials for 1838-1880, and he is not in the 1865 census under Gunder Sivert* or among the men named Gunder born 1803-1813 in Frosta, Skogn, Levanger, Nedre Stjørdal or Trondheim. Next to search: the Ekne burials of 1850-1877 under his name variants, and the Skogn 1865 census for Martha Eriksdatter. ¶14
