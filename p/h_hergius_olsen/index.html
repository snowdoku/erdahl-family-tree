Life story

Bygdebok (Åmli ætt og heim IX, Ne(r)garden gnr. 40, canvases 667-668): 'Herjus... (Fyresdal?)', b. (1744), d. 1832, m. 1776 Anne of Gjøvdal; he took over Ne(r)garden in 1796 and sold it in 1801, then probably moved to Gangsei; the book says he signed his own name 'Hergius'. It does not know his parents (his origin in Fyresdal is queried; a Kjetil is mentioned as a possible link, unread). Vol II (canvas 349) also gives 'Herjus Olson Fyresdal, 1744-1832', m. 1775 Anne Halvorsdtr. b. 1742. No parents added. ¶6a

Betrothed 27 Dec 1775: 'Herjus Olsen fra [farm illegible, possibly Sjunnerdal]' and Anne Halvorsdatter Omland i Gievedal. They married 24 Mar 1776 (Åmli 1775-1796 p.89-90). His home farm and parents are still unidentified. ¶2

1801 census, Neergaarden, Åmli: head of household, aged 57, 'bonde og gaardbruger og smed' (farmer and blacksmith), in his first marriage, with wife Anne Halvorsdatter (59). Children: Asborg (25), Ole (22), Anne (19) and Halvor (16). ¶1

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed (upgrade sweep D), as father of Ole Herjussen Gangsei. Two records name the links. (1) The 1801 census of Åmli (index, relationship words read) lists at Neergaarden Hergius Olsen (57, huusbonde, 'begge i 1ste ægteskab', farmer and smith) and Anne Halvorsdatter (59, 'hans kone', 'begge i 1ste ægteskab'), with Asborg (25), Ole (22), Anne (19) and Halvor (16) Hergiusen/Hergiusdatter, each entered as 'Deres børn' (their children), 'tiener forældrene' (working for the parents). As both spouses were in their first marriage, these are children of both. (2) The probate after Halvor Herjussen of Gangsei, 4 Feb 1829 - 12 Mar 1830 (Østre Råbyggelag skifteprotokoll 23a, fol. 276b, card 3489), names 'Avd's bror: Ole Herjusen Gangsei'. So the tree's Ole Herjussen of Gangsei (married 1813, alive 1830) is the brother of Halvor and thus the 1801 son Ole of Hergius and Anne; Ole also named a son Herjus (1824). The rare patronymic Herjussen and the probate's sibling statement join the census family to the Gangsei brothers, not age or farm alone. ¶4

Searches that found nothing 2

Not found baptised in the Åmli church book 1741-1756 (9921, mid-1741 to Nov 1756 read, including all of 1744-45). He may have been born in another parish (his 1775 home 'S…tterødal'/'Sj…dal' is unidentified; a 'Tergie Gundersen fra S…tterødal' was a godparent in Gjøvdal in 1742). ¶3

Probate search (negative): the whole Negården i Åmli block of the Østre Råbyggelag probate card index (DA 39942, views 9343-9381, URN sk11214091 + view+4) was read. Its cards run from 1688 to 1829: 1707-1712 (Kari Thellefsdatter, Thellef Biørnsen, Ole Pedersen), 1738 (Martha Omundsdatter, wife of Ole Knudsen, 'Neergaarden'), 1765 (Svenning Olsen; Ole Aslachsen of øvre Neergaarden), 1768 (Ole Rollefsen), 1773 (Ingebor Joensdatter, wife of Petter Knudsen), 1782-83 (Knud Nielsen and Bergitte Andersdatter) and 1828-29 (Anders Knudsen). None is Hergius Olsen or Anne Halvorsdatter, and no card names them as heirs. So neither of them had a registered probate filed under Negården; his link to his children still rests on the 1801 census. ¶5a
