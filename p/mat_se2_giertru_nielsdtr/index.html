About the family

First wife of Peder Bersvendsen of Eidem, and therefore NOT an ancestor - she is recorded here because her marriage is what dates Peder's presence at Eidem and establishes that Mali Evensdatter was a second wife. ¶1

Children: Bersvend, born 27 March 1820, and Giertru/Gjertrue, baptised 27 January 1822, who died on 1 June 1823, twelve weeks before her mother (Giertru Pedersdatter). ¶5

Life story

Marriage: married at Selbu church 14 June 1819 Peder Bersvendsen, residence Eidem. Selbu parish register 1816-1831, marriages pages 390-391 serial no. 9. She is entered with birth year 1794 calculated and residence Garborg, which is the farm Garberg in Selbu. She must have died before 1834, when Peder's children are by Mali Evensdatter. ¶2

Death and burial, Probable: 'Gjertrue Nilsd.', aged 29, died 23 August 1823 and buried 31 August 1823 (Selbu 1816-1824, pp. 218-219 no. 18); the parallel book (1816-1831, pp. 240-241 no. 18) has 'Giertrud Nielsdt', aged 29, died 25 August, buried 31 August. No residence in either index; scan not read. Age 29 fits her calculated birth in 1794 at the 1819 wedding; it is the only Giertru Nielsdatter burial in the Selbu index 1801-1862; her husband is without a wife in the November 1825 census and married again on 5 June 1826 as a widower ('Enkemand'), 'after the probate was completed'. ¶4a

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Not the same person as Giertrud Nielsdatter (see her page), wife of Paul Pedersen Garberg and mother of Karen Povelsdatter (baptised 1758): that woman was a mother by 1758, while this Giertru was born about 1794. This Giertru may be her granddaughter, a daughter of Niels Povelsen Garberg (married 1794, presumably the Niels baptised to Paul and Giertrud in 1761); inference only - her baptism, about 1794-95 in Selbu ministerialbok 1757-1801 (source 16311, canvases 165-180), would test it. ¶3
