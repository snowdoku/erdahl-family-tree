Life story

1666 vicar's census: 'Frich bruger... 26 aar' at Langeteig (Tiugum sogn N. 21; 35519 view 170, p. 314) with his son 'Niels 1 1/2 aar', the Niels of Langeteig who was his heir in 1701. So born ~1640 and holder at Langeteig by 1666. Parents added as Speculative (Peder and Brite Olsdatter of Langeteig, 1645). His own grade is unchanged. Langeteig evidence. (1) 1645 poll tax, Langeteig in Tiugum sogn: four farmers with wives, 'Guldbrandt' + 'Gurj Joensdtr', 'Peder' + 'Brite Olsdtr', 'Østen' + 'Ingier Frederichsdtr', 'Lauridtz' + 'Ellj Jonsdtr' (p. 24); the farm's sons and daughters (p. 29) include five children of Guldbrand: Johans, Peder, Olle, Astri and 'Marete Guldbrandsdtr'. No other Guldbrand at Langeteig. (2) 1666 vicar's census, Tiugum sogn N. 21 Langeteig (35519 view 170, p. 314): holders Ola 59, Harmund 45, 'Gulbrand bruger... 86 aar' (b. ~1580) and 'Frich bruger... 26 aar' (b. ~1640) with his son 'Niels 1 1/2 aar' (b. ~1664/65). No Peder is left on the farm. (2b) 1664 vicar's census, Tiugum sogn, Langeteig (35519 view 152, p. 281): farmers 'Ola Erichsen', 'Gulbrand', 'Harmund', 'Frich'; servant 'Lasse Andbiørnsen' 17; husmann 'Engelbart'; again no Peder. (3) 21 Mar 1701, Langeteig: joint probate after Frich Pedersen and his wife Marethe Guldbrandsdatter, heirs their son Niels 'boende Langeteig' and daughter Britte, wife of Jørgen Langeteig (1b p. 275a). So in 1666 the young holder Frich (= Frich Pedersen, d. by 1701) farms beside the old Gulbrand, with a son Niels (= the 1701 heir Niels of Langeteig), and his wife Marethe Guldbrandsdatter carries the patronymic of the only Guldbrand on the farm, whose daughter Marete is listed there in 1645. ¶2

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Graded Confirmed on the link to the daughter Brite Friksdatter (see her page). Frich (Frik) Pedersen died before the joint probate of 21 Mar 1701 at Langeteig, Tjugum skipreide, held 'efter 2de nu hensovne Ægtefolk... sal. Frich Pedersen... og hans Hustru sal. Marethe Guldbrandsdatter, imellem deris efterlatende Børn', the son Niels of Langeteig and the daughter Britte, wife of Jørgen Langeteig. Continuity: (1) 21 Mar 1701, Langeteig: the joint probate after Frich Pedersen and his wife Marethe Guldbrandsdatter names their children 'Niels... boende Langeteig, og Britte, Jørgen Langeteigs [hustru], deris Sønne og daatter' (Ytre Sogn 1b p. 275a, 24154 view 59); (2) 1701 census: the only Jørgen at Langeteig is 'Jørgen Knudsen', bonde, 31 (pc00000000627700); (3) 14 Feb 1750, Langeteig: probate after Jørgen Knudsen Langeteig 'imellem Enken Brite Friksdatter og deris sammen avlede 3de Døttre' (6b fol. 635a). The pair Jørgen + Brite at Langeteig at both ends (audit 8 rule 3) and her patronymic Friksdatter (= daughter of Frich) bridge the 49-year gap; no other Jørgen or Brite pair is known at Langeteig. Parents unknown; no probate protocol before 1698. ¶1
