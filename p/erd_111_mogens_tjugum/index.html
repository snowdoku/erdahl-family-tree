About the family

Probable father of Anders Monsen Tjugum: Anders, 12 in 1666 (born about 1654, Laberg's date), and his brother Niels are listed in the sons column under Mogens at Tjugum; in 1701 Anders Monsen (52) and Niels Monsen (b. ~1653) were the two bønder at Tjugum/Grøneng, and Anders was later lensmann like his father. The 1701 age (52) gives about 1649, five years earlier. Not Confirmed because the 1666 entry does not write 'søn' (column position only). ¶2

Life story

Vicar's census 1666 (23 May), Tjugum sogn N.13: 'Tiugum... Mogens bruger 42 aar', sons 'Anders 12 aar', 'Niels 7 aar' and a third, 3, whose name reads like a second 'Anders' (Laberg 1985 reads Endre); servant Lavrits 18. Bailiff's census 21 Jun 1664: 'Tiugumb, Mogens xx Lendsmand' (lensmann, rural sheriff), servants Erland[?] Olsen 30 and Niels Andersen 18; the vicar's 1664 list has servants Hans Hansen 20 and Niels Andersen 19. Mogens is the written form of Mons. Birth about 1624 from the 1666 age. ¶1

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1645 poll tax (negative): on Tiugum, Tjugum sogn, the only farmer is 'Niells' (no wife listed, pt00000000056619), with the farmers' children Sambson Nielsen, Andfind Nielsen and Brite Nielsdatter (pt00000000056978-056982). Mogens (about 21) is not listed on Tjugum. A farmer 'Mogens' is on Meland in the same sogn (pt00000000056659), almost certainly another man since Mogens Tjugum was only about 21. So Mogens probably came to Tjugum after 1645; Niels is not shown to be his father. ¶3
