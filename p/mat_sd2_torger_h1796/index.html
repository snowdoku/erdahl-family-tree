About the family

Eldest children of Haldo Torgersen and Ingebor Jonsdatter of Svinaas, entered as 'Deres born' in the 1801 census of Selbu at rural residence 0015 Svinaas, household 4: Ingebor Haldorsdatter 6, Torger Haldorssen 5 and Magli Haldorsdatter 2. Their sister Marit, born about 1810 and the mother of Oline Slind's mother, came later. ¶1

The son is named Torger for his paternal grandfather Torger Erikssen and the eldest daughter Ingebor for her mother - or, in the usual Selbu practice, for her maternal grandmother. ¶2

Life story

Baptism: Torger, born 18 September 1796 and baptised on the 18th Sunday after Trinity, 25 September 1796: '4. Et Drengebarn, kaldet Torger, föd d: 18de Sept:; Forældre: Haldor Torgersen Svinaas og Ingeborg Jonsdatter'; godparents Haldor Pedersen Svinaas, Jon Povelsen ibid., Ole Jonsen Sandvig, Karen Thomesdatter ibid. and Brönille Olsdatter Berge (Selbu ministerialbok 1757-1801, canvas 196 right page, book p. 193). He is Torger Haldorssen, 5, of the 1801 household. Birth corrected from 'about 1796' to 18 September 1796. ¶3
