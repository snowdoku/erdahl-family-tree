About the family

Family book: the Wahl index lists Johann Farowsky, born 1803, on page 83 of the Beresina family book 1841-60 (FHL 1766557 item 2), and as house 83a, with the remark 'Pohlen' (Poland), in a later Beresina book (FHL 1768263 item 1 p.100). His is the only Farowsky household indexed at Beresina. ¶2

First wife and daughter (Oct 2026; the link to Wilhelmine stays Speculative): the Consistory extracts record his daughter Wilhelmina Farofsky, born at Beresina on 7 Jan 1838 to Johann and Rosina née Bomm (FHL 1882648/2 p.72 no.3), and the death at Beresina on 13 Dec 1850 of Rosina, wife of Johann, née Bomm, born Poland (no age given in the extract) (surname printed 'Jaronsky'; FHL 1883181/1 p.307 no.49). He married secondly Caroline Zarbock at Tarutino on 7 Sep 1851 (printed 'Tarowsky, Johann'; FHL 1883181/1 p.260 no.56). The 1838 daughter is the best candidate for Wilhelmine, wife of Ludwig Knodel, but a namesake Wilhelmina Farowsky, mother of Louisa Dorsch at Beresina in Dec 1858, is unresolved; see the note on Wilhelmine Tarowsky. ¶5

Father (new, Probable): both extracts of his first marriage (Kulm 31 Dec 1834, FHL 1882643/1 p.68 no.173; Tarutino 1 Jan 1835, FHL 1882645/2 p.1 no.131) call him the son of the late Andreas ('s +Andreas'). Andreas Farowsky (see his page) is entered as his father. Index-level only; the marriage images would confirm it. ¶13

Life story

Marriage: as Johann Tarowsky he married Caroline Zarbock (born 1827 at Tarutino) at Tarutino on 7 September 1851 (FHL 1883181/1 p.260 no.56). Children of Johann and Karolina Zarbock born at Beresina (Consistory birth extracts): Friederike 18 Nov 1853, Christian 26 Feb 1856 (surname extracted 'Far---s?'), Friedrich 22 Dec 1858, Christina 15 Oct 1861 (remark 'pb 83', his family-book house), a child 30 Nov 1863 ('pb 83'), and probably Justina 2 Feb 1868 (extracted 'Kreawske', mother 'Zarbock, Julian'). Aged 48 in 1851, he was almost certainly a widower then. ¶3

Marriage (1): as Johann Farofski, of Kulm colony, born in Poland, son of the late Andreas, he married Rosina Schmolke née Bomm, widow, aged 38, born in Poland, daughter of the late Michael, at Tarutino on 1 Jan 1835 (FHL 1882645/2 p.1 no.131). The same marriage is entered for Kulm on 31 Dec 1834 (Sarofski, Johann, 23y x Schmolke, Rosine, 38y, widow; FHL 1882643/1 p.68 no.173), probably the groom's colony recording the same event. Their only joint child found is Wilhelmina, born at Beresina on 7 Jan 1838 (FHL 1882648/2 p.72 no.3). Rosina died at Beresina on 13 Dec 1850. ¶6

Marriage (2): as Johann Tarowsky he married Caroline Zarbock at Tarutino on 7 Sep 1851 (FHL 1883181/1 p.260 no.56); his 1881 death entry calls him husband of Karoline Zarbock. ¶7

Household: the Wahl index places Luisa Dorsch (b. 1858), his stepson Johann Schmolke (b. 1826) and his stepdaughter Luisa Schmolke in his Beresina family-book entry (83a p.100 no.106, FHL 1768263 item 1). Luisa Dorsch is probably his granddaughter by his daughter Wilhelmina (inferred; see the note on Wilhelmine Tarowsky). ¶9

Records read 1 Nov 2026 (signed-in lane): (1) the death of his wife Rosina née Bomm at Beresina on 13 Dec 1850, aged 56 years 10 months, calls him 'hiesiger Colonist' (local colonist) Johann Farowsky (vol. 828-14/63 p.307 no.49; image read). (2) The 1851 Tarutino marriage list (same volume, p.260, entry 56; image 253) records 'Johann Farowsky mit Caroline Zarbock' on 7 Jul 1851; the list gives names only, no ages or parents. (3) The Beresina baptism of 1838 (vol. 828-14/18, p.72, entry 3; FamilySearch DGS 4280442 item 2, image 72; index 1:1:Q242-8KYL) reads '(Farowsky) Wilhelmine', born 7 Jan, baptised 12 Jan 1838, parents 'Johann Farowsky, Colonist, u. dessen Ehefrau Rosina geb. Bomm, luth.', so the index extract naming Johann and Rosina as the parents is verified from the image. This proves that Johann and Rosina had a daughter Wilhelmine born 1838; it does not by itself prove that she is the Wilhelmine who married Ludwig Knodel in 1860 (aged 21, single), so the parent link is unchanged. ¶11

Marriage, the Tarutino register image (read 1 Nov 2026,; S0170/S0174), partial: the St Petersburg Consistory duplicate for Tarutino parish, 1835 volume (vol. 828-14/10; coll. 1469151, waypoint 'Tarutino: Tarutino' > 1835, image 25 of 50), marriages 'Getraute im Jahre 1835', folio 121, right-hand page, first entry: groom 'ledig, 23 Jahr' (single, 23; the second digit could be 5), bride 'Wittwe 38 Jahr' (widow, 38); banns 16, 23 and 30 December; 'getraut d. 1 (ersten) Januar im Bethaus Tarutino v. Past....' (married 1 January in the Tarutino prayer house by the pastor); 'beide meldeten sich persönlich zum Aufgebot' (both applied for the banns in person). The left-hand page with the names and fathers is not in the filmed sequence (image 25 pairs this page with a births page; image 26 is folio 122, entries 8 onward), so the identification with Johann Farowsky x Rosina Schmolke née Bomm is inferred from the date (1 Jan 1835, entry no. 1 in the Klöstitz retrospective list, vol. 828-14/32 p.158, which names 'Farowsky, Johann, mit Schmolke, Rosina') and the widow aged 38 (Rosina's age stated elsewhere). If it is their entry, the groom's age of 23 (or 25) puts his birth about 1809-11, not 1803; the 1803 estimate is not changed until the names page is read. ¶12

Death: Johann Farowsky died at Beresina, Bessarabia, on 28 January 1881 (Julian date as extracted), aged 78 years 23 days, so born about early January 1803. Birthplace 'Poland'; husband of Karoline Zarbock. Index extract, FHL 1897592/2 p.540 no.4; image not seen. The surname is spelt Farowsky, Farowske, Faroske and Tarowsky in the extracts. ¶1

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Speculative link: he is entered as the father of Wilhelmine (see her page), wife of Ludwig Knodel, by a first wife whose name is unknown. For: (1) her children's baptisms spell her maiden name Farowski, Farrowski, Farowskye and Farowsky, the Beresina form; (2) the 1860 marriage was recorded by Kloestitz parish, which served Beresina, and the Consistory extract carries the remark 'PB 2-84'. In the Kloestitz marriages of 1860, 'PB 2-' cites a Beresina family-book house: sometimes the groom's (Bossert 2-120, Mantey 2-73, Woessner 2-68, Gehring 2-2) and sometimes the bride's (Decker 2-43, Rothacker 2-15). Ludwig was from Friedenstal, outside Kloestitz parish, so the bride's household is the likelier reading, though a non-colonist groom working at Beresina cannot be ruled out. Either way the remark places the marriage in a Beresina household; (3) a 'Wilhelmina Farowsky' bore a daughter Louisa Dorsch at Beresina on 4 December 1858 (entry no. 94), three weeks before Johann's own son Friedrich (no. 100, same page), which places a Wilhelmina Farowsky in or near his household 14 months before the Knodel marriage. Against or missing: no record names Wilhelmine's father; house/page 84 is the household of Samuel Roessler (b. 1809, also from Poland), not Johann's: Johann's own children of 1861 and 1863 are marked 'pb 83', and he is house 83a in the later book. So the remark does not place Wilhelmine in Johann's household in 1860. A rival reading is that she lived with the Roesslers, as a servant, a foster child or a stepdaughter; and the rival candidate, Wilhelmina Tarnaske born at Tarutino on 10 February 1843 (daughter of Gottlieb Tarnaske and Susanna Schmalz), cannot be excluded, though Tarutino had its own parish. ¶4a

Link to Wilhelmine (see her page) upgraded from Speculative to Probable (Oct 2026): the argument is set out in full on her record. In short, his daughter Wilhelmina (b. 1838) is the only Wilhelmina Farowsky born in the extracts, the 1858 mother of Louisa Dorsch is best read as the same woman because Louisa was raised in his household, and the 1860 bride's age and parish fit. ¶10a

Records that disagree 1

Birth, conflicting ages: his death at Beresina on 28 Jan 1881 aged 78 years 23 days (FHL 1897592/2 p.540 no.4) gives a birth about 5 Jan 1803, and the Wahl index of the Beresina family books also gives 1803. The 1835 marriage entries give the groom as 25 (Tarutino) and 23 (Kulm), which would put his birth about 1810-11. The 1803 date is kept: two independent sources agree on it, and the Wahl index places the stepchildren Johann (b. 1826) and Luisa Schmolke in his household (83a p.100 no.106), which ties the 1835 groom to the man who died in 1881. The 1835 ages may be extraction errors or understated; the marriage image (FHL 1882645/2 p.1 no.131) would show which. ¶8

Open questions and the next record to read 2

What would settle it: the 1860 marriage entry (Kloestitz no. 191, FHL 038902; or FHL 1884080/1 p.450 no.17), which normally names the bride's father, or the Beresina family book 1841-60 page 83-84 (FHL 1766557 item 2). ¶4b

What would confirm it: the left page of the 1860 Klöstitz marriage (FHL 1884080/1 p.450 no.17) or the family-book entry 83a p.100 no.106 (FHL 1768263 item 1). ¶10b
