Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Graded Speculative on his link to his possible daughter Mari Hermundsdatter (see her page). New lead: the Leikanger register (11564) has the betrothal (canvas 133 R, Apr 1718) and wedding (canvas 135 L, Jul 1718) at Tjugum of 'Ole Esse' and 'Mari Farnes', which is the 1718 marriage of Ole Povelsen of Ytre Esse and Mari Hermundsdatter given by the bygdebok and borne out by his 1758 probate ('Mari Hermunds datter'). So Mari came from Farnes and her father was a Hermund there. In the 1701 census Hermund Hermundsen, 42, was the farmer (bonde) of Indre Farnes, the only Hermund holding a Farnes farm; a 'Christi Hermunds d. Farnes' was baptised at Leikanger in Oct 1692 (11564 c11 L), named, it seems, after the 1645 Farnes wife Christi Kieldsdatter (koppskatt pt56623-56624: 'Hermand' of Farnes and 'Christj Kieldsdtr'), which fits this Hermund as the son of that couple. A Birthe Hermundsdatter was the wife of Ole Olsen at Indre Farnes when she died in 1765 (8a fol. 280a), perhaps a daughter who stayed on her father's farm; her probate names only her children. ¶1

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Rival, named: Hermund Olsen, 55 (b. 1646), a cottar (huusmand) at Farnes in 1701 with a son Olle Hermundsen b. 1689. An 'Ole Hermundsen' is a creditor in the 1762 probate of Mari's son Paul Olsen, which could point to this family instead. Nothing read names Mari's father, so this is a candidate only. ¶2a

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Test record: Mari's baptism in Leikanger 11564 c.1690-1700 ('Mari Hermunds d. Farnes', which may say Indre Farnes), a probate after Hermund Hermundsen or his wife at Indre Farnes before 1720 (none in the Ytre Sogn register 1698-1765 under Farnes), or a Farnes deed in the Ytre Sogn tingbok naming Ole Povelsen Esse as son-in-law. ¶2b
