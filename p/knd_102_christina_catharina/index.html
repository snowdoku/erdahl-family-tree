Life story

Possible marriage and origin, Speculative: the only marriage of a Jacob Christoph to a Christina Catharina indexed anywhere in the Württemberg index in 1676-82 is at Großsachsenheim on 3 Jun 1679: groom row 'Herr Jacob Christoph Schwenger', relative 'Christina Catharina' (record 6178196, image 1184619-00244), and bride row 'Christina Catharina Lugers', relatives 'Johann Burkhard, Herr Jacob Christoph' (her father, then the groom; record 906178196, same image). It falls 13 months before the baptism of Johann Christoph 'Sundermeyer' (18 Jul 1680, film 1184615) and the surname 'Schwenger' occurs nowhere else in this district (all other Schwenger rows are in the Murrhardt-Welzheim area, 1602-1905), so it is probably another misreading of Schumaÿer. Speculative because the groom's surname as indexed does not match and the parish of film 1184615 is unidentified. Her father is entered at Speculative (Johann Burkhard Luger); her name is left without a surname. ¶3a

Probable widowhood and remarriage: on film 1184615 the bride row 'Christina Catharina Schumayers', marriage 28 Apr 1696, relatives 'Christoph, Herr Remmerdus' (record 905439934, image 1184615-00093), groom row 'Herr Remmerdus Merck', relative 'Christina Catharina' (5439934). A daughter of Jacob Christoph x Christina Catharina (married June 1679) would be at most 16 in 1696, so this is very probably she herself, as widow of (Jacob) Christoph Schumayer (widows' bride rows in this index name the late husband first; compare Elisabetha Margaretha Haubert). The groom is Reymundus Meerboth/Merck, who married Margaretha in 1676 (5439838) and baptised children 1679 and 1688; 'Reymundus Merecks' was buried 1 Nov 1715 (5445696). A burial of 'Christina Catharina', relative 'Harm Reimund' (= Herrn Reimund), 2 May 1729 (record 5445925, image 1184615-00576), is probably hers. Neither date is entered, since the rows do not name Schumayer and the reading is inferred. So Jacob Christoph died between Oct 1685 and Apr 1696 (a 'Christoph', no surname or relatives, was buried on this film on 19 Aug 1691, 5441642: candidate only). ¶4a

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

3 on the grade · 1 alternative set aside · 4 open questions

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 3

Speculative mother of Johann Christoph Schumayer (see his page). ¶1a

Graded on that link: she is the wife of Jacob Christoph Schumayer (his father, from the 1713 marriage row 4776645) at the baptisms of Maria Judith (5 Sep 1683, 5440638) and Esther Regina (2 Oct 1685, 5440685), film 1184615. Johann Christoph's own baptism is not indexed and he married in 1713, so he was born about 1685-92, inside or near her childbearing years; but a different (earlier or later) wife cannot be ruled out, so Speculative. Surname unknown. ¶1b

Upgrade to Probable, graded on her link to Johann Christoph Schumayer (see his page). His own baptism is now found: 'Johann Christoph Sundermeyer' (misread Schumaÿer), 18 Jul 1680, relatives 'Jacob Christoph, Christina Catharina' (record 5440561, image 1184615-00166), on the same film as the couple's children Maria Judith 'Schumaÿer' (1683) and Esther Regina (1685). The 1713 Vaihingen groom Johann Christoph is son of Jacob Christoph (4776645), and no other Johann Christoph son of a Jacob Christoph is indexed. ¶2a

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Not Confirmed: unlabelled index rows (audit 5 rule 3), and the 1680 child is joined to the 1713 groom across parishes by name and father's name only (audit 4). The earlier doubt (that Johann Christoph might be a child of another wife) is removed, since his own baptism names her. ¶2b
