About the family

Named as the father on two Washington death certificates filed fifteen years apart: his daughter Ingeborg Slind's (9 Aug 1930, Colfax, Whitman County - "Father name: Ole Klegseth") and his son Jorgen O. Klegseth's (25 Jan 1945, Colfax - "Father name: Ole J. Klegseth"). The J. is presumably a patronymic, and his son being named Jorgen suggests his own father was a Jorgen. ¶1

Life story

Klegseth (also Klegset) is a farm in Selbu, Sor-Trondelag. There is no evidence he came to America himself; both children's certificates record only the parents' names, not their residence. ¶2
