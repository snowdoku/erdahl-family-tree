Life story

Marriage: as Einar A Paulsen he married Clara Larsen (widow of Albert Mandius Larsen) on 24 Oct 1953 in Whatcom County, Washington (Whatcom County Auditor's marriage index, document nwwtcmcv54_88, filed 9 Dec 1953). ¶1

Death (Probable): the Washington death index lists Einar A. Paulsen, died 21 Oct 1963 in Bellingham (reference de_1963022597); it is the only Einar Paulsen death in the state index. The index gives no age, so the identification rests on name, place and date. His full middle name Amandus and the life dates 1895-1963 come from Clara's Find a Grave memorial (a lead, not evidence). The death certificate (hand search H0079) would give his birth date and birthplace and confirm him. ¶2

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
