Life story

Baptism: 11 May 1764 in Treungen, Targie, son of Osmund Evindsen Hiemdal (Kviteseid church book 1754-1773 p.89). His age in 1801 (34) and at burial in 1842 fit. ¶4

First marriage, the mother of Osmund: betrothed on the 5th Sunday in Lent 1791 at Kirkenes, Nissedal, Targie Osmundsen Hiemdal and pige Birgit Targiesdatter Frontved. Cautioners: Torjus Mule and Knud Halvorsen Frontved. They married on the 8th Sunday after Trinity 1791 in Treungen church (Kviteseid 1787-1799). The children of this first marriage in 1801 (Gundbiør ~1792, Osmund ~1795, Gunvor ~1797) fit this marriage. ¶5

1801 census, Hiemdal (Kviteseid prestegjeld): Targie Aasmundsen, aged 34, farmer, in his 2nd marriage, with wife Turi Halvorsdatter (27). His children by the first marriage were Gundbiør (9), Osmund (6) and Gunvor (4). Also in the household: his stepmother Signe Tellevsdatter (58), a widow. ¶1

Paid the 1816 silver tax at Hiemdal as Tarje Osmundsen. ¶2

Buried May 1842 in Treungen as Torjes/Tarje Osmundsen, b. ~1765. ¶3
