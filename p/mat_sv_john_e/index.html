Life story

Named as the father in the baptism of Erik Johns Eidem, born 28 October 1817 and baptised 9 November 1817, Selbu parish register 1816-1824, pages 44-45 serial 26, with the mother Brynhild Jensdatter, residence Eidem. ¶1

1825 census: 'John Eriks.', aged 38, HMD. (husmand, cottager), household 016 at rural residence 0059* Eidem, with 'Brynhild' 34 as Hustru and children Erik Johnsen 8, Ragnhild Johnsd. 5 and Maglie Johnsd. 2. ¶2

He is the head of the OTHER Johnsen line on the Eidem farm - the cottager family whose son married into the freeholder family in 1866. He is not related by blood to Sivert Johnsen Eidem on any evidence yet found. ¶3
