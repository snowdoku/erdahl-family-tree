About the family

Widow of Lars Gregoriussen of Store Ringdal, Sunnylven, at his probate of 26 Sep 1727 ('hans efterladte Enke Maret Andersdatter'), which names 'deres sammenavlede Børn' Gregorius Larsen and Brit Larsdatter (Sunnmøre skifteprotokoll 3A 09 fol. 787, 24551/410, scan read). ¶1

Mother of Brithe Larsdatter (see her page), Probable; graded on her link to her. Same reasoning as for Lars Gregoriussen: the 1727 probate names the daughter Brit Larsdatter of Store Ringdal; Brite Larsdtr. of Rindalen married Knud Olsen in 1742 and died at Store Ringdal by 1759. The identification spans 15 years with unread ages, so Probable. ¶2

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Regrade 2 (1 Nov 2026): upgraded to Confirmed as mother of Brithe Larsdatter (see her page); graded on her link to Brithe Larsdatter. Naming record: the 26 Sep 1727 probate after Lars Gregoriussen at Store Ringdal (3A 09 fol. 787, 24551/410, scan) names her as widow and the children Gregorius Larsen and Brit Larsdatter as 'deres sammenavlede Børn' (their children together), so it states her motherhood, not a stepmother's role. Continuity as for her husband: Brite Larsdtr. of Rindalen (Ringdal) married Knud Olsen in Dec 1742 (15 years, same farm) and died at Store Ringdal by 1759; no named rival. ¶3a

Records that disagree 1

Resolved discrepancy: the typed probate card 77797/2429 (Rindal store, 26/9-1727) gives the widow as 'Marithe Pedersdtr.'. The protocol was re-read at full resolution (3A 09 fol. 787, 24551/410), and it clearly reads 'Marett Anders datter', with her guardian Ingebrigt [..] Moe. The card is a transcription error, and her patronymic Andersdatter stands. The card also gives the children's guardians as Halvor Olsen Herdahl (for Gregorius, 1) and Joen Olsen Herdahl (for Brite Larsdatter). ¶4
