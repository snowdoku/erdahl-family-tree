About the family

Father of Susanna Margaretha Schumayer (see her page), of Vaihingen an der Enz. Her 1737 bride row gives 'Joh. Christoph' as first relative (the father, by this index's order; roles unlabelled; record 904777562), and her birth of 18 Sep 1716 names 'Joh. Christoph, Anna Sibylla' (record 4768896). Another daughter: Susanna Catharina, b. 26 Dec 1714 (record 4768783). Probable: index roles unlabelled; images not read. Graded on his link to Susanna Margaretha. ¶1

Family: his wife Anna Sibylla probably died at Vaihingen on 4 Apr 1719 (death row 'Anna Sibylla', relative 'Joh. Christoph', record 4400295). Other children: 'Christian Margaretha Schamaÿer' b. 3 Jan 1714 (record 4768716) and Susanna Catharina b. 26 Dec 1714 (record 4768783). A Joh. Philipp Schumaÿer x Ursula Catharina had children at Vaihingen 1707-1714 (possibly a brother). His marriage (about 1712-13) is not indexed under Schumayer/Schuhmaier/Schumacher. ¶3

Mother entered at Speculative: Christina Catharina, wife of Jacob Christoph Schumayer in 1683-85 (see Christina Catharina). A 'Johann Christoph Schumager' remarried at Vaihingen on 15 Aug 1719 to Maria Judith (4776817), after Anna Sibylla's death on 4 Apr 1719; that bride cannot be his sister Maria Judith (b. 1683), so the name match is coincidence or a different family. ¶7

Life story

Marriage and father: 'Johann Christoph Schumeze' (misread Schumayer), son of Jacob Christoph, married Anna Sibylla 'Hernery' (Herner), daughter of Carl, at Vaihingen in 1713 (groom row 4776645, bride row 904776645, image 1184562-00494). This is the marriage could not find under Schumayer spellings; the bride's name, the place and the children from 1714 identify it. His own baptism is not indexed. A Jacob Cristoph Schumajer, relatives 'Cristoph Ulrich, Elisabetha', died at Vaihingen 13 Jun 1754 (4404678): a later namesake, probably a grandson. ¶6

Baptism: row 'Johann Christoph Sundermeyer', baptism, 18 Jul 1680, place 'Kirchenbuch', relatives 'Jacob Christoph, Christina Catharina' (record 5440561, image 1184615-00166). 'Sundermeyer' is an index misreading of Schumaÿer: the same couple Jacob Christoph x Christina Catharina baptise Maria Judith 'Schumaÿer' (1683, image 00173) and Esther Regina 'Schumaÿer' (1685, image 00177) on the same film, and no other Johann Christoph with a father Jacob Christoph is indexed. His 1713 marriage row at Vaihingen names his father 'Jacob Christoph' (4776645), and an age of 33 at marriage fits. Birth date entered, place left blank: film 1184615 is indexed only as 'Kirchenbuch' (parish not identified; it holds baptisms 1602-1790s, marriages 1645-1801, burials 1685-1789). The identification of the 1680 child with the 1713 groom spans two parishes, so it is Probable (audit 4). ¶8a

Parish of film 1184615: not identified. Unterriexingen, Untermberg and Bissingen an der Enz are not indexed under their own names in Ancestry 61023, and the film sits next to Oberriexingen (1184613); its entries include a noble marriage (Georg Adam von Sterner, 1678) and men styled 'Herr', which would fit Unterriexingen (seat of the Leutrum family), but this is a guess. Signin S0245 asks for the catalog entry. ¶9
