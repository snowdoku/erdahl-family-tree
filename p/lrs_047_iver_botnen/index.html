About the family

Children: Lisbeth, baptised 5 Jan 1748 (godparents Johannes in Taskle, Hans Persen in Taskle, Jon Bendixsen in Nordal[?], Johannes Olsen and Borni Nielsdatter of Hjartholm); Rasmus, baptised 24 Feb 1750 (godparents Madame Sommer and four people of Taskle). A Johannes Iversen and a Lisbeth Iversdatter of Botnen were godparents together on 1 May 1774; Johannes is probably a younger son. ¶2

First wife (Probable): Lisbeth Eilevsdatter. On 7 Oct 1735 'Iver Botnens Søn N. Endre, Mat: Lispet Ellevsd.' (Iver Botnen's son named Endre, mother Lisbeth Eilevsdatter) was baptised in Gulen; a godmother was 'Berthe Ellevsd.'. On 4 Nov 1735 at Brekke 'Iver Botnens g[ift]e Lispet' (Iver Botnen's wife Lisbeth) was churched. In probate no. 46 the line on his first marriage reads 'med den … Kone Lisbeth [E]ilefs D[atte]r' (the patronymic is a probable reading). She was therefore the mother of Endre (bapt. 7 Oct 1735), and probably of Anna (born about 1728) and Eli (born about 1736). Married before 7 Oct 1735; she is not linked as a person. Gulen ministerialbok 1729-1735 (source 11440), canvases 85-86. ¶9

Pedigree collapse: Iver Monsen appears twice in this line. Through his second marriage he is the grandfather of Iver Rasmussen of Botnen (son of Rasmus Iversen); through his first marriage he is the great-grandfather of Marthe Olsdatter (daughter of Sigrid Johannesdatter, daughter of Anna Iversdatter). Iver Rasmussen married Marthe Olsdatter on 13 Jun 1813, so the couple were half first cousins once removed, and their descendants descend from Iver Monsen by both lines. ¶12

Parentage (Probable, strong): son of Mons Johansen of Indre Oppedal (see his page) and Randi Iversdatter (see her page); he is the '1. Søn Iver' of their 1739 probates. Evidence: (1) at the probate after his first wife at Botten on 8 Oct 1739 (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll 5, Nr. 78, fol. 125a) the court names 'Farbr[oder]: Peder Monsøn Indre Tackle' (the children's father's brother, Peder Monsen of Indre Takle) as present for his minor children, so Iver Monsen of Botten had a brother Peder Monsen; (2) Mons Johansen and Randi Iversdatter's third son was Peder ('3d. Peder', fol. 121b; '3 Søn Peder', fol. 124b; the heading of Nr. 77 on fol. 123b writes '3 Søn Mons', a scribal slip), and a creditor 'Peder Monsøn Tackle' is paid from Mons Johansen's own estate (fol. 122a; he is listed among the creditors, not called a son), which places a Peder Monsen of Takle among those dealing with that estate; (3) Randi Iversdatter's lifetime division at Indre Oppedal (Nr. 77, 7 Oct 1739) and the Botten probate (Nr. 78, 8 Oct 1739) were held on consecutive days by the same court officers (Anders Bøe and the bailiff Ananias Christopher Harberg), and Iver is the eldest son in Nr. 77; (4) Iver named a son Mons and a daughter Randi by his first marriage (both in Nr. 78), after the paternal grandparents, and a son Johannes by his second marriage, after Mons's father Johannes Larsen (naming inference); (5) at his second betrothal on 13 Sep 1739 a cautioner was 'Torchil Asheim', very probably Torkel Monsen, who had married at Asheim earlier in 1739 and is himself a Probable son of Mons Johansen (circumstantial). No record read states 'Iver Botten, son of Mons Johansen' in so many words, so the link is Probable rather than Confirmed. ¶16a

Pedigree collapse, extended: with this link Mons Johansen and Randi Iversdatter are ancestors of this line by three routes: through their youngest son Torkild Monsen of Asheim (see his page), and through their eldest son Iver Monsen of Botnen twice, by his first marriage (Anna Iversdatter, then Sigrid Johannesdatter, then Marthe Olsdatter) and by his second marriage (Rasmus Iversen, then Iver Rasmussen of Botnen). (Supersedes the 'Possible son of Mons Johansen... not linked' note: the link is now made, grade Probable.) ¶19

Father raised from Probable to Confirmed: Mons Johansen of Indre Oppedal (see his page). Records that moved the grade: (1) the baptism of 15 Aug 1704 (Gulen ministerialbok 11433, canvas 150) is a primary record that names the child Iver's father as Mons Johansen of Indre Oppedal, with patronymic and farm; it is the direct parent record that the audit said was missing; (2) the baptismal date fits Iver Monsen of Botnen's burial age of 81 years [9] months on 15 May 1786 (Gulen ministerialbok 11444, side 117a) to the month; (3) the probate after Mons Johansen of 16 May 1739 (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll 5, Nr. 76) and Randi Iversdatter's lifetime division of 7 Oct 1739 (Nr. 77) name Iver as Mons's eldest son; (4) at the probate after Iver's first wife at Botten on 8 Oct 1739 (Nr. 78) the children's 'Farbroder' (father's brother) was Peder Monsen of Indre Takle, and Peder was Mons Johansen's third son in Nr. 76 ('3d. Peder') and in the Nr. 77 allotments ('3 Søn Peder'; the Nr. 77 heading writes '3 Søn Mons', a scribal slip). The baptism and the Farbroder entry are independent of each other and agree. Only one Mons was named as a father at Indre Oppedal on the canvases (11433 c129, c143, c149, c150, c152; not a full search of 1704), and the other Mons of the farm, Mons Iversen, was a boy of about 13 in 1704 (aged 10 in the 1701 census). (Supersedes, as to the father, the 'Parentage (Probable, strong)' note, whose 'would confirm' records are now answered by the baptism.) ¶26

Mother kept at Probable: Randi Iversdatter (see her page). For: Mons Johansen's probate of 16 May 1739 (Nr. 76) divides the estate between 'Enken Randi Iversdatter' and the children begotten together ('deris [deres] sammenavlede Børn', the children they begot together; read 'deres' and re-read from the 121b crop; 'hendes [med ham]' is a misreading), first among them 'Sønnen Iver'; and her own division of 7 Oct 1739 (Nr. 77) is 'imellem hendes 5 Sønner, 1. Søn Iver', each son taking a near-equal share of her estate, which a stepson would not normally receive. Iver also named his second daughter Randi (Nr. 78). Why not Confirmed: the baptism of 1704 and the churching of 19 Sep 1704 do not name the mother, so no record of his birth names her; and two facts leave room for a first wife of Mons who bore Iver: (a) Randi Iversdatter, daughter of Iver Lassesen of Indre Oppedal, was unmarried in 1699 and probably in 1701, and as the second of five daughters she may have been born about 1689, which would make her about 15 in Aug 1704; (b) a 'Randi Ifversdr: ibid' of Indre Oppedal stood godmother on 10 Apr 1712 with no husband named (see the next note). The probate wording 'sammenavlede' is a legal statement of joint parentage and is the strongest evidence; it outweighs these doubts, but it is one clerk's formula, not a birth record. ¶27a

Mother raised from Probable to Confirmed: Randi Iversdatter (see her page). Records that moved the grade: (1) the betrothal of 3 Jun 1703 at Brekke, 'Juv: Mons Johansøn Indreoppedahl, Virg: Rannei Iversdatter ibid.' (Gulen ministerialbok 1699-1705, Digitalarkivet 11433, canvas 112, left page and), shows that his father was a bachelor fourteen months before his baptism of 15 Aug 1704, so no earlier wife of Mons can be his mother; (2) his mother was Mons's wife, who was churched on 19 Sep 1704 (11433, canvas 152); (3) the probate of 16 May 1739 (Nr. 76) names him first among 'deris sammenavlede Børn' (the children begotten together) of Mons Johansen and 'Enken Randi Iversdatter', and her division of 7 Oct 1739 (Nr. 77) calls him her '1. Søn Iver'. This answers the 'Would confirm' of the note 'Mother kept at Probable': the betrothal is the marriage-side record it asked for, though the wedding entry itself is still unread. The 1712 godmother 'Randi Ifversdr' was probably a different woman (inference; see the note on Randi Iversdatter); the grade does not depend on her. (Supersedes the 'Mother kept at Probable' and the 'Godmother of 1712, a homonym or not' notes as to the grade.) ¶31

Life story

Baptism: on Friday 15 Aug 1704 in Eivindvik church, Gulen parish, 'Mons Johans: Indreoppedahls Iver' (Iver, son of Mons Johansen of Indre Oppedal) was baptised. Godparents as read: 'Suscept: Ontorm[?] (perhaps Gutorm) Indreoppedahl' (Suscept[ores] heads the list of godparents), 'Hans ibid.' (of the same farm), 'Hermand[?] [Ryst?]adsh[?] ibid.', the wife ('Qvinde') of Anders Larsen of the same farm, and Barbra, wife of Henrich of the same farm. The mother is not named and no birth date is given. Gulen ministerialbok 1699-1705 (Digitalarkivet 11433), canvas 150, left page, printed p. 199. Henrich Indre Oppedal, whose wife Barbra stood here, was himself a godparent of Iver's brother Torkel in 1719 and an appraiser of Randi Iversdatter's estate in 1739 (Nr. 77). ¶23

Churching: on Friday 19 Sep 1704 at Brekke church 'Introduceret Mons Indreoppedahls Qvinde' (the wife of Mons of Indre Oppedal was churched, i.e. returned to church after childbirth), five weeks after Iver's baptism. The wife is not named. Gulen ministerialbok 1699-1705 (Digitalarkivet 11433), canvas 152, left page, printed p. 203. ¶24

Birth, corrected: before 15 Aug 1704, the date of his baptism, probably at Indre Oppedal, his father's farm (place inferred). This replaces the index-derived 'ABT 1705' (and the 'ABT 1704' proposed). It agrees with his burial entry of 15 May 1786, which gives his age as 81 aar and 9 maaneder (the 9 confirmed from the crop): 15 Aug 1704 to 15 May 1786 is exactly 81 years 9 months, so he was born in the summer of 1704, at most a few weeks before the baptism (inference). ¶25

Godparent: 'Iver Botnen' was godfather on 25 Sep 1735 at Brekke to Hendrich, son of Lars of Ytre Hiertholm, beside 'Jon jndre Hiertholm', probably the father of his later daughter-in-law Synneve. ¶11

Second marriage: to Lisbet Rasmusdatter, after about 1736 (Eli of the first marriage was born about then) and before 5 Jan 1748, when their daughter Lisbeth was baptised. Not found in the Gulen registers read from Jul 1735 to Jun 1749. ¶10

Second marriage, corrected: betrothed to Lisbet Rasmusdatter at Brekke on 13 Sep 1739 ('trolovet Iver Monsen Botnen og Lisbet Rasmusd.', cautioners Lars Breche and Torchil Asheim) and married 13 Nov 1739 (margin 'd. 13 Nov: Copl.'). Gulen ministerialbok 11441, canvas 15. (Supersedes the date range in the earlier 'Second marriage' note.) ¶18

Superseded: the 'Parentage (Probable, strong)' note now stands only as the evidence list for the 1739 probates; the father link is Confirmed (see the 'Father raised' note) and the mother link remains Probable (see the 'Mother kept at Probable' note). The 'Burial (per index)' and 'Burial record page ' statements that his birth year 1705 or age of about 81 came from the index only are superseded by the 1704 baptism and the burial age. ¶29

Name: Recorded as 'Iver Botnen' (Iver of the farm Botnen, Brekke) in the baptisms of his children Lisbeth (5 Jan 1748) and Rasmus (24 Feb 1750), with his wife Lisbeth named as mother each time. His patronymic is not proved. An 'Iwer Mogensen Botnen' stood godfather on 9 Jul 1774 together with 'Lisbeth Rasmus D. Botnen' (the 'Mogensen' is overwritten in the register and only probably read). He is therefore probably Iver Mogensen, but the name is left as plain 'Iver' until his marriage, burial or probate is found. ¶1

Trap: Iver Olsen of Botnen, betrothed in Jan 1748 to Kari Arnesdatter of Kjekevoll (married Dom. 5 p. Epiph. 1748), is a different man: our Iver's wife Lisbeth bore children in Jan 1748 and Feb 1750. Botnen may have held more than one household; check the mother's name on every 'Iver Botnen' child. ¶4

Godparent: 'Iver botnen' was the first godfather of Synneve, daughter of Jon Hiertholm, baptised 3 May 1749, who married his son Rasmus in 1773. ¶3

Name: Iver Monsen. Probate no. 45, held at Botten on 22-23 Sep 1779 after his wife Lisbet Rasmusdatter, names the widower (*enkemand*) as Iver Monsen and their six children, including the second son 'Rasmus Iversen boende paa … Botten'. He is the 'Iwer Mogensen Botnen' (Mogens is a form of Mons) who stood godfather on 9 Jul 1774. Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll A 9b, p. 1041. ¶7

Probate of 1779 (no. 46): on 25 Sep 1779, after his wife's probate was finished, Iver Monsen, who was to live with his son Rasmus Iversen at Botten, divided his estate among his children of both marriages. Children of the first marriage: Endre Iversen of Uthle, of age and present; Anna Iversdatter, eldest daughter, married to Johannes Herløffsen of Ytre Takle; and Eli Iversdatter, aged 43[?], whose guardian (*lauverge*) was her half-brother Rasmus Iversen Botten. Children of the second marriage, with Lisbet Rasmusdatter: Mons, Rasmus, Bendix, Johannes, Brithe and Lisbeth. Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll A 9b, p. 1044. ¶8

Burial scan: the burial register in Gulen ministerialbok 1779-1788 (Digitalarkivet source 11445, burials, canvas 50, book page 320, under 'Aar 1786') has entry Nr. 525, 'Tirsdag d 6te Junii i Brekke', 'Mand Iver Monsson Botnen' (Tuesday 6 June [1786], at Brekke church, married man Iver Monsen of Botnen); no age column. The record page pg00000000768623, which belongs to this book (11445, 1779-1788), not to the 1776-1787 book as earlier cited, gives the same day in its interment field and the remark 'Likpr: Tirsdag den 6te Junii' (funeral sermon Tuesday 6 June). Read from the scan and row. ¶21

Burial (per index): 'Iver Monssøn', Mand, of Botnen, Brekke, buried 15 May 1786; the index gives a birth year of 1705. Gulen church book 1776-1787 (index pg00000000768623; a second row in the 1779-1788 book gives the year only). Index-level; the book was not read. Name and farm match, and he was alive on 25 Sep 1779 (probate no. 46). Birth about 1705 is taken from the index birth year and is approximate; it would make him about 30 at his son Endre's baptism in 1735. ¶14

Burial record page: the burial of 'Iver Monssøn', Mand (married man), Botnen, Brekke, 15 May 1786 (the burial date and field already in the tree) has a record page (pg00000000768623) with the age left blank and the remark 'Likpr: Tirsdag den 6te Junii' (funeral sermon Tuesday 6 June). His age of about 81 at burial is therefore inferred from the index birth year 1705 only, not read from the record. The scan (Gulen ministerialbok 1776-1787) was not read; it may give the age. ¶20

Records that disagree 2

Burial date conflict: the burial field (15 May 1786) comes from an index row cited to the Gulen book 1776-1787 (source 11444), whose 1786 entry has not been read on the scan. Book 11445 and the record page both give Tuesday 6 June 1786, and 6 Jun 1786 was indeed a Tuesday, while 15 May 1786 was a Monday, an unusual day for a rural funeral. The burial field is left at 15 May for now, because the 6 June entry is explicitly a funeral sermon (likpreken), and in this period the sermon could be held some weeks after a quiet interment, so the two dates can both be true; the 15 May date may also be a transcription slip. Which is the burial: unresolved. His death is safely before 15 May 1786 on either reading, or before 6 Jun 1786 at the latest. ¶22a

Burial date conflict resolved: the 11444 entry (age 81 aar 9 maaneder; the 9 confirmed) reads '15te Maji' on its first line (the burial) and '6te Junii' on its second (the interment with funeral sermon, likpreken), and the index row pg00000001734176 gives burial 05-15 and interment 06-06. The 15 May burial date stands and the 6 Jun 1786 date (Tuesday, in book 11445 and record pg00000000768623) is the later church interment and sermon, so the conflict noted is resolved. The 15 May date should be cited to pg00000001734176, not to pg00000000768623. ¶30

Alternatives considered and set aside 2

Possible son of Mons Johansen of Indre Oppedal (not linked): he is a candidate for 'Sønnen Iver', the eldest son of Mons Johansen (see his page) and Randi Iversdatter in the probate of 16 May 1739 at Indre Oppedal. The 1739 probate after Mons Johansen of Indre Oppedal (skifteprotokoll 5, fol. 121b-122a) names his eldest son only as 'Sønnen Iver': it gives no age and no residence for any son, and the estate was insolvent, so no shares were assigned that might name farms. A probable reading 'og som umyndige' (and, as minors) was judged doubtful and cannot be used to exclude or include an adult Iver. The dates fit: Mons Johansen was 22 in the 1701 census (born about 1679) and Iver Monsen of Botnen was born about 1705 (burial index), when Mons would have been about 26; Iver of Botnen named sons Mons and Johannes by his second marriage, which fits Mons Johansen and his father Johannes Larsen, but these are common names. Against or neutral: nothing places Iver of Botnen at Indre Oppedal, none of his daughters (Anna, Eli, Brithe, Lisbeth) is named Randi after Mons's widow Randi Iversdatter, and his first son was named Endre. If he was that Iver, he would also be the brother of Torkild Monsen of Asheim, and Mons Johansen and Randi Iversdatter would be ancestors of this line through him as well as through Torkild (a further pedigree collapse; he already appears twice). The identity rests on the name only and is not linked. Records that would settle it: the probate of Lasse Monsen, Indre Oppedal, 1782-91 (index pa00000000525057); Iver Monsen's own probate after May 1786; the index row Randi Iversdatter, Oppedal Indre, 1738-39 (pa00000000520993); or his first marriage (about 1727, inferred from his daughter Anna's birth about 1728). ¶15

Godmother of 1712, a homonym or not (inference): on 10 Apr 1712 at Brekke 'Randi Ifversdr: ibid' (Randi Iversdatter of Indre Oppedal) was a godmother at the baptism of Anders, son of Iver Gutormsen of Indre Oppedal, and a 'Randi ibid' stood on 26 Sep 1712 (Gulen ministerialbok 11436, canvases 23 and 27). In this register a wife is normally entered as 'N.N.s qvinde' (the wife of N.N.), so this Randi was probably unmarried. She is taken to be a different Randi Iversdatter from Mons Johansen's wife, because Mons had a wife by Aug 1704 (the churching of 19 Sep 1704), and the 1739 probate says that wife was the mother of all five sons, Iver (b. 1704) included; Indre Oppedal had more than one Iver who could have had a daughter Randi (Iver Gutormsen, father in 1712, and an 'Iver Indreoppedahl' named beside Mons Johansen on 30 Jul 1704). That reasoning assumes the mother link it is meant to protect, so it is circular: if the 1712 godmother is Mons's later wife, Iver was the son of an unrecorded first wife. This is why the mother link stays Probable. ¶28
