About the family

Parents: the probate after her father Helge Knudsen of Midtstrand, 11 Oct 1739 (card 9297; protocol DA 25286 p. 279, view 151, read) names her as 'Datteren Ragnild Helgesdatter 4 aar gl.' among the 3 children of the deceased and his widow Barbro Haagensdatter. The 1754 bride 'Ragnild Helgesdr. Mitstrand' is the same woman: same forename, patronymic and farm 15 years later, and the bygdebok's birth year 1735 for Ole Arnesen's wife matches the age 4 in 1739. ¶3

Her husband Ole Arnesen was dead by 12 Jul 1799, when she was a widow at Strand (Midtstrand is filed under Strand in that card and protocol). She was alive on that date. ¶5

Life story

Burial: 'Ragnild Helgesdatter', Enken (the widow), of Midstrand, aged 75 (b. about 1735), buried in July 1810, day not given in the index (Nord-Aurdal ministerialbok 1808-1815; index pg00000001088453). Farm, widowhood (Ole Arnesen d. about 1791) and age (4 in her father's 1739 probate = b. about 1735) all fit. Replaces the ABT 1810 death. ¶6

Marriage: the Aurdal church book (DA 9361, p. 93, canvas 315) records the betrothal on 23 May 1754 of 'Ole Arnesøn Bøe og Ragnild Helgesd[atte]r Mitstrand' (Ole Arnesen of Bø and Ragnild Helgesdatter of Midtstrand), sureties Henrich Bøe and Herraad[?] Agervold, married 1 July 1754. This supplies the forename that fell in a gap of the bygdebok rebuild, where Ola Arneson of Midtstrand (1729-1791) married in 1754 a '[..] Høljesdotter' (1735-1810); Hølje and Helge are the same name. ¶1

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Graded on her link to her daughter Marit Olsdatter (see her page): Probable. The marriage record proves who Ole Arnesen's wife was in 1754, but the mother-daughter link rests on the bygdebok's children list (Svenes vol., canvas 278: '1 Marit 1754, g 1780 m Eivind Julsson, gnr 76'), a compiled source. A son Hølje (b. 1756, d. young; a second Hølje b. 1757) carries her father's name, which fits. ¶2a

Upgrade: graded on her link to her daughter Marit Olsdatter (see her page): Confirmed. Two primary records name the relationship. (1) The Aurdal church book records at Svenes on 6 Oct 1754 the baptism of 'Marit', child of 'Ole Arnesøn Mitstrand og Ragnild Helgesd[atter]' (DA 9361 p. 435, canvas 225 left page), three months after their wedding of 1 Jul 1754. (2) The division at Strand of 12 Jul 1799 (Valdres skifteprotokoll 1798-1810, DA 25294 pp. 82-83; card 13047) was held at the request of 'Enken Ragnild Helgesdatter', widow of Ole Arnesøn, between the widow's children, 3 sons and 5 daughters, the fourth heir being 'den ældste Datter Marit Olsd[atte]r gift med Even Juelsen Haverud'. Even Julsen of Hovrud is Marit's husband in the tree (see his page, m. 9 Oct 1780), so the 1799 record ties Ragnild directly to the Marit of the tree, with no gap to bridge; the 1754 baptism shows that this eldest daughter was born of Ole and Ragnild's marriage. ¶4a

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

No rival: the 1799 heirs include only one Marit. ¶4b

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Test record: Marit's baptism in the Aurdal church book (DA 9361, baptisms of mid-1754 to 1755, about canvases 222-235) naming the parents Ole Arnesen and Ragnild Helgesdatter, or Ragnild's own probate at Midtstrand about 1810 (Valdres card index DA 39948, Midtstrand cards 9299 on; saw an 1810 Midtstrand card, card 9299, not yet tied to her). ¶2b
