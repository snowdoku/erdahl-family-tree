Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Speculative as mother of Marethe Guldbrandsdatter (see her page). She is 'Gurj Joensdtr', the wife of Guldbrand at Langeteig in the 1645 poll tax (p. 24), the year the daughter 'Marete Guldbrandsdtr' is listed with the farm's children. Guldbrand was born ~1580, so his children (born before ~1633) may be of an earlier marriage; nothing shows that Guri bore Marete. Langeteig evidence. (1) 1645 poll tax, Langeteig in Tiugum sogn: four farmers with wives, 'Guldbrandt' + 'Gurj Joensdtr', 'Peder' + 'Brite Olsdtr', 'Østen' + 'Ingier Frederichsdtr', 'Lauridtz' + 'Ellj Jonsdtr' (p. 24); the farm's sons and daughters (p. 29) include five children of Guldbrand: Johans, Peder, Olle, Astri and 'Marete Guldbrandsdtr'. No other Guldbrand at Langeteig. (2) 1666 vicar's census, Tiugum sogn N. 21 Langeteig (35519 view 170, p. 314): holders Ola 59, Harmund 45, 'Gulbrand bruger... 86 aar' (b. ~1580) and 'Frich bruger... 26 aar' (b. ~1640) with his son 'Niels 1 1/2 aar' (b. ~1664/65). No Peder is left on the farm. (2b) 1664 vicar's census, Tiugum sogn, Langeteig (35519 view 152, p. 281): farmers 'Ola Erichsen', 'Gulbrand', 'Harmund', 'Frich'; servant 'Lasse Andbiørnsen' 17; husmann 'Engelbart'; again no Peder. (3) 21 Mar 1701, Langeteig: joint probate after Frich Pedersen and his wife Marethe Guldbrandsdatter, heirs their son Niels 'boende Langeteig' and daughter Britte, wife of Jørgen Langeteig (1b p. 275a). So in 1666 the young holder Frich (= Frich Pedersen, d. by 1701) farms beside the old Gulbrand, with a son Niels (= the 1701 heir Niels of Langeteig), and his wife Marethe Guldbrandsdatter carries the patronymic of the only Guldbrand on the farm, whose daughter Marete is listed there in 1645. ¶1a
