About the family

Children (Probable): Rannei Ormsdatter Tvedt (see her page), betrothed 10 Nov 1775 to Thore Joensen Midbø. She was betrothed in Treungen on the day her probable brother Egild Ormsen Tvedt (see his page, line 4) married there. In 1790 'Gulov Omundsen' of Treungen, that is Gulloug Ormsen of Tvedt (60 in 1801), and another Omundsen/Ormsen of Treungen were appointed guardians of her orphaned daughters (Vest-Telemark skifteprotokoll 1790-94, source 25679, skifte No 21). A probate after Orm Tvedt, or after his wife, in the Øvre Telemark protocols would name all his children and would confirm the link. ¶3

Wife: the probate after Targiej Torrofsen at Sønderland in Treungen, 15 Sep 1756 (25673 view 331, register No 195), names among the heirs 'Anne Torgiejsdatter gift med Orm Egilssøn Tvedt af Treungen'. This adds his wife Anne Targiesdatter (see her page) and confirms his patronymic Egilsen a third time. Her parents' names explain his children Sigri (b. 1758) and Targgi (b. 1760, 1762). His own grade (on his link to Rannei) is unchanged. ¶17

Wife's burial: 'Orm Egildsen Tveds Hustrue Anne', aged 68, was buried at Treungen church on or about 28 May 1786 (Kviteseid ministerialbok 7982, canvas 183 right). So Orm was alive in May 1786, and his own probate, if any, falls after that date. He does not appear among the Treungen burials of Oct 1783 to Oct 1786 (canvases 177-184). The skifte after Anne (mid-1786 or later, Vest-Telemark skifteprotokoll 1784-90, source 25678) is the record that would name his children. ¶20

Life story

1720 probate of his father: 'Orm Eigildsøn' was the only son of Eigild Biørensøn and Rannei Haavarsdatter of Tvedt in Treungen, 26 Apr 1720 (25692 fol. 420b, view 432; card 6856 view 1310); his age is not written (the sisters were 9, 7 and ½). Mother added: Rannei Haavorsdatter. This grades the parents' link to him (Probable, by the identification of the probate's Orm with Orm Egilsen Tvedt(en) of 1755-69: same farm, patronymic and age, but a gap of about 26 years); his own grade is unchanged. ¶10

Deeds and land registers: the Vest-Telemark panteregister I 1 (source 10915; Kviteseid farm index at view 19, 'Tvedt i Treungen' fol. 34, 141, 268, 314/315, 331) gives him by full name on the farm four times: (1) 12 Mar 1746, a document 'Wi underskrevne Targier Nottofsøn og Orm Eigelsøn boende paa gaarden Tvedt udi Treungens annex' about the plads Tvedtstuen, held from Sergeant Johan Valentin Bech and his wife, signed by both men, published at Fjellestad ting 7 Mar 1748 (pantebok I 4, source 10869, no. 575, view 213; register fol. 141); (2) a skjøde to 'Orm Egilsøn' on land in Tvedt for 200 rdl, tinglyst 11 Oct 1758 (fol. 141); (3) 'Orm Igieldsøn Tvedt' received a skjøde from the heirs of Madame B.. for land at Tvedtstuen for 220 rdl, 8 Mar 1775 (fol. 268b); (4) 'Igild Ormsøn erholdt Skiøde af sin Fader Orm Igildsøn paa Pladsen Tvedtstuen under denne Gaard for 240 rd, datt. og tinglyst 14de Octobr 1777' (fol. 315, view 358). ¶12

1729 probate: 'Orm Igildsøn 12 aar' was the only son among the four heirs of his aunt Margith Bjørnsdatter of Tvedt in Treungen, 7 Nov 1729 (25668, skifte no. 188, fol. 272a). Born about 1717; birth changed from 'BEF 1720' to 'ABT 1717'. Sisters (Probable, same record): Tone (about 1709), Kari (about 1712) and Walborg (about 1719) Egilsdøtre. His daughter Kari (buried 1765) may be named after the sister or the aunt's family (inference). Identification with the later Orm Egilsen Tvedt is by farm, patronymic and age (Probable). ¶9

Orm Tvedt's patronymic: protocol 10, image 491, in the debts of the 1767 Nissedal skifte no. 645 (deceased Sara Ericksdatter), cites an earlier document involving 'Orm Egildsøn Tvedt', Ole Nielsøn Nordalle of Treungen and a date of 8 Mar 1763; Anders Evensen, Astiør Jørgensdatter Tvedt and Jørgen Sigursøn Hiemdall are among the creditors listed below. The patronymic Egildsøn is clearly written there. The 1775 cautioner reading '[T..]lefsen' is blotted. If he is Orm Egildsen, his son Egild Ormsen carries his paternal grandfather's name in the usual Norwegian pattern, which strengthens the Probable link (inference). The context of the 1763 document was not read. ¶4

Patronymic: the probate of Taarand Giermundsdatter of the cotter's place Napper under Søndre Aarak in Treungen (Vest-Telemark skifteprotokoll 1768-76, source 25675, skifte No 924, registered 1769, closing text on fol. 92b, view 95 left) appoints as guardian of the daughter Gundbiør Haalesdatter 'Formynder Orm Egilssøn Tvedten'. He is the only Orm known at Tvedt in Treungen at this time (Treungen burials 1755-65 and the 1761 and 1775 cautioner entries), so this is Orm Tvedt, and his father was named Egil. The blotted 1775 patronymic read '[T..]lefsen' (7982 c109L) should be re-read as Egilsen. It also fits the name of his probable son Egil Ormsen (Egild Ormsen Tveit, line 4), named after his grandfather. Name changed to Orm /Egilsen/ Tvedt; the farm stays as a byname because the records use it. ¶6

The 1777 entry is the first record found that names one of his children as his child: Egild Ormsen (see his page, line 4) is 'sin Fader Orm Igildsøn's' son. It settles the father of Egild (line 4's link, graded on this record); his link to Rannei Ormsdatter (line 2) is still by patronymic and farm, so Probable. Grade left at Probable here for the coordinator to decide: Confirmed is justified on the link to Egild Ormsen Tveit (deed abstract names 'sin Fader'; Egild Ormsen of Tvedt married 1775, the deed 1777 and the 1801 census, all on Tvedt, no other Egild Ormsen there), but not on the link to Rannei. ¶13

Land sale: panteregister I 1 (10915, fol. 126, view 168 right), 'Ibrache under Tvetten' in Nissedal: 'Orm Eigilsøn Tvedt' sold 7½ [settinger?] with bygsel in Ibrache for 103 rdl (deed dated 12, tingl. 15 Oct 1765). Another dated record of him by full patronymic; it does not help the 1730-45 gap. ¶16

Deed to his son: pantebok I 9 (source 10875) No 521, view 135 right: 'Jeg Underskrevne Orm Egilsen Tvedt' sells to 'min Søn Gunlog Ormsen' his property Tvedt in Treungen annex, matr. No 26, which he had bought by deed dated 16 Oct 1758 (tingl. 17 Oct 1758), for 300 rdl. Dated 6 Oct 1783, tingl. 9 Mar 1784; signed 'Orm Egilsen Tvedt'. Witnesses Biørne Ormundsen Groven and Jon Ormundsen (their kinship to him is not stated). So he was alive on 6 Oct 1783, he owned Tvedt matr. 26 from 1758, and Gulloug Ormsen (about 1741-1807) was his son by name in a primary record. His probate should fall after 1783, in the 1784-90 protocol (25678) or later; his wife Anne is not mentioned in the deed. ¶19

Burial: '4 Tr. Sønd. ved Triungens K. paa Orm Egildsen Tvedt gl. 76 Aar' (on the 4th Sunday after Trinity, at Treungen church, [earth cast] on Orm Egildsen Tvedt, aged 76). Kviteseid ministerialbok 1786-1801 (7983), p. 243, canvas 240 left page, in Kirke-Aaret 1793 between 18 Jun and 28 Jun entries; Trinity Sunday 1793 fell on 26 May, so the 4th Sunday after was 23 Jun 1793. Aged 76 gives birth about 1717, exactly as the 1729 probate ('Orm Igildsøn 12 aar') implies, which ties the 1793 burial to the boy of 1720/1729. He outlived his wife Anne (buried May 1786) by seven years. The burial names no child, so grades are unchanged. ¶21a

The case for this identification 2

Identity: Orm of the farm Tvedt (Tveit) in Treungen, Nissedal annex of Kviteseid prestegjeld, Telemark. The Treungen baptisms and burials of 1755-1765 in Kviteseid ministerialbok 1754-1773 (source 7981), read by a line 4 worker, show him burying children: a son Halvor (1755), a daughter Sigri (1759), sons Targgi (1761 and 1763) and a daughter Kari (1765). He was godfather at several Treungen baptisms of 1759-1761. He is not among the Treungen burials up to 1769. Birth before 1720 is estimated from a probable son Gulloug Ormsen, aged 60 in 1801 (born about 1741). ¶1

Identity: Orm Tvedt's patronymic is Egilsen (Egildsøn). Two independent primary records give it: the 1769 Vest-Telemark probate No 924 (source 25675, view 95 left, 'Formynder Orm Egilssøn Tvedten'), and a document of 8 Mar 1763 in Øvre Telemark protocol 10, image 491 ('Orm Egildsøn Tvedt', read by the line 4 worker). The blotted 1775 reading '[T..]lefsen' is superseded. ¶5

Corrections 1

Correction: the earlier reading 'Orm [T..]lefsen Tvedt' (Tellefsen) of the 10 Nov 1775 cautioner is contradicted by the 1769 probate, written in a clear hand, which gives 'Orm Egilssøn Tvedten'. Egilsen is preferred. ¶7

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Cautioner: 'Orm [T?]..lefsen Tvedt' stood cautioner at a Treungen betrothal of 10 Nov 1775 (Kviteseid ministerialbok 1773-1786, source 7982, canvas 109 left page, last entry). If the reading is right he was Orm Tellefsen (Tollefsen), son of a Tellef, and alive in Nov 1775. The patronymic is blotted and uncertain, so it is not used in his name. ¶2
