Life story

Her baptism entry of 1 Feb 1795 names her parents as Ole Arentsen and Kirsten Larsdatter of Gedderaas and lists five godparents: Sigri of Okkelberg, Anne of Almoen, Riborg of Frigaard, Thomas of Frigaard and Peder of Stræte. Two of these are telling. 'Anne of Almoen' is Anne Olsdatter, who had married Thomas Andersen of Almoen seven months earlier and would in 1816 become Maret's mother-in-law - so the two households already knew one another twenty years before the marriage. 'Peder of Stræte' comes from her mother's home farm. ¶2

Her known siblings, from the 1801 census of Geiteråsen and the baptism register: Gunnil (b. about 1789), Arnt (bapt. 1792), a first Marit (bapt. 1 Feb 1795 - this one), a second Marit (b. about 1796-97, and an earlier infant Marit who died in September 1801), Lars (b. 1799) and Paul (bapt. 1802). ¶3

Her 1816 marriage gives her birth as 1795 at Gedderaas. The only matching baptism is 'Marit', baptized 1 Feb 1795, daughter of Ole Arentsen and Kirsten Larsdatter of Gedderaas; one godmother was 'Anne, Almoen', probably her future mother-in-law. In the 1801 census she is 6, at Giedderaas with Ole Arntsen and Kirsten Larsdatter. A younger sister Marit (b. 1796) died in Sept 1801. ¶1

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: Records: (1) marriage at Værnes 4 Jun 1816 (Stjørdal ministerialbok 1815-1819, p. 195 no. 35): bride 'Mærit Olsdatter', Pige (unmarried woman), born 1795 in Stjørdal, residence Gedderaas; groom Ole Tomesen, bachelor cottager of Almoeaasen. (2) 1801 census, Stjørdal, rural residence 0007 Giedderaas: household 01 is Ole Arntsen 45, gaardmand, and Kirsten Larsdatter 42, both in their 1st marriage, with 'Deres børn' (their children) Gunnil 12, Arnt 9, Marit 6, Marit 4 and Lars 2; household 02 is the cottager Iver Iversen 61, his wife Gullov Joensdatter 69 and their daughter Anne Iversdatter 27. No other Marit Olsdatter lives at Giedderaas. (3) baptism 1 Feb 1795: Marit, daughter of Ole Arentsen and Kirsten Larsdatter of Gedderaas. The 1816 bride, an unmarried Marit Olsdatter of Gedderaas born 1795, can only be one of the two Marits in Ole Arntsen and Kirsten Larsdatter's household in 1801, since the farm's only other household had no Marit. Both of those Marits were the couple's own daughters ('Deres børn'), and the 1795 baptism names the parents, so her parentage rests on three primary records and moves from Probable to Confirmed. Which of the two Marits she was (the one baptised 1 Feb 1795, or the one aged 4 in 1801) does not change the parents; the stated birth year 1795 favours the elder. ¶4

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Burial candidate, not adopted: 'Marit Olsdatter', husmandskone (cottager's wife) of Gedderaasplads, aged 69 (b. about 1800), died 4 Sep and was buried 9 Sep 1869 at Hegra (Øvre Stjørdal ministerialbok, Hegra 1863-1879; index pg00000002377036). The place fits (a croft of Geiteråsen), but the age is five years short of her 1795 baptism, she would have to have remarried after Ole Tomasen died in 1822, and her younger sister Marit Olsdatter of Geiteråsen (b. about 1796-97) is an equal rival. No date entered. ¶5
