Life story

Baptism: Joen, son of Joen Joensen of Stuefloten and Anne Pedersdatter, was baptised in Lesja on 4 Jul 1816 (index only, pp. 844-845; remark 'Lægd No 1'). ¶1

Confirmation: confirmed at Grytten 11 Sep 1831 as John Johnsen of Setnæs (no. 4, p. 486), son of John Johnsen of Setnæs and Anne Pedersdatter (index only; the index calculates his birth year as 1815). ¶2

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the grade

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Probable: both records are index readings. The Lesja and Grytten families are tied together by his brother Peder's Lesja baptism and Grytten confirmation and his sister Kari's. Reading the 1831 confirmation scan for his stated birth date would confirm it. ¶3
