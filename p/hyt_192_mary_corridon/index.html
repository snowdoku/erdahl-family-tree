Life story

Mary, daughter of Philip Corridon of Kilmacaha (Co. Kerry), wife of John Collis and mother of William Collis of Lisedoge, according to Burke (Landed Gentry of Ireland, 1912, 'Collis of Tieraclea'). Added. ¶1
