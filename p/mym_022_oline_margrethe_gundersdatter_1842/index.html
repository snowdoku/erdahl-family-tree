Life story

Baptism: born 7 June 1842 and baptised at Ekne on 31 July 1842, legitimate daughter of 'Huusmand Gunder Sivertsen Søndre Kjølen' and wife Karen Helgesdatter (Ekne Døbte 1842, p. 207, no. 3). Godparents: the farmer Johan Olsen Moan and his wife Berethe M. Johnsdatter, the cottager Peder Helgesen of Føraasvald and his wife Ane Erichsdatter, the farmer Hans Erichsen Skaaraas, and Cicilie Erichsdatter of the same farm. Five years later Hans Erichsen Skaaraas was a witness at her father's second marriage, to Martha Erichsdatter Skaaraas. ¶1

Death (Probable): her mother died when she was five months old. 'Pigebarn Oline Margrethe Iversdatter', aged 3/4 year, of Kjølenval, died on 6 March 1843 and was buried at Ekne on 12 March 1843 (Ekne Begravede 1843, p. 421, no. 2). The patronymic is written Iversdatter, but the name, the age and the holding all fit her, and no other Oline Margrethe was baptised in Ekne in 1836-49. No 1842 Ekne baptism has a father named Iver, and no Oline Margrethe appears in the Alstadhaug baptism index for 1836-49. Rated Probable for the death only. The likeliest explanation is that the motherless infant was being nursed by an Iver's household at Kjølenvald, or that the clerk erred. ¶2
