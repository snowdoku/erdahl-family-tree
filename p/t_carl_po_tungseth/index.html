Life story

He lived to 95. Find a Grave gives his dates as 3 Sep 1883 to 23 Mar 1979 and his burial as Chelan Fraternal Cemetery in Chelan County, Washington, plot 'South Fraternal Row 67 BLK 65 L5A'. Like his brother Erling, who died at Bellevue, and like the Valder family at Ferndale, he ended his life in Washington state - part of the general movement of this Norwegian-American network from the Red River valley to the Pacific Northwest. ¶3

His full Norwegian name was Karl Petter Olai Tungsæt, recorded that way in both the 1885 census at Toldbodbakken and the 1891 census at 47 Toldbodsmujet in Kristiansund, born 1883 in Kristiansund. In America he went by Carl P. O. Tungseth. ¶1

He emigrated with his mother and the other children from Kristiansund on 2 May 1894 for Minnesota, aged ten; the emigrant clerk's calculated birth year of 1885 is two years out. ¶2

1895 Minnesota census: Carl Tungseth, 11, born in Norway, in his father Peter's household in Skree Township, Clay County (p. 6, read from the image). ¶5

1900 census: Carl Tungseth, son, born September 1883 in Norway, 16, single, immigrated 1894, at school, in his mother Caroline's household in Skree Township (ED 316, sheet 185A, line 3, read from the image). ¶6

1905 Minnesota census: Carl Tungseth, 21, born in Norway, farmer (ditto mark under his father's 'Farmer', inferred), at home in his father Peder's household in Skree Township, 11 years in Minnesota (Enumeration District 10, schedule sheet III, line 32, read from the image). ¶7

1910 census: Carl P. Tungseth, son, 26, single, born in Norway, immigrated 1894, speaks English, farmer on the home farm, working on his own account, in his father Peter P O Tungseth's household in Skree Township (ED 59, sheet 187A, line 36, read from the image). ¶8

His marriage, occupation and obituary have not been found; the Lake Chelan Mirror and Washington state death records for March 1979 are the next sources. ¶4
