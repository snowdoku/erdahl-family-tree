About the family

Her father Thomas Pattishull is added as Speculative (see his page). Only Lodge names him, and the 1569 visitation pedigree of Pateshall of the Ford in Pudleston (Weaver, p. 64) shows neither a Thomas of the right generation nor a daughter Jane. Her own grade is unchanged. Added. ¶3

Life story

Jane, daughter of Thomas Pattishull (Pattishall) of Herefordshire, Esq., wife of Roger Boyle of Hereford and mother of his four sons and his daughter Elizabeth (Lodge, Peerage of Ireland, 1789, vol. 1, p. 144). Added. ¶1

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the grade · 1 open question

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Probable, graded on her link to her son Michael Boyle. This rests on Lodge alone (a compiled source), whose wording 'married Jane... and had a daughter Elizabeth, and four sons' makes her the mother of all of them. Cotton does not name Roger's wife. ¶2a

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Test record: the Visitation of Herefordshire (1569 or 1634), s.v. Boyle and Pattishall; the will of Roger Boyle of Hereford, or of Thomas Pattishall (Hereford diocesan or PCC wills). Added. ¶2b
