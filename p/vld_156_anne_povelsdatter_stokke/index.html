Paul (Poul) Ellingsen Stokkec. 1726 – bef. 1779Confirmed · Stokke

About the family

Wife, then widow, of Elling Johansen of Stokke, Gjemnes, Kvernes parish. Living at Stokke as a widow in 1751, 1762 and 1765. ¶1

Parents found: Nordmøre skifteprotokoll 24640 (no. 7, 1731-36), fol. 988 (view 500, right page): 'Anno 1734 den 8de December' a registration was held 'paa gaarden Stocke' in Gimnes tinglag after 'afgangne Povel Olsen', between his surviving widow 'Synnøve Iversdatter' on the one side and on the other 'deres sammen avlede Døttre' (their daughters begotten together, reading of the last word slightly uncertain) and the daughters' children, namely: the late Gunild Povelsdatter's two children (a son Povel, 8, and a daughter Marite, 11, per the index card); 'Kirsti Povelsdatter 37 aar gl.'; and 'Anne Povelsdatter gift med Elling Johansen Bøge[?]' (the card reads the byname as 'Bogen'), for whom her husband Elling Johansen acted. The index card (77795 view 3789) adds the widow's statement that her late husband had before his death conveyed to his third daughter what little he owned, and that the unmarried daughter Kirsti had received 1 øre of land rent in the farm Stokke against her married sister Anne for 22 rdl. The typed card: 'Stokke, Gjemnes. 1734 8/12 Pouvel Olsen. Enke: Synnøve Iversdatter. Barn: 1 Afgangne Gunild Povelsdatters 2 barn... 2 datteren Kirsti Pouvelsdatter 37 år gl, 3 [datteren] Anna [Pouvelsdatter] g.m. Elling Johansen Bogen'. Continuity to the Anne of the tree: 17 years later, on 11 Nov 1751, the estate of 'Elling Johansen' at the same farm Stokke was registered with his widow 'Anne Povelsdatter' (24644 fol. 74a): the same couple, same farm, no other Elling Johansen or Anne Povelsdatter at Stokke in either probate or in the Stokke card series 1677-1744 (77795 views 3777-3793, all read). The 1734 entry also explains how Elling came to Stokke: through his wife's share of her father's farm. Anne's children carry her parents' names: eldest son Povel (b. about 1726) and a daughter Synnøve Ellingsdatter (1751 probate). The parents are graded on this record; her own grade (on her link to her son Paul) is unchanged. Her elder sister Kirsti was 37 in 1734, so Anne was probably born about 1698-1705. ¶6

Life story

Probate found: Nordmøre skifteprotokoll 24644 (1750-55), fol. 74a (view 83, right page), No. 100: 'Anno 1751 den 11te Novemb.' a registration was held 'paa gaarden Stocke' in Gimnes tinglag after the deceased 'Elling Johansen', whose widow was 'Anne Povelsdatter'; the children are listed as the son 'Povel Ellingsen' (age read as 25), 'Johan Ellingsen' (about 20), 'Arne Ellingsen', 'Ole Ellingsen', and the daughters 'Christie Ellingsdatter' and '[Synnøve] Ellingsdatter' (ages after the first two only partly legible). The typed register of the volume lists it as 'Elling Johansen Stoche i Gjemnes 74a'. ¶2

Later appearances (inferred to be her, same name, farm and widowhood): 'Enken Ane' in Paul Ellingsen's household at Stokke in the 1762 extra-tax list (47939 view 56); 'Enken Ane Paulsdatter Stokke' as godmother at Advent 1764 (16076 view 56, p. 107) and on the 20th Sunday after Trinity 1765 (view 59, p. 113), beside Paul each time. Her patronymic Povelsdatter explains her eldest son's name, Povel. Her burial (after 1765) is not yet found. ¶4

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Graded on her link to her son Paul (Poul) Ellingsen Stokke: Confirmed, because the 1751 probate names her as Elling's widow and Povel Ellingsen among 'their' children (the heirs are the couple's children; no earlier marriage of Elling is mentioned in the part read). Continuity, shown record by record with no rival: (1) the 1751 son Povel Ellingsen of Stokke is the only Povel/Paul Ellingsen at Stokke or in Kvernes in the eighteenth century (70 index hits, all nineteenth century); (2) in the 1762 extra-tax list for Stokke (source 47939, view 56) 'Paul Ellingsen og hustru' head a household whose 'Børn over 12 aar' column holds 'Arne Ellingsen', 'Ole Ellingsen' and '[?] Ellingsdatter', with 'Enken Ane' (the widow Ane) at the same line in the column for others living in the house: the same siblings and widowed mother as in 1751, eleven years later on the same farm; (3) the widow 'Ane Paulsdatter' of Stokke stood godmother beside Paul in 1764 and 1765 (16076 views 56 and 59); (4) Paul's own probates name 'Farbroderen Johan Ellingsen' (1763, 24647 p. 735) and 'Farbroderen Ole Ellingsen Stoche' (1779, 24650 fol. 365a) as his children's father's brothers, matching the 1751 sons Johan and Ole. Paul's son Johan/Elling naming also fits (son Elling b. 1767 named for this grandfather). ¶3

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Not the same woman as Anne Povelsdatter (Haram, married Jørgen Aamundsen in 1694); the duplicate warning from fleet.py check is a name match only. ¶5
