About the family

Brother of Oline G. Slind Eidem, and one of the three siblings who stayed in Norway. Her October 1955 obituary names him living in SELBU, NORWAY at that date: 'Gunder, Marit and Karen Slind of Selbu, Norway.' ¶3

Life story

Oline Slind's younger brother, born 2 October 1887 and baptised in Selbu on 30 October 1887, his father then entered as Gunder Olsen, gaardmand, of 'Slinde'. ¶1

The 1910 census records him, aged twenty-three, as 'udygtig til erhverv' - unfit for gainful employment - and he was still in the household at Slind nordre in 1920, by then his widowed mother's. He is the child of this family who could neither take the farm nor emigrate, and his presence is part of why his mother was still working a leased farm at seventy-three. ¶2

In the 1910 census, at twenty-three, he was recorded as 'udygtig til erhverv' - unfit for gainful work - and he was still at home in 1920. That he nonetheless held the farm for a quarter of a century, with his sister, says something about how the household absorbed him. ¶7

He took over the farm and died in 1947. Selbu i fortid og naatid vol. 7, p. 100: in the children's list, 'Gunder, f. 1887,... over bruket' - took over the holding - and in the holder list, '1921-1963 Gunder... Slind, f. 1887, d.... 1947,... bruket med... Marit... Slind, f. 1878.' He and his unmarried sister Marit held gnr 141 bnr 2, Slindan nordre (Nedpaa-Garden), from 1921, the year their widowed mother bought the freehold, until his death; the holder line runs on to 1963 under his sister or a successor. Rated Probable, from a published gaardshistorie rather than a register. ¶5

He was still alive in October 1955 on the evidence of his sister Oline's obituary, which names 'Gunder... Slind of Selbu, Norway' among the survivors - which, if the gaardshistorie's 1947 is right, is an error in the American obituary rather than in the book, or else refers to a nephew of the same name. Both readings are recorded; the Selbu burial register for 1947 decides. ¶6
