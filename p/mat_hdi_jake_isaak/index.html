Life story

Known as Jake Isaak Sr. Husband of Rosebud Hayter, Raymond Hayter's sister. His Find a Grave memorial gives his father as Peter Jacob Isaak (1873-1938) and lists eleven brothers and sisters, among them Abraham, Peter, David S., Henry S. and John W. Isaak and sisters married into the Schmidt, Schroeder, Friesen, Lehman and Wiebe families - a Russian-Mennonite family of Aberdeen, Idaho. ¶1

Gravestone: a double upright stone in Homestead Cemetery, Aberdeen, read from the Find a Grave photograph: 'ISAAK / JAKE SR / APR 20 1901 / NOV 16 198[9]' beside 'ROSEBUD H'; the reverse names their children Jake Jr., Ronald, Gloria and Phyllis. ¶2

Proved husband of Rosebud Hayter: the Social Security claims index entries of both their sons, Jacob Lyle (1927) and Ronald Rex (1930), name their parents as Jacob Isaak and Rosebud Hayter. ¶3

1950 census: Jacob Isaak, 48, born Russia, naturalized, a dragline operator and foreman with the canal company at Aberdeen, Idaho, head of the household that included his wife Rosebud and their four children. ¶4
