About the family

Widow of Torbjørn Larsen: named as his surviving widow (efterlevende Enke) 'Giertrud Ols datter' in his probate at Ytre Takle on 25 Aug 1785, with her son Lars Torbjørnsen as her laugværge (legal guardian). She was alive on that date. Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll A 10a, fol. 135a, no. 105. ¶1

Mother of Ole, Lars and Maritha Torbjørnsen/-datter (Confirmed): Torbjørn's 1785 probate calls the heirs 'deres sammen-avlede 2de Sønner og een Datter' (their jointly begotten two sons and one daughter), per audit. ¶2a

Parents not found. Checked and ruled out as her father: Ole Kolbeinsen of Ytre Takle (probate 23 Mar 1771, 8b fol. 656b-657a: widow Maritha Olsdatter and one daughter, Brithe Olsdatter, 5; Johannes Herløvsen of Ytre Takle was an appraiser); Ole Torsen of Midtre Takle (probate 28 Aug 1728, 3b fol. 318a: children Ole, Kari and Brithe by his first wife; his widow Ragnilde Larsdatter's children by her earlier husband were Lars, Henrik, Hans, Anders and Ana). The 'Giertrud Olsdatter' aged 12 in the 1776 probate of Barbra Herløfsdatter of Stølsbotten (A 9 p. 659) was born about 1764 and is not this woman, whose son Ole was born about 1753. ¶7

Life story

Bygdebok (Ytre Takle gnr. 64 bnr. 77, canvas 874): 'Torbjørn Larsson' (1728-1785), brukar 1756-1786, married 1756 'Gjertrud Olsdtr.', followed by '... Bø... 1741... 1815' (her origin and dates: probably from Bø, b. 1741, d. 1815, but the words between are not rebuilt). Children: [Ole] Torbjørnson b. 1758 (bnr. 75, m. 1784 Sigrid Johannesdtr. Takle), Lars Torbjørnson 1760, Marta Torbjørnsdtr. 1764; the 1785 division 'etter Torbjørn Larsson Takle mellom... Gjertrud Olsdtr.' and the children. No parents linked: her father's name is not in the rebuilt text. Note the book's 1758 for Ole against the tree's ABT 1753 (Ole is held by another draft, so not changed here). ¶10

Burial (per index): 'Giertrud Olsdr', Kone, of Yttre Takle, aged 74 (b. about 1741), buried 1815, interment '[03-23]' (the bracketed date was supplied by the transcriber). Gulen church book 1809-1825 (index pg00000000771954). Identity as Torbjørn Larsen's widow is Probable: name, patronymic and farm fit, and no other Giertrud Olsdatter of Takle is indexed. The scan was not read. ¶3

Godmother 1752: a 'Gjertrud M[id]tackle' (Midt-Takle) stood godmother at Brekke in Oct 1752 to the twins of Johannes of Midt-Takle, in the same entry as 'Torbiørn M. Tackle' (11443, canvas 30, right page top). This is probably she, and it would mean she was adult by 1752, which argues against the burial (about 1741 birth) noted in the age conflict above. Not proved, since she is not called Torbjørn's wife there. Her marriage and the baptism of her son Ole were not found in 11443 canvases 18-36 (Jun 1751 - Aug 1753, full width). ¶8

For her motherhood: Torbjørn's 1785 probate calls the heirs 'deres sammen-avlede 2de Sønner og een Datter' (their jointly begotten two sons and one daughter), which names her as the mother of Ole, Lars and Maritha. Against it: the burial age, if right, rules her out for Ole; then either the index age is wrong (74 for 84 would give b. about 1731, which fits), the 1815 burial is another Giertrud Olsdatter, or the probate's formula is loose and Ole was a son of an earlier wife. The probate is the stronger, primary record; the index age is unchecked. ¶5

1801 census lead (overnight sweep F): the 1801 census for Eivindvik (1411P), Takle Yst, household 01 (Ole Olssen, 45, skattebonde, and Brite Lassesdatter, 43, with children Lars 15, Ole 13, Ingerborg 11, Lasse 7), has as person 007 'Giertrud Olsdatter', 68 (born about 1733), 'Kaare kone', 'Enke 2. g.' (a widow after her second marriage, living on a retirement allowance) (Digitalarkivet pf01058391004268). She is the only Giertrud Olsdatter of her generation at Ytre Takle in 1801; the 1801 Giertrud Olsdatter born 1792 at Takle Yst is a child. A birth about 1733 would make her about 20 when her son Ole Torbjørnsen was born (about 1753), which removes the age conflict raised by the 1815 burial ('Giertrud Olsdr', Kone, 74). Not adopted as a fact: the census does not name Torbjørn, she is in the household of an Ole Olsen rather than of her son Ole Torbjørnsen, and 'Enke 2. g.' implies a second husband (before or after Torbjørn) not yet found. Also note that the 1815 burial calls its Giertrud 'Kone' (a married woman), not 'Enke', so it may be another woman. ¶9a

Burial scan read: Gulen ministerialbok 1809-1825 (11449), female burials 1815, p. 94 (canvas 80, left page), no. 15, interment 23 Mar 1815: 'Kone Giertrud Olsd. Yttre Takle, gammel 74 Aar'. The age is plainly 74 on an enlarged crop (the same 4 as in no. 2's '64'), not 84, so the index was right and the age conflict noted above is NOT resolved by a misreading: if this is Torbjørn Larsen's widow, the clerk's age is about 10 years too low, or she was not Ole Torbjørnsen's birth mother despite the probate's 'deres sammen-avlede' wording. No other Giertrud Olsdatter of Takle appears in the 1815 list. Grade unchanged (Probable, conflict open); death date now from the scan (buried 23 Mar 1815, so died before). ¶11

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the grade · 1 record that disagrees · 1 alternative set aside · 2 open questions

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Confidence: lowered from Confirmed to Probable, as Ole Torbjørnsen's mother, because of the age conflict above. Her existence as Torbjørn Larsen's widow in 1785 remains proved by the probate. ¶6a

Records that disagree 1

Conflict: if this burial is hers, she was born about 1741 and was only about 11 or 12 when her son Ole Torbjørnsen was born (about 1752-1753, from his age of 48 at the census of 1 Feb 1801; he was the eldest son and of age in 1785). That is impossible for a birth mother. ¶4

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Caution: Maritha was only 22 in 1785 and Ole was born about 1753, so Giertrud bore children over roughly ten or more years. That is plausible, but her birth year is unknown. ¶2b

Open questions and the next record to read 2

Would settle it: the scan of the 1815 burial (to check the age) or her confirmation or marriage entry. ¶6b

Next: her remarriage after 1785 in the Gulen books (11443 onward) and the household of Ole Olsen, Ytre Takle, b. about 1756. ¶9b
