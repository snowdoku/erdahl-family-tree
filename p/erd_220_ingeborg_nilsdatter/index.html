Life story

Marriage found: betrothal at Tjugum on the 2nd day of Easter, 11 Apr 1757 (Leikanger 11566 c8, p. 8): 'Anfind Olsøn Bahle & Ingeborg Røe'. So before her marriage she lived at Røe; the curator of her daughter Christie in 1781, Ole Nielsen R[ø]e, was probably her brother (unproved), and a Knud Nielsen Røe was betrothed at Leikanger in June 1758 (11566 c20). ¶6a

Death: before 18 Oct 1781 (date of the registration); the bygdebok's 1781 agrees. ¶5

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Graded Probable on her link to her daughter Christi Anfindsdatter (see her page). Balestrand 1: Ættesoge (1985), canvas 132: Arnfinn Olson Bale 'vart [i] 1757 gift [med] Ingeborg Nilsdtr. Arnfinn døydde [i] 1800, kona var [død?] 1781. Dei hadde [...] 2 born: Kristi som vart gift [med] Gjest Gjestson Ese, [og] Nils' (b. 1766). Christi (b. c.1762) falls inside the marriage. Her death year is read from 'kona var [...] 1781' with the verb not recovered. ¶1

Upgrade: graded on her link to her daughter Christi Anfindsdatter (see her page), Probable -> Confirmed. Her probate was found by searching the Ytre Sogn probate index by farm (domicile=Tjugum*, 1776-1806): 'Ingeborg Nilsdtr, Kalvetreet u. Tjugum, 1776-1782, 9b p. 1479'. The protocol page (Ytre Sogn 9b p. 1479, 24167 view 739): 'Anno 1781 den 18de October' registration and valuation on the cottar's place ('Huusmands Pladset') Kalvetræet under the farm Tjugum, after the deceased wife ('Kone') Ingeborg Nielsdatter, between her surviving widower ('efterlevende Enkemand') Anfind Olsen on the one side and on the other their 'udi Egteskab tilsammen avlede 2 Børn, 1 Søn og 1 Datter': 'Sønnen Niels Anfindsen 15 aar', guardian Niels Ellingsen Tjugum, and 'Datteren Christie Anfindsdatter 19 aar gammel', for whom a curator was named (p. 1480 top: 'Ole Nielsen R[ø]e', reading not secure). This names the relationship directly and matches the bygdebok's two children, Kristi and Nils (b. 1766). Continuity (how the 1781 girl is followed to the tree's Christi): (1) Oct 1780, Tjugum confirmation list: 'Christi Anfindsdr: Troet 18 aar' (11567 c234). The farm word has a clear 'r' after the T ('Troet'), i.e. Trøet/Træet, the clerk's short form of Kalvetræet; earlier passes read it 'Tvet'. (2) 18 Oct 1781, this probate: Christie Anfindsdatter, 19, at Kalvetræet under Tjugum, one year later, same place, age advancing by one. (3) 25 Sep / 11 Oct 1790: 'Pigen Christie Anfinds Datter Tr[ø]det' marries the soldier Giest Giestsen Esse (11567 c202-203), 9 years later, same place; the second letter of the farm word is formed like the 'r' of 'Trolovet' in the same entry, so it is Trødet = Treet, not Tvedt (reading, now corrected). One of the sureties is 'Niels... Tjugum', and Kalvetræet lay under Tjugum (the 1781 guardian was Niels Ellingsen Tjugum, and her brother was Niels Anfindsen). (4) Census ages of Gjest's wife: 38 in 1801 and 52 in 1815, b. 1762/63, matching 19 in Oct 1781. Gaps: 1 and 9 years, all at the same place. ¶4a

Alternatives considered and set aside 2

No rival: the other Christi Anfindsdatters of the district were the Feten girl (b. c.1762, confirmed 1777 at Feten, married Marcus Marcussen 1797), Anfind Samsonsen Grinde's Christi (b. c.1773) and Anfind Olsen Hov's Christi (b. c.1775); the Tveiten couple (Anfind Monsen and Kari Anfindsdatter) had no Christi (probates 1781 and 1813). The Balestrand bygdebok (1985, c132) independently makes Kristi, daughter of Arnfinn Olson and Ingeborg Nilsdtr., the wife of Gjest Gjestson Ese, and its Ese chapter (c102, phrase-checked) writes that Gjest Gjestson 'vart [i] 1790 gift med Kristi Arnfinnsdtr. [...] (Kalvetre)', naming the same place. ¶4b

No rival: no other Ingeborg Nielsdatter married to an Anfind Olsen is known in Tjugum skipreide. Røe identified: Røe is a farm in Leikanger parish, Tjugum skipreide (1801 census 'Leganger: Røe'); the Ytre Sogn probate index spells it 'Rød' (Lars Olsen and Magdeli Hansdtr of the 1801 Røe household appear there as 'Rød' 1809-13), which is why searches for 'Røe' found nothing. The probate after Niels Olsen Røe, 17 Sep 1745 (Ytre Sogn 6b fol. 480a-481a, No. 5, 24162 views 128-129) names among the children he had with his widow Anne Jørgensdatter '3die Datter Ingebor Nielsdatter', 12 (b. about 1733), with her brothers Knud Nielsen (15) and Ole Nielsen. Continuity: (1) 1745, Røe: Ingebor Nielsdatter 12, brothers Knud and Ole Nielsen; (2) 11 Apr 1757, Tjugum: betrothal of 'Anfind Olsøn Bahle & Ingeborg Røe' (11566 c8), 12 years later, same farm, no patronymic; Knud Nielsen Røe betrothed in 1758 (11566 c20); (3) 18 Oct 1781, Kalvetræet: probate after Ingeborg Nielsdatter, wife of Anfind Olsen, with 'Ole Nielsen Røe' as curator of her daughter Christie (24167 view 740). So the 1745 sister pair Ingebor + Ole Nielsen of Røe recurs in 1781 as Ingeborg Nielsdatter and Ole Nielsen Røe, curator for her child (curators were usually the nearest kin), and the 1757 'Ingeborg Røe' links the farm to the marriage. (This 1745-1757-1781 chain alone would be Probable; the 1769 probate of her mother, above, is what confirms it.) Her age fits (b. ~1733; daughter Christie b. ~1762, son Niels b. ~1766, named after her father). Further records: Ole Nielsen Røe's probate, 30 May 1810 (Ytre Sogn 14 fol. 119a, 24174 view 121; the heirs there are his own widow and children, read only at heading level), the probate after Anne Jørgensdatter (not yet found; search domicile 'Rød' 1745-1790), or Knud Nielsen's probate, any of which might name 'Søsteren Ingeborg Nielsdatter' or her children Niels and Christi Anfindsbørn. Her own grade (link to Christi Anfindsdatter) is unchanged. ¶7b
