Life story

Probate (index card): her estate was held at Tveit in Lille Tovdal on 9 Jun 1700 (Østre Råbyggelag skifteprotokoll nr. 1, fol. 183b), with her husband Nære Joensøn living. Children: Joen; Gunsteen (later Skjeggedal); Olle (later Austenaa i Tovdal); Asbør (m. Ole Tellefsen Harstveit); Anna; and Ingebør (later m. Salve Svendsen Askland). The estate held 2 huder of odel land in Tveit and movables. Her patronymic points to a father Gunsteen; her son Gunsteen was named for him. Probable mother of Ole Næresen (see his notes). ¶1

Protocol check: the 1758 estate of her son Gunsten Næresen (Skp. 12b fol. 374b) names his brothers Joen Nærresøn Tved and 'Ole Nærresøn Østenaae' and the sister Ingeborg Nærresdatter. This agrees with her 1700 estate card ('Olle, senere Austenaa i Tovdal'). Her link to Ole Næresen of Oland (d. by 1761) remains Probable; see his notes. ¶2

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
