1 on the grade

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Speculative father of Iver Jonsen Søvig. The 1701 manntall lists 'Joen Knuds.' b. 1621 as an opsidder at Søeuig, entered directly before 'Ifuer Jons.' b. 1665. No relationship is stated. The Gjemnes bygdebok (bind 10, Søvik, canvas 351) has 'Brukar 3: Jon, brukar frå ca. 1663 til ca. 1683. Jon f. ca. 1619. Gift [med enkja] etter Tørris [Halvorson]' (brukar 1, about 1620-1661; his widow was brukar 2 from 1661), then Johan Knutson (about 1686-95), then Iver Jonson from about 1695. The rebuilt text does not show the bygdebok calling Jon Iver's father. A patronymic plus the same farm is all the evidence, so this stays Speculative. If Jon married Tørris Halvorson's widow about 1662, she may be Iver's mother, but that is not shown. ¶1
