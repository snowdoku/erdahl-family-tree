Life story

Aaland (Innvik 1974 ed., Hilde bruk nr. 3, p. 225) gives Ola Olson Hilde (d. 1735) the wife 'Gunnh. Andersd. Skåden, d. f. 1750' (from Skåden, Innvik). Speculative as mother of Jartrud Olsdatter (see her page): she is the only wife Aaland gives Ole, but Aaland does not say when they married or which children were hers, and the 1735 probate extract does not name the widow legibly. Would settle: Gunnhild's own probate (before 1750, Nordfjord A 8/A 9) naming her children. ¶1

How sure are we?

Speculative. A plausible candidate only. Treat this link as a question, not a fact.
