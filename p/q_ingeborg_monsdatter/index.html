About the family

Wife of Anders Olsen Esse and mother of Ole Andersen 'Flaten'. Ole's 1895 Faribault Co. death register names his parents as 'Andrew & Ingeborg Olson'. Ole named his first two daughters Ingeborg (1846, 1850), in line with naming a first daughter after the father's mother. ¶1

Her parents are speculative (see Mons Eriksen). ¶4

Life story

1815 census: 'Ingeborg Monsd.', 26, a servant in Knut Hansen's household at Esse. Anders Olsen, 27, was a servant and day laborer on his brother Povel's part of Esse. Their marriage (about 1819) falls in a gap in the indexed Leikanger marriages (1815-1820) and was not found. ¶3

Burial register, Leikanger 1846 (no. 48): 'Ingeborg Monsdatter, husmannskone', 56, of Esse, died 29 Apr, buried 1 Jun 1846. Six days earlier (no. 46) 'Mons Andersen, husmannssøn', 25, of Esse was buried, probably her son (b. ~1821, named after her father Mons). This fits Ole's marriage on 10 Jun 1846, right after these deaths. ¶2

Burial 1846 (scan read): no. 48, 'Ingeborg Monsdatter, Husmandskone' (a cottager's wife), 56, of Esse, died 29 April, buried 1 June 1846; her husband is not named. No. 46 on the same page is 'Mons Andersen, Huusmandssøn' (a cottager's son), 25, of Esse, died 23 May, buried 27 May 1846. Her husband is unknown; he may be a different cottager named Anders at Esse, the father of this Mons. It is also possible, but unproved, that the burial clerk wrote 'Monsdatter' for Ingebor Gjestsdatter, wife of the cottager Anders Olsen of Esse: her age (born about 1790-91) and status fit, and no Ingeborg born 1786-1798 appears in the Leikanger or Balestrand index after 1846. Mons Andersen's baptism (about 1820-21, Leikanger register 11569, unindexed) would settle it. ¶6

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 correction

Corrections 1

Correction: this woman is not the mother of Ole Andersen. Ole's baptism (23 July 1820) and his brother Johannes's (1832) name the mother Ingeborg Gjestsdatter of Esse, and Anders Olsen Esse was a bachelor when he married Ingebor Gjestsdatter on 16 January 1826, so he cannot have married Ingeborg Monsdatter about 1819. The mother link and the marriage to Anders Olsen Esse should be removed unless she is shown to be the same woman as Ingebor Gjestsdatter. ¶5
