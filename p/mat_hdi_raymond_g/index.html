Life story

Infant son of Glenn Gordon Hayter and Mary Marguerite Hoffer, and Raymond Hayter's nephew and namesake. He lived nineteen days. His grave marker in Aberdeen Cemetery reads 'Our Darling Son Raymond G. Hayter. Feb 19, 1930 - Mar 10, 1930' (Find a Grave transcription). His parents are named by the memorial's family links, not by the stone; rated Probable. ¶1

Death certificate (Idaho death records index): Raymond Jordon Hayter, born 19 February 1930, died 10 March 1930 at Aberdeen, Bingham County; parents given as Glen Gordon and Marie Margaret [Hayter]. The middle name is as the indexer read it; the gravestone gives only the initial G., so it may be Gordon, after his father. ¶2
