About the family

NEW ANCESTOR. Farmer at Eppeland in Åmli, father of Niels Olsen Eppeland (estate 1755) and of Eli Olsdatter, who married Aslak Tellefsen of Nedre Eppeland. ¶1

His father was an Orm — an unusual old Norse name that recurs locally (a Berthe Ormsdatter was at Øvre Ramse in the 1780s). He is the earliest Eppeland ancestor so far identified, born about 1670. ¶3

Brother: the card for the oppgitt skifte of 11 Dec 1747 (Skp. 12a fol. 6, card 39942/2379) names 'Mannens bror: Aslak Ormsen' and 'Mannens brorsønn: Gierul Aslaksen Biornes'. That gives a sibling, not the parents. No Eppeland estate of an Orm appears in the cards 2355-2386 (1698-1755). ¶8

Father, Probable: Orm Olsen (Orm Olsøn 34 in the 1664-65 bailiff's list, DA 35567 view 25), aged 36, user of half a hud at Øvre Eppeland in the 1666 vicar's census (DA 35510 view 30, p. 38, no. 17), the only Orm on the farm. See Orm Olsen. This raises the parent link only; his own grade on his children is unchanged. ¶9

Life story

He and his wife Anna Svenningsdatter both had their estates relinquished to the court ("oppgitt skifte") on 11 December 1747 — the same day, which usually means they died close together. The date is quoted on two separate Østre Råbyggelag cards, one for his son Niels's 1755 estate and one for his daughter Eli's husband's 1767 estate, both reading "Ole Ormsen Eppeland / Anna Svenningsdtr., oppg. sk. 11/12-1747". ¶2

Death (bygdebok, Probable): Åmli ætt og heim V, Eppeland (canvases 211-212): 'Ole Orms. Eppeland... d. 1755, g.m. Anne [?] Rostveit... d. 1758'; the author says the church book gives their ages at death as 96, which he doubts (Ole is not in the 1666 census, so b. after 1666). The 1747 skifte (card 2387, 'oppg. sk. 11/12-1747') was therefore an oppgjevingsskifte (handover while living): the book says that at the 1747 skifte the son Orm received the farm 'men foreldra gav seg' (the parents retired). Children per the book: Orm b. 1695 (m. 1715 Anne [?] Tveit), Niels b. (1700) (m. 1726 Inger), a son m. 1742 Ingeborg [?] Eppeland, a daughter b. 1704 (m. 1728 Aslak [?] Eppeland/Ekra), a child b. 1712. Mother added: Kirsti (Probable, bygdebok). Test record for the death date: Åmli burials 1755 (DA 9921, free). ¶10

Corrections 1

Correction: the 11 Dec 1747 Eppeland record (Østre Råbyggelag Skifteprotokoll nr. 12a fol. 6, card 2377) is an oppgitt skifte in which 'Ole Ormssen og hustru Anna Svenningsdaatter' surrendered their estate while living ('oppgir boet'); he was alive on that date, so his death date is changed to 'after 11 Dec 1747'. The card back (2378/2379) names 'Mannens bror: Aslak Ormsen' and 'Mannens brorsønn: Gierul Aslaksen Biørnes'. This confirms his patronymic and gives a brother, Aslak Ormsen, and a nephew, Gierul Aslaksen of Bjørnes; it does not name his parents. ¶5

Searches that found nothing 2

Search: Eppeland probate cards 2363-2375 (1721-1742) read. None is an estate of an Orm or names Ole Ormsen's parents. Card 2375 (1742) calls him 'Ole Ormssøn øvre Eppeland', lagrettesmann, father of the widower Niels Olsøn and of Orm Olsøn. Cards before 2363 (Eppeland, 1697-1720) not yet read. ¶6

Search: the Eppeland i Åmli probate cards from the block divider (39942/2349) to 2363 cover 1707-1721 (Ingebor Salvesdatter 1707, Osmund Sveningsøn Gieveland 1709, Aslak Halvorsøn 1712, Knud Evindsøn 1721). None is an estate of an Orm or names an Ormsen heir, so his father Orm did not die at Eppeland with a probate after 1697. Orm probably died before 1697 (earlier Råbyggelag probates are lost) or elsewhere. ¶7
