Life story

Baptism (Probable): Dom. 25 p. Trinit. 1761 (c. 8 Nov 1761) in Gievedal, Tøri, daughter of Ole Olsøn and Signe B[ørj]esdatter(?) of Omland. Godparents included Jon Omland and a Torgersdatter of Omland (Åmli 1756-1775 p.49 #54). Her age (40 in Feb 1801) and her farm match. ¶3

DEATH NARROWED (10 Oct 2026). The Østre Råbyggelag probate card for her first husband's estate records her, as "Torie Olsdtr. (g.2.g.m. Tallak Eriksen)", with the annotation "oppg. sk. 1817" — her estate was surrendered to the court in 1817. She therefore died in or shortly before 1817, aged about 56. The same note covers her second husband Tallak Eriksen. ¶4

Married (1) Niels Aadnesen Omland 27 Jun 1784 at Gjøvdal church, as 'Turi Olsdatter Omland'. He died in Apr 1789. Married (2) Tallak Erichsen about 1790-1791 (their daughter Targier was b. ~1792). ¶2

The card confirms both her marriages in a single line and names her two children by Niels Aadnesen: Aadne, four years old in 1789 and himself dead by 1811, and Anne, an infant, "g.m. Ole Herjusen Gangsei". ¶5

Baptism re-read 13 Oct 2026: mother 'Signe Bör[i]usdatter' (Børjusdatter); godparents Neern(?) Haavlestad(?), Jon Omland, Børju Jørundland, Neern(?)'s wife and Eldri Torgersdatter Omland (Åmli 1756-1775 p. 49 no. 54). ¶6

Parent test, negative. In the 1789 probate of her first husband (Skifteprotokoll nr. 16b fol. 257a, read on the scan), her guardian (lauverge) is Gunsten Nielsen Omland, and no relationship is stated. No brother or parent of hers is named. The 1817 oppgitt skifte by Tallak Eriksen and Tøri Olsdatter (Skp. 22 fol. 1a, DA 24372 view 2; cards 10143/10145) is a hand-over by a living couple to their children (Niels Tallaksen 23, Ole 19, Targier Tallaksdatter, wife of Svenning Svenningsen Gjeveland, and the children of the late Aadne Nielsen), so it does not name her parents either. ¶8a

1801 census, Ohland, Åmli: wife, aged 40, in her 2nd marriage to Tallak Erichsen (46), farmer. Children by her first marriage: Aadne Nielsen (16) and Anne Nielsdatter (12). Children by Tallak: Targier (9), Niels (6) and Ole (2). ¶1

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: Confirmed as the mother of Anne Nielsdatter (see her page). The probate card for her first husband Niels Aadnesen of Oland (Østre Råbyggelag skiftekort 10107, Skifteprotokoll 16a fol. 257, 1789), already read by an earlier worker, names the widow 'Torie Olsdtr. (g.2.g.m. Tallak Eriksen)' and the children Aadne, 4, and Anne, an infant, 'g.m. Ole Herjusen Gangsei', which is the marriage of the Anne in the tree. The 1801 census of Ohland lists Anne Nielsdatter, 12, as a child of Tøri Olsdatter's first marriage. The same card was used to raise Anne's parentage to Confirmed on 10 Oct 2026; this brings the mother's grade into line. Her own baptism (Nov 1761, daughter of Ole Olsen and Signe Børjusdatter of Omland) stays a Probable identification. ¶7

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Lead only: Oland card 10115 (Skp. 19b fol. 414, 1805) is the estate of an Astrid Olsdatter of Oland, married (1) to an Even and (2) to Rolf Olsen; her children were Ole Evensen Oland, Vraal 23 and Ulvild 25.
