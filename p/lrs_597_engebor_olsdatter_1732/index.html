About the family

Mother of Ole Hansen of Langager (see his page), Probable: same household evidence as for Hans Siursen, and Ole named his daughter Ingeborg Malene (1801 census), Ingeborg being the standard form of Engebor. ¶2

Life story

In the 1758 soul register of Skudenes parish she is listed second in the householders column at Langager, aged 26 (born about 1732, inferred; the age figure reads '26' on the scan), after Hans Siursen, 34, so evidently his wife; children Ole Hansen 3 and Marte Hansdatter 5. Read from the scan (p. 17). ¶1

Bygdebok: Bygdebok for Karmøy: Skudenes og Skudeneshavn (1982), Langåker bnr. 7, canvases 129-130: Ingeborg, ca. 1732, daughter of user no. 5 Ola Anderssen (b. ca. 1697) and Malene Isaksdtr. (b. ca. 1688), 'g.m. Hans Sjursen, [neste] brukar'; no. 6: 'Hans Sjursen, ca. 1724, d. ca. 1785. G.m. Ingeborg Olsdtr., ca. 1732, d. ca. 1765, dotter til førre brukar. Ho var ikkje mor til borna hans? Born: a. Marta, ca. 1753; b. Ola, ca. 1755, [neste] brukar; c. Torbjørn, ca. 1764.' The author's question mark and 'd. ca. 1765' look like a conflation with the 1765 probate after another Ingebor Olsdatter of Langager (: heirs Mallene and Anne Rasmusdatter). The 1758 soul register (Engebor 26 with Ole 3 and Marte 5 in Hans Siursen's household) does not support the doubt, but it is noted here and her own grade stays Probable. Parents added: Ola Anderssen and Malene Isaksdatter (both Probable). The 1762 ekstraskatt (49646 view 601) shows her alive in 1762 as 'Ingebor Olsd.', wife of Hans Sieursen at Langager, with Ole Andersen and Malene Isachsd. in the household, which also argues against the bygdebok's death year ca. 1765 being hers. ¶5

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed, graded on her link to Ole Hansen. The probate after Hans Siursen, Langager, 23 Feb 1785 (card index 39936 view 866; protocol D 7b, source 23892, fol. 385a) names the widow 'Ingborg Olsdatter' and the children Ole (of age), Torbjørn 21 and Marthe 31, with no split by marriage, so she was the mother of all three. Continuity as for Hans Siursen: 1758 Langager household (Engebor Olsdatter 26 with Ole 3, Marte 5), 1785 probate on Langager, Ole Hansen farmer on Langager 1788-1801; no rival Ole. ¶6

Audit 9 (2 Nov 2026): Confirmed kept, graded on her link to her son Ole Hansen (see his page). The raise rested on the card (39936 view 866), which lists the widow and then 'Børn' with no split by marriage. Under audit 8 rule 2 that is not enough on its own, so the auditor read the opening of the protocol entry: Ryfylke D 7b (23892) fol. 385a, view 98, right page, left column, 'Anno 1785 den 23 Februarii' (binarised scan; /b98.jpg, crops in and hs_line2.jpg). After 'den afdøde... Hans Siursen' the entry divides between 'hans efterlevende Enke qvinde Ingeborg Ols datter' and the children 'som hun og [den Sal. Mand?] sam[m]e[n] afavlet' = 3 Børn, 2 Sønner og 1 Datter: the eldest son Ole Hansen, of age; the younger son Torbiørn Hansen, 21; and the daughter Marthe Hansdatter, 31. The words 'hun og' and 'afavlet' are legible; the word between them is not. The reading is good enough to name her as the mother of the three children. Continuity is as stated in the upgrade (1758 household, 1785, 1788 and 1801 on Langager), with no rival Ole Hansen. ¶9

Corrections 2

Correction: the 'Skiftebrev efter Ingeborg Olsdatter Langagen' of 12 Mar 1765 (pantebok p. 132, noted as possibly hers) is NOT her probate. The Ryfylke probate designation register 1757-1766 (source 23888, view 95, fol. 89, Stangeland skipreide 1765) lists on 12 [Mar] 1765 at Langager the deceased 'Ingebor Olsdatter' with heirs Mallene Rasmusdatter 30 and Anne [Rasmusdatter] 22, guardian Wellum Hacke of Stangeland. These are a Rasmus's daughters, not Hans Siursen's children Marte (about 12) and Ole (about 10), so that woman was another Ingeborg Olsdatter of Langager. No probate for this Engebor has been found; the D 10 designation register 1740-1754 (source 23887) has no estate at Langager naming her or Hans Siursen as heirs (only Anne Jacobsdatter, 18 Mar 1752, heir Marthe Osmundsdatter, guardian Morten Langager). ¶4

Correction: the bygdebok's 'd. ca. 1765' and its query 'Ho var ikkje mor til borna hans?' are wrong. They rest on the 12 Mar 1765 Langager estate of a different Ingebor Olsdatter (heirs Mallene and Anne Rasmusdatter). She was alive as Hans Siursen's widow on 23 Feb 1785, and the probate makes her the mother of his children. This resolves the noted contradiction. Her probate (after 1785) is not in the 1774-88 card index; look in Karmsund sorenskriveri A1-A3 (sources 23911-23913), Langager, Stangeland skipreide. ¶7

Searches that found nothing 1

Negative: no probate after her in the Karmsund og Hesby protocols 1789-1795. The 1809 back register of D 8a/8b (source 23894, views 382-383; Stangeland skipreide by year) has at Langager only 1791 Ole Knudsen (fol. 280), 1792 Iver Hansen (fol. 440) and Anna Davidsdatter (fol. 441). Karmsund A1 1793-1800 (front register E/I) and A2 1798-1803 (back register) also have none. She may have remarried or died after 1803. ¶8

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Lead, unproven: the Ryfylke pantebok A3 (source 23013, p. 132) records, among skiftebrev tinglyst 10 June 1766, no. 11, 'Skiftebrev efter Ingeborg Olsdatter Langagen', her land (jordegods) in Midvig, valued 13 rd 2 ort 20 s, 'udlagt' 12 March 1765. If this is Engebor Olsdatter, wife of Hans Siursen at Langager (aged 26 in 1758), she died about 1764-65 and the probate would name Hans Siursen and their children (Marte, Ole). The probate itself was not found in the 1764 Stangeland designations (views 88-89 of source 23888); next to read: the 1765 Karmsund/Stangeland block of the same register (about views 90-96) and Karmsund skifteprotokoll for 1764-66. ¶3
