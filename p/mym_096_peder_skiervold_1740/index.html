About the family

Children indexed to a Peder of Skiervold (no mothers indexed): Anders, bapt. 24 Jan 1768; Golla, 28 Jul 1771. Gullov (b. about 1775) is not in the index. ¶3

Father of Gullov Pedersdatter (see her page), still Probable but much stronger: the skifte proves a Gullov Pedersdatter, daughter of Peder Jonsen of Skjervold, born about mid-1771 and alive in 1773; no other Gullov Pedersdatter appears at Skjervold. In 1801 Peder Jonsen's widow Marit Olsdatter, 67, is the wife (her 2nd marriage) of the Skjervold farmer Peter Larsen in household 01, and Gullov Pedersdatter and her husband Peder Pedersen are lodgers (inderster) in household 02 on the same farm. The remaining gap: Gullov's 1801 age (26, so b. about 1775) is four years off a 1771 birth; no record read names her father directly. ¶9

Life story

Negative. The probate after the dragoon Joen Pedersen Hegge, 27 Oct 1752 (24753 fol. 465a; register fol. 464), names his widow Mali Hansdatter and three young sons, Hans, Peder and Joen Jonsen, all minors. That Peder Jonsen is far too young to be Peder Jonsen of Skjervold (b. by c.1715). No probate in the registers 1742-1802 names Peder Jonsen of Skjervold's parents. ¶12

'[Per] Jonsen Skervauran' was a surety at the betrothal of the widower Peder Erichsen Baar and Mali Olsdatter Vold, 17 Mar 1753 (16370 canvas 235 left). So he was at Skjervold by 1753. This adds a residence only; parents still open. ¶19

Burial found. Stjørdal ministerialbok 1755-1780 (16371), p. 212, burials at Værnes ('Wernæss'), 15 Dec 1771: 'Peder Jonsen', residence Skiervold, aged 67 (index pg00000007158743; the index has the patronymic). This is the man whose estate was registered at Schiervold on 15 Jan 1772, a month later (same name with patronymic, same farm; no other Peder Jonsen at Skjervold), so death is set BEF 15 DEC 1771. Contradiction on his birth year: the burial age (67) gives about 1704, the 1765 dragoon roll (51) about 1714. His first marriage by about 1739-40 (son Jon 25 in 1765) fits either. The same 1765 roll also gives Peder Arentsen of Hegge an age (33) far below his burial age (60), so the roll may understate the ages of older men who had served; the birth estimate is left at ABT 1714 until a baptism settles it, and his own baptism should be looked for in 1703-15 (16368 starts in 1714; earlier Stjørdal books are lost). Graded link unchanged (Probable, on his link to Gullov Pedersdatter). ¶17

Land records (panteregister sweep): the Stjørdal tinglag panteregister 1762-1800 (source 22318, image 216, 'N:o 70 Schiervold', 1 øre 12 mrk skyld) lists under deeds (Lit. B, pantebok series B, 1762-77): 'Proprietær Niemanns Auctionsskiøde til Peder Joensen', fol. 18 (about 1762-63, pantebok B-1, source 19753); 'Peder Joensens Skiøde til S[ønnen?] Joen Pedersøn', fol. 177 (B-1, about 1767-68); 'Peder Joensen og Joen Pedersens Skiøde til Lieut. Jacob Schultz', fol. 240; and 'Lieut. Schultzes Skiøde til Peder Joensen', fol. 245 (B-2, about 1769-70). So Peder Jonsen bought Skjervold at the Niemann auction about 1762 and owned it, with his son Jon Pedersen (b. about 1741) as co-owner by about 1768. The deed of fol. 177 may name his other children (Gullov's link) or say 'my son'; read it at 19753 about view 178. No skifte after Peder Jonsen is entered on the farm page, so the 1772 registration did not convey land. ¶13

1762 extra-tax list (48401 image 204, Skatval no. 15 Schiervold): 'Peder Jons[en] og Hu[stru]', children over 12 Jonn Pedersen 21, Mali, Kirsten; farmhand Halvor Jons[en] 15. The neighbouring Schiervold household had a maid Marit Olsdatter (probably his later second wife). ¶16

First marriage: the typed register of skifteprotokoll 24755 has a probate after 'Gollov Andersdatter, Peder Jonsens hustru', Skjervauran, Skatval, about 1765 (fol. 511b, not read). She was presumably the mother of Jon (b. about 1741), Malli (b. about 1746) and Kirstie (b. about 1749), and the 1771 daughter was given her name. Inferred from the register and the 1772 skifte; the 1765 skifte itself was not read. ¶6

Probate after his first wife, 7 Oct 1765 at Schiervold (Skjervold), Stjørdalen: 'Goller Anders Datter', widower Peder Jonsen; children '1. Jon Pedersen 25 aar, 2. Mallie Peders Datter 20 aar, 3. Kierstie Peders Datter 16 aar' (Stjør- og Verdal skifteprotokoll 5, 24755 fol. 511b, scan read). His first marriage was therefore by about 1739-40, so he was born by about 1715 at the latest. The same three children reappear as first-marriage heirs in his own 1772 probate (fol. 428b). ¶11

In the 1765 manntall- og legdsrulle of the Nordre Stjørdal dragoon company (source 51337, image 47, quarter no. 23 'Schier Wold', Skatval), the holders are 'Peder Joensen', 51, 'tient' (has served), and Ole Pedersen, 41, landvern. The young men include 'Joen Pedersen G.m.s.' (gaardmandssøn, farmer's son), 22, height 67 1/2, dragoon no. 46. Joen fits his son Jon Pedersen (25 in the 1765 probate after Goller Andersdatter). The age 51 puts Peder Jonsen's birth at about 1714 (the burial-free estimate was 'before 1721'), so his own baptism should be looked for in Stjørdal 16368 (1714-38) about 1713-15, Skatval entries. The birth estimate is changed to ABT 1714 on this basis. ¶14

Land records: the deed of pantebok B-1 fol. 177 (no. 2, read at Stjørdalen sommerting 1767) is Peder Jonsen Schierrold's skjøte, dated 6 Feb 1767, selling half of 'min... tilhørende Gaard Schierrold' (Skjervold, in Stjørdal prestegjeld) 'for den Summa 150 rdr til min Søn John Pedersen'; signed 'Peder Jonsen Schierrold'. It confirms Jon Pedersen as his son (already shown by the 1765 and 1772 probates) but names no other children, so it does not touch Gullov's link. Graded link unchanged (Probable, on his link to Gullov Pedersdatter). ¶15

The two youngest heirs match the index baptisms at Skatval of Anders, son of Peder of Skiervold, 24 Jan 1768, and Golla, daughter of Peder of Skiervold, 28 Jul 1771 (Golla is half a year old in Jan 1772). So the index's 'Peder of Skiervold' is Peder Jonsen, and the mother of Anders and Golla is his second wife Marith Olsdatter (see her page). ¶5

Peder of Skiervold (Skjervold), Skatval, Stjørdal: father of Golla, baptised 28 Jul 1771 (index; no mother indexed). His own patronymic is not yet known; birth year estimated. ¶1

Birth estimate: his son Jon was of age in 1772 and is the Joen Pedersen, 60, farmer at Schiervold in 1801 (b. about 1741), so Peder was born before about 1721. The earlier 'ABT 1740' was a guess and is replaced. ¶7

A later note on the skifte (påtegning, skifteprotokoll 7 fol. 514b, 1773) names 'Gullau Pedersdatter, myndling' and 'Anders Pedersen, myndling' of Skjervauran (typed register), so the daughter Gullau was alive in 1773. ¶8

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Probable father of Gullov Pedersdatter (see her page), married 1798 as 'Pige Gullov Petersdatter Skiervolde': patronymic Peder plus the farm. Golla (1771) is probably her elder sister (Golla/Gullov are forms of one name, so the 1771 child may have died and the name been reused - unproved). Gullov's own baptism (about 1773-77) is not in the index. ¶2

The case for this identification 1

Identity: 'Peder of Skiervold' is Peder Jonsen, holder (opsidder) of Schiervold (Skjervold, indexed by the archive as Skjervauran) in Skatval. The registration and valuation (Registering og Vurdering) of his estate began on 15 Jan 1772 'paa Gaarden og Opsidder Gaarden Schiervold i Skatvolds Sogn' after 'den ved Døden afgangne Mand Peder Jonsen' (the man Peder Jonsen, deceased), between his surviving widow Marith Olsdatter and the heirs: 1. the son Jon Pedersen, of age (myndig); 2. the daughter Malli Pedersdatter, 26; 3. the daughter Kirstie Pedersdatter, 23, 'af 1ste Ægteskab' (these three by his first marriage); 4. Anders Pedersen, 3; 5. Gallov Pedersdatter, ½ year old. The widow's guardian (lagverge) was Christopher Pedersen Winge; Jon Pedersen was guardian for his sisters Malli and Kirstie; Lars Pedersen Wigen was named guardian (formynder) for Anders Pedersen and Gollov Pedersdatter. Appraisers Lars Torjing and Niels Wigendahl. (Stjør- og Verdal skifteprotokoll 6-2, fol. 428b-429a, scan.) ¶4

Corrections 1

Correction: he is not the father of Gullov Pedersdatter (see her page). His daughter Golla (bapt. 28 Jul 1771, '½ Aar gl.' in his 1772 estate) was buried in 1773 (Digitalarkivet index, Stjørdal 1755-1780: 'Golla Pedersdatter, b. 1771, Skiervold, avdød'); this is probably why the 1773 påtegning on his skifte (skifteprotokoll 7 fol. 514b) mentions her. The Gullov who married in 1798 was baptised 14 Aug 1774 as 'Peter Schjervolds b. Gudlov' (16371 canvas 251), daughter of his widow's second husband Peter Larsen (now Peter Larsen). Gullov's father pointer has been moved, so Peder Jonsen is no longer a direct ancestor; confidence lowered to Speculative because the link that put him in the tree is refuted. ¶18

Searches that found nothing 1

Negative: the Stjør- og Verdal probate card index for 1689-1742 (source 77804, one card per probate, filed by farm, giving heirs, ages and guardians) has no card for Skjervold/Skiervold: the cards run Skjelle (Skogn, view 1755) - Skjesol (Åsen, view 1757). So no probate in skifteprotokoll 1 (1689-1701) or 2 (1727-1742), nor a 1692-1714 skifte designation, survives for a Jon of Skjervold who could be Peder Jonsen's father. A farm-name variant (e.g. a place under Skjervold such as Olderen) was not checked. ¶10
