Life story

Her probate, Skifteprotokoll 5 (24755) fol. 246a: 'Anno 1762 den 17 Juny' a lawful skifte was held on the farm Sandwigen in Selbu after the late wife of Ole Jonsen, 'Ingebor Michels Datter', who 'efterladt sig med hende een Søn Navnlig Joen Olsen 14 aar' (left one son with him, Joen Olsen, 14). The widower chose Ole Floner[?] as his guardian, and the son's guardian was 'hans Farbroder Ole Jonsen Sandwig' (his father's brother). The appraisers were Niels Belle and Anders Størset; lensmann Ole Thomassen was present. The typed register of the volume lists the entry as 'Ole Jonsen, Sandvik, Selbu, fol. 246'. ¶1

She is the first wife of Ole Jonsen of Sandvik (d. 1767) and the mother of the direct ancestor Jon Olsen Sandvik (b. about 1747). Ole's second wife, Kari Olsdatter, was his widow in 1767 and was Jon's stepmother. ¶2

Open questions and the next record to read 2

Next: her burial in Selbu ministerialbok 16311 (1757-62) for her age, and a Selbu probate for a Michel naming a daughter Ingebor married to Ole Jonsen at Sandvik. ¶3b

Lead: the 1762 extra tax (canvas 130) lists two households under the same matrikkel no. 709 Sandvig: Ole Jonsen with his second wife Kari Olsdatter, and 'Ingebor Michelsdatter, Enke' (widow) with three sons, Peder Thomassen (married to Ingebor Jonsdatter), Michel and Jon. That widow shares her name and patronymic and holds the other half of Ole Jonsen's bruk. She cannot be the same woman, since this Ingebor left only one son, Joen Olsen, in June 1762. She may be a relative (a cousin or an aunt). Not a link. The registers list no Selbu probate for a Michel between 1742 and 1767 except Michel Sivertsen Velve (1767), whose wife was Brynild Pedersdatter. ¶4
