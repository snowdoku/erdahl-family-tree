About the family

Second wife of Lars Samuelsen Loen: his first wife Ane Olsdatter was dead by the probate of 12 [Mar?] 1749 (A 10 fol. 335b), so Marthe's marriage to Lars falls after early 1749 (inferred). ¶4

Parents found: the probate after Ane Rasmusdatter of Beenes, 18 May 1751 (A 11 fol. 160b-161a), names the widower Rasmus Joensen and their three children: Joen Rasmussen on the farm, 'Marithe Rasmusdatter gift med Lars Samuelsen Loen', and Barbra Rasmusdatter, 24. This settles which Beinnes household she came from (Aaland's Rasmus Jonson Sunde x Anne Rasmusdatter, not Rasmus Olsen x Christi Knudsdatter, whose 1731 probate (A 6 fol. 505a) names only Ole, Synnøve and Anne). It also dates her marriage to Lars Samuelsen to between early 1749 and May 1751. Her brother Jon Rasmussen (d. 1775 aged 65) held the Beinnes bruk from 1732. ¶5

Life story

Probate, 1772: the probate after Marthe Rasmusdatter Loen in Olden skipreide was registered 5 Oct 1772 (Nordfjord skifteprotokoll A 15b, fol. 768b; indexed 1770-1774). It was held between her widower Lars Samuelsen and her child Anne Larsdatter, married to Guttorm Andersen Hilde. ¶1

Aaland 1932 (Stryn volume, Loen bruk nr. 15, printed p. 322) names her as Lars Samuelsen's second wife, 'Marte Rasmusd. Beinn.', died 1772 aged 58, so born about 1714 and of Beinnes. Her farm of origin and age rest on Aaland only; her parents are not known. ¶2
