Life story

A younger sister of Berith Gundersdatter Eidem, Oline Slind's paternal grandmother, and so a great-great-aunt of Oline. The four sisters are entered together in the 1825 census of Selbu at rural residence 0060 Eidem, household 1: Beret 10, Maglie 9, Sigrid 6 and Marit 2, children of the lodger Gunder Johnsen and Brynhild Eriksdatter. ¶1

Nothing further has been traced for her. She would have married in Selbu in the 1840s and her marriage entry, which names her father, is the next record. ¶2

Baptism: 'Maglie Gundersd Brennan', baptised 28 July 1816, Selbu baptisms 1816 pp. 14-15 no. 24, father Gunder Johnsen, mother Brynhild Eriksd. She carries the name of her maternal grandmother Magli Christensdatter. Index only. ¶3
