Life story

Thomas Pattishull (Pateshall) of Herefordshire, Esq., named by Lodge (Peerage of Ireland, 1789, vol. 1, p. 144) as the father of Jane, wife of Roger Boyle of Hereford. The Pateshall family were seated at the Ford in Pudleston, Herefordshire. The birth year is a rough estimate from his daughter's marriage (her sons were born about 1520-45). Added. ¶1

Tested against the Pateshall pedigrees; still Speculative. Duncumb (1804, vol. 1, p. 379, from Collins) makes Roger Boyle's wife 'a daughter of Thomas Pateshall, of the Ford, in this county'. But the 1569 Herefordshire pedigree of Pateshall of the Ford in Pudleston (Weaver, p. 64, Harl. MS 615) has no Thomas in the right generation. It runs John Pateshall (17 Henry VII), William (married Sybil Gomond, heiress of Byford), and then William's sons Roger, a third son, William (4th son, will proved at Hereford 1543) and John of the Ford (M.P. 1557). A Thomas appears only two generations later. If Jane was of the Ford family, her dates (born about 1505-20) put her among William and Sybil's children, as a sister of John of the Ford, not a daughter of any Thomas. A rival family existed too. The Northamptonshire visitations of 1564 and 1618-19 show Pateshalls of Riseley, Bedfordshire, in the same generation: Robert Pateshall of Riseley, whose daughter Jane married Richard Humfrey of Barton Seagrave, and a Pateshall of Riseley whose daughter Sibyl married William Pemberton of Rushden. The Bedfordshire visitations of 1566-1634 have no Pateshull pedigree; the medieval Pateshulls of Bletsoe ended in co-heiresses in the 1300s. Lodge's 'Thomas Pattishull of Herefordshire' therefore matches no recorded pedigree. ¶3a

How sure are we?

Speculative. A plausible candidate only. Treat this link as a question, not a fact.

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Speculative, graded on his link to his daughter Jane Pattishall. Only Lodge names him. The 1569 visitation pedigree of Pateshall of the Ford (Harl. MS 615, ff. 39v-40; Weaver p. 64) has no Thomas in the right generation. It runs John Pateshall (living 17 Henry VII, 1501-02), then William, husband of Sybell Gommonde, whose sons were Roger, William (will 1543) and John of the Ford (MP 1557). The only Thomas is a grandson of John of the Ford, far too late, and the pedigree lists no daughter Jane. He may have been a younger son of this house, or Lodge may have the first name wrong. ¶2a
