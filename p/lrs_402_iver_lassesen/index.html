About the family

Children (sibling note; only Randi is created): the 1699 and 1701 probates name, in this order, the sons Hindrich (Hendrich), Lasse and Mons (Mognus), and the daughters Torbiør (Torberg), Randi, Britha (Britte), Anna (Anne) and Ingeborg (written 'Bergi' in the 1699 heading, 'Ingeborg' in the 1699 lots and 'Bigri[?]' in 1701). In the 1701 census of Indre Oppedal (only men and boys listed) 'Henrich[?] Ivers[en]' 24[?] heads a household with 'Lasse Iversen' 16 and 'Torberg[?] Ivers[en]' 12, and a 'Mons Iversen' 10, born on the farm, is a servant with Anders Larsen; that these are Iver's children is an inference from the names and farm. Torbiør is a daughter in both probates (she draws a sister's lot), so the census entry 'Torberg' 12 among the males is either a girl listed by exception or a misreading. A 'Hindrich Iversen' is a creditor in the probate after Agate Monsdatter at Indre Oppedal on 8 Oct [1700]. ¶5

Father (Probable): son of Lasse of Indre Oppedal (see his page). For: (1) his patronymic Lassesen fits a father named Lasse; (2) he lived and died on Indre Oppedal (probate there, 1 Jun 1699), and Lasse was a farmer on Indre Oppedal in 1664 with a son Ifver (Iver) aged 13; (3) the age fits: Iver born about 1651 would be about 25 at a marriage about 1676 and about 48 with eight under-age children in 1699; (4) Iver named his second son Lasse, which fits naming after his father (inference). Rivals checked in the whole of Brekke sogn in 1664: the only other Lasse is the farmer at Hudisdall, whose listed son is Guttorm 14 (no Iver); the other Ivers are Ifver Colbensen 30, servant at Lettre (patronymic Colbensen), Ifver Bendixsen 14, servant at Fustefjord (patronymic Bendixsen), Ifver Hermundsen 17, servant at Hudisdall, and the farmer Ifver at Indre Brekke; none is an Iver son of Lasse. ¶8a

Son Henrik's probate (supporting): Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll 6a, No. 66, fol. 150a, 19 Aug 1741, at Indre Oppedal, Ladvig skipreide, after Henric Iversen, between the widow Sigri Aslachsdatter and their two children, the daughters Eli (married to Peder Torsen[?]) and a second daughter (name read 'Orsouv[?]', perhaps Ingeborg/Ursula) married to Guttorm Lassesen, and the son Iver by the late first wife Barbra Jensdatter. Henrik named his first daughter Eli, the name of Iver Lassesen's widow Eli Monsdatter, and his son Iver, which fits Henrik being Iver Lassesen's eldest son Hindrich of the 1699 probate (inference from names and farm). No siblings of Henrik are named in the heading, so it does not name Randi or Britte Iversdatter. ¶9

Mother (Probable): Sigri Torbensdatter (see her page), the wife of the farmer Lasse on Opedahl in the 1645 poll tax (Digitalarkivet 100387, p. 66). Iver, born about 1651, named a daughter Torbjørg ('Torbiør'), the female form of her father's name. See her record for the argument and what is open. ¶10

Life story

Probate: on 1 June [16]99 the lensmann Niels Hansen with the lagrettemenn (sworn court assessors) Jørgen Aspnæs, Johannes Oppedal and Iver Sætter[?] (or Dahle[?]) met on Indre Oppedal, Lavik skipreide, to hold the probate (arveskifte) after the late Iver Lassesen, between his widow 'Ell[?] Mons daatter' and their jointly begotten under-age ('u-myndige') children Hindrich, Lasse, Mons, Torbiør, Rendj (Randi), Britha, Anna and Bergi (Ingeborg), 'Ifvers Sønner og døttre'; present for the children was the wife's brother Olle Monsen [?]dal. Gross estate 49 rd 4 ort 10 s; each son's lot 5 rd 5 ort, each daughter's lot 2 rd 5 ort 8 s; the lots on fol. 46a are 1 Hendrich, 2 Lasse, 3 Mons, 4 Torbiør, 5 Randj, 6 Britte, 7 Anne, 8 Ingeborg. No ages are given. Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll 1a, fol. 45a-46a. The year is written 'Ao 99' (read with medium confidence; the protocol covers 1698-1700). ¶1

Birth (inference): before about 1657. His eldest son Hindrich is probably the 'Henrich[?] Ivers[en]' aged 24[?] who heads a household on Indre Oppedal in the 1701 census with 'Lasse Iversen' 16 (born about 1677 and 1685), so Iver was probably married by about 1676. ¶3

1664 census (supporting, inference): in the bailiff's census (fogdemanntall) of Brekke sogn, 21 Jun 1664, the farm Indre Oppedal has a farmer 'Lasse' (no age or patronymic given) with one son 'Ifver 13', and on the next row a farmer 'Johans'. Ifver son of Lasse at Indre Oppedal, born about 1651, fits Iver Lassesen of Indre Oppedal, who d. before 1 Jun 1699 with an eldest son born about 1677; that they are the same man is an inference from name, patronymic and farm. If so, Iver was born about 1651 (the birth field 'BEF 1657' is left as is). Not tested: whether the farmer Johans of 1664 is Johannes Larsen (b. about 1631) and, if so, whether he and Iver were both sons of this Lasse, which would make Randi Iversdatter a first cousin of her husband Mons Johansen; nothing read supports or rules this out. ¶7

Death (from the probate): he died at or near Indre Oppedal before 1 Jun 1699. His burial was not searched (no Gulen church book survives this early). ¶2

Place in the line (Probable): father of Randi Iversdatter (see her page), wife of Mons Johansen of Indre Oppedal. For: (1) his 1699 probate names an under-age daughter 'Rendj'/'Randj' (Randi), so she was unmarried in 1699; (2) the 1701 probate after his widow Eli Monsdatter and her second husband Lars Johansen again names 'Randj' among Iver's children, and Lars was a son of Johannes Larsen of Indre Oppedal, whose son Mons Johansen 22 in the 1701 census is probably (Probable) Randi's husband; Randi Iversdatter was therefore Mons's brother's stepdaughter on the same farm; (3) Randi Iversdatter lived out her life on Indre Oppedal (husband's probate 16 May 1739, her lifetime division 7 Oct 1739); (4) Randi named her eldest son Iver, which fits naming after her father, and that son, Iver Monsen of Botnen, named daughters Anna, Randi and Eli (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll 5, fol. 125a, Nr. 78, 1739), which fits naming after Randi's mother Eli Monsdatter and her sister Anna (inference; weak, since his wife's family could also account for Eli); (5) no other Randi Iversdatter is known on Indre Oppedal. Of the other Ivers on Indre Oppedal in the 1701 census, Iver Tollaksen 26 (a servant and soldier) and Iver Bendixsen 20 are too young to have had a daughter who bore a son about 1705, and Iver Gutormsen 30 very probably so; only Iver Jensen[?] 45 (born about 1656), who farmed there with no sons listed, is a possible rival father, and no record names a daughter of his. ¶4a

Possible estate in 1700 (uncertain): among the creditors in the probate after Agate Monsdatter at Indre Oppedal, 8 Oct [1700] (skifteprotokoll 1b, fol. 258b), is a name read 'Iver Lassesen[?]', perhaps meaning his estate. Reading uncertain; not used. ¶6

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 record that disagrees · 1 alternative set aside · 2 open questions

Records that disagree 1

Against or open: the rival search covered Brekke sogn only, not the other sogns of Eivindvik prestegjeld; 35 years separate the 1664 list from the 1699 probate, Lasse is a common name, and boys under about 12 were not listed, so a younger Iver Lassesen of another farm cannot be fully excluded. Not Confirmed because no record names Iver's father. ¶8b

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Not Confirmed: no record read says that Randi Iversdatter, wife of Mons Johansen, was the daughter of Iver Lassesen, and no marriage record of Mons and Randi has been found. ¶4b

Open questions and the next record to read 2

Would confirm: the betrothal or marriage of Mons Johansen and Randi Iversdatter (about 1701-1705; the Gulen church books begin later), a court or land record naming Mons Johansen's wife as Iver Lassesen's daughter, or a later probate after one of Randi's siblings naming her as Mons Johansen's wife. ¶4c

Would confirm: a court, land or probate record naming Iver as Lasse's son or heir (Ytre Sogn tingbøker and skifteprotokoller before 1699; the 1661-1665 matrikkel for Indre Oppedal). ¶8c
