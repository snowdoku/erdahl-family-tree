TWIN QUESTION RESOLVED, not twins: Karen A. 'Carrie' Hanson Engen was born 31 Oct 1875 in Waseca County, Minnesota. Her own Find a Grave page gives her father as a different Peder Hanson (15 Jul 1821 - 14 Jan 1908, buried Sacred Heart, Renville Co., MN), married to Anna Marie Nilsdatter. Amelia was born 9 Aug 1875 in Mount Valley and baptized at Winnebago to Peder Hansen and Andrine Eriksd. Two births 12 weeks apart in different states cannot share a mother. The Find a Grave link from Andrea to Carrie is wrong and should be ignored. ¶9

Life story

PROBABLE baptism: Andrea, born 6 Jan 1839 and baptized 3 Mar 1839 in Nord-Aurdal parish, daughter of Erik Olsen and Marit Olsdatter of Sørstrand. Her brother Ole E. Dakken's 1927 obituary names their parents as Eric and Margret (Olson) Dakken. Ole was baptized in 1849 to Erik Olsen and Marit Olsdatter in Svennes, Nord-Aurdal. ¶6

Find a Grave links her to children Peder P. Hanson (1866-1951) and Karen A. 'Carrie' Hanson Engen (1875-1969). Carrie's 1875 birth year falls in the same year as Amelia's (9 Aug 1875). This may mean twins, a wrong year, or a misattribution, and is unresolved. ¶3

Name forms: the Winnebago church book calls her 'Andrine Eriksd.' (baptisms of 1867-1878) and 'Andrea Eriks' (1886 confirmation). Her husband's obituary says 'Andrina Erickson', the 1884 county history 'Anna Erickson', and her grave 'Andrea'. Her patronymic was Eriksdatter, and 'Dakken' is the family's farm name (spelled Dokken in Norway). ¶5

The 1884 county history names Peter Hanson's wife as Anna Erickson, married 1855. Find a Grave shows his wife as 'Andrea Hanson' (1839-1925), buried beside him. Andrea and Anna are taken here as the same woman, so this is probable, not proven. Amelia's obituary names only 'Mrs. Peter Hanson'. ¶1

Her identity is now confirmed. Her obituary (Lake Mills Graphic, 14 Oct 1925, p. 7, 'Mrs. Peter Hanson') says she was born in Valdres, Norway, and died 1 Oct 1925. Her maiden name was 'Andrea Dakken'. She married Peter Hanson in 1855 and came to America with her parents at about age 14. A brother, Ole Dakken, lived in Lake Mills. The obituary names five surviving children: Hans and Peter Jr. of Driscoll, N. Dak., Mrs. Gust Nelson of Forest City, Mrs. Wm. Nelson of Lake Mills, and Mrs. M. O. Webber of Marquette, Iowa. She had moved to North Dakota a year earlier to live with her son P. P. Hanson. The funeral was at Winnebago church. ¶4
