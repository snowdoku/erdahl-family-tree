About the family

Wife of George Hicken, the immigrant, on the account of the compiled Island Register Hicken genealogy, which has them married about 1795 at St Margaret Pattens, England. Not yet found in any record; a granddaughter Phebe Hicken (c.1839-1876) carried her name. ¶1

PEI evidence: no George or Phebe Hicken of the immigrant generation appears in any PEI index, whether the baptism index (1788-1943), the baptisms/marriages/burials index (1780-1982), the marriage registers (1832-88) or the death cards (1810-1913). Every one of the five English-born Hicken siblings who settled around Three Rivers and Gaspereaux by 1831 gave the names George and Phebe to early children: Eliza Wise's son George Hicken Wise (1832); William's Phebe (1835) and George Samuel (1839); Thomas's George Benjamin (1834); George's Phebe (c.1839); and Mary Ann Moore's George (1840) and Phebe (c.1841). This naming pattern supports the compiler's statement that their parents were George and Phebe. The surviving 1841 census return for Lot 61 (PARO) is the record that could show the elder George as a head of household in P.E.I. ¶3

Mother of children baptised 1796-1814 at Canterbury and Deal (Kent), Little Cowarne (Herefordshire) and Doddenham (Worcestershire) - see her husband George. Her own baptism, age and origin are unknown; at least eight children are recorded across eighteen years, so she was probably born about 1770-1778. ¶4

Life story

A Phebe, wife of George Hicken, private in the Royal Artillery, is named as mother at the baptisms of James (Canterbury, 1800), Jane (Deal, 1801) and Eliza (Deal, 1804) in Kent parish registers transcribed by FreeREG - very probably this woman. Her maiden name Allbut rests only on the compiled genealogy; the registers give none. ¶2

Context: Allbut families lived in exactly the district where she and George settled after his army service - at Stockton on Teme, Worcestershire (children of John and Ann Allbut baptised 1742-1755) and at Bromyard, Herefordshire, three miles from Little Cowarne (James, son of James and Mary Allbut, baptised 1794). That makes the compiled genealogy's "Allbut" plausible as her maiden name and would explain the family's move to this corner of Herefordshire and Worcestershire, but no baptism of a Phebe (or Jane) Allbut of the right age has been found, so it remains unproved. ¶7

1841 census: she is probably the woman 'upwards of 60' in George Hicken Senr's two-person household on Lot 61 (1841 census of PEI, Kings County District 15, image 512), entered as Church of England (image 513). If so, she was alive in 1841 and born before about 1781, consistent with a first child in 1796 and a last known child in 1814. The census names only the head, so she is not named, and her identity with this woman is an inference. ¶8

Will: her husband George Hicken's will of 8 Aug 1847 (Lot 61, PEI) provides for 'my wife Phoebe' if she survives him and calls her the mother of his son William ('after the death of his mother my Wife Phoebe'). It names sons Thomas, George and William. She was therefore alive in Aug 1847 and George's wife then. ¶9

Burial (lead): the will asks for burial at St Andrew's (Lot 59/61 area). Her burial would be after Aug 1847. ¶11

Original will read: George Hicken's original will of 1847 (PEI Probate Estate Case Files, H testate no. 5, images 1012-1014) calls her 'my wife Phoebe' and, like the typescript, does not call her George Jr's mother. Grade unchanged (Probable). ¶12

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

2 on the grade · 1 correction · 1 open question

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Confidence raised from Speculative to Probable as the mother of the Prince Edward Island siblings: her name is on the baptisms of William (1806) and Mary Ann (1814), who match Island settlers by name, date and age, and four of the five English-born siblings named a daughter Phebe. ¶6

Grade (unchanged, Probable) on her link to George Hicken junior (see his page): the will names her as George senior's wife and William's mother, but not in words as George junior's mother. George junior was born c.1811-12; she is named as mother at the baptisms of William (Little Cowarne, Aug 1806) and Mary Ann (Doddenham, Aug 1814), which bracket his birth, but those baptisms are in England and their identification with the PEI family rests on the matching sons' names and dates plus the compiler's abstract of the 1822 land petition (Royal Artillery service). ¶10a

Corrections 1

Correction: her maiden name "Allbut" rests only on the compiled Island Register genealogy, and the marriage it points to - George Hicken and an Allbut bride at St Margaret Pattens, City of London, December 1794/1795 - is indexed with the bride's name as JANE Allbut, on two separate register images. Unless the register image shows that Jane and Phebe are the same woman, Phebe's maiden name is unknown. The surname has been removed here for that reason. ¶5
