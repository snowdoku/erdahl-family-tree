Life story

Baptism: Baptised 9 Aug 1813 in Gulen parish, daughter of Iver Rasmussøn and Marthe Olsdatter of Botnen; the mother was churched on the 11th Sunday after Trinity, 29 Aug 1813. Godparents: Iver Iversøn, Iver Olssøn, Lasse Olssøn and Brithe Lassesdatter of Ytre Takle, and Lasse Rasmussøn of Botnen. Gulen church book 1809-1825, p. 63a, no. 30, index. ¶1

Burial: Synneve Iversdatter, pigebarn (girl child), of Botnen, aged 2/3 year, was buried 24 Apr 1814 (Gulen church book 1809-1825, burial index). She was probably named after her paternal grandmother, Synneve Joensdatter. ¶2
