About the family

Her children, from the same page: Brynhild f. 1744; Marit f. 1746; Ingeborg f. 1749; JOERN f. 1752, who took over the bruk and is David Erdahl's ancestor; Siri f. 1757; a daughter f. 1761 who married Per Persen EIDEM; and a daughter f. 1765 who married Kristoffer Olsen. Seven children, of whom the fourth carried the line. ¶8

Her daughter Brynill's baptism, early 1744 (Selbu 16315 canvas 57 col. 1, read by the orchestrator), ends with 'madamn Marithe Pedersdatter' - the mother carrying the child - which supports the patronymic Pedersdatter against the 1762 ekstraskatt's 'Tors-' reading. ¶11

Her brother is most likely the 'Olle Ped. Hoem' buried at Cantate 1782, aged 55 (16311 c107R), so born about 1727, nine years younger than she was. He was not yet at Hoem in 1762: the extra tax shows Øvre Hoem held by Erik Pedersen and Ingebor Olsdatter, with married sons Ole and Peder Eriksen, and Nedre Hoem as an officer's farm with no occupants listed. Erik Pedersen (b. by about 1710) could be another brother. Her father is therefore a Peder, probably of the Hoem or neighbouring farms. ¶22a

Life story

The book's 1720 against the 1801 census's implied 1718 is a two-year difference of the ordinary kind; the census age of 83 is the earlier and more direct statement and ABT 1718 is kept, with the book's 1720 recorded. ¶7

Checked (16310 canvas 80 right, full read): 16th Sunday after Trinity 1720, 'Døbt Peder Hegsets barn Marit', godparents [Stieren?] Smeltenach[?], Gunne Bach[?], Guri Rønsberg, Marit..., Ane Hegset; 19th Sunday after Trinity 1720, 'Døbt Peder Vigens barn Marit', godparents Jon [Sem?],... Kilsæt[?], Ane Vigen, Marit Vigen, Marit [Sem?]. Neither entry names Hoem, and none of the godparents is from Hoem, so neither child can be chosen as her; both remain candidates only. ¶20

Marriage: married on the 8th Sunday after Trinity, 15 July 1742 (date computed from Easter 1742), as 'Pigen Marethe Pedersdatter', the maiden Marit Pedersdatter, to the soldier Ole Jørgensen (Selbu klokkerbok 16315, canvas 35 col. 1). This is the 1742 register entry the earlier note asked for, and it gives her patronymic as Pedersdatter, supporting the 1801 census against the 1762 extra tax's 'Tors[?]datter'. ¶12

1746: carried her daughter Marit at baptism on 14 Aug 1746 (16315 c90) and was churched about Michaelmas 1746 as 'Ole Slindens Qvinde Marite Pedersdatter' (16315 c92 col. 2). Both entries give her patronymic as Pedersdatter, which goes against the 'Marit Tors[?]datter' of the 1762 extra-tax list (a probable misreading or slip there). ¶13

HER DEATH YEAR AND HER MARRIAGE, from the Selbu gaardshistorie. Selbu i fortid og naatid vol. 7, p. 116, holder line '1756-1774': the holder was born 1717 and died 1772, and 'G. 1742 m. Marit..., f. 1720, d. 1808'. So she married in 1742, she was widowed in 1772, and SHE DIED IN 1808 - which replaces the BET 1801 AND 1825 I had derived from her presence in the 1801 census and absence from the 1825 one. She was eighty-eight, and she had been a widow for thirty-six years. ¶6

Probate after her husband Ole Jørgensen, held 17 Oct 1772 on the farm Slinde in Selbu (Skifteprotokoll 6-2, 24757, fol. 530a, view 236): the widow 'Marithe Peders Datter' declared that she took as her guardian ('Laugværge') 'Broderen Ole Pedersen Hoem[?]' - her brother Ole Pedersen of Hoem[?] (farm name not certain; Hoem or Hoen). Heirs: son Jørgen Olsen (married), daughters Brønille Olsdatter married to Christen Christensen Stor-Evjen, Marthe Olsdatter married to Ingebrigt [Christensen?] Stor-Evjen, Ingeborg Olsdatter married to Jon Olsen Sandvig[?], Siri Olsdatter 15, Margaretha Olsdatter 12 and Maren Olsdatter 7. Lead for her father: a Peder whose son Ole Pedersen lived at Hoem[?] in 1772; with Peder Bersvendsen Slinden now excluded (his 1760 probate does not name her), the next check is a Peder at Hoem/Hoen in Selbu with children Ole and Marit (b. c.1718). ¶16

Widow in her husband Ole Jørgensen's probate at Slinde, 17 Oct 1772 (24757 fol. 530), as 'Marith Peders Datter', with the seven children listed there. Her guardian ('Laugværge') was 'hendes Broder Ole Peder-sen Hoem[?]', her brother Ole Pedersen. The farm word is clear as 'Hoem' but no Selbu farm of that name is known to us, so it may be misread. This is the first record to name a sibling of hers, and it is the key to her father. ¶17

Re- of both leads. (1) 24757 fol. 530 (1772): the guardian is clearly 'Broderen Ole Peder-sen Hoem'. Hoem (Øvre and Nedre Hoem) is a real Selbu farm, so this is not a misreading of Fløne. (2) 24755 fol. 101a (1760, skifte after Peder Thomassen Fløne): the heirs are 1. Ole Pedersen Fløne 45; 2. Thomas Pedersen, dead, leaving children; 3. a dead son, Thomas[?]; 4. Anna Pedersdatter Fløne; and last 'Marit Peders-datter Fosrum[?]' (perhaps Fossum). That Marit is placed at another farm, not at Slinden, and her brother Ole was born about 1715. So the Fløne family is not hers, and Peder Thomassen is ruled out as her father. ¶21

That one of her daughters married a Per Persen Eidem about 1780 is worth noticing: it is the EARLIEST Slind-Eidem marriage in this tree, two generations before Berith Gundersdatter Eidem married into the family in 1841 and four before Oline Slind married John P. Eidem in Minnesota in 1901. ¶9

The oldest ancestor now reached on David Erdahl's Slind line, and the sixth generation above Oline Slind. The 1801 census of Selbu enters her in her son's household at Slinden - rural residence 0083, household 3, person 021 - as 'Marit Pedersdatter, 83, Hans moder, Enke efter 1. aegteskab': HIS MOTHER, widow after her first marriage. Aged 83 at 1 February 1801 she was born about 1717-1718. ¶1

What her record implies about her husband. Her son's patronymic is Olssen, so her husband's given name was OLE. 'Enke efter 1. aegteskab' means she had married once only and was a widow in 1801, so Ole was dead by February 1801 and she never remarried. Her own patronymic, Pedersdatter, makes her father a PEDER, born in the 1680s or 1690s. Neither man is entered as a person here: a patronymic is a clue, not a record. ¶3

Context, flagged as context: a Norwegian farm widow of her generation would normally have been on KAAR - a pension in kind reserved out of the farm when she and her husband handed it to the next holder, specifying grain, a cow's keep, firewood and a room. The 1801 census does not give her an occupation or a kaar note, which is usual for a woman of 83 simply living in her son's house. ¶5

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Confirmed, not inferred: the census itself states the relationship in the family-position column, and it does so inside the household of Jorgen Olssen, so there is no ambiguity about whose mother she was. This is exactly the kind of line - an aged parent entered among the household's members - that gives a free generation and is missed whenever a census is read as a list of names rather than as a household. ¶2

Speculative father: Peder Bersvendsen Slinden (d. 1760 aged 71), godfather 'Peder Slinden' at her daughter Marit's baptism in 1746 - see that person's notes. ¶14

Corrections 1

Correction: her Speculative father Peder Bersvendsen Slinden is withdrawn. His probate (Skifteprotokoll 5, 24755, fol. 97b, Slinden 1760) lists his heirs as three sons, Bersvend, Reinold and Ole, and two unmarried daughters, [Lisbet?] and Ingebor. She is not among them, though she was married and living at Slinden. Her father, a Peder, remains unknown. She was not necessarily born at Slinden: she 'married into' the farm in 1742. ¶15

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

1762 extra tax (conflict, not adopted): her husband Ole at 706 Slinden is entered 'gift med Marit Tors[?]datter'. On a zoomed crop the first letter of the patronymic is the clerk's J/T form, so it reads Torsdatter (Torstensdatter) or possibly Jonsdatter; it does not look like the P he wrote in 'Peder' on the facing canvas. This conflicts with the 1801 census's 'Marit Pedersdatter'. The 1801 entry is kept, because it is the direct statement of her own household; the 1742 marriage in the Selbu register 1740-1746 (Digitalarkivet source 16315) would settle her father's name. ¶10

Searches that found nothing 2

Death: she was alive on 1 February 1801 and is absent from the 1825 census of Slinden, so she died between those dates, aged somewhere between 83 and 107. Her burial has not been found. The indexed Selbu burial register for 1801-1815 contains only about twenty entries for the whole Slinden farm, so her absence from it is a gap in the transcription rather than evidence; the entry is on the scanned pages of the Selbu ministerialbok 1801-1815. ¶4

Negative: the 77804 probate card index (1689-1742) has no Hoem/Hoen card. Its H run goes Hjelsvold (canvas 783), Hofsdalsve (785), Hofstad (787), Hogstad (789-799), Hojem (Skogn, 801-803), so a Selbu Hoem card would have to sit between 783 and 785, and none does. No probate after a Peder of Hoem was held in 1689-1742, or it was filed elsewhere. Her father therefore needs a church-book route (16310 baptisms 1716-20 for a Marit, daughter of a Peder at Hoem) or the bygdebok. ¶23

Open questions and the next record to read 4

Lead, not a link: the skifte after Peder Thomassen Fløne, Selbu, 6 Feb 1760 (Skifteprotokoll 5, 24755, fol. 101a) names among his heirs '1. Sønnen Ole Pedersen Fløne 45 aar' (born about 1715) and, last, a daughter 'Marit Peders-datter' followed by a farm or husband word that reads like 'Tofsrum[?]', not Slinden. A man named Ole Flønes was also the widow's guardian in the Sandvik probate of 1767. If 'Hoem' in 1772 is a misreading of 'Fløen', Marit would be Peder Thomassen's daughter, but the 1760 entry does not place its Marit at Slinden, so no parent is set. ¶18a

Next: a full-resolution re-read of the last line of 24755 fol. 101a and of the guardian line of 24757 fol. 530. ¶18b

Lead for her father, not adopted. The typed registers of Stjør- og Verdal skifteprotokoller 3-12 (1742-1802) have no probate at Hoem in Selbu, so her brother Ole Pedersen Hoem[?] (1772) cannot yet lead to her father that way. In the 1701 manntall the Hoem farms held a 'Peder' aged 7 (son of Ole Pedersen, Over Hoem) and a 'Peder' aged 1 (son of Gunder Olsen, Ner Hoem). The Selbu register 1699-1737 (16310) has no baptism of a Marit daughter of a Peder at Hoem between Dec 1717 and Nov 1719, but in 1720 it has two Marits with fathers named Peder: 'Peder Hegset barn Marit', 16th Sunday after Trinity 1720, and 'Peder Vigen barn Marit', 19th Sunday after Trinity 1720 (c80 R;, read at overview level only). A 'Peder Olsen og Marit Vigen' married on the 1st Sunday after Trinity 1719 (c75 R). The bygdebok's birth year for her is 1720. These are candidates only. Next step: read the two 1720 baptisms in full, with their godparents, and look for a later Peder Olsen at Hoem. ¶19

Next: a Hoem probate (the typed registers have no Selbu Hoem estate in 1742-1802, so look in the 77804 card index for 1689-1742 or the unindexed 24763), and the Selbu baptisms of 1716-20 (16310 c62-80) for a Marit, daughter of a Peder at Hoem. ¶22b
