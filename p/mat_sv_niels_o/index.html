About the family

His patronymic says his own father was an Ole of Langli. Langli is gnr 41, 42 and 43 in Selbu; which of the three bruk he held cannot be fixed from the 1801 census, which carries no loepenummer, and the 1865 census of Langli is where to resolve it. ¶5

Children: the first child, Elen, was baptised in Selbu on Oculi Sunday, 22 March 1778, 'Par: Niels O. Langli Guri Olsd.'; godparents Jon O[lsen] of the same farm (Langlie), Tosten Renaa, Mali Langlie, Anne Seth[er] and Karen Langl[ie]. His wife was churched on Maundy Thursday 1778 as 'Niels Ols. Langlies Hustru'. Elen is the Elen Nielsdatter, 23, unmarried, of his 1801 household. Jon Olsen of Langlie may be his brother (same patronymic and farm), which is not proved. ¶12

Parents proposed at Speculative only: Ole Jonsen Langlie and Eli Nielsdatter, chosen by the naming pattern of Niels's children (Elen, Ole, Jon) and of Niels himself (for Eli's father Niels). No record names Niels as their son; a second Ole Jonsen Langlie (x Mali Andersdatter) lived on the same farm in 1758-61. See the notes on the two parent records for what would settle it. ¶17

Child not previously known: a daughter, name read 'Agnile' (probably Agnis/Agnes), baptised Fer. II Pasch. 1782 (Easter Monday, 1 Apr 1782): 'Döbt Agnile[?] Par: Niels O. Langl. Guri Olsd. T. Michel Drax. Olle Langl. Bryn[?] Stok[?] Sigri Langl. Marit ib.' Read from the scan (source 16311, canvas 107 right page); the wife 'Niels Ols. Langlies Hust.' was churched on 3 p. Trin. 1782 (canvas 108). ¶18

Life story

Bygdebok lead (word boxes only): on the Langli pages a block with 'Jonsen', 'Eli' and 'G.1746' (married 1746) has 'Nils 1748' in its child list - an independent (secondary) pointer to the parents Ole Jonsen and Eli Nielsdatter already rated Probable from the 1762 extra tax list. The head's printed dates (1747-1792) need the page text to interpret; 'Ole' has no box on these pages, so the father's given name is not confirmed by the book. ¶23

1762 extra tax list: a 'Niels' over 12 is among the children of Ole Jonssen and Eli Nielsdatter at Langlie (matr. 692), with brothers Jon and Arnald[?]; the rival Ole Jonssen x Mali Andersdatter household has no child over 12. Parents now rated Probable (was Speculative). ¶22

Marriage: Selbu church, 1st Sunday after Trinity, 1 June 1777 (Easter 1777 = 30 March): 'Copul. Niels Ols. Langlie J. Guri Olsd.' - Copul. (copulati) means married. The copulation line gives no ages, sponsors or farm for the bride. No betrothal (trolovelse) was found in the lists read for April 1776 - May 1777. Read from the scan (source 16311, canvas 82, left page). This confirms the bygdebok lead 'G.1777' and replaces the estimate 'before 1778'. ¶11

Baptism of his son Jon: 'Döbt. Jon. Par: Niels Ols. Langlie Guri Olsd.', Selbu, Dom. 1 p. Trin. 1785 (29 May 1785); his wife churched 26 June 1785 ('Niels Ols. Langl. Hust.'). Selbu ministerialbok 1757-1801, read from the scan. This states the parentage of John Nielsen Langlie directly, alongside the 1801 census. ¶16

1801 census: person 001 at rural residence 0026 Langlie, Selbu - 'Niels Olssen', aged 54, 'Husbonde', both in their first marriage, occupation 'BONDE OG GAARD BEBOER' = farmer and occupier of the farm. Seven children in the house and a servant girl, Elen Sivertsdatter, 17. ¶1

He is the father of Jon Nielssen, 16, the only man of that name at Langli in 1801 and therefore the John Nielsen Langlie who married Brynnild Johnsdatter's mother and gave the bride her byname in 1841. ¶2

Stood godfather as 'Niels Olsen Langlie' to Maglie, daughter of Niels Povelsen Garberg, baptised on the 20th Sunday after Trinity (11 October) 1812. His grandson Niels, son of Ole Nielsen Langlie and Brynille Jonsdatter, was born 5 February 1812 and baptised on Shrove Sunday 1812, and a second grandson Nils, son of John Nielsen Langlie, was born 19 June 1815, three months before his death. ¶10

Death and burial: the Selbu burial register for 1815 (page 222) enters 'Nils Olsen, Gmd., Langlie', aged 69, died 9 September and buried on the 17th Sunday after Trinity, which in 1815 fell on 17 September (date worked out from Easter 1815 = 26 March). ¶6

The scanned burial entry reads: 'og begravet Gmd. Nils Olsen Langlie dødd d. 9de Septbr. 69 Aar gl.' - buried on the 17th Sunday after Trinity (17 September) 1815. It names no wife or kin. It agrees with the index entry already cited. ¶9

Probate: the skifte after 'Niels Olsen Langlie' of Selbu was closed on 21 June 1816 (Strinda og Selbu district court, skifteprotokoll 10b, fol. 713); the estate was registered on 28 December 1815, after his death on 9 September 1815. Heirs: the sons Ole Nielsen, John, a second Ole, and Peder; the daughters Elie[?] (married, 'gift'), Agnis[?] and Ingeborg. 'Elie' and 'Agnis' are faint readings. No share for a widow is shown in the share list, although his widow Guri Olsdatter lived until 1832; why is not explained on this page. ¶19

Probate hearing: at the skifte meeting held at the thing place Eidum on 23 February 1816 (proceedings protocol 1805-1835, fol. 102b) 'Ole Olsen Langlie' and 'Sønnen Ole Nielsen' (the son Ole Nielsen) attended for the estate, and the guardian of the minor heirs appeared, probably Gunder Gundersen Langlie represented by his brother Ole Gundersen Langlie (the guardian clause is a probable reading only). The page says the heirs are listed in the registration of 28 December 1815, which was not read. ¶20

Probate and parentage: the 1816 skifte names John among his sons, a third independent statement that John Nielsen Langli (see his page) was his son, after the 1785 baptism and the 1801 census. The sibship in the skifte matches the seven children of the 1801 census exactly by name, except that the eldest daughter Elen appears to be written 'Elie'. ¶21

Two of his children, OLE NILSEN LANGLIE and AGNIS NILSDATTER LANGLIE, stood godparents at his granddaughter Brynhild's baptism in 1818, which corroborates the household from an independent record. ¶3

1825 census: his widow 'Guri Olsd.', 70, 'Gmd. Enke' (a gaardmand's, i.e. farmer's, widow), heads household 01 of residence 0047* Langlie, with their married son Ole Nilsen ('Gift soen') and their son John Nilsen, a husmand, and both sons' wives and children - 16 persons in all. ¶15

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Rated Probable rather than Confirmed: the link rests on a census household plus the godparent pattern, not on a document that states 'Jon Nielssen, son of Niels Olssen'. The marriage of John Nielsen and Marit Johnsdatter, about 1812-1818, would say it outright. ¶4

Confidence raised from Probable to Confirmed: the 1801 census of Langlie states the relationship. Re-reading the residence page, every one of the seven children in Niels Olssen's household 01, including 'Jon Nielssen', 16, carries the family position 'Deres boern' (their children), after 'Niels Olssen, Husbonde' and 'Guri Olsdatter, Hans kone'. The remaining step - that this Jon Nielssen is the John Nielsen Langlie who married in 1815 and died in 1862 - rests on: he is the only Jon Nielssen at Langlie in 1801; his age (16, so born about 1785) agrees exactly with his burial age of 77 in 1862; 'Jon Nielsen ibidem' attested in 1813 for Ingebor Nielsdatter of Langlie, the 2-year-old of the 1801 household; his brother Ole and sister Agnis of the 1801 household stood godparents to his children in 1815 and 1818; and in 1825 he lived in one household with the widow Guri Olsdatter and her census-stated son Ole Nilsen. No record contradicts it. A probate (skifte) after Niels in 1815-16, or John's own baptism about 1784-85 in Selbu book 16311, would state it a second time. ¶14

The case for this identification 1

Identification of the 1815 burial: the name, the status of gaardmand (farmer) and the farm Langlie all match; his age of 69 gives a birth year of about 1746, against 54 in the 1801 census (about 1747). In 1832 a 'gaardmand's widow' Guri Olsdatter of Langlie was buried, which fits him as her late husband. No other Niels or Nils Olsen was buried from Langlie in 1801-1840. The burial identifies the 1801 farmer of Langlie, but it does not prove that he was John Nielsen's father. The rating stays Probable. A record naming John's parents, or the heirs, would settle it: the marriage of John Nielsen and Marit Johnsdatter (about 1812-1818), or a probate (skifte) after Niels in 1815-1816 in the Strinda og Selbu district court protocols, which are scan-only. ¶7

Searches that found nothing 1

Baptism not found - no register survives: Selbu has no surviving baptism register for 1747-1756. Book 16310 ends in 1737, book 16315 covers only 1740-1746, and book 16311 begins on 1 January 1757 (who also read every baptism from 1 Jan 1757 to 25 Mar 1758 without finding him). Born about 1746-1747 by his ages in 1801 (54) and at burial in 1815 (69), he falls in that gap, so his own baptism, and with it the names of his parents, is lost. What remains: the confirmation lists in book 16311 (about 1764-1775, now being read - they usually give the father or the farm), and probate (skifte) - the Strinda og Selbu probate protocols cover Selbu only from 1797, so a skifte after his death in 1815 or his widow's in 1832 could name heirs but not the parents. ¶13

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Lead, not read: the orchestrator reports that a word-box profile of Selbu bygdebok vol. 3, p. 718 (located by nb.no content search, not read) shows 'Nils, Olsen, 1748, 1815, G.1777, Olsdt.' followed by 'Pigarden, 1756, 1832'. That would mean a marriage in 1777 to an Olsdatter born 1756 who died in 1832. The death year 1815 agrees with this register entry. The marriage year is a lead only; Selbu marriages before 1816 are scan-only. ¶8
