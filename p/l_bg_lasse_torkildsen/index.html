Life story

Baptism: Baptised 20 May 1803 in Gulen parish (Brekke annex), son of Torkild Monssøn and Mari Torsteinsdatter, both of Indre Oppedal; the mother was churched (introduced) on 19 Jun 1803. Godparents (dåpsvitner): Mons Lassessøn (his grandfather), Torstein Olssøn, Hans Olssøn, Guttorm Henrikssøn, Mons Torkildsøn and two women named Giertrud, all of Indre Oppedal. Read from the Digitalarkivet index of the Gulen church book 1798-1808, p. 240-241, no. 39; the scan was not read. ¶2

Married 22 Jun 1828 as 'ungkarl og soldat' of Indre Oppedal, age about 25, to Ingeborg Monsdatter of Asheim. His father is named as Torkeld Monsøn of Indre Oppedal. Farmer at Asheim. Named as Kari's father at her baptism and at her 1848 marriage. Children include Kari (1828) and Marie (1832). ¶1

Corrections 1

Correction: The birth date moves from 'ABT 1803' to 'BEF 20 MAY 1803', from the baptism. Identity is clear: the 1828 marriage names his father as Torkeld Monsøn of Indre Oppedal and gives his age as about 25, and this is the only Lasse baptised to a Torkild Monssøn of Indre Oppedal. The mother link to Mari Torsteinsdatter is Confirmed by this baptism, which names both parents. ¶3
