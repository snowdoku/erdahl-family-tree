About the family

Wife (married about 1726) and from 1742 widow of Erich Pedersen of Tuntland (see his page), and mother of Peder Erichsen (see his page, b. about 1730); Probable, graded on her link to Peder, from the bygdebok alone. Hjelmeland: gardar og folk 2, Tuntland chapter (canvas 378): Erik Pederson (f ca. 1696, skifte 1742), married [1726]; 'Barn: Peder f ca. 1730'; 'Etter at ho [blei] enkje 1742, gifte Siri seg opp [att med] Ola Toreson [frå] Årdal 7'. Årdal 2, gnr 7 (canvas 520): Ola Toreson married 'enke Siri..., enke etter Erik Pederson'. Her patronymic was not recovered (about 200 patronymic forms tested on the marriage line, canvas 378 y 930-1030, without a hit). Probable rather than Confirmed: compiled source only, and the rebuild does not show whether Peder (b. about 1730) was born after the 1726 marriage to Siri in the text itself, only by date. Rebuilt from nb.no content-search word boxes (harvests and ar2.json, 3,011-word vocabulary); dates from the rebuild are given as ABT. ¶1a

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
