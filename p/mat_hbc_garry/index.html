About the family

First cousin of Harry. Death registration, BC 1999-09-023515: Verner Garreth Thomsen, cement-truck driver, of Hayman Road, Naramata, married, died at Penticton on 11 November 1999, aged 57, born 11 August 1942 at Naramata. His father was Verner Thomsen, born Denmark, and his mother Vera Hayter; the record gives her birthplace wrongly as British Columbia. He was cremated. He is the 'Garry Thomsen, son' who reported his father's death in 1987. ¶1
