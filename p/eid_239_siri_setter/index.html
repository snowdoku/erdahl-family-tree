About the family

Mother of Kari Torstensdatter: churched Easter Tuesday 1705 as 'Tosten Kiøsnes qv. Siri efter 1ste barn Kari' (16310 c22). Married Torsten Kjøsnes on the 3rd Sunday after Trinity (8 Jun) 1704, as Siri 'Setter' - from Sæther (canvas 19 right). Patronymic not given. A godmother 'Ingebor Setter[?]' at Kari's baptism may be her kin. Probable for the same reasons as her husband. ¶1

Parents added from the bygdebok, Probable: Selbu i fortid og nåtid vol. 6, Sæter (canvas 378) lists 'Siri, f. ca. 1680, [g.I m.] Torsten Jonsen Kjøsnes, [g.II m.] Hans Jonsen [Renå]' as the eldest child of Gunnar Olsen of Sæter (b. c.1650, d. 1726) and Goro (d. 1727). Vol. 7, Kjøsnes (canvas 318): Torsten Jonsen (b. c.1678, d. 1719) m. 1704 'Siri [...] Sæter, f. ca. 1680, d. 1744', who remarried Hans Jonsen Renå and moved; their daughter Kari (b. 1705) married Ola Gunnarsen Overvik. ¶10a

Her parents' probate read: skifte 19 Feb 1727 after Gunder Olsen Sætter (24750 fol. 1) lists 'Sigre Gunders datter Hans Rennaas Hustru' among the children he and Gjøroe Peders datter had together. If she is this Siri (the bygdebok says Siri of Sæter married first Torsten Kjøsnes and then Hans Jonsen Renå), her patronymic is Gundersdatter. Unproved: no record of the widow Siri Kjøsnes marrying Hans Renå has been read. ¶11a

Life story

Marriage entry re-read at full resolution (16310 canvas 19 right, top): 'Tosten Kiøsnes og Siri Setter', in a list of couples married on the 3rd Sunday after Trinity 1704. No patronymic is written for either, so this entry cannot settle whether she is the 'Sigri Gundersdtr., gift m/ Hans Renaam' of the 1727 Sætter probate (card 77804 c1973, re-checked from the saved image: heirs Ole Gundersen 31, Sigri married to Hans Renaam, Ingeborg widow of Peder Pedersen Kolseth, Kirsti married to Thomas Grimsen Rønsberg). Naming pattern, inference only: her daughter Kari named her own children Torsten and Siri (probate after Ole Gundersen Wigen, 1760). ¶4

The 1704 marriage entry was re-read at 1500 px (16310 c19 right page, 3 p.Trin 1704): 'copulerede... Tosten Kiøsnæs og Siri Setter'. No patronymic is written, so her father cannot come from this entry. ¶6

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the identification · 2 alternatives set aside · 1 search with no result · 8 open questions

The case for this identification 1

Re-read, no bridge: the Trinity Sunday 1724 marriage (16310 canvas 94 right, top) was fetched at full resolution. It reads '(4) Hans Renaa og Siri Garberg', the fourth of four couples; the bride is not called a widow ('Enke') and no patronymic or former husband is written. Canvases 93 and 94 left (Jan to May 1724) hold no betrothal list, so there is no fuller entry. The marriage therefore cannot show that Siri, widow of Torsten Kjøsnes, is the 'Sigre Gunders datter Hans Rennaas Hustru' of the 1727 Sæter probate. Gunnar Olsen Sæter and Goro Pedersdatter stay Probable as her parents. ¶12a

Alternatives considered and set aside 2

Lead (not linked): the probate after 'Gunder Olsen Sætter', Sæter, Selbu, 1727 (card 77804 c1973; read by the orchestrator) names his widow Goroe Pedersdatter and children Ole Gundersen 31 (on the farm), 'Sigri Gundersdtr., gift m/ Hans Renaam', Ingeborg (widow of Peder Pedersen Kolseth) and Kirsti (married Thomas Grimsen Rønsberg). Gunder Olsen is the farmer 'Gunder Olsen 55' at Sætter in the 1701 manntall. Siri 'Setter' (married Torsten Kjøsnes 1704) could be this Sigri only if Torsten died and she remarried Hans Renaa before 1727 - unproved; Torsten's burial has not been found. ¶2

Ruled out as a route to her identity: the probate after Hans Joensen Reinaaen (Renå), 25 Oct 1751 (Skifteprotokoll 3, 24753, fol. 418b, view 442) names his widow Kiersten Rogersdatter and two children of that marriage, Joen Hansen 12 and Siri Hansdatter 9. It does not mention an earlier wife Sigri/Siri or any stepchildren, so it neither proves nor disproves the lead that the Sætter daughter 'Sigri' who married Hans Renaa by 1727 was Siri, widow of Torsten Kjøsnes. ¶5

Searches that found nothing 1

Negative: the 77804 probate card index (1689-1742) has only two cards for Sæter in Selbu (cards 1973 and 1975). The first is the skifte of 1727 after Gunder Olsen Sætter (fol. 1): widow Goroe Pedersdatter; children Ole Gundersen 31, married, living on the farm; Sigri Gundersdatter, married to Hans Renaas; Ingeborg Gundersdatter, widow of Peder Pedersen Kolseth; and Kirsti Gundersdatter, married to Thomas Grimsen Rønsberg. The second is the skifte of 19 Feb 1727 after Peder Pedersen Kolsat (fol. 4): widow Ingeborg Gundersdatter; sons Peder 5 and Peder junior 2; guardian their Farbroder Ole Pedersen Kolsat. No Siri married to Torsten Kjøsnes, and no Kjøsnes child, is among Gunder Olsen's heirs. So she was not his daughter, unless she had died with no surviving issue, which her daughter Kari (living in 1773) rules out. Her parents are not in this index under Sæter. ¶9

Open questions and the next record to read 8

Biography gaps: her exact birth date (before 1686) and baptism predate the 1699 Selbu register. Her death, death place, burial and probate are not found; the 1727 Sætter probate (77804 card 1973) names a daughter Sigri married to Hans Renaa, but that link is unproved. The next checks are a full-resolution reading of the 1704 marriage entry (16310 canvas 19 R) for her patronymic, and the later 16310 entries (canvases 56-147) for a widow Siri Kjøsnes, which were read without the link being noticed. ¶3

Lead: the skifte after Gunder Olsen Sætter, 19 Feb 1727 (24750 fol. 1), names the widow Guroe Pedersdatter and their children Ole Gundersen 31, 'Sigre Gundersdatter Hans Rønaas' hustru', Ingeborg (widow of Peder Pedersen Kolset) and Kirsti (wife of Thomas Grimsen Rønneberg). If Siri Setter, who married Torsten Kjøsnes in 1704, was later the wife of Hans Rønaas, she would be Sigre Gundersdatter, and Gunder Olsen and Guro Pedersdatter her parents. Not linked: Torsten's death and a remarriage of a widow Siri to Hans Rønaas are not yet found. ¶7a

Next: 16310 marriages 1705-26 for 'Hans Rønaas' or 'Renaa'. ¶7b

Lead strengthened but not linked. (1) The 1727 skifte after Gunder Olsen Sætter (24750 fol. 1) calls all four children 'deres tilsammen avlede' with the widow Goroe Pedersdatter, so Sigre Gundersdatter, 'Hans Re[n]aas' hustru', was Guro's daughter too. (2) A Siri Gundersdatter, 'en gammel Kvinde fra Renaaen', was buried on the 14th Sunday after Trinity 1744 aged 64 (16315 c64, left column), so born about 1680. That fits Siri Setter, married 1704 and born before 1686. (3) But the only marriage of a Hans Renaas found in 16310 is at Trinity 1724, 'Hans Renaas og Siri Garberg[?]' (c94 R), and the bride's byname reads Garberg, not Kjøsnes. No death of Torsten Kjøsnes has been found. So nothing yet shows that the Kjøsnes wife of 1704-05 was widowed and remarried. Not linked: the candidate parents (Gunder Olsen Sætter and Guro Pedersdatter) stay a lead. ¶8a

Would settle it: Torsten Kjøsnes's burial (1705-24), or a 1705-24 baptism or churching at Garberg that names Siri as Torsten's wife or widow. ¶8b

Test record: the 77804 probate card after Gunnar Olsen Sæter (1726) or Goro (1727). ¶10b

Next: that marriage in 16310 (c. 1719-26). ¶11b

Would settle it: a record placing Torsten's widow at Garberg 1719-24, or a probate after Siri (Hans Renå's wife, d. before about 1738 when he remarried, or the Siri Gundersdatter of Renåen buried 1744 aged 64) naming Kari Torstensdatter as her heir; no Renå card for her in 77804 (1727-42) and no Renå/Siri entry in the 24753 register (1742-60). ¶12b
