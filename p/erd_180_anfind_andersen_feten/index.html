About the family

Parents: the guardian 'Farbroder Ambiørn Andersen Ulvestad' identifies him with the 'tredie Søn Anfind Andersen, myndig og tilstede' of the probate of Christi Anfindsdatter of Ulvestad, 14 Apr 1761, whose eldest son was Ambiørn (Stabiørn) Andersen Ulvestad. He was godfather 'Anfind Andersøn' of Ulvestad, with Ambiørn Ulvestad, at Pentecost 1761 (11566 c56). ¶3

His father's probate: 'Tredie Søn Anfind Andersen, myndig og nærværende' at the probate of Anders Truelsen of Ulvestad, 30 Apr 1753 (Ytre Sogn 7a fol. 162b, No. 12), so he was of age (born before about 1728) in 1753. His parents' link is now a primary record (father's and mother's probates). ¶4

Life story

Leikanger 1761: 'Anfind Ulvestads qv[inde]' was churched at Tjugum on 22 Nov 1761 (11566 c61 R), which implies a birth about early October 1761. That fits his daughter Christi, '6 aar gl.' in Aug 1768. No baptism of a child of his was found in the Tjugum and Leikanger lists of Jul-Nov 1761 (c58-c61); the 3 Oct 1761 baptism of Brithe Anfindsdatter is at Bachen, not Ulvestad. A second Anfind lived at Ulvestad: Anfind Hansen 'under Ulvestad', whose daughter Mari was baptised on 13 Dec 1761 with Anfind Andersøn Ulvestad as godfather and whose wife was churched on 24 Jan 1762 (c63 L, c64 R); the two must be kept apart. Christi's link to him stays Probable until a record names her at both Feten and Esse. ¶5

Probate 16 Aug 1768 at Feeten in Tjugum skipreide after Anfind Andersen, between his widow Britte [Endres?] datter and their four children: eldest son Anders Anfindsen [14?], second son Ole Anfindsen [7?], eldest daughter 'Christi Anfindsdatter 6 aar gl.', youngest daughter [Giøre?] Anfindsdatter 2; the children's lawful paternal uncle (Farbroder) Ambiørn Andersen Ulvestad; the widow's guardian (lagverge) Ole [Endresen?] Torsnes; appraisers Ole Pedersen Wangsnes and Jacob Larsen ibid. (Ytre Sogn 8b p. 528, No. 36). Sons' ages partly unclear at this zoom. ¶1

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Probable as father of Christi Anfindsdatter: his daughter Christi was 6 in Aug 1768 (born about 1762), which matches exactly the 1801 (38) and 1815 (52) census ages of Christi Anfindsdatter, wife of Gjest Gjestsen at Esse in the same Tjugum skipreide; she named her elder son Anfind (b. about 1794). The two other Anfind probates in the district with children of this age (Anfind Nielsen Vangsnes 1774; Anfind Gundersen Njøs 1784) have no Christi. No record yet names both Feten and Esse; her 1780s marriage or the baptism of about 1762 (Leikanger 11566, c. 1762-63) would settle it. ¶2

The case for this identification 2

Supporting: his children Ole Anfindsen Feten, 18, and Christi Anfindsdatter ibid., 16, were confirmed at Tjugum on 30 Nov 1777 (Leikanger 11567, canvas 233, fol. 229). This carries the two children of his 1768 probate forward at Feten to 1777. It does not tie Christi to Gjest Gjestsen of Esse, so his link to Christi Anfindsdatter stays Probable. ¶6

Supporting/caution: the 1790 marriage (Leikanger 11567, 25 Sep and 11 Oct 1790) gives the bride as 'Christie Anfinds Datter Tvedt', not Feten, and names no father. Rival checked: the probate after Anfind Monsen of Tveiten, Tjugum skipreide (Ytre Sogn 9b p. 1255-1256, about 1780-81) names his widow Kari Anfindsdatter and 'deres sammen avlede Børn, 3 Sønner og 2 Døttre': Mons Anfindsen (of age), Anfind Anfindsen, Sjur Anfindsen 20, Anna Anfindsdatter 15 and Sigrie Anfindsdatter 11. There is no Christi, although a daughter born about 1761-63 would have been 17-20 and listed. So the 1790 bride 'Christie Anfindsdatter Tvedt' was not a daughter of the Tveiten Anfind; most likely she was in service at Tveiten (unmarried women were entered at the farm where they served). Two cautions remain: the names of her later children (Anfind, Anna, Kari, Sjur) echo the Tveiten family (Kari, Sjur), which might point to kinship with Kari Anfindsdatter; and the step Feten (1768 probate, 1777 confirmation) to Tveiten (1790) is a change of farm with no record naming her parents. Kari Anfindsdatter Tveit's own probate (Ytre Sogn protocol 14 = source 24174, p. 549b, 1809-13) would list her heirs and test this. Graded on his link to Christi Anfindsdatter: stays Probable. ¶7

Corrections 1

Correction: the Christi Anfindsdatter of Feten belongs to another family, the household of Marcus Marcussen. (1) Leikanger ministerialbok 11568, canvas 93 right (fol. 91), Tjugum sogn, about 19-22 June 1797: 'Copuleret Unge-Karl Marcus Marcussen Thue med Pigen Christi Anfinds Dr Fethen'. (2) On 24 Sep 1797 at Tjugum (11568, canvas 98 left) two girls were baptised one after the other. The first was Anna, 'Forældrene: Giest Gjestsen Esse og Christi Anfinds D.', with godparents Sieur Kieldsen Meneß, Ellend Andersen Esse, Mari Olsdatter ibid, Britha Kieldsdatter Toftedahl and Anna Marcusdatter Esse: no one from Feten or Ulvestad. The second was Giøri, 'Forældrene: Marcus Marcussen Moland og Christi Anfinds D.', with godparents Ole Moland, Endre Thorsen Fethen, Britha Endresdatter ibid [Feten], Anna Johannesdatter Ulvestad and Guri Hansdatter Moland. Britha Endresdatter of Feten is the name of the widow in the 1768 Feten probate, and Ulvestad was Anfind Andersen's home farm. The name Giøri repeats the Feten probate's youngest daughter '[Giøre?] Anfindsdatter 2'. (3) In the 1801 census Marcus Marcussen's wife Christi Anfindsdatter is 38 (Esse, household 07). That is the same age as the Feten child (6 in Aug 1768, 16 in Nov 1777). She died before 30 Apr 1813, when her probate was held (Ytre Sogn 14 fol. 538a; daughters Giøri 15 3/4 and Sigrid 9 3/4). Gjest Gjestsen's wife, by contrast, was 'Christie Anfinds Datter Tvedt' when she married in 1790, and she was still alive in 1815. The only Christi Anfindsdatter in the 1768 Feten probate and the 1777 Feten confirmation is therefore, on present evidence, the Christi who married Marcus Marcussen, not Gjest's wife. He is therefore no longer linked as the father of Christi Anfindsdatter. His daughter is recorded as Christi Anfindsdatter, wife of Marcus Marcussen. Kari Anfindsdatter of Tveit's probate: Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll 14, No. 28, p. 549b-550a, 13 May 1813 (Digitalarkivet 24174 view 552), read at the full viewer resolution (3327 px; the scan is bilevel, so finer crops add nothing). She was the widow of Anfind Monsen. Her heirs are her children: the eldest son Mons Anfindsen of Tvedt, who was dead and is represented by his widow Sigrid and his under-age children, 'Anfind Monsen' and a daughter '... Monsdatter'; the 2nd son Anfind Anfindsen (Grindedal); the 3rd son Sieur Anfindsen; and 'eneste Datter' (the only daughter) Anna Anfindsdatter, married to... Nielsen [Hollestad?]. There is no Christi. Taken with Anfind Monsen's probate of 1780-81 (five children, no Christi), this shows that the 1790 bride 'Christie Anfinds Datter Tvedt' was not a daughter of the Tveiten couple. ¶8
