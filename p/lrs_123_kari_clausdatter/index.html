About the family

Parents: Claus Hermundsen of Indre Takle (d. before Sep 1750) and Kari Johansdatter. His probate No. 68 (Ytre Sogn 7a fol. 17a, Sep 1750) names 'Ældste Datter [K/M]ari Clausdatter gift med Iver Gullacksen Engesætter' among the children begotten in the marriage with the widow Kari Johansdatter, and the widow's own lifetime division No. 69 (fol. 17b-18a, same session) names her again among 'hendes Børn' as wife of 'Iver Engesætter'. Siblings: Claus Claussen (Midt-Takle), Hermund Claussen (Nesse), Hans, Ole and Johannes Claussen, Magdeli Clausdatter (m. Niels [Horager?]) and Brite Clausdatter (m. Rasmus Bendixsen). She was the eldest daughter. The first letter of her name in both 1750 entries is not certain (K or M), consistent with her being 'Kari' in 1774 and 'Mari' in 1799. ¶8

Life story

Widow (Enke) of Iver Gullacksen of Engesetter, named in his probate of 12 Mar 1774 (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll A 9, p. 359, no. 8). ¶1

Name: the 1774 probate of her husband Iver Gullacksen calls the widow Kari Clausdatter, and her own 1799 probate calls her Mari Clausdatter. They are one woman. Both probates are held at Engesetter, and both give the widow's patronymic as Clausdatter. The 1799 heirs are exactly the seven children of the 1774 probate, in the same order and with consistent places and spouses: Hermund of Ytre Brekke; Tosten; Jon; Gullow/Gullach; Kari, wife of Iver Larsen/Lassesen Haugeland; Agathe, who is linked both times to Lasse Johannesen Riisnes (an appraiser in 1774, her late husband by 1799); and Ingeborg, 12 1/2 in 1774 and married to Ole Olsen Engeseter by 1799. Both scans have been re-read, and each clerk wrote his form plainly: the 1774 clerk writes the widow's K like that of the daughter Kari on the same page, and the 1799 clerk writes an M unlike the K of Karie on his page. The difference is therefore in the records, not in the reading. Which form is correct is not settled. Kari is kept as the name because it is the earlier record; Mari is the 1799 form. A burial entry would decide it. ¶6

Probate: on 14 Apr 1799 the probate court held lawful probate (no. 21) on the farm Engesæter, Evenvik prestebol (glebe farm), Lavik skipreide and Brekke parish, Ytre Sogn, after the late kårkone (a woman living on a retirement pension from the farm; reading of the word uncertain) Mari Clausdatter, between 'hendes efterlevende Børn' (her surviving children), 4 sons and 3 daughters: I. eldest son Hermund Iversen of Ytre Brække, of age and present; II. second son Tosten Iversen Engeseter, of age, living on the farm Engeseter; III. third son John Iversen of Høje(?), the following word not read; IV. fourth son Gullach Iversen of Hougland, of age and present; V. eldest daughter Karie Iversdatter, dead, who left a daughter Marithe(?) Iversdatter, 20, for whom her father Iver Lassesen Hougland was appointed guardian; VI. second daughter Agathe Iversdatter, apparently widow (word uncertain) of the late lagrettemann (lay juror) Lasse Johannesen Riisnes, whose guardian (laugverge) was her brother Hermund Iversen Ytre Brække, present; VII. third daughter Ingeborg Iversdatter, married to Ole Olsen Engeseter, who was present. Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll 11b (1791-1799), fol. 546; read from the saved full-resolution image of view 152 (opening, heirs I-II), (count of sons, heirs III-VI) and audit (all heirs re-read, heir VII at the head of the second column). ¶4

Death: before 14 Apr 1799, the date of her probate, at Engesætter. No burial has been searched for. ¶5

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 3

Speculative mother of Torstein Iversen (b. about 1734). The probate calls them his children 'tilsammen avlede' (begotten together) in an honourable marriage, which suggests Kari's, but the children's span of about 27 years would put her near 46 at Ingeborg's birth if she bore Hermund, so an earlier wife is still possible. Would decide it: p. 360 of the probate (guardians may be described as maternal kin), a probate after Kari Clausdatter, or Torstein's baptism. ¶2

Confidence: raised from Speculative to Probable. On p. 359 of the 1774 probate the seven children are those Iver Gullacksen and the widow Kari Clausdatter 'udi et ærligt Ægteskab tilsammen avlede' (begot together in an honourable marriage). The arvefald on p. 359 gives 'hver Søn udi Fædrene Arv og Modergave' (each son, in paternal inheritance and mother's gift) the same 16 rd 4 ß, and each daughter 8 rd [2] ß; on p. 360 (top half) the eldest son Hermund Iversen Ytre Brekke's lot is headed the same way. The mother's gift thus went equally to all the sons, Hermund included, which fits Kari being mother of all of them (though a widow could also make a gift to stepchildren), and no heir of an earlier wife, no separate morsarv and no earlier marriage is mentioned in anything read (p. 359 re-read by audit). ¶3a

Confidence: raised from Probable to Confirmed as mother of Torstein Iversen (see his page). Her own probate of 1799, a primary record read from the scan, names Tosten Iversen Engeseter as her son among 'hendes efterlevende Børn' (her surviving children). This agrees with her husband's probate of 1774, which calls all seven children begotten by the two of them together. Inferred: stepchildren were not legal heirs of a stepmother, so the division of her own estate among all seven is strong evidence that they were her children, not stepchildren. Her given name differs (Kari in 1774, Mari in 1799), but the match of all seven heirs makes the two women one. The span of the children's births (Hermund before about 1734, Ingeborg about 1761) still puts her at about 45-50 at the last birth. That is unusual but not a contradiction, and both records name her as the mother. ¶7

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Caution: the children span about 27 years (Hermund, older than Torstein b. about 1734, to Ingeborg, 12 1/2 in 1774, b. about 1761), so if she bore them all she was very young at the first or about 45-47 at the last; this is possible but at the edge, and an unrecorded earlier wife cannot be excluded. Not read: the lower half of p. 360 and p. 361 (the remaining lots, including Tosten's). ¶3b

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Would confirm: Torstein's baptism (about 1734), a probate after Kari Clausdatter naming these children, or p. 361. ¶3c
