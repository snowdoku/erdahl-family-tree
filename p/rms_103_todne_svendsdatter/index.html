About the family

NEW ANCESTOR, graded on her link to her daughter Asbor Evindsdatter (see her page). In the oppgitt skifte of 6 Oct 1736 at Nordre Askland (Skifteprotokoll nr. 9 fol. 134b, read on the scan), Todne Svendsdatter and her second husband Gunsteen Olsøn hand over her share, and the children she had 'med forrige Mand Ewind Jørgensøn' are named, among them 'Asbør Ewindsdatter gift med Aadne Larsøn Søndre Ascheland'. One of the two lagrettemenn was Salve Svendsøn of Nordre Askland, whom card 78 calls 'bror til kona' (the wife's brother) in 1729. Her second husband Gunsteen Olsen's estate was held 30 Jun 1739. Her parents, Svend Ormsen of Askland (estate 1705) and Asbor Tallaksdatter (estate 1707), come from the archivist's note on card 79, so they are Probable. ¶1

Siblings: the 1705 skifte after her father Svend Ormsøn (Skp. 2 fol. 147a) names her brother Salve and her sisters Anløv and Birgitha. ¶2
