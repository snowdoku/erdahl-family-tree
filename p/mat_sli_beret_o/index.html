About the family

Oline Slind's great-grandmother. Named as the mother, 'Beret Ols Datt:', residence Slinden, in the Selbu baptism of 26 December 1816 of her son Ole Gundersen, born 10 December 1816, the father being Gunder Jorgenssen. ¶1

Father: Ole Pedersen Saether (see his page), grenadier, of the farm Saether in Selbu, who died before about 1796. Link rated Probable for the reasons above. Her mother had remarried Reinal Torstenssen by about 1795, and Reinal - 'Reinholt Slind.' - was already a godfather at Slinden on Exaudi Sunday 1792, which explains how Beret came to be raised at Slinden. ¶10

Life story

Baptism, Probable: she is identified as 'Berthe', baptised in Selbu on the 14th Sunday after Trinity, 9 September 1792, daughter of Ole Pedersen Saether and Ragnille Jonsdatter (Selbu ministerialbok 1757-1801, scan canvas 148 left; godparents Peder Aunet, Haldor Kjosnes, Anne Aunet, Maren Saether, Doreth Vigen[?]). Birth date not given; born probably in the weeks before the baptism. ¶8

Her birth of about 1793, from her stated age of 8 in February 1801, replaces the wave-3 BEF 1800, which was derived only from the fact that she bore a child in 1816. ¶6

Her parentage, which wave 3 left blank, now has a candidate solution resting on her own children's names rather than on a bare name-and-date fit. The 1801 census of Selbu, rural residence 0083 Slinden, household 7, contains 'Beret Olsdatter, 8, Deres born, Ugift' in the household of REINAL TORSTENSSEN, 30, and his wife RANGNIL JONSDATTER, 34, whose marriage the census records as 'Hans 1te haendes 2dt aegteskab' - HIS first marriage, HER second. The two younger children of that household are Ane Reinaldsdatter 5 and Ole Reinaldssen 2; Beret alone is an OLSDATTER. She is therefore Rangnil Jonsdatter's daughter by her first husband, a man named Ole who had died before about 1795. ¶3

Death and burial, Probable: 'Beret Olsdatter', aged '74 a' (74 years), died 24 August and was buried 2 September 1866, Selbu burial register 1859-1868, p. 192 no. 56 (index; scan not read). Residence and spouse are not given in the index. For: the name, the calculated birth year 1792, which agrees with her baptism of September 1792, and the parish. The other candidate, 'Beret Olsdr', 68, calculated birth 1795, buried 6 April 1863 (p. 181), fits less well. Reading the scan for a residence (Aune or Slinden) or a 'widow of Gunder' remark would settle it. ¶11

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Rated Confirmed for her own identity and date - she bore a child in 1814 and is the Beret Olsdatter of Slinden named in four baptisms - and the MATERNITY is rated Probable on the reasoning above. No document states that the Beret Olsdatter who married Gunder Jorgensen is the Beret Olsdatter of household 7. The record that would close it is the Selbu marriage register for about 1813, read on the scan, which gives the bride's father; failing that, the probate (skifte) of her father Ole in the Strinda og Selbu sorenskriveri protocols of the 1790s, which would list her by name among his heirs. ¶5

The case for this identification 2

Why this is more than a name-and-year coincidence. Beret Olsdatter and Gunder Jorgensen named their children, in order, Anne (1814), Ole (1816), Jorgen (1819), REINALD (1822), Anne (1825), RAGNILD (1828) and Peder (1833). Anne is Gunder's mother Ane Gundersdatter; Jorgen is Gunder's father Jorgen Olssen; Ole is the name of Beret's own dead father, which her patronymic independently requires; RAGNILD is Rangnil Jonsdatter; and REINALD is Reinal Torstenssen, the stepfather who raised her. Reinald and Ragnild are both uncommon names in Selbu, and the only household in the parish that holds both of them is the one in which a Beret Olsdatter of exactly the right age was living in 1801. A naming pattern that reaches into a stepfather's name is not the sort of thing chance produces. ¶4

Why 'Berthe' of 1792 is Beret of 1801. (1) Name: Berthe, Berte, Birte, Berit and Beret are all forms of Birgitta; Danish-trained clerks wrote the Danish form Berthe/Berte for the spoken Selbu Berit/Beret, and the same register writes 'Berte And.' as a mother in 1790. (2) Parents: her patronymic Olsdatter requires a father Ole, and her mother in 1801 is Rangnil (Ragnhild) Jonsdatter in her SECOND marriage; the 1792 parents are Ole Pedersen and Ragnhild Jonsdatter, she a maiden (P[ige]) at the betrothal of 5 April 1792, so this was her first marriage. (3) Age: baptised 9 September 1792, she was 8 years 5 months old on the census day of 1 February 1801, where she is entered as 8; her burial in 1866 at 74 gives a calculated birth year of 1792. (4) Exclusion: read every entry in the register from 1 January 1790 to the 17th Sunday after Trinity 1792 and found no other Ole x Ragnhild Jonsdatter couple and no other daughter of such a couple; the one other Ragnhild Jonsdatter marrying in 1792 (of Berge, m. Thomas Jonsen Marstad) is a different woman. (5) Naming: her children include Ole (1816) and Peder (1833), the given name and patronymic of Ole Pedersen. Rated Probable, not Confirmed, because no single record ties the Ragnhild of 1792 to the Rangnil of Slinden in 1801, and the register after September 1792 has not been read for a second couple of the same names. ¶9

Corrections 2

Correction: birth was carried as ABT 1793 at Slinden. The year is moved to about 1792 (baptism 9 September 1792; burial age 74 in 1866, calculated 1792; census age 8 on 1 February 1801 allows 1792). The place Slinden was an assumption from the 1801 residence; in 1792 her father was of Saether and her mother of Aune, so the place is given as Selbu only. ¶12

Correction: card cites the 1866 burial as page 192 serial 1; the index record page gives serial no. 56 (re-read 13 Oct 2026). ¶13

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

A near-miss recorded so that nobody else chases it. The Sovlvskatten of 31 August 1816 enters a 'Bereth Olsdat., Myndtling' - a ward under guardianship, that is, an heiress whose property was administered for her - at a farm Aunet, jointly with an Ole Olsen, also a myndling. Since Gunder Jorgensen moved his family to Aune a few years later, this looked like the explanation of how he got a farm. It is not: that record belongs to 'Thydalen Annex sogn', the Tydal annex parish, a different Aunet twenty-five miles up the valley, while Gunder's Aune is in Selbu main parish. The lead is dead and the reason is recorded. ¶7

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Nothing more about her has been established. Her marriage to Gunder Jorgensen, which would name her father, falls in the years the Selbu church-book transcription covers only thinly, and a search of the index for a marriage of a Gunder Jorgensen in Selbu between 1808 and 1818 returns nothing at all. Her birth is set only by the fact that she bore a child in 1816. The record that would break this wall is the Selbu marriage register for about 1812-1816, read on the scan rather than through the index. ¶2
