About the family

What makes that candidate interesting is the naming pattern at Kongshaug itself. Across the whole nineteenth century the farm's family alternates Esten and Tore in the Norwegian manner of naming sons after grandfathers: Esten Thoresen, born 1836 in Kornstad, was a freeholder (gaardbruger selveier) and fisherman at Kongshaug in the 1865 and 1875 censuses and still there in 1900 as a 'føderådsmand', living on a retirement agreement from the farm; his son was Tore Martin Eisteinsen, born 1865, and his grandson Esten Toresen, born 1892. An Esten Toresen dying about 1821 fits the generation above all of them, and would also be a natural father for a Jacob born about 1785-1792. The 1836 Esten Thoresen is far too young to be Jacob's father and is recorded here only so that nobody mistakes him for one. ¶13

Brother (Probable): Thore Estensen, farmer (gaardmand) at Kongshaug, Kornstad, aged 33 in the 1825 census (born about 1792), with wife Kirstina Noesdatter, was bringing up Jacob's daughter Berit Jacobsdatter (10) in 1825. Thore baptised a son Esten at Kornstad on 16 Nov 1822 (born 26 Oct; the child died 23 Dec 1822 and was buried 20 Apr 1823 as 'Esten Toresen, Kongshoug, 8 Uger') and a daughter Marith on 2 May 1824 (born 14 Apr). Both entries were read on the scans of the Kornstad book 1820-1830. Naming a first son Esten fits a father named Esten for both Thore and Jacob; it is not proof. ¶15

Life story

Tolga parentage stays unproven. The Rød family (Esten Amundsen and Marit Jacobsdatter, sons Jacob baptised 5 Jul 1789 and Thore baptised 13 May 1792) still matches the 1865 census entry for Thore Estensen, born 1792 in Tolga, at Kongshaug. But the Rød Jacob appears to be the Jacob Estensen of Røe, 33, who married Marit Jacobsdatter in Tolga on 27 Dec 1822, and the Tufsingdal marriage no longer helps. Thore may have come from Rød without Jacob being his full brother from there. No parents are added. ¶20

No Jacob Estensen appears in the 1801 Kvernes census index. Other Jacob Estensens in Nordmøre (Stangvik/Sandvik born 1797, Tingvoll/Møkkelbostad) do not fit well. ¶5

The Tolga marriage of 13 Aug 1809 was read on the scan (Tolga ministerialbok 1786-1814, p. 259): 'Ungkarl Jacob Estensen Tufsingdahl, og Pige Marit Thoresdatter Nøren', marriage sponsors (forlovere) Bersvend Arnesen Lilleøyen and Jon Knudsen Holm. It gives no ages or parents, and no Esten or Thore stands as sponsor. Because his Kornstad wife's patronymic reads Gorisdatter rather than Thoresdatter (1815 and 1818 scans), this marriage is not accepted as his. The Tufsingdal Jacob was also already a godfather in Tolga in 1798 (index), so he was probably born before about 1780. ¶19

Kvernes baptisms with a father Jacob Estensen: Esten (born 28 Aug 1812, father living at Hoset), and Barbro (born 15 Apr 1815; father 'Jacob Estensen [garbled farm name]', mother 'Marithe ?sd.'). Later ones: Berit Guri (1834) and Ingeborg (1836), whose mother is Ragnild Antonsdatter/Andersdatter of Dale(n). It is not proven these are all one man. ¶3

The Barbro baptized 30 Apr 1815 (father 'Jacob Estensen [garbled]', mother 'Marithe Orisd.') is therefore Tørri's full sister, with the mother's name misread as Orisd. for Gorrisd. By 1834-1836 Jacob had children with Ragnild Antonsdatter/Andersdatter, so Marith had probably died or the couple had separated. That would explain why Tørri was fostered at Tungset by 1825. Kongshaug farm is covered in Averøy bygdebok vol. 1 (Kvernes sokn), which is readable only from Norway on nb.no. ¶7

The exact next sources: (1) the scanned Kornstad local-parish book 1820-1830, burials, pages 282-283, entry no. 4 of 1822, to read the age and farm of Esten Toresen; (2) the scanned 1825 census page for Kvernes, rural residence 013 Kongenshvig, which carries the head's first name that the index could not read; (3) the scanned Kornstad parish book 1789-1819, source 16097, read for baptisms at Kongshaug about 1785-1795 to find Jacob's own baptism and his brother's; (4) the Nordmøre sorenskriveri probate protocols for Kvernes, where a settlement for an Esten of Kongshaug would name all his children; (5) Averøy bygdebok volume 1 (Kvernes sokn) under Kongshaug, on nb.no and readable only from Norway. ¶14

A named candidate for his father, offered as a lead and not as a finding. The Kornstad burial register records an 'Esten Toresen' who died on 23 December (1821) and was buried on 20 April 1822 - a winter death and a spring burial, the normal pattern in Nordmøre when the ground was frozen. He is burial no. 4 of 1822, on pages 282-283 of the Kornstad local-parish book 1820-1830. He is the only Esten whose death is recorded in Kornstad in the years when Jacob Estensen's father would have been dying. The online index gives neither his age nor his farm, so the identification cannot be made from it. ¶12

In the 1825 census a household at 'Kongenshvig' (possibly Kongshaug) is headed by an unnamed '... Estensen', 33, with Kristine Noesdatter 28, Maria Estensdatter 2 and Berit Jacobsdatter 10. Berit may be the Barbro born 1815, which would mean Jacob's children were living with relatives in 1825 (Speculative). ¶4

New evidence on his family at Kongshaug, though his own parents are still not proven. The 1825 census household at 'Kongenshvig' in Kvernes (rural residence 013, matrikkel/gnr. 21, census district 1, household 1) had just four people: a head whose first name the transcribers could not read, entered only as '... Esstensen', aged 33; Kristine Nielsdatter, 28; Maria Estensdatter, 2; and Berit Jacobsdatter, 10. Berit Jacobsdatter, born about 1815, is almost certainly the Barbro baptized 30 Apr 1815 to Jacob Estensen and Marith Gorrisdatter - Tørri's full sister. The toddler Maria Estensdatter is the head's own daughter, so the head's patronymic really was Estensen and he was not Jacob himself. ¶8

The reading that follows (Probable, not proven): Kongshaug was the farm of a family descended from an Esten, and in 1825 a second son of that Esten - a brother of Jacob, aged about 33, so born about 1792 - was running it and bringing up Jacob's daughter Berit. That would explain why Tørri, born 1818, was being fostered at Tungset by 1825: the children of Jacob and Marith were dispersed among relatives, which fits Marith having died or the couple having parted before Jacob took up with Ragnild Antonsdatter of Dale by 1834. ¶9

Kristine Nielsdatter stayed on the farm: the only Kongshaug entry in the whole indexed Kvernes register is 'Kirstine Nielsdtr' of Kongshaug, recorded as a mother on 27 Jan 1833. That is the same woman as the 1825 census wife, and it confirms the household continued there. ¶10

Named as Tørri's father in her 1838 marriage (the index puts him in the 'groom's mother' field by mistake). A Jacob Estensen had children in Kvernes 1812-1836; not proven to be the same man. ¶1

The 1838 marriage names him 'Jacob Estens Kongstong', which is probably a misreading of Kongshaug. The name was indexed in the groom's-mother field by mistake, as Historisk befolkningsregister also notes. ¶2

Tolga lead tested (13 Oct 2026): the Tolga marriage register (source 9222, pp. 384-385, scan read) records Jacob Estensen of Røe, 33, marrying Marit Jacobsdatter of Røe, 26, in church on 27 Dec 1822; marriage witnesses (forlovere) Esten Ingebretsen [farm read as Aaseren, uncertain] and Esten Olsen Røe. The register has no column for parents or birthplace, and none is written. Røe is Rød and age 33 fits the Jacob aged 11 at Rød in 1801, son of Esten Amundsen, but that is inference. Either way, this Tolga man was living and marrying at Røe in 1822 and, by the index, was buried in Tolga in July 1827 (gaardmand, Røe, age 37), while our Jacob was at Kongshaug in Kornstad with children in 1815-1818 and again 1834-1836. The Rød family of Tolga is therefore very probably not his family, and the 1801 Rød household should be dropped as a lead. ¶18

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Confirmed as Tørri's father. The scanned Kornstad parish book (source 16097) records her birth on 20 Apr 1818 to 'Jacob Estensen Kongshaug' and his wife Marith Gorrisdatter. Her 1893 burial gives her birthplace as Kongshaug. ¶6

The case for this identification 1

Why his parents cannot yet be named: there is no Jacob Estensen in the 1801 census index for Kvernes, and a search of every Esten recorded in Kvernes and Kornstad between 1780 and 1830 (15 hits in the parish registers, the 1801 and 1825 censuses and the 1816 silver tax) produced no Esten at Kongshaug and no Esten who can be shown to be his father. The Kornstad parish book 1750-1789 (Digitalarkivet sources 16095 and 16096) is scanned but not indexed, and the farm Kongshaug has only one indexed record in the entire register. ¶11

Searches that found nothing 1

1801 census (negative): neither Jacob nor Thore Estensen, nor any adult Esten, appears in the 1801 census for Kvernes prestegjeld, which includes the Kornstad farms. The only Esten is Esten Pedersen, born 1785, a son at Christvig. Kongshaug in 1801 held Anders Pedersen (80), farmer, and the landless cottager Elling Tronsen (46), with their families. So the Esten family probably came to Kongshaug after 1801, and Jacob's and Thore's baptisms may not be in Kornstad. ¶16

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Lead only, not a finding: the only 1801 household in Norway's census index with brothers Jacob (11) and Thore (9) Estensen is at Rød in Tolga, Hedmark: Esten Amunsen (43), farmer, and Marit Jacobsdatter (38), with sons Knud 19, Jacob 11, Thore 9 and Jon 2. The ages and names fit, and Thore's first daughter at Kongshaug was named Marith. But Tolga is far from Kornstad, Esten, Thore and Jacob Estensen are common names in Østerdalen, and no record yet shows anyone from Tolga moving to Kornstad. Settling it needs the moving-out lists (utflyttede) of Tolga about 1801-1812, or Thore's or Jacob's Kornstad marriage or burial entry with a birthplace. ¶17
