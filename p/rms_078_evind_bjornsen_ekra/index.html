Life story

Deed of 6 Nov 1714, registered 13 Nov 1714 (Pantebok A5, DA 17715 view 116): Evind Biørnsøn of Ekra in Gjøvdal sells odel land in Haugsjå, Treungen, inherited 'effter min Fader, nu sl. Biønne [E]vensøn'. ¶4

Estate settled 3 Apr 1724 at Ekra under Askland (Østre Råbyggelag Skifteprotokoll nr. 6 fol. 226a, read on the scan; probate cards 65/67). Widow Thodne Larsdatter (she handed over the estate). Heirs: Biønne Evindsøn (later of Askland, estate 13 Sep 1749), Tallak 16, Marg[ith] wife of Tergie Salvesøn Askland, Thargier wife of Ole Tollachsøn nordre Askland, Margitta wife of Halvor Jensøn Hugsiaa in Treungen. 6 1/2 skylds-kalveskinn of land. The archivist's back note (card 67) gives his father as Biønne Evensen Haugsjaa in Treungen (see deed registered 13 Nov 1714 for Haugsjaa) and a brother Biønne Biønnesen Harstveit, husband of Birgitha Wraalsdatter (estate 1713). That father is gen 10 and is left for the later-generation worker. ¶1
