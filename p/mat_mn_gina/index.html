About the family

Daughter of Anders A. Eidem and Brynhild Kulsetmo, and wife of her cousin Paul M. Eidem. Her Minnesota death certificate index entry (1967-MN-012398, died 5 May 1967 in Rice County) gives her mother's maiden name as KULSETHMOE, which is what attaches her to Brynhild. ¶3

Life story

Her birth in 1891, ten years before her parents' recorded Minnesota marriage of 6 May 1901, is unexplained; either Anders and Brynhild married first in Norway and repeated the ceremony on arrival, or the 1901 entry is a second marriage. Rated Probable for that reason. ¶7

1905 state census: Augusta Township, Lac qui Parle County, schedule page 691, line 24 - 'Gerza Eidem, F, 14, born Norway', the eldest child in Andrew (Anders A.) Eidem's household. Aged 14 in June 1905 she was born about 1891, agreeing with the 15 March 1891 on her Zoar Lutheran stone, and born in Norway rather than Minnesota. ¶4

Marriage: Lac qui Parle County certificate F13207, 'EIDEM, PAUL' and 'EIDEM, GURINE', 29 December 1913. ¶5

Named as the mother, with her maiden name given as 'Gena Eidem' and her birthplace as Norway, on the 1923 registration of her stillborn son (cert. 1923-15352), where she is aged 31. ¶6

Named with her maiden surname — which was also her married surname — as mother on six Social Security applications made by three of her children between 1942 and 1963: 'MOTHER'S FIRST NAME: GINA / MOTHER'S LAST NAME: EIDEM', the father on every one of them being PAUL EIDEM. She married a man of her own name. NARA AAD, NUMIDENT Application (SS-5) Files 1936-2007, rids 421612, 421682, 421686, 1896965, 755846, 3545646. ¶1

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 correction · 1 alternative set aside

Corrections 1

Correction: her father was NILS KLEGSETH, not Anders Andersson Eidem, who was her stepfather. She was born in 1891, ten years before Brynhild married Anders on 6 May 1901, and the biography on her mother's memorial says plainly that Brynhild's three daughters by Nils Klegseth 'all took the Eidem name from their stepfather'. This tree had her linked to Anders as a natural daughter, which the dates alone rule out. ¶8

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Not to be confused with the GINA EIDEM / GINA G. EIDEM / GINA I. EIDEM who married STEPHEN STEVENS of McHenry County, North Dakota and whose children Luella C. (b. 14 Jun 1912 Balfour ND), Roy Arnold and Ross Walter (both b. 10 Apr 1920) and Vivian Luverle (b. 12 Apr 1923 Bergen ND) are also in the file. Two women of the same unusual name in the same Norwegian-American diaspora, one in Minnesota and one in North Dakota; I checked and the children's birthplaces keep them apart. ¶2
