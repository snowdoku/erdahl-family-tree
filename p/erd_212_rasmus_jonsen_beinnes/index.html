About the family

Family detail: A 8 image 141 (Jul-Dec 1744 list, Olden skipreide), Røstøen 9 Oct 1744. The widow Marrit Olsdatter divided the estate of her late husband Christopher... of Røstøen. A share went to 'Sal: Synneve Christophersdatter, hendes efterlevende eene datter ved navn Marrite Joensdatter', 'som hendes fader Joen Beenes strax til sig annammede'. Guardianship protocol no. 3, p. 236 (pa00000001241767) has 'Joen Beenes' as guardian for this inheritance of 9 Oct 1744, but transcribes the girl as 'Marrite Rasmusdatter'. Joen Beenes is most likely his son Jon Rasmussen, who took over Beinnes in 1732. If so, Jon's first wife was Synneve Christophersdatter of Røstøen, dead by 1744. This is collateral detail and does not change any grade. ¶6

Life story

Guardianship protocol no. 2, p. 102 (scan read): 'Rasmus Joensen Beenes' was guardian for Gunilde Olsdatter Bruvold (inheritances of 28 May 1723, 27 Jun 1727 and 8 Oct 1728; relationship not stated), and 'efter Skiftebrev af dato 5 Octob 1743 har hand under Forvaring for sin Broder Halsteen Joensen, som nu skal være i Bergen' (6 rd). Protocol no. 3, p. 202 repeats it ('sin Broder', still unclaimed on 18 May 1751). So Rasmus had a brother Halsteen Joensen, and a skifte of 5 Oct 1743, probably after one of their parents (Jon...), gave Halsteen a 6 rd share. ¶4a

Aaland (Innvik 1932, Beinnes): 'Rasmus Jonson Sunde, [d.] f. 1750, b. 1708, n. b. 1721 p. 2 pd. 22 mk. * Anne Rasmusd., d. 72 a. g. 1750. Jon Rasmusson, d. 65 a. g. 1775, br. 1732.' So he came from Sunde in Olden, leased the bruk in 1708 and again in 1721, and his son Jon took it over in 1732. Aaland's 'f. 1750' (before 1750) for his death conflicts with the 1751 probate, where he is alive as the widower; the probate is preferred. A later 'Rasmus Jonsen, Bennes' probate (A 19, 1790-92) is probably his grandson, son of Jon Rasmussen (inference). ¶2

Probate after his wife Ane Rasmusdatter of Beenes (Beinnes), Olden skipreide, registered 18 May 1751 (Nordfjord skifteprotokoll A 11, fol. 160b-161a): present 'den Sal. Qvindes efterlevende Mand, nemlig Rasmus Joensen', and their three children together ('deres sammen avlede 3de Børn'): Joen Rasmussen, living here on the farm; 'Marithe Rasmusdatter gift med Lars Samuelsen Loen'; and Barbra Rasmusdatter, now 24. Read from the scan. ¶1

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Confirmed as father of Marthe Rasmusdatter (see her page), graded on his link to her: the 1751 probate names her as the child of Rasmus Joensen and Ane Rasmusdatter and as the wife of Lars Samuelsen Loen. Continuity to the woman in the tree: the 1772 probate after 'Marthe Rasmusdatter Loen' (A 15b fol. 768b) was held between her widower Lars Samuelsen and her daughter Anne Larsdatter, so it is the same couple, Lars Samuelsen of Loen and his wife Marthe Rasmusdatter, 21 years apart, with no rival Lars Samuelsen at Loen. Lars's first wife Ane Olsdatter was dead by early 1749 (A 10 fol. 335b), so Marthe married him between 1749 and May 1751. The other Beinnes household of 1714 (Rasmus Olsen x Christi Knudsdatter) is ruled out: Christi's probate of 1 Jun 1731 (A 6 fol. 505a) names only three children, Ole, Synnøve and Anne (22), no Marthe. ¶3

Searches that found nothing 1

Negative, and what A 8 is: Nordfjord 'skifteprotokoll' A 8 (source 24225, 1733-44, only about 144 images) is not a full probate protocol. It is a run of half-yearly 'Specification' lists, sent in by the sorenskriver, of the probates at which minors inherited. Each one gives date, farm, deceased, the minor heirs with their ages and shares, and the guardian. The Jul-Dec 1743 list (images 121-128, signed Totland 21 Dec 1743) has an Olden-skipreide run of 30 Sep-16 Oct 1743 (images 126R-127R: Eide, Abrekke, Mølden, Myklebust/Muri, Loen 3-4 Oct, Melkevoll, Muri). None is at Beinnes and none is dated 5 Oct. So the skifte of 5 Oct 1743, which left 6 rd for his brother Halsteen Joensen 'som nu skal være i Bergen', had no minor heirs and was not copied into A 8. The full text should be in the Nordfjord Arvebok or the court's original probate papers, which are not scanned. ¶5a
