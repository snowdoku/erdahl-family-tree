About the family

Mother of Johanne Joensdatter (see her page), Probable. In the 1801 census Joen Halvorsen, 75, is a widower after his first marriage, so he married only once. This 1762 betrothal of Joen Halvorsen Grimstvedt as a bachelor is that marriage. So Susanne is the mother of his children born after 1762: Christopher Joensen (about 1763) and Johanne (baptised 22 Jul 1770). The baptisms do not name mothers. She died before Feb 1801. Her probate (Øvre Telemark vestfjelske skifteprotokoller 25679-25682, 1790-1803) would confirm it and name her children. ¶2

Life story

Marriage: '44. d. 25 Jan. 1762 Trolovet paa Nordboe i Nissedahl unge Karl Joen Halvorsen Grimstvedt og Enken Susane Anders doettr. Cautions-Mænd: Jacob Nordboe og Alf Tvetan [Tvetene].' She was a widow (Enken) when she married the bachelor Joen Halvorsen of Grimstvedt. The name of her first husband is not given. Kviteseid ministerialbok 1754-1773 (source 7981), canvas 125 left page, entry no. 44. The first name is written with long s ('ſuſane'); 'Sifane' is a less likely reading. The wedding date in the copulation column was not read. ¶1

Marriage date: the copulation column beside the 25 Jan 1762 betrothal (7981 c125L, page 44, 1st entry) reads 'Copuleret i Nissedahls Kirke d. 14 Junii 1762'. So Joen Halvorsen Grimstvedt and the widow Susanne Andersdatter married on 14 Jun 1762 in Nissedal church. Neither column names her first husband. ¶3

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
