About the family

Children (baptized at Borgund): Jørgen Elias (28 Feb 1813, home-baptized, died in infancy), Peter Jørgen Martinus (14 Aug 1814, confirmed 1830), Elias Peter Olaus (1 Dec 1816) and Jørgen Olaus (16 Sep 1820). He is called a farmer (gaardbruger) in 1820. ¶5

Parentage (Probable): 'Joen', son of Jørgen Paulsen of Diub (Djup), was baptized 31 Oct 1779 at Borgund. This matches his age of 35 in the 1815 census exactly. A 'John Jørgensen' of 'Fiup' (a misreading of Diup) was confirmed 30 Jul 1797 at Borgund. He named his first son Jørgen Elias, after his father. The other Borgund candidate, Joen son of Jørgen Jetmundsen of Sandvik (baptized 1784), was confirmed at Sandvik in 1801 and is a different man. The Moldvær servant of 1801 (born about 1785) and the Haram Jon Jørgensen (born 1771) no longer fit. ¶7

Siblings, all baptized at Borgund with father Jørgen Paal(s)sen of Djub: Johan (24 Aug 1768), Synneve (18 Oct 1769), Siver (13 Feb 1771), Paal (11 Oct 1772; confirmed 1791 at Diup), Siver (15 Dec 1774), Ole (25 Feb 1776), Birthe (27 Apr 1777), Joen (31 Oct 1779) and Inger (22 Sep 1782). Ingebrigt Jørgensen, 22, a servant at Diup in 1801, may be another brother (not proven). ¶8

Parentage check (probate of Jørgen Paulsen, Diub, 1 February 1785; Sunnmøre sorenskriveri 3A 27, fol. 401a-402a): the heirs were the widow Ingeborg Siversdatter, a son Poul, a son about 5 years old, and a daughter Berthe. The son's name is written twice (in the list of heirs and in his share). Both times it is a short word with no g, which reads as 'Joen' rather than 'Jørgen', and the age fits Joen, baptized 31 October 1779. The scans are low-resolution black-and-white, though, so the reading is not certain. This supports the parentage link to Jørgen Paulsen without proving it, and the confidence stays as it is. A clearer image of the original protocol (Statsarkivet i Trondheim / Arkivverket) would settle it. ¶11

Life story

Speculative origins: a John Jørgensen b. ~1785, servant at Moldvær (1801 Borgund census), or a Jon Jørgensen b. 1771, confirmed at Haram 1789. ¶2

Married Elisabeth Pedersdatter of 'Sjaang' (Skjong) 17 Jan 1813 at Borgund. He was a bachelor living on Valderøy. The fathers are not given in the index. ¶3

In the 1815 census (30 Apr 1815) at Valdergaard/Valderøy: John Jørgensen 35, Elisabeth Pedersdatter 24 and son Peter Johnsen 1. This puts his birth at about 1779/80, which weakens the earlier 1801 candidate (servant at Moldvær, born about 1785). ¶4

In 1815 his household at Valdergaard also included Ole Olsen (51, retired farmer/kårmann), his wife Margrite Christensdatter and their daughter Olava, which suggests John took over Ole Olsen's holding. It also included the servant Malene Pedersdatter (30), probably Elisabeth's sister Mali (baptized 1782). ¶9

Death resolved: the 1824 burial (died 25 Oct, buried 28 Oct 1824, 'gb John Jørgensen Walderøen') is our John. The scanned Borgund klokkerbok 1816-1827 (p. 222, entry 28) appears to give his age as 44, not the 71 in the index. A probate (skifte) for John Jørgensen of Valderhaug, Valderøy was registered 10 Dec 1824 (Nordre Sunnmøre, 3A 14, fol. 162a). In 1825 'Elisabeth Pedersd., enke, Walderøen' married the bachelor Ole Leenert Gammelsen of Bjørge, which confirms she was widowed in 1824. ¶6

Probate: John Jørgensen's estate at Valderhaug, Valderøy, was registered (registreringsforretning, inventory) on 10 December 1824 by the clerk A. Blix for the district judge (sorenskriver) H. E. Kragh (Nordre Sunnmøre sorenskriveri, 3A 14, fol. 162a-163b). The heirs were his widow Lisbeth Pedersdatter and three sons, Peter Jørgen Martinus Johnsen (10), Elias Peter Olaus Johnsen (8) and Georg (Jørgen) Olaus Johnsen (4). The guardians (formyndere) for the sons were Mons Rasmussen Walderhaug, Lars[?] Hansen Skjong and Ole Siversen[?] Walderhaug; the merchant Elias Rønneberg was a creditor. At the settlement in 1825 the net estate came to about 95 speciedaler; the widow took half and each son 15 speciedaler. The skifte says nothing of John's parents, siblings or age. ¶10

Of Valderøy. Named as father on son Elias's 1832 confirmation. Also had a son Jørgen Elias (baptized 28 Feb 1813, died aged 3 months). ¶1
