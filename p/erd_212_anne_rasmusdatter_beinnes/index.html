Life story

Burial: 'Anne Rasmusdatter' of Beenæs (Beinnes), aged 72 (b. about 1678), buried 26 Dec 1750 (Innvik ministerialbok 1750-1816, burials; index pg00000000460829). Farm, name and age match Aaland ('died 1750 aged 72') and precede her probate of 18 May 1751; two other Anne Rasmusdatters of similar age buried at Innvik 1751-52 lived at Scharsten and Wiig, so the farm decides it. ¶3

Her probate, registered 18 May 1751 at Beenes (Beinnes), Olden skipreide (Nordfjord skifteprotokoll A 11, fol. 160b-161a): widower Rasmus Joensen; their three children together: Joen Rasmussen (on the farm), 'Marithe Rasmusdatter gift med Lars Samuelsen Loen', and Barbra Rasmusdatter, 24. Read. Aaland (Innvik 1932, Beinnes) has her die in 1750 aged 72, so born about 1678; her own parents are not given. ¶1

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Confirmed as mother of Marthe Rasmusdatter (see her page), graded on her link to her: her own probate names Marithe Rasmusdatter, wife of Lars Samuelsen Loen, as her child; continuity to the tree is as for Rasmus Jonsen (the 1772 probate after Marthe Rasmusdatter Loen, widower Lars Samuelsen). Age check: Anne would have been about 36 at Marthe's birth (about 1714) and about 49 at Barbra's (about 1727); the latter is late but within range, and the probate states all three are the couple's children. ¶2
