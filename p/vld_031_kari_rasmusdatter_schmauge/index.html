About the family

Widow of Rasmus Alvsen (scan read): the probate after Rasmus Alvsen of Blindem, 30 Jan 1790, names 'Enken Kari Rasmusd.' (on the facing page 'Enken Karie Rasmusd.', receiving her half) and the daughter Anne Martha Rasmusdatter, 14, with guardian Willum Olsen Blindem. Her first husband, who is not named in her 1798 probate, is therefore Rasmus Alvsen, as inferred. Her own guardian in 1790 is faded; it is possibly Ole [?]sen of Blindem. ¶6

Parentage: the probate after Rasmus Sørensen of Smaage (Smoge), Norddal, 15 Jun 1770 (Sunnmøre 3A 23 fol. 297b, scan read) lists as 4th daughter 'Kari Rasmusdatter', unmarried, guardian her brother-in-law Lars Pedersen Hellesæth. Her siblings were Søren (in Bergen), Rasmus, Johanne (wife of Ole..rvigen, Wolden), Marthe (wife of Isach Andersen) and Helge (wife of Lars Pedersen Hellesæth). Rasmus Sørensen is added as her father at Probable: same name, patronymic and farm as the 1775 bride, and the only Rasmus with a probate at Smoge before 1811. Her own grade (as mother of Ane Marta) is unchanged. ¶7

Life story

Age (scan read): 'Kari Rasmusdatter gl. 36 Aar', unmarried, in her father Rasmus Sørensen's probate at Smaage of 15 Jun 1770 (3A 23 fol. 297b, 24564/300), so born about 1734. Consistent with her confirmation at Norddal on 9 Jun 1748. Her baptism (about 1733-34) predates the surviving Norddal register (1736). ¶9

Of 'Schmauge' (probably Smoge, in Norddal) when engaged on 1 Jul 1775 at Norddal to Rasmus Alvsen of Blinden (Blindem, Borgund); sureties Peder Alstad and Ener Larsen Nacken. Read on the scan. The entry gives no age and no father; her patronymic says her father was a Rasmus. ¶1

Probate read on the scan (13 Oct 2026): 'Blindem i Borgens. Anno 1798 d. 8de Septbr. Skifte efter Kari Rasmusdatter til Deeling imellem Enkemanden Jon Olsen Blindem og den afdødes udi [hendes] forrige Ægteskab avlede [eneste?] Datter Anna Martha Rasmusdatter g[ammel]. 24 Aar med Formynder: Willum Olsen Blindem. Vurderingsmænd Thore Kolvigen og Siur Lille-Nørve.' - an estate division (skifte) after Kari Rasmusdatter between her widower Jon Olsen of Blindem and the daughter of her former marriage, Anna Martha Rasmusdatter, aged 24, with Willum Olsen Blindem as guardian; appraisers Thore Kolvigen and Siur Lille-Nørve. One word before 'Datter' is struck through and unclear. The account on the facing page lists the daughter's inheritance (about 19 rd) and then payments to Siur Rasmussen Lille Nørve, Synneve Rasmusdatter, Borgund church and Mons. Jacob Mechlenborg, which read as debts paid, not heirs (not certain). ¶2a

She survived Rasmus Alvsen (buried 1 Jan 1790) and married again, to Jon Olsen of Blindem, before 1798. So Rasmus did NOT marry again after her. The widow Anne Olsdatter of Blindem who married the bachelor Ole Larsen of Blindem in 1790 (Borgund copulerede 1790, p. 88, read on the scan: 'ungk. Ole Larsen Blindem og Enkekone Anne Olsd. ibid.', no first husband named) must be the widow of another man on the multi-holding farm Blindem, not of Rasmus. No Kari burial at Blindem 1790-1830 is indexed (vld_027 search); she probably died in 1798, shortly before the probate. ¶4

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Why Confirmed: her motherhood of Anne Martha (earlier drafted only as an inference from the 1775 engagement) is stated in a primary record. The 1798 probate, a primary record read on the scan, names Anna Martha Rasmusdatter as her daughter by her former marriage. Her first husband is not named there, but Rasmus Alvsen of Blindem (buried 1 Jan 1790, age 44; index card in vld_027) was the only Rasmus at Blindem in the indexed Borgund burials of 1786-92 (vld_027 search), and his daughter Anne Martha was baptised in 1776. That she is the same Kari Rasmusdatter of 'Schmauge' who was engaged to Rasmus in Norddal on 1 Jul 1775 is a strong inference (same name, same groom, first child about nine months later), but no record says it outright. The age of 24 in 1798 gives a birth about 1774, two years off the 1776 baptism; the probate age is rounded or careless. ¶3

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Caution: a Kari Rasmusdtr. of Smaave was confirmed at Norddal on 9 Jun 1748 (scan read; no parents or age given). If she is this Kari, she was born about 1731-34 and was over 40 at Ane Marta's birth in 1776. The 1770 probate gives her age as 18 or 26 (reading uncertain), which would point to a younger, unconfirmed Kari. Unresolved; nothing changed. ¶8

Open questions and the next record to read 2

Lead: Siur Rasmussen Lille Nørve (also an appraiser) and Synneve Rasmusdatter share her patronymic and could be her siblings; unproved. ¶2b

Lead: a 'John Olsen of Blindheim' was marriage witness at Anne Martha's second marriage, 13 Jun 1819 (Borgund klokkerbok p. 382 no. 20, already on her card); he may be her stepfather Jon Olsen. Unproved. ¶5
