Life story

1664 (, re-read): in the bailiff's census of 21 Jun 1664 (35573 viewer 130, s.241), Indre Takle ('Indretackle') has two farmers, Magnus (son Niels 46) and Olle, and in Olle's sons' column stands '36: Hermund Heinrichs:' (Hermund Hendrichsen, 36, so born about 1628; 1701 gives 68, about 1633). His patronymic differs from the farmer's, so he was probably Olle's stepson or son-in-law, and his father Hendrich was dead or gone by 1664 (inference). The 1645 poll tax lists on 'Tachele' the couples Mogens and Giøe Nielsdatter, and Olle and Brete Nielsdatter (pt00000000059812-16); if Olle of 1645 is Olle of 1664, Brete Nielsdatter may be Hermund's mother, remarried, and Hermund named a daughter Brite (Speculative, not linked). No Hendrich is indexed in the 1645 poll tax on any part of Takle. ¶6

Census 1701 (manntall), Inder Tachle, Ytre Sogn: 'Hermund Hnerichsen' (sic), Bunde (farmer), aged 68 (b. about 1633), with his son Claus Hermundsen, 20 (b. about 1681), and a servant Jacob Povlsen. The other householder on Inder Takle was Gregorius Iversen, 54; there was no second Claus on any part of Takle in 1701. ¶2

Probate, Ytre Sogn 2b fol. 492b (bottom of the left page of view 177; the shares continue on fol. 493a): 'Anno 1716 den 19 May' the court met 'paa Tackle at skifte... efter Sal. Hermund Hendrichsen, imellem hans efterlevende quinde Gietr.[?] Claus Datter paa den ene og deris tilsammen avlede Børn: Hendrich, Claus, Brit[e] og Haldis Hermunds Sønner og Døttre paa den anden side, alle myndige og tilstede.' The right page lists the shares of 'Broder Hendrich' and 'Søster Brithe'. Read from the scan (crops). Index row: 'Hermund Henriksen, Takle, 1707-1719' (pa00000000520283). ¶1

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Graded on his link to his son Claus Hermundsen: the 1716 probate names Claus among the children of Hermund Hendrichsen and his widow, and the 1701 census lists 'Claus Hermundsen', son, aged 20, in Hermund's household on Inder Takle. Continuity to the tree's Claus Hermundsen of Indre Takle (d. before Sep 1750): 1701 Claus Hermundsen, son, Inder Takle; 1716 Claus, heir of Hermund, Takle; 1726 'Morbroder Claus Tackle', guardian of the children of his sister Haldis Hermundsdatter at Indre Takle (3a fol. 202a), Haldis being named as a sister in the 1716 probate; 1750 probate of Claus Hermundsen at Indre Takle. Same full name, same farm throughout. ¶3a

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Rival: none (no other Claus on Takle in 1701; the Takle probate index 1698-1790 has no other Claus Hermundsen). ¶3b

Searches that found nothing 1

Parents not found: the 1645 poll tax for Ytre Sogn indexes two couples on 'Tachele' (Mogens and Giøe Nielsdatter; Olle and Brete Nielsdatter) and no Hendrich; other parts of Takle may be indexed under other spellings. ¶5
