About the family

Father of Anders Andersson Eidem (1852/3-1932) of Zoar Lutheran. Named on Find a Grave memorial 229712492 with the dates 1801-1852, and in his son's biography as 'Anders Andersson', husband of Brynhild Bardosdatter Mogarn, at the Eidem farm just outside the village of Selbu. ¶1
