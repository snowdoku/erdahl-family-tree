About the family

Father entered (Probable): John Edwards, chirurgeon, of Limehouse, London, and Boston. Two silver histories state that the goldsmith John Allen's wife Elizabeth was the sister of his partner John Edwards (1670-1746), son of John Edwards, chirurgeon of Limehouse (Antiques article on the Allen and Edwards standing salt; K. C. Buhler, 'John Edwards, Goldsmith, and His Progeny', 1951). The 1697 marriage was performed by 'Mr. James Allen', the groom's father. No record read names her father. Her own grade (on her link to Jeremiah Allen) is unchanged. ¶2

Life story

'John Allen & Elizabeth Edwards were married by Mr. James Allen July 22', in the Boston marriages printed under 1697 (Record Commissioners vol. 9). Silver reference books say the goldsmith John Allen married Elizabeth Edwards, and his partner was John Edwards. Graded Probable on her link to Jeremiah Allen: the 1699 birth entry calls the mother only 'Eliza.'; she is assumed to be the Elizabeth Edwards of the 1697 marriage. ¶1

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
