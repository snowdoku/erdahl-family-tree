Life story

Burial: He is buried in Murray River Cemetery, where his stone reads 1867–1930 and 'Son of Thomas & Grace A. Hayter' (Find a Grave memorial 144714183, photographed by Karen Dicks Miller). His daughters Violet E. (1898–1970) and Myrtle Florence (1909–1995) both married Crouchers. ¶4

Born May 1868 (1911 census; 1867 on his gravestone, 1869 in 1881/1891), PEI. In his father's household 1881 and 1891 (as Thomas G.). In 1911 in Kings County with wife Florence (b. Apr 1871, USA) and daughters Violet (1899), Jennie (1903), Edith (1905), Florence (1907). Died 1930; buried Murray River Cemetery, whose Find a Grave memorial (144714183) calls him 'son of Thomas & Grace A. Hayter'. ¶1

Rural directory, about 1925–28: The Cummins Atlas of Prince Edward Island lists "Hayter, Thos. P.O. Murray River R.R. 1. Wf. Florence; Ch. Violet, Jennie, Edith, Myrtle." ¶6

Death: Lauretta Brooks's diary for April 1930 records that Thomas George Hayter died on the Alliston Road of heart failure near Leverett Acorn's, on Friday 4 April [1930] about 10 p.m., and was buried from the Baptist church at Murray River on 8 April by Rev. Armstrong. ¶2

Death: Lauretta Brooks's diary for April 1930 records that 'Thomas George HAYTER died on Alliston Road from heart failure near Leverett ACORN's Friday Apr. 4th about 10 p.m. Buried from Baptist ch M. River Apr. 8, by Rev. ARMSTRONG.' Her list of deaths gives 'Hayter, Thomas | | 04-Apr-30 | Murray River | Tom's son', which identifies him as the son of Thomas Hayter. His middle name was George. ¶3
