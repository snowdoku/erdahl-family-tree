Life story

Baptized late 1813 at Ål (1807-1814 church book, p. 58) as Randie. Parents: Sølve Knudsen and Ingebor Olsdatter of Kaarud (Kanafedmyren). The book is scanned but not indexed; the birth day was read as the 8th, probably of October. The censuses give her birth year as 1814 or 1815. ¶1

Baptism: 'Landie', daughter of 'Sølve Knudsen og Ingebor Olsd: Kanerud', was baptised on the 8th of a month following a 28 Oct entry, so in Oct or Nov 1813. Godparents: Ole [E]rbraaten[?] Sata, Knud Slaatten, Anne Borg[?], Ole Soho[?] and Aagot Hanem[?] (surnames after the first two uncertain). Ål ministerialbok 1807-1814 (source 8546), p. 58. Her elder brother Knud was baptised early 1808. ¶5

Married Ole Tostensen of Medgaarden 2 Jan 1835 at Ål; the record names her father as Sølve Knudsen. Also recorded as Randi or Rangde Salvesdatter (Salversd., Solvesd.) in her children's baptisms. ¶2

Died a widow 29 Mar 1887 at Langerø and was buried 5 Apr 1887 at Ål. The burial record gives her birth as 1815 at Kanafedmyren. ¶3
