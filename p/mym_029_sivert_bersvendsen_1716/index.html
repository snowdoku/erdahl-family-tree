Life story

Married in Selbu on Trinity Sunday, 5 Jun 1746: 'Ectviede... N5 Mandsfolket Soldat Siur Bersvendsen og gudsfrygtige [pige] Marith Anders datter' (Selbu klokkerbok 16315, canvas 87, 0-based, col 3); betrothed about 2 p.Pasch. (24 Apr) 1746, 'Soldat Siur B[ersvend]sen [patronymic overwritten] med gudsfrygtige [pige] Marithe Andersdatter' (canvas 84, col 4). He was a soldier. No farm is written; the identification with Siur Bersvendsen Draxen rests on the name pair and the baptism of Kirsten, daughter of Sjur Draxten, in Jun-Jul 1746 (canvas 88), which would be a child conceived before the marriage. Read. ¶9

A 'Siur Draxen' stood godparent at the baptism of Bent, son of Halvar Thomassen Varmdalen, on 16 January 1757 - very probably the same man. ¶2

Ole Bersvendsen of Draxen, a householder there in 1757-58 and forlover (marriage sponsor) at Bersvend Sivertsen's betrothal in 1778, may be this Siur's brother - a lead, not proved. The probate (skifte) after Siur in 1766 would settle the family, but Strinda og Selbu probate survives for Selbu only from 1797. ¶4

1762 extra tax list: 'Siur[?] Bersvendssen, Marit Andersdatter' head the first household at Draxen (matr. 471), with 'Ole Bersvendssen, Agnis Persdatter' as the second couple (Ole Bersvendsen, b. about 1723, soldier 1745-57, guardian of Siur's children in 1766 - probably his brother). ¶8

Burial: the Selbu register enters on the 5th Sunday after Trinity 1766 (29 June 1766) 'Gravf.: Siur Bersvends: Draxen, gl. 50 Aar' - Siur (Sivert) Bersvendsen of Draxen, buried aged 50, so born about 1716 (inferred from the age). Read from the scan (source 16311, canvas 19, right page). ¶1

Probate (skifte) after 'afg. Sivert Bersvendsen ibm.' on the farm Draxen, opened 9 May 1766 (Strinda sorenskriveri skifteprotokoll 6, Digitalarkivet 24739, fol. 21a-22a, images 22-23). Heirs: the widow (not named in the parts read), with laugværge (legal guardian) Mons[?] Andersen Børrig[?]; and 2 children: the son Bersvend Sivertsen (age written but not legible - '13[?]' Aar, alternative readings 6 or 8) and the daughter Ingebor Sivertsdatter (age not legible). Ole Bersvendsen Draxen, present, was appointed formynder (guardian) for both children ('for dem begge'), apparently described as the children's farbroder (paternal uncle) [reading uncertain]. Shares: the son about 27 Rd, the daughter about 13 Rd (a son took a double share). Closed 'Actum anno et die ut supra'. ¶6

Identified as the father of Bersvend Sivertsen of Draxen (see his page) - Probable, not proved. Grounds: Bersvend was confirmed in 1772 as 'Bersv. Siverts. Draxen', so his father was a Sivert and the family was at Draxen; this Siur was the Sivert of Draxen who died in 1766, when Bersvend was about 11-14; Bersvend bears the name Bersvend, this Siur's father's name, as the Norwegian naming custom puts a grandfather's name on the eldest son; and Bersvend named his own first sons Siur (1783) and Sivert (1784). No record states the parentage outright, Bersvend's baptism falls in the 1747-1756 gap in the Selbu registers, and a second Sivert living at Draxen in the 1750s has not been ruled out. ¶3
