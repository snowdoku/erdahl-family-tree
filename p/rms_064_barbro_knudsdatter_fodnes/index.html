Life story

Bygdebok (Svenes vol., Fodnes gnr. 67, canvas 415): Barbro Knutsdotter came from gnr. 50 and lived 1668-1708 (birth ABT 1668), widow of Ola Olsson of Vestre Slidre when she married Ulrik of Fodnes. Her own parents are not in the rebuilt text; next: the gnr. 50 chapter of Valdres bygdebok I (vol. C, Ulnes gnr. 36-57, nb.no 6f9ab7bcdbd69327f32e2f7d3d6878d6). ¶3a

Probate (index card 3243): estate of 'Barbro Knudsdtr. +' at Fodnes nordre i Svenes i N. Aurdal, 9 Oct 1708 (Hadeland, Land og Valdres skifteprotokoll 1704-1709, s. 458b), married 1. Olle Olsen (deceased), 2. Uldrich Thommesen. Children: 1. Orians [reading uncertain], 2. Rangdi, married Erich Andersen Holdal, 3. Guro 23, married Ole Asladsen (a line is drawn under these three, evidently the children of the first marriage); 4. Thommes 12, 5. Knud 7, 6. Ole 5, 7. Erich 6 months, 8. Ingeleff 16, 9. Bergite 14, 10. Siri 9. Gross about 130 rdr, net about 72. Probable mother of Tommes Uldrichsen of Fodnes nordre (b. about 1696), who named his eldest daughter Barbro (b. about 1727). That Thommes 12 of 1708 is the later farmer Tommes Uldrichsen rests on farm, patronymic and age, not on a statement. ¶1

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: the 1740 Fodnes estate (DA 25286, p. 428, view 226) states that Tommes Uldrichsen's odel in Nordre Fodnes came to him by the skifte of 9 October 1708 and names his brothers Knud, Ole and Erich. The skifte of 9 Oct 1708 is Barbro Knudsdatter's own estate (card DA 39948 no. 3243), at which her children by her second husband Uldrich Thommesen were Thommes 12, Knud 7, Ole 5 and Erich 6 months. This identifies Tommes Uldrichsen of 1740 as her son. Her own parents are not named: Fodnes nordre cards 3239 and 3241 (1666) show a Knud of Fodnes whose children Niels (d. 1664) and Astri (d. by 1666) were a generation older than Barbro, so she may be his granddaughter, but no record links them. ¶2

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Test record: the probate after a Knut on gnr. 50 (Valdres card index DA 39948) naming Barbro. ¶3b
