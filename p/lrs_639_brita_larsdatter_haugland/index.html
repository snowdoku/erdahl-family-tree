Life story

Bygdebok (Engesæter, canvas 551): mother of 'Kirsti Jonsdtr. Nedre Haugland', wife of Jon Toreson (Nedre Haugland bnr. 61). ¶1

Probate, Ytre Sogn 2b fol. 292a, headed 'Haugland' (view 6, right page; the entry follows the 31 May 1707 Indre Oppedal probate on fol. 289b, so it is of about May-June 1707; the date words after 'Anno' were not read): the scrivener, the lensmann Jørgen [Tiønnes?] and two appraisers met 'udi Ladvigs Skibrede at skifte efter udi Herren hensovede danne quinde Sal: Briete Lars daatter paa dend eene og dend S. qvindis Børn paa dend anden side, som [er] Jon, [..]sten, Lars, [Haugland?], Briete, Kirsti Gullach Engesettre og Elj, alle fuldmyndige og tilstede' (the late woman's children: Jon, [Tor?]sten, Lars, Brite, Kirsti [wife of] Gullach Engesætter, and Eli, all of age and present). The word after Lars ('Hafland'/'Haugland') may be a farm or a husband's name; the second son's name is partly illegible. No widower is named, so her husband was probably dead by 1707. Read from the scan. A sister 'Eli Jonsdtr, Haugland' has a probate in 1728-1733 (index pa00000000520780, not read), which fits the children's patronymic Jonsdatter. ¶4

Context: the probate after 'Eli Jons datter', Haugland, 19 Jun 1730 (Ytre Sogn 3b fol. 432b, view 146; index pa00000000520780) divides between her widower Jon Torstensen[?] and their children Ole, Jon, Torsten, Henrik and Ingeborg; the guardian is the father's brother Torsten Engesetter. It names none of her siblings, so it does not bridge Kirsti's identity, but it shows an Eli Jonsdatter of Haugland alive into the 1720s, consistent with the daughter Eli of 1707. ¶6

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 4

Graded on her link to Christi Joensdatter (see her page). ¶2a

Graded Probable: a bygdebok is a compiled secondary source and the passage was rebuilt from word boxes with gaps. ¶2b

Audit 7 (1-2 Nov 2026): grade unchanged (Probable, compiled source only). Under the audit-6 rule a bygdebok-only ancestor must name the primary record that would test the link; this note supplies it. ¶3a

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed, graded on her link to her daughter Christi Joensdatter. This is the test record named by audit 7: the 1707 probate after Brite Larsdatter of Haugland names among 'dend S. qvindis Børn' (the late woman's children) 'Kirsti Gullach Engesettre' (Kirsti, [wife of] Gullach of Engesætter). Continuity: Gullach/Gullov To[e]nsen held Engesætter from 1698 to 1741 (1701 census, 38455 viewer 55: 'Gullach To[e]ns[en]', 36, sons Joen 3 and To[e]n 1; bygdebok brukar 1698-1741); his probate of 22 Aug 1741 on Engesætter (6a No. 11, fol. 153a) names his widow 'Christi Joens Datter' and their children Joen and To[e]r Hiertholm, Lars, Agathe and Sirie, the same eldest sons as in 1701. So the Kirsti married to Gullach of Engesætter in 1707 is the Christi Joensdatter married to him in 1741; her patronymic fits the 1707 list, whose first son is Jon and whose daughter Eli is 'Eli Jonsdtr, Haugland' in the probate index, and the Brekke bygdebok (canvas 551) independently calls her 'Kirsti Jonsdtr. Nedre Haugland', daughter of Brita Larsdtr. ¶5a

Alternatives considered and set aside 2

Rival: the only other Gullach on Engesætter, Gullach Lassesen/Tostensen (40 in 1701, Gullach Tostensen), was married from 1693 to Siri Iversdatter (d. 1737; bygdebok canvas 510), so a Kirsti could not be his wife in 1707. ¶5b

Caveat: the 1707-1741 span for the wife rests on her husband's continuous tenure, not on a record naming her in between. ¶5c

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Test record: a probate after Jon Toresen or Brita Larsdatter of Nedre Haugland (Ytre Sogn probate index, search by domicile Haugland), which should name 'Kirsti Jonsdatter gift med Gullak Engesæter' among the heirs. ¶3b
