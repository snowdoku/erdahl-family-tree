About the family

Father of Signe, baptised Dom. 15 p. Trinit. (9 Sep) 1725 at Åmli church: 'Börüf Aadnesön Jörenlands barn Signe' (pp. 74-75, canvas 40). Buried in Gjøvdal Dom. 13 p. Trinit. (15 Aug) 1728: 'Börüf Aadnesön Jörenland 49 aar gammel' (pp. 90-91, canvas 48). The book names fathers only, never mothers; his wife is not named. Børuf = Bjørgulv/Børus. ¶1

His father was presumably an Aadne at Jørundland (inference from the patronymic). Ole Aadnesen Jørenland, buried Jan 1718 aged 58 (b. c. 1660) together with his son Aadne Olsen aged 22 ('overstaaet Ole Aadnesön Jörenland 58 aar gl. og hans Sön Aadne Olsön 22 aar gl.', canvas 19), was probably Børuf's elder brother. This Aadne would be born c. 1630-50; he is not in this book. ¶5

Son Ole: the 1728 probate gives Ole aged 7, i.e. the Ole baptised Palm Sunday 1721, who therefore survived (= 'Ole Børusen Jørenland', godfather 1742 and 1751). Yet the church book also has 'Böruf Aadnesön Jörenlands barn Ole' baptised Dom. 5 p. Epiph. (6 Feb) 1724 and buried Dom. 16 p. Trin. (24 Sep) 1724 aged 1/2 (Åmli 1711-1740 pp. 66, 69, canvases 36-37); unexplained duplicate name. ¶8

Father from the bygdebok (Probable): Åmli ætt og heim II, Jørundland L.nr. 35 (canvases 86-87) makes him a son of 'Lille-Ådne' Jørundland (skifte 1689), not of Stor-Ådne Ådne Olson (b. 1624, skifte 1699). The book gives him b. 1682 (his burial age implies b. about 1679) and lists his siblings Anne (1680-1767), Olav (d. 1712), Olav (b. 1684, m. 1708 Birgit Svennsdtr.) and a child b. 1687. His wife is 'Anne [Vrålsdtr.] f 1681 d 1743', and his estate was settled in 1748 by Anne Vrålsdtr. His own grade is unchanged. ¶13

Life story

Sons' baptisms FOUND (round 6), all in Gjøvdal, father only named: Ingebor, Dom. 9 p. Trin. (2 Aug) 1711 'Böruf Jörenlands barn Ingebor' (canvas 3) — so Børuf was married by 1710, before the book begins (Jan 1711), and his marriage is not in it; Aadne, 2 Oct 1714 'Böruf Jörenlands barn Aadne' (canvas 11); Tellef, Dom. Quadragesimae (c. 6 Mar) 1718 'Böruf Jörenlands barn Tellef' (canvas 19); Ole, Palm Sunday (c. 6 Apr) 1721 'Böruf Aadnesön Jörenlands barn Ole' (canvas 27); then Signe 1725. Asbor (m. 1730) not found 1711-Feb 1724, so probably born before 1711 or in Feb-Oct 1724 (canvases 36-37 unread). Mothers are never named in this hand, so Signe's mother is still unknown; the probate after Børuf (d. 1728) is the next source for her name. ¶4

PROBATE FOUND: Østre Råbyggelag Skifteprotokoll 8 fol. 21, 11 Oct 1728 (index cards 5997-5999): 'Børu Aadnesön + g.m. Anna Vraalsdtr.'; children Aadne Børusön 14 (archivist: + probate 7 Mar 1775; probate after his wife Gunvor Taralsdatter 29 Feb 1764), Tellef 11, Ole 7, Asbør 22, Ingeborg 19, Zigne 3. Widow's brother Giermund Vraalsen Omland; widow's half-brother Bendix Evindsøn Miaaland; guardian Lars Ellefsøn Hummedrum; net estate 228 rd. 'Bror til mannen: Ole Aadnesen Stedjen, ref. til aasætesbrev 3/11-1704' - his brother was Ole Aadnesen of Stedjen, alive in 1728. This faithful index confirms the whole sibling group (Asbor b. c. 1706, Ingeborg, Aadne, Tellef, Ole, Signe) as his children and supports raising this card's confidence. His father Aadne is not named; the åsetesbrev (allodial-right deed) of 3 Nov 1704 is the next source for him. ¶6

1664/1666 censuses: Jørundland (no. 4, 4 huder) in 1666 has users Aadne 52 (sons Stor Oluf 8 and Lill Oluf 4), Tallak 35, Giermund(?) 66 and Birgitha Olsdatter (son Aslak Olsen 6) (DA 35510 view 32, p. 43); the 1664 bailiff's list has 'Aadne 50' there (DA 35567 view 57). This Aadne, born about 1614, would have been about 65 when Børuf was born about 1679, so he could be Børuf's father only if Børuf's age is off or Aadne fathered him late; a younger Aadne (a son of this one, not listed in 1666) is as likely. No record decides it, so his father is not added. ¶11

1664-65 men-under-50 list (DA 35567 view 26, p. 14): 'Jørrenland': users Aadne(?) 50, Olluff 44, Thallach 30, Jørgen 40, all without patronymics, and no sons or servants listed. The list therefore does not give Aadne's patronymic or any young Aadne. Børuf's father remains unidentified. ¶12

Further Jørundland Børusen children seen (round 5), supporting the sibling group: Aadne Børusen Jørenland betrothed Dom. Cantate (29 Apr) 1736 and married Fest. Visit. Mariae (2 Jul) 1736 in Gjøvdal to Gundvor Taraldsdatter Berge (canvases 70-71); his son Børu bapt. 15 Mar 1738 (canvas 75) — named for Børuf — and two sons Børu buried All Saints 1739. Tellef Børusen betrothed c. Apr 1740 and married 11 Jul 1740 in Gjøvdal to Thore Nielsdatter Jørenland (canvases 82-83); godparents to their son Niels (b. 26 Feb 1740) included Aadne Børusen Jørenland and Asbor Jørenland. YEAR CORRECTION: Ingeborg Børusdatter married Tallak Evindsen Ekre on 3rd day Easter 1733 (not 1735; 'Anno 1734' heading on canvas 65 fixes canvases 63-64 to 1733). Sons' baptisms (Aadne, Ole, Tellef, before Oct 1724) and Børuf's marriage still unread (canvases 1-37). ¶3

Confidence raised to Confirmed (orchestrator, 13 Oct 2026): his 1728 probate (Skifteprotokoll 8 fol. 21, card 5997, checked) lists 'Zigne' aged 3 among his children by Anna Vraalsdatter at Jørundland, which matches the baptism of 'Börüf Aadnesöns Jörenlands barn Signe' on 9 Sep 1725 and the 1754 marriage of 'Signe Börusdatter Jörenland'; her brothers Aadne, Tellef and Ole Børusen of the probate also appear at Jørundland in the 1740s. ¶9

Corrections 1

Correction: the Ole Aadnesen (Aanonsen) of Jørundland buried Jan 1718 aged 58 was probably NOT his brother. That man's probate (14 Feb 1718, Skifteprotokoll 5 fol. 261, cards 5991-5993) is indexed as 'Ole Aanonsen'; heirs widow Anna Olsdatter, children Erik, Ole 19, Oslov, Thodne; widow's guardian Christgier Aanonsen Østenaa; guardians Aasul Knudsen Skjeggedal and two first cousins of the children from Treungen - Børuf is not named. The brother named in 1728 is Ole Aadnesen Stedjen, living. ¶7

Searches that found nothing 1

Search: the Jørundland probate cards 5979-5988 (39942) were read in full. They hold Gunild Osmundsdatter (Skp. 1 fol. 275, 22 Nov 1701; husbands Olle Thallacksen and Halvor Ormsen), Evind Evindsøn (Skp. 2 fol. 148, 1704-05; wives Zire Olsdatter and Birgitha Nielsdatter), Gunder Tolfsøn (Skp. 2 fol. 188b, 1705-07; wife Ziire Jørgensdatter) and a 1711 cross-reference (fol. 44 of Skp. 3). There is no estate of an Aadne at Jørundland from 1697 to 1712, so his father's estate is either earlier than the surviving protocols or was filed under another farm. ¶10
