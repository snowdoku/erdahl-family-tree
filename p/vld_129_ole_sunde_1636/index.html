Life story

Manntall 1701 (index): 'Oelle', 65 (born about 1636), 'Opsider eller Leilending, Bøxlet Jord' (leaseholder on leased land), remark 'verit Soldat' (has been a soldier), farm Sunde, Borgund, p. 5; sons Peder Oels. 25 (sailor), Oelle Oels. 19, Knud Oels. 14. His patronymic is not given. ¶1

Possible kin (scan read): Borgund 15995 canvas c146 (about Feb 1720): '(2) Povel Olss. Sunde, sin datter Else', godparents Peder Olss. Sunde, Lars Solevog, Ingebor Olsdatter Solevog (carried the child), Berite Olsdatter ibid., Dorothe Olsdatter Sunde. A Povel Olsen at Sunde with Peder Olsen Sunde as first godfather and three Olsdatter godmothers suggests a sibling group of children of an Ole; Povel is not among the sons listed at Sunde in 1701 (Peder 25, Ole 19, Knud 14), so this is unproved. ¶4

Checked (scan read) for sibling proof of Peder Olsen Sunde: the Sunde probates of Askjeld Olsen (13 Feb 1741, Skifteprotokoll 10, s. 11b-12b, source 24554 views 17-18) and Arne Olsen (31 Oct 1738, Skifteprotokoll 9B, s. 524b-525b, source 24553 views 259-260) are inventories and creditors' claims; neither lists heirs or names a Peder Olsen. Arne's widow is Giøri[?] Arnesdatter with guardian Simon Olsen [..]Wolden. No upgrade. ¶3

2 on the grade · 1 alternative set aside

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Probable father of Peder Olsen Sunde (d. by Dec 1741): same farm, the only Peder Olsen there in 1701, consistent age, and Peder named a son Ole. Not proved by a record naming the link. A probate of 'Ole Olsen, Sunde' (index 1751, held 5 Sep 1749, 3A 17 fol. 34b) is a different, younger man with a widow and joint children (Ole and Hans Olsen among them), possibly the son Oelle Oels. b. about 1682 (not read in detail). ¶2

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed, graded on his link to his son Peder Olsen Sunde (see his page). The record that names the relationship is the 1701 manntall for Sunde, Borgund (p. 5), which enters under the farmer 'Oelle', 65, in the column 'Søner' (sons): Peder Oels. 25, a marine serving Knud Hafnen, Oelle Oels. 19 and Knud Oels. 14. Continuity to the tree's Peder: (1) the 1704 list of marines for Sunde (index, source 58583) has Peder Ols. as bonde (farmer) of Sunde with his 'brødre' (brothers) Ole Ols. 22 and Christen Ols. 17; Ole 22 is the Oelle 19 of 1701, so the farmer Peder of 1704 is Ole's son Peder of 1701 (the 17-year-old brother is called Christen where 1701 has Knud, aged 14); (2) Peder Olss. of Sunde married at Borgund on 4 Jan 1705 and had children baptised there from 1705 to 1724, including Siri on 11 Nov 1714; (3) Peder Olss. of Sunde was buried 17 Dec 1741. Each step is on the same farm and no more than about ten years apart. ¶5a

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

No rival: the 1704 list names every man on Sunde (Rasmus 55; Christian 51 with sons Joen and Hans; Peder Ols.; Tore 73 with sons Niels 33 and Peder 27; stepson Joen Nielsen 43), and Peder Olsen is the only Peder son of an Ole there; the baptism index has one Peder Olss. as father at Sunde 1705-1724. The burial index's calculated birth year (1668) is eight years earlier than the census ages give; burial ages of old men are often overstated. ¶5b
