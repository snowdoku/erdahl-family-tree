Life story

1624 leidang roll for Selbu (39890 views 76R-78): no Alset entry was found (a line in view 78 first read as Alset is 'Oluff Bulsett'). So the roll gives no Jon at Alset as a candidate father. ¶5

Father added from the bygdebok (Selbu i fortid og nåtid vol. 3, Alset canvases 771, 774), Probable: son of Jon Eriksen (user from 1626, d. 1658), took the bygsel in 1658, born about 1625. 'Brukere': '-1661-1701 [Ola] Jonsen, f. ca. 1625'; children Sjurd ca. 1661, Kari (m. Anders Bjørnsen Solem), [a daughter m.] Guttorm Olsen, Bjørn ca. 1673. ¶7a

1666 vicar's manntall (35528 v21 R, no. 11 Alsett): 'Olluff Jonsen bruger ½ ørt 6ß er 42 Aar' - confirms his patronymic Jonsen (born about 1624); no sons listed, as Siur (b. c.1681) and Bjørn (b. c.1673) were not yet born. 1664 and 1665 lists: Ole, 40, the only farmer at Alset. His father was a Jon (not identified). ¶3

Court book 28707 view 136 left (Selbu session, late 1692): 'Ole Alset 4 D.' in a merchant's list of Selbu debtors. This places him at Alset in about 1692 and does not bear on his parents. The 1646-47 crown-tenant list (51306 view 181) has a 'Baard A[l]setten[?]', reading uncertain, so it is not a usable lead. ¶6

1701 manntall, Selbu, farm Aldset (canvas 118): farmer 'Oller Jonsen', aged 80 (so born about 1621), with sons Siur Olsen 20 and Bjørn, soldier, 28 ('begge Sønner'). The census states his fatherhood of Siur explicitly; he is Speculative only because Siur's own link to Ole Sivertsen Alset is Speculative. Do not chain further generations above him without a direct record. ¶1

1 correction · 2 open questions

Corrections 1

Correction: raised from Speculative to Confirmed. His son Siur is now Confirmed as Ole Sivertsen Alset's father (baptism 1703), and the 1701 manntall states 'Siur Olsen 20... begge Sønner' of Oller Jonsen (80) at Alset. Probable burial: 'Olaf Alset', buried 1704 aged 78 (16310 c18 right) - the age is close to the census 80; no other old Ole at Alset is known. ¶2

Open questions and the next record to read 2

Biography gaps: his exact birth date (c.1621) and his marriage (before the 1699 register) are not in any record read, and his wife is not named. His burial is only probable, a 1704 entry in 16310 canvas 18 R matched by name, farm and age, so a full-resolution re-read would firm it up. His probate is not found in the 77804 odd-numbered cards 3-23 or the every-fourth-card sample of 25-95, so the remaining odd-numbered cards 25-95 are the next check, for a probate c.1704 (series A, 1692-1714). ¶4

Next: '1702-1731 Sjurd Olsen, f. ca. 1661, død [...] 1757(?). G.1702 [med] Gjertrud [...] ca. 1676, [d.] 1731'. His wife's name is not in the recovered words. ¶7b
