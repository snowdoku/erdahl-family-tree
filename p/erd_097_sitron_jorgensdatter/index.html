Life story

Burial: same book, canvas 126 R, 27 Apr 1717: 'Maren Jørgensd. Lunde / Karen Halvorsd. ibd. og hendes dødfødde Barn - sep.' Karen Halvorsdatter, Jørgen Lunde's bride of Dec 1716, was buried four months after the wedding with her stillborn child, together with a Maren Jørgensdatter of Lunde (probably a child of Jørgen's, perhaps given emergency baptism; her age is not stated). This rules the Jørgen Lunde x Karen Halvorsdatter marriage out as Sitron's parentage. If Maren was not the infant, she is evidence of an older Jørgen at Lunde with daughters (a possible father for Sitron); the entry does not settle which. ¶10

Third marriage: Leikanger ministerialbok 11564, p. 350 (canvas 167 R), 1732, Sponsalia about Dom. Jubilate-Cantate (Apr-May 1732): 'Jon Tiugum Lensm[and] / Citone Lunde', sponsors 'Mons og Ole Aase'. So the widow of Halvor Pedersen (buried Nov 1731) was betrothed in spring 1732 to Jon, lensmann at Tjugum (probably Jon Andersen, whose brother Ole Andersen Valsvik is the farbror of her son Halvor Jonsen in 1752). ¶3

Marriage: same book, canvas 168 L, 1732, Nupt. about July 1732: 'Jon Anders. Tiugum / Citone Lunde' - Jon Andersen Tjugum, lensmann. And in Nov 1732 the baptism 'Halver Jons. Lunde' (same page): her son Halvor Jonsen, who died unmarried in 1752 aged about 19. ¶4

Widow ('Citrona Jør=gensdatter') of Halvor Pedersen in his probate at Lunde, 20 March 1733 (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll 3b, fol. 505b), mother of his four sons Peder, Jørgen, Ole and Samuel. She was judged fit to be guardian of the two youngest, Ole and Samuel. ¶1

Widowed again and a fourth betrothal: canvas 169 L, 1733, burial about July 1733: 'Jon Anders. Lunde 28' - her husband Jon Andersen, only about 28, died a year after the wedding. Canvas 169 R, 1733, Sponsalia 'd. 21 Xbr. Lars Vestrem / Citone Lunde': betrothed on 21 Dec 1733 to Lars of Vestrem (Vestreim, Tjugum). Her residence in the 1752 probate, read 'i Wadheim[?]', is very probably 'i Vestrem'. Her marriages known now: Halvor Pedersen Lunde (d. Nov 1731), Jon Andersen Tjugum (m. Jul 1732, d. Jul 1733), Lars Vestrem (betrothed Dec 1733). ¶5

Fourth marriage: canvas 170 R, 1734, a Thursday in Feb 1734: 'Festivitas nuptiarum Lars Schachs[?]. Vestrem / Citone Vidua' (the patronymic may be Isachsen). She was still his widow or wife at Vestrem in 1752. ¶6

Remarried: she is 'Moderen Sitron Jørgensdatter' in the probate of her unmarried son Halvor Jonsen Lunde at Valsvik, 17 May 1752 (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll 7a, fol. 138b, No. 69), whose heirs were she and his half-brothers Peder, Jørgen, Ole and Samuel Halvorsen Lunde. Her second husband was a Jon, inferred to be Jon Andersen because Ole Andersen Valsvik is called Halvor Jonsen's farbror (father's brother). Her residence in 1752 is read 'i Wadheim[?]', uncertain. Samuel's daughter Zithone/Citrone (b. ~1753) bears her name. ¶2

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

3 on the grade · 1 correction · 1 search with no result

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 3

Upgrade: to Confirmed as mother of Samuel Halvorsen, on the same chain as Halvor Pedersen: the 1733 probate names the widow Citrona Jørgensdatter with the 4th son Samuel in her guardianship; the 1728 baptism of 'Samuel Halverson Lunde' and the 1731 burial of 'Halver Pedersen Lunde' fix the family at Lunde; the 1732 betrothal of 'Citone Lunde' fixes her there; the 1752 probate names her as mother of 'Samuel Halvorsen Lunde'; and Samuel's daughter Zithone carries her name. ¶8

Audit (1 Nov 2026): downgraded from Confirmed to Probable, for the same reason as Halvor Pedersen. The 1733 and 1752 probates prove that she was the mother of Samuel Halvorsen Lunde, but no record read ties that man to Samuel Halvorsen of Balholmen (see his page), whose 1801 age points to 1724, not Dec 1728. The naming of his daughter Zithone is circumstantial support only. ¶11

Re-proof attempt: no change; same gap as Halvor Pedersen. No probate after her is indexed in the Ytre Sogn register after 1752. ¶12

Corrections 1

Correction: Halvor Jonsen was not a son of an earlier marriage. Jon Tjugum was her husband after Halvor Pedersen, so Halvor Jonsen (d. 1752, unmarried) was born after 1732 and named after her late husband Halvor. Her 'first marriage to a Jon' c. 1700, sought in earlier waves, does not exist; whether Halvor Pedersen was her first husband is open. Her baptism, if in Leikanger, would be about 1690-1705 (her sons were minors in 1733 and the youngest Samuel was born Dec 1728). ¶7

Searches that found nothing 1

Search: Leikanger ministerialbok 11564 read for 1715-1716 (canvases 115-124) and Jul 1721-Jul 1723 (canvases 149-155, pp. 334-340; 1722-23 are sparse, columnar, and stop at about Dom. VII p. Trin. 1723, so part of 1723 was never entered). No marriage of a Halvor (Pedersen) of Lunde and no Sitron/Citrone in those spans. A Jørgen of Lunde was betrothed and married at the turn of 1716-17: canvas 124 L, 'Feria III Nat. ved Vangsnæs af Tiugum: Jørgen Lunde / Karen Haldorsd. spons.' and 'd. 28 Dec: Jørgen Lunde / Karen Haldorsd. nupt.' (27-28 Dec 1716). This is very probably the Jørgen Jensen of Lunde, b. about 1694, of the 1701 census. A daughter of this couple would be born 1717 or later, too young to have borne Halvor Pedersen's son Samuel in 1728 (or the Samuels of 1724-25), so the 1716 couple are not her parents unless the identification of her children is wrong. Not a parent link; recorded so the next reader does not chase it. The bride's patronymic Haldorsdatter (Halvorsdatter) at Lunde is suggestive of a kinship with Halvor Pedersen Lunde (b. about 1664 by his burial age), for instance a daughter by an earlier marriage, but nothing states it. ¶9
