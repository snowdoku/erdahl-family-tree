About the family

Bailiff's census 1664-66 (p. 149, view 82): 'Heel gaard Hilde' had nine farmers (Jacob, Ole Olsen, Harald, Anders Olsen, Abell, Anders, Arne, Hans, Rasmus), sons Colben, Oluff, Mogens and Arne[?], lodger Lauritz Smed. Guttorm (about 1) is not listed; the bailiff, like the vicar, omits small boys. Ole Olsen is the only Ole farming Hilde (b. ~1634 per the vicar), so he is the obvious candidate father, but no record links them, and Aaland's '(Vs.)' suggests Guttorm married into Hilde, so his father may have lived elsewhere. ¶5

Life story

1664 vicar's census (p. 217): 'Heel Gaard Hilde' had nine farmers (Jacob Torgilsen 60, Oluf Olsen 30, Harald Kolbeinsen 60, Anders Olsen 50, Abel [?] Knudsen 60, Anders A[rne?]sen 30, Arne Danielsen 50, Hans Rasmussen 46, Rasmus Andersen 60), four farmers' sons aged 15 to 36, and a lodger Lauritz [smith?]. Guttorm, then about 1, is not listed; the vicar did not list small boys. The only farmer named Ole there is Oluf Olsen, 30; nothing links him to Guttorm, and Aaland says Guttorm married into Hilde. ¶4

Aaland 1932: Aaland's Hilde bruk nr. 4 'Guttormbruket' lists '(Vs.) Guttorm Olson, f. ikr. 1668, br. f. 1688' (born about 1668, tenant before 1688; the bracketed 'Vs.' means Aaland thought he married into the farm). Aaland names his wife as 'Kari (Hansd.)', who owned bruk nr. 2 at Lunde in Stryn, and gives the same three sons from the 1701 census. The birth year conflicts: the census age of 38 gives about 1663, Aaland about 1668. The census is the record read and is preferred. ¶2

Probate: none found. The Nordfjord probate index for Hilde (1700-1757) has no entry for him, and the register of protocol A 6 (Nov 1721-Oct 1731) lists no Hilde estate. Anders took over the bruk in 1715 (Aaland), so Guttorm may have died or retired around then. There is no surviving probate protocol for 1715-1721. ¶3

1701 census: 'Guttorm Ols.', opsidder (farm occupier) at Hilde, Innvik parish, aged 38 (born about 1663), with sons Hans 16, Anders 6 and Rasmus 3 (the 1701 manntall lists only males). ¶1
