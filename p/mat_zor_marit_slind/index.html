Life story

Gravestone, photographed and read: a flat granite marker, weathered, inscribed 'MRS. MARIT E. SLIND / APR. 16, 1832 / DEC. 7, 1917'. The 'Mrs.' is cut into the stone, so she is commemorated as a married woman or a widow; no husband's stone stands with her. In the digitised photograph the third digit of the birth year is at the edge of legibility and could be read as an 8; 1832 is Find a Grave's reading and is the one that fits her neighbours in the lot. ¶1

That a Slind woman born in 1832 lies in the Eidem family lot alongside Kari Slind who married Peder P. Eidem is the strongest thing on the ground pointing to Marit E. Slind being Karen's mother, and so the Slind immigrant matriarch of this colony. It is not proved and no record has been found that states a relationship. The 'E.' is a patronymic initial in the Norwegian manner — Eriksdatter or similar. ¶3

She is one of only four Slind burials in the whole of South Dakota, and all four are in this churchyard. ¶4

She was the mother of Kari Eliasdatter Slind (b. 1863, wife of Peder P. Eidem), of Beret 'Betsy' Eliasdatter Slind (b. 1872, wife of Mikal P. Eidem) and of Lars Eliason Slind of Reiemoen. She crossed the Atlantic as a widow of seventy-five to live out her last decade with her daughters at Marietta. ¶6

Burial plot: Zoar Lutheran Cemetery, lot 2 — South Dakota state burial register, 'MARIE E SLIND 12-07-1917, lot 2, block 00'. Lot 2 is the Peder P. Eidem lot: it also holds Peder P. Eidem (d. 1935), his wife Karen P. Eidem née Kari 'Carrie' Slind (d. 1915), Peder's mother Kari P. Eidem (d. 1925), and Lilla Eidem (d. 1915). ¶2

Selbygbogen (Minneapolis, 1921) p. 174: 'Marit Estensdatter Slind, Marietta, Minn., born 16 Apr. 1832 at GULDSETH, was married to Elias Larsen Slind who died in Selbu. She came to America in 1907 and lived here with her daughters and died 7 Dec. 1917.' Her birth date and death date as already recorded in this tree are confirmed to the day by that entry, and it adds her birth farm (Guldseth in Selbu), her patronymic (Estensdatter) and her husband. ¶5
