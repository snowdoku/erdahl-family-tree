Life story

Known only from the patronymics of Tore Thronsen and Astri Thronsdatter and from his widow Gunild Andersdatter (1801 census, Felle). His farm is assumed to be Felle but not proven. ¶1

Felle is now supported as the family farm: five children with his patronymic were 'of Felle' when they married in Treungen 1778-81 (Hæge, Ingebor and Groe Thronsdatter; Andres and Thore Tronsen). Kviteseid ministerialbok 1773-1786, images 117, 121, 122, 126, 130. Tron himself does not appear, so he had probably died by 1778 (inferred). ¶2

Named as the heir 'Trond Toresøn' (of age) at his mother Ingebor Jonsdatter's probate on Felle, 7 Aug 1743 (Øvre Telemark skifteprotokoll nr. 6, image 283). He took over the farm and livestock and paid the debts, which matches his own 1757 probate, which cites this skifte. His siblings in 1743: Jon Toresøn (under the curator Halvor Ormestad), Helje Toresdatter (married to Knud Knudsøn) and probably Torbiør Toresdatter. ¶10

Patronymic variant: on 25 Jan 1756 in Treungen church 'Troen Torofsen Felles Qvinde' (the wife of Tron Torofsen Felle) was churched, in the same entry as Toruv Hiemdal's and Orm Tvedt's wives (Kviteseid ministerialbok 1754-1773, introductions, image 72; read on the image by the orchestrator). His 1757 burial calls him 'Troen Torsen'. Torofsen (son of Toruv/Thoruf) and Torsen (son of Tor) are different names, so his father's name is uncertain: Toruv or Tor. Either way he is not a brother of Toruv Andersen Heimdal. The 1781 marriage of his son Tore to Hæge Toruvsdatter needed a licence for kinship in the 2nd degree (first cousins), which fits the existing hypothesis that his wife Gunild Andersdatter was Toruv Andersen's sister (not proved). ¶5

Patronymic settled: the probate writes 'Tron Toresøn', agreeing with the burial ('Torsen'); the 1756 churching's 'Torofsen' is a slip. That favours the existing Speculative link to Tore Trondssen of vestre Felle (d. by 1733). ¶8

Probate details: creditors included Joen Toresøn Fæle (43 rd, lent and owed) and Torof Andersøn Hiemdahl (33 rd). Joen Toresen is probably his brother, the 'Jon 11' at the 1733 Felle probate (inferred). The deceased had bought a share of Felle; it had been allotted to the late Laurs Brinck at a skifte passeret 7 Aug 1743, 'ved det at hand paatagit sig at betale sin Moders Gield' (because he had undertaken to pay his mother's debt), and Brinck deeded it to Tron on 16 Apr 1751. That 1743 skifte is probably the estate of Tron's mother (inferred; next record). ¶9

Buried at Treungen church on the 1st Sunday after Trinity (12 Jun) 1757: 'kast Jord paa Troen Torsen Fælle, som blev borte i [?] ved Tvedsundet, gl. 47'. That is, Tron Torsen Felle, who was lost (drowned) at Tvedsundet, aged 47 (Kviteseid ministerialbok 1754-1773, burials p.10, image 157). The identification is inferred, but strong: he is the only Tron at Felle in 1754-1786; b. ~1710 fits children Astri (~1739) and Tore (~1741), with Tore carrying Tron's father's name; his widow Gunild Andersdatter (b. ~1717) was still a widow after one marriage in 1801; and his children married 1778-81 with no father named. This gives his patronymic Torsen (son of Tore). ¶3

Name: his 1757 burial entry calls him 'Troen Torsen Fælle', which supplies the patronymic Torsen. The name field is changed from 'Tron Felle' accordingly (the identification is inferred, see above). ¶4

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: his probate settles his identity and family. Øvre Telemark (vestfjelske) skifteprotokoll nr. 9, skifte no. 244, concluded 19 Nov 1757 (begun with registration and valuation on 6 Oct): 'Skifte efter afgl. Tron Toresøn Fælle' on the farm Felle in Treungen annex of Hvidesøe (Kviteseid) prestegjeld, between his widow Gunnild Andersdaatter and their children: sons Tore Tronsøn 16 and Anders Tronsøn 4; daughters Astri, Anne, Ingeborg, Hage and Gro Trondsdøttre, aged 19, 13, 11, 6 and about 1. This ties the man buried 12 Jun 1757 to the widow and to the son Tore (60 in 1801) already in the tree. Read on the image. ¶7

Records that disagree 1

Against: his burial age (47 in 1757) implies birth about 1710, five years earlier than the probate age; and one 1756 entry writes his patronymic 'Torofsen'. ¶6b
