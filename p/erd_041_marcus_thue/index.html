About the family

Father proved: the probate after Lars Marcussen of Eitungjerde, 1 May 1758 (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll A 7b, fol. 408a-408b), lists among the heirs 'Ældste Søn Marcus Larsen Thue' (eldest son Marcus Larsen Thue), of age and present. So Marcus Thue was Marcus Larsen, born at or connected with Eitungjerde, which strongly supports his identity with the groom 'Marcus Larsøn Eithungierde' who married Siri Pedersdatter Øyre on 27 Nov 1751. His siblings in that probate: Giøe Larsdatter (wife of Niels [H]ansen Eitungjerde), Hermund Larsen (very probably the Hermund Larsen of Thue who was godfather to both of Marcus's sons named Lars, 1755 and 1761) and the then-minor Christi Larsdatter (probably the 'Christie Larsdatter' who was godmother to Anna Marcusdatter Thue in 1759). His stepmother or mother: Unni Hermundsdatter, Lars's widow (relationship to Marcus not stated). The probate does not name Marcus's wife. ¶13

Mother (Speculative): his father Lars Eitungjerde was betrothed to 'N. Christensd.' at Tjugum about October 1724 (Leikanger ministerialbok 1690-1734, canvas 137 R). No other wife of Lars is known. Marcus's own baptism (after 1727) is still to be found in canvases 156-170 of that book (1728-1734). ¶16

Mother: his father's betrothal to N. Christensdatter is of Oct 1719, not 1724 (see Christensdatter), so Marcus, the eldest son, was very probably born of that marriage about 1720-23, and the Christensdatter moves to Probable as his mother. His baptism was not found in 11564 for Jul 1721 to Jul 1723 (canvases 149-155; 1722-23 are thin) and should be sought in 1720 to Jul 1721 (canvases 143 R-148). ¶17

Life story

Possible marriage (Probable lead, not proved): the only betrothal or marriage of any Marcus in Leikanger between April 1748 and December 1751 (ministerialbok 1735-1756, canvases 84-124 read in full) is 'Sponsalia Marcus Larsøn Eithungierde / Siri Peders Daattr Øyre', sureties Lars Dahlen and Anders Hambre (autumn 1751), married 27 November 1751 ('Nupt:... item Marcus Larsøn Eitung: Siri Pedersd: Øyre', folio 124). Ingeri Marcusdatter Thue was baptised five and a half months later. Naming fits: Ingri's eldest full brother was Peder (bride's father Peder), a brother Lars (groom's father Lars), and Peder's and Marcus jr.'s daughters were both named Sigri/Sigrid. Not proved because no record yet calls Marcus Thue 'Marcus Larsen' or names his wife; the farm differs (Eitungjerde in 1751, Thue in 1752). ¶7

Baptism of daughter: on Exaudi Sunday, 14 May 1752, the Leikanger ministerialbok (chronological list 1735-1756, folio 129) records 'Bapt: Ingeri Marcus Daattr Thue', a daughter of Marcus of the farm Thue (Tue). Godparents: Hans Thue, Anfind Kolbensen Brynild[?], Margrete Stipshammer[?], [Mari?] Thue and Sygri Einersdatter Qvame. The book names the father only by first name and farm; the mother is not named. ¶1

Churching of his wife: on the 3rd Sunday after Trinity, 18 June 1752, 'Marcus Thues Qv[inde]' (Marcus Thue's wife) was introduced (churched after childbirth) at Tjugum church (same book, folio 130). So the family belonged to Tjugum church, the parish where Ingri Marcusdatter's brothers Peder (Tenningen) and Lars (Menes) later lived. ¶2

Baptism of son Peder, 16 September 1753, Tjugum: 'Bapt: Peder Marcussøn Thue', godparents Monsr. Hans Leganger, Lars Eithungierde, Ole Nielssøn Supramer, Jomfru Anne Leganger and Anne Nielsdatter Supramer (ministerialbok 1735-1756, folio 141). His wife was churched at Tjugum on 21 October 1753 ('Introd: Marcus Thues Qvinde puerp.', folio 142). This is Ingri's eldest full brother Peder Marchussen, later of Tenningen. ¶9

Baptism of son Lars (the first), 31 August 1755, at Rinde: 'bapt: Lars Marcussøn Thue', godparents Niels Nypham, Anfind Nielssøn Orglem[?], Hermund Larssøn Thue, Anna Stockeboe[?] and Giertrud Thue (ministerialbok 1735-1756, folio 166); wife churched at Tjugum at Michaelmas 1755 (folio 167). This Lars was buried on 24 May 1759. ¶10

Baptism of a second child, 18 March 1759: 'Bapt: Anna Marcus Daattr Thue' (Leikanger ministerialbok 1756-1770, folio 30), godparents Baar Bothebøe[?], Ole Thue, Margareta Nypham, Agathe Baarsdatter and Christie Larsdatter. Margareta Nypham(mer) was also godmother at Ingeri Marcusdatter Thue's baptism on 14 May 1752 (re-read: 'Margrete Nyphammer', not 'Stipshammer'; the other godparents there read 'Anfind Kolbens: Brugield' and 'Unni Thue'). His wife was churched at Tjugum on Easter Monday, 16 April 1759 ('introd: Marcus Thues... qvinder puerp:', folio 33). ¶4

Burial of a son, Ascension Day 24 May 1759, Tjugum: 'Lars Marcussøn Thue, 3 an 6 m.' (aged 3 years 6 months, so born about Nov 1755; folio 34). Ingri's proved brother Lars Marchussen of Menes (aged 41 in 1801) is therefore very probably a later son given his dead brother's name. ¶5

Godfather: 'Marcus Thue' stood godfather on 19 November 1760 at the baptism of Ole Hansøn Thue (ministerialbok 1756-1770, folio 51). No baptism of a child of his was found from December 1759 to December 1760; the surviving son Lars (aged 41 in 1801) was probably born 1761-62. ¶8

Baptism of son Lars (the second), Maundy Thursday 19 March 1761, Tjugum: 'Bapt: Lars Marcussøn Thue', godparents Niels Brugield, Hans Helgesøn Thue, Hermund Larsøn of Thue, Madam Dorthea Leganger and Sara Nielsdatter Qvalem (ministerialbok 1756-1770, folio 55); wife churched at Tjugum on Whit Monday, 11 May 1761 (folio 57). This is Ingri's brother Lars Marchussen of Menes (d. 1811), named after the brother who died in 1759. ¶11

Probate: 'A° 1764 d. 6te October' (last digit an old-style 4; earlier read as 1769 but the children's ages and the volume's 1763-1766 span fit 1764) a registration and division was held on the farm Thue in Qvamsøe skibrede after 'Marcus Larsen', between his widow 'Sigri Pedersdatter' and their 4 sons and 2 daughters: eldest son Peder Marcussen 11 years, second son Johannes Marcussen 5 (or 8) years, third son Lars Marcussen 4 years, fourth son Marcus Marcussen 1 year, eldest daughter Ingerie Marcusdatter 11 years and second daughter Anna Marcusdatter 5 years. Guardian for the children: Niels Sa[m]sonsen(?) Eithungierde; the widow's lauverge (legal guardian): Niels Larsen Thue. Gross estate carried forward 130 rd. (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll L0008a, 1763-1766, fol. 249a, entry no. 4, Digitalarkivet 24165 view 251.) The ages match the baptisms of Peder (Sept 1753), Ingeri (May 1752), Anna (Mar 1759) and the second Lars (Mar 1761); Johannes and Marcus jr. (born about 1763) are new children. No mother or sibling of Marcus is named in the parts read. ¶14

Continuation 8 Feb 1765 (fol. 249b-250a, view 252): the widow Sigri Pedersdatter produced three earlier probate letters proving land she had inherited: after her father 'Peder Johannesen(?) Øre', held at Øre in Indre Sogn 8 Nov 1727; after her sister Kari Pedersdatter, Øre 10 Nov 1727 (Sigri inherited from her alone); and after her mother 'Anna Torbiørnsdatter', Øre in Indre Sogn and Norum(?) skibrede, 30 May 1733. Her land shares lay in Qvalem (Ytre Sogn), Bariøynes(?) and Rinde (Indre Sogn). The right-hand page lists the distribution (udlæg), with 'Hermund Larsen Thue' among the takers. ¶15

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Why Probable: he is the father of Ingri Marcusdatter (see her page) if the 1752 baptism is hers. It fits her name, patronymic and parish, but her census ages (52 in 1801, 67 in 1815) point to birth about 1748-49, three to four years earlier, and his other children (Peder, Marcus, Lars, Anna, named as her full siblings in the 1811 probate of Lars Marchussen) have not yet been found as children of Marcus Thue. A baptism of Lars (about 1759-60) or Peder as a son of Marcus Thue, or Marcus Thue's marriage or burial, would settle it. ¶3

Confidence moved Probable to Confirmed: two baptisms name him as father of two of the five full siblings proved by the 1811 probate of Lars Marchussen (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll A 14, fol. 280a-281a): 'Ingeri Marcus Daattr Thue', 14 May 1752 (ministerialbok 1735-1756, folio 129) and 'Anna Marcus Daattr Thue', 18 March 1759 (ministerialbok 1756-1770, folio 30), with a shared godmother (Margareta Nyphammer) and his wife churched at Tjugum after both; plus a son Lars (buried 1759), the name of a third proved sibling. The identification of each child with the probate siblings rests on name, patronymic and parish (Tjugum). ¶6

The case for this identification 1

Identity with Marcus Larsen Eitungjerde (Probable): Marcus Larsøn Eitungierde married Siri Pedersdatter Øyre on 27 November 1751, the only marriage of any Marcus in Leikanger from April 1748 to December 1751. Marcus Thue's first child Ingeri was baptised five and a half months later; his first son (1753) was named Peder, like Siri's father, and had a Lars Eitungjerde as godfather; his next sons were named Lars, like Marcus Larsen's father. A Hermund Larsen of Thue was godfather to both sons named Lars (1755, 1761) and may be a brother. ¶12a

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Not proved: no record names Marcus Thue's wife or calls him 'Larsen'. His father was very probably the Lars of Eitungjerde (inferred, not stated in any record). A probate after Marcus Thue or his wife, or a burial entry giving the wife's name, would settle it. ¶12b
