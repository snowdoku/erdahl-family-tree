Life story

John Eveleigh, M.A., of Devon, gent. Matriculated at Exeter College, Oxford, 9 Dec 1575, aged 16; fellow 1578-93; B.A. 1581, M.A. 1584; Principal of Hart Hall from 1599 until his death; buried in Magdalen chapel (St Mary Magdalen), Oxford, in 1604 (Foster, Alumni Oxonienses). He married Prudence, youngest daughter of Robert Barnes, M.D.: Barnes's will of 1 Jan 1603/4 names 'Prudence, wife of John Eveleigh, M.A. and principall of Hart hall' (Wood, Survey of Oxford, vol. 3, p. 248). The Heverly family history gives children Frances (baptised Oxford 21 Apr 1603), George (baptised 11 Jun 1604) and John, later Dean of Ross. Added. ¶1

Leases of one Magdalen College house (St John's Hospital property) on Canditch, St Mary Magdalen parish, Oxford, printed by Salter (Cartulary of the Hospital of St John the Baptist, vol. 2, pp. 314-315): 28 Jun 1588 to Robert Barnes, doctor in physic; 27 Jul 1604 to 'John Eveleighe, M.A., principal of Hart Hall' (so he took over his father-in-law's house within months of Barnes's death); 7 Dec 1615 to 'Prudence Eveleighe, widow', renewed 1632 and 1638; and, after Prudence's burial in 1652, on 10 Aug 1654 to 'John Eveleigh, of St Giles, Oxford, clerk'. The rentals (vol. 3, pp. 319, 324, 327) follow the same house: 'Domus in tenura doctoris Barnes [Joh' Eueleigh]', then 'Prudence Eveleigh, widow', then 'Mr. Eveleigh'. The 1654 lessee, a clergyman of St Giles, fits the Dean of Ross (John Eveleigh), who was out of Ireland in the 1640s-50s and later left a St Giles lease and money to the poor of St Giles. This is a continuity of tenure from Barnes to the Principal, to his widow, to a clerk John Eveleigh, but no lease calls the 1654 lessee their son. Grade unchanged (Probable, on his link to John Eveleigh). Wood's St Mary Magdalen register extract has 'Mr. John Eveley, buried 10 Aug. 1604' (Survey, vol. 3, p. 224). ¶3a

Superseded: the notes above saying that 'no record found names the Dean as his son' and that the link must stay Probable because the marriage date is unknown. Nicholas Evelegh's codicil of 1611 now names the relationship. The St Mary Magdalen register (Scarr) is still worth reading for the Dean's baptism date. ¶8

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 3

Probable, graded on his link to his son John Eveleigh (Dean of Ross): the Heverly family history (pp. 39-40, drawing on Vivian's Visitations of Devon) makes the Dean the son of this John and Prudence, with the Dean's wife Mildred Caldwell and the same five daughters as the Blennerhassett Pedigree. The circumstances fit: the Dean was born about 1598-1600 (B.A. Wadham 1618-19), held a lease of Blackhall in St Giles, Oxford, and left money to the poor of St Giles, where 'Mris.... Eveleigh, widdow' (Prudence) was buried on 14 Aug 1652. But no record found names the Dean as his son. ¶2a

Prudence's will read, grade unchanged (Probable, graded on his link to his son John Eveleigh). Her nuncupative will (PROB 11/224/477, image read) names 'her sonne John Eveleigh', heir to her Blackhall lease, who is the Dean (see Prudence Barnes). It does not name the boy's father. That the Dean's father was this John, her husband, is inferred: she was his wife by 1 Jan 1603/4 (her father's will), his widow from 1604 to 1652 under the surname Eveleigh, and no other husband is known. But their marriage date is unknown, so it cannot be shown that the marriage covers the Dean's whole birth window (he was a vicar by 1620, so born by about 1597; audit 5 rule 1). ¶5a

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed, graded on his link to his son John Eveleigh (Dean of Ross). Record: the will of his brother Nicholas Evelegh of the Middle Temple, TNA PROB 11/131/535, read on the register image. In the codicil of 3 Nov 1611 (9 James I) Nicholas, after leaving his grist mills in Bovey Tracey to his wife Alice for life, writes: 'And after her decease I give bequeath and devise all those my grist milles And all other the landes which I have to me and my heires unto John Evelegh sonne of John Evelegh my brother late of Oxford deceased to have and to hold to him and his heires for ever'. The will itself is dated 13 Oct 1600 (witnesses Josias Evelegh, Gervase Ommingham [?], Richard Todman [?]); proved May 1618. The record names the relationship: the Principal ('John Evelegh my brother late of Oxford deceased') had a son John, alive in Nov 1611. ¶6

The case for this identification 1

Continuity: the testator's 'brother late of Oxford deceased' is the Principal, who died at Oxford in 1604. He is the only John Eveleigh known at Oxford in 1575-1611, and Vivian (p. 336) makes him a brother of Nicholas (2nd and 5th sons of John Eveleigh of Holcombe). The son John is then followed to the Dean through the Principal's widow. (1) Magdalen College leased the Principal's Canditch house to 'Prudence Eveleighe, widow' on 7 Dec 1615 (Salter), four years after the codicil. (2) Her own will of 1652 (PROB 11/224/477, image read) names 'her sonne John Eveleigh', who is the Dean (Blackhall lease chain; see Prudence Barnes). (3) In 1654 the same Magdalen house was leased to 'John Eveleigh, of St Giles, Oxford, clerk'. No rival is named: no other son John of the Principal is known, and the Heverly/Vivian pedigree gives him only the one marriage, to Prudence. The nephew of 1611 and Prudence's son John of 1652 could be different people only if the Principal had two living sons both named John, and no record suggests that. This replaces the earlier birth-window argument (audit 5, rule 1): the father-son relationship is now named in a record, so the date of the Eveleigh-Barnes marriage no longer matters. ¶7

Searches that found nothing 1

Oxford registers on FamilySearch: no Eveleigh is indexed in Oxford 1580-1660 (England Births and Christenings, England Marriages, or the England, Oxfordshire, Parish Registers 1538-1904 collection). The St Mary Magdalen register exists in J. R. Scarr's typed transcript, The Parish Registers of St Mary Magdalen, Oxford, vol. 1 (baptisms 1577-1726, marriages 1574-1726, burials 1565-1726), FHL film 1559432 item 2, DGS 7955220, but the images are locked from home. That volume should show the baptisms of Frances (1603), George (1604) and perhaps John (the Dean, c.1598-1600) with their father's name, and John Eveleigh's burial in 1604. ¶4a
