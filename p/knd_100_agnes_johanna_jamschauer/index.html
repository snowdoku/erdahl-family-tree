Life story

Marriage: Agnes Johanna 'Jamschauer' (surname as indexed; reading uncertain), daughter of Herr Johann Jacob, married Joh Christoph Lindenmaier, son of Johann Christoph, at Bietigheim on 1 Dec 1696 (bride row 907270021, groom row 7270021, image 1184899-00367). Children: Nicolaus 1703, Sophia Rosina Catharina 1706 (7268511). ¶2

Probable burial: row 'Agnes Johanne Nicolaus Liedmanners', burial, Bietigheim, 21 Apr 1748, no relatives (record 7057866, image 1184901-00752). The indexer seems to have run together her name and that of her son Nicolaus Lindenmaier ('Liedmanners' for Lindenmaiers; e.g. 'Agnes Johanna, Nicolaus Lindenmaiers Mutter'). Not entered as a death date, because the surname reading is uncertain. Her own baptism (about 1670-78) is not indexed under any Agnes Johanna of 1664-80 (only Böckeler, Backnang 1673; Kemm, Altburg 1679). ¶3a

Burial (image read): died 19 Apr 1748, buried 21 Apr 1748 at Bietigheim, aged 77 years and some months (the month digit looks like 7; the FS index gives 77 years, birth 1671). The guess that the index row 'Agnes Johanne Nicolaus Liedmanners' stood for 'Nicolaus Lindenmaiers Mutter' is wrong: the entry calls her the widow of the coppersmith (Johann) Christoph Lindenmeier, and the 'Nicolai' in it is a struck-out slip. Earlier notes holding her at Probable, including the Correction note of (Audit 10), are superseded. ¶9

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 4

Graded on that link: the Bietigheim baptism of 'Nicolaus Lindenmeister', 23 Jan 1703, names 'Johann Christoph, Agnes Johanna' (Ancestry 61023 record 7268393, image 1184899-00264). The 1703 child is identified with the 1737 groom Nicolaus, son of 'Hn Johann Christoph' (record 5517933), by name, parish and father's name; the only other Nicolaus Lindenmaier there is a generation older. Ancestry 61023 index rows do not label roles (BRIEF audit 5 rule 3), so this rests at Probable until the image or a labelled FamilySearch twin is read; images not seen. ¶1b

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed, graded on her link to her son Nicolaus Lindenmaier (see his page). FamilySearch, 'Germany, Births and Baptisms, 1558-1898', V4LM-GV4: Nicolaus Lindenmeyer, born 23 Jan 1704, christened Bietigheim 24 Jan 1704, Parents 'Johann Christoff Lindenmeyer, Agnes Jo[hanna]'. Continuity: his burial at Bietigheim 23 Jul 1767 gives birth 1704 from his age (QPGP-PDG3), and his 1737 marriage names his father 'Hn Johann Christoph Lindenmajers' (QPGY-D23L); he baptised children at Bietigheim 1738-61. The rival Johann Christoph Lindenmaier of Bietigheim (m. 1694) has no Nicolaus. Her marriage: FamilySearch, 'Deutschland, ausgewählte evangelische Kirchenbücher 1500-1971' (= 'Germany, Lutheran Baptisms, Marriages, and Burials, 1500-1971') QPGP-8VBK, Bietigheim 1 Dec 1696, Wife 'Agnes Johanna Jamschauer', Father-in-law 'Herrn Johann Jacob Jamschauer'. Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed, graded on her link to her son Nicolaus Lindenmaier (see his page). This answers the Audit 10 test records. Naming record: the 1704 baptism image names Nicolaus's parents as 'H. Johann Christoff Lindenmeyer, Kupfferschmid' and 'ux. Agnes Johanna'. Continuity from the 1704 child to the 1737 groom in the tree, which is what Audit 10 found missing: the father is identified at both ends by full name AND trade, not by name alone. In 1704 he is a coppersmith (Kupferschmid) with wife Agnes Johanna; in 1737 Nicolaus is himself a coppersmith and the son of 'Hn Johann Christoph Lindenmajer, Feldmesser und Kupferschmid zu Bietigheim'; the surveyor is shown at Bietigheim inside the gap (1721, 1731); and in 1748 Agnes Johanna dies as the widow of the coppersmith ('[Johann] Christoph Lindenmeiers gewesenen Burgers und Kupferschmids'). So the man who fathered Nicolaus in 1704 with Agnes Johanna and the father named in 1737 are the same coppersmith, married to Agnes Johanna from 1696 until his death before 1748. Window (audit 5 rule 1): Nicolaus's burial age (63 years 2 months in July 1767) puts his birth between about Jul 1703 and Jul 1704; the coppersmith's marriage to Agnes Johanna (1696) and her widowhood (1748) cover the whole window, so whichever birth is meant, she is the mother. Rivals checked: the other Johann Christoph Lindenmaiers of Bietigheim are the elder Burgermeister (already dead in 1696, 'gewesen... hinterlassener Sohn'), the 'Herr Joh Christoph' who married Maria Löffler in 1694 (no son Nicolaus; probably the same elder man), and the Johann Christoph, son of Johann Christoph, who married in Feb 1737 (a generation too young). None is called a coppersmith, and no other Nicolaus baptism of 1703-04 is indexed at Bietigheim. The 1748 scribe first wrote 'Nicolai' before her husband's name, which fits her living in Nicolaus's household, but the grade does not rest on that. Image read signed in on FamilySearch, 2 Nov 2026. ¶7

Audit 12 (2 Nov 2026): Confirmed KEPT (raised back), graded on her link to Nicolaus Lindenmaier (see his page). Audit 10 lowered her because the father's full name was shared at Bietigheim. The images now show the father by name AND trade at each step: coppersmith with wife Agnes Johanna in 1704, 'Feldmesser und Kupferschmid' named as Nicolaus's father in 1737 (Nicolaus himself a coppersmith), surveyor at Bietigheim in 1721 and 1731, and Agnes Johanna dying in 1748 as the coppersmith's widow. None of the other Johann Christophs is called a coppersmith. That meets audit 10 point 2 (a name unique in the town once the trade is added), and the 1696-1748 marriage covers Nicolaus's whole birth window (audit 5 rule 1). ¶12

The case for this identification 1

The chain, all from images read: (1) 1696 Bietigheim marriage: 'Joh. Christoph Lindenmaier, Kupferschmidt, H. Johann Christoph Lindenmaiers, gewesenen [...] und Burgermeisters allhier hinterlassener ehelicher Sohn' and 'Agnes Johanna, Herrn Johann Jacob J[amschauer/Jenisch]s, gewesenen Gerichtsverwandten und Kupferschmids zu Brackenheim hinterlassene eheliche Tochter'. (2) 1704 Bietigheim baptism, 23 Jan [born] / 24 Jan [baptised]: child 'Nicolaus', parents 'H. Johann Christoff Lindenmeyer, Kupfferschmid [...], ux. Agnes Johanna'. (3) LABW finding aids: 'examinierter Feldtmesser Johan Christoph Lindenmajer von Biettigh[eim]', map of 29 Sep 1721 (HStA Stuttgart A 249 Bü 270), and a Feldmesser Lindenmaier of Bietigheim [1731] (A 249 Bü 268), so the man is at Bietigheim inside the gap. (4) 1737 Metterzimmern marriage (p.60): 'Nicolaus Lindenmajer, Kupferschmid, Hn Johann Christoph Lindenmajers, Feldmessers und Kupferschmids zu Bietigheim ehl. Sohn' married Catharina, daughter of Hn Jacob Schopf, gewesener Schultheiß there. (5) 1748 Bietigheim burial: 'd. 19. [April] Starb Agnes Johanna, [a forename struck through, apparently 'Nicolai', with a correction written above, apparently 'Christoph'] Lindenmeiers gewesenen Burgers und Kupferschmids allhier [widow], aetat. 77 Jahr [7?] Monath, und wurde d. 21. begraben'. (6) 1767 Bietigheim burial: Nicolaus Lindenmajer died 21 Jul, buried 23 Jul 1767, aged '63 Jahr 2 Mon. [..] Tag'. ¶8

Corrections 2

Correction: Audit 10 (2 Nov 2026): lowered from Confirmed to Probable, graded on her link to her son Nicolaus Lindenmaier (see his page). The baptism V4LM-GV4 (Bietigheim 24 Jan 1704, parents 'Johann Christoff Lindenmeyer, Agnes Jo[hanna]') names her as the mother of a Nicolaus born 1704. The tree's Nicolaus is the groom of 3 Sep 1737 (son of 'Hn Johann Christoph') and the coppersmith who died by 1774. The step from the 1704 child to the 1737 groom spans 33 years with no record in between that names Nicolaus with these parents or a sibling. It rests on (1) the father's forename, which is not unique: at least two other men named Johann Christoph Lindenmaier lived at Bietigheim then, the 'Herr Joh Christoph' who married Maria Löffler on 19 Aug 1694 (possibly the elder Hans Christoph, buried 16 Dec 1694, but not proved) and a Johann Christoph, son of Johann Christoph, who married on 5 Feb 1737; (2) the 1767 burial age (birth '1704' derived from age); and (3) the absence of another Nicolaus in the index. That is an identification by age and uniqueness (BRIEF grading rule: elimination stays Probable; audit 3: 20 years or more with no record in between gives Probable). Audit 9 rule 2 (father and child named at both ends) does not rescue it, because the father is not shown at Bietigheim inside the gap and his forename is shared. Test records, either of which would restore Confirmed: (a) the burial image of 21 Apr 1748 (Ancestry 1184901-00752, indexed 'Agnes Johanne Nicolaus Liedmanners'), if it reads 'Nicolaus Lindenmaiers Mutter' or similar; (b) Nicolaus's burial image of 23 Jul 1767 (QPGP-PDG3), if it names his parents; or (c) a record of 1705-1736 naming Nicolaus as a son of Johann Christoph and Agnes Johanna (confirmation, a sibling's probate or marriage). The upgrade note is superseded on this point. Her own marriage (1696, labelled) and her father Johann Jacob Jamschauer's grade are not affected. ¶5

Correction: maiden name changed from 'Jamschauer' to Jenisch, and birth set to about Oct 1670 at Brackenheim (christened there 14 Oct 1670, NVZ2-B9Z). 'Jamschauer' was an indexer's reading of her 1696 Bietigheim marriage. That entry calls her the daughter of the late Johann Jacob J., Gerichtsverwandter and coppersmith at Brackenheim, and the Brackenheim register shows exactly one such man: Hanß/Johann Jacob Jenisch, coppersmith and burgher there from 1667, of the town court and a widower in 1682, and dead by 1695 (images read). Her 1670 baptism names 'Hanß Jacob Jenisch, Maria Juliana', and her burial age (77 years and about 7 months in April 1748) points to a birth about Sep 1670, which agrees. The id keeps the old spelling. Her grade (link to her son Nicolaus) is unchanged. Image read signed in on FamilySearch, 2 Nov 2026. ¶11

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Baptism and surname (identification Probable, so the baptism date is not entered as her birth date): FamilySearch NVZ2-B9Z, 'Germany, Births and Baptisms', Agnes Johanna, christened at Brackenheim 14 Oct 1670, Father's Name 'Hanss Jacob Jenisch', Mother's Name 'Maria Juliana' (image read: 'Hanß Jacob Jenisch, Maria Juliana', child 'Agnes Johanna', no trade given). It fits: the 1696 marriage calls her the daughter of the late Johann Jacob J., Gerichtsverwandter and coppersmith at Brackenheim; her burial age (77 years and about 6-7 months in April 1748) points to autumn 1670; and the two independent 'Germany, Marriages' extractions read her surname 'Janisch' (JH63-T8W) and 'Jenisch' (VCYM-3QZ, placed at Brackenheim). Her maiden name was most likely Jenisch; 'Jamschauer' is the Lutheran-index reading of the 1696 entry, whose surname word is hard to read on the image. Kept as Jamschauer in the name field until the 1696 word is settled. ¶10a

Gap evidence, grade unchanged (Probable): Audit 10 noted that her husband Johann Christoph Lindenmaier was not shown at Bietigheim between Nicolaus's baptism (1704) and his marriage (1737). A Landesarchiv finding aid now shows an 'examinierter Feldtmesser Johan Christoph Lindenmajer von Biettigh[eim]' signing a map on 29 Sep 1721 (HStA Stuttgart A 249 Bü 270), and a Feldmesser Lindenmaier of Bietigheim about 1731. This narrows the gap for the father (1704-1721-1737, 17 and 16 years) but his forename is still shared and the catalogue does not identify which Johann Christoph he was (Audit 10: a father bridges only if his name is unique or a record shows him, identified, inside the gap). The test records named by Audit 10 still stand (the 21 Apr 1748 burial image 1184901-00752; Nicolaus's 1767 burial). ¶6
