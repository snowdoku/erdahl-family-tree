Parents (Probable): Tallef Gundstensen and Aslaug Salvesdatter of Søftestad. Her probate of 15 Nov 1728 (25668, skifte no. 154, fol. 233a) names their son 'Ole Tallefsøn 9½ aar' (born about 1719) and a younger son also named Ole (5). See the notes on Tallef Gundstensen Søftestad. This grades the parents' link to him; his own grade (his link to Tellev Olsen) is unchanged. The probate supports reading his patronymic as Tallefsen (Tellevsen). ¶11

Other children (same page): 'Johannes [Olsen] erholdt Skiøde af sin Fader Ole Tallefsen' on 1 tønde of Søftestad, dated 9 Jun 1787, tingl. 31 Aug 1787 (No 1663); Johannes Olsen sold it on to Tallef Olsen in 1788. This proves Johannes Olsen Søftestad (married 1787) a son of Ole Tallefsen and a brother of Tellev. ¶13

His father's probate, Nordbø in Nissedal, 23 Oct 1755 (25673 No 154, views 272-273), names him 'Ole Tallevsøn Myndig', eldest son of Tallev Günstensøn by his first wife Aslaug Saavesdaatter; his brothers Saave and the younger Ole were then 'i Holland'; other siblings Torgiej and Sigri, half-sister Aaslaug (by the widow Sigri Torbiørnsdaatter). This bridges 1728 to his 1754 Søftestad purchase. ¶16

Life story

Burial: 'Gaardmand Ole Tallevsen[?] Søftestad af 80 Aar' (farm owner Ole Tallevsen of Søftestad, aged 80, so b. about 1719) was buried in Nissedal church on 28 Jun 1799. Kviteseid ministerialbok 1787-1799 (source 7983), burials, canvas 255, right page from the overview image (patronymic uncertain; not cropped). The patronymic, if read correctly, agrees with 'Ole Tallefsen Søftestad', godfather in 1760, and supports Tallefsen as his patronymic. ¶4

His 1754 deed: the Søftestad page of panteregister I 1 (10915 fol. 44b, view 87 left) abstracts it as 'Gunsten Halvorsøn [Naa?] solgt [..] til Ole Tallefsøn efter Brev dat. 7[?] Mai 1754'. The seller was Gunsten Halvorsen, not Ole's father, so this deed will not name his father. Pantebok I 5 (10870) was checked for it: the October 1754 entries run about views 136-151 (Nos 349-405, mixed tinglag), and the entry was not reached; its full text might still state a kinship with the seller. Not needed for his own grade. ¶14

Baptism of a son: as 'Ole Søftestad' he had a son Tallef baptised in Nissedal church on 24 Mar 1760; godparents Ole Midbøe, Hans Næs, Sigri Søftestad, and Sigri and Aslaug Holt. The mother is not named (Kviteseid ministerialbok 1754-1773, p. 58). ¶1

Patronymic (inference): on 18 Apr 1760, on the same page, 'Ole Tallefsen Søftestad' stood godfather in Nissedal church to Hans, son of Aslach Grovum (other godparents Joen Olsen Midbøe, Hans Næs, Ragnild Kittilsdatter, Ragnild Aakhuus[?]). He is the only Ole at Søftestad seen in the pages read, so Ole Søftestad was probably Ole Tallefsen. That would make his son Tallef a namesake of his grandfather. A Tallef Søftestad baptised a daughter Anne in Nissedal church on 9 Nov 1759 (p. 55); he may be Ole's father or a brother, which is not determined. The name is recorded here as 'Ole Søftestad' because the patronymic is not proved. ¶2

Still alive in 1792: 'Ole Søftestad' stood godfather, with 'Anne Søftestad', at the baptism of Tellev Olsen Søftestad's daughter Anne in Nissedal about 16 Sep 1792 (Kviteseid ministerialbok 1787-1799, canvas 63). ¶6

Burial, re-read (crops of canvas 254): 'Den 28 Juni: i Nissedals Kirke paa [?] / [Gaard- or Lehns-?]mand Ole Tellevsen Søftestad af 80 Aar' (28 Jun [1799], in Nissedal church, Ole Tellevsen of Søftestad, [farm owner (gaardmand) or sheriff (lehnsmand)?], aged 80; so born about 1719). The entry is at the foot of the right page of canvas 254 (printed p. 258), not canvas 255 as earlier cited. Age '80 Aar' is clear. The word at the end of the first line, before 'mand', is not certain: it was read 'Gaard-' (farm owner), but on the crop its letters (tall looped initial, then something like 'ehns') fit 'Lehns-' (lehnsmand, sheriff) at least as well. A list of Nissedal/Kviteseid lensmenn about 1760-1799 would settle it. ¶8

Patronymic: the burial reads 'Ole Tellevsen' (Tellev's son; Tellev = Tallev, Tallef). All letters are legible; the first vowel (e or a) and the letter before 's' (v or f; it has a descender) are slightly uncertain, which does not change the name (Tellevsen/Tellefsen). This agrees with 'Ole Tallefsen Søftestad', godfather in Nissedal on 18 Apr 1760, and supports naming his son Tallef (b. 1760) after Ole's own father (inference). The person's name in the tree is left as 'Ole Søftestad' in this draft; the orchestrator may rename him Ole Tellevsen Søftestad. ¶9

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Confidence: Probable, as father of Tellev Olsen of Søftestad. The farm matches Tellev's 1801 household at Søftestad, and Tallef of 1760 is the only Tellev son of an Ole read in the Nissedal baptisms of 1759-1763. No record yet names Tellev Olsen's father directly. ¶3a

Upgrade: Confirmed, graded on his link to his son Tellev (Tallef) Olsen (see his page). The Vest-Telemark panteregister I 2 (source 10916, p. 783, view 131 right), under Mat. No 12 Søftestad in Nissedal, abstracts a contract registered 3 Mar 179[2] (No 2252): 'Tallef Olsen... Contract med hans Forældre Ole Tallefsen og Anne Johannesdatter', dated 13 Oct 1791. The record names the relationship (parents and son). Continuity to the Tellev Olsen in the tree: the same Søftestad page has Tallef Olsen buying the farm share from Johannes Olsen in 1788 (No 1750) and the probate after 'Kari Torjusdatter, Tallef Olsens' wife, 1803-04, whose heirs are Tallef Olsen Søftestad and children Ole, Ole, Torjus, Halvor and Jacob Tallefsen; the tree's Tellev Olsen was baptised 1760 as 'Ole Søftestads Søn', was 41 at Søftestad in 1801 with wife Kari Torjusdatter and sons Ole, Ole, Torjus and Halvor. Same farm, no gap, no rival Tallef Olsen there. Ole Tallefsen himself is on the same page from his deed of 1754 (tingl. 24 Oct 1754, No 479) to 1791, and he was buried in 1799 as Ole Tellevsen Søftestad, aged 80. ¶12

Searches that found nothing 1

Deed search: the reference 'No 479' for his 1754 deed does not hold. In pantebok I 5 (10870) No 479 (view 157 right) is a deed by Øver Halvorsen of Haugland annex, Forrisdal, read at Dahlen 10 Mar 1755. Entries there are numbered by ting round, not by date: Nos 490-492 (view 161) carry dates 26 Oct 1754, 17 Mar 1755 and 19 Jul 1754, and Nos 499-524 (views 164-172) fall in Oct 1755. The Søftestad deed (dated 7 May 1754, from Gunsten Halvorsen) was not found in views 152-172. Not needed for his own grade. ¶15
