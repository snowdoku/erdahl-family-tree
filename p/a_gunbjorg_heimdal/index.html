About the family

Her parents found. The probate after her father Targei Halvorsen at Sønderland, 25 May 1759 (skifteprotokoll 9, DA 25673 s. 472b, view 481, No. 321), lists 'Gundbiør Targeisdatter 17 aar' among the children of Targei and his widow Torbor Halvorsdatter. Her siblings were Halvor Targeisen (20), Kittil Targeisen (19, reading uncertain), Aasild Targeisdatter (15) and Sigri Targeisdatter (11). Her own grade is unchanged: it covers her link to her husband and son. ¶5

Life story

Betrothed on the 3rd day of Pentecost (12 May) 1761 in Treungen, and married there 1 Jul 1761: 'unge Karl Aasmund Evindsen Hiemdahl og Pigen Gundbjør Targies datter Sonderland'. The sureties were Jørgen Haugestad and Joen Hiemdahl (Kviteseid ministerialbok 1754-1773, p.39, image 123, read on the image). This gives her patronymic as Targiesdatter (Tarjeisdatter) and her home farm as Sonderland in Treungen, so her father was a Targie (Tarjei) of Sonderland. Her son Targie (1764) was named after him, as custom required. A Halvor Targeisen Sonderland married at Treungen in 1761 and again as a widower in 1763; he is probably her brother (not proven). The orchestrator may wish to rename her 'Gundbjør /Targiesdatter/ Sonderland'. ¶2

Name: her 1761 betrothal and marriage entry calls her 'Pigen Gundbjør Targies datter Sonderland' — the maid Gundbjør Targiesdatter of Sonderland — which supplies the patronymic and her home farm; the name field is changed from 'Gunbjørg (Gundborg) Heimdal' accordingly (and checked by the orchestrator). ¶3

Buried in Treungen on 11 May 1764 as Osmund Hiemdal's wife, aged 22, the same day her son Targie was baptized (Kviteseid 1754-1773 burials). Her given name is hard to read and may be Gundborg or Gunbjørg. Her patronymic is not stated. Tarje's daughter Gundbiør (b. ~1792) was probably named after her. ¶1

Buried at Treungen on 11 May 1764: 'Osmund Hiemdals Hustrue Gundbiør, gl. 22 aar' (Kviteseid ministerialbok 1754-1773, burials, image 169). The name Gundbiør is clear; the entry gives no patronymic, which rests on the 1761 betrothal. Her son Tarje's recorded birth date is the same day, so she probably died in or soon after childbirth (inferred). ¶4

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
