Life story

Baptism: Kirsti, child of Gulbrand Amundsen and Elli Arnesdatter of Hvinden, was baptised at Gran church on New Year's Day (1 Jan) 1761. Godparents: Torsten Hvinden, Hans Kirkestad (farm name uncertain), Christopher Hvinden, Ingeri Larsdatter Hvinden and Anne Hans... (the rest of the name is cut off). Gran church book 1759-1775, fol. 19v (not indexed; scan). ¶2

Was living at Hvinden when she married Peder Nielsen of Fallang on 3 Nov 1789 at Gran. She was 40 in the 1801 census at Øvre Fallang. Mother of Eli (bapt. 1793). Hans Gulbrandsen and Jøel Hansen of Hvinden Eie, probably her brother and a relative, were Eli's godparents. Her parents (father Gulbrand...) are not identified, because Gran baptisms before 1787 are not transcribed. ¶1

The 1789 marriage scan (Gran 1787-1824 p. 447) gives no age or father. Her father had been buried in February 1789. ¶4

Probate (index cards): at the estate of Gulbrand Amundsen of Hvinden, Gran, held 16 Apr 1789 (Hadeland, Land og Valdres skifteprotokoll 1783-1802, f. 305a), the heirs included Kirsti, 28, with widow Ellie Arnesdatter. At the widow's estate in April 1798 (same protocol, f. 924a) she is 'Kirstie g.m. Peder Nilsen Fallanger'. Together with her 1761 baptism and her 1789 marriage to Peder Nielsen of Fallang, this names her parents outright. She is now Confirmed by these probate cards (raised from Probable). ¶5

1 on the identification

The case for this identification 1

Identification: the child's name, the father's name and the farm match her 1789 marriage, where she was a maiden of Hvinden. The year fits her 1801 census age of 40 exactly. Her brother Hans Gulbrandsen (b. about 1773) lived with their widowed mother Eli Arnesdatter at Hvinden Søndre in 1801. Hans and Joel Hansen of Hvinden, who was married to Anne Gulbrandsdatter, were godparents to Kirsti's daughter Eli in 1793, and that daughter was named for Kirsti's mother. She was baptised two weeks before her parents married. The entry does not call her illegitimate. Probable rather than Confirmed only because the link rests on matching names, farm and age, not on a record that states it outright. ¶3
