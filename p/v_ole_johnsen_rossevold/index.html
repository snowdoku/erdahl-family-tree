About the family

Parents (Probable): he is taken to be this Ole, son of Jon Arnesen of Melset in Ørskog and his wife Giertrud Knudsdatter. The grounds are: his 1851 Borgund burial says he was born at Melset in Ørskog parish, aged 68 (born about 1782-83); at his 1819 marriage he was of 'Mælseth'; the 1801 census of Melseth, Ørskog, household 03, lists Ole Jonsen, 18, as a child of the householder Jon Arnesen (index); an Ole Jonsen of Mælseth stood as godparent at Ørskog in 1815 and 1816 (index rows only); and the 1782 baptism above is the only Ole baptised to a Melset father in the index searches made, which the searchers note may not be a complete list. Against it: his 1819 marriage gives his age as 32 (born about 1786-87). That age fits better another Ørskog boy, Ole son of Jon of Strømsem, baptised 28 Oct 1787 (index pd00000010525773) and still living at Strømsem, aged about 13, in the 1801 census (index). That Ole is the only rival found. He is not preferred, because the burial names Melset as the birthplace and the 1819 groom was of Mælseth, not Strømsem. His later life has not been traced, though, so he is not ruled out. No record states the link outright, so it stays Probable. A confirmation or a probate after Jon Arnesen naming Ole of Røssevold would settle it; the 5 Jun 1818 Sunnmøre probate of a 'John Arnesen' is a candidate, not yet read. This supersedes the earlier notes saying his parents were not found and that no Ole of the right date was found at Melset. ¶9

Siblings (index): in the 1801 Melseth household 03 with him were Knud, 23, and Birgte, 10. Birgte is probably the Birgte Eline baptised Jan 1791 to Jon of Mælset (index; the father row has no surname). Children Arne (baptised 4 Apr 1778) and Maren (1784) were born to a Jon Arnesen of Westre (index rows). He may be the same man before he settled at Melset; that is not shown, and neither child is in the 1801 household. ¶10

Life story

Marriage: The Borgund clerk's register (klokkerbok 1816-1827, p. 382 no. 20) records the marriage in church on 13 June 1819 (1st Sunday after Trinity) of the bachelor and farmer (ungkarl og jordbruger) Ole Johnsen of 'Mælseth' (the first vowel could also be read as 'a', Malseth), aged 32, and the widow Anne Martha Rasmusdatter of Røssevold, aged 40. The marriage witnesses (forlovere) were John Olsen of Blindheim ('Blinden') and Ananias Tyggesen of Røssevold. His father is not named. Age 32 points to a birth about 1786-87. ¶4

Probate of Jon Arnesen, 1818 (partly read, no change): the skifte held at Mælsæth (Melset) on 5 June 1818, naming farmer John Arnesen, was examined for a son Ole. The opening and parts of the continuation were read, and an 'Ole' appears in the continuation, but his patronymic, his residence and his place among the heirs could not be read. No 'Ole Jonsen' of Røssevold or Borgund was identified. Confidence stays Probable until the heirs list is read properly. ¶11

Probate of Jon Arnesen, 1818, closer reading (no change): a line-by-line reading of the continuation of the 5 Jun 1818 skifte at Mælsæth (skifteprotokoll 3A 12, fol. 5a right column and fol. 5b first column, Nordre Sunnmøre) found an 'Ole...' with the words 'den afdødes [Søn?]' and '33 Aar' nearby, but his patronymic, his residence and the word after 'afdødes' could not be read on the high-contrast scan. Neither Røssevold, Borgund nor Anne Martha Rasmusdatter was read anywhere in the passage, and no formal heirs list was found. The age 33 would fit his 1819 marriage age of 32 if this is he, but that is not shown. Confidence stays Probable. ¶12

Farmer at Røssevold, Borgund. Named as father of Gertrud Berthe (baptized 1819) and, as 'Ole Jons.', as Gjertrud's father in her 1842 marriage. ¶1

Married 13 Jun 1819 at Borgund. He was a bachelor farmer (jordbruger) of 'Mælsæth' (probably Melset), aged about 32. His bride was Anne Martha Rasmusdatter, widow of Baard Johnsen of Røssevold, whose farm he took over. His own parents are not found. ¶2

Narrowing down 'Mælsæth', the farm he came from when he married in 1819. The only farm indexed under the name Melset in the Møre og Romsdal registers is in Ørskog parish, east of Ålesund, and a search of all Ole entries at Melset in Ørskog found no Ole Johnsen of the right date - so either the Ørskog identification is wrong or his baptism is not indexed. The likelier reading is a Borgund-district farm whose name the clerk wrote as Mælsæth: Mjelset or Melsæter. Settling it means reading the 1819 marriage entry in the page image, where the clerk's own spelling and the groom's age will be clearer than in the index. ¶3

Burial: Both Borgund registers record the burial on 27 February 1851 of Ole Johnsen of Røssevold, aged 68, who died 24 February. The clerk's book (klokkerbok 1844-1854, p. 187 no. 20) writes 'Ole Johnsøn', of Røssevold, 'født paa Melset i Ørskougs Præstegjeld' (born at Melset in Ørskog parish); his title there is abbreviated, probably 'Enkm.' (enkemand, widower). The pastor's book (ministerialbok 1843-1852, p. 189 no. 44) writes 'Ole Jonsen' of Røsevold, aged 68, with an unclear title word (possibly Lægdsmand, a man kept on the parish poor-relief rotation), and gives 15 June as the date of the graveside rite. ¶5

Birthplace: the 1851 burial entry settles the farm 'Mælseth' named at his 1819 marriage: he was born at Melset in Ørskog parish. His baptism, about 1782-87, should therefore be sought in the Ørskog registers (an earlier search of indexed Ole entries at Melset found none, so it is probably unindexed). ¶7

Baptism (Probable identification): the Ørskog pastor's register (ministerialbok 1773-1809, p. 221) records on the 20th Sunday after Trinity, 13 October 1782, at Ørskog church: 'Døbt Jon Arnesen Melsets Barn: Ole' (baptised: Jon Arnesen of Melset's child Ole). The godparents (fadder) were Hans Kirkedahl (surname uncertain), Ole Kniven, Lars Ramstad, Marte Melset and Ingeborg Ramstad. The entry does not name the mother; the clerk wrote only the father. Read on the scan. ¶8

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Confidence stays Probable. Ruling out the Strømsem namesake removes the only known rival, but it does not identify the 1819 groom. His link to Jon Arnesen of Melset still rests on the residence Mælsæth and his bachelor status. Against it, his baptism of 1782 and the 1801 census (born 1783) make him 36-37 in 1819, not the 32 the marriage gives. The page image of the 13 Jun 1819 Borgund marriage (pv00000001124434) would settle the age and the farm spelling. ¶14

Upgrade: Confirmed as the father of Gjertrud Birgitte Olsdatter Røssevold (see her page), under the grading rule that a person's grade covers the link to the child in the tree. Her confirmation on 5 Oct 1834 at Borgund is entered in two registers, both read on the Digitalarkivet index: (1) Borgund ministerialbok 1828-1839 (1531P), p. 127 no. 4: 'Gjertrue Birgitte Olsdtr.', born 1819 at Røssevold, father 'Ole Jahnsen', mother 'Anne Rasmusdtr.', 'Hustru'; (2) the resident chaplain's register 1833-1844, p. 36 no. 4: 'Gjertru Bergithe Olsdtr.', born 1819 at Røssevold, father 'Ole Johnsen', of Røssevold, mother 'Martha Rasmusdtr.', 'Hustru'. Together they name Ole Johnsen of Røssevold and Ane Marta Rasmusdatter as her parents, and they carry the middle name Birgitte that her 1819 baptism ('Gertrud Berthe') lacked, which settles the baptism identification as well. This grade covers only his link to Gjertrud: his own parentage (Jon Arnesen and Giertrud Knudsdatter of Melset, Ørskog) keeps its earlier grade of Probable. ¶17

Records that disagree 1

Age discrepancy: age 68 at death in Feb 1851 points to a birth about 1782-83, while age 32 at his June 1819 marriage points to about 1786-87. A gap of four years between two ages given by others is common in these registers; the birth year stays approximate until his baptism is found. ¶6

Corrections 1

Correction: the siblings note's Arne (baptised 4 Apr 1778) and Maren (1784), children of a Jon Arnesen of Westre, are not Ole's siblings. The 28 Oct 1788 Sunnmøre skifte (3A 29 fol. 104a, scan read) shows that Westre Jon dead in 1788, leaving widow Inger Paulsdatter and children Arne Jonsen and Martha Giertrud Jonsdatter, while Ole's father Jon Arnesen of Melset lived until 1818. ¶16

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Rival ruled out (cards): the namesake Ole Jonsen of Strømsem in Ørskog, baptized 28 Oct 1787, is not the bachelor of 'Mælsæth' who married the widow Anne Martha Rasmusdatter at Borgund on 13 Jun 1819. He married at Ørskog on 8 Jul 1821 and was then entered as 'afskedig. Soldat Ungkarl' - a discharged soldier and bachelor. The bride was Olave Ellingsdatter of Kjemphoel (Ørskog marriages 1816-1832, p. 387, no. 17; index pv00000000250717). He was not a widower in 1821, so he cannot be the 1819 groom. He stayed at Strømsem all his life. Index rows show him there as a godparent from 1816 to 1849 and as a farmer (gaardbruger) and father in 1823, 1836 and 1852. An Ørskog burial-index row records Ole Johnsen, farmer of Strømsem, born 1787, as deceased on 16 Sep 1860. One caveat: the bachelor status comes from a single index entry, and the page image has not been read. A second index of the same marriage (pv00000008986064) shows no status. If the page says otherwise, this question reopens. ¶13

Searches that found nothing 1

Probate search 1814 (negative): the hoped-for 22 Jul 1814 estate division after Giertrud Knudsdatter at Mælsæth was not found in the sitting headings on images 205-210 of Nordre Sunnmøre skifteprotokoll 3 (1809-1814). The volume is not in date order, so it is not ruled out. No record yet names Ole as Giertrud's son. Confidence stays Probable. ¶15
