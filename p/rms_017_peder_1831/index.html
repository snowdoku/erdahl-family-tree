Life story

Baptism: Peder, born and baptised 18 Dec 1831, son of Niels Thorsen and Malene Nielsdatter, Nordre Sunde, Malde sogn, Håland (two index copies). Confirmed as their child. ¶1

Presumed died young, before Feb 1833, when the name was given again (Peer, b. 22 Feb 1833, later Peter S. Nelson); no burial found in the index, so this is inference. ¶2
