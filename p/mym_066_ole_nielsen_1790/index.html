Life story

1801 census: 'Ole Nielssen', 11, one of 'Deres boern' (their children) of Niels Olssen and Guri Olsdatter at Langlie, Selbu, household 01 - the second son of that name, his elder brother Ole being 21. ¶1

Probate: a second son 'Ole' is among the heirs in the skifte after his father Niels Olsen Langlie, closed 21 June 1816 (skifteprotokoll 10b, fol. 713), separate from the elder son Ole Nielsen. Not the same man as his brother Ole Nielsen Langli (see his page), b. about 1780. ¶2
