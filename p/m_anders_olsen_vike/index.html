About the family

Parents (Speculative): Ole Erichsen Wiike and Berrit Lassesdatter, parents of twins Erich and Lasse baptised 8 Aug 1741 at Veøy with mostly Wiike godparents. Argument: he later farmed Wiike, and named children Ole, Bereth and Lasse. No baptism of Anders to this couple was found. Baptisms searched: Jan 1733-Oct 1734 (canvases 66 right-73 of Veøy ministerialbok 1721-1764). Anders baptisms found and ruled out: Oct 1733 son of Just paa Laven (canvas 69, zoomed; earlier read as 'Ole'), Feb 1734 son of Joen Darø[?] (canvas 71), Aug 1734 son of Siur[?] Vesnes (canvas 73). His own confidence is lowered from Probable to Speculative, because a person's confidence carries the link to their parents and this link is only Speculative. His own facts (1801 census, marriages, 1818 probate) are unaffected. ¶5

Life story

Marriages: (1) 16 Jun 1766, Veøy, as bachelor and soldier, to Hille Knudsdatter (see her page), who died in childbed 1 Dec 1784; (2) 30 Jan 1786, Veøy, as widower, to Anne Olsdatter of Søer-sylte (see her page). ¶2

Probate of Gjertrud Erichsdatter, Ytre Wiige, 11 Sep 1770 (Romsdal 3A 09 fol. 537b, scan), tested as a possible sister of Ole Erichsen Wiike: she was the widow of a Morten, and her heirs were her own four children (Anders, Erich and Børre Mortensen, and Giertrud Mortensdatter, married to Lasse Krogset), so it says nothing about Ole Erichsen's family. The Romsdal probate index has no estate of Ole Erichsen or of Berrit Lassesdatter at Vike 1730-1825 (domicile searches Vike, Vike ytre, Vike indre). Not read: Bridt Eriksdatter, Vike, 1797 (3A 12, sk_read/24621/278), perhaps another sister of Ole Erichsen. ¶7

Baptism of son (scan read): 'Anders Olsen Wiikes Søn, kaldet Lasse' was baptised 5 Dec 1784 at Vesnes church, Veøy. ¶4

Probate of his first wife read on the scan: Romsdal skifteprotokoll 3A 11, fol. 198a, 27 Dec 1785, Yttre Wige: division after Hille Knudsdatter between the widower Anders Olsen and their six children Ole (19), Knud (16), Brit (12), Anders (8), Ole the younger (4) and Lasse (1). The guardian of the youngest son Lasse was the lensmann Erich Lassesen of Leg[ernes]; his patronymic matches that of Anders's proposed mother Berrit Lassesdatter, so he may be her brother, but no relationship is stated and his own parentage stays Speculative. ¶6

Farmer at Wiike (Vike), Veøy, in the 1801 census, age 67, in his second marriage. Household: wife Anne Olsdatter (57), children Anders (24), Lasse (17) and Bereth (26), and a foster child Johan Knudsen (3). ¶1

Probate (scan read): his estate as Anders Olsen Wiige, kårmann (retired on a farm pension, per the index), was registered 19 Dec 1818 (Romsdal skifteprotokoll 3A 14 p. 394a), with the widow Anne Olsdatter[?]. The division names six children of his first marriage: 1 Ole Andersen (dead, of Gjermundnæs; children Hans Olsen Gjermundnæs, Hille Olsdatter married at Sørsylte, and Ellen[?] Olsdatter married at Gjelvig[?]); 2 Knud Andersen Willa; 3 Anders Andersen Vikbakken[?]; 4 Ole Andersen Wiige; 5 Lasse Andersen; 6 Bereth Andersdatter, married to Ole...sen Brøeghaugen[?]. Bracketed readings are uncertain. ¶3

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the grade · 1 alternative set aside

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: Speculative to Probable, graded on his link to his son Lasse Andersen Skorgen (see his page); his own parentage (Ole Erichsen Wiike and Berrit Lassesdatter) stays Speculative and is graded on their cards. His earlier Speculative grade reflected that upward link. Records naming the relationship: the 5 Dec 1784 Veøy baptism 'Anders Olsen Wiikes Søn, kaldet Lasse'; the 27 Dec 1785 probate after his wife Hille Knudsdatter (Romsdal 3A 11 fol. 198a) between the widower Anders Olsen and their six children, the youngest Lasse (1); the 1801 census of Wiike, Lasse 17 among his children; and his own probate of 19 Dec 1818, heir no. 5 'Lasse Andersen', of age. Not Confirmed because the step from Lasse of Wiike to Lasse of Skorgen crosses farms and parishes: it rests on the 3 Dec 1806 Veøy marriage of 'Lasse Anderssen, bachelor of Wiche' to Guraa Olsdatter of 'Kaarsan' (a Probable identification on Lasse's card) and on his age at death in 1829. A record of Lasse Skorgen naming his father, or naming Wiike as his birthplace (for example his 1829 burial entry read on the scan), would settle it. ¶8

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Burial candidate, not adopted (dates sweep 2): 'Anders Olsen', aged 79 (b. about 1739), died 24 Mar and was buried 31 Mar 1818 at Veøy (Veøy ministerialbok, Vestnes 1818-1847, pp. 192-193, no. 1; index pg00000006602708). The year fits his probate of 19 Dec 1818, but the index gives no residence, and the age is five years more than his 1801 census age of 67 (b. about 1734); read the scan for the farm before adopting it. Birth estimate kept at ABT 1734. ¶9
