About the family

Father of Rolf Christensen of Ula (see his page), Probable; graded on his link to Rolf.: the 1664 vicar's census for Lesja (Gudbrandsdal deanery no. 6, p. 168, Digitalarkivet transcription) lists on Houvoch (Hovauk, gnr. 121) 'Chresten Jenssøn', ovsidder, b. 1604 (calc.), with three sons marked 'Sønn': Jens Chestenss. b. 1637, Rolf Chestenss. aged 25 (b. 1639) and Hans Chestenss. b. 1645. Rolf Christensen of Ula (d. before 26 Sep 1694; bygdebok 'ca. 1639') fits the 1664 son by name, patronymic and birth year, and he and his brother Hans appear together at both ends: the 1723 skifte at Ula after 'Sl. Hans Christensen', who left no children, gives his estate to his brothers and sisters' lines, among them 'Sl. Rolf Christensens Børn' (Nord-Gudbrandsdal skifteprotokoll 1719-1726, source 25156, view 136). Rolf Chestenss. is the only Rolf with patronymic Christensen in the indexed 1664-66 censuses for Gudbrandsdal (among the 42 hits of a Rolf* search of the 1664-66 census indexes). ¶1a

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Probable, not Confirmed: the census names the father-son relationship, but following the 1664 Rolf of Hovauk to the Rolf of Ula crosses farms and 30 years; the Rolf-Hans pair at both ends supports it but the 1723 record does not name their father. ¶1b

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Test record: a skifte after Christen Jensen of Hovauk (Nord-Gudbrandsdal probate before 1690; card index 39945 under Hovauk/Hovog), the 1666 census entry for Hovauk, or the 1723 skifte's group A/E heirs, which might name the father of the Christensen siblings. ¶1c
