About the family

NEW ANCESTOR. Named as the deceased wife ('g.m. Øli Sveningsdtr. †(1690)') of Svening Giermundsøn of Rostveit, and as mother of his children Knud and Anna, on the card for his 1712 estate (Østre Råbyggelag Skifteprotokoll nr. 3 fol. 223, card 11385). Card 11387 cites the 'skiftebrev etter avl. Øli Sveningsdtr., dat. 29/10-1690', so she died before 29 Oct 1690. That skiftebrev predates the surviving protocols (from 1697) and is not on Digitalarkivet. The 1747 Eppeland card (2377 back) repeats: 'Konas mor: Øli Svenningsdtr. + sk. 1690'. ¶1

Her patronymic makes her father a Svenning (not identified). ¶2

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

2 on the grade · 2 open questions

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Audit (1 Nov 2026): downgraded from Confirmed to Probable. The card (DA 39942 card 11385, image re-read by the auditor) reads 'Svening Giermundsön †, g.m. Øli Sveningsdtr. †(1690)'. The '(1690)' and the marriage appear to be the 20th-century card writer's interpretation of a referenced skiftebrev after Øli dated 29 Oct 1690, which is not extant. The 1712 protocol entry itself, as, names only the heirs Knud and Anne and does not call Øli Svenning's wife. The 1747 card ('Konas mor') comes from the same card index. A wife whose patronymic matches her husband's given name is possible but unusual, and the skiftebrev might instead concern a daughter. ¶3a

Re-proof attempt (overnight 1-2 Nov 2026), no change: still Probable. Re-read the 1712 protocol entry after Svening Giermundsøn (Skp. 3 fol. 223a-223b, DA 24351 views 224-225). The document list on 223b recites a contract of 3 Dec 1686 and, further down, a skiftebrev 'paa slet Papiir' written at Eppeland on 29 Oct 1690 by a lensmann [name uncertain, '...søn Giøvreland'?], and a passage that appears to say Anna received 'baade sin Fædrene og Mødrene' inheritance (her paternal and maternal shares; reading uncertain). In the lines read, the 1690 skiftebrev is not said to be after Øli and her name does not appear. The 1747 Eppeland probate (Skp. 12a fol. 6a-6b, DA 24360 views 7-8) was located: 11 Dec 1747, after Anna Svenningsdatter, wife of Ole Ormsen, with Orm Olsen, Niels Olsen, Eli Olsdatter (Aslach Tellefsen) as heirs; its document list (deeds of 1680, 1713, 1733-35) was not read closely enough to find a mention of Anna's mother. ¶4a

Open questions and the next record to read 2

Would confirm: the 1712 protocol's own wording about the 1690 skiftebrev (fol. 223), or the 1747 protocol (Skp. 12a fol. 6) if it names Anna's mother. ¶3b
