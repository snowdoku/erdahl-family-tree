Life story

Baptism: on 10 Oct 1762 at Ål church, 'Asle Knudssøn Medgaardehuus B: Ole' (Asle Knudsen of Medgaardehuus, child Ole) was baptised. Godparents: Knud Streite[?], Lars Øyvindsen [Rund?], Tosten Tostensen of Sando, 'Enken Guro Dybsjorde' (the widow Guro of Dybsjorde) and Birgeth [?]datter [Rund?]. The mother was churched on 31 Oct 1762. ¶1

Probates: named as Ole, 10, in the skifte of 12 Oct 1773 after his father Asle Knudsen at Medgaarden, and as Ole Aslesen, 23, in the skifte of 18 Feb 1785 after his mother Kari Tostensdatter (probate index cards, Ringerike og Hallingdal skifteprotokoll 19 fol. 651a and 20b fol. 674a). The 1773 protocol text (fol. 651a) reads 'Ole Aslesen 10 aar'. Confidence raised from Probable to Confirmed by the 1773 protocol and these cards; the stated ages run one year below his baptism of 10 Oct 1762, which is within the usual error. ¶3

1801 census: Ole Aslesen, 38, unmarried, day labourer (*dagarbeider*), lived at Medgaarden in the household of his brother Tosten Aslesen (*husbondens broder*, the householder's brother). Age 38 in Feb 1801 fits a baptism in Oct 1762 exactly. Another Ole son of an Asle was baptised in Ål in Sep 1762 (son of Asle Olsen of Mandshuus), but that Asle had no son Tosten, so the brother pair fits only Asle Knudsen of Medgaardehuus. Probable, on the same evidence as his brother Tosten. ¶2
