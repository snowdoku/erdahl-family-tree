Life story

Bygdebok (Vik H. 1 canvas 43, Unde chapter; rebuilt text, so dates ABT): 'Ola Ivarson [...] (1665-1733) kom [...] til Undi' and married Sigrid Lassedtr Undi (1660-1742); children Hermund 1688, Brita, Ivar 1690, Lasse 1694, Sjur 1696, Anna 1698. He came to Unde from elsewhere (the farm name falls in a gap of the rebuild), so his own parents (an Ivar) are not at Unde. Grade unchanged (Probable on his link to Hermund). ¶2a

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the grade · 2 open questions

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Graded Probable on his link to his son Hermund Olsen of Gulteigen (see his page). The 1701 census for Vik lists at Vndi (Unde) the bonde Olle Ifversen, 36, with sons Hermund Olsen 13 (b. about 1688), Ifver 11, Lasse 7 and Siver 5; Unde is listed close to Gulteig in the same census (ids 626678 and 626695). The Vik bygdebok (Gards- og ættesoge for Vik i Sogn H. 1, 2002, canvas 35, Gulteig; rebuilt text, so dates ABT) says that one of the sons of Ola Ivarson Undi, Hermund Olason, became the farmer at Gulteig, and gives 'Hermund Olason Undi (1688-1743)' married to Eli Oladtr (1681-1759) with the children Sjur 1712, Agata, Anna, Ola 1721, Anders 1723, Sigrid 1727 and one b. 1729, which matches the children in Hermund Olsen's 1743 and Eli Olsdatter's 1759 Gulteigen probates and the census age of 13 in 1701. Why only Probable: the census names the relationship, but the identification of the 1701 boy with the Gulteigen Hermund rests on the bygdebok and the matching age and patronymic (a different farm, 42 years later). ¶1a
