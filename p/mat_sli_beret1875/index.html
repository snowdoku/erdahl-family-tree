Life story

Oline Slind's elder sister. Born about 1875 - she is entered as 'Deres d', their daughter, aged under one, in the 1875 census of her father's household at Slinden, lopenr 184, and in the 1891 census as 'd ug hjv. dtr.', daughter at home helping in the house. ¶1

Gravestone, Selbu Kirke Cemetery, Mebonden, Selbu, Section L: 'Beret G. Slind, 20 FEB 1875 - 31 OCT 1895'. The Selbu gaardshistorie's bare 'Berit, f. 1875, d. 1895' now has exact dates: born 20 February 1875, died 31 October 1895, aged twenty. Not to be confused with 'Beret E. Slind, 18 Jun 1880 - 29 Nov 1916', buried in Section J of the same churchyard. ¶5

Death: the Selbu burial register for 1895, page 309 no. 20, records 'Berit Gundersdtr, born 1874 at Slindan, Ugift grdmdsdt' - an unmarried farmer's daughter - who died 31 October 1895 and was buried 10 November 1895. That is almost certainly this woman: the right name, the right farm, the right status, dying at twenty or twenty-one. The burial entry's birth year of 1874 is one off the 1875 given in both censuses, which is why this is rated Probable rather than Confirmed. ¶2

She died in 1895, aged about twenty: Selbu i fortid og naatid vol. 7, p. 100, 'Berit, f. 1875, d. 1895.' Wave 3 could only say she was at home in 1891 and gone by 1900 and left open whether she had married or emigrated. She had died. The gaardshistorie spells her Berit; the 1891 census has Beret Gundersdtr. ¶4

Her death at twenty explains her absence from the 1900 census, where only four of her parents' seven children are still on the farm. ¶3
