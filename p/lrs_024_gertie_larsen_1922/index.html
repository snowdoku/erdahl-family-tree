Life story

1930 census (index only): Gertie I Larson, daughter, born about 1923 in North Dakota, West Bank, Williams County, the eldest child at home. ¶1

Marriage: as Gertie I Larson she married Jean (Gene) Archie Erdahl, brother of Clarice's husband William, on 10 Feb 1945 in Whatcom County (document nwwtcmcv38_42). ¶2

Death: the Washington death index lists Gertie I. Erdahl, died 20 Jan 2013 in Snohomish County, age 90 (born Jan 1922 - Jan 1923). The family's own record (cited in the line notes) gives 25 Dec 1922 at Temple, North Dakota; not read here. ¶3

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
