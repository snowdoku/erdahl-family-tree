About the family

Oline Slind's paternal grandfather. Baptised in Selbu on 26 December 1816, born 10 December 1816, son of Gunder Jorgensen and Beret Olsdatter of Slinden - entered at pages 32-33 serial 5 of the Selbu local parish register 1816-1831. His burial entry independently gives his birthplace as Slindan, confirming that he was born on the farm. ¶1

Life story

Marriage: 24 May 1841 in Selbu, aged 24, to Berith Gundersdatter Eidem, born 1816 in Selbu. The entry, at page 337 serial 7 of the Selbu register 1824-1842, names the groom's father as GUNDER JORGENSEN AUNE and the bride's father as GUNDER JOHNSEN EIDEM. That the groom was baptised at Slinden in 1816 but his father is styled 'Aune' in 1841 means the family had moved, or held land at both places; both farm names are recorded here rather than one being preferred. ¶2

The Slind-Eidem connection is two generations older than the marriage of Oline Slind to John P. Eidem in Minnesota. Oline's own paternal grandmother was a daughter of the Eidem farm in Selbu. The two farms had been intermarrying in Selbu long before their descendants farmed side by side in Mehurin Township, Lac qui Parle County, Minnesota. ¶3

1865 census: head of the household at Slinden, rural residence 0103, lopenr 188, aged 49, 'Gaardbr. Leilaending' - farmer and tenant. He sowed one barrel of barley, three of oats and four of potatoes and kept two horses, eleven cows, fourteen sheep, ten goats and one pig, and the holding fed eight people. 1875 and 1891 find him still at lopenr 188. In 1891 he is 'grdbr. leilaending, smed' - tenant farmer and SMITH, and two of his sons, Gunder born 1849 and Peder born 1854, are smiths as well. Slinden nordre was a smithing family: the neighbouring Slindan sondre household in 1900 is headed by a 'Gaardbruger, Smed og Sagbygger', farmer, smith and sawmill builder. ¶4

A distinction worth keeping straight, because the Selbu gaardshistorie makes it. He was NOT the holder of the bruk his son Gunder took in 1871. He held the Slind bruk at lopenr 188 (residence 0103 in 1865, residence 004 in 1891) from before 1865 until his death in 1897, as 'Gaardbr. Leilaending' and later as smith; the bruk at gnr 141 bnr 2, SLINDAN NORDRE, NEDPAA-GARDEN, which his son Gunder held from 1871 to 1920, had a different holder until 1868 - a man born 1821, who had it from 1839 - and the gaardshistorie says Gunnar Olsen came to it 'fra... Slindan', from elsewhere on the farm cluster. Father and son were tenants of two different Slind holdings at the same time. ¶7

His own father's household can now be put in the 1801 census with confidence: Gunder Jorgensen, born 1792, was a child in household 3 at rural residence 0083 Slinden, the household of Jorgen Olssen and Ane Gundersdatter, with Jorgen's own mother Marit Pedersdatter, aged 83, in the house. Ole Gundersen was therefore a great-grandson of Marit Pedersdatter, born about 1718, which is as far back as this line now reaches. ¶8

Death: died 30 November 1897 and buried 9 December 1897 at Selbu, entered at page 319 no. 18 of the burial register, described as 'Enkemand, kaarmand' - a widower living on a kaar, the pension in kind that a retiring farmer reserved for himself out of the holding he had handed on. His wife had therefore died before that date. ¶5

Printed source: his marriage into Eidem is on printed page 203 of Selbu i fortid og naatid, bind 4 (1992), in the chapter EIDEM (YSTER-EIDEM) - the page carries his own birth year 1816, his given name in the 1992 form Ola, the farm name Slind, his wife's birth years 1815 and 1816, the marriage year 1841 and his son Gunder's birth year 1844. His own farm is written up separately: SLINDAN NORDRE, volume 7, chapter heading on printed page 92, with the holder tenures 1701-1712, 1713-1742, 1743-1750, 1751-1773, 1774-1783, 1774-1805, 1806-1811, 1812-1836 and 1828-1845. The tenure 1812-1836 on printed page 94 of volume 7 is the one that should be his father Gunder Jorgensen's, and 1828-1845 or the following one his own. ¶6
