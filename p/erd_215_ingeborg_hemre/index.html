Life story

New: wife of Gunder Johansen Hemre (see his page) and mother of Gjertrud Gundersdatter (see her page), graded on her link to Gjertrud: Probable. Hegra gårds- og slektshistorie B. 1, Hembre Øvre (canvas 183): 'Gunnar Johansen Hembre, [f.] 1688, gravlagt 20/5 1760... Gift 13/7 1718 m. Ingeborg...' followed by 'Born: Johan, brukar. Gjertrud, f. 16/1 1721, g. m. David Andersen... Stjørdal; Randi, f. 30/3 1723, g. m. Ola... Stjørdal; [child] f. 26/8 1725, d. 1727; [child] f. 15/6 1727; Ingeborg, f. 1730 [d.]; Ingeborg, f. 2/1 1731, gravlagt 6/7 1760, ugift; [Gunnar, later at] Skatval, f. 23/11 1733.' Her patronymic falls in an unharvested gap (about 70 patronymic forms were queried without a hit). Her marriage in 1718 and Gjertrud's birth in 1721 put Gjertrud inside this marriage, but the identification rests on the bygdebok alone. ¶1

Tested without success: the Stjørdal forlover (betrothal-surety) list 1718 (16367 canvases 24-26) has no Gunder Johansen; the Hegra baptisms of Jan-Feb 1721 in the chronological register 16368 (canvases 81-82: 12 Jan, 26 Jan) do not include a Gjertrud, so the bygdebok's date 16/1 1721 is unverified. These baptisms DO name the mother ('Mod.'), so Gjertrud's baptism (try 16368 c.79-80 for late 1720 and c.83-86 for Feb-Apr 1721) would confirm Ingeborg as her mother. ¶2a

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Upgrade: graded on her link to her daughter Gjertrud Gundersdatter (see her page), Probable -> Confirmed. Three primary records: (1) the marriage, Stjørdal ministerialbok 16368 canvas 53, 13 Jul 1718 at Hegra: 'Copul. Gunder Johansen Hemra og Ingeborg Rollofsd. Bjørgaard' - so her patronymic is Rolfsdatter (Rollofsdatter) and she came from Bjørgård (probably Bjørgan, Lånke/Hegra); (2) the baptism, 16368 canvas 83, Dom. Oculi (2 Mar) 1721: 'Gunder Hemræs b. Gjertrud. Mod. Ingborg' - a baptism naming the mother; (3) the 1744 Frøyum probate (24753 fol. 102b-103a), which names Gjertrud Gundersdatter's father as 'Morfaderen Gunder Johans. Hamre'. Continuity: Gunder Johansen is on Hemre in 1718 (marriage), 1721 (this baptism) and 1737 (deed for Øvre Hemre, pantebok A-1 fol. 242b), so the Gunder Hemre of 1721 is the Gunder Johansen Hamre of 1744; Gjertrud's burial (27 Jan 1786, indexed birth year 1721) matches this baptism exactly. Window: Gjertrud was born about 1721 (burial age) and married 1743; the 1718 marriage covers 1718 onwards, the bygdebok (Hegra B.1 c183) gives Gunder only this one marriage and has Ingeborg bearing children to 1733, so any Gjertrud of Gunder's born 1718-33 is Ingeborg's. No rival Gunder on Hemre is named in 1718-21 (the 1717 Christmas baptism of a Giertrud to 'Gunder Trøite[?]', c45, is another farm and before this marriage). The only residual doubt is an unrecorded pre-1718 marriage of Gunder (b. about 1688), for which there is no evidence. The bygdebok's birth date '16/1 1721' was presumably the birth; the baptism is 2 Mar 1721 (Lånke communion Sunday, entry listed with the Hegra families). ¶3

Audit 9 (2 Nov 2026): Confirmed kept, graded on her link to her daughter Gjertrud Gundersdatter (see her page). This rules on the AUDIT-QUEUE flag for the re-raise. (1) The named rival of audit 8 is resolved, as that audit's note required: on 29 Sep 1720 (16368 c78) 'Gunder Trøites b. Birrite, Mod. Mali' has 'Gunder Hemra' as a separate godfather, so Gunder Trøite (still married to Mali in 1720) is not Gunder Johansen Hemre, and the Giertrud of Christmas 1717 is Gunder Trøite's daughter. (2) The 22-year gap from the baptism (2 Mar 1721, 'Gunder Hemræs b. Gjertrud. Mod. Ingborg') to the betrothal (2 Oct 1743, 'pigen Geertrud Gundersd. Hemre') is bridged as a pair (audit 8 rule 3), not by the 16-19-year rule: father and daughter appear together on Hemre at both ends. At the near end, Gunder Hemre and his daughter Gjertrud in 1721; at the far end, the 1744 Frøyum probate names Gjertrud's father 'Morfaderen Gunder Johans. Hamre', and 'Gunder og Ranni Hemre' stand godparents to her daughter Lisbet in Sep 1744. Gunder Johansen is on Hemre in between (1718, 1721, the deed of 1737). (3) The window rule (audit 5): the Hegra baptisms of 1714-17 are unread, but a Gjertrud of Gunder's born before 1721 cannot be the 1743 bride. Naming the 1721 child Gjertrud (his own mother's name) means that no earlier daughter Gjertrud was then alive, so the 1743 bride was born in 1721 or later, inside the marriage to Ingeborg (children to 1733). Residual risk, stated openly: a second Gjertrud, born about 1722-25 after the death of the 1721 child, to a wife other than Ingeborg. Nothing suggests one: the bygdebok gives Gunder one marriage, and Ingeborg bore children to 1733. Note that Gjertrud's own link down is Probable, so the line through her is no stronger than Probable at that step. ¶7

Corrections 1

Correction (audit 8, 1-2 Nov 2026): Confirmed lowered to Probable, graded on her link to Gjertrud Gundersdatter (see her page). The 1718 marriage and the 2 Mar 1721 baptism ('Gunder Hemræs b. Gjertrud. Mod. Ingborg') are sound, but the tree's Gjertrud is identified only as a daughter of Gunder Johansen Hamre (the 1744 Frøyum probate, 'Morfaderen'), 23 years later and on another farm, so the audit-5 window rule applies. Her birth window rests on a burial age (1786, indexed birth year 1721), which tonight's audits treat as only approximate. Inside that window there is a named rival: the auditor read Stjørdal ministerialbok 16368, canvas 45 (fol. 50), Christmas Day 1717 at Hegra: 'døbt Gunder Tröites b. Giertrud. Mod: Mali', godparents Erich Tröite, Ollof Ollofsen Biørngaard, Mali Carlsdatter and others. That is a Giertrud, daughter of a Gunder by a wife Mali, seven months before Gunder Johansen married Ingeborg. One godparent came from Bjørgaard, Ingeborg's home farm. Nothing yet shows whether Gunder Trøite is Gunder Johansen (that is, whether he was a widower in 1718), or whether that Giertrud died young. ¶4a

Alternatives considered and set aside 2

Caution: Christmas 1717 at Hegra (16368 c45) a 'Gunder Trøite[?]' had a daughter Giertrud baptised, a named rival Gjertrud Gundersdatter; the 1744 probate (father Gunder Johansen Hamre) excludes her as our Gjertrud, but she must not be taken for our Gjertrud's baptism. ¶2b

Upgrade (resolving the audit-8 rival): graded on her link to her daughter Gjertrud Gundersdatter (see her page), Probable -> Confirmed. Audit 8 lowered her only because of a named rival: the Giertrud baptised at Christmas 1717 (16368 c45) to 'Gunder Tröite' and his wife Mali, and its own note said Confirmed could be restored if Gunder Trøite were shown to be another man. Two primary records now settle that. (1) Stjørdal ministerialbok 16368 canvas 78, Festo Michaelis (29 Sep) 1720: 'døbt Gunder Trøites b. Birrite, Mod. Mali; fadd. Gunder Hemra, Lars Trøita,... Heigra, Birrit Bjørngaard, Ane Trøita', and on Dom. 22 p. Trin. 1720 at Hegre 'introd. Gunder Trøites h. Mali'. So in 1720 Gunder Trøite still had his wife Mali living and bearing children, two years after Gunder Johansen Hemre married Ingeborg (13 Jul 1718), and 'Gunder Hemra' stands as a separate godfather in the same entry: two different men. Gunder Trøite's line continues on Trøite: in the 1743 trolovede list (16370 canvases 260-261) a 'Gunder Rollufsen Trøita' signs as cautioner and 'Berit Gundersdatter Trøita' (b. 1720, the child above) is betrothed to Anders Olsen Øster on 26 Oct 1743; the bygdebok (Hegra B.1 c255-256) has Gunnar Trøite m. 20/6 1714 Mali Bjørnsdtr. Trøite, he buried 28/2 1764, she d. 1765, with a daughter b. 29/10 1720 who married an Anders. So the 1717 Giertrud is Gunder Rolfsen Trøite's and Mali's daughter, not Gunder Johansen's. (2) The betrothal itself, 16370 canvas 262 (p. 261), 2 Oct 1743 at Værnes: 'ungkarl David Anderson Frøyum med pigen Geertrud Gundersd. Hemre', married at Værnes 28 Nov 1743. This puts the tree's Gjertrud (widow of David Andersen Frøyum in the 1744 probate, whose father is 'Morfaderen Gunder Johans. Hamre') on Hemre, not Trøite. Chain and continuity: 13 Jul 1718 marriage Gunder Johansen Hemra x Ingeborg Rollofsd. (16368 c53) -> 2 Mar 1721 'Gunder Hemræs b. Gjertrud. Mod. Ingborg' (16368 c83) -> Gunder on Hemre by deed of 19 Dec 1737 (pantebok A-1 fol. 242) -> 2 Oct 1743 'Geertrud Gundersd. Hemre' -> 17 Nov 1744 probate (widow Gjertrud Gundersdatter, morfar Gunder Johans. Hamre). The far-end record ties her to the farm by patronymic plus farm, the father is on the farm inside the gap, and no rival Gjertrud Gundersdatter of Hemre is named. Window: she was a maiden ('pigen') in 1743, so born about 1713-1725; the 1718 marriage covers 1718 onwards. For 1713-17: the 1718 marriage entry carries no widower mark (this register rarely marks status, so that is weak), the bygdebok gives Gunder Johansen only this marriage, and the one Gjertrud Gundersdatter baptised at Hegra in that span (Christmas 1717) is now shown to belong to Gunder Trøite. Residual, stated openly: Hegra baptisms 1714-1717 have not been read page by page for a child of Gunder Hemre. ¶6

Searches that found nothing 1

Audit-8 follow-up, negative: the chronological list in 16370 for Oct-Dec 1743 (canvases 47-48) records no marriage of David Andersen and Gjertrud, so it gives neither the bride's farm nor her father. David's burial (21 May 1744, c54) gives only his own age. The rival Giertrud (Christmas 1717, father Gunder Trøite[?], mother Mali) is still unresolved. Her link to Gjertrud stays Probable. Records still to read: the 16370 trolovede list for Nov 1743 (about canvases 258-261), which may name the bride as Gunder Hemre's daughter (still not separating mothers), and later baptisms of Gunder Trøite and Mali in 16368 after Jul 1718. A Trøite child born after 1718 would prove Gunder Trøite a different man from Gunder Johansen Hemre (married to Ingeborg from 13 Jul 1718). ¶5

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Test record: burials at Hegra/Stjørdal 1717-1721 (16368) for Mali, wife of Gunder Trøite, or for the child Giertrud; a widower marker on the 13 Jul 1718 marriage (16368 c53); or Gunder Johansen Hemre's own probate (after his burial on 20 May 1760), which would show which marriage Gjertrud came from. If the 1717 child is shown to be dead, or Gunder Trøite to be another man, Confirmed can be restored. ¶4b
