About the family

Father of Erich Pedersen of Tuntland (see his page): the 1725 probate names 'Erich Pedersen' as the eldest son and heir of Peder Erichsen of Tuntland, of age, holding land and houses on Tuntland. Erich Pedersen of Tuntland died before 31 Jan 1742 leaving land on Tuntland to his son Peder Erichsen, aged 12 (Ryfylke D 9, fol. 25b and 118a). The same name and farm, the land passing in the male line, and the naming of Erich's son Peder (b. about 1730) after this Peder Erichsen identify the two Erichs. Graded Probable under the grading rule: the 1725 record names the father-son link, but the identity of the 1725 heir with the man who died by 1742 rests on name, farm and land, not on a record that names both. Added. ¶2

Parents found: the vicar's census of 1664-1666 for Hjelmeland (Ryfylke prosti, DA source 35515) lists on Tuntland in 1666 'Erich', opsidder, aged 80, with his sons ('Sønner') 'Ole', 40, and 'Peder', 21 (born about 1645); the 1664 list for Tuntland also names Erich, Lauridtz, Peder and Ole. Peder son of Erich on Tuntland, aged 21 in 1666, is taken to be this Peder Erichsen, aged 52 on Tuntland in 1701 (the four-year difference in implied birth year is ordinary in these lists). The other Peders on Tuntland in 1666 are an opsidder aged 60 and a farmhand Peder Nielsen, 22, so no rival Peder Erichsen. Father graded Probable: the 1666 record names Erich as Peder's father, but the identification of the 1666 Peder with the 1701 Peder Erichsen spans 35 years and rests on name, patronymic and farm. ¶7

Life story

1701 manntall (index): Ryfylke fogderi, Hjelmeland, farm 'Thuntland', p. 177: 'Peder Erichsen', opsidder eller leilending, aged 52 (born about 1649), with 'Erich Pedersen', søn, aged 5 (born about 1696), at home; also on the farm the user Haldvor Olsen (35) and two farmhands. This is the same father-son pair as the 1725 probate (Peder Erichsen of Tuntland and his only son Erich), on the same farm, 24 years earlier, and it dates Erich's birth to about 1696. ¶6

Probate (scan): Ryfylke skifteprotokoll D 4 (DA 15920), fol. 246b, a registration and division begun 'Anno 1725 d. 27 Septemb.' on the farm 'Tundtland udi Hielmelands Skibrede' after the deceased man 'Peder Erichsen'. The parties are the surviving wife ('efterlevende Hustru') 'Anna Daniels Datter' on one side, and on the other side 6 children, named in the preamble: first 'Erich Peder-sen', of age; then (partly legible) a second son; and daughters 'Britta Peders Datter', married; a daughter married to [?Ingebrigt] Andersen [?]; 'Anna Peders Datter', married to [?Rasmus] Osmundsen; and 'Kari Peders Datter', unmarried. In the division (fol. 247a) Erich Pedersen is credited for houses he had built on the farm Tuntland ('udi gaarden Tuntland'), and his brother's share ('Broderlod') is paid out in land, including 10½ marklag of butter-rent land, valued with the farm's land. The typed register in front of the volume files it as 'Tundtland i Hjelmeland, skf. efter Peder Eriksen, fol. 246b'. Whether Anna Danielsdatter was the mother of all six children, or a second wife, is not stated in the lines read. ¶1

Preamble re-read, D 4 fol. 246b: 'Anno 1725 d. 27 Septembr.' at 'Tundtland udi Hielmelands [Skibrede]' after 'den Sl. Mand Peder Erichsen', between 'hans efterlevende Hustrue Anna Daniels Datter paa den ene side og deris [?Echteskabs] sammen avlede 6 Børn paa den anden side, som er 1 Søn og 5 Døttre, nemlig Erich Pedersen, [?3x] Aar myndig; Anna Pedersdatter den ældre, gift med Ole [?Gunnersen/Gudmundsen], boende paa [?Eckersvold] gaard i Hielmelands Skibrede; Britta Pedersdatter gift med Peder [?Braaten]; Ingre [Peders]datter gift med [?] [?Eidrevsen/Andersen]; Anna Pedersdatter den yngre gift med [?Rasmus] Osmundsen, boende paa [?Grane] gaard; og Kari Pedersdatter ugift'. So there was only ONE son (the earlier reading of a 'second son' was wrong; the 'Broderlod' in the division is Erich's own son's share, the double share of a brother), and the six children are 'deris sammen avlede', i.e. children of Peder Erichsen AND Anna Danielsdatter together. Erich Pedersen being the only son strengthens the identification with the Erich Pedersen who held Tuntland land until his death before 1742. Grade unchanged (Probable). ¶5

Not a duplicate of Peder Erichsen (b. about 1730): this Peder Erichsen died before 27 Sep 1725 and is that man's grandfather; the grandson was named after him. ¶3

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade (regrade 3): Probable to Confirmed as father of Erich Pedersen of Tuntland (see his page); graded on his link to Erich Pedersen. Naming record: the probate after Peder Erichsen at Tuntland, Hjelmeland, 27 Sep 1725 (Ryfylke skifteprotokoll D 4, DA 15920, fol. 246b, scan and), between his widow Anna Danielsdatter and 'deris... sammen avlede 6 Børn... 1 Søn og 5 Døttre, nemlig Erich Pedersen... myndig'; in the division Erich is credited with houses he had built 'udi gaarden Tuntland' and takes the brother's share in Tuntland land. Continuity, all on Tuntland: the 1701 manntall has 'Erich Pedersen, søn, 5' with his father Peder Erichsen, 52; the tree's Erich is the 'afgangne Mand Erich Pedersen' whose probate was held at Tuntland on 31 Jan 1742 (D 9, DA 23886, fol. 25b and 118a, scan), his son Peder Erichsen, 12, inheriting movable and real property (the Tuntland land). Erich was Peder's only son, and no other Erich Pedersen is recorded on Tuntland; no named rival. ¶8a
