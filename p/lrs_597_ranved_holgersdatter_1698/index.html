About the family

Mother of Oline Pedersdatter (see her page): Speculative. She would have been about 47 at Olene's birth (about 1745), possible since her son Rasmus was born about 1738. Supporting: Olene named her daughter Ranveig (Randvej Karine, b. about 1784). Against certainty: Olene may be Peder Knudsen's daughter by an earlier wife; no record states the relationship. ¶2

Life story

In the 1758 soul register of Skudenes parish she is listed second in the householders column at 'Hejen eller Ranvoldsjord' (Heien or Randvoldjord), aged 60 (born about 1698, inferred), after Peder Knudsen, 58, so evidently his wife. The first-name reading 'Ranved' is clear on the scan; it is probably a form of Ranveig/Ragnveig. Rasmus Rasmussen (20) and Engebor Rasmusdatter (39) in the children column of the same household were evidently her children by an earlier husband named Rasmus. ¶1

1762: the 1762 ekstraskatt for Skudenes (source 49646, view 607) lists at farm 39 'Randvoldiord' the farmer Siur Pedersen with Kirstine Hansd., and, in the column 'Andre for sig selv' (others living on their own means, i.e. the retired couple), the group 'Peder Knudsen / Randveg Holgersd. / Olene Pedersd.'. So she was alive in 1762 and still living with Peder Knudsen and Olene, then about 17. The list writes no relationship words and gives no ages, so it is consistent with, but does not prove, her being Olene's mother; her grade (Probable, on her link to Oline Pedersdatter) is unchanged. Her name reading 'Randveg' (Ranveig) confirms the bygdebok form Rannveig. Rasmus Rasmussen (her son by an earlier husband, 20 in 1758) is listed as a separate householder on the farm, with a servant 'Knud Rasmussen'. ¶5a

The 1764 probate after Peder Knudsen of Randvoldjord (designation register 23888, view 88) names only him as deceased and only Olene Pedersdatter (17) as heir. It does not mention Ranved, so it neither proves nor rules out that she was Olene's mother; confidence left Speculative. Rasmus and Engebor Rasmusdatter are not heirs, consistent with their being Ranved's children by an earlier husband. ¶3

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: Speculative to Probable, graded on her link to Oline Pedersdatter. Bygdebok for Karmøy: Åkra (1980), farm 21 Heiå (= 'Hejen eller Ranvoldsjord'), rebuilt from nb.no word boxes: on canvas 186, '7. 1733-1756: Peder Knutsen, ca. 1705, d. ca. 1764 [...] G.m. Rannveig Holgersdtr., ca. 1698. Born: a. Sjur, ca. 1734, [8.] brukar; b. Knut, ca. 1745, [...] bnr. 6; c. [name not recovered], ca. 1745, [...] bnr. 4'; and on canvas 178, Heiå bnr. 4: '1. 1765-1795: Erik [...] ca. 1731. G.m. [Oline] Pedersdtr., ca. 1745, dotter [av] Peder [og] Rannveig [...] bnr. 15. Born: [...] d. Rannveig, ca. 1784, [g.m....] Åkra.' The bygdebok thus states that Oline was the daughter of Peder and Rannveig. A compiled secondary source, so Probable, not Confirmed; the 1764 probate after Peder Knudsen does not mention her. The book also makes Sjur (b. ca. 1734) her son by Peder, which fits Sivert/Sjur Pedersen Ranvoldjord being Olene's guardian in 1764; it is silent on Rasmus Rasmussen and Engebor Rasmusdatter of the 1758 household. ¶4

Searches that found nothing 2

Negative, with page map: no probate after her found. (1) The Ryfylke probate card index 1774-1788 (source 39936; it covers D 7a and D 7b, 23891-23892, so a page-by-page read of D 7a is not needed) has, in the Stangeland skipreide section (views ~815-950, filed by farm), no Heien/Heiå/Hejen card (H runs Hagen 845, Halholmen 846, Hildesland 846-848) and only one Randvoldjord card: 879b, 'Randvaldsjord i Stangeland, side 260, 1782 4/6, + Rasmus Rasmussen g. Malene Christophersd., Børn: Peder 10½, Christopher 8, Rasmus 1½' (her son by her first husband; she is not on the card). (2) The register of minors' estates 23888 has no Randvoldjord/Heien entry in the 1763 Stangeland block (views 63-69), the 1765 block (views 95-96) or the 1766 block (views 109-110); 1764 (view 88) has only Peder Knudsen. (3) The D 6 register (1767-74) has none. Since 23888 lists estates with minor heirs, and Olene (b. ~1745) was a minor until about 1770, Ranveig probably did not die in 1763-66 with a formal probate. Remaining test records: Karmsund probates 1788-1800 (D 8a/8b, 23893-23894; A1, 23911), and Rasmus Rasmussen's 1782 probate, D 7a fol. 260, which may mention his mother if alive. ¶6

Negative: (1) The probate after her son Rasmus Rasmussen of Randvoldjord, 4 June 1782 (D 7a, source 23891, fol. 260a, view 261), was read from the scan. Its preamble names the deceased, his widow Malene Christophersdatter and their 3 children (as on card 879b); no mother Ranveig/Randveg is named among those present or as heir, so she was very probably dead by June 1782. The scan is high-contrast and the inventory columns were not read line by line. (2) The 1809 back register of the Karmsund og Hesby probate protocols D 8a/8b (source 23894, views 382-383) lists every Stangeland skipreide estate for 1789-1795. The only Randvoldjord entry is 1791 'Kirsten Hansdatter Randvoldsjord' fol. 289 (wife of Sjur Pedersen). No Ranveig Holgersdatter. So no probate for her has been found in 1757-1795 (23888, D 6 register, card index 39936 for 1774-88, and this register). Grade unchanged (Probable, on her link to Oline Pedersdatter). Remaining test record: the probate after Sjur Pedersen of Randvoldjord, her son by Peder Knudsen per the bygdebok, if it names Oline as his full sister. His wife's 1791 probate (D 8a, fol. 289) is the nearest unread lead. ¶7
