About the family

Wife of Siur Olsen Alset and mother of Ole Sivertsen Alset: churched 2nd Sunday in Advent 1703 as 'Siür Alsets qv. Gertrud efter det 1ste barn [Olaf]' (16310 canvas 17 right, crop checked by the orchestrator). Married Siur Alset on the 4th Sunday after Trinity (9 Jul) 1702 (canvas 13 left), where read her byname as 'Ugl..' - probably Uglem; uncertain. Her patronymic is not given. Godfather Olaf Uglum at her son's baptism may be a relative. ¶1

Life story

Marriage entry re-read at full resolution (16310 canvas 13 left, bottom): '(4) Siur Alset og Gertrud Ugli[m?]' under the 4th Sunday after Trinity 1702, in a list of couples married that day. The byname is clearly 'Ugl-' followed by an abbreviation stroke, so she came from Uglem; no patronymic is written. The probate card 77804 c21 (re-checked from the saved image) reads 'Giertrud Joensdtr.' plainly. Lead, not linked: her father was a Jon living at Uglem before 1702; the 1701 manntall Uglem farmers include Jon Larsen 45, who is the right age for a daughter born about 1675-84. Unproved. ¶5

The 1702 marriage entry was re-read at 1500 px (16310 c13 left page, 4 p.Trin 1702): the couple are 'Siur Alset og Gertrud Uglen'. So she came from Uglem. Her probate card (77804 c21) reads 'Giertrud Joensdtr.', so her father was a Jon, probably of Uglem. Candidates in the 1666 Uglem list (35528 v21R) include Jon Paalsen 40 and the son Jon Olsen 7. In the 1701 list, Ole Jonsen (28) farmed at Uglem and may have been her brother. None is proved. The probate card names only her husband and children (guardians: the father, and the 'farbroren' Biørn Olsen), so the protocol text at skifteprotokoll 2, fol. 401b (24750/24751), is the next check for any kin of hers named there. ¶6

Probate (skifte) after her, 11 Feb 1732: 'Alset, Selbu. 11/2-1732: Giertrud Joensdtr. Enkemann: Siver Olsen Alset. Barn: Ole Siversen, 30 år gl. - formynder: faren, Siver Olsen Alset; Anders Siversen, 25 år; Baro Siversen, 15 år; Ole Siversen, 13 år; Joen Siversen, 13 år [?]; Mali Siversdtr., 2[6?] år, ugift; Inger Siversdtr., 20 år, ugift; Berit Siversdtr., 18 år - farbroren, Biørn Olsen.' (card 77804 c21; read by the orchestrator). This gives her patronymic Jonsdatter and her death before 11 Feb 1732, and names 'Ole Siversen, 30 år' - Ole Sivertsen Alset, born 1703 - as her eldest son (a younger son was also called Ole, 13). ¶3a

Open questions and the next record to read 2

What would carry this further: the marriage entry at full resolution (16310 c13 L) for her byname; the 1701 manntall lists only men, so her father would be an Uglem farmer of 1701 (Ole Jonsen 28, Ole Estensen 56, Siund Erichsen 52, Jon Larsen 45 - see findings/.md) - unproved. ¶2

Biography gaps: her exact birth date and place (before 1685) predate the 1699 Selbu register, so no baptism of hers survives in it. Her exact death date and burial are not found, and the 16310 burial entries for late 1731 to early 1732 (under Alset, Gjertrud or Giertrud) are the next check. Her byname in the 1702 marriage entry is still uncertain, so a full-resolution read of 16310 canvas 13 L is the step that would settle her origin. ¶4
