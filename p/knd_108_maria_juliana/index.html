About the family

Mother of Agnes Johanna (see her page), wife of Hans Jacob Jenisch (Johann Jacob Jenisch). Graded on her link to Agnes Johanna: Probable. Naming record: FamilySearch 'Germany, Births and Baptisms, 1558-1898' NVZ2-B9Z, Agnes Johanna, christened Brackenheim 14 Oct 1670, Father's Name 'Hanss Jacob Jenisch', Mother's Name 'Maria Juliana' (image read signed in: 'Hanß Jacob Jenisch, Maria Juliana', child 'Agnes Johanna'). Why only Probable: the step from this baptism to the 1696 Bietigheim bride crosses 26 years and two towns. It rests on the father's forename and town (the 1696 entry: daughter of the late Johann Jacob J., Gerichtsverwandter and coppersmith at Brackenheim), on her burial age (77 years and about 6-7 months in April 1748, so born about autumn 1670), and on the surname readings Janisch/Jenisch of the 1696 marriage; no record from Brackenheim in between has been read, and it is not shown that Hans Jacob Jenisch was the only Johann Jacob J. there. Surname unknown. ¶1a

Life story

The Brackenheim chain, all from images read except the sibling baptisms (labelled index rows): (1) 1667 marriage no. 752: Hanß Jacob Jenisch, son of 'H. M. Joseph Jenisch, wohlverordneter Pfarrer zu Münchingen', 'Kupferschmid und Burger allhier', married Maria Juliana, daughter of the late Heinrich Friderich Cast[en]. (2) 14 Oct 1670 baptism: Agnes Johanna, parents 'Hanß Jacob Jenisch, Maria Juliana'. (3) Baptisms of further children of Hans Jacob Jenisch and Maria Juliana at Brackenheim 1674, 1675, 1677, 1679, 1681 (index rows). (4) 1682 marriage no. 978: 'Herr Johann Jacob Jenisch, des Gerichts, Burger und Kupferschmid allhie, Wittwer' married Magdalena (indexed Barth), at Münchingen on 22 May; a son Johan Christoph followed at Brackenheim on 4 Nov 1684 (Anna Magdalena). (5) 1695 marriage no. 1263: his daughter Juliana, 'H. Johann Jacob Jenischs, gewesenen Burgers, des Gerichts und [Kupfer]schmids allhie nachgelassene eheliche Tochter'. (6) 1696 Bietigheim marriage: 'Agnes Johanna, Herrn Johann Jacob J[enisch]s, gewesenen Gerichtsverwandten und Kupferschmids zu Brackenheim hinterlassene eheliche Tochter'. (7) 1748 Bietigheim burial of Agnes Johanna, aged 77 years and about 7 months, so born about Sep 1670. ¶3

Burial found (image read): Brackenheim burials 1681, DGS 101835507 (item image 92): 'Starb H. Joh. Jacob Jenisch[s] Haußfr[au] Maria Juliana aetatis 33 Jahr'. The date is cut off in the margin of the image, and the index (QPGW-WTKH) gives 23 Mar 1681. The index misreads the surname as 'Miescher' and labels the husband as her father. Her youngest child Anna Catharina was christened at Brackenheim on 13 Mar 1681, so she died ten days after that christening. Death set to 23 Mar 1681 (her note said only 'before 22 May 1682'; that is superseded) and birth to ABT 1648. This fits her father's death at Hörden in March 1649. Mother not found: the Gernsbach baptisms of about 1647-48 are not indexed under Kast. ¶4a

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed, graded on her link to her daughter Agnes Johanna (see her page). Naming record: the Brackenheim baptism of 14 Oct 1670 (image read) names the parents of Agnes Johanna as 'Hanß Jacob Jenisch, Maria Juliana'. Continuity from the 1670 child to the 1696 Bietigheim bride in the tree, which left open: the father is identified at both ends by full name, trade, office and town, and he is shown at Brackenheim inside the gap (Audit 10 point 2). In 1667 Hanß Jacob Jenisch is 'Kupferschmid und Burger' at Brackenheim when he marries Maria Juliana (image read); in 1670-1681 he and Maria Juliana christen children there; in 1682 'Herr Johann Jacob Jenisch, des Gerichts, Burger und Kupferschmid allhie' marries again as a widower (image read); in 1695 his daughter Juliana marries as the daughter of the late 'Johann Jacob Jenisch, Burger, des Gerichts und [Kupfer]schmid allhie' (image read); and in 1696 Agnes Johanna marries at Bietigheim as the daughter of the late 'Johann Jacob J[enisch], Gerichtsverwandter und Kupferschmid zu Brackenheim'. No other Johann/Hans Jacob Jenisch appears at Brackenheim 1660-1700 in the indexes (the only other Jenisch father there is Eberhard Friderich, 1679), so there is no named rival. Window (audit 5 rule 1): her burial age (77 years and about 7 months, 19 Apr 1748) puts Agnes Johanna's birth about Aug-Oct 1670, and in any case within 1670-1671. Hans Jacob Jenisch's marriage to Maria Juliana (autumn 1667) lasted until she died before 22 May 1682, when he remarried as a widower. The whole window lies inside that marriage, so whichever Agnes Johanna is meant, Maria Juliana is the mother; a daughter of the second wife (from 1682) would have been at most 14 in 1696 and about 65 in 1748, which the burial age rules out. Her earlier Probable note is superseded. Surname: the 1667 marriage writes her father as 'Heinrich Friderich Casten' (genitive), so the surname is Cast. Image read signed in on FamilySearch, 2 Nov 2026. ¶2
