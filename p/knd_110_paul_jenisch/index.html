Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Graded on that link: Probable (compiled sources). The Pfarrerbuch Württemberg (Nr. 3905) names Joseph's parents as 'Paul, KiPfleger Augsburg, in Lauingen 1595-1608, in Stuttgart Hofdiener und Lautenist ab 1608' and 'Helene Keller'; the Leube-Liste (Tübingen Stift roll, 1630) gives 'V.: Paul J.' and Joseph's birthplace Lauingen. The national authority file (GND 117101915) describes Paul Jenisch, born 17 Jun 1558 at Antwerp, died 18 Dec 1647 at Stuttgart, as a musician, philologist and theologian, a student at Tübingen, Propst (church warden) at Augsburg until 1595, and later lutenist and court servant at Stuttgart. He kept two Stammbücher (autograph albums) now in the Württembergische Landesbibliothek. The careers match (Augsburg until 1595, then Lauingen, then Stuttgart), and the indexed Stuttgart christenings of 1609 and 1611 name 'Paulus Jenisch, Helena'. Dates are from the GND. A family tree on GEDBAS gives an earlier marriage (1585, Maria Bossart) and the marriage to Helene Keller at Lauingen on 15 Oct 1604; neither has been checked. ¶1b
