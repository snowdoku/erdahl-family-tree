Life story

Not in the 1801 census household of her parents at Eidum, which lists Jon 20, Jon 18, Ole 15, Ingebor 12 and Ingebor 9. Her birth year and husband are unknown. Her baptism (Selbu book 16311, about 1778-1800) or the registrations of 30 September 1814 and 8 June 1820 (registreringsprotokoll 57562, not read) would settle them. ¶2

Probate: 'Marit Siwertsdatter' is a daughter and heir in the skifte after her father Sivert Johnsen Eidem, closed 5 April 1815, and 'Mali Sivertsdatter', married ('gift'), in the skifte after her mother Helge Johnsdatter, closed 22 September 1820 (Strinda og Selbu district court, skifteprotokoll 10b, fol. 705b and 765b). Mali is a common Selbu form of Marit. Her husband is not named on either page. ¶1
