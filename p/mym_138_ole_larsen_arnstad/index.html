Life story

There is no Arnstad card in the 1689-1742 probate card index 77804. Views 41-45 run Amundsvig, Amundsvigen, Asklund with no Arnstad, so no probate after a Lars of Arnstad survives from those years. In the 1762 extra-tax list (48401 view 80) Arnstad is held by 'Arenstad Peder, idem Johan', probably the sons Peder and Johan Olsen. ¶5

The surety 'Oluf Lars. Mæle' in the Stjørdal betrothal book 16367 (1714-19). He signs as surety at least six times: 14 Feb 1714 (canvas 1, with Oluf Thomesen Mæle), 10 Sep 1714 for the soldier Johan Ellefsen (canvas 6 left, with Ole Thomassen Mæle), autumn 1714 (canvas 6 right), 2 May 1718 for Oluf Iversøn Mæle x Kirsti [Knuts?]datter Mæle (canvas 23, with Oluf Thomass. Mæle), early 1718 for the dragoon Mads Joensen Qvale x Giertrud [Thomas?]datter Mælen (canvas 23), and 31 Aug 1718 for the widower Halvar... of Skatval (canvas 26, again with Ole Thomas. Mæle). Oluf Thomassen Mælle was also one of the two sureties at Ole Larsen's own betrothal to Kari Pedersdatter on 2 Jan 1721 ('Oluf Larsøn Præstgaarden', canvas 42), and Ole Larsen was 'Oluf Larss. Mælle' at the baptism of his daughter Ane in 1722 and still at Mælle in 1724 (16368). So the 1714-18 surety and the 1721 groom are very probably one man, living at Mæle (Præstmælen, the plass under Værnes prestegård) beside Oluf Thomassen; if so he was of age by Feb 1714 and born before about 1690, which fits a wife born about 1692. ¶15a

Betrothal found in the forlover book (Stjørdal 16367, which earlier passes had not used): 'D. 2 Januarij 1721 Bleef Oluf Larsøn Præstgaarden troloved ved Værnæs med en Pige Karen Pedersdatt. Dybvad, og ere vi underskrevne forlovere for, at intet derimod er forhindrligt', signed by Peder Hognæs and Oluf Thomass[en] Mælle; margin: copulated 11 Jun 1721 at Værnes by the curate (16367 canvas 42). The surety from Mælle fits the couple's home at 'Mælle' in 1722-24. Mælle here is probably 'Præstmælen', a plass under Værnes prestegård (Stjør- og Verdal skifteprotokoll 5, source 24755, fol. 17b, holds a probate of 14 Jul 1753 after 'Mali Lars Datter' of 'Præstmælen under Wærnes Prestegaard', wife of Ole Erichsen Prestmoen; heirs are only her own four sons Lars 31, Paule, Jørgen and Anders Olsen, and the curator is Ole Erichsen Prestmoen, so it gives no Larsen siblings. Read; the typed register's year column misplaces it), which would explain why Ole is 'Præstgaarden' in 1721 and 'Mælle' in 1722. No parents are named. The surety Peder Hognæs might be Kari's father (she is Peder's daughter), but nothing in the record says so; untested. The card index 77804 has no Hognes card (Hofstad -> Hogstad) and no Præstegård/Præstmælen card for 1689-1742; the Mæle cards are 1699 (Biørn Hansen) and 1711 (Gjertrud Larsdatter Ofuer Mehle, heirs Lars, Hans and Peder Pedersen), neither naming an Ole or Kari. ¶11

Collateral, not yet linked. In 1723 an 'Ole Arnstad' was already at Skatval: on 6th Sunday after Easter he was godfather to Mali, daughter of Johan Wald, and on the 1st Sunday after Trinity his son Ole was baptised, mother Ingeborg (churched 'Oluf Arnstads pt. Ingeborg', 16368 pp. 168-170). If he is Ole Larsen, Kari Pedersdatter was a second wife; the 1754 probate lists six children 'sammen avlede' (theirs together) and no son Ole. The burial of 'Tior[?] Jonsen Arnstad 85 aar' in May 1723 (16368 p. 168) is also noted. ¶6

The 1723 'Ole Arnstad' with wife Ingeborg is NOT Ole Larsen. Stjørdal 16368 shows that he was Oluf Hansen: 'Die 8 Julij. Past: Scatvold... Copul. Oluf Hansøn Arnstad og Ingeborg Olsdatt.' (8 Jul 1722, canvas 98; betrothed 17 Jun 1722 as 'Ole Hans[en] Arnstad og Ingeborg Olsd. Flornes'). Their son Ole was baptised in 1723. A third Ole also married into Arnstad in 1722: 'troloved Oluf Pittersen Arnstad og Giertrud Pedersd.' (20th Sunday after Trinity), married at Skatval on the 1st Sunday in Advent 1722 (canvases 101-102). So Arnstad had at least three men called Ole in the 1720s (Hansen, Pedersen, Larsen), and an undated 'Ole Arnstad' in a later baptism cannot be assigned to Ole Larsen without a patronymic or the mother's name Kari. Ole Larsen's marriage to Kari Pedersdatter is not in 1722 (all of canvases 96-103, May-Dec 1722, read). ¶7

Mali's own baptism found in the separate 1730-39 baptism book (Stjørdal 16369, which the earlier passes had not opened; 16368 holds only churchings from c.1730). Skatval, 3rd day of Pentecost [Tuesday 2 Jun] 1733: 'Item Oluf Arnstads barn Mali, Moderen Kari, fadd: Jens[?] Skatvold, Ane og Thore Skatvold, Marit og Siri Myhr' (16369 canvas 41, p. 564). Of the three men called Ole at Arnstad in the 1720s-30s, only Ole Larsen had a wife Kari (Oluf Hansen's wife was Ingeborg, Oluf Pedersen's Giertrud/Kirsti), so this is Ole Larsen and Kari Pedersdatter, now living at Arnstad (they were at Mælle/Prestegården on the Værnes side in 1721-24). It fits the tree's Mali to the year: 68 in the 1801 census (Feb 1801) and 70 at burial in March 1803 both give a birth in 1732-33, while the rival Mali Olsdatter (bapt. spring 1727, daughter of Oluf Pedersen Arnstad and Kirsti; 16368 canvas 160) would have been 73-74 and 75-76. Grade kept at Probable on the link to Mali Olsdatter: the baptism and the 1754 probate together prove that Ole Larsen and Kari had a daughter Mali born 1733, but the step from her to the 1755 bride 'Pigen Malli Olsdatter Arnstad' still rests on the ages, because the 1727 Mali Olsdatter of Arnstad has not been accounted for (no burial or marriage of hers found). ¶10a

Burial found. Stjørdal 16370 canvas 206 (p. 205 right), after the 3rd day of Christmas 1753: 'd. 28 Decembr. Graffæstet Ole Larsen Arnstad 63[?] aar ved Wærnes' (buried 28 Dec 1753 at Værnes; the second digit of the age looks like 3, possibly 8). This is the man whose estate was divided at Arnstad on 21 Mar 1754 (24753 fol. 522a), less than three months later, so it is his burial: same name with patronymic and farm, and no other Ole Larsen of Arnstad is known. It gives a birth about 1690 (or 1685 if the age is 68). He was buried at Værnes, the church of the Mæle/Prestegården side where he had lived in 1721-24, not at Skatval. The age makes him of age by 1714 and fits the frequent surety 'Oluf Lars. Mæle' of 1714-18 in the betrothal book 16367 being this same man. ¶16

The age in the burial entry, 16370 canvas 206 (28 Dec 1753, 'Ole Larsen Arnstad... aar ved Wærnes'), re-read on the 3000 px crop: '63 aar'. The second digit is an open 3, unlike the closed-loop 8s in '28 Decembr.' and in the entry just above it ('Mali Larsdatter 18 aar'). So he was born about 1690, as already entered. Also found: Ole and Kari as godparents together at Værnes at Christmas 1726 ('Ole Lars. Mælle... Kari Mælle', 16368 canvas 155 right), and Kari's churching at Candlemas 1727 ('Ole Lars. Mælles h. Kari', canvas 157 left), so a child (not yet found) was born about Dec 1726 - Jan 1727. ¶17

Holder of the dragoon quarter (Dragon Qvarteer) Arnstad in Skatval, Stjørdal. Dead before 21 Mar 1754, when his estate was divided between his widow Kari Pedersdatter and their six children: Hans Olsen (farmer, of Brenset[?]), Johan Olsen, a third child (name not read), Anne Olsdatter (married), Guru Olsdatter (married to Ole Olsen Wold) and Mali Olsdatter (under age) (Stjør- og Verdal skifteprotokoll 3, 24753 fol. 522a, scan). Father of Mali Olsdatter, wife of Peder Pedersen Walstad. His own birth and parents are not yet known; the patronymic shows his father was a Lars. ¶1

The 1754 probate re-read at full size (24753 fol. 522a and the share list on 522b). Child no. 3 is Peder Olsen: '3. Peder Olsens lod... paa hans arvelod efter sin fader afg. Ole Larsen Arnstad' (Peder Olsen's share of his inheritance from his father, the late Ole Larsen Arnstad). Child no. 4 is 'Datter Anne Olsdatter, Peder Gre[ger]sen[?]s hustru'. Hans Olsen, no. 1, farmed Bremset: '30. Bremset Hans' in the 1762 extra-tax list. The text gives no ages, only Mali 'umyndig' (under age). Guardians: Mali's was her sister's husband Ole Olsen Wold. The widow's guardian (laverge) was a man 'fra Forbord', apparently Iver [?]sen, whose relationship to her is not stated ('Forbord Iver' in the 1762 list). No uncle (farbror or morbror) is named, so the probate gives no clue to Ole's or Kari's own parents. ¶4

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 4

Coordinator review (1 Nov 2026): kept at Probable under the overnight grading rule. The 1754 Arnstad probate proves that this couple had a daughter Mali Olsdatter, but her identity with the 1755 bride 'Malli Olsdatter Arnstad' (wife of Peder Pedersen Walstad) rests on the same name and farm 19 months apart and a fitting age. No record states the relationship outright. ¶2

Audit 2 (1 Nov 2026): upgrade restored, Probable to Confirmed; the coordinator's review was too strict here. His grade covers his link to his daughter Mali Olsdatter, and a primary record names it: the arveskifte of 21 Mar 1754 at the dragoon quarter Arnstad, Skatval (Stjør- og Verdal skifteprotokoll 3, 24753 fol. 522a; the auditor re-read the saved crop, which shows '6. Malle Olsd[atte]r' beside '... gift med Ole Olsen Wold') lists her among 'deres sammen avlede 6 børn' as under age. The tree's Mali is the 'Pigen Malli Olsdatter Arnstad', a maiden of the same farm, betrothed on 14 Oct 1755, nineteen months later, and her burial age (70 in 1803, so born about 1733) fits a daughter under age in 1754. This is ordinary record linkage of one person across consecutive records (same name, same single holding, short interval, no rival found), the same kind of link by which a baptism is joined to an adult. It is not identification by elimination. ¶3a

Probable marriage found. 'D. 11 Junij. Comm. Wærn. Copul: Ole Larss. Præstegaard og Kari Pederss. Dybvad' (Værnes, 11 Jun 1721; Stjørdal 16368 canvas 86). The same couple had a daughter Ane a year later: 'Døbt Oluf Larss. Mælles b. Ane, Mod. Kari' (Værnes, 2nd Sunday after Trinity, 21 Jun 1722, godparents from the vicarage household: Hr Johan Fron, Anna Maria Fron, Charlotta Friis, Jens and Giertrud Stoberget), and the mother was churched as 'Oluf Larss. Mællis h. Kari' on 10 Jul 1722 (canvases 97-98). Ole Lars. Mælle was still at Værnes as a godfather in Feb 1724 (canvas 118). The 1754 Arnstad probate names Ole Larsen's widow Kari Pedersdatter and a daughter Anne Olsdatter (child no. 4, by then married), so the pair Ole Larsen x Kari Pedersdatter with a daughter Ane fits exactly; Kari, born c.1692 by her burial age, was 29 in 1721. This is Probable, not Confirmed: the couple is at Prestegaarden/Mælle on the Værnes side in 1721-24, not yet at Arnstad, and no record found so far shows the move (16368's chronological list carries no baptisms from about 1730, only churchings and communions, so the children Hans, Johan, Peder, Guru and Mali could not be followed). If right, it makes Ole's birth before c.1700 and gives Kari's residence before marriage as Dybvad (Værnes). ¶8

Upgrade: Probable -> Confirmed, graded on his link to the daughter Mali Olsdatter (see her page). The record naming the relationship is Mali's baptism at Skatval on the 3rd day of Pentecost [2 Jun] 1733, 'Item Oluf Arnstads barn Mali, Moderen Kari' (Stjørdal 16369 canvas 41, p. 564), with the father's identity fixed by the 1754 arveskifte at Arnstad after Ole Larsen, which names the widow Kari Pedersdatter and among 'deres sammen avlede 6 børn' a daughter Mali Olsdatter, under age (24753 fol. 522a-b). Continuity to the tree's Mali: the baptised child of 1733 is the under-age Mali of the March 1754 probate, and she is the 'Pigen Malli Olsdatter Arnstad' betrothed at Skatval on 14 Oct 1755 to Peder Pedersen Walstad (16370 canvas 244), nineteen months later, of the same farm; her age at the 1801 census (68) and at burial in 1803 (70) both point to a birth in 1732-33. The only named rival, Mali daughter of Oluf Pedersen Arnstad and Kirsti (bapt. spring 1727), was buried on 14 Sep 1727 as 'Ole Pederss. Arnstads b. Mali' (16368 canvas 164), so no rival remains. Of the three men named Ole at Arnstad in the 1720s-30s only Ole Larsen had a wife Kari (Ole Hansen x Ingeborg, Ole Pedersen x Giertrud/Kirsti). Residual caveat, not a named rival: a later replacement daughter Mali of Ole Pedersen was looked for in the 16368 baptisms Sep 1727 - Apr 1728 (canvases 164-172, none) but May 1728 - 1731 were not re-read; however no Mali of Arnstad of any age is buried in 1730-37 and none other appears in the 1755 records. ¶14

Corrections 1

Correction: a rival candidate for the 1755 bride now exists, so this person's link to Mali Olsdatter (see her page) is lowered from Confirmed to Probable under the audit-2 rule (no rival candidate). Spring 1727 at Skatval: 'døbt Oluf Peders. Arnstads b. Mali, Mod. Kirsti', godparents Joen Myhr, Joen Opøfran, Marrit Myhr, Ingeborg Arnstad, Marrit Nielsd. ibid. (Stjørdal 16368 canvas 160). That is a second Mali Olsdatter of Arnstad, born about 1727, daughter of Ole Pedersen Arnstad (married 1722 to Giertrud Pedersdatter; wife named Kirsti in 1727). No burial or marriage of hers is known (the Stjørdal index starts 1755; no probate after Ole Pedersen or his wife is in the 1742-1802 registers). The 1755 'Pigen Malli Olsdatter Arnstad' could be either woman on name and farm alone. The ages favour Ole Larsen's daughter: the tree's Mali was 68 in the 1801 census and 70 at burial in 1803, both giving birth about 1733, while the 1727 child would have been 74 and 76. Ole Larsen's Mali is also 'umyndig' in March 1754, consistent with an unmarried young woman. Graded on the link to Mali Olsdatter. To restore Confirmed: Mali's own baptism c.1732-33 naming Ole Larsen and Kari (16368 has no baptisms in its chronological list from about 1730; find the separate baptism section or 16369), or a burial of the 1727 Mali Olsdatter before 1755. ¶9

Alternatives considered and set aside 3

The rival 1727 Mali Olsdatter of Arnstad (bapt. late Mar 1727, between Judica and 4 Apr, daughter of Oluf Pedersen Arnstad and Kirsti; 16368 canvas 160 left top): later burials checked. All burials of 1730-37 in 16369 (canvases 90-109 left, pp. 658-695; the book has only the first half of 1735, and burials end in Dec 1737, after which canvas 109 right starts the 1738 baptisms) read: the only Arnstad children buried are 'Oluf Arnstads 2de pigebørn 3 og 5 dage' at Skatval on St John's Day 1730 (canvas 91; two newborn girls, not the 3-year-old Mali), an unnamed 'Oluf Arnstads barn' at Christmas 1731 (canvas 95, no age) and 'Oluf Arnstads søn Peder 10 dage' at New Year 1732 (canvas 95). None is Mali, and no Mali Olsdatter or Mali of Arnstad of any age is buried at Skatval in those years. The indexed marriages begin 1781 for Stjørdal (Mali Olsdatter brides 1735-80 not indexed). ¶12

The rival is accounted for: she died as an infant. Stjørdal 16368 canvas 164 (p. 288 right), Skatval, 14th Sunday after Trinity [14 Sep] 1727: 'Graffæstet Ole Rydes qv[inde] Sunni 47 aar gl., it[em] Ole Pederss. Arnstads b[arn] Mali 13 uger[?] gl.' This is the Mali baptised at Skatval about 30 Mar-3 Apr 1727 as 'Oluf Peders. Arnstads b. Mali, Mod. Kirsti' (canvas 160): same father with patronymic and farm, same name, same year. (The stated age, read as 13 weeks, is short of the c.24 weeks since the baptism; the age word is cramped, but no second Mali was born to this couple in 1727, so the identification stands.) No other Mali Olsdatter of Arnstad is buried at Skatval in 1730-37 (16369 burials, canvases 90-109 read). ¶13
