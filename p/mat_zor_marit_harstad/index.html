About the family

Wife of Andreas A. Harstad of Selbu, married 1880 in Norway (the 1900 census records them as married twenty years and their eldest son John as 20). Enumerated as 'Marie Harstad', 40, in Augusta Township, Lac qui Parle County, Minnesota in 1900 with nine children at home. ¶1

Her patronymic Jonsdatter is preserved on her Find a Grave memorial, which is unusual for an immigrant woman of her generation in an American churchyard and suggests a family that kept its Norwegian forms. ¶2

Life story

Burial: Zoar Lutheran Cemetery, LOT 24, BLOCK 0 — and this is the memorial whose biography quoted the South Dakota state burial register verbatim and so led me to that database: 'https://apps.sd.gov/dt58cemetery/ — MARIT HARSTAD 1939 24 0 ANTELOPE VALLEY DEUEL ZOAR LUTHERAN'. It is the only Zoar memorial on Find a Grave that carries a plot number. ¶3

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
