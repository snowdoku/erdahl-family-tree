Life story

Baptized 15 Jan 1735 at Borgund as Jørgen, son of Povel Jørgensen of Store Kalvøen (Confirmed). As 'Jørgen Paulsen, born at Store Kalvøen', a bachelor of Diub, he was engaged 1 Jan 1768 at Borgund to Ingeborg Siversdatter, a maid of Diub. The witnesses were Rasmus Nilsen of Diub and Jon Jonsen of Store Kalvøen. ¶1

Probate: the 1749 probate after his father Poul Jørgensen of Store Kalvøen (3A 17, fol. 66b) names 'Jørgen Poulsen gammel 14 Aar', the eldest son listed, with guardian Ole Olsen Gamlem. That matches his baptism of 15 January 1735. His mother, Brithe Olsdatter, was the surviving widow. ¶6

Bygdebok: the Haram bygdebok, Band 2b, Store Kalvøy bruk 5, p. 377 (nb.no b709b66866b959f610ebd56887cb57fe, canvas 377; word boxes) lists among the children of Pål Jørgenson (ca. 1702 - 1749) and Berte (Ole Jetmundson's daughter, ca. 1695 - 1777): '6. Jørgen, ca. 1735 - 1784, [g.] 1768 [m.] Ingeborg... 1744', with children Jørgen 1768, Synnøve 1769, Siver 1771, Pål 1772, Siver 1774, Ole 1776, Berte 1777, Jon 1779, Inger 1782. This matches his record (m. Ingeborg Siversdatter, b. 1744, in 1768; d. 1784) and so places him in the bygdebok as Berte's son. Secondary source; his grade and his mother's are unchanged. ¶8

He married the daughter of the farm. Ingeborg Siversdatter, the 'maid of Diub' he was betrothed to on 1 January 1768, was the daughter of Sivert (Siur) Ingebrigtsen, the tenant at Djup, whose estate had been registered on 14 August 1766 - eighteen months before the wedding. Marrying the heiress of a holding whose master had just died is the ordinary way a landless young man of the outer islands got a farm, and it explains how a man born at Store Kalvøy came to be farming at Djup on Giske. ¶4

Farmer at Djup (Diub/Dyb) on Giske. His wife Ingeborg Siursdatter was churched ('introdusert') at Giske church on 25 May 1777. Children were baptized 1768-1782. The Haram probate index gives him as 'Jørgen Paulsen Djup', born 1735, died 1784, an heir in his aunt Brite Jørgensdatter Alvestad's estate (1775). His own probate was registered 1 Feb 1785 (Sunnmøre, 3A 27, fol. 401a; farm Dyb, Giske). His widow remarried in 1786. ¶2

Probate: the division (skifte og deling) of Jørgen Paulsen's estate was held at the farm Diub (Djup), Giske, on 1 February 1785 (Sunnmøre sorenskriveri, skifteprotokoll 18 = 3A 27, fol. 401a-402a, signed J. S. Bull). The widow was Ingeborg Siversdatter, with her legal guardian (lauverge) Siverd [..]sen of Diub. The heirs were: (1) the son Poul (Paal, baptized 1772), whose guardian (formynder) was Rasmus [..]sen of Diub; (2) a son about 5 years old, most probably Joen, with a guardian from Gudøen; (3) the daughter Berthe Jørgensdatter, about 7 (Birthe, baptized 1777), whose guardian was Jacob Christensen Gilsvær[?]. The other children baptized between 1768 and 1782, including Inger (1782), are not named and were presumably dead. The estate's debts were paid out in goods ('udlagt'), and each heir's share was assigned in the same way: Berthe's came to 3 riksdaler 9 skilling. The son's name is the only doubtful point. It is written twice, both times as a short word with no g, so 'Joen' is preferred over 'Jørgen', but the scans are low-resolution and black-and-white (fol. 401b-402a only 1411 px). Readings marked [..] or [?] are illegible or uncertain. ¶5

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

His link to John Jørgensen of Valderøy is Probable (see John Jørgensen). ¶3

Confidence review (13 Oct 2026): left at Probable. His identity and his father link are explicit. He was baptized on 15 Jan 1735 as son of Povel Jørgensen of Store Kalvøen. The 1749 probate of his father names 'Jørgen Poulsen gammel 14 Aar'. His 1768 engagement calls him 'born at Store Kalvøen'. The mother link to Berethe Olsdatter is the part that is not explicit. The baptism names only the father. In the 1749 probate the words describing the children as hers ('med hende avlede') are a bracketed, uncertain reading. A clear reading of that phrase in the 1749 probate (3A 17, fol. 66b) would allow Confirmed. So would an indexed churching of Brithe Olsdatter at Store Kalvøen in early 1735. ¶7
