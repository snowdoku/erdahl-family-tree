Life story

Primary record, user only: the 1646-47 kongsjordebok for Trondheim len lists 'Ingebrigt Vigge' among the Selbu tenants (51306 view 181 right, last entry before 'Stichtens Godz'), which places an Ingebrigt at Vigen in 1646-47, where his two Ingebrigtsen sons were users in 1665-66. The 1624 leidang roll has only 'Bersuend Wiigen' at Vigen (39890 view 78), so Ingebrigt may have taken over after 1624; Bersvend of 1624 is a possible father (naming pattern only; not added). No record names the relationship: Probable. ¶2

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the grade · 1 open question

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Graded Probable on his link to his son Ole (Olluf) Ingebrigtsen Vigen (see his page). The 1666 vicar's manntall (35528 view 20, Selbu no. 11 'Øffuer Wiggen') has two users on Over-Vigen, 'Olluf Ingebrichtsen' 50 and 'Bersvend Ingebrichtsen' 46, so two brothers (by patronymic) shared the farm. The bygdebok (Selbu i fortid og nåtid vol. 6, Overvik, canvas 324) says '[...] 1647 [...] Ingebrigt [...] bruker [på] Overvik. Etter [...] Ola [og] Bersvend [...] født [ca.] 1580' and later mentions 'En [...] tredje sønn av Ingebrigt', i.e. Ola and Bersvend were his sons and he was born about 1580 (ABT; gap text). ¶1

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Test record: the 1645 poll tax for Selbu (77038; the full-farm men not yet located) for Ingebrigt at Vigen with his household, and a court or lensregnskap entry for Ole Ingebrigtsen's bygsel of Over-Vigen (about 1650-61, per the bygdebok) 'efter sin fader'. ¶3
