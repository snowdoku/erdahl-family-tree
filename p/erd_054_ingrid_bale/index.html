Life story

Laberg 1985 (Bale, printed p. 60): wife of Anders, holder on Bale until about 1690, and mother of their son Ola; after Anders died she remarried and had a daughter Brita. Her patronymic was not read on the page (kgroenha.net calls her Ingrid Palnesdatter; the book gives 0 hits for Palnesdotter, Pålsdotter and Pallesdotter, but the word after 'Ingrid' at the line end is unread). ¶1

1701 census context: at Balle the index lists Palme Andersen (b. 1686) and Olle Andersen (b. 1689) as 'Sønn' under head Johanes Andersen (b. 1639). The probate shows Palme and Ole were Ingeri's sons by her first husband (Anders), so the census 'Sønn' is relative to the household or farm grouping, not proof that Johannes was their father; Johannes's own sons would be Johannessen. Johannes was not her 1720 husband (Hermundsen). Whether he was a stepfather in 1701 is unknown. ¶7

Other Palmesdatter/Palmesen of her generation in Ytre Sogn (Digitalarkivet person search, 1600-1730): Brite Palmesdatter of Trytten, Vik, whose skifte of 22 Oct 1710 (Ytre Sogn 2b fol. 400b, 24156 view 86 left) was between her widower Olle Trytten and their children, with son Palme; 'paa Børnenes Side var tilstæde deris Morbroder Johannes [Breche?]' (so Brite had a brother Johannes); Palme Gutormsen (b. 1651, Midlang); Palme Johansen (Vikøren, probate 1707-19, 2b 558a, unread). Nothing yet makes Brite or Endre Palmesen Ingeri's sibling. ¶10

Probate: Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll 3a (1720-1728), fol. 6a-6b. On 3 May 1720 sorenskriver Niels Wiborg with two lagrettemenn (Christen Siurstad and Mons [?]) met at Balle to divide the estate of the late Ingeri Palmesdatter, between her surviving husband [Niels?] Hermundsen on one side and, on the other, the deceased woman's child by her former husband, a son [name partly read, 'Anders...'], and by her last husband a daughter. The heirs' lots name '1 Broder Olle Andersøn Balle' (brother Ole Andersen of Bale) and '1 Søster Britte [Niels?]datter' (sister Brite); a further passage says both siblings received an inheritance after their late brother Palme Andersøn [reading Probable], and lists a second lot for 'Broder Olle Andersøn'. The estate was valued at about 40 riksdaler gross (reading uncertain). ¶4

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Probable: compiled bygdebok read through nb.no word boxes; no primary record read. ¶2a

Confidence moved Probable to Confirmed: her own 1720 probate, a primary record read from the scan, names her son Ole Andersen of Bale (the Ola Andersen Bale who held Bale until 1760) and a daughter Brite by her second husband, which matches Laberg's account (Ingrid, widow of Anders, mother of Ola, remarried, daughter Brita). Her sons' patronymic Andersen supports a first husband named Anders. ¶5

Corrections 1

Correction: her patronymic is Palmesdatter, read from her own probate (index 'Ingeri Palmesdtr, Bale'; text 'Sal: Ingeri Palmes datter'). kgroenha.net's 'Palnesdatter' was a near miss. Name entered as Ingeri, the probate's spelling of Ingrid. ¶3

Alternatives considered and set aside 2

Ruled out: Palme Johansen of Vikøren (Vik skipreide), probate 24 Jul 1719 (Ytre Sogn 2b fol. 558a), was a sergeant whose heirs were his widow and his siblings (Johansen); he is not her father. No other Palme probate 1698-1740 in Ytre Sogn. ¶8

Lead only, Speculative, not linked: the 1645 poll tax for Ytre Sogn (Digitalarkivet st=KOPP, firstname Palm*) has just two Palmes: 'Palme', a farmer at Brecke in Vik parish (page 40, pt00000000057830), with 'Birte Johansdtr' listed next after him (pt00000000057831), probably his wife; and 'Palme Johansen', a farmer's son at Henium (Henjum, Leikanger; pt00000000056272). The Henjum Palme fits the Henjum origin of Endre Palmesen (b. 1669). The 1664-66 censuses have no Palme in Sogn. So there are at least three candidate Palmes (Brekke in Vik; Henjum; the Palme who married Endre Fjærland's daughter). Ingeri named a daughter Britte, which would fit Birte Johansdatter as her mother, but that is a naming pattern only. ¶13a

Searches that found nothing 2

Negative, and a correction to the lead: the Ytre Sogn index row 'Endre Palmesen, Henjum Fosshage, 1728-1733, 3b p. 556a' (pa00000000520899) is not Endre's own probate. The entry (24158 view 269, right page, read) is dated 17 Mar 1733 at Fosshage in Sjøstrand skipreide, after 'Qvinden Guri Jonsdatter', who had no children with her late husband 'Endre Palmesen Henium'. Her heirs are her own siblings and half-siblings (Jonsen/Jonsdatter) and their children, several at Mundal. It names no Palmesen kin, so it does not test Ingeri's parentage. It does show that Endre Palmesen came from Henjum (Leikanger). ¶12

Open questions and the next record to read 4

What would confirm it: Anders Bale's probate (about 1690) or her own probate naming the children of both marriages. ¶2b

Lead for her father Palme (not linked). Laberg, Balestrand 1 (1985), Fjærland, gard no. 54 (canvases 335-336; the same text is in Balestrand: bygd og ætter, 1934, canvas 598): Endre, born about 1595, came to the farm about 1620 and died about 1672; of his children the book names Ivar Endreson (b. about 1630, d. 1707, m. Gjertrud Ellingsdtr.) and Anders, and says a daughter 'var gift med Palme'. This is the only 'Palme' in either book (phrase search). A Palme who married Endre's daughter (b. about 1625-40) would be of the right age to be Ingeri's father, and the Sogn name Endre Palmesen (b. 1669, Henjum, 1701 census; probate 1728-33) fits a grandson of Endre. Speculative only: no record ties Ingeri to this Palme. ¶9
