About the family

Great-uncle of Lydia Adelaide (Hicken) Hayter on the compiled account. 1881 census (Township 61, household 45): William Hicken, 75, farmer, Wesleyan Methodist, born England, with Henry (34), Jane (27), and children. The compiled Hicken genealogy gives his birth as 24 August 1806 in England, marriage to Mary Phebe Dixon (Dickson) of Scotland, seven children (John 1831, Agnes 1833, Phebe 1835, William W. 1837, George Samuel 1839, Thomas 1844, Henry 1847), and death after 1881. Henry Hicken's stone at Sturgeon reads 'died May 5, 1882, age 35 yrs'. His parentage is the compiler's, not yet proved. ¶1

Parentage: rated Probable. The baptism names George and Phebe; the same couple had children baptised in Kent 1796-1804 (when George was a private in the Royal Artillery) and a daughter Mary Ann at Doddenham, Worcestershire, in 1814, who matches William's sister Mrs Mary Ann Moore of Albion, PEI (died 1889 aged 75). William named a daughter Phebe (born 1835), and his brother George did the same. No record yet read on the Island names William's parents. ¶3

Life story

Baptism: William, son of George and Phebe Hicken, was baptised on 24 August 1806 at Little Cowarne, Herefordshire (England Births and Christenings index, from the parish register). The compiled Island Register genealogy gives William's birth as exactly 24 August 1806, so this is the record the compiler used; his age of 75 in the 1881 census of Township 61, Prince Edward Island (born England) agrees. ¶2

1841 census: 'William Hicken', carpenter, Lot 61 (1841 census of PEI, Kings County District 15, image 512), headed a household of seven. It comprised a man aged 16-45 (William was about 34), a woman aged 16-45, three boys and two girls under 16. All seven are entered in the column read as 'Church of Scotland' (image 513, as written; the rest of the Hicken family is Church of England, so this may be a misplaced entry). He held 50 acres on a 999-year lease with 11 years expired (so from about 1830), at 1s 2d an acre, a rent of 2 pounds 18s 4d. ¶4

William Hicken, carpenter, headed a Lot 61 household in 1841 and was the only William Hicken there; the 1881 census has him 75, born England. ¶5
