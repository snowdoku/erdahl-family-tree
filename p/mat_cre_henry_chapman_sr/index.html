About the family

Parentage analysis: He is Speculative as the father of Mary Jane (Chapman) Creed, born PEI about 1832. He is the only Chapman of the right generation known at Murray Harbour, and her age, place, religion and daughter's name all fit, but no record names her father (see her entry). He is also a possible father of Grace Chapman, born England about 1827-30, 'of Lot 63' when she married Samuel Moore in 1847. Her birth in England and Mary Jane's on PEI would place the family's emigration about 1828-32. That too is unproved. His estate file (PARO), the 1841 census of Lot 63, and Mary Jane's 1906 death registration are the records that could settle it. ¶7

Wife: Elizabeth Herring (see her page), married at Minster, Cornwall, 19 Oct 1812. His own baptism is not identified: the OPC index has Henry, son of John and Grace Chapman, baptised at Davidstow (near Minster) in 1784/85 and again 1790, among several other Cornish Henry Chapmans of 1775-1794; lead only, not entered. An Anne Chapman, 58, of Boscastle, was buried at Minster on 8 Oct 1823 (Exeter BTs, OPC burial id 4863836); she could be his mother (born about 1765), but nothing links her; lead only. His confidence stays Speculative because it reflects his link to Mary Jane (Chapman) Creed, which this family does not prove. ¶10

Life story

Family (compiled, Johnny Chapman, 'The Chapmans of Murray Harbour'): sons John (born about 1816 in Cornwall, died 10 May 1903 at Murray Harbour North, married Margaret Miller 1839) and Henry (born 1817 in England, died 4 February 1899, married Ellen Smallwood). The family therefore came from England, apparently Cornwall, to Murray Harbour after about 1817. Only John and Henry junior appear as Chapman heads at Lot 63 in the 1861 census, so Henry senior had probably died, or was living with a son, by then. ¶2

1854 marriage read: the register entry for James Creed and Jane Chapman (DGS 004594918, image 272) reads: James Creed, bachelor, and Jane Chapman, spinster, 22 February 1854, by licence, witnesses William Creed and Philip Lees(?), married by W. B. Aitken, J.P. The printed form has no columns for parents or residence and no Chapman signed, so it says nothing about her father. Negative for the parentage question. ¶18

Henry Chapman 'Sen.' of Murray Harbour: The Islander of 9 November 1855 reports the marriage at Murray Harbour on 23 October 1855, before John Dalziel, J.P., of Mr. Henry Colcroft to 'Elizabeth, eldest daughter of Mr. Henry Chapman, Sen., of Murray Harbour'. 'Senior' distinguishes him from his son Henry Chapman (about 1817-1899). ¶1

1861 census: Henry Sen. was not living with either of the Chapman heads of Lot 63. John's household of 7 and Henry's household of 2 have no one aged over 60, and each has only one native of England, the head himself. He is also absent from Henry Colcraft's household (3 people) and James Creed's household (6 people) in Lot 59. He had probably died between November 1855 and 1861, though he may have been living elsewhere. Henry the younger's 30 acres in Lot 63 were held on a 999-year lease from Sir Samuel Cunard that had run 8 years, so he took the lease about 1853; John's lease had run 20 years, from about 1841. ¶5

Chapman households 1861-1901: Lot 63 had two Chapman farms, John's (Presbyterian; wife Margaret Miller of Scotland, married 1839; children Jane 1841, Edward 1846, William 1850 and Jessie 1855) and Henry the younger's (Methodist or Bible Christian; wife Ellen or Helen, born PEI about 1830; no children of their own). Henry and Ellen had Elizabeth Grace Chapman (born 21 April 1864, baptised 12 February 1866), whom the compiler calls adopted. Her two names are those of Henry Sen.'s eldest daughter Elizabeth and of Grace (Chapman) Moore, which is a pointer only. 'Thomas Chapman, 60' in the 1881 transcription is Henry the younger. Edward the sea captain is John's son. No separate Thomas Chapman, born 1821, lived at Murray Harbour. ¶6

Cornwall family identified (Probable): the Cornwall OPC index has one couple, Henry Chapman, labourer, and his wife Elizabeth, whose children match the Murray Harbour Chapmans: William, bapt. Forrabury 25 May 1815; Henry, bapt. Minster 5 Oct 1817; Elizabeth, bapt. Minster 26 Feb 1822; Grace, bapt. Minster 26 Apr 1828 (Minster and Forrabury are adjoining parishes at Boscastle, north Cornwall). Henry Chapman married Elizabeth Herring at Minster on 19 Oct 1812 (Phillimore transcript, via the OPC index). Why this family: Henry Chapman junior of Lot 63 was born in England in 1817 (death card 1817-1899; 74 in May 1891); Henry Sen.'s 'eldest daughter' was Elizabeth (Islander, 1855), and Elizabeth is the first daughter baptised to this couple; Grace Chapman, born in England about 1827-30 and 'of Lot 63', married Samuel Moore in 1847, matching Grace of 1828; the compiled history says the family came from Cornwall; and no further children of Henry and Elizabeth are baptised at Minster after April 1828, consistent with emigration before Mary Jane's birth on PEI about 1832. Mary Jane (Chapman) Creed named daughters Elizabeth Chapman Creed (1857) and a son William Henry Creed (1860), names that echo this family (pointer only). Against or unexplained: the compiled history's John, born about 1816 in Cornwall, has no baptism in this family in the OPC index (William May 1815 and Henry Oct 1817 leave only a narrow gap); a second couple, Henry Chapman and Elizabeth Lang (m. Cardinham 1808; John 1808, Mary Ann 1810, Henry 1814 Braddock, William 1819 Braddock), also had a John and a Henry, but its eldest daughter was Mary Ann and it has no Grace, so it fits less well. No emigration record or PEI record names Elizabeth Herring. ¶9

Cornwall OPC checks (2 Nov 2026): (1) No John Chapman baptised 1812-1822 anywhere in Cornwall has a father Henry (21 John Chapman baptisms; fathers John, James, William, Edmund, Thomas, Jonathan, Arthur, Matthew, George). So the compiled family's 'John, born about 1816 in Cornwall' is not a baptised son of Henry and Elizabeth; their son of that slot is William (Forrabury, 25 May 1815). Either John came from another Chapman family or he was not baptised in an indexed register. (2) No Mary or Mary Jane Chapman baptised in Cornwall 1828-1836 has a father Henry (16 entries), which fits a birth on PEI about 1832 after the family emigrated. (3) Neither Henry nor Elizabeth Chapman was buried at Minster, Forrabury, Davidstow, Trevalga, Lesnewth, Tintagel, Otterham, St Juliot or St Teath between 1823 and 1850 (OPC burial index: the only Chapman burials there are Digory and Eliza at Davidstow, 1823 and 1825, Anne at Minster, 1823, and John at St Teath, 1825). This is consistent with the couple emigrating together after Grace's baptism in April 1828, and with Elizabeth being alive to bear a child on PEI about 1832. It does not prove either point, and his link to Mary Jane stays Speculative. ¶12

Herring kin at Murray Harbour: his wife's family, the Herrings of Minster and Forrabury, also emigrated to Murray Harbour. William Herring (bapt. Forrabury 20 Sep 1812), Francis (bapt. Minster 14 Nov 1821, indexed 'Frances, dau') and Pasco (bapt. Forrabury 10 May 1824), children of William and Mary Herring of Minster, are in Lot 64 by 1846-47, when all three married there (PEI marriage registers; Pascoe x Isabella 10 May 1847), and William (68, born England) and Francis (59, born England), Bible Christians, head households in Lot 64 in the 1881 census, beside Isabella Herring and her son Pascoe. See Elizabeth Herring for the details. ¶14

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 3

Speculative as the father of Mary Jane (Chapman) Creed: see her entry for the reasoning. Birth estimated from his son John's birth about 1816. ¶3

Upgrade: Speculative to Probable, moved by the Cornwall OPC baptisms of the Minster Herrings and their 1846-47 PEI marriages and 1881 Lot 64 census entries (see the note above), graded on his link to Mary Jane (Chapman) Creed (see her page). The relationship is still inferred, not recorded, which is exactly the Probable standard: (1) she was born on PEI about 1832 (24 May per the 1901 census) and was 'of Murray Harbour' at her marriage in 1854; (2) his is the only Chapman family at Murray Harbour, and his sons John (b. c.1816) and Henry (b. 1817) were too young to be her father, so no named rival fits; (3) the Minster family's baptisms stop after Grace (April 1828), and the family's kin, the Herrings, left Minster after November 1830 for Murray Harbour, which places the Boscastle group's emigration about 1831-32, just before her birth on the Island; (4) she was Bible Christian, the north Cornwall connexion of the Chapmans and Herrings; (5) she named children Elizabeth Chapman Creed (1857) and William Henry Creed (1860), after the Minster couple Henry and Elizabeth and their eldest son William. Still open: whether Elizabeth (Herring) Chapman was alive in 1832, and the 'of Lot 61' in the 1854 notice. ¶15a

Herring marriages read (images read; grade unchanged, Probable). The PEI marriage registers (DGS 004594918) show the Minster Herrings at Murray Harbour earlier than thought, with the father present: (1) Walter Miller x Mary 'Herron' [Herring], 31 March 1840, by licence, before James Richards J.P., witness William Herring (image 252); Mary Herring was baptised at Forrabury in 1815, daughter of William and Mary; (2) Francis Herring x Ellen Sencebaugh, 25 June 1846, witnesses 'William Herring Senr', John Sencebaugh Senr, Walter Miller and William Graham Junr (image 591); (3) William Herring x Ruhannah Sensebaugh, 23 March 1847, witnesses 'Wm Herring Senr', Benj. Sencebaugh and Walter Miller (image 624). So William Herring senior (who married Mary Parsons at Forrabury in 1806, and was perhaps Elizabeth (Herring) Chapman's brother) emigrated too, and the family was on PEI by March 1840. No Chapman witnessed any of these marriages, and none names Elizabeth Chapman, so they add corroboration of the chain migration only. (Pascoe Herring's 1847 marriage is not in the FamilySearch index; not read.) ¶17

Corrections 1

Correction: The compiled page 'The Chapmans of Murray Harbour' does not name Henry Chapman Sen. Its first generation is an unnamed 'Mr Chapman', the father of John (born about 1816 in Cornwall, died 10 May 1903) and Henry (born 1817 in England, died 4 February 1899). Henry Sen. is linked to those two men only by inference: in 1855 The Islander called him 'Mr. Henry Chapman, Sen., of Murray Harbour', and the only other Henry Chapman there was the man born in 1817. That makes Henry Sen. very probably the father of John and Henry, but no record says so. The compiled page lists no daughters. ¶4

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

His own baptism (lead, refined): at Davidstow, John and Grace Chapman had sons named Henry baptised on 26 April 1783, 28 June 1785 (a Henry of 20 July 1784 on a second, probably faulty, bishop's transcript sheet) and 7 January 1790. The Henry of 1783 was buried at Davidstow on 7 April 1784, so the candidates for the Henry Chapman who married Elizabeth Herring at Minster in 1812 are the Henrys of 1785 and 1790 (the latter would have been 22). The name Grace, given to the Minster daughter of 1828, matches the Davidstow mother. No record ties either Davidstow Henry to Minster, and no Henry Chapman burial is indexed at Minster, Forrabury or Davidstow between 1812 and 1850, which fits emigration (Cornwall OPC index). Lead only; no parents entered. ¶11

Searches that found nothing 3

Murray Harbour Methodist registers (negative): the circuit baptisms (FamilySearch DGS 005240355, 1820s to July 1841, then a gap to 1867) and the Wesleyan society member lists of about 1821-1839 contain no Chapman. The 1842-1866 baptisms, when a Chapman child of Henry would most likely appear, are missing from this register. ¶8

Probate (negative): the only Chapman will of Murray Harbour North found in the PEI Probate Estate Case Files (full-text search) is that of Henry Chapman of Township 63, farmer. He leaves his 30-acre farm and everything else to 'my wife Ellen Chapman' and names William Smallwood of the Baltic Road and Ellen as executors. Ellen (Smallwood) was the wife of Henry the younger (d. 1899), so this is the son's will. It names no other kin. No estate file for Henry Sen. was found. ¶16

Estate files (negative): the estate file of John Chapman of Murray Harbour North, Lot 63 (C testate no. 627, PEI Probate Estate Case Files 1786-1920, images 351-352) is his will. It names only his own children: daughters Jessie and Elizabeth (50 dollars each, paid by his son William), son Edwin (a cow), and son William (the 50-acre homestead in Township 63), with the residue to his surviving children. It names no siblings, so it does not mention Mary Jane Creed. Full-text searches for estate files of William, Francis or Pasco Herring in the PEI probate collections found none, and 'Mary Jane Creed' occurs only in 1930s will books (a later Mary Jane, widow of William Henry Creed of Albion). ¶19
