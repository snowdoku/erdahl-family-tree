About the family

Elder brother of Severt S. Eidem, so Mayme (Eidem) Eidem's paternal uncle. Linked as a sibling on Severt's Find a Grave memorial; the Norwegian birth place is inferred from the family's Selbu origin and not yet read in a register. ¶1

No children were baptised to this couple in Selbu after the wedding, so their children Gilbert (1877) and Minnie (1879) were born in America. ¶10

Life story

Baptism: baptised in Selbu on 27 FEBRUARY 1842 - Selbu parish register, Selbu local parish 1824-1842, births and baptisms, PAGE 165, SERIAL NO. 19. Child 'John Sivertsen', birth year 1842; father 'Sivert Johnsen'; mother 'Brynnild Johnsdatter'. The parallel register (1832-1842, serial 21, baptism entered 28 February) names the child 'John Siverts EULEM' - the transcriber's mis-reading of EIDEM, and the earliest record in which this family carries the farm name. ¶4

Born 30 January 1842 by the Elk Point memorial and baptised four weeks later: an exact fit, and it confirms the American date from a Norwegian register. He was his parents' eldest surviving child, born twelve weeks after their wedding of 8 November 1841. ¶5

1865 census: person 003 at oestre Eidem, Selbu - 'John Sivertsen', aged 24, son, unmarried, occupation 'Faderens Hjaelp' = his father's help, the grown son working the family farm. ¶6

MARRIAGE, 3 APRIL 1866, SELBU: he married BRYNILD ERIKSDATTER, b. 1843, of Eidem. Selbu parish register 1860-1877, marriages, page 18 serial no. 9, with the duplicate at Selbu local parish 1859-1868, page 149 serial 7. The entry gives the groom as 'John Sivertsen', b. 1842 (calculated), birthplace Eidem, with his father as 'S. Johnsen'; and the bride as 'Brynild Eriksdatter', b. 1843 (calculated), birthplace Eidem, with her father as 'E. Johnsen'. He was 24 and she 23. ¶7

This marriage is the last dated record of the family in Norway, and it closes the emigration window: the household cannot have left before 3 April 1866, and it was gone by the 1875 census. ¶8

His bride is the ERIK JOHNSEN EIDEM family's daughter - see the note on Brynild Eriksdatter - which makes the Union County land patentee Erick Johnsen Eidem his father-in-law and not, as the patronymic might suggest, his father's brother. ¶9

1870 census, probable: Brule Creek, Union County, Dakota Territory, 23 August 1870, family 91 (dwelling 86 as printed): John Severson, 28, farmer, real estate $600, personal estate $100, born Norway; Betsey, 27, keeping house, born Norway; Bidrine, 2, female, born Dakota. Severson is the patronymic Sivertsen, his age fits his January 1842 birth exactly, and the household is two doors from his father's (family 89). Conflict: the wife is written 'Betsey', not Brynild, the name in his 1866 Selbu marriage entry. The same enumerator wrote 'Emma' for his mother Brynhild, so a mangled Brynild is likely but not proved. The 1875 or 1880 census, or a baptism of Bidrine (born about 1868), would settle it. ¶13

Land patent, 1873: on 10 JUNE 1873 he received a United States land patent, document number 259, accession STA-SD1050__.242, for the SOUTH-EAST QUARTER OF THE SOUTH-EAST QUARTER OF SECTION 35, TOWNSHIP 93 NORTH, RANGE 50 WEST, and the WEST HALF OF THE NORTH-WEST QUARTER OF SECTION 1, TOWNSHIP 92 NORTH, RANGE 50 WEST, 5th Principal Meridian, Union County, Dakota Territory (three descriptions in all). He was 31. ¶2

The patent immediately preceding his, document 260 of the same day, is his mother's: Brynhild Jonsdatter, indexed as "Brynel Johnson Eidem", widow of Sivert Johnson Eidem who had died in April 1872. Consecutive document numbers on the same day for land in the same section are as close as the land records come to saying outright that two people are one household. ¶3

He died in 1881, aged 39, in Union County, South Dakota, nine years after his father and nine before his mother. ¶12

Cemetery register, probable: the DT58 register of Union County lists JOHN S EIDEM, 1881 (year only), SOUTH ST PAULS LUTHERAN cemetery, lot 23, block 1. Name, initial and year fit him. The same lot holds BRENHILD EIDEM, 1913, and SAMUEL J EIDEM, 1940. ¶14
