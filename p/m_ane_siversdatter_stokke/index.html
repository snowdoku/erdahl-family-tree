About the family

Wife of Poul Ellingsen of Stokke, churched in Feb 1766. She is a speculative mother of Elling Povelsen. ¶1

Her patronymic means her father was named Sivert (Sivard, Syvert). The naming argument for her being Elling Povelsen's mother stands as before - Elling's eldest daughter was named Ane - but no record has been found that links her to him. ¶3

Husband's probate: Nordmøre skifteprotokoll nr 15 (source 24650), fol. 365a, 25 Jan 1779, names her as 'Enken Anne Sivertsdatter', the widow of Povel Ellingsen Stoche, with their seven children born in the marriage, Elling (12), Sivert (8), Ole (6), Povel (4), Anne (10), Christie (17) and Maria (3). Graded on her link to Elling Povelsen Lillemark: already Confirmed; this is a second probate naming her as Elling's mother. ¶10

Parents added: Sivert Nielsen of Overland as Probable father and his wife (in 1767) Ane Iversdatter as Speculative mother; the reasoning is on their records. Her own grade is unchanged. ¶14

Father confirmed: Ekstraskatt 1762 (source 47939), view 57, farm no. 61 Øverland, Kvernes sogn: 'Sivert Nielss[en] og hustrue' with 'Niels Sivertss[en]' and 'Ane Sivertsd[atte]r' in the column for children over 12, so she was born before 1750 and her father is Sivert Nielsen Overland (now Confirmed as her father; the reasoning is on his record). Her mother is not named there ('og hustrue'), so Ane Iversdatter stays Speculative. Her own grade is unchanged. ¶15

Life story

Baptism found: Kvernes 16075 view 7, p. 8, Dom. 16 p. Trin. (21 Sep) 1738: 'Døbt Sivert Øverlands b[arn]: Ane. Mod[er]: Ane'. Godparents Peder Søvigen, Ole Søvigen, Elling Iversen ibid., Iver Søvigen's wife Ragnhild, Marrit [..]sdatter Øverland, Marrit Iversdatter Søvigen. Her mother was churched on 26 Oct 1738. This fits her as Sivert Nielsen's daughter over 12 in 1762 and unmarried at her marriage in 1764 (aged about 25). No other daughter Ane was baptised to Sivert in 1737-48. Her own grade (on her link to her husband and son) is unchanged; the record raises her mother Ane Iversdatter. ¶16

Baptism found (scan read): Kvernes chronological book 1753-1780 (source 16076), p. 122, Laetare Sunday 1767 (29 Mar 1767, computed from Easter 19 Apr 1767; the neighbouring entries are Oculi and Judica): 'Paul Ellingsen Stokkes og hustru Ane Sivertsd: b[arn]: Elling'. Godparents: Hørcken[?] Olsen of Stokke, Ole Johnsen Exstran, Arne[?] Ellingsen Stokke, Ane Iversdatter wife of Sivert Nielsen Overland, and the girl Ane Olsdatter Stokke. Confidence moved Speculative to Probable because this record names a son Elling of the couple, and no other Poul/Paul Ellingsen is indexed in eighteenth-century Kvernes, so the 1767 Elling is the natural candidate for Elling Povelsen. It is not Confirmed: no record yet ties this child to Guldset or Lillemork, and the 1801 census age (35 in Feb 1801, so born 1765-66) is about a year earlier than this baptism. The Feb 1766 churching shows an earlier child born c. Dec 1765-Jan 1766, baptism not found; if that child was also Elling and died, the link would still hold through the 1767 namesake. ¶4

Marriage found: Kvernes 16076 view 53, p. 101 (1764): under 'Fer. 2 Pasch.' [Easter Monday, 23 Apr 1764] 'trolovede: 1 Enkemand Paul Ellings[en] Stokke med pigen Ane Sivertsd[atte]r Øverland'; and under 'Dom. 4 a Pasch.' [20 May 1764] 'Copuleret Paul Ellingsen Stokke og pigen Ane Sivertsd[atte]r Øverland'. (The clerk's capital St in Stokke looks like H, as in 'Hoxxe'; the same hand writes Stokke this way for Jacob Olsen and others known to live at Stokke.) Graded on her link to her husband Paul (Poul) Ellingsen Stokke: already Confirmed; this is the primary marriage record. It also gives her as unmarried ('pigen') and of Overland in 1764, which ties her to Sivert Nielsen of Overland's household (his wife Ane Iversdatter was godmother to her son Elling in 1767) and to Ole Sivertsen Øverland, guardian of her son Ole in 1779 and 1785. ¶12

Overland family in the same book: Ole Sivertsen Øverland's wife Maren Olsdatter was churched 1764 (view 54, p. 103); Ole Sivertsen Øverland was godfather in Sep 1764 (view 55, p. 104) with 'Petter Sivertsen Søvig' named in the next lines of the 1765 Stephen's-day baptism of Ole's own daughter Ane (view 60, p. 114), where a '[?] [?]sen Stokke' (first name unclear, possibly Paul) also stood. So the Sivertsen siblings Ole (Overland) and Petter (Søvig) were both close to Ane and to the Stokke household. ¶13

Remarriage and death (same probate): after Paul Ellingsen died (between the birth of Maria, about 1775-76, and 1785), she married Erich Olsen of Stokke, who was her widower. She died before 21 Jan 1785. ¶8

Maternal-kin pattern (inferred): the guardians again include Peter (Petter) Sivertsen Søvig and Ole Sivertsen Øverland, both with her patronymic, and this time beside a guardian expressly called 'Farbroderen' (Ole Ellingsen) for the paternal side. The court called no one 'Morbroder', so the two Sivertsens are only probable brothers of hers. If so, her father was a Sivert, probably Sivert Nielsen of Overland (whose wife Ane Iversdatter was godmother at Elling's 1767 baptism). Not proved; no probate after Sivert Nielsen Overland was found in the registers of 24649-24651. ¶11

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 3

Still Speculative. A search of all indexed Kvernes and Kornstad records found no Ane or Anne Sivertsdatter at Stokke in the eighteenth century, and none in the 1801 census for Kvernes. The 1801 census does record two unrelated women at Stokke with similar names, Ane Knudsdatter (born about 1731, a widow, buried 1801) and Ane Svensdatter (born about 1795), neither of whom can be her. Her only record remains her churching (introduksjon) after childbirth in February 1766 in the scanned chronological Kvernes book 1753-1780. ¶2

Confidence basis restated (13 Oct 2026): her level is kept at Probable. The raise was argued from her son Elling's 1767 baptism, but that link is carried by Elling Povelsen Lillemark (now Probable). Her own parents are unknown (Sivert Nielsen Overland is a lead by patronymic only), so her level expresses only her identity as Ane Sivertsdatter, wife of Paul Ellingsen of Stokke, attested at her churching on 16 Feb 1766 (Kvernes source 16076, p. 114) and at the 29 Mar 1767 baptism (p. 122). Whether she should be graded Confirmed on those entries is left to David. ¶6

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed. Nordmøre skifteprotokoll 1783-87 (source 24652), fol. 321b: 'Anno 1785 den 21de Januari' a lawful registration was held 'paa... gaarden Stokke i Gimnes Tinglag' after the late Anne Sivertsdatter; the parties were 'Enkemanden Erich Olsen Stokke' on one side and her children on the other. Fol. 322a lists the heirs: '1. Sønnen Elling Paavelsen 17½ aar med Formynder Størcher Olsen Stokke; 2. Sønnen Sivert Paavelsen 14 aar med Formynder Sivert Pettersen Søvig; 3. Sønnen Ole Paavelsen 11 aar med Formynder Ole Sivertsen Øverland; 4de Datteren Maria Paavelsdatter 9 aar med Formynder Nils Bottolfsen Røvig'. Elling Paulsen, 17½ in Jan 1785, was born about mid-1767, which matches the 29 Mar 1767 baptism of Elling son of Paul Ellingsen Stokke and Ane Sivertsdatter (16076 p.122). The probate shows that this Elling survived to adulthood as her only son of the name. It is the record that the notes on Elling Povelsen Lillemark named as the one that would settle the link (a probate after Paul or Ane naming Elling as heir). Elling Povelsen of Guldset (1801 census aged 35, so born about 1766, the only Elling Povelsen in eighteenth-century Kvernes) named his children Ane and Paul. The identification of the heir with the Guldset man still rests on name, parish and age, because the probate cannot say where he lived later. ¶7

Corrections 1

Correction: her churching (p. 114) was on Invocavit Sunday 1766, which is 16 Feb 1766 (Easter 30 Mar 1766), not about 9 Feb. ¶5a

Open questions and the next record to read 3

Lead: a godmother at the 1767 baptism was Ane Iversdatter, wife of Sivert Nielsen Overland; Sivert Nielsen fits as Ane Sivertsdatter's father by patronymic, but no record says so. ¶5b

Leads for her own parents (inferred, not proved): the guardians named in her probate were Ole Sivertsen Øverland (for her son Ole) and Sivert Pettersen Søvig (for her son Sivert). At Elling's 1767 baptism a godmother was Ane Iversdatter, wife of Sivert Nielsen Overland. So she may have been a daughter of Sivert Nielsen Overland, with Ole Sivertsen Øverland her brother; her son Sivert carried the name. The probate gives no relationship words, so this is Speculative. ¶9a
