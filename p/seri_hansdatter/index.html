Life story

Married Lars Ormsen 3 Apr 1845 in Skudenes. She was 25 1/2, born at Akre. Her father was 'Huusmand Hans Ols. Akre'. Her given name was corrected in the register to 'Siri Karine'. ¶2

In the 1875 census she is 'Siri Karine Hansdatter', b. 1818 'Akre S. Skudesnæs Prgj.', at Stølen/Stegeberg. In 1891 she is a widow ('Siri Karina', b. 1817). She died 25 Jun 1893 at Stegeberg as a husmann's widow; the burial gives her birth year as 1819 and her birthplace as Skudenes parish. ¶3

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

CONFIRMED as John's mother by his 1853 baptism ('Serine Hansdatter'). The 1865 census gives 'Seri K. Hansdatter', 48, born Falnæs. Falnes lies in the Åkra/Skudenes area. ¶1

Searches that found nothing 1

Her baptism (about 1817-1819) was not found. Skudenes baptisms for those years are not indexed. The c.1842 Åkra household register lists the family of husmann Hans Olsen at Akre (children Erik 1815, Oline 1825). Siri, then about 23, was not living at home. Her parentage is therefore Probable: it rests on her father's name and residence in the 1845 marriage and on naming patterns. Her second name Karine comes from her mother Ranveig Karine, and Siri from her paternal grandmother Siri Jakobsdatter. ¶4
