Life story

Birth: born 8 September 1910 at 8 a.m. in Cameron Township, Murray County (post office Lake Wilson), registered under the surname Erdahl with 'Maynard Edwin' added by hand. He was the fifth child of Berent Erdahl, farmer, 30, and Anna Quame, 27, and all five children were then living. The physician's signature reads '[initials unclear] Baldwin' of Lake Wilson, and the certificate was filed on 9 September 1910. A note says it was amended by the State Registrar following a filing of 31 October 1944. ¶1

1930 U.S. census (index only): indexed as 'Magnard E Erdahl', son, born about 1911 in Minnesota, he was in his parents' household in South Malta, Phillips County, Montana. The image has not been read. ¶3

1950 U.S. census: on 1 April 1950 he was 'Maynard E.', son, 39, never married, born in Minnesota, a farmer working the farm, in his parents' household on LaBounty Road, rural Whatcom County, Washington (enumeration district 37-37, dwelling 25). Read from the page image. ¶4

Death (probable): the Washington death index lists 'Maynard E Erdahl', died 1989, King County. This agrees with the family list (1910-1989, from Find a Grave). The record has not been opened. ¶2
