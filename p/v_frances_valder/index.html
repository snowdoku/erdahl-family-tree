Life story

Born on the Lutheran Brethren mission field at Siangyang (modern Xiangyang) in Hupeh province, China, on 28 February 1921, two years after her brother Palmer was born in Honan. Her birthplace, together with Otto's in 1922, fixes Siangyang as a station the family lived at in the early 1920s. ¶1

Married Eilert Ingenan Walla (1917-2004) in 1944. She died at Everett, Washington - the same town where her aunt Caroline Aadland had settled. Gravestone inscription: 'BELOVED WIFE MOTHER AND GRANDMOTHER / SAVED BY GRACE'. ¶2

1930 census: Frances E. Valder, 9, daughter, born China ('Am Cit'), at school, in her parents' household at Ferndale, Washington. Read from the census image; the Ancestry index did not return her under 'Valder'. ¶3

1940 census (read from the image): 'Francis' Valder, entered as daughter, female, 19, single, born in China, attending school, highest grade read as 'H-2' (uncertain), in her parents' household at Ferndale, Whatcom County, Washington, in the same house as in 1935. The index's 'Francis' is the census spelling of Frances. ¶5

Marriage: the Washington marriage index lists Eilert I. Walla and Frances E. Valder, 1944, in King County, with a second line for the same couple in Whatcom County spelled 'Walls'. Index entries only; the certificate was not read. ¶4
