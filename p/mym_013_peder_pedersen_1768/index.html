About the family

Daughter's marriage: The 1828 marriage index for Doret Elisabet Pedersdatter, born 1799 at Skiervold, names her father ('bruras far') as Peder Pedersen. ¶2

Son: A son, Peder, was baptised at Skatval on 31 October 1802. The entry names the father as Peder Pedersen of Skiervold and the mother as Gullov Petersdatter (Stjørdal 1797-1815, p. 157 no. 2). ¶3

Children with Gullov besides Daaret (1799): a stillborn son buried 18 Oct 1801, and Peder, baptised 31 Oct 1802 at Skatvold (Skatval) church. ¶7

Parents: the skifte of 1 Jul 1790 at Baar after Peder Erichsen (24763 p. 222-223) names 'Sønnen Peder Pedersen 21 aar gl.' among the children 'af 2det Egteskab med den igienlevende Enke' Mali Olsdatter; his guardian was Jon Pedersen Schiervold. So his father is Peder Erichsen of Baar and his mother Mali Olsdatter (see her page). ¶10

Life story

Baptism, probable: 'Peder', son of Peder of Baar, baptised at Skatval on 16 Oct 1768 (index, pd00000042581758). Fits his burial age (37 in 1806) and his description as a soldier 'of Baa/Baer' at his 1798 marriage. Father linked at Probable. ¶8

In June 1792 'Peder Pedersen Baard', still a minor, gave a quitclaim (afkald) to his guardians Jon Pedersen Schiervold and Ole Pedersen Baard for his inheritance under a skifte of 13 Mar [1791?] (pantebok C-3 fol. 614b, source 19757 view 7). His co-guardian Jon Pedersen Schiervold was the half-brother of Gullov Pedersdatter, whom Peder married in 1798; his other guardian Ole Pedersen Baar was his elder brother (bapt. 1756). His sister Marit Pedersdatter's husband gave a matching quitclaim to Ole Pedersen Baard the same day, for her share under the skifte of 1 Jul 1790 / 13 Mar 1791, read as 'after the father'. This ties the soldier Peder Pedersen of Baar to Peder Baar's family between his baptism (1768) and his marriage (1798). ¶9

Marriage: He married Gullov Petersdatter of Skjervold on 14 June 1798 (engaged 24 April), Stjørdal 1797-1815 marriages p. 606 no. 25 (scan read). He is entered as 'Ungkarl og Soldat Peder Pedersen Baer', a bachelor and soldier from the farm Baar (Baa) in Skatval, so he came to Skjervold from Baa. The sureties (caverer) were Otter V[?]stad and Ole Nordlandet; the surnames are uncertain. Neither his father nor hers is named. ¶5

1801 census: Peder Pedersen, 33, is head of household 02 on the farm Schiervold (Skjervold), Størdalen, as an inderst (a lodger without land of his own), in his first marriage. The household is his wife Gullov Pedersdatter, 26, and their child Daarethe Pedersdatter, 2. The farm's owners that year were Peter Larsen, 56, in household 01 and Joen Pedersen, 60, in household 04. No record read here says whether Peder was related to either of them. ¶1

Death: buried (index date 23 Mar 1806) in Stjørdal as 'Peder Pedersen, avdød, Gaardmand', of Schiervold (the index adds his old farm Baar), aged 37 (b. about 1769) - a farm holder (gaardmand) by then, no longer an inderst. His widow Gullov remarried in Oct 1806. ¶6

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Not yet found: his death and his own parents. There are several men of this name in Stjørdal; for example, another Peder Pedersen, 35, was a husmand at Lille Valstad in 1801. Do not merge any of them with this man without a record that ties them to Skjervold. ¶4
