Life story

Nevell connection explained, maiden name still unknown: John Josselyn's mother Helenor had married John Nevell of Fyfield (d. 1537), and Gilbert Nevell of Braintree (d. 1550), John Nevell's brother, called John his brother-in-law (Canon E. R. Nevill, The Genealogist n.s. 35:81-87). So 'Alice Nevell Widow', whom John married at Fyfield 15 Jan. 1544/5, was very likely the widow of a Fyfield Nevell of his stepfather's family. She cannot be Helenor's daughter 'Alice Nevell', who is named in Helenor's will of 1547 under that surname and was Gilbert Nevell's wife 'Alys' in 1550. Canon Nevill's Fyfield register extracts include a William Nevell married to Margaret Brooke, and the burials of John Nevill and of a William Nevell (dates not legible in the scan). Her first husband is not identified, so her birth surname is not known. John's children run from Feb. 1545/6 to Aug. 1566 (Phillip, bapt. Roxwell, later Phillip Gooday), so a second wife is possible, and John's son John of Rivenhall (1596/7) does not name his mother. Grade unchanged (Speculative). ¶2a

How sure are we?

Speculative. A plausible candidate only. Treat this link as a question, not a fact.

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Graded on her link to her son Ralph Josselyn. Married at Fyfield, Essex, 15 Jan. 1544/5, as 'Alice Nevell Widow', John Josselyn (Fyfield register, printed by Crisp 1896, extract in NEHGR 71:32); Nevell is her first husband's surname, and her maiden name is unknown. John had children from 1545/6 (Helenora, bapt. Fyfield 1 Feb. 1545/6) to 1566 (Philippa, bapt. Roxwell 21 Aug.), Ralph being born about 1556 with no recorded baptism. A 'Josselyn vid.' is taxed at Roxwell in 1598 and the 'Widow Joslin' buried there 31 Jan. 1600/1 is taken by French as Alice. No second wife of John is known, but no record names Alice after 1545 or as any child's mother, and Ralph's birth window has no baptism. Speculative. ¶1a

Fines checked, grade unchanged (Speculative). Feet of Fines for Essex vol. 5 (1547-1580) has John Josselyn only as a purchaser (plaintiff), so no wife is named: Nalynghurst Hall in Braintree and Great Rayne from John Holmested and Alice (the John of Mashbury of Gilbert Nevell's will); 24 acres in Shellow Bowells and Willingale Doe from William Walter, esq.; Bollinghatch in Roxwell and Newland from William Walter, gent. (the fine of 1573); a messuage in Braintree from Ralph Turnor and Margery. No fine in which 'John Josselyn and Alice his wife' convey land was found, so no record ties Alice to John after the 1544/5 marriage. Test record unchanged. ¶3

Open questions and the next record to read 2

Test record: the Roxwell and Chignal Smealy registers and the Roxwell manor court rolls (Newland Hall) for 1550-1579, for a wife of John named between the marriage and Ralph's birth. ¶1b

Test record: Fyfield register 1538-1545 (printed by Crisp, 1896) for the burial of Alice's first husband Nevell, and that husband's will (Archdeaconry of Essex or Middlesex, c.1540-44) naming his wife Alice; for her as Ralph's mother, unchanged. ¶2b
