About the family

Daughter Siri married: in Oct 1735 'Henrich Tostensøn Oppen og Siri Knudsdaatter Loe' were betrothed in Ourdal, sureties Ole Nordager and Christen Ruud, and married 21 Mar 1736 (DA 9361 p. 30, canvas 284). She is the third child of the 1754 Lo division (Siri, married to Hendrik Tostensen), so the Lo family was at Lo by 1735. 'Knud Loe' stood surety at Svenes marriages in 1736 (Tollef Samsonsen Fodnes), 1738 (Ammund Ammundsen Tveit) and 1739 (two couples) (canvases 285-289). Marriage search: Aurdal marriages DA 9361 canvases 284-289 (Jul 1735 to Sep 1739) and 312-313 (Sep 1752 to Dec 1753) read in full: no Ole Knudsen x Astri Juelsdatter and no Ole Juelsen x Ragnild Knudsdatter. With the earlier passes (c269-283, c290-296, c300-309, c311, c314-316) the Aurdal register 1730-1754 is now read except c297-299 (1744-45) and c310 (1752). Both marriages were probably held in another parish (the bride's), or in the unread canvases. ¶6

Life story

Of the farm Lo (Loe) in the Svenes annex of Aurdal. Graded on his link to his son Ole Knudsen. Lifetime division of Loe: the Valdres probate card 8651 (DA 39948) and the protocol entry it indexes (DA 25287 fol. 539b, view 552, 'Skiftebrev paa Garden Loe udi Svennes Annex og Ourdahls Prestegield i Walders', 19 Feb 1754) record a skifte held in the lifetime ('i levende live') of the couple Knud Olsen and Marit Arnesdatter of Loe. The card lists their children: 1. Ole; 2. Ragnild, married to Ole Juelsen; 3. Siri, married to Hendrik Tostensen; 4. Guri, deceased, married to Niels Tronsen, with children Knud 10, Arne 7 and Engebret 5; 5. Gunild, married to Hendrik Juelsen. Gross 626-2-20, net 564-1-18 rdr. The protocol text was checked for the heading, date, farm and the couple's names, and for the daughters Ragnild, Siri and Guri; the son's line is too faint at the scan's resolution to read beyond the card. Identification: eleven months later, in Jan 1755, 'Ole Knudsen Loes og Astri Juelsd.' baptised a son Juel (DA 9361 p. 452, canvas 228), so the son Ole of the 1754 division is very probably this Ole Knudsen: same farm, same name, under a year apart. Guri's son Arne carries the name of her mother's father, which suggests Marit Arnesdatter was the mother of the children, not a stepmother. ¶1a

Ole Juelsen of Garli: 'Ole Juelsen Gardlie' stood surety in 1740 for Ole Biørnsen Gardlie and Marit Knudsdatter (DA 9361 p. 46, canvas 292), and 'Ole Juelsen Garlie' in Nov 1742 for 'Niels Troensen Garlie og Guri Knuds[datter] Loe' (married 28 Dec 1742; canvas 295). Guri is the fourth child of the 1754 Lo division (married to Niels Tronsen); her own probate at Garli i Skrautvaal, 3 Oct 1752 (Valdres card, fol. 432a; protocol DA 25287 view 444) lists children Knud 9, Arne 4 1/2, Engebret 3 1/2 and Guri 1/2, named after her parents Knud Olsen and Marit Arnesdatter; the father Niels Tronsen Garlie is guardian, so no Lo uncle is named. Ole Juelsen standing for Guri fits his being already married to her sister Ragnild (married to Ole Juelsen in the 1754 division) by 1742, and places him at Garli. The Garli probate cards of 1735 (Nils Olsen), 1740 (Anne Christophersdatter), 1742 (Olle Biørnsen), 1744 (Ole Olsen, nedre Garli) and 1754 (Peder Knudsen) name no Juel, so Juel's estate is not under Garli either. 'Knud Loe' stood surety at two Svenes marriages in 1740-41 (canvas 293): Knud Olsen was alive and active then. ¶5

Ole Knudsen Mitstrand stood surety for Gunild: at the betrothal on 4 Jun 1748 of 'Henrick Juulsøn Maanum og Gunild Knudsd. Loe' (Svenes; married 1 Jul), the sureties were 'Klokkeren Ole Halbiørnsen og Ole Knudsøn Mitstrand' (DA 9361 p. 70, canvas 304). Gunild is the fifth child of the 1754 Lo division (married to Hendrik Juelsen), and a bride's brother was the usual surety, so this joins Ole Knudsen 'Mitstrand' to the Lo family six years before Ole Knudsen 'Loes' baptised his son Juel. It does not name him as her brother, so the grade stays Probable, but it removes the doubt in about whether the Mitstrand Ole and the Lo Ole were one man. ¶4

Birth estimated: a daughter Guri was married with a son aged 10 in 1754, so the children were born from about the 1720s, which puts Knud's birth before about 1705. ¶2

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Probable, not Confirmed: the farm Lo had several households (four in 1801), the Ole Knudsen 'Mitstrand' who stood surety in 1748 and 1752 is not yet shown to be the same man, and the card does not say Ole was married. The later Lo cards fit: in 1818 the estate of Sigrie Thoresdatter Loe names children Ole, Juel, Ragnild, Marit (married to Syver Engebretsen Fodnes) and Astrie (card 8653), and an Ole Knudsen, b. 1777, farmed Lohe in 1801. That is the naming you would expect from a son Knud of Ole Knudsen and Astri Juelsdatter, carrying the family on at Lo. ¶1b

Audit 3 (1 Nov 2026): confidence raised from Probable to Confirmed (upgrade restored), graded on his link to his son Ole Knudsen (see his page). The worker's hold was stricter than the operative grading rule (BRIEF.md, clarified after audit 2). Naming record: the lifetime division of 19 Feb 1754 at Loe, Svenes (DA 25287 fol. 539b; faithfully indexed on probate card 8651, DA 39948), which lists Ole first among the children of Knud Olsen. Continuity: eleven months later, in Jan 1755, 'Ole Knudsen Loes og Astri Juelsd.' baptised their son Juel (DA 9361 p. 452, canvas 228). The tree's Ole is defined by that baptism, and the son is confirmed in 1769 as 'Juel Olsøn Lo[e]'. Same farm, a gap of under a year, and no rival: no other Ole Knudsen is recorded at Lo. The Ole Knudsen 'Mitstrand' of 1748 and 1752 is either this man or a man of another farm, so he is not a rival at Lo. That Lo had several households is not, by itself, a rival candidate. ¶3a
