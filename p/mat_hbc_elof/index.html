About the family

First husband of Edith Rita Hayter; he signed as Elof Borgstrom. In 1939 he was a truck driver of Summerland, aged 27, of the Swedish Mission Church, born at Ferintosh, Alberta, son of Nels Alavander Borgstrom, born Sweden, and a mother read as Dorah Hanson, born at Sioux Falls, USA. The marriage was dissolved at Penticton on 26 May 1950. Death, BC 1990-09-017351 (read): Eric Elof Borgstrom, a heavy-equipment operator in logging, divorced, died in the Salmon Arm district on 13 September 1990, aged 78. He was born 2 June 1912 at Ferintosh, Alberta, the son of Nels Borgstrom, born in Sweden. The record names his spouse as Ellen Mary Georgina Roach, evidently a later wife. He was cremated at Vernon. ¶1
