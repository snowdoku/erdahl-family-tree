Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Graded on that link: the groom row of Matthäus's own marriage at Illingen on 10 Jul 1725 reads 'Matthäuß Luntzelman', relatives 'Martin, Agnes Cathar.' (father, then bride; record 8393662, image 1187139-00162). Continuity: Matthäus and Agnes Catharina baptise at Illingen in 1732, 1748 and 1750; the 1748 child is the 1771 bride who names her father Mattheus. Ancestry 61023 index rows do not label roles (BRIEF audit 5 rule 3), so this rests at Probable until the image or a labelled FamilySearch twin is read; images not seen. Possibly the Martin Conzelmann x Margaretha of Tailfingen (Balingen) whose son Matthæus was baptised 3 Jun 1701 (8634059); not proved. ¶1b

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed, graded on his link to his son Matthäus Conzelmann (Mattheus Conzelmann). Illingen marriage register 1725 (image read), entry of 10 July: 'Matthäus Kuntzelman, ein Wagner[?], Martin Kuntzelmans Burgers [...] ehl. Sohn, und mit Ihme Agnes Catharina Hoffin, weyl. Joh. Conrad Hoffen Burgers und Becken[?] allhier hinterlassene ehl. Tochter' (trades partly uncertain). The register names the relationship (son of Martin Kuntzelman, burgher; his residence word is uncertain in the reading). Continuity to the man in the tree: the 1725 couple Matthäus x Agnes Catharina baptise at Illingen in 1732, 1748 (Friderica Magdalena, QPGP-CH5H, labelled) and 1750, and the 1771 marriage of Friderica Magdalena names her father as the late 'Mattheus Conzelmanns, gewesenen Burgermeisters, Wagners [...]' (image 346 read; labelled QPGR-XQL7). No other Matthäus Conzelmann at Illingen is indexed. Image read signed in on FamilySearch, 2 Nov 2026. ¶2
