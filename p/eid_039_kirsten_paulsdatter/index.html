About the family

Mother of Daardi Olsdatter (see her page); see Ole Paulsen for why the link is Confirmed. Both she and her husband are Paul's children by patronymic; whether they were related is not known. Her birth, parents and death are not found. ¶2

Parents added from the bygdebok, Probable: Selbu i fortid og nåtid vol. 5, Flora (canvas 509) lists 'Kirsti, f. 1725, [g.m.] Ola Pålsen Berge' among the children of Pål [...] of Flora (b. c.1686, d. c.1756) and his first wife Gunhild [...] of Marstad (b. c.1687, d. 1728); the Berge chapter (canvas 48) has 'Kirsti [...] fra [...] i Flora, f. 1725, d. 1803' as Ola Pålsen's wife (m. 1749), mother of Ingeborg 1757, Brynhild 1760 and Dordi 1761. Note the book's 1725 against her burial age (born about 1722). ¶4a

Her father is now proved by a primary record: the joint probate of 23 Nov 1756 after 'Povel Torchilsen' and his (second) wife Brynild Jensdatter (24753 fol. 732b) names 'Datteren Kiersten Povelsdatter gift med Ole Poulsen Berge'. Her siblings named there: Torchel, Einer (40), Jens (25), Guru (about 34) and Gunild (married). Her mother is not named in that record (her father's first wife Gunhild, d. 1728, per the bygdebok, stays Probable). Her own grade, on her link to her daughter Dordi, is unchanged. ¶5

Her mother is now proved by a primary record: the probate after 'Gunild Einers datter' of Flackne (Flaknan = Flora), 30 Jul 1728 (24750 fol. 193), names 'Kirste Powelsdatter, 3 aar' among the children 'tilsammen avlede' by Gunild and Povel Torchelsen; the same sibling set (Torchel, Einer, Guru, Kirsten) reappears in her father's 1756 probate, where she is married to Ole Poulsen Berge. Her mother Gunhild Einersdatter is raised to Confirmed on that record. Note the probate age (3 in July 1728, so born about 1725, as the bygdebok has) against her 1801 census age (b. 1721) and burial age 81 (b. 1722). ¶6

Life story

Named as mother, with Ole Paulsen of Berge, at the baptisms of Ingebor (1757, 'Kirsti Paulsd.'), Brynill (1760) and Dordi (22 February 1761, 'Kirsten Paulsd.'), Selbu ministerialbok 1757-1801, canvases 4, 9 and 11 (the 1761 crop). ¶1

Probable death: indexed Selbu burials 1803, p. 34 no. 3: 'Kirsten Povelsd.', widow, residence Berge, born 1722, aged 81, died 8 Aug 1803, buried 10th Sunday after Trinity. In the 1801 census she is 'Kiersten Poulsdatter', b. 1721, at Berge, the wife's mother, 'enke efter 1te ægteskab' - widow of her first marriage, which fits Ole Paulsen Berge (d. 1778). Identification by name, farm, widowhood and age. ¶3
