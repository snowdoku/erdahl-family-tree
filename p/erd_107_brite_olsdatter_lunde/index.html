About the family

Her brother's farm name on 2b fol. 445a re-read at higher zoom: 'Tolleff T[ondmes?]' (letters T-o-n-d-?-e-s, middle letter m/n uncertain). Not matched to a known farm. ¶3

Brother and father found: her husband's probate heading, 29 May 1713 (2b fol. 445a), re-read at 2x: 'paa Enchens vegne hendis broder Tollach Torsnæs' (the line earlier read only as 'Tollef...'). Tollach Olsen, b. about 1651, bonde at Toesnes (Torsnes) in Tjugum in the 1701 manntall, with Olle Olsen b. 1662 as a second bonde there; Tollef Olsen's 1729 probate at Torsnes names a Farbroder Ole Torsnes. Her patronymic Olsdatter and his Olsen give their father as an Ole, entered as Ole Torsnes (Probable; his own holding of Torsnes is inferred). Her mother is not named. ¶5

Life story

Named as the surviving wife 'Brede Ols dat:' in the probate after Hans Hansen, Lunde, 29 May 1713 (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll 2b, fol. 445a), mother of Hans, Johannes, Britte and Anne. Her brother Tollef T[?]... (surname/farm unread) acted for her. ¶1

Burial: Leikanger ministerialbok 11564, p. 345 (canvas 160 R), 1728, last entry of the burial column, in Advent 1728: 'Brite Olsd. Lunde' (no age). Fits her probate of 12 Feb 1729. ¶4

Her own probate was held 12 Feb 1729 at Lunde after 'den Sal. Qvinde Brite Ols datter', between her children Hans, Johannes, Britte and Anna (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll 3b, fol. 339a). Same name, farm and four children as the 1713 widow, so the same woman. ¶2
