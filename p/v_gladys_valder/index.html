Life story

Second child of Peter and Olga Valder, born 9 July 1916, the year her parents sailed for China; no birthplace is recorded on her memorial, so whether she was born in the United States before they sailed or in China after they arrived is not yet settled. ¶1

Married name Behnke. Buried in Oak Grove Cemetery, Fergus Falls, in the same cemetery as her parents. ¶2

Death: the Minnesota death index records Gladys H. Behnke, born 9 July 1916 outside Minnesota, died 18 October 1991 in Hennepin County, certificate 1991-MN-026508, mother's maiden name TUNGSETH. The birth date, the married name and the mother's maiden name all agree; no image is online. The index is the state's abstract of the certificate. ¶5

1930 census: Gladys H. Valder, 13, daughter, born China ('Am Cit'), at school, in her parents' household at Ferndale, Washington. Read from the census image. The middle initial is H here, and again on her death index entry, although the tree and her marriage entry use the name Margie; the difference is not resolved. ¶3

Marriage: the Washington marriage index lists Harvey M. Behnke Jr. and Margie G. Valder, Clark County, 1947. Index entry only; taken to be Gladys Margie because of the surname Behnke she died with. Probable. ¶4
