About the family

Widow of Erik Olsen of Øvre Haarstad, Selbu, at his skifte on 29 March 1742 (24752 fol. 1116b, view 383 left): 'hans efterladte Enke Ingeboer Olsdatter' and their four children Torgier Erichsen 8, Anne 17, Ingeboer 14 and Marit 11. Her lauverge was Peder Olsen Eidum (no relationship word); the guardians for the children were 'Moderens Søskende Barn' (her first cousins, or her siblings' children) Ole Pedersen Kolset and Torgier Mebust[?]. Birth before about 1707 is inferred from a daughter aged 17 in 1742. Her patronymic shows her father was an Ole. ¶1

Parents found, Probable: the skifte after Oluf Jonsen at Mebust, 8 Jun 1691 (24749 fol. 14-15b; card 77804 c1309) names his widow Gunnild Barroesdatter and their minor children Bård, Joen and Oluf Olsønner and Ingeborr Olsdatter, with Olluf Kolset as one guardian. That Ingeborg, a minor in 1691, fits 'Ingebor Olsd. Mebust, gl. 70 aar' buried 22 Jun 1760 (born about 1690), and the 1742 guardians of Erik Haarstad's children were 'Moderens Søskende Barn' from Kolset and Mebust. The link of the 1742 widow to the 1691 child is inferred (51 years, different farms), so the parents are Probable. Her own grade (on her link to Torger Eriksen) is unchanged. Birth narrowed from 'BEF 1707' to 'ABT 1690' on the 1691 skifte and the 1760 burial age; this assumes the identification. ¶7

Life story

Kolset side: card 77804 c1053, Kolset 19/5-1696, skifte after Paul Haldorsen, widow 'Ingeborg Rolfsdtr' (archivist's reading), one daughter Marithe Paulsdatter, guardian her farbror. Marithe Povlsdatter later married Ole Pedersen of Kolset (her skifte 1753, 24753 fol. 515 by the typed register), so the 1742 guardian Ole Pedersen Kolset was Marithe's husband. Views 536-539 of 24753 (fol. 513-515b) hold the end of a Selbu skifte and the skifte after Iver Evensen Børset (26 Sep 1753); the Marithe Povlsdatter entry was not found there (the register folio may be wrong); not read. If the widow of 1696 was really an Olufsdatter, Ole Pedersen Kolset's wife would be her niece; untested. ¶9

Kolset skifte found: the skifte after Marithe Povelsdatter, wife of Ole Pedersen Kolset, is No. 24, registration 27 Sep 1753 at Kolset, Selbu, in 24753 fol. 515 (stamped), which is view 539 RIGHT (URN sk20090213680540), not v538 as the folio offset suggested. Heirs: the widower Ole Pedersen Kolset and their four children, 1. Peder Olsen (of age), 2. a daughter [Ingeborg?] Olsdatter about 8, 3. Kirsten Olsdatter 7, 4. Ingeborg Olsdatter 3 (readings of the ages tentative); a Peder Olsen Kolset acts for the daughters. It names no Mebust kin, so it does not bridge Ingeborg Olsdatter Haarstad to the 1691 Mebust family. ¶10

See the note on Erik (Erich) Olsen Haarstad: the 1785 Solemshagen probate after Anne Erichsdatter (24761 fol. 405) has children named Erich, Ingeborg and Marit but no relationship word to Haarstad or to Torger. Grade unchanged. ¶12

24763 fol. 318 (the 'Anne Erichsd. i Salboe' row) is the 1792 Sanden probate after Anne Erichsdatter, wife of Thorsten Olsen. It does not name Torger or Haarstad, and Anne is probably too young to be Torger's sister. Grade unchanged. ¶13

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the grade · 3 alternatives set aside · 2 searches with no result · 3 open questions

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Graded Probable on her link to Torger Eriksen Svinaas (see his page), for the reasons given on her husband Erik (Erich) Olsen Haarstad: the skifte proves her son Torger born 1733, but identifying him with Torger of Svinaas spans farms and decades. Torger Svinaas named a daughter Ingeborg (about 1779) and two sons Ole (1774, 1775). ¶2

Alternatives considered and set aside 3

Not the Ingebor Olsdatter, wife of Ingebrigt Gundersen, whose estate was settled at Kvello, Selbu, on 15 Feb 1764 (24755 fol. 427b, view 447 left because Torger lived at Kvello in 1767). That woman's heirs were only her children by Ingebrigt (Gunder, Karen, Berithe and Sophia, aged about 4 to 12), with no Eriksen children, and a mother of a child born about 1725 could not have borne children in the 1750s. ¶3

Origin lead, Mebust (not entered as parentage). The guardian passage of the 1742 Haarstad skifte (24752 fol. 1116b, re-read) says the guardians for Torgier and Anne Erichsen/datter were 'Moderens Søskende Barn Ole Pedersen Kolset og Torgier Mebust' (the mother's sibling's children), with her lauverge Peder Olsen Eidum. That points to Mebust as her family's farm. Mebust records read: (1) skifte 26 Sep 1742 after Peder Olsen Mebust (24753 fol. 1b, view 19 R): children Ole Pedersen 28, Joen 26, Anne and Kari 21 - an Ole Pedersen of the right age to be her nephew 'Ole Pedersen Kolset', which would make Peder Olsen Mebust her brother, but the skifte does not place this Ole at Kolset; (2) skifte 13 Feb 1745 after Torgier Olsen Mebust (24753 fol. 114): widow Gjertrud and four minor children, guardian Torkild Olsen Mebust - no mention of Ingeborg; (3) burial 3rd Sunday after Trinity (22 Jun) 1760 of 'Ingebor Olsd. Mebust gl. 70 aar' (16311 c10 L): born about 1690, which is old for the mother of children born 1725-33 but possible. If these fit together, Ingeborg was a daughter of an Ole of Mebust and sister of Peder, Torger and Torkild Olsen Mebust - a hypothesis only. ¶6a

The coordinator's hypothesis that Peder Olsen Mebust (skifte 1742) and Torger Olsen Mebust (skifte 1745) were her brothers is NOT supported: her brothers in 1691 were Bård, Joen and Oluf. 'Moderens Søskende Barn' means her siblings' children, so Torger Mebust was more likely a nephew (a son of her brother Oluf Olufsen?), untested. Peder Olsen Mebust's children (Ole 28, Joen 26 in 1742) are named like grandchildren of Oluf Jonsen, but no record ties him in. ¶8

Searches that found nothing 2

Negative: the 1762 ekstraskatt for Selbu (Digitalarkivet 49639, canvas 136, matr. 672 Øvre Haarstad) lists the farmer Ole Jonsen and his wife, a daughter Kirsten Thomasdatter, Thomas Torgersen with Helge Olsdatter, and cottagers Anders Halvorsen and Ingebor Ingebrigtsdatter with son Bersvend and 'Mandens moder' Ingebor [Ingebrigts?]datter. No Ingeborg Olsdatter and no Eriksen children are there, so she had died, remarried or moved by 1762. ¶4

Negative: the full heading and guardian passages of the Mebust skifter after Peder Olsen (26 Sep 1742, 24753 fol. 1b, view 19 R) and Torgier Olsen (13 Feb 1745, fol. 114, view 135 R) were re-read: no Ingeborg Olsdatter, no Erich Haarstad and no Oluf Olufsen appear, and no relationship words beyond the deceased's own children (Peder: widow, children Ole 28, Joen 26, Anne married at Morset, Kari 21; Torgier: lauverge Jørgen Hansen Mebust, guardians Joen Torgiersen Mebust and Torchild Olsen Mebust). Her link to the 1691 Mebust family therefore still rests on name, age and the 1742 'Moderens Søskende Barn'. ¶11a

Open questions and the next record to read 3

Leads for her kin and death, not proved: (1) the guardian 'Torgier Mebust' of the 1742 skifte, one of her 'Søskende', was Torger Olsen Mebust, whose own estate was settled on 13 Feb 1745 at Mebust, Selbu (24753 fol. 114, view 135 right; widow Gjertrud S[..]sdatter, four children including Simon, Biørn and Siri Torgiersen/-datter, with Torkild Olsen Mebust among the guardians). Like her he was a child of an Ole, so he was probably her brother; but his skifte names only his own children. (2) On the 3rd Sunday after Trinity (22 Jun) 1760 a funeral sermon was held for 'Ingebor Olsd: Mebust, gl: 70 aar' (16311 c10 L, zoomed), born about 1690, which fits a mother of a daughter born about 1725 who had returned to her brother's farm as a widow. This would explain her absence from Øvre Haarstad in the 1762 ekstraskatt. No probate for an Ingebor Olsdatter at Mebust is in the typed registers of 24753-24758. Both points are leads only. ¶5

Next records: the probate after Marithe Povlsdatter, Ole Pedersen's wife, Kolset (24753 fol. 515), which may identify Ole Pedersen Kolset's parents; a skifte after Ingebor Olsdatter Mebust c.1760 (not in the typed registers of 24753/24755 under Mebust); and the Ole Mebust of the 1701 manntall (38461). ¶6b

Test record: a probate after her mother Gunnild Baardsdatter or a brother (Bård, Joen or Oluf Olsen) naming 'Ingeborg Olsdatter, Erich Haarstads hustru'; no such card under MEBOST in 77804 (only c1309). ¶11b
