About the family

Father of Kari Rasmusdatter (Probable): Kari Rasmusdatter of 'Schmauge' was engaged at Norddal on 1 Jul 1775 to Rasmus Alvsen of Blindem. Five years earlier this probate names an unmarried daughter Kari Rasmusdatter of Rasmus Sørensen of Smaage, the same farm. The Sunnmøre probate index has only two deceased at Smoge 1690-1830 (this man in 1770 and Rasmus Rasmussen in 1811, probably his son). ¶2a

His wife (Kari's mother) is not named; she had evidently died before 1770. Her probate is not in the index under Smoge. ¶3

Life story

Burial: 'Rasmus Sørensen' of Smauge (Smoge), aged 78 (b. about 1692), buried 20 May 1770 at Døving church (Norddal ministerialbok 1769-1773, p. 7, no. 30; index pg00000001729446). Name, farm and year fit his probate at Smaage held 15 Jun 1770, and he is one of only two Rasmus Sørensens in the national burial index with a birth year near 1692. His birth is not entered; the burial implies about 1692. ¶9

Confirmations: Rasmus Rasmussen of Smoge was confirmed at Norddal on 16 Apr 1747, and Kari Rasmusdtr. of Smaave on 9 Jun 1748. The 1748 list (scan read, p. 61) gives only her name and farm, with no parents or age. Both are probably children of Rasmus Sørensen. Rasmus Rasmussen of Smauge married in 1756 and was a father from 1762. ¶6a

1762 extra tax list (scan read): the Norddal section of DA 49637 gives FIRST NAMES only. View 232, pp. 23-24 (dated October 1762), 'Smouge' (Smoge), priest's benefice ('Pres Ciær'): household 1 'Rasmus og Mette' (the son Rasmus Rasmussen and his wife Mette Gundersdatter) with a farmhand Gunner, a maid Anna and, in the column 'Andre hos sig havende Folk og deres Navne' (others living with them), 'Rasmus': 5 persons; household 2 'Siur og Eli' with a child over 12 Laurentse: 3 persons. The lone 'Rasmus' in the others column is presumably this Rasmus Sørensen, retired on his son's farm, and he is entered without a wife, so Kari's mother was probably dead by 1762 (inference). Kari Rasmusdatter (about 28) is not listed at Smoge, so she was in service elsewhere. The list does not name Kari's mother and does not change his grade (Confirmed, on the 1770 probate). ¶8

Probate (scan read): headed 'Smaage i Dahle Sch[ibrede]', 'Anno 1770 d. 15 Junii' a probate was held after the late Rasmus Sørensen, between his 6 surviving children ('efterladte 6 Børn'; no widow is mentioned): 1st son Søren Rasmussen, of age, said to be in Bergen; 2nd son Rasmus Rasmussen, of age; 1st daughter Johanne Rasmusdatter, married to Ole [N..]rvigen in Wolden; 2nd daughter Marthe Rasmusdatter, married to Isach Andersen Schagge[?] of Haaem[?]; 3rd daughter Helge Rasmusdatter, married to Lars Pedersen Hellesæth; and 4th daughter 'Kari Rasmusdatter g[ammel] [18 or 26?] Aar', unmarried, whose guardian was Lars Pedersen Hellesæth (her brother-in-law). The age figure is cramped. Image. ¶1

Age recheck (scan read at full resolution): in the 1770 probate (3A 23 fol. 297b, 24564/300, left page, col. 1) the line reads 'Kari Rasmusdatter gl. 36 Aar', with the 3 in the flat-topped form this clerk uses. So Kari was about 36 in June 1770, born about 1734. This agrees with her confirmation at Norddal on 9 Jun 1748 (Kari Rasmusdtr. of Smaave), at about 14, and removes the conflict flagged. She was thus about 42 at the birth of Ane Marta in Mar 1776, late but possible. ¶7

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Regrade (1 Nov 2026): upgraded to Confirmed as father of Kari Rasmusdatter (see her page); graded on his link to Kari Rasmusdatter. The naming record is his probate at Smaage (Smoge), Dale skipreide, Norddal, 15 Jun 1770 (Sunnmøre skifteprotokoll 3A 23 fol. 297b, Digitalarkivet 24564 view 300, scan), which lists among his six children his '4th daughter Kari Rasmusdatter', unmarried, with her brother-in-law Lars Pedersen Hellesæth as guardian. Continuity: five years later, on 1 Jul 1775, 'Kari Rasmusd.' of 'Schmauge' (the same farm name, spelled Smaage in the probate) was betrothed at Norddal to Rasmus Alvsen of Blindem, and that Kari is the tree person (widow at Rasmus Alvsen's probate of 1790; her own probate at Blindem in 1798). Same name, patronymic and farm, a gap of five years, and no rival: the 1701 manntall shows one family at Smoge, the Sunnmøre probate index has only this Rasmus (1770) and his son Rasmus Rasmussen (1811) as deceased at Smoge 1690-1830, and the son (of age in 1770) is too young to have a marriageable daughter by 1775. graded this Probable 'by elimination' under the stricter reading; under the clarified rule (cf. the restore in AUDIT-2) it is Confirmed. Flags: Kari's age in the probate (18 or 26) is cramped and unread; 'Rasmus' quinde' Mætte Gundersdatter of 1769 is untested as Kari's mother. ¶4

Alternatives considered and set aside 3

Not Confirmed: no record yet names Kari of Blindem as daughter of Rasmus Sørensen; the link rests on name, patronymic and farm. ¶2b

Lead closed: Mætte (Mette) Gundersdatter, 'Rasmus' quinde' (Rasmus's wife) at Stranda in 1769, was the wife of a younger Rasmus of Smoge, presumably the son Rasmus Rasmussen. She was a mother at Schmauge with husband Rasmus on 31 Mar 1774 and 5 Jan 1777, after Rasmus Sørensen's death in 1770 (index records read). So she is not Kari's mother. ¶5

Caution: a Kari confirmed in 1748 would have been born about 1731-34. That makes her about 42-45 at the birth of Ane Marta in Mar 1776, and it fits the 1770 probate age only if it reads 36, not 18 or 26. Either the 1748 confirmand is an older Kari, or the probate age is misread. Recheck the age on 24564/300. ¶6b
