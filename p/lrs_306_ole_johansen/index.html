About the family

Child: Johannes, baptised at Brekke on Dom. Exaudi, 6 May 1742, 'Ole Johansen Asseims B[arn] N[omine] Johannes', mother Ingeber (11441 c24R). This Johannes is taken to be Johannes Olsen Asheim (see his page), father of Mons Johannessen; that identification is Probable (see Johannes's notes), so this person's place in the direct line is Probable. ¶2

Father (Probable): Johan (or Jon) of Asseim, whose estate is entered in the Stiftamtmann's table of Ytre Sogn probates for 1733 (24159 view 20 right, dated the 30th, between 26 Oct and 1 Dec 1733) with heirs the widow Maria Jo[h]sdatter and the sons Ole, Johannes, Hermund and Endre and the daughter Sigri. See the notes on Johan for the reasoning. Ole's mother is not identified; Maria is not proved to be her. ¶10

Child of 1748 (scan read): on 28 Jul 1748 at Brekke was baptised 'Ole Johansen Asseims B[arn] N[omine]: Hermun[d], Mat[er]: Ingebor', godparents 'Mattheas Asseim, Guttorm ibid, Torchel, Lasse, Kari, Guri Knudsd[atter] Asseim' (Gulen ministerialbok 11442, canvas 67, left page). This shows Ole Johansen of Asseim and his wife Ingeber still having children in 1748. The name Hermund fits both a brother Hermund (the 1733 Stiftamt table) and Britte Hermundsdatter, mother of the Ole Johansen born about 1724, so it does not decide which Ole Johansen this is. It does not name Johannes (1742), so the confidence is unchanged. ¶12

Life story

Godfather: on Palm Sunday 1719, at the baptism of Brudi[?], child of Lars Asheim and Anna, the godparents were Ole Clasen Asheim, Birthe, 'Gulig Assheim', 'Ole Johanss: ibid' and Birthe [P]edersdr ibid (Gulen ministerialbok 11438, canvas 34, right page). Ole Johansen and Gullov (Gulig) Johansen of Asheim stand next to each other. This is consistent with their being kin, but it does not prove it. ¶11

Two Ole Johansens at Asheim in 1726 (neither linked): (1) the heir 'Ole 2 Aar gl.' (b. about 1724), the only son of Johannes Nielsen Ese and his second wife Britte Hermundsdatter, in the probate after Johannes at Asheim on 17 Sep 1726 (Ytre Sogn 3a, fol. 200b) and again, as 'Ole umyndig' (a minor), in the probate after Britte on 25 Aug 1728 (3b, fol. 313a, which closes 'Sønnen Ole Johansön for tilfalden arv 16 Rd 4 ort 15 ß udlagt'); (2) an adult 'Ole Johanss.', owed servant's wages (Tienesteløn) in the 1726 estate, beside Guttorm Johanss. and Philippus Gullofss. Either could be this Ole Johansen of Asseim, father of Johannes (bapt. 6 May 1742); the heir of 1726 would have been only about 18 in 1742, so the adult servant is at least as good a candidate. No record yet says which, and Johannes Nielsen is not made his father. Nothing in these probates links Ole to Gullov Johansen. ¶9

Asseim, 1741: on 25 Jun 1741 at Brekke he was a cautioner (forlover, a man vouching that there was no impediment) with Ole Clausen Asseim at the betrothal of Lasse Colbeinsen Asseim and Mari Torsdatter Asseim; each signed with a house mark. On the same day he stood godfather, with an Ingeber among the godmothers, to Johanne, daughter of Jens Tjønning and Binthe (Bente) of Asseim. ¶4

Patronymic: his own father was named Johan or Johannes (from the patronymic Johansen). No further record yet. ¶6

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

2 on the grade · 1 on the identification · 1 alternative set aside · 1 search with no result · 1 open question

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Probable elder son: the child Johannes Olsen of Asseim, aged 8, buried at Brekke about 26 Feb 1741 (11441 c20R); no parent named. Also buried at the same service: '[Siur?/Ivar?] Olsen Asseim', aged 25, and on 21 Apr 1742 'Endre[?] Olsen Asseim' (age illegible); their father is not stated and could be Ole Clausen, so they are not attributed. ¶3

Confidence: Probable as father of the direct ancestor Johannes Olsen, for the reasons given there. ¶7a

The case for this identification 1

Identity: Ole Johansen of Asseim (Asheim), Brekke, Gulen parish, Sogn, a man who signed with a house mark (bumerke) in 1741. Birth before about 1712 is inferred only from a probable son born about 1732-33 (before about 1722 if that boy was not his, from Johannes, 1742); no baptism, census or burial has been found. ¶1

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Not to be confused with Ole Clausen Asseim, the other Ole on the farm in 1741, whose children are named in his wife Brite Joensdatter's probate of July 1751 and include no Johannes (Ytre Sogn A 7a, no. 36). The godfather 'Mons Ols[en]' of Asseim at the 1742 baptism was probably Ole Clausen's son Mons Olsen Asheim, who died in 1751 (A 7a, no. 37); inference from name and farm. ¶5

Searches that found nothing 1

Rival search complete (assessed): every Brekke baptism from Jun 1739 to May 1742 has now been read (11441 canvases 14-18, 19-24), and the 6 May 1742 baptism of his son Johannes is the only one of a Johannes son of an Ole of Asseim. His link as father of Johannes Olsen Asheim (see his page) stays Probable, because no record yet names him as the adult Johannes's father (see Johannes's notes for the reasoning and what would confirm it). The 1740 probate after Gullov Johansen Asseim (Ytre Sogn 6a No. 17) names an appraiser Ole Asheim but does not say which Ole, and it does not connect Ole Johansen to Gullov's family. ¶8

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Would confirm: a probate after him or his wife (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoller after 1742), his burial with age in the Gulen books, or his and Ingeber's betrothal (before about 1732, in Gulen 11440 or earlier if it survives). ¶7b
