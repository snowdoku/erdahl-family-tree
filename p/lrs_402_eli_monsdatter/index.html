Life story

Birth (inference): before about 1660, since her eldest son Hindrich was probably born about 1677 (1701 census, 24[?]). ¶6

Name: written 'Ell[?] Mons daatter' (with a flourish, perhaps 'Elj') in the 1699 probate of her first husband and 'Elj Mognsdaatter' in the 1701 probate of herself and her second husband; read as Eli Monsdatter. Mogens/Mogns is a form of Mons. ¶1

First marriage: widow of Iver Lassesen (see his page) at his probate on Indre Oppedal, 1 Jun [16]99, with their eight under-age children Hindrich, Lasse, Mons, Torbiør, Randi, Britha, Anna and Ingeborg. Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll 1a, fol. 45a-46a. ¶2

Second marriage and death: on 30 May 1701 a probate was held on Indre Oppedal after 'Sal. Lars Johansen, og hans Hustrue Sal. Elj Mognsdaatter' (the late Lars Johansen and his wife, the late Eli Monsdatter), both dead. On the husband's side the heir was his father Johannes Larsen of Indre Oppedal (so Lars left no children); on her side her children by her first husband Iver. She therefore remarried Lars Johansen (see his page) between 1 Jun 1699 and 1701 and died before 30 May 1701. Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll 1b, fol. 331b-333a. ¶3

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
