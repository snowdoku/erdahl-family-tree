About the family

Child: Synneve, baptised 3 May 1749, mother Borni, who married Rasmus Iversen of Botnen on 31 Dec 1773. ¶4

First wife's probate: on 31 Mar 1742 the probate court divided at Indre Hjertholm the estate of Kari Iversdatter between her husband 'Joen Gullach[sen] Hiertholm' and their three boys, Thore 11 (possibly 14), Iver 9 and Johannes 3; the guardian was apparently the boys' paternal uncle (Faders Broder), name only partly legible ('Gull...sen'). Appraisers Hans Brekke and Johannes [Tackle?]. Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll 6a (1740-1743), no. 10, fol. 198a-198b (partial reading). ¶12

Parents: the probate No. 11 held on 22 Aug 1741 on the farm Engesætter, Lavik skipreide, after Gullov [T?]ensen (patronymic partly illegible; the index reads 'Loensen') was divided between [his wife/widow, the word read 'Qvinden' or 'Konen', uncertain] 'Christi Joens Datter og deres sammen avlede Børn, Nemlig: 1ste Søn Joen Hiertholm, 2den Søn To[e]r[?] Hiertholm og den 3die Søn Lars Engesætter, alle myndige og tilstede' (Christi Joensdatter and their jointly begotten children, namely the 1st son Jon Hiertholm, 2nd son To[e]r Hiertholm and the 3rd son Lars Engesætter, all of age and present), and two daughters (read 'Agathe', married to Colbein[?] Johansen Engesætter, and 'Sirie', betrothed to Torke[?] Jonsen Engesætter; both readings uncertain). One of the appraisers was Iver Engesætter. Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll 6a, fol. 153a, read from the scan. ¶15

Life story

1701 census: on Engesetter, Eivindvik (Gulen), the householder 'Gullach Torkils[en]' 36 had the sons 'Jon Gullachs[en]' 3 and 'Tour Gullachs[en]' 1 (the 3 has the same form as the 3 in 36). This is the same pair of brothers, Jon and Tor, as the 1741 probate's 1st and 2nd sons, on the same farm, so Gullach Torkilsen is taken to be the Gullov of 1741 (the 1741 patronymic is partly illegible; see the father's notes). The 1701 age gives a birth about 1698, two years later than the burial age of 77 in 1773; burial ages of the period are often rounded up. ¶17

Residence: 'Jon jndre Hiertholm' (Jon of Indre, inner, Hjartholm) stood godfather at Brekke on 25 Sep 1735 to Hendrich, son of Lars of Ytre (outer) Hiertholm, together with Iver Botnen. By 1735 the farm was divided into Indre and Ytre Hiertholm, and Jon lived at Indre Hiertholm. An 'Athlet Olsd. jndre Hiertholm' was godmother on the same day; she is probably the 'Athlet i Hiertholm' who was godmother to Synneve in 1749 (relationship to Jon not known). Gulen ministerialbok 1729-1735 (source 11440), canvas 84. ¶5

First marriage: Kari Iversdatter therefore died before 31 Mar 1742. The sons Thore and Johannes match Thor and Johannes Jonsen, the first-marriage heirs in Jon's 1773 probate; the third son Iver (about 9 in 1742, b. about 1733) is new and is not named in 1773, so he may have died before then. ¶13

Patronymic: the 1742 probate reads 'Gullach[sen]', the 1773 probate 'Gullovsen'. Both forms are recorded; the 1742 reading is partial, and the 1773 form is kept. ¶14

Name: Recorded as 'Jon Hiertholm' (Jon of the farm Hjartholm, Brekke), father of Synneve, baptised 3 May 1749. His patronymic is unknown. ¶1

Burial: 'Sepult[us] Jon Hiertholm gl. 77 Aar' (Jon Hjartholm, aged 77) under the heading Dom. 12 p. Trin., service at Brekke, 6 communicants, in the Gulen ministerialbok 1749-1776, fol. 165. In 1773 Easter fell on 11 Apr, so the 12th Sunday after Trinity was 29 Aug 1773 (the entries just above are dated 27 and 28 Aug; the next heading on the facing page, Dom. 13 p. Trin., is 5 Sep). The evidence card of gave 'about 5 Sep'; 29 Aug is the computed date. Birth about 1696 is inferred from the age. ¶2

Probate: on 1-2 Oct 1773 the probate court registered, valued and divided at Indre Hjartholm (Indre Hiertholm) the estate of 'den ved Døden afgangne Dannemand Jon Gullovsen' (the late yeoman Jon Gulløvsen) between his widow Borne Nielsdatter and his seven children by two marriages, 3 sons and 4 daughters. Gross estate 144 riksdaler, debts 102, net 42. Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll A 9, p. 282-283, no. 17. ¶6

Name: Correction: his patronymic is Gulløvsen (indexed 'Gullovsen'), from the 1773 probate; earlier notes called it unknown and recorded him by his farm name only, as 'Jon Hiertholm'. His father was therefore called Gulløv; nothing else is known of him, and no person is created for him. His son Gulow (Gulløv) Jonsen carries the name on. ¶7

First marriage: to Kari (Kare) Iversdatter, 'forlængst bortdøde' (long since dead) in 1773. Their sons were Thor Jonsen of Skaravigen, the eldest son, and Johannes Jonsen (farm read 'Brec[ke?]' on p. 282 and 'Kroslevig?' on p. 286; uncertain), both of age and present in 1773. ¶8

Second marriage: to Borni (Borne) Nielsdatter, the surviving widow in 1773. Their children were Gulow Jonsen of Indre Hjartholm, of age, who had taken over the farm; Synneve Jonsdatter, the eldest daughter, then trolovet (betrothed) to Rasmus Iversen of Botnen; Christj (Christe) Jonsdatter, 20; Kari Jonsdatter, 16; and Haldis Jonsdatter, 13 (audit: both pages read '13 aar'). Gulow is probably the Gulak Joenssen, b. about 1742, head of a household at Indre Hiertholm in 1801, but that link rests on name and farm only. ¶9

Death: before 1 Oct 1773, the date of his probate. The probate is held at Indre Hjartholm, where 'Jon jndre Hiertholm' lived in 1735, and so supports the identification of the burial of 'Jon Hiertholm, aged 77' on 29 Aug 1773 (see the earlier note) as his. The death date BEF 29 AUG 1773 is kept, since the probate is consistent with it. ¶10

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Confidence: raised from Probable to Confirmed. The 1773 probate, a primary record read from the scan, names Synneve Jonsdatter, betrothed to Rasmus Iversen of Botnen, as the eldest daughter of Jon Gulløvsen and his widow Borne Nielsdatter. This proves the parent links of Synneve (see her page) to Jon and to Borni. ¶11

Upgrade: parents found and Confirmed. The 1741 probate after Gullov of Engesætter, a primary record read from the scan, names 'Joen Hiertholm' as the eldest son of Gullov and Christi Joensdatter ('deres sammen avlede Børn'); the 1742 probate after Jon's first wife names his brother To[e]r Gullachsen as the boys' paternal uncle, tying the 1741 heir to this Jon of Indre Hjartholm. ¶18

The case for this identification 2

Identity: Probable. The burial is linked to Synneve's father by name and farm only: a Jon of Hjartholm of the right generation. He would have been about 53 at Synneve's birth, which is possible for a man, especially if Borni was a second wife. No other Jon at Hjartholm has been seen in the Gulen register 1741-1751 or 1773-1776, but the farm had at least two households (Iver Larsen i Hiertholm married Jertrud Gjermundsdatter Sande in 1745, and Ole Johannesen of Indre Hiertholm died in 1774), so a farm name does not prove a family. Note that Hiertaas (Hjartås) is a different farm. His probate (Nordhordland/Ytre Sogn skifte, 1773-74) or an earlier marriage record would confirm it. ¶3

Identity: the 1741 'Joen Hiertholm', eldest son of a Gullov, is this Jon Gulløvsen (Gullachsen) of Indre Hjartholm. His patronymic is Gullach[sen] in 1742 and Gullovsen in 1773, he lived at Hjartholm from at least 1735, and seven months after the 1741 probate, at the probate after his first wife Kari Iversdatter on 31 Mar 1742, the guardian of his sons was 'deres Fader Broder To[e]r[?] Gullachsen' (their father's brother), which matches the 2nd son 'To[e]r Hiertholm' of 1741. The first name in 1742 is written with a looped capital and a superscript and is not certain; had read only 'Gull...sen'. ¶16

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Not a brother of Iver Gullacksen of Engesetter: the 1741 probate lists only three sons (Jon, To[e]r, Lars), and Iver Engesætter acted as appraiser, not heir. This supports Iver being the son of the other Engesetter householder of 1701, Gullach Lassesen (son 'Iver Gullachs' 2). ¶19
