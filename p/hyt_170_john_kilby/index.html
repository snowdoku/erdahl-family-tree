Life story

His marriage at Boston, 9 May 1662, to Elizabeth (Joslin), widow of Edward Yeomans, is entered in the town register as 'Edward Kilby & Elizabeth Yeomans widow were married 9th May by Major Generall Denison' (vol. 9); TAG 27:193 argues that 'Edward' is a clerk's error for John, as no Edward Kilby is found in New England before 1760. ¶2

Origin check: the printed Parish Registers of St. Albans Abbey 1558-1689 have no baptism of a John Kilby about 1625-1640 (Bartlett, NEHGR 57:415, suggests St. Albans). The Kilby entries of that period are children of Robert Kilby and Ellen his wife: Robert (1630) and Sara (1631). A John Kilbie and Elizabeth had children baptised there 1662-1675, so that John stayed in England. The register shows a Christopher son of Robert Kylbie baptised 1604/5 (Christopher being a name the Boston family used), but nothing ties the Boston tailor to Robert and Ellen. No parents entered. A further negative: the Records of the Suffolk County Court 1671-1680 (Col. Soc. Mass. 29-30) have no Kilby entry, and the Aspinwall Notarial Records mention only 'Henry Kilbie late of the said Citty [Coventry] Mercer deceased' as an abutter (Rec. Comm. 32:56). ¶3

Suffolk probate index read on the page image: Index to the Probate Records of the County of Suffolk (1895), vol. 2, p. 209, 'Kilby, etc.' lists only: Caroline F. 1872; Catharine 1744 guardianship 8046; Christopher 1742 will 7782; Cushing 1777; Ella J. 1872; Fanny 1777; Hulda 1777; John 1784 will 18195; John 1876; John S. 1872; Maria A. 1880; Martha 1780; Mary E. 1872; Michael 1801; Samuel 1784; Sarah 1744 guardianship 8047; Sarah 1771; Sarah 1786; Thomas 1733 administration 6363; Thomas 1813; William 1753 will 10520; William 1771; William 1801 (plus Kilbey/Kilborn cross-references). So there is NO Suffolk probate docket for John Kilby Sr. (d. 6 Dec. 1710), nor for John Jr.; this agrees with TAG 27:194 ('no deeds or probate'). The probate route to 'son Christopher' is closed. Grade unchanged (Probable). Crop:. Remaining tests: Suffolk deeds by Christopher or Deacon John Kilby reciting land 'of my father John Kilby', and SCJ file 451 (Wallis v. Kilby, 1697/8). ¶5

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Graded on his link to Christopher Kilby. John Kilby Sr., tailor, of Boston, died there 6 Dec. 1710 aged 78; possibly from St. Albans, Hertfordshire (NEHGR 57:415, Bartlett). TAG 27 (1951): 193-196 (W. L. Holman, compiled) makes him the father of Deacon John Kilby (b. about Jan. 1664), Christopher (b. about 1665) and Sarah (m. Richard Draper 1689). Supporting records cited there: 'John Kilbie Taylor & his family' (surety bond, 28 Sept. 1685, Boston Rec. Comm. 10:78); the Boston tax lists of 1687, 1691, 1692 and 1695 listing John Kilby Sr., John Kilby Jr. and Christopher Kilby; John Kilby Sr. admitted to the Brattle Church 4 Feb. 1699/1700; and 'John Kilby' witnessing the deed of Joseph Green to Christopher Kilby, baker, 5 Oct. 1699 (Suffolk Deeds 19:200). Christopher in turn witnessed deeds of 'his brother' John in 1712 and 1716. Probable: no record read names John Sr. as Christopher's father; there are no deeds or probate for John Sr. (TAG 27:194). ¶1

Searches that found nothing 1

FamilySearch full-text searches (negative for kinship): '+"John Kilby" +"Christopher Kilby"', '+Kilby +"son Christopher"' and '+"John Kilby" +"late of Boston"' found no Suffolk will, administration or deed of John Kilby Sr. (d. 1710) naming a son Christopher. Hits read: a deed of 5 Oct 1699 by Joseph and Mary Green to Christopher Kilby, witnessed by a John Kilby (Suffolk Deeds 1698-1704, image 110; no relationship stated); a 1712-13 Sewall deed and a 1706-15 court case of 'John Kilby of Boston, Shopkeeper' (the son, Deacon John); and Thomas Wallis's will (Suffolk probate 1699-1709, image 157), administered by 'John Kilby of said Boston Shopkeeper', which names Wallis's brother William and only daughter Elizabeth but no Kilby kin. No probate of John Kilby Sr. surfaced in the full text; the printed Suffolk probate index (vol. 2) Kilby entries are garbled in the archive.org OCR and should be read on the page image to see whether a docket exists for him in 1710-11. ¶4
