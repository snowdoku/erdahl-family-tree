About the family

Wife of 'Siur[?] Bersvendssen' at Draxen (matr. 471) in the 1762 extra tax list (the given name is hard to read; Siur is the better reading). He is Sivert Bersvendsen (see his page), d. 1766, whose probate names the children Bersvend and Ingebor but not the widow's name legibly. ¶1

Life story

Married in Selbu on Trinity Sunday, 5 Jun 1746: 'Ectviede... N5 Mandsfolket Soldat Siur Bersvendsen og gudsfrygtige [pige] Marith Anders datter' (Selbu klokkerbok 16315, canvas 87, 0-based, col 3); betrothed about 2 p.Pasch. (24 Apr) 1746, 'Soldat Siur B[ersvend]sen [patronymic overwritten] med gudsfrygtige [pige] Marithe Andersdatter' (canvas 84, col 4). He was a soldier. No farm is written; the identification with Siur Bersvendsen Draxen rests on the name pair and the baptism of Kirsten, daughter of Sjur Draxten, in Jun-Jul 1746 (canvas 88), which would be a child conceived before the marriage. Read. ¶3

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
