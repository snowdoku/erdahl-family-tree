Life story

Heir, with her brother Bersvend Sivertsen, in the probate after her father Sivert Bersvendsen of Draxen opened 9 May 1766 (Strinda skifteprotokoll 6 fol. 21; her share about half her brother's, as usual for a daughter); guardian Ole Bersvendsen Draxen. ¶1

Confirmed in Selbu on the 3rd Sunday after Epiphany (24 Jan 1768) as 'Ingebor Siurs dat. Draxen' (clerk's book 16316, no. 52; the pastor's book 16311 has 'Ingeborg Sivertsd.' without farm). Birth year estimated from confirmation age. ¶2

Probably the 'Ingeborg Siursdatter' of Draxen, wife of Jacob Hansen Draxen, whose daughter Marit was baptised in Selbu at Cantate 1783 (16311 c110 right). Identification by name and farm only (inference). ¶3
