Life story

His own children by Rangnil Jonsdatter in that census are Ane Reinaldsdatter 5 and Ole Reinaldssen 2; her daughter by her first marriage, Beret Olsdatter 8, is listed with them. He is entered in the 1825 census of Slinden as 'Renald Tostens., 69, Gmd.' and in the Sovlvskatten of 1816, 1819 and 1821 as 'Reinholdt / Bernholdt Tostensen, Gaardmand, Slinden'. ¶2

Marriage: betrothed 13 April 1794 as 'Soldat Reinholdt Torstensen Slinden' - a soldier, of Slinden - to the widow Ragnhild Jonsdatter Saether, forlovere Sivert Haftorsen Slinden and Peder Bersvendsen Slinden, and married 18 May 1794 in Selbu. 'Reinholdt Slinden' was a godfather at the baptism of a child of Niels Erichsen Aunet (Oeunet) in Advent 1794, Selbu ministerialbok 1757-1801, canvas 172 left. ¶4

He is recorded here because the uncommon name Reinald, given to Beret Olsdatter's fourth child in 1822, is one of the two names that tie her to this household. ¶3

Records that disagree 2

Death and burial (scan): 'Renald Tostensen Slinden', Gaardmand (farmer), aged 72, died 2 August and buried 8 August 1841, Selbu burials 1841 p. 403 row 39; his widow Ragnild Johnsdatter died ten days later. The age gives birth about 1769, as does the 1825 census age (56); the 1801 age of 30 gives about 1771, which is kept. Check: an earlier note here gives his 1825 age as 69, which conflicts with reading of 56 and his burial age; the 1825 census page should be re-read. ¶5

Against: the soldier Torsten Olsen of Slinden and Ane Erichsdatter (m. 1766) also had children in these years (Kari 1767, Guri 1769), lived on his own farm Slinden, and Ane's first husband was Reinald Persen Slinden, so they too could have named a son Reinald. His baptism, about 1769-71 (Selbu ministerialbok 16311, canvases after c39), would decide between the two Torstens. ¶6b

Corrections 1

Correction: an earlier note gives his age in the 1825 census of Slinden as 69 ('Renald Tostens., 69, Gmd.'). The 1825 census page reads 'Renald Tostens.', age 56, Gmd. (farmer), first in the residence list, with his wife Ragnhild Johnsd. 60 and married sons Ole Renaldsen 27 and Tosten Renalds. 22 (from the Digitalarkivet transcription on 13 Oct 2026, agreeing with). The 69 is wrong; 56 gives a birth about 1769, agreeing with his burial age of 72 in 1841 and within two years of his 1801 age of 30. ¶7

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Not an ancestor but the evidence: he is the STEPFATHER who raised Beret Olsdatter, Oline Slind's great-great-grandmother, and the man she named a son for in 1822. The 1801 census of Selbu, rural residence 0083 Slinden, household 7, enters him as 'Reinal Torstenssen, 30, Mand, Hans 1te haendes 2dt aegteskab, I faellesskab med husbonden' - his first marriage and her second, and he worked the farm jointly with the householder, who was his father Torsten Olssen, 72, 'Husbonde, Enkem. efter 1te aegteskab, Bonde og gaardbeboer' in household 6. ¶1
