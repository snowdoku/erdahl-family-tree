About the family

Youngest of the eight children of Peter and Olga Valder, born 1928 and died 2015. His own Find a Grave memorial (#189873262) was not readable when this was compiled; the years are from the linked family list on his parents' and siblings' memorials, so they are recorded as Probable. ¶1

Life story

1930 census: 'Gotfried M.' Valder, 1, son, born Washington, the youngest of the eight children in his parents' household at Ferndale, Washington, on 5 April 1930. Read from the census image. At one year old he was born between April 1928 and April 1929, which agrees with the 1928 of his memorial. ¶3

1940 census (read from the image): 'Gotfred M.' Valder, son, 11, born in Washington, attending school, highest grade completed 5, the youngest in his parents' household at Ferndale, Whatcom County, Washington, in the same house as in 1935. ¶6

1950 census: 'Gotfred M.' Valder, 21, son, never married, born Washington, lived with his parents Peter and Olga in Otter Tail County, Minnesota, and worked 48 hours a week as a sales clerk in a retail grocery. Read from the census image. ¶4

He was the only sibling still living when his brother Palmer died in January 2014: Palmer's obituary names 'brother Fred' among the survivors and lists all six other siblings as having died before him. ¶2

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Confidence moved from Probable to Confirmed: two census images twenty years apart place a son 'Gottfried M.' of Peter and Olga, born in Washington about 1928-29, in the right place in the family, and Fred is the usual short form of Gottfried. His middle initial M fits Martin. The full name Gottfried is inferred from the two census spellings and is not yet on a birth record. ¶5
