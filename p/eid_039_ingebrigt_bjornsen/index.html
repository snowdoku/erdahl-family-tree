About the family

Other children of the same couple in the register: Ole, Cantate Sunday (23 April) 1758; Ingebrigt, 1st Sunday after Trinity 1760; Gunnar, 18th Sunday after Trinity 1762. A Mali Ingebrigtsdatter of Store Evjen buried on Judica Sunday 1759 aged 2 days was probably theirs. There was a second Ingebrigt (Engelbrecht) at Store Evjen - Engelbrecht Engelbrechtsen, died 13 October 1783 aged 73 1/2 - and a stillborn child of 'Ingebricht Storefjen' buried in March 1758, five weeks before Ole's baptism, must be his or another man's, not this couple's. The sons are traceable as adults under the form Engelbrecht: 'Grenadier Olle Engelbrechts. Stor Efjen' was betrothed in 1784 with Thomas Bjornsen of Evjen as sponsor and died on 17 August 1784 aged 27 (born about 1757); 'Gunder Engelbr. Ef[jen]' was confirmed in 1780 and 'Gunder Engelbr. Stor Efjen' was buried in March 1789 aged 26 (born about 1762-63). An 'Engelbrict Efjen' was godfather to Jon's son Ole in 1793 - probably Jon's brother Ingebrigt of 1760 (inference). ¶2

Family (inference only): Bjorn Ingebrigtsen of Store Evjen, buried Jubilate Sunday 1764 aged 78, was probably his father, and Thomas Bjornsen and Halvor Bjornsen, both baptising children at Store Evjen 1758-65, probably his brothers. A 'Siur Bjornsen' was godfather at Jon's baptism. None of this is proved. His own birth and death are not found. ¶4

Life story

Baptism of his son: '(3) Jon. Forældr: Ingebricht Biörns: Stor Efjen, Mali Olsd: Faddr: Siur Biörns: Thomas Jons: Guri Haarsd[?]' - Jon, parents Ingebrigt Bjornsen of Store Evjen and Mali Olsdatter, baptised on the day of the Eucharist (13 January) 1765. Selbu ministerialbok 1757-1801, canvas 17 left page and the crop. ¶1

Baptism (Probable identification): Laetare Sunday, 11 March 1725, 'Døbt Biørn Efjens barn K[aldet]: Ingebrick' (Selbu ministerialbok 1699-1737, canvas 99 left page, crop); his mother was churched on Cantate Sunday, 29 April 1725, as 'Biørn Stor Efjens qvinde' (canvas 100 left). Father: Bjørn Ingebrigtsen of Store Evjen (see his page), buried 13 May 1764 aged 78. This replaces the earlier note that his birth was not found; it is the only Bjørn Evjen baptism in read of 1724-1726. Born 1725, he was about 33 at his first known child's baptism in 1758. ¶5

1762 extra tax list: at holding 681 'Storesien', Selbu, in the household of his father Biørn Ingebrigtsen and Brynild Halvorsdatter, 'Ingebrigt gift med Mali Olsdatter, deris datter Kari', with a brother Halvor married to Brit Olsdatter. His mother is still unproved; Brynild Halvorsdatter is a candidate (see her record). ¶7

Bygdebok (Selbu i fortid og naatid vol. 5, Stor-Evjen chapter, printed p. 182; nb.no word boxes only): '1764-1801 Ingebrikt Bjørnsen,... 1725,... 1805.... 1751... Mali' - holder of Stor-Evjen 1764-1801, born 1725, died 1805; the 1751 beside Mali is probably their marriage year (inferred, unqueried words). The birth year agrees with his baptism of 11 March 1725. Death in 1805 rests on the book alone (Probable); his burial has not been read. ¶6a

Widower in the probate after his wife Mali Olsdatter, Stor-Evjen, 14 Apr 1794 ('Enkemanden Ingebrigt Biørnsen'), which lists their children Ole (dead), Jon, Karen, Ranni, Brynnild and Ingeborg (24763 fol. 537b, view 553). ¶9

Probable death: indexed Selbu burials 1807, p. 84: 'Ingebrict Biørnsen', widower, residence Stor Efjen, born 1722, aged 85, died 12 Feb [1807], buried 2nd Sunday in Lent. In the 1801 census he is 'Ingebrigt Biørnssen', b. 1726, at Storevjen, the head's father, a widower after his first marriage. Identification by name, farm and age. ¶8

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Why the link to Jon Ingebrigtssen (see his page) is Confirmed: Jon lived at Store Evjen at his marriage in 1789 and in 1801, the farm named at this baptism; he is the only Jon son of an Ingebrigt in complete read of January 1757 - February 1767 (the other Store Evjen Jon, 1760, is a son of Halvor Bjornsen); and his son Ingebrigt (1799) carries this man's name. His 1801 age of 37 is one year more than a January 1765 baptism gives, a usual census error. ¶3
