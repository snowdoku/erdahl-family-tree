About the family

Wife of Jens Andersen on gn. 65, Frosta, previously married to a Gunder. Probable mother of Anne Jensdatter (see her page), graded on that link. ¶1

Life story

Probable only, from Frostaboka VIII p. 516 (compiled source, word-box rebuild). Supported by the 1765 probate of Anne's full sister Mali Jensdatter (24755 fol. 495a, scan read), whose heirs include a half-brother Gunder Gundersen: a half-sibling with the patronymic Gundersen means a shared mother who had an earlier husband Gunder, as the bygdebok says. No record names Ingeborg as Anne's mother. ¶2a

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
