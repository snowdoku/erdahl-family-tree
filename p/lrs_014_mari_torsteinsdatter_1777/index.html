About the family

Children: Lasse (baptised 20 May 1803), Torstein (1804), Else (baptised 2 Jan 1806) and Mons (1808), all at Indre Oppedal; each baptism names her as 'Mari Torsteins Dr'. She was also a godmother at Indre Oppedal in 1805. Naming her second son Torstein follows the custom of naming a son after the mother's father. ¶3

Parents: Probable, not proved. The 1802 marriage places her at Engesætter, and the 1801 census there lists Mari Torsteinsdatter, 24, as a daughter of Torstein Iverssen and Sigrid Larsdatter. No record yet names her father in adulthood. An earlier guess that she belonged to Torstein Olssen's family at Indre Oppedal is weaker, because that household had no Mari in 1801. Her baptism about 1777 in the Gulen book (1776-1787), naming her parents and farm, or her burial with age, would confirm it. ¶4

Parents (update): The baptism names her father, Torsten Iversen of Engesetter, matching the 1801 householder Torstein Iverssen of Engesætter, so the father link now rests on a primary record. Her mother is still identified only by the 1801 census (Sigrid Larsdatter, his wife, with Mari as their 'felleds datter'); this register does not name mothers in 1776-1780. ¶7

Life story

Baptism: Baptised 27 Mar 1778 at Brekke church as 'Torsten Iwersen Engesetters D[atter] Nom: Mari', the daughter of Torsten Iversen of Engesetter. Godparents (testes): Mons Dalgreen; Iver Guttormsen of Engesetter; Halvor Olsen of Verkland(?); Brithe Torkildsdatter and Agatha Iversdatter, both of Engesetter. The mother is not named; her churching on 18 Apr 1778 calls her only 'Torsten Engesetters Hustrue' (wife). Gulen ministerialbok 1776-1787, folio 15a. ¶5

1801 census: Mari Torsteinsdatter, 24, unmarried, is a joint daughter (felleds datter, a child of both spouses) in the household of Torstein Iverssen, 67, skattebonde (tax-paying farmer), and Sigrid Larsdatter, 64, at Engesætter, Brekke. Birth about 1777 is inferred from this age. She is the only Mari Torsteinsdatter among the 536 people in the 1801 Brekke district. ¶1

Marriage: Engaged 6 Jun 1802 and married 27 Jun 1802 at Brekke church to Torkild Monssøn of Indre Oppedal; she was then of Engesetter (Gulen church book 1798-1808, marriage index). ¶2

Widowhood: at the probate after her husband Torkild Monsen of Indre Oppedal, 25[?] Mar 1809, she is 'den afdødes efterladte Enke Mari Tostensdatter' (the deceased's surviving widow), with her father-in-law Mons Lassesen as her lauværge (legal spokesman). She was appointed guardian of her daughter Else, 3, and signed 'med paaholden Pen' (with a held pen; she could not write). Iver Tostensen of Engesætter, probably her brother, was guardian of her youngest son Mons. ¶8

1812: the probate after her mother-in-law Else Torkildsdatter, closed 2 Jun 1812, again names her as Torkild Monsen's wife and their four children Lasse 9, Tosten 8, Else 6 and Mons 4 as heirs. ¶9
