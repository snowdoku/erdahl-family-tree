About the family

Her parents, Simon Eberle (20 August 1882 - 7 November 1931) and Mary Hildebrandt (3 March 1884 - 5 April 1933), both died at Eatonia, Saskatchewan, and are buried in Eatonia Cemetery, as are her husband's parents. A Find a Grave biography of her youngest sister Elsie Kelley says that both parents were born in Bessarabia. A 1959 obituary abstract for her sister Frieda Eberle names among the surviving sisters 'Mrs. Jack Knodel, Oliver' and 'Mrs. Gus Knodel, Dawson Creek, BC'. ¶3

Parents: Simon Eberle and Maria Hildebrandt, married at Pfeilsdorf in February 1903 (Standesamt Pfeilsdorf 1903 no. 5); she came to Canada with them aged about three on the Prinz Adalbert, Quebec 30 Oct 1910 ('Emilie'). ¶6

Life story

Birth: Born at Płużnica (Pfeilsdorf) at 1 p.m. on 23 October 1907, daughter of Szymon (Simon) Eberle and Maria née Hildebrandt, both Evangelical; registered the same day as no. 64 by her father, who signed. Her BC death record gives the same date and parents but 'Romania' as birthplace - the family's earlier homeland, not hers. The Eberles were fellow settlers of the Knodels at Pfeilsdorf: Simon Eberle registered a son Daniel there, born 1 October 1903 (1903 no. 49), so the two families had known each other since before 1905. ¶4

Her younger sister Sarah Marie Eberle (born 1916 at Mendham, Saskatchewan) married Gottlieb's brother Jacob Knodel. ¶2

1931 census: 'Amelia' Knodel, 23, wife of Gottlieb jr, born Germany, father born Rumania, mother Russia, immigrated 1911, naturalised 1915 (through her father). Her parents' household on the same page-run gives Simon Eberle's immigration as 1910 and naturalisation 1913. ¶5

Death, BC 1984-09-021226: Emilia Knodel, housewife, of 439 Winnipeg Street, Penticton, married to Gottlieb Knodel; born 23 October 1907 in Romania; father Simon Eberle and mother Maria Hilderbrand [Hildebrand], both born in Romania. She was cremated at Penticton. The informant was her daughter. She was known as Millie. ¶1
