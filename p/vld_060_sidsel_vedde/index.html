Life story

Churchings: Sidsel, wife (the index's ektemann row names her husband Ole) at Vedde, Borgund, was churched (introdusert, a mother's first return to church after a birth) on 16 Dec 1703, six weeks after Zidzel was baptised to Ole Endress. of Vedde on 4 Nov 1703. As 'Sidtzel', wife of Ole at Vedde, she was churched again on 26 May 1709, six weeks after Henning was baptised to Ole Endress. of Vedde on 14 Apr 1709. The index gives her no patronymic or age. From the index; the scans have not been read. ¶1

Baptism read on the scan: the 4 Nov 1703 baptism of Zidzel, daughter of Ole Endresen of Vedde, does not name the mother; the register names only fathers. The churching of 16 Dec 1703 is not on that page or the next (p. 169, baptisms to Feb 1704); churchings are kept in another part of the book, not yet located. Her link stays index-based and Probable. ¶3

Probate (scan read): 'Anno 1713 d. 17 Juny' a probate (skifte og deling) was held after the late Ole Endresen Wedde in Borgund skipreide, between his widow (efterladte hustru) 'Sidzelle Hans datter', with her guardian Erich Pedersen [Bruunvagen?], on one side, and 'deris sammen auflede børn' (their jointly begotten children) on the other: Hans Olsen aged 13, Henning Olsen aged [2½?], Ole Olsen aged [4?] (unit unclear), [Linde/Brita?] Olsdatter aged 17, and 'Zidselle Ols datter gammel 10 aar' (Sidsel Olsdatter, aged 10). How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Confidence is Probable: she is taken as the mother of Sidsele Olsdatter because the churching six weeks after the 1703 baptism names a wife Sidsel and a husband Ole at Vedde, and no other Ole is indexed at Vedde then. Her daughter bearing her own name is unusual but happens; a Sidsel born about 1630 was buried at Wedde on 11 Jun 1702, so the name may instead honour a grandmother (unproved). ¶2a

Upgrade and new patronymic: the 1713 probate after Ole Endresen of Vedde calls his widow 'Sidzelle Hans datter' and calls the heirs 'deris sammen auflede børn', the couple's joint children, including Zidselle Olsdatter aged 10. This makes her the mother of the Zidzel baptised 4 Nov 1703, as the churchings had suggested, and shows her father was named Hans. The son Hans Olsen (aged 13) may carry her father's name (inference). Her own baptism, remarriage and burial are not traced. ¶5

Notes on method and coordination

Coordinator grade fix (1 Nov 2026), graded on the link to Sidsele Olsdatter: lowered to Probable. The 1713 Vedde probate proves this couple had a daughter Zidselle, aged 10. Her identity with Sidsele Olsdatter Hessen (married 1731, said to be born at Vedde) crosses farms and nearly two decades, with no record that names the relationship. That is the same reasoning that kept Sidsele at Probable (AUDIT-1, AUDIT-2, re-proof pass). ¶6
