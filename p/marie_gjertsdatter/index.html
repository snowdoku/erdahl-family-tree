Life story

1801 census, Balholmen: 'Mari Giertsdatter', 23, unmarried, listed among the children of Giert Nielsen Falch (68, innkeeper, married twice) and Zithone Samuelsdatter (48, first marriage). On 5 Jul 1802 'Marie Giertsdtr. Falch, pige' married Jon Biørnsen, bachelor of Gjerde. Her daughter Gjertrud was named after Giert. Lokalhistoriewiki gives her life as 1778-1860. ¶2

Probate: in the probate of her father Giert Nielsen Falck at Balholmen, registered 9 October 1805 (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll A 12, fol. 510b-511a, No. 11), she is 'Myndig tredie Datter Marie Giertsdatter, gift med Giestgiver John Biörnsen Ballholmen' (third daughter, of age, married to the innkeeper John Biørnsen of Balholmen), one of the 7 children of Giert and his widow Citrone (Zithone) Samuelsdatter in lawful marriage. Her husband was made guardian of her under-age sister Guri. ¶3

John's wife in 1815; would have been about 46 at Gjertrud's birth, so motherhood is probable rather than confirmed. ¶1
