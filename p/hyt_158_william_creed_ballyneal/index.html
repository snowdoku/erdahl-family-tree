Life story

William Creed of Ballyneal (Ballyneale, in the parish of Ballingarry), Co. Limerick. Known from his widow's death notice: Faulkner's Dublin Journal, 15 May 1762, 'Died in Limerick after a tedious illness, Mrs Mary Creed relict of William Creed late of Ballyneal, Co. Limerick'. He was therefore dead before her burial on 8 May 1762. The Blennerhassett Pedigrees name his wife as Mary Bennett, with daughters Anne, Jane and Sarah by 1733, and the Ballingarry parish register has William and Mary Creed baptising Ann on 26 July 1733 and Jane on 23 September 1735 (all as abstracted in Noel Murphy's Creed family notes; the originals have not been read). ¶1

Place name: the pedigree's 'Ballindall' is read by Noel Murphy as Ballyneal(e), Ballingarry parish, the residence given in the 1762 death notice of his widow. The Ballingarry baptisms of Ann (1733) and Jane (1735), daughters of William and Mary Creed, match two of the three daughters the pedigree names. ¶6a

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

3 on the grade · 2 searches with no result · 1 open question

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 3

Speculative as the father of Captain William Creed (d. Charlottetown 1809), who was a 'native of County Limerick' (Limerick Chronicle, 21 June 1809) and who, by family tradition, crossed the Atlantic with an uncle named Bennett and named his son Joseph Bennett Creed. No record names Captain William as his son. Added. ¶2

Upgrade (Speculative to Probable), graded on his link to William Creed: his wife Mary was the eldest daughter of Samuel Bennett junior of Ballincollow and Anne Collis. The Blennerhassett Pedigree of about 1733-36 calls him 'William Creed of Ballindall in the County Lymerick' (Hickson, Old Kerry Records, 1872, p. 56). Her brother Joseph Bennett is identified with the Rev. Joseph Bennett of Windsor, Nova Scotia. In 1765 that Joseph mortgaged Cork property and his share of Ballincollow to 'William Creed, then late of Limerick city' (Registry of Deeds memorial 160893), three years after William's widow died in Limerick city. Together with the captain's tradition of a Bennett uncle and his son's name, Joseph Bennett Creed, this makes William of Ballyneal the probable father. The relationship is inferred and no record names the captain as his son (see the notes on Mary Bennett for the full argument and the 'aged 80' question). This supersedes the 'Speculative' and 'Grade unchanged (Speculative)' statements in the earlier notes. ¶5

Audit 11 (2 Nov 2026): Probable kept, at the low end, on the same reasoning as his wife (see the audit 11 note on Mary Bennett). Reading the pedigree's 'Ballindall' as Ballyneal is supported by the Ballingarry baptisms (Ann 1733, Jane 1735; Murphy's abstract) and by the Bennett settlement lands lying in Ballingarry parish (memorial 420, image). That is acceptable for identifying the couple. It does not bear on his link to the captain, which rests on the Bennett-uncle convergence. ¶7

Searches that found nothing 2

Searches for a will or deed of William Creed of Ballyneal, all negative: (1) Phillimore and Thrift, Indexes to Irish Wills, vol. 3, Diocese of Limerick 1615-1800 (the diocese that includes Ballingarry): the only Creed testators are John Creed senior, gent., of Uregare, and John Creed, gent., of Uregare; there is no William. (2) The Registry of Deeds Index Project (irishdeedsindex.net, about 700,000 index entries, searched 1700-1830 for the surname Creed, 131 entries): no Creed of Ballyneal(e) in any deed, and the residence search 'Ballyneal' finds only places in Tipperary, Waterford, Kilkenny, Meath and Derry. So the 1762 newspaper notice and the Ballingarry baptisms (per Noel Murphy) remain the only records of him. A rival William is excluded: William Creed of Ballygrennan, son of Francis Creed of Ballygrennan (a life in a lease of 1769, executor in 1796 and 1801), died at Ballygrennan in March 1797 (Clare Journal, per Murphy), so he is not the captain who died in Charlottetown in 1809. Grade unchanged (Speculative). ¶3

FamilySearch searches (2 Nov 2026), all negative for this couple: (1) historical records, surname Creed (exact), Co. Limerick and Ireland 1690-1800: only Catholic-register baptisms from 1746 and the Diocesan and Prerogative Wills index (John Creed sen. 1712, John 1737, Francis 1747); no William Creed of Ballyneal will or administration indexed; (2) Full-Text (Registry of Deeds memorials): 'Creed' with Ballyneal / Ballyneale / Ballineale / Ballindall / Ballingarry gave no 18th-century hit (the full-text deed coverage found is mostly 1800+). The Ballingarry Church of Ireland register (S0220) was not reached. Still Speculative. ¶4
