Life story

Probate (skifte no. 154): '[1728] Novembris 15de blev paa Gaarden Søfftestad Nissedals annex og Hvideseid præstegield' the sheriff Hans Ellefsen [Lung?] with the two men Andbiørn [..] and Nottov Grimstvedt registered and valued the estate 'efter afgangne Aslouge Salvesdaatter' and divided it between 'hendes efterladte mand Tallef Gundsteensøn og deris tilsammen avlede børn, Nafnlig: Ole Tallefsøn 9½ aar, Salve Tallefsøn 7 aar gammel, Ole Tallefsøn den yngre 5 aar gammel, Torgier Tallefsøn 2 aar gammel, samt Sigre Tallefsdaatter 12 aar gammel' (her surviving husband Tallef Gundstensen and the children they had together: Ole 9½, Salve 7, Ole the younger 5, Torgeir 2, Sigri 12). 3 on the grade

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 3

Confidence: Probable as parent of Ole Søftestad (see his page), Ole Tellevsen Søftestad, buried 28 Jun 1799 aged 80 (born about 1719). The 1728 probate names an elder son Ole Tallefsen, 9½ in Nov 1728 (born about spring 1719), on the same farm; Ole's burial age fits it to the year, his patronymic Tallefsen/Tellevsen is attested in 1760 and 1799, and he named his son Tallef (b. 1760). Either Ole of the probate would be a son of this couple (both are listed as 'deris tilsammen avlede børn'), but the age points to the elder. Not Confirmed because the identification spans 1728 to 1760 (32 years) with no record in between naming Ole's father. ¶2

Upgrade: graded on her link to Ole Søftestad (see his page). Her widower's probate, Nordbø in Nissedal, 23 Oct 1755 (25673 No 154, views 272-273), names the children 'med første Hustrue Aslaug(?) Saavesdaatter avlede': Ole Tallevsøn (myndig, at home), Saave and Ole Tallevsønner (in Holland), Torgiej Tallevsøn and Sigri Tallevsdaatter, the same set as her own 1728 probate. The eldest son Ole is followed to Ole Tallefsen of Søftestad (1754 land purchase, 1760 baptism of son Tallef, buried 1799 aged 80) with short gaps and no rival (the younger Ole is abroad in 1755 and too young for the burial age). This replaces the earlier 'Confidence: Probable' note. ¶4

Audit 12 (2 Nov 2026): Confirmed KEPT, graded on her link to Ole Søftestad (see his page). She is named as the mother twice: 'deris tilsammen avlede børn' in her own 1728 probate, and 'med første Hustrue Aslaug(?) Saavesdaatter avlede Børn' in her widower's 1755 probate. Either Ole of 1728 is her son. The raise stands, but its continuity paragraph should be read with this rival check. The 1755 probate after Tallev Günstensøn (25673 No 154) names 'Ole Tallevsøn Myndig' without a residence, and the father's estate was on a plass (Holt?) registered at Nordbø, not on Søftestad. So the step to Ole Tallefsen of Søftestad rests on more than the farm. Named rivals: the two other Tallevs of Søftestad, Tallev Gunsteensen the younger (b. about 1714, of Grøven in Brunkeberg by 1754) and his half-brother Tallev Bjørgulvsen Søftestad (b. after about 1715). Neither could have had a son of age to buy Søftestad land by deed of 7 May 1754, and neither fits the burial age (80 in 1799). Inside the gap the family is also seen around Ole: at his son Tallef's baptism in 1760 the godmothers include 'Sigri Søftestad' and 'Aslaug Holt', the names of his sister Sigri and half-sister Aaslaug of the 1755 probate (whose father's estate lay at Holt). The burial age is not the only thing that excludes the rivals, so audit 8 rule 4 is met. Gaps 1754-1755-1760 are short. Test record (for her own parents): a Salve of Nissedal or Kviteseid whose probate (Vest-Telemark, before 1728) names a daughter Aslaug married to Tallef Gunstensen. ¶5
