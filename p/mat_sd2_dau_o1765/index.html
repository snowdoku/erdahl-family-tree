About the family

Youngest child of Ola Jornsen and Marit Pedersdatter, given name not recovered. Selbu i fortid og naatid vol. 7, p. 116: '[N.], f. 1765, g.m. KRISTOFFER OLSEN [N.], se...' Quite possibly the 'Marit Olsdatter, 36, Hans kone' who in the 1801 census is the wife of the cottager Kristoffer Olssen in household 5 at Slinden - the right husband's name on the right farm, and an age implying 1765 - but the census does not name her father and the identification is not made. ¶1

Life story

A brother or sister of Jorn (Jorgen) Olssen Slind, born 1752, David Erdahl's maternal fifth-great-grandfather. ¶2a

Named from her father's probate, Slinde, 17 Oct 1772 (24757 fol. 530): '7. Datteren Maren Olsdatter 7 aar' (born about 1765), the youngest child. ¶3

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
