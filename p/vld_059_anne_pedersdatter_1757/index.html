About the family

Mother, inferred: Probable mother of Anne Pedersdatter (see her notes). ¶2

Parents entered from the Lesja bygdebok (vol. 1, nb.no item 8e238b3c93fae7ff68ee91598c918b4d, canvas 183, chapter Einbu, word-box rebuild): Peder Pedersen of Eneboe (ca. 1727-77; Probable) and his second wife Guri (b. 1730; Speculative, see the 'Barbara' contradiction on her record), whose children include 'Anne, 1757, sjå... her (21-1)', entry 21-1 being 'Peder Engebrets. frå Sør-Einbu (13-3), 1753-1804, g. 1783 m. Anne Pedersd.... 1757-?' (canvas 205). This makes the 4 Mar 1757 baptism of 'Peder Eneboes Anna' (9236 c209) hers. Her father was a brother of Anne Pedersdatter of Slettum, the mother of Kari Rolvsdatter, so the two Lesja lines meet in Peder Hansen of Nordre Eneboe. ¶5

Life story

The 1757 page (9236 canvas 209) re-read: the churching first read as Peder Eneboe's wife 'Barbara' is in fact 'Peder Eners. Qvinde Barbara' (another man); Peder Eneboe's wife Guri was churched at Palmarum 1757. So baby Anna (b. 4 Mar 1757) was the daughter of Peder Eneboe and Guri, matching the bygdebok family. Her own grade (link to her child) is unchanged. ¶6

Confirmed at Lesja church on the 19th Sunday after Trinity 1775 as 'Anne Pedersd. Eneboe', aged 18 (9236 p. 47, canvas 25, scan read). This bridges her 1757 baptism at Eneboe to her 1783 marriage from Kiønsletten under Eneboe, and her parents are raised to Confirmed on it. Her own grade (her link to her child Anne Pedersdatter) is unchanged. ¶7

Marriage (scan read): Lesja ministerialbok 1777-1819 (0512P, source 9237, canvas 84), p. 169, 8th Sunday after Trinity 1783, Verket (Lesjaverk) annex: 'Copul. Soldat Peder Ingebrigtss. Søreneboe og Pige Ane Pedersd. Kiønsletten' - the soldier Peder Ingebrigtsen of Søreneboe and the maiden Ane Pedersdatter of Kiønsletten. No sureties, ages or fathers are written. The index gives the wedding as 10 Aug 1783 (pv00000001981136) and an engagement on 15 Jun 1783 with the bride 'of Eneboe' (pv00000001983480; the scan linked to that index row is a 1795 page, so the engagement entry itself was not read). This is the marriage of the couple of the 1801 Eeneboe household; their children Gurri (bapt. 1 Jan 1784, Sør Eneboe, father 'Peder Engebrigts.'), Peder (bapt. 1 Jan 1786, mother Anne Pedersd., Søreneboe, pd00000009245548) follow it. The bride's home Kiønsletten, and the farm-name pattern (a 'Torger Siversen Kiønsletten' married on the same day), are leads for her own parents, whose baptisms (c.1753-57) fall in the unindexed Lesja book of 1732-1776. ¶3

1801 census (scan read): Anne Pedersdatter, 44, 'Hans Kone' (his wife), married once, in the household of Peder Ingebretsen at Eeneboe, Verket annex, Lesja. Her birth year about 1757 is inferred from that age. She is written 'Ane Pedersd.' at the 1784 baptism of her daughter Gurri and 'Anne Pedersd.' (residence Søreneboe) at the 1786 baptism of her son Peder. ¶1

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 alternative set aside · 1 open question

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Baptism lead, not entered: Lesja ministerialbok 1732-1776 (source 9236), p. 433 (canvas 209), Dom. Oculi [13 Mar] 1757 at Verket: 'Peder Eneboes B[arn]: Anna, fød d. 4 Martii', godparents Siver Molmen, Eric Sangroe, Gurri Molmen, Marit Kiønsletten, Kari Enstad; the churching of Peder Eneboe's wife Barbara is entered on the same page (scan read). The date fits Anne's age (b. about 1757), a godmother came from Kiønsletten, where Anne lived at her 1783 marriage, and the family was at Eeneboe in 1801. But the father here is 'Peder Eneboe', not 'Peder of Kiønsletten', and Lesja had several Peders, so this is a candidate only. Canvases 208-209 (Jan - Mar 1757) were read; no Anne daughter of a Peder of Kiønsletten seen there. ¶4a
