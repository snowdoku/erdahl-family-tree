Life story

Surname uncertain: index 'Fessels', possibly 'Feyels' (a Friederich x Margaretha Feyel baptised a child at Enzweihingen on 18 Nov 1762, record 4608559). Father of Jacobina Friederike Fessel (see her page), first wife of Christoph Ludwig Knodel. The bride row of their marriage at Vaihingen an der Enz on 25 Jan 1791 reads 'Jacobina Friederika Fessels', relatives 'Friedrich, Christoph Ludwig' (first relative = bride's father, last = groom, by this index's order). Probable: the roles are not labelled in the index and the image (1184562-00936) is not read. Probably of Vaihingen. Graded on his link to Jacobina Friederike. ¶1

Corrections 2

Correction: 'Friedrich Fessels' in the 1791 marriage row is very probably Jacobina Friederike's LATE HUSBAND, not her father. FamilySearch's labelled index (QPGK-ZG18, Vaihingen, 25 Jan 1791, register page 144) does put 'Friedrich Fessels' in the Father field (person QPGK-ZG1P). But: (1) FamilySearch QPG2-WGRS/QPG2-WGRM, Jacobina Wilhelmina Fressel, born 7 Oct 1788 at Vaihingen (page 462), parents Christoph Friedrich Fressel x Jacobina Friederika, shows a Jacobina Friederika married to a Christoph Friedrich Fressel 2.5 years before the Knodel marriage. (2) Two later records of her son Johann Christoph Knodel give her maiden name as Rampacher: his marriage at Vaihingen on 2 Jul 1822 (QPGK-RPRG, page 70, mother's surname indexed 'Rampacher') and his burial there on 12 Aug 1854 (QPG2-WR7B, 'Jakobine Friedrike Ramsacher'). (3) The FS Family Tree attaches the 1791 bride to 'Jacobina Friedrika Rempacher' (PC7H-YNP). The likely reading of the register is 'Jacobina Friederika, Friedrich Fessels [Fressels] hinterlassene Witwe', which the indexers took as a father. Lowered to Speculative; this person should probably be withdrawn as her father once the 1791 image (Ancestry 1184562-00936) is read. Graded on his link to Jacobina Friederike. ¶5

Correction: not her father. The 4 Jul 1786 Vaihingen marriage rows show that Jacobina Friederika was born Rampacher (daughter of Jacob Friedrich) and that her first husband was Johann Christoph Fußel/Fessel, son of Johann Friedrich (groom row 4787916, bride row 904787916). 'Friedrich Fessel' of the 1791 row is therefore her first husband's father (Johann Friedrich Fessel) or a reading of the husband's own name ('Christoph Friedrich' Fressel in 1788, record 4785060). He is no longer her father in the tree; her father is now Jacob Friedrich Rampacher. This person can be dropped or kept as kin only. ¶6
