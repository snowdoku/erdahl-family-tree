About the family

Her family, from the same probate: a guardian for her daughter Mali was 'hendes Morbroder Ole Olsen M[ok?]nes[?]', her mother's brother, so Helge had a brother Ole Olsen. The farm word is hard to read; it may be Moksnes (Moxnes) in Selbu, which would point to her birthplace. Her 1740 marriage entry gave her farm as 'Stødren[?]'. ¶7a

Life story

Married 12 Jun 1740 (Trinity Sunday) to the soldier Haldvor Jansen, as 'pigen Helge Ols datter Stødren[?]' (16315 c3) - Probable identification; her farm of origin may be 'Stødren' (reading uncertain). ¶3

Lensmann Ole Thomassen took part in the probate, and the estate was small; Halvor's goods had been seized in 1761 (see Halvor Jonsen Velve). ¶8

1762 extra tax list: head of holding 797 Velve, Selbu (landlord Thomas Angell), as 'Helge[?] Olsdatter', followed by a mark that probably stands for enke (widow), with her children Jon Halvorsen and Mali Halvorsdatter. Her first name is uncertain in the reading; Helge (Helga) is the best reading. ¶1

Burial, 3rd Sunday in Advent (16 Dec) 1764: 'Helge Olsd: Vælve gl: 53 aar' - aged 53, so born about 1711 (16311 canvas 17 left on a zoom). ¶4

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Why Probable as mother of Jon Halvorsen Velve (see his page): he is the only Jon Halvorsen at Velve, his age (58 in 1801, so born about 1743) fits a child over 12 in 1762, and he married and stayed at Velve. ¶2a

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed. Her probate, Skifteprotokoll 5 (24755) fol. 451a, reads: 'Anno 1764 den 17de Decembr. blev Skifte holdt paa gaarden Welve i Selboe efter afg. Helge Ols Datter, som til Arvinger haver efterladt sig en Søn og en Datter, Søn Jon Halvorsen 20 aar gl., D. Mallie Halvors Datter 16 aar'. The typed register of the volume calls her 'Helge Olsdatter, avd. Halvors hustru', Velve, 1764. This is a primary record naming her as the mother of Jon Halvorsen (b. about 1744) at Velve, the direct ancestor Jon Halvorsen Velve, and it matches her burial (16 Dec 1764, aged 53) and the 1762 extra tax (widow at Velve with Jon and Mali Halvorsdatter). ¶6

Open questions and the next record to read 3

Would confirm: a probate (skifte) after her or after her husband Halvor, or a better reading of the 797 head cell. ¶2b

Biography gaps: her exact birth date and baptism (about 1711 from the 1764 burial age of 53) are not found, and her baptism is not in 16310 (read in full); the next step is a full-resolution re-read of the 1740 marriage for her farm, then that farm's baptisms in 16310 c40-47, or the Velve bygdebok vol. 5 pp. 84-105 (Norway-only). Her confirmation is not recorded, and no probate for her or her husband Halvor has been read (Stjor- og Verdal 24753, unindexed). ¶5

Next: Moksnes baptisms about 1711 in 16310, and Ole Olsen Moksnes in the 1762 extra tax. ¶7b
