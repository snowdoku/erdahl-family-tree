About the family

Mother now proved: the skifte after Kirsten Joensdatter, øvre Frontvedt, 11 Nov 1765 (DA 25674 No. 695, view 575) lists 'Targei Asgiersøn myndig' first among the children of her and her widower Asger Targeisøn. As he was of age in 1765, he was born before about 1740. ¶7

Life story

NEW, Speculative father of Birgit Targiesdatter Frontved (see her page); graded on that link. Birgit (b. about 1769-70, 'pige Birgit Targiesdatter Frontved' at her betrothal 10 Apr 1791) gives only the patronymic and the farm. 'Targie Asskiersen Frontved' is a cautioner at Kviteseid/Nissedal betrothals in 1778-80 (notes on Birgit). He is 'Tarje Asgiersen', one of the heirs who received land in Frontved at the skifte after Asgier Tarjesen begun 26 Oct 1781 (Panteregister I 1 fol. 299a, DA 10915 view 342). He is the only Targie known on Frontved in these years, but no record yet names him as Birgit's father, and the farm is not proved to have had only one Targie (in 1801 'Trontvedt' had adult Targiesen children Nottov 38, Jørgen 30, Ole 28 and Else 25, possibly her siblings). So Speculative. ¶1

Father now proved. Skifte after Asgier Tarjesen, øvre Frontvedt, begun 26 Oct 1781 (DA 25677 No. 448, view 229) names 'Tarje Asgiersen forlængst myndig' first among 'hans efterlevende Børn', guardian for his brother Joen; Tarje declared he had bought part of Frontved from 'hans Fader Asgier Tarjesen' at the 11 Nov 1765 skifte after Asgier's first wife Kirsten Jonsdatter. Siblings: Joen, Lars, Hans, Knud, Jon the younger. His own grade (his link to his daughter Birgit) is unchanged by this: it rests on her 1769 baptism ('Targie Frontveds daatter Birgit') and the 22-year gap to her 1791 betrothal. Note: the outlying farm Store Mule belonged to the family; Birgit's 1791 cautioner was Torjus Mule, which fits but proves nothing. ¶6

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: Raised Speculative to Probable, graded on his link to his daughter Birgit (see her page). Record: her baptism, Nissedal church, Aug 1769 (Kviteseid ministerialbok 7981, p.120, canvas 53): 'Targie Frontveds daatter Birgit'. This names a Targie of Frontved as the father of a Birgit born in 1769, matching Birgit Targiesdatter Frontved, who married in 1791 and was buried at 29 in Feb 1799. Why still not Confirmed: (1) 22 years pass between the baptism and her 1791 betrothal with no record of her between them (the audit's short-gap limit is about 15 years); (2) the baptism gives 'Targie Frontved' with no patronymic, so his identity with Targie Asgiersen Frontved (a cautioner 1778-80 and heir at the 26 Oct 1781 skifte after Asgier Tarjesen) rests on name and farm only. No other Targie on Frontved has been found in 1769-91. A godmother, Hæge Frontvedt, may be kin (his wife or sister); this is not proved. The 1801 Targiesen adults at Trontvedt (Nottov 38, Jørgen 30, Ole 28, Else 25) are probably her siblings; their baptisms (c.1763, 1771, 1773, 1776) may give him a patronymic. ¶3

Searches that found nothing 2

Searched without success: the 1781 Asgier Tarjesen skifte in skifteprotokoll 13 (DA 25677). That volume is not chronological. Entries are numbered and written up when the skifte closed, and fol. 1 continues a skifte begun 6 Jul 1781. Landmarks: No. 371 at fol. 7b; No. 374 (begun 25 Oct 1779) at fol. 15b; No. 379 (9 Feb 1782) at fol. 26a; 18 Feb 1782 (Kvaleberg, Moland) at fols 38a-40a; No. 383 (4 Mar 1782) at fol. 40b; No. 397 (16 Mar 1782) at fol. 76a; No. 414 (29 Apr 1782) at fol. 116b. View n = fol. (n-4)b/(n-3)a; the volume runs past view 480. The panteregister's closing date, read earlier as '19 Febr.', looks more like '19de Julii' on re-reading (10915 view 342). Fols 26-44 (Feb-Mar 1782) were read and hold no Frontved entry, so the closing was probably 19 Jul 1782, around fols 160-230 (views 165-235). Views 190 and 210 (fols 185b-186a, 205b-206a) were opened, but no headings could be read on them at low resolution. ¶4

Frontved probates read, neither is his family. (1) No. 95, 11 Sep 1754 (DA 25673 s. 186a, view 191): after Tødne(?) Kittilsdatter, between the widower Lars Pedersen and their daughter Hage Larsdatter, 5. (2) No. 269, 24 Mar 1779 (DA 25676 s. 431b, view 439): after Inger(i) Jonsdatter, wife of Lars Pedersen Frontvedt; children Peder, Asse(?), Anne and Guro Larsdatter. So a second household (Lars Pedersen's) lived on Frontved; the 1769 godmother 'Hæge Frontvedt' may be Hage Larsdatter (b. about 1749), but nothing ties her to Targie. The 25676 register (Nos. 84-306, 1776-80) has no other Frontved entry. Asgier Tarjesen's 1781 skifte in DA 25677 is still unread: views 165-180 hold Nos. 433-436 (Mar 1781-Mar 1782; No. 436, Fiene in Nissedal, begins at fol. 174b), and views 195 and 210 are long non-Nissedal estates; next try views 181-194 and 196-235. ¶5

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Would settle: his own skifte (he was apparently dead before 1801), or Birgit's baptism about 1769-70 (Kviteseid 7981 baptisms near canvases 51-57), which would name the father in full. The 1781 skifte after Asgier Tarjesen is in Øvre Telemark skifteprotokoll 13 (DA 25677, 1781-84); its date (26 Oct 1781) is now known. ¶2
