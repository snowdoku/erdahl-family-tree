About the family

Son of Ole Arntsen and Kirsten Larsdatter, baptized 19 April 1799 at Gedderaas, and two years old in the 1801 census. He bears his maternal grandfather's name, the second son's name by the Trøndelag rule, which is part of the evidence that Kirsten Larsdatter's father was a Lars. ¶1
