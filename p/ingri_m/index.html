About the family

Mother of Anders Olsen Esse, and therefore a direct ancestor: Anders -> Ole Andersen Flaten -> Mary Olsdatter Anderson -> Olianna Quame -> Berent Erdahl. The link had been mis-entered and was corrected in October 2026. ¶4

Her brother Lars Marcussen in the 1801 census: householder on Meness (Menes), Leikanger, aged 41 (b. about 1760), farmer and occupier, with wife Gietloug Andersdatter 41, stepson Anders Botholdsen 6, two servants, and his parents-in-law Anders Hansen 85 and Synneva Botholdsdatter 80. No parent named Marcus lives with him; their father was probably dead by 1801. ¶8

Her brother Marcus Marcussen, cottager (husmann) under Ytre Esse, died by 1819: his probate was closed 10 November 1819 (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll A 15, fol. 307b-309a). His heirs were his widow Marte Solfest[s]datter[?] and two daughters, Gjøri Marcusdatter (22, serving at Grinde in Systrand) and Sigrid Marchusdatter (about 16). Paul Olsen Esse, Ingri's son, was a creditor of the estate. That probate does not name siblings; the identification rests on the 1811 description of Lars's brother as husmann under Ytre Esse. ¶11

Mother (Probable): Siri Pedersdatter, of Øyre, who married Marcus Larsen Eitungjerde on 27 November 1751. Ingri's father Marcus Thue is very probably the same man: the only Marcus marriage in Leikanger 1748-51, Ingri's baptism five and a half months later, her eldest brother Peder (bapt. 16 Sep 1753) had a Lars Eitungjerde as godfather, and her brothers were named Peder and Lars after the bride's and groom's fathers. No record yet names Marcus Thue's wife. ¶15

Life story

Baptism (probable): 'Ingeri Marcus Daattr Thue' was baptised on Exaudi Sunday, 14 May 1752, in Leikanger parish (ministerialbok 1735-1756, folio 129), daughter of Marcus of the farm Thue; her mother was churched at Tjugum on 18 June 1752 as 'Marcus Thues' wife. Godparents: Hans Thue, Anfind Kolbensen Brynild[?], Margrete Stipshammer[?], [Mari?] Thue and Sygri Einersdatter Qvame. This is Probable, not Confirmed: the census ages (52 in 1801, 67 in 1815) imply birth about 1748-49, and her siblings have not yet been found as children of Marcus Thue. Ages of the elderly in the 1801 and 1815 censuses are often overstated by a few years. No other baptism of an Ingri Marcusdatter was found in the Leikanger book for 1751-52 (canvases 117 and 125-129 read); 1747-1750 were not read. ¶13

No earlier baptism: every entry of the Leikanger ministerialbok 1735-1756 from April 1748 to December 1751 (canvases 84-124) was read; no child of any Marcus was baptised then. The 14 May 1752 baptism of 'Ingeri Marcus Daattr Thue' is the only candidate; her census ages (b. ~1748-49) are overstated. Her proved youngest sister Anna was baptised 'Anna Marcus Daattr Thue' on 18 March 1759 with the same godmother (Margareta Nyphammer), which supports that the 1752 entry is hers. ¶14

Probate: named as 'Ingerie Marchus Datter', widow of Ole Paulsen of Ytre Esse, in his probate opened 1 April 1799 (as written) and closed 1 April 1800 (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll A 12, fol. 548a). The guardian of her son Anders was Peder Marchussen of Tenningen, who shares her patronymic and is very probably her brother. He had died by 1800, and his own probate is indexed at Ytre Sogn A 12, fol. 320b. The line naming her own legal guardian (lauverge) could not be read. ¶6

Widow at Esse, Leikanger in 1801. ¶1

Sibling proved, 1803: in the probate of her mother-in-law Sigri Olsdatter Berge (12-13 April 1803, Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll A 12, fol. 161b-163a, No. 9) she is 'efterlevende Ingerie Marchusdatter boende paa Gaarden Esse' (the surviving widow, living at Esse), and her son Anders's guardian Peder Marchussen, tenant at Tendingen, is called 'Myndlingens Morbroder' (the ward's mother's brother). So Peder Marchussen of Tendingen was her brother, and Lars Marchussen of Meenes (Menes), whom Peder's 1804 probate calls his son's farbror (father's brother), was also her brother. Their father was a Marcus (Marchus); he is not named. ¶7

Probate 1804 (search for her family): the probate of Peder Marchussen of Tendingen, Tjugum parish, guardian of her son Anders, was read (23 June 1804; Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll A 12, fol. 320b-321a, No. 46). His heirs were his widow Giöa[?] Knudsdatter and two children, Marchus 9[?] and Sigri 13; Ingri is not named, and Peder's parents are not named. The guardian of his son was 'Lars Marchussen, Gaardmand paa Gaarden Meenes' in Tjugum skipreide, the boy's farbror (father's brother). So Peder and Lars Marchussen were brothers. That Ingri Marcusdatter was their sister remains a hypothesis (shared patronymic, the guardianship of her son Anders, and her son named Marcus); it would be settled by a baptism or a probate of a Marcus in Tjugum/Leikanger naming his children. ¶5

Probate 1811 (siblings proved): in the probate of her brother Lars Marchussen, tenant at Meenæss (Menes), Tjugum skipreide (begun 27, closed 29 May 1811; Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll A 14, fol. 280a-281a, No. 27), Lars left no children and his heirs were his full siblings ('Kiødsøskende', same father and mother) and their children: (1) his late eldest full brother Peder Marchussen of Tendingen (widow Giöa Knudsdatter), represented by his children Marchus and Sigrid Pedersdatter; (2) his second full brother Marchus Marchussen, cottager (husmann) under Ytre Esse; (3) 'Myndig ældste Kiødsøster Ingerie Marchusdatter gift med Gaardmand Ole Andersen Ytre Esse' (his eldest full sister Ingerie, of age, married to the farmer Ole Andersen of Ytre Esse), who was present; and (4) his late second and youngest full sister Anna Marchusdatter of [Flat]estad in Kvamsøy skipreide, wife of the lagrettemann and conciliation commissioner Christen Christensen [Flat]estad the elder, with four children (Marchus, Christen, Johannes 18, Synneve). Ole Andersen Ytre Esse signed the closing. The parents (a Marcus and his wife) are not named. ¶9

Second marriage: the 1811 probate of her brother Lars proves that by May 1811 she was married to the farmer Ole Andersen of Ytre Esse, which agrees with the 1815 census. ¶10

1815 census: 'Ingeri Marcusd.', 67, married to Ole Andersen, 52 (b. ~1763), a retired farmer (kårmann) at Esse. So she remarried between 1801 and 1815. Ole Andersen was a 38-year-old unmarried day laborer at Esse in 1801. The marriage is not in the indexed Leikanger records. ¶2

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Confidence moved Probable to Confirmed: three primary probates read in full now agree on her: the 1799/1800 probate of her first husband Ole Paulsen Esse (widow Ingerie Marchusdatter, mother of Anders), the 1803 probate of Sigri Berge (Peder Marchussen called Anders's mother's brother) and the 1811 probate of her brother Lars Marchussen (Ingerie Marchusdatter, eldest full sister, wife of Ole Andersen Ytre Esse). ¶12

Searches that found nothing 1

Her parents were not found. The Leikanger church books before 1792 are not indexed, and the 1762 extra tax for Leikanger was not found in the online index. ¶3
