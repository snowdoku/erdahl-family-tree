Life story

Nord-Gudbrandsdal probate cards (39945) for Kvam in Lesja run from image 7519 to about 7600. The first run is in date order: 7519 1670 Ingelbret Lauridsen (fol. 217b, 1658-73), 7521 1681 Elli Amundsdatter, 7523 1681 Oluf Biertesen, 7525 1696 Jon Ingelbretsen, 7527 1708 Bethe Lauridsdatter (Nordre Kvam), 7529 1720 Rønnaug Tostensdatter (Søndre Gudbrandsdal 1682-1721 fol. 233a), 7531 1726 Endre Qvam, 7533 1746 Kvam nordre, 7535 1757 Kvam nordre, 7537 1765, 7539 1777, 7541 1797, 7543 1808; later images mix in a second 'A.' series (7563 1838, 7565 Kvam i Lesje 1824-43 fol. 645a, 7569 Kvam søndre 1721-36 fol. 310a, 7573 Kvam søndre 1752-64 fol. 285a, 7581 Kvam søndre 1836). No card for a skifte about 1674 or about 1715 was seen in 7519-7543, 7561-7591 (odd images sampled). So the test record for Alf (skifte about 1715, protocol 1690-1719, source 25155) is not on the Kvam cards sampled; read protocol 25155 for 1711-1719 by farm, or the Sør-Kvam card at 7569 (1721-36, fol. 310a, which may be after his widow Sigrid or a later holder). ¶2

Kvam cards 39945/7545-7553 were read: all are from protocol 1824-1843 (Kvam nordre 1832 and Kvam 1837-38, Endre Olsen and his family), none early. Card 7569 (Kvam søndre i Lesje, 'Qvam', Søndre Gudbrandsdal 1721-36 fol. 310a, 30 Sep 1728) is the skifte after Gjertrud Olsdatter, married 2nd Knud Jacobsen (dead) and 1st Jon Engelbretsen; children Guri 25 (by Knud) and Engelbret 41, Ole 37, Lars 35, Jon 32, Anne 39 (by Jon); this is the Jon Ingelbretsen family of the 1696 skifte, not Alf Jakobsen's. So no card for a skifte after Jakob (about 1674) or Alf (about 1715) exists in the Kvam run; the remaining route is reading protocols 25154 (1673-76) and 25155 (1711-19) page by page. ¶3

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
