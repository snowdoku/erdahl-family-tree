Ingeborg RolfsdatterConfirmed

Life story

New father of Ingeborg Rolfsdatter (see her page), graded on his link to her: Probable. Inferred, not named: she is 'Ingeborg Rollofsd. Bjørgaard' at her marriage to Gunder Johansen Hemra on 13 Jul 1718 (16368 canvas 53), and a 'Rollof Bjørgaard' stands second godfather at her daughter Gjertrud's baptism, Dom. Oculi 1721 (16368 canvas 83) - patronymic plus residence plus godparent, the usual pattern for a maternal grandfather, but no record yet says 'her father'. Bjørgård is probably Bjørgan in Lånke (several Bjørgan probates in the Stjør- og Verdal registers are filed under Lånke) or Bjørgen, Stjørdal (Stjørdalsboka 141/3). ¶1a

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the identification · 1 search with no result · 4 open questions

The case for this identification 1

Supporting, not a grade change: at Hegre on Bededag 1744, Tosten Biørngaard's daughter Marit was baptised with godparents including (rough reading) 'Rolluf' and 'Guru Biørngaard' (16370 canvas 53 right). A Rolf was living at Bjørgaard in 1744, possibly the same man or a son. 77804 card 171 is Bjørgan, Verdal (1738, Marit Pedersdatter). The Hegra/Lånke Bjørgaard cards should be close by (about cards 163-175) and were not read. ¶2

Searches that found nothing 1

Negative, plus leads: the 77804 Bjørgan cards 165-173 (Leksvik 1727, 1738; Lånke 23 Sep 1741, Kilber Olsen, widow Gunild Thomasdatter; Stjørdal 1 Oct 1733, Joen Larsen, widow Ragnild Jensdatter) name no Rolf as deceased, heir or guardian. Leads, not proof: (1) 16368 c79, 1720 at Hegre: 'Copul. Niels Olsen Cartum og Mali Rollofsd.', a possible second daughter; (2) the Trøite Gunder is 'Gunder Rollufsen' (16370 c260, 1743), and his children's godparents include Bjørgaard people (Ollof Ollofsen Biørngaard, Christmas 1717; Birrit Bjørngaard, 1720) together with 'Gunder Hemra' (1720). Gunder Rolfsen Trøite may be Ingeborg's brother, and so another son of Rolf Bjørgaard. ¶3a

Open questions and the next record to read 4

Test record: a probate after Rolf Bjørgaard or his wife naming 'datter Ingeborg, gift med Gunder Johansen Hemre' (pre-1727 protocols are missing; try the 77804 card index under Bjørgan and the Lånke/Stjørdal panteregister 22315 farm page for Bjørgan), or a 1720s Bjørgan baptism with Gunder/Ingeborg Hemre as godparents. ¶1b

Test record: a pre-1727 Bjørgaard probate (protocols missing) or the Bjørgan page of panteregister 22315. ¶3b

Lead, no grade change: the Stjørdal entries under R in panteregister 22315 (view 193) include 'Rolf Rolfsens udg. Pante Obligation for 50 rd paa Bjørngaard til Lorentz Angel f. 257'. The obligation itself (pantebok A-1, 19750 view 259, fol. 257) is by 'Rolluf Rolfsen Biørngaard', who mortgages 'min paaboende' farm Biørngaard in Stjørdal fogderi to Mr Lorentz Angel for 50 rd, Trondhjem 27 Aug 1738, signed 'Rolluf Biørngaard'. So a Rolf Rolfsen owned and lived on Bjørgaard in 1738, and the farm was in Stjørdal fogderi (supporting Bjørgan/Bjørgård in Hegra/Lånke rather than Verdal). A Rolf was still at Bjørgaard as godfather in 1744 (16370 c53) and in 1758 ('Rolluf Biørngaard' at Jon Kleven's daughter's baptism, 16371 c33), and 'Rolluf Biørngaard's' wife was churched at Hegre in May 1761 (16371 c65), so by 1761 the Rolf there was a younger man having children. Whether the 1738 Rolf Rolfsen is Ingeborg's father (b. about 1660s) or her brother (son of an elder Rolf) is open; if he is her father, her father's patronymic is Rolfsen. Neither record names children. ¶4a

Test record: the 1738 deed by which Rolf Rolfsen acquired Bjørgaard, or his later deed passing it on (22315 under R and under B 'Biørngaard', pantebok A-1/A-2), and a probate after Rolf Rolfsen Bjørgaard (typed registers - under 'Rolluf'/'Bjørg'). ¶4b
