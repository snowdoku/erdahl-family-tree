About the family

NEW ANCESTOR. Mother of Ole Evindsen Hillestad. Graded on her link to Ole Evindsen Hillestad: the skifte after his son Ole Olsøn Hillestad (Skp. 3 fol. 269b, 1712) cites a deed of 9 Jun 1681 from Biørn Olufsøn Haugen in Hillestad to 'avl. Ole Olsøns far, Ole Evindsøn Hillestad' for 1 geitskinn in Haugen under Hillestad, 'som Ole Evindsøns mor, Wilborrig Olufsdaatter, hadde ått' (card 4995, read on the image). The relationship is named in the record; the man is the same Ole Evindsen of Hillestad named in the same skifte as the deceased's father (no rival). Her patronymic and the seller Biørn Olufsøn suggest she was from Haugen under Hillestad and Biørn was perhaps her brother (not proved). ¶1

Records that disagree 1

Card 39942/4995 re-read on the scan to settle the conflict noted (the Tovdal bygdebok makes Vilborg Ole Evindsen's wife). The card (Hillestad i Tovdal, Skp. 3, references 5-7 of Ole Olsøn Hillestad's 1712 probate) reads: transport (deed) 'til avl. Ole Olsøns far, Ole Evindsøn Hillestad, frå Biørn Olufsøn Haugen i Hylestad, på 1 geitsk. i Haugen, Hylestad, som Ole Evindsøns mor, Wilborrig Olufsdaatter, hadde átt. Dat. 9/6-1681.' So the record calls her Ole Evindsen's MOTHER, and the bygdebok's reading as wife is the error; no change to her link. Lead only (not added): the grantor Biørn Olufsøn of Haugen in Hylestad (Setesdal), who conveyed land she had owned, shares her patronymic and may be her brother, which would put her father, an Oluf, at Haugen in Hylestad. ¶2a

Searches that found nothing 1

Negative: no early Setesdal (Vestre Råbyggelag) probate material is online on Digitalarkivet (the Setesdal sorenskriveri probate series in the source list starts in 1830), and the indexed person search finds no Biørn/Oluf at Haugen before 1720 outside Heggen og Frøland. So the lead that Biørn Olufsøn Haugen of Hylestad (grantor of the 1681 deed) was her brother is untested. ¶3a
