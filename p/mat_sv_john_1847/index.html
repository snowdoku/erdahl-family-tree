Life story

Baptism: baptised in Selbu on 20 FEBRUARY 1847 - Selbu parish register, Selbu local parish 1842-1858, births and baptisms, page 150 serial no. 16. Child 'John'; father 'Sivert Johnsen'; mother 'Brynnild Johnsd'. The indexed residence reads 'Pechon', one of this transcription's manglings of Eidem. ¶1

A second son named John, born while the first John (baptised 1842) was alive and would live until 1881 in South Dakota. Two living brothers of the same name is not a Norwegian pattern, so this infant died before 1854, when the name was tried a third time. The 1865 census, which contains exactly one John, rules out all three surviving. ¶2

Kept in the tree rather than dropped because a baptism to the right parents on the right farm is evidence, and because the three dead infants of this marriage are part of its real history. ¶4
