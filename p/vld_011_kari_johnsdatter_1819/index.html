Life story

Baptism: Kari, daughter of Joen Joensen of Kloppen and Anne Pedersdatter, was baptised on 28 Nov 1819 at the Lesjaverk (Verket) annex church in Lesja (index only, p. 937). ¶1

Confirmation: confirmed at Grytten 19 Oct 1834 as Kari Johnsdatter of Setnæs (no. 5, p. 495, read on the scan), parents John Johnsen of Setnæs and his wife Anne Pedersdatter 'fra Lesjøe' (from Lesja). The age, knowledge and vaccination columns are blank. ¶2

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
