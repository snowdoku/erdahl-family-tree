About the family

Mother of Lydia (inference): Lydia was baptised on 18 May 1602, while Margaret was Richard's wife; Richard's second marriage, to Johan Robinson, was on 28 Feb 1602/3, after Margaret's burial. So Lydia was Margaret's daughter. NEHGR 151 (1997) reaches the same conclusion. The baptism entry as indexed names the father only. ¶3

Mother of Lydia: Lydia was baptised at Terling 18 May 1602 as daughter of Richard; 'Margaret, wife of Richarde Gaymer' was buried there 2 Jul 1602, and Richard remarried Joan Robinson at Fairstead 19 Feb 1602/3 (NEHGR 151: 287-289, from the Strutt transcripts and ERO registers). So Margaret was Lydia's mother. ¶6

Parents, lead only: an Essex archdeaconry will of a Terling testator, proved about 1563 (it falls between Terling wills of 1563-1570 in Emmison's abstracts), names 'my brother Richard MASON and my brother-in-law Richard PORKES' as overseers 'for the use of my children'. Only a full-text-search snippet could be read (the volume is lending-only), so the testator's name is not known. This puts an adult Richard Mason at Terling in the early 1560s, the man whose children were baptised there 1560-1570 and who was buried there 6 Oct 1607 (FreeREG); he remains the only candidate father, unproved. A different Richard Mason was a curate and will-writer in mid-Essex 1560s-1580s. ¶7a

Life story

S0264: no Margaret Mason baptism of 1555-1573 at Fairstead, Terling, Witham or Hatfield Peverel is in FamilySearch's index. The only Essex ones are Margaret daughter of John Mason, Chelmsford, 6 Oct. 1564, and Margarett Mason, Epping, 15 Mar. 1560/1 (no parent indexed). The Terling baptisms are not on FamilySearch at all, so the test is still the Terling and Fairstead registers at the ERO. Neither candidate is entered. ¶13

Richard Mason at Terling: Emmison's abstracts of the Bishop of London's Commissary Court wills, 1558-1569, include a Terling will (it leaves money 'to 20 of the poorest in Terling' and 'to my brother James FRANCKE my house and land in Terling') witnessed by 'William SMITHE, Richard HARTE, Richard MASON curate'. So the Richard Mason whose children were baptised at Terling in 1560-1570 was very probably the curate there, and he is the same man as the will-writer 'Richard MASON' of the later Commissary Court abstracts (1578-1588) and probably the 'curate of Canewdon' of another will. This adds to the picture of the only Mason household at Terling, but no record names Margaret as his daughter, so no father is entered. His burial at Terling, 6 Oct 1607, falls after the last printed Emmison volume (1603). His will or administration (ERO D/ABW, D/ACW or D/AEW, 1607-08) is the record to read. ¶9

Marriage: Margaret Mason married 'Richarde Gaymer' at All Saints, Terling, Essex, on 23 July 1590 (FreeREG, file line 253). ¶1

Burial: 'Margaret GAYMER, wife of Richarde Gaymer' was buried at Terling on 2 July 1602 (FreeREG, file line 819), six weeks after the baptism of her daughter Lydia on 18 May 1602. ¶2

Richard Mason's will read: 'I Richard Mason of Terling in the county of Essex yeoman', dated 2 May 1607 (ERO D/ACW 4/214 and a second copy D/ACW 4/255, originals read on the free watermarked images; buried Terling 6 Oct 1607). He leaves his lands in Terling and Fairstead to 'Richard Mason my eldest sonne', in tail, then to Abraham, then to John Mason for life, 'and after his decease to my daughter Jone[s] children'; goods and £8 to 'Abraham my son'; a legacy to John his son; money to 'Jone my daughter' (to be divided among 'her saide children' if she died first); 2 bushels each of wheat and malt to his servant... Ellis at her marriage; son Richard sole executor. He signed with a mark. These children match the Terling baptisms (Richarde 1561, John 1564, Johane 1566/7, Abraham 1570), so this is the only Mason householder at Terling. The will names NO daughter Margaret and nothing for any Gaymer grandchild, although five of Margaret's children (John, Sara, Elizabeth, Andrew and Lydia) were alive in 1607, and it settles the reversion of his land on Joan's children only. This weakens Richard Mason as Margaret's father: a dead daughter's children were often, but not always, remembered. He is not entered. ¶10

Correction to the note calling the Terling Richard Mason 'very probably the curate': the testator of 1607 was a yeoman who signed by mark, so he was probably not the 'Richard MASON curate' who witnessed a Terling will in the 1560s. There may have been two men of the name. That note's identification is superseded. ¶11

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

Corrections 1

Correction: the Emmison snippet cited as 'a Terling will of about 1563 naming my brother Richard MASON and my brother-in-law Richard PORKES' is not a Terling will. Walking the archive.org full-text snippets phrase by phrase gives the whole entry: 'JOHN MASON [abode not given], 1566. (85) To be buried in Barking churchyard. I make my wife Katherine my executrix, to whom I give half of my goods and the house within and without and the other half to my four sons, i.e. William, John, Nicholas and Lawrence. I make my overseers my brother Richard MASON and my brother-in-law Richard PORKES for the use of my children... I owe my cousin John MASON of London 6s.' (Emmison, Essex Wills, Archdeaconry Courts 1565-1571). Barking is in south-west Essex, far from Terling, and the Terling entries appeared in the same snippet only because the search window joins separate pages. The lead therefore says nothing about Margaret. Richard Mason of Terling (children bp 1560-1570, bur. 6 Oct 1607) remains the only candidate father, and is not entered. ¶8

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Possible family (lead only, not entered): a Richard Mason was the only Mason father at Terling in the generation before her marriage. FreeREG's Terling transcription has baptisms of children of 'Richarde Mason': John 15 Aug 1560 (buried 12 Sep 1560 as 'son of Richard'), Richarde 14 Sep 1561, John 17 Sep 1564, Johane 5 Feb 1566/67, George 27 Jul 1569 (bur. 19 Aug 1569) and Abraham 7 Oct 1570. Johane Mason married James Burles at Terling on 7 Dec 1590, four months after Margaret's marriage to Richard Gaymer there (23 Jul 1590). A Richard Mason, householder, was buried at Terling on 6 Oct 1607. No Mason entries occur at Terling before 1560, and no baptism of a Margaret Mason is indexed at Terling; she may have been born in a gap year (1562-63, 1571-72) or before the family came to Terling. Emmison's Essex will abstracts show a Richard Mason acting as curate and will-writer in mid-Essex in the 1560s-80s, possibly the same man. Not entered as her father: no record names her as his daughter. Richard Mason's will (if any, after 1607; ERO D/ABW or D/ACW) is the record to read. ¶5

Searches that found nothing 1

Mason wills (negative): Essex Archives Online lists no will of a Mason of Terling, Fairstead, Hatfield Peverel, Witham, Boreham, Faulkbourne or the Notleys, 1550-1630, other than Richard Mason of Terling, yeoman, 1607 (D/ACW 4/214 and 4/255), which names no Margaret. TNA Discovery has no PCC or other Mason item for Terling or Fairstead in 1540-1640. Her husband's will of 1613 (ERO D/ACW 6/45, read on the image) names his children but no Mason kin in the legible parts. So no will names her parents. Test record (unchanged): a Margaret Mason baptism about 1560-1572, Fairstead first (FamilySearch Essex parish registers). ¶14

Open questions and the next record to read 2

Next: the ERO original (Essex Archives Online, D/ACW or D/AEW c.1563, testator of Terling) and Richard Mason's own will or administration after Oct 1607. ¶7b
