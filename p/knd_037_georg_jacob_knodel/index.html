About the family

Life story

Dates: none known. He was presumably born before about 1715 (inferred: a son married in 1759). ¶3

Marriage (Speculative identification): Georg Jacob Knodel married Susanna Margaretha (surname not indexed) at Vaihingen an der Enz on 3 August 1737 (row 4777562; relatives 'Adam, Susanna Margaretha', read as the groom's father and the bride by the 61023 column order). No witnesses are indexed. Entered against this person only on the identification argued in the Birth note. ¶5

Possible same man, not proved: a Georg Jacob Knodel and his wife Susanna Margaretha had a daughter Maria Sara born at Vaihingen an der Enz on 5 October 1749, who died there on 1 February 1750 (relatives 'Georg Jacob, Margaretha'). That places a Georg Jacob Knodel at Vaihingen, Christoph Adam's later home, ten years before the marriage. Susanna Margaretha could be Christoph Adam's mother or a later wife; she is not entered. A later Georg Jacob Knodel (wife Catharina Barbara, daughter Anna Maria baptised Vaihingen 23 December 1805, index record 4256199) is a different, younger man. Other Georg Jacob Knodels in the index (Feldrennach 1709-1741, Derdingen 1735, Neuenbürg 1787) are in other districts and are not linked. ¶2

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Probable, not Confirmed: the index does not label roles. He is read as the groom's father because 61023 marriage rows list the indexed person's parents and then the spouse, a two-name row being one parent plus the spouse (tested on about 25 two-name bride rows, whose second name is always the groom). A male first name in such a row is therefore the father. Maria Dorothea is the bride, being the mother of the 1761-1765 children. This link sits above a Probable link (Christoph Adam to Christoph Ludwig). The image of either marriage entry (1184901-00036 or 1184562-00588) would confirm it. ¶1b

Upgrade: graded on his link to his son Christoph Adam Knodel (see his page). Christoph Adam's own marriage, FamilySearch 'Germany, Marriages, 1558-1929' record VCY7-WYZ (1760, Bietigheim), has the labelled field 'Father's Name: Georg Jacob Knodel'. Also his baptism: FamilySearch QPGK-HRCY (FamilySearch, 'Deutschland, ausgewählte evangelische Kirchenbücher 1500-1971' (also shown as 'Germany, Lutheran Baptisms, Marriages, and Burials, 1500-1971')), Christoph Adam Knodel born 4 Oct 1740, Vaihingen an der Enz, mother Susanna Margaretha, her husband Georg Jacob Knodel. The groom's own marriage record names his father, so no bridge is needed. ¶9

The case for this identification 1

Identity: father of Christoph Adam Knodel, from the 1759 Bietigheim marriage row (relatives 'Georg Jacob, Maria Dorothea') and its 1760 Vaihingen duplicate. ¶1a

Alternatives considered and set aside 2

Possible birth (Speculative identification,; not entered as his birth date): a Georg Jacob Knodel was born at Vaihingen an der Enz on 11 July 1711, son of Joh. Adam and Anna Margaretha (index row 4768553; a brother Johannes followed on 26 October 1714, row 4768768). That child is probably the Georg Jacob Knodel who married Susanna Margaretha at Vaihingen on 3 August 1737, whose father is indexed as 'Adam' (row 4777562): same name, same parish, father's name agrees (he would have been 26 at that marriage). Whether he is also this man, the father of Christoph Adam Knodel (married 1759), is only Speculative. The case rests on four index facts: (1) the 1759 groom's marriage was also entered in the Vaihingen marriage book (1760 row 4778794), which points to Vaihingen as the groom's home parish; (2) the only Georg Jacob Knodel household found at Vaihingen between 1737 and 1760 is Georg Jacob x Susanna Margaretha (children 1742-1757); (3) that groom's father was 'Adam' (1737 row), matching the father Joh. Adam of the 1711 child, so the grandson's name Christoph Adam would follow a naming pattern; (4) Christoph Adam himself settled at Vaihingen by 1765. Against it: if Christoph Adam was a son of the August 1737 marriage he was born no earlier than mid-1738 and married in February 1759 at about 20 or younger, young for a Württemberg man; no Christoph Adam Knodel baptism is indexed anywhere; and older Georg Jacob Knodels with households in the 1730s lived nearby (Derdingen, children 1731 and 1735; Feldrennach, marriages 1722 and 1727, children 1730-1744; Ottenhausen, marriage 1734). The 61023 'Georg Jacob Knodel' list is capped at 300 of 410 readable rows, so other rivals may be unread. Because the identification is only Speculative, the 1711 date is kept in this note and not in his birth field; his Speculative parents (Johann Adam and Anna Margaretha) are linked on the same hypothesis. His link to Christoph Adam stays Probable on the 1759 marriage rows, independent of it. The 1737 marriage image (1184562-00524), a Vaihingen birth of Christoph Adam about 1738-41 (film 1184562, about images 00285-00316), or an age at Christoph Adam's 1813 burial (1184565-00603) would settle it. Born before about 1715 in any case (inferred from a son married in 1759). ¶4

Ruled out as this man: the Feldrennach Georg Jacob Knodel baptised 16 January 1713 (parents Georg Jacob and Ursula, row 3484938), whose father is not an Adam and whose family stayed in the Neuenbürg district; the Tisheim Georg Jacob baptised 1717 (parents Georg Friederich and Anna Maria); and Georg Jacobs born after 1740 (Feldrennach 1744, Neuenbürg 1756, Ensingen 1758), too young to father a son married in 1759. Derdingen (children 1731, 1735), Feldrennach (married 1722 and 1727) and Ottenhausen (married 1734) Georg Jacob Knodels are not ruled out as Christoph Adam's father, but none is tied to Vaihingen or Bietigheim in the index. ¶8

Death: not found. A Georg Jakob Knodel was buried at Vaihingen in 1794 (row 4409792, year only, no relatives); he would have been about 83 if he is the man born in 1711 and if that man is this one. Not linked; the image (1184565-00454) would give the age. ¶7
