Life story

Baptism (Speculative): Elli, daughter of Arne (Halvorsen) of Sørum and Anne Hansdatter, baptised at Gran on 5 Jan 1738, is a plausible baptism for Eli Arnesdatter. For it: she is the only Eli or Elli daughter of any Arne in the Gran baptism index 1728-1747 (Gran, Brandbu and Tingelstad churches); Eli's age of 62 in the 1801 census implies birth about 1738-39; and Eli's second daughter, Anne Gulbrandsdatter (34 in 1801), bears the candidate mother's name, as custom expected for the maternal grandmother. ¶2a

Of Hvinden in Gran when she was betrothed to Gulbrand Amundsen on 18 Nov 1760. They married 15 Jan 1761. In the 1801 census she was 62, a widow of her first marriage living on føderaad (a retirement maintenance agreement) at Hvinden Søndre. With her were her unmarried children Hans Gulbrandsen 28, Abigael Gulbrandsdatter 20 and Elina Gulbrandsdatter 18. Joel Hansen, a cottager with land, and his wife Anne Gulbrandsdatter, 34, also lived at Hvinden Søndre; Anne is probably another daughter. Eli's parents (father Arne) and her death have not been searched. ¶1

Probate (index cards): she is the widow 'Ellie Arnesdtr.' at Gulbrand Amundsen's estate on 16 Apr 1789 (f. 305a), which names Kirsti among the children. Her own estate, in April 1798 (f. 924a), was held while she was still living ('sk. ilm.', reading unclear), perhaps on remarriage or retirement. That card reads her name as 'Elie Andersdtr.'. This is probably a clerk's or indexer's slip for Arnesdatter, since the husband, farm and children are the same, but it should be checked in the protocol. As Kirsti's mother she is now Confirmed by the 1789 probate card. Her own parentage (Arne Halvorsen of Sørum) remains Speculative. ¶3
