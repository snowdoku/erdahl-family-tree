Life story

1900 federal census: Brule township, Union County, South Dakota - son, b. July 1877, in his widowed mother Brynhild's household. ¶1

1910, 1920, 1930 and 1940 federal censuses: head of his own household in Brule township, with a Danish-born wife Mary (b. abt 1884-87, Denmark) and a very large family. Across the censuses their children are Herbert J. (1906), Alma J. (1907), Blanche C. (1909), Gladys (1911), Alice (1913), Florence (1915), Grace (1917), Mildred (1919) and Marjorie (1923) - nine, of whom the first three are already in the 1910 schedule and the rest accumulate. His mother Brynhild was living with him in 1910. ¶2

Parent links set on the strength of the Selbu marriage of 3 APRIL 1866 between John Sivertsen (b. 1842, of Eidem, son of S. Johnsen) and Brynild Eriksdatter (b. 1843, of Eidem, daughter of E. Johnsen), which identifies the Union County widow 'Brynhild J. Eidem b. June 1843' as John Sivertson Eidem's wife. His own patronymic middle initial J is Johnsen - son of John. ¶4a

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
