About the family

Father of Hølje Knudsen (see his page); Probable, graded on his link to Hølje, from the bygdebok alone. Valdres bygdebok I F, gnr 106 Bø (canvas 266): Knut, user of Bø from 1612 to 1643 (left column: 'Knut... 1612 [til] 1643'), 'd 1643, g m Bergit Høljesdotter. Born: 1 Hølje f om 1619, [fekk] halve garden. 2 Eivind f om 1623, halve garden etter mora. 3 Marte f om 1631, g m Hølje Gudbrandsson... gardpart 2'. Canvas 267 adds that after his death the widow Bergit ran the farm with her son Hølje, paying the 1645 koppskatt, and that the 1647 matrikkel shows the half 'etter Knut'. His son Eivind Knutsson (b. about 1623, m. Sigrid Olsdotter) is treated on canvas 268. Rebuilt from nb.no content-search word boxes; dates from the rebuild are given as ABT. ¶1a

Life story

Vicar's census 1664 for Aurdal (DA 35504, p. 10, view 14): 'Helje Knudsøn 45' and 'Eivind Knudß. 41' hold the same farm side by side, each with 1 hud 4 skind, which fits the bygdebok's two sons of Knut (Hølje b. about 1619 and Eivind b. about 1623, who split the farm). The census does not name their father and reads the farm as '[?]raberg' (the bailiff's list of 12 May 1664, DA 35563 p. 53, writes 'Helge Aabreig' and 'Eivind Aabreig'), not Bø (see the note on Hølje Knudsen). Grade unchanged. ¶2

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
