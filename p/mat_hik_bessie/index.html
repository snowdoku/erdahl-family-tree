About the family

Mother of Lydia Adelaide (Hicken) Hayter. Her daughter's 1969 BC death registration gives the mother's maiden name as Creed, given name 'Bexie', born P.E.I. ¶1

Children: Lydia Adelaide (1882-1969, m. Benjamin Hayter), Edith Gordon (1887-1916, m. Austin Locke Jackson) and George Howard (1889-1966), all by William John Hicken. No children by Johnston or Cleary appear in the 1901, 1911 or 1921 households. ¶16

Life story

Name: 'Bexie' on her daughter's death registration is not a typing slip for Bessie. Her name was Bexey (also spelled Bexie), a pet form of Rebecca used repeatedly among the Creeds (a niece was 'Rebecca (Bexie) Jane Davey'); she was also called Bessie. Maiden name Creed and birthplace P.E.I. are Confirmed by the 1969 registration; the remaining details come from compiled genealogies and Find a Grave, hence Probable overall. ¶2

Birth and baptism: born 18 May 1862 at St Mary's Bay (Find a Grave gives 19 May), baptised 27 April 1864 at the Sturgeon Bible Christian Church, daughter of James Benjamin Creed and Mary Jane Chapman, according to the compiled Creed and Hicken genealogies. ¶3

Baptism: Bexey Jane Creed, born 19 May 1862, baptised 27 April 1863, daughter of James and Mary Creed, in the Bible Christian register of the Murray Harbour/Sturgeon circuit (PEI baptism index, FamilySearch film 5240355, frame 150). This corrects the compiled genealogies, which give born 18 May 1862 and baptised 27 April 1864; the 19 May date is confirmed again by the 1901 census. ¶7

Birthplace: the compiled genealogies say St Mary's Bay (Lot 61), and her daughter Lydia also gave St Mary's Bay as her own birthplace; but Bexey's parents were 'of Lot 59, Three Rivers' at their marriage in 1854 and were heads of household in Lot 59 in 1861 and 1881. Her baptism entry gives no place. The birthplace is therefore left as Kings County until a record names it. ¶8

1881 census: 'Bexy Hicken', 19, Wesleyan Methodist, born P.E.I., in her father-in-law George Hicken's household in Township 61 with her husband William. Her parents' household that year was in Township 59: James Creed 49, farmer, Bible Christian, and Mary 48 (of English origin), with Catherine 25, Elizabeth 23, William 21, Ellen 17, James 15, Mary 11 and Margaret 7. ¶6

1891 census (index): 'Bery' Hicken, about 29, wife of William Hicken, Lot 61, Kings County, with daughters Adelaide ('Adilada', about 9) and Edith (about 4), son George H. (about 2), and her father-in-law George Hicken, about 79, born England. ¶9

Second marriage: by the 1901 census (taken 29 May 1901, Township 61, Sturgeon) she was the wife of Shadrach W. Johnstone ('Shedrick W. Johnston' in the census), a Baptist farmer and mariner born 12 March 1859. The household was Shedrick W. Johnston, head, 42; Bexy, wife, born 19 May 1862, 39; and her children by her first husband, Edith Hicken, born 21 April 1887, 14, and George Hicken, born 27 February 1889, 12. Her daughter Lydia had married Benjamin Hayter that February. The marriage to Johnston took place between William Hicken's death in August 1891 and May 1901; its record has not yet been found. ¶10

Widowed again: Shadrach W. Johnstone died at Boston, Massachusetts, on 12 January 1906 and was buried at Lower Montague (Massachusetts death index; compiled Johnstone genealogy). Her father James Creed died on 4 January 1906 and her mother on 17 June 1906, so she lost her husband and both parents within six months. ¶11

1911 census (2-3 June 1911, Gaspereaux, Kings County, image read): Johnston, Bexey, head, widow, born May 1862, 49, Scotch origin, Baptist, with her son George Hicken, single, born February 1889, 22, farmer. Her daughter Lydia and son-in-law Benjamin Hayter lived in the next house. Shedrick Johnston had therefore died between 1901 and 1911. ¶12

Third marriage: the 1921 census (Lot 61, Gaspereaux) lists James Cleary, head, 65, Roman Catholic, of Irish origin; Bexey, wife, 59, Baptist; and George A. Hicken, stepson, 32, farm labourer, next door to Benjamin and Della Hayter. Find a Grave names this husband James Clarey. ¶13

1931 census (June 1931, Gaspereaux, page 11, image read): living in the household of her son George Hicken, farmer, as 'Cleary, Bexy', mother, widowed, 69, born PEI of PEI-born parents, of English origin and United Church, with George's wife Georgie, their sons Lyle (7) and Gerald (4), and Muriel Jackson, 16, the daughter of her late daughter Edith. James Cleary had therefore died between 1921 and 1931. Her daughter Lydia Hayter's family was enumerated on the same page. ¶14

Death: 16 May 1939 at Charlottetown, of pneumonia, aged 76 (Find a Grave memorial, which appears to draw on her PEI death registration); buried at Sturgeon. No gravestone appears in the 2002 transcription of the Sturgeon Baptist Cemetery. ¶5

Death and burial: died at Charlottetown on 16 May 1939, aged 76, of pneumonia, and buried on 19 May 1939 in the Sturgeon United Baptist church cemetery (compiled Johnstone genealogy and Find a Grave, which agree; the PEI death registration has not been read). No gravestone is recorded for her. ¶17
