Life story

Baptised in Drangedal on the 2nd Sunday after Easter, 22 Apr 1787: 'Drengebarnet Thorulf', son of Thorulf Thorulfsen Jyserøe and Ragnild Johnsdatter. Godmothers: Gunnild Tollefsdatter Jyserøe, Kari Johnsdatter of the same place, and Anne Halvorsdatter Grytting. Godfathers: Knud Knudsen Jyserøe and John Torulfsen [or Torjusen?] of the same place. His mother was churched on Whit Sunday, 27 May 1787 (Drangedal ministerialbok 1768-1814, p. 80). ¶1

He is the Thoruf aged 14 in his parents' household at Jyserø in the 1801 census. ¶2

All five godparents are from his mother's side: her probable mother Gunnild Tellefsdatter, her sister Kari, a Grytting woman, and Knud Knudsen Jyserø. None is from Heimdal or Treungen, so this baptism gives no evidence for his father's parentage. The last godfather's surname is unclear; his brother John was an infant in 1787, so the godfather is more likely his grandfather Jon Torjusen (an inference). ¶3
