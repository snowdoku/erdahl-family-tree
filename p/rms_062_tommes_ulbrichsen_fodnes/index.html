About the family

Parents (Probable): he is evidently 'Thommes 12 aar' (b. about 1696) at the 9 Oct 1708 estate of Barbro Knudsdatter of Fodnes nordre, wife of Uldrich Thommesen (card 3243; skifteprotokoll 1704-1709, s. 458b). His first wife was Kiersti Johansdatter (d. before 21 Jun 1740); the 1745 lifetime division (card 3249) shows a second marriage. ¶3

Life story

Lifetime division (index card): Tommes Ulbrichsen of Fodnes nordre in Aurdal divided his estate in his lifetime on 8 Oct 1745 (Hadeland, Land og Valdres skifteprotokoll 1744-52, fol. 69a). Children: Ulbrich 16 (reading uncertain), Johannes 8, Barbro 18, Marit 16, Sigri 14, Rangdie 12. Gross 582 rdr, with odel land in Fodnes. Probable father of Uldrich Thommesen Fodnes (d. 1812): same farm, matching patronymic, and Uldrich named his eldest son Thommes. No record states the link. His wife is not named on the card. ¶1

How sure are we?

Confirmed. A record that names this person and this family link has been read.

1 on the grade

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: the 21 Jun 1740 estate of his wife Kiersti Johansdatter at Nordre Fodnes (card 3247; skifteprotokoll 1737-1743, s. 427) lists their son 'Uldrick 4 1/2 aar' (born about late 1735). The 1801 census for Aurdal lists 'Uldrik Tommesen', b. 1735, at Fodnes as the husband's father, on føderaad, in his son Tommes's household; this is Uldrich Thommesen who died at Fodnes nordre in 1812. The same name, farm and an exactly matching age tie the 1740 child to the man in the tree, so Tommes is Confirmed as Uldrich's father (raised from Probable). ¶2
