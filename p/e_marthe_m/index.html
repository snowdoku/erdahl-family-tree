About the family

Mother of Truls Larsen Aaning's children Christi (1750), Jørgen (1753), Synneve (1757) and Salve (1763). The 1783 probate after Truls's wife divides the estate among the same children, including Birthe, so she is Probably Birthe's mother. Her name was not legible in the probate image. ¶1

Life story

Aaland 1932: Aaland's Aaning history names Truls Larsen's wife 'Marte Monsd., † 63 a. g. 1782': Marte Monsdatter (Mons is the everyday form of Mogens), who died in 1782 aged 63, so she was born about 1719. This agrees with the tree's death before 30 April 1783. Her son Mons Trulsen (died 1806 aged 61) carries her father's name. Aaland gives no parents or farm of origin for her. Her 1782 burial (Innvik) and the opening lines of the 1783 probate would give her farm. ¶2

Nordfjord skifteprotokoll A 18b, Digitalarkivet 24238 view 69 (about fol. 374): heading 'Aaning i Stryns Skibrede, Opstryns Sogn'. On 21 May 1788 the estate of the late farmer 'Lars Thrulsen Aaning' was registered, between his widow Mallene Rasmusdatter (her guardian Lars Rasmussen Erdahl) and their children Thruls Larsen 12, Rasmus Larsen 5 and Marthe Larsdatter 10. This is Marthe Monsdatter's eldest son Lars (heir in her 1782 probate). He named his daughter Marthe after her, and he died before 21 May 1788. Collateral detail only; it gives no parents for Marthe. ¶4

Origin found: she is 'Marrite Monsdatter Grønfur' of the Nordfjord guardianship protocols. Protocol no. 2, p. 111 (scan read): guardian Ole Andersen Guddal, in place of the late Rasmus Folvem; she inherited on 3 Jul 1727, 16 May 1729 and 11 Oct 1732; '1743 den 8 Octob: befandtes denne pige at være bleven gift med Truels Aaning', who received her means from 'hendes Stiffader Poul Grønfur'. Protocol no. 1, p. 93: Rasmus Folvem was her first guardian (inheritance 3 Jul 1727). Protocol no. 2, p. 235: Truls Aaning as guardian for 'sin versøster' Marrite Monsdatter Grønfur (13 rd in land in Nessie and Grønfur). So she grew up at Grønfur in Oppstryn, married Truls Larsen Aaning between about 1736 and 8 Oct 1743, and her father Mons was dead by 1735, when her mother's second husband Paul Kristensen took the bruk. Parents linked as Probable: Mons Andersen Grønfur and Brite Rasmusdatter (Aaland, Grønfor bruk nr. 2). The 3 Jul 1727 date is also the date on which the Erdal boys inherited from their morfar 'Lars Grønuur' and Rasmus Monsen Grønfur from a probate at fol. 495 (see Lars Rasmussen), so several Grønfur estates were settled that day. ¶5

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: Nordfjord skifteprotokoll A 17 (1781-1785), fol. 246b-247a (Digitalarkivet 24236 view 248; index pa00000000056721 'Marte Monsdtr, Aaning, prot. 17 p. 246b'). Heading 'Aaning i Stryns Skib., Oppstryns Sogn': sorenskriver Johan Daniel Stub records that on 24 Oct 1782 the estate of the late 'Qvinde Sal. Marthe Mons d[atte]r Aaning' was registered and divided between her surviving husband Thruls Larsen Aaning and the seven children and heirs 'med hende sammenavlede' (begotten with her): sons Lars Throlsen Aaning (of age), Mons Throlsen (of age), Jørgen Throlsen (of age), a fourth son Throlsen [given name unclear], and the youngest son Salve Throlsen, 20; the eldest daughter Brithe Thrulsdatter; and the late daughter Christie Thrulsdatter Nessie, who left three children (Lasse Ericsen 8, Erich Ericsen and Thruls Ericsen 3). The record names her as the wife of Truls Larsen and the mother of Brithe (Birthe Trulsdatter), so her motherhood moves from Probable to Confirmed. Her name, which an earlier reading found illegible, is clear in the heading as Marthe Monsdatter (Mons = Mogens). The registration date 24 Oct 1782 also narrows her death to before that day, which agrees with Aaland's death in 1782 aged 63. ¶3
