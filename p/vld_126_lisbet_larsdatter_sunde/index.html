Life story

Burial: 'Lisbeth Larsd.' of Sunde, aged 79 1/4 (b. about 1679), buried 3 Mar 1758 at Borgund (Borgund ministerialbok 1739-1761; index pg00000002547685). Name and farm match the widow of Peder Olsen Sunde (probate 18 Dec 1741), and the age suits a woman with married children in 1741. The only other Lisbet Larsdatter buried at Borgund in the period lived at Myklebust (1767, aged about 68). Her birth is not entered; the burial implies about 1679. ¶3

Probate (scan read): widow ('efterladte Enche Lisbet Larsdatter') at the probate after Peder Olsen Sunde, 18 Dec 1741 (Sunnmøre 3A 14 fol. 43a). The heirs on the other side are called 'deris sammen avlede Børn', the couple's jointly begotten children, and include Siri Pedersdatter married to Knud Jensen Myklebust. This makes her Siri's mother. The word read 'deris' is slightly uncertain, but the formula 'sammen avlede Børn' itself means children of the deceased and the widow together, and no earlier marriage of Peder is mentioned. ¶1
