About the family

First wife of Anton Johannes Tungseth, married in 1915 according to his Find a Grave memorial, and buried as 'Ida Elizabeth Amundson Tungseth', 1885-1924, in Zion Lutheran Cemetery, Skree Township, Clay County, Minnesota. She died at about 39, probably the mother of the four children born between 1916 and 1919. Her exact dates and her parents have not been looked for. ¶1
