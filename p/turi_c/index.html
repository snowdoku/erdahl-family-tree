About the family

Parents Christen Persen Sværen and Randi Andersdatter of Tjugum are probable. kgroenha.net gives them as her parents (married 19 Jun 1752). The 1801 census supports this: at Sværen, Randi Andersdatter, 74, a twice-widowed retired mother, lived with her son Peder Christensen (b. ~1761, who had children Christen and Randi) and her daughter Britha Christensdatter. Turi named a son Christen. ¶1

Life story

Death: 'Thuri Christensdatter', aged 89 (b. about 1753), died 27 Nov and was buried 2 Dec 1842 (Leikanger parish register 1838-1851, p. 282, no. 65; index pg00000006658779). No farm is given. Balestrand (Tjugum) was still an annex of Leikanger prestegjeld until 1849, so Gjerde falls in this register. The age agrees with her estimate of about 1754, and she is one of only two Turi Christensdatters born 1748-1760 in the national burial index, the other not in Sogn. ¶5

Probate 1810: in the probate after her mother Randi Andersdatter Sværen (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll A 14, fol. 116a-117a, No. 26, 28 May 18[10?]), the third heir is 'Afdødes anden Datter Turi Christens Datter gift med Gaardmand Biørn Johnsen Gierde' (the deceased's second daughter Turi Christensdatter, married to the farmer Bjørn Johnsen Gjerde), a child of Randi's first marriage with Christen Pedersen Sværen. ¶2

Probate 1828: in the probate after her husband Bjørn Johnsen Gjerde (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll A 18, fol. 29b-30a, No. 46, 21 Oct 1828) she is 'Enken Thuri Christensdatter Gjerde', who inherits half the estate. ¶3
