About the family

Parents: Probable (strong), not Confirmed. She is the only unmarried Martha Olsdatter at Ytre Takle in 1801; the 1788 baptism and the 1801 census name Ole Torbjørnsen and Sigrid Johannesdatter as parents; and her own first child's godparents in 1813 include Iver Olssøn of Ytre Takle, her brother. No adult record yet names her father. A record that gives her age or her father (her burial, or a probate after Ole Torbjørnsen naming his heirs) would confirm it. The 1819 godmother 'Johane Olsdatter' may be her sister Johanna, baptised 1784, but another Johanna Olsdatter, 37, wife of Joen Johannessen, lived at Midt Takle in 1801, so that clue is ambiguous. ¶6

Parents Confirmed (upgrade from Probable): in the estate division (skifte) of Ole Torbjørnsen and his wife Sigrid Johannesdatter of Ytre Takle, held 15 Jun 1824 at Larsendahl, Lavik skipreide, the parties who appeared were 'Ole Torbjørnsen ytre Tackle og Iver Rasmussen Botten gift med Datteren Marthe Olsdatter' (Iver Rasmussen Botten, married to the daughter Marthe Olsdatter). On fol. 358b a lot goes to 'Datter Marthe Olsdatter gift med Iver Rasmussen Botten'. This names her as the couple's daughter and as Iver Rasmussen's wife, which proves the link. Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll A 16, fol. 358a-358b. ¶10

Siblings named in the 1824 division: the estate was shared among 2 sons and 4 daughters. Named on the pages read: her brother Iver Olsen of Ytre Takle; her sister Giertrud Olsdatter of Ytre Takle, 31 (so born about 1793, which fits the baptism of 8 Sep 1793), with Endre Clausen Midttakle named in her lot (probably as guardian); and her sister Anna Olsdatter of Ytre Takle. The other son and daughter were not read by name. ¶11

Life story

Baptism (probable identification): Martha, daughter of Ole Torbjørnsen and Sigrid Johannesdatter of Ytre Takle (yst=Takle), was baptised 9 Jul 1788 at Eivindvik, and the mother was churched 25 Jul 1788. Godparents: Joen Johannessøn (Midt Takle), Peder Monssøn (Indre Takle), Carl Olssøn, Iver Hanssøn and Orlog Johannesdatter (Ytre Takle). Gulen church book 1788-1798, p. 177, no. 38, index. ¶2

1801 census: Martha Olsdatter, 14, joint daughter (felleds dr.) of Ole Torbiørnssen, 48, skattebonde, and Sigrid Johannesdatter, 43, at Ytre Takle (Takle Yst), Brekke. With her are her siblings Johanna 20, Torbiørn 18, Iver 12, Giertrud 9 and Anna 6, and her grandmother Anna Iversdatter, 73, the wife's mother, living on a farm pension. The children's census ages run about two years above their baptisms (Martha baptised 1788, Iver 1790, Giertrud 1793, Anne 1796). ¶3

Confirmation: A 'Marthe Olsdr' of Midt Takle was confirmed at Brekke on 10 Mar 1805, on the same day as Iver Olssøn and Ole Olssøn of Ytre Takle. Iver is probably her brother, baptised 1790. The Midt Takle residence probably means she was in service there; the 1801 Midt Takle households have no unmarried Martha Olsdatter. This is unresolved until the scan is read. ¶4

Of Ytre Takle when she married Iver Rasmussen on 13 Jun 1813. Named as mother of Rasmus at his 1819 baptism and 1834 confirmation. Godparents at the 1819 baptism were Takle people (Claus Endresen, Ole Olsen, Johane Olsdatter, Kari Iversdatter). Her parents were not found. ¶1

Marriage: Engaged 2 May 1813 and married 13 Jun 1813 as Marthe Olsdatter, pige (unmarried woman), of Ytre Takle, to Iver Rasmussøn, bachelor, of Botnen. The index gives no father or age. ¶5

How sure are we?

Confirmed. A record that names this person and this family link has been read.

1 on the identification · 1 correction · 1 alternative set aside

The case for this identification 1

Supporting link: at the baptism of her brother Iver (20 Aug 1790, Brekke, Gulen church book 1788-1798, p. 222-223, no. 46, index), the godparents included Rasmus Iverssøn and Synneve Joens Dr of Botnen, her future parents-in-law. This ties Ole Torbjørnsen's household at Ytre Takle to the Botnen family. ¶7

Corrections 1

Correction: The birth moves from unknown to ABT 1788, from the probable baptism; the 1801 census age of 14 alone would give about 1787. ¶9

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Homonyms, not excluded by record: two other Martha Olsdatters lived in the Brekke district in 1801: Marthe Olsdatter, 16, joint daughter of Ole Lassessen and Martha Johannesdatter at Tynning (Tynjum), and Martha Olsdatter, 23, servant (tieneste pige) in Hans Olsen's household at Indre Oppedal. Residence at marriage can be a service place (compare her 1805 confirmation at Midt Takle), so the Ytre Takle residence in 1813 does not by itself exclude them. The godfather Iver Olssøn of Ytre Takle in 1813 and the 1790 Botnen godparents favour Ole Torbjørnsen's daughter. ¶8
