Life story

Marriage: He married Amelia Hanson on 7 June 1894 (her 1934 obituary; his own gives only the year 1894). ¶7

Probably the 'John W. Nelson' recorded with wife 'Emilia M. Hansen' in the Beaver Creek Lutheran congregation about 1902. ¶3

Find a Grave links him to his siblings Gust S. Nelson (1864-1953, b. Fillmore Co., Minnesota; married Annie Elaine Hanson, Amelia's sister) and Malinda 'Linda' Nelson Reveland Hanna (1873-1939). His grave marker reads 'Father'. ¶4

1925 Iowa census (images read): indexed as 'J W Nilson', head, 54, married, owner of a mortgaged home, in Buffalo Township, Winnebago County, with his wife Emilia 49 and children Rube (daughter, 11) and Vern (son, 6). The parents' section (image 193) gives his father as 'Nilson Peter', born Norway, dead, and his mother's full maiden name as 'Johnson Andria', born Norway, dead, with the parents married in Norway. The father agrees with the tree; for the mother see the note on Ellen K. Nelson. ¶10

1934: His wife Amelia died on 5 July 1934 at the home of their daughter Wilma (Mrs. Theodore Ramsey), near Joice. The 1934 obituary lists him among the survivors and their son Vern as living 'at home' (Lake Mills Graphic, 18 Jul 1934, p. 5). ¶9

Known as William Nelson. His obituary (Lake Mills Graphic, 22 Oct 1958, p. 4, 'John Nelson, 88') says he died Thursday at the Good Samaritan home in Mason City. Funeral at Anderson Funeral Home and Winnebago Lutheran Church (Rev. Dean Whitney, Rev. O. D. Thompson). ¶1

Same obituary: except for nine years, he lived his whole life in Winnebago County. His parents were Peter and Ellen Nelson of Mount Valley Township. He married Amelia Hanson in 1894. They had six children: Wilma (Mrs. Theodore Ramsey), Ruby (Mrs. Robert Brakke), Ada (Mrs. Martin Bang), Florence (Mrs. Otis Tweed), Vern (Mason City) and Victor (died 1916). His wife died in 1934. Survivors included 22 grandchildren and 32 great-grandchildren. ¶2

Death date: His obituary says he died 'Thursday' at the Good Samaritan home in Mason City and that the funeral was on Monday. In the Wednesday 22 October 1958 issue that places the death on Thursday 16 October and the funeral on Monday 20 October 1958 (inferred from the weekdays). ¶5

Burial: Neither his 1958 obituary nor any record read so far states his place of burial. The Winnebago Lutheran Cemetery burial rests on Find a Grave and on the funeral being held at Winnebago Lutheran church. ¶8

Death certificate (read on the image): Iowa State File No. 114-58-20610. John W. Nelson died 16 Oct 1958 at the Good Samaritan Home, Mason City, after 7 years there; usual residence Lake Mills, Winnebago Co.; born 8 Sept 1870 in Winnebago Co., Iowa; widowed; retired farmer; father Peter Nelson; mother's maiden name 'Caroline' (surname left as dashes); informant Vern E. Nelson, Mason City; burial date on the image read as '10-20-58' (FamilySearch index says 26 Oct 1958; the obituary puts the funeral on Monday 20 Oct), cemetery written as 'Winnebago Cemetery' [reading of a faint typed line], Winnebago Co. This supports the Winnebago Lutheran Cemetery burial that earlier rested on Find a Grave. ¶11
