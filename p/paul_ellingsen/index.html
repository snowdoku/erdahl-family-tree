About the family

Children: Elling (1839, took over the farm), Gunhild Maria (1841), Johanna (1847), Sivert (1850), Peder Olaus (1853), Johannes (1857, emigrated 1882). ¶3

His son Elling took the farm: the 1900 census for Tungset nedre, gnr. 129 bnr. 1 in Kornstad, is headed by Elling Paulsen Tungset, born 1839, 'Gaardbruger S.' (farm owner), with his wife Ingborg Torgersdatter, born 1847, and five of their children. Paul himself had died in January 1894 and so does not appear. ¶7

Son's death certificate: the Minnesota death certificate of his son Peder O. Tungseth (died Clay County, Minnesota, 2 Jan 1915; FamilySearch index, page 100, certificate 2539) names Peder's father as 'Paul E. Tungseth', born in Norway - Paul Ellingsen Tungset, with the farm name as a surname. This independently corroborates the parent link already Confirmed from Peder's 1853 baptism. ¶9

Life story

Baptized 28 Apr 1805 (baptism identification probable). Living with his parents at Lillemark in 1825. ¶1

Married Tørri Jakobsdatter Tungsæt 15 Jul 1838 at Kvernes; freeholder (selveier) of Tungset nedre in 1865. ¶2

Another daughter, Ingeborg Paulsdatter Tungset, married 11 Jun 1871. In 1825 he lived with his parents at Lillemark. ¶5

Death found in the scanned Kornstad parish book 1885-1900 (source 5311), burials 1894, men no. 3. 'Kaarmand Paul Ellingsen', born 1805 at Guldset in Kvernes and living at Tungset, died 14 Jan 1894 and was buried 20 Jan 1894 (committal 20 Apr). The death was reported 15 Jan. His wife Tørri had died 20 Jun 1893. ¶6

Still living at Tungset in the 1891 census (born 1805) and not in the 1900 census. The Kornstad parish book for 1885-1900 (Digitalarkivet source 5311) is indexed for baptisms only, so his burial and Tørri's are not searchable. They must be read in the scanned burial pages of that book or the 1901-1914 book. The 'Paul Ellingsen, Grdbrs' who stood godfather at Tungset 1891-1899 is his grandson Paul (born 1871). ¶4

Death register (scan read): the Nordmøre probate court's death register (dødsfallsprotokoll no. 3, p. 129, Kvernæs 1st half-year 1894, no. 1) records 'Paul Ellingsen Tungseth, Kårmand' (retired on a farm pension), aged 89. He died 14 Jan 1894 and the death was reported 15 Jan 1894. No spouse was surviving. The heirs were '6 myndige, hvoraf 2 i Amerika' (6 adults, 2 of them in America), and the remark is 'Registrering snarest mulig' (register the estate as soon as possible). The children are not named. The age is one year high: he was 88. Tørri's entry seven months earlier counted only one child in America. So a second child (perhaps Peder Olaus, later of Minnesota) may have emigrated in the second half of 1893, or the first clerk missed one; this is inference only. An 1894 estate registration, which would name the heirs, has not been searched. ¶10

The household in 1900 shows what sort of place Tungset nedre was in a Nordmøre fishing district: the eldest son at home, Pål Ellingsen, born 1871, is entered as 'Skreifisker Jordarbeider' - a skrei fisherman and field labourer. Skrei is the migrating Arctic cod that comes to the Norwegian coast to spawn in late winter, and a skreifisker worked the winter cod season, in Nordmøre usually on the Romsdal and Nordmøre grounds or north in Lofoten, and farmed the rest of the year. His brother Tore Kristian, born 1877, was a house carpenter and cabinetmaker. The farm also carried a widow on a retirement agreement (føderåd), Kannutta Knutsdatter, born 1819. ¶8
