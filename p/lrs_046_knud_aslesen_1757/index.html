About the family

Family: the first son of Asle Knudsen and Kari Tostensdatter, born nine months after their 1756 wedding, and named, by Norwegian custom, after his paternal grandfather Knud (inferred). He is linked to this couple on the same evidence as his brothers (Probable). He is not in his brother Tosten's household in 1801, and his later life has not been traced. ¶2

Life story

Baptism: on 16 Jan 1757 at Ål church, Knud, son of Asle Knudsen of Medgaardehuus, was baptised. Godparents (readings partly uncertain): Ole Nielsen [Lie?], Iver Gundersen, Tosten [?] of Sando, Margith of Sando and Guri Gundersdatter of Sando. The mother was churched on 27 Feb 1757. The first reading of the father's patronymic as 'Gunders.' was revised to 'Knuds.' together with the baptisms of his brothers Tosten (1758) and Ole (1762). ¶1

Probates: named as Knud, 17, the first child, in the skifte of 12 Oct 1773 after his father Asle Knudsen at Medgaarden, and as Knud Aslesen, of age (myndig), in the skifte of 18 Feb 1785 after his mother Kari Tostensdatter (probate index cards, Ringerike og Hallingdal skifteprotokoll 19 fol. 651a and 20b fol. 674a). The 1773 protocol text (fol. 651a) reads 'Søn Knud Asle[sen] 17 aar'. Confidence raised from Probable to Confirmed by the 1773 protocol and these cards; the age 17 in Oct 1773 (16 if born in Jan 1757) is within the usual error. ¶4

Confirmation: 'Knud Asles: Medgaarden' (Knud son of Asle, of Medgaarden) was confirmed in Ål on Ascension Day 1775, aged about 18 if he is the child baptised 16 Jan 1757. The list gives no ages or parents; the identification rests on the patronymic and the farm. Ål church book 1771-1806 (source 8545), p. 46. ¶3
