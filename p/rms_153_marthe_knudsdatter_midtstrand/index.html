About the family

Wife of Helge Gulbrandsen of Midtstrand; died before 7 Feb 1701, when her probate was held (card 9291; DA 25283 s. 316, view 319): 'efter Martha Knudsdatter, som haver efterladt sig med hendes Igienlevende Mand Helge Gulbrandsen 4 Børn, Nafnlig Knud', Gulbrand, Even and Berrit Helgesdatter (married to Ole Hendrichsen Hegge). ¶1

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the grade · 1 open question

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Graded Probable on her link to her son Knud Helgesen (see his page): her probate names Knud as her son, but the identity of that Knud with the Midtstrand widower of 1721 rests on patronymic plus farm (see Helge Gulbrandsen). ¶2

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Test record: as for her husband (a Midtstrand deed of about 1701-1705 naming Knud Helgesen as heir to the farm). Her father's name Knud is from her patronymic only. ¶3
