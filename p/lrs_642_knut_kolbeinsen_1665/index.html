Life story

Bygdebok for Karmøy: Åkra (1980), farm 21 Heiå (the farm called 'Hejen eller Ranvoldsjord' in the 1758 Skudenes soul register; the book quotes the alias '... eller Randvoldjord' from 1723), old-farm section 'bnr. 15', canvas 186, rebuilt from nb.no word boxes: '6. 1699-1733: Knut Kolbeinsen, ca. 1665, son [av] Kolbein Larssen [...] bnr. 7. G.m. [enkja] etter førre [brukar]. Born: a. Ola, ca. 1699, lever 1716, soldat då; b. Peder, ca. 1705, [7.] brukar. Knut tok over halve [garden] då han [gifte] seg med [enkja] etter Torbjørn.' The previous user (no. 5, 1675-1692) was Torbjørn, b. ca. 1659, son of the user before him; so Knut married Torbjørn's widow, whose name the book does not give (the gap before 'etter førre' is one short word). ¶1

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the grade · 1 search with no result · 1 open question

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Graded on his link to his son Peder Knudsen: Probable, from the bygdebok statement alone (a compiled secondary source). The book's no. 7, 'Peder Knutsen, ca. 1705, d. ca. 1764, son [av] førre, G.m. Rannveig Holgersdtr., ca. 1698', is the tree's Peder Knudsen (58 in 1758 with Ranved Holgersdatter 60 on 'Hejen eller Ranvoldsjord'; probate 18 Apr 1764 at Ranvoldiord). The book's birth year (ca. 1705) differs from the 1758 age (58, i.e. about 1700); the identification rests on farm, wife and death date. No primary record of the father-son link has been read. ¶2a

Searches that found nothing 1

Negative: no probate after Knut Kolbeinsen found. The hand-written farm register of Ryfylke skifteprotokoll Karmsund og Hesby 1724-1736 (source 23889) has no Heien/Hejen/Randvoldjord entry under H, R or S (the only 'Heijen' row, 'Sedberg paa Heijen', 16 Apr 1733, fol. 299b, read: deceased Berge Olsen, widow Randveig Endresdatter, daughters Randveig and Ely Bergesdøttre; not this family); the typed farm register of the Karmsund og Hesby probates 1740-1754 (source 23972) has none under H or R. Earlier passes found none in the designation register D 10 (23887, 1740-54). Test record unchanged: a probate after Knut (or his wife) naming Peder Knudsen; if none exists, the 1701 manntall for Skudenes (Knut, about 36, with son Ola). ¶3

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Next: Knut's own probate (he left the farm in 1733), in the Ryfylke/Karmsund skifteprotokoller of the 1730s-40s, which would list Peder among the heirs; and the 1701 manntall for Skudenes (Knut, about 36, with son Ola, about 2). ¶2b
