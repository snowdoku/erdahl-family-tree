About the family

NEW ANCESTOR, graded on his link to his daughter Asbor Evindsdatter (see her page). The 6 Oct 1736 oppgitt skifte at Nordre Askland (Skifteprotokoll nr. 9 fol. 134b, read on the scan) names the children of Todne Svendsdatter and her former husband 'Ewind Jørgensøn': Jørgen 25, Gullow 21, 'Asbør Ewindsdatter gift med Aadne Larsøn Søndre Ascheland', and Anlow 14. His own estate was held 3 Aug 1729 (Skp. 8 fol. 67, card 77: children Jørgen 18, Gulov 14, Asbor 12, Anlov 7, Guro 2½; guardians Bendix Mjåland, Ole Olsøn Askeland and Gunder Omland). Card 77 also notes an earlier skifte of 13 Mar 1709 after his first wife Ulvild Olsdatter. He was an appraiser at Søndre Askland in 1716 ('Evind Jørgensøn og Tergie Salvesøn Askland', Skp. 5 fol. 25a). His parents (Jørgen Olsen Oland and Guro Evindsdatter, d. by 18 Mar 1707) come from the archivist's note on card 79, so they are Probable. Do not confuse him with Evind Bjørnsen of Ekra (d. 1724). ¶1

Siblings: the 1707 skifte after his mother Guroe Evindsdatter (Skp. 2 fol. 186b) names his brothers Salve Jørgensøn (Oenland), Ole and Andgrim and his sister Siri Jørgensdatter, wife of Ole Evindsøn Oenland. He was already 'Askeland' in 1707. His first wife was Ulvild Olsdatter (skifte 13 Mar 1709, card 77). ¶2
