Life story

Not listed in the bailiff's census 1664-66 for Erdal (p. 142): the only farmers' sons are Peder[?] and Erich, adults per the vicar's list. The volume has one Nordfjord list only (no separate 1665 or 1666 list), so no census of 1664-66 names him. ¶7

Aaland 1932: Aaland lists him on Erdal bruk nr. 3: '(Vs.) Rasmus Olson, br. 1678, sk. 1698 p. 3½ pd. * Brite (Jørgensd.)': tenant from 1678, with a deed (skøyte) in 1698 to 3½ pund; the couple owned silver (rings, a belt, spoons). Aaland calls him, with a query in brackets, the son-in-law of the previous holder Ola Jørgensen, and gives his wife as Brite, with the patronymic Jørgensdatter bracketed as Aaland's own inference. He names their sons Ola (eldest; a soldier before 1714, later infirm, kept on the farm) and Jørgen (born about 1681). The holding, dates and sons agree with the 1701 census and the 1714 probate. The wife's name does not agree with the probate's widow Kirsti/Christi Bjørnsdatter; Brite may have been a first wife who died before 1714, which Aaland does not discuss. Aaland gives no parents for Rasmus. ¶4

1701 census (manntall), Erdal, Oppstryn: Rasmus Olsen, occupier (opsidder), aged 50, with sons Olle (23) and Jørgen (20). ¶1

Probate after 'the late Rasmus Olsen, who lived on the farm Erdahl in Stryn skipreide', held 30 May 1714 (skifteprotokoll A 5b, fol. 327-328). The parties were his surviving widow (Kirsti/Christi Bjørnsdatter, reading uncertain) and the heirs. The heirs include Ole Rasmussen, Jørgen Rasmussen (of age), Barbra Rasmusdatter (married), Kari Rasmusdatter (married), Brite Rasmusdatter (married) and Mari Rasmusdatter (unmarried; a ward from 1714 under Rasmus of Folven). ¶2

Probate 1714, division (reader, A 5b fol. 328b-330): the estate totalled 217 rd 12 sk, the debts and costs 32 rd 4 sk, and the net 185 rd 8 sk. It was divided wholly among the children: Ole and Jørgen 46 rd 1 ort 2 sk each ('Broderlod'), Barbra, Kari, Brite and Mari 23 rd 13 sk each ('Søsterlod'). Ole's lot was to be held until he 'fra Kongelig Tieniste hiemkommer' (came home from the King's service). The widow Christiane Bjørnsdatter took no share, which suggests she was a later wife. Among the charges was 2 rd for the keep of 'den gamle Qvinde Mari Rasmusdatter' (the old woman Mari Rasmusdatter), with a clause that the farm's heirs maintain her for life, each by his share. Being old, she is not the unmarried daughter Mari, and she may be Rasmus's sister (an inference). ¶6

Probate 30 May 1714, creditor list (reader, Nordfjord A 5b fol. 329a, Digitalarkivet 24222): a claim by Ane Rasmusdatter names 'hendes Broder Rasmus Rasmussen Erdale' (her brother Rasmus Rasmussen Erdal) — Probable reading. This suggests two further children of Rasmus Olsen, Ane and Rasmus, who are not in the heirs list read on fol. 327 (Ole, Jørgen, Barbra, Kari, Brite, Mari); whether they were children of an earlier marriage already paid out is not stated in the lines read. ¶8

Corrections 1

Correction: the 1714 probate (A 5b fol. 327-327b) re-read. The widow is 'Christiane Biørnsdatter' (not Kirsti/Christi). Heirs in order: Ole Rasmussen, 'udcommanderet i Kongelig Tieniste' (on the King's military service), represented by 'den Dannemand Erich Fiellov[?]'; Jørgen Rasmussen, 'myndig og self tilstede' (of age and present); Barbra Rasmusdatter, married to Jørgen [?]; Kari Rasmusdatter, married to Ole [?]; Brite Rasmusdatter, married to Gul[l]ow [?]son; Mari Rasmusdatter, 'endnu ugift' (still unmarried). Lagrettesmenn (court witnesses) Joen Maaland and Arne Sunde. No earlier wife is named. Ole's military service agrees with Aaland's 'utkom. fyrr 1714'.
