About the family

Wife of Knud Olsen of Lo (Loe), Svenes annex, Aurdal. Graded on her link to her son Ole Knudsen. Lifetime division of Loe: the Valdres probate card 8651 (DA 39948) and the protocol entry it indexes (DA 25287 fol. 539b, view 552, 'Skiftebrev paa Garden Loe udi Svennes Annex og Ourdahls Prestegield i Walders', 19 Feb 1754) record a skifte held in the lifetime ('i levende live') of the couple Knud Olsen and Marit Arnesdatter of Loe. The card lists their children: 1. Ole; 2. Ragnild, married to Ole Juelsen; 3. Siri, married to Hendrik Tostensen; 4. Guri, deceased, married to Niels Tronsen, with children Knud 10, Arne 7 and Engebret 5; 5. Gunild, married to Hendrik Juelsen. Gross 626-2-20, net 564-1-18 rdr. The protocol text was checked for the heading, date, farm and the couple's names, and for the daughters Ragnild, Siri and Guri; the son's line is too faint at the scan's resolution to read beyond the card. Identification: eleven months later, in Jan 1755, 'Ole Knudsen Loes og Astri Juelsd.' baptised a son Juel (DA 9361 p. 452, canvas 228), so the son Ole of the 1754 division is very probably this Ole Knudsen: same farm, same name, under a year apart. Guri's son Arne carries the name of her mother's father, which suggests Marit Arnesdatter was the mother of the children, not a stepmother. ¶1a

Daughter Siri married: 'Henrich Tostensøn Oppen og Siri Knudsdaatter Loe', betrothed Oct 1735, married 21 Mar 1736 (DA 9361 p. 30, canvas 284). This puts the family at Lo by 1735. Her grade is unchanged: the record names neither parent. ¶3

Life story

Ole Knudsen Mitstrand stood surety for Gunild: at the betrothal on 4 Jun 1748 of 'Henrick Juulsøn Maanum og Gunild Knudsd. Loe' (Svenes; married 1 Jul), the sureties were 'Klokkeren Ole Halbiørnsen og Ole Knudsøn Mitstrand' (DA 9361 p. 70, canvas 304). Gunild is the fifth child of the 1754 Lo division (married to Hendrik Juelsen), and a bride's brother was the usual surety, so this joins Ole Knudsen 'Mitstrand' to the Lo family six years before Ole Knudsen 'Loes' baptised his son Juel. It does not name him as her brother, so the grade stays Probable, but it removes the doubt in about whether the Mitstrand Ole and the Lo Ole were one man. ¶2

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the grade

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Probable, not Confirmed: the farm Lo had several households (four in 1801), the Ole Knudsen 'Mitstrand' who stood surety in 1748 and 1752 is not yet shown to be the same man, and the card does not say Ole was married. The later Lo cards fit: in 1818 the estate of Sigrie Thoresdatter Loe names children Ole, Juel, Ragnild, Marit (married to Syver Engebretsen Fodnes) and Astrie (card 8653), and an Ole Knudsen, b. 1777, farmed Lohe in 1801. That is the naming you would expect from a son Knud of Ole Knudsen and Astri Juelsdatter, carrying the family on at Lo. ¶1b
