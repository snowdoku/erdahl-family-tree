About the family

Heirs in the 1766 probate, as read ([?] = uncertain): eldest son Iver Gullachsen, Wirkeland[?], of age (myndig) and present; 2nd son Niels Gullachsen, dead, leaving a daughter Birthe Nielsdatter, whose guardian (værge) was her uncle Claus Gullachsen of Yttre Oppedal; 3rd son Thor[?] Gullachsen, dead, leaving a daughter Lisbeth Thorsdatter, 4 years old, for whom her mother Ana Torchildsdatter Brecke[?] appeared, under the oversight (Tilsyn[?]) of Lasse Monsen Oppedal; 4th son Claus Gullachsen, Yttre Oppedal, of age and present; eldest daughter Anna Gullachsdatter, married to Lasse Monsen of Indre Oppedal, both present; 2nd daughter Magnilde Gullachsdatter, married to Lasse Olsen Tøning[?]; 3rd daughter Maritte Gullachsdatter, married to Johannes Clausen Asem[?] (Asheim?); 4th daughter Britte Gullachsdatter, married to Ole Johannesen of Yttre Oppedal. These siblings of Anna are recorded here only and are not created as persons. ¶4

Father of Anna Gullaksdatter (see her page), Confirmed: the probate names his 'ældste Datter Anna Gullachsdatter gift med Lasse Monsen Jndre Oppedall, begge tilstede' (eldest daughter Anna Gullaksdatter, married to Lasse Monsen of Indre Oppedal, both present). Lasse Monsen of Indre Oppedal married Anne/Anna in 1739, and on 3 Jun 1783 he and his wife 'Anna Gullachs[datter]' divided their estate at Indre Oppedal (Ytre Sogn 11a fol. 122b-123a). No other Lasse Monsen is known at Indre Oppedal 1739-1788 (burial-index sweep), so the heir of 1766 is the Anna of 1739, 1741, 1742 and 1783. ¶6

Wife's probate: Britte Iversdatter's own probate, 16 Sep 1769 (Ytre Sogn 8b No. 55, fol. 563b), lists the same 4 sons and 4 daughters as his 1766 probate, all as her children (shares called Moderarv). ¶9

Children: baptisms at Brekke of 'Gulig ytter Opdals barn N: Anna, Mat: Birthe' about 25 Jun 1719 (buried 12 Aug 1719, aged 7 weeks or days) and of 'Gulich ytter opdals barn N: Anna, Mat: Birthe' on 16 Jun 1720 (Gulen 11438 c37 R, c39 L, c48 R). Godparents in 1719: Colben Asseim, Harald ytter Opdal, Rand[?] Hansdatter ibid, Anna Vatne, Birthe Lassesdatter ytter Opdal; in 1720: Johannes Vatne, Harald ytter Opdal, Marthe ytter Opdal, Anna N[iels?]datter ibid, Birthe Lassesdatter ibid. ¶12

Life story

Birth (inference): before about 1700. His eldest daughter Anna married in Nov 1739 and so was born by about 1721, and his eldest son Iver was probably older; a father of a child born by 1721 was born by about 1700. No baptism or census age is known. ¶3

Probate court: lagrettemenn (sworn lay assessors) and appraisers partly read as Lasse Siursen[?], Mons Jo[?]..., Torkild Iversen of Indre Oppedal and Niels Nielsen of Yttre Oppedal. Gross estate about 317 Rd, deductions (Afgang) about 135 Rd (figures uncertain). ¶5

1701 manntall and father (Probable): 'Gullach Nielsen', aged 12, son of 'Niels Andrs:' (Niels Andersen) 54, farmer on Ytre Oppedal, with a brother Matias Nielsen 16 (Manntall 1701, Digitalarkivet 38455, viewer 54, s.91). He is the only Gullak on Ytre Oppedal in 1701; same farm where he died before 28 Jul 1766, and he named his second son Niels. Birth changed from 'BEF 1700' to 'ABT 1689', inferred from the census age; identity Probable (name, patronymic, farm, age). See Niels Andersen. ¶8

Betrothal: at the New Year service in Brekke church, Festo Novi Anni (1 Jan) 1716, after the churching and a royal ordinance on collections for captives in Algiers: 'Trolovet Gullach Nielss: Yttreopdal og Birthe Ifversd: B[...]' (betrothed Gullach Nielsen of Ytre Oppedal and Birthe Iversdatter of B...), 'Test[es] Lasse [his mark] Breche, Jörgen [his mark] Tringlen[?]'. The bride's farm begins with B and the rest is lost under an ink blot on the page edge; Brekke ('Breche', the farm of the first witness) or Bøle are the likely readings, not Indre Oppedal. No marriage (copulation) margin note was seen beside it; the wedding normally followed within a few months. Gulen ministerialbok 1716-1722 (source 11438), canvas 1, left page, upper third. ¶10

Birth and naming: in 1716 he is 'Gullach Nielss.' (Nielsen) of Ytre Oppedal, the patronymic and farm of the 1701 census entry (son of Niels Andersen, aged 12), which further supports Niels Andersen as his father. ¶11

Probate (reading, date): 'Ao 1766 d. 28 og 29 Julij' the court registered, valued and divided at the farm Yttre Oppedal, Ladvig skipreide (Proprietairs gods, a landlord's farm), the estate of the late Dannemand (honest yeoman) Gullach Nielsen, between the widow (Enken) Britte Iversdatter and the 4 sons and 4 daughters. The month was read as May with a query; reads 'Julij' (July): the word has the same shape as 'Julij' in the heading of the very next entry, Nº 69, dated 30 Jul 1766, which opens 'da Skiftet var tilendebragt efter Gullach Nielsen' (when the probate after Gullach Nielsen was concluded). Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll 8b, Nº 68, fol. 392b-394a. ¶1

Death: before 28 Jul 1766, the first day of the registration of his estate. This bound holds whether the month is July or (as first queried) May. No burial has been searched for (next record: Gulen burials 1765-1766, Ytre Oppedal). ¶2

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Rivals: `fleet.py find` for 'Gullak' and 'Gullach' returns no other Gullak Nielsen. Gullach Lassesen (see his page) and Gullach Torkilsen, both of Engesetter in 1701, have other patronymics. Iver Gullacksen of Engesetter (see his page, died before Mar 1774, widow Kari Clausdatter) shares the name of Gullak Nielsen's eldest son Iver, but the son of 1766 is placed at 'Wirkeland[?]', not Engesetter, and lrs_123's father is argued from the 1701 census to be one of the Engesetter Gullachs; they are not assumed to be the same man (untested). ¶7
