Life story

Marriage: Henry Chapman married Elizabeth Herring at Minster, Cornwall, on 19 October 1812 (Cornwall OPC index, from the Phillimore transcript; no ages, witnesses or residences given). Birth BEF 1795 inferred from the marriage. ¶1

Her own baptism is not identified: Elizabeth Herring baptisms of 1780-1793 in the OPC index are at Altarnun and Pillaton only (several fathers); none at Minster or Forrabury. Lead only. ¶4

Herring kin at Murray Harbour (new): the Herring family of Minster and Forrabury, the Boscastle parishes where she married Henry Chapman in 1812, emigrated to Murray Harbour, PEI, like the Chapmans. William Herring and his wife Mary (married Forrabury 1806; possibly her brother, see above) had children baptised there from 1807 to 1830, among them William (Forrabury, 20 Sep 1812), Frances [sic] (Minster, 14 Nov 1821, indexed 'dau'; father a labourer of Minster), Pasco (Forrabury, 10 May 1824; father a carpenter of Minster) and Anne (Minster, 3 Nov 1830) (Cornwall OPC index, from the Exeter bishop's transcripts). In Lot 64 (Murray Harbour), Kings County, PEI, the 1881 census lists William Herring, 68, born England, farmer, Bible Christian (household 285), and Francis Herring, 59, born England, farmer, Bible Christian, with his wife Ellen (household 284); next door (household 286) is Isabella Herring, 58, with sons John, William, Arthur, Pascoe (14) and Daniel. The PEI marriage registers have Francis Herring x Ellen Sencebaugh (25 June 1846), William Herring x Ruhannah Sensebaugh (23 March 1847) and Pascoe Herring x Isabella (10 May 1847). The ages fit the Minster baptisms of William (1812) and Pasco (1824); 'Francis', 59 in 1881, is evidently the child baptised 'Frances' in November 1821 (no other Francis Herring was baptised in the Boscastle parishes). The rare name Pasco in both places makes the identification firm. So the Minster Herrings left Cornwall after November 1830 and settled at Murray Harbour, the same district and the same Bible Christian connexion as Henry Chapman Sen. This is chain migration from Boscastle to Murray Harbour, and it strengthens the identification of Henry Chapman Sen. of Murray Harbour with Henry Chapman, husband of Elizabeth Herring, of Minster. It does not name her or her children, so it is corroboration, not proof. Correction (secondary source): a modern Beach Point local history ('Chester Mackenzie's Book', archive.org item chester-mackenzies-book) says William Herring and his family came from England 'later in the 1860s' and settled on Indian (Gordon's) Island. The PEI marriage registers show Herrings married in Lot 64 in 1846-47, so the family was there twenty years earlier; the book's date is a community memory and is not followed. ¶7a

Herring kin at Murray Harbour, images read (grade unchanged, Probable): 'William Herring Senr' witnessed the marriages of Francis Herring (25 June 1846) and William Herring (23 March 1847), and a William Herring witnessed the marriage of Walter Miller and Mary Herring [indexed 'Herron'] on 31 March 1840 (PEI marriage registers, DGS 004594918, images 252, 591, 624). Mary was baptised at Forrabury in 1815, daughter of William and Mary Herring. So William Herring of Minster (married Mary Parsons, Forrabury 1806), who may have been her brother, emigrated with his children and was on PEI by March 1840. This narrows the Herrings' emigration to between November 1830 and March 1840 and strengthens the chain-migration case, but no record yet names her on PEI. ¶9

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Grade: her link to Mary Jane (Chapman) Creed (see her page) is now Probable, on the same footing as her husband's (see Henry Chapman): it rests on inference (the only Chapman family at Murray Harbour, ages, religion, the naming of Mary Jane's children Elizabeth Chapman Creed and William Henry Creed), with the Minster-to-Murray-Harbour migration of the Herring kin now shown. This supersedes the earlier 'Speculative' wording on this record. ¶8a

Searches that found nothing 2

Origin (negative): no Herring baptism is indexed at Minster, Forrabury, Trevalga, Lesnewth or Tintagel before 1806, so she was not born in the Boscastle parishes. From 1806 a William Herring, who married Mary Parsons at Forrabury that year, baptised children at Minster and Forrabury (1807-1830); he may have been her brother, but nothing links them. Herring families of her generation lived at Altarnun, Jacobstow, Advent, St Clether and North Tamerton in north Cornwall, and at Pillaton in the south-east (Cornwall OPC index). Lead only. ¶5

No burial: no Elizabeth Chapman was buried in the Boscastle parishes or their neighbours (Minster, Forrabury, Davidstow, Trevalga, Lesnewth, Tintagel, Otterham, St Juliot, St Teath) in 1823-1850 (Cornwall OPC burial index). This fits her emigrating with Henry after April 1828, so she could be the mother of Mary Jane (born PEI about 1832), but it is negative evidence only. ¶6

Open questions and the next record to read 3

Lead: the PEI baptisms-marriages-burials index also has a William and Ruth Chapman of Kings County (two children baptised and Ruth, b. c.1825, buried, all 22 Feb 1869; DGS 005240343 images 137, 165); he may be William, bapt. Forrabury 1815, the eldest son of Henry and Elizabeth Chapman. Unread (sign-in S0251). ¶7b

Test record: Mary Jane Creed's PEI death registration, 17 June 1906 (PARO, H0016), which should name both parents. ¶8b

Lead tested: the William and Ruth Chapman of the 1869 baptisms are in the St George's (Anglican) register, Georgetown parish, Kings County (DGS 005240343, image 137): on 22 February 1869 Adeline and Mary Ann, children of William and Ruth Chapman of Mount Stewart, fisherman, were baptised. Mount Stewart is about 40 km from Murray Harbour and the family is Anglican, so nothing ties this William to Henry and Elizabeth Chapman's son William (bapt. Forrabury 1815). Lead weakened; not followed further. ¶10
