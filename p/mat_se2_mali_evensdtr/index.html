About the family

Kari Pedersdatter's mother, named as 'Mali Evensd:' in Kari's baptismal entry of 29 October 1834, and found living in the household thirty-one years later as the holder of the cottage. ¶1

Siblings from the parish register (Selbu ministerialbok 1757-1801), all children of Even Pedersen of Uglem and Karen Povelsdatter, who married on 13 June 1784: Giertrud, baptised at the Annunciation 1786 (apparently died young); Brynild, baptised 5 August 1787; Peder, baptised 14 March 1790; Giertrud, baptised 2 December 1792 - the sister Gjertru still at Uglem in 1865; and Povel, baptised 29 March 1795, who died on 30 April 1795 aged 6 weeks. A younger brother Poul (2 in 1801) followed her. Read. ¶8

Father's death: her father Even Pedersen died at Uglem on 3 July 1812, when she was about 16 (Selbu ministerialbok 1801-1815, p. 176). ¶10

Children: Giertrug, born 23 October 1830, baptised 14 November 1830 (father entered 'Peter Bersvendsen', mother 'Magli Evensdtr', Eidem; Giertrud Pedersdatter), and Kari, born 20 October 1834. ¶18

Life story

Baptism: born 20 June 1796 and baptised on the 5th Sunday after Trinity, 26 June 1796 (Sunday computed from Easter, 27 March 1796): '2) döbt et Pige-Barn kaldet Magli, föd den 20de Juni, Forældren: Even Peders. Uglem og Karen Povels d:' - baptised a girl named Magli (Mali), born 20 June; parents Even Pedersen of Uglem and Karen Povelsdatter. Godparents (faddere): Erich Anders. Uglem, Jon Povelsen Garberg, Anna Erichs d. Uglem, Jertru[d] Anders d. Garberg and Jertru[d] Ole d. Uglem. Selbu ministerialbok 1757-1801, canvas 194 right page (book p. 191), crop. Her mother was churched (introduced) on 24 July 1796 (canvas 195). ¶11

Identity and confidence of the parent link (13 Oct 2026). The chain from Kari's mother to the Uglem child is: (1) Kari's baptism, 29 October 1834, names her mother 'Mali Evensd:' and her father Peder Bersvendsen; (2) the 1865 census has Mali Evensdatter, widow, born 1796 in Selbu, holding Braendhaugen with Kari's husband as her son-in-law; (3) the 1801 census of Selbu has exactly one Magli/Mali Evensdatter born 1793-1798 - a fresh search on the forename Magli returns eleven girls of those years, only one of them an Evensdatter, and Mali/Malene return none - and she is the daughter of Even Pederssen and Karen Poulsdatter at Uglan; (4) this baptism gives that daughter a birth in 1796, the year the 1865 census gives; (5) the bygdebok (Selbu i fortid og naatid vol. 4, p. 76, Uglan, word-box reconstruction, page not read) lists among Even Persen's children 'Mali, 1796' with 'Per' and 'Brennhaugen' in her line, i.e. married to Per of Brennhaugen. Nothing conflicts. The link nevertheless stays Probable on Even and Karen: no primary record yet names the parents of the Mali who married Peder Bersvendsen. See Even Pederssen for what would confirm it. ¶13

1801 census: Selbu, rural residence 0030 UGLAN, household 04, person 022 - 'Magli Evensdatter', aged 5, child of Even Pederssen, 42, 'Bonde og gaardbeboer' (farmer and occupier of the farm), and Karen Poulsdatter, 43, with siblings Brynil (14), Peder (11), Giertrue (9) and Poul (2). Five on 1 February 1801 means born 1795 or 1796, and the 1865 census gives her birth year as 1796: the two agree. She is the ONLY Evensdatter of that age in the whole 1801 census of Selbu - a search on the patronymic returns eight women aged 5, 9, 14, 33, 33, 50, 54 and 62. ¶5

Independent corroboration: her elder sister Gjertru Evensdatter, b. 1792, is still at Uglem in the 1865 census, the same farm sixty-four years later. ¶6

She came off a farm and married a cottager's son - the same downward step her daughter made, and the ordinary story of nineteenth-century Selbu, where the population grew and the number of farms did not. ¶7

Marriage: married in Selbu church on 5 June 1826, Peder's second marriage. Two parallel church books record it. (1) Selbu 1816-1831, pp. 428-429 no. 10 (scan source 16317, canvas 177): groom 'Huusmd. og Enkemd. Peder Bersvendsen Eidem, gl. 36 aar' - cottager and widower Peder Bersvendsen of Eidem, aged 36; bride 'Bonde Datter Pigen Magl. Evensd. Auglan[d], gl. 30 aar' - the farmer's daughter, the maiden Magli (Mali) Evensdatter of Auglan (Uglem), aged 30; forlovere (sureties) 'g.md. Niels Paulsen Garberg' and 'Huusmd. Gunder Johnsen Eidem'; performed in the church; 'Huusmd.' and 'Enkemd.' in the groom's line are read from abbreviations. (2) Selbu 1824-1842, p. 305 no. 10 (scan source 16314, canvas 224): 'Enkem. Peder Bersv. Eidem' and 'Pig. Maglie Evensd. [farm]', both 'Selbo', aged 36 and 30; groom's father 'Bersvend Peders.', bride's father 'Even Peders.'; forlovere 'Gunder Johns. Eid.' and 'Niels Pauls. Garb.'; banns read in April and on 1 May 1826 (column partly read); remark 'Efter afsluttet Skifte' - after the probate (of the first wife's estate) was completed. The bride's farm in book (2) is written with a looped capital that the index renders 'Hylem'; book (1), in a different hand, plainly reads 'Auglan', the form used for Uglem in the 1792 baptism of her sister Giertrud ('Even Pedersen Auglem'). Scans; crops in. The two books agree on date, names, ages and sureties. ¶14

She is the hinge of the whole identification: the woman named as Kari's mother in the 1834 baptism is the woman living as Kari's mother and as Paul's mother-in-law in the 1865 census, which is what proves that the Karen Pedersdatter baptised on 29 October 1834 is Paul Eidem's wife. ¶3

1865 census: Selbu, census district 005 'Femte', rural residence 0065 Braendhaugen. She is person 001, 'Mali Evensdatter', aged 70, born Selbu parish, WIDOW, family position Husmoder, occupation Pladsbrugerske - a woman holding a husmannsplass (cottage holding) in her own right, not through a husband. Her son-in-law Paul Ingebrigtsen is entered beneath her as 'hendes Svigersoen Husfader', her son-in-law, head of the house. That her daughter's husband is recorded as the son-in-law of the holder rather than as the cottager himself is the census's way of saying the old widow still held the land while the younger man worked it. ¶2

Death: she is in the 1865 census and gone from the 1875 census of Braendhaugen, where Paul himself is now named as the holder. She was Peder Bersvendsen's second wife and she outlived him by at least a decade. The only child of hers in the indexed Selbu baptisms is Kari. ¶4

Widowhood: she was a widow by the 1865 census and a cottager's widow at her death in 1872. Her husband's probable burial is 7 June 1855 (died 2 June, aged 65, Eidum; see Peder Bersvendsen). ¶17

Death and burial: 'Mali Evensdr', Husmandsenke (cottager's widow), residence Eidem, aged 75, died 22 January 1872 and buried 28 January 1872 (Church book 1860-1877, burials 1872, p. 41 no. 11; second transcription 1869-1888, p. 316 no. 8). Age 75 fits her birth on 20 June 1796. No other Mali Evensdatter burial is in the Selbu index 1865-1871. ¶15a

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Confidence raised to Confirmed (13 Oct 2026), applying the audit standard of (the adult's own marriage names the baptism farm and the fit is unique). Peder Bersvendsen's second marriage, 5 June 1826, gives his bride as Magli Evensdatter of Auglan (Uglem), aged 30 (born about 1796), a farmer's daughter, and the second church book names her father as 'Even Peders.' (Even Pedersen). Magli, daughter of Even Pedersen of Uglem and Karen Povelsdatter, was baptised on 26 June 1796 (born 20 June); she is the only Magli/Mali Evensdatter born 1793-1798 in the 1801 census of Selbu, and Even was a farmer (gaardmand) at Uglem, matching 'Bonde Datter'. A surety at the wedding was Niels Paulsen (Povelsen) of Garberg, Karen Povelsdatter's brother (baptised 1761), i.e. the bride's maternal uncle. The same Magli Evensdatter is the mother of Giertrug (1830) and Kari (1834) by Peder Bersvendsen of Eidem, and Mali Evensdatter, widow, born 1796, lives with Kari in 1865. The marriage thus ties Kari's mother to the 1796 baptism by name, father, farm and age, with no rival. ¶19

Corrections 4

Correction: birth moved from 'about 1795' to 'about 1796'. Her baptism is not in the register from September 1792 to 24 March 1796, and her brother Povel was born in March 1795, so she was born after about April 1796. The 1865 census gives her birth year as 1796, and the bygdebok skeleton (vol. 4, p. 206) has a 'Mali... 1796,... 1872'. Her age of 5 in February 1801 is about a year high. Her baptism is expected in canvases 191-205 of source 16311 (April 1796 - 1797), not yet read. ¶9

Correction: birth changed from 'about 1796' to 20 June 1796 at Uglem. This is exactly the window the earlier note predicted (after about April 1796, the register having been read to 24 March 1796 without her). The 1865 census birth year 1796 is exact; her 1801 census age of 5 was rounded up (she was four years and seven months old on 1 February 1801). ¶12

Correction: this replaces 'between 1866 and 1875'. ¶15b

Correction: the 1865 census note saying she held the Braendhaugen cottage 'in her own right' overstates the record. The household (persons pf01038330002182-187) is headed by Paul Ingebrigtsen, 40, 'Husfader'; Mali, 70, is entered first, as 'Husmoder', widow (marital status 'e', enke), occupation Pladsbrugerske, with Paul described as 'hendes Svigersoen' (her son-in-law); then Kari, 31, and the children Peder 8, Anne 6 and Ingebrigt 3. She was living with her daughter's family; whether the holding was still in her name is not stated. ¶16
