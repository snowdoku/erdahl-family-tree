Life story

The probate of 20 Apr 1744 at Hielden (Hjellan østre) after Berit Jonsdatter, first wife of Ole Olsen Hielden (24753 fol. 57a, view 75, scan read; poor ink), names as her heirs her own brothers and sisters. They include a sister Kirsti, married to Augustinus Olsen Moenes, and the children of her late brother Arnt Jonsen: Arn Arntsen Hjelden, and Gunild Arntsdatter, wife of Ole Olsen Vangberg. As far as it could be read, the entry does not name a Lars Olsen, so it does not test Frostaboka's statement that Lars was Ole Olsen's brother. The typed register 24753 (1742-60) has no probate for Ole Olsen of Hjellan; his widow Anne Jensdatter remarried Lars in 1751, probably without a probate, so the brother link remains bygdebok-only. ¶10a

Burial: 'Lars Olsen' of Hille, aged 54 1/2 (b. about 1721), buried 16 Jun 1775 at Frosta (Frosta ministerialbok 1750-1778; index pg00000001200734). Name, farm and year fit his probate at Øvre Hille of 23 Aug 1775, and the age agrees with the 1765 legdsrulle (45, b. about 1720) and Frostaboka (b. 1720). He is the only Lars Olsen of that age in the Frosta burial index. Birth estimate left at ABT 1725; the burial and the 1765 roll point to about 1720-21. ¶11

The Kvarme lead re-read (24753 fol. 145b, 8 Jul 1748): Marit Larsdatter, wife of Jens Wigalsen, Qvarme; her children by an earlier husband: 1. Kari Olsdatter, wife of Willum Willumsen [farm unclear]; 2. Ingebor Olsdatter 23; 3. Lars Olsen 21; 4. a married daughter (husband apparently Haagen Jacobsen); 5. Berit, married. Still nothing ties this Lars to Hillan. The godparents at the 1756 baptism of Lars of Hillan's son Ole (Guner of Qvarme, i.e. Anne's brother Gunder Jensen, and Zecel of Walberg, i.e. Lt. Benjamin Gundersen's wife Cecilia) are all from the wife's side; the other three (Anders Alsta, Ole Raflan, Mali Mosta) are unidentified and none matches a name in the 1748 Kvarme probate. ¶7a

Married 1751 at Laugtu (Frosta) church to Anne Jensdatter: groom 'Lars', residence Hillan; bride 'Anne Jensdr'; sureties Lars (Vign) and Colbanus (Jubr); betrothal on the 19th Sunday after Trinity 1751 (index, Frosta 1750-1778). That this is the same Lars of Hillan who fathered Ole in 1756 rests on the farm and the timing. No burial for a Lars of Hillan 1756-1801 is indexed (he appears only as a betrothal surety 1759-1773). ¶3

Lars of Hillan (Hellan/Hjelle), Frosta: father of Ole, baptised at Loctu (Frosta) in 1756 (index; mother not named) - the only Hillan father among the 83 baptisms with a father Lars in Frosta 1750-78. Patronymic unknown; birth year estimated. ¶1

The 1765 probate after his sister-in-law Mallie Jensdatter at Hielden names 'Anne Jens Datter gift med Lars Olsen Jellan' (24755 fol. 495a), a second record for his patronymic and marriage. Lead for his parents (unproved): the probate of 8 Jul 1748 at the soldier's legd farm Qvarme (Kvarme), Frosta, after Marit Larsdatter, wife of Jens Wigalsen, lists her children by an earlier husband (an Ole): 1. Kari Olsdatter, wife of Willum [?], 2. Ingebor Olsdatter 23, 3. 'Lars Olsen 21 aar' (b. c.1727), and two more daughters (24753 fol. 145b, scan read; ages hard to read). Lars was named for his mother's father by the naming custom, and his wife's brother Gunder Jensen later held Kvarme, but nothing yet ties this Lars Olsen to Hillan. ¶6a

In the 1765 manntall- og legdsrulle of the Nordre Stjørdal dragoon company (source 51337, image 52, quarter no. 48 'Øster Hilden', Frosta), the holders are Hans Jonsen, 44, and 'Lars Olsen', 45, both 'tient' (have served); the husmann is Elias Sevaldsen[?], 39, dragoon. Lars Olsen, 45, gives a birth year about 1720 (tree estimate ABT 1725). Vester Hilden (quarter 49) was held by Ole Arnsen, 38. Biographical only: no parent named. ¶8

Frostaboka VIII p. 429 (Hellan, gn. 63; word-box rebuild) gives Lars Olsen as bruker, b. 1720, d. 1775, m. 1751 Anne Jensdtr., with the son Ole Larsen b. 1756, and seems to call him a brother of the previous bruker Ole Olsen (the rebuilt text is gappy: 'Lars Olsen... bror.. Ole'). Ole Olsen's first wife Berit Jonsdatter's probate (24753 fol. 57, 1744) names her own siblings as heirs. ¶9a

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Probable father of Ole Larsen of Hellan (see his page), who was 45 in 1801 and married at Hellan in 1779 as a bachelor. Lead (unverified): a Sigrie Larsdatter, b. 1719, in her second marriage at Hiellan Vester in 1801. ¶2

Upgrade: the probate of 1775 proves him. 'Anno 1775 den 23de Augustij' a registration and valuation was held 'paa Gaarden og Dragon Qvarteret Øver Hilde i Frostens Præstegield' after 'den sammested ved Døden afgangne Mand Lars Olsen' (the man Lars Olsen, who died there). Besides his surviving wife Anne [Jensdatter], he left as his only heir the son born in their marriage, 'Ole Larsen som var 19 aar gl.' (19, so b. about 1756). Lieutenant Benjamin Gundersen acted for the widow Anne Jensdatter; a curator was named for the son (Stjør- og Verdal skifteprotokoll 7, fol. 369b-370a, scan). This ties Lars of Hillan, father of Ole baptised in 1756, to the patronymic Olsen, his wife Anne Jensdatter and his son Ole Larsen (see his page). The skifte register lists it as 'Lars Olsen, Hjellan, Frosta' (24758 fol. 369b). ¶4

The case for this identification 1

Identity: name changed from 'Lars /Hillan/' (a farm label from the baptism index) to 'Lars /Olsen/', the patronymic stated in the 1775 probate. He died before 23 Aug 1775 on Øvre Hillan, a dragoon quarter (dragonkvarter). His son Ole was named for Lars's father Ole (naming pattern, inferred). ¶5
