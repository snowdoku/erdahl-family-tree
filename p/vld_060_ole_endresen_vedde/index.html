About the family

Reusing the name Henning after the infant died is a normal naming practice. ¶2

Life story

Residence: Ole Endresen lived at Vedde (Wedde) in Borgund parish. The Borgund church book index names him (as 'Ole Endress.', an abbreviation of Endresen, son of Endre) as father at Vedde at baptisms on 10 Sep 1699 (Hans), 4 Nov 1703 (Zidzel), 14 Apr 1709 (Henning) and 30 Nov 1710 (Henning), and as 'Ole Endresen' of Vedde as trulovar (betrothal surety) in 1704, 1709 and 1712. From the index; the scans have not been read. ¶1

Wives: an Ole of Vedde had a wife Ingebor who was churched (introdusert, a mother's first return to church after a birth) on 9 Oct 1698, and a wife Sidsel who was churched on 16 Dec 1703 and 26 May 1709. No other Ole is indexed at Vedde 1698-1712 (Ole Einerss. appears from 1713), so Ingebor was probably an earlier wife of this Ole. That is an inference; the mother of Hans (1699) is not established. ¶4

Henning Olsen (not added as a person): the guardian of the widow Sisille Olsdatter in the 1750 probate after Anders Olsen of Hessen was Henning Olsen of a farm read 'H[a]lv[?]estad'. A Henning Ols. was father at Kalvestad, Borgund, at baptisms from 13 Dec 1742 to 16 Mar 1760, including 12 Apr 1750. He may be the Henning baptised to Ole Endresen at Vedde on 30 Nov 1710, which would make him Sidsele's brother, but no record links the Kalvestad man to Vedde, and the farm reading is uncertain. Unproved. ¶6

1701 census (inferred identification): the 1701 manntall (census of males) for Vedde lists one Ole only, 'Oelle', born about 1663, opsider eller leilending (cottager or tenant farmer), with a son Hans Oelsen born about 1700. Hans fits the Hans baptised to Ole Endress. of Vedde in Sep 1699, so Oelle is probably Ole Endresen; his birth year of about 1663 comes from that census age. The census gives him no patronymic, so this is an inference. ¶3

Baptism read on the scan: the Borgund register for the 22nd Sunday after Trinity, 4 Nov 1703, reads 'Ole Endresson Vedde en Zidzel', meaning that Ole Endresen of Vedde had a daughter Zidzel baptised. The godparents were Knud Olsen of Jafnen [farm uncertain], Poul Olsen of Vedde, Berete Andersdatter of Vaagnes (who carried the child), Mette Sørensdatter [patronymic uncertain], a maid at Vaagnes, and Ernder [?] Hansdatter, a maid of Frantzholmen. The mother is not named; this priest wrote only the father. The index row is confirmed: father 'Ole Endresson' of Vedde. Poul Olsen of Vedde also stood godparent at another baptism on 2nd Christmas Day 1703 (same book, p. 169); he may be a kinsman (unproved). ¶7

Probate (scan read): 'Anno 1713 d. 17 Juny' a probate (skifte og deling) was held after the late Ole Endresen Wedde in Borgund skipreide, between his widow (efterladte hustru) 'Sidzelle Hans datter', with her guardian Erich Pedersen [Bruunvagen?], on one side, and 'deris sammen auflede børn' (their jointly begotten children) on the other: Hans Olsen aged 13, Henning Olsen aged [2½?], Ole Olsen aged [4?] (unit unclear), [Linde/Brita?] Olsdatter aged 17, and 'Zidselle Ols datter gammel 10 aar' (Sidsel Olsdatter, aged 10). Fol. 270b, viewer image 24547/276 (urn sk20090123680729). ¶8

Card versus protocol: the typed card 77797/3541 for his probate (Vedde, 17/6-1713) gives the daughter Zidselle Olsdtr. as 6 years old and calls the eldest daughter Brithe (17). The protocol was re-read (24547/276), and it clearly reads 'Zidselle Olsdatter gammel 10 aar'. That agrees with the baptism of 4 Nov 1703, so the card's 6 is a transcription error and the existing identification stands. The guardians were Simen Riise, Jetmund Aarset and Peder Vidahl. ¶11

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Confidence is Probable: he is the father of Sidsele Olsdatter by a strong index fit with no contradiction. The 1731 marriage index says Sidsele Olsdatter was born at Wedde (Vedde); Olsdatter means daughter of Ole, and 'Endress.' is Ole's own patronymic, so it is exactly what is expected and not a conflict. He is the only Ole indexed as a father at Vedde 1698-1712, and Zidzel (1703) is the only Sidsel baptism with a Vedde father in Borgund 1698-1715 (the other Sidsel of 1703 was the daughter of Arne Olsen of Skarpøvigen). Born 1703, she was 28 at her marriage in 1731. ¶5a

Upgrade: the 1713 probate names his widow Sidsel Hansdatter and their joint children, among them Zidselle Olsdatter aged 10 (born about 1703, matching the baptism of Zidzel to Ole Endresen of Vedde on 4 Nov 1703) and Henning Olsen (matching the 1710 baptism). Hans Olsen aged 13 matches the son Hans baptised 1699/1700 and listed with him in the 1701 manntall. This is his own primary record, read on the scan, tying him to the daughter in the tree. ¶9
