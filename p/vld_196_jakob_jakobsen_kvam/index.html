About the family

Father of Guro (Goro) Jakobsdatter (see her page), graded on his link to her: Probable (compiled source only). The Lesja bygdebok (vol. 1, canvas 141, Sør-Kvam entry 4-2, word-box rebuild) makes him 'Jakob Jakobs., ca 1604 - ca. 1674', user of Sør-Kvam from 1628, with children alive 1674 including '2. Guro, 1642, sjå Einbu (1-3)', the Einbu entry of Bjerte and Guro Jakobsd. The year 1642 agrees exactly with the burial index ('Goro Jacobsd', Eneboe, bur. 13 Dec 1733, b. 1642) and her patronymic with his name. He is also the bygdebok's father of '3. Alf, ca. 1650, tok over' (see his page), whose daughter Brit was the mother of Alf Olsen of Nystuen (see his page), so he is an ancestor twice over. His wife's name falls in a gap of the rebuild (she held the farm from 1628, so probably the heiress; not entered). Dates ABT (word-box rebuild). ¶1a

Life story

Nord-Gudbrandsdal probate cards (39945) for Kvam in Lesja run from image 7519 to about 7600. The first run is in date order: 7519 1670 Ingelbret Lauridsen (fol. 217b, 1658-73), 7521 1681 Elli Amundsdatter, 7523 1681 Oluf Biertesen, 7525 1696 Jon Ingelbretsen, 7527 1708 Bethe Lauridsdatter (Nordre Kvam), 7529 1720 Rønnaug Tostensdatter (Søndre Gudbrandsdal 1682-1721 fol. 233a), 7531 1726 Endre Qvam, 7533 1746 Kvam nordre, 7535 1757 Kvam nordre, 7537 1765, 7539 1777, 7541 1797, 7543 1808; later images mix in a second 'A.' series (7563 1838, 7565 Kvam i Lesje 1824-43 fol. 645a, 7569 Kvam søndre 1721-36 fol. 310a, 7573 Kvam søndre 1752-64 fol. 285a, 7581 Kvam søndre 1836). No card for a skifte about 1674 or about 1715 was seen in 7519-7543, 7561-7591 (odd images sampled). So the test record for Jakob (skifte about 1674) is still not found; the unsampled even/odd images 7545-7559 and 7593-7600 and the card for Kvam søndre 1721-36 (7569) remain to read. ¶2

Kvam cards 39945/7545-7553 were read: all are from protocol 1824-1843 (Kvam nordre 1832 and Kvam 1837-38, Endre Olsen and his family), none early. Card 7569 (Kvam søndre i Lesje, 'Qvam', Søndre Gudbrandsdal 1721-36 fol. 310a, 30 Sep 1728) is the skifte after Gjertrud Olsdatter, married 2nd Knud Jacobsen (dead) and 1st Jon Engelbretsen; children Guri 25 (by Knud) and Engelbret 41, Ole 37, Lars 35, Jon 32, Anne 39 (by Jon); this is the Jon Ingelbretsen family of the 1696 skifte, not Alf Jakobsen's. So no card for a skifte after Jakob (about 1674) or Alf (about 1715) exists in the Kvam run; the remaining route is reading protocols 25154 (1673-76) and 25155 (1711-19) page by page. ¶3

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
