About the family

Mother of Paul (Povel) of Indre Hildre (see his page), Probable; graded on her link to Paul. Two independent bygdebøker agree: Vatne bygdebok 1 p. 312-313 (canvases 313-314, rebuilt) has Didrik Nilssen 'gift med Ingeborg', who outlived him and 'døydde ca. 1667', who held land at 'Indre-Hildre', and whose son Paul became brukar of Hildre 'som han hadde [arva] av mor si'; the Haram bygdebok Band 5 p. 175 (Indre Hildre bruk 4-4) independently says Paul 'arva' a share of the farm after 'mor si, Ingeborg' with a cross-reference to the Vatne book ('V-sl 1-312'). Death ABT 1667 from rebuilt text. Her patronymic is in a gap ('Ingeborg... dotter...', canvas 313 y 2659); not recovered with 45 common patronymic phrase queries. ¶1

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
