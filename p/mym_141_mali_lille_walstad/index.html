About the family

Wife of Peder (Lille) Walstad, Skatval. Named as mother ('Mod. Mali') of Peder, baptised 11 Jul 1723, and churched as 'Peder Lillevalstads [hustru] Mali' on 29 Aug 1723 (Stjørdal 16368, pp. 172, 174). Patronymic not given. Probable mother of Peder Pedersen Walstad (b. 1723), on the same identification as her husband. Peder Pedersen's daughter Mali (b. c.1771) may carry her name, though his wife was also a Mali. ¶1

Life story

The marriage of Peder and Mali of Lille Walstad is not in the Stjørdal betrothal book 16367 for Jan 1714 - Aug 1719 (canvases 1-29 read in full; canvases 30-44, Aug 1719 - May 1721, were). With their first known child born in Feb 1722, they must have married outside Stjørdal parish (in the bride's home parish) or before 1714. An indexed search of all marriages 1708-21 with a bride Mali found no Skatval-area groom Peder; the nearest is Peder of Salotter x Mali Olufsdatter of Grav at Ytterøy, 11 Jan 1711, which nothing ties to Walstad. ¶4

The marriage of Peder and Mali of Lille Walstad is not in the forlover book 16367 for Aug 1719 - Apr 1721 (canvases 30-44 read). The only Skatval pair Peder x Mali there is 'Peder Lars[?] Aijke[?]', a soldier of Capt. Heideman's company, and 'Pigen Mali Hansdatt. Hammer', married at Skatval on the 16th Sunday after Trinity 1720 (canvas 37); nothing ties that couple to Lille Walstad, so it is a Speculative candidate only and is not used. Also at Lille Walstad: 'Enkemand Hans Pedersøn Lille Valstad', a dragoon, betrothed Oct 1719 to Ingeborg Haagensdatter (canvas 31) - perhaps a relative (brother or father) of Peder; untested. ¶3a

Bygdebok (Skatval vol. 1, canvas 237 left): 'G. 15.09.1720 [m.] Mali Hansdtr. [...] før 1739' as wife of Peder Larsen, Lille Valstad, with children Anne 1721, Marit 1722, Peder 1724. Gives the patronymic Hansdatter (book only). The 'før 1739' may be her death limit; the tree has her dead before Aug 1724 from the second marriage of 1725. ¶9

See the note on her husband (Peder Larsen) on the 1665-66 Lille Valstad households; nothing there bears on her own parents. ¶11

An older child of the couple found: 'Døbt Peder Lillewalstads b. Marrit. Mod. Mali', Sexagesima Sunday [15 Feb] 1722, godparents Jon Valstad, Haagen Vold, Mali Vollum, Marrit Vold, Ane Vold (Stjørdal 16368 canvas 93); the mother was churched at Skatval on Lætare Sunday 1722 as 'Peder Lillewalstads h[ustru] Mali' (canvas 94). So Peder Pedersen (b. 1723) had an elder sister Marit, and Peder and Mali were married by about May 1721. Their marriage is not in Mar 1721 - Dec 1722 (canvases 84-103 read in full); look in 16368 before Mar 1721 (canvases 0-83, 1714-early 1721). The godfather Jon Valstad (of Store Walstad?) may be a relative of her husband's family; not tested. ¶2

A second marriage at Lille Walstad, probably Peder's. 16368 canvas 134 left, Palm Sunday [21 Mar] 1725, at Skatval: 'Copuler: Peder Larss. Lillevalstad og Karen Arnsdatt.' (Peder Larsen of Lille Walstad married Karen Arnesdatter). Peder Lillewalstad x Mali had children in Feb 1722 and Jul 1723, and on the 8th Sunday after Trinity 1724 'Mali Hansdatter Lillevalstad' was buried at Værnes (canvas 124 right; the age, read '88[?]', looks at 4000 px like '83' or '33' - the two digits are near-identical loops and cannot be settled). A godmother 'Kari Lille Walstad' appears at Christmas 1724 (canvas 128), three months before the wedding. If the age is 33, the burial is Peder's wife Mali (b. c.1691), her patronymic was Hansdatter, and the widower Peder (Larsen) remarried Karen Arnesdatter in 1725; Peder Pedersen's patronymic would then make Peder Lillewalstad's father a Lars. But the farm also had other households in 1725 (churching of 'Ole Johans[?]. Lillevalstads h. Brynnille' at Skatval, canvas 132 right; burial of 'Ingeborg Torstensd. Lillevalstad 63 aar', mid 1725, canvas 137 right), and no 1725-27 baptism (canvases 132-158 read) shows Peder Lillewalstad with either Mali or Karen. The church-book entry alone does not prove the identity; the next note (betrothal book 16367, canvas 63) supplies the missing link. Also checked: no Lillevalstad card in the probate card index 77804 L section (views 1233-1243), and earlier Valstad cards are Verdal only. ¶5

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the identification · 2 searches with no result · 1 open question

The case for this identification 1

The chain behind Peder's patronymic Larsen and Mali's patronymic Hansdatter. (1) 16367 canvas 37: on Judica Sunday 1720 a soldier of Capt. Heideman's company, 'Peder Lars[?] Aijke[?]', was betrothed to 'Pigen Mali Hansdatt. Hammer' (an unmarried girl), and they married at Skatval on the 16th Sunday after Trinity 1720. (2) Peder Lillewalstad and his wife Mali had children baptised at Skatval in Feb 1722 (Marit) and Jul 1723 (Peder) (16368 canvases 93, 110). (3) On the 8th Sunday after Trinity 1724 'Mali Hansdatt. Lillevalstad' was buried at Værnes (16368 canvas 124 right). The age is two near-identical loops; read as 33, she was born about 1691, and that is the only reading that fits a girl married in 1720 (83 would not). (4) 16367 canvas 63 right, 18 Oct 1724: 'Soldat og Enkemand af Hr Capit. Heidmans Compagnie ved Nafn Peder Larsøn Ajcle[?]' (a soldier and widower of the same Capt. Heideman's company) asked to be betrothed at Værnes to 'en Pige af Nafn Karen Arentzdatt.'. The margin note says they were married 'Dom. Palmar. 1725 ved Skatvold'. (5) The marriage itself is in the church book on that same Palm Sunday 1725 at Skatval: 'Copuler: Peder Larss. Lillevalstad og Karen Arnsdatt.' (16368 canvas 134 left). So the soldier Peder Larsen of Heideman's company, who married Mali Hansdatter in 1720, was a widower by Oct 1724 and was 'Peder Larsen Lillevalstad' in 1725. That fits him being the Peder Lillewalstad whose wife Mali bore children in 1722-23 and who was widowed when Mali Hansdatter of Lille Walstad was buried in Jul 1724. The links are the same name, the same company, the widowhood and the dates; no other Peder x Mali couple at Lille Walstad has been found. No single record names Peder Larsen as the father of the 1723 child, so this stays Probable. The names are changed to Peder Larsen and Mali Hansdatter on that basis. Mali's father was a Hans; her 1720 residence was Hammer (Skatval), which is the lead for her parents. ¶6

Searches that found nothing 2

Negatives and context for her father Hans: (1) The Stjør- og Verdal probate card index 1689-1742 (77804) has only these Hammer cards: Ellef Joensen Hammer, Åsen, 1738 (view 663); Joen Olsen Hammer, dragoon rider, 1738, widow Siri Larsdatter, children Ole 12, Lars 8, Jon 6, Guri 3 (view 665; filed under Hegra but annotated 'Rygh: no farm Hammer in Hegra, but in Skatval'); Jon Jonsen Hammer, Selbu, 1701 (view 667); then Hammersberg (view 669). No card for a Hans of Hammer, so no probate after her father survives in 1689-1742 under that farm. (2) Hammer in Skatval was the seat of the Heideman family (a 1770s probate in 24758 is of a cotter 'under Heidemans enke, Hammer, Skatval'), and her first husband was a soldier of Capt. Heideman's company. So 'Pigen Mali Hansdatter Hammer' in 1720 may have been a servant on the captain's farm, not a daughter of the farm; Hammer is a weak lead for her parents. (3) A candidate father not yet tested: the dragoon 'Enkemand Hans Pedersøn Lille Valstad', betrothed Oct 1719 (16367 canvas 31). The couple settled on Lille Walstad by 1722; if that was her father's holding, it would explain why. Speculative only; not added. (4) The 1701 manntall for Stjørdal fogderi is not in the Digitalarkivet person index (no. 14 covers Strinda and Selbu, no. 15 Inderøy and Namdal), so the Hans households of 1701 cannot be searched there. ¶7

Negative: a different Mali Hansdatter is in the probate card index 77804 (view 1455): at the probate of 20 Feb 1742 after Haftor Hansen of Mælesmarken, a plass under Mæle, Stjørdal (skifteprotokoll 2 (1727-1742) fol. 1093b), his heirs are his sisters Mildri Hansdatter (married to Niels Mahrsgjerde), Marit Hansdatter, and the children of the deceased sister Mali Hansdatter, wife of Erich Bolchen: Bendi Erichsdatter, 14, whose guardian was her father. That Mali bore a daughter about 1728, after our Mali Hansdatter of Lille Walstad was buried (1724), so she is not ours, and this family is not hers. No probate in 77804 (Hammer, Lillevalstad, Mæle) names our Mali or her father Hans. ¶8
