About the family

Son Iver (Probable): mother of Iver Lassesen of Indre Oppedal (see his page), born about 1651. For: (1) in 1645 she was the wife of Lasse of Opedahl, who is taken to be the farmer Lasse on Indre Oppedal in 1664 with his son Ifver 13; Lasse is the only Lasse on Opedahl in 1645 (the others there are Bendigt, Colbenn, Laurids, Jenns, Olle and the husmann Christopher Joensen); (2) Iver was born only about six years after the poll tax; (3) Iver named a daughter Torbjørg ('Torbiør', 'Torberg' in the 1699 and 1701 probates), the female form of her father's name Torbjørn (Torben), alongside a son Lasse after his father. ¶2a

Life story

1645 poll tax: 'Sigrj Thorbensdtr' (Sigri Torbensdatter, i.e. Torbjørnsdatter) is listed on Opedahl, Brekke, Eivindvik prestegjeld, in the section of farmers and their wives, directly after the farmer 'Lasse'; the list pairs each husband with his wife, so she was Lasse's wife in 1645. Birth before about 1627 is inferred only from her being a married woman taxed in 1645. ¶1

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 record that disagrees · 1 open question

Records that disagree 1

Against or open: no record names Iver's mother; Lasse could have remarried between 1645 and 1651; the daughter Torbjørg could also be named for someone on his wife Eli Monsdatter's side. ¶2b

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Would confirm: an Indre Oppedal probate after Sigri or Lasse (before the first Ytre Sogn protocol of 1698, so only in the unindexed court journals), or the 1666 vicar's list for Eivindvik if it survives elsewhere (it is not in 35518 or 35519). ¶2c
