Life story

1801 census: Sigrid Larsdatter, 64, wife (dennes hustru) of Torstein Iverssen at Engesætter, Brekke, married for the first time, mother of the joint children Iver, Lars, Mari, Martha, Brite, Kari and Ragnilde. Birth about 1737 is inferred from her age. ¶1

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Probable mother of Mari Torsteinsdatter (see her husband's note for the reasoning). ¶2

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed (upgrade sweep D), as parent of Mari Torsteinsdatter. Three records name or fix the link. (1) Her baptism at Brekke on 27 Mar 1778, 'Torsten Iwersen Engesetters D[atter] Nom: Mari' (Gulen ministerialbok 1776-1787, fol. 15a), names Torstein Iversen of Engesetter as her father; this register does not name mothers. (2) The 1801 census of Brekke (index, relationship word checked) lists Mari Torsteinsdatter, 24, unmarried, as 'Felleds datter' (daughter of both spouses) of Torstein Iverssen, 67, and Sigrid Larsdatter, 64, at Engesætter, household 02. (3) She married from Engesetter on 27 Jun 1802 (Torkild Monssøn of Indre Oppedal and Mari Torsteinsdatter, Gulen marriages 1802, index), and her second son was named Torstein; Iver Tostensen of Engesætter, the 1801 son Iver, was guardian of her youngest son at her husband's probate in 1809. For Sigrid the naming record is the 1801 census word 'Felleds datter', which states that Mari was a child of both spouses, not a stepdaughter; Torstein was in his first marriage ('gift 1ste gang'). ¶3
