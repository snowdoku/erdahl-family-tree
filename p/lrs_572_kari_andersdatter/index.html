Life story

Name and marriage: she is 'Kari Anders-datter', wife of Iver Gutormsen of Indre Oppedal, at her churching after a son Lars in 1706 (Gulen 11435 c33), and 'Kari Andersdr:' as mother at the baptism of her son Anders at Brekke on 10 Apr 1711 (11436 c23; the year is contested, 1711 or 1712), and 'Karj' as mother of a second Anders baptised at Brekke on 11 Nov 1712 (11436 c42 left page). On 17 Feb 1709 (Invocavit Sunday) 'Ifvers Indreopdals qvinde Karj' (Kari, wife of Iver of Indre Oppedal) was churched at Brekke after a boy named Torchel (11436 c5). ¶1

Churching, 2 Jan 1718: at the service in Brekke church on 2 Jan 1718: 'Introduceret Ifver Gutormss: Inderopdalens qv[in]de Kari, efter at [hun fø]dt[?] barn N: Lasse' (churched the wife Kari of Iver Gutormsen of Indre Oppedal after a child named Lasse). So Kari was alive on 2 Jan 1718 and had borne a son Lasse in late 1717; the son Lars of 1706 had probably died, since the name was reused. ¶3

Alive 1720: 'Iver Gutorms: Inder opdals qvinde Kari' was churched in Jul 1720 after the birth of a son Johannes (Gulen 11438 c48 L, c49 L). This argues against identifying her with the Kari Andersdatter of Indre Oppedal buried aged 39 on 10 Dec 1717; her patronymic is not given in 1718 or 1720. ¶5

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 alternative set aside · 1 open question

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Not her burial: on 10 Dec 1717 a 'Kari Andersdr: Indreopdal', aged 39, was buried at Brekke (Gulen 11438 c20 left, with Johannes Larsen of Indre Oppedal). Name, patronymic and farm match her, but she cannot be this Kari if the churching three weeks later (2 Jan 1718) is of the same woman, as the churching names Iver Gutormsen's wife Kari as living. Either two women named Kari Andersdatter lived on Indre Oppedal (for example a daughter of Anders Larsen, 60, of Indre Oppedal in 1701) or the churching names a second wife Kari. Unresolved; no death date is set. ¶4a
