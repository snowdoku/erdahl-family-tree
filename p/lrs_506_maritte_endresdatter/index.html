About the family

Parents and death (bygdebok, Probable): Brekke herred bygdebok vol. 2 (1944), Botnen bnr. 78 (canvas 884): Eilef's wife '[Marite] Endresdtr.' d. 1739 aged 82 (b. about 1657), daughter of Endre Jørgenson and Agate Andersdtr.; the 1701 division after Endre is cited. The forename itself falls in a gap of the rebuilt text. Linked parents Endre Jørgensen and Agate Andersdatter. ¶4

Parents' probate read: Endre Jørgensen of Sognes, 2 Jun 1701 (Ytre Sogn 1b fol. 343b), names among the children of Endre and his widow Aagaatha Andersdatter 'Mar[...] Eillefs Botnes' (forename cut by the binding). This confirms the bygdebok parents. Her own grade (her link to Lisbeth Eilevsdatter) is unchanged. ¶5

Life story

Probate of her husband: she was the widow ('efterladte Hustrue Maritte Endres datter') of Eilef (Elløf) Olsen of Botten at his probate of 20 Jun 1730, and took half the estate; the children named are hers with him ('hendes med den Sal. Mand Sammen auflede børn'): Ole, Giertrud (dead), Elisabeth and Brithe. Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll 3b, fol. 433b-434a. ¶1

Alive on 20 Jun 1730. Her patronymic makes her a daughter of an Endre; her granddaughter by Giertrud was also named Maritte Endresdatter. Nothing else is known of her. ¶2

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Confidence: Probable as the mother of Lisbeth Eilevsdatter (see her page); the 1730 probate states that Elisabeth was her daughter, so the grade rests on whether that Elisabeth is our Lisbeth (see the parent note on Lisbeth). ¶3
