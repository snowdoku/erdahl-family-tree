Life story

Death: 'Thore Throndsen', aged 85 (b. about 1739), died 20 Mar and was buried 23 May 1824 at Treungen (Nissedal ministerialbok, Treungen 1814-1862, p. 101, no. 6; index pg00000006881850). No farm is given, but the age fits his 1801 census age of 60 at Felle within two years, and he is one of only two Tore Tronsens born 1735-1747 in the whole burial index (the other is outside Telemark). Birth kept at ABT 1741 (census); the burial gives ABT 1739. ¶5

Betrothed 30 Mar 1781 at Tvedsund, Treungen, as bachelor 'Thore Tronsen Fælle' to Hæge Toruvsdatter Hiemdal; married in Treungen church 24 Jun 1781 (2nd Sunday after Trinity) by royal licence, the couple being blood relatives in the second degree (Kviteseid ministerialbok 1773-1786, image 130, p.125). ¶2

1801 census, Felle (Kviteseid prestegjeld): husband, aged 60, both in their first marriage, with wife Hæge Toruvsdatter (43). Children: Gunild (19), Anne (15), Gunild (10), Ingebor (6) and Toruv (2). Also in the household: his mother Gunild Andersdatter (84, widow) and Astri Thronsdatter (62). ¶1

Upgrade: named as 'Tore Tronsøn 16 aar gl.' at the probate of his father Tron Toresøn Fælle, 19 Nov 1757, with his mother Gunnild Andersdaatter as widow (Øvre Telemark skifteprotokoll nr. 9, skifte no. 244). Age 16 in 1757 matches 60 in 1801, and the 1801 Felle household holds the same mother (Gunild Andersdatter, 84, widow) and sister (Astri, 62; 19 in 1757). This proves both parent links. ¶4
