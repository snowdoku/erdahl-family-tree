About the family

Husband of Groe Evindsdatter, daughter of Even (Evind) Tellefsen Ramse, and father of Tellef Nielsen Tveit (estate settled 1703), Olle Nielsen Tveit and Rolf Nielsen Tveit. ¶1

He is named only as "Nils" on the Østre Råbyggelag card abstracting the Ramse deed of 28 February 1704: "Groe Evindsdtr. g.m. Nils. Deres barn: Olle Nielsen Tveit, Rolf [Nielsen Tveit], Groe Nielsdtr. Høgeli, Thargier [Nielsdtr.] g.m. Sofren Omundsen." His own patronymic and farm of origin are unknown. ¶2

Life story

1645 poll tax (DA 76706 view 122, p.14 'Lille Thopdal', Tveit as a half farm): '[O?]leuff och hans quinde och en pige, Niels och hans quinde, Niels Oelsen och hans quinde'. So two married men named Niels lived at Tveit from at least 1645 to 1661, one of them called Niels Olsen in both lists, the other named without a patronymic. The 1610 tax list (DA 100550) has at Tveit ('Thuedt'/'Thuede') Annond, Oluff and Rolff. ¶11

1655-56 odelsjordebok, Lille Topdal section (DA 76715 view 23, right page): two entries for a man of Tveit, 'O[?leff] Tued eyer i N[?idre]dall Ramsse ¼ b., i Moen i Omblj ¼ b.' and 'O[?leff] Tued eyer i Søndre Skiegdall ¼ b.'. The forename is not certain; Niels is not legible there. If the farm is Nedre (Ytre) Ramse, this is odel land at Ramse held from Tveit by 1655, which fits Groe Evindsdatter's inherited huder; not linked. ¶7

1661 land register (Alminnelig jordebok for Råbyggelag, Lille Topdal, fol. 58b, DA 38714 view 63, read on the scan): 'Tued 3 huder': owners Åmli prestebol, 'Olle Tued' (7 g[?]sk. 1 k.sk.), Niels (3 g[?]sk.) and a Tølle[?] (2 k.sk.); users 'Niels bruger 1 hud 4 k.sk., Tallach bruger 8 k.sk. oc føder deris fader [i ?]' (Niels and Tallak work their shares and keep their father) and 'Niels Tel[l]sønn bruger ½ hud'; a husmann Tiøll. So two men named Niels worked Tveit in 1661: one a brother of Tallak whose father (probably the owner Olle Tveit) was still alive and kept by his sons, the other a Niels Tel[l]søn (probably Tellefsen; the letters read 'Teelsønn'). Either could be this Niels (60 in 1664, son Rolf 20). His sons Tellef and Olle Nielsen fit either grandfather. Not resolved; next, the 1664 bailiff's census for Tveit (DA 35567 view 28, Lille Topdal, with patronymics in the sons' column) and the 1664 vicar's census Tveit entry read in full for a second Niels or a Tallak. The 1655 odelsjordebok's two 'O[lle] Tued' entries (see above) are this Olle Tveit. ¶8

1664 vicar's census, Lille Topdal p.14, 'Tued 3 Huder': opsiddere Niels 60, Thollach 30, Tolleff 29[?], Kiøll 37, Aso[?] Effuindsdatter; sons Kiøstell 14 and 'Rolff Nielssøn er 20 Aar'; husmænd Valde 38, Oluff 77. 1666 list (35510/34): Niels 62. This Niels was born about 1604, not 1635. Rolff Nielssøn (b. c.1644) has the name of Rolf Nielsen Tveit of card 10365, but that conflicts with Groe Evindsdatter's estimated birth (1645-55): either this Niels is the tree's Nils and Groe's dating is wrong (or she was a second wife), or the tree's Nils is a younger man. Unresolved. ¶5

1664 bailiff's census (DA 35567 view 28, p.19): this list gives only men under 50. Tveit ('Tued'): Tallach 32, Tiøl[?] 36, Tellef 22; in the sons/servants column next to it only Halfuor Pedersøn 20. Niels (60 in the vicar's census) is excluded by age, and his son Rolf (20 in the vicar's census) is not listed here under any patronymic, so the census does not say whose son Niels was. Reasoning from it: Tallak, 32 here, is the Tallak of the 1661 land register who with a brother Niels 'føder deris fader'; a brother of a 32-year-old is unlikely to be 60, so Niels Tveit (b. about 1604) is probably not that Niels but the other one, 'Niels Tel[l]sønn', or else the old father himself (the vicar's census has a husmann Oluff 77 at Tveit, who fits the owner 'Olle Tued' kept by his sons). Not resolved. ¶9

What this means for his father (reasoning, not proved): by 1664 only one Niels remained (Niels 60, vicar's census), with a husmann Oluff 77. The Niels without a patronymic was, in 1661, a brother of Tallak (b. about 1632) and both kept their father; a brother of Tallak is unlikely to be born about 1604. That leaves two readings: (a) this Niels (b. about 1604, son Rolf b. about 1644) is Niels Olsen, son of an Ole of Tveit (the 1610 Oluff or the owner Olle Tued); or (b) he is the Niels without a patronymic, and the 1661 brother pair is Niels Olsen and Tallak, sons of the old Olle. His sons were named Tellef, Olle and Rolf; Olle fits (a), and Rolf fits the 1610 holder Rolff. No record names his father, so no parent is added. In the 1664 vicar's census (DA 35510 view 18, p.14) the Tveit opsiddere are Niels 60, Tallach 30, Tolleff 29, Kiøll 37 and a woman 'A[s]o Effuindsdatter'; a woman holder is usually a widow, so she may be the widow of whichever Niels had died by 1664 (not proved). Negative: the 1657 contribution roll for Råbyggelag (DA 76716 views 256-268) gives farm names and skyld only, no persons. ¶12a

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the identification · 3 corrections · 1 open question

The case for this identification 1

The identification as the father of our ancestor Tellef Nielsen Tveit is Probable: two of the sons on this card, Olle and Rolf Nielsen Tveit, are exactly the two men named as the deceased's brothers in Tellef Nielsen's 1703 probate at Tveit. Tellef himself is missing from the 1704 list because he had died the year before. ¶3

Corrections 3

Correction: The Ramse deed that names him (card 10365) was registered on 28 July 1704 ("tgl. 28/7 1704"), not 28 February 1704. ¶4

Correction: birth changed from ABT 1635 to ABT 1604, from his ages in the 1664 and 1666 vicar's censuses (60 and 62). The age conflict with his wife Groe Evindsdatter is resolved by the Tovdal bygdebok, which makes Gro a daughter of Eivind Ramse's first wife Signe (d. about 1639; 'Gro... Nils... Tveit'), so she was born before 1639, not 1640-60. The 1664 son 'Rolff Nielssøn 20' is then very probably Rolf Nielsen Tveit, Groe's son of card 10365. ¶6

Correction: the second Niels in the 1661 land register (DA 38714 view 63, fol. 58b, re-read at full resolution) is 'Niels Oelsønn bruger j hud', i.e. Niels Olsen, not 'Niels Tel[l]sønn'. The Tveit entry reads: owners Åmli prestebol, 'Olle Tued eyer 7 g.sk. j k.s.', 'Niels eyer 3 g.sk.', 'Tøll[?] eyer 2 k.s.', each with his own bygsel; users 'Niels bruger j hud 4 k.s., Tallach bruger 8 k.s. och føder deris fader', and 'Niels Oelsønn bruger j hud'. ¶10
