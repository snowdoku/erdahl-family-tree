Life story

Found by the probate-by-farm method: Ryfylke probates are not in the Digitalarkivet probate index (domicile=Tuntland, Fister*, Øye return no Ryfylke rows), so the typed farm register at the front of D 6 (DA 23890, 1767-1774) was read letter by letter. That volume is labelled 'Karmsund' but holds Hjelmeland, Jelsa, Årdal, Sjernerø (which includes Fister farms such as Byre and Bukken) and the other Ryfylke skipreider, and no earlier pass had read it. ¶1

Known from his children's patronymic and from the 1771 probates on Nord Øye (D 6 fol. 350a-b and 352b): Susanna Jacobsdatter's children of her first marriage are all Olsen/Olsdatter (an adult son read as [Niels] Olsen, and daughters aged 18 and 14 in 1771, so born about 1753 and 1757), and her daughters Kari and Siri Olsdatter (died before Jan 1771) also had paternal half-sisters Marthe Olsdatter (35) and K[irsten?] Olsdatter (33), so Ole was married before, about 1735. He died after about 1756 and before the 1762 ekstraskatt, which shows his widow remarried to Søren Sørensen at Nord Øye. No probate after him is in the typed registers 23971 (1731-54) or 23973 (1757-66). ¶2

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
