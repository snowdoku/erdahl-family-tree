About the family

Son of John P. and Oline (Slind) Eidem, and therefore brother of Palmer Gilbert Eidem. 1950 census: 'Peter C., son, W M 40, never married, born Minnesota', living with his parents in household 8, Mehurin Township, Lac qui Parle County, Minnesota, enumeration district 37-25, sheet 2, line 22, and working. Still at home at forty, which is the usual shape of the son who stays on the family farm. ¶1

Life story

A collateral relative recorded because the Eidem households in this one township are the evidence that proves the links between the generations. Find a Grave's listing for Zoar Lutheran Cemetery in Deuel County includes both a Peder P. and a Peder Conrad Eidem; whether either is this man is not established. ¶2

Minnesota death index: born 6 October 1909, 'child of SLIND (mother)', died 10 December 1995 in Chippewa County, Minnesota. SSN [number withheld] (Minnesota); claim filed at Marshall, Minnesota 12 August 1971; last ZIP [withheld] (Montevideo). ¶4

Baptism, the record located but not yet read: born 6 October 1909 in Lac qui Parle County, Minnesota, his baptism falls within the pastorate of the Rev. A. O. Utheim (1907-12) over the Marietta call of the United Norwegian Lutheran Church, comprising Augusta congregation at Marietta and Mehurin congregation. Augusta's ministerial records, 1898-1927, are on Minnesota Historical Society microfilm M678, reel 44 [ALC 129]. He is buried at Zoar Lutheran Cemetery, the churchyard of the Hauge Synod Zoar congregation of 1896, whose own ministerial register of 1895-1957 is on the same reel. ¶7

Social Security, retirement claim: PEDER C. EIDEM, born 6 Oct 1909, filed a life claim at the MARSHALL, MINNESOTA Social Security field office in 1971 — the year he turned 62, the earliest age for a reduced retirement benefit. Marshall is the field office that served Lac qui Parle and Chippewa counties. NARA AAD, NUMIDENT Claim Files (Last Names D-G), rid 1352303. ¶8

Social Security death record: PEDER C EIDEM, b. 6 Oct 1909, d. 10 Dec 1995, residence ZIP [withheld], which is MONTEVIDEO, Chippewa County, Minnesota. Posted 19 Dec 1995. NARA AAD, NUMIDENT Death Files (Last Names E-G), rid 300294. This agrees exactly with the tree's 'Chippewa County' and narrows it to Montevideo. The spelling in the federal file is 'PEDER C', not 'Peter C' as the 1950 census enumerator wrote it. ¶9

Birth certificate: State of Minnesota, Division of Vital Statistics, Record of Birth no. 12539, registered no. 27/12, County of Lac qui Parle, Township of Mehuren. 'Full name of child: Peder Conrad Eidem. Sex Male. Single. Number in order of birth 5. Legitimate yes. Date of birth Oct 6, 1909. FATHER: full name John P. Eidem, residence Township of Mehuren, colour White, age at last birthday 33, birthplace Norway, occupation Farmer. MOTHER: full maiden name Oline Eidem, colour White, age at last birthday 28, birthplace Norway, occupation Housekeeper. Number of child of this mother 5. Number of children of this mother now living 5.' There being no attending physician, his father signed the return himself, at Marietta. Filed 27 January 1910 by G. L. Shackelford, registrar, Marietta. ¶10

That certificate moves him from Probable to Confirmed as a son of John P. Eidem and Oline Slind, and fixes his place in the family as the fifth of their children. Note that the mother's 'full maiden name' box carries her married name, Oline Eidem - which is why the Minnesota birth index shows his mother's maiden name as 'Eidem' and not as 'Slind'. It is a clerical slip by his own father, not evidence of a cousin marriage. ¶11

Censuses: present in his father's Mehurin household in EVERY census of his adult life - 1910 (a newborn, indexed 'Peter C Eidem'), 1920 (as 'Peder', the Norwegian form), 1930, 1940 and 1950. He never left home. ¶17

Two ids were created for this man by agents working independently, and they are reconciled here into one. The 1950 census calls him 'Peter C., son, 40'; the Minnesota death index calls him Peder Conrad Eidem, born 6 October 1909. An age of 40 in April 1950 requires a birth between April 1909 and April 1910, so the two are the same person, and the fuller name and exact date are taken from the index. ¶3

1950 census: aged 40 and still at home with his parents in Mehurin Township, Lac qui Parle County, working as a FARM HELPER for wages (class of worker P) 35 hours a week. ¶5

1950 federal census: Mehurin Twp, ED 37-25, sheet 1, line 22 - 'Eidem, Peter C., son, white male, 40, NEVER MARRIED, born Minnesota, working, 35 hours last week, farm helper, Farm, private wage or salary worker'. He was a paid hand on his 73-year-old father's farm and worked exactly the same 35 hours his father did. ¶18

Marriage: on 22 October 1960, aged 51, he married Alma Jeanette (Gilbertson) Oakland, a widow of Boyd, Minnesota, and she moved to Marietta. In 1969 they moved to Montevideo, where she assisted him with ten years of custodial work at Trinity Lutheran Church. They had no children together; her obituary calls him 'Peder C. Eidem of Marietta'. ¶6

Marriage: Lac qui Parle County certificate N60514, 'EIDEM, PEDER CONRAD' and 'OAKLAND, ALMA JANNETTE', 22 October 1960 - the exact date already known from his widow's obituary, now confirmed against the county's own marriage index. ¶13

Burial: Zoar Lutheran Cemetery, Antelope Valley Township, Deuel County, South Dakota. Married Alma Jeannette (Gilbertson) Oakland in 1960; no children. ¶16

Death: 10 December 1995 in Chippewa County, Minnesota; Minnesota death certificate index 1995-MN-033675, birth date 6 October 1909, birthplace Minnesota, mother's maiden name SLIND. That index entry independently confirms both the birth date on the 1909 certificate and his mother's maiden name. ¶12

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Confirmed as a son of John P. Eidem and Oline G. Slind on two independent grounds: Find a Grave memorial 161507905 records his parents as John P. Eidem and Oline G. Slind Eidem with a sister Cora Belinde Eidem Peterson, and his mother's 1955 obituary names 'Peder' among her eight children, 'of Marietta vicinity'. ¶14

Confirmed as distinct from Peder P. Eidem (1858-1935): the elder man's photographed stone gives his dates as 4 November 1858 to 23 October 1935 and names his wife Karen P. on the same monument, while this Peder Conrad was born 6 October 1909 in Lac qui Parle County and died 10 December 1995 at Montevideo, Chippewa County. Peder P.'s Find a Grave memorial carries no family links at all; Peder Conrad's carries the full set. ¶15
