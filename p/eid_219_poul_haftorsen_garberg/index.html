About the family

Father of Peder Poulsen Garberg (b. about 1692): the census names a son Peder aged 9 at Garberg in 1701, which matches the bygdebok's birth year 1692 for the Garberg holder Per; no other Peder Poulsen is known at Garberg; and Peder's sons were named Pål/Poul (1724, 1729) and Håvard/Haftor (1733) - after this Poul and after Poul's own father or eldest son Haftor. Rated Confirmed for this link (explicit census statement + Peder's own Garberg records + unique fit); note that Peder's own link to his son Paul is Probable. ¶2

Life story

Bygdebok (Selbu i fortid og nåtid vol. 3, Garberg 35/1, canvases 628, 631-632): mother Anne (wife of Haftor Jonsen); Pål b. 1663 (canvas 628) or 'ca. 1661' (Brukere list) 'overtok etter moren' about 1693; '-1701-1718 Pål Haftordsen, f. ca. 1661, d. 1719, [...] kona Goro, [...] 1719'; children Haftord ca. 1685, Jon ca. 1689 (holder 1719-1725), Per ca. 1692 (holder 1741-1759). Death 1719 is the book's date; the burial (16310, 1719) has not been read. The mother link is Probable (bygdebok only). ¶6

1666 vicar's manntall (35528 v21 L, Garberg no. 8): 'Paaffuel Haftorsen 3 Aar', son of Haftor Jonsen (64) - born about 1663, against about 1661 from his 1701 age of 40 (crop checked by the orchestrator). ¶4

1701 manntall, Selbu, farm Garberg (canvas 118, book pp. 214-215, crop checked by the orchestrator): farmer 'Poule Haftorsen', aged 40; 'Haftor Poulsen 16, Jonn 12, Peder 9, alle Sønner' - all sons. So born about 1661; his father was a Haftor. ¶1
