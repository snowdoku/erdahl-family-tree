Life story

Baptised 21st Sunday after Trinity 1776, son of Greis and Guru Pedersdatter of Wigleeret, Frosta. In the 1801 census 'Jens Greisen', unmarried schoolmaster (skoleholder), at Moe, Frosta. Probably the 'Jens Greisson' who was godfather to Sivert Greisen's son Siver in 1803, and so probably Sivert's brother. ¶1

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
