About the family

Mother of Marit, baptized 25 Sep 1814 in Nord-Aurdal (father Ole Juelsen, Midstrand). She is the probable mother of Marit Olsdatter, wife of Erik Olsen Dakken, and is linked only through that baptism. Her dates and parents are not yet researched. ¶1

Life story

Confirmed 12 Jul 1795 in Aurdal parish as 'Astri Evensd., Hovlrud', aged 15 (b. about 1780). In the 1801 census for Aurdal, farm Haarud (Hovrud), she is Astri Evensdatter, 21, an unmarried daughter living with her parents, the farmer Even Julsen (48) and Marit Olsdatter (48). Also in the household were her siblings Jul (19), Jens (5), Ragnild (16) and Sigri (11), and her paternal grandmother Astri Jonsdatter (90), for whom she was named. ¶2

She married Ole Juelsen of Midtstrand at Ulnes on 11 Apr 1803 (bride 'Astri Evensdatter, pige, Hovrud'). Her death has not been found. ¶3

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: she is named as mother ('Astri Evensd.') at the 25 Sep 1814 Ulnes baptism of Marit, daughter of Ole Juelsen of Midstrand; the 1812 Marit of Midstrand was buried aged 6 weeks in March 1812, so the 1814 child is Marit Olsdatter who married Erik Olsen in 1838 (bride's father Ole Juulss., born Milstrand). That baptism ties her to her daughter in the tree; confidence raised from Probable to Confirmed. Her own parentage is stated by the 1801 census for Aurdal, Haarud household 01, where she is person 005, 'Datter', unmarried, 'hos forældrene' (living with her parents), aged 21. ¶4
