Life story

He was the youngest of the four children of Hergius Olsen and Anne Halvorsdatter recorded at Neergaarden in the 1801 census, where he is aged 16. He was named after his maternal grandfather, a Halvor of Omland in Gjøvdal, and he in turn named his second son Herjus after his own father. ¶3

He married Ragnild Aslaksdatter in 1820 and died young, before February 1829, leaving four children under ten. ¶4

His estate was settled at Gangsei in Gjøvdal from 4 February 1829 to 12 March 1830 (Østre Råbyggelag skifteprotokoll no. 23a, folio 276b; also entered as no. 20, folios 505, 510b, 516 and 522b on 4 April 1829). The card names him and his widow Ragnild Aslagsdatter, and lists four children with their ages: Aslag Halvorsen, 8; Herjus, 6; Ommund, 4; and Ane Halvorsdatter, 2. It ends "Avd's bror: Ole Herjusen Gangsei". ¶2
