Life story

Probate: on 15 Sep 1756 the estate of 'Torgiej Torrofssøn' was registered and divided at the farm Sønderland in Treungen annex of Hvideseid prestegjeld, between the widow 'Sigri Evensdatter' and their children: Gunnil Torgiejsdatter, wife of Aamund Ellingsen Tvedt in Omblie (?); Anne Torgiejsdatter, wife of 'Orm Egilssøn Tvedt af Treungen'; Asloug Torgiejsdatter, wife of Halvor Asbjørnsen Tvedt in Nissedal; and Margith Torgiejsdatter, wife of Joen Siversen Hiemdahl in Treungen. All heirs were present. Vest-Telemark skifteprotokoll 1752-1759 (source 25673), register No 195, view 331 right page. The lead came from the line 4 worker. ¶1

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Confidence: Confirmed, graded on his link to his daughter Anne Torgiejsdatter (see her page): the probate names her as his child and heir and names her husband Orm Egilsen Tvedt of Treungen, who is in the tree on his own records (1763 and 1769 documents give 'Orm Egildsøn Tvedt'). Birth before 1700 is inferred from four married daughters in 1756. ¶2

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Not to be confused with Targie (Tarjei) Halvorsen Sønderland (see his page, line 4), whose own probate at Sønderland is register No 321 of 1759 (fol. 944) in the same protocol. Sønderland had more than one household. ¶3
