Life story

Of Balholm(en), Balestrand. The 1934 Balestrand bygdebok (canvas 729, Balholm chapter) mentions 'Karen Jensdtr., enkja etter Nils Erikson Falck' (the widow of Nils Eriksen Falck) in a sentence with the years 1712 and 1743 (she apparently remarried in 1743, perhaps to a man from Bergen), and further down the same page 'Gjert Nilsson Falck', with 1735, 1770 and 'Kari Olsdtr.' (Giert's first wife, who died in 1775). ¶1

How sure are we?
