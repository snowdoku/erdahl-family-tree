About the family

Probable father of Peder Pedersen Walstad (see his page): the only Skatval baptism of a Peder son of Peder found in Jan-Aug 1723, at the same farm (Lille Walstad) that Peder Pedersen later held, and at an age (b. 1723) that matches his 1801 census age of 78. Peder Pedersen's son Ole (b. 1758) was born at Lille Walstad and called odelsbaaren (heir by birthright) to Walstad at his marriage in 1791, which implies the family held Walstad by inheritance. No record states the relationship outright, so Probable, not Confirmed. ¶2

Life story

The marriage of Peder and Mali of Lille Walstad is not in the Stjørdal betrothal book 16367 for Jan 1714 - Aug 1719 (canvases 1-29 read in full; canvases 30-44, Aug 1719 - May 1721, were). With their first known child born in Feb 1722, they must have married outside Stjørdal parish (in the bride's home parish) or before 1714. An indexed search of all marriages 1708-21 with a bride Mali found no Skatval-area groom Peder; the nearest is Peder of Salotter x Mali Olufsdatter of Grav at Ytterøy, 11 Jan 1711, which nothing ties to Walstad. ¶6

The marriage of Peder and Mali of Lille Walstad is not in the forlover book 16367 for Aug 1719 - Apr 1721 (canvases 30-44 read). The only Skatval pair Peder x Mali there is 'Peder Lars[?] Aijke[?]', a soldier of Capt. Heideman's company, and 'Pigen Mali Hansdatt. Hammer', married at Skatval on the 16th Sunday after Trinity 1720 (canvas 37); nothing ties that couple to Lille Walstad, so it is a Speculative candidate only and is not used. Also at Lille Walstad: 'Enkemand Hans Pedersøn Lille Valstad', a dragoon, betrothed Oct 1719 to Ingeborg Haagensdatter (canvas 31) - perhaps a relative (brother or father) of Peder; untested. ¶5a

An older child of the couple found: 'Døbt Peder Lillewalstads b. Marrit. Mod. Mali', Sexagesima Sunday [15 Feb] 1722, godparents Jon Valstad, Haagen Vold, Mali Vollum, Marrit Vold, Ane Vold (Stjørdal 16368 canvas 93); the mother was churched at Skatval on Lætare Sunday 1722 as 'Peder Lillewalstads h[ustru] Mali' (canvas 94). So Peder Pedersen (b. 1723) had an elder sister Marit, and Peder and Mali were married by about May 1721. Their marriage is not in Mar 1721 - Dec 1722 (canvases 84-103 read in full); look in 16368 before Mar 1721 (canvases 0-83, 1714-early 1721). The godfather Jon Valstad (of Store Walstad?) may be a relative of his husband's family; not tested. ¶4

Farmer of Lille Walstad (Valstad), Skatval, in 1723; patronymic not recorded. 'Pastor Scatvold. Døbt Peder Valstads b[arn] Peder. Mod[er] Mali. Fadd[ere]: Hans Vold, Torsten Scatvold, Peder Johansen Vold, Gurru Vold, Mali [?] Scatvold' - baptism at Skatval on the 7th Sunday after Trinity, 11 Jul 1723 (Stjørdal 16368, p. 172); his wife was churched seven weeks later, 29 Aug 1723: 'Pastor Scatvold: introd. Peder Lillevalstads [hustru] Mali' (p. 174). Read on the scans. ¶1

Lille Walstad context in 16368, not yet tied to Peder. (1) 8th Sunday after Trinity 1724 (about 23 Jul), Værnes: 'Graffæsted Mali Hansdatter[?] Lillevalstad 88[?] aar gl.' (canvas 124 right). An old woman of Lille Walstad, born about 1636 if the age is right: too old to be Peder's mother, possibly a grandmother or a widow of an earlier holder; the dragoon 'Enkemand Hans Pedersøn Lille Valstad' (betrothed Oct 1719, 16367 canvas 31) may be her son. (2) Christmas 1724, Skatval: a godmother 'Kari Lille Walstad' at the baptism of Jon Walstad's child (canvas 128 right). Neither names Peder, so no parent is proposed. ¶7

A second marriage at Lille Walstad, probably Peder's. 16368 canvas 134 left, Palm Sunday [21 Mar] 1725, at Skatval: 'Copuler: Peder Larss. Lillevalstad og Karen Arnsdatt.' (Peder Larsen of Lille Walstad married Karen Arnesdatter). Peder Lillewalstad x Mali had children in Feb 1722 and Jul 1723, and on the 8th Sunday after Trinity 1724 'Mali Hansdatter Lillevalstad' was buried at Værnes (canvas 124 right; the age, read '88[?]', looks at 4000 px like '83' or '33' - the two digits are near-identical loops and cannot be settled). A godmother 'Kari Lille Walstad' appears at Christmas 1724 (canvas 128), three months before the wedding. If the age is 33, the burial is Peder's wife Mali (b. c.1691), her patronymic was Hansdatter, and the widower Peder (Larsen) remarried Karen Arnesdatter in 1725; Peder Pedersen's patronymic would then make Peder Lillewalstad's father a Lars. But the farm also had other households in 1725 (churching of 'Ole Johans[?]. Lillevalstads h. Brynnille' at Skatval, canvas 132 right; burial of 'Ingeborg Torstensd. Lillevalstad 63 aar', mid 1725, canvas 137 right), and no 1725-27 baptism (canvases 132-158 read) shows Peder Lillewalstad with either Mali or Karen. The church-book entry alone does not prove the identity; the next note (betrothal book 16367, canvas 63) supplies the missing link. Also checked: no Lillevalstad card in the probate card index 77804 L section (views 1233-1243), and earlier Valstad cards are Verdal only. ¶8

Bygdebok (Skatval gårds- og slektshistorie vol. 1, 37 Lille Valstad, canvas 237 left column): holder entry 'Peder Larsen [...] 30.03.1729; skjøte 06.03.1737. [G.] 15.09.1720 [m.] Mali Hansdtr. [...] før 1739'; children 'Anne, døpt 26.01.1721, [g.m.] Ola Pedersen (77/1)', 'Marit, døpt 08.09.1722, [d.] før 1739', 'Peder, [døpt] 1724'; next holder 'Peder Pedersen Valstad, [f.] 1724, [d.] etter 1801, skjøte 17.04.1753, [g.] 1755 [m.] Mali Olsdtr., døpt 26.05.1733'. The book's marriage date (15 Sep 1720) and the bride's patronymic Hansdatter agree with the 1720 betrothal 'Peder Lars[?]... x Mali Hansdatt.' (16367 c37). The book's Marit 08.09.1722 and Peder 1724 differ from the register (Marrit 15 Feb 1722, Peder 11 Jul 1723); the '30.03.1729' is unexplained (not a death: he lived to 1762-77). ¶13

Negatives for his father, the bygdebok's 'Lars Pedersen Lille Valstad, d. 1738, aged 92': (1) Burial. There is no 1738 burial list in Stjørdal 16369. Its 1738 section (canvases 109-113, p. 696-704) has baptisms only, the 1737 burials end on canvas 109 left (total 110), and the 1739 list follows on canvas 122. 16368 ends with the 1737 churchings (canvas 260). The 1737 burials (canvases 106-109) and the 1739 burials (canvases 122-124) have no Lars of Walstad. (2) Probate. The typed register of Skifteprotokoll 2C (24752, 1737-42; OCR in) has no Valstad/Walstad entry. Its only 1738 Lars Pedersen, fol. 773 (view 34 right), is the dragoon Lars Pedersen of Hammerstrøen, a plass under Hammer (Hegra annex), who left children Ingeborg 3 and Niels 1, so he is not the 92-year-old. The probate card index 77804 has no Lillevalstad card. The bygdebok claim stays untested. ¶16a

Peder Larsen was alive and living at Lille Walstad in 1762. The detailed 1762 extra-tax list for Skatval (48401, viewer image 211, farm no. 66 'Lille Walstad', leilending) has the householder 'Peder og Hu[stru]' (Peder and his wife; no wife's name), the farmhand Ole, 16, the maid Ane, and in the column 'Andre hos sig havende' (others living with them) 'Peder Larsen og Hu[stru]' (Peder Larsen and his wife). The householder is Peder Pedersen (b. 1723, married Mali Olsdatter 1755). An old couple, Peder Larsen and wife, living with him on the farm fits Peder Larsen as his father retired on the holding (kår), with his second wife (Karen Arentsdatter, m. 1725) or a later one. This is the only Peder Larsen recorded at Lille Walstad after 1725. It supports the link but the list gives no relationship word, so the grade stays Probable. It also shows Peder Larsen was born well after 1690 (alive 1762); the 'BEF 1702' birth limit stands. Note on the 1762 list: the Stjørdal/Skatval detailed household lists of 48401 are at viewer images about 203-212 (farm nos. about 1-68; nos. 21-29 image 205-207, 30-39 image 208, 48-57 image 210, 58-68 image 211). Earlier passes had found only the summary (images 80-81). Here wives are written 'og Hustru' without names, but children over 12, servants and lodgers are named. ¶10

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Probable burial. Stjørdal ministerialbok 1755-1780 (16371), p. 278 right (IIIF canvas 280, 0-based), Skatval, 1st Sunday after Epiphany [12 Jan] 1777: 'Begr. Peder Walstad 90 aar' (buried, Peder Walstad, 90 years; index pg00000007159601 has no patronymic). This is very probably Peder Larsen: he was the old lodger 'Peder Larsen og Hu.' with Peder Pedersen at Lille Walstad in 1762 (48401 image 211), and no other old Peder is known at either Walstad farm then (the 1762 list for Store Walstad, no. 36, image 208, has the householders Jon and Christopher Jonsen, no Peder; Peder Pedersen, b. 1723, was alive in 1801). The age gives a birth about 1687, so he was about 33 at his first marriage (1720) and about 36 at the birth of Peder Pedersen (1723). Birth changed from 'BEF 1702' to 'ABT 1687' and death set BEF 12 JAN 1777, both on this identification (name and farm only; Probable). No probate after him is in the typed Stjør- og Verdal registers for 1776-79 (24760-24761). Graded link unchanged (Probable, on his link to Peder Pedersen, Peder Pedersen). ¶12

The case for this identification 1

The chain behind Peder's patronymic Larsen and Mali's patronymic Hansdatter. (1) 16367 canvas 37: on Judica Sunday 1720 a soldier of Capt. Heideman's company, 'Peder Lars[?] Aijke[?]', was betrothed to 'Pigen Mali Hansdatt. Hammer' (an unmarried girl), and they married at Skatval on the 16th Sunday after Trinity 1720. (2) Peder Lillewalstad and his wife Mali had children baptised at Skatval in Feb 1722 (Marit) and Jul 1723 (Peder) (16368 canvases 93, 110). (3) On the 8th Sunday after Trinity 1724 'Mali Hansdatt. Lillevalstad' was buried at Værnes (16368 canvas 124 right). The age is two near-identical loops; read as 33, she was born about 1691, and that is the only reading that fits a girl married in 1720 (83 would not). (4) 16367 canvas 63 right, 18 Oct 1724: 'Soldat og Enkemand af Hr Capit. Heidmans Compagnie ved Nafn Peder Larsøn Ajcle[?]' (a soldier and widower of the same Capt. Heideman's company) asked to be betrothed at Værnes to 'en Pige af Nafn Karen Arentzdatt.'. The margin note says they were married 'Dom. Palmar. 1725 ved Skatvold'. (5) The marriage itself is in the church book on that same Palm Sunday 1725 at Skatval: 'Copuler: Peder Larss. Lillevalstad og Karen Arnsdatt.' (16368 canvas 134 left). So the soldier Peder Larsen of Heideman's company, who married Mali Hansdatter in 1720, was a widower by Oct 1724 and was 'Peder Larsen Lillevalstad' in 1725. That fits him being the Peder Lillewalstad whose wife Mali bore children in 1722-23 and who was widowed when Mali Hansdatter of Lille Walstad was buried in Jul 1724. The links are the same name, the same company, the widowhood and the dates; no other Peder x Mali couple at Lille Walstad has been found. No single record names Peder Larsen as the father of the 1723 child, so this stays Probable. The names are changed to Peder Larsen and Mali Hansdatter on that basis. Mali's father was a Hans; her 1720 residence was Hammer (Skatval), which is the lead for her parents. ¶9

Records that disagree 1

Negative test of the bygdebok lead that his father was a Lars Pedersen, b. about 1646, son of an earlier Peder at Lille Valstad: the 1665 vicar's census (35528 view 38, Skatval p. 56) has 'Lillvalsta, 2 øre: Per 38 aar' with no sons listed (that year's list gives sons from about 12 only); the 1666 list (view 87, p. 155, no. 164 'Lillvalstad'), which gives all sons, has 'Per 39' with 'Per Jons. 11', 'Johan Jons. 8', 'Oluf Pers. 6' and 'Jon Pers. 4'. There is no Lars at Lille Valstad in either year, so a Lars Pedersen aged about 19-20 was not living there as Per's son in 1665-66. (A husmann 'Lars Pers.' is listed at Lien in 1665, aged 28, and in 1666, aged 40; the ages disagree and nothing ties him to Valstad.) The lead stays Speculative and unlinked. ¶17
