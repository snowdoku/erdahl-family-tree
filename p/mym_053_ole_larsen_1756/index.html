Life story

Baptism, probable: Ole, son of Lars of Hillan, baptised at Loctu (Frosta) in 1756 (index pd00000006472707). Fits his age (45 in 1801) and farm. Father linked at Probable. Nothing earlier than 1750 survives indexed or scanned for Frosta. ¶4

Death: 'Ole Larsen Øster Hjelde', kaarmand (retired farmer on a life-lease) of Hellan, aged 79 1/2 (b. about mid-1756), died of old age 19 Dec 1835 and was buried 4 Jan 1836 at Frosta (Frosta ministerialbok 1827-1839, p. 214; index pg00000002490245). Name, farm (Øster Hjelde = Hiellan Øster, where he was the farmer in 1801, aged 45) and age (his baptism is of 1756) all agree. The earlier search covered only 1801-1830, which is why it missed him. ¶7

Married Magnild Pedersdatter at Frosta on 17 Jun 1779 (betrothed 27 Mar 1779), as 'Ole Larsen, Ungekarl' (bachelor) of Hellan. Confidence upgraded to Confirmed as father of Anne Olsdatter: her 1780 baptism names father Ole and mother Magnild Pedersdatter of Hellan, and the 1801 census lists her as the couple's daughter. ¶3

1801 census: Ole Larsen, 45, householder (huusbonde) and farmer (gaardmand) at Hiellan Øster (Hillan/Hjelle), Frosta, in his first marriage, with his wife Magnhild Pedersdatter, 42. Their children at home were Anne 21, Malena 19, Lars 16, Peder 14, Martha 12, Mette 9, Gunild 5 and Siver 2, and Siver Griesen, 34, was their servant. His birth year of about 1756 is calculated from this age. ¶1
