About the family

Mother of Jon (Joen) Alvsen (see his page), Probable; graded on her link to Jon. Lesja ministerialbok 1732-1776 (source 9236), p. 293 (canvas 139), read on the scan: on Dom. Jubilate [9 May] 1745 at Verket the churching 'Introd: Alf Nyestuens Aaste' is entered immediately before the baptism of 'Alf Nyestuens Jon', born 17 Apr 1745, so she was the mother of that child. Her patronymic is not given. Not Confirmed for the same reason as her husband: the 1745 baby is tied to the 1775 groom Joen Alvsen Nystuen across 30 years. Jon named his first daughter Aase (b. 9 Jan 1776). Birth before 1725 is an estimate (a son baptised 1745, an infant Iver buried 1738 if hers). ¶1

Parents found (Probable, graded on the parents): Iver Andersen (d. by 1732) and Margrethe Biørnsdatter (bur. 9 Jun 1743) of Stueflotten (Stueflaaten), Lesjaskogen. Nord-Gudbrandsdal probate cards (source 39945): skifte after Iver Andersen, 18 Jul 1732, fol. 116b, children Biørn 4, Anders 1 3/4, 'Aaste 11' (image 13171); skifte after Margrethe, 19 Feb 1744, fol. 663a, children Biørn Iversøn 16, Anders 13, 'Aase Iversd. 22' (image 13173). Ages give b. about 1721-22, as her 1797 burial (b. 1722). The Lesja bygdebok (vol. 1, canvas 42, entry 1-6) also has 'Åse, ca. 1721, sjå Nystugu, her (3-3)' under Iver and Margrete. Birth date revised to ABT 1721 (from BEF 1725). ¶5

Life story

Churching found. Lesja ministerialbok 9236 p. 241 (canvas 112), Dom. 5 p. Epiph. [early Feb] 1740 at Werket: 'Introd:... Alf Nystuens Aaste' - the wife of Alf of Nystuen was named Aaste in 1740, after the birth of the son Ola (bygdebok: Ola 1740). This places the couple Alf and Aaste at Nystuen five years before Jon's birth (1745). Her grade (link to Jon) is unchanged. Note for the 1738 marriage: in 9236 the 1730s-40s pages carry only baptisms, churchings and burials (canvas 106 = Advent-Christmas 1738, canvas 107 = Jan-Mar 1739, canvas 103 = spring 1738, canvas 100 = 1737); no marriages were found on canvases 106, 107 or 112, so the 1738 marriages are probably kept in a separate list (not located; 9236 has 359 canvases, and by 1774-76, canvases 330-352, marriages are entered inline with a 'Copul.' margin label). ¶7

Primary record for her name and widowhood. The 1763 Lesja manntall of the extra tax (Ekstraskatt Gudbrandsdal 1762-66, source 45390, view 286, read on the scan) lists under No. 1 Aaefletten, in the column 'Husmænd og Inderster med Børn og deres Navne', '3tio Enken Aaste Iversd. Børn, Ole, Soldat Iver, John, Margrete, Anne' (the third inderst household: the widow Aaste Iversdatter; children Ole, the soldier Iver, John, Margrete, Anne; the list gives only children over 12). So by 1763 she was a widow, and John (Jon, b. 1745) was named as her child. The same eldest children appear on the 1767 Nystuen probate card (Ole of age, Iver 24, John 22, Margrethe 20, then Ole 11, Sigri 11, Brit 7, too young to be listed in 1763), so that card lists her children. Grade unchanged (Probable, graded on her link to Jon (Joen) Alvsen Rånåhaugen): the 1763 John is tied to the 1745 baby by name and his mother's first name only (18 years, Nystuen vs Aaefletten), and the 1767 card still names the deceased as 'Ole Olsen' (contradiction noted below), so the chain is not yet clean. ¶3

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the identification · 2 records that disagree · 1 open question

The case for this identification 1

Probable identification as Aaste Iversdatter: (1) Nord-Gudbrandsdal probate card 'Nystuen i Lesje' (source 39945, image 9591): probate 1 Jun 1767, fol. 195b, after Ole Olsen, 'g.m. Aaste Iversdtr.', children Ole (of age), Iver 24, John 22, Ole 11, Margrethe 20, Sigri 11, Brit 7. Two sons called Ole point to children of two marriages. 'John 22' (b. about 1745) fits Jon b. 17 Apr 1745; Iver 24 fits a second Iver after the infant 'Iver Alfs' of Nystuen buried 1738 (a son named for her father Iver); Ole 11 and Sigri 11 fit Ole Alvsen (45 in 1801, so b. about 1756) and Sigri Alfsdatter, a bride in 1781. (2) 'Aaste Iversd.' of Nyestuen was buried 23 Apr 1797, given as b. about 1722. So Alf's widow Aaste seems to have married Ole Olsen of Nystuen about 1757-59 and kept her first husband's children in the house. The protocol entry (25159, image 203 = fol. 195b) is a bitonal scan and largely illegible; heirs' names as 'Iver...', 'Joen...', 'Ole Olsen 11 aar' and a 'Peder Alfsen' among the men named, but the patronymics of Iver and John cannot be read with certainty. The card is an archivist's summary (audit 3: counts towards Probable only). A remarriage entry 'Enke Aaste Iversd.' with Ole Olsen in Lesja 9236 about 1756-60 would settle it. ¶2

Records that disagree 2

Contradiction still open: the probate card (39945 img 9591) reads 'Ole Olsen [died], g.m. Aaste Iversdtr.', but Aaste was already 'Enken' (the widow) in 1763 with the same children, while the 1745 baptism makes Jon the son of Alf of Nystuen. Either (a) the 1767 skifte is a late division after Alf (the card's 'Ole Olsen' a misreading of a bitonal protocol in which 'Peder Alfsen' also appears), held perhaps before her remarriage, or (b) she married an Ole Olsen after 1763 who died by June 1767, and the skifte also settled the father's inheritance of her Alfsen children. The protocol scan (25159 image 203, fol. 195b-196a; the only scan, bitonal at 3181 px, no greyscale version on the viewer, IIIF manifest 404) was re-read at 2x and the deceased's name and the heirs' patronymics are still illegible. ¶4a

The 1767 'Ole Olsen' contradiction: the Lesja bygdebok (vol. 1, canvases 45-46, entry 3-3 Nystugu 'ca. 1738 - 1766', rebuilt from word boxes) gives Alf 'g. 1738 m. [Åse] frå [Stueflotten] (1-6), 1721-97', says all their children were baptised in Lesja, and lists them: 1. Iver 1738-38; 2. Ola 1740, took over; 3. Iver 1742; 4. Jon 1745, sjå Nørdre Rånåhaugen (6-2); 5. Margrete 1747, 'ved skiftet etter faren 1767'; 6. Sigrid 1749-50; 7. Johan 1751; 8. Sigrid 1753; 9. Ola 1756; 10. Brit ca. 1760. It treats the 1767 skifte as the skifte after Alf ('ved skiftet etter faren 1767'; 'Om skiftet etter Alf, sjå... (5-6)'). These are exactly the heirs of the 1767 card (Ole of age, Iver 24, John 22, Margrethe 20, Ole 11, Sigri 11, Brit 7): both Oles are Alf's sons by baptism, so no second husband is needed to explain them. So the card's deceased 'Ole Olsen' is probably a misreading for Alf, and the 1767 skifte a late division after Alf, who was dead by 1763 ('Enken Aaste Iversd.' in the 1763 manntall; the bygdebok's 'Alf døydde i 1766 eller 1767' is inferred from the skifte date, since it says his burial is not in the church book). The contradiction is resolved only by a compiled source, so her grade stays Probable (graded on her link to Jon (Joen) Alvsen Rånåhaugen). ¶6
