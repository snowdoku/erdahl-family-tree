Life story

Baptised 10th Sunday after Trinity 1746 (14 Aug 1746; Easter 10 Apr): 'N:1 Ole Jørgens[en] Slindens barn ved nafn Marite. Test: Peder Slinden, Gunder Wiggen, Mali Slinden, Margrethe Slinden, Siri Kiøsnes, madamn Marite Pedersdatter' (Selbu klokkerbok 16315, canvas 90 col. 4, crop checked by the orchestrator). Her mother was churched about Michaelmas 1746 as 'Ole Slindens Qvinde Marite Pedersdatter' (c92 col. 2). Raised to Confirmed: the baptism names the father and the farm, the 1762 extra tax lists her as Ole's daughter Marit at Slinden, and there is no other Marit born to an Ole at Slinden in 1746. ¶4

A brother or sister of Jorn (Jorgen) Olssen Slind, born 1752, David Erdahl's maternal fifth-great-grandfather. ¶2a

1762 extra tax: listed as 'Marit' among Ole's children over 12 in her grandfather Jørgen (Jørn) Olsen's household at 706 Slinden; she was about 16. ¶3
