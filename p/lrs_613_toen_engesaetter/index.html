About the family

Son Gullach/Gullov (Probable): father of Gullov of Engesætter (see his page), born about 1665, d. before 22 Aug 1741. For: (1) Gullov's patronymic is written 'To[e]ns[en]' in the 1701 manntall (Gullach, 36, on Engesetter, sons Joen 3 and To[e]n 1; from the scan; the Digitalarkivet index has 'Gullach Løensen' with a son 'Løen', the looped T misread as L), '[T?]ensen' in his 1741 probate heading, and 'Gullig Toens: Engesætter' as a godfather at Brekke on 31 Mar 1719 (Gulen 11438 c34); (2) he named his second son To[e]n; (3) this is the only farmer on Engesetter in 1664 whose name can be Toen, and none of the other Brekke sogn farmers of 1664 has that name; (4) Gullov was born about 1665, so he could not appear in a list of 21 Jun 1664 in any case. ¶3a

Life story

1664 census: farmer (bonde) on Engesetter ('Ingesetter'), Brekke kirkesogn, Eivindvik prestegjeld, in the bailiff's census of 21 Jun 1664, third of four farmers; the name reads 'T[ö]en' or 'T[ö]rn' (: 'Tørn[?]'). No sons aged 12 or over or servants are listed with him. Toen (Tøen, Tjøn) is taken to be the name; 'Tørn' is not a known forename. Birth before about 1640 is inferred only from his being a householder in 1664. ¶1

Reading: the Engesetter farmers in the 1664 bailiff's list are, in order, '[Th]osten', 'Claus' (sons Hans 14 and Jørgen 12 Claussønner), 'T[ö]rn' or 'T[ö]en', and 'Jorn' (servant Olle Knudtzen 44). read the first as 'Østen[?] (Ehsten)' and the third as 'Tørn[?]'. On a 2.2x crop the first name opens with the same looped 'Th' ligature as the next farm heading 'Thouning' directly below, so it is 'Thosten' (Tosten). The third opens with the same looped capital T without the h; the letters after it are 'ö' and then 'rn' or 'en', which this hand barely separates. This list gives farmers no age and no patronymic and omits boys under about 12. ¶2

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 record that disagrees

Records that disagree 1

Against or open: the 1664 forename is not fully legible ('-en' or '-rn'); no record names Toen as Gullov's father; Toen is not on Engesetter in the 1645 poll tax, so he came after 1645, and he is not there in 1701. ¶3b
