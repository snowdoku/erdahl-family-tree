Rasmus Olsen Erdalc. 1651 – bef. 1714Confirmed · Oppstryn

About the family

Father of Rasmus Olsen (born about 1651), known only from the patronymic. The 1664 vicar's census for Erdal ('Heel gaard Erdall', Oppstryn) lists five farmers: Oluff Nielsøn (40), Oluff Jørgensøn (60), Lauritz (24), Oluff Pedersøn (40) and Rasmus Olssøn (60). Rasmus, then about 13, is not listed by name. ¶1

Life story

Aaland 1932: Aaland's Erdal farm history (bruk nr. 3, 'Larsbruket') lists the holders before Rasmus Olsen as Jørgen Haraldsen (from 1603, married Eufemia, patronymic Olsdatter inferred by Aaland) and his son Ola, who took a lease of 4 pund of crown land in 1646 and was fined in 1644 for striking a man with a tankard. This Ola Jørgensen is the 'Oluff Jørgensøn, 60' of the 1664 census. Aaland then lists '(Vs.) Rasmus Olson, br. 1678': the brackets mean Aaland himself was unsure that Rasmus was Ola's son-in-law (verson). Aaland does not name Rasmus Olsen's father. So the book neither proves nor rules out that Ola Jørgensen was his father; it shows only that Rasmus succeeded Ola on the same holding. Aaland's Gutdal history adds a 'Jørgen Olson Erdal' who took Gutdal bruk nr. 1 in 1673, another Ole-son leaving Erdal in that decade. Identity stays Speculative; the deeds and leases of 1673-1698 named by Aaland (Rasmus's lease 1678, deed 1698) in the Nordfjord court and tax records would say whom Rasmus succeeded. ¶3

Re- (1664 vicar's census, p. 210): the five Erdal farmers are confirmed as Olluff Nielsøn 40, Olluff Jørgensøn 60, Lauridtz [patronymic illegible] 24 (more probably 74, since 'Erich Lauridtsøn 30 aar' stands beside him in the farmers' sons column), Olluff Pedersøn 40 and Rasmus Olsøn 60. The sons' column holds only Peder Ellingsøn 40 and Erich Lauridtsøn 30. This vicar listed no young boys on this opening (on Hilde, p. 217, the youngest son listed is 15), so the absence of Rasmus Olsen, then about 13, says nothing about his father. No relationship to any Oluf is stated. ¶4

Bailiff's census 1664-66 (p. 142, view 79): 'Heel gaard Erdall' lists farmers Oluff Nielsen, Oluff Jørgensen, Dittmer[?], Oluff Pedersen and Rasmus (first names, no ages); farmers' sons Peder[?] and Erich; cottager/lodger Knud Aardall. Like the vicar's list it omits small boys: Rasmus Olsen (then about 13) is not there, and no son is tied to any Oluf. The bailiff's census does not identify Rasmus Olsen's father. ¶5

Any of the three Olufs could be his father. Oluff Jørgensen would fit the later family name Jørgen (Rasmus's son, born 1681), but this is Speculative. A placeholder only; identity unproven. ¶2

How sure are we?

Speculative. A plausible candidate only. Treat this link as a question, not a fact.
