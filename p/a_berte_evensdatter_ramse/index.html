About the family

NEW ANCESTOR. Grandmother of Birgitte Terjesdatter (1761-1812), who was almost certainly named after her — Berte, Birte and Birgitte are the same name in these records. ¶1

Wife of Tjøstel Tergiesen of Nedre Eppeland. At his estate settlement on 2 March 1765 she is the widow who "oppgir boet", surrenders the estate. She was still living on 10 March 1768, when she is named as the mother and an heir in the joint estate of two of her under-age daughters, Ingeborg and Anna Tjøstelsdøtre. ¶2

Her father is given on the archivist's card for the 1765 settlement: "Enkas far: Evind Svendsen Ramse + sk. 1722" — the widow's father was Evind Svendsen of Ramse, whose estate was settled in 1722. This is the link that carries Birgitte Terjesdatter's ancestry back into the Ramse farm and, through it, to Even Tellefsen Ramse in the early 1600s. ¶3

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Confirmed link: the archivist's card for her father's estate (card 10423, Skifteprotokoll no. 6, fol. 189b, 19 November 1722) lists "Birgitta Evindsdtr." among the children of Evind Svendsen and his first wife Anna Gunnusdatter, and adds that she was later "g.m. Tiöstel Torgiesen Rostveit i Åmli" — married to Tjøstel Tergiesen of Rostveit, who is Tjøstel Tergiesen of Nedre Eppeland (born at Rostveit). This ties Berte of Eppeland to the 1720 and 1722 Ramse estates directly, not just by her name and her father's name. ¶5
