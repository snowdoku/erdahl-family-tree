About the family

Parents: the lifetime division (skifte i levende live) held at Steensland in Tørdal annex on 24 Feb 1762 (date from the Bamble card index; the register heading reads 'Anno 176[?]') for the elderly couple Joen Thoresen Steensland and his wife Anne Torbiørnsdatter names as the eldest daughter 'Aasil Joensdatter gift med Alff Halvorsen boende paa Toe i Tørrisdahl' (Aasil Jonsdatter, married to Alf Halvorsen, living at Toe in Tørdal). Her lot was 73 rd 1 ort 20 8/9 sk, paid partly by cancelling 24 rd 8 8/9 sk that her husband owed the estate and partly in land and houses at Steensland. This agrees with her byname Steensland at her 1753 marriage. Bamble skifteprotokoll I 6a, fol. 252-253a. ¶10

Siblings: the 24 Feb 1762 division names six children, 3 sons and 3 daughters: Thore Joensen, eldest son, married and of age, living at 'Lonnedt[?]', curator for the youngest brother's share; Torbiørn Joensen, married and of age, living at Søftestad[?]; Tolleff Joensen, aged 20 in the register reading (23 on the card index), unmarried, at Tørredal (Tørdal); Aasil, eldest daughter (this person); Ingerie (Ingri) Joensdatter, married to Lars Trontvedt in Nissedal parish (the Lars Pedersen Frontvet to whom Ingri Jonsdatter Steensland was betrothed at Candlemas 1758); and a third daughter, read 'Aase' on fol. 252 but 'Aasil[e] Joens Daatter, den yngre' (the younger) on fol. 253a and 'Aassil' on the card index, married to Knud Kraalstad[?] in Tørdal. The family thus very probably had two daughters named Aasil, distinguished as eldest (wife of Alf Halvorsen) and younger (wife of Knud Kraalstad[?]). ¶11

Life story

Burial: 'Aasil Jons Datter Toe', aged 36 (so born about 1728), was buried in Drangedal on 17 Mar 1764. The prefix before her name, probably marking a married woman, is an uncertain reading. Drangedal ministerialbok 1697-1767, burials p. 26. ¶2

Marriage: on Whit Tuesday 1753 'Alf Halvorsen Dale' was betrothed in Drangedal to 'Aasil Jonsdatter Steensland' (Aasil, also Åshild, Jonsdatter of the farm Steensland); cautioners (sureties) Halvor Torredal and Jørgen Steensland. They were married (copulated) on the Feast of the Visitation of Mary, 2 Jul 1753. The year is inferred from the page sequence (p. 17 is headed 1752, p. 18 restarts at Septuagesima). Drangedal ministerialbok 1697-1767, p. 18. ¶1

Possible mother of John Alfsen, b. about 1755, householder at Thoe in 1801 (inferred by date only): if his census age of 46 is right, he was born about 11 years before Alf's second marriage, while Aasil was Alf's wife. The 1801 census itself names Tore Torkildsdatter as his mother ('Mandens moder'), which would then mean stepmother; this is unresolved until John's baptism (about 1754-55, Drangedal) is read. See the note on Alf Halvorsen. ¶7

Death in childbed (inferred): on Annunciation Day, 25 Mar 1764, eight days after her burial, 'Alf Toes Datter Aasil', aged 3 weeks, was buried at Drangedal. A daughter born in early March and named Aasil, with the mother buried in mid-March, points to Aasil dying after the birth. The cause is not stated. ¶3

Probate (skifte): 2 Oct 1764 at Toe in Tørdal after 'den afgangne sal. Qvinde Aasel Jonsd(atte)r' (the late woman Aasel Jonsdatter), between her widower Alv Halvorsen and their four sons Jon 11, Torgen 9, Halvor 6 and Gunlich 3; guardians Jon Dahle, Engvold Torredahl, Thore Steensland and the father Alf Toe. The estate held 8 Bet. (a land-tax unit) in Toe with bøksel and herlighet (owner's rights); the widower took half, each son about 37 rd 3 ort 20 ß. Her parents are not named and no guardian is called her brother; Thore Steensland's relationship is not stated. Bamble skifteprotokoll I 6b, fol. 455a-457a. ¶8

Guardian Thore Steensland at her 1764 probate (inference): he was very probably her eldest brother Thore Joensen, though the 1764 probate does not state the relationship and the 24 Feb 1762 division places Thore at 'Lonnedt[?]'. Not to be identified, without further evidence, with Tore Joensen Midbø (see his page), father of Turi, the wife of her son Gunleik Alvsen; nothing yet links the two, and if they were the same man Gunleik would have married his first cousin. ¶12

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Probable mother of Gunleik Alvsen (b. 1761) and of Haavor (Halvor), baptised 17 Dec 1758, sons of Alf Toe. She was Alf's only known wife between 1753 and 1764: the Drangedal marriages for 1753 to Jan 1763 hold no other marriage of Alf, and the burials for 1755 to 1763 hold no burial of Aasil or of any wife of Alf (the burial search is a medium-confidence negative). Neither baptism names the mother. Haavor's godparents included Marj Jørgensdatter Steensland, of her home farm. ¶6a

Confidence raised from Probable to Confirmed as mother of Gunleik Alvsen (see his page): the probate names Gunlich, 3, as her son with Alv Halvorsen. This supersedes the note 'Probable mother of Gunleik Alvsen (b. 1761) and of Haavor (Halvor)', which is now proved for both. ¶9a

The case for this identification 1

Identity: the Aasil Jonsdatter of Toe buried in 1764 is taken to be the bride of 1753 because the name and age agree, Alf Halvorsen had moved from Dale to Toe by 1758, and Alf was a widower (Enkke-Mand) when he was betrothed again on 1 Apr 1766. ¶4

Corrections 1

Correction: it also supersedes 'Possible mother of John Alfsen, b. about 1755': her eldest son Jon was 11 in Oct 1764, so born about 1753 (the year of her marriage), and he is the John Alfsen of Thoe in 1801. A further son Torgen, b. about 1755, is added from the probate. The infant daughter Aasil (bur. 25 Mar 1764) is not in the probate, as she had died. Death place refined to Toe in Tørdal (Tørdal was an annex of Drangedal). Her place in the direct line runs through Gunleik, whose identification as Gunleik Alvsen of Midbøe, Nissedal, remains Probable. ¶9b

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Possible sister: Ingri Jonsdatter Steensland, betrothed at Candlemas 1758 to Lars Pedersen Frontvet of Nissedal, with Alf Halvorsen Toe and Jørgen Steensland as cautioners (p. 24). Inferred from the shared patronymic and farm and from Alf standing surety; not stated. ¶5

Would confirm: a probate after Aasil (1764) or after Alf (1801) naming the children. ¶6b
