About the family

Her parents found. Her own 1721 probate continues on fol. 480a (DA 25285 view 489, left column): the widower 'angiver' that land in the farm Sæbu (odelsgods, 1 3/4 skind, which he had redeemed from her brother 'Johannes Biørnsen Sæbur') 'var arffveligh tilfalden efter hendis S. Forældre Biørn Johansen og Ingebor[..]daatter', according to the skifte after Biørn Johansen dated 17 Mar 1698. That skifte (DA 25282 s. 133b, view 137; card 13395) is at Sæbu, Skrautvål, after Ole Østensen and his late wife Ingebiør Reyersdatter, and names her children by her first husband Biørn Johansen: Johannes 19, 'Rangnilde 21 aar gammel' and Giertrude 14. Born about 1677. Supersedes the earlier 'Test record: her own parents' note. ¶4

Life story

Married 18 Jan 1702 Knud Helgesen of Strand (Aurdal church book DA 9360, index pv00000000706841, 'Ragnild Biørnsd.'). Died before 9 Oct 1721, when her probate was held at Midtstrand (card 9283; DA 25285 fol. 479a, view 488): 'afgangne S: Quinde Ragnild Biørnsdatter', her widower Knud Helgesen and 'begge deris sammen auflede og i Live værende 4re Børn': Helge Knudsen 12, Marit 17, Ingebiør 8, Gertrud 6. The 1727 Midtstrand skifte (card 9287, fol. 900b) repeats the four children and calls her 'Rangdi Biørnsdtr. +'. ¶1
