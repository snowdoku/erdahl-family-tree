Life story

Betrothal or marriage, late 1739 or 1740: 'Soldat Torkel Nielsen Klagstad og pigen Seri Mickelsdatter' (the soldier Torkel Nielsen of Klagset and the maid Siri Mikkelsdatter), Selbu klokkerbok 16315 canvas 3 (0-based). The names are clear; whether it is the betrothal or the wedding, and the exact Sunday, need a zoom. This is the same couple who head Klagset 749 in the 1762 extra tax list, and it fits a daughter Lisbet b. about 1753-55 (confirmed 1769 'of Klagset'). ¶2

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
