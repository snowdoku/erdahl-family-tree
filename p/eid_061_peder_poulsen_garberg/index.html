About the family

Other children (register): 'Peder Garbergs [Barn] Ingebor', Reminiscere Sunday, 25 February 1725 (canvas 98 right; his wife churched Easter Monday 1725); twins 'Guroe & Ellen', 'døbt Peder Pouls: Garbergs Tvillinge Børn', Quasimodogeniti Sunday, 28 April 1726 (canvas 104 left; crop), mother churched at Exaudi 1726; and 'Håvard[?]', son of Peder Poulsen Garberg, 1733 (canvases 118-130). A Haftor Pedersen - Haftor is the Selbu form of Håvard - stood godfather at the baptism of Paul Pedersen's daughter Kari in 1758, which fits a brother. The 1725 Ingebor may belong to another Peder: a Peder Bjørnsen Garberg also baptised at Garberg (Anne, 1728). ¶2

Father of Paul (Povel) Pedersen of Garberg (see his page), rated Probable. For: Poul of 1729 is the only Poul/Paul child of a Garberg father in the complete read of the register from October 1724 to November 1733; the bygdebok (vol. 3, p. 632) gives the Garberg holder Pål Persen the birth year 1729 and places him on the same page as a Per born 1692; Paul's children carried the name Peder (his son Peder Povelsen, married 1784). ¶3a

Life story

1701 manntall (Selbu, Garberg, canvas 118): listed as 'Peder', aged 9, the youngest of the three sons of Poule Haftorsen (40) - brothers Haftor Poulsen 16 and Jon 12. Read; crop checked by the orchestrator. Age 9 in 1701 agrees with the bygdebok birth year 1692. ¶5

Baptism of his son Poul (Paul): on Jubilate Sunday, 8 May 1729, 'döbt [struck word, probably Peder] Pouls: Garbergs B: Nom: Pål' - the forename is struck through and not cleanly legible, so the entry reads, probably, Peder Poulsen Garberg's child, named Poul (Selbu ministerialbok 1699-1737, canvas 117 left page, crop). On Whit Monday, 6 June 1729, 'Peder Pouls Garbergs' wife was churched (canvas 117 right page; crop), which fixes the father's patronymic. ¶1

Bygdebok, a lead (vol. 3, Garberg chapter p. 632, nb.no word boxes only): '1741-1759 Per... 1692,... 1741. G.1719... Mali', with children in years 1721, 1722, 1724 ('Pål, 1724, død' - a first Pål who died) and 1729 ('Pål, 1729,... bruket' - the second Pål, who took over the farm). The birth year 1692 rests on the book alone. The second date 1741 conflicts with a tenure ending in 1759 and is not settled; it could be his death or a start of tenure. Reading the printed p. 632 would settle it. ¶4

Probate (skifte) after him, 20 Jun 1741: 'Garberg, Lectoratets Beneficerede Gaard, Selbu. 20/6-1741: Peder Paulsen Garberg. Enke: Mali Haavers dtr. Barn: Povel Pedersen, 12 år gl.; Haavar Pedersen, 7 år; Haftor Pedersen, 7 mndr.; Mali Pedersdtr., 15 år, Guru Pedersdtr., 15 år (tvillinger); Brynild Pedersdtr., 4 år.' Guardians: the mother's sister's husband Ingebrigt Ingebrigtsen Kjelstad; the father's cousin Havtor Olsen Uglem; Anders Siversen Garberg (Stjør- og Verdal probate index card 77804 c545, abstract of skifteprotokoll 2 fol. 1088b; read by the orchestrator from the card). This dates his death to before 20 Jun 1741 - the bygdebok's '1741' - and names Povel (Paul) Pedersen, aged 12 (born 1729), as his son. The children match the register: twins Guru and Ellen/Mali (1726), Paul (1729), Haftor (the 'Haldor/Haktor' of Oct 1740). ¶6a

Protocol entry read (24752 fol. 1088b, view 352): 'Ao 1741 d. 20 Junij' at 'Lectoratets Beneficerede gaard Garberg' after 'Peder Poulsen Garberg', between his widow 'Mali Haavarsdatter' and their children '1. Povel Pedersen 12 aar, 2. Haaver Pedersen 7 aar, 3. Haftor Pedersen 7 maaneder, 4. Elle Pedersdatter 15 aar og 5. Guru Pedersdatter og saa 15 aar, Tvilling med Elle, og 6te Brynild Pedersdatter 4 aar'. Correction to the card abstract (77804 c545), which gives the twin as 'Mali': the protocol has Elle (Ellen), as in the 1726 baptism of the twins 'Guroe & Ellen'. The widow's lauverge was Ole Olsen (no relationship stated); 'Enkens Søstermand Ingebrigt Ingebrigtsen Kielstad' took Povel and Elle, 'Haftor Olsen Ouglen den afg. Mands Søskende Barn' took Haaver and Guru, and Anders Siversen Garberg took Haftor and Brynild. No parent of Mali is named. ¶7

Mother added from the bygdebok (Selbu i fortid og nåtid vol. 3, Garberg canvas 631): his father Pål Haftordsen's wife was Goro; Probable only. The same pages give '1741-1759 Per Pålsen, f. ca. 1692, d. 1741. G.1719 [med] Mali' (canvas 632), children Pål 1724 d., Goro 1726, Eli twin 1726, Pål 1729, Håvard 1733 (d. unmarried 1799), Brynhild 1737 (m. Jon [...] Alset), Haftord 1740. ¶8
