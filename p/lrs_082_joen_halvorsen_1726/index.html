About the family

Children: Christopher Joensen (about 1763), farmer at Grimstvedt in 1801; Johanne (bapt. 22 Jul 1770), probably the wife of Ole Olsen at Ibræke in 1801. ¶3

Parents: the probate after Halvor Torgiersen of Nordre Grimstvedt, Nissedal, 17 May 1756, names his widow Taarand Bjørnsdatter and their children Gunder Halvorsen (of age), Joen Halvorsen (22), Torgiei Halvorsen (13) and Margith Halvorsdatter (16). Joen's share lay in Grimstvedt. The 1790 co-juror 'Targe Halvorsen Grimstvedt' is the brother Torgiei. Vest-Telemark skifteprotokoll 1752-1759 (source 25673), fol. 300a, view 304R. This grades the link to his parents (see their records); his own grade, which covers his link to his daughter Johanne, is unchanged. ¶7

Life story

Burial (per index): 'Joen Halvors.', Enkemand (widower), of Grimstved, aged 82 (b. about 1726), interred on the 2nd Sunday after Trinity 1808 (26 Jun 1808, computed). Kviteseid ministerialbok 1800-1814, burials 1808, p. 163a, Nissedal (index pg00000001619528). The age agrees with 75 in the 1801 census. The scan was not read. ¶5

Marriage: betrothed on 25 Jan 1762 at Nordbøe in Nissedal as 'unge Karl Joen Halvorsen Grimstvedt' to the widow Susane Andersdatter (Susanne Andersdatter). The cautioners were Jacob Nordbøe and Alf Tvetene (Kviteseid 7981 c125L, entry 44). As a bachelor in 1762 and a widower after his first marriage in 1801, she was his only wife. He was a lagrettemann (court juror) in 1790: 'Laugrettemænd Targe Halvorsen Grimstvedt og John Halvorsen Grimstvedt' valued the estate of Ole Olsen of Ibrække on 7 Jun 1790 (25679 skifte No 31). Targe Halvorsen Grimstvedt, probably his brother, married Maria Nielsdatter in Nissedal in 1774 with Ambjøn Grimstvedt and John Grimstvedt as cautioners (7982 c106L), and in 1791 he was a cautioner at the betrothal of Joen's daughter Johanne. ¶6

Deeds: panteregister I 2 (source 10916, p. 802, view 141 left), Nordre Grimstvedt in Nissedal (matr. 23): Joen Halvorsen took a deed to 5 settinger of the farm dated 15 Oct 1759 (tingl. 6 Mar 1760, No 251), and a settlement 'med sin Moder Taarand Biørnsdatter' dated 4 Mar 1762 (tingl. 5 Mar 1762, No 340) about the shares that fell at the skifte on the farm of 17 May 1756 (No 176). This names Taarand Bjørnsdatter as his mother in a record of his own. Later: 'Christopher Joensen erholdt Skiøde af sin Fader Joen Halvorsen' on the same farm, tingl. 4 Mar 1794 (No 1864), and 'Anders Joensen erholdt Skiøde af sin Fader Joen Halvorsen' on a plass (Tikkestuel?), tingl. 17 Oct 1794 (No 2075). So his sons Christopher and Anders Joensen are proved; his daughter Johanne (the link that puts him in the tree) is not named on this page, and his grade is unchanged. The page lists no skifte after him or his wife Susanne Andersdatter. ¶9

Name: 'Joen Halvorsen Grimstvedt' (Joen son of Halvor, of the farm Grimstvedt, Nissedal) at the baptism of his daughter Johanne on 22 Jul 1770 in Nissedal church. His wife is not named. ¶1

Appraiser in 1788: 'John Halvorsen Grimstvedt' and Ole Olsen Ibrech were the appraisers at the registration (11 Jan 1788) of the estate of Ole Torgrimsen of Nordre Grimstvedt, Nissedal (25678 No 650, view 385R). Ole Torgrimsen's heirs were his own children (Ansteen, Hans 20, Torand married to Kittil Bendixsen Rollefstad, Ingeborg), so the probate shows no kinship with Joen's family. It does show Ole Olsen Ibrekke (see his page) and Joen Halvorsen acting together two years before Johanne Joensdatter's betrothal into the Ibrekke family. His grade is unchanged. ¶13

1801 census: Joen Halvorsen, 75, widower after his first marriage, 'husbondens fader' (the householder's father), living on support from the farm (nyder ophold af gaarden) in the household of his son Christopher Joensen, 38, farmer at Grimstvedt, H.no. 02, Kviteseid. Birth about 1726 is inferred from his age. That the 1770 father and the 1801 widower are the same man rests on name, patronymic and farm; no other Joen Halvorsen was found at Grimstvedt in 1801. ¶2

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Confidence: Probable. He is the father of the child Johanne of 1770 by the baptism; that she is our Johanne Joensdatter is Probable (see her notes). ¶4a

Records that disagree 1

Age conflict: the 1756 probate gives Joen as 22 (born about 1734); his 1801 census age (75) and burial age (82) give about 1726. The birth field is left as ABT 1726 pending his baptism; a birth about 1734 fits better with his being an 'unge Karl' at his betrothal in 1762. ¶8

Searches that found nothing 2

Confirmation search: the 1786 Nissedal-church confirmation list (Kviteseid ministerialbok 1773-1786, source 7982, canvas 228, p. 253) has boys Christian Nielsen Grimstved (18), Tjøstov Aanondsen Dale, Ole Torkildsen Frøstlie, Jørgen Targiesen Tvetene, Targie Nielsen Mule and Hans Olsen Grimstved (17), and only one girl, Birgit Targiesdatter Trontved (17½); canvas 229 goes straight on to Treungen. So his daughter Johanne (baptised 1770) was not confirmed in Nissedal church in 1786; try 1785 and 1787. His grade is unchanged. ¶11

Confirmation search: the 1785 Nissedal-church confirmation list (Kviteseid ministerialbok 1773-1786, source 7982, canvas 226 right, p. 251) has no Johanne Joensdatter among its girls (who include Hage Torbjørnsdatter Midbøe and Ragnil Haavorsdatter Brække); 1784 (c225) and 1786 (c228) have none either. The 1787 list is in the next book (7983), not 7982, and was not reached. His grade is unchanged. ¶12
