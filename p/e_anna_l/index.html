About the family

Parents: Aaland's 'Vs.' and 'verfaren' make Lars Samuelsen of Loen her father. Aaland names two wives of Lars, Anne Olsdatter Svingeset (died about 1749) and Marte Rasmusdatter Beinnes (died 1772 aged 58), without saying which was her mother. The probate after Marthe Rasmusdatter Loen of 5 Oct 1772 (Nordfjord skifteprotokoll A 15b, fol. 768b) settles both: it was held between the widower Lars Samuelsen and the deceased's child 'Anne Larsdatter gift med Guttorm Andersen Hilde'. See the notes for the confirmation. ¶7

Parents: father Lars Samuelsen of Loen and mother Marthe Rasmusdatter (of Beinnes, by Aaland) are Confirmed by the 1772 probate after her mother. Her brother Samuel, who died unmarried in 1772 aged 26 (Aaland), was born about 1746 and so was probably a son of Lars's first wife Anne Olsdatter; he is not an heir in this probate (inference). ¶9

Life story

Of Loen when she married Guttorm Andersen of Hilde on 3 Jun 1772. Mother of Samuel (baptized 1774). ¶1

Probate, 1772: the probate after Marthe Rasmusdatter Loen in Olden skipreide, registered 5 Oct 1772 (Nordfjord skifteprotokoll A 15b, fol. 768b), was held between the widower Lars Samuelsen and the deceased's child 'Anne Larsdatter gift med Guttorm Andersen Hilde' (Anne Larsdatter, married to Guttorm Andersen Hilde). No other child is named. This names her mother and confirms her father. ¶8

Aaland 1932: in the Loen farm history (Stryn volume, printed p. 322, bruk nr. 15 'Hallsteinbruket') Aaland lists Lars [Samuelsen], died 1789 aged 75, and then: 'Eldste son. Samuel, † ug 26 a. g. 1772. — Vs. Gutorm A. Hilde sk. 1773 p. br., erva etter verfaren og svogeren. Var ikkje brukar.' Guttorm A. Hilde, Lars's son-in-law (verson), took the deed to the holding in 1773, having inherited it after his father-in-law and his brother-in-law (Lars's eldest son Samuel, who died unmarried aged 26 in 1772); Guttorm did not farm it himself. Guttorm Andersen Hilde's only known wife is Anne Larsdatter of Loen (married 1772), so Aaland makes her the daughter of Lars Samuelsen of Loen. ¶6

Aaland 1932: Aaland's Hilde farm history (Del 2 3 2, p. 33, bruk nr. 4) names her 'Anne Larsd. Loen, † ett. 1812', the wife of Guttorm Andersson: she was of Loen and died after 1812. Aaland gives no parents for her here; the Loen farm history is in the Stryn volume (Del 2 3 3), not the Innvik volume. Her Innvik burial entry would fix her death. ¶2

Probate, 1812: on 29 Apr 1812 she and her husband Guttorm Andersen Hilde, both living, divided their property among their children (skifte i levende live): Anders, Samuel (living on Loen), Guttorm (21), Giertrud (30) and Anne (19). Her half of the estate (631 riksdaler 2 ort 12 skilling) was divided in a second session in May 1812 'at the request of Anne Larsdatter Hilde' (Nordfjord skifteprotokoll A 21b, pp. 1591-1593). This is the basis of Aaland's 'died after 1812'. Her own parents are not named. ¶3

Probate, 1818: she is not named in the probate after her husband (registered 27 May 1818), which names no widow, so she probably died between May 1812 and May 1818. This is an inference; her burial has not been found (Nordfjord skifteprotokoll A 22b, fol. 595-596). ¶4

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Confidence raised from Probable to Confirmed: the 1812 lifetime division (A 21b pp. 1591-1593) names her as the wife of Guttorm Andersen Hilde and mother of Samuel Guttormsen of Loen. ¶5

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Burial candidate, not adopted: 'Anne Larsdatter', kaarkone of Skaare, aged 89 (b. about 1745), died 26 Oct and buried 30 Oct 1834 in Innvik (Innvik ministerialbok 1832-1846; index pg00000000926016). She is the only Anne Larsdatter born 1735-1755 buried in Innvik after 1812 (the other, of Hammer, was buried in 1803, when this Anne was still alive), but Skaare is not Hilde or Loen and nothing in the index ties her to Guttorm Andersen, so no date is entered. The scan may name a husband. ¶10
