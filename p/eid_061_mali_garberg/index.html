About the family

Mother of Poul (Paul) Pedersen, baptised 8 May 1729 (see his page), rated Probable. For: the 1729 churching names only 'Peder Pouls Garberg's' wife; in 1737 that wife is Mali Halvarsdatter, and the bygdebok also gives Peder's wife as Mali. Peder Poulsen Garberg had children in 1724, 1725, 1726 (twins), 1729, 1733 and 1737, and the complete reads of the register from October 1724 to March 1738 found no burial of a wife of his and no remarriage of his, so nothing shows a second wife. The Garberg wife who died in that span, Guri, aged 25 in the early 1730s, was the wife of the other Peder, Peder Bjørnsen Garberg. Not yet excluded: a first wife before October 1724, which matters for the older children but not for Poul of 1729. ¶5a

Parents found: the skifte after 'Bryniel Olsdatter' at Kjelstad, Selbu, on 20 Jun 1744 (Stjør- og Verdal Skifteprotokoll 3, 24753, fol. 94-95, views 114-115 right) names, after her children by her widower Ole, her children by her late former husband: '1 Berit Haaversdatter', wife of Poul Pedersen Rønsberg, and '2 Hustru Mali Haaversd: Garberg'; fol. 95 gives 'Hustru Mali Haaversdatter Garbergs tilfaldende Arvelod efter hendes afg. Moder Bryniel Olsdatter Kielstad'. Her mother Brynhild (Bryniel) Olsdatter is Confirmed (the probate names Mali as her daughter, three years after the 1741 Garberg probate names Mali as Peder Paulsen's widow). Her father is Haaver Andersen Kjelstad (see his page), Probable: probate card 77804 c971 gives his skifte of 7 Nov 1701 at Kjelstad with heirs 'Ellen Hoversdtr., Birgete, Mali', all with their mother; so she was born before Nov 1701 at Kjelstad. This settles the lead of: Berette Haaversdatter Rønsberg (skifte 24755 fol. 55) was her full sister, and Haaver Poulsen Rønsberg, appraiser of her estate in 1761, her nephew. The 1741 'mosterens mann' Ingebrigt Kjelstad was the husband of her half-sister Kari Olsdatter. Her own grade (Confirmed, on her link to Paul Pedersen) is unchanged. ¶14

Life story

Marriage, an open question: the bygdebok gives 'G.1719 Mali' for Peder (Per) Garberg, but the register's only Garberg wedding of 1719 reads, on the 1st Sunday after Trinity, 'Ectevigde Peder Olss: og Marit Vigen. Jon Garberg og Mali Gjelle' - married Peder Olsen and Marit Vigen; Jon Garberg and Mali Gjelle (Selbu ministerialbok 1699-1737, canvas 75 right page, crop; the bride's farm is uncertain, Gjelle or Gielle). The groom is clearly Jon, not Peder, since the same line writes Peder in full for the first groom. This bride is not identified with Mali Halvarsdatter: that would need proof that Mali Gjelle was a Halvar's daughter, or that the clerk or the bygdebok confused the grooms. read all of 1719 (canvases 74-77 left) and found no wedding of Peder Poulsen Garberg, so the 1719 marriage year in the tree rests on the bygdebok alone. The marriage may lie between 1720 and October 1724 (canvases about 77-96, not yet read for it). ¶6

Baptism of her daughter: 6th Sunday after Trinity, 28 July 1737, 'Døbt Peder Povels. Garberg hans datter, Brynnill, Moder Mali Halvorsd.' - baptised Peder Poulsen Garberg's daughter Brynhild, mother Mali Halvorsdatter; godparents Ingbricht Ingbrichtsen, Per Haldorsen, Inger Olsdatter, Guri Olsdatter, Berit Arntsdatter (Selbu ministerialbok 1699-1737, canvas 145 right page). ¶3

Churching: 14th Sunday after Trinity, 22 September 1737, 'introd:... Peder Povels: Garbergs kone Mali Halvarsd:' - churched Peder Poulsen Garberg's wife Mali Halvarsdatter (Selbu ministerialbok 1699-1737, canvas 146 left page, crop). This is the first record to give her patronymic; Halvar, Halvor and Halvard are forms of one name, and the tree uses the churching's spelling. ¶4

On 23 Oct 1740 'Peder Garbergs barn ved nafn Haldor' was baptised, carried by 'madame Mali Haldors datter' (16315 c10). In this book the woman carrying is usually the mother, so Peder Poulsen Garberg's wife appears here as Mali Haldorsdatter - Haldor and Halvar are the same name in Selbu usage - and she named this son Haldor, presumably after her father. A 'Haldo Garberg' (wife Marit) baptised a child in Jan 1714 (16310 c52) - a possible father or brother; not proved. ¶7

Named as the widow 'Mali Haavers dtr.' (Håvardsdatter - the same name as Halvarsdatter) in the probate after Peder Paulsen Garberg, 20 Jun 1741, with their son Povel Pedersen, 12 (card 77804 c545; read by the orchestrator). ¶10a

Probable burial, not proved: Selbu ministerialbok 16311 c10 L, 3rd Sunday after Trinity (22 Jun) 1760, funeral sermon for '(3) Mali Haavarsd. gl. 62 aar' (zoomed: no farm is written). Aged 62 gives birth about 1698, which fits her 1719 marriage and her last known child in 1737, and the 1744 skifte at Kjelstad shows her alive then as 'Hustru Mali Haaversd. Garberg'. No other Mali Haaversdatter is known in Selbu, but without a farm the burial is not tied to her; a Garberg probate c.1760 (none found under Garberg in the typed registers of 24753 and 24755) would settle it. ¶16

Her own probate: Skifteprotokoll 5 (24755) fol. 110b-111a, No. 1 for 1761. A skifte was held on 21 Oct 1761 at Garberg in Selbu after the late widow 'Mali Haaversdatter Garberg'. She left six children, three sons and three daughters, from her marriage to her late husband Peder: 'Sønnen Povel Pedersen 30 aar', '2 Haaver Peders. 28 aar', a third son H[aldor?] Pedersen about 25, and daughters including Guri Pedersdatter and 'Brynnild Pedersdatter 24 aar'. The appraisers were Peder Biørnsen Garberg and Haaver Rønsberg, and a Haaver Rønsberg also appears among the estate's debts. No relationship word is given for him. This is a second primary record that names her as mother of Povel (Paul) Pedersen (see his page), after his father's 1741 probate. It gives her death as before 21 Oct 1761, as a widow at Garberg. It also rules out the 1771 'Mali Haldorsd. Stokan' burial as hers. The ages are rounded: Povel was baptised 8 May 1729. Her son Haaver carries her father's name. Her father, a Håvard, is still not named; the Rønsberg appraiser is a lead for kin only. ¶12

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Speculative wife of Peder Poulsen Garberg (see his page) and mother of Paul Pedersen (see his page). The only source is the Garberg chapter of the bygdebok (Selbu i fortid og naatid vol. 3, p. 632, nb.no word boxes only): 'Per... 1692... G.1719... Mali' - Per, born 1692, married in 1719 to Mali. The register's churchings of 1725, 1726 and 1729 name her only as Peder (Poulsen) Garberg's wife. ¶1a

Corrections 2

Correction: name changed from 'Mali' to 'Mali Halvarsdatter', and confidence raised from Speculative to Probable, by the churching of 22 September 1737 ('Peder Povels: Garbergs kone Mali Halvarsd:', 16310 canvas 146 left) and the baptism of 28 July 1737 ('Moder Mali Halvorsd.', canvas 145 right), with the absence of any second wife in the reads of 1724-1738 (see the notes below). ¶2

Correction: name set to Mali Håvardsdatter (Halvarsdatter), and raised to Confirmed as mother of Paul Pedersen Garberg. Her sister was married to Ingebrigt Ingebrigtsen Kjelstad ('mosterens mann'), guardian of two of her children. Her father was a Håvard/Halvar - not the Haldor Bjørnsen Garberg of the 1739 probate, whose heirs do not include her. ¶10b

Alternatives considered and set aside 3

Not her: 'Haldo[r]s Garbergs barn Mali', first child of Haldor and Marit, baptised Exaudi 1712 (16310 c46) - too young to marry Peder Poulsen in 1719. A different Haldo Garberg with wife Mali had children 1702-03. Her own baptism (before about 1700) is not found. ¶8

Possible burial: 'Mali Haldorsd. Stokan, døv og dum[?]', died 14 Jan 1771 aged 82 (b. c.1689) (16311 c42 R) - the age fits Peder Poulsen Garberg's widow, but the farm is Stokkan, not Garberg; unproved. ¶9

Possible burial, not proved: 3rd Sunday after Trinity (22 Jun) 1760, funeral sermon for '(3) Mali Haavarsd: gl: 62 aar', no farm given (16311 c10 L, zoomed). She died a widow before her skifte of 21 Oct 1761, and this is the only Mali Haaversdatter burial seen in 1757-61. The age (born about 1698) fits her being an heir of her father before Nov 1701 (77804 c971). No farm is given, so it stays a lead and her death date is not changed. ¶15

Open questions and the next record to read 5

Missing: her patronymic, her birth and her death. ¶1b

What would settle it: the 1719 marriage in the Selbu ministerialbok 1699-1737 (source 16310), and the printed p. 632. ¶1c

What would confirm it: Mali's burial with an age, or a probate (skifte) after her or Peder naming the children. ¶5b

Biography gaps: her birth date, birth place and baptism (about 1689) predate the 1699 start of the Selbu register, so they cannot be found in the parish books; no Mali daughter of Halvor appears in the 1701 manntall (38461 c111-131), so the next records are the 77804 probate card gaps and the 1689-1701 protocol 24749 (unindexed, not read). Her marriage to Peder Poulsen Garberg is dated only by the bygdebok (1719), since the register's 1719 wedding is a different couple, so the 1720-Oct 1724 canvases (about c77-96) are the next to read. Her death and burial are not found (she was a widow in the 1741 probate, and the 1771 'Mali Haldorsd. Stokan', aged 82, is a farm mismatch), and her confirmation is not recorded. ¶11

Lead for her kin, not a link: the skifte after Berette Haaversdatter, wife of Poul Pedersen Rønsberg, at Rønsberg, Selbu, dated 1755[/56] (Stjør- og Verdal Skifteprotokoll 5, 24755, fol. 55, view 71 right), names seven children: Peder Poulsen and Haaver Poulsen (both of age), Jon Poulsen, Ole Poulsen, Ingebor Poulsdatter, Brynild Poulsdatter (married to Jon Pedersen Melbuen[?]) and Brynnill Poulsdatter the younger. The 'Haaver Rønsberg' who appraised Mali Haaversdatter Garberg's estate in 1761 is very probably this Haaver Poulsen (his own estate is 24761 fol. 323b, 1785). Berette and Mali share the patronymic Haaversdatter and lived in the same parish, so they may have been sisters, and Haaver Poulsen her nephew; but no record seen names a relationship. Poul Pedersen Rønsberg was guardian of his own daughters, so the skifte names no kin of Berette. Patronymic alone, so this is a lead only. ¶13
