About the family

Father of Peder Madsen Uggedal (see his page), Probable; graded on his link to Peder. Inferred, not stated: the 1666 list puts a son Peder under Matts at Uggedal; Peder Madsen (patronymic from a 1699 Borgund engagement and the 1715 godparent entries) holds Uggedal in 1701 and named his son Mads (born about 1685). Same farm, consistent patronymic and naming, and no other Mads or Peder at Uggedal in 1664-66. ¶2a

Life story

1666 vicar's census, Sunnmøre deanery (Sogneprestenes manntall 1664-66 no. 26), Borgund, p. 336, farm 'Vggedall' (Uggedal): 'Matts', bønder (holder), aged 70 (born about 1596); in the sons column under him 'Peder', aged 16 (born about 1650). They are the only two people listed at Uggedal (index rows pc00000001599302-303; Aarsett precedes, Holmesett follows). The 1664 bailiff's census of Romsdal and Sunnmøre fogderi (no. 16), p. 56, Gryta skipreide, Borgund, lists one holder at 'Uggedall' (half farm, fisk 2½ v), 'Madtz' (pc00000003914806). No patronymic is written in either list. ¶1

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 record that disagrees · 1 open question

Records that disagree 1

Against: the 1701 manntall gives Peder's age as 62 (born about 1639), but the 1666 list says 16 (born about 1650); the record does not state the relationship. A different man at Holmesett, 'Matts' aged 20 in 1666, is not this Mads. ¶2b

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Would settle it: a Sunnmøre probate after Mads of Uggedal (he would have died after 1666, before the probate indexes begin in earnest) or after Peder Madsen Uggedal naming his siblings; the 1657 kvegskatt for Borgund. ¶3
