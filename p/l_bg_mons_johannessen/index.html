About the family

Parents: Not resolved. The census suggests he is a brother of the head, Ole Johannessen, but the father there is written Johannes Olssen, which fits Ole's patronymic but makes Mons's relationship uncertain. Not linked. ¶3

Children: Ingeborg, baptised 20 Jan 1805 at Asheim (see her record). ¶5

Parents (13 Oct 2026, supersedes 'Not resolved'): Linked to Johannes Olsen of Asheim and Randi Torkieldsdatter, both Probable. The baptism names Johannes Olsen Asheim as father. In 1801 Mons, 29, lives in the Asheim household of Ole Johannessen, 32, whose father ('Mandens fader') is Johannes Olssen, 60. The census lists Mons only as a farm servant (tieneste dreng), not as a son. Johannes Olsen was the only Johannes Olsen at Asheim; the other Johannes there in 1773 was Johannes Clausen, whom the patronymic Olsen rules out. Not Confirmed because no record yet states that Mons Johannessen of 1801-1804 is this baptised Mons, and 1771, the year his census age suggests, has not been read for a Mons son of Johannes Clausen or Johannes Olsen. His burial or a probate naming heirs would confirm it. ¶8

Siblings: The 1801 Asheim household 02 also holds Ole Johannessen 32 (the head, explicitly Johannes Olssen's son), Johannes Johannessen 27, Ingerborg Johannesdatter 23 and Torkield Johannessen 16. The last three are listed as servants, like Mons; that they are Johannes Olssen's children is inferred from their patronymics and the household. Hermund Johannessen, 36, head of Asheim household 04, is probably NOT a full brother: his mother is the widow Marthe Gulaksdatter, 70, and he is probably a son of the other Asheim Johannes, Johannes Clausen (inferred). ¶9

Parents: Father Johannes Olsen of Asheim raised from Probable to Confirmed. The 7 Oct 1773 baptism names the father as 'Johannes Olsen Asheim' (scan read twice, and auditor). The 1844 burial of Mons Johannesen, farmer of Asheim, gives an age that puts his birth at about Sep 1773, a few weeks before that baptism, on the same farm. He lived there throughout: in 1801 in the household of Ole Johannessen, son of Johannes Olssen; as a bachelor of Asseim at his 1804 marriage; at Asheim for his daughter Ingeborg's baptism in 1805. No rival Mons son of a Johannes was baptised in Gulen in 1771-1773 outside two short unread windows (Jan to early Feb and mid-Apr to May 1773), which the burial age argues against. The probate after the other Asheim Johannes, Johannes Clausen (opened 1 May 1801), lists his sons Claus and Hermund and four daughters, and no Mons. The 1801 census age of 29 is the only conflict, and the same household over-ages his brother Johannes by a year as well. The mother, Randi Torkildsdatter, stays Probable: no record names Mons's mother. ¶12

Mother: Randi Torkildsdatter, Probable (strong). His father Johannes Olsen Asheim married Randi Torkildsdatter on 5 Jul 1767 at Brekke church (betrothed 17 May 1767), six years before Mons's baptism, and she was still his wife in 1801, both then in their first marriage. No record names his mother. See her notes for the reasoning and the remaining check (marriages and burials Apr 1769 - Jul 1773). ¶13

Life story

Baptism: Baptised 7 Oct 1773 in Gulen parish as Mons, son of Johannes Olsen of Asheim: 'Johannes Olsen Asheims Søn N: Mons'. The godparents were Lasse Matthiesen, Lasse Colbeinsen, Rognald Asheim, and Hermund and Birthe, also of Asheim. The mother is not named; this register does not name mothers at baptism. A wife of a Johannes of Asheim was churched (introduced back into church after childbirth) on 5 Nov 1773, four weeks later (Gulen ministerialbok 1749-1776, Digitalarkivet 11443, canvas 166). ¶6

1801 census: Mons Johannessen, 29, unmarried, farm servant (tieneste dreng) in the household of Ole Johannessen, 32, skattebonde at Asheim (Asseim), Brekke. The household also holds Johannes Olssen, 60, called 'Mandens fader' (the husband's father), living on a farm pension (kår), with Randi Torkieldsdatter, 58, and three more Johannessen servants: Johannes 27, Torkield 16 and Ingerborg 23. Birth about 1772 is inferred from his age. ¶2

Marriage: Engaged 2 Jun 1804 and married 1 Jul 1804 at Brekke church, as a bachelor (unge-karl) of Asseim, to Kari Johannesdatter, an unmarried woman (pige) of Tynjum (Gulen church book 1798-1808, marriage index). ¶4

Of Asheim, Brekke. Named as the bride's father at Ingeborg Monsdatter's 1828 marriage. Not researched further. ¶1

Burial (per index): Mons Johannesen, Gaardmand (farmer), of Asheim, died 23 Aug 1844, buried 26 Aug 1844, aged 70 11/12 (Gulen church book 1837-1847, burials 1844, p.264, no. 25; index pg00000002263972, card). The age gives birth about Sep 1773. No wife, parents or children are named. ¶10

Correction (supersedes the earlier 'Correction:' and 'Parents (13 Oct 2026...)' notes where they say 1771 is unread): every Gulen baptism of Jan-Oct 1771 has now been read. The only boys named Mons were sons of Knud Breivigen (26 Mar 1771, buried as an infant in Jul 1771) and Jens Udvær (15 Oct 1771); no Mons son of any Johannes, and no rival son of Johannes Clausen of Asheim. With Nov-Dec 1771, all of 1772, Feb-Apr and Jul-Dec 1773 already read, the audit read canvases c162 (7 Feb - 13 Apr 1773) and c164 (about 1 Jun - 18 Jul 1773) with no Mons. The only unread windows in 1771-1773 are 1 Jan - 6 Feb 1773 (about c161) and 14 Apr - end of May 1773 (c163). A Mons born in those weeks would have been about 71 3/12 to 71 7/12 at death, not 70 11/12. The 1774 baptisms are not reported as read. ¶11

Corrections 1

Correction: The birth was previously given as about 1772, inferred from his age of 29 in the 1801 census. The 7 Oct 1773 baptism makes him 27 at the census (Feb 1801); his brother Johannes (27 in 1801) would also be over-aged by a year, so the household's ages look inflated. Searched: every Gulen baptism of 1772, Feb-Apr 1773, Jul-Dec 1773 and Nov-Dec 1771. No other boy named Mons had a father named Johannes. NOT searched: Jan 1771-Oct 1771, the years his census age points to most, and Jan and May-mid-Jul 1773 (canvases c162, c164). Until 1771 is read, a Mons born then to the other Asheim Johannes (Johannes Clausen, whose children were also Johannessen) is not excluded. ¶7
