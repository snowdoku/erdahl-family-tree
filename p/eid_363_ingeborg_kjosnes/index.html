How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the grade · 1 alternative set aside · 1 open question

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Graded Probable on her link to her son Ola (Ole) Olsen Kjøsnes (see his page). Churched on the 19th Sunday after Trinity (15 Oct) 1702 as 'Olaf Bers. Kiøsnæs qv. Ingebor efter det 7de barn Olaf' (16310 canvas 14 left), seven weeks after the baptism of 'Olaf Kiøsnæs barn Olaf' (27 Aug 1702, canvas 13 right). The record names her as the mother of the 1702 Olaf. The identification of that child with the tree's Ola Olsen (holder 1728-1773) is Probable for the reasons given on her husband (Ole (Olaf) Bersvendsen Kjøsnes): no record 1702-1728, and a rival Ole (son of Ole Guttormsen, b. about 1699) at Kjøsnes. The bygdebok (Selbu i fortid og nåtid vol. 7, Kjøsnes, canvas 299) calls the holder's wife Ingeborg and numbers Ola, b. 1702, as the seventh child, as the churching does. ¶1

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Do not confuse her with Ingebor, wife of Ole Guttormsen Kjøsnes, churched on Palm Sunday 1702 after her third child Siri (16310 canvas 12 right); a godmother 'Ingebor Kiøsnæs' at the August 1702 baptism is presumably that other Ingebor, since a mother was not her own child's sponsor. Birth: before about 1662, since she had a seventh child in 1702 and the eldest (Brynhild, per the bygdebok) about 1679. Her patronymic, parents and death are not found. ¶2

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Test record: a probate after her or her husband (after 1713), or a later probate naming Ola Olsen Kjøsnes as her son. ¶3
