Life story

Another child: Lesja 9236, p. 305 (canvas 145), Dom. Rogate 1746 at Verket, 'item Rolf Slettums Knud', godparents Anders Kieldshuus, Poul Enstad, Hans Eneboe, Aase Enstad, Eli Slettum, born 29 Apr 1746; 'Rolf Slettums Anne' was churched at Verket on Trinity Sunday 1746 (p. 306, canvas 146). A son Knud fits Rolv's own patronymic Knudsen. ¶5

New records, read on the scan of Lesja 9236: 'Rolff Slettums Hans', born 20 Mar 1750 (p. 348, canvas 167); 'Rolv Knudsøn Slettum og Anne Pedersdatter', child born 31 May 1756 (p. 421, canvas 203), and the churching of 'Rolf Slettums Anne' (p. 422, canvas 204). These give his full name at Slettum in 1756 (tying the 1783 burial of Rolf Knudsen of Slettom to the father of children there in 1750-56) and his wife Anne Pedersdatter (see her page). Grade unchanged (Probable, graded on his link to Kari Rolvsdatter): none of these names Kari. No probate card for Slettum exists in the Nord-Gudbrandsdal sorenskriveri probate card index (source 39945, filed alphabetically by farm; the Lesja cards run from Sletten (images 12293-12295) directly to Sletviken (12297)), so no probate of Rolv or of a Knud of Slettum is indexed. ¶3

Probate after his first wife (Nord-Gudbrandsdal skifteprotokoll 1752-1764, fol. 428a, 22 May 1759, 'Sletten nordre i Lesjeskogen (Nordre Slettum)', probate cards 39945 images 12275-12277): Anne Pedersdatter, married to Rolf Knudsen; children Peder 14, Knud 13, Hans 9, Endre 5, Rolf 1, Kari 11, Marit 7, Anne 1/4. This names him as father of Kari (see her page) in a second primary record. Parents (Probable, from the Lesja bygdebok vol. 1, canvas 370): Knut Rolfsen (b. about 1685, at Nordre Slettum 1718-1745) and Kari from Nordre Kvam, married 1717; the bygdebok gives him as 'Rolf, 1720-83', who took over Slettom in 1745 and married Anne Pedersd. that year, then in 1761 Guri (cf. the 1760 betrothal to Goro Jesbretsdatter). ¶7

Burial (index): 'Rolf Knuds[en]' of Slettom, buried 12 Oct 1783, birth year calculated 1720, Lesja ministerialbok 1777-1819 (0512P) (pg00000000422951). ¶1

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Probable father of Kari Rolvsdatter (graded on his link to Kari). Kari Rolvsdatter (b. about 1751) was 'of Slettum' at her 1775 betrothal and marriage, and Peder Rolvsen of Slettum (b. about 1745, at Slettum with son Rolv in 1801, father there 1779 and 1782) was betrothed on the same page and stood first godfather to her first child in 1776. Rolf Knudsen is the only man named Rolv found at Slettum in the indexed Lesja records 1725-1801, and his age fits a father of children born about 1745-51; Peder named his son Rolv (b. about 1781). No record yet names Kari or Peder as his child, so the link is inferred (identification by farm, patronymic and age). ¶2a

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed, graded on his link to Kari Rolvsdatter. The 1763 Lesja manntall of the extra tax (Ekstraskatt Gudbrandsdal 1762-66, source 45390, view 287, read on the scan) lists at No. 12 'Slette' [Slettum], self-owner, the couple 'Rolv Knudsøn' and 'Guro Jesbr.d.' [his second wife, betrothed 1760, 9236 c230] with 'Børn over XII Aar' (children over 12) 'Peder, Kari'; an inderst household on the farm is 'Knud Rolvsøn, Kari Endresd.' The column heading names them as the couple's children, and Guro married Rolv only in 1760, so Kari (over 12) is Rolv's child by his first wife. Continuity on the one farm with no gap over 15 years: 25 Feb 1748 'Bapt: Rolf Slettums Kari', born 19 Feb (9236 p. 325, c156) -> 1763 Kari, child over 12, in Rolv Knudsen's household at Slette (15 years) -> 1775 Kari Rolvsdatter 'of Slettum' betrothed and married to Joen Alvsen Nystuen while Rolv still lived at Slettum (12 years; he was buried from Slettom 1783). ¶6a
