Life story

Another Eidem farming in Mehurin Township alongside John P. Eidem, and a member of the same Lac qui Parle cluster; his exact place in it is not established. ¶1

1895 state census: Augusta Township, Lac qui Parle County, page 758, line 38 - 'Gunder Eidem, M, 6, born Norway', enumerated close to Andrew Eidem, 42, and Peter P Eidem, 36. 1905 state census: Augusta Township, page 691, line 31 - 'Gunder Eidem, M, 16, born Norway', in or beside the household of Peter P Eidem, 45, and Carrie Eidem, 41. ¶2

He is living with Peder P. Eidem's family in 1905 but cannot easily be Peder P.'s son: Gunder was born in Norway about 1889, while Peder P. had emigrated in 1880 and married Kari Slind in Lac qui Parle County in 1888. A nephew brought over, or a son of a fourth brother, fits better. No parentage is assigned here for that reason; the record that would settle it is the Norwegian emigration list or the Selbu parish register for a Gunder Eidem born about 1889. ¶3

Named as father on the birth certificate of his first child, read in full from the record image: 'County of Mehurin, Lacquepearl [sic]. Township of Mehuren. Registration district 2712. Full name of child: Konrad Bertrand Eidem. Sex Boy. Single. Date of birth March 5, 1912. FATHER: Gunder Eidem, post office Marietta, White, age at last birthday 27, birthplace Norway, occupation Farming. MOTHER: full maiden name Emma Wold, post office Marietta, White, age at last birthday 25, birthplace S.D., occupation Farming. Number of child of this mother: One; now living One.' Attended by Mrs W. R. Tuohy, midwife; filed 11 April 1912 by C. B. Ufford, registrar, Marietta. Certificate 1912-13933. ¶4

Speculative, flagged as such and not patched as a relationship: this is the obvious home for the Eidem men of the 1907-1910 cohort whom Oline's 1955 obituary excludes from her own family. LUDWIG EIDEM (b. 30 October 1907), JOSEPH EIDEM (b. 7 June 1910) and the register's unplotted ALVIN GORDON EIDEM (d. 1922) would all fit as his children, and the recurrence of 'Gordon' in both 'GORDON A. EIDEM d. 1919' and 'ALVIN GORDON EIDEM d. 1922' points the same way. ¶8

He and Emma lost two sons in succession, both named Gordon Alvin: Gordon Alven, born 23 October 1919 (cert. 1919-14695) and died 20 January 1920 (death cert. 1920-MN-006874), and Gordon Alvin, born 16 August 1922 (cert. 1922-16030) and died 17 March 1923 (death cert. 1923-MN-006894). Giving a second child a dead child's name was ordinary practice and is a useful warning when reading these indexes. ¶5

An Eidem head of household at Zoar who was completely unknown to this project and who has no Find a Grave memorial. He is in the South Dakota state burial register: 'GUNDER EIDEM 09-22-1958, lot 11, block 0, ANTELOPE VALLEY, DEUEL, ZOAR LUTHERAN'. ¶6

LOT 11 IS A COMPLETE FAMILY LOT and nothing else is in it: Gunder (d. 22 September 1958), ANNA EIDEM (d. 23 March 1965), IRENE EIDEM (d. 1913) and GORDON A. EIDEM (d. 1919). The pattern — a husband, a wife, and two children who died in 1913 and 1919 — is a household, and Anna is his wife. ¶7

The next record: Deuel County, South Dakota and Lac qui Parle County, Minnesota death certificates for 1958 and 1965, and the 1910, 1920 and 1930 censuses of Antelope Valley Township, Deuel County, which would show the household entire. ¶9
