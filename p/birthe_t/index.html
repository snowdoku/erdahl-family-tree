Life story

A maid of 'Aaning' when she married Lasse Rasmussen in 1773. ¶1

Remarried 5 Jan 1783 (betrothed 26 Dec 1782) Arent Knudsen, bachelor, of Nesje. The church-book index gives the bride as 'Berthe Knudsd., widow, of Aaning', which looks like a slip for Trulsdatter. Children with Arent: Martha (baptized 20 Mar 1784) and two baptized 27 Aug 1786. Arent remarried as a widower in 1798. ¶3

Death: Probable. The burial of Birthe Trolsdatter at Nessie, 23 Nov 1791, age 40, fits. ¶4

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Confirmed daughter of Truls Lassesen (Larsen) Aaning. He was her legal guardian in her husband Lasse's 1774 probate. The 1783 probate after his wife at Ånning names 'eldest daughter Brite Trulsdatter, married to Arent Knudsen Nesje'. ¶2

Corrections 1

Correction: Aaland 1932: Aaland's Erdal history names Lasse Rasmussen's wife 'Brite Trulsd. Aan.' (Brite Trulsdatter of Aaning), in agreement with the tree. His Nesje history (bruk nr. 3) lists Arent Knutsen (died 1814 aged 62, tenant from 1782), married first 'e. Brite Knutsd. Aan., † 40 a. g. 1791': a widow from Aaning, died 1791 aged 40. That is her, but Aaland repeats the church-book index's slip 'Knutsdatter'. Her father's 1783 probate names 'Brite Trulsdatter, married to Arent Knudsen Nesje', so Aaland's patronymic is wrong there. Her death in 1791 aged 40 agrees. ¶5
