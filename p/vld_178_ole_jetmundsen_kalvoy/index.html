About the family

Siblings (scan read): the probate held 8 Jun 1691 at Kalvøen after 'Sl. Berette Jetmundsdatter' (Sunnmøre skifteprotokoll 3A 01 fol. 34b-35a, view 24544/47) was between her surviving husband ('efterlevende Mand') Jetmund Knudsen, who took half the estate, and 'den Sal: Qvindes Brødre' (the deceased woman's brothers) 'Joen, Peder, Knud og Olle Jetmundsønner', with the two children (a boy and a girl) of the dead brother 'Søffren Jetmundsen', who took their father's share. Each heir's share is set out under his name ('Olle Jetmundsøn... 3 ort 24 sk'). So in 1691 an Ole Jetmundsen with brothers Jon, Peder, Knud and Søren and a sister Berethe was heir on Kalvøen, which fits the bygdebok's Ole Jetmundson of Store Kalvøy (same farm, the year after he leased his bruk). His sister's name, Berethe, is the name he gave his daughter about 1695, four years after this sister died. Identification of the heir with the 1704 bonde is by farm and name only (Probable). The bygdebok (p. 376) instead treats Ole and these brothers as sons of Jetmund Knutson (ca. 1655 - 1727) and Berte; that is ruled out by the protocol wording 'Qvindes Brødre' and by Ole's age (see Jetmund). ¶3

Father of Berethe Olsdatter (see her page), Probable; graded on his link to Berethe. The relationship is stated only by the bygdebok; it fits the primary records (she was an unmarried woman of Kalvøy in 1720 and a maid there at her 1725 engagement; he was the only Ole farming Store Kalvøy in 1701-1704; the Berte of the bygdebok has her exact marriage year, husband and death year), but no primary record names her as his daughter. The Sunnmøre probate index has no probate after him (no Ole at Kalvøy store, 1691-1790), so a Confirmed link would need the Borgund baptism about 1695 (not in the surviving register, which starts 1698) or a court or land record. ¶4

Life story

Bygdebok: the Haram bygdebok, Band 2b, Store Kalvøy chapter, p. 370 (nb.no b709b66866b959f610ebd56887cb57fe, canvas 370, text rebuilt from the content-search word boxes, so with gaps) has a brukar 'Ole Jetmundson, ca. 1658 - 1735', who took up the bruk on lease ('bygsla bruket') about 1690 and is called a son of a Jetmund. He is paired with a Karen ('Ole Jetmundson... Karen'), and his children are listed as: 1. Jetmund, ca. 1689 - 1761, later brukar; 2. Hans, ca. 1693 - 1763, probably brukar; 3. Berte, ca. 1695 - 1777, married 1725 Pål Jørgenson, ca. 1702 - 1749 (cross-reference to bruk 5). Berte's dates and husband match Berethe Olsdatter of Store Kalvøy exactly (engaged to Povel Jørgensen 22 Apr 1725; Poul's probate 1749; buried 16 Nov 1777, b. calc. 1695). ¶1

Context: the Haram bygdebok Band 5 (p. 192, Ytre Hildre bruk 9-5) has Jetmund [Knutsen] of Kalvøya (ca 1655-1727) marrying in 1692 Inger [Paulsdatter] of Indre Hildre, which is the 1692 remarriage that Band 2b gives to Jetmund Knutson; it concerns the widower of Berette Jetmundsdatter, not Ole. ¶6

Card index: the Sunnmøre probate card for the 3/6-1691 skifte (source 77797, image 1491) summarises it as: Berette Jetmundsdtr. Kalføen; 'Enkemann: Jetmund Knudsen'; 'Brødre: Joen Jetmundsen, Peder, Knud, Olle'; 'Brorbarn: Simen Hofuersen, Gunild Hofuersdtr.' (one share); 'Farbrødrene skal ha tilsynet med de 2 barna'. This agrees with reading of the protocol (heirs are the deceased woman's brothers, so Ole's father was a Jetmund), except that the card reads the dead brother's name as Hofuer (Håvard), not Søffren; the children's patronymic Hofuersen supports Håvard. Check the protocol (24544/47) for the brother's name. ¶7

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the identification · 1 correction · 2 searches with no result · 1 open question

The case for this identification 1

Primary support: the 1704 list of marines has on Store Kalvøy 'Ole, bonde, 50' (b. about 1654) with sons Jetmund Ols. 14 and Hans Ols. 8, the same two sons as the bygdebok's Jetmund (ca. 1689) and Hans (ca. 1693). The probate after Jetmund Olsen of Store Kalvøy, 1761 (3A 19 fol. 471b), is that elder son. The bygdebok gives about 1658 for his birth; the 1704 roll implies about 1654. ¶2

Corrections 1

Correction (scan read): the dead brother in the 3/8 Jun 1691 Kalvøen probate is Hoffuer (Håvard) Jetmundsen, not Søffren. On the scan (24544/47, fol. 35) the heir heading reads 'Hoffuer Jetmundsens Børen' with the names 'Simen og Gunnilde' beneath, and the introduction lists the brothers Joen, Peder, Knud and Olle Jetmundsen 'samt Hoffuer Jetmundsens børn' Simen and Gunild. This agrees with the card index (77797/1491, 'Simen Hofuersen, Gunild Hofuersdtr.'). Ole's own share is headed 'Olle Jetmundsøn'. No change to his grade. ¶9

Searches that found nothing 2

Negative: (1) A farm search of the Sunnmøre probate index (domicile 'Kalvø*', probate filter) lists for Kalvøy store only Berte Jetmundsd. 1691, Mari Knutsd. 1713, Hans Jetmundsen 1726, Brite Guttormsd. 1749, Paul Jørgensen 1749, Berte Jørgensd. 1752, Einer Einersen 1752, Jetmund Olsen 1761 and later; no probate after Ole (d. about 1735) or Karen. The Hans Jetmundsen of 1726 is not Ole's son Hans (ca 1693-1763). (2) The views either side of the 1691 Kalvøen probate (24544/46 and /48) belong to other probates (24 Apr 1691 and 18 Aug 1693); the 1691 entry itself gives no ages for the heirs. ¶5a

Negative: no probate after Ole Jetmundsen of Store Kalvøy (d. about 1735) or Karen in the typed registers to Sunnmøre skifteprotokoll 9A (1732-1735, 24552) and 9B (1735-1739, 24553); neither register has any Kalvøy entry. 9A has an 'Ole Jetmundsen, Braute, Valde [skipreide], 1733, fol. 60', a different man on Brautene in Sykkylven, not Kalvøy. No Sunnmøre panteregister exists before 1744. ¶8
