Life story

Farmer at Sandvik, Selbu, in the 1701 manntall (38461 c111, pp. 200-201): 'Paul Pedersen', his age read 26 but more likely 46 since his eldest son was 17, with sons Peder Paulsen 17, Jon 11 and Gunder 8. ¶1

Father added from the bygdebok (Selbu i fortid og nåtid vol. 3, Sandvik canvases 373, 375), Probable: '1701-1713 Pål Pedersen, f. ca. 1656, d. 1715, [...] kona [...] 1718', son of Per (Peder) Pålsen (b. ca. 1610), who held the bruk from 1637 to the 1680s, 'da sønnen Pål overtok'. Children listed: Goro ca. 1680 (d. 1703?), Per ca. 1684, Brynhild ca. 1689 (m. Ola Jonsen Sandvik), Jon ca. 1690, Gunnar ca. 1693 (m. Berit) - matching the 1736 skifte family. Then '1714-1727 Jon [Pålsen], f. ca. 1690, d. 1727. G.1714 [med] Marit [...] ca. 1690' and '1728-1754 Marit [...] enke etter Jon Pålsen Sandvik'. His wife's name is in words not recovered. Death 1715 is the book's date (the tree had 'before 1728'). ¶4

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the grade · 1 alternative set aside

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Graded Probable on his link to Joen Povelsen Sandvik (see his page). For: the skifte after Joen Olsen Sandvigen (24751 fol. 649, Feb 1736) names on the mother's side three Povelsen brothers of Sandvik, 'Morbroderen Peder Povelsen Sandvigen', 'Morbroderen afgangne Joen Povelsen' (with Joen's five children, exactly the children of the 1728 skifte) and 'Morbroderen afgangne Gunder Povelsen'; and the 1735 skifte after their sister Brynild Povelsdatter (24751 fol. 641) calls Peder Povelsen her son's 'Morbroder'. So Peder, Joen, Gunder and Brynild were siblings, children of a Povel. The 1701 manntall has at Sandvik one Paul Pedersen with sons Peder 17, Jon 11 and Gunder 8 - the same three brothers in the same order, on the same farm, and no other Paul/Povel on the farm (the other Sandvik household is Jon Jonsen 86 and sons, the father's side of Joen Olsen). ¶2a

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Not Confirmed: the 1701 list names Jon as Paul's son, but the next record of Joen Povelsen is 1728, a 27-year gap (rule: 20+ years gives Probable). The sibling-set match makes the identification very strong. ¶2b
