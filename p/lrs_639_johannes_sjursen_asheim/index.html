Life story

Bygdebok (Asheim gnr. 50, bnr. 32, canvas 247): 'Gullak Johannessen' (1667-1743 per the book), brukar 1690-1740, is called son of 'bnd. Johannes Sjurson' and 'Kari Arnesdtr.' (citation 'T.'). Lower on the same page: 'Johannes Sjurson... 1642 d. 1706' and, '8... 1706', 'Kari Arnesdtr.... 1639 d. 1709', followed by their children, among them 'Gullak Johannessen', a '...Johannesdtr. 1673 d. 1709', 'Ola Johannessen' and 'Brita Johannesdtr.' (Sande), with 'T. arv... rdl.' (an inheritance division cited from the court/probate records). ¶1
