About the family

Daughter Marie was married in 1814 to Hans... of Oppedal. If she is the Mari Torsteinsdatter who married Torkild Monsen in 1802 (he was dead by 1809), this was her second marriage; that is consistent but not proved. ¶8

Father's probate: on 12 and 13 Mar 1774 the probate court registered and appraised at Engesetter the estate of the late Iver Gullacksen, between his widow Kari Clausdatter and 'his children begotten in an honourable marriage', 4 sons and 3 daughters: eldest son Hermund Iversen of Ytre(?) Brekke, present; second son 'Tosten Iversen', of age (myndig) and present; third son Jon Iversen, apparently in the King's service (reading uncertain); fourth son Gullow Iversen, of age; eldest daughter Kari Iversdatter, with her husband Iver Larsen Haugeland, both present; second daughter Agathe Iversdatter, 23, present; and third daughter Ingeborg Iversdatter, 12 1/2. Appraisers Lasse Johannesen Riisnes and Ole(?) Johnsen Engesetter. Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll A 9 (1772-1782), p. 359, no. 8; the heirs' list continues on p. 360, not read. ¶9

Father (Probable): the 'Tosten Iversen' of the 1774 probate is taken to be this Torstein Iversen because he was of age in 1774 (fits b. about 1734), the probate was held on the farm Engesetter where Torstein was skattebonde in 1801 and died in 1813, and his eldest son was named Iver. ¶10a

Mother (Speculative): Kari Clausdatter is named in the 1774 probate as Iver Gullacksen's widow. The probate calls them his children 'tilsammen avlede' (begotten together) in an honourable marriage, which suggests Kari's, but the children's span of about 27 years would put her near 46 at Ingeborg's birth if she bore Hermund, so an earlier wife is still possible. The children's span, from Torstein (b. about 1734) and his elder brother Hermund to Ingeborg (b. about 1761), is about 27 years or more, which suggests that Iver may have had an earlier wife. His daughter Kari (16 in 1801) may be named after her; that is an inference only. ¶11

Siblings (from the 1774 probate): Hermund (eldest, of Ytre[?] Brekke), Jon, Gullow, Kari (married Iver Larsen Haugeland), Agathe (b. about 1751) and Ingeborg (b. about 1761). This supersedes the earlier untested idea that he was a brother of Rasmus Iversen of Botnen. ¶12

Mother: now Probable (was Speculative). The 1774 probate p. 359 calls the seven children begotten together by Iver Gullacksen and Kari Clausdatter, and the arvefald on p. 359 gives each son, Hermund the eldest included, an equal share 'udi Fædrene Arv og Modergave' (paternal inheritance and mother's gift); no first-marriage heir is seen. The 27-year span of the children remains a caution; see Kari Clausdatter's notes. Tosten's own lot (lower p. 360 or p. 361) was not read. ¶14

Mother's probate: on 14 Apr 1799 the probate court at Engesæter divided the estate of the late Mari Clausdatter among 'her surviving children', 4 sons and 3 daughters, of whom the second son is Tosten Iversen Engeseter, of age, living on the farm Engeseter. The other heirs are the same as in his father Iver Gullacksen's probate of 1774: Hermund Iversen of Ytre Brekke, John Iversen, Gullach Iversen of Haugland, Karie Iversdatter (dead, leaving a daughter of 20 with her father Iver Lassesen Haugland as guardian), Agathe Iversdatter, linked to the late Lasse Johannesen Riisnes, and Ingeborg Iversdatter, married to Ole Olsen Engeseter. Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll 11b, fol. 546 and audit. ¶15

Mother: now Confirmed (was Probable). Her own probate names him as her son, so she was his mother, not a stepmother. The 1774 probate calls her Kari, the 1799 probate Mari; the identical list of heirs shows they are one woman (see Kari Clausdatter's notes). ¶16

Father: Iver Gullacksen, now Confirmed (was Probable). His 1774 probate names Tosten Iversen as his second son, and the 1799 probate after the widow names the same seven children, with Tosten living at Engeseter. This ties the 1774 heir to this Torstein of Engesætter (see Iver Gullacksen's notes). ¶17

Siblings in the 1801 census (Probable identifications; the census index names no fathers): Hermund Iverssen, 68 (b. about 1733), head (huus fader), skattebonde, 'Gift 1ste gang', at Brekke Yttre household 04, with wife Anna Torkieldsdatter, 58, 'Gift 2dn gang'; Joen Iverssen, 61 (b. about 1740), head, skattebonde, 'Givt 2dn gang' (married for the second time), at Høye household 01, with wife Lisbeth Monsdatter, 41, and children of his first marriage born about 1778-1789; Gulak Iverssen, 59 (b. about 1742), head, skattebonde, 'Gift 1ste gang', at Hougland household 05, with wife Ragnilde Larsdatter, 47, in her second marriage. These match the 1799 probate heirs after his mother (Hermund Iversen of Ytre Brekke, eldest son; John Iversen Høje[?], third son; Gullach Iversen of Hougland, fourth son) by name, farm and birth order: Hermund about 1733, Torstein about 1734, Joen about 1740, Gulak about 1742. Agathe Iversdatter was not found in the Gulen 1801 index; a candidate outside Gulen, Agathe Iversdatter, 53, wife in her second marriage at Riisnes, Askvoll, is not yet proved. ¶18

Life story

Burial (per index): 'Torsten Iversøn', Mand, of Engesætter, aged 77 5/6 (b. about 1735), interred 1 Nov 1813; Gulen church book 1809-1825, index pg00000000771734. The 1801 census age of 67 gives b. about 1734, one to two years off. He is the only Torsten/Tosten/Torstein with an Iver patronymic in the Gulen burial index for 1812-1814. Scan not read. ¶4

Problem: in the probate the figure '16' follows the name of the third daughter, Brithe. If it is her age (b. about 1798), it cannot fit Sigrid Larsdatter, who was 64 in 1801 (b. about 1737), nor the Brite who was 18 in the 1801 household (b. about 1783). It is more likely a share or sum than an age, but the reading is unresolved. The probate's daughters (Marie, Marit, Brithe, Kranie[?]) otherwise match the 1801 daughters Mari, Martha, Brite and Kari or Ragnilde. ¶7

1801 census: Torstein Iverssen, 67, head of household (huus fader), married for the first time, skattebonde (tax-paying farmer) at Engesætter, Brekke, with wife Sigrid Larsdatter, 64, and their joint children Iver 28, Lars 26, Mari 24, Martha 22, Brite 18, Kari 16 and Ragnilde 14. Birth about 1734 is inferred from his age. ¶1

Context: his patronymic makes him a son of an Iver. listed him among the Brekke Iverssens of the right generation to be brothers of Rasmus Iversen of Botnen; that is untested and is not a link. ¶3

Probate: on 22 Oct 1814 the probate court registered at Engesæter, Lavik skipreide, the estate of the late tenant (Leilænding) Tosten Iversen, for his widow Sirie Larsdatter, assisted by Ole Olsen Engesæter. Heirs: eldest son Iver Tostensen and second son Lars Tostensen, both of age; eldest daughter Marie Tostensdatter, married to Hans K?ersen of Oppedal; second daughter Marit Tostensdatter, married to Lars Olsen Haugland(?); third daughter Brithe Tostensdatter; and the youngest daughter Kranie(?) Tostensdatter. Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll A 16, fol. 5a; continued on fol. 5b, not read. ¶5

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 3

Probable father of Mari Torsteinsdatter, wife of Torkild Monsen, because she married from Engesætter in 1802 and is the only Mari Torsteinsdatter in the 1801 Brekke district. Her baptism (c.1777) would confirm it. ¶2

Current grade (from patch 015, audit): both parent links are Confirmed. His father Iver Gullacksen (see his page) is proved by the 1774 probate after Iver and the 1799 probate after the widow, which name the same seven children; his mother Kari (Mari) Clausdatter (see her page) is proved by her own 1799 probate, which names Tosten Iversen Engeseter as her son. The grades in the older notes 'Father (Probable)', 'Mother (Speculative)' and 'Mother: now Probable' are superseded; see 'Mother: now Confirmed' and 'Father: Iver Gullacksen, now Confirmed'. Still Probable: his identity in the 1813 burial and probate (note 'Identity (Probable)') and his fatherhood of Mari Torsteinsdatter. ¶19

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed (upgrade sweep D), as parent of Mari Torsteinsdatter. Three records name or fix the link. (1) Her baptism at Brekke on 27 Mar 1778, 'Torsten Iwersen Engesetters D[atter] Nom: Mari' (Gulen ministerialbok 1776-1787, fol. 15a), names Torstein Iversen of Engesetter as her father; this register does not name mothers. (2) The 1801 census of Brekke (index, relationship word checked) lists Mari Torsteinsdatter, 24, unmarried, as 'Felleds datter' (daughter of both spouses) of Torstein Iverssen, 67, and Sigrid Larsdatter, 64, at Engesætter, household 02. (3) She married from Engesetter on 27 Jun 1802 (Torkild Monssøn of Indre Oppedal and Mari Torsteinsdatter, Gulen marriages 1802, index), and her second son was named Torstein; Iver Tostensen of Engesætter, the 1801 son Iver, was guardian of her youngest son at her husband's probate in 1809. The baptism names him; the census names him and his wife as her parents; the marriage keeps the bride on his farm. ¶20

The case for this identification 1

Identity (Probable): the burial and the probate are taken to be Torstein Iversen of the 1801 census because the farm (Engesætter), the widow's name (Sirie = Sigrid Larsdatter) and the sons Iver and Lars all match his 1801 household. The probate calls him a tenant, while the 1801 census calls him a skattebonde (tax-paying farmer). ¶6a

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Not to be confused: a second probate held at Engesetter (Evenvik prestebol) on 18 Oct 1785 concerns Iver Guttormsen and his widow Ragnilde Botelsdatter, whose children were all born 1764-1783. He is a different man. ¶13
