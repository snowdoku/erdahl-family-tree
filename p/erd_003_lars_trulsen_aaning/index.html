About the family

Father of Truls Larsen Aaning (Probable). His ward-son Truls Larsen Aaning was credited with an inheritance from this same probate (inheritance book fol. 146, 4 July 1728). Truls was already a ward in October 1724, so Lars probably died about 1724; the probate of 23 October 1724 may be his, but it lies in the 1715-1727 gap in the scanned probate protocols. His daughter, Truls's sister, is not named. ¶2

Life story

1701 census (Innvik prestegjeld, p. 250, Aaning; Digitalarkivet pc624062 and following): the household of 'Troels Lars.' (Truls, opsidder, born 1638) lists his sons Lars (born 1687) and Jørgen (born 1693), with a servant Rasmus Simensen (born 1677). This agrees with Aaland's age at death (36 in 1723, so born about 1687) and with the 1728 probate of Brynilde Trulsdatter naming her brothers Lars and Jørgen. Note the census abbreviates the father's patronymic as 'Lars.', while the 1664 census reads 'Lauridtzsøn'. ¶5

Aaland 1932: Aaland's Aaning history (bruk nr. 2): 'S. Lars, † 36 a. g. 1723, br. 1714 heile g. * (Synnøve Olsd., † 92 a. g. 1768).' Lars, son of the previous holder Truls, farmed the whole of Aaning from 1714 and died in 1723 aged 36 (born about 1687). His wife, bracketed by Aaland, was Synnøve Olsdatter, who as widow held the farm in 1724-35. His brother Jørgen ('Br. Jørgen', died 1774 aged 84) is the Jørgen Trulsen Aaning of the 1728 probate. The death in 1723 fits the tree's inference that he was dead before the October 1724 guardianship; the probate of 23 October 1724 (in the 1715-27 gap) is probably his. (Auditor: death entered as ABT 1723, since Aaland's year has no surviving church record behind it (Innvik burials start in 1750); the primary bound is dead before 23 Oct 1724 / 4 Jul 1728.) ¶3

Aaland 1932: Aaland gives his father as 'Truls, br. 1664 heile g.', who farmed the whole of Aaning from 1664 and was the son of Laurits Simonsen Brekke and Kristi Lassesdatter: see Truls Lauritsen Aaning. ¶4

Probate of his sister: in the probate after the unmarried Brynilde Trulsdatter of Aaning, held 4 July 1728 (Nordfjord skifteprotokoll L0006, fol. 146), the heirs include 'Lars Troelsen Aaning's' two surviving children, one son and one daughter. He was therefore dead by 4 July 1728 (his children inherit in his place; the place of death is not stated, but he was 'of Aaning'), and his siblings were Brynilde, Jørgen Trulsen Aaning and Christi Trulsdatter (married to Jørgen E[?]jem). ¶1

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
