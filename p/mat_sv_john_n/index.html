About the family

Named as the bride's father in the Selbu marriage entry of 8 NOV 1841: 'bruras far: John Nielsen Langlie'. He also stood as one of the two FORLOVERE (witnesses) at that wedding, which is legible only on the scanned page. ¶1

His patronymic means his own father was a Niels of Langli, and the 1801 census names him: Niels Olssen. ¶6

Parents: the 1801 census lists him as 'Jon Nielssen', 16, one of 'Deres boern' (their children) of Niels Olssen and Guri Olsdatter at Langlie, which is why his parents are now rated Confirmed (see their records). ¶13

Life story

1801 census: person 005 at rural residence 0026 Langlie, Selbu - 'Jon Nielssen', aged 16, 'Deres boern', in the household of Niels Olssen (54, Bonde og gaard beboer) and Guri Olsdatter (45). He is the only Jon Nielssen at Langli in that census. His brothers and sisters are Elen 23, Ole 21 (a 'National soldat ved skieloeberne', one of the Trondelag ski troops), Ole 11, Peder 9, Angnis 6 and Ingebor 2. ¶3

The marriage register for 1812, 1813 and 1814 (canvases 161-220 of the 1801-1815 book) has no earlier marriage or banns for him, so the 1815 marriage is his first, and their son Nils (b. 19 June 1815) is the first child found. ¶11

In the 1813 Selbu confirmation list he attests, with Ole Olsen Langlie, that his sister 'Ingebor Nielsd: Langlie, 15 Aar' had had natural smallpox - 'Jon Nielsen ibidem', still unmarried at Langli. ¶10

Married Marit Johnsdatter in Selbu church on Trinity Sunday, 21 May 1815: 'Egtev. 1. Arbeider John Nils. Langlie og Pigen Marithe Johnsd. Auglem' (Selbu ministerialbok 1801-1815, scan-only). The banns were read on Palm Sunday 19 March ('1ste Gang for Arbeider John Nielsen Langlie og Marethe Johnsd. Langlie'), Maundy Thursday 23 March and Easter Monday 27 March 1815. The register gives no ages, fathers or forlovere (witnesses) for marriages in these years. He is called 'Arbeider' (labourer) in 1815 and 'Gaardmand' in 1818. ¶9

Probate: 'John' is a son and heir in the skifte after his father Niels Olsen Langlie, closed 21 June 1816 (skifteprotokoll 10b, fol. 713). ¶15

Named again in his daughter's baptism of 29 November 1818, where the register calls him 'GAARDMAND Joh[n] Nielsen Langlie' - so he was a farmer, not a cottager. ¶2

Two of those brothers and sisters, OLE NILSEN LANGLIE and AGNIS NILSDATTER LANGLIE, stood godparents at his daughter Brynhild's baptism in 1818 - which ties the 1801 household to the 1818 register independently of either. ¶4

Silver tax: 'Joh. Nilsen', husmand (cottar), Langlie, 1 ort in the rounds of 1819 and 1821. (the 1816 silver tax was levied on property holders in several rounds, 1816-1821.) ¶16

1825 census: residence 0047* Langlie, household 01, person 012 - 'John Nilsen', 42, 'Hmd.' (husmand, cottar), in the household headed by his widowed mother 'Guri Olsd.', 70, 'Gmd. Enke', with his brother Ole Nilsen, 46, 'Gift soen' (married son), and Ole's family. With him: his wife 'Marit Johnsd.', 42, 'Hustru', and their children 'Nils Johns.', 11, 'Brynhild Johnsd.', 8, and 'John Johnsen', 3. The census column gives his status (husmand), not his relationship to Guri. His age of 42 implies a birth about 1783, against 16 in 1801 and 77 at his death in 1862 (both about 1785); the 1825 ages in this household run one to two years high. ¶12

Baptism: Selbu ministerialbok 1757-1801, Dom. 1 p. Trin. 1785 (29 May 1785): 'Döbt. Jon. Par: Niels Ols. Langlie Guri Olsd. T. Esten ib. Erich ib. Siur Langset Guri Langlie Birthe Sletaen[?]' - baptised Jon, parents Niels Olsen Langlie and Guri Olsdatter; godparents Esten and Erik of Langlie, Sivert (Siur) Langset, Guri Langlie and Birthe Sletaen[?] (last farm uncertain). His mother was churched ('Introd. 1. Niels Ols. Langl. Hust.') on Dom. 5 p. Trin., 26 June 1785. Read from the scan. It is the only Jon of Niels Olsen Langlie baptised January 1784 - December 1787, and the date agrees with his age of 16 in 1801 and 77 at his burial in February 1862. Birth date changed from 'ABT 1785' to 'ABT MAY 1785': the register gives only the baptism date, and births were normally baptised within days. This is a second, independent statement that his parents were Niels Olsen and Guri Olsdatter. ¶14

He was still alive in September 1859, when a 'John N. Langli' stood godfather to his grandson Sivert at Eidem - the Severt S. Eidem who died at Elk Point in 1940. ¶5

Death and burial: the Selbu register for 1860-1877 (burials 1862, folio 9, no. 20) enters 'Husmand John Nilsen', aged 77, of Langli, died 11 February and buried 16 February 1862. The parallel book for 1859-1868 (folio 177, no. 22) enters the same burial as 'Hmd. John Nielsen', 77, Langli, with the same dates. Neither gives a remark or cause of death. Both were read from the scans. ¶7

1 on the identification

The case for this identification 1

Identification of the 1862 burial: his age of 77 gives a birth year of about 1785, against 16 in the 1801 census; he lived at Langli; and he was alive in September 1859, when 'John N. Langli' stood godfather at Eidem. No other John Nilsen or Nielsen of Langli was buried in 1859-1880. Discrepancy: both 1862 books call him HUSMAND (cottager), while his daughter's 1818 baptism calls him gaardmand (farmer). Neither burial entry explains the change. That he had handed the farm on, or lost it, is a possibility only, not stated in any record read. The 1865 census of Langli (who then held his farm) or a deed (skjoete) in the Strinda og Selbu mortgage register would show it. ¶8
