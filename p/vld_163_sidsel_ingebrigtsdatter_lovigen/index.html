About the family

Mother of Jon Arnesen (see his page), Probable; graded on her link to Jon. Jon son of Arne of Løvigen was baptised at Sunnylven on 7 Feb 1751, the day Arne Løvigen's wife (not named) was churched. Arne's wife was Sidsel in 1745 and again in 1762, and no other wife of Arne or a remarriage is recorded between those dates, so she is taken to be Jon's mother. ¶3a

Life story

Marriage (scan): Norddal 1 Oct 1745, 'Arne Nielsen Berge og Zidsele Ingebrigtsd. Frøse' (engaged 23 May 1745). Their daughter Ingeborg was baptised at Strand in Aug 1745, and a daughter Siri was baptised at Norddal on 9 Oct 1746 to Arne Nielsen of Berge. In 1762 the couple at Løvigen are Arne and Zitzele with a daughter Siri over 12 (b. 1746 fits, aged 16); an Ingeborg Arnesdtr. of Løvig, aged 16, was confirmed at Sunnylven in 1763. That ties Arne Nielsen of Berge to Arne Nilsen of Løvigen (penance 1761) and shows that his wife in 1762 was still the Sidsel he married in 1745. ¶2

1762 extra tax (scan read): Sunnylven sogn, p. 37 of the Norddal kall manntall (source 49637, view 239), farm Løvigen, bondegods: 'Mand og Hustrue': 'Arne og Zitzele'; 'Børn over 12 aar': 'Siri'; three persons in all. Zitzele is the spelling of Sidsel used for 'Zidsele Ingebrigtsd.' at her marriage in 1745. ¶1

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Not Confirmed: no record names Jon's mother, and Jon himself (11 in 1762) is not listed in the household; his identification with Jon Arnesen of Melset is also only Probable. She came from Frøse (Frøysa); the 1762 list has an Ingebrigt and Guri on Frøse (view 238), a possible but unproved father. ¶3b

Searches that found nothing 3

Negative (scan read): the 1762 'Ingebrigt og Guri' of Frøse (49637 v238) is Ingebrigt Halkjeldsen of Frøysa, whose probate was held 9 Jul 1765 (Sunnmøre skifteprotokoll 3A 21 fol. 362a, view 24562/375; index pa00000000799260). The heirs are his widow, a Larsdatter (guardian Halkield Larsen Frøise), and five daughters, all young: Brithe about 20, Anne about 18, a second Anne(?) about 16, Guri about 13 and Helie(?) about 5, with guardians Halkield Larsen Frøise, Nils Erasmussen(?) Frøise, Halkield Olsen(?) Tronstad and Anders Andersen Aasen. Sidsel (married 1745, alive 1762) is not among them, so he was not her father. Her father must be an earlier Ingebrigt of Frøse; the Sunnmøre probate index has no Ingebrigt at Frøysa 1690-1764 (Frøysa rows: Iver Halkjeldsen 1710, Amund Jakobsen 1707, Elling Olsen 1707, Anne Gammelsd. 1736, Anne Serkvesd. 1738, Erik Iversen, Peder Isaksen and his widow Guri Endresd. 1743, Åmund Larsen and Christi Ellingsen 1751, Helga Jonsd. 1754). Possible brothers in Sunnylven: Niels Ingebrigtsen of Hole (godfather 1743, 1745), later of Aagernes (1755) and Liøen (1760), farms next to Løvigen, and Johannes Ingebrigtsen of Hole (godfather 1741). Not tested. ¶4

Negative: (1) 'Sunnylven og Geiranger 1' (nb.no e863833fa8d4a450599fa0f9671c6af7) is a general history volume (tax lists, sales and local administration), not a farm-by-farm genealogy. Its pages 78-86, 185, 214, 239, 341 and 408, rebuilt from about 2,000 queried words, hold lists of names (for example 'Ingebrigt Ingebrigtson Hole' in a list of 1776, p. 185), and no family statements. Sidsel/Sissel does not occur except on pp. 477 and 480 (as Sissel). It will not give her parents; the farm volumes are needed. (2) The Sunnmøre probate index has no Ingebrigt at Hole, Ågotnes, Liøen or Løvik, and no Ingebrigtsdatter wife at Frøysa before 1760. The only Nils Ingebrigtsen before 1760 is at Hoel in Dale skipreide (Stranda), probate registered 29 Aug 1753 (3A 18 fol. 513a, view 24559/47-48, scan read in part: widow Gure Arnesdatter). He is not the Niels Ingebrigtsen of Hole, Sunnylven, who was alive in 1755 and 1760. Heirs not read (on the following views). ¶5

Negative: 'Sunnylven og Geiranger 2: Gard og ætt' (nb.no f431f4f556226b9288864b7e09113da6) was harvested with about 3,000 words. Its farm chapters for Frøysa (pp. 76-79) and Hole/Bjørdal (pp. 52, 97, 171) give the 1700s brukarar only briefly; 'Sissel' occurs only on pp. 41, 52, 97, 171, 229, 320, 365 and 377, always in 19th-20th century families; 'Løvik' occurs once (p. 372), and no 'Arne Nilsson' with a Sissel appears. The volume does not give her parents. ¶6
