Life story

Laberg 1985 (Bale, printed p. 60): 'So vart det ein Anders til [about] 1690. Kona hans heitte Ingrid... Med [her] hadde han ein son, Ola. Då Anders døydde, vart enkja [remarried]...' - an Anders held this bruk on Bale until about 1690; his wife was Ingrid and their son was Ola (Ola Andersson, born about 1690). His patronymic and birth were not read. kgroenha.net gives the same (Anders, leased Bale about 1680-90, died about 1690). ¶1

1664-1666 censuses: an Anders held one share of Bale (1666: 'Anders bruger j lb Sm, 40 aar', no sons listed; bailiff 1664 puts 'Hallie[?] 26' under him). The other holders were Jon (83), Ole (24), Eirik (63), Anthon[?], Niels Eiriksen (26) and Johannes. If this Anders (b. ~1626) is Laberg's Anders who held Bale until about 1690, he was about 64 when Ola was born (~1690); a younger namesake is as likely. Not identified; no parent added. ¶3

Probate of his widow (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll 3a, fol. 6a-6b, 3 May 1720): Ingeri Palmesdatter of Balle left children by her 'forige Mand' (former husband), namely Olle Andersøn and the late Palme Andersøn. The first husband's given name is not read in the text; Anders rests on the sons' patronymic (and on Laberg). The 1701 census lists Palme (b. 1686) and Olle (b. 1689) Andersen at Balle, so Anders was dead or absent by then (inferred). Confidence stays Probable. ¶4

Widow's probate re-read (full-resolution crop of the preamble, Ytre Sogn 3a fol. 6a, viewer 24157 image 8): 'Anno 1720 d. 3 Maij... paa Balle, at skifte efter afg. Sal: Ingeri Palmesdatter imellem hendes efterlevende Mand [Lisel? / Niels?] Hermundsøn paa den ene og den Sal: Qvindis med forige Mand avlende Børn, [Olle?] Andersø[n], og med i Egtemand nu værende Datter Brite paa den anden side'. The preamble does NOT give the former husband's given name; it says only 'forige Mand' (former husband). So Anders rests on the children's patronymic (Andersøn) and on Laberg's bygdebok, and the grade stays Probable (graded on his link to Ola Andersen Bale). This closes the lead that the fol. 6a preamble might name him. What would still confirm it: a Bale probate after Anders himself about 1690-1700 (Ytre Sogn protocols before 1720 are partly unindexed), or the 1701 census entry for Ola at Bale with 'Anders' named as father. ¶5

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
