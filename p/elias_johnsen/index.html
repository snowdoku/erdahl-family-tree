Life story

Baptized 1816 at Borgund as 'Elias Petter Olaus', born 1 Dec 1816. Parents: John Jørgensen of Valderøy and Elisabeth Pedersdatter. ¶3

Confirmed 21 Oct 1832 at Borgund, naming parents John Jørgensen and Elisabeth Pedersdatter. ¶1

Married Berte Marie Knudsdatter of Lille Nørve 13 Jul 1846 at Borgund. He was a bachelor of Valderhaug, born about 1817; the fathers are named as Jon Jørgensen and Knut Larsen. ¶4

Farmer, fisherman and tenant at Valderhaug (1865, 1875 censuses). Other children: Helene (1854), Karl (~1858). ¶2

1865 census: head of household and 'Gaardbruger' (farm holder) at Valderhoug, rural residence 20, holding 135, in the Valderøen enumeration district of Borgund. With him were his wife Maria Knudsdatter (52) and their sons Martinus (15) and Karl (7). ¶6

He was 50 in 1865 and 75 in 1891, consistent throughout with his baptism on 1 December 1816. ¶8

1891 census: still head of his own household at Valderhaug, holding 135aab, aged 75, with his wife Marie Knutsdatter (1814) and their unmarried son Karl (1859), alongside the separate household of his son Martinus on the same farm - the usual two-generation arrangement on a Sunnmøre farm, where the old couple kept a house and a share of the land. ¶7

Death register: the Nordre Sunnmøre district court's register of deaths (dødsfallsprotokoll no. 5, p. 13, Borgund 1896 no. 26) enters Elias Johnsen of Valderhaug, kaarmand (a retired farmer living on a farm pension), aged 82, died 12 April 1896. The age is about three years high for his 1816 birth. His heirs are given as 'Enke og 3 myndige børn tilstede' (his widow and 3 adult children, all present), so his wife was still living. The informant was Martinus Valderhaug (the middle initial is unclear), presumably his son. A remark says a registration or other step would be reported; the reading is uncertain. ¶9
