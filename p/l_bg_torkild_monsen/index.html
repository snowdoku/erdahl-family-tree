About the family

Children: Lasse (baptised 20 May 1803), Torstein (1804), Else (baptised 2 Jan 1806) and Mons (1808), all at Indre Oppedal, mother Mari Torsteinsdatter (Gulen church book 1798-1808, index). ¶4

Parents: The census calls him the joint son of Mons Lassessen and Else Torkildsdatter, so the link rests on a primary record. Identity is clear: he is the only Torkild Monssen at Indre Oppedal in 1801; his father Mons Lassessøn was godfather to his first son and home-baptised his fourth; his sister Kari Monsdatter was godmother in 1808; and his children's names (Lasse, Else and Mons) repeat his father's patronymic, his mother and his father. ¶5

Parents (probate confirmation): the probate after his mother Else Torkildsdatter, closed 2 Jun 1812, names as her heirs 'Afdødes ældste Søn Torchild Monsen' (the deceased's eldest son Torkild Monsen), married to Mari Tostensdatter, whose children Lasse 9, Tosten 8, Mons 4 and Else 6 inherit in their dead father's place, beside the surviving widower Mons Lassesen. The 1823 probate of Mons Lassesen again gives his estate to Torkild's three sons and daughter Else. These two primary probate records, independent of the 1801 census, confirm Torkild as the son of Mons Lassesen and Else Torkildsdatter. The parent links and his parents' confidence were already Confirmed from the 1801 census and remain Confirmed, now on three primary records. ¶10

Siblings (from the probates): Anna Monsdatter, the eldest daughter, married to Jens Gulltormsen (of Gjerd[e]slætten[?] in 1808; Grytten[?] or Miaanes[?] in 1812), dead by 1812 and survived by Gulltorm Jensen 22, Hendrik 16, Mons about 10, Torkild and Else (Eli) Jensdatter; Giertrud Monsdatter, married by 1823 to Iver Tostensen of Engesætter, who was probably Mari Tostensdatter's brother; and Kari Monsdatter, 18 in 1808, married by 1823 to L[asse?] Pedersen of Ørneberg[?]. ¶11

Life story

Of Indre Oppedal, Brekke. Named as the groom's father at Lasse Torkildsen's 1828 marriage. Not researched further. ¶1

1801 census: Torkield Monssen, 22, unmarried, is a joint son (felleds søn) of Mons Lassessen, 60, skattebonde, and Else Torkieldsdatter, 53, at Indre Oppedal (Oppedal Indre), Brekke. His sisters Giertrud, 16, and Kari, 12, are in the same household. Birth about 1779 is inferred from his age. ¶2

Marriage: Engaged 6 Jun 1802 and married 27 Jun 1802 at Brekke church to Mari Torsteinsdatter of Engesætter; he was of Indre Oppedal (Gulen church book 1798-1808, marriage index). ¶3

Military service: on 26 Sep 1808, at his parents' barneskifte (a division of the estate with the children while the parents were alive), the court recorded that 'Benævnte Torchield Monsen er som Soldat commanderet til Bergen' (the said Torkild Monsen is ordered to Bergen as a soldier), so a guardian was appointed to watch his interest. This was during the war of 1807-1814. ¶7

Death: between 26 Sep 1808, when he was alive and serving as a soldier, and the probate of Mar 1809; the registration of his estate on 18[?] Jan 1809 suggests he was dead by mid-January 1809. No burial has been found. That he died in military service, or in Bergen, is an inference from the timing only; no record states it. ¶9

Probate: his estate was settled at Indre Oppedal (matr. no. 66) on 25[?] Mar 1809, after a registration on 18[?] Jan 1809; both day numbers are uncertain readings. The heirs were his widow Mari Tostensdatter, with Mons Lassesen of Indre Oppedal as her lauværge (a legal spokesman for a woman), and four children: Lasse 6, Tosten 4, Else 3 and Mons 1. Guardians (formyndere): Anders Torchieldsen for Lasse, Gulltorm Henrichsen of Indre Oppedal for Tosten, Iver Tostensen of Engesætter for Mons, and the mother for Else. The names and ages agree exactly with the baptisms of 1803, 1804, 1806 and 1808, which rules out the homonym of Yttre Oppedal (wife Brite Olsdatter). ¶8

Anders Torchieldsen: guardian of the eldest son Lasse in 1809, and present again in 1812 as Anders Torchildsen Ringereide[?]. Ringereide was the lensmann's (district constable's) farm, and in 1823 the lensmann was Nils Andersen Ringereide, so Anders was probably acting as lensmann. No kinship to Else Torkildsdatter is claimed. ¶12

Burial (per index): 'Torkeld Monsøn', Mand (married man), of Indre Oppedahl, interred 3 Jan 1809, aged 28 1/2, remark 'Omkom paa søen' (perished at sea). Gulen church book 1809-1825, burials 1809 (index pg00000000771319); the book was not read. Name, patronymic and farm match; the other Torkild Monsen of Yttre Oppedal is a different man. The age gives a birth about mid-1780, one or two years later than the 1801 census age of 22; the birth field is left at ABT 1779. ¶13

1 correction · 1 alternative set aside

Corrections 1

Correction: his death is now before 3 Jan 1809, at sea, from the burial index, replacing the earlier inferred window 'between 26 Sep 1808 and Mar 1809'. The earlier inference that he died in military service or in Bergen is withdrawn: the burial says only that he perished at sea. He had been ordered to Bergen as a soldier in Sep 1808, so he may have been lost on a passage, but no record says so. The probate registration of 18[?] Jan 1809 fits a death in late 1808. Whether his body was recovered is not stated. ¶14

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Homonyms, not this man: (1) Torkield Monssen, 29, son of Mons Torkieldssen, 69, at Yttre Oppedal in 1801, who had a daughter Anna baptised 24 May 1806 with wife Brite Olsdatter; (2) Torkild Monssøn, bachelor, of Lund, who became engaged on 10 Dec 1803 to the widow Ingerborg Knudsdatter of Myre. Neither should be merged with this Torkild of Indre Oppedal. ¶6
