Erich Bondesenc. 1586 – ?Probable

Life story

Known only from the patronymic of his son Erich Bondesen (written 'Erich Bundersen'), aged 78, farmer on Tuntland, Hjelmeland, in the bailiff's census of 10 May 1664 (DA source 35571, p. 59). Born before about 1566, since his son was born about 1586. Where he lived is not stated; he may have held Tuntland before Erich. Graded Probable on his link to Erich Bondesen: the patronymic names the father's given name, but nothing else about him (farm, dates) is known from a record. ¶1a

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
