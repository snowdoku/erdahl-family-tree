Life story

She is probably the mother of Elias Larsen Slind (b. 20 Nov 1881), the Zoar Lutheran pallbearer, and of Gunder Larson Slind (1886-1947) of Fort Snelling - but nothing read says so, so this is Probable. ¶2

Named as the mother on the 30 January 1959 Washington death certificate of Gunder Slind of Port Blakely, Kitsap County, with the father given as Lars Slind. Her maiden name is given as HANSTAD, which is a Selbu farm. ¶1

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
