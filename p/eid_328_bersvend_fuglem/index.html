About the family

Proposed (Probable) as the father of Peder Bersvendsen Fuglem (see his page), graded on his link to Peder: Peder Bersvendsen (52 in the 1666 vicar's list, 50 in 1664, so born about 1614) is one of the two users of Fuglem in 1664-66, beside Jon Olsen (54), exactly where 'Jon Fuglemb' and 'Bersuend' held the farm in 1624. Farm succession plus the patronymic make Bersvend of Fuglem the likely father, but no record names the relationship, and other Bersvends were taxed in Selbu in 1624 (Langlien, Vigen, Stokke, Rodstad?), so this stays Probable. Peder named a grandson Bersvend (b. c.1686, 1701 manntall), consistent with the naming pattern. ¶2

Life story

1624 leidang roll for Selbu tinglag (jordebok for Trondheim len 1624, Digitalarkivet 39890, view 76 right from the scan): the third holding under 'Selbo Tinglaugs' is 'Jon Fuglemb' and 'Bersuend ibid' bracketed together as one holding at Fuglem. So a Bersvend was a user of Fuglem in 1624, and must have been born before about 1600. ¶1

Bygdebok lead (Selbu i fortid og nåtid vol. 3, Fuglem canvases 213-214), Speculative only: '[i] 1590 [...] Bersvend [...] Jon' and '[...] 1600 [...] Peder [...] antakelig' (213); '[...] Bersvend Pedersen [...] som [...] i 1612'; '[...] av Bersvend. [Var] det deres mor? Kanskje [...] Marit Fuglem' (214); '1645 [...] Bersvend [hadde] kone, en datter'; 'Han døde [...] av 1650-åra'; '[I] 1660 fikk Peder Bersvendsen, f. ca. 1612, bygsel'. So the book calls him Bersvend Pedersen (of 1612), perhaps son of a Peder who used Fuglem about 1600, and floats a Marit Fuglem as mother - with a question mark. Not added as parents. ¶3

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
