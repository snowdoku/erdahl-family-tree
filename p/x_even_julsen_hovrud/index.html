About the family

Father (Probable): Jul Evensen of Hovrud, b. about 1717, son of Even Jonsen and Marit Juulsdatter (Valdres probate cards 6179, 1728, and 6181, 1751). See the note on Jul Evensen for the argument. Even's son Jul (b. 1782) and daughter Astri (b. 1780) carry the names of Jul Evensen and Astri Jonsdatter. ¶4

Life story

Burial: 'Even Juelsen Hovrud', aged 68 (b. about 1753), buried 28 Oct 1821 in Nord-Aurdal. He was named as the groom's father 'Even Juulsen Hovrud' at a marriage in 1829, although that record is dated after his burial. His father was named Jul, but no further identification is possible, because Aurdal baptisms and marriages for about 1750-1780 are not transcribed. ¶2

Baptism: baptised as a twin with his sister Marit in the Svenes annex of Aurdal in summer 1754 (entry 'Ditto' after 30 June; churching 28 July), 'Juel Evensøn Hoverud og Astri Joensdr. Tvilling børn N. Even og Marit' (DA 9361 p. 439, canvas 222). Godparents included Lars Hoverud (his father's brother) and Ingri [Mid]tstrand. This fits his confirmation in 1771 at 17 and replaces the estimate 'about 1753' (from his census age of 48 in 1801, which is a year high). ¶7

Confirmation: 'Even Juelsøn Haavrud', 17, confirmed at the 1771 confirmation at Svenes [annex list] (Aurdal ministerialbok 1763-1781, DA 9362, p. 405, canvas 193), so born about 1754. His parents Juel Evensen Hovrud and Astri Jonsdatter Dahle married on 15 Oct 1753 (DA 9361 p. 88, canvas 313). A 'John Juelsøn Haavrud', 15 1/2, confirmed at Bruflat the same year (p. 404), is probably his younger brother. ¶6

Marriage: 'Even Juelsøn Haavrud' married 'Marit Olsdat. Mitstrand' (Marit Olsdatter of Midtstrand) on 9 Oct 1780 in Aurdal parish. The entry records only the wedding: no betrothal, sponsors (forlovere), fathers or ages are given. (Aurdal ministerialbok 1763-1781, source 9362, p. 253, read from the scan.) ¶3

1801 census, Aurdal, Haarud (Hovrud): Even Julsen, 48, husbond and gaardbruger, with wife Marit Olsdatter (48), children Jul (19), Jens (5), Astri (21), Ragnild (16) and Sigri (11), and his mother ('mandens moder') Astri Jonsdatter, 90, a widow of her second marriage living on føderaad (retirement support from the farm). ¶1

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: the 1801 census for Aurdal, Haarud, household 01 (read on Digitalarkivet), lists Astri Evensdatter, 21, as 'Datter', unmarried, 'hos forældrene', in the household of which Even Julsen is head (person 001). She married Ole Juelsen of Midtstrand in 1803 as 'Astri Evensdatter, pige, Hovrud'. The census names her as the head's daughter, so Even is Confirmed as Astri's father (raised from Probable). His own father (Jul Evensen) is Probable. ¶5
