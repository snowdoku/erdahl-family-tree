About the family

Parents entered (Probable as her parents): 'Eliza', aged 6, is listed among the 'children of the said Tho: Jostlin' with Rebecca his wife in the Increase of April 1635, which puts her birth about 1629; Thomas Joslin's will of 9 May 1660 names his daughter Elizabeth 'Emons' (NEHGR 71:253; 'Elizabeth wife of Edward Yeamans', NEHGR 55:303), Emmons being the variant of Yeomans used for her first husband. Her own grade on her link to Christopher Kilby is unchanged (Speculative). Searched without result for a record of her alive as Kilby's wife after 1662: the Records of the Suffolk County Court 1671-1680 (Col. Soc. Mass. 29-30) have no Kilby entry at all; the Josselyn articles (NEHGR 55, 71) give her no Kilby children and no death; her brother Abraham Josselyn's will (1669/70) names only his wife and children; and the 1917 article does not mention her after 1662. The Boston register has 'Elizabeth of John Kilby born Sept. 15' under 1686 (Rec. Comm. 9:170), with no mother named; if this was John Sr.'s child, his wife in 1686 was not Elizabeth (Joslin), then about 57, which fits a second wife such as the elder Sarah Kilby (born about 1642) by then. But Deacon John Jr. was having children with Rebecca from 1687, so the 1686 child may be his. Either way it says nothing about who bore John Jr. (about 1664) and Christopher (about 1665), and her grade stays Speculative. ¶3

Life story

Josselyn genealogies checked for her marriages: French (NEHGR 71:256) gives her m. (1) at Boston 21 June 1652 Edward Yeomans or Emmons, (2) at Boston 9 May 1662 'Edward' Kilby, with one child by the first husband, Edward, b. Boston 6 May 1657 (Boston register: 'Edward of Edward & Elizabeth Yeomans born 6th May'), and nothing after 1662. Zubrinsky (2026) dates her only 'c1629->1661'. Wessler (1961) lists 'Elizabeth Emmons' among Thomas's daughters with no Kilby data. Her father's will, read in Holman's verbatim transcript (Middlesex Probate 12967), says 'I do likewise give unto my daughter Elizabeth Emons ten pounds' (9 May 1660), when she was the wife or widow of Edward Yeomans. None of these sources gives her any Kilby children or a death date, so her grade on her link to Christopher Kilby stays Speculative. ¶4

How sure are we?

Speculative. A plausible candidate only. Treat this link as a question, not a fact.

1 on the grade · 1 alternative set aside

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Graded on her link to Christopher Kilby. Daughter of Thomas and Rebecca Joslin (NEHGR 71:256, per TAG 27:193); married (1) at Boston 21 June 1652 Edward Yeomans; (2) at Boston 9 May 1662 John Kilby Sr. (register: 'Edward Kilby & Elizabeth Yeomans widow'). Speculative as Christopher's mother: Christopher (b. about 1665) and his siblings John (b. about Jan. 1664) and Sarah (b. about 1668) were born within this marriage, but no record names their mother, and TAG 27:196 notes an elder 'Sarah Kilby, aged 55' in 1698 who may have been a second wife of John Sr. Her death date is unknown. ¶1

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Rival mother noted: TAG 27:196 abstracts Superior Court of Judicature file 451 (Wallis case, 1697/8): the deposition of 'Sarah Kilby, aged 55' (so born about 1642), dated 8 Jan. 1697/8, and Sarah Wallis's declaration referring to 'old Sarah Kilby'. A Sarah born about 1642 could have borne John (about Jan. 1664) and Christopher (about 1665) as easily as Elizabeth (born about 1628). No Kilby birth appears in the Boston register before 1685 (vol. 9 checked again), and no death of Elizabeth Kilby or second marriage of John Kilby Sr. was found. Speculative stays. The named rival is the elder Sarah Kilby; what would settle it is a record of Elizabeth Kilby's death, or of John Kilby's marriage to Sarah, before about 1664. ¶2
