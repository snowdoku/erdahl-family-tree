Life story

Bygdebok: Haram bygdebok, Band 5 'Hildrestranda', Alvestad (gnr 27) bruk 8-1, '1695-1734', p. 296: 'Jørgen Amundss., ca 1662, d. 1736', married (betrothal 26.12.1694) Anne Paulsd. of Indre Hildre (ca 1668-1738); children on p. 298: Brite 1695, Amund 1698, Ole 1700-1704, a child of 1701 (bruk 8-2), Paul 1702-1749 who married 1725 Berte of Store Kalvøy, and Berte 1705-1751. The rebuilt text gives no place of origin for Jørgen (the word after 'Amundss.,' is not in the harvested vocabulary). This agrees with the church-book and census evidence already noted; a compiled source, so no grade change. Test record (unchanged): a probate after him (d. 1736) or his widow (d. about 1738), not in the Haram or Sunnmøre probate indexes. ¶10

The father of Paul (Povel) Jørgensen of Store Kalvøy (born about 1701), known only from the patronymic. Two Haram probates (1752 and 1775) list Paul's sisters as Berte/Brite Jørgensdatter (born about 1692, married to Jon Olsen Alvestad), Synnøve Jørgensdatter (born about 1698/1701, of Alvestad, died 1785) and Brite Jørgensdatter of Store Kalvø (born about 1705, died about 1752). ¶1

Identified as Jørgen Aamundsen, tenant farmer at Alvestad in Haram parish (Probable, and a considerable strengthening of what was previously only a name from a patronymic). The reasoning: the Haram probates of 1752 and 1775 name Paul Jørgensen's sisters as a Berte/Brite Jørgensdatter born about 1692 who married Jon Olsen Alvestad, a Synnøve Jørgensdatter of Alvestad, and a Brite Jørgensdatter of Store Kalvø born about 1705. The Haram church book 1689-1737 contains exactly two matching baptisms - Berrete, baptized 27 April 1695, and Birrette, baptized 24 July 1705 - and both are daughters of Jørgen Aamundsen of Alvestad. No other Jørgen at Alvestad appears in the records of those years. ¶3

1701 male census, Alvestad in the Vatne district of Haram: 'Jørgen', aged 39 (born about 1662), 'Opsider eller Leilending, Enroullered' - a tenant farmer enrolled for naval service, with the remark 'verit marin' (has been a marine/sailor). The only other person in his household was his son Aamund, aged 3. Two other tenants, Iver (born about 1656) and Oelle Joensen (born about 1672), held the rest of the farm. ¶4

1704 list of marines (index): Alvestad, Haram: Jørgen, bonde, 42 (b. about 1662), with son Aamund Jørgensen, 6. No older Aamund is listed on the farm, so his father was dead or no longer counted by 1704. Paul (b. about 1701) is not listed (boys under about 5 were often omitted). No grade change. ¶9

He was a godparent at Alvestad on 3 February 1710 and again on 18 February 1721, and stood as a betrothal witness (trulovar) at an Alvestad wedding on 8 January 1701. ¶6

Burial: 'Jørgen Aamundsen', Alvestad, aged 74, interred 10 June 1736 at Haram (page 88, entry 257). Age 74 in 1736 gives a birth year of 1662, exactly matching the 1701 census. ¶5

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the grade · 1 on the identification · 1 search with no result

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Speculative identification: the 1701 male census for Haram (Vatne district) lists a Jørgen, 39 (born about 1662), tenant at Alvestad, with a son Aamund (3). Two of the daughters lived at Alvestad. No Jørgen appears at Kalvøen in 1701. This is not proven. ¶2

The case for this identification 1

Why his son Paul ended up on Store Kalvøy: the 1701 male census shows no Jørgen at Kalvøen in Borgund. The farm was then held by three tenants - Peder (born about 1652), Jetmund (about 1655) and Ole (about 1654) - with Ole's sons Jetmund (1689) and Hans (1694). Paul Jørgensen therefore came to Store Kalvøy from outside, which is consistent with an Alvestad origin and explains why two of his sisters lived at Alvestad while a third lived at Store Kalvø. ¶7

Searches that found nothing 1

Negative: no probate after Jørgen Aamundsen of Alvestad (bur. before 10 Jun 1736) in the typed register to Sunnmøre skifteprotokoll 9B (1735-1739, source 24553, images 2-8; no Jørgen at all under J) or 9A (1732-1735, 24552). The only Alvestad entry in 9A is Iver Olsen, Alvestad (Vatne skipreide), 1732, fol. 7b. Panteregistre for Sunnmøre begin in 1744. ¶11
