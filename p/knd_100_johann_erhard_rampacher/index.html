Life story

Birth (Speculative identification): Johann Erhardt Ramprecht, born Vaihingen 19 Oct 1682 to Johann Christoph x Maria Margaretha (record 4767443). The only Johann Erhard Rampacher born at Vaihingen in the index, but his second marriage (to Magdalena Barbara) is not indexed, so it is not proved that the 1704 groom and the 1720 father are one man. ¶3

Life (index, Vaihingen; 'Hl.'/'H' = Herr, a man of standing): married first Anna Margaretha on 9 Sep 1704 (record 4776351, no parents indexed); children Maria Elisabetha b. 29 May 1706 (a death row dated 21 May 1706, record 4399563, is presumably misdated) and Christina Elisabetha b. 25 Oct 1707 (d. 21 Jan 1710, record 4399744). Second wife Magdalena Barbara by 1720 (marriage not indexed). 'Hl Joh. Erhard Rampacher' died at Vaihingen 19 Jun 1722 (record 4400556); a Christina Elisabetha Rambacher, relative Joh. Erhard, died 18 May 1732 (record 4401710). ¶2

Widow, Probable identification: a 'Magdalena Barbara Rambacher' married Johann Friedrich [surname not indexed] at Eberdingen on 9 Nov 1723 (records 904729779/4729779, image 1184575-00442), 17 months after his death at Vaihingen on 19 Jun 1722; see Magdalena Barbara. No children of Johann Friedrich x Magdalena Barbara are indexed at Eberdingen, Vaihingen or Enzweihingen 1724-34. ¶8a

Erhard, Magdalena Barbara' (record 4769125, image 1184562-00180), and Jacob Friedrich's own 1744 marriage row names his father 'Erhard' (record 4777928). Ancestry 61023 index rows do not label roles (BRIEF audit 5 rule 3), so this rests at Probable until the image or a labelled FamilySearch twin is read; images not seen. ¶1b

The case for this identification 1

Continuity of the 1704 groom with the 1720 father: the first wife 'F[rau] Anna Margaretha', relative 'H Joh Erhard', died at Vaihingen on 8 Jan 1717 (record 4400121, image 1184565-00173), and a first child of the second marriage, Jacob Heinrich 'Kangacher' (= Rampacher), was born at Vaihingen on 29 Apr 1718 to 'Joh Erhard, Magdalena Barbara' (record 4769004, image 1184562-00172). So the 'H Joh Erhard' who married Anna Margaretha in 1704 (4776351), lost children in 1706 and 1710, and was widowed in Jan 1717 is the same man who had Jacob Heinrich (1718) and Jacob Friedrich (1720) by Magdalena Barbara and died as 'Hl Joh. Erhard' in 1722: one Johann Erhard Rampacher at Vaihingen throughout, with the title Herr each time. The second marriage (about 1717) is not indexed (searched: Magdalena Barbara and Erhard marriages 1709-1724 nationwide, Vaihingen marriages 1716-18). ¶4

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Rivals for his baptism: the Cannstatt Rampachers also used the name. Johann Erhard Rampacher x Maria Elisabetha of Cannstatt had sons Johann Erhard baptised there in 1687 (3293499), 30 Jan 1691 (3293500) and 15 May 1692 (3293504); one was buried there 27 Apr 1694 (3296258). A Cannstatt son of 1687 would have been 17 at the Vaihingen wedding of 1704, and the 1691-92 sons 12-13, so none fits the 1704 groom. The Vaihingen boy of 19 Oct 1682 (son of Johann Christoph x Maria Margaretha, 4767443) would have been 21. A 'Johnn Erhard Rampacher' buried at Enzweihingen in 1743 (5160833, relative Johann Christoph) is a later man. So his parents (see his page, Maria Margaretha) are raised to Probable on an elimination argument; this person's own grade (on his son Jacob Friedrich) is unchanged. ¶5
