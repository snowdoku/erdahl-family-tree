Life story

Baptism (Probable): Peder, son of the late ('afgangne') Peder Olsen of Giske-Ødegård, was baptized 24 Jul 1760 at Borgund, a posthumous son named after his father. This fits his age (41 in 1801, 55 at burial in 1814) and his lifelong residence on the farm. Another Peder, son of Peder Pedersen of Skarbøen, was baptized 15 Jul 1759 but has no link to Giske-Ødegård. Date given is the baptism date. ¶5

First marriage: Peder Pedersen, a bachelor of Giske-Ødegård, was engaged 28 Jun 1789 at Borgund to Birthe Knudsdatter of Giske-Ødegård. In 1801 her father Knud Olesen (68) lived with them. ¶6

Probate: as 'Søn Peder Pedersen', about 18 weeks old, he was the only child and heir in the skifte after his father Peder Olsen of Giske-Ødegård on 28 November 1760, with Ole Pedersen of Giske-Ødegård as his guardian (formynder) (Sunnmøre sorenskriveri, 3A 19, fol. 394a). The age fits his baptism on 24 July 1760. ¶7

Farmer at Giske-Ødegård, age 41, in the 1801 census, then in his first marriage. ¶1

As a widower he married Gurri Olesdatter of Giske-Ødegård 18 Aug 1811 at Borgund. He was buried 21 Aug 1814, age 55, about two months before his son Peder Johannes was baptized (30 Oct 1814). ¶2

This identification follows from the 1842 marriage of his son: groom from Giske-Ødegård, father Peder Pedersen. ¶4

Marriage: The Borgund ministerialbok entry for his second marriage confirms he was a widower of Giske-Ødegård when banns were asked on Whit Sunday, 2 June 1811, for him and Gurri Olesdatter of Giske-Ødegård. They married 18 August 1811 (10th Sunday after Trinity), with Lars Andersen and Ole Eriksen of Giske-Ødegård as marriage witnesses (forlovere). ¶8

Confirmation checked, no upgrade: the Borgund confirmation of 3 Oct 1830 (1531P 1828-1839, p. 108 no. 19, scan read) names 'Peter Johan Pedersen', born 1813 at Gidskeødegaard, son of 'Peder Pedersen og Gurri Larsdatter'. The father's name and farm fit him, but the 1813 birth year and the mother 'Gurri Larsdatter' disagree with the 1814 posthumous baptism of Peder Johannes and with his 1811 marriage to Gurri Olesdatter, so the entry may belong to another family on the farm. Grade unchanged (see the note on Guri (Gurri) Olesdatter). ¶9

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: Confirmed, graded on his link to his son Peder Johan Pedersen Røssevold (see his page). Records naming the relationship: (1) the Borgund baptism of 30 Oct 1814 (Borgund ministerialbok 1801-1826, 1531P, pd00000009040783) of 'Peder Johannes', father 'Peder Peders.' of Gidskeødgaard, remark 'K: afgangen Peder Pedersen Gidskeødgaard' (the late Peder Pedersen, who was buried 21 Aug 1814); (2) the 1842 Borgund marriage of the tree's Peder Johan, a bachelor from Giske-Ødegård born about 1814, naming his father as Peder Pedersen; (3) the 1830 Borgund confirmation of 'Peter Johan Pedersen' of Gidskeødegaard, parents 'Peder Pedersen og Gurri [Larsdatter]'. Continuity: the 1815 census of Giske-Ødegård (household 9) has 'Peder Pedersen', 1/2, 'B. hendes' (her child) of Gurri, with Peder Pedersen's sons of his first marriage Knud (17) and Peter (11) as 'stedbørn' (stepchildren), so the baby is the posthumous son of this Peder; the same boy is confirmed from Giske-Ødegård in 1830 and married from there in 1842, with ages (1865: 52; 1875: born 1814) that fit. ¶10a

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

No rival: the only baptisms in the Borgund/Giske index for 1813-1814 with a father Peder at Giske-Ødegård is this one (others: Sæter, Langved, Alnæs and Peder-fathered girls); the stepbrother Peter Pedersen (born about 1803) and Peter Pedersen 6 1/2 of household 2 (son of Peder Jenssen) are too old to be the 1842 groom. ¶10b
