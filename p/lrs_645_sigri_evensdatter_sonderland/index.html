Life story

Widow ('Sterbboets Enke') of Targiej Torrofsen at the probate held at Sønderland in Treungen on 15 Sep 1756, at which their four daughters Gunnil, Anne, Asloug and Margith Torgiejsdatter shared the estate with her (25673 view 331 right, register No 195). She was alive on that date. ¶1

Birth: her daughter Anne, wife of Orm Egilsen Tvedt, was buried in May 1786 aged 68 (born about 1718; Kviteseid ministerialbok 7982, canvas 183 right), so Sigri was born before about 1698, not merely before 1710. ¶3

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Confidence: Confirmed, graded on her link to her daughter Anne Torgiejsdatter (see her page): the probate divides the estate 'imellem Sterbboets Enke Sigri Evensdatter og den Salig Mands og Hendes efterladte Børn', i.e. the children of the dead man and of her, and Anne is the second child named. Birth before 1710 is inferred from four married daughters in 1756. ¶2
