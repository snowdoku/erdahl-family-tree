About the family

Mother of Mons: kept Probable, strongly supported. (1) She married Johannes Olsen Asheim on 5 Jul 1767 (church book, read from the scan); (2) in the 1801 census Randi Torkieldsdatter, 58, is still his wife and both are 'Gift 1ste gang' (married for the first time); (3) her 1821 burial age agrees with the 1801 age, so the 1767 bride, the 1801 wife and the 1821 widow are very probably one woman, and then she was his wife without a break from 1767 to 1801; (4) Mons was baptised 7 Oct 1773 as the son of Johannes Olsen Asheim, with no mark of illegitimacy, and a wife of a Johannes of Asheim was churched on 5 Nov 1773. The only alternative is that the 1767 Randi died, Johannes remarried before 1773, and later married a second Randi Torkildsdatter of the same age, with the census wrong about first marriages. No record names Mons's mother (this register names no mothers at baptism, and no probate after Johannes or Randi is indexed), so the link rests on this argument. Searched for the alternative: Jul 1768 - Apr 1769 (canvases 131-133) and the Ytre Sogn probate index for Asseim 1760-1830, with no remarriage of Johannes and no death of a Randi. Not yet searched: marriages and burials Apr 1769 - Jul 1773. Raise to Confirmed if that read finds no conflict. The same reasoning makes her the probable mother of the Johannessen children born 1768-1784 (Ole, Johannes, Ingerborg, Torkield), whom the tree does not yet link. ¶7

Parents (Confirmed): Torkild (Torkel) Monsen of Asheim (see his page), b. about 1719, buried 1793, and his first wife Ingeborg Gullaksdatter (see her page), long dead by 1793. The probate after Torkild, 11 Dec 1793, names 'Randei Torkildsdatter, gift med Johannes Olsen Asheim, tilstede' (Randi, married to Johannes Olsen Asheim, present) as his second daughter by Ingeborg; the division of 13 May 1793 names her 'Rannei'. The husband, the farm and the dates agree with every other record of her, so the identity is unambiguous (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll A 11b, Nos. 25 and 79). Her baptism of 12 Apr 1744 as the child of Torkil of Asheim and Ingeborg agrees independently with both parents. Her mother's probate of 6 Mar 1754 (Ytre Sogn A 7a No. 10) names her 'Rannej', 10, second daughter of Torkild Monsen Asheim and Ingebor Gullo[v]sdatter, which agrees with her 1744 baptism. ¶10

Siblings (from the 1793 probates; a godmother at her baptism was Siri Asheim, possibly her elder sister Sigri, unproved): full sisters Sigri Torkildsdatter, the eldest, married to Mons Torkildsen of Ytre Oppedal (son of Torkild Guttormsen, whose 1769 probate names him), and Anna Torkildsdatter, married to Iver Nielsen(?) of Plougland; a full brother Mons Torkildsen, an infant in Mar 1754 (very probably the Mogens buried 1783); half-sisters by her father's second wife Brithe Olsdatter: Ingeborg Torkildsdatter, married to Lars Knudsen, farmer at Asheim, and Belaug Torkildsdatter, 25 in Dec 1793. Her sister's marriage into Ytre Oppedal explains the two cautioners of Opdal at her own betrothal in 1767. ¶11

Mother of Mons (review): stays Probable. The 1793 probates add that the Randi Torkildsdatter who was Johannes Olsen Asheim's wife in 1793 was Torkild Monsen's daughter, and her sister's Oppedal marriage fits the Oppedal cautioners of the 1767 betrothal, so the 1767 bride and the 1793 wife are now even more clearly one woman. That further weakens the only alternative (a first Randi dying and Johannes remarrying a second Randi Torkildsdatter before 1773). But no new record names Mons's mother, and the gap set by audit is unchanged: the Gulen marriages and burials Apr 1769 - Jul 1773 are still unread. Raise to Confirmed when that read shows no remarriage of Johannes and no burial of a wife of his. ¶12

Life story

Baptism: on Quasimodogeniti (1st Sunday after Easter), 12 Apr 1744, at the service in Brekke church (52 communicants), 'Torchil Assejms born N. Rand[i]' was baptised: Torkil of Asheim's child named Randi; mother (Mater) 'Ingeber' (Ingeborg); godparents (testes) Guttorm Mathisen, Bendict Assejm, Ingeber Olsdatter and Siri Assejm. The mother was churched (introdusert) on Exaudi Sunday, 17 May 1744. Dates computed (Easter 1744 = 5 Apr). The last letter of the name is unclear (Randi or Rande). She was the only Randi baptised to a Torkil in Brekke Jan-Jul 1744. Gulen ministerialbok for Bo, Brekke, Lavik 1736-1746 (source 11441), canvas 32, left page. ¶8

Marriage: Betrothed (trolovet) on Rogation Sunday (Dom. Rogate), 17 May 1767, at the service in Brekke church, Gulen, and married (Cop., copulated) on 5 Jul 1767, per the margin note: 'Trolovet Johans Olsen Asheim og Randj Torchilsd.' The groom is of Asheim; no residence is given for the bride and no parents are named for either. The cautioners (forlovere, men who vouched that there was no impediment) were both of Opdal (Oppedal farm, Gulen); their forenames are uncertain, read either as 'Johans Ivers. Opdal' and 'Lasse... Opdal' or as three men. The margin day '5 July' is confirmed by the preceding betrothal at the same service, which carries the same margin date (Gulen ministerialbok 1749-1776, canvases 124-125). No other betrothal of a Johannes Olsen or a Randi was found Feb 1767 - Feb 1768. The entry gives no ages. From her 1801 census age (58) and her 1821 burial age (77 2/3) she was about 23-24 at the wedding. ¶5

Godparent: 'Randj Asheim' stood godmother on 11 Sep 1767 at the baptism of Niels, child of [forename uncertain] Asheim, beside a Johannes. Probably she, two months after her marriage to Johannes Olsen Asheim, but other Asheim households existed, so this is probable only. ¶6

1801 census: Randi Torkieldsdatter, 58, wife (Dennes hustrue) of Johannes Olssen, 60, both married for the first time (Gift 1ste gang), living on a farm pension at Asheim (Asseim), Brekke, Gulen parish, household 02. Birth about 1743 is inferred from her age. ¶1

Burial (per index): Randie Torkeldsdatter, Gaardemands Kone (farmer's wife), of Assem, died 24 Nov 1821, buried 28 Nov 1821, aged 77 2/3 (Gulen church book 1816-1825, burials 1821, no. 51; index pg00000001043690, card). The age gives birth about spring 1744, against about 1743 from the 1801 census age of 58: within a year. No husband is named; the identification rests on name, farm and her status as a farmer's wife after Johannes Olssen's death at Assem in 1817. ¶3

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Confidence: Probable, and only as strong as the father link. The 7 Oct 1773 baptism of Mons does not name his mother. She is linked as mother because the 1801 census says both she and Johannes Olssen were in their first marriage, so if Johannes is Mons's father she was his only wife and Mons's mother, unless the census marital status is wrong. She would have been about 30 in 1773. The son named Torkield (16 in 1801) fits naming after her father. A wife of a Johannes of Asheim was churched on 5 Nov 1773, four weeks after Mons's baptism, but that entry does not name her. Confirming record: the marriage of Johannes Olsen and Randi Torkildsdatter (probably 1760s, Gulen), not yet searched. ¶2

Confidence: stays Probable as mother of Mons. The father link is now Confirmed, but no record names Mons's mother; the link rests on the 1801 census statement that she and Johannes Olssen were both in their first marriage, with children from about 1768 (Ole) to about 1784 (Torkield) spanning Mons's birth in 1773. A 'Randi Asheim' was a godmother on 5 Apr 1771, possibly her (unproved). Would confirm it: the marriage of Johannes Olsen and Randi Torkildsdatter (Gulen, probably 1760s), or the 5 Nov 1773 churching if a fuller copy names the wife. ¶4

Corrections 2

Correction: birth changed from 'ABT 1743' (from her 1801 census age of 58) to before 12 Apr 1744, her baptism, at Asheim. This agrees with her burial age of 77 2/3 in Nov 1821 (born about spring 1744). The id keeps its 1743 suffix. ¶9

Correction (applied): her baptism on Quasimodogeniti 1744 was on 5 Apr 1744, not 12 Apr, and her mother's churching on Exaudi Sunday was on 10 May 1744, not 17 May. The register (Gulen 11441, canvas 32) gives only the feast names. The earlier dates were computed from the Gregorian Easter (5 Apr 1744), but in 1700-1775 Denmark-Norway used the 'improved calendar', whose Easter in 1744 fell one week earlier, on 29 Mar (section 4;, from two secondary sources that agree for 1744). Quasimodogeniti = Easter + 7 days = 5 Apr; Exaudi = Easter + 42 days = 10 May. The birth field is changed from 'BEF 12 APR 1744' to 'BEF 5 APR 1744'. The date is Probable rather than certain: a margin '5/4' beside the heading in 11441 c32 would confirm it, and has not been read at full size. ¶13
