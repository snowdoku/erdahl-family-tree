The Anne of 1771 is not the 'half-sister Anne, 36' of the 1801 census; she may have died young (not checked). Godparent Svend Olsen Kaarsand may be kin, perhaps an elder son, but this is not shown. ¶4

Life story

First marriage (scan read): Romsdal sorenskriveri skifteprotokoll 3A 09 (1761-1773), fol. 529b-530a. On 28 Feb 1770 a probate was held at the farm 'Kaarsen i Rødven Otting', in the presence of the appraisers Svend Alfsnes and Biørn Kaarsen, after 'afg. Anne Steensd.' between 'Enkemanden Ole Svendsen' (the widower Ole Svendsen) and the children: 1. Svend Olsen (age not legible), guardian his father; 2. Steen Olsen, 14, guardian Lars Landre; 3. Erich Olsen, 11, guardian Ole Landre; 4. Ole Olsen, 1/2 year, guardian Torfind Brudeset; 5. Ingebor Olsdatter, 9, co-guardian Knud Iversen Scheje; 6. Gullaug Olsdatter, 7, guardian Knud Steensen Moen; 7. Anne Olsdatter, 5, guardian Iver Steensen Moen. The gross estate was 98 rdl 2 ort 14 sk. Among the debts are sums to Knud Moen and to 'Anne Steensd. Moen', so Knud and Iver Steensen Moen and Anne Steensdatter Moen were very likely the deceased's siblings, and she probably came from Moen. This proves Anne Steensdatter was his first wife and that she died between mid-1769 (son Ole aged 1/2 year) and Feb 1770. ¶12

Second marriage found (scans read): Grytten ministerialbok 1764-1806 (1539P), Eid church. Engaged 1770 (16 Apr in the index), p. 110: 'Enkemand Ole Svendsøn Korsen av Rødvens Sogn' and 'P. Birgite Mats D. Moe'; sureties Svend Botholphsøn Alfarnæs (the 'Svend Alfsnes' who appraised his first wife's estate in Feb 1770) and Knud Olsøn Rødven. Married Jubilate Sunday (6 May) 1770, p. 111, the bride now written 'P. Marite Mats D. Moe'. The bride came from Moe, the same farm as his first wife Anne Steensdatter's brothers Knud and Iver Steensen Moen. She was a daughter of Mads Johansen Moe (d. 1749); see Brit (Birgitte) Madsdatter. This explains why the marriage was not in the Veøy book (negative). ¶18

Bygdebok (Veøy i fortid og nutid, Bind II, 1926, nb.no item 603d693d0e91668e2e7b400eedf28ef9, open worldwide, full OCR text; Korsan gnr. 61, pp. 296-299, canvases 306-309): 'Ola Sveinsen Korsan fik bygselbrev av hr. Børing [Røring] 1752' for bruk no. 2 (lno. 135), his son Ola Olsen got the lease from the priest in 1798, and the 1801 household has 'Vilkaarsmand Ola Sveinsen 71 og hustru Anne Larsdr. 54'. It gives no origin or parents. ¶22

Ellen Svendsdatter of Korsand is not his sister (scan read): her probate, Romsdal skifteprotokoll 3A 09, fol. 403b, 1 May 1767, at 'Korsen i Rødven Otting', was held after 'døde Pige Elen Svendsdatter' (the deceased maiden), and her heirs were only the four children of a Peder: Ole Pedersen Dalset (of age), Marit, Giertrud and Berit Pedersdatter, evidently her sister's children. A full or half brother would have inherited before nephews and nieces, so Ole Svendsen, who is not named as an heir, was not her brother. 'Ole Korsen' is paid from the estate for 17 weeks of her board and care, so she died in his household. ¶15

Marriages (inference from the Romsdal probate index): the index has a probate for Anne Stensdatter of Korsan, registered 28 Feb 1770 (skifteprotokoll 3A 09, fol. 529b, Rødven otting), and one for Ellen Svendsdatter of Korsan, 1 May 1767 (3A 09, fol. 403b). Anne Stensdatter is probably his first wife, since his eldest son of the first marriage was named Steen; Ellen Svendsdatter may be his sister. Neither page has been read. If so, his second marriage, to the mother of Guri Olsdatter, fell between Feb 1770 and about Aug 1770 (daughter Anne baptised May 1771). It is not in the Veøy marriage index 1765-1799 under any Ole Sv* groom (search of Romsdal parishes), so it should be read on the scan of Veøy ministerialbok 16043, canvases 39-42 (pp. 76-84, Mar-Dec 1770; canvas 38 = pp. 74-75, Jan-Mar 1770, read only at reduced resolution, no marriage of his noticed). ¶11

What the 1770 probate settles about his marriages: the first-marriage children in 1770 (Svend, Steen, Erich, Ole, Ingebor, Gullaug, Anne) match the first-marriage heirs of his 1806 probate (Steen, Ole, Ingeborg, 'Gurie' married to Bottolf Hunnes, Anne). No Guri was born to the first marriage by 1770. The 1806 first-marriage 'Gurie, married to Bottolf Hunnes' must therefore be the Gullaug Olsdatter, 7 in 1770 (b. c.1763), with the name misread or changed, and the only Guri among his children is the second-marriage Guraa baptised 20 Aug 1774. Anne, 5 in 1770, is the 'half-sister Anne, 36' of the 1801 census. His second marriage, to the mother of Guri, Anne (May 1771) and Peder (Jan 1773), therefore fell between Feb 1770 and about Aug 1770. ¶13

Daughter Guraa baptised at Veøy church 20 Aug 1774 as 'Ole Svendsen Kaarsands Datter, kaldet Guraa' (Veøy 16043, p. 130, canvas 66); his wife was churched later that summer (p. 131), unnamed. This is Guri Olsdatter Skorgen, whose link to him is now Confirmed. His own parents are not affected. ¶10

1801 census (scan read): at Korsand, Veøy (1541P), p. 410, Ole Svendsen, 71, is the father of the head of household, Ole Olsen, 32, and is in his third marriage, with his wife Ane Larsdatter, 54. Also in the household: Peder Olsen, 26, and Guraa Olsdatter, 29, siblings of the head, and Anne, 36, the head's half-sister. ¶1

Probate (scan read): Romsdal sorenskriveri skifteprotokoll 13 (1802-1812), s. 172b-173a. On 18 Jul 1806 the estate of 'afgangne Mand Ole Svendsen Kaarsan' was registered and valued for division between his widow Anne Larsdatter and his children. Heirs: 'Udi første Ægteskab' (by the 1st marriage): a) son Steen Olsen, myndig (of age); b) son Ole Olsen, myndig; c) daughter Ingeborg Olsdatter, 'gift med' (married to) Halvord Pedersen Steenen[?]; d) daughter Gurie Olsdatter, married to Bottolf Hunnes; e) daughter Anne Olsdatter, guardian Bottolf Hunnes. 'Udi andet Ægteskab 3 Børn' (by the 2nd marriage, 3 children): a) son Peder Olsen Grøven[?], myndig; b) daughter Anne Olsdatter, guardian Steen Olsen Leran[?]; c) daughter Gurie Olsdatter, guardian Lasse Holmanvig[?] (byname uncertain, alt. Kolmanvig). With the widow Anne Larsdatter, his last marriage, he had no children. In the share list (p. 173) No 17, 'Giørie Olsd. Arvinne', is paid in part by a sum owed 'Hos Halvsøsteren Giørie' (by her half-sister Giørie), which confirms two half-sisters of that name. Neither the first nor the second wife is named. The scan is heavily binarised; bracketed readings are uncertain. ¶6

Burial (scan read): 'Ole Svendsen Kaarsan', kårmann (retired farmer living on a retirement agreement), died 28 Apr 1806 and was buried 18 May 1806 at Rødven, aged 76. Veøy ministerialbok 1799-1818 (source 16044), p. 167. Ages 71 in 1801 and 76 in 1806 give a birth about 1729-30. ¶2

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 3

Speculative father of Guri (Giøri, Guraa) Olsdatter. For: Guraa Olsdatter, 29 in 1801, lived at Korsand as a sister of the head Ole Olsen, son of Ole Svendsen, so by inference she was Ole Svendsen's daughter. Guri Olsdatter Skorgen married from 'Kaarsan' (Korsand) at Veøy on 3 Dec 1806, the year Ole Svendsen died there, and her burial age (68 in Jan 1842, b. 1773-74) matches the 1801 age (b. c.1772) within one or two years. Against or missing: no record names her as his daughter; the 1801 census gives relationships to the head only, and the 1806 marriage names no father. Her mother is not identified: Ole's earlier wives are unknown, and Ane Larsdatter, whom he married in 1797, is not her mother. Her burial age (68 in 1842) makes her about 45-46 when her son Ole was baptised at Grytten on 15 Oct 1819, whatever her origin; this is late but possible, and it no longer argues for a younger woman than the Korsand Guraa. The 1842 burial is known from the index only, and its attribution to her rests on the farm name Skorgen and the widow status. ¶3a

Why this raises his link to Guri (Giøri, Guraa) Olsdatter to Probable: the probate names a daughter Gurie Olsdatter of his 2nd marriage who was still unmarried in July 1806 (she has a guardian, not a husband) and whose guardian was a Lasse. Less than five months later, on 3 Dec 1806, 'Pigen Guraa Olsd. Kaarsan' (the maiden Guraa Olsdatter of Kaarsan) married Lasse Andersen Wiiche at Veøy: same name, same farm, same year, unmarried. The only other Gurie in the family was already married to Bottolf Hunnes, and a 'Bukolf Bukolfsen Hundnes' stood as forlover (marriage surety) at the 1806 wedding, very likely that brother-in-law (Bottolf/Bukolf are forms of one name). Not Confirmed because no record states outright that the bride was this daughter, and the guardian Lasse's byname as read (Holmanvig?) is not Wiiche, so he cannot be shown to be the groom. This note supersedes the earlier statement that the link stays Speculative. ¶7

Upgrade: to Confirmed. His link to Guri (Giøri, Guraa) Olsdatter rests on two primary records already read on the scan: the Veøy baptism of 20 Aug 1774, 'Ole Svendsen Kaarsands Datter, kaldet Guraa' (16043 p. 130), and his own 1806 probate, which names an unmarried daughter Gurie of his second marriage, the 'Pigen Guraa Olsd. Kaarsan' who married Lasse Andersen Wiiche five months later. The overnight audit (AUDIT-1) accepted these as proving Guri's parentage when it approved her upgrade, so his grade is raised to match. The note above that his link 'stays Speculative' or is 'Probable' is superseded. ¶17

Records that disagree 2

Unresolved age conflict: both of Guri's recorded ages fall in the window her full siblings and the negative baptism search exclude (about Aug 1770 to Jun 1774). Her 1801 age of 29 gives b. 1771-72, and her 1842 burial age of 68 gives b. Jan 1773 - Jan 1774. A birth before mid-1770 would make the 1801 age about 2 years low (the same error seen on Peder) and the burial age about 3 years low; a birth after mid-1774 would make the burial age at least 1 year high and the 1801 age 2-3 years high. Either way at least one recorded age is wrong by 2 years or more. Such errors are common, so this is not treated as a contradiction of the probate link, but her baptism (Veøy 16043, c30-c43 for 1768-early 1771, or c65-c72 for mid-1774-1775) is still needed to settle her birth. ¶9

Corrections 1

Correction: the 1801 census relationships in this household do not fit the probate. The census calls Peder and Guraa siblings of the head Ole Olsen and Anne, 36, his half-sister; the probate puts Ole Olsen (and an Anne) in the 1st marriage and Peder, a second Anne and Guraa in the 2nd. So Peder and Guraa were Ole Olsen's half-siblings, and the 1801 Anne, 36 (b. c.1765), is most likely the 1st-marriage Anne, his full sister. The probate is preferred. The Anne baptised May 1771 is the 2nd-marriage Anne and was living in 1806. The 2nd marriage therefore began by 1770; Guri, as a full sister of Anne (1771) and Peder (1773), was born before about mid-1770 or after mid-1774. ¶8
