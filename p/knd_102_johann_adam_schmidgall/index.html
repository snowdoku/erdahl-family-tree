Life story

His marriage to Anna (before 1697) is not indexed (Adam Schmidgall marriages 1684-1700 searched; the Enzweihingen marriage index starts about 1700). An 'Adam Schmidgallen' was buried at Enzweihingen in 1718 (record 5160132, image 1184570-00730; no relatives indexed): possibly he or his wife (the genitive form is often used for a wife), not tied by the row. Other Schmidgall marriages naming a father Johann Adam: Johann Georg 1700 (4607448) and Maria Margaretha 1703 (904607461); these may be children of an older Johann Adam, so his own generation is unclear. ¶2
