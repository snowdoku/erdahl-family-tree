About the family

Children indexed: Gottlieb Friedrich, bapt. Vaihingen 5 May 1823 (d. Friedenstal, Bessarabia, 1856); at Güglingen, Carl Friederich (buried 27 Mar 1826), Karoline Wilhelmine (bapt. 28 May 1829), Johann Gottlieb (bapt. 1 May 1834) and Franz Ludwig (undated). The Güglingen mother is taken to be the same woman (same names, no other such marriage indexed; Probable). Index only; images not seen (S0111 is the 1834 baptism). ¶2

Parents (index only, not yet entered as people): the Bietigheim marriage index names her parents as Johann Melchior Lindenmaier and Katharina Kuffer. The Vaihingen index names Johann Melchior Lindenmajer and 'Luisa Catharina', which may repeat the bride's own name in error. Her Bietigheim baptism of about 1805 and her parents' marriage would settle this. ¶5

Parents entered: father Johann Melchior Lindenmaier, mother Louisa Catharina Finck. The father is named 'Johann Melchior' in both the Vaihingen index (FS 1:1:QPG2-871S) and the FamilySearch 'Germany, Marriages' row for Bietigheim (1:1:VCYQ-MRB) of her 1822 marriage; the Ancestry 61023 row of the Bietigheim entry (record 907054817, image 1184901-00228) reads him 'Johann Mathias', with mother 'Katharina'. Her mother is 'Luisa Catharina' (Vaihingen) / 'Katharina' (Bietigheim, both indexes) / 'Katharina Kuffer' (FS Bietigheim only). ¶8

Parents' marriage found: Johann Melchior Lindenmaier, son of Johann Melchior, married Louisa Catharina Fincken, daughter of Johannes, at Illingen (Oberamt Maulbronn) on 28 Apr 1803 (Ancestry 61023 records 8261325 and 908261325, image 1187140-00632). The bride's names match the Vaihingen index's 'Luisa Catharina' and the daughter's own names, and the date fits a daughter born about 1805. 'Kuffer' in the FS Bietigheim row is probably the father's trade, Küfer (cooper), taken for the mother's surname (inferred; to be checked on image 1184901-00228). Her own baptism (Bietigheim or Illingen, about 1804-06) is not in the Ancestry index. ¶9

Kin hint: a godfather at the Freudental baptism of her son Carl Friedrich Knodel on 15 Jan 1841 was 'Carl Friedrich Finck' (reading). Finck is her mother's maiden name; if he was a maternal relative, this ties the Freudental Louisa Catharina to the Lindenmaier x Finck family. Inference only; it does not affect her Speculative link to Ludwig Knodel. ¶10

Life story

Marriage: Luise Catharina (surname not indexed; also 'Luisa Catharina', 'Luise Katharine', 'Louise Katharine', 'Katharina') married Johann Gottlieb Knodel at Vaihingen an der Enz on 4 July 1822; her parents are not indexed. Index entry only; image not seen; S0110 queued for the signed-in lane. ¶1

In Russia by 1841 (S0111): a son Carl Friedrich Knodel was born in 1841 at 'Freudenthal, Russia' to Gottlieb Knodel and Louisa Lindemeier (FamilySearch Russia Lutheran births index, 'p539-6'). She therefore emigrated with her husband between 1837 and 1841. Her death is not found. ¶6

Freudental, 1841: the Consistory extracts for Freudental parish, Odessa district, give her full name as 'Louisa Catharina Lindemeie[r]' at the birth of her son Carl Friedrich at Freudental on 8 Jan 1841 (FHL 1883193/1 p.538 no.6), and 'Louisa Catharina Lindeman[n]' at his death there on 6 Jul 1841 (FHL 1883193/1 p.574 no.52). She was therefore alive in July 1841, in the Freudental colony near Odessa (not Friedenstal in Bessarabia). A Johann Gottlieb Knodel married the widow Catharina Fetzer née Straub at neighbouring Grossliebental on 9 Jul 1843 (FHL 1882594/2 p.319 no.16); if he is her husband, she died between July 1841 and July 1843. Her death is not in the Freudental or Grossliebental death extracts of 1841-43, which are incomplete. ¶7

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Speculative link: entered as the candidate mother of Ludwig Knodel (see his page), on the same grounds as his candidate father Johann Gottlieb: she is the only wife of Johann Gottlieb on record, and the index gives her as mother of a son Franz Ludwig at Güglingen. Unproved: Franz Ludwig is undated, and no record yet names the Friedenstal Ludwig's mother. Her death date and place are unknown; whether she reached Bessarabia is not known. ¶3a

The case for this identification 1

Name and origin (S0110, Oct 2026): her maiden name was Lindenmaier. The Vaihingen marriage index of 4 July 1822 writes 'Luise Catharina Lindenmajer'; the Bietigheim marriage index writes 'Louise Katharine Lindenmaier', born 1805 at Bietigheim; the Güglingen baptisms of her children write 'Lindenmeier' (Franz Ludwig, 1828) and 'Lindenmaier' (Johann Gottlieb, 1834); and the 1841 Russian birth index writes 'Lindemeier'. Her surname is now entered as Lindenmaier, the form of two of the five entries. Her birth is entered as about 1805 at Bietigheim (Oberamt Besigheim) from the Bietigheim marriage index; that year is an index field, probably derived from her stated age, and her baptism has not been seen. This corrects the earlier note that her surname was not indexed. ¶4
