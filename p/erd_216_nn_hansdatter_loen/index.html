About the family

Wife of Samuel Larsen Loen (see his page) between his wife Britte Rasmusdatter (d. before 7 Jun 1709) and his widow Ane Paulsdatter; first name not known. Aaland's Loen history lists Samuel's wives as '1....Hansd., 2. Brite Rasmusd., 3. Anne Paulsd.' (as quoted); the records below show the Hansdatter must be the wife between 1709 and Ane Paulsdatter, so Aaland's numbering of the first two is probably reversed. ¶1

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the grade · 1 record that disagrees

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Probable as mother of Lars Samuelsen Loen (see his page), graded on her link to him. The relationship is inferred by elimination, so not Confirmed: (1) Samuel's probate, 15 Sep 1747 (A 10 fol. 16b), enlarged: the heirs are 'den Sal. Mand med hans tvende forhen afdøde Hustruer avlede Børn' (the children the late man begot with his TWO formerly deceased wives): Hans Samuelsen abroad, Lars Samuelsen married and living on the farm, Brite Samuelsdatter Brecke (her guardian 'Halvbroderen' Lars Samuelsen Loen), Synneve married to Rasmus Olsen, the younger Brite, and Ane married to Rasmus Olsen. So the living widow Ane Paulsdatter was mother of none of them. (2) Britte Rasmusdatter's probate of 7 Jun 1709 (A 4a fol. 171a) names only her children Britte 16 and Synneve 6, and Lars is called the elder Brite's half-brother. (3) Lars was born about 1714 (Aaland: d. 1789 aged 75) and his sister Ane about 1713 (Aaland: d. 1783 aged 70), after Britte's death. Their mother was therefore the other deceased wife, the only one left, Aaland's...Hansdatter. The eldest son Hans Samuelsen would carry her father's name. Would settle: her own probate (Loen, between about 1714 and 1747; not in the scanned protocols, since 1715-27 is the unscanned gap) or a tingbok marriage settlement. ¶2

Records that disagree 1

Correction of a noted conflict (Lars Samuelsen Loen): the uncertain word after 'med hans' in the 1747 heirs paragraph is 'tvende' (two), followed by 'forhen afdøde Hustruer'. This removes the contradiction with Aaland's birth year for Lars: he is a son of the post-1709 wife, not of Ane Paulsdatter and not of a wife before 1692. ¶3
