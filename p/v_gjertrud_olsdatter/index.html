Life story

Baptized 1819 at Borgund as 'Gertrud Berthe', born 10 Oct 1819. Parents: Ole Johnsen, farmer at Røsevold, and Ane Marta Rasmusdatter. The middle name differs (Berthe here, Birgitte in later records), but the farm, the father's name and the year all agree, so this is Probable. ¶2

Widow and head of household at Røssevold in 1891. Died 25 Mar 1900 as a retired widow (kårenke), born 1819, of old age. ¶3

Death register: the Nordre Sunnmøre district court's register of deaths (dødsfallsprotokoll no. 5, p. 26, Borgund 1900 no. 34) enters Gjertrud Olsdatter of Røssevold, kaarenke (a widow living on a farm pension), aged 81, died 25 March 1900. Her heirs are given as 6 adult children, one of them in America; the words after that are uncertain but may say his or her whereabouts were unknown. The informant was Peter P. Røssevold, presumably her son. The remarks column, which mentions a letter from the lensmann (the local sheriff) dated 11 June 1900, was not fully read. ¶4
