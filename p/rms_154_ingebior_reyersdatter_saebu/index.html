Life story

Of Sæbu, Skrautvål, Aurdal. Married first Biørn Johansen (children Ragnild b. about 1677, Johannes about 1679, Giertrud about 1684), second Ole Østensen (son Østen Olsen about 1686). Both she and Ole Østensen were dead when their joint skifte was held at Sæbu on 17 Mar 1698 (DA 25282 s. 133b, view 137: 'efter Ole Østensen og hans Sal. Hustru Ingebiør Reyersdaatter'). Odelsgods 10 1/2 skind in Sæbu (card 13395). ¶1

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade:, new person entered as Confirmed, graded on her link to her daughter Ragnild Biørnsdatter (see her page). The 1698 skifte, her own, names 'Rangnilde 21 aar gammel' among her children by her first husband Biørn Johansen; Ragnild's own probate of 9 Oct 1721 (DA 25285 fol. 480a, view 489) names 'hendis S. Forældre Biørn Johansen og Ingebor[..]daatter' and cites the 1698 skifte by date. The patronymic in the 1721 entry is hard to read; the 1698 protocol spells it 'Reyersdaatter' (card: Reijersdaatter). No rival. ¶2
