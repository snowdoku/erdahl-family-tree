Life story

John Southcote, esq., of Bovey Tracey (Indio), Devon, Clerk of the Peace for Devon, M.P. for Lostwithiel (House of Commons 1509-1558: 'SOUTHCOTE, John I (by 1504-56), of Bovey Tracey'). Westcote saw his gravestone at Shillingford St George: 'John Southcote, Esquyer, sometymes lord and patron of this church, who departed this present life the 6th of September, Anno Domini 1556'. The 1620 visitation makes him son of Nicholas Southcott and Margaret Bossom, and gives his wife as a daughter of Hankford, widow of Sydenham; the History of Parliament speaks of a son 'by 1st w.', so he married twice, and which wife was Joane's mother is unknown. His children included Thomas Southcote of Bovey Tracey (died 1600, three wives), George of Calwoodley, John II (born by 1533), Mary (wife of Thomas Cary of Cockington), Agnes (wife of Richard Reynell of Ogwell), Elizabeth (married 24 Nov 1539, per HoP), Barbara (Pomeroy), Grace, Margaret, and Joane (wife of John Eveleigh of Holcombe). Added. ¶1

Will of his son Thomas Southcote of Bovey Tracey read (TNA PROB 11/96/208, image read). Grade unchanged (Probable, on his link to Joane Southcott). The test record named above does NOT name Joane or any Eveleigh. It names 'Thomas Southcote the sonne of George Southcote my eldest sonne' (left the testator's tin works in Devon), 'Thomas Southcote my second sonne' (leases of two farms in Bovey Tracey), 'Margarett Southcote my daughter' and 'Alice Southcote one other of my daughters', his wife Elizabeth (sole executrix; the manor of Mourkton [Monkton?] and lands in Colyton), burial in Shillingford church, and overseers 'my Nephewe George Carie Esquier' (Mary Southcote's son by Thomas Cary of Cockington), 'my brother in lawe Edward Drewe Sargeant at lawe' and 'my sonne in lawe Thomas Rudgwaye Esquier'. The will does not mention his father. Next test record: an Eveleigh or Southcote deed or Chancery suit (C 1, C 2, C 3) over Bovey Tracey land naming Joane as John Southcote's daughter; or John Southcote's own will (1556, PCC or Exeter). ¶3

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the grade · 1 open question

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Probable, graded on his link to his daughter Joane Southcott. Vivian (p. 336) and Westcote (p. 564) both call her 'daughter of John Southcott of Bovey Tracey' (Westcote: 'esq.'). Westcote elsewhere uses the same style, 'John Southcot of Bovey-Tracey, esq., clerk of the peace', for the father of Mary Cary and Agnes Reynell, i.e. John I. He is the only John Southcote of Bovey Tracey of the right generation: his son John II was born only by 1533, too young to be the father of a woman married by about 1556 (her son George was born about 1556) and still bearing children in 1575. Her son Nicholas Eveleigh later bought Parke in Bovey Tracey, and her husband's bondsman in 1586 was Robert Connante of Bovey Tracey. But no record naming Joane as his daughter was read. ¶2a

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Test record: the will of her brother Thomas Southcote of Bovey Tracey, PROB 11/96/208 (proved 3 Oct 1600), which may name his sister Joane Eveleigh or her children; John Southcote's inquisition post mortem (C 142, 1556-7); the Southcott pedigree in Vivian (1895), pp. 700ff. Added. ¶2b
