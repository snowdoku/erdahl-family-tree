Life story

Baptism found: Albert Mandius, born 12 May 1889, baptised 19 May 1889 in Laksevåg parish (Askøy). Parents named as Jon Larsen Skreå (b. 1853) and Ingeborg Rasmusdtr. (b. 1851), living at Laxevåg. This confirms his parentage. ¶5

In Norway he was 'Albert Mandus Johnsen' (1891 and 1900 censuses), son of John Larsen, boilermaker. Parentage probable (strong). ¶3

Brother Sigvald emigrated in 1903 as 'Sigvald Johnsen Larsen (Skreaa)'. Brother Rasmus emigrated with Albert in 1905 as 'Rasmus Larsen (Skreaa)'. ¶7

Emigrated from Bergen 15 May 1905 as 'Albert Mandius Larsen (Skreaa)', errand boy, 'travels to his brothers'; arrived Ellis Island 29 May 1905, went to Evanston, IL. Brother Rasmus (b. 1890) on the next line. ¶1

Emigration: on 15 May 1905 (the contract date; the departure date and ship are blank) the Bergen police emigrant protocol no. 6, p. 56, line 10, recorded Albert Mandius Larsen (Skreaa), born 1889, single, of Askøen (Askøy), occupation løbergut (errand boy), wishing to work as a joiner (snedkerarbeid), bound for 'Amerika (S Dak) Ill' with a ticket sent from abroad, for better prospects; remark 'Reiser til sine Brødre' (travelling to his brothers). His brother Rasmus Larsen (Skreaa), born 1890, of Askøen, son of a boilermaker, is the next person in the same group (00423). Read from the Digitalarkivet transcription; the scan was not read. The South Dakota destination agrees with his 1917 draft registration at Aberdeen, South Dakota. ¶14

WWI draft registration (card read): on 5 Jun 1917 at Aberdeen, Brown County, South Dakota, he registered as Albert Mandius Larsen, age 28, of 13½ South Main, Aberdeen, born 12 May 1889 at 'Bergen', Norway, a farm labourer with no employer, single, with no dependants. He was a 'declarant' (he had filed his first naturalisation papers). The registrar described him as of medium height and build with gray eyes and light hair, and remarked that he had tried to enlist but was not accepted. 'Bergen' fits his baptism in Laksevåg (Askøy), beside Bergen. ¶10

Served in France in WWI. Married Clara Olson 1921; 12 children. ¶2

1930 census (index only, image not read): Albert M Larson is head of a household in West Bank Township, Williams County, North Dakota, born in Norway, with wife Clara (abt 1903, North Dakota) and children Gertie I (abt 1923), Thelmer J (abt 1924), Annie C (abt 1925), 'Claice L' (abt 1927, Clarice) and Bennie M (abt 1929), all born in North Dakota. Four Olsons listed as his brothers-in-law live with them: Sam (abt 1892), Ole T (abt 1893), Carl (abt 1900) and Tom (abt 1905). The index gives the head's birth year as 'abt 1929', which is impossible for a father of five and is treated as an index slip for 1889; the image (queued as S0115) will settle his age, immigration year and age at first marriage. ¶12

WWII draft registration (index only, card not read): the 1942 'Old Man's' registration indexes Albert Mandus Larsen, born 12 May 1889, Bergen, Norway, living in Bellingham, Washington. ¶11

U.S. death record: the Washington Department of Health death-certificate index lists Albert Mandius Larsen, died 18 Jan 1945, age 55, male, spouse Clara Olson, father John Larsen, mother Ingeborg Rasmussen. This is the U.S. record that ties him to his Norwegian parents John Larsen Skrede (Jon Larsen Skreå) and Ingeborg Rasmusdatter Botn (Ingeborg Rasmusdatter): the full name with the rare middle name Mandius, the age (born 1889) and both parents' names agree with his 1889 Laksevåg baptism. ¶8

Obituary index: a notice for 'Albert M Larsen', relatives 'Clare' and 'Gertie' (as indexed), was published in Bellingham, Washington on 23 Jan 1945, five days after his death. The index gives his birth as about 1881, against 12 May 1889 in the tree; the newspaper itself has not been read. U.S., Newspapers.com Obituary Index. ¶13

CONFLICT (death place): the certificate index gives the place of death as Seattle, King County, Washington; this tree (from Find a Grave) has Whatcom County, and he is buried at Greenacres, Ferndale, Whatcom County. Both can be true (death in a Seattle hospital, home in Whatcom). The death-place field is left as Whatcom County until the certificate itself is read (hand search H0077, the reCAPTCHA-gated PDF of record DE1C882C1D3CDB93512F2D438B19273C). ¶9
