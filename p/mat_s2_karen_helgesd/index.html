About the family

Mother of Gunder Gundersen Satter. Named as the mother in all three of her children's baptisms at Frosta — 'Karen Helgesd', of Snytan in 1831 and 1833 and of Moxnes in 1835. ¶1

No Karen Helgesdatter of the right age appears anywhere in the 1865 census of Norway, so she was probably dead before her son emigrated. ¶3

Children born in Skogn: Hanna, born 6 January 1838 and baptised at Ekne on 28 February 1838, and Oline Margrethe, born 7 June 1842 and baptised at Ekne on 31 July 1842. Both entries name her as the mother and give the residence as Søndre Kjølen. Among Oline Margrethe's godparents was a cottager, Peder Helgesen of Føraasvald, Ekne, who shares her patronymic. It is a speculative lead that he was her brother, and nothing proves it. ¶5

Life story

Her birth year of about 1803 comes from the Frosta out-migration entry of 26 February 1838, which records her aged 35 when she and her husband left for Skogn. ¶2

Scan read (Frosta ministerialbok 1827-1839, banns/marriages p. 168, image 320b5326): 'Pige Karen Helgesd[atter] Juberg 28 Aar Datter [af] Helge Pedersen af Bedstaden, fød i Bedstad efter Attest af 30te October 1830 af Pastor Fyhn'; banns 16, 23, 30 Jan 1831; married Thursday 3 Feb 1831; she was vaccinated, the groom had had natural smallpox; forlovere og cautionister (sponsors and guarantors) Ole Larsen Hellan and Sivert [Greisen?] Snytan. The scan settles the index conflict: Juberg is Karen's own residence and 'af Bedstaden' (of Beitstad) is her father's; his patronymic Pedersen is written out. No mother is named. The Beitstad baptism of 10 Oct 1802 (p. 219, scan read) says only 'Helge og Martha Fosliepladsens Barn: Karen', with no patronymics or godparents, so the link to that baptism stays Probable. ¶11

Origin and parents (Probable): Probable: Karen's marriage at Frosta on 3 Feb 1831 gives her birthplace as Bedstad (Beitstad) and her father as 'Helge Peders af Bedstaden' (a second index transcription reads 'Helge, Juberg'); the Beitstad baptism of 10 Oct 1802 of Karen, daughter of Helge and Martha of Fosliepladsen, fits her age (35 in Feb 1838). A rival Karen Helgesdatter baptised at Åmot on 13 Feb 1803 (father Helge Taralsen) does not fit the Beitstad birthplace. The Frosta 1801 couple Helge Pedersen 51 and Martha Olsdatter 39 (Augdahl) may be the same pair but nothing yet ties them to Beitstad; Martha's patronymic is unproved. Both scans (marriage p. 168, baptism p. 219a) are queued for reading. ¶9

Marriage: 3 Feb 1831 at Frosta to Gunder Sivertsen (Frosta p. 168). ¶10

Move to Skogn, 1838: she is entered with her husband in the Skogn in-migration list of 1838 as 'Kone Karen Helgesdatter', aged 35, coming from Frosta to live as cottagers on Søndre Kjølen in Ekne sokn. ¶4

Death: she died on 10 November 1842 and was buried at Ekne on 20 November 1842, entered as 'Huusmands Kone Karen Helgesdatter', aged 40, of Kjølenval (Ekne burials 1842, p. 421, no. 2). This is her: her husband's 1847 remarriage entry states that his first marriage ended 'ved Konen Karen Helgesdatters Død 10de Novemb. 1842' (by the death of his wife Karen Helgesdatter on 10 November 1842). An age of 40 in November 1842 means she was born about 1802, which agrees with the age of 35 in February 1838. The cause of death, one word in the right-hand column, was not read with confidence. ¶6

1 correction · 1 alternative set aside

Corrections 1

Correction: earlier notes inferred that she 'was probably dead before her son emigrated' because she is absent from the 1865 census. She actually died in 1842, when her son Gunder was seven. The 1852 confirmation entry that lists 'K. Karen Helgesdatter' with no deceased mark copies the parents' names and is not evidence that she was alive. ¶7

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Her origin is unproved. The indexed baptisms of a Karen with father Helge are at Beitstad (10 October 1802) and Åmot in Hedmark (13 February 1803). No record yet ties either one to her, and no record names her parents. The book that would hold a Frosta baptism, Frosta before 1827, has not been located in the index. ¶8
