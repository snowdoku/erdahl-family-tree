About the family

Naming evidence, inference only: Beret Olsdatter named her sons Ole (1816) and, as her last child, Peder (1833). Ole fits her father; Peder fits her father's patronymic (Ole PEDERSEN), the paternal grandfather Selbu naming custom would honour. This supports, but does not prove, the identification. His father Peder is not entered here. ¶5

Parents linked, Probable: Peder Andersen Svee/Saether (see his page) and Berit Olsdatter (see her page), on his baptism candidate of 1 January 1767, the Svee-Sæter tie (Berete Olsd. buried 1805 from 'Saeter-Sveet') and the Sæter godparents and sponsors on both sides. The rival baptisms and what would settle it are set out in Peder's notes. The earlier note calling Peder only a 'possible father, not entered' is superseded by this link. ¶13

Life story

Birth, inferred: ABT DEC 1766 from the burial age of 26 years 5 months on 19 May 1793. His baptism, about November-December 1766 in the same register, has not been read. ¶9

1762 extra tax (note only, no link): at 676 Wiigen (Vigen) stand 'Peder Andersen, Berit Ols datter' with children over 12 'Ole, Anders, Helge, Berit, Marit', so that couple's Ole was born by about 1750. This does not undermine the 1767 baptism candidate. Peder Andersen Saether was buried in 1793 aged 60 (born about 1733) and was fathering children in 1761 and 1767; a man born about 1733 could not have five children over 12 in 1762. So the Vigen couple is very probably an older Peder Andersen and Berit Olsdatter - possibly the parents of one of the 1761/1767 couple - and not the parents of this Ole. Their burials, or a Svee/Sæther entry naming Peder's father, would decide it. ¶12

Betrothal: on Maundy Thursday (Die Viridium), 5 April 1792, the Selbu register enters 'Trolovet Grenadier Olle Pedersen Saether & P[ige] Rhagnild Jonsd. Aune' - betrothed, the grenadier Ole Pedersen of Saether and the maiden Ragnhild Jonsdatter of Aune - with 'Sponsores' (forlovere, sureties for the marriage) Erich Neelsen Aune and Olle Siursen Sethar. A grenadier was a soldier of the enrolled grenadier company of the Trondhjem infantry, drawn from the farm population. The marriage (copulation) entry itself was not found in the pages read, 5 April to the 8th Sunday after Trinity 1792; it may have been solemnised in the Tydal annex church or later in 1792. ¶1

Baptism of his daughter: on the 14th Sunday after Trinity, 9 September 1792, 'Doebt et Pigebarn kaldet Berthe: Foraeldre: Ole Pedersen Saether og Ragnille Jonsdatter' - baptised a girl named Berthe, parents Ole Pedersen Saether and Ragnhild Jonsdatter; godparents (faddere) Peder Oeunet [Aunet], Haldor Kioesnes [Kjosnes], Anne Oeunet [Aunet], Maren Saether, Doreth Wigen[?] [Vigen]. This Berthe is identified as Beret Olsdatter (see her page); the argument is in her notes. ¶2

Death and burial, Probable: on Trinity Sunday, 26 May 1793, the Selbu register enters '2: Jord paa og holdt Ligtale over Ole Pedersen Saether, gl. 26 Aar 5 Maaneder, doed d. 19de Maji' - (the priest) cast earth on and held a funeral oration over Ole Pedersen Saether, aged 26 years 5 months, died 19 May. Born, by that age, about December 1766. For the identification: a young man of the right name and farm, eight months after Berthe's baptism; and in April 1794 Ragnhild Jonsdatter is styled 'Enken... Saether', the widow of Saether, at her betrothal to Reinholdt Torstensen. ¶7a

Death, inferred: before about 1796. Not found; inferred because his widow Rangnil Jonsdatter was in her second marriage, to Reinal Torstenssen, by the 1801 census, and their eldest child Ane Reinaldsdatter was 5 on 1 February 1801 (born about 1795-96). The second marriage could in principle follow a separation, but none is recorded and death is by far the likelier cause. ¶4

Saether (Sæter) is a farm in Selbu; the betrothal sponsor Olle Siursen Sethar and the godmother Maren Saether were of the same farm. His birth year is unknown; as a serving grenadier and a groom in 1792 he was presumably born in the 1760s, but no record of it has been read. ¶6

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: his father's probate, Sveet under Sæter, registered 11 July 1793 (Stjør- og Verdal Skifteprotokoll 10, Digitalarkivet 24763, p. 391), lists among the heirs the deceased son [Ole] Pedersen, represented by 'et Pigebarn nemlig Berith Olsd. 1 aar gl.' - his only child, a girl of one year. That fits Berthe, baptised 9 Sep 1792 to Ole Pedersen and Ragnhild Jonsdatter, and the Beret Olsdatter, 8, in Rangnil Jonsdatter's household at Slinden in 1801. A primary record now names him as the father of a daughter Berith Olsdatter born c.1792, and as son of Peder Andersen and Berithe Olsdatter; Confirmed. ¶15

The case for this identification 1

Identification rated Probable. The chain is: the 1801 census of Selbu, Slinden household 7, has Beret Olsdatter, 8, as the only Olsdatter in the household of Rangnil Jonsdatter, 34, whose marriage to Reinal Torstenssen is HER SECOND; the 1792 register gives a couple Ole Pedersen and Ragnhild Jonsdatter, she a maiden at betrothal, with a daughter Berthe baptised seven months after the betrothal and exactly 8 years 5 months before the census day. No document names Rangnil of Slinden as Ole's widow. ¶3a

Corrections 1

Correction: death was carried as 'BEF 1796', inferred from his widow's remarriage. The burial entry gives 19 May 1793, and her betrothal of 13 April 1794 as a widow agrees. ¶8

Alternatives considered and set aside 3

Caveat: a second Ole Pedersen lived at Saether - 'Ole Pedersens Hustrue af Saether' was churched (introduced after childbirth) on the 1st Sunday in Advent 1794, after the buried man's death - so the burial could in principle belong to another man of the name. Rated Probable; his occupation is not given in the burial entry. ¶7b

Possible father, not entered: Peder Andersen Saether, aged 60, died 19 June and buried 23 June 1793, a month after Ole. Born about 1733, he is of an age to be Ole's father, and the betrothal sponsor Olle Siursen Sethar and godmother Maren Saether show the family's ties to the farm; nothing names Peder as Ole's father. The probate (skifte) after either man in the Strinda og Selbu sorenskriveri would settle it. ¶10

Baptism candidate, not linked: on the Circumcision, 1 January 1767, 'Döbt Ole. Forældr: Peder Anders: Kvee[?], Berit Ols: Fadd: Jon Aftreit, Anders Kveen[?], Jon Kiøsnes, Brynild Seter' - Ole, son of Peder Andersen and Berit Olsdatter (Selbu ministerialbok 1757-1801, canvas 20 right page, crop). The date fits his burial age of 26 years 5 months on 19 May 1793 (born about December 1766), and the father's name fits Peder Andersen Saether (buried June 1793 aged 60). But the farm word is not 'Sæther': it is a short word read Kvee/Hvee/Svee. The same couple had a daughter Anne baptised on 16 August 1761, there written 'Per Anders: Sveen, Berit Olsd.' with godparents Siur Seter, Ole Kiøsnes and Ingebor Sesseng (canvas 11 right, baptism no. 1), so the 1767 word is probably Sveen (Svean) as well. The Sæther ties are the godmothers Brynild Seter (1767) and the godfather Siur Seter (1761) - and Ole's own betrothal sponsor in 1792 was Olle Siursen Sethar. A 'Peder And: Sæther' and his wife took communion on Misericordias Sunday, 17 April 1768 (canvas 25 right), so a Peder Andersen was at Sæther in 1768. Whether the Sveen couple and the Sæther Peder are one man who moved, or two men, is not shown; Berit Olsdatter's burial or Peder Andersen Sæther's wife's name would decide it. ¶11

Searches that found nothing 1

Probate route tested, negative: the 1841-53 Strinda og Selbu probate register (24746) has no estate for his widow Rangnil Jonsdatter or her second husband Reinal Torstensen of Slinden (d. 1841), so no heirs list names Beret Olsdatter as Ole's daughter. ¶14

Open questions and the next record to read 1

What would settle it: the probate (skifte) after Ole Pedersen Saether in the Strinda og Selbu sorenskriveri protocols c.1793-96, which would name his widow and his daughter as heir, or his burial entry in the same register c.1792-96 (canvases 149-200, not yet read). ¶3b
