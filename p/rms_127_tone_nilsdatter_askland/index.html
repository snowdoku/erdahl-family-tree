About the family

NEW ANCESTOR, graded on her link to her son Orm (see his page). Åmli ætt og heim II, Nordre Askland (canvas 155): Orm Askland's son [Svenn] 'g m Tone Nilsdtr.'; canvas 156: 'Vi veit om 4 born etter [Svenn] Ormson og Tone Nilsdtr.', one of them 'Orm, til L.nr. 50 Askland'; 'Tone Askland' is named in a document with Salve Jørgenson and Lille Orm Askeland (1650s, canvas 156); gard 16 (canvas 58) quotes 'Svend Ormsen og hans [hustru] Tone Nilsdtr. 1 hud i Askeland'. Orm named his son Svend after his father (Svend Ormsen b. 1654, Svend Ormsen). Bygdebok only (compiled secondary source, text rebuilt from word boxes, dates ABT), so Probable at most. ¶1a
