Life story

Emigration: the compiled Creed genealogy says she came to PEI in 1810, citing her obituary in The Argus, Charlottetown, 27 April 1875, page 3 (not yet read). ¶3

Named as Joseph's 'beloved wife Catherine' in his will of 13 February 1868, which provides for her maintenance for life. Mother of James, Mary and William Creed. ¶2

Gravestone (Find a Grave 172996878/172997141, photograph read): 'Also his wife Catherine Morrison, died Mar. 21, 1875, aged 85 years, a native of the Isle of [Mu]ll, Scotland' - on the stone of Joseph Bennett Creed. ¶1
