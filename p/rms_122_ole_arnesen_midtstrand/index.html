Life story

The five Aurdal baptisms of an Ole with father Arne, 1731-33 (DA 9361 index) were checked: 27 Dec 1731 Skrautvål (father Arne Olsen Higdem, pd00000003646525), 5 Jan 1732 Hedalen (Arne, Grov), 9 Mar 1732 Reinli (Arne, Eirstad), 23 Jun 1733 Hedalen (Arne Olsen, Grof; mother Ingri Sifversdatter), 4 Oct 1733 Bruflat (Arne, Higdem; mother Dorthe Olesdatter). None is at Bø, so all five are ruled out. He was probably born before 1730, when DA 9361 begins, which fits ABT 1729. ¶11a

Bygdebok (Svenes vol., gnr. 64 Midtstrand, canvas 278): '1754 selde Ola Tordsson gardparten' to 'Ola Arneson'; Ola Arneson 'Bø', 1729 - drowned ('drukna') 1791, married 1754 a [forename not rebuilt] Høljesdotter, 1735-1810. Their children (right column): '1 Marit 1754, g 1780 m Eivind Julsson, gnr 76' (Hovrud), 2 Hølje 1756 d. at 5 weeks, 3 Hølje 1757 brukar, 4 Arne 1760 (m. 1801 Sigrid Nilsdotter), [5] 1763, [6] 1764, 7 Håkon 1768, [8] 1771, [9] 1776, 10 Ingrid 1780 ugift. The Hovrud chapter (gnr. 76, canvas 644) agrees: Eivind Julsson 1754-1821 married 1780 Marit Olsdotter of gnr. 64, 1754-1830. ¶1

Marriage read: DA 9361 p. 93 (canvas 315): betrothed 23 May 1754 'Ole Arnesøn Bøe og Ragnild Helgesdr. Mitstrand', married 1 Jul 1754. This confirms the bygdebok's marriage year 1754 and his origin at Bø ('Ola Arneson Bø'), and gives his wife's forename (Ragnild), lost in the rebuild. His grade stays Probable: it is graded on his link to Marit Olsdatter, and no primary record yet names her as his daughter. His father is probably an Arne of Bø (gnr 106); the bygdebok's Bø chapter has Arne Trondsson, b. about 1689, m. 1714 Kari, son of Trond Høljesen (canvas 268), but the rebuild does not show an Ola among Arne's children, so this is not adopted. ¶5

Death correction: he was alive on 20 Feb 1788, when he and Ragnild made a contract with Ole Tollefsen about money standing in Midtstrand (cited in the 1799 division), and dead before 12 Jul 1799. The bygdebok's 'drowned 1791' fits this window. The 1799 record is a division of 100 rdr held at Strand between his widow and children, not a probate held at his death; his own probate (if any, about 1791) is not carded under Midtstrand or Strand. ¶8

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 3

Graded on his link to Marit Olsdatter (see her page). ¶2a

Graded Probable: a bygdebok is a compiled secondary source and the passage was rebuilt from word boxes with gaps; dates from it are ABT. It fits the 1780 marriage entry 'Marit Olsdat. Mitstrand' and the 1779 Svenes baptism where a Helje Olsen Mitstrand (her brother Hølje b. 1757 by the book) stood godfather. It is not the Ole Knudsen of Midstrand already in the tree (see his page). ¶2b

Upgrade: graded on his link to his daughter Marit Olsdatter (see her page): Confirmed. The baptism at Svenes, 6 Oct 1754 (DA 9361 p. 435, canvas 225 left page), names the parents of the child Marit as 'Ole Arnesøn Mitstrand og Ragnild Helgesd.'. The division at Strand, 12 Jul 1799 (DA 25294 pp. 82-83, view 44; card 13047 'Ole Arnesøn + g.m. Ragnild Helgesdtr.', 'Barn:... Marit g.m. Even Juelsen Hoverud'), names his widow Ragnild Helgesdatter and their children, the eldest daughter being 'Marit Olsd[atte]r gift med Even Juelsen Haverud', the wife in the tree. The card's 'Barn' column labels the heirs as the couple's children, and the protocol calls the deceased Ole Arnesøn the widow's husband. Continuity: the baptised Marit of 1754 is the only Marit among the eight children of 1799 and is called the eldest daughter, which fits a birth three months after the wedding of 1 Jul 1754. No rival. ¶7

Searches that found nothing 2

Probate search note: found no Ole estate at Midtstrand 1757-1810 in the Valdres cards 9283-9321, so the 'drowned 1791' probate named as his test record is probably not carded under Midtstrand. ¶6

Parent search (negative): no probate of Arne Trondsen or of a second wife of his was found. The Aabjør cards (DA 39948, 15283-15309) were; the Bø i Aurdal cards 1669-1673 (1730 Vestre Bø, Knud Knudsen; 1735 Gulbrand Helgesen; 1735 Ole Knudsen) and 1679-1687 (1784-1888) have no Arne. No baptism of an Ole with father Arne is indexed for Aurdal 1725-1729 (DA 9360); five Ole baptisms with a father Arne in 1731-1733 (DA 9361 index: 27 Dec 1731, 5 Jan 1732, 9 Mar 1732, 23 Jun 1733, 4 Oct 1733) were not opened. ¶10a
