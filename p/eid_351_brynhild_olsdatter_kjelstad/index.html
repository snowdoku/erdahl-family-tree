About the family

Mother of Mali Haaversdatter Garberg (see her page). Her estate was settled on 20 June 1744 at Kielstad (Kjelstad), Selbu (Stjør- og Verdal Skifteprotokoll 3, 24753, fol. 94-95, views 114 right and 115 right). The heirs were first her widower Ole [..]sen and their children: Kari Olsdatter, wife of Ingebrigt at Kjelstad; Guru Olsdatter, wife of Peder Biørnsen; a daughter married to corporal Ole Pedersen; and the unmarried Peder Olsen 14, Ingebor Olsdatter 18, Bryniel Olsdatter 12 and Anne Olsdatter 9. Then 'den afgangne Kones' children by her late former husband: '1 Berit Haaversdatter', wife of Poul Pedersen Rønsberg, and '2 Hustru Mali Haaversd: Garberg'. On fol. 95 the division gives 'Hustru Mali Haaversdatter Garbergs tilfaldende Arvelod efter hendes afg. Moder Bryniel Olsdatter Kielstad' (Mali Haaversdatter Garberg's share after her late mother Bryniel Olsdatter of Kjelstad). ¶1

Kin who prove the family: Berit (Berette) Haaversdatter, wife of Poul Pedersen Rønsberg, whose own skifte is 24755 fol. 55 (1755/56), is Mali's full sister. Her son Haaver Poulsen Rønsberg, the appraiser of Mali's estate in 1761, was Mali's nephew. Guru Olsdatter, wife of Peder Bjørnsen (the Peder Biørnsen Garberg who was also an appraiser in 1761), was Mali's half-sister. ¶4

Life story

Birth before about 1683 is inferred: her daughters Ellen, Birgete (Berit) and Mali by her first husband Haaver Andersen Kjelstad were already born when he died before 7 Nov 1701 (77804 card 971: 'samtl. hos Moderen', all living with their mother). Unresolved: my reading of the ages of her four unmarried children by Ole in 1744 (Ingebor 18, Peder 14, Bryniel 12, Anne 9; the image is faint) cannot all be right for a woman who bore children before 1701, so either the ages are misread or some were Ole's by another wife. This does not touch the explicit statement that Mali Haaversdatter Garberg inherited 'efter hendes afg. Moder Bryniel Olsdatter Kielstad'. Her own father, an Ole, is known only from her patronymic. ¶3

Resolution of the 1744 ages: a high-contrast re-read of the heir lines (24753 fol. 94, view 114 R) shows that the four minors Peder Olsen 14, Ingebor Olsdatter 18, Brynield Olsdatter 12 and Anne Olsdatter 9 are not Brynhild's own children but the 'efterladte Børn' (surviving children) of a deceased daughter [..] Olsdatter, wife of Corporal Ole Pedersen [Engenes?] - so they are her grandchildren. This fits the baptism 'døbt Ole Peders. Corporals barn Nom: Peder', Oculi Sunday (20 Mar) 1729 (Selbu 16310 c116 R), who would be 15 in mid-1744. The apparent contradiction (a mother bearing children both before 1701 and in 1735) therefore disappears: her children by Ole run from about 1702 (Kari, Guru, the corporal's late wife), and her daughters by Haaver Andersen from before 1701. The daughter's first name before 'Olsdatter' is not legible on this scan. ¶5

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Graded Confirmed on her link to her daughter Mali Haaversdatter Garberg (see her page): the probate names Mali as her daughter and heir. Continuity: 'Hustru Mali Haaversdatter Garberg' is the 'Mali Haavers dtr.' who was the widow at Garberg in the probate after Peder Paulsen Garberg on 20 Jun 1741 (card 77804 c545), three years earlier, and who died a widow at Garberg before 21 Oct 1761 (24755 fol. 110b). No other Mali Haaversdatter at Garberg is known. The 1741 card's 'mosterens mann' Ingebrigt Ingebrigtsen Kjelstad (husband of Mali's sister) fits Kari Olsdatter, wife of Ingebrigt at Kjelstad, her half-sister in this probate. ¶2
