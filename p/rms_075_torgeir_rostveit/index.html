About the family

NEW ANCESTOR. Father of Tjøstel of Rostveit, known from his son's patronymic in the 1664 bailiff's census of Åmli sogn (Fogdenes manntall 1664-66, Råbyggelaget, p.11), where the Rostveit ('Roestued') entry lists 'Thiøstel Thorgiersøn 30 aar' in the column for farmers' sons and servants. The same farm has a Tallak Torgeirsen: the 1664 vicar's census (p.7) lists 'Tallack Thorgeirsøn er 28 aar' among the opsiddere (farm holders) of Rostveit, and the 1666 vicar's census (p.39) lists 'Tallack Thorgeirsøn er 32 Aar, bruger 10 kalfskind' with a son 'Torgei' aged about 1½. Tallak was probably Tjøstel's brother (inferred; the census states no sibling relationship). ¶1

Life story

Birth is inferred: his son Tjøstel was born about 1632-34 (ages 32 in 1664 vicar, 30 in 1664 bailiff, 34 in 1666), so Torgeir was born before about 1610. He does not appear at Rostveit in the 1664 or 1666 lists, so he was probably dead or living elsewhere by 1664 (inferred). He is not in the 1610 tax list at any Rostveit spelling (negative). ¶3

1645 poll tax (DA 76706 view 119, right page, p.9 'Omblj Sogen'): 'Roestued: Thorgier och hans quinde, [G]iermund och hans quinde och en daatter'. Torgeir and his wife were at Rostveit in 1645; his son Tjøstel Torgeirsen (b. about 1633) was then about 12. Wife not named. Identification by name and farm only; grade unchanged. ¶4

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the grade

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Why Probable: the 1664-66 Tjøstel of Rostveit is identified with Tjøstel, father of Tergie, Knud and Ulf Tjøstelsen, because (1) he worked Rostveit, where Tergie Tjøstelsen later held the land and where the brothers' 1711 settlement was made; (2) in 1666 he had a son Knud aged ½ (b. about 1665), matching Knud Tjøstelsen Homstøl of the 1711 avkallsbrev; and (3) Tjøstel's eldest surviving-line son was named Tergie (Torgeir), the usual naming after a paternal grandfather, as Tallak Torgeirsen also named a son Torgeir. No record names Torgeir directly as grandfather. ¶2
