About the family

Mother of Mikkel (Michel) Siursen Velve: churched at Trinity 1711 as 'Siur Welfuet[?]s qv. Ingebor efter 3die barn Michel' (16310 c43), and at Christmas 1709 after her 2nd child Birit (c39 L). Married to Siur Velve by about 1707. Patronymic and origin not given. Probable for the same reasons as her husband. ¶1

Life story

Widow at the probate after Siver Michelsen Velve, 18 Jun 1742: 'Enke: Ingebor Svendsdatter' (card 77804 c2131). ¶3a

Protocol entry of her husband's skifte read (24752 fol. 1126b, view 393, 18 Jun 1742): the widow Ingebor Svendsdatter took as her lauverge 'sit Søskende Barn Erich Michelsen' - her first cousin Erik Michelsen - and Berit Siversdatter's guardian was her cousin Peder Andersen [Vigen]. No parent or sibling of Ingebor is named, so her parents remain unknown. Peder Andersen Vigen (23 in 1728) was the son of Anders Pedersen Vigen and Helge Michelsdatter (skifte 2 Aug 1728, 24750 fol. 215b, view 223: children Peder 23, Michel 19, Arne 18, [Gunder?] 14, Berit 22, Marit 12, Mali 8; guardian their farbroder Ole Pedersen), so he was a cousin of Siver's children through Helge Michelsdatter, probably Siver's sister (inference). ¶5

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Biography gaps: her exact birth date (before 1690) and baptism predate the 1699 Selbu register. Her death, death place and burial are not found after her last record read, the 1742 Velve probate, and the 77804 index stops at 1742; a death in the 1747-56 register gap would appear in no Selbu book. The next free checks are the 16315 burials (1739-46) and then the 16311 burials from 1757, neither reported searched for her. ¶4
