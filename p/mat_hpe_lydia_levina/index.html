Life story

Per the compiled Hicken genealogy: born 12 May 1853 at Gaspereaux and baptised 12 August 1853 at the Vernon River Bible Christian Church. The compiler pairs her as a twin with John William, but his baptism index gives his birth as 24 January 1855, so they were not twins. Her baptism is not in the online indexes. She is not in the 1881 household. Fate unknown. ¶1
