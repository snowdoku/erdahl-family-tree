About the family

Widow of Michel Christensen of Austenaa/Oland (probate 14 Nov 1731), by whom she had Even Michelsen (later of Austenaa) and Ane Michelsdatter; then wife of Ole Næresen of Oland, Gjøvdal (probate 9 Feb 1761), by whom she had Gunild, Sigri, Astri, Ingri/Ingeborg and Nære (Østre Råbyggelag skiftekort, Skp. 12b fol. 439). ¶1

Life story

The archivist's note on the 1761 card gives her parents as Efwind (Even) Tolfsen Askland (probate 16 Nov 1724) and Astri Gullougsdatter; not added as people (index annotation only). ¶3

1762 extra-tax roll (ekstraskatt, Hovedmanntall), Lille Topdal (Tovdal) sogn, farm 517 Østenaae (Austenaa), canvas 164: household 'Even og H: Anna' with farmhands Christen and Michel, boy over 12 'Nore' (Nære), girl over 12 Gunild, and 'Uldvild' (Ulvild) listed on her own account. The roll gives given names only. Identified as Ulvild with her son Even Michelsen and her children Nære and Gunild by Ole Næresen, because all these names match the 1761 probate family on its own farm (Probable). Her daughter Ingri/Ingebor (about 10) was under 12 and so not listed. So in 1762, a year after Ole Næresen's estate was settled, Ulvild lived at Austenaa with her son Even. ¶4

In the 1776 probate after her sister's son Even Tellefsen (Hushovd, Mykland), her children by Ole Næresen are listed as Astrie (wife of Even Wrolsen Oland), Sigrie (wife of Ole Kiddelsen Austenaa) and Ingeborg (wife of Torgius Ormsen Skjeggedal) (Skp. 14a fol. 73). Her sisters there are Ingeborg Evensdatter (wife of Ole Olsen Austad) and Berthe Evensdatter (wife of Jørgen Tolfsen Harstveit). ¶2
