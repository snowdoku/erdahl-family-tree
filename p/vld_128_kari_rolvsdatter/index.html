About the family

Wife of Jon Alvsen of Raannaahougen (Rånåhaugen), Lesja; mother of Joen, baptised 28 Nov 1784 at the Verket annex church (Lesja ministerialbok 1777-1819, p. 192, read on the scan). Aged 50, married once, in the 1801 census (Lesja, Raanaae, household 3). ¶1

Mother (Speculative): Anne Pedersdatter, the wife named at Rolv Knudsen Slettum's child's baptism of 13 Jun 1756 (9236 p. 421). Kari's own baptism is still unread: searched on the scan without a hit, 9236 canvases 160-161 (Sep 1748-Feb 1749), 165-168 (Oct 1749-Sep 1750) and 170-171 (Dec 1750-Jan 1751); unread: c152-159 (1747-48), 162-164, 169, 172-178. ¶6

Life story

In 1763 she is 'Kari', a child over 12 of Rolv Knudsøn and his (second) wife Guro Jesbr.d. at No. 12 Slette, Lesja (Ekstraskatt Gudbrandsdal 1762-66, source 45390, view 287, 1763 Manntall for Lesja). This bridges her 1748 baptism to her 1775 marriage on the same farm, and raises her father Rolv (Rolf) Knudsen Slettum to Confirmed. Her own grade (link to John Johnsen Sætnes) is unchanged. ¶8

Home farm found: at her betrothal (30 Apr 1775) and marriage (9 Jul 1775) to Joen Alvsen she was 'Pige Kari Rolvsd. Slettum' (Lesja 9236, pp. 689 and 692, scan read). On the same betrothal page, immediately before her, is 'Peder Rolvsen Slettum' with Marit Erlandsdatter Ødegaarden (married Sep 1775, p. 696), and 'Peder Slettum' was first godfather to her daughter Aase in Jan 1776: Peder Rolvsen of Slettum was evidently her brother. The only Rolv found at Slettum is Rolf Knudsen, buried from Slettom 12 Oct 1783, b. about 1720 (pg00000000422951), entered as her probable father. Grade unchanged (Speculative, graded on her link to John Johnsen Sætnes). ¶3

New evidence: the family moved from Raanaahaugen to Klevstuen (Klevstuen in Lesjaskogen), the very place from which 'Ungkarl Joen Joensen Klevstuen' married Anne Pedersdatter of Kloppen on 13 Mar 1816. (1) Alv Joensen of Klevstuen, b. about 1780, was buried 22 May 1803: this is the son Alv, baptised 6 Feb 1780 to Jon of Raanaahougen and Kari Rolvsd., and the tailor Alv, 20, of the 1801 Raanaa household. (2) Rolv Joensen, bachelor of Klevstuen, married Rønnog Olsdatter of Qvam on 12 Jun 1808 and had a son Joen at Klevstuen that Nov (children again 1811): he is the son Rolv baptised 1 Mar 1778 to Jon of Raanaahogen and Karen Rolvsd., named for Kari's father Rolv. (3) 'John Alvsen Klavstuen' was buried 4 Dec 1832, aged 78, and 'Kari Rolvsdr Klevstuen' died 25 Dec 1832 and was buried 10 Mar 1833, aged 85 (Lesja ministerialbok 1832-1850, pp. 334-335 and 340-341; also in the 1830-1842 book, p. 489). So the Raanaa family of 1801 was at Klevstuen by 1803 and stayed there until both parents died, and the only Joen Joensen recorded at Klevstuen, the 1816 groom, lived there with them. The previous Klevstuen occupant, Joen Olsen (b. about 1724), had been buried 13 Jul 1800. Rival check: Lesja baptisms 1776-96 of a Joen son of Joen/Jon give one other boy of the right age, Joen b. 17 Jul 1784, son of the shoemaker Jon Olsen (buried 15 Aug 1784) and Giesken Knudsdatter; nothing links him to Klevstuen and he is not in the 1801 census under that name (the only Joen Joensen b. 1783-86 in the Lesja 1801 census is ours at Raanaa). No probate was found: the Nord-Gudbrandsdal sorenskriveri probate card index (source 39945, filed by farm) has only one Klevstuen card (Hans Jonsen, 1756, fol. 283b), none for 1832-33. ¶4

Baptism found, Lesja ministerialbok 1732-1776 (source 9236), p. 325 (canvas 156), read on the scan: under 'Dom. Esto Mihi Lesjød' 1748 [25 Feb], 'Bapt: Rolf Slettums Kari', godparents including Guro Slettum and Kari Enstad, 'fød d. 19 Febr.' The mother's churching follows two weeks later at the Verket annex, 'Dom. Oculi Wærket... item Rolff Slettums Anne' [10 Mar 1748]. Her burial age of 85 in March 1833 points to a birth in 1747-48, which fits this entry (the 1801 census age of 50 understates it by three years). This is the only Kari of a Rolv Slettum found in the Lesja baptisms 1747-56 that have been read. Her own grade is unchanged (Probable, graded on her link to John Johnsen Sætnes): this record concerns her parents, not her son. ¶7

Possible origin (Speculative): Peder Rolvsen, 56, farmer at Slettum in 1801, may be her brother; four Slettum people stood godparent to John Johnsen's sons in 1816-1817. Unproved; her baptism about 1751 is in the unindexed Lesja book of 1732-1776. ¶2

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
