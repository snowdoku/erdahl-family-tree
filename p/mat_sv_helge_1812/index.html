Life story

A child named Helge was baptised to Jon Sivertsen and Siri Bersvensdatter of Eidem on the 22nd Sunday after Trinity 1812, Selbu parish register 1801-1815, page 182 serial 2 - almost certainly this girl, named for her paternal grandmother Haelge Jonsdatter. Rated Probable because 'Helge' was borne by both sexes in Trondelag and a second Helge was baptised at Eidem in 1815 to the other John Sivertsen, so the two must be kept apart. ¶2

Baptism: Selbu, 22nd Sunday after Trinity, 25 October 1812, no. 2: 'et Pigebarn, kaldet Helge, foedt d: 12te Octbr. Foraeldre: Jon Siverthsen Eidem og Siri Bersvensd:. Fadd: Sivert Bersvensen Draxen, Jon Siverthsen Eidem, Maglie Sivertsd: Warmdal, Brynnille Olsdatter Eidem, Helge Bersvensd: Draxen' - a girl named Helge, born 12 October; godparents (faddere) her mother's brother Sivert and sister Helge from Draxen, a Jon Sivertsen of Eidem (presumably her father's elder brother of that name), Maglie Sivertsdatter of Varmdal and Brynnille Olsdatter of Eidem. Her mother was churched as 'Jon Siversens Hustrue af Eidem' on the 26th Sunday after Trinity (22 Nov) 1812. Read from the scan (source 16312, canvases 180-181). ¶4

Probate: 'Datteren Helge Johnsdatter, 19 1/2, ugift' (the daughter, 19 1/2, unmarried) is an heir in the skifte after her father John Sivertsen Eidum, closed 8 November 1832 (skifteutlodningsprotokoll 1, fol. 129b-130). Counted from the registration of 8 March 1832 the age agrees with her birth on 12 October 1812. ¶6

1825 census: 'Helge Johnsd.', aged 13, Datter, in the household of Joh Siverts., gaardmand at Eidem, and Sigrid Bersvendsd. She is the eldest of the four children in the house and the elder sister of Sivert Johnsen b. 1815. ¶1

Married by 1854 to Ole Ingebrigtsen (farm name read as Lerum, uncertain). He signed the 1854 deed of Eidem as 'gift med Afdødes Datter Helge', husband of the late John Sivertsen's daughter Helge (Pantebok 11 fol. 167b). ¶7

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Confidence raised from Probable to Confirmed: the baptism names her mother Siri Bersvendsdatter, which separates her from the Helge baptised in 1815 to the other Jon Sivertsen of Eidem (whose wife was Elen Sivertsdatter), and her age of 13 in the 1825 census fits the 1812 birth. She was named for her paternal grandmother Haelge Jonsdatter, and shares the name with her mother's sister Helge, her godmother. ¶5
