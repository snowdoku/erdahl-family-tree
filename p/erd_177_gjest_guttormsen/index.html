About the family

His parents: no Guttorm at Nauteskreen/Nødskreen in the Ytre Sogn probate register 1720-1775 (domicile searches Naut*, Nød*, *skreen*: 0 hits). ¶7

Father found: the Ytre Sogn probate after the cottager Guttorm Gjestsen of Vikøren, Vik skipreide, registered 9 Jun 1775 (skifteprotokoll 9a No. 67, p. 601-602, Digitalarkivet 24167 view 302), lists among the heirs 'Sønnen Giest Guttormsen', dead, represented by his only son 'Giest Giestsen', about 14 (share heading 'Sønne Sønnen Giest Giestsen'). That fits this man (buried 1761 aged 28, one son Giest baptised May 1761) exactly in name and age, but the probate names neither Nauteskreen nor his widow, so the father link is Probable. His own grade (as father of Gjest Gjestsen) is unchanged; the 1775 probate does, however, independently show a 'Giest Giestsen' aged about 14, son of a dead Giest Guttormsen, consistent with the Esse man (b. 1761). ¶11

Mother found: his father Guttorm Gjestsen (then of Seim) married 'sin besvangrede Synneve Arnes Daatter Seimsøren' on 28 Dec 1732 in Vik (11752 c30 left), and she was still his widow at the 1775 Vikøren probate that names 'Sønnen Giest Guttormsen'. The 1761 burial age (28) puts Gjest's birth in Jul 1732 to Jul 1733, matching the pregnancy at the wedding. Probable (no baptism naming him has been read; the 1727-40 Vik baptisms are only partly indexed). A child of 'Gutorm Giests: Vigøren' was baptised in March 1736 with the name left blank (11752 c51 right), a younger sibling. ¶12

Parents: his father Guttorm Giestsen and mother Synneve Arnesdatter of Vikøren are now Confirmed (see their notes). Continuity: the probates after his unmarried daughters at Vikøren, Brithe Guttormsdatter ([26?] Oct 1807, Ytre Sogn 13 p. 271b, 24173 view 274) and Christi Guttormsdatter (3 Oct 1809, 13 p. 551b, No. 54, 24173 view 554), each name as first heir their 'Fuldbroder Giest Guttormsen, der boede og døde paa gaarden Nødskreen i Tiugum Skibrede, og var gift med... Anna Hermundsdatter', and his one son 'Giest Giestsen, boende som huusmand paa g. Indre Esse i Tiugums Skibrede', present (he signed the 1809 entry 'Giest Giestsen Indre Esse'). Brithe, Christi and Rande are the daughters, and Giest Guttormsen (dead, son Giest Giestsen 14) the son, of Guttorm Giestsen and Synneve Arnesdatter in the 1775 Vikøren probate, held 'imellem Enken Synneve Arnesdatter og deres tilsammen avlede Børn'. The 1807/1809 records name the same sibling set (Giest, Rande m. Ole Iversen, Christi, Brithe) at the same farm Vikøren 32-34 years later, so this is a pair/sibling-set bridge (audit 8 rule 3), and 'Fuldbroder' makes Giest a child of both parents. The Nødeskreen husband of Anna Hermundsdatter is the tree's Gjest Guttormsen (probate 18 Sep 1761, Nauteskreen; burial 5 Jul 1761). ¶16a

Life story

Betrothal found: 'Spons: Giest Guttormsøn / Anna Hermunds D. Kleen[?]', Tjugum, Dom. III post Trin. [1 Jul 1759] (Leikanger 11566 c34 left). One of the two sureties was 'Mathias Vigøren', from the farm where Guttorm Gjestsen lived. A cottager Mathias Giestsen of Vikøren died before 22 May 1778 (Ytre Sogn 9b p. 939, No. 11, 24167 view 473; widow Ingeleif Olsdatter, children Johannes, Giest and Siur Mathiassen, Ragnilde, Brithe 7 and Angaric 4 1/2); he is probably Guttorm's brother (same patronymic and farm), which would make the surety Gjest's uncle, but neither record states it. This ties the Tjugum Gjest Guttormsen to Vikøren for the first time, so the father link (on Guttorm Gjestsen) is stronger. It stays Probable, because no record names the relationship across the two farms. He was also godfather at Tjugum on 20 Jul 1760 as 'Giest Gutthormsøn Schreen' (11566 c46 right), so he was at Nauteskreen a year before his death. ¶13

Bygdebok (Vik H. 3 canvas 14, Vikøyri chapter for Guttorm Gjestson «Seim», 1706-1775): the children of Guttorm and Synneva begin with [Gjest] '1732-61', and the text says 'Sonen Gjest gifte seg og kom til plassen [...]' and '[...] han Gjest 1761'. This is the bygdebok's statement of the same family: a son Gjest born 1732, married, moved to a plass, died 1761, with a son Gjest born 1761. It supports his link to Guttorm and Synneva but is a compiled source, so no grade changes. ¶17a

Probate registration 18 Sep 1761 (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll 7b, fol. 546b, No. 57): 'Ao 1761 den 18de Septbr. var jeg paa [Nødskreen/Nauteskreen], Proprietair gods udi Tiugums Skibrede... efter afgangne Huusmand Giest Guttormsen imellem hands efterlevende Enke Anne Hermunds datter, og den Sal. Mands med hende auflede Søn Giest Giestsen 1/2 aar gl.' Present: the lensmann of Tjugum skipreide Ole Pedersen Wangsnes; appraisers Anders Christensen [Hiernestad?] and Anders Hansen [Meene?]. The estate was small; a guardian (formynder) for the child is named at the foot of the entry but not legible. Farm name read from the probate register as Nauteskreen; the scan looks like 'Nødskreen'. ¶1

Burial found: Leikanger ministerialbok 11566, Dom. VII post Trinitatis, 5 Jul 1761, Tjugum: 'Giest Guttormsøn Schreen 28 An:', so born about 1733. He died some seven weeks after the baptism of his son 'Giest Giestsøn Nødeschreen' (17 May 1761), not before it as earlier guessed; the boy was named after a living, dying father. Buried the same day at Tjugum: Hermund Nielsøn ibid. [Schreen], 15 years 2 days(?), and Ole Larsøn Meenes, 7 weeks 1 day; Hermund Nielsen at Schreen may be kin of his wife Anne Hermundsdatter, unproved. ¶6a

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 6

Probable as father of Gjest Gjestsen: the probate names his only child Giest Giestsen, half a year old in Sep 1761 (born about early 1761). The 1801 census for Leikanger has exactly one Giest Giestsen, the cottager at Esse aged 40 (born 1761; 1815 census age 54), and Esse lies in the same Tjugum skipreide. Unique given name and patronymic, birth year and district agree; the probate does not name Esse, so the identity rests on that match rather than on one record naming both. ¶2

Upgrade: as father of Gjest Gjestsen. Two primary records now agree: the Leikanger baptism of 'Giest Giestsøn Nødeschreen' on 17 May 1761 (11566 c56, p. 57) and the probate at Nauteskreen of 18 Sep 1761 naming the widow Anne Hermundsdatter and the son 'Giest Giestsen 1/2 aar gl.' (7b fol. 546b). The baptism fixes the birth to spring 1761, which matches exactly the 1801 (40) and 1815 (54) census ages of Giest Giestsen at Esse in the same Tjugum skipreide, the only man of that name in Leikanger in 1801; his daughter Anna (1801 census age 4) bears the grandmother's name. The link to Esse rests on that uniqueness, not on one record naming both places. ¶3

Coordinator review (1 Nov 2026): kept at Probable under the overnight grading rule: the 1761 baptism and the probate prove this couple's posthumous son Giest, but his identity with Giest Giestsen of Esse rests on the unique name and an exact age match, which is identification by elimination. ¶5

Grade unchanged: the burial gives his age, not his link to Giest of Esse. ¶6b

Re-proof attempt (overnight 1-2 Nov 2026), no change: still Probable. Searched for a record that names Giest Giestsen of Esse as son of Anne Hermundsdatter or of Nauteskreen. The Leikanger register 1770-91 (11567) is unindexed, so his confirmation (about 1776-78) and marriage (before 1797) were not reached. Probates of every Anne Hermundsdatter in the Ytre Sogn register were checked: Hanevik, 5 Apr 1780 (9b p. 1090, view 24167/548, scan read) is the wife of Niels Andersen with a son Peder Hansen by an earlier husband Hans Erichsen, not ours; Hundsrøven under Bale, 1810s (15 fol. 54b, view 24175/46, scan read) is the widow of a Mons, with children Erich, Anders (18), Hermund (15) and Ingeborg Monsdatter (23), not ours. ¶8a

Upgrade: graded on his link to his son Gjest Gjestsen (see his page), Probable -> Confirmed. The record his notes asked for (one naming Giest Giestsen of Esse as son of the Nødeskreen Giest Guttormsen) was found by searching the Ytre Sogn probate index by farm (domicile Vikøren 1763-1810): the probates after his sisters Brithe Guttormsdatter, Vikøren, [26?] Oct 1807 (Ytre Sogn 13 p. 271b, 24173 view 274) and Christi Guttormsdatter, Store Vikøren, 3 Oct 1809 (13 p. 551b, No. 54, 24173 view 554) both name 'Afdøde Fuldbroder Giest Guttormsen, der boede og døde paa gaarden Nødskreen i Tiugum Skibrede, og var gift med ligesaa afdøde Anna Hermundsdatter, med hvem han i et lovligt Egteskab har avlet og efterladt sig een Søn', namely 'Brodersøn Giest Giestsen, boende som huusmand paa g. Indre Esse i Tiugums Skibrede', present and signing 'Giest Giestsen Indre Esse'. Continuity: the tree's Gjest Gjestsen is the cottager at Esse in the 1801 (40) and 1815 (54) censuses, the only Gjest at Esse; 1809 lies between them at the same farm. The records name farm, wife and son of the Nødeskreen Giest, so the identification no longer rests on name uniqueness. ¶15

The case for this identification 1

Supporting: 'Giest Giestsen', 18-19, living at Tue, Tjugum sogn, was confirmed on 26 Sep 1779 (Leikanger 11567, canvas 234, fol. 230), the only man of the name in the 1771-1781 confirmation lists. This carries the posthumous son of the 1761 Nauteskreen probate forward to 1779 within Tjugum sogn, but at a third farm and without parents' names, so the link to Gjest Gjestsen stays Probable (graded on that link). No probate at Tue in Tjugum 1761-1800 is indexed (Ytre Sogn index: the Tue rows of 1782-99 are Tue in Kvamsøy). ¶9a

Alternatives considered and set aside 2

Parent candidate ruled out: the Ytre Sogn probate after Guttorm Simonsen of Tveit (Tvedt), Tjugum skipreide, registered 5 May 1752 (skifteprotokoll 7a, fol. 93a, No. 14; Digitalarkivet 24163 view 93), names his widow Ingebor Olsdatter and their children: one son, Simon Guttormsen (of age), the eldest daughter Brite Guttormsdatter, 27, and the younger daughter Ingegierd Guttormsdatter. There is no Gjest, although Gjest Guttormsen (b. about 1733 by his 1761 burial age) would have been about 19 and listed. So he was not a son of this couple; Simon Guttormsen (1801: 80, at Tvedt) was not his brother. Other Guttorm probates in the Ytre Sogn index that could list him or his son Giest Giestsen as heirs are still unread: Guttorm Gjestsen, Vikøren (9a p. 601, 1772-76; the name pattern Gjest Guttormsen son of Guttorm Gjestsen would fit; view 24167/301 shows p. 600-601 but the entry was not found on the page), and Guttorm Pedersen, Vangsnes (10a p. 239, 1783-86). Also ruled out: Guttorm Bentsen of Hove, Tjugum skipreide, probate No. 55, 30 Jun 1766 (skifteprotokoll 8a fol. 381b-382a, Digitalarkivet 24165 view 384): widow Gjøe Nielsdatter, and the heirs are his brothers and sisters and their children (Bent Jonsen Tenold, Ole Jonsen, Anders Jonsen 18, Anders Bentsen Orvedal's children, Anna Bentsdatter m. Hermund... Medlang, etc.). He left no children, so neither Gjest Guttormsen nor his son Giest Giestsen is an heir. Grade unchanged. ¶10

No rival: no other Giest Guttormsen is known in Vik or Tjugum. ¶16b

Searches that found nothing 2

Not found among the Leikanger burials of Jan-May 1761 (11566 c53-c57); he probably died between the conception (spring/summer 1760) and the probate, perhaps before the May 1761 baptism given that the child carries his name. His own burial is the next canvas to read (11566 c50-c52 for late 1760, c57-c63 for summer 1761). ¶4

Baptism still not found. The 8 Feb 1733 entry in Vik 11752 (canvas 31 left), re-read at high resolution, says only '2. Døbt Sexagesima Søndag gutorm hofsvigs barn': the child's name, sex and mother are not given, and the father is identified by the farm Hofsvig, not Seim (where Guttorm Gjestsen lived at his marriage five weeks earlier) or Vikøren. It cannot be used for him. His marriage to Anne Hermundsdatter is not in Leikanger 11566 for Jul-Nov 1759 or in Vik 11754 for Jul 1759-Jan 1760 (see Anne Hermundsdatter). Grade unchanged. ¶14
