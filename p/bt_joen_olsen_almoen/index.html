Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Speculative father of Ane Jonsdatter, the first wife of Anders Larsen of Almoen. He was buried from Almoen on 5 October 1783, entered as an 'inderst' - a lodger with no holding of his own - born about 1704. ¶1

The case for this identification 1

The case for the link is circumstantial: his given name matches Ane's patronymic; his age fits a man whose daughter was born in 1737; he was lodging on the very farm where Ane was the gårdkone; and Ane's youngest son, baptized in August 1781, was named Joen. Ingeborg Joensdatter, born about 1702 and buried from Almoen in October 1780, may have been his wife. No record has yet been found that states the relationship. ¶2
