Life story

Marriage found: Stjørdal ministerialbok 1755-1780 (16371), marriage section p. 328 (canvas 330). She was betrothed at Værnes on 16 Feb 1761 as 'Pigen Anne Jonsdatter Elmoen' (the maid Anne Jonsdatter, of Almoen) to the bachelor soldier Anders Larsen Setran, who had leased half of Almoen, and married him at Hegre on 9 Apr 1761. She was therefore already living on Almoen, unmarried, before her marriage. This supports the speculative identification of the elderly inderst couple on Almoen, Joen Olsen (b. ~1704, buried 1783) and Ingeborg Joensdatter (b. ~1702, buried 8 Oct 1780), as her parents: when a farmer's daughter married, her husband commonly took over part of the lease of her home farm. A third person on the farm fits the same family. Malli Olsdatter, born about 1700, was buried from Almoen on 14 Dec 1766 (Hegre, 16371 p. 149); she was plausibly Joen Olsen's sister. Stjørdalsboka V 1 p. 526 has an 'Ola Olsen' who leased Almoen in 1701 (named to 1737), a possible father of both Joen and Malli. Ane's children by Anders include Jon (bapt. 16 Feb 1766) and Mali (bapt. 31 Jul 1768), names that fit this family. The link is still Speculative: no record yet read names her father. ¶6

Burial (per index, Hegre parish, church book 1781–1797, p. 269): buried 24 Oct 1790, 'Ane Jonsdatter', gaardkone (farmer's wife), of Almoen, aged 53, so born about 1737. ¶3

Farmer's wife (gårdkone) at Almoen, buried 1790 aged about 53. She is presumed to be the first wife of Anders Larsen and mother of his children born 1764-1781. The 1770 baptism index names only the father. ¶1

Her probate, 13 Nov 1790 (24763 view 247, top lines) with enhanced contrast: the curator for her son Thomas and guardian for Ole, Jon, Mali, Berithe, Randi and Anne was 'Sambeboeren' (the co-resident on the farm), whose name reads Tollef[?]. No 'morbroder' or 'morfader' (maternal uncle or grandfather) is named. So the guardian line gives no kin of hers, which closes that lead. The appraisers were Anders H...[?] and Ole Arntsen Giederaas. ¶7

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Confidence moved Probable to Confirmed: the probate after her husband Anders Larsen Almoen, opened 23 May 1808 (Stjør- og Verdal skifteprotokoll 19, fol. 93, Digitalarkivet 57508), lists 'by his first wife Anne Jonsdatter' the children Lars, Thomas, Ole, Jon, Mahlie (dead), Gjerthe and Randie (30). Thomas is our line (see his page). Her own probate (d. Oct 1790) was not found in protocol 10; this 1808 entry does not cite one. ¶4

Corrections 1

Correction: her probate WAS found in protocol 10 (the 1808 note said it was not). Stjør- og Verdal skifteprotokoll 10, printed p. 232 (Digitalarkivet 24763 views 246-247; register entry 'Anne Jonsd. Almaaen i Stjørd.'): 'Anno 1790 d. 13de November' on the farm Almaaen after 'den afgangne Kone Anne Jonsdatter', between her widower Anders Larsen and their eight children: Lars (of age), Thomas 21, Ole 16 (age uncertain), Jon 9, Mali 22, M[e]rthe(?) 18 (the 1808 probate calls this daughter Gjerthe), Randi 15 and Anne 13. A curator was appointed for Thomas and a guardian for the younger children; their names are not securely legible, and no parent of Anne is named in the legible parts. Read. ¶5

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Candidate parents, Speculative: an elderly couple were living as inderster (lodgers with no land of their own) on Almoen in exactly her parents' generation. Joen Olsen, born about 1704, was buried from Almoen on 5 October 1783 as an 'inderst', and Ingeborg Joensdatter, born about 1702, was buried from Almoen on 8 October 1780. A retired couple lodging on the farm where their married daughter was the gårdkone is the commonest of arrangements, and Joen Olsen's given name matches Ane's patronymic. Her son Joen, baptized 1781, would then be named for his maternal grandfather within two years of that grandfather's death. This is a fit, not a proof: no record yet read links them. ¶2
