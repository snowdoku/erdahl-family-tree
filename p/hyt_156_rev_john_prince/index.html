About the family

Father of Elder John Prince of Hull (Probable): the obituary of Elder John's son Samuel Prince (d. Middleborough, 3 July 1728), written by Samuel's son the Rev. Thomas Prince and printed in the New England Weekly Journal of 16 July 1728, says Samuel 'was the 4th son of Mr. John Prince Ruling Elder of the Church of Hull, in New England who was the eldest son of the Rev. Mr. John Prince Rector of East Shefford in Barkshire in England, in the Reign of King James I. and King Charles I.' It adds that the rector was a conforming Puritan who 'had Married Elizabeth a Daughter of Dr. Tolderbury D.D. of Oxford, by whom he had 4 Sons & 7 daughters that all grew up', all of whom became nonconformists, and that Archbishop Laud's persecution drove 'their eldest son' to Massachusetts, where, 'having studdied at the University of Oxford 2 or 3 years', he took to husbandry and married Alice Honor (NEHG Register 5 (1851): 378-379). The Hull gravestone record of Elder John ('eldest s. of Rev. John') repeats the claim. ¶1

Life story

Testing the claim against English records: (1) Foster's Alumni Oxonienses has 'Prince, John; B.A. from Magdalen Coll. 6 July, 1601; perhaps rector of East alias Little Shefford, Berks, 162[?]' (the last digit is illegible in the OCR), so a John Prince, an Oxford graduate of the right generation (born about 1580), is found as rector of East Shefford in the 1620s, under James I and Charles I as the obituary says; Foster's 'perhaps' links the graduate to the rector, not the rector to the emigrant. (2) A John and Elizabeth Prince had children baptised at Great Shefford, the adjoining parish: Joane on 9 Oct 1614 and Dorothye on 2 Mar 1615[/16] (Ancestry 9841 index), matching the obituary's 'Elizabeth' and its many daughters. (3) No baptism of Elder John (born Aug 1610-Aug 1611, from his age at death) was found; FreeREG has almost no Berkshire Prince entries for 1595-1640. The claim is therefore a family statement made by the emigrant's grandson within the lifetime of the emigrant's son, consistent with the English records but not proved by them: Probable. ¶2

Career and burial (compiled source): Noyes-Gilman Ancestry (1907), pp. 306-307, says he was B.A. of Magdalen 6 July 1601, married about 1609, probably at Oxford, Elizabeth Toldervey; was ordained at Oxford in September 1611; was curate of Little or East Shefford in 1613; became rector in 1619 and held the living until his death; and was buried at East Shefford, quoting the register, 'XVI day of Sept. 1644'. It lists his children from the registers: Joane bp. Great (West) Shefford 9 Oct 1614; Dorothye bp. there 2 Mar 1615[/16]; Ursula bp. there 20 Feb 1619[/20]; Edward b. East Shefford 7 Feb 1622, bp. 24 Nov 1622; George b. and bp. East Shefford 4 Feb 1626; Katherine bp. East Shefford 26 Jul 1629; Maria bp. there 26 Sep 1630. The Great Shefford baptisms of Joane and Dorothye agree with the Ancestry 9841 index already cited, which supports the book's use of the registers. Death entered as before the burial of 16 Sep 1644. The book says nothing of his parents: it records only the claim in John Prince's Worthies of Devon (1701) that the family descended from a knightly Shropshire family, and states 'we know nothing of Rev. John Prince's ancestry, or early life'. Confidence unchanged (Probable): the ordination, institution and burial entries have not been read in the original (CCEd still returned 503 on 1 Nov 2026). ¶4

Oxford register: Clark's Register II pt 3 (degrees) has 'Prince (Prinse, Prynce), John; adm. B.A. 6 July 1601' of Magdalen College, but no matriculation entry for him could be found in Register II pt 2 (OCR searched for Prince/Prynce/Prinse), so his county and father's status are not known. The only Prince matriculand of the period noticed is 'Prynce, Francis; Salop, arm. f., 15', which bears on the family tradition (Prince's Worthies of Devon, 1701) of descent from a Shropshire armigerous family, but it does not name John. ¶5a

Marriage and eldest son (reported via WikiTree, not read): John Prynce married Elizabeth Toldervey at Great Shefford on 26 Sep 1606 (Findmypast Berkshire Marriages index, bishop's transcript), and a John son of John Prince was baptised at Great Shefford on 3 Sep 1612 (Findmypast Berkshire Baptisms index). WikiTree treats the latter as Elder John of Hull, but Elder John's age at death (d. 16 Aug 1676 in his 66th year) points to Aug 1610-Aug 1611, so the identification is unproved. WikiTree also lists 'Frances', son of John and Elizabeth Prince, bp. Compton, Berkshire, 27 Mar 1618 (FS 1:1:JQ1X-9MQ), and George bp. East Shefford 11 Feb 1626/7 (Noyes-Gilman: 4 Feb 1626). The 1630 Inkpen administration of Walter Toldervey reportedly names 'John Prince, clerk, of Great Shefford' with Walter's son-in-law Edward Gerrard, and Edward Garrard's will (PCC 1633, PROB 11/163) reportedly names John Prince as his brother-in-law. ¶9

Origin, still open: the Magdalen College registers of demies (Bloxam, Register of Magdalen College, demies volumes, archive.org aregisterpresid04oxfogoog and 05) have no Prince, so the B.A. of 6 July 1601 was probably a commoner or servitor; his matriculation is not in Clark's Register II pt 2. CCEd (theclergydatabase.org.uk) again returned 503 on 2 Nov 2026, so his 1611 ordination (which may give age and birthplace) and his 1619 institution are unread. ¶10a

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

Alternatives considered and set aside 2

Not confirmed: the institution records of East Shefford (Clergy of the Church of England Database, CCEd; Salisbury diocesan registers) were not reachable (CCEd returned 503 on 1 Nov 2026), and the East Shefford parish register for about 1608-1615 has not been read. ¶3

Lead only, not entered (FamilySearch index, 2 Nov 2026): 'England, Births and Christenings, 1538-1975' (IGI, no image) has John, son of John Prince, christened at St Mary, Reading, Berkshire, on 3 Aug 1609 (1:1:NP2Y-847). It is about the right date for Elder John Prince of Hull (born about 1610). Against it: the same Reading John Prince had children baptised there from Harie (28 Feb 1607/8) to Richard (Nov 1621), with Elizabeth 1615 (died), Mathew 1616 and Elizabeth 1619, so he was a Reading householder throughout, while the rector was curate and then rector of East Shefford from 1613. He was probably not this man. ¶8a

Searches that found nothing 2

Probate search: the Archdeaconry of Berks index of wills and administrations, 1508-1652, has no Prince of East or Little Shefford. Its only Prince entry is Joan Prince of Warfield, 1618. So the rector, buried at East Shefford in Sept 1644, left no will in the local court. A PCC will or administration of 1644-1646 is the next place to look (TNA PROB 11 or PROB 6; the PCC was disrupted in 1644-45 by the Civil War). The same index has an administration for Walter Toldervie of Inkpen, gent., 1630 (see Walter Toldervey). ¶6

East Shefford records on FamilySearch (2 Nov 2026): the England, Berkshire, Parish Registers index (Berkshire Archives) has the burial of John Prince at East Shefford, 16 Sep 1644 (1:1:6DS5-6B52), and the baptism of Edward, son of John and Elizabeth Prince, East Shefford, 24 Nov 1622, born 17 Nov (1:1:6DSR-3LL9). Edward fits a younger son of the rector and Elizabeth. No Prince baptism 1605-1621 is indexed at East or Great Shefford, so Elder John's baptism (c.1610-11) is still unread. The East Shefford register (FHL film 251841, DGS 8098779, 1603-1734) is center-only (all 81 images locked from home); the bishop's transcripts (FHL 1279468 items 3-6, DGS 8067124) are center-only too and lack 1608-1613 anyway. As he became rector only about 1619, the baptism of c.1610-11 may be elsewhere (Great Shefford, where Joane 1614 and Dorothye 1615/16 were baptised, has no FamilySearch index). Grade unchanged (Probable). ¶7
