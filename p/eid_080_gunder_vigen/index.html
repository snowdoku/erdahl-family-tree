About the family

Children baptised in Selbu as 'Gunder Vigens barn' (the register gives the father only): Anne on the 7th Sunday after Trinity 1729, buried on the 22nd Sunday after Trinity 1729 aged 14 weeks (canvases 118 left and 119 left); Ole on the 17th Sunday after Trinity 1730 (canvas 121 right); Joen on Misericordia Sunday 1732 (canvas 126 right); Anne on the Sunday after the Circumcision, 2 January 1735, with the mother churched at Septuagesima 1735 (canvases 134 right and 135 left); and Ingebor on the 8th Sunday after Trinity, 22 July 1736, with the mother churched on the 13th Sunday after Trinity (canvas 140 left). ¶1

Parentage unknown, and deliberately not linked. He is not a known son of Gunnar Olsen: the bygdebok's child list under Gunnar Olsen (word-box fragments with Ola 1701, Marit, a death in 1707, an Ola against 1709, and Marit 1712) shows no Gunder, as far as the word boxes reveal; the page itself has not been read. His first daughter was named Anne and his first son Ole, which under the usual naming custom points to parents named Ole and Anne. At Vigen at this date there is exactly such a couple: Ole Nielsen Vigen, whose wife Anne Gundersdatter was buried at Cantate 1730 aged 62 (canvas 120 right), and who may be the 'Olof Wigen' with wife Ane whose first child was baptised in September 1701 (canvas 10 right). That is a lead only. ¶3

Life story

'Gunder Setter fra Vigen' stood godfather twice in 1713 (16310 c49, c50) - a Gunder from Sæter living at Vigen, which may be this man (and may mean he was not a son of Gunnar Olsen Vigen). Unresolved; a probate after Gunnar Olsen Vigen (d. 1731; Stjør- og Verdal skifteprotokoll 2a/2B, sources 24750-24751) would settle it. ¶5

How sure are we?

Speculative. A plausible candidate only. Treat this link as a question, not a fact.

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Rated Speculative as the father of Ane Gundersdatter of Alset (see her page): see the note on her. What is missing is any record that names her farm of birth or her father's full name. ¶4

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Distinct from Gunnar Olsen Vigen (see his page), the father of Ola Gunnarsen in 1701: that Gunnar died in 1731 (bygdebok) and was probably the 'Gunder Vigen æt 75' buried on 8 July 1731, while this Gunder was still having children in 1732-1736. A run of children from 1729 to 1736 points to a man born about 1700-1710 who married about 1728. ¶2

Searches that found nothing 1

Negative: the Stjør- og Verdal probate card index 1689-1742 (Digitalarkivet 77804), V section, odd canvases c2141-c2189, read. The only Selbu Vigen card is c2167, '"Wiigen", Selbu, 2/8-1729: Anders Pedersen Wiigen og hustru Helge Michelsdatter'. There is no card for Gunnar Olsen Vigen (d. 1731) or for any Gunder Vigen, so the hoped-for 1731 probate naming a son Gunder is not in the card index. Link stays Speculative. Not checked: the even canvases (card backs) between c2166 and c2168, and the unindexed protocol 24750/24751 itself for 1731-1732. ¶7

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Biography gaps: his exact birth date, his baptism and his marriage are not found (no marriage card for him in 16310 c96-130, and his own baptism is not recorded in the notes), and his death and burial after his last child in 1736 are not recorded; the Selbu book has no entries for Mar 1738-autumn 1739 or 1747-56 that could hold them. The next record is the probate of his putative father Gunnar Olsen Vigen (Stjor- og Verdal 24750-24751, unindexed, not read), which could name a son Gunder and fix his parentage. His confirmation and any probate of his own are not recorded. ¶6
