About the family

Father of Gunder Johnsen Eidem (see his page), rated Probable. For: (1) the baptism of 24 September 1786 names 'Jon Mich: Moen' as the father of a Gunder, and it is the only Gunder son of any Jon baptised in Selbu between October 1783 and March 1790 (full read of 16311 canvases 112-135); (2) the 1801 census enters Gunder Jonssen, 15 (born about 1786), as a child of Jon Mikkelssen at Moen; (3) Gunder Jonssen of Moen is one of only two Gunder Jon*sens in the whole 1801 Selbu index (the other, born 1780 at Overvik, is a son of Jon Nielssen), and in 1825 the only Gunder Jons. born 1781-1791 in Selbu is our man, 'Gunder Johns., 39, Indst.' at Eidem; (4) Gunder named a daughter Siri (1819) and a son Jon (1835), the names of the Moen couple. Against or unexplained: in 1815 he married as 'unge Mand Gunder Johns: Bromman', and no place Bromman has been located (not a Selbu census domicile, not indexed in bygdebok vol. 3), so the step from Moen in 1801 to Bromman in 1815 and Eidem in 1825 is bridged only by name, age and uniqueness, not by a record that names Moen and Eidem together. ¶3a

Pedigree collapse: this draft makes Jon Mikkelsen Moen (see his page) and Beret (Birthe) Mikkelsdatter (see her page) brother and sister, both children of Mikkel Siursen and Brynild Pedersdatter of Velve 791. Both are already direct ancestors of Palmer Eidem: Jon > Gunder Johnsen Eidem > Beret Gundersdatter > Gunder > Oline > Palmer, and Beret > Dordi Jonsdatter Stokke > Ole Jonsen > Kari > Oline > Palmer. So Gunder Johnsen Eidem and Dordi Jonsdatter were first cousins, Oline's parents Gunder and Kari were third cousins, and Mikkel and Brynild stand twice in Palmer's tree as his 4x-great-grandparents. This rests on two Probable links, not on a record that names the relationship; if either link fails, the collapse disappears. ¶11

His wife's probate: 24744 image 235, fol. 778, Moen, October 1821: 'Enkemanden John Mikkelsen' is the widower of the kår-wife Sigrid Gundersdatter, and the heirs include their son Gunder Johnsen, with Gunder Johnsen Eidum as guardian. This is a second primary record (besides his mother's 1786 probate) naming him as the father of Gunder Johnsen Eidem. ¶19

Life story

Baptism and parents (Probable): on the 12th Sunday after Trinity, 5 September 1745, 'Michel Wel[?]stads barn ved Nafn Jonn' was baptised, and the mother was churched as Brynill Pedersdatter (Selbu klokkerbok 16315, canvas 77 and 79). In the 1762 extra tax list Mikel Siursen and Brynild Pedersdatter of Velve 791 have a son Jon over 12. The birth year fits his age 56 in 1801, his children Mikkel, Brynild and Peder repeat both parents' names, and 'Michel Velve' was godfather to his son Gunder in 1786. Why not Confirmed: no record links Jon of Velve to Jon Mikkelsen of Moen directly (he is not at Moen in 1762); his marriage record or a probate after Mikkel Siursen would settle it. ¶10

Probate of his father Michel Sivertsen, Velve, 21 May 1767 (Skifteprotokoll 6-1, 24756, fol. 65b): '4. John Michelsen ligesaa myndig', one of eight children of Michel and Brönild Pedersdatter. 'Myndig' (of age) fits a son aged 21 if the clerk used the word loosely, but strictly it means 25 or over (born 1742 or before). This is the only Jon among the children. ¶13

Marriage: to Siri Gundersdatter (see her page); the marriage entry has not been found. It is entered as 'about 1778' only by inference from their eldest child in 1801, Rangnil, aged 22 (born about 1779), and the census statement that it was the first marriage of both. ¶5

Baptisms of his children: Gunder, 15th Sunday after Trinity (24 September) 1786, 'Par: Jon Mich: Moen, Elen Gund:' (16311 canvas 123 right) and Brynil (Brynhild), 22nd Sunday after Trinity (8 November) 1789, 'Par: Jon Mich: Moen Segri Gund:' (16311 canvas 134 right); his wife was churched on the 2nd Sunday in Advent 1789 as 'Jon Mich: Moens [hustru]'. Read from crops; the 1786 entry was. ¶2

Bygdebok lead (not read on the page): Selbu i fortid og naatid vol. 3, MOEN chapter, p. 469 shows in nb.no word boxes 'Jon Mikkelsen' and a line '1789... Jon Mikkelsen... Siri', and p. 470 'Jon Mikkelsen... Marit'. These skeletons do not say who his parents were or which wife was which; a read of pp. 469-470 would. ¶6

1801 census: Selbu, rural residence 0018 Moen, household 01 - 'Jon Mikkelssen, 56, Husbonde, Begge i 1te ægteskab, Bonde og gaardbeboer' - householder, both spouses in their first marriage, a farmer in possession of the farm. With him are his wife Siri Gundersdatter, 44, and six children entered as 'Deres børn' (their children): Rangnil 22 (unmarried), Mikkel 19, Gunder 15, Brynild 11, Jon 8 and Peder 4. His age gives a birth about 1745. (Census transcription, residents block.) ¶1

1801 census: Jon Mikkelssen, 56, head at Moen. No indexed burial 1801-70 matching Moen or a birth 1738-52. ¶12

1825 census (Probable identification): Selbu, Moen, rural residence 0035*, household 01, person 005 - 'John Michels.', 78 (born about 1747), occupation transcribed 'Hmd.??', alive on the census date 27 November 1825. He is not the head: person 001 is Marit Jørgensdatter, 46, followed by John Michelsen, 19, 'Søn', and Siri Michelsdatter, 11. Taken to be Jon Mikkelsen because the name and farm agree, the age is within two years of his 1801 age of 56, and the domicile search for Moen in 1825 also returns a Peder Johnsen, born 1797, 'gift søn' (married son), with Guri Olsdatter, born 1800 - matching his son Peder, 4 in 1801 (whether Peder is in the same household was not checked). Marit Jørgensdatter's children John and Siri Mikkelsen carry the names of Jon Mikkelsen and Siri Gundersdatter, which suggests she was the widow of his son Mikkel, 19 in 1801 (inference only). His wife Siri is absent, as expected after her burial in March 1821. The death field is set to 'after 27 November 1825' on this record. ¶7

Death narrowed: he died after the census of 27 November 1825 and before the probate registration of 2 December 1833. The grandson John Mikkelsen of the probate fits the 'John Michelsen, 19, Søn' of the 1825 Moen household in which 'John Michels., 78' lived, which supports identification of that 78-year-old as this Jon. This does not settle the link to Gunder Johnsen Eidem, because the estate named no heirs. Not checked: his burial 1826-1833 (Selbu ministerialbok 1824-1842, indexed) for the exact date. ¶16

A second register entry, 'John Moen Mikkelsen, Selbu, fol. 414', in the same protocol (24745) was not read; it is probably the grandson's estate (inference only). ¶17

Death and burial: 'John Michels Moen', died 30 October 1833, buried 3 November 1833, aged 88 (born about 1745, calculated). Selbu ministerialbok 1824-1842, burials 1833, p. 782 no. 52 (Digitalarkivet index record pg00000007125816; scan kb20070920640262 not read). The age of 88 agrees exactly with the baptism of 5 September 1745 taken to be his, and the date fits the probate registration of 2 December 1833 for 'Huusmand John Mikkelsen Moen'. This replaces 'after 27 Nov 1825'. ¶14

Probate: 'Skifte efter Huusmand af Selbo John Mikkelsen Moen' - estate of the cottager John Mikkelsen Moen of Selbu, opened 11 March 1834; registration (Registrering) 2 December 1833, settlement meeting (Skiftesamling) 25 February 1834. Movables valued at 11 spd 72 sk. Payments ('Udgift') include 6 spd 'til den afdødes Sønnesøn John Mikkelsen' (to the deceased's son's son John Mikkelsen) and 2 spd to 'Svigersønnen Liigsanger[?] Sesberg' (the son-in-law, [?]Sesberg). By agreement the grandson John Mikkelsen paid the debts and costs and took all the movables, so no heirs list was written. Strinda og Selbu sorenskriveri, Skifteutlodningsprotokoll 1 (1827-1840), fol. 167b, Digitalarkivet source 24745, image 184 (left page). Found through the protocol's typed alphabetical register (images 3-9: 'John Mikkelsen Moen, Selbu, 167'). ¶15

Update to the 'Unresolved' note about Gunder Johnsen Uglum: the Uglem burial of 5 January 1845 is now taken (Probable) to be his son Gunder's, on the 1835 baptism scan that names Gunder's wife Brynhild Eriksdatter and the husmann status shared with the burial; see the notes on Gunder Johnsen Eidem. There is therefore no rival Gunder Jonsen of about 1784-86 competing for the Moen baptism. ¶9

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed. The probate of Brynille Pedersdatter, widow, at Velve, 20 Feb 1786 (Skifteprotokoll 9-3, 24762, fol. 521b), lists as her heir '3. Sønnen Jon Michelsen myndig og opholder sig paa gaarden Moen', and appoints 'Broderen Jon Michelsen Moen' guardian of his unmarried sisters Ingeborg, Beritte and Anne Michelsdatter. This is a primary record naming Jon Mikkelsen of Moen as the son of Brynild Pedersdatter and the late Mikkel Siursen Velve. It closes the gap noted earlier (that no record tied Jon of Velve to Jon of Moen), and it confirms the pedigree collapse with his sister Beret Mikkelsdatter, who is named in the same probate as the wife of Jon Haldorsen Welve. ¶18

Searches that found nothing 1

Death not found: a search of the Digitalarkivet person index for Selbu church books, events 1801-1870, under Jon/John with Mikkelssen/Mikkelsen/Michelsen (86 hits, both pages read) found no burial of a man born about 1740-1750 and none at Moen. Not yet searched: burials under 'Moen' as a byname, and the scanned burial books 1825-1840. ¶8

Open questions and the next record to read 2

What would confirm it: Gunder's confirmation about 1801-1803 (16311/16312, Moen), a probate (skifte) after Jon Mikkelsen Moen naming his heirs, or an Eidem/Uglem record of Gunder naming his father or Moen. ¶3b

Unresolved for the identification: a Gunder Johnsen 'Uglum', aged 60, was buried on 5 January 1845 (Selbu 1843-1859, burials p. 360 no. 3), and the Eidem bygdebok (vol. 4 p. 203, word boxes only) gives our Gunder's death as 1844. The tree treats the Uglem man as a different person. If he was a separate Selbu-born man of about 1784-86 he would compete with our Gunder for the Moen baptism and census entry, yet he does not appear as a Gunder Jon* of that age in either the 1801 or the 1825 Selbu index; the simplest reading is that he is our Gunder, but that is not settled here. ¶4
