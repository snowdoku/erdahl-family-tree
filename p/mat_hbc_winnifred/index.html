Life story

PEI records: born 31 May 1916 (PEI baptism index); baptised in PEI 3 December 1933; in the 1931 census of Gaspereaux as Winnifred, daughter, 15, student. ¶4

Marriage, BC 1944-09-580195: Winnifred Eunice Hayter, aged 28, a restaurant cook, United Church, of 312 Whyte Avenue, Penticton, born Gaspereaux, Prince Edward Island; father Benjamin Hayter, mother Lydia Adeline Hicken, both born PEI. She married Anders Edvin Oland, a Swedish-born railway section foreman of Penticton, on 27 December 1944 at Penticton United Church, Rev. R. R. Stobie officiating. The witnesses were Leonard Curtis Stevenson of Summerland (who married her sister Bertha six months later) and her sister Edith Rita Borgstrom of West Summerland. ¶2

She was alive in April 1997, when, as Winnifred E. Oland of Penticton, she reported her husband's death. Find a Grave records 'Winnifred "Winnie" Oland', 1916-2011, in the cremation section of Lakeview Cemetery, Penticton, on a stone shared with Anders Edvin Oland. Her 2011 death year comes from that memorial only; the BC death index is public only to about 2006, so it cannot yet be checked there. ¶3

Burial: the City of Penticton's interment register for Lakeview Cemetery lists 'Oland, Winnifred Eunice (nee Hayter), AKA Winnie', interred 31 May 2011 in cremation plot CS-17-28, the plot where her husband Anders Edvin Oland was interred on 21 June 1997. This municipal record confirms the 2011 death year that rested on Find a Grave alone, and gives her maiden name. She died in the spring of 2011, aged about 94; the exact date is not yet found. ¶5

Her youngest sister Rena's obituary of December 2015 says Rena was 'the last of her nine siblings to pass away', which agrees. ¶6
