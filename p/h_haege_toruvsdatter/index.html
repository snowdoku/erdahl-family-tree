About the family

Wife of Tore Thronsen at Felle in the 1801 census, aged 43. ¶1

Father (Probable): Toruv Andersen Hiemdal. She was living at Hiemdal when betrothed, and Toruv Andersen is the only Toruv seen at Hiemdal in the 1773-86 marriage register; he was cautioner in the entry just before hers. The entry does not name her father, and her cautioners came from other Hiemdal households. Her sister would be Gundbjør Toruvsdatter, also of Hiemdal (m. 1775). The second-degree blood kinship (first cousins) fits if Tore's mother Gunild Andersdatter (b. ~1717) was a sister of Toruv Andersen (b. ~1720); that is an inference. Would be confirmed by her baptism (~1758, Kviteseid ministerialbok before 1773), the royal marriage licence (bevilling) of 1781, or a probate (skifte) after Toruv Andersen naming his heirs. ¶4

Pedigree collapse (if the Probable link holds): Toruv Andersen Heimdal is then reached twice in Ingebor Heimdal's ancestry — through his daughter Gundbiør Toruvsdatter (mother of Orm Egildsen Tveit) and through Hæge (mother of Ingebor Toresdatter Felle). Orm Egildsen and Ingebor Toresdatter would be first cousins. ¶5

Mother added: Gunild Olsdatter (see her page), Probable. 'Hæge Torofsdatter' is an heir with her father Torof Andersen in the skifte after Gunild Olsdatter on søndre Hiemdahl (5 May, between Oct 1773 and 1779; Panteregister I 1 fol. 318, DA 10915 view 361). See the mother's record for the grading. ¶10

Life story

Baptized 11 Mar 1759 (2nd Sunday in Lent) in Treungen church: 'Toruvs Hiemdals daatter Hæge'. Godparents: Orm Tvedt, Joen Hiemdal, Joran Aarack, Margit Hiemdal and Margit Olsdatter (Kviteseid ministerialbok 1754-1773, p.49, image 18). This names her father as Toruv of Hiemdal; the father's name is read as 'Toruvs' with slight uncertainty. Her 1801 age of 43 implies a birth year of about 1758; the 1759 baptism is close enough. Toruv Andersen is the only Toruv seen at Hiemdal, so this is strong support for the link to Toruv Andersen Heimdal. ¶6

Confirmed in Nissedal church on the 22nd Sunday after Trinity (12 Nov) 1775 by Hr. Windfeld, in the Treungen list: 'Hæge Torufs daatter Hiemdal, gl. 16 aar' (Kviteseid ministerialbok 1773-1786, confirmations 1775, canvas 212, right page). The Kviteseid lists give name, farm and age only, never parents. ¶8

Named in the skifte after her mother Gunild Olsdatter, søndre Hiemdahl, 15 Feb 1775 (DA 25675 fol. 827a, No. 71, view 831): 'Hæge Torrofsdatter 16 aar', one of 'hendes 7 Børn' with the widower Torrof Andersøn; her brother Anders Torrofsøn was made her guardian. Her parents are now Confirmed on this link (see their records). ¶11

Betrothed 30 Mar 1781 at Tvedsund, Treungen: 'Ligesaa trolovede ungkarl Thore Tronsen Fælle og Pigen Hæge Toruvsd: Hiemdal. Caut: Jørgen Olsen Hiemdal og Knud Evindsen Hiemdal.' Married in Treungen church on the 2nd Sunday after Trinity (24 Jun 1781). 'De vare beslægtede i andet Leed og derfor Kongl. Allernaadigst Bevilling': they were blood relatives in the second degree, and so married by royal licence (Kviteseid ministerialbok 1773-1786, image 130, p.125). ¶3

Card DA 6856 image 252 (Øvre Telemark skifteprotokoll 17a, 1810-1815, S. 427b): Felle, Treungen: skifte 17 Jan 1815 (second date 6 Apr 1814), 'Skifte forlangt av Thore Tronsen o.h. Hage Torrufsd.'; children Torruf Thoresen 14, Anne Thoresdatter m. Ole Andersen Fælle, Gunnild Thoresdatter 22, Ingeborg Thoresdatter; gross 868 rd 8 s, net 401 rd 1 ort 23½ s; the farm Tvedt had been sold to Ole Andersen Fælle. On the pattern of the other cards in this index the second date is the death date, which would put her death at 6 Apr 1814, but the card does not say who died, so the protocol page (17a S. 427b, in DA 25684 or its companion volume) must be read before a death date is entered. ¶9

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Speculative: she may be a daughter of Toruv Andersen of Hiemdal (b. ~1720), like Gundbjør Toruvsdatter (m. 1775). Her marriage to Tore Thronsen Felle (c. 1780) was not found in a skim of the Kviteseid 1778-1781 marriages. ¶2

Confidence raised to Confirmed (orchestrator, 13 Oct 2026): her own baptism entry of 11 Mar 1759 (Kviteseid ministerialbok 1754-1773, p. 49, image 18, read on the image) names her father as Toruv of Hiemdal, and her 1781 betrothal (same farm, Hiemdal) and 1801 census age fit; Toruv Andersen is the only Toruv at Hiemdal in the registers read. The mother is not named in the baptism. ¶7
