About the family

Parents not found. She is NOT shown to be a daughter of Hermund Hendrichsen of Indre Takle (see his page): his probate of 19 May 1716 (2b fol. 492b-493a) names only Hendrich, Claus, Brite and Haldis as his children, all of age, and lists the shares of brothers Hendrich and Claus and 'Søster Brithe Hermunds datter' with no husband. Else is absent there, so a Hermund of another farm is more likely (inference). ¶4

Father (Probable, bygdebok): Hermund Nilsson. Ættebok for Lavik (1983, nb.no 8d133e3cb711f2ec5c05edb513806d88), Østerbø bruk 1, canvas 295 (gaps are words not recovered): the owner text says '... Mons [..] Grøneng kjøpte dette...' (Mons of Grøneng bought this [part of the farm]); the family table gives 'Mons [..] Grøneng', married 1705, '(1671—1745)'; '1. Elsa Hermundsdtr. ([16?8]—1740) dotter til Hermund Nilssson [..]'; '2. Synneva Andersdtr. (1719—1759)', married 1742 (children Johannes 1742, Ole 1743, Else 1744); among the first marriage's children are Nils 1723-1771 Østerbø, d. Gjertrud, and no. 4 b. 1712 d. 1793 Oppedal (Britte, wife of Torkel Iversen of Indre Oppedal). Dates are ABT (rebuilt text). Elsa's birth year is OCR-damaged ('1628'): it cannot be 1628, and with children to about 1724 it is more likely about 1680-85 than 1668, so no birth year is entered. Her own grade (Confirmed on her link to Britte Monsdatter) is unchanged; the link up to Hermund is graded on his record. ¶5

Life story

Probate No. 5, Ytre Sogn 6a fol. 6a (right page of view 6): 'Anno 1740 d. 5 April... i Ladvigs Skibrede paa Gaarden Østerbøe' the estate was divided 'efter den Sal. afdøde Qvinde Else Hermundsdatter imellem hendis efterlevende Egtemand Mons Torbersen og hendis med ham [sammen] avlede Børn': 1 son Niels, 1[3 or 6] years old; 1st daughter Ingeri, married to Hans Gramme[?]; 2nd daughter Dordej, dead, leaving a son Hans Johansen, [3?] years old, whose guardian was his father Johannes Krogen[?]; 3rd daughter Giertrud, married to Ole Nielsen [farm unread]; '4de Datter Brite gift med Torchel [Iversen] Indre Oppedahl'. Appraisers Peder Olsen [farm] and Mons Pedersen Østerbø; gross estate 560 rd 5 ort 13 s, including the farm Østerbø with the sub-holding Hukkelen. Read from the scan (crops, e1.jpg). Index row 'Else Hermundsdtr, Østrebø, 1740-1743' (pa00000000521006), listed but unread. ¶1

This makes Mons Torbersen's 1745 widow Synneve Andersdatter his second wife, married between Apr 1740 and 1745, and the mother of the minor daughter Else (named after the first wife, inference). Brekke herred bygdebok vol. 2 (1944, nb.no e7f2f67381e0134712fa2d00022d765d, canvas 41) calls Brita Monsdatter of Østerbø (b. 1712) a daughter of 'bnd. Mons [gap] [gap] Hermundsdtr.', which agrees. ¶3

Probable origin: 'Elsse', daughter of Hermund Nielsen and Dorethe Olsdatter of Bruheld (Bruield) in Tjugum sogn, unmarried at home when her mother's estate was divided on 27 May 1701 (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll A 1b, fol. 329a, Digitalarkivet 24154 viewer 113). Siblings: Andfind, Samson, Gietlof, Agatha, Sara, Sigri, and the half-brother Niels Hermundsen. The link rests on name, the Ættebok's 'Hermund Nilssson', her husband's origin in the same sogn, and her children's names Niels and Dordej; it spans two farms, so her parents are Probable. Her own grade (on her link to Britte Monsdatter) is unchanged. ¶6

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Graded on her link to her daughter Britte Monsdatter: the probate names '4de Datter Brite gift med Torchel... Indre Oppedahl' among the children she had with Mons Torbersen ('hendis med ham avlede Børn'). Continuity: Britte was betrothed on 15 Jun 1737 as 'Birthe Monsd: Østerbøe' to Torchil Iversen of Indre Oppedal; this 1740 probate on Østerbø (3 years later) names her as Else's daughter married to Torkel of Indre Oppedal; Mons Torbersen's own probate of 10 May 1745 on Østerbø repeats 'Brite gift med Torchel Iversen Indre Oppedal' with the same siblings (Niels, Ingeri, Giertrud, Dorte with son Hans Johansen). ¶2a
