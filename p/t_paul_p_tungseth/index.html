About the family

Eldest child of Peder Olaus Tungsæt and Hendrikke Karoline Gurine Sætnes, and Olga's eldest brother. He appears as 'Paul Tungsæt', born 1881 in Kristiansund, in both the 1885 census at Toldbodbakken and the 1891 census at 47 Toldbodsmujet in Kristiansund. ¶1

Life story

He is buried as 'Paul P Tungseth', 1881-1934, in Zion Lutheran Cemetery in Skree Township, Clay County, Minnesota, the same rural cemetery as his parents. Find a Grave records his marriage in 1906 to Leva Ophaug, 1878-1942, and links eight children to them: Caroline P., 1907-1991; Ivy Marie (Gilfoy), 1908-1980; Paul Egil, 1910-1994; Peer Balder, 1913-1976; Thelma Jeanette (Golden), 1914-1997; Leva Theresa (Ellefson), 1917-1984; Daphne Grace (Johnson), 1919-1980; and Gudrun Diana (Wyatt), 1921-2006. ¶3

He emigrated with his mother and the other seven children, leaving Kristiansund on 2 May 1894 by the Dominion Line for Minnesota, aged thirteen. The emigrant clerk calculated his birth year as 1882 from a stated age; the two Norwegian censuses both give 1881. ¶2

1895 Minnesota census: Paul Tungseth, 14, born in Norway, in his father Peter's household in Skree Township, Clay County (p. 6, read from the image). ¶6

1900 census: Paul Tungseth, son, born February 1881 in Norway, 19, single, immigrated 1894, at school, reads, writes and speaks English, in his mother Caroline's household in Skree Township (ED 316, sheet 185A, line 2, read from the image). This gives his birth month, February 1881. ¶7

1905 Minnesota census: not in his parents' household in Skree Township; not searched for elsewhere. ¶8

1910 census: probably the Paul P Tungseth, head, born 1881 in Norway, farming at Stone View, Divide County, North Dakota, with his wife Liva (born 1879, Norway) and children Caroline (born 1907, North Dakota), Ivy (1908, North Dakota) and Egil (1910, Minnesota). Index only, image not read. The identification rests on his birth year and country, the middle initial P (Pedersen), a wife Liva who matches his wife Leva Ophaug (1878-1942) married in 1906, and a first daughter named Caroline after his mother. Probable; reading the image (NARA T624, Divide County, ND) would settle it. ¶9

His exact birth and death dates and his obituary have not been found; the Hawley and Moorhead newspapers for 1934 would be the next source. ¶5

Corrections 1

Correction to a published record: Find a Grave's memorials for his parents do not list him as their son, although his own memorial is in their cemetery and the Norwegian censuses and the 1894 emigrant list put him in the family. A Marie Caroline Tungseth who lived only in 1906 is buried in the same cemetery and is probably his first child, dying in the year of his marriage. ¶4
