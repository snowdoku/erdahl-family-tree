About the family

NEW ANCESTOR, graded on his link to Todne Larsdatter (see her page). He is the father of the sisters Todne Larsdatter (wife of Evind Bjørnsen of Ekra under Søndre Askland) and Siirj Larsdatter (widow of Stian Madsøn of Søndre Askland, 1708). The Skifteprotokoll nr. 3 fol. 46b entry of 16 Mar 1708 calls Evind Bjørnsen's wife 'Enkens Søster' (the widow's sister). The father's first name comes only from the patronymic the two sisters share. They could in principle be half-sisters through their mother, so this stays Probable. Nothing is known of his farm. ¶1a

Life story

Skp. 3 fol. 46a re-read, 'Faste jordegods' section of the 16 Mar 1708 skifte after Stian Madsøn (DA 24351 view 47; view = folio + 1 for a-pages): 'Udi Gaarden Sønder Askeland, som Enken... efter hendis [fader?]... [arvet?]... ½ kalvskind(?); men ingen breve hafde derpaa at anviisning'. The land at Søndre Askland is described as the widow Siirj Larsdatter's own inheritance with no deeds shown. The key word (fader?) is not certain on the scan, so this is not proof, but if right it puts her father on Søndre Askland, which fits the 1664 user 'Laurids 40' there (b. about 1624). 1666 vicar's census, Søndre Askland 'med Ekra' (DA 35510 view 33, p. 44, no. 7): users Torkell 67, Lauritz 42, Knud 24, Salve 32 and a fifth user aged 26 (name not read); daughters are not listed. Grade unchanged. ¶3

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

2 open questions

Open questions and the next record to read 2

Next: Søndre Askland before Stian Madsøn (the 1647/1657/1664 lists for Gjøvdal), to see whether a Lars held the farm Siirj and Stian held in 1708. ¶1b

Lead: the 1664 manntall lists 'Laurids 40 aar' (Lars, born about 1624) as one of three users of Askeland søndre, Gjøvdal (Råbyggelaget fogderi, s. 80, DA 35567 view 58, read on the scan). His daughters would include Siirj Larsdatter, who held part of Søndre Askland with Stian Madsøn in 1708, and Todne Larsdatter (born about 1666), wife of Evind Bjørnsen of Ekra under Søndre Askland. Name, farm and age fit, but no record names him as their father, so this stays a lead and his grade is unchanged. ¶2
