Life story

Bygdebok: father of 'Sigrid Rognaldsdtr.' (1670-1740), the 1697 wife of Gullak Johannessen of Asheim ('dt.a. bnd. Rognald Ivarson' and 'Ingeborg Bendiksdtr.', see Asheim bnr. 49; canvas 247), and of 'Lasse Rognaldson' (1673-1715), brukar on Asheim bnr. 30 1699-1715 ('Rognald Ivarson og Ingeborg Bendiksdtr.... 49', canvas 295). ¶1

The estate of the son named as the test record has now been read. Ytre Sogn 2b fol. 459b (view 145), 'Anno 1714 den 15 Maj' the court met 'paa Asem at skifte efter Lasse Rogniels: imellem hans efterlevende Quinde Karen Mons datter paa den ene og deris tilsammen avlede Børn Peder, Mathias, Joen, Niels og Brette Lasses Sønner og datter paa den anden Side; paa Børnenes Side Fader broderen Olle Mickland[?]'; estate 76 rd. It gives Lasse a wife Karen Monsdatter and five children, and a brother Ole of 'Mickland' (farm reading uncertain) as guardian; it does not name Lasse's parents or a sister Sigri, so it neither confirms nor contradicts the bygdebok. It shows that this Lasse (married with five children by 1714) is the older Lasse Rognaldsen (28 in 1701; bygdebok 1673-1715), not Rognald Andersen's son Lasse (8 in 1701). Grade unchanged (Probable). ¶4a

How sure are we?

Graded on his link to Sigri Rognaldsdatter (see her page). ¶2a

Graded Probable: a bygdebok is a compiled secondary source and the passage was rebuilt from word boxes with gaps. It agrees with the lead that the soldier Lasse Rognaldsen of Asheim (1701 aged 28; estate 1714/15) was her brother, and it makes her father an older Rognald, as argued, not Rognald Andersen (45 in 1701). His own entry (Asheim bnr. 49) was not rebuilt. ¶2b

Audit 7 (1-2 Nov 2026): grade unchanged (Probable, compiled source only). Under the audit-6 rule a bygdebok-only ancestor must name the primary record that would test the link; this note supplies it. ¶3a
