About the family

Father of Alf Olsen of Nystuen (see his page), graded on his link to Alf: Probable (compiled source only). Lesja bygdebok vol. 1, canvas 141, Sør-Kvam entry 4-4: Brit (ca. 1682-1761) 'g.m. ein Ola Ols.', who took over Sør-Kvam about 1710; children Ola (ca. 1700, took over), '2. Alf, sjå Nystugu (3-3)', Ragnhild and Tore (1729). Alf's patronymic 'Ols.' in the Nystugu entry (canvas 45: 'Alf Ols. truleg frå [___] (4-4)') agrees; Alf named his sons Ola (1740 and 1756), as for this man. The bygdebok hedges the link ('truleg'). His birth and death years fall in gaps of the rebuild. ¶1a

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
