Confidence: the grade and how it changed 3

Probable mother of Maria Dorothea Stierlin (see her page). ¶1a

Graded on that link: inferred, not named. She married Joh Caspar Stierlein at Bietigheim in 1718 (bride row 907270350 'Elisabetha Margaretha Majer', relatives 'Tobias, Joh Caspar'; image 1184899-00384) and is his wife at the baptism of Maria Justina in 1728 (7269723). Maria Dorothea's baptism is not indexed, and she married in 1738, so she may have been born before 1718 to an earlier wife. Ancestry 61023 index rows do not label roles (BRIEF audit 5 rule 3), so this rests at Probable until the image or a labelled FamilySearch twin is read; images not seen. Surname Majer = Maier/Mayer. ¶1b

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed, graded on her link to her daughter Maria Dorothea Stierlin (see her page). The link is no longer inferred: FamilySearch, 'Germany, Births and Baptisms, 1558-1898', VH35-TSN: Maria Dorothea Stierlein, christened Bietigheim 26 Feb 1720, Father's Name 'Johann Caspar Stierlein', Mother's Name 'Elisabetha Margaretha' (record also carries a death date 6 Aug 1803). Continuity (18 years, audit 7 rule 1): the parents stay at Bietigheim in the gap (Maria Justina 1728 and Philipp Jacob, both to Johann Caspar x Elisabetha Margaretha, V4LM-LDX, V4LM-K1W); the far record, VCYQ-KKB (Bietigheim 1738), names the bride's parent 'Johann Caspar Stierlen'; no other Maria Dorothea daughter of Johann Caspar is named. Maiden name: the independent extraction FamilySearch, 'Germany, Marriages, 1558-1929' JH63-T9D reads the 1718 bride 'Elisabetha Margretha Herbort' (Bietigheim, 21 Aug 1718), supporting reading that she was born Haubert and that 'Majer' was her first husband's name. The Lutheran index QPGG-16KL labels 'Tobias Majer' as Father-in-law: that is the indexer's reading of the same ambiguous entry, and it is the point corrected (Tobias = first husband, buried 2 Jun 1718). The relationship to Maria Dorothea does not depend on it. Read signed in, 2 Nov 2026; labelled index fields, image not seen (partner-only). ¶3

Corrections 1

Correction: 'Tobias' in her 1718 bride row (907270350, 'Elisabetha Margaretha Majer', relatives 'Tobias, Joh Caspar') is very probably her late FIRST HUSBAND, not her father. Tobias Mayer, son of Martin (baptised Bietigheim 12 Mar 1692 to 'Martin, Anna Catharina', record 7267975), married at Bietigheim on 29 Oct 1715 (groom row 7270306, 'Tobias Mayer', relatives 'Martin, Elizabetha Margarethe'; image 1184899-00382), and the bride row of that marriage is 'Elizabetha Margarethe Hauberten', relatives 'Johann Christoph, Tobias' (her father, then the groom; record 907270306). Tobias Mayer was buried at Bietigheim on 2 Jun 1718 (record 7276045, image 1184899-00508), and in the same year 'Elisabetha Margaretha Majer' (carrying her late husband's surname, as widows did) married Joh Caspar Stierlin (image 1184899-00384, two images after the 1715 entry). So she was born Haubert (index 'Hauberten', genitive), daughter of Johann Christoph, and Majer was her married name. Rival reading: a Tobias Mayer/Mauer born at Bietigheim in 1653 or 1655 (7266391, 7266486) could in theory have had a daughter Elisabetha Margaretha, but no such baptism is indexed and the dates fit the widow reading closely. Her father link is moved from Tobias Majer to Johann Christoph Haubert (Speculative), and her surname changed. Her own grade (on Maria Dorothea Stierlin) is unchanged. ¶2a
