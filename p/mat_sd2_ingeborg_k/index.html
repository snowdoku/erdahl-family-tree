About the family

FIRST wife of Jorn (Jorgen) Olsen Slind, married 1771, and therefore NOT an ancestor of David Erdahl - but she is the reason the 1801 census of Slinden cannot be read at face value, and she accounts for the two eldest children of that household. Selbu i fortid og naatid vol. 7, p. 116: '1775-1814 Jorn Olsen, f. 1752, d. 1832. G.I 1771 m. INGEBORG [Kjosnes] d.y., f. 1750, d. 1786, SOESTER TIL INGEBORG KJOESNES' - the younger Ingeborg, born 1750, died 1786, SISTER of [another] Ingeborg Kjosnes. Two sisters both called Ingeborg, distinguished as d.e. and d.y. (den eldre, den yngre - the elder, the younger), is a real Selbu practice and not a misprint. ¶1

Her children by Jorn Olsen, from the same page: Marit f. 1773 (d. 1774), Marit d.e. f. 1775, Marit f. 1777 (d. 1778), Marit d.y. f. 1780, and Ola f. 1783. FOUR daughters called Marit in seven years, three of whom survived infancy only briefly - the name was being re-given after each death, which is what Norwegian parents did. ¶2

Life story

Burial lead (cross-line sweep pass 2), Probable identification, no field changed:, reading every Selbu burial of 2 Advent 1785 to 4 p. Trin. 1786 (16311 canvases 120-122) for her namesake Ingeborg Olsdatter Sandvik (see her page), noted and set aside 'Ingeborg Olsd. Slinden', died 19 May 1786 aged 36 (canvas 122 left, Ascension Day), as 'probably a namesake of another Slinden household'. She fits this Ingeborg, the first wife of Jorn Olsen of Slinden: the bygdebok (Selbu i fortid og naatid vol. 7 p. 116) gives her 'f. 1750, d. 1786', and age 36 in May 1786 gives a birth in 1749-50; the farm is her husband's; the year is her death year; and Jorn remarried in April 1787, eleven months later. If so, her patronymic is Olsdatter. It is not the Sandvik Ingeborg (see her page), who was buried from Sandvik at Palm Sunday 1786 aged 37 with her 12-day-old child. ¶4a

She is entered here because the 1801 census lists all four of Jorn Olsen's surviving children as 'Deres boern', their children, although two of them - Marit b. 1780 and Ola b. 1783 - were hers and not Ane Gundersdatter's. Recording her is what keeps the next researcher from giving Ane Gundersdatter children she never had. ¶3

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
