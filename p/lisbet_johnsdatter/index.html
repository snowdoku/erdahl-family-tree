About the family

Parents speculative. Two baptisms fit: (A) Lisbet, baptized 20 Sep 1778, daughter of Joen of Kleven. Joen Joensen of Storkleven (69 in 1801, married three times) and Lisbet Davidsdatter had children David, Mali and Joen. Mali Joensd. and Beret Joensd. of Storkleven married in 1806 and 1808, and David Joensen was at Storkleven in 1809. Lisbet's own daughters Mali (1810) and Lisbet (1813) also fit, so option A is preferred. (B) Lisbet, baptized 10 Jan 1779, daughter of Joen of Hegre. A Lisbet Joensdatter, 22, lived at Hegre in 1801 with her twice-widowed mother Anne Olsdatter and siblings Joen, Giertru, Sigri, Ane and Sigri. Her daughter Sigri (1821) would fit option B. A probate or 1815 record could settle it. ¶2

Parentage decided for option A (baptized 20 Sep 1778 at Værnes, daughter of Joen of Kleven). Four independent points agree. (1) At her son Svend's baptism at Hegre on 9 Oct 1808 she is 'Pige Lisbet Joensdatter Storkleven', and the godparents were 'Haagen og Joen Kleven, Giertrud Schielstad, Lars og Marit Rolsenggierdet' - two men of the Kleven farm stand for her side, the Rolseng-gjerdet pair for Lars Eriksen's (read from the scan; the index omits godparents). (2) She married from Storkleven on 14 Nov 1808. (3) Stjørdalsboka V 1 (Hegra herad, 1953), Kleven section, lists among the children of John Johnsen of Kleven (b. 1732) 'Lisbet, 1778, [m.] Lars Eriksen Almlitrøen' (page rebuilt by the nb.no word-box method). (4) Naming: her children Svend (1808), Mali (1810) and Lisbet (1813) repeat Joen Joensen's sons Svend (1761, 1766), his daughter Mali (1782) and his wife Lisbet Davidsdatter. ¶5

Mother still open between the bygdebok and the 1801 census: the bygdebok lists Lisbet (1778) among the children born after John Johnsen's 1765 marriage to Lisbet Davidsdatter, but the 1801 census of Storkleven shows two unmarried daughters both called Beret (26 and 23) labelled 'Mandens børn' (the husband's children) and says Joen was in his third marriage. The 23-year-old 'Beret Joensdatter' (b. about 1778) is probably Lisbet herself under a wrong name; the census scan of household 01 would settle the name, and Joen Joensen's probate would settle the mother. ¶7

Life story

The stated birth year 1779 in that burial entry very slightly favours the second of the two candidate baptisms (Lisbet, baptized 10 Jan 1779, daughter of Joen of Hegre) over the first (20 Sep 1778, daughter of Joen of Kleven). Against that, she was living at Storkleven in 1808 at both her son Svend's baptism and her own marriage, which points back to the Kleven family. Burial ages in this register are routinely a year out, so the residence evidence is the stronger of the two. The question is still open. ¶4

Rejected: option B (Lisbet, baptized 10 Jan 1779 at Hegre, daughter of Joen of Hegre, godparents Joer Trøsje, Peder..gres, Gudrun Hegre, Gertrud Hoed). That Lisbet was living at Hegre in 1801, aged 22, with her twice-widowed mother Anne Olsdatter; none of her godparents or family names (Joen, Giertru, Sigri, Ane) tie her to Storkleven, and only Sigri (1821) could echo that family - but Joen Joensen of Kleven also had a daughter Sigrid (1768). The 1843 burial's birth year 1779 (age 64) is the usual one-year drift in this register's burial ages and does not outweigh the residence and godparent evidence. ¶6

Buried 13 Aug 1843 as a cottager's widow of Almlietrøe, born 1779. She lived at Storkleven in 1808 (son Svend's baptism and her marriage). ¶1

Her burial entry, 13 Aug 1843, describes her as 'husmandsenke' - a crofter's widow - of Almlietrøe, born 1779. She had been a widow thirteen years. ¶3

Burial (per index, Stjørdal p. 518): buried 13 Aug 1843, died 6 Aug 1843, 'Lisbeth Johnsdatter', husmandsenke (cottager's widow), of Almlietrøe, aged 64 (index birth year 1779; her baptism was 20 Sep 1778). ¶8

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: Stjør- og Verdal skifteprotokoll 19, s. 247b-248 (Digitalarkivet 57508 view 263): skifte at Stjørdalshalsen in early December 1810 (day read as the 3rd, uncertain) in the 'opgivende Boe' of Lisbet Davidsdatter of Kleven - a widow handing her estate over to her heirs in her lifetime. The heirs are listed as the children she had 'med førhen afdøde Mand John Johnsen Kleven' (with her late husband John Johnsen Kleven): 1. David Johnsen Kleven, of age, living on Kleven; 2. Joen Johnsen, of age; 3. Sigrie Joensdatter, dead, who left seven children by her husband Hans Olsen Forsen (Mallie 16, Sigrie 14, Ole 13, Joen 11, Lisbet 9, Svend 7, Ole 1½); 4. Giertrud Joensdatter, married to Joen Hafstad in Verdalen; 5. Berethe Joensdatter, married to Ole Pedersen Moe[?]; 6. Lisbeth Joensdatter, married to the cottager (huusmand) Lars Erichsen Kleven; 7. Mallie Joensdatter, married to Mads Andersen Forbregd[?]. Heir no. 6, 'Datteren Lisbeth Joensdatter, gift med Huusmand Lars Erichsen', is this woman (she married Lars Eriksen in 1808). The record names both parents and ties her to her husband, so her parentage moves from Probable to Confirmed and the open question of her mother is settled: she was Lisbet Davidsdatter's own daughter, not a child of Joen's earlier marriages. ¶9
