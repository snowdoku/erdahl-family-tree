Life story

Known only from the patronymic of his son Niels Aadnesen Omland (married 1784, died 1789). Aadne was probably at Omland (Ohland) in Gjøvdal; an Ole Aadnesen was also there in 1784. Not otherwise researched. ¶1

THREE SONS NOW DOCUMENTED, not one. The 1789 probate of his son Niels Aadnesen at Oland in Gjøvdal (Østre Råbyggelag skifteprotokoll no. 16a, folio 257) names the deceased's brothers as "Gulloug Aadnesen Walleberg i Fyresdal" and "Angrim Aadnesen Engenes i Åmli". So his sons were Niels Aadnesen of Oland (died 1789), Gullov Aadnesen who settled at Valleberg in Fyresdal in Telemark, and Angrim Aadnesen of Engenes in Åmli. This replaces the previous position that he was "known only from the patronymic" of a single son. ¶2

An Angrim Aadnesen of Engenes in Åmli appears independently in the Oland probate records as the second husband of Anne Troensdatter, widow of Even Olsen of Oland, in a settlement of 1777 — the same man. ¶3

His own records have still not been found. No estate under his name appears among the 74 Østre Råbyggelag probate cards for Oland in Gjøvdal, which were read in full. He was most likely at Oland in the 1740s-1770s. The next sources are the Åmli church book 1741-1775 read page by page for Gjøvdal entries, and the 1762 ekstraskatt (extra tax) roll for Råbyggelag, which lists every adult by farm. ¶4

Marriage FOUND: betrothed Fer. 2 Pasch. (2 Apr) 1736 in Gjøvdal ('Aadne Larsön Asceland og Asbor Evindsdatter Asceland'), married Dom. 5 p. Trin. (24 Jun) 1736 in Gjøvdal ('Cop: … item Aadne Larsön og Asbor Evindsdatter Asceland') (Åmli 1711-1740 pp. 134-137, canvases 70-71). Children: Evind, bapt. 5 Oct 1737 Gjøvdal ('Aadne Larsön Ascelands barn Evind', canvas 74; named for Asbor's father); ANGRIM, bapt. 17 Feb 1740 at Åmli church ('Aadne Larsön Gangseys barn Angrim'; godparents Niels Jensen Gangsei, Ole Eielsen Gangsei, Lars Gangsei's wife, Niels Gangsei's wife and Marthe Larsdatter; 1740 no. 10, canvas 81) — the Angrim Aadnesen Engenes of the 1789 probate. The family moved from Asceland (Askeland/Åsland, Gjøvdal) to Gangsei between Oct 1737 and Feb 1740. Lars Gangsei's wife and Marthe Larsdatter as godmothers suggest Aadne's mother and sister. Aadne's own baptism (son of a Lars, c. 1705-15) not yet searched (canvases 1-37). ¶9

Supporting evidence, graded on his link to Niels Aadnesen (see his page). In the 1789 Oeland probate of Niels Aadnesen (Skifteprotokoll nr. 16b fol. 257a, read on the scan), Angrim Aadnesen Engenes and Gulleug Aadnesen Wolsberg (Morgedal) are both named 'Farbroder' (father's brother) to Niels's children. Angrim is one of Aadne Larsen's sons on the Gangsei card 3469. This repeats the sibling link; the grade is unchanged. ¶13

Third son found (round 3): Ole, baptised Dom. Jubilate (13 May) 1753 at Åmli church: 'Aadne Larsön og Asbor Evindsdatter Gangseys barn Ole'; godparents Lars Gangsei, Halvor Olstad, Dalle[?] Olstad's wife, Gulou Dalsaas[?]'s wife and Ole Gangsei's wife (1753 no. 38, canvas 40). Angrim's baptism was NOT found in Mar 1745 - Nov 1756 (all of the 9921 chronological list from that date); he may be born 1741-44 or after 1756. ¶8

Estate found: skifte at Hommedrum in Gjøvdal, 10 Nov 1762, 'efter afdøde Aane Larsen' (Skifteprotokoll nr. 13a fol. 11b, DA 24362 view 26, read on the scan; Homdrum cards 5147/5149). Heirs: the widow Asbor Evendsdatter and their children Even 24, Angrim 21, Niels 17, Gullov 15, Ole 10 and Guroe 19. This is a direct record of him as Niels's father. The card gives no parents of his ('avd.s far' is blank). His wife was 'gift med Aadne Larsøn Søndre Ascheland' in 1736 (Skp. 9 fol. 134b), so he lived at Søndre Askland at his marriage. Lead for his father: a Lars at Søndre Askland, such as Lars Stiansen of Ekra under Søndre Askland (son of Stian Madsøn and Siirj Larsdatter, married 1714), but no Aadne was baptised to him in 1714-1724, so this is unproven. ¶14

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed (upgrade sweep D), as father of Niels Aadnesen Ohland. The link rests on records that name each relationship, not on age or farm elimination. (1) The probate after Niels Aadnesen at Oland, 1789 (Østre Råbyggelag skifteprotokoll 16a, fol. 257, card 10107), names the deceased's brothers 'Gulloug Aadnesen Walleberg i Fyresdal' and 'Angrim Aadnesen Engenes i Åmli'. (2) The Åmli church book names Aadne Larsen of Gangsei as father of exactly that set of sons: Angrim, bapt. 17 Feb 1740 ('Aadne Larsön Gangseys barn Angrim', 1740 no. 10, canvas 81 of source 9920); Niels, bapt. 2 Feb 1746 ('Aadne Larsön Gangseys barn Niels', 1746 no. 10, canvas 15 of source 9921); Gullov, bapt. 16 Feb 1749 ('Aadne Larsön og Asbor Evindsdatter Gangseys barn Gulöu', 1749 no. 13, canvas 26). The rare names Angrim and Gullov occur together as sons of one father only here, and the probate names them as Niels's full brothers, so the 1746 Niels is the Niels of Oland. The 1789 burial age (read as 46, uncertain) fits a 1746 birth. ¶12

The case for this identification 1

IDENTIFIED (Probable, 13 Oct 2026): Aadne Larsen of Gangsei, Gjøvdal, husband of Asbor Evindsdatter. He had sons baptised at Åmli church: Niels, Purification Sunday (2 Feb) 1746 ('Aadne Larsön Gangseys barn Niels'; godparents Rolf[?] Baas[?], Lars Gangsei, Ole Østad's wife, Niels Gangsei's wife, Ambor Evindsdatter Gangsei; 1746 no. 10, canvas 15) and Gullov, Sexagesima (16 Feb) 1749 ('Aadne Larsön og Asbor Evindsdatter Gangseys barn Gulöu'; 1749 no. 13, canvas 26). These are exactly the brothers Niels and Gullov Aadnesen named in the 1789 Oland probate. 'Aadne Gangsei' was a cautioner at Gangsei in 1747 and his wife a godmother in 1748. Angrim's baptism was not found in Mar 1745 - Jul 1750. ¶6

Corrections 1

Correction (re-read, 13 Oct 2026): the marriage of Aadne Larsen and Asbor Evindsdatter, 'Dom. 5 p. Trinit.' 1736 in Gjøvdal (Åmli 1711-1740 pp. 136-137, canvas 71), fell on 1 Jul 1736, not 24 Jun: Easter 1736 was 1 Apr, so Trinity Sunday was 27 May and the 5th Sunday after Trinity was 1 Jul. The entry follows ones dated Fest. Joh. Bapt. (24 Jun), 28 Jun and 29 Jun. Crop:. ¶11

Alternatives considered and set aside 2

Candidate (Possible): Aadne Børusen of Jørundland (Jörenland), Gjøvdal, had a son Børu baptised 17 Nov 1743 (Åmli 1741-1775, 1743 no. 60; godparent his brother Ole Børusen Jørundland, also Vraald Omland and Aslak Omland's wife). No baptism of a Niels, Gullov or Angrim was found in Gjøvdal entries from mid-1741 to Dec 1744; a second Aadne family (Vraald and Peder Aadnesen of Mjåland) also existed, so identification is open. ¶5

The earlier candidate Aadne Børusen of Jørundland is RULED OUT as Niels's father: his children were Børu (1743) and Ool/Ole (10 Mar 1746), and no Niels or Gullov. Consequently the speculated first-cousin marriage of Niels Aadnesen and Tøri Olsdatter (via the Børusen family) is not supported. ¶7

Searches that found nothing 1

No baptism of an Aadne son of a Lars appears in Åmli Jan 1711 - Feb 1724 (canvases 2-35, round 6). Lars fathers seen in Gjøvdal are Lars Olsen Hommedrum (son Halvor 1715), Lars Stiansen Ekre (m. 1714 Kristi Olsdatter; daughters Torbor 1715 and Birgitte 1721/1723) and Lars Mjåland (daughter Anne 1719). Svend Larsen Hommedom and Torbor Larsdatter Ekre were betrothed on the same day as Aadne in 1736, which hints at one of these families. Aadne was probably born before 1711 or in Feb-Oct 1724 (canvases 36-37 unread). ¶10
