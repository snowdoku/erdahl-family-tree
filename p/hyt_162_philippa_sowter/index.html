Life story

Sowter context at All Saints, Derby (free sources; supplements baptism of Philippa daughter of John Sowter, 10 Jul 1568). The All Saints register, as printed in The Reliquary 5 (1865), 'Traders' Tokens of Derbyshire' (George Sowter's token of 1667), records that in October 1592 'The Plague begun in Darby in the house of William Souter bootcher in the p'she of All: Sts', and the burials 'ex peste' in December 1592 of John son of William Sowter, 'Alicia uxor Will'i Sowter', Edward and Mary children of William Sowter, and 'Wili'mus Sowter' himself. That household was in All Saints parish while Philippa and William Ouldham were marrying (1588) and baptising (John 1592). This William may have been a brother or kinsman of Philippa (William Sowter's John buried 1592; Philippa's father was John); nothing states it, and he is not entered. The Lichfield consistory calendar, Series IV (Phillimore, Index Library, page image n384 read), gives NO place names for these years, so its Sowter entries cannot be tied to Derby from the print: 'Sowter, John, ad.' 1571/2 (fol. 150) and 1573/4 (fol. 164), 'Sowter, John' will 1574 (fol. 32) and 1583 (fol. 188), 'Sowter, William' 1576 (fol. 76), 'Sowter, William, ad.' c.1593-94 (perhaps the plague victim), 'Sowter, Thomas' 1569/70, 1574, 1580, 1593 (ad.), 1602/3. Test record for her father John: the Lichfield Record Office originals B/C/11 for John Sowter 1571-1583; a will naming 'my daughter Philippa' would confirm identification. ¶8

Baptism (image read): All Saints, Derby, 'July 1568... Bap. Philippa filia Johis Sowter bap. x° die', i.e. 10 July 1568 (DGS image 19 of 772, ark 3:1:S3HY-DTM3-VSM; index 1:1:KBD6-8Q4). She is the only Philippa Sowter in the FamilySearch England baptism indexes 1550-1580, and she married William Ouldham at the same church in November 1588, aged 20. Her father John Sowter (see his page) is entered as Probable (twenty-year gap to the marriage). This settles the 'her own parents' lead above: she was John's daughter, not the daughter of Thomas Sowter and Alice Turnar. Her own grade (as Lucretia's mother) is unchanged. ¶9

Derby film read (2 Nov 2026), graded on her link to Lucretia Oldham; grade unchanged. William's baptisms of John (July 1592, image 51 of 272) and Elizabeth (Feb 1594/5, image 60) name the father only, as does the burial of 'William Ouldam' on 26 Jun 1636 (image 117), so none names Philippa. Correction to the Reliquary extract: on the image (53 of 272, ark 3:1:S3HY-DTM3-J6N) the plague memorandum follows 'Ricus filius wilmi Sowter sep. 31 die ex peste' (31 Oct 1592) and reads 'The plauge begun in Darby in the house of william Sowter bocher in the p[ar]ishe of All S[ain]ts in Darby, Robert Woode ironmonger & Robert Brookhouse tanner beinge then bayliffs, and so continued in the towne the space of 12 monethes as by the register may appeare'. Under November 1592 follow 'Alicia vxor wilmi Sowter sep. ex peste 25 die', 'Edwardus filius wilmi Sowter sep. ex peste 29 die' and 'maria filia wilmi Sowter sep. ex peste eodem die'. So the son buried on 31 October was Richard, not John, and the wife and two children were buried in November, not December. No entry calls William Sowter kin of Philippa. ¶13

Married William Ouldham at All Saints, Derby, on 17 Nov 1588 ('Married 17th November 1588, William Ouldham and Philippa Sowter', published All Saints register, quoted by J. G. Hunt, NEHGR 111 (1957): 242). Hunt prints next to it the All Saints marriage of Thomas Sowter and Alice Turnar, 19 May 1560, apparently as her possible parents; that is not entered. ¶1

Death: buried at All Saints, Derby, on 22 March 1616/17 as 'the wife of William Oldham' (image read). If her birth was about 1568 (the FamilySearch Family Tree has 1568-1616, unsourced), she was about 49. Her husband outlived her; a William Ouldam was buried at Derby on 26 June 1636. ¶7

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

3 on the grade · 1 correction · 1 alternative set aside · 2 searches with no result · 4 open questions

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 3

Speculative as mother of Lucretia Ouldham (bapt. All Saints 14 Jan 1600/1): the baptism names only the father, William. Philippa is his only recorded wife, and a compiled sketch of Lucretia calls her 'daughter of William and Phillippa (Sowter) Ouldham' (Colonial American Doctresses, p. 21), but the baptism is twelve years after the marriage and no burial of Philippa or second marriage of William has been searched, so a later wife cannot be ruled out. TAG 42 (1966): 212-215 discusses the Sowter family with Philippa, William and Lucretia Ouldham (index only seen); reading it may settle her. ¶2

Upgrade: Speculative to Probable, graded on her link to her daughter Lucretia (Ouldham) Brewster. Three compiled works independently state the relationship: Colonial American Doctresses, p. 21 ('daughter of William and Phillippa (Sowter) Ouldham of Derby, England'); The Ancestry of Fannie May Johnson ('dau. of William and Phillipa (SOWTER)'); and The American Genealogist 42 (1966): 212-215, whose index puts 'Philippe' Ouldham on p. 215 beside Lucretia, in the article on the Sowter family. The primary evidence is still indirect: the All Saints, Derby, baptism of 14 Jan 1600/1 names only the father, William Ouldham, and Philippa is his only recorded wife (m. All Saints 17 Nov 1588). No burial of Philippa or second marriage of William before 1601 is known, but the All Saints register for 1588-1601 has not been searched for one. The relationship is inferred, so the grade stays Probable, not Confirmed. ¶3

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed, graded on her link to her daughter Lucretia Oldham. (FamilySearch signed in, 2 Nov 2026) The burial named as the blocker in the notes above has been found and read on the image. The All Saints, Derby, register has, under March at the end of the year 1616, 'Buried Phillip[a] the wife of William Oldham' on the 22nd, i.e. 22 March 1616/17 (FamilySearch DGS image 99 of 772, image ark 3:1:S3HY-DTM3-KZ8; index 1:1:KBD6-YSL, which labels William Oldham as her husband). The page is signed by the churchwardens, and the next page opens the 1617 entries, including 'Phillippa daughter of Thomas Ouldham' baptised 10 Aug 1617. So Philippa was William's wife from their marriage at All Saints on 17 Nov 1588 (index 1:1:KBD6-XWY) until her burial in March 1617. The whole window for Lucretia's birth (she was baptised at All Saints on 14 Jan 1600/1 as 'Lucretia filia Willimi Ouldam' on image 75) lies inside that marriage, as do the baptisms of all of William's recorded children (John 1592, Elizabeth 1594/5, James 1597, Lucretia 1600/1, Grace 1604). Continuity: William Ouldam/Oldham is the only man of that name in the All Saints indexes 1580-1640, apart from his own burial in 1636. The indexes show no other marriage for him in 1588-1617, and the only other Ouldham householder at All Saints is Thomas, married 1615. No rival wife exists. The baptism names only the father, so her motherhood rests on her being William's wife throughout that window. The burial closes the gap that was left open before ('a later wife cannot be ruled out'), and the earlier 'Speculative as mother' note is superseded. ¶6

Corrections 1

Correction: Audit 11 (2 Nov 2026): lowered from Confirmed to Probable, graded on her link to her daughter Lucretia Oldham. Two separate reasons. (1) No record names her as Lucretia's mother. The baptism names only the father. The 1588 marriage and the burial of 22 Mar 1616/17 ('Phillip ye wife of William Oldham', crop checked by the auditor) show that William had a wife named Philippa at both ends of the birth window, with no other marriage indexed between. That makes her the mother by inference (BRIEF: 'Probable: the relationship itself is inferred'; compare audit 8 rule 2: a wife listed beside the children is not named as their mother). Also, the gap 1588-1617 was searched only in the index (marriages), not on the images. (2) Her link to the tree's Lucretia, the 1624 Plymouth bride, carries the same cross-place identification that lowers William Ouldham's link (see the audit 11 note on William Ouldham). ¶11a

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Her own parents (lead only): Hunt (NEHGR 111: 242) printed beside her marriage the All Saints marriage of Thomas Sowter and Alice Turnar, 19 May 1560, without saying they were her parents. The TAG 42 index lists the Sowters in that article as Agnes, John, Nathaniel and Philippa, but not Thomas or Alice, so the article may make her the daughter of a John Sowter. Neither couple is entered. The Lichfield consistory calendars (1516-1652) list Sowter wills or administrations of Thomas (1569/70, 1574, 1580, 1593, 1602), John (1570/1 admin. twice, 1573/4, 1583, and John of Kedleston 1630), Agnes (1571/3) and others, but the OCR loses the places, so none can be tied to Derby without the calendar page images. ¶4a

Searches that found nothing 2

FamilySearch indexes, 2 Nov 2026: the All Saints, Derby, marriage of 'Wilms Ouldam' and Philippa Sowter, 17 Nov 1588, is indexed (1:1:KBD6-XWY). William's children were baptised there 1592-1604 (John, Elizabeth, James, Lucretia, Grace), and the indexes show no burial of a Philippa Ouldham/Oldham and no second marriage of a William Ouldham at Derby 1588-1610. The baptisms name only the father, and the burial register was searched only through the index, so she stays Probable as Lucretia's mother. ¶5a

Bridge search (2 Nov 2026), graded on her link to Lucretia Oldham: the records of John Oldham's estate in Massachusetts Bay and Connecticut (1636-1641), the Winthrop Papers and the Lichfield consistory calendar 1624-1652 name no heir or English kin of the Oldhams (details on William Ouldham). No record names Philippa as Lucretia's mother, and the cross-place step from the Derby baptism to the Plymouth bride is still unbridged. She stays Probable. Test records as on her husband's record. ¶12

Thomas Ouldham, who married Elizabeth Roades at All Saints 25 Jan 1615/16, had 'Phillippa daughter of Thomas Ouldham' baptised there 10 Aug 1617, five months after Philippa's burial. Thomas may be her son (no baptism of a Thomas son of William Ouldam is indexed at Derby), but this is not shown. ¶10

The upgrade note is superseded on the grade only; the burial reading stands and is the strongest support for the Probable. ¶11b
