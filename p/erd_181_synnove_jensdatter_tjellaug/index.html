About the family

Mother of Rasmus Kristoffersen Tjellaug. Deed read: Nordfjord pantebok 12493 pag. 146-147, dated and read at the Stryn ting at Hegdal 30 Oct 1714. 'Kiendes jeg underskrevne Erich Erichsen Tiellov... at jeg... haver solgt og afhendt til min kiere Stifsøn den Dannemand Rasmus Christophersen Tiellov... som den rette Odelsmand... [16] Marcker Smørs Leye, som mig efter og ifølge Skifte-Brev efter min sal. Hustrue Synneve Jensdatter tilfalt udi gaarden Tiellov', conveyed to 'min kiere Stifsøn Rasmus Christophersen, hans Hustrue og Arvinger'. Witnesses Ole... Loen(?) and Ole... Aaland. Translation: Eirik Eiriksen of Tjellaug sells to his dear stepson Rasmus Kristoffersen of Tjellaug, as the rightful odel heir, the 16 marks of butter-rent in Tjellaug that fell to Eirik by the probate after his late wife Synnøve Jensdatter. A contract of the same day (pag. 145-146) between Eirik and 'Rasmus Lauritsen Brekke, on behalf of my brother-in-law (Svoger) Rasmus Christophersen Tiellov' has Eirik give up the third of Rasmus's holding that he had for life, to leave it in spring 1716, for 20 rdl. ¶1

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Upgrade: Confirmed as mother of Rasmus Kristoffersen Tjellaug. The 1714 deed (pantebok 12493 pag. 146) has her widower Eirik Eiriksen call Rasmus Christophersen 'min kiere Stifsøn' (my stepson) and 'den rette Odelsmand' to the share in Tjellaug that Eirik received by the probate after 'min sal. Hustrue Synneve Jensdatter'. A stepson of Eirik is a son of Eirik's wife, and the only wife named is Synnøve, whose land Rasmus is entitled to redeem as odel heir. Aaland (1932, Kjellog bruk 1) independently gives Kristoffer Rasmussen's wife as Synnøve Jensdatter (Grov), remarried to Eirik Eiriksen. Her probate (before Oct 1714) is in the lost 1713-15/Arvebok series or unread. ¶2

Coordinator review (1 Nov 2026), graded on her link to Rasmus Kristoffersen Tjellaug: Probable. The 1714 deed calls Rasmus the seller's stepson and the rightful odel heir to land that came through Synnøve's probate. That makes him almost certainly her son, but the deed never says so in words. A record naming her as his mother (his probate, Brite's 1754 probate, or a later odel case) would make this Confirmed. ¶4

The case for this identification 1

Supporting evidence, graded on her link to Rasmus Kristoffersen: she stays Probable. The Nordfjord guardianship protocols call Eirik Eiriksen's daughter Ane Erichsdatter Rasmus Christophersen's 'Søster' (protocol 1, p. 81, 1712). At her death in 1740 they call Rasmus Tiellov 'hendes eeniste broder', and he inherited her land in Tjellaug (protocol 2, p. 205). Because the two have different fathers, they must share a mother, the wife whom Eirik married after Kristoffer died. With the 1714 deed (Eirik's 'Stifsøn' and odel heir to land from the probate after 'min sal. Hustrue Synneve Jensdatter'), this points firmly to Synnøve as Rasmus's mother. No record yet names her as his mother in words. Ane inherited by the skiftebrev of 4 Jun 1712, probably Synnøve's own probate, which is in the unscanned Arvebok fol. 70. ¶5a
