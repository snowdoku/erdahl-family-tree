About the family

Sister of Raymond Hayter. She was known as Vera, and she is the 'Mrs. V. Thomsen of Naramata' who witnessed Raymond's wedding in 1939. ¶1

Children: a son, Verner Garreth 'Garry' Thomsen (1942-1999), born at Naramata. Living descendants are not recorded here. ¶5

Life story

Marriage, BC 37-09-443446: Alice Vera May Hayter, aged 24, at home, United Church, of Penticton, born Gaspereaux, Prince Edward Island; father Benjamin Hayter, born PEI; mother Lydia Adelaide Hicken, born Gaspereaux, PEI. She married Kristian Verner Bak Thomsen, a farmer of Naramata born in Denmark, on 24 February 1937 at the United Church, Penticton, Rev. Robert R. Morrison officiating. The witnesses were her brother Raymond Hayter and her sister Bertha Hayter, both of Penticton. ¶2

PEI record: in the 1931 census of Gaspereaux as Vera Hayter, daughter, 19, housework at home - the family called her by her second name. ¶6

Grave: the City of Penticton's Lakeview Cemetery register lists 'THOMSEN, ALICE VERA MAY' in cremation plot CS-13-08, with her husband Verner Kristian Thomsen and her son Garry Thomsen (interred 22 April 2000). ¶7

In 1945 she and her husband witnessed her sister Bertha's wedding, signing as Alice Vera Mae and Kristian Verner Bak Thomsen of Naramata. ¶3

Death, BC 82-09-018294: Alice Vera May Thomsen, housewife, of 1196 Moose Jaw Street, Penticton, wife of Verner Thomsen, died at Penticton Regional Hospital on 3 November 1982, aged 70, born 10 June 1912 in PEI; father Benjamin Hayter. She was cremated on 8 November 1982. ¶4
