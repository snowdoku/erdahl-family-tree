About the family

Widow of Gullak Nielsen (see his page): named 'Enken Britte Iversdatter' in the probate after him at Yttre Oppedal, 28-29 Jul 1766 (Ytre Sogn 8b Nº 68). ¶1

Mother of Anna Gullaksdatter (see her page), Probable: the 1766 heading reads 'imellem Enken Britte Iversdatter og deres[?]...' followed by the 4 sons and 4 daughters; 'deres' (their, i.e. the couple's) is a likely but not a certain reading. The words after it are partly as 'udi[?]... og', and the next line as '[Æg]teskab avlede[?] 4 Sønner og 4 Døttre' (begotten in marriage). This favours the eight children being hers, but the unread words could in theory be a formula such as 'udi 1ste og 2det Ægteskab' (in first and second marriage), so the link stays Probable. Anna married in 1739 and was born by about 1721, so Britte would have been born by about 1700 and been a widow aged at least about 66 in 1766; that is possible, but a first wife of Gullak as Anna's mother and Britte as a second wife is not excluded. No record of Gullak and Britte's marriage has been found. ¶3a

Parentage (open): the betrothal places her at a farm beginning with B (probably Brekke) on 1 Jan 1716, not at Indre Oppedal. This neither proves nor rules out that she is the under-age 'Britha' daughter of Iver Lassesen of Indre Oppedal (probates 1699 and 1701), since that family's orphans may have gone into service elsewhere after 1701. The first witness, Lasse of Brekke, is probably the Lasse Lassesen, 45, farmer at Brekke in the 1701 census (s.94); whether he was kin (for example a brother of Iver Lassesen, which would make him her uncle) is untested. In the 1701 census no Iver farmed at Brekke; Gregorius Iversen, 54, farmed at Inder Bøle (girls are not listed). No parent is added. ¶8

Children's births (inference): with a betrothal on 1 Jan 1716, the eldest daughter Anna Gullaksdatter (married 1739) was born between about late 1716 and 1721. ¶9

Parents (Probable link; she herself stays Confirmed as Gullak Nielsen's widow): daughter of Iver Lassesen of Indre Oppedal (d. before 1 Jun 1699) and Eli Monsdatter. Record: the probate of 30 May 1701 at Indre Oppedal after Lars Johansen and his wife Eli Monsdatter names, on the wife's side, 'hendes børn med første mand auflet, Nemblig Hindrich, Lasse, Mognus, Torberg, Randj, Britte, Anne og Bigri[?], Ifvers Sønner og døtter', with their mother's brother (Morbroder) Ole Monsen [Leirh?]eland appointed guardian of the under-age children (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll 1b fol. 331b-333a from and -332b-333a.jpg from that reading). So a Britte Iversdatter, a minor in 1701 (born about 1685-1700), grew up on Indre Oppedal, next door to Ytre Oppedal, where Gullak Nielsen married a Birthe Iversdatter in 1716. Why Probable and not Confirmed: no record names Britte's parents together with Gullak. Supporting evidence: (1) the age fits a 1716 marriage; (2) her sister Anna Iversdatter of Indre Oppedal stood godmother in Sep 1717 to Lasse, son of Mons Johansen and Randi Iversdatter, another sister (11438 c18); (3) 'Gullach Ytre Oppedahl' was an appraiser at the 1739 probate of Mons Johansen, husband of her sister Randi; (4) the couple's eldest son was named Iver; (5) the two other Brite Iversdatters in the Ytre Sogn probate index died too early to be her: Brite Iversdtr of Orvedal (estate 1707-19) and Brite Iversdtr of Humledalen (estate 1720-28, probably the Birthe Iversdatter who married Lars Nielsen Hamledal in 1716). ¶12a

Mother at baptisms: 'Mat: Birthe' at the baptisms of two daughters named Anna of Gullig of Ytre Oppedal, about 25 Jun 1719 (died, buried 12 Aug 1719) and 16 Jun 1720 (the Anna who married Lasse Monsen in 1739); churched 12 Aug 1719 as 'Gulig ytter Opdals qvinde Birthe' and on 3 Aug 1720 as 'Gulig Niels[en] ytre opdals q[vind]e Birthe' (Gulen 11438 c37 R, c39 L, c48 R, c50 L). At both baptisms a 'Birthe Lassesdr' of Ytre Oppedal stood godmother. No new evidence on her parents. ¶14

Life story

Betrothal: at the New Year service in Brekke church, Festo Novi Anni (1 Jan) 1716, after the churching and a royal ordinance on collections for captives in Algiers: 'Trolovet Gullach Nielss: Yttreopdal og Birthe Ifversd: B[...]' (betrothed Gullach Nielsen of Ytre Oppedal and Birthe Iversdatter of B...), 'Test[es] Lasse [his mark] Breche, Jörgen [his mark] Tringlen[?]'. The bride's farm begins with B and the rest is lost under an ink blot on the page edge; Brekke ('Breche', the farm of the first witness) or Bøle are the likely readings, not Indre Oppedal. No marriage (copulation) margin note was seen beside it; the wedding normally followed within a few months. Gulen ministerialbok 1716-1722 (source 11438), canvas 1, left page, upper third. ¶7

Godmother, 11 Mar 1718: at the service in Brekke church on 11 Mar 1718, 'Christnet Tor Yttreopdalens barn N: Anna' (baptised Anna, child of Tor of Ytre Oppedal), godparents Johannes Satre[?], Harald Yttreopdal, Lasse Gutormsøn ibid, Magnilde ibid and 'Birthe Ifversdr: ibid' (Birthe Iversdatter of Ytre Oppedal) (Gulen ministerialbok 11438, canvas 23, left page, top). So by March 1718, two years after her betrothal, she was living at Ytre Oppedal as Gullak Nielsen's wife. ¶11

Living 30 Jul 1766 (reading of Nº 69, fol. 394a): 'Ao 1766 d. 30te Julij, da Skiftet var tilendebragt efter Gullach Nielsen Yttre Oppedall i Ladvigs Skibr., begierede hands Enke Britte Iversdatter...' (when the probate after Gullach Nielsen was concluded, his widow Britte Iversdatter requested...). The request concerns the farm and the '4 Sønner og 4 Døttre' named in the probate document (Skiftebrevet); its exact terms (perhaps her maintenance or the farm's tenancy) are not read. Nº 69 is therefore not her own probate, and she was alive on 30 Jul 1766. Witnesses partly read: Lasse Jo[ha]nsen[?], Mons [?], Torchild Oppedal and Botte[?] ibidem. ¶2

Death: before 16 Sep 1769, the date of her probate; after 30 Jul 1766, when she petitioned as Gullak's widow. Gross estate 139 Rd 3 ort 6 s, deductions 6 Rd 3 ort 6 s, net 133 Rd 2 ort 8 s. The 1769 probate is in the period of Gulen ministerialbok 11443 (1749-1776), so her burial should be there, scan-only. This also settles the two index rows: pa00000000523439 (8b p. 563) is her own estate, and pa00000000523230 (pp. 394, 563) points to the 1766 follow-on entry and this one. The 'Brithe Iversd:' of Ytre Oppedal who stood godmother in 1779 was therefore another woman. ¶5

Reading notes: in the 1769 heading the scribe first wrote the children's patronymic as 'Guttermsen/Guttermsdatter' and corrected it above the line to 'Gullachsen/Gullachsdatter' in each case; the husbands of the 2nd to 4th daughters are only partly read. Her own parentage is not stated (no father or siblings named). ¶6

How sure are we?

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: raised from Probable to Confirmed as mother of Anna Gullaksdatter (see her page). Her own probate, Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll 8b, No. 55, fol. 563b, held at Ytre Oppedal, Ladvig skipreide, on 16 Sep 1769 after the late 'Kone Britte Ivers[datter]', lists as heirs the same 4 sons and 4 daughters as Gullak Nielsen's 1766 probate (Iver, Niels dead with daughter Britte, Thore dead with daughter Lisbett, Claus of Ytre Oppedal; Anna, Magnilde, Maritte, Britte), among them 'Datteren Anna Gullachsdatter gift med Lasse Monsen Indre Oppedale', and every share is called Moderarv (inheritance from a mother): each son 22 Rd 1 ort 5 1/3 s, Anna 11 Rd 10 2/3 s, a daughter's half share. So all eight children were hers, and the alternative that Anna was a first wife's child is excluded. Read from the scan (Digitalarkivet 24166, view 182). ¶4

The case for this identification 1

Index check: a probate-index search for firstname Brite/Britte, lastname Ivers*, Gulen 1700-1770 gives 8 rows: Orvedal 1707-19, Vamraak 1740-43, Humledalen 1720-28, Oppedal Ytre 1766-72 (twice; her own probate), and three Nordhordland extracts (1744-48). The index lists the deceased only, not the heirs. ¶13

Records that disagree 1

Against: at the betrothal on 1 Jan 1716 the bride's farm begins with 'B' (Brekke?), not Indre Oppedal, and the eldest daughter was named Anna, not Eli. The first witness, 'Lasse Breche', may be a paternal kinsman (Iver Lassesen's brother Lasse?), but this is not proved. ¶12b

Searches that found nothing 1

Sibling search, negative: three Lavik probates of women named Iversdatter were read from the scans to look for Britte as a sister and heir; none names her. (1) Anna Iversdatter, Bruaas, 7 Sep 1741 (Ytre Sogn 6a No. 86, fol. 163b): heirs her widower Anders Østensen and her children by her first husband Ole Pedersen, Iver, Peder and Anna. (2) Anna Iversdatter, Bachen, 29 Sep 1743 (6a No. 102, fol. 347b): widower Lasse Josephsen; heirs her brothers Hans, Lasse and a second Hans Iversen (one dead, leaving a son Iver) and her sister Eli Iversdatter. (3) Anders Nielsen and Ingebor Iversdatter, Ødejorden, 14-15 Sep 1745 (6b No. 44, fol. 473b): children Iver, Brite (8) and a second daughter; guardians the father's brother Niels Andersen Kolgrov and the mother's brother Jacob i Gierden. None is a daughter of Iver Lassesen of Indre Oppedal (whose daughters included Britha, Anna and Ingeborg) as far as these headings show. Also noted: the index has a probate after another Brite Iversdatter of Humledalen/Humlevaag (1720-28), probably the Birthe Iversdatter who married Lars Nielsen Hamledal in 1716, not this woman. ¶10

Open questions and the next record to read 2

Would settle it: the marriage of Gullak Nielsen in any Gulen register earlier than the 1736-1746 ministerialbok (11441) used so far, a probate after a first wife of Gullak, or Britte's own probate or burial naming her children. ¶3b

Lead on her 1716 home 'B[reche]' (not a proof): the probate of Lasse Lassesen of Indre Brekke, 5 Jul 1741 (Ytre Sogn 6a No. 36, continued fol. 121b-122a, read from the scan), opens on fol. 121b with heirs of his 'første afdøde Kone Halde Hans[datter]', among them [a daughter, name not read] 'gift med Jørgen Tringlen', both of age and present; the appraisers were Lars Brecke and Iver Engesetter. The guardians at the end are Iver Engesetter for the son Anders, and the mother (Rachel Andersdatter) for the daughter Halde 'under opsiun af Lars Brecke'; no father's or mother's brother is named. Lasse Lassesen married Rachel Andersdatter of Marnes[?] (betrothed about late Jun 1719, 'Trolovet Lasse Lass: Innerbracke og Rachel Andersdr: Marnes', married 4 Nov 1719; Gulen 11438 c37 R, c42 L). The two witnesses at Britte's 1716 betrothal, 'Lasse Breche' and 'Jörgen Tringlen', were thus probably father-in-law and son-in-law, which suggests that Britte was living in Lasse Lassesen's household at Indre Brekke. If Lasse Lassesen (45 in 1701) was a brother of her father Iver Lassesen, he was her uncle; this is untested. ¶15
