About the family

Siblings: the same couple, John (Joen) Johnsen and Anne Pedersdatter, had Joen (baptised Lesja 4 Jul 1816, confirmed Grytten 1831 as John Johnsen of Setnæs), Peder (1817), Kari (baptised Lesja 28 Nov 1819, confirmed Grytten 1834 as Kari Johnsdatter of Setnæs, her mother 'Anne Pedersdatter from Lesja'), Anders (baptised Grytten 17 Aug 1823 at Isterdal, confirmed 1838 at Setnæs), Iver (baptised 13 Aug 1826 at Aag) and Ingebrigt (baptised Feb 1829 at Aag). ¶12

Daughter's death certificate: the Minnesota death certificate of his daughter Carolina Gurine Tungseth (died Clay County, Minnesota, 14 Aug 1928; FamilySearch index, ref 2744) names her father as 'Peder Johnson', corroborating the link already Confirmed from her 1877 marriage. ¶14

Life story

Baptism: Peder was baptised on 26 Oct 1817 at the Lesjaverk (Verket) annex church in Lesja parish, Gudbrandsdalen, as the child of Jon Jonsen of Kloppen and Ane Pedersdatter, born 29 Sep 1817. The godparents were Raalv (Rolv?) Slettom, Erland Slettom, Ole Nyj...setten (reading uncertain), Rønnog Slettom and Marit Larsdatter Enebør; the family belonged to poor-relief rotation (lægd) no. 6. His mother was churched (introduced back into the church after childbirth) the same day. Read on the scan, p. 875. ¶7

Confirmation: confirmed 15 Sep 1833 at Grytten as Peder Johnsen of Setnæs (no. 4, p. 492), son of the lodger (inderst) John Johnsen and his wife Anne Pedersdatter. The clerk first wrote 'Peder Pedersen', struck it out and wrote 'Johnsen'. Read on the scan (canvas 149). ¶10

Married Anne Lassesdatter of Skorgen 28 Apr 1842 in Grytten. Children: Lars (1846), Henrikke Caroline Gurine (1851), Anna Petrine (1853), Andreas Olaus (1860) and Gustav Andreas (both buried 11 Oct 1865). ¶2

At his marriage on 28 Apr 1842 at Grytten he was 25 years old and described as 'Ungkarl og Inderste' - a bachelor and a lodger, that is a man renting house-room on a farm without holding land. His residence is given as Setnæs. The bride, Anne Lassesdatter, was entered as 'Pige' (a maidservant) of Skorgen, with her age put at 27. The entry is on page 395, no. 3 of the register, and names neither father; the bondsmen (forlovere) are not in the online index and would have to be read on the scanned page. ¶5

Both his parents came from landless households at Sætnes: his father John Johnsen was a lodger and then a cottager there, and his mother Anne Pedersdatter was the daughter of Peder Amundsen of the neighbouring smallholding Brækken. Peder himself left farming for the town, and by the early 1850s the family was in Kristiansund, where his daughter Hendrikke Karoline Gurine was born in February 1851. ¶6

Marriage: on 28 Apr 1842 in Grytten church he married Anne Lassesdatter of Skorgen (p. 395, no. 3, read on the scan). He is entered as bachelor and lodger (Ungkarl og Inderst) of Setnæs; his age is written '2?' with an unclear second digit (read as 23 or 25 by different readers; 24 would be exact). She is a maiden of Skorgen aged 27. The forlovere (bondsmen who vouched there was no impediment to the marriage) were Anders Lassesen of Skorgen (reading of the patronymic uncertain; probably the bride's brother, as her father was Lasse Andersen) and John Johnsen of Setnæs, very likely the groom's father (inferred: the entry does not state the relationship, and a namesake Jon Jonsen, husband of Aleth Rasmusdatter, also lived under Sogge-Setnes). The remark column says, by ditto, that both were vaccinated. ¶11

Death: Probable. 'Peder Satnos' (a misreading of Sætnes) was buried 13 Jun 1865 in Kristiansund; the index gives no age. Anne Larsdatter was a widow in the 1865 census (31 Dec 1865). The Oct 1865 burial of their sons Andreas Olaus and Gustav Andreas still lists 'Peder Johns Sætnøs' and Anne Larsdatter as the parents. ¶3

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Confirmed as Caroline's father in her 1877 marriage. Grytten birth and parents probable (name, farm and son Lars's Grytten birth all fit). ¶1

Confidence raised from Probable to Confirmed. His parents are named in two primary records read on the scan: the 1817 Lesja baptism (Jon Jonsen and Ane Pedersdatter of Kloppen) and the 1833 Grytten confirmation (John Johnsen and Anne Pedersdatter of Setnæs). The two are one family because the Lesja and Grytten records share three children (Joen 1816, Peder 1817, Kari 1819) and the 1834 confirmation calls the mother 'from Lesja'. His own identity runs unbroken: confirmation 1833 at Setnæs, marriage 1842 at Setnæs with (very likely) his father as bondsman, and his daughter Caroline's 1877 marriage naming him as her father. The 1833 date error (Oct for Sep) is a copying slip and does not weaken the link. ¶13

Corrections 1

Correction: his birth date is 29 Sep 1817, not 29 Oct 1817. The 29 Oct date comes from his 1833 Grytten confirmation (p. 492, no. 4, which reads '29 Octob. 1817'). The Lesja register itself, written at the time, says born 29 September and baptised 26 October; a birth on 29 Oct cannot follow a baptism on 26 Oct, so the confirmation clerk most likely carried the baptism month into the birth date. The contemporaneous baptism entry is trusted. Birthplace corrected from Sætnes to Kloppen in Lesja. ¶9

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Possible brother: Iver Johnsen 'Satnos', father of a child baptized in Kristiansund in 1878. ¶4

Searches that found nothing 1

Baptism (negative in Grytten): there is no baptism for him in Grytten. The 1817 Grytten baptisms (pp. 247-256, IIIF canvases 92-96 of source 16028) were read on the scan entry by entry for Sep-Dec 1817 and match the index for the rest of the year. The Lesja entry explains why: the family lived in Lesja from their marriage in March 1816 until at least November 1819 and came to Grytten afterwards. ¶8
