Life story

1801 census, Odland (Skjold): Ragnille Pedersdatter, 23, unmarried, daughter of Peder Aslagsen and Angona Iversdatter. Her son Peder (1806) is named after Peder Aslagsen. ¶2

Probate: named as heir 'anden og yngste Datter Rongnilla Pedersdatter, gift med Orm Jonsøn under Aadland' in the 1806 skifte after her mother Angona Iversdatter, Holen under Aadland (Ryfylke skifteprotokoll D 15, Digitalarkivet 23897, fol. 493b-494a, view 500). Her siblings: Iver Pedersen (seafarer, Bergen), Aslak Pedersen (Christianssand), Peder Pedersen (husmann under Gismervig) and Marthe Pedersdatter (m. Helge Olsen Alvestad, Hetland skipreide). ¶4

Spelled Ragla, Ragnilla, Rognald or Ragnille. Buried 1824 as 'Ragla Peders Dtr Yttre Eide', age about 46. Orm Johnsen is listed as next of kin. ¶1

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: Confirmed as the mother of Lars Ormsen (see his page). Lars's baptism on 16 Dec 1818 (Avaldsnes ministerialbok 1816-1825, p. 26 no. 4) names his parents as Orm Jonsen and 'Ragla Peersdtr.' of Ytre Eide. His 1845 marriage as 'Lars Ormssen Yttreeide', aged 25 and born at Ytre Eide, ties the baptism to him. Her link to the 1801 Odland household of Peder Aslagsen stays Probable. ¶3
