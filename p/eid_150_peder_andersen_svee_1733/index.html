About the family

Children in the register by Berit Olsdatter: Anne, baptised 16 August 1761 to 'Per Anders. Sveen' and Berit Olsd., godparents Siur Seter, Ole Kiosnes and Ingebor Sesseng; and Ole, baptised on New Year's Day 1767 to 'Peder Anders: Svee' and Berit Olsd., godparents Jon Aftreit, Anders Sveen, Jon Kiosnes and Brynild Seter (Sæter). Svee (Sveen) is a holding on or beside Sæter: the 1805 burial of Berete Olsd. gives her residence as 'Saeter-Sveet'. ¶1

Father of Ole Pedersen Saether (see his page), rated Probable. For: (1) Ole's burial age, 26 years 5 months on 19 May 1793, puts his birth about mid-November to mid-December 1766, and the 1 January 1767 baptism of Ole, son of Peder Andersen Svee, fits it; (2) farm continuity - this Peder is 'Saether' in 1768 and at his death in 1793, Ole is 'Saether' at his betrothal (1792), his daughter's baptism (1792) and his burial (1793), and Svee is part of Sæter (Saeter-Sveet, 1805); (3) Sæter people stand round both families - godmother Brynild Seter (1767) and godfather Siur Seter (1761) for this couple, sponsor Olle Siursen Sethar and godmother Maren Saether for Ole in 1792; (4) Ole's daughter Beret named her last son Peder (1833). Against, and why not Confirmed: no record names Ole's father. A rival baptism of the same weeks, Ole son of Peter Petersen Ofre-Oine and Sigri Jensdatter (Sunday after New Year 1767), fits the date as well and is set aside only because nothing ties that family to Sæter; two later rivals (Ole son of Peder Olsen, Annunciation 1767; Ole son of Peder Pedersen Uthus, Easter 1767) are three to four months too late for the burial age. A second Ole Pedersen lived at Sæter in 1794 (his wife was churched in Advent 1794), so the 1793 burial could in principle be his namesake's. The older Peder Andersen and Berit Olsdatter at Vigen in the 1762 extra tax, with five children over 12, are a different, older couple. The probate (skifte) after Peder Andersen Saether, 1793, or after Ole Pedersen Saether, 1793, in the Strinda og Selbu sorenskriveri would settle it. ¶4

Life story

Saether, 1768: at Misericordias Domini (17 April) 1768 the communicant list has 'Peder And: Saether' and his wife, so by then the Svee man was entered under the farm Sæter (inference: the same Peder Andersen, from the Svee-Sæter tie and the Sæter godparents of 1761 and 1767). ¶2

Death and burial: 'Peder Andersen Saether', aged 60, died 19 June and buried 23 June 1793 (4th Sunday after Trinity), a month after the burial of Ole Pedersen Saether. Birth about 1733 is inferred from that age; his baptism falls before the surviving Selbu books used here. ¶3

Burial confirmed: 'Kastet Jord paa og holdt Ligtale over Peder Andersen Sæther, gl: 60 Aar, død d: 19de Junii', 4th Sunday after Trinity (23 Jun) 1793 (16311 c155) - aged 60, born about 1733. ¶6

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: his probate, Stjør- og Verdal Skifteprotokoll 10 (Digitalarkivet 24763) p. 391, registered 11 July 1793: 'Anno 1793 den 11te Julii blev paa Huusmandspladsen Sveet kaldet under gaarden Sæter i Selbo Tinglaug og Strinde Fogderie beliggende foretaget Registrering og Vurdering efter den sammesteds ved Døden afgangne Huusmand Peder Anders[en]', between 'den efterlevende Enke Berithe Olsdatter' and the children of the marriage: 1. the son [Ole] Pedersen, deceased, who left 'et Pigebarn nemlig Berith Olsd. 1 aar gl.'; 2. a daughter [name not read] Pedersd., married to Niels Erichs[en] [farm uncertain]; 3. Anne Pedersd. (reading of the son's name and of the second daughter partly uncertain; the 'Berith Olsd. 1 aar' phrase is clear). This names him as husband of Berithe Olsdatter and as the father of a son Ole Pedersen who had died leaving a one-year-old daughter Berith Olsdatter, which is Ole Pedersen Saether (buried May 1793) and his daughter Berthe (baptised 9 Sep 1792). The probate ties this Peder to Ole by a primary record, so the link is Confirmed. ¶7

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Not the Peder Andersen of Stubbe: that man, aged 63 in the 1801 census in his second marriage to Ane Jonsdatter, was buried in 1802; this Peder was buried at Sæter in 1793. ¶5
