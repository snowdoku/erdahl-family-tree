Life story

Nicholas Southcott of Southcott (in Winkleigh), Devon, who married Margaret, daughter of John Bossom (Bossond, Bosome) of Bossom; father of John Southcott of Bovey Tracey (Visitation of Devon 1620, Harleian Society vol. 6, pp. 266-269, in two separate Southcott pedigrees, one entered by Phillip Southcott and one by Richard Southcott of Calwoodley, John's grandson). Secondary accounts (e.g. Wikipedia, 'John Southcote (died 1556)') call John 'second son of Sir Nicholas Southcott of Southcott'. Added. ¶1

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the grade · 1 open question

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Probable, graded on his link to his son John Southcote: a heralds' visitation pedigree, entered in 1620 by John's grandson, and repeated in both Southcott entries. No record naming the relationship was read. ¶2a

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Test record: the Southcott pedigree in Vivian, Visitations of Devon (1895), pp. 700ff, with its cited deeds and inquisitions; an inquisition post mortem or Chancery suit (C 1) over the Southcott lands naming John as Nicholas's younger son. Added. ¶2b
