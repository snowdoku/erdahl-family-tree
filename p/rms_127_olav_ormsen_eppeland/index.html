About the family

NEW ANCESTOR, graded on his link to his son Orm Olsen (see his page). Åmli ætt og heim V (Vestbygda), Eppeland gnr. 65 (canvas 209): 'frå 1626 kjem [det] inn ein ny person på Øvre Eppeland... Han heitte Olav Ormson'; summary: 'Olav Ormson, [frå] Askland, Gjøvdal, f. (1598), d. etter 1666, g.m. [?] Nedre Eppeland?' (the book suggests his wife came from Nedre Eppeland but does not know her name); children Knud f. (1625) g.m. Ingeborg; Orm f. (1630) g.m. Kirsti [?] Nedre Eppeland; a child f. (1640). Vol. II (Askland, canvas 155) lists 'Olav, til Øvre Eppeland' among the 4 children of Orm Askland. Primary support: the 1666 vicar's census of Åmli has 'husmann Oluf Ormsøn 68' at Øvre Eppeland (b. about 1598, matching the book), and the 1664-65 men-under-50 list has 'Knud Olsøn 40, Orm Olsøn 34' there (see Orm Olsen). No record names Orm as his son. Bygdebok only (compiled secondary source, text rebuilt from word boxes, dates ABT), so Probable at most. ¶1
