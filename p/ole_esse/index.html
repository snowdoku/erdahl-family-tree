Life story

The Ytre Sogn probate register (Tjugum district) has an estate for 'Ole Paulsen, Ese Ytre' in 1800-1805, protocol 12, folio 548a. This fits Ingri Marcusdatter's husband, who died before the Feb 1801 census. His eldest son was Povel/Paul (b. ~1777), named in the usual way after the paternal grandfather. So his patronymic was very probably Paulsen. ¶2

Probate: Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll A 12, fol. 548a, No. 24, read from the scan. Probate (skifte) after the late Ole Paulsen of Ytre Esse in Tjugum skipreide (court sub-district), Leikanger; opened 1 April 1799 as written, closed 1 April 1800 per the margin. The heirs are his widow Ingerie Marchus Datter and 6 children: Pål (Paul) 21, Marchus 14, Anders 11, Sigrid 16 or 18, Ingeborg 9 and Aase 7. Guardians (formynder): Knud Hansen Ytre Esse (curator of Paul and guardian of Marcus); Peder Marchussen Tenningen for Anders (since died, replaced by Niels Olsen Balle); Biørn Johnsen Gierde for Sigrid; Mons Andersen Indre Esse for Ingeborg; Anders Hansen Tjugum for Aase. So he died before 1 April 1800, probably before 1 April 1799. ¶4

Known only as the deceased husband of Ingri Marcusdatter in the 1801 census. ¶1

Probate of his mother, 1803: in the probate of Sigri Olsdatter Berge (12-13 April 1803, Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll A 12, fol. 161b-163a, No. 9) he is her dead son 'Ole Poulsen, som boede og døde paa Gaarden Esse i Tjugums Skibrede', by her first husband Paal Olsen, married to the surviving 'Ingerie Marchusdatter' of Esse, leaving 3 sons and 3 daughters: Paal 25 (married, occupier of Ytre Esse), Marchus 20, Anders 16 (serving at Tendingen), Sigrid 22 (serving at Distad), Ingeborg 15 (serving at Esse) and Aase 10 (with her brother Paal). His father is thereby proved to be Paul Olsen of Ytre Esse (see his page). His sister was Ingeborg Paalsdatter (d. before 1803), and his half-sisters were Mari Larsdatter (wife of Ole Hermundsen, Distad) and Brithe Hermundsdatter (wife of Hans Andersen, Berge). ¶8

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Confidence moved Probable to Confirmed: the probate text names him as Ole Paulsen of Ytre Esse, husband of Ingri Marcusdatter and father of Anders Olsen, aged 11. That matches the 1801 census household and Anders Olsen Esse. ¶5

Corrections 1

Correction: his birth was estimated as about 1745, an estimate that no record supports. If he is the minor 'Ole Pouelsen' in Paul Olsen's probate of May 1762 (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll A 7b, fol. 589b, No. 29), which is Probable (see Paul Olsen Esse), his age there was a single digit, 4 or 9 (uncertain), so he was born about 1753-1758. That fits his eldest son Paul being born about 1778. The birth range is therefore given as 1752-1758, resting on that Probable identification. ¶6

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Not the same man as Ole Paulsen Esse, the Ole Povelsen of Ytre Esse who died before May 1758 with grown children. That man is this Ole's probable grandfather. ¶7
