About the family

Eldest of the six children of Sivert and Anna (Satter) Eidem of Brule Creek, Union County, South Dakota, and so an elder sister of Mayme O. Eidem and a great-great-aunt of David Erdahl. ¶1

Her children, from the same notice: two sons, BRUCE LEER OF YANKTON and a second son at JACKSONVILLE, FLORIDA, and a daughter, MRS. SHIRLEY PETERS, in Minnesota; seven grandchildren at her death. ¶12

Life story

Named as 'Bertha Leer' in the list of predeceased siblings in her youngest sister Myrtle's obituary of 2013, and linked to both parents on Find a Grave, where her memorial gives her dates and birthplace. ¶2

Censuses: 'Bertha Agnes Eidesen, daughter, born March 1900, South Dakota' in the 1900 federal census of Brule township, taken when she was three months old - the earliest record of her. Then Bertha A. (1910), Bertha (1920, indexed 'Eiden'). She is gone from the household by 1930, which fits her 1923 marriage to Milton Rudolf 'Pat' Leer. ¶7

She married Milton Rudolf 'Pat' Leer (1904-1993) in 1923 and had two sons, Forrest Weldon Leer (1927-2012) and Sergeant Bruce Moreland Leer (1930-1968). She died at Sioux City, Iowa, aged 57, and is the one of the six buried at Vermillion rather than in the family churchyard at Elk Point. ¶3

Mayme (Eidem) Eidem's eldest sister. Named as 'Bertha Leer' in the list of predeceased siblings in Myrtle (Eidem) Overdahl's 2013 obituary, and linked as a child of Severt S. Eidem and Anna Olene Satter Eidem on Find a Grave. ¶4

She married Milton Rudolf 'Pat' Leer in 1923 and is buried not at Elk Point with her parents but at Vermillion in Clay County. Two sons are recorded: Forrest Weldon Leer (1927-2012) and Sgt Bruce Moreland Leer (1930-1968). ¶5

Her death notice in the Elk Point press, 1957, names her three surviving sisters and where each then lived: '[Selma] Nelson of Faith, this state, MRS. MAYME EIDEM OF MARIETTA, MINN., MRS. MYRTLE OVERDAHL OF WORTHINGTON, MINN.' Leader-Courier (Elk Point, South Dakota), 1955-1958 volume, page 0463 of the Fulton History scan. Read so far as a search snippet plus the page; the full column is the next thing to transcribe. ¶8

Cause of death, new: she was killed in a TWO-CAR COLLISION in Sioux City, Iowa, on 8 August 1957. Leader-Courier (Elk Point, South Dakota), Thursday 22 August 1957: 'Bertha ("Patsy") Leer... passed away... August 8... as [the result] of a two-car collision at a Sioux City corner of Ha[mil]ton and [-] in Sioux City, Iowa.' The project had the date and the place but not the cause. ¶9

Funeral and burial from the same notice: 'funeral service was held AUGUST 12, at 2 p.m. at [St. Paul's Lutheran]... interment was in BLUFF [VIEW CEMETERY] AT VERMILLION.' ¶10

Her life before marriage, new: 'she taught school [several] years in CLAY AND [UNION] COUNTIES'; she 'attended high school IN ELK POINT and was a [member] of the high school GLEE [CLUB]'. She married Milton 'Pat' Leer in AUGUST at ST. PAUL'S LUTHERAN in Union County. ¶11

Her surviving siblings in August 1957, with their homes: 'three [sisters] Mrs. [Selma] NELSON OF [FAITH], THIS STATE, MRS. MAYME EIDEM OF MARIETTA, MINN., Mrs. [Myrtle] OVERDAHL OF WORTHING[TON, MINN.]; two brothers, SAM OF ELK POINT AND AMOS OF BURBANK' - Burbank being the Union County village, not the California city. ¶13

Birth record: none found in the South Dakota birth index over 100 years under surname Eidem, Eiden or Eidesen or mother's maiden name Satter or Sather (searched 13 Oct 2026). The only Eidem girl of 1899-1900 in the index is the other Bertha, daughter of Edward Eidem and Mary Hofland. ¶15
