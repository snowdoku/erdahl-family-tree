About the family

PARENTAGE RAISED TO CONFIRMED (10 Oct 2026). Her baptism identification was previously only Probable. Two Østre Råbyggelag probate records now name her outright as the child of Tergie Tjøstelsen and Christi Nielsdatter of Nedre Eppeland. Her father's estate of 10 January 1769 lists one child, "Berte Tergiesdtr., 8 år" — eight years old, so born about 1761, exactly matching the baptism of 7 November 1761. Her mother's estate of 29 July 1774 to 28 July 1775 lists "Barn i 1. ekteskap: Birgittha Thergiesdtr." as the only child of the first marriage. ¶7

Life story

Probable burial: 'Bergitthe Tergiesdtr.', a widow, of Tvet, aged 52 (b. ~1760), died 4 Feb 1812 and was buried 29 Feb 1812 at L. Topdal. Her age matches the 1801 census. ¶2

Baptism (Probable): on 7 Nov 1761 in Omblie, Birte was baptized, daughter of Tergie Thiøstelsen and Kristi Nielsdatter of Eppeland. Godparents: Aslak and Eivind Thiøstelsøn Eppeland, Jomfru Schütter(?), Giermund Rostland's wife, and Ingeborg Thiøstelsdatter Eppeland (Åmli 1756-1775 p.49 #53). Her farm (Eppeland), her patronymic and her age (41 in 1801, 52 at burial in 1812) all match. ¶5

She was an only child, and an orphan by the age of fourteen: her father died in 1769, when she was eight, and her mother in 1774. Both estates were insolvent — her father's showed 453 riksdaler of assets against 517 of debt. Her mother had brought the odel, the hereditary right to Eppeland, into the marriage, but the debts swallowed it. That is why Birgitte, heiress to a farm on paper, ended her life as a pauper's widow at Øvre Ramse. ¶8

Her step-father was Niels Joensen of Eppeland, her mother's second husband, who died in 1780 leaving two small daughters by a later wife. ¶9

ANCESTRY NOW FOUR GENERATIONS DEEPER: Birgitte Terjesdatter < Tergie Tjøstelsen (about 1732-1769) and Christi Nielsdatter (about 1734-1774) < Tjøstel Tergiesen of Nedre Eppeland (d. 1765) and Berte Evensdatter of Ramse, and Niels Olsen Eppeland (d. 1755) and Ingri Knudsdatter (d. 1742) < Tergie Tjøstelsen of Rostveit (d. 1728) and Oslaug Aanonsdatter, Evind Svendsen Ramse (d. 1722) and Anna Gunnersdatter, and Ole Ormsen Eppeland and Anna Svenningsdatter (both d. 1747) < Svend Evensen Ramse (d. 1696) and Ingeborg Olsdatter (d. 1712) < Even Tellefsen Ramse. ¶10

Married Thor Tellefsen 8 Jan 1787 in Åmli church, as pige Birgitte Tergiesdatter of Eppeland. She is named as Tellef's mother ('Birthe Tergiesdatter') at his 1795 baptism. ¶4

Widow receiving poor relief at Øvre Ramse in 1801. ¶1

The paternal aunt Berthe Tergiesdatter (68) at Øvre Ramse in 1801 is not her sister, since the patronymic belongs to a different generation. ¶6

THE 1801 PUZZLE IS SOLVED. The earlier note wondered whether the "paternal aunt Berthe Tergiesdatter (68)" in Tergie Aslaksen's household at Øvre Ramse in 1801 was related to her. She is a different woman from a different family: the 1773 probate of Halvor Tergiessen, who drowned in the Topdalselva, names his father as Tergie Aslachsen Ramse and his mother as Aasne Nielsdatter, and his sister as "Birgitte Tergiesdtr. g.m. Ole Jørgensen øvre Ramse". That is the Berthe Tergiesdatter of the 1801 census, born about 1733. The coincidence of name is just that. ¶11
