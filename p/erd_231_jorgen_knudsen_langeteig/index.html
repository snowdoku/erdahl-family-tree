Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Graded Confirmed on his link to his daughter Anne Jørgensdatter (see her page). His probate, 14 Feb 1750 (Ytre Sogn 6b fol. 635a, No. 27, 24162 view 283), was held at Langeteig or Midtun in Tjugum skipreide 'imellem Enken Brite Friksdatter og deris sammen avlede 3de Døttre', the eldest being 'Anne den eldre Jørgensdatter gift med Lars [Siur?]sen Røe'. Found by searching the Ytre Sogn probate index by farm (domicile Langeteig*). Continuity: (1) 17 Sep 1745, Røe: the widow Anne Jørgensdatter of Niels Olsen Røe (6b fol. 480a; her guardian there was a Jørgen of Langeteig); (2) 14 Feb 1750, Langeteig: Jørgen Knudsen Langeteig's probate names 'Eldste Datter Anne den eldre Jørgensdatter gift med Lars [Siur?]sen Røe' (5 years later, same farm Røe, now remarried); (3) 28 Aug 1769, Røe: the probate after Anna Jørgensdatter is held between 'hendes efterladende Enkemand Lars [Siur?]sen Røe' and her children with her late husband Niels Olsen (19 years later, same farm, same second husband). So the 1750 daughter married to Lars of Røe is the same woman as the 1745 widow and the 1769 decedent: pair match (Anne + Lars at Røe at both ends, audit 8 rule 3) plus the Langeteig guardian in 1745. ¶1a

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Rival: her younger sister, also Anne Jørgensdatter, but the 1750 record places her at Langeteig as wife of Michel Johansen, so she is excluded by name of husband and farm. Reading caution: Lars's patronymic is unclear in both entries (1750 'Lars O/S..ürsen', 1769 'Lars..rson'); the farm Røe and the first name agree. ¶1b

Searches that found nothing 1

Negative: no Knud holds land at Langeteig in the 1666 vicar's census (holders Ola 59, Harmund 45, Gulbrand 86, Frich 26; 35519 view 170), so Jørgen Knudsen (b. ~1670) was probably born elsewhere and married into Langeteig through Brite Friksdatter. The Langeteig probates of 1728-29 were read and do not name his kin: Johannes Jonsen, 11 Oct 1728 (3b p. 333a, 24158 view 46; widow Brite Andersdatter, children Frich and Maritte) and Samson Langeteig, 30 Oct 1729 (3b p. 376b, 24158 view 90; widow... Nielsdatter and children); in both 'Jørgen Langeteig' appears only among the creditors. ¶5a
