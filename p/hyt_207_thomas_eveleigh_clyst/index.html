John Eveleighc. 1511 – 1586Probable · Clyst St Lawrence

Life story

Thomas Eveleigh of Clyst St Lawrence, Devon, eldest son and heir of John Eveleigh 'of West Eveleigh' (Broadclyst). He married Thomazine, daughter of Michael Moore of Clyst St Lawrence, and their son and heir was John Eveleigh of Holcombe in Ottery St Mary (Vivian, Visitations of Devon, 1895, p. 336, in the portion taken from Harl. MS 1080; Westcote, View of Devonshire in 1630, pp. 563-564: 'Thomas Evelegh, of Clist St. Lawrence married Thomasin, daughter of Michael Moor of Clist St. Lawrence, and had issue John'). Birth year estimated (Heverly history: 'about 1480-85'). Added. ¶1

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the grade · 1 open question

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Probable, graded on his link to his son John Eveleigh. Two compiled pedigrees agree: Westcote (written about 1630, while John of Holcombe's younger children were alive) and Vivian (from the heralds' Harl. MS 1080). John of Holcombe was himself living at the 1564 visitation, so this generation was within his own knowledge. Pole (via the Nicholas Eveleigh literature) says John acquired Holcombe by foreclosing a mortgage on a Moore, consistent with the Moore marriage. No primary record naming the relationship was found. ¶2a

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Test record: the 1564 visitation entry itself (Colby, Visitation of Devon 1564, 1881, pp. 93-94, not free online); the Devon lay subsidy of 1524-25 (Devon & Cornwall Record Society, 1979) for Thomas and John Eveleigh at Clyst St Lawrence; Chancery proceedings (C 1) over Holcombe or Clyst St Lawrence lands naming John as Thomas's son and heir. Added. ¶2b
