About the family

Widow of Peder Nielsen of Halsnøe at his probate on 19 Mar 1787 (D 7b fol. 507a, scan read); the heirs are 'their jointly begotten children' ('sammen avlede Børn'), among them 'Anna Peders Datter gift med Thore Thorsen Buchen', dead, who left 3 children. Alive on 19 Mar 1787. ¶2

Parents found: Biørn Reyersen of Halsnøy (d. before 23 May 1731) and Kari Jonsdatter (d. before 17 May 1751), both Confirmed as her parents. Her father's probate, 23 May 1731 (D 9 fol. 50; D 4 fol. 355b), names 'Sophi Biørnsdatter 17 aar gl.', so she was born about 1714. Her mother's probate, 17 May 1751 (D 9 fol. 206), names 'Sofi Biørnsdatter', married. Her siblings were Rasmus Biørnsen (b. about 1720, a farmer on Halsnøy), Siri (b. about 1725) and Anne (b. about 1728). Continuity from these records to Sophie Biørnsdatter in the tree (see her page, widow of Peder Nielsen of Halsnøy, probate 19 Mar 1787): (1) 1731, Halsnøy: Sophie Biørnsdatter, 17, daughter of Biørn Reyersen and Kari Jonsdatter, with her brother Rasmus Biørnsen, 11. (2) 1751, Halsnøy, 20 years later but at her mother's probate on the same farm: 'Sofi Biørnsdatter', married, with the same brother Rasmus Biørnsen and sisters Siri and Anna. (3) 1762 ekstraskatt, Halsnøy, 11 years later: 'Sophie Biørndatter', wife (Hustr) of the farmer Peder Nielsen, listed beside her brother Rasmus Biørnsen, also a farmer on Halsnøy; the couple's children over 12 are Biørn, Niels, Marthe and Ane Peders-. (4) 1787, Halsnøy, the same couple: the probate after Peder Nielsen names his widow Sophie Biørnsdatter and the children Biørn, Niels, Peder, Marthe, Anna and Britha. No other Sophie Biørnsdatter is named on Halsnøy or in the Sjernerø probates in these years. The couple's eldest son was named Biørn, after her father. ¶5

Life story

That index is filed by skipreide and then farm; the Sjernerø section (which holds the Fister farms Byre, Buchen/Bukken, Halsnøy, Hille, Fure and others) runs at about views 946-1000. Image urls: view n = urn sk(20110328670000 + n + 335). Earlier passes had sampled only the Hjelmeland section. ¶1

1762 ekstraskatt (index): Halsnøe, 'Peder Nielsen, Opsidder' and 'Sophie Biørndatter, Hustr', with the children over 12 Biørn Pedersen, Niels Pedersen, Marthe Pedersdatter and Ane Pedersd. Ane is Anna Pedersdatter (b. about 1749), later the wife of Thore Thorsen Buchen. Her own grade (on her link to Anna) is unchanged at Confirmed. The 1762 list adds a second record that names her as Peder Nielsen's wife while Anna was in the household. ¶6

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Graded Confirmed on her link to her daughter Anna Pedersdatter: the probate names her as the widow and the children as born of the marriage, and identifies Anna by her husband Thore Thorsen Buchen and her three children (same names and ages, one year on, as in Anna's own 1786 probate on Lille Buchen). No earlier wife of Peder is mentioned. ¶3

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Not her: the D 12 register entry 'Holle i Stjernerø, Sophia Biørnsdtr., 22/11 1800, fol. 4' is a different woman. The entry (D 12, DA 23895, fol. 4a-5a, read on the scan) is headed 'Skifte Forretning efter Sophia Biørns Datter Holle, Stiernerøe Skibrede'; the registration was held on 20 Apr 1791 and the settlement on 22 Nov 1800. Her heirs are her children by Ole: 1. the son Knud Olsen Holle, of age; 2. the son Ole Olsen, dead, who left 2 sons and 3 daughters (Biørn Olsen, Ole Olsen 10, Helga Olsdatter m. Erich Knudsen Tastad, Berthe Sophia Olsdatter, Kari Olsdatter). No Pedersen/Pedersdatter children, so she is not Peder Nielsen's widow. ¶7

Open questions and the next record to read 2

Lead: Karen Biørnsdatter, an unmarried woman of Halsnøe, died before 24 Feb 1768 (Ryfylke skifteprotokoll D 6, DA 23890, fol. 156b-157a); her heirs are her siblings. She may be Sophie's sister, and reading the heirs there could name Sophie and so their father Biørn. Partly (fol. 157a, 'Anno 1768 den 24de February', registration on Halsnøe after the late 'Pige Karen Biørnsdatter', heirs her siblings): a brother [?] Biørnsen, sisters married or dead with children (one deceased sister left 5 children, among them a Biørn aged 19 and married daughters Marthe and Britta), and a youngest sister married to an Ole. The binarised scan does not let the sisters' given names be read with confidence, so Sophie is not yet shown there. ¶4a

Test record: D 6 fol. 157a, lines 3-14 of the entry. ¶4b
