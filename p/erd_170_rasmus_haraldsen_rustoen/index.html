About the family

He appears as Rasmus Haraldsen Røstøen in the Nordfjord guardianship protocols as a guardian (formyndar) in 1734 (protocol 1) and 1745-54 (protocol 3, p. 126, for Janniche Andersdatter Yhri), and as legal guardian (lavverge) of the widow at Rasmus Christophersen's probate at Røstøen on 10 May 1760. A Harald Rasmussen Røstøen is a guardian in protocol 4 (1755-73); he is probably a son named after Rasmus's father Harald (inference from the naming pattern only). ¶3

Parents: 1701 census Røstøen lists Rasmus b. 1700 as 'Sønn' of Harald Claes. (b. 1661); Aaland says Rasmus took the bruk in 1737 'som faren sa opp' and gives Harald's wife (bracketed) as Barbro Olsdatter Kårstad, widow of Rasmus Hallsteinson. Aaland also gives Rasmus Haraldsen's wife: 'K.: Marte Ingebr.d., d. 89 år g. 1787. Gravf. same dag' (buried the same day as Rasmus). ¶10

Life story

1701 census, Røstøen (Olden, Innvik): in the household of Harald Claesen, 40 (b. about 1661), the sons are listed as Rasmus 1 (b. about 1700), Halsteen Rasmussen 10 (b. about 1691) and Olle 8 (b. about 1693). Halsten and Olle carry the patronymic Rasmussen, so they are probably Harald's stepsons from his wife's earlier marriage to a Rasmus; this Rasmus aged 1 is Harald's own son, Rasmus Haraldsen. The census names no wife (the 1701 manntall lists males only). ¶1

Innvik baptism 4 Jun 1760 (Martha, daughter of Harald Rasmussen Rustøen and Siri Antonisdatter; ministerialbok 1750-1816, IIIF 11506 canvas 37, girls no. 39): one godfather is 'Claus Rasmussen Rustøen'. A Claus Rasmussen at Rustøen alongside Harald Rasmussen fits a family of a Rasmus at Rustøen naming sons Harald and Claus after Harald Claesen (Rasmus Haraldsen's father in the 1701 census). Inference from naming only; it does not involve Halsten, who is not a godparent at the 1760 or 1770 baptisms of Harald's children. ¶5

Guardianship protocol no. 1, p. 170 (transcription pa00000001190606): 'Rasmus Haraldssen Røstøen' was guardian for Christi Rasmusdatter of Avlem, for an inheritance of 19 May 1734. The transcription does not give the relationship; she could be a kinswoman of his wife's or his own. The scan of p. 170 has not been read. No grade change. ¶11

Burial: Rasmus Haraldsen of Rustøen, widower (enkemand), aged 88, was buried at Olden on 17 May 1787 (Innvik church book, index). Age 88 gives birth about 1699, which fits the 1701 census boy aged 1. ¶2

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Speculative father of Halsten Rasmussen (see his page). Halsten married in 1773 as a bachelor 'of Rustøen' (Innvik marriage 14 Nov 1773); he was born about 1737. Rasmus Haraldsen (b. about 1700, alive until 1787) is of the right generation and lived at Rustøen. The 1701 census shows only one other Rasmus at Røstøen, Rasmus Christophersen (aged 7), and his probate of 10 May 1760 lists five heirs with no Halsten, which rules him out. The given name Halsten occurs in Rasmus Haraldsen's own childhood household (his half-brother Halsteen Rasmussen, b. about 1691), which fits naming a son after him. Against this: no record names Halsten's father, Halsten may simply have served at Rustøen in 1773, and his surviving children (Lars, Else Catrine, Marthe, Randei, Giertrud) include no Rasmus or Harald. No probate after Rasmus Haraldsen is in the Nordfjord probate index (searches Rust* 1737-1800 and Røstø* 1700-1800). Plausible candidate only. ¶4

Upgrade: Speculative to Probable, graded on his link to his son Halsten Rasmussen (see his page). New evidence: at the baptism of Elen, daughter of Harald Rasmussen Rustøen and Siri Anthonisdatter, on 7 Jun 1766 (Innvik ministerialbok, IIIF 11506 canvas 61, p. 62, girls no. 27), the godparents were Knud Rustøen, 'Halsteen Rasmus. Rustöen', Christi Høyalmen(?), Birthe Knudsdatter Høyalmen(?) and Christi Andersdatter Myklebust. So in 1766 a Halsten Rasmussen lived at Rustøen and stood godfather to a child of Harald Rasmussen Rustøen, who is probably Rasmus Haraldsen's own son (named after Harald Claesen). Taken with the 1773 marriage (Halsten, bachelor, of Rustøen), the 1760 probate of the only other Rasmus at Rustøen (Rasmus Christophersen, no son Halsten among his heirs) and the name Halsten in Rasmus Haraldsen's own 1701 household, this makes Rasmus Haraldsen the likely father. It stays Probable: no record names Halsten's father; the link rests on residence, the godparent tie and naming. ¶7

Negative: the Rustøen probate of Marite (Marrite) Olsdatter, registered 9 Oct 1744 (Nordfjord skifteprotokoll A 9, source 24226, fol. 203b-204a, view 202; index pa00000000758803 and pa00000000053429), was read as a possible estate of Rasmus Haraldsen's wife.
