Life story

Probate: the skifte of 9 Apr 1740 at Midbø (25669 fol. 539, views 359-361) was held after 'Ole Torsøn og Aasild Torsdatter' and divided among their children Jon, [Turi/Kari], Torbjør, Inger and Ragnild Olsen/Olsdøtre and 'Aasild Torsdaatters 2de Sønner' by her first husband (a Halvor), 'Ansten og Targier Halvorssønner'. The first name is written 'Aasild' (possibly 'Aaslid'); the 1753 deed (pantebok I 5, No 311) refers to this skifte as being after 'vore Hustruers [Moder]'. ¶1

Earlier marriage: to a Halvor, by whom she had Ansten (Anstan) Halvorsen, later of Nes, and Targier (Targei) Halvorsen, later of Giøveland/Heimdal(?), both of age in 1740. So she was born before about 1690. Her first husband's patronymic and farm are not stated in the part read. ¶3

Birth: her son Joen Olsen Midbø was buried on 27 Oct 1786 aged 83 (born about 1703; Kviteseid ministerialbok 7982, canvas 184 right), and she had two older sons, Ansten and Targier Halvorsen, by an earlier marriage, so she was born before about 1685. ¶4

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Confidence: Confirmed, graded on her link to her son Jon (Joen) Olsen Midbø (see his page): the probate names him as her and Ole Torsen's son. Continuity: the heir 'Sønnen Jon Olsøn' of 1740 is the Jon Olsen of Midbø who on 28 Jun 1753 bought out the shares that his half-brothers Anstan and Targei Halvorsen and his sisters' husbands (Ole Jonsen Midbø, Torgrim Olsen and others) had received at the skifte of 9 Apr 1740 (pantebok I 5, No 311), and the same Joen Olsen of Midbø was the widower in the 13 May 1756 probate after Turi Jørgensdatter that names his son Tore: same farm, gaps of 13 and 3 years, the same sibling group in 1740 and 1753, and no rival Jon Olsen at Midbø. His children's names fit: the youngest daughter in 1756 was Aasild, after his mother, and the eldest son Tore, after his paternal grandfather Tor (Ole Torsen's father). ¶2
