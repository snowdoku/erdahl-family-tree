Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Graded Speculative on his link to his son Sjur (see his page). The bygdebok (Selbu i fortid og nåtid vol. 5, Berge, canvas 19) names an early user Tosten at Berge (the rebuilt line reads '[...] før 1609. Tosten') and then 'Tostens sønn, Ola Tostensen' who held the farm at the 1645 poll tax and was 60 in 1665. Sjur is attached to him only because the book calls Torsten Sjursen (b. 1651) 'bror[sønn] til Ola Torstensen', which makes Sjur a brother of Ola and so a son of this Tosten. No record names Sjur at all, and the nephew statement may be the author's inference from the succession: Speculative. ¶1

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Test record: the 1645 koppskatt (77038, Selbu full-farm men not yet located; sons are counts there, not names), the 1624 leidang roll (39890 v76-78; v76R, no Berge holder on that page) and the 1647 kongsjordebok (51306 v181) for a Tosten or Ola at Berge; and any court entry on Berge naming Torsten Sjursen as Ola Tostensen's brother's son. ¶2
