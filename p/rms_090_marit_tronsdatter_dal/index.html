About the family

Widow of Joen Knudsen of Dal (Dahl), Aurdal, at his probate in 1738. Graded on her link to her daughter Astri Jonsdatter. The card calls her 'g. Marite Tronsdtr.' without saying whether she was a first or second wife; her son Tron (22) carries her father's name, which points to her being the mother of at least the younger children. Dal probate: the Valdres probate card 2043 (DA 39948) indexes the estate of Joen Knudsen (deceased), of Dal ('Dahl') in Nordre Aurdal, held 15 Feb 1738 (Hadeland, Land og Valdres skifteprotokoll 1737-1743, DA 25286, s. 121; the entry heading at the foot of p. 121, view 71, reads 'paa gaarden Dahl i Ourdahls Prestegield i Walders'). Widow Marite Tronsdatter. Children: 1. Syfver; 2. Tron, 22; 3. Jøran (married to Ole Hilmen); 4. Ingerie (married to Knud Knudsen Breie); 5. Groe (married to Olle Andersen); 6. Marite, 19; 7. Astrie, 10. Gross about 568, net about 216 rdr; land in Dahl with Granefoss. Identification: the bride of 1753, 'Astri Joensd[atte]r Dahle' (DA 9361 p. 88, canvas 313), carries the same forename, the patronymic Jonsdatter and the farm Dal as Astrie, daughter of Joen Knudsen of Dal, aged 10 in 1738 (so born about 1728). ¶1a

Mother of all Joen's children: the probate text (DA 25286 p. 122) calls the seven heirs 'begge deris sammen avlede Børn', the children begotten by both Joen Knudsen and his widow Marite Tronsdatter, so she was the mother of Astria (10) and not a stepmother. That settles the stepmother question left open in. Her own grade stays Probable, because the identification of Astria, 10 in 1738, with the bride Astri Jonsdatter Dahle of 1753 spans 15 years with no record in between. A probable sister: Astri Tronsdatter, widow of Christen Knudsen of Rogne (Joen's brother), card 11107 (1741) and her lifetime division of 1757 (card 11109). Their father Tron is not yet found. ¶2

Her father's probate was looked for and is not in the card index (see the note on Trond Høljesen). Her parents stay Probable on the bygdebok alone. ¶8

Life story

Marriage read: Aurdal ministerialbok DA 9360, canvas 213 (p. 229, left page), under the service of 27 Dec 1701 ('Fer. 3 Nativ.', third day of Christmas; the last service of 1701 before 'Saa endis det gamle Aar' and the title page 'anno 1702'): 'Copulerede: Thron Siffuersen Bergier og Marthe Knudsdatter. Jon Knudsøn og Marthe Throndsdatter Dahl.' The Digitalarkivet index (pv00000000706837, 'Joen Knuds.' x 'Marthe Throensd.') gives the date as 27-11; the scan shows it is 27 Dec. No betrothal for the couple was found in the Aurdal entries of Oct-Dec 1701 (canvases 208-212). Her own grade (link to her daughter) is unchanged. The marriage gives her husband's farm Dal at the wedding, so the wife of Jon Knudsen of Dal from 1701 to 1738 is one woman. The link up to Trond Helgesen and Jøran Arnesdatter stays Probable, for the reason given on Jøran Arnesdatter. ¶10

Guardians read: the close of the 1738 Dal estate (DA 25286 p. 126, view 74) appoints guardians for the two minor daughters: for Marite Jonsdatter a man read as 'Jacob Haatvet[?]', for Astrie Jonsdatter a man '[Lars?]... Strand'. Neither is given a kinship word (no morbroder), so the estate does not name Marit Tronsdatter's family. The estate's division (pp. 124-125, view 73) speaks of the children's 'Faders og Moders arv', which fits her being the mother of all of them. The Rogne estate of 5 Feb 1741 (her probable sister Astri Tronsdatter's husband) is on DA 25286 p. 561, view 294, right page, but the scan is too faint at the URN resolution to read the guardians; it needs the IIIF full-resolution image or a better scan. Her father Tron is still not found. ¶4

Rogne 1741 re-read: the URN image of DA 25286 view 294 is already the full resolution (3215 px; the IIIF manifest for this protocol returns 404). The Rogne entry opens in the lower half of p. 561 ('... paa gaarden Rogne udi Rogne Annex... Valders... 1741'), but only its heading and opening formula are legible; the heirs and guardians are on the following pages (view 295 on), not read. Whether a Tronsen (her brother) stood as morbroder there is still open. ¶5

Bygdebok, Probable for her parents: the Valdres bygdebok I F, Dal chapter (gnr 107, canvas 494, right column), has 'Jon Knutsson Dal, f om 1665, d 1737, g 1701... Marit Trondsdotter frå [Bø] 106, d 1756, 76 år gamal', with children Jøran? (b. 1702, m. 1724 Ola Andrisson), Sjugurd 1704, Ingrid 1707 (m. Knut Knutsson), Gro 1712 (m. 1732 Ola Andrisson), Trond 1716, Marit 1719, Astrid 1721 (m. 1753 Jul Eivindsson). The Bø chapter (gnr 106, canvas 268, left column) lists her as the eldest child of Trond Høljesson: '1 Marit f om 1680, g 1701 m Jon Knutsson Dal, sjå Dal 107', and her sister '2 Astrid f 1685, g 1706 m Kristian Knutsson Dal' (= Astri Tronsdatter, widow of Christen Knudsen Rogne in 1741, card 11107). So the bygdebok makes Marit and Astri Tronsdatter sisters, which the two probates only suggested. Rebuilt from nb.no content-search word boxes; dates from the rebuild are given as ABT. ¶6a

Rogne 1741: DA 25286 view 294, p. 561 (the probate after Christen Knudsen, husband of Astri Tronsdatter) is too faint to read the guardians even at the largest image offered (3215 px); no IIIF manifest exists for this source. See the note on Trond Høljesen. ¶9

Her death about 1756 aged 76 (bygdebok) agrees with 'Marit f om 1680' in her father's chapter, two independent places in the book. ¶7

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Probable, not Confirmed: the two records are 15 years apart, and no record yet names Astri the bride as Joen Knudsen's daughter (her marriage entry names no father). A birth about 1728 also fits her son's birth in 1754 far better than the census age of 90 in 1801 (which would put her birth at 1711, and her son's birth at her age 43). The 1738 continuation (DA 25286 view 72, p. 122) should name the guardians of the minor children, Astrie among them; not read. ¶1b

Upgrade: upgraded to Confirmed by the coordinator (1 Nov 2026), graded on the link to Astri Jonsdatter, applying the audit-3 threshold (about 15 years or less on the same farm, no named rival). The 1738 Dal probate (DA 25286 p. 122) names 'Astrie, 10' among 'begge deris sammen avlede Børn' (both their children together). The 1753 Aurdal betrothal names the bride 'Astri Joensdr. Dahle', from the same farm 15 years later, and no other Astri Jonsdatter of Dal is known. ¶3
