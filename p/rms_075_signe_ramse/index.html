About the family

NEW ANCESTOR. First wife of Even (Evind) Tellefsen Ramse, according to the Tovdal bygdebok (Åmli: ætt og heim I, chapter 'Ytre Ramse', canvas 326). The reconstructed text reads: Eivind... før 1610... Born:... Olav.... Jon... Vehus. Gro... Nils... Tveit.... Eivind... Signe... Born:... Anne... Risland. 'Då Eivind skulle skifte etter Signe... 1639... han eit... med... born' (when Eivind was to divide the estate after Signe... 1639), and 'Borna med Signe' (the children with Signe), with Olav and Gro each receiving huder (land shares). (read from nb.no content-search word positions, not from a page image, so only the words listed are certain; the book is a published secondary source.) ¶1

Life story

So the bygdebok makes Signe the mother of Gro (Groe Evindsdatter, wife of Nils Tveit), Olav and Jon (of Vehus), and dead about 1639. Her patronymic and origin are not in the words recovered. ¶2

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
