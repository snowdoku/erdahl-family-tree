About the family

Father of Arne Nilsen of Løvigen (see his page), Probable; graded on his link to Arne. His 1738 probate names a son Arne Nielsen, about 18 (b. about 1720), at Berge. Seven years later 'Arne Nielsen Berge' married Zidsele Ingebrigtsd. of Frøse at Norddal (1 Oct 1745) and had a daughter baptised at Strand in Aug 1745. The guardian Gunder Pedersen Selle is from Selle, a Stranda farm, which fits the Berge of Stranda (an annex of Norddal, in Dale skipreide). No other Arne Nielsen at Berge is known. ¶2a

Life story

Probate (scan read): 'Berge i Dahle Schibbreede. Anno 1738 den 10de October' a probate was held after the late Niels Ellingsen, who lived and died on the farm Berge, between his surviving wife ('efterladte Hustru') Brite Olsdatter, with her appointed guardian Knud Arnesen Øverje[?], on one side, and the deceased's children on the other: the sons Elling Nielsen, of age ('myndig'), and Arne Nielsen, aged about 18 (age figure cramped), guardian Arne Weiberg[?]; and the daughters Giøre Nielsdatter, guardian Torsten Ellingsen Aursæt[?], and Ragnilde Nielsdatter, guardian Gunder Pedersen Selle. Sunnmøre skifteprotokoll 3A 12 fol. 528b (Digitalarkivet 24553 view 263). The entry calls the children 'the deceased's'; it does not say they were Brite Olsdatter's, and Elling was already of age, so she may be a second wife and is not added as Arne's mother. ¶1

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Not Confirmed: the step from Arne Nielsen of Berge (1745-46) to Arne Nilsen of Løvigen (1751-1762) rests on the 1762 extra-tax entry 'Arne og Zitzele' with a daughter Siri, not on a record naming Arne as Niels's son at Løvigen; and the age figure in the probate is not certain. Torsten Ellingsen Aursæt[?], guardian of the daughter Giøre, may be Niels's brother (same patronymic), which would make Niels's father an Elling. Not tested. ¶2b

Searches that found nothing 1

Negative: the probate after Svein Ellingsen of Aursnes, 19 May 1763 (Sunnmøre skifteprotokoll 3A 20 fol. 860a, view 24561/377, scan read in part), is 'Aursnes i Voldens Skibrede' (Volda), not Aursæt in Stranda: the parties are the widow Marte Andersdatter with her guardian Wernich Ellefsen and 'den afdødes efterladte Arvinger', whose names were not legible in the part read (they follow on 24561/378). No link to Niels Ellingsen of Berge or to Torsten Ellingsen Aursæt shows; low priority. ¶3
