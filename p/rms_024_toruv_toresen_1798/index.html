Life story

In the 1801 census at Felle he is Toruv Thoresen, 2, one of 'deres børn' (their children) of Tore Thronsen and Hæge Toruvsdatter, so born about Feb 1798 to Feb 1799. His baptism is not in the Treungen services of Oct 1798 to Feb 1799 (Kviteseid ministerialbok 1787-1799, images 133-136); Feb-Oct 1798 (images 125-132) has not been read. ¶1
