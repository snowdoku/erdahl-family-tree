Life story

Birth about 1638 from the 1701 census: Hans Hansen, bonde, 63, at Lunde, Leganger parish, with sons Hans (about 1685), Olle (about 1697) and Johanis (about 1700). The son Olle of 1701 is not among the heirs of 1713 (probably dead by then; inferred). Laberg 1985 p. 116 has Hans Hansson, Lunde, b. about 1638, d. 1713. ¶2

Bailiff's census 21 Jun 1664, Tjugum Kirke-Sogn: 'Lunde, Hans' with farmer's sons '32: Hans' and '12: Olluf'. The 1664 and 1666 vicar's lists give the Lunde farmer Hans (54 in 1666) only sons Ole (14/17) and Johannes (5). Father Hans of Lunde added as Probable on farm, name and patronymic; the 1664 age 32 (b. ~1632) disagrees with the 1701 age 63 (b. ~1638). Birth left at ABT 1638. ¶3

Probate (skifte) held 29 May 1713 at Lunde in Tjugum skipreide after 'Sal: Hans Hansen', between 'hans efterlevende Quinde Brede Ols dat:' (his surviving wife Brede Olsdatter) and 'deris Børn, Hans, Johannes, Britte og Anne hans Sønner og døttre' (their children Hans, Johannes, Britte and Anne, his sons and daughters); the widow's brother Tollef T[?]... acted for her. Half the estate went to the widow, the rest in brother's and sister's shares ('1 Søster Anna' on fol. 446a). No ages or guardians given (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll 2b, fol. 444b-446a). ¶1
