Life story

Married Eli (Elen/Elin) Nielsdatter in Selbu on Trinity Sunday, 5 Jun 1746: 'Ectviede... N3 Mandsfolket Soldat Ole Jonsen med pigen Elen Niels datter' (Selbu klokkerbok 16315, canvas 87, 0-based, col 3). Betrothed (Trolovede) at Easter / 2nd Easter Day (10-11 Apr) 1746: 'N2 mandsfolket Soldat Ole Jonsen med pigen Elin Niels datter' (canvas 84, col 2). He was a soldier. No farm is written in either entry; the identification with Ole Jonsen of Langlie rests on the name pair, the bygdebok's 1746 marriage year and 'Ole Jons. Langlien' as a communicant at Whitsun 1746 (canvas 86). This fits a son Niels born about 1747-48. Read. ¶5

Ole Jonsen of Langlie, Selbu, husband of Eli Nielsdatter. Father of Ole (bapt. 13 Feb 1757), Kari (bapt. 8 Apr 1759) and a child bapt. 7 Feb 1762 whose name is blotted (Selbu ministerialbok 16311, canvases 3, 7, 12). Godfather at Selbu in Jan 1757 and 26 Apr 1761. Birth range inferred: father of a child of 1762, and, if Niels's father, of a son b. about 1747. Not the same man as the Ole Jonsen Langlie married to Mali Andersdatter (children 1758 and 1761 interleave with Eli's). Possibly a son of Jon Reinaldsen Langlien (buried 10 Jul 1757 aged 67) - inference from patronymic and farm only. ¶1

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 4

Speculative, by naming pattern only. Niels Olsen of Langlie (see his page, b. about 1747, in the 1747-56 gap of the Selbu registers) named his children Elen (bapt. 22 Mar 1778), Ole (~1780) and Jon (~1784): the names of an Ole JONSEN and his wife ELI/Elen, and Niels's own name would come from Eli's father NIELS. Ole Jonsen Langlie and Eli Nielsdatter are the only Langlie couple in the Selbu book 1757-62 whose names fit: children Ole (13 Feb 1757), Kari (8 Apr 1759) and one illegible (7 Feb 1762), none of them named Niels - consistent with an elder Niels born before the book starts in 1757. What is missing: no record names Niels as their son. A second Ole Jonsen Langlie, married to Mali Andersdatter, had children Anders (30 Apr 1758) and Jon (11 May 1761) at the same farm, so the patronymic and farm alone cannot choose between the two; the Mali couple's children look like a young marriage begun about 1757, which makes them less likely parents of a child of about 1747, but this is not evidence. Decisive records not yet read: the 1762 extra-tax (ekstraskatt) household lists for Langlie (Digitalarkivet 49639, canvases 132-133, children over 12 named; being read by worker), the burials of Ole Jonsen and Eli Nielsdatter in 16311 (1767-1801), and the Selbu bygdebok (vol. 3, Langli). ¶2

Confidence upgraded Speculative -> Probable by the 1762 extra tax list: Ole Jonssen and Eli Nielsdatter of Langlie (matr. 692) have children over 12 named Jon, Niels and Arnald[?], while the other Langlie couple, Ole Jonssen and Mali Andersdatter (matr. 765), have none over 12. Niels Olsen Langlie (b. about 1746-50) is therefore most probably their Niels. No record names Niels's parents outright. ¶3

Speculative lead (1723 matrikkel, Digitalarkivet 39200): the 1723 examination protocol for Selbu lists two Langlie holdings. Row 644 (canvas 134): user 'Jonn' read as Jon [?] (alternative Jørn), owners Pastor tie Selboe 2 øre 12 mark and Thomas Angel 1 øre 12 mark, the pastor holding the bygsel. Row 702 (canvas 144): user Ole Jonsen, owner Commerceraad Schøller, skyld 2 øre. Fit: a Jon on Langlie in 1723 is the right farm, name and generation to be this man's father, and matches Jon Reinaldsen Langlien (b. c.1690, bur. 1757, aged about 33 in 1723). The 1723 Ole Jonsen of row 702 was already a farm user, so born before c.1700, and cannot be this man (b. 1710-28); he may be an older kinsman. No patronym is given for the Jon of row 644 and the first name is not certain, so this is a name-and-farm match only: Speculative. ¶4a

Upgrade: Confirmed, graded on his link to his son Niels Olssen of Langlie (see his page), not on his own parentage (unknown). The record naming the relationship is the 1762 extra tax (ekstraskatt) list for Selbu (Digitalarkivet 49639, canvas 132): Langlie matr. 692, column 'Mand og Hustrue, Deres Antal og Navne' Ole Jonsen and Eli Nielsdatter, column 'Børn over 12 Aar og Deres Navne' (children over 12 and their names) Jon, Niels and Arnald[?]. The column heading itself states that Niels is the couple's child. Continuity to the tree's Niels Olssen (see his page): he is next found on the same farm, baptising his daughter Elen at Selbu on 22 Mar 1778 as Niels Olsen of Langlie, is farmer at Langlie in the 1801 census (54, first marriage) and is buried from Langlie in 1815 aged 69, all pointing to a birth about 1746-47, which fits a child over 12 in 1762 born soon after the couple's marriage on 5 Jun 1746. ¶6a

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

No rival: the only other Langlie couple in 1762 with an Ole as husband (Ole Jonsen and Mali Andersdatter, matr. 765) had no child over 12, and the third Langlie household (matr. 811, Gutorm Børgesen) has no Niels. His naming of his children Elen, Ole and Jon agrees. ¶6b
