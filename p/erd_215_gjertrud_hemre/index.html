About the family

New mother of Gunder Johansen Hemre (see his page), graded on her link to Gunder: Probable. Hegra gårds- og slektshistorie B. 1, Hembre Øvre (canvas 183): Johan Olsen (b. about 1659, buried 4 Apr 1717) 'Gift m. Gjertrud. Born: Gunnar, brukar....'. Her patronymic is not given in the rebuilt text. Gunder named his eldest daughter Gjertrud (b. about 1721), fitting the naming pattern. Primary records that would test it: a 1701 manntall for Stjørdal fogderi (not found among Digitalarkivet's 1701 volumes 38450-38466; no. 13 = 38460 is Orkdal/Gauldal); Johan Olsen's probate after 1717 (falls in the 1714-1727 gap in the Stjør- og Verdal skifteprotokoller; the 77804 card index has a single Hemre card, 1733, not his); Gunder's own baptism c.1688 (Stjørdal church books begin 1714). ¶1

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
