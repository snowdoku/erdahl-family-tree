About the family

Probable wife's estate: the estate No. 16 immediately before, ending on fol. 19a, divides a share in 'Gaarden Asseim' among the same heirs (Philippus Dahle, the 2nd son of Medelfiord, Johanne, [Kari] and the 3rd daughter Ingeborre, 8 rd 3 ort 9 6/7 sk). It is probably the probate of his wife, the children's mother. Its heading on fol. 18a (24161 view 18) was not read, so no mother is created. ¶2

Children (sibling notes only; not created as persons): Philippus Gullovsen of Dahle; a 2nd son of Medelfiord, forename read Siur[?] (read Iver[?]); Johanne Gullovsdatter, wife of Jens Tynning (Tjønning), buried at Brekke on Dom. 3 p. Trin. (3 Jul) 1740 as 'g[ift]a Johanne Gulligsd. Tynning gl. 35 aar' (married woman Johanne Gulliksdatter of Tynning, aged 35; Gulen ministerialbok 11441, canvas 18 left and zoom-); Kari[?] Gullovsdatter, wife of Niels[?] Medelfiord; and Ingeborg Gullovsdatter (see her page), wife of Torkild Monsen of Asheim. The name is variously written Gullov, Gullig, Gullik and Gullak (Gudleiv/Gullak). ¶4

Wife: Sigri Rognaldsdatter (see her page), d. April 1740. Her probate, No. 16 in Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll 6a, fol. 18a, held at Asheim on 2[6 or 9] Apr 1740, calls him 'hendes efterlevende Mand Gullov Johansøn Asheim' (her surviving husband) and lists their children Philippus Dahle, Siur, Johanne (m. Jens[?] Tinning), Kari (m. Niels) and Ingeborre (m. Torkel Asheim). This replaces the earlier note that No. 16 was 'probably' his wife's: its heading has now been read. 'Efterlevende' means he outlived her; his own probate No. 17 is clearly dated 29 Apr 1740, so he died after Sigri and before 29 Apr 1740, the two estates evidently being divided at the same visit (see Sigri's notes); no marriage record has been found. ¶7

Son Philippus a servant in 1726: among the creditors of the same 1726 estate is 'Philippus Gullofss. Tienesteløn' (servant's wages owed to Philippus Gullovsen). He is taken to be Gullov's son Philippus (later of Dahle), named in the 1740 probates; this is inferred from the patronymic and the farm, and the record does not name his father. ¶11

Parents (bygdebok, Probable): Brekke herred bygdebok vol. 2 (1944), Asheim gnr. 50 bnr. 32 (canvas 247), gives 'Gullak Johannessen', brukar 1690-1740, as son of Johannes Sjurson (1642-1706) and Kari Arnesdtr. (1639-1709); linked as Johannes Sjursen and Kari Arnesdatter. The book says he married first a Lisbet (1660-1697) and in 1697 Sigrid Rognaldsdtr. (1670-1740); children listed include Johanne 1707, Kari 1709 (Instefjord, m. 1735 Nils Toreson Instefjord) and Ingeborg 1711 (m. 1740). It cites the 1740 division 'etter bnd.kone Sigrid Rognaldsdtr. Asheim'. Its death year for him reads 1743 in the rebuilt text, against his probate of 29 Apr 1740: probably a misread or a slip in the book; the probate date stands. ¶16

Life story

1701 census: on Aasem (Asseim, Bondegaard), Eivindvik (Gulen) parish, 'Gullach Johans:' (Gullak Johansen), aged 34, with the son 'Phillipus Gullachs:' aged 3, and the servants Mons Philippussen, 'fød paa gaarden' (born on the farm), aged 28[?], and Ole Jonsen[?], 'fød i Sogn' (born in Sogn), aged 14. left the son's age as 3 or 8; on an enlarged crop reads 3: the digit has the same form as the 3 in Gullach's own age 34, and differs from the looped 8 in 'Philippus Pedersen 8' on the line directly above. Manntall 1701, Digitalarkivet 38455, viewer 55, printed s.92. His birth is therefore about 1667 (inferred from the age of 34), replacing the estimate 'before 1685', and his son Philippus Gullovsen (later of Dahle, heir in 1740) was born about 1698. Identity: he is the only Gullak/Gullov at Asseim in 1701, he holds a household of his own, and his son Philippus matches the eldest son of the 1740 probates; his father is not named in this census (only men and boys are listed, and no Johannes at Asseim has a son Gullak). Name: Gullak (Gudleik) and Gullov (Gudleiv) are strictly distinct names, but this man's own records already mix them: Gullov and Giellof in the 1726 and 1740 probates, and Gullig and Gulligsdatter for his grandson and his daughter Johanne in 1740. So the 1701 spelling 'Gullach' does not count against the identification, which rests on the patronymic, the farm and the son Philippus. A different Gullach (Kirstensen, aged 12) was at Ytre Oppedal in 1701. ¶12

Neighbours on Asseim in 1701 (context): Ole Jensen[?] 50; Claus[?] Thoresen 70; Ole Clausen[?] 34; the soldier Lasse Rognaldsen 28 with stepsons[?] Mons and Philippus Pedersen; Johannes Nielsen 60 with sons Kolbein 18 and Anders 13 (probably the Johannes Nielsen Ese whose estate he appraised in 1726); and Rognald Andersen 45 with Mons Larsen[?] 16 and sons Lasse 8 and Anders 4. Johannes Nielsen is not shown as Gullak's father. ¶13

Alive and of age in 1726: at the probate after Johannes Nielsen Ese on the farm Asheim, 17 Sep 1726 (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll 3a, fol. 200b, Digitalarkivet 24157, view 203, read from the scan), the lensmann Anders Bøe sat 'med 2de Vurderingsmænd Giellof Asheim og Colben Asheim' (with two appraisers, Gullov Asheim and Kolbein Asheim). An appraiser had to be an adult man, so Gullov of Asheim was alive, of age and living at Asheim in 1726. He was not an heir of Johannes Nielsen; neither that estate nor the widow's of 1728 names a Gullov among the children. ¶10

Surety in 1738 (context only): at the betrothal of Brand Mathisen Mollisdal and Bertha Larsdatter of Asheim at Brekke on Dom. XII p. Trin. 1738 (married 10 Sep 1738), one cautioner was 'Gulliug [mark] Osseim', very probably he, as the only Gullov known at Asheim (11441 canvas 10). ¶8

Probate: on 29 Apr 1740 a probate (skifte, estate division) was held on the farm Asseim after '[afdøde Enke?]mand Gullov Johanssen Asheim' (the late widower[?] Gullov Johansen of Asseim), whose estate was divided among his children: the son Philippus Dahle; a 2nd son of Medelfiord, forename read Siur[?] (read Iver[?]); the 1st daughter Johanne, married to Jens Tynning; the 2nd daughter Kari[?], married to Niels[?] Medelfiord; and the '3die Datter Ingeborre gift med Torkel Asheim' (3rd daughter Ingeborg, married to Torkel of Asheim). No spouse is named and no heir is a minor. Present: the lensmann Johannes Eichen; the appraisers were Kolbein and Ole Asheim. Gross estate 64 rd 2 ort 14 sk, debts 17 rd 1 ort. Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll 6a (Digitalarkivet source 24161), fol. 19a-19b, No. 17. The index codes the deceased as a woman ('k'); the text clearly names a man. Ole Asheim, one of the appraisers, is not called an heir or relative. ¶1

Birth and death (inference): his eldest known daughter Johanne was buried 3 Jul 1740 aged 35 (b. about 1705), so he was born before about 1685. He died before 29 Apr 1740, the date of his probate; his burial was not searched. His own father was named Johan or Johannes (from the patronymic). ¶3

Grandson named after him (zoom-): in July 1740 Torchil Monsen Asheim's child 'N. Gullig' (named Gullik), mother Ingeber, was baptised at Brekke, three months after this probate (11441 canvas 18 left). ¶5

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Confidence Confirmed as father of Ingeborg Gullaksdatter (see her page): the 1740 probate, read from the scan, names his 3rd daughter Ingeborre as the wife of Torkel Asheim. no other Torkel/Torkil of Asheim has been found in the Brekke books read for 1739-1744 (the other Torkil seen, of Opdal, had a wife Anne) and his wife was Ingeber; her own 1754 probate calls her Ingebor Gullovsdatter and names a maternal uncle Gullovsen as guardian; and the couple named a son Gullig in Jul 1740. ¶6a

Records that disagree 1

Against: (1) in the 1701 census this man already heads a household on Asseim with his son 'Phillipus Gullachs[en]' aged 3 (born about 1698), and the 1740 probate of his wife Sigri Rognaldsdatter lists Philippus among her children with him; a son of Gullov and Sigri born about 1698 cannot be fitted around a marriage to Agate that lasted until her death, childless, in 1700, unless Philippus was born out of wedlock or the census age is wrong; (2) no Gullov or Gullak Johansen is listed on Indre Oppedal in the 1701 census. So the 1700 widower is probably a different Gullov Johansen, and no son-of-Johannes link is made, not even as Speculative. Had it been made, his daughter Ingeborg and her husband Torkild Monsen (son of Mons Johansen) would have been first cousins. ¶15b

Corrections 1

Correction (applied): in the 1740 probate No. 17 (Ytre Sogn 6a, fol. 19a) the word read '[afdøde Enke?]mand' is 'Dannemand' (honest man, a customary epithet): 'efter [one word unread] Dannemand Gullov Johanssen Asheim'. He is therefore not called a widower; No. 17 is his own probate, dated 29 Apr 1740. The image was; the word before 'Dannemand' is still unread. ¶9

Alternatives considered and set aside 3

A Gullov Johansen at Indre Oppedal in 1700 (not linked): on 8 Oct [1700] a probate was held on Indre Oppedal after 'Sal. Aagaathe Mons daatter' (the late Agate Monsdatter) between her widower 'Gullowe Johansen [Indre?]' and her father 'Mons [?] Lo[r]fsnes[?]'; no children; gross 59 rd 2 ort, debts 53 rd, the remaining 6 rd divided between the husband and her father. Creditors include Mons Johansen, Lars Johansen and Hindrich Iversen of the Indre Oppedal families. Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll 1b, fol. 257b-258b. ¶14

Identity with this Gullov of Asseim, and parentage, not linked: it is tempting to make the 1700 widower this man and a son of Johannes Larsen of Indre Oppedal (see his page): same name and patronymic, born about 1667, against Johannes born about 1631, and Mons and Lars Johansen are creditors in Agate's estate. ¶15a

Not the Gullov Johansen of Indre Oppedal (1700 widower of Agate Monsdatter): the same bygdebok (Indre Oppedal gnr. 47, canvas 71) has Agate Monsdtr. (1669-1700) married to a son of Johannes Larsson of Indre Oppedal, which fits the reading; this Gullak of Asheim is given different parents. ¶17

Open questions and the next record to read 2

Next records: the heading of estate No. 16 (24161 view 18) for his wife's name; fol. 20a for the shares of Kari and Ingeborg. ¶6b

Would settle it: a high-zoom read of Agate's probate heading (1b fol. 257b, view 42) for the widower's farm, any later record of Gullowe Johansen of Indre Oppedal, or a record naming Gullov of Asseim's father. ¶15c
