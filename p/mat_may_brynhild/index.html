About the family

David Erdahl's maternal great-great-great-grandmother on the Eidem-of-Union-County side, the mother of Sivert 'Severt' S. Eidem. Reached 11 Oct 2026. ¶1

Her patronymic Jonsdatter means 'daughter of Jon'. Note that a second Brynhild - Brynhild Eriksdatter Balstad Eidem (1843-1913) - is buried in the same cemetery and is a different woman. ¶4

Her patronymic in that census is JONSDATTER, as her husband's is Jonsen - two unrelated Jons. The byname Langli already held for her is a farm name and the 1865 census does not give it; the Selbu gaardshistorie's Langli chapter is where to confirm it. ¶19

Life story

Her byname Langli is a farm name, as Eidem is; her memorial carries it as 'Brynhild Jonsdtr Langli Eidem', the Norwegian patronymic Jonsdatter (daughter of Jon) with the farm she came from. Rated Probable for the same reason as her husband: Find a Grave family links plus a gravestone, not yet a parish register. ¶2

Mayme (Eidem) Eidem's paternal grandmother, named as the mother of Severt S. Eidem on his Find a Grave memorial, where she appears as Brynhild Jonsdtr Langli Eidem. Langli is a farm name in Selbu; the place of birth is inferred from the byname and from her husband's and son's Selbu origin, and has not been read in a parish register, so this is rated Probable. ¶3

Baptism: born 23 NOVEMBER 1818 and baptised in Selbu church on 29 NOVEMBER 1818 - Selbu parish register, Selbu local parish 1816-1824, 'Foedte Qvindekjoen', PAGE 63, SERIAL NO. 32. Child 'Brynhild Johnsd Langlie'; parents 'Gaardmand Joh[n] Nielsen Langlie og Hustrue MARIT JOHNSD'. THE BIRTH DATE IS THE DATE ON HER ELK POINT GRAVESTONE, TO THE DAY - a Norwegian parish clerk in 1818 and an American stonecutter in 1890, with no possible contact between them, give 23 November 1818. ¶9

Godparents (faddere), read from the scanned page and carried by no index: Nils Ol[sen] Langlie (reading uncertain), OLE NILSEN LANGLIE, Brynhild Johnsdatter Langlie, AGNIS NILSDATTER LANGLIE, and a fifth whose name is illegible. Ole Nilsen and Agnis Nilsdatter are two of the children of Niels Olssen and Guri Olsdatter in the 1801 census of Langlie - the baby's own paternal uncle and aunt - which corroborates her grandparents independently of the census. ¶10

This baptism names her mother, MARIT JOHNSDATTER of Langli, who appears in no other record found so far. ¶11

1825 census: residence 0047* Langlie, household 01, person 015 - 'Brynhild Johnsd.', 8, 'Datter', with her parents John Nilsen (42, husmand) and Marit Johnsdatter (42), her brothers Nils (11) and John (3), and her grandmother, the widow Guri Olsdatter (70). Age conflict: 8 implies a birth about 1817, but her baptism gives her birth as 23 November 1818, so she was about 7 at the census. The register is preferred; the ages of the other children in the household are also one to two years high. ¶25

Marriage: married in Selbu church on 8 NOV 1841 to Sivert Johnsen Eidem, aged 23. The entry (Selbu parish register 1824-1842, marriages, page 339, serial 30) enters her as 'Pigen Brynnild Johnsdatter LANGLIE', birth year 1818 (calculated), birthplace 'Saelboe', and names her father as JOHN NIELSEN LANGLIE - who also stood as one of the two forlovere (witnesses) at the wedding, a detail legible only on the scan. ¶12

Langli proved a farm name: the register writes both her own byname and her father's as 'Langlie', and she is 'Brynhild Johnsd Langlie' in her baptism at seven days old. Langli is a farm in Selbu - gnr 41, 42 and 43 in the modern numbering - as are Eidem, Slind, Stokke and Kulsetmo, the other farm names documented in this tree. She was a Selbu girl of the Langli farms who married into the Eidem farms two or three miles away. ¶13

1865 census: at oestre Eidem, Selbu, loepenr 62, entered as 'Brynild Jonsdatter', aged 48, 'hans Kone' (his wife), with five children in the house - John 24, Sigri 17, Marit 15, Ole 10 and Sivert 7 - her husband's widowed mother Sigri Bersvendsdatter, 81, and an elderly unmarried kinswoman. ¶14

Found in Norway, 12 Oct 2026 (slind-deep agent). The 1865 census of Selbu, rural residence 0058 ostre Eidem, household 1, person 002: 'Brynild Jonsdatter, 48, hans Kone, g' - wife of the freehold farmer Sivert Jonsen, aged 48, which gives a birth about 1817-18 and agrees with the 23 November 1818 already held for her. Her five children in that household are John 24, Sigri 17, Marit 15, Ole 10 and Sivert 7. ¶18

1870 census: as 'Emma', 52, keeping house, wife in the household of 'Seivert Johnson', 55, on Brule Creek, Union County, Dakota Territory, 23 August 1870 (page 10, lines 20-25), with Sarah 21, Mary 19, Ole 14 and Seivert 11 - her children Sigri, Marit, Ole and Sivert. 'Emma' is the enumerator's rendering of Brynhild: the household matches the 1865 Selbu census name for name and in order, her age is right for a birth in November 1818, and she lived until 1890, so there is no room for a second wife. Her first record in America. ¶24

Land patent as a widow, 1873: her husband Sivert Johnson Eidem died 15 April 1872, and fourteen months later, on 10 JUNE 1873, the United States issued a land patent in her own name - entered in the General Land Office index as "BRYNEL JOHNSON EIDEM", the clerk's rendering of the Norwegian patronymic Brynhild Jonsdatter - for the SOUTH HALF OF THE SOUTH-EAST QUARTER OF SECTION 1 AND THE NORTH HALF OF THE NORTH-EAST QUARTER OF SECTION 12, TOWNSHIP 92 NORTH, RANGE 50 WEST OF THE 5TH PRINCIPAL MERIDIAN, Union County, Dakota Territory. Document number 260, accession STA-SD1050__.243. ¶5

Context, why a widow could patent: a homestead or pre-emption entry did not lapse when the entryman died. The widow could complete the required residence and cultivation and take the patent in her own name, and that is what happened here. It is also why her name, and not her dead husband's, is the one in the federal land records. ¶6

Land, with her son: her patent is document 260 and her eldest son John Sivertson Eidem's is document 259, issued the SAME DAY, 10 June 1873, for land in the same section 1 of T92N R50W. Mother and son proved up together at the Vermillion land office. This is independent documentary corroboration, from the land side, of the mother-son relationship. ¶7

1880 census: widowed and head of her own household in Brule Township, Union County, Dakota Territory, enumerated 4 June 1880 as 'JOHNSON, Brynhild', white, female, 62, keeping house, born Norway of Norwegian parents, with her sons Ole 23, farmer, and Sivert 20, 'working on railroad' (ED 4, sheet 393D, lines 30-32, dwelling and family 34). There is a tick in one of the 'cannot read / cannot write' columns against her name; the FamilySearch index gives her marital status as widowed. Age 62 fits her November 1818 baptism (she was 61 on the census day). ¶26

1880 census, the sons placed: Ole 23 is her son Ole Sivertsen, baptised 12 October 1856 (see his page), who was in her household in the 1865 Selbu census (10) and the 1870 Union County census (14); Sivert 20 is Severt S. Eidem (see his page). The Ole of the 1880 entry is not Ole Sivertson Eidem of Elk Point (see his page), who was still in Selbu in the 1875 census. ¶28

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Confidence raised from Probable to Confirmed: her own baptism names both parents and gives a birth date identical to her gravestone, and the 1841 marriage entry and the 1865 census household place her with her husband and children. ¶17

Confidence raised from Probable to Confirmed: her son's obituary names her, Leader-Courier (Elk Point, South Dakota), 1 August 1940 - 'His parents were Sivert Johnson Eidem and BRYN[H]ILD JOHNSDATTER EIDEM.' The obituary writes the patronymic Johnsdatter where this file has Jonsdatter; they are the same name. ¶21

Records that disagree 1

Contradiction between her gravestone and the Norwegian register, recorded: the stone at Saint Paul Cemetery, Elk Point, is cut 'BRUNHILD JOHNSON EIDEM, FODT 15 SEP 1820, DOD 15 FEB 1890'. This file carries her birth as 23 November 1818 from the Selbu baptism of 29 November 1818. An American stone cut forty-eight years after emigration is weaker than a baptismal entry, so the 1818 date stands and the stone is noted as wrong by two years and three months. ¶22

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Caution for the land records: the Union County homestead patent to 'Brynel Johnson Eidem' (document 260) is more likely her daughter-in-law's than hers. See the note on Brynild Eriksdatter (see her page), John Sivertson's wife, whose Selbu marriage of 3 April 1866 is now documented and who was also a widow in that county. ¶16

Searches that found nothing 2

She is NOT in the 1875 census of Selbu. Every Brynild and Brynhild in the parish born between 1814 and 1822 was listed and checked: Andersdatter b. 1818 of Setsaas, Jonsdatter b. 1814 of Mebust oevre, Pedersdatter Hoeiaas b. 1817 of Eggahaugen, Johnsdatter b. 1820 of Meraaker, Pedersdatter b. 1816 of Lekvold, Sivertsdatter b. 1821 of Vonhellen, Pedersdatter b. 1820 of Samstadberget, Evensdatter b. 1818 of Sirmoen and Olsdatter b. 1821 of Lilletroeen. None of them is hers. With her husband dead in Union County by 15 April 1872, she had emigrated with the family and was widowed in America, surviving him by eighteen years. ¶15

1880 census, why surname searches failed: the household is entered under the patronymic JOHNSON, not Eidem - Brynhild's own patronymic Johnsdatter (Jonsdatter), which the enumerator also gave to her sons. Searches for Eidem in the 1880 census of Union County will not find it. ¶27
