About the family

The head of that 1865 household is Sivert Jonsen b. 1815, so she is his mother, the wife of John Sivertsen Eidem, and the great-grandmother of Severt S. Eidem of Elk Point, South Dakota. ¶2

Parentage - why it is Confirmed. (1) Her own baptism of 6 Aug 1786 names both parents, Bersvend Sivertsen of Draxen and Lisbet Torkelsdatter. (2) That couple's 1801 household at Draxen holds a Siri aged 14, the right age to the month. (3) In 1812 Jon Sivertsen of Eidem married the maiden Siri Bersvendsdatter of Draxen. (4) At the couple's first child's baptism, 25 Oct 1812, two of the five godparents were Sivert Bersvensen Draxen and Helge Bersvensd. Draxen - by name and farm the Sivert (17) and Helge (12) of the same 1801 household, i.e. her brother and sister. (5) The 1815 baptism of her son Sivert names his mother 'Hustrue Siri Bersvends d:', and the 1825 census of Eidem gives Jon's wife as Sigrid Bersvendsdatter, 39 - again b. 1786. (6) She was buried in 1872 as Sigrid Bersvendsdatter, kaarenke, of Eidem, aged 87. Every link names her, her farm or her siblings; the ages in 1801 and 1825 agree exactly with the 1786 baptism. ¶9

Children with Jon Sivertsen: Helge (b. 12 Oct 1812), Sivert (b. 20 Aug 1815), Anders (b. 28 Jun 1820) and Marit (b. 18 Sep 1823). ¶13

Her parents' marriage: Bersvend Sivertsen and Lisbet Torkelsdatter were betrothed on 1 March and married on 21 June 1778 in Selbu church (source 16311, canvases 87 and 89). Neither parent's baptism survives - Selbu has no baptism register for 1747-1756, the years in which both were born - so their parentage must come from the confirmation lists of about 1764-1775 in the same book or from probate after 1797. ¶14

Life story

Baptism: Selbu, 8th Sunday after Trinity 1786, which fell on 6 August (Easter 1786 = 16 April). 'Doebt Sigri Par: Bersv. Siurs. Drax: Lisbete Torch: T. Olle Ped. vig, Jon Egg, Marit Drax., Berthe Varmd[al], Lisb. Ham:' - baptised Sigri, parents Bersvend Siursen (Sivertsen) of Draxen and Lisbet Torkelsdatter; godparents (T. = testes) Ole Pedersen Vig, Jon Eggen, Marit Draxen, Berthe Varmdal and Lisbet Hamar[?]. Her mother was churched on 3 Sep 1786. Read from the scan (source 16311, canvas 123). The birth date is not stated; this replaces the estimate 'about 1784'. ¶6

1801 census: 'Siri', aged 14, daughter in household 04 of Draxen, Selbu - Bersvend Sivertsen 45 and Lisbet Torkelsdatter 46, with her brothers and sisters Marit 21, Sivert 17, Helge 12, Peder 10, Beret 5 and a younger Siri aged 1. Siri, Sigri and Sigrid are one name in Selbu usage. ¶7

Marriage: banns were called in Selbu church on the 3rd and 4th Sundays after Easter and on Ascension Day 1812 (19 April, 26 April and 7 May) for 'Jon Siverthsen Eidem og Pigen Sirie Bersvensd: Draxen'. The sureties (forlovere, here 'Caut.'), who also attested that both had had smallpox, were Erich Olsen Eidem and Christen Olsen of the same farm. They were married on the 9th Sunday after Trinity, 26 July 1812: 'Ungkarl... Jon Siverthsen Eidem med Pigen Siri Bersvensd: Draxen' - bachelor and maiden (pige). Read from the scans (source 16312, canvases 169-171 and 175). ¶8

Rejected candidates for the 1812 bride. The other Siri at Draxen in 1801, aged 6 in household 02 (daughter of Bersvend Olssen and Karen Olsdatter), married Ellev Nilsen in Selbu on 17 July 1818 as 'Pigen Siri Bersvendsd. Draxen, 23 Aar' - still a maiden six years after our marriage, so she is not Jon's wife. The younger Siri of household 04, aged 1 in 1801 (b. about 1800), would have been about 12 in 1812; she is probably the Sigrid Bersvendsdatter b. 1800 who was a wife at Uglem in 1825 and was buried in 1877 aged 77. The 1786 Sigri was evidently still living in 1801, when her parents gave the same name to a new baby - an unusual but not unknown practice. ¶10

Named in three records fifty years apart. 1815: 'Hustrue Siri Bersvends d:' in the baptism of her son Sivert, Selbu parish register 1801-1815, page 221 no. 2, read from the scan. 1825: 'Sigrid Bersvendsd.', aged 39, Hustru, in the household of Joh Siverts., gaardmand at Eidem. 1865: 'Sigri Bersvendsdatter', aged 81, birthplace Selbo Prgj., family position 'Husfaderens Moder' - the head of the household's mother - marital status 'e' = enke, widow, occupation 'Foederaadslem', at rural residence 0058 oestre Eidem. ¶1

Her patronymic names her own father: a BERSVEND, an uncommon Trondelag given name and so a strong handle for pushing this line back one more step. Her brother is in all probability the PEDER BERSVENDSEN DRAXEN who stood godfather at her son Sivert's baptism in August 1815. ¶3

Foederaadslem: a person living on the farm's foederaad, the provision of board and lodging for life that was written into a Norwegian farm deed when the holding was handed to the next generation. She was in her eighties, a widow, living in the same house as her son, her daughter-in-law and five grandchildren, one of whom was the six-year-old Sivert who would die at Elk Point, South Dakota in 1940. ¶4

Aged 39 at the 1825 census and 81 at the 1865 census, she was born about 1784-86; the 1865 enumerator of this district entered ages about a year high throughout the household. ¶5

Probate: she is the widow 'Sigrid Bersvendsdatter' in the skifte after her husband John Sivertsen Eidum, closed 8 November 1832 (skifteutlodningsprotokoll 1, fol. 129b-130). The farm Eidum (matrikkel no. 120, valued at 500 speciedaler) was laid out to her, and she kept the movables. The skifte does not name her father. ¶15

Second marriage: 21 Jun 1832 in Selbu to Peder Thommesen Eidne (b. 1802), as 'Siri Bersvendsdtr Eiden', aged 46, bride's father 'Bersvend Siverts.' (16314 p. 319 no. 20). This also names her father again, agreeing with her 1786 baptism. Widowed again before 23 Feb 1854. In 1865 she lived at østre Eidem, aged 81, as 'Husfaderens Moder', widow, føderaadslem (retired on maintenance) with her son Sivert. ¶18

Widow's settlement, 1854: on 23 February 1854, with her legal guardian (lavverge) Sivert Monsen Voldsæth (reading uncertain), she and her children conveyed Eidem to her eldest son Sivert. The same day Sivert granted her a kårbrev (retirement contract) for life: a room in the kår house, firewood, a horse to church, care in sickness and old age, winter fodder for a cow, potatoes, milk, one speciedaler each March and a garden plot. The deed values her føderåd at 25 speciedaler a year. She signed with a guided hand (m.p.p.). ¶16

The kår stayed in force after Sivert sold the farm in 1866; the buyer took it over. The land register cancelled it in 1874 'efter Attest om hendes Død', on a certificate of her death (Panteregister 19 fol. 117b), which is consistent with her death in 1872. ¶17

Death and burial: the Selbu register for 1872 (folio 41, no. 21), read from the scan, enters 'Kaarenke Sigrid Bersvendsdr', aged 87, of Eidem, died 23 February and buried 3 March 1872. A kaarenke is a widow living on a kaar - the board-and-lodging pension reserved in a farm deed (the foederaad of the 1865 census). This replaces 'after 31 December 1865'. Her true age was 85; the 1865 census (81) and the burial (87) both overstate it, as is common for the very old, while the 1801 (14) and 1825 (39) ages fit her 1786 baptism exactly. ¶12

Corrections 1

Correction: the earlier working hypothesis (H1 in the line notes) made her a daughter of Bersvend Pederssen and Kiersten Ingebrigtsdatter of Eidem (see his page, Kiersten Ingebrigtsdatter; line 7), and so a sister of Peder Bersvendsen of Eidem, which would have made her line and line 7 share ancestors. Her baptism, her 1801 household and her marriage entry all place her at Draxen as the daughter of Bersvend Sivertsen and Lisbet Torkelsdatter; Bersvend Pederssen's 1801 household at Eidum contains no Siri. H1 is retired. The godfather 'Peder Bersvends: Draxen' at her son Sivert's baptism in 1815 is therefore most likely her own brother Peder, b. about 1791, of Draxen household 04 - though a second Peder of the same age lived in Draxen household 02. ¶11
