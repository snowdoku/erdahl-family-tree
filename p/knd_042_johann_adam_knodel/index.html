Life story

Marriage found: 'Johann Adam Koodel' (= Knodel), son of Georg, married Anna Margaretha 'Bechers' (Becher), daughter of Joh. Jacob, at Vaihingen an der Enz on 15 Nov 1703 (groom row 4776330, relatives 'Georg, Anna Margaretha'; bride row 904776330, relatives 'Joh. Jacob, Johann Adam'; image 1184562-00486). Continuity: Joh. Adam Knodel x Anna Margaretha baptise at Vaihingen from 21 Sep 1705 (Elisabetha Margaretha, 4768221), then Maria Barbara 'Keddel' 20 Jul 1708 (4768381), Georg Jacob 11 Jul 1711 (4768553) and Johannes 26 Oct 1714 (4768768); no other Johann Adam Knodel married at Vaihingen. The marriage names his father Georg, but both baptism candidates (Vaihingen 4 Jul 1679, Georg x Elisabethe, 4767377; Ensingen 1 Apr 1683, Georg x Catharina, 5148106) have a father Georg, so it does not decide between them; his mother is therefore not entered. His own grade (on Georg Jacob) is unchanged. ¶6

Probably born at Vaihingen an der Enz on 4 Jul 1679 to Georg Knodel x Elisabethe (record 4767377). The rival baptism (Ensingen, 1 Apr 1683, Georg x Catharina, 5148106) is ruled out by that child's burial at Ensingen on 1 Jun 1683 (5150844). He died at Vaihingen 3 Nov 1761 (4405534); the index gives no age. Birth date entered as Probable (identification by elimination). ¶7

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Upgrade: Speculative to Probable, graded on his link to his son Georg Jacob Knodel (see his page). The obstacle in the earlier note (was Christoph Adam's father the Georg Jacob who married in 1737?) is removed by FamilySearch QPGK-HRCY: Christoph Adam Knodel, born Vaihingen 4 Oct 1740, mother Susanna Margaretha, her husband Georg Jacob Knodel, i.e. the couple of the 3 Aug 1737 Vaihingen marriage (QPGK-7T8Y; Ancestry 61023 row 4777562). That groom row reads 'Georg Jacob Knodel', relatives 'Adam, Susanna Margaretha' (father, then bride; image 1184562-00524), and the only Georg Jacob Knodel baptised at Vaihingen in the index is the child of 11 Jul 1711, parents 'Joh. Adam, Anna Margaretha' (row 4768553; re-paged: Vaihingen Knodel births 1700-1720 are Elisabetha Margaretha 1705, Georg Jacob 1711, Johannes 1714, all of Johann Adam x Anna Margaretha). Two independent rows thus point to Adam. Still Probable, not Confirmed: the 61023 rows do not label roles (BRIEF audit 5 rule 3), and the 1711 child is identified with the 1737 groom by name, parish and the father's name only. A labelled FamilySearch twin of the 1737 marriage giving the groom's father would confirm. His own marriage to Anna Margaretha (about 1700-05) is not indexed under Knodel; his baptism remains undecided between Vaihingen 4 Jul 1679 (Georg x Elisabethe, 4767377) and Ensingen 1 Apr 1683 (Georg x Catharina, 5148106). ¶5

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed, graded on his link to his son Georg Jacob Knodel (see his page). FamilySearch, 'Deutschland, ausgewählte evangelische Kirchenbücher 1500-1971' (= 'Germany, Lutheran Baptisms, Marriages, and Burials, 1500-1971'), QPGK-7T87: marriage at Vaihingen an der Enz 3 Aug 1737, groom 'Georg Jacob Knodel', field Parents = 'Adam Knodels', spouse 'Susanna Margaretha Schämayers'. The groom is the tree's Georg Jacob. His birth is labelled too: QPGK-DBXQ, Georg Jacob Knodel, born Vaihingen 11 Jul 1711, father 'Joh. Adam Knodel' (mother Anna Margaretha). Death, labelled: QPGK-2X5H, Johann Adam Knodel, died Vaihingen 3 Nov 1761, Age '82 years' (Notizen u Tote 1609-1827, page 651), which fits the Vaihingen birth of 4 Jul 1679 (QPGK-XCVT, Parents 'Georg Knodel, Elisabethe') and not the Ensingen twin of 1683 (died 1683). Read signed in, 2 Nov 2026; labelled index fields, image not seen (partner-only). ¶8

The case for this identification 1

Identity (Speculative): father of the Georg Jacob Knodel born at Vaihingen an der Enz on 11 July 1711 (index row 4768553, parents 'Joh. Adam, Anna Margaretha') and of Johannes born there 26 October 1714 (row 4768768). He is plausibly the 'Adam' named as the groom's father at the 1737 Vaihingen marriage of a Georg Jacob Knodel (row 4777562). He is entered as the father of Georg Jacob Knodel only on the Speculative identification of that man with the 1711 child (see Georg Jacob's 'Possible birth' note). Generation count upward from Christoph Ludwig Knodel (1765): Christoph Adam (Probable), Georg Jacob (Probable), Johann Adam (Speculative), so this is the first Speculative generation on this path; at most one further Speculative generation may be added above him, never a third (chain rule). The 1711 birth image (1184562-00144) would give his trade and the sponsors. ¶1

Alternatives considered and set aside 2

Possible death, not linked: a Johann Adam Knodel died at Vaihingen an der Enz on 3 November 1761 (row 4405534, no age or relatives indexed). Other Johann Adam Knodels lived at Ensingen in the same years, so this is not tied to him. The image (1184565-00336) would give the age. ¶2

Possible death (not entered): a Johann Adam Knodel died at Vaihingen an der Enz on 3 Nov 1761 (Ancestry 61023 record 4405534, image 1184565-00336; no relatives or age indexed). If he is the Johann Adam born at Vaihingen on 4 Jul 1679 (son of Georg and Elisabethe, record 4767377), he was 82. The image would give the age and settle which of the two baptism candidates (Vaihingen 1679 or Ensingen 1683) is this man. ¶4

Searches that found nothing 1

Birth: not found; presumably before about 1690 (inferred from a son born in 1711). ¶3
