Life story

Bygdebok, Speculative parents: Hjelmeland: gardar og folk 2, Tuntland chapter (canvas 378): Peder Erikson 'g m Marta Steinsdtr. f ca. 1717 [frå] Årdal 10, d 1782'. Årdal 2: Gardane og folket, gnr 10 (canvas 682): '10. Stein [patronymic lost] [frå] Fister, f ca. 1688, g m Marta Jonsdtr. f ca. 1688, d 1754; in the 1701 manntall Stein sits on the whole [farm]'; children in the rebuilt list: Nils f 1710, Anna f 1720 (m. Henrik Henrikson, no. 11), Stein f 1730, Torborg; the 1754 skifte after Marta names 'Stein Steinson 20 år, Gunhild...'. Marta (b. about 1717) is NOT visible in that rebuilt child list, so the link rests on the Hjelmeland book's cross-reference 'Årdal 10' plus her patronymic and age fitting the only Stein who held Årdal gnr 10 in the period (the earlier no. 9 Stein, b. about 1650, is the other candidate). Speculative. Rebuilt from nb.no content-search word boxes (harvests and ar2.json, 3,011-word vocabulary); dates from the rebuild are given as ABT. ¶8a

Probate (card): Ryfylke probate card index 1774-1788 (DA 39936, view 441), Tuntland i Hjelmeland, protocol side 396b, 16 Nov 1785: deceased Marthe Steensdatter, wife of Peder Eriksen. Her heirs are her children 1. Erik (of age), 2. Sten (married) and 3. Mallene (married). The estate held 2 løp smør in the farm; gross estate 496 rd. This proves she is the mother of Mallene Pedersdatter of Tuntland, who married Niels Olsen of Øye in 1775. She died before 16 Nov 1785. Added. ¶2

Probate entry read: Ryfylke skifteprotokoll D 7b (DA 23892), fol. 296b, view 9 (binarised scan, read in zoomed crops): 'Anno 1782 den 16de Novbr.' a registration and valuation was held on the farm Tuntland in Hjelmeland skipreide after the deceased woman 'Marthe Steensdatter', between her surviving husband ('Ectemand') 'Peder Erichsen' and their children: Erich Pedersen, of age (myndig); Steen Pedersen, married; and 'Mallene Pedersdatter', married. The valuation men were lagrettemenn. The gross estate, written in the next column, is 496 rd, matching the card; the closing lines name the widower Peder Erichsen again. Mallene's husband is not named in the parts that could be read. This is the primary record behind the card: it names Peder Erichsen and Marthe Steensdatter of Tuntland as the parents of a married daughter Mallene Pedersdatter in 1782, seven years after 'Mallene Peders Datter Tuntland' married Niels Olsen of Øye (Hjelmeland marriages 1775, p. 240, no. 6). Marthe died before 16 Nov 1782, so Peder's second marriage to Berthe Pedersdatter (d. by 1788) falls between 1782 and 1788. ¶4

Corrections 1

Correction: the probate card (DA 39936, view 441) reads 'side 296b' and '1782 16/11', not 396b and 1785; the card-writer's 2 looks like a 5 or 3 (compare '2. Sten' on the same card). Ryfylke skifteprotokoll D 7b (DA 23892) fol. 396b (view 110) holds two unrelated 1785 entries, while fol. 296b (view 9) holds the Tuntland entry, headed 'Anno 1782 den 16de Novbr.'. ¶3

Searches that found nothing 3

Search note: two Steens in the Ryfylke probate protocol D 4 (1719-1732) were read as possible fathers, and both are ruled out. (1) Steen Hemnes, Hemnes in Jelsa, fol. 283b, Feb 1728: the widow is Giertrud Evertsdatter and the heirs named on the other side are Anne Olsdatter and Maale Olsdatter (with husbands Ole Haaffsen and Haavel Nielsen), not daughters of his; no Marthe. (2) Steen Joensen, Tvede in Jelsa, fol. 285b, 5 Feb 1728: widow [?Helene] Rasmusdatter; one son Joen Steensen (3) and six daughters, including 'Marthe Steensdatter gift med Peder [?Steffensen]', then Eli (22, unmarried), Malene (18), Ingeri, Britte (8) and Kirsti (5). That Marthe was already married to another Peder in 1728, so she was born by about 1708, a generation older than Peder Erichsen (b. about 1730); she is not this Marthe. The typed register of D 4 (views 3-8, A-Ø) has no other Steen as deceased in Hjelmeland. Her father's probate, if any, should be in D 5 onwards (1732-), or she came from outside Ryfylke's indexed volumes. ¶5

Search note: no Steen appears as deceased in Hjelmeland skipreide in the typed register to the Ryfylke probate designations 1731-1754 (DA 23971, R-T sections read), and the 1762 ekstraskatt household on Tuntland has no Steen. The index has no marriage of a Marthe Steensdatter 1740-1758 anywhere in Norway, and Hjelmeland has no church book before 1760. Her father remains unknown. Untried: the rest of the 23971 register (A-Q, U-Ø) for a Steen in Hjelmeland, Jelsa or Årdal, and the register to D 11 (1757-1766, DA 23973). ¶6

Search note: the whole typed register to the Ryfylke designations 1731-1754 (DA 23971, letters A-Ø, urns...670101-136) and the whole register to the designations 1757-1766 (DA 23973, urns...670165-198) were OCR'd and searched. No Steen is a deceased in Hjelmeland, Jelsa or Årdal skipreide in either register (Steens elsewhere: Osmund Steensen, Selen under Vaage, Hetland; Steener Olsen, Vaage, Skare). One possible lead: 'Giertrud Steensdatter' died at Fossaaen (Fossan), Hjelmeland, probate 20 Sep 1759 (D 11, DA 23888, fol. 27, scan read); the designation lists only her minor son 'Aaser Biørnsen' aged 5 1/2, so her siblings are not named there. She could be a sister of Marthe (same patronymic, same skipreide, same generation), but nothing ties them; not added. ¶7a

Open questions and the next record to read 4

Next: the full 1759 Fossan probate in the Ryfylke skifteprotokoll for 1757-1766 (the protocol behind D 11), for Giertrud's heirs and any 'Marthe Steensdatter, Peder Erichsen Tuntlands hustru' as a sister or aunt of the child. A second lead from the Ryfylke probate cards 1682-1700 (DA 39935, urn...670314, card 'Vatland i Jelsø', 12 Oct 1696, fol. 61): after Bergette Joensdatter (husband apparently Sevat Naversen), heirs 1. Steen, 2. Gunele, 3. Maritte. This Steen of Vatland, Jelsa, born before 1696, is of the right generation to be Marthe's father (Marthe married Peder Erichsen, b. about 1730), but no record links them; not added. The 1701 manntall for Ryfylke (DA 38452, index searched for St*n/Sten*) has a 'Sten Sevatsen' aged 17 on Byre in Fister, Hjelmeland parish, who is probably this heir. But the same list has several other Steens of the right age in Hjelmeland and Jelsa: Sten Rejersen 12 (Nor Knudsvig), Sten Larsen 37 (Klefveland), Steen Steensen 9 (under Sigmundstad, Fister), Steen Olsen 24 (Hemnes), Steen Jonsen 42 (Tvedene; died 1728, his daughter Marthe already married then), Steen Larsen 12 (Berreqvam), Steen Thollachsen 24 (under Viig), Sten Jensen 22 (Marvig) and Steen Danielsen 4 (under Sandene). With so many rivals none is a candidate yet. ¶7b
