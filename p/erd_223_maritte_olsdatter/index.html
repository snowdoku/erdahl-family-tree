Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Graded Probable on her link to her son Anfind (Arnfinn) Olsen (see his page). The probate after her husband Ole Anfindsen of Bale (Ytre Sogn 8a fol. 211b, No. 101, Oct 1764) names her as his first wife, 'den sal. Kone Maritte Olsdatter', by whom he had '1 Søn ved Navn Anfind Olsen myndig'. She was dead before his second marriage (his 2nd wife Ingrid Knutsdotter died in 1736, bygdebok c78). The bygdebok's first-wife line is only partly recovered ('[...] Olsdotter'). Identification of the 1764 heir with the tree's Anfind Olsen of Kalvetræet (Arnfinn (Anfind) Olsen Bale) rests on the Balestrand bygdebok, which calls him 'Arnfinn Olson Bale' (c132, came to his place in 1757, b. 1724, d. 1800) and lists Arnfinn among Ola Arnfinnson's children ('Av borni hans Ola... Arnfinn... døydde i 1800', c78), and on the 1764 probate's note that Anfind Olsen had received goods at his marriage (fol. 212a), consistent with a son already married off the farm (m. 1757). It spans two places (Bale 1764, Kalvetræet under Tjugum 1781) and other Anfind Olsens lived in Tjugum skipreide (Anfind Olsen Hov d. 1795, Anfind Olsen Sætre d. 1785), so the link is Probable. ¶1a

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Test record: a deed or tingbok entry naming Anfind Olsen of Kalvetræet as Ole Anfindsen's son, or Anfind Olsen's own probate c.1800 if one exists (not in the Ytre Sogn index under Kalvetreet, Tjugum or Bale). ¶1b
