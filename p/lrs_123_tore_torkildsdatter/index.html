Life story

Marriage: on the 3rd Easter Day (1 Apr 1766) 'Pigen Tore Torkilds Datter' (the maiden Tore Torkildsdatter) was betrothed in Drangedal to the widower Alf Halvorsen Toe; cautioners Torr Fraae and Joen Dale; married 12 Jul 1766. Drangedal ministerialbok 1697-1767, p. 43. She was a maiden then, which fits 'widow in her first marriage' in 1801. ¶4

1801 census: Tore Torkildsdatter, 64 (b. 1737 per index), widow in her first marriage (Enke 1te gang), living at Thoe, Drangedal, as the mother of the householder (Mandens moder) John Alfsen, 46, farmer, whose wife was Kari Gun[nars?]datter, 52. 1801 census for 0817P Drangedal, person pf01058307001979 (index; scan not read). ¶1

1801 'Mandens moder': the census calls her the mother of the householder John Alfsen, 46. Unless his age is wrong by more than ten years, he was born about 1755 to Alf's first wife, and she was his stepmother. Children of Alf and Tore born after 1766 have not been searched for. ¶7

Burial (Probable;, index row only): 'Thore Terkildsd.', enke (widow), residence Tho, birth year 1734 in the index, buried in Drangedal on 28 Oct 1804 (Drangedal church book 1768-1814, source 7850, index pg00000001577811; the record page and the scan were not read). Identity inferred from the name (Terkild is a spelling of Torkild), the farm Tho and her status as a widow: Alf Halvorsen of Tho, her husband, was buried 1 Feb 1801. Age conflict: the index birth year 1734 disagrees with her 1801 census age of 64 (b. about 1737) by three years; the birth field stays ABT 1737 from the census. The record page or the scan of the burial (with the age as written) would settle the identity and the age. ¶8

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Speculative mother of Gunleik Alvsen (b. 1761): see Gunleik's notes. Her age fits a mother of children born 1755-1764 (about 18 to 27). ¶3

Confidence raised from Speculative to Probable: the 1766 marriage record now names her as Alf Halvorsen Toe's wife. That the bride of 1766 is the 1801 widow at Thoe rests on the name, the farm and her age (b. about 1737); no conflicting Tore Torkildsdatter has been seen. ¶6

Corrections 1

Correction: she is no longer Gunleik Alvsen's mother. She married Alf in 1766, five years after Gunleik's baptism (Aug 1761), so she was his stepmother. His mother was Alf's first wife, Aasil Jonsdatter (see her page). ¶5
