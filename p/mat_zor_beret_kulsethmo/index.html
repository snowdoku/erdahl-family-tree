Life story

Gravestone, photographed and read: a rose-granite flat marker, large plain capitals, 'BERET / KULSETHMO / 1841 - 1929'. No 'Mrs.', no relationship line, no month or day on the stone; the South Dakota state burial register supplies 2 July 1929. ¶1

Burial plot: Zoar Lutheran Cemetery, LOT 15 — the same lot as ANDERS A. EIDEM (d. 1932) and BENDA ELVINA EIDEM (d. 1999). Anders was the husband of Brynhild Kulsetmoen and Benda was their daughter. ¶2

Kulsetmo is a Selbu farm. The spelling is unstable even within this one family — Kulsetmoen, Kulsethmo, Kulsethmoe all occur. ¶4

1 on the grade

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Speculative, and stated as such: a Kulsethmo woman born in 1841, buried in the Anders Eidem family lot, is of exactly the right age to be the mother of Brynhild Kulsetmoen Eidem (b. 1870, Selbu). Brynhild came to Marietta a young widow 'at her brother's invitation', which means she had kin here already. No record naming the relationship has been found. She is the only Kulsethmo burial in the whole of South Dakota. ¶3
