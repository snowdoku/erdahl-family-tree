About the family

Parents added, Probable: the test record named in these notes was found and read. The probate after Joen Gundersen at Stokkan, 22 May 1693 (24749 fol. 108a; card 77804 c1885), was held between his widow Kirsti Olsdatter and 'deris barn Ingeborre Joensdatter... Myndig', the only heir. The parents are Probable because the 1693 heiress is linked to Ola Bjørnsen's wife Ingebor (churching 1708, 16310 c35) by first name, farm and the bygdebok. 'Myndig' in 1693 sits uneasily with the bygdebok's birth about 1677 (she would be about 16); the birth year is the book's estimate. Her own grade (link to Jon Olsen Stokke) is unchanged. ¶4

Life story

Burial: Sexagesima 1759 (18 Feb), 'grafsat (1) Ingebor Jonsd: Stockun 82 aar gl.' (Selbu ministerialbok 16311, canvas 7, right page). Age 82 gives birth about 1677, matching the bygdebok. Death set to BEF 18 FEB 1759 from this burial; her own grade is unchanged. ¶5

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
