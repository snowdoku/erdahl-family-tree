Life story

Tenant of Vangberg nedre, Frosta, from 1723 (Frostaboka). Father of Peder Olsen of Breiset (see his page). ¶1

Frostaboka VII, nb.no 00dc33af4fcabfe1f5ce20381f994feb, canvas 543, rebuilt from word boxes harvested to: '[...] Ole Jakobsen [...] fikk bygsel på garden [i] 1723. Døde før 1762. G.m. Gunhild Nilsdtr., død [i] 1765. [...] barn hadde de: Jakob Olsen, f. 1710; [...] Olsen, f. 1712; Kirsti Olsdtr., f. 1717; Peder Olsen, f. 1718, til Breiset; Nils Olsen, f. 1719, til Breiset; Ole Olsen, f. 1723; [...] Olsen, f. 1724, senere bruker; Arnt Olsen, f. 1726.' The preceding user was a Peder (from 1698). The book gives no parents or origin for Ole. A son born 1710 puts Ole's birth before about 1688 (ABT; book only). Peder Olsen b. 1718 'til Breiset' fits his son Peder of Breiset (see his page). Grade unchanged. ¶4

Negative, 1665 vicar's census, Frosta, 35528 views 137-140 = pp. 255-261, read on the scans: Vangberg (p. 255) has users Olluf 44, Niels 60, Olluf 50 and Povel 40, with no sons listed and no Jakob; so no Jakob was a user or listed son at Vangberg in 1665. A 'Jacob 43' (b. about 1622) is a user at another farm on p. 257; nothing ties him to Ole, and he would be about 60 at Ole's birth. Test record for Ole's father is still a probate or bygsel entry naming him 'Jakobsen' with a farm (Frosta tingbok; Stjør- og Verdal skifte 24754/24756 unread). ¶5

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the grade · 1 search with no result · 2 open questions

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Probable, graded on his link to Peder of Breiset. The 1765 probate of his widow Gunild Nielsdatter at Vangberg (24755 fol. 455b, scan read) calls her a widow and names her children, all with the patronymic Olsen/Olsdatter, among them 'Peder Olsen Breset 47'; but it does not name the husband. His name Ole Jakobsen, the 1723 bygsel and death before 1762 come from Frostaboka VII p. 543 (nb.no item 00dc33af4fcabfe1f5ce20381f994feb, compiled source, rebuilt from word boxes). ¶2a

Searches that found nothing 1

Searched for a record naming him. (1) The 1765 probate after Gunild Nielsdatter at Vangberg (24755 fol. 455b, opening re-read) calls her only 'en sammesteds afgangne... Gunild Niels Dater' and does not name her late husband; the children's patronymic Olsen gives his first name only. (2) The pre-1742 probate card index (77804) has a single Vangberg, Frosta card (view 2097: Lauritz Vangberg, 1693, prot. 1 fol. 88), so there is no probate after Ole there; the typed register 24753 (1742-60) has none either, though it lists Barbro Mogensdatter, wife of Jacob Olsen, Vangberg, fol. 536b, and Anne Larsdatter, wife of Ole, Vangberg, fol. 751, both daughters-in-law. (3) Frostaboka VII p. 543 (rebuild) gives 'Ole Jakobsen... bygsel på garden 1723. Døde før 1762. G.m. Gunhild Nilsdtr., død 1765', with children Jakob 1710, Augustinus 1712, Kirsti 1717, Peder 1718 (to Breiset), Nils 1719 (to Breiset), Ole 1723,... Olsen 1724, Arnt 1726, but not where he came from. (4) The 1701 manntall is not indexed for Frosta (domicile Vangberg*/Wangberg* returns 0). He stays Probable. ¶3a
