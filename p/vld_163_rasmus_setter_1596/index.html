About the family

Father of Alf Rasmussen of Store Sætre (see his page), Probable; graded on his link to Alf. Alf's patronymic shows his father was a Rasmus; in 1666 the only Rasmus on Setter is this man of 70, entered on the same holding as the young farmer Alf. Neither census states the relationship, so this is inferred (patronymic, shared holding, ages 48 years apart), not Confirmed. Alf's age is given as 22 in 1666 but 65 in 1701, 68 in 1704 and b. 1643 in his burial; the 1666 census often understates ages, and he is the same Alf on the same farm in 1664, 1666, 1701, 1704 and 1706. ¶2

Life story

1666 vicar's census (scan read): Skodje, 'Num. 6. Setter', an ødegård. The farmers' column has 'Oluff 65', then 'Alff 22' and 'Rasmus 70' joined by one bracket and sharing one holding (the tax figure is written once against the bracket); Oluff's sons Størker 16 and Nels 14 are in the sons' column. Index: Rasmus, bonde, b. 1596 (pc00000001600481); Alff, bonde, b. 1644 (pc00000001600480). The 1664 bailiff's census of the same farm names only the farmers Olluff and Alff, so by then Alf was the working farmer and Rasmus the old man on his holding. ¶1

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
