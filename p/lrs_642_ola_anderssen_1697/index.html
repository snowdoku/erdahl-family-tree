Life story

Bygdebok for Karmøy: Skudenes og Skudeneshavn (1982), Langåker bnr. 7, canvas 129 (rebuilt from nb.no word boxes): '5. 1718-17[..] Ola Anderssen, ca. 1697, son til Anders Eriksen [på] Langåker bnr. 9. G.m. Malene Isaksdtr., ca. 1688, dotter til Isak [og] Ingeborg [på] Langåker bnr. 37. Born: a. Anna, ca. 1719, g.m. Lars Toresen [...] bnr. 10; b. Anders, ca. 1722, [til] Stol bnr. 1; c. Ingeborg, ca. 1732, g.m. Hans Sjursen, [neste] brukar. Ola bygsla 9 spann [i] Langåker [...] 1718.' The next entry (canvas 130): '6. 17[..]-1780: Hans Sjursen, ca. 1724, d. ca. 1785. G.m. Ingeborg Olsdtr., ca. 1732, d. ca. 1765, dotter til førre brukar.' ¶1

1762 ekstraskatt: Skudenes manntall (Digitalarkivet 49646, view 601), farm '2 Lang Ager': the couple 'Hans Sieursen / Ingebor Olsd.' (total 5 persons) with 'Ana Olsd.' in the girls-over-12 column and 'Ole Andersen / Malene Isachsd.' in the next column (other people kept in the household). So in 1762 Ole Andersen and Malene Isaksdatter were living in the household of Hans Siursen and Ingebor Olsdatter on Langager, as the bygdebok's son-in-law succession implies. The list does not write the relationship. ¶3

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

2 on the grade · 1 alternative set aside · 1 open question

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Graded on his link to his daughter Engebor Olsdatter: Probable, from the bygdebok statement, supported by the 1758 Skudenes soul register (Hans Siursen 34 with Engebor Olsdatter 26 heading the Langager household, i.e. the son-in-law succession the book describes) and by naming: Engebor's son Ole Hansen named a daughter Ingeborg Malene (1801 census), after his mother Ingeborg and, on this reading, his maternal grandmother Malene. ¶2a

Audit 8 (1-2 Nov 2026): grade unchanged (Probable, compiled source only). Under the audit-7 rule every bygdebok-sourced ancestor must carry a test record; this note supplies it. ¶4a

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Caution: the bygdebok itself adds 'Ho var ikkje mor til borna hans?' (was she not the mother of his children?), apparently because of the 1765 probate after an 'Ingebor Olsdatter' of Langager whose heirs were Mallene and Anne Rasmusdatter (showed that probate is not hers). No primary record names Ola as Engebor's father. ¶2b

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Test record: a probate after Ola Anderssen or Malene Isaksdatter of Langåker (after 1762; Ryfylke/Karmsund probate registers), which should name Ingeborg Olsdatter, wife of Hans Sjursen, as their child; failing that, the 1758 Skudenes sjeleregister (source 4501), Langager household, if it gives the relationship of Ole Andersen and Malene to Engebor Olsdatter. ¶4b
