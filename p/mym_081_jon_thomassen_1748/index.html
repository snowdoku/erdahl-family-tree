About the family

Parents linked at Probable: Thomas Jonsen and Birit Haldorsdatter of Lien (matr. 770). Probable, not proved (orchestrator, cards): in the 1762 extra tax list the only Lien household with a son Jon over 12 is Thomas Jonssen and Birit Haldorsdatter (matr. 770; children over 12 Jon, Helge, Birit, Gjuri). Jon Thomassen of Lien (b. about 1748, so about 14 in 1762) named his own children Thomas (b. about 1781), Beret (b. about 1783, = Birit) and Hælge (b. about 1789), matching his father's and mother's names and his sister Helge. The other Lien households of 1762 (matr. 698, 697, 769) are headed by a Lars, two Peders and a Jon Pedersen. ¶4

Life story

Farmer (bonde og gaardbeboer) at Lien, Selbu; aged 53 in the 1801 census, in his first marriage with Brynil Pedersdatter. Children in 1801: Peder 24, Thomas 20, Beret 18 and Magli 18 (twins), Marit 16, Brynil 14, Hælge 12, Ingebrigt 10, Jon 8, Rangnil 5, Ingebor 3. ¶1

Silver tax: 'John Thomasen', gaardmand, Lien, 4 ort 102 sk in 1816, and again in the 1819 and 1821 rounds - alive in 1821 (identification by name and farm). (the 1816 silver tax was levied on property holders in several rounds, 1816-1821.) ¶5

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the grade · 2 alternatives set aside

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Probable father of Marit Johnsdatter Langli (see her page) and of Brynhild Johnsdatter (see her page). Parentage argument (orchestrator, line 8): (1) Marit, baptised 12 Sep 1784 to Jon Th[omassen] Lien and Brynild Pedersdatter, fits her burial age (69 in June 1854); (2) in the 1801 census that couple's household at Lien holds 'Marit Jonsdatter 16' among 'Deres børn', with siblings Peder (24), Brynil (14) and Ingebor (3); (3) at the baptism of Marit's first son Nils (25 Jun 1815) two godparents were 'Peder Johnsen Lien' and 'Ingebor Johnsdatter Lien', and at her daughter Brynhild's baptism (1818) a godparent was 'Brynhild Johnsdatter Langlie' (née Lien, wife of her husband's brother Ole Nielsen) - i.e. three of the Lien siblings stand sponsor to her children; (4) she named her first daughter Brynhild, the name of the Lien mother; (5) her second son is John (after a father Jon). ¶2a

Alternatives considered and set aside 2

Rival: Marit (b. 1785) daughter of Jon Halvorsen Dyrdal and Karen Estensdatter, also aged 16 in 1801, but no Dyrdal kin appear at her children's baptisms. Unresolved: 'Auglem' as her farm in 1815 (probably service) and the clerk's slip 'Marith Jens d.' in June 1815. No record states her parents outright, so the link is Probable. ¶2b

Not the same man as Jon Thomassen 17 in household 01 of Lien (son of Thomas Jonssen and Guri Olsdatter). ¶3
