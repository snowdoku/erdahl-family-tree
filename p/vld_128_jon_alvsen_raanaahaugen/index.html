Life story

In 1763 'John' is listed among the children over 12 of the widow Aaste Iversdatter, an inderst at No. 1 Aaefletten, Lesja, with Ole, the soldier Iver, Margrete and Anne (Ekstraskatt Gudbrandsdal 1762-66, source 45390, view 286, 1763 Manntall for Lesja). This fits his age 22 on the 1767 Nystuen probate card. It narrows the gap between his 1745 baptism and his 1775 marriage (now 1745 -> 1763 -> 1767 -> 1775), but the parents stay Probable while the 1767 card's 'Ole Olsen' is unresolved. ¶10

Probably the 'John 22 aar' among the children of Aaste Iversdatter in the 1767 probate after her (second?) husband Ole Olsen of Nystuen (probate card 39945 image 9591, fol. 195b); see Aaste Iversdatter. Correction to, which ruled this probate out because the heir appeared to read 'Joen Olsen': the protocol scan is too poor to read the patronymic, and the card's two sons named Ole show that the estate had children of two marriages. ¶8

Eldest child found: Lesja 9236, p. 703 (canvas 346), Dom. 4 post Epiph. [28 Jan] 1776: 'Joen Alvsøn og Kari Rolvsd. Raannaahougen B[arn]: Aase, fød d. 9 Jan.', godparents Peder Slettum, Ole Raannaa, Ingri Enstad, Marit Øverlien, Marit [Kn?]eslette[n]; the churching of 'Joen Alvsen Raannaahougens q[vinde] Kari' is entered the same day. This is the daughter Aaste, 23, of the 1801 household (age understated by two years). Grade unchanged (Speculative, graded on his link to John Johnsen Sætnes): these records concern the couple, not the identification of John Johnsen Sætnes with their son Joen of 1784. ¶4

New evidence: the family moved from Raanaahaugen to Klevstuen (Klevstuen in Lesjaskogen), the very place from which 'Ungkarl Joen Joensen Klevstuen' married Anne Pedersdatter of Kloppen on 13 Mar 1816. (1) Alv Joensen of Klevstuen, b. about 1780, was buried 22 May 1803: this is the son Alv, baptised 6 Feb 1780 to Jon of Raanaahougen and Kari Rolvsd., and the tailor Alv, 20, of the 1801 Raanaa household. (2) Rolv Joensen, bachelor of Klevstuen, married Rønnog Olsdatter of Qvam on 12 Jun 1808 and had a son Joen at Klevstuen that Nov (children again 1811): he is the son Rolv baptised 1 Mar 1778 to Jon of Raanaahogen and Karen Rolvsd., named for Kari's father Rolv. (3) 'John Alvsen Klavstuen' was buried 4 Dec 1832, aged 78, and 'Kari Rolvsdr Klevstuen' died 25 Dec 1832 and was buried 10 Mar 1833, aged 85 (Lesja ministerialbok 1832-1850, pp. 334-335 and 340-341; also in the 1830-1842 book, p. 489). So the Raanaa family of 1801 was at Klevstuen by 1803 and stayed there until both parents died, and the only Joen Joensen recorded at Klevstuen, the 1816 groom, lived there with them. The previous Klevstuen occupant, Joen Olsen (b. about 1724), had been buried 13 Jul 1800. Rival check: Lesja baptisms 1776-96 of a Joen son of Joen/Jon give one other boy of the right age, Joen b. 17 Jul 1784, son of the shoemaker Jon Olsen (buried 15 Aug 1784) and Giesken Knudsdatter; nothing links him to Klevstuen and he is not in the 1801 census under that name (the only Joen Joensen b. 1783-86 in the Lesja 1801 census is ours at Raanaa). No probate was found: the Nord-Gudbrandsdal sorenskriveri probate card index (source 39945, filed by farm) has only one Klevstuen card (Hans Jonsen, 1756, fol. 283b), none for 1832-33. ¶5

Baptism found, Lesja ministerialbok 1732-1776 (source 9236), p. 293 (canvas 139), read on the scan: under 'Dom. Jubilate Wærket' 1745 [9 May, Verket annex church], 'Introd: Alf Nyestuens Aaste' (the churching of the mother), then 'Bapt: Alf Nyestuens Jon. Faddere Eric Sørenebøe[?], Guttorm Biorlien, Ole Stueflotten, Giertrud Sandum, Anne Olsdatter Nyestuen, fød d. 17de Apr.' Born 17 Apr 1745, he was 55 at the census of 1 Feb 1801, exactly the age given there (the burial age of 78 in 1832 is too low by nine years). He married as 'Joen Alvsen Nystuen' in 1775, named his eldest daughter Aase (b. Jan 1776) after his mother Aaste and his son Alv (b. 1780) after his father, and Ole Alvsen of Nystuen (father 1777) was a godfather to his son Joen in 1784. No other Jon son of an Alf has been seen in the Lesja baptisms read for 1745-48. His own grade is unchanged (Probable, graded on his link to John Johnsen Sætnes). ¶7

Cottager with land (huusmand med jord) at Raannaahougen (Rånåhaugen) under Rånå, Lesja. Father of Joen, born 12 Nov 1784 and baptised 28 Nov 1784 at the Verket annex church (Lesja ministerialbok 1777-1819, p. 192, read on the scan). In the 1801 census (Lesja, Raanaae, household 3) he is Joen Alvsen, 55, married once, with wife Kari Rolvsdatter, 50, and children Aaste 23, Alv 20 (tailor), Joen 16 and Anne 14. ¶1

Marriage found in the unindexed Lesja ministerialbok 1732-1776 (0512P, Digitalarkivet source 9236), read on the scan. Betrothal, Dom. Misericordias Domini [30 Apr] 1775, p. 689 (canvas 339): 'Despons: Joen Alvsen Nystuen og Pige Kari Rolvsd. Slettum', sureties (Caut.) Even Molmen and Joen [Blæs?]stuen. Marriage, Dom. 4 post Trinit. [9 Jul] 1775 at Lesja, p. 692 (canvas 341): 'Copul: Joen Alvsen Nystuen og Pigen Kari Rolvsdatter Slettum'. He was thus of Nystuen before he settled at Raannaahougen, which fits Ole Alvsen of Nystuen (godfather 1784) being his brother. Neither entry names a father. ¶3

Rival check for his son Joen b. 12 Nov 1784: the other Joen of 1784, son of the shoemaker Jon Olsen and Giesken Knudsdatter, belongs to Øvre Folldal, the far eastern annex of the parish: his parents married at Øvre Folldal (index: 28 Sep 1783, p. 439, bride 'Giertrud Knudsd.'; 22 Feb 1784, pp. 178-179), and he was baptised there on 15 Aug 1784 (pp. 186-187), the day his father was buried. No burial of a child Joen Jonsen 1784-1801 is indexed in Lesja, and neither Giesken nor her son is found later under those names (the only Joen Joensen of a fitting age in the Lesja 1801 census besides ours is a lodger and day labourer aged 19 at Jøndalen, whose baptism about 1782 is not indexed). So the rival is not excluded, but nothing connects him with Lesjaskogen. The Lesja ministerialbok 1777-1819 (source 9237) has no confirmation section: acts are entered chronologically and no confirmations appear in Feb-Oct 1799 (canvases 262-266) or in 1819 (canvases 448-470), so a confirmation of Joen about 1799-1801 naming his father is not to be had from it. ¶9

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

2 on the grade · 1 alternative set aside

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Speculative as an ancestor: he is the father of the Lesja-born Joen Joensen who is the best candidate for John Johnsen Sætnes (see his page); see that record for the grounds. Ole Alvsen of Nystuen, godfather in 1784, was probably his brother. ¶2

Upgrade: Speculative to Probable, graded on his link to John Johnsen Sætnes. The 1784 baptism and the 1801 census name Joen as his son; the move of the whole household to Klevstuen by 1803 (son Alv buried there 1803, son Rolv there 1808-11, he and Kari buried from there 1832-33) puts the 1816 groom 'Joen Joensen Klevstuen' in his household, and that groom's age of 38 in the 1822 Lesja and Grytten migration lists fits the 12 Nov 1784 birth. ¶6a

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Not Confirmed: no record names John Johnsen of Kloppen/Sætnes as his son; the identification still runs across farms (Raanaahaugen, Klevstuen, Kloppen) and the shoemaker's son Joen b. 17 Jul 1784 is not accounted for. A probate or a moving list naming the father would settle it. ¶6b
