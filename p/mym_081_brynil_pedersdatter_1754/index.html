Life story

Death: 'Brønil Pedersd:', aged 65 (so b. about 1755), died 30 May and was buried 4 Jun 1820 at Selbu (Selbu ministerialbok 1816-1831, p. 212-213, no. 12; index pg00000007095699). The entry gives no farm, but the age fits her 1801 census age of 47 (b. about 1754), and she is the only Brynil/Brønil Pedersdatter of that age in the 1801 census of Selbu (pf01058451001503) and the only one buried in the Selbu index with a birth year 1748-1760. ¶3

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
