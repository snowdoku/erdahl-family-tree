About the family

Father of Aaste (Aase) Iversdatter (see her page), Probable; graded on his link to Aaste. Primary (index of the probate, read on the card): Nord-Gudbrandsdal probate card index (source 39945, image 13171), 'Stueflaaten i Lesjeskogen, Lesje', skifte 18 Jul 1732, fol. 116b of skifteprotokoll 1732-1740: the deceased Iver Andersen, married to Margrethe Biørnsdatter; children 1. Biørn 4 years, 2. Anders 1 3/4, 3. Aaste 11 (so b. about 1721). The 1744 probate after the widow (card 13173) again lists 'Aase Iversd. 22', with Biørn Iversøn 16 and Anders 13, so the three children are the same set. ¶1

Life story

Bygdebok for Lesja 1 (Lesjaskogen, 1987; nb.no item 8e238b3c93fae7ff68ee91598c918b4d, canvas 42, rebuilt from word boxes, gaps in the text): entry 1-6 'Ca. 1720 - 1783': 'Iver ___ frå ___, ca. 1690-1731, g.m. Margrete ___ frå ___ i ___, ca. 1698-1743. Dei [hadde] heile garden frå ca. 1720, men med bygsel fyrst frå 1725. Barn: 1. Åse, ca. 1721, sjå Nystugu, her (3-3). 2. Anders, 1725-25. 3. ___, 1728, sjå ___ (5-6). 4. Anders, 1730... I 1732 var det skifte etter Iver, og i 1744 etter Margrete.' So the bygdebok makes the same identification (Åse of Stueflotten = Åse wife of Alf in Nystugu). His origin ('frå ___') was not recovered. Dates ABT (rebuilt text). ¶3

The 1732 protocol entry (source 25157, fol. 116b) was read: Iver Andersen of Stueflotten, dead; widow Margrethe Biørns Datter; they 'sammen avlede 3 Børn' - Biørn 4, Anders 1 3/4 and the daughter 11 (her name is cramped in the bitonal scan; the card reads 'Aaste'). The 1744 entry (fol. 663a) lists '3. Aase Iversdatter 22 aar' with no husband legible. Grade unchanged (Probable, graded on his link to Aaste): same reason as for Margrethe. ¶4a

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the grade · 1 open question

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Why Probable, not Confirmed: the card names Aaste as Iver's daughter, but it ties her to the tree's Aaste (wife of Alf of Nystuen, mother of Jon b. 1745) only by name, patronymic and age: 'Aase Iversd.' 22 in Feb 1744 (b. about 1722) = 'Aaste Iversd.' of Nyestuen buried 23 Apr 1797, given as b. 1722; Nystugu was a husmannsplass on the same farm complex as Stueflotten (bygdebok); an Ole Stueflotten stood godfather to Alf and Aaste's son Jon in 1745. No record found names both her father and her husband, and Aaste's own link to Jon is Probable. The test would be the 1732 and 1744 probate entries themselves (fol. 116b and 663a) for a husband's name on the 1744 heir 'Aase' (the bygdebok gives her marriage as 1738, so she may have been married by 1744). ¶2
