Life story

Found by the probate-by-farm method: Ryfylke probates are not in the Digitalarkivet probate index (domicile=Tuntland, Fister*, Øye return no Ryfylke rows), so the typed farm register at the front of D 6 (DA 23890, 1767-1774) was read letter by letter. That volume is labelled 'Karmsund' but holds Hjelmeland, Jelsa, Årdal, Sjernerø (which includes Fister farms such as Byre and Bukken) and the other Ryfylke skipreider, and no earlier pass had read it. ¶1

Timeline: her second marriage to Søren Sørensen took place after her first husband Ole's youngest child was born (about 1757) and before the 1762 ekstraskatt; it is not in the Hjelmeland marriages 1760-62 (DA 1599), so about 1757-1760. Søren Sørensen Øye married Susanna Olsdatter on 26 Feb 1775 (Hjelmeland marriages, no. 3). ¶5

Probate (D 6 fol. 352b): 'Anno 1771 den 16de Julij' registration and valuation on the farm Nord Øye in Hjelmeland skipreide after the deceased woman 'Sussanne Jacobsdatter', to be shared between her surviving husband Søren Sørensen and the children 'udi 2de Ægteskaber avlede' (born in two marriages). Of the first marriage: a son of age (name partly legible, read as [Niels] Olsen, 'myndig'), and two daughters, '[...] Olsdatter' 18 and '[...] Olsdatter' 14. Of the second marriage: Søren Sørensen 5 and Berthe Sørensdatter 7. Guardian for the minors: Johannes Larsen [?]. Gross estate 411 rdl 1 ort 14 sk. The scan is binarised and hard; the names of the first-marriage children are only partly legible. ¶2

Probate of her daughters (D 6 fol. 350a-b): 'Hielmelands Schibrede, Anno 1771 den 10de Januarij', registration on the farm Nord Øye after the deceased Kari and Siri Olsdøttre. The heirs are read as: a full brother [Niels] Olsen (name partly legible); their mother 'Sussanne Jacobsdatter gift med Søren Øye'; full sisters [...] Olsdatter 18 and [...] Olsdatter 14; maternal half-siblings Søren Sørensen 4 and Berthe Sørensdatter 6 1/2; and half-sisters Marthe Olsdatter 35 and K[irsten?] Olsdatter 33. Marthe Olsdatter is the maid in the 1762 ekstraskatt household, so Ole had children by an earlier wife (born about 1736-38). ¶3

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the grade · 1 on the identification

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Graded Probable on her link to her son Niels Olsen. The 1762 ekstraskatt (N. Øye no. 33) lists Niels Olsen as 'Stif Søn' of Søren Sørensen and Susanna Jacobsdatter, with Siri and Kari Olsdatter. The 1771 probates name Kari and Siri's mother as Susanna, wife of Søren Øye, and an adult son of her first marriage whose name reads as [Niels] Olsen. Niels Olsen of Øye married in Hjelmeland in 1775 (4 years later, same farm). Because the son's given name cannot be read with certainty on the scan, and Ole also had children by an earlier wife (so a 'stepson' Olsen could in principle be Ole's son by that wife), this stays Probable. A clearer image of fol. 350b line 2 and fol. 352b line 9 would settle it. ¶4

The case for this identification 1

Re-read: the URN 'max size' image (urn.digitalarkivet.no...sk20081104640382 and...384) is the same 3483 px binarised scan as the viewer, so no better image exists online. Zoomed crops of fol. 350b line 3 read 'Heel Broderen N[ie]ls Olsen myndig' (full brother of Kari and Siri), and of the 16 Jul 1771 entry (heading on fol. 352b, right column; the earlier citation 'line 9' counts from that heading) read the first-marriage son as 'N[ie]ls Olsen myndig'. In both places the name is a short word with a capital N-like initial and a long-s ending, which fits Niels and not Ole, Lars or Jacob, but the middle letters are lost in the binarisation. The 'Heel Broderen' wording does show that this adult son was Susanna's own child, not a child of Ole's earlier marriage. Kept Probable (graded on her link to Niels Olsen): the reading of the given name is not certain. ¶6
