About the family

Parentage found. 'Ingeborg', daughter of Sifvert Ingebrigtsen of Diub, was baptized at Borgund on 18 June 1744 (entry 47). Sivert Ingebrigtsen was the tenant farmer at Djup, and he is the only man of that name recorded on the farm. Everything about Ingeborg Siversdatter points to the same woman: she was a 'maid of Diub' when she was betrothed in January 1768, she bore her children at Djup, she was 'the widow Ingeborg Siversdatter of Diub' when she remarried in 1786, and she was still at Diup in the 1801 census. The date given here is the baptism date. ¶4

Her father's estate was registered on 14 August 1766 (Sunnmøre sorenskriveri, protocol 3A 22, folio 736b), which is why the farm was available to the young man she married eighteen months later. ¶6

Life story

An Ingeborg Syversdatter was confirmed at Borgund 26 Oct 1760, which fits a birth about 1740. Her parents are not identified. ¶3

A sister, Marthe, was baptized at Borgund on 27 April 1738; other siblings were baptized in 1728, 1733, 1735, 1747 and 1749. ¶7

Probate: as the widow Ingeborg Siversdatter, with her legal guardian (lauverge) Siverd [..]sen of Diub, she shared in the division of her husband Jørgen Paulsen's estate at Diub (Djup), Giske, on 1 February 1785 (Sunnmøre sorenskriveri, 3A 27, fol. 401a-402a). The other heirs were the children Poul, a son about 5 (most probably Joen) and Berthe, about 7. Their guardians (formyndere) were Rasmus [..]sen of Diub, a man of Gudøen, and Jacob Christensen Gilsvær[?]. She remarried in 1786. ¶8

Marriage: the Borgund marriage index (Borgund local parish 1762-1800) records the marriage on 29 May 1768 of Ingeborg Sivertsd. of Diub, a maid (pige), to Jørgen Paalss. (Paulsen) of Diub, following their engagement on 1 Jan 1768. This gives the exact date for her first marriage to Jørgen Paulsen Djup. Read from the index row, 13 Oct 2026. ¶11

Probate: her father's estate registration at Diub on 14 August 1766 (Sunnmøre sorenskriveri, 3A 22, fol. 736b-737) names 'Ingeborg Siurs Datter', unmarried, as a daughter and heir of the late Siur Ingebrigtsen, with Rasmus Olsen of Diub as her guardian (formynder). The widow is Inger Rasmusdatter, possibly her mother (Speculative); see that person for why this is not certain. Her brothers Nils Siursen of Valderøy and Hans Siursen, a married sister (perhaps Synneve) and a sister Elisabeth are co-heirs. The entry gives no ages. ¶9

A maid at Diub (Djup) when she was engaged to Jørgen Paulsen 1 Jan 1768 at Borgund. She was churched as his wife ('Ingeborg Siursdtr.') in 1777. ¶1

As the widow Ingeborg Siversdatter of Diub she married Lars Einersen of Aase, a bachelor and militia soldier, at Borgund (engaged 20 Aug, married 29 Oct 1786). In the 1801 census at Diup she is 61, wife of Lars Einersen (47), 'his first, her second marriage'. ¶2

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Confidence raised from Probable to Confirmed. The 1766 probate, a primary record read from the scan, names her as Siur Ingebrigtsen's daughter, unmarried, at Diub. Her baptism (18 Jun 1744, daughter of Sifvert Ingebrigtsen of Diub) and her 1768 engagement as a maid of Diub fit the same woman. ¶10

Records that disagree 1

The 1744 baptism also resolves the age discrepancy in her favour: a girl born in June 1744 would be 16 at her confirmation in October 1760, which is exactly the normal age, whereas the 1801 census age of 61 (suggesting about 1740) is the sort of rounding that is common in that census. ¶5
