How sure are we?

1 on the grade · 1 open question

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Graded Probable as mother of Malena Pedersdatter (see her page): bygdebok statement only. Malena's baptism of 25 Feb 1770 names only 'Peder Hegge' (the bygdebok's 25.12.1770 is probably a misreading or a rebuild error), and its first godmother is 'Mali Hegge', who would not normally be the mother; in 1759 a second Peder (Peder Olsen, 37, son of Ole Joensen) lived at Hegge. ¶2

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Test record: the Skatval/Værnes marriage of Peder Arntsen and the widow Mali Nilsdatter, 28 Nov 1759 (Stjørdal 16371, scan), and a probate after Mali Nilsdatter, wife of Lars Eriksen, Hegge, 1788 (Stjør- og Verdal skifteprotokoll 9-10, 24762/24763; the probate would list Malena among the heirs as 'Malena Pedersdatter, g.m. Ole Pedersen Lille Valstad'). ¶3
