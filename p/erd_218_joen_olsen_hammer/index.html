Life story

New father of Joen Joensen Kleven (see his page), graded on his link to him: Probable. Primary-record index: the Stjør- og Verdal probate card (77804, image 665) abstracts the skifte of 11 Mar 1738 after 'Joen Olsen Hammer, dragonrytter' (a dragoon): widow Siri Larsdtr. Hammer; children Ole Joensen 12, Lars Joensen 8, Joen Joensen 6 - whose guardian was 'Joen Kleven' - and Guru Joensdtr. 3 (skifteprotokoll 2, fol. 771b). The probate's Joen Joensen, 6 in Mar 1738, was born about 1731-32, which matches the tree's Joen Joensen of Storkleven (69 in the 1801 census, so b. about 1732); his guardian was a man of Kleven, the farm where Joen later lived. Hegra gårds- og slektshistorie B. 2 says the same independently: Kleven (canvas 77) 'John Johnsen ([frå Hammer] 119, Stjørdal), [f.] 1732, [d.] 1804', and the Hammer chapter (canvas 401) 'John Olsen, [f.] 1690, gravlagt... 1738. Gift 1724 m. Sigrid Larsdtr. Born: Ola...; Lars (... 244/1), f. 31/7 1729; John Kleven (... 261), f. 1732; Guru, f. 27/12 1734'. Probable, not Confirmed, because identifying the 6-year-old of Hammer with the Kleven farmer crosses farms and a 19-year gap (1738 to his marriage at Kleven on 30 Jun 1757) with no record yet read in between; no rival Joen Joensen b. about 1732 is known at Kleven. The bygdebok's dates are ABT (rebuilt text); the farm name before '119' is a gap in the rebuild, identified as Hammer because the B.2 farm index puts Stjørdal farm 119 at pp. 398-413, where 'Hammer' has 134 hits. Bygdebok lead for the next generation (Speculative, not entered): an Ingeborg Johnsdtr., b. about 1660, buried 18 Dec 1718, with children John (brukar), Ivar (Skatval) and Svend (182/3 Lånke, b. 1695), on the same page. ¶1a

The probate protocol entry itself: Stjør- og Verdal skifteprotokoll 2C (Digitalarkivet 24752) fol. 771b, view 33 left, headed 'N:o 11... 1738'. The district judge Laurits Bruun records a skifte held on 17 Mar 1738 (the card's 11/3 is probably the registration date) at the dragoon quarter 'Gaarden Hammer udi Stiørdals Præstegield, Hegre annex', under Obristl. Motz's regiment, after the late dragoon 'Joen Olsen Hammer', 'imellem hans efterladte Enke Siri Lars datter Hammer og deres tilsammen avlede nu i Live værende 4 Børn': A. Ole Joensen 12, B. Lars Joensen 8, C. Joen Joensen 6, D. Guru Joensdatter 3. The widow chose as her guardian her sister's husband ('hendes Søsters Mand', name not securely read). The registration on the same folio is signed by Captain P. Zincke, with appraisers A. Eeg, Anders Davidsen Frøyum and Mads Bang. The division (fol. 772, view 33 right) gives the widow half (41 rdl.) and each child about 11 rdl. The guardians (fol. 773a, view 34 right) are a man named as 'den afg. Mands Moster Mand' (the deceased's mother's sister's husband) for Ole; Mads Bang for Lars; 'Joen Kleven[?], den afg. Mands Søskende Barn' (the deceased's first cousin) for Joen Joensen; and Joen Kaall[?] for Guru. This settles the audit-8 rule: the protocol says 'deres tilsammen avlede' Børn, so Siri Larsdatter is the mother of all four, Joen Joensen included. ¶2

The 1757 marriage in Stjørdal 16371, marriage section p. 322 (canvas 324 right, top): 'eodem Die et loco trolovet Ungkarl og Dragoun af Liv Compagniet Jon olsen[?] Hamer, som haver... Dragoun Qvarteret Kleven og havde Obriste-Lieutenant Møllerops Tilladelse at ægte Pigen Siri Svendsdatter Kleven'; forlovere Haagen Hamer and Baar Jonsen Øyen; 'Copuleret ved Værnes d. 30te Junij' [1757]. So the dragoon who took over the Kleven dragoon quarter and married Siri Svendsdatter Kleven came from Hammer, and a Hammer man stood surety. This is the couple of the 1761 probate (Sirie Svendsdatter, wife of Joen Joensen, Stor Kleven). Hammer origin is now shown by a primary record, which narrows the 1738-1757 bridge to a same-name question. Contradiction, unresolved: the groom's patronymic in the 1757 entry reads 'olsen' (lower-case o, not the J of 'Jonsen' written by the same hand in 'Baar Jonsen'), although every later record (the 1761 and 1810 probates, the 1765 marriage and the 1801 census) calls him Joen Joensen. It is probably a slip (his father was Joen Olsen), but under the audit-8 rule a noted contradiction blocks the raise until it is resolved. ¶3a

No grade change: the 1758-61 baptisms of Jon Kleven's children (16371 c33, c55, c69) give the father only as 'Jon Kleven', so they cannot settle the 1757 'Jon olsen Hamer' reading. Supporting, circumstantial only: at the 1760 baptism the godfathers include 'ole Hamer, Lars ibid' (the names of this man's sons Ole and Lars in the 1738 probate), and at the 1758 baptism 'Haagen Hamer' and 'Randi Hamer[?]'. The older Joen Joensen of Hammergierdet (m. 1744) is excluded as the Kleven man: a 'Jon Hammer' was having a son Haagen baptised 30 Apr 1758 (16371 c30) while Jon Kleven was married at Kleven. Remaining blocks to Confirmed: (a) the lone 1757 'olsen'; (b) the identification of the 6-year-old Joen of 1738 with the Kleven man spans farms (Hammer to Kleven) and 19 years with no record in between that names him, which under the audit-4 and audit-7 rules is Probable whatever the patronymic. ¶5a

How sure are we?

Records that disagree 1

Contradiction stands; no grade change: the 1757 marriage entry (16371 c324 right, p. 322) re-read from a native-resolution IIIF crop (pct:53,3.5,16,3.5). The groom is clearly 'Jon olsen Hamer': a small o, a long s, then 'en'. It is not 'Jonsen' or 'Joensen'. The rest of the entry fits the tree's Joen Joensen (an unmarried dragoon of the Life Company from Hammer, quartered at Kleven, where the 1738 guardian of the 6-year-old Joen Joensen was 'Joen Kleven, den afg. Mands Søskende Barn'; marrying Siri Svendsdatter Kleven; forlover Haagen Hamer). Possible explanations: (a) a clerk's slip, writing the father's own patronymic (the father was Joen Olsen Hammer); (b) a different Jon Olsen from Hammer. No other Jon Olsen of Hammer has been found yet, but none has been searched for either. Related, from the 1743 trolovede list (16370 c261, 24 Aug 1743 at Hegre): 'Joen Joensen Hammergierdet, som har pladt under Hammer kaldet lille Lund, med Pigen Siri Svend[s]d.[?] Gedderaas' (married at Værnes 19 Mar 1744), cautioners Peder Frøyum and Haagen Hammer. That is an older Joen Joensen of Hammer (adult in 1743, so not the 6-year-old of 1738). He is a second Joen Joensen tied to Hammer, which must be kept apart from the tree's man. ¶4a

Test record: a better reading of the 1757 patronymic, or a record between 1738 and 1757 (the Hammer dragoon's son at Kleven) that calls him Joen Joensen. ¶3c

Test record: baptisms of the 1757 couple's children in 16371 (1758-62), which may give the father's patronymic, or Joen Joensen Kleven's 1765 marriage to Lisbet Davidsdatter, which may give his patronymic and widower status. ¶4b
