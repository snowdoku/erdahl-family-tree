Life story

Life (images read): son of M. Joseph Jenisch, pastor at Münchingen. Coppersmith (Kupferschmid) and burgher at Brackenheim by 1667, when he married Maria Juliana Cast (no. 752). Children with her at Brackenheim 1670-1681, including Agnes Johanna (14 Oct 1670). A member of the town court (des Gerichts) and a widower when he married Magdalena (indexed Barth) on 22 May 1682 at Münchingen (no. 978); a son Johan Christoph was christened at Brackenheim on 4 Nov 1684. Dead by his daughter Juliana's marriage in 1695 ('gewesenen... nachgelassene Tochter'). His Brackenheim burial (1684-1695) and his own baptism (about 1641, probably at Stuttgart or Münchingen, where his father's other children were christened 1643-1648) were not found in the indexes and were not reached on the images. ¶5a

The Brackenheim chain, all from images read except the sibling baptisms (labelled index rows): (1) 1667 marriage no. 752: Hanß Jacob Jenisch, son of 'H. M. Joseph Jenisch, wohlverordneter Pfarrer zu Münchingen', 'Kupferschmid und Burger allhier', married Maria Juliana, daughter of the late Heinrich Friderich Cast[en]. (2) 14 Oct 1670 baptism: Agnes Johanna, parents 'Hanß Jacob Jenisch, Maria Juliana'. (3) Baptisms of further children of Hans Jacob Jenisch and Maria Juliana at Brackenheim 1674, 1675, 1677, 1679, 1681 (index rows). (4) 1682 marriage no. 978: 'Herr Johann Jacob Jenisch, des Gerichts, Burger und Kupferschmid allhie, Wittwer' married Magdalena (indexed Barth), at Münchingen on 22 May; a son Johan Christoph followed at Brackenheim on 4 Nov 1684 (Anna Magdalena). (5) 1695 marriage no. 1263: his daughter Juliana, 'H. Johann Jacob Jenischs, gewesenen Burgers, des Gerichts und [Kupfer]schmids allhie nachgelassene eheliche Tochter'. (6) 1696 Bietigheim marriage: 'Agnes Johanna, Herrn Johann Jacob J[enisch]s, gewesenen Gerichtsverwandten und Kupferschmids zu Brackenheim hinterlassene eheliche Tochter'. (7) 1748 Bietigheim burial of Agnes Johanna, aged 77 years and about 7 months, so born about Sep 1670. ¶6

Burial found (image read): Brackenheim burials 1686, DGS 101835507 image 96: 'den 1. Apr. Starb Herr Johann Jacob Jenisch, des Gerichts und Kupferschmid allhie, seines alters 45 Jahr'. He is the man of the tree: full name, title Herr, town-court seat and trade are those of the 1682 widower (no. 978) and of the father named in 1695 and 1696. Death set to 1 Apr 1686 (it was BET 1684 AND 1695), and birth to ABT 1641 from the age of 45. This supersedes the line in his 'Life' note that the burial was not found. The index row (QPGQ-CYR1) misreads the surname as 'Henigh'. The Pfarrerbuch entry for his father (Nr. 3905) gives the same date: '+ 1.4.1686 Brackenheim, Kupferschmied und des Ger. ebd.' Read signed in on FamilySearch, 2 Nov 2026. ¶7

Occupation and surname (images read): the 1696 Bietigheim marriage calls him 'Herr Johann Jacob J[amschauer/Jenisch]', 'gewesener Gerichtsverwandter und Kupferschmid zu Brackenheim', dead by 1696. A daughter Agnes Johanna was christened at Brackenheim on 14 Oct 1670 to 'Hanß Jacob Jenisch' and Maria Juliana (FamilySearch NVZ2-B9Z; image read), which fits her burial age. His surname was most likely Jenisch (the 'Germany, Marriages' extractions read Janisch/Jenisch). Identification of the 1670 father with the 1696 Brackenheim coppersmith is Probable (same forename, same town, daughter's age; no trade in the baptism). ¶3a

'Herrn' marks a man of standing (official, clergy or notable burgher). Surname as indexed; the reading is uncertain and should be checked on the image. Corrections 1

Correction: surname changed from 'Jamschauer' to Jenisch. 'Jamschauer' was the FamilySearch/Ancestry indexers' reading of the 1696 Bietigheim marriage; the Brackenheim register, which he kept appearing in as a burgher, writes 'Jenisch' every time: his own marriages of 1667 ('Hanß Jacob Jenisch') and 1682 ('Herr Johann Jacob Jenisch'), his children's baptisms 1670-1684, and his daughter Juliana's marriage in 1695 ('H. Johann Jacob Jenischs'). The 'Germany, Marriages' extractions of the 1696 entry also read Janisch/Jenisch. The id keeps the old spelling. Image read signed in on FamilySearch, 2 Nov 2026. ¶4
