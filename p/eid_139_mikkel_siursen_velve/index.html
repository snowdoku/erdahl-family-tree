About the family

Pedigree collapse: this draft makes Jon Mikkelsen Moen (see his page) and Beret (Birthe) Mikkelsdatter (see her page) brother and sister, both children of Mikkel Siursen and Brynild Pedersdatter of Velve 791. Both are already direct ancestors of Palmer Eidem: Jon > Gunder Johnsen Eidem > Beret Gundersdatter > Gunder > Oline > Palmer, and Beret > Dordi Jonsdatter Stokke > Ole Jonsen > Kari > Oline > Palmer. So Gunder Johnsen Eidem and Dordi Jonsdatter were first cousins, Oline's parents Gunder and Kari were third cousins, and Mikkel and Brynild stand twice in Palmer's tree as his 4x-great-grandparents. This rests on two Probable links, not on a record that names the relationship; if either link fails, the collapse disappears. ¶4

Life story

Probable baptism: Misericordia Sunday (about 3 May) 1711, 'ditto Siur Welfuer[?] barn Michel', third child of Siur and Ingebor of Velve (16310 c43; crop checked by the orchestrator). Godmother 'Guru Welfuer' may be the Guri Siursdatter Velve who died 1772 aged 74. ¶11a

Baptism of a son Peder: Palm Sunday (26 March) or Easter week 1741, 'Michel Welthes barn ved nafn Peder', godparents Haldor Lille-Efjen, Erich Welthe, Jon Welthe, Ingebore Kolset[?] and Sigrete[?] Welthe, the child carried by 'madamn Brynel Peders datter' (the mother, by this book's usage); she was churched as 'Michel Welthe's wife Brynel Pedersdatter' about Ascension 1741 (16315 canvases 15 and 17). The farm spelling Welthe is read as Welve = Velve (inference). This is presumably the Peder of the 1762 list, and shows the couple married by 1740. ¶5

Named 'Michel Siversen, 31 år gl.' as son and heir to the farm in the probate after his father Siver Michelsen Velve, 18 Jun 1742; his mother was Ingebor Svendsdatter (card 77804 c2131). ¶13

Guardians named in his probate: Ole Pedersen Fu[glem?] for the two elder daughters, together with Baroe Pedersen Hegset, and Esten Kolset for the two younger daughters. The widow's guardian is not read. Two of these men are Pedersens and may be Brynild's brothers, but no relationship is written. Ole Thomassen took part as lensmann. ¶15

Baptism of a son Svend: Quinquagesima Sunday, 24 February 1743, 'Michel Welde[s] barn ved Nafn: Svend', godparents Baard Hagset[?], Niels Welde[?], Frederich Welde[?], Anne Biørnsdatter[?] and Mali Welde[?], carried by 'madamn Brynil Pedersdatter'; she was churched in Lent 1743 as 'Michel Welde[s] Qvinde Brynel Pedersdatter' (16315 canvases 42-43). The child's name is read Svend; Siur is a less likely reading. ¶6

Baptism of his son Jon: 12th Sunday after Trinity (5 September) 1745, 'Michel Wel[?]stads barn ved Nafn Jonn'; the mother was churched about Michaelmas 1745 as 'Michel Wel[?]stads Qv[inde] Brynill Pedersdatter'. The farm name begins with the clerk's W/K ligature; with the same wife's name it is read here as Welve = Velve (inference). ¶2

With Jon (baptised 5 September 1745), these give three sons baptised in 1741, 1743 and 1745 to Michel of Welthe/Welde/Wel[?]stad and Brynild Pedersdatter, the same couple as the 1762 Velve household. The repeated wife's name across three entries supports identifying the couple with Mikkel Siursen and Brynild Pedersdatter of Velve 791 (still Probable: no entry gives Mikkel's patronymic). ¶7

1762 extra tax (ekstraskatt) list: head of the farmer-owned (Bunden Eier) holding 791 Velve, Selbu, as 'Mikel Siursen', with his wife Brynild Pedersdatter and six children over 12: Siur, Peder, Osmund[?], Jon, Ingebor and Brit. The list gives no ages; the children were therefore born before about 1750. ¶1

Burial, Quasimodogeniti Sunday (26 Apr) 1767: '(2) Michel Siverts: Velve gl 56 a.' - Mikkel Sivertsen Velve, aged 56 (16311 canvas 21, crop checked by the orchestrator). Age 56 fits his baptism of May 1711 exactly, and his own record gives patronymic and farm. His son Sivert Michelsen married Mali Olsdatter on Trinity Sunday 1767 and named a son Michel in Sep 1767 (c21-22). ¶12

Velve relatives in 16311: 'Guri Siursd. Velve' died 28 Oct 1772 aged 74 (b. c.1698; c53) - probably his sister; and a 'Siur Mikkelsen Velve' with wife Mali Olsdatter baptised a daughter Brynild in Jan 1772 (c48) - probably his son, the daughter named for Mikkel's wife Brynild Pedersdatter. Both are hypotheses from name and farm only. ¶9

1701 manntall lead, Velve (canvas 120, book p. 219): a widow's household (sons Lars Arnesen 26, Jon reserve soldier 22, Haldor 20, a son whose name looks like 'Siur' 18, Arn 10?) and a second household whose head read as 'Øvind Olsen' 26 with a son 'Ole' 6 - the orchestrator's zoom suggests the head's name and the son's patronymic may instead be 'Siur'/'Siursen'. Either a Siur born about 1675 or about 1683 at Velve could be Mikkel's father (and Guri Siursdatter Velve, d. 1772 aged 74, his sister). Unresolved; needs an expert re-read of the c120 Velve rows, or a 16310 baptism of 'Siur Velves barn Michel'. ¶10

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Why Probable: the 1762 household (Mikkel Siursen and Brynild Pedersdatter of Velve, with a son Jon and a daughter Brit) matches the 1745 baptism parents and fits both Jon Mikkelsen Moen (b. about 1745) and Beret Mikkelsdatter of Velve (b. about 1747). A 'Michel Velve' was godfather in 1786 to Jon Mikkelsen Moen's son Gunder. ¶3a

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed. His probate, Skifteprotokoll 6-1 (24756) fol. 65b, reads: 'Anno 1767 den 21de Maij blev lovlig Skifte holdt... paa Gaarden Welve i Selboe efter afgangne Michel Sivertsen, som foruden hans efterlevende Kone Brönild Peders Datter haver med hende til Arvinger efterladt sig efterskrevne 8 Børn, nemlig: 1. Sønnen Siur Michelsen myndig, 2. Peder Michelsen ligeledes myndig, 3. Svend Michelsen myndig, 4. John Michelsen ligesaa myndig, 5. Datteren Ingeborg Michelsdatter [20?] aar gl., 6. Datteren Berit Michelsdatter 18 aar gl., 7. Datteren Berit Michelsdatter den yngre [?] aar gl., og 8. Datteren Anne Michelsdatter [?] aar gl.'. This primary record names his wife Brynild Pedersdatter and the two children through whom he descends to David: John Michelsen (Jon Mikkelsen Moen, b. 1745) and Berit Michelsdatter, aged 18 (Beret Mikkelsdatter, b. about 1747-49, who married Jon Halvorsen Velve in 1772). ¶14

Corrections 1

Correction: birth set to before 3 May 1711 at Velve. ¶11b

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Possible son Svend: the 1801 census of Selbu has Svend Mikkelssen, born about 1741 (calculated), head of a household at Wælve (Velve), a farmer in his first marriage, with his wife Soffi Thomasdatter. He fits the Svend baptised in February 1743 (an ordinary two-year census error). He is not in the 1762 list, whose eldest son is entered as Siur; whether the 1762 'Siur' is the same son is unresolved. Not linked as a child. ¶8
