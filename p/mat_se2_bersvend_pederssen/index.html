About the family

Kari Pedersdatter Eidem's paternal grandfather, and the man who puts this family on the farm Eidem. 1801 census, Selbu, rural residence 0034 EIDUM, household 07: 'Bersvend Pederssen', 47, Mand, HUUSMAND MED JORD (cottager with land), 'Begge i 1. aegteskab' (both in their first marriage), with his wife Kiersten Ingebrigtsdatter, 46, their sons Peder, 14, and Torsten, 5, and his own widowed mother Marit Jonsdatter, 73, entered as Fattig (poor). ¶1

Children found in the 1783-1795 register (Selbu ministerialbok 1757-1801): a child born about February-March 1784, inferred from the churching of 'Bersv. Ped. Eidems Hustru' on Maundy Thursday 1784 (canvas 113; the baptism was not seen); Marit, baptised Trinity Sunday, 22 May 1785 (canvas 118), buried 5 February 1786, 'Marit Bersv. Eidem død 2 Febr, 1 aar gl.' (canvas 120); Peder, baptised 23 September 1787 (canvas 127), buried 21 June 1789 aged 2 (canvas 133); Peder, baptised 14 February 1790 (canvas 135), Kari Pedersdatter's father; and Torsten, born 10 September 1795 and baptised 20 September 1795, 'Bersven Peders: Eidem og Kirsten Ingebrictsd:', godparents Anders Torstensen Berge, Erich Olsen Eidem, Jon Olsen Eidem, Marite Jonsdatter Sannerstad[?] and Giertrud Andersdatter Berge (canvas 183). Torsten is the Torsten Bersvendsen, 5, of the 1801 household. The first daughter carries the name of Bersvend's mother Marit Jonsdatter. ¶11

Twins born 1796: Karen and Jon, born 5 November 1796 and baptised 13 November 1796, parents 'Bersven Pedersen Eidem' and 'Kirsten Engelbricts d:' (Selbu ministerialbok 1757-1801, canvas 199 right page; Jon home-baptised). Karen died 2 August 1797 aged nine months, buried 6 August 1797 (canvas 212 right page). Jon is absent from the 1801 household, so he probably died young - an inference, his burial not yet seen. Read. Full details on Kiersten Ingebrigtsdatter. ¶14

Life story

Correction to two father leads. (1) The 1762 Svinaas couple: the probate after Peder Baardsen of Svinaas, registered 16 Mar 1790 (Stjør- og Verdal skifteprotokoll 10, 24763 p. 223, view 237 right), settles the patronymic of the 1762 'Peder B?rsen' as Baardsen (Bårdsen) and names his widow Marithe Jonsdatter. But it names as 'deres udi Ægteskab sammenavlede' child ONE heir only: the daughter Marithe Pedersdatter, 35 (born about 1755), whose guardian was her farbror (father's brother) Jon Baardsen Sandvig; the widow's lagverge was Ole Baardsen Fossum. Bersvend Pedersen, alive and about 36 in 1790, is not an heir, so he was not a child of that marriage. Either the widow is a different Marit Jonsdatter from his mother, or he was her son by another man before that marriage (which the 1801 census, calling his mother 'widow after her first marriage', does not support). The estate was insolvent and Svinaas was sold to Jon Paulsen, and a poor widow Marit Jonsdatter is with Bersvend at Eidem in 1801 - suggestive, not proof. Peder Baardsen is therefore NOT entered as his father. ¶18

(2) Peder Olsen Eidem, who took over Eidem on the 1743 probate after his father Ole Pedersen (panteregister 22743 image 53 right, 'Ole Pedersen Eidems Skifte, hvorved Sønnen Peder Olsen Eidem er udlagt Gaarden Eidem', pantebok A-2 fol. 432): in the 1762 extra tax list his wife was an Ane (Svendsdatter?), not Marit Jonsdatter, while Bersvend's mother Marit Jonsdatter was alive until 1809. He is therefore a weak candidate. The panteregister (images 8-14 B, 53-54 O, 59-60 P) has no deed or probate entry naming Bersvend; it shows a 'Peder Eidem den Eldre' (deed on Overdahl, pantebok fol. 772, c.1760) and that Ole Pedersen received a deed on Eidem from Niels Belle (pantebok B fol. 179, c.1767). No probate after Peder Olsen Eidem is in the typed Stjør- og Verdal registers (24753, 24755, 24757-24765); the 1766-70 protocol 24756 has no typed register and is the next place to look. Father still unknown. ¶19

Marriage: betrothed on Maundy Thursday, 17 April 1783, 'Sold. Bersvend Ped. Eidem' and 'Kersten Engelbrechtsd. Berge' - soldier Bersvend Pedersen of Eidem and Kirsten Engelbrechtsdatter of Berge; forlovere (sponsors) Mogens D[?]sfor[?] and Olle Jonsen Eidem (canvas 110 left page). Married on Ascension Day, 29 May 1783: 'Copul. Soldat Bersvend Pedersen Eidem & Kirsten Engelbrechtsd. Berge' (canvas 111 left page). Selbu ministerialbok 1757-1801. This replaces the inferred 'before 1787'. Engelbrecht is a form of Ingebrigt, so she is the Kiersten Ingebrigtsdatter of the 1801 census. He was a soldier at the time of his marriage. ¶10

Bygdebok (Probable source only): the word-box reconstruction of Selbu i fortid og naatid vol. 4 (page image not read) has, under the heading Brennhaugen on p. 206, a holder '1792-1825... Persen... 1754,... 1825' with a wife 'Kirsti' '1755,... 1836', the year 1783, and children 'Marit, 1785, 1786', 'Per, 1787, 1789', 'Per, 1790', 'Torsten, 1795' (with a Ragnhild of Yster-Eidem), 'Kari, 1796, 1797' and 'Jon, 1796, 1798'. The years 1754-1825, the 1783 marriage, the wife Kirsti and four of the children agree exactly with the parish register, including the two children who died young. So the entry is almost certainly this man. If so, Brennhaugen (Braendhaugen) was the family's cottage holding from his time, passing to his son Peder (holder 1826-1867 on the same page), and later held by Peder's widow Mali Evensdatter and by Kari and Paul Ingebrigtsen. On p. 199 a 'Torsten, 1795,... 1883' is holder at Haugen 1827-1874, married to a Ragnhild born 1799; he is probably the son Torsten. ¶12

Eidem in the 1801 census was a large farm of eleven households and seventy-seven people, of which this is one of the cottager households. His father was a Peder, dead before February 1801. ¶2

1801 census: Selbu, rural residence 0034 Eidum, household 07: Bersvend Pederssen, 47, husband, both in first marriage, 'huusmand med jord' (cottager with land); Kiersten Ingebrigtsdatter, 46, his wife; their children ('Deres boern') Peder Bersvendsen, 14, unmarried, and Torsten Bersvendsen, 5; and Marit Jonsdatter, 73, 'hans moder' (his mother), widow after her first marriage, 'fattig' (poor). There is no daughter in the household. A Tosten Bersvends., 30, 'Indst.' (lodger) at Amdal in the 1825 census, with a two-year-old son named Bersvend, is a possible later trace of the younger son (not proved). ¶8

Silver tax (soelvskatten) 1816: 'Bersvend Peders.', husmand at Eidem*, paid on 7 September 1819 and again entered 3 March 1821 (deposit 0-1-0, remark 'Kioebt af Banken'), Digitalarkivet pt00000000322924 and pt00000000323259. If he is our man, he was alive in March 1821. The second Bersvend Pedersen of Eidum (b. about 1761) could also be this taxpayer, so the identification is probable, not proved. ¶9

Death, inferred: before 27 November 1825. His wife Kiersten (Kirsten) Ingebrigtsdatter is entered in the 1825 census of Selbu, 0060* Eidem, as 'Hmd. Enk.' - a cottager's WIDOW - living with their son Peder Bersvendsen. In 1801 both were in their first marriage. Whether or not she married again after 1801, the word Enke means Bersvend was dead by the census day. No burial has been found yet; the indexed Selbu burials 1801-1825 are the next search. WikiTree 'Eidem-41' (d. 1840) conflicts with this and is not followed. ¶4

Death and burial: died 22 May 1825 and buried 29 May 1825 at Selbu, aged 71. Minister's register (ministerialbok) 1824-1842, burials 1825, p. 362 no. 21, indexed as 'Bersvend Pedersen Eidem', age 71, birth year 1754 calculated. The clerk's duplicate (klokkerbok) 1816-1831, burials 1825, pp. 262-263 no. 14, indexed as 'Bersvend Pederssoen', gives death 23 May and the same burial date, with no age. The minister's date is preferred. Index only; the scans were not read. This replaces the inferred 'before 27 November 1825' that rested on his wife being a widow in the 1825 census. ¶6

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Rated Probable: the household is certain, the link to the Peder Bersvendsen who married at Eidem in 1819 rests on his being the only candidate of that name on that farm. His own baptism is in the untranscribed Selbu register 1757-1801. ¶3

Confidence raised from Probable to Confirmed, and why. The 1801 census (Eidum household 07, re-read 13 Oct 2026) lists Peder Bersvendsen, 14, as 'Deres boern' - the child of both Bersvend and Kiersten. The 1825 census places that Peder at Eidem, called 'Son' of the widow Kirsten, with his son Bersvend aged 5 1/2. That boy's baptism (27 March 1820, father Peder Bersvends, mother Gertrud Nilsd of Eidem) identifies him as the Peder Bersvendsen who married Giertru Nielsdatter in 1819 and later fathered Kari Pedersdatter. The step that rested on 'only candidate of that name on that farm' now rests on records. ¶5

The case for this identification 1

Identity of the burial: age 71 gives a birth year of about 1754, exactly that of our Bersvend (47 in the 1801 census). The 1801 census also has a second Bersvend Pedersen at Eidum, born about 1761, a widower 'husmand og nyrydder' (cottager and new-clearer); he would have been about 64 in 1825, so the age points to our man. His wife Kirsten was a widow by 27 November 1825, which agrees. ¶7

Records that disagree 1

Conflicts in the bygdebok reading, to settle on the page image (nb.no vol. 4, p. 206, top): the boxes show the surname 'Persen' but the given name was not captured, and 'Bersvend' has no hit anywhere on pp. 184-207, so the book may give him another form of the name or the search may have missed it; a 'Marit' stands on the same line as the 1783 marriage year and 'Kirsti' - perhaps his mother Marit Jonsdatter, perhaps a misplaced box; the 1801 census places the household at 'Eidum' rather than naming Brennhaugen, which is consistent with a cottage under Eidem but is not proof of it; the twins Kari and Jon of 1796 are not yet found in the parish register; and the book's death year 1836 for Kirsti is untested (see Kiersten Ingebrigtsdatter). The holding's parent farm is disputed elsewhere (LINE.md traps); this note does not settle it. ¶13

Alternatives considered and set aside 2

1762 extra tax list lead (no link made): at Svinås 739, Selbu (landlord Conferentz Raad Schiøller), the couple 'Peder B?rsen' and Marit Jonsdatter have no children over 12 (Bersvend, b. about 1754, was too young to be listed). The patronymic is uncertain: the orchestrator read 'Bi-ersen' or 'Bä-rsen', split over a line break. It could be Bersen (Bersvendsen), which would fit the naming of a son Bersvend after his grandfather, or Biersen (Bjørnsen); read Baarsen (Bårdsen). ¶15a

Caution: in 1758 'Paul Svinaas og Marit Jonsd.' had a son Jon baptised. No Peder with a wife Marit Jonsdatter is at any of the five Eidem holdings in 1762. ¶15b

Searches that found nothing 1

Negative: the 1766-70 protocol 24756 does have a typed register (images 2-9, read in full), and it contains no probate at Eidem and none for a Peder Olsen of Selbu, so Peder Olsen Eidem's probate is not there. The 1743 skifte after his father Ole Pedersen Eidum (24753 fol. 42, 28 Jun 1743) gives Peder Olsen's age as 22 (born about 1721), with sisters Helge 25 and Gunild 10. A father born 1721 fits a son born about 1754, but nothing ties Peder Olsen Eidem to Bersvend, and his 1762 wife was an Ane, not Marit Jonsdatter. Father still unknown. ¶20

Open questions and the next record to read 4

Would settle it: Bersvend Pedersen's confirmation (about 1770) or marriage (1783) naming his home farm, or a probate after Peder. ¶15c

Lead only, no link: the probate after Thorkild Pedersen of Eidum, Selbu, held 15 Oct 1790 (24763 fol. 248b, view 262), lists his widow Brynnild Ingebrigtsdatter, whose lagverge (legal guardian) was 'Peder Pedersen Eidum', and the children Ole Thorkildsen 22, Ingeborg (married Jon Olsen S[ef]saas), Karen (married Christen Pedersen Holm[?]), Brynnild 17 and Siri. The son's curator was his mother's brother ('Morbroder') Ole Ingebrigtsen Kielstad. Bersvend Pedersen is not named as an heir or guardian in the lines read. Thorkild Pedersen and Peder Pedersen of Eidum are possible brothers of Bersvend, the son of a Peder at Eidem, but no record read ties them together. ¶16

Lead (not a parent claim): the Selbu panteregister 22743 (image 57, under P) shows a Peder Olsen Eidem who took over Eidem by inheritance: 'Peder Olsen Eidem udlagt Gaarden Eidem paa Skifte efter hans afg. Fader Ole Pedersen' (pantebok fol. 432, series A-2, c.1743-44; the Stjør- og Verdal skifte register has Ole Pedersen, Eidem, Selbu, 24753 fol. 42, 1743), then a deed on Eidem from 'Marit Pedersdatter, Ingeborg Pedersdatter, Helge Pedersdatter og Jon Thomesen Lien' (fol. 470/71), a mortgage on Eidem (fol. 431) and a later obligation on Eidem to Justitsraad Schiøller (fol. 560). He was a Peder holding Eidem in the 1740s-50s, so a candidate for Bersvend's father Peder (Bersvend b. c.1754 at Eidem, mother Marit Jonsdatter). Nothing yet ties him to Marit Jonsdatter or to Bersvend, and the Eidem farm had other households (Haldo Bersvendsen d. 1757, Esten Haldosen d. 1765). ¶17a

Next: pantebok A-2/A-3 fol. 560 (Stjør- og Verdal 19751/19752) and any later P- or B-entry in 22743 (images 59-61, 8-14 unread) for a skifte after Peder Olsen Eidem. ¶17b
