Life story

1801 census: Peder Olsen, aged 1, is listed as a child of Ole Pedersen and Malena Pedersdatter at Valstad Lille. He was the younger brother of Peter Olsen Hvalstad. ¶1

1863: He is very probably the 'Peder Ols[en] ibid' (of Valstad) who stood as witness at his niece Guri Anna Petersdatter's wedding on 19 June 1863. ¶3

1865 census: Peder Olsen, 65, born in Nedre Stjørdal, is head of household 01 at Lillevalstad as gaardbruger selveier (farm owner, freeholder). His wife is Marit Pettersdatter, 55. His brother Peter's widow, Doret Elisabet Pedersdatter, and her daughter Mette also live in the household. In the 1875 census Peder Olsen, born 1802, is a kaarmand (retired on a kår) at Valstad lille. He is identified with the infant of 1801 by his name, his age and the farm. This is Probable, because no baptism or other record linking the two has been read. ¶2

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
