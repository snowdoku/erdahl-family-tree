Life story

Married Marie Gjertsdatter Falch 5 Jul 1802 in Tjugum. Burial in Nov 1873 probably his. Parents probable (1801 census). ¶2

Probate 1828: 'Myndig Søn John Bjørnsen Kvalem' (son of age) is an heir, with a share of 10 riksdaler 2 ort 12 skilling, in the probate after his father Bjørn Johnsen Gjerde (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll A 18, fol. 29b-30a, No. 46, 21 Oct 1828). His mother, the widow Thuri Christensdatter Gjerde, is named in the same probate. ¶3
