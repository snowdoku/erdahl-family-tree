Life story

Betrothed 23 Feb 1776 in Treungen as 'Anders Torofsen Hiemdal' to Gunild Svennungsdatter Haugstøl; cautioners Christopher(?) Haugstøl and Halvor Sondeland; married in Treungen church on the 5th Sunday after Trinity 1776 (Kviteseid ministerialbok 1773-1786, image 111, p.106). The same couple appears in 1801. ¶2

As 'Andres Toruvsen Hiemdal' he was cautioner (surety) for Ingebor Thronsdatter Fælle (1779) and Andres Tronsen Fælle (1780), sister and brother of Tore Thronsen Felle. ¶3

1801 census, Hiemdal (Treungen): Anders Toruvsen, 56, husbonde (farmer), with wife Gunild Sveinungsdatter (50), both in their first marriage, children Sveinung (19), Gunnild (17), Ole (13), Turi (8), and his father Toruv Andersen (81, 'husbondens fader'). ¶1
