About the family

Mother of Dordi Johnsdatter Stokke (see her page), rated Probable on the same chain as her husband Jon Halvorsen Velve: Daardi Jonsdatter, 28, is listed in this household among 'Deres born' (their children), and the 1808 bride 'Dordie Jonsdatter Welve' and the 1809 godfather 'Michel Jonsen Velve' tie Dordi of Stokke to this family. ¶2a

Pedigree collapse: this draft makes Jon Mikkelsen Moen (see his page) and Beret (Birthe) Mikkelsdatter (see her page) brother and sister, both children of Mikkel Siursen and Brynild Pedersdatter of Velve 791. Both are already direct ancestors of Palmer Eidem: Jon > Gunder Johnsen Eidem > Beret Gundersdatter > Gunder > Oline > Palmer, and Beret > Dordi Jonsdatter Stokke > Ole Jonsen > Kari > Oline > Palmer. So Gunder Johnsen Eidem and Dordi Jonsdatter were first cousins, Oline's parents Gunder and Kari were third cousins, and Mikkel and Brynild stand twice in Palmer's tree as his 4x-great-grandparents. This rests on two Probable links, not on a record that names the relationship; if either link fails, the collapse disappears. ¶6

Her mother's probate, 20 Feb 1786 (24762 fol. 521b), names '4. Datteren Berithe Michelsdatter gift med Jon Haldorsen Welve', which confirms her marriage to Jon Halvorsen Velve and her place among the children of Brynild Pedersdatter. Her brother Jon Michelsen Moen is named in the same entry. ¶9

Life story

1762 extra tax list (Probable identification): the daughter 'Brit' among the six children over 12 of Mikel Siursen and Brynild Pedersdatter at Velve 791 is very probably her; Brit, Birthe and Beret are forms of one name. She married at Velve in 1772 as Birthe Mikkelsdatter, and she lived at Velve all her married life. In 1762 she would have been about 15, so she was correctly listed as over 12. Why not Confirmed: her baptism (about 1747) falls in the 1747-56 gap in the Selbu church books; a probate after Mikkel Siursen or Brynild Pedersdatter naming her as heir would settle it. At the 1809 baptism of her grandson Ole, Dordi's son, the godfather Michel Jonsen Velve was her own son, not her father. ¶5

Written 'Birthe Michelsdatter' / 'Birthe Micheldd.' at her betrothal (8 Jun 1772), marriage (26 Jul 1772) and her daughter Dorethe's baptism (4 Apr 1773), Selbu ministerialbok 16311; Birthe and Beret are the same name. Confidence raised to Confirmed as Dordi's mother. ¶4a

1801 census: 'Beret Mikkelsdatter', 54, 'Hans kone' (his wife) of Jon Halvorssen at Waelve (Velve) farm 0048, household 05, Selbu, in her first marriage (his second). Birth about 1747 is inferred from the census age. ¶1

1801 census: Beret Mikkelsdatter, 54, at Wælve with Jon Halvorssen. Burial candidates: 'Beret Michelsdatter', aged 82, 26 Dec 1830, residence blank (pg00000007125622 - best fit, b. c.1748); 'Bereth Michelsd.', 10 Nov 1816, name only (pg00000007058478). Unresolved. ¶8

Burial lead, not identified: 'Beret Michelsdatter', aged 82, died 22 December and buried 26 December 1830 (Digitalarkivet index pg00000007125622, no residence). Name and age (birth about 1748 against about 1747 from the 1801 census) fit, and she is the only Beret or Birthe Mikkelsdatter of the right age in the Selbu burial index; the other entries are 1805 at Efjen aged 53, an undated-age 1816 entry indexed twice, and a woman born 1833. Without a residence it is a name-only match. If it is she, Jon Halvorsen was a widower by December 1830. The scan (Selbu ministerialbok 1824-1842, burials 1830) would show farm and husband. ¶7

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Caution: Jon was in his second marriage, and a census 'their children' column can include stepchildren, so Beret's maternity rests on that column and on her age (about 25 at Dordi's birth in 1772). Dordi's baptism about 1772 (Selbu ministerialbok 16311) would name the mother and settle it. The naming of Dordi's brother Mikkel (b. ~1791) after Beret's father Mikkel fits but does not prove it. ¶2b
