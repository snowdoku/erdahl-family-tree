About the family

Daughter of Captain Job Prince of Boston and Elizabeth Allen; widow of David Higgins (died April 1783), by whom she had sons including James and David Higgins; married Captain William Creed about 1783-1785 (compiled genealogy and Murray's study). ¶1

Mother of Samuel Prince Creed, baptised 1802 as son of William and Elizabeth Creed (PEI baptism index, read). ¶4

Life story

Birth: 'Elizabeth daughter of Job Prince and Elizabeth his Wife', born in Boston on 28 May 1753 (Boston Births, Record Commissioners vol. 24, p. 282, read from the page image). She is the only Elizabeth Prince in the Boston birth register between about 1745 and 1758. The link of this birth to Elizabeth (Prince) Higgins Creed is Probable. It rests on her brother James (born 1758) settling her share of her father's estate, her son Job Prince Creed's name, the family's 'Prince's Pasture' in Boston, and William Creed's partnership with Capt. Job Prince of Boston. It would be confirmed by Job Prince's Suffolk probate (docket 20807, will 1798, or 19540, administration 1790) or by her own administration file (docket 23931). ¶5

First marriage: 'David Higgins Esq. & Elizabeth Prince' were married in Boston on 3 June 1773 by the Rev. Simeon Howard (Boston Marriages 1752-1809, Record Commissioners vol. 30, p. 65, read from the page image). She was then 20. The record names no parents. It is taken to be hers because Murray names her first husband as David Higgins, who died in April 1783, and their son, also named David Higgins, was her administrator in 1812 and the 'half brother' of her Creed sons in 1817. No probate for a David Higgins was found in the printed Suffolk County probate index (vol. 2, Higgins). ¶6

PEI land, 1775 (enrichment, no grade change): the Prince Edward Island land register of 1795-1797 re-enters a deed by which 'David Higgins of the Island of Saint John Esqr', for £1,000 sterling paid 'By Job Prince of Boston, in the County of Suffolk... Merchant', conveyed to Job Prince one undivided third part of a lot or township in Kings County (bounded north by the tract of Number fifty seven), with mills and buildings. 'Elizabeth my wife' joined to release her dower; David Higgins acknowledged it at Boston on 18 February 1775 and John Furnace swore on 13 August 1784 that he saw 'David Higgins + Elizabeth his wife' sign; it was registered at Charlottetown on 13 September 1784 by Thos Desbrisay (FamilySearch, PEI Land Registers 1795-1797, images 325-326, ark 3:1:3Q9M-CSRV-G96J; transcript read, image 325 seen). It shows her, twenty months after her 1773 marriage, as the wife of David Higgins conveying Island land to Capt. Job Prince of Boston. The deed states no relationship between the parties, so it supports but does not by itself prove that Job was her father. ¶15

Diary evidence: Hezekiah Prince's diary, 18 Nov 1793 (New England Magazine n.s. 9 (Feb. 1894): 727-728), lists the eight children of his uncle Capt. Job Prince of Boston (m. Elizabeth Allen, d. 1790) as 'Hezekiah..., Capt. Job, Jr.,... Thomas, James, and Samuel; also three daughters, Sarah, Elisebeth, and Abigal'. This proves Job and Elizabeth (Allen) Prince had a daughter Elizabeth who lived to adulthood, matching the Boston birth of 28 May 1753, but it says nothing of her marriage, so it does not by itself tie her to William Higgins or William Creed. Confidence unchanged. ¶11

Her estate: on 19 April 1812 her son David Higgins, as administrator, filed an inventory with the Suffolk County, Massachusetts, Probate Court (docket 23931, Records Book 110, page 389), describing her as 'formerly of Boston but last of Prince Edward Island, a singlewoman, deceased intestate' - the only asset being USD 1,048.88 received from her brother James Prince, her share of her father's estate (cited by Murray; not yet read). ¶2

Probate index: the printed Suffolk County probate index lists 'Creed, Elizabeth, 1812, administration, 23931' (Index to the probate records of the County of Suffolk, vol. 1, p. 250, read from the page image), confirming the docket number and year cited by Murray. ¶7

Caution on 'singlewoman': in Massachusetts probate usage the word normally means an unmarried woman, a widow usually being styled 'widow'. The inventory therefore fits this Elizabeth (a widow) less neatly than an unmarried Elizabeth Creed would. It remains consistent with her - a Higgins administering a Creed estate funded by a Prince - and the stronger link is still the 1817 deed in which her Creed sons act 'by our half brother, David Higgins'. The administration bond and docket file 23931 would state the administrator's relationship. ¶9

On 26 May 1817 her sons 'Job Prince Creed, Joseph Bennett Creed and Samuel Creed, all of Prince Edward Island, by our half brother, David Higgins, who is our lawful Attorney', conveyed their interest in Prince's Pasture, Boston, later the site of Massachusetts General Hospital (Suffolk County Registry of Deeds, quoted by Murray). That deed is the record that would prove Joseph Bennett Creed her son. ¶3

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Upgrade: graded on her link to her son Joseph Bennett Creed. Records read on FamilySearch: (1) Power of attorney dated at Three Rivers, PEI, 1 Oct 1806 (recorded Suffolk 28 July 1809; image 529 bottom right, ark 3:1:3QSQ-G9Z3-QXNT, image read): 'Know all men by these presents that we William Creed of Three Rivers in Prince Edward Island Esquire and Elizabeth Creed otherwise Prince his wife have made ordained constituted and appointed... James Prince of Boston... Esquire and David Higgins of Boston... Mariner' their attorneys to sell lands and take out administration on any estate; witness Job Creed, who swore on 1 Oct 1806 that he saw 'William Creed and Elizabeth Creed otherwise Prince his wife' execute it. (2) Quitclaim of 26 July 1809 (images 530-531, transcript read): 'We James Prince Esquire and Thomas I. Prince Merchant both of Boston... and Elizabeth [Creed] formerly of said Boston but now of... Three Rivers in Prince Edward Island... Widow acting by her attornies James Prince Esquire and David Higgins... by virtue of her power... October the first Eighteen hundred and six' release to Samuel Parkman and Harrison Gray Otis land at West Boston on Cambridge Street and Charles River (Prince's Pasture land); signed 'Elizabeth Creed by her attornies James Prince, David Higgins'. (3) Release of 26 May 1817 (images 72-73, transcript read): 'we Job P. Creed, Joseph Bennett Creed, and Samuel Creed... all of Prince Edwards Island... Gentlemen' to 'Mrs Elizabeth Prince of Boston... Widow' of their rights in 'Princes Pasture... the part which it is contemplated to be sold to the Trustees of the General Hospital by the heirs of Job Prince', signed 'by our half brother David Higgins, who is our lawful Attorney'; acknowledged before Saml D. Parker JP 30 May 1817. (4) Deposition of Samuel D. Parker, attorney, sworn 7 Nov 1823 and recorded in the Suffolk deeds (image 262, ark 3:1:3QS7-99Z3-9THJ, image read; image 263): 'it having been discovered that the Title to part of the land called Princes Pasture lying in said Boston was in James Prince, Samuel Prince, Mrs Tucker the wife of John Tucker Esquire, David Higgins and others heirs at Law of old Captain Job Prince the father of James Prince, the said heirs made & executed a deed of release or conveyance to the late Mrs Elizabeth Prince' (the widow of Job Prince Jr.); Parker drew those deeds, which is the 1817 Creed release that he took the acknowledgment of. (5) Elizabeth Creed's administration, docket 23931 (file papers images 2029-2034, read): bond 23 Mar 1812 of David Higgins with John Tucker and Henry D. Sedgwick; inventory '$1048.88 received of James Prince'. Continuity: the 1806 power names William Creed's wife as 'Elizabeth Creed otherwise Prince' (born Prince), with David Higgins as one of her attorneys; by July 1809 she is the widow Elizabeth Creed of Three Rivers, joint owner with James and Thomas I. Prince of the Prince land; in 1812 David Higgins administers her estate; in 1817 Joseph Bennett Creed and his brothers Job P. and Samuel, all of PEI, act 'by our half brother David Higgins' on the same Prince's Pasture rights, as heirs of old Capt. Job Prince (deposition 1823). Half brothers surnamed Creed and Higgins share their mother, and the wife of William Creed of Three Rivers in 1806 was this Elizabeth. No other wife of William Creed is named in any record. The 'singlewoman' caution note is superseded: she was a widow (1809 deed), and the 1812 clerk's word does not change the identification. ¶13

Audit 11 (2 Nov 2026): Confirmed kept, graded on her link to her son Joseph Bennett Creed. No record calls Joseph her son in so many words. But the 1817 release, named in her notes as the test record and now read, has Joseph Bennett Creed and his brothers act 'by our half brother David Higgins'. A relationship word between siblings proves a shared parent (as the BRIEF accepts for a 'Farbroder' on a probate card). Here the shared parent must be the mother, since the surnames differ. David Higgins's mother is fixed by records naming the relationships: he is an heir at law of old Capt. Job Prince (1823 deposition, image checked by the auditor) and an heir in part of Job's wife Elizabeth (1814 deed). The only daughter of Job's who married a Higgins is the Elizabeth Prince who married David Higgins in 1773 (with the 1775 deed of 'David Higgins + Elizabeth his wife'). Sarah married Tucker, and Abigail died unmarried in 1792. In 1806 William Creed's wife was 'Elizabeth Creed otherwise Prince' (image checked), and her attorney was David Higgins. The Creed brothers released as heirs of Job Prince in the same set of deeds. There is no rival mother: Joseph, born about 1788, was born after William Creed's union with her (Job Prince Creed, born 1785). Clean-up: the sentence 'Her grade (Probable) is unchanged' is superseded by the upgrade. ¶16

Corrections 2

Correction: Her estate inventory has now been read (Suffolk County Probate Records, Records Book 110, p. 389, docket 23931; FamilySearch DGS 7703159, image 580). It reads: 'Inventory of the Estate of Elizabeth Creed late of Prince Edwards Island deceased by David Higgins administrator... 1812 April 19th Received of James Prince in Cash $1048..88', 'the whole of the personal Estate within my knowledge', signed by David Higgins at Boston on 23 June 1812 and sworn at the Probate Court on 29 June 1812, where she is called 'Elizabeth Creed formerly of Boston... but late of Prince Edwards Island singlewoman deceased intestate'. So 19 April 1812 is the date the cash was received from James Prince, not the filing date, and the record itself does not call David Higgins her son, James Prince her brother, or the money a share of her father's estate; those are Murray's readings. ¶8

Correction: she was already a widow on 26 July 1809 (Suffolk deed, image 530), which agrees with Capt. William Creed's death on 11 April 1809. ¶14

Searches that found nothing 2

Death search (newspaper index, negative so far): the AAS index of obituaries in the Boston Centinel 1784-1840 has no entry for an Elizabeth Creed or Elizabeth Higgins, nor for Capt. William Creed, in vols. 1, 2, 4 and 5; vol. 3 could not be read online. Her death in Prince Edward Island before April 1812 may simply not have been reported in Boston. ¶10

Probate docket (negative for heirs): the Suffolk docket book entry for her estate reads '23931 Elizabeth Creed | 1812 Mch 23 Bond Adm 206:244 | Letter 188:156 | June 30 Inv 110:389' (Suffolk probate docket book nos. 23600-28799, p. 92; FamilySearch DGS 7703037 image 54; image read). The file held only the bond, the letter of administration and the inventory (already read). No account, distribution or list of heirs was ever recorded, so her probate cannot name her heirs or state whose estate her share came from. Her grade (Probable) is unchanged. The record that can still settle her parentage is a Suffolk deed: the 1817 Prince's Pasture conveyance by her Creed sons, or a deed by William Creed and his wife. The Suffolk grantor index 1639-1799 lists William Creed as grantor in five Boston deeds: 8 Jan 1783 to Joseph Robinson (Lib. 136 fol. 235), 23 Sep 1783 to John Ballard (139:263), 22 Apr 1784 'et ux.' to Joseph Lee by executor (142:242), 19 May 1784 to Anne Conant (143:113) and 12 Jul 1788 'et ux.' to John Williams (163:87) (DGS 7453722 image 389; image read). The two 'et ux.' deeds should name his wife; they have not been read (142:242 was not found at fol. 242 of DGS 7442680 images 533-534, so the folio or volume reading needs checking). ¶12
