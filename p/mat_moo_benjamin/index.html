About the family

Probable brother of Grace Amelia Moore: one of the sons aged 16-21 in Richard Moore's 1861 household. ¶1

Children baptised later in life (PEI baptisms index, parents Benjamin and Sarah): William John b. 7 December 1876 at Abney, bapt. 10 August 1918; Richard Herbert b. 19 April 1878, bapt. 8 June 1902; John Sidney b. 5 June 1887, bapt. 3 June 1889. The son named Richard supports the identification of Richard Moore as his father. ¶6

Life story

Residence: Abney, Lot 64, the settlement called Toronto on Lake's 1863 map. A compiled Bull family genealogy (lead, not proof) gives his birth as 8 January 1841, matching the 1901 census, and lists ten children with Sarah Bull, including Margaret McCauley (b. 20 March 1872, baptised 5 December 1872 at the Bible Christian church, Murray Harbour) and Martin L. (b. 23 December 1883, who married his cousin Mary Ann, daughter of Albert Moore and Annie Bull). ¶8

Marriage: Benjamin Moor married Sarah Bull on 16 August 1864 (PEI marriage registers index, rec 13697, image FS_004594919_00191). ¶2

1880 Meacham atlas: Benj. Moore's farm was a narrow strip at Toronto, Lot 64, between Wm. Moore's and Jno. Moore's. ¶5

Sons' deaths recorded by Lauretta Brooks as 'Ben's son': Martin L. Moore (67, 4 December 1950, Abney) and Herbert Moore (74, 5 December 1951). Reuben Moore Sr. 'dropped dead' at High Bank on 11 July 1935. ¶7

1881 census, Lot 64: Benjamin Moore, 39, Baptist, farmer; wife Sarah, 34, Methodist; children Mary 15, Reuben 12, Margret 10, Joseph 7, William 5, Richard 3, Lucy 0; and Jane Bull, 66, born Scotland, Anglican (his mother-in-law). ¶3

1901 census, Lot 64 (page 7, dwelling 68, LAC z000123387, read): Benjamin Moore, head, born 8 January 1841, aged 60, Baptist, farmer; wife Sarah; sons John (b. 19 June 1887) and Martin, daughter Jane (b. February 1899). The 1881 age (39) and 1891 index (about 1842) agree within a year. ¶4

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
