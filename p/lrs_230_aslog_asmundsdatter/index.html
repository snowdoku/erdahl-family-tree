Life story

Marriage: betrothed 8 May 1756 and married 11 Jul 1756 in Nissedal church to Torgius (Torjus) Alfsen Tvetene (see his page); cautioners Jacob Nordbøe and Tor Tvettene (Kviteseid ministerialbok 1754-1773, source 7981, p.9). Her father was presumably Asmund (Osmund) of Fjone, from her patronymic and farm name; the couple's son Osmund (Jan 1757) carries the name. Fjone people stood godparent to Torgius's children (Sigri Fjone 1767; Joen Fjone and Signe Fjone 1769; Jøren[?] Fjone 1764). ¶2

Widow: named as the widow of Torjus Alfsen of Tvetene, Nissedal, at his probate registered 16 Oct 1786 (25678 No 591, view 196; name read 'Aslet Osmundsdatter'); their children were only Margith Torjusdatter (m. Alf Thoresen) and Kari Torjusdatter, 19. She is the mother of those two daughters, not of Kari Torjusdatter of Søftestad. ¶4

How sure are we?

Speculative. A plausible candidate only. Treat this link as a question, not a fact.

1 on the grade · 1 on the identification

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Confidence: Speculative. Her marriage to Torgius Alfsen Tvetene (Torjus Alfsen) in 1756 is from a primary record. She is entered as his wife only. She is not linked as the mother of Kari Torjusdatter of Søftestad (see her page), because Torgius's own link to Kari is Speculative and no baptism entry read names the mother of his children. Would support a link: the Nissedal burials of 1757-1769 (source 7981) with no burial of hers, the marriages of 1757-1769 with no second marriage of Torgius, a churching entry naming her after a birth, or a probate after Torgius or Asløg naming Kari. ¶3

The case for this identification 1

Identity: Asløg Asmundsdatter of Fjone, Nissedal (then Kviteseid prestegjeld), Telemark. The 1756 marriage entry writes her forename 'Aslet' (or 'Aslev'); it is taken to be Asløg. Born before about 1740: inferred only from her marriage as a maiden (pige) in 1756; her baptism (before 1754, earlier than the surviving book read) has not been found. ¶1
