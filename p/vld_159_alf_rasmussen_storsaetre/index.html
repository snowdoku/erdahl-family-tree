About the family

Father of Rasmus Alfsen of Blindem (see his page), Probable; graded on his link to Rasmus. The 1706 probate names a son Rasmus Alfsen aged 30 (b. about 1676), who in 1701 and 1704 was a servant at Holen in Borgund (the sorenskriver Castberg's farm), recorded as 'Rasmus Alfsen Seter' and 'Rasmus Alfsen Stoorseter af Gryten skibbered'. Rasmus Alfsen of Blindem married in Borgund on 1 Jan 1710 and raised children at Blindem from 1712; his first son was named Alf. Rasmus Alfsen Seter is the only Rasmus Alfsen in the indexed 1701 manntall for Sunnmøre, and no other Rasmus Alfsen appears in the Borgund records 1698-1730 except the Blindem man. ¶3a

Life story

1664-66 censuses: Alf is on Setter in Skodje in 1664 (farmers Olluff and Alff) and in 1666 ('Alff 22', bracketed with 'Rasmus 70' on one holding, p. 372). Father, Probable: that Rasmus, b. about 1596 (see his page). His wife is still unknown; no women are named in these censuses. ¶4

Earlier records of the same family: the 1701 manntall lists Alf, leilending, aged 65, on Setter (Skodje); the 1704 list of marines for Sunnmøre lists, on Seter in Grytten skipreide, Alf, bonde, 68, with sons Rasmus Alfs. 26 (servant to the sorenskriver), Lars 21, Aamund 16 and Knud 14, the same sons as in the 1706 probate. He was buried at Skodje in 1706 as Alf Rasmussen of Store Sæter; the burial index gives a birth year of 1643, the censuses about 1636. His patronymic shows his father was a Rasmus. ¶2

The 1 Apr 1701 skifte after Alf Rasmussen's wife cannot be read in the probate protocols: Sunnmøre Skifteprotokoll 01 (source 24544) ends with entries of Sep 1700 (views 317-319; view 320 is the end target), and Skifteprotokoll 02A (source 24545) and its typed register begin in 1703. The protocol for 1700-1703 has not survived. Test record (revised): the morsarv clause of the 1706 protocol entry itself (24545, fol. 300b, view about 311), which may name the dead mother; otherwise the Sunnmøre tingbok for 1701 or a 1701-02 guardianship record (overformynderi). ¶6

Probate (scan read): 'Stor Settren i Gryttens Skibbrede. Anno 1706 d. 10 Sept.' a registration and division was held after the late 'Alf Rasmussen Stor Settren' between his 7 children, 'nemlig Rasmus Alfsen gl. 30 aar, Lars Alfsen', 'Aamund Alfsen gl. 18 aar, Knud Alfsen gl. 16 aar, samt Brite Alfsdatter gl. 28 aar, Brite yngre gl. 23 aar og Marithe Alfsdatter gl. 7 aar' (Lars's age is hard to read). No widow is named, so his wife had died. Sunnmøre skifteprotokoll 3A 02 fol. 300b, image 24545/311. ¶1

The Sunnmøre probate card for the 1706 skifte (77797/2983, filed under 'Storsæteren', Skodje; it is the only Storsæteren card 1689-1728) adds a line the protocol summary did not record: 'Gjeld + morsarv til barna iflg. skifte av 1/4-1701 - 26 rdr', i.e. the estate owed the children 26 rdr of mother's inheritance under a skifte held on 1 Apr 1701. So Alf's wife, the mother of the children, died before April 1701 and a skifte after her was held then. That skifte has no card of its own under Storsæteren, and Sunnmøre skifteprotokoll 01 (source 24544) is labelled 1689-1700 (its view 300 is s. 287b-288a); the 1701-1702 entries must be at the end of 24544 or in a separate volume. The card's ages (Lars 20, Knud 13) differ slightly from the protocol reading (Knud 16), which the 1704 marine list (Knud 14) supports. ¶5a

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Not Confirmed: no record states that the Rasmus of Blindem came from Store Sætre (different farms, four years between the 1706 probate and the 1710 marriage). ¶3b

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Test record: the skifte of 1 Apr 1701 at Stor-Sætre (Grytten skipreide), which should name the mother of Rasmus Alfsen and the 'deres Børn'. ¶5b
