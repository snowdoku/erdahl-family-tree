About the family

Brother of Andrea (Mrs. Peter Hanson). Her 1925 obituary names 'a brother, Ole Dakken living in Lake Mills'. His own obituary (Lake Mills Graphic, 5 Oct 1927) says he was born in Norway on 1 Sep 1849, son of Eric and Margret (Olson) Dakken. The family emigrated in 1851 to Dane County, Wisconsin. ¶1

Life story

He was baptized 16 Sep 1849 in Svennes, Nord-Aurdal, with a recorded birth date of 1 Sep 1849, son of Erik Olsen and Marit Olsdr. The date matches his gravestone exactly. ¶3

He came to Winnebago County in 1869 and farmed in Mount Valley Township for six years, then in Worth County for seventeen years, before retiring to Lake Mills. He married first Signe Halvorsdatter Onstad on 12 Jun 1870 (she d. 16 Oct 1900), and second Lena (Thompson) Larson in 1906 (she d. 1925). He had eight children, including Edward, Henry, Martin and Sena (Mrs. Reveland, d. 1888). ¶2
