Life story

Baptized 'Anne' at Borgund on 10 March 1745 (entry 20), daughter of Alv Rasmussen of Blindem. The date given is the baptism date; the transcription names only the child and the father. ¶1

Probate: the 1778 probate after her father Alv Rasmussen of Blindem (3A 26, fol. 193a) names a daughter 'Anne Alfs datter', married to Einer Larsen; his farm is read tentatively as Nakken. A debt to 'Anne Alfsdatt.' is also entered on fol. 193b. This is very probably her, as the only Anne baptized to Alv. ¶3
