About the family

ONE OF THE TWO 'MISSING' CHILDREN. The 1904 printed biography of her brother Peter P. Eidem says the family had five children; three were known to the family in America (Peder, Mikal and John) and two were not. Anne is one of the two. ¶1

She is named for her paternal grandmother Anne Paulsdatter, the eldest daughter taking the husband's mother's name on the ordinary Selbu custom. ¶3

Life story

Baptism: born 17 November 1860, baptised 27 December 1860 at Selbu church. Selbu parish register 1860-1877, page 11 serial no. 161. Father Paul Ingebrigtsen, mother Karen Pedersd:, residence Eidum. The parallel entry is in the 1859-1868 volume. ¶2

Baptism: baptised 27 December 1860 in Selbu, the second of the five children of Paul (Pal) Ingebrigtsen and Kari (Karen) Pedersdatter, cottagers at Brennhaugen under the farm Eidem. The register entry gives the father as 'Paul Ingebrigtsen' and the mother as 'Karen Pedersd', with the mother's residence written 'Eidum' - the older spelling of Eidem. Church book from Selbu parish 1860-1877 (1664P), births and baptisms, page 11, serial no. 161. Her birth date is not carried in the index; Selbu children were normally baptised within three to eight weeks, so she was born in November or December 1860. ¶10

1865 census: Braendhaugen, Selbu, person 005, 'Anne Paulsdatter', 6, their daughter, born Selbu parish. ¶4

1865 census: aged 6 at Brendhaugen (rural residence 0065, census district 005 'Femte'), in the household of her grandmother Mali Evensdatter, the widowed pladsbrugerske (a woman holding a cottager's plot in her own right), with her parents and brothers Peder and Ingebrigt. ¶11

Context: she is one of the 'two unidentified' of the five children of Paul Eidem that the 1904 printed biography of her brother Peter counted (Compendium of History and Biography of Central and Northern Minnesota, p. 469). She had not previously been given a place in this tree. Whether she emigrated is unknown: she is not in the party of 25 July 1900 that took her brother Peder and Peder's wife Kari back to Marietta, Minnesota. ¶13

Printed source: the family is written up in Selbu i fortid og natid, bind 4 (Selbu kommune, 1992), chapter BRENNHAUGEN, pages 205-207. The name 'Anne' and the year 1860 both occur on page 206, together with her parents' names and her brothers' birth years. The pages are readable only from a Norwegian IP address; see the selbuboka dossier for the method. ¶14

Two agents found this person independently on the same day, one from the Selbu birth register and one from the baptism entry, and gave them separate ids; the records are merged here under the register's id, which carries the exact birth date. ¶15

Godmother: she stood as one of the six faddere at the baptism of her youngest brother John on 29 October 1876, entered as 'Ane Paulsdtr.' in the Selbu register 1860-1877, folio 26 entry 122. She was fifteen. This is independent proof that she was alive and in the parish in October 1876, which matters because she is missing from the 1875 census. ¶9

Death and burial: died 21 August 1885 and was buried 23 August 1885 at Selbu, aged 24 years 9 months. The minister's register (Selbu 1878-1891, burials p. 387 no. 33) enters her as 'Ane Poulsdtr', birth year 1860, place Eidim (Eidem); the parish clerk's copy (Selbu 1869-1888, burials p. 350 no. 60) as 'Anne Paulsdr', age '25 a'. She is given no husband's name and no married style, so she died unmarried. ¶6

This is why nobody in America had ever heard of her. She died at Eidem on 21 August 1885; her mother and her two youngest brothers sailed from Trondheim on 8 April 1886, seven and a half months later. Her father had been dead three and a half years. Of the six children she is the only one buried in the same Selbu churchyard as her father. ¶7

1875 census: absent from the Braendhaugen household at the age of 15, so out in service somewhere in the parish - the normal fate of a cottager's daughter at that age. Her later life is a brick wall; see the findings dossier. ¶5

She is NOT in the 1875 census of Selbu: a search of the whole 1875 Selbu return for any female surnamed Paulsd- born 1855-1865 returns eight women and none is hers, and a search on the given name An- born 1859-1862 returns a hundred with no match. At fourteen she was out of the parish, or the enumerator missed her. Her confirmation, about 1875-76, is the next record that would place her. ¶8

1875 census: absent from the Brendhaugen household. The wave-3 selbu-eidem dossier records her as 'out in service' at 15, which is the ordinary course for a cottager's daughter in Selbu at that age. ¶12
