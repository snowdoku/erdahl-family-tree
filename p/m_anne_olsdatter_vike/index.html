Life story

Probate (scan read): the registration of Anders Olsen Wiige's estate on 19 Dec 1818 names his widow (Enken) as Anne Olsdatter (reading probable), so she outlived him. ¶3

Bygdebok (Gards- og slektshistorie for Vestnes - Tresfjord 1: Tresfjord, 1973, nb.no item ae4aba9fff7927209ca0129fc93fb7a0, canvases 150-151, rebuilt from content-search word boxes): under Sør-Sylte, holder no. 3, 'OLE OLSON... SØR-SYLTE (1711-1772)', married 1740 to Berit (about 1725-1785); children 'Ole 1741-, Anne 44-, Marit 47-,... Berit 61-'; skifte after Ole 1773 (he died 15.12.1772, aged 62). Child no. 2: 'Anne... 1785 gift til Vike m.... Anders Olsen Vike' - this is her, the maiden Anne Olsdatter of Søer-sylte who married the widower Anders Olsen of Wiike in Advent 1785/30 Jan 1786. The book thus names her parents as Ole Olsen Sør-Sylte and his wife Berit, and her birth as about 1744, which matches her 1801 age of 57. She is the stepmother of Lasse Andersen, so these are not direct ancestors and are not added. ¶4a

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 correction · 1 open question

Corrections 1

Correction: Anne Olsdatter, maiden (Pigen) of Søer-sylte, married Anders Olsen, widower (Enkemand) of Wiike, on 30 Jan 1786 at Veøy church, after banns in Advent 1785 (index); not before 1775. She was his second wife. She is the stepmother, not the mother, of Lasse Andersen (b. 1784), whose mother Hilde Knudsdatter died in childbed 1 Dec 1784. The 1818 probate after Anders names all six of his children as 'af første Ægteskab' (of the first marriage), so no surviving child of Anders was hers. ¶2

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Test record: the Romsdal skifte after Ole Olsen Sør-Sylte, 1773 (the bygdebok cites it), which should list a daughter Anne aged about 28. ¶4b
