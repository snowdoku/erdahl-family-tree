About the family

Parents found. Sør-Strand probate of 6 Oct 1722 after Tor Helgesen (card 13727; DA 25285 fol. 558b-559a, view 571): 'begge deris Sammen Auflede... 2de Børn... Sønnen ved Navn Helge Torsen 14 aar gl., Daatteren Jngebor Torsdaatter 8te aar gl.', the mother being the widow Jngebor Joensdatter. So he was born about 1708 (fits ABT 1709), and had a sister Ingebor (about 1714). His paternal uncles Jul and Ole Helgesen were present. Grandfather: Helge Skafhelden of Søndre Strand (d. about Jan 1707 in Christiania; probate DA 25284 s. 331b). ¶14

Life story

Bygdebok (Svenes vol., gnr. 65, canvas 310; the farm is Strand by the density of that name on canvases 303-364, the heading word itself is not rebuilt): 'Hølje Tordsson', 1709-1785, brukar, 'g 1728 m Kari Haraldsdotter'; their children: 1 [name unread] 1729 (m. Arne Julson), 2 Åste 1730 d. 1731, '3 Åste 1732, g 1755 m Ulrik', 4 Tord 1734 brukar, 5 Harald 1737 d. 1769, and (canvas 311) 6 Berit 1740 (m. 1763 Kristoffer Nilsson). The Fodnes chapter (gnr. 67, canvas 423) has the other end: Ulrik, 1735-1812, married 1755 'Åste Høljesdotter' of gnr. 65, 1732-1809. The book makes him son of Tord Høljesson (brukar from 1707, d. young). ¶1

Baptism found: at Svenes ('ved Svennes'), Dom. Oculi, 16 Mar 1732: 'Døbt Hælge Strands barn Aaste'. The godparents were Ole Knudsen [Rø]nnaas[?] or Svennes, Ole Juulsen Stavelien[?], Ingri Nordagre, Anne Ondstad and Martha Kyø[?]; the mother was churched on 14 April. Source: DA 9361 p. 37 (canvas 18, 0-based, right page, second entry). This fits the bygdebok's '3 Åste 1732' (the earlier Åste of 1730 died in 1731) and the 1755 bride 'Aaste Helgesdatter Strand' (p. 100). The page names only the father, so it says nothing about Kari Haraldsdatter. ¶8

His own probate, the test record named above, is now read. Card 13055/13056 (Strand, søndre i nordre Aurdal, Hadeland, Land og Valdres skifteprotokoll 1783-1802, fol. 148b, 8 Feb 1786) and the protocol (DA 25288 fol. 148b-149a, view 155): 'Skifte Forretning paa Gaarden Strande i Ourdahl efter afgangne Mand Helge Tordsen, begyndt 12te Octobr. [1785] og sluttet 8de Febr. 1786', between 'hans Efterlevende Enke Kari Haraldsdatter og deres i Ecteskab tilsammen avlede og i live[?] værende 5 Børn': 1. Thord Helgesen, myndig, farmer at Strand; 2. Harald Helgesen (dead; sons Helge 19 and Harald 15); 3. Ingebor Helgesdatter (dead), married Arne Granum (sons Juel 22 and Helge 19); 4. 'Datteren Aaste Helgesdatter gift med Ulrich Fodnæs'; 5. Berith Helgesdatter (dead), married Christopher Nielsen Rammen (Niels 17, Helge 14, Anne 21, Kari 18). The note of that no carded probate after him exists is superseded: his card is filed under 'Strand, søndre' (13055), and the 17th-18th-century Sør-Strand cards under 'Sør-Strand' (13721-13739). ¶15

Panteregister checked: the Aurdal panteregister 1786-1827 (DA 13864) starts after his death; the farm Strand of the bygdebok's gnr. 65 is probably No. 15 Sørstrand there (view 33, fol. 15), where 'Tord Helgesen' (his son Tord, b. 1734, per the bygdebok) owned 1 hud 1 skind 3/4 mæler and deeded it to his 'Søn Helge Tordsen' on 10 Apr 1799 (tinglyst 22 Oct 1802), and earlier sold a part to Mons Arnesen (tinglyst 17 Jul 1790). No entry names Hølje himself or Kari Haraldsdatter. The other Strand farms are No. 13 Mitstrand (view 31) and No. 14 Nordstrand (view 32). ¶6a

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 5

Graded on his link to Aaste Helgesdatter (see her page). ¶2a

Graded Probable: a bygdebok is a compiled secondary source and the passage was rebuilt from word boxes with gaps; dates from it are ABT. Support: her son Helge (Hølje) Uldriksen is named for him, as the Fodnes probate card of 1812-13 already suggested. ¶2b

Grade kept at Probable, graded on his link to Aaste Helgesdatter (see her page). A primary record now ties a Hølje/Helge of Strand to a daughter Aaste born in 1732. But the bride of 1755 is linked to that child only by her forename, patronymic and farm, 23 years later, with no record in between, which is over the 15-20 year limit. Bridge needed: a confirmation (about 1746-48) or a godmother entry naming 'Aaste Helgesdatter Strand' between 1745 and 1755, or a probate after Hølje (about 1785) naming Aaste, wife of Ulrich Fodnes. DA 9361 baptisms 1732 are now fully read for this purpose: canvases 13-28 (read 21, 23, 25 and 27; read 17-20, 22, 24 and 26; 13-16 fall in January and February 1732, before this entry, and were fetched but not needed). ¶10

Upgrade (near-miss):, graded on his link to Aaste Helgesdatter (see her page). New record: the Aurdal confirmation list for Quasimodogeniti 1751 (DA 9361, confirmation section p. 14, canvas 373) has 'Aaste Helgesd: Strand - 22' among the girls; her future husband 'Ulrich Thomesen Fodnæs - 18' is confirmed the same day. Chain: (1) baptism at Svenes, 16 Mar 1732, 'Døbt Hælge Strands barn Aaste' (9361 p. 37, c18; index: father Helge, residence Strand), the record that names the relationship; (2) inside the gap, the father is on the farm: 'Helge Thors.' of Strand has children baptised 12 Dec 1734 and 2 Jul 1737 (index pd00000003647724, pd00000003648898), and he had married Karen Haraldsdatter as 'Helje Thords.' of Søndrestrand on 20 Jun 1728 (9360, pv00000000708027); (3) the 1751 confirmation, 'Aaste Helgesd. Strand', 19 years after the baptism; (4) the betrothal of 11 Aug 1755, 'Ulrich Thommesøn Fodnes og Aaste Helgesdatter Strand' (9361 p. 100), 4 years later; then the Fodnes probate of 1812-13 (her son Helge named for him). The 19-year step meets all three audit-7 conditions: (a) the father is shown on Strand inside the gap (1734, 1737); (b) the far-end record ties the child to that farm by patronymic plus farm; (c) no named rival: the other Helges on Strand farms in the 1730s were Helge Knudsen of Midtstrand (his 1739 probate, DA 25286 p. 279, names no Aaste) and Helge Erichsen of Nordstrand (index: only a daughter Anna, 1 Feb 1733; the book writes his farm 'Nordstrand', and the 1750-51 confirmation lists also write Nordstrand/Mistrand/Sønde Strand separately). All seven Aaste baptisms in the 1730-40 index were checked: only those of Helge Strand (20 Dec 1730 and 16 Mar 1732) have a father Helge. Age contradiction resolved: the list gives her age as 22, not 19. If the age were literal (born about 1728-30), the only Aaste daughter of a Strand Helge in that window is Helge Strand's own Aaste of 20 Dec 1730, whose name was reused in 1732, so she was dead (audit 9 rule 3). Whichever birth is meant, the father is the same man. Ages on this list are estimates (it has catch-up confirmands of 28 and 32), and Ulrich's '18' also looks rounded. Residual, not a named rival: baptisms of 1728-29 (DA 9360) are not indexed and were not read, and Helge Erichsen of Nordstrand's marriage was not found in the 1720-30 marriage index or the 9361 betrothals to Oct 1731. This supersedes the note 'Grade kept at Probable', which asked for exactly this confirmation. ¶11

Upgrade: (2 Nov 2026), back to Confirmed per the test set by Audit 10 (AUDIT-10.md, ruling on Hølje (Helge) Tordsen; draft). Graded on his link to Aaste Helgesdatter (see her page). The test record was the Aurdal baptisms of Jun 1728 - Dec 1729 in DA 9360. I read the scans canvas by canvas (IIIF canvases 751-784, 1-based in the manifest; book pp. 775-808): c752 (p. 776) has his own marriage, 'Copulerede... Helje Thords. Sønderstrand og Karen Harald[sd.]' (Dn. 4 Trin. 1728), which fixes the start; c761 (p. 785) closes the year with 'Saa endis det gamle Aar 1728'; c762 (p. 786) opens 1729; and c784 (p. 808) ends at Christmas 1729 (c785 is a different section in another hand, dated 1730). In that whole window there is NO baptism of an Aaste whose father is a Helge/Hølje of any Strand farm. The children of Strand Helges in the window are: (1) Hølje's own first child, at Aurdal, Invocavit (about 6 Mar) 1729, '1 barn fra Helje Sønderstrand naufn Ingeborr', godparents Knud[?] Strand, Helje Ourstad, Marthe Løø, Ingr. Nordager, Anne Kaboe (c766, p. 790, left page; checked at high resolution); this is the bygdebok's first child '[name unread] 1729 (m. Arne Julson)'; his wife's churching follows on c770 ('Helje Sønderstrands [wife]'); (2) '1 barn fra Helje Mitstrand naufn Anne' (c764, p. 788, left page, early 1729; checked at high resolution), the child of Helge Knudsen of Midtstrand, who is already excluded by his 1739 probate. No child of Helge Erichsen of Nordstrand appears in the window. The only other Aaste/Aase-like names read are an 'Aase' whose father is a Haaver[?] Gopleand (c755) and an 'Aste[?]' whose father is an Ole (c782); neither father is a Helge. So the confirmation age of 22 (1751) cannot point to another Strand Helge's daughter born in 1728-29, the audit-5 window is now read in full (Hølje's own Aaste of 20 Dec 1730 died before the 1732 namesake; the 1730-40 index was), and the chain stands: baptism 16 Mar 1732 'Hælge Strands barn Aaste' (9361 p. 37), the father on Strand in 1734 and 1737, confirmation 1751 'Aaste Helgesd: Strand' (19 years; audit 7's three conditions met), betrothal 1755 to Ulrich Thommesen Fodnes. This supersedes the Audit 10 'Correction' note on the grade only; its reasoning stands and its test has now been run. ¶13a

Corrections 1

Correction: Audit 10 (2 Nov 2026): lowered from Confirmed to Probable, graded on his link to Aaste Helgesdatter (see her page). This rules on the AUDIT-QUEUE flag for the raise. The chain is good: the baptism of 16 Mar 1732 ('Hælge Strands barn Aaste'), the father on Strand in 1734 and 1737, the 1751 confirmation 'Aaste Helgesd: Strand', and the 1755 betrothal. It also meets audit 7's three conditions for a 19-year step, if the confirmand is the 1732 child. But the confirmation gives her age as 22, which points to a birth in 1728-29. The raise answers this with the argument 'whichever birth is meant, the father is the same man'. That is the audit 5 rule 1 window argument, and the rule needs the whole window to be read. The Aurdal baptisms of 1728-29 (DA 9360) are neither indexed nor read. A daughter Aaste born then to another Helge on a Strand farm would fit the stated age exactly. Helge Erichsen of Nordstrand is the named candidate: his marriage was not found, and only his 1733 daughter Anna is indexed. Name reuse closes Hølje's own 1730 Aaste but not another man's child. Under audit 5 rules 1 and 4, the age contradiction is not fully resolved, so the grade is Probable. ¶12a

Alternatives considered and set aside 2

Rival checked: the other Helge on a Strand farm in 1732 was Helge Knudsen of Midtstrand (see his page). His probate of 11 Oct 1739 (DA 25286 p. 279) names his children with his widow as Haagen 9, Ragnild 4 and Aagaat 8 weeks. It names no Aaste, who would have been 7 and was still alive in 1755. So he is excluded as the father of this Aaste. The baptism also writes 'Strand', where this book writes 'Mitstrand' for Helge Knudsen's farm. ¶9

Caveat: the window was read at reduced resolution (1800 px per spread), scanning each baptism's child name; the two Strand entries were re-read at full resolution. ¶13b
