Life story

1801 census, Jevnaker parish, farm Biørgesætren: Hans Hansen, aged 5, 'hendes børn', listed as a parish-supported pauper (lægdsfattig). He is in household 2 with his sister Marte Hansdatter (3). The head of that household is his widowed mother Walborg Pedersdatter (35), who held the cottar's plot and is described as 'deres sønnekone', the daughter-in-law of the farmer Christian Hansen (70) and Kari Christoffersdatter (48) in household 1. His father was therefore a son of Christian Hansen who had died before 1801. That fits Hans Christiansen, an artillerist, who married Walborg Pedersdatter at Lunner church on 26 Dec 1793. The Jevnaker baptisms for 1752-1799 are not transcribed, so his own baptism (about 1795/96) was not found. ¶5

Marriage: Gran parish church book, page 429, no. 4: 'Hans Hansen, ungkarl, Biørgesæteren', aged 25, married 'Elie Peders Datter, pigen, Fallang', aged 25, on 7 Dec 1820. The sponsors (forlovere) were Peder Rundelen, Halvor Hanaknæ, Guldbrand Fallang and Stephen. Both transcriptions file it under year 1821 because the church year began at Advent; neighbouring entries confirm it was December 1820. This resolves the earlier gap ('marriage not found'). The bride's uncle Gulbrand Nilsen lived at Fallang in 1801. ¶6

Probate: heir, aged 19, at his mother Walborg Pedersdatter's estate, Bjørgefjeldet, 19-31 Oct 1814 (Hadeland og Land skifteprotokoll 1802-1821, fol. 701b), with his sister Marthe Hansdatter, 17. At his grandfather Christian Hansen's estate at Bjørgesæteren, 11 Feb - 1 May 1822 (skifteprotokoll 1800-1843, fol. 47b; card DA 39947 no. 655/659), he is 'Hans Bjørgesæter, myndig', heir with his sister Marte (24, unmarried) in place of their dead father Hans. These records settle his Norwegian parentage; his link to Peter Hanson still rests on the 1853 emigration and the American records. ¶11

Naming patterns support the parentage. The couple's daughter Valborg (1826) is named for his mother Walborg Pedersdatter, Kirsti (1822) for Eli's mother Kirsti Gulbrandsdatter, and Hans (1830) for his father. There was also a daughter Marta, baptized 8 Jun 1829 in Jevnaker (father Hans Hansen, mother 'Elisa Pedersdatter', Bjørgesæter), not previously listed. She has the same name as his sister Marte. ¶7

Baptism: "Drengebarn Hans af Hans Christians[en] Biørgesæteren og Walborg Pedersd[atte]r. L.N.54" (the boy Hans, son of Hans Christiansen of Bjørgesæter and Walborg Pedersdatter; L.N. is the father's military enrolment number). He was baptised at Lunner on 15 Nov 1795, the 24th Sunday after Trinity (Jevnaker parish church book 1752-1799, p. 422; IIIF canvas 215). Godparents: Hans Monsen Haregstuegaard[?], Anders Pedersen Mutta[?], Christopher Christensen Biørgesæteren (his uncle), Kari Christophersdatter ibid. (his grandmother) and Anne Jacobsdatter Mielven[?]. This agrees with his age of 5 in the 1801 census. ¶9

Farmer or crofter at Bjørgesæter (farm no. 117a), Lunner, Jevnaker parish, Oppland. He was 50 in the 1845 census, and the 1853 emigration entry gives his age as 57 (b. about 1796). ¶1

Jevnaker parish recorded on 15 Apr 1853 that he, his wife Eli Pedersdatter and son Peder were leaving for 'Amerika'. Peter Hanson's 1922 obituary says Peter came in 1853 'with his parents'. Probable father of Peter Hanson. The link rests on the farm name 'Bjørgesætren' in the Winnebago church book together with the 1853 emigration (see Peter Hanson's notes for the birth-date conflict). ¶2

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: Confirmed as the father of Peter Hanson. The 1836 baptism (Jevnaker ministerialbok 1815-1837, p. 179 no. 20, scan read by an earlier worker) names Peder's parents as 'Hans Hansen og Eli Pedersdr. Bjørgesæteren'; the Jevnaker out-migration list of 15 Apr 1853 enters Hans Hansen (57), his wife Eli Pedersdatter (58) and their son Peder of Bjørgesæter, with Peder's birth as '1836, 2/2'; and in America Peter Hanson had his son baptised in 1867 in the Winnebago Lutheran church as 'Peder Hansen Bjørgesætren', and his 1922 obituary says he came in 1853 'with his parents'. Peter Hanson (see his page) is already Confirmed on this chain, with the birth-date conflict resolved from the 1836 scan; these primary records name the parent with him, so the parent link is upgraded with it. ¶10

Searches that found nothing 1

His death in America was not found. He emigrated in 1853 aged 57, and the family settled near Perry, Dane Co. / Iowa Co., Wisconsin. Digitalarkivet has no transcribed Dane or Iowa County, Wisconsin, church books. ¶8

Open questions and the next record to read 1

What happened to him in America is not known (probably Iowa or Dane County, Wisconsin). ¶4
