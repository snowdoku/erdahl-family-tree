Life story

Baptism: 5 July 1849 at the Sturgeon Bible Christian Church, per the compiled genealogy. It is not in the online indexes. ¶1

Marriage: to William W. Stewart, February 1879. The marriage-register index (Ancestry 61502) reads '29 Feb 1879', an impossible date because 1879 was not a leap year. The compiled genealogy gives 20 February 1879, at the Bible Christian manse, Murray Harbour. ¶2

1891 census (Township 61, page 40, lines 22-25 and page 41, lines 1-3; image read): Stewart, William, 32, born P.E.I., father born Scotland, Methodist, general servant; Margret, 42, wife, born P.E.I., father born England; George W., 11; Cora, 9; Edna, 6; Mabel, 4; Wilma, 2. The Stewarts lived two dwellings from her brother William Hicken and their father George. A father born in England and an age of 42 match George's daughter. ¶3
