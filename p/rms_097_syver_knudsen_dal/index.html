Life story

Siuffuer Knudtzøn of Dal i Nordre Aurdal, probate 30 Nov 1664 (Valdres probate card 2041 = index of Hadeland, Land og Valdres skifteprotokoll 1659-1666 s. 312). Married first Dorte Erichsdatter (dead by 1664), second Audbiør Haldorsdatter. Children: 1. Erich (of Kiörsta), 2. Rangvild (dead, 3 children), 3. Knud Dahl, 4. Dorte (of Gladimb?), 5. Marit, 6. Ingeborre. Graded on his link to his son Knud Syversen: the probate index names Knud Dahl as his child, so the link itself is Confirmed. The card does not say which wife was Knud's mother, so no mother is added. Note that Knud's own link down to Joen Knudsen is only Speculative. ¶1
