About the family

Father: Even (Eriksen) Garberg (see his page), rated Probable; the argument and the records that would confirm it are on Even's page. The 'Giertrud' buried in 1728 as Even Garberg's wife, aged 53, is probably not Per's mother (she would have been about 51 at his birth); his mother's name is not known. ¶10

Mother found (Probable): the skifte concluded 15 Feb 1731 after Giertrud Iversdatter, wife of Evind Erichsen Avildgaard, husmann under Garberg (24750 fol. 366-366b), names their children Peer Evindsen ([5?]; the digit may read 13), Erich Evindsen 11, Anders Evindsen 1 and Guru Evindsdatter 19. Siblings named: Erik (born about 1720), Anders (about 1729-30) and Guru (about 1712). ¶13

Life story

Baptism: 13 March 1726, 'døbt Even Garbergs Barn Nom: Peder' (Selbu ministerialbok 1699-1737, canvas 103 right page, crop); his mother was churched on Jubilate Sunday, 12 May 1726 (canvas 104 left page). The identification is Probable: the bygdebok gives Per Evensen of Garberg the birth year 1726, and he is the only Peder child of an Even in the register from October 1724 to November 1733. This refines his birth from 'ABT 1726' (bygdebok) to 'before 13 March 1726' at Garberg. ¶9

Marriage: betrothed on Quasimodogeniti Sunday (2 April) 1758 and married on the 3rd Sunday after Trinity, 11 June 1758, 'Copul. (2) Peder Evens. og Brynill Erichsd.' (dates computed from Easter 26 March 1758; no farm and no 'widow' mark written). Selbu ministerialbok 1757-1801, canvas 6 and the copulation line. Thomas Bjornsen of Store Evjen married Guri Baarsdatter in the same service. ¶1

Baptism of his son: 'Döbt (2) Even. Forældr: Per Evens: Garberg og Brynill Erichsd: faddr: Erich Sletnum, Ole Garberg, Giertru[d] ibm.' - Even, parents Per Evensen of Garberg and Brynill Eriksdatter, 15th Sunday after Trinity, 23 September 1759 (canvas 8 right page; crop). Further children in the same register: Erik, 21st Sunday after Trinity 1761, and Mali, 6th Sunday after Epiphany 1764, both to 'Per Evens. Garberg og Brynill Ericsd.'. ¶2

1762: Selbu ekstraskatt (49639 c132, matr. 735 Garberg, farmer Peder Biørnsen), cottar column: 'Peder Evensen med Qvinde Brynild Erichsdatter samt hans Moder Anne Madsdatter'. His stepmother Anne Madsdatter, Even Eriksen's second wife (m. 2 Jul 1730), lived with him. This ties him to Even Eriksen's family by a relationship word and confirms his parents (see Even Eriksen Garberg and Gjertrud (Giertrud) Iversdatter). His brother Erik Evensen and Brynild Olsdatter are at matr. 810 Sletne on the same page, with daughters Giertrud and Ingebor over 12. ¶14

Bygdebok: Selbu i fortid og naatid vol. 4, Uglan chapter p. 76 (read only as nb.no word boxes) has '1769-1791 Per Evensen... Garberg... 1726... 1812' as holder of the Uglan farm, with children Even 1759 (took over the farm), Erik 1761, Mali 1764 (of Stor-Evjen) and Jon 1769. The children agree with the register. His birth year ABT 1726 rests on the book alone, and its death year 1812 is unverified - it is also the year Even died, so it may be a misplaced date. ¶5

Godfather 'Even Ugl.' at the 1769 baptism cannot be Even of 1759 (then nine years old); he is an older Even of Uglem, perhaps Peder's own father or brother - an inference only, but it suggests the Evensen family belonged to Uglem before Peder's time at Garberg. ¶8

Probably alive in the 1790s: 'Per/Peder Evensen Uglem' was a sponsor in spring 1792, Jul 1795 and May 1797 (16311 c146, c182, c208) - possibly a younger namesake. No burial for him in 16311 1759-1801. ¶11

Probable death: indexed Selbu burials 1813, p. 185 no. 1: 'Peder Evensen', widower, residence Uglem, born 1725, aged 88 years 10 months, died 28 Dec 1812, buried the Sunday after New Year 1813. In the 1801 census he is 'Peder Evensen', b. 1725, at Uglan, 'husbondens fader, enkemand efter 2det ægteskab' - the household head's father, a widower after his second marriage. Identification by name, farm and age (the burial age points to 1724, his baptism to before Mar 1726). ¶12

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 3

Why the link to Even Pederssen (see his page) is Probable (lowered from Confirmed by the auditor): Even was buried in 1812 aged 53 and was 42 in February 1801, a birth in 1758-59; this Even is the only Even son of a Peder in complete read of January 1757 - February 1767 (the other Even of 1759 is a son of Erik Evensen Sletnum). Even married in 1784 a woman of Garberg, the farm where he was baptised. His first son was Peder (1790) and his second daughter Brynild (1787), after Per and Brynill. The family moved to Uglan: Even was 'of Uglum' by 1784, a Peder Evensen Uglum stood forlover there in 1785-88, and Mali Pedersdatter of Uglum - the Mali of 1764 - married Bernt Thomsen of Store Evjen in 1791 and was godmother ('Maglie Efjen') to Even's son Povel in 1795. An 'Erich Auglem' was godfather to Even's children in 1787 and 1792, plausibly Even's brother Erik of 1761. ¶3

Why not Confirmed: unlike the other three baptisms in this patch, no primary record yet ties the Even of Uglem to the Garberg family explicitly - he married in 1784 as 'Even Pedersen Uglum', not 'of Garberg', and the move of Per Evensen from Garberg to Uglem rests on the bygdebok word boxes and on a 'Peder Evensen Uglum' in the 1780s. Records that would confirm: a baptism or burial of a child of Per Evensen Uglum and Brynill Eriksdatter after 1764 (Selbu ministerialbok 1757-1801, canvases 21-60, 1767-1776, e.g. the Jon of 1769), Per Evensen's burial or probate naming his son Even, or Even's own 1812 probate. ¶4

Confidence raised from Probable to Confirmed, on the record the auditor named as the test. On Ascension Day, 4 May 1769, 'Döbt Jon. Par: Peder Evens: Ugl: Brynild Erichsd. Test. Even Ugl:, Ped: Aftreit, Torkiel Ugl., Ingeborg Forberg, Kari Ugl:' - baptised Jon, parents Peder Evensen of Uglem and Brynild Eriksdatter (Selbu ministerialbok 1757-1801, canvas 31 right page, crop). In September 1769 'Ped: Evensøn Uglum' - the farm written out in full - stood sponsor at the betrothal of Anders Iversen and Birthe Baarsdatter (canvas 33 right page). This is the couple married in 1758 (Peder Evensen x Brynill Eriksdatter) and called 'of Garberg' at the baptisms of Even (1759), Erik (1761) and Mali (1764): the same pair of names, now at Uglem, ten years before the 1784 betrothal of 'unge Mand Even Pedersen Uglum'. Together: Even of 1759 is the only Even son of a Peder in the complete read of January 1757 - February 1767; his parents' household moved from Garberg to Uglem by 1769; the adult Even was of Uglem from 1784 and died there in 1812 aged 53 (born 1758-59); he named children Peder and Brynild; and his sister Mali of 1764 is 'Mali Pedersdatter of Uglum' at her 1791 marriage. The residence gap that kept the link Probable is closed by a primary record. The 1769 baptism also confirms the bygdebok's child 'Jon 1769' (vol. 4 p. 76). ¶7

Records that disagree 1

Contradiction - the wife: the same bygdebok boxes give Per's wife as 'Mali... 1728'. The register names Even's mother Brynill Eriksdatter three times (1759, 1761, 1764) and records Per's marriage to her in 1758. The register is preferred. The book's Mali may be a second wife after Brynill's death (Per's child Jon of 1769 is not yet seen in the register, and nor is Brynill's burial), or a misreading of unqueried words. To settle: Brynill's burial and any second marriage of Per Evensen, Selbu ministerialbok 1757-1801 canvases 20-60 (1767-1776), and the printed p. 76 read in full. ¶6
