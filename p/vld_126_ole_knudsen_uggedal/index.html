About the family

Father of Martha Olsdatter (Probable): he is the widower in the 1742 probate after Marthe Pedersdatter, whose heirs are three children with the patronymic Olsen/Olsdatter, among them Martha Olsdatter married to Alf Rasmussen of Blindem. The words that would say they were the couple's joint children are not legible on the scan, so a former husband of Marthe also named Ole cannot be fully excluded, though nothing suggests one. His patronymic shows his father was named Knud. He is presumably one of the fathers named Ole at Uggedal in the Borgund baptism index 1700-1725; those rows were not re-read here. ¶2

Other children (index): Anders, son of Ole Knudss. of Uggedal, baptised 17 Sep 1717 (pd00000001506339). The 1742 probate's deceased son represented by his heirs is probably this Anders: a separate probate after Anders Olsen of Uggedal was held the same day (17 Apr 1742, 3A 14 fol. 143a, view 24555/157), with his widow Birthe Rasmusdatter and her guardian on one side; Birthe Rasmusdatter of Uggedal was churched 3 Jun 1742 (posthumous child) and remarried in 1742 as a widow. Martha's own baptism (c.1715-20) is not in the index under father Ole Knud*. ¶5

Life story

Probate (scan read): 'Anno 1742 den 11 Ma[rtii or ji]' (the index gives 17 Apr 1742) a probate was held at Uggedal, Borgund skipreide, after the late Marthe Pedersdatter (the index reading; the underlined first name is hard to read), between the widower (Enkemanden) Ole Knudsen on one side and the 3 children on the other: (1) a son [Knud?] Olsen, who has since died ('ved Døden er afgangen'), represented by his heirs; (2) Peder Olsen, aged 20, with curator Peder Hansen [?]; and (3) 'Martha Olsdatter gift med Alf Rasmussen Blindem' (Martha Olsdatter, married to Alf Rasmussen of Blindem). Fol. 142a, viewer image 24555/156 (urn sk20090126630568). ¶1

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: Ole Knudsen of Uggedal was engaged at Borgund on 5 Jul 1714 to 'Pige Martha Persdtr.' (Martha Pedersdatter, maiden), witnesses (trulovere) the parish clerk Ludvig Sørensen and Stephan Knudsen of Brevig (pv00000010161808), and married her on 1 Jan 1715 (pv00000002827319; bride 'Mathea Pedersd.'). Martha Pedersdatter of Uggedal was buried 19 Nov 1741 aged 53 (pg00000002546285), and in 1743 Ole Knudsen of Uggedal remarried as a widower (pv00000002823251, to the widow Synneve Olsdatter). So the marriage lasted from 1715 to her death, and since she was a maiden in 1714, every child of hers named Olsen/Olsdatter is his. The heir 'Peder Olsen aged 20' in her 1742 probate matches Peder, son of Ole Knudss. of Uggedal, baptised 14 Sep 1721 (pd00000001506901), which ties the probate's widower to these church-book entries. With the probate's heir 'Martha Olsdatter gift med Alf Rasmussen Blindem', this proves him the father of Martha Olsdatter. Index rows only (not read on the scan) for the 1714/1715/1721/1741/1743 entries. ¶4
