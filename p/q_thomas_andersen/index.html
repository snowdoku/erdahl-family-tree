About the family

Parents probable: the only matching baptism is 'Thomas', baptized 25 Mar 1770, son of Anders of Almoen. The farmer Anders Larsen of Almoen (1733-1807) had sons Lars (bapt. 1764) and Joen (bapt. ~1768, d. 1780) and a wife Ane Jonsdatter (d. 1790). Thomas named a son Jon (1798), which fits a mother named Ane Jonsdatter. Lars Andersen, 36, a cottager at Almoen in 1801, was probably his brother. ¶2

His brothers and sisters, reconstructed from the Almoen entries in the registers: Lars (an infant who died in 1761); Lars, baptized 30 Sep 1764, later a cottager at Almoen, who married Kiersten Larsdatter of Skordalen in 1792; Mali, baptized 31 Jul 1768, who married Christopher Davidsen of Frigaard in 1793; Thomas himself, baptized 25 Mar 1770; Ole, baptized 13 Mar 1774; Anne, born about 1777, who died unmarried at Almoen in 1802; Joen, baptized 26 Aug 1781, still a servant on his father's farm in 1801; a Joen who died in 1780 aged about twelve; and further children baptized 16 Feb 1766, 9 Feb 1772, 18 Jun 1775 and 23 Feb 1777 whose names the index does not give. Beret Andersdatter of Almoen, who married the widower Peder Olssen of Øver Hofstad in 1802, is probably another sister. ¶5

Life story

His baptism has now been read from the register page. Under the Hegra section of the Stjørdal chronological register for 1770, page 191: 'Døbt: Anders Almoens B: Thomas', with the witnesses including Lars of Almoen and Golla of Geiteråsen among others. The presence of a Lars at Almoen in 1770, alongside the fact that Anders Larsen named his very first son Lars (an infant who died in 1761) and then named the next son Lars again in 1764, is consistent with Anders's own father having been a Lars who was still alive and living on the farm. That makes the identification of Anders Larsen of Almoen as Thomas's father a good deal firmer than the patronymic alone. ¶3

Probate after his mother Anne Jonsdatter, Almoen, 13 Nov 1790 (Stjør- og Verdal skifteprotokoll 10, p. 232, Digitalarkivet 24763 view 246): 'Thomas' aged 21, so born about 1769, with a curator appointed for him. Read. ¶6

Cottager (husmann med jord) at Almoen/Almoåsen. As a widower of Almoen he married Anne Olsdatter, a maid of Hofstad, on 14 Jul 1794 (his first wife is unknown). 1801 census: 30, in his 2nd marriage, with children Ole 6, Jon 3 and Randi 1. Buried 12 Jun 1828, given as born 1773. ¶1

His marriage on 14 July 1794, as a widower of Almoen to Anne Olsdatter, a maid of Hofstad, was bonded by Jon Rolfseng and Ole Fossen as forlovere (the two bondsmen who swore there was no impediment). ¶4
