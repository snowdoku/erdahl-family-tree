Life story

Baptism (index): the Evangelical parish of Villisaß, Kreis Culm, has a baptism of Wilhelm Knodel on 15 Oct 1908, with the relatives indexed as 'Jakob, Katharina'. The civil birth at Pfeilsdorf was on 4 Oct 1908, 11 days earlier, and the relatives' names repeat the parents' given names, so this is very probably this child's baptism. This is an index entry only (Ancestry 'Germany, Lutheran Baptisms, Marriages, and Burials, 1567-1945', dbId 61250); the register image has not been read, so the index does not say whether the two names in its unlabelled 'Relatives' field are the parents or the godparents, and it gives no sponsors. Index recordId 3649393, image id 0245317-00148. ¶2

Birth: Standesamt Pfeilsdorf civil birth act (Polish 1949 certified copy), declared by the settler Jakob Knodel; mother Katharina née Reister (image read by the w5 knodel-origin agent). A first cousin of Erna Knodel only if Jakob proves to be Gottlieb's brother. ¶1
