About the family

Children by the 1728 probate: Sigri (12), Ole (9½), Salve (7), Ole the younger (5), Torgeir (2). He was alive and a widower in Nov 1728. A 'Tallef Søftestad' who baptised a daughter Anne in Nissedal on 9 Nov 1759 is more likely a younger man (a son or grandson); not determined. ¶3

Parents (bygdebok, Probable): Nissedal bygdesoge [1], Søftestad (canvases 451-453): 'Tellev omlag 1700', son of Gunstein [Olsen] and Sigrid Gjermundsdtr., married 1. Aslaug Salvesdtr. (d. 1728), 2. Sigrid Torbjørnsdtr. (d. 1767 aged 82); he died 1755 ('Tellev Gunsteinson d 1755, f omlag 1700'), with a skifte. Linked as Gunstein Olsen and Sigrid Gjermundsdatter. Birth ABT 1700, death ABT 1755 from the book. ¶7a

His parents' link (graded on the parents, not on him): his mother's skifte, Søftestad 8 Oct 1701 (østfjelske 25699 No 39, view 64) names 'hendes efterlevende mand Gunsteen Olsen og sammen avlede børn Tollef Gunsteensøn og Sigri Gunsteensdaatter' and a half-sister Kari(?) Tollefsdaatter by her first husband Tollef Tallachsen. This is the test record named in the note. The identification of that Tollef with this Tallef Gundsteensen of 1728 rests on farm, patronymic and naming (daughter Sigri), across 27 years with no record in between naming him, so the parents stay Probable. His grade (link to Ole) is unchanged. ¶8

Life story

Probate (skifte no. 154): '[1728] Novembris 15de blev paa Gaarden Søfftestad Nissedals annex og Hvideseid præstegield' the sheriff Hans Ellefsen [Lung?] with the two men Andbiørn [..] and Nottov Grimstvedt registered and valued the estate 'efter afgangne Aslouge Salvesdaatter' and divided it between 'hendes efterladte mand Tallef Gundsteensøn og deris tilsammen avlede børn, Nafnlig: Ole Tallefsøn 9½ aar, Salve Tallefsøn 7 aar gammel, Ole Tallefsøn den yngre 5 aar gammel, Torgier Tallefsøn 2 aar gammel, samt Sigre Tallefsdaatter 12 aar gammel' (her surviving husband Tallef Gundstensen and the children they had together: Ole 9½, Salve 7, Ole the younger 5, Torgeir 2, Sigri 12). Vest-Telemark (Øvre Telemark vestfjelske) sorenskriveri, skifteprotokoll 1722-1734 (Digitalarkivet source 25668), skifte no. 154, fol. 232b-233a (view 236). Found via the 6856 card (view 1262). Crop. ¶1

An earlier record of the couple: pantebok I 2 (14504) No 305, entered about Oct 1692 (view 5 left): 'Kiendes Jeg Undertegnede Gunsten Olsen Søftestad paa Min Hustrues Sigri Giermundsdatters, samt Stifdatters Rannou(?) Tallefsdatters vegne' (I, Gunsten Olsen of Søftestad, on behalf of my wife Sigri Gjermundsdatter and my stepdaughter Rannou(?) Tallefsdatter). Knud Andersen joins for his wife Margit Tallachsdatter, about a mortgage their late father(-in-law) Tallach had given on half of 1½ tønder in Nedre Fione. So Gunstein was on Søftestad and married to Sigri by about 1692. With Tallef's daughter aged 12 in 1728, Tollef Gunsteinsen was born about 1685-1697; the bygdebok's 'omlag 1700' is too late. The stepdaughter reads Rannou here; the 1701 reading 'Kari(?)' is uncertain and is probably the same girl. The deed does not concern Vik. The bygdebok's 1692 Vik sale was not found in views 2-9 of 14504. ¶9

The 1755 probate does NOT name his parents or his sister Sigri Gunsteinsdatter, and he held no land (Fastegods): his estate was on the plass Holt(?) (Ole's share 'paa Pladsen Holt', reading uncertain), registered at Nordbø. So it does not bridge the 1701-1728 gap for Gunstein Olsen and Sigrid Gjermundsdatter as his parents (graded on them; they stay Probable). His death date: before 23 Oct 1755 (bygdebok: 1755). Second wife Sigri Torbiørnsdaatter (widow) and their daughter Aaslaug are named. ¶11

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 3

Confidence: Probable as parent of Ole Søftestad (see his page), Ole Tellevsen Søftestad, buried 28 Jun 1799 aged 80 (born about 1719). The 1728 probate names an elder son Ole Tallefsen, 9½ in Nov 1728 (born about spring 1719), on the same farm; Ole's burial age fits it to the year, his patronymic Tallefsen/Tellevsen is attested in 1760 and 1799, and he named his son Tallef (b. 1760). Either Ole of the probate would be a son of this couple (both are listed as 'deris tilsammen avlede børn'), but the age points to the elder. Not Confirmed because the identification spans 1728 to 1760 (32 years) with no record in between naming Ole's father. ¶2

Upgrade: graded on his link to Ole Søftestad (see his page). His own probate, Nordbø in Nissedal, 23 Oct 1755 (Vest-Telemark skifteprotokoll 25673, No 154, views 272-273; register 25675 view 861), reads: 'Anno 1755 den 23 Octbr. var efter Lovl. Beramelse paa gaarden Nordbøe i Nissedalle forsamlet med tvende Laugrettesmænd Kittil Tvedt og Jørgen Tvedt... efter afg. Tallev Günstensøn... [Registering og Vurdering] imellem hans efterlevende Enke Sigri Torbiørnsdaatter og den Sal. Mands med første Hustrue Aslaug(?) Saavesdaatter avlede Børn Ole Tallevsøn Myndig, Saave og Ole Tallevsønner i Holland, og Torgiej Tallevsøn alle myndige, samt Sigri Tallevsdaatter, og med den igienlevende Hustrue avlet Aaslaug Tallevsdaatter'. The shares (view 273) list 'Ældste Søn Ole Tallevssøn', 'Anden Søn Saave', 'Tredie Søn Ole', 'Yngste Søn Torgiej', 'Ældste Daatter Sigri' and 'Yngste Daatter Asloug'. This is the 1728 family exactly (Sigri 12, Ole 9½, Salve 7, Ole the younger 5, Torgeir 2, mother Aslaug Salvesdatter), now named as his children 27 years on. Continuity to Ole Søftestad: the eldest son Ole is the one at home (the younger Ole is 'i Holland' with Salve); Ole Tallefsen buys land in Søftestad in 1754 (panteregister 10915 fol. 44b) and Ole Tellevsen of Søftestad baptises a son Tallef in 1760, and Ole Søftestad's burial age (80 in 1799, b. about 1719) fits the elder Ole (9½ in Nov 1728) and not the younger (5). Gaps 1754-1755-1760 are short; the only rival Ole is placed abroad in 1755 and does not fit the burial age. This replaces the earlier 'Confidence: Probable' note, whose 1728-1760 gap is now bridged. ¶10

Audit 12 (2 Nov 2026): Confirmed KEPT, graded on his link to Ole Søftestad (see his page). The raise stands, but its continuity paragraph should be read with this rival check. The 1755 probate after Tallev Günstensøn (25673 No 154) names 'Ole Tallevsøn Myndig' without a residence, and the father's estate was on a plass (Holt?) registered at Nordbø, not on Søftestad. So the step to Ole Tallefsen of Søftestad rests on more than the farm. Named rivals: the two other Tallevs of Søftestad, Tallev Gunsteensen the younger (b. about 1714, of Grøven in Brunkeberg by 1754) and his half-brother Tallev Bjørgulvsen Søftestad (b. after about 1715). Neither could have had a son of age to buy Søftestad land by deed of 7 May 1754, and neither fits the burial age (80 in 1799). Inside the gap the family is also seen around Ole: at his son Tallef's baptism in 1760 the godmothers include 'Sigri Søftestad' and 'Aslaug Holt', the names of his sister Sigri and half-sister Aaslaug of the 1755 probate (whose father's estate lay at Holt). The burial age is not the only thing that excludes the rivals, so audit 8 rule 4 is met. Gaps 1754-1755-1760 are short. The naming record is his own 1755 probate ('med første Hustrue... avlede Børn'), which lists the 1728 set exactly. ¶12

Alternatives considered and set aside 2

Father candidates (no link made): the Søftestad page of panteregister I 1 (10915 fol. 44b, view 87) shows 'Gunsten Olsøn' on Søftestad in Nissedal from 1696 to 1718 (deeds of 1696 with Ole Gunstensen, a mortgage of 1701/1707, a sale of 1712 to Jørgen Egilsen Meuile(?), and a mortgage of 24 Oct 1718 to Hans Ellefsen Lung). He held the farm that Tallef held by 1728, and fits a father of a man born before about 1696. The other candidate is Gunsteen Tallefsen of Moen under Søftestad (dead by 1731; a son Tallev b. about 1714 by Ellen Asbjørnsdatter). With two named candidates, neither is added. ¶5a

Not him: 'Tallev Gunsteensøn boende paa Grøven i Brunkeberg Annex' signs a deed in 1754 and is a signer and guardian in the Torkel-heirs' sale dated 21 Oct 1754 (pantebok I 5, source 10870, views 136 right and 150-151, entry No 403), together with 'Tallev Biørgusøn Søftestad'. This is the younger Tallev Gunstensen of Moen under Søftestad (born about 1714, son of Gunsteen Tallefsen and Ellen Asbjørnsdatter), with his half-brother Tallev Bjørgulvsen; it does not bear on the elder Tallef Gundstensen, father of the Søftestad children of 1728. ¶6

Searches that found nothing 1

Searched for his parents: the only other early Søftestad card in 6856 (views 1250-1269) is the probate of 9 Nov 1731 at Moen under Søftestad after Ellen Asbjørnsdatter, whose first husband was 'Gunsteen Tallefsøn' (dead) and whose children by him were 'Tallev og Hans Gunsteensønner 17 og 15 aar' and 'Egelev og Aasild Gunsteensdøttre 20 og 25 aar' (25668 fol. 365a, view 369; read from the scan). That Tallev Gunsteensen, born about 1714, cannot be this Tallef, who had a daughter of 12 in 1728. Gunsteen Tallefsen of Søftestad is still the obvious candidate for this Tallef's father by an earlier marriage (same farm, patronymic fits, the half-brother then named Tallef after the same grandfather), but nothing names the link, so he is not added (Speculative at best). ¶4a
