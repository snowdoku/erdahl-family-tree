About the family

His wife in 1801 is Magli Guttormsdatter, 68, and with them is 'Ingebor Torgersdatter, 22, HANS datter, Ugift' - HIS daughter, not theirs. The 1801 enumerator of Selbu distinguishes 'hans' from 'deres' carefully elsewhere in the same listing, so Ingebor was a child of his first marriage, and so, almost certainly, was Haldo born about 1770, when a woman born 1733 would have been thirty-seven and in what the census calls her first marriage. His first wife's name is not recorded and Magli Guttormsdatter is NOT entered here as the mother of his children. ¶2

Son: Haldo Torgersen (see his page), household 4 on the same residence in the 1801 census, working the farm 'i faellesskab med faderen'. The link was argued in 2026 but was not written into the tree until this patch. ¶5

His daughter Ingebor: the Selbu confirmands of 1794 include no. 19 'Ingeborg Torgersdatter[?] Svinaas, 15 aar' (the T of the patronymic is not certain). Born about 1779, she agrees with 'Ingebor Torgersdatter, 22, HANS datter' in his 1801 household, so she is very probably the same girl and a sister of Haldo. Not entered as a separate person. ¶8

First wife: Mali Haldorsdatter (see her page), named with him at the baptisms of Marit (27 September 1767, he 'Torger Erichs: Qvello', of Kvello) and Haldor (about 18-25 November 1770, he 'Torger Erichsen Svinaasen'), and churched as his wife in late December 1770 (Selbu ministerialbok 1757-1801 canvas 23 left; Selbu klokkerbok 1766-1776 canvases 65-66). So he lived at Kvello in 1767 and had moved to Svinaas by November 1770. Their marriage has not been found and is entered only as 'before 27 September 1767' (Marit's baptism). The 1757-1767 copulations (16311 canvases 2-20) have not yet been searched for it under his name; if the Erich Torgersen below was their son, it was before about 1760. ¶10

Son: Ole, a younger brother of Haldo (see his page), baptised in April 1774 (between the burials of early April and the 2nd Sunday after Easter, 17 April), 'Döbt: Ole. Foræld: Torger Erichsen Svinaas, Mali Haldorsd. Fadd: Ole B. Fossum[?], Povel Eidem, Erich Lien, Anne Solum, Kirsten Svinaas' - parents Torger Eriksen of Svinaas and Mali Haldorsdatter (Selbu klokkerbok 1766-1776, canvas 105 right page, col. 3, crop). The wife was churched in May 1774 (canvas 107). The first godfather is probably Ole Barosen Fossum, later a forlover (surety) at Torger's second marriage in 1787 and Haldo's in 1794. Ole is not in the 1801 Svinaas households; his fate is not traced. ¶12

His second wife's burial: 'Maglie Guttormsd.', 'Qvinden' (the wife), of Svinaas, aged 72, was buried in Selbu on the 15th Sunday after Trinity 1802 (26 September 1802, computed from Easter 18 April 1802), by the Digitalarkivet index of the 1801-1815 church book (scan not read). The residence, surname and status agree with Magli Guttormsdatter, 68, his wife in the 1801 census (see her page); the age implies birth about 1730 against about 1733 from the census. It is the only Magli or Maglie burial at Svinaas in that register. So Torger was a widower for the last six years of his life. ¶15

Wife Mali Haldorsdatter: 'Døbt Olle, Par: Torger Erichs. Svinaas, Mali Haldorsd.', Quasimodogeniti 1774 (16311 c62); the child was buried 19 Jun 1774 aged 10 weeks (c64). Another infant, Olle Torgersen, died 24 Dec 1775 aged 8 days (c73). ¶17

Parents added, Probable: Erik Olsen of Øvre Haarstad, Selbu (died before 29 Mar 1742) and Ingeborg Olsdatter. The skifte after Erik on 29 Mar 1742 (Stjør- og Verdal Skifteprotokoll 2C, 24752, fol. 1116b, view 383 left) names '1 Torgier Erichsen 8 aar gl:' first among the four children, with sisters Anne 17, Ingeboer 14 and Marit 11. He is the 'Erich Haarstads barn Nom: Torger' baptised 27 Sep 1733 (16310 c130 R), the only Torger baptised in Selbu in 1729-37. Probable, not Confirmed, because the step from the Haarstad boy of 1742 to Torger of Kvello (1767) and Svinaas (1770 on) crosses farms and 25 years; see the notes on the parents for the naming evidence (sons Erik and Ole, daughters Marit and Ingeborg). His own grade (Confirmed, on his link to Haldo) is unchanged. ¶22

Life story

The oldest ancestor reached on the Stokke-and-Svinaas side of Oline Slind's tree, born about 1731 and still master of his farm at seventy. The 1801 census of Selbu, rural residence 0015 Svinaas, household 3: 'Torger Erikssen, 70, Husbonde, Hans 2det haendes 1te aegteskab, Bonde og gaard beboer' - householder, his second marriage and her first, a farmer in possession of a farm. His son Haldo Torgersen, in household 4 of the same residence, is explicitly working the farm 'i faellesskab med faderen', in partnership with the father, which is what identifies them as father and son. ¶1

His own patronymic makes his father an ERIK, born in the 1690s or early 1700s. That is as far as the record reaches; see the findings file for why nothing for Selbu survives in the index between 1701 and 1801. ¶3

Context, flagged as context: a farm worked 'i faellesskab' - in common - by father and grown son, each enumerated as a separate household on the same holding, is the ordinary Trondelag arrangement for a handover in progress. The father kept the title and a share of the produce while the son took the labour, and the formal transfer, with a kaar pension reserved for the old couple, came later or at death. ¶4

Possible correction to his birth, not applied: if he is the Haarstad Torger, he was baptised on 27 Sep 1733, not born about 1731. His 1801 census age (70) and 1808 burial age (77) both point to 1730-31. That two-to-three-year gap is unresolved; it is the reason the parents stay Probable. The baptism date is not entered as his birth. ¶23

Land records: Selbu panteregister 22743 image 73 (under T) has 'Torger Erichsen Swinaases Oblig. til Carl Falck for 64 rd' (fol. 495), 'Torger Erichsen... kiøbt Skiøde af General Lieutenant Krogh paa Gaarden Swinaas' (fol. 511), and his obligation to Directeur Wensch for 600 rd secured on Svinaas, dated 23 Jan 1790 (fol. 511). He BOUGHT Svinaas from the owner Krogh rather than inheriting it, so the deed register gives no clue to his parents. ¶25a

Marriage found: betrothed on Palm Sunday (30 Mar) 1760, 'Trolov: (1) Torger Erichs: og Mali Haldorsd:', and married on the 3rd day of Pentecost (27 May) 1760, 'copul: (1) Sold: Ingebricht Ols: og Ingebor Haldorsd: (2) Torger Erichs: og Mali Haldorsd:' (16311 c9 R, zoomed). Neither entry gives a farm, a father or a sponsor, so it does not test his origin at Øvre Haarstad. His eldest son was baptised on the 22nd Sunday after Trinity (18 Oct) 1761: 'Døbt Eric. Kælder: Torger Erics: Mali Haldorsd: Faddr: Einar Flonæs, Esten Eidum, Kari Flonæs' (16311 c11 R, zoomed; again no farm). This Erik is the 'Erik Torgersen 26' of his mother's 1786 skifte and the 'Erich Torgersen Svinaas, 27' buried in 1787, so the 1786 age was rounded up by a year. The 1757-61 register (16311 c6-c11) shows no other Torger Eriksen marrying or baptising. ¶26

Probate of his first wife: Skifteprotokoll 9-3 (Digitalarkivet 24762), fol. 613b-614a, October 1786, on Svinaas: the widower 'Gaardmand Torger Eriksen' and their children Erik Torgersen 26, Haldo Torgersen 16 and Ingeborg Torgersdatter 7. The marriage was therefore made by about 1760. ¶20

Second marriage: betrothed on Palm Sunday, 1 April 1787, 'Torger Erichs. Svinaas & Mali Guttormsd. Stamnes' - Torger Eriksen of Svinaas and Mali (Magli) Guttormsdatter of Stamnes - with forlovere Jon Barosen Sandvig and Ole Barosen Fossum, and married on the 2nd Sunday after Trinity, 17 June 1787. This is the marriage the 1801 census calls his second and her first. It confirms that Haldo, born about 1770, and the daughter Ingebor, born about 1779, were children of his first marriage; the first wife's name is still not known, and she died before 1787. ¶6

Alive in 1793: 'Torger Svinaas' stood first godfather at baptisms in Selbu on 1 January 1793 (Ole, son of Gunder Olsen Svinaas) and 5 May 1793 (Ole, son of Ole Halvorsen Ovre Solem). Identification as Torger Eriksen rests on his being the only Torger at Svinaas in the 1801 census. ¶7

Alive in Jan 1793 as a godparent (16311 c151). ¶18

Alive in 1799: godfather 'Torger Erichsen Svinaas' at the baptism of Ole, b. 14 Jun 1799, son of Guttorm Iversen Grøttem and Marite Bersvensdatter (Selbu 16311 c243 companion). His son Haldor Torgersen Svinaas and Ingebor Jonsdatter baptised a daughter Maglie, b. 22 Apr 1799 (c240). ¶16

1801 census, household detail: household 3 at Svinaas on 1 February 1801 is Torger Erikssen, 70, Husbonde (householder), farmer in possession; Magli Guttormsdatter, 68, 'Hans kone' (his wife), both noted 'Hans 2det, haendes 1te aegteskab' (his second marriage, her first); and Ingebor Torgersdatter, 22, 'Hans datter', unmarried. His son Haldo's household 4 is separate. ¶13

Death and burial: 'Torger Erichsen', kaarmand, of Svinaas, aged 77, buried in Selbu in 1808 (church book 1801-1815, burials 1808, p. 105, no. 1; Digitalarkivet index, scan not yet read). The age gives a birth about 1731, the same as his 1801 census age of 70, and he is the only Torger at Svinaas in the burial index of that period (the other, Torger Haldosen, was born 1795 and buried 1868). The index shows the date of death as '05-10' without a clear format, so only the year 1808 is entered; the scan would give the day. 'Kaarmand' (kaarmann) means a retired farmer living on a kaar, the pension reserved from the farm when it was handed over - which fits the 1801 arrangement in which his son Haldo already worked the farm with him. Replaces the earlier entry 'after 1 February 1801'. ¶14

Alternatives considered and set aside 4

Possible son, not proved: an Erich Torgersen Svinaas, 27, died 6 September 1787 and was buried on the 14th Sunday after Trinity 1787. Born about 1760, he could be an elder son of Torger (then about 29) - he bears the name of Torger's father Erik - but no record names his parents. ¶9

The Erich Torgersen Svinaas confirmed in 1779 (Peder Holmmaster's district, canvas 96 right) and buried in 1787 aged 27 may be another son of Torger and Mali; not linked. ¶11

Rival check not done: 16310 c107-117 (1727 to mid-1729) still need reading for another Torger, as noted. ¶28

Rival check done: Selbu ministerialbok 16310 canvases 107-117 (January 1727 to June 1729) were read in full, and no child named Torger was baptised in that span. With read of 1729-37 (only the Torger of Erich Haarstad, 27 Sep 1733), the whole window 1727-1737 now holds a single Torger baptism. The same canvases show 'Erich Haarstads B. Nom: Ole' baptised Rogate Sunday (22 May) 1729 (c116 R), an elder brother who is absent from the 1742 skifte (so presumably dead by then); Torger named a son Ole. This strengthens, but does not make Confirmed, the identification of Torger Eriksen of Kvello/Svinaas with the 8-year-old Torger of the 1742 Haarstad skifte: no record yet names his parents or Haarstad after 1742. ¶29

Searches that found nothing 4

Marriage search, partial negative: no betrothal or marriage of Torger Erichsen in 16311 canvases 20-21 (July 1766 to May 1767). His son Haldor Torgersen (Lerinas) Svinås has an estate in the 1827-40 Strinda og Selbu probate protocol (24745, fol. 161; not read). ¶19

Negative: the 77804 probate card index (1689-1742) has no Svinås card. Its S run goes from Svarte/Svorte (Lånke, cards 1961 and 1963) straight to Sæter (cards 1965 onward), so no probate after an Erik of Svinås, naming a son Torger, was held in 1689-1742 or indexed there. His father, if he died before Torger's marriage, more likely died after 1742. The typed registers of 24753-24762 have no estate of an Erik at Svinås either. The Svinås estates they do list are Ole Bjørnsen and his wife Gjertrud Olsdatter (24753 fol. 25 and 468, 1740s), Poul Jonsen (24755 fol. 215b) and Ole Nilsen, a cottager (24759 fol. 441b). Fol. 25 is not at 24753 view 53 (both entries there are from Verdal), so it was not read. It could show whether Torger's mother remarried there. ¶21

Negative: the skifte after Ole Biørnsen Svinaas, 15 Jul 1752 (24753 fol. 468, view 491 right), names his widow Ragnild Olsdatter (the 'Ragnild Olsd. Svinaas' buried in May 1768 aged 60) and six children (Ole the elder 10, Ole the younger 8, a third son 4, Peder 14 days, Sesell 17, Gjertrud 5). No Torger and no Eriksen child, so it does not bear on his parents. The typed register's fol. 25 for 'Ole Bjørnsen og hustru Gjertrud Olsdatter, Svinås' (1743) is not on 24753 views 53-56: views 53-54 hold Verdal entries of Nov 1743 and an entry for Halvor Andersen of Aug 1743. That estate (probably Ole's first wife) is still unread, but by its date and names it is unlikely to concern Torger. ¶24

Lead tested, negative: the 'Torger Eidum' godfather of 1760-61 is not explained by the Eidem probates. Esten Haldosøn Eidem's skifte (2 Sep 1765, 24755 fol. 561b) names only his widow Berit Gundersdatter, five children and guardians Bersvend Haldosen and Peder Pedersen Eidem; Ole Pedersen Eidum's skifte (28 Jun 1743, 24753 fol. 42) names only Peder 22, Helge 25 and Gunild 10. No Torger in either. ¶30
