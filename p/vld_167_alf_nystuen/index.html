About the family

Father of Jon (Joen) Alvsen (see his page), Probable; graded on his link to Jon. Lesja ministerialbok 1732-1776 (source 9236), p. 293 (canvas 139), read on the scan: Dom. Jubilate [9 May] 1745 at Verket, 'Bapt: Alf Nyestuens Jon', born 17 Apr 1745, godmother Anne Olsdatter Nyestuen; his wife's churching 'Alf Nyestuens Aaste' is entered just above. ¶1a

Parents entered (both Probable, compiled source): Ola Olsen and Brit (ca. 1682-1761) of Sør-Kvam, Lesjaskogen. The Nystugu entry in the Lesja bygdebok (vol. 1, canvas 45) calls him 'Alf Ols. truleg frå [___] (4-4)'; entry 4-4 of the Sør-Kvam chapter (canvas 141) is 'Brit, ca. 1682-1761, g.m. ein Ola Ols.', with '2. Alf, sjå Nystugu (3-3)' among their children. Through Brit he descends from Alf Jakobsen (ca. 1650 - ca. 1715) and Jakob Jakobsen (ca. 1604 - ca. 1674) of Sør-Kvam - the same Jakob whom the bygdebok makes father of Guro Jakobsdatter of Einbu, so lines of Jon Alvsen and of his wife Kari Rolvsdatter's grandmother Anne Pedersdatter Slettum meet in Jakob. ¶8a

Life story

Other probable children at Nystuen: Iver, buried as an infant in 1738 ('Iver Alfs of Nystuen'); Ole Alvsen of Nystuen, a father in 1777 and godfather to Jon's son in 1784 (probably the Ole Alvsen, b. about 1756, huusmand at Bjørlien in 1801); Sigri (Siri) Alfsdatter of Nystuen, a bride in 1781. Birth before 1720 is inferred from a child buried in 1738. No probate for him has been found: the Nord-Gudbrandsdal probate card index (source 39945, Nystuen cards at images 9585-9595) shows only the 1767 probate of Ole Olsen of Nystuen. ¶2

Lesja 9236 p. 241 (canvas 112), early Feb 1740 at Werket: churching of 'Alf Nystuens Aaste', so Alf was at Nystuen with his wife Aaste in 1740. Grade unchanged. ¶7

Dead before 1763: his widow appears as '3tio Enken Aaste Iversd.' with children Ole, Soldat Iver, John, Margrete and Anne at No. 1 Aaefletten in the 1763 Lesja extra-tax manntall (source 45390, view 286). If Ole 11, Sigri 11 and Brit 7 of the 1767 Nystuen probate are his (b. about 1756 and 1760), he died about 1760-62; the probate of 1 Jun 1767 may be his late skifte (see Aaste Iversdatter for the 'Ole Olsen' contradiction). Grade unchanged (Probable, graded on his link to Jon (Joen) Alvsen Rånåhaugen). ¶4

Lesja bygdebok (Bygdebok for Lesja 1, Lesjaskogen, 1987, nb.no item 8e238b3c93fae7ff68ee91598c918b4d, canvases 45-46, entry 3-3 'Ca. 1738 - 1766' under Nystugu, text rebuilt from word boxes): 'Alf ___ truleg frå ___ (4-4)... g. 1738 m. [Åse Iversdotter] frå [Stueflotten] (1-6), 1721-97... I 1751 fekk dei bygselsetel' [lease on Nystugu]. Children (all baptised in Lesja): Iver 1738-38; Ola 1740, took over (g. 1766 m. Anne); Iver 1742; Jon 1745, 'sjå Nørdre Rånåhaugen (6-2)'; Margrete 1747; Sigrid 1749-50; Johan 1751 (m. 1782 Guri); Sigrid 1753 (m. 1782 Erik Jenss. Kvam); Ola 1756; Brit ca. 1760 (m. 1790 Anders Knuts.). Åse stayed in Nystugu after the next user took over in 1783 and died there in 1797. The bygdebok thus agrees that Jon b. 1745 is the Jon of Nørdre Rånåhaugen (the tree's Jon (Joen) Alvsen Rånåhaugen); this is a compiled source, so the grade stays Probable. His origin, bruk '(4-4)' of the same farm complex, was not recovered from the word boxes (gaps). ¶5

Death. The bygdebok says he died 'i 1766 eller 1767' and that his burial is not in the church book, so its date is inferred from the skifte of 1 Jun 1767, which it treats as his. The 1763 manntall (source 45390, view 286) already calls his wife 'Enken' (the widow), so he died between about 1759 (Brit b. about 1760, if his posthumous child is not meant) and 1763. Death date kept as BET 1755 AND 1763 pending his burial (9236, about canvases 225-245, Jun 1759 - Mar 1763). ¶6

Death inferred, not recorded: if Ole 11 and Sigri 11 of the 1767 Nystuen probate (Ole Olsen, g.m. Aaste Iversdtr., card 9591) are his children Ole Alvsen (b. about 1756) and Sigri Alfsdatter, he was alive about 1755 and dead before his widow's remarriage to Ole Olsen (child Brit b. about 1760). His burial would be in the indexed Lesja burials 1732-1776 only if indexed under another spelling; none for Alf/Alv was found. ¶3

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Not Confirmed: the baby of 1745 is tied to the 1775 groom 'Joen Alvsen Nystuen' by name, patronymic, farm and his exact 1801 census age (55), with 30 years and no record naming the relationship between (audit 3); his patronymic is not yet known. Supporting pattern: Jon named his first daughter Aase (1776) and a son Alv (1780). ¶1b
