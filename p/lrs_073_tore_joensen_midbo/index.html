About the family

Family: two of Turi's godparents, Jørgen Jonsen Midbøe and Tone Jonsdatter Midbøe, share his patronymic and farm and were probably his brother and sister. This rests on name and farm only. ¶2

Children: Turi (bapt. 6 Jan 1777), wife of Gunleik Alvsen at Midbøe Nordre in 1801. In 1801 Guro Toresdatter, aged 13 (born about 1787-1788), lived in Turi's household as 'Konens søster' (the wife's sister), so Tore was probably still alive about 1787. ¶3

Siblings (heirs of the bachelor Jørgen Johnsen, Nordre Midbø, d. 1790, buried at Nissedal aged 52 per 7983 c231R, so born about 1738): four full brothers and four full sisters. Brothers: Thore (eldest, dead), Haavor (Halvor) Johnsen, Ole Johnsen and Helje (Hølje?) Johnsen, all of age and present. Sisters: Gunnild Johnsdatter, dead, married to Hans Ansteensen Nøst (sons Ansteen Hansen Nøst, the lensmann, John Hansen, and Jørgen Hansen 22); Anne Johnsdatter, dead, married to Targe [Leifsen?] Strand (sons Ole, John, Jørgen and Leif Targersen and daughter Thone); Thone Johnsdatter, married to Jørgen [Leifsen?] under Muule; and Aasild Johnsdatter, dead, married to Søren Torvildsen (sons Søren 10 and John 8, guardian Ole Johnsen Lovig). Spellings as read from the scan; bracketed words are uncertain. Their parents (a Joen/John, b. before about 1715) are not named in the pages read. The 1777 godparents Jørgen Jonsen and Tone Jonsdatter Midbøe are this brother and sister. ¶7

Parents: the probate after Turi Jørgensdatter at Midbø, Nissedal, 13 May 1756, names her surviving husband Joen Olsøn and their children, the eldest being 'Tore Joensøn' (age partly blotted, apparently [2]1, which would give birth about 1735), followed by Jørgen 18, Helje 13, Ole 8, Halvor, Gunnil, Anne, Todne 10 and Aasild. Torgiei Halvorsen was appointed his curator. The sibling set matches the heirs of his brother Jørgen Johnsen in 1790. Vest-Telemark skifteprotokoll 1752-1759 (source 25673), fol. 304b-306a, views 309-310. This grades the link to his parents (see their records); his own grade is unchanged. A birth about 1735 also fits his being a soldier who married in 1762. ¶11

Life story

Marriage detail: at full resolution the 1775 betrothal entry (7982 c109R, 1st entry) reads 'Samme Dag Samme Sted Trolovet Enkemand Tore [Joen]sen Midbøe og Pigen Rannei Orms datter Tvedt', so Tore was a widower when he married Rannei. The copulation note in the right column reads 'Copulerte i Nissedal... 1776'; the day and feast are under an ink blot and could not be read. A probable first marriage: 26 Feb 1762, Nissedal, 'unge Karl og Soldat Tore Joensen og Pige Tone Olsdatter', cautioners Ole Midbøe and Niels Grovum, copulated in Nissedal church 29 Mar 1762 (7981 c125L, 2nd entry). Ole Midbøe as cautioner fits a Midbø son; the identification rests on name, place and the 'Enkemand' of 1775. ¶9

Earlier wife's probate: Vest-Telemark skifteprotokoll 1776-81 (source 25676), skifte No 155, fol. 110a (view 113 right), proceedings 'Aar 1775 [or 1776] den 2[.] Junii' at 'Gaarden Midbøe i Nissedals Annex' after Signe Olsdatter, who had died in April 177[4?]. The estate was divided between 'hendes efterlevende Ektemand Tore Joensen' (her surviving husband Tore Joensen) and her own kin, since she left no children: a brother Ole Olsen [Gravthe?] in Mo, a half-brother Jon Olsen, and the children of a deceased sister Ingeborg Olsdatter (among them Anne Olsdatter, 9). A debt in the estate names 'Thore Joensen Midbøe' (dated 17 Feb 1772). This proves that Tore Joensen of Midbø was a widower before his 1776 marriage to Rannei Ormsdatter, as the 1776 betrothal ('Enkemand Tore') says, and that Signe left no children, so his daughter Turi (b. about 1776) and her sisters were Rannei's. Whether Signe Olsdatter is the 'Tone Olsdatter' he married in 1762 (7981 c125L) is not settled; the names differ. Read from the scan; header. ¶10

Name: recorded as 'Thore Joensen Midböe' (Tore Joensen of the farm Midbø, Nissedal) when his daughter Turi was baptised on 6 Jan 1777 in Nissedal church. Tore and Thore are spellings of the same name. His wife is not named in the entry. ¶1

Death (inference only): Tore is not in any Midbøe household in the 1801 census, and his daughter Guro, aged 13, lived with her married sister rather than with a parent. This suggests that he was dead by Feb 1801, but no burial has been found and he may have lived elsewhere. ¶4

Marriage: wife [R/K]annei Omundsdatter (Rannei Ormsdatter), named in the same probate. Gulov Omundsen of Treungen, guardian of one daughter, was probably her brother. ¶8

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Confidence: Probable. His existence and his fatherhood of the child Turi of 1777 are stated in the baptism read from the scan; his link to our Turi is Probable (see her notes). ¶5a

Upgrade: from Probable to Confirmed. The probate after his brother, the bachelor Jørgen Johnsen of Nordre Midbø (Vest-Telemark skifteprotokoll 1790-94, source 25679, skifte No 21, registered 12 Jun 1790 by the deputy Christian Brandt with the appraisers Ansteen Hansen Nøst and Ole Johnsen Midbøe), names as first heir 'Ældste Broder Thore Johnsen Midbøe', dead, who left five daughters by his wife [R/K]annei Omundsdatter: Thuri 13, Thone 10, Anne 8, Gunnild 5 and Guroe 2. Thuri is our Turi Torsdatter (baptised 1777) and Guroe is the Guro Toresdatter who lived with Turi in 1801. So he is her father by a record that states it, and he died before 12 Jun 1790 (the earlier note inferred death by 1801). ¶6
