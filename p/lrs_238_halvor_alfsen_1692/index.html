About the family

Father of Alf Halvorsen (Speculative): Alf Halvorsen was of Dale at his marriage in 1753, a Halvor Dale stood cautioner twice in 1757, Halvor's own patronymic Alfsen fits the naming pattern Alf, son Halvor, grandson Alf, and Alf named a son Haavor (Halvor) in Dec 1758. Halvor was about 36 when Alf was born (about 1728). What is missing: no record names Alf's father, and other Halvors may have lived at Dale. ¶2a

Life story

Burial: 'Halvor Alfsen Dale', aged 67 (so born about 1692), was buried in Drangedal on 14 Dec 1759. Drangedal ministerialbok 1697-1767, burials p. 22 (men's column, late 1759). The patronymic is read Alfsen; Alsen is possible but less likely. ¶1

Probate (skifte): 20 Feb 1760 at Dale in Tørdal after 'afg. Halvor Alfsøn Dahle', by the sheriff Joen Nielsen and the jurors and appraisers Jørgen Thorsen Lonne(?) and Brynield Tellefsen Onnestad. No widow is named; the estate was divided among his '2 Sønner og 4re Døttre' (2 sons and 4 daughters): eldest son Joen Halvorsen Dahle, of age (myndig); 'Anden og yngste Søn' (second and youngest son) Alf Halvorsen, of age; eldest daughter Groe Halvorsdatter, 'været gift med' (formerly married to) Halvor Torredal; second daughter Live, married to Tov Traae; third daughter Inge[bor?], married to Gunnuld Olsen Hülen(?); fourth daughter Aase, married to Jørgen Kittilsen Tvedt. Jørgen Thorsen spoke for the minor Halvor Halvorsen Torredahl (probably Groe's son; inference). The land: the farm Dale with bøksel and herlighet, houses and mill, valued 450 rd; Joen and Alf each received 85 rd, Groe 42 rd 2 ort (daughters took half shares). Bamble skifteprotokoll I 6a, fol. 130a-132a (the remaining daughters' lots on fol. 132b-133a were not read). ¶4

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the grade · 1 correction · 1 alternative set aside · 1 open question

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Confidence raised from Speculative to Probable as father of Alf Halvorsen of Toe (see his page): his probate names a son Alf Halvorsen, of age in 1760, and the Toe family's guardians, cautioners and godparents (Jon Dahle, Halvor Torredal, Tov Traae) are his son and sons-in-law. See the 'Father (Probable)' note on Alf. This supersedes the note 'Father of Alf Halvorsen (Speculative)', whose missing piece (a probate naming his heirs) is now found. It stays Probable because neither probate says that Alf of Toe and Alf the son of Halvor Dale are one man. ¶5

Corrections 1

Correction: the note 'Possible daughter: Aasil Halvorsdatter Dale, betrothed late 1759 to Jørgen Kittilsen Tveit' is superseded. The probate names his fourth daughter 'Aase', married to Jørgen Kittilsen Tvedt, who is that bride (same husband; Aase here is taken as a form of Aasil). She is a daughter (stated), not a possible one. No daughter is named Aasil in the probate. ¶6

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Possible daughter: Aasil Halvorsdatter Dale, betrothed late 1759 to Jørgen Kittilsen Tveit of Hviteseid parish (Drangedal marriages p. 27). Inferred from the patronymic and farm only. ¶3

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Would confirm: Alf's baptism (about 1728, Drangedal), Alf's confirmation, or a probate after Halvor Alfsen Dale (1759-60) naming his heirs. ¶2b
