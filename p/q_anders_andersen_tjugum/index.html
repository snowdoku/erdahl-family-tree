Life story

The 1701 census, at 'Tyom og Grøneng' (Tjugum and Grøneng), lists Anders Andersen, 12 (b. ~1689), son of the farmer Anders Monsen. kgroenha.net no. 934 makes him Randi's father (born about 1688 at Tjugum, married Brita Knutsdatter Næsse, died 1749 at Grøneng). The link to Randi is unsourced. ¶1

1701 census, re-read: on his own page Anders Andersen is 'Sønn', age 12 (born about 1689, calculated), listed directly under the bonde Anders Monsen, 52, at 'Tyom og Grøneng', Leganger parish, page 31, before his brother Mons Andersen (born about 1693). That makes the 1701 boy a son of Anders Monsen on a primary record. It does not raise this person's confidence: nothing in the census, in Randi Andersdatter's 1801 census entry or in her 1752 marriage (as recorded so far) names her father beyond the patronymic Anders, so the step 'Randi's father = this 1701 boy' still rests only on kgroenha.net no. 934. The census does agree with kgroenha.net's birth year (about 1688) and farm (Tjugum). Stays Speculative. ¶2a

Probate (skifte): held 7-9 December 1750 at Tjugum, Tjugum skipreide, after the late Anders Andersen Tjugum, between the widow [Kari?] Clausdatter and 'deres sammenavlede Børn' (their joint children): the eldest son Ole Andersen Nesse, dead, leaving Claus, Mons and [Ane?] Olsdatter (their mother Giertrud Monsdatter Nesse, remarried to Hans Nielsen); Hans Andersen Esse, of age; Claus Andersen, of age; Anders, 18; Johannes[?], 16; Mons, 12; and one daughter, read 'Rangdi Andersdatter', aged 2[3?]. Her guardian was her brother Hans Andersen Esse. Appraisers were the lagrettemenn Mons Larsen Ese and Sjur Asbjørnsen Haugsnes. Gross estate 691 riksdaler, net 298. ¶3

Laberg 1985 (Tjugum, printed p. 104): Anders Andersson, who got the farm after his father Anders Monsson, was born [about] 1688 and married 'ei Brita' (patronymic unread), who was probably from [an unread place]; he died 1749 leaving a widow and children: Ola, who came to [Nesse?]; a son Klaus, [by] the 'andre' (second) [wife]; Hans; Klaus Andersson, who got the holding after his brother Hans and died 1757; Jon Andersson, unmarried, died 1763; Mons; and Randi, who married Kristen... Sværen. The 1750 probate (7-9 Dec 1750) is contemporaneous and is preferred where they differ: it names the widow [Kari?] Clausdatter and calls all seven children the couple's joint children. The book's 'Jon Andersson' suggests the probate's doubtful 'Johannes[?] Andersen, 16' is Jon. ¶8a

Widow's probate: the probate after Brite Clausdatter at Tjugum, 24 Apr 1765 (Ytre Sogn 8a fol. 258a, No. 12), calls her 'afg. Anders Andersens Enke' and names her children Hans Andersen Tjugum, Anders Andersen Berdall, Mons Andersen Haugland and the daughter Randi Andersdatter, married to a Christen (byname read 'S..rn', probably Sværen), husband present. This bridges the 1750 daughter 'Rangdi' to Randi, wife of Christen Pedersen Sværen (m. 1752), and so supports his own link to Randi Andersdatter Tjugum on the same grounds as Brite's upgrade; his grade is left for review. ¶10

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Confidence raised from Speculative to Probable on the 1750 probate (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll A 7a, fol. 34a, No. 90). 'Rangdi' is a normal 18th-century spelling of Randi (from Ragndid), and an age of 23 would put her birth about 1727, as the 1801 census does for Randi Andersdatter (74). His sons Mons and Anders repeat the names of the 1701 household (father Anders Monsen, brother Mons), so this is very probably the 1701 boy born about 1689. Not Confirmed because the daughter's name could also be read 'Rangild' (Ragnhild), the age digit after 2 is unclear, and no record yet joins this daughter to the Randi who married Christen Persen Sværen in 1752. ¶4a

Confidence raised from Probable to Confirmed as father of Randi Andersdatter Tjugum on two records read from the scans. (1) His probate of 7-9 Dec 1750 (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll A 7a, fol. 34a, No. 90) names a daughter 'Rangdi Andersdatter', in her twenties, with her brother Hans Andersen Esse as guardian. (2) The Leikanger church book (source 11565, canvases 128-129) records the betrothal at Tjugum on 5 May 1752 and the marriage on 19 June 1752 of 'Randi Andersd: Tiugum' to Christen Pedersen Sværen, with Hans Æse (Hans Andersen Esse) as her sponsor (forlover). Same name, the farm Tjugum, the right age, and the same brother acting for her. ¶7

Corrections 2

Correction: the tree had him dying in 1749 at Grøneng (kgroenha.net). The probate index and the probate heading place him at Tjugum (the 1701 census lists 'Tyom og Grøneng' as one holding), and the probate was held in December 1750, so he died before 7 December 1750, probably in 1750 (the index lists no Anders Andersen at Grøneng). ¶5

Correction: kgroenha.net gives his wife as Brita Knutsdatter Næsse (see her page). The 1750 probate names his widow, the mother of all his surviving children, as a Clausdatter (Brite Clausdatter). The Brita marriage is kept in the tree only as the published claim; no record found supports it. ¶6

Open questions and the next record to read 2

What would settle it: the probate after Anders Andersen of Grøneng, about 1749 (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll for the 1740s), naming a daughter Randi, or Randi Andersdatter's own probate (Ytre Sogn, 1809-1813, Sværen) naming her siblings or her father's farm. ¶2b

What would settle it: the 1752 Leikanger marriage entry giving the bride's farm or father, or the probate's closing pages naming the daughter's later husband. ¶4b
