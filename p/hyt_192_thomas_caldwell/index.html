Life story

Thomas Caldwell (Coldwell), esq., husband of Jane Boyle and father of Mildred, wife of Dean John Eveleigh of Ross (Brady, Clerical and Parochial Records, vol. 2, p. 428; the Heverly family history also names 'Thomas Caldwell, Esq., and Jane, his wife'). The Blennerhassett Pedigree of c.1733 gives Mildred's maiden name as Coldwell. Added. ¶1

Possible kinsman, not a proved link: Cotton's Fasti (vol. 1) and Brady (vol. 2) record a 'Charles Caldwell, or Coldwell', ordained deacon in 1625 and priest in 1626, prebendary in Cork from 1626, and vicar choral of Cloyne from 1628 to 1634 or later. He served in the same Boyle-patronage dioceses as Dean John Eveleigh, who was Vicar of Knockmourne, Cloyne, from 1620. He may have been a son of Thomas and Jane, and so Mildred's brother, but no record says so. Test record for Thomas's parents: the Visitation of Berkshire 1623 or 1665-66 (Harleian Society vols 56-57), s.v. Caldwell or Coldwell; the Newbury (St Nicholas) parish registers; and PCC wills of Caldwell of Newbury, 1590-1650. Added. ¶4

Lodge (Peerage of Ireland, 1754 and 1789, vol. 1, p. 144) calls him 'Thomas Caldwell of Nowbry in Berkshire', that is, Newbury, husband of Jane, daughter of Michael Boyle, merchant of London. His own parents were not found. Lodge and Cotton's Fasti give no Caldwell pedigree. The Visitation of Berkshire was not reached, and an archive.org full-text search for Caldwell or Coldwell of Newbury found nothing before 1700. Added. ¶3

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

2 on the grade · 1 on the identification · 2 searches with no result · 2 open questions

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Probable, graded on his link to his daughter Mildred Coldwell: the father's name comes only from compiled sources (Brady, citing Cork wills and MSS; the Heverly history), and the contemporary pedigree confirms only the surname. ¶2a

Grade unchanged: Probable on his link to Mildred Coldwell (the link rests on Brady and Lodge, not on this identification). ¶5b

The case for this identification 1

Identification: the only Thomas Coldwell/Caldwell found at Newbury in the right period is the Rev. Thomas Coldwell, M.A. Venn: of Kent; matriculated from Corpus Christi, Cambridge, 1582; B.A. 1585-6, M.A. 1589; Fellow 1588-93; incorporated at Oxford 1592; Rector of Newbury 1592/3 (instituted by Bishop John Coldwell of Salisbury, a native of Faversham, Kent, 'probably a kinsman', on the Queen's presentation); Subdean of Salisbury 1595-9, succeeding Richard Hooker; Rector of Shaw-cum-Donnington from 6 Apr 1605. He was still Rector of Newbury in 1613 (trustee of Margaret Cross's charity) and in 1615 (Attorney-General v. Coldwell, King's Bench). Shaw had a new rector 'on death of Thomas Coldwell' on 15 Feb 1618 [probably 1618/19]. Lodge's 'Thomas Caldwell of Nowbry in Berkshire', husband of Jane Boyle, fits him in place and date: Jane's brothers were born in the 1570s-80s, and her daughter Mildred married Dean Eveleigh (born about 1598). This identification is NOT proved: no record found names the Rector's wife, and Brady styles Mildred's father 'esq.' rather than clerk. If he is the man, he died about 1618 at or near Newbury, and the Cork clergyman Charles Coldwell (prebendary 1626, will 1661, Cork and Ross) could be his son. ¶5a

Searches that found nothing 2

Searched with no result: the Visitations of Berkshire 1532-1665/6 (Harleian 56-57) have no Caldwell/Coldwell pedigree; the only near hit is 'Eliz. da. & coheire of Tho. Colwell of Feversham in Kent', wife of Thomas Brickenden of Inkpen, Berks (aged 80 in 1664; their son Richard aged 42 in 1664), in Ashmole's 1665-66 visitation, p. 179. Feversham is the Coldwell home town, and Inkpen is 6 miles from Newbury, so this Thomas Colwell may be a kinsman (or even the Rector, if Elizabeth was Mildred's sister and co-heir), but nothing connects them. No Coldwell will in the Archdeaconry of Berks index 1508-1652 (British Record Society) and none in PCC (PROB 11) 1595-1660. ¶6a

FamilySearch: no Coldwell/Caldwell/Colwell entries for Newbury or Shaw-cum-Donnington 1585-1630 in any FamilySearch index, and no Mildred Coldwell or Caldwell baptism anywhere in England 1595-1625. The Newbury and Shaw registers (Berkshire Record Office) must be read on site or through another index (S0277 closed as blocked). Added (FamilySearch, signed in, 2 Nov 2026). ¶7

Open questions and the next record to read 2

Test record: Mildred Eveleigh's will (1681/2) or the Dean's will (1678), if a copy survives; or a Caldwell will in the Prerogative or Cork indexes naming a daughter Mildred Eveleigh. Added. ¶2b

Test record: the will or administration of Thomas Coldwell, clerk, of Newbury or Shaw, about 1618-19, in the Salisbury Consistory or Dean of Salisbury peculiar courts (Wiltshire and Swindon History Centre); the Newbury (St Nicholas) and Shaw parish registers, for a baptism of Mildred, daughter of Thomas Coldwell, about 1600-1615, and his burial about 1618. ¶6b
