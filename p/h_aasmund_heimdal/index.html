About the family

First wife: 'Osmund Hiemdals hustru', aged 22, buried in Treungen on 11 May 1764, the day of Targie's baptism, so probably she died in childbirth. Her name appears to be Gundborg (Gunbjørg), but the reading is uncertain. ¶3

His daughter Ingebor, aged 17 weeks, was buried at Treungen in late January 1773: 'Aasmund Evindsen Hiemdahls daatter Ingebor, gl. 17 uger' (Kviteseid ministerialbok 1754-1773, burials p.54, image 180). She was born about Sep 1772, a child of his second marriage (to Signe Tallefsdatter Homme, 1766). ¶7

His daughter Torbor was baptized in Treungen at Christmas 1761: 'Samme dag i Trüngen dobt Osmund Hiemdahls daatter Torbor'; godparents Tostof Aarack, Knud Joensen(?), Asi(?) Olsen, Margit Hiemdahl, Liv(?) Steel (Kviteseid ministerialbok 1754-1773, baptisms, image 28). She was probably the first child of his first marriage (1 Jul 1761, to Gundbjør Targiesdatter Sonderland). The mother is not named. ¶8

Mother (bygdebok, Probable): Nissedal bygdesoge [2] Treungen (canvas 361) lists 'Åsmund 1732, [g.] 1761 [m...] Tarjeisdtr. Sønderland' first among the children of Eivind Andersson Heimdal and Hæge Knutsdtr. Heimdal; linked mother Hæge Knudsdatter. Birth ABT 1732 from the book. The book (canvas 362) gives a skifte after Åsmund in 1792 ('Åsmund Eivindson d 1792 f 1732'), so he died about 1792; his second wife Signe (d. 1812, b. 1744). Test record for the mother: Eivind Andersen's 1766-67 skifte (DA 25674/25675) and Åsmund's own 1792 skifte (Øvre Telemark skifteprotokoll no. 15, DA 25679, 1790-94). ¶10

Life story

Known only from the patronymic of his son Targie Aasmundsen and from his widow, Signe Tellevsdatter (b. ~1743), who was Targie's stepmother. She lived in Targie's household at Hiemdal in 1801. Signe was his second (or later) wife; Targie's own mother is unknown. ¶1

First marriage: betrothed 12 May 1761 (3rd day of Pentecost) in Treungen, married 1 Jul 1761 in Treungen: ungkarl (bachelor) Aasmund Evindsen Hiemdahl and pige (maid) Gundbjør Targiesdatter Sonderland (Kviteseid 1754-1773 p.39, image 123). This confirms the first wife's name and gives her patronymic. ¶5

Named as Osmund Evindsen Hiemdal at his son Targie's baptism, 11 May 1764, Treungen. ¶2

Second marriage: on 24 Feb 1766 in Treungen, the widower Aasmund Evindsen Hiemdal was betrothed to pige Signe Tallefsdatter Homme. They needed a royal dispensation for kinship. They married 25 Jun 1766 in Treungen church (Kviteseid 1754-1773 p.62). She is the stepmother Signe Tellevsdatter (58) in Targie's 1801 household. ¶4

The 1766 betrothal entry (p.62, image 135, read on the image) gives the reason for the royal letter as 'de ere besvogrede, og derfore er mig leveret Kongl. Brev' ('they are related by marriage, and therefore a royal letter was delivered to me'). This is affinity, not blood kinship, and no degree is stated. For a widower, affinity probably means Signe Tallefsdatter Homme was a blood relative of his first wife, Gundbjør Targiesdatter Sonderland (inference). The sureties were Gunder Tovelsen(?) Homme and Knud Wraalsen(?) Haugsjoe. ¶6

Named 'Aasmund Evensøn myndig' first among the children of the late Even Andersen and his widow Haye Knudsdatter at the skifte held on Hiemdahl 20 May 1767 (DA 25674 No. 775, view 735). This confirms both his parents (graded on their records). Siblings named: Knud Evensen (of age), Anders 22 1/2, Niels 12 1/2, Gundbør (m. Tiøstof Aarach), Torbiør 19. ¶11

Alive and a landowner in 1776-77. 'Osmund Evensen' mortgaged 6[3/4] Bdtg of nordre Hiemdall, with bøxel and odel right, to Sr. Anders Dedekam for 227 Rd 1 Mk 12 Sk (dated 25 Jun 177[6?], registered 4 Mar 1777; Panteregister I 1 fol. 269b, DA 10915 view 312). On the same page Knud Evensen, who had land 'udlagt' (laid out to him, evidently at a skifte, probably after Evind Andersen, d. 1766), bought a further 4½ Bdtg on 5 Mar 1781 and so held 9 Bdtg. Knud is probably Aasmund's brother; the page gives no relationship word, so he is not added. The name Knud fits Evind's wife Hæge Knudsdatter. So Aasmund died after Mar 1777 (and before 1801). The Evind Andersen estate (protocol 10, DA 25674, 1766-67) remains the record that would name Aasmund's mother. ¶9
