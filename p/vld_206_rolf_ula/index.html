About the family

Father of Knut Rolfsen of Nordre Slettum (see his page), Probable; graded on his link to Knut (compiled source only). Lesja bygdebok vol. 3 (nb.no b5a76aea1b65fda9b28e08b3852f9e55), canvas 326, chapter Ula, word-box rebuild with gaps: a man 'ca. 1639 - ca. 1694' with children including Tore (ca. 1663), Kristen, Ola (took over), 'Knut, ca. 1685, [sjå] Nørdre Slettom', Lars and Magnhild, and 'Om skiftet etter Rolf i 1694'. The name of the head of the entry falls in a gap; Rolf is taken from 'skiftet etter Rolf i 1694' and from Knut's patronymic Rolfsen (vol. 1 canvas 370: 'Knut Rolfs.'). Knut named his eldest son Rolf (Rolv Knudsen, b. 1720) and a daughter Magnhild (1727), as for this family. The wife may be an Anne (the word 'Anne' stands in the entry), but she is not entered. Dates ABT. ¶1

Father entered as Christen Jensen of Hovauk (see his page), Probable: the 1664 Lesja census lists 'Rolf Chestenss.', sønn, aged 25 (b. 1639), under 'Chresten Jenssøn' on Houvoch (Hovauk, gnr. 121) with brothers Jens (1637) and Hans (1645); Rolf's brother Hans Christensen is the man whose 1723 skifte at Ula names Rolf's children as heirs. Rolf's own grade (his link to Knut) is not affected by this link upward. ¶7

Life story

The skifte after him was found and read: Nord-Gudbrandsdal skifteprotokoll 1690-1719 (source 25155), fol. 23b (view 27), 26 Sep 1694, 'Arfueskifte efter Sl. Rolf Christenss:' on the farm (read as Ula-gaarden; the word is underlined and partly struck), 'imellem hans efterlatte Hustru Anne Arnesd: og deris 6 Børn, Tore, Christen, Oluf, Knud og Lars Rolfss: samt Magnild Rolfsdatter'. The six children are exactly the six that the Lesja bygdebok (vol. 3 canvas 326, Ula) lists, in the same order, so this is the skifte the bygdebok cites ('Om skiftet etter Rolf i 1694'). It names a son Knud Rolfsen, a minor, which fits Knut b. ABT 1685. His patronymic is Christensen, and he named a son Christen. Grade kept at Probable, graded on his link to Knut Rolfsen of Nordre Slettum (see his page): the record names the relationship to a Knud Rolfsen of Ula in 1694, but identifying that boy with the Knut Rolfsen who held Nordre Slettum from about 1718/20 crosses farms and about 25 years (audit 4: the cross-place identification belongs to the parent's grade). Knut named his eldest son Rolf and a daughter Magnhild, as here; no rival Knud Rolfsen is known in Lesja. This supersedes the earlier note that the head of the entry was named only from a bygdebok gap. ¶3

Correction to the Test record. An exact-phrase search of the Lesja bygdebok (vol. 3 canvas 326) reads 'I 1723 var det skifte etter bror til Rolf', i.e. the 1723 skifte was after a BROTHER of Rolf (an uncle of Knut), not after an unmarried son; the entries for Rolf's children Kristen and Lars say 'død utan arvingar før skiftet etter [gap] i 1723' and Magnhild 'død før skiftet etter [gap] i 1723', so Rolf's surviving children (among them Knut of Nordre Slettum) were heirs in it. It would still bridge the gap if it names 'Knud Rolfsen Slettum' as a brother's son. Search so far: 25156 views 140-189 read header by header; 1723 entries with Lesja headers are at views 143, 146, 149 (21-24 May) and 179 (7 Oct), and none is at Ula or after a single man. The deceased may be filed under another farm. Test record (still open): the 1723 skifte after Rolf Christensen's brother (25156; unread so far: views 3-139 and 190-229; the sorenskriver Lauritz Brun's Lesja sessions were 21-24 May and 7 Oct 1723). ¶5

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Upgrade: Probable -> Confirmed, graded on his link to Knut Rolfsen of Nordre Slettum (see his page).: The 1723 skifte (25156 view 136, fol. 131b-132a; spring Lesja session of Lauritz Brun, May 1723) is after 'Sl. Hans Christensen' of 'Ulsjordet i Læssøe Sogn' (Ula), who left no children; his heirs are his brothers and sisters or their children, set out in groups A-G. Group B reads 'Arfvinger Sl. Rolf Christensens Børn 1. Thoer Rolfsen 60 aar 2. Ole Rolfsen... 40 aar 3. Knud Rolfsen Slett[?] 38 aar og 4. Arfvinger Magnild Rolfsdatter Slett[?]s Børn' (three Ingebrigtsen children). Knud's farm word is a cramped abbreviation (tall looped capital, as in this hand's 'Sl.', then -ett- and a flourish); the same word follows Magnild's name, and the Lesja bygdebok independently places Magnhild at Slettom ('ho hadde før vore [gift på] Slettom', vol. 3 canvas 326) and Knut at Nørdre Slettom from 1718 (vol. 1 canvas 370), so the reading 'Slett(om)' is well supported though not letter-certain. Knud's age 38 gives b. ABT 1685, as for Knut of Nordre Slettum, and Thoer's 60 gives b. ABT 1663, as the bygdebok has for Tore. This is a sibling-set bridge (audits 8/9): the 1694 skifte after Rolf Christensen at Ula names 'deris 6 Børn, Tore, Christen, Oluf, Knud og Lars Rolfss: samt Magnild'; the 1723 skifte at the same farm, naming the same father, lists Thoer, Ole and Knud Rolfsen and the children of the dead Magnild, while Christen and Lars are absent, as the bygdebok says ('død utan arvingar før skiftet... 1723'). No rival Knud Rolfsen is known in Lesja. The 1723 record itself names 'Knud Rolfsen Slett[om]', aged 38, in 1723 (during Knut's tenure of Nordre Slettum 1718-45) as a child of the late Rolf Christensen, and the four-sibling match ties him to the Knud son of Rolf Christensen and Anne in 1694. This supersedes the earlier notes that the identification crossed farms and decades with no bridging record, and the open Test record for the 1723 skifte, which is now read. ¶6

Audit 12 (2 Nov 2026): Confirmed KEPT, graded on his link to Knut Rolfsen of Nordre Slettum (see his page). The 1723 skifte after Hans Christensen at Ulsjordet (25156 view 136) is a far-end record that names the parent: 'Sl. Rolf Christensens Børn... 3. Knud Rolfsen Slett[?] 38 aar' (audit 11: a link across places needs a record at the far end naming the parent). The four-sibling match (Thoer, Ole, Knud, Magnild) ties it to the 1694 skifte, and Knud's age 38 fits the 1694 minor. The identification of that Knud with the tree's Knut of Nordre Slettum then rests on the farm word 'Slett[?]', which the 1726 skifte after Endre Qvam writes in full for the same man ('Knud Rolfsen Slettums Hustru'), plus his age, plus Magnild's same farm word; no rival Knud Rolfsen is named in Lesja. ¶8a
