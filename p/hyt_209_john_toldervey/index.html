About the family

Father of Walter Toldervey (see his page), Probable: Noyes-Gilman Ancestry (1907), p. 306, makes Elizabeth (Toldervey) Prince the 'granddaughter of Rev. John Toldervey, "Bedell of Arts, University of Oxford," and his wife Alyce', through her father Walter. J. B. Prince, The Prince Family of Hull and Boston (1976), Appendix 2, reportedly abstracts John's 1567 will as naming Walter, one of the younger children (reported by WikiTree Tolderbury-2; not read here). Consistent with the Oxford records: the family were Oxford citizens of St Peter-in-the-East (Alice Toldervey, widow, of that parish, will 1597, 'probably of John'; Henry Toldervey, son of a widow Alice, freeman 1572-3, mayor 1613-14), and Walter took the citizens' oath in 1613, Henry's mayoral year. The relationship rests on compiled sources only, so Probable. ¶2

Life story

Esquire bedell of Arts of the University of Oxford: elected 1540, and dead by 10 May 1567, when Thomas Hutchinson was elected in place of 'Tollerbie' deceased (Clark, Register of the University of Oxford II pt 1). Foster has him as B.A. 1537. His will was proved in the Chancellor's Court in 1567 ('Toldervey, John, one of the Bedels', Griffiths' index). ¶1

Note: William Toldervey of Oxford's will (PROB 11/119/320, 1612, image read) leaves five pounds to 'my uncle Walter Tolderbey' and to 'my brother James Tolderbey'; his mother was then 'Joane Burrett'. It shows a second Toldervey line in Oxford in Walter's generation (William's father, presumably Walter's brother), but it does not name John the bedell. Grade unchanged. ¶4

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 open question

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Test record: John Toldervey's will, Oxford University Chancellor's Court, 1567 (Oxford University Archives, Hyp/B, wills T), which should name his children; and the 1612 PCC will of William Toldervey of Oxford (TNA PROB 11/119), which reportedly calls Walter his uncle. ¶3
