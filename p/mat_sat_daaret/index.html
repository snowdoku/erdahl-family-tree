About the family

Wife of Peter Olsen at Lille Hvalstad in Skatval, Nedre Stjørdal. Recorded as 'Daaret Pedersdatter, kone' at Guri Anna's baptism in 1836, and in fuller form as 'Daaret Elisabet Pedersdatter' and 'Doret Elisabet Pedersdatter' at baptisms from the same farm in 1828 and 1831. ¶1

Attached as Guri's mother at Probable, on the same evidence as her husband. ¶2

Mother of Guri Anna Pettersdatter Walstad, and wife of Peter Olsen of Lille Hvalstad (Valstad), Skatval, Nedre Stjørdal. Named as the mother in all six of her children's baptisms, 1828 to 1842. ¶3

Parentage: Her father, Peder Pedersen, is Confirmed by name: her 1828 marriage index names him, and the 1801 census shows her as the child of Peder Pedersen at Schiervold. Her mother, Gullov (Gullaug) Pedersdatter, is Probable: the 1801 census calls Daarethe the couple's child and both are in their first marriage, but no record of Daaret's own baptism naming her mother has been found. ¶9

Mother: the baptism names the mother as G[...] Pedersdatter, wife of Peder Pedersen Skiervold, and a Gullov Skiervold stands godmother. Together with the 1801 census household, this supports Gullov Pedersdatter as her mother. The first name is still partly illegible on this scan; the 16372 klokkerbok copy may show it clearly. ¶15

Life story

Baptism not yet found: every baptism in the Stjørdal register (all five churches) from June 1798 to the end of June 1799 was read on the scans (16373 canvases 27-42) and none is hers. The only Daaret in that window, baptised at Skatval on 27 January 1799, is the daughter of Zacharias Pedersen Hiland and Malena, so she is not this woman. She may have been born before her parents married on 14 June 1798, or baptised outside Stjørdal. ¶12

Birth: About 1799 at Skjervold in Skatval. The 1801 census gives her age as 2 and her 1828 marriage entry gives her age as 29, birth year 1799 and birthplace Skiervold, so both put her birth in about 1798-99. The 1865 census gives her age as 69, which implies about 1796-97 (the index calculates 1797). The two early records agree and are preferred. Her baptism was not found in the indexed Stjørdal book of 1797-1815. That index begins on 28 May 1797, so a birth early in 1797 would fall before it. The other possibility is that her entry is indexed under a spelling that was not tried. ¶5

Baptism found: baptised 28 Jul 1799 (10th Sunday after Trinity) at Værnes church, Stjørdal, as 'Dordi Elisabet', the 2nd child baptised that day. Entry: '2. Dordi Elisabet. Fad. Peder Peds. Skiervold Mod. G[...] Pedersd. Fadd. [...] Se[l?]ahtvold, Ole, Giertrud, Baard og Gullov Skiervold, Mali Wigaunet.' Godparents (fadder): [a blotted name] Schatvold(?), Ole, Giertrud, Baard and Gullov Skiervold, and Mali Wigaunet. The mother's first name is blotted, leaving only the initial G, which fits Gullov; 'Pedersd.' is clear. The entry is written in as an insertion in the Værnes section, not the Skatvold section, which is probably why earlier searches missed it. Stjørdal ministerialbok 1797-1815 (Digitalarkivet 16373), p. 64, read from the scan. ¶13

1801 census: 'Daarethe Pedersdatter', aged 2, is listed as 'Deres barn' (their child) in household 02 on the farm Schiervold (Skjervold), Størdalen. The household is Peder Pedersen, 33, inderst (a lodger without land), and his wife Gullov Pedersdatter, 26, both in their first marriage. The name, the father's name, the farm and the age all agree with her 1828 marriage entry. ¶6

Her name is written differently every time: 'Daaret Elisabet Persdatter' (1828), 'Doret Elisabet Pedersdatter' (1831), 'Doret Pedersdatter, kone' (1833), 'Daaret Pedersdatter, kone' (1836), 'Daareth Pedersdatter, hustru' (1840 and 1842). The fullest form, Daaret Elisabet Pedersdatter, is the one carried here. ¶4

Marriage: Married Peter Olsen of Lillehvalstad at Værnes, Stjørdal, on 5 June 1828, as 'pige' (an unmarried woman) aged 29, born 1799 at Skiervold. The index names her father as Peder Pedersen ('bruras far'). ¶7

Marriage date and first child: The marriage on 5 June 1828 came after the birth of their first child, Mette, born 10 March 1828 and baptised 30 March 1828. Mette's baptism is indexed as legitimate ('e', ekte). Whether that mark is the clerk's own or reflects a later note can only be settled by reading the scan. ¶8

1865 census: Doret Elisabet Pedersdatter, aged 69, born in Nedre Stjørdal, is listed as 'enke' (a widow) and 'føderaads enke' (a widow living on a føderåd, a retirement pension from the farm) at Lillevalstad, Skatval. The household is that of Peder Olsen, 65, gaardbruger selveier (farm owner, freeholder), probably her late husband's brother. Her unmarried daughter 'Mæthe Pettersdatter', aged 40, lives with her and is described as 'hendes Datter' (her daughter). ¶10

Corrections 1

Correction: the earlier note that her baptism was not found is superseded. She was baptised 13 months after her parents' marriage of 14 Jun 1798, so she was not born before it. The baptism agrees with the 1801 census age (2) and the 1828 marriage (29, born 1799). It does not agree with the 1865 census age of 69. ¶14

Searches that found nothing 1

Death: Not found. She is not in the 1875 census at Valstad, and no burial for her was found in the indexed Nedre Stjørdal burial registers for 1859-1913, searched by name and by farm. She probably died or moved away between the end of 1865 and the end of 1875. ¶11
