About the family

Youngest of the six children of Sivert and Anna (Satter) Eidem, and the sister whose obituary broke this whole brick wall. She outlived all five of her brothers and sisters, so her obituary lists the entire sibship - and it names 'MAYME EIDEM' among them, which is how Mayme O. Eidem's maiden name was finally established. A great-great-aunt of David Erdahl. ¶1

Survived by four daughters, fourteen grandchildren, thirty great-grandchildren and one great-great-grandson. ¶9

Life story

Birth record: South Dakota state birth file no. 64424, filed 7 January 1911, records a girl born 4 January 1911 in Union County, mother ANNA SATTER, father GIVERT EIDEN. The index leaves her first name blank (shown as '*') and spells the surname EIDEN; 'Givert Eiden' is a misreading or misspelling of Sivert (Severt) Eidem. The date, county and mother identify this as Myrtle Sadie: it matches the birth date in her 2013 obituary exactly. The index gives county only, not town. ¶11

Censuses: Myrtle Eiden, daughter, b. abt 1912, in the 1920 federal census of Brule township; 'Myrtle S Eidem, b. 1912' in the 1925 South Dakota state census; 'Myrtle Sadie Eidem, daughter, b. abt 1911' in the 1930 federal census. The 1930 index records 'Sadie' as a MIDDLE NAME, not merely the nickname her 2013 obituary used. ¶10

Married Ernest Overdahl on 2 June 1939 at St Paul Lutheran Church, rural Elk Point - the church of her baptism, confirmation and marriage, whose cemetery the obituary calls 'her family's burial ground for three generations and 150 years'. They farmed in the area, bought a farm near Worthington, Minnesota in 1946, and raised four daughters as members of First Lutheran Church, Worthington. In 1975 they served on a mission station in Liberia, West Africa. They moved to St Cloud in 1991; Ernest died in 2002; she moved to Plymouth, Minnesota in 2005 at the age of 94 - which, the obituary records, 'necessitated her giving up the early morning water aerobics routine at a local gym' - and returned to St Cloud in 2011. She rosemaled, sewed, wrote and painted watercolours. Died 3 October 2013 aged 102. ¶3

Her four daughters - Solveig Goldstrand of Plymouth, Minnesota; Mary Ratliff of Eagan, Minnesota; Signe Lupfer of Amelia Island, Florida; and Gretchen Hutton of St Cloud - are living, and so are recorded here by name only, as the obituary printed them, and given no ids. ¶4

Her obituary has been REMOVED from the funeral home's live website, which has since been rebuilt on a different platform; the old permalink millercarlin.com/?p=4215 now redirects to the home page. It survives only in the Internet Archive, which holds fourteen captures of millercarlin.com/myrtle-sadie-overdahl/ between January 2021 and April 2024. Anyone re-checking this must use the archived copy. ¶5

Mayme (Eidem) Eidem's youngest sister, and the witness who proves the whole line. Her obituary, published when she died at St. Cloud, Minnesota on 3 October 2013 aged 102, says she 'was born January 4, 1911 near Elk Point, SD to Sivert and Anna (Satter) Eidem' and that she was preceded in death by her parents and by all five of her siblings, 'Bertha Leer, Samuel Eidem, Selma Nelson, Mayme Eidem, and Amos Eidem'. That sentence is the only record found anywhere that names Mayme as a daughter of this couple, and it names her under the surname Eidem. ¶6

Life: nine years teaching in South Dakota rural school districts - Lennox, Richland, North Spink and South Sioux Falls. She married Ernest Overdahl on 2 June 1939 at St. Paul Lutheran Church, rural Elk Point, the church in which she had been baptised and confirmed. They farmed, moved to Worthington, Minnesota in 1946 and were members of First Lutheran there for 46 years. In 1975 they served on a mission station in Liberia, West Africa. They moved to St. Cloud in 1991; after her husband's death in 2002 she lived at Plymouth, Minnesota, and spent her last two years at the Talahi Care Center in St. Cloud. ¶7

She was buried in the churchyard at rural Elk Point which her obituary calls 'her family's burial ground for three generations and 150 years' - the same Saint Paul Cemetery that holds her parents, her brothers Samuel and Amos, her grandfather Sivert Johnson Eidem and her great-uncles and aunts. ¶8

Biography from her own obituary (Miller-Carlin Funeral Home, St Cloud, Minnesota, October 2013): born 4 January 1911 near Elk Point, South Dakota, to Sivert and Anna (Satter) Eidem, and grew up on the Brule Creek on the farm her Norwegian immigrant father settled. Graduated from Augustana Academy, Canton, South Dakota, in the class of 1928, then took teacher training at Augustana College, Sioux Falls, and taught nine years in South Dakota country schools at Lennox, Richland, North Spink and South Sioux Falls. ¶2
