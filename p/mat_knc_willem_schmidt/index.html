About the family

Father of Anna Karoline (Schmidt) Knodel, named as 'Schmidt, Willem' on her 1974 BC death registration; his birthplace was unknown to the informant. Geni's public listing (a lead only) calls him Wilhelm Schmidt and names his wife Anna Maria. ¶1

Parents (Speculative): his mother is entered as the widow Luise Schmidt, born about 1832, in whose Atmagea household the Wilhelm born 1854 lived in June 1879; that link holds only if the identification above is right. His father was probably Michel Schmidt of Atmagea (grain-levy list 1877; dead by June 1879), but that is too uncertain to enter. ¶7

Children: Anna Karolina (b. 19 Nov 1881, Anna Karolina Schmidt), Wilhelmine (b. 9 Jan 1885 Ciucurova; compiled family book and EWZ index; Probable, not entered as a person) and Anna (b. 24 Nov 1888 Romania, d. 31 Jul 1906 Pfeilsdorf, Anna Schmidt). ¶13

Children (updated 13 Oct 2026; supersedes the earlier Children note): Anna Karolina (b. 19 Nov 1881, Anna Karolina Schmidt), Wilhelmine (b. 9 Jan 1885 Ciucurova; compiled family book and EWZ index; not entered as a person), Friedrich (b. 3 Nov 1886 Ciucurova, Friedrich Schmidt; Lissewo marriages 1909 no. 6), Anna (b. 24 Nov 1888, d. 1906 Pfeilsdorf, Anna Schmidt) and Rosa Therese (b. 30 Jun 1890 Atmagea, Rosa Therese Schmidt; Pfeilsdorf marriages 1913 no. 15). ¶20

Life story

Context, Heinrich Schmidt: a settler and owner (Besitzer) Heinrich Schmidt, born about mid-1865 (aged 46 in Oct 1911 and May 1912, 52 in Aug 1917, 53 in Aug 1918), lived at Pfeilsdorf and witnessed at least five marriages there in 1911-1918 of families from the Romanian Dobruja, three of them together with Jakob Knodel (Pfeilsdorf marriages 1911 no. 11, 1912 no. 3, 1917 no. 3, 1918 nos. 5 and 8; images read). His kinship to Wilhelm, if any, is unknown; he is not in the 1879 Atmagea household of the widow Luise Schmidt, and he is not entered as a person. ¶22

Place: Bilau, Kreis Culm, is Bielawy, gmina Lisewo. It was a manor district (Gutsbezirk) in the Amtsbezirk Lissewo from 1887 and was turned into a rural commune (Landgemeinde) on 29 Jun 1912, probably once the estate had been divided among settlers. Its births, marriages and deaths were registered by the Standesamt Lissewo, whose registers are in the State Archive in Toruń, fond 69/1170 (1874-1935) and 69/1916 (1936-1939); the 1908 and 1909 acts above name Bilau ('Bielawy') explicitly. ¶24

Death, lead only (Oct 2026): the Ancestry dbId 61250 index has a burial of a Wilhelm Schmidt at Culm on 23 Nov 1918, with no relatives named (record 2007572, image 0245394-00191, not read; queued as S0182). This is a name-only candidate: nothing in the index ties it to the settler of Bilau, and the image should give age, residence and wife. ¶26

1906: The Standesamt Pfeilsdorf death act of his daughter Anna Schmidt (1906 no. 23, registered 1 Aug 1906) names her father as Wilhelm Schmidt, 'living' (żyjący) at a place in Romania written 'Kurtbelli' (reading uncertain, also possible 'Kurbelli'; not yet identified in a Dobruja gazetteer), and describes him as a master blacksmith (mistrz kowalski). His wife is named as Anna Maria née Adam. He was therefore alive and still in Romania in the summer of 1906, when at least two of his daughters were at Pfeilsdorf in West Prussia. The act was declared by his son-in-law Gottlieb Knodel, who calls the deceased his unmarried sister-in-law; that makes her a sister of Karolina Knodel née Schmidt and this Wilhelm Schmidt the same man as Karolina's father 'Willem Schmidt' of her 1974 BC death registration. ¶8

Bilau, 1908: on 13 Nov 1908 the settler (Ansiedler, osadnik) Wilhelm Schmidt of Bilau, identified by a certificate of the estate administration at Bilau, declared at the Standesamt Lissewo (sitting at Heimbrunn) that his unmarried daughter Teresa Schmidt ('córka zgłaszającego', daughter of the declarant), Evangelical, living with him, had given birth in his dwelling at Bilau on 10 Nov 1908 at 5 a.m. to a boy named Jakob; he was present at the birth and signed (Lissewo births 1908 no. 124, Polish certified copy of 1949). The child was baptised at the Evangelical church of Lissewo on 16 Dec 1908 (index only, relative 'Therese'). He died at Bilau on 31 Mar 1909, aged 4 months 21 days, 'son of the unmarried settler's daughter Therese Schmidt'; Wilhelm reported the death and signed (Lissewo deaths 1909 no. 17, German original), and the child was buried at Lissewo on 3 Apr 1909 (index only). Wilhelm was therefore living at Bilau by 10 Nov 1908, six months before the earliest record known until now (his son Friedrich's marriage in May 1909). ¶23

1909: on 13 May 1909 at Heimbrunn, seat of the Standesamt (civil registry office) Lissewo, Kreis Culm, his son Friedrich Schmidt, a settler's son (Ansiedlersohn) born 3 Nov 1886 at Ciucurova in Romania, married Anna Carolina Schmidt. The act names Friedrich's parents as the settler Wilhelm Schmidt and his wife Marie née Adam, living at Bilau, Kreis Culm, and the first witness was 'der Ansiedler Wilhelm Schmidt', 54 years old, of Bilau. The act does not call him the groom's father, but the same name, title and residence make that all but certain. The second witness, the settler Heinrich Habermann, 36, of Bilau, was identified by Wilhelm (Lissewo marriages 1909 no. 6; image read). So the family was settled at Bilau by May 1909, and the witness's stated age puts his birth between 14 May 1854 and 13 May 1855. ¶16

Birth fields entered (13 Oct 2026, revised by the auditor): his birth date is entered as 'ABT 1854' and the place as Atmagea, marked '- probable'. This supersedes the earlier note that both should be kept out of the fields. The date field rests on a primary record of the man himself: his stated age, 54, as a witness at Lissewo on 13 May 1909 (marriages 1909 no. 6), which brackets his birth to 14 May 1854 - 13 May 1855. The exact date 24 Aug 1854, which falls in that bracket, is deliberately kept out of the date field and recorded here only: it comes from the 1940 EWZ index of his (probable) daughter Wilhelmine Frank's file (EWZ51-B048 frame 2286, 'father Wilhelm Schmidt b. 24 Aug 1854 Atmagea'), and Wilhelmine's link to Marie Adam rests on the compiled Ciucurova family book, now corroborated by primary records (Friedrich b. 1886 at Ciucurova, Rosa b. 1890 at Atmagea) but not replaced by them. The place Atmagea likewise comes from that index and keeps its '- probable' marker. Enter the exact date when his death act, Wilhelmine Frank's full EWZ file, or another record naming him and his wife gives it. ¶18

1913: in the marriage act of his daughter Rosa Therese (Standesamt Pfeilsdorf, marriages 1913 no. 15, 9 Nov 1913; image read) he is 'der Ansiedler Wilhelm Schmidt', a settler living with his wife Marie née Adam at Bilau, Kreis Culm, West Prussia. Rosa was born at Atmagea in Romania on 30 June 1890. He had therefore left Romania, where the 1906 death act of his daughter Anna places him at 'Kurtbelli' (reading uncertain), and settled in West Prussia between summer 1906 and May 1909 (see the 1909 note below). Bilau is Bielawy, gmina Lisewo, powiat chełmiński, a former manor district in Amtsbezirk Lissewo that bordered Pfeilsdorf's neighbour Hochdorf, a few km from his daughter Karolina's family at Pfeilsdorf (territorial.de, Amtsbezirk Lissen, as reported). 'Ansiedler' at this date usually means a settler on land of the Prussian Settlement Commission (Ansiedlungskommission). ¶14

How sure are we?

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Identification, upgraded from Speculative to Probable (13 Oct 2026), moved by the Standesamt Pfeilsdorf death register, 1906 no. 23 (Polish certified copy of 1949, AP Toruń 69/1205/0/2.3/1013, scan 221): the act gives his wife's name, Anna Maria née Adam. The Ciucurova village family book, a compiled work and so a lead, citing the Heimatortskartei card series Cogealia, has a Wilhelm Schmidt married to Maria Adam as the parents of Wilhelmine Schmidt, born 9 Jan 1885 at Ciucurova. The 1940 resettlement (EWZ) index of Wilhelmine's own file, an index of a primary declaration, gives her father as Wilhelm Schmidt born 24 Aug 1854 at Atmagea. A Wilhelm Schmidt with a wife (Anna) Maria Adam, in the Romanian Dobruja, with daughters born 1881, 1885 and 1888, is a close fit in a small Evangelical population, and no other Wilhelm Schmidt x Adam couple is known. It is not Confirmed, because the link from his wife's name to Wilhelmine rests on the compiled family book, and no record yet names Karolina or Anna together with the Atmagea birth date. ¶9

The case for this identification 4

Identification status (Speculative): what is Confirmed about this person is only that Karolina's 1974 BC death registration names her father 'Willem Schmidt'. His identification with Wilhelm Schmidt born 24 Aug 1854 at Atmagea, husband of Maria Adam, rests on matching names (Wilhelm, and a wife Maria against Geni's 'Anna Maria'), the right age and confession, and the Atmagea-Ciucurova-Cogealia colony cluster, which fits the family's Dobruja origin and the memory 'Cogelea'. The birth date 24 Aug 1854 and place Atmagea are therefore kept in these notes and deliberately not entered as his birth fields, because entering them on a Confirmed person would present a guess as fact. Enter them only when a record names Karolina as this Wilhelm's daughter. ¶4

Identification, cautions: (1) Anna's birthplace in 1888 (read 'Tulcke', earlier 'S?li?ke'; possibly Tulcea, unproved) and his residence in 1906 ('Kurtbelli', unidentified) are not Ciucurova or Cogealia, where the family-book family is placed in 1885 and 1899; Dobruja colonists moved often, so this is not a contradiction, but both place readings are uncertain and the places should be identified. (2) The family book gives this Wilhelm only as 'born before 1867', a compiler's estimate; the 1854 date comes from the EWZ index of his daughter Wilhelmine. (3) The rival Wilhelm Schmidts listed earlier (born 1833 at Katzbach, wife Karoline Liebelt; and the husband of Karoline Siebert) are now unlikely, since neither is known to have had a wife Anna Maria Adam; they remain possible only if he married twice. ¶10

Identification, reassessed after the 1913 act (13 Oct 2026; still Probable, but strengthened): the earlier step from the couple named in 1906 (Wilhelm Schmidt x Anna Maria Adam) to the Wilhelm born 24 Aug 1854 at Atmagea ran only through the compiled Ciucurova village family book (Wilhelm x Maria Adam as parents of Wilhelmine, b. 1885 at Ciucurova) and the EWZ index of Wilhelmine's file (father Wilhelm b. 24 Aug 1854 Atmagea). A primary record now places the couple named in 1906 at Atmagea itself in 1890, independently of the family book. The family's known places now form one cluster in the Tulcea district: Atmagea (Wilhelm b. 1854; in his mother's household there in 1879; daughter Rosa b. there 1890), Ciucurova, the neighbouring colony (Wilhelmine b. 1885), and 'Tulcke' (Anna b. 1888, possibly Tulcea). The rival Wilhelm Schmidts (b. 1833 at Katzbach with wife Karoline Liebelt; the husband of Karoline Siebert) had other wives. What still keeps it short of Confirmed: no primary record yet gives the birth date or parents of the Wilhelm who married Marie Adam; the 24 Aug 1854 date comes from an index entry in his daughter Wilhelmine's file, and Wilhelmine's link to Marie Adam rests on the compiled family book (now corroborated by the 1913 act, but not replaced by it). ¶15

Identification, after the 1909 act (13 Oct 2026; Probable, close to proved): two facts in the 1909 act agree independently with the Atmagea identification. (1) His age, 54 in May 1909 (as the witness who is taken to be him), fits the birth date 24 Aug 1854 given in the EWZ index of his daughter Wilhelmine's file; this is the first record of his own age. (2) His son Friedrich was born at Ciucurova in Nov 1886, the colony where the compiled Ciucurova village family book places Wilhelm Schmidt x Maria Adam with their daughter Wilhelmine in Jan 1885; the family-book couple is therefore this couple on the evidence of a primary record, not only the family book. With Rosa's birth at Atmagea in 1890 (1913 act), every primary record now fits the Wilhelm Schmidt born 24 Aug 1854 at Atmagea, and no rival fits. It is still not Confirmed, because no record read gives his own birthplace or parents, and the exact date comes from an index of his daughter's file. His death act (Lissewo deaths from 1911 on are not scanned) or Wilhelmine Frank's full EWZ file would confirm it. ¶17

Corrections 1

Correction: the earlier notes on this record that call his identification with the Atmagea Wilhelm 'Speculative', and that say Wilhelm's wife is not named in any source, are superseded by the 1906 Pfeilsdorf death act of his daughter Anna, which names his wife as Anna Maria née Adam. The identification is now Probable. ¶12

Alternatives considered and set aside 6

Candidate identification (Speculative, not proved): Wilhelm Schmidt, born 24 Aug 1854 at Atmagea in the Dobruja (EWZ 1940 index, film EWZ51-B048 frame 2286). He was the son of the widow Luise Schmidt (born 1832) and very probably of Michel Schmidt, who headed the household in the Atmagea grain levy list of February 1877. In the Atmagea soul list of June 1879 Wilhelm is unmarried in his mother's house, with his brother Karl (born 1853) and sister Karoline (born 1860). ¶2

Candidate, continued: by Maria née Adam he had a daughter Wilhelmine, born at Ciucurova on 9 Jan 1885. She was confirmed at Constanța in 1899, then living at Cogealia (Lumina), married Daniel Frank there in 1905, and died at Brackenheim, Württemberg in 1954. His brother Karl Schmidt (1853-1940) and Karl's wife Juliana Ponto also lived at Cogealia. Geni's 'Anna Maria' as Karolina's mother fits 'Maria Adam'. Another Wilhelm Schmidt in the same district (born 1833 at Katzbach, Bessarabia, died 1917 at Cataloi; wife Karoline Liebelt) is a weaker candidate, because his wife's name does not match and he already had a daughter named Karoline. ¶3

Rival candidates: (1) Wilhelm Schmidt born 22 Mar 1833 at Katzbach, Bessarabia, died 1917 at Cataloi, married to Karoline Liebelt, with children in the Atmagea-Cataloi-Ciucurova area 1859-1884 and a gap 1879-84 (Ciucurova village family book I10828); weaker, because his wife was not a Maria and he already had a daughter Karoline. (2) Wilhelm Schmidt married to Karoline Siebert, whose son Johann was born 7 Jan 1867 at 'Fardan, Walachei' (St Petersburg Consistory extracts, film 1884091/3 p.221 no.59); weaker, because his wife was not a Maria and his later whereabouts are unknown. (3) A Schmidt of the colony Fachria, whose families lived at Cogealac 1877-84 (Traeger 1922 pp.85-86); no Wilhelm is named. Karolina's 1931 Saskatchewan census entry says both her parents were born in Rumania, which fits an Atmagea-born father (the Dobruja became Romanian in 1878) better than a Bessarabia-born one, though such census answers are often loose. Common names make all four possible on present evidence. ¶5

Birth fields: the birth date 24 Aug 1854 and place Atmagea are still deliberately kept in the notes and not entered as his birth fields, because this record is Confirmed only as Karolina's named father and the Atmagea identification is now Probable, not proved. Enter them when a primary record ties Karolina, Anna or Anna Maria Adam's husband to the man born at Atmagea in 1854, for example Wilhelmine Frank's full EWZ file naming her mother, or the Constanța church book. ¶11

Possible cousin marriage (a lead, not entered as a link): the bride of his son Friedrich in 1909, Anna Carolina Schmidt, born 25 Mar 1888 at 'Cogeali' (Cogealia, now Lumina) in Romania, was the daughter of the farmer (Landwirt) Karl Schmidt and his wife Julianna, living at Cogealia (Lissewo marriages 1909 no. 6). Wilhelm's (probable) brother Karl Schmidt, born 23 Sep 1853 at Atmagea, died 1940 at Cogealia (EWZ index), also lived at Cogealia. If the bride's father was that Karl, the couple were first cousins; the act does not say so. The bride's mother's maiden name is written clearly as 'Bandau' in the 1909 act. For Karl's wife Juliana (b. 2 May 1859) the 1940 EWZ index gives 'Bondo' in three entries (EWZ51-B049 frame 0408, Karl's own; EWZ58-D020 frames 2300 and 2370) and 'Ponto' in two (EWZ58-D020 2278; EWZ51-A084 2436), and the compiled Ciucurova family book gives 'Ponto'. 'Bandau' and 'Bondo' sound alike, and Ponto/Bondo differ only in the softening of p/b and t/d, so the earlier apparent conflict is most likely spelling variation of one surname; the first-cousin reading is strengthened and probable. It still rests on the match of names and place, and on Wilhelm and Karl being brothers (an inference from the 1879 Atmagea household). Karl is not in the tree, so he is not entered as a person and the bride is not linked to him. Karl's EWZ file (EWZ51-B049 frame 0408; EWZ58-D020 frames 2278, 2300, 2370) or the Cogealia Heimatortskartei card would decide it. ¶19

Lead, a possible sister: on 2 Nov 1912 Roberta Schmidt, born 9 Apr 1893 at 'Carachule' (reading uncertain) in Romania and living at Pfeilsdorf, married Michael Schlenker of Cogealia at Pfeilsdorf; her mother was Karoline Stürzeberger née Schmidt of 'Widau' (reading uncertain), Kreis Gnesen, and no father is named, so Roberta was probably born before her mother's marriage. One of the two witnesses was Wilhelm's son-in-law, the settler Gottlieb Knodel, 33 (Pfeilsdorf marriages 1912 no. 13; image read). Karoline Stürzeberger née Schmidt could be Wilhelm's sister Karoline, born 1860, who was in the Atmagea household of the widow Luise Schmidt in June 1879. That is unproved: Schmidt is a very common name. The Stürzeberger marriage in Kreis Gnesen, or Roberta's baptism, would test it. ¶21

Lead, Tschukurow (Ciucurova): the chronicle of the colony of Tschukurow by Johann Adam (Jahrbuch der Dobrudschadeutschen 1972, pp. 29-35) lists a 'W. Schmidt' among the families there by 1877, and a 'W. Schmidt (Schmied)', a blacksmith, among the families who came by 1880 (p. 33). No first name or birth year is printed. He is a plausible match for this Wilhelm, a master blacksmith in 1906, whose children were born at Ciucurova in 1885 and 1886, but the chronicle does not identify him, so this is a lead only. ¶25
