About the family

Father of Anne, baptised Cantate Sunday (22 Apr 1742) in Gjøvdal church: 'Item Halvor Olsön Omlands barn Anne', godparents Vraald Giermundsön Omland, Tergie Gundersön fra S…tterødal(?), Tergie Gundersön Omland, Tergie Omland's wife and Signe Tergiersdatter Omland (Åmli ministerialbok 1741-1775, 1742 no. 13, IIIF canvas 3). Identified as the father of Anne Halvorsdatter (Anne Halvorsdatter Omland, betrothed 1775, aged 59 in 1801) — Probable: the only Anne of a Halvor at Omland found in 1741-1744. ¶1

Life story

Halvor Olsen Øy in mid-1741: godfather 'Halvor Olsön Öy' at the baptism of Asbor, child of Haaver[?] Tellefsen Øy, Dom. 4 p. Trin. (18 Jun) 1741, Åmli church (Åmli 1741-1775, 1741 no. 33, canvas 0). So the Halvor Olsen Øy who married Anne Olsdatter on 30 Jun 1739 was still at Øy ten months before 'Halvor Olsön Omland' baptised Anne in Gjøvdal (22 Apr 1742). No other Halvor Olsen in 9921 canvases 0-3 (Jun 1741 - Jun 1742). Identity Øy = Omland rests on name and timing only. ¶6

A 'Halvor Omland' stood godparent in Gjøvdal in Nov 1742 and 1743 (same book). ¶2

Asbor is therefore a candidate mother for his daughter Anne (bapt. 1742), but the roll does not prove it: Halvor may have married more than once, and none of the children listed in 1762 is named Anne (she may have married or left by then). ¶5

'Halvor Oneland' stood godfather in Gjøvdal in Nov 1750 to Jørgen Olsen and Sigri Bjørnsdatter Oneland's daughter Gunild (canvas 32). No further child of his was baptised Mar 1745 - Nov 1756. ¶3a

1762 extra-tax roll, Gjøvdal sogn, farm 505 Omland, canvas 161: 'Halvor og H: Asbor' (Halvor and his wife Asbor), with children over 12 Asl[?]d and Torgier, a boy over 12 of the same name Asl[?]d, and girls Ambor and Siri; 7 persons. He is the only Halvor on 505 Omland. The roll gives given names only, so the identification with Halvor Olsen Omland rests on the farm and the given name (Probable). This gives his wife's given name as Asbor; her patronymic is unknown. Note that the roll has 501 Oeland (Oland) and 505 Omland as two separate farms. ¶4

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 record that disagrees · 2 open questions

Records that disagree 1

Contradiction: Åmli ætt og heim II (Gjøvdal), canvas 190, has no Halvor Olsen on Omland; it gives Anne (b. 1741, m. 1775 Herjus Olson Gangsei) to 'Halvor Aslakson Eppeland' (1711-1789), married 1734 to Asborg (1721-1799), and he is the 'Halvor og H: Asbor' of the 1762 roll. The baptism of 22 Apr 1742 says 'Halvor Olsön Omland'. If the bygdebok is right, this person is Halvor Aslaksen and his patronymic in the baptism is a slip; if not, the book has merged two men. ¶7a

Open questions and the next record to read 2

Lead only: a Halvor Olsen of Jørundland had a daughter Anne baptised Lætare 1722 (Åmli 1711-1740, 9920 canvas 30) — possibly the same man earlier in life (then Anne b. 1742 would be a second Anne); unproved. ¶3b

Next records: the Gjøvdal/Åmli marriage of 1734 (Åmli ministerialbok 1711-1740, source 9920) and burials 1789 and 1799 (Åmli 1775-1813), and any Nedenes probate after Halvor Omland 1789. ¶7b
