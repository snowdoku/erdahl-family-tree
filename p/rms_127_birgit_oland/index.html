About the family

NEW ANCESTOR, graded on her link to her daughter Guro Evindsdatter (see her page). Åmli ætt og heim II, Oland L.nr. 62 (canvas 264): Eivind Gunnarson Oland (1594-1650) 'g m Birgit'; Guri (Guro), who married Jørgen Olson Oland, is listed among their children. After Eivind's death Birgit married Olav Eivindson of Oland (L.nr. 60, canvas 268), and the book says she was 'truleg dotter av Knut' (her father not proved). Her patronymic is left blank. 4 of the children of the second marriage owned land in 1670, so the book thinks Birgit was dead by then. Bygdebok only (compiled secondary source, text rebuilt from word boxes, dates ABT), so Probable at most. ¶1a

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
