About the family

Parents' ages (answering the validator's 63-year father gap): her father Tosten was born in Aug 1840 and was 63 when she was born; her mother Gunhild (b. 1862) was about 41. The census evidence supports this: in 1900 Tosten (59) and Gunhild (38) had five children at home born 1891-1898, the youngest in Apr 1898 when Tosten was 57, so a child in 1903 is in line with the family. Her WA death record (father's surname Olson, mother's maiden name Hansen = Gunhild Hansdatter) and the West Bank, Williams County setting of both the 1920 household of 'Tosten O' and 'Sunhil' Olson and her own 1930 household with her Olson brothers fit the same family. Her own 1920 row is not yet found. ¶7

Life story

Obituary index: a notice for 'Clara Larsen Paulsen', birth date given as 2 Dec 1903, relatives Albert and Thelmer, was published in Bellingham, Washington on 16 Aug 1988, two days after her death. Further notices for 'Clara Paulsen' (birth about 1904, relatives James, Albert, Clarice) were published there on 17 and 18 Aug 1988. The index's day and month of birth (2 Dec) differ from the tree's 2 Sep 1903; the newspaper itself has not been read. U.S., Newspapers.com Obituary Index. ¶9

1920 census, found (image read): 'Clara O. Langerude', daughter, 19, single, born North Dakota, in the household of her father Tosten Langerude (Olson) and mother 'Sunhil' (Gunhild) in ED 250, Williams County, North Dakota, with her brothers Ole T., Samuel, Carl O., Alfred O. and Thomas O. and sister Inga T. This places her as a daughter in Tosten and Gunhild's household. Her age of 19 implies about 1900, three years earlier than her birth on 2 Sep 1903 given by later records; the younger children's ages on this sheet appear inflated generally (Alfred, 20, is absent from the June 1900 household). This supersedes the note that her 1920 row had not been found; the Orthell servant 'Clara J Olson' (S0117) is not her. ¶10

Married (1) Albert Mandius Larsen 1921; (2) Einar Amandus Paulsen (1895-1963). ¶1

1930 census (index only, image not read): Clara Larson, wife, born about 1903 in North Dakota, in the household of Albert M Larson, West Bank Township, Williams County, North Dakota, with five children and four Olson brothers-in-law of the head (Sam, Ole T, Carl and Tom) — her brothers, three of whom appear with Tosten and Gunhild in 1900 (Sam, Carl) and 1920 (Ole T). ¶5

1950 census: 'Larsen, Mrs. Clara', head of household, white, female, 46, widowed, born North Dakota, in Whatcom County, Washington (ED 37-28, sheet 11), with six of her children at home. The census does not name her late husband; the identification rests on her name, age, birthplace, widowhood and her children's first names, which match those of Clarice's siblings. Transcribed from the sheet image. ¶8

Second marriage: the Whatcom County Auditor's marriage index records Einar A Paulsen and Clara Larsen, married 24 Oct 1953 and filed 9 Dec 1953 (document nwwtcmcv54_88). She was then the widow of Albert Mandius Larsen (d. 1945). ¶4

U.S. death record: the Washington Department of Health death-certificate index lists Clara L. Paulsen, died 14 Aug 1988 in Whatcom County, residence Whatcom County, age 84, female, father's surname Olson, mother's maiden name Hansen (certificate DE_1988021747; a duplicate entry is in the state death index). The age fits her birth on 2 Sep 1903. The death city is blank in the index; Bellingham in this tree comes from Find a Grave. ¶2

Mother's maiden name 'Hansen': this is consistent with her mother being gunhild. Gunhild was baptised Gunild Hansdatter (daughter of Hans Thorjussen) in Nissedal in 1862 and left Kristiansand on 25 Apr 1884 as 'Gunhild Hansen'. Hansen is her patronymic used as a surname in America; 'Underberg' is a later farm-derived name from the WPA biography. The record therefore supports, rather than contradicts, the link to gunhild. ¶3
