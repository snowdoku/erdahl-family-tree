Life story

John William Nelson's 1958 obituary names his parents as Peter and Ellen Nelson of Mount Valley Township, so the identity is confirmed. The dates and the middle initial come from Find a Grave, whose family links tie him to John William, Gust S. and Malinda; those are probable. ¶1

Find a Grave marks him as a veteran and the grave photo shows a GAR star. He may be the Norwegian-born Peter Nelson, age 29, of Norway Township, Fillmore Co., Minnesota, drafted into the 2nd Minnesota Infantry on 31 May 1864 and discharged 11 Jul 1865 (Vesterheim Civil War database). His son Gust was born in Fillmore Co. in 1864. This link is speculative. ¶2

Norwegian origin (Probable): he is probably Peer (later Peder Nielsen), born 22 Feb 1833 at Nordre Sunde in Madla annex parish, Håland prestegjeld, Stavanger amt (now Stavanger/Sola, Rogaland), and baptised 3 Mar 1833 in Madla church, son of the cottager Nils (Niels) Thorsen and Malene Nielsdatter. The baptism (Håland ministerialbok 1815-1845, pp. 324-325, no. 20) is on a re-used printed burial form, so the index wrongly shows the child as female; the scan clearly reads a boy, Peer, born 22 February. ¶28

The IAGenWeb list 'Civil War Veterans Who Lived in or near Winnebago County' gives 'Nelson, Peter S.', born 23 Feb 1834 and died 20 Jan 1898. It lists him as a Private in Co. H, 2nd Minnesota Infantry, entering service 31 May 1864 and mustered out at Louisville, Ky., 11 Jul 1865, buried 'Oakwood' (the list's name for Forest City's Oakland cemetery). An IAGenWeb overview also names Peter S. Nelson among Civil War veterans buried in Forest City cemeteries in 1908. The list's 20 Jan 1898 was a Thursday, but the newspaper says he died on a Sunday, which points to 23 Jan, 30 Jan or 6 Feb 1898. The death date is therefore entered as a range. ¶13

Find a Grave also lists later wives Kari Monson Nelson (1841-1896) and Bodil Christine Anderson Nelson (1851-1922, married 1896). These are unverified. ¶3

Find a Grave's bio for his second wife Kari Monsdatter (b. 19 May 1841, daughter of Mons Nielsen and Anne Mathiasdatter) says she married 'Peter S. Nilsen' on 23 May 1878 at Winnebago Lutheran Church. That is consistent with the church book form 'Peder Nilsen'. ¶10

He was confirmed in Madla church on 3 Oct 1847 as 'Peder Nielsen' of Nordre Sunde, son of Niels Thorsen, huusmand (cottager), and Malene Neelsdatter, with the same baptism date (Madla ministerialbok 1842-1853, fol. 103, boys no. 15). ¶29

No Norwegian origin has been found yet. Several searches came up empty: Digitalarkivet's Rushford (Fillmore Co., MN) church book 1856-1896 (no Peder Nilsen marriage in 1863 and no Gustav baptism in 1864); the Minnesota Official Marriage System for 1860-1866 (no Peter or Peder Nelson/Nilsen marriage in Fillmore County, which may not be covered for that period); the 1884 county history Mount Valley chapter (no Nelson); and the Oakland Cemetery transcription. The cemetery transcription only confirms Block OW, Lot 19, with Peter S. Nelson as lot owner, Ellen K. as his '1st wife' and Kari as his '2nd wife'. ¶7

Residences in order, from the sources above: Norway to 1857; Quebec 1857; Fillmore County, Minnesota, about 1857-1866 (son Gust born there 1864; drafted from Norway Township 1864); Center Township, Winnebago County, Iowa, from 1866 (1870 census indexed as Iowa Township); section 5, Ellington Township, Hancock County, Iowa, from 1878 (1880 census, 1884 sketch); Winnebago County again by 1895; Mount Valley Township at his death in 1898. ¶23

The Madla register of people moving out (udflyttede) for 1857, no. 7, records Peder Nielsen of N[ordre] Sunde, aged 24, leaving in spring 1857 'til Amerika' (for America) on a certificate dated 11 Apr 1857, taking 30 speciedaler by his own statement. Two other Madla men (Christian Gabrielsen, 28, and Svend Andersen of Søndre Sunde, 29½) left for America on certificates of early April 1857 on the same page (Madla ministerialbok 1854-1870, fol. 266). ¶30

Superseded (13 Oct 2026): earlier notes on this record saying his Norwegian origin was not traced, or that he could not be found in Digitalarkivet, are superseded by the identification above with Peder Nielsen of Nordre Sunde, Håland (Madla church), Rogaland (Probable; audited). ¶35

The Winnebago church book (baptisms 1864-1891, transcribed on Digitalarkivet) has no baptisms for John William (1870) or Malinda (1873) under Peder Nilsen. The baptisms may have been recorded in another congregation, or not transcribed. ¶6

The 1864 draftee Peter Nelson (age 29, Norway Twp., Fillmore Co., MN, 2nd Minnesota Infantry) is still only a speculative match. Vesterheim gives no Norwegian place of birth for him. ¶9

His Norwegian origin (parents, parish, and what the 'S' stands for, perhaps a patronymic) has not been traced yet. The next step is the 1870 census and his Civil War pension file, which may give a birthplace. ¶4

Open leads: the 1870 and 1880 US censuses; his Civil War pension file (NARA), which may name a birthplace; the 1865 Minnesota state census for Fillmore County; his and Ellen's original 1863 marriage record from Fillmore County; and Gust S. Nelson's death certificate (1953), which may name Ellen's maiden name. ¶11

1870 census (index only; the image has not been read): Iowa Township, Winnebago County, Iowa, page 7, household 44: Peter S Nelson 35, born Norway; Caroline 34, born Norway; Nicholis 8, born Minnesota; Gustave 6, born Minnesota; Andrew 4, born Iowa; Thomas M 2, born Iowa. Ancestry's index of the same household gives Peter S born about 1835 and Caroline about 1836. John William is absent because he was born on 8 Sep 1870, after census day (1 June). The 1884 sketch puts the family in Center Township; the township name in the index is Iowa Township, and the image should be read to settle where the 1870 enumeration district lay. ¶20

Winnebago Norwegian Lutheran marriage register, pp. 154-155, 1878 no. 6 (running no. 80), read on the image by the signed-in lane (1 Nov 2026): groom 'Peder S. Nilsen', 45; his parents 'Nils Sunde' and 'Maline Nilsdatter'; married 23 Mai 1878 in Winnebago church ('do'); witnesses 'O. Gudmundsen' and 'Mathias Monsen'; the last column (Udvandringssted, place emigrated from) reads 'Haalands Præstegjeld Norge'. Bride 'Kari Monsdatter', 36, parents 'Mons Nilsen' and 'Anne Mathiasdatter'. This is our man: the Iowa GAR card for Peter S. Nelson (Co. H, 2nd Minn. Inf., d. Forest City 1898) gives his 2nd wife as 'Kari Monson m. My 23, 1878', the same date and bride. This is the first American record to name his parents and his Norwegian parish. It makes the 'S.' = Sunde (the farm) and agrees with Peer, b. 22 Feb 1833 at Nordre Sunde, Madla annex of Haaland prestegjeld, son of Niels Thorsen and Malene Nielsdatter, who left Madla for America in April 1857. ¶37

Iowa, Military Discharge Records (FS ark:/61903/1:1:6ZF8-969R; DGS 007897301 image 24): 'Peder S Nelson', 44, discharge recorded 1878 at Forest City. Image restricted to FamilySearch centers and affiliate libraries; a recorded Civil War discharge usually states 'born in ___', so it may repeat his birthplace. ¶39

1880 census (index only): Ellington Township, Hancock County, Iowa, ED 108, sheet 291C: Peter Nelson 46, born Norway; wife Carrie 39, born Norway; sons Nicholas 18, Gust 16 (born Minnesota), Andrew 13, Thomas 10, William 9; daughters Anna 13 (born Wisconsin) and Malinda 7. Carrie is his second wife Kari Monsdatter (born 1841, married 23 May 1878). Anna, born in Wisconsin, is probably her daughter from an earlier marriage (an inference; she is not one of the six children the 1884 sketch gives to Ellen). William is John William. The township and the section-5 farm agree with the 1884 sketch. ¶21

Biography (1884): The History of Kossuth, Hancock and Winnebago Counties (1884), in its Hancock County chapter under Ellington Township, prints a sketch of Peter S. Nelson. It says he was a native of Norway, born 23 Feb 1833, who emigrated to America in 1857, landing at Quebec, Canada, and went from there to Fillmore County, Minnesota. In 1864 he enlisted in Company H, 2nd Minnesota Volunteer Infantry, and served until July 1865, taking part in the siege of Atlanta, Georgia. He went back to his home in Minnesota until 1866, then moved to Center Township, Winnebago County, Iowa. In 1878 he moved to section 5 of Ellington Township, Hancock County, where in 1884 he had 80 acres and carried on mixed farming. He was a member of the Methodist Episcopal Church. A county-history sketch was usually supplied by the subject himself, so it is good evidence for his own story but is not a primary record. ¶16

Church: in 1884 he was a Methodist Episcopal. His family events nonetheless appear in the Norwegian Lutheran congregation at Winnebago: his second marriage, to Kari Monsdatter on 23 May 1878 (per her Find a Grave biography), and the confirmation of son John William on 18 Jul 1886 under the father's name 'Peder Nilsen'. Fillmore County had Norwegian Methodist Episcopal congregations at Newburg (organised 1860) and Preble (History of Fillmore County, 1912), which are untested for him. ¶25

Sons' marriages (index only; images unavailable): the Winnebago County marriage registers name the groom's parents as 'Peder Nelson & Karina Nelson' for Gust (20 May 1889) and 'Peder Nelsen & Karine' for John William (7 Jun 1894). This confirms the father's name Peder. The mother's name is discussed under Ellen K. Nelson. ¶26

The Winnebago Lutheran church book records the confirmation of 'John William' on 18 Jul 1886, born in Winnebago, father 'Peder Nilsen' (no mother listed, since Ellen died in 1874). In Norwegian records Peter S. Nelson was therefore Peder Nilsen, which implies his father was named Nils. What the 'S.' stands for is still unknown. It could be a second patronymic, a farm name, or an initial adopted in America. ¶5

1895 Iowa state census (index only): Peter S Nelson, born about 1834, resident in Winnebago County. Sons Gust S (about 1864) and John W (about 1871) are indexed in Winnebago County the same year. So by 1895 he had moved back from Ellington Township, Hancock County, to Winnebago County, where he died in early 1898 three miles east of Forest City. ¶22

CORRECTION to the death year. He died in early 1898, not 1896. The Lake Mills Graphic of 9 Feb 1898 (p. 1, 'County Hub', reprinted from the Forest City Summit) says 'P. S. Nelson living three miles east of town, died quite suddenly last Sunday morning.' He and his wife had set out for church when he was taken ill, and he died soon after reaching home. A follow-up on 16 Feb 1898 (p. 8) says the Hancock County coroner came because some feared another suicide, but Dr. Hanson called it apoplexy and no inquest was held. The place, three miles east of Forest City, is Mount Valley Township, and 'his wife' would be Bodil (married 1896). Find a Grave's 1896 is wrong. ¶12

Death date settled: the Iowa GAR membership card (typed, 20th-c. compilation) gives 'Ja 30, 1898', Forest City, Ia, burial Oakwood [Oakland] Cem. 30 Jan 1898 was a Sunday, matching the Lake Mills Graphic report that he 'died quite suddenly last Sunday morning' (9 Feb 1898). The earlier range is replaced by 30 Jan 1898. The card also gives birth 'Feb 23, 1833, Norway' and lists a third wife '[?] Carrie Johnson m. Oct 22, 188[1?]' (last digit unclear), which conflicts with Bodil Christine Anderson (m. 1896) and needs checking. ¶38

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 3

Upgraded from SPECULATIVE to PROBABLE: he is the Norwegian-born Peter Nelson, aged 29, of Norway Township, Fillmore Co., Minnesota, who was drafted into the 2nd Minnesota Infantry on 31 May 1864. The county veterans list gives exactly that regiment and those dates for Peter S. Nelson of Forest City. Ages agree (29 in 1864 fits a birth in 1834/35), and his son Gust was born in Fillmore Co. on 8 Mar 1864. Vesterheim gives the company as G or H, and the 1912 History of Fillmore County (p. 146) as a further source. A pension file at NARA should exist (Co. H, 2nd Minn. Inf.) and is the best remaining hope for his Norwegian birthplace. ¶14

Why this is the same man (argument): (1) the 1884 county-history sketch, supplied in his lifetime, gives birth 23 Feb 1833 and emigration in 1857 via Quebec; Peer was born 22 Feb 1833 and left for America on a certificate of 11 Apr 1857. Every 1857 emigrant sailing from Stavanger listed by Norway Heritage went to Quebec: Prindsesse Louise (left 19 Apr, arrived 7 Jun), Talisman (29 Apr, 15 Jun), Leif (Egersund-Stavanger 19 May, 26 Jun) and Hebe (9 Jun, 15 Jul). The ship he took is not known. (2) In America he was 'Peder Nilsen' (Winnebago confirmation of John William, 1886; and 'Peter S. Nilsen' at his 1878 marriage, according to Kari Monsdatter's Find a Grave biography), so his father was a Nils. (3) Naming pattern: his eldest son was Nicholas (Nils, after the paternal grandfather), and a son Thomas M. echoes the name Thor (Peer's grandfather was a Thor, inferred from the patronymic Thorsen, and his eldest brother was Thore). (4) No son of Nils born 1833 in Håland was found in the 1865 census index (a soft negative), while his widowed mother and brother Niels are at Nordre Sunde. (5) A Norwegian-born Peter Nelson, aged 24, farmer, is in the Minnesota territorial census of Sep 1857 for Township 101, Range 8, Fillmore County; that age fits a birth in Feb 1833, and the county and year fit the 1884 sketch (his identity with Peter S. Nelson is probable, not proved). (6) Uniqueness: nationwide Digitalarkivet search found no son of a Nils born on 23 Feb 1833 or 1834, and no other Peder or Peter Nilsen/Nielsen born 1832-35 recorded as emigrating in 1856-58; these indexes do not cover every parish, so this narrows the field but does not exclude a namesake. The one-day difference in the birth date (22 against 23 Feb) is a common kind of discrepancy between a baptism entry and a man's own statement, but it is a difference. The US censuses and veterans list point to 1834, but the 1884 sketch, his own account, says 1833. ¶31

Upgrade (signed-in lane, 2 Nov 2026): graded on his link to John William Nelson. Two primary records name the relationship. (1) John W. Nelson's Iowa death certificate, State File No. 114-58-20610 (Iowa Death Certificates 1958, FS image 615 of 5,010, read on the image): John W. Nelson, died 16 Oct 1958 at the Good Samaritan Home, Mason City, Cerro Gordo Co., usual residence Lake Mills, Winnebago Co.; born 8 Sept 1870 in Winnebago Co., Iowa, aged 88, widowed, retired farmer; father's name 'Peter Nelson'; mother's maiden name 'Caroline' followed by dashes (surname not given); informant his son Vern E. Nelson of Mason City. The birth date and residence are exactly those of the tree's John William. (2) The 1880 US census of Ellington Township, Hancock Co., Iowa (ED 108, sheet 291C; FamilySearch index with labelled relationship fields) lists Peter Nelson, 46, born Norway, head, with 'William Nelson, Son, 9, Iowa' among the children Nicholas, Gust, Andrew, Thomas and Malinda, the household the 1884 county-history sketch describes. These agree with John William's 1886 Winnebago confirmation (father Peder Nilsen), his 1894 marriage register (parents Peder Nelsen and Karine) and his 1925 Iowa census (father Nilson Peter). No rival Peter Nelson with a son John William born 8 Sep 1870 in Winnebago Co. is named in any record. His brother Gust S. Nelson's death certificate (Iowa 1953) likewise names the father 'Peter S. Nelson'. ¶40

The case for this identification 1

Identity of the 1884 sketch: the sketch is our man, not a namesake. It names his wife Ellen Caroline, her death in 1874, and sons Gus and John W.; it gives Company H, 2nd Minnesota, which the IAGenWeb veterans list gives for Peter S. Nelson of Forest City; and it gives Fillmore County, where son Gust was born in 1864. It also settles the company as H (Vesterheim had G or H). ¶17

Records that disagree 1

Birth date conflict: the 1884 sketch gives 23 Feb 1833; the IAGenWeb Winnebago veterans list (and Find a Grave) give 23 Feb 1834. The censuses favour 1834: he was indexed as 35 in June 1870, 46 in June 1880 (exactly right for Feb 1834, one year short for 1833) and 'abt 1834' in the 1895 Iowa census, and the 1864 draft record gives age 29. The birth date is kept as 23 Feb 1834; 1833 is recorded here as the 1884 sketch's reading. The day, 23 February, agrees in both sources. ¶18

Corrections 1

Correction: the marriage year is 1861, not 1863. The 1884 sketch says he married Ellen Caroline Gudman in 1861. The 1863 date in this tree came from Find a Grave. The censuses support 1861: the eldest son Nicholas was 8 in June 1870 and 18 in June 1880, so he was born about 1861-62, in Minnesota according to the 1870 index. The marriage year is therefore entered as 1861. No marriage record has been found; the Minnesota Official Marriage System 1860-1866 and the Rushford church book were searched without result. ¶19

Alternatives considered and set aside 2

Not the same man: a 'Peter Nelson' who lived near Norman for nearly 40 years was killed by a train in Nov 1896 (Lake Mills Graphic, 18 Nov 1896). That man was buried at Lime Creek, not Oakland Cemetery. ¶8

Tested and set aside: in the 1857 Minnesota territorial census (Fillmore Co., T101 R8, p. 13) the Peter Nelson aged 24 shares a household with 'Soren Andersson', 30. One of the two Madla men who left for America with Peder Nielsen in April 1857 was Svend Andersen of Søndre Sunde, aged 29 and a half, which fits the age exactly; but the image reads Soren, and the same enumerator writes 'Swend Nelson' on that page, so the housemate is not shown to be Svend. The only Gabrielson in the township (Gaberiel, 30, with an Illinois-born wife) does not match the other companion, Christian Gabrielsen, 28. Neither result is evidence for or against the identification. ¶36

Searches that found nothing 2

Searches with no result this round: Digitalarkivet transcribed church books for Rushford (Fillmore Co.), Silver Lake (Worth Co.) and Winnebago, Iowa (only Rushford is transcribed for Fillmore; the Peder Nilsen entries there in 1858-1871 belong to other men, with wives Sønøve Larsd., Anne Roarsd. and Karen Olsd.). A Digitalarkivet search for a Peder born 23 Feb 1834 found no son of a Nils. The Forest City Summit archive (forestcity.historyarchives.online) and the Iowa WPA graves registration site (iowawpagraves.org) are behind bot checks and could not be searched. ¶15

Earlier search note: the note saying the 1884 county history has 'no Nelson' referred only to its Mount Valley Township chapter. The same book does carry his biography, in the Hancock County chapter under Ellington Township. ¶27

Open questions and the next record to read 2

What is missing: no American record yet names Madla, Håland or Sunde. The link would be confirmed by his Civil War pension file (Co. H, 2nd Minnesota Infantry, NARA), a Fillmore County congregation register naming his Norwegian parish, or an American record (obituary, church record, naturalization) naming Sunde, Madla or Håland. A ship list cannot help: no passenger list survives for the 1857 Stavanger-Quebec voyages, because Canadian arrival lists were kept only from 1865 (Norway Heritage, Hebe 1857 ship page). The Jæren (Håland) passport protocols for the 1850s are not online. ¶32

Lead, not evidence: the FamilySearch Family Tree attaches his 1870 and 1880 census entries to a person named 'Peter Sunde Nilsen Nelson' (seen while signed in, S0031, 13 Oct 2026). That would make 'S.' the farm name Sunde, which fits Nordre Sunde, but the tree's sources were not checked and a published tree is not proof. Sunde is also a common farm name elsewhere in Norway. ¶34
