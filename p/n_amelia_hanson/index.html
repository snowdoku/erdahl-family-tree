Life story

Probably the 'Emilia M. Hansen' recorded in the Beaver Creek Lutheran congregation about 1902. Her grave marker reads 'Mother'. ¶3

Baptism: The Winnebago, Iowa church book for 1864-1891 records the baptism in 1875 of 'Emilie Martine', child of Peder Hansen and Andrine Eriksd. (Andrea Eriksdatter Dakken). The Digitalarkivet transcription does not give the baptism date or sponsors. Her obituary says she was baptised and confirmed by Rev. J. M. Dahl, the Winnebago pastor. ¶4

1925 Iowa census (image read): as 'Emilia Nilson', wife, 49, in J. W. Nelson's household, Buffalo Township, Winnebago County. The parents' section names her father 'Hanson Peter', born Norway, dead, and her mother 'Erickson Annie' (Andrea/Andrine Erickson), born Norway, dead, married in Norway. This is a further record naming both her parents. ('Dead' for her mother is wrong if the census reflects 1 Jan 1925, since Andrea died 1 Oct 1925.) ¶8

Obituary (Lake Mills Graphic, 18 Jul 1934, p. 5, 'Amelia Martina Hanson'): born 9 Aug 1875 in Mt. Valley, south of Winnebago church, to Mr. and Mrs. Peter Hanson. Baptized and confirmed by Rev. Dahl. She married William Nelson on 7 Jun 1894 and they had six children. Son Victor died in July 1916, and a sister, Mrs. Knudt Reveland, died in 1916. She died after a long illness with cancer. ¶1

Same obituary, survivors: her husband; daughters Wilma (Mrs. Theodore Ramsey), Ada (Mrs. Martin Bang), Florence (Mrs. Otis Tweed) and Ruby (Mrs. Albert Brakke), all of near Joice; son Vern at home; 12 grandchildren; sisters Anne (Mrs. Gust Nelson) of Forest City and Clara. ¶2

Death: She died on 5 July 1934 at the home of her daughter Wilma, Mrs. Theodore Ramsey, after a long illness. The notice does not name the town; the daughters are described as 'all of near Joice', so the death place is inferred as near Joice, Worth County (Lake Mills Graphic, 18 Jul 1934, p. 5). ¶5

Funeral: The funeral was held on 10 July 1934 at Winnebago church. Out-of-town relatives at the funeral were Sumner Hanson of Eau Claire, Wisconsin, Irving Hanson of Driscoll, North Dakota, and Warner Hanson of Litchville, North Dakota (1934 obituary). ¶6
