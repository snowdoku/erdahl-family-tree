Life story

Olga's elder sister by two years. She appears as 'Marie Lovise Tungsæt', born 1888 in Kristiansund ('Kr, sunds By'), daughter, in the 1891 census at 47 Toldbodsmujet, and she emigrated with her mother and the other children from Kristiansund on 2 May 1894 for Minnesota, where the emigrant clerk calculated her birth year as 1891. ¶1

Her life in America has not been traced. She is not on any Find a Grave memorial in the family, she is not among the four children listed on her parents' memorials, and no Tungseth of her dates is buried in Zion Lutheran Cemetery in Skree Township - so she either died before memorials were kept, or, much more likely, married and is buried under a married name. A user-built Geni tree lists her among the siblings as 'Marie Louise Tungset', which corroborates the sibling group but adds no dates. ¶2

1895 Minnesota census: Mary Tungseth, 6, born in Norway, in her father Peter's household in Skree Township, Clay County, between her brother Anton and her sister Olga (p. 6, read from the image). This is the last record of her found. ¶4

The exact next sources: the 1900 and 1905 census households of Peder O. Tungseth in Clay County, Minnesota, which would show whether she was alive and at home; the Zion Lutheran congregation's own register (Skree Township, Clay County) for a confirmation and marriage; and Clay County marriage records about 1906-1915. ¶3

1900 census: she is not in her mother's household in Skree Township on 14 Jun 1900, and she is not among the eight Tungseth entries in the 1900 census index (all in her mother's household except Annie in Hawley). Her mother reported eight children born and seven living; the six at home and Anna (Annie, a servant in Hawley) make seven, so Marie is probably the child who had died, between 1895 and 1900. This is an inference from the census, not a record of death; Minnesota's statewide death index begins only in 1904. The Zion Lutheran (Skree) burial and church records, and the Clay County register of deaths for 1895-1900, would settle it. ¶5

1905 and 1910: not in her parents' household in either census (1905 Minnesota census, Enumeration District 10, sheet III; 1910 census, ED 59, sheet 187A). ¶6
