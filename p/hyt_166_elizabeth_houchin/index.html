About the family

Wife of Rev. James Allen of Boston; mother of 'John of James & Elizabeth Allen', born 29 Feb. 1671/2, and of Jeremiah, born 29 March 1673 (Boston register, vol. 9). Her maiden name Houchin is from the Waldo Genealogy i:118 (compiled); no marriage record was read. Graded Probable on her link to John Allen. ¶1

Father added: Jeremiah Houchin of Boston, tanner (Probable). The Boston town register names her as 'Elizabeth Houchin daughter of Jeremiah Houchin of Boston' at her first marriage, 9 Nov. 1653, to John Endicott, son and heir of Gov. John Endecott (vol. 9); John Endecott Jr.'s will (recorded 24 Feb. 1667/8) names 'my ffather in Lawe Jeremiah Howchin'. Her own grade (link to John Allen) is unchanged: the birth of 'John of James & Elizabeth Allen' names an Elizabeth as his mother, but that this Elizabeth Allen was the widow Elizabeth (Houchin) Endicott rests on compiled sources (Savage via Knoff; Perley, Essex Inst. Hist. Coll. 51:375; Endicott memoir). ¶2

Life story

Death and age: the Granary Burying Ground tomb of Rev. James Allen reads 'Mrs. Elizabeth Allen wife of Mr. James Allen Aged 35 years. Deceased April ye 5 1673' (quoted by Knoff and the Endicott memoir), so she was born about 1637/8. Her first husband John Endecott Jr. died without issue about Feb. 1667/8 and left her the Bishop farm in Salem Village, which Allen sold to Francis Nurse in 1678 (Perley). ¶3

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: graded on her link to John Allen. Her father's will of 7 Apr. 1670 (proved 31 May 1670; attested copy in Suffolk Court Files vol. 36 case 3126, DGS 008293221 image 42, read) names her as 'Elizabeth the wife of the Reverend James Allen' among 'all my Children' and calls 'Mr James Allen' his son-in-law. The Boston register then records 'John of James & Elizabeth Allen' born 29 Feb. 1671/2 (and Jeremiah, 29 Mar. 1673), and the Granary stone reads 'Mrs. Elizabeth Allen wife of Mr. James Allen... Deceased April ye 5 1673'. Continuity: wife of Rev. James Allen in April 1670, mother of his son John 22 months later, dead as his wife in April 1673; no rival wife (Hannah Dummer d. Feb. 1667/8; Sarah married after 1673). The earlier note that the Endicott-widow = Mrs Allen identification rests on compiled sources is superseded: the will states it. Her maiden name is no longer from the Waldo Genealogy alone. ¶4
