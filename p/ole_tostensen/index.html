Life story

Baptized June 1805 at Ål (1771-1806 church book, entry no. 26 of 1805) as Ole. Parents: Tosten Aslesen and Birgit Olsdatter of Medgaarden in Sata. The book is scanned but not indexed; the baptism was read from the scan. ¶1

Married Rangdi Sølvesdatter 2 Jan 1835 at Ål. He was a bachelor of Medgaarden in Liabygda, age 28; she was a girl of Myro, age 21. The record names his father as Tosten Aslesen and hers as Sølve Knudsen. This confirms his parentage. ¶2

Lodger and day labourer (inderst, daglønner) at Langerud (Langerøe) in the 1865 and 1875 censuses. The censuses put his birth year at 1803, and the 1835 marriage age points to about 1806. ¶3

Died 17 Jun 1882 and buried 22 Jun at Ål as 'Markarb. Ole Tostensen', married, born 1805 at Medgaarden, living at Langerø. ¶4
