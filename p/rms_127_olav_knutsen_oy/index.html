About the family

NEW ANCESTOR, graded on his link to his daughter Ziri (Sigrid) Olsdatter (see her page). Åmli ætt og heim II (Gjøvdal), gard nr. 16 (Øy; later Treungen gnr. 49), L.nr. 32 (canvas 59): [Olav] Knutson was owner from 1646 and lived on the farm 'med kona [og] far sin'; user to 1651 and owner of 1 hud to 1656; 'Olav er nemnt i 1680, men 2 år seinare er det skifte etter han.' By his first wife (name unknown; perhaps a Jakobsdotter) 3 children: Birgit (m. Olav Bjørnson Øy), Tone (m. Eilev [?] Homdrom), a third. Summary: 'Olav Knutson. Skifte 1682. g 2. m Ingeborg [?] Engenes. Skifte 1707. Born: Sigrid g 1. m Vrål Bendikson, g 2. m Eivind Eivindson Jørundland; Liv g m Olav Knutson Haugsjå; Gunvor f 1665...; Ingeborg f 1669 m Olav Bendikson.' Sigrid = Ziri: card 5981 (Skp. 2 fol. 148, Jørundland 1704-05) gives Evind Evindsøn's first wife as 'Zire Olsdatter +' with son Bendix Evindsen, and the bygdebok (canvas 72) says Eivind Eivindson's first wife was Sigrid, widow of Vrål Bendikson, with son Bendik. Bygdebok only (compiled secondary source, text rebuilt from word boxes, dates ABT), so Probable at most. ¶1a

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

Searches that found nothing 2

Negative: no 'Øy' block was found in the Østre Råbyggelag probate card index (DA 39942) where Ø-farms are filed: Øines ~16480 (divider 16450), Ørteland 16498-16526, Ørum divider 16529, Østebø divider 16535 and cards 16541-16590, then Aamdal from 16593. So the Gjøvdal Øy farm (now Treungen gnr. 49) is either filed under another spelling or its probates were handled outside Østre Råbyggelag (Nedre Telemark). ¶2a

Negative: the Øvre Telemark (vestfjelske) probate card index (DA 6856, 1602 views) has no Øy card for Treungen or Gjøvdal. The Ø-block runs Ødegaarden (1510-1511), Øde-Vaagsli, Øen (1514 Øen i Tinje 1715; 1515 Øen, Vrådal; 1516 Øen, Hans Jensen lensmand, 1722-34), Østenaa (1517-1521, Kviteseid), Østgaarden, Øverbø (1524-1530), Øverland (1531-1540, including 1538 'Øverland i Treungen i Nissedal' 1717, fol. 275, and 1539 the same farm 1815), then Aa (1541-). So no probate after Olav Knutsen (1682, before the index begins in 1689) or after Ingeborg (1707) at Øy is indexed in either the Østre Råbyggelag (39942) or the Øvre Telemark (6856) card index. ¶3a
