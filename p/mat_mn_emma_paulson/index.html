About the family

Wife of Elias Slind of Mehurin Township. Named as the mother, with her maiden name written out, on the 1919 birth certificate of their daughter Clara Beatrice: 'MOTHER: full maiden name Emma B. Paulson, post office Marietta, W, age at last birthday 32, birthplace S.D., occupation Housework.' Aged 32 in October 1919 she was born about 1887, in South Dakota. ¶1

Wife of Elias Larsen Slind of Selbu, named on his 1942 draft card as 'Emma Slind, Marietta, Minn. Wife'. Born in Grant County, South Dakota, so American-born and a generation into the settlement. ¶4

Life story

Five further Lac qui Parle birth registrations between 1921 and 1933 carry her maiden name Paulson, which is how the whole sibship can be reconstructed from the index alone. ¶2

She is an American-born wife in a township of Norwegian immigrants, and her South Dakota birth is a reminder of how porous the state line was here: Marietta is three miles from it, and the family's church and burial ground, Zoar Lutheran, is on the South Dakota side. ¶3

Death: South Dakota death certificate number 265225, page 394, 2 April 1952, Grant County, as transcribed onto her Find a Grave memorial. ¶5

Burial: Zoar Lutheran Cemetery, lot 22, with her husband — South Dakota state burial register, 'EMMA SLIND 1952, lot 22, block 00'. Rated Probable rather than Confirmed because the photographs on her memorial are general views of the churchyard rather than a legible reading of her own stone; the joint SLIND stone is visible in the background of the Kathryn Hayter photographs but not readable. ¶6
