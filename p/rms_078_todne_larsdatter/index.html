About the family

Widow of Evind Bjørnsen of Ekra (Søndre Askland, Gjøvdal); she handed over the estate at the skifte of 3 Apr 1724 (Østre Råbyggelag Skifteprotokoll nr. 6 fol. 226a; probate card 65 'g.m. Thodne Larsdtr. oppgir boet'). Mother of Bjøne, Tallak, Margith (wife of Tergie Salvesen), Thargier and Margitta. Card 125 (her son Bjøne's 1749 estate) repeats 'mor: Todne Larsdtr., oppgir boet'. Her parents are not named. ¶1

Life story

Sister found. The 16 Mar 1708 Søndre Askland skifte after Stian Madsøn (Skifteprotokoll nr. 3 fol. 45b-46b, read on the scan; card 37) names the widow Siirj Larsdatter. The guardians appointed were 'Evind Biødnesøn [Ekre], som haver Enkens Søster' (who has the widow's sister [as wife]), for the son Lars Stiansøn, and 'Thargier Salvesøn, som haver Enkens Søsterdatter' (who has the widow's sister's daughter [as wife]), for the daughter Thargier Stiansdatter. Evind Bjørnsen's wife was Todne Larsdatter, and Tergie Salvesen's wife was Evind's daughter Margithe. So Todne was a sister of Siirj Larsdatter, and both were daughters of a Lars. Their father's farm and identity are not stated. ¶2

Burial: Åmli register 1711-1740 (DA 9920, canvas 52, p. 99), Anno 1730, 'Fer. 3 Pentec.' (3rd day of Pentecost, 30 May 1730), buried at Gjøvdal: 'Thone Larsdatter Eckra 64 aar gl.' (aged 64, so born about 1666). This is the right name and place: Ekra was her widow's holding after Evind Bjørnsen died in 1724. ¶3
