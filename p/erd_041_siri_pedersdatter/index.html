About the family

Parents and sister (from the continuation, 8 Feb 1765, fol. 249b-250a, view 252): she showed probate letters after her father 'Peder Johannesen(?) Øre' (held at Øre in Indre Sogn, 8 Nov 1727), her sister Kari Pedersdatter (Øre, 10 Nov 1727; Sigri her sole heir) and her mother 'Anna Torbiørnsdatter' (Øre, 30 May 1733), inheriting land in Qvalem, Bariøynes(?) and Rinde. So she was born before Nov 1727 at Øre (Øyri) in Indre Sogn, probably Sogndal/Norum skibrede. The father's patronymic is uncertain in my reading. ¶5

Life story

Marriage: betrothed (sponsalia) in autumn 1751 as 'Siri Peders Daattr Øyre' to 'Marcus Larsøn Eithungierde', sureties (forlovere, marriage guarantors) Lars Dahlen and Anders Hambre; married 27 November 1751 ('Nupt:... item Marcus Larsøn Eitung: Siri Pedersd: Øyre', Leikanger ministerialbok 1735-1756, folios 123-124). She was then of the farm Øyre; her father was named Peder. ¶1

Strengthened, still Probable: the 1758 probate after Lars Marcussen of Eitungjerde (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll A 7b, fol. 408a-408b) names his eldest son as 'Marcus Larsen Thue', so Marcus Thue was the Marcus Larsen of Eitungjerde who married 'Siri Peders Daattr Øyre' on 27 Nov 1751. What is still not proved is that Siri was the wife alive in 1752-61 and the mother of Ingri; the probate does not name Marcus's wife. ¶3

Orchestrator: father and mother added as Probable, not Confirmed, only because the father's patronymic ('Johannesen(?)') is an uncertain reading; the 1765 entry itself states the parent relationship. ¶6a

2 on the grade · 1 open question

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Why Probable as wife of Marcus Thue and mother of Ingri Marcusdatter: hers is the only Marcus marriage in Leikanger April 1748 - December 1751 (all entries read); Marcus Thue's daughter Ingeri was baptised 14 May 1752; his first son, baptised 16 September 1753, was named Peder (her father's name) and had Lars Eitungjerde as godfather (the groom's farm); his later sons were named Lars (the groom's father's name); and Peder's and Marcus jr.'s daughters were named Sigri/Sigrid. No baptism or churching names Marcus Thue's wife (the books give only 'Marcus Thues Qvinde'). Would be settled by a probate after Marcus Thue or Siri, her burial entry, or a census/probate naming her as Marcus Thue's wife. ¶2

Confidence moved Probable to Confirmed as wife of Marcus Larsen Thue and mother of his children. The probate after 'Marcus Larsen' at Thue, 6 Oct 1764 (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll L0008a fol. 249a no. 4, Digitalarkivet 24165 view 251) names his widow 'Sigri Pedersdatter' and their children Peder 11, Johannes 5 (or 8), Lars 4, Marcus 1, Ingerie 11 and Anna 5. Her lauverge was Niels Larsen Thue (relationship not stated). ¶4
