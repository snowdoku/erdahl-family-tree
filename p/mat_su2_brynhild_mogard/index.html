Life story

Named as the mother on the 1959 Colfax, Washington death certificate of her daughter Ingeborg Andersdatter Mogard. The certificate gives her married surname, so her own farm name is unknown. BRYNHILD is a distinctively Selbu name - it recurs across this whole settlement (Brynhild Jonsdatter Langli, Brynhild Kulsetmoen Eidem, Brynhild Hanstad Slind) - and is weak but real corroboration that this family is from the same parish. ¶1

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
