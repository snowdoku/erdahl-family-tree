Life story

Baptism and parents (Probable identification): 'Maria Jacobina Glott', baptised at Enzweihingen (the next parish to Vaihingen, 3 km) on 4 Dec 1719 to 'Joh. Jacob, Anna Maria' (record 4606317, image 1184569-00054). The surname is indexed many ways for this one couple at Enzweihingen: Johann Jacob 'Gloss', 18 Jan 1722, to 'Johann Jacob, Anna Maria' (4606374); Maria Elisabetha 'Bloss', 28 Jan 1723, to 'Jacob, Anna Maria' (4606405); Eva Maria 'Blos', 29 Jan 1724, to 'Johanna[sic], Anna Maria' (4606431); Anna Maria 'Bloss', 20 Apr 1731, to 'Jacob, Anna Maria' (4606656). A sister: Maria Elisabetha 'Gloss', daughter of Jacob, married at Enzweihingen on 7 Feb 1747 (904607782). Other Glossers there: Anna Catharina 'Glosser', daughter of Nicolas, married 1711 (904607515); a 'Glosser' burial 1731 (5160444). She is the only Maria Jacobina of this surname found anywhere (Glosser/Gloser/Gloßer/Closser/Klosser/Glott/Gloss/Bloss checked), she would be 24 at the 1744 Vaihingen marriage, and her father's name matches the 1744 bride row ('Jac.'). Named rival for the father, excluded: a second Jacob Bloss/Bluss of Enzweihingen married Catharina on 21 Oct 1719 (4607568/904607568) and baptised children by Katharina from 1724 (4606456 etc.); a December 1719 child by Anna Maria cannot be his. Probable: the identification crosses parishes and rests on unlabelled index rows. Ancestry 61023 index rows do not label roles (BRIEF audit 5 rule 3), so this rests at Probable at most until the image or a labelled FamilySearch twin is read; images not seen. ¶2

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Graded on that link: the Vaihingen birth of 6 Apr 1759 names 'Jacob Fridrich, Maria Jacobina' (record 4775787; FamilySearch QPGK-LDW2). She is the bride of Jacob Friedrich Rampacher's 1744 Vaihingen marriage: bride row 'Maria Jacobina Glosser', relatives 'Jac., Jacob Friederich' (record 904777928, image 1184562-00542). Whole window: the couple married in 1744 and every indexed child 1749-1759 names the mother Maria Jacobina; no second marriage of Jacob Friedrich is indexed before 1759. The 1759 child's identity with the woman in the tree rests on the 1786 marriage naming her father (see Jacob Friedrich Rampacher). Ancestry 61023 index rows do not label roles (BRIEF audit 5 rule 3), so this rests at Probable until the image or a labelled FamilySearch twin is read; images not seen. Surname 'Glosser' as indexed (perhaps Closser/Klotzer); spelling to be checked on the image. Her own baptism not found. ¶1b

Re-check, grade held at Probable (graded on her link to her daughter Jacobina Friederike, Jacobina Friederike Rampacher). The 1791 'Fessels' contradiction is resolved (see Jacobina Friederike Rampacher and the upgrade of her husband Jacob Friedrich Rampacher), so the tree's Jacobina Friederike is the 1786 bride, daughter of Jacob Friedrich Rampacher. Her mother is named only in the 1759 baptism (QPGK-LDW2: Jacob Fridrich x Maria Jacobina), 27 years before the 1786 marriage, which names only the father. Whole window (audit 5 rule 1): the bride could be born about 1750-1768; Jacob Friedrich's children with Maria Jacobina are indexed 1745-1759 (Johann Jacob 1745, Jacob Friedrich 1749 and 1752, Jacobina Friderica 1759), and a FamilySearch search for him 1745-1800 (signed in, page 1 of 132 results) showed no second marriage; but her burial is not found, so a second wife before 1768 is not excluded. ¶4a

The case for this identification 1

Labelled evidence: her 1744 marriage QPGK-GFW8 labels Wife 'Maria Jacobina Glosser', Father-in-law 'Jac. Glosser', groom's Father 'Erhard Rambachers'. Grade held at Probable for the same reason as her husband (the 1786 bride / 1791 bride chain; see Jacob Friedrich Rampacher).
