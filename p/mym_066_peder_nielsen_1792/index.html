Life story

1801 census: 'Peder Nielssen', 9, one of 'Deres boern' (their children) of Niels Olssen and Guri Olsdatter at Langlie, Selbu, household 01. ¶1

Probate: 'Peder' is a son and heir in the skifte after his father Niels Olsen Langlie, closed 21 June 1816 (skifteprotokoll 10b, fol. 713). ¶2

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Namesake: not the same man as Peder Nielsen of Fallang, Gran, Hadeland (see his page, line 4). That man belongs to an Oppland family; this Peder was a child at Langlie in Selbu in 1801 and an heir there in 1816. ¶3
