About the family

Wife of Christian Hansen at Bjørgesæter in 1801 (aged 48). Probably the Kari Christoffersdatter of Tømmeraas who married Christian Hansen on 2 Nov 1769 in Jevnaker. Her mother-in-law relationship to Walborg Pedersdatter comes from the census ('deres sønnekone'). Whether she was the biological mother of Hans Christiansen is uncertain because of her age. ¶1

Mother of Hans: She is named as the mother at the baptisms of Hans (25 Aug 1771) and Christoffer (21 Aug 1774). That settles the earlier doubt about whether she was Hans's biological mother. Her 1801 census age of 48 is probably understated, since she married in 1769. ¶2

Family of origin (probable): She stood godmother on 19 Jan 1772, as "Kari Xstoffers dtr Song", for Mari, daughter of Ole Christoffersen Tømmeraas and Eli Haraldsdatter. Jacob Christoffersen Tømmeraas was a fellow godparent. Ole Tømmeraas and Jacob Christoffersen were in turn godparents to her son Hans. Ole and Jacob Christoffersen of Tømmeraas were probably her brothers, and her father a Christoffer of Tømmeraas. ¶3

Life story

1762 extra tax checked: the only Hadeland volume, Fogderegnskap Toten, Hadeland og Vardal 1762-1783 (Digitalarkivet source 45523, about 600 views, no IIIF manifest), holds receipts, monthly parish totals per parish (Gran, Jevnager, Toten...) and 1770s voluntary-contribution lists. Views sampled across the whole volume show no household list naming children over 12, so the ekstraskatt cannot be used for her parents. Her baptism must be read on the scan: Jevnaker 1725-1751 (DA 9334) is unindexed for baptisms; year map c30 = Nov 1731-Jan 1732, c45 = Dec 1734-Jan 1735, c62 = Feb-Apr 1739, c66 = Jan-Apr 1740, c69 = Nov 1740-Jan 1741, c70 = Feb-Mar 1741 (about 5 canvases a year), so 1745-51 lies at about c90-c141. A 'Christopher Tömmeraas' and a 'Marthe Tömmeraas' were godparents on 10 Apr 1740 (p. 129, c66), so Christoffer was at Tømmeraas by 1740. ¶6

Death: 'Kari Christofersdtr', aged 82 (b. about 1746), died 21 Mar and was buried 3 Apr 1828 in Jevnaker (Jevnaker ministerialbok 1815-1837, no. 14; indexed twice, pg00000006734056 and pg00000006236973). No farm is given. She is the only Kari Christoffersdatter born 1744-1756 in the Jevnaker burials, and an age of 82 suits a woman who married in 1769 better than her 1801 census age of 48 (which her notes already call understated). Birth estimate left at ABT 1750; the burial suggests about 1746. ¶8

Probate: widow at her husband Christian Hansen's estate, Bjørgesæteren, 1822 (Hadeland og Land skifteprotokoll 1800-1843, fol. 47b; card DA 39947 no. 659), with sons Hans (deceased; children Hans and Marte), Christian and Christopher. ¶5
