Life story

Kgroenha.net no. 930: Ola Andersson, born about 1690, married 1715 Anna Hansdtr. Lunde, died 1760 at Bale. The same page gives his parents as Anders (died about 1690, leased Bale about 1680-90) and Ingrid Palnesdatter. Unsourced and not checked; no matching Ola was found at Bale in the 1701 census. ¶1

Laberg 1985 (Bale, printed p. 60): after holders to about 1680, an Anders had the holding until [about] 1690; his wife was Ingrid, and with her he had a son, Ola. After Anders died his widow remarried and had a daughter Brita (who married a Knut and died the year after). Ola Andersson then got the holding. He was born [about] 1690 and married in 1715 Anna... Lunde. Their known children were Hans, who [stayed] at Lunde, and Gurid, who married Jon Bjørnson from Sande; Jon got the holding on Bale after [Ola], who died in 1760. ¶2

Laberg 1985 (Lunde, printed p. 116) names the wife of Ola Andersson Bale as Brita, daughter of Hans Hansson of Lunde (born about 1638, died 1713), and mother of Hans and of Gurid who married Jon Bjørnson Bale. The book thus gives his wife two names (Anna on p. 60, Brita on p. 116); see Anna Hansdatter Lunde. ¶3

Betrothed (sponsalia) at Tjugum on the 5th Sunday after Epiphany, 10 Feb 1715 (date computed from the church calendar): 'Ole Bale / Ane Lunde } spon' (Leikanger ministerialbok 1690-1734, source 11564, canvas 112 L). No patronymic is written, so the identification with Ola Andersen is probable, resting on the year, the two farms and the bride's name all matching Laberg p. 60. The marriage entry itself was not yet read. ¶7

Married (nuptiae) at Tjugum church on the 1st Sunday after Trinity, 23 Jun 1715 (date computed: Easter 1715 = 21 Apr): 'Dn. I post Trin. / Ved Tiugum. Ole Bale / Ane Lunde } nupt' (Leikanger ministerialbok 1690-1734, source 11564, canvas 115 L). Same couple as the betrothal of 10 Feb 1715 (canvas 112 L). The entry gives no patronymics, fathers or marriage witnesses (forlovere). This verifies Laberg's marriage year 1715. ¶8

Probate of his mother: in the probate after Ingeri Palmesdatter at Balle, 3 May 1720 (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll 3a, fol. 6a-6b), he is heir as '1 Broder Olle Andersøn Balle', her son by her former husband; his half-sister is Britte [Niels?]datter and his late brother was Palme Andersen. The 1701 census lists him at Balle as Olle Andersen, 'Sønn', age 12, under head Johanes Andersen (b. 1639); the probate shows his father was an Anders, so Johannes was not his father (the census term placed him in the household, it did not name his parent). ¶9

Probate (skifte) held 22 Feb 1760 at Bale after 'afg. Dannemand Ole Andersen' between his surviving children and heirs, a son and a daughter: the son Hans Olsen, living at Lunde in Tjugum parish, of age; the daughter Gurj (Guri) Olsdatter, married to Jon Biørnsen at Bale. Each inherited 11 rd 4 ort 12 s. No widow is named and none takes a share, so his wife had died before Feb 1760; the probate does not give her name. ¶5

Confidence raised from Speculative to Probable: Laberg 1985 states his parents, marriage, death and daughter Gurid in two separate farm histories. Bygdebok read by nb.no word boxes, so not above Probable. ¶4a

Confidence raised from Probable to Confirmed on the 1760 probate (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll 7b, fol. 465a, No. 5), a primary record naming him, his farm, his death and his daughter Gurid with her husband Jon Bjørnsen. ¶6
