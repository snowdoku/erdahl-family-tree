About the family

His wife is now confirmed from a primary record. The probate after Marit Magnusdatter Boxaspen at Stokke, 1749 (Nordmøre skifteprotokoll 8c, 24643 fol. 1084b, view 241), names among the deceased's sisters 'Anne Magnusdatter gift med Niels Endresen Stoche'. So Niels Endresen of Stokke was married to Anne Monsdatter (see her page) in 1749. This does not name his children, so his grade on his son Endre Nielsen stays Probable. ¶9

Life story

Bygdebok account: Gards- og ættesoge for Gjemnes, bind 9, Stokke, canvases 213-214: 'Brukar 8: Nils Endreson var brukar frå ca. 1714 til 1760... Nils Endreson var [f.] 1684, d. 1760. Gift 1714 med Anne [Monsdotter] ca. 1682, d. 1756, 82 år gamal. Nils var son til Endre Nilsson [og] Ane Olsdotter Stokke.' Children: Ole 1714 (brukar 9, m. 1744 Ane Olsdotter, d. 1763), Endre 1716 (m. 1748 Ingeborg Andersdotter Grønset, settled at Grønset bnr. 5), Torstein 1719. As early as 1743 Ole got part of the farm from 'faren Nils Endreson'. This strengthens his link to Endre Nielsen, but it is a secondary source, so the grade stays Probable. His death in 1760 falls in the gap of the Nordmøre probates (24645 lacks 1757 to June 1760). Parents and wife added from this account (Probable). ¶8

The 1738 probate card after Jens Mogensen of Vebenstad (77795 view 4367) also names 'fullsösteren Anna Mognsdr. g.m. Niels Endresen Stokke', so the marriage is proved by two primary records, 1738 and 1749. Deed: on 11 Dec 1743 Niels Endresen Stokke (18 marklag), Peder Pedersen Kiøl (7 marklag) and Anne Olsdatter, widow of [..] Knudsen Sandmørvigen (7 marklag), sold their odel land in Stokke, 2 øre 8 marklag in all, to 'velagte unge Karl Ole Nielsen' (Nordmøre panteregister 22675 view 92; pantebok 1b, 20326 view 73, left page, fol. 284b). The deed does NOT call Ole his son and does not mention Endre, so it does not change any grade. It shows that Niels held odel in Stokke jointly with Peder Pedersen Kiøl and Anne Olsdatter, which fits the bygdebok's statement that his mother was an Ane Olsdotter of Stokke; Anne Olsdatter here may be his mother's sister or his own sister. Lead only. The panteregister 22675 views 53-92 (1730-1743) were read for Stokke, Overland, Søvig and Grønset (Gjemnes): besides this deed only a Grønset deed of 19 Jun 1735 (Erich Joensen Folland to Ouden Olsen Grønset, fol. 114b, view 68) and a Stokke deed of 15 Dec 1738 (Svend Ellingsen Dahlen to Elling Johannesen, 1 øre, view 76). Views 93-116 (1744-50) not read. ¶10

Of Stokke, Gjemnes tinglag, Kvernes parish, Nordmøre. In May 1742 he was appointed guardian (formynder) for Kari Størchersdatter in the probate after Størcher Gundersen, who had lived and died at Stokke (Nordmøre probate card index, source 77795, view 3791: 'Til stede: Kari Størchersdr. m. formynder Niels Endresen Stokke'; protocol 24642, 1741-46, fol. 522-523, the end of the entry at view 94 top left: 'Til Formynder for Kari Størchers Daatter... Niels Endresen Stokke'). ¶1

'Endre Nielsen Stokke' stood godfather at Kvernes on 4 p. Pascha 1742 (a Hoksæt baptism, 16075 view 22), at Michaelmas 1743 to Ole, son of Elling [Johansen] Stokke and Ane [Povelsdatter] (view 28), and on 3 p. Trin. 1745 to Niels, son of Ole Nielsen Stokke and Ane (view 37). So Endre was adult and living at Stokke by 1742. Ole Nielsen Stokke (child Niels 1745, Kirsti 1747, wife Ane Olsdatter) and Torsten Nielsen Stokke (godfather 1744 and 1747; head of a Stokke household in 1762) were probably Endre's brothers, sons of Niels Endresen, but no record says so. ¶3

Størcher Gundersen's probate, opening (24642 view 93, fol. 522, 10 May 1742): the heirs of the deceased Størcher Gundersen of Stokke are his kin on the mother's side: Kari Størchersdatter (unmarried), Giertrud [..]datter, and Gunnild Endresdatter and Berit Endresdatter, described as daughters of the deceased's mother's brother; Kari's guardian is Niels Endresen Stokke. So an Endre (Størcher's mother's brother) had daughters Gunnild and Berit Endresdatter, very possibly Niels Endresen's sisters, but Niels himself is not called an heir or a relative there. Lead only. Grade unchanged (Probable). ¶5

Størcher Gundersen's probate re-read (24642 view 93, fol. 522, 10 May 1742): the heirs are Kari Størchersdatter, unmarried; Giertrud Endresdatter, 'den Afdødes Moders Broders Datter', married (husband Hans [B..]sen); Gunnild Endresdatter likewise (husband Gunder [..]sen); and Berit Endresdatter, also a daughter of the deceased's mother's brother, married (husband Arnt Hansen). Niels Endresen Stokke appears only as guardian for the minor Kari. ¶6a

The 1762 extra tax for Stokke (47939 view 56) lists households headed by [Østen?] Nielsen, Iver Østensen, Ole Nielsen, Halvor Gundersen, Jacob Olsen, Paul Ellingsen and Tosten Nielsen: no Niels Endresen, so he had probably died or retired by 1762. ¶4

The probate after the boy Anders Torstensen at Grønset, 18 Oct 1769 (Nordmøre skifteprotokoll nr. 13, 24648, no. 36, fol. 347a, view 361 right page) names his siblings Niels Torstensen (14) with guardian Knud Andersen Skardet (the morbror named in the 1755 probate of Anne Andersdatter Stokke), Knud Torstensen with guardian Ouden Oudensen Grønset, Torsten Torstensen with guardian [Lars?] Knudsen, and Anne Torstensdatter (12). Endre Nielsen Grønset, who lived on the same farm and would be the children's farbror if Torsten Nielsen were his brother, is not appointed for any of them and is not called farbror. This neither proves nor disproves that Torsten and Endre were both sons of Niels Endresen; no relationship word links them. Grade unchanged (Probable). ¶14

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the grade · 1 alternative set aside · 3 searches with no result · 2 open questions

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Graded on his link to his son Endre Nielsen: Probable, inferred. In Oct-Nov 1748 'Endre Nielsen Stokke' was betrothed and married to Ingebor Andersdatter Grønset (Kvernes 16075 view 1, p. 2), so Endre grew up at Stokke; Niels Endresen is the only Niels found at Stokke in these years (the Stokke cards 77795 views 3777-3793, 1677-1744, name no other Niels), six years earlier, and Endre bears the name of Niels's own father Endre, as the naming custom expects of an eldest son. No record yet names Niels as Endre's father, so this stays Probable. ¶2a

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Caution: had Niels been a son of the same Endre (Størcher's mother's brother), he would have shared in the estate with his sisters, so this entry points against him being their brother. The Endre family of this probate is therefore probably not his. Grade unchanged (Probable). ¶6b

Searches that found nothing 3

Negative searches: no probate after Niels Endresen in the registers of Nordmøre skifteprotokoll 10a, 1750-55 (24644, register views 3-9: Niels entries are Rostvold, Jøtøen, Høgset, Berkåk, Langgrøen and Gangnæs) or 10b, 1755-60 (24645, register views 5-7: Rokstangen, Nesset, Schonsøen, Lippervig, Sveggen). The 24645 register notes that probates from 1757 to 22 Jun 1760 are missing from the volume. No burial of a Niels Endresen Stokke was seen in Kvernes 16075 for 1737-41 or 1748-49 (views 4-19, 51-56). ¶7

Negatives: no probate after Niels Endresen Stokke in skifteprotokoll 10c, 1760-1762 (24646, register views 2-5; the N page has only Niels Olsen Hovstad, Niels Olsen Hopet, Niels Nielsen Sandal and others). The Nordmøre panteregister nr. 1 (22675) views 93-116 (1744-1750) have no Stokke deed. A Grønset deed of 25 Jan 1749 (22675 view 111) from Anders Knudsen Grønset to Endre Nielsen, 1 øre 12 marklag, pantebok 1b fol. 384 (DA 20326, about view 173, not read), shows Endre buying into his bride's farm; it is the likely place for a 'Svigersøn' wording, but would not name Endre's own father. Panteregister nr. 2, 1751-1784 (DA 22676), is a first-name index of grantors in several sub-series: view 54 (E, about 1761) has 'Endre Nielsen med flere Skiøde [paa] 1 øre 12 mkl i Grønset til Knud Pedersen Kiøl', pantebok 2 fol. 202 (DA 20327). The co-sellers ('med flere') may be Endre's co-heirs; read that deed next. Its O and T pages (Ole and Torsten Nielsen, about 1760-61) may hold a deed of Niels Endresen's heirs at Stokke, which would settle Endre's parentage. ¶11

Negative for his link to Endre: the probate after Anne Andersdatter of Stokke, wife of his son Torsten Nielsen (24645 no. 54, fol. 328b, 17 Apr 1755, views 58-59), names the widower Torsten Nielsen and the children Niels and Anders Torstensen; the guardian is 'Morbroderen Knud Andersen Skardet'. No Endre Nielsen and no grandfather is named. The panteregister nr. 2 (22676) O pages for pantebok 2 (views 169-174) have no Ole Nielsen Stokke deed; the T page (view 230) has 'Tosten Nielsen Stoche med flere' selling land in Grønset to Knud Pedersen Kiøl at pantebok 2 fol. 202, the same 1761 deed as Endre Nielsen's. That deed is by the heirs of Anders Knudsen Grønset, so Torsten and Endre Nielsen sold as husbands of two Andersdatter sisters (Anne and Ingeborg), not as heirs of Niels Endresen. It shows the two men acting together but names no father. ¶12

Open questions and the next record to read 2

Next: the 1762 extra tax for Stokke (47939 view 56) and the Nordmøre probate registers 24643-24647 for a probate after Niels Endresen Stokke naming a son Endre. ¶2b

Leads: 24648 fol. 347a 'Anders Tostensen Grønset i Gjemnes' (about 1768; probably Torsten Nielsen's son Anders, who would have Endre Nielsen Grønset as farbror) and 24649 fol. 338a 'Arnt Tostensen Stoche' and fol. 343a 'Ingebor Knudsdatter Stoche' (about 1774). A guardian or heir written as 'farbroder Endre Nielsen' would prove that Endre and Torsten were brothers. It would still not name Niels, so it would support but not by itself confirm Niels as Endre's father. ¶13
