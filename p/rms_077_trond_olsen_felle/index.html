Life story

Farmer at vestre Felle, Treungen. Dead before 7 Jul 1711, when the skifte (probate) after him was held by sorenskriver Hans Mor[tensen?] (cited in his widow's 1721 skifte; the 1711 entry itself was not read). On 22 Jul 1721 his widow, 'den gamle og bedagede quinde Gunvor Halvorsdatter', divided her property at Vester Felle among their children: the sons Tore, Alf, Johannes and Halvor Trondsønner, all of age, Jens Trondsen, under age (his guardian, the late Biørn Haug[stvedt?], was replaced by his eldest brother Tore Trondsen), and a daughter Torbiør Trondsdatter. Fixed property 6 settinger in vestre Felle (Øvre Telemark skifteprotokoll no. 3, fol. 456a; card DA 6856 image 244). ¶1

Records that disagree 1

Correction/discrepancy: the 1731 probate of his son Alph Trondsen (protocol no. 4, fol. 335a) says Alph's land in Vestgaarden Felle came 'efter den sal. mands fader afgangne Tron Alphsøn' by the skifte of 7 Jul 1711, and his mother's share by the skifte of 22 Jul 1721. The patronymic there reads Alphsøn, against Olsøn in the 1721 entry and on the card. The 1721 reading (Olsen) is taken here; the 1711 skifte in protocol no. 3 (DA 25692, about fol. 150-200, not located) would settle it. His son Alf may be named after Trond's father, which would favour Alphsøn. ¶2

Searches that found nothing 1

Negative: the skifte after him dated 7 Jul 1711, cited in his widow's 1721 division, is not recorded in the Øvre Telemark protocol no. 3 (DA 25692). The 1710-12 run was read by heading: 12 Sep 1710 at fol. 101b (image 105); 13 Apr 1711 at fol. 105b (image 109); 12 Jun 1711, Aslestad, Fyresdal, at fol. 107a (image 110); then 14 Jul 1711, Medgaarden, at fol. 108a (image 111); Oct 1711 at fol. 109b-110a (image 113); Feb 1712 at fol. 111b-112b. Nothing between 12 Jun and 14 Jul 1711. So the 1711 skifte was probably held privately or by the lensmann and never copied into the court book, and the Olsen/Alfsen patronymic conflict stays open. The original skiftebrev, if it survives, would be among Felle farm papers, not on Digitalarkivet. ¶3
