Life story

Emigration: As 'Wilhelm Knodel', farmer, single, he landed at Quebec on 26 July 1928 from the Canadian Pacific liner Montrose, out of Southampton, four days after his parents. His destination is indexed as 'Estonia, Sask.', a misreading of Eatonia. Keyword tests of the index entry give last residence Płużnica and a contact recorded as 'uncle Simon' (surname not matched; probably Simon Eberle of Eatonia, who had emigrated from Pfeilsdorf in 1910). ¶8

Emigration: the passenger-list index (Ancestry 'Canada, Incoming Passenger Lists, 1865-1935') has Wilhelm Knodel, b. abt 1904, arriving 26 July 1928, four days after his parents, destination 'Estonia, Sask.' [an indexer's misreading of Eatonia]. Keyword tests on the index match Quebec, Southampton, Polish, Farmer and 'Scotland' (probably the Canadian Pacific Empress of Scotland - Probable only). Image not seen. ¶9

1931 census: William Knodel, 27, single, born Rumania, immigrated 1928, farm labourer on his father's farm (unpaid), in his parents' household on NW 27-25-24-W3, RM of Newcombe No. 260, Saskatchewan. He spoke English. ¶7

Known as William or Bill. Marriage, BC 1944-09-580138: on 7 December 1944, at St. John Ev. Lutheran Church, Oliver, Wilhelm Knodel married Olga Joan Kieling, a registered nurse of Oliver. He was a farmer, a bachelor of 40 and a Lutheran, residing at Eatonia, Saskatchewan, and born in Roumania. His father Gottlieb Knodel was born in Russia and his mother Caroline née Schmidt in Roumania. The witnesses were his brother Jakob Knodel of Oliver and a woman of Oliver whose name is hard to read. ¶1

Death, BC 1984-09-015934: a farmer of 402-803 Fairview Road, Penticton; died 28 September 1984, aged 80; born 5 April 1904 at 'PFEILSDORF - GERMANY' (block capitals, clearly legible); parents Gotthieb [sic] Knodel and Caroline Schmidt. The informant was his widow Olga Joan Knodel. He was cremated at Vernon. ¶2

A Find a Grave biography in Geni's format gives his birthplace as 'Cogelea, Romania', probably Cogealac (Kodschalak), a German Lutheran colony in the northern Dobruja. That is unsourced, and his death record says 'Pfeilsdorf'. ¶4

Birthplace: His 1984 BC death record (informant his widow) gives 'Pfeilsdorf - Germany', but his birth is not in the Pfeilsdorf civil register: the acts of 3 to 22 April 1904 were read one by one, and the declarant line of every 1904 act was read, without finding a Knodel. The first Knodel birth registered at Pfeilsdorf is his brother Gottlieb's in November 1905. His own 1944 marriage record says 'Roumania', and an unsourced online biography says 'Cogelea', i.e. Cogealac (German Kodschalak) in the Dobruja. So he was most probably born in Romania shortly before the family moved to Pfeilsdorf, which was the home he grew up in and the place his widow remembered. ¶5

Birthplace note (schmidt-dobruja, 2026): 'Cogelea' fits Cogealia (German Kodschalie, now Lumina, 15 km north of Constanța) at least as well as Cogealac (Kodschalak). Cogealia is named by Traeger (1922) among the villages that sent families to the Prussian settlement colonies, and his mother's probable kin, the Schmidts, lived there. A 1904 civil birth there would be in the registers of the commune of Cicârciu (Sibioara), held by the Constanța county branch of the Romanian National Archives. ¶6
