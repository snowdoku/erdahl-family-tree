About the family

Family books: Ludwig does not appear as a head of household in the indexed Friedenstal family books of 1847, 1841-1860 or 1881-1900 (Wahl index). The 1861-1880 book (FHL film 1767875 item 1), in which his household should stand, has never been indexed. His absence from the 1881-1900 book suggests that he had died or left Friedenstal by the 1880s. ¶3

Parentage (Speculative, 2026 review): Johann Gottlieb Knodel (c.1796-1859), born in Württemberg (possibly Ensingen), who died at Friedenstal in 1859, is entered as Ludwig's candidate father; the link is unproved. Two Friedenstal Knodel families were weighed. (a) The Leonberg/Lwówek brothers, sons of Friedrich Christoph Knodel and Katharina Bodmer: their children are marked 'Friedensthal kol.' (colonist) in 1861-64, while Ludwig's son in 1870 was 'not a colonist'; a colonist's sons normally inherited that status, so a younger brother would be expected to be a colonist (the 'kol.' remarks, though, appear only on deaths outside Friedenstal, and many Friedenstal entries carry no remark); and none of Ludwig's children carries the names Friedrich, Christoph, Heinrich or Katharina. (b) Johann Gottlieb Knodel's family: also 'not a colonist' (son Gottlieb Friedrich, d. 1856), also absent from the colonist family books, and arrived from Württemberg, which fits Ludwig's absence from both the Bessarabian and the Polish birth indexes; Ludwig's sons Johann Ludwig and Gottlieb echo his name. Option (b) is the better fit but no record names Ludwig's father. Settle with the Friedenstal family book 1861-80 (FHL 1767875 item 1) or the 1860 marriage entry (Kloestitz no. 191, FHL 038902). ¶7

Parentage, update (Speculative, Oct 2026): his candidate father Johann Gottlieb Knodel is now identified, at Probable, as the man baptised at Vaihingen an der Enz on 14 March (indexed 1795, probably 1796), who married Luise Catharina there on 4 July 1822 and lived at Güglingen 1826-34. That couple had a son baptised Franz Ludwig at Güglingen (Ancestry dbId 9866, record 97829178; no date in the index), and Johann Gottlieb's father was named Christoph Ludwig. Luise Catharina is entered as Ludwig's candidate mother. Both links stay Speculative: Franz Ludwig is undated, so he may have been born before Ludwig's estimated birth of c.1832-40 (itself inferred from his 1860 marriage and children 1861-79); the Güglingen children run 1826-34 in the index; and no record names Ludwig's parents. What settles it: the Güglingen baptism of Franz Ludwig (the Güglingen register on Ancestry film 1184769 or Archion; S0111 is the neighbouring 1834 baptism, image 1184769-00225), the 1860 Klöstitz marriage entry (FHL 1884080/1 p.450 no.17), and the Friedenstal family book 1861-80 (FHL 1767875 item 1). Index entries only; images not seen. ¶8

Father of Gottlieb Knodel (1879-1958), reassessed (Oct 2026; stays Probable): the Friedenstal baptism of 1879 (St Petersburg Consistory duplicate, FHL film 1895629 item 1, p.300, entry 12; image read) names the child's parents as Ludwig Knodel and Wilhelmine née Turowsky, verifying the earlier extract. The link stays Probable because Gottlieb's identity with that child is Probable, not Confirmed (see his notes): the bridge rests on a passenger-list index keyword, not yet on the manifest image. His marriage to Wilhelmine on 4 February 1860 is now read in both the Klöstitz register and the Consistory duplicate. His own parentage is still unproved. ¶13

Family books (images read 2026, superseding the earlier index-only note): Ludwig appears in none of the Friedenstal family books. He is not in the 1847 book or the 1841-60 book and its index, and not in the index of the 1861-80 book. He is not in the surname register of the 1881-1900 book. The households of the Leonberg Knodel brothers (Heinrich b. 1818, Friedrich, Gottlieb b. 1826, Wilhelm b. 1828 and Johann b. 1832) list no Ludwig as son or brother in 1847 or 1841-60. These books listed only enrolled colonist households, which fits the 'not a colonist' remark of 1870. ¶14

Parentage, reassessed (Oct 2026; stays Speculative): the candidate parents remain Johann Gottlieb Knodel (c.1796-1859) and Luise Catharina, of Güglingen 1826-34 and later Friedenstal. The new birth estimate of about 1829/30 helps the case. Ludwig would have been born while that couple lived at Güglingen, where a son Franz Ludwig was baptised (index entry, undated). The indexed Güglingen baptisms leave gaps in 1827-28 and 1830-33 into which an undated Franz Ludwig could fall, allowing for an age at marriage that was a year or two out. The Leonberg alternative is weaker. A son born about 1829/30 to Friedrich Christoph Knodel and Katharina Bodmer would be expected among the Sanniki baptisms indexed to 1830 and in his brothers' Friedenstal households of 1847 and 1841-60, and he is in neither. Ludwig's family shared the 'not a colonist' status of Johann Gottlieb's son Gottlieb Friedrich (d. 1856), and like Johann Gottlieb's family it is absent from every Friedenstal family book. The name Gottlieb of his son of 1879, and of that child's godfather, echoes Johann Gottlieb, but Gottlieb was common in both Knodel families. The case against Probable: no record yet names Ludwig's parents, and Franz Ludwig's baptism is undated. In 1860 Ludwig was also enrolled in Klöstitz parish (family book II/84, which earlier work reads as the Beresina book, Roessler household), not at Friedenstal. So the other Bessarabian Knodel lines, at Alt Elft (from Poland, 1816) and Teplitz (from Tuttlingen, 1817), have not been excluded. Any of three records would settle it: Franz Ludwig's baptism date (Güglingen register, FHL 1184769, or Ancestry 9866 record 97829178), the readable left page of the 1860 Klöstitz marriage entry (which should name the groom's father), or the Klöstitz/Beresina family book II p.84. ¶15

Father of Gottlieb Knodel (1879-1958), upgraded to Confirmed (Oct 2026): two primary records, both read, now join up. (1) The Friedenstal baptism of 1879 (St Petersburg Consistory duplicate, FHL 1895629/1 p.300 no.12) names the child Gottlieb's parents as Ludwig Knodel and Wilhelmine née Turowsky. (2) The Megantic passenger manifest of 22 Jul 1928 (LAC T-14745 frame 00366, vol 15 p 110) gives the Canadian Gottlieb Knodel's birthplace as Friedenstal and his age as 49. These were the missing bridge that kept this link at Probable. Ludwig's own parentage is unchanged and stays Speculative. ¶16

Parentage, weighed again (Oct 2026,; stays Speculative): the candidate parents remain Johann Gottlieb Knodel (1796-1859) and Luise Catharina, through their son Franz Ludwig, baptised at Güglingen. For the link: (1) Franz Ludwig's index birth year is 1828 (Ancestry 9866, record 97829178, found by a birth-year filter calibrated as a one-year window). His brother Carl Friederich was born 11 March 1826 and his sister Karoline Wilhelmine on 26 May 1829, so Franz Ludwig was born between about early 1827 and about August 1828 (an inference from the sibling spacing). (2) No burial of Franz Ludwig is indexed in the Württemberg sets, though the Güglingen index is thin. (3) Among the Friedenstal Knodel households of 1838-60 (Heinrich x Gust, Gottlieb x Ackermann, Wilhelm x Neumann, Johann x Klein, Michael x Neumann), the heads of known age (Heinrich b. 1818, Gottlieb b. 1826, Wilhelm b. 1828, Johann b. 1832) were too young to father a son born about 1829, and Michael's age is not given but his only indexed child was born in 1855; no Ludwig birth to any of them appears in the extracts. Their own father, Friedrich Christoph Knodel, has no Ludwig among his Sanniki baptisms indexed to 1830, and the 1847 and 1841-60 family books list no Ludwig in the brothers' households. Johann Gottlieb (b. 1796) is the only Friedenstal Knodel father of the right generation. (4) The 1860 wedding at Klöstitz no longer points away from Friedenstal: Friedenstal belonged to Arzis parish, and Klöstitz parish then covered Klöstitz, Borodino, Beresina and Hoffnungstal, so a bride from Beresina explains the place. (5) Ludwig's household, like Johann Gottlieb's son Gottlieb Friedrich (d. 1856), was 'not a colonist'. Against Probable: (a) Ludwig's stated age of 30 in February 1860 puts his birth between February 1829 and February 1830, while Franz Ludwig was born about 1827-28, a gap of one to three years; that is a common size of error in such registers, so it is not a contradiction, but it is not agreement either. (b) No record names Ludwig's parents, and no Franz Knodel appears anywhere in the Bessarabian extracts 1835-88. (c) The Knodel households of Klöstitz parish (Gottlieb x Straub, Friedrich x Stauber, Friedrich x Kron, Andreas x Reule) and the Alt Elft and Teplitz lines are not excluded, because their heads' ages and children before 1838 are not in the extracts. (d) The 1879 godfather Gottlieb Knodel (with Andreas Gall and Friedrike Engminger, reading uncertain) could be the Leonberg-line Gottlieb (b. 1826) or Johann Gottlieb's son Johann Gottlieb (bapt. 1834, not found in Bessarabia), so he points to neither family. Decision: the link stays Speculative. It is the leading hypothesis and is not weakened. It would rise to Probable if the Güglingen baptism register gives Franz Ludwig a date in 1828 and the Güglingen burials show he did not die there, and it would be settled by the readable left page of the 1860 Klöstitz marriage entry (FHL 038902 item 2, 1860 no. 11), which should name the groom's father, or by the Friedenstal family book 1861-80 (FHL 1767875 item 1). ¶18

Parentage, weighed again after the 1861 and 1875 images (Oct 2026,; stays Speculative): the candidate parents remain Johann Gottlieb Knodel (bapt. Vaihingen an der Enz 1795, d. Friedenstal 1859) and Luise Catharina, through their son Franz Ludwig, baptised at Güglingen. For the link: (1) Ludwig was a foreigner (Ausländer) in 1861 and 1875, and his household was 'not a colonist' in 1870. The only other Friedenstal Knodel household described in such terms is Johann Gottlieb's, whose son was 'not a colonist' and came from Vaihingen in Württemberg. The Leonberg Knodel colonists, who came from Russian Poland, do not fit this profile. (2) Johann Gottlieb (b. 1795) is the only Friedenstal Knodel father of the right generation. (3) His son Franz Ludwig has the index birth year 1828, and no other Ludwig Knodel born 1826-32 is indexed in Ancestry's Württemberg baptisms (dbId 9866). A boy baptised Franz Ludwig would commonly be called by his second name in Württemberg (general naming practice, not a record). (4) Two of Ludwig's daughters were named Wilhelmine and Karolina, and Franz Ludwig had a sister Karoline Wilhelmine (b. 1829), but Wilhelmine was also the mother's name, so this is a weak echo at most. ¶22a

Parentage, weighed again with the exact dates (Oct 2026,; stays Speculative). Age: Ludwig was stated to be 30 at his marriage on 4 February 1860. Russian registers used the Julian calendar, so that day was 16 February 1860 in the Gregorian calendar used in Württemberg. Franz Ludwig, born 21 February 1828, was then 31 years old and five days short of 32. A man aged 30 in February 1860 was born between February 1829 and February 1830, so Franz Ludwig was born about one to two years before the earliest date the stated age allows. The age figure itself is one year too low (30 for 31). Such errors are common in these registers, especially for a man whose parents were dead (his candidate father died in April 1859), so this is a real but small tension, not a contradiction. New for the link: the couple's son Carl Friedrich was born in 1841 at 'Freudenthal, Russia' (FamilySearch index, mother Louisa Lindemeier; S0111), so the family left Württemberg between 1837 and 1841 and was in South Russia when Franz Ludwig was about 13. A boy who came to Russia with foreign parents and was never enrolled as a colonist fits Ludwig's 'Ausländer' (foreign national) and 'not a colonist' descriptions. Franz Ludwig is also the couple's only Ludwig: no son Ludwig born 1830-40 is indexed to them (S0111, S0118). Still against or missing: no record shows Franz Ludwig alive after his baptism or leaving Württemberg, no record names the Friedenstal Ludwig's parents or birthplace, and a Ludwig Knodel born about 1827 in Württemberg is indexed as arriving at New York on 2 January 1866 ('Germans to America', not opened), which is a reminder that other Württemberg Ludwig Knodels of this age existed. Decision: the link stays Speculative. The 1841 record moves the family to South Russia in Franz Ludwig's childhood, which is the strongest fit yet, but it remains a fit of circumstance, not a record of identity. It would rise to Probable if the 1841 entry (presumably St Petersburg Consistory duplicate 1841, p.539 no.6) or any later record shows Franz Ludwig with the family in Russia, or if the Güglingen register shows no death for him; it would rise further if the left page of the 1860 Klöstitz entry (FHL 038902 item 2, 1860 no. 11) or the Friedenstal family book 1861-80 names his father or birthplace. ¶24

Parentage, new evidence (1 Nov 2026,; stays Speculative): (1) the 1841 'Freudenthal' birth of a son of Johann Gottlieb Knodel and Louisa Catharina Lindemeier is in the Freudental parish extracts (FHL 1883193/1 p.538 no.6), so the candidate family was at Freudental near Odessa in 1841. (2) A Johann Gottlieb Knodel married the widow Catharina Fetzer née Straub at Grossliebental on 9 Jul 1843 (FHL 1882594/2 p.319 no.16), and a Gottlieb Knodel x Katharina Straub had children at Klöstitz in 1845 and 1848. If that is the same Johann Gottlieb (unproved), then the 'Gottlieb x Straub' household of Klöstitz, previously counted as a rival candidate family, is the candidate father's own second marriage, and Ludwig, then aged about 16-19, would have lived in Klöstitz parish, where he married in 1860. This removes one competing household and adds a Klöstitz tie, but no record yet names Ludwig's parents. Test: the 1843 marriage image (groom's age and origin) and any Klöstitz confirmation of a Ludwig Knodel about 1843-46. ¶26

Life story

Birth (estimated): about 1829. He was single and 30 years old at his marriage on 4 February 1860 (Klöstitz marriage register 1860, entry 11; image read), so he was born between February 1829 and February 1830 if the stated age is exact. ¶9

Marriage, the register images (read 2026): the Klöstitz marriage register for 1860 (FHL film 038902 item 2, entry 11) states that the groom was single, aged 30, and the bride single, aged 21. Banns were read on 17, 24 and 31 January 1860, and they were married on 4 February 1860 in the prayer house (Bethaus) at Klöstitz by the local pastor (Pastor loci; name read as Börner, uncertain). The register cites the parish family book (Personalbuch) as 'II, 84'. The left-hand page, with the couple's full names, residence and parents, is too faded to read on the available scan. The St Petersburg Consistory duplicate (FHL 1884080, fol. 450, no. 17; image read) lists the couple as 'Knodel, Ludwig mit Jarowsky [or Tarowsky], Wilhelmine', with the same family-book reference II/84 and a cross-reference to folio 9, entry 11 of the main register. No witnesses are recorded in the parts that can be read. ¶11

Status, from the baptism register images (read Oct 2026 by the signed-in lane, S0154): at the baptisms of two of his children at Friedenstal the register calls Ludwig an 'Ausländer', that is, a foreign national who was not a subject of the Russian Empire. (1) Wilhelmine, born 27 August and baptised 30 August 1861 (Old Style): the parents are entered as 'Ludwig Knodel, Ausländer; Ehefrau Wilhelmine, geb. [T/F]arowsky; beide ev.-luth.' (wife Wilhelmine née Tarowsky or Farowsky, both Evangelical Lutheran). The godparents (Pathen) were Friedrich H?eiser (surname uncertain), Jakobine Litz and Maria Knodel, described as 'Kolon[isten]' (colonists). The baptism was 'bestätigt' (confirmed) by Pastor Bienemann at Friedensthal, so the child was probably first baptised at home. St Petersburg Consistory duplicate, FHL 1884084/2 (DGS 4280505, image 400), stamped p.408, entry 43. (2) Anna Maria, born 29 July and baptised 3 August 1875 (Old Style): the parents are entered as 'Ludwig Knodel, Ausländer, u. seine Ehefrau Wilhelmine [T/F]arowsky, beide ev.-luth.'. The godparents were Gottlieb Knodel, Anna Maria Gall and Friederike E?nzminger (reading uncertain, probably Enzminger), described as 'Ansiedl[er]' (settlers). FHL 1884121/3 (DGS 4280537, image 196), p.202, entry 32. In both years the godparents were members of the colony, while Ludwig himself was a foreigner. No odessa3 Consistory extract row for any Knodel in Bessarabia 1838-85 prints the word Ausländer; it is known only from these two images. ¶19

Context: Friedenstal (Friedensthal), Akkerman district, Bessarabia, was founded in 1834 and belonged to Arzis parish. It is now Myrnopillia, Bilhorod-Dnistrovskyi raion, Odesa oblast, Ukraine. ¶6

Status: the death entry of his infant son Ludwig (1870) calls the family 'not a colonist'. In Bessarabian registers this means the household was not enrolled as a land-holding colonist family of the village. Such families were usually latecomers or landless. The only other Friedenstal Knodel so described is Gottlieb Friedrich Knodel (d. 1856), son of Johann Gottlieb Knodel, who was born in Wuerttemberg. ¶4

Status, a parallel: the death of his infant son Ludwig at Friedenstal on 24 January 1870 describes the household as 'not a colonist' (Consistory extract, FHL 1884111/2 p.267 no.7). The only other Friedenstal Knodel row with a status word is the death of Gottlieb Friedrich Knodel, son of Johann Gottlieb Knodel, on 18 June 1856, which gives his origin as 'Vaihingner Wurtt.' (of Vaihingen, Württemberg) and also says 'not a colonist' (FHL 1884101/1 p.186 no.35). The other Friedenstal Knodel heads (Heinrich, Gottlieb, Wilhelm, Johann and Michael) carry no status word. The family book records Heinrich and Gottlieb as born in the colony of Leonberg (Lwówek) in Russian Poland, so they came as colonists from within the Empire rather than as foreigners. Neither Ludwig nor Johann Gottlieb stands in any Friedenstal family book, which fits households that were not enrolled colonists (an inference). ¶20

Godparents, identified as far as possible (Oct 2026): the 1861 godmother Maria Knodel is unidentified. Anna Maria née Kron, wife of Friedrich Knodel, died at Borodino on 11 January 1861 and is excluded; Maria Spielhag, who married a Friedrich Knodel at Friedenstal in 1854, has not been checked. The 1875 (and 1879) godfather Gottlieb Knodel is probably the Leonberg-line Gottlieb (b. 1826), husband of Barbara Ackermann. Anna Maria Gall is probably Anna Maria née Eichele, wife of Andreas Gall, who was a godfather in 1879. None of the godparents is a known member of Johann Gottlieb Knodel's household, so the sponsors neither support nor exclude that parentage. A Leonberg-line Knodel sponsoring two children could mean a family tie, or only that he was a neighbour of the same surname; the records do not say which. ¶21

Records that disagree 1

Against: (a) Ludwig was 30 at his marriage on 4 February 1860, which puts his birth between February 1829 and February 1830. Franz Ludwig was born in 1828, by August 1828 at the latest (his sister was born in May 1829), so he would have been 31 or 32. An error of one or two years in a stated age at marriage is common in these registers, so this is a small tension, not a contradiction. (b) No record names Ludwig's parents or birthplace. (c) No godparent links him to Johann Gottlieb's family, and Johann Gottlieb's other surviving sons are not found in Bessarabia. (d) The Ausländer status shows that Ludwig came from outside the Empire, probably from Germany, but it does not pick out Johann Gottlieb's family. Any single Württemberg Knodel who emigrated in the 1840s or 1850s would carry the same status, and Knodel is a common name around Vaihingen (578 Johann Gottlieb Knodel rows alone in Ancestry dbId 61023). Decision: the link stays Speculative. Every new fact fits the hypothesis, and none contradicts it. But it is a fit of category (a foreign, non-colonist household from Württemberg), not of identity, and no single record ties the two men together. That is short of the 'strong circumstantial fit' the fleet requires for Probable. It would rise to Probable if the Güglingen baptism register or a Württemberg emigration record shows Franz Ludwig leaving with his parents or surviving to adulthood. It would rise to Probable or Confirmed if the left page of the 1860 Klöstitz marriage entry (FHL 038902 item 2, 1860 no. 11) or the Friedenstal family book 1861-80 (FHL 1767875 item 1) names Ludwig's father or birthplace. ¶22b

Alternatives considered and set aside 2

Origin (unproved): Ludwig was probably born about 1832-1840. His baptism is not among the Bessarabian extracts for 1833-1849, nor among the Knodel baptisms indexed for Sanniki parish, Poland. Two Friedenstal Knodel families could be his. The first is the colonist household of brothers from Leonberg (Lwowek), Sanniki parish, Poland: Heinrich (b. 1818/19), Friedrich, Gottlieb (b. 1825/26), Wilhelm (b. 1828) and Johann (b. 1832). They were sons of Friedrich Christoph Knodel and Katharina Bodmer, who married at Sanniki in 1819 (Geneteka index). A compiled tree (Kolewe, on GEDBAS; a lead only) gives Friedrich Christoph's birth as 15 November 1794 at Ochsenburg, Wuerttemberg, and his father as Johann Adam Knodel of Ensingen. The second is the family of Johann Gottlieb Knodel (c.1796-1859), born at 'Unfingen?' in Wuerttemberg, probably Ensingen near Vaihingen an der Enz. Both families trace to the Ensingen/Ochsenburg Knodels of Wuerttemberg, so the German homeland is probably that district. Ludwig's own link to either family is speculative. ¶5

Candidate baptism, exact dates (S0118, FamilySearch index read by the signed-in lane, Oct 2026): Franz Ludwig Knodel, son of Johann Gottlieb Knodel and Luise Katharine Lindenmeier, was born on 21 February 1828 and baptised on 29 February 1828 at Güglingen, Oberamt Brackenheim, Württemberg (FamilySearch 'Germany, Births and Baptisms, 1558-1898', the twin of Ancestry dbId 9866 record 97829178). Index only; no image. These dates replace the 'index birth year 1828' and 'by August 1828 at the latest' wording of the earlier parentage notes. ¶23
