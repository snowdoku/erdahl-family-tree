About the family

Husband of Selma (Eidem) Nelson, the Eidem daughter who left Union County for the far west of the state. The project had her only as 'Selma (Eidem) Nelson', from her sister Myrtle Overdahl's 2013 obituary, with no husband's name and no place. ¶1

Life story

Two Elk Point newspaper obituaries name him. Her mother's, Leader-Courier, 18 March 1937, lists among the survivors 'Mrs. ROY NELSON of FAITH, S. D.'; her father's, the same paper, 1 August 1940, lists 'Mrs. RAYMOND NELSON of FAITH'. One of the two given names is a slip by the paper, and there is no way from these two notices alone to say which. Recorded with both, Roy first because it is the earlier printing. ¶2

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
