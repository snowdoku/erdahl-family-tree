Life story

Married Ole Andersen 10 Jun 1846, Leikanger parish. In the 1875 MN census ('Gjertru', 52) and 1880 U.S. census ('Gertrude', 57). Death and burial not yet found. ¶1

Emigration: entered in the Balestrand parish register's 1856 out-migration list, 12 April 1856 (no. 56), as 'Gjertrud Johnsd., hans Hustru', aged 33, the wife of the cottager Ole Andersen of Grøneng. She left for North America with him and their children Ingeborg, Anders and Marie. ¶2

1870 U.S. census (index only): 'Getrude Anderson', born about 1822 in Norway, in Ole Anderson's household in Emerald, Faribault County (Ancestry db 7163, record 41887520). ¶3

1905 Minnesota census (image read): 'Anderson Gjertrud', female, 82, born in Norway of Norwegian parents, 44 years in Minnesota and 44 years in the district, is line 59 of Emerald sheet 6 (Faribault Co., ED 22, census day 1 June 1905, enumeration finished 9 June), at the same rural route address (R.1 Frost) as the lines above. The 1905 schedule has no relationship column, but she is entered directly after the family of Mons K. Johnson, 46, farmer, born Minnesota, and Johane M. Johnson, 44, born Minnesota of Norwegian parents, with their children Gusta C., 23, Gjertrud T., 21, Olever M., 14 and Clara J., 11 (plus a boarder child and a hired laborer). Johane M. is very probably her daughter Johanna, so the widowed Gjertrud was probably living with her daughter's family. Her occupation looks like 'Capitalist', struck through. ¶4

1 correction

Corrections 1

Correction: her death was given as 'after 1880'. The 1905 Minnesota census shows her alive in Emerald in June 1905, aged 82, so the death date is moved to after 1 June 1905. The identification rests on name, age (born about 1823), Norwegian birth, the township where her husband died in 1895, and her place beside her daughter's family. Her death record (probably 1905-1915, Faribault County; Minnesota death certificates began in 1908) and grave have not been found. ¶5
