Life story

Lesja bygdebok (vol. 1, canvas 259, entry 13-3 under Sør-Einbu; word-box rebuild): Guri, widow of Erik, 'g. 1747 m. Engebret Larss., ca. 1705-83', who was perhaps a brother of a man at... Einbu (6-1) and bought the farm in 1748; children Marit 1748, Lars 1751 (took over), Peder 1753 ('sjå... Einbu (21-1)', the entry for Peder Engebretsen, 1753-1804, m. 1783 Anne Pedersd.), Guri 1755-56. So the bygdebok names Peder (see his page) as his son. Grade unchanged (Probable; compiled source). Birth ABT 1705, death 1783 per the bygdebok (ABT, rebuilt text). ¶2

The baptism of his son is found: 9236 p. 379 (canvas 182), 5 Aug 1753, Verket, 'Engebret Søreneboes Peder'. Chain: 1753 baptism (Engebret of Søndre Eneboe, son Peder) -> 1763 manntall (Engebret Larsøn and Guri Pedersd. still at Søndre Eneboe; the only Engebret on either Eneboe farm) -> 1783 marriage of 'Soldat Peder Ingebrigtss. Søreneboe' -> 1801 census, Peder Ingebretsen 48 at Eeneboe. Graded on his link to Peder (see his page): stays Probable, because the baptism-to-marriage step (30 years) rests on patronymic, farm and age, with no record in between that names Peder (he was under 12 in 1763). ¶3a

Skifteprotokoll 25159 (Nordre Gudbrandsdal, probate no. 3, 1765-85) images 978-1002 searched; every 'Anno 178x den...' heading read on crops (map in findings/.md, section). Image 1003 is the end card, so the volume is now read at heading level to its end. No skifte after Engebret Larsen Eneboe/Einbu or Guri Pedersdatter. The Lesja/Dovre entries in 978-1002 are Landem (Anna Hansdatter, widower Ole Pedersen, 7 Aug 1784, Dovre annex), Hage (Iver Ellevsen, widow Marit Andersdatter, 29 Aug 1784, Lesja), Langdalen (27 Aug 1784, Dovre annex), Killie (Joen Erichsen, 27 Dec 1784, Dovre annex) and Brandlien (Jørgen Jacobsen, 18 Jan 1785). The Lesja session pages 966R-969L were also read past the headings: Bjørnegaard (Dovre annex, 5 Apr 1784), Moe in Bredden (Vaage), and the 22 Apr 1784 Mellem Stuen Eneboe skifte after Ambiør (widower Peder Syversen, daughter Guri Pedersdatter 6), with its sessions on 10 Jul, 6 Aug and 18 Dec 1784. No unheaded Eneboe entry follows. Grade unchanged. A skifte after him, if one was held, would be in 25160 (1786-1804) or was never recorded. ¶6

Skifteprotokoll 25159 images 899-977 searched (headings located on montages and read on crops; map in findings/.md, section). The volume runs on to 1785 (images 991-1002), not to 1781 as noted before. No skifte after Engebret Larsen Eneboe or his wife Guri Pedersdatter was found. The only Eneboe heading is 968R, 22 Apr 1784, Mellem Stuen Eneboe, after Ambiør [Tostensdatter], widower Peder Syversen (the known 1784 card). A Folldal division concluded 11 Dec 1782 (918L) lists children Engebret, Peder, Knud, Syver, Morten, Ole Engebretsen, Anna, Ingeborg, Brit, Marit Engebretsdatter, but it is a Dovre annex (Folldal) estate with no Lars or Guri, so it is not this family. Grade unchanged. ¶5a

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the grade · 2 alternatives set aside · 1 search with no result · 3 open questions

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Probable father of Peder Ingebretsen (graded on that link). The 1763 Lesja manntall of the extra tax (Ekstraskatt Gudbrandsdal 1762-66, source 45390, view 287, read on the scan) lists at No. 6 'Søndre Eneboe', self-owner, the couple Engebret Larsøn and Guri Pedersd., with children over 12 Erich, Lars, Marit and Marit (Peder, about 10, was too young to be listed). The link is inferred, not named: Peder Ingebrigtsen married in 1783 as a soldier 'of Søreneboe' and lived there with his family in 1784-1801; Engebret Larsen is the only Engebret on either Eneboe farm in 1763; and Peder's first child (bapt. 1 Jan 1784) was named Gurri. Birth before 1730 is estimated from children over 12 in 1763. ¶1a

Alternatives considered and set aside 2

Caution: another Peter Ingebretsen lived at Morken in the 1790s. ¶1b

Near-miss attempt, not raised: Peder Engebretsen's confirmation cannot be found by name before Nov 1772. Before then, 9236 records confirmations as counts only, e.g. 12 Nov [1766] 'Confirmeret 22 Drenger 23 Piger' (c272). The named lists begin on 21 p. Trin. 1772 (p. 39, canvas 21; main register c320: 'vid. pag. 39'). Read: c21-25 (1772-75). There is no Peder Engebretsen of Eneboe in them. The 1774 catch-up list of older youths (p. 45, canvas 24) has 'Peder Engebretsen S?nstebø 19', a farm reading that is not Eneboe and an age 2 years short for b. Aug 1753, so it was not used. Grade unchanged. ¶7
