About the family

Wife of Even Pederssen of Uglan and Kari Pedersdatter Eidem's maternal grandmother. 1801 census, Selbu, rural residence 0030 Uglan, household 04, aged 43, 'hans kone', with five children. Her youngest son, born about 1799, is Poul Evensen, named for her father. ¶1

Son Peder's baptism: 'Karen Poulsd.' is the mother, with Even Pedersen the father, of Peder baptised 14 March 1790 (Laetare Sunday), Selbu ministerialbok 1757-1801, canvas 135; godparents of Uglum, Garberg and Fors. He is Peder Evenssen, 11, of the 1801 Uglan household and Mali Evensdatter's elder brother. ¶2

Children: Giertrud (baptised Annunciation 1786), Brynild (5 August 1787), Peder (14 March 1790), Giertrud (2 December 1792), Povel (29 March 1795, died 30 April 1795) and, by the 1801 census, Magli (about 1796) and Poul (about 1799). See Even Pederssen for the references and for why her confidence is left at Probable. ¶4

Daughter Mali's baptism: 'Magli', born 20 June 1796, baptised 26 June 1796, parents Even Pedersen Uglem and Karen Povelsdatter (Selbu ministerialbok 1757-1801, canvas 194 right page). Karen was churched on 24 July 1796 (canvas 195). ¶9

Kin, an inference: Jon Povelsen Garberg was a godfather at her daughter Mali's baptism in 1796 ('Jon Garb.' also stood for her son Peder in 1790), and 'Karen Povelsd. Uglem' was godmother in 1797 to a child of Niels Nielsen of Garberg-plassen alongside 'Jon Povelsen ibid.' (canvas 213), and at Uglem in July 1797 to a daughter of Jon Hansen Uglem (canvas 212). Jertrud Andersdatter Garberg, another of Mali's godmothers, is recorded as the wife of Niels Povelsen Garberg (canvas 190). Since Karen was 'of Garberg' when she married in 1784 and named a son Povel (1795) and Poul (about 1799), Jon Povelsen Garberg is probably her brother, and the Povelsens of Garberg (Jon, Niels, Peder, Guttorm) her siblings - unproved; her own baptism (about 1757-58) and a Povel of Garberg's household would settle it. ¶10

Daughter's marriage: her daughter Magli (Mali) Evensdatter of Auglan (Uglem), aged 30, married the widower Peder Bersvendsen of Eidem on 5 June 1826; one of the two sureties was 'Niels Paulsen Garberg', Karen's brother, baptised 1761 to Paul Pedersen Garberg and Giertrud Nielsdatter. Scans. ¶12

Life story

Baptism: 'Kari', daughter of Paul Pedersen Garberg and Giertrud Nielsdatter, baptised 12 November 1758 (25th Sunday after Trinity), Selbu ministerialbok 1757-1801, canvas 7 left page (crop). Kari and Karen are the same name in this register. Parents linked as Confirmed: she married in 1784 as Karen Povelsdatter of Garberg; her burial age (84) fits late 1758 and her 1801 age (43) is within a year of it; she is the only Kari/Karen daughter of a Paul at Garberg in the complete read of 1757-67; her children are named Giertrud (twice) and Povel/Poul after these parents; and her brothers Niels (b. 1761) and Jon (b. 1764) Povelsen of Garberg stood sponsor to her children in 1787, 1790 and 1796. Her birth date is now 'before 12 November 1758' in place of ABT 1758. ¶6

Marriage and origin: betrothed 9 May 1784 (Cantate Sunday) and married 13 June 1784 (1st Sunday after Trinity) to Even Pedersen of Uglem, entered as 'Karen Povelsd. Garberg' and 'Karen Poulsd. Garberg' - she was of the farm Garberg before her marriage (Selbu ministerialbok 1757-1801, canvases 114-115). Her father Povel (Poul) is therefore to be sought at Garberg; her son Poul (about 1799) carries his name. Godparents from Garberg at her children's baptisms (Mali Garberg 1786, Niels and Mali Garberg 1787, Jon Garberg 1790, Inger Garberg 1792) are probably her kin. ¶3

Widowhood: her husband Even Pedersen died at Uglem on 3 July 1812 (Selbu ministerialbok 1801-1815, p. 176). Her own burial is not yet found. ¶5

Death and burial: 'Karen Paulsdatter Uglum', aged 84, died 14 January and was buried 22 January 1843 (Selbu burials 1843, p. 356 no. 5; index only, residence and status blank, scan not read). Same name, the farm Uglem where she lived from 1784, and an age that fits a November 1758 baptism; no other Karen Paulsdatter of that age is in the Selbu burial index 1812-1845. The bygdebok (vol. 4 p. 76, word boxes) also gives her years as 1758-1843. Reading the scan would make this certain. ¶7

Death and burial: died 14 January 1843 and buried 22 January 1843: 'Karen Paulsdatter Uglum', aged 84, residence Uglum, followed by an abbreviated status not read with certainty (possibly lægdskone, a pauper boarded round the farms in rotation). Church book from Selbu parish 1843-1859, burials 1843, p. 356 no. 5; index entry, scan. The identification is Probable but strong: same name (Paul is the later form of Povel), same farm, a widow since 1812, and age 84 gives a birth in 1758-59, against 43 in February 1801 (born 1757-58). found no other Karen Pauls-/Povelsdatter burial in Selbu 1812-1855 born 1750-1766. The bygdebok skeleton (vol. 4, p. 76) gives Even's wife 'Kari... Garberg' the years 1758 and 1843. ¶8

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Confidence left at Probable for the reason given on Even Pederssen: the link from her daughter Mali of Uglem to Kari Pedersdatter's mother is a strong fit with nothing against it, but not yet stated in a primary record. ¶11

Confidence of her link as Mali Evensdatter's mother raised from Probable to Confirmed (13 Oct 2026), applying the audit standard of (the adult's own marriage names the baptism farm and the fit is unique). Peder Bersvendsen's second marriage, 5 June 1826, gives his bride as Magli Evensdatter of Auglan (Uglem), aged 30 (born about 1796), a farmer's daughter, and the second church book names her father as 'Even Peders.' (Even Pedersen). Magli, daughter of Even Pedersen of Uglem and Karen Povelsdatter, was baptised on 26 June 1796 (born 20 June); she is the only Magli/Mali Evensdatter born 1793-1798 in the 1801 census of Selbu, and Even was a farmer (gaardmand) at Uglem, matching 'Bonde Datter'. A surety at the wedding was Niels Paulsen (Povelsen) of Garberg, Karen Povelsdatter's brother (baptised 1761), i.e. the bride's maternal uncle. The same Magli Evensdatter is the mother of Giertrug (1830) and Kari (1834) by Peder Bersvendsen of Eidem, and Mali Evensdatter, widow, born 1796, lives with Kari in 1865. The marriage thus ties Kari's mother to the 1796 baptism by name, father, farm and age, with no rival. ¶13
