Life story

The oldest ancestor now reached on David Erdahl's Slind line, and the first of it born in the SEVENTEENTH CENTURY. Selbu i fortid og naatid vol. 7, p. 116: 'Brukere... 1729-1755 JORN OLSEN... ca. 1755... G. 1717 m. Brynhild... fra... Flora, f. 1700, d. 1765.' He held the Slindan bruk from 1729 to about 1755 and married in 1717, so he was born about 1690-1695 - say ABT 1692, which is what is entered, flagged as an estimate from the marriage year. ¶1

The narrative on p. 113 of the same volume says how he got the farm: the previous holder, an Olsen born about 1684, DIED IN 1728, and 'Brynhild... var fra... Flora. Hun og [Jorn] Olsen [kom] til [bruket] etter [at han] dode. 1729 fikk de... BYGSELBREV paa [x] spann Slindan' - she was from Flora, she and Jorn Olsen came to the holding after the previous man died, and in 1729 THEY WERE GRANTED A BYGSELBREV, a lease deed, on so many spann of Slindan. So this family's four-generation tenancy of Slind begins in 1729 with a lease granted to an incoming couple, not with an inheritance. ¶2

His own patronymic makes HIS father an Ole, born in the 1660s. The gaardshistorie's holder list for the same bruk begins '1701- Ola [N.], f. ca. 1668, og Berit [N.], f. ca. 1671' - a man born about 1668 - but nothing in the reconstructed text connects that Ola to this Jorn, and the 1717-1728 holder in between was 'son of Ola Jonsen Mebost d.y.', a different family. So the link is NOT made and no eighth generation is entered. ¶3

The bruk is named: SLINDAN VESTRE. The chapter heading 'SLINDAN VESTRE GNR...' stands at the foot of printed page 112 of Selbu i fortid og naatid vol. 7, and the holder list on pages 113-117 that begins with him is its holder list. His great-great-grandson Gunder Olsen Slind left Slindan vestre in 1871 for a different holding on the same farm, Slindan nordre (Nedpaa-Garden), which is where Oline Slind was born in 1881. ¶6

Probably the 'Jørgen Smed' (Jørgen the smith) whose son Olaf was baptised on 19 December 1717 and whose wife Brynel was churched after 'the legitimate child Olaf' on 23 January 1718 (Selbu ministerialbok 1699-1737, canvas 69, crops). Names and date fit Jorn Olsen x Brynhild, married 1717, son Ola born 1717; no farm or patronymic is given, so this supports but does not raise his Probable rating. See Ola Jornsen Slind. ¶7

Betrothal, about 3rd-4th Sunday after Trinity 1717 (June, just before St John's Day): 'detrolovede: Jon Vigen Morstad[?] og Ane Siursd., ditto Jørgen grof-|smed og Brynel Olsd.' - betrothed Jørgen the blacksmith (grovsmed) and Brynhild Olsdatter (16310 canvas 66 right, crop checked by the orchestrator). This agrees with the bygdebok's marriage year 1717 and with the baptism of 'Jørgen Smeds barn Olaf' on 19 Dec 1717 (c69 left; godparents Bard Uthus, Peder Grøtlien[?], Reinholt Be[..], Brynil Uthus, Mali Efvien). He was a smith by trade. ¶11

1762 extra tax: 'Jørgen Olsen, Brynild Ols datter' head the household at 706 Slinden (owner Thomas Angell); under children over 12 stands 'Ole gift med Marit Tors[?]datter, Børn, Brynild, Marit, Ingebor' - his son Ole, married to Marit, and Ole's children Brynild, Marit and Ingebor. The household also had a maid (pige) Ingebor Olsdatter and the cottager (husmann) Jon Bersvendsen, widower, with his married son Østen; 11 persons in all. The 1762 ekstraskatt was a poll tax listing each household's man and wife, children over 12, servants and lodgers; children under 12 were not listed. Identification Probable: names of the couple and of the married son Ole, and the farm, agree with the bygdebok. ¶9

Corrections 1

Correction: death changed from 'ABT 1755' to 'AFT 1762'. The bygdebok's c. 1755 is the end of his tenure as holder, not a recorded death: in the 1762 extra tax (ekstraskatt) list he is alive at Slinden, matrikel no. 706, as 'Jørgen Olsen' with his wife 'Brynild Ols datter'. Jørgen is the written form of Jørn. His burial has not been found; the Selbu register 1757-1801 (Digitalarkivet source 16311) from 1762 onward would give it. ¶8

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Not in the 1701 manntall: the full Selbu transcription (737 men) has no Jørn or Jørgen Olsen of any age, and no farm Flora. Together with his trade (blacksmith) this suggests he came to Selbu from another parish after 1701; his origin is open. What would carry this further: a Stjør- og Verdal probate after him or Brynhild (skifteprotokoll 5, 1760-66, source 24755, or 6-1/6-2), which would name heirs and possibly his origin; the 1701 manntall of neighbouring parishes (Strinda, Klæbu, Stjørdal) for a Jørgen Olsen born about 1690. ¶12

Searches that found nothing 3

Burial searched and not found in 16311 for 1767-1779 (canvases 21-94 read in full for burials). A 'Jørgen Slinden' stood sponsor in Jan 1770 and Mar 1771 (c35 R, c43 L) - this may be his grandson Jørgen Olsen Slind (b. 1752). His death therefore falls 1762-1767 (16311 canvases 8-20, burials not yet read) or he was buried elsewhere. ¶13

Burial not found in the main register 16311 from Palm Sunday 1759 to mid-1779: canvases 8-20 read in full for burials (canvas 20, Jul 1766-Feb 1767, read by the orchestrator) and canvases 21-94. He was alive at the extra tax of Nov 1762; his wife was buried Dec 1765. Either he died after mid-1779 (aged about 87+; 16311 canvases 95-141 not read for burials) or his burial was entered only in the parallel klokkerbok 16316 (1766-76, unread) or elsewhere. ¶14a

Burial searched and not found in 16311 for the whole span Palm Sunday 1759 - May 1791 (all canvases 8-141 read in full for burials:; canvas 20 by the orchestrator). By 1791 he would have been 99. He was alive at the extra tax of Nov 1762. His daughter Ingeborg Jørgensdatter Slinden died 25 Apr 1789 aged 70 (c132; the register writes his name Jørgen). Remaining places: the parallel klokkerbok 16316 (1766-76; unread), a burial outside Selbu (e.g. Tydal annex or his unknown home parish), or a probate in Stjør- og Verdal skifteprotokoll 5-7 (24755-24758, unindexed). ¶15
