Life story

Born 27 May 1842, baptised 9 April 1851, son of Thomas and Martha Elizabeth Hayter (PEI baptism index). Probably the James Hayter who married Jemima 'Leloshine' [prob. Lelacheur] on 23 December 1863 (PEI marriage registers index). ¶1

His bride of 23 December 1863, indexed 'Jemima Leloshine', was almost certainly a Le Lacheur. Bartholemew and James Le Lacheur were farmers at White Sands, Lot 64, in the 1864 Hutchinson's Directory, alongside the other Guernsey families of White Sands (Brehaut, Machon, Nicolle, Robin). James and Jemima were not found in the 1871 or 1881 Canadian censuses, or in the US censuses of 1870, 1880 or 1900, so they probably left PEI. ¶2

Marriage and later life: The James Hayter who married Jemima "Leloshine" on 23 December 1863 (PEI marriage register) is very probably this James, then aged 21. The LeLacheurs were a White Sands family: Bartholemew LeLacheur, farmer, and James LeLacheur, blacksmith, are listed at White Sands in Hutchinson's 1864 directory. No James and Jemima Hayter have been found in any Canadian census from 1871 to 1901 or in the US censuses of 1870, 1880 and 1900. The only Hayter household in PEI in 1881 is that of his brother Thomas. So the couple most likely left the Island, or one of them died, soon after 1863. ¶3
