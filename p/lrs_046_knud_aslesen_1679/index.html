About the family

Father of Asle Knudsen (Probable): Asle Knudsen of Dybsjorde (confirmed 1747, aged 20, b. about 1727; married 1756) is taken to be his son because of the patronymic Knudsen, the same farm, a fitting age (Knud would have been about 48 at Asle's birth), and the naming pattern: Asle bears the name of Knud's own father Asle, and Asle named his first son Knud. ¶2a

Wife: Rangdi (Rangde) Halvorsdatter (see her page), d. by 16 Jun 1742. Children named in 1742: Ole Knudsen (of age; d. 1761 at Dybsjorde, aged 44, so the 'probable elder son' of earlier notes is now proved), Asle Knudsen (14) and Rangdi Knudsdatter (married to Thomas Aslesen). This explains why no probate was held after Knud in 1751: the farm had passed to the children in 1742. ¶8

Siblings: the skifte of 14 Oct 1741 at Dybsjorde after 'den Enungkarl afg. Syver Aslesen', who had lived ('hold') on Dybsjorde (Ringerike og Hallingdal skifteprotokoll 13, fol. 107a; register entry 'Gjuvsjordet i Ål, Ungk. Syver Aslesen'; panteregister 14666 p. 48 '1½ Løbe i Gd. Dybsjorde udlagt til Arvingerne', pantebok 9 fol. 127 [the pantebok abstract itself was not found on s. 126-127]), divides his estate 'imellem hans igienlevende Søskende og Arfvinger som ere: Knud Aslesen Dybsjord, Amund[?] Aslesen tienende paa Dybsjord, Birte Asles Daatter gift med Knud Knudsen Breen, Anne Asles Daatter Kolthshaug Enke, og Christi Asles Daatter gift med Ole Rasmussen Ba[...]'. So Knud Aslesen of Dybsjorde was one of at least six children of an Asle (Syver, Knud, Amund, Birte, Anne, Christi); no parent was alive to inherit. Rangdi of Dybsjorde (Knud's wife) is named later in the same skifte. The text also cites a skiftebrev of 20 Feb 1734 'efter den afg. Karls Forældre[?]' (after the deceased bachelor's parents[?]), pages 21-23, in which Syver's inheritance was 35 rd 3 ort 8 sk: this is probably the probate after Asle (Knud's father) and his wife, and would name them. It was not found at fol. 10b-12a or 20b-22a of skifteprotokoll 10 (1734-1737, source 25545), whose Feb 1734 Ål sessions sit around fol. 10-12. ¶9

Life story

Burial register: 'Knudt Aslesøn Dybsjoret', 72 years, is entered under 29 Jun 1751 in the Ål burial register (p. 311); the Digitalarkivet index files that date as his date of death and computes a birth year of 1679. The age 72 sits between his row and a struck-through row above, but the index agrees with this reading. He is the only Knud buried from Dybsjorde in the indexed Ål burials 1745-1764. ¶1

Land records: the Ål tinglag panteregister (source 14666, p. 48, Dybsjorde) opens with a 'Contract imellem Knud Aslesen og mange flere' about the farm's outlying land, dated 23 Jun 1741 and tinglyst 20 Oct 1741 (pantebok 9 fol. 109), which places him as the Dybsjorde owner in 1741. It also lists a skifte after a...Aslesen (first name unclear, possibly Sjurd or Tjøstel) of 14 Oct 1741 by which 1½ løb in Dybsjorde went to his heirs (pantebok 9 fol. 127), and the skifte of 16 Jun 1742 after Rangdi Halvorsdatter of Dybsjorde, whose children received the farm with life use for 'Faderen' (pantebok 9 fol. 162). There is no deed or skifte after Knud himself in 1751, consistent with the farm having passed to the children in 1742. Rangdi Halvorsdatter is added as his probable wife (no marriage record; inferred). ¶7

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Probable elder son: Ole Knudsen of Dybsjorde, a married man (*Manden*), died 2 Feb 1761 aged 44 (b. about 1717) and was interred 24 May 1761. He is probably Asle's elder brother (same farm and patronymic), not proved. 'Enken Guro Dybsjorde', the widow Guro of Dybsjorde, godmother in Oct 1762, may be either Knud's widow or Ole's; no burial for her was found in Ål up to 1764. ¶3

Upgrade: Confirmed, graded on his link to his son Asle Knudsen. The probate after his wife Rangde Halvorsdatter, held at Dybsjorde on 16 Jun 1742 (Ringerike og Hallingdal skifteprotokoll 13, source 25548, image 289), names 'hendes efterlevende Mand Knud Aslesen Dybsjorde' and 'deris i Ægteskab sammen avlede Børn Ole Knudsen [fuld]myndig, Asle Knudsen 14 aar og Rangdi Knudsdatter gift med Thomas Aslesen'; 'Enkemanden Knud Aslesen' attended. This replaces the naming-pattern inference and the earlier note that no probate would name Asle's father. Continuity (no rival): Asle Knudsen, 14 at Dybsjorde in Jun 1742 (b. about 1727-28), is 'Asle Knudsøn Dybsjord' aged 20 at his confirmation on 1 Oct 1747 (five years later, same farm, same age); he married in 1756 as 'Asle Knudssen Dybsjorde', and his 1773 probate names Knud Olsen of Dybsjorde (son of his brother Ole Knudsen, d. 1761) as his children's first cousin. The other Asle Knudsens of Ål (Tangen, Skurdalen, Skarsgaard) have no Dybsjorde tie. The 1751 burial residence conflict (Stave vs Dybsjorde) does not affect the link: Knud was alive and at Dybsjorde in 1742 and kept life use of the farm. ¶6

The case for this identification 1

Supporting evidence (1773 probate): the probate index card for the skifte of 12 Oct 1773 after Asle Knudsen of Medgaarden names Knud Olsen of Dybsjorde as the children's first cousin (søskendebarn) (index card wording; not yet found in the protocol text). As Asle's wife came from Sando, the cousin from Dybsjorde must be a child of Asle's sibling, which makes Asle a brother of Ole Knudsen of Dybsjorde (died 1761, aged 44, born about 1717). This ties Asle to the Dybsjorde family of Knud Aslesen, but it still does not name Asle's father, so the link stays Probable. ¶4a

Records that disagree 1

Conflict: a Digitalarkivet burial-index row for 1751 (Ål church book 1744-1769, search forenamn=Knud) gives 'Knudt Aslesen' with residence Stave, not Dybsjorde, and no age. The earlier reading has 'Knudt Aslesøn Dybsjoret', 72, under 29 Jun 1751. It may be the same man with a residence error in one of the two readings, or another Knud Aslesen of Stave. Ole Knudsen of Dybsjorde, buried 1761 aged 44 (b. 1717), is still taken to be Asle's brother, and his probable son Knud Olsen (55, Gaardmand at Dybsjorde in 1801) was named as the 'søskendebarn' (first cousin) of Asle's children in 1773, which ties Asle's family to Dybsjorde. Knud Aslesen stays Probable as Asle's father. Would resolve it: the page image of the 1751 burial (Ål church book 1744-1769, source 8544). ¶5

Corrections 1

Correction: the skiftekort for the 1741 skifte after his brother Syver Aslesen (3917 image 965) reads the second sibling as 'Svend Aslesen, s[amme]steds' (of the same place, Dybsjorde), not Amund; Birte is also given as Birgit, and Anne Aslesdatter Krøthshaug as widow of a Niels. Read the protocol (prot. 13 fol. 107a) to settle Svend vs Amund. ¶10

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Not proved: no record names Asle's father, the indexed burials start only in 1745, and the Ål registers before 1744 are lost. ¶2b

Searches that found nothing 2

Searched (negative): the skiftebrev of 20 Feb 1734 'after the deceased bachelor's parents', cited in Syver's 1741 skifte, is not in skifteprotokoll 10. The protocol's farm register (views 3-6) has no Dybsjorde/Gjuvsjordet i Ål entry and no deceased Asle; its only Gjuvsjordet entry is in Hol (Sander Tostensen, 1734, fol. 43b). The Feb 1734 Ål sessions in the protocol are Sal (17 Feb, fol. 9b-13b) and Strand (22 Feb, fol. 13b ff.), read on the scans; nothing on 20 Feb. The skiftekort under Gjuvsjordet i Ål start with Syver's 1741 card (no 1734 card). So it was probably a private skifte. ¶11a

Searched (negative): the 20 Feb 1734 skiftebrev after the parents of Syver Aslesen (and so probably of Knud) is not registered in pantebok 7 at either Ål ting of 1734. The Ål summer ting (opened 19 Jul 1734 at Søndre Tingstue, s. 214-217) and the Ål autumn ting (12-16 Nov 1734, s. 233-239, ending before the Nes ting of 18 Nov on s. 240) were read deed by deed. Their documents concern Hol farms (Rogeton, Nordre Hagen, Vand, Kaae, Store Haugegaard), Søndre Brøyn, Halvorsgaard, Ulshagen and Nedre Hagen; none concerns Dybsjorde or an Asle. No skiftekort exists for it either, so it was a private skifte that was apparently never tinglyst. Remaining chance: the Ål winter ting of early 1735 in the same pantebok (after s. 240), or the Dybsjorde page of the Ål panteregister (14666 p. 48) for a 1734-35 entry. ¶12
