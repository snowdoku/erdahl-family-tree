Life story

Possibly the Peder Mikelsen of Stok who married Mari Hansdatter of Kokkest at Jevnaker on 28 Dec 1746 (indexed marriage pv00000002196086; the only Peder Mikelsen groom indexed 1725-1799); the farm differs, so this is not adopted. ¶2

Scan read: Jevnaker 1752-1799 (DA 9335) canvas 103, p. 198, 3 Jul 1768 [Lunner]: 'Item til Daaben Per Mikelsen Volla og Mari Hans datter, deres Drengebarn Ole. Fadd: Povel Mikelsen Biøralt Engen, Anders Jensen Hage, Ole Jørgensen Biøralt, Gubiør Ols datter Biøraltstuen, Giertrue Joens datter Leeker'. Povel Michelsen married Gubiør Olsdatter of Raastad at Lunner 8 Oct 1750 (Flasla Stuen; pv00000002196291), so Povel and Gubiør are a couple standing godparents together: Povel Mikkelsen is very probably Per's brother (patronymic plus godparent role; not proved). The 1746 Stok marriage (Peder Mikelsen x pigen Mari Hansd. of Kokkest, sureties Jørgen Stok and Niels Lia; pv00000002196085) remains the candidate marriage. A Peder Michelsen of Sveen appears in Opplandene lagting court journal 7 (1757, pc00000000382657). ¶4

Per (Peder) Michelsen of Volla, Lunner, Jevnaker parish. Father of Anders, baptised at Lunner on 24 Jun 1763 as son of 'Per Michelsen Volla' and Mari Hansdatter (Jevnaker church book 1752-1799, Digitalarkivet source 9335, canvas 68 left, p. 130). Probably the father of a Walborg baptised at Lunner on Palm Sunday, 23 Mar 1766, to '[Per?] Michelsen Holla[?]' and Mari Hansdatter (canvas 88 right, p. 169); the first name is overwritten in the register. ¶1
