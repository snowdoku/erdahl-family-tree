About the family

His parents are now named: Tron Jonsen ('Thron Joensøn') of Jelden and Birgitte Bjørnsdatter of Eide. His father's patronymic comes from the 1751 betrothal and the 1752 marriage entries, both of which call him Thron Joensøn of Jelden. ¶7

Life story

Baptism (Probable): 'Joen Thronsøn', son of Thron of 'Jelden el. Strømme', baptized 28 Dec 1751 at Grytten. That matches his residence at Strømme in 1782, although the census and burial ages put his birth about 1749. ¶5

His baptism, 28 Dec 1751, took place at Eid church (Eids kirke) in Grytten parish. The godparents were Halvor Joensøn of Mættit, Ole Larsøn of Dahle, Jacob Jacobsøn of Mitit, Maritte Pedersdatter of Hamre and Maritte Berntsdatter of Strømme. The clerk noted that his parents were betrothed and had been due to marry that same day; they in fact married on 4 Apr 1752. ¶6

He was therefore born out of wedlock in the narrow sense, the child of a betrothed couple whose wedding was delayed by about fifteen weeks - a common and unremarkable situation in Romsdal at the time, and not treated as illegitimacy by the parish. ¶8

Married Olava Knudsdatter 7 Dec 1782 at Grytten as 'John Trons.' of Strømme. ¶3

Named as father in the Grytten baptisms of Søren (1790) and Joen (1792) at Sætnes-Ødegård, with Olava Knudsdatter as mother. The link to John Johnsen is now Probable. ¶2

Note: his son Joen, baptised at Sætnes-Ødegården on 8 Sep 1792, is most likely the Jon Jonsen Setnes, bachelor and soldier, who married Aleth Rasmusdatter of Sauseth in Grytten church on 15 Oct 1816 (cautioner 'Jon Setnes', perhaps this Jon Tronsen), not John Johnsen Sætnes (see his page), who came from Lesja in 1822. If so, this couple is not in the direct line. ¶11

Landless cottar at Sætnes, Grytten, in the 1801 census (age 52, first marriage), with wife Olava Knudsdatter and sons Søren (10) and Jon (8). He is a speculative father of John Johnsen Sætnes. ¶1

Died 24 Dec 1817, a cottar (husmann) under Setnes, age 68; buried 11 Jan 1818. ¶4

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

2 on the grade

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Confidence lowered from Probable to Speculative (audit, 13 Oct 2026). The records about this person stand, but his identification as the father of John Johnsen Sætnes is now Speculative: see the note of the same date on John Johnsen Sætnes, which names the records (Grytten confirmation 19 Oct 1834, p. 495 no. 5, 'Anne Pedersd. fra Lesjøe'; Lesja marriage 13 Mar 1816, pp. 834-835; Grytten marriage of the soldier Jon Jonsen Setnes, 15 Oct 1816, p. 613). ¶9

Confidence adjusted: Speculative restored to Probable (13 Oct 2026). The lowering by audit expressed doubt about whether John Johnsen Sætnes is his son; under the rule that a person's confidence carries the link to his own parents, that doubt now sits on John Johnsen Sætnes (Speculative). His own level rests on his link to Tron Jonsen of Jelden and Birgitte Bjørnsdatter: the Grytten baptism of 'Joen Thronsøn', 28 Dec 1751 at Eid church, son of Thron of 'Jelden el. Strømme', which matches his residence at Strømme at his 1782 marriage. It stays Probable rather than Confirmed because the 1801 census age (52) and burial age (68) point to a birth about 1749. The earlier note calling his link to John Johnsen Probable is superseded: that link is Speculative. ¶10
