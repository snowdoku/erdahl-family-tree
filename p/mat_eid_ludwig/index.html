About the family

His daughter Joyce Orene's Social Security application gives her birthplace as a 'farm home, Deuel County, South Dakota' — the family farmed on the Dakota side of the line while worshipping and burying at Zoar. ¶3

Parentage CONFIRMED in print. Selbygbogen (Minneapolis, 1921) p. 150 lists the children of Mikal P. Eidem and Beret Eliasdatter Slind, from the family's own answers, and the seventh is 'LUDVIG, f. 30 Okt. 1907'. That is this man's recorded birth date to the day. The reassignment away from John P. and Oline Eidem, made on circumstantial grounds in an earlier wave, is now proved from a contemporary printed source, and his confidence moves from Probable to Confirmed. ¶12

Life story

Minnesota death index: born 30 October 1907, 'child of SLIND (mother)', died 6 April 1979 in Lac qui Parle County. Social Security number [number withheld] (South Dakota), so he took out his card across the state line. ¶1

1950 census: head of his own household in Deuel County, South Dakota, enumeration district 20-3, with his wife Lilla and daughter Joyce. ¶2

Named as father, with his wife's maiden name, on his daughter Joyce Orene Eidem's Social Security application of June 1952: FATHER: LUDWIG EIDEM, MOTHER: LILLA A HARKINS. NARA AAD, NUMIDENT Application (SS-5) Files 1936-2007 (Last Names E-G), rid 421621. A federal primary record of this marriage. ¶4

Who his parents were instead: the same death index entry gives his mother's maiden name as SLIND, so he belongs to one of the other Eidem-Slind marriages of this family. Lac qui Parle County marriage certificate B93398 records 'EIDEM, MIKAL' and 'SLIND, BERETH E.' on 22 March 1893, and that couple had crossed the state line into Antelope Valley Township, Deuel County, South Dakota by 1912, where their daughter Christina Paulina was born on 23 December 1912. A son born out of Minnesota in 1907 to a Slind mother fits them and fits no one else: the only other Eidem-Slind couple, Peder P. Eidem and Kari Slind, were enumerated in Augusta Township, Lac qui Parle County, in 1905 and their children are Minnesota births. ¶7

Burial: Zoar Lutheran Cemetery, Antelope Valley Township, Deuel County, South Dakota, lot 23, with his wife Lilla (d. 2002) and their son Donald Michael (d. 1940). The joint stone, visible in the background of the photographs of the Kathryn Hayter grave, is inscribed 'EIDEM' with 'LILLA A.' and 'LUDWIG' side by side. ¶10

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Rated Probable, not Confirmed: the reassignment rests on exclusion plus a good positive fit, not on a record that names his parents. Next record that would settle it: his Minnesota death certificate itself (1979-MN-008524, Lac qui Parle County), which names both parents, or his Social Security SS-5 application. ¶8

Corrections 2

Correction: Ludwig Eidem is not a son of John P. Eidem and Oline Slind, as this tree and the wave-2 dossier had him. Two records rule it out. First, John P. and Oline's daughter Cora Belinde was born on 25 September 1907, thirty-five days before Ludwig, and their next child Peder Conrad's birth certificate of October 1909 states 'number of child of this mother 5, number of children of this mother now living 5' - a sequence with no room for him and no infant death to make room. Second, the Minnesota death certificate index entry for Ludwig (1979-MN-008524) gives his birthplace as 'OUT OF STATE', while John P. and Oline were registering births in Mehurin Township, Minnesota, in 1904, 1906, 1907, 1909, 1911 and 1914. ¶6

Correction: Ludwig Eidem is NOT a son of John P. Eidem and Oline G. Slind. Oline's obituary, printed in October 1955 while Ludwig was alive and farming in the same township, names her surviving children — 'eight children, namely: Mrs. Julius Jornlin (Clara)... Mrs. Peder Lerseth (Olga)... Mrs. Elmer Peterson (Cora)... Mrs. Cle Kyllo (Barbara)... Mrs. Herbert Gieseke (May) and Palmer, Peder and John Jr.' — and Ludwig is not among them. He died in 1979, twenty-four years later, so he cannot have been omitted as predeceased. The wave-2 attribution rests on the Minnesota death index's mother's-maiden-name field reading 'Slind', but that does not point uniquely at Oline: this one colony holds at least three Slind wives — Oline G. Slind who married John P., Kari 'Carrie' Slind who became Karen P. Eidem and married Peder P., and Beret 'Betsy Slinde' who married Mikal. His parentage should be treated as unknown until a birth or baptismal record is found. ¶9

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

A candidate worth testing, offered as speculation only: the South Dakota state burial register shows an otherwise unknown Eidem household in Zoar lot 11 — GUNDER EIDEM d. 22 September 1958, ANNA EIDEM d. 23 March 1965, and two children who died small, IRENE EIDEM d. 1913 and GORDON A. EIDEM d. 1919. None of the four has a Find a Grave memorial. Ludwig (b. 1907), Joseph Eidem (b. 7 June 1910) and the register's unplotted ALVIN GORDON EIDEM (d. 1922) would all fit as children of that couple. ¶11

Searches that found nothing 1

Clean negative: Ludwig himself has no NUMIDENT record of any kind. He died 6 Apr 1979, which is inside the Death Master File's good coverage, so his absence from the Death file means his death was never reported to the Social Security Administration — which happens when a person was not drawing a benefit. ¶5
