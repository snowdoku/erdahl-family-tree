About the family

Probable father: Ole Povelsen of Ytre Esse (d. before May 1758). His probate names 'Povel Olsen Ytre Esse' as his eldest son. Paul Olsen of Ytre Esse who died 1758-62 is very probably that son: same farm, same name, and no second Paul Olsen in the Ytre Esse probate index for 1740-1810. ¶4

Mother: Probable Mari Hermundsdatter, his father's widow, who in 1758 moved in with 'sin Søn' Paul Olsen at Ytre Esse (Ole Povelsen's probate, A 7b fol. 545b). See Mari Hermundsdatter for what would confirm it. ¶6

Life story

The Ytre Sogn probate register has entries for both 'Paul Olsen, Ese Ytre' (protocol 7b, fol. 589b) and an elder 'Ole Paulsen, Ese Ytre' (7b, fol. 545b) in 1756-1763. This suggests a line Ole Paulsen (d. ~1756-63), then Paul Olsen (d. ~1756-63), then Ole Paulsen (d. ~1800). The father-son link to Ole Paulsen Esse rests on farm continuity and naming only; the probate texts have not been read. ¶1

A son 'Einer Paulsøn Esse' was baptised at Leikanger on 15 Nov 1760 (godmother Mari Hermundsdatter of Esse, godfathers Ole Bottelsen, Ole Olsen and Ole Hermundsen of Esse) and buried at Tjugum on 6 Jan 1761 (11566 c50 R, c53 R). The name Einer points to his wife Sigri's father (Einarsdatter). ¶7

Probate: Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll A 7b, fol. 589b, No. 29, read from the scan. Probate held 24-25 May 1762 at Ytre Esse after the late Pouel (Paul) Olsen. The heirs are his widow Sigri Einersdatter (patronymic uncertain) and their 2 children: Ole Pouelsen, aged 4 or 9 (digit uncertain), and Ingebor Paulsdatter, aged about 2. The estate was 311 riksdaler gross and 244 net. He appears as the adult eldest son 'Povel Olsen ytre Esse' in his father Ole Povelsen's probate of 6-8 May 1758 (A 7b fol. 545b, No. 56), where his widowed mother Mari Hermundsdatter moved in with 'sin Søn Pouls Olsen ytre Esse'; he was present as an heir, 'Poule Olsen Esse', when that probate closed on 14 Sept 1761. So he died between 14 Sept 1761 and 24 May 1762. ¶2

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Confidence moved Speculative to Probable. The 1762 probate shows a son Ole Paulsen at Ytre Esse who was born about 1753-1758. Ole Paulsen Esse (d. 1799/1800) is Ole Paulsen of Ytre Esse. Ole Paulsen Esse named his eldest son Paul, a daughter Ingeborg (the name of this Ole's sister) and a daughter Sigrid (this Ole's mother's name). New support: the 1804 probate of Peder Marchussen Tendingen (Ytre Sogn A 12, fol. 320b-321a, No. 46) names 'Farmoderen Sigri Berge' (paternal grandmother) of Anders Olsen Esse, son of Ole Paulsen Esse; so Ole Paulsen Esse's mother was a Sigri, as was Paul Olsen's 1762 widow. Still, these records do not state outright that the 1762 boy is the man who died in 1800. The 1772-1799 Ytre Esse records would settle it: probates, the mortgage book (pantebok) or a farm transfer to Ole Paulsen; so would Sigri Berge's own probate (closed 13 April 1803, Ytre Sogn A 12) if it names her son Ole Paulsen Esse or his children. ¶3

Confidence moved Probable to Confirmed: the probate of his widow Sigri Olsdatter Berge, 12-13 April 1803 (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll A 12, fol. 161b-163a, No. 9), names her first husband 'Paal Olsen' and their son 'Ole Poulsen, som boede og døde paa Gaarden Esse', husband of Ingerie Marchusdatter and father of Paal, Marchus, Anders, Sigrid, Ingeborg and Aase. This proves he was the father of Ole Paulsen Esse. Their daughter Ingeborg Paalsdatter had died before 1803 at Hjaastad[?], leaving a daughter Synneve Sjursdatter. ¶5
