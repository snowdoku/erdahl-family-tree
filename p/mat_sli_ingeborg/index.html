About the family

Wife of Ole Gundersen Slind and so Oline (Slind) Eidem's sister-in-law. Emigrated with her husband and six children on 7 June 1922: Trondheim police office emigrant protocol no. 15 (1916-1926), page 140, line 27, group 00883, sequence 663 - 'Ingeborg Slind', b. 1889, married, household position 'hustru' (wife), residence Selbu, destination 'U.S. Amerika', ticket paid Cash, with a minister's certificate (Pr.att.); ship Stavangerfjord, Norwegian America Line. ¶1

Wife of Ole O. Slind, the Eidems' neighbour. 1950 US census, Mehurin Township, Lac qui Parle County, ED 37-25, household 4: 'Slind, Ole O., head, W M 66, born Norway' and 'Ingeborg, wife, W F 65, born Norway'. The two of them were alone in the house. ¶8

Wife of Ole Gundersen Slind, born 24 January 1884, and so Oline Slind's sister-in-law. 1920 census for Selbu: 'Ingeborg Olsadatter Slind', born 20 January 1889 in Selbu, 'hu g Husmor' - wife, married, housewife - at Kjosnes sondre, gnr 151 bnr 2, in the census district named Slind. Her patronymic Olsdatter means her father was an Ole; her maiden farm name has not been established and is the obvious next thing to find, in the Selbu marriage register for about 1909. ¶11

Her children, from the 1920 census of her household: Gilbert born 1 August 1910, Ole born 30 March 1912, Johan and Kristine, twins, both born 21 February 1914, Johanne born 2 October 1916, and Bjarne born 17 April 1919. ¶14

Life story

1950 census: Minnesota, Lac qui Parle County, Mehurin Township, ED 37-25, sheet 2, line 10 - Ingeborg Slind, wife, W F 65, married, born Norway, column 14 'naturalized?' YES. Her stated age of 65 in April 1950 implies a birth about 1884-85 against the protocol's 1889; stated ages in the American censuses of elderly immigrants are commonly out by several years, and the household, the parish and the husband all agree, so the identification is Probable rather than certain. ¶2

Independently confirmed on Find a Grave, where the memorial had been sitting unsearched: "Ingeborg O KLEGSETH Slind, 20 Jan 1889 - 9 Aug 1930, Selbu Cemetery, La Crosse, Whitman County, Washington" (memorial 32434368). The birth date is to the day what the 1920 Selbu census gives her, and the maiden name is in the memorial's own title. Nobody had ever searched Find a Grave for this surname: a sweep of all 180 memorials worldwide carrying the exact surname SLIND finds 74 in Sor-Trondelag (Selbu) and nine at Selbu Cemetery, Whitman County. ¶21

Her grave is found, and it is in WASHINGTON STATE, not Minnesota: 'Ingeborg O Klegseth Slind, 20 JAN 1889 - 9 AUG 1930, Selbu Cemetery, La Crosse, Whitman County, Washington'. The birth date agrees TO THE DAY with the 20 January 1889 the 1920 Norwegian census gives her, and the maiden farm name KLEGSETH is independently corroborated by the 1920 household itself, which employed a nursemaid called Gurine Oline Olsdatter KLEGSETH - a kinswoman brought from the wife's home farm, which is what Norwegian farm households did. ¶22

Her maiden name is not recorded in either source. The Selbu marriage register for about 1908-1910 would give it, together with her father's name. ¶3

MERGE NOTE FOR THE COORDINATOR: this is the 'Ingeborg, wife, 65, born Norway' of household 4 in the 1950 census of Mehurin Township, Lac qui Parle County, Minnesota, and the 'Ingeborg Olsdatter Slind, b. 20 Jan 1889, Selbu' of the 1920 Norwegian census household at Kjøsnes søndre that selbu-slind transcribed. If selbu-slind creates her under their own prefix these two records are one person and should be merged. ¶4

HER MAIDEN NAME, which no record in this project had before: KLEGSETH. Her daughter's Social Security application — an original application the daughter signed in April 1971 — records 'MOTHER'S FIRST NAME: INGEBORE / MOTHER'S LAST NAME: KLEGSETH', the father on the same card being OLE G SLIND and the daughter's birthplace SELBU, NORWAY. NARA AAD, NUMIDENT Application (SS-5) Files 1936-2007 (Last Names E-G), rid 733121. Klegset / Kleggset is a farm in Selbu, so she is a Selbu woman named for her farm exactly as her husband was named for Slind. ¶5

This also explains a detail selbu-slind transcribed without being able to account for it: the 1920 census of Ole Gundersen Slind's household at Kjøsnes søndre lists a resident nursemaid (barnepike) called GURINE OLINE OLSDATTER KLEGSETH, b. 15 Jan 1906. A Klegseth girl minding a Klegseth woman's children is a kinswoman placed with family, which is how farm households were staffed — so Gurine is very likely Ingeborg's niece or young cousin, and the Klegseth family is now a line to work on in the Selbu registers. ¶6

KLEGSETH IS A SELBU FARM, and the connection is already visible in the Norwegian record. The 1920 Selbu census household at Kjosnes sondre, where she was living with Ole and the six children, contained a barnepike (nursemaid) named GURINE OLINE OLSDATTER KLEGSETH, b. 15 January 1906 - the same patronymic and the same farm name. She is almost certainly Ingeborg's younger sister. Her brother JORGEN O. KLEGSETH, b. 15 July 1900, followed the family to Colfax and died there on 25 January 1945; his own death certificate names the same parents, "Ole J. Klegseth" and "Johanna Klegseth". ¶18

She emigrated with her husband and all six children on 7 June 1922 on the Stavangerfjord of the Norwegian America Line, entered in the Trondheim protocol as 'Ingeborg Slind, b. 1889, married, of Selbu'. ¶12

She is almost certainly the 'Ingeborg, wife, 65, born Norway' of household 4, Mehurin Township, Lac qui Parle County, Minnesota, in the 1950 census. The stated age of 65 implies a birth about 1884-85 and is four years out from her documented date of 20 January 1889; her husband's stated age in the same entry is exactly right. The discrepancy is recorded rather than explained away. ¶13

HER MAIDEN NAME WAS KLEGSETH, and this is new. Her Washington death certificate names both her parents outright: "Ingeborg Slind, date of death 9 Aug 1930, age 41, female, death place Colfax, Whitman, Washington. Father name: Ole Klegseth. Mother name: Johanna Klegseth. Spouse name: Ole Slind." Washington State Dept. of Health, Death Certificates July 1 1907-2001, DA reference {014166D8-26AF-41F2-BFE0-BB161678D2D3}, image 207, document 5, document reference id 154. An age of 41 in August 1930 agrees with the 20 January 1889 birth date the 1920 Selbu census gives her. ¶15

Confirmed independently, sixty-three years later: her son Gilbert's Washington death certificate (DE_1993011918, Skagit County, 5 April 1993, aged 82) records Fatherlastname SLIND and MOTHERMAIDENNAME KLEGSETH. A third record agrees - her daughter Kristine's Social Security SS-5 application names her mother as "INGEBORE KLEGSETH". Three records, filed in 1930, 1971 and 1993 with no possible contact between them, all say Klegseth. ¶16

Death and burial: she died at Colfax, Whitman County, Washington on 9 August 1930, eight years after landing in America, aged 41, and is buried at Selbu Cemetery, Whitman County, on a stone shared with her husband and his second wife ("Ingeborg O Slind, b. 1889 d. 1930, s/w Ingeborg A and Ole G", reference hrp-cem-whi-selbu_0122). She left six children, the youngest eleven. ¶19

She died 9 AUGUST 1930, eight years after the family landed, aged forty-one, leaving six children of whom the youngest was eleven. Her husband appears to have remarried: buried in the same churchyard is 'Ingeborg A Mogard Slind, 1884 - 1959'. ¶23

This is where the Selbu gaardshistorie's stray '1930' belongs. The book's paragraph for this family, as reconstructed from the National Library's word coordinates, puts a 'd. 1930' near the line for her husband Ole, which contradicted his gravestone of 1975 and looked like a serious problem. It is her death year, sitting in the same paragraph. Recorded because the apparent contradiction is the sort of thing that derails a line, and because it is a caution about the limits of reading a page from word coordinates. ¶24

Not in the 1940 Alien Registration index. The only Slind woman in it of near the right age is OLINA SLIND, b. 1886 Norway, A4509120, registered at Minneapolis — a different person. Ingeborg had either naturalised or, more likely given the family arrived only in 1922, was registered under a spelling the Flexoline index does not key as Slind. ¶7

Corrections 2

Correction: the middle initial on her gravestone, "Ingeborg O Slind", is not a given name but her patronymic OLSDATTER - she was Ole Klegseth's daughter. The tree previously carried her as "Ingeborg Olsdatter /Slind/" with Slind in the byname slot; Slind was her married name, Klegseth her own farm name. ¶17

Correction: the four-year discrepancy that selbu-slind recorded - the 1950 census making Ole's wife "Ingeborg, 65" when the Norwegian records give Ingeborg Olsdatter 20 January 1889 - was not a census error. Ingeborg Klegseth had been dead for twenty years in 1950. The woman enumerated beside Ole was his SECOND wife, Ingeborg Andersdatter Mogard, b. abt 1884, who was indeed sixty-five. ¶20

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Her maiden name is not established. The most promising candidate in the Lac qui Parle marriage index is certificate F13170, 'SLIND, BERSVEND' and 'HARSTAD, INGEBORG BERTINA', 5 July 1913 - the only Ingeborg to marry a Slind in the county - but the groom's name is Bersvend and not Ole, so this would require her to have been widowed and remarried, and nothing yet supports that. There is no Ole Slind marriage in the Lac qui Parle index at all, and there is no Ingeborg Slind in the Minnesota death index 1904-2001 under any spelling of the surname, so she probably died in South Dakota. ¶9
