About the family

Mother of Martha Olsdatter: in a woman's estate the heirs are her own children (stepchildren do not inherit), so Martha Olsdatter, wife of Alf Rasmussen Blindem, is her daughter. Her own parents are not found; her patronymic shows her father was named Peder. ¶2

Father, Speculative: the 1701 manntall (male census) for Uggedall lists Peder, 62 (born about 1639), leaseholder and former soldier, with his son Mads Pers., 16. A Peder Madtzen of Uggedal was an engagement witness in 1699, and Mads Pedersen of Uggedal married in 1712 and was still there in 1720-31. Martha, born about 1688 and marrying a man of Uggedal in 1714, fits as Peder's daughter and Mads's sister, but no record names her father; the marriage gives no residence for her. A probate after Peder of Uggedal is not indexed. ¶4

Life story

Burial (index): Martha Pedersd. of Uggedal was buried at Borgund on 19 Nov 1741, aged 53 (born about 1688; pg00000002546285). Her probate followed in spring 1742. She was engaged as 'Pige Martha Persdtr.' to Ole Knudsen of Uggedal on 5 Jul 1714 and married him 1 Jan 1715 (pv00000010161808, pv00000002827319). ¶3

Godmother (scan read): 'Marithe Pedersdatter Uggedal' stood godmother with Mads Uggedal and Ole Knudsen Uggedal at the baptism of the twin daughters Marthe and Johanna of Thore Andersen Halsnesholmen, Saturday 20 Apr 1720 (Borgund 15995 canvas c152). Her father is probably Peder Madsen Uggedal, first godfather to her daughter baptised 20 Oct 1715 (c128). ¶5

Probate (scan read): 'Anno 1742 den 11 Ma[rtii or ji]' (the index gives 17 Apr 1742) a probate was held at Uggedal, Borgund skipreide, after the late Marthe Pedersdatter (the index reading; the underlined first name is hard to read), between the widower (Enkemanden) Ole Knudsen on one side and the 3 children on the other: (1) a son [Knud?] Olsen, who has since died ('ved Døden er afgangen'), represented by his heirs; (2) Peder Olsen, aged 20, with curator Peder Hansen [?]; and (3) 'Martha Olsdatter gift med Alf Rasmussen Blindem' (Martha Olsdatter, married to Alf Rasmussen of Blindem). Fol. 142a, viewer image 24555/156 (urn sk20090126630568). ¶1
