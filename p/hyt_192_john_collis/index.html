Life story

John Collis, son of William Collis, an officer in Hierome Sankey's regiment of horse in Cromwell's army. He married Mary, daughter of Philip Corridon of Kilmacaha, and was the father of William Collis of Lisedoge (Lissodigue), Co. Kerry (Burke, Landed Gentry of Ireland, 1912, 'Collis of Tieraclea'). Added. ¶1

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Speculative, graded on his link to William Collis: the only source is Burke's lineage, which gets the next generation's marriages wrong. ¶2a

Searches that found nothing 2

Searched, with nothing new found, so the grade stays Speculative. Hickson's Old Kerry Records, second series (1874), has no free scan or full text on archive.org; only the first series (selectionsfromol00hick) is there. All the Collis rows in the Irish Registry of Deeds index for 1708-37 were reviewed: no John Collis appears as father of William of Lisodigue. In 1713 (memorial 6291) William is styled 'William (Sen)' of Lisodiege, and no older Collis appears in Kerry in the index. MacLysaght (More Irish Families) notes a Collis already provost of Tralee by 1661, but does not name him. ¶3a

Searched with no result: Kerry Archaeological Magazine vol. 1 (1908) has no Collis or Corridon entry; Hickson's first series names only the next generations (the 'John Collis' married to Elizabeth Cook of Cork in the Blennerhassett Pedigree is William's son, not his father); the Registry of Deeds index 1708-60 has no Corridon in Kerry at all (only Robert Corridon in Donegal, 1733, and Nicholas Corridan in Cork city, 1727). Pender's Census of Ireland c.1659 is lending-only on archive.org. Grade stays Speculative. ¶4a
