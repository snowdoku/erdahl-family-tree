About the family

Family: wife Hester/Esther; children baptised at the First Church or born in the Boston register include Mehetabel, William, Hanna(?) (bapt. 19 May 1650; OCR 'liana'), Jeremiah (several of this name, two died young), Sarah, John (bapt. 2 Dec. 1655), Nathaniel, Mary (m. Nathaniel Greene) and Rachel (bapt. 16 Dec. 1660). Elizabeth (born about 1638) was born before the parents joined the church and has no baptism there. Whether Hester was Elizabeth's mother is not shown by any record read (the Endicott memoir says so, compiled); the First Church list marks 'Hester Houchin dead'. She is therefore not entered as Elizabeth's mother. ¶4

Father entered: William Houchin of Harleston, Norfolk, from compiled abstracts of Jeremiah's 1670 will (Knoff p. 131; Sumner pp. 110-111). Birth ABT 1616 from his deposition of 1665 'aged about 49' (Sumner, compiled; the deposition itself not read). Sumner also says he came over in 1635 with wife Esther and perhaps one or two children; the will left to 'Elizabeth, wife to Mr James Allen', Esther wife to Samuel Wheelwright, and Mary wife to Nathaniel Greene (Sumner's abstract). If the will text confirms 'Elizabeth wife to Mr James Allen', it also settles the Endicott-widow = Mrs Allen identification noted above. ¶6

Life story

Baptism lead and mother: no record names Jeremiah's mother. Candidate baptism, not entered: Rickinghall Superior, Suffolk, '1 Dec. 1617 - Jeremy s. of William Houchin' (History of the Houchin Family p. 9, a compiled extract; the register was not seen; the extract gives no mother's name, and the printed extracts give mothers' names only from 1657). It is supported by a will of 'Howchin, Wm. (sen.)' of Rickinghall Superior registered at Bury St Edmunds c.1652 (register 'Ashton' fol. 587, calendar page image read), a tanner Robert Howchin at Rickinghall Superior in 1618 (Suffolk Archives FB121/G5/3), and a William Howchen there who named a son Jeremy in 1647; against it, the compiled tradition places the father at Harleston, Norfolk. His deposition of 29 Jan. 1664[/5] gives his age as 'about forty nine' (Col. Soc. Mass., Dec. 1918, transcript read), i.e. born about 1615-16. Birth left as ABT 1616. ¶9a

Life: tanner of Boston (Suffolk Deeds I, 'Jerremy Houchin of Boston Tanner'); freeman 13 May 1640 (NEHGR 5:401, compiled); admitted to the First Church in Boston with 'Hester his wife' in 1644; member of the Ancient and Honorable Artillery Company 1641; Boston selectman in the 1640s-50s; deputy for Hingham in the General Court in most sessions 1651-1660 (Lincoln, History of Hingham); present at the General Court's settlement of Gov. Endecott's estate, 23 May 1666 (Essex probate records 2:40). Compiled sources (Roberts, AHAC; NEHGR 5:401) make him son of William Houchin of Harleston, Norfolk; not checked against an English record. ¶3

Death: died at Boston in 1670, after 7 April and before the probate of his will on 31 May 1670 (Knoff p. 132, snippet only; will not read). ¶5

Will details: wife Esther gets household goods and the life use of the dwelling house, four-acre pasture, tanyard, leather, hides and pits; son Nathaniel (£400 and a double share, plus 'all my Lands, Housing and Estate due and appertaining unto me by Will and Testament of my deceased Father William Howchin left unto me by him', in tail male) and daughter Rachel (£200) under 21; daughter Esther wife of Samuel Wheelwright £60; daughter Mary wife of Nathaniel Green £20 for her care in his sickness; Mr John Westgate of Harleston, Norfolk, £10 and appointed executor for the English estate; executors in New England: wife Esther, sons-in-law Nathaniel Green and Samuel Wheelwright. On 25 Nov. 1693 Green and Wheelwright told the probate court that 'their mother in Law Esther Howchin', co-executor, who alone administered, was dead, and took administration d.b.n. The will does not say which wife was mother of which child. ¶8

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 3

Graded on his link to Elizabeth Houchin. The Boston town register (printed transcript, Record Commissioners vol. 9, 1653 marriages) enters: 'John Endicott son and heir to the worshipful John Endicott was married to Elizabeth Houchin daughter of Jeremiah Houchin of Boston 9th - 9th month by Richard Bellingham' (9 Nov. 1653). The record names the bride's father. John Endecott Jr.'s will, recorded at Boston 24 Feb. 1667[/8], also names 'my ffather in Lawe Jeremiah Howchin' as an overseer (Essex Inst. Hist. Coll. 25, 1888; FTS snippet read). ¶1

Why Probable, not Confirmed: the step from Elizabeth (Houchin) Endicott, widowed Feb. 1667/8, to Elizabeth, wife of Rev. James Allen and mother of 'John of James & Elizabeth Allen' (b. 29 Feb. 1671/2) and Jeremiah (b. 29 Mar. 1673), rests on compiled sources: Savage (via Knoff p. 131: she married Allen 'before 21 June 1669'), Perley (Essex Inst. Hist. Coll. 51:375: John Endecott Jr. devised the Bishop farm in Salem Village to his widow Elizabeth Howchins; she married secondly Rev. James Allen, who sold it to Francis Nurse 29 Apr. 1678), and the Endicott memoir (the widow of John Endecott Jr. lies in Allen's tomb in the Granary, where the stone reads 'Mrs. Elizabeth Allen wife of Mr. James Allen Aged 35 years Deceased April ye 5 1673'). Her age at death (born about 1638) fits Elizabeth Houchin. No rival Elizabeth is known: Allen's first wife Hannah (Dummer) died 26 Feb. 1667/8 and his third wife was Sarah. What would confirm: Jeremiah Houchin's will (proved 31 May 1670, Suffolk Probate; Knoff p. 132; queued as S0237) if it names 'my daughter Elizabeth Allen', or the Essex deed of 1678, Allen to Nurse (Essex Deeds book 4 leaf 189; queued as S0238), if it recites the title from his late wife Elizabeth. ¶2

Upgrade: graded on his link to Elizabeth Houchin. His own will, read in an attested copy filed in Suffolk Court Files vol. 36 case 3126 (DGS 008293221 images 41-42), made by 'Jeremy Howchin of Boston in ye Massachusetts Colony of New England this seventh day of Aprill... one thousand six hundred and seventy', divides the residue 'among all the other Children, namely, Elizabeth the wife of the Reverend James Allen, Esther the wife of Samuel Wheelwright, Mary the wife of Nath[aniel] Green & Rachel Howchin', and names 'my said Son in Law Mr James Allen' an overseer (with Anthony Stoddard and Simon Lynde). Proved by Robert Howard and Samuel Shrimpton '31 of the 3d m[onth] 1670' before Maj. Gen. John Leverett, Maj. Eleazer Lusher and Edward Tyng. The will NAMES the relationship (daughter Elizabeth, wife of Rev. James Allen), which settles the identification of the widow Elizabeth (Houchin) Endicott with Mrs Elizabeth Allen that kept him Probable; the earlier 'Why Probable' note is superseded. Continuity: the 1653 marriage names Elizabeth daughter of Jeremiah Houchin; John Endecott Jr.'s will (1667/8) calls Jeremiah his father-in-law; Jeremiah's 1670 will calls her wife of Rev. James Allen; no rival daughter Elizabeth is named. ¶7
