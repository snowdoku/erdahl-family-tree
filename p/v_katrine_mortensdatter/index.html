Life story

Marriage: Not in Borgund. Every page of banns and marriages in the Borgund pastor's register (ministerialbok 16003) from early 1808 to about July 1814 was read on the scan (canvases 23-34), and the clerk's copy (klokkerbok 16007) for 1811-1812 was checked too. Neither has a marriage of Knud Larsen of Lille Nørve or of any bride with the patronymic Mortensdatter. Knud Larsen appears in these years only as a marriage witness (forlover), in 1810 and 1811. Because couples normally married in the bride's parish, this strengthens the view that Katrine came from outside Borgund (the Ålesund town church book is the first place to look), and that the marriage happened between about 1808 and 1814. ¶6

Still unresolved. No baptism, confirmation or marriage to Knud Larsen (about 1812-1814) is indexed for any Katrine/Cathrine/Karen Mortensdatter in Borgund, Giske, Vigra or Haram. No matching girl appears in the 1801 census for Møre. Knud Larsen of Lille Nørve had first married Anne Maria Larsdatter of Ytterland 1 Jan 1802. The 1815 household also had a servant, Morten Ellingsen (17), in the neighbouring household. Her marriage probably needs to be read in the scanned Borgund register for 1812-1814. ¶3

Still unresolved after a systematic search. Every record indexed on Digitalarkivet for a woman with the patronymic Mortensdatter in Borgund parish was checked; only two belong to her - the 1815 census, where she is 'Katrina Mortensdatter', wife, born about 1789, at Lille Nørve, and her burial on 17 October 1850 as 'Katreine Mortensd.', retired widow (kaarenke) of Lille Nørve, with birth year calculated as 1792. There is no baptism, no confirmation and no marriage entry for her anywhere in the Borgund index, and no Katrine, Cathrine or Karen Mortensdatter of the right age in any neighbouring parish index. ¶4

This pattern - an adult appearing from nowhere in a parish - usually means she was born outside it. The next sources are the scanned Borgund marriage register for 1812-1814 (her marriage to Knud Larsen would name her father and her home farm) and, failing that, the Ålesund town and Vigra registers. ¶5

Marriage not in Borgund 1814-1815 either (13 Oct 2026): the banns pages (Forlovede) of Borgund ministerialbok 16003, canvases 34-36 (11th Sunday after Trinity 1814 to 21 Oct 1815), were read on the scan; no Knud Larsen of Nørve and no bride Katrine/Catharina Mortensdatter. With the earlier reads (canvases 23-34, 1808 to July 1814), the Borgund banns are negative from 1808 to Oct 1815. As her daughter Berte Marie was born about Oct 1814, the marriage was probably about 1812-1814 in another parish (likely her own). ¶12

Godparents of her children (scan read): Borgund klokkerbok 1816-1827 (source 16011). (1) Ole Martines, born 12 Jul 1816 and baptised in church in July (p. 2, boys no. 1). The parents are Knud Larsen of Nørve and Catrine, gaardbruger (farmer). The godparents are of the town gentry: 'Hr C. L. K…' (surname illegible), '… Simonsen' or Simonsdatter, 'Hr Nathan', '… Lindholm', 'Madme A. E. …' and 'Jomf[ru] Elisabeth …'. (2) Andrine Maria Laurentze, born 30 Oct 1818 and baptised on the 25th Sunday after Trinity (p. 33, girls no. 32). The parents are 'Jordb. Knud Larsen og h. Katrine', Lille Nørve. The first godparent is Peder Knudsen, Lille Nørve; the next is Arne (or Arnt) A…. The remaining four or five names are too faint to read. The hand on both pages is cramped and the readings are partial. ¶9

No Mortensen or Mortensdatter can be read among the godparents of either baptism, so these entries give no sign of a brother such as Christopher Mortensen standing for her children. On the 1818 entry the lower names are illegible, so a Mortensen there cannot be ruled out. The third baptism, Ingeborg Andrine Sicilia (3 May 1822), was not found. Canvas 50 (pp. 95-96) holds Dec 1821 to Jan 1822, so May 1822 lies in a later canvas, about 52-55. ¶10

Probate (searched, not named): the probate of 25 Nov 1817 at Lille Kalvø after the farmer (gaardmand) Morten Gregoriussen (Nordre Sunnmøre skifteprotokoll 5, fol. 44a) was read on the scan. It names his widow Ellen Christophersdatter and three heirs: the eldest son Christopher Mortensen (surname partly illegible; probably the Christopher Carolus Mortensen of Lille Kalvø who married in 1815, per the marriage index), married and of age; a younger son, given name uncertain (Charlinus?), Mortensen, 22, in service elsewhere; and the daughter Marie Elisabet Mortensdatter, 19. No daughter Katrine or Cathrine, and no Knud Larsen of Nørve, is named. This weighs against Morten Gregoriussen of Lille Kalvø as her father, although the following page (image 46) was not read. The reading is partial (cramped hand); the second son's name and the guardians' names are uncertain. ¶8

Godparents of her daughter Ingeborg Andrine Sicilia (scan read): Borgund klokkerbok p. 102, girls 1822 no. 20. Born '[1]3 Mai 1822' - the index says 3 May, and a stroke before the 3 may be a 1. Baptised on the 5th Sunday after Easter (12 May 1822). Parents 'Gm. Knud Larsen Nørve og Kone Catrine Mortensdr.' (Gm. = gaardmand, farmer). The godparents column is badly faded: 'Johan Brandt[?], Gunder [illegible], Mad. Nicoline Brandt[?], Anne R[illegible], [illegible] Knudsd[?]'. No Morten, Mortensen or Mortensdatter can be read, so this baptism gives no lead to her own family. ¶11

Buried 17 Oct 1850 as a retired widow (kårenke) of Lille Nørve; her birth year is calculated as 1792. Her own parents are not yet identified. ¶2

Lead (unproved): a 'Christopher Mortensen Lille Nørve' served as a marriage witness at Borgund about 1817-18, and a 'Christopher Mortensen Kalvøen' about late 1818 (Borgund ministerialbok 16003, canvases 45-46; cramped hand, reading probable). A Mortensen living on her own farm could be a brother. Nothing in either entry states kinship; the 1815 census for Lille Nørve and Kalvøy would test it. ¶7
