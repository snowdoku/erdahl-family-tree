Life story

Baptized 23 Aug 1840 at Torpo church, Ål parish, as Tosten Olsen, born 6 Aug 1840 (no. 117). Parents: Ole Tostensen, lodger (inderst), and his wife Randi Sølvesdatter, of Langerud (Løyte in Liabygda). The index gives the father as 'Ole Tolfsen', but the scan clearly reads Ole Tostensen. The church book's birth date of 6 Aug differs from the 1 Aug on his grave and in US records. ¶3

1865 census, Ål: age 26, unmarried day labourer at Langerud. Emigrated 1873 (WPA biography, 1938). ¶1

Possible lead, not yet linked: a Torsten Olsen, age 30, labourer, residence 'G. Aal', left Kristiania on 15 Apr 1870 bound for Fulton (Kristiania emigrant protocol). He may or may not be this Tosten, whose family biography says he emigrated in 1873. ¶5

Married Gunhild Underberg June 1889 near Grand Forks; lived at Devils Lake, then West Bank (Temple), ND. ¶2

1900 census (image read): at Center Township, Nelson County, North Dakota (ED 101, sheet 3B, enumerated 11 Jun 1900), dwelling 39: Tosten Olson, head, born Aug 1840 in Norway, age 59, married 10 years, immigrated 1870, 30 years in the U.S., naturalised, farmer renting a farm; wife Gunhild (Jun 1862), and children Henry (May 1891), Ole J. or T. (Oct 1892), Rachael (Aug 1894), Sam (Jul 1896) and Carl (Apr 1898), all born in North Dakota. FamilySearch and Ancestry index the head as 'Foster'; on the image the capital is the enumerator's looped T and the name reads Tosten. ¶6

Immigration year: the 1900 census gives 1870 (30 years in the U.S.), not the 1873 of his WPA biography. This supports the lead that he is the Torsten Olsen, 30, labourer of 'G. Aal', who left Kristiania on 15 Apr 1870. ¶7

1920 census (index only): in West Bank Township, Williams County, North Dakota, Ole T Olson (about 1893) and Inga T Olson (about 1895), both born in North Dakota, are indexed as children of 'Tosten O' and 'Sunhil' (Gunhild misread). The head's own row has not been found in the index; the image is queued (S0116). ¶8

1920 census, head found (image read): indexed under the farm name as 'Tosten O Langerude'. In ED 250, Williams County, North Dakota (sheet 14A, dwelling 253), Tosten Langerude, head, 79, married, born Norway, immigrated 1870, naturalisation column 'Pa' (first papers filed), year 'Un' (unknown), farmer on his own account, home owned with a mortgage; with his wife 'Sunhil' (Gunhild), 57, immigrated 1883, and Ole T. Olson 27, Inga T. Olson 25, Samuel 23, Carl O. 21, Alfred O. 20, Clara O. 19 and Thomas O. 13, all born North Dakota and surnamed Langerude except Ole and Inga. This supersedes the note that his own 1920 row was not found. ¶9

Immigration year: the 1920 census again gives 1870, agreeing with the 1900 census. Two census entries now give 1870 against the WPA biography's 1873, which further supports (but does not prove) the Kristiania emigrant of 15 Apr 1870. The 1920 'Pa' conflicts with 1900's 'naturalised'. ¶10
