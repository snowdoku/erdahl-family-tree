Life story

Origin, Probable lead: the 1801 census of Lien, Selbu, has a 'Brynil Jonsdatter', b. about 1787, among 'Deres boern' of Jon Thomassen (53, farmer) and Brynil Pedersdatter (47); her brothers and sisters there include Peder (b. 1777), Hælge (1789), Ingebrigt (1791) and Ingebor (1798). Ingebrigt Jonsen Lien and Helge Jonsdatter Lien stood godparents to her son in 1812, and Berith Johnsd. Lien to her daughter in 1815, which fits. Not added as a parent link: the census age (14 in 1801, b. about 1787) differs from 40 in 1825 (b. about 1785), and no record names her father. Her marriage, about 1806-1811 (Selbu marriages 1801-1815 are indexed), would settle it. A lead not opened: a Selbu burial index entry 'Brynhild Johnsd., b. 1786, Kaarenke, Langli, d. 14 Aug 1864' (pg00000004447062). ¶3

Named as the mother, 'Brynille Jonsdatter', at the baptism of her son Niels on Shrove Sunday 1812, with Ole Nielsen Langlie as father. ¶2

1825 census: residence 0047* Langlie, household 01, person 003 - 'Brynhild Johnsd.', 40, 'Hustru', wife of Ole Nilsen (46), in the household of her mother-in-law, the widow Guri Olsdatter. Eight children: Anne 18, Guri 16, Nils 14, Brynhild 11, Beret 8 1/2, Eli 5 1/2, John 3, Peder 1. ¶1
