Life story

Baptism of a daughter: the Borgund index has Anne, daughter of Ole Olss. of Skiong (Skjong), baptised 14 March 1734 (serial 18). The index also has an Ole Olss. of Schiong as a father on 25 June 1725. The mother and the godparents are not in the index and the page image was not read. ¶1

Probate (scan read): 'Anno 1749 den 16 Decemb.' a probate was held at Schiong (Skjong), Giske skipreide, after the late Ole Olsen, between his surviving widow Synneve Larsdatter, with her guardian Peder Rasmussen Sætre, and 'den afdøde Mands efterladte 6 Børn' (the deceased man's six surviving children): Ole Olsen, of age; Siur Olsen, a minor, guardian Peder Rasmussen Sætre; Barbra Olsdatter, married (husband's name partly illegible, [Hans...sen] of Giske?); Synneve Olsdatter, aged 2[1?], guardian Peder Andersen Schiong; Eli Olsdatter, aged 19, guardian Ole Olsen Schiong; and 'Anne Ols datter gammel 15 aar', guardian Ole Olsen Skiong. Fol. 77a, viewer image 24558/90 (urn sk20090126650090). ¶4

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

3 on the grade · 1 search with no result · 1 open question

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 3

Speculative father of Anne Olsdatter of Skjong (see her page). The evidence: in 1759 she was an unmarried maid ('Pige') of Schiong, and the 1734 Anne is the only Anne whose father, an Ole, is listed at Schiong/Skiong among the Borgund index rows read for 1725-1760; she would have been about 25 at her engagement, a normal age. Name, patronymic, farm and age all agree. Limits: the Schiong/Skiong searches were only partly read (pages 1-3 of 125 rows and page 1 of 81), and a maid's residence can be where she served rather than where she was born. In 1760 her legal guardian was Ole Jonsen of Giske-Ødegård, not her father, which may mean Ole Olsen was dead or simply that a neighbour acted. ¶2a

Audit: lowered from Probable to Speculative. The fit rests on one index entry for two very common names, and the search behind 'the only Anne' was only partly read. Skjong held several households at once. An index search for women named Anne with residence Skiong/Schiong finds an Anne Olsdatter churched there (introdusert, the mother's first church visit after a birth) on 1 September 1726 and on 17 January 1734. The 1734 churching came two months before Anne's baptism of 14 March 1734, so it belongs to another birth and probably to another household. The same search finds a married woman (hustru) Anne Olsdatter, born about 1693, recorded at Schiong as deceased on 18 October 1759, two weeks before the maid's engagement. Whether she was the maid's mother, and whose wife she was, is not known. The guardian in 1760 was not her father. Together these leave room for another Anne Olsdatter of Skjong. What would move it to Probable: the 1734 page image naming the mother, the 1759 burial entry, or Ole Olsen of Skjong's probate. ¶3

Upgrade: from Speculative to Probable. The audit named this probate as the record that would move him. It shows that the Ole Olsen of Skjong who fathered children in the 1730s had a daughter Anne aged 15 in Dec 1749, born about 1734, which matches the baptism of Anne, daughter of Ole Olss. of Skiong, on 14 Mar 1734. It also explains why Anne Olsdatter's guardian in 1760 was not her father: he had died by 1749. It is not Confirmed, because the probate does not mention Giske-Ødegård or Peder Olsen, and the Anne Olsdatter engaged in 1759 is tied to this family only by name, farm and age (about 25 in 1759). The widow Synneve Larsdatter is not called the children's mother (the formula is 'the deceased man's children', not 'their joint children'), so she is not added as Anne's mother. ¶5

Searches that found nothing 1

Negative: no son Peder is among the six heirs, so Peder Olsen of Skjong (b. about 1740, Peder Olsen Skjong) was not a son of this Ole Olsen, unless he died before 1749, which his later life rules out. ¶6

Open questions and the next record to read 1

What would confirm it: Ole Olsen of Skjong's probate naming a daughter Anne married at Giske-Ødegård, or Anne's burial with an age. ¶2b
