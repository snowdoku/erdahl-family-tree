Life story

Elder full sister of Tørri Jakobsdatter Tungsæt. She was born 15 Apr 1815 and baptized 30 Apr 1815 in Kvernes, daughter of Jacob Estensen and his wife, whose patronymic the index reads as 'Marithe Orisd.' - almost certainly a misreading of Gorrisdatter, the form the 1818 baptism of her sister Tørri uses. ¶1

In the 1825 census she appears as 'Berit Jacobsdatti', aged 10, in a four-person household at Kongshaug headed by a man the transcribers could read only as '... Esstensen', aged 33, with his wife Kristine Nielsdatter and their two-year-old daughter Maria. She was therefore not living with her own father at that date, which matches her sister Tørri being fostered at Tungset in the same census. Barbro and Berit are used interchangeably for the same name in these registers. ¶2

Her later life, marriage and death have not been traced. ¶3

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
