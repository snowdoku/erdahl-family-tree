About the family

Father of Peer (Peder Nielsen), born 22 Feb 1833 at Nordre Sunde, Madla, Håland, and of Thore (1821), Niels (1825), Anna Malene (1828) and an elder Peder (1831), all with Malene Nielsdatter (Håland baptisms, index; the 1833 entry read on the scan). He is called Niels Thorsen, huusmand (cottager), at Peder's confirmation in 1847, and was the groom's father at son Niels's marriage on 21 Jul 1847. ¶1

Parents (Probable): Thore Thorsen of Buchen and Anna Pedersdatter of Halsnøy, married in Fister church on 26 Jun 1768 (Hjelmeland marriages 1768, p. 233, no. 10). The only Tore/Thor of Buchen to marry in Hjelmeland 1763-1777; three Halsnøe godparents at Niels's 1777 baptism tie the mother's farm to him. The baptism itself names no parents. ¶13

Parents confirmed for the Buchen child: the probates of his mother Anna Pedersdatter (Lille Buchen, 10 Aug 1786, D 7b fol. 457a: 'Neels Toresen', 8, son of Tore Toresen) and of his maternal grandfather Peder Nielsen (Halsnøe, 19 Mar 1787, fol. 507a: 'Niels Thorsen', 9, son of Anna and Thore Thorsen Buchen) name the parents of the Niels Thorsen of Lille Buchen baptised on 16 Nov 1777. His siblings were Siri (b. about 1770) and Peder (b. about 1774). The siblings Samuel and Britta Thorsdatter noted earlier are NOT children of this marriage (only three children in 1786-87). The remaining weak point is the identification of this Buchen Niels with the Madla Niels Thorsen (1796 confirmation to 1816 marriage, then the move to Madla). ¶22

Father's probate: Ryfylke D 12 (DA 23895) fol. 142a, Lille Buchen, 2 Aug 1791, after Thore Thorsen: the heirs are the widow Karie Erichsdatter and the children Samuel (of age), Peder 18, 'Nils Torsen' 15 (guardian Neels Byre) and Siri 23 (m. Thore Clemensen). It confirms the Buchen family in 1791 but does not bridge the gap to Madla. ¶23

Life story

Baptism (scan read): Hjelmeland baptisms, church year 1777, p. 62, no. 48: 'Niels Torsen Buchen døbt d. 16 Nov' (16 Nov 1777); godparents Lars Vig[s]nes[?], Peder Halsnøe, Reier Halsnøe, Marthe Halsnøe, Aase Buchen. The entry names no parents: the father is a Thor/Tore of Buchen by patronymic only and the mother is not stated. Identified with the 1796 confirmand 'Niels Thorsen Buchen' as probable (same name and farm, age 18); no other Niels son of a Thor was found in baptisms 1778-1781 and 1782 nos. 6-58. ¶9

Probate check: Ryfylke probate D 8b (DA 23894, 1791-1795) is the Karmsund og Hesby protocol. Its register (views 382-391) covers only the skipreider of Karmsund og Hesby (Avaldsnes, Vardal, Talsø, Holte, Hausken, Ølsland and others), with no Hjelmeland section. So Thore Buchen's 1791 probate is not in that volume, and the Karmsund og Hesby volumes D 8a, D 8b and D 12 can be dropped from his search. ¶20

Birth (Speculative): a Niels Thorsen of Buchen was confirmed in Hjelmeland on 30 Oct 1796 (confirmations p. 191, no. 4, index, no age), which implies birth about 1779-1782 if he is the 1816 groom of the same farm. The 1801 census of Hjelmeland has an unmarried servant Niels Thorsen aged 22 at Meele (b. about 1779), not shown to be him. The Madla burial index of 1844 gives no age; reading the burial scan would test this. ¶5

Confirmation (scan read): Hjelmeland confirmations 1796, p. 191, under 'Den 23de Søndag efter Trinit. 1796' (30 Oct 1796), boys no. 4, 'Niels Thorsen Buchen'. The list gives names and farms only: no age and no parents. Baptisms in the same book for the church years 1778 (from no. 19) to 1781 (canvases 37-45, pp. 64-81) contain no Niels son of a Thor/Thore, so his birth year and parents remain unproved; 1778 nos. 1-18 and 1782-83 were not read. ¶8

1801 census (index): no Tore/Thor, Niels or Per Thorsen lives at Buchen or Buchen Indre in 1801 (all 26 residents in 5 households checked). His father had probably died or moved by then. A Niels Thorsen, 22, unmarried, servant at Meele, Hjelmeland, is probably him; his age implies birth c.1779, one to two years off the 1777 baptism. A Britha Thorsdatter, 22, servant in another Meele household, may be his sister Britta. Not adopted as proved. Other Niels Thorsens of Hjelmeland in 1801 were already married (b. 1765, 1768) or a widower (b. 1774), so they cannot be the bachelor who married in 1816. ¶11

Marriage: as 'Niels Thors.', a bachelor (ungkarl) living at Buchen, he married Malene Nielsdatter of Øye, an unmarried woman (pige), on 17 Nov 1816 in Fister church, Hjelmeland prestegjeld, after banns on the 14th Sunday after Trinity (Hjelmeland marriages 1816, pp. 256-257, no. 26, index; no ages, parents or witnesses are indexed). A week earlier, on 10 Nov 1816, Anna, daughter of Niels Thorsen and Malene Nielsdatter 'Buchen', was baptised in Hjelmeland. That this Hjelmeland couple is the Madla couple is Probable: both names agree, the bride lived at Øye in Hjelmeland, and the Madla widow Malene was born in Hjelmeland (1865 census). No indexed child of theirs falls in 1817-1820, the years when they presumably moved to Nordre Sunde in Madla (first Madla baptism 1821). A scan of the Madla baptisms or a moving-in entry would settle it. ¶4

Migration (scan): the Håland in-migration list (Tilgangs-Liste) for 1822, p. 627, enters together 'Hans Nilsen, Kisti Andersdatter, Neils Thorsen, Malin Nilsdatter' as arriving in the parish; the origin column apparently reads '... i Høylands Sogn' (Høyland parish), with the farm names and occupation only partly legible. This is a primary record of the couple's move into Håland. It was entered late (son Thore was baptised in Håland on 22 Jul 1821). It does not name Hjelmeland, so it neither proves nor contradicts the identification with Niels Thorsen of Buchen; it suggests the couple lived in Høyland parish for part of 1817-1820, the years with no known child. Hans Nilsen may be a brother of Malene (inference from the patronymic and the joint entry only). ¶17

Bukken byname: his parents-in-law's probate of 1824-25 (Ryfylke D 19, fol. 293b) calls him 'Niels Thorsøn Bukken', husband of Mallene Nielsdatter, 'boende paa en Plads under Gaarden S[u]nde... under Jæderen og Dalernes Skriveri'. So the Madla Niels Thorsen is the man from Bukken (Buchen) who married Malene of Øye in 1816; the Hjelmeland-to-Madla step is now a record, not an inference. What remains between him and the Buchen boy (son of Thore Thorsen, aged 15 in the 1791 probate) is the stretch from the 1796 confirmation at Buchen to the 1816 marriage at Buchen with no record naming him in between. ¶24

Death: a Niels Thorsen died 21 Dec 1844 and was buried at Madla on 26 Dec 1844 (burials p. 163, no. 35); he is the only Niels Thorsen in the Håland death index for 1844-45, but the index gives no age, residence or status, so the identification is Probable only. ¶6

Death: before the 1865 census, where Malene is a widow. A Niels Thorsen was buried at Madla on 26 Dec 1844 (died 21 Dec; burials p. 163, no. 35, index gives no age or residence), but that is not shown to be him; the 1847 entries name him without marking him as dead. His father was presumably a Thor (inferred from the patronymic). ¶3

Probable elder sister Siri Thors Datter baptised 1 Jan 1770 in Hjelmeland, godparents incl. Niels Halsnøe and Anna Buchen. The 1777 marriage of Tosten Børgesen Buchen and an Anna Pedersdatter (15 Jun 1777) shows no widow status and does not make Niels Tosten's son; no correction. ¶14

Probable father Thore Buchen buried 21 Jul 1791 in Hjelmeland, aged 60. Baptisms of probable brothers Samuel (c.1771-72) and Per (c.1774-76) not found in canvases 24-26 and 32; canvases 33-35 unread. ¶15

Upgrade: graded on his link to Peter S. Nelson. Gudmundsen' and 'Mathias Monsen'; the last column (Udvandringssted, place emigrated from) reads 'Haalands Præstegjeld Norge'. Bride 'Kari Monsdatter', 36, parents 'Mons Nilsen' and 'Anne Mathiasdatter'. The record names the relationship: the groom's father is 'Nils Sunde' and his mother 'Maline Nilsdatter', and the groom came from Haaland prestegjeld. Continuity: the groom is Peter S. Nelson of the tree (same marriage date and bride on his GAR card); the parents named are Niels Thorsen, cottager at Nordre Sunde in Madla annex of Haaland, and Malene Nielsdatter, whose son Peer was born there 22 Feb 1833, confirmed 1847 as Peder Nielsen of Nordre Sunde, and left for America in April 1857 (Madla ministerialbok). No rival couple named Nils and Maline/Malene Nilsdatter at Sunde in Haaland has been found. The 1857-1878 gap is bridged by the record itself naming the farm, parish and both parents, not by residence. ¶21

Corrections 1

Correction: the Madla burial of 26 Dec 1844 (Håland ministerialbok Madla 1842-1853, fol. 163, no. 35), which earlier notes offered as his probable death, is not his. The scan reads 'Niels Thorsen Inderstbarn | 2 Dage | Nord-Sunde': a lodger's child aged two days, died 21 Dec 1844 at Nordre Sunde. It was very probably a grandson named after him (a son of Thore Nielsen, b. 1821, or of Niels Nielsen, b. 1825, both of Nordre Sunde; the entry names no parents). His own death date is therefore unknown: after 21 Jul 1847 (named as the groom's father at son Niels's marriage) and before the 1865 census, where Malene is a widow. ¶16

Alternatives considered and set aside 4

Rejected namesakes: the Stavanger Niels Thorsen who married Agnete Olsdatter on 20 Nov 1817 (aged 26, of Eegvaag); the Niels Thorsens with wives Gunild Olsdatter (Stavanger 1820), Cesselia/Sissele Olsdatter (Hjelmeland 1813-1820) and the Jøssang family in Strand; and the 1816 silver-tax payer at Vareberg, whose entry is filed under Askøy parish. None has a wife Malene, and the 1816 marriage puts our man at Buchen. ¶7

Possible siblings (confirmations, names only, scan read): Siri Tors d. Büchen (1787, girls no. 51), Per Torsen Buchen (1791, boys no. 9), Britta Tors d. Buchen (179[3?], girls no. 23). Not linked: no parents or ages are given, and a 'Tore Buchen' stood godfather in 1777, so more than one Thor may have lived at Buchen. ¶10

More possible siblings (confirmation index, names only): Samuel Thoresen Buchen and Siri Thors. Buchen, confirmed 16 Sep 1787. Siri Thorsdatter, b. c.1770, was the wife of Lars Andersen at Buchen in 1801. These are not linked, because the entries name no parents. ¶12

Burial candidate, not adopted (dates sweep 2): 'Niels Thorsen', aged 64 (b. about 1777), died 14 Nov and was buried 21 Nov 1841 in Håland parish (Madla) (Håland ministerialbok 1815-1845, burials p. 155, no. 27; index pg00000006388905, also pg00000006377001). The age fits his birth (8 in 1786, 15 in 1791) exactly, and earlier searches covered only 1844 and 1847-1866. The index gives no residence or spouse, so it is unproved; read the scan of p. 155 for the residence (Sunde?) and status before adopting it. ¶27

Searches that found nothing 2

Death not found: the Madla burial index for 1847-1866 has no Niels/Nils Thorsen (deceased-role searches under Niels, Nils and Neils all checked). His widow Malene Nielsdatter was buried at Madla in 1868 (p. 245, no. 6). He may have died outside Madla, or be indexed without a patronymic. ¶19

Bridge search, negative: (1) The 'Peder Thorsen Skorpe' probate (D 17b, DA 23900, fol. 576b, No. 16, begun 21 Jan 1817) is after a leaseholder of Skorpe who left a widow (?Helga Siursdatter) and his own children: sons Nils Pedersen (eldest, married), Peder Pedersen (18) and Nils Pedersen the younger, and daughters including Siri and Anna Pedersdatter and an infant Ingeborg. With children of his own as heirs, no brother is named in the legible part of the heir list, so it does not bridge 1796-1816, and nothing shows that he is Niels's brother Peder (b. c.1774). (2) Siri Thorsdatter (widow of Lars Andersen, Bukken, 1824) has no probate in the name registers of D 19 (1822-27), D 20a/20b (1827-37; the D 20a register covers both halves) or D 21 (1837-45). (3) The 1801 census for Bukken was already (no Niels). The 1796-1816 gap stays open; the grade is unchanged. ¶25

Open questions and the next record to read 2

Lead: the Håland out-migration list for 1820 (p. 576, no. 6) has 'Kari Thors D., Sør-Sunde, 23, tjenestepige', moving to Fister parish (Hjelmeland). A Thor family at Søndre Sunde with ties to Fister may explain why Niels Thorsen of Buchen settled at Nordre Sunde, but Kari (b. c.1797) cannot be a daughter of Thore Buchen (d. 1791), and her father is unidentified. ¶18

Next records for the bridge: the Sjernerø/Fister legdsrulle (conscription roll) c.1800-1815 for a Niels Thorsen Buchen, and a deed or bygsel on Lille Buchen 1791-1816 in the Ryfylke mortgage register II A 2 (DA 23069) naming Thore's sons. ¶26
