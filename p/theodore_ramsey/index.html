About the family

Siblings: Torjus (1875), Turi 'Tilde' Oline (1877-1914), Anne Theoline Tweed (1880-1937), Gunbjorg Tomine Rusley (1881-1948), Thonette Alvina (1884-1904), Ida Sophia Amundson (1889-1975), Oscar Toye (1892-1975). ¶4

CHILDREN: four. Beryl Phyllis (2 Feb 1918 - 15 Apr 2015), who married Rev. Lloyd Vernon Sand and served as a Lutheran missionary in Cameroun; Wava Lorene (19 Jun 1922 - 17 Jan 2018), who married Palmer Valder; Dean Ulysses (March 1927 - 6 Jun 1927), who died at three months; and Lynn M. (20 Apr 1929 - 20 Oct 2010), who stayed on the original Ramsey farm. His obituary names "an infant son, Dean" among those who predeceased him. ¶14

SIBLINGS AT HIS DEATH. The obituary says he was predeceased by "one brother, and four sisters". The one brother is Oscar Toye Ramsey, who had died just six days earlier, on 26 July 1975; the four sisters are Turi (d. 1914), Thonette (d. 1904), Anne Tweed (d. 1937) and Gunbjorg Rusley (d. 1948). The count implies his sister Ida Sophia Amundson was still alive on 1 August 1975, and that the eldest brother Torjus (b. 1875) had died in infancy and was not counted. ¶15

Life story

Farmer, rural Joice, Worth County, Iowa. ¶1

Baptized 'Theodor Ulysses' in the Winnebago County Norwegian Lutheran church book; father recorded as Tellef Torjusen. ¶2

The obituary gives his life in outline: born 25 July 1886 on a farm near Joice to Tellef and Ingeborg (Heimdal) Ramsey; baptised and confirmed by Pastor Dahl; married Wilma Nelson at Albert Lea on 1 October 1913; farmed near Joice until 1952, then moved into Joice; about 1955 took a second farm just west of Beaver Creek Church, where he lived until about 1965, then returned to Joice. (The two middle dates are damaged in the scan and should be treated as probable.) ¶7

GODPARENTS: none are recorded. The Digitalarkivet entry for his baptism is a transcription by Lars E. Øyane of the Winnebago, Iowa church book 1864-1891, and that transcription captured only the child and the two parents (father "Tellef Torjusen", mother "Ingeborg Thorafsen"); the baptism date, birthplace and page number are all blank and no sponsors were keyed. No page images are served for this source. The sponsors can only be recovered from the original Winnebago parish register, held at the ELCA Region 3 Archives, Luther Seminary, St. Paul, Minnesota. ¶9

MARRIAGE RECORD FOUND. The Minnesota Official Marriage System indexes the marriage of RAMSEY, THEODORE U and NELSON, WILMA ALMIRA in Freeborn County on 1 October 1913, certificate reference Book F, page 258. This gives Wilma's full middle name, Almira. The register return itself, which would give both parties' ages and residences, is with the Freeborn County Recorder in Albert Lea. ¶10

CHURCH AND CIVIC LIFE. He was baptised in the Winnebago congregation and confirmed at Beaver Creek, where his father had served on the 1889 building committee; by the 1930s the family worshipped at Bethel Lutheran Brethren, rural Joice, where both his and Wilma's funerals were held. In July 1963 the Lake Mills Graphic lists him among the officers of the bank at Joice. ¶13

CENSUSES. 1900, Fertile Twp., Worth Co., in his father's house, aged 13, at school; the household also contains his father's sister Annie Ramsay (b. April 1850, immigrated 1863) and his maternal grandmother Gundbyr Heimdal (b. June 1827, immigrated 1862). 1905 Iowa state census, Fertile Twp., card 115, address Joice. 1910, Fertile Twp., family 110, aged 23, single, still at home. 1920, Fertile Twp., family 175, head of household, aged 33, farmer, with wife Wilma (24) and daughter Beryl (1). 1930, aged 43, farmer, with Wilma (35), Beryl (12), Wava (7) and Lynn (11 months); his parents Tollef (76) and Ingeborg (76) were then living with his brother Oscar nearby. 1940, family 31, aged 53, farm operator, home OWNED, with Wilma (45), Beryl (22, a rural school teacher), Wava (17) and Lynn (11). 1950, enumerated 11 April, house 105, "Theodore U Ramsey", 63, farm, with Wilma (55) and a 19-year-old Acey Haroldson, probably a hired hand. ¶11

LAND. No federal land patent was issued to any Ramsey in Fertile or Bristol Township — the government land there had all gone by 1867, before the family could buy, so they bought improved land privately. The 1913 plat of Fertile Township shows "T. T. Ramsey — 139.28" acres and an adjoining "Mart[h]a Ramsey — 60" acres in Section 7, with "O. T. Hiendal — 79.79" (Heimdal) just north and Beaver Creek Church immediately east on land given by the Bang family. By the 1930 plat the Ramsey holding in Section 7 was 189.66 acres with a further 87.28 acres in Section 18. The map confirms the obituary's phrase about a farm "just west of Beaver Creek Church". ¶12

Marriage: The Lake Mills Graphic of 1 October 1913 (p. 5) reports that he married Miss Wilma Nelson at Trinity Lutheran church in Albert Lea, Minnesota, by Rev. Edward Nervig, at 9:30 a.m. on Wednesday 1 October 1913. The couple, 'both from Joice and... of the most highly respected young folks of that community', left on a honeymoon trip to Battle Lake, Minnesota. ¶17

1942: He gave his daughter Wava away at her wedding to Palmer Valder at Zoar Lutheran church, Lake Mills, on 14 February 1942, and the reception was held at his home (Lake Mills Graphic, 18 Feb 1942, p. 6). ¶19

1950 census: Fertile Twp., 'Theodore U. Ramsey', 63, farmer. Married Wilma A. Nelson about 1917. ¶3

DEATH DATE ESTABLISHED (10 Oct 2026): Friday 1 August 1975, at his home in Joice, aged 89. His gravestone and Find a Grave give only "Aug 1975". His obituary says he "died Friday morning at his home in Joice" and that the funeral was on Monday 4 August; in 1975 the only Friday three days before Monday 4 August was Friday 1 August. ¶5

FUNERAL AND BURIAL: 2 p.m. Monday 4 August 1975 at Bethel Lutheran Church south-west of Joice, Pastor Morris Vold officiating, with burial in the family plot at Beaver Creek Cemetery. ¶6

CONTRADICTION over the baptism church. The obituary says he was "baptized and confirmed by Pastor Dahl at Beaver Creek Lutheran Church", but the Beaver Creek congregation was not organised until 15 February 1889, two and a half years after his birth. The baptism must have been in the mother congregation, Winnebago, which is exactly what the Digitalarkivet church-book entry shows. The confirmation, about 1900, will have been at Beaver Creek. Rev. J. M. Dahl was the Winnebago parish pastor from 1873 to about 1906 and chaired the meeting that founded Beaver Creek, so he plausibly performed both. ¶8

Corrections 1

Correction: The statement in his 1950-census note that he married Wilma 'about 1917' is superseded. The Minnesota marriage index (Freeborn County, Book F p. 258) and the 1913 newspaper report both give 1 October 1913. ¶18
