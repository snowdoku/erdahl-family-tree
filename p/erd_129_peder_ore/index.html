About the family

Father of Siri (Sigri) Pedersdatter: on 8 Feb 1765, at the probate after her husband Marcus Larsen Thue, she produced the probate letter after her father 'Peder Johannesen(?) Øre', held at Øre in Indre Sogn on 8 Nov 1727 (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll L0008a fol. 249b-250a, 24165 view 252; reading). The patronymic is an uncertain reading. A daughter Kari Pedersdatter died at Øre by 10 Nov 1727, Siri her sole heir. ¶1

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
