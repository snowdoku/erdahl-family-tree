About the family

Second wife of Gunder Sivertsen. She married him at Ekne on 5 September 1847, entered as 'Pige Martha Erichsdatter Skaaraas', born in Skogn, aged 35, daughter of the farmer Erik Hansen Skaaraas. She had been vaccinated on the strength of Jacobsen's certificate of 31 August 1828. Her brother (probably, by patronymic and farm) Hans Erichsen Skaaraas was a witness. ¶1

Child: Gunelie Margrethe, born 2 July and baptised 28 August 1853 at Ekne, with the mother entered as Martha Eriksdatter of Kjølen (index only). ¶2
