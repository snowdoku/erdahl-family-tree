About the family

First husband of Tøri Olsdatter; known only from the patronymics of Aadne Nielsen (b. ~1785) and Anne Nielsdatter (b. ~1789) in the 1801 Ohland household. His son's name suggests his father may have been an Aadne, but that is speculative. ¶1

His patronymic confirms his father was an Aadne, the name of his son Aadne Nielsen (b. ~1785). ¶4

NEW SIBLINGS, and confirmation of his father's name. The card ends: "Avd.s bror: Gulloug Aadnesen Walleberg i Fyresdal" and "Angrim [Aadnesen] Engenes i Åmli" — his brothers Gullov Aadnesen of Valleberg in Fyresdal (Telemark) and Angrim Aadnesen of Engenes in Åmli. This is direct documentary proof that his father was named Aadne, which until now rested only on his own patronymic. ¶7

His son Aadne Nielsen, four years old in 1789, had his own estate settled in 1811, aged about 26. ¶8

Life story

Baptism (Probable identification): Purification Sunday (2 Feb) 1746, Åmli church — 'Festo Purif. Mariæ i Omblie döbt Aadne Larsön Gangseys barn Niels'; godparents Rolf[?] Baas[?], Lars Gangsei, Ole Østad's wife, Niels Gangsei's wife, Ambor Evindsdatter Gangsei (Åmli 1741-1775, 1746 no. 10, canvas 15; 'Anno 1746' heading on canvas 14). His brother Gullov was baptised to the same father in 1749. This makes him about 43 at death in 1789 (burial age read as 46, uncertain). No other Niels son of an Aadne was baptised Jul 1741 - Jul 1750. Mother inferred from the 1749 entry. ¶9

Probate of his father: the 10 Nov 1762 skifte at Hommedrum after Aane Larsen (Skp. 13a fol. 11b, read on the scan) names 'Niels Aanesen 17 aar' among the children of Aane Larsen and his widow Asbor Evendsdatter, with brothers Even, Angrim, Gullov and Ole and sister Guroe. ¶11

Married 27 Jun 1784 in Gievedal (Gjøvdal) church: ungkarl Niels Aadnesen Omland and pige Turi Olsdatter Omland (Åmli 1775-1796, marriages 1784 #11). ¶2

Buried 17 May 1789 at Gjøvdal: Niels Aadnesen Ohland, died 8 Apr 1789 (Åmli 1775-1796 p.128 #35). The age column appears to read 46 years (b. ~1743), but the alignment is not certain. ¶3

Probate read on the scan: the estate of 'Niels Aadnesen Oeland af Giervedals Sogn', begun 18 May and closed 18 Nov 1789, is in Skifteprotokoll nr. 16b fol. 257a (DA 24366 view 262). The card index (card 10107) cites it as 16a fol. 257. Heirs: the widow Torie Olsdatter, the son Aadne Nielsen, 4, and the daughter Anne Nielsdatter, 9 weeks old. Aadne's guardian was 'hans eneste Farbroder' (his only father's brother) Gulleug Aadnesen Wolsberg of Morgedal in Øvre Telemark, with Jacob Tellefsen of Øvre Ramse acting for him. Anne's guardian was her father's brother Angrim Aadnesen Engenes of Åmli parish. The widow's guardian (lauverge) was Gunsten Nielsen Omland, and the appraisers were Tellef Torsen Oeland and Ole Evensen Oeland. The two Aadnesen guardians, named as the children's father's brothers, show that Niels was a son of an Aadne. That matches the Gangsei card 3469, which names Angrim among the sons of Aadne Larsen (see his page). ¶10

Ohland/Omland is the same farm (Gjøvdal). Tøri remarried Tallak Erichsen by about 1791. ¶5

PROBATE FOUND (10 Oct 2026). His estate was settled at Oland in Gjøvdal between 16 May and 18 November 1789 (Østre Råbyggelag skifteprotokoll no. 16a, folio 257; also entered as no. 15, folio 278 on 5 August 1789 and no. 17, folio 22b on 18 November 1789). The card names him, his widow "Torie Olsdtr." with the note that she married secondly Tallak Eriksen, and two children: "Aadne Nielsen, 4 år (+ sk. 1811)" and "Anne Nielsdtr.", an infant, with the note "(g.m. Ole Herjusen Gangsei)". The estate held 10½ skinn in Oland, with assets of 439 riksdaler 3 ort 19 skilling against debts of 394-0-22. ¶6
