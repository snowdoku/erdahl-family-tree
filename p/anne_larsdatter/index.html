Life story

Baptized 21 Jul 1816 at Hen church, Grytten, born 8 Jun 1816. Parents: Lasse Andersen, cottar (husmann) at Skorgen, and Giøri (Guri) Olsdatter. ¶2

Her baptism on 21 Jul 1816 took place at Hen church (Heen kirke) in Grytten parish, not at Grytten church itself. Her father is entered as 'Lasse Anders.' with the status 'Hmd.' (husmann, a cottager holding a small plot from a larger farm) at Skorgen, and her mother as 'Giøri Olsd.', 'Hustrue' (wife). The register gives her birth as 8 Jun 1816 and records no godparents; the baptism is on page 244 of the book. ¶5

Confirmed 11 Sep 1831 at Grytten as Anne Lassesdatter of Skorgen. Parents: Lasse Andersen of Skorgen and Guri Olsdatter. The confirmation index gives her birth as 8 Jun 1815; the baptism register says 1816. ¶3

At her marriage on 28 Apr 1842 she was entered as 'Pige' - a maidservant - of Skorgen, and her age was given as 27, which would mean a birth in 1815. Her baptism register says 1816 and her 1831 confirmation entry says 1815. Taken together with the baptism being the primary record of the event, 8 Jun 1816 remains the best date, and the two 1815 readings are the sort of drift normal in stated ages. ¶6

Widow by 1865; living with son Lasse in 1885; in Kirkelandet poorhouse, Kristiansund, in 1900. ¶1

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Confidence raised from Probable to Confirmed (13 Oct 2026). Her parentage and her identity rest on one chain of primary records, each read on the scan or in a faithful Digitalarkivet index, and nothing contradicts it. (1) The Hen church baptism of 21 Jul 1816 names the child Anne, born 8 Jun, daughter of Lasse Andersen, husmann (cottager) at Skorgen, and his wife Giøri Olsdatter. (2) The Grytten confirmation of 11 Sep 1831 (book 1818-1853, p. 487, no. 8; index pk00000002439027, re-read 13 Oct 2026) names the confirmand Anne Lassesdatter of Skorgen, born 8 Jun 1815, with father Lasse Andersen of Skorgen and mother Guri Olsdatter. (3) The Grytten marriage of 28 Apr 1842 (p. 395, no. 3) gives the bride as 'Pige Anne Lassesd. Skorgen 27 Aar': the same patronymic and farm, and an age that points to 1815, the year the confirmation gives. Her bondsman (forlover) was Anders Lassesen of Skorgen, read as her brother Anders, baptized 27 Jul 1813 to the same parents. (4) The 1865 census of Kristiansund (pf01038278005133, re-read 13 Oct 2026) lists the widow Anne Larsdatter Zæthness, 51, born in Romsdal county, with her children Lars Pedersen, 19, and Henrikke Caroline Pedersdatter, 16. This ties the 1842 bride to Caroline's mother. The two remaining weak points are that the forlover's given name is not a certain reading, and that no Grytten-wide search for a second Anne Lassesdatter born about 1813-1818 has been logged. A second girl of that name at Skorgen would reopen the link; none is known. ¶8
