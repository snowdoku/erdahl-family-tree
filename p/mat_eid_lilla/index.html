Life story

Social Security, retirement claim: LILLA A. EIDEM, b. 3 Mar 1909, filed a life claim at the MARSHALL, MINNESOTA field office in 1971, the year she turned 62. NARA AAD, NUMIDENT Claim Files (Last Names D-G), rid 1352271. ¶3

Social Security death record: LILLA A EIDEM, b. 3 Mar 1909, d. 25 Jun 2002, residence ZIP [withheld], which is DAWSON, LAC QUI PARLE COUNTY, MINNESOTA. Posted 6 Jul 2002. NARA AAD, NUMIDENT Death Files (Last Names E-G), rid 285045. She died in the county her husband's family farmed in, and the death date matches the tree's 25 JUN 2002 to the day. ¶4

Burial: Zoar Lutheran Cemetery, lot 23, beside her husband Ludwig and their son Donald Michael. The South Dakota state burial register enters her under her full given name as 'LILLIAN AGNES EIDEM, 06-26-2002, lot 23'. The project's existing death date of 25 June 2002 comes from an index; the state register says the 26th. Both are indexes and the conflict is recorded, not resolved. ¶5

Named with her maiden surname as mother on her daughter Joyce Orene Eidem's Social Security application of June 1952: 'MOTHER'S FIRST NAME: LILLA / MOTHER'S MIDDLE NAME: A / MOTHER'S LAST NAME: HARKINS'. Father on the same card: LUDWIG EIDEM. NARA AAD, NUMIDENT Application (SS-5) Files (Last Names E-G), rid 421621. ¶2
