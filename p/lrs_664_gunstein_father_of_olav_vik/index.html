About the family

Father of Olav Gunsteinson of Vik (see his page). His forename is given by Olav's patronymic and by the bygdebok (Nissedal sokn, Vik gnr. 7, canvas 143: 'Olav Gunsteinson var son til Gunstein [..]'); the rest of the sentence (his farm) falls in a gap of the word-box rebuild. The book adds that Olav 'was probably married to a daughter of [..] Vik', so Olav seems to have come into Vik by marriage. Born before about 1600 (son born about 1624). ¶1

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
