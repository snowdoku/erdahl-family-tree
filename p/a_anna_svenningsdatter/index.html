About the family

NEW ANCESTOR. Wife of Ole Ormsen of Eppeland and mother of Niels Olsen Eppeland (estate 1755) and Eli Olsdatter. ¶1

Her father was a Svenning. The name passed to her grandson Svenning Nielsen, a son of Niels Olsen by his second marriage, and probably explains why Niels's second wife was also a Svenningsdatter. ¶3

Parents found: her father's 1712 probate card (Østre Råbyggelag Skifteprotokoll nr. 3 fol. 223, 26 May 1712, card 11385) lists the children of Svening Giermundsøn † and Øli Sveningsdatter † as '1. Knud Sveningsøn Rostved. 2. Anna Sveningsdtr. (g.m. Ole Ormsøn Eppeland)'. The marriage to Ole Ormsen of Eppeland ties her to the tree. The back of her own 1747 card (2378/2379) gives the same: 'Konas far: Svenning Gjermundsen Rostveit i Åmli (+ sk. 1712); mor: Øli Svenningsdtr. + sk. 1690.' Brother: Knud Svenningsen Rostveit. ¶4

Her father's estate protocol (Skifteprotokoll nr. 3 fol. 223a, 26 May 1712, Rostved) names the heirs as Knud Svenningsøn Rostved and Anne Svenningsdatter, with her husband Ole Ormsøn Eppeland. This is the primary record (not only the card) for her parentage. ¶6

Corrections 1

Correction: the 11 Dec 1747 record is an 'oppgitt skifte' in which the living couple surrendered their estate to the heirs ('Oppg. sk. e: Ole Ormssen og hustru Anna Svenningsdaatter, oppgir boet', Skifteprotokoll nr. 12a fol. 6, card 2377), not a probate after death. She was therefore alive on 11 Dec 1747; the death date 'before 11 Dec 1747' is changed to 'after 11 Dec 1747'. Heirs: sons Orm Olsen and Niels Olsen (both of age) and Elbie (Eli) Olsdatter, married to Aslach Tellefsen Eppeland (+ sk. 7 Oct 1767). Estate: 1 hud odelsgods in Øvre Eppeland and 6 kalvskinn in Nedre Eppeland; assets 168-2-20, debts 16-1-8. ¶5
