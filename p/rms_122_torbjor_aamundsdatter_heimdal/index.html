About the family

Wife of Knud Bjønnesen of nordre Heimdal; her probate was held in July 1733 (Øvre Telemark skifteprotokoll no. 4, fol. 338b; card DA 6856 img 478), with 'Hage g.m. Evend Anderssen Heimdal' as her eldest child. ¶1

Life story

Bygdebok (Treungen vol., Heimdal nordre, canvases 359-360): 'Torbjørg Åsmundsdtr. Heimdal', daughter of Åsmund Høljeson Heimdal and a Larsdtr. (d. 1697); 'Det var skifte etter Torbjørg Åsmundsdtr. 1733'. ¶3
