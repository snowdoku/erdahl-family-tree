About the family

On the reading argued in the note on Beret Olsdatter, she is Oline Slind's great-great-grandmother: her daughter by her first marriage, Beret Olsdatter born about 1793, married Gunder Jorgensen of Slinden and was the mother of Ole Gundersen Slind born 1816. Rated Probable; the evidence is the naming of Beret's own daughter Ragnild in 1828 and of her son Reinald in 1822, together with the patronymic Olsdatter in a household whose other children are Reinaldsdatter and Reinaldssen. ¶2

Life story

Origin and first marriage, Probable: betrothed (trolovet) in Selbu on Maundy Thursday, 5 April 1792, as 'P[ige] Rhagnild Jonsd. Aune' - the maiden Ragnhild Jonsdatter of Aune - to the grenadier Ole Pedersen Saether (see his page); sponsors Erich Neelsen Aune and Olle Siursen Sethar. Their daughter Berthe (Beret) was baptised 9 September 1792 with Aunet godparents (Peder and Anne Oeunet). So in 1792 she lived at Aune(t), and her father Jon should be sought there. Which Aune is not settled: Aune in Selbu main parish and Aunet in the Tydal annex were both served by this register. The identification with the Rangnil of Slinden in 1801 rests on the reasoning in the notes on Beret Olsdatter (see her page). ¶4

Strengthened, still Probable: the father side of her daughter is now proved. The probate after Peder Andersen of Sveet under Sæter, 11 July 1793 (Stjør- og Verdal Skifteprotokoll 10, Digitalarkivet 24763, p. 391), confirms that his son Ole Pedersen had died leaving one daughter, 'Berith Olsd. 1 aar gl.' - the Berthe baptised 9 Sep 1792 to Ole Pedersen and Ragnhild Jonsdatter. The probate's opening lines, as read, do not name Ole's widow, so the identity of the 1792 Ragnhild Jonsdatter (of Aune) with Rangnil Jonsdatter of Slinden in 1801 still rests on the census household (Beret Olsdatter, 8, the only Olsdatter, with Rangnil in her second marriage). Ole's own probate (d. 19 May 1793), if one was held, would name his widow; it was not found in 24763 views 395-410. ¶16

Second marriage: betrothed on Palm Sunday, 13 April 1794, 'Soldat Reinholdt Torstens: Slinden med Enken Ragnil Jonsdatter Saether' - the soldier Reinholt Torstensen of Slinden with the widow Ragnhild Jonsdatter of Saether - forlovere Sivert Haftorsen Slinden and Peder Bersvendsen of the same farm; banns read 17 April, 27 April and on Misericordias; married ('Aegteviet') on Cantate Sunday, 18 May 1794. This dates the marriage the 1801 census calls her second, and the word 'Enken' (the widow) at Saether ties the Rangnil of Slinden to the widow of Ole Pedersen Saether, who was buried 26 May 1793. It strengthens, without quite proving, the identification argued in the notes on Beret Olsdatter. ¶6

Her first husband Ole Pedersen Saether died before about 1796; she had remarried Reinal Torstenssen by about 1795, as their daughter Ane Reinaldsdatter was 5 in the census of 1 February 1801, where the census calls this marriage her second. The marriage to Ole was not found in the register between April and August 1792; the date is given here as about 1792 from the betrothal. ¶5

Entered in the 1801 census of Selbu at rural residence 0083 Slinden, household 7, as 'Rangnil Jonsdatter, 34, Hans kone', wife of Reinal Torstenssen, 30, who worked the farm 'I faellesskab med husbonden' - in common with the householder. The census gives the marriage as his first and HER SECOND. ¶1

Her own patronymic makes her father a JON, and her first husband's given name was OLE. Her first marriage is not in the Digitalarkivet index, which holds effectively nothing for Selbu before 1801; the Selbu marriage register of about 1790 on the scan, and the probate of her first husband, are the records that would name both men. ¶3

1801 household detail: at Slinden, household 7, with her husband Reinal Torstenssen, 30, who farmed in partnership with the householder Peder Bersvendsen, and the children Beret Olsdatter, 8 (her daughter by Ole Pedersen Saether), Ane Reinaldsdatter, 5, and Ole Reinaldssen, 2. 1825: at Slinden with Renald Tostens., 56, and their married sons Ole Renaldsen, 27, and Tosten Renalds., 22, each with a child. ¶11

1825 census: 'Ragnhild Johnsd.', born 1765, wife ('Hustru'), at Slinden - the only Ragnhild Jonsdatter born 1760-1772 in the 1825 index for Selbu. ¶7

Death and burial, Probable: 'Ragnild Johnsdt', aged 76, died 12 August and buried 15 August 1841, Selbu burial register 1824-1842, p. 403 (index; scan not read). For: the calculated birth year 1765 is the same as in the 1825 census entry at Slinden, and she was the only Ragnhild Jonsdatter of that age alive in the parish in 1825. ¶8a

Burial, scan read: Selbu burials 1841, p. 403, row 41: 'Ragnild Johnsd.t Slinden', Enke (widow), aged 76, residence Slinden, died 12 August, buried 15 August 1841. Row 39 on the same page is her husband 'Renald Tostensen Slinden', Gaardmand (farmer), aged 72, died 2 August, buried 8 August 1841, ten days before her. The scan supplies the residence and widowhood the index left blank, and ties this burial to the Ragnhild of Slinden. ¶10

Branches meeting: her grandson Ole Gundersen Slind (see his page, son of her daughter Beret Olsdatter) married on 24 May 1841 Berith Gundersdatter Eidem (see her page), granddaughter of Erik Torstensen of Uglem (see his page); they are the parents of Gunder Olsen Slind. If her second husband Reinholdt Torstensen is Erik's brother, Ole's step-grandfather was Berith's great-uncle. That is a tie by marriage only: Reinholdt is not Beret Olsdatter's father, so the Ragnhild and Erik lines share no ancestor through it and there is no pedigree collapse. The brotherhood itself is unproven (see Reinal's page). ¶13

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 record that disagrees · 1 correction · 1 alternative set aside · 4 searches with no result · 3 open questions

Records that disagree 1

Against: residence and husband are not in the index. The near miss 'Ragnild Johnsdr', 65 (born about 1775), buried 5 July 1840, is ten years too young. Her birth year is therefore about 1765 by the 1825 census and the burial, against about 1767 from her age of 34 in 1801; the existing ABT 1767 is kept. ¶8b

Corrections 1

Correction: birth changed from ABT 1767 to ABT 1765. Two records give 1765 - the 1825 census (60) and the burial scan of 1841 (76) - against one, the 1801 census age of 34 (about 1767). Born about 1765 she was about 27 at her betrothal in 1792. ¶9

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Candidate baptism, not linked: on the 13th Sunday after Trinity 1766, 'Døbt Ragnild. Forældr: Jon Ols: Hove, Kari Erichsd:' (16311 c20 left page). Her age fits (34 in 1801, 60 in 1825), but the farm is Hove, not Aune, where she lived when betrothed in 1792 ('Rhagnild Jonsd. Aune'), and the 1765 and 1767 baptisms (16311 c18-19, c21-22) have not been searched entry by entry for a Ragnild daughter of a Jon at Aune. So Jon Olsen Hove and Kari Eriksdatter are only a lead. Note: 'Erich Niels: Aune' (her 1792 forlover) was the son of Niels Eriksen Aune (skifte 1732) and was at Aune by 1757 (16311 c3). ¶18

Searches that found nothing 4

1801 census, negative: Digitalarkivet person search of the 1801 census, Selbu, residence Aune*, first names Jon*, Joh* and Jo*: the only Jon/Jons there are Jon Olssen (b. 1782 and 1789, children), Jon Jonssen (b. 1758, husmann, and b. 1794, his son) and Jon Pederssen (b. 1774, soldier). No Jon aged 55-80 lived at Aunet in 1801, so her father, if a Jon of Aune, was dead or elsewhere by then. This closes the 'unread Aunet rows' lead. ¶14

Probate search, negative: the R page of the Strinda og Selbu skifteutlodningsprotokoll 2, 1841-1853 (Digitalarkivet 24746), handwritten register at the back, one letter per image (A = image 479, B 480, C 481, F 484, J 487, L 490, R 495, S 496) has no estate of Rangnil/Ragnild Jonsdatter or Reinal Torstensen of Slinden (both died August 1841); its only 'Ragnild Johnsdt.' is of Skarnæs (fol. 5), not Slinden. A probate listing Beret Olsdatter as her daughter of the first marriage, which would confirm Ole Pedersen Saether as Beret's father, is not in this protocol. ¶15

Re-read, negative: the heirs' lines of the Peder Andersen probate (24763 p. 391, view 406 right, lines 1-6) were read at full resolution with raised contrast. The son Ole Pedersen is recorded as dead, leaving 'et Pigebarn nemlig Berith Olsd. 1 aar gl.' (one girl, Berith Olsdatter, 1 year old). Ole's widow is not named in these lines, so the probate does not settle Rangnil's identity as Ole's widow. ¶17

Negative: the 1765, 1766 and 1767 baptisms in 16311 (canvases 18, 19, 21 and 22, with canvas 20) were read entry by entry. There is no Ragnild daughter of a Jon at Aune. The only Ragnild daughter of a Jon in these years is the 1766 child of Jon Olsen Hove and Kari Eriksdatter (c20 L). That couple first had an illegitimate daughter Ane, baptised on the 2nd Sunday after Trinity 1765 ('2.) Ane. Kaldr: Jon Ols: Hove, Kari Erichsd: Morsæt', among 'uægte Børn', c18 L), and married on the 22nd Sunday after Trinity 1765 ('Copul: Jon Ols: Kari Erichsd:', c18 R). The Hove Ragnild is still only a lead, because nothing yet places her at Aune by 1792. ¶19a

Open questions and the next record to read 3

Her father, a Jon of Aune(t), is not identified. The 1801 census of Selbu indexes the farm as Aunet (67 persons); the 50 read show no Jon aged 55-80 (heads Erik Nielssen, Ole Olssen, Ellev Nielssen, two Peder Jonssens, Peder Olssen, Lars Olssen); 17 rows are unread. Two Peder Jonssens at Aunet (b. 1740 and 1750) could be brothers, and 'Peder and Anne Oeunet' were godparents to her daughter in 1792. ¶12a

Lead only: a Dordi Jonsdatter (b. about 1773-74), a servant at Slinden in 1801 and a widow there in 1825, might be a sister; not tested. ¶12b

Next: her confirmation (about 1780-82; Selbu lists give name, farm and age only) or a Hove or Aune probate naming her. ¶19b
