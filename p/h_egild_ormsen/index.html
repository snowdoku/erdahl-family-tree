About the family

Father linked at Probable (on the coordinator's cross-line lead): Orm Tvedt (see his page). The grounds: (1) at his betrothal on 6 Jun 1775 he is 'Egild Ormsen Tvedt', son of an Orm, of the farm Tvedt; (2) Orm Tvedt is the only Orm at Tvedt in the Treungen registers of 1755-1775 (burials and baptisms of his children 1755-1765, godfather 1759-61, cautioner 10 Nov 1775, the day Egild married), and the only other Orm of Tveit in the card index, Orm Torbjørnsen, was dead by 1726, before Egild was born (about 1751); (3) Egild named a son Orm (12 in 1801); (4) Gulloug Ormsen (60 in 1801), Orm Tvedt's probable son, farmed the neighbouring Tvedt household in 1801; (5) Rannei Ormsdatter Tvedt, Orm's probable daughter, was betrothed in Treungen on 10 Nov 1775, the day of Egild's wedding. No record read names Orm and Egild together; Egild's baptism (about 1751) is lost with the pre-1754 Kviteseid book. Orm's probate (after Nov 1775, Øvre Telemark vestfjelske protocols DA 25675-25681) would settle it, and was not reached tonight. ¶7

Mother linked at Probable: Anne Targiesdatter (see her page), named as the wife of 'Orm Egilssøn Tvedt af Treungen' in her father Targiej Torrofsen's probate at Sønderland, 15 Sep 1756 (25673 view 331). No record names her as Egild's mother; see her notes for the grounds and the possible earlier marriage of Orm. His own grade is unchanged. ¶11

Life story

Death: 'Egild Ormsen', aged 75 (b. about 1750), buried 23 Jun 1825 at Treungen (Nissedal ministerialbok, Treungen 1814-1862, p. 102, no. 5; index pg00000006881868). The index gives the death as 8 Jun with year 1826, which cannot be right for a burial in June 1825 (probably died 8 Jun 1825); the death is entered as BEF 23 JUN 1825. The age fits his 1801 census age of 50 at Tvedt, and he is the only Egild Ormsen born 1745-1757 in the burial index. ¶10

Orm Tvedt, his probable father, buried children at Treungen: a son Halvor on 7 Feb 1755 (image 153), and a daughter Sigri(?), aged 22½ weeks, on 11 May 1759 (image 161; she was baptized 1 Dec 1758). These show Orm was raising a family at Tvedt in the 1750s, which fits Egild (b. ~1751) but does not prove the link. No Kviteseid church book before 1754 survives on Digitalarkivet, so Egild's baptism cannot be checked. ¶3

Orm Tvedt, his probable father, had two sons named Targie baptized in Treungen church: 'Orm Tvedts Søn Targei' on the 3rd day of Christmas (27 Dec) 1760 (image 25), and 'Orm Tvedts Søn Targ[ie]' on the 3rd Sunday after Trinity (20 Jun) 1762 (image 30); both died as infants. Neither entry names the mother (this register gives only the father). Godparents in 1760 were mostly of Haugsjaa and Tvedt; in 1762 Aase(?), Jørgen, Margit and Birget Hiemdahl and Groe Tvedt. Orm was also godfather at several Treungen baptisms 1759-1761 (images 21, 22, 27), as was a Gulloug Tvedt / Gulloug Ormsen (1761), probably the Gulloug Ormsen (60 in 1801) who lived beside Egild at Tvedt. ¶5

Orm Tvedt, his probable father, buried more children at Treungen: a son Targgi, aged 13 weeks, on Whitsunday (10 May) 1761 (image 164); a second son Targgi, aged 1, at Candlemas (2 Feb) 1763 (image 167); and a daughter Kari, no age given, on 29 May 1765 (image 171). Orm himself is not in the Treungen burials up to 1769 (images 153-175 read). None of these entries names Egild. ¶4

Orm Tvedt's patronymic: protocol 10, image 491, in the debts of the 1767 Nissedal skifte no. 645 (deceased Sara Ericksdatter), cites an earlier document involving 'Orm Egildsøn Tvedt', Ole Nielsøn Nordalle of Treungen and a date of 8 Mar 1763; Anders Evensen, Astiør Jørgensdatter Tvedt and Jørgen Sigursøn Hiemdall are among the creditors listed below. The patronymic Egildsøn is clearly written there. The 1775 cautioner reading '[T..]lefsen' is blotted. If he is Orm Egildsen, his son Egild Ormsen carries his paternal grandfather's name in the usual Norwegian pattern, which strengthens the Probable link (inference). The context of the 1763 document was not read. ¶8

Betrothed 6 Jun 1775 in Treungen: ungkarl Egild Ormsen Tvedt and pige Gundbjør Toruvsdatter(?) Hiemdal. They married 10 Nov 1775 in Treungen church (Kviteseid 1773-1786, image 109). ¶2

1801 census, Tvedt (Kviteseid prestegjeld): husband, aged 50, both in their first marriage, with wife Gundbiør Toruvsdatter (49). Children: Guro (24), Gunild (19), Astri (15), Orm (12) and Toruv (6). The neighbouring Tvedt household of Gulloug Ormsen (60) may be related. ¶1

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: Confirmed as the father of Orm Egildsen Tveit (see his page). The 1801 census for Kviteseid (0829P), census district 005, farm 0003 Tvedt, household 05, read on the Digitalarkivet transcription, lists 'Egild Ormsen', 50, 'Husbonde', 'Begge i 1te egteskab', bonde; 'Gundbiør Toruvsdatter', 49, 'Hans kone'; and among 'Deres børn' (their children) Gunild Egildsdatter 19, Astri Egildsdatter, 'Orm Egildsen', 12 (born about 1789), and Toruv Egildsen. The census names the relationship directly: Orm is the child of Egild and his wife Gundbiør, at Tvedt, which is the Orm Egildsen Tveit of Treungen already in the tree. This grade covers only his link to Orm; his own parentage (Orm Egilsen Tvedt, Orm Egilsen Tvedt) keeps its earlier grade of Probable. ¶9

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Probable father (not linked; proposed to line 4 via fleet tell): Orm Tvedt (see his page), the Orm Tvedt named in the notes above. A sister is probably Rannei Ormsdatter Tvedt (see her page, line 2), who was betrothed in Treungen on 10 Nov 1775, the day of Egild's wedding there, to Thore Joensen Midbø. The 1790 probate after Thore's brother names 'Omundsen' men of Treungen, Gulloug Ormsen and one other, as guardians of her daughters (25679 skifte No 21). This ties line 2 (Clarice Larsen) to line 4 (Wava Ramsey) at Tvedt in Treungen, if confirmed. ¶6
