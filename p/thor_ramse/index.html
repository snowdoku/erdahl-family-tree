About the family

PARENTAGE INDEPENDENTLY CONFIRMED (10 Oct 2026) from a source outside the church books. The probate of Gunlek Aslaksen at Tveit in Tovdal, 7 December 1792 to 30 January 1794 (Østre Råbyggelag skifteprotokoll no. 16b, folio 515), names among the heirs on the father's side "Tremenning: Tellef Thorsen Tveit +. Hans barn: Thor Tellefsen Tveit, Erich, Aase Tellefsdtr." He is that Thor Tellefsen Tveit. ¶7

NEW SIBLINGS: Erich Tellefsen Tveit and Aase Tellefsdatter, both alive in December 1792. Nothing further is yet known of either. Aase was named for their great-great-grandmother Aase Thorsdatter of Øvre Ramse. ¶8

Life story

Baptism (Probable match): Dom. 3 p. Epiph. 1760 (27 Jan 1760) in Topdal, Thor, son of Tellef Thorsøn and Margitte Olsdatter Tvedt. Godparents: Aanund Ramse, Eivind Hillestad, Ole Kittelsøn Østenaae, Eivind Hillestad's wife, and Thor Ramse's wife (Åmli 1756-1775 p.32 #12). Tellef Thorsøn Tved and Margitte Olsdatter Ramse were betrothed at Ramse in Dec 1755. Name, age at death (39), farm (Tvedt) and the naming of his son Tellef all fit. ¶5

Marriage (Åmli 1787 #4): on 8 Jan 1787 in Omblie (Åmli) church, ungkarl (bachelor) Thor Tellefsen married pige (unmarried woman) Birgitte Tergiesdatter of Eppeland. ¶3

The family lived at Tvet in 1795 and 1800. The widow and her sons were at Øvre Ramse at the Feb 1801 census. ¶6

His ancestry now runs back five further generations: Thor Tellefsen < Tellef Thorsen Tved (b. about 1728) < Thore Tellefsen Tveit (d. 1740) and Torbor Terjesdatter < Tellef Nielsen Tveit (d. 1703) and Aase Thorsdatter of Øvre Ramse < Thor Jørgensen of Øvre Ramse (about 1614-1681). ¶10

He and his wife Birgitte Terjesdatter were distant cousins. On the present reconstruction both descend from Even Tellefsen Ramse, who was at Ramse in the early 1600s — he through Groe Evindsdatter and the Tveit family, she through Svend Evensen Ramse and the Eppeland family. That makes them fourth cousins, outside the degrees for which the church required a royal dispensation, which is why no licence appears for their 1787 marriage. ¶11

Burial (Åmli 1796-1816 p.201 #22, Lille Topdal): 'Manden og Almisselemmen' (husband, on poor relief) Thor Tellefsen Tvet, aged 39, died 25 [Mar] and buried 19 Apr 1800. Entry #23 on the same day is his child Margitthe, aged 45 weeks. This page comes before the indexed section (index starts at p.202, 1801), which is why the burial was missed earlier. The month is read from the ditto column after 30 Mar and 1 Apr entries, and the year from the page sequence (p.200 = 1798/99). His age, 39, fits a Jan 1760 birth. ¶4

In the 1801 census his widow Birgitte and sons Terje (~1788) and Tellef (~1795) were at Øvre Ramse. ¶1

The case for this identification 1

IDENTIFIED: Thor's patronymic is TELLEFSEN. His son Tellef's baptism (1st Sunday after Trinity, 7 Jun 1795, L. Topdal; Åmli church book 1775-1796 p.107) reads: child from Tvet, Tellef, born 3 May, parents Thor Tellefsen and Birthe Tergiesdatter. Godparents: Torgius Tvet and wife, Salve and Giermund Skieggedahl, and Guro Ramse. ¶2

Searches that found nothing 1

NO PROBATE WAS REGISTERED FOR HIM. All 118 Østre Råbyggelag probate cards for Ramse in Tovdal and all 36 for Tveit in Tovdal were read, and neither his estate nor his widow's appears. This is consistent with the burial register, which calls him "Manden og Almisselemmen" — the husband and the pauper, a man receiving parish poor relief. Estates below a minimum value were not registered, so the absence of a probate is itself evidence of how poor the family had become. ¶9
