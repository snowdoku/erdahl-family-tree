Life story

Birth: born 26 February 1908 at 4 a.m. in the village of Lake Wilson, Murray County, the fourth child of Berent Ludwig Erdahl, farmer, 29, born Rome Township, Minnesota, and Annie Quame, 25, housewife, born Faribault County, Minnesota. ¶1

Death: the family list recorded under her brother William (from Find a Grave) gives her life as 1908-1995 and her married name as Kluken. No death record has been read yet. ¶4

1930 U.S. census (index only): 'Bernice O Erdahl', daughter, born about 1908 in Minnesota, was still unmarried in her parents' household in South Malta, Phillips County, Montana. This agrees with her later marriage in 1941. The image has not been read. ¶5

Marriage (probable): the Whatcom County, Washington marriage index lists Jeremiah Kluken and 'Bernice O Erdahl', 1941, which agrees with her married name Kluken in the family list. Her residence at Malta, Montana in 1944 has not yet been reconciled with this. ¶3

Delayed registration: her birth was not registered at the time. Her father signed a delayed registration on 14 June 1944 from Custer, Washington. On 30 October 1944 the Clerk of District Court of Murray County examined her baptismal certificate and a school certificate from Phillips County, Montana, which were in her possession at Malta, Montana. The registration was filed on 24 November 1944. ¶2
