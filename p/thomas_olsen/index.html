Life story

Baptized 17 Feb 1822. Husmann (cottager) on the Beitland crofts in Hegra 1848-62. ¶1

His first marriage, 6 June 1844: he is entered as a bachelor of Almoetrø, born 1822 in Stjørdal, with his father named as Ole Thomassen; the bride is Bereth Madsdatter, a maid of Bakmo, born 1820 in Stjørdal, with her father named as Mads of Bakmo. She must have died within two years, because by June 1846 he is a widower. ¶5

Married Eli Larsdatter 18 Jun 1846 at Værnes as a 24-year-old widower of Bakmon. ¶3

Second marriage, 18 June 1846 at Værnes: a 24-year-old widower then living at Bakmon, to Eli Larsdatter of Husebye. His father is again named as Ole Thomassen, which is what fixes his own parentage. ¶6

Complete list of his children by Eli Larsdatter, all baptized in the Hegra annex of Stjørdal and all entered as legitimate: Ole, born 3 Mar 1848 and baptized 2 Apr 1848 (Beitlandsgjerdet); Beret Martha, born 7 Oct 1849 and baptized 4 Nov 1849 (Beitlandsgjerdet); Lisbeth, baptized 12 Sep 1852 (Beitlandsbjørgen); Lorentz, born 30 Jan 1855 and baptized 11 Mar 1855 (Beitlandsgjerdet); Matias, born 5 Jun 1858 and baptized 4 Jul 1858 (Beitlandsbjørgen); and Kirstine, born 23 Aug 1862 and baptized 12 Oct 1862 (Beitlandsplads). ¶7

In every one of those six entries he is 'husmand' - a crofter - and the residence changes from Beitlandsgjerdet to Beitlandsbjørgen to Beitlandsplads. These are three separate cottar's holdings carved out of the same farm, Beitland, and the moves show a family renting its roof rather than owning it. ¶8

Dating the emigration: his last dated Norwegian records are his son Ole's confirmation at Hegra on 5 October 1862 and Kirstine's baptism on 12 October 1862. He, his wife and all six children are absent from the Norwegian census of 31 December 1865. The family therefore left Hegra between October 1862 and the end of 1865. ¶10

Emigration, the 1862 gap: the Hegra register for 1850-1862 records no departures after 12 May 1862 (it records arrivals until 2 May 1862). The next register's out-migration list (1863-1879) begins with no. 1 on 28 January 1863, and only two people left before June 1863, neither from this family. No other Hegra register with migration lists survives for these years. So if the family left between the summer of 1862 and January 1863, which fits Kirstine's baptism on 12 October 1862, the parish register never recorded the departure. The emigration has to be dated from American records. ¶14

Context: the Beitland crofts lay under the farm Beitland in Hegra. The 1865 census shows the main farm split into Beitland vestre and Beitland østre, each worked by a 'Gaardbruger, Leilending' - a tenant farmer, not an owner. A husmann under a leaseholder stood one more rung down again: he held a patch of the farm's outfield in return for labour days, and could be given notice. The Norwegian names of the three holdings describe exactly what they were - 'gjerdet' the fenced enclosure, 'bjørgen' the hillside, 'plads' simply the place or croft. ¶9

He died 5 Feb 1887 in Faribault County, Minnesota, aged 65, and is buried at South Blue Earth Lutheran Cemetery, Bricelyn, with his wife, his daughter Beret Martha, his son Lorentz, his daughter Kirstine and his infant grandson Lauris. ¶12

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Caution for future researchers: a different Thomas Olsen appears at Beitland in December 1864 as the father of an illegitimate daughter Bergitha by Anne Bjørnsdatter of Sørkilgjerdet. He is not this man. The 1865 census identifies him as 'Tomas Olssen', born about 1844 in Selbu, an unmarried farmhand at Beitland vestre. ¶11

Searches that found nothing 1

Emigration, negative: the Hegra parish register's list of people moving out ('Udflyttede') was read row by row for 1863 to 1866 (folios 175-177). It has no entry for Thomas Olsen, Eli Larsdatter or any of their children, and no Beitland household. The family's departure was not registered in Hegra in those years. It may have been registered in late 1862 (in the 1850-1862 register), in another parish, or not at all. ¶13
