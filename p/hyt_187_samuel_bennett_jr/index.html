Life story

Samuel Bennett junior, gent., of Ballincollow (Ballincallow), Co. Limerick. Son and heir apparent of Samuel Bennett senior of Coss, Co. Limerick. His marriage settlement with Anne, daughter of William Collis of Liseaige (Lissodigue), Co. Kerry, is dated 13 Feb 1708/9 (Registry of Deeds memorial 420). In 1713, as Samuel Bennett the younger of Ballincallow, he leased Ballincallow (233 acres) from George Evans the younger, for the lives of himself, Ann his wife and George his son (memorial 10967). He was dead by 17 Feb 1734/5, when the marriage articles of his eldest son George Bennett of Castlecreagh call him 'Samuel Bennett junior of Ballincollow, deceased' (memorial 55996). The Blennerhassett Pedigree (about 1733-36) gives his children by Anne Collis as George, William and Joseph, and Mary, Isabella, Martha and Prudence. ¶1

(no grade change; graded on his link to Mary Bennett, which still rests on the Blennerhassett Pedigree). Memorial 420, read on the image, calls him 'Samuel Bennett Jun[io]r Son and Heir Apparent of the said Samuell Bennett Sen[io]r', and his bride 'Anne Collis Eldest Daughter of the said William Collis'. The settled lands (Castlereagh, Cloghasty and Mitchelstown) lay 'in the Parish of Ballingarry', Co. Limerick. Murphy's abstract places the baptisms of William and Mary Creed's daughters (1733, 1735) in that same parish. That fits the pedigree's marriage of Mary Bennett to William Creed, but it does not name Mary. ¶3

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the grade · 1 open question

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Probable, graded on his link to his daughter Mary Bennett: the Blennerhassett Pedigree names her as 'Mary Bennett, eldest daughter of Samuel and Anne', wife of William Creed of 'Ballindall', Co. Limerick, with daughters Anne, Jane and Sarah by 1733. The pedigree is a contemporary family manuscript printed verbatim, not an official record. Its author, Captain John Blennerhassett, witnessed Samuel's marriage settlement. The deeds bear out its account of Samuel's family (wife Anne Collis; eldest son George, who married Sarah Hilliard). Probable rather than Confirmed because the pedigree's 'Ballindall' must be read as Ballyneal to match the tree's William Creed of Ballyneal, and the Ballingarry baptisms (Ann 1733, Jane 1735) that support the match are known only from Murphy's abstract. ¶2a

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Test record: the Ballingarry register baptisms of 1733 and 1735 (RCB Library), or a Bennett will or deed naming Mary Creed. Added. ¶2b
