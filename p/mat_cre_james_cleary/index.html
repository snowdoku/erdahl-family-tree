Life story

Third husband of Bexey Jane (Creed) Hicken Johnston. 1921 census (Lot 61, Gaspereaux): Cleary, James, head, 65, Roman Catholic, Irish origin, with his wife Bexey, 59, and his stepson George A. Hicken, 32. Find a Grave spells the name Clarey. ¶1

Bexey was a widow, 'Bexy Cleary', in the 1931 census, so he died between June 1921 and June 1931. Possible identifications (unproved): a James Clary born about 1861 in Lot 59 in 1881, or the James Clarey born October 1860 in PEI who was at New Westminster, BC, in 1911. Their marriage (between 1911 and 1921) has not been found. ¶2
