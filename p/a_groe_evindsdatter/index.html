About the family

Daughter of Even (Evind) Tellefsen Ramse by his second marriage, to Anna Evindsdatter, who was still living in 1704. She married a man named Nils and settled at Tveit in Tovdal. ¶1

Her children are listed on the Østre Råbyggelag card for the Ramse deed registered 28 February 1704: Olle Nielsen Tveit, Rolf Nielsen Tveit, Groe Nielsdatter of Høgeli, and Thargier Nielsdatter, married to Sofren Omundsen. Our ancestor Tellef Nielsen Tveit, who had died in 1703, is not listed, which is why the link to him is Probable rather than Confirmed. ¶2

Mother: The archivist's card abstracting the Ramse deeds of 28 July 1704 (card 10365) places Groe under the small heading "Barn i 2. ekteskap" — child of Even's second marriage, to Anna Evindsdatter, who was living in 1704. That makes Anna her mother. The link is Probable: the card says it in words, but it is an abstract and the archivist queried (with '?') whether this deed family is Svend Evensen Ramse's. The 1704 deed itself is the confirming record. The '?' on card 10417 is about her being Svend's sister, not about her mother. ¶4

Life story

A second card confirms her place in the family: the abstract of the Ramse nedre ytre pantebok entry states "Mannens far: Even Tollefsen Ramse" and then "søster: ? Groe Evindsdtr. g.m. Niels. Se skjøte på Ramse tgl. 28/7-1704" — identifying her as a sister of Svend Evensen Ramse and therefore a daughter of Even Tellefsen Ramse. ¶3

Birth widened to BET 1615 AND 1639: born before her mother's death about 1639 (bygdebok), and old enough to have a son Rolf born about 1644 (1664 census, if he is hers). ¶10

1704 deed: On 7 April 1704 at Tveit "S. Groe Evindsdaatters arvinger" — the heirs of the late Groe Evindsdatter — namely Oler and Rolf Nielsen Tveit, Groe Nielsdatter of Høgeli (acting with her son Ole Olsen) and Søfren Omundsen for his wife Thargier Nielsdatter, sold to Nere Jonsen Tveit their share of "our late mother Groe Evindsdatter's inheritance, which came to her after her late father Evind Tellefsen". This confirms her father and her children, and that she was dead by April 1704. The deed was witnessed by Evind Svendsen (her brother Svend's son) and Even T[orvild?]sen of Øvre Ramse. It does not name her mother; see Anna Evindsdatter. ¶7

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

Corrections 4

Correction: Birth estimate widened from ABT 1640 to BET 1640 AND 1660. No record gives her age. Her son Tellef Nielsen Tveit was a father by about 1700 and died in 1703, so she was born by about 1660. Her mother Anna was still alive in July 1704. A birth about 1640 would put Anna born about 1620 or earlier, and so over 80 at the deed. That is possible but strained. About 1645-1655 fits all the facts best. ¶5

Correction: The Ramse deed on card 10365 was registered on 28 July 1704 ("tgl. 28/7 1704", with a crossed continental 7), not on 28 February 1704 as some earlier notes say. Card 10417 gives the same date, 28/7-1704. ¶6

Correction: The card's "Anna Evindsdatter living in 1704" no longer constrains Groe's birth: Anna was dead by March 1704. The estimate BET 1640 AND 1660 stands on her son Tellef's dates alone. ¶8

Correction: mother changed from Anna Evindsdatter to Signe. The Tovdal bygdebok (Åmli: ætt og heim I, 'Ytre Ramse', canvas 326) lists 'Gro... Nils... Tveit' among Eivind's children by his first wife Signe, whose estate was divided about 1639, when Gro received a share in huder. Anna (Anne) was Eivind's last wife, married before 1655 (canvas 327). This fits the primary records better than the archivist's card 10365 ('Barn i 2. ekteskap'): the 1704 deed of Groe's heirs claims only through her father and never calls Anna her mother, while Rolf's deed calls Anna his stepmother; and Nils Tveit was 60 in 1664 with a son Rolf Nielssøn aged 20, which needs a wife born well before 1640.
