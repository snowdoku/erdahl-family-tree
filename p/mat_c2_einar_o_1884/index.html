1900 federal census: Brule township, Union County - 'Einer Eidem, son, b. September 1884, South Dakota'. He is the eldest American-born child and his birth date is the latest possible date for his father's crossing. By 1910 he was head of his own house next door, with wife Clara; in 1920 with Clara and daughter Ethel; in 1940 with a wife named Ollie. ¶1
