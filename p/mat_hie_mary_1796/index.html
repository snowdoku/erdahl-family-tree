Life story

Baptism: Mary, daughter of George and Phebe Hicken, was baptised on 17 April 1796 at St Mildred, Canterbury, Kent (England Births and Christenings index, from the parish register). She is the earliest known child of the couple and the first record of them anywhere; Canterbury was a garrison town with an artillery barracks, and her father was a private in the Royal Artillery when her brother James was baptised there in 1800. ¶1

Not in the compiled Island Register genealogy of the Prince Edward Island Hickens, which lists no Mary of this generation (it does list Mary Ann, born 1814). Her fate is unknown - she may have died young, or married in England before the family emigrated. ¶2

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
