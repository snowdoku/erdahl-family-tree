Life story

Burial (funeral sermon), 1st Sunday after Trinity 1760: 'Ligpræd. af (1) Peder Bersvends: Slinde 71 aar gl.' - aged 71, so born about 1689 (16311 canvas 10 left). Probably the 'Peder', 6, son of Bersvend Haldorsen (56) at Slinden in the 1701 manntall (canvas 115) - born about 1695; ages of this period often differ by several years. ¶1

Probate read: Skifteprotokoll 5 (24755) fol. 97b, entry No. 7, registered 1761: 'Gior vitterligt, at Anno 1760 d. 3 [Sept?] blev paa gaarden Slinden i Selboe forrettet Skifte efter afdøde Mand Peder Bersvendsen Slinden', who left as heirs '3 Sønner og 2 Døttre, Nemlig 1 Bersvend Pedersen myndig, 2 Reinold Pedersen myndig, 3 Ole Pedersen umyndig[?], 4 [Lisbet?] Pedersdatter ugift... og 5te Ingebor Pedersdatter lige ledes ugift'. Guardian for the two unmarried daughters: lensmann Ole Thomassen [Hoff?]; his brother Halvor Thomassen is also named. His wife Mali Reinelsdatter died before him (her own skifte is in Skifteprotokoll 3, 24753, fol. 270b, per the typed register of that volume; not yet read). ¶4

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Speculative father of Marit Pedersdatter (see her page, b. about 1718), wife of Ole Jørgensen Slinden: a 'Peder Slinden' was first godfather at the baptism of her daughter Marit in Aug 1746 (16315 c90), she married into Slinden in 1742, and he is the only elder Peder at Slinden in these years; an 'Ole Pedersen Sli[nden]' married at All Saints 1759 (c10) could be her brother. Nothing names her father; her baptism (about 1716-20) has not been found in 16310. Do not chain further generations above him. ¶2

Corrections 1

Correction: the Speculative link to Marit Pedersdatter (see her page) is withdrawn. His 1760 probate lists exactly three sons and two daughters, Bersvend, Reinold, Ole, [Lisbet?] and Ingebor, and both daughters were then unmarried. Marit, married since 1742 to Ole Jørgensen Slinden and alive until 1808, would have been an heir and is not named, so she was not his daughter. The 'Peder Slinden' who stood godfather in 1746 was a neighbour, not her father. ¶5

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Biography gaps: his birth (1689-95) predates the 1699 Selbu register for the whole range, and his marriage and wife are not identified. His death is known only as 1760, and his burial rests on a funeral-sermon entry (16311 canvas 10 L) that gives his age but no exact death date. His probate is not found and the 77804 index stops at 1742, so the next record is Stjør- og Verdal skifteprotokoll 5 (24755, 1760-66, unindexed). ¶3
