About the family

Father of Louisa Catharina Finck (see her page), of Illingen, Oberamt Maulbronn. Her bride row of 28 Apr 1803 gives relatives 'Johannes, Johann Melchior' (father, then groom; roles unlabelled; record 908261325) and her baptism of 13 Feb 1774 names 'Johannes, Friederica Magdalina' (record 8396225). Children with Friederica Magdalena in the index: Johann Gottfried (1772), Louisa Catharina (1774), Georg Carl (1780). Probable: index roles unlabelled; a second Johannes Finck (wife Maria Barbara) lived at Illingen in 1774-75. ¶1

Kin: his son Johann Gottfried Fink married Katarina Dorothea Knodel, daughter of Christoph Adam, at Illingen on 9 Sep 1794 (records 8261187/908261187). ¶3

(1) FamilySearch, 'Deutschland, ausgewählte evangelische Kirchenbücher 1500-1971' (= 'Germany, Lutheran Baptisms, Marriages, and Burials, 1500-1971'), QPGR-8VHN: marriage at Illingen 28 Apr 1803 (pages 90-91), groom Johann Melchior Lindenmaier, field Wife = 'Louisa Catharina Fincken', Father-in-law = 'Johannes Fincken'. (2) QPG5-KNXH: baptism of Louisa Catharina Finckh, Illingen 13 Feb 1774, Parents 'Friederica Magdalina, Johannes Finckh'. (3) QPGL-X2R5: burial of 'Louisa Catharina Lindermajerin', Bietigheim 19 Sep 1813 (page 62), Age '39 years' (so born about 1774), Father 'Johannes Finck', Mother 'Magdalena', Spouse 'Joh Mathias Lindermajer'. The rival noted earlier, a second Johannes Finck at Illingen whose wife was Maria Barbara, is excluded: the 1813 burial names her mother Magdalena, so she is the daughter of Johannes x Friederica Magdalena. Continuity: 1774 baptism, 1803 marriage (bride's father Johannes) and 1813 burial (age 39; parents Johannes and Magdalena; husband Lindenmaier) are one woman. The earlier grading note (unlabelled rows, rival) is superseded. Read signed in, 2 Nov 2026; labelled index fields, image not seen (partner-only). ¶4
