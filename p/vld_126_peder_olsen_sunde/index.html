Life story

Manntall 1701 (index): on the farm Sunde, Borgund (p. 5), the leaseholder Oelle, 65, former soldier, is listed with his sons 'Peder Oels.' aged 25 ('Søner, er marin', a sailor; remark 'tien Knud Hafnen', serving Knud Hafnen), Oelle Oels. 19 and Knud Oels. 14. He is the only Peder Olsen on Sunde in 1701, and Peder Olsen of Sunde was raising children there from 1714 (Siri) to about 1722 (youngest daughter aged 19 in 1741). A son of his was named Ole. On these grounds Oelle of Sunde is entered as his Probable father, and his birth as about 1676 (inferred from the 1701 age of 25). No record yet states the link, and his 1741 probate does not name his parents. ¶4

1704 list of marines (index): on Sunde, Peder Ols., bonde, with his brothers ('brødre') Ole Ols., 22, servant, and Christen Ols. Sunde, 17 (source 58583, searched by farm). Together with the 1701 manntall (son of Oelle, 65) this ties him to his father Ole (see his page) and gives two brothers. ¶6

Baptism link: he is the 'Peder Olss.' of Sunde named as father at the Borgund baptism of his daughter Siri on 11 Nov 1714 (pd00000001505946, index). ¶2

Probate (scan read): 'Anno 1741 d. 18 Decemb.' a probate was held after the late Peder Olsen Sunde, who lived on the farm Sunde in Borgund skipreide, between his widow Lisbet Larsdatter, with her guardian at law (lauværge), on one side and 'deris sammen avlede Børn' (their jointly begotten children) on the other. Heirs as read (several names uncertain): a son [Arne?] Pedersen, of age and present; a son Ole Pedersen; a daughter [Martha?] Pedersdatter married to Jon Olsen; '4 Siri Peders datter gift med Knud Jensen Møckelbust', for whom Johannes Christensen Sallevaag appeared; a daughter married to Rasmus Rasmussen [S?]inde; an unmarried daughter aged 22; and a daughter [Kirsten?] Pedersdatter aged 19, guardian John Olsen Sunde. The appraisers were Ole Olsen Weibust and Erik Sollevaag. Folio 43a, viewer image 24555/55 (urn sk20090126630467). ¶1

Age (inference): with a child of age in 1741 and a daughter born 1714, he was born before about 1690. His own parents are not found. ¶3

Corroboration (scan read): the probate after Jon Olsen of Sunde, 6 Jan 1745 (3A 15 fol. 249b), names his widow 'Martha Pedersdatter' with their children (Elling Olsen, Ole Olsen, Dorthe, Anne and Margrethe Olsdatter). This is the daughter '[Martha?] Pedersdatter married to Jon Olsen' of Peder's 1741 probate, so that reading is confirmed. Jon Olsen was Peder's son-in-law, not his brother, so the entry does not test Ole of Sunde as Peder's father. No probate of a Povel/Paul Olsen at Sunde is indexed 1720-1780 (Paul Olsen entries are at Dyb and Giskegrøften, 1745, unread). ¶5
