About the family

Mother of Halvor (1829), Hans (1833) and Ole (1836) Torjussen at Nordgarden, Nissedal. Her name in Ole's 1836 baptism varies by copy ('Gunnil Olsdatter' in one, 'Gurnild Halvorsdatter' in the other). Her eldest son was named Halvor after her father, and her granddaughter Gunhild after her. ¶1

Parents (Speculative, identification unproven): she may be Gunild, born 30 Nov 1794 and presented in Lunde church on 14 Dec 1794, daughter of Halvor Tempelen and Ingeri Hansdatter (Bø ministerialbok 1785-1815, Kirke-Aaret 1795 no. 12), who in 1801 was Gunild Halvorsdatter, 6, in the household of Halvor Torkildsen, 31, and Ingeri Hansdatter, 28, at Tempelen. In favour: the Lunde birthplace given at her burial ('Lundeherred'), and no rival candidate has survived the checks below. ¶6a

Life story

Marriage scan read: Bø ministerialbok 1815-1831, Copulerede 1824, p. 405 no. 10: 'Ungkarl og Soldat Torjuls Tollefsen Søftestad, 25 Aar' and 'Pige Gunnild Halvorsdatter, 28 Aar'; forlovere (sureties) Ole Grinsrud(?) and Lars Moe(?); married 9 May 1824 in the church. The entry gives no residence for the bride and names no fathers, so it does not decide between the baptism candidates. A note in the remarks column of the facing page 404 ('Tilstaaen 1824... Nov 1823') appears to belong to another entry. The sureties' farms (Grinsrud? and Moe) are the only new lead. ¶10

Marriage (per index): Torjuls Tollefsen of Soshestad (Søftestad) married Gunnild Halvorsdatter, 28, at Bø on 9 May 1824 (Bø church book 1815-1831, pp. 404-405, no. 10, pv00000009079104). No fathers or sureties are indexed. A second Gunnild Halvorsdatter, also 28, married Torkild Tollersen on the same day (pp. 406-407, pv00000009693998), so any citation of this marriage must be checked carefully. ¶7

Death: 'Gunnuld Halvorsdatter', føderaadskone (wife of a man retired on a farm pension), of Rønningen in Nissedal, died 26 Mar and was buried 19 Apr 1863, age 60, born 'Lundeherred' (Lunde, Telemark, now Nome; probable reading). She is identified as Torjus Tellefsen's wife by the farm (Torjus lived at Rønningen as a føderaadsmand), the status, and the timing: Torjus remarried as a widower on 16 Nov 1863, citing a probate court certificate of 31 Oct 1863. ¶3

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Speculative: she may be the Gunhild Halvorsdatter buried in Nissedal in May 1848; age not checked. Torjus had remarried Ingebjørg Bendiksdatter by 1865. Her marriage to Torjus (ca 1820-1829) and her origin were not found; very many women of this name lived in Kviteseid/Nissedal. ¶2

Records that disagree 1

Against: her 1824 marriage age of 28 (born about 1796) and her 1863 burial age of 60 (born about 1803) both conflict with a birth in Nov 1794; she would have been 29 and 68. Nothing ties this baptism to Torjus Tellefsen's wife by kin, godparent or farm. ¶6b

Corrections 1

Correction: an earlier note suggested she might be the Gunhild Halvorsdatter buried in Nissedal in May 1848. The scan shows that burial (died 21 May, buried 25 May 1848, Løvigen) was of an infant aged 14 weeks, so it is not her. ¶4

Alternatives considered and set aside 2

Baptism candidates (unresolved): the Bø/Lunde register (Bø ministerialbok 1785-1815, which covers Lunde) has two plausible baptisms. (1) Gunnild, born 3 Mar 1804, baptised 4 Mar 1804 at Bø, daughter of Halvor Halvorsen of Lia and Birgit Svendsdatter; she would have been 59, not 60, at death. (2) Gunnild, born 20 Dec 1800, baptised 26 Dec 1800 at Lunde, daughter of Halvor Olsen of Huustoften under Enggrav and Else Olsdatter; she would have been 62. Neither is adopted: nothing yet distinguishes them. A Gunild b. 1805 (father Halvor Hellikssen, Espedalen) is ruled out on age. The Torjus × Gunnil marriage (before 1829, perhaps in Lunde or Bø) or the 1863 skifte after her would settle it. ¶5

Candidates set aside: (1) Gunild b. 20 Apr 1796 at Røklev (Halvor Jørgensen x Ingebor Jensdatter, later of Østerlie), who fitted the marriage age exactly, married Halvor Olsen Hægna on 19 Dec 1831 and lived with him in 1865, while Torjus's wife was bearing children in Nissedal, so she is not Torjus's wife. (2) Gunnild b. 19 Feb 1797 at Øren (Halvor Nirisen x Margit) is probably the widow aged 68 at Skoe, Bø, in the 1865 census, alive after 1863; that identity is unproven, so she is set aside rather than ruled out. (3) Gunild b. 10 Nov 1796 at Folkestad died aged 10 months in 1797. The earlier candidates from the burial age (1800 Huustoften, 1804 Lia) remain unresolved. ¶8

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Would settle it: the scan of the 9 May 1824 marriage (Bø 1815-1831, p. 404-405), which may name her father or sureties; a probate (skifte) after her in 1863, which would name her heirs and perhaps her parents' farm; or a probate after Halvor Torkildsen or Ingeri Hansdatter of Tempelen naming a daughter Gunnild married to Torjus. ¶9
