About the family

Youngest of 11 children of Berent and Annie Erdahl; family moved from Malta, MT to Custer/Ferndale, Whatcom Co., WA in 1939. ¶1

Siblings: Luren Oliver (1901-1983), Minnie Bertina (1903-1924), Otis Merin (1905-1975), Bernice Olivia Kluken (1908-1995), Maynard Edwin (1910-1989), Inez Esther Lando (1913-1993), Elma Frida Njaa (1915-2013), Frances Elvina Montgomery (1917-1976), Bert Rueben (1919-2013), Gene Archie (1921-2009). ¶4

Brother's marriage (same year): four months earlier the same county index records 'Jean Archie Erdahl' and 'Gertie I Larson', married 10 February 1945 and filed 20 February 1945 (document nwwtcmcv38_42). This is probably William's brother Gene Archie Erdahl marrying Gertie Inga Larsen, the sister of William's wife Clarice, so two Erdahl brothers married two Larsen sisters in 1945. The index does not give ages or parents, so this is not yet confirmed. ¶6

Life story

Social Security Death Index (per index): 'William E. Erdahl', born 1 July 1923, died 2 December 1988, last residence Portland, Multnomah County, Oregon. The birth and death dates agree exactly with the tree and with his gravestone. Two other William Erdahls in the same index are different men by birth date (William M., born 1903, died 1991 in Tulsa; William R., born 1915, died 1996 in Bemidji). The record page with his Social Security number and the state of issue has not been opened. ¶10

Oregon death index (per index): 'William Ervin Erdahl', born 1 July 1923, died 2 December 1988 in Multnomah County, Oregon, with a spouse whose name begins 'Claric' (the rest is cut off in the index view). This is the first record to give his middle name Ervin in full, and the spouse fits his wife Clarice Louise Larsen. The index gives the county only, so the tree's death place, Portland, rests on the Social Security last residence and the gravestone, not on the death record itself; the death certificate would give the exact place. ¶11

1930 U.S. census (index only): 'William I. Erdahl', son, born about 1924 in Montana, was in his parents Berent and Annie's household in South Malta, Phillips County, Montana. The middle initial 'I' is probably a misreading of E (Ervin). The image has not been read. ¶8

1940 U.S. census (index only): he is indexed as 'William Edell' (alternative Erdahl), son of 'Ben L' and Annie, born about 1924, in South Malta, Phillips County, Montana. The index gives his birthplace as Minnesota, which conflicts with Malta, Montana in the tree and with 'Montana' in the 1930 index. The 1930 entry is preferred, because the family had left Minnesota by 1913. The 1940 page image (signed-in request S0113) would show whether 'Minnesota' is the enumerator's or the indexer's error. ¶9

Marriage record: the Whatcom County Auditor's marriage records index lists 'William E Erdahl' and 'Clarice L Larsen', married 1 June 1945 and filed 4 June 1945 (document nwwtcmcv38_246). This confirms the date on their headstone and places the marriage in Whatcom County, Washington. The index gives no ages, parents, witnesses or officiant, and the image has not been read. ¶5

Elder siblings' birth records: the Minnesota birth certificates of his five eldest siblings, all born in Murray County, survive: Luren Oliver (7 Jul 1901), Minnie Bertina (14 Jul 1903), Otis (1905 or 1906), Bernice Olivia (26 Feb 1908) and Maynard Edwin (8 Sep 1910). They are entered as separate people. ¶7

1950: glue mixer at a plywood factory, Ten Mile Twp., Whatcom Co., WA. ¶2

Shared headstone with Clarice: "Together Forever" / "Married 6-1-45" / "Complete In Thee". ¶3

1950 U.S. census, read from the page image: on 1 April 1950 he was head of a household in Whatcom County, Washington (enumeration district 37-21, dwelling 116, agriculture questionnaire 87). The street column appears to read 'Guide' (probably the Guide Meridian road; uncertain), with the house location given as a distance south of Smith (Road). He is 'Erdahl, William E.', 26, married, born in Montana, who worked 40 hours the week before as a glue mixer in a plywood factory. His wife Clarice L., 23, was born in North Dakota and kept house; two young sons were in the household. The index read the surname as 'Erdall'; the image clearly reads Erdahl. ¶13

1950 census, agreement with known dates: his age of 26 fits his birth on 1 July 1923, and Clarice's 23 fits hers on 19 December 1926. The birthplace 'Montana' agrees with the tree and the 1930 census index, and goes against the 'Minnesota' of the 1940 index. His parents and his brother Gene were then on LaBounty Road in a different enumeration district (37-37), so by 1950 William had his own farm household. ¶14

Records that disagree 1

Residence 1939-1940, a conflict: the family account says the family moved from Malta to Custer, Whatcom County, Washington in 1939, but the 1940 census index places William, aged about 16, in South Malta, Montana with his parents, while his mother and brother Gene are also indexed at Custer. Both are recorded. For William's own whereabouts on 1 April 1940 the census, made at the time, is preferred over the family's year for the move; the move was probably gradual, with part of the family still on the Malta farm. This is inferred from index entries only; the 1940 page images (signed-in request S0113) would settle it. ¶12
