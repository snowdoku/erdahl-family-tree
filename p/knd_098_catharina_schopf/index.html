Confidence: the grade and how it changed 4

Probable mother of Johann Melchior Lindenmaier the elder (see his page). ¶1a

Graded on that link: inferred. She is the only wife of Nicolaus Lindenmaier found: married Metterzimmern 3 Sep 1737, her bride row 'Catharina Schopfen', relatives 'Hn Jacob, Nicolaus' (record 905517933, image 1184926-00850), and named as mother in his children's baptisms at Bietigheim from 1738 to 1761. The elder Johann Melchior's baptism (about 1745-50) is not indexed, so her motherhood is inferred from the marriage span; it also carries Nicolaus's Probable grade. ¶1b

Labelled evidence, grade unchanged (Probable; same identification issue as her husband Nicolaus Lindenmaier): V4LM-H8L, Johann Melchior Lindenmajer christened Bietigheim 1 Feb 1748, Parents 'Nicolaus Lindenmajer, Catharina'; other 'Births and Baptisms' rows name Nicolaus's wife 'Catharina Schopf' / 'Catharina Schopff' (VH35-TTF, V4LM-FNG). Her baptism is labelled: FamilySearch, 'Germany, Births and Baptisms, 1558-1898' V4RW-6HS (twin QPGY-685L), Catharina Schopf, christened Metterzimmern 1 Sep 1718, Parents 'Jacob Schopf, Anna Catharina'; marriage QPGY-D23L names her Father-in-law field 'Hn Jacob Schopfen'. Read signed in, 2 Nov 2026; labelled index fields, image not seen (partner-only). ¶3

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed, graded on her link to her son Johann Melchior Lindenmaier the elder (see his page). Record: baptism V4LM-H8L ('Germany, Births and Baptisms, 1558-1898', labelled), Johann Melchior Lindenmajer christened Bietigheim 1 Feb 1748, Parents 'Nicolaus Lindenmajer, Catharina'. Continuity: the tree's elder Johann Melchior is the groom of 8 Feb 1774 (now settled from the images, see Nicolaus Lindenmaier), described as 'weil. Nicolaus Lindenmajers... ehel. led. Sohn'. Whole window (audit 5 rule 1): a single son marrying in 1774 as a master shoemaker was born about 1735-1754. Nicolaus married her on 3 Sep 1737 at Metterzimmern; the image of that entry calls him 'Hr Johann Christoph Lindenmajers... ehl. Sohn' with no widower marker, so there was no earlier marriage. She is named as the mother at baptisms from 1738 through 1761 (Christina Catharina 1738... Nicolaus 1761), so the marriage covers the whole window from Sep 1737 on. A groom born 1735-Aug 1737 would be illegitimate, and the record calls him 'ehel.'; the 1748 baptism shows the son of that name. Name reuse means there were not two living sons named Johann Melchior. The burial of the elder Johann Melchior (after 1814) was not found indexed. Her earlier Probable notes are superseded. Image read signed in on FamilySearch, 2 Nov 2026. ¶4
