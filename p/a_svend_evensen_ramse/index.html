About the family

NEW ANCESTOR. Farmer at Nedre Ytre Ramse in Tovdal. His estate was settled on 13 March 1696, a date cited repeatedly in later Ramse files as "skiftebrev etter Svend Evensøn Ramse, dat. 13/3-1696". ¶1

His wife Ingeborg Olsdatter survived him by sixteen years; her estate, settled 25 June 1712 (Skifteprotokoll no. 4, folio 41), divided the inheritance among their children and lists them: Evind of Nedre Ytre Ramse (our ancestor, estate 1722); Tellef of Øvre Ramse, who died in 1712 leaving children Sven and Anna; Niels, of age, living at Ramse (estate 1757 at Sunde in Gjerstad); Signe, married to Salve Folkesen of Strømme in Hylestad (estate 1737); Anna, married to Wraal of Mykland (estate 1730); and Birgitha, 17. ¶2

His father is stated outright on the archivist's cross-reference card: "Mannens far: Even Tollefsen Ramse", with a further note referring to a deed registered 22 March 1715 on Haugeland in Mykland. ¶4

His son Tellef Svendsen is the "Tellef Svendsen" of yttre Ramse, Lille Topdal who received a royal marriage licence in 1704 to marry Liv Gullovsdatter despite their kinship in the second and fourth degree. Liv's father was Gullov Tellefsen of Haagedal, named in the 1712 probate as her laugverge (legal guardian) — the same Haagedal where Tellef Torjusen, the future Tellef T. Ramsey, was born in 1853. ¶5

Mother not determined: Even Tellefsen Ramse married twice, first to an unnamed wife and then to Anna Evindsdatter, who was living in 1704 (card 10365). None of the Ramse cards says which marriage Svend came from. Card 10365 lists only Rolf (first marriage) and Groe (second marriage). It also leaves out Svend's known siblings Knud Evindsen Risland and Signe Evindsdatter, so it records the parties to the 1704 deed, not a full list of heirs, and its silence about Svend proves nothing. Two pointers lean weakly towards the second marriage. He named a daughter Anna, perhaps after his mother. He was still fathering children about 1695: his daughter Birgitha was 17 in 1712. Neither pointer is proof. His own 1696 skiftebrev (probate settlement), or the 1725 estate of his brother Knud Evindsen Risland, could settle it. ¶6

Family: Card 10415 (pantebok A5 fol. 94b, deed registered 22 March 1715) adds a daughter, Wilbør (Vilbør) Svendsdatter, to the 1712 list of children. She married first Thellef Gullovsen Haakondal (estate 1712) and second Aslak Tellefsen Engenes (estate 1755). The card also lists Birgitha as married to Ole Gundersen Ose in Bygland (estate 1745). Card 10383 names his brother Knud Evindsen Risland (estate 1725) and his sister Signe married to Joen Aslaksen of Tverstøl (estate 1720). Card 10417 calls Signe's husband Joen Aslaksen Gunelifjell. ¶7

Mother (Probable): Anna (Anne) Evindsdatter, Even Tellefsen Ramse's last wife. The Tovdal bygdebok ('Ytre Ramse', canvas 327) lists the children of Eivind and Anne (married before 1655) as a son born 1656 married to Ingeborg (linked with Hillestad), Knut b. 1662 (Risland), and Signe (m. Jon Aslakson). Svend's age of 10 in the 1666 vicar's census (b. about 1656) and his brother Knud, 4 in 1666 and later of Risland, fit. Life story

The 1712 estate held 1 hud 9 skinn in Nedre Ytre Ramse, 3 skinn in Øvre Ramse, 1½ skinn in Baas in Åmli and a share in Bjørnli — a scattered holding typical of these inland farms, where each heir took a fraction of several farms rather than a whole one. ¶3

Probate protocol reading: both the 1712 skifte after his widow (Skifteprotokoll no. 4, fol. 42) and the 1720-21 skifte after his daughter-in-law (no. 6, fol. 3) cite his own skiftebrev of 13 March 1696, made by the then sorenskriver of Råbyggelaget, the late Mathias Christensen Schrøder, with two lay assessors. In 1712 the paternal inheritances of his daughters Anna (6 rd) and Birgitha (15 rd 12 sk) under that skiftebrev were still owing from the estate. His son Evind received 9 kalveskinn in Nedre Ytre Ramse under it. The 1696 skiftebrev itself is cited, not copied, in these protocols. ¶8

1666 vicar's census of Lille Topdal (Sogneprestenes manntall 1664-66 bd 14, p.46), farm no. 3 'Ytre Ramsøa Nedra, 4 huder': opsiddere Effuind 80 aar (uses 3 huder) and Tollef 30 aar (uses 1 hud); sons column, against Effuind: Svend 10 aar and Knud 4 aar; servants: Throen Rasolfsøn 26, [Hal?]da 18[?], Rolff 26[?16]. Svend, aged 10 (born about 1656), is listed as a son at Nedre Ramse under Effuind. Identification with Svend Evensen Ramse (d. 1696) is probable: same farm, father Effuind; patronymic not written. ¶9
