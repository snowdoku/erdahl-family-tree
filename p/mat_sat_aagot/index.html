About the family

Second wife of Gunder Gundersen Satter, married 1900, five years after the death of his first wife Guri. She was thirty-two years younger than he was. Known so far only from the spouse listing on his Find a Grave memorial, which is an index entry with no photograph, so Probable at best. ¶1

Life story

A 'Miss Wangberg' accompanied 'Mr. and Mrs. G. Sether' to the Ahlsborg church near Spink (Union County Leader, 1 August 1901), and 'Misses Walborg and Wangberg of Sioux City' visited Spink in January 1902 (Leader, 23 January 1902). These support a Sioux City Wangberg family connected with Gunder by 1901. Gunder was already 'Mr. and Mrs.' by then, so the marriage to Aagot most likely took place between mid-1900 and 1 August 1901. ¶3

Found in a record of her own: the 1905 South Dakota state census lists 'Sather, Mrs. Gunder, aged 35' on card 218, immediately after Gunder himself on card 217, in Spink Township, Union County, Section 21, T93N R50W. The stated age is two or three years younger than her 1867 birth year implies, which is ordinary. This confirms she was living as his wife ten years after Guri's death. ¶2

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
