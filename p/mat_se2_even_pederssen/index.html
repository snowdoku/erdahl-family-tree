About the family

Kari Pedersdatter Eidem's maternal grandfather. 1801 census, Selbu, rural residence 0030 UGLAN, household 04: 'Even Pederssen', 42, Husbonde, 'Bonde og gaardbeboer' - a farmer occupying the farm, which is a distinctly better standing than the husmenn (cottagers) on the other three sides of this family. His wife is Karen Poulsdatter, 43, and their five children are Brynil (14), Peder (11), Giertrue (9), Magli (5) and Poul (2). ¶1

Son Peder's baptism: 'Doebt Peder. Par: Even Ped. Karen Poulsd.', baptised on Laetare Sunday (the Fourth Sunday in Lent), 14 March 1790, Selbu ministerialbok 1757-1801 (canvas 135, right page). Godparents: Anders Uglum, Jon ibidem (of Uglum), Jon Garb(erg), Karen Ugl(um) and Marit Fors. He is the Peder Evenssen, 11, of the 1801 census, Uglan household 04, and an elder brother of Mali Evensdatter (see her page), Kari Pedersdatter's mother. The baptism puts Even Pederssen and Karen Poulsdatter at Uglem as a couple by 1789, which agrees with the census. ¶3

Children in the 1784-1795 register (Selbu ministerialbok 1757-1801): Giertrud, baptised at the Annunciation 1786, godparents Anders of Uglem, Peder Forsan, Karen Uglem, Mali Garberg, Mali Uglem (canvas 120); Brynild, baptised 5 August 1787 (canvas 127); Peder, baptised 14 March 1790 (canvas 135); a second Giertrud, baptised 2 December 1792 as daughter of 'Even Pedersen Auglem og Karen Povelsdatter' (canvas 149); and Povel, baptised 29 March 1795, buried after dying on 30 April 1795 aged 6 weeks (canvases 175 and 177). Brynil 14, Peder 11 and Giertrue 9 in the 1801 household are the children of 1787, 1790 and 1792; the second Giertrud implies the first had died, though her burial has not been seen. Read. ¶5

Daughter Mali's baptism: 'Magli', born 20 June 1796 and baptised 26 June 1796 (5th Sunday after Trinity), parents 'Even Peders. Uglem og Karen Povels d:' (Selbu ministerialbok 1757-1801, canvas 194 right page). She is the Magli Evensdatter, 5, of his 1801 household. Godparents Erich Andersen Uglem, Jon Povelsen Garberg, Anna Erichsdatter Uglem, Jertrud Andersdatter Garberg and Jertrud Olsdatter Uglem. He himself stood godfather in September 1796 to Even, son of Haftor Evensen and Guru Nielsdatter ('Even Pedersen Uglem', canvas 196). ¶10

Daughter's marriage: his daughter Magli (Mali) married the widower Peder Bersvendsen of Eidem on 5 June 1826; the Selbu 1824-1842 book names the bride's father 'Even Peders.', and the 1816-1831 book calls her a farmer's daughter (Bonde Datter) of Auglan (Uglem), aged 30. Her uncle Niels Paulsen Garberg stood surety. Scans. ¶12

Parents now Confirmed: the baptism of his brother Jon on 4 May 1769 to 'Peder Evens: Ugl: og Brynild Erichsd.' (Selbu ministerialbok 1757-1801, canvas 31 right page), and 'Ped: Evensøn Uglum' as a sponsor in September 1769 (canvas 33 right page), show his parents Per Evensen and Brynill Eriksdatter - of Garberg at his own baptism in 1759 - living at Uglem by 1769. See Per (Peder) Evensen. ¶14

Life story

Baptism: 'Even', son of Per Evensen Garberg and Brynill Eriksdatter, baptised 23 September 1759 (15th Sunday after Trinity), godparents Erik Sletnum, Ole Garberg and Giertrud of Garberg; Selbu ministerialbok 1757-1801, canvas 8 right page (crop). His parents had married on 11 June 1758. Parents linked as Probable; the reasoning, and the records that would confirm it, are on Per (Peder) Evensen (unique Even son of a Peder in the complete read of 1757-67, age at death 53 and 1801 age 42, sons and daughters named Peder and Brynild, the family's move from Garberg to Uglan, sister Mali of 1764 at Store Evjen as godmother in 1795). His birth date is now 'before 23 September 1759' in place of ABT 1759; a September 1759 birth makes him 41, not 42, in February 1801 and 52, not 53, in July 1812 - both within the usual error. ¶8

Bygdebok cross-check: Selbu i fortid og naatid vol. 4, p. 76 (word boxes only) lists Even, b. 1759, as a son of Per Evensen, holder of Uglan 1769-1791, and has Even himself holding the farm 1792-1814. It gives Per's wife as 'Mali, b. 1728', which conflicts with the register's Brynill Eriksdatter; the register is preferred (see the note on his mother). ¶9

Marriage: betrothed on Cantate Sunday, 9 May 1784 (date computed from Easter; the entry gives only the Sunday), 'unge Mand Even Pedersen Uglum' and 'Karen Povelsd. Garberg' - the bachelor Even Pedersen of Uglem and Karen Povelsdatter of Garberg; forlovere Svend Erichsen Uglum and Anders Jensen Uglum (canvas 114 right page). Married on the 1st Sunday after Trinity, 13 June 1784: 'Copul. Even Pedersen Ugl. & Karen Poulsd. Garberg' (canvas 115 left page). Selbu ministerialbok 1757-1801 and the crops. This replaces the inferred 'before 1787'. ¶4

Uglan (Uglem) was a large Selbu farm; the 1801 census has four households on it. His daughter Gjertru was still there in 1865. ¶2

Death and burial: buried on the 7th Sunday after Trinity, 12 July 1812 (date computed): 'Gaardmanden Even Pedersen Uglen, død d. 3 Julii, gl. 53 Aar' - the farmer Even Pedersen of Uglem, died 3 July, aged 53. Selbu ministerialbok 1801-1815, p. 176, left page; the index entry (pg00000001727819) was and the scan on 13 Oct 2026. The identification is strong: same name, same farm, the same standing (gaardmand, farmer; 'bonde og gaardbeboer' in 1801), and age 53 gives a birth year of 1758-59, exactly that of the Even Pederssen aged 42 at Uglan in February 1801. No other Even Pedersen at Uglem is known. Mali was about 16 when he died. ¶6

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 3

Confidence left at Probable, and why. The new records prove Even and Karen as a married couple at Uglem from 1784 with the children of the 1801 household. They do not touch the one inferential step on which his place in the tree rests: that 'Magli Evensdatter', 5, in his 1801 household is the Mali Evensdatter who was Kari Pedersdatter's mother (1834) and the widow at Braendhaugen (1865). That step rests on her being the only Evensdatter of that age in the 1801 census of Selbu, on her 1865 age and birthplace, and on her sister Gjertru still at Uglem in 1865 - a strong fit with nothing against it, and the bygdebok skeleton (vol. 4, p. 206) adds a 'Mali 1796-1872' as Peder Bersvendsen's wife. Her baptism has not been found: read the register from September 1792 to 24 March 1796 without it, and her brother Povel was born in March 1795, so she was born after about April 1796. Records that would settle it: her marriage to Peder Bersvendsen (about 1826, the scan of the Selbu marriages, which should give her residence and age); Even's probate (skifte) of 1812 in the Strinda og Selbu sorenskriveri skifteprotokoll, which would list Mali among his heirs; or her baptism in canvases 191-205 of source 16311. ¶7

Confidence left at Probable after the baptism, and why. The baptism gives the Uglem Magli an exact birth, 20 June 1796, matching the birth year 1796 that the 1865 census gives Kari Pedersdatter's mother, and a new search of the 1801 census finds no other Magli or Mali Evensdatter born 1793-1798 anywhere in Selbu. The bygdebok skeleton (vol. 4, p. 76) puts 'Mali, 1796' among his children with 'Per... Brennhaugen'. Every element agrees and nothing conflicts, but no primary record that was read names Mali of Braendhaugen as his daughter: the rule for Confirmed (a record that states the link) is not met. ¶11a

Confidence of his link as Mali Evensdatter's father raised from Probable to Confirmed (13 Oct 2026), applying the audit standard of (the adult's own marriage names the baptism farm and the fit is unique). Peder Bersvendsen's second marriage, 5 June 1826, gives his bride as Magli Evensdatter of Auglan (Uglem), aged 30 (born about 1796), a farmer's daughter, and the second church book names her father as 'Even Peders.' (Even Pedersen). Magli, daughter of Even Pedersen of Uglem and Karen Povelsdatter, was baptised on 26 June 1796 (born 20 June); she is the only Magli/Mali Evensdatter born 1793-1798 in the 1801 census of Selbu, and Even was a farmer (gaardmand) at Uglem, matching 'Bonde Datter'. A surety at the wedding was Niels Paulsen (Povelsen) of Garberg, Karen Povelsdatter's brother (baptised 1761), i.e. the bride's maternal uncle. The same Magli Evensdatter is the mother of Giertrug (1830) and Kari (1834) by Peder Bersvendsen of Eidem, and Mali Evensdatter, widow, born 1796, lives with Kari in 1865. The marriage thus ties Kari's mother to the 1796 baptism by name, father, farm and age, with no rival. ¶13
