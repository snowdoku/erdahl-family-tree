Life story

Baptism (index): Siri, daughter of Peder Olss. of Sunde, was baptised at Borgund on 11 Nov 1714 (pd00000001505946). The date given is the baptism date. This identification rests on three points: the 1739 marriage calls her Siri Pedersd. of Sunde, the father's residence in this baptism is Sunde, and her burial age (71 in Feb 1785) gives a birth year of about 1714. The only other Borgund candidate, Siri baptised 29 Sep 1707 to Peder Perss. of Hessen (pd00000001504982), fits the patronymic as well but not the residence or the burial age. The baptism scan has not been read, and her father, Peder Olsen of Sunde, is not added to the tree. For these reasons her link to him is Probable. ¶1

Marriage (index): on 22 Nov 1739 at Borgund she married Knud Jenss., a bachelor of Mychlebust (Myklebust) (pv00000002822979). The register notes that she was already pregnant by him ('NB af ham besvangrit'). ¶2

1762 extra tax list (scan read; DA 49637 view 42, p. 71, Borgund sogn, Mychlebust household 5): 'M: Knud Jensen, H: Siri Pedersd.', children over 12 'Jens' and 'Christiana'. This is the first record that names her (not only Knud) as Christiana's mother; the 1765 probate names her only as Knud's widow, alongside 'the deceased's children'. Grade unchanged (Probable), graded on her link to Christiana Knudsdatter, for the same reason as Knud: the step from the Myklebust daughter of 1762-1767 to the Skjong bride of 1777 crosses farms and rests on her own 1766 remarriage to Peder Andersen of Skjong and the 1775 debt entry. ¶10

1762 extra tax (scan read): Myklebust, Borgund: 'M: Knud Jensen; H: Siri Pedersd.', children over 12 Jens and Christiana (source 49637, view 42). She is the wife and the children are listed under the couple; no grade change, for the same reason as her husband's. ¶11

Widow (scan read): at the probate after Knud Jensen of Myklebust on 3 Aug 1765 (Sunnmøre Skifteprotokoll 15A = 3A 21, fol. 428b), she is the surviving wife, 'efterlevende Hustrue Siri Peders datter', and her legal guardian (laugverge) was Guttorm Larsen. The heirs on the other side are 'the deceased's children': Jens (of age), Lisbeth (22) and Christhana (15). ¶3

Second marriage (index): on 23 Oct 1766 at Borgund, Siri Pedersd., a widow of Myklebust, married Peder Anderss., a widower of Schiong (Skjong) (pv00000002824764). ¶4

Burial (index): Siri Pedersd., a widow of Schiong, was buried at Borgund on 13 Feb 1785, aged 71 (pg00000002550546). No probate after her has been found in the index. ¶5

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

3 on the grade

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 3

Upgrade: the probate after her father Peder Olsen Sunde, 18 Dec 1741 (Sunnmøre sorenskriveri Skifteprotokoll 11 = 3A 14, fol. 43a; scan), names as heir no. 4 'Siri Peders datter gift med Knud Jensen Møckelbust' (Siri Pedersdatter, married to Knud Jensen of Myklebust). This ties the wife of Knud Jensen Myklebust, already in the tree, to Peder Olsen of Sunde and to the widow Lisbet Larsdatter, whose 'jointly begotten children' the heirs are said to be. It confirms the 1714 Sunde baptism identification, which until now rested on residence and burial age. ¶7

Audit (1 Nov 2026): downgraded from Confirmed to Probable. The 1741 Sunde probate proves her parentage and her marriage to Knud Jensen Myklebust, and Peder Olsen Sunde and Lisbet Larsdatter stay Confirmed. Her own place in the pedigree, as mother of Christiana Knudsdatter, is not proved: the 1765 probate calls the heirs only 'the deceased's children', and the link from Knud's daughter to the wife of Peder Olsen Skjong is circumstantial (see Knud Jensen). The upgrade record addresses the wrong link. ¶8

Re-proof attempt (overnight 1-2 Nov 2026), no change: still Probable. No probate after her is indexed (Sunnmøre probate index, Skjong 1776-1800: Peder Gundersen 1776, Knut Pedersen 1777, Gunder Madsen 1778, Hans Pedersen 1779, Marthe Pedersd. 1784, Siri Jonsd. 1784, Peder Jonsen 1788, Amund Sjursen 1788, Inge Johansen 1793, Nils Pedersen 1799; no Siri Pedersdatter). See Knud Jensen for the other sources checked. ¶9
