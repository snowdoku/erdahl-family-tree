About the family

Wife: Lydia (see her page), Probable, from the Terling baptism of their son John. This supersedes the earlier 'not entered' lead notes on the wife; her maiden name Gaymer remains Speculative and is not entered. ¶7

Wife's family: his wife Lydia (see her page) is now entered as Lydia Gaymer, daughter of Richard Gaymer, miller, of Terling, and Margaret Mason (Probable), on the evidence printed in NEHGR 151 (1997) and checked in FreeREG. His own parentage is still unknown. NEHGR 151 notes a Terling couple, John and Joan (Lily) Turner, who had seven children baptised there in 1579-1594, four of them named John, Joseph, Nathaniel and Thomas, as Humphrey's sons were, and suggests he might be of that family. No baptism of a Humphrey Turner was found, so no parents are entered (lead only). ¶10

Life story

Marriage read on the image: Sandon, Essex, 24 Oct 1618, 'Humphry Turner and Lidda Gamar' (FamilySearch DGS 004298638 item 24, image 70 of 93). Parents: the FamilySearch indexes (England, Essex, Parish Registers; England Births and Christenings) have no Humphrey Turner baptised in Essex 1585-1600 (searched 2 Nov 2026). ¶14

English residence (Probable): a Humfry Turner and his wife Lydia had a son John baptised at Terling, Essex, on 24 Mar 1621/22 (FreeREG; Ancestry 9841), and a Humphry Turner had a daughter Lydia baptised at Little Baddow, Essex, on 17 Feb 1629/30 (Ancestry 9841 index). This is taken to be him, before emigration: see the identity note on John Turner. The 1618 Sandon marriage reported by NEHGR 151 (1997) was not found in FreeREG or Ancestry 9852. ¶6

Deed of gift: on 1 October 1668 'Humphrey Turner of Scittuate … Tanner', for natural affection, gave his sons Joseph and Nathaniel Turner a lot of upland on the east side of the Taunton River, the second lot from the Fall River, together with his share of the Freemen's grant of upland and meadow (the Freemen's Purchase). The witnesses were Thomas Pinson Sr (his mark), Thomas Pinson and James Cudworth. On 3 July 1669 he acknowledged the deed before Josiah Winslow, Assistant, and 'att the same time Lydia his wife did passe over her right in lands above mensioned to her abovenamed sonnes'. On 20 February 1669/70 Nathaniel, a tanner, sold half the lot to his brother Joseph, planter (Plymouth Colony Deeds vol. 3 pp. 175-176). ¶8

Origin, literature check: no article after Great Migration Begins (1995, 'ORIGIN: Unknown') giving Humphrey's English baptism was found. The full-text search covered TAG, NEHGR, the Great Migration Newsletter 1990-2006 and the Mayflower Descendant on archive.org. The newsletter's 'Recent Literature' notice of V. D. Turner's 1997 NEHGR article mentions only the identity of his wife. Turner's article itself opens by saying that Humphrey's origins 'have been the subject of considerable research', from Deane (1831) on, and it does not give a baptism. FreeREG indexes no baptism, marriage or burial of a Humphrey Turner in Essex 1610-1640 apart from his son John's baptism at Terling (24 Mar 1621/22, father 'Humfry'). ¶11

Will: 'The last day of february 1669 I Humphery Turner of Scittuate.... Tanner.... make this my last will'. He left twelve pence to 'my eldest son John Turner'; twelve pence more to his son Joseph Turner, already given £40 as his portion; £5 to 'my son Young John Turner'; twelve pence to 'my son Daniell Turner'; £50 and all his livestock to 'my son Nathaniel: Turner'; his wearing clothes and a wool bed to 'my son Thomas Turner'; £10 to 'my Daughter Mary Parker'; twelve pence to 'my Daughter Lydia Doughtey'; £5 to 'my Grandchild Humphery Turner'; £10 to 'my Grandchild Mary Doughtey'; £15 to 'my Grand Child Abigaill Turner the Daughter of My son Nathaniel'; and ten shillings apiece to 'my Grand Children Jonathan Turner Joseph Turner and Ezekiell Turner, being the sonnes of my eldest sonne'. Young John and Nathaniel were joint executors. Witnesses: Mathew Gannett (by a mark), Nathaniel Tilden and James Cudworth. Captain James Cudworth gave oath to the will at the court at Plymouth on 5 June 1673 (The Mayflower Descendant 24:42-43, abstract of the Plymouth Colony will book, p. 71). No wife is named in the will. ¶1

Death: after he made his will on the last day of February 1669/70 (Old Style; the year then began on 25 March) and before the will was proved on 5 June 1673, presumably at Scituate. Inferred from the will dates only; no death record was read. ¶2

Identity (Probable) as father of John Turner Sr. ('the Elder') of Scituate (see his page): the will names 'my eldest son John' and gives legacies to 'Jonathan Turner Joseph Turner and Ezekiell Turner, being the sonnes of my eldest sonne'. John Turner Senior's own will of 1696 names his sons Jonathan, Joseph and Ezekiel (MD 5:41-45), and the Scituate births give Jonathan (1646) and Joseph (1649) as sons of John Sr. The will also distinguishes a second son 'Young John', which matches Scituate's John Sr./John Jr. pair; editor Bowman's footnote says the elder John married Mary Brewster and the younger married Ann James. Not Confirmed because the will does not write 'Senior' or 'the Elder' after the eldest son's name; the grandsons' names make the identification. ¶3a

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed, on two primary wills read together. Humphrey Turner's will of the last day of February 1669/70 gives twelve pence to 'my eldest son John Turner', £5 separately to 'my son Young John Turner', and ten shillings each to 'my Grand Children Jonathan Turner Joseph Turner and Ezekiell Turner, being the sonnes of my eldest sonne' (MD 24:42-43). John Turner Senior's own will of 4 March 1695/6 names his sons Jonathan, Joseph and Ezekiel Turner (with John, Elisha, Amos and Benjamin) (MD 5:41-45). The testator thus himself identifies his eldest son John as the father of Jonathan, Joseph and Ezekiel, and distinguishes him from 'Young John'; John Senior's will shows that the father of those three sons is John Turner Senior of Scituate, who married Mary Brewster in 1645 as 'John Turner of Situate the Elder'. The earlier objection, that Humphrey's will does not add 'Senior', is answered by the grandsons' names, which the will itself ties to the eldest son. The rating covers Humphrey as father of John Turner Senior; his English origin (Terling/Sandon) is still Probable. ¶9

Alternatives considered and set aside 2

Wife (lead, not entered): compiled works call his wife Lydia Gamer or Gaymer. Vernon Dow Turner's article in the NEHG Register 151 (1997) says a Scituate land record (Plymouth Colony Deeds, vol. 3) names Humphrey's wife as Lydia. It also quotes English parish registers: the marriage of 'Humphry Turner and Lidda Gamar' at Sandon, Essex, on 24 October 1618; the baptism of 'John the sonne of Humfry Turner & Lydia his wife' at Terling, Essex, in March 1621/2; the baptism of Lydia, daughter of Humphry Turner, at Little Baddow, Essex, on 17 February 1629/30; and the baptism of Lydia, daughter of Richard Gaymer, at Terling in May 1602. None of these registers or the deed has been read, so no wife and no English origin are entered. The next step is to read the Sandon, Terling and Little Baddow registers (Essex Record Office) and Plymouth Colony Deeds vol. 3. ¶5

Lead only, not entered: Emmison's abstract (Bishop of London's Commissary Court, no. 717) of the will of Humphrey Turner of Bocking, Essex, dated 13 Oct 1563, about 10 miles north of Terling. It names his wife Agnes; his sons John 'my 2nd son', Edmund 'my youngest son' (executors), Robert, Thomas, Harry (under 17, to have the house in Bocking) and 'John the elder'; and his daughters Mary, Margery, Elizabeth, Alice, Agnes and Rose. He also held the lease of Langford Mill. The rare forename makes this family a candidate for Humphrey of Scituate's grandfather (born about 1593-95, he could be a son of one of these sons), but no record connects them. Bocking registers (from 1558; ERO D/P 268) and the wills of the sons are the place to test it. ¶12

Searches that found nothing 3

Negative: FreeREG Essex Turner baptisms 1578-1602 (253 rows) list at Terling Katherin 1578/9, John 1581, Joseph 1583, Augustine 1586, Nathanaell 1589, Thomas 1591, George 1594, John 1598 and William 1601, but no Humphrey (or Humfrey/Umfrey) anywhere in Essex. So his baptism is not in FreeREG's Essex coverage; the John and Joan Turner family of Terling remains a lead only. ¶13

Search for his baptism (FamilySearch, signed in). Sandon, where he married Lydia Gamar on 24 Oct. 1618: the register's baptisms for 1578-1600 were READ ON THE IMAGE (FamilySearch DGS 004298638, item 24, ERO D/P 253/1/1, images 10-13 of 93, opened through the indexed marriage 1:1:XKZW-K5C). There is no Turner baptism; in this period the register gives only the child's name ('Humfrey Prevent the sonne of Thomas Prevent' in 1596 is the only Humphrey of the 1590s). The Sandon Turners in the index are all later: Edward Turner x Jsabell Dores 12 Jan. 1617/8, Deborah Turner (m. 1623 and 1624/5), Mary Turner (m. 1625), and Alice Turner buried 29 Oct. 1636. Little Baddow (index from film 560908, baptisms and burials 1585-1600 indexed) and Danbury have no Turner baptisms 1575-1605. The Terling baptisms are not on FamilySearch (only C. R. Strutt's typescript, DGS 7942689, which is locked), and the Little Baddow and Great Baddow images are center-only. So no parents can be entered from FamilySearch. ¶15a

Origin, search (negative): (1) FreeREG, nationwide, Humphrey Turner (all spellings) baptisms 1575-1605: only Kingsley, Staffs. 1582; Ashbourne, Derbys. 13 Jun 1585; Bodmin, Cornwall 27 Oct 1594; St Peter Cornhill, London 29 Oct 1598; Manchester 3 Jun 1599; Wigan 26 Apr 1601. None in Essex, Hertfordshire or Suffolk, and nothing ties any of them to Sandon or Terling. (2) Essex Archives Online catalogue (ERO wills): no will of a Turner of Terling, Sandon, Danbury, the Baddows, Hatfield Peverel, Boreham or Witham before 1650. The only Essex Humphrey Turner testator is Humphrey Turner of Bocking (ERO D/ABW 37/137, 1566; already noted as a lead). (3) The Essex Turner tanners in the ERO catalogue are of Saffron Walden. The will of Robert Turner of Audley End, Walden, tanner (ERO D/ACW 8/207, 1619), read on the free image, names his brother-in-law Bennet Burton (executor), Thomas Burton, his sister Jane Payton and William Turner of Walden, but no children and no Humphrey. The will of Edmund Turner of Brook Walden, tanner (ERO D/ACW 9/209, 1625) names 'Edmund Turner my eldest son'. The free image is too small to read the rest, so it has not been checked for a Humphrey. (4) The archive.org full-text search found no Essex will abstract naming 'my son Humphrey' Turner. No parents are entered. ¶16a
