About the family

Her father William Peacock, mercer of London, is added as Probable on Lodge's statement alone (William Peacock). The London visitations of 1568 and 1633-35, searched in full, have no Boyle pedigree and no William Peacock, mercer. The Earl of Cork's diaries mention 'my cozen Peacock' several times (Lismore Papers, 1st series, vol. 2), but the 1633-35 London visitation explains that cousinship through the Naylors: William Peacocke, merchant, married Elizabeth, daughter of John Nayler of Kent, the family of the Earl's mother. So it is no evidence for Jane. Her own grade is unchanged. Added. ¶3

Life story

Jane, daughter and coheir of William Peacock, mercer of London, and wife of Michael Boyle, merchant of London. She was the mother of his eleven sons and two daughters, including Archbishop Richard Boyle of Tuam, Bishop Michael Boyle of Waterford, and Jane, wife of Thomas Caldwell (Lodge, Peerage of Ireland, 1789, vol. 1, p. 144: 'married Jane, daughter and coheir to William Peacock, mercer of London, by whom he left eleven sons and two daughters'). Added. ¶1

The 1550 PCC will of William Pecoke (PROB 11/33/60), is not her father's. It names a son Angell and one daughter (not Jane), and he died at Haddington in Scotland. Grade unchanged (Probable, on her link to Jane Boyle). ¶4

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the grade · 1 open question

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Probable, graded on her link to her daughter Jane Boyle. Lodge (a compiled source) names her as the mother of all thirteen children, including Jane. No second marriage of Michael Boyle is recorded there. Her father William Peacock is not added, because only Lodge names him and nothing tests it. ¶2a

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Test record: the will of William Peacock, mercer of London (PCC, 16th century), naming his daughter Jane Boyle; or the Boyle pedigree in the Visitation of London. Added. ¶2b
