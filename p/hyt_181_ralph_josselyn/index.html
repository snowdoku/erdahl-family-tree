About the family

Father link to Thomas Joslin, new evidence: a trust deed of September 1613 made between 'Ralph Josselin Senior of Roxwell, Essex, and John Jude of Radwinter' names 'Thomas Josselin, one of the sons of me the aforesaid Ralph Josselin the elder, and Rebecca Jude' (quoted in the Great Migration Newsletter review of R. D. Joslyn, NEHGR 158 (2004):320-340). Symon Joceline's will of 1649 (NEHGR 71:30) recites a deed dividing Ralph's farm Bollinghatch between 'Ralph Joceline of Roxwell, yeoman, Thomas Joceline of Barham, co. Suffolk, yeoman, and Rebecca his wife', and Symon; Barham baptised 'Mary daughter of Thomas Josline and Rebecca his wife' 16 Mar. 1633/4, and the Increase in 1635 carried Thomas 43, Rebecca his wife 43 and Mary 1. The 1626 will also leaves £5 to 'my son Thomas'. This corrects the earlier note that no record names the emigrant as Ralph's son: the 1613 deed names a son Thomas paired with Rebecca, and the Thomas-and-Rebecca pair can be followed to Barham and the ship. Still Probable, graded on his link to Thomas Joslin: the deed was read only as quoted in a newsletter review, and the identification spans Roxwell, Barham and Hingham. ¶2a

Mother of his children: Ralph married (1) at Roxwell 21 May 1583 Mary Bright and (2) Dorothy, buried at Roxwell 16 Oct. 1634. French (NEHGR 71:250) could not say which wife bore which child. Zubrinsky (2026) makes Mary the probable mother of all eleven children, born at about two-year intervals 1584-1604, and has her living after 1603. Against that, the 1646 Chancery answer of Simon Josselyn (born about 1594) says he and his brother Ralph (bapt. 1590) 'paid their mother Dorothy £20 a year' (NEHGR 71:231). That may mean a stepmother, but it means Mary Bright as Thomas's mother is only Speculative (see her page). No burial of Mary appears in the Roxwell Josselyn extracts for 1558-1649. ¶3

Parents entered: John Josselyn of Fyfield, Chignal Smealy and Roxwell (bur. Roxwell 18 Feb. 1578/9) and Alice, widow Nevell, married at Fyfield 15 Jan. 1544/5. Ralph is called brother of Thomas Josselyn of Rochford in Thomas's will of 5 Mar. 1604/5 ('my brother Rafe Joslin's children of Roxwell'), in the PCC sentence of 17 June 1607 (Ralph and Simon 'brothers of the testator') and in the 1646 Chancery bill ('Ralph Joslyn... brother of Thomas Joslyn'). The 1580 inquisition after John's death finds 'Thomas Josselyn is his son and next heir' (all NEHGR 71). Graded on those parents' own records. ¶4

Siblings that tie him to John's family: his brother John Joslynne of Mountnessing (will 1602) names 'my brother Rafe Joslynne of Roxwell', 'my brother Thomas Josslynn of Rochford' and 'my brother Simon', and another brother, John of Rivenhall (will 1596/7), says that 'Jhon Josheline, my father' left him £100 out of Bollinghatch, the farm Ralph later held. Graded on John Josselyn (see there). Also, the Dorothy Josselin buried at Roxwell 17 Jan. 1606/7 was his daughter (1646 Chancery bill: Joseph's legacy increased 'by the death of his sister Dorothy'), not his second wife, who was the 'Widow Joscelyn' buried 16 Oct. 1634. ¶7

Life story

Register search (FamilySearch Essex parish-register index, signed in; images of these films are center-only, so INDEX ONLY, not read on the image): two children of 'Rafe Josselin' were baptised at GOOD EASTER, the next parish to Roxwell: Symon, 1 Apr. 1594 (index 1:1:XK8K-1Q9, FHL film 1472220, p. 21; also 1:1:XKDX-Z2K, film 857070) and Dorathie, 2 Apr. 1598 (1:1:XK86-YMJ and duplicates). Symon of 1594 fits Simon Josselyn, b. about 1594, who in his 1646 Chancery answer called Dorothy his mother; Dorathie is very probably the 'Dorothie Josselin' buried at Roxwell 17 Jan. 1605/6 (1:1:XKDC-XJJ, film 571184). So Ralph's family was at Good Easter in the mid-1590s, between the Roxwell baptisms of Ralph (8 Jul. 1590) and Josephus (16 Jul. 1600). This adds no grade change: it bears on the mother question (see Mary Bright), not on Ralph's link to Thomas. ¶5

Thomas's baptism: no baptism of a Thomas Josselyn of about 1591-92 that could be Ralph's son is indexed anywhere in Essex. The Roxwell baptisms of 1589-1594 are indexed (about 60 names, e.g. Richard Thurgood 16 Jan. 1590/1, Dorothie Bright 20 Oct. 1591, William Aylett 13 Nov. 1592) with no Josselyn; the only Essex Thomas Josselin of those years is the son of Jeffery, bapt. Good Easter 2 Jul. 1592 (several index copies; one dates it 11 Jul. 1592), who is not Ralph's son. Good Easter itself is the most likely unread place (the index there names the father, and Ralph's children of 1594 and 1598 are there). ¶6a

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Graded on his link to his son Thomas Joslin. Ralph Josselyn of Roxwell, Essex, yeoman, testator of 1626, buried at Roxwell 19 Mar. 1631/2; married (1) at Roxwell 21 May 1583 Mary Bright, (2) Dorothy, buried at Roxwell 16 Oct. 1634 (NEHGR 71:250, compiled by Elizabeth French from Roxwell registers, wills and Chancery depositions). His will of 1626 left his son Thomas only £5. The article identifies that Thomas with the emigrant of 1635 (aged 43, so born about 1592, the article's 'probably born at Roxwell late in 1591'); an 1886 conjecture to the same effect (NEHGR 40:293) was unproved, and the 1917 article gives no record that names the emigrant as Ralph's son. The article says 'lack of evidence makes it impossible to determine which wife was the mother of each child', so no mother is entered. Probable on the compiled identification only. ¶1a

Upgrade: Audit 11 (2 Nov 2026): Probable to Confirmed, graded on his link to his son Thomas Joslin (the emigrant of 1635). Naming record: the Essex Record Office calendar of D/DHt T531, feoffment to conditional uses dated 29 Sep 1613 (in the ERO online catalogue): 'Ralph Josselin senior and Simon Josselin, both of Roxwell, to John Jude of Radwinter and Ralph Josselin, jun., of Roxwell... to the use of Ralph Josselyn, sen, for life with successive remainders to Thomas Josselyn, a son of the feoffor, and Rebecca Jude for life in survivorship and their issue', with £100 payable 'at the mansion house called Bollinghatche in Roxwell'. An archive's item calendar of a single deed, which labels the parties' roles (feoffor; 'a son of the feoffor'), is accepted as a faithful index of that deed. It differs from the archivists' notes of audit 3, which add relationships taken from other sources. The relationship is confirmed independently by R. D. Joslyn's verbatim quotation of the deed ('Thomas Josselin, one of the sons of me the aforesaid Ralph Josselin the elder, and Rebecca Jude'; NEHGR 158 (2004), as quoted in the Great Migration Newsletter review), and by Ralph's 1626 will, which leaves £5 to 'my son Thomas' (French, NEHGR 71:250). Continuity from the 1613 Thomas to the emigrant runs by a pair and the family land, not by name alone (audit 8 rule 3). (a) Symon Joceline's will of 28 Sep 1649 (PCC Berkley 54; abstract with quotations, NEHGR 71:30) recites 'the deed of the division of two parts of the farm called Bouleinghatch made between Ralph Joceline of Roxwell, yeoman, Thomas Joceline of Barham, co. Suffolk, yeoman, and Rebecca his wife, and me, the said Symon'. So Thomas of Barham and his wife Rebecca shared Bollinghatch, the farm charged in the 1613 settlement on Thomas and Rebecca Jude, with Ralph's sons Ralph and Symon (both parties to the 1613 deed). (b) Barham register: 'Mary daughter of Thomas Josline and Rebecca his wife baptized 16 March [1633/4]' (printed extract, NEHGR 71:227). (c) The Increase of London, April 1635: 'Tho: Jostlin, husbandman, aged 43, Rebecca his wife, aged 43', and among his children 'Mary, aged 1' and 'Eliza, aged 6' (Hotten, printed). So three people (Thomas, his wife Rebecca, and their infant Mary) match exactly from Barham to the ship. (d) In New England, Thomas Joslin of Hingham and Lancaster, whose will of 1660 (image) names his wife Rebecca, his son Nathaniel and his daughters Rebecca, Mary and Elizabeth. Named rival: Thomas son of Jeffery Josselin, baptised at Good Easter in 1592, is the right age, but he is excluded by the land. Only a son of Ralph took a share of Bollinghatch, and the emigrant's wife Rebecca is the Rebecca of the 1613 settlement. Residual caveats, none of them a contradiction: the marriage of Thomas and Rebecca Jude has not been found (the Radwinter register before about 1638 is lost); the date of the Bollinghatch division deed is not given in the abstract (after Ralph senior's death in 1632, while Thomas was of Barham, so about 1632-35); and the will and register were read in printed abstracts, which count as faithful transcriptions here. The earlier 'Probable on the compiled identification only' and 'still Probable... the deed was read only as quoted in a newsletter review' notes are superseded: the deed's calendar has now been read, and his notes named it as the test record. Thomas's own grade (Probable, on his link to his daughter Elizabeth Joslin) is not affected. ¶8

Test record: the September 1613 trust deed (reference in NEHGR 158:320-340); reading it, with the Bollinghatch division deed, would meet the pair-bridge standard (audit 8, ruling 3). ¶2b

Test record: Good Easter register 1590-1593 on the image (FHL film 857070 / 1472220, center-only; ERO D/P register). Index-quality warning: the Roxwell index (batch V00695, film 571184) records Marie Joselin 4 Sep. 1586 and Agnes Joslin 25 Aug. 1588 as BURIALS on the exact dates French gives as their baptisms, and has Josephus as both a christening of 16 Jul. 1600 and a burial of 6 Jul. 1600, so its event types are unreliable. ¶6b
