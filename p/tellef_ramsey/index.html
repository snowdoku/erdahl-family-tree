About the family

His sister Annie, born April 1850 in Norway, is the daughter Ulvild born in Tovdal in 1850 to Torjus Tellefsen and Tøri Olsdatter, who was confirmed in Winnebago County in December 1868. The 1900 census places her, unmarried at 50, in her brother's house. ¶4

Children: The obituaries count eight children: Torjus, who died in infancy; Turi; Alvina (Thonette A.); Annie (Mrs. Ole J. Tweed); Ida (Mrs. Lars Amundson); Theodore; Oscar; and Tomine (Mrs. J. J. Rusley). In March 1933 Annie, Ida, Theodore and Oscar lived at Joice and Tomine at Lake Mills. ¶14

Life story

Baptized 31 Jul 1853, Åmli. Confirmed 23 Mar 1871 in the Winnebago Co., Iowa Norwegian church as 'Tollef', born 1853 in Omlid (Åmli). ¶1

Emigration: he is on the Tovdal out-migration list to America under the year 1862 as 'Tellef Torjusen', aged 9, with his parents Torjus Tellefsen and Thorid Olsdatter and siblings Ragnel, Anne, Ulvild, Ole and Thorid. No exact date is given. His age fits a departure after 3 Jul 1862, and the ages of others in the household fit spring 1863, which matches his 1900 census immigration year of 1863. ¶19

Residence: About five years after the marriage, so about 1880, the couple moved to the Worth County farm where they lived for the rest of their lives, apart from six years in the village of Joice (obituaries of 1933 and 1937). ¶13

Used the patronymic Torjusen in Iowa until the 1890s; 'Ramsey' comes from the family farm Øvre Ramse in Tovdal. On the 1889 Beaver Creek church building committee as 'T. T. Ramsey'. ¶2

CHURCH. His place on the 1889 Beaver Creek building committee is confirmed verbatim. The congregation was organised on 15 February 1889 at the Fjeld schoolhouse, with Rev. J. M. Dahl in the chair, and took the name "Beaver Creek Norwegian Lutheran Congregation, Worth County, Ia." The building committee was "O. T. Heimdal, T. T. Ramsey, S. A. Berge, Nels A. Fjeld, Lars O. Strommen". The site was given by Halvor Bang and his wife and the hitching lot by Ashill and Martha Billeson; the church was built in 1890. The congregation was founded because the mother church at Winnebago had passed 1,900 baptised members and resolved to build a second church for those living to the south-east. ¶7

1900 CENSUS (Fertile Twp., Worth Co., Iowa): "Tollef F Ramsay", born July 1853 in Norway, immigrated 1863, farmer, married 25 years — which dates the marriage to 1875 and confirms the date already held. His wife Ingebor is recorded as born August 1853 in Norway, immigrated 1862. Living in the household besides the children were his sister "Annie Ramsay", born April 1850 in Norway and immigrated 1863, and his mother-in-law "Gundbyr Heimdal", born June 1827 in Norway and immigrated 1862. ¶3

The year of immigration stated in 1900 is 1863 for Tellef and his sister but 1862 for his wife's family. If the 1863 figure is right for the Ramse family it narrows the emigration window, which other records put between August 1862 and 1868, to the winter or spring of 1862-63. Stated immigration years in US censuses are often approximate and should be treated as a strong hint rather than proof. ¶5

LAND. The 1913 plat of Fertile Township shows "T. T. Ramsey — 139.28" acres in Section 7, with an adjoining "Mart[h]a Ramsey — 60" acres, and "O. T. Hiendal — 79.79" acres (a Heimdal, his wife's kin) immediately north. Beaver Creek Church stands just east of his land. By 1930 the family held 189.66 acres in Section 7 and 87.28 in Section 18. No federal land patent was ever issued to a Ramsey in this township: the government land was gone by 1867, so he bought improved farmland privately. ¶6

In 1930, aged 76, he and Ingeborg were living with their youngest son Oscar, a tenant farmer in the same township. ¶8

Obituary: The Lake Mills Graphic of 8 March 1933 (p. 10, Joice column, 'Death Calls T. Ramsey At Age 80') says 'Tollef T. Ramsey' was born at 'Ramso Tordal' (Ramse in Tovdal), Norway, on 3 July 1853 to Torjus and Turi Ramsey, was baptised as an infant, and as a child came with his parents first to Dane County, Wisconsin, and then to a farm south of Lake Mills in Winnebago County, Iowa. ¶9

Birthplace: The obituary names Ramse in Tovdal, the farm the family took its name from; the birthplace held here (Haagedal, Åmli) rests on the Norwegian records of 1853 and is kept; the family moved to Øvre Ramse before emigrating, which explains the obituary's wording (inferred). ¶10

Confirmation: The 1933 obituary gives 'March 22, 1871', by Rev. T. A. Torgeson (checked in the page image); the Winnebago church-book transcription on Digitalarkivet gives 23 March 1871. The church book was written at the time and the obituary 62 years later, so 23 March 1871 is kept. ¶11

Marriage: Both his 1933 and his wife's 1937 obituaries say he married Ingbor Heimdal on 24 February 1875, by Rev. J. M. Dahl (printed 'L M. Dahl' in 1933). Dahl was pastor of the Winnebago congregation from 1873 (Norlie), so the marriage was probably in that parish (inferred). The 1900 census ('married 25 years') agrees. ¶12

Church: He was a member of Beaver Creek Lutheran church and a regular attendant at its services and at the Lord's Supper (1933 obituary). ¶16

Death: He fell ill about four weeks before his death and had an operation at Mercy Hospital, Mason City. At his own wish he was brought home on Thursday 2 March 1933 and died there, near Joice, the next day, Friday 3 March. The headline gives his age as 80; from his birth date he was 79 (1933 obituary). ¶17

Funeral and burial: The funeral was on Tuesday 7 March 1933 at Beaver Creek church, Rev. L. E. Kleppe officiating, with burial in Beaver Creek cemetery. He left his wife, three daughters, two sons, 25 grandchildren and 13 great-grandchildren (1933 obituary). The WPA's 1939 survey of Worth County stones records 'Ramsey,... Tellef T. 1853 to 3-3-1933; Beaver Ck. cem., Fertile twp.' on the same stone list as his wife. ¶18

Records that disagree 1

Conflict over Turi's age: The 1933 obituary says Turi 'died at the age of 27 years'; her mother's 1937 obituary says 37, and the WPA transcription of her stone reads 'Turi Olive 1877 to 8-22-1914' (Beaver Creek cemetery), which makes her 37. The '27' is a misprint. The same WPA page gives 'Tonette A. 3-5-1884 to 3-21-1904', agreeing with Alvina's death 'at the age of 20'. ¶15
