Life story

Marriage: on 17 March 1924 at St Saviour's Church, Nelson, BC, she married William Arthur Andrews, a CPR telegraph operator. She was a waitress, a spinster aged 31, Roman Catholic, living at Sirdar, BC, born at Standish, Lancashire; her parents were John Henry Chadwick and Elizabeth Hayes, both born in England. She was eight years older than her husband. ¶2

Death: she died on 19 April 1974 at the South Okanagan General Hospital, Oliver, aged 81, born 18 February 1893 in England, a housewife, wife of William Arthur Andrews, of #2 A Road East, one mile south of Oliver. Her parents are named as John Chadwick and Elizabeth Hayes. Her son 'W A Andrews Jr.' of P.O. Box 308, Oliver was the informant. She was buried on 22 April 1974 in Oliver Public Cemetery. ¶3
