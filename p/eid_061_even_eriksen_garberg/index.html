About the family

Father of Per (Peder) Evensen (see his page), rated Probable. For: Per Evensen of Garberg was born in 1726 by the bygdebok and married in 1758 as 'Peder Evens.'; this Peder is the only child of an Even at Garberg in the complete read of the register from October 1724 to November 1733, and the only Peder son of an Even seen there; the patronymic Evensen and the farm agree. ¶2a

Wife, a lead only: on the 24th Sunday after Trinity, 7 November 1728, the register buried 'Even Garbergs qvinde Giertrud æt: 53' - Even Garberg's wife Giertrud, aged 53 (canvas 114 left page, crop). Correction of date: card gives 31 October 1728, which is the 23rd Sunday after Trinity (Easter 28 March 1728); the burial stands under 'Dom 24 p. T.', which is 7 November. Giertrud, born about 1675, would have been about 51 at Per's birth in March 1726, which is very unlikely, so she is probably not Per's mother: either the 1728 age is overstated, or Even had an earlier or later wife. She is not linked. To settle: Even's marriage(s) in the Selbu ministerialbok 1699-1737 and any remarriage after November 1728. ¶4

Life story

Baptism of his son: on 13 March 1726, a Lenten Wednesday sermon day ('passions Præd.'), 'døbt Even Garbergs Barn Nom: Peder' - baptised Even Garberg's child, named Peder (Selbu ministerialbok 1699-1737, canvas 103 right page, crop). His wife was churched with the wives of Bjørn Garberg, Ole Fossum and Ole Fuglum on Jubilate Sunday, 12 May 1726 ('introd: Even Garbergs, Biørn Garbergs, Ole Fossums, Ole Fuglums qvinder', canvas 104 left page), two months after the baptism. ¶1

Patronymic: the register writes only 'Even Garberg'. The patronymic Eriksen comes from the bygdebok alone: Selbu i fortid og naatid vol. 3, Garberg chapter p. 649, has '1721-1751 Even Eriksen,... 1751' as a holder on the same page as '1752-1768 Per Evensen... 1726' (nb.no word boxes). The farm of that holder block is not in the boxes, and the end year 1751 may be his death or the end of his tenure. Reading the printed p. 649 would settle both. ¶3

Second marriage: on Visitatio Mariae, 2 Jul 1730, the Selbu register (16310 c121 L) records 'Copulerede... Even Erichs: [og] Anne Mattis[?] Datter' (surname of the bride uncertain). So Giertrud Iversdatter was dead before July 1730, and the patronymic Eriksen is now in the parish register as well as the 1731 skifte. A read of every entry Jul 1729 - Oct 1730 (c118-121) found no other burial of a Garberg wife, so the 7 Nov 1728 burial of 'Even Garbergs qvinde Giertrud' (c114 L) is hers and the skifte (concluded Feb 1731) was held late. Grade unchanged (Probable on his link to Per Evensen): the skifte lists the sons 'Peer, Erich 11, Anders 1', and a list in age order would make Peer the eldest son (13, b. about 1718), which does not fit the 1726 baptism; the order argument and the uncertain digit leave the identification Probable. ¶8

Patronymic and wife from a primary record: the skifte concluded 15 Feb 1731 after his wife Giertrud Iversdatter (24750 fol. 366-366b) calls him 'Evind Erichsen Avildgaard boende paa huusmands pladzen under Garberg' - Even Eriksen, a cottar under Garberg, with the byname Avildgaard (perhaps from an Avelsgård farm). Children: Peer Evindsen ([5?] or 13), Erich 11, Anders 1, Guru 19. The patronymic Eriksen no longer rests on the bygdebok alone. Still Probable on his link to Per Evensen (see his page): the skifte names a son Peer, and the 1726 baptism 'Even Garbergs Barn Peder' fits an age of 5, but the age digit is uncertain and the next record of Per is his marriage in 1758, 27 years later. ¶7

Widower 'Even Eriksen Hvildsgaard[?]' (the byname is unclear on the card) at the probate after Giertrud Iversdtr., Garberg plass, 15 Feb 1731 (card 77804 c535; prot. 2 fol. 366), with children Iver 13, Erik 11, Peder 4 and Guru 19. So he was alive in Feb 1731. The card gives no parent of his. ¶13

Even Eriksen was probably dead by 1762 (his widow lived with Peder). His probate is not in the typed registers of 24753 (1742-60) or 24755 (1760-66) under Even, Evind or Garberg (grep); the bygdebok's '1721-1751 Even Eriksen' may mark his death about 1751. ¶12

Possible later mention, an inference only: an 'Even Ugl.' stood godfather to Per Evensen's son Jon at Uglem in 1769 (16311 canvas 31 right). If the bygdebok's 1751 is only the end of Even Eriksen's tenure, he could be this man; he would then have been very old. Nothing ties them. ¶5

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed, graded on his link to his son Per (Peder) Evensen (see his page). The 1762 ekstraskatt for Selbu (Digitalarkivet 49639, canvas 132, matr. 735 Garberg) lists among the cottars under Peder Biørnsen's Garberg 'Peder Evensen med Qvinde Brynild Erichsdatter samt hans Moder Anne Madsdatter' - Peder Evensen with his wife Brynild Eriksdatter (the wife of the tree's Per, married 1758 and named at the baptisms of 1759-64 as 'Per Evens. Garberg og Brynill Erichsd.') and HIS mother Anne Madsdatter. Anne Madsdatter is Even Eriksen's second wife, married 2 Jul 1730 as 'Even Erichs: [og] Anne Mattis Datter' (16310 c121 L; the 1762 list settles the doubtful reading 'Mattis' as Mads/Mathis). Per was baptised 13 Mar 1726 as 'Even Garbergs Barn Peder' while Even was married to Giertrud Iversdatter, so Anne is his stepmother, written 'Moder' as usual; the relationship word ties the Per of 1758-1812 to Even Eriksen's household. Continuity: the 1726 baptism names Even as Peder's father; the skifte of 15 Feb 1731 (24750 fol. 366b) names 'Pedr Evindsen 4 aar' as a child of Even Erichsen, cottar under Garberg; the 1762 list puts Peder Evensen still a cottar at Garberg with Even's widow. The earlier blocking contradiction (Peer '5 or 13') was removed re-read (Peder 4 years 4 months, matching the 1726 baptism). ¶11a

Records that disagree 1

Correction and resolved contradiction: the 1731 skifte after Giertrud Iversdatter (24750 fol. 366b, view 377 L) was re-read at full resolution. The children are '1 Jvor Evindsøn 13 aar, 2 Erich Evindsen 11 aar, 3 Pedr Evindsen 4 aar og 4 [months?], Guru Evindsdatter 19 aar'. That is Iver 13, Erik 11, Peder 4 and Guru 19, not 'Peer [5?] or 13... Anders 1' as. The Stjør- og Verdal card index (77804 c535) independently reads Iver 13, Erik 11, Peder 4, Guri 19. Peder aged 4 years 4 months fits the baptism of 'Even Garbergs Barn Peder' on 13 Mar 1726 if the ages were taken at the registration about July 1730, just before Even remarried on 2 Jul 1730. So the age contradiction is resolved, and there was no son Anders. Further continuity: Iver Evensen of Børset, d. 1753, named a daughter Giertrud, and his sons' guardian was 'deres Farbroder Erich Evensen' (24753 fol. 514b). At Erich Evensen's skifte on 3 Feb 1764 (24755 fol. 378b) his daughter Giertrue was put under the guardianship of 'Peder Evensen Garberg' (no relationship word). So the 1731 brothers Iver and Erik are followed to 1753 and 1764, and a Peder Evensen of Garberg acted for Erik's children. Grade kept Probable on his link to Per Evensen (see his page), for the reasons given on his wife Gjertrud (Giertrud) Iversdatter (gap of more than 20 years between the 1731 skifte and the next record of Per, and no relationship word in 1764). ¶10

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

No rival: Even had no other son Peder, and no other Peder Evensen appears at Garberg 1726-1764. The 1764 skifte after Per's brother Erich Evensen (24755 fol. 378b) has 'Peder Evensen Garberg' as guardian, and the 1753 skifte after Iver Evensen (24753 fol. 514b) names 'deres Farbroder Erich Evensen', consistent with the 1731 sibling set. ¶11b

Searches that found nothing 1

Checked against the logs of 16310 c110-114 (Oct 1727 - Feb 1729): they list no baptism of an Anders, son of Even Garberg. So the 'Anders 1' of the skifte after Giertrud Iversdatter (24750 fol. 366) cannot be dated from the register, and Peer's age in that skifte (5 or 13) is still not settled. The 1726 'Even Garbergs barn Peder' (c103 R) and the churching of 'Even Garbergs qvinde' on 12 May 1726 (c104 L) fall within his marriage to Giertrud (buried 7 Nov 1728). Even remarried Anne in Jul 1730. ¶9

Open questions and the next record to read 2

What would confirm it: Per's confirmation (about 1741-43) or burial with an age, or a probate (skifte) after Even naming his son Per. ¶2b

Biography gaps: his birth date, birth place, baptism and parents are not found; the patronymic Eriksen and the father Erik rest only on the bygdebok (vol. 3 p. 649, word boxes, Norway-restricted). His marriage to Giertrud is not dated, and his death and burial are not found after the complete read of October 1724 to November 1733. The Stjor- og Verdal probate for Even and Giertrud (skifte 2a, source 24750, 1727-31, unindexed, not read) would name the heirs and may fix his death; his confirmation is not recorded. ¶6
