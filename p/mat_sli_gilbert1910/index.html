About the family

Eldest child of Ole Gundersen Slind and Ingeborg Olsdatter, and so Oline Slind's nephew. Born 1 August 1910 in Selbu; in the 1920 census of his father's farm at Kjosnes sondre; emigrated with the whole family on the Stavangerfjord on 7 June 1922, aged eleven. ¶7

Eldest son of Ole G. Slind and Ingeborg Klegseth; came to America with the whole household on the Stavangerfjord, 7 June 1922, aged eleven. ¶9

Children identified: ORIS JEROME, b. 8 Apr 1935, d. 23 May 1953 aged eighteen at Mount Vernon, Skagit County (his death certificate names "Father: Gilbert Slind, Mother: Magna Walli"), buried at Selbu Cemetery; IRENE ANN, b. 18 Oct 1942, d. 14 Feb 1943 at Colfax (same two parents named on her certificate), buried at Selbu Cemetery; GERALD WALLI, b. 1 Nov 1936, d. 12 Apr 2021, A1C US Air Force, Korea, buried "Selby Lutheran, LaCrosse, WA" per the VA Nationwide Gravesite Locator - which is this same Selbu Lutheran churchyard, and whose middle name Walli is his mother's; MARVIN GILBERT, of Whitman County, who married three times (Carol Jean Bichon 1970, Kathleen Clara Lee 1982, Anne McGarry Hanzel 1993); ALAN W., who married Helen Marie Hibler in King County in 1966; and ARLEN. ¶13

Life story

A MAGNA G. SLIND, b. 21 Jul 1909, filed a life claim at the SAME Bellingham field office in 1973 and died 1 May 2005 at ZIP [withheld] (Port Angeles, Clallam County, Washington). Same surname, same field office, a year apart, right ages — she is very probably his wife, but no record read states it, so she is recorded in the findings file and not given a person here. ¶6

Social Security, retirement claim: GILBERT SLIND, born 1 Aug 1910, filed a life claim at the BELLINGHAM, WASHINGTON Social Security field office in June 1972 — the year he turned 62. NARA AAD, NUMIDENT Claim Files 1936-2007 (Last Names R-T), rid 2602702. ¶2

Social Security death record: GILBERT SLIND, b. 1 Aug 1910, d. 5 Apr 1993, residence ZIP [withheld], which is BOW, Skagit County, Washington. Posted 14 Apr 1993. NARA AAD, NUMIDENT Death Files 1936-2007 (Last Names S-T), rid 2473014. ¶3

That he retired at Bellingham and died at Bow, Skagit County, is worth flagging on its own account: that is WHATCOM and SKAGIT COUNTY, WASHINGTON — the same corner of the Pacific Northwest the Erdahl side of this family migrated to. A Slind first cousin of Palmer Eidem's generation living there is the first documented Eidem/Slind presence in that county. ¶5

Context worth noting: his name Gilbert is an Americanism, given in Selbu in 1910 by a father who had already spent two years in Minnesota. It is the same name as Palmer GILBERT Eidem, Oline Slind's son, born in Minnesota in 1904. ¶8

Emigrated with his parents and five siblings, Trondheim 7 June 1922 on the Stavangerfjord (Norwegian America Line): emigrant protocol no. 15 (1916-1926), page 140, line 28, group 00883, household position 'barn' (child), residence Selbu, destination 'U.S. Amerika', ticket Cash, minister's certificate. ¶1

Marriage: MAGNA WALLI, 25 JANUARY 1934, in Whitman County, Washington - the date is cut on both their stones at Selbu Cemetery ("s/w & h/o Magna Walli m. Jan 25 1934"). He was twenty-three and had been in America twelve years. Magna, b. 21 July 1909, d. 1 May 2005. ¶10

1950 census, Whitman County, Washington, ED 38-25 (NARA schedule 6309513): the household is "Slind Gilbert, Magna L, Oris J, Gerald, Alan, Marvin, Arlen" - five sons living at home. The transcription is NARA's volunteer OCR and the forenames are approximate. ¶12

The family moved from Whitman County to Skagit County in north-west Washington at some point after 1950: his son Gerald W. married in Skagit County in 1962, and Gilbert himself died there in 1993. He was nevertheless brought back to Selbu Cemetery for burial, as his son Oris had been in 1953. ¶14

Death certificate: Washington DOH, DE_1993011918, Skagit County, 5 April 1993, aged 82, county file no. 253. FATHERLASTNAME: SLIND. MOTHERMAIDENNAME: KLEGSETH. This is the record that independently confirms his mother's maiden name sixty-three years after her death. Buried at Selbu Cemetery, Whitman County (hrp-cem-whi-selbu_0119), b. Aug 01 1910 d. Apr 05 1993 - the birth date matching the 1920 Selbu census to the day. ¶11

The case for this identification 1

The identification. The 1920 census of Selbu, district 018 Slind, residence Kjøsnes søndre, lists GILBERT OLSEN SLIND, b. 1 AUGUST 1910, eldest son of Ole Gundersen Slind and Ingeborg (transcribed from Digitalarkivet by the sister agent selbu-slind), and the whole household emigrated on 7 June 1922. The birth date matches the Social Security record TO THE DAY, and Gilbert is a rare enough given name among Slinds that there is exactly one in the entire federal file. Rated Probable rather than Confirmed only because no single document carries both the Norwegian birth and the Washington death. ¶4
