About the family

Identified as the father of Guri Ingebrigtsdatter. Guri Ingebrichtsdatter lived at Bøe (Grytten, Vold parish) when she stood godmother on 26 Jan 1726 and when she married Mads of Moen on 18 Oct 1730. The only Ingebricht at Bøe in the Grytten index of those years is 'Ingebricht, Bøe', godfather at Grytten on 28 Dec 1725 (to Anna d. of Ole, Moen, alongside Mads of Moen; pd00000042616220) and on 1 Apr 1729 (pd00000042617325). Hans Ingebrichtsen of Bøe, who married Anna Olsdatter on 20 Nov 1735 (pv00000010058701), named his first son Ingebricht (bapt. 6 May 1736) and chose 'Guri Ingebrichtsd.' of Moen as godmother (pd00000042619670); a 'Hans Bøe' was guardian for Guri's daughter Anna in both the 1749 and the 1758 probates. Hans was evidently Guri's brother and the two were children of Ingebricht of Bøe. His patronymic, age and death are not found: no Ingebrigt at Bøe in the 1701 manntall (only Stener Andersen and Anders Olsen are listed as tenants there), no Ingebrigt probate at Bøe 1700-75 in the Romsdal probate index, and no indexed burial. Probable (farm, godparents and naming pattern, no record naming the relationship). ¶1

Life story

Patronymic, age and death found. 1701 manntall, Romsdal fogderi (index): 'Ingebrigt Hans.', aged 40 (b. about 1661), tenant ('Opsidder eller Leylending') and soldier, on Nerebøe (Nedre Bøe), listed in the same household block as the tenant Tosten (b. 1663) and his sons Bendix and Erik (pc00000005080454). The same list has 'Madz Johansen' as servant on Moen eleven entries further on (pc00000005080465), so this is the Grytten Bøe beside Moen, not a namesake farm. The earlier note that no Ingebrigt was at Bøe in 1701 was wrong: the farm is indexed as 'Nerebøe'. He had no sons at home in 1701; his son Hans Ingebrichtsen (married 1735) was evidently born after the census. ¶2

Burial (index): 'Ingebricht Hansen' of Bøe, buried 17 Dec 1741 at Vold church (Wolds kirke) with a funeral sermon, birth year calculated 1654 (aged about 87). Grytten ministerialbok 1725-1763 (1539P), p. 96 (pg00000007161705). He is the only Ingebricht buried from Bøe in the indexed Grytten burials 1725-1763. The ages disagree (40 in 1701 gives b. about 1661; 87 at burial gives b. about 1654); old men's burial ages are commonly overstated, and a man of about 80 is still a plausible father for Guri (b. about 1699) and Hans (b. after 1701). ¶3

Patronymic as a guide (inference): his son Hans Ingebrichtsen of Bøe was named Hans, the name of Ingebricht's own father by this patronymic, which fits the usual naming pattern and supports the identification of 'Ingebricht, Bøe' of the 1725-29 baptisms with Ingebrigt Hansen of Nedre Bøe. His wife is not named in any record found. Women buried from Bøe who were of an age to be Guri's mother include Guri Knudsdatter (bur. 5 Feb 1741 at Vold, aged 82, pg00000007161677) and Ingebor Aslagsdatter (bur. 14 Jan 1748 at Vold, aged 85, pg00000007162120); neither entry names a husband, so neither is entered. No Romsdal probate after Ingebrigt Hansen (searched Ingebr* 1741-44, all 9 pages) or after a Bøe resident 1725-65 except Erik Mortensen (1741). ¶4

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
