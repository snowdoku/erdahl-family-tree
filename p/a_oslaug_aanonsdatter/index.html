About the family

NEW ANCESTOR. Wife of Terje Tjøstelsen of Rostveit in Åmli and mother of Tjøstel Tergiesen of Nedre Eppeland. ¶1

Her husband's estate (Østre Råbyggelag Skifteprotokoll nr. 8 fol. 26, 15 Oct 1728, card 11395) names her as the widow, 'Oslog Aanonsdtr.', and lists their sons Tiøstel Tergiesøn, 25, and Hølgie Tergiesøn, 21. This is the primary-record basis (via a faithful card index) for her marriage and motherhood. Previously she rested only on cross-reference cards. ¶3

Life story

Named on the archivist's cross-reference card for the 1765 Eppeland probate: "mor: Oslaug Aanonsdtr." Her father was an Aanon (Ånund). Her name recurs in the family as Oslou/Aaslöf Tjøstelsdatter, a granddaughter alive in 1768. ¶2
