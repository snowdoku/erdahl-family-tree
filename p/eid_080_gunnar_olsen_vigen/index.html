Life story

1666 vicar's manntall (35528 v20, Over-Vigen no. 11): 'Gunder Olsen 6 aar', son of Olluf Ingebrichtsen (50); brothers Bersvend Olsen 9 and Ingebrigt Olsen 6 months (page checked by the orchestrator). The census age points to birth about 1660; the 1701 manntall (46) and his burial (75 in 1731) to about 1655-56. His birth stays about 1655. ¶10

Churching: on the 26th Sunday after Trinity, 20 November 1701, 'Introd. Gönner Wigens q. Ulü[?] efter det 1ste barn Olof' - Gunner Vigen's wife Ulü[?] was churched (introduced back into church after childbirth) after her first child Olof (same register, canvas 11 left page). The marriage was therefore about 1700; the marriage entry itself has not been found in the 1699-1700 pages read so far. ¶2

Bygdebok: the Overvik chapter lists Gunnar Olsen as holder of the farm 1701-1728, died 1731, with a child list that opens with Ola born 1701; his son Ola Gunnarsen took over the holding in 1729 (Selbu i fortid og naatid vol. 6, pp. 325-326, read from full-text word boxes, not from the page). The patronymic Olsen comes from the bygdebok only; the church book calls him just Gunner (Gönner) Wigen. ¶3

Vigen and Overvik: the clerks of 1701-1759 write this household as 'Vigen' (Ola's burial in 1759 is 'Ole Gunars. Vigen'). 'Vigen' (the bay) is the clerk's collective name for the farms at Vika in Selbu, and several unrelated households were living under it at the same time (Peder Andersen, Niels, Jon, Arent, Ole Nielsen and Peder Nielsen Vigen all appear in 1729-1736). Overvik is one holding within it. A plain 'Gunder Vigen' entry is therefore not automatically this family. ¶4

1701 manntall, Selbu, farm Over-Vigen (Overvik) (canvas 115): farmer 'Gunder Olsen', aged 46 (so born about 1655; about 76 at his burial in 1731, which agrees with the 'aged 75' of that burial); no sons listed (his son Ola, baptised Oct 1701, was not yet born or not counted); servants Bersvend Olsen 49 and Peder Olsen, reserve soldier, 22 - possibly brothers. Confirms his patronymic Olsen. ¶8a

Alive in 1713-14: 'Gunner Vigen' godfather 13 p.Trin 1713 (16310 c51), and 'Gunner Vigens datter aetat 2 aar, Marit' buried 5 p.Trin 1714 (c54) -. A 'Birthe Olsd. Vigen' died 13 Sep 1774 aged 74 (b. c.1700; 16311 c64) - too young to be his sister unless the age is wrong; possibly a niece. ¶9

Burial (probable): on the 7th Sunday after Trinity, 8 July 1731, 'Gjfæstede Gunder Vigen æt: 75' - buried Gunder Vigen, aged 75 (Selbu ministerialbok 1699-1737, canvas 124 right page). This is probably Gunnar Olsen, because the bygdebok gives his death as 1731 and his holding ended in 1728, a natural retirement for an old man. ¶5a

The card names no widow, so his wife Ulli Nielsdatter (see her page) had probably died before Feb 1732. Her grade is unchanged. Test record for her: the protocol entry itself, 24751 fol. 403 (about view 36-37; view 34 is fol. 400b-401a), which may say whose children they were ('med hans afg. Hustru...'). ¶13

His probate protocol entry was read (24751 view 30, fol. 403a, 12 Feb 1732, 'efter afgangen Gunder Olsen Wigen' at Øvervigen): heirs his three children Ola Gundersen 30, Nils Gundersen 28 and Giertrud Gundersdatter 25 (the card 77804 c2323 gives Giertrud 23). No widow is mentioned. This is the protocol text behind the upgrade; his grade (Confirmed) stands. ¶14

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Rated Probable: the baptism and churching are primary records read on the page, and they tie Olof to Gunner Vigen explicitly. The only step that is inferred is that this Olof is the adult Ola Gunnarsen, which rests on the name, the patronymic, the farm and the exact age at his 1759 burial; found no other Ole son of a Gunder at Vigen in 1699-1702. The 1701 Selbu manntall (male census) would confirm it by listing Gunnar with his son Ola, aged under one year. ¶7

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed, graded on his link to his son Ola (Ole) Gunnarsen Overvik (see his page). Record: the Stjør- og Verdal probate card index 77804, canvas 2323, filed under the spelling 'Øvervik, Selbu' at the end of the index (Ø section), which is why earlier searches under Vigen, Overvik and the O-run missed it. It abstracts the skifte of 12/2-1732 (Skifteprotokoll 2, fol. 403) after 'Gunder Olsen Wigen': 'Barn: Ole Gundersen, 30 år gl., Niels Gundersen, 28 år, Giertrud Gundersdtr. 23 år, ugift'; estate 19-2-11, each son 6-1-14 2/5, the daughter 3-0-19 1/5; 'Formynder for Giertrud: Broren, Ole Gundersen'. The card's fields label the deceased and his children, so it is a faithful index of the relationship. ¶11

The case for this identification 1

Continuity for the upgrade: Ole Gundersen, 30 in Feb 1732, was born about 1701-02, which matches the baptism of 'Gönner Wigens barn, Oluff' on 16 Oct 1701 (16310 c11) and the burial of 'Ole Gunars. Vigen' in 1759 aged 58. Ola Gunnarsen took over the Overvik holding in 1729 (bygdebok), so father and son are on the same holding at both ends, with three years between. No other Ole son of a Gunder at Vigen is named. The probate also fixes Gunnar's death before 12 Feb 1732, which supports the burial of 'Gunder Vigen æt: 75' on 8 Jul 1731 (16310 c124). This supersedes the 'Rated Probable' note above. ¶12

Records that disagree 1

Against: the stated age implies birth about 1656, which makes him about 45 at his first child in 1701. That is possible for a late first marriage, and ages at burial in this register are often rounded up. The record that would settle it is the probate (skifte) after Gunnar Olsen Overvik, Strinda og Selbu sorenskriveri, about 1731-1732, or the 1701 male census (manntall) for Selbu, which gives ages of men and sons. ¶5b

Corrections 1

Correction: birth refined from 'before 1681' to about 1655. ¶8b

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Not the father of the 1729-1736 children: a Gunder Vigen had children baptised in 1729, 1730, 1732, 1735 and 1736. The Joen of 1732, Anne of 1735 and Ingebor of 1736 were born after this Gunnar's death in 1731, and his wife was churched after her first child in 1701, so she would have been nearly sixty by 1736. That younger man is recorded separately as Gunder Vigen. ¶6
