Life story

First child of Lasse Lassesen 'Lewis' Erdahl and Beret Martha Thomasdatter Beitland, born eleven months after their marriage. He lived three and a half months and died on 11 September 1877 in Rome Township, Faribault County. ¶1

Baptism and father: the Blue Earth church book records the baptism in 1877 of 'Laurits', son of Lasse L. Erdahl and Berthe T. Erdahl (Beret Thomasdatter). This names his father, so the missing link to lasse is added. The index has no day and no godparents. ¶3
