About the family

NEW ANCESTOR. Father of Ole Evindsen Hillestad. Graded on his link to Ole Evindsen Hillestad: Probable. The link rests on Ole's patronymic (Ole Evindsøn Hillestad, card 4993) and the farm, with the Tovdal bygdebok (Hillestad chapter, canvases 205-207, word positions only), which names Eivind as the holder of Hillestad from about the 1630s: in the 1645 tax list 'Effuind' and his wife lived on the farm, in 1647 'Effuind' is listed there with an 'Olluff', he was born about 1600, alive in 1661 and dead before 1664, and his children were Olav, Kjetil, Sigrid and Guro among others. No record read here names him as Ole's father. The bygdebok says he was probably married to a Signe, probably from Tveit; the 1712 skifte (card 4995) names Ole Evindsen's mother as Wilborrig Olufsdatter, so either the bygdebok's Signe is a misreading by me of the word positions, or Evind married twice. ¶1

Life story

Bygdebok (Åmli ætt og heim I, Hillestad gard nr. 9, canvas 207): Eivind [patronymic a gap] came to Hillestad from Austenå a few years after 1624 ('der hadde [det] ikkje vore skifte på Austenå etter faren'); 1644-46 he owned 14 [?] in Hillestad, 1 hud in [?] Gjerstad and 1 hud in Tveit, Åmli; 'Han var fødd [kring] år 1600. Han var truleg gift med den Signe Kjetilsdtr. som i 1666 [åtte ?] hud i Hillestad. Signe var truleg frå Tveit i Åmli. Eivind levde i 1661, men er borte før 1664.' Children known: Olav, Kjetil, Jakob, Sigrid, Guro. Contradiction noted (no change): the book (canvas 209) says Olav Eivindson Hillestad was first married to 'Vilborg Olsdtr. frå [?] Valle, enke etter Olav [?]', whereas the 1681 deed cited in the 1712 skifte (card 4995) calls 'Wilborrig Olufsdaatter' Ole Evindsen's mother (Vilborg (Wilborrig) Olufsdatter). The probate card wording should prevail, but card 4995 / Skp. 3 fol. 269b should be re-read for 'mor' versus 'hustru'. Father: only 'the man at Austenå' (not named in the rebuilt text); test record: the 1624 skattemanntall for Austenå (Lille Topdal) and the vol. I Austenå chapter (canvases 137-183). ¶4

Primary records for him. 1645 poll tax (koppskatt olsok 1645, Råbyggelag, p.14 'Lille Thopdal', DA 76706 view 122): under Hillestad (a half farm) 'Effuind och hans quinde och en... dreng', then Knud and his wife and another man and his wife. Wives are not named in this list. 1661 land register (DA 38714 view 63, fol. 58b, read on the scan): Hillestad 4 huder: Åmli prestebol owns 1 hud 3 k.s., 'Effuin eyer j hud 2 k.s.' and 'raader bøxell for gaarden', 'Inger Hyllestad eyer med sinne børn 5½ k.s.', Michell owns 3 k.s.; users: Effuin 2 huder 3½ k.s., Lauritz 5½ k.s., Michell 3 k.s. So Evind was alive, owner and user at Hillestad in 1661, which supports the bygdebok's 'alive in 1661, dead before 1664'. The 1610 tax list (DA 100550) has only 'Annond' at Hyllestad. None of these records names his children. ¶3

Context: 1664 vicar's census, Hillestad (DA 35510 view 17, p.13): opsiddere Lauritz 37, Michell 24, the women Signi Kiedilsdatter and Sigri Torgeirsdatter; in the sons column 'Jacob Effuindsøn 20' (bailiff's census 1664, DA 35567 view 28: 'Jacob Effuindsøn 16' at Hillestad), possibly a younger son; servant Jon Tollefsøn 24; husmann Jørgen 50. No Evind and no Ole. The 1661 land register's 'Inger Hyllestad med sinne børn' (DA 38714 view 63) is probably the widow of his son Kjetil. ¶2

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
