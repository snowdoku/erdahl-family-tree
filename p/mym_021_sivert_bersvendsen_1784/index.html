Life story

Baptism: Selbu, Easter Monday, 12 April 1784, 'Sivert', parents 'Bersv. Siverts. Draxen' and 'Lesbeth Torchilsd.'; godparents Michel, Iver, Jon, Karen and Brynild, all of Draxen. Read from the scan (source 16311, canvas 113). The birth date is not given. ¶1

1801 census: 'Sivert', aged 17, son in his father Bersvend Sivertsen's household 04 at Draxen. ¶2

Godfather, as 'Sivert Bersvensen Draxen', at the baptism on 25 Oct 1812 of Helge, the first child of his sister Siri and Jon Sivertsen of Eidem. ¶3

1825 census: in his father's household at Draxen og Tangvold, married to Beret Olsdatter (b. about 1790), with children Ole (1810), Marit (1815), Bersvend (1817), Siri (1820) and Beret (1823). The baptism index names him as father with Beret Olsdatter in 1818, 1820 and 1830. His own marriage (before 1816) and his death have not been searched. ¶4
