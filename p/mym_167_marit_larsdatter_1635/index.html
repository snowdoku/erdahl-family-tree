About the family

Wife of Håkon Pedersen of Holan, Skatval, per the bygdebok (Skatval vol. 1, canvas 132 left): 'Marit Larsdtr., [f.] 1635?, [d.] 25.12.1731'. Speculative as mother of Lars Håkonsen (b. 1683): the book itself queries her birth year, and a birth in 1635 would make her 48 at Lars's birth, so she may be a second wife or the year may be wrong. ¶1
