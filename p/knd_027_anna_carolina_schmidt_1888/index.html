Life story

Marriage: on 13 May 1909 at Heimbrunn (Standesamt Lissewo, Kreis Culm) the farmer's daughter (Landwirtstochter) Anna Carolina Schmidt ('Anna' written above the line), Evangelical, born 25 Mar 1888 at 'Cogeali' (Cogealia) in Romania, living at Bilau, Kreis Culm, married Friedrich Schmidt of Bilau. She was the daughter of the farmer (Landwirt) Karl Schmidt and his wife Julianna née Bandau (maiden name as written; see below), living at Cogealia in Romania (Lissewo marriages 1909 no. 6; image read). ¶1

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Parents, not linked: her father Karl Schmidt of Cogealia may be the Karl Schmidt born 23 Sep 1853 at Atmagea (died 1940 at Cogealia), the probable brother of her father-in-law Wilhelm Schmidt; that would make her husband her first cousin. That Karl's wife was Juliana, born 2 May 1859; the 1940 resettlement (EWZ) index spells her maiden name 'Bondo' in three entries (EWZ51-B049 frame 0408, Karl's own entry; EWZ58-D020 frames 2300 and 2370) and 'Ponto' in two (EWZ58-D020 2278; EWZ51-A084 2436), and the compiled Ciucurova family book has 'Ponto'. This act's 'Bandau' sounds like 'Bondo', so the maiden name is probably one surname in variant spellings, and the identification of her parents with Karl Schmidt (b. 1853 Atmagea) and Juliana Bondo/Ponto is probable. It is not proved: the EWZ index lists Karl's children who were still in Romania in 1940 (Maria 1882, Rosina 1894, Sophia 1900), not her, and no record read names her together with them. Karl is not in the tree, so no parents are entered for her; Karl's EWZ file or the Cogealia Heimatortskartei card would settle it. She is a different person from Anna Schmidt, born 24 Nov 1888, Friedrich's sister, who died at Pfeilsdorf in 1906 (Anna Schmidt). ¶2
