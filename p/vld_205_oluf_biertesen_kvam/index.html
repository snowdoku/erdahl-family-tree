About the family

Father of Endre Olufsen Kvam (see his page), Probable; graded on his link to Endre. Primary (index of the probate, card read): skifte 5 Jul 1681 at Kvam i Lesje (Nord-Gudbrandsdal skifteprotokoll 1673-1688, fol. 106a; probate card 39945 image 7523) after 'Oluf Biertesen', married to 'Karj Endresdtr.'; Barn: 1. Endre Olufsen, 2. Ingelbret, 3. Pofuel. Why Probable: the step from the 1681 heir Endre Olufsen of Kvam to Endre Olufsen of Nordre Kvam, widower in 1708 and dead in 1726, rests on name, patronymic and farm across 27 years with no record read in between (audit 3: twenty years or more gives Probable). No rival Endre Olsen at Kvam is known. The Lesja bygdebok (vol. 1, canvas 215) makes the same identification: 'Ola... ca. 1608-ca. 1681, g.m. Kari Endresd.', children '1. Endre, ca. 1649, tok over', a child ca. 1652 who died before the 1681 skifte, Engebret, and '4. Pål, g. 1691 m. Ågot Larsd., N.Rånå'; it gives his tenure of Kvam from about 1623 and says he was perhaps from Einbu (the patronymic Biertesen fits the Einbu family of Pål Bjertesen, Pål (Pofuell) Bjertesen, b. about 1604, but no record links them; not entered). ¶1

Life story

Inside the 1681-1708 gap, the skifte after Jon Ingelbretsen at Qvam on 25 May 1696 (skifteprotokoll 1690-1719, source 25155, s. 31a, read on the scan) names 'Ender Qvam' as one of the two lagrettemenn who valued the estate, so a man called Endre lived on Kvam in 1696. This narrows the gaps to 15 and 12 years, but the 1696 entry gives no patronymic, and a rival is named: the 1681 skifte after Elli Amundsdatter, wife of Jon Ingelbretsen of Kvam (card 39945/7521, fol. 105b), lists her brothers Morten and 'Endre Amundsen' as heirs (residence not given). So the 1696 'Ender Qvam' is not proved to be Endre Olufsen, and the grade stays Probable. Own parents: Bjerte (see his page) and Magnhild (see her page) of Einbu by the bygdebok (canvas 180, 'Ola, ca. 1608, sjå Nørdre Kvam', hedged 'kanskje'); his patronymic Biertesen is from the 1681 card. That link up is Speculative to Probable. ¶3

Protocol 25154 fol. 106a read (5 Jul 1681): the skifte 'paa Quamb' (the next word, perhaps 'Ødeg.', is unclear) after 'Sl: Oluf Bier[te]ss:' between his wife Karri Endersd. and 'deris 3 Børn, Ender, Ingelbret og Poffuell Olufss:'. No ages and no guardians are given, so it does not bridge the 1681-1708 gap; grade unchanged. ¶4

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Test record: the protocol entry itself (skifteprotokoll 1673-1688, source 25154, fol. 106a) and any 1681-1708 court or probate record that names 'Endre Olsen Kvam' as a son of Oluf or a brother of Engebret or Povel Olsen; also the 1664-66 vicar's census for Lesja (Ola at Kvam with a son Endre about 15). ¶2
