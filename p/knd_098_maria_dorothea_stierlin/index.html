About the family

Mother (Probable, inferred): her father Joh Caspar 'Stierlein', son of Jacob, married Elisabetha Margaretha 'Majer', daughter of Tobias, at Bietigheim in 1718 (groom row 7270350, relatives 'Jacob, Elisabetha Margaretha'; bride row 907270350, relatives 'Tobias, Joh Caspar'; image 1184899-00384). Maria Dorothea married in May 1738, so was probably born about 1712-20; the 1718 marriage covers only the end of that window and an earlier marriage of Joh Caspar is not excluded (none is indexed at Bietigheim). The couple's daughter Maria Justina followed in 1728 (7269723). Her own baptism is not indexed. ¶2

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 3

Probable mother of Maria Dorothea Creutz (see her page), wife of Christoph Adam Knodel. ¶1a

Graded on that link: inferred. Maria Dorothea 'Stierlens' (genitive of Stierlen/Stierlin), daughter of Johann Caspar, married Johann Jacob Kreutz at Bietigheim on 13 May 1738 (bride row 907053511, groom row 7053511, image 1184901-00013). The daughter's 1759 bride row names only her father Jacob (record 907053778); the identification of that Jacob with the 1738 groom rests on his being the only Jacob Kreutz indexed at Bietigheim, the date, and the daughter's bearing her name. The daughter's baptism is not indexed. A Hanß Caspar Stierlin x Elisabetha Margaretha had a daughter at Bietigheim in 1728 (record 7269723); a Stierlin child of Hanß Caspar was buried there 18 Jan 1736 (record 7057255). ¶1b

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed, graded on her link to her daughter Maria Dorothea Creutz (see her page), wife of Christoph Adam Knodel. Record naming the relationship: V4LM-7HM (labelled), Maria Dorothea Creutz born 9 Nov 1740, christened Bietigheim 10 Nov 1740, Parents 'Johann Jacob Creutz, Maria Dorothea Stierler'. The in-gap records that lacked are found (FamilySearch 'Germany, Births and Baptisms', Bietigheim, same parents): Johannes Creutz christened 18 Sep 1742, died 4 Mar 1748; Christina Sophia christened 10 Jan 1744, died 24 Sep 1744; Johann Jacob Creuz christened 15 Jun 1745 (parents Johann Jacob Creuz x Maria Dorothea). So the Creutz family is at Bietigheim until at least March 1748, leaving about 11 years to the far record, the Bietigheim marriage of 12 Feb 1759 (VCY7-WYZ, bride Maria Dorothea Creuz, Parents 'Jacob Creuz'; Vaihingen twin QPGK-T6TG 'Jacob Creubzen'). That is within the 15-year rule; the far record ties the bride to the family by her father's name and the place; no rival Maria Dorothea Creutz was found (search 1738-44, and this search 1738-70 at Bietigheim). The 1740 child carries no death date in the index while her dead siblings do, and name reuse means Jacob Creutz had only one living daughter of that name. The earlier notes holding her at Probable are superseded. Read signed in, 2 Nov 2026; labelled index fields, images not opened. ¶4

The case for this identification 1

Labelled evidence: her link to her daughter is no longer inferred. FamilySearch, 'Germany, Births and Baptisms, 1558-1898', V4LM-7HM: Maria Dorothea Creutz, born 9 Nov 1740, christened Bietigheim 10 Nov 1740, Parents 'Johann Jacob Creutz, Maria Dorothea Stierler'. This also proves the Jacob Creutz of the 1759/60 marriage was married to a Maria Dorothea Stierlin. Grade held at Probable: the gap from this baptism to the bride of 12 Feb 1759 (father 'Jacob Creuz', VCY7-WYZ; 'Jacob Creubzen', QPGK-T6TG) is 18 years, and no record inside the gap shows the Creutz family at Bietigheim (audit 7 rule 1, first condition). No rival Maria Dorothea Creutz was found (search 1738-44). ¶3a
