About the family

NEW ANCESTOR, graded on his link to his son Orm Tallaksen (see his page). Åmli ætt og heim I (Tovdal), Øvre Skjeggedal gard nr. 4 (canvases 91-92): Peder [?] sold his part in 1704 to Tallak Ormson; in 1708 another part came to Tallak Ormson, and he then held the whole bruk (half of Øvre Skjeggedal); 'Der var [usemje] mellom dei 3 Orm-sønene' (Tallak, Gunder Ormsen and another), settled in 1712. Summary: 'Tallak Ormson f 1665 d 1719. g m Gro Mikkelsdtr. [?] f 1671 d 1717. Born: Orm f 1693 g 1726 m Ragnhild Nilsdtr. Vrålstad; Jakob f 1696 g 1726 m Gunhild [?]; Knut d.æ. f 1700 g 1730; Gunstein f 1706 g 1730 m Gunhild Nilsdtr. [Ytre] Ramse...'. Then 'Orm Tallakson [Øvre] Skjeggedal f 1693 d 1764, g 1726 m Ragnhild Nilsdtr. Vrålstad', whose children include Torjus f 1744 (= Torjus Ormsen). Vol. I canvas 106 says 5 of the 6 children of Tallak Ormson inherited from their father. His own father Orm is not identified in the rebuilt text. Bygdebok only (compiled secondary source, text rebuilt from word boxes, dates ABT), so Probable at most. ¶1

Father: Orm Aanonsen Tveitli (see his page), Probable. On the archivist's back of card 12321 (Gro's probate): 'Syster til Thallak Ormssøn: Helge Skeiggedal. Far: Orm Aanonsen Tveitli + sk. 28/1-1699', citing a 'skiftekontrakt mellom Thallak Ormssøn og syskina hans, dat. 28/1-1699'. In the probate after Salve Ormsøn, Øvre Skjeggedal, 27 Sep 1717 (Skp. 5 fol. 178, card 12323), the guardians of Salve's children include 'Farbroren Thallak Ormsøn Øvre Skieggedal', so Tallak was Salve's brother; and card 12324 gives 'Avd.s far: Orm Aanonsen Tveitli + sk. 28/1 1699'. Card 12317 (Skp. 4 fol. 192b, 1713) names 'Gunder Ormsøn Tvedlie' as morbror to Gundsteen Thorgiussøn, son of Helge Ormsdatter. ¶4

Life story

His own probate is found and read. Østre Råbyggelag Skifteprotokoll nr. 5 (DA 24353), fol. 272a (view 279), 'Anno 1719 dend 7 Junij' at Øfre Skieggedal, after the late 'Thallak Ormsøn', between his 'efterladte Børn': Orm Thallachsøn (adult; the card adds 'g.m. Ragnild Nielsdtr.'), Jacob Thallachsøn 23 aar (a soldier in Capitain Bodenhaupt's company), Knud Thallachsøn 19, Gundsten Thallachsøn 13, Knud Thallachsøn the younger 6, Errich Thallachsøn 2½, and Ingeborrig Thallachsdaatter. Card 39942/12331 indexes it the same way and refers to the skiftebrev after his wife Guro Michelsdatter, 25 Sep 1717. Guardians (fol. 273b-274a, view 281, read on the scan): for Knud the elder, 'Asuf Olsøn Løbdal', who is the children's first cousin on the father's side ('paa fædrenes side syskendebarn til Børnene'); for Gundsten, 'Morbroderen Paal Michelsøn Haartved i Nisdal'; another 'Morbroder', Aane Michelsøn, for Knud the younger. This is the probate his Test record named. It confirms again his link to his son Orm Tallaksen (see his page), Orm Thallachsøn being the eldest son, as in the 1717 probate; his grade (Confirmed) is unchanged. Death changed from ABT 1719 to BEF 7 JUN 1719. ¶5

What the 1719 probate says about his own father. The protocol pages read (fol. 272a-274a) do NOT name his father or the 1699 division. Among the debts owed by the estate is one to 'Gunder Ormsøn Tvedalie' (Tvedelien) concerning land at Tvedelien, and the land section refers only to the 1717 skifte after his wife. The archivist's typed back, card 12333, repeats 'Avd's far: Orm Aanonsen Tveitli + sk. 28/1 -1699' (an archivist's note, Probable only). ¶6

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: graded on his link to his son Orm Tallaksen (see his page). Østre Råbyggelag Skifteprotokoll nr. 5 (DA 24353, view 123), fol. 118a: 'Anno 1717 dend 25 Septemb.' at Øfre Skieggedal, probate after 'Guroe Michelsdaatter', divided between her surviving husband 'Thallach Ormsøn' and 'deris sammen avlede Børn': Orm Thallachsøn 24 aar; Jacob Thallachsøn 21 aar (a soldier in Capitain Sommerschiel's company); Knud Thallachsøn 17; Gundsten Thallachsøn 11; Knud the younger, 6; Errich ½; and Ingeborrig Thallachsdaatter, unmarried. The father is guardian. Card 39942/12319 indexes it the same way and adds '(+ sk. 1719)' for Tallak. Continuity to Orm Tallaksen in the tree (see his page, b. about 1693): Orm Thallachsøn, aged 24 in 1717 (so b. about 1693), at Øvre Skjeggedal; then 'Orm Tallachsøn Scheggedal', betrothed 15 Feb 1726 and married 21 Jun 1726 (DA 9920 canvas 42); then 'Orm Tallachsøn øfre Skiegedals barn Tallach', 1729 (canvas 50). That is 9-12 years on the same farm, and no other Orm Tallaksen is known in Tovdal. The relationship ('deris sammen avlede Børn', Orm Thallachsøn) is named in a primary record. Confirmed. This tests and confirms the bygdebok (Åmli ætt og heim I, canvas 92). He was alive on 25 Sep 1717; card 12319 says his own probate was in 1719 (not read). ¶3
