Life story

Death: 'Magnild Pedersd Østre Hjelde', kaarkone (retired farmer's widow on a life-lease), of Hjelde, aged 83 (b. about 1758), died 18 Aug and was buried 27 Aug 1841 at Frosta (Frosta ministerialbok 1838-1851, p. 254; index pg00000002690635). Østre Hjelde is Hiellan Øster, where she was Ole Larsen's wife in 1801 (aged 42), and the age agrees within a year. She is the only Magnhild Pedersdatter born 1753-1765 in the indexed Frosta burials. Birth estimate kept at ABT 1759 (census); the burial gives ABT 1758. ¶5

Married Ole Larsen of Hellan at Frosta on 17 Jun 1779. Named as mother 'Magnild Pedersdtr' of Hellan at her daughter Anna's baptism, Oculi 1780. Confidence upgraded to Confirmed as Anne Olsdatter's mother (baptism + 1801 census). ¶3

Frostaboka VIII p. 429 (canvas 429) has 'gift 1779 [med] Magnhild Pedersdtr. ____ 1760 [d.] 1841', with one unread word, about 8 characters wide, between 'Pedersdtr.' and '1760'. Word-box tests show the gap is not a farm number of the form (gn/bnr), for any gn 1-120. It is also not one of about 60 Frosta farm names and nearby parish names that were tested (Åsen, Leksvik, Skogn, Levanger, Stjørdal, Skatval, Verdal, Inderøy, Mosvik, Ytterøy, Lånke, Hegra, Selbu, Strinda, Trondheim, Malvik, Sparbu, Rissa, Stadsbygd, among others), nor 'fra' or 'født'. Her origin is therefore still unknown. The other Magnhild found in Frostaboka (VI p. 451) is a different woman, wife of Jens Ingebrigtsen. ¶6a

1801 census: Magnhild Pedersdatter, 42, wife of the farmer Ole Larsen at Hiellan Øster (Hillan), Frosta, in her first marriage. Her birth year of about 1759 is calculated from this age. ¶1
