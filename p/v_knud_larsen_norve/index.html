Life story

1st marriage: as a bachelor of Lille Nørve he married Anne Maria Larsdatter of Ytterland 1 Jan 1802 at Borgund. Children Larenze (~1802), Hans Andreas (~1805) and Lars Andreas Severin (~1807) were living with him in 1815. ¶2

Farmer at Lille Nørve, Borgund. Named as Berte Marie's father in her 1814 baptism, 1829 confirmation and 1846 marriage. ¶1

2nd marriage: Katrine (Catrine) Mortensdatter, before 1814. Children: Berte Marie (1814), Ole Martines (born 12 Jul 1816) and Andrine Maria Laurentze (born 30 Oct 1818). ¶3

Age 44 in the 1815 census, so born about 1771. Parentage (Probable): in 1801 an unmarried Knud Larssen, 30, was a son in the household of Lars Olesen (66) and Cæcilia Andersdatter (62) at Nørve Lille, the same farm. ¶4
