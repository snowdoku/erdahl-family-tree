About the family

David Erdahl's maternal great-great-grandmother, the mother of Mayme O. Eidem. Identified 11 Oct 2026. ¶1

Her parents and siblings, below, rest on Find a Grave's family links and are rated Probable. ¶5

David Erdahl's maternal great-great-grandmother, the mother of Mayme O. (Eidem) Eidem. Named as ANNA SATTER in the mother's-maiden-name field of her children's South Dakota birth certificates - Mamie's of 18 December 1906 (State File Number 14515), Samuel's (771318) and Amos's (39448) - and as 'Anna (Satter)' in her daughter Myrtle's 2013 obituary. ¶6

Her middle name Olene is the source of her daughter Mayme's lifelong middle initial 'O.' ¶7

She was born at Spink, a settlement in the south-east corner of Union County, South Dakota - so she was American-born, the daughter of Norwegian immigrants, while her husband was born in Norway. ¶9

Her parents were Gunder Gundersen Satter and Guri Anna Pettersdatter Walstad of the Satter homestead a mile north of the Spink store, Union County; her brothers and sisters were Gust (1864), Fred (1868), Lizzie (1870) and Lewis. ¶18

Life story

Born 21 March 1872 at Spink, Union County, South Dakota - so she was born in the Dakota Territory, of immigrant parents, while her husband was born in Norway. Died 8 March 1937 at Elk Point, aged 64. Buried Saint Paul Cemetery, Elk Point, under the joint stone 'EIDEM, Anna O 1872 MOTHER 1937, Severt S 1859 FATHER 1940'. ¶3

Gravestone: she and her husband share one stone in Saint Paul Cemetery, Elk Point, inscribed 'EIDEM / Anna O 1872 MOTHER 1937 / Severt S 1859 FATHER 1940'. ¶8

Cross-check on the surname: searching the South Dakota birth index on mother's maiden name Satter returns, besides her own three children, seven ABRAHAM children born in Union County between 1889 and 1905 - Gust 1889, Albert 1890, Anna 1892, Alfred 1894, Laura 1896, Edwin 1900, Richard 1905 - who are the children of her sister Lizzie Satter Abraham. The state index and the cemetery record agree without either having been built from the other. ¶10

Age at death as printed: '64 years, 11 months and 1[7] days'. From 21 March 1872 to 8 March 1937 is 64 years, 11 months and 15 days; the OCR of the day-figure is poor and the discrepancy is trivial. ¶24

1880 census: 'Anna C.', daughter, white, female, 8, at school, born Dakota Territory, in her father Gunder Satter's household at Spink Township, Union County, 15-16 June 1880 (ED 5, sheet 407A, line 8). Age 8 fits her birth of 21 March 1872. Her middle initial is written C., not O. (Olene), the only record so far to give it so. ¶29

1880 census, her siblings in the house: Gustav 16, Godfried 12, Eliza R. 10, Sophia 6, Mattilda 4 and Lewis 2. Sophia and Mattilda do not appear in her 1937 obituary, which lists surviving brothers Lewis, Fred and Gust and says (in a partly reconstructed passage) that one sister and one brother had died before her. ¶30

She was a bridesmaid at her brother Gust Satter's wedding to Sophia Larson at the Dale church, Union County, about 1894: the newspaper report names 'Miss Anna Setter' as one of the two bridesmaids. She was then about twenty-two, four years before her own marriage to Severt S. Eidem in 1898. ¶17

She married Sivert Eidem in 1898 and bore at least six children, of whom Mayme, born 18 December 1906, was the fourth. Her own daughter was named Mamie OLENE after her. ¶4

Marriage: 'In 1898 she was united in marriage to SIVERT SIVERTSON EIDEM and to this union were born six children, four [girls] and two boys.' This is the record that gives her husband his full Norwegian name and settles that the middle 'S.' of 'S. S. Eidem' is the patronymic Sivertson. ¶22

Residence: 'Since [her] marriage, with the exception of TWO AND A HALF [YEARS] IN CAN[ADA], she had resided with her husband at their home EIGHT MILES NORTH OF ELK POINT, where she died Monday, March 8 after several years of ill health, endured cheerfully and uncomplainingly.' The couple's two and a half years in Canada are new and unexplained; no child's birthplace in this file is Canadian, so the stay probably falls between 1911 and 1920. (The same paper's first notice, 11 March 1937, says 'seven miles north of Elk Point'; the full obituary says eight. Both are the same Brule Creek farm.) ¶23

1900 federal census: Brule township, Union County, South Dakota - 'Anna Ilene Eidesen, wife, born MARCH 1872, South Dakota'. The month and year agree exactly with her recorded birth, 21 March 1872 at Spink, Union County, and the census independently confirms that she was born in Dakota and not in Norway. ¶11

Census name forms: 'Anna Ilene' (1900), 'Anna O' (1910), 'Annie' (1920), 'Anna' (1915 and 1925 state), 'Anna OLIVIA' (1930), 'Anna O' (1935). The 1930 'Olivia' is the same hand that turned her daughter's 'Olene' into 'Ovedia'. ¶13

1905 South Dakota state census: 'Eidem, Anna, 33', Brule Township, Union County, Section 1, Township 92 North, Range 50 West, card 0028, next to her husband 'Eidem, Sivert, 45' on card 0027 and followed by the children Bertha 5, Samuel 4 and Salma 2. Her stated age is exactly right for a birth on 21 March 1872. ¶19

Named as the mother on her daughter's South Dakota birth registration - 'MAMIE EIDEM, state file no. 14515, b. 18 Dec 1906, Union County, mother's name ANNA SATTER' - and again in the 2013 obituary of her youngest daughter Myrtle, 'born January 4, 1911 near Elk Point, SD to Sivert and Anna (Satter) Eidem'. The surname is also spelled Sather and Satler in American records; it is the Norwegian farm name Saeter. ¶2

Named in her sister Lizzie Abraham's March 1934 obituary as the surviving sister, 'Mrs. S. S. Eidem, of Elk Point' — a contemporaneous printed source placing her in this sibship independently of Find a Grave. ¶16

1935 South Dakota state census: 'Anna O Eidem, b. 1872, South Dakota, Brule, Union, South Dakota'. The 1935 collection is the only South Dakota state-census index that records a place. She died on 8 March 1937, so this is her last census. ¶14

Obituary, Confirmed, and it names her parents: Leader-Courier (Elk Point, South Dakota), 18 March 1937. 'Anna [Olene] Satter, daughter of GUNDER and GURI ANNA SATTER, was born March 21, 1872 in Union county, South Dakota, ON THE FARM JUST WEST OF SPINK now occupied by Ira Peterson. She was BAPTIZED BY REV. CHRISTENSON and CONFIRMED AT THE BRULE CREEK CHURCH IN THE SPRING OF 1888 BY REV. E. O. DALE.' ¶20

Her working years, before marriage: 'After working for a number of [years] in SIOUX CITY, SIOUX FALLS and VERMILLION, [she] returned to KEEP HOUSE FOR HER UNCLE, OLE VOL[STAD]'; she also cared for him in his last years 'until his death at her home'. Walstad was her mother's maiden name, so Ole Walstad/Volstad was her mother's brother - and the 1880 federal census of Spink Township puts the Satter household 'living next door to Ole Walstad'. He is added to this file as mat_n2_ole_walstad. ¶21

How she was found: first notice, Leader-Courier, 11 March 1937, 'Long Time Resident of County Dies Suddenly' - 'Mrs. S. S. Eidem, a long time resi[dent] of this community, died sud[den]ly early Monday morning [at] her farm home 7 miles north [of] Elk Point. She had been ailing for a [num]ber of years and when her [hus]band came to the house after [do]ing his chores he found her [dead].... An obituary will be given [in the] next issue.' So Severt found her alone in the house. ¶25

Church life: 'Since her marriage she had been affiliated with the ST. PAUL LUTHERAN CHURCH and had been active in all its efforts. She loved her church and her Ladies Aid... She was one of the first St. Paul members interested in the WOMEN'S MISSIONARY FEDERATION... It was largely through her efforts that A FENCE WAS ERECTED AROUND THE ST. PAUL CEMETERY. In recognition of her services she received from the Ladies Aid a LIFE MEMBERSHIP IN THE WOMEN'S MISSIONARY FEDERATION, BEING THE FIRST ST. PAUL MEMBER TO RECEIVE SUCH a [recognition].' She attended a Ladies Aid meeting two weeks before she died. ¶26

Survivors, 1937: 'her husband, Sivert Eidem; six children, MAMIE and MYRTLE EIDEM, Mrs. MILTON LEER, and AMOS EIDEM [of] Elk Point, SAM EIDEM of YANKTON and Mrs. ROY NELSON of FAITH, S. D.; three brothers, LEWIS SATTER OF IDAHO, FRED SATTER OF MONTANA and GUST SATTER OF VERMILLION; five grandchildren; many [nieces and nephews]. [Her parents, one sister] and one brother have preceded her in death.' LEWIS SATTER OF IDAHO IS A BROTHER THIS PROJECT DID NOT HAVE; he is added as mat_n2_lewis_satter. Note that in March 1937 the Faith, South Dakota daughter is 'Mrs. ROY Nelson' and in the 1940 obituary of her father she is 'Mrs. RAYMOND Nelson' - one of the two is a slip. ¶27

Funeral: 'Funeral services were March 12 at the home at 1:30 and at the South St. Paul church at 2:15. Rev. [-] L. Aamoth officiating.' Burial in the church cemetery. ¶28

Cemetery register: the DT58 register of Union County lists ANNA O E EIDEM, 1937 (year only), at SOUTH ST PAULS LUTHERAN cemetery, lot 7, block 1 (city column Elk Point). The name and year fit Anna Olene, who died 8 March 1937. The register's name for the cemetery is recorded as given; 'South St. Paul' is also what her obituary calls the church of her funeral. The same lot holds earlier Eidem burials (Brunilo 1870, Alice C and Alice B 1874, Thomas S 1883, O S 1928), whose relationship to her is not known. ¶31

Corrections 1

Correction: the 1910 census index gives her birthplace as NORWAY. That is wrong. The 1900, 1920, 1930 and 1935 censuses all say South Dakota, and her birth at Spink in March 1872 is documented. The 1910 entry is an enumerator or indexer error - the Norway in the column above was carried down. ¶12

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Not yet read: the 1910 schedule asks a married woman how many children she has borne and how many are living. That column would settle whether Severt and Anna lost a child between their 1898 marriage and Bertha's birth in March 1900. It is on the record-detail page and is not free. ¶15
