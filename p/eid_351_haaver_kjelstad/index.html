About the family

Father of Mali Haaversdatter Garberg (see her page). The skifte after Bryniel Olsdatter at Kjelstad on 20 Jun 1744 (24753 fol. 94-95) lists, after her children by her widower Ole, 'den afgangne Kones' children by her late former husband: '1 Berit Haaversdatter', wife of Poul Pedersen Rønsberg, and '2 Hustru Mali Haaversd: Garberg'. The same farm and the same two names (Birgete = Berit, and Mali) as the daughters of Hover Andersen Kielstad in 1701 identify Bryniel's first husband as this Haaver; Ellen had presumably died by 1744. ¶2

Life story

Farmer at Kjelstad, Selbu, who died before 7 Nov 1701. The Stjør- og Verdal probate card index (77804, card 971, KJELSTAD), abstracting the skiftedesignasjon 1692-1714 (Trondhjems Stiftamt pk. 131), reads: 'Hover Andersen Kielstad, "værit gift", Selbu. Skifte nr. 98, 7/11-1701. Arv: 38 rd. 42 sk. Arvinger: Ellen Hoversdtr., Birgete [Hoversdtr.], Mali [Hoversdtr.], "samtl. hos Moderen"' - his heirs were three daughters, all living with their mother. ¶1

The unresolved 1744 ages noted are explained: the minors aged 9-18 in Brynhild Olsdatter's 1744 skifte are her grandchildren (children of a late daughter married to Corporal Ole Pedersen), not her own children, so nothing in that skifte conflicts with her having borne Haaver's daughters before 1701. His grade stays Probable (card abstract; the 1701 skiftedesignasjon, Trondhjems Stiftamt pk. 131, is still unread). ¶4

Checked: Trondhjems Stiftamt pk. 131 itself is not scanned on Digitalarkivet. Its card register (Digitalarkivet 77836, 'Index cards, probate designations, Stjør- og Verdal fogderi, 1692-1714'; first image URN sk10151611360962) has the same card format as the 77804 abstract (deceased, skifte nr., date, 'Arv', heirs, 'hos Moderen'), with no widow's name, so it adds nothing to card 971. The protocol entry 'skifte nr. 98, 7/11-1701' remains the test record; queue for the Trondheim archive if needed. ¶5

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Graded Probable on his link to Mali (see her page): the 1701 card is an archivist's abstract of a list of estates whose heirs are given by patronymic without a relationship word, and the identification of the 1701 girl at Kjelstad with the Garberg widow of 1741-61 rests on the 1744 probate (which does not write the first husband's name) plus the matching sister Berit. The record that would confirm it is the 1701 skiftedesignasjon itself (Trondhjems Stiftamt pk. 131) or the protocol entry 'skifte nr. 98' of 7 Nov 1701, or the Kjelstad chapter of the Selbu bygdebok. His patronymic makes his father an Anders. ¶3
