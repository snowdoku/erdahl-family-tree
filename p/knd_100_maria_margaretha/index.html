Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Speculative mother of Johann Erhard Rampacher (see his page): the Vaihingen birth of Johann Erhardt 'Ramprecht', 19 Oct 1682, names 'Johann Christoph, Maria Margaretha' (record 4767443). Same identification caveat as for her husband. Ancestry 61023 index rows do not label roles (BRIEF audit 5 rule 3), so this rests at Probable until the image or a labelled FamilySearch twin is read; images not seen. ¶1

Upgrade: Speculative to Probable. Graded on her link to her son Johann Erhard (see his page): the Vaihingen birth row of 19 Oct 1682 names 'Johann Christoph, Maria Margaretha' (record 4767443), and that child is now tied to the 1704-1722 Johann Erhard by the chain set out under her husband (first wife's death 8 Jan 1717, 4400121; second family from 1718, 4769004; no rival Johann Erhard of fitting age). She is the mother named at Vaihingen in every indexed baptism of the couple, 1677-1687 (4767350, 4767443, 4767487, 4767552). Elimination argument on unlabelled rows, so Probable. Her surname, marriage (about 1676) and death are not indexed. ¶2
