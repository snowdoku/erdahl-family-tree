Life story

Birth registered in the South Dakota index: born 25 Jul 1913 in Union County, father ANDREW ABRAHAM, mother LIZZIE SATTER, the certificate filed 17 OCT 1942. Seven of the thirteen children were registered in a single batch on 17 October 1942, the wartime delayed-registration pattern of a family obtaining papers all at once. ¶2

Living at Sioux City, Iowa in 1934 ('Evelyn and Elnora, residing in Sioux City'). ¶3
