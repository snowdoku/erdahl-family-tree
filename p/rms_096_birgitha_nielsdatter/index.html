Life story

Collateral. Wife of Ole Evindsen Hillestad and mother of Ole Olsen Hillestad (d. 1712): the skifte after her son refers to 'skiftebrevet etter mor til avl. Ole Olsøn Hillestad, - Birgitha Nielsdtr., dat. 12/6 - 1699' (card 5001, also card 4999 back). Graded on her link to Ole Olsen Hillestad. ¶1

Not attached as mother of Ingeborg Olsdatter Ramse. She may be: Ingeborg named a daughter Birgitha and a son Niels. But the Tovdal bygdebok says Ole Evindsen married twice (second wife a Birgit, probably from Risland), and does not show (in the words recovered) which wife bore Ingeborg, who was born about 1650-1660 and perhaps older than her brother Ole (married by 1689). ¶2
