About the family

Children: at the same service on 5 Dec 1784 'Anders Olsen Wiikes Søn, kaldet Lasse' was baptised; the register never names mothers, but a child baptised the week his father's wife died in childbed is hers (inference). The 1818 probate after Anders Olsen Wiige states that the deceased had six children 'af første Ægteskab' (of his first marriage): Ole (dead by 1818, of Gjermundnæs), Knud (Willa), Anders, Ole (Wiige), Lasse and Bereth. Anders was a bachelor (Ungkarl) when he married Hille in 1766, she was still his lawful wife when she died in 1784, and he remarried as a widower in 1786, so his first marriage is this one and all six children are Hille's. No record names her as Lasse's mother in words; Confirmed rests on this chain of primary records (1766 marriage index, 1784 burial and baptism scans, 1818 probate scan). A granddaughter named Hille and a son named Knud fit her name and her father's. ¶3

Life story

Probate index check: no Knud with domicile Legernes, Legernæs or Lægernæs is in the Romsdal index. Knud Rasmussen of 'Vike', 1755 (3A 08 fol. 389a), read on the scan, was of 'gaarden Vige i Eidsfiords Otting' (Eresfjord), not Veøy, so he is ruled out as her father. ¶7

Marriage (index): Anders Olsen, bachelor and soldier (Ungkarl og Soldat) of Wiike, married Hille Knudsdatter, maiden (Pigen), on 16 Jun 1766 in Veøy. ¶1

Probate (skifte) read on the scan: Romsdal sorenskriveri, skifteprotokoll 3A 11 (1782-1789), fol. 198a, registered 27 Dec 1785 at the farm Yttre Wige (Vike) in Vesnes tinglag: 'holdet Skifte efter afg[angne] Hille Knudsdatter imellem Enkemanden Anders Olsen og deres 6 Børn', namely 1. son Ole Andersen, 19, guardian (formynder) Baard Hansen Legernes; 2. son Knud Andersen, 16, guardian Christopher Saltkammer[?]; 3. son Anders Andersen, 8, guardian Ole Jonsen Gjelvig[?]; 4. son Ole Andersen the younger, 4, guardian Peder Knudsen Leg[ernes]; 5. son Lasse Andersen, 1, guardian the lensmann (parish constable) Erich Lassesen Leg[ernes]; 6. daughter Brit Andersdatter, 12, guardian Knud Hansen Aas. The entry confirms her marriage to Anders Olsen and her six children, but states no relationship for any guardian. ¶6a

Burial (scan read): on the 2nd Sunday in Advent, 5 Dec 1784, at Vesnes church, Veøy, earth was cast on 'Anders Olsen Wiikes ægte Hustrue Hilde Knudsdatter, som døde i Barsel-Seng den 1ste Decbr. 43 aar gammel' (Anders Olsen Wiike's lawful wife Hilde Knudsdatter, who died in childbed on 1 Dec, 43 years old). The age gives a birth about 1741. ¶2
