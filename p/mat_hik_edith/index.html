About the family

Younger sister of Lydia Adelaide (Hicken) Hayter. Per the compiled Hicken and Creed genealogies: born 21 April 1887 at St Mary's Bay, baptised 15 May 1890 at the Sturgeon Methodist Church; married Austin Locke Jackson (b. 1863 Point Pleasant, d. 1936 Montague) at Georgetown on 8 June 1906; died 19 December 1916 (at Point Pleasant per the Hicken compiler, Gaspereaux per the Creed compiler) aged 29. Children included Alonzo (1907-1985, d. Langley, BC), Daisy (1909-1984), Marshall 'Red' (1911-1997) and Woodrow Wilson Jackson (b. 1913). Her middle name, Gordon, is her paternal grandmother's maiden name. Not yet checked against a primary record. ¶1

Life story

Baptism: Edith Gordon Hicken (indexed 'Hickin'), born 21 April 1887 at Sturgeon, baptised 15 May 1890 together with her brother George Howard. The parents are given as William J. and Bexy (PEI Baptism Index; Sturgeon register, FamilySearch film 005240351, image 213). ¶2

Name: her middle name Gordon was the given name of her great-grandfather Gordon Thompson (1780-1815), father of her grandmother Frances Carpenter Thompson. ¶3

1891 census: Edith, 4, daughter, in her father William Hicken's household, Township 61. 1901 census (29 May 1901, Township 61, Sturgeon): Hicken, Edith, daughter, born 21 April 1887, 14, in the household of her stepfather Shedrick W. Johnston and her mother Bexy. ¶4

Marriage: to Austin Locke Jackson, 8 June 1906, Georgetown (PEI BMB index, FamilySearch film 005240344, image 95). He was a widower, born September 1863. His first wife Annie had been with him next door to Edith's mother in 1901. ¶5

1911 census (5-6 June 1911, Gaspereaux, Township 61, page 3, dwelling 20): Jackson, Austin, head, 47, Scotch, Baptist, fisherman; Edith, wife, born April 1887, 24, English, Baptist. The household held Austin's children by his first wife, Margaret (1897), Shedrick (1898), Mark (1899) and Wilfrid (1901), and Edith's own children Alonzo (4) and Daisy (2). ¶6

Death: 19 December 1916 at Point Pleasant, aged 29, per the compiled genealogies. Her PEI death registration (1906-onward series, at PARO) has not been seen. ¶7
