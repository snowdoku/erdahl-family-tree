Primary records that would test it: the skifte after Knut Rolfsen or his wife Kari (Nord-Gudbrandsdal probate cards, source 39945, 'Sletten nordre' cards at images ~12263-12289 show none before 1759; perhaps under another farm spelling or in the Lesja Jernverk protocol), or Rolv's baptism about 1720 (before the surviving Lesja books, which start 1724). ¶1

Life story

Origin found. The Lesja bygdebok vol. 3 (canvas 326, chapter Ula) lists 'Knut, ca. 1685, [sjå] Nørdre Slettom' among the children of a man (ca. 1639 - ca. 1694) whose skifte was held in 1694 ('Om skiftet etter Rolf i 1694'). Father Rolf of Ula entered as Probable (compiled source). Knut's own grade is unchanged. ¶2

The 1694 skifte after his (Probable) father Rolf Christensen at Ula was read (source 25155 fol. 23b): heirs the widow Anne Arnesd. and 'deris 6 Børn', among them Knud Rolfsen. Mother Anne Arnesdatter entered as Probable. Knut's own grade is unchanged. ¶3

The 1723 skifte after his paternal uncle Hans Christensen at Ula (Nord-Gudbrandsdal skifteprotokoll 1719-1726, source 25156, view 136, fol. 131b-132a, May 1723) names him among the heirs: 'Sl. Rolf Christensens Børn... 3. Knud Rolfsen Slett[om] 38 aar', with his brothers Thoer Rolfsen 60 and Ole Rolfsen, and the children (Lars, [Guri?] and Anne Ingebrigtsen/-datter) of his dead sister Magnild Rolfsdatter Slett[om]. This gives him b. ABT 1685 and confirms his parents (Rolf Christensen Ula, Anne Arnesdatter Ula). His own grade (his link to Rolv Knudsen) is unchanged. ¶4

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
