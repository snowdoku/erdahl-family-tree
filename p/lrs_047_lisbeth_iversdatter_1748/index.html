Life story

Baptism: Baptised 5 Jan 1748 in Gulen parish as 'Iver botnens B[arn] N[omine] Lispet, Mat[er]: Lispet', Iver Botnen's child named Lisbeth, mother Lisbeth. Godparents: Johannes in Taskle, Hans Persen in Taskle, Jon Bendixsen in Nordal[?], Johannes Olsen and Borni Nielsdatter of Hjartholm. The mother was churched on 17 Feb 1748. Gulen church book 1736-1749, canvas 62. She is the elder sister of Rasmus Iversen (same parents, baptised 1750). ¶1

Later life: Probably the 'Lisbeth Iwers D[atter] Botnen' who stood godmother on 1 May 1774 together with Johannes Iwersen Botnen. A Lisbeth Iversdatter of Botnen appears in the Gulen marriage index in 1783 (pv00000000616046); that entry has not been read and is not linked to her. ¶2
