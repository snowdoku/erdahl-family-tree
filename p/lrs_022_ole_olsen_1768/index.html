About the family

Mother (from the 1801 census): Ingebor Torkelsdatter (see her page), 68, widow after her first marriage, listed in his household at Ibræke as 'Husbondens moder' (the householder's mother). ¶8a

Father (Probable): the elder Ole Olsen of Ibrække (see his page), buried at Nissedal in Apr/May 1790 aged 69 (Kviteseid 7983 c231L). The identification rests on farm, patronymic, the son's bachelor status at Ibrække in 1791 and his widowed mother in 1801; see the father's notes. ¶9

Father's probate: the 1790 skifte after Ole Olsen of Ibrække (died 27 Apr 1790; 25679 skifte No 31, view 84R) names as sole heir with the widow Ingeborg Torkelsdatter their son Ole Olsen, aged 22, with John Halvorsen as his curator. This proves his father, and with the 1801 census it proves his mother. It also argues against the Tvetene baptism candidate of 10 Jul 1768 unless the family moved to Ibrække after 1768. The appraisers were his future father-in-law John (Joen) Halvorsen Grimstvedt and Targe Halvorsen Grimstvedt, who was later a cautioner at his 1791 betrothal. ¶10

Life story

Marriage (dates recomputed): on the 5th Sunday in Lent (Judica), 10 Apr 1791, at Kirkenes in Nissedal, the bachelor (U.K., ungkarl) Ole Olsen of Ibrække was betrothed to the maiden Johanne Joensdatter of Grimstvedt; cautioners (forlovere, sureties) Targie Halvorsen Grimstvedt and Hølje Tovsen Ibrække. They married on Whit Monday ('2 Pintse D.'), 13 Jun 1791, in Nissedal church. The register gives only the feast names; the dates are computed from Easter 1791 = 24 Apr (Gregorian, used in Denmark-Norway from 1776). Kviteseid ministerialbok 1787-1799 (source 7983), marriages 1791, p. 188. ¶6

1801 census: Ole Olsen, 33, householder (husbonde), farmer-occupier (bonde og gaardbeboer) of household 2 at Ibræke, Nissedal (then Kviteseid prestegjeld), in his first marriage. Household: wife Johanne Joensdatter 32; children Ingebor Olsdatter 8, Ole Olsen 6 and Joen Olsen 3; servant Ragnild Tellefsdatter 16; and his mother Ingebor Torkelsdatter, 68, widow after her first marriage. Household 1 on the same farm was Hølje Tovsen's. ¶1

Baptism of son: father of Torkild, baptised 9 Aug 1801 in Nissedal church ('Ole Olsen Ibrækes Søn Torkild'). ¶2

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Graded Confirmed: the census states the relation, and he is identified as the household head by name, age, farm and wife Johanne Joensdatter. His father, an Ole, is not identified. ¶8b

Upgrade: Confirmed as the father of Torkild Olsen (see his page). The Nissedal confirmation list for 1822 (ministerialbok 1814-1845, source 8019, p. 246, canvas 160, boys no. 12) reads 'Torkild Ibrek | Ole og Johanne Ibrek | døbt 1801 d. 10 Sønd. e. Trin.', with the verdict 'ringe i Kundskab men god i Dyd og Forhold' (poor in knowledge but good in conduct) and vaccination on 4 Mar 1806 by A. Hansen. This names his parents as Ole and Johanne of Ibrække and his baptism as the one of the 10th Sunday after Trinity 1801 ('Ole Olsen Ibrækes Søn Torkild', 9 Aug 1801). A year later 'Torkeld Olsen Ibrække', 22, married Aasild Gunleiksdatter of Midbø, so name, farm and age join the confirmand to the groom. His own parents are proved by the 1790 probate and the 1801 census (see the earlier notes). ¶11

The case for this identification 1

Identification as the father of Torkild Olsen (see his page) is Probable: the 1801 baptism fits Torkild's 1846 burial age exactly and his lifelong residence at Ibrække, and 'Ole Olsen Ibrække' (this man or his son Ole) witnessed Torkild's 1823 marriage. A skifte (probate) or burial entry for Ole Olsen of Ibrække, or Torkild's confirmation entry, would confirm it. Not searched: Ole's burial in the Nissedal registers. ¶3

Records that disagree 1

Against: the farm is Tvetene, not Ibræke, and with no mother named it cannot be tied to his mother Ingebor Torkelsdatter. No Ole son of an Ole of Ibræke was found in the Nissedal baptisms of Sep 1767 to Sep 1770. Would decide it: his confirmation or marriage entry, or a record naming Ingebor Torkelsdatter as the wife of Ole Olsen Tvetene. ¶5b

Corrections 1

Correction: the evidence card gives the dates as 20 Mar 1791 (betrothal) and 23 May 1791 (marriage), computed from a wrong Easter date (3 Apr 1791). The correct Easter is 24 Apr 1791 (recomputed), so the 5th Sunday in Lent was 10 Apr and Whit Monday 13 Jun 1791. The marriage date, previously only 'BEF 1793', is set to 13 Jun 1791. ¶7

Alternatives considered and set aside 3

Homonym: not the same man as Ole Olsen (Ole Olsen of Bøe, Nord-Aurdal, Valdres, father of Erik b. about 1811) or Ole Olsen Omland. Ole Olsen is a very common name; this man is identified only by the farm Ibræke/Ibrække in Nissedal and his wife Johanne Joensdatter. ¶4

Baptism candidate (possible only, not linked): on the 6th Sunday after Trinity 1768 (10 Jul 1768) Pastor Børresen baptised in Nissedal church 'Ole Olsen Tvetenes Søn Ole' (Ole, son of Ole Olsen of Tvetene). Godparents: Kittil Nordboe, Ambjøn Grimstvedt, Gunild Grimstvedt, Aage Li[a?], Aage Tvetene. The mother is not named. Kviteseid ministerialbok 1754-1773, baptisms 1768, p. 115 (source 7981, canvas 50, left page). For: the names and the date fit his 1801 age of 33. ¶5a

Burial candidates, none adopted: Nissedal burials give an Ole Olsen aged so as to be born 1768, buried 13 Jul 1837 (pg00000006405084, also indexed as pg00000008652352), and another born about 1773, buried 4 Sep 1837 (pg00000006405102); Treungen has one born about 1770, buried June 1842 (pg00000006882116). None gives a farm. The first fits his 1801 age (33) exactly, but with two rivals in the same parish no date is entered; the full entries (scan) may name a farm (Ibræke) or a widow. ¶12
