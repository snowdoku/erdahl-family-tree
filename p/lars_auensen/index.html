Life story

Burial: 'Gift Husmand Lars Ornesen', b. 1818 Eide, died 3 Sep 1884, living at Stegeberg. His widow Siri Karine also lived at Stegeberg, and this links the burial to him. Baptism: Lars, born 13 Dec 1818 and baptised 16 Dec 1818, son of Orm Jonsen and Ragla Peersdatter, husmannsfolk under Ytre Eide. Name, birth year and birthplace all match. The identification of the baptism and the burial is rated Probable (strong). ¶7

Married 3 Apr 1845 in Skudenes parish (Åkra), as 'Ungkarl Lars Ormsen Yttre Eide, husmandssøn', born Ytre Eide in Avaldsnes, age 25 1/2. The bride was Siri Karine Hansdatter of Åkra. Witness: Paul Ormsen Aadland, probably his brother Poul (b. 1809). Banns were first read 6 Aug 1844. ¶4

At Slettetræ, Avaldsnes in the 1865 census with a son Jahn of the right age. Name, age and parish fit, but no record yet links him to the Skrede family. ¶1

His patronymic is written in many ways: Aanensen, Aauensen, Annesen, Arntsen, Auensen (1865), Aarentsen (1875), Aanonsen (1874), Ormsen (marriage 1845) and Ornesen (burial 1884). His father was recorded as 'Orm', 'Arent' and 'Aanen' Johnsen. A daughter of that father named her son 'Aanen' in 1839, so the father was probably known locally as Ånen/Arnt, while the clergy wrote Orm. ¶3

1875 census: 'Lars Aarentsen', b. 1817 Avaldsnes, husmann with land at Stølen under Stegeberg, with his wife Siri Karine Hansdatter. ¶6
