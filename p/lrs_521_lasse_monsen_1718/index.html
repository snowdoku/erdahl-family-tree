About the family

Parents (Confirmed): the probate after Mons Johansen of Indre Oppedal (registered 16 May, closed 7 Oct 1739) and the division in his widow Randi Iversdatter's estate (Nr. 77, 1739) name 'Sønnen Lasse' / '4. Søn Lasse' among 'alle Monses Sønner' (all Mons's sons). Within weeks, about 4 Oct 1739, 'Lasse Monsen indre Opdal' was betrothed at Brekke. In 1754 'Farbroderen Lasse Monsen Jndre Op[pedal]' (the paternal uncle) was guardian for the children of Torkild Monsen of Asheim (see his page), whose parents Mons Johansen and Randi Iversdatter are Confirmed by his baptism (patch 032). Mons Johansen was a bachelor when betrothed to Randi on 3 Jun 1703 (patch 036), so a son born about 1718 is hers. The Gulen burial index sweep for bustad=Indre Oppedal, etternamn=Monsen finds only Lasse Monssøn; no second Lasse Monsen is known at Indre Oppedal 1739-1788. The grade rests on the identity of the 1739 son with the man of the same name and farm in 1739, 1741, 1754, 1783 and 1788, which no record contradicts; an auditor may prefer Probable because no single record calls him Mons's son by full name. ¶1

Children: a daughter Rand was baptised 16 Feb 1741 ('Lasse Monsen indre Opdals barn N. Rand. Mat: Anna'; godparents Torchil, Guttorm, Gregorius, Hans Hansen, Eli Hendrichsdatter, all of Indre Oppedal). The 1783 division names the children Mons Larsen (eldest son), Niels, Johannes, Randi (married to Peder Olsen), Gurbind[?] (dead, leaving two daughters), Magnilde (married to Hendrich Gut[tormsen?] of Indre Oppedal) and Elie, 23. Randi of 1783 is very probably the Rand of 1741. ¶3

Pedigree collapse (corrected by audit): with this link, Mons Johansen and Randi Iversdatter (see his page, Randi Iversdatter) are the paternal grandparents of Mons Lassesen (see his page). They also enter the line through their son Torkild Monsen of Asheim (see his page) and his daughter Randi Torkildsdatter (see her page), and through their son Iver Monsen of Botnen (see his page) and his daughter Anna Iversdatter (see her page). So the couple appears at least three times in the pedigree. Mons Lassesen's wife Else Torkildsdatter (see her page) is NOT a daughter of Torkild Monsen. Her father is Torkel Iversen of Indre Oppedal (see his page, Confirmed from his 1775 probate), and her mother Britte Monsdatter (see her page) has no proved parents. Mons and Else would be related through Mons Johansen and Randi only if Britte were their daughter. That is unproved (patch 035 on hold), and the 1739 heirs, 'alle Monses Sønner', name no daughter, which weighs against it (inference). ¶6

Son Mons: his son Mons was baptised at Brekke on Dom. Oculi, 25 Feb 1742 ('dobt Lasse Monsen ind: Opdals b. N. Mons Mat: anne'); the mother was churched on 1 Apr 1742. This is his eldest son Mons Larsen of the 1783 division and Mons Lassesen (see his page), whose link to him is now Confirmed (see the note 'Parents (Confirmed)' on Mons). ¶7

Life story

Baptism: at the service in Brekke church on 3 Sep 1717 (margin 'Anno 1717'): 'Christnet Mons Inderopdals b: N: Lasse, Mat: Randi; Test: Gutorm Inderopdal, Grimmund Inderopdal, [T?]ster[?] Gutormss. Inderopdal, Siri Aslachsdr: ibid, Anna[?] Ifversdr: ibid' (christened Mons of Indre Oppedal's child named Lasse, mother Randi; godparents...). This is the baptism of Lasse Monsen, son of Mons Johansen and Randi Iversdatter: same farm and parents' names, and it fits his burial age of 70 1/2 in Feb 1788 (born about Aug 1717). The godmother Siri Aslaksdatter was the wife of Henrik Iversen of Indre Oppedal (his 1741 probate, Ytre Sogn 6a No. 66), very probably Randi's brother, and 'Anna Ifversdr.' may be Randi's sister Anna (reading of the first name uncertain). Birth changed from 'ABT 1718' to 'BEF 3 SEP 1717'. ¶8

Marriage: betrothed about 4 Oct 1739 (Sunday number unclear; the entry follows one of 2 Oct) and married 1 Nov 1739 at Brekke to Anne of Indre Oppedal (see her name note). Cautioners (forlovere, who vouched there was no impediment): Hendrich indre Opdal and Torchil [Iversen?] indre Opdal (Gulen ministerialbok 11441, canvas 15). ¶2

1783 division (reading of the heading): on 3 Jun 1783 the court held 'Skifte og Deeling' at Indre Oppedal at the request of Lasse Monsen and his 'Hustrue' (wife) Anna Gullachs[datter], who had long since sold and ceded their land at Indre Oppedal to their eldest son Mons Larsen and now, in old age, wanted their estate divided among the children. Both were therefore living; this is a division in their lifetime (skifte i levende live), not a probate after a death. Some words of the heading are uncertain, but 'Hustrue' and the reference to their old age are clear enough to rule out the reading that either was dead. ¶4

Burial: 'den 12te Febr: Engravnt i Brekke Mand Lasse Monssøn Indre Oppedal gammel 70½ Aar, jordfæst[et] Festo 1ste Pasch. d. 23de Martii': he was interred (engravet) at Brekke on 12 Feb 1788, and the earth-casting ceremony (jordfestelse) by the priest followed at Easter, 23 Mar 1788 (re-read by audit; the entry above it uses the same 'date: Engravnt' form). He therefore died before 12 Feb 1788. Age 70½ gives a birth about 1717-18, consistent with his brother Torkild's birth in Oct 1719 (index birth year 1718). ¶5
