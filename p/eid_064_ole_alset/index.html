About the family

Father of Siur (Sivert) Olsen Alset (see his page), rated Probable. For: Siur's patronymic makes his father an Ole; he was 'Siur Olsen Alset' at his burial in 1780; Ole Alset is the only father of a Sivert at Alset in read of every entry July 1729 - November 1733 (16310 canvases 118-130); Siur's burial age of 51 (born about 1728-29) is within one to three years of either baptism, a usual margin for burial ages of the period. Uncertain: which of the two Siverts is Siur (most probably the 1731 child, if the 1730 child died), and whether Siur was born at Alset at all - he may have married into Alset through his wife Ane Gundersdatter, and the baptisms before July 1729 (16310 canvases about 110-117) have not been read for a Sivert to another Ole. ¶2a

Further children: on the 14th Sunday after Trinity, 26 September 1734, 'döbte Ole Alsets barn Nom: Joen' - baptised Ole Alset's child Jon; his wife was churched on the 19th Sunday after Trinity 1734 together with Erik Langlien's wife (Selbu ministerialbok 1699-1737, canvas 134 left and right). On Rogate Sunday, 26 May 1737, 'Nock døbt Ole Siverthsen Alsets Søn Povel' - also baptised Ole Sivertsen Alset's son Povel; godparents Erich Langlien, [Hal]vard Berge, Baro Alset, Ingebor Garberg and Berit Alset; his wife was churched at the Visitation, 2 July 1737, as 'Oluf Siverts[en] Alsets qvinde' (canvas 144 left and 145 right). ¶4

Life story

Baptism found: 21st Sunday after Trinity (28 Oct) 1703, 'døbt Siür [..] Alsets barn Olaf, fadd: Jon Lauglo, Olaf Uglum, Biørn Alset, Brynel Kalstad[?] og Ingebor Alset' - baptised Olaf, child of Siur Alset (16310 canvas 17 right). On the 2nd Sunday in Advent 1703 'introd: Siür Alsets qv. Gertrud efter det 1ste barn [Olaf]' - Siur Alset's wife Gertrud was churched after her first child (same canvas; crop checked by the orchestrator). Godfather Bjørn Alset is Siur's brother (the soldier Bjørn, 28, in 1701). This makes him Ole Sivertsen (Siursen) Alset by birth as well as by later usage; his first sons were named Sivert (1730, 1731) after this father. ¶8a

Baptisms of two sons named Sivert: on Invocavit Sunday, 26 February 1730, 'døbt Ole Alsets B: Nom: Sivert' - baptised Ole Alset's child, named Sivert - with 'introd: Ole Alsets qvinde', his wife churched, on Judica Sunday 1730 (Selbu ministerialbok 1699-1737, canvas 120 left page); and on 31 October 1731, 'døbt Ole Alsets barn N: Siverd', his wife churched on the 1st Sunday in Advent 1731 (canvas 125 left and right). Found, crops. This period of the register records no godparents and no mother's name. The second Sivert implies the first died young; his burial is not yet found. ¶1

Patronymic Sivertsen, rated Probable. The 1730-1734 entries call him only 'Ole Alset', because this period of the register gives no patronymics; the 1737 entry is the first to name him Ole Sivertsen Alset. He is taken to be the same man because: no other Ole at Alset appears as a father, husband or burial in the complete reads of October 1724 - March 1738; the births of February 1730, October 1731, September 1734 and May 1737 are evenly spaced, as in one household; Erik Langlien, whose wife was churched with Ole Alset's in 1734, stood first godfather in 1737; and a man whose father was Sivert would, by Selbu naming custom, name his first son Sivert, which explains the two Siverts of 1730 and 1731. This also makes Siur (Sivert) Olsen Alset (see his page) a namesake of his grandfather - inference. ¶5a

Named 'Ole Siversen, 30 år gl.', eldest son, in the probate after his mother Gjertrud Jonsdatter, 11 Feb 1732 (card 77804 c21). ¶10

1762 extra tax list: head of Alset 732, Selbu, as 'Ole Siursen', with his wife Ingri Svendsdatter and six sons over 12 (Jon, married to Brynild Pedersdatter; Siur; Svend; Povel; Ole; Erik[?]) and a daughter Gjertrud (head cell). This confirms that he was alive in 1762 and that the sons Siur, Jon and Povel, baptised 1730-37, were still at Alset. ¶6

Burial, 1st Sunday after Epiphany 1768: 'Begravet Olle Sivertsøn Alset død d: 6 Jan: 64 aar gl.' - Ole Sivertsen Alset, died 6 Jan 1768, aged 64 (16311 canvas 24). The age fits his baptism of 28 Oct 1703 exactly and the patronymic confirms Sivertsen. ¶9

His probate, Alset, 4 Feb 1768 (24756 fol. 92a): widow Ingri Svendsdatter; children Syvert, Svend, Joen, Ole, Erich and Giertrud Olsdatter, 23. The guardian of the daughter Giertrud was 'hendes Farbroder Joen Syvertsen Garberg', so he had a brother Jon Siursen at Garberg. ¶12

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Speculative father: Siur (Sivert) Olsen, aged 20 in the 1701 manntall at Alset, son of Ole Jonsen (80) - the only Sivert at Alset (canvas 118). See Siur (Sivert) Olsen Alset. ¶7

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed (as the father of Siur Olsen Alset). The probate after his widow Ingri Svendsdatter (Skifteprotokoll 7, 24758, fol. 477b, Alset, 12 Jan 1777) names her eldest son 'Siver Olsen gift og boende her paa gaarden Aleseth', and the 1762 extra tax names Ole Siursen and Ingri Svendsdatter as the couple at Alset 732 with Siur among their sons. His own probate (Skifteprotokoll 6, 24756, fol. 92, Alset 1768, per the typed register) has not yet been read. ¶11

Corrections 2

Correction: name changed from 'Ole Alset' to 'Ole Sivertsen Alset' by the baptism of his son Povel on Rogate Sunday, 26 May 1737, 'Ole Siverthsen Alsets Søn Povel' (16310 canvas 144 left); the identity of this Ole Sivertsen with the Ole Alset of 1730-1734 is Probable, for the reasons below. ¶3

Correction: birth set to before 28 Oct 1703. ¶8b

Open questions and the next record to read 2

What would confirm it: the burial of the 1730 Sivert (16310 canvases 120 right - 125 left), Siur's confirmation about 1745-48, or his marriage before 1763. An Ole Olsen Alset buried in 1781 aged 41 may be a younger son - inference only. ¶2b

What would confirm it: Ole's own burial with his age or patronymic, or a probate after him naming his children. ¶5b
