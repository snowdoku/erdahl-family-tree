Life story

Stepney register checked further (FreeREG St Dunstan detail pages read). (1) No more children of Thomas Jolls are baptised after William (12 Sep 1651): the Jolls-type baptisms of 1652-1675 belong to other mariner households, namely Robert Jolls of Wapping and Sarah (Robert, 13 Dec 1652); Valentyne Jyles of Old Gravel Lane and Judith (Valentyne, 30 Jul 1657; a Valentine Jowles of Stepney died beyond the seas by 1667, TNA PROB 4/20522); Richard Jewleis of Ratcliff and Mary (Mary, 9 Apr 1658); Richard Jolls (m. Sara Steevens, 5 Aug 1662) of Nightingale Lane and later Limehouse (Frances, 31 Aug 1664; John, 11 Aug 1668); John Jewls, painter-stainer of Ratcliff, and Anne (Joseph, 4 Sep 1666); and Steven Jowles of Love Lane and Hanna (Robert, born 13 Aug 1671, and Sarah, both baptised 16 Dec 1673). (2) FreeREG has no Thomas Jolls marriage in Middlesex or London 1660-1690 (fuzzy), and no Abigail Jolls marriage anywhere 1655-1690, so the Boston captain's first marriage is not found in London. (3) FreeREG holds no Stepney burials for these years, so it cannot show whether Thomas, baptised 17 Jun 1642, died young. Rival family: Robert Jolls, mariner of Wapping (m. 6 Apr 1640; will PROB 11/373/435, 1683), used the names Sarah and Robert, as the Boston captain did. His first recorded child is Steven (1644), so a son Thomas born 1640-43 and baptised elsewhere cannot be ruled out. Stays Speculative. ¶4

Both Stepney Jolls wills read on the images. Neither names a Thomas or anyone in New England. Grade unchanged (Speculative, on his link to Thomas Jolls). (1) PROB 11/373/435 is the will of the YOUNGER Robert Jolles, 'of the parish of Stepney... marryner' (27 Jul 1683, proved 2 Aug 1683). He leaves 'To my loving Brother Stephen Jolles' 10 li. and the residue to 'my deare Mother Sarah Davies of Stepney aforesaid widdow', his executrix. Correction to the note above: this is not the will of Robert Jolls who married Sarah Helligan in 1640. It is the will of their son Robert (baptised 1652), whose father was dead by 1683; his widow Sarah had remarried a Davies. (2) PROB 11/387/40, Elizabeth Jolls 'of Wapping in the parish of Whitechapell... widdow the Relict of Robert Jolls late of the same place deceased' (4 Feb 1685/6, proved 11 Apr 1687). She names her sisters Martha Barnes, widow, and Mary Tipp; 'my Brother Mathew Jolls'; 'Sarah Jolls daughter of my Brother John Jolls'; 'my Cozen ffrances Jolls daughter of Richard Jolls deceased', 'John Jolls her Brother' and 'Sarah Jolls his sister' (Richard Jolls's children of 1664 and 1668 in the Stepney register); and her husband's freehold and copyhold 'in the parish Towne and ffields of Ringsted in the County of Norfolke', which she leaves to 'my Cozen Robert Borton of Ringsted'. New lead: this third Robert Jolls (brothers Mathew and John) came from Ringstead, Norfolk, the only other place where FreeREG has Thomas Jolls baptisms in 1620-55 (two, 19 Sep and 13 Dec 1635). ¶5a

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Speculative candidate for the father of Thomas Jolls (Capt. Thomas Jolls, ship-master of Boston, d. 1686); graded on that link. FreeREG's transcript of the Stepney (St Dunstan) register has Thomas Jolls x Sarah Perkins, married 9 Sep 1641, and the baptisms of their children, the father each time 'Maryner' of Ratcliff: Thomas, 17 Jun 1642 ('6 days old'); Martha, 24 Dec 1644 (mother 'Sara'); John, 7 Jan 1646/7 (abode Ratcliff Crosse); Sarah, 11 Oct 1649; William, 12 Sep 1651. An earlier Susanna, 7 Apr 1639, daughter of Thomas Jowles, maryner of Ratcliff, and Alice, may be his child by a first wife. ¶1

Why this family: Capt. Thomas Jolls of Boston married first about 1671 (children from 1672, so born about 1640-50), was a ship-master who commanded the Richard from London and carried passengers from England, and named his children Thomas, Jonathan, Sarah, Robert and John; Thomas, John and Sarah repeat the Ratcliff family's names, and his only daughter Sarah would be named for this Sarah. Jolls is a rare name: FreeREG has no other Thomas Jolls baptised 1620-1655 except two at Ringstead, Norfolk (19 Sep and 13 Dec 1635). Rival in the same parish: Robert Jolls, mariner of Wapping/Blackwall (m. Sarah Helligan 6 Apr 1640; children Steven 1644, Sarah 1650, Robert 1652; will PROB 11/373/435, 1683) had no son Thomas in the register. No record yet ties the Boston captain to Stepney, so Speculative. ¶2

Open questions and the next record to read 2

Test record: an English record that names a son Thomas 'in New England' or 'of Boston' - e.g. the PCC will of Elizabeth Jolls, widow, of Whitechapel, 11 Apr 1687 (TNA PROB 11/387/40), or any Stepney administration of Thomas Jolls, mariner of Ratcliff - or a Boston record giving Capt. Thomas Jolls's age or birthplace (his 1686 inventory, Suffolk Probate 9:265, unread). ¶3

Test record: the Ringstead (Norfolk) registers and manor court rolls (Norfolk Record Office) for the Jolls family, and any Ringstead will naming a Thomas 'beyond the seas' or 'in New England'. ¶5b
