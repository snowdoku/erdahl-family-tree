About the family

Parents: Ole of Nord Øye (d. 1756-62) and Susanna Jacobsdatter (d. before 16 Jul 1771), both Probable. Sources: probates on Nord Øye in Ryfylke skifteprotokoll D 6 (DA 23890): Kari and Siri Olsdatter, 10 Jan 1771 (fol. 350a-b; heirs include a full brother read as [Niels] Olsen and their mother 'Sussanne Jacobsdatter gift med Søren Øye'), and Susanne Jacobsdatter, 16 Jul 1771 (fol. 352b; heirs: husband Søren Sørensen, first-marriage children [Niels] Olsen (of age) and two Olsdatters aged 18 and 14, second-marriage children Søren 5 and Berthe 7). His sisters Kari and Siri Olsdatter (in the 1762 household) died unmarried before Jan 1771. ¶14

Life story

Hjelmeland marriages 1761-62 (ministerialbok 1760-99, DA 1599, pp.226-227) read: no marriage of Søren Sørensen and Susanna Jacobsdatter. They were married by the 1762 ekstraskatt, so it is earlier; 1599 is the earliest Hjelmeland church book found on Digitalarkivet. ¶4

Confirmation (index): 'Niels Olsen', of Øye, confirmed in Hjelmeland church on 1 Nov 1761 (Hjelmeland ministerialbok 1760-1799, p. 169, no. 1); no age is given. This fits birth about 1744. The Hjelmeland confirmation lists give name, farm and sometimes age only, never parents, so this cannot name his father. ¶8

1762 ekstraskatt, No 33 N. Øye, Hjelmeland: head (opsidder) Søren Sørensen with wife Susanna Jacobsdatter; Niels Olsen is 'Stif Søn' (stepson); also Siri and Kari Olsdatter (døttre) and the maid Marthe Olsdatter. Ane Osmundsdatter is the wife of the cottager Michel Jensen, not Niels's mother. Mother probably Susanna Jacobsdatter, father an Ole (not named); not added as persons (relationship to Susanna not stated explicitly). Read. ¶3

Marriage: 1775 in Hjelmeland church, to Mallene Pedersdatter of Tuntland (see above). On 26 Feb 1775 the same register married 'Søren Sørensen Øye og Susanna Ols Datter ibid.' (no. 3); how this couple relates to his stepfather Søren Sørensen and mother Susanna Jacobsdatter of 1762 is not established. ¶6

Deeds on Øye checked: the Ryfylke mortgage register II A 2 (DA 23069) lists for Øye a deed at the Hjelmeland ting of 12 Oct 1790 (no. 139, pantebok fol. 60) and a probate deed at the ting of 6 Jun 1792 (no. 346, pantebok fol. 173). In mortgage book C 2 (DA 23021), fol. 60 is a deed between Daniel [?] Knudsen Øye and Svend Svendsen for Øye, matr. no. 45; fol. 173 is a skiftebrev after Svend Svendsen [?] of Øye, dated 12 Jun 1791, sharing land in Øye between the widow Kari Johnsdatter, the son Svend Svendsen and the daughter Lisbeth Svendsdatter. Neither concerns Niels Olsen. ¶9

1801 census: Niels Olsen, 57, householder (huusbonde) and farmer with land at Øye, Hjelmeland, with his wife Malene Pedersdatter (49) and children Niels 23, Martha 20, Siri 18, Peder 12 and Malene 10. ¶1

Alive in 1807: 'Niels Olsen Øye' was one of the two sworn appraisers at the registration on Tuntland on 19 Mar 1807 after Erik Pedersen (Ryfylke D 16, fol. 65b-66b, No. 5; Erik's widow Marta Osmundsdatter, four young daughters), and 'Niels Nielsen Øye' was guardian of the eldest daughter. Search note: no probate after Niels Olsen or Malene Pedersdatter of Øye is listed in the farm register to D 15/D 16 (1798-1810; Øye has only Thore Christophersen) or in the name registers of D 17a/17b (1810-c.1816) and D 18 (1817-1822), letters M, N and P read. Test record still open: their probate after 1822 (registers to D 19, 1822-27, and D 20a, 1827-31, are scanned at the front of DA 23902 and 23903). ¶15

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 6

Linked as father of Malene Nielsdatter (wife of Niels Thorsen) as Speculative only: same name and age as the 1801 daughter, and the 1816 bride lived at Øye; no record names him as her father. Her Hjelmeland baptism (about 1790-91) would settle it. ¶2

Upgrade to Probable: three records now tie him to the 1816 bride. (1) Hjelmeland marriages 1775, p. 240, no. 6 (DA 1599, IIIF canvas 138, scan read): 'Niels Olsen Øye og Mallene Peders Datter Tuntland', married in Hjelmeland church on the 1st Sunday after Trinity 1775. (2) Hjelmeland baptisms 1791, p. 114, no. 8 (canvas 61, scan read): 'Mallene Niels D. Øye', baptised 23 Jan 1791. (3) The 1801 census: he was the only Niels at Øye, with a daughter Malene aged 10. So Malene Nielsdatter of Øye, the bride of 1816, is his daughter. That she is the Madla widow Malene Nielsdatter is Probable, which caps this link at Probable. ¶5

Re-proof attempt (for), no change: still Probable, graded on his link to Malene Nielsdatter. New: the 1801 census index gives the 10-year-old 'Malene Nielsdatter' at Hielmeland: Øye the family position 'Deres børn' (their children) in his household, so a primary record names her as the child of Niels Olsen and Malene Pedersdatter, and the 1791 baptism ('Mallene Niels D. Øye') agrees. Rival check: the Hjelmeland church-book index 1803-1817 has only three Malene Nielsdatters: an infant at Torgen (b. 1805/06, buried 1807), a married mother at Houschevaag (1809, 1813), and 'Malene Nielsd., P[ige], Øje', bride of Niels Thorsen Buchen in Fister church 17 Nov 1816 (pp. 256-257 no. 26). The marriage scan was read: sureties 'Lars og Niels Buken', no father named and no age. The step from the 1801 child to the 1816 bride stays on the same farm with no rival, but spans 15 years with no record between (her confirmation, about 1805-07, is not in the Hjelmeland index), so under the grading rule it stays Probable. ¶11a

Upgrade: upgraded to Confirmed by the coordinator (1 Nov 2026), graded on the link to Malene Nielsdatter, applying the audit-3 threshold (about 15 years or less on the same farm, no named rival). The 1801 census for Øye lists Malene, 10, as 'Deres børn' (their child) of this couple. The 1816 marriage has the bride Malene Nielsdatter 'of Øye', and no other Malene Nielsdatter at Øye appears in the index. That is 15 years on one farm with no rival. Whether this Malene is the later Madla widow is graded on her own record, not here. ¶12

Audit 4 (1 Nov 2026): the coordinator's raise to Confirmed is reversed; Probable, graded on his link to his daughter Malene Nielsdatter. The coordinator held that whether the 1816 bride 'of Øye' is the later Madla widow 'is graded on her own record, not here'. That misreads the grading rule: a person's own grade covers her link DOWN to her child (here Malene's link to Peter S. Nelson, now Confirmed by the 1878 Winnebago marriage, which names the Madla couple 'Nils Sunde' and 'Maline Nilsdatter'). Her link UP to her parents is graded on the parents' records, so it is graded here. The tree's Malene is the Madla widow (Nordre Sunde, Håland, d. 1868). The chain is: 1801 census, Øye, Malene 10, 'Deres børn' (names the relationship) -> 1816 Fister marriage of Malene Nielsdatter of Øye to Niels Thorsen Buchen (15 years, same farm, no rival: this step alone would pass) -> the Madla couple Niels Thorsen and Malene Nielsdatter from 1821. The last step crosses parishes (Hjelmeland to Håland) and rests on the couple's matching names, the 1816 Hjelmeland baptism of Anna to Niels Thorsen and Malene 'Buchen', and the 1865 census birthplace Hjelmeland. The 1822 Håland in-migration list gives the couple's origin as Høyland, not Hjelmeland. No record names the Madla Malene as Niels Olsen's daughter, so the identification spans places and is Probable (audit 3 gave the same verdict: 'capped by the daughter's Probable identity'). ¶13a

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed, graded on his link to his daughter Malene Nielsdatter. Ryfylke skifteprotokoll D 19 (DA 23902), fol. 293a-b, No. 7 (scan): 'Skifteforretning efter de afgangne Selvejer Niels Olsøn og Hustrue Mallene Pedersdatter af Gaarden Øye i Hielmelands Skibrede', begun 12 Oct 1824 and closed 27 May 1825. The heirs are 'Boets Arvinger, nemlig': 1. the eldest son Niels Nielsen Øye, farmer and freeholder; 2.-3. the sons of a dead son Peder Nielsen (by his marriage with [?Gjertrud] Pedersdatter of Næs under [?Sørbø] in Årdal skipreide): Niels Pedersen 6 and Peder Pedersen 4; 4. the eldest daughter Susanna Nielsdatter, married to the cottager John Andersen, Houen under Gehland, Hjelmeland; 5. the second daughter Siri Nielsdatter, married to the farmer Niels Nielsen Øvrebøe, Årdal; 6. 'Tredie Datter Mallene Nielsdatter, gift med Niels Thorsøn Bukken, boende paa en Plads under Gaarden S[u/a]nde i H[?] Sogn under Jæderen og Dalernes Skriveri'; 7. the fourth and youngest daughter Martha Nielsdatter, married to Michel Larsen, Haneraand, Hausken skipreide. (The farm name reads 'Sande' or 'Sunde' and the parish name is cramped; 'under Jæderen og Dalernes Skriveri', the husband's name and Malene's name are clear.) Continuity to the tree's Malene Nielsdatter (see her page): she is the Malene, 10, 'Deres børn' of this couple at Øye in 1801; she married Niels Thorsen of Buchen in Fister church on 17 Nov 1816 as an unmarried woman of Øye; she and Niels Thorsen entered Håland parish (Jæren og Dalane sorenskriveri) in 1822, and their children were baptised from 1821 at Nordre Sunde, Madla, where she died a widow in 1868. The 1824-25 probate places her, by name and with her husband Niels Thorsen, on a cottar's place under the farm Sunde in Jæren og Dalane, two to three years after the move, exactly where the tree has her. No other Malene Nielsdatter married to a Niels Thorsen is known in Jæren; the 1801 sibling list (Niels, Martha, Siri, Peder, Malene) matches the probate heirs (Niels, Peder, Siri, Malene, Martha, plus Susanna). This answers the audit-4 objection: the probate itself ties the Øye daughter to the Madla wife of Niels Thorsen, so the identification no longer rests on the 1816 bride's residence alone. ¶16

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Burial candidate, not adopted (dates sweep 2): 'Niels Olsen', aged 80 (b. about 1745), died 1 Jan and was buried 7 Jan 1825 at Hjelmeland (Hjelmeland ministerialbok 1816-1841, p. 98, no. 1; index pg00000006650153). Age and parish fit (57 in 1801), but the index gives no residence, and a death on 1 Jan 1825 conflicts with the death BEF 12 OCT 1824 now in the tree; check what the 12 Oct 1824 date rests on (it may be his wife's probate only) and read the scan for the farm (Øye?) before adopting it. ¶17

Searches that found nothing 2

Probate search for his father Ole: the typed register to the Ryfylke designations 1731-1754 (DA 23971, Ø section, urn sk20081110670134-135) has Øye entries in Hjelmeland for 1742 (Susanne Jonsdatter and Siri Olsdatter, Øie søre, fol. 119b; Niels Omundsen and Marite Olsdatter, and Siri Jørgensdatter, Øie nord) and 1746 (Jørgen Samsonsen, Øye søre, fol. 181b), but no Ole. The register to the designations 1757-1766 (DA 23973, urn sk20081110670192-193) has Aad Jørgensen and Samson Jørgensen of Øye (16 Apr 1757, fol. 2) and no Ole either. So his father's probate, if one was held, falls in 1754-1757 (no register found) or was never held. His mother Susanna Jacobsdatter had remarried Søren Sørensen by 1762. ¶7
