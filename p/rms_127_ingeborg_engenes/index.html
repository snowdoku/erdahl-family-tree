Life story

NEW, Speculative, graded on her link to Ziri (Sigrid) Olsdatter (see her page). Åmli ætt og heim II, gard 16 (Øy), canvas 59: Olav Knutson's second wife 'Ingeborg [?] Engenes. Skifte 1707', with Sigrid listed first among 'Born' under that marriage (then Liv, Gunvor b. 1665, Ingeborg b. 1669). Olav had children by an unnamed first wife too, and Sigrid, who married Vraal about 1677, may have been born before Gunvor (1665); the book's grouping is all that puts her under Ingeborg, so Speculative. She sat with the farm until 1700. Bygdebok only (compiled secondary source, text rebuilt from word boxes, dates ABT), so Probable at most. ¶1a
