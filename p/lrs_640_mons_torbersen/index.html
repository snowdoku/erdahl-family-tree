About the family

Wife: the widow was Synneve Andersdatter, but the heading calls the heirs 'den Sal. Mands Børn' (the deceased man's children), not 'deris Børn', and the youngest daughter Else was a minor in 1745 while the eldest children were married, so Synneve may be a second wife. She is therefore not linked as Britte's mother. ¶3

First wife: Else Hermundsdatter, d. before 5 Apr 1740; her probate at Østerbø (Ytre Sogn 6a No. 5, fol. 6a) divides between 'hendis efterlevende Egtemand Mons Torbersen' and their children Niels, Ingeri (m. Hans Gramme[?]), Dordej (dead; son Hans Johansen), Giertrud (m. Ole Nielsen) and Brite (m. Torchel Indre Oppedal). Synneve Andersdatter, his widow in 1745, was a second wife married after Apr 1740; the minor daughter Else of 1745 was hers. The 1745 probate's 'Giertrud gift med Hans Nesse' vs 1740 'Giertrud gift med Ole Nielsen' suggests Giertrud remarried in between (inference; the 1740 farm word not read). ¶5

Life story

Probate No. 9, Ytre Sogn 6b fol. 433a (right page of view 81): 'Ao 1745 den 10de May... udi Ladvigs Skibr. paa Østerbøe skifted efter Mons Torbersen, imellem Enken Synneve Anders Datter og den S[al.] Mands Børn, som ere: 1 Søn Niels, myndig; 1 Datter Ingeri[?] gift med Hans Gramer[?]; 2den Datter Giertrud gift med Hans Nesse; 3die Datter Brite gift med Torchel Iversen Indre Oppedal; 4de Datter Dorte død og efterlatt sig 1 Søn Hans Johansen 8 aar gl.; og 5te Datter Else 2[?] aar gamle; alle Mons Torbersens Søn og Døttre'. Guardians for the two minors: Johannes Krogen and Ole Raae[?]. Gross estate 427 rd 3 ort 12 s (movables 287-5-10, land 139-4-2), net 283-4-4: the widow's half 141 rd, a brother's lot 40 rd 3 ort, a sister's lot 20 rd 1 ort. Read from the scan (crops). Index row 'Mons Torbersen, Østrebø, 1743-1750' (pa00000000521553), noted but unread. ¶1

Origin (Probable, bygdebok): the Ættebok for Lavik (1983), Østerbø bruk 1 (canvas 295), calls him 'Mons [..] Grøneng' (patronymic not recovered from the word boxes), b. 1671, d. 1745, who bought part of Østerbø; married (1) 1705 Elsa Hermundsdtr. and (2) 1742 Synneva Andersdtr. (1719-1759), by whom he had Johannes 1742, Ole 1743 and Else 1744. This agrees with the 1745 probate (widow Synneve Andersdatter, minor daughter Else) and supports the identification, noted earlier, with the 'Mons Torbergsen', 28 (b. about 1673), servant on Tyom og Grøneng in the 1701 census (pc00000000627632). That Grøneng is in Tjugum skipreide (Balestrand): the 1729 probate after Giertrud Monsdatter of Grøneng (Ytre Sogn 3b fol. 351a, view 64) is headed 'paa gaarden Grøneng udi Tiugums Skibrede' and concerns a different family (children Mons, Erik and Lisbet, minors). A husmann Nils Torbensen under Grøneng died in 1745 (probate No. 34, 16 Jun 1745, 6b fol. 462b, view 111: wife Anna Nilsdatter, children Anders (dead), Johannes, Kari, Brite); he may be a brother of Mons (same patronymic and farm; inference only, the probate names no siblings). Mons's parents are not found; the next record would be the 1664 manntall or 1660s-80s probates for a Torber/Torbjørn on Grøneng, Tjugum skipreide. ¶6

(1) The 1664 manntall for Tjugum sogn (35573 viewer 115, s.211) lists Grøneng with farmer Arne, sons 19 Østen and 28 Lasse, and husmann 46 Niels Jacobsen: no Torber/Torbjørn on Grøneng in 1664. On the next farm, 'Gierde' (Gjerde; farmer Michel), the husmenn are 42 Tollach[?] Schreenes, 60 Peder ibm, 51 Arne and 45 'Thorber [Fol?]sbrug'. A Torber aged 45 (b. about 1619) living as a husmann next to Grøneng in 1664 is a possible father for Mons (b. about 1671/73) and for Nils Torbensen, husmann under Grøneng (d. 1745); this is a candidate only, not added. (2) The probate after Anfind Hermundsen of Skrenes, 9 Oct 1715 (2b fol. 466a, view 151), names as appraisers 'Mons Grøneng og Endre Giære', so a Mons was a householder on Grøneng in 1715; it may be Mons Torbersen before he bought into Østerbø (the Ættebok calls him 'Mons [..] Grøneng'), but Mons Andersen (8 in 1701, son of Anders Monsen on Tyom og Grøneng) is a rival reading. ¶7

Burial (read from the scan): Gulen ministerialbok 11441, canvas 35 left page, 'd. 13 Martij tienisten i Lovig Com: 17. grafv[fest] Mand Mons Torbers[en] Østerbøe gl. 74 aar' (buried at the Lavik service of 13 Mar 1745, a married man, aged 74, so born about 1670-71). The same entry buries 'Mons Niels[en] Østerbø gl. 8 dag[e]' (an infant grandson, son of Nils Monsen). This narrows his death from before 10 May 1745 (probate) to before 13 Mar 1745, and the age agrees with the Ættebok's birth year 1671 and roughly with 'Mons Torbergsen', 28, servant on Tyom og Grøneng in 1701 (b. about 1673). ¶8

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Graded on his link to his daughter Britte Monsdatter: the probate names '3die Datter Brite gift med Torchel Iversen Indre Oppedal' as his child and heir. Continuity: Britte was betrothed at Lavik church on 15 Jun 1737 as 'Birthe Monsd: Østerbøe' to 'Torchil Iversen Indre opdal' (married 3 Jul 1737), so she came from this farm; this 1745 probate on Østerbø names her as Mons Torbersen's daughter married to Torkel Iversen of Indre Oppedal; Torkel Iversen's own probate of 7 Jun 1775 names Britte Monsdatter as his widow. Same couple by full name and farm, 8 years from the betrothal; rival: none (no other Brite Monsdatter married to a Torkel Iversen of Indre Oppedal is known). ¶2

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Possible identity, not linked: a 'Mons Torbergsen', 28 (b. about 1673), was a servant (tjener) on Tyom og Grøneng in the 1701 census of Ytre Sogn (pc00000000627632). Same uncommon patronymic and district; not tested. ¶4
