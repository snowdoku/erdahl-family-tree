About the family

Wife of Erik Amundsen, Randvoldjord. Age 54 in 1801. ¶1

Parents: father Peder Knudsen (Probable) because she is listed as 'Pedersdatter' in the children column of his household on her own later farm. Mother: Ranved Holgersdatter is Speculative only. She was 60 in 1758 (born about 1698), so about 47 at Olene's birth; Rasmus Rasmussen (20) and Engebor Rasmusdatter (39) in the same column are evidently her children by an earlier husband named Rasmus, so the household is a second marriage, and Olene may be Peder's child by Ranved or by an earlier wife. In favour of Ranved: Olene named her own daughter Ranveig ('Randvej Karine', 1801 census), which matches the name Ranved. ¶3a

Mother still unproven: the designation row read names only the father as deceased and does not name her mother. ¶5

Life story

1758 list: the 1758 soul register of Skudenes parish (a household list giving every person's name and age in columns for householders, children and servants) has, on the farm 'Hejen eller Ranvoldsjord' (Heien or Randvoldjord), the household of Peder Knudsen, 58, and Ranved Holgersdatter, 60. In the children column stand 'Olene Pedersdatt.' 13, Rasmus Rasmussen 20 and Engebor Rasmusdatter 39; in the servants column Engebor Tormodsdatter 40 and Gunele Hansdatter 33. This is the same farm where Olene Pedersdatter, 54, lived with her husband Erik Amundsen in 1801, and her age (13 in 1758, so born about 1744-45) agrees within two years with her 1801 age of 54. Read from the scan (source 4501, view 7, p. 13). ¶2

1762: 'Olene Pedersd.' is listed at Randvoldjord in the 1762 ekstraskatt (source 49646, view 607) together with 'Peder Knudsen' and 'Randveg Holgersd.' as one group in the column for people living on their own means. No relationship words; this supports, but does not prove, Ranveig Holgersdatter as her mother. ¶6

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: probate designation 18 April 1764 (Ryfylke designation register 1757-1766, source 23888, view 88, Stangeland skipreide): after Peder Knudsen of Ranvoldiord (Randvoldjord), the sole heir is 'Olene Pedersdatter' 17, guardian Sivert Pedersen Ranvoldjord. This proves her father as Peder Knudsen from a primary record. Her age (17 in April 1764, so born about 1746-47) agrees with the 1758 list (13) and with the 1801 census (54 at Randvoldjord, wife of Erik Amundsen), on the same farm. Read. ¶4
