Life story

Marriage found: betrothal at Tjugum 11 Apr 1757 (Leikanger 11566 c8): 'Anfind Olsøn Bahle & Ingeborg Røe', sureties Jon and Haavard(?) Bahle. In Aug 1758 'Anfind Olsøn Bahle' was a godfather to Martha Olsdatter Bahle (c22), and in Aug 1757 Ole Anfindsøn Bahle stood surety at a betrothal (c11). This places the man who married Ingeborg at Bale in 1757-58, the farm of Ole Anfindsen, whose 1764 probate names his son Anfind Olsen, married. It supports the identification with Ole Anfindsen's son, but Ingeborg's patronymic is not written in 1757, and the 1781 Kalvetræet couple are followed back to it by the pair Anfind + Ingeborg across a change of farm and 24 years, so the link to Ole and Maritte stays Probable. Grade unchanged. ¶7

Fit and cautions: Christi's 1780 confirmation (aged 18, at 'Tve[dt]', 11567 c234) and her census ages put her birth about 1762-63, inside the 1757 marriage. Earlier workers read every Anfind probate in the Ytre Sogn register 1762-1799 without finding her; that is consistent, since Arnfinn died in 1800 and his wife's probate (c.1781) would be indexed under her name. The farm word for his holding after 1757 is not recovered, and the book gives only two children; Christi's own son Anfind (b. c.1794) carries his name. ¶2

His own probate (bygdebok: d. 1800) is not in the Ytre Sogn index under Kalvetreet, Tjugum or Bale (searched by domicile 1776-1810); Nils Arnfinnson took over after him. Parents added (Probable): Ole Anfindsen of Bale and his first wife Maritte Olsdatter, from Ole's probate of Oct 1764 (Ytre Sogn 8a fol. 211b, '1 Søn ved Navn Anfind Olsen myndig'). ¶6

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Graded Probable on his link to his daughter Christi Anfindsdatter (see her page). Balestrand 1: Ættesoge (1985), Tjugum section, canvas 132: '[...] kom Arnfinn Olson Bale [...] 1757. [Han] var fødd [...] 1724 [og] vart [i] 1757 gift [med] Ingeborg Nilsdtr. Arnfinn døydde [i] 1800, kona var [...] 1781. Dei hadde [...] 2 born: Kristi som vart gift [med] Gjest Gjestson Ese, [og] Nils som [...] etter faren. Nils Arnfinnson var fødd 1766 [og] gifte [seg] 1795 [med] Sigrid Johannesdtr. Dei hadde 4 born [...]'. The same book (canvas 102, Indre Ese) has the husmann Gjest Gjestson of 1801, born 1761, who 'vart [i] 1790 gift med Kristi [...]' - the tree's Gjest Gjestsen (see his page), married 11 Oct 1790 to 'Christie Anfinds Datter Tvedt'. So the book makes Christi the daughter of Arnfinn Olsen and Ingeborg Nilsdatter. ¶1

Upgrade: graded on his link to his daughter Christi Anfindsdatter (see her page), Probable -> Confirmed. The probate after his wife Ingeborg Nielsdatter (Ytre Sogn 9b p. 1479-1480, 18 Oct 1781, 24167 view 739) is held between 'hendes efterlevende Enkemand Anfind Olsen' and their two children begotten in the marriage, 'Sønnen Niels Anfindsen 15 aar' and 'Datteren Christie Anfindsdatter 19 aar gammel', at the cottar's place Kalvetræet under Tjugum. So in 1781 he was a husmann at Kalvetræet (Treet) under Tjugum; the bygdebok's 'kom Arnfinn Olson Bale [til...] 1757' refers to his coming to this place from Bale. Continuity (how the 1781 girl is followed to the tree's Christi): (1) Oct 1780, Tjugum confirmation list: 'Christi Anfindsdr: Troet 18 aar' (11567 c234). The farm word has a clear 'r' after the T ('Troet'), i.e. Trøet/Træet, the clerk's short form of Kalvetræet; earlier passes read it 'Tvet'. (2) 18 Oct 1781, this probate: Christie Anfindsdatter, 19, at Kalvetræet under Tjugum, one year later, same place, age advancing by one. (3) 25 Sep / 11 Oct 1790: 'Pigen Christie Anfinds Datter Tr[ø]det' marries the soldier Giest Giestsen Esse (11567 c202-203), 9 years later, same place; the second letter of the farm word is formed like the 'r' of 'Trolovet' in the same entry, so it is Trødet = Treet, not Tvedt (reading, now corrected). One of the sureties is 'Niels... Tjugum', and Kalvetræet lay under Tjugum (the 1781 guardian was Niels Ellingsen Tjugum, and her brother was Niels Anfindsen). (4) Census ages of Gjest's wife: 38 in 1801 and 52 in 1815, b. 1762/63, matching 19 in Oct 1781. Gaps: 1 and 9 years, all at the same place. ¶5a

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

No rival: the other Christi Anfindsdatters of the district were the Feten girl (b. c.1762, confirmed 1777 at Feten, married Marcus Marcussen 1797), Anfind Samsonsen Grinde's Christi (b. c.1773) and Anfind Olsen Hov's Christi (b. c.1775); the Tveiten couple (Anfind Monsen and Kari Anfindsdatter) had no Christi (probates 1781 and 1813). The Balestrand bygdebok (1985, c132) independently makes Kristi, daughter of Arnfinn Olson and Ingeborg Nilsdtr., the wife of Gjest Gjestson Ese, and its Ese chapter (c102, phrase-checked) writes that Gjest Gjestson 'vart [i] 1790 gift med Kristi Arnfinnsdtr. [...] (Kalvetre)', naming the same place. ¶5b

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Test record: the probate after Arnfinn (Anfind) Olsen, d. c.1800 (Ytre Sogn sorenskriveri skifteprotokoll A 12/13, 1800-1805), or after his wife Ingeborg Nilsdatter, d. c.1781 (Ytre Sogn 9b/10a, 1779-1783), which would list 'Christi Anfindsdatter' among the children; a first-name probate-index search (Anf*, 1798-1808) shows no Ytre Sogn Anfind Olsen, so the protocol pages must be read.
