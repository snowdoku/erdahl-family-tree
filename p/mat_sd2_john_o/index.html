About the family

Oline Slind's great-great-grandfather on her mother's side, reached 12 Oct 2026, and the first of this line to be put on paper. He is named as the groom's father - 'John Olsen Stokke' - in the Selbu marriage register for 21 June 1831, page 317 serial 19, when his son Ole Johnsen Stokke married Marite Haldorsdatter of Svinaas, and he is found in his own household in the 1825 census of Selbu at rural residence 0147 Stokke, household 1: 'John Olsen, 67, Gmd.' - gaardmand, farmer - born about 1758. ¶1

Parents added from the bygdebok, Probable: Selbu i fortid og nåtid vol. 6, Midti-Stokkan (canvases 18-20) lists '3. Jon, f. 1759' among the children of Ola Jonsen (b. 1733, d. 1768) and Kari [Kristensdatter] from Flora (b. 1729, d. 1769), and says the widow Mali (b. 1757, d. 1807; = Magli Nielsdatter) married in 1794 'Jon Olsen Stokke, f. 1759, d. 1827, som var brorsønnen til [...] mannen hennes' - her late husband Jon Jonsen (b. 1726, d. 1792) was Ola Jonsen's brother. This resolves the 'father not chosen' problem above in favour of none of the seven 1757-59 candidates: no baptism of Jon to Ole Jonsen and Kari was found in 1757-59, so the record is missing or misread. Marriage to Mali: 1794 per the book (16311 marriages 1795-1801 were read without finding it; read 1794). ¶14a

Life story

Father not chosen. Seven baptisms of a Jon son of an Ole in Selbu/Tydal 1757-1759 (Selbu ministerialbok 16311): (1) 22 May 1757, Ole Jonsen Sandvig x Marit Jonsdatter - probably died, as the same parents baptised another Jon in Oct 1759; (2) 4 Sep 1757, Ole Jonsen Lillefjen x Berit Tostensdatter; (3) 14 Jan 1759, Ole Biornsen x Helge Olsdatter, no farm - fits the 1801 and burial ages if born late Dec 1758 or early Jan 1759, but not the 1825 age; (4) 11 Mar 1759, Ole Barosen Uthus x Siri Absolonsdatter; (5) 8 Apr 1759, Ole Persen x Ingebor Jonsdatter, no farm; (6) 6 Jul 1759, Ole Olsen Aas (Tydal) x Ragnhild Ingebrigtsdatter; (7) 14 Oct 1759, Ole Jonsen Sandvig x Marit Jonsdatter. A Palm Sunday 1757 Jon of Kolset is also on file. None is tied to Stokke by farm, godparents or naming, and none fits all three ages. A Thomes Olsen Uthus stood godfather to his son Ole in 1809, a weak pointer to candidate (4) that does not fit the ages. ¶13a

Probate after Ole Jonsen, Stokke, 25 Oct 1768 (24756 fol. 219b) names 'Sønnen Jon Olsen gl. 10 aar' with the widow Karen Christensdatter and siblings Christen 3, Mette 11, Berete 7: the bygdebok's family of Ola Jonsen and Kari from Flora, with a son Jon born about 1758, the year his own three ages agree on. The other Jon Olsen at Stokke (b. about 1746) was the son of Ole Olsen, Stokkan (probate 1774, 24758 fol. 247b). His parents stay Probable on the 33-year gap between 1768 and 1801. Bridging records to read: the 1794 marriage (16311), and his confirmation c. 1773-75 (16316; name, farm and age only). ¶15

Bridging attempts, none settles it. (1) Betrothal I Advent 1793 (16311 c159 R): 'ungkarl Jon Olsen Stokkan' and the widow Maglie Nielsdatter Mogaard, cautioners Ole Gundersen Kolsath and Anders Udhuus; marriage Palm Sunday, 13 Apr 1794 (16311 c163 R, read): 'Copuleret Ungkarl Jon Olsen Stokkan med Enken Maglie Nielsdatter Mogaard'. Neither names his father, and neither notes a royal licence (bevilling), though marrying a father's brother's widow needed one; a dispensation register might name the relationship. (2) Selbu confirmation lists: 1772 (c82) and 1773 (c94) have no Jon Olsen Stokkan; the 1775 list (16316 c118, 81 confirmands, read) has none either, but has 'Christopher Olsen Stokkan' - a son of Ole Christophersen Stokkan (child baptised 1772, 16316 c90), so a THIRD Ole had sons at Stokkan in the 1750s-70s; any Jon son of his is a possible rival not yet excluded. 16316 ends in April 1776 with no confirmation list; 1774's list was not seen (16316 c106-108) and 1776's would be in 16311 (about c37-40, unread). Kept Probable. ¶16

First marriage: by 1801 to Magli Nielsdatter (see her page), in his first and her third marriage. 1801 census, Stoche (Stokke) farm 0069, household 03, Selbu: 'Jon Olssen', 42, Husbonde, 'Bonde og gaard beboer' (farmer and farm occupier); wife Magli Nielsdatter 45; daughters Angneta Jonsdatter 10, Karen Jonsdatter 3 and Elen Jonsdatter 1; servants Ole Pederssen 16 and Ane Andersdatter 45; widow Kiersten Andersdatter 77. The marriage date is not found; it was before about 1790-91 if Angneta, 10 in 1801, is his. Magli died 6 Dec 1807 as 'Konen' at Stokke, aged 52. ¶8

Second marriage: 3 July 1808, Selbu, as 'Enkemand' (widower) Jon Olsen Stokke, to 'Pigen Dordie Jonsdatter Welve' (see her page). Selbu ministerialbok 1801-1815 p. 108 (scan read). ¶11

His household in the 1825 census was his wife 'Dordi Johnsd., 54' and two sons at home, 'Ole Johnsen, 16' (Oline's grandfather) and 'John Johnsen, 12', with a 'Marit Olsd., 49' of unstated relation. He was sixty-seven and his youngest son twelve, so he had married late or married twice. ¶2

His own patronymic makes his father an OLE, born about the 1720s or 1730s. That man is not entered here, because a patronymic is not a record. ¶3

Context, flagged as context: his son held Stokke as a 'Gaardbr. Selveier' - owner-occupier - in 1865, while the Slind family his granddaughter married into were leaseholders for four generations. Freehold peasant ownership was the normal condition in inland Trondelag by the mid-nineteenth century and leaseholding the exception, usually on land that had belonged to the church or the crown. Kari Olsdatter of Stokke married down in tenure when she married Gunder Olsen of Slind in 1871. ¶5

Death and burial: 'John Olsen Stokke' (index spelling 'Stokke' with o-umlaut; gender mis-keyed 'k'), aged 68, died 24 January and buried 4 February 1827, Selbu burial register 1824-1842, p. 365 no. 5. The parallel register 1816-1831 (pp. 274-275 no. 4) enters the same burial as 'Jon Olsoen', 68, died 29 January, buried 4 February 1827; the two death dates differ and the date is given as a range. Identification strong: the farm name Stokke is in the entry, the age 68 gives birth about 1759 against about 1758 from the 1825 census, and he was alive in November 1825. Rated as part of a Confirmed person; the residence and wife are not in the index, and the scan has not been read. ¶6

The case for this identification 1

Identification of the 1801 household: a second, older Jon Olssen (b. ~1743, wife Beret Estensdatter) headed household 01 on the same farm. This John is the younger man of household 03: his 1801 age 42 fits the 1825 census age 67 and the 1827 burial age 68, and his 1813 baptism entry calls him 'Jon Olsen yngre' (the younger). ¶9

Records that disagree 1

Birth estimate, kept at ABT 1758: the 1801 age 42 (1 Feb) gives birth Feb 1758 - Jan 1759; the 1825 age 67 (27 Nov) gives Nov 1757 - Nov 1758; the burial age 68 (24 or 29 Jan 1827) gives Jan 1758 - Jan 1759. All three overlap only in Feb-Nov 1758, so ABT 1758 stands (the index's calculated 1801 birth year 1759 is just 1801 minus 42). No Jon son of an Ole was baptised in Selbu or Tydal in 1758, so the baptism is either just outside the window, from another parish, or missed. ¶12

Corrections 2

Correction: death was carried as 'AFT 27 NOV 1825' (alive at the 1825 census); the burial register now gives the death as 24 or 29 January 1827. ¶7

Correction: an earlier note here says the 1801 census of Selbu has no residence called Stokke. It has one: the farm is transcribed 'Stoche' (farm 0069), where this John is household 03. ¶10

Searches that found nothing 2

A documented negative that will save the next researcher an afternoon: THE 1801 CENSUS OF SELBU HAS NO RESIDENCE CALLED STOKKE. A full dump of every indexed record whose domicile is Stokke in Selbu - 817 rows across twenty-one sources from 1801 to 1940 - contains not one row from the 1801 census, and direct searches on Stokke, Stokkan, Stokken, Stokkaas, Stokkaasen, Stokkenes, Stokkvold, Stokkoyen, Stocke, Stokke* and Stok* all return zero for 1801. The farm is in the 1801 enumeration under some other name; the way to find it is to read the 1801 census district listing against the 1825 one, where Stokke is residence 0147 and Slinden 0182. Until that is done, John Olsen's 1801 household is unlocated. ¶4

Search note: a royal marriage licence (kongelig bevilling) for Jon Olsen Stokkan's 1794 marriage to the widow Mali Nielsdatter Mogaard was looked for on Digitalarkivet (source searches for Stiftamtmannen i Trondheim and bevilling registers, 1790-96): no digitised Trondhjem stiftamt licence register or copybook for 1793-94 turned up. The licences were issued through Danske Kancelli (Norske Registre/Tegnelser, Rigsarkivet, Copenhagen), which is not online here. The 1794 entries (16311 c159 R, c163 R) note no licence. The 1776 confirmation list was not read, because Selbu confirmation lists (16311) give name, farm and age only, with no parents. ¶17
