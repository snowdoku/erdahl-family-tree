About the family

Mother of Anne (baptised 16 August 1761) and Ole (baptised 1 January 1767) by Peder Andersen Svee; the identification of that Ole with Ole Pedersen Saether (see his page), and so her place as his mother, is argued in Peder's notes and rated Probable. ¶1

Parents added from the bygdebok, Probable: Selbu i fortid og nåtid vol. 7, Kjøsnes (canvas 300) lists '3. Berit, f. 1735, [g.I m.] Per Anderssen [...] [g.II m....] Gunnarsen [Mølnhus, på] Sæter' among the children of Ola Olsen of Kjøsnes (b. 1702, d. 1774) and Anne Persdatter from Flora (b. 1706, d. 1779); vol. 6, Sveen under Sæter (canvas 394), calls Per Anderssen's wife 'Berit [...] Kjøsnes, [f.] 1735, [d.] 1805' and has her remarry in 1795 to [...] Gunnarsen Mølnhus. Her godparent at her daughter Anne's 1761 baptism, 'Ole Kiosnes', fits this origin. ¶6a

Her parents are now proved by a primary record: the probate after Ole Olsen, Kjøsnes, 27 Sep 1774 (24758 fol. 255) names among his heirs 'Datteren Berithe Ols Datter gift med Peder Andersen Sæter', with the widow Anne Peders Datter. Her siblings named there: Peder (married), Joen (married, at Wigen), Haldoe, Bersvend (25), the late Ole (children Ole and Anne) and the late Ingebor (daughter Helge Jonsdatter). This raises her father (Ola (Ole) Olsen Kjøsnes) to Confirmed; her own grade, on her link to her son Ole, is unchanged. ¶7

Life story

Remarriage: betrothed 27 Apr 1795 as 'Enken Berete Olsdatter Sæter' to the bachelor Arnt Gundersen Mølnhuus (16311 c177) - matches the 1801 census, where she is the wife of Arent Gundersen at Sæter, 'hendes 2det ægteskab'. This confirms her as Peder Andersen's widow. ¶4

Later life, Speculative identification: the 1801 census of Selbu lists at Sæter 'Beret Olsdatter', born 1735, a wife in her second marriage (her husband's first), with the cottar Peder Nielssen, born 1720, a widower, husmand uden jord (cottager without land) receiving poor relief, as the likely husband; the list does not group households, so the pairing is inferred from the status words. In 1805 'Berete Olsd.', born 1735, a husmannskone (cottager's wife), was buried from 'Saeter-Sveet'. Name, farm and age fit the widow of Peder Andersen (d. 1793) remarried; no record names her first husband. Birth about 1735 and death in 1805 come from these two records and stand only if the identification is right. Her age fits a mother of children in 1761-1767. Peder Nielssen's later marriage and the 1805 burial scan (age, husband) would test it. ¶2

1801 census (indexed), Sæter, Selbu: 'Beret Olsdatter', b. 1735, wife of Arent Gundersen (b. 1755), described as 'hans kone, 1. hendes 2det ægteskab' - his wife, his first and her second marriage. Probable: she remarried after Peder Andersen's death in 1793. ¶3a

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: named as 'den efterlevende Enke Berithe Olsdatter' in the probate after her husband Peder Andersen, cottager at Sveet under Sæter, registered 11 July 1793 (24763 p. 391). The heirs are the children of that marriage, including the deceased son [Ole] Pedersen, represented by his daughter 'Berith Olsd. 1 aar gl.' (Ole Pedersen Saether's daughter Berthe, b. 1792). This primary record ties her to Ole as his mother; Confirmed. ¶5
