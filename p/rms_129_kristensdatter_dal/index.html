About the family

Wife of Knud Syversen (Knut Sjugurdsson) Dal (see his page) and probably the mother of Joen Knudsen Dal (see his page); Speculative, graded on her link to Joen. Valdres bygdebok I F, gnr 107 Dal (canvas 494, left column foot): 'Knut Sjugurdsson Dal f om 1638, d etter 1693, g m... Kristensdotter frå... 109' (exact phrase "Kristensdotter frå" confirmed on canvas 494). Her forename was not recovered: single-word and exact-phrase tests of about 60 women's names ('X Kristensdotter') all failed for this canvas. The bygdebok does not say in the rebuilt text whether she was the mother of all Knut's children; her son Christen (m. 1706) carrying her father's name points that way. Speculative because the forename is missing and motherhood is inferred. Rebuilt from nb.no content-search word boxes; dates from the rebuild are given as ABT. ¶1a
