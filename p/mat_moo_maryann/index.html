About the family

Parents: Richard and Sarah Moore. Her adult baptism on 20 April 1877, in the Murray Harbour Bible Christian circuit register, gives her birth as 20 September 1845 and names her parents as Richard and Sarah (PEI baptisms, marriages and burials index, record 72100, image FS_005240355_00173). Rated Confirmed on that index entry; the register image itself has not been seen. Upgraded from Speculative: the earlier rating rested only on the 1861 census household. ¶1

Place in the family: she is the younger of the two daughters in Richard Moore's 1861 household. She was 15 and a half at the census, counted in the 16-21 column; her sister Grace was 23. ¶2

Her daughter Lauretta (Machon) Brooks (1882-1964) of Murray Harbour kept a diary from 1929 in which she listed local deaths, adding family tags to the Lot 64 Moores ('Ben's son', 'Will's son') and to Grace Moore's Hayter children ('Tom's son'). That is how a first cousin would write, which supports the identification. Her husband John Machon died at Murray Harbour on 15 July 1921, aged 86 and a half. ¶5

Life story

Marriage (Probable identification): seven months after her baptism, Mary Ann Moore married John Machon (indexed 'Marchon') on 1 December 1877 (PEI marriage registers index, image FS_004594920_00575). He was a widower about ten years older, with children from a first marriage. Adult baptism shortly before marriage was usual in this Bible Christian community. ¶3

1881 census, Lot 64: Mary Machon, 37, Bible Christian, wife of John Machon, 46, farmer. 1891 census: Mary, about 47, wife of John, with Peter, Rebecca and her own daughter Lauretta (b. 28 December 1882). ¶4
