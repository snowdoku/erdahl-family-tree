Life story

Name: the surname is very probably Reuter (Reitter), not Render. In the 1774 marriage image her father is 'Jacob Re?ters', and the letter shapes match 'Johann Conrad Re?ter' in entry 2 on the same page; the 1778 baptism (Reuter), the 1814 marriage (geb. Reitter) and the 1821 burial (Reitter) all read Reuter/Reitter. The 1731 Bietigheim marriage image of Conrad Jacob 'Re?ter' (son of Johann Conrad) x Maria Sophia, daughter of Johann Albrecht Haussmann, Bürger und Metzger (QPGG-23PM, indexed 'Render'), shows the same spelling, so the parents' family is the same whatever the reading; 'Render' is the FamilySearch indexer's reading of an n/u-ambiguous Kurrent 'Reuter/Renter'. Name field left as it stands; a renaming to Reuter is suggested for the linker. Image read signed in on FamilySearch, 2 Nov 2026. ¶5

Burial (Probable identification): 'Elisabetha Regina Reitter', buried at Bietigheim on 17 Apr 1821, relatives 'Jakob, Maria, Melchior' (record 7063442, image 1184901-01166). Württemberg burial entries of married women give the maiden name, the parents and the husband, so this reads as Elisabetha Regina, daughter of Jakob and Maria, wife of Melchior. She matches the 1774 bride 'Elisabetha Regina Renters', daughter of Jacob, wife of Johann Melchior Lindenmaier (907053963) in her own name, her father's and her husband's; 'Reitter' is taken as a misreading of Renter/Render. No other Elisabetha Regina with a husband Melchior is indexed at Bietigheim. Her own grade (on her son Johann Melchior) is unchanged. Ancestry 61023 index rows do not label roles (BRIEF audit 5 rule 3), so this rests at Probable at most until the image or a labelled FamilySearch twin is read; images not seen. ¶2

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 3

Probable mother of Johann Melchior Lindenmaier the younger (see his page). ¶1a

Graded on that link: inferred. She married Johann Melchior Lindenmaier (son of Nicolaus) at Bietigheim on 8 Feb 1774; her bride row reads 'Elisabetha Regina Renters', relatives 'Jacob, Johann Melchior' (record 907053963, image 1184901-00062). The younger Johann Melchior's own records name only his father; his mother is inferred from the 1813 Bietigheim marriage of a Johann Melchior, son of 'Johann Melchior, Elisabetha' (record 7054597), identified with him by elimination. Children indexed: Johann Jacob (buried 7 Sep 1777), Beata Friederica (bapt. 28 May 1782, buried 18 Dec 1782, record 7280048), Johann Friedrich (buried 18 Sep 1847, record 7233096). Surname 'Renters' is the genitive of Renter/Render; a Jacob Render buried a child, Philippina Friderica, at Bietigheim on 29 Jul 1754 (record 7058234). ¶1b

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed, graded on her link to her son Johann Melchior Lindenmaier the younger (see his page). Records: (1) Bietigheim marriage register 1814, entry 3 (image read): groom 'Johann Melchior Lindenmaier', Bürger und Schuster allhier, born 12 Sept 1778 zu Bietigheim, status 'Wit[wer] der Louise Catharina geb. Fink'; his parents 'Johann Melchior Lindenmaier, B[ürger] und Schuster allhier' and 'Elisabetha geb. Reitter(in)'; bride 'Rosina Catharina geb. Kienzlin', ledig, born 13 Nov 1778 zu Hofeneck, daughter of the late Johann Caspar Kienzlin and Johanna Maria geb. Luz; married 22 Feb 1814; dispensation of the banns dated 19 Febr. 1814. The entry stands under the year heading 1814 on the page headed 1813/1814. (2) Baptism V4L9-W4H (labelled): Johann Melchior Lindenmajer, born 12 Sep 1778, christened Bietigheim 13 Sep 1778, Mother 'Elisabetha Reuter'. The 1814 image ties the tree's man (widower of Louise Catharina Fink) to that birth date and to his mother Elisabetha née Reitter by name, so the elimination step noted is replaced by a direct record, and the 1813/1814 date conflict is resolved (1814). Continuity to her as the 1774 bride: the 1774 marriage image (entry 3, 8 Feb 1774) gives 'Elisabetha Regina, Jacob Reuters [or Renters] Bürger und Weingärtners allhier ehel. led. Tochter', bride of the shoemaker Johann Melchior Lindenmajer; four years later, same couple, same town, at the 1778 baptism; 'Elisabetha Regina' again at the 1782 baptism (7280048) and at her burial in 1821 as 'Elisabetha Regina Reitter', wife of Melchior. No rival wife of a Johann Melchior Lindenmaier at Bietigheim in 1774-1821. Previous Probable notes are superseded. Image read signed in on FamilySearch, 2 Nov 2026. ¶4

The case for this identification 1

Labelled evidence, grade unchanged (Probable): the younger Johann Melchior's baptism (V4L9-W4H, Bietigheim 13 Sep 1778) names Mother 'Elisabetha Reuter', and the remarriage record VCYQ-S6T names his mother 'Elisabetha Reitter'. Still Probable because the 1778 child is tied to the 1803 groom by elimination, and the remarriage date (22 Feb 1813 Lutheran index vs 1814 'Germany, Marriages') conflicts with the first wife's burial of 19 Sep 1813 (audit 5 rule 4). ¶3a
