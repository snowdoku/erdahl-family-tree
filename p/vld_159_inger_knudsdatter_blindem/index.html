About the family

Mother of Alv (Alf) Rasmussen of Blindem (see his page), Confirmed; graded on her link to Alv. In a wife's estate the heirs are her own children, and her probate names the son Alf Rasmussen. Continuity: she married Rasmus Alfss. of Blindem on 1 Jan 1710 (index, as 'Ingeborg Knudsd.'; his first marriage); Alf son of Rasmus Alfss. was baptised on 3 Apr 1712; she was churched at Borgund as Inger Knudsdtr. of Blindem on 29 Sep 1720 and 2 Feb 1727, after Rasmus Alfss.'s baptisms of 21 Aug 1720 and 22 Nov 1726; she died in 1738 still his wife. Rasmus had no other wife in 1710-1738, so there is no rival mother for the 1712 child. ¶2

Life story

Probate (scan read): 'Blindem udi Borgens Skibbreede. Anno 1738 den 13de May' a registration, valuation and division was held at Blindem after the late Inger Knudsdatter between her surviving husband ('hendes efterladte Mand') Rasmus Alfsen and their children. In the division the son's share is entered as 'Sønnen Alf Rasmussen' (first letters of 'Alf' blotted); the daughters include Marit Rasmusdatter and two daughters aged 16 and 14. Appraisers included Knud Pedersen Hasund. Sunnmøre skifteprotokoll 09B (3A 12) fol. 481b-482a, image 24553/216. The index (pa00000000780356) calls her 'Rasmus Alfsens hustru'. ¶1

Burial (index): Inger Knudsd. of Blindem buried at Borgund 27 Apr 1738, birth year calculated 1678 (pg00000002546164). She stood godmother as 'Inger Knudsdtr. Blindem' (carried the child) on 5 Nov 1713. Her own parents are not found; her patronymic shows her father was a Knud. ¶3
