Life story

Probably another daughter of Anders Larsen. She married Peder Olssen, a widower of Øver Hofstad, on 21 June 1802, when she is entered as a maid of Almoen. Whether she was a daughter of Anders Larsen or of one of the two cottager households on the farm cannot be settled from the entry itself, so the link is speculative; her mother, if she was Anders's daughter, would have been Ane Jonsdatter. ¶1

How sure are we?

Speculative. A plausible candidate only. Treat this link as a question, not a fact.
