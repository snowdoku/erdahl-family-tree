About the family

Father of Johann Christoph Schumayer (see his page). ¶1a

Family: a Jacob Christoph 'Schumaÿer' x Christina Catharina baptised Maria Judith on 5 Sep 1683 (record 5440638, image 1184615-00173) and Esther Regina on 2 Oct 1685 (record 5440685, image 1184615-00177). The index gives the place only as 'Kirchenbuch' (FamilySearch film 1184615, a parish of the Vaihingen/Besigheim area filmed next to Oberriexingen 1184613; parish not identified). He is the only Jacob Christoph of the surname in the index, so very probably this man. Johann Christoph's own baptism (about 1685-92) is not indexed on that film (Johann Christoph baptisms 1684-94 there checked: Looß, Mösch, Stumpp, Ziboll only). His wife is entered at Speculative (Christina Catharina). ¶2a

Life story

Son Johann Christoph baptised 18 Jul 1680, indexed 'Johann Christoph Sundermeyer', relatives 'Jacob Christoph, Christina Catharina' (record 5440561, image 1184615-00166). Daughter Maria Judith married on 8 Jun 1706 on the same film: bride row 'Maria Judith Schumajers', relatives 'Jacob Christoph, Conrad Christoph' (her father, then the groom; record 905439976, image 1184615-00098). So the family lived in the film-1184615 parish 1680-1706. His own marriage (about 1679) and baptism not yet found. ¶3

Possible marriage, Speculative: 'Herr Jacob Christoph Schwenger' married Christina Catharina, daughter of Johann Burkhard 'Luger', at Großsachsenheim on 3 Jun 1679 (records 6178196 / 906178196, image 1184619-00244); see Christina Catharina. If this is he, the title 'Herr' marks him as a man of some standing. ¶4a

Death: probably before 28 Apr 1696, when 'Christina Catharina Schumayers' (relatives 'Christoph, Herr Remmerdus', 905439934) remarried on film 1184615, apparently as his widow (see Christina Catharina). Not entered as a date. His burial is not found under Schumayer; a 'Christoph' with no surname was buried there 19 Aug 1691 (5441642), candidate only. ¶5

Continuity: Johann Christoph x Anna Sibylla are the parents of Susanna Margaretha (b. Vaihingen 1716, 4768896), whose 1737 marriage names her father Joh. Christoph (FamilySearch QPGK-7T8Y). Ancestry 61023 index rows do not label roles (BRIEF audit 5 rule 3), so this rests at Probable until the image or a labelled FamilySearch twin is read; images not seen. Surname as in the family's other rows (Schumayer/Schämayer); the 1713 row's 'Schumeze' is a misreading. ¶1b
