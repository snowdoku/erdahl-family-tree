About the family

Mother of Truls Larsen Aaning: Speculative. She is the only wife Aaland gives Lars Trulsen. Truls (born about 1715) would be her son by date, and Truls was a ward (with a court guardian) from 1724, which fits a widowed mother. ¶2a

Life story

Aaland 1932: Aaland's Aaning history gives the wife of Lars Trulsen (holder 1714, died 1723) as '(Synnøve Olsd., † 92 a. g. 1768)'; the brackets mean the name is Aaland's inference. She died in 1768 aged 92 (born about 1676) and as widow ran the whole farm in 1724-35, and half of it after 1736. ¶1

How sure are we?

Speculative. A plausible candidate only. Treat this link as a question, not a fact.

Missing: any record naming her as Truls's mother. Aaland gives no source. Her age at death puts her about 11 years older than Lars (born about 1687) and about 39 at Truls's birth; possible, but it is another reason to treat Aaland's bracketed name as a guess. To settle: her 1768 burial (Innvik), the 1736-44 guardianship entries for Truls, or the probate of 23 October 1724 (probably Lars's), which would name the widow and children. ¶2b
