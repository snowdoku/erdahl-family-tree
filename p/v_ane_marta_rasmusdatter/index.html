About the family

Mother: Kari Rasmusdatter (new link, Confirmed by the 1798 probate below). The probate names the daughter outright; that this Anna Martha is the Anne Martha of this tree rests on the matching name and patronymic, the farm Blindem, the 1776 baptism to Rasmus Alvsen of Blindem, and her living at Blindem unmarried in 1803. The age 24 in 1798 implies birth about 1774, two years before the 1776 baptism (and the 1819 age of 40 implies about 1779); the ages are taken as rounded. Probate read on the scan (13 Oct 2026): 'Blindem i Borgens. Anno 1798 d. 8de Septbr. Skifte efter Kari Rasmusdatter til Deeling imellem Enkemanden Jon Olsen Blindem og den afdødes udi [hendes] forrige Ægteskab avlede [eneste?] Datter Anna Martha Rasmusdatter g[ammel]. 24 Aar med Formynder: Willum Olsen Blindem. Vurderingsmænd Thore Kolvigen og Siur Lille-Nørve.' - an estate division (skifte) after Kari Rasmusdatter between her widower Jon Olsen of Blindem and the daughter of her former marriage, Anna Martha Rasmusdatter, aged 24, with Willum Olsen Blindem as guardian; appraisers Thore Kolvigen and Siur Lille-Nørve. One word before 'Datter' is struck through and unclear. The account on the facing page lists the daughter's inheritance (about 19 rd) and then payments to Siur Rasmussen Lille Nørve, Synneve Rasmusdatter, Borgund church and Mons. Jacob Mechlenborg, which read as debts paid, not heirs (not certain). She was unmarried and living at Blindem when she married in 1803 (index, pv00000000942628; card in vld_027). ¶9

Father's probate (scan read): on 30 Jan 1790 the estate of Rasmus Alvsen of Blindem was divided between his widow Kari Rasmusdatter and 'den Afdødes... Datter Anne Martha Rasmusd. gl. 14 Aar med Formynder Willum Olsen Blindem'. The age of 14 fits her baptism of 24 Mar 1776 exactly. The record names her father in so many words, and the same guardian appears at her mother's probate in 1798. No other child is named. ¶10

Life story

Baptism (Probable): Anne Martha, daughter of Rasmus Alvsen of Blindem, was baptized 24 Mar 1776 at Borgund. That fits her residence at marriage in 1803; the 1819 age (40) is three years off. Date given is the baptism date. She is not the Anne Marta Rasmusdatter, 18, at Spilkevikgjære in 1815, who was born about 1797. ¶4

Her Blindem family is now three generations deep. Her father Rasmus Alvsen (about 1746-1790) was the son of Alv Rasmussen of Blindem (baptized 1712, buried 1778), whose second wife, married 29 July 1742, was Martha Olsdatter of Uggedal; Alv was in turn the son of Rasmus Alfsen. Her own double name, Anne Martha, repeats the names of her father's sisters Anne (1745) and Martha (1747) and of his mother Martha - which is itself part of the evidence for the link. ¶5

Second marriage: as the widow Anne Martha Rasmusdatter of Rossevold (born about 1779 by the index) she married Ole Johnsen, a bachelor farmer of Mælsæth (born about 1787), 13 Jun 1819 at Borgund. Gjertrud was born 10 Oct 1819. ¶3

She lost her father when she was about 14: he was buried at Blindem on 1 January 1790, aged 44. ¶6

First marriage: Anne Martha Rasmusdatter, a maid of Blindem, married Baard Johnsen of Røssevold, a bachelor, 17 Jul 1803 at Borgund. Baard Johnsen, farmer (jordbruger) at Røssevold, died 5 Jul 1817, age 38, and was buried 7 Jul 1817. ¶2

Named as mother of Gertrud Berthe, born 10 Oct 1819 at Røsevold, Borgund. ¶1

Marriage: At her second marriage, in Borgund church on 13 June 1819, she was the widow (enke) Anne Martha Rasmusdatter of Røssevold, aged 40; the groom was the bachelor farmer Ole Johnsen of 'Mælseth', 32. The marriage witnesses were John Olsen of Blindheim and Ananias Tyggesen of Røssevold (Borgund klokkerbok 1816-1827, p. 382 no. 20). ¶7

Baptism read on the scan (13 Oct 2026): on Annunciation Day, 24 Mar 1776, Borgund: '(2) Rasmus Alvsen Blindems Datter, N: Anne Martha. Test: M: Alv Rasmuss: ibm. Dr: Anders Ols: Uggedal. [Kromag?] Joens Hustr: Maria Cathrina Bagge bar hende. Qv: Ragnille Willumsdatt: Blindem. Pig: Siri Olsdatt: Furremyr.' Her godparents were Alv Rasmussen of Blindem (almost certainly her grandfather), the young man Anders Olsen Uggedal, Maria Cathrina Bagge, wife of Joen [word unclear], who carried her to the font, the wife Ragnille Willumsdatter of Blindem, and the maid Siri Olsdatter of Furremyr. The register names only fathers, so her mother is not stated. The entry is on printed pp. 161-162 (canvas 164 of source 15997), not 'p. 22' (22 is the index serial). ¶8

First marriage re-checked (13 Oct 2026): the Borgund marriage index row (Borgund local parish 1801-1825) gives the exact date 17 Jul 1803, bride Anne Martha Rasmusd., groom Baard Johns.; it agrees with the tree. Baard Johnsen (Røssevold) is not a person in the tree, so the date stays in the notes. ¶12

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Confidence raised from Probable to Confirmed (13 Oct 2026). Records already read on the scan name both of her parents explicitly. Her father: the Borgund baptism of 24 Mar 1776 names 'Rasmus Alvsen Blindems Datter, N: Anne Martha'. His probate of 30 Jan 1790 names the 'Datter Anne Martha Rasmusd. gl. 14 Aar' as heir, with guardian Willum Olsen Blindem. Her mother: the probate of 8 Sep 1798 after Kari Rasmusdatter names 'den afdødes udi [hendes] forrige Ægteskab avlede [...] Datter Anna Martha Rasmusdatter g[ammel]. 24 Aar', with the same guardian. These two probates make her the only child named in either estate. The 1803 marriage puts an unmarried Anne Martha Rasmusdatter at Blindem (index). As widow of Baard Johnsen of Røssevold she remarried in 1819. That chain carries her to the woman in this tree. The stated ages (14 in 1790, 24 in 1798, 40 in 1819) drift by up to three years, which is ordinary for the period. No other Anne Martha Rasmusdatter of a suitable age is known at Blindem. One gap remains: she is not found in the 1801 Borgund census. ¶11

Searches that found nothing 1

Burial not found (13 Oct 2026): the Digitalarkivet burial index for Borgund, 1820-1860, was searched as Anne Rasmusdatter (11 rows, none born 1774-1778 and none at Røssevold; nearest is Anne Kristine Rasmusd., born 1782, Bjørgeholm, buried 1842, who does not fit), as Martha Rasmusdatter (2 rows, neither fits), and as Rasmusdatter with residence Ros* (0 rows). Burial residence is blank in about half the rows, and the index is thin for 1820-1827, so this is a coverage gap rather than proof; the scanned Borgund burial book is the next record. ¶13
