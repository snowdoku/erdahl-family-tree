About the family

NEW ANCESTOR. Father of Evind Bjørnsen of Ekra under Søndre Askland (estate 3 Apr 1724). Graded on his link to Evind Bjørnsen Ekra: the archivist's back note on probate card 67, the abstract of Evind's own estate (Østre Råbyggelag Skifteprotokoll nr. 6 fol. 226), names him outright: 'avd:s far: Biønne Evensen Haugsjaa i Treungen. Se skjøte tgl. 13/11-1714 på Haugsjaa' (the deceased's father: Biønne Evensen Haugsjå in Treungen; see the deed registered 13 Nov 1714 for Haugsjå). Read on the card image. The same card names Evind's brother as Biønne Biønnesen Harstveit, husband of Birgitha Wraalsdatter, estate 1713. ¶1a

His father was an Even (patronymic only). ¶2

Life story

1664 vicar's census of Treungen (annex of Kviteseid), farm 'Hougsiøe 4 h' (DA 35508 view 78, p.128): six opsiddere, Knud 60, Bent 59, Biørn 44, Biønne 48, Torbiørn 46, Tallach 49; sons/servants column Evert 20, Bent 12, Erich 16, Knud 12, Torn[?] 13, Torn 14. The census gives no patronymics and does not pair the sons with their fathers. Biønne, 48 (b. about 1616), is the only Biønne on the farm, so probably this man; but if he is, Evind (whose children were born about 1700-1708) was born when Biønne was about 50, and the 1714 deed would be by his heirs. Not used for the birth estimate, which is left as a range. ¶3

Negative, for his father Even. The 11 Sep 1693 Haugsjå deed (registered at Vig on 4 Oct 1704; Panteregister I 1 fol. 14a) is not in pantebok I 2 (DA 10867), which begins 'Anno 1705 den 2 Martii' at Vinje (view 4). With negative for 10866 views 135-152, the next place to look is the court book (tingbok A 18) for the Vig ting of 4 Oct 1704. ¶9

Deed read: Østre Råbyggelag Pantebok A5, entry headed 'Dend 13 November 1714 tinglyst' (DA 17715 view 116, left page foot and right page top, signed 'EB' and dated 6 Nov 1714): 'Jeg underskrevne Eivind Biørnsøn, boende paa gaarden Ekra udi Giøvdal... kiendes... at som jeg... effter min Fader, nu sl. Biønne [E?]vensøn... arffueligen tilfalden' an odel share in 'gaarden Hougsiøe' in Treungen, together with (?) 'min... Broder Biønne Biønnesøn', living on a farm in Gjøvdal. The bitonal scan is hard; the words for the father's name and 'Hougsiøe' are legible, the rest of the clause is partly guessed. This is the primary record behind the archivist's note on probate card 67, so it confirms the link to Evind Bjørnsen Ekra from the deed itself, and shows Biønne was dead by 6 Nov 1714. It does not name Biønne's own parents beyond the patronymic. ¶6

Haugsjå: Panteregister I 1 fol. 14a (DA 10915 view 55, read on the scan) records that 'Biørn Evensøn' sold his half part in Hogsiøe (and fields/'Fæhoug' in Barchen?) to Jens Michelsøn in Arendal for 40 rd, deed dated Hogsiøe 11 Sep 1693, registered at Vig 4 Oct 1704. So he owned and lived on Haugsjå in 1693. Next on the farm, 1704-05, 'Aasmund Biønnesøn' mortgages and sells from his part of østregaarden Hougsiøe to the same Jens Michelsøn: probably a son (not added; a 1723 Haugsjå card has heirs Aasmund Biørnsen and Magnild g.m. Alf Olsen). Neither entry names Biønne's father Even. The 1693 deed itself, registered 4 Oct 1704, should be in Pantebok I 1 (DA 10866, 1697-1705, ends near view 152); not found in views 135-152, which are mostly Hjartdal/Seljord 1703-04. Try Pantebok I 2 (DA 10867, 1705-22) at its start, or the court book A 18 (1704-10) for the Vig ting of 4 Oct 1704. ¶8

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Audit 3 (1 Nov 2026): lowered from Confirmed to Probable, graded on his link to his son Evind Bjørnsen of Ekra (see his page). The only statement of the relationship is the archivist's back note on probate card 67, 'avd:s far: Biønne Evensen Haugsjaa i Treungen. Se skjøte tgl. 13/11-1714 på Haugsjaa'. It is a twentieth-century research note that cites a deed nobody has read. It is not an index of the 1724 skifte text: read the protocol (Skifteprotokoll nr. 6 fol. 226a) and attributes the father only to the card's back note. The same footing was rejected for Øli Svenningsdatter Rostveit in audit 1 (a card annotation as the only evidence), and AUDIT-2 flagged a later card annotation for Tøri Olsdatter Ohland. The note is specific and plausible, and Evind's brother's patronymic 'Biønnesen' fits it, so Probable is well supported. ¶5a

Upgrade: restored to Confirmed by the coordinator (1 Nov 2026), graded on his link to Evind Bjørnsen Ekra. Audit 3 lowered him because the only evidence was an archivist's note on a probate card citing an unread deed. That deed has now been read: Pantebok A5, DA 17715 view 116, recorded 13 Nov 1714. In it Evind Biørnsøn of Ekra sells Haugsjå odel land inherited 'effter min Fader, nu sl. Biønne [E]vensøn' (after my father, the late Biønne Evensøn). The primary record names the relationship. ¶7

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

No rival: the card itself is the abstract of Evind's skifte, so the link needs no farm-to-farm continuity. ¶1b

Open questions and the next record to read 2

Next: the deed registered 13 Nov 1714 for Haugsjå. Treungen then belonged to Kviteseid prestegjeld in Øvre Telemark, so the deed may be in an Øvre Telemark pantebok rather than Østre Råbyggelag Pantebok A5 (DA 17715; view 104 = fol. 79b-80, c. 1714, not read through). ¶4

Would confirm: the deed registered 13 Nov 1714 for Haugsjå (Øvre Telemark or Østre Råbyggelag pantebok, about DA 17715 fol. 80-90), if it names Evind as Biønne's son or heir. ¶5b
