About the family

Wife of Amund Hansen of Søndre Hvinden, Gran. Her estate was settled at Søndre Hvinden on 25 Apr 1741 (Hadeland, Land og Valdres skifteprotokoll 1737-1743, s. 585-586). The protocol lists the heirs as the children of her and the widower Amund Hansen: Halvor; Kirste, wife of Amund Gulbrandsen; Borgitta (Bergitte), wife of Hans Nielsen Skierstad; Kari, wife of Gulbrand Nielsen; [Mari], wife of Torger Olsen Racken; and the youngest, Ane, wife of Niels Gulbrandsen Skare. ¶1

Life story

Name: the forename is read as 'Marit' from a heavily blotted line of the opening paragraph on s. 585. That reading is uncertain. The patronymic was not legible; the following word may be 'Hans[datter]', but that is a guess. Her origin and kin are not stated in the parts read. No morbror (maternal uncle) or other kin of hers appears there. The land, Søndre Hvinden, is Amund Hansen's own odel (ancestral land), so the estate gives no hint of her farm of origin. The existence and motherhood are Confirmed; the name is not. ¶2

Probate card checked: the Hadeland probate card index, DA 39947 img 3491 ('Hadeland, Land og Valdres Skifteprot. 1737-1743, Hvinden i Gran, Søndre Vinden, S. 585, 1741 25/4'), names only the widower ('enkemand') Amund Hansen, 'lever, opgir boet' (living, gives up the estate), and the six children: 1. Halvor, 2. Kierste (m. Amund Gulbrandsen), 3. Bergitte (m. Hans Nielsen Skierstad), 4. Kari (m. Gulbrand Nielsen), 5. Mari (m. Torger Olsen Racken), 6. Ane (m. Niels Gulbrandsen Skare). Gross about 296 rdr, net 203 rdr; land in Søndre Hvinden valued at 220 rdr. The card does not give the deceased wife's name at all: the card-maker did not record it either. Her forename stays an uncertain reading of the protocol. ¶4

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Confidence set to Probable by the orchestrator (gatekeeping): the 1741 protocol establishes that Amund Hansen's late wife was the mother of Kirsti and her siblings, but her forename 'Marit' is read from a heavily blotted line (orchestrator also viewed the crop: 'Marit' plausible) and her patronymic is not legible. The name, not the relationship, is the uncertain part. ¶3
