About the family

NEW ANCESTOR, graded on his link to Vraal Bendixsen (see his page). The 1664 manntall for Giefuedal sogn lists three users of Omland (4 huder): Halfuor 63, Wrool 37 and Bendix 62 (Råbyggelaget fogderi 1664-65, s. 78, DA 35567 view 57, read on the scan). The manntall gives no relationships between the users. Bendix is the only man of that name in Gjøvdal on the page, and he shared Omland with Vraal Bendixsen, 25 years younger. Patronymic, farm and age make him Vraal's father; no record states it, so Probable. Vraal named his son Bendix (Bendix Evindsen was Ziri's son by her second husband, so that naming is not evidence). ¶1a

Life story

Bygdebok (Åmli ætt og heim II, Omland L.nr. 55, canvases 202-203): 'Bendik Vrålson f 1603', took the bruk in the 1640s and held it into the 1670s; wife Ragnhild Gunsteinsdtr. (Bygland sokn?), who was over 80 in 1708. His father was therefore a Vrål (not identified in the rebuilt text; no person added). ¶2a

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
