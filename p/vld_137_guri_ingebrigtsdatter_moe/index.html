Life story

Marriage (index): Grytten ministerialbok 1725-1763 (1539P), p. 31, Eid church, 18 Oct 1730: 'Mads', Moen, married 'Guri Ingebrichtsd.', Bøe (pv00000010058199). Mads's first wife ('Qvinde', name not given) had been buried from Moen at Vold church on 10 Dec 1729 with a funeral sermon (p. 27, pg00000007161140). Guri Ingebrichtsd. of Bøe was already godmother at Grytten on 26 Jan 1726 (pd00000042616259). ¶4

Probate (scan read): Romsdal sorenskriveri skifteprotokoll 3A 08 (1752-1761), s. 531b-532a (source 24617, view 535). On 19 Jan 1758 the appraisers Olle Knudsen Bøe, Knud Svensøn Skeie and Peder Sæbøe registered the estate at 'Moen i Wolds Otting' after the deceased woman 'Guri Ingebrigts Dotter', to be divided between her surviving husband Jørgen Ellingsøn and her children, namely 1. Endre Madsøn, guardian Knud Samset; 2. 'Brit Mads Dotter den ældre', guardian Elling Brudeset; 3. 'Brit Mads Dotter den yngre', guardian Olle Brudeset; 4. Anne Mads Dotter, guardian Hans Bøe. This proves she was the mother of both Briths, Endre and Anne by Mads Johansen Moe, and not the mother of his children Peder and Marith. ¶2

Burial (index): 'Guri Ingebrichtsd.', Moe, buried 1757, birth year calculated 1699 (Grytten ministerialbok 1725-1763, pg00000007162575). She remarried as a widow in 1751 ('E.', pv00000010060094) to Jørgen Ellingsen. ¶1

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

2 on the grade

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Why only Speculative as mother of Brit (Birgitte) Madsdatter: she is the bride's mother only if the bride was one of the two Briths, as the engagement entry, the 1768 godmother Birgite Madsd. Moe and the name Guraa given to the 1774 granddaughter suggest. The wedding entry calls the bride Marite, and Marith Madsdatter was a stepdaughter of Guri, not her child. A later record naming Ole Svendsen's second wife (her burial, or a Moe probate listing her as 'gift med Ole Svendsen Korsand') would settle it. ¶3

Upgrade: Speculative to Probable as mother of Brit (Birgitte) Madsdatter. The doubt was whether the 1770 bride, called 'Birgite' at the engagement and 'Marite' at the wedding, was one of Guri's two Briths or her stepdaughter Marith. The Grytten baptism index now dates the Moe children: Peder 5 Nov 1726 (first wife; pd00000042616532); then, after the 1730 marriage to Guri, Birgitthe 12 Nov 1730 (buried 1731), Birgitthe 23 Mar 1732 (buried 1732), Birgitthe 4 Aug 1733 (Brit the elder; pd00000042618666), Endre 17 Dec 1735 (pd00000042619483), Birgitthe 7 Apr 1738 (Brit the younger; pd00000042620427), Anna 7 Oct 1740 (pd00000042621409) and Mads c.1743 (buried 1748). Marith, a child of the first wife who died in Dec 1729, has no baptism in the index from 1725 on, so she was born before Dec 1729 and most likely before 1725; she would have been at least 45 in 1770 and about 49 or more when the bride bore Guraa in Aug 1774, after children in 1771 and 1773. The bride was therefore almost certainly one of the Briths (b. 1733 or 1738), both of whom Guri's 1758 probate names as her own daughters. In addition, 'Birgitte Matsd.' of Moe stood godmother at Grytten on 30 Jul 1757, 18 Oct 1761, 9 Apr 1768, 12 Jan, 27 Mar and 18 Jun 1769 (pd00000042628082, pd00000042629920, pd00000042635893, pd00000042636061, pd00000042636174, pd00000042636321) and is not seen at Moe after the May 1770 wedding, while no Marit Madsdatter of Moe appears in the Grytten index at all after 1749. This is an argument from ages and from absence, so it stays Probable under the grading rule; Brit's burial or a sibling's probate naming her as Ole Svendsen's wife would make it Confirmed. ¶5
