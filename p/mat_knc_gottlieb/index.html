Life story

Birth: Born at Płużnica (Pfeilsdorf), West Prussia, on 2 November 1905 in the evening, in his father's dwelling; registered 3 November 1905 as no. 47 by his father, the settler ('osadnik' in the 1949 Polish copy = Ansiedler) Gottlieb Knodel, Evangelical, who signed; mother Karolina Knodel née Schmidt. This confirms the birth date on his 1985 BC death record and shows that his 'Germany' there was correct for 1905. ¶5

Baptism (index): the Evangelical parish of Villisaß, Kreis Culm, has a baptism of Gottlieb Knodel on 24 Dec 1905, with the relatives indexed as 'Gottlieb, Karoline'. The civil birth at Pfeilsdorf was on 2 Nov 1905, 52 days earlier, and the relatives' names repeat the parents' given names, so this is very probably this child's baptism. This is an index entry only (Ancestry 'Germany, Lutheran Baptisms, Marriages, and Burials, 1567-1945', dbId 61250); the register image has not been read, so the index does not say whether the two names in its unlabelled 'Relatives' field are the parents or the godparents, and it gives no sponsors. Index recordId 3649069, image id 0245317-00093. ¶10

He married Emelia ('Millie') Eberle (born 23 October 1907 in Romania, daughter of Simon Eberle and Maria Hildebrand; died Penticton 23 December 1984). Find a Grave gives the marriage year as 1929. His brother Jacob married Sarah Eberle, probably Millie's sister. ¶2

1926 Prairie census: 'Godlie' [Gottlieb] Knodel, 20, single, a farm servant in the household of Simon Kelm on 28-26-24-W3, RM of Newcombe, Kindersley district, Saskatchewan. Born Germany, parents born Russia, immigrated 1925 (last digit overwritten), citizenship Germany; he spoke no English. His arrival record has not been found in the index. ¶7

1931 census: Gottlieb Knodel ('Gottleib'), 26, married, born Germany, father born Russia, mother Rumania, immigrated 1926 (last digit overwritten; possibly 1925), farmer on his own account (employer) on 25-26-24-W3, RM of Newcombe No. 260, Saskatchewan, in an owned two-room wooden house valued at $600. With him were his wife Amelia (23, born Germany, immigrated 1911) and daughter Irene (about 1, born Saskatchewan). ¶6

Voters list: 'Gottlieb Knodel Jr' is on the 1945 federal list for Kindersley, so he had been naturalised by then. ¶8

Known as Gus. Death, BC 1985-09-015717: died 8 September 1985 at Chilliwack General Hospital, of 9090 Newman Road, Chilliwack; a widower; worked as a bull cook (camp cook) for the Canadian National Railways; born 2 November 1905, 'Germany'. His father's surname was Knodel and his mother's maiden surname Schmidt, both born in Germany according to his daughter, who was the informant. He was cremated at Victory Memorial Park, Surrey. ¶1

Corrections 1

Correction: Find a Grave memorial 286729505 dates his death 8 August 1985; the BC registration says 8 September 1985. ¶3

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Not a duplicate: Gottlieb Knodel born 25 October 1906 at Pfeilsdorf (see his page) is a different person, a namesake cousin: his civil birth act names his parents as Jakob Knodel and Katharina Reister, whereas this Gottlieb's 1905 act (no. 47) names Gottlieb Knodel and Karolina Schmidt. ¶9

Searches that found nothing 1

1926 census, FamilySearch index (S0062; index only, image not opened): the FamilySearch index of the 1926 Census of the Prairie Provinces lists 'Godlia Knodel', born 1906 in Germany, who is evidently this man (the 'Godlie' Knodel, 20, farm servant of the LAC image), not his father, at Kindersley, Saskatchewan. This agrees with the LAC census image already read and adds no new fact. ¶11
