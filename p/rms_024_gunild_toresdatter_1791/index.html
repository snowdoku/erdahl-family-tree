Life story

Baptized on the 1st Sunday after Epiphany (8 Jan 1792) in Treungen church by the curate Storm: 'Tore Felles Datter Gunild. Test: Gunild Hiemdal, Targier Fælle, Turi Halvorsd. Hogstøl, Egild Sundet, Evind Evindsen Fælle.' (Kviteseid ministerialbok 1787-1799, left page facing p.53, image 55). The entry names the father only as Tore Felle. ¶1

She had an elder sister of the same name: the 1801 census lists two daughters called Gunild, aged 19 and 10. ¶3

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the grade

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Probable rather than Confirmed because a second Tore lived at Felle (the widower Tore Knudsen, who married Liv Olsdatter in 1788). The 1801 census of Felle, however, lists a Gunild Thoresdatter aged 10 among the children of Tore Thronsen and Hæge Toruvsdatter, and has no Tore Knudsen. Her godparents Targier [Osmundsdatter] Felle and Evind Evindsen also stood for her sister Ingebor in 1795. ¶2
