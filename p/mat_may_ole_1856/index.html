Life story

Baptism: 12 October 1856 at Eidem oestre soendre in Selbu, an elder brother of Severt S. Eidem and so a great-great-great-uncle of David Erdahl. He is one of the five children of John Sivertsen Eidem's son Sivert Johnsen Eidem and Brynhild Jonsdatter Langli who survived infancy. ¶1

He is recorded here as a separate person because this project had conflated him with a different Ole Sivertson Eidem - the Elk Point, South Dakota farmer who died in 1948, whose gravestone gives 8 October 1857 and who fits a Baktroen baptism of 25 October 1857. Two men of the same name and patronymic on the same cluster of farms, a year apart. What became of this one is not known: no emigration, marriage or death record has been matched to him, and he may have died in Selbu or emigrated under a name the indexes render differently. ¶2

1870 census: 'Ole', 14, son in the household of 'Seivert Johnson', 55, and 'Emma' [Brynhild], 52, on Brule Creek, Union County, Dakota Territory, 23 August 1870 (page 10, line 24). He has emigrated with his parents, as the 1866 Neptunus passenger list lead suggested. This also confirms that the Elk Point Ole Sivertson Eidem of 1857-1948 (see his page) is a different man: that Ole was still with his parents at Baktroen in Selbu in the 1875 census, while this Ole was in Dakota by 1870. ¶5

1880 census: 'Ole', son, white, male, 23, farmer, born Norway, in the household of his widowed mother Brynhild Johnson, 62, with his brother Sivert 20, in Brule Township, Union County, Dakota Territory, 4 June 1880 (ED 4, sheet 393D, line 31). Age 23 fits his baptism of 12 October 1856. This is the third census in a row with him in his mother's household (Selbu 1865, aged 10; Brule Creek 1870, aged 14), so the identification is secure. ¶7

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Rated Probable: the baptism entry places him in this family, but nothing retrieved follows him beyond it. The next records are the Selbu confirmation register about 1871, the 1875 census of the farm, and the Selbu burial register. ¶3

Confidence raised from Probable to Confirmed: his baptism of 12 October 1856 names his parents Sivert Johnsen and Brynild Johnsdatter of Eidem; the 1865 census of oestre Eidem has him, aged 10, in their household; and the 1870 census of Brule Creek has him, aged 14, in the same household in Dakota Territory with the same parents and siblings in the same order. What became of him after 1870 is still not known. ¶6

Corrections 1

Correction: the signed-in reader of this census (worker, S0078) matched 'Ole 23' to Ole Sivertson Eidem of Elk Point (see his page, b. 8 October 1857). That is wrong. The widow's son is this Ole, Ole Sivertsen Eidem; Ole Sivertson Eidem is the son of Sivert Olsen and Marit Torstensdatter of Baktroen and was still in Selbu in the 1875 census. The 1880 entry is recorded here only. ¶8

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Namesake: not the same man as Ole Sivertson Eidem of Elk Point, South Dakota (see his page, 1857-1948). This Ole was baptised 12 October 1856, son of Sivert Johnsen and Brynild Johnsdatter of Eidem (Selbu baptisms 1842-1858, p. 210 no. 2; parallel register 1843-1859, p. 107 no. 102), and is 'Ole Sivertsen', son, aged 10, born 1856, in his father's household at 0058 oestre Eidem in the 1865 census. A lead, not yet a record: a Find a Grave transcription of the Neptunus passenger list (Trondheim 5 May 1866, Quebec 9 June 1866) lists 'Ole Sivertsen 11' with Sivert Johnsen Eidem's family, so this boy most likely crossed with his parents in 1866. The Elk Point man is the other Ole Sivertsen of the farm, baptised 25 October 1857, son of Sivert Olsen and Marit Torstensdatter of the cottage Baktroen: he was still at Baktroen with those parents in the 1875 census ('Ole Sivertsen', b. 1857, 'Hjelper Foraeldrene', helping his parents), married Marit Einersdatter of Stavnes in Selbu on 24 April 1882 and had a son Sivert baptised there on 26 March 1883 - the wife Maret b. Dec 1852 and son Sivert O. b. 1883 in Norway of the Elk Point household. Different parents, different birth year, and still in Selbu in 1875, when this boy's family was already gone from the parish. ¶4
