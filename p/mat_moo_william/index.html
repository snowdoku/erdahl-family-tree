About the family

Probable brother of Grace Amelia Moore: one of the sons aged 5-16 in Richard Moore's 1861 household. ¶1

His son Richard Moore died aged 69 on 14 July 1951 ('Will's son. Wood Island'), and 'Mrs. Will Moore (Iris)' died on 18 March 1932 aged 82 years 1 month (Lauretta Brooks's diary). ¶5

Life story

1880 Meacham atlas: Wm. Moore held a 26-acre strip at Toronto, Lot 64, between Chas. Moore and Benj. Moore. ¶4

1881 census, Lot 64: William Moore, 34, Baptist, farmer; wife Mary, 31; Sarah Moore, 79 (his probable mother); children Samuel 2 and William 0; and Albert Moore, 19, plasterer (relationship not stated; born about 1862, too late to be Sarah's son). ¶2

1891 census index: William Moore (about 1847) with wife Mary and children Samuel (1879), Watson (1881), Elsie (1883), Richard (1885), Sarah (1887) and Margaret (1889). A son named Richard and a daughter named Sarah repeat the names of his probable parents. ¶3

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
