Life story

Bygdebok for Karmøy: Åkra (1980), farm 21 Heiå, canvas 186 (rebuilt from nb.no word boxes), names Knut Kolbeinsen (b. ca. 1665, user of the farm 1699-1733) as 'son [av] Kolbein Larssen', followed by a gap and 'bnr. 7', so Kolbein is placed on a bnr. 7 whose farm name was not recovered from the word boxes. Graded on his link to Knut Kolbeinsen: Probable, from the bygdebok statement alone. Not to be confused with the Kolbein (b. ca. 1634) who was a son of the Heiå user no. 4, '[...] Kolbeinsen, ca. 1604' (canvas 184). ¶1

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
