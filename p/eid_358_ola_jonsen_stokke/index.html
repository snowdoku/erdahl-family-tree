Life story

Fit with the tree: John's 1801 wife was 'Magli Nielsdatter', 45, in her third marriage (1801 census, Stoche hh 03), matching the book's 'Mali, f. 1757 [...] tredje gang'; John's 1808 second marriage to Dordi Jonsdatter Welve and his death in 1827 also match (canvas 20: '1808-1830 Jon Olsen [...] G.II 1808 m. Dordi [...] f. 1773, d. 1839'). ¶2a

Probate read, the bygdebok's test record: 'Anno 1768 den 25de Octob.' at 'Stocke i Selboe' after the late Ole Jonsen, with the widow Karen Christensdatter and the children 1. the son Jon Olsen, 10 years; 2. the son Christen Olsen, 3; 3. the daughter Mette Olsdatter, 11; 4. the daughter Berete Olsdatter, 7; Einer Kolset guardian for the minors (Stjør- og Verdal skifteprotokoll 24756 fol. 219b, lower half of the left page of view 229, found through the typed register 'Ole Jonsen, Stokkan, Selbu, 1768, 219 b'; creditors on fol. 220, including Jon Halvorsen Stokke). This matches the bygdebok's family exactly (Marit 1757, Jon 1759, Berit 1762, Kristen 1765). Jon aged 10 in Oct 1768 gives birth about 1758, the same year as the tree's John Olsen Stokke (see his page: 1801 age 42, 1825 age 67, burial 1827 age 68, all overlapping only Feb-Nov 1758). Rival excluded: the other Jon Olsen at Stokke, the older man of 1801 household 01 (b. about 1743), is the son of Ole Olsen, Stokkan, whose probate of 1774 (24758 fol. 247b) names 'Sønnen Joen Olsen 28 aar' (b. about 1746) with brothers Ole, Ole the younger, Mons and Thomas; the tree's John is called 'Jon Olsen yngre' in 1813. Kept Probable, graded on his link to John: the probate names the son, but the step from the boy of 1768 to the man at Stokke in 1801 spans 33 years with no record read in between (the 1794 marriage to the widow Mali, 16311, is still unread). Death: before 25 Oct 1768. ¶5

(not attempted beyond review; graded on his link to John Olsen Stokke, unchanged). Kari died in 1769 and Ola in 1768, so even a 1776 confirmation 'Jon Olsen Stokkan' (16311) could not bridge the next step, 1776 to the 1793-94 betrothal: about 18 years with no parent on the farm (audit 7a). Selbu confirmation lists also give no parents. A record that would close it: a legdsrulle (Trondhjem regiment roll, about 1775-90) listing Jon Olsen Stokkan with his father's name, or a guardianship (overformynderi) entry for Einer Kolset's wards from the 1768 skifte. ¶8

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the grade · 2 alternatives set aside · 1 search with no result · 1 open question

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Graded Probable on his link to his son John Olsen Stokke (see his page). Selbu i fortid og nåtid vol. 6, Midti-Stokkan 'Brukere' (canvas 19): '1753-1769 Ola Jonsen, [f.] 1733, d. 1768. G. ca. 1753 m. Kari [...] fra Flora, f. 1729, d. 1769'; children (canvas 20): '1. Jon, f. 1747 far: [...] Eidem [...]' (Kari's son before the marriage), '2. Marit, f. 1757, g.m. Ola Tomassen', '3. Jon, f. 1759, [se] over', '4. Berit, f. 1762, [g.m.] Tomas Olsen Stokke, [...] Uthus', '5. Kristen, f. 1765, d. 1786'. The next holder (1770-1793) was Ola's brother Jon Jonsen (b. 1726, d. 1792 aged 66), and (canvas 20) '1794-1807 Mali [...] f. 1757, d. 1807, enke etter Jon Jonsen [...] G.II 1794 m. Jon Olsen Stokke, f. 1759, d. 1827, som var brorsønnen til [...] mannen hennes' - i.e. John Olsen Stokke was the nephew (brother's son) of Mali's first husband Jon Jonsen, which makes him the son of Ola Jonsen; canvas 18 adds that the widow Mali married 'tredje gang i 1794' Jon Olsen 'som var født [...] på Midti-Stokkan'. 'Brorsønnen' was confirmed by an exact-word query (hits on canvases 18 and 20 only). ¶1

Alternatives considered and set aside 2

Caution: read every Selbu/Tydal baptism of a Jon son of an Ole in 1757-1759 and found none with parents Ole Jonsen and Kari, so the book's 'f. 1759' probably comes from the census age, and John's baptism is unread or lost. ¶2b

Near-miss attempt, not raised: no registered probate after the unmarried son Kristen/Christen Olsen Stokke (b. about 1765; 'Christen 3' in the 1768 skifte; d. about 1786) in the Stjør- og Verdal registers. Checked: 24761 (typed register, complete, 1783-85: Selbu C/K entries are only Christen/Kristen Svendsen Kallarstrøen); 24762 (typed register, view 3 C and view 4 K, 1785-87: Kristen Olsen there is of Ålberg, Leksvik, 1787, fol. 618b, not Selbu); 24763 (handwritten register, view 3 C list and view 7 K list, 1785-96: no Christen Olsen; the Selbu C entries are Christen Christensen Lien fol. 179 and Christen Christensen Stenigaard fol. 592). So the audit-9 sibling-probate bridge to Jon Olsen is not available from these registers. Remaining tests: the 1776 confirmation 'Jon Olsen Stokkan' (16311 c37-40), which would close 1768-76; and a burial of Kristen about 1786 (Selbu 16312). Grade unchanged. ¶7

Searches that found nothing 1

Negative: the register of Stjør- og Verdal skifteprotokoll 10 (24763, 1785-96), I/J section (register canvas 6, right page, image), was read in full for a probate after his brother Jon Jonsen Stokke (d. 1792), which should have named 'brorsønnen' Jon Olsen among the heirs if Jon Jonsen died childless. There is none: the Selbu Jon entries are Jon Jonsen Mogaard (349, 466), Jon Michelsen Mebust (357), Jon Siversen Setzenget (128, 137), Jon Pedersen Øster Aunet (186), Jon Jonsen Grøttem (483), Jon Jonsen Gr[...] (522) and Jon Baarsen Sandvig (522). ¶6

Open questions and the next record to read 1
