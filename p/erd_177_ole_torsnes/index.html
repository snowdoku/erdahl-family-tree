About the family

Father of Brite Olsdatter (wife of Hans Hansen Lunde) and of Tollef (Tollach) Olsen of Torsnes, b. about 1651. Known only from his children's patronymic: the 1713 Lunde probate names the widow 'Bredte Olsdat.' and, acting for her, 'hendis broder Tollach Torsnæs' (2b fol. 445a); the 1701 manntall lists Tollach Olsen, 50, bonde at Toesnes (Torsnes), with a second bonde Olle Olsen, 39, very probably another brother (Tollef's children had a 'Farbroder Ole Torsnes' as guardian in 1729). So the father was an Ole; that he himself held Torsnes is inferred from two sons farming it, not stated. Byname Torsnes is a working label. ¶1

Life story

1645 poll tax: 'Olle', gaardmand at Toesznesz (Torsnes), Tjugum skipreide, with his wife 'Brite Iffuersdtr'; the other farmer there was Omund (wife Ingebret Johansdtr), which may explain the fostersøn 'Aamund 6 aar' in Ole's 1666 household. Ole was thus farmer at Torsnes from at least 1645, aged about 39 by the 1666 age. Grade unchanged (his link to Brite Olsdatter Lunde is still inferred). The caution that the 1664 '[T?]arnæs' (35519 v151) may be Farnes is confirmed by the 1645 list, where Farnesz has the farmer Hermand (wife Christj Kieldsdtr) and a son Olle Hermandtzen, matching the 'Ola' and 'Hermund' of that 1664 farm; it is not Torsnes. ¶8

Possible sighting, not identified: the 1664 vicar's census for Tjugum sogn (35519 view 151, p. 279, saved earlier as) ends with a farm whose name is cut at the margin, read '[T?]arnæs' (probably Torsnæs), holding 2 lb. Sm., bønder 'Ola' and 'Hermund', sons 'Ola Olsen 15', 'Niels Hermundsen 16' and 'Jon Olsen 25'. If this is Torsnes, its Ola is probably this man; but Tollach (13 in 1664 by the 1701 age) is not listed, and Ole Olsen of 1701 (b. 1662) is too young to be the 15-year-old, so the match is unproved. Check the bailiff's list (35573 view 115) for the same farm. ¶3

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 3

Upgrade: the 1666 vicar's census of Tjugum sogn (35519 view 170, p. 314, N. 18) lists the farm 'Torsnæs', skyld 3 lbr. Sm. 1 hud, with 'Ola bruger, 60 aar' and in the sons' column 'Marcus 16 aar, Tollach 8 aar, Ola 2 1/2 aar' and 'Aamund 6 aar fostersøn'; a cottager Ola, 45, with two boys, is in the right-hand column. The bailiff's list of 1664 (35573 view 116, p. 212) has 'Thosnes' with the one farmer 'Olluf' and a servant (tjenestedreng) Anders Hansen, 19. So an Ola held Torsnes in 1664-66 and had sons Tollach and Ola: these are the Tollach Olsen (bonde at Toesnes 1701) and Olle Olsen (bonde there 1701, b. 1662 by the 1701 age, 2 1/2 in 1666) of the 1701 manntall, and Tollach is 'hendis broder Tollach Torsnæs' of the 1713 Lunde probate, i.e. the brother of Brite Olsdatter (daughters are not listed in this census). The earlier inference that he held Torsnes is now stated by a record, and his fatherhood of Tollach is read from it; Brite's descent rests on her Olsdatter patronymic and the 1713 'broder'. ¶4

Coordinator review (1 Nov 2026): kept at Probable under the overnight grading rule: the 1666 census proves him the father of Tollach Olsen, and the 1713 probate calls Tollach Brite's brother. No record names Brite as Ola's daughter, so the link to her is inferred from the sibling relation and her patronymic. ¶6

Re-proof attempt: no change. The Ytre Sogn probate register has no probate at Torsnes before 1740 other than Tollef Olsen's (1729). Brite Olsdatter's own probate, 12 Feb 1729 (3b fol. 339a), lists only adult children and no guardian, so it names no uncle (morbror) that would tie her to Ola of Torsnes; the 1713 'hendis broder Tollach Torsnæs' stays the only link. ¶7

Records that disagree 1

Age conflict noted: Tollach is 8 in 1666 (b. about 1658) but 50 in 1701 (b. 1651); the 1701 ages of older men are often overstated, and the son Marcus (16 in 1666) recurs as the name of Tollef's own son Marchus (1701), so the identification stands. The 1664 vicar's list sighting '[T?]arnæs' (35519 v151) is probably another farm (Farnes, 'Ola 45' in 1666 N. 15), not Torsnes. Born about 1606 from the 1666 age. ¶5
