About the family

Mother and family (bygdebok, Probable): Nissedal bygdesoge [2] Treungen, Tveit nordre (canvases 104-105): son of Bjørn Tveit (b. about 1620, skifte 1672) and Kari Torbjørnsdotter; married a woman 'of Felle' (the tree's Rannei Haavarsdatter, whose lauverge in 1720 was Gulløv Haavorsen of Felle); children Tone 1711, Kari 1713, Orm 1717, Valborg 1720; skifte after him 1720. Linked mother Kari Torbjørnsdatter. ¶9a

Daughter Kari: the Treungen burial of 2 Sep 1787 of 'Kari Egilsd.', 73, wife of Bendix Hogsaaestrandet (7983 c223R) gives a woman born about 1714, which fits his daughter Kari (7 in Apr 1720, 17 in Nov 1729). The one rival Egil in Treungen, Egild Torkildsen of Strand under Aarak (bur. Mar 1788 aged 87, so born about 1701), is ruled out: the probate after him (25678 No 677, held 8 May 1788) says his only heirs besides the widow Aslaug Tellefsdatter were 'den Afdødes Søskende eller Søskende Børn' (his siblings or their children), so he had no children, and in any case he was only about 13 years older than Kari. Kari Egilsdatter Hogsaaestrandet is not in the tree; her identity with Egil Bjørnsen's daughter remains an inference (name, age, parish). This does not change his own grade, which covers his link to Orm (see his page). ¶10

Life story

Known only from the patronymic of his son Orm Egilsen Tvedten (see his page) of Tvedt in Treungen, named as a guardian in a 1769 probate (Vest-Telemark skifteprotokoll 1768-76, source 25675, skifte No 924). A patronymic in a primary record gives the father's first name; the link of that record's Orm to our Orm Tvedt rests on him being the only Orm at Tvedt in the records read, so the link is Probable. Birth before 1700 is estimated from Orm's own birth before about 1720. Possibly of Tvedt in Treungen himself; not verified. ¶1a

Probate 1720 (read from the scan, 25692 fol. 420a foot to 420b, views 431-432): the entry opens 'Anno 1720' with the sorenskriver Cornelius Hansen, the lensmann Hans Ellefsen Lung and two Nissedal appraisers, and continues: 'forsamblet paa... gaarden Tvedt i Treungens annex under Hvideseid Præstegield... for at [holde] Registering, Vurdering, Skifte og Deling... efter den Salig Mand Eigild Biørensøn... imellem hans efterladte hustru Rannej Haawarsdatter', her lauverge the dannemand Gulløug [Haavorsøn?], 'og deris tilsammen avlede børn een Søn og 3 døtre, Nafnlig Orm Eigildsøn', his guardian the dannemand Ewend Dale(?), 'Tone og Kari samt Walborg Eigildsdøtre, af hvilke den første er 9 aar, den anden 7 aar og den 3die ½ aar'. Odelsgods in the farm Tvedt is in the estate. The card (6856 view 1310) gives the date 26 Apr 1720. Some words are faint; the names, the relationships ('efterladte hustru', 'tilsammen avlede børn') and the patronymic 'Biørensøn' are clear. ¶4

Found: his own probate. He is 'Eigild Biørensøn' (Egil Bjørnsen), dead before 26 Apr 1720, at Tvedt in Treungen; wife Rannei Haavorsdatter; children Orm, Tone (b. about 1711), Kari (about 1713) and Walborg (about 1719). This settles that the guardian-led heirs of 1729 were already fatherless and that his patronymic is Bjørnsen (it was inferred from his sister's name before). Name changed to Egil /Bjørnsen/. ¶6

Grading: his link to Orm Egilsen Tvedt (see his page) stays Probable. The probate names his son Orm Eigildsøn, but the step from that boy (1720, 1729) to the Orm Egilsen Tvedt(en) who buried children from 1755 and was guardian in 1769 spans about 26 years with no record in between (BRIEF audit 3: 20+ years gives Probable). ¶7a

Bridge record: 'Orm Eigelsøn boende paa gaarden Tvedt udi Treungens annex' signs a document of 12 Mar 1746 with Targier Nottofsen (pantebok I 4, source 10869, no. 575, view 213), and the panteregister I 1 (10915) has Orm Egilsøn/Igildsøn on Tvedt in Treungen in 1748, 1758, 1775 and 1777. This cuts the unrecorded gap between his son Orm Eigildsøn (probates of 1720 and 1729) and Orm Egilsen Tvedt (see his page) from about 26 to 17 years. Graded on his link to Orm Egilsen Tvedt: still Probable (gap over about 15 years; identification by patronymic and farm). ¶8a

Hogsaae skifte of 26 Mar 1782: a re-read of panteregister I 2 p. 813 shows that Wraal Egildsen's inheritance 'ved Skifte paa samme Gaard den 26 Martii 1782' is entered as ward money held by Knud Wraalsen of Hogsaae, and 'samme Gaard' refers to the farm named in the entry above it (Matr. No 71, name hard to read), not to Hogsaae. So it was not a skifte at Hogsaae, and Wraal Egildsen was a minor in 1782. He is not of Kari's generation and is not evidence for this family. The 1782 entry was not found among the headings of 25677 Nos 383-385, 388-395, 397-414 and 444-453 (views 45-53, 60-120, 218-246; closings 4 Mar - 1 May 1782 and Mar-Oct 1782). Probate of his sister: Margith Bjørnsdatter of Tvedt in Treungen, d. before 7 Nov 1729, left no children; her heirs were her brother's children Orm Igildsøn 12, Tone Igilsdatter 20, Kari 17 and Walborg 10 (Vest-Telemark skifteprotokoll 1722-34, source 25668, skifte no. 188, fol. 272a; card 6856 view 1315 'Broderbørn'). So Egil (Igild) was her brother and probably Egil Bjørnsen, and he had children Tone (b. about 1709), Kari (about 1712), Orm (about 1717) and Walborg (about 1719). He may have been dead by Nov 1729 (the children appear through a guardian, Salve [..]land; the word before 'broders' looks like 'afdøde' but is faint). This places his family at Tvedt in Treungen, as guessed in his first note. ¶2

Grading: his link to Orm Egilsen Tvedt (see his page) stays Probable. The 1729 probate names a son Orm Igildsøn, aged 12, in a Tvedt (Treungen) estate, which matches the Orm Egilsen Tvedt(en) of 1763 and 1769 by farm, patronymic and age (Orm buried children from 1755), but the identification spans 34 years with no record in between. ¶3

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the identification · 4 open questions

The case for this identification 1

Continuity: the four children of 1720 (Orm, Tone 9, Kari 7, Walborg ½) are the four heirs of Margith Bjørnsdatter at Tvedt in Treungen in Nov 1729 (Orm 12, Tone 20, Kari 17, Walborg 10, 25668 skifte no. 188), 9½ years later on the same farm, so the two probates are one family. Gulløv Haavorsen, Rannei's lauverge in 1720, was Margith Bjørnsdatter's husband in 1729. ¶5
