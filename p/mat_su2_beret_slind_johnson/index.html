Life story

So a Beret Slind married a Sven Johnson and had a daughter in Norway in 1879 who later lived in America under the name KULSETH - Kulset being the Selbu farm beside Kulsetmo/Kulsethmo, which is the farm name of Brynhild Kulsetmoen Eidem (4 Feb 1870 - 21 Mar 1959), wife of Mikal Paulsen Eidem. Bessie's own married or farm surname Kulseth therefore ties the two names together in one family, which is the first documentary connection between SLIND and KULSETH/KULSETMO found anywhere. ¶2

She is unlikely to be Kari "Carrie" Slind's mother on dates alone, but a Beret Slind with a daughter born in 1879 is exactly the generation Kari (b. 7 Sep 1863) came from, and this is the sort of household the slind-deep agent should look for in the Selbu registers. ¶4
