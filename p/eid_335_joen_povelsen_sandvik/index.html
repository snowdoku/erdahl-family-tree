About the family

Brother: Peder Poulsen (Povelsen) of Sandvik, the children's farbroder in 1728. A 'Brynild Povelsdatter', widow of an Ole, at Sandvik has a skifte in 24751 fol. 641 (typed register, about 1735-36) - probably his sister; not read. His father was a Povel (Poul), probably of Sandvik; not identified. ¶3

Siblings and father: the 1736 skifte after his nephew Joen Olsen Sandvigen (24751 fol. 649) lists, on the mother's side, 'Morbroderen Peder Povelsen Sandvigen', 'Morbroderen afgangne Joen Povelsens Børn 1. Ole Joensen 22, 2. Povel 16, 3. Gunder 14, 4. Ole Joensen 12, 5. Ingebor 10' and 'Morbroderen afgangne Gunder Povelsen[s] Søn Povel Gundersen 18'; the 1735 skifte after the mother, Brynild Povelsdatter (24751 fol. 641), names Peder Povelsen as the boy's Morbroder. The same five children (ages advanced 8 years) re-confirm the 1728 skifte. Birth narrowed to about 1690 from 'Jon 11' in the 1701 manntall (38461 c111), son of Paul Pedersen, Sandvik; his father is Probable (see Paul (Povel) Pedersen Sandvik). His own grade (on his link to Ole Jonsen Sandvik) is unchanged. ¶4

Life story

Probate (skifte) after him, Stjør- og Verdal Skifteprotokoll 2a (24750) fol. 117b, held 'Anno Christi 1728 den 25 Februarij' on the farm 'Sandweg udi Selboe Præstegield efter afg. Joen Povelsen Sandweg', between his widow 'Marit Olsdatter' and their five living children: '1. Ole Jonsen 12 aar, 2. Povel Jonsen 8 aar, 3. Gunder Jonsen 6 aar, 4. Ole Jonsen Junior 3 aar og 5te Ingebor Jonsdatter [1?] aar gl.' The guardian for the minors was 'deres Farbroder Peder Poulsen Sandweg', with the deceased father's cousin ('afg. Faders Sødskende Barn') Biørn [Haarstad?]. On fol. 119b-120 the shares are set out for 'Ole Joensen' and 'his brother Poul Joensen' as 'fæderne arv'. ¶1

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 3

Graded on his link to Ole Jonsen Sandvik (see his page). The probate names two sons called Ole: Ole, 12 (born about 1716), and 'Ole Jonsen Junior', 3 (born about 1724-25). These are exactly the two Ole Jonsens of Sandvik known later: Ole Jonsen Sandvik, buried 18 Jun 1767 aged 50, and his brother Ole Jonsen of Øster Sandvik, buried Jun 1768 aged 44, whom the 1762 skifte after Ole's first wife calls 'hans Farbroder Ole Jonsen Sandwig' (24755 fol. 246a). Same farm, matching ages, and the unusual pair of brothers both named Ole; no other Jon at Sandvik with sons of these names is known. The two Oles were full brothers, sons of Joen and Marit, which also explains the 1762 'Farbroder'. ¶2

Audit 4 (1 Nov 2026): lowered from Confirmed to Probable, graded on his link to his son Ole Jonsen Sandvik (see his page). The 25 Feb 1728 skifte (24750 fol. 117b) names the relationship: sons 'Ole Jonsen 12' and 'Ole Jonsen Junior 3'. But the next record of either Ole is the 17 Jun 1762 skifte after Ole's first wife (24755 fol. 246a), 34 years later, with nothing read in between. That record names 'hans Farbroder Ole Jonsen Sandwig', so it shows the two later Oles were brothers. It does not name their father. Joining the 1728 boys to the 1762 men rests on the unusual pair of brothers named Ole, matching burial ages (50 in 1767, 44 in 1768) and the farm. That is a strong fingerprint, but it is an identification across decades without a bridging record. Under the audit-3 detail, a gap of 20 years or more with no record in between gives Probable, as for Ole Olsen Lille Nørve (24 years) and Marit Olsdatter (25 years). There is also an unread possible rival father at the same farm: 'Joen Olsen, Sandvik, 1736' (24751 fol. 649, typed register). ¶5a

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed, graded on his link to his son Ole Jonsen Sandvik (see his page). Audit 4 asked for a record of Ole between 1728 and about 1750 that bridges the 1728 skifte to the man of 1762-67. Found: the Selbu klokkerbok 1740-1746 (DA 16315, IIIF canvas 91, left half), St Michael's Day (29 Sep) 1746. Under 'Kirkegang' (churching of mothers): 'No. 2 Ole Sandwigens Kone Ingebor Michelsdatter' ('Sand' written above the line). Among the communicants of the same day: 'No. 22 Ole Junior Sandvig', 'No. 23 Jon Sandvigen', 'No. 24 Marithe Sandvigen', 'No. 26 Peder Sandvigen'. Continuity, step by step: (1) 25 Feb 1728 skifte at Sandvik names the relationship: Joen Povelsen's sons 'Ole Jonsen 12' and 'Ole Jonsen Junior 3'. (2) Feb 1736 skifte at Sandvigen (24751 fol. 649) again names 'afgangne Joen Povelsens Børn 1. Ole Joensen 22... 4. Ole Joensen 12' (8 years). (3) Sep 1746 at Sandvik: an Ole whose wife is Ingebor Michelsdatter, and alongside him an Ole still distinguished as 'Junior', the same label the 1728 skifte gave the younger brother (10 years after 1736, same farm). (4) 17 Jun 1762 skifte at Sandvigen (24755 fol. 246a): Ingebor Michelsdatter, wife of Ole Jonsen, with 'hans Farbroder Ole Jonsen Sandwig' (16 years; identified by the couple, both spouses' names matching, not by a man's name alone). (5) 1767 skifte and burial of Ole Jonsen Sandvig, aged 50 (born about 1716 = the Ole of 12 in 1728). ¶6a

Alternatives considered and set aside 2

No rival: the only other candidate, Joen Olsen of Sandvik (skifte Feb 1736, 24751 fol. 649), was the 19-year-old only son of Brynild Povelsdatter in 1735 and died unmarried; his heirs were uncles and cousins, so he left no son Ole. No other Ole Jonsen is known at Sandvik. ¶6b

Ruled out: the 1742 Sandvik probate cited as 'Ole Joensen Sandvik (husband of Brynild Povelsdatter)' was read (24752 view 407 right, fol. about 1140b-1141). It is a skifte of 16 Jun 1742 after 'Ole Joensen Sandvigen' between his widow Gyrtrud[?] Pedersdatter and their four children: Joen Olsen 16, Ole Olsen 10, Anne Olsdatter 13 and Marit Olsdatter 8. A '[..] Joensen Sandvig' was guardian for the sons; his relationship word is not legible. A Pedersen was 'Morbroderen' for the daughters, and Niels Belle and Ole Pedersen Fuglem were appraisers. This Ole Joensen, born before about 1705, cannot be Joen Povelsen's son Ole, who was 12 in 1728 and lived until 1767. Joen's 1728 skifte names no older son. So this is another Sandvik household of the same generation, and the widow is not a Povelsdatter. The estate gives nothing on Joen's parents. The '[..] Joensen Sandvig' guardian could be a brother of the deceased; his name would show whether an older Joen held part of Sandvik before 1700. ¶7

Open questions and the next record to read 2

Would confirm: any record of Ole between 1728 and about 1750 that names his father or places him in Joen Povelsen's family, for example his marriage to Ingebor Michelsdatter (about 1745-47), the deed to Sandvigen of 4 Nov 1751, or Brynild Povelsdatter's skifte (24751 fol. 641); or reading the 1736 Joen Olsen skifte to rule him out. ¶5b

Flag: the 1746 entry gives no patronymic, and the 1746-1762 step is 16 years, bridged by the couple's two names on the same farm (the grey zone audit 4 accepted for, where both spouses matched). ¶6c
