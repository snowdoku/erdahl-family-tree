About the family

Widow of Capt. Nicholas Simpkins (d. 1656), who had bought the house from Leonard Buttles on 22 Oct. 1652 (same deed). Her daughter Rebecca had married William Therrell in 1654 as 'Rebecca Simpkins daughter of Capt. Nicholas Simpkins' (Boston register, vol. 9). TAG 28:87 says she testified in 1654; a deposition by 'Isabel Simpkins, aged 44 years' is printed in NEHGR 5 (1851) (not read in full), which would put her birth about 1610. Maiden name unknown. ¶2

Life story

Birth: she deposed in June 1654, in the estate of Maj. Holmes, as 'Isabel Simpkins, aged 44 years' (NEHGR 5:4, abstract of the Suffolk file), so she was born about 1610. She was 'Esbell Simpkins the Wife of Mr Nicolas Simpkins' when she released her thirds in his Scituate house on 1 March 1648/9 (Plymouth Colony Records 12). ¶3

London lead (2 Nov 2026): 'Isabell', wife of Nicholas Simkin(s), had Sarah (chr. 6 Oct 1629, d. 7 Oct) and Mary (chr. 12 Jun 1631) at St Benet Fink, London (FamilySearch index; images center-only, not read). This would fit Isabel (aged 44 in 1654, so born about 1610), but it is a name match only, a second Nicholas Simkins (wife Elizabeth Saule, m. 1628) was in London at the same time, and no Nicholas x Isabel marriage is indexed 1615-1636; her maiden name is still unknown. Her grade (on her link to Pilgrim Simpkins) is unaffected. ¶7

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Graded on her link to her son Pilgrim Simpkins. The arbitration deeds of 21-24 Jan. 1669/70 (Suffolk Deeds IX:224-226, printed transcript of the register) recite that 'it pleased god to take out of this life m[rs]. Izable Simkins of Boston aforesd sometime in September last past who left Issue viz[t] Pillgram Simkins her sonne Debora the now wife of George Burrell and the sd Rebecca Terill her two daughters', and that the children, 'for the auoyding of all future differences betweene them', referred the division of 'the dwelling house and estate left by there said mother' to arbitrators, who awarded the house to Pilgrim. The record names her as Pilgrim's mother. Continuity: Pilgrim Simpkins is the only man of that name in Boston; he was then living in the house ('the sd Pillgram seuerall yeares in possession'), had married Katherine Richardson there in 1661, and appears as owner of the neighbouring land in later deeds; no rival. ¶1

Records that disagree 1

Age problem (not a contradiction of her link): Pilgrim Simpkins's gravestone says he died in 1720 'aged about 96', i.e. born about 1624, when Isabel would have been about 14. Pilgrim married first by 1659 (son Nicholas b. 1660) and secondly in 1661, which suggests a birth nearer 1635. The gravestone age is probably overstated. Her link to Pilgrim rests on the 1669/70 deeds that name him 'her sonne' (Suffolk Deeds IX:224), which this does not touch. ¶4

Searches that found nothing 1

Negative search: FreeREG, nationwide and fuzzy, has no marriage of an Isabel to a Simpkins, nor of a Nicholas Simpkins, 1618-1640, so her maiden name is still unknown. Her deposition age (44 in June 1654, NEHGR 5:4) puts her birth about 1610. ¶5

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Lead: a Nicholas Simkin(s) and Isabell had daughters baptised at St Benet Fink, London: Sarah, 6 Oct 1629 (died 7 Oct), and Mary, 12 Jun 1631 (FamilySearch index 1:1:JQLG-YBF, 1:1:JW7Y-GJ9). If this is the Boston couple, Isabel (aged 44 in 1654, so b. c.1610) married about 1628 in or near London. Not proved; no marriage indexed. Grade unchanged. ¶6
