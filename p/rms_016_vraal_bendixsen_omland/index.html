Life story

Named only in the archivist's back note to the 1728 probate of his son-in-law Børu Aadnesen: 'Enkens far: Wraal Bendixsen Aamland + sk. 1687' (probate held 1687; date of death not given). Children: Anna Vraalsdatter (m. Børu Aadnesen) and Giermund Vraalsen Omland (named on the 1728 card as the widow's brother). His own 1687 probate (Østre Råbyggelag, Omland/Aamland i Gjøvdal cards) not yet read. Probable rather than Confirmed because this link comes from the archivist's cross-reference, not the text of the 1728 skifte. ¶1

Bygdebok (Åmli ætt og heim II, Omland L.nr. 55, canvas 203): 'Vrål [Bendikson] f 1656. Skifte 1687. [g m] Sigrid [Olsdtr.] (Øy)'; son of Bendik Vrålson (b. 1603) and Ragnhild Gunsteinsdtr.; children Gjermund (1680-1741, m. 1. Signe Gunsteinsdtr., 2. Anne [?] Austenå/Øvre Ramse), Signe b. 1678 (m. Olav [?] Askland), Anne b. 1681 (m. [Børuf] Ådneson Jørundland); after his death his brother Olav took over the farm. Correction (no date changed): the book's birth year 1656 conflicts with the tree's ABT 1627 (from 'Wrool 37' in the 1664 census). The 1656 date fits his children (born 1678-81) and the 'Vraar 10' among the sons at Omland in 1666 better; the 1664 Wrool 37 may be another Vraal (already found a rival Vraal Evindsen). Mother added as Speculative until this is settled. ¶7a

1664 bailiff's census, Gjøvdal, Omland 4 hud: opsiddere Halfuor[?] 63, Vr[aa?]l 37, Bendix 62 (readings of the first two names uncertain; no relationship stated). If Vraal, born about 1627. The Omland 3 hud entry has a [Vraal?/Berul?] 32. Read. ¶2

Vicar's census, Gjøvdal, Omland 4 huder: 1664 (35510/16 p.10) Valde[?] 63, Bendix 62, Vraar 37; 1666 (35510/32 p.43) Volche[?] 64 (2 huder), Bendix 63 (1 hud), Vraar 38; sons on the farm in 1666 Gunsten 11, Effuind 8, Vraar 10, Oluff 2 (not assigned by name). Fits Vraal b. c.1627 beside a Bendix b. c.1602, but no patronymic or relationship is written. ¶3

New card. The Askland card 43 (the back of card 41: the 23 Apr 1716 estate of Zigne Wraalsdtr., wife of Ole Tiøelsøn of Søndre Askland, Skp. 5 fol. 25) lists the documents produced: (1) a transport from Knud Eivindsøn Rüsland to Ole Tiøelsøn Askland, 8 Nov 1701; (2) 'skiftebrevet etter avd.'s far, Wraal Bendixøn Ombland, dat. 7/9-1687'; (3) the same 'etter avd.'s mor, Siirj Olufsdtr. Ombland, dat. 15/11-1688'. This gives his estate date as 7 Sep 1687, shows that his wife was Siirj (Ziri) Olufsdatter, and adds a daughter, Zigne Vraalsdatter (d. by 1716). Graded on his link to Anna Vraalsdatter (see her page): Anna's mother was Ziri Olsdatter (card 5999), and this card makes Ziri the wife of Vraal Bendixsen, so Vraal fits Anna's patronymic. No record names Anna as his child, so he stays Probable. The 1687 skiftebrev itself is not in the protocols (the pre-1697 Råbyggelag probates are lost). ¶4

1664 manntall, read on the scan: Giefuedal sogn, 'Omland - 4 hud.': users Halfuor 63 aar, Wrool 37 aar and Bendix 62 aar, each using one hud; servants Guttorm 26 and Ole 32; husmann Gunder 8x (Råbyggelaget fogderi 1664-65, s. 78, DA 35567 view 57). Wrool (Vraal), 37, is Vraal Bendixsen, so he was born about 1627 and was already a user of Omland in 1664. The Bendix aged 62 on the same farm is taken as his father (see Bendix). Note the ages: born about 1627, he would have been about 60 when his daughter Anna (see her page, birth estimated about 1688) was born, shortly before his death in 1687; his wife Ziri was evidently much younger, since she remarried and bore a son, Bendix Evindsen, before her own death in 1688. Anna's birth estimate may be too late. ¶5

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Rival named: the 1664-65 bailiff's list of men under 50, with patronymics (DA 35567 view 26, read at zoom), has TWO Gjøvdal entries. p. 14 'Omland': 'Vraald Effuindsøn 30 aar', with 'Olluff Effuindsøn 24' in the sons/servants column. p. 15 'Omeland': '[Vraal?] 36' and Knud 38, with Tarald Olsøn 30. So there was a younger Vraal Evindsen (b. about 1634) on an Omland holding, beside the older Vraal (36-37, b. about 1627) whose patronymic this list does not give. The tree's man is called 'Wraal Bendixøn Ombland' on card 43, so he is not Vraal Evindsen, but the two Vraals on Omland mean any census age or 1660s reading of 'Vraal Omland' must be checked against both. The reading of the p. 15 first name is uncertain. The younger man is a separate, documented person: the Harstveit probate cards name 'Wraal Evensen Aamland' as father of Asbør Wraalsdatter (widow of Nære Jørgensøn Harstveit, 1708, card 4257) and of Birgitha Wraalsdatter (widow of Tiøstøl Tolfsøn Harstveit, 1709-1713, card 4267), with their brother Gunder Wraalsen (Omland, later Askland, oppg. sk. 1732/1737). The children of Vraal Bendixsen are a different set: Zigne (card 43), Anna (card 5999) and Giermund Vraalsen Omland (card 5999; 'Morbror... Giermund Wraalsøn Ombland' for Zigne's children, card 59). So the two Vraals and their children are kept apart in the cards. Grade unchanged. ¶6
