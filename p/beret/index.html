Life story

Baptized 4 Nov 1849, Værnes (Stjørdal) parish as Beret Martha Thomasdatter. ¶1

Born 7 Oct 1849 and baptized 4 Nov 1849 at Værnes church, the parish church of Stjørdal, entered as entry no. 280 on page 78. Her father is given as 'Hm. Thomas Olsen Beitlandgjerdet' - Hm. for husmand, a crofter. ¶6

Her godparents, read from the register page, were: Margrete Olsdatter of Kleven; Marit Olsdatter of Fossum (a married woman); Kirsti Jensdatter of Beitland (an unmarried woman - a Kirsti Jensdatter of Beitland appears again in the Hegra register in 1857); Anders Larsen of Bakmoen; Peter Johnsen of Fossgaard; and Rollaug of Fosshammer. Anders Larsen of Bakmoen also stood godparent to her elder brother Ole in 1848; Bakmo was the home farm of her father's first wife. ¶7

Listed directly under her in the same hotel household is '[Thompson] Elizabeth', 17, female, waiter, born Norway (surname by ditto), so born about 1852-53. She may be Beret's sister Lisbeth (baptized 1852), but nothing in the record states a relationship. A FamilySearch search of the 1870 census for Norway-born Thompsons in Faribault County (47 hits) found no Thomas Thompson of parental age (only boys born 1857 and 1865) and no Eli/Ellen of mother's age, so the parents were not found in the county in 1870 under that surname. ¶16

She was the second of six children. The family left Hegra between October 1862 and December 1865 (see her father's record) and settled in Faribault County, Minnesota. ¶8

1870 census, probable identification: in the 1870 U.S. census of Blue Earth City, Faribault County, Minnesota (dwelling 98, the hotel of Hen. P. Constaus, hotel keeper, born France), 'Betsey Thompson', 20, female, cook, born Norway, is listed among the hotel's servants and laborers. This is probably Beret Martha: the age 20 fits her birth in October 1849, 'Thompson' is the usual anglicising of her patronymic Thomasdatter, 'Betsy M. Thompson' is the name under which she married in this same county in 1876, and she was Norway-born. What does NOT prove it: the 1870 census has no relationship column and gives no parents, no year of immigration and no Norwegian place, and Betsey/Betsy Thompson is a common name among Norwegian immigrants (a second Betsey Thompson, b. c.1856, lived in Rome township in the same county). ¶15

Married Lasse Lassesen Erdahl 1876. Died in childbirth with Berent. ¶2

Her baptism index names the mother 'Elisabeth Hansdatter'; all five sibling baptisms say Eli Larsdatter, so this is likely an indexing error. ¶4

Married Lasse Lassesen 'Lewis' Erdahl on 12 June 1876 in Faribault County, Minnesota. The county marriage index enters them as 'Lasse Lasseson Erdahl' and 'Betsy M. Thompson', certificate B-439 - 'Betsy' is the usual American rendering of Beret/Berit, and 'Thompson' is her Norwegian patronymic Thomasdatter anglicised. This identification is confirmed by the same index entering her brother two rows apart as 'Lorence T. Beitland'. ¶9

Two children, both born in Minnesota: Lauris L. Erdahl, born 29 May 1877 and dead 11 Sep 1877 at three months, buried in block 40 of South Blue Earth Lutheran Cemetery; and Berent Ludvig Erdahl, born 31 October 1878. ¶10

She died in childbirth. Her death date, 31 Oct 1878, is the same day her son Berent Ludvig was born - the two dates together are the evidence, and the family tradition recorded on Berent's memorial says the same. She was 29. Her husband remarried on 17 Jan 1880, to Brita Myklebust 'Betsy' Engebrigtson, who raised Berent. ¶11

She is buried in South Blue Earth Lutheran Cemetery at Bricelyn, apart from her husband, who lies in Bethany Cemetery at Frost. Her parents, her brother Lorentz, her sister Kirstine and her infant son Lauris are all in the same Bricelyn cemetery. ¶12

Blue Earth baptisms of her sons: the Blue Earth congregation's church book (Faribault County, transcribed on Digitalarkivet) names her as the mother at both of her children's baptisms. In 1877 she is 'Berthe T. Erdahl', at the baptism of Laurits; in 1878 she is 'Martha Thomasdatter Erdahl', at the baptism of 'Bernt Ludvik'. The father is Lasse L. Erdahl both times. The two forms are her two given names (Beret/Berthe and Martha) with her patronymic Thomasdatter. This is the primary record of her motherhood of Berent. The index gives no days or godparents. ¶13

Corrections 1

Correction, settled from the page image: the 'Elisabeth Hansdatter' problem is a transcription artefact, not a second wife. The original register page (Stjørdal/Værnes ministerialbok 1845-1858, page 78, entry no. 280) shows that the pastor first wrote a wrong mother on the line and then corrected it: the first name 'Margrete' is struck through with 'Eli' written above it, and the patronymic that follows is struck through and overwritten (the overwriting reads 'Larsdr'). The Digitalarkivet index 'Elisabeth Hansdatter' was read off the cancelled, overwritten text. The corrected entry agrees with the other five sibling baptisms, with the parents' 1846 marriage record (bride Eli Larsdatter, her father named as Lars Eriksen) and with Ole's 1862 confirmation (mother Eli Larsdatter). There was never an Elisabeth Hansdatter in this family. ¶5
