About the family

Mother of Marit Biertesdatter (see her page), graded on her link to Marit: Probable, from the Lesja bygdebok (vol. 1, c181: 'Bjerte, ca 1644-..., g.m. Guro Jakobsd.', children including '4. Marit, ca. 1683') with support from primary records: 'Goro Jacobsd', Eneboe, buried 13 Dec 1733, age implying b. 1642 (Lesja burial index); a 'Guro, Eneboe' stood godmother beside 'Bierte, Eneboe' on 28 Jul 1726. Her age would make her about 41 at Marit's birth (about 1683), late but possible; the bygdebok places Marit after Synnøve (ca. 1665), Pål (ca. 1671) and a child of ca. 1678, and before Ågot (ca. 1684) and two younger children (ca. 1694 and Anne), so the later children may belong to a second wife - an unresolved point. ¶1a

Father entered (Probable): Jakob Jakobsen of Sør-Kvam, ca. 1604 - ca. 1674 (see his page), from the Lesja bygdebok vol. 1, canvas 141, Sør-Kvam entry 4-2, which lists '2. Guro, 1642, sjå Einbu (1-3)' among his children alive in 1674 - the year matching her burial index entry (b. 1642). Her mother's name is in a gap of the word-box rebuild. Her own grade (link to Marit) is unchanged. Not found: a skifte after her (Dec 1733), after Bjerte or after Poul Biertes (Apr 1735) in skifteprotokoll 25157; checked views 150-210 only by sample (view 194 = fol. 190, about Apr 1735; view 200 = 1735 Søndre Bottheim; view 210 = 3 Dec 1735; view 230 = 21 Apr 1736) without a full read, and none is carded under Einbu (39945). ¶2
