About the family

Life story

Bygdebok, Skatval vol. 1, 35 Bå, canvases 221 right - 222 left: 'Peder Eriksen, [f.] 1711, [d.] 14.06.1790, var [fra] 79... 20.12.1743 [lease/deed]. 1. gang 08.04.1744 Mali Andersdtr. [d.] 29.05.1752 [fra] 77/3-4' (children Anders 1745, Erik 1746, Ola 1748, Mali 1751); '2. gang 05.06.1753 Mali Olsdtr. [fra] 29'. Vol. 3 canvas 306 left (a husmannsplass, 'O-19', apparently under gnr. 79) lists a household 'Erik, kone [name not recovered]' with a son 'Peder, 1711, 35' - so the book makes him son of a husmann Erik. Not added as a person: no patronymic, dates or wife. ¶23a

Peder Arntsen's family (scan read): the probate (arveskifte) held 4 Jul 1743 'paa gaarden Baard udi Størdahlens Præstegield, Schatvolds Annex' after the woman Berit Pedersdatter names her children as heirs: '1. Sønnen Peder Arnsen boende paa gaarden' (the son Peder Arnsen, living on the farm) and '2. Berit Arnsdatter 23 aar gl., tiener paa gaarden' (23, a servant on the farm); the rest of the long entry was not read. So Peder Arntsen was already living at Baar in 1743, as son of Arnt Joensen and Berit Pedersdatter. If he is the 'Peder of Baar' who fathered Peder (1768), these are the next generation; that is not yet shown, so they are not added. Other men of similar name in the Stjørdal burial index: Peder Arnsen of Hembre, buried 8 Apr 1757 aged 46 (b. about 1711), and Peder Arentsen of Hegge, buried 5 Dec 1774 aged 60. Whether any of them is the Baar widower of 1755 is open. ¶5

The probate (registrering) of 3 Jul 1752 at Baard (Baar), Skatval, after Mali Andersdatter, wife of Peder Erichsen (Stjør- og Verdal skifteprotokoll 3, 24753 fol. 449b-450a, scan read), names the widower Peder Erichsen and four minor children: '1. Anders Pedersen 7 aar, 2. Erich Pedersen 5 aar, 3. Ole Pedersen 3½ aar, 4. Mali Pedersdatter 1½ aar'. Erich Pedersen (b. c.1747) and Mali Pedersdatter (b. c.1751) of Baar were both buried 11 Apr 1756 (Stjørdal burial index pg00000007156985, pg00000007156986). So Peder Erichsen had a living son Ole (b. c.1748) in 1752; the 'Peder Baar' whose son Ole was baptised 25 Jul 1756 is therefore more likely Peder Arntsen (married Jun 1755), unless the elder Ole had died. ¶8

His second marriage. 16370 canvas 235 left, 17 Mar 1753 at Værnes: 'Enkemand [Per] Erichsen Baar' (first name under an ink blot) betrothed to 'Pigen Mali Olsdatter Vold'; married at Værnes 5 Jun 1753. Sureties: [Per] Jonsen Skervauran (= Peder Jonsen Skjervold, Peder Jonsen) and Andreas [Olsen?] Skervauran. This dates the start of the second marriage named in the 1790 skifte ('af 2det Egteskab med den igienlevende Enke'). It adds a date only; the graded link is unchanged. ¶24

Patronymic still unknown; two candidates. (1) Peder Arnsen (Arntsen) of Baar, a widower ('Enkemanden Peder Arnsen Baar'), was betrothed at Værnes on 22 Feb 1755 to Berethe Jacobsdatter of Röan (sureties Jon Persen Röan and Jon Bersvendsen Röan) and married on 5 Jun 1755 (Stjørdal 16370, betrothals 1755, scan read). The skifte register also has a probate in 1743 after 'Berit Pedersdatter og mann Arnt Joensen', Bår (24753 fol. 46b), so an Arnt Joensen held Baar before him. (2) Peder Erichsen of Baar, whose wife Mali Andersdatter died about 1752 (skifte register 24753 fol. 449b), buried from Baar 14 Jun 1790 as a 'Fledføring' (poor old man boarded round the farms) aged 79; a Mali Olsdatter of Baar was buried 8 Feb 1791 aged 67. ¶4

The index has one father 'Peder of Baar' for baptisms of 25 Jul 1756 (Ole), 16 Jul 1758, 1 Feb 1761, 27 Mar 1766 and 16 Oct 1768 (Peder), but with two Peders at Baar these could belong to either man, or be split between them. The first child, Ole, born 13 months after Peder Arntsen's marriage of June 1755, fits Peder Arntsen x Berethe Jacobsdatter; this is a timing inference only. Ole is the Ole Pedersen, gaardmand of Baar, buried 23 Mar 1806 aged 50. Do not assign a patronymic or a wife until a record names the mother or the father's patronymic beside one of these children. ¶6

Two more 'Peder Baar' baptisms read on the scans (Stjørdal 16371): 25 Jul 1756 (6th Sunday after Trinity, Skatval, p. 11) '2 Peder Baars Barn Ole, Test: Mali [B?]ølvang, Berit Wing, Ole Skiervold, Jon Hofstad, Jan Winge'; 27 Mar 1766 (Skatval, p. 140) 'Peder Baars B. Marit, Test: Gunil Wibstad, Brude[?] Nordland, Malli Walstad, Johan Wibstad, Anders Baar'. Neither names the mother or a patronymic, and no Röan sponsor appears (so the sponsors give no support to Peder Arntsen x Berethe Jacobsdatter Röan). Patronymic still open. ¶11

Patronymic solved: the 'Peder Baar' of the 1756-68 Skatval baptisms is Peder Erichsen, not Peder Arntsen. The 1790 skifte names Peder Pedersen (21, so b. c.1769), Ole Pedersen (of age, on the farm; the Ole bapt. 25 Jul 1756), Mali Pedersdatter (married to Ole Christophersen, as the Mali of 1758 married in 1786) and Marithe Pedersdatter (the Marit bapt. 27 Mar 1766, married Ole Arntsen Nordlandet 1792) as his children by his second marriage with the widow Mali Olsdatter. His first marriage (to Mali Andersdatter, d. c.1752) left the son Anders Pedersen (7 in 1752) and a deceased son, evidently the Ole Pedersen (3½ in 1752), whose daughter Mali Olsdatter (8) inherited. He is the Peder Erichsen, 'Fledføring', of Baar buried 14 Jun 1790 aged 79 (b. c.1711), 17 days before the skifte; birth year changed from ABT 1735 to ABT 1711 and death set BEF 14 JUN 1790 on that basis. The note of that the 1792 quitclaim reads 'efter Faderen' is right: the father was Peder Erichsen. Peder Arntsen x Berethe Jacobsdatter Röan (1755) are not this family. Name changed to Peder Erichsen on this skifte. Graded link unchanged (Confirmed, on his link to Peder Pedersen), now resting also on the skifte naming 'Sønnen Peder Pedersen 21 aar gl.' at Baar. ¶21

The 'Peder Baar' child baptised 16 Jul 1758 at Skatval was named Mali (16371 p. 30, scan read: 'Peder Baars barn Mali', sponsors Mali Lille Walstad, Siri Walstad, Gunild [?], Jon [?]); Ingbor, 1 Feb 1761 (16371 p. 59: sponsors Gunild Walstad[?], Ingbor Baar[?], Giertru Winnan[?], Ole Hegge[?] and one more). A daughter Mali born 1758, two years after Peder Erichsen's daughter Mali and his first wife Mali Andersdatter had died, fits Peder Erichsen and his second wife Mali Olsdatter (b. c.1724, buried from Baar 1791) by the naming custom; this is an inference only. Neither baptism gives a patronymic or the mother. Patronymic remains unresolved; the sponsors do not settle it. ¶9

In the 1762 extra-tax summary for Værnes/Skatval only one householder is listed at Baar: '51. Baard Peder', 6 persons (48401 view 81, first names only). So all the 1758-68 'Peder Baar' baptisms probably belong to one man, but the list gives no patronymic. ¶13

The 1762 extra-tax list supports the identification of 'Peder Baar' with Peder Erichsen (the skifte of 1 Jul 1790, settles it). Skatval no. 51 'Baar', beneficeret (48401 viewer image 210): 'Peder og Hu[stru]' with children over 12 'Anders' and 'Ole', and three maids. Anders and Ole match Peder Erichsen's sons by Mali Andersdatter in the 1752 registration (Anders 7, Ole 3½, so 17 and 14 in 1762). This is the only household at Baar in 1762, so no second Peder (e.g. Peder Arntsen) lived there between the 1761 and 1768 baptisms. ¶19

Peder of Baar (Baa/Bå), Skatval, Stjørdal: father of a son Peder baptised 16 Oct 1768 (index; no mother indexed) and of another child baptised 27 Mar 1766 (not yet opened). His own patronymic is not yet known; birth year estimated. ¶1

Other Baar entries in the 1781-97 index: Mali Pedersdatter, pige, Baar, married Ole Christophersen of Winnan 12 Oct 1786 (pv00000003290761; probably the Mali of 1758); Marite Pedersdatter, pige, Baar, married the widower Ole Arentsen of Nordlandet 18 Jun 1792 (pv00000003291821; probably the Marit of 1766); Ole Pedersen, ungkarl soldat, Baar, married Giertrud Olsdatter of Hegge 4 Jun 1787, sureties 'Peder Baar' and Ole Hegge (pv00000003290882) - so a Peder of Baar, the groom's father or a brother, was alive in 1787. None names a father's patronymic. ¶10

He (or his estate) was evidently dead by 1 Jul 1790, if the skifte of that date was after the children's father, as the 1792 quitclaim seems to say. Not entered as a death date because the reading is uncertain. ¶18

The skifte (Stjør- og Verdal skifteprotokoll 10, 24763 p. 222 bottom - 223, scan): 'Anno 1790 den 1te Julii blev paa Selveiergaarden Baard i Schatvolds Annex og Nordahlens [?] Tinglag' registration and valuation held 'efter den samme Tid ved Døden afgangne Mand Peder Erichsen' between 'den efterlevende Enke Mali Olsdatter' on the one side and 'den Afdødes... avlede og efterladte Børn' on the other, namely 'af 1te Egteskab: 1. Sønnen Anders Pedersen fuldmyndig, 2. Sønne-datteren Mali Olsdatter 8 aar gl.' and 'af 2det Egteskab med den igienlevende Enke: 3. Sønnen Ole Pedersen, fuldmyndig og boende paa Gaarden, 4. Sønnen Peder Pedersen 21 aar gl., 5. Datteren Mali Pedersdatter gift med Ole Christophersen Wal[stad]trøen, 6. Datteren Marithe Pedersdatter [age not clearly read]'. The widow's lagverge was Christian Gravold; Jon Pedersen Schiervold was appointed curator and guardian for the son Peder Pedersen and the granddaughter Mali Olsdatter, and the brother Ole Pedersen Baard guardian for Marithe Pedersdatter (the same guardians who paid them out in the June 1792 quitclaims, pantebok C-3 fol. 614b). Valuers Otter Wibstad and Ole Christophersen Winge. In the debts on p. 223, items 12-14 are shares 'tilfalden udi Arv efter Faderen Peder Erichsen' (to the sons Anders and Ole), showing the settlement was continued after a later death (the session of 13 Mar 1791, after the widow's burial of 8 Feb 1791, not read). ¶20

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Probable father of Peder Pedersen (see his page), the soldier 'of Baa/Baer' who married Gullov Pedersdatter in 1798 and was buried in 1806 aged 37 (b. about 1769) with the old farm name Baar added in the index. ¶2

Upgrade: Probable -> Confirmed, graded on his link to his son Peder Pedersen (see his page). The relationship is named in the baptism of 16 Oct 1768 at Skatval ('Peder of Baar', son Peder). The new record bridges that child to the man in the tree: two quitclaims of June 1792 in pantebok C-3 fol. 614b (indexed in the Stjørdal panteregister 22318, image 21). (1) 'Peder Pedersen Baard' acknowledges that his guardians (formyndere) Jon Pedersen Schiervold and Ole Pedersen Baard have paid him what fell to him under a skifte brev of 13 March [1791?]; being under guardianship he was a minor, i.e. born after about 1767. (2) Ole Arntsen Nordlandet acknowledges for his wife Marit Pedersdatter (the 'Marite Pedersdatter, pige, Baar' he married in 1792) that her guardian Ole Pedersen Baard has paid her share under the skifte brev 'efter [Faderen?] sal.' of 1 Jul 1790 and 13 Mar 1791 (the word read as Faderen is uncertain). So in 1790-92 Peder Pedersen, Marit Pedersdatter (bapt. 27 Mar 1766, 'Peder Baar's' child) and Ole Pedersen Baar (bapt. 25 Jul 1756, 'Peder Baar's' child) shared one estate settlement at Baar, with Ole as guardian of the younger two. Continuity: baptism 1768 -> minor heir at Baar 1791-92 -> 'soldier of Baa' who married Gullov Pedersdatter in 1798 -> buried 1806 aged 37 (b. about 1769) with the farm Baar added; same farm, gaps of 6-24 years bridged by records at 1791-92 and 1798, and no rival Peder Pedersen at Baar (only one householder there in 1762). The father's patronymic (Erichsen or Arntsen) is still open; the skifte of 1 Jul 1790 / 13 Mar 1791 (Stjør- og Verdal skifteprotokoll 10, source 24763, somewhere in views 236-246, not yet found) would name him, his widow and all heirs. ¶17

Corrections 1

Correction to the lead: the quitclaim of pantebok C-1 fol. 144b (no. 1, Halsen ting 21 Jun 1779) reads 'At min Hustrues Lisbet Olsdatter Arnstad hendes Formynder Peder Erichsen Baar haver mig... [her inheritance] efter Skifte Brev af 22 Octob. 1772... betalt', signed Hans Joensen Hegge. So Peder Erichsen of Baar paid out the inheritance as GUARDIAN of Lisbet Olsdatter (of Arnstad), not as her father; the entry gives no weight toward him (rather than Peder Arntsen) as the 'Peder Baar' of the 1756-68 baptisms. It shows Peder Erichsen alive and at Baar in June 1779. ¶16

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

The parents of the other Baar candidate are now known. Probate 4 Jul 1743 at Baar, Skatval, after Berit Pedersdatter (the typed register reads 'Berit Pedersdatter og mann Arnt Joensen'). Her heirs: '1. Sønnen Peder Arntsen paa gaarden' (of age, so b. by c.1718) and '2. Berit Arntsdatter 13 aar' (13 years old), with Peder acting as guardian for his sister (24753 fol. 46b, scan read). So Peder Arntsen of Baar was the son of Arnt Jonsen and Berit Pedersdatter. This settles nothing for this person until the Baar patronymic is solved. Do not add them as his parents. ¶14

Searches that found nothing 1

The confirmation route will not settle this: the 1781-97 Stjørdal register (16372) lists confirmands by first name and farm only (e.g. Værnes, 10 Jun 1787, p. 164-165), with no father's name. ¶12

Open questions and the next record to read 5

Next records: the Skatval baptism scans for 1756-68 in Stjørdal 16371 may name sponsors who are kin (Röan people would point to Berethe Jacobsdatter); the confirmation of Peder Pedersen about 1784-86 and any skifte after Peder Arntsen or Berethe Jacobsdatter (Stjør- og Verdal registers checked for 1743-84 show none at Baar after 1752; 24763 (1785-96) and 24764 (1796-1802) have no register and must be paged); the skifte after Mali Andersdatter, 24753 fol. 449b, to see whether Peder Erichsen's children were grown by 1752. ¶7

Lead (panteregister sweep): the quitclaim list ('Afkald') of the Stjørdal tinglag panteregister 1762-1800 (source 22318, image 15) has 'Hans Joensen Hegge paa sin Hustrues [vegne] til Peder Erichsen Baard', pantebok C-1 fol. 144 (about 1778; source 19755, about view 145). Hans Jonsen Hegge gave a quitclaim for his wife's inheritance to Peder Erichsen of Baar, so Peder Erichsen was alive and at Baar about 1778, and very probably paid out an inheritance to his daughter (Hans's wife). With the 1758 'Peder Baar' daughter named Mali (Peder Erichsen's first wife was Mali Andersdatter, d. 1752), this weighs toward Peder Erichsen, not Peder Arntsen, as the 'Peder Baar' of the 1756-68 baptisms, but it does not prove it. Read fol. 144 for the wife's name and the estate she inherited from. Baar itself was not found among the 'Baa-' farm pages of 22318 (images 24-25 are Baaraas and Baarset); Skatval farms may be in the Skatval tinglag registers 25731-25732. ¶15
