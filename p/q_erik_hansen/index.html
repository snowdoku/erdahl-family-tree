About the family

His son Lars's baptism in February 1782 (entered simply as 'Erich, Schielstadgierdet') had as godparents Thomas of Raaen, Ingeborg of Raaen, Anders of Skjelstad, Johan of Leerfald and Marit of Kylloe. His daughter Mali's baptism on 7 Nov 1794 names both parents and has as godparents Giertrud of Bræmset, Gunnil of Skjelstad, Elen of Skjelstad, Anders of Skjelstad and Lasse of Skjelstad. ¶3

Children at Christloch before the move to Skjelstadgjerdet (indexed Stjørdal register 1755-1780, Hegre): Hans, baptised 15 Jan 1775, father 'Eric, Christloch' (p. 254 no. 3) - the first son, named, as the Trøndelag pattern expects, for the paternal grandfather Hans; and Lars, baptised 29 Sep 1776, father 'Eric, Christloch' (p. 274) - the second son, named for the maternal grandfather Lars. Stjørdalsboka's list (Lars 1776, died 1777; Lars 1782; Mali 1794) matches: the name Lars was given again to the son baptised in February 1782 at Skjelstadgjerdet, who is Lars Eriksen. A 'Lars Erichsen', born 1776, was buried at Hegre on 9 Mar 1777 from 'Aunet' (pg00000007159615); the residence differs from Christloch and the identification is not certain. The surety Ole Hansen may be Erik's brother (lead only). His father was evidently named Hans; no record yet read names him. ¶8

Life story

Marriage found: Stjørdal ministerialbok 1755-1780 (Digitalarkivet 16371), marriage section, canvas 379, right page (p. 376, year heading 1774), 2nd entry - the canvas that skipped as a supposed duplicate; it covers the weeks between Lætare and Palm Sunday 1774. 'Samme dag trolovet ved Wernes: ungkarl og tambour af Hr. Capitain von Coldevins Compagnie Erich Hansøn Christlock, med Pigen Mali Larsd[atte]r', who had the military session's permission to marry and to settle as a cottager at Christlock; sureties ('cavered') Ole Hansøn Hoset[?] and Lars Iversøn Morset[?] (farm names uncertain; 'Børset' possible for the second). Margin: 'Copuleret ved Hegre d. 5te April'. The betrothal is dated by the preceding entries to about 19 March 1774 and the wedding was at Hegre on 5 April 1774. This is the 1774 marriage that Stjørdalsboka V 1 gives for Erik Hansen (b. 1745) and Mali Larsdatter, and both were single ('ungkarl', 'pige'), agreeing with the 1801 census, where both are in their first marriage. He was then a drummer (tambour) in Captain von Coldevin's company. ¶7

1762 extra tax list (ekstraskatt; Digitalarkivet 48401, Hegra 'old dragoon quarters' views 239 and 244-246): it gives mostly first names only. The farm Schielstad (no. 37) had Haagen and his wife as farmers; the cottagers were Halvor[?] and his wife, and Lars and his wife with Anders. No Erik or Hans was found on the farms read (Leerfald, Hegre, Trøyta, Kylloe, Græset, Skjelstad, Klefven, Hamer, Qvaal, Bangen, Wennen, Moe, Berg, Mæhle). Christloch and Almoen were not reached. ¶10

Cottager and shoemaker at Skjelstad(gjerdet). 1801 census: 54, in his 1st marriage, with wife Mali Larsdatter 50 and daughter Mali 6 (bapt. 7 Nov 1794 as a child of Erich Hanssen and Mali Larsdatter of Skjelstadgjerdet). Buried 1806 at Rolseng-gjerdet, born 1746. ¶1

The 1801 census gives his occupation in full: 'Huusmand med jord og skomager' - a cottager with land who also worked as a shoemaker. A trade on top of the croft was the usual way a Hegra husmann kept a family of five fed. ¶2

The 'Anders of Skjelstad' who appears at both baptisms is probably Anders Pedersen, born about 1718, husmand at Skjelstadgjerdet, buried 13 Oct 1793 - the other cottager on the same holding. ¶4

Burial (per index, Hegre parish p. 267): buried 1 Jun 1806, 'Erich Hansen', huusmand (cottager), of Rolseng gjerdet, aged 60, so born about 1745–46. ¶6

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 alternative set aside · 2 searches with no result

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Ruled out: the marriage of an 'Erich Hansen, Ungkarl soldat' to Margrete Kjelsdatter of Stoken on 17 June 1784 is a different man. This Erik's wife was Mali Larsdatter and their son was already born in February 1782. ¶5

Searches that found nothing 2

Search for his father Hans, all negative. (1) The Christloch probate of 13 Jul 1753 for Giertru Olsdatter, wife of Ole Iversen (Stjør- og Verdal skifteprotokoll 3, source 24753, fol. 499a), lists five children: Iver Olsen, married and living on the farm; Ole Olsen, married and a husmann (cottager) under Christloch; Giertrud 36; Berethe 32, in service at Ingstad; and Kirstie 30. No Hans appears, so Erik was not of the farm family. (2) The 1801 census for Christloch has the households of Ole Guttormsen, Haagen Iversen and Christen Olsen (b. 1725), with no Hans. (3) Ole Hansen of Hoset, Hegra: his probate of 27 May 1778 (skifteprotokoll 8, source 24759, fol. 8b) names his widow Mali Olsdatter and his children Hans Olsen 14, Marithe and Berethe. A Hans Joensen and Lars Joensen Raaen are named in the opening lines, and Peder Pedersen Schielstad is present. No Erik Hansen appears in the part read. This Ole Hansen may be the surety 'Ole Hansøn Hoset' at Erik's 1774 betrothal, but no kinship is shown. ¶9

Probate search, negative: no estate after Erik Hansen (d. May 1806, Rolseng-gjerdet) was found. The typed register of Stjør- og Verdal skifteprotokoll 12 (24765) has no 1806-1808 Stjørdal entries at all, the register of no. 19 (57508, 1808-13) has none for him or his widow Mali Larsdatter, and the Digitalarkivet probate index (Eri*/Hans*, 1806-09) has no Stjørdal hit. Source id 57502, the unidentified volume in the 57500-57515 block, returns 404. Graded on his link to Lars Eriksen: stays Probable. A probate naming 'Søn Lars Erichsen, dragon' would settle it; the Stjørdal volume for 1806-08, if it survives, is still unidentified. ¶11
