About the family

Son: Gottlieb Friedrich Knodel, who died at Friedenstal on 18 June 1856, is recorded as the son of Johann Gottlieb, born at 'Vaihingner Wurtt.' (Vaihingen, Württemberg), with the remark 'not a colonist' (FHL 1884101/1 p.186 no.35). A son born in Württemberg shows that this family came to Bessarabia directly from Württemberg, and later than the Friedenstal colonists. ¶2

Parents: Christoph Ludwig Knodel (b. Vaihingen 6 Feb 1765, buried there 14 Aug 1833) and his first wife Jacobina Friederike (married 25 Jan 1791). Linked at Probable: the baptism, the 1822 marriage and his brother Johann Christoph's 1854 burial all name this couple. The index writes the father both as 'Ludwig Christoph' (1795/96 baptism) and 'Christoph Ludwig' (1794 baptism, 1822 marriages, 1854 burial); one couple. Generation rule: Ludwig (see his page, Probable) to Johann Gottlieb is a Speculative link; Johann Gottlieb to his parents rests on Probable evidence, so no second Speculative generation is added. ¶10

Son Ludwig (still Speculative, but stronger): the Güglingen couple Johann Gottlieb x Luise Katharine had a son baptised Franz Ludwig (Ancestry dbId 9866, record 97829178; no date indexed). Ludwig Knodel of Friedenstal (see his page) also named his eldest sons Ludwig and Johann Ludwig, and Ludwig echoes the grandfather Christoph Ludwig. ¶11a

Children, with dates (FamilySearch indexes read by the signed-in lane, S0111 and S0118, Oct 2026): Gottlieb Friedrich, baptised 5 May 1823 at Vaihingen (mother Luisa Catharina); and at Güglingen, Carl Friederich, born 11 March 1826, baptised 17 March, died 25 March 1826; Franz Ludwig, born 21 February 1828, baptised 29 February 1828 (mother Luise Katharine Lindenmeier); Karoline Wilhelmine, born 26 May 1829, baptised 28 May 1829; Johann Gottlieb, born 24 April 1834, baptised 1 May 1834 (mother Luise Catharine Lindenmaier); Gottlob Wilhelm, born 25 January 1837, baptised 5 February 1837. No son Ludwig born 1830-40 is indexed to this couple; Franz Ludwig is their only Ludwig. Index entries only; no images. ¶20

Life story

Marriage: Johann Gottlieb Knodel married Luise Catharina (surname not indexed) at Vaihingen an der Enz on 4 July 1822, two days after his brother Johann Christoph married Magdalena Dorothea there (2 July 1822). The index relatives are 'Christoph Ludwig, Jacob Friedrica, Luise Catharina': the groom's parents and the bride, by the index's column order (the bride's role is inferred, not read). Index entry only; image not seen; S0110 queued for the signed-in lane. ¶8

Güglingen, 1826-34 (Probable as the same household): a couple Johann Gottlieb Knodel and Luise Katharine (also 'Louise Katharine', 'Katharina') is indexed at Güglingen, Oberamt Brackenheim, about 20 km north of Vaihingen: burial of son Carl Friederich 27 March 1826; baptism of Karoline Wilhelmine 28 May 1829; baptism of Johann Gottlieb 1 May 1834; and a baptism of Franz Ludwig, undated in the index. No other marriage of a Johann Gottlieb Knodel to a Luise Catharina is indexed, and his half-brother Ludwig Friedrich also married in the Brackenheim district (1839) and had a son baptised at Güglingen in 1842. No burial of Johann Gottlieb or Luise Catharina in Württemberg was returned by the index searches, which fits emigration after 1834. Index entry only; image not seen; S0109-S0111 queued for the signed-in lane. ¶9

In Russia by 1841 (new, S0111): a son Carl Friedrich Knodel is indexed as born in 1841 at 'Freudenthal, Russia' to Gottlieb Knodel and Louisa Lindemeier (FamilySearch 'Russia, Lutheran Births and Baptisms, 1755-1917', source detail 'p539-6'). The mother's rare maiden name matches, so this is almost certainly the same couple (an inference; the index gives no birthplace for the parents). It narrows the family's emigration from 'between 1837 and 1856' to between February 1837 (Gottlob Wilhelm's baptism at Güglingen) and 1841, and shows them in South Russia when Franz Ludwig was about 13. 'Freudenthal' may be Freudental in the Glückstal district (Kherson government, near Odessa) or an index form of Friedenstal in Bessarabia. No Carl Knodel appears in the Bessarabian Consistory extracts of 1835-88, which leans toward Freudental near Odessa, but that is unproved. 'p539-6' is presumably page 539, entry 6 of the St Petersburg Consistory duplicate for 1841; that entry has not been read. ¶21

Freudental, 1841 (St Petersburg Consistory extracts, odessa3 Freudental parish files): 'Knodel, Carl Friedrich', born 8 Jan 1841 at Freudental, parents Johann Gottlieb and Louisa Catharina Lindemeie[r], FHL 1883193/1 p.538 no.6, remark 'EL, PBN:59'; and his death, 'Knodel, Carl Friedrich', 6 Jul 1841 at Freudental, parents Johann Gottlieb and Louisa Catharina Lindeman[n], FHL 1883193/1 p.574 no.52 (the extract prints the age as '0y.0m.1d.', which disagrees with the birth date; one of the two is misread; remark 'Fa: Pastor', meaning unclear). This settles the colony of the 1841 FamilySearch index entry (p539-6): it is Freudental in the Odessa district of Kherson province, seat of Freudental parish, not Friedenstal in Bessarabia. 'PBN:59' is probably the family's number in the Freudental parish family book. The full given names Johann Gottlieb and Louisa Catharina match the Vaihingen marriage of 1822, so the Vaihingen and Güglingen family is proved to have settled in South Russia by January 1841. ¶22

Freudental 1841 (images read 1 Nov 2026): Carl Friedrich Knodel was born at Freudental (Odessa district) on 8 Jan 1841 and baptised 15 Jan, son of 'Gottlieb Knodel und seine Ehefrau Louisa geb. Lindemeier, beide [Lutheran] — Ausländer' (foreign nationals); godparents the colonists Friedrich Jauch(?), Carl Friedrich Finck(?) and Catharina Schmidt (FHL 1883193/1 p.538 no.6). He died there on 6 Jul 1841 aged 6 months, 'Sohn des Ausländers Johann Gottlieb Knodel, Weber[?], u. s. Ehefrau Louisa Catharina geb. Lindemann' (p.574 no.52). So Louisa Catharina was alive in July 1841, and Johann Gottlieb was then a foreign national, not an enrolled colonist. Ludwig Knodel (see his page) is likewise styled 'Ausländer' at Friedenstal in 1864: consistent with kinship, not proof of it. ¶24

Grossliebental 1843 (image read 1 Nov 2026): the Consistory duplicate lists marriage no.16, 9 [July] 1843, 'Knodel, Johann Gottlieb, Klöstitzer[?] Kolonist, mit Catharina Fetzer geborene Straub' (FHL 1882594/2 p.319 no.16; main register p.76 no.13). The duplicate is a names-only summary: it gives no age, birthplace, widower status or parents, so it cannot by itself show that this groom is the Freudental man of 1841. The colony word reads most likely 'Klöstitzer' (about 80% sure), which fits the Klöstitz household of Gottlieb Knodel and Katharina Straub in 1845-48. Not yet proved to be the same man: that needs Louisa Catharina's death between July 1841 and July 1843, or the parish main register entry. ¶25

Status: Johann Gottlieb does not appear in any indexed Friedenstal family book (Familienbuch, the register of enrolled colonist households) of 1847 or 1841-60, although he died there in 1859. This fits the 'not a colonist' remark on his son: the household lived at Friedenstal without being enrolled as colonists. ¶3

Context, unproved: the Leonberg Knodels of Friedenstal descend, according to a compiled tree (Kolewe, GEDBAS; a lead only), from Hans David Knodel of Ensingen (b. 1745). Johann Gottlieb is not among that tree's children of Johann Adam Knodel (b. 1768), so if he was from Ensingen he was probably a cousin of the Leonberg brothers' father rather than a brother. His own parents are not known. ¶5

Death: Johann Gottlieb Knodel died at Friedenstal, Bessarabia, on 12 April 1859 (date as extracted; the registers used the Julian calendar), aged 63 years 29 days, so born about 14 March 1796. His birthplace was extracted as 'Unfingen? in Wuerttemberg'; the extractor's query mark shows the word was unclear. In German script a long s is easily misread as f, so one possible reading is Ensingen, Oberamt Vaihingen (now part of Vaihingen an der Enz); this is a guess that no record has yet checked. Index extract of the St Petersburg Consistory duplicate, FHL film 1884077/1 p.684 no.8; the image has not been seen. ¶1

Death (extract): the St Petersburg Consistory extract records the death of Johann Gottlieb Knodel at Friedenstal on 12 Apr 1859, aged 63 years 29 days, birthplace printed 'Unfingen? in Wuttemberg' (the question mark is in the extract), parents not given (FHL 1884077/1 p.684 no.8). The age gives a birth on about 14 Mar 1796, which matches his Vaihingen baptism. 'Unfingen?' is evidently the transcriber's uncertain reading of the place, perhaps of Vaihingen; his birth field is not changed. The image would settle the reading. ¶13

Güglingen children, fuller dates (Oct 2026; index entries only, images not seen): Carl Friederich, born 11 March 1826, baptised 17 March 1826 and buried 27 March 1826 (9866 record 40579857; 61023 record 5843493); Franz Ludwig, no date indexed, birth year 1828 by the index's birth-year filter, so born about 1827 to about August 1828 given his sister's birth in May 1829 (9866 record 97829178); Karoline Wilhelmine, born 26 May 1829, baptised 28 May 1829 (9866 record 9891080; 61023 record 5646830); Johann Gottlieb, baptised 1 May 1834 (9866 record 71162833; 61023 record 5647102); and Gottlob Wilhelm, baptised 5 February 1837, parents Johann Gottlieb x Luise Catharine (9866 record 71178050). All are recorded at Güglingen. ¶14

Emigration (inferred, Oct 2026): the baptism of his son Gottlob Wilhelm at Güglingen on 5 February 1837 shows the family was still in Württemberg in 1837. Their first trace in Bessarabia is the death of his son Gottlieb Friedrich at Friedenstal on 18 June 1856, so they arrived between 1837 and 1856. No Carl, Franz or Gottlob Knodel appears in the Bessarabian Consistory extracts of 1835-88. ¶15

Birthplace, the 'Unfingen?' reading (Oct 2026): his Friedenstal death extract gives his birthplace as 'Unfingen? in Wuerttemberg'. No Württemberg place of that name has been found. There is no Wikipedia article for Unfingen, and Meyers Gazetteer has no entry (the nearest is Undingen, a different place). The GOV gazetteer, which would be decisive, is behind a bot check and is queued for David as hand search H0161. The best explanation is a misreading of Ensingen (Oberamt Vaihingen): in German script E and U are easily confused, and a long s reads as f. Ensingen is a guess and is unproved. The Vaihingen baptism of 1795 points to Vaihingen an der Enz itself, and Ensingen is a village of the same district (Oberamt). The original death entry (FHL 1884077/1 = DGS 4280477, p.684 no.8) has not been read. ¶17

Marriage, the bride's surname and parents (S0110, read on FamilySearch by the signed-in lane, Oct 2026): the Vaihingen an der Enz register ('Taufen (Uneheliche), u Heiraten 1808-1895', p.70) is indexed with the marriage on 4 July 1822 of Johann Gottlieb Knodel, son of Christoph Ludwig Knodel and Jacobina Friederica (indexed 'Jacob Friedrica'), and Luise Catharina Lindenmajer, daughter of Johann Melchior Lindenmajer and Luisa Catharina. The same marriage is indexed separately from the register of Bietigheim, the bride's home parish: groom Johann Gottlieb Knodel, born 1796 at 'Naihingen' (an index misreading of Vaihingen), father Christoph Ludwig Knodel; bride Louise Katharine Lindenmaier, born 1805 at Bietigheim, daughter of Johann Melchior Lindenmaier and Katharina Kuffer. Two registers of two parishes, indexed independently, agree on the couple and the groom's father. This confirms that Luise Catharina was the bride, which an earlier note only inferred from the index's column order. The bride's mother is given as Luisa Catharina in the Vaihingen index and as Katharina Kuffer in the Bietigheim index; the Vaihingen form may repeat the bride's own name in error (an inference). Index entries only; FamilySearch has no images, and the groom's occupation and the witnesses are still unread (Ancestry 61023 image 1184564-00908, or Archion). ¶18

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Speculative link: he is entered as the father of Ludwig Knodel (see his page) because he is the better of two candidate fathers, not because any record names Ludwig's parents. For: (1) Ludwig's household was also 'not a colonist' (death of his son Ludwig, 24 Jan 1870), whereas the other Friedenstal Knodels, the Leonberg/Lwówek brothers, are marked 'Friedensthal kol.' (colonist) in 1861-64; a colonist's sons normally inherited that status, so a son of that family would be expected to be a colonist (a general rule, not shown by a record for this family). ¶4a

Records that disagree 1

Birth year, a further index: the Bietigheim marriage index gives the groom's birth year as 1796, agreeing with the age at his death (63 years 29 days on 12 April 1859) rather than with the Vaihingen baptism index (14 March 1795). Two of three sources now point to 1796, though both of them are derived figures (a stated age and an index field), while 1795 is the indexed baptism date itself. The birth field is left at 14 MAR 1795 until the baptism image (film 1184563-00049) is read. ¶19

Corrections 3

Correction: his birthplace was previously entered as 'Württemberg (extract reads Unfingen?; possibly Ensingen)'. He is now identified, at Probable, with Johann Gottlieb Knodel baptised at Vaihingen an der Enz, son of Christoph Ludwig Knodel and Jacobina Friederike. The evidence: (1) the Vaihingen baptism is indexed under 14 March, the same day and month as the birth date implied by his Friedenstal death age (63 years 29 days on 12 April 1859 = 14 March 1796; the death date is Old Style (Julian), so the match assumes the pastor subtracted the dates without converting the calendar, which was the usual practice); (2) his son Gottlieb Friedrich, who died at Friedenstal in 1856 and was 'born Vaihingen', son of Johann Gottlieb, is indexed as baptised at Vaihingen on 5 May 1823 to Johann Gottlieb and Luisa Catharina; (3) that couple married at Vaihingen on 4 July 1822, and the groom's parents are again Christoph Ludwig and Jacobina Friederike; (4) no other Johann Gottlieb Knodel of about this age with a wife Luise Catharina is in the index. Two other Vaihingen boys of about this age were checked: Johann Gottlieb Friedrich, b. 1792, son of Johann Michael, married there in 1818 and 1848 and was buried there in 1849; and 'Joann' Gottlieb, bapt. 2 February 1794, son of Johannes and Gottliebin (record 4254825), who has no later index entry but whose birth date does not fit the death age; (5) 'Unfingen?' is the extractor's uncertain reading and can stand for 'Vaihingen' (same -ingen ending and similar length in German script) as well as for Ensingen. Index entry only; image not seen; S0109-S0111 queued for the signed-in lane. ¶6

Correction: birth year. The Ancestry index gives the baptism as 14 March 1795, but 1796 is preferred. The death age gives 14 March 1796; his full brother Johann Christoph was baptised on 13 October 1794, only five months before a March 1795 date; and the entry sits on film image 1184563-00049, fifteen images after October 1794 but only four before a baptism of 22 July 1796 (the film runs about 7-11 images a year in 1792-1804, and 19 images cover October 1794 to July 1796), which places it about February-March 1796. The year 1795 is therefore probably an indexing slip. This is inference from the index; the image (S0109) will settle the year and give the godparents. ¶7

Correction (Oct 2026): his birth is now entered as 14 March 1795, replacing 14 March 1796. The 1796 date was never read in any record. It was worked out from his age at death (63 years 29 days on 12 April 1859 at Friedenstal). The only Vaihingen record of his birth, Ancestry dbId 61023 record 4255028, indexes his baptism as 14 March 1795 (Taufe, 'Enz u. Vaihingen', parents Ludwig Christoph and Jacobina Friederike). No Johann Gottlieb Knodel baptism dated 1796 was found in 300 rows of the same index. The baptism record is preferred because it was written when he was born, while the age at death was given 63 years later, probably by his family, in another country. The two sources agree exactly on the day, 14 March, which suggests that the pastor in 1859 knew the birthday and made a one-year slip in the year (or the death extract's '63' is a copying slip). The date 14 March is the baptism date in the index; the birth was the same day or a day or two earlier. The 1795 year still rests on an index entry, not the image (film image 1184563-00049). The image would settle it. His record id keeps '1796' because ids are never renamed. ¶16

Alternatives considered and set aside 2

Caution: the extractors record these remarks unevenly. The 'Friedensthal kol.' remarks appear only on deaths outside Friedenstal, and most Friedenstal Knodel entries, including Ludwig's son Johann (d. 1871), carry no remark at all, so the contrast rests on two 'not a colonist' entries. (2) Ludwig, like Johann Gottlieb, is absent from every indexed Friedenstal family book. (3) Ludwig, married 1860 and so born about 1832-40, is not among the Bessarabian births of 1833-49 or the Sanniki (Poland) births, which fits a birth in Württemberg. (4) Ludwig's sons were named Ludwig, Johann Ludwig and Gottlieb, echoing Johann Gottlieb; none was named after Friedrich Christoph, the father of the Leonberg brothers. (5) Ludwig married in February 1860, ten months after Johann Gottlieb's death. Against or missing: no record yet names a son Ludwig, and Johann Gottlieb's wife is unknown. ¶4b

Possible second marriage (candidate, unproved): the Grossliebental parish extracts record 'Knodel, Johann Gottlieb' marrying 'Fetzer, Catharina', a widow née Straub, at Grossliebental on 9 Jul 1843 (FHL 1882594/2 p.319 no.16; remark 'Wf - (nee Straub)'). Grossliebental is the neighbouring parish to Freudental. Her first husband was probably Johann Leonhard Fetzer, who died at Grossliebental on 3 Dec 1840, aged 50, born at Szekitsch, Hungary (FHL 1883190/3 p.405 no.82). The couple 'Gottlieb Knodel x Katharina Straub' then appear in Klöstitz parish, Bessarabia: sons Christoph, born 23 Dec 1845 at Klöstitz (FHL 1882638/1 p.49 no.4), and Christian, born 26 Feb 1848 (FHL 1882641/2 p.261 no.20); Katharina Knodel née Straub, wife of Gottlieb, died at Klöstitz on 21 Jul 1848 (FHL 1882641/2 p.286 no.60). Earlier workers treated this Klöstitz household as a separate Knodel family. If the 1843 groom is this Johann Gottlieb, then Luise Catharina died between Jul 1841 and Jul 1843, and the family's route was Freudental (1841) to Grossliebental (1843) to Klöstitz (1845-48) to Friedenstal (by 1856). For: same full given name, in the next parish two years after the 1841 Freudental entries; no other Johann Gottlieb Knodel is found in the odessa3 Consistory extracts of the 1840s for Freudental, Grossliebental, Glückstal, Odessa, Neusatz, Rohrbach-Worms or Bessarabia (the other Gottlieb Knodels of the 1840s are children, or the Leonberg-family Gottlieb born 1826, who married Barbara Ackermann at Arzis on 3 Apr 1847 while Katharina Straub was still alive, FHL 1882639/1 p.147 no.7). Against or unknown: the extract does not give the groom's age, origin or status (widower or not), and Luise Catharina's death is not in the Freudental or Grossliebental death extracts 1841-43. ¶23a
