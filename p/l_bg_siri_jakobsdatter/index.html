Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: Confirmed as the mother of Hans Olsen (b. 25 Mar 1788). The c.1840-42 Skudenes household register (source 1653, Akre, canvas 273, re-read from the saved crop) names his parents as 'Gaardm. Ole Hansen Langager og h. Siri Jakobsdatter', and the 1801 census of Langager lists Hans, 13, as a son of Ole Hansen and Siri Jacobsdatter. Her own parents are unknown. ¶3

Searches that found nothing 1

1758 list (negative): no Siri (Ziri) Jakobsdatter aged 0-2 was found in the 1758 soul register of Skudenes parish (source 4501, views 1-13 = sheet pages 1-25, folios 87-99, read at overview scale: Skudenes main parish, Åkra and Ferkingstad annexes and the start of Bokn; any later Bokn pages beyond view 13 were not read). Born about 1757, she may have been born after the list was drawn up, or have lived outside the parish. Jacob households seen at Langager: Jacob Helgesen, 30, and Gunele Simensdatter, 33 (children Sven 5 and Marte 2), not hers on this evidence. ¶2
