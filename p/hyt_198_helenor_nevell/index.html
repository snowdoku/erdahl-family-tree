Life story

Maiden name: still unknown after this search. The abstracts of John Nevell's will (1537, Archdeaconry of Essex, Bretton 69) and of Gilbert Nevell's (1550) in Canon Nevill's article (The Genealogist n.s. 35:84-85) name only 'Ellenor Nevell my wife', John's daughter Margaret, his brother Gilbert, and in Gilbert's will the brothers-in-law John Josselyng the younger of Mashbury and John Josselyng the elder of Chignal; no Josselyn first husband and no kin of Helenor's own family appear. Her will's 'Johan Joslyn my neece' may be a granddaughter ('niece' could then mean grandchild), so it gives no clue to her birth family. No Josselyn fine with a wife Helenor appears in Feet of Fines for Essex vol. 5 (1547-80), and vol. 4 (1423-1547) is not online. Test record unchanged: her registered will D/AER 5/86/1 (searchroom microfiche) and John Nevell's D/AER 6/69/1, read in full. ¶3

Essex Record Office catalogue: D/AER 5/86/1 is the 'Registered copy of will of Eleanor Nevell, widow', dated 20 May 1547 (register Bastwyck; 'Wills at Chelmsford' vol. I gives her forename as Helena; no abode is stated). It is available only on microfiche in the searchroom, not online. Gilbert Nevell's original will is D/ABW 27/33 (1550); its online image is a 400-pixel thumbnail, too small to read the brother-in-law clause. John Nevell's will is D/AER 6/69/1 (1537). Test record, made exact: D/AER 5/86/1 and D/ABW 27/33, read at the searchroom or from a FamilySearch film of the Archdeaconry of Essex registers. ¶2

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Graded on her link to her son John Josselyn. Will of 'Helenor Nevell wid.', 1547 (Archdeaconry of Essex, register Bastwick f. 36; abstract by Canon E. R. Nevill, The Genealogist n.s. 35:85): 'My son Thomas Joslyn £10 & a camblett jackett. My daughter Alice Nevell £10... my daughter Agnes the bed on which I doe lye. Johan Joslyn my neece £10 at day of marriage. The residue among my 3 sons John Joslyn, Symond & John the younger. John the Elder and Symond Exors. John the younger Supervisor'; witness Richard Joslyn, curate. She had married first a Josselyn (not named in the will) and second John Nevell of Fyfield, whose will of 1537 (Bretton 69) is witnessed by 'Ellenor Nevell my wife' and names his brother Gilbert Nevell executor; 'Elenor Nevill, widow' was taxed at Fyfield in 1539. Continuity to John in the tree: (1) Gilbert Nevell of Nalinghurst in Braintree, will 1550, calls 'John Josselyng the elder of Chykenayle' and 'John Josselyng the younger of Massheburie' his brothers-in-law; John Josselyn was taxed at Chignal Smealy in 1547, 1550/1 and 1551/2 and is the John of the tree (bur. Roxwell 1578/9); (2) her sons John and Symond are the John and Simon taxed together at Fyfield 16 June 1546, who married at Fyfield on 15 Jan. 1544/5 and 12 Oct. 1544, and her daughter Agnes the Agnes Joselyn who married John Turnishe there 14 Sept. 1544 (Fyfield register, NEHGR 71:32). There were two sons named John; ours is 'the elder', fixed by Gilbert's 'of Chykenayle'. ¶1a

Probable, not Confirmed: the wills were read only as abstracts in a printed article, and the abstract of Helenor's will gives no probate. ¶1b
