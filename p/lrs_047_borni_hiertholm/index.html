About the family

Siblings found: probate No. 67, Ytre Sogn 7a fol. 16b, 3 Sep 1750, on the farm Sætre[?] (reading of the farm name uncertain; 'proprietaire gods' in Lavik skipreide), after the unmarried 'Pige Haldis Niels Datter', divided among her heirs: 'Broder Gregorius Nielsen Sætre, myndig; 1te Søster Dordej Nielsdatter 44 aar gl.; 2de Søster Borni Nielsdatter gift med Joen Gulløvsen Indre Hiertholm; 3die Søster Mari Nielsdatter 3[?] aar gl.; 4de Søster Magdeli Nielsdatter gift med Henrich [?] Takle'. Read from the scan. This proves that Borni Nielsdatter, wife of Jon Gulløvsen of Indre Hjartholm, was a sister of Gregorius Nielsen of Sætre[?], Dordei (b. about 1706), Mari, Magdeli and Haldis Nielsdatter. Their father was a Niels (shared patronymic; he is not named in the entry and no father is linked yet). ¶11a

Parents: Niels Johansen of Sætre, Lavik skipreide (d. before 29 Jul 1748) and Synneve Gregoriusdatter. His probate No. 31 (Ytre Sogn 6b fol. 550b, 29 Jul 1748) names '2den Datter Borni Nielsd[atter] gift med Joen Gulløvsen Indre Hiertholm' among the children of the widow Synneve Gregoriusdatter and the deceased. Siblings: Gregorius Nielsen (of Sætre), Dordei (b. about 1706), Mari (b. about 1718), Magdeli (m. Anders Lassesen of Takle) and Haldis (b. about 1727, d. unmarried by 1750). This supersedes the 'Father (not found)' note. ¶12

Mother's probate: Synneve Gregoriusdatter's estate at Sætre, Aug 1759 (Ytre Sogn 7b No. 44, fol. 452b), names 'anden Datter Borni Niels Datter gift med Joen Gulløvsen Indre Hiertholm' among her heirs. ¶13

Life story

Name: Recorded as 'Mat[er]: Born[i]' (mother Borni; the last letters are a probable reading) in the baptism of her daughter Synneve, child of Jon Hiertholm, on 3 May 1749. ¶1

Patronymic: Probably Nielsdatter, but not proved. A 'Borni Nielsd[atter] Hiertholm' was godmother on 5 Jan 1748 to Lisbeth, daughter of Iver Botnen, and a 'Borni Niels D[atter]' of Hjartholm was godmother again in spring 1775 to a child of Colbein Hiertholm. She is the only Borni seen at Hjartholm, and Synneve named a daughter Bornie (23 in 1801), which fits a grandmother's name. A mother's surname is never given in these baptisms, so the identification rests on name and farm only; Synneve's parents' marriage record or a Hjartholm probate would settle it. ¶2

Name: Correction: her patronymic is Nielsdatter, now proved. The probate after her husband Jon Gulløvsen of Indre Hjartholm, 1-2 Oct 1773, calls her his widow 'Borne Niels datter'. Earlier notes gave Nielsdatter as probable only. ¶4

Probate in her lifetime: on 2 Oct 1773, right after her husband's estate was divided, Borne Nielsdatter, being old and weak, and having already handed the use of the farm to her son Gulow Jonsen in return for her kår (a retirement pension of board and lodging from the farm), asked for her own share to be divided among her five children by Jon: Gulow, Synneve (betrothed to Rasmus Iversen of Botnen), Christj 20, Kari 16 and Haldis 13[?]. Net 18 riksdaler 4 ort 14 skilling: the son received 6 riksdaler 10 skilling and each daughter 3 riksdaler 5 skilling. Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll A 9, p. 286, no. 18. ¶5

Stepsons: Thor Jonsen of Skaravigen and Johannes Jonsen, Jon's sons by his first wife Kari Iversdatter, were not her children; the 1773 division counts only five children for her. ¶6

Burial (per index): 'Borni Nielsd:', Qvinde (woman), of Indre Hiertholm, Brekke, buried 1779; the index gives a birth year of 1712. Index-level only; the book was not read. She is the only Borni in the Hjartholm burial list, and she was not at Hjartholm in the 1801 census. Birth about 1712 is taken from that index birth year and is approximate. It would make her about 37 at Synneve's birth (1749) and 48 or more at Haldis's (about 1760-1765), which is at the edge of possibility. ¶7

Searches that found nothing 2

Father (not found): Niels Jørgensen of Indre Hjertholm, the only Niels in the Hjertholm probate index list compiled and so a possible father, does not name her. His probate of 31 Mar 1742 (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll 6a, no. 11, fol. 199a) was held between his widow Anna Andersdatter, with Joen Hiertholm as guardian (laugverge; the card does not say for whom), and one heir only, the daughter Synneve Nielsdatter, 12; no Borni and no earlier wife are named, and the entry closes on fol. 199a. A Borni born about 1712 is therefore not a child of this estate as recorded. This does not prove she was unrelated (an adult daughter already provided for, or a daughter by an unrecorded first marriage, could be omitted), but no link is made. Her father remains unidentified. ¶9

Marriage search: her marriage to Jon (Gullovsen) Hiertholm is not in Gulen 11442 canvases 66-71 (Jul 1748 - Mar 1749), read across the full width of both pages, so it predates Jul 1748 (their daughter Synneve was baptised on 3 May 1749). The 1748 betrothal of 'Knud Larsen i Hiertholm og Anne Olsd[atter] N[ordre] Opdal' (11442 c68, right page) is a different couple. ¶10a
