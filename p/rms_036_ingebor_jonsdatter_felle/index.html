Life story

Alive 24 Oct 1740: Panteregister I 1 fol. 9b (Felle østgaarden) records that 'Ingebor Jons Datter' mortgaged land and buildings in Felle to Lars Brinch of Arendal by deed dated 24 Oct 1740. Fol. 160a (DA 10915 view 202) then has her son Tron Toresen Felle mortgaging his ½ share in 1743-45, and fol. 171a (view 213) a 1752 entry on 'Ingebor Jonsdaater' and her debts at Felle. No kin of hers is named. ¶5

This is the skifte 'passeret 7 Aug 1743' that the 1757 probate of her son Tron cites, at which a share of Felle went to Laurs Brinck in return for paying 'his mother's debt'. She was therefore dead by 7 Aug 1743 and still a widow. ¶4

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Upgrade (Speculative to Probable): her son Tron Toresen Felle's 1757 probate shows that he had taken on 'sin Moders Gield' (his mother's debt) at a skifte passeret 7 Aug 1743, and his brother Joen Toresen Felle was then still at Felle. The 1743 skifte is probably her estate (inferred, not read). See the note on Tore Trondsen. ¶2

Upgrade: her own probate. Øvre Telemark (vestfjelske) skifteprotokoll nr. 6 (1740-1743), image 283 (s. 278b-279a): 'Anno 1743 den 7de Aug.', after lawful notice, on the farm Felle in Treungen annex of Hvidesøe prestegjeld, with the appraisers Ole and Knud Felle, to register, value and divide the estate of the late Ingebor Jonsdatter. Her heirs are her sons Trond Toresøn (of age) and Jon Toresøn, and her daughter Helje Toresdatter with her husband Knud Knudsøn. Halvor Ormestad attends as curator (guardian) for Jon Toresøn. In the division a share also goes to Torbiør Toresdatter, probably another daughter (inferred; her heading was read, the wording that ties her in was not). The son Tron Toresøn takes over the farm and the livestock and pays the debts. These children are the Trond 18, Jon 11 and Helge 16 of the 1733 probate of Tore Trondssen, whose widow was Ingebore Jonsdatter. So this is the same woman, and she is the mother of Tron Toresøn Felle (d. 1757). Read on the image. ¶3
