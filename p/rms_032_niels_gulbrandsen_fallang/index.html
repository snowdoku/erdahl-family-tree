Life story

Godparent: Niels Guldbrandsen (Guldbrandsøn) of Fallang, Gran parish, Hadeland, stood godfather at Gran on 4 Aug 1709, 10 Jan 1712, 2 Sep 1714, 23 Feb 1716, 6 Mar 1718 and 19 Jan 1721 (Gran church book 1707-1731, index). In 1718 he is described as 'Sergiant' (sergeant). His birth year is inferred from his being an adult sponsor in 1709. ¶1

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Speculative as father of Peder Nielsen of Fallang: he is the only Niels recorded at Fallang in the indexed Gran records 1707-1760. ¶2a

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Not yet matched: a Niels Guldbrandssøn of 'Sörwattum' (Vattum?) married Elj (Eli) Nielsdatter of Shierstad (Skjerstad) at Gran on 30 Dec 1721. The wedding entry gives no rank, sureties or parents, and the farm is not Fallang, so it is not shown to be the Fallang sergeant (Gran church book 1707-1731, p. 134a, read on the scan). The betrothal entry, earlier in 1721, has not been found. ¶3

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Missing: Peder's baptism (probably before 1707, when the Gran index begins) and any record naming Niels as a father. A probate after Niels, or the Hadeland military rolls for the sergeant, would test it. ¶2b
