About the family

Oline Slind's great-great-grandmother on her mother's side. Entered in the 1825 census of Selbu at rural residence 0147 Stokke, household 1, person 002, as 'Dordi Johnsd., 54, Hustru' immediately after her husband John Olsen, 67, and immediately before their sons Ole, 16, and John, 12. ¶1

Her patronymic makes her father a JOHN. A 'Daardi Jonsdatter, b. 1765, Saelboe: Kiosnaes, Hans kone' in the 1801 census of Selbu is the right name and roughly the right age, but that census gives her marriage as her first and her husband would then not be John Olsen, so she is not identified with her and the lead is recorded as unresolved. ¶3

Children's baptisms (scans read): Ole, born 10 Aug 1809, baptised 11th Sunday after Trinity 1809, parents 'Jon Olsen Stokke og Dordi Jonsdatter', godparents Thomes Olsen Uthus, Michel Jonsen Velve, Peder Pedersen Stokke, Karen Pedersdatter and others; Jon, born 17 Aug 1813, baptised 11th Sunday after Trinity 1813, parents 'Jon Olsen yngre Stokke og Dordi Jonsdatter', godparents Jon Halvorsen [farm uncertain] aelder, Osten Jonsen Stokke, Jon Michelsen Eidem, Brynnil Jonsdatter [Andal?], Marite Olsdatter [Gjolten?]. The 1809 entry names her as Ole's mother, which settles the maternity question raised above. A 'Brynnil Jonsdatter' godmother in 1813 matches her sister Brynil (b. ~1779), unconfirmed. ¶9

Parents, Probable: Jon Halvorsen Velve (b. ~1743) and Beret Mikkelsdatter (b. ~1747), on the chain of the 1801 census, the 1808 marriage 'of Welve', the 1809 godfather brother and the 1813 godfather father. Her own baptism, about Feb-Aug 1772 in the Selbu ministerialbok 1757-1801 (source 16311), would confirm it. ¶10

Life story

1801 census: 'Daardi Jonsdatter', 28, unmarried ('Ugift'), listed among 'Deres born' (their children) in the household of Jon Halvorssen, 58, and Beret Mikkelsdatter, 54, at Waelve (Velve) farm 0048, household 05, Selbu; siblings at home Brynil Jonsdatter 22, Marit Jonsdatter 14 and Mikkel Jonssen 10. Identified with Dordi of Stokke through the 1808 marriage entry, which calls her 'Pigen Dordie Jonsdatter Welve', and through her son Ole's 1809 godfather 'Michel Jonsen Velve' (her brother Mikkel, b. ~1791). ¶7

Marriage: 3 July 1808 (3rd Sunday after Trinity), Selbu: 'Enkemand Jon Olsen Stokke med Pigen Dordie Jonsdatter Welve' - widower Jon Olsen of Stokke and the unmarried girl Dordie Jonsdatter of Velve. Selbu ministerialbok 1801-1815 p. 108, no. 2 of that Sunday (scan read). No witnesses or ages are given in the entry as read. ¶8

Death and burial, Probable: 'Dordie Johnsdatter', aged 67, died 2 August and buried 4 August 1839, Selbu burial register 1824-1842, p. 397 no. 84 (index; scan not read). For: the name is uncommon (38 Dordi/Daardi burials in all Selbu indexed years), and the age 67 gives birth about 1772 against 54 in the census of 27 November 1825 (birth about 1771). ¶4a

Burial: buried 4 August 1839, Selbu (died 2 August), aged 67, Selbu burials 1839 p. 397 no. 84 (index). ¶11

Baptism found (crop re-read by the orchestrator): 'Döbt Dorethe. Par: Jon Halvors. Velve, Birthe Micheld. Test: Torkild Ko[e]s., Siur Welve, Birthe ib., Ingeb. ib., Marit ib.' - Palm Sunday, 4 April 1773, Selbu (ministerialbok 16311, canvas 56 left). Her later ages (28 in Feb 1801, 67 at her Aug 1839 burial) each run about a year high. ¶12a

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Rated Probable rather than Confirmed for the MATERNITY of Ole Johnsen born 1809: the 1825 census of Selbu, unlike that of 1801, records no family-position column at all for this farm, only occupations, so that she is shown as 'Hustru' - wife - without the document saying whose children the boys were. A wife of 54 in the household with a son of 16 is as plain a reading as a census allows, and there is no competing woman, but the register itself does not state it. The record that would close it is the baptism of Ole Johnsen at Stokke about 1808-1809 in the Selbu ministerialbok, which names the mother. ¶2

Records that disagree 1

Against: residence and husband are not in the index, so Stokke is not shown. The only other Dordi Johnsdatter buried near the window is 'Dordi Johnsdt', 82 (birth about 1757), 12 May 1839, who is fourteen years too old. Reading the scan for a residence or 'widow of John Olsen' would raise this to Confirmed. ¶4b

Corrections 3

Correction: death was carried as 'AFT 27 NOV 1825'; replaced by 2 August 1839 from the burial entry above, at Probable. ¶5

Correction: birth was carried as ABT 1771 from the 1825 census age 54. Changed to ABT 1772: the 1801 census age 28 (1 Feb 1801) gives birth 2 Feb 1772 - 1 Feb 1773 and the burial age 67 (2 Aug 1839) gives 3 Aug 1771 - 2 Aug 1772; the two overlap only in Feb-Aug 1772. The 1825 age 54 (27 Nov) gives Nov 1770 - Nov 1771, a year off, which is common in that census. ¶6

Correction: birth moved from ABT 1772 to before 4 Apr 1773. The link to her parents is now Confirmed: the baptism names Jon Halvorsen Velve and Birthe (Beret) Mikkelsdatter; her own 1808 marriage calls her 'Pigen Dordie Jonsdatter Welve'; she is the Daardi in their 1801 household; and her son's 1809 and 1813 godparents include Michel Jonsen Velve and Jon Halvorsen the elder. The parallel klokkerbok 16316 was read only to Feb 1773, which is why it showed no entry. ¶12b
