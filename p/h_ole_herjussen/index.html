About the family

Father of Tøri: named in her 1816 baptism. The 1838 marriage index has 'Ole Horsusen'. ¶1

Children baptized at Åmli with residence Gangsei: Anne (12 Jun 1814), Tøri (b. 18 Jan 1816), Signe (25 Dec 1817), Sigri (8 Oct 1820), Nils (24 Nov 1822), Herjus (28 Jun 1824), Asborg (3 Jun 1827) and another child (29 Aug 1830). ¶3

His parentage is Probable: in the 1801 census, Ole Hergiusen, aged 22, is at Neergaarden as a child of Hergius Olsen and Anne Halvorsdatter. His brother Halvor Herjussen (b. ~1785) also settled at Gangsei and married Ragnild Aslaksdatter in 1820. Asbor Horjusdatter of Gangsei, who married Gunder Syversen in 1813, is probably his sister Asborg (b. ~1776). ¶4

Life story

His marriage to Anne Nielsdatter is also recorded in the Norwegian probate records, on the card for her father's 1789 estate at Oland: "Anne Nielsdtr.... (g.m. Ole Herjusen Gangsei)". ¶7

Married 27 Dec 1813 at Åmli: Ole Horjusen, ungkarl (bachelor), of Gangsei, to Anne Nielsdatter, pige (unmarried woman), of Oland. ¶2

His death has not been found in the Åmli burial index. ¶5

BROTHER CONFIRMED BY PROBATE (10 Oct 2026). The estate of Halvor Herjussen of Gangsei, settled 4 February 1829 to 12 March 1830 (Østre Råbyggelag skifteprotokoll no. 23a, folio 276b), ends "Avd's bror: Ole Herjusen Gangsei" — the deceased's brother, Ole Herjussen of Gangsei. This is independent documentary proof of the brotherhood that was previously inferred from the 1801 census, and so corroborates his parentage as a son of Hergius Olsen and Anne Halvorsdatter of Neergaarden. ¶6

He was still alive in March 1830, when his brother's estate closed. His death has still not been found in the Åmli burial register, and no estate under his name appears among the 30 Østre Råbyggelag probate cards for Gangsei in Gjøvdal, which were read in full. ¶8
