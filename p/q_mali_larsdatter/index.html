Life story

Marriage found: betrothed at Værnes about 19 March 1774 as 'Pigen Mali Larsdatter' to the bachelor drummer Erich Hansøn Christlock of Captain von Coldevin's company, and married at Hegre on 5 April 1774 (Stjørdal ministerialbok 1755-1780, Digitalarkivet 16371, marriage section p. 376, canvas 379). The entry gives no residence or father for her. The sureties were Ole Hansøn Hoset[?] and Lars Iversøn Morset[?] (readings uncertain); a 'Lars Iversen', b. ~1743, was farmer at Børset in 1801, too young to be her father but possibly a kinsman - a lead only. Her first two sons were Hans (bapt. 15 Jan 1775) and Lars (bapt. 29 Sep 1776, apparently died 1777), so her father was a Lars, as already inferred. ¶5

She is first named in a record in November 1794, at the baptism of her youngest child Mali at Skjelstadgjerdet; in her son Lars's 1782 baptism entry the clerk recorded only the father. In 1801 she is 50, in her first marriage, with one child of six still at home. ¶2

Stjørdalsboka V 1 (1953), Skjelstad cottagers, gives Erik Hansen (b. 1745, d. 1805) a marriage in 1774 to Mali Larsdatter, born 1750, and children Lars (1776, d. 1777), Lars (1782), Lars (?-1790) and Mali (1794, m. Peder); no origin farm for Mali is legible in the word-box reconstruction. Her parents remain unknown. The 1774 marriage or banns entry in the Stjørdal 1755-1780 book (source 16371, scan only, no marriage index) and baptisms of 1749-1751 in source 16370 (scan only) are the records to read. ¶4

1801 census: 50, wife of Erik Hansen at Skjelstad, in her 1st marriage. Her son Lars presumably carries the name of her father Lars. ¶1

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 alternative set aside · 1 search with no result · 1 open question

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Ruled out as her baptism: Mali, baptized at Hegre 7 Dec 1755, daughter of Lars of Sørkil-gjerdet. That child was buried at Hegre on 18 Jul 1756 - '10 Mali Larsd. Sørkilmoen 31 uger' (31 weeks old) - and Stjørdalsboka V 1 lists her in the family of Lars Olsen (b. 1710, d. 1772, husmann at Sørkil) as born 1755, died 1756. The 1801 age gap was therefore not the real problem. ¶3

Searches that found nothing 1

Burial search, Mali/Malene Larsdatter in the Stjørdal and Hegra burial indexes 1806-1845: no entry is clearly hers. Candidates and objections: Mali Larsd., Raaen, buried 30 May 1814 aged 74 (b. ~1740, eleven years older than her 1801 age; but Thomas and Ingeborg of Raaen were godparents at her son Lars's baptism in 1782); Mali Larsdatter, lægd (parish pauper), Aasan, buried 13 May 1821 aged 64 (b. ~1757, scan read: no further remark); Mali Larsdatter, lægdsfattig kone, Knotplads, buried 19 Sep 1830 (b. ~1757, 'kone', so a wife, not a widow). The probate protocols 20 (24766, 1815-23) and 21 (57509, 1818-32) were not leafed through: without a death date, a farm and an index the search is unbounded, and a cottager's widow on poor relief would rarely leave a skifte. ¶6

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Lead only: the 1774 surety Lars Iversen 'Morset/Børset' is in the 1801 census at Børsæth, Stjørdal. He is 58, a farmer in his first marriage, with his wife Marit Larsdatter, 68, and children Mali Larsdatter 29 and Iver Larsen 26. Marit Larsdatter could be an elder sister of Mali, which would explain her husband standing surety, but nothing proves it. A Lars was a cottager with his wife at Skjelstad in the 1762 extra tax list (48401, farm no. 37), also unlinked. ¶7
