Life story

Probable baptism found: Skatval, 7th Sunday after Trinity, 11 Jul 1723, 'Døbt Peder Valstads b. Peder. Mod. Mali', godparents Hans Vold, Torsten Scatvold, Peder Johansen Vold, Gurru Vold, Mali [?] Scatvold; mother churched 29 Aug 1723 as wife of Peder Lille Valstad (Stjørdal 16368 pp. 172, 174, scans). It is the only baptism of a Peder son of Peder at Skatval found in Jan-Aug 1723 (the 1723 Skatval entries are on canvases 104-111). Name, farm and year (his 1801 age 78) fit. This gives his parents as Peder (Lille) Walstad and Mali, at Probable. ¶9

A 'Peder Walstad' and a 'Mali Walstad' stood godparents together in Skatval on Septuagesima (6 Feb 1757) at the baptism of Ole, son of Hans Brandt[?] (16371 c17) - consistent with this couple living at Walstad by 1757 (name match only). ¶4

Betrothal: 'Den 14de Octobr trolovet med Schatvold [Skatval] Ungkarl og Quartermester Peder Pedersen Walstad, som haver sin Demission fra Dragoun Tienesten, med Pigen Malli Olsdatter Arnstad. Derfor Caverer: Jon Stoer Walstad, Christopher Stoer Walstad' - the bachelor and quartermaster Peder Pedersen of Walstad, discharged from dragoon service, betrothed at Skatval on 14 Oct 1755 to the unmarried Mali Olsdatter of Arnstad; sureties Jon and Christopher of Store (Big) Walstad (Stjørdal 16370, betrothals 1755, scan). No wedding date is entered in the margin. So he served as a dragoon quartermaster before taking over (Lille) Walstad. Christopher Jonsen of Store Valstad, whose wife Gurrue Pedersdatter died about 1765 (skifte 24755 fol. 474, unread), may be kin: her probate could name siblings. ¶8

Probably the 'Peder Lille Walstad' whose daughter Ellen was baptised in Skatval on 8 Jan 1758 and whose wife was churched 26 Feb 1758 (16371 c27-c28). ¶3

In the 1762 extra-tax summary for Værnes/Skatval he is '66. Lille Woldstad Peder', 6 persons in the household (48401 view 81). The probate after Gurrie Pedersdatter, wife of Christopher Jonsen of Store Walstad (29 Mar 1765, 24755 fol. 474a), names only her own children (Jon Christophersen 15, Merit, married to Hans Erichsen under Wigen, and Sirrie, unmarried, 23); 'Peder Lille Valstad' appears only as one of the two appraisers. So it does not show her to be his sister. ¶10

In the 1762 extra-tax list (48401 image 211, Skatval no. 66 Lille Walstad) he is the householder 'Peder og Hustru', with 'Peder Larsen og Hustru' living with them (others in the household). Supports Peder Larsen as his father; no relationship word is given. ¶13

In the 1765 manntall- og legdsrulle of the Nordre Stjørdal dragoon company (source 51337, image 47, quarter no. 21 'Lille Walstad', Skatval, 'Odels Eier'), the holder is 'Peder Pedersen', 39, marked 'tient' (has served). No husmenn and no young men are listed on the farm. This fits the discharged dragoon quartermaster of the 1755 betrothal; the roll age (39) is about three years short of his 1723 baptism. Biographical only: the roll names no parent. ¶12

Farmer of (Lille) Walstad, Skatval; in the 1801 census a kaarmand (retired farmer on a life-maintenance agreement), aged 78, at Valstad Lille, household 02, in his first marriage with Mali Olsdatter (68), with unmarried children Mali (30), Anne Juditha (27) and Peter (24). ¶1

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Probable father of Ole Pedersen Walstad (see his page). Probable, not proved (orchestrator, line 8): in the 1801 census Ole Pedersen (43) is the farmer of Valstad Lille (household 01), and the only other household on the farm (02) is that of the retired farmer (kaarmand) Peder Pedersen, 78, and Mali Olsdatter, 68, both in their first marriage, with children Mali (30), Anne Juditha (27) and Peter (24). At his marriage in 1791 Ole was called 'Odelsbaaren til Gaarden Walstad' (heir by birthright to Walstad), so his own family held the farm; a retired couple on the farm the son works is the classic father-to-son handover. Ole named his first son Peter and a daughter Mali, as the naming custom would predict for parents Peder and Mali. 'Peder Lille Walstad' had a daughter Ellen baptised 8 Jan 1758 and a Peder Walstad a son Halvor 24 Apr 1757 (Skatval); Ole's own baptism (about 1757-58 by his ages) has not been found in 16371 c13-c30, so his birth year may be off by one or two years. Peder's wife Mali Olsdatter could in principle be Malena Pedersdatter's (Ole's wife's) mother instead, but Malena was 'of Hegge' at marriage and the odel right ties the farm to Ole's line. No record states the relationship. ¶2

Confidence upgraded Probable -> Confirmed as Ole's father: Ole's baptism (8 Jan 1758) names his father as Peder of Lille Walstad, and the 1801 census shows Peder Pedersen (78) as the retired farmer on Valstad Lille beside Ole's household, the only Peder there. ¶5

Corrections 1

Correction: the child baptised to 'Peder Lille Walstad' on 8 Jan 1758 was Ole (this man's son, Ole Pedersen), not a daughter Ellen as an earlier note here says; the name was misread on the scan (Gothic e resembles n). ¶6
