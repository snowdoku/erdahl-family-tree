About the family

Daughter: Randi Torkildsdatter (see her page). Confidence Confirmed as Randi's mother: the probate states that the three eldest daughters, Randi among them, were 'avlede' (begotten) with Ingeborg. ¶4

Father: Gullov Johansen of Asseim (see his page), d. before 29 Apr 1740. His probate at Asseim on 29 Apr 1740 names as an heir the '3die Datter Ingeborre gift med Torkel Asheim' (3rd daughter Ingeborg, married to Torkel of Asheim). Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll 6a (source 24161), fol. 19a, No. 17. Her husband and farm match, her 1754 probate calls her Gullovsdatter and names a maternal uncle Gullovsen, and she named a son Gullig in 1740. Confidence Confirmed. ¶5

Siblings (from her father's 1740 probate; not created as persons): Philippus Gullovsen of Dahle; a 2nd son of Medelfiord, forename read Siur[?] (read Iver[?]); Johanne, wife of Jens Tynning (Tjønning), buried at Brekke 3 Jul 1740 aged 35 as 'Johanne Gulligsd. Tynning'; and Kari[?], wife of Niels[?] Medelfiord. Ingeborg was the 3rd (youngest named) daughter. The estate No. 16 preceding her father's, divided among the same heirs, is probably her mother's; its heading was not read. ¶7

Son Gullik (zoom-): in July 1740 at Brekke 'Torchil Monsen Asheims B. N. Gullig, Mat. Ingeber' (Torkil Monsen Asheim's child named Gullik, mother Ingeborg) was baptised; godparents Mathis Asheim, Guttorm Asheim, Lisbe of the same place, Ingeber Olsd. and Binthe Olsd. of the same place. The day is read 9 or 10 Jul (the entry lies between those of 3 Jul and 22 Jul; Dom. 4 p. Trin. was 10 Jul 1740) (Gulen ministerialbok 11441, canvas 18 left). The boy is named after her father, three months after his probate. He is not among the heirs at her 1754 probate, so he very probably died young (inference; burial not searched). ¶8

Mother: Sigri Rognaldsdatter of Asheim (see her page), d. before the end of April 1740. Her probate at Asheim in April 1740 (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll 6a, fol. 18a, No. 16) names as heirs her surviving husband Gullov Johansen Asheim and children ('og børn'; as heirs in her own estate they are her children), the 3rd daughter being 'Ingeborre gift med Torkel Asheim'. Confidence Confirmed: a probate read from the scan names Ingeborg, married to Torkel of Asheim, as the daughter of Sigri and Gullov; the other heirs are the same as in Gullov's own probate No. 17. ¶9

Life story

Probate of her husband: the 1793 probates after Torkild (Torkel) Monsen of Asheim name her as his first wife, 'Ingeborg Gullacksdatter' (both 1793 probates), 'for lang Tid siden ved Døden afgangne' (long since dead), and as mother of his three eldest daughters: Sigri (m. Mons Torkildsen, Ytre Oppedal), Randi (m. Johannes Olsen Asheim) and Anna (m. Iver Nielsen(?) Plougland). Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll A 11b, fol. 502b No. 25 and fol. 533b-534a No. 79. ¶1

Marriage: betrothed (trolovet) at Brekke church on Dom. Quasimodogeniti 1739, 'ante sacra' (before the service), as 'Ingebor Gulligsd: Osseim' (Ingeborg Gullovsdatter of Asheim) to 'Torchil Monsen Indre Opdal' (Torkil Monsen of Indre Oppedal); cautioners (sureties) Colbein Osseim (Kolbein of Asheim, signing by mark) and Torcel[?] Breche (Torkel[?] of Brekke); married 24 Jul 1739 (margin 'd. 24 Jul. Copul.'). Gulen ministerialbok 11441, canvas 13. Quasimodogeniti (first Sunday after Easter) is computed, not written: Easter 1739 fell on 29 Mar, so the betrothal was on 5 Apr 1739 (gave about 12 Apr, a week too late). No parents are named. ¶11

Baptism of daughter Randi: named as mother 'Ingeber' at the baptism of Torkil Asheim's daughter Randi at Brekke on 12 Apr 1744; she was churched on 17 May 1744 (Gulen ministerialbok 1736-1746, source 11441, canvas 32). She was therefore alive and married to Torkild by 1744; the marriage date is set before 1744 from this. ¶3

Probate (skifte): 6 Mar 1754 at Asheim after 'afdøde Qvinde Ingebor Gullo[v]sdatter', between the widower Torkild Monsen Asheim and their four children: the son Mons Torkildsen, an infant (¼ or ¾ year), the eldest daughter Sigri, about 12, the second daughter 'Rannej' (Randi), 10, and the third daughter Anne, 7. Guardians, as far as read: her brother (morbror) [Siur?] Gullo[v]sen and Torkild's brother (farbror) Lasse Monsen of Indre Oppedal. Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll A 7a, p. 196a, No. 10 (Digitalarkivet 24163, view 196; index pa00000000522199, 'Ingeborg Gullovsdtr'). She died before 6 Mar 1754, probably soon after Mons's birth. Her burial was not searched. Her patronymic is written Gullovsdatter here and Gullacksdatter in both 1793 probates. Her father is Gullov (Gudleiv) or Gullak, not identified. ¶2

Probate 1754, re-read (from the scan, Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll A 7a, fol. 196a, No. 10, Digitalarkivet 24163, view 196): the year is confirmed as 1754 ('Ao. 1754 den 6te Martj', the final digit a clear closed 4; Randi's age of 10 fits her 1744 baptism). The estate is that of 'Sal. Danne Kone Ingebor Gullowsdatter', between the widower Torkild Monsen Asheim and their son Mons Torkildsen, ¾ year, and daughters Sigri, 12[?], 'Ran-nej' (Randi), 10, and Anne, 7[?]. The guardians are 'Morbroderen Siur Gullowsen Indre H[e]sfiord[?]' (her brother Siur Gullovsen; the farm name is uncertain) and 'Farbroderen Lasse Monsen Indre Oppedale' (Torkild's brother Lasse Monsen of Indre Oppedal), the latter read clearly. Appraisers: Lasse Eriksen Lundeland and Elias Lassesen Asheim; Lasse Johansen Liones[?] is named as lagrettemann (lay assessor). Estate 132 Rd, debts 42 Rd, net 89 Rd. This supersedes the 'as far as read' and '[Siur?]' readings in the earlier probate note. ¶13

Name forms (from audit): the 1754 probate after her calls her 'Ingebor Gullowsdatter' (and her brother [Siur?] Gullowsen), her mother Sigri Rognaldsdatter's probate of 2[6 or 9] Apr 1740 calls her father 'Gullov Johansøn Asheim' and his own probate of 29 Apr 1740 'Gullov Johanssen Asheim'; the betrothal of 1739 has 'Ingebor Gulligsd:', and the two 1793 probates after her husband call her 'Ingeborg Gullacksdatter'. Gullov, Gullig and Gullak are treated as variants of one name (see patch 027). The record name is kept as Gullaksdatter; Gullovsdatter is the form of her own 1754 probate, and Gullov the form of her parents' 1740 probates. ¶15

4 corrections

Corrections 4

Correction: the earlier statement that her father 'is not identified' is superseded; he is Gullov Johansen of Asseim (see above). The probate also shows that she was already married to Torkild by 29 Apr 1740, earlier than the 'before 1744' set from Randi's baptism; the marriage itself has not been found (it should be searched in the Gulen books before Jun 1739). ¶6

Correction: the earlier note that estate No. 16 is 'probably her mother's' and that 'its heading was not read' is superseded: the heading has now been read and names Sigri Rognaldsdatter. ¶10

Correction (applied): in the note 'Baptism of daughter Randi', the dates 12 Apr 1744 (baptism, Quasimodogeniti) and 17 May 1744 (churching, Exaudi) were computed from the Gregorian Easter. Denmark-Norway's improved calendar put Easter 1744 on 29 Mar, so the baptism was on 5 Apr 1744 and her churching on 10 May 1744 (Probable until the '5/4' margin in 11441 c32 is read at full size). ¶12

Correction (, agreeing with): the wedding of Torkild Monsen and Ingeborg Gullaksdatter was on 4 Jul 1739, not 24 Jul 1739. The margin beside their betrothal in Gulen ministerialbok 11441, canvas 13, reads 'd. 4 Jul. / Copul.' (d. = den, the; Copul. = married). The first glyph is the scribe's looped 'd', the same glyph that opens every other margin note in that column ('d. 18 April introd.', 'd. 17 April introd.', 'd. 25 Jul. sepult'); took it for a '2'. This supersedes the date '24 Jul 1739' in the 'Marriage ' note and in patch 025; the marriage is set to 4 JUL 1739 at Brekke church. High confidence. ¶14
