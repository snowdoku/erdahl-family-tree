Life story

Probable baptism: Easter Monday (Feria II Paschae), 13 Apr 1716 (computed: Easter 1716 = 12 Apr), at Vangsnes church: 'Gÿri[?] Olsd. Bale bapt.' (Leikanger ministerialbok 1690-1734, source 11564, canvas 120 R). Gyri is a form of Gurid (the 1760 probate writes Gurj), but the first letter might be S (Sigri). The date falls ten months after Ole Bale and Ane Lunde married (23 Jun 1715). No mother or godparents are written, and at least one other Ole lived at Bale then, so the birth date is left at ABT 1716 until a second record (e.g. her confirmation or burial age) agrees. ¶4

Laberg 1985: Gurid, daughter of Ola Andersson of Bale, married Jon Bjørnson from Sande in 1740; their children included Bjørn, who came to Gjerde, and Brita (p. 60). The Lunde section (p. 116) also names her as the daughter of Ola Andersson Bale's wife and as wife of Jon Bjørnson Bale, but calls that wife Brita (daughter of Hans Hansson of Lunde) where p. 60 says Anna. The father link is stated twice; the mother's given name is in doubt. 'Kona hans [?] døydde i 1773' on p. 60 may give her death (or that of a later wife of Jon's successor). ¶2a

Named as widow 'Enken Guri Olsdatter' in her husband Jon Bjørnsen's probate at Bale, 1 Oct 1770, with children Biørn Joensen and Brithe Joensdatter (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll 8b, fol. 636a-636b). ¶5

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Confidence raised from Probable to Confirmed on her father's 1760 probate (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll 7b, fol. 465a, No. 5), which names 'Datteren Gurj Ole Datter gift med Jon Biørnsen' at Bale as heir of Ole Andersen Bale. Her mother is not named there (dead by 1760); see Anna Hansdatter Lunde. ¶3
