About the family

NEW ANCESTOR, graded on his link to his son Orm (see his page). Åmli ætt og heim II (Gjøvdal), Nordre Askland L.nr. 50 (canvas 143): Orm, who held the bruk in the 1600s, 'var [eldste?] son av Svenn Ormson', b. about 1618 (the census gives Orm 46 in 1666, so b. about 1620). L.nr. 49 (canvas 155-156): '[Svenn] Ormson sat med [bruket] på Nordre Askland til han [døydde?] frå kring 1640'; 'Vi veit om 4 born etter [Svenn] Ormson og Tone Nilsdtr.': Olav (to L.nr. 49), Orm (to L.nr. 50), Torbjørn (to L.nr. 52), and a daughter m. Knut Ormson Oland. Gard 16 (canvas 58) quotes a document about the 1639-56 dispute: 'sat Svend Ormsen og hans [hustru] Tone Nilsdtr. 1 hud i Askeland'. In the 1650s lists the book cites 'Orm Svendsen ½ h' among the users of Nordre Askland (canvas 156), which is this man's son. The word read 'Svenn' was recovered by phrase search ('Svenn Ormson', canvas 143, two hits). Bygdebok only (compiled secondary source, text rebuilt from word boxes, dates ABT), so Probable at most. ¶1
