About the family

Her son Torkild named his eldest daughter Else (baptised 2 Jan 1806), and he himself carries her father's name, Torkild. ¶2

Children (from the probates): Torkild (dead by Jan-Mar 1809), Anna (m. Jens Gulltormsen; dead by 1812), Giertrud (m. by 1823 Iver Tostensen of Engesætter) and Kari (about 1790; m. by 1823 L[asse?] Pedersen of Ørneberg[?]). ¶5

Parents: the probate after Torchild (Torkel) Iversen, held at Indre Oppedal on 7 Jun 1775 between his widow Britte Monsdatter and his 2 sons and 3 daughters, names as heir the second daughter 'Else Torchildsd. gift med Mons Lassesen Indre Oppedale' (married to Mons Lassesen of Indre Oppedal), present. Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll A 9, no. 47, pp. 575-576. Father: Confirmed. Her eldest son Torkild was named after him. ¶8

Mother (Probable): the probate calls Britte Monsdatter the widow (Enken), not explicitly the mother of the children, and the words for the joint children ('egte avlede') are only partly legible. She is taken to be Else's mother because no earlier wife is mentioned, the youngest daughter Dordei's guardian was her 'Morbroderen' (maternal uncle) at Oppedal, the eldest son was named Mons, and Britte's burial age (b. about 1712) fits a mother of Else (b. about 1748). ¶9a

Mother: Confirmed. Her mother Britte Monsdatter's probate of 29 Aug 1793 (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll 11b, pp. 524a-524b, no. 58) names 'Else Torkelsdatter gift med Mons Lassesen Indre Oppedahl', present with her husband, as Britte's second daughter and heir. This replaces the Probable mother link in the note above. Her father Torkel Iversen is not named in the 1793 probate; that link stays Confirmed on his own 1775 probate. ¶10

Siblings (1793 probate of her mother Britte Monsdatter, 11b no. 58): Mons Torkelsen (dead by 1793, 4 daughters), Anders Torkelsen Ringereide[?], lensmann (rural sheriff) of Lavik skipreide, Kari Torkelsdatter (dead by 1793, children Torkel, Synneve and Ingeri Hansen/Hansdatter), and the youngest sister Dordei Torkelsdatter, m. Peder Aslaksen (Dordei also in 1775). The Anders Torchildsen Ringereide[?], lensmann, present at her own probate in 1812, is therefore very probably her brother Anders (same name, office, district and farm name). This supersedes the earlier note that any kinship to him was unproved. ¶11

Life story

1801 census: Else Torkieldsdatter, 53, wife (dennes hustrue) of Mons Lassessen at Indre Oppedal, Brekke, married for the first time, mother of the joint children Torkield 22, Giertrud 16 and Kari 12. Birth about 1748 is inferred from her age. ¶1

1808 barneskifte: on 26 Sep 1808 she and her husband, both living, asked the court to register and divide their estate among their children (a barneskifte); the body of the entry calls her 'Konen Else Torchelsdatter' (the wife). The margin miswrites her as 'Else Mons Datter', and the probate index has a separate entry 'Else Monsdtr' (pa00000000526752) that is only this margin slip, not another person. The heirs were Torkild Monsen (a soldier ordered to Bergen), Anna Monsdatter (married to Jens Gulltormsen), Giertrud Monsdatter (married to a man of Engesætter) and Kari Monsdatter, 18, unmarried. ¶3

Probate: the skifte (probate) No. 20 after 'afgangne Else Torchildsdatter Indre Oppedahl' (the late Else) was closed on 2 Jun 1812 between her widower Mons Lassesen and her heirs: the four children of her dead eldest son Torkild Monsen, the five children of her dead eldest daughter Anna Monsdatter, and her daughters Giertrud and Kari. The son's line took a double share. She therefore died after 26 Sep 1808 and before 2 Jun 1812; the registration date, which would narrow this, was not read. ¶4

Anders Torchildsen Ringereide[?], present at her probate in 1812, was probably acting as lensmann (district constable); Ringereide was the lensmann's farm. Any kinship to her is unproved and not claimed. ¶6

Burial (per index): 'Else Torkeldsdr', Kone (wife), of Indre Oppedahl, interred 31 Mar 1812, aged 63. Gulen church book 1809-1825, burials 1812 (index pg00000000771665); the book was not read, and the record does not name her husband. The age gives a birth about 1748-1749, within a year of the 1801 census age of 53; the birth field is left at ABT 1748. This narrows her death from 'between 26 Sep 1808 and 2 Jun 1812' to before 31 Mar 1812; her probate was closed on 2 Jun 1812. ¶7
