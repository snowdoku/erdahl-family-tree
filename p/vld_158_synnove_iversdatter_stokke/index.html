Life story

Bygdebok account: Gards- og ættesoge for Gjemnes, bind 9, Stokke, canvas 376: 'Paul Olson f. ca. 1670, d. 1734... Gift [med] Synnøve Iversdotter Røvik f. ca. 1661, d. 1745. Ole [Iversen Stokke var?] farbror hennar.' Their children are Gunhild (d. about 1734, m. about 1722 Jon Iverson), Kirsti (b. about 1695, d. 1753, m. 1735 Svein Ellingson Dalen) and '3. Ane f. ca. 1701' (brukar 6, wife of Elling Johanson). Canvas 441 of the same volume, in a list of owners, calls her 'Synnøve Iversdotter Kolset, f. 1661', d. 1745, beside a 'Marit Iversdotter Kolset'. So the book places her at Kolset in one place and Røvik in another. The 1666 vicar's census for Kolset lists Iffuer b. 1622 with a son Lars b. 1661 (pc 5051604). If Ole Iversen of Stokke (d. 1679) was her father's brother, her father was an Iver Iversen, not Ole's son Iver Olsen (the lead). These are leads only, and no parent is added: the bygdebok contradicts itself on her farm, and no record names her father. ¶6

The probate cited on the Stokke card of 1679 is Nordmøre skifteprotokoll nr. 2, 1676-1680 = Digitalarkivet source 24636 (not Romsdal 24610); fol. 159a-160a is views 165-166. 9 Aug 1679, Stokke, Gjemnes: probate after Ole Iversen; heirs his son Iver Olsen, now dead, who left two children (not named in the entry), for whom their mother Giertrud [Jons]datter acted, and the daughters Ingebor and Anne Olsdatter. The children of Iver Olsen are not named, so this neither proves nor rules out that Synnøve Iversdatter (wife of Povel Olsen of Stokke, mother of a daughter born about 1697) was one of them. The 1666 vicar's census for Stokke (pc 5051540) lists Oluff Pederss. b. 1615 with son Peder b. 1656; Peder b. 1617 with the servant Iffuer Olss. b. 1657; and Iffuer b. 1605 with son Erich b. 1654. No Povel; Povel Olsen's father is still unknown. ¶5

Probate found: Nordmøre skifteprotokoll 24640 (no. 7, 1731-36), fol. 988 (view 500, right page): 'Anno 1734 den 8de December' a registration was held 'paa gaarden Stocke' in Gimnes tinglag after 'afgangne Povel Olsen', between his surviving widow 'Synnøve Iversdatter' on the one side and on the other 'deres sammen avlede Døttre' (their daughters begotten together, reading of the last word slightly uncertain) and the daughters' children, namely: the late Gunild Povelsdatter's two children (a son Povel, 8, and a daughter Marite, 11, per the index card); 'Kirsti Povelsdatter 37 aar gl.'; and 'Anne Povelsdatter gift med Elling Johansen Bøge[?]' (the card reads the byname as 'Bogen'), for whom her husband Elling Johansen acted. The index card (77795 view 3789) adds the widow's statement that her late husband had before his death conveyed to his third daughter what little he owned, and that the unmarried daughter Kirsti had received 1 øre of land rent in the farm Stokke against her married sister Anne for 22 rdl. The typed card: 'Stokke, Gjemnes. 1734 8/12 Pouvel Olsen. Enke: Synnøve Iversdatter. Barn: 1 Afgangne Gunild Povelsdatters 2 barn... 2 datteren Kirsti Pouvelsdatter 37 år gl, 3 [datteren] Anna [Pouvelsdatter] g.m. Elling Johansen Bogen'. ¶2

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Graded on her link to her daughter Anne Povelsdatter: Confirmed, because the probate describes the heirs as the daughters begotten by Povel Olsen and his widow Synnøve Iversdatter together ('deres sammen avlede Døttre'), Anne among them. If the word read as 'sammen avlede' were doubted, the link would drop to Probable (a widow could be a stepmother); the daughter Synnøve Ellingsdatter, Anne's child, named after her, supports the reading. Continuity to the Anne of the tree: 17 years later, on 11 Nov 1751, the estate of 'Elling Johansen' at the same farm Stokke was registered with his widow 'Anne Povelsdatter' (24644 fol. 74a): the same couple, same farm, no other Elling Johansen or Anne Povelsdatter at Stokke in either probate or in the Stokke card series 1677-1744 (77795 views 3777-3793, all read). The 1734 entry also explains how Elling came to Stokke: through his wife's share of her father's farm. Anne's children carry her parents' names: eldest son Povel (b. about 1726) and a daughter Synnøve Ellingsdatter (1751 probate). ¶3

Open questions and the next record to read 4

Lead only, not a parentage claim: the Stokke probate card of 9 Aug 1679 (77795 view 3781; Romsdal skifteprotokoll 2, 1676-80, fol. 159-159b) after Olle Iffuersen of Stokke lists his children as '1. Iffuer Olsen +, gift m. Giertrud Jonsdaatter, 2 barn', 2. Ingeborg Olsdatter, 3. Anne (under age); the estate held 20 marklag of land in Stokke, and Knud Stokke was Ole's son-in-law. An Iver Olsen who died before 1679 leaving two children at Stokke would fit as the father of a Synnøve Iversdatter born in the 1670s (her daughter Kirsti was born about 1697), and land in Stokke passed through Synnøve's family in 1734. But the card does not name the two children. ¶4a

Next record: the protocol the card cites, 'Romsd. skifteprot. 2 (1676-1680) fol. 159-159b', for the names of Iver Olsen's two children. It is not Digitalarkivet 24610 (Romsdal sorenskriveri skifteprotokoll no. 1, 1677-1683, 148 views; view 86 is already 1680 and view 100 is about fol. 91), so the 1676-80 volume still has to be located. ¶4b

Lead, not a parentage claim: on 27 Oct 1725 her husband Povel Olsen Stocke, with Halvor Knudsen Stocke, Erich Iversen Kaldset, Iver Joensen Echrem, Størcher Ellingsen Røevigen and Størcher Pedersen Myrset, 'with our wives', sold their odel land in Afset (Kvernes) to their brother-in-law Jon Iversen and his wife Gunild Povelsdatter, Synnøve's daughter (Nordmøre pantebok B, 20324 view 147, fol. 145b). If the odel came through the wives, Synnøve was one of a set of co-heirs that included Halvor Knudsen (grandson of Ole Iversen of Stokke) and Erich Iversen of Kaldset, which would fit the idea that she was a child of Iver Olsen of Stokke (d. before 1679). The deed does not say so. ¶7a

Next: the Afset and Stokke probates c.1700-1725 in the Nordmøre card index (77795) for the person whose heirs these six were. ¶7b
