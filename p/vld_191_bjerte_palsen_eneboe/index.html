About the family

Father of Marit Biertesdatter (see her page), graded on his link to her: Probable. Her patronymic 'Biertesdatter' is primary (1745 skifte card 39945/2437 'Marit Biertesdtr.'; 1758 protocol 25158 fol. 380a 'Moderen Marit Biertesdatter'), so her father was a Bierte. The Lesja bygdebok (vol. 1, c181) makes her '4. Marit, ca. 1683' among the children of Bjerte (ca. 1644) and Guro Jakobsdatter of Nørdre Einbu, which she and her husband Peder Hansen bought in 1718/20 and held as odelsgods (1745 card). Circumstantial support from primary records: a 'Bierte' of Eneboe stood godfather in 1726 (with 'Guro, Eneboe') and twice in 1727; 'Goro Jacobsd' of Eneboe was buried 1733 aged about 91 and 'Poul Biertes' (b. about 1674, the bygdebok's son Pål ca. 1671 who took over Nørdre Einbu) of Eneboe in 1735; 'Synnev Bertesd' (b. about 1665, the bygdebok's eldest daughter Synnøve ca. 1665) of Eneboesgrinden in 1733. Marit named her second son Bierte (b. about 1725). The relationship itself rests on the bygdebok and the patronymic, so Probable. ¶1a

Parents: he is '1. Bierte Poffuelsen' among the children in the skifte after Pofuell (Pål) Biertesen of Einbu, 18 Dec 1676 (card 39945/2433), whom the bygdebok makes Bjerte's father (Pål ca. 1604 - ca. 1676, children alive 1676: Bjerte ca. 1644, who took over; Kristoffer; Lars; Jon; Marit; Magnhild; Sølvi). Grade of that link is on the parents' records. ¶3

Life story

Death: the rebuilt bygdebok text gives 'ca 1644-1726', but a Bierte of Eneboe was godfather on 5 Oct and 2 Nov 1727 (indexed baptisms). If that godfather was this man (the only adult Bierte known on Eneboe then; Marit's son Bierte was about 2), he died after Nov 1727; recorded as AFT 2 NOV 1727. No Bierte burial is in the 1732-43 burial index. ¶2

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
