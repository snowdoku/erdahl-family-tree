About the family

Children's baptisms (godparent test of the Volla identification): Kari, daughter of "Hans Christiansen Biørgesæteren og Walborg Peders d[atte]r", was baptised at Lunner on 26 Oct 1794 (Jevnaker parish church book 1752-1799, p. 412; IIIF canvas 210). Godparents: Gulbran Gulbrandsen Klyppen[?], Anders Pedersen Schøyen, Jonas[?] Christiansen Biørgesæter, Anne Gulbrandsdatter Klyppen[?] and Giertrud Helgesdatter Rija[?]. Kari is not in the 1801 household and probably died young. Hans was baptised at Lunner on 15 Nov 1795 (p. 422; canvas 215), with godparents Hans Monsen Haregstuegaard[?], Anders Pedersen Mutta[?], Christopher Christensen Biørgesæteren, Kari Christophersdatter ibid. and Anne Jacobsdatter Mielven[?]. Christopher and Kari are Hans Christiansen's brother and mother. ¶8

Parents (SPECULATIVE, orchestrator 13 Oct 2026): probably Per Michelsen of Volla and Mari Hansdatter. For: (1) a Walborg was baptised at Lunner on 23 Mar 1766 to '[Per?] Michelsen' and Mari Hansdatter, which fits her 1801 age of 35; (2) the same couple had a son Anders, baptised 24 Jun 1763, and Anders Pedersen (b. 1763, at Frøsli in 1801, married Berthe Olsdatter at Lunner 26 Dec 1790) stood godfather at both of Walborg's children's baptisms in 1794 and 1795, as a brother would; (3) no other Walborg daughter of a Peder was found in the Jevnaker baptisms Oct 1761 - Jul 1767. Against or missing: the 1766 father's first name is overwritten; her 1814 burial age (52) points to about 1762; none of her children's godparents is from Volla (Anders Pedersen is written of Schøyen in 1794 and Mutta in 1795); no record names her parents. Sources: Jevnaker church book 1752-1799 (source 9335) canvases 68 L, 88 R, 210, 215; indexed marriages pv00000002197923 and pv00000002198064. ¶10

Daughter Marte: baptised at Lunner 29 Oct 1797 as daughter of the late Hans Christiansen Biørgesæter and 'Walborg Pedersd.' (DA 9335, p. 441, canvas 224); her husband had been buried there on 30 Apr 1797 (p. 437, canvas 222). The godparents legible in the entry (Hans Monsen Ø[?]gaarden, Erich Olsen Fr[?]elshammen, Anne N[?]elsd.) include no one from Volla and no Pedersen, so this baptism gives no support to the Speculative Volla parents. ¶13

Life story

Baptisms searched: All Jevnaker baptisms from mid-May 1760 to Mar 1769 (pp. 90-209) have now been read. The only other girls named Walborg were the daughter of Ole Erichsen Grumstad (about 28 Mar 1762) and the daughter of Jens Lagesen Wang and Gubior Erichsdatter (29 Sep 1767). No Peder at Stryken had a child baptised in that span. ¶7

Baptism not yet found: All Jevnaker baptisms from Oct 1761 to Jul 1767 (church book 1752-1799, pp. 108-185) were read. No child of any Peder at Stryken appears. Two other girls named Walborg were baptised in that span. One, about 28 Mar 1762, was the daughter of Ole Erichsen Grumstad. The other, on 23 Mar 1766 at Lunner, was the daughter of a man whose first name is overwritten (Ole or possibly Per) Michelsen Holla[?] and Mari Hansdatter. The 1766 girl is only a weak candidate. The baptisms of 1760 to Sep 1761 and Aug 1767 to Mar 1769 have not been read. ¶3

Probable burial: 'Walborg Pedersdr', a widow of Biørgefieldet aged 52 (b. about 1762), buried 20 Mar 1814 in Jevnaker parish. The 1801 census age of 35 implies a birth about 1766. Her parents are unknown. Her granddaughter Valborg Hansdatter (b. 1826) was named for her. ¶2

Was living at Stryken when she married Hans Christiansen on 26 Dec 1793. In 1801 she was a widow (1st marriage), aged 35, holding a cottar's plot at Bjørgesæter with children Hans (5) and Marte (3), who were supported by the parish. She was described as the son's wife of Christian Hansen and Kari Christoffersdatter. ¶1

Betrothal and marriage: She was betrothed on 17 Nov 1793 at Lunner (Jevnaker parish church book 1752-1799, p. 401) as "Pige Walborg Peders d[atte]r Strygen", to "Ungkarl og Soldat Hans Xstensen Miör". The sureties (forlovere) were Abraham Skøyen and Peder Hal[?]sen Kiørren; the patronymic is probably a contraction of Haraldsen, since Bjørn and Iver Haraldsen Kiørren appear in 1768. They married on 26 Dec 1793 (p. 402). The marriage entry gives no ages, fathers or sureties. ¶4

Stryken households: No Peder or Per who married in Jevnaker from 1752 to 1770 lived at Stryken, according to the GDS-Hadeland marriage index. In 1801 the farmer at Stryken was Tommes Rolfsen. His wife, Mari Pedersdatter (b. about 1748), had married him in 1776 while living at Svenbalerud. She may have been an elder sister with whom Walborg lived in 1793, but this is unproved. ¶5

Syver Thoresen's estate read: DA 25289 fol. 508a (view 531), 'Skifte efter Syver Thoresen Biørgesæterfieldet', 26 Feb 1810. The widow is 'Walborg Pedersdatter' (with a lawful guardian). The heirs listed are the children Syver Thoresen had in marriage with Dorthe Hansdatter: sons Thore Syversen and Halvor Syversen (22), daughters Rangdi Syversdatter (married, at Halvorsrud) and Dorthe Syversdatter, among others. Hans Hansen is not an heir, which fits his being Walborg's son by an earlier marriage (her own 1814 estate at Biørgefieldet names Hans Hansen 19 and Marthe 17). The two estates are on the same holding four years apart, so the 1810 widow is very likely the same Walborg, Syver's second wife; the 1810 entry says nothing about her parents. Reading of the heir list is partial (faint hand). Grade unchanged. ¶14

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Upgrade: Confirmed as the mother of Hans Hansen of Bjørgesæter (see his page). His baptism at Lunner on 15 Nov 1795 (Jevnaker ministerialbok 1752-1799, p. 422, canvas 215, scan read by an earlier worker) names his parents as Hans Christiansen of Biørgesæteren and Walborg Pedersdatter, and the 1801 census of Jevnaker lists Hans, 5, in the household of the widow Walborg Pedersdatter, 35, at Biørgesætren. The grade applies to her link to her son. Her own parents (the Volla candidates) remain Speculative and her 1814 burial Probable, as stated in the earlier notes. ¶11

Upgrade: her estate, Hadeland og Land skifteprotokoll 1802-1821 (DA 25289) fol. 701b, 'Skifte efter Walborg Pedersdatter Börgefjeldet i Jevnager', registered 19 Oct 1814 and closed 31 Oct 1814, names as her heirs 'en Søn Hans Hansen 19 Aar' (present) and 'en Datter Marthe Hansdatter 17 Aar', unmarried, living at Bjørgefjeldet; index card DA 39947 no. 655 adds that her husband Hans was dead. Together with the 1795 baptism of Hans (son of Hans Christiansen and Walborg Pedersdatter) and the 1801 census, this primary record ties her to her son Hans Hansen of Bjørgesæter. Peder Erichsen Rundelen was appointed guardian of Marthe and curator of Hans; he is not called their uncle, so the entry does not name Walborg's own family. Gross estate 90 rd 68 s. ¶12

Alternatives considered and set aside 2

Possible baptism (updated): Walborg, daughter of Per (Peder) Mikkelsen of Volla and Mari Hansdatter, was baptised on 23 Mar 1766 at Lunner (p. 169). The father's first name is overwritten in that entry. The same couple, with the farm read Holla/Volla, had Per baptised on 29 Mar 1761 (p. 100) and Ole on 3 Jul 1768 (p. 198, clear hand: "Per Mikelsen Volla og Mari Hans daatter"). So the father of the 1766 Walborg was Peder. Her date fits this Walborg's 1801 age of 35 exactly. Magnie Aastensdatter Kiørren was godmother to two of the couple's children, and a Kiørren man was surety at the 1793 betrothal. No record yet ties the Volla family to Stryken, so the identification remains possible, not proved. ¶6

Assessment of the Volla candidate (Oct 2026): Neither baptism names anyone from Volla, nor Per or Ole Pedersen, nor Mari Hansdatter, although Ole Pedersen of Volla was living and married in the parish in 1795 (his wife was churched on 22 Feb 1795). The one godparent who stood at both baptisms is Anders Pedersen, a Peder's son of Schøyen (Skøyen) in 1794. The 1793 betrothal surety Abraham Skøyen came from the same farm. Anders was probably Walborg's own kinsman, perhaps a brother, but that is not proved; an Anders Pedersen b. about 1763 was a cottager at Frøsli in 1801. No child named Anders was found among Per Mikkelsen Volla's known children (Per 1761, Walborg 1766, Ole 1768). The 1801 census for Jevnaker has no Volla household, no Per Mikkelsen, and no Per (b. 1761) or Ole (b. 1768) Pedersen. Her ages also conflict: 35 in 1801 (b. about 1766) against 52 at burial in 1814 (b. about 1762). The identification with the Walborg baptised 23 Mar 1766 to Per Mikkelsen Volla and Mari Hansdatter therefore stays Possible only and is not adopted. A Peder of Skøyen is now an equally good candidate for her father. ¶9
