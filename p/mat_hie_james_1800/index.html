Life story

Baptism: James, son of George and Phebe Hicken, was baptised at All Saints, Canterbury, on 7 September 1800; the register gives the father as a private in the Royal Artillery. ¶1

Burial: James, son of George and Phebe Hicken, was buried at All Saints, Canterbury, on 28 September 1800, aged five weeks; the burial entry again notes the father as a private in the Royal Artillery. ¶2
