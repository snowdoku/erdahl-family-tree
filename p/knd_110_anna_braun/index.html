About the family

Mother of Hans (Johann) Jacob Jenisch (see his page), first wife of M. Joseph Jenisch (see his page). Graded on her link to Hans Jacob: Probable. Sources: the Pfarrerbuch Württemberg (compiled) entry for Joseph Jenisch (Nr. 3905) lists 'Johann Jakob, * um 1641, + 1.4.1686 Brackenheim, Kupferschmied' as child 5 of the first marriage (1635) with 'Anna BRAUN, gt. 19.2.1606 Weinsberg, + 3.3.1645 Stuttgart, Va. Blasius (Nr. 834)'. The entry for her father (Nr. 834) lists her as his child 1, married 1635 to Joseph Jenisch. Supporting records: indexed Stuttgart christenings of 1638 (Anna Helena) and 1643 (Johann Heinrich) name the parents 'Joseph Jenisch, Anna'; Hans Jacob's Brackenheim burial (1 Apr 1686, image read) gives his age as 45, so he was born about Apr 1640 to Apr 1641; and the second wife, Agnes Engel, did not marry Joseph until 13 Aug 1645. The whole birth window from his burial age (1640-41) lies inside her marriage (1635 to Mar 1645), and no other wife is known in it (audit 5 rule 1). But no record that names her as his mother has been read, so the link stays Probable. 'gt.' (getauft) means the 19 Feb 1606 date is her baptism. ¶1a
