About the family

Mother of Tøri (bapt. Nov 1761). Her patronymic is hard to read; it may be Børjesdatter, Børresdatter or Børulsdatter. Her name, Signe, is not in doubt. ¶1

Family (Probable): a Børus family lived at Jørundland — Tellef Børusen (1742, later at Omland with wife Thore Nielsdatter), Ole Børusen and Aadne Børusen. 'Signe Jörenland', unmarried, was godmother to Tellef Børusen's daughter Anne, 1 Dec 1748 (1748 no. 55, canvas 25), so she was probably their sister. Godparent Børju Jørundland at her daughter Tøri's 1761 baptism may be her father or a brother's son. Her own baptism (c. 1720-35) would be in the Åmli church book 1711-1740 (Digitalarkivet 9920). ¶8

Mother FOUND (13 Oct 2026): the probate after her father Børu Aadnesen, Jørundland, 11 Oct 1728 (Østre Råbyggelag Skifteprotokoll 8 fol. 21; index cards 5997-5999) names the widow Anna Vraalsdatter and the children Aadne 14, Tellef 11, Ole 7, Asbør 22, Ingeborg 19 and 'Zigne 3' - Signe, baptised 9 Sep 1725. Maternal grandparents (archivist's note): Wraal Bendixsen Aamland (+ probate 1687) and Ziri Olsdatter (+ probate 1688); maternal uncle Giermund Vraalsen Omland; paternal uncle Ole Aadnesen Stedjen. ¶11

Life story

A Børuld/Børulsen family did live in this district — Børuld Børuldsøn of Vraalstad, later of Tviland, appears as a guardian in a 1714 Ramse probate, and Nære Børuldsøn was at Øvre Ytre Ramse at the same date — so "Børulsdatter" is the most plausible of the three readings. This is a suggestion, not evidence. ¶3

Baptism found: Dom. 15 p. Trinit. (9 Sep) 1725 at Åmli church: 'Börüf Aadnesön Jörenlands barn Signe' (Åmli 1711-1740 pp. 74-75, canvas 40); no mother or godparents recorded (this book never gives them). Identity with the Signe Børusdatter Jørenland who married Ole Olsen Mjåland in 1754 is Probable: same farm and patronymic, and no other Signe daughter of a Børus at Jørundland was seen 1724-35. Her father died in 1728 aged 49. ¶10

Unmarried 'Signe Börüsdatter Jörenland' stood godmother in Gjøvdal at Advent 1753 to Niels Evindsen and Gundvor Aslaksdatter Jørundland's daughter Signe (canvas 41). Betrothed in Gjøvdal on Lady Day (25 Mar) 1754, by royal licence: 'Trolovet Ole Olsön Miaaland og Signe Börüsdatter Jörenland efter Kongl. Bevilling. Caus: Aadne Jörenland og Evind Bendixsön Miaaland' (1754 betrothal no. 9, canvas 42) — the surety Aadne Jørundland is presumably her brother Aadne Børusen. A second son Ole was baptised Invocavit (7 Mar) 1756 in Gjøvdal: 'Ole Olsön og Signe Börüsdatter Miaalands barn Ole', godfathers Evind Bendixsen Mjåland and Ole Børusen Jørundland (1756 no. 22, canvas 49); the Ole of Dec 1754 presumably died. ¶9

MARRIAGE FOUND: married Ole Olsen Miaaland, 1754 (prob. 30 Jun), as 'Signe Börusdatter Jörenland' — of Jørundland, Gjøvdal (Åmli 1741-1775, 1754 marriage no. 12, canvas 43). Her son Ole's baptism, 27 Dec 1754, again writes 'Signe Börüsdatter'. Patronymic is thus Børusdatter (= Børjusdatter, from Børju/Børjus, Agder form of Bjørgulv). ¶7

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the identification · 1 alternative set aside · 1 search with no result · 1 open question

The case for this identification 1

Re-read 13 Oct 2026: the 1761 entry (Åmli 1756-1775 p. 49 no. 54) reads 'Signe Bör[i][u]s datter' — best reading Børjusdatter (Børju(s) = Agder form of Bjørgulv); letters B-ö-r-i-u-s with no descender or l, so Børres-/Børulsdatter are not supported; Børings-/Børinsdatter are weak alternatives. Godparents at that baptism: Neern(?) Haavlestad(?), Jon Omland, Børju Jø[ru]nland (Jørundland), Neern(?)'s wife, Eldri Torgersdatter Omland. The godparent Børju of Jørundland is probably her father or brother (lead). ¶5

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Context (lead, not proof): in 1742-43 sons of a Børu(s) lived at Jørundland (Jörenland) in Gjøvdal — Tellef Børusen, Ole Børusen and Aadne Børusen (Åmli 1741-1775, 1742 no. 42, 1743 no. 60). If Signe is their sister and Aadne Børusen is Aadne Larsen Gangsei, Tøri Olsdatter and Niels Aadnesen were first cousins (speculative). ¶6

Searches that found nothing 1

Searched for and NOT found in this round, together with her husband, in all 74 Østre Råbyggelag probate cards for Oland in Gjøvdal. Her patronymic remains unread; the 1761 baptism entry has been variously read as Børjesdatter, Børresdatter and Børulsdatter. ¶2
