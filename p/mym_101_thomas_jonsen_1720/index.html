1 on the grade

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Probable father of Jon Thomassen of Lien (see his page). Probable, not proved (orchestrator, cards): in the 1762 extra tax list the only Lien household with a son Jon over 12 is Thomas Jonssen and Birit Haldorsdatter (matr. 770; children over 12 Jon, Helge, Birit, Gjuri). Jon Thomassen of Lien (b. about 1748, so about 14 in 1762) named his own children Thomas (b. about 1781), Beret (b. about 1783, = Birit) and Hælge (b. about 1789), matching his father's and mother's names and his sister Helge. The other Lien households of 1762 (matr. 698, 697, 769) are headed by a Lars, two Peders and a Jon Pedersen. ¶2
