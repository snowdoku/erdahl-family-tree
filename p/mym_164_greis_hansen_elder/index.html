About the family

Family: Gurie Hansdatter (d. 1771, wife of Hans Nielsen of Riset) was his brother Hans's daughter; the same probate names a 'Faster' (father's sister) Anna Hansdatter. So Greis the elder, Gurie's father Hans and Anna Hansdatter were siblings, children of a man named Hans (patronymic). Not found yet: his farm, wife and dates. ¶3

Life story

Grandfather of Greis Hansen (see his page); father of Hans Greisen (see his page). Died before 24 Aug 1771. ¶1

Possible parentage found. The 1693 Alstad probate card (77804 view 23) lists the children of Aren Thoresen of Alstad østre, Frosta, by an earlier marriage: Hans Arensen (of age), Greigers Arensen and Anne Arensdatter (minors), with their father's brother Gunder Hogstad as guardian. This is the same trio of siblings, Hans, Greis and Anna, that the 1771 Riset probate gives for Gurie Hansdatter's father and his brother and sister. Her mother's brother Sivert, with his daughters Ingeborg and Lisbet, also matches Sivert, the next bruker of Alstad østre (Frostaboka X p. 12). If this is right, Greis the elder was born before about 1690 as Greigers Arentsen, and the patronymic 'Hansen' in 1771 is a clerk's error. His father is linked as Aren Thoresen at Speculative only, because the 1771 patronymic contradicts it (see that record). His mother, Aren's first wife, is not named; Ragnild Mortensdatter was his stepmother. ¶4

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Graded on his link to his son Hans Greisen (see his page). The 1771 probate after Gurie Hansdatter of Riset, Frosta (24757 fol. 381b, scan read) names him as her 'Farbroder' (father's brother), deceased, and lists his children: 'a) Hans Greisen, er død' (with Hans's three children, among them Greis Hansen 28, husmann under Storlein) and 'b) Gunild Greis Datter, gift med Huusmand Ole Hansen i Narvigen'. The record itself names the father-son relationship. ¶2
