About the family

NEW ANCESTOR. Farmer at Nedre Ytre Ramse in Tovdal; father of Berte (Birgitha) Evensdatter, who married Tjøstel Tergiesen of Nedre Eppeland and was the grandmother of Birgitte Terjesdatter (1761-1812). ¶1

He is named as the eldest child in the 1712 estate of his mother Ingeborg Olsdatter: "1. Evind, nedre ythre Ramse (+sk. 1722)". His own estate is dated 1722 on that card and 1723 on another; the discrepancy is between the date of death and the date the settlement closed. ¶2

His wife was Anna Gunnersdatter (also written Gunnusdatter), whose estate was settled at Ramse on 15 June 1720. Their joint probate of 15 June 1720 to 29 April 1721 (Skifteprotokoll no. 6, folio 1) names four children: Svend Evindsen, 11; Tellef, 8; Birgitha Evindsdatter; and Ingeborrig. Berte Evensdatter of Eppeland is almost certainly that Birgitha. The estate held 1 hud 10 skinn in Nedre Ytre Ramse and half a hud in Baas, with movables of 340-3-11 against debts of 151-1-7. ¶3

His father is stated on the same card: "Enkemannens far: Svend Evindsen nedre Ramse + sk. 13/3 1696." ¶4

Life story

Context: his brother Tellef Svendsen of Ytre Ramse obtained a royal licence to marry in 1704 because he and his bride Liv Gullovsdatter were "udi andet og fierde leed... beslegtede" — related in the second and fourth degree. Such dispensations, recorded in the Danish Chancery's Norwegian registers, are the clearest sign of how closely intermarried the Tovdal farms were. ¶5

Probate protocol reading, his wife's estate 1720-21 (Skifteprotokoll no. 6, fol. 3-5): he proved title to 1 hud 10 kalveskinn (skin units of land tax) in Nedre Ytre Ramse — 9 skinn by the skiftebrev after his father Svend Evensen of 13 March 1696 (held by the late sorenskriver Mathias Christensen Schrøder), 7 skinn as maternal inheritance by the skiftebrev after his mother Ingeborg of 29 April 1713, and 6 skinn bought from his siblings — and to half a hud in Baas "efter oprettet Contract med Wraal On[?]søn Mogland" (by contract with Wraal...sen of Mykland, his sister Anna's husband). The land was divided: Evind 11 + 3 skinn; each son 3⅔ + 1; each daughter 1⅚ + ½. The record states that "Faderen forbliver efter Loven sine Børns Formynder" — the father remains his children's guardian by law. ¶6

His own estate (cards 10423-10425 and Skifteprotokoll no. 6 fol. 189b) was opened at Nedre Ytre Ramse on 19 November 1722 and closed on 11 February 1723 — which explains why some cards say 1722 and others 1723. He had remarried: his widow was Thodne Giermundsdatter (later married to Petter Nielsen Vraalstad). The children of his first wife, Anna Gunnusdatter (estate 1720), were Svend 12, Tellef 10, Birgitta and Ingeborg; by Thodne he had Giermund, 2 (died with an estate at Vraalstad in 1783), and a posthumous daughter Anna, since the widow was pregnant when he died. The estate was insolvent: movables 605 rd against debts of 672 rd. ¶7

The guardians (formyndere) named in his estate are all from his own side of the family: his paternal uncle Knud Evindsen Risland ("farbror til den sl. mand"), the children's paternal uncle Nils Svendsen Sunde, and Aslak Tellefsen Haugedal, who was married to Evind's sister. Kittel Olsen Hillestad and Jon Næresen Tveit were guardians for the children of the second marriage, and Ole Aslaksen Vraalstad was the widow's legal guardian (laugverge). No mother's brother or mother's father of Anna Gunnusdatter's children appears, so this estate does not identify her family. ¶8
