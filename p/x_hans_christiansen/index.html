Life story

Baptism: On 25 Aug 1771 (13th Sunday after Trinity) at Lunner church the home baptism of a boy Hans was confirmed in church: "Confirmeret et hiemmedøbt Drengebarns Daab N: Hans. Parent: Xstian Hansen Song og Kari Xstoffers Dtr." His parents were Christian Hansen of Sogn and Kari Christoffersdatter. He had been baptised at home shortly before, so he was born in the summer of 1771. The godparents were Ole Tømmeraas, Jacob Christoffersen of Tømmeraas, Halvor Rolfsen Sogn, Anne Hansdatter Sogn and Berthe Christensdatter Haga (Jevnaker parish church book 1752-1799, p. 232). ¶3

Probate: his father Christian Hansen's estate at Bjørgesæteren, 11 Feb - 1 May 1822 (Hadeland og Land skifteprotokoll 1800-1843, fol. 47b; index card DA 39947 no. 659), lists as the first heir '1. Hans + [og] hans barn: a. Hans Bjørgesæter myndig, b. Marte 24 aar ugift', then the sons Christian and Christopher, both of age. His mother is named there as Kari Christophersdatter. ¶6

Married Walborg Pedersdatter of Stryken at Lunner church on 26 Dec 1793 ('2den Jule Dag'), when he was an unmarried artillerist living at Miør (Jevnaker parish church book 1752-1799, p. 402-403). ¶1

Burial: 'begravet Hans Christiansen Biørgesætren', entered under the 2nd Sunday after Easter (30 Apr) 1797 at Lunner, among the acts read out before the 3rd Sunday after Easter (7 May) (Jevnaker ministerialbok 1752-1799, DA 9335, p. 437, canvas 222). The age column reads '32 aar gammel', which conflicts with his baptism in Aug 1771 (he would be 25); the age is probably a clerk's error or misreading, since the farm, the name and the later baptism of his posthumous daughter (below) fit. This narrows his death from 'before 1801' to about April 1797. ¶7

Posthumous daughter: Marte, daughter of 'afdøde [the late] Hans Christiansen Biørge[sæter] og Walborg Pedersd.', was baptised at Lunner on the 20th Sunday after Trinity, 29 Oct 1797 (DA 9335, p. 441, canvas 224). Godparents begin Hans Monsen Ø[?]gaarden (also godfather to Hans in 1795) and Erich Olsen Fr[?]elshammen. She is the Marte, 3, of the 1801 census and the Marthe Hansdatter, 17, of her mother's 1814 estate. ¶8

By the 1801 census his widow Walborg Pedersdatter was living at Bjørgesæter, where she is described as the 'sønnekone' (son's wife) of the farmer Christian Hansen and Kari Christoffersdatter. Her children Hans Hansen (5) and Marte Hansdatter (3) carry his patronymic. He is identified as Christian Hansen's son because of that census relationship together with the 1793 marriage. His baptism (about 1765-1772) and burial (about 1797-1800) are in the untranscribed Jevnaker books for 1752-1799. ¶2

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Confidence: Upgraded from Probable to Confirmed by the 25 Aug 1771 baptism, which names both parents. It is tied to the 1793 marriage and the 1801 household through his brother Christoffer (baptised 1774, aged 26 in 1801). ¶5

The case for this identification 1

Identification: This is the only Hans born to Christian Hansen and Kari Christoffersdatter in the register from their marriage in Nov 1769 to July 1776. He would have been about 22 when he married Walborg Pedersdatter in 1793. In 1801 Walborg is called the son's wife of Christian Hansen and Kari at Bjørgesæter. Christian and Kari's younger son Christoffer (baptised 1774) is also named in that 1801 household, which ties the 1771 couple to the 1801 couple. The child-parent link is therefore Confirmed. ¶4
