About the family

Wife of Jon Ingebrigtssen, cottager at Store Evjen; 1801 census, Selbu, rural residence 0050 Storevjen, household 06, aged 39, 'hans kone', with children Siri, Ole and Ingebrigt. Probably Paul Eidem's paternal grandmother. ¶1

Son Ingebrigt's baptism: 'Dordi Olsdatter' is the mother of Ingebrigt, born 11 February 1799 and baptised 17 February 1799 at Selbu, father Jon Ingebrigtsen of Store Evjen (Selbu ministerialbok 1757-1801, p. 232, no. 2). Confidence raised from Probable to Confirmed: see Ingebrigt Johnsen. Her granddaughter Dortie (Daardi) Ingebrigtsdatter, baptised 1831, carries her name. ¶2

Children: Sigri, baptised 29 November 1789 (canvas 134); Ole, baptised 29 September 1793 (canvas 157); and Ingebrigt, born 11 February 1799 (p. 232). These are the Siri 11, Ole 7 and Ingebrigt 2 of the 1801 Storevjen household. See Jon Ingebrigtssen. ¶4

Life story

Baptism: 'Dordi', daughter of Ole Paulsen Berge and Kirsten Paulsdatter, baptised on Oculi Sunday, 22 February 1761, godparents Per Aftreit, Siur Berge, Mali Berge and Siri Flones; Selbu ministerialbok 1757-1801, canvas 11 left page (crop). Sisters Ingebor (1757) and Brynill (1760). Parents linked as Confirmed: she married in 1789 as Dorete Olsdatter of Berge; aged 39 on 1 February 1801 fits a February 1761 baptism exactly; she is the only Dordi daughter of an Ole in the complete read of 1757-67 (the other, 1761, is a daughter of Esten Olsen Kyllo); her first son was named Ole (1793), with Halvor Berge as godfather. Her birth date is now 'before 22 February 1761' in place of ABT 1762. ¶5

Marriage and origin: betrothed 12 July 1789 and married 27 September 1789 to Jon Engelbrechtsen (Ingebrigtsen) of Store Evjen, entered as 'Dorete Olsd. Berge' - she was of the farm Berge before her marriage, so her father Ole is to be sought at Berge (Selbu ministerialbok 1757-1801, canvases 133-134). A Halvor Berge was godfather to her son Ole in 1793 and a Gunild Berg[e?] godmother to her daughter Sigri in 1789; both may be her kin. ¶3

1801 census re-checked: Selbu, rural residence 0050 Storevjen, household 06, persons 022-026 - Jon Ingebrigtssen, 37, husband, cottager with land (husmand med jord), both in their first marriage; Daardi Olsdatter, 39, his wife; their children Siri Jonsdatter 11, Ole Jonssen 7 and Ingebrigt Jonsen 2. The birthplace column is blank for all. ¶6

1825 census (27 November 1825): 'Daardi Olsd.', female, birth year 1760 (calculated), at Marstad, Selbu, status 'Gm. E.' - read as gaardmands enke, a farmer's widow (the abbreviation is not expanded in the index). The birth year fits her February 1761 baptism, and Marstad is where her son Ingebrigt lived at his 1824 marriage and where a married Jon Ingebrictsen, born 1764 (calculated), was buried in 1813 - taken as her husband's burial (Probable; see Jon Ingebrigtssen). Probable identification; the census page would show the household head and confirm the status. ¶8

Death: not set. If she was the Marstad widow of 1825, she was alive in November 1825. The 1841 burial of 'Dordi Olsdatter, 84' noted earlier remains the only weak lead and is not adopted (the 1825 census row for a Dordi Olsd. b. 1758 at Røset matches that burial better). ¶9

Searches that found nothing 1

Death not found; one weak lead only, not proved. A burial of 'Dordi Olsdatter', aged 84, on 17 October 1841 (Selbu ministerialbok 1824-1842, p. 404 no. 49) is the only Dordi Olsdatter burial in the Selbu index that could be hers. Its age gives a birth about 1757, four years before her February 1761 baptism, and the index gives no residence, husband or widow's remark. No death date is set from it. A search of the Selbu burial index for Dordi, Dorte, Dorthe and Dorete, all years, found no burial naming Jon Ingebrigtssen or Store Evjen. Reading the scan of that entry (source 16314, p. 404 no. 49) for a residence would settle it. ¶7
