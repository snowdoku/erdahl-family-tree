About the family

Parents found: Knud Helgesen and Ragnild Biørnsdatter of Midtstrand (married 18 Jan 1702). Her probate, 9 Oct 1721 (card 9283; DA 25285 fol. 479a, view 488), names 'begge deris sammen auflede... 4re Børn... Sønnen med Nafn Helge Knudsen 12 aar gammel' (so born about 1709); the 1727 skifte at Mitstrand (card 9287; fol. 900b, view 917) names 'Sønnen... Helge Knudsen 18 aar' and hands over the farm in his father's old age. Sisters: Marit (17 in 1721; married by 1727 to Biørn Knudsen), Ingebiør (8) and Gertrud (6). This settles the earlier note 'Own parents not identified'. His grandparents (Probable) are Helge Gulbrandsen and Marthe Knudsdatter of Midtstrand (1701 probate, card 9291). ¶5

Life story

Own parents not identified. The Midtstrand card 9291 (1701) is after Marthe Knudsdatter, wife of Helge Gulbrandsen, with children Knud, Gulbrand, Even and Berit; that family is a generation older, and a link to Helge Knudsen is not shown. ¶3a

Farmer at Midtstrand, Svenes annex of Aurdal; dead before his probate of 11 Oct 1739 (DA 25286 p. 279), which values the estate at about 281 rdr gross, 97 net, including 14 2/5 skinn in Mitstrand for 150 rdr (card 9297). Children: Haagen 9, Ragnild 4, Aagaat 8 weeks. His widow Barbro Haagensdatter was given Andreas Morland as her laugværge (guardian at law). ¶1

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Graded on the link to the daughter Ragnild Helgesdatter (see her page): Confirmed. The probate after Helge Knudsen of Midtstrand, 11 Oct 1739 (DA 25286 p. 279, view 151; card 9297), is held between the widow Barbro Haagensdatter and 'deres sammen avlede 3de Børn' (their children together), the second being 'Datteren Ragnild Helgesdatter 4 aar gl.'. Continuity: the bride of 23 May / 1 Jul 1754, 'Ragnild Helgesdr. Mitstrand' (DA 9361 p. 93), has the same forename, patronymic and farm 15 years later (within the 15-year same-farm threshold); no other Ragnild Helgesdatter of Midtstrand is known, and the bygdebok's birth year 1735 for Ole Arnesen's wife agrees with the age 4 in 1739. Note that Ragnild's own link to her daughter Marit Olsdatter rests on the bygdebok and is Probable, so the line through these two to the tree is no stronger than Probable at that step. ¶2

Audit 9 (2 Nov 2026): Confirmed kept, graded on his link to his daughter Ragnild Helgesdatter (see her page). The record is the 1739 probate ('deres sammen avlede 3de Børn', Ragnild 4), followed by the 1754 bride 'Ragnild Helgesdr. Mitstrand' 15 years later on the same farm, with no rival. Her widowhood as 'Enken Ragnild Helgesdatter' in 1799 agrees. Clean-up: the earlier caveat on this record, that Ragnild's own link to Marit Olsdatter is only Probable, is out of date. raised that link to Confirmed on the 6 Oct 1754 baptism and the 1799 Strand division. ¶4
