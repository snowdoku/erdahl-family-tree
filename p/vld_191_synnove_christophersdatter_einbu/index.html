About the family

Mother of Bjerte Pålsen (see his page), graded on her link to him: Probable. The skifte after Pofuell Biertesen of Einbu, 18 Dec 1676 (card 39945/2433, fol. 48b) names her as his widow ('g.m. Sønne Christophersdtr.') with the children Bierte, Christopher, Lauritz and Jon Poffuelsen. The protocol entry (25154 fol. 48b, view 51) settles that the children were hers: the skifte is 'imellem hans hustru Sønne Christophersd. og deris 7 Børn', naming Bierte first, already married by 1676. She stays Probable only because identifying that Bierte with Marit Biertesdatter's father (of Nørdre Einbu, d. after 1727) rests on farm, name and the bygdebok across 50 years; Bjerte's eldest daughter Synnøve (b. about 1665) carries her name. ¶1a

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 open question

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Test record: the 1664/66 vicar's census for Lesja (35502) for Bierte's age, and any later record naming Bjerte of Einbu as Pål's son. ¶1b
