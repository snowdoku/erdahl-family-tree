Life story

Named as the wife of John Toldervey the bedell, and grandmother of Elizabeth (Toldervey) Prince, by Noyes-Gilman Ancestry (1907), p. 306 ('his wife Alyce'). An Alice Toldervey, widow, of St Peter-in-the-East, Oxford, had her will proved in the Chancellor's Court in 1597; the index editor adds 'probably of John' (Griffiths' index). Oxford History's account of Henry Toldervey (mayor 1613-14) calls him the son of Alice, who leased the octagonal chapel with him in 1583 as a widow, and notes a 'Mrs Toldervey' buried in 1596. ¶1
