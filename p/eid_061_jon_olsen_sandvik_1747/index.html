About the family

Children in the register by Ingeborg Olsdatter: Ole, baptised spring 1771, 'Jon Ols. Sandvig, Ingebor[g] Olsd.', godparents Torchel Sandvigen, Erich Sandvigen, Jørgen Slinden, Ingeborg Sandvig and Gidsille Sandvigen (Selbu klokkerbok 1766-1776, canvas 68); and Marit, baptised on Oculi Sunday, 14 March 1773, 'Döbt Marit. forældr: Jon Olsen Sandvig, Ingeborg Olsd.', godparents Engelbr. Mel[?]sen, Peder Sandvig, Brynild Lien, Elen Bolstad and Marit Sandvig (canvas 93, crop). ¶1

Father of Ingebor Jonsdatter (see her page), wife of Haldor Torgersen Svinaas, rated Probable. For: (1) she married in 1794 as 'Pigen Ingeborg Jonsdatter Sandvig', a daughter of a Jon living at Sandvik; (2) the bygdebok's Sandvik chapter lists 'Ingeborg, 1775', married to 'Haldor Torgersen Svinås', among the children of this Jon Olsen; (3) he is the Jon of Sandvik of the right age in the 1801 census (the forlover Jon Barosen Sandvig is excluded on her own page). Conflict: her 1801 census age of 28 points to 1772-73, the book to 1775. The register favours the book: this couple had Ole in spring 1771 and Marit in March 1773, leaving no room for a daughter in 1772 or early 1773, and the better-fitting burial candidate on her page ('Ingeborg Johnsdr', 79, buried 1853) points to 1774. Her baptism, about 1774-75 (Selbu klokkerbok 1766-1776, after canvas 108, June 1774, not yet read), would confirm it. ¶4

Parents (Probable): Ole Jonsen of Sandvigen (buried 18 Jun 1767, aged 50) and Kari Olsdatter. The probate after Ole, 9 Oct 1767 (Skifteprotokoll 6-1, 24756, fol. 86b), names one son and heir, 'Jon Olsen [20?] aar gl.'. Jon took over Sandvik in 1768. The other Sandvik Ole Jonsen (d. 1768) had a son Jon Olsen who died unmarried in 1777 (Skifteprotokoll 7 register, fol. 556b), so he is ruled out as this Jon's father. ¶11

Life story

Bygdebok (Selbu i fortid og naatid vol. 3, Sandvik chapter p. 353; nb.no word boxes only): '1768-1811 Jon Olsen... Sandvik,... 1747,... 1818' - holder 1768-1811, born 1747, died 1818; a wife Ingeborg '1749, død' with '1770' (probably their marriage); children 'Ola... 1771', 'Marit,... 1773,... Haldor', 'Ingeborg,... 1775,... Haldor Torgersen Svinås', and further children 1777-1788. Ola 1771 and Marit 1773 agree exactly with the register. His death in 1818 rests on the book alone. ¶3

Marriages (book, word boxes): G.I 1770 Ingeborg from Slind, born 1749 (see her page), who died, apparently in 1786; G.II 1787 'Ingrid' - the Inger Olsdatter, 34, of the 1801 census. The Slindan chapter (vol. 7, p. 116) independently marries Ingeborg 1749 to 'Jon Olsen... Sandvik'. Identity caution: a younger 'Joen Ols. Sandvig', aged 20, was buried 4 September 1773 in the epidemic (burial card), so there were two Jon Olsens at Sandvik; that one died before Ingebor's birth and is excluded. Jon Olsen Garberg (with Mali Barosdatter, 1771) and other Jon Olsens are excluded by the farm and the wife's name. ¶5

1801 census: Sandvig (Selbu), household 1, 'Jon Olssen', 55, in his second marriage (his wife's first), a farmer with land (bonde med jord), with his wife Inger Olsdatter, 34, and children Mikkel 19 and Ingebor 10; in household 2 his son Ole Jonssen, 30, farmed in partnership with his father ('i fællesskab med faderen'). ¶2

1801, Ole beside him: his son Ole Jonssen, 30, 'Mand, begge i 1. aegteskab, i faellesskab med faderen' - married, both in their first marriage, farming in partnership with his father - heads the second Sandvig household (household number inferred from the status order). He is the Ole baptised in spring 1771 and the only Ole Jonsen at Sandvig in 1801. In household 1 Jon, 55, is in his second marriage (her first) with Inger Olsdatter, 34, children Mikkel 19 and Ingebor 10, and servants Ingebor Jensdatter, Guri Pedersdatter and Niels Jonssen. ¶9

Death and burial: 'John Olsen Sandvig', aged 72, died 2 September and buried 13 September 1818, Selbu burials 1816-1824 p. 202 no. 27 (scan and index); indexed again in the parallel book (source 16317) as 'Jon Ols'. The residence column was not legible in the crop, so the place Sandvik rests on the surname Sandvig and the bygdebok. Age 72 gives birth about 1746, agreeing with the 1801 census (55) and the existing ABT 1746, against the bygdebok's 1747; the birth estimate is unchanged. Near miss: another 'Jon Ols', aged 72, died 4 June and buried 7 June 1818 (index pg00000007095606), has no farm and was not checked on the scan. ¶7

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Confidence raised from Probable to Confirmed, as father of Ingebor Jonsdatter (see her page). Her baptism on the First Sunday after Epiphany (8 January) 1775 names her parents as 'John Olsen Sandvig' and 'Ingeborg Ols d.' (Selbu klokkerbok 1766-1776, canvas 115 right page, column 3). His wife was churched on 29 January 1775. The adult Ingebor married in 1794 as Ingeborg Jonsdatter of Sandvig, and no rival Sandvik Ingeborg was found in the baptisms of 1771 - mid-1774 or 1775-76; the full argument, and the one unread span (June - December 1774), is on her page. The baptism places Ingebor between Marit (1773) and his second marriage (1787), so her 1801 census age of 28 was two years high. He was also godfather, as 'John O. Sandvig', to Anne, daughter of Peder Bjørnsen Svinaas, on 18 June 1775 (canvas 121), an early tie to the Svinaas farm she married into. ¶6

Upgrade of the link to his father Ole Jonsen (d. 1767), now Confirmed. (1) In 1762 Ole Jonsen of Sandvik had one son, Joen Olsen, 14. (2) In 1767 the same Ole left one son, Jon Olsen, about 20. (3) His father-in-law's probate, Slinde, 17 Oct 1772 (24757 fol. 530), names 'Ingeborg Olsdatter gift med Jon Olsen Sandvig'. (4) The only other Jon Olsen at Sandvik was the son of the other Ole Jonsen, of Øster Sandvik (d. 1768): that probate gives him as 'Jon Olsen 16 aar' in 1768, and the endorsement of 1777 (24758 fol. 556b) records that 'Myndlingen Joen Olsen' had died, with his share passing to his mother Gurue Pedersdatter and his siblings Peder, Marithe and Ingri. That Jon was a minor and unmarried, so he cannot be the husband of Ingeborg Olsdatter, who lived to 1818. All. ¶13

Corrections 2

Correction: death changed from '1818' (bygdebok) to 2 SEP 1818 from the burial entry above. ¶8

Correction: his mother was Ingebor Michelsdatter, not Kari Olsdatter. The skifte after 'Ingebor Michels Datter', wife of Ole Jonsen, at Sandvigen, 17 Jun 1762 (Skifteprotokoll 5, 24755, fol. 246a), names her only child as 'Joen Olsen 14 aar' (born about 1748), with his father's brother Ole Jonsen Sandwig as his guardian. Kari Olsdatter, the widow in Ole Jonsen's probate of 1767, was therefore Ole's second wife and Jon's stepmother. ¶12

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Not his father: the probate after Ole Jonsen Sandvigen, 20 Jun 1742 (card 77804 c1715; read by the orchestrator), names children Jon Olsen 16, Ole Olsen 10, Anne 13, Marit 5 - this Jon was born about 1726, twenty years too early. Remaining candidates for his father: the Ole Jonsen Sandvig buried 18 Jun 1767 aged 50 and the Ole Jonsen Sandvigen buried Jun 1768 aged 44 (16311 c21, c26). ¶10
