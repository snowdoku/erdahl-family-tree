Life story

Birth estimate: her ages give a birth of about 1726-28, namely 1726 from the burial age of 83 in February 1809 and about 1728 from the census age of 73 in February 1801. The birth field keeps the census estimate; her baptism has not been found. ¶7

The earliest ancestor so far identified on Kari Pedersdatter's side, and the earliest person in this whole patch. 1801 census, Selbu, rural residence 0034 Eidum, household 07, person 035: 'Marit Jonsdatter', 73, 'HANS MODER' (his mother, i.e. Bersvend Pederssen's), 'Enke efter 1te aegtesk.' (widow after her first marriage), and the census records her condition in one word: FATTIG - poor. She was living in her son's cottage on the Eidem farm at seventy-three, four generations above the man who would be buried from Zoar Lutheran in South Dakota. ¶1

Burial index re-read: Digitalarkivet record pg00000001727635, Selbu ministerialbok 1801-1815, burials 1809, p. 117 no. 3 - 'Marite Jonsd.', 'Enken' (the widow), residence Eidem, aged 83, died 28 February 1809, buried on the Third Sunday in Lent, which in 1809 (Easter 2 April) was 5 March. The burial date is set from it. The index names no relative and the scan has not been read. ¶5

1801 census re-checked: Selbu, rural residence 0034 Eidum, household 07, person 035 - Marit Jonsdatter, 73, 'Hans moder' (mother of the head, Bersvend Pederssen, 47, cottager with land), widow after her first marriage (enke efter 1te aegteskab), poor (fattig). ¶6

Death and burial, probable: 'Marite Jonsd.', 'Enken' (the widow), residence Eidem, aged 83, died 28 February 1809 and buried on the Third Sunday in Lent, which in 1809 was 5 March. Selbu ministerialbok 1801-1815, burials 1809, p. 117 no. 3 (index only; scan not read). ¶3

Burial scan read (by the orchestrator): Selbu ministerialbok (Digitalarkivet source 16312), burials 1809 no. 3 - 'Pauperfrue[?] Marite Jonsd. Eidem, død d. 25[?]de Febr., gl. 83 Aar' - Marit Jonsdatter of Eidem, died the 25th[?] of February, aged 83. The first word is a status word read with doubt, possibly marking her as a pauper, which agrees with 'fattig' (poor) in the 1801 census. No son is named. ¶8

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Why this burial is taken to be hers (Probable): she was a widow of 73 at Eidum in the 1801 census, living with her son Bersvend Pederssen, and this is a widow Marit Jonsdatter of Eidem who died eight years later aged 83 - two years older than the census age implies (birth about 1726 against about 1728), an ordinary slip for a woman of that age. The other two Marit Jonsdatters at Eidum in 1801 were born about 1761 and 1784 and cannot be 83 in 1809. A widow of the same name moving onto the farm between 1801 and 1809 cannot be ruled out from the index alone; the scan entry, if it names a relative, would settle it. ¶4

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed. The link to her son rests on a primary record already read and cited: the 1801 census of Selbu, rural residence 0034 Eidum, household 07, enters her as person 035, 'Marit Jonsdatter', 73, with family position 'Hans moder' (his mother), in the household of Bersvend Pederssen, 47 (see his page). The census states the mother-son relationship outright, which is the Confirmed standard (a primary record tying this person to the child already in the tree). The doubts recorded in her notes concern her burial (which Marit Jonsdatter of Eidem died in 1809) and her own parents, not her motherhood of Bersvend. ¶11

Records that disagree 1

Discrepancy over the death day: the scan reading gives 25 February 1809, with the digit marked uncertain, while the Digitalarkivet index of the same entry (pg00000001727635) gives 28 February. The death field keeps 28 February 1809, because the index transcriber's reading is unqualified and the scan reading is itself marked doubtful; neither is better than the other by much. A high-resolution crop of the date on 16312, burials 1809 no. 3, would settle it. The burial date, 5 March 1809, is not affected. ¶9

Alternatives considered and set aside 2

1762 extra tax list lead (no link made): at Svinås 739, Selbu (landlord Conferentz Raad Schiøller), the couple 'Peder B?rsen' and Marit Jonsdatter have no children over 12 (Bersvend, b. about 1754, was too young to be listed). The patronymic is uncertain: the orchestrator read 'Bi-ersen' or 'Bä-rsen', split over a line break. It could be Bersen (Bersvendsen), which would fit the naming of a son Bersvend after his grandfather, or Biersen (Bjørnsen); read Baarsen (Bårdsen). ¶10a

Caution: in 1758 'Paul Svinaas og Marit Jonsd.' had a son Jon baptised. No Peder with a wife Marit Jonsdatter is at any of the five Eidem holdings in 1762. ¶10b

Searches that found nothing 1

Probate search, negative: no probate after her was found in Strinda og Selbu skifteprotokoll 10b (Digitalarkivet 24744) among the Selbu sessions of August 1809 (images 103-104: Berge, Welve, a Mali Gundersdatter of a cottage under Eidum, Aunet) and of 1810 (images 107-113). The Eidum entry at image 103 (fol. 646) is for 'Huusmandskone Mali [G?]undersdatter', not her. A pauper widow may have left no estate to divide. ¶12

Open questions and the next record to read 2

Would settle it: Bersvend Pedersen's confirmation (about 1770) or marriage (1783) naming his home farm, or a probate after Peder. ¶10c

Lead tested, no link made: the 1762 Svinaas couple 'Peder B?rsen' and Marit Jonsdatter are Peder Baardsen and Marithe Jonsdatter. His probate (registered at Svinaas 16 Mar 1790, Stjør- og Verdal skifteprotokoll 10, 24763 p. 223-224, views 237-238) names his widow Marithe Jonsdatter (lagverge Ole Baardsen Fossum) and their only child, Marithe Pedersdatter, 35, with her farbror Jon Baardsen Sandvig as guardian. The estate was insolvent (sold at auction; Svinaas went to Jon Paulsen). Bersvend Pedersen is not an heir, so if this widow were our Marit, Bersvend would have to be her son by another man; the 1801 census entry 'enke efter 1te aegteskab' argues against that. The identification of this widow with Bersvend's mother is unproven and not entered. ¶13
