About the family

Widow of Tor Helgesen of Sør-Strand. His probate (DA 25285 fol. 558b-559a, view 571, opened 6 Oct 1722; card 13727) is held between 'hans efterlatte og nu igienlevende Egte quinde Jngebor Joensdatter og begge deris Sammen Auflede... 2de Børn', the son Helge Torsen 14 and the daughter Jngebor Torsdaatter 8. The guardian chosen for the widow is written 'Jngebiør Jonsdaat[ter]... til sin Lauværge' (name of the guardian not read). ¶1

Life story

The 1722 opening (DA 25285 fol. 558b, view 571, lower part of the right-hand column) was re-read for her guardian: 'forbemeldte Jngebiør Jonsdaat[ter]... selff Nærværende [ink blot]..., tillige med dend afdøde S. Mands Brødre Giul Helgesen Skafwelden og Ole Helgesen [Agnerud?], samt Søster Mand [Niels?] Olsen [Gaukj?]'. She was present herself. The guardian's name, if one was written, is under the blot. No kin of hers is named in the opening, so her father is not given. Her parents are still unknown. ¶4
