About the family

Father of Bexey Jane (Creed) Hicken and grandfather of Lydia Adelaide (Hicken) Hayter. Gravestone at Sturgeon (Find a Grave 75268502, photographs read): 'James Creed, died Jan. 4, 1906, aged 75' and on the same obelisk 'Jane Chapman Creed, died June 17, 1906'. ¶1

Children, from the baptism registers and censuses: Catherine (born 27 November 1855, baptised 29 March 1856), Elizabeth Chapman (born 11 October 1857, baptised 14 April 1858), William Henry (born 16 September 1860), Bexey Jane (born 19 May 1862, baptised 27 April 1863), Ellen (born 22 May 1864, baptised 15 June 1865; died 1884 per the compiler), James Joseph (born 23 or 24 June 1866, baptised 9 July 1867), Mary Isabella (born 20 January 1870, baptised 11 February 1871) and Margaret Ann (born 3 October 1873, baptised 23 July 1874). That is eight children. ¶9

Parentage: son of Joseph Bennet Creed (about 1788-1868) and Catherine Morrison (about 1790-1875). Joseph's will, made at Township 59 on 13 February 1868, leaves three pounds to 'my dearly beloved son James', two pounds to his daughter Mary and the residue to his son William, with maintenance for his wife Catherine. Joseph and Catherine are buried in the same Sturgeon Baptist cemetery as James; their stone reads 'a native of P. E. Island' for him and 'a native of the Isle of Mull, Scotland' for her - which is why James's family gave its origin as Scotch. James named his eldest daughter Catherine. Moved from Probable to Confirmed on the strength of the will (Island Register transcription of the registered copy). ¶11

Life story

Per the compiled Creed genealogy (Robert P. Murray, Island Register): born 1831 at Sturgeon, son of Joseph Bennett Creed and Catherine Morrison; married Mary Jane Chapman on 22 February 1854 at Georgetown; children Effie, Catherine (1855), Elizabeth Chapman (1857), Bexey Jane (1862), Ellen (1864-1884), John (1865), James Joseph (1868-1946), Mary Isabella (1870-1952) and Margaret Ann (1873-1957). The same compiler descends Joseph Bennett Creed from Captain William Creed (c.1739-1809), the Limerick-born ship-owner and member of the Island assembly; that descent has not been checked. ¶3

Wedding notice, The Islander, 10 March 1854: 'At Georgetown, on Wednesday, the 22d ult., by W. B. Aitken, Esq., J.P., Mr. James Creed, of Lot 59, Three Rivers, to Miss Jane Chapman, of Lot 61 [as transcribed], Murray Harbour.' ¶5

Marriage: James Creed married Jane Chapman on 22 February 1854 (PEI marriage register index, record 10090; image FamilySearch film 4594918, frame 272). The compiled genealogy places the wedding at Georgetown. ¶6

1881 census (Township 59, King's County, household 309): James Creed, 49, farmer, Bible Christian, of Scottish origin, born P.E.I., with his wife Mary (48, of English origin) and children Catherine 25, Elizabeth 23, William 21, Ellen 17, James 15, Mary 11 and Margaret 7. ¶2

1891 census (index): Lot 59, Kings County: James Creed, about 59, with his wife Jane Mary, his daughter Elizabeth and his son Joseph James [James Joseph]. His married daughter Margaret Ann Davey and his son William Henry Creed were living next door. ¶7

Birth: the 1901 census gives his birth date as 17 June 1831, which agrees with his gravestone (died 4 January 1906, aged 75). The compiled Creed genealogy gives his middle name as Benjamin; the 1901 census has 'James B.' ¶4

1901 census (16 April 1901, Township 59, Kings County, page 5, image read): Creed, James B., head, born 17 June 1831, 69, born PEI, Baptist, farmer working on his own account; Mary J., wife, born 24 May [1832/33], born PEI, English origin; James J., son, married, born 24 June 1866, 34; Maggie E., daughter-in-law, born 11 August 1867; Lizzie, daughter, single, Methodist; Effie L., granddaughter, born 31 May 1890; Essie M., granddaughter, born 14 November 1897. His sons-in-law Cephas Davey (blacksmith) and his son William H. Creed farmed in the neighbouring households. ¶8

Corrections 1

Correction: the compiled Creed genealogy omits his son William Henry Creed (born 16 September 1860; in James's household in 1881, aged 21, and his neighbour in 1891 and 1901). It wrongly includes a son John born 9 July 1865 - the baptism index gives that child to John and Anne Creed - and lists 'Effie' as a daughter, whereas Effie Lavinia Creed, born 31 May 1890 at Sturgeon and baptised in 1893 with only her mother named, is James's granddaughter in the 1901 census, evidently the child of his unmarried daughter Elizabeth (Lizzie). ¶10
