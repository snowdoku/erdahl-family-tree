Life story

Washington Dept. of Health death certificate, DA reference {052531D4-9FFF-4570-B006-CA665B846C06}, image 1410, document 8, doc. ref. id 1096: "Gunder Slind, date of death 30 Jan 1959, age 68, male, death place Port Blakely, Kitsap, Washington. FATHER NAME: LARS SLIND. MOTHER NAME: BRYNHILD HANSTAD." Age 68 in January 1959 gives a birth in 1890 or 1891. ¶1

Marriage: CLARA HOLM, 1945, Snohomish County, Washington (two index entries, Snohomish County Auditor marriage records). ¶2

He is very probably a younger brother of ELIAS LARSEN SLIND (b. 20 Nov 1881), who was a pallbearer at Oline Slind Eidem's funeral at Zoar Lutheran in 1955, and the patronymic is the only thing that links them - so the brotherhood is a lead rather than a fact. He is NOT the Gunder Slind, b. abt 1907, of the Lacrosse, Whitman County household, nor Gunder Larson Slind (30 Nov 1886 - 6 Dec 1947) of Fort Snelling, although the last of those shares the same father's name. ¶3

Port Blakely, on Bainbridge Island, was a sawmill town - one of the standard destinations for Norwegian men who did not take up land, and a very different kind of place from the Whitman County wheat farms. ¶4
