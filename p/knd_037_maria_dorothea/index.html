About the family

Parents (identification Probable): her father Jacob is probably the Johann Jacob Kreutz who married Maria Dorothea, daughter of Johann Caspar 'Stierlen', at Bietigheim on 13 May 1738 (groom row 7053511, bride row 907053511, image 1184901-00013). Life story

Marriage: she married Christoph Adam Knodel at Bietigheim on 12 February 1759 (also entered at Vaihingen under 1760). The row 'Georg Jacob, Maria Dorothea' is read as groom's father and bride. The alternative, that Maria Dorothea is the groom's mother (Georg Jacob's wife) and the bride is not indexed, was tested and is unlikely: no marriage row in the dumps lacks the spouse, and two-name bride rows always end with the groom. Her role as Christoph Adam's wife does not depend on this row: the 1761, 1762 and 1765 children's rows name her as their mother. Her maiden surname and parents are in no indexed row; the marriage images 1184901-00036 (Bietigheim) and 1184562-00588 (Vaihingen) should give them. ¶2

Burial (Probable identification): a 'Maria Dorothea' with the single relative 'Christoph Adam' was buried at Vaihingen an der Enz on 24 Jan 1812 (Ancestry 61023 record 4412566, image 1184565-00586). The row has no surname, which is why the earlier searches for 'Maria Dorothea Knodel' missed it. A one-relative burial row normally names the husband (or father of a child); a Maria Dorothea married to a Christoph Adam at Vaihingen fits her. Age not indexed; the image would give it and settle whether she is the wife of 1759 (then about 70-75). Entered as death BEF 24 JAN 1812, Probable. ¶6

Surname: the independent FamilySearch extraction 'Germany, Marriages, 1558-1929' (VCY7-WYZ, source 2:1G7C3SP) reads her surname 'Creuz', so the Ancestry readings 'Treuzen'/'Creubzen' are genitive forms of Creuz/Kreuz. The FamilySearch Family Tree has her as 'Maria Dorothea Kreuzer', 1740-1812 (KCHP-1XC; user tree, not evidence). ¶11

The case for this identification 2

Identity: wife of Christoph Adam Knodel and mother of Christoph Ludwig Knodel (born Vaihingen an der Enz 6 February 1765). The 1765 birth entry and Christoph Ludwig's 1833 burial entry both name her 'Maria Dorothea', and the Bietigheim baptisms of 1761 and 1762 name the same parents. ¶1a

Surname and father (Nov 2026): the bride's own row of the 12 Feb 1759 Bietigheim marriage in the Württemberg Lutheran index (Ancestry 61023, record 907053778, image 1184901-00036) reads 'Maria Dorothea Treuzen', relatives 'Jacob, Christoph Adam' (her father, then the groom). The Vaihingen entry of the same marriage under 1760 (record 904778794, image 1184562-00588) reads 'Maria Dorothea Creubzen', relatives 'Jacob, Cristoph Adam'. Both are genitive forms of one surname, read here as Creutz (Kreuz); 'Treuzen' and 'Creubzen' are the indexers' readings of the same Fraktur word, and the exact spelling needs the image. This also settles the earlier doubt whether Maria Dorothea was the bride or the groom's mother: she has her own bride row naming Christoph Adam as spouse. Her grade (on her link to Christoph Ludwig, b. 1765) is unchanged at Probable, because the 1765 birth row still gives the relatives without role labels. ¶5
