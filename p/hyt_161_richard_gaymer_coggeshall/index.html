Life story

Will: 'RICHARD GAYMER of Coggeshall fuller, 3 April 1571' (Emmison, Essex Wills, Bishop of London's Commissary Court, abstract no. 347). The abstract, read in archive.org full-text snippets, begins 'To Richard my son £26 6s.8d. which is in the hands of one John OYLY of Terling, £10 to be paid at Michaelmas 1572. To Richard a flockbed, 2 of the best hutches, 2 Flanders brass pots, the greatest kettle, and a malt quern and all things belonging, 2 pewter platters, 2 pewter dishes...'. The book's index lists 'Gaymer, Al, Rd* 347', i.e. an Alice is also named in the will. The rest of the abstract (executor, witnesses, probate date) was not reached in the snippets. ¶1

Burial: 'Ric Gaymer' buried at Coggeshall 4 Apr 1571 (FreeREG, noted), the day after the will. Marriage: Richard Gaymer and Alice Webbe, Coggeshall, 8 Feb 1561/2 (FreeREG). An Alice Gaymer married John Thorison at Terling on 25 Jun 1571 (FreeREG), very probably his widow. ¶2

Will, rest of the abstract, rebuilt phrase by phrase from archive.org full-text snippets of Emmison no. 347: after the legacies to Richard the son ('... a cupboard, and a table with the benchboard; if he die before 20, to Alice my wife. To her as much yarn as will make a remnant, and a bed. The rest of my goods to my Wife, whom I make ex'rix. I ordain William FULLER overseer. Wit. William FINCHE, George BROWNING, John CATTERALL writer.' The next abstract (no. 348, George Carrington of Mundon) follows directly, so no probate date is printed. The will names no parent, sibling or Webbe kin, so it gives no lead to his own parents or to Alice's. The son Richard was under 20 in April 1571. ¶4

Probate index check: the Essex Record Office's printed index Wills at Chelmsford, vol. 1 (1400-1619), read in full-text snippets, lists only two Gaymer testators: 'Gaymer, Richard, fuller, Coggeshall 1571' and 'Richard, miller, Terling 1613'. So no earlier Gaymer will (a possible parent of this Richard) survives in the Essex courts held at Chelmsford, and the Terling miller is the only other Richard Gaymer testator of the period, which fits his identification as this fuller's son. The index has no Webbe of Coggeshall entry in the snippets seen. ¶5

Will read on the original: ERO D/ABW 16/203, free watermarked image (Essex Archives Online id 488962). 'In the name of god Amen the 3 day of Aprill in the yeare of our lord god 1571 and in the 13 yeare of... Quene Elizabeth... I Rychard Gaymer of Coggeshall in the County of Essex fuller', to be buried in the parish church of Coggeshall; 'unto Rychard Gaymer my sonne' £26 6s 8d of lawful money, paid to him at 20, 'in the hands of one John Oyly of Terling'; household goods to 'the said Rychard my sonne'; 'and if the said Rychard my sonne shall depart before his age of xx yeares I will that all that I have given him [shall] remayne to Alis my wiffe'; yarn and a bed to Alice; 'All the residue of my goods not bequethed I geve to Alis my wiffe whom I make my sole executrix'; overseer William Fuller. Witnesses William Finche, George Browning and Iames [James] Catterall (Emmison's abstract prints 'John CATTERALL'; the image reads James). This verifies the Emmison abstract (no. 347) used. Like the abstract, the original calls Alice only 'my wiffe', never the son's mother, and names no parent, sibling or Webbe. Grade unchanged (Probable). ¶8

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

2 on the identification · 2 searches with no result

The case for this identification 2

Why Probable as father of Richard Gaymer of Terling (see his page, m. Margaret Mason at Terling 1590, d. 1613): (1) his son Richard was a minor in April 1571, so born about 1560-1571, which fits a first marriage in 1590; (2) the son's legacy was money held by John Oyly of Terling, and the widow Alice remarried at Terling within three months, so the family moved to Terling, where the younger Richard is found from 1590; (3) no other Richard Gaymer appears in the Essex registers or wills of the period (FreeREG Essex Gaymer 1540-1640, 63 rows; Emmison index). No record names the Terling miller as son of the Coggeshall fuller, so this stays Probable. ¶3

Continuity to Terling strengthened: his widow Alice married John Thorison of Terling, miller, on 25 Jun 1571 and was buried there as Thorison's wife on 20 May 1572, nine days after the baptism of twins. Thorison's will of 1 Jan 1590/1 (ERO D/ACW 5/51, original read on the free image) appoints 'Richarde Gaymer' overseer, and that Richard Gaymer was a miller of Terling by 1608 (ERO Q/SR 186/48, 'Richard Gaymor and John Road, millers, all of Terling') and at his death in 1613. This places the fuller's under-age son Richard in his stepfather's household and trade at Terling, the bridge that the identification lacked. Grade unchanged (Probable): the overseer clause carries no relationship word, and no record names the Terling miller as the fuller's son. ¶6

Searches that found nothing 2

Parents (negative): the ERO catalogue lists no Gaymer will or other Gaymer document in Essex before 1571 other than his own (D/ABW 16/203); the spelling variants Gaymor, Gamer, Gamar and Gaymar gave nothing earlier. TNA Discovery has no Essex Gaymer item before 1640 (the Gaymers it lists are of Rye, Sussex). Test record for his parents: the Coggeshall manor court rolls (Coggeshall Abbey manor, post-Dissolution) and the 1524/25 lay subsidy for Coggeshall (TNA E 179/108), neither available free online. ¶7

Parents (negative): a fresh Essex Archives Online search for Gaymer and Gaymor found no Essex Gaymer document before this will, and an archive.org full-text search for 'GAYMER' or 'GAMER' with Coggeshall found no earlier Gaymer in printed Essex sources (only the Rye, Sussex family). Test record for his parents: the Coggeshall manor court rolls and the 1524/25 lay subsidy for Coggeshall (TNA E 179/108), not online free. ¶9
