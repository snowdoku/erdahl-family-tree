About the family

NEW ANCESTOR, graded on his link to his daughter Asbor Tallaksdatter (see her page). Åmli ætt og heim II (Gjøvdal), Jørundland L.nr. 36 / bruk nr. 2 (canvas 78): 'Tallak var på Jørundland i 1661 som brukar av 1 hud. Han var fødd 1631, og skiftet etter han var i 1701. Han var gift 2 gonger. Med første kona (namn ukjent) hadde Tallak sonen Olav, som døydde før far sin.' Summary: 'Tallak Olson Jørundland. Skifte 1701. g 2. m Anne Eivindsdtr. Born: Asborg g m Svenn Ormson Askland; Gunvor g m Knut Jørgenson Mjåland.' The son Olav Tallakson married Gunhild Ånonsdtr., who then married Halvor Ormson from Askland (Svend Ormsen's brother). Primary support: the 1666 vicar's census has 'Tallak 35' among the users of Jørundland and the 1664-65 list 'Thallach 30' (notes on Børuf Aadnesen Jørundland), and the Jørundland probate of 22 Nov 1701 (Skp. 1 fol. 275, card) is after Gunild Osmundsdatter, 'husbands Olle Thallacksen and Halvor Ormsen', i.e. the widow of Tallak's son Olav. No record yet names Asbør as his daughter. Bygdebok only (compiled secondary source, text rebuilt from word boxes, dates ABT), so Probable at most. ¶1

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: graded on his link to his daughter Asbor Tallaksdatter (see her page). Østre Råbyggelag Skifteprotokoll nr. 1 (DA 24349, view 278), fol. 274a, 22 Nov 1701: 'bleve paa Jørunland Registerit og werderit hvis ringe middel som fandtis efter salige Thallack Oelsen... imellem Creditorerne, saa som hans Efterlatte Hustru, Anna Evindsdaatter, og deris sammen avlede Børn, Nemlig Asbør og Gundvor Thalacksdøtter som nu er giffte, efter Svend Ormsen Askland[s] beretning (som har dend Eldste Daatter til Ehte)'. Card 39942/5977 indexes it as 'Fol. 274... Thallack Oelsen + / Anne Evindsdatter, Barn Asbør, Gundvor'. (The probate on fol. 274b-276a of the same day is a different one, after Gunild Osmundsdatter, widow of Olle Thallacksen and Halvor Ormsen, card 5979.) The probate itself names Asbør as Thallack Oelsen's daughter and says that Svend Ormsen Askland, who was present and spoke for the heirs, is married to the eldest daughter. Continuity: Svend Ormsen Askland is the husband in the tree (his own probate, Askland, 13 Oct 1705, Skp. 2 fol. 147b, and Asbør's of 1707). There is no rival: the only other daughter, Gundvor, is the younger one. The relationship is named in a primary record, so Confirmed. This tests and confirms the bygdebok (Åmli ætt og heim II, canvas 78). ¶3
