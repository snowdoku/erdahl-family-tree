Life story

A stillborn son of Paul Eidem and Gurine (Gina) Eidem, registered without a given name. Minnesota birth certificate 1923-15352, Lac qui Parle County: 'Sex M. Legitimate Yes. Date of birth Feb 26, 1923. FATHER: Paul Eidem, residence Marietta, W, age at last birthday 29, birthplace Minn., occupation Farming. MOTHER: full maiden name Gena Eidem, residence Marietta, W, age at last birthday 31, birthplace Norway, occupation Housewife. Number of children of this mother: born alive and now living 1; born dead 1.' Attended by H. Duncan; filed 18 March 1923 by Glenn Ward, registrar, Marietta. The matching death record is cert. 1923-MN-006806, 28 February 1923. ¶1

He is the 'Brother Eidem 1923-1923' of Zoar Lutheran Cemetery. ¶2

This certificate is also the record that proves Paul Eidem was born in Minnesota and not in Norway: his own son's registration gives the father's birthplace as 'Minn.' and his age as 29 in February 1923. ¶3
