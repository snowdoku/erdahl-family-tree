Life story

FIRST AMERICAN-BORN SLIND of this family. 1910 federal census, Whitman County, Washington, Lacrosse precinct, ED 276, page 78A line 4: "Alma Slind, 2, BORN WASHINGTON". Her three elder siblings were all born in Norway, so her birth dates the family's arrival in Whitman County to 1907 or 1908 and makes her the earliest Slind known to have been born in the United States. ¶1
