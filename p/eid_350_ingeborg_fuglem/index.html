Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Graded Probable on her link to her son Ole Pedersen Fuglem (see his page). Selbu i fortid og nåtid vol. 3, Fuglem, 'Brukere' (canvas 217): '1701-1711 Per Persen, f. ca. 1651, d. 1715, [...] kona Ingeborg, [...] død før 1699. Barn: 1. [...] ca. 1686, 2. Ola, f. ca. 1689' (then '1712-1735 [Bersvend] Persen, f. ca. 1686, d. 1732' and '1736-1760 Ola Persen, f. 1689, d. 1773, korporal'). This matches the 1701 manntall (38461 c112): Peder 55 at Fuglem with sons Bersvend 15 and Olle 12. ¶1

Audit 7 (1-2 Nov 2026): grade unchanged (Probable, compiled source only). Under the audit-6 rule a bygdebok-only ancestor must name the primary record that would test the link; this note supplies it. ¶4a

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Caution: 'død før 1699' follows 'kona Ingeborg' on the next line, so the book may say that Ingeborg died before 1699 (before the Selbu register begins) or that an unnamed first child died; the words between are not recovered. Her patronymic is not recovered either. ¶2

Searches that found nothing 1

Negative: the Stjør- og Verdal skifte card index 1689-1742 (77804) has only three Fuglem cards (c487-491): Jon Olsen, 23/5-1693 (widow Giertrud Sivertsdtr; children Gunder, Olluf, Siver, Ingebret Jonsen, Birgitte Jonsdtr; fol. 115b); Bersvend Pedersen Fuglum, 20/6-1732 (widow Marit Pedersdtr; son by his first wife Kjersti Jonsdtr, Peder Bersvendsen 14; by the widow Kjersti 5 and Mali 1 1/2; fol. 483); Jon Jonsen Fuglem, 20/6-1742 (fol. 1123b). There is no card for Ingeborg or for Peder Pedersen Fuglem, so the test record named in her notes does not exist in this index (Peder died 1715, in the 1701-1727 gap between protocols). Bersvend Pedersen (d. 1732) is presumably the 'Bersvend Pedersen 15' of the 1701 manntall at Fuglum, her son by the bygdebok; his skifte names no grandparents. Grade unchanged. ¶5

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Test record: the probate after Per Persen of Fuglem (d. 1715), or after Ingeborg herself if she died between 1689 and 1699, in the Stjør- og Verdal probate card index 1689-1742 (Digitalarkivet 77804), FUGLEM cards; either would show whether Ola Persen (b. about 1689) was her son. ¶4b
