About the family

Daughter: Brynhild (Brynil) Eriksdatter, born 5 March 1796, baptised at the Annunciation service 1796 as daughter of 'Erik Torstensen Uglum og Magli Christens d:', who married Gunder Johnsen in 1815 (Brynhild Eriksdatter Eidem). ¶4

Siblings (inference only, from godparent groups): Ole Torstensen of Uglem, Reinholdt (Reinal) Torstensen of Slinden and Guru Torstensdatter of Gulseth stand together as godparents in 1796. If Reinholdt is Reinal Torstenssen Slind, Erik is his brother; this is not proved and no link is made. ¶5

Parents, hypothesis only - no link is made: Erik may be a son of the soldier Torsten (Tosten) Olsen Slinden and Ane (Anne) Erichsdatter, betrothed on 11 May and married on 15 June 1766 in Selbu. The names fit: Erik after Ane's father, and his brothers Ole Torstensen (born about 1770, of Uglem/Uglan in 1801) and Reinholdt (Reinal) Torstensen (born about 1771, of Slinden) after Torsten's father Ole and, perhaps, Ane's first husband, the late Reinald Persen Slinden (an identification of the two Anes that is itself inference). The couple's known children are Kari, baptised 24 May 1767 (godparents Ole Pedersen Slinden, Hans Vigen, Siri Seter), and Guri, baptised 22 January 1769 (godparents Bersvend Slinden, Ole Slinden, Kari Aglum and others). ¶9

Brother Ole: Ole Torstensen of Uglem was buried on Palm Sunday 1811, died 26 March, aged 41, birth year 1770 (Digitalarkivet burial index, Selbu 1801-1815, p. 152). This fits the Olle baptised in 1770 to Torsten Eriksen Uglum and Kari Reinholtsdatter, and the Ole Torstensen aged 31 at Uglan in 1801, and so supports Erik's placement in this family. ¶14

Son's confirmation: Selbu confirmations of 1808 (16312, canvas 105 right), boys no. 3, 'Torsten Erichsen Uglem, 15 Aar', the son Torsten of the 1801 household. Selbu confirmation lists of this period give only the name, the farm and the age, with no parents, so they cannot prove parentage on this line. ¶17

Life story

1801 census: Selbu, rural residence 0030 Uglan, household 01, 'Erik Torstensen, 35, Husbonde, Bonde og gaard beboer' (farmer in possession), 'begge i 1. aegteskab' (both in their first marriage), with wife Magli Christensdatter 32 and their children Torsten 7, Brynil 5 and Christen 2. Household 02 on the same residence is Ole Torstensen 31, probably his brother. ¶1

Burial, second index entry: the same burial is indexed again in the overlapping register for 1824-1842, burials 1829, p. 370, no. 7, as 'Erich Torstenson Uglum', aged 63, died 27 January and buried 1 February 1829. This entry carries the farm name Uglum (Uglem), which ties the 1829 burial to Erik of Uglem in 1825. Neither index entry gives his marital status, so whether he was a widower (enkemand) is not yet known; the scan would answer it. ¶7

1825 census: Selbu, Uglem, household 01, 'Erik Tostens., 60, Gmd.' (gaardmand, farmer), with his married son Tosten Eriksen 32, Tosten's wife Helge Johnsd. 37 and their son Erik 5, and Christen Eriks. 27, Hmd. (husmann, cottager) - the sons Torsten and Christen of 1801. No wife is listed with him. ¶2

1825 census, household detail: at Uglem, household 01, on 27 November 1825, 'Erik Tostens.', 60, gaardmand (farmer), heads the household with his married son Tosten Eriksen, 32, Tosten's wife Helge Johnsd., 37, their son Erik, 5, and Christen Eriks., 27, husmann (cottager). No wife of Erik is listed. Tosten married Helga Jans Datt, both of Auglem (Uglem), on 21 June 1819. ¶8

Baptism (crop re-read by the orchestrator): 'Döbt (1) Erich. Fædr: Torsten Erichs. Uglum, Kari Reinholts D. Fadd: Svend Uglum, Thomas Belle, Torkild Uglum, Ragnild Rønsberg, Johana Hove.' - 7th Sunday after Trinity, 13 July 1766 (Easter 30 Mar 1766; the worker's 20 Jul is a slip), Selbu ministerialbok 16311, canvas 20 left. A brother Olle was baptised Whit Tuesday, 5 June 1770, parents 'Tosten Erichs. Ugl., Karen Reinholdsd.' (canvas 39 left). The parents, soldier Tosten Erichsen and Kari Reinholtsdatter, married on the 2nd Sunday after Trinity, 16 June 1765 (canvas 18 left). ¶11

Death and burial: 'Erick Tostenssoen', aged 63, died 27 January and was buried 1 February 1829 (Selbu burials 1829, pp. 286-287 no. 5, index; farm not read on the scan). His age fits 35 in 1801 and 60 in 1825, and he is the only Erik Torstensen of that age in the Selbu index. ¶3

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 3

Why Probable, and what would settle it: no single record names Erik Torstensen as the father of Gunder Johnsen's wife. The link is a chain of primary records: (1) she married Gunder on 11 Jun 1815 as 'Pigen Brynhild Eriksd. Auglem' - a maid of Uglem; (2) she is the widowed mother of Ole Gundersen (bapt. 1826, son of Gunder Johnsen and Brynhild Eriksd.) at Grindhaugen in 1865 and 1875, born 1796; (3) her 1880 burial gives her birthplace as Uglem and birth year 1795; (4) the only Brynhild Eriksdatter born at Uglem in the 1790s is Brynil, born 5 Mar 1796 to Erik Torstensen Uglum and Magli Christensdatter, who is 5 in their Uglan household in 1801; (5) the bygdebok (vol. 4 p. 203) calls Gunnar Jonsen's wife Brynhild of Uglan, b. 1796. The one rival, Brynhild Eriksdatter born 12 May 1797 at Langli to Erik Pedersen, is eliminated: she married Arnt Olsen of Kval on 4 Jun 1819 with her father as witness, and was his widow at Sletne in 1865. Her second daughter was named Maglie (1816), after Magli Christensdatter, as Selbu naming custom expects. A probate (skifte) after Erik Torstensen of Uglem, d. 1829, naming his heirs (Strinda og Selbu sorenskriveri, scan-only) would make it Confirmed. ¶6

Why Probable: the farm Uglum matches Erik's adult life (Erik Torstensen Uglum in 1796; at Uglan in 1801 with his brother Ole Torstensen, 31, who fits the 1770 Olle; at Uglem in 1825; buried 1829 as 'Erich Torstenson Uglum'); the 1766 birth fits all his ages (35 in 1801, 60 in 1825, 63 at death); and his brother Reinal/Reinholdt (30 in 1801) carries the maternal grandfather's name Reinholt. ¶12a

Upgrade: Confirmed, graded on his link to his daughter Brynhild Eriksdatter Eidem (see her page), not on his own parentage (Probable). The record naming the relationship is the Selbu baptism at the Annunciation service 1796 (Selbu ministerialbok 1757-1801, source 16311, canvas 190) of Brynil, born 5 Mar 1796, daughter of 'Erik Torstensen Uglum og Magli Christens d:'. Continuity to the tree's Brynhild Eriksdatter Eidem (see her page), all on the farm Uglem: the 1801 census of Uglan (Uglem), household 01, has Erik Torstensen, 35, and his wife Magli Christensdatter, 32, both in their first marriage, with their children Torsten 7, Brynil 5 and Christen 2; she married Gunder Johnsen on 11 Jun 1815 as 'Pigen Brynhild Eriksd. Auglem' (a maid of Uglem); her 1880 burial gives her birthplace as Uglem, and the 1865 and 1875 censuses her birth as 1796. ¶18a

Records that disagree 1

Age conflict with that hypothesis: Erik's ages - 35 in February 1801, 60 in November 1825, 63 at his death in January 1829 - all put his birth about 1765-1766, before the June 1766 marriage, while the couple's first two children after it were Kari (May 1767) and Guri (January 1769). If he is their son he was born before the marriage or in 1770 or later, several years younger than all three of his recorded ages. No baptism of an Erik son of a Torsten was found in Selbu from Easter 1766 to Cantate 1770 (some page bottoms in spring 1770 are damaged). Worker is reading the following baptisms; his baptism, or a probate after Torsten Olsen Slinden naming him, would settle it. ¶10

Corrections 1

Correction: the earlier hypothesis that Erik was a son of soldier Torsten Olsen Slinden and Ane Erichsdatter (married 15 Jun 1766, children Kari 1767 and Guri 1769) is withdrawn; their known children are girls and Erik's ages predate that marriage. That couple is not Erik's family. ¶13

Alternatives considered and set aside 2

Not Confirmed: his own marriage (which might name his birth farm) is not found, and 1767-69 have not been checked for a rival Erik of Uglum. ¶12b

No rival: the only other Brynhild Eriksdatter of that age in Selbu, born 12 May 1797 at Langli to Erik Pedersen, married Arnt Olsen of Kval in 1819 with her father as witness and was his widow at Sletne in 1865 (eid_ notes on Erik). The probate after Erik (1829) that earlier notes asked for is not in the Strinda og Selbu skifteutlodningsprotokoll 1827-1840 register, so the grade rests on the baptism naming both parents and the unbroken Uglem residence 1796-1815. ¶18b

Searches that found nothing 2

Probate search, negative: no estate of Erik Torstensen of Uglem (died 27 Jan 1829) is entered in the Strinda og Selbu skifteutlodningsprotokoll 1, 1827-1840 (Digitalarkivet 24745), typed alphabetical register at images 3-9 (name | farm | tinglag | folio); the E pages list 'Ellen Eriksdr Uglum, Selbu, fol. 284' and 'Erik Olsen Eidum, fol. 170', but no Erik Torstensen. Either no probate was held or it was entered elsewhere. A probate naming his daughter Brynhild as Gunder Johnsen's wife, which would settle the Probable link, is therefore not available from this protocol. ¶15

Probate search, negative: the alphabetical register at the front of Stjør- og Verdal skifteprotokoll 11, 1796-1802 (Digitalarkivet 24764, canvas 11, T section, read on the scan) has no estate of his father Torsten Erichsen Uglem (died 11 Jun 1797). The T entries are Thor Andersen Aunes (Verdal, fol. 9), Tomas Pedersen Kallor[?] i Salboe (fol. 11), Torsten Jensen (fol. 44), Thomas Iversen Over Eidum (Stjørdal), Torsten Pedersen Storsveet i Aasen (fol. 758), Thorge Jonsen Bratmoe, Torris Torrisen Brend and Thomas Thorkildsen Brertem (Verdal, fol. 645). This register does include Selbu estates ('i Salboe'), so the volume was a real place to look. The remaining place is Strinda og Selbu skifteprotokoll 9 (24742, 1797-1802), which has no register. ¶16
