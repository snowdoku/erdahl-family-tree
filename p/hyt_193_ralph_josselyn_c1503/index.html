Life story

New evidence on the mother of his supposed children: the will of Helenor Nevell, widow, 1547 (Archdeaconry of Essex, Bastwick 36; abstract in The Genealogist n.s. 35:85) names 'my son Thomas Joslyn', daughters Alice Nevell and Agnes, and 'my 3 sons John Joslyn, Symond & John the younger'; Gilbert Nevell's will of 1550 calls John the elder 'of Chykenayle'. So John (see his page), Simon and Agnes, the three who married at Fyfield in 1544-5, were children of Helenor by a first husband named Josselyn, who was dead before she married John Nevell of Fyfield (his will, 1537, names her as his wife). If French's Ralph (born about 1503) was that first husband, he died before 1537, which fits his absence from the 1542-46 subsidies; but it would give him six children (Thomas apparently the eldest, two sons called John) by his early thirties, and neither will names Helenor's first husband. French's identification of the father therefore stays a reconstruction. Grade unchanged (Speculative). ¶2a

How sure are we?

Speculative. A plausible candidate only. Treat this link as a question, not a fact.

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Graded on his link to his son John Josselyn. Ralph, son of Ralph Josselyn of Great Canfield, is named in his father's will of 31 May 1525 (left the tenement called 'louedens' in Canfield and £3 6s. 8d.). French (NEHGR 71:245, pedigree no. 22) makes him the father of Simon Josselyn of Fyfield and Felsted (m. at Fyfield 12 Oct. 1544 Margaret Poole, will 1577/8), of John (see his page, m. at Fyfield 15 Jan. 1544/5) and of Agnes (m. at Fyfield 14 Sept. 1544 John Turnishe), because the three married at Fyfield within four months and Ralph 'probably died before 1546, as his name is not found in the very complete subsidy for that year, although the names of his mother and his sons appear'. French says 'No will or administration of his estate is now extant. The name of his wife is unknown.' The 1604/5 will of Thomas of Rochford (John's son) remits a debt to 'Jefferye Joslyn', whom French makes Simon's son, but uses no kinship word. Speculative: a reconstruction from residence, dates and the 1546 subsidy; no record names John as his son. ¶1a

Open questions and the next record to read 2

Test record: the 1546 lay subsidy for Fyfield and Great Canfield (TNA E 179/110) and Great Canfield manor court rolls for a surrender or admission naming Ralph's sons. ¶1b

Test record: the registered copies of Helenor Nevell's will and of John Nevell's will (Bretton 69, 1537), and the Great Canfield court rolls c.1525-1537 (At the Courts of Great Canfield) for a Ralph Josselyn junior with a wife Helenor or for his heirs. ¶2b
