About the family

Father of Anne Povelsdatter (see her page), Probable; graded on his link to Anne. The relationship is stated only by the bygdebok (a compiled secondary source); it fits her patronymic, her age (bygdebok ca 1668), her groom's residence 'Ildre' in 1694 and the Ildre witness, but no primary record read names her as his daughter. ¶4a

Parents: Vatne bygdebok 1 p. 312-313 names his father as Didrik Nilssen (first name recovered by the exact-phrase query "Didrik Nilssen", canvas 313 y 2286) and his mother as Ingeborg; added as Didrik Nilssen and Ingeborg (Probable). His son Didrik (b. 1665) carries the grandfather's name. ¶6

Life story

Bygdebok: the Haram bygdebok, Band 5 'Hildrestranda', Indre Hildre (gnr 24), p. 175 (nb.no 1a85520cfb9659617e20d02dae47a323, canvas 175, text rebuilt from content-search word boxes, so with gaps) has as bruk 4-4, 'Kring 1656 - kring 1675', a Paul, with a cross-reference to the Vatne bygdebok ('1-312'; his own origin or his wife's, the gap does not show which). He 'arva' a share of the farm after 'mor si' (his mother), an Ingeborg; in 1658 he had a horse and 6 cows. Children listed: 1 Knut, ca 1646 (bruk 4-5); 2 Guro; 3 Inger, 'sjå Ytre Hildre (9-5)'; 4 Marit, ca 1662, d. 1731, gift 1692 an Ole of Vigra (ca 1655-1712); 5 [name in gap], ca 1665, d. before 7 Jan 1707 (skifte), married Brite Pedersd.; 6 'Anne, ca 1668, sjå Alvestad (8-1)'. Alvestad bruk 8-1 (p. 296) is Jørgen Amundss. and his wife Anne Paulsd. (see Anne Povelsdatter). ¶1

Circumstantial support: at the 1694 betrothal of Jørgen Aamundsen and Anne Povelsdatter (Haram, index) the trulovar were Hans of Myren and Jetmund of Ildre (Hildre). The bygdebok (p. 192, Ytre Hildre bruk 9-5, c. 1692-1699) has Inger, Paul's daughter from Indre Hildre (4-4), married 1692 to Jetmund [Knutsen] of Kalvøya (ca 1655-1727), who farmed Ytre Hildre in those years. So the Jetmund of Ildre who witnessed Anne's betrothal was very probably her sister's husband. ¶3

Own parents (not added as people): Vatne bygdebok 1 (nb.no 4b04be8bf506ff0889a50e7bf852742b), book p. 312, canvases 313-314, rebuilt from word boxes, has a '[first name not recovered] Nilssen' farming a Vatne bruk from about 1607 until about 1656-57, when he died, married to an Ingeborg, who outlived him and died about 1667. Ingeborg inherited land at Indre-Hildre. Their children include one born about 1606, one born about 1622 (a brukar), 'Amund, f.ca. 1624, brukar', and 'Paul. Han [vart] brukar av [Indre] Hildre, som han hadde [arva] av mor si'. So Paul's parents were probably this Nilssen of Vatne and Ingeborg. The father's first name lies in a gap and is still unread. ¶5a

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the identification · 1 search with no result · 3 open questions

The case for this identification 1

Primary support: the 1666 vicar's census (index) has at Indre Hildre 'Poffuel', bonde, aged 50 (b. about 1616), sharing the tithe with 'Enken' (the widow of the previous tenant, as the bygdebok says: 'enkja brukte halvparten 1666'), and a son Dirick, b. 1665, who matches the bygdebok's child no. 5 born ca 1665. Daughters were not counted in that census, so Anne (b. about 1668) cannot appear. ¶2

Searches that found nothing 1

Negative on his own Test record: the skifte after his son Didrik (bygdebok 'd. før 7.1.1707') was not found. (1) The Sunnmøre probate card index 1689-1728 (source 77797, cards filed by farm) has for Hildre only 29/10-1724 Kari Nielsdtr. (Hildre ytre) and 29/5-1726 Knud Eriksen (images 1247-1251); no Ildre/Hildre card 1700-1720. (2) Skifteprotokoll 2A (24545) browsed from 9 Oct 1706 (image 325) to 17 Feb 1707 (image 356): the only Grytten (Haram) skipreide probates are Valderhaug 6 Dec 1706 (image 340) and a farm read 'Skiaaneg' 22 Jan 1707 (Guri Andersdatter, image 354); January 1707 otherwise has Hessen 3 and 8 Jan (images 349, 351) in Borgen skipreide. The phrase in the rebuilt Band 5 text is '... slik at... då vart... skifte), gift... Brite Pedersd.', which may say there was no skifte or that it was held later. ¶7a

Open questions and the next record to read 3

Test record: the probate (skifte) after his son, the child no. 5 of the bygdebok (Dirick, b. 1665?), of Indre Hildre, registered before 7 Jan 1707, whose heirs would be his siblings including 'Anne Povelsdatter, gift med Jørgen Aamundsen Alvestad'. It is not in the Sunnmøre or Haram probate indexes (no Hildre probate before 1720 in either; searched by farm), so it must be found by browsing the Sunnmøre skifteprotokoll for Dec 1706-Jan 1707 (3A 02/03) or the court book. ¶4b

Test record: the 1645 or 1647 tax lists for Vatne (Sunnmøre), and Band 5 p. 175 with more vocabulary. Note that Paul's brother Amund (b. about 1624) is a possible father for Jørgen Amundsen (b. about 1662). That would make Jørgen and Anne first cousins, a marriage that needed a royal licence at the time, so do not assume it without a record. ¶5b

Next: skifteprotokoll 2B (24546, 1707-1710) from its start, and the Sunnmøre tingbok for 1706-07. ¶7b
