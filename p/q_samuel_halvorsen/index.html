About the family

Parents (Probable): Halvor Pedersen of Lunde (d. before 20 Mar 1733) and Citrona/Sitron Jørgensdatter. Halvor's probate at Lunde, 20 Mar 1733 (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll 3b, fol. 505b-508a), names '4de Søn Samuel', a minor under his mother's guardianship. The probate after his maternal half-brother Halvor Jonsen Lunde, Valsvik, 17 May 1752 (protokoll 7a, fol. 138b, No. 69), names 'fierde Halvbroder Samuel Halvorsen Lunde', present, with brothers Peder (officer[?] in Christiania), Jørgen (Foot Guards[?], Copenhagen) and Ole. His daughter Zithone/Citrone (b. ~1753) is named after his mother. ¶3

Life story

Baptism: Leikanger ministerialbok 11564, printed p. 345 (canvas 160 R), year headed 1728 on p. 344: in the Bapt. column, on the line of 'D. III et IV [Advent]' just before 'Fest. Nat.', 'Samuel Halverson Lunde' - baptised between 12 and 25 Dec 1728. The book records two earlier sons of the same name: 'Samuel Halverson Lunde' baptised at Tjugum on Sunday Esto mihi, 5 Mar 1724 (p. 342, canvas 157 R, in the year 1724 that the priest entered out of order on pp. 341-343), and another at Dom. Invocavit, 18 Feb 1725 (p. 323), with a burial of 'Samuel Halvers. Lunde' about June 1725 (p. 324). A Samuel baptised in 1725 means the 1724 child had died, and the June 1725 burial removes the 1725 child, so the minor 4th son Samuel of Halvor Pedersen's 1733 probate is the son baptised in Dec 1728. The 1801 census age (77) points to 1724 and is about four years too high; the 1724 Samuel is ruled out only by the 1725 namesake. No burial of a Samuel at Lunde found 1729-1734 (pp. 346-35x). ¶5

1801 census at Balholmen: 77, widower after his first marriage, father-in-law of Giert Falch. kgroenha.net (no. 470) gives his birth as 1725 at Lunde. Wife unknown. ¶1

Laberg 1985 (printed p. 109): the wife of Arnfinn..., who married in 1785 (the bride is probably the 'Anna' named in that line), 'var dotter til Samuel Halvorson Lunde' (was the daughter of Samuel Halvorson Lunde); their children included Gurid, Kari and a Samuel who died unmarried. This supports his origin at Lunde and gives him a probable further daughter besides Zithone. The book does not name Zithone or Giert Falch. ¶2a

1 alternative set aside

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Not his baptism: Leikanger ministerialbok 1690-1734, printed p. 323 (canvas 138 R), under 'Anno 172[5]' (last digit overwritten, best read 5), column Bapt., Dom. Invocavit: 'Samuel Halvorson Lunde'. But the same book, p. 324 (canvas 139 R), burial column, about Trinity II-III of the same year (June 1725): 'Samuel Halvers. Lunde'. So Halvor of Lunde had a son Samuel baptised early in 1725 who died that summer. The Samuel who was the 4th son and a minor in Halvor Pedersen's probate of 1733 must be a later son given the dead brother's name, born between mid-1725 and 1733 (the 1801 census age of 77 is then at least two years too high). His own baptism should be in canvases 140-170 of the same book (1725-1734), not yet read. The 1725 entries do show that Halvor of Lunde used the name Samuel for his sons, which fits the 1733 probate. ¶4
