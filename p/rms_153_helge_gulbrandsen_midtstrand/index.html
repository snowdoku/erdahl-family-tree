Life story

Farmer at Midtstrand. Alive 7 Feb 1701, when the probate after his wife Martha Knudsdatter was held there (card 9291; DA 25283 s. 316, views 319-320, read): she 'haver efterladt sig med hendes Igienlevende Mand Helge Gulbrandsen 4 Børn, Nafnlig Knud' (of age and present), 'Gulbrand' (of age, absent), 'Even' (of age, present) and 'Berrit Helgesdatter', married to Ole Hendrichsen Hegge. The next page names 'Sønnen Knud Helgesen' among the creditors, claiming pay for his work on the farm. The estate held 2 skind in Midtstrand. ¶1

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the grade · 1 open question

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Graded Probable on his link to his son Knud Helgesen (see his page). The 1701 probate names Knud Helgesen as his and Martha's son ('efterladt sig med hendes Igienlevende Mand... 4 Børn'). The identification of that Knud with the Knud Helgesen who married Ragnild Biørnsdatter on 18 Jan 1702 (of 'Strand') and was widower at Midtstrand in 1721 rests on patronymic plus farm: the 1702 entry writes only 'Strand' (Midtstrand is one of the Strand farms), and no record calls the 1721 widower Helge Gulbrandsen's son. Short gaps (1701 to 1702; 1721 on the same farm), and no rival Knud Helgesen is known at Strand, but audit 3 keeps patronymic plus residence at Probable. ¶2

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Test record: a deed or tingbok entry by which Knud Helgesen took over Midtstrand from his father Helge Gulbrandsen (about 1701-1705), or Helge Gulbrandsen's own probate (no Midtstrand card between 1701 and 1709). Note: a Helge Gulbrandsen at Bø i Ulnes (cards 1673, 1675; wives Siri Olsdatter d. 1738) is a different, younger man. ¶3
