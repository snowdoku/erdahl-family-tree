About the family

The deceased's brother: the Dal probate itself (DA 25286 p. 122, view 72, read from the scan) opens 'Anno Christi 1738 den 15 Febr. vare vi forsamlede paa gaarden Dahl... efter afgangne sal. Mand Jone Knudsøn, som ved Døden fra sig efterlod hans efterladte Hustrue Marite Tronsdatter og begge deris sammen avlede Børn, hvilke ere 2de Sønner og 5 Døttre', lists the seven children (Syfver; Tron Jonsøn, 22; Jøran Jonsdatter, wife of Ole Hilmen; Ingerie, wife of Knud Knudsen [Breie]; Groe, wife of Ole Andersen; Marite Jonsdatter, 19; Astria Jonsdatter, 10), and says the widow Marite Tronsdatter was present with 'den sal. Mands Broder Christen Knudsen Rogne' as her guardian. Christen Knudsen of Rogne in Østre Slidre died before 5 Feb 1741 (Valdres probate card 11107: widow Astri Tronsdatter; children Knud, Tron 23, Syver 19, Olle 13, Ingerie (formerly married to Peder Hovi) and Jøran (married to Tomes Vaarum)). So Joen Knudsen's father was a Knud, and the two brothers married two women called Tronsdatter, Marit and Astri, and gave their children the same names (Syver, Tron, Jøran, Ingerie). Marit and Astri Tronsdatter were very likely sisters, but no record seen says so. ¶3

Father: Speculative. The Dal probate of 30 Nov 1664 (Valdres card 2041) after Siuffuer (Syver) Knudsen of the same farm Dal in Nordre Aurdal names a son 'Knud Dahl'. Joen Knudsen of Dal (d. before 1738) had a brother Christen Knudsen, and both brothers named a son Syver, which fits a grandfather Syver. So Knud Syversen Dahl is the obvious candidate for Joen's father. Speculative only: no record between 1664 and 1738 shows Knud at Dal with sons Joen and Christen, and Dal had several holdings. ¶4a

Son married: 'Thron Jonsøn Dahl', betrothed in Nes prestegjeld, Hallingdal, married Birthe Olsdatter on 2 Jul 1739 by licence (DA 9361 p. 42, canvas 290); he is Tron, 22 in 1738, son of Joen Knudsen. A 'Thron Dahl' stood surety on 15 Oct 1739 and a 'Siver Dahl' (probably the eldest son Syfver) at a betrothal of 15 Dec 1739. The debts and division pages of the 1738 Dal estate (DA 25286 pp. 124-125, view 73) were read for a recital of how Joen held Dal (which might name his father Knud): none found, only debts and shares. The Valdres cards for Dal run from 1664 (card 2041) straight to 1738 (card 2043), so no Dal probate of Knud is indexed. Knud Syversen stays Speculative. The Valdres panteregister begins only in 1786 (DA 13864, Aurdal tinglag 1786-1827), too late for this gap. ¶6

Life story

Of the farm Dal (Dahl) in Aurdal. Graded on his link to his daughter Astri Jonsdatter. Dal probate: the Valdres probate card 2043 (DA 39948) indexes the estate of Joen Knudsen (deceased), of Dal ('Dahl') in Nordre Aurdal, held 15 Feb 1738 (Hadeland, Land og Valdres skifteprotokoll 1737-1743, DA 25286, s. 121; the entry heading at the foot of p. 121, view 71, reads 'paa gaarden Dahl i Ourdahls Prestegield i Walders'). Widow Marite Tronsdatter. Children: 1. Syfver; 2. Tron, 22; 3. Jøran (married to Ole Hilmen); 4. Ingerie (married to Knud Knudsen Breie); 5. Groe (married to Olle Andersen); 6. Marite, 19; 7. Astrie, 10. Gross about 568, net about 216 rdr; land in Dahl with Granefoss. Identification: the bride of 1753, 'Astri Joensd[atte]r Dahle' (DA 9361 p. 88, canvas 313), carries the same forename, the patronymic Jonsdatter and the farm Dal as Astrie, daughter of Joen Knudsen of Dal, aged 10 in 1738 (so born about 1728). ¶1a

Bygdebok: Valdres bygdebok I F, gnr 107 Dal (canvas 494): 'Jon Knutsson Dal, f om 1665, d 1737, g 1701 m Marit Trondsdotter frå [Bø] 106', son of Knut Sjugurdsson Dal (b. about 1638) and his wife [forename not recovered] Kristensdotter from farm 109; 'Sonen Jon kjøpte Dal' (a purchase of 1707 is mentioned). Children as in the 1738 probate, with bygdebok birth years: Jøran? 1702 (m. 1724 Ola Andrisson), Sjugurd (Syver) 1704, Ingrid 1707, Gro 1712, Trond 1716, Marit 1719, Astrid 1721; canvas 495 adds '8 Knut' (a Knut Jonsson buried 1722) and the later holders Sjugurd Jonsson 1704-1763 and Trond Jonsson 1716-1757 (married 1739 in Nes, Hallingdal). The mother link is Speculative (forename missing). Rebuilt from nb.no content-search word boxes; dates from the rebuild are given as ABT. ¶7a

Birth estimated: a son Tron was 22 in 1738 and three daughters were married, so his children were born from about 1710 or before. ¶2

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Probable, not Confirmed: the two records are 15 years apart, and no record yet names Astri the bride as Joen Knudsen's daughter (her marriage entry names no father). A birth about 1728 also fits her son's birth in 1754 far better than the census age of 90 in 1801 (which would put her birth at 1711, and her son's birth at her age 43). The 1738 continuation (DA 25286 view 72, p. 122) should name the guardians of the minor children, Astrie among them; not read. ¶1b

Upgrade: upgraded to Confirmed by the coordinator (1 Nov 2026), graded on the link to Astri Jonsdatter, applying the audit-3 threshold (about 15 years or less on the same farm, no named rival). The 1738 Dal probate (DA 25286 p. 122) names 'Astrie, 10' among 'begge deris sammen avlede Børn' (both their children together). The 1753 Aurdal betrothal names the bride 'Astri Joensdr. Dahle', from the same farm 15 years later, and no other Astri Jonsdatter of Dal is known. ¶5
