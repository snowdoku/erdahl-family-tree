Life story

Baptism: baptised in Selbu on 16 JULY 1854 - Selbu parish register, Selbu local parish 1843-1859, births and baptisms, page 91 serial no. 96, with the parallel entry at 1842-1858 page 197 serial 93. Child 'John'; father 'Sivert Johnsen'; mother 'Brynhild Johnsdatter', residence EIDEM. The duplicate register renders the mother as 'Brgnenld Johansdr Eieslean', which is the same name and farm through a worse hand. ¶1

A third son named John to this couple, and the only one of the three whose entry gives the residence cleanly as Eidem in both registers. He is not in the 1865 household, where the only John is the 1842 son, so he died in childhood between 1854 and 1865. ¶2

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
