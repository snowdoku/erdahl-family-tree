About the family

Probable child of Tron Felle (see his page): his patronymic is Tronsen and he lived at Felle, where Tron's son Tore Thronsen and daughter Astri Thronsdatter lived with Tron's widow Gunild Andersdatter in 1801. Five Tronsen/Thronsdatter children of Felle married 1778-81 (Hæge, Ingebor, Groe, Andres, Thore), and no other Tron was seen at Felle in the marriage register 1773-86 (other Felle households: Knud, Christopher, Biørn). His father is not named in the entry. His mother is presumably Gunild Andersdatter, but not shown. Would be confirmed by his baptism (Kviteseid ministerialbok before 1773) or by Tron's probate (skifte) listing heirs. ¶2

Life story

Betrothed 17 Mar 1780 at Tvedsund, Treungen, as bachelor 'Andres Tronsen Fælle' to the widow Eldri Høljesdatter Velhuus(?); cautioners Niels Halvorsen Onstad and Andres Toruvsen Hiemdal; married in Treungen church on the 8th Sunday after Trinity 1780 (Kviteseid ministerialbok 1773-1786, image 126, p.120b). ¶1
