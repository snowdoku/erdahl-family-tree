About the family

First wife of Thomas Olsen Beitland. They married on 6 June 1844; the register enters her as a maid of Bakmo, born 1820 in Stjørdal, with her father named as Mads of Bakmo. ¶1

Her family at Bakmo kept up with Thomas's second household: Anders Larsen of Bakmoen stood godfather to both Ole (1848) and Beret Martha (1849), the first two children of the second marriage. ¶3

Life story

She must have died within two years, because at his second marriage on 18 June 1846 Thomas is entered as a widower. No burial record has been identified for her yet. No children of this marriage have been found. ¶2
