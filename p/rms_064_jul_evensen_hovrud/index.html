Life story

Probate (index card): at the estate of Marit Juulsdatter, wife of Even Jonsen, at Haaverud (Hovrud) i Aurdal, held 7 Oct 1728 (Hadeland, Land og Valdres skifteprotokoll 1716-1737, fol. 914a), the children were '1. Juul 11 aar, 2. Jon 5, 3. Lars 3, 4. Anne 8'. Gross 83-2-0, net 33-3-10 rdr; odel land 6 skind. So Jul was born about 1717 at Hovrud. ¶1

Probate (index card): at the estate of Sigrid Olsdatter (deceased), second wife of Even Johnsøn of Haaverud ('Hovrud') i Aurdal, 12 Feb - 12 Nov 1751 (skifteprotokoll 1744-52, fol. 353b), the card lists 'mandens barn av 1 egteskab' (the husband's children by his first marriage, to Marit Juelsdatter, deceased): 1. Juel, 2. John, 3. Lars, 4. Anna (married to a man read as Thorsud Thordsen Faaen). The estate was divided in the husband's lifetime ('skiftet i hans live'). Gross 99-2-0, debts 111-0-4; odel land 3 skind. Jul was alive in 1751. ¶2

Marriage: betrothed at Aurdal on 3 May 1753 as 'Juel Evensøn Hovrud' to 'Astri Joensd[atte]r Dahle' and married 15 Oct 1753 (DA 9361 p. 88, canvas 313). Astri Jonsdatter is the 'mandens moder' of Even Julsen in the 1801 census at Hovrud, and Even was confirmed in 1771 as 'Even Juelsøn Haavrud', aged 17 (DA 9362 p. 405, canvas 193), i.e. born about 1754, within a year of this wedding. This settles that Even's mother was the wife of Jul Evensen of Hovrud from 1753. It stays Probable, not Confirmed, because Even's own baptism (which would name the father) has not been read: it should fall between Oct 1753 and late 1754 in DA 9361, canvases about 213-225, after the window searched (to Jul 1753). read canvases 217 (Dec 1753 - Feb 1754) and 219 (Feb - Mar 1754) without finding him; still to read: c213-216, c218, c220-222. ¶5

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Upgrade: graded on his link to his son Even Julsen. Even's baptism is found: Aurdal ministerialbok 1730-1762 (DA 9361) p. 439, IIIF canvas 222 (left page), in the Svenes annex section of summer 1754 (entered 'Ditto' after a baptism of 30 June; the mother's churching 28 July): 'item Døbt Juel Evensøn Hoverud og Astri Joensd[atte]r Tvilling børn N[omine] Even og Marit'. Godparents: Trond He[s]jegaard[?], [Ju]el[?] Hoverud, Engebret Oppen, Ingri Midtstrand[?] and Joran Torsd[atter] Rilske[?]; and for the second twin Joen Gusager, Lars Hoverud (Jul's brother Lars, b. about 1725, of the 1728 probate), Kari Bendiksd[atter] Nordstrand, Anne Helgesd[atter] Espelien and Lisbet[?] Tollefsd[atter]. Continuity: the parents are the couple betrothed 3 May and married 15 Oct 1753 (DA 9361 p. 88, canvas 313, 'Juel Evensøn Hovrud og Astri Joensdr. Dahle'); the son is 'Even Juelsøn Haavrud', 17, at the 1771 confirmation (DA 9362 p. 405, canvas 193), which matches a June 1754 birth; and the 1801 census at Hovrud has Even Julsen with his mother Astri Jonsdatter. Same farm, same names, no gap, and no rival: and this pass read every Aurdal baptism from Jul 1752 to Oct 1754 (canvases 204-222) and found no other Even of Hovrud. A baptism naming both parents settles the link. ¶6

The case for this identification 1

Identification as father of Even Julsen (Probable): Even Julsen (b. about 1753) farmed Hovrud with odel land, his patronymic gives his father as Jul, and his mother Astri Jonsdatter (90 in 1801) lived with him there. Jul Evensen is the only Jul of Hovrud in the Valdres probate card index; he was born at Hovrud about 1717, was alive in 1751, and belonged to the odel family of the farm. Even Julsen's name repeats Jul's father Even Jonsen, as the naming custom expects for a first or second son. No record yet states that Even Julsen was Jul Evensen's son: Aurdal baptisms and marriages c.1735-1760 are not indexed (person search for Jul/Juel/Juul Evens* in Aurdal 1735-1801 returns only the 1801 census grandson), and no estate of Jul or Astri was found among Haaverud cards 6179-6223. ¶3

Searches that found nothing 1

Search for Even Julsen's baptism (negative): Aurdal ministerialbok 1730-1762 (DA 9361) was read page by page from 6 Jul 1752 to 22 Jul 1753 (pp. 403-420, IIIF canvases 204-212). That window holds both his age at the 1801 census (48) and his age at burial in 1821 (68). The baptism section is chronological with running totals: 137 baptisms in 1752. There is no Even and no baptism from Haaverud/Hovrud in those pages. Even was therefore baptised outside this window, outside Aurdal, or entered under another farm name. The marriage section of 9361 (it starts about canvas 269; canvas 315 = 1754, pp. 92-93) was not searched for a marriage of Jul Evensen and Astri Jonsdatter. Jul stays Probable as Even's father. ¶4
