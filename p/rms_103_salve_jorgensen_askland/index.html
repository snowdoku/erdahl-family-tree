About the family

NEW ANCESTOR, graded on his link to Tergie Salvesen (see his page). Listed in the 1664 manntall as a user of Askeland søndre, Gjøvdal sogn (3 huder), 'Salve 30 aar', beside Torkild 65 and Laurids 40 (Råbyggelaget fogderi, s. 80, DA 35567 view 58, read on the scan). No sons over 12 are listed with him. His patronymic and wife come from Askland probate card 49 (Skp. 6 fol. 73b, 22 Sep 1722, estate of Ole Svenkesøn): the widow Guro Salvesdatter's parents were 'Salve Jørgensøn Askland og Gunild Gjermundsdtr.' That is an archivist's note, so only Probable. Tergie Salvesen (born about 1675) of Søndre Askland is taken to be his son from patronymic, farm and age; no record states it. ¶1a

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
