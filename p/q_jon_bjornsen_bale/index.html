Life story

Kgroenha.net (ahnentafel no. 464): John Biørnsen, born 1711 at Sande, married 1740 Gurid Olsdatter Bale, died 1770 in Balestrand. Lokalhistoriewiki has an article 'Jon Bjørnsen (d. 1770)', son of Bjørn Jonsen of Sande and Skåsheim (seen via search summary only; the site could not be opened directly). ¶1

Laberg 1985 (Bale, printed p. 60): 'Jon Bjørnson frå Sande' married Gurid, daughter of Ola Andersson Bale, and got the holding on Bale after Ola died in 1760. Jon Bjørnson was born 1711 and died 1770; he married Gurid [?] Bale in 1740; their children included Bjørn, who came to Gjerde, and Brita. His widow remarried. This agrees with the tree and with Bjørn Johnsen Gjerde's 1828 probate. ¶3a

Baptised at Vangsnes church on Jubilate Sunday, 26 Apr 1711 (date computed: Easter 1711 = 5 Apr): 'Joen Biørnss. Sande bapt.' (Leikanger ministerialbok 1690-1734, source 11564, canvas 89 R). The book enters each baptism as child's name, patronymic and farm, so this is Jon, son of Bjørn, at Sande. No mother or godparents (faddere) are written. Birth date set to 'before 26 Apr 1711' from this entry (was ABT 1711). The 1729 probate's age 17 is about a year low, within normal probate rounding. ¶5

Death narrowed from '1770' to 'before 1 Oct 1770' by his probate: on 1 Oct 1770 the court met at Bale in Tjugum skipreide to register the estate after 'Joen Biørnsen Bälle', between his widow Guri Olsdatter and their children; heirs' headings 'Enken Guri Olsdatter', 'Sønnen Biørn Joensen', 'Datteren Brithe Joensdatter' (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll 8b, fol. 636a-636b). The legible parts of the heading name no brother (Anders Bjørnsen) and no mother, so this probate does not by itself prove his father; Hans Olsen Gierde, Anders Han[sen], Ole Pedersen [Wangsnes?] and Anders Christensen [Eide?] are also named, roles unread. Confidence unchanged. ¶6

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Probable rather than confirmed: the sources are secondary (probably based on Laberg, Balestrand ættesoge, 1985). The naming pattern supports it (Bjørn's sons John and Guri; grandson Bjørn). ¶2

Confidence raised from Probable to Confirmed on two probates: Ole Andersen Bale's 1760 probate (7b fol. 465a) names him as husband of Gurid Olsdatter at Bale, and Bjørn Jonsen's 1729 probate at Skåsheim (3b fol. 343a) names 'Jon 17 aar gl.' (born about 1711/12) as son of Biørn Jonsøn and Brithe Andersdatter. Identity of the 1729 boy with the Bale husband rests on name, age (b. about 1711) and Laberg's 'Jon Bjørnson frå Sande'; a strong match, not stated in one record. ¶4
