About the family

David Erdahl's maternal great-grandfather. Father of Kathryn, Stewart and Charles, named as such on his own gravestone: 'EIDEM / MAYME O. 1906-2002 / PALMER G. 1904-1990 / PARENTS OF KATHRYN, STEWART, CHARLES', in Zoar Lutheran Cemetery, Antelope Valley Township, Deuel County, South Dakota. ¶1

His parents are identified by his neighbours on that same census sheet. Household 4 is 'Slind, Ole O., head, W M 66, born Norway' with his wife 'Ingeborg, 65, born Norway'; household 8 is 'Eidem, John P., head, W M 73, born Norway' with his wife 'Oline S., 69, born Norway' and his son 'Peter C., 40, born Minnesota'. Because the California Death Index independently records Palmer Eidem's mother's maiden name as SLIND, John P. and Oline S. Eidem are his parents and the Slinds next door are his mother's kin - a Norwegian immigrant farming cluster living side by side in the township and buried side by side in the churchyard. ¶3

Parentage: the California Death Index 1940–1997 gives his mother's maiden name as SLIND; in April 1950 he was living four dwellings from John P. Eidem (b. Norway 1876) and Oline S. Eidem (b. Norway 1881) in the same enumeration district of Lac qui Parle County, and the family burial ground is the one his parents lie in. Probable rather than Confirmed because no birth or church record has been retrieved. ¶9

Parentage upgraded from Probable to Confirmed, 11 Oct 2026, and the reason it moved: the Minnesota death index records him as a 'child of SLIND (mother)'; the California Death Index independently gives his mother's maiden name as Slind; two of his brothers' Social Security applications name the parent couple as JOHN P EIDEM and OLINE G SLIND by name; and he is buried in the family plot at Zoar Lutheran with them. Four record sets, no contradiction. ¶16

He was the second child of the marriage. His elder sister Clara Pauline, registered in 1902 as Alvira A. Eidem, is 'number of child 1' on her own card; the sequence then runs Palmer (2), Olga J. (3), Cora Belinde (4), Peder Conrad (5), May Chrisine (6) and John Alvin (7), each number taken from the face of that child's own registration. ¶31

Life story

Birth: 11 August 1904 in Minnesota — almost certainly Mehurin Township or Marietta, Lac qui Parle County, where his parents farmed. His Social Security number, [number withheld], was issued in Minnesota. ¶8

Baptism, the record located but not yet read: he was born 11 August 1904 in Mehurin Township, Lac qui Parle County, Minnesota, and his baptism was therefore performed by the pastor of the Marietta pastoral call of the United Norwegian Lutheran Church - in August 1904 that was the Rev. O. O. Lokensgaard or the Rev. William Coll, who together succeeded the Rev. I. Tharaldson in that year. The call comprised Augusta congregation at Marietta and Mehurin congregation, which shared one pastor throughout, and before its church was built in 1910-11 the Mehurin congregation met in Lund's school house. Augusta's ministerial records, 1898-1927, in Norwegian and English, survive on microfilm: Minnesota Historical Society, Manuscripts Collection M678, reel 44, original American Lutheran Church number [ALC 129]. No separate Mehurin register appears anywhere in that collection, so this is where the entry should be looked for. A Norwegian Lutheran baptismal entry names the faddere, the godparents, who are almost always close kin. ¶18

Social Security, retirement claim: Palmer G. Eidem, born 11 Aug 1904, filed a life claim at the Social Security Administration field office in PASADENA, CALIFORNIA, in May 1969 — the year he turned 65. The claim file records the field office where the claim was lodged, which is the office serving where the claimant was then living, so this places him in the Pasadena area in 1969. NARA, Access to Archival Databases, Social Security Numerical Identification (NUMIDENT) Claim Files 1936-2007, Last Names D-G, record 1352267-series (rid 1352292). His account was issued in Minnesota. ¶19

Social Security, death record: PALMER G EIDEM, b. 11 Aug 1904, d. 20 Oct 1990, residence ZIP [withheld] — which is east PASADENA, Los Angeles County, California. The death was reported to the Social Security Administration by a field office and posted on 2 Nov 1990. NARA AAD, NUMIDENT Death Files 1936-2007, Last Names E-G, rid 304072. This narrows the tree's 'Los Angeles County' to Pasadena, and it puts him and Mayme in the same corner of the county (she died at ZIP [withheld], Arcadia, four miles away). ¶20

Social Security record, new in wave 3: 'EIDEM, PALMER G was born 11 August 1904, was assigned Social Security number [number withheld] (indicating Minnesota), and died 20 October 1990, while residing in ZIP [withheld]. The Social Security field office at PASADENA CA received the claim 12 May 1969.' ZIP [withheld] is east Pasadena. The 1969 claim is the key detail: a retirement claim filed at the Pasadena office in May 1969 means the family had already left Minnesota and settled in Pasadena by the spring of 1969, twelve years before any of them appears in the Sierra Madre newspaper. His death place can accordingly be narrowed from 'Los Angeles County' to Pasadena. ¶22

This fixes where to look for his daughter Kathryn and her husband Harry: the household's Los Angeles County base was Pasadena, and later Arcadia, and not Sierra Madre, which was only where Kathryn went to church. ¶23

Birth registration: Minnesota birth card no. 12239 of 1904, Lac qui Parle County, read from the record image itself. 'BIRTH, 1904, Aug 11, Mehurin, L.q.P. Name: Palmer Gilbart Eidem. White. Male. Father's name: John. Occupation: Farmer. Birthplace: Nor. Age 28. Mother's maiden name: Lena Lind. Birthplace: ditto. Age 23. Number of child: 2. Reporter: G. L. Shackelford, Marietta.' This is the record that turns his parentage from an inference into a fact: it names his father John Eidem, farmer, born Norway, aged 28, and his mother as Lena (Oline) Slind, born Norway, aged 23, and it puts his birth in Mehurin Township rather than merely 'Minnesota'. ¶29

1905 state census: enumerated 8 June 1905 in Mehurin Township, Lac qui Parle County, enumeration district 1, schedule sheet 4, page 243, line 7, as 'Eidem Palmer, M, 1, W, born Minn., father born Norway, mother born Norway'. The household on lines 4-7 is his father John P. Eidem, 28, farmer; his mother Lena, 25; his sister Clara, 3; and himself. The enumerator was Wm. F. Bates. ¶32

The birth registration was reported not by a doctor but by G. L. Shackelford of Marietta, the town registrar, who handled almost every Eidem and Slind registration in Mehurin and Augusta townships between 1902 and 1910. Three of Palmer's younger siblings were delivered by Dr. Henry Duncan of Marietta. ¶33

Memorial stone at Zoar, photographed and read: a dark polished granite die on a rough-dressed base, a cross-and-lily emblem in the centre, inscribed 'EIDEM' across the top, 'MAYME O. 1906 - 2002' to the left and 'PALMER G. 1904 - 1990' to the right of the emblem, and along the base 'PARENTS OF KATHRYN, STEWART, CHARLES'. ¶34

The inscription is independent granite-cut confirmation of the sibling group: Palmer and Mayme's children were Kathryn, Stewart and Charles, and there was no fourth. ¶36

1905 Minnesota state census: Mehurin township, Lac qui Parle County, ED 1, schedule sheet 4 (page stamp 243), line 7 - 'Eidem Palmer, M, 1, W, born Minnesota, father born Norway, mother born Norway'. This is his first appearance in any record after his birth: a ten-month-old on the fourth line of his father's household. ¶38

1910, 1920 and 1930 federal censuses: at home in Mehurin township throughout, indexed 'Palmer G Eidem', b. abt 1905 Minnesota. In 1930, aged 25, he was still on his father's farm. ¶39

Land, 1940: he is on the Lac qui Parle County tax roll in his own right at 35. The Atlas of Lac Qui Parle County, Minnesota (1940), in the county treasurer's certified list for Mehurin Township as of 15 March 1940, enters "Joseph & Palmer Eidem. 1 1/2 acres in NW1/4 corner Lot 1 - Section 20" - a parcel carved out of the north-west corner of government Lot 1 of section 20, which is part of his father John P. Eidem's own description in the same list. An acre and a half on a road corner is not a farm but a business site, and it fits what the 1950 census records him doing ten years later: "Manager", industry written first as "general store" and then amended to "Oil Station", class of worker O (own business), 70 hours in the census week. The co-owner Joseph Eidem is the Joseph Eidem, 7 Jun 1910 - 28 Feb 1961, buried at Zoar Lutheran Cemetery. ¶28

1940 federal census: Mehurin township, Lac qui Parle County, NARA T627 roll 1932 image 392 - he is listed as COUSIN, aged about 34 and unmarried, in the household of Joseph Eidem (head, b. abt 1911 South Dakota), Joseph's wife Myrtle (b. abt 1914 Iowa) and their daughter Lileen M. (b. abt 1939). Joseph is Joseph M. Eidem, son of Mikal Eidem of Antelope Valley township, Deuel County, South Dakota, so the enumerator's word 'cousin' is literally correct and it independently confirms the Mehurin-to-Deuel-County family link. On 1 April 1940 there was no Mayme and no child: the marriage falls between April 1940 and 1942. ¶40

1940, residence and business: the Elk Point paper's report of his wedding (Leader-Courier, 27 June 1940) gives him as 'Mr. Palmer G. Eidem of Marietta, Minn.' and says he operated a general store and gas station at State Line on U.S. Highway 212 near Marietta, where a new home was being completed for the couple. His wife's 2002 obituary calls it the 'State Line Station'. The announcement of 13 June 1940 says the couple would make their home at Marietta. ¶44

Marriage: he married Mayme Eidem, daughter of S. S. Eidem of Brule Creek, Union County, South Dakota, on Thursday 20 June 1940 at St. Paul Lutheran church, rural Elk Point. His brother Peder Eidem of Marietta was best man, Eugene Willis of Marietta his attendant, and his parents Mr. and Mrs. John Eidem of Marietta were present; other Marietta guests were Mr. and Mrs. John Eidem Jr. and the Joseph Eidem family (Leader-Courier, Elk Point, 27 June 1940, page 1 and last page). ¶45

WWII draft registration: registered on 16 February 1942, the third national registration day (men aged 20 to 44), as Palmer Gilbert Eidem, born 11 August 1904 at Lac qui Parle, Minnesota, residence Mehurin, Lac qui Parle County, Minnesota (Ancestry, U.S. World War II Draft Cards Young Men 1940-1947, collection 2238, index row 16561336, card image 44018_06_00004-02401). Every indexed field agrees with his birth card and the 1940 and 1950 censuses. Only the index was read; the card itself, which would give his employer and the person who would always know his address, is queued for the signed-in lane as item S0103. ¶46

Death indexes re-read, October 2026: the California Death Index 1940-1997 on Ancestry (collection 5180, row 2093130) gives Palmer Gilbert Eidem, born 11 August 1904 in Minnesota, died 20 October 1990 in Los Angeles County, mother's maiden name Slind; Ancestry's view of that row has no sex column, so the female coding noted above could not be re-checked there. The Social Security Death Index (collection 3693, row 17518311) gives Palmer G. Eidem, born 11 August 1904, died 20 October 1990, last residence Pasadena, Los Angeles County. Both agree with the NUMIDENT death file already cited. ¶47

1950 census: farming in Mehurin Township, Lac qui Parle County, Minnesota, enumeration district 37-25, sheet 2, dwelling and household 5. Enumerated as 'Eidem, Palmer G., head, W M 45, married, born Minnesota', working. The household: wife Mayme O., 43, born South Dakota, keeping house; daughter Kathryn J., 7; sons Stewart J., 5, and Charles P., 3, all three born Minnesota. ¶2

1950 census: Mehurin Township, Lac qui Parle County, Minnesota, enumeration district 37-25, sheet 1, dwelling and household 5, lines 11–15, enumerated 20 April 1950 by S. H. Magnus. 'Eidem, Palmer G.', head, white, male, 45, married, born Minnesota. Occupation MANAGER; the industry column was first written 'general store' and then amended to 'Oil Station'; class of worker 'O' — his own business. He worked 70 hours in the census week. Occupation code 290, industry code 646. The household was not on a farm. ¶10

Household in 1950: wife Mayme O., 43, born South Dakota, keeping house; daughter Kathryn J., 7, born Minnesota; son Stewart J., 5; son Charles P., 3. ¶11

Context: a 1950 country gas station in a township of a few hundred people was the general store, the post-office drop and the farm-fuel depot at once; seventy hours a week is what running one single-handed cost. ¶15

1950 federal census: Mehurin Twp, ED 37-25, sheet 1, dwelling and household 5, line 11 - 'Eidem, Palmer G., head, white male, 45, married, born Minnesota, working, 70 hours last week, MANAGER, general store and oil station, working on own account'. Columns 3 and 4 - 'is this house on a farm?' and 'is this house on a place of three or more acres?' - are both NO. His father and every neighbour on the sheet are 'yes / Farmer / Farm'. By 1950 Palmer had left farming altogether and was running a store and filling station seventy hours a week. ¶41

California city directories, 1965-1968 (Probable; index only; added by line 8): Ancestry's U.S. City Directories 1822-1995 (collection 2469) indexes 'Palmer G Eidem' in a California directory for 1965, 1966, 1967 and 1968, with spouse 'Mayme Eidem' in 1966, 1967 and 1968 (for example records 3467545524 and 1442942804). The index gives no city; Pasadena is likely, from his 1969 Social Security claim there, but is not confirmed. ¶53

Migration to California: his Social Security retirement claim was taken by the PASADENA, CALIFORNIA field office on 12 May 1969, three months before his sixty-fifth birthday, and his wife's by the same office on 9 September 1971. The family was therefore in the Pasadena–Arcadia district of Los Angeles County by the spring of 1969 at the latest. ¶12

Residence, June 1987: the obituary of his sister May Christine (Eidem) Gieseke in the Thief River Falls Times of 17 June 1987 lists among her survivors 'three brothers, Palmer Eidem, South Pasadena, CA, Peder Eidem, Montevideo, and John A. Eidem, Marietta'. So he was alive in June 1987 and living in South Pasadena, beside Pasadena; his last residence in October 1990 is given by the Social Security records as Pasadena, ZIP [withheld]. ¶49

Death: 20 October 1990 in Los Angeles County, California, aged 86. The California Death Index 1940-1997 gives his birth as 11 August 1904 in Minnesota and his mother's maiden name as Slind. The index miscodes his sex as female; the census, the gravestone and his given name all show otherwise. ¶4

That a Minnesota farmer died in Los Angeles County is the fact that ties this family to Sierra Madre: he had followed a child to California. His wife died there too, in Arcadia, the town where his daughter Kathryn was living. ¶5

Origin: Eidem and Slind are both farm names in Selbu, Sor-Trondelag, Norway, and his parents were both born in Norway. Norwegian-relief subscription lists printed in Decorah-Posten in 1913 carry Ole, A. C., Mikal and Anders Eidem alongside Uglem and Slind names - the same Selbu cluster. ¶7

Death: 20 October 1990 in Los Angeles County, aged 86 years 2 months 9 days; last residence ZIP [withheld], Pasadena. Buried at Rose Hills Memorial Park, Whittier. ¶13

Not in the Sierra Madre News. Unreconciled (line 8, index only): the same Ancestry directory collection (2469) has three 1990 rows for 'Palmer G Eidem', one in 'California' (record 3095143370) and two in 'East Los Angeles, California' (records 3187046670, 3187541938), with no spouse. Printed source, and a striking one: his name 'Palmer Gilbert' is printed TWICE on page 207 of Selbu i fortid og naatid, bind 4 (1992), in the chapter BRENNHAUGEN - the farm history of the cottager's holding under Eidem in Selbu that his grandparents worked. A man born in Mehurin Township, Lac qui Parle County, Minnesota in 1904 is named in the printed history of a Norwegian farm, because the compilers of the 1992 volume followed the emigrant families and listed their American children. The page also carries 1904, his parents John (1876) and Oline Slind (1881), their marriage year 1901, his sisters Clara and Olga, and the words Amerika and Minnesota. This is the only place in this tree where an American-born member of the family appears in a Norwegian printed source. ¶42

Records that disagree 1

Burial contradiction, recorded rather than resolved. Find a Grave places him at Rose Hills Memorial Park, Whittier, Los Angeles County, California, while his name and dates are cut on the family stone at Zoar Lutheran Cemetery in Deuel County, South Dakota. Searches that found nothing 2

Clean negative, and a structural one: Palmer has NO record in the NUMIDENT Application (SS-5) file, only in the Death and Claim files. The SS-5 application record is the one that names both parents, so NUMIDENT cannot be made to name his parents directly. NARA states plainly of this series that 'these files do not contain records of all Social Security Number applications' — the Application extract is incomplete, not a census of applicants. His parentage is instead carried by his siblings' SS-5s (see John Alvin Eidem and Barbara Ann Eidem), each of which names JOHN P EIDEM and OLINE G SLIND outright. ¶21

Obituary search, negative (October 2026): the Elk Point, South Dakota papers on fultonhistory.com (Leader-Courier 1990-1991 and Union County Leader 1988-1990) carry no death notice or obituary for him for October 1990 - January 1991. Searched as 'Palmer Eidem', 'Palmer G. Eidem', 'Palmer Gilbert Eidem', Palmer within 3 words of Eidem, Eidem within 5 words of Pasadena and Eidem within 10 words of Marietta; the 1990-1991 folder is OCR-indexed (other Eidem hits come from it), so this is a true negative for that paper, not a coverage gap. This closes the lead recorded above as 'where I would go first'. ¶50
