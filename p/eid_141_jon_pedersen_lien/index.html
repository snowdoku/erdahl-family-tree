Life story

1801 census: householder (husbonde) of Lien household 02, Selbu, aged 44 and in his first marriage, with his wife Brynil Olsdatter, 57, in her second marriage, her daughters Soffi and Brynil Kristensdatter, his brother Jon Pederssen, 49, and two servants. Birth year about 1757 is calculated from that age. ¶1

The bygdebok, Selbu i fortid og naatid vol. 7 p. 116, names him 'Jon Persen Lien, se Nordsto' as the second husband of Brynhild Olsdatter of Slindan, born 1744; Nordsto is a holding on Lien. His wife was a widow in the 1825 census, so he probably died before November 1825 (inference); his own burial has not been searched. Rated Confirmed as the husband of the 1801 Brynil Olsdatter; her identity with the Slindan Brynhild is Probable (see her notes). ¶2
