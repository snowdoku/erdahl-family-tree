Life story

Find a Grave records two marriages: to Ida Elizabeth Amundson, 1885-1924, in 1915, and to Thea Sophia Halverson, 1887-1965, in 1926. Five children are linked: Marie, who was born and died in 1916; Paul Allan, 1918-1926, who died at eight; Rev. Norman Harold, 1919-2000, who became a Lutheran pastor; Agnes Valborg, 1927-2014; and Peter Ole, 1928-2011. His first wife died in 1924 leaving him with young children, and the little boy Paul Allan died two years later. ¶3

His is the Find a Grave memorial that carries the fullest set of family links on this side of the family, and it is the one that names Anna, Carl and Gustav as his brothers and sister - but it too omits Paul, Marie Lovise, Olga and Erling. ¶4

Born 3 Jan 1887 in Kristiansund, where he appears as 'Anton Johannes Tungsæt' in the 1891 census at 47 Toldbodsmujet. He emigrated with his mother and the other children on 2 May 1894 for Minnesota, aged seven. ¶1

Death: the Minnesota death index records Anton Jonas Tungseth, born 3 Jan 1887 outside Minnesota, died 11 Nov 1965 in Otter Tail County, certificate 1965-MN-028378, mother's maiden name SATNES (Sætnes). Index only; no certificate image is online, so no cause of death is known. The birth date and mother's maiden name prove he is Caroline Sætnes's son Anton. ¶9

1895 Minnesota census: Anton Tungseth, 8, born in Norway, in his father Peter's household in Skree Township, Clay County (p. 6, read from the image). ¶5

1900 census: Anton Tungseth, son, born January 1887 in Norway, 13, single, immigrated 1894, at school, in his mother Caroline's household in Skree Township (ED 316, sheet 185A, line 5, read from the image). ¶6

1905 Minnesota census: Anton Tungseth, 18, born in Norway, farmer (ditto mark, inferred), at home in his father Peder's household in Skree Township, 11 years in Minnesota (Enumeration District 10, schedule sheet III, line 34, read from the image). ¶7

1910 census: Anton J. Tungseth, son, 23, single, born in Norway, immigrated 1894, naturalization column possibly 'Na' (not clear), speaks English, farmer on the home farm, own account, in his father Peter P O Tungseth's household in Skree Township (ED 59, sheet 187A, line 38, read from the image). ¶8

Name: the death index gives his middle name as Jonas and the 1910 census the initial J; the tree's Anton Johannes comes from the Norwegian 1891 census. Johannes and Jonas were used interchangeably in Norway, so both refer to the same man; the name in the tree is left unchanged. ¶10

Deaths of his first wife and son: the Minnesota death index, as found by the signed-in lane, lists a Mrs. Ida Tungseth who died on 12 Apr 1924 and a Paul Tungseth who died on 26 Mar 1926, both in Clay County. These match his first wife Ida Elizabeth (Amundson), who died in 1924, and his son Paul Allan, 1918-1926, as Find a Grave gives them (index entries only; identification by name, date and county). ¶11

He died in Otter Tail County, Minnesota on 11 Nov 1965, aged 78, and was brought back for burial in Zion Lutheran Cemetery in Skree Township, Clay County, beside his parents. Otter Tail County is where his sister Olga and her husband also ended their lives, at Fergus Falls. ¶2
