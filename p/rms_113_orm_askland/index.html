About the family

NEW ANCESTOR, graded on his link to his son Svend Ormsen (see his page). The 1666 vicar's census of Gjøvdal (DA 35510 view 33, p. 44, read on the scan at zoom) lists under no. 8 'Nordre Askland, 3 huder' the users Tolf 32, Salve 52, Torbiørn 39, Orm 46 and Asbiørn 68, each using half a hud. In the sons' column, set against the users in order: Jørgen 11 and Oluf 8 (with Salve); Svend 10 and Torkild 2 (with Torbiørn); 'Svend er 12 aar', Laurits 6 and Halvor 4 (with Orm); Oluf 17 and Joen 15 (with Asbiørn). The 1664 bailiff's census (DA 35567 view 58, p. 80) has the same farm, 'Askeland nordre, 3 hud', with Torbiørn 37, Orm 44, Asbiørn 66, Tolf and Torkel. So Orm was born about 1620 and in 1666 had sons Svend (b. about 1654) and Laurits (b. about 1660). ¶1

Life story

Bygdebok corroboration (grade unchanged, Probable): Åmli ætt og heim II (Gjøvdal), Nordre Askland L.nr. 50 / bruk nr. 2 (canvas 143): Orm, son of Svenn Ormson, b. about 1618, held the bruk until the 1660s; 'Vi veit om 3 søner etter Orm': Svenn b. 1654 m. Asborg Tallaksdtr. Jørundland; Lars b. 1660 m. Jorunn Taraldsdtr. Mjåland (to Mjåland); Halvor b. 1662 m. Gunhild Ånonsdtr. (to Jørundland). Canvas 144: Orm stood as owner and user of ½ hud in 1661 and 1666; in 1670 [Svenn] Ormson stood as owner. These sons match the 1666 census (Svend 12, Laurits 6, Halvor 4) and the 1705 guardians Lauritz and Halvor Ormssøn, and Halvor Ormsen is the second husband of Gunild Osmundsdatter in the Jørundland probate of 22 Nov 1701 (Skp. 1 fol. 275), so the book and the primary records agree. Parents added: Svenn Ormsen and Tone Nilsdatter (Probable, bygdebok). Test record for his link to Svend: Lars (Lauritz) Ormsen's or Halvor Ormsen's own probate (Halvor d. 1714 per the bygdebok, Jørundland; Østre Råbyggelag Skp. 4-5, 1714-15), which should call Svend's children brother's children. ¶7

Stronger evidence, still Probable, graded on his link to Svend Ormsen (see his page). In the 13 Oct 1705 skifte after Svend Ormsøn of Askeland (Skp. 2 fol. 147b, DA 24350 view 148, read on the scan at zoom) the guardian paragraph begins with two names, read as 'Lau[ritz] Ormssøn og Halvor Ormssøn' (the first name is partly blotted; 'Halvor Ormssøn' is clear): 'efftersom de war nominerit at waere formyndere for Børnene, men de for deris incapacitet... bliver de forskaanet' (they had been nominated as guardians of the children but were excused for incapacity). In their place Svend Torbiørnsøn Askeland was made guardian for Salve and Anløv, and a Knud...søn Omland for Thoene and Birgitha. Two men named Ormsen, a Lauritz and a Halvor, nominated together as guardians of Svend Ormsen's children, match exactly the sons of Orm of Nordre Askland in the 1666 vicar's census: 'Svend er 12 aar', Laurits 6 and Halvor 4 (DA 35510 view 33). Being nominated first as guardians is what father's brothers would be. The protocol still does not write 'farbror' or name Orm, so the relationship is inferred: Probable. No rival Orm has been found in Gjøvdal in 1664-66. ¶4

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the identification · 1 alternative set aside · 2 searches with no result · 1 open question

The case for this identification 1

Why he is taken as Svend Ormsen's father: Svend Ormsøn died at Askeland before 13 Oct 1705 (Skp. 2 fol. 147a), and the other Svend of the 1666 list, Svend Torbiørnsøn, is named separately as present at that 1705 skifte, so the two Svends are kept apart in the records. At the skifte after Svend's widow Asbør Thallaksdatter, 28 Mar 1707 (Skp. 2 fol. 196b, DA 24350 view 197, read on the scan), 'Lauritz Ormsøn' acts as formynder (guardian) for the under-age children and is allotted shares on their behalf. A guardian Lauritz Ormsen fits Orm's son Laurits of 1666, i.e. the children's farbror. The protocol does not write the word farbror and does not name Orm, so this is a census household plus a guardian with a matching patronymic: Probable, not Confirmed. ¶2a

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Rival: none named; no other Orm appears in Gjøvdal in 1664 or 1666. ¶2b

Searches that found nothing 2

Search: Lauritz Ormsen's own probate is not in the Askland cards (DA 39942 cards 39-133 read, 1709 to 1754: no Lauritz or Halvor Ormsen). A Digitalarkivet name search (Lauritz/Lars/Halvor Ormsen, 1690-1760) finds nobody in Agder. So the brothers probably died or settled outside Askland. ¶5

Search: Harstveit i Gjøvdal probate cards 4271-4285 (DA 39942; 1712-1765) name no Lauritz or Halvor Ormsen and no 'brodersøn'. Still no record of the brothers after the 1705 guardianship. ¶6

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Next: the Askland probate cards (39942, from card 23) before 1705 for an Orm estate (pre-1697 protocols lost); Lauritz Ormsen's own probate, which may call Svend's children his brother's children; 1657 kvegskatt / 1647 matrikkel for Nordre Askland. ¶3
