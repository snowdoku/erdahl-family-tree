About the family

Parents searched: (1) Baptism: Gulen ministerialbok 11441, canvases 33-35 (Jul 1744 to Jul 1745, Brekke, Bø and Lavik services) read in full: no baptism for a Jens of Tynning; his birth window from his ages (56 in Feb 1801; 76 1/4 at burial in Oct 1822) is about 1744-46, so canvases 36-40 (Jul 1745-1746) remain. (2) The probate index writes Tynning as 'Tauning' (Lavik skipreide): it has a probate after Jens Jørgensen, Tauning, 1772-1776, skifteprotokoll 9a p. 363 (pa00000000523863), a candidate father; 9a is not among the scanned Ytre Sogn protocols found (24166 = 8b 1766-72, 24167 = 9b 1772-82, 24168 = 10a), so it was not read. Also indexed: Johannes Jensen, Tauning, 1813-1826 (pa00000000527906) and Marte Johannesdtr, Tauning, 1813-1826 (16 p. 113b, pa00000000527684), probably his own and his wife's probates; and a Jørgen Jensen, Tynning, 1728-33. In 1745 a 'Mand Jørgen [..] Verckland' aged 36[?] was buried at Brekke (11441 c35 left page, 23 Apr 1745), the Verkland/Tynning family with the Jørgen/Jens names. ¶5

Parents found: the probate after his father, 'Ao 1772 d. 13de Martij' on the farm 'Tøiningen' in Lavik skipreide (Ytre Sogn skifteprotokoll A 9, L0009b, No. 9, p. 363; Digitalarkivet 24167 viewer 184), is held 'efter den ved Døden afgangne Mand Jens Jørgensen' between his widow and 'deres udi et kiærligt Ægteskab tilsammen avlede' four children, 3 sons and 1 daughter: 'ældste Søn Johannes Jensen, myndig og tilstede', 'anden Søn Rognald Jensen, myndig og tilstede', 'tredie Søn Lasse Jensen 23 aar' and 'Datteren Agaathe Jensdatter 19 1/2 aar'. Guardians/witnesses: Lars Johannesen [Ūnes?], Bolse[?] Jensen Engesetter and Lars Olsen Tøining. On p. 364 the widow takes her half: 'Enken Brithe Johannes[datter] for hendes Hovedlod og halve Boe 38 Rd', and each heir's share follows ('ældste Søn Johannes Jensen', 'anden Søn Rognald Jensen', 'tredie Søn Lasse Jensen'). The index row (pa00000000523863) cites this as '9a p. 363'; the scanned volume is labelled L0009b (24167), and p. 363 is the page. This probate was already listed but not found on the scan. ¶6

Life story

Death: 'Johannes Jensøn', kaaremand (retired farmer on a life-lease) of Tynning, aged 76 1/4 (b. about 1746), buried 1822 (Gulen ministerialbok 1816-1825, burials 1822 no. 58; index pg00000001043803). The index writes the death and burial days as '210' and '610', probably 2 and 6 October 1822, so only the year is entered until the scan is read. Name and farm match the 1801 householder of Tynning, aged 56 (b. about 1745); he is the only Johannes Jensen born 1739-1751 in the Gulen burials. ¶4

1801 census: Johannes Jenssen, 56, head of household, married for the first time, skattebonde at Tynning (Tynjum), Brekke, with wife Martha Johannesdatter, 53, and seven joint daughters: Brite 24, Kari 23, Johanna 20, Orloug 18, Barbra 15, Martha 12 and Jørnille 8; also the servant Mons Bendixsen, 46. Birth about 1745 is inferred from his age. ¶1

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Probable father of Kari Johannesdatter, who married Mons Johannessen from Tynjum in 1804. Her baptism would confirm it. ¶2

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed (upgrade sweep D), as parent of Kari Johannesdatter. Three records name or fix the link. (1) Her baptism on 15 Dec 1779, 'Johannes Jenssen Tynnings D[atter] N[omine] Kari' (Gulen ministerialbok 1776-1787, fol. 35b), names Johannes Jensen of Tynning as her father; this register does not name mothers. (2) The 1801 census of Brekke (index, relationship word checked) lists Kari Johannesdatter, 23, unmarried, as 'Felleds dr' (daughter of both spouses) of Johannes Jenssen, 56, and Martha Johannesdatter, 53, at Tynning, household 01. (3) She married from Tynjum as a maid on 1 Jul 1804 (Mons Johannessøn of Asseim and Kari Johannesdatter, Gulen marriages 1804, index); the other Kari Johannesdatter of 1801 (34, at Asheim) did not live at Tynjum. The baptism names him; the census names him and his wife as her parents; the marriage keeps the bride on his farm. ¶3
