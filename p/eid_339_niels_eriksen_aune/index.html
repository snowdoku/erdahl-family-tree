About the family

His son Erik Nielsen Aune (7 in 1732) and Erik's wife Ane Thomasdatter had a son Niels baptised on Invocavit Sunday, 27 Feb 1757 (16311 c3 L), with godparents Ole Aune, Elle[n?] of the same place, Jon Samstad, Kari Brøndhaugen[?] and Giertrud Garberg. This continues the Aune family into 1757, and the Garberg godmother supports, without proving, the identification of his daughter Giertrud (6 in 1732) with Giertrud Nielsdatter of Garberg (see her page). Grade unchanged (Probable). ¶3

His son Erik Nielsen Aune and Anne Thomasdatter had a son Jon baptised on the third day of Christmas 1765, with Paul Garberg among the godparents (16311 c18 R). This supports, without proving, the identification of his daughter Giertrud (6 in 1732) as Paul Pedersen Garberg's wife. Grade unchanged. ¶4

Life story

Farmer at Aune (Aunet), Selbu. His probate was held at Aune on 26 Sep 1732 (Skifteprotokoll 2B, 24751, fol. 481b): 'efter afg. Niels Erichsen Aune imellem hans efterladte Hustrue Kari O[la]fsdatter og deres tilsammen avlede 5 Børn: 1. Erik Nielsen 7 aar, 2. Ole Nielsen 2 aar, 3. Ennil[?] Nielsdatter 11 aar, 4. Elen Nielsdatter 9 aar, 5. Giertrud Nielsdatter 6 aar' (first). Lensmann Thomas Løe; appraisers Ola Joensen Aune and Thomas Joensen Samstad. ¶1

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the grade · 1 open question

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Graded Probable on his link to Giertrud Nielsdatter (see her page). The probate names his daughter Giertrud, 6 in Sep 1732, which fits the baptism 'Niels Aunes barn Nom: Giertrud' on Invocavit Sunday, 23 Feb 1727 (16310 c107 R). That Aune child is identified with Giertrud Nielsdatter, wife of Paul Pedersen Garberg from about 1757, by inference only: earlier waves read every 1699-1737 baptism in 16310 (c3-147) and found no other Giertrud with a father Niels, and her first daughter (Kari, 1758) and first son (Niels, 1761) carry the names of Niels and Kari. The 26-year gap and the change of farm (Aune to Garberg) keep this at Probable. ¶2a

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Would settle it: Giertrud's burial with an age, or a probate after Kari at Aune or after Paul Pedersen Garberg that names her. ¶2b
