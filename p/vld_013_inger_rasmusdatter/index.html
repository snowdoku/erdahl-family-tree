Life story

Probate: widow of Siur (Sivert) Ingebrigtsen of Diub (Djup), Giske. She is named as 'Enke Inger Rasmusdatter' with her legal guardian (lauværge) Peder Jørgensen in the registration of his estate on 14 August 1766 (Sunnmøre sorenskriveri, 3A 22, fol. 736b-737). The entry gives no age or origin for her. ¶1

Bygdebok: Borgund og Giske bd. 3 (canvas 496, Dyb, rebuilt from word boxes and the exact-phrase query "Inger Rasmusdtr. Karlsbakk") gives Sjur Ingebrigtsen's second wife as 'Inger Rasmusdtr. Karlsbakk (1707-80)', married 1743. This agrees with the 1744 marriage index ('Inger Rasmusd.', Pige, of Carlsbachen) and adds her birth about 1707 and death about 1780 (ABT, rebuilt text). The same book (canvas 154) has a 'Marit Rasmusdtr. Karlsbakk (1709-55)' married 1739, possibly a sister. Her father was therefore a Rasmus of Karlsbakk (Borgund). ¶10a

Burial: 'Inger Rasmusd.' of Diub (Djup), 'Ind Ek' (widowed lodger), aged 73 (b. 1707), buried 6 May 1780 at Borgund (Borgund ministerialbok 1762-1800; index pg00000002550093). Farm, widowhood and age all fit: she was Sivert Ingebrigtsen's widow at Diub from 1766, her baptism is 24 Jul 1707, and the farm history gives '1707-80'. She is the only Inger Rasmusdatter of that age in the burial index. Replaces the ABT 1780 death. ¶13

Marriage: Borgund church book 1739-1761 (1531P), married 1 January 1744: 'Sifvert Ingebrigtss.', Enchemand (widower), of Diub, and 'Inger Rasmusd.', Pige (unmarried woman), of Carlsbachen (index pv00000002823273 / pv00000002823274; the betrothal is indexed under 1743, pv00000010136443, with Hans Nilsen of Diub among the trulovar). So she was Sivert's second wife, married during the 1738-1744 gap in his children's baptisms, and his children baptised 1728-1738 (Nils, Hans and the elder daughter) were by his first wife. ¶3

Churching: Borgund church book 1739-1761, introduced women, 2 August 1744: 'Inger Rasmusd.', residence Diub (index py00000000009232). This is six and a half weeks after the baptism of Ingeborg on 18 June 1744 (father 'Sifvert Ingebrigts.', Diub, pd00000003229033), the usual interval before churching, and Sivert's is the only Diub baptism between January and October 1744 in the index (the others are Anders Knudsen's on 11 Jan and Knud Jacobsen's on 5 Oct). ¶4

Marriage (index): she married Sifvert Ingebrigtss., widower, of Diub, at Borgund on 1 Jan 1744, as 'Inger Rasmusd.', Pige, of Carlsbachen (pv00000002823274). The farm history's '1743' (Borgund og Giske bd. 3 canvas 496) is probably the engagement year. The residence ties her to Karlsbakken, where Inger, daughter of Rasmus Vilichss., was baptised on 24 Jul 1707; see Rasmus Villiksen. That link is graded Probable, because it spans 37 years. ¶12

1762 extra tax, Borgund, Diub (Djup) household 1 (49637 view 25): 'M: Siur Ingebrigtsen, H: Inger Rasmusdatt.', and in the column 'Børn over 12 aar og deres Navne' (children over 12 and their names): '1 Datt: Ingeborg'; with a servant, others supported and a houseman's widow ('Enke Guri Rasmusdatter Alnes'); 6 persons taxed. Also of interest: 'Lisbeth Sivertsd: Diub' is a servant at Hessen (49637 view 33), possibly a sister or half-sister of Ingeborg. ¶8

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 3

Speculative mother of Ingeborg Siversdatter (baptized 18 June 1744). Her marriage to Siur is stated in the 1766 probate, but her motherhood is not. She is the only widow named, and the entry mentions no earlier marriage. However, it calls the heirs only 'the deceased's surviving children' (den afdødes efterladte Børn), whereas the same court's 1760 skifte after Peder Olsen of Giske-Ødegård spells out the widow's own children. Siur's marriage is dated to about 1727 from a first baptism in 1728, and his children were baptized from 1728 to 1749, with a six-year gap between 1738 and 1744. That span leaves room for two wives, and Inger could be a second wife, possibly married during that gap. If so she would still be the mother of Ingeborg, but not of the older heirs Nils, Hans and the married daughter. Her own guardian (lauværge) was a court juror, Peder Jørgensen, not one of the grown sons. The Borgund baptisms of 1728-1749 name only the father. A Borgund marriage record for Siur and Inger, a probate after Inger herself, or a probate after an earlier wife of Siur would settle it. ¶2

Upgrade: Speculative to Confirmed as mother of Ingeborg Siversdatter. The marriage record makes her Sivert's wife from 1 Jan 1744, and the churching record of 2 Aug 1744 names her as the Diub woman delivered in the weeks before, which matches Ingeborg's baptism of 18 June 1744 to Sivert. The earlier doubt (that she might be a second wife, mother only of the younger children) is resolved: she was indeed the second wife, married about five months before Ingeborg's birth, so she is Ingeborg's mother but not the mother of Nils, Hans and the married daughter. Not read on the scan: the index rows only. ¶5

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed, graded on her link to her daughter Ingeborg Siversdatter (see her page). The 1762 extra-tax list for Djup names Ingeborg as a daughter of the couple 'Siur Ingebrigtsen' and 'Inger Rasmusdatter' (49637 view 25, household 1). Continuity: Ingeborg was baptised to Sivert Ingebrigtsen of Diub on 18 Jun 1744, confirmed in 1760, is here in 1762 at 18, is named his unmarried daughter in his probate of 14 Aug 1766 (Inger his widow) and was a maid of Diub at her engagement in Jan 1768: the same farm throughout, gaps of six years or less, and no other Ingeborg in the Djup households. The step-daughter doubt is excluded by the dates: Sivert married Inger, as a widower, on 1 Jan 1744, so a child born to him in June 1744 was born after his first wife's death; Inger was churched at Diub on 2 Aug 1744. ¶9
