About the family

Wife of Even Julsen at Haarud (Hovrud) in the 1801 census, aged 48. Mother of Astri Evensdatter, who named daughters Marit (1812, 1814) after her. Her parents and death have not been found. ¶1

Father (bygdebok, Probable): Valdres bygdebok I D (Svenes), Midtstrand gnr. 64 (canvas 278) lists 'Marit 1754, g 1780 m Eivind Julsson, gnr 76' as eldest child of Ola Arneson (1729-1791, drowned) and his wife [forename unread] Høljesdotter (1735-1810), married 1754; the Hovrud chapter (canvas 644) has Eivind Julsson 1754-1821 married 1780 Marit Olsdotter of gnr. 64, 1754-1830. Linked father Ole Arnesen. Birth set ABT 1754 (the 1801 age 48 gives about 1753), death ABT 1830 from the book. ¶6a

Mother added: Ragnild Helgesdatter, who married Ole Arnesen of Bø on 1 Jul 1754 (Aurdal church book DA 9361 p. 93, canvas 315; betrothal 23 May 1754, 'Ole Arnesøn Bøe og Ragnild Helgesdr. Mitstrand'). The bygdebok (Svenes vol., gnr 64, canvas 278) lists Marit, b. 1754, as the couple's eldest child. Probable. Her 1801 census age 48 (born about 1752-53) would put her birth before this marriage; the bygdebok year 1754 would put it in the first months of the marriage. ¶7a

Parents confirmed: the division at Strand of 12 Jul 1799 (DA 25294 pp. 82-83; card 13047) names her as 'den ældste Datter Marit Olsd[atte]r gift med Even Juelsen Haverud', among the children of the widow Ragnild Helgesdatter and the late Ole Arnesøn. Her own grade (her link to her daughter Astri Evensdatter) is unchanged. ¶10

Life story

Baptism found: baptised at Svenes on 6 Oct 1754 as 'Marit', daughter of 'Ole Arnesøn Mitstrand og Ragnild Helgesd.'; godparents Ole Knudsen Mitstrand, Erich Gulbrandsen Berger, Ingri Thordsdatter Mitstrand, Giertrud Knudsdatter Oppen and Barbro Engebretsdatter[?] Bøe; mother churched 13 Oct (DA 9361 p. 435, canvas 225 left page; had read only the right page). The earlier canvases 223-224 (Jul-Sep 1754) have no Svenes entries. ¶9

Context: on 7 Nov 1779 at Svenes (DA 9362, p. 298, canvas 147), Erik Olsen of Midtstrand and Berit Olsdatter had a daughter Barbra baptised. The godparents included 'Marit [Ju?]elsdatter Haavrud' and 'Helje Olsen Mitstrand'. This shows contact between Midtstrand and Hovrud a year before Even Julsen of Hovrud married Marit Olsdatter of Midtstrand. It does not name Marit's father. ¶5

Marriage: as 'Marit Olsdat. Mitstrand' (of Midtstrand) she married Even Julsen of Hovrud on 9 Oct 1780 in Aurdal parish. Before her marriage she lived on Midtstrand, the farm of Jul Olsen (see his page). That she was his sister is possible but unproven: the record names no father. (Aurdal ministerialbok 1763-1781, source 9362, p. 253, read from the scan.) ¶2

Age note: the 1801 census age 48 (born about 1752-53) is two years high against the baptism of Oct 1754. The 1770 confirmation list for the Svenes youth (DA 9362 p. 403, canvas 192) has 'Marit Olsdat. Strand, 16', which fits a birth in 1754; the family is filed under Strand in 1799 too. Her husband's 1801 age (48 for a birth of Jun 1754) is likewise high, so the census ages in this household run about a year or two over. ¶11

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Kept at Probable: in the 1801 census Astri (21, so born about 1779-80) is 'Datter... hos forældrene' in the household of Even Julsen and his wife Marit Olsdatter (48). Even and Marit married only on 9 Oct 1780, so Astri may have been born before the wedding (a premarital child of the couple) or, less likely, of an earlier union of Even's. Her confirmation in July 1795 at 15 fits either. Astri named two daughters Marit (1812, 1814), which supports Marit as her mother. Astri's baptism (Aurdal, c.1779-80, church book 9362, not indexed) would settle it. ¶3

Searches that found nothing 2

Search for Astri Evensdatter's baptism (negative): Aurdal ministerialbok 1763-1781 (DA 9362) was read page by page. (1) Legitimate baptisms from 7 Nov 1779 to Mar 1781 (pp. 298-313, canvases 147-154): no Astri and no child of Even Julsen or of Haaverud/Hovrud. (2) The illegitimate-baptism section ('For Uægte Börns Daabe'): pp. 210-212 (canvases 108-109) cover 1771-1777, and the rest is carried over to pp. 325-326 (canvases 160-161), which cover 1777, 1778 and 1779. The section has only four entries for 1779 and no entry for 1780, and none is Astri or a child of Even Julsen or Marit Olsdatter. Legitimate baptisms for Jan-Oct 1779 (about canvases 140-146) were not read. Astri may have been baptised before Nov 1779 or outside Aurdal, so the link to Marit stays Probable. ¶4

Baptism search (negative so far): the Aurdal baptisms from 6 Oct 1754 to the end of January 1755 (DA 9361 canvas 225 right page, canvases 226 and 227, 0-based) have no child of Ole Arnesen and Ragnild Helgesdatter. Not yet read: canvas 225 left page and canvases 223-224 (July-September 1754, which would be a birth before or just after the 1 Jul 1754 wedding) and canvases 228-235 (February 1755 on; images already cached at). The Svenes and Skrautvål entries are headed 'Ved Svennæs' / 'Ved Schrutvold'. ¶8
