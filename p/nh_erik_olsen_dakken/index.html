About the family

His son Ole E. Dakken's obituary (Lake Mills Graphic, 5 Oct 1927, p. 1) calls him 'Eric' Dakken. He and his wife Margret (Olson) emigrated with the family in 1851 and bought land in Dane County, Wisconsin. He was an invalid for two years before he died there. The OCR of the death year is garbled ('186?'). Andrea's 1925 obituary says she came with her parents at about age 14, which would be about 1852-53. ¶1

Life story

PROBABLE Norwegian record: Erik Olssen (age 27, born at 'Bøe', father Ole Olssen) married Marit Olsdatter on 28 Dec 1838 in Nord-Aurdal parish. Children baptized to Erik Olsen and Marit Olsdatter in Nord-Aurdal: Andrea (b. 6 Jan 1839, Sørstrand), Astrid (b. 9 Aug 1843, Svennes), Ingeborg (b. 14 Aug 1846, Svennes) and Ole (b. 1 Sep 1849, Svennes). Ole's 1 Sep 1849 birth matches Ole E. Dakken's gravestone and obituary exactly. ¶2

No emigration entry for the family was found in the transcribed Nord-Aurdal out-migration lists for 1849-1857. ¶4

Emigration: on 26 Feb 1853 the Svennes register lists among those leaving for America 'Ole Eriksen' [sic, for Erik Olsen], 42, of Sørstrand, with Marit Olsdatter, 38, and their children Andrea 14, Astrid 10, Ingeborg 7 and Ole 5 (born 1 Sep 1849). The age of 42 points to a birth about 1810-11. The record gives no birth date or birthplace. This conflicts with the 1851 given in Ole E. Dakken's 1927 obituary; the church record is preferred. (Nord-Aurdal ministerialbok, Svennes 1842-1863, source 8986, Udflyttede 1853 nos. 21-26, read from the scan.) ¶6

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

SPECULATIVE: an Erich was baptized 20 Oct 1811 in Sør-Aurdal, son of Ole Olsen (Langødegaard) and Andrea Østensdatter. If this is him, it would explain his daughter's rare name Andrea. The residence does not match 'Bøe', so this link is not adopted. ¶3

Upgrade: Confirmed as the father of Ole Erickson Dakken (see his page). Ole's baptism in the Svennes register (Church book from Nord-Aurdal parish, Svennes local parish 1842-1863, 0542P, p. 16 no. 16), read on the Digitalarkivet index, gives child 'Ole', born 1 Sep, baptised 16 Sep 1849, father 'Erik Olsen', mother 'Marit Olsdr'. The 1 Sep 1849 birth date is the one on Ole E. Dakken's gravestone and obituary, and the Svennes emigration list of 26 Feb 1853 (already in his notes) carries the same family to America: the father aged 42 of Sørstrand, Marit Olsdatter 38, and children Andrea 14, Astrid 10, Ingeborg 7 and Ole 5. A primary record therefore names Erik Olsen and Marit Olsdatter as the parents of the Ole born 1 Sep 1849. This grade covers only his link to Ole; his own birth and parentage (Ole Olsen of Bøe, Ole Olsen) keep their earlier grade, and his 1811 baptism is still unidentified. ¶10

Alternatives considered and set aside 2

Birth candidates, none adopted (scans read): (1) Erich, born 2 Oct 1811, baptised 20 Oct 1811 at Bagn, Sør-Aurdal, son of Ole Olsen Langødegaard and Andrea Østensdatter (9372 p. 43). The father's name and the age at the 1838 marriage (27) fit. But no godparent or residence is a Bøe farm (godparents of Kobbervig, Erstad and Norødegaard), so nothing ties him to the groom 'of Bøe'. (2) Nord-Aurdal baptisms 2 Jun to 26 Dec 1811 (9365 pp. 34-41) have only one Erich: 13 Oct 1811, son of Ole Tidemandsen and Eli Pedersdatter (the Merket boy, ruled out). With the earlier sweep from 26 Dec 1810, Nord-Aurdal has no Erik son of an Ole Olsen in his birth window, apart from a gap with no entries 29 Jul to 24 Aug 1811. The mother remains unidentified. ¶7

Candidate (not proved to be him): an Erich Olsen was confirmed in Sør-Aurdal on 13 Jul 1828, list no. 25: 'Erich Olsen Holtersæter' [residence read as Holtersæter/Hollersæter], parents 'Ole Olsen Langødegaarden og Andrea Østensd.', born 2 Oct 1811, 'slet Kundskab, god Opførsel' (poor knowledge, good conduct), had natural smallpox. This is the boy baptised at Bagn 20 Oct 1811 (b. 2 Oct 1811), son of Ole Olsen Langødegaard and Andrea Østensdatter. The birth date fits the 1838 marriage age of 27 and the father's name fits, and the Erik Olsen family that emigrated in 1853 had a daughter Andrea (b. about 1839), which fits naming a first daughter after the paternal grandmother. But the confirmation gives no Bøe residence, and no out-migration to Nord-Aurdal has been found yet, so the link to the 1838 groom 'Bøe i S. A.' is still unproven. (Sør-Aurdal ministerialbok 1825-1840, source 9380, p. 494, IIIF canvas 205.) ¶8

Searches that found nothing 2

Search update (Oct 2026): no baptism of an Erik, son of an Ole Olsen, living at Bøe was found in the transcribed Nord-Aurdal books for 1806-1816. Erich son of Ole Olsen (Maanums-Eje) and Jørand Erichsd. was baptized 18 Jun 1809 at Skrautvål, but he would have been 29, not 27, in 1838. Erik son of Ole Jonssøn of Sørbøeeje and Dorthe Olsdat. was baptized 2 May 1806 at Aurdal; he would be 'Erik Olsen' by patronymic, but his father was Ole Jonsen, not the 'Ole Olssen' named in the marriage record. The Sør-Aurdal candidate (Erich, bapt. 20 Oct 1811, son of Ole Olsen and Andrea Østensdatter) remains attractive because of his daughter's rare name Andrea. That family lived at Garthus, Langødegaard and Foss in Hedalen and Bagn (children Anders 1804, Ole 1807, Ingebor 1808, Erich 1811, Ole 1816), not at Bøe, and Andrea Østensdatter was buried in Sør-Aurdal in Jan 1840. Still SPECULATIVE. Next steps: the Nord-Aurdal confirmations for about 1826-1828 (not transcribed; read the scans), and the 1838 marriage scan, which may give a residence. ¶5

Search result, run 4: the Sør-Aurdal out-migration lists (Afgangs-Lister, 9380 pp. 580-591, canvases 249-254, years 1826-1839) name no Erik/Erich Olsen leaving the parish. The Sør-Aurdal marriage register (Copulerede, 9380 pp. 424-425, canvas 192) lists no Erik Olsen and Marit Olsdatter for Jul 1838 to spring 1839, so the 28 Dec 1838 wedding was not entered in Sør-Aurdal. No record yet ties Erich Olsen (b. 2 Oct 1811, son of Ole Olsen Langødegaarden and Andrea Østensdatter) to Bøe or to this marriage. The index shows that family at Tofs in 1816 (son Ole bapt. 30 Dec 1816) and Erich at Holtersæter in 1828. Several Ole Olsens lived at Bøen in Sør-Aurdal 1827-1837 (wives Marit Andersdatter, Ingeborg Olsdatter, Sigri Olsdatter), none with Andrea Østensdatter. ¶9
