Life story

The 1666 vicar's list (35528 v21L) has Haftor Jonsen aged 64 with 'Paaffuel Haftorsen 3', so Haftor was about 60 when Pål was born; a much younger second wife fits. No primary record naming Anne has been read: the 1645 koppskatt for Selbu gives wives as 'hans hustru' only. ¶2

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

2 on the grade · 1 open question

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Graded Probable on her link to her son Poul Haftorsen Garberg (see his page). Selbu i fortid og nåtid vol. 3, Garberg 35/1 (canvas 628): '[...] 1634 [...] Haftor Jonsen [...] bruker. Han [...] født [...] 1600 [...] Han [var] gift med Anne, [og] de hadde [...] Pål [...] sønnen [...] Haftorsen, [...] 1663. Haftor døde [i] 1678, [...] Anne [...] 1690.' and 'Pål Haftorsen [f.] 1663 overtok etter moren, [...] 1693.' So the book makes Anne Haftor's wife and Pål's mother, still holding the farm as a widow until Pål took over (about 1693). Her patronymic is not in the recovered words. ¶1

Audit 7 (1-2 Nov 2026): grade unchanged (Probable, compiled source only). Under the audit-6 rule a bygdebok-only ancestor must name the primary record that would test the link; this note supplies it. ¶4a

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Test record: a probate after Anne, widow of Haftor Jonsen, at Garberg about 1690-93 (before her son Pål Haftorsen took over about 1693), in the Stjør- og Verdal probate card index 1689-1742 (Digitalarkivet 77804), GARBERG cards; it would list Pål among her heirs. ¶4b
