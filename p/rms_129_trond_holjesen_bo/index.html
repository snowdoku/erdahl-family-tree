About the family

Father of Marit Tronsdatter (see her page); Probable, graded on his link to her, from the bygdebok alone. Valdres bygdebok I F, gnr 106 Bø (canvas 268, left column): 'Trond Høljeson, bruk A, f om 1647, d 1712, g m Jøran Arnesdotter, d 1701, 52 år gamal. Born: 1 Marit f om 1680, g 1701 m Jon Knutsson Dal, sjå Dal 107. 2 Astrid f 1685, g 1706 m Kristian Knutsson Dal. 3 Arne f om 1689, g 1714 m Kari... 4 Berit f 1693. 5 [name lost] f 1695, g 1719 m Tord Olsson... 6 Eivind f om 1699, bruk...'. He had half of Bø after his father Hølje Knutsson (canvas 267-268; he bought out a part from Sjugurd in 1699 and sold to Arne Eriksson in 1703). In the parents' chapter (canvas 267, right column) he is '1 Trond f om 1647' among Hølje Knutsson's children. Rebuilt from nb.no content-search word boxes; dates from the rebuild are given as ABT. ¶1a

Collateral note: his daughter Astrid (b. 1685) married in 1706 Kristian (Christen) Knutsson of Dal, the brother of Marit's husband Jon Knutsson; Christen later farmed Rogne (Valdres probate card 11107, 1741). ¶2

Life story

Rogne 1741: DA 25286 has no IIIF manifest (404 on the nye manifest and on the image's info.json). The viewer image of view 294 (media.digitalarkivet.no/image/ade59ded-0722-4587-92ef-e5689cc37894, 3215 x 2543 px, the largest offered) shows the Rogne entry starting two-fifths down p. 561, 'Skiftebrev passeret paa gaarden Rogne i Rogne annex i Midre Slidre Præstegield i Waldres... 5 Februar 1741', but the hand is so faint and the bleed-through so heavy that the guardians cannot be read even with contrast and sharpening. View 295 (p. 562) is the valuation and closing. This record cannot be read from the online scan; a guardian Tronsen there would need the original or a better scan. ¶6

Correction:. His test-record note expected the 1664/66 manntall to list him as Hølje's son aged about 17-19. Both 1664 lists that give ages have him younger: the bailiff's list b) of 12 Nov 1664 (DA 35563, p. 212, view 114) has 'Thrond Helgesen 12 aar' (clearly written) beside his brother 'Knud Helgesen 16 aar', under 'Helje Knudsen 45' on the farm 'Aaberigs'; the vicar's list of 5 Nov 1664 (DA 35504, p. 10) has 'Trond Helgeß. 1[2?]'. So he was born about 1652, not about 1647 as the bygdebok rebuild has it (the rebuild also made him older than Knut, b. 1648, whom both lists show as the elder). Birth changed to ABT 1652. The farm is written Aaberg in 1664, distinct from Bø (see the note on Hølje Knudsen). His own grade (link to Marit Tronsdatter) is unchanged: Probable. A marriage to Jøran Arnesdatter (b. about 1649) at about 27 fits. ¶9

Alive 23 Sep 1701 at Aabjør as the 'Igienlevende Mand Trond Helgesen' at the probate after his wife Jøran Arnesdatter (card 15291; DA 25283 s. 360b-361a, view 364), with children Even 2, Arne 12, Marit Trondsdatter 19, Astri 16, Berit 8, Ingebiør 6. This is the probate of his wife, not his own; it names Marit as his daughter, but his grade (link to Marit Tronsdatter of Dal) stays Probable for the reasons given under Jøran Arnesdatter (different farm, 37 years). His link up to Hølje Knudsen: the 1664 son 'Thrond Helgesen 12' on 'Aaberigs' and the 1701 'Trond Helgesen' on Aabjør are on the same farm, 37 years apart, with no relationship word at the far end; parents stay Probable. ¶10

Marriage read: Aurdal ministerialbok DA 9360, canvas 213 (p. 229, left page), under the service of 27 Dec 1701 ('Fer. 3 Nativ.', third day of Christmas; the last service of 1701 before 'Saa endis det gamle Aar' and the title page 'anno 1702'): 'Copulerede: Thron Siffuersen Bergier og Marthe Knudsdatter. Jon Knudsøn og Marthe Throndsdatter Dahl.' The Digitalarkivet index (pv00000000706837, 'Joen Knuds.' x 'Marthe Throensd.') gives the date as 27-11; the scan shows it is 27 Dec. No betrothal for the couple was found in the Aurdal entries of Oct-Dec 1701 (canvases 208-212). Same grade reasoning as on Jøran Arnesdatter: Probable. ¶12

He was alive on 31 Jan 1707. The notarial contract made in Christiania that day between lagmann Hannibal Stockfleth and 'Tron Bøe og Olle Ondstad paa egne og Consorters vegne' of Aurdal, about timber floated past Stockfleth's farms in Etnedal (DA 10262 s. 113-115, views 115-117), was read at the Aurdal ting of 3 Mar 1707. 'Tron Bøe' signs as a leading party for the Aurdal farmers. This fits the bygdebok's Trond Høljesen, holder of half of Bø until about 1712. ¶7

Alive and at Aabjør in Feb 1707: in the Søndre Strand estate after Helge Skafhelden (DA 25284 s. 336a, view 340) 'Tron Helgesen Aabiør fordrer for Tømmer' (claims for timber). This fits the notarial 'Tron Bøe' of 31 Jan 1707 and suggests the bygdebok's Bø and the probate's Aabjør are the same holding. ¶13

Collateral, Aabjør cards DA 39948: card 15285, Aabjør 1727 (protocol 1716-37 fol. 893b): 'Kari Biørnsdtr. +, g. Arne Trondsen', children Tron (infant), Biørn 6, Ingri 11, Jøran 9, Marit 8 (Arne = his son, 12 in 1701; Jøran named after her grandmother). Card 15301, Aabjør 25 Jun 1743 (protocol 1737-43 S. 1107): 'Even Trondsen +, g. Ingeri Erichsdtr.', children Trond, Erick, Helge 21, Jørand 28, with 8¼ skind in Aaberg (Even = his son, 2 in 1701). Both sons named a daughter Jøran and a son Trond, which supports the 1701 family. ¶14

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

Corrections 1

Correction (farm name): the 1701 probate is held 'paa Aabiør i Ourdals Præstegield' and the card files it under 'Aabjør i Aurdal i Nordre Aurdal' (card 15291), with land 'i Aabiør 1 hud 4½ skind'. This matches the 1664 bailiff's 'Aaberigs' (Helje Knudsen 45, son Thrond Helgesen 12) and the vicar's '[?]raberg'. So the family's farm is Aabjør (Aaberg), not Bø; the bygdebok's gnr 106 heading should be checked (it may be Aabjør itself). Places left unchanged pending that check. ¶11

Searches that found nothing 4

Test record searched, negative: the Valdres probate card index (DA 39948) has no card for a probate after Trond Høljesen of Bø about 1712-13. The 'Bø i (Nordre) Aurdal' run reads, in card order: 1665 = 25 Sep 1693, Erich Nielsen g. Gure Knudsdatter (Toten, Vardal og Biri protocol 1692-95, fol. 92a); 1667 = 13 Oct 1729, Gulbrand Helgesen g. Astri Olsdatter (protocol 1716-37, fol. 958b); 1669 = 1 Feb 1735, lifetime division of the widow Astri Olsdatter; 1673 = 15 Oct 1735, Ole Knudsen g. Beritte Torchelsdatter (fol. 1272a); 1677 = 19 Jul 1754, Engebret Engebretsen (Bø i Ulnes); 1681 = 20 Jul 1784, Guri Erichsdatter. Even numbers are blank backs, so nothing lies between 1693 and 1729. So either no skifte was held after Trond (the bygdebok says his sons divided the farm, 'med 8 skinn kvar', when he died), or it is in an uncarded protocol (the 1708-1716 Valdres protocol). ¶3a

Search note: Digitalarkivet's probate series for the district runs Hadeland, Land og Valdres skifteprotokoll no. 4, 1704-1709 (DA 25284), then no. 5, 1716-1737 (DA 25285); no sorenskriver probate protocol for 1709-1716 is scanned, and the prosti series (DA 35450) starts in 1716. So the 'uncarded 1708-1716 protocol' does not exist online, and a probate after Trond Høljesen (about 1712) or Anne Knudsdatter (about 1713) cannot be read there. The record that could still test these links is the mortgage book (pantebok) for Hadeland, Land og Valdres 1704-1715 (DA 10262), for a deed or division among the heirs, read page by page (not indexed). ¶4

Search note: the mortgage book for Hadeland, Land og Valdres 1704-1715 (DA 10262, 'Pantebok nr. 4') is scanned two pages per view, with view n carrying printed page about n-2 (view 2 = s. 1, 28 Oct 1704; view 40 = s. 39; view 100 = s. 97-98, September-November 1706). The deeds are copied in full under short underlined headings ('X og Consortes... til Y'), grouped by tinglysing session with a parish heading. So 1707 starts at about view 105, and 1712-13 is well past view 200. Not read further this pass; the next reader should scan the session headings for 'Ourdals Prestegield' from view 105 (1707, Dal) and from about view 220 (1712-13, Bø). ¶5

Division search, negative: no deed, division or quitclaim among Trond Høljesen's heirs was found in the Aurdal sessions of DA 10262 read this pass: 3 Mar 1707 (views 112-116), 11 Jul 1707 (views 130-134), 2 Mar 1713 (views 265-267), 10 Jul 1713 (views 274-276) and 6 Nov 1713 (views 287-290). Not read: the 1708-1712 Aurdal sessions (about views 140-260; v200 = Jul 1710; v260 = Jan-Apr 1712, Hadeland) and the 1714-15 sessions (views 300 on; v320 = Jul-Aug 1714, Hadeland). The session headings read 'Ourdals Prestegields Tinglyste Breve... paa Soerums Tingstue' and come after Slidre and before Land in each round. ¶8
