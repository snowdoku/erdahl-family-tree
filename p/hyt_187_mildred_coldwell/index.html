Life story

Mildred Coldwell, wife of Dean John Eveleigh of or near Bandon, Co. Cork, and mother of his five daughters. The pedigree describes her as 'daughter of * * * * Boyle who was cousin german to Primate Boyle'. The surname given for her father is left blank in the printed text, so her parentage is not taken further (Blennerhassett Pedigree, in Hickson, Old Kerry Records, 1872, p. 53). ¶1

Brady (vol. 2, p. 428) calls her 'Mildred, dau. of Thomas Caldwell, esq., by Jane, youngest dau. of Michael Boyle', and gives her will as dated 28 Jan 1681 and proved 8 Mar 1681 (probably 1681/2, Old Style). Phillimore's index of Cork and Ross wills lists 'Eveleigh, Mildred, Cork'. The Blennerhassett Pedigree (c.1733) independently calls her 'Mildred Coldwell (daughter of * * * * Boyle who was cousin german to Primate Boyle)', so both sources make her mother a Boyle. Added. ¶3

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
