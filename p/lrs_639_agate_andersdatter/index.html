About the family

Widow of Endre Jørgensen of Sognes at his probate of 2 Jun 1701 (1b fol. 343b): 'hans efterlevende danne Qvinde Aagaatha Anders datter'; the heirs are 'deris tilsammen avlede børn' (their jointly begotten children), among them 'Mar[...] Eillefs Botnes'. Alive 2 Jun 1701. Read. ¶3

Life story

Bygdebok (Botnen, canvas 884): widow of Endre Jørgenson at the 1701 division, mother of the wife of Eilef of Botnen. ¶1

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 5

Graded on her link to Maritte Endresdatter (see her page). ¶2a

Graded Probable: a bygdebok is a compiled secondary source and the passage was rebuilt from word boxes with gaps. ¶2b

Upgrade: Probable to Confirmed, graded on the link to the daughter Maritte Endresdatter. The 1701 probate (a primary record, read) names a daughter 'Mar[...]' of Endre Jørgensen and Agatha Andersdatter ('deris tilsammen avlede børn') as of 'Eillefs Botnes' (Eilef's, of Botnen). Continuity: Eilef Olsen held Botnen from the 1690s to his death (bygdebok canvas 884; probate 20 Jun 1730, 3b fol. 433b-434a), and that 1730 probate names his widow 'Maritte Endres datter' as the mother of his children, so the 'Mar[...]' of 1701 is the same woman by husband, farm and patronymic. ¶4a

Audit 7 (1-2 Nov 2026): grade unchanged (Probable, compiled source only). Under the audit-6 rule a bygdebok-only ancestor must name the primary record that would test the link; this note supplies it. ¶5a

Audit 8 (1-2 Nov 2026): Confirmed kept, graded on her link to Maritte Endresdatter (see her page). The 1701 probate calls the heirs 'deris tilsammen avlede børn', so it names her as the mother as well. The forename cut off after 'Mar' does not block the grade; see the audit-8 note on her husband, Endre Jørgensen. The audit-7 note on her record that asks for this probate is now answered. ¶6
