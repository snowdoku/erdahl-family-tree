Life story

Roger Boyle of Herefordshire. Lodge (Peerage of Ireland, 1789, vol. 1, p. 144) says he married Jane, daughter of Thomas Pattishull of Herefordshire, Esq., 'and had a daughter Elizabeth, and four sons': John of Hereford (who married Alice, daughter of Alexander Hayworth of Burden Hall); Roger, ancestor of the Earl of Cork (that is, Roger Boyle of Preston, Kent, husband of Joan Naylor); Michael, merchant of London; and Hugh, mercer of London, who died without issue. Cotton's Fasti (vol. 1, p. 126, note) independently lists 'Roger Boyle (I.), of Herefordshire' with four sons, Michael, a merchant of London; Roger (II.); John; and Hugh. The birth year is an estimate from his grandsons' births in the 1560s-80s. Added. ¶1

The 1569 Herefordshire visitation papers back up Lodge's account of Roger's family. The Boyle pedigree (Harl. MS 1140, f. 103) lists '2. Roger Boyle' as the second son of Lewis Boyle of Bidney, whose eldest son John's will was proved in 1551 (TNA PROB 11/34/206). The Haworth pedigree (Harl. MS 615, ff. 19v-20) has 'Ales, maryed John Boyle, son of Roger second son of Lewes'. That is Lodge's 'John of Hereford, who married Alice, daughter of Alexander Hayworth', named as Roger's eldest son and Michael's brother. The visitation does not name Michael. The PCC will of 'Barrowe or Barrow Boile' of St Bartholomew the Less, London, 1598 (PROB 11/91/192), belongs to a son of John and Alice (the visitation lists 'Barrow' among their sons), so a nephew of Michael was also in London. His grade is unchanged (Probable, on his link to Michael). This note supersedes remark that Lodovic was too early to be his father: the visitation dates Lewis to Henry VII. Added. ¶3

Three Boyle wills read on the images. Grade unchanged (Probable, on his link to Michael Boyle). (1) His putative brother John Boyle of Hereford (PROB 11/34/206, 1551) names a wife Anne, seven sons and two daughters, but no brother. (2) Barrowe Boile of St Bartholomew the Less, London (PROB 11/91/192, nuncupative, Feb 1597/8) left 'to his brother Roger Boile seaven poundes', to 'his sister Atkinson', 'to his mother Alice Boile widow three poundes', 'his sister Marie Boile', 'his sister Elizabeth Boile', and the residue to 'Jeronime Boile his sister', who took administration. A widowed mother Alice fits Barrowe being a son of Roger's eldest son John and Alice Haworth (Haworth pedigree), but the will names no father, grandfather or uncle. (3) James Boyle, mercer of London (PROB 11/117/199, 1608/11), names no parents (see Michael Boyle). None of the three names Michael the merchant or Roger himself. ¶5

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Probable, graded on his link to his son Michael Boyle. Two compiled works (Lodge, and Cotton, who drew on the Earl of Cork's papers) both name Michael the London merchant as his son. Cotton's description of Bishop Michael Boyle as 'cousin-german' of the 1st Earl of Cork also requires the merchant and the Earl's father to be brothers. Lodge carries the line further back, through Lodovic Boyle of Bidney, living under Henry VI, and Elizabeth Russell. That is not added: Lodovic's sons would have been born before about 1460, which is too early for a man whose grandsons were born in the 1560s-80s, so a generation is probably missing. ¶2a

Searches that found nothing 1

FamilySearch: the Hereford St Nicholas register (England Marriages index) has several Boyle brides 1562-1574 (Maud x William Challis 1562; Elyzabeth x Rychard Barrow 1563; Elyzabeth x Rychard Jenkins 1564; Jane x Harry Wathen 1571; Elynor x Harry Voghan 1571; Jame x Edward Dauncy 1571; Margret x John Hopwod 1574) and James Boyle x Kateryn Voghan 1571, which shows a Boyle family living in Hereford city in the next generation. None names Roger, and his marriage to Jane Pattishall (c.1530s) predates the surviving registers. Grade unchanged. Added (FamilySearch, signed in, 2 Nov 2026). ¶4

Open questions and the next record to read 1

Test record: the Herefordshire Boyle pedigree in the Visitation of Herefordshire (1569 or 1634), or the 1st Earl of Cork's own 'True Remembrances' or Lismore diaries naming his uncle Michael. Added. ¶2b
