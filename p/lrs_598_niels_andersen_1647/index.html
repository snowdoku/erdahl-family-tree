About the family

Son Gullak (Probable): father of Gullak Nielsen of Ytre Oppedal (see his page). For: (1) 'Gullach Nielsen' 12 (b. about 1689) is the only Gullak on Ytre Oppedal in 1701, and Gullak Nielsen lived and died on Ytre Oppedal (probate there 28 Jul 1766); (2) the age fits a man whose eldest daughter Anna married in 1739; (3) Gullak named his second son Niels. ¶2a

Other son: Matias (Mathias) Nielsen, 16 in 1701 (b. about 1685); not created. Wife not known; the 1701 manntall lists only men and boys. ¶3

Life story

1701 manntall: on Ytre Oppedal (Ytreoppedale, a Bondegaard), Eivindvik (Gulen) prestegjeld, 'Niels Andrs:' (Niels Andersen), aged 54, farmer, with the sons Matias Nielsen 16 and Gullach Nielsen 12, and the soldier Thore Pedersen 24. Birth about 1647 is inferred from the age. Manntall 1701 no. 8, Digitalarkivet 38455, viewer 54, printed s.91. ¶1

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Regrade (1 Nov 2026): upgraded to Confirmed as father of Gullak Nielsen (see his page); graded on his link to Gullak Nielsen. The naming record is the 1701 manntall for Ytre Oppedal, Eivindvik (Digitalarkivet 38455, viewer 54, s.91), which lists 'Niels Andrs:' 54, farmer, with his sons Matias Nielsen 16 and Gullach Nielsen 12. Continuity: the boy is followed on the same farm by consecutive records of one life: the betrothal of 'Gullach Nielss: Yttreopdal' at Brekke on 1 Jan 1716 (same patronymic and farm, 15 years later, at a fitting age of about 27); his children's baptisms at Brekke in 1719 and 1720 as 'Gulig/Gulich ytter Opdal'; and his probate on Ytre Oppedal, 28-29 Jul 1766. ¶5a

Records that disagree 1

Against or open: no record names Gullak's father directly; the 1664 census has no Anders among the Ytre Oppedal farmers, so Niels may have come from elsewhere. ¶2b

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

No rival: he is the only Gullak on Ytre Oppedal in 1701, and found no other Gullak Nielsen in the area. The earlier Probable grade treated the join as elimination; under the clarified rule a record that names the relationship, followed by same-farm records with no rival, is Confirmed. Open: his marriage entry of 1716 does not name the groom's father. ¶5b

Searches that found nothing 1

1664 bailiff's census, negative: the rest of Eivindvik prestegjeld in the bailiff's census of 21 Jun 1664 (Digitalarkivet 35573, viewers 133-137, printed s.246-255: Eivindvik sogn from Barbache onward, the 'Botners-Lenn' husmenn and the Hyllestad-sogn farms, Bø sogn and Lavik sogn up to Eicken) was read from the scans: no 'Niels Andersen' of about 17 appears in the sons' or servants' columns. Together with table for Brekke sogn and skim of s.242-245, this leaves no 1664 household in Gulen with Niels as a son, so his father Anders is still unidentified. (The 1645 poll tax has a 'Niells Andersen' among the farmers' sons at Brendisdall, pt00000000060385, but he would have been born before about 1633, far too early for a man aged 54 in 1701.) ¶6

Open questions and the next record to read 3

Would confirm: a probate after Niels Andersen or his wife naming Gullak (not in the Ytre Sogn probate index for Oppedal 1698-1739; the protocols for about 1729-37 may be missing from the index), or Gullak's marriage to Britte Iversdatter (about 1715-21, Gulen 11437-11439) if it names his father. ¶2c

Lead for his wife (not a proof): 'Magnild Ytter opdal ætat 84 aar' was buried at Brekke on 24 Jan 1721 (Gulen 11438 c54 right page), so she was born about 1637. Gullak Nielsen named a daughter Magnilde, and a 'Magnilde ibid' (Ytre Oppedal) stood godmother there in 1718. She could be Niels Andersen's wife (ten years older than him) or an older relative; untested. ¶4a

Next: a probate after a Magnild of Ytre Oppedal, 1721-1723, in the Ytre Sogn index. ¶4b
