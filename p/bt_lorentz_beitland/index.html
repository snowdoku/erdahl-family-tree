Life story

Beret Martha's younger brother. Born 30 January 1855 and baptized 11 March 1855 in the Hegra annex, entry no. 18 on page 30; his father is entered as a husmann at Beitlandsgjerdet. ¶1

He emigrated with the family and kept the farm name Beitland as a fixed American surname - the reason the whole family is findable in Minnesota at all. ¶2

Married Anna O. Hauge in Faribault County, Minnesota, on 19 November 1877, certificate C-101. The county index enters him as 'Lorence T. Beitland', which carries his Norwegian patronymic as a middle initial. ¶3

Twelve children are recorded on his memorial: Thomas L. (1879-1931), Martin (1881-1910), Alphine (1885-1976), Betsy Olina (1888-1960), Marie Gurine (1890-1967), Lovisa (1892, died in infancy), Louise Amanda (1894-1970), Albert (1896, died in infancy), Almer Leonard (1897-1974, whose stone spells the name Baitland), Mathias L. (1900-1953), Magda (1902-1986, married Siefken) and William (1904-1970). The eldest son was named Thomas for his grandfather and a later son Mathias for the brother Matias left behind. ¶4

1885 Minnesota census (index only): 'Lewis Beitland', 30, born Norway, with Thomas, 5, Martin, 3, and Ella, 2, all born Minnesota, in Seely Township, Faribault County (MNHS cert. 1885-mnsc_25-0232-10, -14, -15, -16). Lines 11-13 (probably his wife) are not in the surname extract. Age, birthplace and the sons Thomas (1879) and Martin (1881) fit Lorentz and his children; the image has not been read. 'Lewis' here is not Lasse 'Lewis' Erdahl, who was in Rome Township in 1885 aged 34. ¶6

1895 Minnesota census (index only): 'L. T. Beitland', 40, born Norway, with Thomas, 15, Ella, 11, Alphina, 9, Marie, 4, and Louisa, 1, all born Minnesota, in Seely Township (MNHS cert. 1895-mnv290_57-0669-24, -26, -28, -29, -31, -32). The initials L. T. (Lorentz Thomasen) and the children Thomas, Alphine, Marie and Louise of his memorial identify him. Lines 25, 27 and 30 are probably his wife and other children, indexed under another spelling; the image has not been read. ¶7

Died 27 June 1933, aged 78, and buried beside his parents at Bricelyn. ¶5
