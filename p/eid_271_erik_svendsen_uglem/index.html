About the family

His sister was the wife of Ole Sivertsen Alset ('fasterens mann' = the children's father's sister's husband) - i.e. Ingri Svendsdatter (see her page), who is recorded as Ole's wife in 1746 and 1762. His first cousin was Haaver (Håvard) Olsen of Store Evjen. ¶2

Life story

Mother added from the bygdebok (Selbu i fortid og nåtid vol. 4, Uglan bruk 49, canvas 26): Svend Eriksen (1691-1722 user, b. ca. 1650, d. 1723) 'G. m. Ragnhild [...], f. ca. 1659, d. 1742', children Erik ca. 1688, Pål ca. 1693 (m. Ingeborg [...] Berge), Ola ca. 1697, Ingrid 1701 (m. Ola [...] Alset). Probable only (bygdebok). ¶8

1701 manntall, Uglem (canvas 117-118): 'Siund Erichsen' 52 with sons 'Erich Siundsen 13, Poule 8, Olle 4' - Erik, born about 1688, son of Svend (written Siund) Eriksen; this is taken to be him (Probable identification of the 1701 household; the probate patronymic Svendsen and the farm agree). ¶3

Probate (skifte) after him, 20 Jun 1741: 'Uglan, Selbu. 20/6-1741: Erik Svendsen Uglen. Enke: Guru Tostensdtr. Barn: Svend Eriksen, 10 år gl. - Verge: fasterens mann, Ole Siversen Alset. Tosten Eriksen, 8 år - farens fetter, Haaver Olsen Stor-Evjen. Ragnild Eriksdtr., 17 år; Brynild Eriksdtr., 13 år - morbroren, Siver Tostensen Berge. Anne Eriksdtr., 5 år - farens fetter, Haaver Olsen Stor-Evjen. Marit Eriksdtr., 2 år - fasterens mann, Ole Siversen Alset.' Jordegods in Uglen 12 mrkl. (card 77804 c2055; read by the orchestrator from the card). Father of Torsten (Tosten) Eriksen Uglem, aged 8 (born about 1733). ¶1

Daughter Brynild (13 in 1741) is very probably Brønild Erichsdatter, wife of Bersvend Pedersen of Slinden, who died before her probate on 11 Jun 1762 (24755 fol. 241b, view 258). In it 'Svend Ericksen Uglen' was guardian of her daughter Mali, and her children were Peder Bersvendsen 9, Mali and Guri (bapt. Aug 1757). This is Probable, since the relationship word for the guardian is not read with certainty. It rules her out as Brynill Eriksdatter, wife of Per Evensen (see her page), who was alive in 1769. ¶5

Protocol entry read: the register's 'fol. 1049' is right. Erik's skifte starts at the foot of fol. 1049 (view 312 right; the folio-to-view offset here is fol - 737, not the fol - 739 of, which is why view 310 looked wrong) and runs onto fol. 1049b. 'Anno 1741 d. 20 Jun... Skifte efter afg. Erich Svendsen Uglen... mellem hans efterlatte Hustru Guri Tostensdatter og deres sammen avlede Børn: 1. Svend Erichsen 10 aar, 2. Tosten Erichsen 8 aar, 3. Ragnild Erichsdatter 17 aar, 4. Brynild Erichsdatter 13 aar, 5. Anne Erichsdatter 5 aar, 6. Marit Erichsdatter 2 aar.' Guardians: 'Børnenes Fastersmand Ole Siversen' for Svend and Marit; 'Børnenes Morbroder Siver Tostensen Berge' for Ragnild and Brynild; 'Haaver Olsen Store Evjen, den afg. Mands Søskendebarn' for Tosten and Anne. The protocol agrees with card 77804 c2055 and adds that Haaver Olsen Store Evjen was Erik's first cousin. It names no parent of Erik's. ¶6

Land record: Selbu panteregister 22743 image 72: Uglem was conveyed by inheritance to 'Torsten Erichsen med flere arvinger... paa Skifte efter Faderen Erich Svendsen' (pantebok A-1 fol. 361, c.1741). A second record naming Torsten as his son; grade unchanged. ¶7
