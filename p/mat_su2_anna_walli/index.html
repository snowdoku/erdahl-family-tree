Life story

She was the widow of JOHAN WALLI (b. 1885, d. 1959). Both her husbands' wives died in 1959, and the two widowed people married fifteen months later. She is buried not with Ole but with Johan - "Anna Walli, b. 1882 d. 1971, s/w Johan", Selbu Cemetery reference hrp-cem-whi-selbu_0146 - even though she died as Anna W. Slind. ¶2

The Walli connection runs twice through this family. Ole's eldest son GILBERT had married MAGNA WALLI, b. 21 July 1909, on 25 January 1934. Magna is very probably Johan and Anna's daughter, which would make Anna the mother-in-law Ole married. Twelve Wallis are buried at Selbu Cemetery; the parentage is Probable, not proved, and the record that would settle it is Magna's own death certificate or Whitman County birth return. ¶4

THIRD WIFE of Ole G. Slind, married at Colfax, Whitman County, Washington on 15 January 1961, when he was seventy-six and she about seventy-nine. Whitman County Auditor, Marriage Records 1872-2002, document eawhmc5357: groom "Ole Gunderson Slind", bride "Anna Walli". ¶1

Death: 2 October 1971, Whitman County. Washington DOH death certificate DE_1971024819 and DOH Death Index reference 31E9BD84-33D7-8443-8688-6154E44A146E, both as "SLIND, ANNA W" - the W being Walli. The age field on both is blank. ¶3
