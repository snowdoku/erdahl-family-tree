Confidence: the grade and how it changed 3

Speculative father of Jacob Render (see his page). ¶1a

Graded on that link: the groom row of the Bietigheim marriage of 30 Jan 1731 reads 'Conrad Jacob Render', relatives 'Johann Conrad, Maria Sophia' (father, then bride; record 7270554, image 1184899-00394). Identifying that groom with the Jacob Render who fathered Elisabetha Regina (married 1774) rests on the first name of the wife ('Maria' in the 1821 burial, 7063442) and the absence of another Jacob Render at Bietigheim; with two inferential steps on unlabelled rows this is Speculative. Ancestry 61023 index rows do not label roles (BRIEF audit 5 rule 3), so this rests at Probable at most until the image or a labelled FamilySearch twin is read; images not seen. ¶1b

Upgrade: Speculative to Probable, graded on his link to Jacob Render (see his page): FamilySearch, 'Deutschland, ausgewählte evangelische Kirchenbücher 1500-1971' (= 'Germany, Lutheran Baptisms, Marriages, and Burials, 1500-1971'), QPGG-23PM: marriage at Bietigheim 30 Jan 1731, groom 'Conrad Jacob Render', field Father = 'Johann Conrad Renders'. So he is certainly the father of Conrad Jacob Render; what remains Probable is that Conrad Jacob is the tree's Jacob Render, father of Elisabetha Regina (same name and wife's first name, 1731-1774). ¶3a
