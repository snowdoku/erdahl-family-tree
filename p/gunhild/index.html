Life story

Identified (Probable, strong) as Gunhild (Gunild) Hansdatter, born 9 Jul 1862, baptized 21 Jul 1862 in Nissedal, daughter of gaardmand Hans Thorjussen of Ibrække and Thuri Thorkildsdatter (Nissedal ministerialbok 1861-1887 p.4 no.19, scan read; also klokkerbok 1846-1870 p.201 no.37). Same birth date (9/7/62) and parents in her confirmation 28 Apr 1878, Nissedal (no.38, p.86, scan read). ¶1

Emigration (Probable): 'Gunhild Hansen', unmarried servant (tjenestepige), of Nissedal, born 1864 (calculated), left Kristiansand 25 Apr 1884 on the American Line, in a group with Ole Nottosen of Hornnes. Destination not stated. Her WPA biography implies she emigrated about 1885-86 at age 22. 1884 is two years earlier, but she was in fact 21 in 1884. ¶4

The 'Underberg' name: the Nissedal farm Undeberg is at/part of Rønningen. Her father Hans lived at Rønningen when he married in 1858. Her uncle Halvor Thorjussen farmed Rønningen. Halvor's wife Sigrid Paulsdatter is called 'Undeberg' as a godparent in 1862, and Halvor's sons Torjus, Paul and Tellef H. Rønningen/Undeberg lived at Undeberg in 1910-1920. So 'Hans Underberg' in the WPA biography fits Hans Thorjussen of Rønningen/Undeberg. ¶5

Census: 1865 at Ibrække øvre, east part, Nissedal, with parents and sisters Aasild (7) and Kari (2). 1875 a 'jæterpige' (herd girl/servant) at Grimstvedt søndre, Nissedal. ¶3

Jacob O. Erickson (1887-1956), linked as her child on Find a Grave #26528595, was born before her 1889 marriage. Not researched here. ¶7

1900 census (image read): wife of Tosten Olson at Center Township, Nelson County, North Dakota; born June 1862 in Norway, age 37 or 38 (overwritten), married 10 years, mother of 6 children of whom 6 were living, immigrated 1885 (15 years in the U.S.), could read and write but not speak English. Five children were at home: Henry (1891), Ole (1892), Rachael (1894), Sam (1896) and Carl (1898). The sixth living child is not in the household; Jacob O. Erickson (b. about 1887, linked to her on Find a Grave) would fit, but this is unproven. ¶9

Birth year, further evidence: the 1900 census's 'June 1862' agrees with her Norwegian baptism (born 9 Jul 1862) and not with the 1864 of her emigration record, Find a Grave and the WPA biography (the census month, June, is one month earlier than the baptismal July). It is the earliest U.S. record found and it keeps the Norwegian year. The 1900 immigration year 1885 is a year after her Kristiansand departure on 25 Apr 1884. ¶10

1920 census (image read): 'Sunhil Langerude', wife, 57, born Norway, immigrated 1883, in Tosten's household in ED 250, Williams County, North Dakota. Age 57 in early 1920 fits a birth in 1862. ¶12

Records that disagree 2

CONFLICT: US sources (Find a Grave/WPA) give birth 9 Jul 1864. The day and month match exactly; the year differs by two. Her Norwegian emigration record gives a calculated birth year of 1864, so she seems to have used 1864 from the time she emigrated. ¶2

CONFLICT/OPEN: the WPA biography says she worked a year for her 'brother' in Benson, MN. Norwegian records show no surviving full brother. Her only brother, Torjus (b. 1869), died in 1871. The 'brother' may have been a cousin or brother-in-law. Not resolved. ¶6
