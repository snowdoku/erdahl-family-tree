About the family

Children found in the Lesja church book (0512P): Gurri, bapt. 1 Jan 1784 at Verket, father 'Peder Engebrigts.' of Sør Eneboe, mother 'Ane Pedersd.' (index, p. 175); Peder, bapt. 1 Jan 1786 at Verket, father 'Peder Ingebrigts.', mother Anne Pedersd. of Søreneboe (index, p. 213); possibly Ingebret, born 20 Sep and bapt. 5 Oct 1794 at Verket, 'Peder Morkens og Hustrue Annes B: Ingebret' (scan read, p. 427), godparents Lars Morken, Siugurd Eeneboe, Hans Qvam, Eli Eeneboe and Guri Qvam. The 1794 entry gives no patronymics; it is linked to him by the son's name and the Eeneboe godparents (inference). The parents are written as of Morken, not Eeneboe, and another Peter Ingebretsen lived at Morken in 1798 with a wife Guri Pedersdatter, so the 1794 couple may instead be that man with an earlier wife Anne. The 1794 link is unproved. That child is not in the 1801 census and probably died; the Ingebret aged 2 in 1801 would then be a second son of the name, whose baptism (about 1798-99) has not been found. ¶2

Father, inferred: Probable father of Anne Pedersdatter (see her notes). ¶4

Life story

Marriage (scan read): Lesja ministerialbok 1777-1819 (0512P, source 9237, canvas 84), p. 169, 8th Sunday after Trinity 1783, Verket (Lesjaverk) annex: 'Copul. Soldat Peder Ingebrigtss. Søreneboe og Pige Ane Pedersd. Kiønsletten' - the soldier Peder Ingebrigtsen of Søreneboe and the maiden Ane Pedersdatter of Kiønsletten. No sureties, ages or fathers are written. The index gives the wedding as 10 Aug 1783 (pv00000001981136) and an engagement on 15 Jun 1783 with the bride 'of Eneboe' (pv00000001983480; the scan linked to that index row is a 1795 page, so the engagement entry itself was not read). This is the marriage of the couple of the 1801 Eeneboe household; their children Gurri (bapt. 1 Jan 1784, Sør Eneboe, father 'Peder Engebrigts.'), Peder (bapt. 1 Jan 1786, mother Anne Pedersd., Søreneboe, pd00000009245548) follow it. The bride's home Kiønsletten, and the farm-name pattern (a 'Torger Siversen Kiønsletten' married on the same day), are leads for her own parents, whose baptisms (c.1753-57) fall in the unindexed Lesja book of 1732-1776. ¶5

The Lesja bygdebok (vol. 1, nb.no item 8e238b3c93fae7ff68ee91598c918b4d, word-box rebuild) independently makes him the son of Engebret Larsen and Guri of Sør-Einbu (Søndre Eneboe): canvas 259, entry 13-3, Guri (widow of an Erik) 'g. 1747 m. Engebret Larss., ca. 1705-83... kjøpte garden 1748. Barn: Marit, 1748; Lars, 1751, tok over; Peder, 1753, sjå... Einbu (21-1); Guri, 1755-56'; canvas 205, entry 21-1, 'Peder Engebrets. frå Sør-Einbu (13-3), 1753-1804, g. 1783 m. Anne Pedersd.... 1757-?', children Guri 1783, Peder 1785, Anne ca. 1790 (took over), Engebret 1794 (probably dead before 1801), Engebret 1798. This agrees with the 1763 manntall (Engebret Larsøn and Guri Pedersd. at Søndre Eneboe, children over 12 Erich, Lars, Marit, Marit; Erich = Guri's son by her first husband, b. 1746) and gives his birth year 1753 (fits 48 in 1801) and his death 1804 (so the burial 'Peder Ingebrets of Kloppen', 5 Feb 1804, aged 47, is probably his, with an age error). His parents' grade stays Probable (compiled source). No probate after Engebret Larsen (d. 1783) was found on the Nord-Gudbrandsdal probate cards for Eneboe (source 39945, images 2623-2679 checked: only 'Enebo i Lesje' 1842-75); Eneboe lay under the Lesja ironworks, whose own skifteprotokoll 1752-1802 (cards interfiled as 'Lesja Jernverk') is the next place to look, then his baptism 1753 in 9236 (c176-185). ¶7

Baptism found. Lesja ministerialbok 1732-1776 (9236), p. 379 (canvas 182), Dom. 8 p. Trin. [5 Aug] 1753 at Verket: 'Bapt: Engebret Søreneboes Peder', godparents Siver Olsen, Anders Søreneboe, Knud Gram, Guri Eneboe and Mari...; the churching of 'Engebret Søreneboes' wife is entered the same day. The year fits his age 48 in the 1801 census and the bygdebok's 1753; it does not fit the burial lead 'Peder Ingebrets of Kloppen, 5 Feb 1804, aged 47' (b. about 1757), which may therefore be another man or an age error. Peder's own grade (his link to his child) is unchanged. ¶8

1801 census (scan read): Peder Ingebretsen, 48, husbonde (householder), married once (gift 1ste gang), husmann med jord (cottager with land), at Eeneboe in the Verket annex of Lesja, household 4, with wife Anne Pedersdatter 44 and children Peder Pedersen 15, Ingebret Pedersen 2 and Anne Pedersdatter 10. His birth year about 1753 is inferred from that age. ¶1

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the grade · 1 alternative set aside

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Probable parents Engebret Larsen and Guri Pedersdatter of Søndre Eneboe (see his page, Guri Pedersdatter), from the 1763 Lesja extra-tax manntall (source 45390, view 287): No. 6 'Søndre Eneboe', Engebret Larsøn and Guri Pedersd., children over 12 Erich, Lars, Marit, Marit. He (b. about 1753, so about 10) was too young to be listed. Inferred: he married in 1783 as 'Soldat Peder Ingebrigtss. Søreneboe' and lived on at Søndre Eneboe; Engebret Larsen is the only Engebret on either Eneboe farm in 1763; and his first child (1784) was named Gurri, as for Guri Pedersdatter. His own baptism (Lesja 9236, about canvases 175-186, 1752-54; 'Engebret Eneboes Peder') is unread. ¶6

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Homonym warning: a different Peter Ingebretsen of Morken, with wife Guri Pedersdatter, had a son Ingebret baptised at Verket on 7 Oct 1798 (born 29 Sep; scan read, p. 514). That child is not the Eeneboe Ingebret. ¶3
