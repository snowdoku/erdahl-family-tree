About the family

Father of Anna Margaretha Becher (see her page). ¶1a

Life story

Horrheim Becher rows read: a child of Augustinus x Anna Maria (22 Dec 1679), Augustin (1708), Wilhelm Christian (1740), and the 1712 burial of Hanß Jacob Becher, relative 'Hanß Jerg' (so probably a child of Hanß Jerg, not her father). No Anna Margaretha Becher/Becker baptism 1668-88 with a father Jacob is indexed anywhere. Still no record of him. ¶3

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

1 on the grade · 1 alternative set aside

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Graded on that link: her own marriage row at Vaihingen an der Enz on 15 Nov 1703 reads 'Anna Margaretha Bechers', relatives 'Joh. Jacob, Johann Adam' (her father, then the groom Johann Adam Knodel; record 904776330, image 1184562-00486). Continuity to the woman in the tree: Joh. Adam Knodel x Anna Margaretha baptise at Vaihingen 1705-1714, including Georg Jacob (1711). Ancestry 61023 index rows do not label roles (BRIEF audit 5 rule 3), so this rests at Probable at most until the image or a labelled FamilySearch twin is read; images not seen. Spelling 'Becher' as indexed; no other record of him found. ¶1b

Alternatives considered and set aside 1

Leads, not linked: a 'Joh. Jacob Bocker' died at Vaihingen on 26 Jul 1722 (record 4400564, image 1184565-00181); a Joh Jacob Becker x Maria Catharina baptised Elisabetha Catharina there on 7 Jan 1706 (4768234), and a Johann Jacob Becker married Anna Barbara there on 8 Feb 1718 (4776765). A Hanß Jacob Becher was buried at Horrheim on 13 Jun 1712, relative 'Hanß Jerg' (5532736). None names Anna Margaretha; no baptism of an Anna Margaretha Becher/Becker about 1670-88 is indexed anywhere near Vaihingen (the indexed Vaihingen baptisms begin about 1679, so she was probably born before that or elsewhere). ¶2
