About the family

Wife of Lars Olesen at Nørve Lille, age 62 in the 1801 census. ¶1

Father's probate read on the scan (13 Oct 2026): the estate of Anders Olsen of Hessen was registered on 12 Sep 1750: 'Anno 1750 den 12 Septembr.... efter afg. Anders Olsen... imellem hands efterladende Enke Sisille Ols Datter med Laugverge Henning Olsen H[a]lv[?]estad paa den eene Side og den Sal. Mands med hende avlede eeniste[?] Barn Cisille Anders datter gammel [age not written or illegible]. Hvorfor[?] Skifteretten til Barnets[?] Formynder denomineres de skiønsomme Mænd Rasmus Gundersen Godøen og Ole Rasmussen Hessen paa den anden Side.' The heirs were his widow Sisille Olsdatter, with her guardian (laugverge) Henning Olsen of a farm whose name is uncertain, and the couple's child Cisille Andersdatter, apparently the only one ('eeniste', uncertain reading); her guardians (formyndere) were Rasmus Gundersen of Godøya and Ole Rasmussen of Hessen. The clerk left her age blank or it is illegible. No son is named as heir, so Henning was dead by then, as his burial says. ¶10

Life story

Baptism: Sisille, daughter of Anders Olsen of Hessen, was baptised at home and the baptism was confirmed in Borgund church on Whitsunday, 5 June 1740 ('Samme dag læst ofver Anders Olsøn Hessens hiemme døbte Datter, N: Sisille'). Her godparents were Ole Jonsen, Ole Rasmussen, Kirsten Pedersdatter (who carried her), Inger Jetmundsdatter and Hille Olsdatter, all of Hessen. Read on the page image. ¶5

Confirmation: she was confirmed at Borgund on the 17th Sunday after Trinity, 2 October 1757, entered among the girls as 'Siedsele Anders d: Hessen', age 16 (no. 8 of 9 girls; 25 confirmands in all). The entry gives only her patronymic and farm: it does not name her father, her mother or her birth date. Age 16 points to a birth about 1740-41; the Sisille baptised 5 June 1740 would have been 17 years 4 months, a one-year gap within the usual rounding of confirmation ages. No other Sidsele/Sissel and no other confirmand of Hessen appears in the 1756, 1757 or 1758 groups on the same opening. This is supporting evidence that the maid of Hessen engaged in 1759 grew up at Hessen and was born about 1740, but because her father is not named, the link to Anders Olsen stays Probable. Read on the page image (canvas 244). ¶9

Engagement: on the 18th Sunday after Trinity, 14 October 1759, she was engaged (trolovet) at Borgund as the maid Sidsele Andersdatter of Hessen to the bachelor Lars Olsen of Lille Nørve. The sponsors (forlovere, sureties that there was no impediment) were Jon Larsen of Lille Nørve and Aslak Ingebrigtsen of Godøya. Read on the page image. ¶7

Married Lars Olsen 1 Jan 1760 at Borgund as Sidsele Andersdatter, a maid of Hessen. Age 75 in the 1815 census. Died 27 Jun 1821 as a retired wife (kårkone) at Lille Nørve; the index gives age 70, but the censuses give born about 1739/40. Probate registered 21 Jul 1821 (Nordre Sunnmøre, 3A 13, p. 63). Her parents are not found. ¶2

A lead for her parentage, not yet proven. She was a maid of Hessen in Borgund when she married on 1 January 1760, and the censuses put her birth about 1739-1740. In the Borgund church books for 1698-1761 the only man named Anders who fathered children while living at Hessen is Anders Olsen, with children baptized on 1 June 1732, 29 November 1735, 3 May 1739, 5 June 1740 and 24 January 1745. Two of those baptisms - 3 May 1739 and 5 June 1740 - fall exactly on her calculated birth years. The children's given names in those two entries have not yet been read, and that single check would either confirm or eliminate Anders Olsen of Hessen as her father. ¶3

For context, the 1701 male census shows Hessen held by Lauritz (born about 1675) with a son Einer Johansen (1690), another son Oelle Jetmundsen (1693), Lars Pedersen (1697) and a servant Oelle Nielsen (1683) - a crowded multi-tenant farm of the kind common close to Ålesund. ¶4

In the 1762 extra tax (49637 view 37, Lille Nørve household 4) she is 'Sidsle Andersdatter', wife of Lars Olsen, with his parents Ole Olsen and Elen Ingebrigtsdatter and his sisters Synneve and Birthe in the household. ¶12

Probate: her estate was registered at Lille Nørve on 21 July 1821, when she was a retired farm-pension wife (føderådskone). The heirs were her children: (1) Knud Larsen, married, farmer at Lille Nørve; (2) Ole Andreas Larsen, married, living at Skammerwold[?] in Nordmøre; (3) Karen Larsdatter, married to Ole Rasmussen Leene in Søndre Sunnmøre; (4) the late Cicilia Larsdatter, who had been married to Knud Hansen Alnæs, survived by three daughters, Birthe Andrine[?], Laurentze Cicilia and Henninge Sidsine Knudsdatter; and (5) Henninge Larsdatter, married to Ingebrigt Monsen Nyenstad[?] in Wolden[?]. The probate names no siblings and no parents. Read on the page image (Nordre Sunnmøre sorenskriveri, Skifteprotokoll 7, fol. 63a); place names marked [?] are uncertain readings. ¶8

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 1

Confidence raised from Probable to Confirmed by the 1750 probate (3A 17, p. 136b, read on the scan). It shows that the Sisille baptised 5 Jun 1740 to Anders Olsen of Hessen was alive in September 1750 and was then the only child of Anders Olsen and Sisille Olsdatter named as heir, and the clerk spelled her name 'Cisille', the form she used later (Cicilia/Cæcilia). Seven years later a 'Siedsele Anders d. Hessen', 16, was confirmed at Borgund (1757), and in 1759 the maid Sidsele Andersdatter of Hessen was engaged. The only other Anders with children at Hessen before 1761 married in 1756, too late to be her father. Her mother's guardian in 1750 was a Henning Olsen, probably the widow's brother (not proved), and that rare name recurs in her daughter Henninge Larsdatter and granddaughter Henninge Sidsine. No record says in so many words that the bride of 1760 is the child of 1750, but no other girl fits, so the identity is effectively certain. The one alternative left open, a Sidsele Andersdatter who came to Hessen as a servant from another parish, is not supported by any record. ¶11

Corrections 1

Correction: earlier notes say her parents were not found and that the 1739 and 1740 Hessen baptisms had not been read. Both have now been read, and she is identified with Sisille, baptised 5 June 1740, daughter of Anders Olsen of Hessen. The grounds: Sisille, Sidsele and Cæcilia/Cicilia are forms of one name. She married in 1759-60 as a maid (pige) of Hessen. Her census ages point to a birth about 1738-40 (age 62 in the 1801 census suggests about 1738-39, a year or two early; age 75 in 1815 suggests about 1739-40); the burial index's age of 70 in 1821 cannot be right for a woman engaged in 1759 and is disregarded. Anders Olsen is the only man named Anders with children at Hessen in 1698-1761 except a younger Anders Olsen who married in 1756, and his only daughter of this name was born in 1740; the 1739 Hessen baptism is a son, Joen. An indexed-records search of Digitalarkivet for a Sidsele/Sisille/Sissele daughter with a related Anders, 1734-1743, found only this Borgund baptism and one in Haus, Hordaland; the same search for Cecilia/Cicilia found no baptism. Not tested: unindexed baptisms in neighbouring parishes (Vigra, Haram, Giske) for a Cæcilia/Sissel Andersdatter who might have come to Hessen as a servant, and a burial of the 1740 Sisille before 1759. So 'only candidate' holds for Hessen and for indexed records only. Her 1821 probate names a daughter Henninge Larsdatter and a granddaughter Henninge Sidsine Knudsdatter. Henning is a rare name, and it was the name of Anders Olsen's son Henning (baptised 1735), who drowned with his father in 1750. Confidence is Probable, not Confirmed, because no record states in so many words that the bride of 1760 is the child of 1740. A Borgund confirmation entry for about 1754-56 naming her father would settle it. ¶6
