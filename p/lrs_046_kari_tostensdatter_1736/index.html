About the family

Parentage of Tosten (Probable): the baptisms of Knud (1757), Tosten (1758) and Ole (1762), sons of Asle Knudsen of Medgaardehuus, do not name the mother. She is identified as their mother through the 1756 marriage, the Sando godparents at every baptism, the name Tosten given to the second son, and the wife of Asle of Medgaarden standing godmother to a Sando child in 1762. ¶3a

Family of origin: her father was a man named Tosten of Sando, not otherwise identified; no Tosten of Sando was buried in Ål 1745-1764, and a child of a 'Tosten Sando' was buried in 1750. Probable brothers (inferred from patronymic, farm and their sponsorship of her children): Tosten Tostensen of Sando (confirmed 1745 aged 18, b. about 1727), Ole Tostensen of Sando (confirmed 1747 aged 18, a soldier, married 14 Feb 1762), and Halvor Tostensen of Sando. Her father is not added to the tree because no record gives his patronymic. ¶4

Parents (Speculative): Tosten Thordsen and Bergith (Berte) Olsdatter of Sando. The basis is the probate index card of 12 Feb 1781 after Halvor Tostensen of Sando, which names among his kin a half-brother (halvbror) Tosten Tostensen Sando, a stepfather 'Tosten Thordsen Sando (?)' and a mother 'Bergith Olsd. (?)'; the question marks are the card-maker's own. The 1723 card after Halvor's father, Torsten Halvorsen of Sando, names his widow Berte Olsdatter and one minor son, Halvor. Kari's brother in 1773 was likewise Tosten Tostensen of Sando (confirmed in the protocol text: 'Børnenes Morbroder Tosten Tostensen Sanda', the children's maternal uncle and guardian; skifteprotokoll 19 fol. 651a). If he is the same man as Halvor's half-brother, then Kari and Tosten were children of Berte Olsdatter's second marriage, to Tosten Thordsen, and Halvor Tostensen of Sando (a godfather to Kari's sons in 1758) was her maternal half-brother. ¶9a

Parents now Confirmed: Tosten Thorsen and Birgit Olsdatter of Sando. The 1791 deed of their heirs (pantebok 15, s. 308, no. 8) is made by their children Tosten Tostensen, Ole Tostensen Skjerven and Astri Tostensdatter (through her husband Knud Engebretsen Skrinde) and by Kari's children Tosten Aslesen Medgaarden, Birgit Aslesdatter and Ole Aslesen, who sell what they inherited 'efter... vores Salig afdøde Forældre og Bedste Forældre Tosten Thorsen og Birgit Olsdatter Sandoe'. This supersedes the 'Parents (Speculative)' note. Siblings: Tosten Tostensen, Ole Tostensen (Skjerven), Astri Tostensdatter, and half-brother Halvor Tostensen (d. 1780). ¶11

Life story

Confirmation: 'Karj Tostensdr. Sando', aged 17, was confirmed in Ål church on Trinity Sunday (17 Jun 1753), which places her birth in about 1736. She is the only Kari Tostensdatter of Sando confirmed in Ål from 1745 to 1769. ¶1

Marriage: as 'Pig: Kari Tostensdtr: Sando' (the maiden Kari Tostensdatter of Sando) she was betrothed on 9 Apr 1756 and married on the 11th of June or July 1756 (the month is torn) in Ål to the bachelor Asle Knudsen of Dybsjorde. ¶2

Death: between 12 Oct 1773, when she was a living widow at her husband's probate, and 18 Feb 1785, the date of her own probate. This supersedes the earlier note that her death was not found. The Ål burials 1773-1785 (ministerialbok 1771-1806, source 8545) have not been read and would give the date. ¶7

Probate: the probate index card for Medgaarden in Ål records the skifte of 18 Feb 1785 after 'Kari Tostensd. †' (deceased), wife of the late Asle Knudsen, whose own skifte it cites as 12 Oct 1773. The heirs were her children Knud Aslesen, of age; Tosten Aslesen, 26; Ole Aslesen, 23; Ragndie Aslesdatter, married to Lars Tollefsen Opsata[?]; and Bergit Aslesdatter, 20, unmarried. Gross 275 riksdaler, net 257 riksdaler 0 ort 6 skilling; land Medgaarden, 3 løbel, valued at 175 riksdaler. Ringerike og Hallingdal skifteprotokoll 20b, fol. 674a (index card; protocol text not read). In the 1773 probate after her husband she appears as his widow, 'Karie Tostensdatter', with her brother Tosten Tostensen of Sando ('Børnenes Morbroder Tosten Tostensen Sanda', the children's maternal uncle and guardian; skifteprotokoll 19 fol. 651a, protocol text). ¶6

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Confidence: raised from Probable to Confirmed by the probate index cards of 1773 and 1785, which name her as Asle Knudsen's wife and the mother of Knud, Tosten and Ole, at ages that fit their baptisms, and name her brother Tosten Tostensen of Sando, confirming her as Kari Tostensdatter of Sando who married Asle Knudsen in 1756. ¶8

Current grade (from patch 010,; stale-note list section A): her father is linked, Speculative, to Tosten Thordsen (see his page), and her mother, Speculative, to Bergith Olsdatter (see her page); see the note 'Parents (Speculative)'. This supersedes the older sentence 'Her father is not added to the tree because no record gives his patronymic.' ¶10
