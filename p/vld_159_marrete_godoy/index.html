Life story

Churching (index): 'Marrete', wife of Ingebret of Godøen, was introduced (churched) at Borgund on 20 Nov 1701 (Borgund ministerialbok 1698-1739, p. 439 no. 45; py00000000089013). That is ten weeks after the baptism on 7 Sep 1701 of Siur, son of Ingebret Siurss. of Godøen; Ingebret Siursen is the only father named Ingebret at Godøen in the Borgund baptisms of 1701, so she is very probably Siur's mother. ¶1

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.

2 on the grade · 1 open question

Confidence: the grade and how it changed 2

Speculative mother of Sivert (Siur) Ingebrigtsen of Djup (see his page); graded on her link to Sivert. This rests on the same Speculative identification as Ingebret Siursen (see his page): that the Siur baptised at Godøy in 1701 is Sivert of Djup. Nothing yet ties that child to Djup. Her patronymic is not recorded. ¶2

Upgrade: Speculative to Probable, graded on her link to Sivert (Siur) Ingebrigtsen of Djup (see his page). The Giske farm history (Borgund og Giske bd. 3, canvas 469, rebuilt from word boxes) gives Ingebrigt Sjursen of Godøy (brukar 1690-1730) a wife 'Margrete' who died in 1735 aged 75 (so b. about 1660); 'Marrete' is the church book's spelling of Margrete. The same book (canvas 496) makes the Djup tenant 'Sjur Ingebrigtsen Godøy (1701-66)'. She was churched as Ingebret's wife on 20 Nov 1701, ten weeks after Siur's baptism. No earlier wife of Ingebrigt is mentioned and their known children run from about 1695 (Lars) to 1704, so she is the mother of the 1701 child. Probable, because the identification of the 1701 child with Sivert of Djup rests on the compiled book, and the dates (b./d.) are from rebuilt text (ABT). ¶3
