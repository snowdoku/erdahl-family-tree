About the family

First husband of Brynhild Kulsetmoen. The biography on her Find a Grave memorial, written by a descendant of her second husband's brother, says: 'Brynhild was born in Selbu, Norway. She married Nils Klegseth and had three daughters. Nils died when the girls were very young; and Brynhild accepted her brother's invitation to come to Marietta, MN to live.' A granddaughter's account adds that he died in a work accident. Klegseth is itself a Selbu farm name, and the Lac qui Parle County clerk used 'Klegseth' for Kulsetmo when registering Brynhild's second marriage, so the two names are easily confused in the American records. ¶1

Life story

His three daughters by Brynhild - Gina (1891-1967), Oline Klegseth Eidem Rask (1892-1993) and Ingeborg M. Klegseth-Eidem Olson (1897-1978) - all took the surname Eidem from their stepfather Anders Andersson Eidem, which is why they appear in American records as Eidems. ¶2

How sure are we?

Probable. The records fit well and nothing contradicts them, but no single record states it outright.
